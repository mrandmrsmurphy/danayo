> 
> The motto emphasizes two principles:
> 1. **[[韓国]][[音韻]][[基準]]** — back when 어 meant /e/ rather than /ʌ̹/, and before coda /t/ became /l/.
> 2. **<ruby>[[注音符号]]<rt>ㄐㄨㄛㄇㄅㄨㄏㄚㄨ</rt></ruby>** as the phonological script — ensuring that every sound in <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> is **[[明確]]・[[精密]]**.   
>
> Thus **【<ruby>朝鮮正音<rt>ㄐㄚㄨㄙ⼶ㄋㄐㄧㄫ·ㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>】** stands as a Classical reminder that **[[発音]][[権威]]** lies with the Korean tradition, continuing the legacy of King Sejong’s reforms.

# 音韻論
The 単亜語[[音韻]] is determined in two ways. In some cases it represents **[[平均]]** or **[[中庸]]** across **[[中日韓越]][[言語]]**. In other cases, when no middle ground exists, it follows **[[簡易]][[原則]]**, choosing **[[学習者]][[容易]][[発音]]**.

[[音韻]][[体系]] of CJKV [[差異]]大.  漢語・韓語・越語 are fundamentally based on a contrast between **送気声母** vs. **不送気声母**, with [[韻母]] that often include [[中央]][[母音]]. [[日本語]], by contrast, is more similar to **[[西洋]]語**, organized around a contrast of **有声音** vs. **無声音** and a system of **[[基本]]母音** for rimes.

**[[音素]][[結合]][[規則]]** also varies across the region. Some languages prefer the simple **子音–母音**, while others allow **子音–拗音–母音–子音** (CGVC: Consonant–Glide–Vowel–Consonant).

| -------------------| ----------- | -------------- | -------------- | ------------ |
| ** （鼻音）**       | ㄇ `/m/`     | ㄋ `/n/`        |                | ㄫ `/ŋ/`     |
| ** （濁塞音）**     | ㄅ `/b/`     | ㄉ `/d/`        | ㄐ `/ʥ/`        | ㄍ `/g/`      |
| ** （送気塞音）**   | ㄆ `/pʰ/`     | ㄊ `/tʰ/`       | ㄑ `/cʰ/`       | ㄎ `/kʰ/`     |
| ** （摩擦音）**     | ㄈ `/f/`     | ㄙ `/s ~ ɕ/`     |                | ㄏ `/x ~ h/`  |

単亜語子音 are organized by **四部位** and **五調音法**. Consonants may be **唇音**, **歯茎音**, **硬口蓋音**, or **舌背音**. They may be **鼻音**, **濁塞音**, **送気塞音**, **摩擦音**, or **接近音／弾音**. The complete inventory is shown in 図2.1.

A high degree of variance (**[[異音]][[差異]]**) is tolerated among the “dialects” (**[[方言]]**) influenced by each **原言語**. The phoneme /x/ is often realized as /h/ for **非漢語話者**. **日本語以外**, speakers may **塞音非送気化** but not consistently **有声化不安定**. **日本語話者** show the opposite tendency, sometimes **送気不十分**. Despite these differences, all realizations are **相互理解可能** across the region.
### 歯擦音

The phoneme /s/ (**無声音歯擦音**) undergoes **硬口蓋化** to /ɕ/ (**硬口蓋擦音**) before the high front vowel /i/ (**前舌高母音**) or the glide /j/ (**半母音**). This process is widely found in **東亜諸語** and is not **非音位的過程** in <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby>.

**漢語話者** often extend this change to **sw音節**, producing a retroflex onset (**硬口蓋化音頭**). This extension is optional but should be recognizable for speakers of <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby>.

### 硬口蓋音

**硬口蓋塞音** may also be realized as **後部歯茎破擦音** without restriction (**自由異音**).

### 日本語話者の注意点

For **日本語話者**, certain substitutions are **許容異音**, while others are **回避すべき異音**.

- As with all speakers, the /f/ may be realized as /ɸ/ or  /ɸᵝ/ freely.
- The nasal /ŋ/ (**軟口蓋鼻音**) may be pronounced as /ɴ/ (**口蓋垂鼻音**), following **日本語古層**, and this remains **理解可能**.
- The fricative /h/ (**声門摩擦音**) should not be replaced by /ɸᵝ/ (**両唇摩擦音**), a **日本語異音** in their own language.
- The palatal stop /j/ (**硬口蓋塞音**) may be realized as /dʑ/ (**硬口蓋破擦音**) — **問題なし**.
- Japanese speakers should be attentive to **送気**, since <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> maintains **送気・不送気の対立**, which is weaker in Japanese phonology.
### 越南語話者の注意点

For **越南語話者**, certain features of native pronunciation appear in <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> as well.

- **塞音** may sometimes be produced as **内破音**, though this is non-standard. This remains broadly **理解可能** across the region.
- The common lenition /g/ → /ɣ/ (**有声軟口蓋破裂音の弱化**, becoming **有声軟口蓋摩擦音**) should be **禁止**, since it causes confusion with the distinct phoneme /x/ (**無声音摩擦音**).
- Vietnamese speakers should attend to **語末子音**, since <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> retains **音節末対立** that Vietnamese phonology tends to **中和**.
### 声門閉鎖音

To maintain **音節分界**, especially in **形態音韻論的環境**, speakers may insert a glottal stop /ʔ/ (**声門閉鎖音**) at the beginning of syllables with a null onset (**無声母音節**).

However, in practice most **語** are intelligible — **挿入不要**. For example, forms such as 児児 or 異音 are normally **文脈上明瞭** and do not require a glottal onset.

Thus **母音[[連続]]** is permitted in <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby>, as long as the surrounding context provides **文脈理解可**. This mirrors practices in many East Asian languages, where it is tolerated.

##  母音

**漢語話者** must adjust to **基本母音** in nearly every foreign language they study — whether <ruby>[韓国語](/words/韓国語.md)<rt>ㄏㄚㄋㄍㄛㄎ·⼄</rt></ruby>, <ruby>[日本語](/words/日本語.md)<rt>ㄋㄧㄊㄅㄛㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby>, <ruby>[英語](/words/英語.md)<rt>ㄝㄫ⼄</rt></ruby>, or others. <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> adopts **簡明かつ包摂的体系**, as shown in 図2.2.

**図 2.2: 単亜語母音表**

|                |  （前舌）     |  （中舌）    |  （後舌）      |
|----------------|--------------------|---------------------|--------------------|
| ** （高母音）** | ㄧ `/i ~ ɪ/`         |                     | ㄨ `/u ~ ʊ/`        |
| ** （中母音）**  | ㄝ `/e ~ ɛ/`         |                     | ㄛ `/o ~ ɔ/`        |
| ** （低母音）**  |                    | ㄚ `/a ~ ä ~ ɑ/`    |                    |

### 長母音

Two so-called **長母音** are possible in the 単亜語音節構造: ㄧㄧ and ㄨㄨ. Because not all **聴取者** can reliably distinguish **母音[[長短]]**, speakers must indicate an **後続滑音** in some manner (**方法不定**).

**具体音価非限定**. For example, _iǝ_ may be pronounced as /iɪ/, /iə/, or /iɨ/, while _uo_ may be realized as /uʊ/, /uɔ/, or /uə/. All of these are **容認異音**.

### 介音母音

**東亜諸語** supply different **介音母音** when breaking up **子音連続**.

- <ruby>[韓国語](/words/韓国語.md)<rt>ㄏㄚㄋㄍㄛㄎ·⼄</rt></ruby> inserts /ɯ/ (**無円唇後舌母音**).    
- <ruby>[日本語](/words/日本語.md)<rt>ㄋㄧㄊㄅㄛㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby> inserts /ɯᵝ/ (**両唇化無円唇後舌母音**).    
- **漢語普通話** inserts /ɨ/ (**中舌無円唇母音**).    
- <ruby>[越南語](/words/越南語.md)<rt>⼔ㄊㄋㄚㄇ·⼄</rt></ruby> inserts /ə/ (**中央母音**).    
- **広東語** inserts /ʊ/ (**後舌狭母音**).    

In **[[羅馬字]][[表記]]**, epenthetic vowels are written as **ǝ** (ǔ母音) to distinguish them from the normal vowel _e_ (母音). In <ruby>注音符号<rt>ㄐㄨㄛㄇㄅㄨㄏㄚㄨ</rt></ruby> **[[表記]]**, they are written as **ㄜ**.

The transcription of foreign words （外来語転写） should follow 単亜語音素結合法, breaking up consonant clusters as necessary （子音群分割）.  Degrees of foreignness can be emphasized or de-emphasized by either A) writing above an ◌, as if the syllable was just missing a character, or B) purely vertically, still showing syllable boundaries but marking a word as entirely foreign.  Note that no hanji has the ㄜ vowel, so that is always above a ◌.

For example:
- _Bach_ → <ruby>琶<rt>ㄅㄚ</rt></ruby><ruby>◌<rt>ㄏㄜ</rt></ruby> vs <ruby class="vert">⁠<rt>ㄅ<br>ㄚ</rt></ruby> ⁠ <ruby class="vert">⁠<rt>ㄏ<br>ㄜ</rt></ruby>
- _bibimbap_  → <ruby>琵<rt>ㄅㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>泛<rt>ㄅㄧㄇ</rt></ruby><ruby>乏<rt>ㄅㄚㄆ</rt></ruby> vs <ruby class="vert">⁠<rt>ㄅ<br>ㄧ</rt></ruby> ⁠ <ruby class="vert">⁠<rt>ㄅ<br>ㄧ<br>ㄇ</rt></ruby> ⁠ <ruby class="vert">⁠<rt>ㄅ<br>ㄚ<br>ㄆ</rt></ruby>
- _natto_  → <ruby>捺<rt>ㄋㄚㄊ</rt></ruby><ruby>兎<rt>ㄊㄛ</rt></ruby> vs <ruby class="vert">⁠<rt>ㄋ<br>ㄚ<br>ㄊ</rt></ruby> ⁠ <ruby class="vert">⁠<rt>ㄊ<br>ㄛ</rt></ruby>

## 音素結合規則

The basic syllable structure of <ruby>[単亜語](/words/単亜語.md)<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby>音節構造 follows a **子音＋母音** pattern with several optional elements. An onset (**声母**) is normally required, though a null onset (**無声母**) is also possible. After the onset, an optional **介音** (y,w) may appear. The nucleus (**核母音**) is one of the six vowels (母音: ㄧ, ㄝ, ㄚ, ㄛ, ㄨ, ㄜ). The coda or final (**韻尾**) may be absent, or may consist of a **鼻音** (ㄇ, ㄋ, ㄫ), a high vowel off-glide (**高母音後続**: y, w), or an unreleased voiceless stop (**無放出清塞音**: ㄆ, ㄊ, ㄎ).

- [[声母]] (onset)
- [[韻母]] (final)
	- [[介位]] (glide-in)
	- [[核位]] (nucleus)
	- [[尾位]] (coda)

Thus **最大音節式** is **(C)(G)V(C)**, where G = glide and C = consonant. This system is broadly comparable to other **東亜諸語** but intentionally **包摂的** of both **漢語韻尾** and **韓国語語末子音**.

**図 2.3: 単亜語音節構造**

|  位置         |  音素表                     |
|---------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| **声母**        | m, n, s, h, l, ∅, p, t, c, k, b, d, j, g, f |
| **介音, 任意** | y, w, ∅                                   |
| **母音**        | i, e, a, o, u, ǝ                         |
| **韻尾, 任意**   | m, n, ŋ, u, i, p, t, k, ∅                |
### 軟口蓋鼻音

The velar nasal /ŋ/ (**軟口蓋鼻音**) is permitted in **音節末** but does not occur in **音節頭**.

### 分布の制限

Certain **分布空隙** exist in 単亜語音節体系:

- The consonants **ㄉ, ㄊ, ㄑ, ㄐ** (**歯茎・硬口蓋塞音**) may not combine with a **y介音**.    
- The consonant **ㄈ** may not take any on-glide.
- A **y介音** may not be followed by the vowel ㄧ.    
- A **w介音** may only precede the vowels **ㄚ, ㄝ**.    
- In syllables with a null onset (**無声母**), **ㄨㄧ+** sequences are permitted (**許容**).    
- A **w介音** may not co-occur with the initials ㄋ, ㄌ, ㄅ, ㄆ, ㄇ (**鼻音・流音・両唇音**).

These restrictions reflect articulatory constraints rather than missing phonemes.

### 形態音韻論

Because many **話者** find **語末子音** difficult, <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> prohibits **音節境界の連音禁止**.

- <ruby>[日本語](/words/日本語.md)<rt>ㄋㄧㄊㄅㄛㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby>話者 may struggle to distinguish **語末鼻音**.    
- **漢語話者** may struggle to produce **語末塞音**.    

Therefore, no **[[連声]]** or **音節間同化** is permitted. Each syllable remains **[[音節]][[独立]]**.

Care must be taken to avoid forms like _ningan_ ㄋㄧㄋㄍㄚㄋ, where a velar nasal /ŋ/ (**軟口蓋鼻音**) alters meaning. The rule is absolute: **音節相互影響禁止**.

## 声調・韻律

Because **[[声調]]** is not present in all **東亜諸語** and learning new tones is **習得困難**, 単亜語 is **非音位的** for tone. Speakers should not attempt to convey **意味・感情情報** through intonation alone. Instead, **文末詞** (**終助詞**) are mandatory to indicate any **法** other than the **単純陳述文**.

<ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> employs pitch-accent as a prosodic aid for **語境界の区別**. Pitch-accent refers to the relative height of syllables across a phrase, rather than lexical tone.  Pitch is realized at the **句・文単位**, and no minimal pairs of words exist based on pitch alone. For example:

- <ruby>大<rt>ㄉㄚㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>学生<rt>ㄏㄚㄎㄙㄚㄫ</rt></ruby> “big student” → pitch **H HL** （高→下降）
    
- <ruby>大学生<rt>ㄉㄚㄧㄏㄚㄎㄙㄚㄫ</rt></ruby> “college student” → pitch **HLL** （高→下降→低）
    

### 高低原則

- The **述部** of an utterance is its [[最高点]].
- The **句頭**  is its 最高点.    
- The **音節主要母音** is its 最高点.    
- The **[[複合語]]初字** is its 最高点.    

This system benefits 東亜話者有利, but may be confusing for 外来話者困難. A sentence with **目的語・助詞** ends with a 下降調, similar to **陳述文終止下降** in many languages. However, because many sentences are **動詞終止文**, the intonation may resemble a **付加疑問文** — especially when **単音節動詞**.

For this reason, even **非対立疑問文** must end with the particle **乎** （乎助詞） to ensure clarity.
