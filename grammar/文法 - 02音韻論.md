>

> 2. **<ruby>注音符号<rt>ㄐㄨㄛㄇㄅㄨㄏㄚㄨ</rt></ruby>** as the phonological script — ensuring that every sound in <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> is **<ruby>明確<rt>ㄇ⼶ㄫㄎㄚㄎ</rt></ruby>・<ruby>精密<rt>ㄐㄝㄫㄇㄧㄊ</rt></ruby>**.

# 音韻論
The 単亜語<ruby>音韻<rt>ㄨㄇ·ㄨㄋ</rt></ruby> is determined in two ways. In some cases it represents **<ruby>平均<rt>ㄅ⼶ㄫㄍ⼜ㄋ</rt></ruby>** or **<ruby>中庸<rt>ㄐㄨㄫ⼄ㄫ</rt></ruby>** across **<ruby>中日韓越<rt>ㄐㄨㄫㄋㄧㄊㄏㄚㄋ⼔ㄊ</rt></ruby><ruby>言語<rt>ㄝㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby>**. In other cases, when no middle ground exists, it follows **<ruby>簡易<rt>ㄍㄚㄋ·⼶ㄎ</rt></ruby><ruby>原則<rt>⼔ㄋㄐㄨㄎ</rt></ruby>**, choosing **<ruby>学習者<rt>ㄏㄚㄎㄙㄜㄆㄑㄚ</rt></ruby><ruby>容易<rt>⼄ㄫ⼶ㄎ</rt></ruby><ruby>発音<rt>ㄈㄚㄊ·ㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>**.

<ruby>音韻<rt>ㄨㄇ·ㄨㄋ</rt></ruby><ruby>体系<rt>ㄊㄝㄧㄏㄝㄧ</rt></ruby> of CJKV <ruby>差異<rt>ㄑㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>大.  漢語・韓語・越語 are fundamentally based on a contrast between **<ruby>送気<rt>ㄙㄛㄫ·ㄎㄧㄜ</rt></ruby><ruby>声母<rt>ㄙㄧㄫㄇㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>** vs. **<ruby>不<rt>ㄅㄛㄊ</rt></ruby><ruby>送気<rt>ㄙㄛㄫ·ㄎㄧㄜ</rt></ruby><ruby>声母<rt>ㄙㄧㄫㄇㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>**, with <ruby>韻母<rt>ㄨㄋㄇㄛㄨ</rt></ruby> that often include **<ruby>中央<rt>ㄐㄨㄫ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>母音<rt>ㄇㄛㄨㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>**. <ruby>日本語<rt>ㄋㄧㄊㄅㄛㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby>, by contrast, is more similar to **<ruby>西洋<rt>ㄙㄝㄧ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>語**, organized around a contrast of **<ruby>有声<rt>⼜ㄙㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>音** vs. **<ruby>無声<rt>ㄇㄜㄙㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>音** and a system of **<ruby>基本<rt>ㄍㄧㄅㄛㄋ</rt></ruby><ruby>母音<rt>ㄇㄛㄨㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>** for rimes.

**<ruby>音素<rt>ㄨㄇㄙㄛ</rt></ruby><ruby>結合<rt>ㄍㄝㄊㄍㄛㄆ</rt></ruby><ruby>規則<rt>ㄍㄨㄧㄐㄨㄎ</rt></ruby>** also varies across the region. Some languages prefer the simple **子音–母音**, while others allow **子音–拗音–母音–子音**.

### 歯擦音

The phoneme /s/ (**<ruby>無声<rt>ㄇㄜㄙㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>音<ruby>歯<rt>ㄑㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>擦音<rt>ㄑㄚㄊ·ㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>**) undergoes **<ruby>硬口蓋<rt>ㄚㄫ·ㄎㄛㄨ·ㄍㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>化** to /ɕ/ (**<ruby>硬口蓋<rt>ㄚㄫ·ㄎㄛㄨ·ㄍㄚㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>擦音<rt>ㄑㄚㄊ·ㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>**) before the high front vowel /i/ (**前舌高母音**) or the glide /j/ (**半母音**). This process is widely found in **東亜諸語** and is not **非音位的過程** in <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby>.

**<ruby>漢語<rt>ㄏㄚㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby><ruby>話者<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄧ·ㄑㄚ</rt></ruby>** often extend this change to **sw音節**, producing a **<ruby>硬口蓋<rt>ㄚㄫ·ㄎㄛㄨ·ㄍㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>化音頭**. This extension is optional but should be recognizable for speakers of <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby>.

### 日本語話者の注意点

For **<ruby>日本語<rt>ㄋㄧㄊㄅㄛㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby><ruby>話者<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄧ·ㄑㄚ</rt></ruby>**, certain substitutions are **許容異音**, while others are **回避 xx 異音**.

- The **<ruby>軟口蓋<rt>ㄋㄝㄋ·ㄎㄛㄨ·ㄍㄚㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>鼻音<rt>ㄅㄧㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>** may be pronounced as **<ruby>口<rt>ㄎㄛㄨ</rt></ruby><ruby>蓋<rt>ㄍㄚㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>垂<rt>ㄐㄨㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>鼻音<rt>ㄅㄧㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>**, following **日本語古層**, and this remains **理解可能**.
- The **<ruby>声門<rt>ㄙㄧㄫㄇㄛㄋ</rt></ruby><ruby>擦音<rt>ㄑㄚㄊ·ㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>** should not be replaced by **<ruby>両唇<rt>ㄌ⼘ㄫㄙㄨㄋ</rt></ruby><ruby>擦音<rt>ㄑㄚㄊ·ㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>**, a **<ruby>日本語<rt>ㄋㄧㄊㄅㄛㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby>異音** in their own language.
- The **<ruby>硬口蓋<rt>ㄚㄫ·ㄎㄛㄨ·ㄍㄚㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>塞音<rt>ㄙㄚㄧ·ㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>** may be realized as **<ruby>硬口蓋<rt>ㄚㄫ·ㄎㄛㄨ·ㄍㄚㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>破擦音<rt>ㄆㄜㄑㄚㄊ·ㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>** — **問題xx**.
- Japanese speakers should be attentive to **<ruby>送気<rt>ㄙㄛㄫ·ㄎㄧㄜ</rt></ruby>**, since <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> maintains **<ruby>送気<rt>ㄙㄛㄫ·ㄎㄧㄜ</rt></ruby>・<ruby>不<rt>ㄅㄛㄊ</rt></ruby><ruby>送気<rt>ㄙㄛㄫ·ㄎㄧㄜ</rt></ruby>ti対立**, which is weaker in Japanese phonology.

For **<ruby>越南語<rt>⼔ㄊㄋㄚㄇ·⼄</rt></ruby><ruby>話者<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄧ·ㄑㄚ</rt></ruby>**, certain features of native pronunciation appear in <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> as well.

- The common lenition /g/ → /ɣ/ (**<ruby>有声<rt>⼜ㄙㄧㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>軟口蓋<rt>ㄋㄝㄋ·ㄎㄛㄨ·ㄍㄚㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>破<rt>ㄆㄜ</rt></ruby><ruby>裂<rt>ㄌㄝㄊ</rt></ruby>音ti<ruby>弱<rt>ㄋ⼘ㄎ</rt></ruby>化**, becoming **<ruby>有声<rt>⼜ㄙㄧㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>軟口蓋<rt>ㄋㄝㄋ·ㄎㄛㄨ·ㄍㄚㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>擦音<rt>ㄑㄚㄊ·ㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>**) should be **<ruby>禁止<rt>ㄍㄧㄇㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>**, since it causes confusion with the distinct phoneme **<ruby>無声<rt>ㄇㄜㄙㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>音<ruby>擦音<rt>ㄑㄚㄊ·ㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>**.

### <ruby>声門<rt>ㄙㄧㄫㄇㄛㄋ</rt></ruby><ruby>閉<rt>ㄅㄝㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>鎖<rt>ㄙ⺢</rt></ruby>音

To maintain **<ruby>音節<rt>ㄨㄇㄐㄝㄊ</rt></ruby>分界**, especially in **形態音韻論的<ruby>環境<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄋㄍ⼶ㄫ</rt></ruby>**, speakers may insert a **<ruby>声門<rt>ㄙㄧㄫㄇㄛㄋ</rt></ruby><ruby>閉<rt>ㄅㄝㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>鎖<rt>ㄙ⺢</rt></ruby>音** at the beginning of syllables with a **<ruby>無声<rt>ㄇㄜㄙㄧㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>母<rt>ㄇㄛㄨ</rt></ruby><ruby>音節<rt>ㄨㄇㄐㄝㄊ</rt></ruby>**.

### 長母音

Two so-called **長母音** are possible in the 単亜語音節構造: ㄧㄧ and ㄨㄨ. Because not all **聴取者** can reliably distinguish **<ruby>母音<rt>ㄇㄛㄨㄨㄇ</rt></ruby><ruby>長短<rt>ㄐㄚㄫㄉ⺢ㄋ</rt></ruby>**, speakers must indicate an **後続滑音** in some manner (**<ruby>方法<rt>ㄈㄚㄫㄈㄚㄆ</rt></ruby><ruby>不定<rt>ㄅㄛㄊㄐㄝㄫ</rt></ruby>**).

**具体音価非限定**. For example, _iǝ_ may be pronounced as /iɪ/, /iə/, or /iɨ/, while _uo_ may be realized as /uʊ/, /uɔ/, or /uə/. All of these are **容認異音**.

### 介音母音

**東亜諸語** supply different **介音母音** when breaking up **<ruby>子音<rt>ㄐㄜㄨㄇ</rt></ruby><ruby>連続<rt>ㄌ⼶ㄋㄙ⼄ㄎ</rt></ruby>**.

- <ruby>韓国語<rt>ㄏㄚㄋㄍㄛㄎ·⼄</rt></ruby> inserts (**無円唇後舌母音**).    
- <ruby>日本語<rt>ㄋㄧㄊㄅㄛㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby> inserts (**両唇化無円唇後舌母音**).    
- **<ruby>漢語<rt>ㄏㄚㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby><ruby>普通話<rt>ㄆㄛ·ㄊㄛㄫㄏ⺢ㄧ</rt></ruby>** inserts  (**中舌無円唇母音**).    
- <ruby>越南語<rt>⼔ㄊㄋㄚㄇ·⼄</rt></ruby> inserts (**中央母音**).    
- **広東語** inserts (**後舌狭母音**).    

In **<ruby>羅馬字<rt>ㄌㄛ·ㄇㄚㄐㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>表記<rt>ㄅ⼘ㄨㄍㄧ</rt></ruby>**, epenthetic vowels are written as **ǝ** 母音 to distinguish them from the normal vowel _e_ 母音. In <ruby>注音符号<rt>ㄐㄨㄛㄇㄅㄨㄏㄚㄨ</rt></ruby> **<ruby>表記<rt>ㄅ⼘ㄨㄍㄧ</rt></ruby>**, they are written as **ㄜ**.

The transcription of foreign words （外来語転写） should follow 単亜語音素結合法, breaking up consonant clusters as necessary （子音群分割）.  Degrees of foreignness can be emphasized or de-emphasized by either A) writing above an ◌, as if the syllable was just missing a character, or B) purely vertically, still showing syllable boundaries but marking a word as entirely foreign.  

For example:
- _Bach_ → <ruby>琶<rt>ㄅㄚ</rt></ruby><ruby>◌<rt>ㄏㄜ</rt></ruby> vs <ruby class="vert">⁠<rt>ㄅ<br>ㄚ</rt></ruby> ⁠ <ruby class="vert">⁠<rt>ㄏ<br>ㄜ</rt></ruby>
- _bibimbap_  → <ruby>琵<rt>ㄅㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>泛<rt>ㄅㄧㄇ</rt></ruby><ruby>乏<rt>ㄅㄚㄆ</rt></ruby> vs <ruby class="vert">⁠<rt>ㄅ<br>ㄧ</rt></ruby> ⁠ <ruby class="vert">⁠<rt>ㄅ<br>ㄧ<br>ㄇ</rt></ruby> ⁠ <ruby class="vert">⁠<rt>ㄅ<br>ㄚ<br>ㄆ</rt></ruby>

## 音素結合規則

The basic syllable structure of <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby>音節構造 follows a **子音＋母音** pattern with several optional elements. An **<ruby>声母<rt>ㄙㄧㄫㄇㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>** is normally required, though a **<ruby>無声<rt>ㄇㄜㄙㄧㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>母<rt>ㄇㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>** is also possible. After the onset, an optional **介音** (y,w) may appear. The nucleus (**核母音**) is one of the six vowels (母音: ㄧ, ㄝ, ㄚ, ㄛ, ㄨ, ㄜ). The coda or final (**韻尾**) may be absent, or may consist of a **<ruby>鼻音<rt>ㄅㄧㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>** (ㄇ, ㄋ, ㄫ), a high vowel off-glide (**高母音後続**: y, w), or an unreleased voiceless stop (**<ruby>無<rt>ㄇㄜ</rt></ruby><ruby>放<rt>ㄈㄚㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>出<rt>ㄑㄨㄊ</rt></ruby><ruby>清<rt>ㄑㄧㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>塞音<rt>ㄙㄚㄧ·ㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>**: ㄆ, ㄊ, ㄎ).

- <ruby>声母<rt>ㄙㄧㄫㄇㄛㄨ</rt></ruby> (onset)
- <ruby>韻母<rt>ㄨㄋㄇㄛㄨ</rt></ruby> (final)
	- <ruby>介位<rt>ㄍ⼶·⼔ㄧ</rt></ruby> (glide-in)
	- <ruby>核位<rt>ㄏㄚㄎ·⼔ㄧ</rt></ruby> (nucleus)
	- <ruby>尾位<rt>ㄇㄨㄧ·⼔ㄧ</rt></ruby> (coda)

Thus **最大音節式** is **(C)(G)V(C)**, where G = glide and C = consonant. This system is broadly comparable to other **東亜諸語** but intentionally **包摂的** of both **<ruby>漢語<rt>ㄏㄚㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby><ruby>韻尾<rt>ㄨㄋㄇㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>** and **<ruby>韓国語<rt>ㄏㄚㄋㄍㄛㄎ·⼄</rt></ruby>語末<ruby>子音<rt>ㄐㄜㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>**.

The velar nasal /ŋ/ (**<ruby>軟口蓋<rt>ㄋㄝㄋ·ㄎㄛㄨ·ㄍㄚㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>鼻音<rt>ㄅㄧㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>**) is permitted in **<ruby>音節<rt>ㄨㄇㄐㄝㄊ</rt></ruby>末** but does not occur in **<ruby>音節<rt>ㄨㄇㄐㄝㄊ</rt></ruby><ruby>頭<rt>ㄊㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>**.

### 分布の制限

Certain **分布空隙** exist in 単亜語音節体系:

- The consonants **ㄉ, ㄊ, ㄑ, ㄐ** (**<ruby>歯茎<rt>ㄑㄧ·ㄎㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>・<ruby>硬口蓋<rt>ㄚㄫ·ㄎㄛㄨ·ㄍㄚㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>塞音<rt>ㄙㄚㄧ·ㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>**) may not combine with a **y介音**.    
- The consonant **ㄈ** may not take any on-glide.
- A **y介音** may not be followed by the vowel ㄧ.    
- A **w介音** may only precede the vowels **ㄚ, ㄝ**.    
- In syllables with a null onset (**無声母**), **ㄨㄧ+** sequences are permitted (**許容**).    
- A **w介音** may not co-occur with the initials ㄋ, ㄌ, ㄅ, ㄆ, ㄇ (**<ruby>鼻音<rt>ㄅㄧㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>・<ruby>流<rt>ㄌ⼜</rt></ruby>音・<ruby>両唇<rt>ㄌ⼘ㄫㄙㄨㄋ</rt></ruby>音**).

These restrictions reflect articulatory constraints rather than missing phonemes.

### 形態音韻論

Because many **<ruby>話者<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄧ·ㄑㄚ</rt></ruby>** find 語末<ruby>子音<rt>ㄐㄜㄨㄇ</rt></ruby> difficult, <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> prohibits **<ruby>音節<rt>ㄨㄇㄐㄝㄊ</rt></ruby><ruby>境界<rt>ㄍ⼶ㄫㄍ⼶</rt></ruby>の<ruby>連<rt>ㄌ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby>音<ruby>禁止<rt>ㄍㄧㄇㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>**.

- **<ruby>日本語<rt>ㄋㄧㄊㄅㄛㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby><ruby>話者<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄧ·ㄑㄚ</rt></ruby>** may struggle to distinguish 語末<ruby>鼻音<rt>ㄅㄧㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>.    
- **<ruby>漢語<rt>ㄏㄚㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby><ruby>話者<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄧ·ㄑㄚ</rt></ruby>** may struggle to produce 語末<ruby>塞音<rt>ㄙㄚㄧ·ㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>.    

Therefore, no **<ruby>連声<rt>ㄌ⼶ㄋㄙㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>** or **<ruby>音節<rt>ㄨㄇㄐㄝㄊ</rt></ruby><ruby>間<rt>ㄍㄚㄋ</rt></ruby><ruby>同<rt>ㄉㄛㄫ</rt></ruby>化** is permitted. Each syllable remains **<ruby>音節<rt>ㄨㄇㄐㄝㄊ</rt></ruby><ruby>独立<rt>ㄉㄛㄎㄌㄧㄆ</rt></ruby>**.

Care must be taken to avoid forms like _ningan_ ㄋㄧㄋㄍㄚㄋ, where a velar nasal /ŋ/ (**<ruby>軟口蓋<rt>ㄋㄝㄋ·ㄎㄛㄨ·ㄍㄚㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>鼻音<rt>ㄅㄧㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>**) alters meaning. The rule is absolute: **<ruby>音節<rt>ㄨㄇㄐㄝㄊ</rt></ruby><ruby>相互<rt>ㄙㄚㄫㄏㄛ</rt></ruby><ruby>影響<rt>⼶ㄫㄏ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>禁止<rt>ㄍㄧㄇㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>**.

## 声調・韻律

Because **<ruby>声調<rt>ㄙㄧㄫㄐㄨㄛ</rt></ruby>** is not present in all **東亜諸語** and learning new tones is **習得困難**, 単亜語 is **非音位的** for tone. Speakers should not attempt to convey **<ruby>意味<rt>ㄜ·ㄇㄨㄧ</rt></ruby>・<ruby>感情<rt>ㄍㄚㄇㄑㄧㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>情報<rt>ㄑㄧㄫㄅㄚㄨ</rt></ruby>** through intonation alone. Instead, **文末詞** (**終助詞**) are mandatory to indicate any **<ruby>法<rt>ㄈㄚㄆ</rt></ruby>** other than the **単純<ruby>陳述<rt>ㄐㄧㄋㄙㄨㄊ</rt></ruby>文**.

<ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> employs pitch-accent as a prosodic aid for **語境界の区別**. Pitch-accent refers to the relative height of syllables across a phrase, rather than lexical tone.  Pitch is realized at the **<ruby>句<rt>ㄍㄨ</rt></ruby>・文単位**, and no minimal pairs of words exist based on pitch alone. For example:

- <ruby>大<rt>ㄉㄚㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>学生<rt>ㄏㄚㄎㄙㄚㄫ</rt></ruby> “big student” → pitch **H HL** （高→下降）
    
- <ruby>大学生<rt>ㄉㄚㄧㄏㄚㄎㄙㄚㄫ</rt></ruby> “college student” → pitch **HLL** （高→下降→低）
    

### 高低原則

- The **述部** of an utterance is its **<ruby>最高点<rt>ㄐ⼔ㄍㄚㄨㄉㄝㄇ</rt></ruby>**.
- The **句頭**  is its <ruby>最高点<rt>ㄐ⼔ㄍㄚㄨㄉㄝㄇ</rt></ruby>.    
- The **音節主要母音** is its <ruby>最高点<rt>ㄐ⼔ㄍㄚㄨㄉㄝㄇ</rt></ruby>.    
- The **<ruby>複合語<rt>ㄅㄨㄎㄍㄛㄆ·⼄</rt></ruby>初字** is its <ruby>最高点<rt>ㄐ⼔ㄍㄚㄨㄉㄝㄇ</rt></ruby>.    

This system benefits 東亜話者有利, but may be confusing for 外来話者困難. A sentence with **<ruby>目的<rt>ㄇㄨㄎㄉㄝㄎ</rt></ruby>語・<ruby>助詞<rt>ㄐㄛㄙㄚ</rt></ruby>** ends with a **<ruby>下降<rt>ㄏㄚㄍㄚㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>調<rt>ㄐㄨㄛ</rt></ruby>**, similar to **<ruby>陳述<rt>ㄐㄧㄋㄙㄨㄊ</rt></ruby>文終止<ruby>下降<rt>ㄏㄚㄍㄚㄫ</rt></ruby>** in many languages. However, because many sentences are **<ruby>動詞<rt>ㄉㄛㄫㄙㄚ</rt></ruby>終止文**, the intonation may resemble a **付加疑問文** — especially when **単音節<ruby>動詞<rt>ㄉㄛㄫㄙㄚ</rt></ruby>**.

For this reason, even **非対立疑問文** must end with the particle **乎** （乎助詞） to ensure clarity.
