# <ruby>音韻<rt>ㄨㄇ·ㄨㄋ</rt></ruby><ruby>論<rt>ㄌㄛㄋ</rt></ruby>
The <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby><ruby>音韻<rt>ㄨㄇ·ㄨㄋ</rt></ruby> is determined in two ways. <ruby>音韻<rt>ㄨㄇ·ㄨㄋ</rt></ruby><ruby>体系<rt>ㄊㄝㄧㄏㄝㄧ</rt></ruby> of CJKV <ruby>差異<rt>ㄑㄚㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>大<rt>ㄉㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>.  <ruby>漢語<rt>ㄏㄚㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby>・[[韓語]]・[[越語]] are fundamentally based on a contrast between **<ruby>送気<rt>ㄙㄛㄫ·ㄎㄧㄜ</rt></ruby><ruby>声母<rt>ㄙㄧㄫㄇㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>** vs. **<ruby>不<rt>ㄅㄛㄊ</rt></ruby><ruby>送気<rt>ㄙㄛㄫ·ㄎㄧㄜ</rt></ruby><ruby>声母<rt>ㄙㄧㄫㄇㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>**, with <ruby>韻母<rt>ㄨㄋㄇㄛㄨ</rt></ruby> that often include **<ruby>中央<rt>ㄐㄨㄫ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>母音<rt>ㄇㄛㄨㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>**. <ruby>日本語<rt>ㄋㄧㄊㄅㄛㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby>, by contrast, is more similar to [[西洋語]], organized around a contrast of **[[有声音]]** vs. **[[無声音]]** and a system of **<ruby>基本<rt>ㄍㄧㄅㄛㄋ</rt></ruby><ruby>母音<rt>ㄇㄛㄨㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>** for rimes.

**<ruby>音素<rt>ㄨㄇㄙㄛ</rt></ruby><ruby>結合<rt>ㄍㄝㄊㄍㄛㄆ</rt></ruby><ruby>規則<rt>ㄍㄨㄧㄐㄨㄎ</rt></ruby>** also varies across the region. Some languages prefer the simple <ruby>子音<rt>ㄐㄜㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>–<ruby>母音<rt>ㄇㄛㄨㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>, while others allow <ruby>子音<rt>ㄐㄜㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>–[[拗音]]–<ruby>母音<rt>ㄇㄛㄨㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>–<ruby>子音<rt>ㄐㄜㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>.
