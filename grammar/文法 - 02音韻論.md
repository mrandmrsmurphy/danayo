This phrase recalls the title **訓民正音** （“Correct Sounds for the Instruction of the People”）, the proclamation of <ruby>世宗<rt>ㄙㄝㄐㄛㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>大王<rt>ㄉㄚㄧ⺢ㄫ</rt></ruby>, <ruby>朝鮮<rt>ㄐㄚㄨㄙ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby><ruby>王朝<rt>⺢ㄫㄐㄚㄨ</rt></ruby> in 1446 that introduced <ruby>諺文<rt>ㄋ⼶ㄋㄇㄨㄋ</rt></ruby>. Just as Hunminjeongeum established **<ruby>正音<rt>ㄐㄧㄫㄨㄇ</rt></ruby><ruby>体系<rt>ㄊㄝㄧㄏㄝㄧ</rt></ruby>** for <ruby>韓国語<rt>ㄏㄚㄋㄍㄛㄎ·⼄</rt></ruby>, <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> adopts <ruby>注音符号<rt>ㄐㄨㄛㄇㄅㄨㄏㄚㄨ</rt></ruby> as **<ruby>音韻<rt>ㄨㄇ·ㄨㄋ</rt></ruby><ruby>文字<rt>ㄇㄨㄋㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>**.
> 
> The motto emphasizes two principles:
> 1. **<ruby>韓国<rt>ㄏㄚㄋㄍㄛㄎ</rt></ruby><ruby>音韻<rt>ㄨㄇ·ㄨㄋ</rt></ruby><ruby>基準<rt>ㄍㄧㄐㄨㄋ</rt></ruby>** — back when 어 meant /e/ rather than /ʌ̹/, and before coda /t/ became /l/.
> 2. **<ruby>注音符号<rt>ㄐㄨㄛㄇㄅㄨㄏㄚㄨ</rt></ruby>** as the phonological script — ensuring that every sound in <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> is **<ruby>明確<rt>ㄇ⼶ㄫㄎㄚㄎ</rt></ruby>・<ruby>精密<rt>ㄐㄝㄫㄇㄧㄊ</rt></ruby>**.   
>
> Thus **【<ruby>朝鮮正音<rt>ㄐㄚㄨㄙ⼶ㄋㄐㄧㄫ·ㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>】** stands as a Classical reminder that **<ruby>発音<rt>ㄈㄚㄊ·ㄨㄇ</rt></ruby><ruby>権威<rt>ㄍ⼔ㄋ·ㄛㄧ</rt></ruby>** lies with the Korean tradition, continuing the legacy of King Sejong’s reforms.

# 音韻論
The 単亜語<ruby>音韻<rt>ㄨㄇ·ㄨㄋ</rt></ruby> is determined in two ways. In some cases it represents **<ruby>平均<rt>ㄅ⼶ㄫㄍ⼜ㄋ</rt></ruby>** or **<ruby>中庸<rt>ㄐㄨㄫ⼄ㄫ</rt></ruby>** across **<ruby>中日韓越<rt>ㄐㄨㄫㄋㄧㄊㄏㄚㄋ⼔ㄊ</rt></ruby><ruby>言語<rt>ㄝㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby>**. In other cases, when no middle ground exists, it follows **<ruby>簡易<rt>ㄍㄚㄋ·⼶ㄎ</rt></ruby><ruby>原則<rt>⼔ㄋㄐㄨㄎ</rt></ruby>**, choosing **<ruby>学習者<rt>ㄏㄚㄎㄙㄜㄆㄑㄚ</rt></ruby><ruby>容易<rt>⼄ㄫ⼶ㄎ</rt></ruby><ruby>発音<rt>ㄈㄚㄊ·ㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>**.

<ruby>音韻<rt>ㄨㄇ·ㄨㄋ</rt></ruby><ruby>体系<rt>ㄊㄝㄧㄏㄝㄧ</rt></ruby> of CJKV <ruby>差異<rt>ㄑㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>大.  漢語・韓語・越語 are fundamentally based on a contrast between **送気声母** vs. **不送気声母**, with <ruby>韻母<rt>ㄨㄋㄇㄛㄨ</rt></ruby> that often include **<ruby>中央<rt>ㄐㄨㄫ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>母音<rt>ㄇㄛㄨㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>**. <ruby>日本語<rt>ㄋㄧㄊㄅㄛㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby>, by contrast, is more similar to **<ruby>西洋<rt>ㄙㄝㄧ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>語**, organized around a contrast of **有声音** vs. **無声音** and a system of **<ruby>基本<rt>ㄍㄧㄅㄛㄋ</rt></ruby>母音** for rimes.

**<ruby>音素<rt>ㄨㄇㄙㄛ</rt></ruby><ruby>結合<rt>ㄍㄝㄊㄍㄛㄆ</rt></ruby><ruby>規則<rt>ㄍㄨㄧㄐㄨㄎ</rt></ruby>** also varies across the region. Some languages prefer the simple **子音–母音**, while others allow **子音–拗音–母音–子音** (CGVC: Consonant–Glide–Vowel–Consonant).

単亜語子音 are organized by **四部位** and **五調音法**. Consonants may be **唇音**, **歯茎音**, **硬口蓋音**, or **舌背音**. They may be **鼻音**, **濁塞音**, **送気塞音**, **摩擦音**, or **接近音／弾音**. The complete inventory is shown in 図2.1.

A high degree of variance (**<ruby>異音<rt>ㄧㄨㄇ</rt></ruby><ruby>差異<rt>ㄑㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>**) is tolerated among the “dialects” (**<ruby>方言<rt>ㄈㄚㄫㄝㄋ</rt></ruby>**) influenced by each 原言語. The phoneme /x/ is often realized as /h/ for **<ruby>非<rt>ㄈㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>漢語<rt>ㄏㄚㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby><ruby>話者<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄧ·ㄑㄚ</rt></ruby>**. 日本語以外, speakers may 塞音非送気化 but not consistently 有声化不安定. **<ruby>日本語<rt>ㄋㄧㄊㄅㄛㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby><ruby>話者<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄧ·ㄑㄚ</rt></ruby>** show the opposite tendency, sometimes 送気不十分. Despite these differences, all realizations are **<ruby>相互<rt>ㄙㄚㄫㄏㄛ</rt></ruby>理解可能** across the region.
### 歯擦音

The phoneme /s/ (**無声音歯擦音**) undergoes **硬口蓋化** to /ɕ/ (**硬口蓋擦音**) before the high front vowel /i/ (**前舌高母音**) or the glide /j/ (**半母音**). This process is widely found in **東亜諸語** and is not **非音位的過程** in <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby>.

**<ruby>漢語<rt>ㄏㄚㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby><ruby>話者<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄧ·ㄑㄚ</rt></ruby>** often extend this change to **sw音節**, producing a retroflex onset (**硬口蓋化音頭**). This extension is optional but should be recognizable for speakers of <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby>.

### 硬口蓋音

**硬口蓋塞音** may also be realized as **後部歯茎破擦音** without restriction (**自由異音**).

### 日本語話者の注意点

For **<ruby>日本語<rt>ㄋㄧㄊㄅㄛㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby><ruby>話者<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄧ·ㄑㄚ</rt></ruby>**, certain substitutions are **許容異音**, while others are **回避すべき異音**.

- As with all speakers, the /f/ may be realized as /ɸ/ or  /ɸᵝ/ freely.
- The nasal /ŋ/ (**軟口蓋鼻音**) may be pronounced as /ɴ/ (**口蓋垂鼻音**), following **日本語古層**, and this remains **理解可能**.
- The fricative /h/ (**声門摩擦音**) should not be replaced by /ɸᵝ/ (**両唇摩擦音**), a **<ruby>日本語<rt>ㄋㄧㄊㄅㄛㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby>異音** in their own language.
- The palatal stop /j/ (**硬口蓋塞音**) may be realized as /dʑ/ (**硬口蓋破擦音**) — **問題なし**.
- Japanese speakers should be attentive to **送気**, since <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> maintains **送気・不送気の対立**, which is weaker in Japanese phonology.
### 越南語話者の注意点

For **<ruby>越南語<rt>⼔ㄊㄋㄚㄇ·⼄</rt></ruby><ruby>話者<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄧ·ㄑㄚ</rt></ruby>**, certain features of native pronunciation appear in <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> as well.

- **塞音** may sometimes be produced as **内破音**, though this is non-standard. This remains broadly **理解可能** across the region.
- The common lenition /g/ → /ɣ/ (**有声軟口蓋破裂音の弱化**, becoming **有声軟口蓋摩擦音**) should be **<ruby>禁止<rt>ㄍㄧㄇㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>**, since it causes confusion with the distinct phoneme /x/ (**無声音摩擦音**).
- Vietnamese speakers should attend to **語末子音**, since <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> retains **音節末対立** that Vietnamese phonology tends to **中和**.
### 声門閉鎖音

To maintain **<ruby>音節<rt>ㄨㄇㄐㄝㄊ</rt></ruby>分界**, especially in **形態音韻論的<ruby>環境<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄋㄍ⼶ㄫ</rt></ruby>**, speakers may insert a glottal stop /ʔ/ (**声門閉鎖音**) at the beginning of syllables with a null onset (**無声母音節**).

However, in practice most 語 are intelligible — **<ruby>挿入<rt>ㄑㄚㄆㄋㄧㄆ</rt></ruby><ruby>不要<rt>ㄅㄛㄊ⼄ㄨ</rt></ruby>**. For example, forms such as 児児 or 異音 are normally **文脈上<ruby>明瞭<rt>ㄇ⼶ㄫㄌ⼘ㄨ</rt></ruby>** and do not require a glottal onset.

Thus **<ruby>母音<rt>ㄇㄛㄨㄨㄇ</rt></ruby><ruby>連続<rt>ㄌ⼶ㄋㄙ⼄ㄎ</rt></ruby>** is permitted in <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby>, as long as the surrounding context provides **文脈理解可**. This mirrors practices in many East Asian languages, where it is tolerated.

##  母音

**<ruby>漢語<rt>ㄏㄚㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby><ruby>話者<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄧ·ㄑㄚ</rt></ruby>** must adjust to **<ruby>基本<rt>ㄍㄧㄅㄛㄋ</rt></ruby><ruby>母音<rt>ㄇㄛㄨㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>** in nearly every foreign language they study — whether <ruby>韓国語<rt>ㄏㄚㄋㄍㄛㄎ·⼄</rt></ruby>, <ruby>日本語<rt>ㄋㄧㄊㄅㄛㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby>, <ruby>英語<rt>ㄝㄫ⼄</rt></ruby>, or others. <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> adopts **簡明かつ包摂的<ruby>体系<rt>ㄊㄝㄧㄏㄝㄧ</rt></ruby>**, as shown in 図2.2.

### 長母音

Two so-called **長母音** are possible in the 単亜語音節構造: ㄧㄧ and ㄨㄨ. Because not all **聴取者** can reliably distinguish **<ruby>母音<rt>ㄇㄛㄨㄨㄇ</rt></ruby><ruby>長短<rt>ㄐㄚㄫㄉ⺢ㄋ</rt></ruby>**, speakers must indicate an **後続滑音** in some manner (**<ruby>方法<rt>ㄈㄚㄫㄈㄚㄆ</rt></ruby><ruby>不定<rt>ㄅㄛㄊㄐㄝㄫ</rt></ruby>**).

**具体音価非限定**. For example, _iǝ_ may be pronounced as /iɪ/, /iə/, or /iɨ/, while _uo_ may be realized as /uʊ/, /uɔ/, or /uə/. All of these are **容認異音**.

### 介音母音

**東亜諸語** supply different **介音母音** when breaking up **<ruby>子音<rt>ㄐㄜㄨㄇ</rt></ruby><ruby>連続<rt>ㄌ⼶ㄋㄙ⼄ㄎ</rt></ruby>**.

- <ruby>韓国語<rt>ㄏㄚㄋㄍㄛㄎ·⼄</rt></ruby> inserts /ɯ/ (**無円唇後舌母音**).    
- <ruby>日本語<rt>ㄋㄧㄊㄅㄛㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby> inserts /ɯᵝ/ (**両唇化無円唇後舌母音**).    
- **<ruby>漢語<rt>ㄏㄚㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby><ruby>普通話<rt>ㄆㄛ·ㄊㄛㄫㄏ⺢ㄧ</rt></ruby>** inserts /ɨ/ (**中舌無円唇母音**).    
- <ruby>越南語<rt>⼔ㄊㄋㄚㄇ·⼄</rt></ruby> inserts /ə/ (**中央母音**).    
- **広東語** inserts /ʊ/ (**後舌狭母音**).    

In **<ruby>羅馬字<rt>ㄌㄛ·ㄇㄚㄐㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>表記<rt>ㄅ⼘ㄨㄍㄧ</rt></ruby>**, epenthetic vowels are written as **ǝ** (ǔ母音) to distinguish them from the normal vowel _e_ (母音). In <ruby>注音符号<rt>ㄐㄨㄛㄇㄅㄨㄏㄚㄨ</rt></ruby> **<ruby>表記<rt>ㄅ⼘ㄨㄍㄧ</rt></ruby>**, they are written as **ㄜ**.

The transcription of foreign words （外来語転写） should follow 単亜語音素結合法, breaking up consonant clusters as necessary （子音群分割）.  Degrees of foreignness can be emphasized or de-emphasized by either A) writing above an ◌, as if the syllable was just missing a character, or B) purely vertically, still showing syllable boundaries but marking a word as entirely foreign.  Note that no hanji has the ㄜ vowel, so that is always above a ◌.

For example:
- _Bach_ → <ruby>琶<rt>ㄅㄚ</rt></ruby><ruby>◌<rt>ㄏㄜ</rt></ruby> vs <ruby class="vert">⁠<rt>ㄅ<br>ㄚ</rt></ruby> ⁠ <ruby class="vert">⁠<rt>ㄏ<br>ㄜ</rt></ruby>
- _bibimbap_  → <ruby>琵<rt>ㄅㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>泛<rt>ㄅㄧㄇ</rt></ruby><ruby>乏<rt>ㄅㄚㄆ</rt></ruby> vs <ruby class="vert">⁠<rt>ㄅ<br>ㄧ</rt></ruby> ⁠ <ruby class="vert">⁠<rt>ㄅ<br>ㄧ<br>ㄇ</rt></ruby> ⁠ <ruby class="vert">⁠<rt>ㄅ<br>ㄚ<br>ㄆ</rt></ruby>
- _natto_  → <ruby>捺<rt>ㄋㄚㄊ</rt></ruby><ruby>兎<rt>ㄊㄛ</rt></ruby> vs <ruby class="vert">⁠<rt>ㄋ<br>ㄚ<br>ㄊ</rt></ruby> ⁠ <ruby class="vert">⁠<rt>ㄊ<br>ㄛ</rt></ruby>

## 音素結合規則

The basic syllable structure of <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby>音節構造 follows a **子音＋母音** pattern with several optional elements. An onset (**声母**) is normally required, though a null onset (**無声母**) is also possible. After the onset, an optional **介音** (y,w) may appear. The nucleus (**核母音**) is one of the six vowels (母音: ㄧ, ㄝ, ㄚ, ㄛ, ㄨ, ㄜ). The coda or final (**韻尾**) may be absent, or may consist of a **鼻音** (ㄇ, ㄋ, ㄫ), a high vowel off-glide (**高母音後続**: y, w), or an unreleased voiceless stop (**無放出清塞音**: ㄆ, ㄊ, ㄎ).

- <ruby>声母<rt>ㄙㄧㄫㄇㄛㄨ</rt></ruby> (onset)
- <ruby>韻母<rt>ㄨㄋㄇㄛㄨ</rt></ruby> (final)
	- <ruby>介位<rt>ㄍ⼶·⼔ㄧ</rt></ruby> (glide-in)
	- <ruby>核位<rt>ㄏㄚㄎ·⼔ㄧ</rt></ruby> (nucleus)
	- <ruby>尾位<rt>ㄇㄨㄧ·⼔ㄧ</rt></ruby> (coda)

Thus **最大音節式** is **(C)(G)V(C)**, where G = glide and C = consonant. This system is broadly comparable to other **東亜諸語** but intentionally **包摂的** of both **漢語韻尾** and **韓国語語末子音**.

**図 2.3: 単亜語音節構造**

|  位置         |  音素表                     |
|---------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| **声母**        | m, n, s, h, l, ∅, p, t, c, k, b, d, j, g, f |
| **介音, 任意** | y, w, ∅                                   |
| **母音**        | i, e, a, o, u, ǝ                         |
| **韻尾, 任意**   | m, n, ŋ, u, i, p, t, k, ∅                |
### 軟口蓋鼻音

The velar nasal /ŋ/ (**軟口蓋鼻音**) is permitted in **音節末** but does not occur in **音節頭**.

### 分布の制限

Certain **分布空隙** exist in 単亜語音節体系:

- The consonants **ㄉ, ㄊ, ㄑ, ㄐ** (**歯茎・硬口蓋塞音**) may not combine with a **y介音**.    
- The consonant **ㄈ** may not take any on-glide.
- A **y介音** may not be followed by the vowel ㄧ.    
- A **w介音** may only precede the vowels **ㄚ, ㄝ**.    
- In syllables with a null onset (**無声母**), **ㄨㄧ+** sequences are permitted (**許容**).    
- A **w介音** may not co-occur with the initials ㄋ, ㄌ, ㄅ, ㄆ, ㄇ (**鼻音・流音・両唇音**).

These restrictions reflect articulatory constraints rather than missing phonemes.

### 形態音韻論

Because many **<ruby>話者<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄧ·ㄑㄚ</rt></ruby>** find **語末子音** difficult, <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> prohibits **音節境界の連音禁止**.

- **<ruby>日本語<rt>ㄋㄧㄊㄅㄛㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby><ruby>話者<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄧ·ㄑㄚ</rt></ruby>** may struggle to distinguish **語末鼻音**.    
- **<ruby>漢語<rt>ㄏㄚㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby><ruby>話者<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄧ·ㄑㄚ</rt></ruby>** may struggle to produce **語末塞音**.    

Therefore, no **<ruby>連声<rt>ㄌ⼶ㄋㄙㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>** or **音節間同化** is permitted. Each syllable remains **<ruby>音節<rt>ㄨㄇㄐㄝㄊ</rt></ruby><ruby>独立<rt>ㄉㄛㄎㄌㄧㄆ</rt></ruby>**.

Care must be taken to avoid forms like _ningan_ ㄋㄧㄋㄍㄚㄋ, where a velar nasal /ŋ/ (**軟口蓋鼻音**) alters meaning. The rule is absolute: **音節<ruby>相互<rt>ㄙㄚㄫㄏㄛ</rt></ruby>影響禁止**.

## 声調・韻律

Because **<ruby>声調<rt>ㄙㄧㄫㄐㄨㄛ</rt></ruby>** is not present in all **東亜諸語** and learning new tones is **習得困難**, 単亜語 is **非音位的** for tone. Speakers should not attempt to convey **<ruby>意味<rt>ㄜ·ㄇㄨㄧ</rt></ruby>・<ruby>感情<rt>ㄍㄚㄇㄑㄧㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>情報<rt>ㄑㄧㄫㄅㄚㄨ</rt></ruby>** through intonation alone. Instead, **文末詞** (**終助詞**) are mandatory to indicate any **<ruby>法<rt>ㄈㄚㄆ</rt></ruby>** other than the **単純<ruby>陳述<rt>ㄐㄧㄋㄙㄨㄊ</rt></ruby>文**.

<ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> employs pitch-accent as a prosodic aid for **語境界の区別**. Pitch-accent refers to the relative height of syllables across a phrase, rather than lexical tone.  Pitch is realized at the **<ruby>句<rt>ㄍㄨ</rt></ruby>・文単位**, and no minimal pairs of words exist based on pitch alone. For example:

- <ruby>大<rt>ㄉㄚㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>学生<rt>ㄏㄚㄎㄙㄚㄫ</rt></ruby> “big student” → pitch **H HL** （高→下降）
    
- <ruby>大学生<rt>ㄉㄚㄧㄏㄚㄎㄙㄚㄫ</rt></ruby> “college student” → pitch **HLL** （高→下降→低）
    

### 高低原則

- The **述部** of an utterance is its **<ruby>最高点<rt>ㄐ⼔ㄍㄚㄨㄉㄝㄇ</rt></ruby>**.
- The **句頭**  is its <ruby>最高点<rt>ㄐ⼔ㄍㄚㄨㄉㄝㄇ</rt></ruby>.    
- The **音節主要母音** is its <ruby>最高点<rt>ㄐ⼔ㄍㄚㄨㄉㄝㄇ</rt></ruby>.    
- The **<ruby>複合語<rt>ㄅㄨㄎㄍㄛㄆ·⼄</rt></ruby>初字** is its <ruby>最高点<rt>ㄐ⼔ㄍㄚㄨㄉㄝㄇ</rt></ruby>.    

This system benefits 東亜話者有利, but may be confusing for 外来話者困難. A sentence with **目的語・助詞** ends with a 下降調, similar to **陳述文終止下降** in many languages. However, because many sentences are **<ruby>動詞<rt>ㄉㄛㄫㄙㄚ</rt></ruby>終止文**, the intonation may resemble a **付加疑問文** — especially when **単音節<ruby>動詞<rt>ㄉㄛㄫㄙㄚ</rt></ruby>**.

For this reason, even **非対立疑問文** must end with the particle **乎** （乎助詞） to ensure clarity.
