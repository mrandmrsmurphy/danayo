# <ruby>文字<rt>ㄇㄨㄋㄐㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>法<rt>ㄈㄚㄆ</rt></ruby>
<ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> is written with <ruby>新字体<rt>ㄙㄧㄋㄐㄧ·ㄊㄝㄧ</rt></ruby> <ruby>漢字<rt>ㄏㄚㄋㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>. ## <ruby>文字<rt>ㄇㄨㄋㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>規範

- **【<ruby>[[一字一音]]<rt>ㄧㄊㄐㄧㄧㄊ·ㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>】** One Character, One Sound
  Every Chinese character （<ruby>漢字<rt>ㄏㄚㄋㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>） has one, and only one, pronunciation （音）.

- **【<ruby>[[毎字明意]]<rt>ㄇㄛㄧㄐㄧ·ㄇ⼶ㄫㄜ</rt></ruby>】** Per Character, Bright Meaning
  Every character has a single meaning （義）. Metaphorical derivations （<ruby>比喩<rt>ㄅㄧㄜ⼜ㄇ</rt></ruby>義<ruby>派生<rt>ㄆㄚㄧㄙㄚㄫ</rt></ruby>） are permitted. Distinction between new meaning and extended metaphor allows natural word formation （<ruby>造語<rt>ㄑㄚㄨ⼄</rt></ruby>）. This accords with the historical tradition （<ruby>伝統<rt>ㄐ⼔ㄋㄊㄛㄫ</rt></ruby>） of semantic derivation （義<ruby>派生<rt>ㄆㄚㄧㄙㄚㄫ</rt></ruby>） in the region.

- **【<ruby>[[単語熟語]]<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·⼄ㄙㄨㄎ·⼄</rt></ruby>】** Simple Character, Familiar Speech
  Some characters are <ruby>単語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby> when standing alone. Others require companions, forming <ruby>熟語<rt>ㄙㄨㄎ·⼄</rt></ruby>. In vocabulary terms, there are <ruby>一<rt>ㄧㄊ</rt></ruby><ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby> words and <ruby>双<rt>ㄙ⺢ㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby> words.

- **【<ruby>四字成語<rt>ㄙㄧㄜㄐㄧㄙㄧㄫ⼄</rt></ruby>】** Four-Character Phrases
  Certain fixed expressions （<ruby>慣用句<rt>ㄍ⺢ㄋ·⼄ㄫㄍㄨ</rt></ruby>） are set in <ruby>四<rt>ㄙㄧㄜ</rt></ruby><ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>. They form a shared idiom across East Asia （<ruby>東亜<rt>ㄉㄛㄫㄚ</rt></ruby><ruby>慣用<rt>ㄍ⺢ㄋ·⼄ㄫ</rt></ruby>）. Each country also developed innumerable unique phrases （<ruby>国別<rt>ㄍㄛㄎㄅㄝㄊ</rt></ruby><ruby>成語<rt>ㄙㄧㄫ⼄</rt></ruby>）, which are not a shared heritage.

These rubrics direct the writing system toward <ruby>明<rt>ㄇ⼶ㄫ</rt></ruby>晰 by multiplying characters （<ruby>多<rt>ㄉㄜ</rt></ruby><ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>化<rt>ㄏ⺢</rt></ruby>）.  At the same time, <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> follows a contrary impulse: to reduce the number of characters （減<ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>化<rt>ㄏ⺢</rt></ruby>） as far as possible.  The higher aim （<ruby>高<rt>ㄍㄚㄨ</rt></ruby>旨） of the language is to maintain the minimum set of characters that still ensures clarity （簡<ruby>而<rt>ㄋㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>明<rt>ㄇ⼶ㄫ</rt></ruby>）.  In practice, this requires a core of about 2,000 characters （<ruby>常用<rt>ㄙ⼘ㄫ⼄ㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>）.  Each core character may gather its <ruby>異体<rt>ㄧ·ㄊㄝㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby> and 古<ruby>形<rt>ㄏㄝㄫ</rt></ruby> under one head.  This follows the same practice as Japanese substitute characters （<ruby>代用字<rt>ㄉㄚㄧ⼄ㄫㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>） and Chinese simplified characters （簡<ruby>化<rt>ㄏ⺢</rt></ruby><ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>）.

### <ruby>常用<rt>ㄙ⼘ㄫ⼄ㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>

This category, though unfortunate （<ruby>不幸<rt>ㄅㄛㄊㄏㄚㄫ</rt></ruby>）, is necessary for the simplicity of the language （<ruby>簡明<rt>ㄍㄚㄋㄇ⼶ㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>之<rt>ㄊㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>為<rt>⼔ㄋ</rt></ruby>）.  Every forbidden character in another country （<ruby>他<rt>ㄊㄜ</rt></ruby>国用<ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>） has a direct substitute （<ruby>代用字<rt>ㄉㄚㄧ⼄ㄫㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>） within <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby>.  A complete list （<ruby>全<rt>ㄐ⼔ㄋ</rt></ruby>表） is given in [[文法 - 98違法字]].

### <ruby>異体<rt>ㄧ·ㄊㄝㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>
**【<ruby>[[異体不容]]<rt>ㄧ·ㄊㄝㄧㄅㄛㄊ⼄ㄫ</rt></ruby>】** - Every character has only one acceptable form. 

Nearly every Chinese character （<ruby>漢字<rt>ㄏㄚㄋㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>） has been written in multiple ways （<ruby>多<rt>ㄉㄜ</rt></ruby><ruby>形<rt>ㄏㄝㄫ</rt></ruby>） across nations （<ruby>諸<rt>ㄐㄚ</rt></ruby>国） and across time （歴代）.  Such forms are called variant characters （<ruby>異体<rt>ㄧ·ㄊㄝㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>）.  Ideally, an input system （<ruby>入<rt>ㄋㄧㄆ</rt></ruby><ruby>力<rt>ㄌㄧㄎ</rt></ruby><ruby>法<rt>ㄈㄚㄆ</rt></ruby>） will recognize a variant when typed, and replace it with the standardized glyph （<ruby>正<rt>ㄐㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>体<ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>）.

This system sometimes produces striking results （奇観）.  Obscure characters that are homophones or near-homophones （同音<ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>） of common characters （<ruby>常用<rt>ㄙ⼘ㄫ⼄ㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>） may be reclassified as variants （転属）.  This removes the statistical “long tail” （<ruby>冗<rt>ㄋ⼄ㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>尾<rt>ㄇㄨㄧ</rt></ruby>） of ultra-rare characters （稀<ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>）.

For example, the phrase <ruby>魑魅<rt>ㄑㄧ·ㄇㄧㄜ</rt></ruby>魍魎 is visually impressive （観<ruby>之<rt>ㄊㄧ</rt></ruby>壮）.  Yet the last two characters, 魍 and 魎, have almost no independent usage （孤用）.  By fiat （<ruby>擬<rt>ㄧ</rt></ruby>制）, <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> declares 魍 a variant of 罔, and 魎 a variant of <ruby>両<rt>ㄌ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>.  These two are in fact components of the originals （構件）.  Thus the idiom therefore appears as [[魑魅罔両]], equally expressive but more economical （簡<ruby>而<rt>ㄋㄧ</rt></ruby>存義）.

### <ruby>代用字<rt>ㄉㄚㄧ⼄ㄫㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>
Substitute characters （<ruby>代用字<rt>ㄉㄚㄧ⼄ㄫㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>） are characters used in place of the "right" character, because the right character is too rare （<ruby>希少<rt>ㄏㄧㄜㄙㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>） to include in the limited inventory of <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby>.  When a word's proper spelling contains a character that is too unruly for the inventory, a more common character stands in for it.  For example, "sea eagle" is 海鷲 elsewhere, but its second character is too rare, so <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> writes <ruby>海就<rt>ㄏㄚㄧ·ㄐㄨㄛ</rt></ruby>; here <ruby>就<rt>ㄑ⺢ㄧ</rt></ruby> is the <ruby>代用字<rt>ㄉㄚㄧ⼄ㄫㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>.  The replaced character is recorded as an alias on the substitute's character page, and every forbidden character （<ruby>違法<rt>ㄨㄧㄈㄚㄆ</rt></ruby><ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>） has such a substitute.  The term comes from Japanese （<ruby>日本語<rt>ㄋㄧㄊㄅㄛㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby>）, where <ruby>代用字<rt>ㄉㄚㄧ⼄ㄫㄐㄧ</rt></ruby> names a character used in place of the standard one.

### <ruby>無<rt>ㄇㄜ</rt></ruby>類<ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>
Unclassified characters （<ruby>無<rt>ㄇㄜ</rt></ruby>類<ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>） lie outside all lists （<ruby>諸<rt>ㄐㄚ</rt></ruby>表外）.  They are too rare （<ruby>希少<rt>ㄏㄧㄜㄙㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>） to be assigned a category （<ruby>不可<rt>ㄅㄛㄊㄎㄜ</rt></ruby><ruby>分<rt>ㄅㄨㄋ</rt></ruby>類）.  They appear only in dictionaries （<ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>書） or historical records （古録）.

For practical purposes （<ruby>実用<rt>ㄙㄧㄊ⼄ㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>上<rt>ㄙ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>）, <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> does not admit them into the active inventory （<ruby>現<rt>ㄏ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby>用<ruby>字庫<rt>ㄐㄧ·ㄎㄛ</rt></ruby>）.  They are studied for reference （<ruby>参照<rt>ㄙㄚㄇㄐㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>） but not employed in writing （<ruby>不用<rt>ㄅㄛㄊ⼄ㄫ</rt></ruby>）.

## 音源<ruby>推<rt>ㄑㄨㄧ</rt></ruby>定
The pronunciation system of <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> is based on <ruby>中古<rt>ㄐㄨㄫㄍㄛ</rt></ruby><ruby>漢語<rt>ㄏㄚㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby>.  The Zhengzhang Shangfang rime table （鄭張<ruby>尚<rt>ㄙ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>芳韻表） serves as the primary reference （<ruby>本<rt>ㄅㄛㄋ</rt></ruby>表）.  
Consensus reconstructions （<ruby>通<rt>ㄊㄛㄫ</rt></ruby>説） are followed in general.

A rule-of-thumb table for initials （<ruby>声母<rt>ㄙㄧㄫㄇㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>） and finals （<ruby>韻母<rt>ㄨㄋㄇㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>） is given in Appendix B （<ruby>附<rt>ㄅㄨ</rt></ruby>録<ruby>乙<rt>ㄜㄊ</rt></ruby>）.  
From these rules, each character receives a systematic pronunciation （<ruby>規則<rt>ㄍㄨㄧㄐㄨㄎ</rt></ruby>的音）.  When the resulting syllable would be overfull （<ruby>過<rt>ㄍ⺢</rt></ruby>繁）, adjustments are made （<ruby>調整<rt>ㄐㄨㄛㄐㄝㄫ</rt></ruby>）.  For example, the character 殆 would yield ㄉㄚㄧ, but <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> follows the Cantonese form _doi_ to avoid excess homophony.

Korean influence also guided certain choices （韓音<ruby>所<rt>ㄙㄜ</rt></ruby>助）.  Many historical ㄙㄧ syllables （詞） became ㄙㄚ in <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby>, reflecting Middle Korean shifts （<ruby>中古<rt>ㄐㄨㄫㄍㄛ</rt></ruby><ruby>韓国語<rt>ㄏㄚㄋㄍㄛㄎ·⼄</rt></ruby><ruby>変化<rt>ㄅ⼶ㄋㄏ⺢</rt></ruby>）.

Occasionally, rare characters were selected solely for their sound value （<ruby>以<rt>ㄧ</rt></ruby>音取<ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>）.  For example, <ruby>耽<rt>ㄉㄛㄇ</rt></ruby> is used to supply ㄉㄛㄇ.  In some cases, inspiration came from unusual Vietnamese readings （<ruby>越<rt>⼔ㄊ</rt></ruby>音異読）.  Thus 𦚖 provides the syllable ㄇㄛㄆ, otherwise unattested.

Through such measures, <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> establishes a balanced and functional sound system （<ruby>均衡<rt>ㄍ⼜ㄋㄏㄚㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>而<rt>ㄋㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>可<rt>ㄎㄜ</rt></ruby>用<ruby>之<rt>ㄊㄧ</rt></ruby>音系）.

## <ruby>新字体<rt>ㄙㄧㄋㄐㄧ·ㄊㄝㄧ</rt></ruby>
Over the <ruby>近<rt>ㄍㄧㄋ</rt></ruby><ruby>百年<rt>ㄅㄚㄎㄋㄝㄋ</rt></ruby>, many reforms of Chinese characters （<ruby>文字<rt>ㄇㄨㄋㄐㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>改革<rt>ㄍㄚㄧㄎㄧㄎ</rt></ruby>） have been proposed and implemented.  <ruby>中国<rt>ㄐㄨㄫㄍㄛㄎ</rt></ruby><ruby>大陸<rt>ㄉㄚㄧㄌㄨㄎ</rt></ruby> adopted the most radical 簡<ruby>化<rt>ㄏ⺢</rt></ruby>, and a further scheme was rescinded in 1986.  Because many such simplifications depend on Mandarin pronunciation （官<ruby>話<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄧ</rt></ruby>音）, they are not equally usable across nations （<ruby>域<rt>ㄨㄧㄎ</rt></ruby>内<ruby>通<rt>ㄊㄛㄫ</rt></ruby>用<ruby>性<rt>ㄙㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>に<ruby>欠<rt>ㄎ⼘ㄇ</rt></ruby>く）.  Some countries retained traditional forms unchanged （<ruby>旧<rt>ㄍ⼜ㄛ</rt></ruby>体<ruby>不<rt>ㄅㄛㄊ</rt></ruby><ruby>改<rt>ㄍㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>）.  The Japanese system of <ruby>新字体<rt>ㄙㄧㄋㄐㄧ·ㄊㄝㄧ</rt></ruby> offers a moderate middle path （<ruby>折衷<rt>ㄐㄝㄊㄊㄨㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>之<rt>ㄊㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>道<rt>ㄉㄚㄨ</rt></ruby>） suitable for <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby>.

### 例外<ruby>規定<rt>ㄍㄨㄧㄐㄝㄫ</rt></ruby>
- 龍 / 竜  
    <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> writes 龍 in all contexts.  
    The simplified 竜 is avoided for cross-regional clarity （<ruby>容易<rt>⼄ㄫ⼶ㄎ</rt></ruby>混同<ruby>亀<rt>ㄍㄨㄛ</rt></ruby>）.    
- 圓 / 円  
    The semantic term “circle” uses 圓.  
    The Shinjitai 円 is closely associated with currency （<ruby>通貨<rt>ㄊㄛㄫㄏ⺢</rt></ruby>） and is not used for the geometric sense.    
- 弗<ruby>部<rt>ㄅㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>ti<ruby>整<rt>ㄐㄝㄫ</rt></ruby>序  
    <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> regularizes the 弗 component （弗<ruby>部<rt>ㄅㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>）: write 佛 not 仏, and 拂 not 払.    
- [[桌]] / [[卓]]      
	- These are distinguished to preserve 【<ruby>[[毎字明意]]<rt>ㄇㄛㄧㄐㄧ·ㄇ⼶ㄫㄜ</rt></ruby>】（<ruby>一<rt>ㄧㄊ</rt></ruby><ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>ㄧㄊ</rt></ruby>義）.    
- 干 / 乾 
	- 干 “desiccate; interfere” and 乾 “heavenly; Qian-trigram” remain distinct.
- 果 / 菓  
    These are conflated as in Chinese usage; 菓 is treated as a variant of 果.
- 貳 / 弍  
    Standard form is 貳.  
    弍 is avoided due to proximity to 弌／弎.
- 缺 / 欠  
    These differ in both sound and meaning; they remain distinct.
- 鼠 / 鼡
	This simplification also look bizarre to other Asians.  It is therefore rejected.

### <ruby>拡張<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄎㄑㄚㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>新字体<rt>ㄙㄧㄋㄐㄧ·ㄊㄝㄧ</rt></ruby>
Shinjitai in practice is not fully consistent （<ruby>一<rt>ㄧㄊ</rt></ruby>貫を<ruby>欠<rt>ㄎ⼘ㄇ</rt></ruby>く）.  <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> therefore applies extended Shinjitai where possible, seeking component-wise consistency （**<ruby>部<rt>ㄅㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>件<ruby>一<rt>ㄧㄊ</rt></ruby>律**） without loss of meaning.

Principles （簡<ruby>化<rt>ㄏ⺢</rt></ruby><ruby>原則<rt>⼔ㄋㄐㄨㄎ</rt></ruby>）:
1. Component parity （**<ruby>部<rt>ㄅㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>件同<ruby>処<rt>ㄑㄛ</rt></ruby>**）: when a component is simplified in one common character, comparable occurrences are simplified in parallel.    
2. Semantic stability （**義位<ruby>不<rt>ㄅㄛㄊ</rt></ruby>乱**）: do not create homographs that violate 【<ruby>[[毎字明意]]<rt>ㄇㄛㄧㄐㄧ·ㄇ⼶ㄫㄜ</rt></ruby>】 or compete with a common character （<ruby>常用<rt>ㄙ⼘ㄫ⼄ㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>字<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>）.    
3. Cross-regional legibility （**<ruby>域<rt>ㄨㄧㄎ</rt></ruby>内<ruby>可読<rt>ㄎㄜㄉㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>**）: avoid forms that impede understanding outside Japan.    

Illustrative contrasts: if 區 → 区 and 圖 → 図 are adopted, <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> extends comparable component reductions where they do not endanger distinctness.  Conversely, extreme or purely graphic leaps that would break meaning or produce confusion are rejected.  In short, when simplification can be made consistent, legible, and semantically safe, <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> adopts it; when any of these three are threatened, the traditional form is retained.

## 音<ruby>訓<rt>ㄏㄨㄋ</rt></ruby>標
**【<ruby>[[声形和決]]<rt>ㄙㄧㄫㄏㄝㄫㄏ⺢·ㄎ⼔ㄊ</rt></ruby>】**

In <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby>, each character （<ruby>漢字<rt>ㄏㄚㄋㄐㄧ</rt></ruby>） carries a ruby gloss （<ruby>振<rt>ㄐㄧㄋ</rt></ruby>仮<ruby>名<rt>ㄇㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>） above it.  The ruby is written in the <ruby>注音符号<rt>ㄐㄨㄨㄇㄅㄨㄏㄚㄨ</rt></ruby>.  This shows the intended pronunciation （<ruby>発音<rt>ㄈㄚㄊ·ㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>） directly.

### Cardinal <ruby>母音<rt>ㄇㄛㄨㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>
Ruby employs cardinal vowels （<ruby>基準<rt>ㄍㄧㄐㄨㄋ</rt></ruby><ruby>母音<rt>ㄇㄛㄨㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>）.  Thus the glyph ·ㄝ represents /e/, unlike in Chinese usage.

### Y Off-Glide （/j/<ruby>後続<rt>ㄏㄨㄛㄙ⼄ㄎ</rt></ruby><ruby>滑音<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄊ·ㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>）
A horizontal/vertical stroke （<ruby>縦<rt>ㄐㄛㄇ</rt></ruby>画） marks the _y_ off-glide.  

### W Off-Glide （/w/<ruby>後続<rt>ㄏㄨㄛㄙ⼄ㄎ</rt></ruby><ruby>滑音<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄊ·ㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>）
<ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> uses the ㄨ as a final glyph （<ruby>終声<rt>ㄐㄨㄫㄙㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>）.


## Extended Bopomofo
For the native letter inventory, see [[grammar/Bopomofo|Bopomofo]]. <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> ruby is sufficient for native usage.  However, <ruby>注音符号<rt>ㄐㄨㄨㄇㄅㄨㄏㄚㄨ</rt></ruby> is capable of rendering Chinese/Vietnamese syllables （<ruby>漢語<rt>ㄏㄚㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby><ruby>拼音<rt>ㄅㄚㄫㄨㄇ</rt></ruby><ruby>音節<rt>ㄨㄇㄐㄝㄊ</rt></ruby>）
- ㄪ is the onset 'v'
- ㄓ is the onset 'zh', IPA /ʈʂ/
- ㄔ is the onset 'ch', IPA /ʈʂʰ/
- ㄕ is the onset 'sh', IPA /ʂ/
- ㄖ is the onset 'r', IPA /ɻ/
- ㄩ is the vowel 'ü', IPA /y/
- ㄭ marks syllabic consonants, IPA /ɻ̩/
- ㄦ is the Chinese rhotic vowel, IPA /ɚ/
- ㄫ can be used for initial 'ng'
- ㆾ can be used for the Cantonese vowel /œː/

ㄗ is held to be the same as ㄐ. ㄘ is held to be the same as ㄑ. ㄒ is held to be the same as ㄙ.  None of these (ㄗ, ㄘ,ㄒ) may be used, even in extended mode.  Nor may the extended diphthongs: ㄞ, ㄟ, ㄠ, ㄡ, ㄢ, ㄣ, ㄤ.  Tones must be marked (if at all) with Arabic numerals.

For other languages' sound, nasal vowels (ㆩ,ㆥ,ㆪ,ㆧ,ㆫ) and glottal stops (ㆷ) may be used.
