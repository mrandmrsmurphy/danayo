<ruby>代用字<rt>ㄉㄚㄧ⼄ㄫㄐㄧ</rt></ruby> are characters used in place of the "right" character, because the right character is <ruby>希少<rt>ㄏㄧㄜㄙㄛㄨ</rt></ruby> to include in the limited inventory of <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby>. ## 音源<ruby>推<rt>ㄑㄨㄧ</rt></ruby>定
The pronunciation system of <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ⼄</rt></ruby> is based on <ruby>中古<rt>ㄐㄨㄫㄍㄛ</rt></ruby><ruby>漢語<rt>ㄏㄚㄋ·⼄</rt></ruby>.  鄭張<ruby>尚<rt>ㄙ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>芳韻表 serves as <ruby>本<rt>ㄅㄛㄋ</rt></ruby>表.
