Christian theological, ecclesiastical, and scriptural vocabulary — not one of Rosenfelder's Lexipedia categories, but necessary given Christianity's historical and continuing presence across the CJKV cultural sphere. This page is a working draft, not yet complete: most theology/clergy/sacrament terms below are already real Dan'a'yo words, but the Scripture and Tribal-name sections mostly record names still awaiting proper word pages — see [[bible/聖経 (book)]] for the full source material and working notes (not itself reorganized as part of this pass). Red (unlinked) entries below name important concepts with no Dan'a'yo word yet — reserved placeholders, not coined here.

## Scripture (聖経)

Dan'a'yo names for the books of [[bible/聖経 (book)]] are still being coined. Three already have full word pages; a handful more have a working `bible/` content page but no vocabulary-standard word page yet; the rest are recorded here — in the source's own order — purely so they have a reserved place once coined. <ruby>[経典](../words/経典.md)<rt>ㄍㄝㄫㄉㄝㄋ</rt></ruby> is the general word for "scripture, classic text," used generically rather than for 聖経 specifically.

Broader scriptural vocabulary with no word yet: [[聖書]] (Bible, as a common noun distinct from 聖経's proper title), [[新約]] / [[旧約]] (New/Old Testament), [[律法]] (law, Torah), [[十戒]] (Ten Commandments), [[福音]] (gospel), [[啓示]] / [[黙示]] (revelation), [[預言]] / [[預言者]] (prophecy, prophet).
