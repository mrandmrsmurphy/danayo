# Physics

Physics vocabulary covers temperature, weight, and the material substance of things, along with the vocabulary of subatomic particles borrowed directly from the periodic table's own domain. It is a thin domain in this vault: the vocabulary that exists is real and precise, but several of the most fundamental concepts in physics — energy, mass, and gravity — have no dedicated Dan'a'yo word yet at all, a genuine gap rather than an oversight in coverage.

## Core Vocabulary (A1–A2)

- <ruby>[冷](../words/冷.md)<rt>ㄌㄚㄫ</rt></ruby>: cool, cold.
- <ruby>[暖](../words/暖.md)<rt>ㄋㄚㄋ</rt></ruby>: warm.
- <ruby>[熱](../words/熱.md)<rt>ㄋ⼶ㄊ</rt></ruby>: to heat up, be hot — see [[Light]] for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[重](../words/重.md)<rt>ㄑㄛㄫ</rt></ruby>: heavy — see [[Dimensions]] for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[軽](../words/軽.md)<rt>ㄎㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>: light, not heavy — see [[Dimensions]] for its own citation there; the weight sense, not illumination (which is [[光]], cited on [[Light]] instead).
- <ruby>[材料](../words/材料.md)<rt>ㄑㄚㄧㄌ⼘ㄨ</rt></ruby>: material, stuff.
- <ruby>[物質](../words/物質.md)<rt>ㄇㄨㄊㄐㄧㄊ</rt></ruby>: material, substance — the physical-matter sense specifically.
- <ruby>[衡量](../words/衡量.md)<rt>ㄏㄚㄫㄌ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>: to weigh, measure, evaluate — see [[Existence]] for its own citation there.

**Not yet coined**: "energy," "gravity," and "mass" (the measurable physical quantity, distinct from [[物質]]'s general "substance") all have no dedicated Dan'a'yo word yet — a real, notable gap in this vault's physics vocabulary, not an oversight in searching.

## Intermediate (B1–B2)

### Temperature

- <ruby>[寒冷](../words/寒冷.md)<rt>ㄏㄚㄋㄌㄚㄫ</rt></ruby>: cold, freezing — more intense than plain [[冷]].
- <ruby>[清涼](../words/清涼.md)<rt>ㄑㄧㄫㄌ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>: cool, refreshing — a pleasant coolness, distinct from [[冷]]'s neutral cold.
- <ruby>[灼熱](../words/灼熱.md)<rt>ㄐㄚㄎㄋ⼶ㄊ</rt></ruby>: burning hot, scorching — more intense than plain [[熱]].

### Matter & Substance

- <ruby>[物理](../words/物理.md)<rt>ㄇㄨㄊㄌㄧ</rt></ruby>: physics, the science — also, in an older and more literary register, "the nature/principle of things" generally, the closest sense to the adjective "physical."

## Semantic Range Notes

**"Light" (weight) and "light" (illumination) are entirely different words, not one word covering both senses**: [[軽]] means light in weight, the opposite of [[重]]; [[光]] means light as illumination, cited on [[Light]] instead. English collapses these into one word; Dan'a'yo, following its source languages, does not.

**"Matter" in the physical sense is not the same word as "matter" in the affair/subject sense**: [[物質]] means matter as physical substance, the sense this page needs. [[事情]] ("affair, situation") and [[事物]] ("thing, subject matter") both also gloss "matter" in English translation, but neither means physical substance — they were not cited here because the sense doesn't match, a case worth flagging explicitly since the English word "matter" itself is the ambiguous one, not a translation error.

**The gap in fundamental-physics vocabulary is real, not a search failure**: "energy," "gravity," and "mass" are checked and confirmed absent, not merely hard to find. This vault's physics vocabulary developed from the practical, everyday direction (temperature, weight, material) and from the periodic table's own particle vocabulary, rather than from classical mechanics — a genuine, specific hole rather than a general weakness.
