# Physics

Physics vocabulary covers temperature, weight, and the material substance of things, along with the vocabulary of subatomic particles borrowed directly from the periodic table's own domain. It is a bounded domain, and many of its words are housed on other pages, such as [[Measurement]], [[Movement]], [[Light]] and [[Water]], which it cites in short. Energy (<ruby>[[能量]]<rt>ㄋㄜㄫㄌ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>), gravity (<ruby>[[重力]]<rt>ㄑㄛㄫㄌㄧㄎ</rt></ruby>), mass (<ruby>[[質量]]<rt>ㄐㄧㄊㄌ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>), force (<ruby>[[力]]<rt>ㄌㄧㄎ</rt></ruby>), pressure (<ruby>[[圧力]]<rt>ㄚㄊㄌㄧㄎ</rt></ruby>) and the states and changes of matter are all covered.

## Core Vocabulary (A1–A2)

- <ruby>[冷](../words/冷.md)<rt>ㄌㄚㄫ</rt></ruby>: cool, cold.
- <ruby>[暖](../words/暖.md)<rt>ㄋㄚㄋ</rt></ruby>: warm.
- <ruby>[熱](../words/熱.md)<rt>ㄋ⼶ㄊ</rt></ruby>: to heat up, be hot — see [[Light]] for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[重](../words/重.md)<rt>ㄑㄛㄫ</rt></ruby>: heavy — see [[Dimensions]] for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[軽](../words/軽.md)<rt>ㄎㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>: light, not heavy — see [[Dimensions]] for its own citation there; the weight sense, not illumination (which is [[光]], cited on [[Light]] instead).
- <ruby>[材料](../words/材料.md)<rt>ㄑㄚㄧㄌ⼘ㄨ</rt></ruby>: material, stuff.
- <ruby>[物質](../words/物質.md)<rt>ㄇㄨㄊㄐㄧㄊ</rt></ruby>: material, substance — the physical-matter sense specifically.
- <ruby>[衡量](../words/衡量.md)<rt>ㄏㄚㄫㄌ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>: to weigh, measure, evaluate — see [[Existence]] for its own citation there.

**How Dan'a'yo says these**: "gravity" is <ruby>[[重力]]<rt>ㄑㄛㄫㄌㄧㄎ</rt></ruby>, the pull of the earth, with <ruby>[[引力]]<rt>ㄧㄋㄌㄧㄎ</rt></ruby> for attraction between any two bodies; "mass" is <ruby>[[質量]]<rt>ㄐㄧㄊㄌ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>, the measurable quantity, distinct from <ruby>[[物質]]<rt>ㄇㄨㄊㄐㄧㄊ</rt></ruby>'s general "substance"; and "energy" is <ruby>[[能量]]<rt>ㄋㄜㄫㄌ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>.

## Intermediate (B1–B2)

### Temperature

- <ruby>[寒冷](../words/寒冷.md)<rt>ㄏㄚㄋㄌㄚㄫ</rt></ruby>: cold, freezing — more intense than plain [[冷]].
- <ruby>[清涼](../words/清涼.md)<rt>ㄑㄧㄫㄌ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>: cool, refreshing — a pleasant coolness, distinct from [[冷]]'s neutral cold.
- <ruby>[灼熱](../words/灼熱.md)<rt>ㄐㄚㄎㄋ⼶ㄊ</rt></ruby>: burning hot, scorching — more intense than plain [[熱]].
- <ruby>[暑](../words/暑.md)<rt>ㄙ⼄</rt></ruby>: hot (of weather).

### Matter & Substance

- <ruby>[物理](../words/物理.md)<rt>ㄇㄨㄊㄌㄧ</rt></ruby>: physics, the science — also, in an older and more literary register, "the nature/principle of things" generally, the closest sense to the adjective "physical."
- <ruby>[位相](../words/位相.md)<rt>⼔ㄧㄙㄚㄫ</rt></ruby>: phase — a wave or periodic function's position within its cycle (位相差, "phase difference").
- <ruby>[鹸](../words/鹸.md)<rt>ㄑㄝㄇ</rt></ruby>: a base, alkali — the chemistry-concept word, also the root of [[鹸素]] (sodium) on [[Periodic Table]].
- <ruby>[中和](../words/中和.md)<rt>ㄐㄨㄫㄏ⺢</rt></ruby>: to neutralize — an acid and a [[鹸]] meeting in a neutral solution; extended to balancing opposed forces, and in phonetics to a contrast collapsing.
- <ruby>[粒子](../words/粒子.md)<rt>ㄌㄨㄆㄐㄜ</rt></ruby>: particle, grain.

## Semantic Range Notes

**"Light" (weight) and "light" (illumination) are entirely different words, not one word covering both senses**: [[軽]] means light in weight, the opposite of [[重]]; [[光]] means light as illumination, cited on [[Light]] instead. English collapses these into one word; Dan'a'yo, following its source languages, does not.

**"Matter" in the physical sense is not the same word as "matter" in the affair/subject sense**: [[物質]] means matter as physical substance, the sense this page needs. [[事情]] ("affair, situation") and [[事物]] ("thing, subject matter") both also gloss "matter" in English translation, but neither means physical substance — they were not cited here because the sense doesn't match, a case worth flagging explicitly since the English word "matter" itself is the ambiguous one, not a translation error.

**The fundamental physics words were never absent, only unlisted here**: <ruby>[[能量]]<rt>ㄋㄜㄫㄌ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby> (energy), <ruby>[[重力]]<rt>ㄑㄛㄫㄌㄧㄎ</rt></ruby> (gravity) and <ruby>[[質量]]<rt>ㄐㄧㄊㄌ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby> (mass) were already in the vault, housed on [Measurement](../lexipedia/Measurement.md) and [Astronomy](../lexipedia/Astronomy.md), and this page now cites them with <ruby>[[力]]<rt>ㄌㄧㄎ</rt></ruby> (force), <ruby>[[引力]]<rt>ㄧㄋㄌㄧㄎ</rt></ruby> (attraction), <ruby>[[圧力]]<rt>ㄚㄊㄌㄧㄎ</rt></ruby> (pressure) and the rest of the mechanics and thermal vocabulary.

**<ruby>[[重力]]<rt>ㄑㄛㄫㄌㄧㄎ</rt></ruby>, <ruby>[[引力]]<rt>ㄧㄋㄌㄧㄎ</rt></ruby> and <ruby>[[質量]]<rt>ㄐㄧㄊㄌ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby> are three different ideas**: <ruby>[[重力]]<rt>ㄑㄛㄫㄌㄧㄎ</rt></ruby> is gravity, the earth's pull that gives a body weight; <ruby>[[引力]]<rt>ㄧㄋㄌㄧㄎ</rt></ruby> is attraction between any two bodies, so gravitation in general; and <ruby>[[質量]]<rt>ㄐㄧㄊㄌ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby> is mass, the quantity of matter, which does not change with place, where weight does.

**<ruby>[[蒸発]]<rt>ㄐㄧㄫㄈㄚㄊ</rt></ruby> and <ruby>[[沸騰]]<rt>ㄈㄨㄊㄉㄜㄫ</rt></ruby> are two ways a liquid becomes a gas**: evaporation happens quietly at the surface at any temperature, and boiling happens through the whole liquid at its boiling point.
