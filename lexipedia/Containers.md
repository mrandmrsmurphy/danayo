# Containers

Containers vocabulary covers vessels and holders of every kind — boxes, bags, bottles, baskets — along with the actions of collecting, including, and locking things away, and the states of being full or empty. Several Rosenfelder terms (open, pack, pocket, cage, cabinet, cupboard) have no Dan'a'yo word yet, flagged inline below rather than skipped silently.

## Semantic Range Notes

**収集 vs. 採取 vs. 採集 vs. 聚集 vs. 蒐集 vs. 㪘 — six words for "collect," differing by object and register**: all six converge on "to collect, gather," but the objects differ — 収集 leans toward organized collecting (stamps, garbage, data), 採取/採集 toward harvesting or sampling from nature (specimens, plants, minerals), 聚集/蒐集 toward assembling a group of things or people, and 㪘 toward the older, more literary sense of restraining or drawing something in. None is a strict duplicate of another; which one is idiomatic depends on what's being collected.

**内容 sits at the center of this domain's own name**: "content, substance" — what a container holds — is the abstract counterpart to every concrete vessel word on this page ([[容器]], [[箱]], [[袋]], etc.).

**個人 vs. 個 — a person vs. a counting classifier**: [[個人]] means "an individual" in the human sense — a single person, as opposed to a group or organization. [[個]] is the general-purpose measure word for counting individual items of nearly any kind (一個, 両個, etc.) — related in the abstract "one distinct unit" sense, but not interchangeable: 個人 is never used to count objects, and 個 does not mean "a person" on its own.
