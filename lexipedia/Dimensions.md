# Dimensions

Dimensions vocabulary covers the physical scales things are measured on — size, length, distance, weight — along with the quantifiers and degree words (few, several, only, very) that measure amounts rather than objects. Rosenfelder splits these into separate lists (Quantifiers, Valuation, Linear extent, Distance, Size); this vault keeps that same internal grouping, since it's a genuinely useful way to organize the material.

## Semantic Range Notes

**不過/只/唯/僅僅 — four "only"s, differing by force and register**: all four narrow a statement down to a single item or a bare minimum, but they don't sound alike. [[不過]] is the mildest, closer to "merely, nothing more than." [[只]] is the everyday, neutral "just, only." [[唯]] is more literary/emphatic — the one and only. [[僅僅]] (reduplicated) stresses bare sufficiency — "barely, just barely enough."

**巨大 vs. 碩大 vs. 宏大 vs. 浩大 — four "huge"s with different connotations**: [[巨大]] is the plain, neutral "gigantic, enormous." [[碩大]] leans toward solid, weighty bigness. [[宏大]] and [[浩大]] both carry a grander, more admiring tone — closer to "magnificent, vast" than merely "big" — with 浩大 specifically evoking something flood-like or overwhelming in scale (cf. [[蒼海]], "the vast ocean," on [Water](Water.md)).

**高 vs. 低下 vs. 矮 vs. 短 — high, tall, low, short**: [[高]] covers both "high" and "tall." Its opposite for height is [[低下]], since the bare character 低 is bound. [[矮]] is short of a person's stature, while [[短]] is short of objects and durations. [[低廉]] and [[廉価]] name a low price, not a low height, and are on [Trade](Trade.md).

**大 vs. 大量 vs. 大小 vs. 規模 — big, a lot, size, scale**: [[大]] is the plain adjective "big." [[大量]] is a large amount, [[大小]] names size, the large and the small together, and [[規模]] is the scale or scope of an undertaking.

**全/全体/全部/一切/皆/各/毎 — whole, all, each, every**: [[全]] is the totalizing quantifier. [[全体]], [[全部]] and [[一切]] name the whole, and [[皆]] is "all, every." [[各]] and [[毎]] are distributive, "each" and "every."

**幾乎 — almost**: [[幾乎]] is Chinese 幾乎 and Vietnamese cơ hồ. The Japanese word for "almost" is written with the single character 殆, which Dan'a'yo keeps bound inside [[危殆]].

**本地 — local**: [[本地]] is "local, of this locality." It is read ほんち in Japanese, not the Buddhist ほんじ; see its page.
