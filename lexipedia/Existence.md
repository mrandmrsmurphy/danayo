# Existence

Existence vocabulary covers the abstract verbs and nouns of being, becoming and ceasing to be — not a physical domain like Metals or Animals, but a philosophical one. It runs from the copulas ("to be," "to have," "to lack"), through reality and the unreal, change, growth and decline, origin and cause, parts and wholes, fate, and identity and difference, to ending, creating and destroying, and the showing of things that exist. Dan'a'yo inherits Chinese's characteristic split of "to be" into several distinct, non-interchangeable copulas rather than merging them into a single English-style verb, and this domain is where that split becomes visible and has to be learned explicitly.

## Core Vocabulary (A1–A2)

### Being

- <ruby>[是](../words/是.md)<rt>ㄙㄝ</rt></ruby>: to be, to equal — the identity copula ("X is Y").
- <ruby>[非](../words/非.md)<rt>ㄈㄧ</rt></ruby>: to not be — 是's direct negative counterpart ("X is not Y").
- <ruby>[在](../words/在.md)<rt>ㄐㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>: to exist, to be present — the locative/existential copula ("X is at Y").
- <ruby>[存在](../words/存在.md)<rt>ㄐㄛㄋㄐㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>: to exist, existence — the general abstract verb/noun for "to exist" (survives, persists), distinct from 在's locative "to be at."
- <ruby>[有](../words/有.md)<rt>⼜</rt></ruby>: to have, to possess — also functions as "there is/are."
- <ruby>[無](../words/無.md)<rt>ㄇㄜ</rt></ruby>: to lack, to not have — the negative existential, "there is not."

### Becoming & the Present

- <ruby>[成](../words/成.md)<rt>ㄙㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>: to become.
- <ruby>[現](../words/現.md)<rt>ㄏ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby>: present, current (adjective).
- <ruby>[現在](../words/現在.md)<rt>ㄏ⼶ㄋㄐㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>: the present, now.
- <ruby>[現実](../words/現実.md)<rt>ㄏ⼶ㄋㄙㄧㄊ</rt></ruby>: reality — what is actually the case, the ground that [[幻想]] and [[虚構]] are measured against.
- <ruby>[現象](../words/現象.md)<rt>ㄏ⼶ㄋㄙ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>: a phenomenon.
- <ruby>[成立](../words/成立.md)<rt>ㄙㄧㄫㄌㄧㄆ</rt></ruby>: to come into existence, be established, gain approval.

### All & Every

- <ruby>[一切](../words/一切.md)<rt>ㄧㄊㄑㄝㄊ</rt></ruby>: everything, the whole, entirety — a general totality quantifier ("all of X, without exception"), more abstract than [[萬物]]'s specific "all existing things"; a near-synonym of [[全体]]/[[全部]], both of which lean toward a concrete whole made of counted parts where 一切 stays inclusive/abstract.
- <ruby>[全体](../words/全体.md)<rt>ㄐ⼔ㄋㄊㄝㄧ</rt></ruby>: the whole, entirety.
- <ruby>[全部](../words/全部.md)<rt>ㄐ⼔ㄋㄅㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>: everything, the entirety.
- <ruby>[各種](../words/各種.md)<rt>ㄍㄚㄎㄐㄛㄫ</rt></ruby>: every kind, all kinds, the full range.

## Intermediate (B1–B2)

### Reality & Existence

- <ruby>[実在](../words/実在.md)<rt>ㄙㄧㄊㄐㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>: real existence, to exist in fact — [[存在]] says a thing is; 実在 says it is really so, the opposite of [[架空]].
- <ruby>[現存](../words/現存.md)<rt>ㄏ⼶ㄋㄐㄛㄋ</rt></ruby>: extant, still existing now — of a thing that has survived from the past.
- <ruby>[実体](../words/実体.md)<rt>ㄙㄧㄊㄊㄝㄧ</rt></ruby>: an entity, substance — the real thing under its appearances, where [[現象]] is how it appears.
- <ruby>[現状](../words/現状.md)<rt>ㄏ⼶ㄋㄐ⺢ㄫ</rt></ruby>: the current state of affairs, the status quo.
- <ruby>[自存](../words/自存.md)<rt>ㄐㄧㄜㄐㄛㄋ</rt></ruby>: self-preservation, to subsist on one's own.
- <ruby>[潜在](../words/潜在.md)<rt>ㄐㄝㄇㄐㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>: potential, latent.

### Nothingness & the Unreal

- <ruby>[莫](../words/莫.md)<rt>ㄇㄚㄎ</rt></ruby>: there is none who, nothing that — a negative existential quantifier over an action or agent ("莫 + V" = "no one Vs"), distinct from 無's static "there is not."
- <ruby>[虚](../words/虚.md)<rt>ㄏ⼄</rt></ruby>: void, emptiness — the philosophical/substantive noun for nothingness, distinct from [[無]]'s grammatical "to lack"; also, by extension, "false, hollow" (虚偽, "falsehood"; 虚弱, "weak, frail").
- <ruby>[虚構](../words/虚構.md)<rt>ㄏ⼄ㄍㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>: fiction, fabrication — something made up without a basis in fact; stresses that it was constructed.
- <ruby>[架空](../words/架空.md)<rt>ㄍㄚ·ㄎㄛㄫ</rt></ruby>: imaginary, fictitious — literally "built on empty air," having no existence outside a story or an idea.
- <ruby>[奇跡](../words/奇跡.md)<rt>ㄍㄨㄧㄐㄝㄎ</rt></ruby>: a miracle, a marvel — an event that ordinary means cannot explain.
- <ruby>[超自然](../words/超自然.md)<rt>ㄊㄚㄨㄐㄧㄜㄋ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby>: the supernatural — what lies beyond [[自然]] and its laws.

### All Things

- <ruby>[萬物](../words/萬物.md)<rt>ㄇㄛㄋㄇㄨㄊ</rt></ruby>: the myriad things — everything that exists, collectively.
- <ruby>[万物](../words/万物.md)<rt>ㄇㄛㄋㄇㄨㄊ</rt></ruby>: the myriad things — the 大字 (anti-falsification) orthographic counterpart of [[萬物]], not a simple alias (matching the established 萬/万 distinct-word convention elsewhere in this vault).
- <ruby>[万象](../words/万象.md)<rt>ㄇㄛㄋㄙ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>: all things, every manifestation of nature — leans toward natural phenomena specifically (part of the [[森羅万象]] family), distinct from [[萬物]]/[[万物]]'s plainer "all existing things."
- <ruby>[事物](../words/事物.md)<rt>ㄐㄧ·ㄇㄨㄊ</rt></ruby>: things, the totality of objects and matters — an academic/philosophical register (事物の本質, "the essential nature of things"), pairing concrete 物 with abstract 事 rather than [[萬物]]'s plainer "myriad things."

### Appearing & Disappearing

- <ruby>[出現](../words/出現.md)<rt>ㄑㄨㄊㄏ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby>: to appear, to emerge.
- <ruby>[顕現](../words/顕現.md)<rt>ㄏㄝㄋㄏ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby>: to manifest, to appear — more formal register than 出現.
- <ruby>[興起](../words/興起.md)<rt>ㄏㄜㄫㄎㄧ</rt></ruby>: to rise up, emerge, flourish.
- <ruby>[興](../words/興.md)<rt>ㄏㄜㄫ</rt></ruby>: to entertain, to rise up, to prosper — the bound root behind [[興起]] above; also, in a separate sense, "glad, pleased" (高興).
- <ruby>[発](../words/発.md)<rt>ㄈㄚㄊ</rt></ruby>: to issue forth, discharge, release — one of the Sinosphere's most productive bound roots, behind [[発生]] ("to arise") above and [[出発]] ("to depart," on [[Movement]]), [[発見]] ("to discover," on [[Knowledge]]), and many more.
- <ruby>[昌盛](../words/昌盛.md)<rt>ㄑ⺢ㄫㄙㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>: prosperous, flourishing.
- <ruby>[興旺](../words/興旺.md)<rt>ㄏㄜㄫ⺢ㄫ</rt></ruby>: thriving, prosperous — actively surging, as of a business or household, near [[昌盛]]'s settled prosperity.
- <ruby>[消](../words/消.md)<rt>ㄙ⼄ㄨ</rt></ruby>: to vanish, to disappear — covers both Rosenfelder line items; Dan'a'yo doesn't split "vanish" from "disappear" as separate words.
- <ruby>[消失](../words/消失.md)<rt>ㄙ⼄ㄨㄙㄧㄊ</rt></ruby>: to vanish, a disappearance — the compound of [[消]] that stresses the loss.
- <ruby>[消滅](../words/消滅.md)<rt>ㄙ⼄ㄨ·ㄇㄝㄊ</rt></ruby>: to vanish, die out, become extinct; (transitive) to annihilate.

#### Concealment — disappearing by intent, not just fading

- <ruby>[潜伏](../words/潜伏.md)<rt>ㄐㄝㄇㄅㄨㄎ</rt></ruby>: to hide, lurk, lie low.
- <ruby>[隠匿](../words/隠匿.md)<rt>ㄜㄋㄋㄧㄎ</rt></ruby>: to conceal, hide, cover up.
- <ruby>[隠蔵](../words/隠蔵.md)<rt>ㄜㄋㄑㄚㄫ</rt></ruby> / <ruby>[隠蔽](../words/隠蔽.md)<rt>ㄜㄋㄆㄝ</rt></ruby>: to hide, conceal — two independently attested near-synonyms.
- <ruby>[隠滅](../words/隠滅.md)<rt>ㄜㄋㄇㄝㄊ</rt></ruby>: to destroy (evidence), to vanish, fade away — carries a specifically legal "destroy the evidence" connotation alongside its general sense.
- <ruby>[隠形](../words/隠形.md)<rt>ㄜㄋㄏㄝㄫ</rt></ruby>: invisible, hidden in form.

### Change & Transformation

- <ruby>[変化](../words/変化.md)<rt>ㄅ⼶ㄋㄏ⺢</rt></ruby>: to change — the general-purpose word.
- <ruby>[悪化](../words/悪化.md)<rt>ㄚㄎㄏ⺢</rt></ruby>: to worsen, aggravate, deteriorate.
- <ruby>[改変](../words/改変.md)<rt>ㄍㄚㄧㄅ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby>: to alter, change.
- <ruby>[更新](../words/更新.md)<rt>ㄍㄚㄫㄙㄧㄋ</rt></ruby>: to renew, replace, refresh.
- <ruby>[更迭](../words/更迭.md)<rt>ㄍㄚㄫㄉㄚㄊ</rt></ruby>: to change, replace, alternate.
- <ruby>[変動](../words/変動.md)<rt>ㄅ⼶ㄋㄉㄛㄫ</rt></ruby>: to change, fluctuate — implies ongoing or unstable movement, not a single discrete change (also <ruby>[起伏](../words/起伏.md)<rt>ㄎㄧㄅㄨㄎ</rt></ruby>, "to undulate, fluctuate," a near-synonym leaning more figurative — "the ups and downs").
- <ruby>[転化](../words/転化.md)<rt>ㄐ⼔ㄋㄏ⺢</rt></ruby>: to change, convert — implies turning into something categorically different.
- <ruby>[変成](../words/変成.md)<rt>ㄅ⼶ㄋㄙㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>: to change into, metamorphosize into.
- <ruby>[化](../words/－化.md)<rt>ㄏ⺢</rt></ruby>: "-ification" — a bound suffix attaching to a noun to mean "to become/turn into X" (as in 転化, 変化 themselves).
- <ruby>[変](../words/変.md)<rt>ㄅ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby>: to change, transform, alter — the bound root behind [[変化]]/[[変動]]/[[変成]] above.
- <ruby>[交替](../words/交替.md)<rt>ㄍ⼄ㄨㄑㄝㄧ</rt></ruby>: alternation, to take turns, to swap.
- <ruby>[代替](../words/代替.md)<rt>ㄉㄚㄧㄑㄝㄧ</rt></ruby> / <ruby>[代用](../words/代用.md)<rt>ㄉㄚㄧ⼄ㄫ</rt></ruby>: to replace, substitute — 代替 the general act, 代用 leaning toward "to use X as a substitute for Y."
- <ruby>[置換](../words/置換.md)<rt>ㄑㄧㄏ⺢ㄇ</rt></ruby>: to permute, substitute, displace — a more technical/mathematical register than 代替 above.
- <ruby>[変遷](../words/変遷.md)<rt>ㄅ⼶ㄋㄑㄝㄋ</rt></ruby>: change over time, transition — the whole course of successive changes, as of a language or a city.
- <ruby>[推移](../words/推移.md)<rt>ㄑㄨㄧ⼶ㄧ</rt></ruby>: to shift, pass, elapse — a situation moving from one state to another, or time going by; the movement within a [[変遷]].

### Growth & Decline

- <ruby>[成長](../words/成長.md)<rt>ㄙㄧㄫㄐㄚㄫ</rt></ruby>: to grow, growth — from small to full, of a living thing, a child or a business.
- <ruby>[進歩](../words/進歩.md)<rt>ㄐㄧㄋㄅㄛ</rt></ruby>: progress, to advance and improve — carries the idea of getting better, not only of going on.
- <ruby>[漸進](../words/漸進.md)<rt>ㄐㄝㄇㄐㄧㄋ</rt></ruby>: gradual advance, gradual — step by step, as in reform that does not come all at once.
- <ruby>[後退](../words/後退.md)<rt>ㄏㄨㄛㄊㄧㄜ</rt></ruby>: to retreat, regress — to move back, or to fall back to a worse state.
- <ruby>[衰退](../words/衰退.md)<rt>ㄙ⼔ㄧㄊㄧㄜ</rt></ruby>: decline.
- <ruby>[減少](../words/減少.md)<rt>ㄍㄚㄇㄙㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>: to decrease, decline in number — the opposite of [[増加]] (on [[Dimensions]]).
- <ruby>[縮小](../words/縮小.md)<rt>ㄙㄨㄎㄙㄛ</rt></ruby>: to shrink, reduce in size — the opposite of [[拡大]] (on [[Dimensions]]).

### Persistence & Traces

- <ruby>[存続](../words/存続.md)<rt>ㄐㄛㄋㄙ⼄ㄎ</rt></ruby>: to continue to exist, persist — whether a thing, such as an institution or a custom, is still there at all.
- <ruby>[残](../words/残.md)<rt>ㄐㄚㄋ</rt></ruby>: remaining, left over; not broken off.
- <ruby>[痕跡](../words/痕跡.md)<rt>ㄏㄜㄋㄐㄝㄎ</rt></ruby>: vestige, trace.
- <ruby>[無常](../words/無常.md)<rt>ㄇㄜㄙ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>: transient, impermanent, mutable.

### Presence & Attendance

- <ruby>[出席](../words/出席.md)<rt>ㄑㄨㄊㄙㄝㄎ</rt></ruby>: present, in attendance.
- <ruby>[缺席](../words/缺席.md)<rt>ㄎ⼔ㄊㄙㄝㄎ</rt></ruby>: absent, not in attendance — the direct opposite of 出席.
- <ruby>[不在](../words/不在.md)<rt>ㄅㄛㄊㄐㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>: absence, not present — not being at the place one is expected; [[無]] says a thing is not there at all, 不在 that it is not here.
- <ruby>[無人](../words/無人.md)<rt>ㄇㄜ·ㄋㄧㄋ</rt></ruby>: depopulated, unmanned.
- <ruby>[目前](../words/目前.md)<rt>ㄇㄨㄎㄐㄝㄋ</rt></ruby>: at present, before one's eyes — literally "eye-front" (also <ruby>[眼前](../words/眼前.md)<rt>ㄚㄋㄐㄝㄋ</rt></ruby>, an independently attested synonym built the same way).

### Origin, Cause & Foundation

- <ruby>[出身](../words/出身.md)<rt>ㄑㄨㄊㄙㄧㄋ</rt></ruby>: origin, background — where a person is from.
- <ruby>[本来](../words/本来.md)<rt>ㄅㄛㄋㄌㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>: originally, by nature — an adverbial sense of origin (also <ruby>[原来](../words/原来.md)<rt>⼔ㄋㄌㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>, an independently attested near-synonym built on a different first character).
- <ruby>[原始](../words/原始.md)<rt>⼔ㄋㄙㄧ</rt></ruby>: primitive, original.
- <ruby>[起源](../words/起源.md)<rt>ㄎㄧ⼔ㄋ</rt></ruby>: origin, source — where and how something began.
- <ruby>[由来](../words/由来.md)<rt>⼜ㄛㄌㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>: origin, derivation — where a thing comes from and how it got its present form.
- <ruby>[根源](../words/根源.md)<rt>ㄍㄜㄋ⼔ㄋ</rt></ruby>: root cause, origin.
- <ruby>[源泉](../words/源泉.md)<rt>⼔ㄋㄐ⼔ㄋ</rt></ruby>: fountainhead, wellspring, source.
- <ruby>[元凶](../words/元凶.md)<rt>⼔ㄋㄏ⼜ㄫ</rt></ruby>: chief culprit, ringleader — also "root cause," the sense that ties it to this cluster.
- <ruby>[追遡](../words/追遡.md)<rt>ㄊㄨㄧㄙㄛ</rt></ruby> / <ruby>[遡及](../words/遡及.md)<rt>ㄙㄛㄍㄨㄆ</rt></ruby>: to trace back, investigate origins — 遡及 also carries the specifically legal sense "retroactivity, to apply retroactively."
- <ruby>[基盤](../words/基盤.md)<rt>ㄍㄧㄅㄚㄋ</rt></ruby> / <ruby>[根本](../words/根本.md)<rt>ㄍㄜㄋㄅㄛㄋ</rt></ruby> / <ruby>[本](../words/本.md)<rt>ㄅㄛㄋ</rt></ruby>: foundation, basis, base — a near-synonym cluster from the concrete (基盤) to the bare abstract root (本).
- <ruby>[基本](../words/基本.md)<rt>ㄍㄧㄅㄛㄋ</rt></ruby>: foundation, base, standard.
- <ruby>[以前](../words/以前.md)<rt>ㄧㄐㄝㄋ</rt></ruby> / <ruby>[従前](../words/従前.md)<rt>ㄐㄛㄫㄐㄝㄋ</rt></ruby>: before, formerly, previously (also <ruby>[従来](../words/従来.md)<rt>ㄐㄛㄫㄌㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>, "hitherto, conventional, existing" — the same temporal root extended to "the way things have always been").
- <ruby>[縁故](../words/縁故.md)<rt>⼶ㄋㄍㄛ</rt></ruby>: reason, cause, the why of something — the stand-in for 故, which cannot appear independently (alias 緣故).
- <ruby>[要因](../words/要因.md)<rt>⼄ㄨ·ㄧㄋ</rt></ruby>: a factor, key cause — one of the causes that together produce a result.
- <ruby>[動机](../words/動机.md)<rt>ㄉㄛㄫㄍㄧㄜ</rt></ruby>: a motive, motivation — the reason in a person's mind that sets them to act; written 動機 in the alias form.

### Parts & Composition

- <ruby>[構成](../words/構成.md)<rt>ㄍㄛㄨㄙㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>: to compose, constitute, composition — to make up a whole from parts; the opposite of [[分解]].
- <ruby>[成分](../words/成分.md)<rt>ㄙㄧㄫㄅㄨㄋ</rt></ruby>: a component, ingredient — one of the parts a whole is made of.
- <ruby>[要素](../words/要素.md)<rt>⼄ㄨㄙㄛ</rt></ruby>: element, factor, component.
- <ruby>[分解](../words/分解.md)<rt>ㄅㄨㄋㄍ⼘ㄧ</rt></ruby>: to decompose, break down — to separate a whole into its parts.
- <ruby>[混然](../words/混然.md)<rt>ㄏㄛㄋㄋ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby>: integrated, unified, undifferentiated (混然一体, "fused into one whole").
- <ruby>[重畳](../words/重畳.md)<rt>ㄑㄛㄫㄉㄝㄆ</rt></ruby>: layering, overlapping — to lay one thing on another; in linguistics, reduplication.

### Fate & Destiny

A real cluster in classical and religious usage, not force-differentiated where the source languages themselves don't draw a hard line.

- <ruby>[運命](../words/運命.md)<rt>ㄨㄋㄇ⼶ㄫ</rt></ruby> / <ruby>[命運](../words/命運.md)<rt>ㄇ⼶ㄫㄨㄋ</rt></ruby>: fate, destiny, doom — two independently attested compounds built from the same two morphemes in reversed order.
- <ruby>[宿命](../words/宿命.md)<rt>ㄙㄨㄎㄇ⼶ㄫ</rt></ruby>: destiny, fate — leans toward the unavoidable, pre-ordained sense.
- <ruby>[運数](../words/運数.md)<rt>ㄨㄋㄙㄨ</rt></ruby>: luck, fortune, fate.
- <ruby>[随性](../words/随性.md)<rt>ㄙㄨㄧㄙㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>: to follow one's own nature — spontaneous, easygoing, going with one's own inclinations; not a fate word, despite its place beside the fate cluster in its source languages' dictionaries.
- <ruby>[因縁](../words/因縁.md)<rt>ㄧㄋ·⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby>: a predestined bond, karma — the Buddhist-inflected member of the cluster.
- <ruby>[因果](../words/因果.md)<rt>ㄧㄋㄍ⺢</rt></ruby>: causal, karmic, fateful.
- <ruby>[幸運](../words/幸運.md)<rt>ㄏㄚㄫㄨㄋ</rt></ruby>: lucky, fortunate.
- <ruby>[契机](../words/契机.md)<rt>ㄎㄝㄧㄍㄧㄜ</rt></ruby>: an opportunity, turning point.
- <ruby>[吉](../words/吉.md)<rt>ㄍㄧㄊ</rt></ruby>: lucky, auspicious, favorable (吉祥, traditionally displayed in calligraphy at the New Year).
- <ruby>[凶](../words/凶.md)<rt>ㄏ⼜ㄫ</rt></ruby>: unlucky, ominous, calamitous — [[吉]]'s direct opposite; by extension also "vicious, murderous" (凶悪, 凶手).

### Decision & Judgment

- <ruby>[判断](../words/判断.md)<rt>ㄆㄚㄋㄉ⺢ㄋ</rt></ruby>: to judge, decide, determine.
- <ruby>[衡量](../words/衡量.md)<rt>ㄏㄚㄫㄌ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>: to weigh, measure, evaluate.
- <ruby>[決](../words/決.md)<rt>ㄎ⼔ㄊ</rt></ruby> / <ruby>[決定](../words/決定.md)<rt>ㄎ⼔ㄊㄐㄝㄫ</rt></ruby>: to determine, decide, choose — 決 the bare root, 決定 the fuller everyday compound.

### Lack, Necessity & Stability

- <ruby>[必要](../words/必要.md)<rt>ㄅㄧㄊ⼄ㄨ</rt></ruby>: necessary, essential (its negative counterpart is <ruby>[不用](../words/不用.md)<rt>ㄅㄛㄊ⼄ㄫ</rt></ruby>, "not necessary, unused, useless").
- <ruby>[必須](../words/必須.md)<rt>ㄅㄧㄊㄙㄨ</rt></ruby>: essential, a must — 必 "surely" + 須 "indispensable."
- <ruby>[欠乏](../words/欠乏.md)<rt>ㄎ⼘ㄇㄅㄚㄆ</rt></ruby> / <ruby>[欠缺](../words/欠缺.md)<rt>ㄎ⼘ㄇㄎ⼔ㄊ</rt></ruby>: to lack, be deficient — near-synonyms alongside Core [[無]] above.
- <ruby>[用尽](../words/用尽.md)<rt>⼄ㄫㄐㄧㄋ</rt></ruby>: to exhaust, use up.
- <ruby>[障碍](../words/障碍.md)<rt>ㄐㄚㄫㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>: obstacle, barrier, handicap.
- <ruby>[安定](../words/安定.md)<rt>ㄚㄋㄐㄝㄫ</rt></ruby>: stable, settled (its negative counterpart is <ruby>[不穏](../words/不穏.md)<rt>ㄅㄛㄊ·ㄛㄋ</rt></ruby>, "unstable, unsettled, ominous").

## Semantic Range Notes

The most consequential feature of this domain is that Dan'a'yo, following Chinese, **splits "to be" into six genuinely distinct, non-interchangeable words** rather than merging them the way English does — confirmed by the vault's own grammar, where all six independently share `pos: 系詞` (copula), not a coincidence of similar meaning but a real shared grammatical class. [[是]] is the identity copula ("this is that"), with [[非]] as its direct negation ("this is not that"). [[在]] is the locative/existential copula ("X is located at Y" / "X exists"), with [[存在]] as the general abstract verb/noun for existence itself — distinct from 在's specifically locative flavor. [[有]] is possessive-existential ("X has Y" / "there is a Y"), with [[無]] as its negation ("there is no Y"). [[莫]] is a sixth, subtler case: a negative existential *quantifier* over an action or agent rather than a static negation of possession — "莫 + verb" means "no one/nothing does [verb]," distinct from 無's flat "there is not [a thing]." A learner cannot substitute any of these for another — "the book is on the table" needs 在, not 是; "there is a book" needs 有, not 在; "no one came" needs 莫, not 無.

**Being, reality and the unreal are four different questions.** [[存在]] says only that a thing is; [[実在]] says that it is really so, in fact and not only in thought; [[現存]] says that it has survived and is still there now; [[実体]] names the substance that underlies how a thing appears ([[現象]]). [[現実]] is reality as the actual state of affairs, and [[不在]] is not being here, where [[無]] is not being anywhere. The unreal has its own pair: [[虚構]] stresses that a thing was constructed (a fiction, a fabrication), [[架空]] says that it exists only in an idea or a story, and both are the opposite of 実在. [[虚]] is the philosophical noun for emptiness itself; the pure fantasy word [[幻想]] lives on [[Mind]].

**Totality is a ladder of words, not one word.** [[萬物]] and [[万物]] are the plain "myriad things" — two distinct words in the vault's 萬/万 convention, not one word with an alias. [[万象]] leans toward natural phenomena, and [[事物]] is the academic register that pairs concrete 物 with abstract 事. [[一切]] is the abstract "all, without exception"; [[全体]] and [[全部]] lean toward a concrete whole made of counted parts, and [[各種]] means "every kind" rather than "every one."

English "present" is a genuine polysemy trap that Dan'a'yo does **not** collapse into one word: the temporal sense ([[現在]], "now"; [[現]] as the adjective; [[目前]]/[[眼前]], "before one's eyes"), the attendance sense ([[出席]], "present at"), the display/verb sense ([[提示]], "to present, show"), and the gift-noun sense ([[礼物]], "a present") are all separate, unrelated words. [[現状]] is the present state of affairs, and [[現実]] the present reality. A learner reaching for "present" in English needs to first decide which of these senses is meant before choosing a Dan'a'yo word.

**Appearing is a pair; vanishing is a ladder.** [[出現]] is the everyday "to appear, emerge" and [[顕現]] the formal "to manifest." For disappearing, [[消]] is the bare root, [[消失]] its compound stressing the loss, and [[消滅]] to die out for good. Past that come the words for ending on a larger scale: [[滅亡]] is to perish, of a people, a state or a line; [[絶滅]] is complete extinction, of a species, leaving no survivors; and [[終焉]] is the literary "last moments," of an era or a life. The plain act of ending (終了) belongs to [[Time]].

The **change cluster is similarly split by manner rather than left as one generic verb**: [[変化]] is the neutral general-purpose "change," [[変動]] specifically implies ongoing fluctuation or instability rather than a single discrete change, and [[転化]] implies converting into something categorically different (a change of kind, not just of state). [[変遷]] names the whole course of change over time, as of a language or a city, and [[推移]] is the passing of a state along that course. The bound suffix [[－化|化]] ("-ification") is the productive engine behind many of these and other coined technical vocabulary items throughout the vault. Along such a course, [[成長]] is growth and [[進歩]] is improvement, with [[漸進]] the gradual pace; their reverses are [[衰退]] (decline) and [[後退]] (retreat). A count that goes down is [[減少]] and a size that goes down is [[縮小]], the reverses of [[増加]] and [[拡大]] on [[Dimensions]].

**Origin words say where, how and why.** [[起源]] is the beginning itself, [[由来]] the line from that source to the present form, [[根源]] the root cause, and [[源泉]] the wellspring a thing keeps drawing from. For cause, [[要因]] is one factor among those that produce a result, [[原因]] (on [[Mind]]) is the cause itself, [[動机]] is the motive in a person's mind, and [[縁故]] is the plain "reason, why."

**Parts and wholes.** [[構成]] makes a whole from parts and [[分解]] takes it apart again; [[成分]] is an ingredient or component, [[要素]] a more abstract element or factor, and [[混然]] a whole in which the parts have fused beyond telling apart.

**Unique has three shades.** [[唯一]] counts: the only one of its class. [[独特]] describes: a character unlike any other. [[特殊]] (on [[Valuation]]) marks the exception to the usual. [[抽象]] is the abstract, with [[具体]] (on [[Mind]]) its concrete counterpart. For comparison, [[差異]] is the neutral noun for a difference and [[差別]] the verb that adds the social sense of discriminating.

**生成 fills the transitive-generate gap.** Rosenfelder's abstract "to generate" had no good match: [[発生]] ("to happen, arise") covers the intransitive neighbor, and [[生産]] is a genuine transitive verb whose everyday register leans industrial/economic ("to manufacture goods"). [[生成]] is the ontological verb, to come into being or to bring into being, and 発生 and 生産 remain the right words for the other two.

**The "Presenting, Showing & Offering" cluster is large because the vault holds a dozen genuine near-synonyms for one Rosenfelder concept.** Most are grouped by register rather than individually differentiated — [[提示]] is the plain default, [[展示]]/[[展覧]]/[[陳列]] are the exhibition register, [[披露]]/[[開示]]/[[顕示]] specifically mean "reveal what was hidden" rather than "show what's already visible." Only two carry a genuinely distinct, worth-flagging nuance: [[誇示]] is pejorative ("show off, flaunt"), and [[陳述]] borders [[Law]]'s own vocabulary (a formal statement, a deposition) rather than staying in this domain's ordinary register.

The **fate/destiny cluster** ([[運命]]/[[命運]]/[[宿命]]/[[運数]]/[[因縁]]) has five real, independently attested words for the same underlying concept, none of which the source languages themselves reduce to a single term, so Dan'a'yo doesn't force one either. [[随性]] sits in the cluster's list but is a different idea: to follow one's own nature, easygoing.
