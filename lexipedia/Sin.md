# Sin

Sin vocabulary covers moral transgression and its consequences — killing, lying, greed, and malice, alongside the shame, forgiveness, and pardon that follow. It draws heavily on words primarily housed on [[Emotions]] and [[Law]], since sin sits at the intersection of feeling (shame, anger, lust) and judgment (guilt, pardon). Temptation, confession, bullying, sadism and the illegitimate child are all covered, with the few English words Dan'a'yo says by strategy (brute, bastard as an insult) explained in the notes below.

## Core Vocabulary (A1–A2)

- <ruby>[罪](../words/罪.md)<rt>ㄐㄛㄧ</rt></ruby>: a sin.
- <ruby>[殺](../words/殺.md)<rt>ㄙㄚㄊ</rt></ruby>: to kill — see [Life](../lexipedia/Life.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[虚偽](../words/虚偽.md)<rt>ㄏ⼄⼔ㄧ</rt></ruby>: a falsehood, lie — see [Knowledge](../lexipedia/Knowledge.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[有罪](../words/有罪.md)<rt>⼜ㄐㄛㄧ</rt></ruby>: guilty — see [Law](../lexipedia/Law.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[正大](../words/正大.md)<rt>ㄐㄧㄫㄉㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>: proper, legitimate, honest.
- <ruby>[承認](../words/承認.md)<rt>ㄙㄨㄫㄋㄧㄋ</rt></ruby>: to admit, acknowledge — the neutral "admit" of a fact; see [Speech](../lexipedia/Speech.md).
- <ruby>[自白](../words/自白.md)<rt>ㄐㄧㄜㄅㄚㄎ</rt></ruby>: to confess, a confession — admitting wrongdoing; see [Speech](../lexipedia/Speech.md).

**How Dan'a'yo says these**: "admit" is <ruby>[[承認]]<rt>ㄙㄨㄫㄋㄧㄋ</rt></ruby> (to acknowledge) and "confess" is <ruby>[[自白]]<rt>ㄐㄧㄜㄅㄚㄎ</rt></ruby>; "tempt" is <ruby>[[誘惑]]<rt>⼜ㄛㄏㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>; "bully" is <ruby>[[欺凌]]<rt>ㄎㄧㄌㄜㄫ</rt></ruby>; "sadist" is <ruby>[[虐待狂]]<rt>⼘ㄎㄉㄚㄧㄍ⺢ㄫ</rt></ruby>; and "bastard" is <ruby>[[私生子]]<rt>ㄙㄧㄜㄙㄚㄫㄐㄜ</rt></ruby> in the literal sense of an illegitimate child (the vulgar insult is not a set word across the languages, so none is coined).

## Intermediate (B1–B2)

- <ruby>[暗殺](../words/暗殺.md)<rt>ㄚㄇㄙㄚㄊ</rt></ruby>: to assassinate, murder — leans specifically toward assassination, the natural companion to [[刺客]] below.

- <ruby>[虐待狂](../words/虐待狂.md)<rt>⼘ㄎㄉㄚㄧㄍ⺢ㄫ</rt></ruby>: a sadist; sadism.
- <ruby>[殺戮](../words/殺戮.md)<rt>ㄙㄚㄊㄌㄨㄎ</rt></ruby>: to slaughter, massacre — the killing itself, stressed as bloodshed, where [[屠殺]] adds the butchering of livestock and the mass killing of people.

### Anger & Malice

- <ruby>[怒気](../words/怒気.md)<rt>ㄋㄛ·ㄎㄧㄜ</rt></ruby>: anger — the plain emotional state. See [Emotions](../lexipedia/Emotions.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[激怒](../words/激怒.md)<rt>ㄍㄝㄎㄋㄛ</rt></ruby>: anger, to enrage — more intense than [[怒気]]. See [Emotions](../lexipedia/Emotions.md) for its own citation there.

- <ruby>[邪心](../words/邪心.md)<rt>ㄙ⼘ㄙㄧㄇ</rt></ruby>: a wicked heart, evil intent — the corrupt inner disposition that wrongdoing flows from; in Korean narrowed to a selfish motive.
- <ruby>[刻薄](../words/刻薄.md)<rt>ㄎㄨㄎㄅㄚㄎ</rt></ruby>: mean — cruel, unkind in character. [[意味]], which also glosses "mean," is an unrelated homograph meaning "to signify, the meaning of a word."

### Shame & Disgrace

- <ruby>[恥辱](../words/恥辱.md)<rt>ㄑㄧ·ㄋㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>: disgrace, shame — the external/reputational sense. See [Society](../lexipedia/Society.md) and [Emotions](../lexipedia/Emotions.md) for their own citations there.
- <ruby>[羞恥](../words/羞恥.md)<rt>ㄙㄨㄛㄑㄧ</rt></ruby>: shame — the internal feeling, distinct from [[恥辱]]'s external disgrace. See [Society](../lexipedia/Society.md) and [Emotions](../lexipedia/Emotions.md) for their own citations there.

### Pride & Villainy

- <ruby>[傲慢](../words/傲慢.md)<rt>ㄚㄨㄇㄚㄋ</rt></ruby>: proud, arrogant. See [Emotions](../lexipedia/Emotions.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[恣肆](../words/恣肆.md)<rt>ㄐㄧㄜㄙㄧㄜ</rt></ruby>: unrestrained, wanton — giving oneself free rein without regard for limits or others; self-indulgent licence, where [[傲慢]] is arrogant pride.
- <ruby>[凶徒](../words/凶徒.md)<rt>ㄏ⼜ㄫㄉㄛ</rt></ruby>: a villain, thug, criminal gang member. See [Conflict](../lexipedia/Conflict.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[野獣](../words/野獣.md)<rt>⼘ㄙ⼜</rt></ruby>: a wild beast, savage animal — also the "brute"; see [Animals](../lexipedia/Animals.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[私生子](../words/私生子.md)<rt>ㄙㄧㄜㄙㄚㄫㄐㄜ</rt></ruby>: a bastard, illegitimate child.

"Brute" is <ruby>[[野獣]]<rt>⼘ㄙ⼜</rt></ruby> (a wild beast) or <ruby>[[凶徒]]<rt>ㄏ⼜ㄫㄉㄛ</rt></ruby> (a thug). **Excluded as a false match**: "brute" also loosely matches [[畜生]], but its primary, documented sense is "domestic animal, livestock" (already housed on [Animals](../lexipedia/Animals.md)) — the insult use is secondary and not cited here.

## Semantic Range Notes

**"Mean" and "excuse" both have false-match candidates that were caught and excluded.** [[意味]] ("mean") is an unrelated homograph meaning "to signify," not the personality trait; [[刻薄]] was cited instead. [[免除]] ("excuse") means administrative exemption from a duty, not forgiving a wrongdoing; [[諒解]] and [[容恕]] were cited instead.

**"Sloth" collides with an unrelated animal homograph.** [[樹懶]] glosses "sloth," but names the actual three-toed sloth, already housed on [Animals](../lexipedia/Animals.md). The deadly sin is instead covered by <ruby>[[怠惰]]<rt>ㄉㄚㄧㄉ⺢</rt></ruby> ("lazy, indolent") and <ruby>[[懶惰]]<rt>ㄌㄚㄋㄉ⺢</rt></ruby> ("lazy"); the vault has no separate noun for sloth-as-vice, so the adjective stands for it.

**<ruby>[[屠殺]]<rt>ㄉㄛㄙㄚㄊ</rt></ruby> and <ruby>[[殺戮]]<rt>ㄙㄚㄊㄌㄨㄎ</rt></ruby> are two shades of "slaughter"**: <ruby>[[屠殺]]<rt>ㄉㄛㄙㄚㄊ</rt></ruby> covers both the literal butchering of livestock and, by extension, the mass killing of people; <ruby>[[殺戮]]<rt>ㄙㄚㄊㄌㄨㄎ</rt></ruby> is slaughter as bloodshed, with no butchering sense.

**業力 and 業報 are the power of sin and the result of sin.** <ruby>[[業報]]<rt>ㄝㄆㄅㄚㄨ</rt></ruby> is the result, the consequence a sin brings back on the one who did it ("the wages of sin is death," Romans 6:23). <ruby>[[業力]]<rt>ㄝㄆㄌㄧㄎ</rt></ruby> is the power: sin as a force that grips a person and drives the deed (Genesis 4:7, "sin is crouching at the door," and Romans 6). The power comes before the deed, <ruby>[[罪業]]<rt>ㄐㄛㄧㄝㄆ</rt></ruby>, and the result comes after it. 業力 is a neologism, attested so far for Chinese only. See [基督敎](../lexipedia/基督敎.md) for the Hebrew senses.

**Shame splits cleanly into external and internal registers**: [[恥辱]] is disgrace as others perceive it; [[羞恥]] is the felt, internal sense of shame. Both genuinely distinct, not a duplicate pair.
