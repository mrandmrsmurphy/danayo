# Government

Government vocabulary spans monarchy and titles of rank, officials and bureaucracy, political parties and systems, elections and assemblies, permission and prohibition, and the compensation and obedience that bind rulers to those who serve them. To rule and to preside are [[主宰]], and to serve is [[伺候]].

## Semantic Range Notes

**郭清 spans political purging and literal surgery**: English Wiktionary glosses Japanese 郭清 as, literary, "removal of evil" and, in medicine, "dissection". The same word is used for a political purge and for surgical dissection.

**允許/准許/兪允 — three "to grant/permit," graded by who's granting**: 允許 and 准許 are largely interchangeable general terms for granting permission. 兪允 is narrower and more formal, specifically an emperor's or sovereign's consent.

**輔佐/輔弼 assist a ruler, 諌議 remonstrates with one**: [[輔佐]] and [[輔弼]] are literary words for assisting a ruler in governing, and 輔弼 also names a counsellor. [[諌議]] is to remonstrate tactfully with a monarch or superior. See [[Society]]'s Assistance & Support cluster for the general words.

**群衆 is deliberately cross-cited, not duplicated**: this exact word already anchors [[Society]]'s Core Vocabulary for the general sense "crowd, gathering." It is cited again here for its political sense, "the masses, the public."

**王妃/皇后/女王 — consort and sovereign**: [[王妃]] is a queen consort or princess consort, [[皇后]] is an empress consort, and [[女王]] is a queen who reigns in her own right. The sovereign empress is [[女皇]].

**大夫 is an official, not a doctor, here**: [[大夫]] is the historical grandee or official of rank. In colloquial Chinese the word also means a doctor, so it is a Split on [文法 - 91疑義語](../grammar/文法%20-%2091疑義語.md).

**国連 is the League of Nations, not the United Nations, here**: [[国連]] follows Mandarin 国联. English Wiktionary glosses Japanese 国連 as "the UN", so it is a Split on [文法 - 91疑義語](../grammar/文法%20-%2091疑義語.md); the United Nations is [[連合国]].

**選挙/投票/議会/国会 — elections and assemblies**: [[選挙]] is an election and [[投票]] is to vote, with [[投票率]] the turnout. [[議会]] is an assembly or legislature in general and [[国会]] is the national one, beside [[衆議院]], the House of Representatives. All are spelled with the Dan'a'yo forms of the characters, with the traditional spellings as aliases.

**保守/急進 — conservative and radical**: [[保守]] is conservative and [[急進]] is radical, with [[極右]] and [[極左]] the extreme ends. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 急進 as "radical; extreme" as well as "to advance rapidly", so the two senses sit close together.

**竄奪 is spelled with 竄**: the word for to usurp is spelled 竄奪, because 竄 reads as 篡 does. 篡 and 簒 are aliases of the character 竄, and 篡奪 and 簒奪 are aliases of the word.
