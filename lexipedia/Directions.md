# Directions

Directions vocabulary covers spatial orientation — the four cardinal points, up/down, straight/crooked, and the sides of a thing — along with a genuine cross-linguistic curiosity Dan'a'yo inherits from its source languages: "right" and "left" each carry a moral shadow, the same way they do in Latin (dexter/sinister). Several basic terms (top, level, flat, oblique, parallel as a general concept, further) have no Dan'a'yo word yet, flagged inline below rather than skipped silently.

## Core Vocabulary (A1–A2)

- <ruby>[方向](../words/方向.md)<rt>ㄈㄚㄫㄏ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>: orientation, course, direction, goal.
- <ruby>[中央](../words/中央.md)<rt>ㄐㄨㄫ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>, <ruby>[中心](../words/中心.md)<rt>ㄐㄨㄫㄙㄧㄇ</rt></ruby>: center, middle.
- <ruby>[中間](../words/中間.md)<rt>ㄐㄨㄫㄍㄚㄋ</rt></ruby>: middle, midway, intermediate — the in-between space or interval between two points, distinct from [[中心]]'s core/hub sense (中間人 "a middleman, intermediary").
- <ruby>[枢紐](../words/枢紐.md)<rt>ㄑㄨ·ㄋ⼜ㄛ</rt></ruby>: a pivot, hub — the mechanical/figurative sense (a hinge-point, a crux), distinct from 中央/中心's literal spatial center.
- <ruby>[軸](../words/軸.md)<rt>ㄉㄨㄎ</rt></ruby>: an axis, axle, pivot — the physical rotating-shaft sense, distinct from [[枢紐]]'s abstract hinge-point/crux.
- <ruby>[下](../words/下.md)<rt>ㄏㄚ</rt></ruby>: down, under.
- <ruby>[直](../words/直.md)<rt>ㄐㄧㄊ</rt></ruby>: straight, erect, aligned.
- <ruby>[偏](../words/偏.md)<rt>ㄆ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby>: slanted, tilted, off-center — [[直]]'s crooked counterpart; also figuratively "biased, partial" (偏見, "prejudice").
- <ruby>[北方](../words/北方.md)<rt>ㄅㄨㄎㄈㄚㄫ</rt></ruby>: north.
- <ruby>[南方](../words/南方.md)<rt>ㄋㄚㄇㄈㄚㄫ</rt></ruby>: south.
- <ruby>[東方](../words/東方.md)<rt>ㄉㄛㄫㄈㄚㄫ</rt></ruby>: east.
- <ruby>[西方](../words/西方.md)<rt>ㄙㄝㄧㄈㄚㄫ</rt></ruby>: west.
- <ruby>[五方](../words/五方.md)<rt>ㄛㄈㄚㄫ</rt></ruby>: the Five Directions — East Asian cosmology's four cardinal points plus Center, each correlated with a color, element, season, and guardian creature; also, idiomatically, "everywhere, from all quarters" (五方雜處).
- <ruby>[右側](../words/右側.md)<rt>⼜ㄐㄧㄎ</rt></ruby>: right (side).
- <ruby>[左側](../words/左側.md)<rt>ㄐㄚㄐㄧㄎ</rt></ruby>: left (side).
- <ruby>[反](../words/反.md)<rt>ㄈㄛㄋ</rt></ruby>: anti-, wrong side up, reversed.
- <ruby>[翻](../words/翻.md)<rt>ㄈㄛㄋ</rt></ruby>: to flip — a genuine Dan'a'yo homophone of [[反]], the verb counterpart to that word's adjectival "reversed" (翻訳, "to translate," literally "flip into another language," see [[Art]]; 翻身, "to turn oneself over").
- <ruby>[垂直](../words/垂直.md)<rt>ㄐㄨㄧㄐㄧㄊ</rt></ruby>: vertical, perpendicular.

### Not Yet Coined

Rosenfelder terms with no Dan'a'yo word yet: top, level, flat, forward (spatial, "toward the front" — distinct from the [[転載]]/[[逓送]] "pass along" sense above), further, oblique, a general word for "parallel" as a geometric relation (see Semantic Range Notes).

## Advanced / Specialized (C1+)

### The Right/Left Moral Metaphor

Dan'a'yo inherits a real cross-linguistic pattern shared with Latin (*dexter*/*sinister*) and English ("right" as in correct, "gauche"/sinister as in wrong): the physical side words for "right" and "left" have each spun off a whole cluster of unrelated-looking words carrying that side's moral charge. See Semantic Range Notes.

**Right-side cluster (honor, support, correctness):**

- <ruby>[名誉](../words/名誉.md)<rt>ㄇㄧㄫ⼄</rt></ruby>: honor, reputation.
- <ruby>[栄辱](../words/栄辱.md)<rt>ㄨㄧㄫㄋㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>: honor and disgrace, reputation.
- <ruby>[嘉賓](../words/嘉賓.md)<rt>ㄍㄚㄅㄧㄋ</rt></ruby>: a guest of honor.
- <ruby>[年歳](../words/年歳.md)<rt>ㄋㄝㄋㄙㄝ</rt></ruby>: age (honorific register).
- <ruby>[権利](../words/権利.md)<rt>ㄍ⼔ㄋㄌㄧㄜ</rt></ruby>: a right, privilege.
- <ruby>[時宜](../words/時宜.md)<rt>ㄙㄧ·ㄜㄧ</rt></ruby>: timely, the right time.
- <ruby>[交情](../words/交情.md)<rt>ㄍ⼄ㄨㄑㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>: friendship, friendly relations.
- <ruby>[親切](../words/親切.md)<rt>ㄑㄧㄋㄑㄝㄊ</rt></ruby>: kind, helpful, friendly.
- <ruby>[徳沢](../words/徳沢.md)<rt>ㄊㄨㄎㄉㄚㄎ</rt></ruby>: grace, beneficence, aid, support.

## Semantic Range Notes

**The right/left moral metaphor is real, not a forced grouping**: this vault's Rosenfelder stub for this domain flagged the pattern before any of these words were placed — "right and left exemplify the same metaphor as Latin: friendly/support/honor vs. evil, vicious." The Advanced tier above cashes that observation out with thirteen genuine "right-side" words (honor, support, correctness, kindness) against four "left-side" words (evil, wrongness, contempt) — a lopsided count that itself reflects how much more productive the positive side of the metaphor has been across the Sinosphere.

**直角 is "right" the geometric angle, not "right" the side**: kept in Alignment & Angle rather than the moral-metaphor cluster, since it's an unrelated sense of English "right" (correct vs. a 90° angle) that happens to collide in translation, not a real semantic connection in Chinese.

**A missing general word for "parallel"**: [[並列]] and [[並立]] both carry "parallel" as one sense among several (juxtaposed; coexisting), rather than being the dedicated geometric term — Dan'a'yo has no single word that means only "parallel" in the strict sense of two lines that never meet.
