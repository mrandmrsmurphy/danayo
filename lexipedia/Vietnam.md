- <ruby>[芽荘](../words/芽荘.md)<rt>ㄚㄐ⺢ㄫ</rt></ruby>: Nha Trang, a coastal resort city of the south-central coast (also written <ruby>芽莊<rt>ㄚㄐ⺢ㄫ</rt></ruby>).
