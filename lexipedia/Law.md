# Law

Law vocabulary spans the whole apparatus of legal order: the abstract concepts (law, justice, right and wrong), the institutions and people that administer it (courts, police, plaintiffs), the process a case moves through (suits, testimony, verdicts), and its consequences (punishment, fines, exile, execution). Dan'a'yo needed several deliberate three-way splits Rosenfelder's flat word list never asked for — a general/compelled/transitive distinction for exile, a building/institution distinction for "court" — cross-checked against real usage rather than left to a single catch-all word.

## Advanced / Specialized (C1+)


### Exile — a deliberate three-way split

Rosenfelder's list names one concept, "exile," but Dan'a'yo splits it by who does what to whom — see Semantic Range Notes for the full writeup.

- <ruby>[流亡](../words/流亡.md)<rt>ㄌ⼜·ㄇㄚㄫ</rt></ruby>: to be exiled, to flee into exile — the general verb.
- <ruby>[亡命](../words/亡命.md)<rt>ㄇㄚㄫㄇ⼶ㄫ</rt></ruby>: to be forced into exile, to defect.
- <ruby>[放逐](../words/放逐.md)<rt>ㄈㄚㄫㄉㄨㄎ</rt></ruby>: to send someone into exile, to banish — the transitive act.
- <ruby>[駆逐](../words/駆逐.md)<rt>ㄎㄨㄉㄨㄎ</rt></ruby>: to drive out, expel, deport — wider than exile; see Semantic Range Notes.
- <ruby>[引渡](../words/引渡.md)<rt>ㄧㄋㄉㄛ</rt></ruby>: extradition, to hand over — a wanted person is handed from one state to another.
- <ruby>[流亡者](../words/流亡者.md)<rt>ㄌ⼜·ㄇㄚㄫㄑㄚ</rt></ruby> / <ruby>[亡命者](../words/亡命者.md)<rt>ㄇㄚㄫㄇ⼶ㄫㄑㄚ</rt></ruby>: an exile, the person — the agentive -者 forms of the first two verbs.


### Legal System & Principles

- <ruby>[法](../words/法.md)<rt>ㄈㄚㄆ</rt></ruby> / <ruby>[法則](../words/法則.md)<rt>ㄈㄚㄆㄐㄨㄎ</rt></ruby>: bare "rule, law" and "law, rule, code" — the roots [[法律]] builds on.
- <ruby>[原則](../words/原則.md)<rt>⼔ㄋㄐㄨㄎ</rt></ruby>: a fundamental principle — the foundational rule one appeals to as a matter of principle, set against the stipulated [[規則]] and the theoretical [[原理]]; compare [[法則]].
- <ruby>[憲法](../words/憲法.md)<rt>ㄏㄝㄋㄈㄚㄆ</rt></ruby>: a constitution.
- <ruby>[大義](../words/大義.md)<rt>ㄉㄚㄧㄜㄧ</rt></ruby>: the principles of justice — the great cause or duty that justifies an act.
- <ruby>[司法](../words/司法.md)<rt>ㄙㄚㄈㄚㄆ</rt></ruby>: the judiciary, administration of justice — the branch of government that applies the law, paired with [[立法]].
- <ruby>[立法](../words/立法.md)<rt>ㄌㄧㄆㄈㄚㄆ</rt></ruby>: to legislate, legislation — the law-making function, where [[法律]] names a law already made.
- <ruby>[法典](../words/法典.md)<rt>ㄈㄚㄆㄉㄝㄋ</rt></ruby>: a code of law — a systematic body of law in one book, containing many [[法律]].
- <ruby>[刑法](../words/刑法.md)<rt>ㄏㄝㄫㄈㄚㄆ</rt></ruby>: criminal law, the penal code — defines crimes and sets their punishments.
- <ruby>[民法](../words/民法.md)<rt>ㄇㄧㄋㄈㄚㄆ</rt></ruby>: civil law — governs private relations such as property, contracts, family and inheritance.
- <ruby>[判例](../words/判例.md)<rt>ㄆㄚㄋㄌㄝ</rt></ruby>: legal precedent — an earlier judgment that guides later cases.
- <ruby>[時効](../words/時効.md)<rt>ㄙㄧㄏ⼘ㄨ</rt></ruby>: the statute of limitations, prescription — the period after which a right or a charge can no longer be enforced.
- <ruby>[責任](../words/責任.md)<rt>ㄐㄚㄎㄋㄧㄇ</rt></ruby>: responsibility, duty — legal liability.
- <ruby>[負債](../words/負債.md)<rt>ㄅ⼜ㄐㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>: debt, liabilities.
- <ruby>[故意](../words/故意.md)<rt>ㄍㄛㄜ</rt></ruby>: intent, purpose — mens rea, criminal intent.
- <ruby>[誓約](../words/誓約.md)<rt>ㄙㄝ⼘ㄎ</rt></ruby>: to swear, take an oath.
- <ruby>[報応](../words/報応.md)<rt>ㄅㄚㄨ·ㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>: retribution, karmic retribution — the consequence that follows a wrong.
- <ruby>[盲従](../words/盲従.md)<rt>ㄇㄚㄫㄐㄛㄫ</rt></ruby>: blind obedience, to follow blindly — the failure mode [[循]] doesn't itself imply.
- <ruby>[除籍](../words/除籍.md)<rt>ㄐㄝㄧㄐㄝㄎ</rt></ruby>: to remove from the register, expunge, expel.

## Semantic Range Notes

**Exile is a deliberate three-way split, cross-checked against real usage rather than left to one catch-all word.** [[流亡]] is the general verb (Mandarin liúwáng, and especially Vietnamese lưu vong, well-attested in reference to the post-1975 Vietnamese diaspora — "chính phủ lưu vong," government in exile). [[亡命]] is the compelled, flee-for-one's-life register (Japanese ぼうめい, Korean 망명, both standard words for political defection). [[放逐]] is the transitive act done *by* an authority *to* someone else (Mandarin/Japanese 放逐, more literary, used figuratively too — 放逐される, "to be ousted"). The agentive -者 forms don't cleanly split the same way in every language: Vietnamese collapses both [[流亡者]] and [[亡命者]] to the same native phrase, người lưu vong ("exiled person," built on 流亡, not a Hán Việt 者-suffixed form) — noted on both words' own pages rather than papered over.

**無罪/有罪 surface a genuine markedness asymmetry.** [[無罪]]'s own Vietnamese field, vô tội, is the living, standard word for "innocent" — but Vietnamese does not mirror this with a parallel Sino-Vietnamese "hữu tội" for [[有罪]] "guilty." Living usage instead says có tội, pairing the Hán Việt tội with the *native* verb có ("to have") rather than Sino-Vietnamese hữu. The negative member of the pair stayed fully Sino-Vietnamese; the positive member didn't.

**天真 was a real, since-corrected false positive.** "Innocent, naive, artless" is a personality trait, not a legal verdict — it had sat in this domain's scratch bucket purely on an English-gloss keyword match until [[無罪]] was coined to properly fill the "not guilty" slot. [[縮]] ("shrink, contract"), [[阻止]], and [[阻碍]] ("thwart, obstruct, block") were caught the same way and rerouted out — none has a specifically legal sense in its own stored gloss.

**地下牢 keeps a fuller form than living Mandarin actually prefers.** Mandarin colloquially shortens "dungeon" to bare 地牢, but Dan'a'yo deliberately keeps the symmetric three-morpheme 地下牢, paralleling [[Buildings]]' own [[地下室]] "basement" (地+下+室). Japanese ちかろう uses the same fuller form, standard in fantasy/game/historical registers; Korean and Vietnamese don't independently attest a fixed compound at all here (Korean prefers the descriptive 지하 감옥; Vietnamese the native hầm tối).

**法/法律/法則 form a near-synonym cluster**, not three unrelated words: bare [[法]] is the root sense ("rule, law"), [[法律]] is the standard word for codified law/statute, and [[法則]] leans toward "a law, a rule, a code" in the more abstract/scientific sense (natural law, a code of rules) as much as the legal one.

**裁判所, 法庭 and 裁判 split the idea of "court" three ways.** [[裁判所]] is the courthouse, the building; [[法庭]] is the court as an institution or sitting; [[裁判]] is the trial, the process held there. Mandarin cáipàn also means "umpire, referee", a sports sense Dan'a'yo does not take.

**無罪, 無実 and 冤罪 separate a verdict, a fact and a wrong.** [[無罪]] is a court's finding that guilt is not proved, [[無実]] is the fact that the person did not do it, and [[冤罪]] is the wrong done when an innocent person is convicted. A person can be 無罪 without being 無実, and a 無実 person can still be convicted, which is the 冤罪.

**控訴 is the appeal.** Japanese こうそ means the first appeal to a higher court, where Mandarin kòngsù means to denounce or accuse. Dan'a'yo takes the Japanese sense: [[控訴]] appeals, [[告訴]] accuses, and [[起訴]] brings the formal charge.

**律師 reads as "lawyer" to Chinese and Vietnamese speakers but not to Japanese ones.** Chinese lǜshī and Vietnamese luật sư both mean lawyer, while Japanese りっし is a Buddhist rank. Japanese and Korean say 弁護士 / 변호사 for a lawyer. The word is kept because it is transparent from its parts, and its Notes record the trap.

**窃盗, 強盗, 盗賊, 劫盗, 狗盗 and 掏模 divide the thief.** [[窃盗]] is theft without confrontation; [[強盗]] is taking by force or threat; [[盗賊]] is the armed bandit or brigand, a type of person rather than a single offence; [[劫盗]] is a dated word for robbery. [[狗盗]] is the sneak thief, as in [[鶏鳴狗盗]], and [[掏模]] is the pickpocket.

**違法, 違反 and 違犯 are three near-synonyms, and no divergence is invented.** [[違法]] names an act contrary to the law, the condition. [[違反]] and [[違犯]] both mean to violate or break a rule. They are homophones in Japanese (いはん) and differ in Mandarin tone (wéifǎn, wéifàn), so the written forms carry the distinction. Modern Vietnamese says vi phạm for all of them.

**規定, 規則, 規範, 章程 and 原則 run from the specific to the general.** [[規定]] is a provision, a specific clause fixed in a text; [[規則]] is a rule laid down by an authority; [[章程]] is the written bylaws of an organisation; [[規範]] is the broader norm that specific provisions express, and need not be written anywhere; [[原則]] is the principle one appeals to.

**処分, 懲罰, 刑罰 and 制裁 sort punishment by who imposes it and how.** [[刑罰]] is the legal punishment for a crime. [[処分]] and [[懲罰]] are the general verbs to punish or discipline, and 処分 also means "to dispose of" in Japanese, a sense Dan'a'yo does not take. [[制裁]] is the broadest, since it need not be criminal and includes sanctions between states.

**Prison words keep register apart.** [[監獄]] is the standard word for prison; [[牢獄]] is an independently attested synonym; [[令圄]] is the classical and literary form (囹圄). [[囚徒]] and [[囚人]] are exact synonyms for the prisoner; 囚徒 is the stand-in that makes the bound character 囚 usable.

**駆逐 sits beside the exile split without joining it.** [[駆逐]] is to drive out or expel, by force, and is wider than banishment; it does not carry the formal sentence of [[放逐]]. It is listed under exile because deportation falls in its range, and [[引渡]], extradition, is the handing of a wanted person from one state to another.

**恩赦 and 赦免 differ in who acts.** [[恩赦]] is an official act of the sovereign, legislature or executive that forgives an offence; [[赦免]], housed on the Sin page, is the general word for pardon, amnesty and absolution. [[減刑]] lowers a sentence without erasing it.

## See Also

**Related domains**:
- [[Government]] — the institutions (courts, police) that administer the law sit at the boundary between the two domains; the judiciary ([[司法]]) is one of the branches of government.
- [[Sin]] — guilt, execution and pardon are shared with the moral vocabulary of sin; [[赦免]] and [[暗殺]] are housed there.
- [[Conflict]] — [[拘禁]], [[監禁]], [[禁錮]], [[脅迫]], [[誹謗]], [[調解]] and [[凶手]] are housed there for their physical or hostile side.
- [[Knowledge]] — [[証拠]], [[証明]] and the discipline of [[律学]] (jurisprudence) are housed there.
- [[Trade]] — [[賠償]], [[報償]] and [[償還]], compensation and repayment, are housed there.
- [[Society]] — [[公民]], citizenship, and [[賭博]], gambling, are housed there.

**Idiomatic uses**:
- [[鶏鳴狗盗]] — "the crowing of a rooster and the stealing of a dog": people skilled only in petty tricks, built on [[狗盗]] above.
- [[殺姦窃偽]] — "murder, adultery, theft, lying": four of the Ten Commandments in one phrase, whose crimes are [[殺人]] and [[窃盗]] above.
