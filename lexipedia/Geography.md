This page gathers the vocabulary of the world's geography: how the earth is divided, the generic words for landforms and borders, and the regions, with their countries, one continent at a time. Places specific to China, Japan, Korea and Vietnam are on their own pages, [China](China.md), [Japan](Japan.md), [Korea](Korea.md) and [Vietnam](Vietnam.md); this page names those countries and sends the reader there.

## <ruby>[世界](../words/世界.md)<rt>ㄙㄝㄍ⼶</rt></ruby>: the world's divisions

### <ruby>[大陸](../words/大陸.md)<rt>ㄉㄚㄧㄌㄨㄎ</rt></ruby> and <ruby>[大洋](../words/大洋.md)<rt>ㄉㄚㄧ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>
In the modern system, the earth is divided into <ruby>[大陸](../words/大陸.md)<rt>ㄉㄚㄧㄌㄨㄎ</rt></ruby> and <ruby>[大洋](../words/大洋.md)<rt>ㄉㄚㄧ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>. The commonly recognized seven continents are:
- <ruby>[亜洲](../words/亜洲.md)<rt>ㄚㄐㄨㄛ</rt></ruby>: Asia
- <ruby>[欧洲](../words/欧洲.md)<rt>ㄛㄨㄐㄨㄛ</rt></ruby>: Europe
- <ruby>[非洲](../words/非洲.md)<rt>ㄈㄧㄐㄨㄛ</rt></ruby>: Africa
- <ruby>[北美洲](../words/北美洲.md)<rt>ㄅㄨㄎㄇㄧㄐㄨㄛ</rt></ruby>: North American continent
- <ruby>[南美洲](../words/南美洲.md)<rt>ㄋㄚㄇㄇㄧㄐㄨㄛ</rt></ruby>: South American continent
- <ruby>[大洋洲](../words/大洋洲.md)<rt>ㄉㄚㄧ⼘ㄫㄐㄨㄛ</rt></ruby>: Oceania
- <ruby>[南極洲](../words/南極洲.md)<rt>ㄋㄚㄇㄍㄧㄎㄐㄨㄛ</rt></ruby>: Antarctica

<ruby>[洲](../words/洲.md)<rt>ㄐㄨㄛ</rt></ruby> is the bound character for a continent (and, in other uses, an islet); <ruby>[大陸](../words/大陸.md)<rt>ㄉㄚㄧㄌㄨㄎ</rt></ruby> is the free word for a continent; <ruby>[美洲](../words/美洲.md)<rt>ㄇㄧㄐㄨㄛ</rt></ruby> is "the Americas", North and South together.

The <ruby>[大洋](../words/大洋.md)<rt>ㄉㄚㄧ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby> are:
- <ruby>[太平洋](../words/太平洋.md)<rt>ㄊㄚㄧㄅ⼶ㄫ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>: Pacific Ocean
- <ruby>[大西洋](../words/大西洋.md)<rt>ㄉㄚㄧㄙㄝㄧ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>: Atlantic Ocean
- <ruby>[印度洋](../words/印度洋.md)<rt>ㄧㄋㄉㄛ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>: Indian Ocean
- <ruby>[北氷洋](../words/北氷洋.md)<rt>ㄅㄨㄎㄅㄧㄫ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>: Arctic Ocean
- <ruby>[南氷洋](../words/南氷洋.md)<rt>ㄋㄚㄇㄅㄧㄫ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>: Southern Ocean

<ruby>[海洋](../words/海洋.md)<rt>ㄏㄚㄧ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby> refers to smaller regional waters, such as the <ruby>[地中海](../words/地中海.md)<rt>ㄉㄧㄜㄐㄨㄫㄏㄚㄧ</rt></ruby> or the <ruby>[南海](../words/南海.md)<rt>ㄋㄚㄇㄏㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>. This model reflects <ruby>[近代](../words/近代.md)<rt>ㄍㄧㄋㄉㄚㄧ</rt></ruby> <ruby>[地理学](../words/地理学.md)<rt>ㄉㄧㄜㄌㄧㄏㄚㄎ</rt></ruby>.

### Poles and lines
- <ruby>[南極](../words/南極.md)<rt>ㄋㄚㄇㄍㄧㄎ</rt></ruby>: the South Pole, distinct from the continent <ruby>[南極洲](../words/南極洲.md)<rt>ㄋㄚㄇㄍㄧㄎㄐㄨㄛ</rt></ruby>.
- <ruby>[北極](../words/北極.md)<rt>ㄅㄨㄎㄍㄧㄎ</rt></ruby>: the North Pole, which, unlike the South Pole, sits in open ocean, not on a continent.

- <ruby>[経緯](../words/経緯.md)<rt>ㄍㄝㄫㄨㄧ</rt></ruby>: latitude and longitude (lit. warp and woof)

### Ancient divisions
In ancient East Asian thought, the world was divided into the Four Seas and the Nine Provinces. Their names, the royal domain and the early states are on [China](China.md). The shift from the Four Seas and Nine Provinces to continents and oceans marks a transition from a civilizational view of the cosmos to a global geographic system.

## Semantic Range Notes

**The seven continents.** Dan'a'yo names a continent with the bound character 洲 (亜洲, 欧洲, 非洲, 大洋洲, 南極洲), while 大陸 is the free word for "continent" and 洲 on its own also means "islet". 美洲 covers North and South America together, and 北美 and 南美 are the halves; 中美 has no word yet.

**Sharing names.** Several names on this page are also names elsewhere: 四国 is both Shikoku and "all under heaven", 江南 is both "south of the river" and Gangnam, and 水原 is both Suwon and Mizuhara. One spelling, one word, several senses; context chooses.

**英国 and 英格蘭.** 英国 is the whole United Kingdom and 英格蘭 only England, though the two are often confused in speech; 英吉利 is the older form of 英格蘭.

**朝鮮 and 韓国.** Both name Korea: 朝鮮 for the North and the Joseon dynasty, 韓国 for the South. Korea's own page sets out the division.

**Country names.** A country is named in Dan'a'yo by a phonetic spelling of its own name (馬来西亜, 巴基斯坦, 荷蘭) or by a semantic coinage (単鷹国 "single-eagle country", 双鷹国 "double-eagle country", 獅子国 "lion country"). The countries still lacking a word are listed under "Not yet coined".
