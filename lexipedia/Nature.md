# Nature

Nature vocabulary covers the physical world outside human construction — landforms, weather, and the countryside — along with the small cluster of words for "nature" itself as an abstract essence or character. It borders [[Elements]] (wind as a physical force) and [[Astronomy]] (sky and earth as celestial bodies), covering the landscape and weather a person actually walks through and lives under.

**Not yet coined or excluded as false matches**: "cave" and "garden" have Intermediate-tier words only (see below); "yard" (a home's own plot of land), "plow," "sow," "crop," and "sleet" all have no dedicated word. [[碼]] also glosses "yard," but only as the Western length unit (see [[Measurement]]), not a plot of land. "Hail" (the weather) is [[雨包]] (Intermediate, below); [[歓呼]], which also glosses "hail," means to cheer or greet (as in "hail Caesar"), not the weather. "Field" (open countryside) is [[田野]] (above); [[球場]], which also glosses "field," means a sports field or stadium, an unrelated sense. "Wild" (untamed wilderness) has no dedicated word; [[奔放]], which glosses "wild," describes an unrestrained *personality*, not untamed land. "Plain" (flat grassland) has no exact dedicated word, though [[草原]] (Intermediate, below) covers the closely related savanna/steppe sense; [[普通]] and [[清淡]], which also gloss "plain," mean "ordinary" and "bland" respectively, unrelated senses.

## Semantic Range Notes

**Three "land/ground" words split by scope, not sense**: [[土地]] is the general word — ground, soil, territory. [[陸地]] narrows to dry land specifically, as opposed to the sea. [[地]] is the bound, classical form that appears mostly in compounds. A learner needs [[土地]] by default and reaches for [[陸地]] only when the land/sea contrast actually matters.

**"Nature" itself is two unrelated concepts in Dan'a'yo, following Chinese**: [[自然]] (Core) is nature in the physical, environmental sense — mountains, forests, weather, the world outside human construction. [[性質]]/[[質素]] (Advanced) mean nature in the abstract sense — the essential character or quality of a thing, as in "the nature of the problem." English uses one word for both; this vault, like its source languages, does not.

**Undocumented near-duplicates cluster around landforms and caves**: [[丘]]/[[山岡]] ("hill"), [[草原]]/[[草地]] ("grassland/meadow"), and [[洞穴]]/[[洞窟]] ("cave") are each pairs glossing the same meaning with no distinction recorded in either word's own Notes — the same pattern already documented on [[Mind]] and [[Movement]], treated the same way [[親族]]/[[親戚]] are on [[Kinship]]: flagged as likely genuine duplicates, not silently invented distinctions.

**"World" splits three ways by register and scope**: [[世界]] is the plain, everyday word — the human and social world. [[地球]] is the planet specifically, a scientific/astronomical framing shared with [[Astronomy]]. [[万邦]] is a literary, almost archaic collective — "the myriad nations" — used where [[世界]] would sound too casual.
