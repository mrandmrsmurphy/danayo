## Metaphors
_consider these; don't accept them blindly_

* a route is movement - "The road goes to Beijing"
* functioning is movement - "The computer is running"
* time is movement - "Time flies.  We're halfway through the year"
* narratives are journeys - "The author takes us from the slums to the penthouse"
* conversion is movement - "I started out an atheist and ended up a believer"
* transfer of possessions is movement - "This watch came from my grandfather"
* leader is movement - "Can he move the party forward"
* benefit is movement - "The country isn't progressing"
