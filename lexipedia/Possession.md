# Possession

Possession vocabulary covers ownership, property, and the relationship between a person and the things that belong to them: having and lacking, getting, lending and borrowing, giving and transferring, losing and taking, and inheriting. It leans on the same few small words as the rest of the sphere, and many of its entries are housed primarily on [[Trade]], [[Law]], [[Conflict]] or [[Existence]]; those are cited here in short, with a pointer, rather than redefined. Under the exception recorded in the lexipedia checklist, this page is `complete` by coverage of the domain, not by the 1,500-word threshold.

## Semantic Range Notes

**Four "have" words graded from state to title**: [[有]] is the bare possessive copula, "there is to me," and it is housed on [[Existence]]. [[所有]] is formal and legal, owning, and its noun [[所有権]] is title. [[占有]] is holding or occupying, which may be without title. [[持]] is physically holding or carrying (it is housed on [[Body]]). A learner reaching for "have" picks by which of these angles is meant.

**公有, 私有 and 共有 are three different kinds of owner**: [[私有]] is owned by a private person, [[公有]] by the public or the state, and [[共有]] by several people together, "co-own." 公有 and 共有 differ in Dan'a'yo only by their vowel (ㄛ against ⼄), and are identical in Korean, so keep them apart by the first character.

**借 is both borrow and lend; 貸出 fixes the lender**: [[借]] covers both directions of a loan, told apart by context or a coverb, as in Chinese. [[貸出]] is the lender's side only. [[賃]] and [[租金]] are rent, the payment for the use, and [[融資]] is lending money as finance.

**Taking without giving is graded by method**: [[窃取]] and [[窃盗]] take secretly (steal, theft), [[奪取]] and [[攫取]] take by force or by grabbing, [[強盗]] takes by threat or violence, and [[没収]] takes by authority and is not a crime. [[喪失]], [[損失]], [[遺]] and [[失物]] are the owner's side: what is lost, rather than who takes it.

**Getting is graded by effort and by means**: [[購入]] and [[購買]] are getting by paying, [[獲得]] is getting by effort or winning, [[取得]] is formal acquisition, [[納]] is receiving or paying in, and [[接受]] is accepting what is offered. The seller's side is [[販売]].

**Giving is graded by relation**: [[贈与]] is a legal gift, [[賜予]] is a gift from a superior, [[礼物]] is a gift between people, [[譲渡]] transfers title, and [[分配]] shares out among several.
