# Possession

Possession vocabulary covers ownership, property, and the relationship between a person and the things (or people, in the case of a household) that belong to them. This page is a new framework, seeded from a handful of already-perfected words rather than a systematic sweep of the domain yet.

## Core Vocabulary (A1–A2)

- <ruby>[主人](../words/主人.md)<rt>ㄐㄨ·ㄋㄧㄋ</rt></ruby>: master, owner, host, proprietor.
- <ruby>[財産](../words/財産.md)<rt>ㄐㄚㄧㄙㄚㄋ</rt></ruby>: property, wealth.
- <ruby>[共有](../words/共有.md)<rt>ㄍ⼄ㄫ⼜</rt></ruby>: to share, co-own.
- <ruby>[共通](../words/共通.md)<rt>ㄍ⼄ㄫㄊㄛㄫ</rt></ruby>: common, shared.
- <ruby>[所有](../words/所有.md)<rt>ㄙㄜ⼜</rt></ruby>: to possess, own; ownership — also "all, every" as a determiner.
- <ruby>[接受](../words/接受.md)<rt>ㄐㄛㄆㄙ⼜</rt></ruby>: to accept, receive.
- <ruby>[占](../words/占.md)<rt>ㄐㄝㄇ</rt></ruby>: to occupy, possess (also <ruby>[占有](../words/占有.md)<rt>ㄐㄝㄇ·⼜</rt></ruby>, a near-synonym compound).
- <ruby>[専用](../words/専用.md)<rt>ㄐ⼔ㄋ·⼄ㄫ</rt></ruby>: dedicated, exclusive use — reserved for one particular person or purpose rather than shared.
- <ruby>[窃取](../words/窃取.md)<rt>ㄑㄝㄊㄑㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>: to steal, filch — taking another's possession illicitly, the negation of rightful ownership.
- <ruby>[蓄](../words/蓄.md)<rt>ㄑㄨㄎ</rt></ruby>: to store, save, hoard, gather — building up a possession over time.
- <ruby>[蓄積](../words/蓄積.md)<rt>ㄑㄨㄎㄐㄝㄎ</rt></ruby>: to accumulate, accumulation — gradual building up through repeated small additions (wealth, knowledge, experience, sediment, fatigue); the process behind [[蓄]]'s storing; the stand-in for the bound character 積.
- <ruby>[携帯](../words/携帯.md)<rt>ㄏ⼔ㄧㄊㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>: to carry with one, portable — to have something on one's person; as a noun, the mobile phone in Japanese (携帯電話, shortened).
