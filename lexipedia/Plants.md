# Plants

Plants vocabulary covers the botanical world — trees, grasses, flowers, and crops — along with their parts (root, stem, leaf, seed), the specialized medicinal and dye plants documented elsewhere in this vault's herbal tradition, and the biome vocabulary describing where plants grow. Flower, bush, weed, herb, nut, log, thorn, rain forest, taiga and tundra are all covered, with the few English words Dan'a'yo says by strategy (twig, hardwood, temperate forest) explained in the notes below.

## Core Vocabulary (A1–A2)

- <ruby>[植物](../words/植物.md)<rt>ㄙㄧㄎㄇㄨㄊ</rt></ruby>: a plant; flora, in general.
- <ruby>[木](../words/木.md)<rt>ㄇㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>: a tree — see [Elements](../lexipedia/Elements.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[草](../words/草.md)<rt>ㄑㄚㄨ</rt></ruby>: grass.
- <ruby>[巻耳](../words/巻耳.md)<rt>ㄍ⼔ㄋㄋㄧ</rt></ruby>: field chickweed, field mouse-ear.
- <ruby>[果実](../words/果実.md)<rt>ㄍ⺢ㄙㄧㄊ</rt></ruby>: fruit, berry — see [Food](../lexipedia/Food.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[堅果](../words/堅果.md)<rt>ㄍㄝㄋㄍ⺢</rt></ruby>: a nut — a hard-shelled dry fruit.
- <ruby>[種子](../words/種子.md)<rt>ㄐㄛㄫㄐㄜ</rt></ruby>: a seed.
- <ruby>[胞子](../words/胞子.md)<rt>ㄅ⼘ㄨㄐㄜ</rt></ruby>: a spore.
- <ruby>[新芽](../words/新芽.md)<rt>ㄙㄧㄋ·ㄚ</rt></ruby>: a bud, sprout.
- <ruby>[根](../words/根.md)<rt>ㄍㄜㄋ</rt></ruby>: a root; foundation.
- <ruby>[葉](../words/葉.md)<rt>⼄ㄆ</rt></ruby>: a leaf.
- <ruby>[豆](../words/豆.md)<rt>ㄉㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>: a bean.

**How Dan'a'yo says these**: "flower" is <ruby>[[草花]]<rt>ㄑㄚㄨㄏ⺢</rt></ruby> ("flowering plants, ornamental flowers") or <ruby>[[花卉]]<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄏㄨㄧ</rt></ruby>, since the character 花 is bound by design and its `stand_in` is 草花; "stick" is <ruby>[[木棒]]<rt>ㄇㄛㄎㄅㄚㄫ</rt></ruby>; "bush" is <ruby>[[灌木]]<rt>ㄍ⺢ㄋㄇㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>; "weed" is <ruby>[[雑草]]<rt>ㄐㄚㄆㄑㄚㄨ</rt></ruby>; "herb" is <ruby>[[薬草]]<rt>⼘ㄎㄑㄚㄨ</rt></ruby>; "nut" is <ruby>[[堅果]]<rt>ㄍㄝㄋㄍ⺢</rt></ruby>; "log" is <ruby>[[原木]]<rt>⼔ㄋㄇㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>; and "thorn" is <ruby>[[荊棘]]<rt>ㄎ⼶ㄫㄍㄧㄎ</rt></ruby> (thorns, brambles), with the verb <ruby>[[刺]]<rt>ㄑㄧㄎ</rt></ruby> "to prick." "Twig" is said as a small branch, 小 and 枝, since 枝 is a bound character and the Chinese 枝條 is attested in Mandarin only.

## Intermediate (B1–B2)

### Plant Parts

- <ruby>[茎](../words/茎.md)<rt>ㄎㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>: a stem, stalk — the general word.
- <ruby>[花梗](../words/花梗.md)<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄍㄚㄫ</rt></ruby>: a pedicel, flower stalk — narrower than [[茎]], specifically the small stem supporting an individual flower.
- <ruby>[枝葉](../words/枝葉.md)<rt>ㄐㄝ⼄ㄆ</rt></ruby>: branches and leaves, together — also, figuratively, "trivial, peripheral details."
- <ruby>[主幹](../words/主幹.md)<rt>ㄐㄨㄍㄚㄋ</rt></ruby>: a (tree) trunk — see [Containers](../lexipedia/Containers.md) for its own citation there; also figuratively the "backbone" of an organization.
- <ruby>[樹皮](../words/樹皮.md)<rt>ㄙㄨㄅㄧ</rt></ruby>: bark — "tree skin."
- <ruby>[株](../words/株.md)<rt>ㄉㄨ</rt></ruby>: a stump — also "stock" and "root" in other registers.
- <ruby>[花弁](../words/花弁.md)<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄅ⼘ㄋ</rt></ruby>: a (flower) petal.
- <ruby>[荊棘](../words/荊棘.md)<rt>ㄎ⼶ㄫㄍㄧㄎ</rt></ruby>: thorns, brambles, briers.
- <ruby>[杪小](../words/杪小.md)<rt>ㄇ⼄ㄙㄛ</rt></ruby>: treetop, extremity — 杪 "tip of a branch" + 小 "small"; the stand-in word that legitimizes 杪.

### Grains, Crops & Vegetables

- <ruby>[禾](../words/禾.md)<rt>ㄏ⺢</rt></ruby>: grain, cereal — the living plant/stalk itself, the cereal radical. See [Food](../lexipedia/Food.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[穀物](../words/穀物.md)<rt>ㄍㄛㄎㄇㄨㄊ</rt></ruby>: grain, cereal crops — the harvested crop collectively, distinct from [[禾]]'s living plant. See [Food](../lexipedia/Food.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[比糠](../words/比糠.md)<rt>ㄅㄧㄜㄎㄚㄫ</rt></ruby>: chaff and bran — worthless dross.
- <ruby>[五穀](../words/五穀.md)<rt>ㄛㄍㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>: the Five Grains — the staple crops of traditional East Asian agriculture (disputed exact membership; typically millet, wheat/barley, soybeans, plus rice or hemp depending on tradition), central to imperial ritual agriculture and the Mandate of Heaven.
- <ruby>[穂](../words/穂.md)<rt>ㄙㄨㄧ</rt></ruby>: an ear of grain — the seed-bearing spike of rice, wheat, or similar cereal (稲穂, "ear of rice").
- <ruby>[苗圃](../words/苗圃.md)<rt>ㄇ⼘ㄨㄅㄛ</rt></ruby>: a plant nursery, seedbed — the plot where seedlings are raised from [[種子]] (seed) before being transplanted; the stand-in for the bound character 圃 (garden plot).
- <ruby>[野菜](../words/野菜.md)<rt>⼘ㄑㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>: vegetables; edible wild herbs.
- <ruby>[蘿蔔](../words/蘿蔔.md)<rt>ㄌㄚㄅㄨㄎ</rt></ruby>: a radish.
- <ruby>[慈姑](../words/慈姑.md)<rt>ㄐㄧㄍㄛ</rt></ruby>: arrowhead, Sagittaria.
- <ruby>[菜蔬](../words/菜蔬.md)<rt>ㄑㄚㄧㄙㄜ</rt></ruby>: vegetables, greens — see [Food](../lexipedia/Food.md) for its own citation there.

### Trees & Woody Plants

- <ruby>[樹木](../words/樹木.md)<rt>ㄙㄨ·ㄇㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>: a tree; plant — the compound form, alongside plain [[木]].
- <ruby>[灌木](../words/灌木.md)<rt>ㄍ⺢ㄋㄇㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>: a bush, shrub — a low woody plant with several stems; the X木 form for woody plants.
- <ruby>[材木](../words/材木.md)<rt>ㄑㄚㄧㄇㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>: lumber, timber — wood as a material, not the living tree.
- <ruby>[原木](../words/原木.md)<rt>⼔ㄋㄇㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>: a log — the unprocessed trunk, before it is sawn into lumber.
- <ruby>[棕枝](../words/棕枝.md)<rt>ㄐㄛㄫㄌ⼄</rt></ruby>: a palm tree.
- <ruby>[棗椰](../words/棗椰.md)<rt>ㄐㄚㄨ⼘</rt></ruby>: the date palm (Phoenix dactylifera) — a specific palm species, beside the general [[棕枝]] "palm tree."
- <ruby>[杉木](../words/杉木.md)<rt>ㄙㄚㄇㄇㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>: a Chinese fir.
- <ruby>[松](../words/松.md)<rt>ㄙ⼄ㄫ</rt></ruby>: pine, fir — the evergreen conifer of longevity and steadfastness (松柏, "pine and cypress"), beside [[柏]].
- <ruby>[針葉樹](../words/針葉樹.md)<rt>ㄐㄧㄇ⼄ㄆㄙㄨ</rt></ruby>: a conifer, softwood tree — pine, cedar and the like.
- <ruby>[針葉林](../words/針葉林.md)<rt>ㄐㄧㄇ⼄ㄆㄌㄧㄇ</rt></ruby>: the taiga, a conifer forest — the cold northern forest belt.
- <ruby>[柳](../words/柳.md)<rt>ㄌ⼜</rt></ruby>: willow — the slender-branched tree of waterside and spring.
- <ruby>[柿木](../words/柿木.md)<rt>ㄙㄜ·ㄇㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>: the Japanese persimmon tree (Diospyros kaki), the stand-in for the bound character 柿.
- <ruby>[柏](../words/柏.md)<rt>ㄅ⼘ㄎ</rt></ruby>: cypress, cedar — evergreen conifer of graveyards and longevity (松柏).
- <ruby>[檀木](../words/檀木.md)<rt>ㄉㄚㄋㄇㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>: sandalwood — a specific hardwood tree species, not a generic word for "hardwood" (the Chinese 硬木 is attested in Mandarin only, so it is not coined); the "softwood" trees are <ruby>[[針葉樹]]<rt>ㄐㄧㄇ⼄ㄆㄙㄨ</rt></ruby>, the conifers.
- <ruby>[檀香](../words/檀香.md)<rt>ㄉㄚㄋㄏ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>: sandalwood as a fragrant material — the aromatic heartwood and incense, as against [[檀木]], the tree and its wood.
- <ruby>[桑木](../words/桑木.md)<rt>ㄙㄚㄫㄇㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>: a mulberry tree.
- <ruby>[朴木](../words/朴木.md)<rt>ㄆㄚㄎㄇㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>: a hackberry.
- <ruby>[竹](../words/竹.md)<rt>ㄐㄨㄎ</rt></ruby>: bamboo.
- <ruby>[梨木](../words/梨木.md)<rt>ㄌㄧㄜㄇㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>: a pear tree.
- <ruby>[実梅](../words/実梅.md)<rt>ㄙㄧㄊㄇㄛㄧ</rt></ruby>: ume, the Japanese apricot.
- <ruby>[松竹梅](../words/松竹梅.md)<rt>ㄙ⼄ㄫㄐㄨㄎㄇㄛㄧ</rt></ruby>: the "Three Friends of Winter" — pine ([[松]]), bamboo ([[竹]]) and plum, the traditional grouping symbolizing resilience through the cold season.
- <ruby>[梨](../words/梨.md)<rt>ㄌㄧㄜ</rt></ruby>: a pear — the fruit; the tree is [[梨木]].
- <ruby>[槐樹](../words/槐樹.md)<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄧㄙㄨ</rt></ruby>: the pagoda tree (Styphnolobium japonicum), a tall shade tree of China and Korea whose flowers are used as food and as a yellow dye; the stand-in for the bound character 槐.
- <ruby>[柊木](../words/柊木.md)<rt>ㄐㄨㄫㄇㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>: the holly osmanthus (Osmanthus heterophyllus), an evergreen shrub with spiny leaves and fragrant white autumn flowers, traditionally hung at doorways to ward off evil spirits; the stand-in for the bound character 柊.
- <ruby>[楊柳](../words/楊柳.md)<rt>⼘ㄫㄌ⼜</rt></ruby>: a willow ("poplar-willow"), the stand-in for the bound character 楊.
- <ruby>[楊梅](../words/楊梅.md)<rt>⼘ㄫㄇㄛㄧ</rt></ruby>: waxfruit, the bayberry (yangmei, *Myrica rubra*), which Japanese writes 山桃; Dan'a'yo's [[山桃]] is the wild peach.
- <ruby>[山桃](../words/山桃.md)<rt>ㄙㄚㄋㄉㄚㄨ</rt></ruby>: the Chinese wild peach (Prunus davidiana) — a peach, not the bayberry that Japanese also writes 山桃 (see [[楊梅]]).
- <ruby>[楊樹](../words/楊樹.md)<rt>⼘ㄫㄙㄨ</rt></ruby>: a poplar or aspen tree.
- <ruby>[楢木](../words/楢木.md)<rt>⼜ㄛ·ㄇㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>: an oak tree — the Konara oak, on the X木 tree pattern.
- <ruby>[兪木](../words/兪木.md)<rt>⼜ㄇㄇㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>: an elm tree — 兪 stands in for 榆 (an alias), on the X木 tree pattern.
- <ruby>[楓樹](../words/楓樹.md)<rt>ㄈㄨㄫㄙㄨ</rt></ruby>: a maple tree; the stand-in for the bound character 楓.
- <ruby>[橄欖](../words/橄欖.md)<rt>ㄍㄚㄇㄌㄚㄇ</rt></ruby>: an olive; the stand-in for both bound characters 橄 and 欖.
- <ruby>[檳榔](../words/檳榔.md)<rt>ㄅㄧㄋㄌㄚㄫ</rt></ruby>: the betel nut or betel palm; the stand-in for both bound characters 檳 and 榔.

### Vegetation, Generally

- <ruby>[草木](../words/草木.md)<rt>ㄑㄚㄨㄇㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>: greenery, plants, vegetation — the collective, blanket term.
- <ruby>[林](../words/林.md)<rt>ㄌㄧㄇ</rt></ruby>: a grove, woods — a stand of trees, smaller than a forest, the character 林 being 木 doubled.
- <ruby>[林業](../words/林業.md)<rt>ㄌㄧㄇ·ㄝㄆ</rt></ruby>: forestry — the industry of growing and harvesting woodland, 林 + 業 "business"; the trade built on [[林]]. Compare [[工業]] under [Trade](../lexipedia/Trade.md).
- <ruby>[枯](../words/枯.md)<rt>ㄎㄛ</rt></ruby>: withered, dried up — of a plant that has lost its moisture and life (also figuratively, of a dry or spent state).
- <ruby>[芝草](../words/芝草.md)<rt>ㄐㄧㄑㄚㄨ</rt></ruby>: grass, lawn/ground-cover vegetation — also, in Chinese, a synonym for the lingzhi mushroom (靈芝), an unrelated auspicious-plant sense carried by the same compound.
- <ruby>[秀](../words/秀.md)<rt>ㄙㄨㄛ</rt></ruby>: to bear fruit, blossom, shoot — originally of a rice plant coming into bloom; by extension "outstanding, excellent" (優秀), an unrelated figurative sense also excluded as a false match on [War](../lexipedia/War.md)'s own "shoot" (the weapon) gap.
- <ruby>[苔](../words/苔.md)<rt>ㄊㄛㄧ</rt></ruby>: moss, lichen.
- <ruby>[葛](../words/葛.md)<rt>ㄍ⼘ㄊ</rt></ruby>: kudzu — a specific climbing vine species.
- <ruby>[女蘿](../words/女蘿.md)<rt>ㄋㄜㄌㄚ</rt></ruby>: dodder (a parasitic vine); beard moss (*Usnea*, a trailing lichen).
- <ruby>[海草](../words/海草.md)<rt>ㄏㄚㄧㄑㄚㄨ</rt></ruby>: seagrass.
- <ruby>[海藻](../words/海藻.md)<rt>ㄏㄚㄧㄐㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>: seaweed — a genuine coincidence gives it the identical Japanese reading かいそう as [[海草]] above, despite the two being distinct plants with distinct characters.
- <ruby>[淡麻](../words/淡麻.md)<rt>ㄉㄚㄇㄇㄚ</rt></ruby>: nettle.

## Climate & Biomes

- <ruby>[草原](../words/草原.md)<rt>ㄑㄚㄨ⼔ㄋ</rt></ruby>: grassland, prairie, steppe, savanna, meadow — one broad word covering the whole open-grassland cluster. See [Nature](../lexipedia/Nature.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[熱帯雨林](../words/熱帯雨林.md)<rt>ㄋ⼶ㄊㄊㄚㄧㄨㄌㄧㄇ</rt></ruby>: a tropical rain forest — dense forest in hot, wet lands.
- <ruby>[苔原](../words/苔原.md)<rt>ㄊㄛㄧ⼔ㄋ</rt></ruby>: the tundra — the cold, treeless plain of moss and lichen.
- <ruby>[沙漠](../words/沙漠.md)<rt>ㄙㄚ·ㄇㄚㄎ</rt></ruby>: a desert. See [Nature](../lexipedia/Nature.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[草地](../words/草地.md)<rt>ㄑㄚㄨㄉㄧㄜ</rt></ruby>: lawn, grassland, meadow — a smaller-scale patch than [[草原]]'s large-scale plain, a distinction by size rather than by climate. See [Nature](../lexipedia/Nature.md) for its own citation there.

**How Dan'a'yo says these**: "rain forest" is <ruby>[[熱帯雨林]]<rt>ㄋ⼶ㄊㄊㄚㄧㄨㄌㄧㄇ</rt></ruby>; "taiga" is <ruby>[[針葉林]]<rt>ㄐㄧㄇ⼄ㄆㄌㄧㄇ</rt></ruby>, the conifer forest; "tundra" is <ruby>[[苔原]]<rt>ㄊㄛㄧ⼔ㄋ</rt></ruby>; "Mediterranean" (as a climate or biome) is <ruby>[[地中海]]<rt>ㄉㄧㄜㄐㄨㄫㄏㄚㄧ</rt></ruby> as a modifier; and "temperate forest" is said as a phrase, 温帯 and <ruby>[[林]]<rt>ㄌㄧㄇ</rt></ruby>, with no word of its own.

## Semantic Range Notes

**"Flower" is a bound character on purpose, and <ruby>[[草花]]<rt>ㄑㄚㄨㄏ⺢</rt></ruby> is its word.** The character 花 has no word page of its own; its `stand_in` is <ruby>[[草花]]<rt>ㄑㄚㄨㄏ⺢</rt></ruby>, "flowering plants, ornamental flowers," and it appears in compounds such as <ruby>[[花梗]]<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄍㄚㄫ</rt></ruby> (flower stalk), <ruby>[[花弁]]<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄅ⼘ㄋ</rt></ruby> (petal) and <ruby>[[花卉]]<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄏㄨㄧ</rt></ruby> (flowers and plants). This is the same pattern as other bound characters in the vault, not a gap.

**"Stick" is <ruby>[[木棒]]<rt>ㄇㄛㄎㄅㄚㄫ</rt></ruby>, a plain wooden rod**, and the two candidates that glossed it are the wrong sense: <ruby>[[杖]]<rt>ㄐㄚㄫ</rt></ruby> ("staff, stick") is a cane or walking staff, and <ruby>[[棍棒]]<rt>ㄏㄛㄋㄅㄚㄫ</rt></ruby> is a club or bludgeon, tool and weapon senses, not a fallen twig or plant stick.

**"Grain" as the living plant and "grain" as the harvested crop are genuinely different words, not an undocumented duplicate.** [[禾]] is the cereal plant itself (the 象形 radical depicting a stalk bent under its ripening head); [[穀物]] is the harvested crop as a foodstuff or commodity. The distinction is real and documented on each word's own page, not an oversight.

**"Poison" the noun and "poisonous" the adjective are now both covered**: <ruby>[[毒]]<rt>ㄉㄛㄎ</rt></ruby> is the adjective "poisonous," and <ruby>[[毒薬]]<rt>ㄉㄛㄎ⼘ㄎ</rt></ruby> is the noun "poison."
