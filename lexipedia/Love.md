# Love

Love vocabulary covers romantic and affectionate love — distinct from the family-bond and marriage-status vocabulary that lives on [[Kinship]]. This page is a new framework, seeded from a handful of already-perfected words rather than a systematic sweep of the domain yet.

## Core Vocabulary (A1–A2)

- <ruby>[愛](../words/愛.md)<rt>ㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>: love.
- <ruby>[恋](../words/恋.md)<rt>ㄌㄝㄋ</rt></ruby>: romance, romantic love.
- <ruby>[愛情](../words/愛情.md)<rt>ㄚㄧㄑㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>: love, affection, romance.
- <ruby>[恩愛](../words/恩愛.md)<rt>ㄜㄋ·ㄚㄧ</rt></ruby>: love — specifically marital or mutual love, deep affection paired with kindness.
- <ruby>[懐抱](../words/懐抱.md)<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄧㄅㄚㄨ</rt></ruby>: to cherish, embrace — holding a feeling, ambition, or idea close.

## Semantic Range Notes

Too few words are placed on this page yet to say whether Dan'a'yo's sense of romantic love diverges from the shared CJKV baseline. One flagged item to place here when it's brought over: [[Kinship]]'s own Semantic Range Notes note [[愛人]] as a genuinely interesting cross-linguistic divergence (neutral "spouse" in mainland Mandarin, "lover" in Taiwan/HK, "boyfriend/girlfriend" in Korean/Vietnamese) that was deliberately left unrouted pending this page's creation.
