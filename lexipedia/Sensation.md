# Sensation

Sensation vocabulary covers the five senses and general perception — appearing, hiding, observing, revealing — along with the specific vocabulary of hearing, sight, taste, and smell. Ignore, perceive, aware, distract, gaze, notice and salty are all covered, with the few English words Dan'a'yo says by strategy (visible, loud, stare) explained in the notes below.

## Core Vocabulary (A1–A2)

- <ruby>[見](../words/見.md)<rt>ㄍ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby>: to see.
- <ruby>[聞](../words/聞.md)<rt>ㄇㄨㄋ</rt></ruby>: to hear.
- <ruby>[感触](../words/感触.md)<rt>ㄍㄚㄇㄑㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>: to feel, sense.
- <ruby>[甘](../words/甘.md)<rt>ㄍㄚㄇ</rt></ruby>: sweet. See [Food](../lexipedia/Food.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[苦](../words/苦.md)<rt>ㄎㄛ</rt></ruby>: bitter. See [Emotions](../lexipedia/Emotions.md) for its own citation there.

## Intermediate (B1–B2)

### General Perception

- <ruby>[出現](../words/出現.md)<rt>ㄑㄨㄊㄏ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby>: to appear, emerge, be revealed — the clean general verb. See [Existence](../lexipedia/Existence.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[観察](../words/観察.md)<rt>ㄍ⺢ㄋㄑㄚㄊ</rt></ruby>: to observe, inspect. See [Mind](../lexipedia/Mind.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[感知](../words/感知.md)<rt>ㄍㄚㄇㄐㄨㄧ</rt></ruby>: to perceive, sense — taking something in through the senses.
- <ruby>[自覚](../words/自覚.md)<rt>ㄐㄧㄜㄍㄚㄎ</rt></ruby>: to be aware, realize.
- <ruby>[察覚](../words/察覚.md)<rt>ㄑㄚㄊㄍㄚㄎ</rt></ruby>: to notice, detect.
- <ruby>[分心](../words/分心.md)<rt>ㄅㄨㄋㄙㄧㄇ</rt></ruby>: to be distracted, distract.
- <ruby>[無視](../words/無視.md)<rt>ㄇㄜㄙㄧㄜ</rt></ruby>: to ignore, disregard.
- <ruby>[意識](../words/意識.md)<rt>ㄜㄙㄧㄎ</rt></ruby>: consciousness, awareness; see [Mind](../lexipedia/Mind.md).
- <ruby>[注意](../words/注意.md)<rt>ㄐㄨㄜ</rt></ruby>: attention, to pay attention; see [Mind](../lexipedia/Mind.md).
- <ruby>[参観](../words/参観.md)<rt>ㄙㄚㄇㄍ⺢ㄋ</rt></ruby>: to visit, tour, observe (a place).
- <ruby>[観測](../words/観測.md)<rt>ㄍ⺢ㄋㄑㄜㄎ</rt></ruby>: to observe, monitor — a scientific/astronomical register, distinct from [[観察]]'s general inspection.
- <ruby>[隠匿](../words/隠匿.md)<rt>ㄜㄋㄋㄧㄎ</rt></ruby>: to hide, conceal, cover up. See [Existence](../lexipedia/Existence.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[隠蔵](../words/隠蔵.md)<rt>ㄜㄋㄑㄚㄫ</rt></ruby>: to hide, conceal — a near-synonym of [[隠匿]]. See [Existence](../lexipedia/Existence.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[隠蔽](../words/隠蔽.md)<rt>ㄜㄋㄆㄝ</rt></ruby>: to hide, conceal — another near-synonym. See [Existence](../lexipedia/Existence.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[開示](../words/開示.md)<rt>ㄎㄚㄧㄍㄝ</rt></ruby>: to show, reveal, indicate. See [Existence](../lexipedia/Existence.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[顕現](../words/顕現.md)<rt>ㄏㄝㄋㄏ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby>: to show, manifest. See [Existence](../lexipedia/Existence.md) and [Trade](../lexipedia/Trade.md) for their own citations there.
- <ruby>[顕示](../words/顕示.md)<rt>ㄏㄝㄋㄍㄝ</rt></ruby>: to reveal, show, illustrate, display, demonstrate. See [Existence](../lexipedia/Existence.md) and [Trade](../lexipedia/Trade.md) for their own citations there.

**How Dan'a'yo says these**: "ignore" is <ruby>[[無視]]<rt>ㄇㄜㄙㄧㄜ</rt></ruby>; "perceive" is <ruby>[[感知]]<rt>ㄍㄚㄇㄐㄨㄧ</rt></ruby>; "aware" is <ruby>[[自覚]]<rt>ㄐㄧㄜㄍㄚㄎ</rt></ruby> (to realize) or <ruby>[[意識]]<rt>ㄜㄙㄧㄎ</rt></ruby> (consciousness); "distract" is <ruby>[[分心]]<rt>ㄅㄨㄋㄙㄧㄇ</rt></ruby>; and "notice" is <ruby>[[察覚]]<rt>ㄑㄚㄊㄍㄚㄎ</rt></ruby> or <ruby>[[注意]]<rt>ㄐㄨㄜ</rt></ruby> (attention).

**Excluded as false matches**: "appear" also loosely matches [[出演]], which means to appear on stage/perform, not the general sense; "hide" also loosely matches [[皮革]], which means leather, the animal-skin noun, not the verb; "observe" also loosely matches [[従]], which means to obey or comply (as in observing a custom), not to watch or perceive; "sense" also loosely matches [[意義]], which means abstract meaning or significance, not sensory perception; "note" (to take note of) also loosely matches [[音符]] (a musical note) and [[札]] (a written note or tag) — neither cited here, since neither means "to notice." [[記録]] ("to record, note down") is a closer but still imperfect fit, offered cautiously for the writing-down register rather than pure sensory noticing; see [Art](../lexipedia/Art.md) for its own citation there.

### Hearing

- <ruby>[音声](../words/音声.md)<rt>ㄨㄇㄙㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>: a sound. See [Speech](../lexipedia/Speech.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[丁丁](../words/丁丁.md)<rt>ㄉㄝㄫㄉㄝㄫ</rt></ruby>: clang, clash — a specific onomatopoeia for a sharp, repeated striking sound (chopping, metal clanging), not a general-purpose "noise" word.
- <ruby>[噌噌](../words/噌噌.md)<rt>ㄑㄨㄫㄑㄨㄫ</rt></ruby>: whoosh, scraping sound — an onomatopoeia for a quick sharp friction noise, like claws scrabbling up bark; the reduplicated syllable gives repeated, rapid motion; a sibling of [[丁丁]]'s clang.
- <ruby>[軣軣](../words/軣軣.md)<rt>ㄍ⼔ㄫㄍ⼔ㄫ</rt></ruby>: rumbling, booming, roaring — the reduplicated sound of something large thundering or echoing (machinery, explosions, drums, a train, fire); its standard written form is 轟轟, and Japanese ごうごう is a very common onomatopoeia.
- <ruby>[騒](../words/騒.md)<rt>ㄙㄚㄨ</rt></ruby>: boisterous, noisy commotion (騒動, "disturbance"; 騒音, "noise") — resolves this page's own previously-flagged "noise" gap; "loud" is <ruby>[[大声]]<rt>ㄉㄚㄧㄙㄧㄫ</rt></ruby>, "a loud voice."
- <ruby>[聴](../words/聴.md)<rt>ㄑㄝㄫ</rt></ruby>: to listen.
- <ruby>[反響](../words/反響.md)<rt>ㄈㄛㄋㄏ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>: an echo.
- <ruby>[沈黙](../words/沈黙.md)<rt>ㄑㄧㄇㄇㄨㄎ</rt></ruby>: silent.
- <ruby>[寂静](../words/寂静.md)<rt>ㄐㄝㄎㄐㄝㄫ</rt></ruby>: quiet.
- <ruby>[静寂](../words/静寂.md)<rt>ㄐㄝㄫㄐㄝㄎ</rt></ruby>: quiet — a near-synonym of [[寂静]].
- <ruby>[安穏](../words/安穏.md)<rt>ㄚㄋ·ㄛㄋ</rt></ruby>: quiet, peaceful, steady — leans toward emotional/physical peace. See [Emotions](../lexipedia/Emotions.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[安静](../words/安静.md)<rt>ㄚㄋㄐㄝㄫ</rt></ruby>: quiet, peaceful — a near-synonym of [[安穏]]. See [Emotions](../lexipedia/Emotions.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[耳聾](../words/耳聾.md)<rt>ㄋㄧㄌㄛㄫ</rt></ruby>: deaf.

**How Dan'a'yo says "loud"**: <ruby>[[大声]]<rt>ㄉㄚㄧㄙㄧㄫ</rt></ruby> is "a loud voice" and <ruby>[[騒]]<rt>ㄙㄚㄨ</rt></ruby> is "noisy"; the language has no separate adjective for loudness. ("Noise" is <ruby>[[騒]]<rt>ㄙㄚㄨ</rt></ruby>.)

**Excluded as a false match**: "sound" (a wholesome, correct judgment) also loosely matches [[健全]], which means sound/wholesome/healthy in the judgment sense ("sound reasoning"), not the auditory noun cited above — not cited here.

- <ruby>[風景](../words/風景.md)<rt>ㄈㄨㄫㄍ⼶ㄫ</rt></ruby>: a view — scenery, landscape. See [Locatives](../lexipedia/Locatives.md) for its own citation there.
- <ruby>[観覧](../words/観覧.md)<rt>ㄍ⺢ㄋㄌㄚㄇ</rt></ruby>: to view, observe — the verb. See [Art](../lexipedia/Art.md) for its own citation there.

**How Dan'a'yo says these**: "gaze" and "stare" are <ruby>[[凝視]]<rt>ㄜㄫㄙㄧㄜ</rt></ruby>, the fixed look; "notice" is <ruby>[[察覚]]<rt>ㄑㄚㄊㄍㄚㄎ</rt></ruby>; and "visible" is 可視, the productive mood prefix <ruby>[[可]]<rt>ㄎㄜ</rt></ruby> "can" plus <ruby>[[視]]<rt>ㄙㄧㄜ</rt></ruby> "look," segmented, not coined. "Watch" is already covered on [Tools](../lexipedia/Tools.md) by <ruby>[[看]]<rt>ㄎㄚㄋ</rt></ruby> (the verb) and <ruby>[[時計]]<rt>ㄙㄧㄍㄝㄧ</rt></ruby> (the noun, a timepiece).

**Excluded as a false match**: "image" also loosely matches [[彫像]], which means a carved statue, not a picture or visual representation. ("Look," the verb, also loosely matches [[顔色]], which means a facial expression or complexion, not the act of looking — not cited here; the real verb "to look" is [[視]], above.)

## Semantic Range Notes

**This page's "hide/conceal" cluster is unusually large**: [[隠匿]], [[隠蔵]], and [[隠蔽]] are near-synonyms with no sharp distinction recorded, all three already housed on [Existence](../lexipedia/Existence.md) for the broader act of concealment — gathered here for their perceptual angle (something hidden from the senses).

**Five false matches were caught**, the densest cluster of any page so far: [[出演]] ("appear" on stage), [[皮革]] ("hide" the leather noun), [[従]] ("observe" an obligation, not perceive), [[意義]] ("sense" as meaning, not perception), and [[健全]] ("sound" as judgment, not the auditory noun). Each is a real word, just for an unrelated sense of the polysemous English gloss.

**"Note" (to take note of) now has a match** — [[音符]] and [[札]] are both wrong senses (musical note, written tag), and even [[記録]], the closest candidate, really means "to record, write down" rather than "to notice." The closest fit is now <ruby>[[察覚]]<rt>ㄑㄚㄊㄍㄚㄎ</rt></ruby>, "to notice, detect."

**<ruby>[[景致]]<rt>ㄍ⼶ㄫㄑㄧㄜ</rt></ruby> and <ruby>[[景色]]<rt>ㄍ⼶ㄫㄙㄧㄎ</rt></ruby> are two shades of "view"**: <ruby>[[景色]]<rt>ㄍ⼶ㄫㄙㄧㄎ</rt></ruby> is the everyday "scenery, view," and <ruby>[[景致]]<rt>ㄍ⼶ㄫㄑㄧㄜ</rt></ruby> is the more literary "scene." Both are real, parallel words with no recorded sharper split.
