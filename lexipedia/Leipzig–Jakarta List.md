# Leipzig–Jakarta List

The Leipzig–Jakarta List contains 100 basic vocabulary concepts selected for stability and resistance to borrowing. The English prompts follow Tadmor’s 2009 list in published order. The Dan’a’yo forms link to their word pages and carry Bopomofo readings, as in [[Swadesh]].

The list comes from Uri Tadmor (2009), “Loanwords in the world’s languages: Findings and results,” Table 9.1. English glosses from [Concepticon’s Tadmor 2009 list](https://concepticon.clld.org/contributions/Tadmor-2009-100) ([CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)).
