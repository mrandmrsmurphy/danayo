## Literal Meaning
- [守](../characters/守.md) — guard, protect, keep watch over
- [株 (char)](../characters/株%20(char).md) — tree stump, root stock
- [待](../characters/待.md) — wait, await
- [兎 (char)](../characters/兎%20(char).md) — rabbit, hare

Literally: "Guard the stump; await the rabbit" — stand by the place where a lucky accident once happened and wait for it to happen again.

## Extended Meaning
Wiktionary gives three figurative senses for the Chinese idiom: (derogatory) "to be conservative; to resist change and innovation", "to sit around and wait", and "to ambush" (for example police waiting and hiding to catch a suspect).

## Source and Origin
**《韓非子·五蠹》** (Han Feizi, chapter "Five Vermin," c. 233 BC):

> 「宋人有耕田者，田中有株，兔走觸株，折頸而死，因釋其耒而守株，冀復得兔，兔不可復得，而身為宋國笑。今欲以先王之政，治當世之民，皆守株之類也。」
>
> "A man of Song was plowing his field. In the field was a tree stump. A rabbit came running, struck the stump, broke its neck, and died. The man then dropped his plow and kept watch by the stump, hoping to catch another rabbit. He never caught another rabbit, and became a laughingstock throughout Song. Now, those who wish to govern the people of today using the policies of the former kings — they are all of the stump-watching type."

The passage ends with its own application: those who think they can govern the people of today by the policies of the former kings are 「守株之類」, "of the stump-watching type".

## Standard Form
**守株待兎** (Dan'a'yo standard; uses 兎, the Dan'a'yo-preferred form of the rabbit character)
- 守株待兔 — form with 兔 (兎 and 兔 are orthographic variants; both accepted); aliased in this vault

## Cultural Notes
Wiktionary notes the idiom is based on this story in the 《韓非子》 and lists Korean 수주대토 and Vietnamese thủ chu đãi thố as descendants, with a Japanese equivalent 株を守る (くいぜをまもる) and a Vietnamese calque ôm cây đợi thỏ.

## Example Sentences
1. 《韓非子·五蠹》:「兔不可復得，而身為宋國笑。」

## Sentiment
Derogatory; it mocks waiting for luck to repeat or resisting change.
