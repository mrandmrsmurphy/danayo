## Literal Meaning
- [東](characters/東.md) — east
- [亜](characters/亜.md) — Asia
- [自](characters/自.md) — self, by itself
- [通 (char)](characters/通%20(char).md) — to communicate, connect

Literally: "East Asia communicates by itself."

## Extended Meaning
東亜自通 states the principle that East Asian languages and cultures possess sufficient shared structure, vocabulary, and literary heritage to enable direct mutual communication without mediation through external languages. It affirms intra-regional intelligibility and cooperation grounded in common classical traditions, rather than reliance on English or other outside linguistic frameworks. In manifesto contexts, it frames an auxiliary language as a facilitating tool for exchange, not a replacement for native or literary languages.

## Source and Origin
東亜自通 is a Dan'a'yo-coined idiom (単亜語), part of the language's founding manifesto material (see [[文法 - 01序文]]). 自通 emphasizes endogenous connectivity rather than isolation, asserting that communication arises from shared inheritance rather than enforced uniformity. The tone is pragmatic and reflective, suitable for linguistic manifestos, prefaces, or design rationales for auxiliary languages. It implicitly contrasts auxiliary communication with native language life, preserving poetry, daily usage, and cultural expression within each community while highlighting a consciously maintained common medium.

## Cultural Notes
東亜自通 is civilizational and functional in scope, addressing the CJKV region as a whole rather than any single nation. Like its companion motto [[合漢再決]], it functions as manifesto language rather than an idiom in everyday circulation — cited when explaining Dan'a'yo's rationale rather than used casually in conversation.

## Example Sentences
1. 東亜自通の理念は、単亜語の存在理由そのものである。("The principle of 東亜自通 is itself the reason Dan'a'yo exists.")
2. Rather than routing every cross-border conversation through English, the founders hoped 東亜自通 could become a living reality again.
3. 그 학자는 동아자통의 이상을 실현하기 위해 이 언어를 설계했다. ("The scholar designed this language to realize the ideal of 동아자통.")

## Sentiment
Pragmatic, reflective, aspirational. Affirms shared regional heritage as a living resource for direct communication, without displacing any community's own native language or literary tradition.
