## Literal Meaning
- [上 (char)](../characters/上%20(char).md) — above
- [下 (char)](../characters/下%20(char).md) — below
- [無 (char)](../characters/無%20(char).md) — none, without
- [偶](../characters/偶.md) — likeness, idol, image

Literally: "Above and below, no likeness."

## Extended Meaning
A concise formulation of the Second Commandment, the prohibition against making images: no likeness of anything in heaven above or the earth beneath. Dan'a'yo-internal; English Wiktionary has no entry for it.

## Source and Origin
**Exodus 20:4** and **Deuteronomy 5:8**, the Second Commandment:

> "Thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image, or any likeness of any thing that is in heaven above, or that is in the earth beneath..." (KJV)

上下無偶 condenses the commandment into four characters, with 上下 for "heaven above... earth beneath". Wiktionary glosses 偶 as "figure; statue; idol", and also "couple; pair" and, in mathematics, "even". The 和合本 (verified on Wikisource) renders Exodus 20:4 as 「不可爲自己雕刻偶像、也不可作甚麼形像、彷彿上天、下地、和地底下、水中的百物」, using 偶像 for "graven image".

## Cultural Notes
The phrase is a Dan'a'yo-internal coinage (`origin: Bible`) summarizing the Second Commandment.

## Example Sentences

## Sentiment
Prohibitive; it states the Second Commandment.
