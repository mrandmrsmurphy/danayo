## Literal Meaning
- [[神経]] — nerves; the nervous system
- [[衰弱]] — weakening, wasting, exhaustion

Literally: "Weakening of the nerves" — exhaustion or depletion of the nervous system.

## Extended Meaning
Wiktionary glosses the Chinese entry 神經衰弱 "nervous breakdown; mental breakdown". Its Japanese entry gives (psychiatry) "neurasthenia" and (cards) "concentration, pelmanism", the card game. It analyses the Japanese word as a compound of 神経 ("nerve") and 衰弱 ("breakdown").

## Source and Origin
The vault marks it as a wasei kango (`origin: 和製漢語`), a Japanese rendering of "neurasthenia". English Wiktionary lists it as a four-character compound (yojijukugo) of 神経 and 衰弱. The coiner and date are not established here.

## Standard Form
**神経衰弱** — Dan'a'yo and Japanese standard form, using shinjitai 経 (for traditional 經).
- 神經衰弱 — traditional Chinese form
- 神经衰弱 — simplified Chinese

Wiktionary notes the card-game sense (concentration, pelmanism) under the Japanese entry.
