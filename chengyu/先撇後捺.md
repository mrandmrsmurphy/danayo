[Dan'a'yo Chengyu](chengyu/Dan'a'yo%20Chengyu.md)

>[!Tip] The second line of the second couplet of the 筆順 verse, with its partner [[先横後豎]] — see the verse on [[Stroke]]

## Literal Meaning
- [先](../characters/先.md) — first, before
- [撇](../characters/撇%20(char).md) — left-falling stroke
- [後](../characters/後%20(char).md) — after
- [捺](../characters/捺%20(char).md) — right-falling stroke

Literally: "Left-falling first, right-falling after."

## Extended Meaning
When a left-falling stroke (撇) and a right-falling stroke (捺) meet or stand together, the left-falling one is written first (人, 入, 文). The rule is the second member of the pair of rules about the order of different strokes, with [[先横後豎]], and is a special case of the left-to-right default of [[左由右至]].

## Source and Origin
Another 先X後Y phrase, built on the stroke names 撇 and 捺, which are words in Dan'a'yo (撇 and 捺). It is the second line of the second couplet of the 筆順 verse on [[Stroke]]. Coined within 単亜語 on 2026-10-03.

## Standard Form
**先撇後捺**

All five readings are compositional: each is the concatenation of the constituent characters' own readings, since the phrase is a coinage and not an attested idiom.

## Cultural Notes
Like the other idioms of [[Dan'a'yo Chengyu|単亜語 origin]], this phrase is vault-internal terminology rather than a phrase with independent life across the CJKV sphere. The rule it names, however, is not Dan'a'yo's own: it belongs to the common stroke-order tradition of the whole region. The same rule is stated in Chinese stroke-order instruction as 先撇后捺, in Japanese as 「左払いが先」, and in Korean as 삐침 먼저, 파임 나중.

## Example Sentences
1. 「人」は先撇後捺で、左払いを先に書く。
2. In 文 the 撇 comes before the 捺: 先撇後捺.

## Sentiment
Neutral and instructional; a mnemonic in the register of a school rule, intended to be recited and remembered.
