## Literal Meaning
- [一](../characters/一%20(char).md) — one
- [目](../characters/目%20(char).md) — eye, glance
- [瞭](../characters/瞭.md) — clear, distinct
- [然](../characters/然%20(char).md) — (adjectival/adverbial suffix, "-ly, appearing as")

Literally: "clear at a single glance."

## Extended Meaning
Immediately obvious; easily understood; perfectly clear without need for further explanation. Often used to describe arguments, charts, organization, logic, or visible facts — anything whose structure or meaning is grasped the instant it's seen.

## Source and Origin
The word **瞭然** itself is old, appearing in Buddhist Chinese translations and Tang prose meaning "clear, bright, transparent":

- **《世說新語·言語》** (compiled 5th c.):
  > "王夷甫神識清澄，**瞭然**自得。"
  > (Wang Yifu's mind was lucid, clearly at ease.)
- **《朱子語類》** (Song dynasty, Zhu Xi):
  > "聖賢之言，昭然瞭然，無一毫隱晦。"
  > (The words of the sages are radiant and clear, without the slightest obscurity.)

The compact four-character form **一目瞭然** itself seems to crystallize later, in Ming–Qing vernacular literature:

- **《醒世恆言》卷二十三** (Ming): "這事一目瞭然，何必多言。"
  ("This matter is clear at a glance, no need for many words.")

So it is a later crystallized idiom, built on much older classical vocabulary.

## Standard Form
**一目瞭然**
- 一目了然 — common simplified form (瞭 → 了); dominant in Mainland China
- Both forms are current; Taiwan and Japan tend to keep 瞭.

## Pronunciations
- **Mandarin (Pinyin):** yī mù liǎo rán
- **Cantonese (Jyutping):** jat1 muk6 liu5 jin4
- **Japanese (on-yomi, hiragana):** いちもくりょうぜん
- **Korean (Sino-Korean):** 일목요연 (il-mok-yo-yeon)
- **Vietnamese (Hán–Việt):** nhất mục liễu nhiên

## Cultural Notes
- Common in academic, administrative, and explanatory contexts.
- Frequently used to praise clear writing, clean design, or logical structure.
- In Korean, the Sino-Korean reading 일목요연 is very common and often used in formal writing.
- In modern Chinese, the simplified character 了 has largely replaced 瞭 in everyday usage — see Standard Form above.

## Example Sentences
1. 其理甚明，一目瞭然。("The principle is entirely clear, obvious at a glance.")
2. The diagram makes the whole process *yī mù liǎo rán*.
3. His argument was structured so clearly that the conclusion was obvious at a glance.

## Sentiment
Strongly positive. Conveys clarity, order, and intellectual precision.
