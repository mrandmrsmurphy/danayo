## Literal Meaning
- [断](../characters/断.md) — cut off, sever
- [章 (char)](../characters/章%20(char).md) — chapter, passage, section of a text
- [取](../characters/取.md) — take, select, extract
- [義](../characters/義.md) — meaning, significance, the sense of a passage

Literally: "Cut the chapter; take the meaning" — sever a passage from its surrounding text and use it to express something the original did not intend.

## Extended Meaning
Wiktionary glosses the Chinese idiom "to take something out of context", and the Japanese 断章取義 "quoting a passage and interpreting it without regard to its context; take out of context".

## Source and Origin
**《左傳·襄公二十八年》** (Zuo Zhuan, 28th year of Duke Xiang, 545 BC):

In the state of Qi, Lu Pugui (盧蒲癸) married the daughter of the powerful minister Qing She (慶舍). Both families bore the surname 姜, and marrying within one's own surname was forbidden. Qing She's retainers pressed him on it, and he answered:

> 慶舍之士謂盧蒲癸曰：「男女辨姓，子不辟宗，何也？」曰：「宗不余辟，余獨焉辟之？賦詩斷章，余取所求焉，惡識宗？」
> "Qing She's retainers said to Lu Pugui: 'Men and women distinguish surnames; you do not avoid your own clan. Why?' He said: 'The clan has not avoided me; why should I alone avoid it? In reciting the Odes one cuts the stanza and takes what one seeks; what do I know of clans?'"

In the passage Lu Pugui answers a question about marrying within his own clan; 賦詩斷章 ("in reciting the Odes one cuts the stanza") is his justification. The passage is the same in several Siku Quanshu texts on Wikisource (for example 左傳紀事本末 juan 21 and 左傳杜林合注 juan 32). The four-character form 断章取義 is a later condensation of this line.

## Standard Form
**断章取義** (Dan'a'yo standard; uses 断)
- 斷章取義 — traditional Chinese form; aliased in this vault
- 断章取义 — simplified Chinese form (義 → 义); aliased in this vault

## Cultural Notes
English Wiktionary has entries for 斷章取義 (Chinese, with Japanese and Korean descendants) and 断章取義 (Japanese). It lists Japanese だんしょうしゅぎ, Korean 단장취의 and Vietnamese đoạn chương thủ nghĩa as descendants, and a Japanese calque 章を断ち義を取る.

## Example Sentences
1. 《左傳·襄公二十八年》：「賦詩斷章，余取所求焉。」
   "In reciting poetry, I cut the chapter; I take from it what I seek."
## Sentiment
Accusatory; it charges that a passage has been quoted without its context.
