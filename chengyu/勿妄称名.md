## Literal Meaning
- [勿 (char)](../characters/勿%20(char).md) — do not
- [妄](../characters/妄.md) — vainly, falsely, recklessly
- [称](../characters/称.md) — to utter, to invoke, to call
- [名 (char)](../characters/名%20(char).md) — name

Literally: "do not vainly invoke the Name."

## Extended Meaning
A concise formulation of the commandment prohibiting misuse of the divine name. It forbids false oaths, irreverent invocation, manipulative religious speech, and casual profanity directed toward God.

## Source and Origin
This idiom condenses the Third Commandment, **Exodus 20:7** and **Deuteronomy 5:11**:

> "Thou shalt not take the name of the LORD thy God in vain; for the LORD will not hold him guiltless that taketh his name in vain." (KJV)

Traditional Chinese Bible translations often render this as "不可妄稱耶和華你神的名" ("You shall not vainly invoke the name of the LORD your God"). The Dan'a'yo form replaces 不可 with the more compact classical negative 勿 for four-character economy, and omits explicit reference to the LORD, since 名 in this context is understood to refer to the divine Name specifically.

## Cultural Notes
In biblical thought, a "name" is not a mere label but represents presence, authority, and character — to misuse the Name is to misrepresent God himself. Historically, Jewish tradition developed reverential practices around the Tetragrammaton, avoiding its pronunciation altogether. Christian theology broadened the commandment to include false or manipulative religious speech, hypocritical appeals to God's authority, casual profanity, and oaths made lightly or deceitfully. The verb 妄 highlights emptiness and falsehood; 称 emphasizes vocal invocation rather than abstract thought, underscoring speech itself as a covenantal act. Within the Dan'a'yo Christian sphere, this chengyu functions both as legal command and ethical warning, reflecting a high theology of divine holiness and the power of words.

## Example Sentences
1. 人若為私利而誓，乃犯勿妄称名之戒。("If a person swears for selfish gain, he violates the command not to invoke the Name in vain.")
2. 「不可妄稱耶和華你神的名，因為妄稱耶和華名的，耶和華必不以他為無罪。」(出埃及記 20:7，和合本 Chinese Union Version)
3. Teachers cite 勿妄称名 when explaining why casual oaths invoking God's name are treated so seriously.
4. 勿妄称名之誡，教人敬畏神之聖名。("The commandment 'do not take the Name in vain' teaches reverence for God's holy name.")

## Sentiment
Solemn, reverent, prohibitive. Carries the gravity of covenant law and the sanctity of the divine Name.
