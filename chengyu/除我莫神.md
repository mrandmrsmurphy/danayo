[Biblical Chengyu](chengyu/Biblical%20Chengyu.md)

## Literal Meaning
- [除](../characters/除.md) — except, apart from
- [我 (char)](../characters/我%20(char).md) — I, me
- [莫 (char)](../characters/莫%20(char).md) — none, no one, nothing
- [神 (char)](../characters/神%20(char).md) — god, deity

Literally: "except me, none is God."

## Extended Meaning
A concise declaration of exclusive monotheism. It affirms that no other being shares divine status, authority, or essence, excluding both rival worship (a devotional claim) and rival ontology (a metaphysical claim about what actually exists as divine).

## Source and Origin
This maxim condenses several biblical affirmations:

> "Thou shalt have no other gods before me." (Exodus 20:3, KJV; paralleled in Deuteronomy 5:7)

> "I am the LORD, and there is none else, there is no God beside me..." (Isaiah 45:5, KJV)

Traditional Chinese Bible translations often render Isaiah 45:5 as "除我以外，再沒有神" ("besides me, there is no other God"). The Dan'a'yo form replaces 無 with the more classically solemn 莫 to avoid modern lexical associations with "atheism," while retaining full existential exclusivity.

## Cultural Notes
In the ancient Near Eastern world, deities were commonly understood as territorial or functional; Israel's confession was distinctive in denying not merely the legitimacy but the very reality of rival gods. The verb-like force of 莫 sharpens the proclamation — it is not merely that no others are to be worshiped, but that none qualifies as God at all — so the maxim bridges covenant loyalty and metaphysical uniqueness in a single stroke. Historically, such declarations led surrounding cultures to accuse Jews and Christians of impiety or even atheism toward the civic gods, though the biblical claim is properly a denial of competitors rather than a denial of deity as such. Within the Dan'a'yo Christian sphere, 除我莫神 functions as a foundational confession, pairing naturally with [[主宰万物]] as a theological axis around which law, worship, and redemption turn.

## Example Sentences
1. 先知之宣告曰：除我莫神，萬邦偶像皆虚。("The prophet proclaims: except me none is God; the idols of the nations are vain.")
2. 「我是耶和華，再沒有別神；除了我以外再沒有神。」(以賽亞書 45:5，和合本 Chinese Union Version)
3. The First Commandment and 除我莫神 both stand as bedrock confessions of biblical monotheism.
4. 除我莫神之言，斷絕一切偶像崇拜之根。("The declaration 'except me, none is God' cuts off every root of idol worship.")

## Sentiment
Absolute, prophetic, uncompromising. Carries the force of divine self-declaration and exclusive sovereignty.
