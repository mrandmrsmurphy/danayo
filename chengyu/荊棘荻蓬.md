## Literal Meaning
- [荊](characters/荊.md) — thorn bush, bramble
- [棘](characters/棘.md) — thorn
- [荻](characters/荻.md) — reed grass
- [蓬](characters/蓬.md) — tumbleweed, coarse weed

Literally: "Brambles, thorns, reeds, and weeds."

## Extended Meaning
荊棘荻蓬 captures God's curse upon the ground after the Fall: agriculture becomes painful toil rather than effortless provision, symbolizing the frustration and futility that entered the world through sin. Human labor now struggles against a creation that resists and wounds. The imagery extends metaphorically to describe any difficult, painful, or cursed situation, invoked in reflections on toil, hardship, and the consequences of sin and judgment.

## Source and Origin
**Genesis 3:18**, part of God's curse on the ground after Adam's sin:

> "Thorns and thistles it shall bring forth for you; and you shall eat the plants of the field." (Gen 3:18, ESV)

荊棘 ("thorns/brambles") and 荻蓬 ("reeds/weeds") together render the verse's hostile, unproductive vegetation — plants that resist cultivation and wound the one who works the land. In Christian theology, Christ's crown of thorns (Matthew 27:29) is read as reversing this curse, as he bears its symbol upon himself.

## Variants
- 地生荊棘荻蓬 — a fuller rendering making the earth the explicit subject ("the earth produces thorns and thistles")
- 棘蓬之咒 — emphasizing judgment ("the curse of thorns and thistles")
- 荊蓬満地 — emphasizing abundance ("thorns and thistles fill the earth")

## Cultural Notes
荊棘荻蓬 functions across the CJKV Christian sphere as a direct scriptural citation for the curse of toil and hardship following the Fall, invoked in sermons and theological writing on sin's consequences and the burden of labor. Korean usage in particular draws on the pre-existing native idiom 형극 (from 荊棘 alone) meaning "a path of thorns" or severe hardship, giving the fuller Biblical compound an extra layer of native resonance.

## Example Sentences
1. 「地必給你長出荊棘和蒺藜來，你也要吃田間的菜蔬。」(Gen 3:18, 和合本 Chinese Union Version)
2. 人因罪受咒，地生荊棘荻蓬。("Humans because of sin received the curse; the earth produces thorns and thistles.")
3. 勞苦之人如耕荊棘荻蓬之地，終日辛苦。("Those who toil are like those plowing ground of thorns and thistles, laboring painfully all day.")
4. Every year the farm demanded more of him for less in return — 荊棘荻蓬, as if the ground itself were fighting back.

## Sentiment
Cursed, toilsome, frustrating. Used to describe difficult labor, cursed conditions, fallen creation, or the painful consequences of sin and judgment.
