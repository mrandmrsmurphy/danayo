## Literal Meaning
- [一](../characters/一%20(char).md) — one
- [帆](../characters/帆%20(char).md) — sail
- [風](../characters/風%20(char).md) — wind
- [順](../characters/順.md) — favorable, following

Literally: "one sail with a favorable wind." The image is of a boat whose sail is perfectly aligned with a following wind, allowing smooth and effortless travel.

## Extended Meaning
A situation proceeding smoothly and without obstacles — used for careers, studies, business ventures, journeys, or life in general. It implies ease, momentum, and the absence of resistance, close to the English "smooth sailing."

## Source and Origin
The metaphor derives from maritime experience, where a favorable wind ensured swift and safe travel. The fixed four-character form became common in late imperial vernacular literature (Ming period) and spread as a conventional auspicious expression across the region. 帆's own reading is sometimes given as *fán* rather than *fān* in this idiom (yīfánfēngshùn), reflecting a colloquial variant tone rather than a different character.

## Standard Form
**一帆風順** — no widely used variant *forms* (the graphs are stable across Traditional and Simplified Chinese, and identical in Japanese, Korean Hanja, and Vietnamese contexts), but Japanese also uses the semantically identical, word-order-reversed 順風満帆/順風滿帆 ("favorable wind, full sail") as a fully current alternative idiom rather than a mere orthographic variant.

## Pronunciations
- **Mandarin (Pinyin):** yī fān fēng shùn

## Cultural Notes
- Frequently used as a blessing or congratulatory phrase, especially at New Year, business openings, departures, or before major undertakings.
- Common in red banners, festive couplets, and formal greetings.
- Though nautical in origin, the expression is now fully abstract and does not evoke maritime imagery for most speakers.
- Slightly formulaic in tone, but still widely used in both speech and writing.

## Example Sentences
1. 祝你一帆風順，萬事如意。("May your journey be smooth sailing, and may everything go as you wish.")
2. His career has been one of steady success, almost *yī fān fēng shùn*.
3. Life is not always *yī fān fēng shùn*; obstacles are inevitable.

## Sentiment
Strongly positive. Auspicious, encouraging, and often ceremonial in tone.
