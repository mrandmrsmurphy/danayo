## Literal Meaning
- [国](../characters/国.md) — nation
- [士](../characters/士%20(char).md) — scholar, gentleman
- [無](../characters/無%20(char).md) — lack, without
- [双](../characters/双%20(char).md) — pair, match

Literally: "A nation's scholar without a match."

## Extended Meaning
Wiktionary glosses the Chinese idiom "distinguished person of talent in the country that is matchless". Its Japanese entry gives "a distinguished person" and a mahjong sense: a 役満 hand of all thirteen terminals and honours plus one more of them (kokushi musō, "thirteen orphans").

## Source and Origin
The idiom comes from the **《史記·淮陰侯列傳》** (Records of the Grand Historian, biography of Han Xin, Marquis of Huaiyin; juan 92).

Han Xin, serving Liu Bang (the future Han Gaozu) in Hanzhong, left in disappointment at not being used, and the chancellor Xiao He (蕭何) chased after him without waiting to report. When Xiao He returned, Liu Bang, half angry and half glad, scolded him for chasing one man when dozens of generals had deserted. Xiao He answered:

> 諸將易得耳。至如信者，國士無雙。王必欲長王漢中，無所事信；必欲爭天下，非信無所與計事者。顧王策安所決耳。
> "Generals are easy to find. But a man like Xin is a 國士 without a peer in the whole country. If Your Majesty means to rule Hanzhong forever, you have no use for Xin; if you mean to contend for the empire, there is no one but Xin with whom to plan. It rests on which course Your Majesty decides."

Liu Bang then made Han Xin his commander-in-chief, and Han Xin went on to conquer much of the empire for him. The passage is repeated in the 《資治通鑑》 (juan 9) and in the Tang commentary to the 《漢書》. The idiom crystallized from Xiao He's words: 國士 means a man of the whole state, the best of the realm, and 無雙 ("without a pair") makes him unique.

## Standard Form
**國士無雙** (traditional) / **国士无双** (simplified) — both forms current.

## Cultural Notes
English Wiktionary has a Chinese entry (simplified 国士无双) and a Japanese entry (国士無双), and glosses 国士 "patriot; respectable person" and 無双 "matchless; unparalleled". It derives the Japanese entry from the 《史記》. It has no Korean or Vietnamese entry.

## Example Sentences
1. 至如信者，國士無雙。（《史記·淮陰侯列傳》）

## Sentiment
Admiring; it praises a person with no equal.
