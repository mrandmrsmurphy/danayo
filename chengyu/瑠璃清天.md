## Literal Meaning
- [瑠](characters/瑠.md) — lapis lazuli
- [璃 (char)](characters/璃%20(char).md) — glass, glaze
- [清](characters/清.md) — clear
- [天 (char)](characters/天%20(char).md) — heaven, sky

Literally: "Lapis lazuli, clear heaven."

## Extended Meaning
瑠璃清天 evokes a vision of divine majesty: a brilliant blue, gemlike surface as clear and vast as the sky itself. It is used to describe divine glory, heavenly beauty, sacred visions, or the overwhelming majesty of God's presence — mystical and awe-inspiring rather than merely decorative or descriptive of an ordinary gemstone.

## Source and Origin
**Exodus 24:10**, describing the theophany on Mount Sinai, where Moses and the elders of Israel saw God and survived:

> "And they saw the God of Israel. There was under his feet as it were a pavement of sapphire stone, like the very heaven for clearness." (Ex 24:10, ESV)

瑠璃 ("lapis lazuli," the established Japanese reading of this compound, るり) evokes the brilliant blue stone, while 清天 ("clear heaven") captures the extraordinary clarity "like the very heaven" of the original text. The sapphire pavement beneath God's feet suggests both transcendent beauty and the firmament of heaven itself.

## Variants
- 蒼玉清天 — emphasizing the stone with a different gem name ("sapphire, clear heaven")
- 瑠璃舗天 — emphasizing pavement ("lapis-paved heaven")
- 足下瑠璃如天 — a fuller rendering ("beneath his feet, lapis like heaven")

## Cultural Notes
瑠璃清天 functions across the CJKV Christian sphere as a direct reference to this rare and mysterious Sinai theophany, invoked in sermons and religious writing to describe visions of divine glory or moments of transcendent encounter with the sacred. 瑠璃 itself (as "glass" or "lapis lazuli") carries independent aesthetic associations in Buddhist art and East Asian material culture more broadly, giving the idiom an extra layer of resonance for readers already familiar with 瑠璃 as a treasured, luminous material.

4. The stained glass caught the afternoon light and for a moment the whole nave seemed 瑠璃清天 — luminous, blue, impossibly clear.
