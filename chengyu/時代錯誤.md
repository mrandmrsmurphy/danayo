[Misc. Chengyu](chengyu/Misc.%20Chengyu.md)

## Literal Meaning
- [[時代]] — era, age, epoch
- [[錯誤]] — error, mistake

Literally: "An error of the age" — a mistake of time-period assignment; something placed or understood in the wrong era.

## Extended Meaning
Anachronism (Wiktionary's gloss for both the Chinese noun and the Japanese noun).

## Source and Origin
Wiktionary's Japanese entry says 時代錯誤 is a compound of 時代 ("age, era, period") and 錯誤 ("error, mistake") that appeared in the 1918 dictionary 《新らしい言葉の字引》 (*Atarashii Kotoba no Jibiki*) as a translation (calque) of English *anachronism*. Its Chinese entry lists 時代錯置 as a synonym. The coiner is not established.

## Standard Form
**時代錯誤** — Dan'a'yo and Japanese standard form.
- 时代错误 — simplified Chinese
- 시대착오 — Korean Sino-Korean rendering; note that 착오 (*chak'o*) is the Korean phonological adaptation of 錯誤, distinct from both Mandarin *cuòwù* and Japanese *sakugo*

## Pronunciations
- **Mandarin (Pinyin):** shí dài cuò wù
- **Cantonese (Jyutping):** si4 doi6 co3 ng6
- **Japanese (on-yomi, hiragana):** じだいさくご
- **Korean (Sino-Korean):** 시대착오 (si-dae-chak-o)

## Cultural Notes
English Wiktionary has a Chinese entry (simplified 时代错误) and a Japanese entry (時代錯誤), both glossed "anachronism". It has no Korean or Vietnamese entry.

## Example Sentences
1. 時代錯誤: "anachronism", from 時代 (era) and 錯誤 (error).

## Sentiment
Mildly critical; it names something out of place in its time.
