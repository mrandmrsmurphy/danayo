[Dan'a'yo Chengyu](chengyu/Dan'a'yo%20Chengyu.md)

>[!Tip] The first line of the 筆順 verse, with its partner [[左由右至]] — see the verse on [[Stroke]]

## Literal Meaning
- [上](../characters/上%20(char).md) — up, above
- [由](../characters/由%20(char).md) — from
- [下](../characters/下%20(char).md) — down, below
- [至](../characters/至%20(char).md) — until, to

Literally: "From the top, until the bottom."

## Extended Meaning
The first and most general rule of 筆順: a character is written from its top toward its bottom, and the same holds within each component and each part of a stroke. Where two parts of a character are stacked, the upper is written first (三, 昌, 志), and the strokes of a single column are likewise taken in descending order. The rule is the vertical counterpart of [[左由右至]], and the two together fix the default direction of writing; every other rule in the verse is an exception or refinement of this pair.

## Source and Origin
Built from the two directional postpositions 由 ("from") and 至 ("until") of the adverbial system described in the grammar, each following its noun: 上由 ("from the top") and 下至 ("until the bottom"). The result is a four-character phrase that is a complete grammatical sentence in miniature and, with [[左由右至]], opens the 筆順 verse on [[Stroke]]. Coined within 単亜語 on 2026-10-03.

## Pronunciations
- **Mandarin (Pinyin):** shàng yóu xià zhì
- **Cantonese (Jyutping):** soeng6 jau4 haa6 zi3
- **Japanese (on-yomi):** じょうゆうかし
- **Korean (Sino-Korean):** 상유하지
- **Vietnamese (Hán–Việt):** thượng do hạ chí

All five readings are compositional: each is the concatenation of the constituent characters' own readings, since the phrase is a coinage and not an attested idiom.

## Cultural Notes
Like the other idioms of [[Dan'a'yo Chengyu|単亜語 origin]], this phrase is vault-internal terminology rather than a phrase with independent life across the CJKV sphere. The rule it names, however, is not Dan'a'yo's own: it belongs to the common stroke-order tradition of the whole region. The same rule is stated in Chinese stroke-order instruction as 从上到下 ("from top to bottom"), in Japanese as 「上から下へ」, and in Korean as 위에서 아래로.

## Example Sentences
1. 「三」は上由下至の順で、一画目から三画目へ書く。
2. The three horizontals of 三 are written 上由下至: the top bar first, the bottom bar last.

## Sentiment
Neutral and instructional; a mnemonic in the register of a school rule, intended to be recited and remembered.
