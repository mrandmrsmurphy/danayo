## Literal Meaning
- [乳](characters/乳.md) — milk
- [蜜](characters/蜜.md) — honey
- [流](characters/流.md) — to flow
- [地 (char)](characters/地%20(char).md) — land

Literally: "Milk, honey flow, land."

## Extended Meaning
乳蜜流地 describes a place of extraordinary abundance, fertility, and divine provision — not merely sufficient but overflowing. It became the archetypal description of the Promised Land and, by extension, any place of divinely-granted blessing and rest; in Christian theology it also prefigures the heavenly kingdom as the ultimate promised land of God's people. Used hopefully and promissorily, invoked to describe fulfilled covenant promise, abundant prosperity, or an idealized destination.

## Source and Origin
The phrase recurs throughout the Torah — **Exodus 3:8, 17; 13:5; 33:3**, **Leviticus 20:24**, **Numbers 13:27**, **Deuteronomy 6:3**, and many others:

> "And I have come down to deliver them out of the hand of the Egyptians and to bring them up out of that land to a good and broad land, a land flowing with milk and honey." (Ex 3:8, ESV)

流 ("flowing") emphasizes overflowing prosperity rather than mere sufficiency. Milk represents pastoral wealth (livestock), while honey represents agricultural sweetness and natural abundance — together the two stand for a complete, self-sustaining plenty.

## Variants
- 流乳流蜜之地 — a fuller rendering making both verbs explicit ("a land where milk flows and honey flows")
- 乳蜜之郷 — emphasizing the destination as homeland ("a milk-and-honey homeland")
- 地流乳蜜 — an alternate word order with the same sense

## Cultural Notes
乳蜜流地 functions across the CJKV Christian sphere as the standard citation for the Promised Land and, by extension, any place of ideal abundance and blessing. Chinese readers may also recognize a structurally comparable — but historically unrelated — native idiom, 魚米之鄉 ("land of fish and rice"), used of naturally fertile regions such as Jiangnan; the two idioms share the same rhetorical shape (place-name built from characteristic abundant produce) without being variants of one another, since 魚米之鄉 has an entirely separate classical Chinese origin and no Biblical connection.

## Example Sentences
1. 「我下來是要救他們脫離埃及人的手，領他們出了那地，到美好、寬闊、流奶與蜜之地。」(Ex 3:8, 和合本 Chinese Union Version)
2. 神許以色列民乳蜜流地，示其豐饒祝福。("God promised the Israelites a land flowing with milk and honey, showing abundant blessing.")
3. 信徒所望天國如乳蜜流地，永遠安息之所。("The heavenly kingdom believers hope for is like a land flowing with milk and honey, a place of eternal rest.")
4. To the refugees, even a small plot of farmland felt like 乳蜜流地 after the famine they had fled.

## Sentiment
Hopeful, promissory, abundant. Used to describe promised blessing, divine provision, abundant prosperity, or the fulfillment of God's covenant promises.
