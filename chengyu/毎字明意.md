>[!Tip] For a usage of this idiom, see [[文法 - 03文字法]].

## Literal Meaning
- [毎 (char)](characters/毎%20(char).md) — each, every
- [字 (char)](characters/字%20(char).md) — character
- [明 (char)](characters/明%20(char).md) — clear, bright
- [意](characters/意.md) — meaning, idea

Literally: "Each character has a clear meaning."

## Extended Meaning
毎字明意 states the principle of semantic determinacy: every character in Dan'a'yo is assigned a single core meaning. Additional senses may arise through transparent metaphorical extension, but true semantic branching is kept distinct from figurative usage. This separation allows productive and intelligible word formation while avoiding uncontrolled polysemy.

## Source and Origin
毎字明意 is a Dan'a'yo-coined idiom (単亜語), stating one of the language's core design principles for character semantics (see [[文法 - 03文字法]]). 明 emphasizes clarity and inspectability rather than reductionism — the goal is not to minimize a character's meaning to a single narrow sense, but to keep its core meaning transparent and traceable even as figurative extensions accumulate. The idiom affirms continuity with the regional tradition of semantic derivation, where metaphor generates new expressions without erasing the underlying conceptual core.

## Cultural Notes
毎字明意 is semantic and methodological in scope, functioning as a design-principle motto rather than an idiom used in ordinary conversation — comparable in role to its companion mottos [[合漢再決]], [[東亜自通]], and [[覧昭和決]]. It is cited specifically to explain or justify Dan'a'yo's approach to lexical semantics, distinguishing the language's discipline from natural languages that freely accumulate unrelated senses on a single character or word.

## Example Sentences
1. 単亜語の語彙設計は毎字明意の原則に基づいている。("Dan'a'yo's vocabulary design is based on the principle of 毎字明意.")
2. When a learner asked why a character never seemed to mean two unrelated things, the answer was simply 毎字明意.
3. 이 언어의 어휘 체계는 매자명의의 원칙을 따른다. ("This language's vocabulary system follows the principle of 매자명의.")

## Sentiment
Methodical, disciplined, clarifying. Affirms transparency and traceability of meaning as a design virtue, rather than treating polysemy as an unavoidable cost of natural language.
