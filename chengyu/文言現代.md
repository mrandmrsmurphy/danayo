[Dan'a'yo Chengyu](chengyu/Dan'a'yo%20Chengyu.md)
>[!Tip] This is the guiding principle for [[文法 - 04句法]].

## Literal Meaning
- [文](characters/文.md) — classical writing, literary language
- [言 (char)](characters/言%20(char).md) — language, speech
- [現 (char)](characters/現%20(char).md) — present, current
- [代](characters/代.md) — era, generation

Literally: "Classical language, present-day era."

## Extended Meaning
文言現代 states the principle that classical written forms and modern communicative needs are not opposites but complementary layers of a single linguistic continuum. It affirms that a language may retain the concision, abstraction, and cultural density of classical style while functioning as a practical medium for present-day communication. In Dan'a'yo discourse, it names an approach that modernizes usage without vernacularizing structure — rejecting the binary between archaic preservation and full vernacular replacement in favor of a layered continuity.

## Source and Origin
文言現代 is a Dan'a'yo-coined idiom (単亜語), serving as the guiding principle for the language's syntax (see [[文法 - 04句法]]). It holds two named sub-principles in balance:
1. [[文言継承]] ("continuity with the classical written standard") — preserving topic–comment structure and concision.
2. [[現代適応]] ("adaptation to modern speech") — requiring verbs in every clause and maintaining SVO order.

Together these two principles let 単亜語 read with the density of classical written Chinese while still functioning as an ordinary, learnable modern spoken/written medium — the idiom names the synthesis, not either extreme alone.

## Cultural Notes
文言現代 is methodological and synthetic in scope, functioning as a design-principle motto alongside [[合漢再決]], [[東亜自通]], [[覧昭和決]], [[毎字明意]], and [[朝鮮正音]] rather than as an idiom in everyday circulation. It is cited specifically when explaining why Dan'a'yo's grammar looks classical on the page while behaving like a modern language in use.

## Example Sentences
1. 単亜語の文法は文言現代の理念を反映している。("Dan'a'yo's grammar reflects the principle of 文言現代.")
2. The language reads like classical prose at a glance, but 文言現代 means every sentence still resolves into an ordinary modern clause.
3. 이 언어의 통사론은 문언현대의 원칙을 따른다. ("This language's syntax follows the principle of 문언현대.")

## Sentiment
Methodical, synthetic, balanced. Rejects a false choice between classical form and modern function, proposing instead a single continuity that honors both.
