## Literal Meaning
- [銀 (char)](../characters/銀%20(char).md) — silver
- [盤 (char)](../characters/盤%20(char).md) — platter, tray
- [呈 (char)](../characters/呈%20(char).md) — to present, submit
- [首](../characters/首.md) — head

Literally: "a head presented on a silver platter."

## Extended Meaning
A vivid reference to the martyrdom of John the Baptist. By extension, it can signify unjust execution at the whim of power, especially when truth-telling provokes hostility. In broader metaphorical usage it may denote delivering something decisively into another's hands, though its primary force remains grim and judicial.

## Source and Origin
The idiom condenses the account found in **Matthew 14:6–11** and **Mark 6:21–28**:

> "And brought his head in a charger, and gave it to the damsel: and the damsel gave it to her mother." (Mark 6:28, KJV)

The underlying Greek ἐπὶ πίνακι means "on a platter/dish." The Chinese Union Version (和合本, checked against the text) renders Mark 6:28 as 「把頭放在盤子裡，拿來給女子，女子就給她母親。」 ("placed the head on a plate and brought it to the girl, and the girl gave it to her mother"); the Greek word is rendered 盤子 there, not 銀盤. The Dan'a'yo form (a coinage; 銀 appears in no Bible text) sharpens the metallic detail with 銀盤 ("silver platter"), reflecting the banquet setting and courtly excess implied by the narrative's context of a royal feast.

## Cultural Notes
John's death occurs within a royal banquet scene marked by oath, pride, and spectacle; the presentation of the head on a platter turns a prophetic life into a courtly display, and the contrast between prophetic austerity and decadent power heightens the moral drama. In Christian theology John is the forerunner not only in preaching but in suffering — his martyrdom anticipates that of Jesus, and the episode warns of political volatility and the cost of moral rebuke. The use of 呈 emphasizes formal, almost ceremonial presentation, while 銀盤 evokes wealth and luxury, intensifying the grotesque contrast between refinement and violence. Within the Dan'a'yo Christian sphere, 銀盤呈首 functions as a somber emblem of prophetic martyrdom and the peril of confronting corrupt authority.

## Example Sentences
1. 忠言逆耳，終至銀盤呈首，然真理未滅。("Faithful speech offended power and ended in a head upon a silver platter, yet the truth did not perish.")
2. 「把頭放在盤子裡，拿來給女子，女子就給她母親。」(馬可福音 6:28，和合本 Chinese Union Version)
3. John the Baptist's fate, 銀盤呈首, prefigures the cost his cousin Jesus would later pay.
4. 先知之命運，常見銀盤呈首之悲劇。("The fate of the prophets often ends in the tragedy of a head upon a silver platter.")

## Sentiment
Grim, tragic, morally charged. Evokes injustice, prophetic courage, and the cost of truth.
