## Literal Meaning
- [事 (char)](characters/事%20(char).md) — thing, matter
- [事 (char)](characters/事%20(char).md) — thing, matter
- [皆 (char)](characters/皆%20(char).md) — all, every
- [旧 (char)](characters/旧%20(char).md) — old

Literally: "Every single thing is old."

## Extended Meaning
事事皆旧 compresses Qohelet's philosophical observation about the cyclical, repetitive nature of human existence. The doubled 事事 ("thing-thing," i.e. everything) emphasizes universality, while 皆旧 ("all-old") captures the absence of genuine novelty. It expresses the pessimistic wisdom of Ecclesiastes: human efforts, achievements, and experiences merely repeat patterns established long ago. Used to express disillusionment with claims of innovation, to emphasize the repetitive nature of history, or to convey existential weariness with life's monotony.

## Source and Origin
**Ecclesiastes 1:9**:

> "What has been is what will be, and what has been done is what will be done, and there is nothing new under the sun." (Eccl 1:9, ESV)

The sentiment resonates with both Biblical and East Asian cyclical views of history, contrasting with progressive or linear narratives of human advancement.

## Variants
- 日下無新事 — a more literal rendering of the verse's own image ("under the sun, nothing new")
- 天下無新事 — an alternate substituting "world" for "sun"
- 万事皆旧無新 — a fuller rendering making both halves explicit ("all things are old, nothing is new")

## Cultural Notes
事事皆旧 functions across the CJKV Christian sphere as a direct citation of Ecclesiastes' most famous line, invoked in sermons, literary reflection, and philosophical commentary on the vanity and repetitiveness of human striving. It remains close to a scriptural citation rather than a broadly circulating secular idiom in most of the daughter languages.

## Example Sentences
1. 「已有的事，後必再有；已行的事，後必再行。日光之下，並無新事。」(Eccl 1:9, 和合本 Chinese Union Version)
2. 世人以為創新，實則事事皆舊，循環不已。("People think they innovate, but in reality everything is old — cycling endlessly.")
3. 傳道者言事事皆舊，示人生之虛空。("The Preacher says everything is old, showing the vanity of human life.")
4. Every "revolutionary" trend she'd seen in twenty years of fashion eventually proved 事事皆旧 — just last decade's idea in a new color.

## Sentiment
Pessimistic, philosophical, weary. Used to express disillusionment with claims of innovation, emphasize the repetitive nature of history, or convey existential weariness with life's monotony.
