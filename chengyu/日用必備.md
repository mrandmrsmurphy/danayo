[Dan'a'yo Chengyu](chengyu/Dan'a'yo%20Chengyu.md)

>[!Tip] The motto of 常用字, the core character inventory of 単亜語 — see [[文法 - 03文字法]]

## Literal Meaning
- [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) — day, sun
- [用](../characters/用.md) — to use
- [必 (char)](../characters/必%20(char).md) — certainly, necessarily
- [備](../characters/備.md) — to prepare, to have ready

Read as two words: [[日用]] (daily use, everyday) and [[必備]] (essential, required, a must-have).

Literally: "Daily use, always needed."

## Extended Meaning
The motto heading the 常用字 section of `文法 - 03文字法`, glossed there "Daily Use, Always Needed". That section says 二千 characters are classed as 常用字, that they form 基礎要素 of the language, and that they are 遍用無制限 (used without limit).

## Source and Origin
Coined within 単亜語 itself (`origin: 単亜語`) as the named motto for 常用字 in [[文法 - 03文字法]]. It joins two existing words, [[日用]] and [[必備]]. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 日用 "of daily use; of everyday use; daily expenses" and Chinese 必備 "to be definitely required; to be essential; to be compulsory". It has no entry for 日用必備 (a 404).

## Cultural Notes
A Dan'a'yo-internal term, not an idiom in any of the five languages.

## Example Sentences
1. 日用必備: the motto heading 常用字 in `文法 - 03文字法`, "Daily Use, Always Needed".

## Sentiment
Doctrinal; a motto naming the 常用字 class.
