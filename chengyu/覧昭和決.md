[Dan'a'yo Chengyu](chengyu/Dan'a'yo%20Chengyu.md)
>[!Tip] Guiding principle of [[文法 - 03文字法]]

## Literal Meaning
- [覧](characters/覧.md) — to examine, survey
- [昭 (char)](characters/昭%20(char).md) — bright, clear (here standing for the Shōwa era)
- [和](characters/和.md) — harmony (here standing for Japan/Japanese)
- [決 (char)](characters/決%20(char).md) — to decide

Literally: "Examine Shōwa and decide."

## Extended Meaning
覧昭和決 states the principle that visual form and orthographic standards are to be determined by practical evaluation rather than ideological alignment. In the context of 単亜語, the idiom refers specifically to the adoption of shinjitai-style character forms as a visual compromise: simplified enough to improve legibility and learning speed, yet conservative enough to preserve continuity with the classical written tradition.

## Source and Origin
覧昭和決 is a Dan'a'yo-coined idiom (単亜語), serving as a guiding principle of the language's script policy (see [[文法 - 03文字法]]). 昭和 functions metonymically here, pointing to the Shōwa-era (1926–1989) Japanese script reforms — most notably the 1946 shinjitai simplification — as a historical case of balanced, evaluative script reform rather than either maximal simplification or full traditionalism. 覧 emphasizes inspection and comparison of the available forms; 決 signals an explicit, deliberate design choice rather than passive inheritance.

## Cultural Notes
覧昭和決 is evaluative rather than historical in intent: it does not simply praise the Shōwa-era reforms, but holds them up as a case study for how script policy should be decided in general — through inspection and comparison, not doctrine. Like its companion mottos [[合漢再決]] and [[東亜自通]], it functions as manifesto and policy language rather than an idiom in everyday circulation, cited specifically when explaining or justifying Dan'a'yo's choice of character forms.

## Example Sentences
1. 単亜語の字体選定は、覧昭和決の原則に従っている。("Dan'a'yo's character-form selection follows the principle of 覧昭和決.")
2. When critics asked why the language used shinjitai rather than full traditional forms, the answer was always 覧昭和決 — evaluate, then decide, rather than default to either extreme.
3. 그 원칙은 람소화결의 정신에 따라 자형을 정했다. ("That principle determined the character forms in the spirit of 람소화결.")

## Sentiment
Technical, policy-oriented, deliberate. Frames orthography as a matter of practical evaluation and inheritance, not ideology — a considered middle path rather than a compromise made by default.
