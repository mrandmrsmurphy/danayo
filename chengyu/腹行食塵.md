## Literal Meaning
- [腹 (char)](../characters/腹%20(char).md) — belly
- [行](../characters/行.md) — to go, to walk
- [食](../characters/食.md) — to eat
- [塵](../characters/塵.md) — dust

Literally: "Go on the belly, eat dust."

## Extended Meaning
The curse on the serpent in Genesis 3:14: "on your belly you shall go, and dust you shall eat all the days of your life". English Wiktionary has no entry for 腹行食塵 (a 404).

## Source and Origin
**Genesis 3:14**, God's curse on the serpent after the temptation (和合本, 創世記 3:14):

> 耶和華　神對蛇說、你旣作了這事、就必受咒詛、比一切的牲畜野獸更甚、你必用肚子行走、終身喫土。
> "The LORD God said to the serpent, 'Because you have done this, cursed are you above all livestock and above all beasts of the field; on your belly you shall go, and dust you shall eat all the days of your life.'" (Gen 3:14, ESV)

The 和合本 (verified on Wikisource) has 喫土 ("eat earth") for the Hebrew "dust", and the vault's 食塵 uses 塵 ("dust") instead. 腹行食塵 is a Dan'a'yo compression of the verse. Wikisource's 和合本 also has 餂土 ("lick the earth") in Psalm 72:9 and Micah 7:17, where Micah 7:17 reads 「必餂土如蛇、又如土中腹行的物」, "they shall lick the dust like a serpent, like crawling things of the earth".
