>[!Tip] One of the two named pillars of [[文言現代]], the guiding motto for [[文法 - 04句法]] — the other being [[現代適応]]
[Dan'a'yo Chengyu](chengyu/Dan'a'yo%20Chengyu.md)

## Literal Meaning
- [文](../characters/文.md) — writing, literary language
- [言 (char)](../characters/言%20(char).md) — speech, words
- [継](../characters/継.md) — to continue, to take over
- [承](../characters/承.md) — to receive, to inherit

Literally: "succession to the Literary Chinese [standard]." Read as two words, [[文言]] (Literary Chinese, the classical written register) and [[継承]] (to inherit, to succeed to, to carry on).

## Extended Meaning
`文法 - 04句法` names 文言継承 as the first of the two parts of its guiding maxim 文言現代, covering 主題–述語構造 (topic–comment structure) and 簡潔性 (concision). The other part is [[現代適応]], covering 述語必須 and SVO語順. The same chapter describes 古雅体 as echoing the terseness of Classical Chinese, omitting copulas and particles whenever possible and favouring compact topic–comment phrasing.

## Source and Origin
Coined within 単亜語 itself (`origin: 単亜語`) to give a standalone name to the first component of 文言現代 in [[文法 - 04句法]]. It joins two existing words: [[文言]] and [[継承]]. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 文言 "Literary Chinese; Classical Chinese" and Japanese 文言 "wording; Classical Chinese literary style", and glosses Japanese 継承 "succession, accession, inheritance; to succeed to, to inherit". It has no entry for 文言継承.

## Standard Form
**文言継承** is the Dan'a'yo standard spelling, using the Shōwa-era simplified form 継 in place of 繼 (see [[覧昭和決]]).
- 文言繼承 — the traditional-character form, preserved here as an alias

## Cultural Notes
A Dan'a'yo-internal design maxim, not an idiom in any of the five languages. The Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese readings given above are compositional readings of the four characters.

## Invocation
Invoke 文言継承 when explaining *why* a Dan'a'yo grammatical choice preserves a Classical-Chinese-derived structure rather than adopting a more modern or more foreign alternative — most often topic-comment ordering, the tolerance for omitted copulas in 古風体, or the preference for compact, lightly-marked clause structure generally. Within the balance struck by [[文言現代]], it is the half that says *keep this from the classical tradition*; reach for its counterpart [[現代適応]] when the point being made is instead about what Dan'a'yo requires of modern usage (obligatory verbs, fixed SVO order) rather than what it preserves from Literary Chinese.

## Example Sentences
1. 文言継承 names the first component of 文言現代: 主題–述語構造 and 簡潔性 (`文法 - 04句法`).

## Sentiment
Doctrinal; a term of Dan'a'yo syntax design, paired with [[現代適応]].
