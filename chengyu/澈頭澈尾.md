## Literal Meaning
- [澈](../characters/澈.md) — to penetrate, to go clear through
- [頭 (char)](../characters/頭%20(char).md) — head
- [澈](../characters/澈.md) — to penetrate, to go clear through
- [尾 (char)](../characters/尾%20(char).md) — tail

Literally: "Penetrating the head, penetrating the tail" — going right through from the beginning to the end.

## Extended Meaning
Wiktionary glosses the Chinese idiom (徹頭徹尾) "thoroughgoing; through and through", with a Hong Kong example calling an incident 「徹頭徹尾嘅暴亂」 ("completely a riot"). Its Japanese entry glosses it "thoroughly, right down the line", and its Korean entry 철두철미 is a hanja-form of "thoroughly".

## Source and Origin
The earliest uses I could verify are in the Song philosopher 朱熹 (Zhu Xi, 1130–1200), who uses 徹頭徹尾 in the 《朱子語類》 and in his letters. Examples checked on wikisource:

- **《朱子語類》 juan 48:** 「見做得徹頭徹尾，與一時一事之仁不同」, "seen as doing it through from head to tail, different from the humaneness of a single moment or a single matter."
- **《朱子語類》 juan 132:** 「讀論語，便徹頭徹尾理會論語；讀孟子，便徹頭徹尾理會孟子」, "when you read the Analects, understand the Analects through from head to tail; when you read Mencius, understand Mencius through from head to tail."
- **《答楊子直》:** 「罕見有究竟得徹頭徹尾者」, "rarely does one find anyone who has got to the bottom of it from head to tail."

So the idiom is a Song-dynasty philosophical term of thoroughness, first applied to study and moral practice, not a Ming–Qing vernacular coinage as this page previously said. I have not established whether Zhu Xi coined it. The vernacular and modern uses (for example 「徹頭徹尾的帝國主義壓迫」 in Republican writing) extend it to any total judgment. The spelling with 澈 is also attested: the Joseon 《承政院日記》 (1754) has 「敬者，聖學之澈頭澈尾成始成終者也」, and Zhou Enlai's 1951 statement has 「澈頭澈尾地破壞」, so 徹 and 澈 were used interchangeably.

## Standard Form
**澈頭澈尾** is the Dan'a'yo spelling, using 澈 for the standard 徹.
- 徹頭徹尾 — the standard traditional form
- 彻头彻尾 — simplified Chinese form

## Pronunciations
- **Mandarin (Pinyin):** chè tóu chè wěi
- **Cantonese (Jyutping):** cit3 tau4 cit3 mei5
- **Japanese (on-yomi, hiragana):** てっとうてつび (tettō tetsubi), written 徹頭徹尾
- **Korean (Sino-Korean):** 철두철미 (cheoldu cheolmi)
- **Vietnamese (Hán–Việt):** triệt đầu triệt vĩ

## Cultural Notes
English Wiktionary has an entry under the standard form 徹頭徹尾 (Chinese, Japanese and Korean), and none under the Dan'a'yo spelling 澈頭澈尾.

## Example Sentences
1. 《朱子語類》卷一三二：「讀論語，便徹頭徹尾理會論語；讀孟子，便徹頭徹尾理會孟子。」
   "When you read the Analects, understand the Analects through from head to tail; when you read Mencius, understand Mencius through from head to tail."

## Sentiment
Emphatic; it means "through and through".
