## Literal Meaning
- [海](../characters/海.md) — sea
- [闊 (char)](../characters/闊%20(char).md) — vast, wide
- [天 (char)](../characters/天%20(char).md) — sky, heaven
- [空 (char)](../characters/空%20(char).md) — empty, open space

Literally: "the sea is wide, and the sky is vast."

## Extended Meaning
Boundless freedom or imagination; an open, unrestrained mind; vastness of vision or feeling. Describes freedom after hardship, mental release, or creative inspiration rather than a literal description of geography.

## Source and Origin
Taiwan's Ministry of Education *Dictionary of Chinese Idioms* gives as the source (典源) the Northern Zhou poet Yu Xin's (庾信) 〈道士步虛詞〉, the tenth of ten poems, where, in the entry read at [教育部《成語典》海闊天空](https://dict.idioms.moe.edu.tw/idiomView.jsp?ID=414&q=1&webMd=2&la=0) (citing 《庾子山集注》卷五), the line is

> 「麟洲一海闊，玄圃半天高。」
> ("Qilin Isle is a single wide sea; the Mystic Garden is half-way up the sky.")

describing the distant immortal mountains of the sea and the sky, an image of the free life of immortals. The entry itself says only that the idiom 「也許就從這裡演變而出」 (may have evolved from here), so the origin is held with that reserve. I read the poem only as quoted on that secondary page, not in a primary edition of 《庾子山集注》. The entry's listed later uses are 《隋唐演義》 chapter 85 (「閒雲野鶴，海闊天空。來去自由，不受樊籠」), 《浮生六記》 vol. 4, 《兒女英雄傳》 chapter 26 and 《文明小史》 chapters 35 and 42; I read them only as quoted in the entry and did not search for earlier uses of the four-character form.

`origin` follows the Ministry's entry; I could not verify any other ancestry for the idiom.

## Cultural Notes
The phrase can be read as resonating, in Daoist thought, with 無拘無束 ("free from constraint"), evoking unity with nature's vastness. In Confucian usage it symbolizes magnanimity (寬容, 包容) — a person whose moral horizon is as broad as sea and sky. The phrase is also the title of the Hong Kong rock band Beyond's 1993 song 《海闊天空》, a modern association I did not check further.

## Example Sentences
*All four examples are constructed, not quotations.*

1. 他胸懷海闊天空，不拘小節。("He has a mind as open as the sea and sky, unconcerned with trifles.")
2. 做人要海闊天空，唔好咁計較。("Be broad-minded — like sea and sky — don't be so petty.")
3. 海闊天空の心で全てを受け入れる。("With a heart as vast as sea and sky, accept all things.")
4. Tâm hồn anh ta hải khoát thiên không, rộng rãi bao dung。("His spirit is vast as sea and sky — open and generous.")

## Sentiment
Liberating, expansive, uplifting. Evokes freedom, open-mindedness, and release from constraint or hardship.
