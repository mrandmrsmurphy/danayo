## Literal Meaning
- [海](../characters/海.md) — sea
- [闊 (char)](../characters/闊%20(char).md) — vast, wide
- [天 (char)](../characters/天%20(char).md) — sky, heaven
- [空 (char)](../characters/空%20(char).md) — empty, open space

Literally: "the sea is wide, and the sky is vast."

## Extended Meaning
Boundless freedom or imagination; an open, unrestrained mind; vastness of vision or feeling. Describes freedom after hardship, mental release, or creative inspiration rather than a literal description of geography.

## Source and Origin
海 ("sea") and 天 ("sky") are classic Chinese symbols of limitlessness individually, and their pairing runs through poetry across dynasties. Its most direct ancestor is a couplet by the Tang-dynasty Chan monk Xuanlan (玄覽), inscribed on bamboo at Zhihu Temple in Jingzhou: "大海從魚躍，長空任鳥飛" ("the great sea lets fish leap freely; the vast sky lets birds fly at will"), preserved in the Song-dynasty compilation **《詩話總龜》** by Ruan Yue (阮閱). The couplet gradually evolved into the more familiar form "海闊憑魚躍，天高任鳥飛" ("the sea being vast, fish leap freely; the sky being high, birds fly at will"), and by the Song dynasty the compressed four-character form 海闊天空 had crystallized into a fixed expression meaning expansive and free.

## Cultural Notes
In Daoist thought the phrase resonates with 無拘無束 ("free from constraint"), evoking unity with nature's vastness. In Confucian usage it symbolizes magnanimity (寬容, 包容) — a person whose moral horizon is as broad as sea and sky. In modern Cantonese culture the phrase is iconic largely because of the Hong Kong rock band Beyond's 1993 anthem 《海闊天空》, which uses it to symbolize spiritual freedom and perseverance under hardship, giving the idiom deep emotional resonance across generations well beyond its classical literary use.

## Example Sentences
1. 他胸懷海闊天空，不拘小節。("He has a mind as open as the sea and sky, unconcerned with trifles.")
2. 做人要海闊天空，唔好咁計較。("Be broad-minded — like sea and sky — don't be so petty.")
3. 海闊天空の心で全てを受け入れる。("With a heart as vast as sea and sky, accept all things.")
4. Tâm hồn anh ta hải khoát thiên không, rộng rãi bao dung。("His spirit is vast as sea and sky — open and generous.")

## Sentiment
Liberating, expansive, uplifting. Evokes freedom, open-mindedness, and release from constraint or hardship.
