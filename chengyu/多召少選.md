## Literal Meaning
- [多 (char)](characters/多%20(char).md) — many
- [召](characters/召.md) — to call, summon
- [少 (char)](characters/少%20(char).md) — few
- [選](characters/選.md) — to choose, select

Literally: "Many called, few chosen."

## Extended Meaning
多召少選 captures Jesus's sobering conclusion to the parable of the wedding feast: despite a wide, genuine invitation, only a few actually respond and are ultimately saved. It touches on the tension between divine sovereignty (election) and human responsibility (response to the call), serving both as a warning against presumption and an encouragement to earnest faith — the call is genuine, but it requires proper response. Used to warn against complacency and to emphasize the seriousness of salvation.

## Source and Origin
**Matthew 22:14**, the closing line of the Parable of the Wedding Feast:

> "For many are called, but few are chosen." (Matt 22:14, ESV)

The Chinese Union Version (和合本) renders the verse 「因為被召的人多，選上的人少。」 (checked on cnbible.com, which shows the same wording in the 現代標點和合本 and the 聖經新譯本; the 中文標準譯本 reads 「蒙召喚的人多，而蒙揀選的人少」). The Chinese Bible's own phrase is therefore 被召的人多，選上的人少.

I found **no attestation of 多召少選 as a fixed idiom** in Chinese, Japanese or Korean sources. It appears to be a Dan'a'yo four-character coinage compressing the verse.

召 ("summon, call") represents God's universal gospel invitation, while 選 ("choose, elect") represents those who respond appropriately and are ultimately saved. The contrast 多/少 ("many/few") compresses the parable's entire moral into four characters — the tragedy that despite wide invitation, few actually enter the kingdom.

## Variants
The attested Chinese rendering is 被召的人多，選上的人少 (Chinese Union Version of Matthew 22:14). Three variants listed here earlier (召者多選者少, 多蒙召少蒙選, 被召多被選少) could not be found in any source and have been removed.

## Cultural Notes
多召少選 is a scriptural citation suited to sermons and theological discussion of election, calling and the narrow path to salvation, in the religious register rather than as a general secular expression. Because it is not attested as a fixed idiom, no claim is made here about how common it is in any particular country.

## Example Sentences
1. 「因為被召的人多，選上的人少。」(Matt 22:14, 和合本 Chinese Union Version)
2. 主言多召少選，示得救之路窄而難。("The Lord said many are called but few are chosen, showing that the path of salvation is narrow and difficult.")
3. 雖多召少選，信徒當努力進入窄門。("Though many are called but few are chosen, believers should strive to enter the narrow gate.")
4. The seminar drew hundreds of applicants, but in the end it was 多召少選 — only a handful were ever going to make the cut.

## Sentiment
Sobering, cautionary, selective. Used to warn against complacency, emphasize the seriousness of salvation, or express the tragic reality that many reject God's invitation despite being called.
