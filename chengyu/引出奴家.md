[Biblical Chengyu](chengyu/Biblical%20Chengyu.md)

## Literal Meaning
- [引出](../words/引出.md) — to lead out, draw forth
- [奴家](../words/奴家.md) — house of slavery

Literally: "led out from the house of slavery." (Note: 奴家 in later vernacular Chinese can also mean a self-reference used in drama, or "slave household" in other contexts; here it strictly renders "house of bondage/slavery.")

## Extended Meaning
A concise summary of the Exodus: God delivering Israel from bondage in Egypt. By extension, it signifies redemptive liberation from oppression more broadly, and functions typologically in Christian usage to refer to spiritual emancipation from sin.

## Source and Origin
This idiom condenses the prologue to the Ten Commandments, **Exodus 20:2** (restated in **Deuteronomy 5:6**):

> "I am the LORD thy God, which have brought thee out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage." (KJV)

Traditional Chinese Bible translations often render this as "將你從為奴之家領出來" ("I led you out from the house where you were a slave"). The Dan'a'yo form preserves the juridical and covenantal weight of this declaration, which precedes and grounds the commandments that follow — the law is given only after, and on the basis of, this prior act of deliverance.

## Cultural Notes
Exodus 20:2 functions as a covenant preamble: the commandments are grounded in prior redemption, so that law follows liberation rather than the reverse. The phrase "house of slavery" signals systemic bondage rather than merely personal hardship, framing Israel's identity as a redeemed people; in Christian theology the Exodus becomes a paradigm of salvation, prefiguring deliverance from sin. 引出 emphasizes guided liberation rather than escape achieved by one's own power — the initiative belongs entirely to the divine redeemer. Within the Dan'a'yo Christian sphere, 引出奴家 often serves as shorthand for redemptive history and covenant identity.

## Example Sentences
1. 律法之始，先述引出奴家之恩，而後立誡命。("The law begins by recalling the grace of being led out from the house of slavery before establishing the commandments.")
2. 「我是耶和華你的神，曾將你從埃及地為奴之家領出來。」(出埃及記 20:2，和合本 Chinese Union Version)
3. Every reading of the Ten Commandments begins with 引出奴家 — the reminder of grace before the giving of law.
4. 神引出奴家之民，立為聖約之族。("God led the people out of the house of slavery and established them as a covenant nation.")

## Sentiment
Grateful, covenantal, foundational. Recalls deliverance as the basis for obedience and identity.
