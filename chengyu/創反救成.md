[Biblical Chengyu](chengyu/Biblical%20Chengyu.md)

## Literal Meaning
- [創](../characters/創.md) — to create, originate
- [反](../characters/反%20(char).md) — to rebel, turn against; fall
- [救](../characters/救.md) — to rescue, redeem
- [成](../characters/成%20(char).md) — to complete, consummate

Literally: "Creation, Fall, Redemption, Consummation."

## Extended Meaning
A compact expression of the four-act structure of biblical theology. Used as theological shorthand for the scriptural metanarrative: God creates a good world; humanity falls into sin and rebellion; God redeems creation through Christ's atoning work; all things are finally consummated in the new creation.

## Source and Origin

Coined within Dan'a'yo (`origin: Bible`) as a theological idiom compressing the biblical metanarrative into four characters. English Wiktionary has no entry for it. The individual referents draw on specific biblical texts:

- **創** (Creation): Genesis 1–2 — God creates the heavens and the earth.
- **反** (Fall): Genesis 3 — humanity rebels against God; sin and death enter the world.
- **救** (Redemption): The Gospel accounts and Epistles — Christ's atoning death and resurrection.
- **成** (Consummation): Revelation 21–22 — the renewal of all things, God dwelling with his people.

> 「起初　神創造天地。」(創世記 1:1，和合本)
> "In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth." (Gen 1:1)
> 「神愛世人、甚至將他的獨生子賜給他們、叫一切信他的、不至滅亡、反得永生。」(約翰福音 3:16，和合本)
> "For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son." (John 3:16)
> 「坐寶座的說、看哪、我將一切都更新了。」(啟示錄 21:5，和合本)
> "And the one seated on the throne said: Behold, I am making all things new." (Rev 21:5)
