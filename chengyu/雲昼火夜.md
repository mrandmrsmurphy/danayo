## Literal Meaning
- [雲 (char)](../characters/雲%20(char).md) — cloud
- [昼](../characters/昼.md) — day
- [火 (char)](../characters/火%20(char).md) — fire
- [夜 (char)](../characters/夜%20(char).md) — night

Literally: "cloud by day, fire by night."

## Extended Meaning
A compact reference to the visible presence of God guiding Israel through the wilderness. It signifies divine guidance, protection, and covenant nearness across changing circumstances, and can be invoked metaphorically for steadfast presence amid uncertainty more broadly.

## Source and Origin
This idiom condenses **Exodus 13:21–22**:

> "And the LORD went before them by day in a pillar of a cloud, to lead them the way; and by night in a pillar of fire, to give them light; to go by day and night." (KJV)

The same image is echoed in Exodus 14:19–20, Numbers 9:15–23, and Nehemiah 9:12, 19. Traditional Chinese Bible translations commonly render this as "日間在雲柱中引導他們，夜間在火柱中光照他們" ("by day he led them in a pillar of cloud, by night he gave them light in a pillar of fire"). The Dan'a'yo form strips away the surrounding narrative syntax to the bare dual sign: cloud paired with day, fire paired with night.

## Cultural Notes
The paired imagery functions as a merism of time — day and night together signal continuous, unbroken presence, and the theophany is both protective and directive at once. Cloud in biblical theology often signals divine concealment and majesty, while fire signals holiness and revelation; the two together balance mystery and illumination. In later biblical reflection this wilderness guidance becomes paradigmatic of providence more generally, shaping liturgical memory in Israel's festivals and recurring imagery in prophetic poetry. Within the Dan'a'yo Christian sphere, 雲昼火夜 serves as shorthand for sustained divine guidance through changing and uncertain circumstances.

## Example Sentences
1. 荒野之中，神以雲昼火夜示其同在。("In the wilderness, God manifested his presence as cloud by day and fire by night.")
2. 「日間，耶和華在雲柱中領他們的路；夜間，在火柱中光照他們。」(出埃及記 13:21，和合本 Chinese Union Version)
3. The wilderness generation walked by 雲昼火夜, never left without a visible sign of God's presence.
4. 縱經黑夜，雲昼火夜之應許猶存，神未曾離棄。("Even through the darkest night, the promise of 'cloud by day, fire by night' remains; God has never forsaken his people.")

## Sentiment
Reassuring, majestic, covenantal. Conveys steadfast guidance through changing times.
