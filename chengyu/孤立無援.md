## Literal Meaning
- [孤](characters/孤.md) — isolated, alone
- [立 (char)](characters/立%20(char).md) — standing
- [無 (char)](characters/無%20(char).md) — without
- [援](characters/援.md) — aid, support

Literally: "Standing alone, without aid."

## Extended Meaning
孤立無援 describes being alone with no help or support — cut off from assistance physically, socially, or morally. Originally used in military and political contexts, the idiom broadened to mean any kind of helpless isolation, emotional, moral, or intellectual. Depending on tone it can convey either pathos or nobility: tragic helplessness of the forsaken, or the steadfast independence of someone isolated precisely because they hold to a principle others have abandoned (cf. the Confucian sentiment 道孤而行, "the Way is lonely, yet I walk it").

## Source and Origin
The idea appears early in classical literature, though the fixed phrase 孤立無援 becomes common in Han–Tang historical writing. **《史記·蘇秦列傳》** uses similar imagery to describe diplomats stranded without allies, and **《後漢書》** contains the phrase directly:

> 「孤立無援，勢窮力竭。」
> "Isolated and without aid, his power was exhausted."

## Standard Form
**孤立無援** (traditional 無) is the Dan'a'yo standard form.
- 孤立无援 — simplified Chinese form (无)

## Cultural Notes
Chinese usage spans politics, warfare, and moral reflection — 他在改革中孤立無援，卻不曾退縮 ("he was isolated in his reform efforts, yet never retreated") shows the idiom's capacity to carry admiration alongside hardship. Japanese 孤立無援 (koritsu muen) is common in literature and journalism for "utterly isolated, with no help." Korean 고립무원 (gorip muwon) is widely used for diplomatic isolation or personal despair. Vietnamese cô lập vô viện retains the same straightforward sense, "isolated with no assistance."

Related in sense (though not variants of the same phrase) are 孤軍奮鬥 ("a lone army fighting bravely"), 寡不敵眾 ("the few cannot withstand the many"), 眾叛親離 ("abandoned by all, betrayed even by kin"), and 孤苦伶仃 ("utterly alone and pitiful").

## Example Sentences
1. 「孤立無援，勢窮力竭。」(《後漢書》)
2. 他在敵人包圍中孤立無援。("He was surrounded by the enemy, alone and without help.")
3. 그는 고립무원의 처지에 놓였다. ("He found himself isolated and without support.")
4. After the vote, she found herself politically 孤立無援 — every ally she'd counted on had quietly slipped away.

## Sentiment
Alone and cut off, yet often still standing. A phrase of hardship and endurance — depending on tone, it can express pity, admiration for courage, or stoic resolve.
