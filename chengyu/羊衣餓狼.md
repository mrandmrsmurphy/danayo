## Literal Meaning
- [羊](../characters/羊.md) — sheep
- [衣](../characters/衣.md) — clothing, garment
- [餓](../characters/餓.md) — hungry
- [狼 (char)](../characters/狼%20(char).md) — wolf

Literally: "a hungry wolf clothed in sheep's garb."

## Extended Meaning
A person who outwardly appears gentle or harmless but inwardly harbors destructive intent. The addition of 餓 ("hungry") underscores predatory appetite rather than mere disguise — the danger is active and hungry, not merely hidden.

## Source and Origin
The idiom condenses **Matthew 7:15**:

> "Beware of false prophets, which come to you in sheep's clothing, but inwardly they are ravening wolves." (KJV)

Traditional Chinese Bible translations render the verse as "防備假先知...外面披著羊皮，裡面卻是殘暴的狼" ("beware of false prophets... outwardly clothed in sheep's hide, but inwardly ravenous wolves"). The underlying Greek λύκοι ἅρπαγες ("rapacious/ravening wolves") already carries the sense of predatory hunger that the Dan'a'yo form makes explicit by adding 餓, sharpening the image beyond simple disguise into active predatory intent.

## Cultural Notes
The imagery belongs to pastoral life familiar in the ancient Near East: sheep symbolize vulnerability and covenant community, while wolves represent destructive intrusion into that community. In Christian theology the warning concerns false teachers who imitate outward piety — the emphasis is not merely moral hypocrisy in general but specifically doctrinal danger, since the wolf's disguise is strategic and directed at the flock. In East Asian literary culture wolves already symbolize ferocity on their own; the added pastoral layer of sheep's clothing introduces the specifically covenantal nuance of betrayal from within a trusted community. Within the Dan'a'yo Christian sphere, the idiom is often applied to spiritual leadership, public morality, or ideological deception more broadly.

## Example Sentences
1. 表面敬虔，內心詭詐，實乃羊衣餓狼。("Outwardly devout yet inwardly deceitful, he is a wolf in sheep's clothing.")
2. 「防備假先知，他們到你們這裡來，外面披著羊皮，裡面卻是殘暴的狼。」(馬太福音 7:15，和合本 Chinese Union Version)
3. History has repeatedly shown that a charismatic leader can turn out to be 羊衣餓狼.
4. 教會當儆醒，恐羊衣餓狼混入羊群。("The church should stay watchful, lest wolves in sheep's clothing infiltrate the flock.")

## Sentiment
Warning, vigilant, morally alert. Calls for discernment in judging character beyond outward appearances.
