[Dan'a'yo Chengyu](chengyu/Dan'a'yo%20Chengyu.md)

>[!Tip] The second line of the 筆順 verse, with its partner [[上由下至]] — see the verse on [[Stroke]]

## Literal Meaning
- [左](../characters/左.md) — left
- [由](../characters/由%20(char).md) — from
- [右](../characters/右.md) — right
- [至](../characters/至%20(char).md) — until, to

Literally: "From the left, until the right."

## Extended Meaning
The horizontal companion to [[上由下至]]: when parts of a character stand side by side, the left part is written before the right, and strokes of equal standing in a row are taken from left to right (川, 林, 外). Together with the top-to-bottom rule it fixes the default direction of writing, to which the remaining rules of the verse are refinements.

## Source and Origin
Built on the same pattern as [[上由下至]], with the directions 左 and 右 and the same pair of postpositions 由 and 至; the two phrases form the opening couplet of the 筆順 verse on [[Stroke]]. 左 and 右 are bound characters in Dan'a'yo, whose words are 左側 and 右側, but a chengyu is built from characters, so the short forms are used. Coined within 単亜語 on 2026-10-03.

## Pronunciations
- **Mandarin (Pinyin):** zuǒ yóu yòu zhì
- **Cantonese (Jyutping):** zo2 jau4 jau6 zi3
- **Japanese (on-yomi):** さゆうゆうし
- **Korean (Sino-Korean):** 좌유우지
- **Vietnamese (Hán–Việt):** tả do hữu chí

All five readings are compositional: each is the concatenation of the constituent characters' own readings, since the phrase is a coinage and not an attested idiom.

## Cultural Notes
Like the other idioms of [[Dan'a'yo Chengyu|単亜語 origin]], this phrase is vault-internal terminology rather than a phrase with independent life across the CJKV sphere. The rule it names, however, is not Dan'a'yo's own: it belongs to the common stroke-order tradition of the whole region. The same rule is stated in Chinese stroke-order instruction as 从左到右 ("from left to right"), in Japanese as 「左から右へ」, and in Korean as 왼쪽에서 오른쪽으로.

## Example Sentences
1. 「川」は三本の縦画を左由右至の順に書く。
2. In 林 the left tree is written before the right: 左由右至.

## Sentiment
Neutral and instructional; a mnemonic in the register of a school rule, intended to be recited and remembered.
