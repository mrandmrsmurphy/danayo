[Biblical Chengyu](chengyu/Biblical%20Chengyu.md)

## Literal Meaning
- [心](../characters/心%20(char).md) — heart
- [性](../characters/性.md) — soul, essential nature (here, not its more common modern sense "gender")
- [意](../characters/意.md) — mind, intention
- [力](../characters/力%20(char).md) — strength, power

Literally: "Heart, soul, mind, and strength."

## Extended Meaning
The four objects of 盡 in the Greatest Commandment: 心 (heart), 性 (soul), 意 (mind) and 力 (strength). English Wiktionary has no entry for 心性意力.

## Source and Origin
Mark 12:30, Luke 10:27 (compare Deuteronomy 6:5):

> 「你要盡心、盡性、盡意、盡力、愛主你的　神。」(馬可福音 12:30，和合本)
> "And you shall love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind and with all your strength." (Mark 12:30, ESV)

The four terms 心性意力 are the four objects of 盡 in the 和合本 of Mark 12:30, in the same order. (The 和合本 of Matthew 22:37 has only 盡心、盡性、盡意; Luke 10:27 lists four in a different order: 盡心、盡性、盡力、盡意. The 和合本 of Matthew 22:40 then says that "these two commandments are the sum of the Law and the Prophets", 「這兩條誡命，是律法和先知一切道理的總綱」.)

Coined within Dan'a'yo (`origin: Bible`) as a four-character form of the four nouns of the Greatest Commandment.

A Dan'a'yo-internal biblical coinage. The wording of Mark 12:30, Luke 10:27 and Matthew 22:37 and 22:40 in the 和合本 was checked on Wikisource.
