[Dan'a'yo Chengyu](chengyu/Dan'a'yo%20Chengyu.md)

>[!Tip] The first line of the third couplet of the 筆順 verse, with its partner [[先填後閉]] — see the verse on [[Stroke]]

## Literal Meaning
- [先](../characters/先.md) — first, before
- [外](../characters/外.md) — outside
- [後](../characters/後%20(char).md) — after
- [内](../characters/内.md) — inside

Literally: "Outside first, inside after."

## Extended Meaning
A character that is enclosed by a frame (or whose parts wrap around other parts) is written outside-in: the surrounding strokes first, then what they contain (同, 月, 国, 問). The rule is closely tied to [[先填後閉]], which governs the closing stroke of an enclosure: the frame is drawn, the inside is written, and only then is the enclosure sealed.

## Source and Origin
A 先X後Y phrase built on the spatial words 外 and 内 (the words 外部 and 内部 are their stand-in forms). It is the first line of the third couplet of the 筆順 verse on [[Stroke]]. Coined within 単亜語 on 2026-10-03.

## Standard Form
**先外後内**
- 先外后内 — variant orthography (traditional, simplified or Japanese form of a constituent character)
- 先外後內 — variant orthography (traditional, simplified or Japanese form of a constituent character)

## Pronunciations
- **Mandarin (Pinyin):** xiān wài hòu nèi
- **Cantonese (Jyutping):** sin1 ngoi6 hau6 noi6
- **Japanese (on-yomi):** せんがいごない
- **Korean (Sino-Korean):** 선외후내
- **Vietnamese (Hán–Việt):** tiên ngoại hậu nội

All five readings are compositional: each is the concatenation of the constituent characters' own readings, since the phrase is a coinage and not an attested idiom.

## Cultural Notes
Like the other idioms of [[Dan'a'yo Chengyu|単亜語 origin]], this phrase is vault-internal terminology rather than a phrase with independent life across the CJKV sphere. The rule it names, however, is not Dan'a'yo's own: it belongs to the common stroke-order tradition of the whole region. The same rule is stated in Chinese stroke-order instruction as 先外后内, in Japanese as 「外側が先」, and in Korean as 바깥쪽 먼저, 안쪽 나중.

## Example Sentences
1. 「同」は先外後内で、外枠から書き始める。
2. In 月 the outer frame comes first: 先外後内.

## Sentiment
Neutral and instructional; a mnemonic in the register of a school rule, intended to be recited and remembered.
