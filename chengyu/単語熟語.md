[Dan'a'yo Chengyu](chengyu/Dan'a'yo%20Chengyu.md)

>[!info] For this idiom in use, see [[文法 - 03文字法]].

## Literal Meaning
- [単](../characters/単.md) — single, simple
- [語](../characters/語.md) — word, expression
- [熟](../characters/熟%20(char).md) — ripened, set, fixed
- [語](../characters/語.md) — word, expression

The first two characters form [[単語]] (a single-unit word) and the last two form [[熟語]] (a fixed compound expression).

Literally: "Single words and compound expressions" — the two tiers of the Dan'a'yo lexicon.

## Extended Meaning
A Dan'a'yo term naming two kinds of lexical item, as stated in `文法 - 03文字法`: "Some characters are 単語 when standing alone. Others require companions, forming 熟語. In vocabulary terms, there are 一字 words and 双字 words."

- **単語** — a character that stands alone as a word.
- **熟語** — a character that appears only inside compounds.

In the vault the distinction is recorded by each character's `boundedness` field (for example 単 is 40 and 熟 is 70) and by its `stand_in`.

## Source and Origin
A Dan'a'yo coinage (`origin: 単亜語`) built from two existing Japanese terms. English Wiktionary has no entry for 単語熟語, but defines its parts. 単語 (Japanese) is "a word, a shortest part that means something in a sentence", and also "one's vocabulary". 熟語 is, in Chinese, "idiom", and in Japanese "a kanji compound made by combining two or more kanji characters; a Sino-Xenic term", "a compound word made by combining two or more words", and "idiom".

## Standard Form
**単語熟語** (Dan'a'yo standard; uses Japanese shinjitai 単 and 語)
- 單語熟語 — traditional Chinese form (單 for 単); aliased in this vault

## Cultural Notes
単語熟語 is self-referential: the four characters are themselves a 熟語 made of 単語 and 熟語. The rule it names is the second item under 文字規範 in `文法 - 03文字法`.

## Example Sentences
1. 単語熟語 itself: 単語 + 熟語, two words joined into one four-character phrase.

## Sentiment
Technical; a term used about the language.
