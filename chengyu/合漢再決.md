[Dan'a'yo Chengyu](chengyu/Dan'a'yo%20Chengyu.md)
>[!info] This is the unifying, highest-level motto for the whole language of <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ·⼄</rt></ruby>. See [[文法 - 01序文]].

## Literal Meaning
- [合 (char)](characters/合%20(char).md) — to unite
- [漢](characters/漢.md) — the Han (shared classical/scriptural) world
- [再](characters/再.md) — again, anew
- [決 (char)](characters/決%20(char).md) — to decide

Literally: "Unite the Han [scriptural world] and decide anew."

## Extended Meaning
合漢再決 calls for the renewed unity of the CJKV cultural sphere through its shared classical and scriptural inheritance, consciously re-chosen rather than merely inherited by inertia. The idiom frames unity not as political consolidation or external alignment, but as a deliberate recommitment to the common textual, philosophical, and linguistic ground that historically linked Chinese, Korean, Japanese, and Vietnamese literati. 漢 refers to the shared classical written tradition rather than to ethnicity or modern state identity; 再決 emphasizes intentional renewal — a conscious choice made after historical rupture, modernization, and divergence, rather than a simple return to the past. The tone is formal and programmatic.

## Source and Origin
合漢再決 is a Dan'a'yo-coined idiom (単亜語), composed as the language's own founding motto — the unifying, highest-level statement of purpose for the whole project of 単亜語 (see [[文法 - 01序文]]). It was deliberately built from classical Sino-Xenic material (合, 漢, 再, 決) so that it would itself read as a plausible classical four-character idiom, embodying in its own form the civilizational continuity it calls for.

## Cultural Notes
合漢再決 is civilizational rather than nationalist in scope: it addresses the CJKV cultural sphere as a whole rather than any single modern nation-state, and grounds its call for unity in shared textual and philosophical heritage rather than political or ethnic identity. As the founding motto of 単亜語, it functions less like an idiom in ordinary circulation and more like a mission statement — cited in explaining the language's purpose rather than dropped casually into conversation.

## Example Sentences
1. 単亜語の理念は、合漢再決の一句に集約される。("The guiding principle of Dan'a'yo is condensed into the single line 合漢再決.")
2. To explain why the language exists at all, its founders point back to 合漢再決 — a renewed, chosen unity, not a nostalgic return.
3. 그는 합한재결의 정신으로 이 프로젝트를 시작했다. ("He began this project in the spirit of 합한재결.")

## Sentiment
Formal, programmatic, aspirational. A civilizational call to renewed, deliberately chosen unity rather than a simple appeal to shared ancestry or nostalgia.
