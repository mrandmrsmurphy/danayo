[Dan'a'yo Chengyu](chengyu/Dan'a'yo%20Chengyu.md)

>[!Tip] The other of the two named pillars of [[文言現代]], the guiding motto for [[文法 - 04句法]] — the first being [[文言継承]]

## Literal Meaning
- [現 (char)](../characters/現%20(char).md) — to appear; present
- [代](../characters/代.md) — generation, era
- [適](../characters/適.md) — to suit, to fit
- [応 (char)](../characters/応%20(char).md) — to respond, to answer

Read as two words: [[現代]] (the modern era, contemporary times) and [[適応]] (adaptation, to adapt, to conform to circumstances).

Literally: "adaptation to the modern era."

## Extended Meaning
`文法 - 04句法` names 現代適応 as the second of the two parts of its guiding maxim 文言現代, covering 述語必須 (an obligatory predicate) and SVO語順 (SVO word order). The first part is [[文言継承]], covering 主題–述語構造 and 簡潔性.

## Source and Origin
Coined within 単亜語 itself (`origin: 単亜語`) to give a standalone name to the second component of 文言現代 in [[文法 - 04句法]]. It joins two existing words, [[現代]] and [[適応]]. English Wiktionary glosses 現代 "modern times; modern; contemporary" (Chinese and Japanese) and Japanese 適応 "adaptation, the quality of being suited to some condition or requirement; adapt, adjust, fit in, conform". It has no entry for 現代適応 (a 404).

## Standard Form
**現代適応** is the Dan'a'yo standard spelling, with 応 already the Shōwa-era simplified form (see [[覧昭和決]]).
- 現代適應 — the traditional-character form, preserved here as an alias

A Dan'a'yo-internal term, not an idiom in any of the five languages.

## Example Sentences
1. 現代適応: the second component of 文言現代, covering 述語必須 and SVO語順 (`文法 - 04句法`).

## Sentiment
Doctrinal; a term of Dan'a'yo syntax design, paired with [[文言継承]].
