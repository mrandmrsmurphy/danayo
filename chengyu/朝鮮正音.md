[Dan'a'yo Chengyu](chengyu/Dan'a'yo%20Chengyu.md)
>[!Tip] The guiding principle of [[文法 - 02音韻論]]

## Literal Meaning
- [朝 (char)](characters/朝%20(char).md) — dynasty, morning (here standing for Joseon)
- [鮮](characters/鮮.md) — fresh (here standing for Joseon/Korea)
- [正 (char)](characters/正%20(char).md) — correct, standard
- [音](characters/音.md) — sound

Literally: "Joseon selects the correct sounds."

## Extended Meaning
朝鮮正音 states the principle that a shared East Asian auxiliary language may ground its phonology in the historically central and systematized sound values of Middle Korean. The idiom asserts phonological choice as an act of clarity and intentional design rather than political preference, emphasizing regularity, learnability, and recoverable historical structure. In this sense, sound is not inherited passively but chosen deliberately.

## Source and Origin
朝鮮正音 is a Dan'a'yo-coined idiom (単亜語), stating the guiding principle behind the language's phonology (see [[文法 - 02音韻論]]). It consciously echoes **訓民正音** (Hunminjeongeum), the 1446 proclamation of **King Sejong the Great** (世宗大王) of the Joseon dynasty (朝鮮王朝), which introduced the Hangul script explicitly to give Korean a phonologically precise, learnable writing system of its own. 朝鮮正音 extends that same logic beyond national script reform to civilizational design, framing phonology as a normative choice made in service of intelligibility and shared heritage rather than a passively inherited accident of history.

## Cultural Notes
朝鮮正音 is both technical and commemorative in tone — a design-principle motto, in the same family as [[合漢再決]], [[東亜自通]], [[覧昭和決]], and [[毎字明意]], rather than an idiom in everyday circulation. It is cited specifically to explain why Dan'a'yo's sound system draws on Middle Korean phonology, honoring Sejong's own precedent of deliberate, systematic phonological design rather than simply inheriting whichever regional sound system happened to dominate.

## Example Sentences
1. 単亜語の音韻体系は、朝鮮正音の理念に基づいて設計された。("Dan'a'yo's phonological system was designed based on the principle of 朝鮮正音.")
2. Just as Sejong once gave his people a script fitted to their own sounds, Dan'a'yo follows 朝鮮正音 in choosing a phonology built for clarity rather than habit.
3. 이 언어의 음운 체계는 조선정음의 정신을 계승한다. ("This language's phonological system inherits the spirit of 조선정음.")

## Sentiment
Technical, commemorative, deliberate. Honors a historical act of phonological design while extending its logic to a new, civilizational purpose.
