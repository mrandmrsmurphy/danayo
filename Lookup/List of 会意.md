# 会意— Compound Ideographs
## Explained
### Definition
**会意** refers to characters formed by **combining two or more elements, each contributing meaning**, so that the whole expresses a new, often more abstract or relational idea. The interpretation arises from the interaction of parts, not from depiction (象形) or symbolic marking (指事), and not from a phonetic component (形声).

Common examples:
- 休 “rest” = 人 (person) + 木 (tree)
- 明 “bright” = 日 (sun) + 月 (moon)
- 武 “martial” (traditionally analyzed as 止 “foot/stop” + 戈 “halberd”)
- 信 “trust” = 人 (person) + 言 (speech)

In practice, 会意 is a productive way to build meaning transparently.

### Structural Characteristics
A character is best analyzed as 会意 when:
1. **Multiple components each contribute semantics**  
    Each part has an identifiable meaning relevant to the whole.
2. **The whole meaning is an interpretive combination**  
    Often metaphorical or relational (e.g., “person + tree” → “rest”).
3. **No component is present primarily for sound**  
    There is no dedicated phonetic element.
4. **The structure is compositional and interpretable**  
    A reader can plausibly recover the intended meaning from the parts.

### Contrast with Other Categories
**vs. 象形 (pictographs)**
- 会意 combines elements to form a concept
- 象形 is a single pictorial form

Example contrast:
- 林 (grove = 木 + 木) → 会意
- 木 (tree) → 象形

**vs. 指事 (simple indicatives)**
- 会意 uses multiple semantic units
- 指事 uses a single form with a symbolic mark

Example contrast:
- 本 (root: mark on 木) → 指事
- 休 (person + tree) → 会意

**vs. 形声 (phono-semantic compounds)**
- 会意: all parts are semantic
- 形声: one part is semantic, one part is phonetic

Example contrast:
- 信 (person + speech) → 会意
- 清 (water + phonetic 青) → 形声

### How to Classify a Character as 会意
Use the following decision process:
1. **Can the character be decomposed into recognizable components?**
    - No → consider 象形 or 指事
    - Yes → continue
2. **Do all components contribute meaning to the whole?**
    - Yes → continue
    - No → likely 形声
3. **Is any component primarily indicating pronunciation?**
    - Yes → 形声
    - No → continue
4. **Does the meaning arise from combining the parts (often metaphorically)?**
    - Yes → 会意
    - No → reconsider analysis

A useful diagnostic is substitution:  
If replacing one component would systematically change the meaning (not just the sound), the structure is likely 会意.

### On the “Lost Phonetic” Problem
Modern paleographic research often shows that some characters traditionally labeled 会意 were **originally 形声**, where the phonetic relationship has been obscured by sound change or graphic evolution.

Implications:
- **Diachronic analysis (historical origin)** may differ from
- **Synchronic analysis (how the character functions now)**

For a working system like Dan’a’yo, the synchronic perspective is typically more useful:

- If no phonetic role is recoverable or operative for users,  
    classify the character as 会意.

In other words:
> Treat “hidden” or obsolete phonetic links as irrelevant unless they are productive.

### Notes for Classification
- 会意 is the **second-largest category after 形声**.
- It is the most **useful category for deliberate character creation**, because it preserves semantic transparency.    

Guidelines:
1. **Prefer clear semantic contributions**  
    Each component should visibly support the intended meaning.
2. **Avoid accidental phonetic drift into 形声**  
    Do not rely on sound resemblance unless you intend a phonetic role.
3. **Favor interpretability over cleverness**  
    If a reader cannot plausibly infer the meaning, the composition is weak.
4. **Use it to reduce reliance on phonographic characters**  
    Especially for new vocabulary, 会意 provides a principled alternative to sound-based borrowing.

### Practical Boundary with 形声
A recurring ambiguity:
- If one component could be read as either semantic or phonetic, decide by **function**:
    - contributes meaning → 会意
    - contributes sound → 形声

When in doubt, ask:
> Would a learner use this component to guess the meaning, or the pronunciation?

That answer determines the classification.

### Summary
- 会意 builds meaning through **semantic composition**
- It contrasts with:
    - depiction (象形)
    - indication (指事)
    - phonetic structuring (形声)
- Historical reanalysis does not invalidate its usefulness
- In a constructed system, it is a **primary tool for controlled, transparent vocabulary expansion**.

69. <ruby>[尖](/characters/尖.md)<rt>ㄐㄝㄇ</rt></ruby> - [[小 (char)|小]] ("small") stacked over [[大 (char)|大]] ("large") — a shape narrowing gradually from small to large: "a tapering point, sharp." (Unlike in Japanese, this vault keeps 尖 distinct from 先 rather than blending them.)
70. <ruby>[戍](/characters/戍.md)<rt>ㄙㄨ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 009|人]] ("a person") bearing [[Radical 062|戈]] ("a dagger-axe") — a person standing watch with a weapon: "to garrison, a border guard."
71. <ruby>[戎](/characters/戎.md)<rt>ㄋㄨㄫ</rt></ruby> - [[甲 (char)|甲]] ("shield, armor") + [[Radical 062|戈]] ("a halberd, a weapon") — armor and weapon together standing for arms in general: "weaponry, military affairs" (also the name of the 西戎, an ancient frontier people, and later a surname).
72. <ruby>[早](/characters/早%20(char).md)<rt>ㄐㄚㄨ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 072|日]] ("the sun") risen above 十 (a stylized plant or marker) — the sun already above the horizon: "early."
73. <ruby>[旬](/characters/旬.md)<rt>ㄙ⼜ㄋ</rt></ruby> - 勹 ("to wrap, to enclose," no vault page) around [[日 (char)|日]] ("the sun, a day") — days wrapped into a complete cycle: the ancient ten-day period of the Chinese calendar (days named by the 十干 heavenly stems).
74. <ruby>[死](/characters/死.md)<rt>ㄙㄧㄜ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 078|歹]] ("death, stripped bone remains") beside 人 ("a person," shown kneeling or bowing) — a mourner kneeling beside the dead: "to die, death."
75. <ruby>[老](/characters/老%20(char).md)<rt>ㄌㄚㄨ</rt></ruby> - [[人 (char)|人]] ("a man") with [[毛 (char)|毛]] ("hair," grown long) leaning on [[匕]] ("a cane") — an elderly man leaning on his cane: "old, aged." Cognate to [[考]] (OC *kʰluːʔ) — the classic textbook example of 転注 ("mutual annotation," where two characters gloss and derive from one another).
76. <ruby>[艮](/characters/艮.md)<rt>ㄍㄚㄋ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 109|目]] ("an eye") atop 人 ("a person") — a person turning to look back — extended to "to resist, to disobey," and to "a limit, a stopping point."
77. <ruby>[色](/characters/色.md)<rt>ㄙㄧㄎ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 087|爪]] ("a hand, a claw") pressing down on [[Radical 026|卩]] ("a kneeling person") — a hand shaping a kneeling person's expression: "complexion, expression," extended to "color, appearance," and to sensory phenomena generally.
78. <ruby>[血](/characters/血%20(char).md)<rt>ㄏ⼔ㄊ</rt></ruby> - [[一 (char)|一]] ("a drop") inside [[皿 (char)|皿]] ("a sacrificial vessel") — a drop of blood inside a ritual chalice, offered to the ancestors: "blood."
79. <ruby>[両](/characters/両%20(char).md)<rt>ㄌ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 001|一]] ("one") over 㒳 (an archaic glyph for a joined pair, no vault page) — two things bound together as one: "both, a pair." Bronze-script forms also resemble two [[丙 (char)|丙]] joined side by side (Baxter 1992), from which 丙 is sometimes cited as an incidental phonetic component, though this remains uncertain.
80. <ruby>[充](/characters/充.md)<rt>ㄑㄨㄫ</rt></ruby> - 𠫓 ("a newborn," an inverted 子, no vault page) growing up over [[Radical 010|儿]] ("legs, a standing person") — a newborn grown to maturity: "fully grown," extended metaphorically to "full, filled up," then "sufficient," and later "to serve, to act as (a role)."
81. <ruby>[光](/characters/光%20(char).md)<rt>ㄎ⺢ㄫ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 086|火]] ("fire") held above [[Radical 026|卩]] ("a kneeling person") — fire raised over a person's head: "radiance, brightness, light." Shuowen Jiezi: 明也，从火在人上 ("brightness; formed from fire above a person").
82. <ruby>[共](/characters/共%20(char).md)<rt>ㄍ⼄ㄫ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 055|廾]] ("two hands") holding up [[Radical 030|口]] (here "an object," not "a mouth") — two hands raising something together: "together, jointly, shared." The object component was eventually written 廿 (not "twenty"), which later fused with 廾 to form the modern 共.
83. <ruby>[再](/characters/再.md)<rt>ㄐㄚㄧ</rt></ruby> - genuinely uncertain and disputed. The modern leading theory reads [[一 (char)|一]] ("one") over an abbreviated 冓 ("a basket repeatedly rewoven from bamboo," no vault page), signifying the repetition of an act once done: "again, a second time." An older theory instead reads 魚 ("fish") + 二 ("two"): "to catch two fish at once."
84. <ruby>[卍](/characters/卍.md)<rt>ㄇㄛㄋ</rt></ruby> - not a depiction of any physical object but a purely geometric symbol built from interlocking 十-like segments: the Buddhist swastika, a mark of auspiciousness. Classified under [[Radical 024|十]] by convention, not because it decomposes into that glyph.
85. <ruby>[印](/characters/印.md)<rt>ㄧㄋ</rt></ruby> - [[爪 (char)|爪]] ("a hand") pressing down on [[Radical 026|卩]] ("a kneeling person") — a hand holding a person down: "to press down, to mark," extended to "a stamp, a seal, to print."
86. <ruby>[向](/characters/向%20(char).md)<rt>ㄏ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 040|宀]] ("a house") over [[口 (char)|口]] ("a mouth," here not "an opening" as in 高 or 谷, but a mouth proper) — sound echoing within a dwelling: "to face, to turn toward." The original form of [[響]] ("echo, sound," OC *qʰaŋʔ); later borrowed to also represent 𠨍 (嚮). Compare 冋.
87. <ruby>[妃](/characters/妃.md)<rt>ㄈㄧ</rt></ruby> - [[女 (char)|女]] ("a woman") beside 卩 ("a kneeling man," no vault page) — a woman paired with a man: "a spouse," specialized to "an imperial concubine, the wife of a prince."
88. <ruby>[尽](/characters/尽.md)<rt>ㄐㄧㄋ</rt></ruby> - 聿 ("a hand holding a brush," no vault page) + 灬 ("bristles," no vault page) scrubbing [[皿 (char)|皿]] ("a dish") — cleaning a dish down to nothing: "to exhaust, to use up completely; empty." The character's own Kangxi indexing radical, 尸, is none of these three components — it reflects the modern simplified 尽 glyph's shape rather than 盡's traditional-form etymology.
89. <ruby>[旨](/characters/旨.md)<rt>ㄐㄧㄜ</rt></ruby> - [[匕]] ("a spoon," i.e. 匙) lifted to originally [[甘 (char)|甘]] ("delicious") — eating something delicious with a spoon: "delicious," extended to "intention, purpose, aim," and to "an imperial decree." The bottom component later drifted to resemble [[日 (char)|日]] ("the sun"), the character's modern but etymologically coincidental Kangxi radical. Shuowen instead reads this as 形声 (phonetic 匕 + semantic 甘), but 匕's own Old Chinese reading (a labial initial) does not support it as a valid phonetic donor for 旨's velar initial, so 会意 is preferred here.
90. <ruby>[此](/characters/此%20(char).md)<rt>ㄑㄜ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 077|止]] ("a foot, to stop") beside [[Radical 021|匕]] ("a person turned around") — the place where a person turns and stops: "here, this." Possibly the original form of 跐 ("to stamp, to stand").
91. <ruby>[灰](/characters/灰%20(char).md)<rt>ㄏㄛㄧ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 086|火]] ("fire") held by [[又 (char)|又]] ("a hand") — fire cooled enough to be touched by hand: "ash, ashes."
92. <ruby>[考](/characters/考.md)<rt>ㄎㄚㄨ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 125|耂]] ("an old man with long hair, bent over" — the abbreviated form of [[老 (char)|老]]) leaning on 丂 ("a walking cane," an alias of this very character, no separate page) — an elderly person leaning on a cane: "aged; to examine, to test" (from checking on one's elders). Cognate to [[老 (char)|老]] (OC *ruːʔ) — the classic textbook example of 転注 ("mutual annotation").
93. <ruby>[好](/characters/好%20(char).md)<rt>ㄏㄚㄨ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 038|女]] ("a woman") beside [[子]] ("a child") — it was good for a woman to have a child: "good," extended from the mutual affection between mother and child.
### 7
94. <ruby>[乱](/characters/乱.md)<rt>ㄌㄚㄋ</rt></ruby> - a hand managing a tangled skein of thread (a simplified form of 𤔔, "hands working a spool") set beside [[Radical 005|乙]] ("a hook, a tool") — originally the methodical untangling of thread: "to put in order," which ironically also gave "disorder, chaos."
95. <ruby>[体](/characters/体.md)<rt>ㄊㄝㄧ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 009|人]] ("a person") + [[本 (char)|本]] ("root, base") — a person reduced to their root: "the body." This is the shinjitai simplification; the traditional form 體 is instead 形声 (semantic [[骨 (char)|骨]] "bone" + phonetic [[豊]]).
96. <ruby>[克](/characters/克%20(char).md)<rt>ㄎㄨㄎ</rt></ruby> - [[由 (char)|由]] ("to emerge from") + [[Radical 107|皮]] ("skin, a hide") — to strip an enemy's hide: "to defeat, to vanquish, to overcome." Also serves as the simplification of 剋.

98. <ruby>[初](/characters/初.md)<rt>ㄑㄛ</rt></ruby> - [[衣]] ("clothes") + [[Radical 018|刀]] ("a knife") — cutting the cloth to begin making a garment: "the beginning, the first, initial."
99. <ruby>[別](/characters/別%20(char).md)<rt>ㄅㄝㄊ</rt></ruby> - 冎 (also written 𠮠, "bone") + [[Radical 018|刂]] ("a knife") — cutting bone and flesh apart: "to separate, to divide, to distinguish."
100. <ruby>[利](/characters/利.md)<rt>ㄌㄧㄜ</rt></ruby> - [[禾 (char)|禾]] ("grain") + [[Radical 018|刀]] ("a knife") — reaping grain with a knife: "sharp, profit, benefit."

102. <ruby>[即](/characters/即%20(char).md)<rt>ㄐㄜㄎ</rt></ruby> - [[皀]] ("an ancient round-mouthed food vessel") beside [[Radical 026|卩]] ("a kneeling person") — kneeling down to eat from the vessel: "to approach, to come near to," and (temporally) "at once, right away."
103. <ruby>[吝](/characters/吝.md)<rt>ㄌㄧㄋ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 067|文]] ("pattern, ornament") + [[Radical 030|口]] ("a mouth") — ornamented, hesitant speech that withholds: "stingy, miserly; to begrudge."
104. <ruby>[吹](/characters/吹%20(char).md)<rt>ㄑㄨㄧ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 030|口]] ("a mouth") + [[欠 (char)|欠]] ("to open the mouth wide, to exhale") — the literal act of blowing air through an open mouth: "to blow, to puff." An iconic rather than phonetic combination.
105. <ruby>[呆](/characters/呆%20(char).md)<rt>ㄇㄚㄧ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 030|口]] ("a mouth") set atop [[木 (char)|木]] ("a tree") — a person standing dumbstruck as a tree trunk, mouth agape: "dull-witted, dazed," and of expression or manner, "wooden, rigid."
106. <ruby>[呉](/characters/呉.md)<rt>ㄛ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 030|口]] ("a mouth") beside 夨 ("a person with a tilted head") — a person shouting with head thrown back: "to shout loudly," extended to "big," and the name of the ancient state of Wu. This is the shinjitai of 吳 (吴 is the mainland simplified form).
107. <ruby>[告](/characters/告.md)<rt>ㄍㄚㄨ</rt></ruby> - genuinely disputed: most likely an abbreviated 壴 ("a decorated drum," no vault page) over [[Radical 030|口]] ("an open mouth") — beating a drum while announcing aloud: "to announce, to inform, to declare." An older, disputed Shuowen reading instead takes the top component as 牛 ("an ox").
108. <ruby>[困](/characters/困.md)<rt>ㄎㄛㄋ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 031|囗]] ("an enclosure") framing [[木 (char)|木]] ("wood") — wood boxed into a frame, probably the original form of 梱 and 閫 (both "a doorsill"). The now-dominant sense "distressed, trapped, difficult" is a later extension ("surrounded, besieged" → "hard-pressed"), not the image originally depicted.
109. <ruby>[声](/characters/声.md)<rt>ㄙㄧㄫ</rt></ruby> - 殸 ("a stone chime, being struck by a hand") + [[耳 (char)|耳]] ("an ear") — a chime's sound reaching the ear: "sound, voice."
110. <ruby>[妥](/characters/妥.md)<rt>ㄊㄚ</rt></ruby> - [[爪 (char)|爪]] ("a hand") set over [[女 (char)|女]] ("a woman") — a hand calming a woman: "to comfort, to placate," shifting over time to "adequate, suitable, settled."
111. <ruby>[孝](/characters/孝%20(char).md)<rt>ㄏ⼘ㄨ</rt></ruby> - 耂 ("an old man with long hair, bent over" — compare [[老 (char)|老]]) supported by [[Radical 039|子]] ("a child") — an elder and a child together: "filial piety," the duty owed by descendants to their elders.
112. <ruby>[孚](/characters/孚.md)<rt>ㄈㄨ</rt></ruby> - [[爪 (char)|爪]] ("a hand, a claw") grasping [[子]] ("a child") — a hand seizing a child: "to capture" (the original form of [[俘]], "a prisoner of war"). Shuowen instead glosses it as 卵孚也, "eggs being incubated," extended to "to hatch, to brood over eggs," and — since a hen's reliable brooding produces hatchlings — further to the abstract sense "to inspire trust, to be sincere."
113. <ruby>[宋](/characters/宋.md)<rt>ㄙㄛㄫ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 040|宀]] ("a roof") over [[木 (char)|木]] ("wood, a tree") — a house of timber; the etymology is unclear beyond this literal composition. The name of the ancient Chinese state, and later the Song dynasty.

115. <ruby>[尿](/characters/尿%20(char).md)<rt>ㄋ⼘ㄨ</rt></ruby> - in the seal script, [[尾 (char)|尾]] ("a tail") + [[水 (char)|水]] ("water") — water trailing from an animal's rear: "urine, to urinate." 尾 later simplified into the character's modern radical 尸, which is now purely formal rather than compositional.
116. <ruby>[弄](/characters/弄%20(char).md)<rt>ㄌㄛㄫ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 055|廾]] ("two hands") holding [[Radical 096|玉]] ("jade") — turning a piece of jade over in both hands: "to play with, to toy with," extended to "to tamper with, to mess with."
117. <ruby>[役](/characters/役.md)<rt>⼶ㄎ</rt></ruby> - 彳 ("to walk, to move," no vault page) + 殳 ("a weapon or tool held in the hand," no vault page) — a person driven to move while bearing a tool or weapon: "forced labor, military service; to serve."
118. <ruby>[戒](/characters/戒.md)<rt>ㄍ⼶</rt></ruby> - [[戈]] ("a halberd") held up by 廾 ("two hands," no vault page) — raising a weapon in warning: "to warn, to caution, to admonish."
119. <ruby>[我](/characters/我%20(char).md)<rt>ㄚ</rt></ruby> - genuinely uncertain: folk etymology reads this as [[Radical 064|手]] ("a hand") gripping [[Radical 062|戈]] ("a halberd"), but the true graphemic story is most likely lost to time. Neither [[吾]] nor [[瓦]] came to serve as the modern first-person pronoun, so 我 fills that role instead — [[Swadesh]] word #1.
120. <ruby>[抑](/characters/抑.md)<rt>ㄧㄎ</rt></ruby> - [[手 (char)|手]] ("a hand") pressing down on 卬 ("a kneeling person," no vault page) — a hand suppressing a kneeling person: "to press down, to repress, to restrain." 121. <ruby>[折](/characters/折.md)<rt>ㄐㄝㄊ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 064|手]] ("a hand," the modern form of an original 屮屮, "a tree cut in half") + [[Radical 069|斤]] ("an axe") — an axe cutting a tree in half: "to break, to snap, to fold."
122. <ruby>[更](/characters/更%20(char).md)<rt>ㄍㄚㄫ</rt></ruby> - in its original form, two [[丙 (char)|丙]] side by side (⿰丙丙) — one thing replacing another: "to change, to replace," hence "again, further, more." Later Shuowen-era orthography reanalyzed it as 形声 instead — [[Radical 066|攴]] ("to strike") as semantic + an abbreviated 丙 as phonetic, the leftover second 丙 stroke corrupting into what looks like 一 or 雨 in the modern glyph — but the vault's stored classification reflects the original two-丙 ideogram.
123. <ruby>[沙](/characters/沙%20(char).md)<rt>ㄙㄚ</rt></ruby> - [[水 (char)|水]] ("water") + [[少 (char)|少]] (here representing scattered dots) — grains scattered along a shoreline: "sand." Compare Tibetan ས (sa) and Burmese သဲ (sai:), both also "sand."
124. <ruby>[牢](/characters/牢.md)<rt>ㄌㄚㄨ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 040|宀]] ("a pen, an animal yard") holding [[牛 (char)|牛]] ("a cow") — livestock penned in an enclosure: "a prison, a jail"; also (adjectival) "firm, durable, secure."
125. <ruby>[男](/characters/男.md)<rt>ㄋㄚㄇ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 102|田]] ("a field") over [[力 (char)|力]] ("strength") — one who exerts strength in the field: "a man" (in the sense of male laborer, later generalized).
126. <ruby>[禿](/characters/禿%20(char).md)<rt>ㄊㄛㄎ</rt></ruby> - [[毛 (char)|毛]] ("hair," later corrupted in shape into 禾) over [[Radical 010|儿]] ("a person") — a person without hair: "bald."
127. <ruby>[秀](/characters/秀%20(char).md)<rt>ㄙㄨㄛ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 115|禾]] ("a rice plant") drawn up by [[引 (char)|引]] ("to draw out, to stretch") — a rice plant blossoming into flower: "to bloom," extended to "outstanding, excellent."
128. <ruby>[系](/characters/系.md)<rt>ㄏㄝㄧ</rt></ruby> - an abbreviated [[手 (char)|手]] ("a hand") joining [[糸]] ("silk threads") — a hand tying threads together: "connection, lineage, a system."
129. <ruby>[舛](/characters/舛.md)<rt>ㄑㄝㄋ</rt></ruby> - 夊 ("a foot") beside 𡕒 (another foot, facing the opposite way, no vault page) — two feet pointing in opposite directions: "to oppose, to deviate, to err." Itself Kangxi radical 136.
130. <ruby>[赤](/characters/赤%20(char).md)<rt>ㄑㄝㄎ</rt></ruby> - [[大 (char)|大]] ("a person") beside [[Radical 086|火]] ("fire") — a person standing by a fire, the color it casts: "red." The original form of this image is 𤆍.
131. <ruby>[足](/characters/足%20(char).md)<rt>ㄐㄛㄎ</rt></ruby> - 口 (here a knee or joint shape, not "a mouth") over [[止]] ("a foot") — the whole leg, from knee down to foot: "a foot, a leg."
132. <ruby>[邑](/characters/邑.md)<rt>ㄧㄆ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 031|囗]] ("a walled enclosure") over 卪 ("a kneeling person," a permanently forbidden glyph per `文法 - 98違法字.md`, cited here descriptively only) — a walled settlement with people inside: "a city, a town, a district." A common semantic component in place-name and administrative-division characters.
133. <ruby>[里](/characters/里%20(char).md)<rt>ㄌㄧ</rt></ruby> - [[田]] ("a field") over [[土 (char)|土]] ("soil, earth") — fields upon the earth: "a village, a measure of land" (later "a mile").
134. <ruby>[冶](/characters/冶.md)<rt>⼘</rt></ruby> - originally [[呂]] ("metal ingots") + 刀 ("a ladle") + 口 (decorative) — casting metal ingots: "to smelt, to cast metal," extended figuratively to "to forge, to temper (one's character)." The ingots simplified into [[Radical 015|冫]] ("ice," the same simplification 金 undergoes as 丷), while 刀 and 口 fused into a shape resembling [[台 (char)|台]] — a coincidental resemblance only (台's own OC *lɯ does not match), not a true phonetic donation.
135. <ruby>[医](/characters/医.md)<rt>ㄜ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 023|匸]] ("a hiding enclosure") + [[矢 (char)|矢]] ("an arrow") — a case for storing a bow and arrows: originally a distinct, older character, now used as the shinjitai simplification standing in for traditional 醫 ("medicine"), which is instead 形声 (phonetic 殹 + semantic 酉 "liquor").
136. <ruby>[吠](/characters/吠.md)<rt>ㄈㄝ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 030|口]] ("a mouth") + [[Radical 094|犬]] ("a dog") — a dog's mouth sounding off: "to bark."
137. <ruby>[図](/characters/図.md)<rt>ㄉㄛ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 031|囗]] ("a walled enclosure") around 啚 ("an early form of 鄙, 'remote areas,'" no vault page) — a bounded territory laid out and marked: "a diagram, a map, a picture, a chart."
138. <ruby>[坐](/characters/坐%20(char).md)<rt>ㄐ⺢</rt></ruby> - [[留 (char)|留]] ("to stay, to remain") over [[Radical 032|土]] ("the ground") — staying put on the ground: "to sit."
139. <ruby>[夹](/characters/夹.md)<rt>ㄍ⼘ㄆ</rt></ruby> - one [[大 (char)|大]] ("a person, arms outstretched") flanked by two smaller [[人 (char)|人]] pressing in from either side — a person squeezed between two others: "to squeeze, to pinch, to clamp between," extended to "a clip, a folder, a holder." (en.Wiktionary explicitly classifies this as an ideogrammic compound rather than phono-semantic.)
140. <ruby>[希](/characters/希.md)<rt>ㄏㄧㄜ</rt></ruby> - originally 𢁫: 爻 ("a crossed, woven pattern," no vault page) + [[Radical 050|巾]] ("cloth") — a finely-woven cloth pattern: "sparse, thin" (of a weave), extended to "to hope, to wish for."
141. <ruby>[床](/characters/床%20(char).md)<rt>ㄙ⼘ㄇ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 053|广]] ("a house, a shelter") + [[木 (char)|木]] ("wood") — a wooden furnishing under a roof: "a bed." Originally an unorthodox variant of 牀.
142. <ruby>[戻](/characters/戻.md)<rt>ㄌㄝㄊ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 063|戶]] ("a door") + [[犬 (char)|犬]] ("a dog") — a dog twisting its body to slip in under a door: "to return, to arrive," and by extension the prefix "re-." This is the shinjitai of 戾.
143. <ruby>[甫](/characters/甫.md)<rt>ㄆㄨ</rt></ruby> - originally [[屮]] ("vegetation, sprouts") over [[田]] ("a field") — plants growing in a cultivated field: "a garden" (the original form of [[圃]]). Extended by borrowing to the temporal sense "just now."
144. <ruby>[見](/characters/見%20(char).md)<rt>ㄍ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 109|目]] ("an eye") atop [[Radical 026|卩]] ("a kneeling person") — a kneeling figure with a large eye for a head: "to see."
### 8
145. <ruby>[並](/characters/並%20(char).md)<rt>ㄅㄝㄫ</rt></ruby> - from the clerical-script form 竝 (从二立, "two [[立 (char)|立]] 'standing persons,'" no vault page) — two people standing side by side: "together, side by side, simultaneously." 并/幷 are genuine graphemic variants; 併/倂 (人 semantic + 并 phonetic) is instead a distinct, narrower-sense derived character meaning "to combine, to merge."
146. <ruby>[侃](/characters/侃.md)<rt>ㄎㄚㄋ</rt></ruby> - composition debated: most sources treat it as an ideogrammic compound of [[人 (char)|人]] ("a person"), [[口 (char)|口]] ("a mouth"), and a further, disputed element — "upright, strong, amiable."
147. <ruby>[卓](/characters/卓.md)<rt>ㄊㄚㄎ</rt></ruby> - either [[子]] ("a child") with a flag on its head, or a man standing on an altar illuminated by the sun (昜) — depictions vary, but both converge on "standing tall, raised high": "lofty, eminent, brilliant." The modern form resembles [[早 (char)|早]], having replaced the original child/altar element.
148. <ruby>[卒](/characters/卒.md)<rt>ㄐㄨㄊ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 145|衣]] ("clothing") marked with an identification tag — the marked garment issued to conscripted laborers: "a servant, a soldier," and (by extension) "to finish, to die."
149. <ruby>[取](/characters/取.md)<rt>ㄑㄛㄨ</rt></ruby> - [[耳 (char)|耳]] ("an ear") grasped by [[Radical 029|又]] ("a hand") — taking the ear of fallen prey, a hunters' rite recorded in the Rites of Zhou: "to take, to obtain."
150. <ruby>[夌](/characters/夌.md)<rt>ㄌ⼜ㄫ</rt></ruby> - 夊 ("a foot") + a "high" element (Shuowen: 越也，从夊从𡴆，𡴆，高也) — a foot climbing to a height: "to exceed, to surpass," extended to "to dawdle." The original form of both [[凌]] ("to surmount") and [[陵]] ("a mound; to encroach"), both independently paged in this vault.
151. <ruby>[奄](/characters/奄.md)<rt>⼶ㄇ</rt></ruby> - [[大 (char)|大]] ("big") + [[申]] ("to extend") — a big thing extending to cover everything beneath it: "to cover, to encompass," and (of time) "suddenly, abruptly." An alternative 形聲 analysis (abbreviated semantic 盍 + phonetic 甲) is attested, but 甲's own Old Chinese reading doesn't plausibly explain 奄's, so 會意 is kept.
152. <ruby>[妾](/characters/妾%20(char).md)<rt>ㄑㄛㄆ</rt></ruby> - [[立 (char)|立]] ("to stand") over [[Radical 038|女]] ("a woman") — a woman standing in attendance: "a concubine, a maidservant."
153. <ruby>[宗](/characters/宗.md)<rt>ㄐㄛㄫ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 040|宀]] ("a house, a roof") over [[示]] ("a spirit tablet, an altar") — an altar housed under a roof: "an ancestral temple, a shrine," extended to "clan, lineage, a school of thought."
154. <ruby>[官](/characters/官.md)<rt>ㄍ⺢ㄋ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 040|宀]] ("a roof") over 𠂤 ("multiple rooms," no vault page) — many rooms under one roof, an official building: "official, a government post."
155. <ruby>[実](/characters/実.md)<rt>ㄙㄧㄊ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 040|宀]] ("a roof") over [[周]] ("carved jade," here in an archaic sense) and [[貝]] ("shellfish," used as currency) — jade and cowrie-shell wealth stored under a roof: "full, substantial," extended to "real, true." (Shuowen instead parsed the lower two strokes as a single component, [[貫]] "a string of cash.")
156. <ruby>[岳](/characters/岳.md)<rt>ㄚㄎ</rt></ruby> - [[山 (char)|山]] ("a mountain") stacked with another hill- or mountain-shaped element (sources disagree whether this is 丘 or an archaic 羋-type glyph) — mountain upon mountain: "a tall peak." No candidate component is phonetically plausible for 岳's own ŋ-initial reading, so this is kept as 會意 (a semantic doubling of "mountain") rather than the 形聲 analysis some sources loosely suggest.
157. <ruby>[幸](/characters/幸.md)<rt>ㄏㄚㄫ</rt></ruby> - 屰 ("reversal, going against," no vault page) + [[夭]] ("a bent or dying person") — misfortune reversed and escaped: "lucky, fortunate."
158. <ruby>[或](/characters/或%20(char).md)<rt>ㄏㄛㄎ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 062|戈]] ("a weapon") defending [[Radical 031|囗]] ("an enclosure, a territory") set on [[Radical 001|一]] ("the ground") — a bounded territory defended by force: "a region." Later borrowed for the indefinite "someone, some, perhaps," which caused the territorial sense to be re-differentiated by adding 土/囗, giving [[域 (char)|域]] and [[国|國]].
159. <ruby>[承](/characters/承.md)<rt>ㄙㄨㄫ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 026|卩]] ("a kneeling person") receiving with [[Radical 055|廾]] ("both hands") — a kneeling figure taking something up with both hands: "to hold, to receive," extended to "to inherit." A further hand radical [[Radical 064|手]] was added in the seal-script form.
160. <ruby>[抛](/characters/抛.md)<rt>ㄆㄚㄨ</rt></ruby> - [[手 (char)|手]] ("a hand") + [[九 (char)|九]] and [[力 (char)|力]] ("force, strength") — flinging something away with the strength of the hand: "to throw, to cast away."
161. <ruby>[拝](/characters/拝%20(char).md)<rt>ㄅ⼶</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 064|扌]] ("a hand") presenting 𠦪 ("herbs," no vault page) as an offering — a hand offering herbs: "to bow, to worship."
162. <ruby>[昏](/characters/昏.md)<rt>ㄏㄛㄋ</rt></ruby> - 氐 ("lowering, sinking") + [[日 (char)|日]] ("the sun") — the moment the sun sinks and sets: "dusk, twilight."
163. <ruby>[昌](/characters/昌.md)<rt>ㄑ⺢ㄫ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 072|日]] ("the sun") + 口 (a mouth, later evolving to 曰) — waking at dawn to shouts and song: "prosperous, flourishing."
164. <ruby>[昔](/characters/昔.md)<rt>ㄙㄝㄎ</rt></ruby> - per Shuowen, 仌 ("two pieces of flesh," no vault page) + [[Radical 072|日]] ("the sun") — meat dried in the sun: the original form of 腊 ("dried meat"); the "former times" sense is a later extension. (A separate, more modern account instead treats 昔 as 形声, phonetic 龷 + semantic 日, but the vault keeps the Shuowen-based 會意 reading.)
165. <ruby>[林](/characters/林%20(char).md)<rt>ㄌㄧㄇ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 075|木]] ("a tree") doubled — many trees together: "a forest" (compare the further-tripled [[森]]).
166. <ruby>[枚](/characters/枚%20(char).md)<rt>ㄇㄛㄧ</rt></ruby> - [[木 (char)|木]] ("wood") + 攵 ("a hand holding a stick") — originally "a wooden peg, a slender stalk," extended to serve as the classifier for countable objects (especially flat, thin, or coin-like things). (A competing 形聲 analysis is also attested — semantic 木 + phonetic 文, later visually corrupted into 攵 — plausible on the phonetic evidence, but the vault keeps the documented 會意 reading.)
167. <ruby>[武](/characters/武%20(char).md)<rt>ㄇㄨ</rt></ruby> - [[戈]] ("a blade, a weapon") set above [[Radical 077|止]] ("a foot, to walk") — an army marching out on expedition with weapons: "military, martial."
168. <ruby>[歩](/characters/歩.md)<rt>ㄅㄛ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 077|止]] ("a foot") + 𣥂 ("a foot turned backward," written 少 in the modern form — not the character [[少 (char)|少]] "few") — two feet moving in sequence: "to walk, a step."
169. <ruby>[沓](/characters/沓.md)<rt>ㄉㄛㄆ</rt></ruby> - [[水 (char)|水]] ("water," here suggesting a flow) + 曰 ("speech, a mouth") — words flowing on and on: "verbose, talkative," extended to "numerous, overlapping."
170. <ruby>[法](/characters/法%20(char).md)<rt>ㄈㄚㄆ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 085|氵]] ("water," level and fair) + [[去 (char)|去]] ("to go away, to expel") — law as level and fair as the surface of water, expelling wrongdoing: "law, method." The full ancient bronze form 灋 additionally included 廌 (a mythical goat-like creature said to gore the guilty), later dropped in simplification.
171. <ruby>[炎](/characters/炎.md)<rt>ㄝㄇ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 086|火]] ("fire") doubled — fire intensified: "flame, blaze," and (of weather) "scorching hot."
172. <ruby>[画](/characters/画.md)<rt>ㄏ⺢ㄎ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 129|聿]] ("a brush, a writing implement") tracing [[Radical 102|田]] ("a field, boundary lines") — drawing boundary lines with a brush: "to draw, to plan," and "a painting, a picture."
173. <ruby>[直](/characters/直%20(char).md)<rt>ㄐㄧㄊ</rt></ruby> - 十 + [[Radical 109|目]] ("an eye") + ∟ — an eye sighting straight along a line: "straight, upright," extended to "honest, forthright."
174. <ruby>[秉](/characters/秉.md)<rt>ㄅㄧㄫ</rt></ruby> - [[禾 (char)|禾]] ("a grain plant") held by [[又 (char)|又]] ("a hand") — a hand holding a stalk of grain: "a handful of crops," extended to "to grasp, to hold; authority."
175. <ruby>[突](/characters/突.md)<rt>ㄊㄛㄊ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 116|穴]] ("a cave, a den") + [[犬 (char)|犬]] ("a dog") — a dog bursting suddenly from its den: "sudden, abrupt; to charge forward." (Japanese shinjitai renders the bottom component as 大 instead, but the original component is 犬.)
176. <ruby>[股](/characters/股%20(char).md)<rt>ㄍㄛ</rt></ruby> - originally written 夃 ("thigh," no vault page), which graphically corrupted into a form resembling [[殳]], with [[Radical 130|肉]] ("flesh, a body part") added as a semantic determiner — "a thigh." (Shuowen mistakenly analyzed this as 形聲 with 殳 as a true phonetic; modern scholarship rejects this, since 殳 is only a graphic descendant of 夃, not a genuine sound-bearing component.)
177. <ruby>[肯](/characters/肯%20(char).md)<rt>ㄎㄨㄫ</rt></ruby> - an abbreviated 冎 ("bone," no vault page) + [[Radical 130|肉]] ("meat") — meat still attached to the bone: extended to "willing, to agree."
178. <ruby>[肰](/characters/肰.md)<rt>ㄋㄝㄋ</rt></ruby> - [[肉 (char)|肉]] ("meat") + [[犬 (char)|犬]] ("a dog") — "dog meat" (an obsolete sense).
179. <ruby>[苗](/characters/苗.md)<rt>ㄇ⼘ㄨ</rt></ruby> - 艸 ("grass," no vault page) rising from [[田]] ("a field") — a sprout emerging in a field: "a seedling, a sprout."
180. <ruby>[采](/characters/采.md)<rt>ㄑㄚㄧ</rt></ruby> - [[爪 (char)|爪]] ("a hand reaching down") + [[木 (char)|木]] ("a tree") — a hand picking fruit or leaves from a tree: "to pick, to gather," extended metaphorically to "coloring, adornment," and from there to "demeanor, bearing, elegance." [[採]] is a later derived form (手 semantic replacing 爪) that has since drifted into its own separate word in this vault, not an alias.
181. <ruby>[𡿺](/characters/𡿺.md)<rt>ㄋㄚㄨ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 047|巛]] ("hair," here) + 囟 ("the fontanel, a baby's skull suture," no vault page) — hair over the soft spot of the skull: a traditional variant form of 腦 ("brain"), used primarily as a component (e.g. in 瑙/碯, both aliases of this page, appearing in "agate" compounds).
182. <ruby>[㑒](/characters/㑒.md)<rt>ㄑㄨㄇ</rt></ruby> - [[合 (char)|合]] ("together, to unite") interlocking with [[人 (char)|人]] ("a person") — everyone united as one: "unanimous, all together." This is Japan's extended shinjitai simplification of traditional 僉 (this page's own alias, the kyūjitai print-standard form), and also appears as a component in Jōyō kanji such as 剣, 検, 験, 倹, 険.
183. <ruby>[事](/characters/事%20(char).md)<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 029|又]] ("a hand") holding [[中 (char)|中]] ("a flagpole with a drum") — a flag held aloft while performing one's duty: "a matter, an affair; to serve, to attend to."
184. <ruby>[佩](/characters/佩.md)<rt>ㄈㄛㄧ</rt></ruby> - [[人 (char)|人]] ("a person") + 凡 ("general, all," a possible but debated phonetic) + [[巾]] ("cloth") — cloth worn by a person: originally "a large sash," extended to "a belt ornament, a pendant," and "to wear at the waist."
185. <ruby>[典](/characters/典.md)<rt>ㄉㄝㄋ</rt></ruby> - [[冊 (char)|冊]] ("books") set on 丌 ("a table," no vault page — an ancient variant of [[其 (char)|其]]) — official books laid out on a table: "law, canon, a classic text."
186. <ruby>[受](/characters/受.md)<rt>ㄙ⼜</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 087|爫]] ("a hand") passing [[Radical 137|舟]] ("a boat," now visually corrupted into a shape resembling [[Radical 014|冖]] "a cover" in the modern form) to [[Radical 029|又]] ("another hand") — one hand handing something to another across a boat: "to receive," and (in the causative) "to give."
187. <ruby>[妻](/characters/妻%20(char).md)<rt>ㄑㄝㄧ</rt></ruby> - 肀 ("a hand grabbing hair," no vault page) over [[Radical 038|女]] ("a woman") — a hand seizing a woman by the hair, traditionally read as symbolizing marriage by capture: "a wife." (Possibly cognate with 齊, "equal," implying "equal to her husband," though this is deemed historically unlikely; Schuessler instead suggests an Austroasiatic origin.)
188. <ruby>[学](/characters/学.md)<rt>ㄏㄚㄎ</rt></ruby> - 爻 ("crossed counting rods") + 冖 ("a roof, a cover") over [[子]] ("a child") — a child studying calculation under a roof: "to learn, to study."
189. <ruby>[宜](/characters/宜.md)<rt>ㄜㄧ</rt></ruby> - [[且 (char)|且]] ("a sacrificial altar") set with [[多 (char)|多]] ("two pieces of meat," here in its original doubled-meat sense) — sacrificial meat properly placed on the altar: "fitting, appropriate, suitable." The modern form writes ⿱宀且.
190. <ruby>[岩](/characters/岩.md)<rt>ㄚㄇ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 046|山]] ("a mountain, a hill") + [[石 (char)|石]] ("rock") — a rocky cliff or stone formation. Originally written 巖, a 形声 compound of the Han dynasty; the simplified 岩 first appeared in clerical script and became the standard form.
191. <ruby>[念](/characters/念.md)<rt>ㄋㄝㄇ</rt></ruby> - 亼 ("an upside-down mouth," no vault page) over [[Radical 061|心]] ("the heart") — listening to one's own heart: "to think of, to recall; an idea, a thought." (Shuowen identifies 今 as a phonetic component, but the Old Chinese phonetics do not actually match.)
192. <ruby>[析](/characters/析.md)<rt>ㄙㄝㄎ</rt></ruby> - [[木 (char)|木]] ("wood") + [[斤]] ("an axe") — an axe splitting wood: "to split, to analyze."
193. <ruby>[牧](/characters/牧%20(char).md)<rt>ㄇㄨㄎ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 093|牛]] ("a cow") driven by [[Radical 066|攴]] ("a whip, a hand holding a stick") — herding cattle with a whip: "to herd, to shepherd; to govern."
194. <ruby>[知](/characters/知%20(char).md)<rt>ㄐㄨㄧ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 111|矢]] ("an arrow," swiftness and directness) + [[口 (char)|口]] ("a mouth, speech") — words spoken as unerringly as an arrow flies: "to know."
195. <ruby>[肩](/characters/肩%20(char).md)<rt>ㄍㄝㄋ</rt></ruby> - 戶 ("a shape representing a shoulder," no vault page) over [[Radical 130|月]] ("flesh, meat," the 肉-radical form) — "a shoulder."
196. <ruby>[表](/characters/表.md)<rt>ㄅ⼘ㄨ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 145|衣]] ("clothing") lined with [[Radical 082|毛]] ("fur") — a fur garment worn on the outside: "outer, the surface," extended to "to show, to express."
197. <ruby>[明](/characters/明%20(char).md)<rt>ㄇ⼶ㄫ</rt></ruby> - [[日 (char)|日]] ("the sun") + [[月 (char)|月]] ("the moon") — the sun just rising while the moon has not yet set: "dawn" (as in 天明, "daybreak"), extended to "bright, clear."
### 9
198. <ruby>[保](/characters/保.md)<rt>ㄅㄚㄨ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 009|亻]] ("a person") carrying [[呆 (char)|呆]] (originally 子, "a child," here) on their back — a person bearing a child: "to protect, to safeguard, to defend."
199. <ruby>[兪](/characters/兪%20(char).md)<rt>⼜ㄇ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 011|入]] ("to enter") over 舟 ("a boat," historically corrupted from 凡) — an early form later reused purely as the phonetic basis for its whole descendant family (by vault convention, this character and its descendants all end in -m, to reduce homophony).
200. <ruby>[則](/characters/則.md)<rt>ㄐㄨㄎ</rt></ruby> - [[鼎 (char)|鼎]] ("a bronze tripod cauldron") + [[Radical 018|刀]] ("a knife") — carving an inscription (rules, laws) onto a bronze vessel with a knife: "a rule, a law, a standard," grammaticalized into "then, thus, in that case." (The modern glyph's left component resembles 貝 "cowrie shell," but is actually a corrupted, simplified 鼎, unrelated to the shell character.)
201. <ruby>[勅](/characters/勅.md)<rt>ㄑㄧㄎ</rt></ruby> - 柬 (also written 束, "a bundle," no vault page) + [[攴]] ("a stick held in the hand, denoting authority") — a superior's bundled orders enforced with a rod: "to admonish, to command," extended to "an imperial decree."
202. <ruby>[卑](/characters/卑%20(char).md)<rt>ㄅㄝ</rt></ruby> - [[甲 (char)|甲]] ("a fan") held by 𠂇 ("a hand," an ancient variant of the hand radical, no vault page) — a servant holding a fan for his master: "low, inferior, humble."
203. <ruby>[厚](/characters/厚%20(char).md)<rt>ㄏㄛㄨ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 027|厂]] ("a cliff, a cave in a cliff") + 𣆪 ("a jug," per Shuowen an inverted 亯, no vault page) — "thick, substantial." The semantic logic bridging these two components to that meaning isn't spelled out in any source; this is an attested component breakdown rather than a fully transparent derivation.
204. <ruby>[叚](/characters/叚.md)<rt>ㄎㄚ</rt></ruby> - [[爪 (char)|爪]] ("a hand") + [[又 (char)|又]] ("a hand") + [[石 (char)|石]] ("stone," indicating the ancient pronunciation) — two hands passing something between them: "to borrow." The original form of 假 ("to borrow"), and also an alternate form of 瑕/遐 in a secondary xiá reading.
205. <ruby>[咸](/characters/咸.md)<rt>ㄏㄚㄇ</rt></ruby> - [[戌]] ("an axe") + [[口 (char)|口]] ("a mouth") — originally "to terminate, to cut off," with 口 later borrowed for the adverbial sense "all, entirely" (a synonym of 皆). Also the simplified form of 鹹 ("salty") in contemporary usage.
206. <ruby>[奏](/characters/奏.md)<rt>ㄙㄛㄨ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 055|廾]] ("two hands") holding up 屮 ("an object") — hands presenting or offering something, whether a musical performance or a memorial submitted to a ruler: "to present, to perform; to submit (a report)."
207. <ruby>[姥](/characters/姥.md)<rt>ㄇㄛ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 038|女]] ("a woman") + [[老 (char)|老]] ("old") — "a maternal grandmother." (Dual etymology: also attested as 形聲, with 老 read as phonetic.)
208. <ruby>[姦](/characters/姦.md)<rt>ㄍㄚㄋ</rt></ruby> - [[女 (char)|女]] ("a woman") tripled — "evil, wicked, treacherous," extended to "to commit adultery."
209. <ruby>[威](/characters/威.md)<rt>ㄛㄧ</rt></ruby> - [[戌]] ("a weapon, a halberd-type graph") + [[Radical 038|女]] ("a woman") — Wiktionary offers no further semantic elaboration of the combination; the character ties into a derivational family alongside 畏 ("to fear," the passive counterpart of 威's active "instill awe") and 鬼 ("ghost"): "might, awe-inspiring power."
210. <ruby>[屋](/characters/屋%20(char).md)<rt>ㄛㄎ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 044|尸]] ("a body, to recline") + [[至 (char)|至]] ("to arrive, to reach") — a place a person arrives at to rest: "a house, a roof."
211. <ruby>[屎](/characters/屎%20(char).md)<rt>ㄏㄜ</rt></ruby> - originally three to five dots (depicting faeces) beneath [[Radical 044|尸]] ("a kneeling figure, a corpse") — a person defecating: "excrement." 尸 here also carries the sound (亦聲, a component that is both semantic and phonetic).
212. <ruby>[建](/characters/建.md)<rt>ㄍㄝㄋ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 054|廴]] ("to walk long distances") + [[Radical 129|聿]] (here originally a hand, 又, planting a pole into a base — the resemblance to a writing brush is coincidental) — a pole driven into the ground: "to build, to construct, to erect," extended to "to establish, to found."
213. <ruby>[彖](/characters/彖.md)<rt>ㄊㄚㄋ</rt></ruby> - Shuowen: 豕走也，从彑从豚省 ("a running pig; from 彑 and an abbreviated 豚") — a boar bolting away: "a running hog" (extended, now obsolete, to "hedgehog, porcupine"). Known today almost exclusively as the *I Ching* technical term 彖/彖辭 ("the Judgment," the commentary on each hexagram), and by extension "to judge, to determine."
214. <ruby>[扁](/characters/扁.md)<rt>ㄆ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 063|戶]] ("a door") + [[冊 (char)|冊]] ("bamboo tablets") — an inscription hung flat on a door: "flat and thin."
215. <ruby>[昼](/characters/昼.md)<rt>ㄐㄨㄛ</rt></ruby> - 聿 ("a writing brush," no vault page — the same unlinkable-component status it has in [[律]]) + [[Radical 072|日]] ("the sun") — "daytime." Wiktionary notes 聿's exact role here is debated, possibly phonetic rather than purely semantic (occasionally written 聿+旦 as a variant form).
216. <ruby>[某](/characters/某%20(char).md)<rt>ㄇㄛㄨ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 075|木]] ("a tree") + [[Radical 099|甘]] ("sweet") — a sweet-fruited tree: originally the Chinese plum (梅); later phonetically borrowed for the indefinite pronoun "a certain, some."
217. <ruby>[枼](/characters/枼.md)<rt>ㄨㄆ</rt></ruby> - [[Radical 075|木]] ("a tree") topped with a depiction of thin, flat leaves — thin flat objects growing from a tree: "a leaf, a thin flat sheet."
218. <ruby>[歪](/characters/歪.md)<rt>⺢ㄧ</rt></ruby> - [[不 (char)|不]] ("not") + [[正 (char)|正]] ("straight, correct") — "not straight": "crooked, askew." A late-arising character (晚起字), postdating the Middle Chinese period as an independent written form.
219. <ruby>[爰](/characters/爰.md)<rt>⼔ㄋ</rt></ruby> - [[爪 (char)|爫]] ("a hand") + 丨 + [[又 (char)|又]] ("a hand") — two hands holding or pulling on something between them: "to lead on to, thereupon."
220. <ruby>[甚](/characters/甚.md)<rt>ㄙㄧㄇ</rt></ruby> - [[甘 (char)|甘]] ("sweetness, satisfaction") + [[Radical 021|匕]] ("a spoon, a ladle") — a ladle heaped full: "excessive, very, tremendously."
221. <ruby>[皆](/characters/皆%20(char).md)<rt>ㄍ⼶</rt></ruby> - genuinely contested: modern Old Chinese reconstruction (OC *kriːl) favors 形声, with semantic [[Radical 141|虍]] and phonetic [[口 (char)|口]] (OC *krilʔ). But the vault follows Shuowen's reinterpretation instead: once the bottom component had visually corrupted from 甘 into 白, Shuowen reanalyzed the character as an ideogrammic compound of [[比 (char)|比]] and [[Radical 106|白]] — "all, every, altogether."
222. <ruby>[相](/characters/相.md)<rt>ㄙㄚㄫ</rt></ruby> - [[目 (char)|目]] ("an eye") looking at [[木 (char)|木]] ("a tree") — an eye observing a tree: "to observe," extended to "mutual, each other."
