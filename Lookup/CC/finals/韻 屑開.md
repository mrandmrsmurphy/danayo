## CJKV Evolution
屑開 [et] is a division-4 final and converges cleanly: 20 of 22 characters land on **ㄝㄊ**, matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`et`).

**迭** dodges a 3-member ㄉㄝㄊ (窒, 姪, 跌, all d/t-initial like 迭itself) via a vowel-shift, landing on **ㄚㄊ**. **截**, the only d͡ʒ-initial character on this final, lands on **ㄛㄊ** with no same-initial neighbor to collide with — an unconditioned singleton.
