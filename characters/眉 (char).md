## Notes
- 象形: 𠃜 ("hair") + [[目 (char)|目]] ("eye") — depicts hair above an eye; "eyebrow." Cognate with Tibetan སྨིན་མ ("eyebrow") and Burmese အမွေး ("hair, fur, feather"). Formerly listed 媚 (女 semantic + 眉 phonetic, "charming, to flatter") and 嵋 (山 semantic + 眉 phonetic, part of the Mount Emei place name) as aliases — both are distinct derived characters that use 眉 as their own phonetic component, not variants of 眉 itself; removed.
