## Notes
- 形声 (OC *ɡron): semantic [[Radical 075|木]] ("tree, wood") + phonetic [[鸛|雚]] (OC *koːns) — originally a wooden steelyard weight, phonetically borrowed for the abstract sense of "standard, authority, power." 雚 has no character page of its own in this vault — it's registered on [[鸛]]'s own page as an alias, so per [[feedback_alias_parent_form]] the citation routes to that parent page rather than being described as an unlinkable pageless glyph. **Correction to a correction**: an earlier pass had changed this field from `鸛` to `雚`, reasoning that 鸛 (雚+鳥) is a separate derived character and therefore a substitution error — that missed the alias registration, which makes `鸛` the right citation regardless of which glyph is historically older. Reverted back to `鸛`.
