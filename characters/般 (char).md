## Notes
- 会意: [[凡]] ("plate," rotated 90°, the original form of [[盤 (char)|盤]] "plate, basin") + 攴 ("hand with a stick") — the modern [[Radical 137|舟]] ("boat") component is a coincidental Kangxi-indexing artifact from later reinterpretation of the rotated 凡/皿 shape, not a genuine semantic "boat" connection. Meaning "sort, kind, category," from a Proto-Sino-Tibetan root "to return, convalesce."
