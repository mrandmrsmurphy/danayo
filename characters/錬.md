## Aliases

### 鍊
金 + 柬, "to smelt, to refine"; the older form of 錬. SKIP 1-8-9 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 17 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 煉
火 + 柬, "to refine metal; to knead over fire"; the form Chinese uses for 錬. SKIP 1-4-8; 13 strokes. Radical 086 火. It ranks 3522 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 炼
Simplified Chinese form of 煉. SKIP 1-4-5 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 9 strokes. Radical 086 火. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
