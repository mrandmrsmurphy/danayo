## Aliases

### 鍼
金 + 咸, "needle, to prick"; a variant of 針 in the sense "needle" and used in acupuncture. SKIP 1-8-9; 17 strokes. It ranks 1393 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 针
Simplified Chinese form of 針. SKIP 1-5-2 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 7 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
