## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*l'il): semantic [[Radical 162|辵]] ("movement") + phonetic [[犀]] (OC \*sliːl, "rhinoceros" — a mnemonic for the way a rhino moves) — "to be slow, to be late." Traditional 遲 was simplified to 遅 (犀 → 𡱝) in Japanese shinjitai and to 迟 (犀 → 尺) in Mainland simplification — both already recorded in this page's own `aliases`.
