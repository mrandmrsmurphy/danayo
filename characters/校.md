## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*kreːws, \*ɡreːws): semantic [[Radical 075|木]] ("tree") + phonetic [[交 (char)|交]] (OC \*kreːw) — 校 originally denoted a wooden frame or shackle, with the sense extended via "to check, to compare" to "to correct" and, in the modern compound [[学校]], to "school."
