## Notes
- 会意 of [[天]] ("person," an archaic use of 天 depicting a frontal human figure, not "sky") + [[雨]] ("rain") — a person getting wet in the rain; the original character was 濡, "to get wet." The 天 component was corrupted into 而 during the Han dynasty, which is why later forms show 而 instead. Extended sense "need, require" — easily confused with 須/须 (xū), which the vault distinguishes as usually taking a verb/adverb, while 需 usually takes a noun.
