## Notes
- [List of 会意](../lookup/List%20of%20会意.md): a doubling of [[Radical 095|玄]] ("deep, profound") — "here, now." 茲 is the orthodox form when 玆 is treated as a variant; 兹 is the mainland-China simplified preference — all three are variants of the same character.
- [SKIP-1-5-5](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-5-5.md) ([Stroke 10](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2010.md))
- 1054th most used character in Classical Chinese, the rank of its alias 茲 (玆 itself is not on the lists). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 精|t͡s]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 之|ɨ]] → [ㄐㄜ](../syllables/ㄐㄜ.md)
- [Grade Name](../lookup/Grade%20Name.md), [HSK No](../lookup/HSK/HSK%20No.md), [Hyōgai](../lookup/Japanese/Hyōgai.md), [Korean HS](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20HS.md)
- In the antiquarian style (古雅体 and 訓読古雅 renderings of old documents), 玆 may stand alone as the word "here; hereby," as in 玆由 for the Japanese 茲ニ in the 1945 surrender broadcast ([玉音放送](../translation/玉音放送.md)). This is a feature of that style, not a property of the character, so 玆 has no word page of its own.

## Aliases

### 兹
The common form of 玆, "this". SKIP 2-3-6 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 9 strokes. Radical 012 八. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 茲
艹 + 玆, "this, now" (literary); the traditional form and kept as an alias. SKIP 2-3-6; 10 strokes. Radical 140 艸. It ranks 1054 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
