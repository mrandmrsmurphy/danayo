## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): originally the head of an animal with a long mouth and horns; simplified from 𩠐, with the hair component 巛 reduced to 丷 over time. The word was eventually replaced by [[頭 (char)|頭]] during the Warring States period, possibly to avoid phonetic overlap with [[手 (char)|手]] ("hand").
