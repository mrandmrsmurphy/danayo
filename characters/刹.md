## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*sʰraːd): semantic [[刀]] ("knife") + phonetic [[杀]] (from 殺, OC \*sreːds) — coined specifically as a transliteration of the Sanskrit *kṣV* sequence (via Khotanese/Gandhari), a clipping of 剎多羅 (*kṣetra*, "land, sacred site"); "Buddhist temple, sacred site," and via a second clipping of 剎那 (*kṣaṇa*), "instant, moment." (剎/刹 also has an unrelated `shā` reading, "to brake a car," derived separately from 煞/殺 — not the sense stored here.)
