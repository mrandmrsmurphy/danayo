## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 061|心]] ("heart") + phonetic 咢 (an alias of this character, since 咢 has no page of its own) — "startled, astonished"; Wiktionary glosses it "startled, alarmed, astonished" and notes that 咢 is itself written for 愕 in 咢然, "startled".
- [SKIP-1-3-9](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-9.md) ([Stroke 12](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2012.md))
- 3908th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 疑|ŋ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 鈬開|ɑk]] → [ㄚㄎ](../syllables/ㄚㄎ.md), the regular outcome, the same as its relatives 鄂, 顎 and 鰐; Cantonese ngok6 and Korean 악 agree.

## Aliases

### 咢
"To argue noisily"; the phonetic that 愕 is built on, kept as an alias of 愕 on the same derived sound. SKIP 2-6-3; 9 strokes. Radical 030 口. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
