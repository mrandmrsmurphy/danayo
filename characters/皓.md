## Aliases

### 晧
A variant form of 皓, "bright". SKIP 1-4-7; 11 strokes. Radical 072 日. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 暠
"Bright, brilliant"; a variant of 皓 and kept as an alias. SKIP 2-4-10; 14 strokes. Radical 072 日. It ranks 3147 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.

### 皜
白 + 高, "bright, luminous"; a variant of 皓 and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-5-10; 15 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
