## Notes
- 形声 (per Shuowen, OC \*lan): phonetic 丿 + semantic 㢟 ("to travel far" — itself 彳 "walking" + [[Radical 077|止]] "foot"). Originally just 㢟 (a 会意); the top stroke was added in the late Warring States period as a distinguishing mark, making the character a 指事 by that account.
- [SKIP-3-3-5](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-3/SKIP-3-3-5.md) ([Stroke 08](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2008.md))
- 578th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 以|j]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 仙A三開|iᴇn]] → [⼶ㄋ](../syllables/⼶ㄋ.md)
