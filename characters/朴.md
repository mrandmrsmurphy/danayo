## Aliases

### 樸
"Simple, plain"; the traditional form of 朴 in that sense, and a separate character kept as an alias. SKIP 1-4-12; 16 strokes. It ranks 1834 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
