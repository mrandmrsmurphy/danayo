## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*kraːʔ, \*kraːs): semantic [[Radical 009|人]] ("person") + phonetic [[叚]] (OC \*kraːʔ) — core sense "to lend, borrow" extended to "simulate, fake, false" and, via a *-s suffixed derivative, to "day off, vacation, leave," and separately to conditional "if, supposing." The modern Japanese shinjitai 仮 graphically substitutes 反 for 叚 as a simplification — unlike 鉄's shinjitai substitution 失 (which coincidentally still corresponds phonetically to TETSU), 反 (HAN) has no phonetic correspondence to 仮's own reading (KA/KE); it is a purely graphic simplification, not a re-phoneticization. `graphemic_classification` is correctly kept at 叚, the character's true etymological/phonetic component, rather than the phonetically inert 反.
