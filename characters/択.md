## Aliases

### 擇
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 択, "to choose"; this is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 1-3-13; 16 strokes. It ranks 1067 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 择
Simplified Chinese form of 擇. SKIP 1-3-5 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 8 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
