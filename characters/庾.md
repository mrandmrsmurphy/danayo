## Aliases

### 斞
Variant of 庾 for the historical unit of volume, written 臾 + 斗 ("dipper, measure"); an alternative form per both en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary. SKIP 1-8-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 12 strokes. Radical 068 斗. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 臾
The phonetic of 庾 (and of 貴 and 腴, 諛, 萸), meaning "a moment" (須臾); Wiktionary also calls it the original form of 腴. It is not a form of 庾, but it has the same Korean sound, 유, and is kept as an alias of 庾 by the project owner's decision, with 庾 as the hub for the characters built on 臾 (see Derived Characters). SKIP 4-9-4; 8 strokes. Radical 134 臼. It ranks 2028 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
