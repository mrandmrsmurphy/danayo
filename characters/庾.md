## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 053|广]] ("shelter, building") + phonetic [[臾]] — an open-air pile of grain or a granary; also a historical unit of volume (alternative form: 斞) and a surname (e.g. Harlem Yu, 庾澄慶). `aliases: 臾` was removed — 臾 is only the phonetic component (already correctly captured via `graphemic_classification`, matching en.Wiktionary's ⿸广臾 breakdown), not a genuine variant of 庾 itself; zh.Wiktionary's own 異體字 list confirms the real variants are 㔱, 㢏, 斔, and 斞, of which only 斞 is independently corroborated by en.Wiktionary too (the other three are single-source and left out per the dual-source convention).
