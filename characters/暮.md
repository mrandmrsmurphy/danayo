## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*maːɡs): semantic [[日]] ("sun") + phonetic [[莫]] ("sunset," OC \*maːɡ) — 莫 was the original pictogram (sun 日 sinking into the grass/bushes 屮), later borrowed for the negative "not"; 日 was added again to disambiguate the original "sunset, dusk" sense.
