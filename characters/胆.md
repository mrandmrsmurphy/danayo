## Notes
- 形声 (OC *daːn): semantic [[Radical 130|肉]] ("flesh, body part") + phonetic [[旦]] (OC *taːns) — "gall bladder," extended (figurative) to "courage, bravery" (the seat of one's nerve).

## Aliases

### 膽
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 胆: 肉 + [[旦|詹]] as phonetic where 胆 uses 旦; Wiktionary calls 胆 its variant form, the Japanese shinjitai and the Chinese simplified form. SKIP 1-4-13; 17 strokes. This form is the one the Classical Chinese lists rank, so the `mc_id` 2315 is 膽's rank.
