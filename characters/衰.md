## Notes
- 象形: a pictogram of a raincoat made from palm fibers, the original form of 蓑 (no vault page); the proper character for "weak, soft, exhausted" also came to be borrowed for "mourning clothes" and "raincoat," until 縗 and 簑/蓑 were later coined to take over those two senses respectively.
