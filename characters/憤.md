## Notes
- 形聲 (OC \*bɯnʔ): semantic [[Radical 061|忄]] ("heart") + phonetic [[奔]] (OC \*pɯːn) — "to resent." The character's primary phonetic ancestor is [[賁]] (whose own etymology names 卉 + 貝), but 賁 was itself historically borrowed to write 奔, and Wiktionary's own derivation table names 奔 specifically as the phonetic component for this bɯn-family reading, matching 憤's OC value directly.
- [SKIP-1-3-12](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-12.md) ([Stroke 15](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2015.md))
- 1615th most used character in Classical Chinese, the rank of its alias 忿 (憤 itself ranks 1905). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 奉|v]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 文|ɨun]] → [ㄅㄨㄋ](../syllables/ㄅㄨㄋ.md)
- [Grade 6](../lookup/Grade%206.md), [Old HSK 2](../lookup/HSK/Old%20HSK%202.md), [Jōyō - Kōtō](../lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kōtō.md), [Korean HS](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20HS.md)

## Aliases

### 忿
分 + 心, "angry, indignant"; a separate character kept as an alias of 憤 for its meaning. SKIP 2-2-6; 8 strokes. It ranks 1615 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 愤
Simplified Chinese form of 憤. SKIP 1-3-9 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 12 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
