## Aliases

### 徵
Traditional form of 徴, with a stroke added to the middle (the 山 + 一 + 壬 + 攵 body of 徵 against 徴's 山 + 王 + 攵). SKIP 1-3-12 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 15 strokes. This form is the one the Classical Chinese lists rank, so the `mc_id` 537 is 徵's rank.
