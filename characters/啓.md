## Aliases

### 啟
Traditional form of 啓, "to open, to begin". SKIP 2-8-3 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 11 strokes. Radical 066 攴. It ranks 1384 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 启
The Chinese simplified form of 啟, "to open". SKIP 3-2-5; 7 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
