## Notes
- 形声 (Zhengzhang OC *sklɯɡ; Baxter-Sagart *[ts]ək): semantic [[Radical 115|禾]] ("grain, cereal plant") + phonetic [[畟]] (OC *sklɯɡ) — foxtail millet (*Setaria italica*); also the name of the god of grain (稷神) in Chinese mythology and, via the compound 社稷 ("altar of soil and grain"), a metonym for the state itself. Not to be confused with [[黍]] ("panicled/glutinous millet"), a distinct grain crop frequently paired with 稷 in classical texts (黍稷) but not a genuine variant form of it — 黍 has its own independent page and use in this vault, so it was removed from `aliases`.
