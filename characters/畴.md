### 疇
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 畴, written 田 + 壽; the shinjitai and simplified form 畴 writes the phonetic as 寿. SKIP 1-5-14 (EDRDG); 19 strokes. This is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank, so the `mc_id` 2164 is 疇's rank. SKIP 1-5-14; 19 strokes. It ranks 2164 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
