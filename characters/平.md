## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): etymology genuinely uncertain — proposed theories include a pictogram of waterweed spread flat on the surface of water, a pictogram of a balance scale, and an ideogrammic compound of 八 ("division") + 亏 ("exhaled air," "the smooth flow of speech"). Core meaning "level, even, flat," extended to "calm, peaceful" and "equal, fair."
