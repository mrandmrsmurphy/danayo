## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*qʰaːn, Zhengzhang): semantic [[Radical 209|鼻]] ("nose") + phonetic [[干]] (OC \*kaːn) — "to snore," the rough noise of breathing through the nose in sleep; en.Wiktionary traces the Chinese word to Sino-Tibetan \*hal, compare Tibetan ཧལ ("to snore").
- [SKIP-1-14-3](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-14-3.md) ([Stroke 17](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2017.md))
- Not present in the Classical Chinese usage ranking. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 曉|x]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 寒|ɑn]] → [ㄏ⼘ㄋ](../syllables/ㄏ⼘ㄋ.md), the otherwise unused syllable *hyan*; the regular outcome ㄏㄚㄋ already holds 恨, 汗, 漢 and seven others.
