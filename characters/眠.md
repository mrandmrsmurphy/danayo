## Notes
- 形声 (OC *miːn): semantic [[Radical 109|目]] ("eye") + phonetic [[民]] (OC *min) — close your eyes and go to sleep.
- [SKIP-1-5-5](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-5-5.md) ([Stroke 10](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2010.md))
- 3688th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 明|m]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 先開|en]] → [ㄇㄝㄇ](../syllables/ㄇㄝㄇ.md). The Dan'a'yo syllable diverges from a plain concatenation of 民's own reading (ㄇㄧㄋ) because it deliberately dodges a crowded slot — [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 先開|韻 先開]]'s own page documents 眠 shifting its coda to avoid overcrowding ㄇㄝㄋ (already holding 麺), landing on ㄇㄝㄇ instead.
