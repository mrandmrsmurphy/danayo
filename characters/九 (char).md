## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md) (OC \*kuʔ, Zhengzhang): a stylized hand, with a bent wrist/forearm — hence the hook stroke at lower right. Earlier forms resemble [[手 (char)|手]] (shǒu) and [[寸 (char)|寸]] (cùn). The original meaning of the glyph was "elbow," now written [[肘 (char)|肘]] (OC \*tkuʔ); after that sense was forgotten, 九 was taken to symbolize a fist tightening to bump up against something — a metaphorical bumping-up of nine against ten, the last number reached when counting on one's fingers. Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary).
