## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 046|山]] ("mountain") + phonetic [[威]] — in Standard Chinese, a dialectal word for the place where a mountain or a body of water bends (often in place names), and "to sprain" (an ankle); English Wiktionary also gives an older reading wēi (uneven?). Here the character appears only in names, chiefly 海参崴 (Vladivostok), where it is read wǎi in mainland usage and wēi in Taiwan. It is a 名専字.
