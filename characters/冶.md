## Notes
- [List of 会意](lookup/List%20of%20会意.md) (OC \*laːʔ): 呂 ("metal ingots") + 刀 ("ladle") + 口 (decorative) — to cast metal; the ingots simplified into [[Radical 015|冫]] ("ice," compare 金 where they appear as 丷) while 刀 and 口 fused into a shape resembling [[台 (char)|台]] — this resemblance is coincidental (台's own OC \*lɯ doesn't match), not a true phonetic donation. Original sense "to smelt, cast metal," extended to "to forge, temper" (figurative) "one's character."
