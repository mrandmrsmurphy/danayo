## Notes
- 象形 ([List of 象形](Lookup/List%20of%20象形.md)): "艸木初生也，象丨出形，有枝莖也" (Shuowen) — a sprouting plant, depicting a stem emerging with branches. Both glyphs ⿻凵丨 (standard) and ⿻凵丿 are used. Also serves as an ancient variant of both 艸 ("grass," [Radical 140](Lookup/Radicals/Radical%20140.md)) and 左 ("left," pageless in this vault) — the latter sense corroborated by en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary, but explicitly denied any kun'yomi by ja.Wiktionary, so `japanese_native` is kept empty despite en.Wiktionary's single-sourced ひだり/ひだりて/め/めばえ claims. Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary).
