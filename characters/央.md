## Notes
- 指事: a person [[Radical 037|大]] with the center (the neck/ waist area) marked — center. Origin unclear: possibly a 象形 of a man with the neck in shackles (original form of 鞅), later borrowed for "center" (假借); Shuowen reads it as a person inside a house.
