## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*ɡʷaːʔ): semantic [[Radical 172|隹]] ("bird") + phonetic [[戸]] — originally "a migratory bird." The modern sense "to employ, hire" properly belongs to a distinct, further-derived character, 僱 (亻+雇, phonetic 雇), for which 雇 now serves as the standard simplified/merged form — hence its listing as an alias here.
