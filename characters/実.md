## Notes
- 会意 of [[Radical 040|宀]] ("roof") + [[周]] ("carved jade," an archaic sense distinct from 周's modern "circumference") + [[貝]] ("shellfish") — jade and cowrie-shell wealth stored under a roof: "full; substantial," extending to "real; true." (Shuowen instead parsed the lower two strokes as a single component, [[貫]] "string of cash.")
