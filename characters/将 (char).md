## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*ʔsaŋ, \*ʔsaŋs): phonetic [[爿]] (no vault page) + semantic ⺼ ("meat," no vault page) + semantic [[Radical 041|寸]] ("hand") — to offer meat as tribute (爿 also suggesting a cutting board or sacrificial table); extended to "to take, to hold, to carry," and eventually "will, be going to." Also used for the aliased 漿 — "thick fluid."
- [SKIP-1-3-7](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-7.md) ([Stroke 10](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2010.md))
- 41st most used character in Classical Chinese, the rank of its alias 將 (将 itself is not on the lists). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 精|t͡s]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 陽開|ɨɐŋ]] → [ㄐ⺢ㄫ](../syllables/ㄐ⺢ㄫ.md)
- [Grade 2](../lookup/Grade%202.md), [HSK Beginner](../lookup/HSK/HSK%20Beginner.md), [Jōyō - Kyōiku](../lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kyōiku.md), [Korean MS](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20MS.md)

## Aliases

### 將
The traditional and kyūjitai form; Wiktionary lists 將 as the form of record, with 将 as its simplified Chinese form and Japanese shinjitai. It carries the classical rank of 41, which is the page's `mc_id`. SKIP 1-4-7; 11 strokes. It ranks 41 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 鏘
A separate character (金 + 將, "to tinkle, clang", the sound of metal or jade) built on 將; no link to 将 on its page. Its rank of 3516 is a higher number than 41. SKIP 1-8-11; 19 strokes. Radical 167 金. It ranks 3516 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.

### 𨪙
The Japanese shinjitai form of 鏘 (金 + 将), an unofficial written form; kept as a bare mention. SKIP 1-8-10 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 18 strokes. Radical 167 金. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 漿
A separate character (將 + 水, "thick fluid"), which the page's graphemic note records as also written with 将's phonetic; no link to 将 on its page. Its rank of 2518 is a higher number than 41. SKIP 2-11-4; 15 strokes. Radical 085 水. It ranks 2518 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.

### 浆
The simplified Chinese form of 漿 (将 over 水); not linked to 将 on Wiktionary. SKIP 2-6-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 10 strokes. Radical 085 水. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
