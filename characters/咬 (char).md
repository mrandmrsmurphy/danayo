## Aliases

### 齩
齒 + 交, "to chew, to bite"; a variant of 咬 and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-15-6; 21 strokes. Radical 211 齒. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 齧
齒 + 契 element, "to bite, to gnaw"; a variant of 咬 and kept as an alias. SKIP 2-6-15; 21 strokes. Radical 211 齒. It ranks 2537 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 噛
The Japanese form of 嚙, "to bite". SKIP 1-3-12; 15 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 嚙
口 + 齒 element, "to bite, to chew"; a variant of 咬 and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-3-15; 18 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 囓
A variant form of 咬, "to gnaw". SKIP 1-3-21; 24 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 啮
Simplified Chinese form of 嚙. SKIP 1-3-8 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 11 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
