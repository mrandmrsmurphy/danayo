## Notes
- 會意 (OC \*ŋroːɡ): [[山 (char)|山]] ("mountain") stacked with another hill/mountain-shaped element (sources vary on whether this is 丘 or an archaic 羋-type glyph) — "tall mountain, mountain peak." No candidate component is phonetically plausible for 岳's own ŋ-initial reading, so this is kept as 會意 (semantic doubling of "mountain") rather than a 形聲 analysis some sources loosely suggest.
