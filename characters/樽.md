## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[木]] ("tree, wood") + phonetic [[尊]] — a wooden wine vessel/goblet, a later disambiguating form of 尊 itself (尊 originally meant "wine vessel," with 木 added once the character's own meaning shifted toward the abstract "honor, respect"); 尊 has extensive independent use and its own page in this vault, so it is cited here only as the phonetic component, not listed as an alias. A second candidate variant pair, 墫 and 罇, is only zh.Wiktionary-sourced and left out per the dual-source convention.
