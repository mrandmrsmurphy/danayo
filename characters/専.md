## Notes
- 形声 (traditional form 專): phonetic 叀 ("spindle") + semantic 又 ("hand," later replaced by [[Radical 041|寸]]) — a hand turning a spindle, bundling many things into one. (Also analyzable as 会意.)
- [SKIP-2-6-3](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-6-3.md) ([Stroke 09](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2009.md))
- 745th most used character in Classical Chinese (rank belongs to the traditional form 專). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 章|t͡ɕ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 仙A三合|iuᴇn]] → [ㄐ⼔ㄋ](../syllables/ㄐ⼔ㄋ.md)
