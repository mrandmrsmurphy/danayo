## Aliases

### 區
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 区, "area, district"; this is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 3-2-9; 11 strokes. It ranks 1582 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 嫗
女 + 區, "old woman"; built on 區 and kept as an alias of 区. SKIP 1-3-11; 14 strokes. Radical 038 女. It ranks 2659 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.

### 妪
Simplified Chinese form of 嫗. SKIP 1-3-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 7 strokes. Radical 038 女. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 嶇
山 + 區, "rugged (崎嶇)"; built on 區 and kept as an alias of 区. SKIP 1-3-11; 14 strokes. Radical 046 山. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 岖
Simplified Chinese form of 嶇. SKIP 1-3-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 7 strokes. Radical 046 山. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 崎
山 + 奇, "rugged" (崎嶇); a separate character kept as an alias of 区 as the partner of 嶇. SKIP 1-3-8; 11 strokes. Radical 046 山. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
