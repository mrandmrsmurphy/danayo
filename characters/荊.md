## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 140|艸]] ("grass, plant") + phonetic [[刑]] — the chaste tree (*Vitex*) and similar thorny shrubs; extended to a rod for flogging, a humble term for "one's own wife" (from the image of a poor woman's plain thorn hairpin), the ancient state name for 楚 (Chu), and a surname.
