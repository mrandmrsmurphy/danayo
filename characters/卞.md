## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): uncertain — en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary offer no confident consensus on what the seal-script ⿱亠卜 shape depicts; proposed readings include an ancient cap/headgear (matching its status as "a variant of [[弁 (char)|弁]]," itself "cap") and a type of measuring vessel. Genuinely attested with two distinct senses: "law, statute" (matching `korean_native: 법` and the kun'yomi のり) and "hasty, impatient, rash" (卞急); also a common surname (卞和, associated with the He Shi Bi jade legend) and a historical place name (卞縣, Bian County). 弁 itself is NOT added as an alias despite the "variant" relationship — it has extensive fully independent use of its own ("cap," a distinct sense) and its own page in this vault, per the alias-is-parent-form convention.
