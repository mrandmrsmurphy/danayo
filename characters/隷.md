## Notes
- 形声 (per Shuowen, 从隶柰聲): semantic [[Radical 171|隶]] ("to catch, servant") + phonetic 柰 (OC \*naːds, no vault page — a distinct character from 奈, though the two share the same reading and are often conflated) — "slave, servant, subordinate." 隸 is the traditional form and 隶 (also the semantic component itself) the standard simplified form.
- [SKIP-1-8-8](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-8-8.md) ([Stroke 16](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2016.md))
- 1355th most used character in Classical Chinese (recorded under the traditional form 隸). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 來|l]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 齊開|ei]] → [ㄌㄝㄧ](syllables/ㄌㄝㄧ.md)
