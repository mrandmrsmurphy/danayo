## Notes
- 形声 (per Shuowen, 从隶柰聲): semantic [[Radical 171|隶]] ("to catch, servant") + phonetic 柰 (OC \*naːds, no vault page — a distinct character from 奈, though the two share the same reading and are often conflated) — "slave, servant, subordinate." 隸 is the traditional form and 隶 (also the semantic component itself) the standard simplified form.
- [SKIP-1-8-8](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-8-8.md) ([Stroke 16](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2016.md))
- 1355th most used character in Classical Chinese (recorded under the traditional form 隸). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 來|l]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 齊開|ei]] → [ㄌㄝㄧ](../syllables/ㄌㄝㄧ.md)
- [Grade 5](../lookup/Grade%205.md), [Old HSK 6](../lookup/HSK/Old%20HSK%206.md), [Jōyō - Kōtō](../lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kōtō.md), [Korean HS](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20HS.md)

## Aliases

### 隸
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 隷, "servant, slave; to be subordinate"; this is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 1-9-8; 17 strokes. It ranks 1355 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 隶
Radical 171 (隶), "to reach"; the component 隷 is built on, kept as a handle for 隷. SKIP 4-8-3; 8 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
