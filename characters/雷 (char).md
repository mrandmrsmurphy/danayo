## Aliases

### 蕾
A separate character (艹 + 雷), "flower bud"; Wiktionary does not link it to 雷. Kept for searchers. SKIP 2-3-13; 16 strokes. Radical 140 艸. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 畾
Listed among 雷's forms on Wiktionary as an alternate form of 雷 ("thunder"), and the phonetic component of 雷's original shape 靁 (雨 + 畾); it also had a historical sense, "soil margin between croplands" ([Wiktionary: 畾](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E7%95%BE)). Same reading, léi. 雷 is the hub page for the characters built on 畾, listed under Derived Characters. SKIP 2-5-10; 15 strokes. Radical 102 田. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
