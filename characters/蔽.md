## Aliases

### 敝
㡀 + 攵 ("a hand beating a cloth"), "worn-out, shabby; decayed" and the polite "my, our", and the phonetic of 蔽, 弊, 幣, 撇 and 瞥. Wiktionary records it as an alternative form of both 蔽 and 弊. It has the same derived Dan'a'yo sound as 蔽, ㄆㄝ (MC 毗祭切, `b` + `iᴇi`), so it is an alias of 蔽, which is the hub for the characters built on it (see Derived Characters). ### 斃
敝 over 死, "to fall down, collapse; to be defeated; to drop dead, die; to kill (by shooting)", simplified 毙; it is built on the phonetic 敝, the head of this page's family. It reads ㄆㄝ (MC 毗祭切, `b` + `iᴇi`), the sound of 蔽 and 幣 and of the Korean 폐, and is on the Korean Name and HSK lists, so by the project owner's decision it is an alias of 蔽 ("die" is already 死) and gets no page of its own.
