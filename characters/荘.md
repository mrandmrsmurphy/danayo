## Notes
- 形聲 (OC \*ʔsraŋ): semantic [[Radical 140|艸]] ("grass") + phonetic [[壮]] (the shinjitai of traditional 壯, OC \*ʔsraŋs) — "flourishing," extended to "estate; village" and "dignified; solemn." 荘 is itself the Japanese shinjitai of traditional 莊 (aliased, alongside simplified 庄); recorded under the alias 莊's rank in `CC 0000.md`, since 荘 itself doesn't appear independently.
