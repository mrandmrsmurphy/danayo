## Notes
- 形聲 (OC \*daːn): semantic [[Radical 032|土]] ("earth, soil") + phonetic [[亶]] (OC \*taːnʔ) — "altar; raised platform."
- In Mainland China, the phonetic component simplifies 亶 → 云, hence this character is written 坛 (listed among this character's `aliases`); by the same simplification 曇 → 云, 坛 also serves as the simplification of the unrelated 壜.
