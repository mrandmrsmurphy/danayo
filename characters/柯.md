## Notes
- 形声 (OC *kaːl): semantic [[木]] ("tree, wood") + phonetic [[可]] (OC *kʰaːlʔ) — an axe handle; also, more broadly, the branch or stalk of a plant (matching `korean_native: 가지`), from which the axe-handle sense derives (伐柯, "to cut an axe handle from a branch"). Also a common surname (e.g. Ko Wen-je). zh.Wiktionary's own 異體字 list names 枯 and 過/过, but both belong to entirely unrelated senses of 柯 under separate etymologies (regional Southern Min senses "withered" and "to pass"), not genuine variant orthography of the "branch/axe handle" word documented here — not added as aliases.
