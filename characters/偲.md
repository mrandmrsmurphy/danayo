## Notes
- 形声 (MC tshoj): semantic [[人]] ("person") + phonetic [[思]] (MC si) — attested in the *Shijing* (詩經) as "able, talented, capable" and, in the reduplicated form 偲偲 (as in the *Analects*' 切切偲偲), "urging one another sincerely toward moral improvement," matching `korean_native: 책선할` and the Japanese sense "to mutually admonish one another." A distinct Japanese-specific kun'yomi しのぶ ("to yearn for, to recall fondly") reflects a third, unrelated extended sense. Genuine variant: 愢 (no independent page).
