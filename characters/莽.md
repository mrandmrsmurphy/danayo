## Notes
- 形声 (OC *maːŋʔ, *moːʔ, *maːʔ): semantic [[Radical 094|犬]] ("dog") + phonetic [[茻]] ("thicket, four 艸 stacked") — a dog running through/hunting in thick grass; en.Wiktionary also allows a 會意 (compound-ideograph) reading of the same two components, with no phonetic role assigned. The page's own [[Radical 140|艸]] radical assignment is a purely graphical/positional classification, unrelated to the etymological semantic role played by 犬 above. Extended senses: certain toxic plants (Illicium henryi/lanceolatum) and "rash, reckless" (莽撞).
