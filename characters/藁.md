## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*kaːwʔ): semantic [[艸]] ("grass") + phonetic 槀 (no independent vault page) — "straw, stalks." Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary) as an alternative written form of [稿 (char)](characters/稿%20(char).md) and of 槁 (resolving the page's own open question "Can it blend with 稿?" — yes, they are documented alternative forms, but kept as separate vault pages per established policy for historically distinct-but-converged forms). 蒿 ("mugwort, wormwood") is only a phonetically-related, semantically distinct character in the same 高 series — not an alias — and has no independent vault page.
