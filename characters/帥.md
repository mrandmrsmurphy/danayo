## Notes
- 形聲 (OC \*sruds/\*srud): semantic [[Radical 050|巾]] ("cloth") + phonetic [[堆]] (the vault-paged descendant of ancient 𠂤, which is 帥's actual documented phonetic donor and has no character page of its own) — originally "to wear a scarf," extended to "commander; marshal."
