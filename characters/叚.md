## Notes
- 會意: [[爪]] ("hand") + [[又]] ("hand") + [[石 (char)|石]] ("stone," indicating the ancient pronunciation) — the two hands suggest "borrowing"; original form of 假 ("to borrow"), also an alternate form of 瑕/遐 in a secondary xiá reading. Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary). `か`, previously misfiled as `japanese_native`, is actually the character's kan-on on'yomi reading (already correctly listed in `japanese`); moved out and replaced with the two genuine, dual-confirmed kun'yomi, かりる/かす.
