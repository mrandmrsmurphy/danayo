## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*kulʔ): semantic [[斗]] ("ladle") + phonetic [[鬼]] — "chief, leader, first," extended to "outstanding, prominent" and literally "soup ladle." Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary). "Oiran" is only the sense of the compound [[花魁]] ("leading flower," metaphorically a top courtesan), not the standalone character.
