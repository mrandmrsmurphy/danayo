## Notes
- 象形 (OC \*ʔsruːʔ): a pictogram of a hand grabbing from above (earlier forms show it from the side; compare [[手 (char)|手]], 寸, 九) — despite the "claw" gloss, the pictogram actually depicts a human hand. "Claw, talon, paw, nail," extended (derogatory/humorous) to "hand" of a person.
