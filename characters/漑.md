## Notes
- 形声 (OC *kɯːds, MC kojH): semantic [[水 (char)|水]] ("water") + phonetic [[旣]] (a variant glyph of 既, OC *kɯds) — "to water, irrigate," extended to "to pour" and, less commonly, "to wash, rinse"; both en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary independently identify 漑 as a variant orthography of the far more common modern form [[溉]] (no independent page in this vault), sharing an identical reading and sense range.
