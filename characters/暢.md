## Notes
- 形聲 (OC \*tʰjaŋs): semantic [[申]] ("to extend, to state") + phonetic [[昜]] (OC \*laŋ, a loose match) — unimpeded, flowing freely; smooth, free, unrestrained. Simplified form [[畅]] and variant [[畼]] both confirmed via both English and Chinese Wiktionary; excluded 昶 and 𣈱 (not corroborated on Chinese Wiktionary). Removed a spurious "鬯 (no longer a radical)" note that had been sitting in the `aliases` field — neither Wiktionary edition corroborates any relationship between 鬯 and 暢, and the character's Kangxi radical is 日, not 鬯.
