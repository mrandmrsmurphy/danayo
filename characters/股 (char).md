## Notes
- 會意: originally written 夃 (OC \*kaːʔ, "thigh," no character page exists for it in this vault); the 夃 component graphically corrupted into a form resembling [[殳]], and [[Radical 130|肉]] ("flesh, body part") was added as a semantic determiner. (Shuowen mistakenly analyzed this as 形聲 with 殳 as a true phonetic — modern scholarship rejects this, since 殳 is only a graphic descendant of 夃, not a genuine sound-bearing component.)
- [SKIP-1-4-4](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-4-4.md) ([Stroke 08](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2008.md))
