## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 077|止]] ("foot") + phonetic 厤 — to pass through (a place, time); hence "experience," "history."
- [SKIP-3-2-12](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-3/SKIP-3-2-12.md) ([Stroke 14](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2014.md))
- 948th most used character in Classical Chinese (ranked under the traditional form 歷). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 來|l]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 錫開|ek]] → [ㄌㄝㄎ](../syllables/ㄌㄝㄎ.md)
- [Grade 2](../lookup/Grade%202.md), [HSK Beginner](../lookup/HSK/HSK%20Beginner.md), [Jōyō - Kyōiku](../lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kyōiku.md), [Korean MS](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20MS.md)
- Do not confuse with homophone [[暦]] (calendar/almanac); Simplified Chinese 历 simplifies both this character and 暦/曆 to the same glyph, but it is deliberately **not** claimed as an alias of either here — see 历's own entry for the reasoning (an ambiguous two-way merge, permanently forbidden rather than aliased to one side).
