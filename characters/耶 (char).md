## Notes
- 耶 is a graphic corruption of [[邪]] (OC \*ljaː), itself semantic 阝/邑 + phonetic [[牙]] (OC \*ŋraː) — the questioning sentence-final particle; also, by extension, a standalone interjection of surprise or joy ("yeah!").
