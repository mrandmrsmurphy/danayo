## Notes
- 会意: [[Radical 040|宀]] ("house") + [[頁 (char)|頁]] ("head") — only one person in the house, alone; extended to "widow(ed), few, scant." Shuowen's seal-script reanalysis as 宀+頒 ("to distribute") is a later misreading of two decorative strokes added to 頁.
