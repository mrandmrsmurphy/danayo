## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*qromʔ): semantic [[Radical 064|手]] ("hand") + phonetic [[奄]] (OC \*qromʔ) — to cover with the hand, conceal; by extension to close, and to fall on by surprise (literary).
- [SKIP-1-3-8](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-8.md) ([Stroke 11](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2011.md))
- 1436th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 影|ʔ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 鹽A三|iᴇm]] → [ㄝㄇ](../syllables/ㄝㄇ.md)
	- Its phonetic 奄 reads ⼶ㄇ; the two are the plain and y-glide outcomes of the same final (鹽A三 splits between them). 掩 takes the plain ㄝㄇ, which also matches its Korean reading 엄.
