## Aliases

### 閅
A rare variant of 門 with an extra inner stroke; Wiktionary's 閅 page redirects to 門, and 門 lists it among its forms. Kept as a bare mention. SKIP 3-8-2 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 10 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 门
The simplified Chinese form of 門, and also a Japanese abbreviation (ryakuji); Wiktionary's 门 page redirects to 門, and 門 lists it as its simplified form. SKIP 4-3-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 3 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 鬥
No link from 門's page; 鬥 (Kangxi radical 191, "to fight") is a separate original character, though Wiktionary notes that some of its derived characters, such as 鬪 and 闘, were written with the look-alike 門 as non-standard variants. Its rank of 1298 is a higher number than 門's 188. SKIP 1-5-5; 10 strokes. Radical 191 鬥. It ranks 1298 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.
