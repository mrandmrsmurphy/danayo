## Notes
- Composition ⿰木匱: [[Radical 075|木]] ("tree, wood") beside 匱 (a phonetic that is not a character in this vault) — "cabinet; cupboard; wardrobe", and in Chinese also "counter"; in Japanese read ひつ, "a chest, an item of furniture used for storage". English Wiktionary glosses it as above and adds, for Hong Kong and Taiwan, a short form of 貨櫃, "shipping container".
- [SKIP-1-4-14](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-4-14.md) ([Stroke 18](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2018.md))
