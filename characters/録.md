## Aliases

### 录
Simplified Chinese form of 録, "to record"; the older form 彔 with the 金 radical dropped. SKIP 2-3-5 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 8 strokes. Radical 058 彐. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 錄
Traditional form of 録, "to copy, to write down, to record"; this is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 1-8-8 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 16 strokes. It ranks 1570 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
