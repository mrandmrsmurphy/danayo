## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*zluːn): semantic [[子]] ("child," this character's own `radical` field — relating to growth/continuation, "exist, survive") + phonetic [[才]] (OC \*zlɯː, \*zlɯːs). Possibly the demonstrative *-n derivation of [[在 (char)|在]] (OC \*zlɯːʔ), a proposed morphological (not merely visual) relationship — the two characters are also each other's closest visual look-alike in the vault.
