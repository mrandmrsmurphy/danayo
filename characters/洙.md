## Notes
- 形声 (OC *djo): semantic [[水 (char)|水]] ("water") + phonetic [[朱]] (OC *tjo) — the name of the Zhu River (洙水) in Shandong, historically paired with the neighboring Si River (泗水) in 洙泗 as a metonym for Confucian learning, since Confucius taught near their confluence; this proper-noun river name is the sole sense attested by either en.Wiktionary or zh.Wiktionary, though Korean hanja pedagogical tradition generalizes it to the broader hydrological gloss `korean_native: 물가` ("waterside").
