## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*los): semantic [[口 (char)|口]] ("mouth") + phonetic [[兪]] — "to inform, explain," extended to "to draw an analogy, liken, metaphor." Dan'a'yo gives this a -m-final reading (⼜ㄇ) rather than a strict MC derivation, by vault policy applied across the whole 兪 phonetic family (愈, 愉, 揄, 諭, 逾, 蝓, etc.) to reduce homophony — noted on [[兪]]'s own page.
