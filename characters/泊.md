## Notes
- 形聲 (OC \*baːɡ): semantic [[水 (char)|水]] ("water") + phonetic [[白 (char)|白]] (OC \*braːɡ) — "a ship stays still after running aground in shallow water"; "to anchor; to moor; to lie at anchor." A separate reading, pō, is used for the unrelated "lake" sense (e.g. 湖泊, 梁山泊).
