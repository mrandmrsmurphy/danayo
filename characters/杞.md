## Notes
- 形声 (OC *kʰɯʔ): semantic [[木]] ("tree") + phonetic [[己]] (OC *kɯʔ) — a willow species (*Salix integra*, 杞柳); the compound 枸杞 names the wolfberry/goji berry (this vault's own [[words/句杞|句杞]] substitutes 句 for 枸 as a homophonous stand-in, per that word's own Etymology note); also the name of an ancient feudal state of the Shang/Zhou period, source of the idiom 杞人憂天 ("a man of Qi worries the sky will fall").
