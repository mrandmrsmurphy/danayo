## Aliases

### 檯
A separate character, 檯 (木 + 臺, "table, desk"), which Chinese simplified to 台; 台 absorbed its "table" sense. SKIP 1-4-14; 18 strokes. Radical 075 木. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 臺
Traditional form of 台 (Wiktionary calls 台 its variant traditional form, the Japanese shinjitai and the Chinese simplified form), covering the "tower, platform, stage" sense; the surname 臺 (Tái) differs from the surname 台 (Yí). SKIP 2-3-11; 14 strokes. Radical 133 至. This form is the one the Classical Chinese lists rank, so the `mc_id` 840 is 臺's rank.

### 颱
A separate character, 颱 (風 + 台, "typhoon"), which Chinese simplified to 台; 台風 is the word it survives in. SKIP 3-9-5; 14 strokes. Radical 182 風. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 坮
Listed among 臺's alternative forms (Wiktionary); in Southern Min it means "to bury". SKIP 1-3-5; 8 strokes. Radical 032 土. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
