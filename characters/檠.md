## Notes
- 形声 (OC *ɡreŋ, MC gjaeng): semantic [[木]] ("wood") + phonetic [[敬]] — originally a frame for holding and straightening bows/crossbows, extended by shared "prop, support" semantics to "lampstand, lamp." Dual-source confirmed genuine variants (both en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary cross-list these as 異體字, not mere phonetic-series co-occurrences): [[擎]], [[擏]], [[㯳]], none of which has independent use in this vault.
