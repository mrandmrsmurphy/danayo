## Notes
- 形声 (MC ɣʌŋ, 匣母江韻): semantic [[Radical 049|己]] ("self"; the lower element is written 巳 in hvdic's decomposition 共 + 巳) + phonetic [[共 (char)|共]] — alley, lane; the classical distinction is that a straight thoroughfare is 街 while a curved one is 巷.
- [SKIP-2-4-5](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-4-5.md) ([Stroke 09](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2009.md))
- 1898th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 匣|ɣ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 江|ɣʌŋ]] → [ㄏㄚㄫ](../syllables/ㄏㄚㄫ.md) — the same derivation path as [[項 (char)|項]], [[行 (char)|行]] and [[幸]], among others in the large ㄏㄚㄫ cluster.
- [Grade Advanced](../lookup/Grade%20Advanced.md), [Old HSK 6](../lookup/HSK/Old%20HSK%206.md) rank 722, [Jinmeiyō](../lookup/Japanese/Jinmeiyō.md) (permitted for Japanese given names, though not part of the core 常用漢字/Jōyō list — hence `joyo_level: 日本人名用漢字`), [Korean HS](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20HS.md).

## Aliases

### 衖
Variant form of 巷, "lane, alley". SKIP 1-3-9; 12 strokes. Radical 144 行. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
