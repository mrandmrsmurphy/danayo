## Notes
- 形声: phonetic 倝 + phonetic [[干]] — originally 榦 (倝 + 木 "tree"); the semantic 木 was later replaced with phonetic 干, making both components phonetic. Trunk, main part.
- [SKIP-1-8-5](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-8-5.md) ([Stroke 13](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2013.md))

## Aliases

### 倝
The phonetic component of 幹 (Wiktionary: 幹 was originally 榦, 倝 + 木, and 倝 alone was an abbreviated form of 榦, "branch, tree trunk"); on its own an obsolete character for "sunrise, dawn" (旦 + 㫃) read gàn, like 幹 ([Wiktionary: 倝](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E5%80%9D)). 幹 is the hub page for the characters built on 倝, listed under Derived Characters. SKIP 1-8-2; 10 strokes. Radical 009 人. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
