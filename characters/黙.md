## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*mlɯːɡ): semantic [[Radical 094|犬]] ("dog") + phonetic [[Radical 203|黒]] (OC \*hmlɯːɡ, "black") — a dog keeping silent watch in the dark; silent, quiet, dark. In this Japanese shinjitai form, 黒 is further split at the bottom into 里 and 灬 purely for stroke simplification, not a change in composition.
- [SKIP-2-11-4](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-11-4.md) ([Stroke 15](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2015.md))
- 1649th most used character in Classical Chinese, the rank of its alias 默 (黙 itself is not on the lists). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 明|m]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 德開|ək]] → [ㄇㄨㄎ](../syllables/ㄇㄨㄎ.md)
- [Grade 5](../lookup/Grade%205.md), [Old HSK 2](../lookup/HSK/Old%20HSK%202.md), [Jōyō - Kōtō](../lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kōtō.md), [Korean HS](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20HS.md)

## Aliases

### 默
The traditional and Chinese form (犬 + 黑, "silent; secretly"); Wiktionary's 黙 page sends readers to 默 and calls 黙 the Japanese shinjitai, and 默 lists 黙 among its forms. It carries the classical rank of 1649, which the page uses. SKIP 1-12-4; 16 strokes. Radical 203 黑. It ranks 1649 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 嘿
Listed on 默's Wiktionary page as an alternative form of 默 (and 黙); on its own it means "hey" (口 + 黑), a separate sense. Its rank of 3304 is a higher number than 默's. SKIP 1-3-12; 15 strokes. Radical 030 口. It ranks 3304 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.
