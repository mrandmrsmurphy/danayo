## Notes
- 形声 (MC trhwin): semantic [[Radical 075|木]] ("tree") + phonetic [[春]] ("spring") — in Chinese, Chinese mahogany/Chinese toon (*Toona sinensis*, matching `korean_native: 참죽나무`), extended figuratively to "father" and, via the *Zhuangzi*'s allegory of the long-lived 大椿 tree, "longevity" (椿齡). In Japanese, however, 椿 (つばき) was reassigned to name an entirely unrelated tree, the camellia (*Camellia japonica*) — a well-documented case of the same character denoting different plants across the two languages. Genuine variants: 杶, 櫄.
