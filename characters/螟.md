## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 142|虫]] ("insect") + phonetic [冥 (char)](characters/冥%20(char).md) — "larva; stem borer." By extension, "adopted child": there was a long-held but incorrect Chinese folk belief that wasps (蜾蠃), thought unable to reproduce themselves, carried moth larvae to their hives and raised them as their own — in fact the wasps take the caterpillars as food for their own larvae, but the metaphor of a moth larva for an adopted child survives.
