## Notes
- Originally 形声 (OC \*tʰaːds), phonetic [[大 (char)|大]] ("big, great") + semantic 廾 ("two hands") + semantic 水 ("water"; 大+廾 later fused into 𡗗) — later reinterpreted as 会意 of the same three components once the phonetic relationship was no longer transparent. "Great and free-flowing"; extended to "peaceful, at ease."
- [SKIP-2-5-5](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-5-5.md) ([Stroke 10](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2010.md))
