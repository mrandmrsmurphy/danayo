## Notes
- 形声 (Baxter-Sagart *m(r)iwk, Zhengzhang *muɡ): semantic [[Radical 115|禾]] ("grain") + phonetic [[㣎]] — originally "standing grain" (an obsolete sense in Standard Chinese), extended to "reverent, solemn, dignified, harmonious, calm" (matching `korean_native: 화목할`), and used as the transliteration syllable for "Mu-" in 穆斯林 ("Muslim") and 穆罕默德 ("Muhammad"); also a surname and posthumous temple-name epithet for rulers (e.g. 穆公, 穆王).
