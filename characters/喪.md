## Notes
- 会意 (OC \*smaːŋs, with an *s- nominalizing/circumstantial prefix, "the circumstances of a death"): [[哭 (char)|哭]] ("to cry, wail") + 亡 (here appearing as its seal-script variant 亾, "to perish, be lost") — Wiktionary notes 亡 supplies both meaning and sound here (會意兼形聲), combining "crying" with "death/disappearance" to depict mourning and loss.
