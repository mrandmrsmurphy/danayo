## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[手]] ("hand") + phonetic [[覺]] — to stir, mix, disturb, agitate. 撹 is Japan's extended shinjitai form of traditional 攪, and was also briefly the ROC's 1935–36 simplified form; mainland China's own simplified form, 搅, is a separate sibling glyph — none of the three has an independent page in this vault, and 撹's own trusted `mc_id` and this page's genuine Old HSK 6 citation (recorded under 搅) both reflect that shared identity.
