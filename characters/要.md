## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): a person (originally 大, later drawn as [[女 (char)|女]]) with two hands pointing to the waist; the upper portion — originally a large eye 目, stylized as a horn 角 — became the modern simplified 覀. Originally meant "waist" (now written [[腰]]); extended to "essential, important; to need."
