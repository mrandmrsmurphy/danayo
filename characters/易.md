## Notes
- [List of 象形](../lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): originally depicted a filled vessel for pouring liquids, the ancestor form of [[賜]] ("to bestow"), later borrowed phonetically for the abstract sense "easy." The modern glyph's resemblance to [[Radical 072|日]] + 勿 is a folk-etymology coincidence, not its real origin, but is why the character is filed under that Kangxi radical.
- [SKIP-2-4-4](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-4-4.md) ([Stroke 08](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2008.md))
- 237th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 以|j]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 昔開|iᴇk]] → [⼶ㄎ](../syllables/⼶ㄎ.md)
