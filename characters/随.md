## Aliases

### 隨
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 随, "to follow"; this is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 1-3-13; 14 strokes. It ranks 615 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 陏
"Melon"; also the name of a Spring and Autumn state in what is now Suizhou, Hubei. Kept as an alias of 随 on the same derived sound. SKIP 1-3-6; 8 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
