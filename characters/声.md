## Aliases

### 聲
The orthodox traditional form of 声, 殸 (a stone chime struck by a hand) over 耳 ("ear"); 声 is the Japanese shinjitai and the Chinese simplified form, dropping 殳 and 耳 (Wiktionary). SKIP 2-11-6; 17 strokes. Radical 128 耳. This form is the one the Classical Chinese lists rank, so the `mc_id` 84 is 聲's rank.
