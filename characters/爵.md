## Notes
- 象形 (OC \*ʔsewɢ): a wine vessel shaped like a sparrow, with variant forms adding a hand ([[Radical 041|寸]]) holding it. "Jue" (a three-legged ceremonial wine vessel used in Zhou-dynasty investiture rituals), extended metonymically to "feudal title, noble rank." (The citing word [[爵位]] describes this as 会意 in its own prose; Wiktionary presents only the 象形 analysis with no alternative given, so the character page follows that here — a discrepancy worth revisiting in a future word-sweep pass.)

### 借代字
- 嚼 "to masticate" — requires 咀嚼, but 咀 is an obscure character outside the Dan'a'yo set, so [[且爵]] (且 + 爵) takes its place as a graphemic substitute for the whole compound.
