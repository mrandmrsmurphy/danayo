## Aliases

### 棱
木 + 夌, "angle, edge"; a variant of 稜 and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-4-8; 12 strokes. Radical 075 木. It ranks 3506 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
