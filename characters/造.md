## Notes
- 形声 (per Shuowen): semantic [[Radical 162|辵]] ("walk") + phonetic [[告]]. Modern paleography (漢語多功能字庫) disputes this on the bronze-script evidence, instead analyzing the earliest forms as semantic 辵 + phonetic 屮 (no character page in this vault) + semantic 口 — documented here since the field follows the traditional Shuowen analysis rather than the more obscure alternative.
