## Notes
- 會意 of [[女]] ("woman") and a doubled basket/headdress element (variously analyzed by en.Wiktionary as hands [[爪]] holding a horn-shaped [[角]] load, or in the simplified later form as [[母]] flanked by two baskets) — a woman carrying baskets/burdens, extended to "to drag, haul, tether" (matching `korean_native: 끌` and the kun'yomi ひく/つなぐ), "to wear/don clothing" (a distinct Japanese-attested sense per ja.Wiktionary), and, as a homophone/loan, the name of a lunar-mansion constellation (婁宿) and a surname; zh.Wiktionary's own entry instead calls this composition 形聲 without naming a single external phonetic component, a documented cross-source disagreement.
