## Notes
- 會意 of [[女]] ("woman") and a doubled basket/headdress element (read as hands [[爪]] holding a horn-shaped [[角]] load, or in the simplified later form as [[母]] flanked by two baskets) — a woman carrying baskets/burdens, extended to "to drag, haul, tether" (matching `korean_native: 끌` and the kun'yomi ひく/つなぐ), "to wear/don clothing" (a Japanese-attested sense), and, as a homophone/loan, the name of a lunar-mansion constellation (婁宿) and a surname. An alternative analysis treats it as 形聲.
