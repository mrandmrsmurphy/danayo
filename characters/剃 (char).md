## Aliases

### 鬀
Alternative form of 剃 with the hair radical 髟 (髟 + phonetic 弟), "to shave" (Wiktionary lists it as an alternative form of 剃 and Unihan as a semantic variant). SKIP 2-10-7 (EDRDG); 17 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists. SKIP 2-10-7; 17 strokes. Radical 190 髟. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 鬄
Alternative form of 剃 with the hair radical 髟 (髟 + phonetic 易), "to shave"; elsewhere a literary word for "wig". SKIP 2-10-8 (EDRDG); 18 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists. SKIP 2-10-8; 18 strokes. Radical 190 髟. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 薙
Japanese jinmeiyō kanji (艹 + 雉), read てい as "to shave" besides its main sense "to weed, to mow"; listed as an alias of 剃 for the shaving sense. SKIP 2-3-13 (EDRDG); 16 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists. SKIP 2-3-13; 16 strokes. Radical 140 艸. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 剔
刂 + phonetic 易. In Chinese a character of its own ("to pick out, to scrape"), which Wiktionary also records as an alternative form of 剃, "to shave"; the page's own note calls 剃 "possibly a post-Han variant of 剔". Kept as an alias of 剃 by the project owner's decision, so that a search for 剔 lands here. It is on Old HSK 6 and the Korean Name list (척). SKIP 1-8-2 (EDRDG); 10 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists. SKIP 1-8-2; 10 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
