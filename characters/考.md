## Notes
- 会意 of [[Radical 125|耂]] ("old man with long hair, bent over" — the abbreviated form of [[老 (char)|老]]) + 丂 ("a walking cane," an alias of this very character, no separate page) — an elderly person leaning on a cane. Cognate to [[老 (char)|老]] (OC \*ruːʔ) — the classic textbook example of 転注 ("mutual annotation").

## Aliases

### 丂
A very old character (an axe handle or cane, the original of 柯 or 杖) and the phonetic element of 考; kept as an alias of 考 for search. SKIP 4-2-1 (EDRDG); 2 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists. SKIP 4-2-1; 2 strokes. Radical 001 一. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 攷
攴 + 丂, "to examine, to test", an alternative form of 考. SKIP 1-2-4 (EDRDG); 6 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists; the `mc_id` stays 776, 考's own rank. SKIP 1-2-4; 6 strokes. Radical 066 攴. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
