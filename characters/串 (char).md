## Notes
- 象形: a vertical line piercing two linked loops, depicting objects strung or threaded together — "to string, thread through," extended to "a skewer" and "a string/bunch of" (classifier). The graph has two distinct historical readings: chuàn (OC \*kʰjons, cognate with [[穿]] "to pierce through," the sense used here) and guàn (OC \*kroːns, cognate with [[貫]] "to penetrate, string of cash"). **Pronunciation is altered** — a pre-existing flag on this page (shared with the word page [[串]]) whose exact referent isn't specified in either page's history; likely points to the chuàn/guàn split above, but retained as documented rather than resolved by guesswork.
