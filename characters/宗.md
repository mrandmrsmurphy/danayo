## Notes
- 会意 of [[Radical 040|宀]] ("house, roof") and [[示]] ("spirit tablet, altar") — depicting an ancestral temple/shrine. Possible Proto-Sino-Tibetan link to "to build," cognate with Tibetan *rdzong* ("castle") and Burmese *hcaung* ("room/hall"); the administrative-division sense (as in Tibetan "dzong") is a separate loan from Tibetan *rdzong*, only distantly related to the native "ancestral temple" sense.
