## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*lonʔ): phonetic [[允]], with the lower element read as [[Radical 010|儿]] ("legs") — the same OC \*l- root that regularly surfaces as MC 以-initial, shared by 允/兗/沿/鉛; the original sense "nock of an arrow" is archaic and obsolete in standard use, and the character survives almost entirely as the name of [[兗州]]. Wiktionary names 允 as the phonetic and identifies no clear semantic component.
