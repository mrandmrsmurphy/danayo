## Notes
- 會意: [[大]] ("big") + [[申]] ("extend") — to cover, encompass; suddenly, abruptly; feeble. Also used as a classical substitute for 閹/阉 ("eunuch, castrate," matching the character's own `english` gloss and its stand-in [[奄人]]) and for 淹 ("to soak, tarry"). An alternative 形聲 analysis (abbreviated semantic 盍 + phonetic 甲) is also attested, but 甲's own OC reading (\*kraːb) doesn't plausibly explain 奄's OC \*ʔjomʔ, so the 會意 analysis was kept.
