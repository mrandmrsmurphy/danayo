## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*kaːl): semantic [[Radical 076|欠]] ("blow") + phonetic [[哥]] (OC \*kaːl) — song. Despite sharing 哥's exact reading here, the two are semantically unrelated, fully independent words — 哥 means "older brother" and keeps its own separate page.
- [SKIP-1-10-4](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-10-4.md) ([Stroke 14](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2014.md))
- 789th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 見|k]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 歌|ɑ]] → [ㄍㄜ](../syllables/ㄍㄜ.md)
- [Grade 1](../lookup/Grade%201.md), [HSK Beginner](../lookup/HSK/HSK%20Beginner.md), [Jōyō - Kyōiku](../lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kyōiku.md), [Korean MS](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20MS.md)

## Aliases

### 欨
句 + 欠, "to breathe upon; to smile"; a separate word kept as an alias of 歌 on the same derived sound. SKIP 1-5-4; 9 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 謌
言 + 哥, an alternative form of 歌, "song". SKIP 1-7-10; 17 strokes. Radical 149 言. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
