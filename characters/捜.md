## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[手 (char)|手]] ("hand") + phonetic 叟 (sōu, "old man," no vault page) — "to search, look for." 搜 is the traditional form. 廋 (sōu, "to hide, conceal," 广 semantic "building" — a distinct character occasionally used as a phonetic loan for 搜/捜 in classical texts, but not a true graphemic variant) was formerly wrongly listed as an alias — removed.
