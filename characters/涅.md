## Notes
- 形声 (OC *niːɡ, MC net̚): semantic [[水 (char)|水]] ("water") + [[土 (char)|土]] ("soil") + phonetic [[日 (char)|日]] (OC *njiɡ) — "black mud," used since antiquity as a black dye (famously in the Analects' 涅而不緇, "though steeped in a dark dye, [the white] is not turned black" — a metaphor for unshaken moral integrity); extended to the verb "to dye black, to blacken," and to "tattoo" by extension of the dyeing sense. Also the transliteration character in [[words/涅盤|涅槃]] "Nirvana." Dual-source confirmed genuine variants: [[湼]], [[㘿]]; two further en.Wiktionary-only candidates, 圼 and 𣵀, were excluded for lacking zh.Wiktionary corroboration.
