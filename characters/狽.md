## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*paːds): semantic [[Radical 094|犬]] ("dog") + phonetic [[貝]] — a legendary wolf-like beast with short forelegs and long hind legs that depends on the wolf 狼 to move; the pairing 狼狽 gave rise to the figurative sense "distressed, in a predicament." Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary).

## Aliases

### 狈
The Chinese simplified form of 狽, "a legendary animal". SKIP 1-3-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 7 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 䟺
A rare form meaning "to walk slowly"; kept as an alias of 狽. SKIP 1-7-7 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 14 strokes. Radical 157 足. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
