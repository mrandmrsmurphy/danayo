## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*paːds): semantic [[Radical 094|犬]] ("dog") + phonetic [貝 (char)](characters/貝%20(char).md) — a legendary wolf-like beast with short forelegs and long hind legs that depends on the wolf 狼 to move; the pairing 狼狽 gave rise to the figurative sense "distressed, in a predicament." Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary); "werewolf" was a genuine mistranslation, corrected in `english`.
