## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*poːbs, \*pʰoːbs): semantic [[Radical 085|氵]] ("water") + phonetic 巿 (OC \*pub) — not the look-alike [市](characters/市.md) "city/market" (OC \*djɯʔ); 巿 ("hem, knee-cover") is a separate character with no page of its own in this vault, analyzed as 一+巾. "Copious, abundant" (of rain or water flow).
