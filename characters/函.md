## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): originally written 圅, depicting an upside-down arrow held inside a quiver or case with one or two handles; en.Wiktionary treats this straightforwardly as a pictograph, while zh.Wiktionary's own entry describes a "phono-semantic structure" without identifying a distinct phonetic component, so the pictographic analysis is kept. Extended from "quiver" to "to contain, hold, envelope" (matching `korean_native: 지닐`), then to "box, case" and, by further extension, "letter" (來函 "incoming letter"). Genuine variants: 圅, 凾.
