## Notes
- 会意 of 林 ("vegetation," later 𦥑) + [[Radical 161|辰]] ("hoe") — to remove weeds; bronze inscriptions add a semantic 田, which became 囟 and merged into 曲 in the clerical script. To farm.
- [SKIP-2-6-7](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-6-7.md) ([Stroke 13](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2013.md))
- 638th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 泥|n]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 冬|uoŋ]] → [ㄋㄛㄫ](../syllables/ㄋㄛㄫ.md)
- [Grade 1](../lookup/Grade%201.md), [HSK Beginner](../lookup/HSK/HSK%20Beginner.md), [Jōyō - Kyōiku](../lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kyōiku.md), [Korean MS](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20MS.md)

## Aliases

### 农
Simplified Chinese form of 農 (Unihan: traditional variant 農). SKIP 2-2-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 6 strokes. Radical 014 冖. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 辳
A variant form of 農, written 曲 over 辰; Wiktionary gives no definition. SKIP 2-8-7 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 15 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 䢉
A rare variant form of 農; Wiktionary gives no definition. SKIP 2-13-7 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 20 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
