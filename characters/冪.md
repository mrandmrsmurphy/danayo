## Aliases

### 幂
Simplified Chinese form of 冪, with 旲 and 巾 below the cover. Radical 050 巾 (冪 itself is filed under 冖); SKIP 2-9-3 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 12 strokes. It has no rank in the Classical Chinese lists, so the `mc_id` 3463 is 冪's own.
