## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[犬 (char)|犬]] ("dog," reflecting the hunting origin of the sense — catching game with a dog) + phonetic 蒦 (this page's alias) — "to catch, seize, obtain." Do not confuse with near-homophone [[穫]] (harvest, cut grain), which shares the same phonetic 蒦; Simplified Chinese 获 simplifies both this character and 穫 to the same glyph, but it is deliberately **not** claimed as an alias of either here — see 获's own entry for the reasoning (an ambiguous two-way merge, permanently forbidden rather than aliased to one side).
- [SKIP-1-3-13](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-13.md) ([Stroke 16](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2016.md))

## Aliases

### 蒦
艹 over 隻 (the Shuowen form is 雈 + 又, "owl and hand"), "to catch, to capture (a bird)" and, historically, "to measure"; it is the original of 獲 and the phonetic of 獲, 穫, 蠖 and 護. It has the same derived Dan'a'yo sound as 獲, ㄏ⺢ㄎ (MC `ɣ` + `wɑk`), and the same sense, so it is an alias of 獲, which is the hub for the characters built on it (see Derived Characters). SKIP 2-3-10; 13 strokes. Radical 140 艸. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
