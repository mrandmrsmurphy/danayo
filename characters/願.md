## Aliases

### 愿
Simplified Chinese form of 願, and a separate older character meaning "honest, prudent". SKIP 2-10-4; 14 strokes. Radical 061 心. It ranks 1884 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.

### 𫖸
A rare variant form of 願; Wiktionary gives no definition. SKIP 1-10-6 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 16 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
