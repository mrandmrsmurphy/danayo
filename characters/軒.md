## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*kaːn): semantic 車 ("carriage") + phonetic [[干]] — originally a high-fronted, curtained carriage used by nobility; extended to "eaves, veranda" and generic "flats/apartments." Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary).
