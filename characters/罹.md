## Notes
- 會意 of [[Radical 122|网]] ("net"), [[Radical 061|忄]] ("heart"), and [[Radical 172|隹]] ("bird") — a bird trapped in a net, its capture felt as anxiety of heart; to be caught up in and suffer misfortune. Shuowen Jiezi calls the phonetic origin "unclear" (未詳) and separately notes the character was anciently often used interchangeably with [[離]].
