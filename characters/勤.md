## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*ɡɯn): semantic [[Radical 019|力]] ("strength, effort") + phonetic 堇 (OC \*ɡrɯn, \*kɯnʔ, "clay, dry earth" — no vault page; easily confused with the visually similar but unrelated [[菫]], "celery, aconite") — 力 grounds the meaning in "toil, exertion," combined with 堇's sound to yield "diligence, hard work."
