## Aliases

### 詹
By Murphy's declaration (2026-11-07), 旦 is treated as the new form of 詹, on the pattern of 膽 → 胆, where 旦 replaces the phonetic 詹 (shinjitai and simplified Chinese). Wiktionary records no such link between the two; 詹 itself (“verbose”) is the phonetic of 膽 and 瞻. SKIP 3-4-9; 13 strokes. Radical 149 言. It ranks 2309 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`, so the page keeps its own rank.
