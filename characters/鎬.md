### 鄗
高 + 阝, "a county in Hebei"; a place name built on 高 as 鎬 is, kept as an alias of 鎬 on the same derived sound. SKIP 1-10-3; 12 strokes. Radical 163 邑. It ranks 3171 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 滈
