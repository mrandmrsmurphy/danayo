## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 085|水]] ("water") + phonetic [[需]] — "to immerse, to moisten; wet, damp." 需 itself began as a person getting wet in the rain, and this character is that older meaning with the water radical added; Japanese keeps it as the verb 濡れる ("to get wet").
- Homophones (注音 ㄋ⼜ㄋ): none. The syllable ㄋ⼜ㄋ was an unused slot before this character.
- [SKIP-1-3-14](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-14.md) ([Stroke 17](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2017.md))
- 1885th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 日|ȵ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 虞|ɨo]] → [ㄋ⼜ㄋ](../syllables/ㄋ⼜ㄋ.md). **Strict derivation would give ㄋㄨ**, the reading of the other 日-initial 虞-final characters 儒 and 乳. The reading ㄋ⼜ㄋ was assigned by fiat (2026-11-04) from the second Cantonese reading nyun5 that dictionaries record for this character, so that the three characters do not share one syllable.
