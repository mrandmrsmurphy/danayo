- 形声 (OC \*l̥ʰiːmʔ, \*l̥ʰiːms): semantic [[心]] ("heart") + phonetic [[天]] (OC \*qʰl'iːn) — "to shame; ashamedly, with disgrace" (literary, self-deprecatory). Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary); the pre-existing prose had these two roles/glosses reversed, since corrected.
