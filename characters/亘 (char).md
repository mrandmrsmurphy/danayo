## Notes
- 會意 of [[Radical 007|二]] ("two," representing horizon lines) and 囘 (an enclosing/circular element, no character page in this vault) — depicting something turning within bounds; "to revolve; whirlpool" (obsolete/archaic sense). In ordinary modern use the glyph is identified instead with the visually similar 亙 ("to extend all the way through, span continuously"), but this vault keeps 亘 assigned to its original whirlpool sense per the one-meaning-per-character policy.
