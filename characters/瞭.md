## Notes
- 形聲 (OC \*reːw/\*reːwʔ): semantic [[Radical 109|目]] ("eye") + phonetic 尞 (pageless in this vault) — "clear; clear-sighted." Two Mandarin readings: liǎo ("clear, understanding," the primary sense stored here) and liào ("to gaze at," as in 瞭望). `aliases` lists [[了 (char)|了]] and 亮 (pageless in this vault) as semantic near-equivalents rather than orthographic variants.
