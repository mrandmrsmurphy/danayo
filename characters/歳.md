## Notes
- 形声 (OC *sqʰʷads): semantic 步 ("foot, movement" — no character page exists for it in this vault) + phonetic [[越 (char)|戉]] ("battle-axe," OC *ɢʷad) — the passing of time as movement; a year, one's age. The phonetic is 戉, not the look-alike 戌 (one of the 12 Earthly Branches), which plays no role in this character's etymology.
- [SKIP-2-4-9](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-4-9.md) ([Stroke 13](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2013.md))

### 歲
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 歳, "year, age"; this is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 2-4-9 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 13 strokes. It ranks 223 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
