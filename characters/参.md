## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[晶]] ("stars") + phonetic [[㐱]] — the Three Stars mansion of Chinese astronomy (Shuowen's analysis of the seal form). The bronze-script form instead shows a figure beneath three shining stars — 晶 + 光 + 彡 — with 彡 doubling as phonetic.
- [SKIP-2-2-6](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-2-6.md) ([Stroke 08](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2008.md))

## Aliases

### 參
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 参, "to take part; three stars"; this is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 2-2-9; 11 strokes. It ranks 701 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 叁
Formal (financial) numeral "three", a variant of 參 and a separate character kept as an alias of 参. SKIP 2-2-6 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 8 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 蔘
艹 + 參, "ginseng"; an alternative form of 參 kept as an alias. SKIP 2-3-11; 14 strokes. Radical 140 艸. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 弎
Archaic formal numeral "three"; kept as an alias of 参 for its meaning. SKIP 3-3-3; 6 strokes. Radical 056 弋. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 鰺
魚 + 參, "horse mackerel"; built on 參 and kept as an alias of 参. SKIP 1-11-11; 22 strokes. Radical 195 魚. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 驂
馬 + 參, "the flanking horses of a team"; built on 參 and kept as an alias of 参. SKIP 1-10-11; 21 strokes. Radical 187 馬. It ranks 2248 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.
