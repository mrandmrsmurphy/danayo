## Notes
- 會意: abbreviated 舂 ("to pound grain") + [禾](characters/禾%20(char).md) ("grain") — depicting husked grain; the oracle-bone form was more complex (午 "pestle" + 廾 "two hands" + two 禾 "grain"), later fusing into the modern shape. Now used exclusively as the name of the Qin dynasty/state and as a surname — too bounded to appear alone.
