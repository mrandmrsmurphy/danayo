## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*teŋ): originally an [象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md) pictogram of a ding-vessel, identical to [[鼎]] (OC \*kleːŋʔ) and phonetically borrowed for "to divine"; later [[Radical 025|卜]] ("divination crack") was added as a semantic radical to disambiguate, giving 形声 卜 + phonetic 鼎. The bottom of 鼎 was stylized into the unrelated [[Radical 154|貝]] during the Warring States period — the Kangxi radical this character is filed under today, though it plays no real semantic role.
- [SKIP-2-2-7](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-2-7.md) ([Stroke 09](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2009.md))
