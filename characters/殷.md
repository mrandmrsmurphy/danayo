## Notes
- 會意 of [[㐆]] ("person with swollen belly") and [[Radical 079|殳]] ("hand holding a weapon/club") — the two combine in oracle-bone forms to depict a resonant blow, associated with grand percussion music, extended to "substantial, numerous, prosperous"; also lent as the name for the Shang dynasty's later capital era (殷代/殷商), and, under separate tonal readings not used by this vault, "deep red" (yān) and "rumbling thunder" (yǐn).
