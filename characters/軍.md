## Notes
- 会意 of [[Radical 159|車]] ("chariot") and 勹 ("to surround," no vault page) — an encircling formation of chariots; "army, military." Genuinely disputed: some sources instead read this as 形声 with phonetic 勹 (an original form of 螾), or even read 車 itself as a corrupted phonetic form of 熊.
- [SKIP-2-2-7](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-2-7.md) ([Stroke 09](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2009.md))
