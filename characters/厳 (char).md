## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*ŋam): semantic 喦/吅 ("talkative," no vault page; simplified to 𭕄 in the shinjitai, the same pattern as 單→単) + phonetic [[敢]] (OC \*klaːmʔ, "daring," retained intact and unchanged in the shinjitai) — the "solemn, stern" sense may be a phonetic-loan development rather than transparent from 敢's own meaning.
- [SKIP-3-5-12](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-3/SKIP-3-5-12.md) ([Stroke 17](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2017.md))
- 817th most used character in Classical Chinese, the rank of its alias 嚴 (厳 itself is not on the lists). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 疑|ŋ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 凡|ɨɐm]] → [⼄ㄇ](../syllables/⼄ㄇ.md)
- [Grade 3](../lookup/Grade%203.md), [HSK No](../lookup/HSK/HSK%20No.md), [Jōyō - Kyōiku](../lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kyōiku.md), [Korean MS](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20MS.md)

## Aliases

### 嚴
The traditional form; Wiktionary lists 嚴, 厳 and 严 together as one character, and 厳 is its Japanese shinjitai (with 𭕄 for 吅, as 単 is from 單). It carries the classical rank of 817, which is the page's `mc_id`. SKIP 2-6-14; 20 strokes. Radical 030 口. It ranks 817 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 严
The simplified Chinese form of 嚴; Wiktionary lists 嚴, 厳 and 严 together as forms of one character. SKIP 4-7-1 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 7 strokes. Radical 001 一. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 儼
A separate literary character (亻 + 嚴, "grave, majestic; as if"), the source of the word 厳然's alias 儼然; Wiktionary's pages do not link it to 厳. Its rank of 3663 is a higher number than 817. SKIP 1-2-20; 21 strokes. Radical 009 人. It ranks 3663 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.

### 𠑊
The Japanese extended shinjitai form of 儼 (亻 + 厳); a rare glyph, kept as a bare mention. SKIP 1-2-17 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 19 strokes. Radical 009 人. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
