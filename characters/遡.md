## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*sŋaːɡs): semantic [[Radicals/Radical 162|辶]] ("movement") + phonetic [[朔]] (OC \*sŋraːɡ). English Wiktionary says the original character is 㴑 (water radical), and that 遡 is a variant form of 溯, "to go upstream; to go against a current; to trace back; to recall". It glosses Japanese 遡 as "go upstream; retrace the past".
- [SKIP-3-3-10](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-3/SKIP-3-3-10.md) ([Stroke 13](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2013.md))
- 3917th most used character in Classical Chinese, the rank of its alias 泝 (遡 itself is not on the lists). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 心|s]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 模|uo]] → [ㄙㄛ](../syllables/ㄙㄛ.md)

## Aliases

### 泝
氵 + 斥, "to go upstream"; a variant of 遡, kept as an alias; it ranks 3917 and sets the page's `mc_id`. SKIP 1-3-5; 8 strokes. Radical 085 水. It ranks 3917 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 溯
氵 + 朔, "to go upstream; to trace back"; the form Chinese uses for 遡. SKIP 1-3-10; 13 strokes. Radical 085 水. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
