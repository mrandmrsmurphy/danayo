## Aliases

### 戰
The traditional form, of which 戦 is the Japanese shinjitai ([Wiktionary: 戰](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E6%88%B0)); 戰 has OC \*tjans. SKIP 1-12-4; 16 strokes. It ranks 270 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 战
The simplified-Chinese form of 戰, listed beside 戦 on Wiktionary ([Wiktionary: 战](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E6%88%98)). SKIP 1-5-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 9 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
