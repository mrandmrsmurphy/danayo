## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 086|火]] ("fire") + phonetic 粦 (this character's own origin form, now listed as an alias) — will-o'-the-wisp, phosphorescent fire; extended to the element phosphorus. 粦 independently gave rise to unrelated siblings sharing the same phonetic: [[隣 (char)|隣]] "next door," [[鱗 (char)|鱗]] "scale," and [[麟]] "mythical beast." Abbreviation for <ruby>[[燐素]]<rt>ㄌㄧㄋㄙㄛ</rt></ruby> "phosphorus" in periodic-table formulae — see [[Periodic Table]].

## Aliases

### 磷
石 + 粦, "phosphorus; will-o'-the-wisp"; the form Chinese uses for 燐. SKIP 1-5-12; 17 strokes. Radical 112 石. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 鏻
金 + 粦, "phosphonium"; a chemical-name variant built on 粦, kept as an alias of 燐 on the same derived sound. SKIP 1-8-12; 20 strokes. Radical 167 金. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 粦
The phosphorescence component, "will-o'-the-wisp"; the original form of 燐. SKIP 2-6-7; 12 strokes. Radical 119 米. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 璘
玉 + 粦, "luster of jade"; a separate word built on 粦 as its phonetic, kept as an alias of 燐 on the same derived sound. SKIP 1-4-12; 16 strokes. Radical 096 玉. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
