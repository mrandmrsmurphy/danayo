## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 086|火]] ("fire") + phonetic 粦 (this character's own origin form, now listed as an alias) — will-o'-the-wisp, phosphorescent fire; extended to the element phosphorus. 粦 independently gave rise to unrelated siblings sharing the same phonetic: [[隣 (char)|隣]] "next door," [[鱗 (char)|鱗]] "scale," and [[麟]] "mythical beast."
- [SKIP-1-4-12](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-4-12.md) ([Stroke 16](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2016.md))
