## Aliases

### 峯
A variant form of 峰, "peak". SKIP 2-3-7; 10 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 烽
"Beacon fire"; kept as an alias of 峰, which supplies its reading (fēng, ホウ, 봉, phong) because 烽 shares the phonetic 夆. Radical 086 (火); SKIP 1-4-7; 11 strokes. It ranks 3092 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
