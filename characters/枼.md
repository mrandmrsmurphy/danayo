## Notes
- 会意 of [[Radical 075|木]] ("tree") and a depiction of thin, flat leaves above it — thin flat objects growing from a tree; "leaf, thin and flat sheet."

## Aliases

### 枽
A variant of 枼 written with 十 and 廿 above 木 (the Japanese dictionaries list it as a form of the same character). SKIP 2-5-4, the same as 枼; 9 strokes. It has no rank in the Classical Chinese lists; the `mc_id` 5336 is 枼's own long-tail rank.
