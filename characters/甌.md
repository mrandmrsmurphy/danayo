## Notes
- 形声 (OC *qoː): semantic [[Radical 098|瓦]] ("tile, earthenware") + phonetic [[区]] (OC *qoː) — the vault's form 区 is the parent of the traditional 區 "vessel", which is why the phonetic is cited as 区. English Wiktionary also analyses the character as ideogrammic, since 區 itself means "vessel"; the earthenware radical then says what it is made of. The meanings are a small bowl, small cup or small tray, and as an abbreviation of 溫州 "Wenzhou" (the Ou River 甌江 runs through it; the old Ou region of 東甌 and 甌越, and the dialect 甌語, are named from it; the history behind these names is from memory, not re-checked), and also a surname; further compounds listed at English Wiktionary include 建甌 and 金甌. Its Middle Chinese reading is 影 ʔ + 侯 əu (from memory, 烏侯切, not re-checked), the same initial and final as its phonetic sibling 欧, whose Mandarin, Cantonese, Korean and Vietnamese readings all match those given here (ōu, au1, 구, âu), so the Dan'a'yo sound is the same ㄛㄨ. The simplified form 瓯 is kept as an alias. In the vault 甌 appears only in the word 金甌 "golden chalice; the nation; Cà Mau", which is its stand-in.
- Homophones (注音 ㄛㄨ): [[欧]] "Europe", [[殴]] "assault", [[呕]] "vomit", [[𧦅]] "eulogize", [[偶]] "idol; image", [[鴎 (char)|鴎]] "seagull" (see also the word [[鴎]]).
