## Notes
- 会意 of 聿 ("hand holding a brush"), 灬 ("bristles"), and [[皿 (char)|皿]] ("dish") — cleaning a dish with a brush: "empty; to exhaust." (Neither 聿 nor 灬 has its own character page in this vault. The character's own Kangxi indexing radical, `尸`, is not one of these three components — it reflects the modern simplified 尽 glyph's shape rather than 盡's traditional-form etymology.)
- open question, unresolved: should this be written 侭 instead, by the Extended Shinjitai Rule? (儘/盡/侭 are all already listed as `aliases`.)
