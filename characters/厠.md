## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*sʰrɯɡs): semantic [[Radical 027|厂]] (here a simplified stand-in for 广, "building, shelter") + phonetic [[則]] — a small structure set apart from the main dwelling.
- [SKIP-3-2-9](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-3/SKIP-3-2-9.md) ([Stroke 11](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2011.md))
- 2787th most used character in Classical Chinese, the rank of its alias 廁 (厠 itself is not on the lists). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 初|t͡ʃʰ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 之|ɨ]] → [ㄑㄧ](../syllables/ㄑㄧ.md)
- [Grade Advanced](../lookup/Grade%20Advanced.md), [Old HSK 2](../lookup/HSK/Old%20HSK%202.md), [Hyōgai](../lookup/Japanese/Hyōgai.md), [Korean Name ㅊ](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20Name%20ㅊ.md)
- The preferred everyday word is 便所 (Japanese べんじょ); 厠 is the more literary/formal term.

## Aliases

### 廁
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 厠, written 广 + 則. SKIP 3-3-9; 12 strokes. Radical 053 广. This form is the one the Classical Chinese lists rank, so the `mc_id` 2787 is 廁's rank.

### 厕
Simplified Chinese form of 廁. SKIP 3-2-6 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 8 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
