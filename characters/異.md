## Notes
- 会意: depicts a person wearing a mask ([[Radical 102|田]], here representing a face-covering disguise rather than "field," compare [[鬼]] where 田 likewise derives from a mask/face) over the face — "disguised, uncommon; different." An alternative theory reads it as a person carrying something atop the head, the original form of [[戴 (char)|戴]] ("to carry on the head"). The modern glyph's apparent 田+共 decomposition is non-etymological.
