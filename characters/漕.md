## Notes
- 形声 (OC *zluː, MC dzaw/dzawH): semantic [[水 (char)|水]] ("water") + phonetic [[曹]] — "to transport (grain) by water/canal" (historically the 漕運 grain-transport system along China's canals), extended to "to row (a boat)." en.Wiktionary's own single-source candidate variant, [[𤅍]], lacks zh.Wiktionary corroboration and was excluded.
