## Notes
- [List of 会意](lookup/List%20of%20会意.md): the ancient form is 㝵 (no vault page), composed of [[貝]] ("cowry") + [[又 (char)|又]] ("hand") — to pick up a cowry, to obtain valuables. [[Radical 060|彳]] was later added to show the cowry being picked up on the road; the hand (又) was replaced with 寸 in Qin script, and the cowry (貝) simplified to 目 then corrupted to 旦 in clerical script. "To obtain, to get."
