## Aliases

### 熔
火 + 容, "to melt"; built on 容 and kept as an alias of 溶. SKIP 1-4-10; 14 strokes. Radical 086 火. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 鎔
金 + 容, "mould"; built on 容 and kept as an alias of 溶. SKIP 1-8-10; 18 strokes. Radical 167 金. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
