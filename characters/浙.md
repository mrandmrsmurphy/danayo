## Notes
- 形声 (OC *ʔljed): semantic [[Radical 085|水]] ("water") + phonetic [[折]] (OC *l'eːl, *ʔljed, *ɦljed) — the Zhe River, the old name of the Qiantang River of eastern China (English Wiktionary: first attested in the Classic of Mountains and Seas; from memory, not re-checked), and from it a short form of the province 浙江 Zhejiang. Its Middle Chinese reading is 章 t͡ɕ + 薛 iᴇt (旨熱切, English Wiktionary), the same initial and final as its phonetic 折, so the Dan'a'yo sound is the same ㄐㄝㄊ; the other siblings of the series that already hold this sound are 哲 and 澈, and the Japanese 漢音 セツ and Korean 절 match 折 as well. The Japanese 呉音 is せち. The character is Hyōgai (not on the Jōyō or Jinmeiyō lists) and a Korean name hanja (eumhun 강 이름 절); English Wiktionary also lists the variant 淛, which is not in the vault. 浙 is a 名専字: in the vault it appears only in the province name 浙江, which does not legitimize it, so it has no use of its own in Dan'a'yo.
- Homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄊ): [[折]] "fold", [[哲]] "philosophy, wisdom", [[澈]] "penetrate", [[節 (char)|節]] "node" (see also the word [[節]]).
