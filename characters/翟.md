## Aliases

### 狄
犭 + 火, "the Di people" (the Zhou-era name for the northern tribes), "low servant" and a surname (狄仁傑); Wiktionary lists it as an alternative form of 翟 and as the phonetic of 荻. It has the same derived Dan'a'yo sound as 翟, ㄉㄝㄎ (MC `d` + `ek`), so it is an alias of 翟, which is the hub for the characters built on it (see Derived Characters). SKIP 1-3-4 (EDRDG); 7 strokes. It ranks 867 in the Classical Chinese lists, better than 翟's 1202, so it sets the page's `mc_id`. SKIP 1-3-4; 7 strokes. Radical 094 犬.
