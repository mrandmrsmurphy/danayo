## Aliases

### 亿
The simplified Chinese form of 億 (亻 + 乙); Wiktionary's 亿 page names 億 as its traditional form, and it is also a gongche music notation symbol. SKIP 1-2-1 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 3 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
