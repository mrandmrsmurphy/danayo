## Notes
- [List of 象形](../lookup/List%20of%20象形.md) (per Shuowen — arguably 指事): a vessel full of plants (𠁳, containing 丰 "abundance") atop another vessel [[Radical 151|豆]] — "abundant, plentiful." Explicitly unrelated to 豊 as an alternate form of 禮 ("ceremony," from 玨+壴), despite the visual merger that makes this glyph stand in for both senses in Japanese shinjitai; the modern top component is popularly (if inaccurately) described as resembling [[曲]].
- [SKIP-2-6-7](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-6-7.md) ([Stroke 13](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2013.md))
- 848th most used character in Classical Chinese (rank belongs to the traditional form 豐). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 滂|pʰ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 東三|ɨuŋ]] → [ㄆㄨㄫ](../syllables/ㄆㄨㄫ.md)
- [Grade 2](../lookup/Grade%202.md), [HSK Beginner](../lookup/HSK/HSK%20Beginner.md), [Jōyō - Kyōiku](../lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kyōiku.md), [Korean MS](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20MS.md)
- 代用字: 鱧 ("eel") borrows this character's reading, since no dedicated Dan'a'yo word/character exists for it.

## Aliases

### 豐
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 豊, "luxuriant, abundant". SKIP 2-11-7; 18 strokes. It ranks 848 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 丰
"Lush, flourishing" and "plump, good-looking"; the simplified form of 豐 and a separate older character, kept as an alias of 豊. SKIP 4-4-3; 4 strokes. Radical 002 丨. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 夆
夂 + 丰, "to resist, to meet"; built on 丰 and kept as an alias of 豊 so it has somewhere to be written. SKIP 2-3-4; 7 strokes. Radical 034 夂. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 鱧
魚 + 豐, "snakehead" (the fish); built on 豐 as phonetic and kept as an alias of 豊. SKIP 1-11-13; 24 strokes. Radical 195 魚. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
