## Aliases

### 啚
口 over 㐭, "a granary outside the city boundary, hence borderland, countryside": the original form of 鄙 (Wiktionary), which later added 邑 ("city"). It is also the component of 図 (囗 around 啚). It has the same derived Dan'a'yo sound as 鄙 for that reading, ㄈㄧㄜ (MC `p` + `ɣiɪ`), and the same meaning, so it is an alias of 鄙, which is the hub for the characters built on it (see Derived Characters). SKIP 2-5-6 (EDRDG); 11 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists, so `mc_id` stays 1101. SKIP 2-5-6; 11 strokes. Radical 030 口. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
