## Aliases

### 畠
A Japanese-made character (国字, 白 + 田) for a dry field, as opposed to 田, a flooded paddy; the vault treats it as a form of 田 and redirects it there. Neither Wiktionary page links the two. SKIP 2-5-5; 10 strokes. Radical 106 白. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 甸
No link to 田 found on Wiktionary; 甸 (人 + 田, "suburbs of the capital; fields") is a separate literary character kept as an alias because it shares the field component. SKIP 3-2-5; 7 strokes. It ranks 2641 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.
