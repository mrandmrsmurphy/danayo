## Aliases

### 綵
糸 + 采, "varicolored silk"; a separate word built on 采 as 彩 is, kept as an alias of 彩 on the same derived sound. SKIP 1-6-8; 14 strokes. Radical 120 糸. It ranks 3188 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
