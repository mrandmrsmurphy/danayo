## Aliases

### 悌
忄 + 弟, "brotherly love, respect for elders"; a separate word built on 弟, kept as an alias of 弟 on the same derived sound. SKIP 1-3-7; 10 strokes. Radical 061 心. It ranks 2162 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.

### 棣
木 + 隶, "Kerria; a cherry tree"; kept as an alias of 弟 on the same derived sound (the brotherhood sense of 棣). SKIP 1-4-8; 12 strokes. Radical 075 木. It ranks 3660 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.

### 鵜
弟 + 鳥, "pelican"; a bird name built on 弟 as its phonetic, kept as an alias of 弟 on the same derived sound. SKIP 1-7-11; 18 strokes. Radical 196 鳥. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
