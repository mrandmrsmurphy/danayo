## Notes
- 會意 of [[倝]] ("branch, tree trunk," an abbreviated form of [[榦]]) and [[Radical 062|戈]] ("dagger-axe") — a multi-pronged, branch-like weapon; zh.Wiktionary instead frames 戟 as 形聲 without naming a specific phonetic component, a cross-source disagreement resolved here in favor of en.Wiktionary's more precise ideogrammic breakdown. A halberd combining a crescent blade with a spear tip, extended to the verbal senses "to pierce, stab" and "to splay (fingers)." Genuine variant: 㦸.
