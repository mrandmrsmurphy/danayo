## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 130|肉]] ("flesh") + phonetic [[乏]] (OC *bob) — "to wink," a graphemic variant of [[眨]] ("to blink," which swaps in the 目 "eye" semantic instead); this vault records 眨's own Old HSK 6 entry under this page since 眨 has no independent page of its own. Attested almost solely through Vietnamese Nôm readings; no genuine Mandarin/Cantonese frequency data or Japanese on'yomi exists for this rare glyph, so `japanese: BOU` is inherited from the phonetic component 乏 by convention rather than independently attested.
