## Aliases

### 謳
言 + 區, "to sing, to eulogize"; the standard form of the word 𧦅 writes. SKIP 1-7-11; 18 strokes. It ranks 2973 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 赞
"To praise"; the simplified Chinese form of 贊/讚, kept as an alias of 𧦅 on the shared meaning of praise. SKIP 2-6-10 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 16 strokes. Radical 154 貝. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 讴
Simplified Chinese form of 謳. SKIP 1-2-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 6 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
