## Notes
- 形声/会意 (OC \*l'iːns): semantic [[Radical 173|雨]] ("rain") + phonetic [[申]] (OC \*hlin, itself pictorially "lightning," so also analyzable as ideogrammic) — lightning, electricity.
- [SKIP-2-5-8](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-5-8.md) ([Stroke 13](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2013.md))
- 2111th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 定|d]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 先開|en]] → [ㄉㄝㄋ](syllables/ㄉㄝㄋ.md)
- [Grade 1](lookup/Grade%201.md), [HSK Beginner](lookup/HSK/HSK%20Beginner.md), [Jōyō - Kyōiku](lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kyōiku.md), [Korean MS](lookup/Korean/Korean%20MS.md)
- lightning is technically <ruby>[[閃電]]<rt>ㄙㄝㄇㄉㄝㄋ</rt></ruby>, but is normally just called [[雷電]]
