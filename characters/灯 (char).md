## Aliases

### 燈
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 灯: 火 + 登 as phonetic where 灯 uses 丁; Wiktionary calls 灯 its variant form, the Japanese shinjitai and the Chinese simplified form. Japanese kept 燈 in the tōyō kanji list until 1981. SKIP 1-4-12; 16 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
