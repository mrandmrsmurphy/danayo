## Notes
- 形声 (OC *ʔsib): semantic [[手]] ("hand") + phonetic [[咠]] (OC *ʔsib) — to bow with hands clasped in front of the chest, a ritual gesture of respect; extended to "to decline politely, modestly refuse." Part of the same phonetic series as 楫/檝/葺/輯/緝, but no dual-source-confirmed variant of 揖 itself was found.
