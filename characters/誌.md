## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 149|言]] ("words, speech") + phonetic [[志 (char)|志]] — "to record, chronicle," extended to "symbolize, mark," and (as a noun) "magazine, journal." 志 itself is a legitimate alternate form in the "record" sense (e.g. 三國志), 言 having been added to 誌 later for clarity; 𰵧 is a further attested rare variant, with no vault page of its own.
