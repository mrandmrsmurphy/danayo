## Aliases

### 构
Simplified Chinese form of 構, written 木 + 勾. SKIP 1-4-4 (EDRDG); 8 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists; the `mc_id` 2327 is 構's own rank. SKIP 1-4-4; 8 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 冓
𠀎 over 冉, 10 strokes, "to interlock": two fish with their mouths facing each other, the original script of 遘 "to meet" (the Shuowen reading, "crossly accumulated woods", is the sense 構 builds on). It is the phonetic of 構, 溝, 購 and 講. It has the same derived Dan'a'yo sound as 構, ㄍㄛㄨ (MC `k` + `əu`), so it is an alias of 構, which is the hub for the characters built on it (see Derived Characters). SKIP 2-5-5 (EDRDG); 10 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists, so `mc_id` stays 2327. SKIP 2-5-5; 10 strokes. Radical 013 冂. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
