## Aliases

### 邨
Wiktionary's 邨 page calls it an alternative form of 村 ("village; hamlet"), and 村's page lists 邨 as an alternative form. It is a late graph, absent from the Shuowen, and Ogawa treats it as a variant of 杶, which stood in phonetically for "village". Searchers still type it, and 村塾 is also spelled 邨塾. SKIP 1-4-3; 7 strokes. Radical 163 邑. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
