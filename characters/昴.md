## Notes
- 形声 (OC *mruːʔ): semantic [[日]] ("sun") + phonetic [[卯]] (OC *mruːʔ) — the Pleiades star cluster; specifically the fourth of the Twenty-Eight Mansions (二十八宿) in Chinese astronomy, under the White Tiger of the west. The Japanese kun'yomi すばる ("to bunch together") describes the cluster's visible appearance and is the origin of the Subaru car brand's name and logo.
