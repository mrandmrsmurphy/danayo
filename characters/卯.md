## Aliases

### 夘
Japanese variant of 卯, written 夕 + 卩. Radical 036 夕; SKIP 1-3-2, the same as 卯; 5 strokes. It has no rank in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 戼
Ancient variant of 卯. Radical 063 戶; SKIP 1-3-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 7 strokes. It has no rank in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 卬
A different character (áng, "I; to look up") that is written as 卯 in Dan'a'yo; it is the phonetic component of 仰, 昂 and 迎 (see Derived Characters). SKIP 1-2-2; 4 strokes. Ranked 1835 in the Classical Chinese lists, which does not beat 卯's own rank, so the `mc_id` 1001 stays 卯's.
