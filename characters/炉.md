## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 086|火]] ("fire") + phonetic [[戸]] — "stove, furnace, hearth." 炉 is a Japanese shinjitai/Chinese simplified form that replaces the traditional phonetic 盧 with 戸; 爐 is the traditional form. Formerly the frontmatter and one duplicate Notes bullet incorrectly cited the traditional phonetic 盧, while a second duplicate bullet correctly cited 戸 — consolidated to the correct, glyph-matching component.
- [SKIP-1-4-4](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-4-4.md) ([Stroke 8](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%208.md))
- Confirmed absent from the Classical Chinese corpus ranking (neither 炉 nor 爐 appears in `lookup/CC/CC 0000.md` through `CC 3000.md`); `mc_id` corrected from a fabricated, out-of-range value (`4539`, beyond the corpus's actual ~4000-entry ceiling) to `0`.
