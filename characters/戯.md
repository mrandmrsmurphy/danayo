## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*qʰrals): semantic [[戈]] (“halberd”) + phonetic 䖒 (xī; an obsolete component, no vault page) — originally referring to the wing or flank of an army, later extended to “play, game, trick.”

## Aliases

### 戲
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 戯, "to play, to jest"; this is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 1-13-4; 17 strokes. It ranks 1299 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 戱
Variant form of 戯, "to play". SKIP 3-4-12 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 16 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 戏
Simplified Chinese form of 戲. SKIP 1-2-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 6 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
