## Aliases

### 營
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 営, "camp, to manage". SKIP 2-8-9; 17 strokes. Radical 086 火. It ranks 875 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 营
Simplified Chinese form of 營. SKIP 2-3-10 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 13 strokes. Radical 140 艸. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
