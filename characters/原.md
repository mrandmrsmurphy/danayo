## Notes
- 会意 of [[Radical 027|厂]] ("cliff") and [[泉]] ("spring") — a spring emerging from a cliff-face, the pictorial source of "origin, source." "Spring bursting from a cliff" → "source" → "origin, original, basic, primary" (a sense that may also overlap semantically with 元); the separate "plain, plateau" sense is attested in classical usage, with 塬 (no vault page) later splitting off as a dedicated variant graph for that sense (esp. loess-plateau terrain).
