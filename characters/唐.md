## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*lˤaŋ): phonetic [[庚]] + semantic [口](characters/口%20(char).md) ("mouth," used here only as a differentiating mark, cf. 古/固, not for its literal meaning) — "the Tang dynasty; China" by extension; a name-only character with no independent stand-in, since two genuine compounds ([[唐突]] "blunt, presumptuous" and [[唐詩]] "Tang poetry") cite it with distinct, unrelated senses — flagged rather than forced to one.
