## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*ŋan): semantic 辵 ("walk") + phonetic [言](characters/言%20(char).md) — originally "to meet" (archaic). Later borrowed for the proximal demonstrative "this" through confusion in medieval handwriting with 遮; that modern sense is not used in Dan'a'yo, which employs native 此 for "this" instead.
