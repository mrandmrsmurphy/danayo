## Notes
- 會意兼形聲 (OC *khaːŋ): a person [[人]] wearing sheep-horns [[Radical 123|羊]] on the head, depicting the sheep-herding Qiang people of the west — Shuowen's own gloss explicitly notes 羊 serves double duty as both the semantic image and the phonetic; also the name of the muntjac deer (山羌) and, in Chu Ci poetry, an obsolete meaningless sentence-initial exclamatory particle (matching the kun'yomi あ, "alas/oh").
