## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 085|水]] ("water") + phonetic [[冘]] (no vault page) — "to sink, submerge," extended to "deep, heavy (of mood), silent." 沉 is a standard variant form (with its own separate Classical Chinese corpus entry) and the more common modern simplified rendering. 瀋 is the traditional form specific to 沈's secondary reading (Shěn, a surname and the historical short name for Shenyang, e.g. 瀋陽/沈陽) — a distinct-reading branch of the same glyph, not a separate character, kept as an alias.
- [SKIP-1-3-4](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-4.md) ([Stroke 7](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%207.md))
