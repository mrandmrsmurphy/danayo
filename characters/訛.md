## Aliases

### 讹
The Chinese simplified form of 訛, "erroneous". SKIP 1-2-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 6 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 吪
口 + 化, "to move, to act"; built on 化, the phonetic of 訛, and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-3-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 7 strokes. Radical 030 口. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 䚰
A rare character meaning "to prepare"; kept as an alias of 訛. SKIP 1-7-2 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 9 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
