## Aliases

### 鮭
魚 + 圭, "salmon, blowfish"; built on 圭 as phonetic and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-11-6; 17 strokes. Radical 195 魚. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 鲑
Simplified Chinese form of 鮭. SKIP 1-8-6 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 14 strokes. Radical 195 魚. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 膎
月 + 奚, "dried meat"; built on 圭 as phonetic and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-4-10; 14 strokes. Radical 130 肉. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 奎
大 + 圭, "the Legs constellation"; built on 圭 as phonetic and kept as an alias. SKIP 2-3-6; 9 strokes. Radical 037 大. It ranks 3206 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.
