## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[缶]] ("vessel/container") + phonetic [[肉 (char)|肉]] (OC \*njuɡ) — "earthenware jar, pitcher" (obsolete). Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary) as genuinely polyphonic: this vault's `mandarin`/`middle_chinese_*` data uses the *yóu* "pitcher" reading, but 䍃 is also read yáo for a second, distinct "kiln for firing pottery" sense that overlaps semantically with the independently-paged [[窯 (char)|窯]] (yáo) — the two are not documented as variants of each other, just thematically adjacent senses of a two-reading character. This vault consistently uses only the *yóu*/"pitcher" reading and sense throughout.
