## Notes
- 形声 (OC *kʷlɯʔ, *kʷlɯs): semantic [[火 (char)|火]] ("fire") + phonetic [[久]] ("long duration") — applying heat for an extended period, the therapeutic practice of moxibustion, most commonly cited in [[words/艾灸|艾灸]] "moxibustion with mugwort" and 針灸 "acupuncture and moxibustion." zh.Wiktionary's own 異體字 listing names 久 itself and 㡱 as variants, but 久 has extensive independent use in this vault (excluded per the alias-is-parent-form convention) and 㡱 lacks en.Wiktionary corroboration — neither added to `aliases`.
