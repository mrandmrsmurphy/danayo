## Notes
- 象形 ([List of 象形](../lookup/List%20of%20象形.md)): a person (亻) standing on soil (土) — "good" (Shuowen: 善也); the original form of [[挺]] ("to stick out, straighten"). Also a variant of 壬, [[王 (char)|王]], and [[徴]], and an ancient form of [[徴]] (all three already have independent vault pages, correctly excluded from this page's own `aliases`); also a variant of 莛/葶 (both pageless, hosted as this page's own aliases), which supplies the `english: stalk` gloss actually used here. Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary). hvdic.thivien.net's sole Vietnamese reading, `sính` ("skillful, talented"), belongs to an apparently disconnected historical sense-cluster from the "stalk/stand" meanings documented here — kept as the character's only attested Vietnamese reading regardless.

## Aliases

### 莛
艸 + 廷, "an erect flower stalk"; kept as an alias of 𡈼 on the same derived sound. SKIP 2-3-7; 9 strokes. Radical 140 艸. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 葶
艸 + 亭, "a stalk (as in 葶藶)"; an alternative form of 莛, kept as an alias of 𡈼 on the same derived sound. SKIP 2-3-9; 12 strokes. Radical 140 艸. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
