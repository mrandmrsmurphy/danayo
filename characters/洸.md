## Notes
- 形声 (OC *kʷaːŋ, MC kwɑŋ): semantic [[水 (char)|水]] ("water") + phonetic [[光]] — primarily "to sparkle, glitter" (of light on water), extended to a "mighty, dazzling" appearance (as in 洸洸). The Korean gloss `korean_native: 성낼` ("to flare up in anger") traces to a distinct classical sense (武也/怒貌, "valiant"/"angry appearance") recorded in the 康熙字典 and reflected in the Shijing's 邶風·谷風 ("有洸有潰"), not documented as a primary sense by either en.Wiktionary or zh.Wiktionary — genuinely traceable, but left out of `english` per this vault's dual-source bar. zh.Wiktionary's own 異體字 candidate, [[潢]], lacks en.Wiktionary corroboration and was excluded.
