## Notes
- 會意 of [[Radical 165|釆]] ("to distinguish, discern") and [[Radical 061|心]] ("heart, mind") — Shuowen: "詳盡也。从心从釆" ("thorough; from 心 and 釆"), a mind that discerns thoroughly, extended to "all, entirely" (the sense Dan'a'yo draws on, matching `korean_native: 다` and the kun'yomi ことごとく) alongside the original "to know/comprehend fully" sense; also a surname.
