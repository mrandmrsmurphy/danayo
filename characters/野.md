## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*laːʔ, \*ɦljaʔ): semantic [[Radical 166|里]] ("village" — itself 田 "field" + 土 "earth") + phonetic [[予]] (OC \*la, \*laʔ) — countryside, wilderness, open country. By evil substitution, 別墅 must become <ruby>[[別野]]<rt>ㄅㄝㄊ⼘</rt></ruby> ("villa").

## Aliases

### 墅
田 + 土 + 予, "villa, country house"; a separate word kept as an alias of 野 on the same derived sound. SKIP 2-11-3; 14 strokes. Radical 032 土. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 埜
林 + 土, "open country, wilderness"; the older form of 野. SKIP 2-8-3; 11 strokes. Radical 032 土. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 壄
Variant form of 野, "field, wilderness". SKIP 2-13-3; 16 strokes. Radical 032 土. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
