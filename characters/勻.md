## Notes
- 形声 (OC *ɢʷin): semantic [[呂]] ("two gold plates," a unit of weight related to 鈞) — long since corrupted into the modern-looking [[二 (char)|二]] — + phonetic [[Radical 020|勹]], itself the corrupted written form of an ancient phonetic ancestor related to 螾, shared with sibling characters in the same phonetic series (旬, 云). "Equal, even, uniform." A stray prior note mislabeled this composition as `會意`; corrected per dual-source confirmation.
