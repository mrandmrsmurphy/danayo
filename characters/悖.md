## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*bɯːds, \*bɯːd): semantic 忄 ("heart") + phonetic 孛 (this page's own alias) — "to go against, be contrary to." Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary); ja.Wiktionary notes the character is now commonly replaced in writing by 背. A secondary historical reading /buət̚/ (MC) and Mandarin běi "stubborn" are also attested by en.Wiktionary alone, from historical dictionaries only.
