## Aliases

### 稱
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 称, written 禾 + 爯; Unihan lists 称 as its Japanese new variant. SKIP 1-5-9 (EDRDG); 14 strokes. This is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank, so the `mc_id` 274 is 稱's rank. SKIP 1-5-9; 14 strokes. It ranks 274 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 爯
爫 over 冉, "a hand holding a fish", "to balance, to weigh"; Wiktionary treats it as the ancient form of 稱 and it is 称's phonetic. It has the same sound as 称, ㄑㄧㄫ (MC `t͡ɕʰ` + `ɨŋ`), so it is an alias of 称. SKIP 2-1-8 (EDRDG); 9 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists, so `mc_id` stays 274. SKIP 2-1-8; 9 strokes. Radical 087 爪. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 秤
禾 + 平, "scales, a balance" (and, archaically or dialectally, "to weigh"); a separate character that Unihan lists as a specialised semantic variant of 稱 and that Japanese lists among 称's forms. SKIP 1-5-5 (EDRDG); 10 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists. SKIP 1-5-5; 10 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
