## Notes
- 形声 (OC *ɡɯn): semantic [[Radical 140|艸]] ("grass") + phonetic [[斤]] (OC *kɯn, *kɯns) — an aquatic herb of the carrot family: the Java waterdropwort (Oenanthe javanica), also the celery (Apium graveolens). It is the plant of the Shijing line 思樂泮水，薄采其芹 "I love the waters of Pan, and pluck its dropwort", which gave 采芹 "pluck dropwort" its later sense of passing the district exam. Its Middle Chinese reading is 群 g + 欣 ɨn (巨斤切), the same initial and final as its siblings 近 and 勤, so the Dan'a'yo sound is the same ㄍㄧㄋ.
- Homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄋ): [[近 (char)|近]] "near", [[斤]] "kilogram", [[勤]] "diligent", [[緊]] "tense, tight, firm", [[僅]] "only, merely, barely", [[謹]] "prudent, cautious", [[筋]] "muscle", [[菫]] "violet; celery, aconite".
