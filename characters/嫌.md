## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*ɡeːm): semantic [[女 (char)|女]] ("woman") + phonetic [[兼 (char)|兼]] — "to be sick of, tired of, fed up with," extended (literary) to "hatred, resentment, grudge" and "to doubt, suspect."
- [SKIP-1-3-10](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-10.md) ([Stroke 13](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2013.md))
- 1186th most used character in Classical Chinese, the rank of its alias 厭 (嫌 itself ranks 2123). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 匣|ɣ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 添|em]] → [ㄏㄝㄇ](../syllables/ㄏㄝㄇ.md)

### 代用字
- 厭 "to be sick of, loathe, surfeit" — a close synonym sharing 嫌's Dan'a'yo syllable ㄏㄝㄇ; used as 嫌 in [[倦嫌]], where the real-world readings (yàn/jim3/EN/염/yếm) follow 厭's own values, not 嫌's.
- 魘 (traditional) / 魇 (simplified) "nightmare incubus" — a ghost-radical character (鬼 + phonetic 厭) for the demon that presses down on a sleeper; used as 嫌 in [[夢嫌]], the Dan'a'yo form of 夢魘/梦魇.

## Aliases

### 厭
"To be sated; to dislike"; a separate character kept as an alias of 嫌 for its meaning. SKIP 3-2-12; 14 strokes. Radical 027 厂. It ranks 1186 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 魘
鬼 + 厭, "nightmare"; built on 厭 and kept as an alias. SKIP 3-2-22; 23 strokes. Radical 194 鬼. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 魇
Simplified Chinese form of 魘. SKIP 3-2-13 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 15 strokes. Radical 194 鬼. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
