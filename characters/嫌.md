### 借代字
- 厭 "to be sick of, loathe, surfeit" — a close synonym sharing 嫌's Dan'a'yo syllable ㄏㄝㄇ; used as 嫌 in [[倦厭]], where the real-world readings (yàn/jim3/EN/염/yếm) follow 厭's own values, not 嫌's.
- 魘 (traditional) / 魇 (simplified) "nightmare incubus" — a ghost-radical character (鬼 + phonetic 厭) for the demon that presses down on a sleeper; used as 嫌 in [[夢嫌]], the Dan'a'yo form of 夢魘/梦魇.
