## Notes
- 形声 (OC *ʔloːɡ): semantic [[Radical 085|水]] ("water") + phonetic 𧶠 — a ditch, gutter; this shinjitai glyph substitutes the visually-simpler [[売]] for 𧶠 (as also seen in the 賣→売 simplification pattern), so [氵]+[[売]] is what's actually written, even though 𧶠 remains the etymologically true phonetic root.
