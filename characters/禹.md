## Notes
- 象形: per the *Shuowen Jiezi* ("禹：蟲也。从厹，象形"), originally a pictographic depiction of an insect or worm-like creature; bronze-script forms show a 虫 component. Now used exclusively as the name of Yu the Great (大禹), the legendary flood-taming sage-king.
