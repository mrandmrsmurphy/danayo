## Notes
- 形声 (OC *deːɡ): semantic [[Radical 140|艸]] ("grass") + phonetic [[狄]] (OC *deːɡ, "northern tribe") — Amur silvergrass (*Miscanthus sacchariflorus*), also a surname. 狄 is genuinely the phonetic component and shares an Old Chinese reading with 荻, but the two are distinct words with unrelated meanings, not variant forms of each other — the stray note left in this page's prior Notes ("狄=C#867") only recorded 狄's own Classical Chinese frequency rank (`Lookup/CC/CC 0000.md`: `867. 狄`), which does not apply to 荻 itself; 狄 has no independent page of its own in this vault, but that is a gap in 狄's own coverage, not a reason to alias it here.
