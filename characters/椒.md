## Notes
- 形声 (MC tsjew): semantic [[木]] ("tree, plant") + phonetic [[叔]] (zh.Wiktionary's own 聲符 field names the older root form 尗, from which 叔 is derived) — a pepper plant, especially Sichuan pepper (花椒); also used generically for chili and other spices, and as a surname. Genuine variant: 茮 (no independent page).
