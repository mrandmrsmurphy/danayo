## Notes
- 形聲 (OC \*preːd, \*praːd): semantic [[Radical 064|手]] ("hand") + phonetic [[別 (char)|別]] (OC \*pred, a close match) — to break apart, disentangle; a formal financial numeral form of 八 ("eight," used in official documents to prevent alteration/fraud, alongside 壹貳參肆伍陸柒玖拾); of goods, to sell well.
