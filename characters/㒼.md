- ⿱廿⿻巾𠓜: "equal, equivalent; covered evenly and tightly" — extended to "cover (completely)" in Japanese usage; a distinct Cantonese-only sense "blocked, obstructed" also exists. Its shinjitai form ⿱卄両 (this page's own alias) has no Unicode codepoint of its own yet.

## Aliases

### ⿱卄両
The ideographic description (卄 above 両) for the shinjitai form 㒼; kept as an alias so a search by its parts lands here. SKIP 4-6-1; 10 strokes. Radical 001 一. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 䓣
"A variety of grass"; kept as an alias of 㒼. SKIP 2-3-9 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 12 strokes. Radical 140 艸. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 𬜯
A rare variant form of 㒼; Wiktionary gives no definition. SKIP 2-3-8 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 11 strokes. Radical 140 艸. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
