## Aliases

### 笋
The Chinese simplified form of 筍, "bamboo shoots". SKIP 2-6-4; 10 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 荀
艹 + 旬, a surname; built on 旬, the phonetic of 筍, and kept as an alias. SKIP 2-3-6; 10 strokes. Radical 140 艸. It ranks 1411 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
