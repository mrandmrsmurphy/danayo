## Notes
- 會意 of [[爪]] ("hand, claw") and [[子]] ("child") — a hand grasping a child, "to seize/capture" (the original form of [[俘]] "prisoner of war"); *Shuowen*: "卵孚也" ("eggs being incubated"), extended to "to hatch, to brood over eggs" (matching `japanese_native: たまご/かえす`), and, since a hen's reliable brooding produces hatchlings, further extended to the abstract sense "to inspire trust, to be sincere/confident" (matching `korean_native: 미쁠` and `まこと`). Genuine variant: 孵 (also used as its second-round-simplification form in mainland China).
