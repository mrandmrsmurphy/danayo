## Aliases

### 黨
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 党, "party, faction"; this is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 2-5-15; 20 strokes. Radical 203 黑. It ranks 768 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
