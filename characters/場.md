## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*l'aŋ): semantic [[Radical 032|土]] ("earth") + phonetic [[昜]] (OC \*laŋ) — originally an open space or threshing floor; by extension, a market, a venue.
- [SKIP-1-3-9](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-9.md) ([Stroke 12](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2012.md))
- 2695th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 澄|ɖ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 陽開|ɨɐŋ]] → [ㄐㄚㄫ](../syllables/ㄐㄚㄫ.md)
- [Grade 1](../lookup/Grade%201.md), [HSK Beginner](../lookup/HSK/HSK%20Beginner.md), [Jōyō - Kyōiku](../lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kyōiku.md), [Korean MS](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20MS.md)
- non-productive suffix for "space for -x", i.e. "court"

## Aliases

### 场
The simplified-Chinese form of 場 ([Wiktionary: 场](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E5%9C%BA)). SKIP 1-3-3 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 6 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 塲
A variant form of 場 listed among its forms on Wiktionary, where 塲 redirects to 場 ([Wiktionary: 塲](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E5%A1%B2)); kept as a bare mention. SKIP 1-3-11; 14 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
