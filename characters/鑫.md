## Notes
- 会意: triplication of [[Radical 167|金]] ("gold, metal"), symbolizing abundant wealth. A modern/folk coinage absent from classical texts — used almost exclusively in Sinophone personal names and business names (41st most common character in Chinese newborn given names, 2018 PRC household-registration data), and separately registered as a South Korean name-use hanja. Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary); ja.Wiktionary explicitly lists no kun'yomi, so `japanese_native` is `ø`.
- [SKIP-2-8-16](Lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-8-16.md) ([Stroke 24](Lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2024.md))
- Absent from all four `Lookup/CC/CC N000.md` files (`mc_id: 0`, genuine sentinel — no Middle Chinese reconstruction is attested for 鑫 itself). Its modern readings (Mandarin xīn, Cantonese jam1/colloquially ham1, Korean 흠, Japanese kin/kon, Vietnamese hâm) align closely with [[歆]] (Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 曉|x]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 侵B|ɣiɪm]]), whose attested Middle Chinese is recorded here as a plausible borrowed ancestry rather than 鑫's own. The Dan'a'yo syllable itself was fixed directly from 鑫's own modern Cantonese/Korean readings — independently of 歆's already-established [ㄏㄨㄇ](syllables/ㄏㄨㄇ.md) — since 鑫's naming pedigree stands on its own → [ㄏㄜㄇ](syllables/ㄏㄜㄇ.md)
