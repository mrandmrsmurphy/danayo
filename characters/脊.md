## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*ʔseɡ): originally a pictogram of a fish spine; the top corrupted into 𠦬 and semantic [[肉 (char)|肉]] ("flesh") was added, with phonetic [[朿]] (OC \*sʰeɡs) — "spine, backbone," extended to "ridge, ridgeline."
- [SKIP-2-6-4](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-6-4.md) ([Stroke 10](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2010.md))

### 借代字
- 瘠 "thin, emaciated; barren" (疒 + phonetic 脊 — same Dan'a'yo syllable, and a transparent metaphor: a body thin enough to show its 脊 "spine/ridge"), hence 肥瘠 -> [[肥脊]]
- 鶺 "wagtail" (鳥 + phonetic 脊, used only in 鶺鴒/鹡鸰 "wagtail") — too narrow to merit its own entry, aliased onto its phonetic parent 脊; not yet used in any vault word.
