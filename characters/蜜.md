## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[虫]] ("insect") + phonetic 宓 (OC \*mriɡ, \*mliɡ, no vault page) — "honey." The word itself is likely an ancient Indo-European loan, possibly from Tocharian B "mit" (ultimately from PIE \*médʰu, cognate with English "mead").
