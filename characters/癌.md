## Notes
- 形聲: semantic [[Radical 104|疒]] ("illness") + phonetic [[嵒]] (a variant of 岩, "rock") — cancer, carcinoma; figuratively, something harmful to society. Originally read identically to 岩 (yán); the modern Mandarin ái is a deliberate 1962 pronunciation change to avoid clinical confusion with the homophonous 炎 ("inflammation") — Dan'a'yo's derivation follows the older, pre-1962 reading shared with 岩, which is why the two land on the identical syllable ㄚㄇ.
