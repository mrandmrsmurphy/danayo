## Notes
- [List of 会意](lookup/List%20of%20会意.md) (OC \*saːnʔ, \*saːns): the oracle-bone original form 㪔 is 会意 — [[林 (char)|林]] ("trees, forest") + [[Radical 066|攴]] ("to knock, tap") — to clear vegetation, to kill; extended via the influence of bamboo's tendency to break apart to "to disperse, scatter." (The Shuowen instead reads the modern glyph as 形声: semantic 肉 + phonetic 𢽳, no vault page for either.)
- [SKIP-1-8-4](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-8-4.md) ([Stroke 12](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2012.md))
- 688th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 心|s]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 寒|ɑn]] → [ㄙㄚㄋ](syllables/ㄙㄚㄋ.md)
- [Grade 2](lookup/Grade%202.md), [HSK Beginner](lookup/HSK/HSK%20Beginner.md), [Jōyō - Kyōiku](lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kyōiku.md), [Korean MS](lookup/Korean/Korean%20MS.md)
- Neologism: 散氷 for hail/sleet.
