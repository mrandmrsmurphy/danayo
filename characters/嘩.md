### 哗
Simplified Chinese form of 嘩. SKIP 1-3-6 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 9 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 譁
言 + 華, "uproar"; a variant of 嘩 and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-7-10; 17 strokes. Radical 149 言. It ranks 3444 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
