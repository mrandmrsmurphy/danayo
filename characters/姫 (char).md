- 形声 (OC \*ɡiŋ): semantic [[Radical 038|女]] ("woman") + phonetic [臣](characters/臣.md) — "princess, court lady." Variant of 姬, the original form for the "princess" sense; originally the surname of the Zhou royal house (姬姓).
