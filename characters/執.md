## Notes
- 会意 of 㚔 ("criminal; handcuffs," no vault page) and 丮 ("hand," no vault page) — the oracle-bone/bronze forms depict 梏人雙手, "the shackled hands of a person," showing restraint/seizure of a captive. In the modern glyph these components have been distorted into 幸-like and 丸-like shapes. "To hold in the hand" → "to hold (power), manage" → "to arrest, capture" — from concrete physical restraint to abstract control.
