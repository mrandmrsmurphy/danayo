## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 149|言]] ("speech") + phonetic [[彦]] (OC *ŋrans) — "proverb, common saying," a saying passed down through speech. Repurposed in Dan'a'yo via [[諺文]], the historical term for Hangul (literally "vernacular script"), reflecting the script's origin as colloquial in contrast to Hanja.
