## Aliases

### 穅
禾 + 康, "chaff, bran"; kept as an alias of 糟 for its meaning of grain husks. SKIP 1-5-11; 16 strokes. Radical 115 禾. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 醩
酉 + 曹, "sediment, dregs"; built on 曹, the phonetic of 糟, and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-7-11 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 18 strokes. Radical 164 酉. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 蹧
足 + 曹, "to spoil"; built on 曹 and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-7-11 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 18 strokes. Radical 157 足. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
