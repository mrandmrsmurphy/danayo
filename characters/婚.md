## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*hmɯːn): semantic [[Radical 038|女]] ("woman") + phonetic [[昏]] (OC \*hmɯːn). The traditional "dusk wedding" explanation (linking to 昏's "dusk" sense, since ancient weddings were held at dusk) dates only to the Han dynasty and is flagged as folk etymology; Schuessler instead favors an association of femininity with darkness (cf. 陰/阴, yīn).
