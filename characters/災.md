## Aliases

### 灾
宀 + 火, an alternative form of 災, "disaster". SKIP 2-3-4; 7 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 魃
鬼 + 犮, "drought demon"; a separate character kept as an alias of 災 for its meaning. SKIP 3-10-5; 14 strokes. Radical 194 鬼. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
