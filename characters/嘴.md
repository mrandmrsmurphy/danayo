## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[口]] ("mouth") + phonetic [[觜]] (OC *ʔseʔ, "beak; horn-like tuft on an owl's head") — a mouth-like protruding part; extended from "beak" to "mouth" generally, "nozzle/spout," "speech, words," and (Cantonese) "to kiss." Genuine variants: 咀, 㭰. `喙` (a distinct, independently-used character meaning "beak/snout of an animal") was removed from a prior `aliases` entry — neither en.Wiktionary's nor zh.Wiktionary's own 異體 lists it, a false positive from the two glyphs sharing a "beak/mouth" sense rather than a genuine variant relationship.
- [SKIP-1-3-13](Lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-13.md) ([Stroke 16](Lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2016.md))
- `mc_id` bug found and fixed: stored `2791` was actually 酋's rank (CC 2000.md: `2790. 斃`, `2791. 酋`, `2792. 喙`) — apparently borrowed from the immediate neighbor of the falsely-aliased 喙 (rank 2792). 嘴 itself is confirmed absent from all four CC frequency files (it emerged as a late vernacular character, largely unattested in the classical corpus this ranking is built from); corrected to `0`. The stray prior note "pronunciation is a guess" is genuine and preserved here: neither source gives 嘴 an attested Middle Chinese reading (only Zhengzhang's Old Chinese reconstruction for its phonetic 觜, *ʔseʔ), so this page's `middle_chinese_initial`/`middle_chinese_final` are a back-derived estimate from modern Mandarin zuǐ, not a genuine historical reconstruction.
