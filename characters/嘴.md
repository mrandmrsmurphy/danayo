## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[口]] ("mouth") + phonetic 觜 (OC *ʔseʔ, "beak; horn-like tuft on an owl's head") — a mouth-like protruding part; extended from "beak" to "mouth" generally, "nozzle/spout," "speech, words," and (Cantonese) "to kiss." Genuine variants: 咀, 㭰. 喙 (a distinct character meaning "beak/snout of an animal") is not an alias — neither en.Wiktionary nor zh.Wiktionary lists it as a variant.
- [SKIP-1-3-13](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-13.md) ([Stroke 16](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2016.md))
- 3943rd most used character in Classical Chinese, the rank of its alias 咀 (嘴 itself is not on the lists). Neither source gives 嘴 an attested Middle Chinese reading (only Zhengzhang's Old Chinese reconstruction for its phonetic 觜, *ʔseʔ), so this page's `middle_chinese_initial`/`middle_chinese_final` are a back-derived estimate from modern Mandarin zuǐ, not a genuine historical reconstruction. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 昌|t͡ɕʰ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 支三合|iuᴇ]] (estimated) → [ㄑㄨㄧ](../syllables/ㄑㄨㄧ.md)

## Aliases

### 咀
口 + 且, "to chew"; kept as an alias of 嘴 for its meaning "mouth, to chew". SKIP 1-3-5; 8 strokes. It ranks 3943 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 㭰
木 + 此, "to know, to hide"; built on 此, the phonetic of 嘴, and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-4-7 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 11 strokes. Radical 075 木. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
