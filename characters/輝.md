## Aliases

### 辉
Simplified Chinese form of 輝. SKIP 1-6-6 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 12 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 暉
日 + 軍, "sunlight, shine"; a variant of 輝 with the same meaning and sound. SKIP 1-4-9; 13 strokes. Radical 072 日. It ranks 2757 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
