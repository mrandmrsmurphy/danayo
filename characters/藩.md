## Aliases

### 樊
棥 ("fence") over 𠬜 ("two hands"), 15 strokes, "fence, hedge, cage; to fence in" and a surname, the phonetic of 攀 and 礬. It means the same as 藩 ("fence"), and MC 附袁切 (`v` + `ʉɐn`) is the slot 藩 occupies, so Dan'a'yo's sound for it would fall in the range of 煩, 礬 and 藩 (ㄆㄛㄋ); it is an alias of 藩, which is the hub for the characters built on it (see Derived Characters). SKIP 2-12-3 (EDRDG); 15 strokes. It has rank 1336 in the Classical Chinese lists, better than 藩's 1786, so `mc_id` is 1336. SKIP 2-12-3; 15 strokes. Radical 075 木.
