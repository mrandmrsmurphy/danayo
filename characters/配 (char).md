## Notes
- **`graphemic_classification` corrected from `己` to `會意`**: oracle-bone and bronze forms show [[Radical 164|酉]] ("wine vessel") + 卩 ("a kneeling person," no vault page) — a person paired with/before a wine vessel. Seal script corrupted the 卩 component into the visually similar 己, which is why the modern glyph appears to contain 己 — but per Shuowen commentators (Xu Xuan, Duan Yucai), 己 was never a functioning phonetic here; the character is properly 会意 (酉+卩), not 形声. Original sense "to blend wine colors in proper proportion," extended to "to pair, to match, to distribute."
