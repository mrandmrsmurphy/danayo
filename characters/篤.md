## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*tuːɡ): phonetic [[竹 (char)|竹]] (OC \*tuɡ, an unusually close match despite the modern zhú/dǔ divergence) + semantic [[馬 (char)|馬]] ("horse") — "sincere, earnest, whole-hearted," extended to "serious, severe" and "solid, firm." (竹 here happens to serve as both the modern Kangxi radical and the phonetic donor — a coincidence of indexing, not a double role in the character's own composition.)
