## Aliases

### 晉
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 晋, "to advance" and the state of Jin. SKIP 2-1-9; 10 strokes. It ranks 178 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 𣈆
A variant form of 晉 (also Vietnamese). SKIP 2-4-8 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 12 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 㬐
The same as 晉, a state in the Spring and Autumn period. SKIP 2-4-10 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 14 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 𡥨
A variant form of 晉. SKIP 2-3-7 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 10 strokes. Radical 039 子. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
