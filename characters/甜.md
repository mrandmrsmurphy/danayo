## Aliases

### 恬
心 + an abbreviated 甜 as phonetic (忄 + 舌), "tranquil, calm; content". It is a separate word, not a form of 甜, but Dan'a'yo would give it the same sound, ㄉㄧㄇ (both are 徒兼切, MC `d` + `em`), and 甜 is its phonetic source, so it is kept as an alias of 甜 by the project owner's decision, with 甜 as the hub for the characters built on it (see Derived Characters). SKIP 1-3-6 (EDRDG); 9 strokes. It has rank 1765 in the Classical Chinese lists, so `mc_id` is 1765. SKIP 1-3-6; 9 strokes. Radical 061 心. It ranks 1765 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
