- 形声 (OC \*qlɯːʔ, \*qrɯː, \*qrɯːʔ): semantic [手](characters/手%20(char).md) ("hand") + phonetic [矣](characters/矣%20(char).md) — "to push against, to nudge"; extended to "dawdle" and, in modern Mandarin, "to endure, suffer through" (挨打 "get hit," 挨饿 "endure hunger").
