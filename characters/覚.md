## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*kruːɡ): semantic [[見 (char)|見]] ("see") + phonetic 𦥯 (OC \*ɡruːɡ, part of the 學/[[学]] family — no character page of its own in this vault; 学 is cited here as the closest attested relative, not a substitution error). Also pronounced /kɣau/ in MC, leading to 2+ pronunciations in most languages (one for "awaken," one for "be aware").
- [SKIP-2-5-7](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-5-7.md) ([Stroke 12](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2012.md))
