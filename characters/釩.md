## Notes
- abbreviation for <ruby>[[釩金]]<rt>ㄪㄚㄋㄍㄧㄇ</rt></ruby> "vanadium" in periodic-table formulae — see [[Periodic Table]]
- 形声 (OC \*pʰomʔ): semantic [金](Radical%20167) (“metal”) + phonetic [凡](characters/凡.md) (OC \*bom). Originally some kind of metal vessel or cup, now taken over for vanadium.
- Pronunciation shamelessly swapped places with [[礬]] (alum). This character is meant to resemble international "**van**adium." It is encouraged to spell this word ㄪㄚㄋ when possible — ㄪ (/v/) is an extremely marginal but legitimate Dan'a'yo phoneme, and the plain ㄈㄚㄋ (/f/) stored here reflects the fact that it's fine to realize this sound as [f] in this position.
