## Aliases

### 畵
A variant of 畫 (聿 over 𰢵); Wiktionary's 畵 page lists 画 as the simplified and shinjitai form of 畫, and in Japanese and Korean 畵 is an alternative form of 画. SKIP 2-6-7 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 13 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 畫
The traditional form; Wiktionary's 画 page sends readers to 畫 as the form of record, and 畫's page lists 画 as its simplified form ("to draw; to paint; to divide by a line"). It carries the classical rank of 1268, which the page already holds. SKIP 2-6-6; 12 strokes. It ranks 1268 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
