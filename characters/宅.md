## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*r'aːɡ): semantic [[Radical 040|宀]] ("roof") + phonetic 乇 (OC \*ʔr'aːɡ, no vault page). Core senses are residence, dwelling, tomb, and the verb "to reside"; the modern slang sense "homebody, shut-in" (宅男/宅女) is a Japanese-orthography borrowing from お宅 (otaku), not a native semantic extension.
