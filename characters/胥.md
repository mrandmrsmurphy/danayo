## Notes
- 形声 (OC *sŋa, *sŋaʔ): semantic [[Radical 130|肉]] ("flesh") + phonetic [[疋]] (OC *sŋraʔ) — originally an official responsible for catching thieves, extended to "minor clerk/official" (胥吏), then to "all, together" and "mutually"; also a surname (famously 伍子胥 Wu Zixu).
