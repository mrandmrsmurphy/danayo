## Notes
- 形聲 (OC \*kuɡ): semantic [[Radical 177|革]] (“leather”) + phonetic 匊 (OC \*kluɡ, a close match; itself [[包|勹]] + semantic [[米 (char)|米]], pageless in this vault) — a historical leather ball used in games (cuju); to bend, to curve; to bow; to rear, to raise. In the narrow sense “ferment, yeast,” 鞠 also functions as an earlier written form of the separately-attested 麴 (Zhao, 2002; pageless in this vault), which has since become the dominant character for that specific sense.
