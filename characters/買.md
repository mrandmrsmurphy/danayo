## Notes
- 会意 of [[Radical 122|网]] ("net") + [[Radical 154|貝]] ("money cowrie") — using a net to gather money/goods; to buy. The original form was 𧵽. (Baxter–Sagart instead propose 形声, an abbreviated phonetic 羅 + semantic 貝.)
