## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): etymology uncertain — Wiktionary offers no firm classification, describing 方 as possibly the original character of 旁 (OC \*baːŋ, "side"), with a form related to 巫 (which depicts "four extremities with indicator symbols"): 方 lacks the indicator symbol on one end and is elongated to denote "side." Core meanings: "side, direction, region"; "square, rectangular"; "method, principle."
- [SKIP-2-2-2](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-2-2.md) ([Stroke 04](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2004.md))
