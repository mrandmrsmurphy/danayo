## Notes
- 會意 (OC \*meːŋ): [[Radical 014|冖]] ("cloth cover") over 日 ("sun") + 廾 ("two hands," later corrupted to 六) — the sun obscured, hence "dark, dim, gloomy," extended to "ignorant, muddled" and "the underworld." Its aliases (溟, 暝, 瞑, 慏) are all pageless phonetic derivatives/variant forms sharing this exact Old Chinese reading — not yet cited by any vault word.
