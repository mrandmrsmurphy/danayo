## Notes
- 形声 (OC *rtʰaːŋs): semantic [[手]] ("hand") + phonetic [[牚]] — to prop up, support, brace; extended to "to pole (a boat)," "to sustain, maintain," and "to fill to the point of bursting." Genuine variant: 撑 (standard simplified form).
