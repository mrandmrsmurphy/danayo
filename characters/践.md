## Aliases

### 踐
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 践, "to tread, to walk on; to carry out"; this is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 1-7-8; 15 strokes. It ranks 1195 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
