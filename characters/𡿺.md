## Notes
- 會意: semantic 巛 ("hair") + semantic 囟 ("fontanel," a baby's skull suture) — a traditional variant form of 腦 ("brain"), used primarily as a component (e.g. in 瑙/碯, both pageless aliases of this page, appearing in "agate" compounds). Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary). Japan's shinjitai form is ⿱𭕄凶 (this page's own alias); zh.Wiktionary also confirms 㐫 (another alias) as the PRC-simplified equivalent. hvdic.thivien.net explicitly denies any Vietnamese reading ("chưa có giải nghĩa"), so `vietnamese` is correctly left blank.
- **Fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` were nuo/눗/ㄋㄨㄛ, an outlier inconsistent with every other `middle_chinese_final: ɑu` character in the vault (all map to Dan'a'yo "-au", e.g. [[脳 (char)|脳]] and [[悩 (char)|悩]], its own semantic siblings for "brain," both read nau/낫/ㄋㄚㄨ) — corrected to nau/낫/ㄋㄚㄨ, moving this character from the now-empty syllable [[syllables/ㄋㄨㄛ|ㄋㄨㄛ]] into [[syllables/ㄋㄚㄨ|ㄋㄚㄨ]].
