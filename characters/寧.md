## Notes
- 形声: built on 寍 ("peaceful," itself 宀 "roof" + 心 "heart" + 皿 "dish" — an untroubled heart with food under a roof) with phonetic 丂 added — "peaceful, calm." 甯 and 宁 are variant forms.
