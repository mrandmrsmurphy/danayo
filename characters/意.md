## Notes
- 会意: the modern glyph combines [[Radical 061|心]] ("heart") + 音 ("sound"), but the top component is etymologically unrelated to 音 — bronze inscriptions instead show the character derived from 言 ("speech") + 中 ("middle"). Meaning, intention.
- [SKIP-2-2-11](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-2-11.md) ([Stroke 13](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2013.md))
