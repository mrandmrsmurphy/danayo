## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*lɯːŋ): semantic [[Radical 187|馬]] ("horse") + phonetic [[朕 (char)|朕]] (OC \*l'ɯmʔ) — a horse's leap, gallop, prance — extended to "to soar, rise, ascend." 腾 is the standard simplified form. Formerly garbled: the semantic component was wrongly labeled 朕 ("horse," which 朕 does not mean) and the phonetic link was empty — corrected.
