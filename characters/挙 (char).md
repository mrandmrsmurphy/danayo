- 形声 (OC \*klaʔ): semantic [[Radical 064|手]] ("hand") + phonetic [[与 (char)|與]] — "to raise, to lift up"; extended to "to elect; to start, initiate."
- [SKIP-2-4-6](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-4-6.md) ([Stroke 10](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2010.md))

## Aliases

### 舉
The traditional form used in traditional Chinese ("to raise, lift up"); Wiktionary's 舉 page lists 挙 among its forms. It carries the classical rank of 227, which is the page's `mc_id`. SKIP 2-11-5; 16 strokes. Radical 134 臼. It ranks 227 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 擧
The Japanese kyūjitai form of 挙, the preferred form in Korean hanja and a variant traditional form in modern Chinese; Wiktionary calls 挙 its Japanese shinjitai. SKIP 2-11-6; 18 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 举
The simplified Chinese form of 舉; Wiktionary's 挙 page lists 举 as the simplified form used in mainland China. SKIP 2-5-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 9 strokes. Radical 003 丶. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
