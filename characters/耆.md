## Aliases

### 嗜
口 + 耆, "to be fond of; to indulge in; to covet"; Wiktionary records 耆 as the original form of 嗜 ("to be fond of"), and the vault writes the word 嗜好 as 耆好, with 耆 standing in for 嗜, so 嗜 is an alias of 耆. 嗜 is read shì in Mandarin, but written with its original form 耆 (a 代用字) it takes 耆's Dan'a'yo sound, ㄍㄧㄜ, in the word 耆好. SKIP 1-3-10; 13 strokes. Radical 030 口. It ranks 1740 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.
