## Notes
- ⿵門東: 門 ("door, gate") enclosing 東 — a corrupted form (訛字) of [[闌]] (this page's own alias), whose own genuine composition is 門+柬; 䦨 arose from a visual confusion of 柬 with the similar-looking 東 inside the same enclosure. Dual-source confirmed as a corrupted/variant form (en.Wiktionary and ja.Wiktionary); no source documents Japanese readings or classification for 䦨 itself, so `japanese: [RAN]`/`japanese_native: たけし`/blank `joyo_level` are all left as pre-existing, unconfirmable-but-uncontradicted values.
