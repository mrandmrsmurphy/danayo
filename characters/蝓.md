## Notes
- 形声: semantic 虫 ("insect") + phonetic [兪 (char)](characters/兪%20(char).md) — "slug." (Cited under the sibling glyph 兪, the only one with a vault page; en.Wiktionary's own citation is 俞, an interchangeable variant of the same character.)
- /m/ was added to the pronunciation to fill an otherwise impossible syllable. It was inspired by the Japanese むし, but it really just for syllable's sake.
