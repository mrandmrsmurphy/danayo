## Notes
- [List of 指事](Lookup/List%20of%20指事.md): a person (人) bathing in warm water (now written 囗) in a container (皿) full of warm water — original form of 溫/[[温 (char)|温]] (OC \*ʔuːn). Simplified from 𥁕 (囚 → 日); also cited as a variant of 𣌬. Alternatively, according to Xu Shen, an ideogrammic compound (會意/会意): 囚 ("prisoner") + 皿 ("dish") — feeding a prisoner from a dish, though the oracle-bone shape argues against this. The vault's `english`/native glosses ("feed a prisoner," めぐむ "show compassion," 어질 "benevolent") follow this second, semantically-distinct thread (via the 𥁕 "kind, benevolent" variant sense) rather than duplicating 温's own "warm" gloss. Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary); ja.Wiktionary's own entry is an unreferenced stub with no reading section, so `japanese`/`japanese_native` remain single-sourced from en.Wiktionary.
