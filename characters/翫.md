## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*ŋoːns): semantic [[習]] ("practice; habit") + phonetic [元 (char)](characters/元.md) — "idleness; negligence" (archaic), also an alternative written form of [[玩]] ("to play; to toy with"). Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary); a raw scratch note asking whether this page "can go to 玩" was resolved by checking: both sources describe 翫/玩 as historically distinct characters that converged in meaning rather than simple traditional/simplified pair, and 玩 already has its own robust independent page in this vault (with its own alias 莞 and its own separate Korean Name cross-reference), so the two stay separate.
