## Notes
- 會意, originally [[大 (char)|大]] ("adult") + [[口 (char)|口]] ("mouth") + [[子]] ("child") — to transmit knowledge; the modern graph, [[知 (char)|知]] ("to know") + [[日 (char)|日]] ("sun"), reflects an exopassive derivation of 知 rather than this literal ancient reading — "what is known," hence "wisdom, knowledge." In Japanese this character is sometimes replaced with 知 as a 代用字 (substitute character).
