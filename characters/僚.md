## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*rewʔ, \*reːw): semantic [[Radical 009|人]] ("person") + phonetic [[尞]] — "official, colleague, associate." 尞 is the shared phonetic root of a wider family (compare [[寮]], [[療]], [[瞭]], [[遼]], below) rather than a variant specific to 僚; 繚 (a distinct character meaning "to wind/wrap around") was formerly wrongly listed as an alias here and has been removed.
- [SKIP-1-2-12](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-2-12.md) ([Stroke 14](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2014.md))
- 1795th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 來|l]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 宵A|iᴇu]] → [ㄌ⼄ㄨ](syllables/ㄌ⼄ㄨ.md)
- [Grade 5](lookup/Grade%205.md), [Old HSK 3](lookup/HSK/Old%20HSK%203.md), [Jōyō - Kōtō](lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kōtō.md), [Korean HS](lookup/Korean/Korean%20HS.md) — added to the Korean high school hanja list in 2000.
