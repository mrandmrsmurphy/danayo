## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): depicts an ancient wine vessel with a pointed or rounded bottom, the original form of [[酒]] ("alcohol"); later borrowed as the tenth of the twelve Earthly Branches (地支) and, by calendrical convention rather than depiction, paired with the rooster of the Chinese zodiac — the source of `english: rooster` and `korean_native: 닭` used on this page.
- [SKIP-4-7-1](Lookup/SKIP/SKIP-4/SKIP-4-7-1.md) ([Stroke 07](Lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2007.md))
- 1013th most used character in Classical Chinese (CC 1000.md: 1012. 亥, 1013. 酉, 1014. 寅). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 以|j]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 尤|ɨu]] → [⼜](syllables/⼜.md) — the Dan'a'yo syllable itself is genuinely notated with the Kangxi radical-supplement glyph ⼜ rather than standard Bopomofo, an established vault-wide convention for this syllable (see `syllables/⼜.md`, home to ten characters including 有/右/油/憂/裕/癒/祐/庾/酉/䍃), not a transcription error.
