## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*ɦleːb): a variant form of [[劦]] ("to collaborate" — supplying both the sound and the meaning) with phonetic [[十 (char)|十]] added purely as an extra sound-component, contributing no semantics of its own. Core sense "to cooperate, work together" is inherited straight from 劦's original meaning.
