## Notes
- 形聲 (OC \*l̥ʰjuʔ): semantic [[鬼 (char)|鬼]] ("ghost, demon" — demons are ugly in Chinese culture) + phonetic [[Radical 164|酉]] (OC \*luʔ) — "ugly; homely." This character is simplified to [[丑]] in modern Simplified Chinese for this specific "ugly" sense, but that merger is not followed in Dan'a'yo — 丑 keeps its own independent page here for its unrelated, much older "second earthly branch; ox" sense.
