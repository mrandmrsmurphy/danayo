## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*tɯːns, \*dɯːns): semantic [[Radical 079|殳]] ("hand holding a weapon") + phonetic 𡱂 (OC \*duːn, no vault page) — Shuowen analyzes 𡱂 itself as 尸+丌 ("body" over "pedestal," i.e. "buttocks"), with 殳 added to emphasize "striking the buttocks"; the modern sense "lofty building, hall, palace" may be a semantic extension of something elevated off the ground, though the connection is debated.
