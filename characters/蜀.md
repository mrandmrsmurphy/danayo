## Notes
- 會意: originally 𦉶 (a large 目 "eye" with a hooked stroke, attested independently in Shang oracle bone script) + 虫 ("insect"), added by Western Zhou times — "Shu kingdom" (說文解字 reanalyzes the whole as a single pictogram of a silkworm, but the compound origin is earlier).
