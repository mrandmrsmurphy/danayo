## Notes
- 形声 (OC *qroːɡ, MC ʔˠʌk̚): semantic [[水 (char)|水]] ("water") + phonetic [[屋 (char)|屋]] (OC *qoːɡ) — primarily "to moisten, to soak" (of dew/rain soaking the earth), extended to "thick, deep, generous" (of favor/kindness, as in the historical phrase 渥恩 "profound imperial favor"); both senses dual-source confirmed by en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary. No genuine dual-source variant form: en.Wiktionary lists none (only the unrelated derived character 𩅵), and zh.Wiktionary's own candidates (捂, 漚) lack en.Wiktionary corroboration.
