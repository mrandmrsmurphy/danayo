## Notes
- 形声 (OC *pʰan): semantic [[巾]] ("cloth") + phonetic [[番]] (OC *pʰan) — a narrow, vertically-hung flag or banner, especially a Buddhist ceremonial banner. 旛 (a common alternate spelling with 旗 "flag" substituted for the semantic component) is a phonetic-series cognate cited by zh.Wiktionary alongside 翻/飜/繙, but neither source explicitly labels it a variant form of 幡 itself, so it was not added as an alias.
