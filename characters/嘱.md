## Aliases

### 囑
Traditional form of 嘱, "to enjoin, to entrust"; it takes the full 屬 phonetic where 嘱 has the simplified 属. SKIP 1-3-21; 24 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
