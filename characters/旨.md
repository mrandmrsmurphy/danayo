## Notes
- 會意 (OC \*kjiʔ): [[匕]] ("spoon," i.e. 匙) + originally [[甘 (char)|甘]] ("delicious") — eating something delicious with a spoon; "delicious," extended to "intention, purpose, aim" and "imperial decree." The bottom component later drifted to resemble [[日 (char)|日]] ("sun"), the character's modern but etymologically coincidental Kangxi radical. Shuowen alternatively analyzes this as 形聲 (phonetic 匕 + semantic 甘), but 匕's own OC reading (\*pilʔ/\*pijʔ, labial initial) doesn't support it as a valid phonetic donor for 旨's velar \*k-, so the 會意 analysis is preferred here.
