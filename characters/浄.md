## Aliases

### 淨
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 浄, with 爭 for 争. SKIP 1-3-8; 11 strokes. Ranked 3445 in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 净
Simplified Chinese form of 淨, written with 冫 ("ice") and 争. Radical 015 冫; SKIP 1-2-6 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 8 strokes. It has no rank in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 滌
Written as 浄 as a 代用字: 滌 "to wash, rinse" (as in 洗滌) is a rare character with no page of its own. SKIP 1-3-11; 14 strokes. This is the lowest-ranked of the forms in the Classical Chinese lists (2885), so the `mc_id` 2885 comes from 滌.

### 涤
Simplified Chinese form of 滌, so it too is written as 浄. SKIP 1-3-7 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 10 strokes. It has no rank in the Classical Chinese lists.
