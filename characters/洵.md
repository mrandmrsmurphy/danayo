## Notes
- 形声 (OC *sqʰʷin, MC sjuin): semantic [[水 (char)|水]] ("water") + phonetic [[旬]] — the classical adverb "truly, indeed" (famous from the Shijing's 洵美且異), also cited as the name of two minor rivers (in Taiyuan, Shanxi and Xunyang, Shaanxi); the two other pronunciations/senses documented in historical dictionaries (均 "equal," 堪 "capable," and an obsolete alternative form of 泫/敻) are not corroborated between en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary as the character's primary usage, so left out of `english`. A zh.Wiktionary-only 異體字 candidate, [[汮]], was investigated and excluded for lacking en.Wiktionary corroboration.
