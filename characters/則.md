## Notes
- 会意 of [[鼎 (char)|鼎]] ("bronze tripod cauldron") and [[Radical 018|刀]] ("knife") — original form 𠟭: carving an inscription (rules, laws) onto a bronze vessel with a knife. The modern glyph's left component resembles 貝 ("cowrie shell") but is actually a corrupted/simplified 鼎, unrelated to the shell character. "Carve a rule onto bronze" → "rule; law; regulation" → "standard; model, example" → the grammaticalized adverbial/conjunctive sense "then; thus; in that case."
- [SKIP-1-7-2](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-7-2.md) ([Stroke 09](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2009.md))
- 17th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 精|t͡s]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 德開|ək]] → [ㄐㄨㄎ](syllables/ㄐㄨㄎ.md)
