## Notes
- 形声 (OC *pin/*bin, MC pjin): semantic [[水 (char)|水]] ("water") + phonetic [[頻]] — primarily "water's edge, riverbank" (matching `korean_native: 물가`), extended to "to border on, to be on the verge of" (as in [[words/瀕死|瀕死]] "near death" and 瀕臨 "to border on"). en.Wiktionary alone frames the character's ultimate origin as an ideogrammic compound of 水 ("water") + 步 ("feet") + 頁 ("person," via 頻's own components), but zh.Wiktionary's own 字源 explicitly analyzes 瀕 itself as 形聲 with phonetic 頻 (MC *pjin) — the vault follows the latter, immediate-composition analysis per its established convention. Also noted (single-source, zh.Wiktionary only) as occasionally interchangeable with the unrelated, independently-used [[濱]] (this vault's own page for 濱's own character), and a zh.Wiktionary-only variant, [[𩕨]], lacking en.Wiktionary corroboration, was excluded from `aliases`.
