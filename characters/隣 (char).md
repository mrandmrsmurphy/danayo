- [SKIP-1-3-13](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-13.md) ([Stroke 16](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2016.md))

## Aliases

### 鄰
Traditional form of 隣, "neighbour; neighbouring"; this is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 1-13-3; 14 strokes. Radical 163 邑. It ranks 1150 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 邻
Simplified Chinese form of 鄰. SKIP 1-5-2 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 7 strokes. Radical 163 邑. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
