## Notes
- 会意: 㫃 ("flag") + 疋 ("foot," no vault page) — depicts marching under a flag, extended to "to revolve, turn, return, go back." 鏇 (a legitimate variant, "to shape on a lathe," specifically the xuàn-toned lathe-turning sense) is a distinct but related character.
