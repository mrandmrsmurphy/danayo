## Notes
- 会意 (uncertain origin): Wiktionary describes the etymology as unclear, possibly derived from an oracle-bone form of 智 composed of 子, [[止]], and 冊. Originally meant "this, that," developing into the copula "to be" by the Eastern Han dynasty. **Corrected here**: the field previously stored `止`, implying 形声 with phonetic 止, but no source describes this character as phono-semantic, and 止's own reading (ㄐㄧ) diverges substantially from 是's own (ㄙㄝ) — no solid phonetic relationship supports the original field value, even though 是 itself serves productively as the phonetic component in a large, well-attested family of later characters (see Derived Characters below).
