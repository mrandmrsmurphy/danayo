## Notes
- 形聲: semantic [[Radical 064|扌]] ("hand") + phonetic [[石 (char)|石]]. A graphically-collapsed character covering two originally distinct Middle Chinese etymologies: (1) 척/tuò, "to expand, widen, open up, pioneer" (as in [[開拓]], kāituò/개척) and (2) 탁/tà, OC \*tʰaːɡ, "to inscribe, take a rubbing; to push, shove, repel" (as in 拓本, tàběn/탁본). This vault derives 拓's Dan'a'yo syllable from the second (탁/\*tʰaːɡ) ancestry, so the `mandarin`/`korean`/`middle_chinese_*` fields track that reading specifically even though [[開拓]] (this character's own `stand_in`) draws its meaning from the first — the same divergence already documented on [[開拓]] (word)'s own page, where the real Sino-Korean 개척 differs from the Dan'a'yo-derived 탁 syllable.
