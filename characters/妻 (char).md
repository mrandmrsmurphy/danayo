## Notes
- 会意 of 肀 ("hand grabbing hair," no vault page) and [[Radical 038|女]] ("woman") — depicting a man seizing a woman's hair, traditionally read as symbolizing marriage by capture/ownership. Possibly cognate to 齊 (OC \*dzêi, "equal"), implying "equal to her husband," though deemed historically unlikely; Schuessler suggests an Austroasiatic origin (\*tshə̂i), comparing Middle Khmer "kansai" (wife), with \*ka(n)- as a female marker.
