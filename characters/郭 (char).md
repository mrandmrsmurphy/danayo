## Notes
- 會意 (OC \*kʷaːɡ): "tall building" (亯/high, no vault page) + [[邑]] ("city") — "outer city wall," extended to "outline." Its alias [[廓]] (no vault page) shares this reading and is used as 郭 in [[郭清]] and [[輪郭]].
