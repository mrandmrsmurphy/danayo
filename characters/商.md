## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): origin genuinely unclear — Wiktionary commits to no compound analysis at all. The base component is consistently 丙, with late-period variants adding [[Radical 030|口]] below; the element atop 丙 varies across six attested oracle-bone/bronze forms (an inverted triangle with a bar, a 辛-like form, a bare triangle possibly the original 樴, paired symmetrical elements, and star-marked variants possibly tied to the Shāng star), with no settled identification of what it depicts. Not a standalone Dan'a'yo word — bound only in 商業; the character is also used as the Shāng dynasty name/a surname.
