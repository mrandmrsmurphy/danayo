## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*pʰaːns): semantic [[Radical 018|刀]] ("knife") + phonetic [[半]] (OC \*paːns, "half") — 半 already carries the sense of splitting something in two; adding the knife reinforces the literal action of cutting/dividing, extended metaphorically to "judge, decide, distinguish" — judging as "cutting" a matter into right/wrong, just as a blade cleaves an object into halves.
- [SKIP-1-5-2](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-5-2.md) ([Stroke 07](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2007.md))
