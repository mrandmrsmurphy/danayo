## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*ɡruːls; traditional form 壞): semantic [[Radical 032|土]] ("earth") + phonetic 褱 (OC \*ɡruːl) — to break, to collapse. The earth radical is used since something that rots eventually becomes earth.
- [SKIP-1-3-13](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-13.md) ([Stroke 16](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2016.md))
- 1126th most used character in Classical Chinese (rank belongs to the traditional form 壞). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 匣|ɣ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 皆合|ɣuɛi]] → [ㄏ⺢ㄧ](../syllables/ㄏ⺢ㄧ.md)
