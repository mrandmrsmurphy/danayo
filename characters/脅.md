## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*hep): semantic [[Radical 130|肉]] ("flesh," originally "ribs, the sides of the body") + phonetic [[劦]] (OC \*ɡreːp) — "the sides of the torso," extended metaphorically to "coerce, threaten" (via physical pressure applied to the ribs/sides). 胁 is the standard simplified form; 脇 is a Japanese kyūjitai variant with its own separate Classical Chinese corpus entry, but no distinct vault page — kept as an alias.
