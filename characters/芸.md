## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*ɢun): semantic [[Radical 140|艸]] ("plant") + phonetic 云 (no vault page) — originally "rue," a fragrant herb. In Japanese, 芸 separately serves as the shinjitai simplification of 藝 ("art, skill, craft") — an unrelated character merged in by simplification, not a semantic extension; the stand-in 芸術 and this page's own english gloss reflect that merged "art" sense, not the plant.
