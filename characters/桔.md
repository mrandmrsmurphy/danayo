## Notes
- 形声 (OC *kiːd): semantic [[木]] ("tree, wood") + phonetic [[吉]] (OC *klid) — a well-sweep, a lever device for drawing water (桔槔); also the balloon flower/bellflower, in the compound [[words/桔梗|桔梗]] (matching `korean_native: 도라지`). Under an entirely separate etymology and reading (jú), 桔 was briefly used as the mainland Chinese second-round (1977) simplified form of 橘 ("tangerine/mandarin orange") — a relationship no longer officially recognized, and 橘 has its own extensive independent use and page in this vault, so it is not listed as an alias here. A third, obscure etymology (xié) appears only in the isolated compound 桔柣, of unclear meaning.
