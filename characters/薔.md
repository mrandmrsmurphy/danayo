## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[艸]] ("grass") + phonetic [[嗇]] — "(obsolete) water pepper" (*Persicaria hydropiper*), also "a surname." Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary). En.Wiktionary gives a second etymology (semantic 艸 + abbreviated phonetic 牆, "only used in [[薔薇]]"); the vault follows the first (phonetic 嗇), matching the character's own attested standalone sense and `japanese_native: みずたで` ("water pepper"). "Rose" is only the compound sense of 薔薇 (#cranberry: neither 薔 nor [[薇]] means "rose" alone).
