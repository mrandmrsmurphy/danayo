## Notes
- 形声 (OC *siːl): semantic [[Radical 075|木]] ("tree") + phonetic [[妻]] (OC *sʰiːl, *sʰiːls) — to perch, roost; to dwell, inhabit (棲息, 棲隱 "to live in seclusion"). En.Wiktionary's own etymology notes the character was originally written simply as [[西]] before the Warring States period, with 木 added later — 西 itself has extensive independent use ("west") and its own page in this vault, so it is not treated as an alias here, only as a historical predecessor form. This vault's own word-coinage convention favors 生-based compounds over 棲-based ones for "to dwell/inhabit" senses generally, with [[words/棲息|棲息]] kept as the sole exception (the characters themselves remain distinct, unmerged).
