## Notes
- 象形 ([List of 象形](Lookup/List%20of%20象形.md)): a pig, from oracle bone/bronze/seal-script pictograms. Compare 亥, 𢑓 ("male pig"), and 豖 ("castrated male pig" — not to be confused with 豕 itself; the "shackled pig" gloss previously stored here actually described 豖). See also the original version on oracle bones of 敢. Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary).
