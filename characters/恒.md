- 形声 (traditional form 恆): semantic [[Radical 061|心]] ("heart") + phonetic [[亙 (char)|亙]] ("partial moon") — lasting, constant. Also analyzable as a 会意; may originally have been written 亙, and in Small Seal Script the 月 in 亙 was written 舟.
- [SKIP-1-3-6](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-6.md) ([Stroke 09](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2009.md))
