## Notes
- 形声: originally written 皇 ("emperor," its own well-attested independent character, kept here as the graphemic phonetic rather than an alias since it has fully independent use); a spurious semantic component 𠘨/几 was added to distinguish this "female phoenix" sense once it was paired with 鳳 ("male phoenix") in the compound 鳳凰. Genuine variants: 䳨, 𬸛 (dual-source confirmed, neither has an independent page).
