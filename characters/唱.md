## Aliases

### 倡
Shares the phonetic 昌 and one sense with 唱: Wiktionary defines 倡 as "to lead (a chorus); to sing" ([Wiktionary: 倡](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E5%80%A1)), though it is a separate character; kept for searchers. SKIP 1-2-8; 10 strokes. Radical 009 人. It ranks 2144 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 娼
Shares the phonetic 昌 with 唱; its historical sense was "female performer of song and dance" ([Wiktionary: 娼](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E5%A8%BC)), though it is a separate character; kept for searchers. SKIP 1-3-8; 11 strokes. Radical 038 女. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
