## Notes
- Composition ⿱艹祘: [[Radical 140|艸]] ("grass, plant") over 祘 — "garlic" (*Allium sativum*; also the Chinese garlic *Allium macrostemon*). English Wiktionary treats the glyph as a duplication of 柰 or 祟 and gives the Chinese word's origin as either Sino-Tibetan \*swa-n or a plant imported from the Western Regions in the Qin–Han era, noting the earlier form 卵蒜; its Old Chinese rhyme matches that of 算, "to calculate".
- [SKIP-2-3-10](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-3-10.md) ([Stroke 13](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2013.md))
- 3816th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 心|s]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 桓|uɑn]] → [ㄙ⺢ㄇ](../syllables/ㄙ⺢ㄇ.md), the otherwise unused syllable *swam*; the regular outcome ㄙ⺢ㄋ already holds 酸, 算 and others.
