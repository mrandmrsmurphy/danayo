## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): depicts a well-frame or the crossed timbers over a well opening, viewed from above.
- [SKIP-4-4-3](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-4/SKIP-4-4-3.md) ([Stroke 04](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2004.md))
- 935th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 精|t͡s]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 清開|iᴇŋ]] → [ㄐㄧㄫ](syllables/ㄐㄧㄫ.md)
- [Grade 3](lookup/Grade%203.md), [Old HSK 2](lookup/HSK/Old%20HSK%202.md), [Jōyō - Kyōiku](lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kyōiku.md), [Korean MS](lookup/Korean/Korean%20MS.md)

井 was added to the 常用漢字 (Jōyō) list in the 2010 revision, then in 2017 incorporated into 教育漢字 (the specific elementary-school curriculum list) as one of the characters needed to fully spell out Japan's prefecture names — 井 for 福井県 — with that grade-school implementation taking effect in 2020.
