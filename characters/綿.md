## Aliases

### 绵
Simplified Chinese form of 綿. SKIP 1-3-8 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 11 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 棉
木 + 帛, "cotton, kapok"; kept as an alias of 綿 for its shared meaning of cotton. SKIP 1-4-8; 12 strokes. Radical 075 木. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
