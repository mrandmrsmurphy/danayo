- 形聲 (OC \*raːʔ): semantic 力 ("strength") + semantic "to pierce" + phonetic [[Radical 141|虍]] (OC \*qʰaː) — to capture someone alive; a captive, a prisoner. Simplified form [[虏]] is a genuine variant; 擄 (no character page in this vault, cited only as a Korean-name entry) is a related but distinct character ("to plunder, to seize"), not confirmed as an orthographic variant of 虜 on English Wiktionary, and excluded as an alias.
