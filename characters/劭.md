## Notes
- 形声 (MC ʑɨɐuH): semantic [[Radical 019|力]] ("strength, effort") + phonetic [[召]] (MC d͡ʑiᴇuH) — to strive/exert oneself diligently (matching `korean_native: 힘쓸` and the kun'yomi つとめる), extended to "excellent, admirable, virtuous" (年高德劭 "advanced in age and eminent in virtue"), often used as a given-name element. `aliases` left blank: zh.Wiktionary places 韶 in the same phonetic-series family (系列#2490) as 劭, but explicitly does not label it a 異體字 (variant form) — a phonetic-series co-occurrence, not a genuine alias, the same false-positive pattern rejected repeatedly this session; a stray link on `Lookup/Korean/Korean Name ㅅ.md` had mistakenly pointed 韶 at this page too, corrected to an unresolved `[[韶]]` wikilink.
