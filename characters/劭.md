## Notes
- 形声 (MC ʑɨɐuH): semantic [[Radical 019|力]] ("strength, effort") + phonetic [[召]] (MC d͡ʑiᴇuH) — to strive/exert oneself diligently (matching `korean_native: 힘쓸` and the kun'yomi つとめる), extended to "excellent, admirable, virtuous" (年高德劭 "advanced in age and eminent in virtue"), often used as a given-name element. 韶 is not an alias: zh.Wiktionary places it in the same phonetic series (系列#2490) as 劭 but does not label it a 異體字 (variant form).
