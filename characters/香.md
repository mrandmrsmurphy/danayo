## Notes
- 会意 of [[Radical 202|黍]] ("glutinous millet") and [[Radical 099|甘]] ("sweet") — the fragrance of millet, used for liquor and sacrifice. In the modern form 黍 has simplified to 禾 and 甘 has come to look like the unrelated 曰.
