## Notes
- 会意 (debated; the oracle-bone form only occurs as a diviner's name): [[Radical 072|日]] ("sun") + [[Radical 181|頁]] ("head; man") — a man under the scorching sun; summer. Later forms added 止 ("foot," reflected in the modern character's assigned Kangxi radical, [[Radical 035|夊]]) or replaced 日 with 𦥑 ("two hands") before the modern glyph dropped these variants.
