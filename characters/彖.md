## Notes
- 會意: Shuowen — "豕走也。从彑从豚省" ("a running pig; from 彑ハ and an abbreviated 豚") — a running boar/hog, extended (obsolete) to "hedgehog, porcupine." Overwhelmingly known today as an *I Ching* (易經) technical term: 彖/彖辭 ("the Judgment"), the commentary attached to each of the 64 hexagrams, and by extension the verb "to judge, determine" (matching `korean_native: 판단할`).
