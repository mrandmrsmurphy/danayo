## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*sɯːŋ): semantic [[人 (char)|人]] ("person") + phonetic [[曽 (char)|曾]] — coined to transliterate Sanskrit संघ (saṃgha, "community") via the clipping 僧伽 (sōgya); "Buddhist monk, bonze," extended to "the sangha, the Buddhist community."
