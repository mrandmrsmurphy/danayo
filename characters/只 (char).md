## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): airflow coming out of a [[Radical 030|口]] ("mouth") — a modal particle; compare 四 and 曰. Alias 衹 is a legitimate alternate traditional form of this same "only, just" word (zhǐ). Alias 隻 (zhī, "classifier for one bird/pair; single") is a genuinely distinct, etymologically unrelated character — 会意 of 隹 ("short-tailed bird") + 又 ("hand"), "to hold one bird" — that merely merges into 只 under mainland Simplified Chinese orthography; listed here to reflect that real-world simplification merger, not a shared etymology.
