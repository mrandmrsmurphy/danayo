## Notes
- [List of 象形](../lookup/List%20of%20象形.md) (OC \*klje): airflow coming out of a [[Radical 030|口]] ("mouth") — a modal particle; compare 四 and 曰. Alias 衹 is a legitimate alternate traditional form of this same "only, just" word (zhǐ). Alias 隻 (zhī, "classifier for one bird/pair; single") is a genuinely distinct, etymologically unrelated character — 会意 of 隹 ("short-tailed bird") + 又 ("hand"), "to hold one bird" — that merely merges into 只 under mainland Simplified Chinese orthography; listed here to reflect that real-world simplification merger, not a shared etymology.

## Aliases

### 衹
An alternate traditional form of the "only, just" word (zhǐ); Wiktionary's 衹 page redirects to 只, and 只's page lists 衹 among its traditional forms (beside 祇 and 祗). 衹 on its own also appears in 衹衼, a monk's robe. SKIP 1-5-4; 9 strokes. Radical 145 衣. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 隻
A distinct, etymologically unrelated character (隹 + 又, "one bird; single") that merges into 只 only under mainland simplification; Wiktionary notes that in traditional script 只 stands for 隻 in only a subset of its senses, notably not for animals. Kept to reflect that real-world merger. SKIP 2-8-2; 10 strokes. Radical 172 隹. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
