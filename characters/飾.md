## Notes
- 形聲 (OC \*hljɯɡ): semantic [[巾]] ("cloth") + phonetic 飤 (OC \*ljɯɡs; a variant of [[飼]], which has the vault page — a person eating/feeding, itself phonetic on [[食 (char)|食]]) — cloth used to adorn; to decorate. The Kangxi dictionary radical is [[Radical 184|食]] rather than the true etymological semantic 巾, since 飤/飼's own 食 component visually dominates the character.
