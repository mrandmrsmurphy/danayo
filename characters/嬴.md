## Notes
- A 名専字 with three unrelated uses in Dan'a'yo: the Qin royal surname 嬴 (as in 嬴政), the mythological island 嬴洲 (properly 瀛洲, so 瀛 is its alias), and, through its alias 郢, the Chu capital Ying.
- 形声 (OC \*leŋ): semantic [[Radical 038|女]] ("woman") + phonetic 夃 — 嬴 itself serves as the phonetic component of *瀛* (semantic 氵 + phonetic 嬴); both 嬴 and [[盈]] descend from the phonetic 夃 and the Old Chinese root \*leŋ.
- [SKIP-2-3-13](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-3-13.md) ([Stroke 16](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2016.md))
- 1637th most used character in Classical Chinese, the rank of its alias 郢 (嬴 itself ranks 2005). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 以|j]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 清開|iᴇŋ]] → [⼶ㄫ](../syllables/⼶ㄫ.md)

## Aliases

### 瀛
氵 + 嬴, "sea, ocean"; built on 嬴 as phonetic and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-3-16; 19 strokes. Radical 085 水. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 郢
邑 + 呈, Ying, the capital of the state of Chu in the Spring and Autumn and Warring States periods; kept as an alias of 嬴 so the name has something to write. It has the same derived Dan'a'yo sound as 嬴, ⼶ㄫ (MC `j` + `iᴇŋ`). SKIP 1-7-3 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 10 strokes. Radical 163 邑. It ranks 1637 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
