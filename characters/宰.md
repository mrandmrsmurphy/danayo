## Notes
- 會意 of [[Radical 040|宀]] ("house") and [[乂]] ("to govern") — "house of government," i.e. "to superintend, to preside over." The modern form's lower component has graphically evolved to resemble [[辛]], though it is not a phonetic and shares no etymological link to that character.
- [SKIP-2-3-7](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-3-7.md) ([Stroke 10](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2010.md))
