## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*mrɯl): semantic [[艸]] ("grass") + phonetic [[微]] — "royal fern" (*Osmunda regalis*). Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary). Standalone 薇 means "fern," not "rose" — "rose" is only the compound sense of [[薔薇]] (#cranberry: neither 薔 nor 薇 means "rose" alone).
