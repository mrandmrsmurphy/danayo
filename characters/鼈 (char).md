### 龞

Variant form of 鼈, "soft-shelled turtle"; this is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 2-12-11; 22 strokes. Radical 195 魚. It ranks 2039 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
