## Aliases

### 皋
The common variant of 皐, written 白 over 夲 (the phonetic of 皐 with a different lower part). SKIP 2-5-5; 10 strokes. This form is the one the Classical Chinese lists rank (1271), so the `mc_id` 1271 is 皋's rank.

### 羔
Written as 皐 as a 代用字: 羔 "lamb" is a different character read gāo like 皐, with no page of its own. Radical 123 羊; SKIP 2-3-7; 10 strokes. Ranked 2205 in the Classical Chinese lists.
