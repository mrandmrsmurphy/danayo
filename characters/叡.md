## Notes
- 會意: abbreviated 㕡 ("gully") + [目 (char)](characters/目%20(char).md) ("eye") — "deep insight; astute; profound." A variant of [[睿]] (which has no independent page in this vault); later texts favor 睿, but Japan retained 叡 as the orthodox Shuowen form. In Japan, this character is sometimes replaced with 英 as a daiyōji (substitute character), though this specific claim was not independently corroborated beyond the vault's existing note.
