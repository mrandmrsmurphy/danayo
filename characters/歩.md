## Aliases

### 步
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 歩; Unihan lists 歩 as its Japanese new variant. SKIP 2-4-3 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 7 strokes. It ranks 651 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 歨
A variant spelling of 步 with an extra stroke; Wiktionary gives no definition. SKIP 2-4-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 8 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
