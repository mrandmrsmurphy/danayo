## Notes
- 會意 (OC \*ɡeː): a hand (爪) grabbing a person (大) by their braids (幺) — originally "servant, slave" (obsolete sense); the graph was borrowed 假借 for its sound to write the interrogative "where, what, how, why," the sense used here. 奚 also serves as the phonetic component in two derived characters: [[鶏 (char)|鶏]] ("chicken") and [[渓]] ("mountain stream").
