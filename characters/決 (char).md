## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*kʷeːd, \*qʰʷeːd): semantic [[Radical 085|氵]] ("water") + phonetic 夬 (OC \*kʷraːds) — originally "to breach (a dike), to channel water"; extended to "decide."
- [SKIP-1-3-4](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-4.md) ([Stroke 07](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2007.md))
- 742nd most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 見|k]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 屑合|wet]] → [ㄎ⼔ㄊ](../syllables/ㄎ⼔ㄊ.md)
- [Grade 2](../lookup/Grade%202.md), [HSK Beginner](../lookup/HSK/HSK%20Beginner.md), [Jōyō - Kyōiku](../lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kyōiku.md), [Korean MS](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20MS.md)
- The phonetic 夬 (also written 叏) has no page of its own — it is aliased here. Its other descendants: [[袂 (char)|袂]], [[快 (char)|快]], [[缺]], [[訣]].

## Aliases

### 决
The simplified Chinese form of 決 and a Japanese variant (冫 + 夬); Wiktionary's 决 page redirects to 決, and 決 lists it as its simplified form. SKIP 1-2-4; 6 strokes. Radical 015 冫. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 叏
A variant spelling of the phonetic 夬 that the page's note records as having no page of its own; no Wiktionary link to 決. Kept as a bare mention. SKIP 2-3-2; 5 strokes. Radical 029 又. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 夬
The phonetic of 決 ("to split; parted, fork"), which the page's note records as having no page of its own; Wiktionary's 夬 page redirects to 決 in some senses, and 決's page lists it as the phonetic. Its rank of 2599 is a higher number than 742. SKIP 4-4-4; 4 strokes. Radical 037 大. It ranks 2599 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.

### 蹶
No link to 決 found on Wiktionary; 蹶 (足 + 厥, "to stumble; to trample") is a separate character kept as an alias for searchers, as it shares the Mandarin reading jué. Its rank of 2431 is a higher number than 742. SKIP 1-7-12; 19 strokes. Radical 157 足. It ranks 2431 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.
