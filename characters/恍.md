## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 061|忄]] ("heart") + phonetic [[光 (char)|光]] — "seemingly, as if" (often paired with 如/若); "absent-minded." Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary). Alias 怳 is an obsolete graphic variant — 恍 displaced its usage in late Middle Chinese; en.Wiktionary also mentions 芒 as a further variant, but 芒 already has its own independent vault page, correctly excluded.
