## Notes
- 形声 (Zhengzhang \*puɑnX): semantic [[米 (char)|米]] ("rice") + phonetic [[反 (char)|反]] (OC \*panʔ) — a cake or dumpling made of rice flour, distinguishing it from the wheat-based [[餅]]. Attested since the *Yupian* (玉篇, Southern and Northern Dynasties); still current today in Hakka (pán) and Cantonese (bun2) for steamed rice cakes.
