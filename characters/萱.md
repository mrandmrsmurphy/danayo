## Aliases

### 諼
言 + 爰, "to deceive"; built on 爰, the phonetic of 萱, and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-7-9 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 16 strokes. Radical 149 言. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 谖
Simplified Chinese form of 諼. SKIP 1-2-9 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 11 strokes. Radical 149 言. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
