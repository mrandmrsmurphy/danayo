## Notes
- 形聲 (OC \*qroːn): semantic [[Radical 085|水]] ("water") + phonetic [[弯]] (a cognate of 彎, "bend, curve") — a bend in a river; a bay, a gulf. This vault keeps 湾 and [[弯]] as distinct characters rather than fully collapsing them the way Japanese shinjitai does, since 弯 retains its own independent "bend, curve" sense.
