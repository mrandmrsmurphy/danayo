## Aliases

### 廣
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 広, "wide". SKIP 3-3-12; 15 strokes. It ranks 325 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 昿
A variant form of 曠, "extensive"; kept as an alias of 広. SKIP 1-4-5; 9 strokes. Radical 072 日. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 曠
日 + 廣, "broad, vast"; a separate character kept as an alias of 広 for its meaning. SKIP 1-4-15; 18 strokes. Radical 072 日. It ranks 1445 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.

### 旷
Simplified Chinese form of 曠. SKIP 1-4-3 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 7 strokes. Radical 072 日. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 广
The Kangxi radical 53 and the simplified form of 廣; kept as an alias of 広. SKIP 4-3-4; 3 strokes. It ranks 3237 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.
