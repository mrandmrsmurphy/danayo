## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 030|口]] ("mouth") + phonetic 漢 (part of the same 𦰩 phonetic series as 難, no vault page) — "to moan, sigh, exclaim." 歎 is a traditional variant form (with its own separate Classical Chinese corpus entry) and 叹 the standard simplified form.
- [SKIP-1-3-10](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-10.md) ([Stroke 13](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2013.md))
- 1199th most used character in Classical Chinese, the rank of its alias 歎 (嘆 (char) itself is not on the lists). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 透|tʰ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 寒|ɑn]] → [ㄊㄚㄋ](../syllables/ㄊㄚㄋ.md)
- [Grade 5](../lookup/Grade%205.md), [Old HSK 3](../lookup/HSK/Old%20HSK%203.md), [Jōyō - Kōtō](../lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kōtō.md), [Korean Name ㅌ](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20Name%20ㅌ.md)

## Aliases

### 歎
欠 + 莫 element, "to sigh"; an alternative form of 嘆 and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-10-4; 15 strokes. Radical 076 欠. It ranks 1199 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 叹
Simplified Chinese form of 嘆. SKIP 1-3-2 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 5 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
