## Notes
- 形声 (OC *hmɯːŋ): semantic [[死]] ("to die") + phonetic [[瞢]] (abbreviated, OC *mɯːŋ) — Shuowen's own analysis, "从死，瞢省聲," the death of a feudal lord or high noble, distinct from 崩 (an emperor's death) and 卒/死 (a commoner's death); also an unrelated onomatopoeic sense for insects swarming/buzzing. Filed under Kangxi [[Radical 140|艸]] purely by graphical top-shape, unrelated to either sense; zh.Wiktionary's own phonetic-series grouping instead lists 夢 as a same-series character, but this reflects a looser Old Chinese rhyme grouping rather than Shuowen's own structural analysis, which this page follows.
