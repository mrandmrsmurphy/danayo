## Notes
- [List of 会意](../lookup/List%20of%20会意.md) (OC \*ŋraːm, as 巖): [[Radical 046|山]] ("hill") + [[石 (char)|石]] ("rock") — a rocky cliff or stone formation. Originally written 巖, a 形声 compound (Han dynasty); the simplified 岩 first appeared in clerical script and became the standard form.
- [SKIP-2-3-5](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-3-5.md) ([Stroke 08](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2008.md))
- 2209th most used character in Classical Chinese, the rank of its alias 巖 (岩 itself is not on the lists). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 疑|ŋ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 銜|ɣam]] → [ㄚㄇ](../syllables/ㄚㄇ.md)

## Aliases

### 巖
The original, traditional form (形声: 山 + phonetic 嚴); 岩 first appeared in the clerical script as a variant ([Wiktionary: 巖](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E5%B7%96)). 巖 has OC \*ŋraːm. SKIP 2-3-20; 23 strokes. It ranks 2209 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 巌
A Japanese variant form of 岩 listed among its forms on Wiktionary ([Wiktionary: 巌](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E5%B7%8C)). SKIP 2-3-17; 19 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 嵒
山 under 品, a variant form of 岩 and 巖 (Wiktionary's own entry just points to both), and the phonetic of 癌. It has the same sound as 岩, ㄚㄇ (MC `ŋ` + `ɣam`), so it is an alias of 岩, which is the hub for 癌 (see Derived Characters). SKIP 2-3-9; 12 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
