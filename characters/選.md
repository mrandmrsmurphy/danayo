## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*sqʰonʔ, \*sqʰons): semantic [[Radical 162|辵]] ("to walk, movement") + phonetic [[巽]] (OC \*sqʰuːns) — Shuowen glosses the whole character as 遣 ("to dispatch, send away"): to respectfully reject and send someone back; choosing implies rejecting others, hence "to choose, select."
- [SKIP-3-3-12](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-3/SKIP-3-3-12.md) ([Stroke 15](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2015.md))
