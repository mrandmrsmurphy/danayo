## Notes
- 会意 of 斦 ("two axes," no character page in this vault) + [[貝]] ("shell, valuables") — originally suggesting an exchange or valuation of objects, extended to "essence, nature, quality." Zhu Junsheng's alternative analysis reads it as phono-semantic, with 斦 as an original form of 櫍 (zhì).
