## Aliases

### 夸
大 + 于, "extravagant, luxurious; handsome"; originally a separate character, and the simplified Chinese form of 誇. It is the phonetic of 誇 and of the characters listed under Derived Characters. SKIP 2-3-3; 6 strokes. Radical 037 大. It ranks 2741 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 侉
亻 + phonetic 夸, "to speak with an accent; big and clumsy", a separate word that shares 夸's phonetic and the sound of 誇. SKIP 1-2-6; 8 strokes. Radical 009 人. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
