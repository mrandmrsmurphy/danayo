## Aliases

### 𦍌
The radical form of 羊 (Wiktionary: "radical form of 羊"), the sheep radical's own glyph; it has the same SKIP as 羊. SKIP 2-3-3 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 6 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 佯
亻 + phonetic 羊, "to pretend, to feign". A separate character that shares 羊's sound and phonetic, filed as an alias so that a search for 佯 lands here. SKIP 1-2-6; 8 strokes. Radical 009 人. It ranks 2700 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.

### 痒
疒 + phonetic 羊, "itchy" (and, archaically, "to fall ill from worry"); 癢 (疒 + 養) is the traditional form. It would only ever occur inside compounds, and ⼘ㄫ is already crowded (羊, 洋, 養, 楊, 瘍), so by the project owner's decision it is an alias of 羊 and gets no page of its own. Dan'a'yo writes "itch" as 掻羊, the 搔痒 of Chinese, Japanese and Korean with 羊 in place of 痒. SKIP 3-5-6; 11 strokes. Radical 104 疒. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
