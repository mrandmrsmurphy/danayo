## Notes
- 象形 (Lin Yiguang 1920: an ancient form of [[経 (char)|経]], its bronze-script form a pictogram of a straight line/water vein passing underground; Shuowen: 穿過土地的水脈, "a water vein penetrating through earth"). Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary) as ancestor of the *keːŋ phonetic series (經/経, 徑, 輕, 涇, etc.); 異體字 confirmed for 坙 and 𢀖 (neither has an independent vault page). Excludes 圣 (a former alias) — confirmed unrelated, being instead a variant/simplified form of [[聖]] where it is correctly listed.
