## Notes
- 会意: 勹 ("to wrap, enclose," no vault page) + [[日 (char)|日]] ("sun, day") — a full cycle/wrapping of days, the ancient ten-day period of the Chinese calendar (days named by the 十干 heavenly stems). Formerly wrongly documented here as 形声 with semantic 螾 (an unrelated earthworm character) — corrected.
