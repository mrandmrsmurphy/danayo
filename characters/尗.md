## Notes
- 象形 (OC \*hljɯwɢ): pictogram of a growing bean plant — Shuowen: "豆也。象尗豆生之形也" ("beans; depicts the shape of beans sprouting"). Later borrowed as a phonetic loan for "uncle, younger brother of father/husband" (a sense properly belonging to [[叔]], which uses 尗 as its phonetic component); also cited as an old/obsolete variant of 菽 (no independent vault page, same core "bean" meaning). Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary).
