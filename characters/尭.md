## Notes
- Japanese shinjitai simplification of 堯: 卉 (visually substituting for the true ancestral component 垚, three stacked 土) over 兀. "Lofty, excessive." Also used as a Dan'a'yo stand-in for its alias 饒 ("abundant") in compounds.

## Aliases

### 堯
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 尭, the emperor Yao and "lofty". SKIP 2-3-9; 12 strokes. Radical 032 土. It ranks 667 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 饒
食 + 堯, "abundant"; built on 堯 as phonetic and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-9-12; 20 strokes. Radical 184 食. It ranks 1509 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.

### 驍
馬 + 堯, "brave"; built on 堯 as phonetic and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-10-12; 22 strokes. Radical 187 馬. It ranks 3535 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.
