## Notes
- 會意: 臣 ("an emphasized eye, looking down") + 人 ("a bent-over person") + [[皿]] ("a basin of water") — a person bending over a basin of water to see their reflection, before bronze mirrors existed; "to look at, observe closely," extended to "to oversee, supervise." 监 is the standard simplified form.
