## Notes

Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary). 藕 ("lotus root") is not an alias: zh.Wiktionary identifies its true phonetic component as 耦 (not 禺 directly), so the two are merely part of the same broader phonetic family (諧聲系), not a genuine 異體字 relationship.
