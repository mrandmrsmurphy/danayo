## Notes
- [List of 象形](Lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): a large long-tailed, red-eyed monkey (obsolete sense); also independently attested as a variant of [[愚 (char)|愚]] ("foolish") and of [[偶]] ("to pair/match"). Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary). **False alias removed**: `藕` ("lotus root") was previously listed as an alias, but zh.Wiktionary's own entry for 藕 identifies its true phonetic component as 耦 (not 禺 directly) — the two are merely part of the same broader phonetic family (諧聲系), not a genuine 異體字 relationship; the unsupported "lotus root" `english` gloss was removed alongside it.
