## Notes
- 會意: [[爪 (char)|爪]] ("a hand reaching down") + [[木 (char)|木]] ("tree") — a hand picking fruit or leaves from a tree, "to pick, gather," extended metaphorically to "coloring, adornment," and thence to "demeanor, bearing, elegance." 採 is a derived form (手 semantic replacing 爪, "to pick" reinforced) and 埰 a rare variant meaning "a fief, feudal estate" (阝/土-adjacent sense, historically conflated with 采 in the sense of a lord's granted land).
