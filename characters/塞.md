## Notes
- 会意: oracle-bone 𡨄 (宀 "house" + 廾 "two hands" + 工 "two items" — stuffing a house with items; no vault page) + [[土]] ("soil," added later per Shuowen) — "to stop up, to block, to seal"; extended to "pass, frontier, fortress" (border fortifications being blocked passes).
