## Notes
- 形聲 (OC \*ʔslin): semantic [[Radical 085|水]] ("water") + phonetic component visually resembling 聿 (pageless in this vault) but etymologically a simplification of 盡 (per Ji Xusheng 2014) — a ferry crossing, ford; a harbor or haven; by extension, saliva and other bodily fluids.
