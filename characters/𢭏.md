## Aliases

### 擣
手 + 壽, "to pound, to pestle; to strike"; the standard form of the word 𢭏 writes. SKIP 1-3-14; 17 strokes. It ranks 3975 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 捣
Simplified Chinese form of 擣. SKIP 1-3-7 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 10 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
