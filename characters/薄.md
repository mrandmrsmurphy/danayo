## Aliases

### 鉑
金 + 白, "thin sheet of metal"; kept as an alias of 薄 for its shared sense of thinness. SKIP 1-8-5 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 13 strokes. Radical 167 金. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 铂
Simplified Chinese form of 鉑. SKIP 1-5-5 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 10 strokes. Radical 167 金. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
