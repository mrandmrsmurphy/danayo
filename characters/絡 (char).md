## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*ɡ·raːɡ): semantic [[Radical 120|糸]] ("thread") + phonetic [[各]] (OC \*klaːɡ) — wrapped in thread, a web of thread; "to enmesh, wrap around." 络 is the standard simplified form. 珞 (王/玉 semantic + 各 phonetic, "jade ornament," as in 瓔珞) was formerly wrongly listed as an alias — it is a distinct derived character, not a variant.
