## Notes
- 形声 (OC *ŋreŋ): semantic [[Radical 038|女]] ("woman") + phonetic 賏 (OC *ŋreŋ, "a string of shells worn as a necklace") — Shuowen itself analyzes 嬰 as a 會意 of 女 and 賏 rather than phonetic, but this vault follows the phono-semantic reading matching 賏's own phonetic-series data; extended from "necklace" to "to encircle, hang around the neck" and, by further extension, "infant" (an infant carried close, encircled at the breast). Also a surname.

### 纓
糸 + 嬰, "tassel, ribbon, cord; to twine" (in Japan the cord of a court cap).
