## Aliases

### 堇
"Clay" (obsolete); a variant of 菫 and kept as an alias. SKIP 2-3-8 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 11 strokes. Radical 032 土. It ranks 3348 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 槿
木 + 堇, "hibiscus"; built on 堇 and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-4-11 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 15 strokes. Radical 075 木. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 瑾
玉 + 堇, "fine jade"; built on 堇 and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-4-11 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 15 strokes. Radical 096 玉. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
