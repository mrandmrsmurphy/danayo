## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[糸]] ("silk thread") + phonetic [[旬]] (OC \*qʰʷiːns) — "brilliant, resplendent, ornate"; also "to adorn" and "to confuse, bewilder." Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary); simplified form `绚` confirmed as the sole genuine alias — en.Wiktionary's additional single-sourced Vietnamese claims `tuân`/`huyền` are not corroborated by hvdic.thivien.net (sole authority), which lists only `huyến` under both Âm Hán Việt and Âm Nôm.
