## Notes
- 象形: an earthenware jar with a lid. Dan'a'yo 缶 is aliased to its kyūjitai/traditional form [[罐]] ("kan," a small cylindrical earthenware vessel, later matched to Western canning); the CJKV readings above follow 罐 (MC \*kuɑnH) rather than 缶's own separate native reading (MC pjuwX). This character is [[Radicals/Radical 121|Radical 121]], and appears as a component inside [[陶]] and [[缸]].
