## Notes
- 形聲 (OC \*l̥ʰoːlʔ/\*l'oːlʔ): semantic [[Radical 032|土]] ("earth") + phonetic 隋 (no character page in this vault, OC \*l̥ʰoːlʔ) — "to fall; to degenerate." Simplified form of traditional 墮 (aliased); 隋 is itself 墮's own genuine ancient form, and also aliased alongside its phonetic siblings 惰/隳/橢, all independently confirmed via Wiktionary as sharing 隋 as a common ancient form (not the unrelated, similarly-pronounced 隨/随 "to follow," which was previously and mistakenly stored here). Recorded under the alias 墮's rank in `CC 1000.md`, since 堕 itself doesn't appear independently.
