## Notes
- 形聲 (OC \*l̥ʰoːlʔ/\*l'oːlʔ): semantic [[Radical 032|土]] ("earth") + phonetic 隋 (no character page in this vault, OC \*l̥ʰoːlʔ) — "to fall; to degenerate." Simplified form of traditional 墮 (aliased); 隋 is itself 墮's own genuine ancient form, also shared as the phonetic ancestor of 隳/橢 (both aliased here) and independently of [[惰]] ("lazy") — the latter is a true sibling derivation sharing this reading, not a variant of 堕 itself, and keeps its own separate page. Recorded under the alias 墮's rank in `CC 1000.md`, since 堕 itself doesn't appear independently.

## Aliases

### 隋
The Sui dynasty and a surname; the phonetic of 堕 and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-3-9; 12 strokes. Radical 170 阜. It ranks 3681 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.

### 墮
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 堕, "to fall". SKIP 2-12-3; 15 strokes. It ranks 1579 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 隳
"To destroy"; a variant of 墮 and kept as an alias. SKIP 2-12-6; 17 strokes. Radical 170 阜. It ranks 3471 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.

### 橢
木 + 隋 element, "oval"; built on 隋 and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-4-12; 15 strokes. Radical 075 木. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
