## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*ljɯs): semantic [[Radical 041|寸]] ("hand," from original 又) + phonetic [[士 (char)|士]] (originally 之/𡳿, "a footprint pointing up," OC \*tjɯ) — originally "to grasp; to hold" (preserved in the derivative [[持 (char)|持]]); phonetic 之/𡳿 became 士 (or 土 in Simplified/Japanese/Korean forms) and semantic 又 became 寸, both in the clerical/Small Seal Script transition. The character later specialized to "government office" (pre-Han) and then "Buddhist temple" (Han onward).
