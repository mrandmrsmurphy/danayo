## Notes
- 會意: one person [[大]] ("person, arms outstretched") flanked by two smaller figures [[人]] pressing in from either side — to squeeze, pinch, or clamp between; en.Wiktionary explicitly classifies this as an ideogrammic compound rather than phono-semantic. Extended to "clip, folder, holder," and (Cantonese) "matched, well-paired." `aliases: [夾]` reconfirmed correct (genuine traditional form). A second candidate, 㚒 (an unrelated character read shǎn, with no documented relationship to 夾/夹 in either source), was removed as a false positive.
