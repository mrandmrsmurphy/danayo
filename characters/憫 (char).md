## Notes
- 形聲: semantic [[Radical 061|心]] ("heart") + phonetic [[閔]] — to pity, to sympathize, to feel compassion for; sorrowful, grieved. Simplified form *悯* and variant *愍* both confirmed via both English and Chinese Wiktionary; excluded 閔/闵, which despite also being listed as a variant, has its own full character page with a distinct dominant meaning ("urge, incite").
- [SKIP-1-3-12](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-12.md) ([Stroke 15](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2015.md))
- 2204th most used character in Classical Chinese, the rank of its alias 愍 (憫 (char) itself is not on the lists). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 明|m]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 眞B開|ɣiɪn]] → [ㄇㄧㄋ](../syllables/ㄇㄧㄋ.md)

## Aliases

### 悯
Simplified Chinese form of 憫. SKIP 1-3-7 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 10 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 愍
A variant form of 憫, "to pity". SKIP 2-9-4; 13 strokes. It ranks 2204 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
