## Notes
- 会意 (OC \*ŋɯ): originally a person with a cane looking around with mouth open, not knowing where to go — to be confused, to doubt. The glyph evolved considerably across scripts (bronze script added semantic 牛; Qin scripts added semantic 子; Han clerical script recombined 子+止 into the modern 龴+[[Radical 103|疋]]) rather than settling on a stable phonetic component. **Corrected here**: the field was stored as `矢`, which no source lists as a real component of 疑 at all (likely a transcription confusion with 矣, one of the character's own historical evolved forms, not a phonetic borrowing) — corrected to `會意`.
