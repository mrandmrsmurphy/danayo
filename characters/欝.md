## Aliases

### 鬱
Traditional form of 欝, the standard form in Japanese, with 缶 ("jar") inside and 鬯彡 below. Radical 192 鬯; SKIP 2-14-15; 29 strokes. This form is the one the Classical Chinese lists rank (1620), so the `mc_id` 1620 is 鬱's rank.

### 鬰
Variant of 鬱 without the 缶. Radical 192 鬯; SKIP 2-12-15 (computed: no source lists it); 27 strokes. It has no rank in the Classical Chinese lists.
