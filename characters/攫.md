## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*kʷaɡ): semantic [[Radical 064|手]] ("hand") + phonetic 矍 (OC \*kʷaɡ) — to snatch, to seize in the hand or claws, as a bird of prey does. 矍 has no page in the vault.
- [SKIP-1-3-20](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-20.md) ([Stroke 23](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2023.md))
- 3210th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 見|k]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 藥合|ʉɐk]] → [ㄈㄛㄎ](../syllables/ㄈㄛㄎ.md)
	- Pronunciation altered to use a free syllable. Strict derivation (見 k + 藥合 ʉɐk) gives ㄍ⺢ㄎ/곽, a homophone of 郭; by fiat the Cantonese fok3 is used instead, on the free syllable ㄈㄛㄎ
