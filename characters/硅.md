## Aliases

### 矽
The Chinese-region character for silicon (xī), written 石 + 夕, read differently from 硅 (guī) but meaning the same element. SKIP 1-5-3 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 8 strokes. It has no rank in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 珪
The older character 硅 was made from, with 王 ("jade") where 硅 has 石; it is the form used in the Japanese 珪素. Radical 096 玉; SKIP 1-4-6; 10 strokes. Ranked 2281 in the Classical Chinese lists, so the `mc_id` 2281 comes from 珪.
