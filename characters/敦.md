## Notes
- 會意: originally 𦎫 (pageless in this vault; 亯 "ancestral shrine" + 羊 "sheep," a sacrificial goat offering), later reanalyzed as semantic [[攴]] + phonetic 𦎫 — honest, sincere, generous, thick; among this character's many other unrelated Classical Chinese senses (a grain vessel, a mound, to administer, clustered), only the "honest, sincere" sense is used here. 敦 also serves as the phonetic component in [[土 (char)|土]]-derived 墩 ("mound, block," pageless in this vault), which has its own unrelated dominant meaning and is not a variant of 敦.
