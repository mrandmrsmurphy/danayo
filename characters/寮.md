## Notes
- 形聲: semantic [[Radical 040|宀]] ("roof, dwelling") + phonetic 尞 (no character page in this vault) — originally a small window or a government office/bureau (surviving in [[僚]], "colleague, official"); narrowed in modern usage to a dormitory or lodging house. No aliases: zh.Wiktionary's variants 竂 and 僚 were both excluded — 僚 has its own full character page with a distinct dominant modern meaning, and 竂 is uncorroborated on English Wiktionary; English Wiktionary's own 㝋 (a defunct "second-round simplification" proposal) is likewise uncorroborated on the Chinese side.
