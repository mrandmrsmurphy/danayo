- 形声: semantic [[Radical 130|月]] ("body") + phonetic [[蔵]] ("storehouse"). Traditional 臟's own phonetic is 藏 (OC \*zaːŋ, \*zaːŋs), of which 蔵 is the shinjitai simplification.

## Aliases

### 臧
"Good, generous" (literary) and the phonetic of 臓; kept as an alias of 臓, and the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 4-15-4; 14 strokes. Radical 131 臣. It ranks 927 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 臟
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 臓, "internal organs". SKIP 1-4-18; 21 strokes. It ranks 3709 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.

### 脏
Simplified Chinese form of 臟, and (as 髒) "dirty". SKIP 1-4-6 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 10 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 髒
骨 + 葬, "dirty, filthy"; a separate character that the Chinese simplification merges with 臟, kept as an alias. SKIP 1-10-13; 21 strokes. Radical 188 骨. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
