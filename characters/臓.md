## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 130|月]] ("body") + phonetic [[蔵]] ("storehouse"). Traditional 臟's own phonetic is 藏 (OC \*zaːŋ, \*zaːŋs), of which 蔵 is the shinjitai simplification.
- [SKIP-1-4-15](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-4-15.md) ([Stroke 19](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2019.md))
- 927th most used character in Classical Chinese (ranked there under 臧, an attested variant character of 臟/臓, not the phonetic component actually drawn in this glyph). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 章|t͡ɕ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 庚二開|ɣæŋ]] → [ㄐㄚㄫ](../syllables/ㄐㄚㄫ.md)
