## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*bɯʔ): semantic [[Radical 038|女]] ("woman") + phonetic [[帚]] (OC \*bɯʔ, "broom"). The Shuowen Jiezi's traditional gloss — "a woman holding a broom," tying 帚 pictorially to domestic/sweeping duties — is a Han-dynasty folk etymology layered onto what is structurally a phono-semantic character; modern reconstruction treats 帚 primarily as the phonetic element, rhyming with 婦's own OC reading.
