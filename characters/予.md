## Notes
- 象形: a pictograph of a shuttle passing thread back and forth in weaving, "to give, hand over" — as a bound character, extended to "in advance, beforehand" (via its 豫/預 phonetic-series relatives). 豫 (a distinct but closely related character, "pleased; in advance," also the name of Henan province) and 預/预 (traditional/simplified "in advance, to reserve") share 予's OC reading and the "in advance" sense with it, historically interchanged in this meaning; kept as legitimate aliases rather than contamination.
