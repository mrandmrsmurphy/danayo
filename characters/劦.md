## Notes
- 會意: three 力 ("plough") stacked together, symbolizing collective effort — "to cooperate; same force." An alternate form of [[協]] (this page's own alias-equivalent relationship; 協 has its own independent vault page and is correctly excluded from `aliases`). Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary). ja.Wiktionary explicitly lists no kun'yomi, despite en.Wiktionary's single-sourced claim (にわか), so `japanese_native` is corrected to `ø`.
