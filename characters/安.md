## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*qaːn): semantic [[Radical 040|宀]] ("roof, shelter") + phonetic [[女 (char)]] — not the ordinary word 女 ("woman," nǚ), but a distinct, now-obsolete homographic morpheme glossed "a seated person" (sitting with legs together, buttocks resting on the heels, OC \*ʔˤan) — its own added strokes depict the other leg and aren't decorative. The popular "woman at rest under a roof" story is a folk gloss, not the scholarly analysis; the modern mismatch between 女 (nǚ) and 安 (ān) reflects this — the phonetic element was never the "woman" morpheme to begin with. (The alias 鮟 appears only in 鮟鱇魚, anglerfish.)
