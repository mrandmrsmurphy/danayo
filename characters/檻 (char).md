## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 075|木]] ("tree, wood") + phonetic [[監]] (MC k; 檻 is read with ɣ-) — "cage (for animal or prisoner)"; English Wiktionary also glosses "fence" and "railing; balustrade", an archaic "prisoner transport cart", and, read kǎn, "doorsill"; Japanese おり.
- [SKIP-1-4-14](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-4-14.md) ([Stroke 18](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2018.md))
