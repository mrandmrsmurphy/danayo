## Notes
- 形聲 (OC \*ŋɯːs): semantic [[Radical 112|石]] ("stone") + phonetic [[疑]] (OC \*ŋɯ, a loose match) — to obstruct, to hinder, to block, to deter; obstruction, hindrance. Corrects an earlier 會意 reading (石+得/㝵, "a rock obstructing one from getting what one wants") that this vault's own Notes had flagged as uncertain and unsupported by Kanjigen — both English and Chinese Wiktionary confirm 疑 as the true phonetic, placing 礙 in the same phonetic series as 疑 itself; 㝵 is a genuine but separate variant glyph of 礙 (and, unrelatedly, also the ancient form of [[得]]).
