## Aliases

### 轉
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 転, "to turn, to revolve"; this is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 1-7-11; 18 strokes. It ranks 862 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 转
Simplified Chinese form of 轉. SKIP 1-4-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 8 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
