## Aliases

### 鬪
Traditional form of 闘, "to fight". SKIP 3-10-10; 20 strokes. Radical 191 鬥. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 鬥
Radical 191 (鬥), "to fight"; the base form of 闘 and the one the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 1-5-5; 10 strokes. Radical 191 鬥. It ranks 1298 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 鬭
Variant form of 闘, "to fight". SKIP 3-10-14; 24 strokes. Radical 191 鬥. It ranks 2473 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.
