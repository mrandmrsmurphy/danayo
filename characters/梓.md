## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[木]] ("tree, wood") + phonetic [[宰]] (abbreviated) — the yellow catalpa (*Catalpa ovata*); by extension, a woodworker/craftsman, one's hometown, and, from the historical use of catalpa wood for printing blocks, "to print, publish" (付梓, "to send to press"). Also a common surname and given-name element. Genuine variant: 杍 (no independent page).
