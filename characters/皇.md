## Notes
- Originally 象形 in oracle-bone script — a single plume of a 鳳凰 ("phoenix, peacock"). Later reanalyzed as 形声 with phonetic [[王 (char)|王]] ("king," OC *waŋ) added to the pictographic feather element, tying the character to a broader word family of "radiant light, great size, vivid colour, resonant sound" alongside its royal sense.
- [SKIP-2-5-4](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-5-4.md) ([Stroke 09](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2009.md))
- 240th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 匣|ɣ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 唐合|wɑŋ]] → [ㄏ⺢ㄫ](../syllables/ㄏ⺢ㄫ.md)
