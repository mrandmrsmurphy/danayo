## Notes
- 象形: the head of a kneeling person, emphasizing the head (首 "head" + 卩 "kneeling person"); the original "head" sense is now obsolete on its own in Standard Chinese, surviving only as a semantic radical. The modern "page, sheet" sense used here is an unrelated phonetic loan for [[葉 (char)|葉]] ("leaf"), which Mandarin *yè* and Cantonese *jip6* both preserve — hence Korean routes to the separate `엽` reading rather than `혈` (the reading tied to the original "head" sense), even though this vault's own Middle Chinese/Dan'a'yo derivation still follows 頁's own native "head"-lineage reading, per its own entry in the Classical Chinese frequency corpus.
