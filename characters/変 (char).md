## Aliases

### 變
The traditional form (䜌 + 攴, "to change; to become"); Wiktionary lists 變, 変 and 变 together as one character, and 変 is its Japanese shinjitai. It carries the classical rank of 318, which is the page's `mc_id`. SKIP 2-19-4; 23 strokes. Radical 149 言. It ranks 318 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 变
The simplified Chinese form of 變 (亦 over 又). Wiktionary notes that this is the correct simplified form, and that 変 (with 夂 at the bottom) is the Japanese shinjitai that may also be seen as a handwritten variant in Chinese. SKIP 2-6-2 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 8 strokes. Radical 029 又. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
