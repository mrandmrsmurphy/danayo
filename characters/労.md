## Aliases

### 勞
The traditional form ("to toil; to trouble"); Wiktionary lists 勞, 労 and 劳 together as one character, and 労 is its Japanese shinjitai. It carries the classical rank of 542, which is the page's `mc_id`. SKIP 2-8-4; 12 strokes. It ranks 542 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 劳
The simplified Chinese form of 勞; Wiktionary lists 勞, 労 and 劳 together as one character. SKIP 2-6-2 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 8 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
