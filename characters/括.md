## Aliases

### 𠯑
氏 over 口, "to block the mouth" (obsolete), read guā; the real phonetic of 括 (手 + 𠯑), 刮, 活 and 話, and the ancestor of the modern 舌 glyph (Wiktionary lists 舌 among its alternative forms). It has the same derived Dan'a'yo sound as 括, ㄍ⺢ㄊ (MC `k` + `uɑt`), so it is an alias of 括, which is the hub for the characters built on it (see Derived Characters). SKIP 2-4-3 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 7 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists, so `mc_id` stays 1919. SKIP 2-4-3 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 7 strokes.
