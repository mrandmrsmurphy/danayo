## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*ɡreːws): phonetic [[交 (char)|交]] (unchanged and fully legible in the shinjitai) + semantic [[Radical 019|力]] ("strength, effort") — the shinjitai's own semantic component; traditional 效/傚 instead pair 交 with 攵 ("to strike; action"), a swap for a graphically/semantically adjacent radical, not a stroke-count reduction. Base sense "imitate, follow, emulate a model" extends to "the result produced by following/applying something" — "result, effect, efficacy."
- [SKIP-1-6-2](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-6-2.md) ([Stroke 08](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2008.md))
- 900th most used character in Classical Chinese (ranked under the traditional form 效). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 匣|ɣ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 肴|ɣau]] → [ㄏ⼘ㄨ](../syllables/ㄏ⼘ㄨ.md)
- [Grade 3](../lookup/Grade%203.md), [HSK No](../lookup/HSK/HSK%20No.md), [Jōyō - Kyōiku](../lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kyōiku.md), [Korean MS](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20MS.md)

## Aliases

### 效
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 効; Unihan lists 効 as its Japanese new variant, "to imitate, to devote". SKIP 1-6-4; 10 strokes. Radical 066 攴. It ranks 900 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 傚
亻 + 效, "to imitate"; a separate character kept as an alias of 効 for search. SKIP 1-2-10; 12 strokes. Radical 009 人. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
