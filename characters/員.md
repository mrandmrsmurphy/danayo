## Notes
- 形声: semantic 鼎 ("ding cauldron," corrupted into 貝 in the seal script) + phonetic 〇 — the circle ideogram, the ancestor of [[圓 (char)|圓]]. An intermediate form of 圓 ("circle"), borrowed for "member, personnel"; Shuowen misreads it as 口 + 貝.
- [SKIP-2-3-7](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-3-7.md) ([Stroke 10](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2010.md))

## Aliases

### 员
Simplified Chinese form of 員. SKIP 2-3-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 7 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 隕
阜 + 員, "to fall"; built on 員 and kept as an alias of 員. SKIP 1-3-10; 12 strokes. Radical 170 阜. It ranks 1745 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.

### 陨
