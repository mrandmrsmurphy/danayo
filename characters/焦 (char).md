## Notes
- 會意: [[隹]] ("bird") + [[火 (char)|火]] ("fire") — a bird roasting over fire, "burnt, scorched." (The graph's deeper history is a 形聲 compound — semantic 隹 + phonetic [[小 (char)|小]], for an unrelated "small bird" sense — that corrupted (小→火) and was repurposed by phonetic loan for today's "burnt" meaning during the Warring States period; per this vault's synchronic-classification policy, the now-inoperative original phonetic link is disregarded in favor of the transparent modern 會意 reading.)
