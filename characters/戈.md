## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): an ancient single-bladed dagger-axe (平頭𢧢), with a horizontal blade (援) for slashing and a curved hook (胡) below it; the vertical handle developed into the long upper-left to lower-right diagonal stroke. Extended by metonymy to "war, warfare" (干戈, literally "shield and dagger-axe"), and used as a surname.
