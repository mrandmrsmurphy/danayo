## Notes
- 形声 (OC *qonʔ): semantic [[女]] ("woman") + phonetic [[宛]] (OC *qonʔ) — gentle, tactful, amiable; also "beautiful, graceful" (matching the kun'yomi うつくしい and ja.Wiktionary's own gloss "a young beautiful woman"), and, as a secondary compliant sense, "to obey, comply" (matching the second kun'yomi したがう). Genuine variants: 䛷, 䩊. zh.Wiktionary does not corroborate the common modern compound 婉曲 ("euphemistic, indirect") as a distinct attested sense of the character itself, so it is left out per the dual-source convention.
