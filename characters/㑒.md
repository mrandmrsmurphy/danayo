## Notes
- 會意 (⿻合人): 合 ("together, unite") + 人 ("person") — "unanimous, all together." This is Japan's extended shinjitai simplification of traditional [[僉]] (this page's own alias), which remains the kyūjitai print-standard form. Also functions as a component in Jōyō kanji like 剣/検/験/倹/険. Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary). En.Wiktionary alone additionally attests kun'yomi みな/ことごとく/ともに, but ja.Wiktionary 404'd for this character, so `japanese_native` is kept at `ø` pending dual confirmation.
- [SKIP-2-2-6](Lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-2-6.md) ([Stroke 8](Lookup/Stroke/Stroke%208.md))
