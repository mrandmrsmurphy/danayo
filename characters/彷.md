## Notes
- 形声 (Zhengzhang OC \*baːŋ, \*pʰaŋʔ): semantic [[Radical 060|彳]] ("step, walk") + phonetic [[方]]. The fǎng reading (used in 彷彿) is an alternative form of 仿 "imitate"; the páng reading (used in 彷徉/彷徨 "wander, hesitate") is etymologically separate.
