## Notes
- [List of 会意](lookup/List%20of%20会意.md): 医 is itself an ancient character distinct from the traditional 醫 it now stands in for as a shinjitai simplification — [[Radical 023|匸]] ("hiding enclosure") + [[矢 (char)|矢]] ("arrow") — a tool for storing a bow and crossbow. (The traditional 醫, by contrast, is 形声: phonetic 殹 + semantic 酉 "liquor.")
