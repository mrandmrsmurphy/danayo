## Notes
- Originally 禾 ("cereal plant") + 口 + 刀 ("knife"), "to cut/harvest cereal plants"; on bronze inscriptions the left side was stylized into a shape resembling [[柬 (char)|柬]] + 刀 (OC \*raːd), and the meaning shifted to "to contradict, to violate"; the "lively" sense used here is specific to compounds like 発剌.
