## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*pruː): semantic [[石 (char)|石]] ("stone") + phonetic [[包]] — originally a catapult that launched stones, later extended to "cannon" as gunpowder weapons replaced siege engines. Its variant [[炮]] swaps the semantic component to 火 ("fire"), reflecting that same historical shift.
