## Notes
- 會意 (OC \*rteːwɢ): a child (子) with a flag on its head, or a man standing on an altar illuminated by the sun (昜) — "tall and erect; profound; brilliant; lofty; eminent." The modern form resembles 早, replacing the original child/altar element. `aliases` lists 㔬 (an ancient variant) and 龺 (both pageless in this vault; 龺 is a variant of the pageless 𠦝, itself a variant of 卓).
