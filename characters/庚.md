## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): depicts an ancient flail or threshing tool, its oracle-bone and bronze forms showing a cross-bar with hanging cords or weights used to beat grain; the pictograph was borrowed for the abstract seventh-heavenly-stem sense and a surname, unrelated to its original threshing-tool meaning. Filed under Kangxi [[Radical 053|广]] ("dwelling") purely by graphical top-shape, unrelated to either sense. `korean_native: 별` ("star") is a genuine third sense, not an error — Korean dictionaries (어문회 훈음 "별 경") attribute it to the compound [[長庚]] (장경, *Jangyeong*), a classical name for Venus as the evening star, alongside its more common "age" sense (as in 庚帖, a matchmaking age-declaration document).
