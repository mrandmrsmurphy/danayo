## Notes
- 形声 (OC *kluɡ): semantic [[手]] ("hand") + phonetic [[匊]] (OC *kluɡ, itself a 會意 character meaning "to scoop with cupped hands") — to grasp or scoop up with both hands, extended to "a handful." zh.Wiktionary's own 異體字 list explicitly names 匊 as a genuine variant spelling of the same word, not merely a phonetic-series relative, so it is kept in `aliases` alongside its citation here as the phonetic component.
