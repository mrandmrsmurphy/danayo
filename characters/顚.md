## Notes
- 形声 (⿰眞頁): semantic [[頁|頁 (char)]] ("head") + phonetic [[真 (char)|眞]] — "summit, top of the head," extended to "to fall, topple" and "epilepsy, insanity." Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary). 顚 is a traditional/kyūjitai variant of 顛 (this page's own `aliases`, along with the simplified 颠 and the ancient variant 㒹). zh.Wiktionary's variant list explicitly excludes 癫/癲 ("insane, mad" — a distinct character), which were removed from `aliases` accordingly.
- [SKIP-1-10-9](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-10-9.md) ([Stroke 19](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2019.md))
- 1719th most used character in Classical Chinese, the rank of its alias 顛 (顚 itself is not on the lists). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 端|t]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 先開|en]] → [ㄉㄝㄋ](../syllables/ㄉㄝㄋ.md)
- [Grade Name](../lookup/Grade%20Name.md), [HSK No](../lookup/HSK/HSK%20No.md), [Jinmeiyō](../lookup/Japanese/Jinmeiyō.md), [Korean Name ㅈ](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20Name%20ㅈ.md)

## Aliases

### 顛
Variant form of 顚, "top of the head; to overturn"; this is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 1-10-9; 19 strokes. It ranks 1719 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 颠
Simplified Chinese form of 顛. SKIP 1-10-6 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 16 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
