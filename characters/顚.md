## Notes
- 形声 (⿰眞頁): semantic [頁 (char)](characters/頁.md) ("head") + phonetic [[眞]] — "summit, top of the head," extended to "to fall, topple" and "epilepsy, insanity." Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary). 顚 is a traditional/kyūjitai variant of 顛 (this page's own `aliases`, along with the simplified 颠 and the ancient variant 㒹). zh.Wiktionary's variant list explicitly excludes 癫/癲 ("insane, mad" — a distinct character), which were removed from `aliases` accordingly.
