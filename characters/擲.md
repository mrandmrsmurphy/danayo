## Aliases

### 掷
Simplified Chinese form of 擲. SKIP 1-3-8 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 11 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 擿
"To select, to pick out"; a variant of 擲 and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-3-15; 17 strokes. It ranks 3583 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
