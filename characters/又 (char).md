## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): depicts a right hand; the original form of [[右]] ("right (direction)"), which later split off as a dedicated derivative. The "again, also" sense is not a direct pictographic extension — Wiktionary treats it as an adverbial derivation of [[有 (char)|有]] (OC \*ɢʷɯʔ, "to have; there is"), sharing its sound and semantic root rather than the hand image itself.
