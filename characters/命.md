## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 030|口]] ("mouth") + phonetic [[令 (char)|令]] — shares a root with 名 ("name"): "name" → "to name, to give (a name to)" → "to order, command" → "order, command" → "destiny, fate, lot" → "life," paralleling Latin *fāta* ("fate") from *fātus* ("spoken").
