## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*ɡraːŋ): phonetic [[Radical 144|行]] (OC \*ɡaːŋ, \*ɡaːŋs, \*ɡraːŋ, \*ɡraːŋs) + semantic [[角 (char)|角]] ("horn") and 大 ("big/person," no vault page) — a person with a single horn (unbalanced, unlike a matched pair), shifted to "to weigh, measure, balance." Formerly wrongly documented here with the semantic/phonetic roles swapped and 行 mislabeled "horn" (行 means "to go, road"; 角 is the true "horn" component).
- [SKIP-1-3-13](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-13.md) ([Stroke 16](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2016.md))
- 809th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 匣|ɣ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 庚二開|ɣæŋ]] → [ㄏㄚㄫ](syllables/ㄏㄚㄫ.md)
- [Grade 5](lookup/Grade%205.md), [Old HSK 3](lookup/HSK/Old%20HSK%203.md), [Jōyō - Kōtō](lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kōtō.md), [Korean HS](lookup/Korean/Korean%20HS.md) — added to the Korean high school hanja list in 2000.
