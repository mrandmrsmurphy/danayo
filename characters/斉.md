## Notes
- 象形 (OC \*ɦdziːl, \*ɦdziːls): a pictogram of evenly sprouting grain ears (or, per some sources, neatly arranged fruits/thorns) — "even, uniform, together, simultaneous." 齊 is the traditional form, 齐 an informal/simplified variant; the vault's own graphemic form is the Japanese shinjitai 斉. 齊 also serves as the phonetic component of the distinct, unrelated-in-meaning character 齎 (jī, "to present in both hands," 齊-phonetic + 貝-semantic) — 齎 was formerly wrongly listed as an alias of 斉 here, but it is a separate derived character, not a variant.
