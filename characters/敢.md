## Notes
- 会意 of [[Radical 029|又]] ("hand") using a capturing tool 𰅱 (no character page exists for it in this vault; later omitted) to hunt [[豕]] ("wild boar") — the components corrupted into a form resembling [[Radical 066|攴]], with a redundant 口/甘 added along the way; that resemblance is why the character is filed under that Kangxi radical, though it isn't a real semantic component. "To dare" is a semantic extension of the original hunting sense.
