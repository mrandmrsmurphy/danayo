## Notes
- 象形 ([List of 象形](../lookup/List%20of%20象形.md)): a newborn baby, shown upside down — the upper portion is an inverted 子, the three lower lines representing amniotic fluid or hair (supported by the comparison of 旒 and 游, originally variants of one another). Functions as a variant form of six distinct characters (荒, 不, 巟, [[旒]], 突, 育); the `english` gloss stored here reflects its use as a variant of 旒 specifically ("jade strings from a crown; ornamental streamers"). Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary). `とつ` is an on'yomi reading (en.Wiktionary lists it under Kan-on), so `japanese_native` is `ø`; the possible kun'yomi あらい/あれる/あらす/すさむ/あれ/はたあし could not be dual-confirmed and are not listed.
