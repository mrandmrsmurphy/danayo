## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*ben, \*beŋ): semantic 艸 ("plant") + phonetic [[平]] — "duckweed" (an alternative form of 萍); also Artemisia argyi (藾蕭). Simplified form of 蘋, under which meaning it was borrowed purely for its sound to write "apple" (苹果), an unrelated Silk-Road loanword.
