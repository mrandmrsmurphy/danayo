## Aliases

### 晕
Simplified Chinese form of 暈. SKIP 2-4-6 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 10 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 煇
火 + 軍, "to weld"; built on 軍, the phonetic of 暈, and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-4-9; 13 strokes. Radical 086 火. It ranks 3568 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
