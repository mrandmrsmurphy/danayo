## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*qʰramʔ, on traditional 險): semantic [[Radical 170|阜]] ("mound, hill") + phonetic 僉 ("to answer together, unanimously," no vault page) — 険 itself is the Japanese shinjitai simplification, where 僉 was further simplified to 㑒 (this page's own stored phonetic value). "Precipitous, dangerous."
- [SKIP-1-3-8](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-8.md) ([Stroke 11](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2011.md))
