## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*raːŋʔ): semantic [[Radical 074|月]] ("moon") + phonetic [[良]] (OC \*raŋ) — clear moonlight; "clear, distinct." **Corrected here**: the components were swapped and misglossed in the old bullet ("semantic 良 ('moon') + phonetic 月") — 良 means "good," not "moon," and Wiktionary confirms 月 is the semantic "moon" component while 良 supplies the sound.
- [SKIP-1-6-4](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-6-4.md) ([Stroke 10](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2010.md))
