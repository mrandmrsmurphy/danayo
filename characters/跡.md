## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 157|足]] ("foot") + phonetic [[亦 (char)|亦]] (an irregular, well-documented phonetic series despite the modern initial divergence) — "footprints, tracks," extended to "vestige, trace." 迹 (with 辶 semantic "to go" instead of 足) is a standard variant, with its own separate Classical Chinese corpus entry; 蹟 and 𫐤 are further attested variant forms (e.g. 奇跡/奇蹟), none with distinct vault pages — all kept as aliases.
- [SKIP-1-7-6](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-7-6.md) ([Stroke 13](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2013.md))
- 1236th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 精|t͡s]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 昔開|iᴇk]] → [ㄐㄝㄎ](../syllables/ㄐㄝㄎ.md)
- [Grade 5](../lookup/Grade%205.md), [Old HSK 3](../lookup/HSK/Old%20HSK%203.md), [Jōyō - Kōtō](../lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kōtō.md), [Korean HS](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20HS.md)

## Aliases

### 迹
Variant form of 跡, "footprint, trace"; 辶 + 亦, the form Chinese standardised and Japanese also uses. SKIP 3-4-6; 9 strokes. Radical 162 辵. It ranks 2841 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.

### 蹟
Variant form of 跡, "footprint, trace; remains"; 足 + 責. SKIP 1-7-11; 18 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
