## Notes
- 會意 (traditional, per Shuowen): [[Radicals/Radical 022|匚]] ("box") + [[斤 (char)|斤]] ("axe") — a craftsman's toolbox; "artisan, craftsman." Modern scholarship (Zhengzhang 2003; Baxter–Sagart 2014) instead treats this as 形声, with 匚 functioning as phonetic and 斤 as semantic — the two analyses remain genuinely contested.
