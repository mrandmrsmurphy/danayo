## Notes
- 形声 (OC *l̥ʰaːl): semantic [[手]] ("hand") + phonetic [[它]] — to drag, tow, haul; extended to "to delay, procrastinate" (拖延, 拖拉). zh.Wiktionary's own entry names the phonetic component 㐌 rather than 它, a graphical-variant naming of the same seal-script element rather than a genuine composition disagreement. Genuine variants: 拕, 扡 (dual-source confirmed; neither has an independent page — 柂/搋, single-source zh.Wiktionary-only, left out).
