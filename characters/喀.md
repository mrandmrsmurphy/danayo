## Notes
- 形声 (OC *kʰraːɡ): semantic [[口]] ("mouth") + phonetic [[客]] (OC *kʰraːɡ) — an onomatopoeic word for the sound of coughing or retching, "to vomit" (matching `korean_native: 토할`); also widely used as a transliteration syllable in place names such as 喀什 (Kashgar) and 喀麥隆 (Cameroon). Genuine variants: 䘔, 衉.
