- 形声 (OC \*kramʔ): semantic [[Radical 075|木]] ("wood") + phonetic [[㑒|僉]] (OC \*skʰlam) — perhaps originally the blade on top of a wooden spear (partly related to 鐱 > 劍, "sword"); extended to "examine, check."
- [SKIP-1-4-8](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-4-8.md) ([Stroke 12](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2012.md))

## Aliases

### 檢
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 検, "to examine"; this is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 1-4-13; 17 strokes. It ranks 2474 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 检
Simplified Chinese form of 檢. SKIP 1-4-7 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 11 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
