## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): a kneeling man or figure with an ugly face (甶) — the ugly face may represent a mask. The lower-right "dot" is a small 厶, a residual tail added later (compare 离). Related to 畏 (variant 𠂽), which originally depicts a ghost standing with a stick in hand. Sense: "ghost, demon"; also used as a neologism short for "cobalt" (see [[鬼金]]).
