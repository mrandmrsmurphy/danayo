## Notes
- Originally a pictogram of a crested, long-tailed mythical bird, later reinforced with phonetic [[凡]] (OC \*bam) — the phoenix, the mythical king of birds and emblem of imperial legitimacy. Variant [[鳯]] confirmed via both English and Chinese Wiktionary; excluded 风 (English Wiktionary's listing was misleading — 风/風 "wind" is a wholly separate, unrelated character that merely shares the same 凡 phonetic root, not a genuine variant of 鳳, confirmed via Chinese Wiktionary).
