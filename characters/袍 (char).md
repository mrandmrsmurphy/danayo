## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 145|衣]] ("clothing", written 衤) + phonetic [[包 (char)|包]] — English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 袍 as "(Chinese-style) long gown, robe, or cloak" and lists it in the phonetic series of 勹 and 包. It glosses Japanese ほう as a round-collared, skirted over-robe, the outermost layer of court dress worn by officials.
- [SKIP-1-5-5](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-5-5.md) ([Stroke 10](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2010.md))
- Not present in the Classical Chinese usage ranking. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 並|b]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 豪|ɑu]] → [ㄆ⼘ㄨ](../syllables/ㄆ⼘ㄨ.md): the aspirated ㄆ is a minority outcome of 並, and ⼘ㄨ is the vowel of 包's own family (砲, 胞, 飽, 跑); the syllable is shared with 票.
