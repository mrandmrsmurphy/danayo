## Aliases

### 靈
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 霊, "spirit, soul"; this is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 2-8-16; 24 strokes. It ranks 621 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 灵
Simplified Chinese form of 靈, "spirit, soul". SKIP 2-3-4; 7 strokes. Radical 086 火. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
