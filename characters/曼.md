## Notes
- 會意: perhaps two hands opening an eye, or two hands over the eyebrows to indicate looking out over a wide place — "long, beautiful; wide, extensive." 借代字 (rebus substitution): also stands in for 鰻 ("eel"), hence [[曼魚]] "eel" — 鰻 itself is absent from the HSK and Japanese lookup lists but does appear on the Korean name list, confirmed by `Lookup/Japanese/Hyōgai.md`'s own "鰻 --> 曼" substitution note. A name-only character with no independent stand-in, since three genuine compounds ([[日耳曼]] "Germany," [[奥斯曼]] "Ottoman," and [[曼魚]] "eel") cite it with distinct, unrelated senses — flagged rather than forced to one.
