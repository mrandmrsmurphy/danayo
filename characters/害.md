## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): etymology genuinely disputed among scholars — this vault follows Guo Moruo and Dai Jiaxiang's reading as a pictogram, the ancient form of 蓋 ("lid, cover"); other proposed theories include 指事 (He Linyi: a spear + a distinguishing mark), 形聲 (Li Xueqin and the Shuowen), and 假借 (Gao Hongying: originally the character for 桷, "rafters," borrowed for sound). Regardless of origin, the meaning is "to harm, injure," extended to "disaster, calamity."
