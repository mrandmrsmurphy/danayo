## Notes
- Perhaps a pictogram (象形) of a crack in an oracle bone, used for divination in ancient China. Unrelated to [[蔔]] ("radish") — its own page explains that 卜 is only a coincidental modern character-merger substitute for it in Mandarin, not a true variant, and is correctly excluded from its aliases.
