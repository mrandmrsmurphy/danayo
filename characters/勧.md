## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*kʰons): semantic [[Radical 019|力]] ("strength, effort") + phonetic 雚 (OC \*koːns, no vault page) — the shinjitai 勧 retains the same phonetic identity, its right-hand component being only a stroke-simplified rendering of 雚, not a substitution. Not to be confused with [[鸛]] ("stork"), itself a *derived* character built from 雚+鳥 — a mistake the field previously made.
