## Notes
- 會意: 人 ("person") + 彡 ("hair") — a variant form of [[鬒]] (this page's own alias); Shuowen glosses this composition as "newly grown feathers for flying," though the character's actual attested meaning is "black hair; thick hair," matching 鬒. Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary); `joyo_level` left blank since neither source gives a Japanese classification for this rare Extension A character.
