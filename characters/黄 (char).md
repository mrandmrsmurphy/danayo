## Notes
- 形声/会意 (per Shuowen, though now considered erroneous — the top component 廿 is a corruption of 口 in the bronze inscription, not a real 田 "field"): phonetic 炗 ("light") + semantic 田. Modern scholarship instead proposes 黄 was originally 象形, either a disabled person or a man wearing a ring of jade on his chest, with "yellow" arising as a rebus/phonetic-loan sense. Fifth most common Vietnamese surname.
- [SKIP-2-4-7](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-4-7.md) ([Stroke 11](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2011.md))
- 228th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 匣|ɣ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 唐合|wɑŋ]] → [ㄏ⺢ㄫ](../syllables/ㄏ⺢ㄫ.md)
- [Grade 1](../lookup/Grade%201.md), [HSK Beginner](../lookup/HSK/HSK%20Beginner.md), [Jōyō - Kyōiku](../lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kyōiku.md), [Korean MS](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20MS.md)

## Aliases

### 黃
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 黄, written with the fuller 廿 + 田 + 八 below; the shinjitai 黄 simplifies the bottom. SKIP 2-4-8 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 12 strokes. Radical 201 黃. It ranks 228 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 簧
竹 + phonetic 黄, "reed of a musical instrument; spring, coil", a separate character that shares 黄's sound and phonetic. SKIP 2-6-12; 17 strokes. Radical 118 竹. It ranks 3774 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.

### 潢
氵 + phonetic 黃, "expanse of water, pond; to dye paper". It is a separate word, not a form of 黄, but Dan'a'yo would give it the same sound, ㄏ⺢ㄫ (MC `ɣ` + `wɑŋ`), and 黄 is its phonetic source, so it is kept as an alias of 黄 by the project owner's decision (黄 is already the hub for the characters built on it). SKIP 1-3-12; 14 strokes. Radical 085 水. It ranks 3554 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.
