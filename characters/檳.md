## Notes
- 形声 (OC *pin, MC pjin): semantic [[木]] ("tree") + phonetic [[賓]] — used chiefly as the bound first half of [[words/檳榔|檳榔]] "betel palm; areca nut," together with its cranberry-tagged sibling [[characters/榔|榔]]; further independent senses exist in living Chinese (Hong Kong/Macau "bun," Malaysia/Singapore's 檳城 "Penang") but have no citation anywhere in this vault. Genuine dual-source variants: [[梹]] and [[槟]] (簡化字); a further single-source-only candidate on each side, 㯽 (en.Wiktionary) and 㰋 (zh.Wiktionary), was excluded for lacking corroboration.
