## Notes
- 形聲/會意: semantic [[Radical 149|言]] ("speech") + phonetic 咨 (a genuine variant of 諮 itself, not a separate character page in this vault) — to consult with, to seek advice from, to ask about. The true phonetic/original form is 咨 (confirmed in English and Chinese Wiktionary), listed among this character's own aliases. Variants *咨* and *谘* both confirmed via both Wiktionary editions.
- [SKIP-1-7-9](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-7-9.md) ([Stroke 16](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2016.md))
- 2149th most used character in Classical Chinese, the rank of its alias 咨 (諮 itself is not on the lists). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 精|t͡s]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 脂A三開|iɪ]] → [ㄐㄧㄜ](../syllables/ㄐㄧㄜ.md)

## Aliases

### 咨
口 + 次, "to consult; an official document"; the older form of 諮 and the phonetic that 諮 is built on. SKIP 2-6-3; 9 strokes. Radical 030 口. It ranks 2149 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 谘
Simplified Chinese form of 諮. SKIP 1-2-9 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 11 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
