## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*mrɯːɡ): semantic 夊 ("footprint pointing down; to walk slowly" — Shuowen: the ancient belief that wheat came from the heavens) + phonetic [[来 (char)|来]] (OC \*m·rɯːɡ) — historically unrelated to 来's own "to come" sense; 麥 was likely coined during the Warring States period to preserve the original "wheat" meaning after 來 was phonetically borrowed away.
