## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*zuːb, on traditional 雜): semantic [[衣]] ("clothing") + phonetic [[集]] (OC \*zub, cognate) — originally a combination of various colors/dyed clothes; "miscellaneous, mixed." Kangxi-indexed under [[Radical 172|隹]] rather than the etymological semantic 衣.
- [SKIP-1-6-8](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-6-8.md) ([Stroke 14](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2014.md))
