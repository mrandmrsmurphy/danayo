### 熒
The phonetic of 螢/蛍 and a character in its own right ("luminous; to shine"), the same reading (OC \*ɡʷeːŋ, Korean 형). 蛍 is the firefly, the luminous insect, so 熒 is kept as an alias of 蛍 by the project owner's decision, and 蛍 is the hub for the characters built on 熒 (see Derived Characters).
