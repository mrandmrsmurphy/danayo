## Notes
- 形声 (OC *la, *laʔ): semantic [[手]] ("hand") + phonetic [[予]] — to draw out, express, pour out (feelings); also "to eliminate, relieve." 抒 and 舒 (a distinct character, "comfortable, to stretch out," semantic 舍 + phonetic 予) are unrelated words sharing only the 予 phonetic series — 舒 has no independent page in this vault.
