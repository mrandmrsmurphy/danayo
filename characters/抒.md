## Notes
- 形声 (OC *la, *laʔ): semantic [[手]] ("hand") + phonetic [[予]] — to draw out, express, pour out (feelings); also "to eliminate, relieve." 抒 and 舒 (a distinct character, "comfortable, to stretch out," semantic 舍 + phonetic 予) are unrelated words sharing only the 予 phonetic series; 舒 is kept as this page's alias, so that a Korean name written with 舒 has a character to use.
- [SKIP-1-3-4](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-4.md) ([Stroke 07](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2007.md))
- 1011th most used character in Classical Chinese, the rank of its alias 舒 (抒 itself ranks 5013, beyond the top ~4000 mirrored locally). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 邪|z]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 魚|ɨʌ]] → [ㄙ⼄](../syllables/ㄙ⼄.md)

## Aliases

### 舒
舍 + 予, "to open up, stretch out, relax; comfortable, leisurely" (as in 舒服 and 舒展) and a surname. It is a separate word from 抒, with which it shares only the phonetic 予 and the Dan'a'yo sound, ㄙ⼄ (MC `ɕ` + `ɨʌ`), but it is on the Korean Name list and in the HSK lists, and a Korean name written with it needs a character to use, so by the project owner's decision it is an alias of 抒 and gets no page of its own. SKIP 1-8-4 (EDRDG); 12 strokes. It ranks 1011 in the Classical Chinese lists, better than 抒's 5013, so it sets the page's `mc_id`. SKIP 1-8-4; 12 strokes. Radical 135 舌.
