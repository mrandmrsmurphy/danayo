## Notes
- 形声 (OC *la, *laʔ): semantic [[手]] ("hand") + phonetic [[予]] — to draw out, express, pour out (feelings); also "to eliminate, relieve." 抒 and 舒 (a distinct character, "comfortable, to stretch out," semantic 舍 + phonetic 予) are unrelated words sharing only the 予 phonetic series — a stray prior note ("舒=C#1011") recorded 舒's own Classical Chinese frequency rank, not 抒's; 舒 has no independent page in this vault, and several lookup files (`Lookup/HSK/HSK Beginner.md`, `Old HSK 1–4.md`, `Lookup/Korean/Korean Name ㅅ.md`) had mistakenly redirected the glyph "舒" to this page — corrected to bare unresolved `[[舒]]` links.
