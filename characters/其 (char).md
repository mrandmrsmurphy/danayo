## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): originally 𠀠, depicting a winnowing basket; bronze script added a stand below (丌, resembling [[Radical 012|八]]'s splayed legs), possibly also functioning as a phonetic hint. The basket sense was later inherited by 箕 (OC \*kɯ, with 竹 "bamboo" added), while 其 itself was phonetically borrowed (假借) to write a demonstrative/possessive pronoun and grammatical particle — the concrete image effectively abandoned for a purely grammatical function.
