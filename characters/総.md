## Aliases

### 總
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 総, "overall"; this is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 1-6-11; 17 strokes. It ranks 1710 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 𰬥
A rare variant form of 総; Wiktionary gives no definition. SKIP 1-3-8 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 11 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 总
Simplified Chinese form of 總. SKIP 2-5-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 9 strokes. Radical 061 心. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 惣
A variant form of 総 used in Japanese, "all". SKIP 2-8-4; 12 strokes. Radical 061 心. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 綜
糸 + 宗, "to put together, to assemble"; a separate character kept as an alias of 総 for its meaning. SKIP 1-6-8; 14 strokes. It ranks 3455 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.
