## Notes
- [List of 象形](../lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): a pictogram of a bolt of lightning — the original form of both [[電]] ("lightning") and [[神 (char)|神]] ("god, deity, spirit"). The lightning sense is now obsolete in standard usage; the character survives instead for its borrowed 地支 (Earthly Branch) sense, "to extend" (describing the waxing half-moon's concave edge), the ninth branch associated with the monkey.
