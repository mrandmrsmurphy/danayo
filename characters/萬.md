## Notes
- 象形: originally a pictogram depicting a scorpion (Shuowen: "萬，蟲也"), later phonetically borrowed for the numeral sense "ten-thousand, myriad." Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary). This is the 大字 (formal/anti-forgery) form of [[万]] ("ten-thousand"), retained as its own independent page rather than an alias — matching this vault's established treatment of anti-forgery numeral pairs (precedent: [[characters/漆 (char)|漆 (char)]] for [[七 (char)|七]]).
