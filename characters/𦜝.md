## Aliases

### 臍
肉 + 齊, "navel; abdomen"; the standard form of the word 𦜝 writes. SKIP 1-4-14; 18 strokes. It ranks 3429 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 脐
Simplified Chinese form of 臍. SKIP 1-4-6 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 10 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
