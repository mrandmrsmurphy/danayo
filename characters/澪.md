## Notes
- 形声 (OC *reːŋ, MC leŋ): semantic [[水 (char)|水]] ("water") + phonetic [[零]] — "the name of a body of water," dual-source confirmed as an alternative form of *泠* (no independent page in this vault); extended in Japanese to a specific "navigable waterway/channel" sense (as in 澪標/みおつくし, channel-marking stakes) and "a ship's wake."

## Aliases

### 泠
氵 + 令, "cool, refreshing"; kept as an alias of 澪, with which it shares the phonetic 令. SKIP 1-3-5; 8 strokes. It ranks 3327 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
