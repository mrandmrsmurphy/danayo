- 形声 (OC \*ŋaʔ, \*ŋˤaʔ): semantic [[Radical 149|言]] ("speech; words") + phonetic [[吾]] (OC \*ŋa, \*ŋˤa) — originally "to face someone with speech," distinguishing it from 言 (the speech act itself); language, speech.
