## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 130|肉]] ("flesh") + phonetic [[包]] (bāo, "to wrap") — "womb, placenta" (that which wraps the fetus), extended to "membrane, sac," and via the modern coinage 細胞 to "biological cell."
