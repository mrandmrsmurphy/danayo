## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 170|阜]] ("mound," a hiding place) + phonetic 㥯 (no vault page) — "to hide, conceal." 隱 is the traditional form; 乚 is a further attested archaic variant, with no vault page of its own. 湮 (yān, "to sink, submerge, obliterate," 水 semantic — a distinct character with a different pronunciation and etymology) was formerly wrongly listed as an alias — removed.
