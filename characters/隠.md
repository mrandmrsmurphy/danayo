## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 170|阜]] ("mound," a hiding place) + phonetic 㥯 (no vault page) — "to hide, conceal." 隱 is the traditional form; 乚 is a further attested archaic variant, with no vault page of its own. 湮 (yān, "to sink, submerge, obliterate," 水 semantic) is a distinct character with a different pronunciation and etymology, not an alias.
- [SKIP-1-3-11](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-11.md) ([Stroke 14](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2014.md))
- 687th most used character in Classical Chinese (recorded under the traditional form 隱). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 影|ʔ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 欣|ɨn]] → [ㄜㄋ](../syllables/ㄜㄋ.md)
- [Grade 5](../lookup/Grade%205.md), [Old HSK 6](../lookup/HSK/Old%20HSK%206.md), [Jōyō - Kōtō](../lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kōtō.md), [Korean HS](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20HS.md)

## Aliases

### 隱
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 隠, written 阜 + 㥯; Unihan lists 隠 as its Japanese new variant. SKIP 1-3-14; 16 strokes. It ranks 687 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 㐆
A reversed 身 (a pregnant woman), "to turn one's body; to return, to submit to"; Wiktionary records it as an alternative form of 隱 and it is the component of 殷 (㐆 + 殳). It has the same derived Dan'a'yo sound as 隠, ㄜㄋ (MC `ʔ` + `ɨn`), so it is an alias of 隠, which is the hub for 殷 (see Derived Characters). SKIP 3-1-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 5 strokes. Radical 004 丿. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 乚
A single-stroke archaic form that Wiktionary records as an alternative form of 隠 (Unihan: a semantic variant). SKIP 4-1-4; 1 stroke. Radical 005 乙. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
