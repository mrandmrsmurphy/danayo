## Notes
- 会意 of [[Radical 085|氵]] ("water," level and fair) + [[去 (char)|去]] ("to go away, to expel") — law as fair as the level surface of water, expelling wrongdoing. The full ancient bronze form 灋 additionally included 廌 (a mythical goat-like creature said to gore the guilty), later dropped in simplification.
