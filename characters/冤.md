## Notes
- 會意 of [[Radical 014|冖]] ("cover") and [[兔]] ("rabbit") — a rabbit trapped/confined under a covering, depicting confinement and helplessness, extended to "wronged, unjustly treated, grievance." en.Wiktionary explicitly labels this an ideogrammic compound (會意), not phono-semantic, matching the phonetic implausibility of 兔 (tù) for 冤 (yuān); zh.Wiktionary nonetheless groups 冤 under 兔's own phonetic-series page (系列#1876), a graphemic grouping convention rather than an assertion of genuine phonetic derivation. Genuine variant: 寃.
