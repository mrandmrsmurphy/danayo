### 粲
米 under 𣦼 as phonetic (the phonetic of 燦), meaning "polished white rice; bright, radiant"; Wiktionary says it is the same word as 燦, which is 粲 with 火 added. Dan'a'yo would give it the same sound as 燦, ㄑㄚㄋ (MC `t͡sʰ` + `ɑn`), so it is kept as an alias of 燦 by the project owner's decision, with 燦 as the hub for the characters built on 粲 (see Derived Characters).
