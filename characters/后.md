## Notes
- 会意: [[人]] (variant form) + [[口]] ("hole") — per Kōjien, originally depicted a person's rear/anus, extending to "behind, rear" and then "ruler, sovereign" (compare 后稷, mythological ancestor); oracle bone forms were often written as 毓 (a woman giving birth), with the meaning extending from "childbirth/succession" to "empress." Distinct from [[後]] (会意: 幺 "thread" + 夊 "foot," a foot bound by rope, "to lag behind") — the two are etymologically unrelated characters that happen to share the modern Mandarin reading hòu; 后 was adopted as 後's simplified substitute in PRC orthography, which is why 後 had been wrongly listed as an alias here.
