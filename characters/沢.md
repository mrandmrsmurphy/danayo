## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 085|水]] ("water") + phonetic [[尺 (char)|尺]] (a Japanese shinjitai simplification of the traditional phonetic 睪, no vault page) — "marsh, swamp, stream," extended metaphorically to "beneficence, grace flowing outward like water." 澤 is the traditional form and 泽 the standard simplified form.
