## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 085|水]] ("water") + phonetic [[尺 (char)|尺]], a Japanese shinjitai simplification of the traditional phonetic 睪 (no vault page). 澤 is the traditional form and 泽 the standard simplified form. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 澤 as "marsh", "pool; pond" and "moist; damp", and Japanese 沢 as "marsh, wetlands", "in abundance; blessed; favored", "blessing; favor", "sheen, lustre" and "mountain stream".
- [SKIP-1-3-4](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-4.md) ([Stroke 07](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2007.md))
- 549th most used character in Classical Chinese (recorded under the traditional form 澤). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 澄|ɖ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 陌二開|ɣæk]] → [ㄉㄚㄎ](../syllables/ㄉㄚㄎ.md)
- [Grade 5](../lookup/Grade%205.md), [HSK No](../lookup/HSK/HSK%20No.md), [Jōyō - Kōtō](../lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kōtō.md), [Korean HS](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20HS.md)

## Aliases

### 澤
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 沢, "marsh, pool"; this is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 1-3-13; 16 strokes. It ranks 549 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 泽
Simplified Chinese form of 澤. SKIP 1-3-5 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 8 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
