## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*ŋoːʔ, \*ŋoːs): semantic [[Radical 009|人]] ("person") + phonetic [[禺]] (OC \*ŋo, \*ŋos) — a statue of a person, an idol; cognate with 遇 ("to meet, encounter"). Extended to "pair, even number" and "by chance, accidental."
