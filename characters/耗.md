## Notes
- 形声: radical [[Radical 127|耒]] ("a plow") + phonetic [[毛 (char)|毛]] (OC \*maːw; 耗 is reconstructed \*hmaːws in the same phonetic series). English Wiktionary says 耗 was originally written 秏. It glosses Chinese 耗 as "to reduce; to decrease; to cut; to minimise", "to weaken; to cripple", "to consume; to use up" (literary, or in compounds), "news (chiefly bad news)" and, regionally, "to delay; to procrastinate". It glosses Japanese 耗 as "decrease; decline; wear down".
