## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): thatched cottage; house. Original form of [[舎|舍]] (OC \*hljaːʔ, \*hljaːs, "house"). Later phonetically borrowed to write the first-person pronoun 余 (OC \*la, "I; me"); the "surplus, remainder" sense used in Dan'a'yo comes from a historically distinct character, 餘 (a 形声 compound), of which 余 is the modern simplification — the two senses ("I/me" and "surplus") merged onto one glyph only through this simplification, not through shared etymology.
