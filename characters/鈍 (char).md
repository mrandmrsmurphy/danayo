## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*duːns): semantic [[金 (char)|金]] ("metal") + phonetic [[屯 (char)|屯]] — "dull, blunt, not sharp," extended to "dull-witted, slow-witted." 鈍 stands alone as a word because it is a common, productive adjective across all daughters (dull, blunt, dull-witted — 鈍感, 遅鈍, 鈍器, 鈍痛) and a real predicative adjective in Mandarin (这刀很钝) and Cantonese (呢把刀好鈍), not a name-restricted character.
