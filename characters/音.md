>[!info] Semi-productive affix
> 音 is a **semi-productive** suffix for a "sound", naming phonetic classes and kinds of sound (有声音, 歯茎音, 内破音). The phonetic vocabulary is a technical set that needs authority control. It is not a [[派生辞]]. Dan'a'yo does not license new coinages with it without authority review, and every derivative has its own page (see Words below). Existing derivatives include [[介音]], [[塞音]], [[子音]], [[拼音]], [[振動音]], [[擦音]].
