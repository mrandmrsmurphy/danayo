## Aliases

### 猴
犭 + 侯, "monkey"; a near-synonym of 猿 kept as an alias on the same derived sound; it ranks higher in the Classical Chinese lists. SKIP 1-3-9; 12 strokes. It ranks 3334 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
