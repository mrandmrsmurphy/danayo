## Aliases

### 过
The simplified Chinese form of 過 (辶 + 寸), also an unorthodox Japanese variant; Wiktionary's 过 page redirects to 過. SKIP 3-3-3 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 6 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
