## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*kʰeŋ, \*kʰeŋs): semantic [[Radical 159|車]] ("carriage") + phonetic [[巠]] — originally "a kind of light carriage," preserved in the idiom 駕輕就熟 ("to drive a light carriage on a familiar route"); light, not heavy.
- [SKIP-1-7-5](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-7-5.md) ([Stroke 12](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2012.md))
- 475th most used character in Classical Chinese, the rank of its alias 輕 (軽 itself is not on the lists). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 溪|kʰ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 清開|iᴇŋ]] → [ㄎㄧㄫ](../syllables/ㄎㄧㄫ.md)
- [Grade 1](../lookup/Grade%201.md), [HSK Beginner](../lookup/HSK/HSK%20Beginner.md), [Jōyō - Kyōiku](../lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kyōiku.md), [Korean MS](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20MS.md)
- abbreviation for <ruby>[[軽素]]<rt>ㄎㄧㄫㄙㄛ</rt></ruby> "hydrogen" in periodic-table formulae — see [[Periodic Table]]

## Aliases

### 輕
The traditional form ("light; slight"); Wiktionary lists 輕, 軽 and 轻 together as one character, and 軽 is its Japanese shinjitai. It carries the classical rank of 475, which is the page's `mc_id`. SKIP 1-7-7; 14 strokes. It ranks 475 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 轻
The simplified Chinese form of 輕 (车 + 圣); Wiktionary lists 輕, 軽 and 轻 together as one character. SKIP 1-4-5 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 9 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
