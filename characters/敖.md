## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): originally a pictogram of a person wearing a headdress; the seal-script form later added [[Radical 066|攴]] ("to strike"). Primarily "to ramble, roam freely, play" (áo, matching the stored `mandarin`), and, under a distinct tonal reading (ào), "arrogant, haughty, conceited" (matching `korean_native: 거만할`, an alternative form of the later-derived [[傲]]). Also the source-form behind several derived characters — 廒 ("granary"), 螯 ("pincer/claw"), 遨 ("to roam") — none with an independent page in this vault, so not added as aliases (they represent distinct derived senses, not orthographic variants of the same word).
