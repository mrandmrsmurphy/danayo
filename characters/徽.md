## Notes
- 形声 (OC *hmɯl): semantic [[糸]] ("silk thread, cord") + phonetic [[微]] (OC *mɯl, abbreviated) — a cord or tie, extended to "insignia-cord, badge, emblem," and, separately, "fine, beautiful, excellent" (matching `korean_native: 아름다울` and the kun'yomi よい); also used for the strings and mother-of-pearl position-markers of the guqin, and as a short form for Anhui province (via Huizhou/徽州). A prior stray duplicate Notes bullet had this composition backwards (semantic/phonetic swapped) and mislabeled 微 as "cloth"; corrected per dual-source confirmation.
