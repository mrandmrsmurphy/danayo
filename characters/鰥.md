## Notes
- 形声 (OC *[k]ʷˁrə[n]): semantic [[Radical 195|魚]] ("fish") + phonetic 眔 (MC dop; the component has no page of its own) — "widower." The fish sense (a yellowcheek, also written 鱤) is obsolete; the widower sense is the living one, and Wiktionary relates the word to 寡 ("alone") plus a nominalizing *-n.
