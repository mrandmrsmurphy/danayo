## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*kraːms): semantic [[Radical 167|金]] ("metal," the bronze of the ancient mirror) + phonetic [[監]] (OC \*kraːm) — the large bronze mirror of antiquity, extended to "to examine clearly, to appraise." 鑒 is the traditional form (with its own separate Classical Chinese corpus entry) and 鉴 the standard simplified form; 𰾫 is a further attested rare variant, with no vault page of its own.
