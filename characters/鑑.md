## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*kraːms): semantic [[Radical 167|金]] ("metal," the bronze of the ancient mirror) + phonetic [[監]] (OC \*kraːm) — the large bronze mirror of antiquity, extended to "to examine clearly, to appraise." 鑒 is the traditional form (with its own separate Classical Chinese corpus entry) and 鉴 the standard simplified form; 𰾫 is a further attested rare variant, with no vault page of its own.
- [SKIP-1-8-15](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-8-15.md) ([Stroke 23](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2023.md))
- 2180th most used character in Classical Chinese, the rank of its alias 鑒 (鑑 itself ranks 3781). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 見|k]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 銜|ɣam]] → [ㄍㄚㄇ](../syllables/ㄍㄚㄇ.md)
- [Grade 5](../lookup/Grade%205.md), [Old HSK 4](../lookup/HSK/Old%20HSK%204.md), [Jōyō - Kōtō](../lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kōtō.md), [Korean HS](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20HS.md)

## Aliases

### 鑒
Variant form of 鑑, "mirror; to inspect"; the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 2-15-8; 22 strokes. It ranks 2180 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 鉴
Simplified Chinese form of 鑑. SKIP 2-5-8 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 13 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
