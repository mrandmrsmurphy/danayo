## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*naːl, per Zhengzhang): semantic [[Radical 009|人]] ("person") + phonetic [[難]] (OC \*n̥ʰaːn, \*naːns) — a masked ritual procession to drive out plague demons and evil spirits, still performed today as 儺戲/儺舞 (Nuo opera/dance) in parts of southern China. Genuinely attested since antiquity — 論語 10.14 records "鄉人儺，朝服而立於阼階" ("when the villagers performed the Nuo exorcism, [Confucius] stood on the eastern steps in his court robes"). A striking case of phonetic-series divergence from its own phonetic parent: 難's Middle Chinese final is 寒 (nasal-coda -ɑn), but 儺's own attested reading drops the coda entirely, landing in the open 歌 rhyme (-ɑ) instead — the same kind of scatter already seen elsewhere in this vault (e.g. 反 vs 板/版).
