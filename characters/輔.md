## Notes
- 形聲 (OC \*baʔ): semantic [[Radical 159|車]] ("cart, chariot") + phonetic [[甫]] (OC \*paʔ) — the wooden reinforcement bars on a cart, extended to "to assist, to support" (a ruler or superior). Simplified form [[辅]] is a genuine variant. Don't make the Japanese mistake of confusing this with 補!
