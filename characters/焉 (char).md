## Notes
- 象形: originally a pictogram of a yellow bird found around the Yangtze and Huai rivers (obsolete sense); the graph was borrowed 假借 for its sound to write a family of grammatical particles — a locative/demonstrative ("in it, here"), an interrogative ("how, why, where"), and, in the sense used here, "thereupon, therefore." 焉 also serves as the phonetic component in two characters pageless in this vault: 鄢 (a place name in Henan) and 嫣 ("beautiful"), neither a variant of 焉 itself.
