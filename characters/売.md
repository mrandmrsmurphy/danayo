## Aliases

### 賣
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 売, 出 over 買; Wiktionary says 売 is simplified from it, and 卖 is its Chinese simplified form. SKIP 2-3-12; 15 strokes. Radical 154 貝. This form is the one the Classical Chinese lists rank, so the `mc_id` 1477 is 賣's rank.

### 卖
Simplified Chinese form of 賣 (十 over 买) (Wiktionary). SKIP 2-2-6 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 8 strokes. Radical 024 十. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
