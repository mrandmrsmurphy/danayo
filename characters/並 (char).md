## Notes
- 会意 (OC \*beːŋʔ): from clerical-script 竝 (从二立, "two 立/standing-person," no vault page), depicting two people standing side by side — "together, side by side, and, simultaneously." 并/幷 are genuine graphemic variants (并 tracked separately in the corpus at rank 652, reflecting real independent usage); 併/倂 (人 semantic + 并 phonetic, own corpus rank 3417, no vault page) are a distinct, narrower-sense derived character meaning "to combine, merge, annex" and were formerly wrongly listed here as aliases.
- [SKIP-2-3-5](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-2/SKIP-2-3-5.md) ([Stroke 08](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2008.md))
- 610th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 並|b]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 青開|eŋ]] → [ㄅㄝㄫ](../syllables/ㄅㄝㄫ.md)
- [Grade 4](../lookup/Grade%204.md), [Old HSK 2](../lookup/HSK/Old%20HSK%202.md), [Jōyō - Kōtō](../lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kōtō.md), [Korean HS](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20HS.md)

## Aliases

### 并
Simplified Chinese form of 並 and a character of its own (Bing Province, an old name of Taiyuan); rank 652. SKIP 2-3-3; 6 strokes. Radical 051 干. It ranks 652 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.

### 竝
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 並 (Wiktionary: the Japanese kyūjitai spelling; Korean hanja 병, "side by side"). SKIP 1-5-5; 10 strokes. Radical 117 立. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 幷
A variant form of 並 and 并 (Unihan: semantic variant); Wiktionary gives no definition. SKIP 2-3-5 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 8 strokes. Radical 051 干. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
