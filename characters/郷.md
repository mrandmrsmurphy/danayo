## Notes
- 会意 of 𠨍 ("two people facing each other," no character page in this vault) + [[皀]] ("a round-mouthed food vessel") — originally depicting a ritual feasting scene, the ancestor of [[饗]] ("to feast"); phonetically borrowed or semantically shifted to "village, hometown, community."
- [SKIP-1-3-8](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-8.md) ([Stroke 11](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2011.md))

## Aliases

### 鄉
Traditional form of 郷, "township, countryside"; this is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 1-3-10 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 13 strokes. It ranks 449 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 鄕
A variant of 鄉, "village, hometown". SKIP 1-3-10 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 13 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 乡
Simplified Chinese form of 鄉. SKIP 2-2-1 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 3 strokes. Radical 052 幺. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
