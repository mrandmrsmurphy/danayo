## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*rnaːŋʔ): semantic [[木]] ("tree") + phonetic [[寧]] (OC \*neːŋ) — a modern coinage for the "lemon tree" sense, near-sole modern use as the bound first half of [[words/檸檬|檸檬]] "lemon."
- [SKIP-1-4-14](Lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-4-14.md) ([Stroke 18](Lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2018.md))
- Confirmed absent from the Classical Chinese corpus (`mc_id: 0`, checked against all four `Lookup/CC/CC *.md` mirror files — only the phonetic component [[寧]] itself appears, at rank 749 on `CC 0000.md`). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 娘|ɳ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 庚二開|ɣæŋ]] → [ㄌㄝ](syllables/ㄌㄝ.md)
- Reading overridden (2026-07-12) from the MC-derived ㄋ⼶ㄫ to **ㄌㄝ**, alongside 檬's ㄇㄛㄫ→ㄇㄛㄋ, so that [[words/檸檬|檸檬]] spells "lemon" directly: `mc_id: 0` confirms this pair is unattested in Classical Chinese, so the MC initial/final are borrowed-by-analogy, not real inheritance, and the daughter languages show no shared inherited reading either — Japanese レモン and Korean 레몬 both borrow the Western word directly, and Vietnamese uses the native word chanh.
