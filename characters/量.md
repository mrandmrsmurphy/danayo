## Notes
- 会意 (disputed etymology): commonly analyzed as combining 日/田 ("sun"/"field") with 東 ("bag," not "east" here) — "to measure something under the sun" (Yu Xingwu). Other theories: original form of [[糧 (char)|糧]] ("grain," Qiu Xigui — matching this vault's own Derived Character); original form of 亮 ("bright," Guo Moruo); or 形声 with phonetic 曏 + semantic 重 (Shuowen).
