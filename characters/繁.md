## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*baːl, \*baːn, \*ban): semantic [[糸]] ("silk," relating to numerous silk threads/hair ornaments) + phonetic [[敏 (char)|敏]] (OC \*mrɯʔ, \*mrɯŋʔ) — "numerous, lush, complicated." Formerly wrongly documented here with the semantic/phonetic roles and glosses swapped (敏 mislabeled "silk," 糸 given 敏's own OC readings) — corrected.
