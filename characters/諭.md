## Notes
- 形聲: semantic [[Radical 149|言]] ("speech") + phonetic [[兪 (char)|兪]] — to instruct, to issue a decree (from a superior to a subordinate); to explain, to make understood.
- [SKIP-1-7-9](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-7-9.md) ([Stroke 16](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2016.md))
- Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 以|j]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 虞|ɨo]] → [⼜ㄇ](syllables/⼜ㄇ.md). By vault policy (documented on [[兪]]), 兪's large phonetic family — including this character — is deliberately given Dan'a'yo readings ending in -m rather than strict Middle Chinese derivation, to reduce homophony; `羅馬字: 'yum`/`諺文: 윰` reflect this by-fiat departure, not an error.
