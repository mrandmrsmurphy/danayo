## Notes
- 形声: phonetic [[用]] — "path (screened by walls on both sides)," extended to a place name (the Yong River / ancient Ningbo). Etymological sources genuinely disagree on the deeper origin: zh.Wiktionary treats 甬 as 形声 using 用 as phonetic (matching this page's stored classification); en.Wiktionary instead claims 甬 is itself 象形, a pictograph of a bronze bell whose lower part later evolved into 用; ja.Wiktionary offers a third account, a purely decorative graphic variant of 用. Kept the existing `用`-phonetic classification, since only one source (en.Wiktionary) attests the pictograph theory and it isn't independently corroborated.
