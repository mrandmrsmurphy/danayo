## Notes
- 會意 (per Shuowen, 从水从步): [[Radical 085|水]] ("water") + 步 ("to step, walk," no vault page) — "to wade across water on foot," extended to "to be involved with, to interfere." 涉 is the standard form (渉 a Japanese shinjitai variant).
