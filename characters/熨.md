## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 086|火]] ("fire") + phonetic [[尉 (char)|尉]] — to iron, press with heat; a flatiron. There seem to have been a variety of ancient pronunciations all around iui/iut/iun. Two Korean readings, split by sense (per user, 2026-07-19): 울 (ul) "to iron" — the reading used in the compound [[熨斗]] (울두) — and 위 (wi) "to apply heat/press medically" (찜질하다, matching `korean_native`); the `korean` field stores the medical reading, with the ironing sense living in the compound.
