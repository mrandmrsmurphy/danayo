## Notes
- 象形 (OC \*ʔraːɡs): an overhead view of a construction, likely an 亞-shaped tomb; the senses "second, inferior, next to" were perhaps borrowed phonetically (cognate with Tibetan རག rag, "dependent, subservient"). 亞/亚 are true historical/simplified variants of the same character; 堊 ("chalk," 亞 phonetic + 土 semantic) is a distinct character that 亜 serves as a Japanese daiyōji stand-in for specifically in the "chalk" sense (see [[白亜]]), not a general variant for the "second/Asia" senses.
- [SKIP-4-7-1](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-4/SKIP-4-7-1.md) ([Stroke 07](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2007.md))
- 1762nd most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 影|ʔ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 麻二開|ɣa]] → [ㄚ](syllables/ㄚ.md)
