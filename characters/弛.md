## Notes
- 形声 (OC *l̥ajʔ): semantic [[Radical 057|弓]] ("bow") + phonetic [[也]] (OC *laːlʔ) — Shuowen: "弓解也" ("a bow unstrung"); to loosen or slacken a bowstring, extended to "become lax, negligent," and "to abandon, neglect." A cross-page bug was found and fixed: `characters/施.md` had wrongly listed 弛 as its own alias, and both `Lookup/Japanese/Jinmeiyō.md` (item 446) and `Lookup/Korean/Korean Name ㅇ.md` had a stray link pointing the glyph "弛" at 施's page instead of this one — 弛 and 施 are historically related (sharing the phonetic 也) but are fully distinct characters in this vault, each with its own independent frontmatter and use; both cross-references corrected to point here.
