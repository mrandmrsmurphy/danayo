## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*nja, \*njas): semantic [[口 (char)|口]] ("mouth") + phonetic [[Radical 038|女]] (OC \*naʔ, \*nas, "woman"). The popular "woman who obeys orders" story is folk etymology, not the scholarly analysis — 女 contributes sound here, not the "woman" meaning; the "like, as, as if, equal to" sense ties to Sino-Tibetan cognates expressing correspondence/likeness (cf. Mizo *na nâ nâ*, "it being so; since") rather than deriving compositionally from the components.
- [SKIP-1-3-3](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-3.md) ([Stroke 06](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2006.md))
