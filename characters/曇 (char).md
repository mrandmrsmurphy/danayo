## Notes
- 会意: [[日]] ("sun") + [[雲]] ("cloud") — "sun behind clouds," hence "overcast, cloudy." Formerly wrongly stored as 形声 with phonetic 旦 — 曇 has no 旦 component at all (旦 is 日+一, an unrelated character); the real analysis is a straightforward ideogrammic compound.
