- 形声 (MC ɡʷaːŋʔ): semantic [[日]] ("sun") + phonetic [[光]] ("light") — over-bright, dazzling light limiting vision; under a distinct tonal reading (huàng), "to sway, shake, move back and forth" (晃動). Genuine variant: 晄.
