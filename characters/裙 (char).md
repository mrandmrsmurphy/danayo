## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 145|衣]] ("clothing", written 衤) + phonetic [[君 (char)|君]] (OC \*klun; 裙 is reconstructed \*ɡlun, in the same phonetic series as 群 and 郡). English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 裙 as "skirt; apron; dress; petticoat", Japanese くん as "skirt hem" and a hip-wrapped pleated under-skirt worn as clerical attire, and Vietnamese quần as "pants".
- [SKIP-1-5-7](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-5-7.md) ([Stroke 12](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2012.md))
- Not present in the Classical Chinese usage ranking. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 群|g]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 文|ɨun]] → [ㄍ⼜ㄫ](../syllables/ㄍ⼜ㄫ.md), the otherwise unused syllable *gyung*; the regular outcome ㄍㄨㄋ already holds 君, 軍, 群, 郡 and 饉.
