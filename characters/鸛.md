## Notes
- 形声 (OC *koːns): originally written as 雚 alone (the phonetic component, no character page of its own in this vault — registered as this page's own alias, see [[feedback_alias_parent_form]]) + semantic [[Radical 196|鳥]] ("bird") added later, once 雚 had drifted into use as a bare phonetic loan elsewhere (as in [[勧]], [[権]], [[歓]], [[観]], [[灌]]) and needed a classifier to keep the "stork" sense distinct. The Japanese extended shinjitai form (a further simplification of 鸛) isn't yet encodable in Unicode; its IDS is ⿰𮥶鳥.
