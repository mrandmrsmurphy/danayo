## Notes
- 形声 (OC *koːns): originally written as 雚 alone (the phonetic component, now this page's alias) + semantic [[Radical 196|鳥]] ("bird") added later, once 雚 had drifted into use as a bare phonetic loan elsewhere (as in [[勧]], [[権]], [[歓]], [[観]], [[灌]]) and needed a classifier to keep the "stork" sense distinct. The Japanese extended shinjitai form (a further simplification of 鸛) isn't yet encodable in Unicode; its IDS is ⿰𮥶鳥.
- [SKIP-1-11-18](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-11-18.md) ([Stroke 29](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2029.md))
- 3200th most used character in Classical Chinese, the rank of its alias 雚 (鸛 itself is not on the lists). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 見|k]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 桓|uɑn]] → [ㄍ⺢ㄋ](../syllables/ㄍ⺢ㄋ.md)

## Aliases

### 雚
"Stork, heron" (archaic); the phonetic of 鸛 and kept as an alias of 鸛. SKIP 2-10-8; 18 strokes. Radical 172 隹. It ranks 3200 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 鹳
Simplified Chinese form of 鸛. SKIP 1-17-5 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 22 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
