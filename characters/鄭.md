## Aliases

### 郑
Simplified Chinese form of 鄭. SKIP 1-6-3 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 9 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 奠
酋 over 大, "to make offerings to the dead; to establish"; the phonetic of 鄭 and a separate word, kept as an alias of 鄭 on the same derived sound. SKIP 2-2-10; 12 strokes. Radical 037 大. It ranks 1317 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.
