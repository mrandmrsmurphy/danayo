## Notes
- [List of 会意](../lookup/List%20of%20会意.md) (OC \*zuɡs): originally 𫢁, combining 享 ("temple") + [[京]] ("tower") — "to sacrifice at a high place." During the Warring States period, 享 was omitted and [[尤]] ("especially, even more") added — "high, to go to a higher place"; extended to "to approach, reach; to undertake; to succeed."
- [SKIP-1-8-4](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-8-4.md) ([Stroke 12](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2012.md))
- 552nd most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 從|d͡z]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 尤|ɨu]] → [ㄑ⺢ㄧ](../syllables/ㄑ⺢ㄧ.md)
- [Grade 2](../lookup/Grade%202.md), [HSK Beginner](../lookup/HSK/HSK%20Beginner.md), [Jōyō - Kyōiku](../lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kyōiku.md), [Korean MS](../lookup/Korean/Korean%20MS.md)
- 就 also serves as a borrowed character (代用字) for 鷲 ("eagle"), hence 鷲鳥 → [[就鳥]] and 禿鷲 → [[禿就]] below; it keeps this character's own single sound ㄑ⺢ㄧ there.

## Aliases

### 鷲
A separate character (就 + 鳥, "eagle; vulture"); Wiktionary's 鷲 page lists 就 as a variant form, which is why this vault borrows 就 for "eagle" in 就鳥, 禿就 and 海就 (a 代用字, as the page's own note records). SKIP 2-12-11; 23 strokes. Radical 196 鳥. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 鹫
The simplified Chinese form of 鷲 (就 + 鸟); not linked to 就 on Wiktionary. SKIP 2-12-5 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 17 strokes. Radical 196 鳥. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
