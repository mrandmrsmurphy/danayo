## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*sʰiːl): semantic [[Radical 015|冫]] ("ice") + phonetic [[妻]] (OC \*sʰiːl, \*sʰiːls) — "cold, bleak," extended to "miserable, desolate." 淒/悽 are graphemic variants (each tracked separately in the corpus, ranks 3876/3291) — the vault's own graphemic form 凄 itself has no separate corpus entry.
- [SKIP-1-2-8](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-2-8.md) ([Stroke 10](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2010.md))
- Not present in the Classical Chinese usage ranking under this exact graphemic form. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 清|t͡sʰ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 齊開|ei]] → [ㄑㄝㄧ](syllables/ㄑㄝㄧ.md)
- [Grade 4](lookup/Grade%204.md), [Old HSK 4](lookup/HSK/Old%20HSK%204.md), [Jōyō - Kōtō](lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kōtō.md), [Korean Name ㅊ](lookup/Korean/Korean%20Name%20ㅊ.md) — 悽 was dropped from the Korean high school hanja list in 2000, demoting it (and 凄) to name-only status.
