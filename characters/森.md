## Notes
- 会意: Triplication of [[Radical 075|木]] ("tree"), to suggest a large number (compare 三, "three") of trees such as one would find in a forest — contrast [[林 (char)|林]] (OC *ɡ·rɯm), which uses only two 木 for a smaller "grove, woods." Dropped from the Korean high-school hanmun list in 2000.
