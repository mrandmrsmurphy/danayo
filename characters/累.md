## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*rul, \*ruls): semantic [[糸]] ("fine silk") + phonetic [[雷 (char)|畾]] (OC \*ruːl) — originally "rope; to bind"; the "tired after work" sense is a folk etymology of the modern simplified shape (畾→田).

## Aliases

### 纍
糸 + 畾, "to link or bind together"; a variant of 累 and kept as an alias. SKIP 2-5-16; 21 strokes. It ranks 2569 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.

### 騾
馬 + 累, "mule"; built on 累 as phonetic and kept as an alias. SKIP 1-10-11; 21 strokes. Radical 187 馬. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
