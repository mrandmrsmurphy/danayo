## Notes
- 會意 of [[人]] ("person") and [[各]] ("to go against, to oppose") — Shuowen: "从人从各，各者，相違也" ("from 人 and 各; 各 means to be at odds with one another"), a person wronged or at odds with another; the oracle/bronze form combined 夊 ("foot, to trample") and 人 ("person") before 口 was added in the Spring and Autumn period, yielding the modern 人+各 analysis. Extended from "calamity, misfortune" to "fault, blame, guilt."
