## Notes
- 形声 (OC *bij, Baxter-Sagart): 說文解字 gives the original sense as "human navel" (人之臍), analyzed as semantic 囟 ("fontanelle," by analogy to the navel's opening — the modern glyph's top component, conventionally written as 田 through later graphic evolution, is not literally "field" here) + phonetic [[比]]; the now-primary sense "to help, assist" (and a secondary "to connect, adjoin") is a later extension.
