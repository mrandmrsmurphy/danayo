## Notes
- 会意: [[Radical 075|木]] ("tree, wood") + surrounding flame/spark marks (reduced to [[Radical 042|小]] in the modern form, the character's own indexing radical) — the earliest oracle-bone forms show a tree or bundle of wood with dots representing flames around it, depicting a bonfire built for sacrifice. Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary).
