## Aliases

### 圖
The traditional form; 図 is the Japanese shinjitai and 图 the simplified-Chinese form ([Wiktionary: 圖](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E5%9C%96), [Wiktionary: 図](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E5%9B%B3)). 圖 has OC \*daː. SKIP 3-3-11; 14 strokes. It ranks 763 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 图
The simplified-Chinese form of 圖, listed beside 図 on Wiktionary ([Wiktionary: 图](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E5%9B%BE)). SKIP 3-3-5 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 8 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
