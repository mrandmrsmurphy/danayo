## Notes
- 形声 (OC *quds, *qud): semantic [[Radical 140|艸]] ("grass") + phonetic [[尉 (char)|尉]] (OC *quds, *qud) — originally a fragrant herb (mugwort), then "lush, luxuriant" of vegetation. Dan'a'yo takes the 紆物切 reading (MC ʔɨut) that the character has in surnames and place names such as 蔚山 and Korean 울, the same final as its phonetic 尉 in that reading, rather than the 於胃切 literary reading wèi (MC ʔjwɨj), which is the one in 蔚藍 "azure". By decision its Dan'a'yo sound is set equal to its phonetic component's reading, so 蔚 is a homophone of 尉 (both MC ʔɨut, ㄨㄊ), unlike the sibling 熨 and 欝, which keep ⼜ㄊ.
- Homophones (注音 ㄨㄊ): [[尉 (char)|尉]] "officer" (see also the word [[尉]]).
