## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*ruːls, \*ruds): semantic [[Radical 094|犬]] ("dog") + phonetic 頪 (OC \*roːds, no vault page) — from Proto-Sino-Tibetan \*rus ("bone"); extended to "kind, category, class."
- [SKIP-1-9-9](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-9-9.md) ([Stroke 18](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2018.md))
- 584th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 來|l]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 脂A合|iuɪ]] → [ㄌㄨㄧ](syllables/ㄌㄨㄧ.md)
- [Grade 2](lookup/Grade%202.md), [Old HSK 2](lookup/HSK/Old%20HSK%202.md), [Jōyō - Kyōiku](lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kyōiku.md), [Korean HS](lookup/Korean/Korean%20HS.md)
- It is important to note that while this character cannot appear alone, it is the only member of the correlative chart (see [[AIOS/grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md]]) to do so, and its uses there seem like it ought to.
