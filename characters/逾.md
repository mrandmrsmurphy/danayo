## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*lo): semantic 辵 ("movement") + phonetic [[兪 (char)|兪]] — "to go over, exceed, transcend." Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary); zh.Wiktionary explicitly confirms 踰 as a genuine 異體 (variant) of this character, but explicitly denies the same for 窬 (a separate, merely cognate character sharing the same OC root), which was previously miscited here as an alias and has been removed.
