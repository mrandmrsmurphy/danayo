## Aliases

### 踰
足 + 俞, "to pass over, to exceed"; a variant of 逾 with the same meaning. SKIP 1-7-9; 16 strokes. Radical 157 足. It ranks 1322 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
