## Notes
- 形声 (OC *kʰmɯː): semantic [[Radical 061|心]] ("heart, mind") + phonetic [[灰]] (OC *hmɯː) — vast, expansive, broad (恢弘/恢宏), extended in modern usage to "to restore, recover" (恢復/恢复); also attested in 天網恢恢, "the net of heaven is vast (and misses nothing)." Genuine variants: 㷇, 烣.
