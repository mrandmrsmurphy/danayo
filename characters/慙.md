## Aliases

### 慚
Variant form of 慙, "ashamed"; this is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 1-3-11; 14 strokes. It ranks 1726 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 惭
Simplified Chinese form of 慚. SKIP 1-3-8 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 11 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
