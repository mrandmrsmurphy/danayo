## Notes
- 会意 (per Shuowen) of 仌 ("two pieces of flesh," no character page of its own in this vault) + [[Radical 072|日]] ("sun") — dried meat in the sun, the original form of 腊; the "former times" sense is an extension. **Corrected here**: the field's classification (`會意`) is right, but the old bullet named the wrong component (𡿧, "flood," which no source supports) — Wiktionary confirms the real Shuowen analysis uses 仌, not 𡿧. (A separate, more modern account treats 昔 as 形声 with phonetic 龷 + semantic 日; kept the vault's existing 會意 classification per Shuowen rather than switching traditions.)
