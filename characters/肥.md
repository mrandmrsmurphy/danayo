## Notes
- Traditionally analyzed as 会意 of [[Radical 130|月]] (肉, "meat/flesh") + [[巴 (char)|巴]], though Ji Xusheng (2004) argues it's 形声 with phonetic [[妃]] (OC \*bɯl), abbreviated to 巴 in the modern glyph.
- [SKIP-1-4-4](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-4-4.md) ([Stroke 8](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2008.md))
