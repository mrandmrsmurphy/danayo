## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*kraːs): semantic [[Radical 009|人]] ("person") + phonetic [[賈]] (OC \*kraːʔ, \*kraːs, "merchant; to trade") — traditional 價 retains 賈 in full; the modern Japanese shinjitai 価 abbreviates it by stripping out 貝 ("shell, money"), leaving a bare graphic residue (⿰亻覀) with no independent phonetic identity of its own — unlike 鉄's shinjitai substitution 失 (a genuine, separately-attested character), this residue isn't even a standalone character in its own right, so `graphemic_classification` is correctly kept at 賈 rather than reassigned to a non-existent component.
