## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*naŋ): semantic [[Radical 038|女]] ("woman") + phonetic [[良]] (OC \*raŋ) — unattested before the Tang dynasty, possibly a fusion of 女郎 ("lady"); "young woman, girl," also "mother" in some compounds.
- [SKIP-1-3-7](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-7.md) ([Stroke 10](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2010.md))
- 5193rd most used character in Classical Chinese (beyond the top ~4000 locally mirrored, trusted long-tail rank). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 娘|ɳ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 陽開|ɨɐŋ]] → [ㄋㄚㄫ](syllables/ㄋㄚㄫ.md)
- [Grade 5](lookup/Grade%205.md), [Old HSK 3](lookup/HSK/Old%20HSK%203.md), [Jōyō - Kōtō](lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kōtō.md), [Korean HS](lookup/Korean/Korean%20HS.md) — similar in sense to [[処女]] (virgin), but not identical.
