## Aliases

### 據
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 拠, written 手 + 豦; the shinjitai 拠 writes the phonetic part as 処. SKIP 1-3-13; 16 strokes. It ranks 975 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 豦
虍 over 豕 ("tiger above boar"), "beasts fighting; a large boar", obsolete on its own; Wiktionary suggests it may be the original form of 劇. It is the phonetic of 拠 (and of 遽 and 劇). It has the same derived Dan'a'yo sound as 拠, ㄍ⼄ (MC `ɡ` + `ɨʌ`), so it is an alias of 拠, which is the hub for the characters built on it (see Derived Characters). SKIP 3-4-9; 13 strokes. Radical 152 豕. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 据
Simplified Chinese form of 據 (手 + 居); it is also a separate Japanese kanji meaning "to set, to place", filed here as the simplified form. SKIP 1-3-8; 11 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
