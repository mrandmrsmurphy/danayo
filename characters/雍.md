## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 172|隹]] ("short-tailed bird") + phonetic *邕* (OC *qoŋ) — a variant form of *雝*, whose phonetic component 邕 corrupted into a 玄-like shape at some point since their forms looked similar in pre-Qin script (resolving a prior editor's mistaken "Components: 亠, 乡, 隹" note — those aren't independent constituents, they're the corrupted remnant of 邕). Harmony, peace; also 雍州 (one of the ancient Nine Provinces), a surname, and an alternative form of 饔 ("cooked food"), 壅/擁 ("to obstruct/hold"), and 廱 ("harmonious"). The stray prior note "pronunciation changed in accordance with the Korean surname" could not be verified against any genuine syllable-placement-override evidence — 옹 is a perfectly regular Sino-Korean reading for this character, and the claim is dropped as unresolved/unsupported rather than carried forward.
