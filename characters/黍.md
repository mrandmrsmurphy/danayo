## Notes
- 象形 ([List of 象形](Lookup/List%20of%20象形.md)): a cereal 禾 with water 水 or kernels under it. The water may symbolize the wine made from millet or the fact that millet is sticky and water-resistant; otherwise, given that millet is a drought-resistant plant that can grow on dry ground, the "water" component remains unexplained (see [[黎]], where this radical appears as a contracted form). Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary). `stand_in: 名専字` — no word anywhere cites 黍 in its own `characters:` field.
