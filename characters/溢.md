## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 085|水]] ("water") + phonetic [[益]] — "to overflow, to brim over." 益 began as water overflowing a vessel and later came to mean "to increase, benefit"; this character is that older meaning with the water radical added back.
- Homophones (注音 ㄧㄊ): [一 (char)](一%20(char).md), [逸 (char)](逸%20(char).md) and [壱 (char)](壱%20(char).md); see [ㄧㄊ](../syllables/ㄧㄊ.md).
- [SKIP-1-3-10](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-10.md) ([Stroke 13](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2013.md))
- 1421st most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 以|j]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 質三|iɪt]] → [ㄧㄊ](../syllables/ㄧㄊ.md)
