## Notes
- 形声 (per Shuowen, 从衣龖省): semantic [[Radical 145|衣]] ("robe," originally "a left-lapped padded robe with overlapping flaps") + phonetic 龖 (abbreviated, an archaic doubled-dragon component, no vault page) — extended from "overlapping layers" to "to attack by surprise, to ambush." 袭 is the standard simplified form.
