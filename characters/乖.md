## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): per Xu Shen's Shuowen, two combined graphic elements (𦫳 + 𠔁). Classically means "perverse, contrary; to violate, to rebel; to diverge" — the reading still preserved in Korean (`어그러질`, "to go awry") and Japanese (そむく/もとる, "to defy"). Modern colloquial Mandarin flipped the sense to its opposite, "well-behaved, obedient, docile" (of children/pets), which is the sense this vault's Dan'a'yo vocabulary actually uses (see [[乖巧]]).
