## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*pɯɡ): semantic [[虫]] ("insect") + phonetic [[畐]] (OC \*pʰrɯɡ, \*bɯɡ) — "bat." An extinct pictogram (象形) of a bat occurs in the oracle bone and bronze scripts; it is thought to write this word but is not ancestral to 蝠. Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary). `#cranberry`: 蝠 carries the "bat" sense in its own right (matching [[蝙]]'s own independent sense), and so does the compound [[蝙蝠]] — genuine transitivity (A=B=AB).
