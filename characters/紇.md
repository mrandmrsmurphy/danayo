## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*ɡɯːd/\*ɡuːd): semantic [[糸]] ("silk") + phonetic [[乞 (char)|乞]] — "inferior silk; tassel, fringe"; today used almost exclusively as the second syllable of 回紇 (Uyghur people). Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary); `纥` (simplified form) is the sole genuine alias (no independent vault page). **Misfiled-reading bug found and fixed**: `げち` (GECHI) was stored in `japanese_native` as if a kun'yomi, but en.Wiktionary lists it as one of four genuine *on'yomi* (こつ/ごち/けつ/げち), with no kun'yomi attested anywhere; moved to `japanese` and `japanese_native` corrected to `ø`.
