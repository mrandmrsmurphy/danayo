## Notes
- 指事: originally an arrow 矢 with a distinguishing mark (now 日); the arrowhead corrupted into 宀 in the modern form. "To revere, to respect" (classical); also the third of the twelve earthly branches, associated with the tiger — possibly an arbitrary pairing, as with 辰 and the dragon.
