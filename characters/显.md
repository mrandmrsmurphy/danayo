## Notes
- 會意 (⿱日业): the PRC-simplified form of [[顕|顯]] ("evident, clear, to appear/manifest" — this page's own alias, whose true independent vault treatment lives at [[顕]], the parallel Japanese shinjitai form). All three sources (en.Wiktionary, zh.Wiktionary, ja.Wiktionary) confirm this identity and its 9-stroke composition; en.Wiktionary additionally attests a secondary sense as a variant of 㬎 (this page's own other alias), "motes visible in a sunbeam." Dual/triple-source confirmed.
