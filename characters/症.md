## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*tjɯŋs): semantic [[Radical 104|疒]] ("disease") + phonetic [[正]] (OC \*tjeŋ) — "disease condition, symptom." A highly productive medical suffix forming condition/syndrome names (炎症 "inflammation," 癌症 "cancer," 自閉症 "autism"). 癥 is a distinct but related traditional-register form. Confirmed absent from the Classical Chinese corpus ranking (neither 症 nor 癥 appears in `lookup/CC/CC 0000.md` through `CC 3000.md`); `mc_id` corrected from a fabricated, out-of-range value (`6300`, far beyond the corpus's actual ~4000-entry ceiling) to `0`.
