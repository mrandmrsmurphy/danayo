## Notes
- 会意 of [[Radical 072|日]] ("sun") + [[進]] ("advance") — the sun advancing, hence "to rise" (English Wiktionary marks this sense obsolete in standard Chinese). The character is now known only as the first character of 暹羅, the name of Siam, from the kingdom of Ayutthaya; English Wiktionary traces 暹 to Thai สยาม, ultimately from Sanskrit श्याम (śyāma, "dark, swarthy"). It is a 名専字: the character appears only in names, 暹羅 (Siam) and 暹京 (Bangkok).
