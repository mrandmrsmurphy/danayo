## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*ɡ·roːlʔ): semantic [[衣]] ("clothing") + phonetic [[果]] (OC \*kloːlʔ) — originally "lacking clothes, naked." Mandarin has the common compound 赤裸 ("stark naked"), but this is not international, so 裸 stands alone as its own stand-in.
