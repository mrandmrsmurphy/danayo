## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): a plate placed vertically on its side — the original form of [[盤 (char)|盤]] ("plate; basin," OC \*baːn). Explicitly unrelated to 几 (no separate vault page) despite the visual resemblance that placed it under that radical for Kangxi indexing, and distinguished from 同 by its asymmetric right stroke. The "ordinary, common, all" sense is not a semantic extension of the plate image — per Schuessler (2007) it is likely Sino-Tibetan in origin, cognate with Mizo *pum* ("whole; all") and Burmese ဘုံ (bhum, "common, public, communal").
