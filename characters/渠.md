## Notes
- 形声 (OC *kʷaʔ): semantic [[水 (char)|水]] ("water") + abbreviated phonetic [[榘]] — a man-made water channel/canal/ditch (as in the historic 靈渠 Lingqu Canal); dual-source confirmed to also carry a distinct, well-documented classical/dialectal usage as a third-person pronoun "he/him/it" (attested from the 3rd–4th centuries CE in texts like the *Records of the Three Kingdoms* and Zhu Xi's *Reflections on Reading*, surviving today in several central/southern Chinese dialects), reflected in `japanese_native`'s なんぞ/なに/いずくんぞ ("what," from its use in classical rhetorical questions) and かれ ("he"). A zh.Wiktionary-only "large" sense lacks en.Wiktionary corroboration and is left undocumented. Genuine dual-source script-simplification variant: [[洰]] (no independent page); the further dialectal-pronoun alternatives 佢/𠍲 (zh.Wiktionary-only, not orthographic variants of 渠 itself) and 伊 (has its own extensive independent page/use in this vault) were excluded per the alias-is-parent-form convention.
