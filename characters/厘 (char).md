## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*rɯ): semantic [[Radical 027|厂]] + phonetic [[里 (char)|里]] (OC \*rɯʔ) — 厘 is a simplified variant of [[釐]] (whose fuller traditional etymology instead analyzes it as semantic 里 + phonetic 𠩺, an obscure component with no page in this vault; both accounts converge on the same reading) — originally a small unit of weight/length, extended to the modern metric prefix "centi-" and to small monetary/interest units.
