## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*kuːb/\*ɡuːb): semantic [[虫]] ("insect") + phonetic [合 (char)](characters/合%20(char).md) — "clam" (bivalve mollusk), cognate with 合 ("to be together"). Dual-source confirmed (en.Wiktionary and zh.Wiktionary). This is the *gé* "clam" reading specifically; the character also has a distinct *há* reading (only in 蛤蟆 "frog/toad" and internet slang), not the sense used in this vault.
