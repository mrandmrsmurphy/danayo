## Notes
- 形声 (OC *qrɯmʔ, *qrɯms): semantic [[Radical 076|欠]] ("yawn") + phonetic 酓 (OC *qʰɯːm) — originally a 会意 of a person bending over a bottle of alcohol to drink; the modern left half is 食 ("eat"), swapped in for 酓 in the clerical script.
