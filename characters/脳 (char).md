## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*nuːʔ): semantic [[Radical 130|月]] (肉, "flesh") + phonetic 𡿺 (a fusion of 巛 "hair" + 囟 "head; fontanel," no character page of its own in this vault). Originally written 匘, a 会意 of 匕 ("spoon/ladle") + 巛 + 囟; 匕 was later replaced with 肉, leaving 巛+囟 (𡿺) as the inherited sound-bearing remnant.
- [SKIP-1-4-7](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-4-7.md) ([Stroke 11](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2011.md))
