## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*ɡreːb): semantic [[犬 (char)|犬]] ("dog") + phonetic [[夹]] ("to press between") — "narrow (of rooms, streets), tight," extended to "narrow-minded."
- [SKIP-1-3-6](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-6.md) ([Stroke 09](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2009.md))
- 2187th most used character in Classical Chinese (recorded under the traditional form 狹). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 匣|ɣ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 洽|ɣɛp]] → [ㄏㄚㄆ](syllables/ㄏㄚㄆ.md)
- [Grade 6](lookup/Grade%206.md), [Old HSK 4](lookup/HSK/Old%20HSK%204.md), [Jōyō - Kōtō](lookup/Japanese/Jōyō%20-%20Kōtō.md), [Korean Name ㅎ](lookup/Korean/Korean%20Name%20ㅎ.md)
- **Flagged, not force-resolved**: `stand_in: 名専字` claims this character is "restricted to proper names, no standalone Dan'a'yo use," but 狭 is a common, productive adjective across all daughters (せまい, 狭量, 偏狭, etc.), not a name-only character. The only word citing it, [[狭窄]], explicitly documents itself as the "viable form" rescuing *窄*, not 狭 — so no existing compound is available to repoint to (unlike the similar broken-`名専字` fix on [[characters/峡|峡]], where a genuine unused compound, [[海峡]], was sitting right there). Left as-is pending a proper Dan'a'yo word coinage for 狭 itself, which is out of scope for character-page perfecting.
