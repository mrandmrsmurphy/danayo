## Aliases

### 嘔
Traditional form of 呕, "to vomit". SKIP 1-3-11; 14 strokes. It ranks 1914 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 㰶
A rare form associated with 嘔 ("to wrinkle the nose when sobbing"); kept as an alias of 呕. SKIP 1-8-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 12 strokes. Radical 076 欠. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
