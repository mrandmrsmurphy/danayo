## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*sʰraːms): semantic [[Radical 061|心]] ("heart") + phonetic 韱 (no character page exists for 韱) — to repent, to confess one's sins. A clipping of 懺摩, the Buddhist transcription of Sanskrit *kṣamā* ("patience, forbearance"); 忏 is the simplified form and 懴 a variant.
- [SKIP-1-3-17](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-17.md) ([Stroke 20](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2020.md))
- mc_id 0: confirmed genuinely absent from all four Classical Chinese frequency lists (CC 0000–3000). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 初|t͡ʃʰ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 銜|ɣam]] → [ㄑㄚㄇ](../syllables/ㄑㄚㄇ.md); this is the same syllable as 惨 (ㄑㄚㄇ), a homophone by default.
