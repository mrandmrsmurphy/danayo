## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*tʰaːds): semantic [[Radical 187|馬]] ("horse") + phonetic [[太 (char)|太]] — "a horse-load, pack," extended to "to carry, pack (as a beast of burden)." 駄 is the modern Japanese shinjitai (simplified) form; contrary to a once-uncertain flag on this page, it is not a Japan-only invention but a genuine ancient Chinese variant of [[馬 (char)|馬]]+[[大 (char)|大]]-phonetic [[馱]] (the OC of 太 and 大 are nearly identical, differing only by regular voicing, since 太 is itself a graphic differentiation of 大).
