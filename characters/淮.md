## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 085|水]] ("water") + phonetic [[隹]] ("bird," OC *tjul) — some scholars (Yang, 1999) trace the phonetic choice to bird-totem worship among the ancient Huaiyi people who inhabited the Huai river basin; the name of the Huai River (淮河), one of China's three major rivers alongside the Yangtze and Yellow rivers, and secondarily a surname.
