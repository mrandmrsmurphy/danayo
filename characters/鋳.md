## Aliases

### 鑄
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 鋳, "to cast metal"; this is the form the Classical Chinese lists rank. SKIP 1-8-14; 22 strokes. It ranks 1507 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
