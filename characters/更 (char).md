## Notes
- 会意 (original form): two chariots, [[丙 (char)|丙]]+丙 (⿰丙丙). Later Shuowen-era orthography reanalyzed the character as 形声 — [[Radical 066|攴]] ("action, to strike") as semantic + an abbreviated 丙 as phonetic, the leftover second 丙 stroke corrupting into what looks like 一/雨 in the modern glyph. The stored field reflects the original ideogrammic (two-丙) identity rather than the later reanalysis. "Change, replace," hence "more, again."
- [SKIP-4-7-1](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-4/SKIP-4-7-1.md) ([Stroke 07](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2007.md))
