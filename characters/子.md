## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): an image of a baby, with a large head and spread arms, legs wrapped in a blanket — compare [[了]], where the arms are wrapped, and 㜽, where a lock of hair is visible on top; compare also 棄 and 毓, where the baby image is reversed upside-down. The bronze/seal-script form is more elaborate, showing a baby with hair on its head (囟) and arms spread to either side, sitting on a stool (几). Also absorbs the sense of its alias 仔: the seed of a plant, or the young of an animal.
- [SKIP-4-3-1](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-4/SKIP-4-3-1.md) ([Stroke 03](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2003.md))
