## Notes
- 会意 of [[Radical 120|糸]] ("silk strings") and [[Radical 075|木]] ("wood") — silk strings on wood: a stringed instrument, hence "music," extended to "pleasure." The oracle-bone form shows two strings connected to the wood; bronze forms add a 白 component between the strings (perhaps a thumb plucking, perhaps phonetic).
