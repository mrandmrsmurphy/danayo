## Aliases

### 稀
形声 (禾 + phonetic 希), "sparse; rare"; Wiktionary lists 希 as an alternate form of it ([Wiktionary: 稀](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E7%A8%80)). SKIP 1-5-7; 12 strokes. Radical 115 禾. It ranks 3110 in the Classical Chinese lists, a higher number than the page's `mc_id`.

### 冀
A separate character meaning "to look forward to; to hope for", which overlaps the extended sense of 希 ("to hope"); neither Wiktionary page links the two. Kept for searchers. SKIP 2-5-11; 16 strokes. Radical 012 八. It ranks 1024 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 鯑
A separate character (魚 + phonetic 希), "herring roe"; neither Wiktionary page links it to 希. Kept for searchers. SKIP 1-11-7; 18 strokes. Radical 195 魚. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
