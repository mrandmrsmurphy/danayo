## Notes
- 形声 (OC *qrɯːɡs, *qɯ): semantic [[Radical 030|口]] ("mouth") + phonetic [[意]] (OC *qɯɡs) — most commonly a classical exclamatory interjection, "alas! oh!" (噫嘻; 噫吁嚱 in Li Bai's 蜀道難; 噫! 菊之愛 in 愛蓮說), also literally "to belch" and, secondarily, "to sigh" (matching `korean_native: 한숨쉴`). A stray prior note claiming 噫 was "dropped from the Korean HS list in 2000" could not be corroborated — 噫 is absent from `Lookup/Korean/Korean HS.md` entirely (consistent with its own `hanmun_edu_level: 名`, never HS-level to begin with) and the claim structurally mirrors an unrelated character's genuine note ([[繋]], iteration 2054 this session, "added to the Korean HS list in 2000") closely enough to suggest confusion between the two; dropped rather than carried forward as unverified.
