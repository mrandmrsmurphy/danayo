## Aliases

### 锤
Simplified Chinese form of 錘. SKIP 1-5-8 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 13 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 鎚
Variant form of 錘, "hammer, mallet". SKIP 1-8-9; 17 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 捶
扌 + 垂, "to beat, to pound"; a verb built on the same phonetic 垂, kept as an alias of 錘 on the same derived sound. SKIP 1-3-8; 11 strokes. Radical 064 手. It ranks 3467 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
