## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*kraːb, \*qraːb): semantic [[Radical 064|手]] ("hand") + phonetic [[甲 (char)|甲]] (originally a pictogram of a shell/carapace/armor) — "to press, to mortgage/pawn, to seal"; cognate with 壓 ("to press down," no vault page). Distinct from 狎 (犬 semantic + 甲 phonetic, "to be intimate with, to trifle with," no vault page) — formerly wrongly listed as an alias here, a case of alias contamination via the shared 甲 phonetic rather than a real variant.
- [SKIP-1-3-5](lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-5.md) ([Stroke 08](lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2008.md))
- 5284th most used character in Classical Chinese (beyond the top ~4000 locally mirrored, trusted long-tail rank). Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 影|ʔ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 狎|ɣap]] → [ㄚㄆ](syllables/ㄚㄆ.md)
