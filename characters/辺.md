## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[Radical 162|辵]] ("to walk, a road") + phonetic 臱 (no vault page) — 臱 originally meant "border, periphery" and lends its sound and sense to 邊/边, the traditional and mainland-simplified forms. The modern Japanese shinjitai glyph 辺 instead replaces 臱 with a reduced 刀-like shape purely for visual simplification — a non-etymological corruption, not a real semantic or phonetic component.
