## Notes
- 会意 of a hand managing a tangled skein of thread and [[Radical 005|乙]] ("hook, tool") — originally the methodical untangling of thread, hence both "to put in order" and, ironically, "disorder, chaos."

## Aliases

### 亂
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 乱, written with 𤔔 ("hands working a spool") over 乚; the shinjitai 乱 simplified the 𤔔 half. SKIP 1-12-1; 13 strokes. This form is the one the Classical Chinese lists rank, so the `mc_id` 202 is 亂's rank.

### 瀾
Written as 乱 as a 代用字: 瀾 "large wave" (as in 波瀾) is a rare character with no page of its own. Radical 085 水; SKIP 1-3-17; 20 strokes. It has no rank in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 爛
Written as 乱 as a 代用字: 爛 "rotten, brilliant" is a rare character with no page of its own. Radical 086 火; SKIP 1-4-17; 21 strokes. Ranked 2357 in the Classical Chinese lists, so it does not supply the `mc_id`.

### 䜌
Ancient form of the "chaos" element in 亂, kept here as a variant. Radical 149 言; SKIP 1-6-13 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 19 strokes. It has no rank in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 烂
Simplified Chinese form of 爛, so it too is written as 乱. Radical 086 火; SKIP 1-4-5 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 9 strokes. It has no rank in the Classical Chinese lists.
