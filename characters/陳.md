## Notes
- 會意: [[Radical 170|阜]] ("mound, earthworks") + an ancient graphic element depicting rows/ranks arrayed along it — "to arrange in rows," extended to "to exhibit, display" and (as a proper name) the ancient state of Chen. 陈 is the standard simplified form.
