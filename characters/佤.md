## Notes
- 形声: semantic [人 (char)](characters/人%20(char).md) ("person") + phonetic [瓦 (char)](characters/瓦%20(char).md) — genuinely names the Wa/Va ethnic minority of China/Myanmar (a respectful replacement for an older form containing the derogatory 犭 "beast" radical). Repurposed in Dan'a'yo as a loanword vehicle for "watt" (the SI unit of power), with its pronunciation deliberately altered to approximate the English word — hence the intentional homophone collision with [[characters/斡|斡]] documented on the [[syllables/⺢ㄊ|⺢ㄊ]] syllable page itself.
- [SKIP-1-2-4](Lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-2-4.md) ([Stroke 6](Lookup/Stroke/Stroke%206.md))
- mc_id confirmed absent from all Classical Chinese frequency lists (rank 0). Genuine ancient initial/final: [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 疑|ŋ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 麻二合|ɣua]]; the Dan'a'yo syllable [⺢ㄊ](syllables/⺢ㄊ.md) is a deliberate override (see above), not the natural MC-derived reading.
