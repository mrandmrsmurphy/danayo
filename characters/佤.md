## Notes
- 形声: semantic [[人 (char)]] ("person") + phonetic [[瓦 (char)]] — genuinely names the Wa/Va ethnic minority of China/Myanmar (a respectful replacement for an older form containing the derogatory 犭 "beast" radical). Repurposed in Dan'a'yo as a loanword vehicle for "watt" (the SI unit of power), with its pronunciation deliberately altered to approximate the English word — hence the intentional homophone collision with [[characters/斡|斡]] documented on the [[syllables/⺢ㄊ|⺢ㄊ]] syllable page itself.
