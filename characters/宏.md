## Aliases

### 厷
𠂇 + 厶, a pictograph of a hand, an arm and an elbow, the original form of 肱 ("forearm"); Wiktionary also records it as a variant of 宏. It is the phonetic of 宏, 紘 and 雄. Dan'a'yo would give it a sound close to 宏's (MC 古弘切, `k` + `wəŋ`, against 宏's `ɣ` + `ɣwɛŋ`), so it is an alias of 宏, which is the hub for the characters built on it (see Derived Characters). SKIP 3-2-2; 4 strokes. Radical 028 厶. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
