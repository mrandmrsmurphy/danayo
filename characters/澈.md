## Aliases

### 徹
Traditional form of 徹, "to penetrate"; kept as an alias of 澈, whose meaning it shares. SKIP 1-3-12; 15 strokes. Radical 060 彳. It ranks 1591 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 彻
Simplified Chinese form of 徹. SKIP 1-3-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 7 strokes. Radical 060 彳. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
