- 形聲 (OC \*mroːn): semantic [[Radical 142|虫]] ("snake") + phonetic 䜌 (no character page in this vault, OC \*b·roːn/\*b·ron/\*b·rons) — "barbarian." Simplified form of traditional 蠻 (aliased); recorded under that alias's rank in `CC 1000.md`, since 蛮 itself doesn't appear independently.
