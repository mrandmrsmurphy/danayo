## Notes
- 形声 (per Shuowen, 从宀从玉从貝，缶聲): [[Radical 040|宀]] ("roof, house") + 玉 ("jade") + 貝 ("cowrie shell, money") — valuables kept within a house — + phonetic [[缶]]. 宝 is the simplified/shinjitai form, which drops both 貝 and the 缶 phonetic shape entirely, retaining only 宀 + 玉; 寶 is the traditional form.
