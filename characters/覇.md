## Notes
- 會意: originally [[革]] ("hide, change") + [[月]] ("moon") in oracle-bone script, later adding [[雨]] ("rain") as a phonetic element in bronze inscriptions — to dominate, exercise hegemony; a historical overlord or hegemon; tyranny. Traditional form *霸* (whose own rank this vault's Middle Chinese corpus records) and variants *䨣*/*䩗*/*䩻*/*𧈉* all confirmed via Chinese Wiktionary; excluded [[覊]]/[[羈]] ("to restrain, bridle"), confirmed unrelated characters despite superficial graphic similarity.

## Aliases

### 霸
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 覇, written 雨 over 革 and 月. SKIP 2-8-13; 21 strokes. Radical 173 雨. It ranks 712 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 灞
氵 + 霸, "a river in Shanxi" (the Ba River near Xi'an), used only in the place names 灞河 and 灞源. It is a proper-name character with no use of its own, kept as an alias of 覇 by the project owner's decision, on the identical sound (ㄅㄚ, MC `p` + `ɣa`). SKIP 1-3-21; 24 strokes. Radical 085 水. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 䨣
A variant of 覇 (Wiktionary: 雨 over 革, "leather moistened by rain"). SKIP 2-8-9 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 17 strokes. Radical 173 雨. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 䩗
A variant of 覇 (Wiktionary's variant list), written 革 + 月. SKIP 1-9-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 13 strokes. Radical 177 革. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 䩻
A variant of 覇 and 霸 ("to be chief; to rule by might"; Wiktionary). SKIP 1-9-10 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 19 strokes. Radical 177 革. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 𧈉
A rare variant of 覇 (Wiktionary's variant list). SKIP 2-6-13 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 19 strokes. Radical 141 虍. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
