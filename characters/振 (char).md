## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*tjɯn, \*tjɯns): semantic [[Radical 064|手]] ("hand") + phonetic [[辰]] (OC \*djɯn) — "to shake, vibrate, flap (wings)," extended to "to rouse oneself, to excite." Distinct from 顫 (zhàn, "to tremble, shiver," 頁 semantic + 亶 phonetic, no vault page) — related in sense but not a variant.
