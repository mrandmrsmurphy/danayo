- [SKIP-1-3-9](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-3-9.md) ([Stroke 12](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2012.md))

## Aliases

### 冢
"Tomb, burial mound"; the original form of 塚 and kept as an alias. SKIP 2-2-8; 10 strokes. Radical 014 冖. It ranks 1415 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.
