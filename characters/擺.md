## Notes
- 形声 (OC *bralʔ): semantic [[手]] ("hand") + phonetic [[罷]] (OC *breːlʔ) — to place, arrange, display, lay out; extended to "to swing, sway" and, as a noun, "pendulum" (a genuine attested sense, per en.Wiktionary and ja.Wiktionary's kun'yomi ひらく, glossed as "to display" and "pendulum" alike). A stray prior scratch note ("Use 振子; HSK/1, 이름") is resolved here: [[words/振子|振子]] (振 "shake" + 子 "small object"), not 擺, is this vault's actual Dan'a'yo word for "pendulum," consistent with `stand_in: 名専字`; the note's "HSK/1" claim does not hold (no genuine entry exists on `HSK Beginner.md`), and "이름" ("name") is already reflected in the correct `hanmun_edu_level: 名`.
