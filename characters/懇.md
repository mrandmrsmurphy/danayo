## Aliases

### 恳
Simplified Chinese form of 懇, with 艮 for 貇. SKIP 2-6-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 10 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 貇
豸 + 艮, the phonetic of 懇 (貇 + 心) and of 墾 (貇 + 土), and recorded by Wiktionary as a variant of 懇. It has the same derived Dan'a'yo sound as 懇, ㄎㄚㄋ (MC `kʰ` + `ən`), so it is an alias of 懇, which is the hub for the characters built on it (see Derived Characters). SKIP 1-7-6; 13 strokes. Radical 153 豸. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
