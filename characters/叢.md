## Notes
- 形聲: semantic 丵 ("plants growing thick and bushy" — no character page of its own in this vault, permanently forbidden) + phonetic [[取]] — to collect, gather, assemble; a bush, thicket, grove; a collection. Simplified form *丛* and variants *樷*/*欉*/*菆*/*藂* all confirmed via Chinese Wiktionary; excluded 从 (an obsolete 1955 draft simplification not corroborated by Chinese Wiktionary's own variant listing, and itself a common independent character with the unrelated meaning "from").

## Aliases

### 丛
Simplified Chinese form of 叢. SKIP 2-1-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 5 strokes. Radical 001 一. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 樷
A variant form of 叢, "cluster". SKIP 2-8-8; 16 strokes. Radical 075 木. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 欉
A variant form of 叢, "cluster of plants". SKIP 1-4-18; 22 strokes. Radical 075 木. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 菆
艹 + 取, "jungle"; a variant of 叢 and kept as an alias. SKIP 2-3-9 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 12 strokes. Radical 140 艸. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 藂
An alternative form of 叢, "to gather". SKIP 2-3-14; 17 strokes. Radical 140 艸. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
