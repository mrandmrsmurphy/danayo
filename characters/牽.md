## Notes
- 形聲: semantic [[牛]] ("cow, ox") + phonetic [[Radical 095|玄]], with an additional 冖 element of uncertain function (possibly a rope/tether, per Wiktionary's own etymology-incomplete note) — leading an ox by hand; to drag, tow, pull; to involve, drag in. **Corrected here**: the field previously had the semantic/phonetic roles swapped (called 玄 the semantic component and glossed it as "cow, ox," which is 牛's meaning, not 玄's). Variant [[𪺮]] confirmed via both English and Chinese Wiktionary.
