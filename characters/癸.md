## Notes
- [List of 象形](lookup/List%20of%20象形.md): uncertain — en.Wiktionary lists competing proposals (the original form of 戣 "a type of halberd" or 葵 "mallow"; a measuring tool related to 揆/季; or a device for measuring solar shadows to fix directions and solstices, making it the ancestor of 規), while zh.Wiktionary cites Shuowen's own gloss, "冬時，水土平，可揆度也。象水從四方流入地中之形" ("in winter, when water and earth level out and can be measured; depicts water flowing into the earth from four directions"). Borrowed for the tenth of the ten Heavenly Stems (天干), unrelated to whichever original depiction is correct.
