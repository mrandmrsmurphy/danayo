## Notes
- 會意 of [[Radical 046|山]] ("mountain") and [[Radical 189|高]] ("tall") — a high, towering mountain; per the *Shiming* (釋名), "a large, high mountain is called 嵩; 嵩 means 竦 (to rise up)." Extended in Japanese usage to "bulk, volume, pile up" (かさ/かさむ). The name of Mount Song (嵩山), one of the Five Great Mountains of China, and a common surname/given-name element. Genuine variant: 崧 (Tang-era interchangeable form, cross-referenced by the Kangxi Dictionary; no independent page).
