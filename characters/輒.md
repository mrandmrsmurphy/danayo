## Notes
- 形声 (OC *teːb): semantic [[Radical 159|車]] ("cart, chariot") + phonetic [[耴]] (OC *ŋrɯd) — originally the side panels/rails of a chariot box; extended by metaphor to an adverbial sense, "then, promptly, at every turn, always" (as in 動輒 "at every turn"), the sense Dan'a'yo draws on, matching `korean_native: 문득` and the kun'yomi すなわち. The original chariot-part noun sense survives in the secondary kun'yomi わきぎ.
