## Aliases

### 續
Traditional form (kyūjitai) of 続, "to continue". SKIP 1-6-15; 21 strokes. It ranks 1621 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 续
Simplified Chinese form of 續. SKIP 1-3-8 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 11 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
