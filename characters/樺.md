## Notes
- 形声 (Proto-Sino-Tibetan *ɢʷra, "birch"; cognate with Tibetan gro ga "birch bark"): semantic [[木]] ("tree") + phonetic [[華]] — the birch tree (matching `korean_native: 자작나무`), extended to describe a reddish-yellow color (樺色). Genuine simplified variant: 桦; a second candidate, 檴, and 華 itself (which has extensive independent use and its own page in this vault) are not added as aliases.
