## Aliases

### 繆
No link to 謬 found on Wiktionary; 繆 (糸 + 翏, "ten bundles of hemp; a type of silk") is a separate character that shares the phonetic 翏 and is classically written for 謬 in some texts. It is the commoner character in the Classical Chinese lists. SKIP 1-6-11; 17 strokes. Radical 120 糸. It ranks 1346 in the Classical Chinese lists, the lowest among the page's forms, so it sets the page's `mc_id`.

### 谬
The simplified Chinese form of 謬 (讠 + 翏). Wiktionary's 谬 page redirects to 謬, and 謬 lists it as its simplified form ("error; erroneous"). SKIP 1-2-11 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 13 strokes. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 缪
The simplified Chinese form of 繆 (纟 + 翏), which Wiktionary does not link to 謬 on either page; kept here with 繆. SKIP 1-3-11 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 14 strokes. Radical 120 糸. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
