## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*hljaŋ, \*hljaŋs): semantic [[Radical 009|人]] ("person") + phonetic [[昜]], shared with a large phonetic family in this vault ([[揚 (char)|揚]], [[湯 (char)|湯]], [[陽 (char)|陽]], [[場]], [[楊]], [[暢]], [[瘍]], [[腸]]). Wiktionary traces the true ancient phonetic to an obscure variant 𥏻 (no vault page). Schuessler (2007) suggests 傷 may be a causative derivative of [[瘍]] (OC \*laŋ, "ulcer, sore").
- [SKIP-1-2-11](../lookup/SKIP/SKIP-1/SKIP-1-2-11.md) ([Stroke 13](../lookup/Stroke/Stroke%2013.md))
- 366th most used character in Classical Chinese. Ancient [[Lookup/CC/initials/聲 書|ɕ]] + [[Lookup/CC/finals/韻 陽開|ɨɐŋ]] → [ㄙ⼘ㄫ](../syllables/ㄙ⼘ㄫ.md)
