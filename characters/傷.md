## Notes
- 形声 (OC \*hljaŋ, \*hljaŋs): semantic [[Radical 009|人]] ("person") + phonetic [[昜]] — shared with a large phonetic family in this vault ([[揚 (char)|揚]], [[湯 (char)|湯]], [[陽 (char)|陽]], 場, 楊, 暢, [[瘍]], 腸). Wiktionary's stricter etymological analysis traces the true ancient phonetic to an obscure variant 𥏻 (not in this database, no independent vault entry), noting the modern glyph merely "resembles 亻+昜" — but since 昜 corresponds phonologically (same -aŋ rime as the rest of its family) and the character has always presented this way across the CJK tradition (not a Japan-only shinjitai substitution like [[characters/仮|仮]] or [[characters/価|価]]), `graphemic_classification` is correctly kept at 昜, consistent with its 8 phonetic-series siblings. Schuessler (2007) suggests 傷 may be a causative derivative of [[瘍]] (OC \*laŋ, "ulcer, sore").
