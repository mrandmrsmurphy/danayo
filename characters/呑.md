## Notes
- 形声 (MC tʰon): semantic [[口]] ("mouth") + phonetic [[夭]] (MC ʔiᴇu) — to swallow whole without chewing, extended to "absorb, annex, engulf, suppress." 呑 is Japan's shinjitai standard printed form; the more classical/Kangxi form 吞 is recorded as an alias (no independent page in this vault), and its own Classical Chinese frequency rank is recorded here per the vault's sibling-glyph convention. Genuinely dual-classified in Japan as both 表外漢字字体表 (non-Jōyō) and 人名用漢字 (name-use approved) — both `Lookup/Japanese/Hyōgai.md` and `Lookup/Japanese/Jinmeiyō.md` correctly redirect 吞 here; this page records `joyo_level: 表外字` as the primary classification.
