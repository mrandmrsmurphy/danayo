## Aliases

### 坉
土 + 屯, "a bag of soil used to hold back water; a ridge between fields"; a separate word built on 屯 as its phonetic, kept as an alias of 沌 on the same derived sound. SKIP 1-3-4 (computed: EDRDG does not list it); 7 strokes. Radical 032 土. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.

### 飩
食 + 屯, as in 餛飩 "wonton"; a separate word built on 屯 as its phonetic, kept as an alias of 沌 on the same derived sound. SKIP 1-9-4; 12 strokes. Radical 184 食. It has no rank of its own in the Classical Chinese lists.
