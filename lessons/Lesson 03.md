## Learning objectives
By the end of this lesson you will be able to:
- Find a character by its shape, using its SKIP code and its stroke counts.
- Count the strokes of a character, and of its parts.
- Know that a character can have more than one form, and which form Dan'a'yo uses.
- Recognize six common radicals.
- Explain why 父 is a character but not a word.
- Recognize, say and write 135 more characters, for 200 in all.

You can now sound out the ruby, and you know the 65 basic characters.  This lesson gives you 135 more, and the tools to find any character you do not know.  The tools come first, because the list at the end is easier to learn when you can see how its characters are built.

## SKIP: Finding a Character by its Shape
You cannot look up a character by its sound if you do not know the sound.  SKIP solves this.  Every character gets a code of three numbers, worked out from what you can see.

**Step 1: the shape.**  Does the character split into two clear parts?
1. Two parts, side by side (left and right).  Example: 明.
2. Two parts, one above the other (top and bottom).  Example: 名.
3. One part surrounds another (enclosed).  Example: 国.
4. None of these: the character is one piece.  Example: 中.

**Step 2: the strokes.**  For types 1, 2 and 3, count the strokes in each part.  The second number is the strokes in the left, top or outer part.  The third number is the strokes in the right, bottom or inner part.

For type 4, the second number is the total strokes.  The third number tells you about the picture: 1 if a line runs across the top (不), 2 if a line runs across the bottom, 3 if a line runs through the middle (中), and 4 for anything else (之, 内).

### Left and right (1)
- 明 = 日 (4 strokes) + 月 (4 strokes) = **1-4-4**
- 林 = 木 (4) + 木 (4) = **1-4-4**
- 語 = 言 (7) + 吾 (7) = **1-7-7**

### Top and bottom (2)
- 名 = 夕 (3) over 口 (3) = **2-3-3**
- 思 = 田 (5) over 心 (4) = **2-5-4**
- 草 = ⺾ (3) over 早 (6) = **2-3-6**
- 家 = 宀 (3) over 豕 (7) = **2-3-7**

### Enclosed (3)
- 国 = 囗 (3) around 玉 (5) = **3-3-5**
- 問 = 門 (8) around 口 (3) = **3-8-3**
- 風 = 几 (2) around 虫 (7) = **3-2-7**

### Other (4)
- 中 has 4 strokes, with a line through the middle = **4-4-3**
- 不 has 4 strokes, with a line across the top = **4-4-1**
- 之 has 3 strokes = **4-3-4**
- 内 has 4 strokes = **4-4-4**

### Counting strokes
Everything in SKIP depends on counting strokes correctly.  A stroke is one line drawn without lifting the brush.  A corner does not start a new stroke.  So 口 has three strokes: the left side, the top and right side drawn as one, and the bottom.

- 人 has 2 strokes, 力 has 2, 口 has 3, 小 has 3.
- 日 has 4 strokes, 木 has 4, 火 has 4, 月 has 4.
- 田 has 5 strokes.

If you are not sure of a count, look up the code one stroke more and one less.  The SKIP index forgives small mistakes in the parts.

Once you have the three numbers, the SKIP index takes you straight to a page of every character with that code.

## Variants
Some characters have more than one form.  The same word can be written in an older, more complicated form, or in a newer, simpler one.  Three systems are in common use: the new forms of Japan (shinjitai), the traditional forms, and the simplified forms of mainland China.

Dan'a'yo is not written in Traditional or Simplified.  It chooses one form for each character, and treats the other forms as **aliases**: you will recognize them when you meet them, but the form in this course is the one to learn and write.

| Dan'a'yo form | Strokes | Other form | Strokes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 国 | 8 | 國 | 11 |
| 気 | 6 | 氣 | 10 |
| 学 | 8 | 學 | 16 |
| 数 | 13 | 數 | 15 |
| 声 | 7 | 聲 | 17 |
| 与 | 3 | 與 | 14 |
| 見 | 7 | 见 | 4 |

The strokes matter.  A different form can have a different shape and a different number of strokes, and so a different SKIP code.  Always count the Dan'a'yo form.

## Radicals
A **radical** is a part that appears again and again inside characters.  Old dictionaries were organized by radical: you found the radical, counted the remaining strokes, and looked in that section.  There are 214 of them.  You do not need to learn them all.  Six common ones will start you off.

| Radical | Meaning | Where it sits | In this lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| 口 | mouth | anywhere | 君 名 問 和 右 |
| 人 (亻) | person | left | 今 以 使 何 作 |
| 水 (氵) | water | left | 漢 海 流 深 |
| 辵 (辶) | walk | bottom left | 道 通 遠 進 近 |
| 言 | word | left | 語 誰 |
| 門 | gate | outside | 門 間 |

The radical usually hints at the meaning: 海 (sea) and 流 (flow) have water.  The other part often hints at the sound, which is a story for later.

## Characters and Words
A character is a shape.  A word is something you say and understand.  Many characters are words on their own: 人, 山 and 水 are all words.  But many are not.

父 is not a word.  You cannot say ㄅㄨ by itself and be understood.  The word for "father" is 父親, ㄅㄨㄑㄧㄋ.  You are not responsible for 親 yet.  Learn only that 父 never appears alone, and that the meaning "father" is carried by the whole word 父親.  The same is true of 母, which lives in 母親, ㄇㄛㄨㄑㄧㄋ.

| Character | Not a word alone | It appears in | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 父 | father | 父親 | ㄅㄨㄑㄧㄋ |
| 母 | mother | 母親 | ㄇㄛㄨㄑㄧㄋ |
| 田 | field | 田野 | ㄉㄝㄋ·⼘ |
| 虫 | bug | 昆虫 | ㄍㄛㄋㄐㄨㄫ |
| 才 | talent | 天才 | ㄊㄝㄋㄐㄚㄧ |
| 世 | world | 世界 | ㄙㄝㄍ⼶ |

Of the 65 characters in Lesson 02, 50 are words on their own and 15 are not: 世, 主, 元, 刀, 子, 才, 文, 次, 母, 父, 田, 糸, 羊, 虫 and 西.  When you meet one of these, expect it inside a longer word.
