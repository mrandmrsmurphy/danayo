# Dan'a'yo Lesson Program
**A Graded Course for English Speakers**

## Overview

This curriculum guides learners from **sound → characters → basic sentences → complex discourse**. The design prioritizes **Topic-Comment structure** as the foundational grammar principle, since it is alien to English and must be internalized early. Each lesson builds on the previous one and includes **worked examples, exercises, and vocabulary**.

## CORE TIER: Basic Sentence Structure (Lessons 04–08)

### Lesson 04: Topic-Comment Structure (The Foundation)
**Goal**: Learner understands that Dan'a'yo is **Topic-prominent**, not subject-prominent, and can construct simple Topic-Comment sentences.

**Why early**: This is the most alien concept for English speakers and underpins all subsequent grammar.

**Core concepts**:
- **Topic** = what you're talking about (extra-clausal)
- **Comment** = what you're saying about it (the predicate)
- Topic-Comment ≠ Subject-Predicate
- **Simultaneous topic and subject** (Japanese は vs が parallels)

**Examples**:
- 天 高。"As for the sky, (it) is high." (Topic: 天 "sky" / Comment: 高 "high")
- 我 市場於 行。"As for me, at the market, go." (Topic-fronting of locative phrase)
- 荘子 智慧太。"As for Zhuangzi, his wisdom-great." (Zhuangzi is topic; 智慧 is subject within comment)

**Exercises**:
- Identify topic vs. comment in 10 given sentences
- Convert 5 English sentences into Topic-Comment structure
- Construct 5 original Topic-Comment sentences from prompts

**Reference**: `grammar/文法 - 04句法.en.md` (Syntax section)

---

### Lesson 05: Nouns, Pronouns & Simple Copulas
**Goal**: Learner can name things, use pronouns, and construct noun-predicate sentences with copulas.

**Vocabulary**:
- Personal pronouns: 我 (I), 君 (you), 我等 (we), 君等 (you-plural)
- Common nouns: 人 (person), 魚 (fish), 木 (tree), 日 (day), 月 (month)
- Demonstratives: 此 (this), 其 (that), 彼 (that-yonder)
- Interrogatives: 誰 (who?), 何 (what?)

**Copulas**:
- 是/非 (equative: to be / not to be)
- 有/無 (possession: to have / not have)
- 在/莫 (existence: to exist / not exist)
- Why Dan'a'yo has **three pairs** (not one copula like English)

**Structures**:
- **[Topic] 是 [Noun]** — "X is a Y"
  - Example: 我 是 人。"I am a person."
- **[Topic] 有 [Noun]** — "X has Y"
  - Example: 我 有 犬。"I have a dog."
- **[Topic] 在 [Location]** — "X exists/is at Z"
  - Example: 魚 在 水於。"Fish exist in water."

**Exercises**:
- Fill-in-the-blank with correct copula
- Translate 10 English sentences involving "to be" or "to have"
- Negate 5 sentences using 非/無/莫

**Reference**: `grammar/文法 - 04句法.en.md` (Copulas section), `grammar/文法 - 05形態.en.md` (Nominal Morphology)

---

### Lesson 06: Verbs (Eventives) & Simple Actions
**Goal**: Learner can construct action sentences and understand verb complements.

**Key concept**: Eventives **require objects** — they are transitive by nature.

**Vocabulary**:
- Common transitive verbs: 捕 (catch), 食 (eat), 行 (go), 創造 (create), 見 (see), 聞 (hear)
- How to recognize that a verb needs an object (no omission without special marking)

**Structures**:
- **[Topic] [Eventive] [Object]** — SVO within the comment
  - Example: 我 食 魚。"I eat fish."
  - Example: 神 創造 世界。"God created the world."

**Passives (preview)**:
- Prefix 被– to convert to passive
- Example: 魚 被 捕。"Fish were caught."

**Exercises**:
- Create 10 transitive sentences with Topic-Comment structure
- Convert 5 active sentences to passive with 被–
- Identify which character is Topic, which is Object, in a list of sentences

**Reference**: `grammar/文法 - 04句法.en.md` (Eventives section), `grammar/文法 - 05形態.en.md` (Verbal Morphology)

---

### Lesson 07: Adjectives & Stative Verbs
**Goal**: Learner can describe qualities and understand that Dan'a'yo adjectives function like verbs.

**Key concept**: Statives are **intransitive verbs** that denote qualities. They don't require complements.

**Vocabulary**:
- Common statives: 大 (big), 小 (small), 白 (white), 黑 (black), 高 (high), 長 (long), 短 (short)

**Structures**:
- **[Topic] [Stative]** — No copula needed
  - Example: 天 高。"The sky is high."
  - Example: 雪 白。"Snow is white."
- **[Topic] 是 [Stative-Noun]** — If nominalizing
  - Example: 我 是 大人。"I am a great person." (大 modifies 人 directly)

**Comparison**:
- **Equative**: 我 君如 高。"I am as tall as you."
- **Comparative**: 我 君比 更 高。"I am taller than you."
- **Superlative**: 此山 第一 高。"This mountain is the tallest."

**Exercises**:
- Describe 5 objects using Topic-Stative structure
- Form comparative and superlative versions of 5 stative verbs
- Translate 10 English adjective sentences into Dan'a'yo

**Reference**: `grammar/文法 - 04句法.en.md` (Statives section), `grammar/文法 - 05形態.en.md` (Degrees of Comparison)

---

### Lesson 08: Case Marking & Postpositions
**Goal**: Learner can mark locatives, datives, instrumentals, and other grammatical relations.

**Why now**: Case marking clarifies the roles of noun phrases and enables more complex Topic-Comment structures.

**Postpositions** (suffixing after noun phrases):
- **於** (locative): where/at — 市場於 "at the market"
- **以** (instrumental): with/by means of — 刀以 "with a knife"
- **于** (dative): to/for (animate) — 友于 "to a friend"
- **由** (ablative): from — 家由 "from home"
- **至** (terminative): until/to — 夜至 "until night"
- **共** (comitative): with (together) — 友共 "with a friend"
- **似** (similative): like — 雪似 "like snow"

**Structures**:
- **[Topic] [Postposition-marked NP] [Verb] [Object]**
  - Example: 我 市場於 食 魚。"I at-the-market eat fish."
  - Example: 神 刀以 創造 世界。"God by-a-knife created the world." (unusual but grammatical)

**Exercises**:
- Reorder scrambled sentences using postposition cues
- Add correct postpositions to 10 sentences
- Translate 10 English sentences with locatives/instrumentals

**Reference**: `grammar/文法 - 05形態.en.md` (Adverbial Morphology), `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md` (Relationals)

## INTERMEDIATE TIER: Modifiers & Complex Structure (Lessons 09–12)

### Lesson 09: Modifiers (Prefixes) & Negation
**Goal**: Learner can negate, express ability, and mark aspect/tense.

**Negation**:
- **不–** (verbal negation): applies to any verb/stative
  - Example: 我 不 行。"I do not go."
  - Example: 雪 不 白。"Snow is not white."

**Ability & Permission**:
- **可–** (ability): can, able
  - Example: 我 可 行。"I can go."
  - Example: 我 不可 行。"I cannot go."
  - Example: 我 可不 行。"I can (choose to) not go."
  - Example: 我 不可不 行。"I cannot (help but) go."

**Aspect/Tense**:
- **已** (perfective): completed — 行已 "went/has gone"
- **未** (negative perfective): not yet — 行未 "has not yet gone"
- **將** (prospective/future): will go — 行將 "will go"
- **中** (continuous): ongoing — 行中 "is going"

**Exercises**:
- Negate 10 sentences
- Express ability/inability for 5 actions
- Mark aspect on 10 verbs and explain the nuance

**Reference**: `grammar/文法 - 05形態.en.md` (Verbal Morphology)

---

### Lesson 10: Relative Clauses & the Particle 之
**Goal**: Learner can construct relative clauses and understand modifier-before-head word order.

**Key principle**: **Modifiers come first** — relative clauses precede the noun they modify.

**Structure**:
- **(Clause + 之) + Noun** → "the Noun which (Clause)"

**Examples**:
- 捕已 魚 之 男人 — "the man who caught fish" (Clause: 捕已 魚, Head: 男人)
- 走 之 馬 — "the running horse" (literally: "the horse which runs")
- 我 之 描 — "my cat" (literally: "the cat which is-mine")

**Exercises**:
- Transform 10 English relative clauses into Dan'a'yo with 之
- Identify the clause and head noun in 10 given sentences
- Construct 5 original relative clause sentences from prompts

**Reference**: `grammar/文法 - 05形態.en.md` (Adnominal Morphology)

---

### Lesson 11: Nominalization & Topic-Shifting
**Goal**: Learner can nominalize verbs and statives, allowing them to serve as topics.

**Why this matters**: A verb cannot naturally be a topic. Nominalization solves this.

**Nominalizing suffixes**:
- **–物** (object/result): 食物 "food" (what is eaten), 創造物 "creation"
- **–事** (event/action): 食事 "eating/meal" (the act), 學習事 "study"

**Structures**:
- **[Nominalized-Verb-as-Topic] [Comment]**
  - Example: 食事 艱難。"Eating is difficult." (Lit: "The act-of-eating is-difficult")
  - Example: 創造事 喜悅。"Creation is joyful."

**Exercises**:
- Nominalize 10 verbs and use each as a topic
- Explain the semantic difference between –物 and –事 for 5 verbs
- Translate 10 English sentences where a gerund is the subject

**Reference**: `grammar/文法 - 05形態.en.md` (Nominalization)

---

### Lesson 12: Three Registers (Classical, Literary, Online)
**Goal**: Learner recognizes the three major stylistic modes and their grammar differences.

**Why now**: Learner can now understand register variation without being overwhelmed by basics.

**Classical Style (古風體)**:
- Terse, concise, omits copulas and particles where context allows
- Often omits topics if clear from context
- Resembles literary Chinese
- Example: 天高。"Heaven high." (vs. 天 是 高。"Sky is high.")

**Literary Style (書面體)** — Standard for formal writing:
- Explicit, requires copulas with noun comments
- Mandatory postpositions
- Requires nominalizers when eventives/statives are topics
- Full ruby annotation
- Example: 天 是 高。"The sky is high."

**Online Style (網語體)**:
- Economical, bopomofo-only or mixed
- SVO dominates; topic-comment rare
- Pro-drop to the extreme
- Example: 高！"(It's) high!"

**Exercises**:
- Convert one sentence across all three registers
- Identify register in 10 given passages
- Write a 5-sentence paragraph in each register

**Reference**: `grammar/文法 - 04句法.en.md` (Modes of Syntax)

## ADVANCED TIER: Complex Discourse (Lessons 13–17)

### Lesson 13: Sentence-Final Particles & Mood
**Goal**: Learner can mark interrogation, exclamation, emphasis, and emotional tone.

**Key Sentence-Final Particles**:
- **乎** (interrogative): forms yes-no questions
  - Example: 汝 行 乎？"Do you go?"
- **矣** (emphatic perfective): "Why has X already happened!"
  - Example: 行 矣！"Why have you gone!"
- **哉** (vocative/exclamative): "O X!" or emphatic
  - Example: 父 哉！"O Father!"
- **也** (declarative emphasis)
- **該** (deontic: should, ought)
- **請** (imperative: please)
- **勿** / **別** (prohibitive: do not)

**Exercises**:
- Mark 10 statements as questions, exclamations, or commands
- Rewrite sentences with different SFPs and explain the mood change
- Translate 10 English questions/exclamations

**Reference**: `grammar/文法 - 04句法.en.md` (Sentence-final Particles), `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md` (Modals)

### Lesson 14: Numbers, Classifiers & Counting
**Goal**: Learner can express quantity and use the correct classifier for any noun.

**Numerals**:
- Cardinals: 一, 二, 三, 四, 五, 六, 七, 八, 九, 十
- Tens/Hundreds: 十, 百, 千
- Ordinals: 第一 (first), 第二 (second), etc.

**Classifiers** (measure words):
- **人** (people), **名** (people formal), **匹** (animals)
- **冊** (books), **台** (machines), **杯** (cups)
- **條** (long-thin: noodles, pencils), **個** (misc.)
- **段** (time intervals), **番** (times/repetitions)

**Structure**: NP + Numeral + Classifier
- Example: 犬 三 匹。"Dogs three-ANIMAL" = "Three dogs."
- Example: 肋骨 六 條。"Ribs six-LONG-THIN" = "Six ribs."

**Exercises**:
- Identify the correct classifier for 20 nouns
- Count from 1 to 99 with classifiers
- Translate 10 English sentences with quantities

**Reference**: `grammar/文法 - 05形態.en.md` (Classifiers), `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md` (Numerals & Classifiers)

### Lesson 15: Conjunctions & Clause Combining
**Goal**: Learner can link clauses and construct complex sentences.

**Conjunctions**:
- **而** (coordinating): and, but; also marks clause boundary
  - Example: 我 食 而 君 睡。"I eat and you sleep."
- **与** (nominal coordination): and (for nouns)
  - Example: 人 与 犬。"People and dogs."
- **乃** (consequential): therefore, then
  - Example: 雨 降 乃 地 濕。"Rain falls, therefore ground is wet."
- **雖** (concessive): although
  - Example: 雖 天 寒 而 我 行。"Although sky is cold, I go."
- **若** (conditional): if
  - Example: 若 雨 降 乃 我 留。"If rain falls, I stay."
- **或** (disjunctive): or
  - Example: 我 食 或 睡。"I eat or I sleep."

**Exercises**:
- Link 10 pairs of sentences with appropriate conjunctions
- Mark the logical relation (cause, concession, condition) in 10 complex sentences
- Translate 10 English complex sentences

**Reference**: `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md` (Linkers/Conjunctions)

### Lesson 16: Idioms & Idiomatic Expressions (成語)
**Goal**: Learner knows a core set of idioms (成語) and understands their etymology.

**What are chengyu**:
- Four-character compound phrases with classical roots
- Often carry cultural weight or metaphorical meaning
- Every educated speaker of CJKV languages knows ~100 core idioms

**Sample idioms** (with etymology and meaning):
- 合漢再決 "The Sinosphere chooses unity" (Dan'a'yo's motto)
- 一刻千金 "A moment worth a thousand gold" = "time is money"
- 切磋琢磨 "Cut and polish" = "mutual refinement" (of jade or scholarship)
- 跳梁跋扈 "Jumping and domineering" = "arrogant upstart"

**Exercises**:
- Learn 20 core idioms and their English equivalents
- Explain the character-by-character etymology of 10 idioms
- Use 5 idioms in original sentences

**Reference**: `chengyu/` directory (individual idiom files with etymological notes)

### Lesson 17: Discourse Structure & Narratives
**Goal**: Learner can follow and construct multi-sentence discourse, including narratives, arguments, and descriptions.

**Discourse principles**:
- **Topic continuity**: Once a topic is established, pronouns or zero-anaphora maintain it
- **Scene-setting**: Location and time adjuncts come early
- **Narrative sequencing**: Use 乃, 然後, 而 to chain events
- **Elaboration**: Use relative clauses (之) to add detail without losing topic

**Narrative structure**:
1. Scene-setting (Where? When?)
2. Introduction of participants (Who?)
3. Complication / action sequence
4. Resolution / conclusion

**Example (4-sentence narrative)**:
- 寺院於 / 月光之夜 / 和也 在。（Scene）
  "At temple / under moonlight-night / Kazuya existed."
- 友 魚三匹 之 籠 被 携。（Introduction）
  "Friend carried basket of three-fish."
- 和也 籠 見 而 喜悦。（Complication）
  "Kazuya saw basket and was joyful."
- 友 與 共 食事 乃。（Resolution）
  "Friend and [Kazuya] together ate, then."

**Exercises**:
- Read 3 short narratives and answer comprehension questions
- Write a 5-sentence narrative using the structure above
- Convert an English short story (100 words) into Dan'a'yo

**Materials to create**:
- Sample narratives in Literary mode with English translation and annotation
- Common narrative connectors reference sheet

**Reference**: `grammar/文法 - 04句法.en.md` (Clause combining), conjunction references

## KEY PEDAGOGICAL PRINCIPLES

1. **Topic-Comment early**: Lesson 04 introduces this alien concept immediately after basic phonology.
2. **Scaffolded complexity**: Each lesson adds one major feature; earlier lessons never feel overcrowded.
3. **English speakers' perspective**: Contrasts with English structure; explains "why" not just "how."
4. **Register-aware**: By Lesson 12, learner knows the language varies by context.
5. **Real discourse**: By Lesson 17, learner can read/write connected text, not isolated sentences.
6. **Three modes of practice**: Recognition (reading), production (writing), and analysis (grammar exercises).
