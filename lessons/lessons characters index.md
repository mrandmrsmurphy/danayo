# Lessons: Character Index

This index records which characters a learner should know by the end of each lesson of the [[lesson-outline]].  A character is "assigned" to the lesson in which it is first taught, and is then expected from that lesson onward.  The word equivalent is [[lessons word index]].

## Goal
By the end of the whole course, every character of Grades 1 to 6 should be assigned to a lesson, along with a selection of the Advanced and Name characters.  Characters are assigned in the order of the grade lists, with the simplest pictograms first.

Grades 1 to 6 together hold 1989 characters.  So far 65 of them are assigned.  The Advanced and Name grades are optional: assign as many as the course needs.

## By lesson

### Lesson 01
No characters are assigned.  The lesson teaches the sounds, using a drill of twenty characters (一二三十人山大口木水火上下女千日有魚雨東) for practice only.
