## Notes
- [珈](../characters/珈.md) "coffee (first syllable)" + [啡](../characters/啡.md) "coffee (second syllable)" + [因](../characters/因.md) "cause; suffix of -ine" — caffeine; this word is the stand-in for 啡.

珈啡因 is caffeine: the bitter alkaloid in coffee and tea. It is [[珈啡]] "coffee" plus 因, the same pattern as the real Chinese word 咖啡因 (Mandarin kāfēiyīn, Cantonese gaa3 fe1 jan1, where 因 renders the suffix of English \"-ine\"), with the vault's 珈 in place of 咖. Wiktionary has no entry for the 珈啡因 spelling, and Japanese and Korean do not form a compound at all: they borrow the English word (`japanese` カフェイン, `korean` 카페인, which are those real words and not readings of these characters). The compound is therefore Dan'a'yo's own templatic coinage, built on the Chinese pattern, and carries the `neologism` tag. It is the stand-in that legitimizes [啡](../characters/啡.md), which is too bound to stand alone and also appears in other coffee-family compounds.
