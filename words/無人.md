## Notes
- [無](../characters/無%20(char).md) "without" + [人](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 無's and 人's own stand-ins are themselves).

無人 means depopulated, unmanned.

Mandarin wúrén, Cantonese mou4 jan4, Japanese ぶにん, and Korean 무인 are all standard, fully compositional readings, and also the real, standard everyday words for "unmanned" in each language.
