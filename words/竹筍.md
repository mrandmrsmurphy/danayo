## Notes
- [竹 (char)](../characters/竹%20(char).md) "bamboo" + [筍](../characters/筍.md) "bamboo shoot"

竹筍 is a bamboo shoot, the young edible sprout of the bamboo. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 竹筍 (alias 竹笋; Mandarin zhúsǔn, Cantonese zuk1 seon2) as "bamboo shoots". The Japanese ちくじゅん, Korean 죽순 and Vietnamese trúc duẩn fields are built from the readings of the two characters.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [筍](../characters/筍.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
