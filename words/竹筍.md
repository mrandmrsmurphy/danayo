## Notes
- [竹 (char)](../characters/竹%20(char).md) "bamboo" + [筍](../characters/筍.md) "bamboo shoot" — 竹筍 is the stand-in word for [筍](../characters/筍.md); 筍 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse.

竹筍 is the young, edible sprout of the bamboo plant, harvested before it hardens into mature cane — a staple ingredient across East and Southeast Asian cuisine, eaten fresh, pickled, dried, or canned, and prominent in Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and Vietnamese cooking alike.

Japanese ちくじゅん and Korean 죽순 are both standard compositional Sino-readings. Vietnamese trúc duẩn is attested, though everyday spoken Vietnamese overwhelmingly prefers the native măng (or măng tre, "bamboo shoot"). No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄨㄎㄙ⼜ㄊ unique across both words and characters).
