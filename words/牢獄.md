## Etymology
- [牢](../characters/牢.md) "prison" + [獄](../characters/獄.md) "prison" — 牢's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 獄's own is [[監獄]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 牢 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

牢獄 means prison, jail.

Mandarin láoyù, Japanese ろうごく (ROU+GOKU), and Vietnamese lao ngục were already correct and fully compositional. Fixed a missing space in `cantonese` (lou4juk6 → lou4 juk6). Fixed a real bug in `korean`: 뇌옥 (a South Korean 두음법칙-shifted form) → 뢰옥, retaining word-initial ㄹ per the vault's North Korean/문화어 convention, matching 牢's own stored citation (뢰). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/`aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄚㄨ⼄ㄎ unique across both words and characters).
