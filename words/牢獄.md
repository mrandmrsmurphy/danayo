## Notes
- [牢](../characters/牢.md) "prison" + [獄](../characters/獄.md) "prison" — 牢's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 獄's own is [[監獄]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 牢 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

牢獄 means prison, jail.

Mandarin láoyù, Cantonese lou4 juk6, Japanese ろうごく and Vietnamese lao ngục are fully compositional. Korean 뢰옥 keeps the word-initial ㄹ of the North Korean convention (not the South Korean 뇌옥), matching 牢's own citation 뢰.
