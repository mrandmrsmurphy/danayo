## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [豚 (char)](../characters/豚%20(char).md) "pig" — no stand-in relationship applies (海's own stand-in is [[海洋]]; 豚's own is itself).

海豚 means dolphin (literally "sea pig").

Mandarin hǎitún, Cantonese hoi2 tyun4, and Korean 해돈 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 海's and 豚's own stored citations exactly. Japanese いるか and Vietnamese cá heo are the real, standard native words for "dolphin" in each language — the same real-world-species-name convention already established on [[龍蝦]]/[[水牛]]/[[河馬]]/[[海牛]].
