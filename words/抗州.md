## Notes
- [抗](../characters/抗.md) "resist" + [州](../characters/州%20(char).md) "state" — Hang prefecture (real spelling 杭州). No stand-in relationship applies (抗's is [[抵抗]]; 州 is its own stand-in).

抗州 is the vault spelling of 杭州 (Hangzhou), the capital of Zhejiang province in eastern China (from memory, not re-checked). 杭 is listed as an alias of 抗 in the character pages, so by the vault's alias rule the filename uses the parent form 抗 and 杭州 is the word's alias; the real-world spelling is 杭州. Wiktionary traces the name to 餘杭, traditionally glossed as a mistaken form of 禹航 "Yu's ferry", but more likely a transcription of a native placename; it also lists Hangzhou Bay (the bay between Shanghai and Ningbo) and a historical prefecture or province.

Japanese こうしゅう is the Sino-Japanese reading; Wiktionary also lists くいしゅう, said to distinguish it orally from 広州 (which is also こうしゅう). Korean 항주 and Vietnamese Hàng Châu are the Sino-Xenic readings (Wiktionary Korean 항주); the usual South Korean name today is 항저우 (from memory, not re-checked). Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese (as 杭州), Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese entries. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄫㄐㄨㄛ unique across both words and characters).
