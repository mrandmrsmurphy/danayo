## Notes
- [[尞]] "bonfire"

尞 itself has no independent life in any modern CJKV language — it survives only as the shared phonetic root behind 僚 ("colleague"), 寮 ("dormitory"), 療 ("treat, cure"), 瞭 ("clear"), and 遼 ("distant, far"). Dan'a'yo revives it as a standalone noun, "bonfire," reclaiming the oracle-bone sense of wood burning for sacrifice that its descendants long ago drifted away from. Assigned to the previously-unused syllable ㄌ⼘ by deliberate placement rather than its organic Middle Chinese outcome (which would have joined 寮/瞭/遼's already 8-member ㄌ⼘ㄨ) — see [[characters/尞|尞's character page]] for the full phonological rationale.
