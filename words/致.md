## Notes
- [致](../characters/致%20(char).md) "cause" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 致 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

致 means to cause, to bring about, as in 致使 (resulting in) and 一致 (unanimous).

Mandarin zhì, cantonese zi3, and korean 치 (chi) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading いた (ita, stem of いたす "to do, cause" humbly) rather than the on-reading チ (CHI) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese trí is one of two stored candidates, matching its use in real compounds like 一致→nhất trí. A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone with [[遅]] ("late, slow") — coincidental, unrelated in meaning. Checked the other ㄑㄧㄜ-reading character, 次 — its own `stand_in` is [[次第]], not a self-citation, so no third homophone.
