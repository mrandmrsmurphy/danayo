## Notes
- [厠](../characters/厠.md) "WC; toilet" + [所 (char)](../characters/所%20(char).md) "place"
- This word is the viable form of [厠](../characters/厠.md): the character's meaning (toilet) is identical, but 厠 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. The compound adds 所 ("place"), making the location explicit.
- Japanese omitted: mechanical SHI+SHO reading ししょ collides with 司書 "librarian".

Mandarin cèsuǒ, Cantonese ci3 so2, and Korean 측소 (cheukso) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese xí sở is likewise compositional, from 厠's xí (one of two attested readings, alongside sí) and 所's own sở; everyday Vietnamese instead overwhelmingly favors native constructions — nhà vệ sinh, nhà xí, hố xí, cầu tiêu — over any Sino-Vietnamese compound. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 치스 diverges from Sino-Korean 측소 in both syllables. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄙㄜ unique among both words and characters).
