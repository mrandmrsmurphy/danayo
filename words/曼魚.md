## Notes
- [曼](../characters/曼.md) "long, beautiful" + [魚](../characters/魚%20(char).md) "fish" — 曼's own Notes document that it serves as a 借代字 (rebus substitution) for 鰻 ("eel") in this specific compound, since 鰻 has no independent character page of its own; 魚's own stand-in is [[魚類]].

曼魚 means eel.

Mandarin mànyú, Cantonese maan6 jyu4, and Vietnamese man are 曼's own compositional readings (matching its stored citation exactly), used here in place of the real character 鰻's very similar but distinct readings (mányú/maan4/mạn) — the real eel character shares 曼 as its phonetic component. Fixed Korean: 뱀장어 was the native, non-compositional real-world Korean word for eel, replaced with the compositional Sino-Korean form 만어 (matching 曼's own 만 + 魚's own 어); the native word remains documented on 曼's own `korean_native` field. Japanese むなぎ is preserved as-is — an attested archaic form of the word for eel (predating modern うなぎ, found in Man'yōshū-era texts), not a typo. Kept `鰻魚` in `aliases` (the standard modern Chinese spelling using the real, avoided character). Added `hsk_level: "無"` (matching 曼's own confirmed no-HSK-level status). No homophones (注音 ㄇㄚㄋ⼄ unique across both words and characters).
