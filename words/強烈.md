## Notes
- [強](../characters/強%20(char).md) "strong" + [烈](../characters/烈.md) "fierce, intense" — "strong and fierce," intense. Their own `stand_in` values: 強's is itself; 烈's is [[激烈]]; none points here, so 強烈 is an independent compound.

強烈 is intense: strong, vivid, felt with force, as of light, color, flavor or feeling. Mandarin qiángliè, Cantonese koeng4 lit6, Japanese きょうれつ and Korean 강렬 all use it. It is intensity of impression, where [[猛烈]] is fierce, driving force and [[激烈]] is violent, heated conflict.
