## Notes
- [繁](../characters/繁.md) "lush, complicated" + [華](../characters/華.md) "gorgeous, splendid" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (繁's is [[繁茂]], 華's is [[華美]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 繁 is individually `kwin: false`.

繁華 means lively or bustling — a prosperous, flourishing scene, as of a busy city district.

Mandarin fánhuá, Cantonese faan4 waa4, Japanese はんか (hanka), and Korean 번화 (beonhwa) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 繁華 is itself a common attested word for "bustling, prosperous" in all four languages. Vietnamese phồn hoa is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
