## Notes
- [郵](characters/郵.md) "mail, post" + [便 (char)](characters/便%20(char).md) "convenience" — "mail, post."

Japanese ゆうびん and korean 우편 are directly attested standard readings. **Found and fixed a wrong-compound reading bug on `mandarin`/`cantonese`**: yóujiàn/jau4 gin6-2 actually belong to the unrelated (though related-meaning) compound 郵件 ("mail item," using 件 rather than 便) — corrected to the honest compositional yóubiàn/jau4 bin6 (郵's own yóu/jau4 + 便's own biàn/bin6). Vietnamese bưu tiện is the honest compositional reading (郵's own bưu + 便's own tiện); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. **Found and removed a bad-aliases-entry**: `郵件` is that same genuinely different compound, not a spelling variant of 郵便; kept `邮便`, the legitimate simplified-character variant (郵→邮). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 웃변 diverges from Sino-Korean 우편 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄨㄛㄅ⼶ㄋ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 郵's own `stand_in` is this exact word (郵便), so 郵 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 郵便; 便's own `stand_in` is instead 便 itself, so this word does not legitimize 便.
