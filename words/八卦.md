## Notes
- [八 (char)](../characters/八%20(char).md) "eight" + [卦 (char)](../characters/卦%20(char).md) "trigram" — "bagua, the eight trigrams," the set of eight symbols used in Taoist cosmology and the I Ching (易經), each built from three stacked broken/unbroken lines and paired with a natural element/attribute:

- ☰ Heaven 乾; _Qián_, warming
- ☳ Thunder 震; _Zhèn_, storming
- ☵ Water 坎; _Kǎn_, pooling
- ☶ Mountain 艮; _Gèn_, jutting
- ☴ Wind 巽; _Xùn_, dispersing
- ☲ Fire 離; _Lí_, dancing
- ☱ Lake 兌; _Duì_, engulfing
- ☷ Earth 坤; _Kūn_, resting

Mandarin bāguà, Cantonese baat3 gwaa3, Japanese はちけ, Korean 팔괘, and Vietnamese bát quái are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 받괘 diverges from Sino-Korean 팔괘 in the first syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄊㄍ⺢ㄧ unique among both words and characters).
