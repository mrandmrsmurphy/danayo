Mandarin bùzú, cantonese bou6 zuk6, japanese ぶぞく, korean 부족, and vietnamese bộ tộc (a directly attested, extremely common Vietnamese word) are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 봇족 diverges from Sino-Korean 부족 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄛㄨㄐㄛㄎ unique among both words and characters).
