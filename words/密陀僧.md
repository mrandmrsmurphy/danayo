## Notes
- [密 (char)](../characters/密%20(char).md) "dense; thick; close; intimate; secret" + [陀](../characters/陀.md) "rough terrain" + [僧](../characters/僧.md) "priest; monk; bonze"

密陀僧 is litharge — lead(II) oxide, a substance used since antiquity in glassmaking, pottery glazes, and traditional medicine. Unlike a normal compositional word, none of the three characters contribute their own meaning here: 密陀僧 is a phonetic transliteration (Wiktionary-confirmed) of Persian مردار سنگ (mordār-sang, literally "dead/impure stone"), borrowed into Chinese along the same overland trade routes that carried the mineral itself — cognate forms exist in Urdu (murdā-sang), Hindi (murdāsaṅkh), and Sanskrit. The three characters were chosen purely for their sounds (mì-tuó-sēng approximating mordar-sang), the same principle behind other sound-based transliterations rather than sense-based compounds.

**Real bug found and fixed 2026-09-29**: 僧's contribution to this word's `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` had drifted to a stale "sung"-pattern (숭/sung/ㄙㄨㄫ), not matching 僧's own canonical character-level reading (승/sǝng/ㄙㄜㄫ, the regular outcome of its phonetic family 曽/増/憎/層/謄/騰/贈). [[僧]]'s own Notes had long flagged this exact cross-word inconsistency, also shared by [[僧侶]]/[[尼僧]]/[[僧伽]] — re-deriving the split fresh found no genuine conditioning pattern behind it (no semantic, positional, or register split explains why [[僧家]]/[[阿僧祇]] alone kept the regular form), so it was simply an uncorrected error across all four. Corrected `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` to 믿다승/middasǝng/ㄇㄧㄊㄉㄚㄙㄜㄫ — the Korean field (밀타승) was already correct throughout, since it always independently used 僧's own regular Sino-Korean 승. Japanese みつだそう and Vietnamese mật đà tăng are both attested as the standard terms for litharge in their respective languages. No HSK level applies to a term this specialized; no homophones (注音 ㄇㄧㄊㄉㄚㄙㄜㄫ unique across both words and characters).
