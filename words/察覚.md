## Notes
- [察](../characters/察.md) "inspect, observe" + [覚](../characters/覚.md) "feel, notice" — "observe and feel," to notice. Their own `stand_in` values: 察's is [[考察]]; 覚's is [[感覚]]; none points here, so 察覚 is an independent compound.

察覚 is to notice: to detect something, to become aware that it is there. Mandarin chájué (察覺) and Cantonese caat3 gok3 are the ordinary words. Japanese says 気付く and Korean 알아차리다, so those two fields hold the character readings. Compare [[感知]] (to perceive) and [[観察]] (to observe).
