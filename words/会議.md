## Notes
- [会 (char)](../characters/会%20(char).md) "meet, gather" + [議](../characters/議.md) "comment, discuss" — "meeting, conference." Directly attested and standard across all five languages (mandarin huìyì, japanese かいぎ, korean 회의, vietnamese hội nghị). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 훠읫 diverges from Sino-Korean 회의 via the AND-rule, since 会's own field independently diverges (despite 議 itself being `kwin: true`). Genuinely distinct at the Dan'a'yo level from its Korean-only near-homophone [[会意]] (注音 ㄏ⼔ㄜ, missing this word's final ㄧ) — the two share a Sino-Korean reading (회의) by coincidence, not a Dan'a'yo-level collision.
