## Notes
- [応](../characters/応%20(char).md) "respond, reply" — this word is the stand-in for its own character. For "ought" (in the sense of Chinese 應該), use [[該]] as an SFP instead.

応 means to respond or reply — 応 is built from [[Radical 053|广]] + [[Radical 061|心]], appears on the Korean Middle School list as 應, and is a Grade 5 character in Japan.

Mandarin yīng, Cantonese jing3, Japanese おう, Korean 응, and Vietnamese ứng are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄧㄫ/'ing/잉) with the unrelated [[鷹]] ("hawk").
