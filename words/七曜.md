## Notes
- [七 (char)](../characters/七%20(char).md) "seven" + [曜](../characters/曜.md) "day of the week" — no `#cranberry`: 曜's own `stand_in` is [[曜日]], 七's is 七 itself; neither points here.
- the seven luminaries (sun, moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter and Saturn)
- (literary) Alternative name for 北斗七星 (Běidǒu Qīxīng, "Big Dipper").
- (archaic) the seven days of the week

Mandarin qīyào, Cantonese cat1 jiu6, Japanese しちよう, Korean 칠요, and Sino-Vietnamese thất diệu (a real term in Vietnamese traditional astrology for the seven celestial luminaries) are all standard compositional readings. `kwin` is false (AND-rule: both 七 and 曜 individually false).
