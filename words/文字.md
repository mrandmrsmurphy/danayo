## Notes
- [文](../characters/文.md) "culture" + [字](../characters/字%20(char).md) "character" — no stand-in relationship applies (文's own stand-in is [[文化]]; 字's own is itself).

文字 means writing, text, script, or character.

Mandarin wénzì, Cantonese man4 zi6, Japanese もじ, Korean 문자, and Vietnamese văn tự are all standard, fully compositional readings. Simplified `korean` from comma-joined "글자, 문자" to compositional 문자, moving native 글자 to prose; fixed erroneous title-case in `vietnamese` (Văn Tự→văn tự, not a proper noun). No homophones (注音 ㄇㄨㄋㄐㄧ unique across both words and characters).
