## Notes
- [戦](../characters/戦.md) "war, battle" + [闘](../characters/闘.md) "struggle, fight, compete, contend"

Neither constituent character legitimizes the other here — `characters/戦.md`'s own `stand_in` is `戦争` and `characters/闘.md`'s own is `闘争`, so 戦闘 is an independent compound rather than a stand-in for either.

Mandarin zhàndòu, Cantonese zin3 dau3, Japanese せんとう (sentō), Korean 전투 (jeontu), and Vietnamese chiến đấu are all the standard, everyday word for "combat, to fight" across the sphere; no native displacement or homophone collision was found. The aliases 戰鬪 and 戰鬥 are legitimate variant spellings, matching the variant forms documented on `characters/闘.md` (鬪, 鬥, 鬭).
