## Notes
- [静](../characters/静.md) "quiet" + [寂](../characters/寂.md) "silent, quiet, still" — 静寂 is 静's own legitimizing compound (静's `stand_in` points here), but since 寂's own `stand_in` is [[寂滅]] instead, transitivity fails and no `#cranberry` tag applies.

静寂 (jìngjì, also read jìngjí) is a real Mandarin word for deep stillness/silence; Cantonese 静寂 (zing6zik6), Japanese せいじゃく (seijaku), and Korean 정적 (jeongjeok) are all standard, everyday words for the same sense in their languages. Sino-Vietnamese tĩnh tịch is likewise attested, most often in a Buddhist register describing the stillness of a settled mind — Chinese and Vietnamese Buddhist usage also freely reverses the order (寂静/tịch tĩnh, corresponding to the vault's own separately-attested [[寂静]]), with both orders carrying essentially the same meaning.
