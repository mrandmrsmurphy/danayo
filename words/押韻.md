## Notes
- [押](../characters/押%20(char).md) "press down" + [韻](../characters/韻.md) "rhyme" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [韻](../characters/韻.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (押's own stand-in is itself).

押韻 means to rhyme.

Mandarin yāyùn, Japanese おういん, Korean 압운, and Vietnamese áp vận are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `cantonese` had the wrong tone (wan5→wan6, matching 韻's own citation). No homophones (注音 ㄚㄆ·ㄨㄋ unique across both words and characters).
