## Notes
- [唐](../characters/唐.md) "Tang, China" + [突](../characters/突.md) "abruptly, suddenly" — no stand-in relationship applies (唐's own `stand_in` is `名専字`, since it's largely restricted to proper-name/Tang-dynasty contexts; 突's own is [[突然]]).

唐突 means blunt, abrupt, or presumptuous.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `mandarin` had the wrong tone mark on the second syllable ("tángtú" → tángtū, matching 突's own citation reading). Cantonese tong4 dat6, Japanese とうとつ, Korean 당돌, and Vietnamese đường đột are all standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄉ⺢ㄫㄊㄛㄊ unique across both words and characters).
