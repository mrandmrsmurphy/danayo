## Notes
- [督](../characters/督.md) "oversee" + [促 (char)](../characters/促%20(char).md) "hurry"
- This word is the viable form of [督](../characters/督.md): the character's meaning (to supervise) is identical, but 督 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 督促 is its `stand_in` compound.

督促 means to oversee and press someone to do what they should: 督 is "to supervise, to oversee" and 促 is "to urge, to hurry". Mandarin dūcù, Cantonese duk1 cuk1, Japanese とくそく (tokusoku; 督促状, "a reminder letter" for an overdue payment) and Korean 독촉 (dokchok; 독촉장) are the same compound, used where an authority or creditor pushes for something owed.

In Dan'a'yo it is the supervisory and official register of urging, above [[催]] (to press someone to hurry) and different from [[要求]]/[[要請]] (to make a request or demand).
