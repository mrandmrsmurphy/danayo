## Notes
- [聴](../characters/聴%20(char).md) "hear, listen" + [族](../characters/族.md) "tribe, clan" — "the Listening Tribe, Simeon." Their own `stand_in` values: 聴's is itself; 族's is [[家族]]; none points here, so 聴族 is an independent compound.

聴族 is the tribe of Simeon, Jacob's second son by Leah, whose name means "hearing": "Because the LORD has heard that I am hated" (Genesis 29:33). It is a Dan'a'yo **neologism**, a meaning-based name, "Listening Tribe." The character 聴 has the alias 聽, so the traditional spelling is accepted. Compare [[長族]] (Reuben) and [[附族]] (Levi). See [[基督敎]].

- Hebrew name: שִׁמְעוֹן (Shim'on) (注音 ㄙㄧㄇ·ㄛㄋ).

- See [基督敎](../lexipedia/基督敎.md).
