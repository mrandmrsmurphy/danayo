## Notes
- [精](../characters/精.md) "refined, pure" + [錬](../characters/錬.md) "refine, temper" — refining.

精錬 is the refining of metal, removing impurities from a crude product: the step after [[冶錬]] (smelting) that turns the smelter's output into pure metal, as [[銑鉄]] is refined into steel ([[鋼鉄]]). The Dan'a'yo headword is the Japanese 精錬; 精煉 and 精炼 are the Mandarin spellings. Mandarin jīngliàn, Japanese せいれん and Korean 정련 are the standard readings.
