## Notes
- [収](../characters/収.md) "receive" + [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" + [机](../characters/机.md) "machine" — "sound-receiving machine." Their own `stand_in` values: 収's is [[回収]]; 音's is [[音楽]]; 机's is [[机会]]; none points here, so 収音机 is an independent compound.

収音机 is a radio receiver. Mandarin shōuyīnjī and Cantonese sau1 jam1 gei1 are the ordinary Chinese words. Japanese says ラジオ and Korean 라디오, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. The act of broadcasting is [[放送]]. Compare [[電話]] (telephone) and [[電視]] (television).
