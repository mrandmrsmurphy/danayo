## Notes
- [企](../characters/企.md) "stand on tiptoe" (here, a phonetic/visual borrowing rather than its usual "plan, scheme" sense) + [鵝](../characters/鵝.md) "domestic goose" — "penguin," from the visual image of a goose-like bird standing upright. `korean: 펭귄` and `vietnamese: chim cánh cụt` ("short-winged bird") are each language's own real, natural everyday term for this animal, preferred over a forced compositional reading. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 커아 diverges from Sino-Korean via the AND-rule, since 企's own field independently diverges (despite 鵝 itself being `kwin: true`). No homophones (注音 ㄎㄝㄚ unique among both words and characters).
