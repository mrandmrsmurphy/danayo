## Notes
- [何 (char)](../characters/何%20(char).md) "what" + [故](../characters/故.md) "happening, reason" — "why, for what reason." `japanese: かこ` is honestly noted as a homograph risk — かこ also reads the common word 過去 ("the past") — though 何故 itself has no independent everyday Japanese reading distinct from なぜ (the real everyday equivalent).
