## Notes
- [何 (char)](../characters/何%20(char).md) "what" + [故](../characters/故.md) "happening, reason" — "why, for what reason." `mandarin: hégù` was already correctly compositional (何's own hé + 故's own gù), matching real classical/literary usage (as in the still-current 何故 in formal Mandarin). `japanese: かこ` is honestly noted as a homograph risk — かこ also reads the common word 過去 ("the past") — though 何故 itself has no independent everyday Japanese reading distinct from なぜ (the real everyday equivalent). `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 하고 now matches the corrected compositional Sino-Korean 하고 exactly (both 何 and 故 independently match their own Sino-Korean readings).

**Real content bug found and fixed**: `cantonese`/`japanese`/`korean`/`vietnamese` had all been filled with the everyday *semantic-equivalent* words/phrases (點解/dim2 gaai2, なぜ, 왜, sao) — different written words, not readings of 何故 itself — the same bug pattern found on [[何人]]/[[何名]]/[[何多]]. Corrected to the compound's own compositional forms: ho4 gu3/かこ/하고/hà cớ. This also corrected `kwin` from false to true, since the compositional Korean 하고 now exactly matches the word's own stored 諺文 하고.
