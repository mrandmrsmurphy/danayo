## Notes
- [漢](../characters/漢.md) "Han (Chinese)" + [族](../characters/族.md) "tribe, clan" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 漢 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (族's own stand-in is [[家族]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

漢族 means the Han Chinese ethnicity.

Mandarin hànzú, Cantonese hon3 zuk6, Japanese かんぞく, Korean 한족, and Vietnamese Hán tộc are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 漢's and 族's own stored citations exactly), and also the real, standard name for the Han ethnicity in each language. `kwin: true` because 諺文 한족 matches Korean 한족 exactly. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄋㄐㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
