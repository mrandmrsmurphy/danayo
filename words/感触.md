## Notes
- [感](../characters/感.md) "feel, sense" + [触](../characters/触.md) "touch, contact" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [感](../characters/感.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (触's own stand-in is [[接触]]).

感触 means to feel, sense, or emote — a tactile or emotional impression.

Mandarin gǎnchù, Cantonese gam2 zuk1, Japanese かんしょく, Korean 감촉, and Vietnamese cảm xúc are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄇㄑㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
