## Notes
- [妨](../characters/妨.md) "obstruct; hinder" + [碍](../characters/碍.md) "obstruct; hinder" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes both [妨](../characters/妨.md) and [碍](../characters/碍.md) as independent Dan'a'yo entries; neither character appears independently (`#cranberry`).

妨碍 means to hinder or obstruct — a near-synonymous doubling of two characters both meaning "to hinder."

Mandarin fáng'ài, Cantonese fong4 ngoi6, Japanese ぼうがい, Korean 방애, and Vietnamese phương ngại are all standard, fully compositional readings. Quoted `mandarin`/`cantonese`/`korean` for consistency. No homophones (注音 ㄈㄚㄫ·ㄚㄧ unique across both words and characters).
