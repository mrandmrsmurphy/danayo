## Notes
- [滋](../characters/滋.md) "thrive" + [生](../characters/生.md) "life" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 滋 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (生's own stand-in is [[生活]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

滋生 means to grow, multiply, thrive (breed/proliferate, as with bacteria or weeds).

Mandarin zīshēng and Cantonese zi1 sang1 are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 滋's and 生's own stored citations. Filled the blank `japanese`(じせい)/`korean`(자생, also the real Korean word for "growing wild, naturally occurring")/`vietnamese`(tư sinh) fields, all compositional. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄜㄙㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
