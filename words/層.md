## Notes
- [層 (char)](../characters/層%20(char).md) "stratum" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [層 (char)](../characters/層%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

層 names a layer or stratum — a stacked level, whether physical (a building's floor, 高層 "high-rise"), social (階層 "social class/stratum"), or abstract (深層 "deep layer, subconscious").

Japanese そう, Korean 층, and Vietnamese tầng (the most common of the character's several Vietnamese variants — the everyday word for a building's floor, tầng lầu) are all standard readings. No homophones among other words (注音 ㄑㄜㄫ unique).
