## Notes
- [甘 (char)](../characters/甘%20(char).md) "sweet" + [粛](../characters/粛.md) "solemn" — a blend of two prefectures, Ganzhou and Suzhou. 粛's own stand-in is [[厳粛]]; 甘 is its own stand-in. 肅 and 肃 are aliases of 粛, so the filename uses 粛; 甘肅 and 甘肃 are kept as the word's aliases.

甘粛 (Gansu) is a long, narrow province of northwest China whose capital is [[蘭州]] (from memory, not re-checked). English Wiktionary says the name is a blend of 甘州 and 肅州 (Ganzhou and Suzhou, the latter not the Jiangsu city 蘇州), coined in 1286 with the founding of a provincial government of that name. The traditional spelling is 甘肅; the Japanese form is the shinjitai 甘粛, which is also the Dan'a'yo form.

Japanese かんしゅく is the on'yomi reading (English Wiktionary lists it under 甘粛, with 甘肅 as the old-form spelling). The Korean field holds the Sino-Korean 감숙 (Wiktionary Korean: hanja form of 감숙); the usual South Korean name today is 간쑤 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Cam Túc is the Sino-Vietnamese name. Cantonese gam1 suk1 is the Jyutping on English Wiktionary.

Attestation, checked 2026-10-11: English and Chinese Wiktionary (Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese sections) list only the province for this name. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄇㄙㄨㄎ unique across both words and characters).
