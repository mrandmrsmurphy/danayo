## Notes
- [唯 (char)](../characters/唯%20(char).md) "only"

唯 means "only, solely" (唯一, "the only one, sole"; 唯有, "only, alone"). As an interjection (a separate, much rarer classical sense not covered by this word's own Dan'a'yo derivation), 唯 also serves as a formal particle of assent, "yes."

Mandarin wéi and Cantonese wai4 preserve the "only" sense directly. Japanese ユイ/イ (YUI/I, on'yomi, formal/philosophical register: 唯物論, yuibutsuron, "materialism"; 唯一, yuiitsu, "unique, sole") alongside native ただ (tada, "only, merely," the everyday word) cover the sense. Korean 유 (yu) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese duy is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in duy nhất ("the only one, sole"), duy trì ("to maintain, sustain"), and duy tâm ("idealism"); a secondary Hán Việt reading duỵ corresponds to the separate "assent" sense. The character page's many other stored candidates (dạ, dói, dúi, duối, gioè, giói, and others) are attested Nôm readings — 唯 reused to phonetically spell unrelated native Vietnamese words — not Hán Việt readings, and were excluded here.

This word shares its reading (yei/예/⼶ㄧ) with [[遺]] ("leave behind; bequeath"), still unperfected and awaiting its own turn in this sweep — the reciprocal half of this callout will be completed when it comes up.
