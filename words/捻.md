## Notes
- [捻](../characters/捻%20(char).md) "twist" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [捻](../characters/捻%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

捻 means to twist.

Mandarin niǎn, Cantonese nip6, Japanese ねん, Korean 념 (North Korean/문화어, retaining the initial ㄴ that South Korean 두음법칙 would shift to 염), and Vietnamese niệm are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: literal `vietnamese: null`, corrected using the character's own citation. Homophone of [[摂]] "vicarious" (both 注音 ㄋㄝㄆ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words.
