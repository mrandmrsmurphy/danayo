## Etymology
- [伯](../characters/伯.md) "uncle" reduplicated — father's older brother and the like.

## Notes
伯伯 (bóbo) is the everyday colloquial Mandarin reduplicated form for "uncle" (father's older brother, or an older man addressed respectfully), commonly used in direct address (张伯伯, "Uncle Zhang"). Since this specific reduplicated form isn't natural usage in Japanese, Korean, or Vietnamese, each of those fields instead documents that language's own real everyday word for the same relationship: `japanese: おじさん` (already present, native), `korean: 큰아버지` (native, "big father," specifically the father's older brother — corrected from a non-idiomatic compositional 백백), and `vietnamese: bác` (native, extremely common). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 박박 diverges entirely from the native-word-based Korean field. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄎㄅㄚㄎ unique among both words and characters).
