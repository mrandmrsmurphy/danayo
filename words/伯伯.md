## Notes
- [伯](../characters/伯.md) "uncle" reduplicated — father's older brother and the like.

伯伯 (bóbo) is the everyday colloquial Mandarin reduplicated form for "uncle" (father's older brother, or an older man addressed respectfully), commonly used in direct address (张伯伯, "Uncle Zhang").
