>[!caution] The senses split — Chinese, Japanese and Korean "witch", Vietnamese "female ghost"
>魔女 is a witch or sorceress in Chinese, Japanese and Korean, and the Dan'a'yo sense. Vietnamese ma nữ is glossed "female ghost", so a Vietnamese reader may hear a ghost. Listed as a Split on [文法 - 91疑義語](../grammar/文法%20-%2091疑義語.md).


## Notes
- [魔](../characters/魔.md) "demon" + [女 (char)](../characters/女%20(char).md) "woman"

魔女 is a witch, a sorceress. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 魔女 (Mandarin mónǚ, Cantonese mo1 neoi5) as "a witch; a sorceress", Japanese まじょ as "a witch, a sorceress" (from 1910), and also, as a rare, possibly obsolete Buddhist sense from 1283, "a woman living in the demonic realm; a demonic woman". Korean 마녀 is glossed "witch; sorceress", and Vietnamese ma nữ "female ghost". The word is listed as a Split on [文法 - 91疑義語](../grammar/文法%20-%2091疑義語.md). Compare [魔羅](魔羅.md).
