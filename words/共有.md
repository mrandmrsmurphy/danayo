## Notes
- [共 (char)](../characters/共%20(char).md) "together, joint" + [有 (char)](../characters/有%20(char).md) "to have, own" — "to share, co-own." `vietnamese: cộng hữu` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese (confirmed via web search), a real legal term for common/joint ownership (sở hữu chung), though modern Vietnamese law more often uses the native calque sở hữu chung in everyday legal writing. Removed a duplicate `品詞` key (redundant with `pos`). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 굥유 diverges entirely from Sino-Korean 공유. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼘ㄫ·⼜ unique among both words and characters).
