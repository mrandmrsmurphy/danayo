## Notes
- [共 (char)](../characters/共%20(char).md) "together, joint" + [有 (char)](../characters/有%20(char).md) "to have, own" — to share, co-own.

共有 means to share or jointly own: to hold property, rights, or something intangible in common with others (共有財産, "common property"; 共有地, "common land"; 共有する, "to share"; 情報共有, "information sharing"). It contrasts with sole ownership (專有, "exclusive possession"). In modern use it is also the everyday verb for sharing digital files and information.

Mandarin gòngyǒu, Cantonese gung6 jau5, Japanese きょうゆう, Korean 공유 and Vietnamese cộng hữu are all attested. Vietnamese cộng hữu is a real legal term for common or joint ownership, though modern Vietnamese law more often uses the native sở hữu chung.
