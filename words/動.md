## Notes
- [動 (char)](../characters/動%20(char).md) "to move"

動 is 形声: semantic [[力]] ("strength") + phonetic [[重]] — a reference to a heavy bag or load being moved by a person's effort; "to move." It heads an enormous compound family: [[活動]] ("activity"), [[運動]] ("exercise, movement, campaign"), [[動物]] ("animal"), [[動詞]] ("verb"), and grammatical terms like [[他動詞]]/[[自動詞]] ("transitive/intransitive verb").

Mandarin dòng, Cantonese dung6, and Japanese どう (dō, on'yomi, fully productive: 運動 うんどう "exercise") alongside native うご(く) (ugo(ku), "to move," the everyday verb) all preserve the sense. Korean 동 (dong) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 움직이다 (umjigida, "to move"). Vietnamese động is the Hán Việt reading, extremely well attested (vận động, "movement, campaign"; hoạt động, "activity"); the character page's other stored candidate, đụng ("to bump, collide"), is a related but distinct native word and was not used here.

This word is part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read dong/동/ㄉㄛㄫ): [[筒]] ("cylinder," still awaiting its own turn) and [[銅]] ("copper," already perfected — its own callout has been added alongside this iteration).
