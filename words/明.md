## Notes
- [明](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [明](../characters/明%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.
- [[Radical 072|日]] + [[月 (char)]] = [[SKIP-1-4-4]] ([[Stroke 08]])

明 means bright.

Mandarin míng, Cantonese ming4, Japanese めい, Korean 명, and Vietnamese minh are all standard, fully compositional readings. Simplified `vietnamese` from a four-way list to minh, the reading matching this word's actual sense; the other three stored variants (mừng, mênh, miêng) belong to unrelated homophone characters, the same kind of stored-field contamination documented on [[皿]]. Three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all myeng/명/ㄇ⼶ㄫ) with [[皿]] ("dish; container") and [[鳴]] ("cry; chirp") — already fully cross-referenced from 皿's side.
