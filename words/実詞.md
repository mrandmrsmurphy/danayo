## Notes
- [実](../characters/実.md) "true, accurate" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "word, expression"

実詞/实词 (content word, as opposed to 虚詞/虚词, function word) is a genuine, standard piece of East Asian grammatical terminology, exactly parallel to sibling pages like [[名詞]] and [[動詞]]:
1. **Mandarin** 实词 (shící) is the standard modern Chinese linguistics term for content word, contrasted with 虚词 (xūcí).
2. **Japanese** 実詞 (じっし) is the traditional term from kanbun (漢文) philology — the study of Classical Chinese texts in Japan — predating the modern Western-linguistics pair 内容語/機能語.
3. **Korean** 실사 (實詞/実詞) is the traditional Korean grammatical term, still cited alongside the native-morpheme equivalent 실질형태소.
4. **Vietnamese** thực từ (paired with hư từ) is an actively-taught term in the Vietnamese national curriculum.

Cantonese is the straightforward compositional reading, used the same way in Cantonese-medium grammar instruction.
