## Notes
- [陰 (char)](../characters/陰%20(char).md) "hidden; dark" + [謀 (char)](../characters/謀%20(char).md) "scheme"

陰謀 is a plot: a secret scheme, especially to do harm. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yīnmóu as "conspiracy; scheme; plot" and Japanese いんぼう as "plot; conspiracy". Vietnamese âm mưu is the same word, and Korean 음모 and Cantonese jam1 mau4 match.

The 陰 is what is done in the dark. It is a hidden plan against someone, where [権謀](権謀.md) is scheming as a skill of statecraft and a conspiracy to overthrow a ruler ends in a [反乱](反乱.md).
