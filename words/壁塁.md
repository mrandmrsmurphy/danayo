## Etymology
- [壁 (char)](../characters/壁%20(char).md) "wall" + [塁](../characters/塁.md) "rampart, base" — Japanese prefers the reversed order 塁壁 (るいへき), also reflected in this word's own `aliases`.

## Notes
- "Rampart, fortification wall." **Real content bug found and fixed on [[壁 (char)]]'s own page**: its `vietnamese` field crammed four readings into one comma-joined string rather than a proper list — split into separate entries. Neither Korean nor Vietnamese calques this exact compound: `korean: 보루` and `vietnamese: bảo lũy` both instead use 堡壘 ("fortress-rampart," built from 堡 "fort" rather than 壁/塁), the real standard term in both languages for a defensive rampart (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 벅뤼 diverges from Sino-Korean's 堡壘-based 보루 entirely — not a meaningful syllable comparison, since Korean lexicalizes a different compound. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄝㄎㄌㄨㄧ unique among both words and characters).
