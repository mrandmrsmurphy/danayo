## Notes
- [複合](../words/複合.md) "compound, composite" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language" (here in its bound "word, lexical item" sense, as in [[単語]]) — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes 複合語 as a multi-character host for 語, alongside its own already-established [[言語]].

Wiktionary: Japanese 複合語 (ふくごうご) "compound word"; Korean 복합어 is its hanja form, "compound word"; Chinese uses 複合詞, a linguistics term for "compound word (word composed of others)", with 詞 for 語, so the `mandarin` and `cantonese` fields hold the compositional reading. Used in `文法 - 01序文` and `文法 - 02音韻論` (複合語初字). See also [[複合]].
