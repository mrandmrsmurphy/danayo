## Notes
- [複合](../words/複合.md) "compound, composite" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language" (here in its bound "word, lexical item" sense, as in [[単語]]) — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes 複合語 as a multi-character host for 語, alongside its own already-established [[言語]].

複合語 means a compound word — a word formed from two or more existing words or roots (複合語初字, "the first character/morpheme of a compound word," already used on [[文法 - 02音韻論]]).

Japanese ふくごうご and Korean 복합어 are both the real, standard linguistics terms. **Genuine Mandarin/Cantonese heteronym**: the real, standard Mandarin linguistics term is actually 复合词/fùhécí (and Cantonese 複合詞/fuk1 hap6 ci4), substituting 詞 ("word, term") for 語 — not 复合语. `mandarin`/`cantonese` above hold the compositional Dan'a'yo-internal reading (fùhéyǔ/fuk1 hap6 jyu5) rather than this real divergent form, matching this vault's standard practice for this kind of split. `vietnamese: phức hợp ngữ` is the compositional reading (phức hợp + 語's own ngữ); the real, standard Vietnamese linguistics terms are từ ghép or từ phức instead — not independently verified here, flagged rather than silently assumed. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 북곱요 diverges from Sino-Korean 복합어.

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot**: the 合|語 boundary is a genuine resegmentation fork — `ㄍㄛㄆ`(合)+`⼄`(語) and the alternate `ㄍㄛ`(a real assigned syllable)+`ㄆ⼄`(also real) are both fully legal syllable sequences — so the dot is required: ㄅㄨㄎㄍㄛㄆ·⼄.
