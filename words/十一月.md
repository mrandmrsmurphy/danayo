## Notes
- [十 (char)](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" + [一 (char)](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — literally "ten-one month" (compare [十一](十一.md) "eleven," the same two characters).

十一月 is the standard Gregorian-calendar name for November, formed the same transparent "number + 月" way as every other month in this vault's calendar vocabulary (compare [十月](十月.md) "October," [十二月](十二月.md) "December").

Mandarin shíyīyuè, Cantonese sap6 jat1 jyut6, and Korean 십일월 (sibirwol) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese じゅういちがつ (jūichigatsu) is a fully Sino-Japanese compound (十 jū + 一 ichi + 月 gatsu). Vietnamese tháng mười một is a native construction ("month ten-one"), not a Sino-Vietnamese compound — the everyday way all Vietnamese month names are formed.
