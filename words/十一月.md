## Notes
- [十](../characters/十.md) "ten" + [一 (char)](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — literally "ten-one month" (compare [[十一]] "eleven," the same two characters).

十一月 is the standard Gregorian-calendar name for November, formed the same transparent "number + 月" way as every other month in this vault's calendar vocabulary (compare [[十月]] "October," [[十二月]] "December"). **Real bug found and fixed 2026-09-22**: this word's own `注音` (and the matching citations on both 十's and 月's own character pages, plus the Calendar.md table) stored a corrupted final syllable, ㄔ instead of ⼔ㄊ for 月 — corrected to ㄙㄧㄆ·ㄧㄊ·⼔ㄊ, the correct concatenation of 十's ㄙㄧㄆ + 一's ㄧㄊ + 月's own ⼔ㄊ.

Mandarin shíyīyuè, Cantonese sap6 jat1 jyut6, and Korean 십일월 (sibirwol) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese じゅういちがつ (jūichigatsu) mixes Sino-Japanese じゅう ("ten") with native いち ("one"), the same irregular mixed-reading pattern seen throughout Japanese month names. Vietnamese tháng mười một is a native construction ("month ten-one"), not a Sino-Vietnamese compound — the everyday way all Vietnamese month names are formed. **Real `kwin` bug found and fixed**: was stored as `true`, but Dan'a'yo 십읻웓 does not exactly string-match Sino-Korean 십일월 — the middle syllable diverges (Dan'a'yo keeps the MC-checked-tone coda ㄷ, 읻, while Sino-Korean regularizes 一's final to ㄹ, 일), the exact same divergence already documented and corrected on [[十一]] itself. Corrected to `kwin: false`.

No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄆ·ㄧㄊ·⼔ㄊ unique among both words and characters).
