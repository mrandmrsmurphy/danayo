## Notes
- [盟](../characters/盟.md) "alliance, covenant" + [誓](../characters/誓.md) "swear, vow" — 誓's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 盟's own is [[連盟]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 誓 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

盟誓 means to swear an oath or vow, evoking the ancient covenant-swearing ceremonies where parties sealed an alliance with a blood oath (血誓盟約, "a covenant in blood") — 盟's own radical, 皿 ("vessel"), alludes to the ceremonial vessel used in these rites.

Mandarin méngshì, Cantonese mang4 sai6, and Japanese めいせい (meisei) are all straightforwardly compositional. Korean is worth a note: the modern standard word is 맹세 (maengse), not the mechanically-compositional 맹서 — a historical vowel shift (서→세) lexicalized specifically in this word, distinct from either character's own citation. Vietnamese minh thệ is compositional and carries the same formal, covenant-swearing register.
