## Notes
- [従 (char)](../characters/従%20(char).md) "obey, follow, collateral" + [妹](../characters/妹.md) "younger sister" — the direct pair to [[従姉]] above, and the female counterpart of [[従弟]].

従妹 is "younger female cousin," generalized in living Japanese usage past its classical restriction to a father's brother's daughter, matching the treatment already established across this whole word cluster. Mandarin cóngmèi and Cantonese cung4 mui6 are real classical/literary compounds, superseded by 堂妹/表妹 in everyday speech; Japanese じゅうまい (jūmai) is real but usually replaced by plain いとこ in casual speech. Korean 종매 is a real but secondary term next to 사촌동생; Vietnamese tòng muội is a real, attested Sino-Vietnamese term (em họ, specifically a female cousin) alongside more common đường muội (堂妹).
