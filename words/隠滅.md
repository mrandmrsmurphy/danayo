## Notes
- [隠](../characters/隠.md) "hide" + [滅 (char)](../characters/滅%20(char).md) "wipe out" — to destroy or vanish without trace; the common legal collocation 証拠隠滅/증거 인멸 ("destruction of evidence"). Attested across Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, and Korean.

The blank `vietnamese` field now holds the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading of the two characters, ẩn diệt (隠 ẩn, 滅 diệt), following the vault's convention for these fields. The Korean 인멸 differs from the Dan'a'yo 諺文 은먿 in both syllables, so `kwin` is false. The Existence page lists the word as "to destroy (evidence), to vanish, fade away — carries a specifically legal 'destroy the evidence' connotation alongside its general sense," and its note on [滅](滅.md) calls 滅 "the bound root behind 隠滅's own 'destroy evidence' sense." Dan'a'yo keeps both senses together as one word. It borders the Law domain, as that page's See Also notes, and the legal collocation takes [証拠](証拠.md) "evidence" as its object. It is related to the concealment words [隠匿](隠匿.md) and [隠蔽](隠蔽.md), which hide something without destroying it. The readings are Mandarin yǐnmiè, Cantonese jan2 mit6 and Japanese inmetsu (いんめつ). The contrast with 隠匿 and 隠蔽 is from memory and has not been checked against a source.
