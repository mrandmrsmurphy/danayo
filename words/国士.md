## Notes
- [国](../characters/国.md) "nation" + [士](../characters/士%20(char).md) "gentleman, scholar" — no stand-in relationship applies (国's own stand-in is [[国家]]; 士 is its own stand-in).

国士 means a gentleman or patriot — a person of outstanding talent or service to the nation.

Japanese こくし, Korean 국사, and Vietnamese quốc sĩ are all standard, fully compositional readings.
