## Notes
- [当](../characters/当%20(char).md) "while" + [世](../characters/世.md) "world, society" — no stand-in relationship applies (当's own stand-in is itself; 世's own is [[世界]]).

当世 means this era, this generation, or this world — the present age.

Mandarin dāngshì, Cantonese dong1 sai3, Japanese とうせい, Korean 당세, and Vietnamese đương thế are all standard, fully compositional readings.
