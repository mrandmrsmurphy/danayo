## Notes
- [飽 (char)](../characters/飽%20(char).md) "sated, full" — this word is 飽's own legitimizing compound (飽's `stand_in` points here).

飽 means "full, sated, satisfied" (after eating). Mandarin bǎo, Cantonese baau2, Korean 포 (po), and Sino-Vietnamese bão all preserve the Sino reading directly. Japanese ほう (hō) survives mainly bound in compounds (飽和, "saturation"); the everyday native adjective is おなかがいっぱい.

**Fixed a real bug**: `vietnamese` was the literal string `null` — replaced with `bão`, the character's own stored reading.
