## Notes
- [算 (char)](../characters/算%20(char).md) "calculate" + [術 (char)](../characters/術%20(char).md) "skill, method, technique" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both 算's and 術's own are themselves), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 算 is individually `kwin: false`.

**Fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字` had dasud, missing 算's own reading entirely — corrected to swansud (算's own swan + 術's own sud; `諺文`/`注音` were already correct).

算術 names arithmetic — the basic mathematical skill of calculation (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division), distinct from more advanced 数学 ("mathematics" broadly).

Mandarin suànshù, Cantonese syun3 seot6, Japanese さんじゅつ (sanjutsu), and Korean 산술 (sansul) are all standard, fully compositional readings, each the ordinary everyday word for "arithmetic" in its language. Vietnamese toán thuật is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form.
