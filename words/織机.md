## Notes
- [織](../characters/織.md) "weave" + [机](../characters/机.md) "machine"

織机 is a loom. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 織機 (alias here; Mandarin zhījī, Cantonese zik1 gei1) as the weaving term "loom", and Japanese 織機 (しょっき) as "loom". The Korean 직기 and Vietnamese chuốc cơ fields are built from the readings of the two characters. The character [机](../characters/机.md), whose alias is 機, is bound, and its stand-in is 机会. To weave is [編織](編織.md).
