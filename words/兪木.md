## Notes
- [兪](../characters/兪%20(char).md) "agree to; (here) elm" + [木](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree, wood" — "elm tree." Their own `stand_in` values: 兪's is itself; 木's is itself; none points here, so 兪木 is an independent compound.

兪木 is the elm tree. Chinese 榆 (Mandarin yú, Cantonese jyu4, Japanese 楡) names the elm and is already an alias of [[兪]] in this vault, so the Dan'a'yo form is 兪 plus 木 "tree," on the vault's convention that trees are named **X木**. The Mandarin and Cantonese words for the tree itself are 榆樹; Japanese says 楡 (にれ). Compare [[楢木]] (oak), [[樹木]] (tree) and [[柿木]] (persimmon).
