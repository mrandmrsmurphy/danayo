## Notes
- [連 (char)](characters/連%20(char).md) "consecutive, repeated" + [年 (char)](characters/年%20(char).md) "year" — "year after year."

Mandarin liánnián, cantonese lin4 nin4, japanese れんねん, and korean 연년 are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. Vietnamese liên niên is the honest compositional reading (連's own liên + 年's own niên); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 련넌 diverges from Sino-Korean 연년 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄌ⼶ㄋㄋㄝㄋ unique among both words and characters).
