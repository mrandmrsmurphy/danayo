## Notes
- [桑](../characters/桑.md) "mulberry" + [木](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree, bush" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 桑 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

桑木 means mulberry tree.

Mandarin sāngmù and Cantonese song1 muk6 are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 桑's and 木's own citations. Fixed real bugs: `mandarin`/`cantonese` had both been copied from the unrelated compound 桑樹/桑树's own readings (sāngshù, sang1 su4) rather than 桑木's own; `korean` was 뽕나무, 桑's own native Korean word (from its `korean_native` field), not the compositional Sino-Korean form — corrected to 상목. Japanese くわ is the genuine, standard native Japanese word for the mulberry tree, kept as-is. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field (tang mộc). Removed `桑樹`/`桑树` from `aliases` — a different, unrelated compound built on the character 樹/树 ("tree"), a near-synonym rather than an orthographic variant of 桑木. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄚㄫㄇㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
