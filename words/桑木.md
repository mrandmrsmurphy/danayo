## Notes
- [桑](../characters/桑.md) "mulberry" + [木](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree, bush" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 桑 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

桑木 means mulberry tree.

Mandarin sāngmù and Cantonese song1 muk6 are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 桑's and 木's own citations. Japanese くわ is the genuine, standard native Japanese word for the mulberry tree, kept as-is.
