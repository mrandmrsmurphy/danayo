## Notes
- [鸚](../characters/鸚.md) "parrot" + [鵡](../characters/鵡.md) "parrot" — **`#cranberry`**: both 鸚's and 鵡's own `stand_in` point to [[鸚鵡]] itself, and both characters already independently mean "parrot" on their own — genuine transitivity (A=B=AB), matching its listed entry on [[lookup/List of 連綿詞]].

Mandarin yīngwǔ, Cantonese jing1mou5, Japanese おうむ, and Korean 앵무 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese vẹt is the real, everyday native word (rather than the compositional Sino form anh vũ, which would exist but is far less commonly used).
