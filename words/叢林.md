## Notes
- [叢](../characters/叢.md) "thicket; clump; collection" + [林 (char)](../characters/林%20(char).md) "grove"

叢林 is "jungle; bush," a dense growth of vegetation. According to Wiktionary, Chinese 叢林 (cónglín, Cantonese cung4 lam4) is a noun "jungle; bush" and, archaic and Buddhist, "Zen Buddhist monastery"; Wiktionary's Japanese entry gives そうりん only for the Zen monastery sense. Dan'a'yo keeps the "jungle; thicket" sense and not the Zen sense. Wiktionary has no Korean or Vietnamese section for it (its descendants list does show 총림 and tùng lâm, but for the Zen sense), so the Korean and Vietnamese fields here are simply the compositional readings of 叢 and 林, not verified as an attested word in the "jungle" sense.

It is a more tangled, wilder counterpart to [森林](森林.md) "forest; jungle; underbrush," and is used in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [China](../lookup/HSK/China.md) (sentence 12, 為 水泥叢林 ...), where it appears in [水泥叢林](../chengyu/水泥叢林.md) "concrete jungle."
