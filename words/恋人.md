## Notes
- [恋](../characters/恋%20(char).md) "romantic love" + [人](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" — "love people," the lovers. Their own `stand_in` values: 恋's is itself; 人's is itself; none points here, so 恋人 is an independent compound.

恋人 is "the lovers": the two people who love each other, a couple in love. Mandarin liànrén (戀人), Cantonese lyun2 jan4, Japanese れんじん (こいびと is the native word) and Korean 련인 (연인) are attested. By decision it names the pair together, where [[愛人]] is a lover or partner and [[情人]] a sweetheart.
