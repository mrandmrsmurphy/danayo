## Notes
- [瞻](../characters/瞻.md), standing in for its phonetically-close relative [[譫]] "delirious talk" (with the alias reading ㄙㄝㄇ, distinct from 瞻's own regular ㄐㄝㄇ) + [妄](../characters/妄.md) "delusion, vain" — 譫妄 is the correct underlying form, aliased here.

瞻妄/譫妄 is the standard medical term for delirium — an acute, fluctuating disturbance of attention and awareness, often accompanied by confused or incoherent speech (the "delirious talk" sense of 譫 itself). Mandarin zhānwàng, Cantonese zim1 mong5, Japanese せんもう (senmō), and Korean 섬망 (seommang) are all standard clinical terms in their respective languages. `kwin` is true here: the alias reading ㄙㄝㄇ was deliberately chosen so that Dan'a'yo's 섬망 would exactly match Korean's real 섬망, unlike most borrowed-glyph substitutions in this vault where the Dan'a'yo and Sino-Korean readings diverge. Vietnamese thiêm vọng is compositional, using 譫's own Sino-Vietnamese reading rather than 瞻's.
