## Notes
- [超 (char)](../characters/超%20(char).md) "exceed" + [過 (char)](../characters/過%20(char).md) "pass; exceed" — excess.

超過 is excess or to exceed: to go beyond a limit, quantity or standard. It is "surpass-pass." Mandarin chāoguò and Cantonese ciu1 gwo3 are a verb meaning "to overtake, exceed, pass"; Japanese ちょうか and Korean 초과 are the noun "excess" and, in Japanese, the する-verb "to exceed." It is the first half of [[超過勤務]] (overtime, "exceeding duty"). Compare [[過]] (than, too) and [[超]] (transcend, surpass).
