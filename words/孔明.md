## Notes
- [孔](../characters/孔%20(char).md) "hole" + [明](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright" — no stand-in relationship applies (孔/明 are both their own stand-ins).

Mandarin kǒngmíng, Cantonese hung2 ming4, Japanese こうめい, Korean 공명, and Vietnamese Khổng Minh are all standard, fully compositional readings.

1. The courtesy name (字) of Zhuge Liang, the famed Shu Han strategist of the Three Kingdoms period.
2. Bright, clean — a literal compositional reading of the two characters.
