## Notes
- [精](../characters/精.md) "spirit, essence, sperm" + [液](../characters/液.md) "fluid, sap" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (精's is [[精神]], 液's is [[液体]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 液 is individually `kwin: false`.

精液 names semen — the fluid containing sperm, combining 精's "essence, sperm" sense with 液's "fluid" sense directly.

Mandarin has two attested readings, jīngyè and jīngyì (both cited across dictionaries). Cantonese zing1 jik6, Japanese せいえき (seieki), and Korean 정액 (jeongaek) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese tinh dịch is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form and is itself the standard medical Vietnamese term.
