## Notes
- [新](../characters/新%20(char).md) "new" + [義](../characters/義.md) "meaning, principle" + [州](../characters/州%20(char).md) "state" — the name of Sinuiju, a city of North Korea on the border river.

新義州 (Sinuiju) is a city of North Korea on the Yalu River (鴨緑江), facing the Chinese city of Dandong (丹東) across the water (from memory, not re-checked). English Wiktionary lists it as the capital of North Pyongan Province ([[平安]]北道) and also as a special administrative region. The name reads "new Uiju", after the older border town 義州 (Uiju) upstream (from memory, not re-checked).

English and Chinese Wiktionary have 新義州 as the city (Mandarin Xīnyìzhōu, Cantonese san1 ji6 zau1). English Wiktionary also has a Korean entry as a hanja form of 신의주 and a Vietnamese entry as a Han form of Tân Nghĩa Châu; its Japanese section gives シニジュ (from the Korean) and a dated reading しんぎしゅう, which is taken here as the Sino-Xenic reading.
