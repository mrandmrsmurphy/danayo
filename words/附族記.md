## Notes
- [附](../characters/附%20(char).md) "attach, join, draw near" + [族](../characters/族.md) "tribe, clan" + [記](../characters/記.md) "record, account" — "the Coming-Near Tribe Story," the record of the tribe attached to the sanctuary. Their own `stand_in` values: 附's is itself; 族's is [[家族]]; 記's is [[記憶]]; none points here, so 附族記 is an independent compound.

附族記 is Leviticus, the third book of the Torah: the record of the laws of sacrifice, priesthood and holiness given to the Levites, the tribe attached to the service of the sanctuary. It is the third book of [[bible/聖経 (book)]], after [[創世記]] and [[出谷記]]. It is a Dan'a'yo **neologism**, a meaning-based name on the vault's translation principles: the English "Leviticus" and Chinese 利未記 only transcribe the name Levi, whose sense is "attached, joined," so the book is named for that meaning, 附 "attach, join, draw near" plus 族 "tribe" plus 記 "record" ("the Coming-Near Tribe Story"; the tribe itself is 附族, "Attached Tribe," Levi). Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and Korean readings in the fields are mechanical, built from the three characters, and are not claims of use; the real names are Leviticus, 利未記, レビ記 and 레위기. See [[基督敎]].
