## Etymology
- [班 (char)](../characters/班%20(char).md) "group, party, section" + [長 (char)](../characters/長%20(char).md) "long" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 班's and 長's own stand-ins are themselves). 長 is polyphonic ("long" cháng/coeng4 vs "chief, elder" zhǎng/zoeng2); this word uses the "chief" sense throughout.

班長 means squad leader.

Mandarin bānzhǎng, Cantonese baan1 zoeng2 (correctly using 長's "chief"-sense reading), and Korean 반장 were already correct. Fixed a real typo in `japanese`: はんちゃう → はんちょう (HAN+CHOU), the correct compositional on'yomi reading. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with ban trưởng (班's own ban + 長's own "chief"-sense reading trưởng). Fixed `hsk_level` formatting (unquoted 2 → quoted "2"). No homophones (注音 ㄆㄚㄋㄐㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
