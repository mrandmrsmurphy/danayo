## Notes
- [方](../characters/方.md) "square" + [頭 (char)](../characters/頭%20(char).md) "head; end" + [捺 (char)](../characters/捺%20(char).md) "right-falling stroke"

Wiktionary has no entry for 方頭捺; it is a compound of 方 "square", 頭 "head; end" and [[捺]], which Wiktionary glosses in Chinese calligraphy as "a stroke that falls downwards towards the right (㇏)". It names the square-headed right-falling stroke, the first of the two named varieties of 捺 on the [[Stroke]] page, beside the [[圓頭捺]]. Its readings are compositional.
