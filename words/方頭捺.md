## Notes
- [方](../characters/方.md) "square" + [頭 (char)](../characters/頭%20(char).md) "head; end" + [捺 (char)](../characters/捺%20(char).md) "right-falling stroke"

**方頭捺** is the *square-headed* right-falling stroke, a long 捺 ([[捺]]) whose foot ends in a square, angular finish. Chinese instruction distinguishes long press strokes by their ends, square, round and pointed: the square end is described as steady and able to bear weight, in contrast to the open, free pointed end. The name is attested in Chinese regular-script teaching, for example in descriptions of the strokes of Ouyang Xun's 九成宮醴泉銘 (*Jiuchenggong liquan ming*).

It is the first of the two named varieties of 捺 on the [[Stroke]] page, beside the [[圓頭捺]].

The Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and North Korean forms are compositional, built from the constituent words and characters. No Vietnamese form is given.
