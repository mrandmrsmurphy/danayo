## Notes
- [群](../characters/群.md) "flock; crowd" + [集](../characters/集.md) "assemble"

群集 is to gather in a mass: to congregate, crowd, flock together. Chinese uses it as a verb (qúnjí), and Japanese as a noun "crowd" and a suru verb (gunshū). In Dan'a'yo it is the verb for animals gathering in numbers, such as insects swarming or birds flocking.
