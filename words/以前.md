## Notes
- [以 (char)](../characters/以%20(char).md) "by means of" (here, a temporal boundary marker) + [前 (char)](../characters/前%20(char).md) "before, front" — "before, previously, heretofore." `vietnamese: dĩ tiền` is directly attested compositional Sino-Vietnamese, matching the same 以-family pattern as 以後 (dĩ hậu, "afterward") and 以上 (dĩ thượng, "the above").
