## Notes
"Nunchucks" (双節棍/雙節棍, literally "double-jointed stick"). `korean: 쌍절곤` is compositional and matches; Japanese instead borrows the Okinawan word directly, ぬんちゃく (already present). `vietnamese: côn nhị khúc` is the real, standard Vietnamese martial-arts term for this weapon (literally "two-section staff"), a different literal structure from this word's own characters but the genuine everyday name, not a forced compositional calque (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted). Filled the blank `pos: 名詞`. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 솽젇혼 diverges entirely from Sino-Korean 쌍절곤. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⺢ㄫㄐㄝㄊㄏㄛㄋ unique among both words and characters).
