## Notes
- [宇宙](宇宙.md) "universe" + [船](../characters/船.md) "boat; ship" — no stand-in relationship applies (none of 宇/宙/船's own `stand_in` fields point here).

宇宙船 is the Traditional-Chinese/Taiwan and Japanese form of "spaceship" — Mainland Simplified Chinese instead defaults to the four-character 宇宙飛船/宇宙飞船 (yǔzhòu fēichuán, listed here as an alias), the same concept with 飛/飞 "to fly" inserted; both forms are current and mutually intelligible, a regional/orthographic split rather than a meaning difference. This vault's choice of the shorter 3-character form matches its general preference for Japanese/Traditional forms elsewhere.

Japanese うちゅうせん (Wiktionary-verified) uses this same 3-character form directly. Korean 우주선 and Vietnamese tàu vũ trụ (the latter combining the native classifier *tàu* "vehicle, ship" with the Sino-Vietnamese [[宇宙]] itself, rather than a fully Sino-Vietnamese compound) are both standard modern terms. **Known open reading question, not resolved here**: 船's own contribution to this word's `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` (줜/jwen/ㄐ⼔ㄋ) does not match 船's canonical character-level reading (쉄/swem/ㄙ⼔ㄇ) — [[船]]'s own Notes now flag this exact cross-word inconsistency, also shared by [[艦船]], calling for a dedicated word-sweep pass rather than a one-page fix. No HSK level, no homophones.
