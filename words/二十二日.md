## Notes
- [[二十]] "twenty" + [二 (char)](../characters/二%20(char).md) "two" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — the twenty-second day of the month; 二 appears twice (once in 二十, once as the ones-digit), so `characters:` lists it only once rather than duplicating the entry.

Mandarin èrshí'èr rì, Cantonese ji6 sap6 ji6 jat6, Japanese にじゅうににち, and Korean 이십이일 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese filled with ngày hai mươi hai (native "day twenty-two"), matching the same native-calendar-date convention already established on [[二十一日]]/[[二十七日]]/[[二十三日]]/[[二十九日]]/[[二十]] itself.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 늬십늬닏 diverges from Sino-Korean 이십이일, since all constituent characters independently carry `kwin: false` on their own character pages.
