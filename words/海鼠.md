## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [鼠](../characters/鼠.md) "rat" — no stand-in relationship applies (海's own stand-in is [[海洋]]; 鼠's own is [[熊鼠]]).

海鼠 means sea cucumber (literally "sea rat" — the same characters used to write Japanese なまこ, echiing the more common alternate term 海參, listed here as an alias).

Filled the blank `mandarin`/`cantonese` fields, compositional: hǎishǔ (海's own hǎi + 鼠's own shǔ) and hoi2 syu2. Japanese なまこ is the real, standard native word for "sea cucumber," matching this exact spelling. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with the compositional hải thử (海's own hải + 鼠's own thử); real-world Vietnamese instead uses hải sâm, built on the alternate term 海參.
