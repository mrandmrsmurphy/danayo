## Notes
- [骨 (char)](../characters/骨%20(char).md) "bone" + [格 (char)](../characters/格%20(char).md) "framework, standard" — no `#cranberry`: both 骨's and 格's own `stand_in` point to themselves; neither points here.

Mandarin gǔgé, Cantonese gwat1gaak3, Japanese こっかく, and Korean 골격 are all standard, fully compositional readings, denoting the literal or figurative skeletal framework. Sino-Vietnamese cốt cách is the compositional form, but has drifted to a figurative sense in everyday Vietnamese — "bearing, character, innate quality" — rather than the literal skeleton (for which native khung xương or bộ xương is used).
