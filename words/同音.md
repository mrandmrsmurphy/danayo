## Notes
- [同](../characters/同.md) "same" + [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" — no stand-in relationship applies (同's own stand-in is [同一](同一.md); 音's is [音楽](音楽.md)).

同音 is the same sound, a homophone, homophonous. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 同音 (Mandarin tóngyīn, Cantonese tung4 jam1) as "homophonous" and, literary and of music, "in harmony". It glosses Japanese どうおん as "a homophone (same or identical pronunciation)", from 772, and as further senses "unison", a type of chorus in a Noh play, and, obsolete, the same reading of a character. Vietnamese đồng âm is glossed "homophonous". The Korean 동음 field is built from the readings of the two characters. In Dan'a'yo the sense is homophony; see the `Homophones` callouts on word pages.
