## Notes
- [桜](characters/桜%20(char).md) "cherry blossom" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 桜 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

桜 means cherry blossom.

Mandarin yīng, Cantonese jing1, Japanese さくら, Korean 앵, and Vietnamese anh are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 桜's own stored citation exactly; さくら is the native everyday word, the standard real-world reading for this concept). Fixed the malformed `羅馬字` (ang → 'ang, restoring the leading apostrophe matching 桜's own citation and 諺文's null-initial ㅇ), added the missing `japanese`/`hsk_level` fields. Discovered a genuine homophone with [[硬]] (both 注音 ㄚㄫ, 諺文 앙) — cross-referenced both pages.
