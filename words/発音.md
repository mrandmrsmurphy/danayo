## Notes
- [発 (char)](../characters/発%20(char).md) "issue forth, discharge" + [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" — literally "to issue forth sound." 発's own stand-in is [[発]] itself and 音's own is [[音楽]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; neither constituent is legitimized by this word.

発音 means to pronounce, or pronunciation itself — a basic, everyday word in every one of these languages' vocabulary for language-learning and phonetics. Mandarin fāyīn, Cantonese faat3 jam1, Japanese はつおん (hatsuon), Korean 발음 (bareum), and Vietnamese phát âm are all fully compositional and semantically identical, with no drift or narrowing anywhere in the sphere — one of the cleanest, most stable words built on the 発- prefix.
