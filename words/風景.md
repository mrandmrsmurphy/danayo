## Notes
- [風 (char)](../characters/風%20(char).md) "wind" + [景](../characters/景.md) "scenery, scene" — "the wind and the view," the look of a place. No stand-in relationship applies (風's own stand-in is itself; 景's is [[景色]]).

風景 is scenery: the landscape or view that lies before the eye, a natural scene or a stretch of countryside taken in as a whole. It is the general word beside its near-synonyms [[景色]] and [[景致]], which also mean scenery or a view; Wiktionary lists 景色 and 風光 as its Chinese synonyms. Chinese 風景 also has an obsolete sense, "circumstances", which Dan'a'yo does not use.

Mandarin fēngjǐng, Cantonese fung1 ging2, Japanese ふうけい and Korean 풍경 are listed on Wiktionary with this meaning. Vietnamese writes the same characters as phong cảnh, the standard word for scenery, so the `vietnamese` field holds that real term.
