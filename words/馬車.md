## Notes
- [馬](../characters/馬%20(char).md) "horse" + [車](../characters/車%20(char).md) "cart" — "horse cart." Their own `stand_in` values: 馬's is itself; 車's is itself; none points here, so 馬車 is an independent compound.

馬車 is a horse-drawn wagon or carriage. Mandarin mǎchē, Cantonese maa5 ce1, Japanese ばしゃ and Korean 마차 all use it. Compare [[車]] (car) and [[貨車]] (truck).
