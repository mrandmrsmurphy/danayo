## Notes
- [引 (char)](../characters/引%20(char).md) "pull; tug" + [入 (char)](../characters/入%20(char).md) "enter" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 引's and 入's own `stand_in` fields point to themselves).

引入 means to lead into or introduce — bringing something (an idea, a technology, a person) into a new context or system (技術を引入する, "to introduce/adopt a technology").

Japanese いんにゅう, Korean 인입, and Vietnamese dẫn nhập (verified — also a standalone Vietnamese literary term for an introductory section of a text) are all standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄧㄋㄋㄧㄆ unique across both words and characters).
