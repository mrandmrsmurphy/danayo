## Notes
- [食 (char)](../characters/食%20(char).md) "eat" + [費 (char)](../characters/費%20(char).md) "expense" — no `#cranberry`: both 食's and 費's own `stand_in` are themselves; neither points here.

食費 (shífèi) is a real Mandarin word for "food expenses," and Cantonese 食費 (sik6fai3) likewise, though the fuller 伙食费 (huǒshífèi, given here as an alias) is also very common. Japanese しょくひ (shokuhi) and Korean 식비 (sikbi) are both standard everyday terms, though everyday Korean also uses the native 밥값 ("rice money") for the same sense. Sino-Vietnamese thực phí is likewise attested for "food expenses."

**Fixed a real bug**: `mandarin` had `huǒ shí fèi`, the reading of the longer alias 伙食费 (with an extra 伙) instead of this word's own two-character reading — corrected to `shífèi`. Fixed comma-joined native-synonym contamination in `korean` (식비, 밥값 → 식비 alone). Added entirely missing `kwin: false`.
