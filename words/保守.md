## Notes
- [保](../characters/保.md) "protect" + [守](../characters/守.md) "guard"

保守 is conservative, conservatism. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 保守 (Mandarin bǎoshǒu, Cantonese bou2 sau2) as "to keep; to guard" and "conservative". It lists Japanese ほしゅ, Korean 보수 and Vietnamese entries. Its opposite pole on the political spectrum is [急進](急進.md), radical.
