## Notes
- [停](../characters/停.md) "halt, stop" + [滞](../characters/滞.md) "stagnate" — "to stagnate" (交通停滞, "traffic congestion"; 経済停滞, "economic stagnation"). Mandarin tíngzhì, Cantonese ting4 zai6, Japanese ていたい, Korean 정체, and Vietnamese đình trệ are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 덩저 diverges from Sino-Korean 정체 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄝㄫㄐㄝ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 滞's own `stand_in` is this exact word (停滞), so 滞 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 停滞.
