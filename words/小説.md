## Notes
- [小 (char)](../characters/小%20(char).md) "small" + [説](../characters/説.md) "speech; theory"

小説 is a novel, or fiction in general: a long prose story invented by the author. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xiǎoshuō as "fiction" and Japanese しょうせつ as "novel, story, fiction". Korean 소설 and Vietnamese tiểu thuyết are the same word. Cantonese siu2 syut3 matches.

It belongs to [文学](文学.md), literature, and its short form is the [短編](短編.md), short story. A tale for children is the [童話](童話.md), and a plain telling of events is a [故事](故事.md).
