## Notes
- [除](../characters/除.md) "exclude" + [外](../characters/外.md) "outside" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes 除 as a bound character (外's own stand-in is [[外部]]; transitivity fails, so no `#cranberry`).

除外 means to exclude, except, or leave out. Mandarin 除外 (chúwài), Cantonese 除外 (ceoi4ngoi6), Japanese 除外 (じょがい), and Korean 제외 (jewoe) all preserve the same compound order as the Dan'a'yo headword (除 + 外, "exclude-outside"). Sino-Vietnamese instead uses ngoại trừ — reversing the order to "outside" + "exclude" — the same kind of native-order reshuffling seen in [[限定詞]]'s từ hạn định, a recurring pattern where modern Vietnamese grammatical/logical vocabulary built from Sino-Vietnamese morphemes doesn't always preserve the original Chinese compound order.
