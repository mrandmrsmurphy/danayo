## Notes
- [拍](../characters/拍%20(char).md) "tap, clap, beat, pulse" + [手](../characters/手%20(char).md) "hand" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 拍 and 手's own stand-ins are themselves).

拍手 means to applaud or clap.

Mandarin pāishǒu, Cantonese paak3 sau2, Japanese はくしゅ, Korean 박수, and Vietnamese phách thủ are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄚㄎㄙ⼜ unique across both words and characters).
