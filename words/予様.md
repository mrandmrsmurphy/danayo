## Notes
- [予](../characters/予.md) "beforehand, in advance" + [様 (char)](../characters/様%20(char).md) "manner, state" — this word is the `stand_in` legitimizer for [[予]], which cannot appear independently.

**A Dan'a'yo-internal coinage, not a translated calque**: like [[乳押]], this word's own two characters aren't the basis for the mandarin/cantonese/japanese/korean/vietnamese fields — those instead record each language's own real, independently existing word for "beforehand." Mandarin/Cantonese use 預先/预先 (yùxiān/jyu6 sin1, already listed among this word's own `aliases`, alongside near-synonyms 提前/先行), Japanese uses the native あらかじめ, Korean the native 미리, and Vietnamese the native trước. No homophones (注音 ⼄·⼘ㄫ unique among both words and characters).
