## Notes
- [賭](../characters/賭.md) "gamble" + [博](../characters/博.md) "gamble, wide" + [場](../characters/場.md) "place, field" — "gambling place," a casino. Their own `stand_in` values: 賭's is [[賭博]]; 博's is [[博大]]; 場's is [[市場]]; none points here, so 賭博場 is an independent compound.

賭博場 is a gambling house or casino. Mandarin dǔbóchǎng, Japanese とばくじょう and Korean 도박장 all use it, though the everyday Mandarin and Cantonese word is 賭場, with the middle character dropped; so the Cantonese field is left blank. Compare [[競技場]] (arena).
