## Notes
- [緑 (char)](../characters/緑%20(char).md) "green" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (緑's is [[緑]] itself, 色's is [[色彩]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply.

緑色 names the color green.

Mandarin lǜsè, Japanese りょくしょく (ryokushoku), and Korean 녹색 (nokssaek) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 緑色 is itself a common attested word for "green" in all three.
