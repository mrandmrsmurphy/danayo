## Notes
- [緑 (char)](../characters/緑%20(char).md) "green" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (緑's is [[緑]] itself, 色's is [[色彩]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 色 is individually `kwin: false`.

緑色 names the color green.

Mandarin lǜsè, Japanese りょくしょく (ryokushoku), and Korean 녹색 (nokssaek) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 緑色 is itself a common attested word for "green" in all three. **Fixed a real bug**: `cantonese` had nu2 se2 — a garbled reading matching neither constituent's own jyutping (緑's is luk6, 色's is sik1) — corrected to luk6 sik1. Fixed a duplicate-headword `aliases` entry (緑色 listed as its own alias). Filled blank vietnamese (lục sắc, compositional). No Dan'a'yo homophones.
