## Notes
- [鰐](../characters/鰐.md) "alligator, crocodile" + [魚 (char)](../characters/魚%20(char).md) "fish" — legitimizing note: 鰐's own `stand_in` is [[鰐魚]] itself, already annotated in its own citation; 魚's own `stand_in` is 魚 itself, so transitivity fails — no `#cranberry`.

Mandarin èyú, Cantonese ngok6jyu4, Japanese がくぎょ, and Korean 악어 (also the everyday Korean word for "crocodile/alligator") are all standard, fully compositional readings. Sino-Vietnamese ngạc ngư is the compositional form; everyday Vietnamese instead uses the native phrase cá sấu.
