## Notes
- [緩](../characters/緩.md) "slow, loose" + [慢](../characters/慢.md) "slow, arrogant" — `#cranberry`: both characters' own `stand_in` point to this exact compound (緩's and 慢's alike), so transitivity holds (A=B=AB) and both are legitimized as independent Dan'a'yo entries specifically by this word. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 緩 is individually `kwin: false`.

緩慢 means slow or sluggish.

Mandarin huǎnmàn, Cantonese wun6 maan6 (also wun4 maan6), Japanese かんまん (kanman), and Korean 완만 (wanman) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 緩慢 is itself a common attested word for "slow, sluggish" in all four languages. Vietnamese hoãn mạn is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
