## Notes
- [連 (char)](../characters/連%20(char).md) "consecutive, link" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day, sun" — "successive days."

Mandarin liánrì, cantonese lin4 jat6, japanese れんじつ, and korean 연일 are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. Vietnamese liên nhật is the honest compositional reading (連's own liên + 日's own nhật); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `连日` is the standard simplified-character spelling variant (連→连), correctly listed as an alias.
