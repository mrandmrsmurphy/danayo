## Notes
- [疑](../characters/疑.md) "doubt" + [惑 (char)](../characters/惑%20(char).md) "confused"

疑惑 is suspicion: doubt about a person's honesty or a matter's truth, especially where wrongdoing is thought. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yíhuò as "suspicion; doubt; misgivings" and Japanese ぎわく as "doubt, suspicion". Vietnamese nghi hoặc is the same word, and Korean 의혹 and Cantonese ji4 waak6 match.

It is aimed at a person or an affair, where [懐疑](懐疑.md) is doubt as a stance of mind and [疑問](疑問.md) is a question. The person under suspicion is a [容疑者](容疑者.md).
