## Notes
- [罷](../characters/罷.md) "quit" + [官](../characters/官.md) "official" — 罷's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 官's own is [[官人]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 罷官 legitimizes 罷 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 罷 is individually `kwin: false`.

罷官 means to quit, be dismissed, or resign from official office.

Mandarin bàguān, Cantonese baa6 gun1, Japanese ひかん (hikan), and Korean 파관 (pagwan) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese bãi quan is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term for being dismissed from office. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
