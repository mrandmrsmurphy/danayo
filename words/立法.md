## Notes
- [立 (char)](../characters/立%20(char).md) "stand; establish" + [法 (char)](../characters/法%20(char).md) "rule; law"

立法 is to make law: to legislate, and the legislation that results. The word is literally "to establish law". Wiktionary glosses Mandarin lìfǎ as "to legislate; legislation" and Japanese りっぽう as "legislation; to enact legislation". Korean 입법 and Vietnamese lập pháp are the matching forms; the Korean reading is given in its North Korean form, 립법.

The word names the law-making function, where [法律](法律.md) names a law that has already been made. It pairs with [司法](司法.md), which applies the law.
