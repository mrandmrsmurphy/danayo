## Notes
- [亡](../characters/亡.md) "die, flee" + [命](../characters/命.md) "life, fate" — literally "to flee with one's life," neither character's own `stand_in` points here (亡's own is [[死亡]]; 命's own is [[運命]]), so 亡命 is an independent compound. `kwin: true` per the AND-rule: `characters/亡.md` and `characters/命.md` are both individually `kwin: true`.

亡命 means "to be forced into exile, to defect" — the compelled, fleeing-for-one's-life register, as distinct from the more general [[流亡]] above. Japanese ぼうめい (bōmei) is the real, standard word for political defection/exile (亡命者, "a defector"); Korean 망명 (mangmyeong) is equally standard. Mandarin wángmìng and Vietnamese vong mệnh are both real, attested readings as well.
