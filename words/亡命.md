## Notes
- [亡](../characters/亡.md) "die, flee" + [命](../characters/命.md) "life, fate" — literally "to flee with one's life."

亡命 means "to be forced into exile, to defect" — the compelled, fleeing-for-one's-life register, as distinct from the more general [流亡](流亡.md). Japanese ぼうめい (bōmei) is the real, standard word for political defection/exile (亡命者, "a defector"); Korean 망명 (mangmyeong) is equally standard. Mandarin wángmìng and Vietnamese vong mệnh are both real, attested readings as well.
