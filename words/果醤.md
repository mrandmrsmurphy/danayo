## Notes
- [果](../characters/果.md) "fruit, berry, candy, pastry" + [醤](../characters/醤.md) "jam, paste, sauce" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 醤 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

果醤 means jam, jelly, or preserves.

Mandarin guǒjiàng and Cantonese gwo2 zoeng3 are the genuine, standard compositional Chinese term. Japanese ジャム and Korean 잼 are English loanword transliterations — no compositional Sino-Japanese/Sino-Korean compound for "jam" is in real-world use, so the loanwords are kept rather than inventing an unattested form (the same pattern documented on [[板球]]). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with the compositional form quả tương. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⺢ㄐㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
