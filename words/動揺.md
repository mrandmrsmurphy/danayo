## Notes
- [動 (char)](../characters/動%20(char).md) "move" + [揺 (char)](../characters/揺%20(char).md) "shake"

動揺 is agitation: to be shaken in mind, losing one's steadiness, or the unrest itself. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin dòngyáo as "to swing; to rock; to shake; to waver; to rattle" and Japanese どうよう as "distress; agitation; swaying; shaking". Cantonese dung6 jiu4 matches, and Korean 동요 is the reading of the characters.

It is the loss of [沈着](沈着.md), composure, and a restless shaking short of [恐慌](恐慌.md), panic. Being shaken by fear is [戦慄](戦慄.md), and the flustered state is [狼狽](狼狽.md).
