## Notes
- [温](../characters/温.md) "warm" + [泉](../characters/泉.md) "spring" — hot spring.

温泉 is a hot spring: natural groundwater heated by the earth, often used for bathing. It is "warm spring." Mandarin wēnquán, Cantonese wan1 cyun4, Japanese おんせん and Korean 온천 are the same compound; Japanese onsen are famous as a bathing culture. This is a literal water spring heated from below, in contrast to [[源泉]], whose meaning has drifted to "source, fountainhead." A cold natural spring has no dedicated word yet.
