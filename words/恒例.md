## Notes
- [恒](../characters/恒.md) "constant, ubiquitous" + [例](../characters/例.md) "example" — no stand-in relationship applies (恒's own stand-in is [[恒久]]; 例's own is [[実例]]).

恒例 means an established practice or custom — a regular, recurring tradition.

Mandarin hénglì, Cantonese hang4 lai6, Japanese こうれい, Korean 항례, and Vietnamese hằng lệ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄨㄫㄌㄝ unique across both words and characters).
