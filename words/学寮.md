## Notes
- [学](../characters/学.md) "learn" + [寮](../characters/寮.md) "dormitory" — this word is the stand-in for [寮](../characters/寮.md), which cannot appear independently. The Vietnamese reading học liêu is compositional, not independently attested via hvdic.

学寮 means student dormitory. The character 寮 alone means dormitory, but is too bounded to stand by itself in Dan'a'yo, so this compound serves as its viable form.
