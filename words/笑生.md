## Notes
- [笑](../characters/笑%20(char).md) "laugh" + [生](../characters/生.md) "born, birth" — "Isaac, the son of Abraham." Their own `stand_in` values: 笑's is itself; 生's is [[生活]]; none points here, so 笑生 is an independent compound.

笑生 is Isaac, the son of Abraham and Sarah, whose name means "he laughs": Abraham laughed at the promise (Genesis 17:17), Sarah laughed (18:12), and at his birth she said "God has made laughter for me" (21:6). It is a Dan'a'yo **neologism**, a meaning-based name, "born of laughter" (笑 laugh, 生 born), and it names the Genesis section 笑生世代記. Compare [[踵祖]] (Jacob) and [[毛父]] (Esau). See [[基督敎]].

- Hebrew name: יִצְחָק (Yitzchak) (注音 ·ㄧㄐㄚㄏㄚㄎ).

- See [基督敎](../lexipedia/基督敎.md).
