## Notes
- [連 (char)](../characters/連%20(char).md) "link, connect" + [続](../characters/続.md) "continue" — following one after another without a break. Attested across Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, Korean, and Vietnamese.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 련쇽 diverges from Sino-Korean 연속 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄌ⼶ㄋㄙ⼄ㄎ unique among both words and characters). Both character-page citations already correctly present; neither 連's `stand_in` (連 itself) nor 続's own `stand_in` (継続) points to this compound.
