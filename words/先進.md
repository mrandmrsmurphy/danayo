## Notes
- [先](../characters/先.md) "prior" + [進](../characters/進.md) "advance, progress" — "advanced" (先進国, "developed/advanced country"; 先進技術, "advanced technology"). Mandarin xiānjìn, Cantonese sin1 zeon3, Japanese せんしん, Korean 선진, and Vietnamese tiên tiến are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 선진 matches Sino-Korean 선진 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄋㄐㄧㄋ unique among both words and characters).
