- [格](characters/格%20(char).md) "case, status" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 格 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

格 means (grammatical) case, or status.

Mandarin gé, Cantonese gaak3, Japanese かく, Korean 격, and Vietnamese cách are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 格's own stored citation exactly).
