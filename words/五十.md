## Notes
- [五 (char)](../characters/五%20(char).md) "five" + [十 (char)](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" — fifty.

Mandarin wǔshí, Cantonese ng5 sap6, Japanese ごじゅう, and Korean 오십 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese ngũ thập is the classical/formal Sino-Vietnamese numeral, distinct from the everyday native năm mươi used in ordinary counting (the same pattern as [[九十]]'s cửu thập vs. chín mươi).

`kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 오십 coincides exactly with Sino-Korean 오십, since both 五 and 十 independently carry `kwin: true` on their own character pages.
