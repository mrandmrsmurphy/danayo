## Notes
- [殺](../characters/殺%20(char).md) "kill" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 殺 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

殺 means to kill: to deprive a person or animal of life (殺人, "murder"; 殺手, "killer, assassin"; 殺菌, "sterilize"), and by extension to destroy or eliminate (殺價, "slash a price"; 殺風景, "spoil the scenery"). In compounds it can also mean to reduce or weaken (相殺, "offset, cancel out"). The traditional graph has the simplified variant 杀.

Mandarin shā, Cantonese saat3, Japanese さつ (殺人/さつじん, "murder"; 暗殺/あんさつ, "assassination"), with the alternate readings さい and せつ in a few compounds (相殺/そうさい, "offsetting"; 殺生/せっしょう, "taking life"), Korean 살 (살인, 암살) and Vietnamese sát (sát nhân, "murderer"; ám sát, "assassinate") are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native verb 殺す (ころす) for the everyday word, so on'yomi さつ is bound to compounds, and Korean uses native 죽이다.
