## Notes
- [殺](characters/殺%20(char).md) "kill" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 殺 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

殺 means to kill.

Mandarin shā, Cantonese saat3, Japanese さつ, Korean 살, and Vietnamese sát (the primary form among the character's own multi-value vietnamese list) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed literal `vietnamese: null` → sát, added the missing `pos`/`japanese`/`hsk_level` fields. No word-level homophones — the bound character [[薩]] shares this same 注音 (ㄙㄚㄊ) but has no independent word page of its own.
