## Notes
- [指](../characters/指.md) "finger; point" + [定](../characters/定.md) "fix, decide" — "to point at and fix": to single something out and settle it as the one meant.

Wiktionary: Chinese "to confirm (a person, time, place, etc.); to appoint, assign, designate, nominate"; Japanese "naming; designation; specification" and, as a verb, "to name, designate, specify"; Korean 지정 "designation, appointment"; Vietnamese chỉ định "indication, instruction; to appoint, assign, designate; demonstrative". Used in `文法 - 03文字法` in 名字指定 and 指定事物. Compare [[決定]], [[規定]], [[推定]].
