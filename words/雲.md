## Notes
- [雲 (char)](../characters/雲%20(char).md) "cloud" — the bare character used directly as a word, its own `stand_in`.

雲 means cloud: a visible mass of water droplets in the sky (雲朵, "cloud"; 烏雲, "dark cloud"; 雲層, "cloud layer"), and by extension anything cloud-like or lofty (雲集, "gather in crowds"; 青雲, "high office"). Mandarin yún, Cantonese wan4, Korean 운, and Vietnamese vân are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese うん (UN) is the on'yomi used in compounds like 雲海 (unkai, "sea of clouds"), while the native 訓読み くも (kumo) remains the everyday word for "cloud" itself; Korean has native 구름.

The character's own Notes trace 雲 back to the simpler original form 云, with the weather radical 雨 added once 云 alone came to be associated with the unrelated sense "speak" — a disambiguating semantic component added to resolve the collision, rather than a purely phonetic derivation.
