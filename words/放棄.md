## Notes
- [放](../characters/放.md) "release, let go" + [棄](../characters/棄.md) "discard" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [棄](../characters/棄.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (放's own stand-in is [[釈放]]).

放棄 means to abandon or reject.

Mandarin fàngqì, Cantonese fong3 hei3, Japanese ほうき, Korean 방기, and Vietnamese phóng khí are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄈㄚㄫㄎㄧㄜ unique across both words and characters).
