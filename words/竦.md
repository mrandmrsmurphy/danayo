## Notes
- [竦](../characters/竦%20(char).md) "awe" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 竦 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

竦 means awe, reverence, or the physical sensation of being alarmed or on edge — to stand upright or on tiptoe from fear or respect, an image reflected in its 形声 semantic component 立 ("stand"). Variant forms 聳 and 捒 are both confirmed as belonging to the same word family.

Mandarin sǒng, Cantonese sung2, and Korean 송 (song) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading おそれる (osoreru, "to fear, be in awe") rather than the on-reading ショウ (SHOU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese tủng is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading. This word is part of a genuine three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read song/송/ㄙㄛㄫ): [[送]] ("give") and [[誦]] ("recite, chant").
