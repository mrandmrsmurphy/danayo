## Notes
- [満](../characters/満%20(char).md) "full" + [族](../characters/族.md) "tribe, clan" — no stand-in relationship applies (満's own stand-in is itself; 族's own is [[家族]]).

満族 means the Manchu ethnicity.

Mandarin Mǎnzú, Japanese まんぞく, and Korean 만족 (also the real Korean term for "Manchu," coexisting with the unrelated homophone meaning "satisfaction") are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 満's and 族's own stored citations exactly.
