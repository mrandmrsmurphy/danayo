## Notes
- [満](../characters/満%20(char).md) "full" + [族](../characters/族.md) "tribe, clan" — no stand-in relationship applies (満's own stand-in is itself; 族's own is [[家族]]).

満族 means the Manchu ethnicity.

Mandarin Mǎnzú, Japanese まんぞく, and Korean 만족 (also the real Korean term for "Manchu," coexisting with the unrelated homophone meaning "satisfaction") are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 満's and 族's own stored citations exactly. Filled the blank `japanese`/`korean`/`vietnamese` fields (vietnamese: mãn tộc, 満's own mãn + 族's own tộc). Filled the blank `cantonese` — already present as mun5 zuk6 (using 族's own zuk6, distinct from [[満足]]'s zuk1 from 足, so these two are not homophones in Cantonese specifically).

**Real Dan'a'yo homophone with [[満足]]**: 族 and 足 happen to share the identical Dan'a'yo reading (jog/족/ㄐㄛㄎ), making 満族 and 満足 genuine homophones in Dan'a'yo, Mandarin, and Korean (though not Cantonese). Reformatted the pre-existing informal tip callout into the canonical `Homophones` format on both pages.
