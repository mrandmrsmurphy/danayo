## Notes
- [霊](../characters/霊.md) "spirit; soul" + [柩](../characters/柩.md) "coffin"

霊柩 is a coffin. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 靈柩 (aliases 靈柩 and 灵柩; Mandarin língjiù, Cantonese ling4 gau6) as "coffin with the body; coffin containing a corpse; bier", and Japanese れいきゅう as a synonym of 棺 (hitsugi, kan), "coffin; casket". Korean 영구 and Vietnamese linh cữu are glossed "coffin".

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [柩](../characters/柩.md), which is too bounded to stand alone. The stand-in of 霊 is [霊魂](霊魂.md).
