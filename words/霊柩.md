## Notes
- [霊](../characters/霊.md) "spirit; soul" + [柩](../characters/柩.md) "coffin" — 霊柩 is the stand-in for [柩](../characters/柩.md), which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo (霊's own stand-in is instead [[霊魂]]).

霊柩 specifically names a coffin *with a body already in it*, ready for a funeral procession or burial — distinct from an empty coffin (棺, a separate, more general word) — the 霊 ("spirit, soul") component marking that the deceased's spirit is understood to still be present. Most familiar today through 霊柩車 (reikyūsha), the distinctively ornate hearse used in Japanese funerals, though that specific compound has no word file of its own yet in this vault.

Japanese れいきゅう is the direct on'yomi compositional reading (霊 REI + 柩 KYUU), matching both characters' own citations. Korean 영구 and Vietnamese linh cữu are likewise standard compositional Sino-readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 렁규 diverges from Sino-Korean 영구 in both syllables. No specific HSK level applies to a term this specialized. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄝㄫㄍ⼜ unique across both words and characters).
