## Notes
- [脚 (char)](../characters/脚%20(char).md) "leg; foot" + [本 (char)](../characters/本%20(char).md) "book; root"

脚本 is a script: the written text of a play, film or broadcast. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin jiǎoběn as "script" and Japanese きゃくほん as "script". Korean 각본 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese goek3 bun2 matches.

It is written by the [劇作家](劇作家.md), and its spoken parts are the [台詞](台詞.md), lines. The play in written form can also be called a [戯曲](戯曲.md).
