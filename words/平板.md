## Notes
- [平](../characters/平.md) "even" + [板](../characters/板.md) "plank; board" — no stand-in relationship applies (平's own `stand_in` is [[水平]], 板's is [[木板]]).

平板 names something flat like a board — a flat panel or slab, and figuratively (in modern usage) a tablet computer (平板電腦). It also carries a figurative sense of "flat, monotonous" when describing writing or speech lacking variation.

Japanese へいばん, Korean 평판, and Vietnamese bình bản are all standard, non-drifted readings. No homophones (注音 ㄅ⼶ㄫㄆㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
