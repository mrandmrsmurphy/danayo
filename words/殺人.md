## Notes
- [殺 (char)](../characters/殺%20(char).md) "kill" + [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person"

殺人 is murder or homicide: the killing of a person. Wiktionary glosses Japanese さつじん as "homicide, especially murder" and Korean 살인 as "murder; killing; homicide". Mandarin shārén is recorded there as a verb, "to kill someone; to murder", and Vietnamese sát nhân there means the murderer or killer, so the Vietnamese word names the person where Dan'a'yo names the act.

Dan'a'yo takes the act, as in Japanese and Korean. [殺](殺.md) is the bare verb "to kill" and [暗殺](暗殺.md) is assassination. The person who commits it is the [犯人](犯人.md), the killer is the [凶手](凶手.md), and the lawful killing of the condemned is [誅殺](誅殺.md), "to execute".
