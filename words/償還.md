## Notes
- [償](../characters/償.md) "repay; recompense" + [還](../characters/還.md) "return; repatriate" — the ordinary legal or financial act of returning what is owed; settling a debt or compensating for a loss

Wiktionary: Chinese 償還 "to repay; to pay back"; Japanese 償還 (しょうかん) "repayment" and, in finance, "redemption (of a bond etc.)", and as a verb "to repay" and, in finance, "to redeem". Dan'a'yo uses 償還 for financial and legal restitution only, not for the Christian concept of redemption, which is [[救贖]].

- See [Trade](../lexipedia/Trade.md); the contrast with [[救贖]] is also marked on [基督敎](../lexipedia/基督敎.md).
