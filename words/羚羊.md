## Notes
- [羚](../characters/羚.md) "antelope" + [羊](../characters/羊.md) "sheep, goat" — 羚's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 羊's own is [[綿羊]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 羚羊 legitimizes 羚 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 羚 is individually `kwin: false`. See also [[麒麟羚羊]] (gerenuk).

羚羊 names the antelope.

Mandarin língyáng, Cantonese ling4 joeng4, and Korean 령양 (ryeongyang) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 羚羊 is itself a common attested word for "antelope" in all three. Japanese れいやう is the historical kana spelling of れいよう (reiyō). Vietnamese linh dương is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term for "antelope." No Dan'a'yo homophones.
