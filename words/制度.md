## Notes
- [制](../characters/制.md) "regulate" + [度](../characters/度.md) "standard"

制度 means a system or institution: a formal, regulated framework within which conduct is organised, from a constitutional arrangement to the institution of marriage or a school grading system. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 制度 (Mandarin zhìdù, Cantonese zai3 dou6) as "system; institution", with examples such as 社會主義制度, "socialist system", 婚姻制度, "institution of marriage" and 種姓制度, "caste system". Japanese せいど and Korean 제도 are glossed the same, and Vietnamese chế độ as "regime; system". Korean 제도 is also, by coincidence, the word for an archipelago, and for drafting.

It joins 制, to regulate, with 度, a standard or measure. Compare [[体制]], a system in the sense of a governing structure or regime, where 制度 is the broader, more neutral word for any institutional framework.
