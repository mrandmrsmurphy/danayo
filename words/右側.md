## Notes
- [右](../characters/右.md) "right (direction)" + [側](../characters/側.md) "side; aspect" — stand-in for [[右]], which cannot appear independently

右側 means the right side: the side of a thing or person on the right hand (右側通行, "keep to the right, right-hand traffic"; 右側面, "right flank"; 道路右側, "the right of the road"). Its counterpart is [[左側]]. The simplified form 右侧 is listed as an alias.

Mandarin yòucè and Cantonese jau6 zak1 are the standard word for "the right side". Japanese uses the native みぎがわ (migigawa) for the everyday word, with the on'yomi うそく (usoku) recorded here for the formal register. Korean 우측 (ujeuk) is the Sino-Korean reading and is interchangeable in everyday Korean with the native 오른쪽 (편의점은 우측/오른쪽에 있습니다, "the convenience store is on the right"). Vietnamese hữu trắc is a real Sino-Vietnamese compound; bare hữu alone is also commonly sufficient for "right" in spatial, political and geographic senses (hữu ngạn, "the right bank of a river").
