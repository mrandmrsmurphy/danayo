## Notes
- [離](../characters/離.md) "depart, leave" + [別 (char)](../characters/別%20(char).md) "don't, distinct, other" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes 離 as a bound character (別's own stand-in is [[別]] itself; transitivity fails, so no `#cranberry`).

離別 means to depart, part ways, or separate — often carrying an emotional, literary weight (a parting between loved ones) rather than a purely neutral logistical sense. Mandarin 離別/离别 (líbié), Cantonese 離別 (lei4bit6), Japanese 離別 (りべつ), Korean 리별 (ribyeol — this vault's standing North Korean/문화어 pronunciation policy; South Korean orthography would instead give 이별, shifting the word-initial ㄹ via 두음법칙), and Sino-Vietnamese ly biệt all preserve this meaning, ly biệt in particular remaining common in poetry and literary Vietnamese.
