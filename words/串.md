## Notes
- [串 (char)](../characters/串%20(char).md) "skewer"

串 is 象形 (pictographic): a vertical line piercing through two linked loops, depicting objects strung or threaded together — "to string, thread through; a skewer; a string/bunch of" (also used as a classifier). It appears in modern compounds like 串烧 (chuànshāo, "skewered grilled food") and 串聯 (chuànlián, "to connect in series").

Japanese has two on'yomi, セン/カン (sen/kan), alongside the everyday native kun'yomi くし (kushi, "skewer" — e.g. 串カツ kushikatsu, skewered fried food). Vietnamese has two attested Hán Việt/Nôm readings: quán (the older, more classical reading) and xuyến (the reading proper to this word's "string together, skewer" sense, as in xuyến châu "to string pearls" and nhất xuyến "a string of," used as a classifier) — xuyến is used here as the sense-appropriate reading.
