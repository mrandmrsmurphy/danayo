## Notes
- [猶](../characters/猶%20(char).md) "furthermore" + [太](../characters/太%20(char).md) "great, extreme" — a phonetic transliteration of "Judea/Jewish"; no stand-in relationship applies (both 猶's and 太's own stand-ins are themselves).

Mandarin Yóutài and Cantonese jau4 taai3 are fully compositional. Japanese ユダヤ (Yudaya) is the real, standard Japanese transliteration loanword rather than a compositional reading. Korean 유태 is a real, standard alternate form (alongside 유대).
