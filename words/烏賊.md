## Notes
- [烏](../characters/烏.md) "crow, raven" + [賊](../characters/賊.md) "thief" — no stand-in relationship applies (烏's own stand-in is [[烏鳥]]; 賊's own is [[盗賊]]).

烏賊 means squid, cuttlefish.

Mandarin wūzéi and Cantonese wu1 caak6 are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 烏's and 賊's own stored citations. Japanese いか and Korean 오징어 are the real, standard native words for "squid" in each language — the same real-world-species-name convention already established on [[龍蝦]]/[[水牛]]/[[河馬]].
