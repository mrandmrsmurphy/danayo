>[!caution] The senses split — Japanese "earlobe", Chinese "ear"
>耳朶 is the earlobe in Japanese, and the Dan'a'yo sense. Chinese 耳朵 means the whole ear, and the earlobe is 耳垂, so a Chinese reader may hear "ear". Listed as a Split on [文法 - 91疑義語](../grammar/文法%20-%2091疑義語.md); for the whole ear use [耳](耳.md).


## Notes
- [耳 (char)](../characters/耳%20(char).md) "ear" + [朶](../characters/朶.md) "flower blossom"

耳朶 is the earlobe. English Wiktionary glosses Japanese 耳朶 (じだ) as "earlobe", and gives みみたぶ (耳たぶ) as a second Japanese word for it. Chinese 耳朶 redirects to 耳朵 (Mandarin ěrduo), glossed "ear (organ of hearing)"; the Chinese earlobe is 耳垂, which Wiktionary glosses "earlobe". The word is listed as a Split on [文法 - 91疑義語](../grammar/文法%20-%2091疑義語.md). The Korean 귓불 field is the native word for the earlobe, and the Vietnamese nhĩ thuỳ is the reading of the alias 耳垂.
