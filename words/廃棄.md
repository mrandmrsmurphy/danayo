## Notes
- [廃](../characters/廃.md) "abrogate; discard" + [棄](../characters/棄.md) "abandon; give up" — this word is the stand-in for [廃](../characters/廃.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

廃棄 is a near-synonym doubling, both halves independently meaning "to discard, abandon" — used both concretely (廃棄物 "waste, discarded material") and legally/formally (廃棄する条約, "to abrogate/void a treaty").

Japanese はいき, Korean 폐기, and Vietnamese phế khí are all standard, non-drifted readings. No homophones (注音 ㄈㄝㄎㄧㄜ unique across both words and characters).
