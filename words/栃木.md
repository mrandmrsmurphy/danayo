## Notes
- [栃](../characters/栃.md) "horse chestnut" + [木 (char)](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree"
- This word is the viable form of [栃](../characters/栃.md): the character's meaning (horse chestnut tree) is identical, but 栃 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 栃木 is its `stand_in` compound.

栃木 is Tochigi, a prefecture of Japan in the Kantō region, north of Tokyo; its capital is Utsunomiya (宇都宮) and it contains the shrines and temples of Nikkō (日光). Its name is written with 栃, a kokuji (a character made in Japan, not found in older Chinese texts) for the Japanese horse chestnut (トチノキ), and 木 "tree", so the word as a place name is 栃木県 (Tochigi-ken). The Japanese reading is とちぎ (tochigi); the frontmatter was corrected from とちき, which lacked the voiced ぎ. The Mandarin lìmù and Cantonese lai6 muk6 readings are only the compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters, since the place is not a Chinese name; Korean uses 도치기 (dochigi), borrowed from the Japanese pronunciation.

In Dan'a'yo it is the place name only, listed with Ehime among the Japanese prefectures; the tree sense of 栃 is the character's own, not a separate use of this word.
