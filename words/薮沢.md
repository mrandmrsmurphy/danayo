## Notes
- [薮](../characters/薮.md) "marsh, swamp" + [沢](../characters/沢.md) "stream, swamp" — 薮沢 is the stand-in word for [薮](../characters/薮.md); 薮 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse.

薮沢 is a near-synonym doubling: both characters independently name wetland terrain (marsh, swamp, bog), and the compound intensifies rather than shifts the sense — a classical, literary term for marshland generally, as opposed to a specific named wetland.

Japanese そうたく and Korean 수택 are both standard compositional Sino-readings. Vietnamese tẩu trạch is compositionally derived from each character's own confirmed reading, though not independently confirmed as an attested fixed compound. No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄛㄨㄉㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
