## Notes
- [薮](../characters/薮.md) "marsh, swamp" + [沢](../characters/沢.md) "stream, swamp"

薮沢 is a marsh. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 藪澤 (alias here; Mandarin sǒuzé, Cantonese sau2 zaak6) as "lake or marsh overgrown with aquatic plants" and "place where people and things gather". The Japanese そうたく, Korean 수택 and Vietnamese tẩu trạch fields are built from the readings of the two characters.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [薮](../characters/薮.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
