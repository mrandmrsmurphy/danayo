## Notes
- [福 (char)](../characters/福%20(char).md) "blessing, good fortune" + [建](../characters/建.md) "build, establish" — from two ancient prefectures, Fuzhou and Jianzhou. No stand-in relationship applies (福 is its own stand-in; 建's is [[建設]]).

福建 (Fujian) is a province on the southeast coast of China, facing Taiwan across the Taiwan Strait, whose capital is [[福州]] and which includes the port city [[廈門]] (from memory, not re-checked). English Wiktionary says the name was first coined in 733 by the Tang court, with the establishment of the Fujian Military Commission, and comes from 福州 (named for Mount Fu) and 建州 (derived from 建安, named for the Jian'an era of 196).

The Japanese reading that English Wiktionary gives is ふっけん, with a geminate (促音) before け, not the plain concatenation ふくけん; the field follows Wiktionary. Okinawan also has a separate reading ふちゃん (not covered here). The Korean field holds the Sino-Korean 복건 (Wiktionary Korean: hanja form of 복건), which is not the same as the Dan'a'yo 諺文 뿍건 because 福's Dan'a'yo reading is 뿍; the usual South Korean name today is 푸젠 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Phúc Kiến is the Sino-Vietnamese name. English Wiktionary adds a usage note: the offshore counties Kinmen (Quemoy) and Lienchiang (Matsu), governed from Taiwan, are counted in or out depending on context.

Attestation, checked 2026-10-11: English and Chinese Wiktionary (Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese sections) list only the province for this name. No homophones (注音 ㄈㄨㄎㄍㄝㄋ unique across both words and characters).
