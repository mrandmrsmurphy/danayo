## Notes
- [幹](../characters/幹.md) "trunk; main part" + [部 (char)](../characters/部%20(char).md) "part" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (幹's is 主幹, 部's is bare 部).

Mandarin gànbù, Cantonese gon3 bou6, Japanese かんぶ, Korean 간부, and Vietnamese cán bộ are all everyday words for "cadre; official."
