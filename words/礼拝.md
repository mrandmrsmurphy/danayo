## Notes
- [礼 (char)](../characters/礼%20(char).md) "manners" + [拝 (char)](../characters/拝%20(char).md) "bow, kowtow" — literally "ritual bowing," a formal act of religious worship.

礼拝 has two genuinely distinct readings in Japanese, reflecting a register split: らいはい (raihai) is used in Buddhist contexts (worshipping the Buddha or deities), while れいはい (reihai) is used for Christian and Shinto worship services. Mandarin lǐbài and Cantonese lai5 baai3 both extend the everyday-usage sense to mean "week" as well (禮拜/礼拜, "week"; 禮拜一/星期一, "Monday"), a colloquial extension unrelated to the religious sense.

Korean 예배 (yebae) is the standard Christian term for a worship service. Vietnamese lễ bái is compositional and carries the general religious-worship sense across the sphere.
