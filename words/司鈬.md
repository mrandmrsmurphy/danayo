## Notes
- [司](../characters/司.md) "oversee, manage" + [鈬](../characters/鈬%20(char).md) "bronze bell" — no stand-in relationship applies (司's own stand-in is [[公司]]; 鈬 is its own stand-in).
- The Catholic ordained priest. The term derives from a phono-semantic match of Latin *sacerdōtem*. Distinct from [[牧師]] (Protestant pastor) and [[神父]] (Eastern Orthodox/Catholic father).

Filled blank `vietnamese: tư đạc` (verified — the real, attested Vietnamese Catholic term for priest, literally "manager of the bell-striker," an allusion to Confucius in the Analects). Japanese したく and Korean 사탁 are both standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄚㄉㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
