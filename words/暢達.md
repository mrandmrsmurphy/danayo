## Notes
- [暢](../characters/暢.md) "smooth, free, unrestrained" + [達](../characters/達%20(char).md) "attain, achieve" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [暢](../characters/暢.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 達's own stand-in is itself (it also has its own independent word page, [[達]]).

Mandarin chàngdá, Cantonese coeng3 daat6, Japanese ちょうたつ, Korean 창달, and Vietnamese sướng đạt are all standard, fully compositional readings (達's own stored `vietnamese` field does include "đạt" — corrected an inaccurate Etymology note that had claimed it was missing). Reformatted `mandarin` to remove the stray space, reformatted `characters` to a block list with the "(char)" suffix on 達 (which also has its own word page), and removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/`aliases` fields.
