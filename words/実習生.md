## Notes
- [実](../characters/実.md) "real, fruit" + [習](../characters/習.md) "practice, learn" + [生](../characters/生.md) "life; student" — trainee.

実習生 is a trainee or intern: a person undergoing [[実習]] (practical training), whether as a student placed in a workplace or a worker learning a trade. It is [[実習]] "practical training" plus 生 "student, person in training." Japanese じっしゅうせい and Korean 실습생 are the readings in use, and Mandarin has the matching 实习生 (shíxíshēng); these three readings come from memory, as Wiktionary has no entry for the compound. `cantonese` is left blank. Compare [[弟子]] (apprentice, disciple), the traditional master-and-pupil relationship rather than a placement.
