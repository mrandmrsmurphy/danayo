## Notes
- [魔](../characters/魔.md) "demon, magic" + [力 (char)](../characters/力%20(char).md) "power, strength" — no `#cranberry`: 魔's own `stand_in` is [[魔鬼]], 力's is 力 itself; neither points here.

Mandarin mólì, Cantonese mo1lik6, Japanese まりょく, Korean 마력, and Sino-Vietnamese ma lực are all standard, fully compositional readings for "magical power."
