## Etymology
- [配 (char)](../characters/配%20(char).md) "match, pair" + [置 (char)](../characters/置%20(char).md) "put, place"

## Notes
Mandarin pèizhì, cantonese pui3 zi3, japanese はいち, and korean 배치 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese phối trí is the honest compositional reading (配's own phối + 置's own trí); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 푀치 diverges from Sino-Korean 배치 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄛㄧㄑㄧ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 配's `stand_in` (配 itself) nor 置's own `stand_in` points to this compound.
