## Notes
- [世](../characters/世.md) "generation, world" + [紀](../characters/紀.md) "century, record" + [末](../characters/末.md) "end" — no `#cranberry`.

Mandarin shìjìmò, Cantonese sai3 gei2 mut6, Japanese せいきまつ, and Korean 세기말 are all standard compositional readings. Vietnamese cuối thế kỷ ("end century," native word-order-reversed) follows the same pattern as sibling [[世紀中]]/[[世紀初]].
