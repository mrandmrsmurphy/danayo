## Notes
- [禿 (char)](../characters/禿%20(char).md) "bald" + [就 (char)](../characters/就%20(char).md), here used as this vault's borrowed stand-in glyph (代用字) for the unlinked character 鷲 ("eagle, vulture") — no stand-in relationship applies (both 禿's and 就's own `stand_in` fields point to themselves).

禿就 (禿鷲) names an Old World vulture — a bald-headed vulture, the bare head being an adaptation for hygiene when feeding on carrion. 就 keeps its own single Dan'a'yo sound (cwai/쵀/ㄑ⺢ㄧ) here; as a character it has no second reading. 鷲 is its alias, and the Mandarin jiù and Cantonese zau6 below are the real word's readings, as on [[就鳥]] ("vulture" generally).

Japanese はげわし (hagewashi, literally "bald eagle/vulture," fully compositional from はげ "bald" + わし "eagle") and Korean 독수리 (doksuri, the standard everyday word for vulture/eagle) are standard readings. Mandarin tūjiù and Cantonese tuk1 zau6 are fully compositional. Vietnamese thốc thứu is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form, using 鷲's real reading thứu (as on [[就鳥]]); everyday Vietnamese instead prefers the native kền kền.
