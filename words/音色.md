## Notes
- [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" + [色](../characters/色.md) "colour"

音色 is timbre or tone colour: the quality that tells one instrument or voice from another. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yīnsè as "timbre" and Japanese ねいろ as "tone color; timbre". Korean 음색 and Vietnamese âm sắc are the same word. Cantonese jam1 sik1 matches.

It is the colour of a sound, and it is what sets a [琴](琴.md) apart from a [笛](笛.md) playing the same note.
