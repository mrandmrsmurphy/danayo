## Notes
- [喫 (char)](../characters/喫%20(char).md) "to eat; to suffer" + [驚](../characters/驚.md) "to startle; to frighten" — this word is the stand-in for [驚](../characters/驚.md), which cannot appear independently.

喫驚 means to be startled. The character 驚 alone means to startle or frighten, but is too bounded to stand by itself in Dan'a'yo, so this compound serves as its viable form.
