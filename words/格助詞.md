## Notes
- [格](../characters/格%20(char).md) "case" + [助](../characters/助.md) "assist" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "word" — no stand-in relationship applies (格's own stand-in is itself; 助's own is [[援助]]; 詞's own is [[単詞]]).

格助詞 means case particle (a grammatical category).

Japanese かくじょし and Korean 격조사 are standard, fully compositional readings.
