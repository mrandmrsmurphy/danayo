## Notes
- [民](../characters/民.md) "citizen" + [法 (char)](../characters/法%20(char).md) "rule; law"

民法 is civil law: the branch of law that governs private relations between people, such as property, contracts, family and inheritance. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin mínfǎ as "civil law, the body of law dealing with the private relations between members of a community", and Japanese みんぽう as "civil law". Korean 민법 and Cantonese man4 faat3 match. The Japanese reading shows a voicing change in the second syllable, みんぽう.

It pairs with [刑法](刑法.md), criminal law, and it is the law behind [契約](契約.md), contracts, and [遺産](遺産.md), inheritance. In a civil case the parties sue each other, and the state does not prosecute.
