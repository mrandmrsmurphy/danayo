## Notes
- [船](../characters/船.md) "ship" + [首](../characters/首.md) "head, first" — bow.

船首 is the bow of a ship: its front end. It is "ship-head," the opposite of [[船尾]] (stern). Mandarin chuánshǒu and Japanese せんしゅ are the readings on Wiktionary; Cantonese syun4 sau2 and Korean 선수 are the same compound and come from memory. Korean 선수 is also a homophone of the common word for "athlete," disambiguated by context.
