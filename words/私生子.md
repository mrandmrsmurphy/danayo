## Notes
- [私](../characters/私.md) "private, illicit" + [生](../characters/生.md) "birth" + [子](../characters/子.md) "child" — "privately born child," an illegitimate child. Their own `stand_in` values: 私's is [[私立]]; 生's is [[生活]]; 子's is [[児子]]; none points here, so 私生子 is an independent compound.

私生子 is an illegitimate child: one born outside marriage. Mandarin sīshēngzǐ, Cantonese si1 sang1 zi2, Japanese しせいし (私生児 is the more usual spelling) and Korean 사생자 (the usual Korean word is 사생아) are attested. This is the literal sense of "bastard"; the vulgar insult use is not a set word across the languages, so Dan'a'yo does not coin one. Compare [[嗣子]] (heir).
