## Notes
- [懲](../characters/懲.md) "punish" + [罰](../characters/罰.md) "penalty" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes both [懲](../characters/懲.md) and [罰](../characters/罰.md) as independent Dan'a'yo entries.

懲罰 means to punish or penalize.

Mandarin chéngfá, Cantonese cing4 fat6, Japanese ちょうばつ, Korean 징벌, and Vietnamese trừng phạt are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄫㄅㄝㄊ unique across both words and characters).
