## Notes
- [天 (char)](../characters/天%20(char).md) "sky" + [底 (char)](../characters/底%20(char).md) "bottom" — literally "sky's bottom." The direct opposite of [天頂](天頂.md) ("zenith," literally "sky's crown/top"): the nadir is the point on the celestial sphere directly beneath the observer, always 180° from the zenith.

Mandarin tiāndǐ, Cantonese tin1 dai2, Korean 천저 (cheonjeo), and Japanese てんてい (tentei) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real astronomical term for "nadir" in its language. Vietnamese thiên để is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese (not thiên đề or thiên đế, as Vietnamese Wikipedia notes in correcting common mistranslations of the term).
