## Notes
- [三](../characters/三%20(char).md) "three" + [角](../characters/角%20(char).md) "corner, angle" + [洲](../characters/洲%20(char).md) "islet, continent" — "three-cornered islet," the triangle of land at a river mouth. All three characters are their own `stand_in`, so 三角洲 is an independent compound.

三角洲 is a river delta: the fan of silt deposited where a river meets the sea, named for its triangular shape (the Greek letter Δ). Mandarin sānjiǎozhōu, Cantonese saam1 gok3 zau1 and Japanese さんかくす (written 三角州 or 三角洲) use it, and Korean 삼각주 is the same word. Compare [[河川]] (rivers) and [[半島]] (peninsula).
