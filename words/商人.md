## Notes
- [商](../characters/商.md) "trade, commerce" + [人](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" — "trade person," a merchant. Their own `stand_in` values: 商's is [[商業]]; 人's is itself; none points here, so 商人 is an independent compound.

商人 is a merchant: someone who buys and sells goods for a living. Mandarin shāngrén, Cantonese soeng1 jan4, Japanese しょうにん and Korean 상인 all use it, and Vietnamese thương nhân does too. It is the seller's side of [[顧客]]. Compare [[商業]] (commerce) and [[商店]] (shop).
