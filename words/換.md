## Notes
- [換](../characters/換%20(char).md) "interchange, exchange" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

換 means to interchange or exchange — swapping one thing for another, as in 交換 ("exchange") and 換金 ("cash in").

Mandarin huàn, Cantonese wun6, Japanese かん, Korean 환, and Vietnamese hoán are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `注音` was stored as the spelled-out literal ㄏㄨㄚㄇ instead of the vault's canonical single-glyph rime notation `ㄏ⺢ㄇ.md` (the same character-vs-word 注音 divergence bug class fixed on sibling [[患]]). Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄏ⺢ㄇ/hwam/홤) with the unrelated [[患]] ("suffer from").
