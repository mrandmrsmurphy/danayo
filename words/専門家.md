## Notes
- [専](../characters/専.md) "special; dedicated" + [門](../characters/門.md) "gate; field" + [家](../characters/家.md) "house; family; -ist"

専門家 is a specialist or expert: one who has deep knowledge of a particular field. Wiktionary glosses Japanese せんもんか as "specialist; expert" and Mandarin zhuānménjiā as "expert; specialist". Korean 전문가 and Cantonese zyun1 mun4 gaa1 match.

It is built from [専門](専門.md), "specialty", and 家 "a person devoted to a field", as in [哲学家](哲学家.md) and [心理学家](心理学家.md).
