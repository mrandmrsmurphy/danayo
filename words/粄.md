## Notes
- [粄](../characters/粄%20(char).md) "rice cake" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

粄 names a cake or dumpling made of glutinous rice flour rather than wheat, distinguishing it from the wheat-based [[餅]] — the same basic distinction as 米 ("uncooked rice") versus 麦/麺 in the vault's staple-food vocabulary. It survives today chiefly in Hakka (pán) and Cantonese (bun2) cooking terms for steamed rice cakes, and is first attested in the *Yupian* (玉篇, Southern and Northern Dynasties) rather than any earlier classical source.

粄 fills what would otherwise be a permanently empty Dan'a'yo syllable slot (ㄅㄧㄇ/bim/빔): bilabial-initial characters essentially never combine with the -im/-ip rhyme class anywhere in real Middle Chinese, so no organically-derived character could ever land here. See [[characters/粄 (char)|粄's character page]] for the full phonological rationale — this is a deliberate exception, not the character's organic reading.
