## Notes
- [拠](../characters/拠.md) "depend upon" + [点](../characters/点%20(char).md) "point" — no stand-in relationship applies (拠's own stand-in is [[依拠]]; 点's own is itself).

拠点 means base or foothold.

Mandarin jùdiǎn, Cantonese geoi3 dim2, Japanese きょてん, Korean 거점, and Vietnamese cứ điểm (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `羅馬字` had a typo (gyudem→gyodem, matching 拠's own citation "gyo"). No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼄ㄉㄝㄇ unique across both words and characters).
