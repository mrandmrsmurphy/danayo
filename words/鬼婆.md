## Notes
- [鬼](../characters/鬼.md) "devil, demon" + [婆](../characters/婆.md) "old woman" — no `#cranberry`: 鬼's own `stand_in` is [[鬼神]], 婆's is [[婆婆]]; neither points here.

Japanese おにばば (oni-baba, "demon hag") is a native kun'yomi compound and a genuine folklore figure (cf. Onibaba, Yamamba), rather than a Sino on'yomi reading. Mandarin guǐpó, Cantonese gwai2po4, Korean 귀파, and Sino-Vietnamese quỷ bà are the compositional readings.
