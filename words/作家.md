## Notes
- [作 (char)](../characters/作%20(char).md) "make" + [家](../characters/家.md) "house; specialist"

作家 is a writer or author, especially of literary works; in Japanese also an artist or craftsperson. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin zuòjiā as "author; writer" and Japanese さっか as "writer". Korean 작가 is the same word. Cantonese zok3 gaa1 matches.

The writer of a single work is the [作者](作者.md), and the writer's output is the [作品](作品.md). Writing as an art is [文学](文学.md).
