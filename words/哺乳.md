## Etymology

## Notes
- "To breastfeed, suckle, nurse" — the root of 哺乳動物/哺乳類 "mammal" (literally "milk-suckling animal"). `vietnamese: bú nhũ` is compositional from 哺's own bú reading (which coincidentally already carries "to suckle" natively in Vietnamese) + 乳's nhũ; no independent attestation of the full two-syllable compound was found — everyday Vietnamese uses the native verb bú/cho bú alone, and the concept of "mammal" is expressed with the fully native động vật có vú ("animal with breasts") rather than a Sino-Vietnamese calque of 哺乳 (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 보누 diverges entirely from Sino-Korean 포유.
