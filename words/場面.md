## Notes
- [場](../characters/場.md) "place; field" + [面](../characters/面.md) "face; surface"

場面 is a scene: one part of a play or film, or a moment of a story. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin chǎngmiàn as "scene" and Japanese ばめん as "scene (in a TV show, play, etc.)". Korean 장면 is the same word. Cantonese coeng4 min6-2 matches.

A play is divided into scenes, each set on the [舞台](舞台.md), and a [演技](演技.md), performance, is judged in them.
