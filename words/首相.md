## Notes
- [首](../characters/首.md) "head" + [相](../characters/相.md) "minister"

首相 is the prime minister. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 首相 (Mandarin shǒuxiàng, Cantonese sau2 soeng3) as "prime minister" and, historical in the Song dynasty, "highest level chancellor". It lists Japanese しゅしょう, Korean 수상 and Vietnamese entries. Compare [総理](総理.md), premier, and [宰相](宰相.md), chancellor.
