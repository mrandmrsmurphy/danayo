## Notes
- [指](../characters/指.md) "finger; toe" + [甲 (char)](../characters/甲%20(char).md) "armor" — the hard covering at a fingertip, "nail," by extension from 甲's own "armor, hard shell" sense.

English Wiktionary gives three Mandarin readings: zhǐjia (standard in Mainland and Taiwan), zhǐjiǎ (standard in Taiwan) and zhījia (a colloquial variant in Mainland China), with the senses "fingernail" and "toenail".

The compositional Korean reading 지갑 (jigap) happens to coincide with the everyday Korean word 지갑 "wallet", which has unrelated hanja. Korean does not use 指甲 as a word; the standard term for fingernail is the native 손톱. Japanese likewise does not use 指甲 as an independent compound (the standard word is 爪, tsume); the field しこう (shikō) is the compositional Sino-Japanese reading and not a claim of real usage. 甲's own `japanese_native` list includes つめ as one of its kun'yomi. Vietnamese chỉ giáp is the Sino-Vietnamese reading; the Korean, Japanese and Vietnamese usage remarks here are from memory, not re-checked.

The only other word with this 注音 (ㄐㄧㄜㄍㄚㄆ/jiǝgab/즤갑) is [[自家]] "one's own family/household", as the callout above says (checked 2026-10-13).
