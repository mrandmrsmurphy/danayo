## Notes
- [万](../characters/万.md) "ten thousand" + [象](../characters/象.md) "sign, phenomenon" — no `#cranberry`: 万's own `stand_in` is [[一万]], 象's is [[大象]]; neither points here.

Mandarin wànxiàng, Cantonese maan6 zoeng6, Japanese ばんしょう, Korean 만상, and Sino-Vietnamese vạn tượng are all standard compositional readings — part of the same family as [[森羅万象]] ("all things in the universe"). **Fixed a real semantic-mismatch bug**: `vietnamese` was capitalized `Vạn Tượng`, which is actually the Vietnamese name for Vientiane, the capital of Laos — a real but completely unrelated proper noun coincidentally built from the same two syllables. Corrected to lowercase `vạn tượng`, matching 象's own stored reading and this word's actual "all things, every manifestation" sense.
