## Notes
- [万](../characters/万.md) "ten thousand" + [象](../characters/象.md) "sign, phenomenon" — no `#cranberry`: 万's own `stand_in` is [[一万]], 象's is [[大象]]; neither points here.

Mandarin wànxiàng, Cantonese maan6 zoeng6, Japanese ばんしょう, Korean 만상, and Sino-Vietnamese vạn tượng are all standard compositional readings — part of the same family as [[森羅万象]] ("all things in the universe").
