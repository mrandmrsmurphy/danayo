## Notes
- [愛 (char)](../characters/愛%20(char).md) "love" + [男](../characters/男.md) "man" — literally "love-man."

愛男 is a Dan'a'yo-original coinage, not an attested compound in any of Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, Korean, or Vietnamese. It was minted by fiat specifically to disambiguate "boyfriend" (an unmarried, romantic-but-not-marital male partner) from [[愛人]], which already exists but carries genuinely contradictory senses across the CJKV sphere — standard "spouse" in mainland Mandarin, "lover" (sometimes illicit) in Taiwan/Hong Kong Mandarin, "mistress/affair partner" in Japanese, and neutral "boyfriend/girlfriend" in Korean and Vietnamese. 愛男 sidesteps that mess with a gender-specific, semantically transparent compound, paired with [[愛女]] for "girlfriend."

All five cross-linguistic readings are compositional, built from 愛's and 男's own stored citations rather than independently attested — the same convention used for every other Dan'a'yo word, coined or classical. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 애남 matches Sino-Korean 애남 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄧㄋㄚㄇ unique among both words and characters).
