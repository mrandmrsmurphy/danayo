## Notes
- [養](../characters/養.md) "foster, raise" + [殖](../characters/殖.md) "breed, spawn, reproduce, increase" — no `#cranberry`: 養's own `stand_in` is [[養育]], 殖's is [[繁殖]]; neither points here.

養殖 (yǎngzhí) is the standard Mandarin word for "aquaculture, breeding," and Cantonese 養殖 (joeng5zik6) likewise. Japanese ようしょく (yōshoku) and Korean 양식 (yangsik) are both standard terms in their languages. Sino-Vietnamese dưỡng thực is compositionally straightforward but not independently attested — modern Vietnamese instead uses the native nuôi trồng (or fuller nuôi trồng thủy sản, "aquaculture").
