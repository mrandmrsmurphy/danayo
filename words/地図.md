## Notes
- [地](../characters/地%20(char).md) "ground" + [図](../characters/図.md) "diagram" — no stand-in relationship applies (地 is its own stand-in; 図's own is [[図表]]).

地図 means a map.

Japanese ちず (rendaku ト→ズ), Korean 지도, and Vietnamese địa đồ are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄧㄜㄉㄛ unique across both words and characters).
