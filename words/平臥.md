## Notes
- [平](../characters/平.md) "flat, level" + [臥 (char)](../characters/臥%20(char).md) "lie down"

平臥 is "to lie supine, to lie flat," the classical-order word for what modern Chinese says 躺平 (lie-flat). According to Wiktionary, Chinese 平臥 (píngwò, Cantonese ping4 ngo6) means "to lie supine." Wiktionary has no Japanese, Korean or Vietnamese section, so `japanese` へいが, `korean` 평와 and `vietnamese` bình ngoạ are compositional readings of the two characters, not attested words. Dan'a'yo puts the modifier before the verb, 平 "flat" + 臥 "lie down," where Chinese 躺平 puts the verb first with a resultative complement; this is the form used in the title 平臥即是正義 of the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [平臥即是正義](../translation/平臥即是正義.md). Related: [臥](臥.md) "lie down" and [即是](即是.md).
