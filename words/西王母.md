## Notes
- [西](../characters/西.md) "west" + [王 (char)](../characters/王%20(char).md) "king" + [母](../characters/母.md) "mother" — the royal mother of the west.

西王母 is the Queen Mother of the West, one of the oldest and greatest goddesses of Chinese myth. She lives on Mount Kunlun in the far west (or by the Jade Pool, 瑤池), and keeps the peaches of immortality, which ripen once in three thousand years, and the elixir of immortality. In the earliest text, the *Classic of Mountains and Seas* (山海経), she is a fierce figure with a tiger's teeth and a leopard's tail who presides over plague and punishment. Later writers, and Daoism after them, turned her into a gracious queen and the chief of the female immortals. King Mu of Zhou is said to have visited her. In the myth of [常娥](常娥.md) it is she who gives the archer 羿 the elixir that his wife then takes, and in *Journey to the West* her Peach Banquet is the feast that Sun Wukong ruins. The short form 王母 is also common.

The three Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese forms given here are the compositional readings of the characters, which agree with the names in actual use (the Korean follows the North Korean readings). The account of her is written from memory and has not been checked online.
