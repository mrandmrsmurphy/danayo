## Notes
- [野](../characters/野.md) "wild" + [心 (char)](../characters/心%20(char).md) "heart"

野心 is ambition: a strong wish to rise to power, fame or success, and in Chinese sometimes the ambition that oversteps. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yěxīn as "wild ambition or schemes; careerism" and Japanese やしん as "ambition". Korean 야심, Vietnamese dã tâm and Cantonese je5 sam1 match.

It is a wish with a goal, where [願望](願望.md) is a wish alone, and the Chinese reading often leans to the [陰謀](陰謀.md), plot. The drive it gives is [意欲](意欲.md), motivation.
