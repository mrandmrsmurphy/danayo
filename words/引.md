## Notes
- [引 (char)](../characters/引%20(char).md) "pull; tug" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [引 (char)](../characters/引%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

引 means to pull or draw — a 会意 character depicting a slack bow with a stroke marking where the bowstring is drawn. Extremely productive as a compound-forming element: [[引出]] "to lead out, extract," [[引入]] "to lead into," 牽引 "traction, towing," 割引 "discount."

Japanese いん, Korean 인, and Vietnamese dẫn (the character's own field also lists several Nôm variants — dận, giận, etc.) are all standard readings. **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 引 and [[銀]] ("silver") share an identical reading ('in/인/ㄧㄋ) — added reciprocal homophone callouts to both pages.
