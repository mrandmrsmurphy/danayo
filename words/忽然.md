## Notes
- [忽](../characters/忽.md) "sudden" + [然 (char)](../characters/然%20(char).md) "so" — `characters/忽.md`'s own `stand_in` points to this word (忽然), meaning 忽 is a bound morpheme that never appears independently outside this compound; 然's own is bare 然.

Mandarin hūrán, Cantonese fat1 jin4, Japanese こつぜん, and Korean 홀연 are the standard readings. The `vietnamese` field is omitted: no standalone compound "hốt nhiên" is attested, and Vietnamese expresses "suddenly" with the entirely different native constructions bỗng nhiên and đột nhiên.
