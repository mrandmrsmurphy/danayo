## Notes
- [鳥 (char)](../characters/鳥%20(char).md) "bird" + [籠 (char)](../characters/籠%20(char).md) "basket, cage" — no `#cranberry`: both 鳥's and 籠's own `stand_in` point to themselves; neither points here.

Mandarin niǎolóng and Cantonese niu5lung4 keep the compositional Sino reading. Japanese とりかご ("bird-basket") and Korean 새장 ("bird-box") are both native compounds rather than Sino readings. Vietnamese lồng chim ("cage-bird") reverses the Sino word order — a native noun-then-modifier pattern, joining the vault's small set of documented Vietnamese word-order-reversal cases (cf. [[限定詞]], [[除外]], [[駐屯]]).
