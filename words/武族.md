## Notes
- [武](../characters/武%20(char).md) "martial, warlike" + [族](../characters/族.md) "tribe, clan" — "the Warring Tribe, Gad." Their own `stand_in` values: 武's is itself; 族's is [[家族]]; none points here, so 武族 is an independent compound.

武族 is the tribe of Gad, the first son of Leah's maid Zilpah, whose name means "good fortune." It is a Dan'a'yo **neologism**, named for Jacob's blessing, "raiders shall raid Gad, but he shall raid at their heels" (Genesis 49:19), "Warring Tribe." Compare [[福族]] (Asher) and [[捷族]] (Naphtali). See [[基督敎]].

- Hebrew name: גָּד (Gad) (注音 ㄍㄚㄊ).
