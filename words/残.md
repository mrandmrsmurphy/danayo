## Notes
- [残 (char)](../characters/残%20(char).md) "remaining, not broken off; to injure" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

残 combines 歹 ("bones, vicious, death") with phonetic 戔, its core sense splitting two ways: "remaining, leftover" (what's left after something is used up or broken away) and, via the 歹 semantic component, "to injure, damage, maim" (as in [残害](残害.md), "damage, harm"). Both senses are genuinely attested and productive across the CJKV sphere, not a vault-specific broadening.

Mandarin cán, Cantonese caan4, and Korean 잔 (jan) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese ざん (zan, on'yomi) is likewise compositional and productive in compounds (残念 zannen, "regrettable"; 残業 zangyō, "overtime work"); the native kun'yomi のこ(る) (noko(ru), "to remain," already 残's own stored `japanese_native` value) is the everyday verb. Vietnamese tàn is the standard Hán Việt reading, attested in tàn tật ("disabled, maimed," the "injure" sense) and tàn dư ("remnant, vestige," the "leftover" sense).
