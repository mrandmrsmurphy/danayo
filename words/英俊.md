## Notes
- [英](../characters/英.md) "hero" is separately legitimized by [[英雄]]. [俊](../characters/俊.md) "outstanding talent" is separately legitimized by [[俊傑]]. No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

英俊 combines 英 ("hero") with 俊 ("outstanding talent") — "wise, heroic, handsome, talented."

**Found and fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字`/`諺文` had a wrong final consonant on 俊's syllable (engjung/엉중, -ng instead of -n) — `注音` (ㄝㄫㄐㄨㄋ) had already stayed correct throughout — corrected to 'engjun/엉준. `japanese` had ひでとし (Hidetoshi), a common Japanese *given name* reading of these same characters, rather than the real word reading — corrected to えいしゅん (eishun). Filled blank pos and vietnamese (anh tuấn, an extremely common real Vietnamese word for "handsome"). Fixed cantonese's stray space. Korean 영준 (yeongjun) was already correct — itself also commonly a Korean given name, a genuine coincidence unrelated to the japanese bug just fixed.
