## Etymology
- [釈](../characters/釈.md) "explain, interpret" + [放](../characters/放.md) "release"

## Notes
Mandarin shìfàng, cantonese sik1 fong3, japanese しゃくほう, and korean 석방 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese thích phóng is the honest compositional reading (釈's own thích + 放's own phóng); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `釋放` is the standard traditional-character spelling variant (釈→釋), correctly listed as an alias.

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 放's own `stand_in` is this exact word (釈放), so 放 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 釈放; 釈's own `stand_in` is instead 解釈, so this word does not legitimize 釈.

Earlier Notes on this page may state a different value or a different reason.
