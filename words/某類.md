## Notes
- [某 (char)](../characters/某%20(char).md) "some; a certain; indefinite" + [類](../characters/類.md) "kind; sort; category; type"

某類 is the indefinite kind/category correlative — "some kind; a certain type; an unspecified sort." Like all 某-row entries, the category identity is deliberately withheld: 某類の問題が発生した ("a problem of a certain kind arose"); 某類に分類されている ("it is classified under a certain category"). This covers both genuine epistemic uncertainty (the speaker doesn't know the category) and deliberate vagueness (the type is known but not disclosed).

Mandarin 某類 (mǒu lèi, "a certain kind/category") is said on this page to match an attested Mandarin form directly (not re-checked against a source) — 某 + 類 are both well-established classical Chinese morphemes and compose naturally. Japanese ある種 (aru shu, "a certain kind/type; some sort of") is the standard indefinite category word: ある種の問題 ("a problem of a certain kind"); ある種の人 ("a certain type of person"). Korean 어떤 종류 (eotteon jongnyu, "a certain kind/type; some sort") is the standard form.

某類 completes the 某 row for the category domain, adding an indefinite kind-pronoun alongside 某人 (someone), 某事 (something), 某物 (some object), 某処 (somewhere), 某時 (sometime), 某様 (somehow), 某多 (some amount), and 某名 (a certain honoured person). Together the 某 row provides Dan'a'yo with a complete paradigm of indefinite pronouns across every semantic dimension in the correlative table.
The Grammar page sets 某類 in its correlative table, in the 類 ("kind") column and the 某 ("a certain") row, beside [某事](某事.md), [某物](某物.md), [某人](某人.md) and [某処](某処.md). 類 is not bound to this word: its `stand_in` is 種類. The word is tagged `neologism`, so no compositional readings were added; its `japanese`, `korean` and `vietnamese` fields hold the spoken equivalents (ある種, 어떤 종류, một loại nào đó) rather than Sino-Xenic readings, as on the sibling correlative pages. `kwin` is false because both characters carry `kwin: false` (某 Dan'a'yo 못 against Korean 모, 類 뤼 against 류). The example sentences are illustrative constructions, not attested quotations.
