## Notes
- [県](../characters/県%20(char).md) "county, district, prefecture, subdivision" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 県 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

県 names a mid-level administrative subdivision. Its meaning and rank vary by country: in Mandarin xiàn (县/縣) it is a county, a subdivision of a prefecture or province; in Japanese 県 (ken) it is a prefecture, the top-level subdivision of Japan (alongside 都/道/府 for Tokyo, Hokkaido, and Osaka/Kyoto); in Korean 현 (hyeon) it named a historical county-level unit under earlier Korean dynasties, now largely obsolete in the modern administrative system; and in Vietnamese huyện it is a district, a subdivision of a province.

The character itself originally meant "to hang" — a head suspended by a string from a tree, per its own component parts — a sense now written with the derived character 懸; the administrative "county/prefecture" meaning is a separate borrowing, explaining the otherwise puzzling composition of the character for a governmental unit.
