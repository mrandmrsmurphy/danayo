## Notes
- [魶 (char)](../characters/魶%20(char).md) "giant salamander" — 魶 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

**Fixed a real bug**: `mandarin` was `dàní`, a two-syllable reading belonging to 大鯢 (the standard modern Mandarin term for "giant salamander") rather than to 魶 itself — corrected to nà, 魶's own single-character reading per the character page. 魶 is obsolete in Standard Chinese for all its senses (giant salamander, whale, fish eagle); modern Mandarin universally uses 大鯢 instead. Japanese おおさんしょううお (ōsanshōuo, native) is the standard everyday word; the character's own on'yomi (NOU, DOU) has no attested standalone Sino-Japanese use for this sense. No attested Vietnamese reading (the character page's own field is explicitly marked ø).
