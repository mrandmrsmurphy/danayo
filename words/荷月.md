## Notes
- [荷](../characters/荷.md) "luggage" is separately legitimized by [[荷物]]. [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month, moon" is separately legitimized by [[月]] itself. No cranberry.

Sixth month of the ancient Chinese poetic calendar. The lotus (荷花) blooms at the height of summer, rising pure and fragrant from muddy water. In Buddhist and Daoist imagery the lotus represents purity amid impurity — an apt symbol for the sweltering heart of summer.

Pronunciation fields (ha'wed/하웓/ㄏㄚ·⼔ㄊ) are the straightforward concatenation of each constituent's own stored reading, including the null-onset syllable break — no bug; `kwin: false` already correct. Mandarin héyuè, cantonese ho4jyut6 (space removed), and korean 하월 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese はすつき reads both halves natively (荷's classical "lotus" kun'yomi はす + 月's kun'yomi つき "month") rather than the Sino-Japanese on'yomi concatenation かげつ, matching the established poetic-month convention (see [[桃月]], [[榴月]], [[杏月]], [[桂月]], [[槐月]]). Vietnamese tháng hà likewise uses the native *tháng* plus 荷's own Sino-Vietnamese name hà, the same pattern. Removed the redundant `品詞` duplicate of `pos`. No homophones.
