## Notes
- [橘](characters/橘%20(char).md) "tangerine" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 橘 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

橘 means tangerine.

Mandarin jú, Cantonese gwat1, Japanese きつ, Korean 귤, and Vietnamese quất (the primary form among the character's own comma-joined vietnamese variants quít/quất/quầng/quắt) are all standard, fully compositional readings.
