## Notes
- [草](../characters/草.md) "grass" + [屋](../characters/屋.md) "roof; house"

草屋 is "a thatched hut," a humble dwelling roofed and walled with grass or straw. According to Wiktionary, Chinese 草屋 (cǎowū, Cantonese cou2 uk1) is "thatched hut; thatched cottage," also "a barn for storing hay," and Japanese 草屋 (くさや, a kun'yomi reading) is "thatched hut." Wiktionary has no Korean or Vietnamese section, so `japanese` そうおく, `korean` 초옥 and `vietnamese` thảo ốc are compositional readings. It is used in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [平臥即是正義](../translation/平臥即是正義.md), in 草屋 於 寝 "sleep in a thatched hut," standing in for the Chinese 草窝 "grass nest." Related: [草](草.md) "grass," [屋](屋.md) "roof" and [住宅](住宅.md) "residence."
