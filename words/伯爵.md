## Notes
- [伯](../characters/伯.md) "count-rank" + [爵](../characters/爵.md) "noble title" — "earl, count," the third rank of the traditional East Asian five-rank peerage system (公侯伯子男). `vietnamese: bá tước` is the standard, widely-used Vietnamese term for this nobility rank, a direct compositional match. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 박작 diverges from Sino-Korean 백작 via the AND-rule, since 伯's own field independently diverges (despite 爵 itself being `kwin: true`).
