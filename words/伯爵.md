## Notes
- [伯](../characters/伯.md) "count-rank" + [爵](../characters/爵.md) "noble title" — "earl, count," the third rank of the traditional East Asian five-rank peerage system (公侯伯子男). `vietnamese: bá tước` is the standard, widely-used Vietnamese term for this nobility rank, a direct compositional match.
