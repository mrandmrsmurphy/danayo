## Notes
- [風](../characters/風%20(char).md) "wind" + [水](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 風 and 水's own stand-ins are themselves, each with its own independent word page).

風水 means feng shui (the geomantic practice).

Mandarin fēngshuǐ, Cantonese fung1 seoi2, Japanese ふうすい, Korean 풍수, and Vietnamese phong thủy are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 風's and 水's own stored citations exactly). Simplified `mandarin` (dropped the extra neutral-tone variant fēngshui), removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. Discovered a genuine homophone with [[楓樹]] (both 注音 ㄈㄨㄫㄙㄨ, 諺文 뿡수) — cross-referenced both pages.
