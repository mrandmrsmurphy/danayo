## Notes
- [孟](../characters/孟.md) "eldest, first" + [子](../characters/子.md) "master, philosopher" — no stand-in relationship applies (孟 is proper-noun-only with no specific stand-in; 子's own is [[児子]]).

孟子 means Mencius — the honorific title of the Confucian philosopher Meng Ke, following the same "master" suffix pattern as [[孔子]]/[[老子]]/[[荘子]].

Mandarin Mèngzǐ, Cantonese maang6 zi2, Japanese もうし, Korean 맹자, and Vietnamese Mạnh Tử are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Fixed**: `羅馬字` had 子's syllable mistyped ji→jǝ; `pos` was 名詞 — corrected to 固有名詞, matching sibling philosopher names. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄚㄫㄐㄜ unique across both words and characters).
