## Notes
- [反](../characters/反%20(char).md) "reverse, in return" + [映](../characters/映%20(char).md) "reflect, project" — no stand-in relationship applies (both characters are independent Dan'a'yo entries, each its own stand-in).

反映 means to reflect or indicate — mirroring or revealing an underlying reality (世相を反映する "to reflect the state of society").

Japanese はんえい, Korean 반영, and Vietnamese phản ánh are all standard, fully compositional readings.
