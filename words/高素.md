## Notes
- [高 (char)](characters/高%20(char).md) "high" + [素](characters/素.md) "element"
- Gallium is named after Gallia/Gaul, the old name for France. It is spelled [[高盧]] and clipped here.
- Avoids the Chinese element character 鎵/镓.

**Form.** `mandarin`/`cantonese` above (jiā/gaa1) give the readings of the avoided standard element character 鎵/镓, not a compositional reading of 高素 — confirmed via reasoning from the character's phono-semantic construction (metal radical + phonetic 家, jiā/gaa1), per the same convention established for [[巨金]]/[[惰素]]/[[燐素]] this sweep. `korean`/`japanese`/`vietnamese` (갈륨/ガリウム/gali) are the loanword names for the element itself.

This word shares its reading (gauso/갓소/ㄍㄚㄨㄙㄛ) with [[告訴]] ("to tell, inform"), still unperfected and awaiting its own turn in this sweep.
