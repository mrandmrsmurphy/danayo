## Notes
- [焦 (char)](../characters/焦%20(char).md) "burned, scorched" + [点 (char)](../characters/点%20(char).md) "point" — no stand-in relationship applies (both are their own stand-ins).

焦点 means focus, focal point: the point where rays converge, and so by extension the centre of attention.

Mandarin jiāodiǎn, Cantonese ziu1 dim2, Japanese しょうてん, Korean 초점 and Vietnamese tiêu điểm are all standard, fully compositional readings.
