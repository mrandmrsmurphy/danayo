## Etymology
- [焦 (char)](../characters/焦%20(char).md) "burned, scorched" + [点 (char)](../characters/点%20(char).md) "point" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 焦's and 点's own stand-ins are themselves).

焦点 means focus, focal point.

Mandarin jiāodiǎn, Cantonese ziu1 dim2, Japanese しょうてん (SHOU+TEN), and Korean 초점 were already correct and fully compositional. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with tiêu điểm (焦's own tiêu + 点's own điểm), also the real, standard Vietnamese term for "focus." Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄛㄨㄉㄝㄇ unique across both words and characters).
