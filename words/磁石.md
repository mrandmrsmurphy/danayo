## Notes
- [磁](../characters/磁.md) "magnetism, magnetic" + [石 (char)](../characters/石%20(char).md) "stone" — magnet.

磁石 names a magnet, and historically the natural magnet, lodestone (magnetite). 磁's own `stand_in` is [[磁性]] (the property), so 磁石 supplies the concrete object: the property is 磁性 and the thing that has it is 磁石. Mandarin císhí, Cantonese ci4 sek6, Japanese じしゃく and Korean 자석 are the standard readings; Mandarin also uses 磁铁 (cítiě) for a magnet, but 磁石 is the compound shared across Mandarin, Japanese and Korean. Compare [[磁金]], the element neodymium, named for the strong permanent magnets made from it.
