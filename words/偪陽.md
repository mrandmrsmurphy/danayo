## Notes
- [偪](../characters/偪.md) "Fuyang" + [陽 (char)](../characters/陽%20(char).md) "shine; sun" — an ancient Spring and Autumn period state located in present-day Shandong. `vietnamese: Phúc Dương` is the honest compositional reading (偪's own phúc + 陽's own dương, capitalized as a proper noun); no independently attested Vietnamese form for this name was found.

Legitimizing note: 偪's own `stand_in` is this exact word (偪陽), so 偪 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 偪陽.
