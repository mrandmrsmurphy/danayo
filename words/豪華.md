## Notes
- [豪](../characters/豪.md) "magnificent, bold" is legitimized by this exact compound (its own stand-in). [華](../characters/華.md) "gorgeous, splendid" is separately legitimized by [[華美]]. No cranberry.

Mandarin háohuá, japanese ごうか, korean 호화, and vietnamese hào hoa (all also the real common words for "luxurious, extravagant") confirmed standard and compositional.
## Etymology
- [豪](../characters/豪.md) "magnificent, bold" + [華](../characters/華.md) "gorgeous, splendid"
