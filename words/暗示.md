## Notes
- [暗](../characters/暗%20(char).md) "dark" + [示](../characters/示.md) "show, reveal, indicate" — no stand-in relationship applies (暗's own stand-in is itself; 示's own is [[開示]]).

暗示 means to suggest or hint.

Mandarin ànshì, Cantonese am3 si6, Japanese あんじ, Korean 암시, and Vietnamese ám thị are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `cantonese` had the wrong tone (am4→am3). No homophones (注音 ㄚㄇㄍㄝ unique across both words and characters).
