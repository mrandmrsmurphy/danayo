## Notes
- [閲](../characters/閲.md) "inspect, review" + [読](../characters/読.md) "read" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes 読 as a bound character (閲's own stand-in is [[検閲]]; transitivity fails, so no `#cranberry`).

閲読 means to read, particularly to examine or peruse a text closely rather than skim it. Mandarin 閱讀/阅读 (yuèdú) is the standard, everyday word for "to read" in Chinese, spanning both casual and formal registers. Korean 열독 (yeoldok) and Sino-Vietnamese duyệt đọc both carry the same core "read" meaning, though somewhat more formally than the plainer native alternatives (읽다 in Korean; đọc alone in Vietnamese).

Japanese 閲読 (えつどく) is comparatively narrow and formal: it specifically implies reading while examining or investigating a text's content for a purpose (e.g. 古文書を閲読する, "to read and examine old documents"), distinct from casual reading (読む) and from the related terms 閲覧 (etsuran, "to view/browse while inspecting") and 校閲 (kōetsu, "proofreading/editing").
