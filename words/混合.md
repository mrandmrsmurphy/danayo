## Notes
- [混](../characters/混.md) "mix, confuse, blend, bumble" + [合 (char)](../characters/合%20(char).md) "join, fit together, combine" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 混 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (合's own stand-in is itself, so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

混合 means to bundle, mix, blend.

Mandarin hùnhé, Cantonese wan6 hap6, Japanese こんごう, and Korean 혼합 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 混's and 合's own stored citations exactly). Vietnamese hỗn hợp (混's own hỗn + 合's own hợp) is also the real, standard Vietnamese term for "mixture." Fixed a malformed comma-joined single-string `vietnamese` value on 合's own character page (`"hợp, cáp, cóp, góp, gộp, họp, hạp"` → a proper seven-item list) while verifying this word's composition. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄛㄋㄎㄚㄆ unique across both words and characters).
