## Notes
- [月](../characters/月%20(char).md) "moon" + [曜日](../words/曜日.md) ("day of the week," itself [曜](../characters/曜.md) "of the week" + [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day") — no stand-in relationship applies (both 月 and 日's own stand-ins are themselves; this compound is itself the stand-in for 曜).
- [Calendar](../lexipedia/Calendar.md)

月曜日 means Monday.

Mandarin yuèyàorì, Cantonese jyut6 jiu6 jat6, Japanese げつようび, and Korean 월요일 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese uses the real native weekday name thứ hai ("the second [day]"), not a Sino-Vietnamese compound — matching the established convention on sibling compounds [[日曜日]] (Chủ nhật), [[火曜日]] (thứ ba), and [[水曜日]] (thứ tư), since Vietnamese does not name weekdays compositionally from the character readings.
