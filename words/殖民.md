## Notes
- [殖](../characters/殖.md) "breed, multiply" + [民](../characters/民.md) "people, citizen" — the settling and rule of a foreign land by transplanted population, i.e. colonization. Forms 殖民統治 ("colonial rule"); the "colony" sense itself is [[植民地]] (spelled with 植 "to plant," this vault's chosen form — 殖民地 is kept there as an equally-attested alias spelling).
