## Notes
- [高 (char)](../characters/高%20(char).md) "high" + [興 (char)](../characters/興%20(char).md) "excite, arouse" — no `#cranberry`: both 高's and 興's own `stand_in` point to themselves; neither points here.

高興 is the calm, everyday "glad, pleased" sense — a platonic counterpart to [[興奮]] ("excited, aroused"), which carries a more intense or charged connotation. Mandarin gāoxìng, Cantonese gou1hing3, Korean 고흥, and Sino-Vietnamese cao hứng are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uniquely lacks this compound as an attested word — despite 興's own character page listing viable on'yomi (KYOU, KOU), 高興 isn't used this way in Japanese, which instead reaches for native 嬉しい (ureshii) for "happy, glad."
