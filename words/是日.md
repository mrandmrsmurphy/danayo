## Notes
- [是](../characters/是%20(char).md) "equal, this" + [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 是 and 日's own stand-ins are themselves).
- This is an archaic expression for [[今日]], cp. English "that self-same day."

Mandarin shìrì, Cantonese si6 jat6, Japanese ぜにち, Korean 시일, and Vietnamese thị nhật are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄋㄧㄊ unique across both words and characters).
