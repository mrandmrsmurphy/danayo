## Notes
- [玄](../characters/玄%20(char).md) "deep, dark" + [米](../characters/米%20(char).md) "rice" + [茶](../characters/茶%20(char).md) "tea" — literally "dark rice tea"; no stand-in relationship applies (玄's and 米's own stand-ins are themselves; 茶's own is itself).

玄米茶 means genmaicha, Japanese brown rice tea.

Mandarin xuánmǐchá, Cantonese jyun4 mai5 caa4, Japanese げんまいちゃ (GEN+MAI+CHA), and Korean 현미차 are all fully compositional, matching 玄's, 米's, and 茶's own stored citations exactly.
