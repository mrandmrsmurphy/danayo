## Notes
- [穿 (char)](../characters/穿%20(char).md) "penetrate, pierce, drill" + [孔 (char)](../characters/孔%20(char).md) "cavity" + [机](../characters/机.md) "machine" — none of the three characters' own `stand_in` points here, so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both 孔 and 机 are individually `kwin: false`.

穿孔机 names a machine that punches or drills holes — a hole puncher, perforator, or boring machine, used in contexts ranging from office paper punches to industrial drilling equipment.

Mandarin chuānkǒngjī, Cantonese cyun1 hung2 gei1, Japanese せんこうき (senkōki), and Korean 천공기 (cheongonggi) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese xuyên khổng cơ is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form; everyday Vietnamese more often uses the native máy đục lỗ ("hole-drilling machine").
