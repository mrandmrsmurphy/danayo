## Notes
- [撃](../characters/撃%20(char).md) "strike, hit, beat" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [撃](../characters/撃%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

撃 means to strike, hit, or beat.

Mandarin jī, Cantonese gik1, Japanese げき, and Korean 격 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bugs: literal `vietnamese: null` and `korean: "null"` — vietnamese corrected to kích (the well-attested standard reading, as in 攻撃 công kích, though notably absent from the character's own stored `vietnamese` field, currently blank — a gap worth a future character-level check). Homophone of [[劇]] "drama; theatre" (both 注音 ㄍㄝㄎ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words; checked two other candidates sharing this reading (戟/激) and confirmed neither has an independent word at this exact reading (戟 is name-only, 激 bound to [[刺激]]).
