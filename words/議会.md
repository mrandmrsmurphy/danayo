## Notes
- [議](../characters/議.md) "debate" + [会 (char)](../characters/会%20(char).md) "meet"

議会 is an assembly, a legislature. It is spelled with the Dan'a'yo forms of the characters, and 議會 and 议会 are aliases. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 議會 (Mandarin yìhuì, Cantonese ji5 wui6) as "parliament; legislative assembly" and Japanese ぎかい as "a legislature". It lists a Korean entry, 의회. Compare [国会](国会.md), the national assembly, and [会議](会議.md), a meeting.
