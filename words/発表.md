## Notes
- [発 (char)](../characters/発%20(char).md) "emit; send out" + [表](../characters/表.md) "show; indicate"

発表 (traditional 發表) is "to announce; to publish; to present," and "to express (an opinion)." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 發表 (fābiǎo, Cantonese faat3 biu2) means "to publish; to issue" and "to express an opinion; to state; to announce," Japanese はっぴょう has the same word, Korean 발표 is "presentation, announcement, release," and Vietnamese phát biểu is "to express; to formulate." It is used in the 1919 Korean Declaration of Independence ([宣言書](../translation/宣言書.md)) (意思를 快히 発表하라, "state your will freely"). Related: [表明](表明.md) "make clear."
