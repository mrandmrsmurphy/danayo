## Notes
- [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal; gold" + [箔](../characters/箔.md) "foil, thin sheet" — gold leaf.

金箔 is gold leaf, gold beaten to a sheet only a fraction of a micrometre thick, used for gilding temples, lacquerware, manuscripts and, in Japan, for edible garnish. It is possible only because gold has such extreme [[展性]]. Compare [[鍍金]], gilding by plating rather than leaf. Mandarin jīnbó, Japanese きんぱく and Korean 금박 are the standard readings.
