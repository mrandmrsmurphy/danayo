## Notes
- [熟 (char)](../characters/熟%20(char).md) "which"

熟 is which, the interrogative pronoun of the classical language. It is spelled with 熟 because 孰, its original form, is an alias of [熟 (char)](../characters/熟%20(char).md). English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 孰 (Mandarin shú, Cantonese suk6) as an "emphatic or interrogative pronoun" and Japanese 孰 as "which, who, how", and lists Korean and Vietnamese entries. The sense "ripe, cooked" is not this word's; it is carried by compounds such as [成熟](成熟.md), to ripen. Compare [何](何.md), what, and [誰](誰.md), who.
