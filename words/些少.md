## Notes
- [些](../characters/些.md) "a bit, a little" + [少 (char)](../characters/少%20(char).md) "few, scarce" — this word is the `stand_in` legitimizer for [[些]], which cannot appear independently. Both halves independently mean roughly the same thing, a synonym-doubling compound emphasizing smallness of quantity.

**Content bug found and fixed**: `japanese` had a malformed value, さ少しょう, mixing kana with a stray leftover kanji character — corrected to the clean reading さしょう (confirmed via web search). Vietnamese ta thiểu (compositional, 些's own confirmed reading ta + [[少]]'s own already-established thiểu) parallels the synonym-doubling structure directly. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 사솟 diverges from Sino-Korean 사소 in the second syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄚㄙㄛㄨ unique among both words and characters).
