>[!caution] The senses split — the right wing or flank, or the political right
>右翼 is the right wing of a body or army, the right flank. Chinese, Japanese and Korean also use it for the political right and for a right winger in sports, so a listener may hear the political sense. Listed as a Split on [文法 - 91疑義語](../grammar/文法%20-%2091疑義語.md).

## Notes
- [右](../characters/右.md) "right (stand-in: [[右側]])" + [翼](../characters/翼.md) "wing (stand-in: [[羽翼]])"

Wiktionary: Chinese 右翼, literally "right wing" and in politics "right wing", in sports "right-winger" and in the military "right flank"; Japanese 右翼 (うよく), politics, "right wing", sports "right wing", military "right flank"; Korean 우익 is the hanja form, "right wing". Its counterpart is [[左翼]].
