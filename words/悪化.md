## Notes
- [悪](../characters/悪%20(char).md) "bad, evil" + [化](../characters/化%20(char).md) "-ification" — no stand-in relationship applies (悪/化 are both their own stand-ins).

悪化 means to worsen, aggravate, or deteriorate.

Mandarin èhuà, Cantonese ok3 faa3, Japanese あっか, Korean 악화, and Vietnamese ác hoá are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `cantonese` had 悪's syllable mistyped ngok3→ok3 (confirmed against 悪's own citation and 6 sibling 悪-compounds). Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄎㄏ⺢ unique across both words and characters).
