## Notes
- **Genuine `#cranberry`**: both [薔](../characters/薔.md) and [薇](../characters/薇.md) — 薔 alone means "water pepper" and 薇 alone means "royal fern," neither meaning "rose" independently — have their own `stand_in` pointing to this exact compound (full transitivity; tag already correctly present).

Mandarin qiángwēi, korean 장미, and vietnamese tường vi are all standard, fully compositional, and genuinely attested.
