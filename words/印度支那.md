## Notes
- [印](../characters/印.md) "seal" + [度](../characters/度.md) "degree" + [支](../characters/支.md) "branch" + [那 (char)](../characters/那%20(char).md) "then" — a phonetic-loan compound, not semantically transparent from its individual characters. Built from [[印度]] ("India") + [[支那]] ("China" — see that word's own Notes for its historically pejorative register).

Mandarin Yìndù-Zhīnà, Cantonese jan3 dou6 zi1 naa4, Japanese インドシナ (Indoshina), Korean 인도지나, and Vietnamese Ấn Độ Chi Na are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 印度's and 支那's own stored citations exactly. `kwin` is false: of the four constituent characters, 印, 度, and 那 are each individually kwin-true, but 支 independently diverges from Korean on its own character page, which alone is enough to make the AND-rule false across the whole compound.

No homophones (注音 ㄧㄋㄉㄛㄐㄝㄋㄚ unique among both words and characters).
