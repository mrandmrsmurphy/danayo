## Notes
- [征](../characters/征.md) "campaign; conquer" + [服](../characters/服.md) "submit; subdue"

征服 is conquest: to bring a people or a land under one's rule by force. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin zhēngfú as "to conquer using military force; to subjugate" and, figuratively, "to captivate", and Japanese せいふく as "subjugation, conquest; to conquer". Vietnamese chinh phục is the same word, and Korean 정복 and Cantonese zing1 fuk6 match.

It is the whole act of making a place subject, where [征伐](征伐.md) is a punitive campaign and [占領](占領.md) is holding the ground taken.
