## Notes
- [膳](../characters/膳.md) "meal" + [食 (char)](../characters/食%20(char).md) "eat"

膳食 means meals, food and diet: what is served and eaten. English Wiktionary lists it only for Chinese (Mandarin shànshí, Cantonese sin6 sik6), glossed "meals; food; diet". It has no Japanese, Korean or Vietnamese entry, so Korean 선식 and Vietnamese thiện thực here are built from the characters, and no Japanese reading is recorded.

This word is 膳's own `stand_in`: the character cannot appear independently, so the compound pairs it with 食, "eat", to make the meaning explicit. It is close in sense to [[飲食]], food and drink.
