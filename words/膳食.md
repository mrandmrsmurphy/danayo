## Notes
- [膳](../characters/膳.md) "meal" + [食 (char)](../characters/食%20(char).md) "eat" — this word is 膳's own `stand_in`: it cannot appear independently, so the compound pairs it with 食 ("to eat") to make the meaning explicit. `vietnamese: thiện thực` is compositional (not independently attested via hvdic).
- Japanese omitted: 膳食 is not standard in Japanese (食事 is preferred).
