## Notes
- [膳](characters/膳.md) "meal" + [食 (char)](characters/食%20(char).md) "eat" — stand-in for [[膳]], which cannot appear independently; the compound pairs it with 食 ("to eat") to make the meaning explicit. `vietnamese: thiện thực` is compositional (not independently attested via hvdic).
- Japanese omitted: 膳食 is not standard in Japanese (食事 is preferred).
