## Notes
- [観](../characters/観.md) "watch" + [客](../characters/客.md) "guest"

観客 is a member of an audience: a spectator at a play, film, match or exhibition. Wiktionary glosses Japanese かんきゃく as "audience, the crowd". Korean 관객 is the reading of the characters. The Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the characters.

The audience as a body is the [聴衆](聴衆.md), and the act of attending is [観覧](観覧.md). The one who reads is the [読者](読者.md).
