## Notes
- [欧](../characters/欧.md) "Europe" + [圓](../characters/圓%20(char).md) "circle, round" (used in its "round object; monetary unit" sense) — no stand-in relationship applies (欧's own stand-in is [[欧羅巴]]; 圓's own is itself).

欧圓 means the Euro (currency).

Mandarin ōuyuán and Cantonese au1 jyun4 are the genuine, standard compositional Chinese term. Japanese ユーロ, Korean 유로, and Vietnamese euro are the real, standard loanword transliterations of "Euro" used in these languages — no compositional Sino-Japanese/Sino-Korean/Sino-Vietnamese term for this currency is in real-world use, kept as-is (the same pattern seen on [[板球]], [[果醤]], [[楊樹]], [[樹懶]], [[橄欖球]]).
