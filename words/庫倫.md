## Notes
- [庫](../characters/庫.md) "storehouse" + [倫](../characters/倫.md) "order, relationship" — a transcription of a Manchu and Mongolian word, so the characters carry no meaning of their own; no stand-in relationship applies (庫's own stand-in is [[倉庫]], 倫's is [[倫理]]). The alias 库伦 is the simplified spelling.

庫倫 is a proper noun with three senses at English Wiktionary. The etymology is a Qing-era transcription of Manchu ᡴᡠᡵᡝᠨ (kuren), borrowed from the oblique form of Middle Mongol ᠬᠦᠷᠢᠶ᠎ᠡ (küriye).

1. Ulan Bator, the capital of Mongolia ([[蒙古]]): a historical name, since the modern Chinese name is 烏蘭巴托, which is not a word in the vault.
2. Hure Banner, a banner of the prefecture-level city of Tongliao in Inner Mongolia, China.
3. As a common noun, an enclosed pasture; 圐圙 is a synonym and 草庫倫 a derived term. Neither is a word in the vault.

Mandarin Kùlún and Cantonese fu3 leon4 are as at English Wiktionary. Japanese ウランバートル and Korean 울란바토르 are the ordinary names of Ulan Bator (from memory, not re-checked), so `kwin` is false. The `vietnamese` field holds the compositional Hán-Việt reading kho luân (each character as its page gives it; not attested for this name).

No homophones (注音 ㄎㄛㄌㄨㄋ unique across words and characters, checked 2026-10-12). The 注音 needs no syllable-boundary dot.
