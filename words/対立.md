## Notes
- [対 (char)](../characters/対.md) "facing, opposite" (stand-in: [[反対]]) + [立 (char)](../characters/立%20(char).md) "stand" (stand-in: itself) — opposition, contrast — standing opposed to one another.

Wiktionary: Chinese 對立 "to oppose; to set something against; to be antagonistic to; opposition, conflict"; Japanese 対立 "opposition; conflict, hostility; (of two parties) to be in conflict"; Korean 대립 and Vietnamese đối lập are listed as readings of 對立 ("to stand in opposition"). Used in `文法 - 02音韻論` for 送気・不送気の対立 and 終声対立.
