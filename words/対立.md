## Notes
- [対 (char)](../characters/対.md) "facing, opposite" (stand-in: [[反対]]) + [立 (char)](../characters/立.md) "stand" (stand-in: itself) — opposition, contrast — standing opposed to one another.

対立 means opposition/contrast, as invoked by `文法 - 02音韻論`'s 送気・不送気の対立 ("the aspirated/unaspirated contrast") and 音節末対立 ("coda contrast"). Mandarin duìlì, Japanese たいりつ, Korean 대립, and Vietnamese đối lập are all real, standard terms, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Cantonese deoi3 lap6 is a straightforward compositional reading. `kwin` is false: inherited from 対 (false), despite 立 itself being true.

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot**: the concatenated string `ㄉㄛㄧㄌㄧㄆ` admits two legal partitions — the intended 対(ㄉㄛㄧ)+立(ㄌㄧㄆ), and a false alternate splitting 対's own syllable (ㄉㄛ+ㄧ) — so the dot at the true 対|立 boundary is required: ㄉㄛㄧ·ㄌㄧㄆ.
