## Notes
- [柔](../characters/柔%20(char).md) "tender, soft, weak" + [道](../characters/道%20(char).md) "way, path" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 柔 and 道's own stand-ins are themselves, each with its own independent word page).

柔道 means judo.

Mandarin róudào, Cantonese jau4 dou6, Korean 유도, and Vietnamese nhu đạo are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `japanese` used an obsolete historical kana spelling (じうだう), not matching the modern reading used consistently across every other 道-compound in the vault (人道/修道/剣道/国道/報道 all use どう) — corrected to じゅうどう. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/empty `aliases: []`. No homophones (注音 ㄋ⼜ㄉㄚㄨ unique across both words and characters).
