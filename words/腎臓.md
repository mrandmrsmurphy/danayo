## Notes
- [腎](../characters/腎.md) "kidney" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 腎 cannot appear independently outside this compound. [臓](../characters/臓.md) "organ, entrails" is separately legitimized by [[内臓]].

腎臓 combines 腎 ("kidney") with 臓 ("organ") — "kidney." No cranberry: transitivity fails since 臓's own `stand_in` doesn't point here.

Mandarin shènzàng, japanese じんぞう (jinzou), and korean 신장 (sinjang) were all already correct, fully compositional readings. Reciprocal homophone callout with [[伸長]] was already in place.
