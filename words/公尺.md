## Notes
- [公 (char)](../characters/公%20(char).md) "public, metric" + [尺 (char)](../characters/尺%20(char).md) "ruler, foot-unit" — "meter, metre." Mandarin gōngchǐ and Cantonese gung1 cek3 are both directly attested standard terms for "meter" in Chinese-speaking regions.

**Real content bugs found and fixed**: `korean` had been filled with a comma-joined pair of loanword variants (미터, 메터, both "meter" from English) instead of this word's own compositional reading — corrected to 공척 (公's own 공 + 尺's own 척), which conveniently also matches this word's own 諺文 exactly, flipping `kwin` from missing/false to true. `japanese` had stored the single-character compatibility ligature ㍍ (U+3350, a square symbol representing メートル) rather than actual text — expanded to メートル; Japanese has no real kanji-compositional reading for this word in practice, matching the pattern of 公噸's own similar Japanese gap. `vietnamese: công xích` is the honest compositional reading (公's own công + 尺's own xích); the real everyday Vietnamese word for "meter" is mét (the English/French loanword). No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄫㄑㄝㄎ unique among both words and characters).
