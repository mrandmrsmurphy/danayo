## Notes
- [韓](../characters/韓.md) "Han (Korean)" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language" — "the Korean language," the compact form of [[韓国語]]. Neither character's own `stand_in` points here (韓's is [[韓国]], 語's is [[言語]]), so no `#cranberry` applies.

Wiktionary: Chinese 韓語 (hányǔ) and Japanese 韓語 (かんご) both mean "Korean language". It is the compact form of [[韓国語]], as [[越語]] is of [[越南語]], and shares its reading with 漢語 (see the homophone callout). Used in `文法 - 02音韻論` in the grouping 漢語・韓語・越語, set against [[西洋語]].
