## Notes
- [韓](../characters/韓.md) "Han (Korean)" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language" — "the Korean language," the compact form of [[韓国語]]. Neither character's own `stand_in` points here (韓's is [[韓国]], 語's is [[言語]]), so no `#cranberry` applies.

韓語 is the short, two-character name of the Korean language: the single-character ethnonym 韓 (Han, the Korean name element seen in [[韓国]]) plus 語, with the country element 国 dropped. It is the shorthand sibling of the full compound [[韓国語]], exactly as [[越語]] is the shorthand of [[越南語]]. Dan'a'yo's phonology chapter uses it in this compact form, in the grouping 漢語・韓語・越語 ("Chinese, Korean and Vietnamese"), the three languages it describes as built on an aspirated/unaspirated consonant contrast with central vowels, set against Japanese and [[西洋語]].

Cross-linguistically, the shorthand is a Chinese usage first of all. Wiktionary records Mandarin 韓語 (hányǔ) and Cantonese hon4 jyu5 as the ordinary short name for Korean, alongside 朝鮮語 (cháoxiǎnyǔ), 韓國語 (hánguóyǔ) and 韓文; the existing [[韓国語]] page notes that in Mandarin the full form is the official, textbook name for the South Korean standard and that 韓語/韩语 is a common colloquial synonym. Japanese has 韓語 (かんご, kango) too, but Wiktionary lists it only as a synonym of the standard terms 韓国語 and 朝鮮語, which are what Japanese speakers actually say. Vietnamese says tiếng Hàn (the native word tiếng, "language," plus the Sino-Vietnamese ethnonym Hàn), the short form of tiếng Hàn Quốc. The Sino-Korean compositional reading 한어 given in `korean` is not how Korean names its own language: that is 한국어 (the endonym recorded on [[韓国語]]), with 조선어 as the usual North Korean name.

Because both 韓 and 漢 are han in Dan'a'yo, 韓語 and 漢語 are homophones (ㄏㄚㄋ·⼄) and are told apart only in writing. They also share the same compositional Sino-Korean reading 한어, and in the 漢語・韓語・越語 triplet they sit side by side. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 한요 differs from Sino-Korean 한어 in the last syllable (語 reads 요, not 어). The dot in 注音 marks the genuine syllable-boundary fork at 韓|語, as on 漢語.
