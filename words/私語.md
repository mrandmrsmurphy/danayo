## Notes
- [私](../characters/私.md) "private" + [語](../characters/語.md) "word, speech" — "private speech," to whisper. Their own `stand_in` values: 私's is [[私立]]; 語's is [[言語]]; none points here, so 私語 is an independent compound.

私語 is to whisper: to speak privately, in a low voice, so that others do not hear. Mandarin sīyǔ (literary) and Cantonese si1 jyu5 are attested, and Japanese しご is the same compound. Korean says 속삭이다, so that field holds the character reading. Compare [[訥]] (to mumble) and [[叫]] (to shout).
