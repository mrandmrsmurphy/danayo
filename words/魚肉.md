## Notes
- [魚 (char)](../characters/魚%20(char).md) "fish" + [肉 (char)](../characters/肉%20(char).md) "meat"

魚肉 is fish meat: the flesh of fish as food. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yúròu as "fish; flesh of fish" and "fish and meat", and Japanese ぎょにく as "fish meat, fish". Cantonese jyu4 juk6 matches, and Korean 어육 is the reading of the characters.

It is the flesh of the [魚](魚.md), as [肉類](肉類.md) is that of land animals. Shellfish and the like are the [海鮮](海鮮.md), seafood.
