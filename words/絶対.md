## Notes
- [絶 (char)](../characters/絶%20(char).md) "discontinue, break off" + [対](../characters/対.md) "oppose" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (絶's is [[絶]] itself, 対's is [[反対]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

絶対 means absolute, total, or unconditional — beyond comparison or exception.

Mandarin juéduì, Cantonese zyut6 deoi3, Japanese ぜったい (zettai), and Korean 절대 (jeoldae) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 絶対 is itself a common attested word for "absolute" in all four languages. Vietnamese tuyệt đối is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term for "absolute." No Dan'a'yo homophones.
