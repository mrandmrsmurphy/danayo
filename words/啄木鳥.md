## Notes
- [啄](../characters/啄%20(char).md) "peck" + [木](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree" + [鳥](../characters/鳥%20(char).md) "bird" — no stand-in relationship applies (all three characters are independent Dan'a'yo entries, each its own stand-in).

啄木鳥 names the woodpecker, literally "wood-pecking bird."

**Real bugs found and fixed**: `japanese` used the archaic pre-orthographic-reform kana spelling てう instead of modern ちょう — corrected to たくぼくちょう. Filled blank `vietnamese: trác mộc điểu` (verified — a real, attested Sino-Vietnamese term, also appearing as a poem title by Chu Khánh Dư). Korean 딱따구리 is the standard native bird name (not a Sino-Korean compound). No homophones (注音 ㄊㄛㄎㄇㄛㄎㄑㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).
