## Notes
- [腫](../characters/腫.md) "tumor, swelling" is separately legitimized by [[腫瘍]]. [脹](../characters/脹.md) "swell, edema" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 脹 cannot appear independently outside this compound. No cranberry: transitivity fails since 腫's own `stand_in` doesn't point here.

腫脹 means swelling.

Mandarin zhǒngzhàng, cantonese zung2zoeng3 (space removed), japanese しゅちょう (shuchou), korean 종창 (jongchang), and vietnamese thũng trướng were all already correct, fully compositional readings.
