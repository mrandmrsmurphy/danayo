## Notes
- [哲](../characters/哲.md) "wise, sagacious" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study" + [者](../characters/者.md) "-er; one who" — together forming [[哲学]] "philosophy" plus the productive suffix [[－者]].

哲学者 is a philosopher in the plain sense of the one who does philosophy: anyone who practises [[哲学]], reasons about it, or studies it, whether or not they hold an academic title. It is the practitioner word, built on the agentive suffix 者 like [[学者]] "scholar" and [[史学者]] "historian". The suffix is fully productive, so a transparent 哲学 + 者 would be valid without a page; this one has its own file because Dan'a'yo gives it a sense distinct from [[哲学家]], which names the philosopher as an acknowledged title.

Japanese てつがくしゃ and Korean 철학자 are the ordinary words for "philosopher" in those languages, and Korean 철학자 also covers what the other languages write with 家. Chinese prefers 哲學家 (zhéxuéjiā) for "philosopher", so 哲學者 is the less common form there. Vietnamese most often says nhà triết học or triết gia; triết học giả is the compositional reading. In Dan'a'yo 哲学者 narrows to the practitioner, and the title of philosopher is 哲学家.
