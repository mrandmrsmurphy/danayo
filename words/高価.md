## Notes
- [高](../characters/高%20(char).md) "high" + [価](../characters/価.md) "price, value" — "high price," expensive. Their own `stand_in` values: 高's is itself; 価's is [[価格]]; none points here, so 高価 is an independent compound.

高価 is expensive: of a high price. Mandarin gāojià (高價), Cantonese gou1 gaa3 and Japanese こうか all use it, and Korean 고가 is the same compound. It is the opposite of [[廉価]] (cheap), with [[価格]] (price) between them. Compare [[便]] and [[貴重]], which is "precious, valuable," not "high-priced".
