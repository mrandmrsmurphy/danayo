## Notes
- [栗](../characters/栗%20(char).md) "chestnut" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

栗 means chestnut.

Mandarin lì, Cantonese leot6, Japanese りつ, Korean 률, and Vietnamese lật are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄌㄧㄊ/lid/릳) with the unrelated [[慄]] ("tremble, shudder, shiver").
