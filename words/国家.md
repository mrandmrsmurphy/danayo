## Notes
- [国](../characters/国.md) "nation" + [家](../characters/家.md) "house; family" — `characters/国.md`'s own `stand_in` points to this word (国家), meaning 国 is a bound morpheme that never appears independently outside this compound (in this Dan'a'yo sense).

Mandarin guójiā, Cantonese gwok3 gaa1, Japanese こっか, Korean 국가, and Vietnamese quốc gia are all everyday words for "country; nation."

国歌 "national anthem" reads ㄍㄛㄎㄍㄜ in Dan'a'yo, so the two words are not homophones here; they are homophones only in Japanese (both こっか), which is a coincidence unrelated to Dan'a'yo phonology.
