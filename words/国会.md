## Notes
- [国](../characters/国.md) "nation" + [会 (char)](../characters/会%20(char).md) "meet"

国会 is a national assembly, a parliament. It is spelled with the Dan'a'yo forms of the characters, and 國會 is an alias. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 國會 (Mandarin guóhuì, Cantonese gwok3 wui6) as "congress; national assembly; parliament; diet" and Japanese こっかい as "a national legislative assembly; parliament; congress". It lists Korean 국회 and Vietnamese entries. Compare [議会](議会.md), the assembly in general.
