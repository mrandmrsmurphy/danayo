## Notes
- [聖](../characters/聖.md) "sacred, holy" + [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (聖's is [[神聖]], 人's is [[人]] itself), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

聖人 names a sage or saint.

Mandarin shèngrén, Cantonese sing3 jan4, Japanese せいじん (seijin), and Korean 성인 (seongin) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 聖人 is itself a common attested word for "sage, saint" in all four languages. Vietnamese thánh nhân is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
