## Notes
- [貪 (char)](../characters/貪%20(char).md) "greedy" + [欲](../characters/欲.md) "desire"

貪欲 is greed: a desire that wants more than it can need, in Buddhism one of the three poisons. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin tānyù as "greed; avarice; passion" and Japanese どんよく as "greedy; greed". Vietnamese tham dục and Cantonese taam1 juk6 match, and Korean 탐욕 is the reading of the characters.

It is [欲望](欲望.md), desire, without limit, and a sin of the kind covered on [Sin](../lexipedia/Sin.md). The miser's side of it is the [吝嗇](吝嗇.md), miserly.
