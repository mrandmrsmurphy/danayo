## Notes
- [飯](../characters/飯.md) "cooked rice, food" + [店](../characters/店.md) "shop, store" — no `#cranberry`: 飯's own `stand_in` is [[米飯]], 店's is [[商店]]; neither points here.

飯店 (fàndiàn) is a real Mandarin word covering both "restaurant" and, especially in Taiwan, "hotel" (traditional Chinese inns served food alongside lodging), and Cantonese 飯店 (faan6dim3) likewise.

Japanese はんてん (hanten) and Korean 반점 (banjeom) have both narrowed the sense specifically to "Chinese restaurant" — interchangeable with 中華料理店/중화요리점 — rather than covering hotels or restaurants generally. Sino-Vietnamese phạn điếm keeps the fuller original range, covering both an eating establishment and (especially a large or upscale) hotel.
