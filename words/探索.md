## Notes
- [探](../characters/探.md) "search" + [索](../characters/索.md) "search" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [探](../characters/探.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (索's own stand-in is [[捜索]]).

探索 means to explore or search.

Mandarin tànsuǒ, Cantonese taam3 sok3, Japanese たんさく, Korean 탐색, and Vietnamese thám sách (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed missing space in `cantonese`. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄚㄇㄙㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
