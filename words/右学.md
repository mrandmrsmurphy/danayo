## Notes
- [右](../characters/右.md) "right" + [学](../characters/学.md) "learn" — the Right School, the higher of a pair of state schools recorded in Shang-dynasty education; see [[左学]] for the *Book of Rites* source and the pairing's overall structure. 右學 cared for the state elders (國老, in Zheng Xuan's gloss the retired high officials) and centered on instruction in ritual and music (禮樂), making it the functional predecessor of the later [[太学]] tier of education.

The school's name reflects Shang cosmological convention rather than simple geography: the Shang dynasty revered the right and treated west as the cardinal direction corresponding to "right," so the Right School was sited in the western suburb of the capital and was known interchangeably as 西學 ("Western School") and 瞽宗 ("ancestral hall of the blind [musicians]"), the latter name reflecting the historical association of blind musicians with the transmission of ritual music in early Chinese court culture. Its status as the senior of the two schools, reserved for the nobility, anticipates the social stratification of education that persisted through the [[小学]]/[[太学]] and even the Tang Six Schools system (see [[律学]]) many centuries later.

The source is the 《禮記·王制》 line "殷人養國老於右學，養庶老於左學" ("the Yin people cared for their state elders at the Right School and for their common elders at the Left School"), which the paired page [[左学]] also quotes. In his commentary Zheng Xuan identifies the Right School with the 大學 (higher school) in the western suburb and the Left School with the 小學 (lower school) in the royal palace area to the east, and the 《禮記·明堂位》 likewise places the 大學 and 小學 of the Yin at the Right and Left Schools. Modern accounts commonly treat 右學 and 瞽宗 as two names of one higher institution, on the reasoning that the Shang favored the right and the west, which is the same reading the Notes above give; the exact identification remains a scholarly inference and not a settled fact.

The Knowledge page lists the word as "the Right School (a historical institution name)," and it appears in the vault's 歴史綱要 outline as well. Dan'a'yo keeps that sense, a proper name for one historical institution. The alias 西学 ("Western School") is the same institution under its directional name. The readings are Mandarin yòuxué, Cantonese jau6 hok6, Japanese yūgaku (ゆうがく), Korean 우학 and Sino-Vietnamese hữu học.
