## Notes
- [倶](../characters/倶%20(char).md) "accompany, be with" + [楽](../characters/楽.md) "joy, music" + [部 (char)](../characters/部%20(char).md) "section, department" — "club," a phonetic transliteration of English "club" into Sino-Japanese characters (倶楽部, kurabu) that also happens to carry an appropriate compositional sense ("a joyful/together section/department"). Mandarin jùlèbù, Cantonese keoi1 lok6 bou6, Korean 구락부, and Vietnamese câu lạc bộ all borrow the same Japanese-coined compound; `japanese: クラブ` reflects that in casual modern Japanese, the katakana loanword クラブ (from English "club" directly) has actually displaced the kanji spelling 倶楽部 in everyday use, though 倶楽部 itself remains attested and understood. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 구락봇 diverges from Sino-Korean 구락부 in the final syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄨㄌㄚㄎㄅㄛㄨ unique among both words and characters).
