## Notes
- [火 (char)](../characters/火%20(char).md) "fire" + [焰](../characters/焰.md) "flame" — this word is 焰's own `stand_in`: the character's meaning (flame) is identical, but 焰 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word, so 火 ("fire") as the head noun licenses the compound.

火焰 is the vault's word for the literal, physical flame — the visible fire itself. Mandarin huǒyàn, Cantonese fo2 jim6, Japanese かえん, Korean 화염, and Vietnamese hỏa diễm are all standard, fully compositional readings across the CJKV sphere.

**Genuine synonym of [[火炎]], not a duplicate entry**: both mean literal flame and both appear side by side on [[lexipedia/Elements]], but they're built on two different characters with two different Dan'a'yo readings (焰 ⼶ㄇ vs. 炎 ㄝㄇ) — 火炎's own `aliases` field lists 火焰 as a variant, but each has its own independent word file here since neither reading is derivable from the other. The character 炎 additionally straddles a second sense that 焰 never touches: its own `stand_in` is [[炎症]] (medical "inflammation," heat and swelling), extending 炎 into a metaphorical register that 焰 stays out of entirely — 焰 only ever means the literal flame.

- See [Elements](../lexipedia/Elements.md).
