## Notes
- [快](../characters/快%20(char).md) "sharp, quick" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

快 means sharp, quick, or happy — swift and pleasing, as in 快樂/快楽 ("happiness, joy") and 痛快 ("gratifying, exhilarating").

Mandarin kuài, Cantonese faai3, Japanese かい, Korean 쾌, and Vietnamese khoái are all standard, fully compositional readings.
