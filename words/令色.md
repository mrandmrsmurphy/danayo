## Etymology
- [令 (char)](../characters/令%20(char).md) "cause (causative)" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color"

## Notes
From the famous Analects (論語) line 巧言令色，鮮矣仁 ("fine words and an ingratiating face — such men are seldom truly benevolent"). `vietnamese: lệnh sắc` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese from this exact quotation (confirmed via web search, as xảo ngôn lệnh sắc). Filled the blank `cantonese: ling6 sik1`, compositional. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 렁식 diverges from Sino-Korean 영색 in the first syllable's initial consonant. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄝㄫㄙㄧㄎ unique among both words and characters).
