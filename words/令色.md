## Notes
- [令 (char)](../characters/令%20(char).md) "cause (causative)" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — from the famous Analects (論語) line 巧言令色，鮮矣仁 ("fine words and an ingratiating face — such men are seldom truly benevolent"). `vietnamese: lệnh sắc` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese from this exact quotation (confirmed via web search, as xảo ngôn lệnh sắc). Filled the blank `cantonese: ling6 sik1`, compositional. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 렁식 diverges from the correct compositional Sino-Korean 령색 in the first syllable's initial consonant. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄝㄫㄙㄧㄎ unique among both words and characters).

**Real content bug found and fixed**: `korean` had been set to the South-Korean 두음법칙-shifted form 영색 instead of the vault's standard unshifted North-Korean 령색 — the same bug pattern just found on [[令圄]] and [[令聞]]. Fixed missing disambiguation (bare `令`→`令 (char)`, since `words/令.md` exists). Removed dangling empty `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/`aliases`. Merged a non-standard `## Etymology` heading into the standard `## Notes` section.
