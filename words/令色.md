## Notes
- [令 (char)](../characters/令%20(char).md) "cause (causative)" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — from the famous Analects (論語) line 巧言令色，鮮矣仁 ("fine words and an ingratiating face — such men are seldom truly benevolent"). `vietnamese: lệnh sắc` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese from this exact quotation (confirmed via web search, as xảo ngôn lệnh sắc).
