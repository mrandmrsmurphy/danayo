## Notes
- [号](../characters/号.md) "cry out" + [泣 (char)](../characters/泣%20(char).md) "weep"

号泣 is to wail: to weep aloud without restraint, as in great grief. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin háoqì as "to sob loudly; to cry uncontrollably" and Japanese ごうきゅう as "wailing, lamentation; to wail". Korean 호읍 is the same word; the Cantonese reading is built from the characters.

It is the loudest of the weeping words, beyond the plain [泣](泣.md), to weep, and the bitter [痛哭](痛哭.md). The quiet sob is the [嗚咽](嗚咽.md).
