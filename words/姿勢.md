## Notes
- [姿](../characters/姿.md) "manner, bearing, figure, appearance" + [勢](../characters/勢.md) "power, influence" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [姿](../characters/姿.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (勢's own stand-in is [[勢力]]).

姿勢 means posture — the manner in which one holds or positions the body.

Mandarin zīshì, Cantonese zi1 sai3, Japanese しせい, Korean 자세, and Vietnamese tư thế are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄜㄙㄝ unique across both words and characters).
