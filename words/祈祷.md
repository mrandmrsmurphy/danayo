## Notes
- [祈](../characters/祈.md) "pray" + [祷](../characters/祷.md) "pray" — stand-in for both [[祈]] and [[祷]], neither of which can appear independently. `#cranberry`: neither character has any independent life anywhere else in the vault, and both `japanese_native` (いの, from 祈る/祷る) and `korean_native` (빌) are identical between them.

祈祷 means to pray — the general, everyday word for prayer across religious traditions, from Shinto and Buddhist rites (祈祷師, "a priest/shaman who performs prayers/exorcisms") to Christian worship (祈祷書, "prayer book").

Mandarin qídǎo, Cantonese kei4 tou2, Japanese きとう (kitō), Korean 기도 (gido), and Vietnamese kì đảo are all fully compositional and are the standard everyday words for prayer across the sphere.
