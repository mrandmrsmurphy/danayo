## Notes
- [継](../characters/継.md) "continue, persist" + [父](../characters/父.md) "father".

継父 names a stepfather — literally "continuing father," the male counterpart of [継母](継母.md) and built on the exact same "continuing [parent]" logic.

Mandarin jìfù, Cantonese gai3 fu6, and Korean 계부 (gyebu) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 継父 is itself a common attested word for "stepfather" in all three, directly paralleling 継母's own cross-linguistic attestation. Japanese ままちち (mamachichi) is the native reading (まま "step-," ちち "father"), the exact structural counterpart of 継母's own ままはは. Vietnamese kế phụ is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading.
