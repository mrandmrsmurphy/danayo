## Notes
- [家](../characters/家.md) "family, house" + [長 (char)](../characters/長%20(char).md) "eldest, chief" — head of household.

家長 is the head of a household: the senior person who holds authority over a family. It is "house-chief." Mandarin jiāzhǎng, Cantonese gaa1 zoeng2, Japanese かちょう (gender-neutral; 女家長 and 男家長 specify), Korean 가장 and Vietnamese gia trưởng are all confirmed on Wiktionary. Mandarin also uses 家長 for the parent or guardian of a student, a sense that Dan'a'yo does not take. It is the person at the top of the [[家族]] and [[家庭]].
