## Notes
- [誘](../characters/誘.md) "lure, entice" + [惑](../characters/惑%20(char).md) "bewilder, delude" — "lure and delude," to tempt. Their own `stand_in` values: 誘's is [[誘発]]; 惑's is itself; none points here, so 誘惑 is an independent compound.

誘惑 is to tempt: to lure someone toward something, especially toward wrongdoing, and the temptation itself. Mandarin yòuhuò, Cantonese jau5 waak6, Japanese ゆうわく and Korean 유혹 all use it. It is the act that leads toward the sins on this page, such as [[情欲]] (lust) and [[強欲]] (greed).
