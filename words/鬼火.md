## Notes
- [鬼](../characters/鬼.md) "devil, demon" + [火 (char)](../characters/火%20(char).md) "fire" — no `#cranberry`: 鬼's own `stand_in` is [[鬼神]], 火's is 火 itself; neither points here.

Japanese おにび (oni-bi, "demon fire") is a native kun'yomi compound, a genuine folklore term for will-o'-the-wisp phenomena. Mandarin guǐhuǒ, Cantonese gwai2fo2, Korean 귀화, and Sino-Vietnamese quỷ hỏa are the compositional readings; everyday Vietnamese also uses the native phrase ma trơi ("wandering ghost").
