## Notes
- [修](../characters/修.md) "cultivate" + [士](../characters/士%20(char).md) "scholar, person" — "cultivating man," a monk. Their own `stand_in` values: 修's is [[修理]]; 士's is itself; none points here, so 修士 is an independent compound.

修士 is a monk or brother: a man who lives under religious vows. Mandarin xiūshì, Cantonese sau1 si6 and Japanese しゅうし are attested, and Korean 수사 is the same compound; in Japanese it is also a master's degree. It is the Christian monk, where [[僧侶]] is the Buddhist monk. Compare [[修女]] (nun) and [[修道院]] (monastery).
