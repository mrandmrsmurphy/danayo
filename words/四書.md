## Notes
- [四](../characters/四%20(char).md) "four" + [書](../characters/書.md) "book" — no stand-in relationship applies (四 is its own stand-in; 書's own is [[書本]]).

四書 (the Four Books) are the core Confucian canon selected by Zhu Xi: the Analects, Mencius, the Great Learning, and the Doctrine of the Mean.

Japanese ししょ, Korean 사서, and Vietnamese Tứ thư are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄜㄙ⼄ unique across both words and characters).
