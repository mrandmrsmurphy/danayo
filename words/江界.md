## Notes
- [江](../characters/江.md) "river" + [界](../characters/界.md) "zone" — the name of Kanggye, a city of North Korea.

江界 (Kanggye) is a mountain city in the north of North Korea and the capital of Chagang Province ([[慈江]]道) (from memory, not re-checked). The name reads roughly "river border".

English Wiktionary has 江界 as a Chinese proper noun for the city (Mandarin Jiāngjiè, Cantonese gong1 gaai3) and has a Korean entry as a hanja form of 강계; Chinese Wiktionary has no entry. Japanese Wiktionary has no entry, so Japanese こうかい is composed from the on'yomi. Vietnamese Giang Giới is composed from the character pages; no Vietnamese dictionary entry was found.
