## Notes
- [銭](../characters/銭.md) "coin, money" + [包](../characters/包%20(char).md) "wrap, bag" — "money bag," a purse or wallet. Their own `stand_in` values: 銭's is [[金銭]]; 包's is itself; none points here, so 銭包 is an independent compound.

銭包 is a purse or wallet: the small case that holds cash and cards. Mandarin qiánbāo (錢包) and Cantonese cin4 baau1 are the ordinary words. Japanese says 財布 and Korean 지갑, so those two fields hold the character readings. Compare [[現金]] (cash) and [[紙幣]] (banknote).
