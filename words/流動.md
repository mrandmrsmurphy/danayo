## Notes
- [流](../characters/流.md) "flow" + [動 (char)](../characters/動%20(char).md) "move" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 流 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

流動 means to flow, spread, or circulate: to move continuously like a liquid (流動的水, "flowing water"; 人口流動, "population movement"; 流動資金, "working capital"; 流動性, "liquidity, mobility"), and by extension to be mobile, not fixed (流動攤販, "mobile vendor"). The simplified form 流动 is listed as an alias.

Mandarin liúdòng, Cantonese lau4 dung6, Japanese りゅうどう (流動性/りゅうどうせい, "liquidity"; 流動食/りゅうどうしょく, "liquid diet"), Korean 류동 (the North Korean/문화어 form; South Korean is 유동), and Vietnamese lưu động (also the real, standard Vietnamese term for "mobile, circulating") are all standard, fully compositional readings.
