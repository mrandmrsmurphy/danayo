## Notes
- [流](../characters/流.md) "flow" + [動 (char)](../characters/動%20(char).md) "move" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 流 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (動's own stand-in is itself, so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

流動 means to flow, spread, circulate.

Mandarin liúdòng, Cantonese lau4 dung6, Japanese りゅうどう, and Vietnamese lưu động (also the real, standard Vietnamese term for "mobile, circulating") are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 流's and 動's own stored citations exactly. Fixed real bug: `korean` was a garbled comma-joined pair (유동,류동) mixing a South Korean 두음법칙-shifted form with the correct North Korean/문화어 compositional form — corrected to the single form 류동, matching 流's own citation. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄌ⼜ㄉㄛㄫ unique across both words and characters).
