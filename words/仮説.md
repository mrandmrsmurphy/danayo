## Notes
- [仮](../characters/仮.md) "borrow" + [説](../characters/説.md) "theory"

仮説 is a hypothesis: a proposal put forward to be tested, before it is proved. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin jiǎshuō as "hypothesis", and Japanese かせつ is the same word. Korean 가설 and Cantonese gaa2 syut3 match. The word is literally "borrowed theory", since 仮 means provisional.

It sits between a guess ([推測](推測.md)) and a proven [学説](学説.md), a theory. It is tested by [実験](実験.md), an experiment, and confirmed by [検証](検証.md). It differs from [仮定](仮定.md), an assumption made for argument.
