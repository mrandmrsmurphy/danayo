## Notes
- [上 (char)](../characters/上%20(char).md) "above (stand-in: itself)" + [下 (char)](../characters/下%20(char).md) "below (stand-in: itself)"

Wiktionary: Chinese 上下 "high and low; from top to bottom; up and down; old and young; relative superiority or inferiority; thereabouts (after round numbers); to go up and down"; Japanese 上下 "ascending and descending, ascent and descent; rise and fall, fluctuation (as a level of something, such as a price)" and, as a verb, "to ascend and descend". Compare [[左右]] and [[前後]].
