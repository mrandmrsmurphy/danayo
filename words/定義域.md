## Notes
- The mathematical "domain of a function" — built by extending [[定義]] ("definition") with 域 ("domain, region"). Lowercased `vietnamese` to `tập xác định` (was capitalized mid-field), the standard Vietnamese mathematical term for this concept.
- **Real derivation bug found and fixed**: `羅馬字` was `jeng'wi'wig`, carrying the same `'wi` (rather than 義's own `'ǝi`) mistake already flagged on [[官僚主義]] and fixed on [[定義]]. The word's own `諺文` (정읫윅) already correctly used 읫; corrected `羅馬字` to `jeng'ǝi'wig`.
- **Left `hsk_level` unset**: a specialized mathematics term, not on any standard HSK list. Added missing `kwin: false` (域 alone is `false`, AND-rule). No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄫㄜㄧㄨㄧㄎ unique among both words and characters).
