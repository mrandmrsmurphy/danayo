## Notes
- [幇](../characters/幇.md) "help" + [助](../characters/助.md) "assist" — stand-in for [幇](../characters/幇.md), which cannot appear independently: its meaning (to help) is identical, but 幇 alone is too bound to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. The compound pairs it with the near-synonym 助 to make it usable.

Mandarin bāngzhù, Cantonese bong1 zo6, Korean 방조 (bangjo), and Japanese ほうじょ (hōjo) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 幇's and 助's own stored citations. Vietnamese bang trợ is likewise compositional, from 幇's bang and 助's own trợ.
