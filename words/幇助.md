## Notes
- [幇](../characters/幇.md) "help" + [助](../characters/助.md) "assist" — this word is the viable form of [幇](../characters/幇.md): the character's meaning (to help) is identical, but 幇 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. The compound pairs it with a near-synonym 助 to make it usable.

Mandarin bāngzhù, Cantonese bong1 zo6, Korean 방조 (bangjo), and Japanese ほうじょ (hōjo) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 幇's and 助's own stored citations. Vietnamese bang trợ is likewise compositional, from 幇's bang and 助's own trợ (a real reading, previously entirely missing from 助's own character page — corrected there, alongside its remaining stored candidates chợ/chữa, which look like unrelated corpus noise). **Real bug found and fixed**: `aliases` had stored 幮助, using 幮 (an unrelated character meaning "curtain, mosquito net") instead of 幫's own traditional form — corrected to 幫助, matching 幇's own stored aliases (幫/帮). **Real `kwin` bug found and fixed**: was stored as `false`, but Dan'a'yo 방조 is byte-identical to Sino-Korean 방조 — corrected to `kwin: true`. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄫㄐㄛ unique among both words and characters).
