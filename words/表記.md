## Notes
- [表](../characters/表.md) "to express; to represent; to show outwardly" + [記](../characters/記.md) "to record; to write down; a written record"

表記 is "notation; orthography; written representation" — the act or system of representing something in written symbols. 表 (to express, to make visible, to represent outwardly) + 記 (to record in writing, to note down). The compound denotes both the process (representing sound or meaning in written form) and the resulting system (an orthographic or notational convention): 音の表記 ("notation of sounds"), 仮名表記 ("kana orthography"), 表記の統一 ("standardisation of notation").

Dan'a'yo's sense is the Japanese/Korean one. Japanese 表記 (ひょうき, hyōki) is a noun and a する-verb meaning "writing, notation, transcription; textual representation, spelling" — prominent in linguistics, publishing, and editorial work: 表記の揺れ (orthographic variation), 外来語の表記 (notation of loanwords). Korean 표기 (pyogi) is likewise the standard linguistic term: 한글 표기 (Hangul orthography), 외래어 표기법 (rules for transcribing foreign words). Example phrases here are from memory and not re-checked beyond the Japanese Wiktionary entry, which lists "writing, notation, transcription" and "textual representation; spelling".

Mandarin 表記 (biǎojì; simplified 表记) is a different word. Wiktionary's Chinese entry lists only "something given as a token; memento" and "mark; sign" — not orthography. Cantonese biu2 gei3 shares that Chinese entry. The Vietnamese reading is the Sino-Vietnamese biểu kí (hvdic.thivien.net: "a sign to record or mark; a token or keepsake; a type of document"), a reading of the compound in the Chinese sense, not of the Japanese/Korean one. For the Japanese/Korean sense Vietnamese typically uses other words (e.g. chính tả "orthography", from memory, not re-checked); ký hiệu "symbol, notation" is an equivalent of the Chinese "mark; sign" sense, not a reading of 表記, so the field now gives the compound's own reading.

表記 is directly relevant to the Dan'a'yo project: the vault uses multiple notation systems for each entry — 注音 (Bopomofo phonetic notation), 羅馬字 (Roman-letter transcription), and 諺文 (Hangul phonetic transcription). Each is a 表記 system: a standardised written representation of Dan'a'yo's phonological inventory. It sits beside [[転写]] ("transcription", rendering a form from one script or sound system into another), [[符号]] ("symbol, mark, sign"), and [[表示]]/[[表現]] — neighbouring words in the same area; 表記 is the Japanese/Korean "written representation, spelling" sense. The simplified alias 表记 retains the simplified-character form of 記 for cross-referencing with mainland Chinese sources.
