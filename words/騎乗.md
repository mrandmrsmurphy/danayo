## Notes
- [騎](../characters/騎.md) "ride (a horse)" + [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "ride" — no `#cranberry`: 騎's own `stand_in` is [[騎馬]], 乗's is 乗 itself; neither points here.

**Fixed a real bug**: `pos` was `動詞`, a general linguistic term (used in this vault's own grammar documentation for "verb phrase"/"verb morphology" broadly) rather than a valid frontmatter category — corrected to `事詞`, matching both constituent characters' own `pos` and the vault's canonical four-way word-class system (名詞/性詞/事詞/固有名詞).

Mandarin qíchéng, Cantonese ke4sing4, Japanese きじょう, and Korean 기승 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Sino-Vietnamese kỵ thừa is the compositional form; the everyday Vietnamese equestrian phrase is instead the native cưỡi ngựa ("to ride a horse").
