## Notes
- [䦧 (char)](../characters/䦧%20(char).md) "quarrel" — stand-in for the character 䦧

Mandarin xì, Cantonese jik1, Korean 혁, and Sino-Vietnamese huých are all standard compositional readings. Japanese せめぐ (semegu, native kun-reading) is the real, everyday word for "to fight, contend," used here over the character's rarer on'yomi (KYAKU/KEKI/GEKI). Fixed a comma-joined `vietnamese` contamination bug: `"huých, huỵch"` had two candidates jammed together — kept huých, the character's own stored reading; huỵch is an unrelated onomatopoeia (a thudding sound) and was removed.
