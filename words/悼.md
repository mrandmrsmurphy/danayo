## Notes
- [悼](../characters/悼%20(char).md) "lament" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

悼 means to lament or mourn.

Mandarin dào, Cantonese dou6, Japanese とう, Korean 도, and Vietnamese điệu are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄉㄚㄨ/dau/닷) with the unrelated [[道]] ("way") — see [[道]]'s own Notes for the fuller account of this syllable's other same-reading (but bound, non-standalone) characters.
