## Notes
- [気](../characters/気%20(char).md) "feeling, energy" + [功](../characters/功.md) "achievement" — no stand-in relationship applies (気's own stand-in is itself; 功's own is [[功績]]).

気功 means qigong.

Mandarin qìgōng, Cantonese hei3 gung1, Japanese きこう, Korean 기공, and Vietnamese khí công are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 気's and 功's own stored citations exactly) — and also happen to be the real, standard international term for this practice across all five languages. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄧㄜㄍㄛㄫ unique across both words and characters).
