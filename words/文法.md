## Notes
- [文](../characters/文.md) "culture" + [法](../characters/法%20(char).md) "law" — no stand-in relationship applies (文's own stand-in is [[文化]]; 法's own is itself).

文法 means grammar.

Mandarin wénfǎ, Cantonese man4 faat3, Japanese ぶんぽう, Korean 문법, and Vietnamese văn pháp (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄨㄋㄆㄚㄆ unique across both words and characters).
