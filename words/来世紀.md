## Notes
- [来](../characters/来%20(char).md) "come" + [世紀](世紀.md) ("century," itself [世](../characters/世.md) "world" + [紀](../characters/紀.md) "record, era") — no stand-in relationship applies (来's own stand-in is itself; this compound builds on [[世紀]], which is itself the stand-in for 紀; 世's own is [[世界]]).

来世紀 means next century.

Mandarin láishìjì, Cantonese loi4 sai3 gei2, and Japanese らいせいき are all standard, fully compositional readings.
