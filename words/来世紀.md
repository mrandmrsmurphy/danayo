## Notes
- [来](../characters/来%20(char).md) "come" + [世紀](世紀.md) ("century," itself [世](../characters/世.md) "world" + [紀](../characters/紀.md) "record, era") — no stand-in relationship applies (来's own stand-in is itself; this compound builds on [[世紀]], which is itself the stand-in for 紀; 世's own is [[世界]]).

来世紀 means next century.

Mandarin láishìjì, Cantonese loi4 sai3 gei2, and Japanese らいせいき are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `korean` was 다음세기, a native descriptive phrase ("next century" using native 다음), not the compositional Sino-Korean form — corrected to 래세기, matching 来's and [[世紀]]'s own citations. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field (lai thế kỷ). No homophones (注音 ㄌㄚㄧㄙㄝㄍㄧ unique across both words and characters).
