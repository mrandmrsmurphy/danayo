## Notes
- [烏](../characters/烏.md) "crow, raven" + [魯](../characters/魯.md) "rash, vulgar" + [斉](../characters/斉.md) "simultaneous" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 魯 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (斉's own stand-in is [[一斉]], so no #cranberry: only one side of this trio is a bound character). `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` are correctly built from all three characters' own citations (오+롬+제).

烏魯斉 is Ürümqi, the capital of Xinjiang, China — spelled here with the shorter three-character Dan'a'yo headword, while the real, standard four-character transliteration (烏魯木齊/乌鲁木齐, including 木) is recorded as an alias, matching the real names in Mandarin/Cantonese/Vietnamese.

Fixed a real bug: `korean` was a malformed semicolon-separated doubled value (우루무치; 우룸치) — corrected to the single real Korean name 우루무치. Filled the blank `cantonese` syllable count to match the real four-syllable name (wu1 lou5 muk6 cai4), consistent with mandarin Wūlǔmùqí and Vietnamese Ô Lỗ Mộc Tề. Japanese ウルムチ is the real, standard phonetic loanword. No homophones (注音 ㄛㄌㄛㄇㄐㄝㄧ unique across both words and characters).
