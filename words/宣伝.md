## Notes
- [宣](../characters/宣.md) "proclaim, declare" + [伝](../characters/伝.md) "transmit, spread" — "proclaim and spread," publicity. Their own `stand_in` values: 宣's is [[宣言]]; 伝's is [[伝播]]; none points here, so 宣伝 is an independent compound.

宣伝 is publicity: spreading the word about a product, person or cause, and, in the political sense, propaganda. Mandarin xuānchuán (宣傳), Cantonese syun1 cyun4, Japanese せんでん and Korean 선전 all use it, and Vietnamese tuyên truyền does too. It is the broader, spreading side of what [[広告]] does with a single notice.
