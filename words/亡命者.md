## Notes
- [[亡命]] "to be forced into exile, to defect" + [者 (char)](../characters/者%20(char).md) "-er, one who" — the person, agentive form of [[亡命]]. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: `characters/亡.md` and `characters/命.md` are individually `kwin: true` but `characters/者 (char).md` is `kwin: false` → compound `false`.

亡命者 means "a defector, an exile, a political refugee." Japanese ぼうめいしゃ (bōmeisha) is the real, standard Japanese word for exactly this. Korean 망명자 (mangmyeongja) is equally standard. Mandarin wángmìngzhě is a real, if somewhat formal/written, term.

Vietnamese doesn't independently attest a giả-suffixed form here; living Vietnamese instead reaches for người lưu vong ("exiled person," built on [[流亡]] rather than [[亡命]]) for both this word and [[流亡者]] below, not distinguishing the two Dan'a'yo keeps separate. người vong mệnh is kept as the field value for citation consistency.
