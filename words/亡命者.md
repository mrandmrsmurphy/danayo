## Notes
- [亡命](亡命.md) "to be forced into exile, to defect" + [者](../characters/者.md) "-er, one who" — the person, agentive form of [亡命](亡命.md).

亡命者 means "a defector, an exile, a political refugee." Japanese ぼうめいしゃ (bōmeisha) is the real, standard Japanese word for exactly this. Korean 망명자 (mangmyeongja) is equally standard. Mandarin wángmìngzhě is a real, if somewhat formal/written, term.

Vietnamese doesn't independently attest a giả-suffixed form here; living Vietnamese instead reaches for người lưu vong ("exiled person," built on [流亡](流亡.md) rather than [亡命](亡命.md)) for both this word and [流亡者](流亡者.md), not distinguishing the two Dan'a'yo keeps separate. người vong mệnh is kept as the field value for citation consistency.
