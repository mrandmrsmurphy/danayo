## Notes
- [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" + [訓](../characters/訓%20(char).md) "interpret, gloss" + [標](../characters/標.md) "mark, sign" — "the sound-and-gloss mark".

音訓標 is a **Dan'a'yo coinage** for the line of phonetic transcription written above each character, the ruby. In [[文法 - 03文字法]] the heading is `## 音訓標`, and the chapter says that above each character appears 音訓標 in [[注音符号]] letters, set in 振り仮名 style (振り仮名法), showing the intended pronunciation directly. No source language has this compound, so like [[関詞]], [[派生辞]] and [[接頭辞]] its `mandarin`, `cantonese`, `japanese`, `korean` and `vietnamese` fields give the literal readings of its own characters 音 + 訓 + 標 in each language (yīnxùnbiāo, jam1 fan3 biu1, おんくんひょう, 음훈표, âm huấn tiêu), not an existing native term for ruby. These are compositional readings and were not separately verified as attested.

The parts are familiar. 音 and 訓 together recall Japanese 音訓 (音読み and 訓読み, the Sino-Japanese and native readings of a character), the two kinds of reading that ruby traditionally records; Korean has the related 훈음 (the "gloss and sound" by which a hanja is taught, e.g. 하늘 천). 標 is a "mark" or "sign" ([[標識]]). The name thus calls the ruby the mark of a character's reading. Dan'a'yo's ruby, however, differs from Japanese 振り仮名 in one respect: it records a single reading, in line with 【[[一字一音]]】 (every character has one and only one pronunciation), so there is no 訓 reading to choose between; the 訓 in the name is inherited from the term's model, not a second reading. That reading of the 訓 is an interpretation of the coinage and is not stated in the chapter.

The existing words for the thing in the source languages are different compounds: Japanese 振り仮名 / ルビ, Chinese 注音 / 拼音 (see [[注音]], [[拼音]]) (Korean has no widely used single term that I could confirm). The 注音 word names the notation system itself, and [[注音符号]] names the script (Bopomofo); 音訓標 is the *role*, the annotation line, whichever script fills it. Within Dan'a'yo it is always filled with 注音符号, which the chapter's own text and every ruby on these pages show.

Note on the 音 part: 音 is a semi-productive suffix whose derivatives are controlled by authority review (see [音](../characters/音.md)). 音訓標 has 音 as its first element, not as a suffix. No homophones (注音 ㄨㄇㄏㄨㄋㄅ⼄ unique across words, chengyu and characters).
