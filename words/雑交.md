## Notes
- [雑](../characters/雑%20(char).md) "miscellaneous" + [交](../characters/交%20(char).md) "mix, exchange"

雑交 means to hybridize or crossbreed — mixing distinct genetic lines to produce offspring with combined traits. Mandarin 雜交/杂交 (zájiāo), Cantonese 雜交 (zaap6gaau1), Japanese 雑交 (ざっこう), Korean 잡교 (japgyo), and Sino-Vietnamese tạp giao are all standard biology/agriculture terms across the CJKV sphere.

**Found and fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字` had jabgyau, mismatched with 交's own correctly-stored gyou (諺文 굣 and 注音 ㄍ⼄ㄨ were both already correct) — corrected to jabgyou. Removed the space from mandarin.
