## Notes
- [仁](../characters/仁.md) "benevolence" + [慈](../characters/慈.md) "compassion" — nhân từ, extremely common everyday Vietnamese for "kind, merciful, benevolent" (of a ruler, parent, or god), a direct and natural match.

In more classical text, 仁慈 focuses on specific actions of benevolence, probably from a superior to an inferior. In Dan'a'yo, the terms are entirely equivalent. 仁慈 is the broad philosophical term, and while it may be applied to a specific act, it remains broad in its denotation. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 닌지 diverges from Sino-Korean 인자 in the second syllable's vowel — the same divergence shared with its homophone [[人事]].
