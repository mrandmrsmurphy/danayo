## Notes
- [肇](../characters/肇.md) "start, begin" + [造](../characters/造.md) "create, make" — 肇's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 造's own is [[創造]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 肇造 legitimizes 肇 alone, which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo without this stand-in. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

肇造 means to found or establish.

Mandarin zhàozào, Cantonese siu6 zou6, Japanese ちょうぞう (chōzō), and Korean 조조 (jojo) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 肇造 is itself a common attested word for "found, establish" in all four languages. Vietnamese triệu tạo is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
