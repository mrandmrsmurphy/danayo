## Notes
One's father's younger brother; a man younger than one's parents with whom one is well acquainted. `japanese: おじ` documents the everyday native Japanese word (already present). `vietnamese: chú` is the real, native Vietnamese word for specifically the father's younger brother — distinct from bá/bác, the father's *older* brother (paralleling [[伯父]]'s own bá phụ), rather than a Sino-Vietnamese calque of these two characters.
