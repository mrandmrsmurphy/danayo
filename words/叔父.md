one's father's younger brother; a man younger than one's parents with whom one is well aquainted

## Notes
`japanese: おじ` documents the everyday native Japanese word (already present). `vietnamese: chú` is the real, native Vietnamese word for specifically the father's younger brother — distinct from bá/bác, the father's *older* brother (paralleling [[伯父]]'s own bá phụ), rather than a Sino-Vietnamese calque of these two characters. Filled the blank `pos: 名詞`. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 숙부 matches Sino-Korean 숙부 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨㄎㄅㄨ unique among both words and characters).
