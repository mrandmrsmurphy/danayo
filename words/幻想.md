## Notes
- [幻](../characters/幻.md) "illusion" + [想](../characters/想.md) "thought, concept"
- This word is the stand-in for [[幻]], which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo.

幻想 is a fantasy or illusion: an unreal picture of things that the mind builds up, whether as pleasant imagination or as a mistaken belief that one takes to be real. Mandarin huànxiǎng (also a verb, "to fantasize"), Cantonese waan6 soeng2, Japanese げんそう (gensō; 幻想的, "fantastical, dreamlike"; 幻想曲, "fantasia") and Korean 환상 (hwansang) are the same compound. 幻 is "illusion, phantom" and 想 is "thought, idea".

Dan'a'yo-specific sense: the shared one, ranging from harmless fantasy to delusion. In Mind it sits beside [[夢]] (a dream while asleep): 幻想 is a waking picture of the unreal.
