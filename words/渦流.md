## Notes
- [渦](../characters/渦.md) "swirl, whirlpool, eddy" + [流](../characters/流.md) "flow" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 渦 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (流's own stand-in is [[流動]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

渦流 means swirl, whirlpool, eddy (also "eddy current" in physics/engineering).

Mandarin wōliú, Cantonese wo1 lau4, Japanese かりゅう, and Korean 와류 (retaining the initial liquid per North Korean/문화어 convention) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 渦's and 流's own stored citations exactly. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with oa lưu (渦's own oa + 流's own lưu). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⺢ㄌ⼜ unique across both words and characters).
