## Notes
- [地 (char)](../characters/地%20(char).md) "earth, ground" + [下 (char)](../characters/下%20(char).md) "below, under" + [道 (char)](../characters/道%20(char).md) "way, road" — "underground passage, tunnel, underpass." **Real gap found and fixed on [[下 (char)]]'s own page**: its `vietnamese` field only listed the native-style "xuống" ("go down"), missing the classical Sino-Vietnamese reading hạ entirely (used in 下旬 hạ tuần, 陛下 bệ hạ, etc.) — added. `vietnamese: địa hạ đạo` is compositional from all three characters' own confirmed readings; not independently confirmed as a fixed three-syllable term — the related two-character 地道 (địa đạo, "underground tunnel") is far better attested in Vietnamese (famously in địa đạo Củ Chi, the Củ Chi tunnels), while everyday Vietnamese for a pedestrian underpass simply says hầm đi bộ (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 듸하닷 diverges from Sino-Korean 지하도 in all three syllables.
