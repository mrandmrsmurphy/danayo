## Notes
- [薬](../characters/薬%20(char).md) "medicine" + [草](../characters/草%20(char).md) "grass, herb" — "medicine grass," a herb. Their own `stand_in` values: 薬's is itself; 草's is itself; none points here, so 薬草 is an independent compound.

薬草 is a herb: a plant used as medicine. Mandarin yàocǎo (藥草), Cantonese joek6 cou2, Japanese やくそう and Korean 약초 all use it. It is the medicinal herb, where a culinary herb is named by its own plant. Compare [[薬]] (medicine) and [[草]] (grass).
