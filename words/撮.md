## Notes
- [撮](../characters/撮%20(char).md) "small amount, pinch" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [撮](../characters/撮%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; is a plain noun (名詞), not a classifier or a unit word: it names a pinch or small amount and is counted like any other noun, e.g. 鹽 一撮 ("a pinch of salt", illustrative).

Mandarin cuō, Cantonese cyut3, Japanese さつ, Korean 촬, and Vietnamese toát are all standard, fully compositional readings. Simplified `vietnamese` from the character's full three-way list to its primary reading. No homophones at the word level (注音 ㄐ⺢ㄊ is shared only with the name-only character 拶).
