## Notes
- [艾](../characters/艾.md) "mugwort" is separately legitimized by [[艾草]]. [灸](../characters/灸.md) "moxibustion" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 灸 cannot appear independently outside this compound. No cranberry: transitivity fails since 艾's own `stand_in` doesn't point here.

艾灸 combines 艾 ("mugwort") with 灸 ("moxibustion") — "moxibustion with mugwort."

Mandarin àijiū, cantonese ngaai6gau3 (space removed), and japanese やいと (yaito, matching 灸's own native reading directly) were all already correct.
