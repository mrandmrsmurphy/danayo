## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" + [覚](../characters/覚.md) "awake, aware" — "self-awareness," to be aware. Their own `stand_in` values: 自's is [[自身]]; 覚's is [[感覚]]; none points here, so 自覚 is an independent compound.

自覚 is to be aware: to realize or be conscious of something about oneself or one's situation. Mandarin zìjué, Cantonese zi6 gok3, Japanese じかく and Korean 자각 all use it, and Vietnamese tự giác does too. Compare [[意識]] (consciousness) and [[感知]] (to perceive).
