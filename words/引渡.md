## Notes
- [引 (char)](../characters/引%20(char).md) "pull; draw" + [渡 (char)](../characters/渡%20(char).md) "transit; ferry"

引渡 is to hand over, and in law extradition: the handing of an accused or convicted person from one state to another. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yǐndù as "to extradite; extradition", with the further sense "to lead across (a river); to guide through". Cantonese jan5 dou6 matches. The real Japanese word is 引き渡し, read ひきわたし, a native reading, so the on'yomi いんど given in the field is the Sino-Japanese reading of the characters.

The word applies to a person wanted for a crime who has fled. It is related to [亡命](亡命.md), the flight into exile that extradition can answer, to [逮捕](逮捕.md), the arrest that precedes it, and to [駆逐](駆逐.md), expulsion.
