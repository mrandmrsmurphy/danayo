## Notes
- [立](../characters/立%20(char).md) "stand, solid" + [体](../characters/体.md) "body" + [声](../characters/声.md) "sound" — "solid sound." Their own `stand_in` values: 立's is itself; 体's is [[体系]]; 声's is [[発声]]; none points here, so 立体声 is an independent compound.

立体声 is stereo: sound reproduced through two channels so that it seems to have depth. Mandarin lìtǐshēng and Cantonese lap6 tai2 seng1 are the ordinary Chinese words, from 立体 "three-dimensional" and 声 "sound". Japanese says ステレオ and Korean 스테레오, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[唱片]] (record) and [[音楽]] (music).
