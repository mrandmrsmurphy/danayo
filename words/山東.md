## Notes
- [山 (char)](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain" + [東](../characters/東.md) "east" — "east of the mountain". No stand-in relationship applies (山 is its own stand-in; 東's own is [[東方]]). 东 is the alias of 東, so 山东 is kept as the word's alias.

This name has older and extended senses, listed first. English Wiktionary gives 山東 as an ordinary noun "east of the mountain", and as a historical geographical area: the land east of the Hangu Pass, east of the Taihang Mountains, or east of Mount Hua. English Wiktionary's Japanese entry also records an obsolete Japanese use for the region east of the Rocky Mountains in the United States (read さんとう).

Shandong, the province, is on the east coast of China, with the Shandong peninsula running into the Yellow Sea, and its capital is [[済南]]; it includes the port city [[青島]] (from memory, not re-checked). English Wiktionary gives the synonym 齊魯 (the old states of Qi and Lu, cf. [[斉国]]). The Japanese field holds さんとう, the on'yomi that English Wiktionary lists first; the same entry also lists シャンドン, borrowed from Mandarin. The Korean field holds the Sino-Korean 산동, matching the Dan'a'yo 諺文 exactly; the usual South Korean name today is 산둥 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Sơn Đông is the Sino-Vietnamese name. It pairs with [[山西]] (Shanxi), "west of the mountains", both names referring to the Taihang range as Wiktionary explains them. Cantonese saan1 dung1 is the Jyutping on English Wiktionary. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄚㄋㄉㄛㄫ unique across both words and characters).

Attestation, checked 2026-10-11: English and Chinese Wiktionary (Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese sections) list the senses above.

1. East of the mountain (in general).
2. The historical region east of the Hangu Pass, the Taihang Mountains or Mount Hua.
3. Santō, an obsolete Japanese name for the region east of the Rocky Mountains.
4. Shandong, the province of China.
