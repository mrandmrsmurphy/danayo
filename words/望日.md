## Notes
- [望](../characters/望.md) "full moon; gaze" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day"

望日 names the full moon day of a lunar month, typically the 15th — the structural counterpart to [[朔日]] (new moon, first day) and [[晦日]] (dark moon, last day), the three anchor points of the traditional lunar calendar.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `japanese` had もちにち, an inconsistent hybrid mixing 望's native kun'yomi もち with 日's on'yomi にち — a reading that matches neither of 望日's two real attested Japanese readings, the primary on'yomi ぼうじつ (matching this compound's own sibling [[朔日]]'s さくじつ) or the alternate native もちのひ. Corrected to ぼうじつ. Korean 망일 and Vietnamese vọng nhật are both standard compositional readings. No specific HSK level applies to a term this specialized. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄚㄫㄋㄧㄊ unique across both words and characters).
