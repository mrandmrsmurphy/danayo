## Notes
- [望](../characters/望.md) "full moon; gaze" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day"

望日 is the full-moon day: the fifteenth day of a lunar month. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 望日 (Mandarin wàngrì, Cantonese mong6 jat6), labelled literary, and Japanese ぼうじつ both as "the fifteenth day of a month in the lunar calendar". For the single 望 it gives, in Chinese astronomy, "the day or night of the full moon". It has no Korean or Vietnamese entry for 望日, so Korean 망일 and Vietnamese vọng nhật here are built from the characters.

The first day of the lunar month is the [[朔日]], and the last day is the [[晦日]].
