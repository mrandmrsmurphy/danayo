## Notes
- [両](../characters/両%20(char).md) "both" + [江](../characters/江.md) "river" — the name of the Ryanggang region of Korea, in the north of North Korea. 兩 is the other form of 両, which is why the filename uses 両; 兩江 and 两江 are kept as aliases.

両江 (Ryanggang) is a province of North Korea (両江道, 량강도) in the far north, on the Chinese border; its capital is [[恵山]] and it contains Mount Paektu (白頭山) (from memory, not re-checked). The name reads "two rivers", the Yalu (鴨緑江) and the Tumen (豆滿江), both of which rise on Mount Paektu (from memory, not re-checked). It was split off from South Hamgyong in 1954 (from memory, not re-checked).

English and Chinese Wiktionary have 兩江 only as the historical Chinese Liangjiang, the Qing-dynasty name for the provinces of Jiangnan and Jiangxi; neither has a Korean Ryanggang entry, so the Korean province sense is unattested on Wiktionary. The readings here are composed from the character pages: Mandarin Liǎngjiāng, Cantonese loeng5 gong1, Japanese りょうこう, Korean 량강 (the North Korean spelling, with the word-initial ㄹ kept; the South Korean spelling would be 양강) and Vietnamese Lưỡng Giang. The Chinese 兩江 is a different place, listed under the same spelling.
