## Notes
- [女](../characters/女%20(char).md) "woman" + [性](../characters/性.md) "nature, gender" — no stand-in relationship applies (女's own stand-in is itself; 性's own is [[性別]]).

女性 means female, or feminine gender — literally "the nature/quality of being a woman."

Mandarin nǚxìng, Cantonese neoi5 sing3, Japanese じょせい, and Vietnamese nữ tính are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `korean` used the South Korean 두음법칙-shifted form 여성 — corrected to the North Korean/문화어 form 녀성, per vault convention. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄜㄙㄧㄫ unique across both words and characters).
