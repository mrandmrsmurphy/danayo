## Notes
- [軽 (char)](../characters/軽%20(char).md) "light; make light of" + [蔑 (char)](../characters/蔑%20(char).md) "scorn"

軽蔑 is contempt: to look on someone as beneath one's notice, or the feeling itself. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin qīngmiè as "to look down upon; to scorn; to disdain" and Japanese けいべつ as "contempt, disdain". Korean 경멸, Vietnamese khinh miệt and Cantonese hing1 mit6 match.

It is cold where [憤怒](憤怒.md) is hot, and it is the feeling behind [蔑視](蔑視.md) and [侮蔑](侮蔑.md). The bound root is [蔑](蔑.md).
