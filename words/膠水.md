## Notes
- [膠](../characters/膠.md) "glue" + [水 (char)](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water"
- This word is the viable form of [膠](../characters/膠.md): the character's meaning (glue) is identical, but 膠 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 膠水 is its `stand_in` compound.

膠水 is glue in its everyday liquid form, a sticky liquid for bonding paper, wood and other surfaces. It is the ordinary Chinese word (Mandarin jiāoshuǐ, Cantonese gaau1 seoi2); the bound character 膠 is "glue, gum", originally the animal-hide glue made by boiling skins and bones, and it also lies behind 塑膠 ("plastic"). The Korean 교수 in the page's data is the compositional Sino-Korean reading, not the ordinary Korean word for glue, and the ordinary Japanese words are 糊 (のり, paste) and 接着剤 (adhesive); Japanese 膠 on its own is にかわ, animal glue.

In Dan'a'yo it is the plain general word for glue as a material.
