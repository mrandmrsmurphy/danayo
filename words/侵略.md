## Notes
- [侵](../characters/侵.md) "invade, encroach, raid, trespass, violate" + [略 (char)](../characters/略%20(char).md) "abbreviate, plan" — "to invade" (侵略戦争, "war of aggression"; 領土侵略, "territorial invasion"). Mandarin qīnlüè, Cantonese cam1 loek6, Japanese しんりゃく, Korean 침략, and Vietnamese xâm lược are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 침략 matches Sino-Korean 침략 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄇㄌ⼘ㄎ unique among both words and characters).
