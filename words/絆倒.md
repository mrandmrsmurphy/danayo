## Notes
- [絆](../characters/絆.md) "fetter" + [倒](../characters/倒%20(char).md) "fall over" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [絆](../characters/絆.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (倒's own stand-in is [[倒]] itself, an unrelated word).
- Primarily a Chinese compound; ばんとう is the mechanical Sino-Japanese reading but not standard Japanese usage.

**A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 絆倒 and [[半島]] ("peninsula") share an identical reading (bantau/반탓/ㄅㄚㄋㄊㄚㄨ) — standardized the non-canonical `[!tip]` callout into the proper reciprocal `>[!warning] Homophones` format on both pages.
