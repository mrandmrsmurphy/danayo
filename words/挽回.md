## Notes
- [挽](../characters/挽.md) "to save, recover, reverse" + [回](../characters/回%20(char).md) "to return" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [挽](../characters/挽.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (回's own stand-in is itself).

挽回 means to recover, restore, or retrieve.

Mandarin wǎnhuí, Cantonese waan5 wui4, Japanese ばんかい, Korean 만회, and Vietnamese vãn hồi (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄛㄋㄏㄛㄧ unique across both words and characters).
