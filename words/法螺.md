## Notes
- [法 (char)](../characters/法%20(char).md) "rule, law" + [螺](../characters/螺.md) "spiral, screw" — no stand-in relationship applies (法's own stand-in is itself; 螺's own is [[螺旋]]).

法螺 literally names the conch-shell trumpet used in Buddhist ritual; by extension, it means to boast, to brag (as in 法螺を吹く, "to blow the conch," i.e., to talk big).

Mandarin fǎluó, Cantonese faat3 lo4, Korean 법라, and Vietnamese pháp loa are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 法's and 螺's own stored citations exactly). Japanese ほら is the real, contracted native reading (from the same compound, rather than the full on'yomi ほうら), the standard everyday Japanese word for "boast." Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases: []`. No homophones (注音 ㄈㄚㄆㄌㄚ unique across both words and characters).
