## Notes
- [里 (char)](characters/里%20(char).md) "village" + [芋](characters/芋.md) "taro, tuber" — "taro" (literally "village tuber").

**Found and fixed a wrong-compound reading bug on `mandarin`/`cantonese`**: lǐyù/lei5 wu6 had instead held yùtou/wu6 tau4-2, the reading of the unrelated (though same-meaning) compound 芋頭 ("taro," using 芋頭 instead of 里芋) — corrected to the honest compositional 里's own lǐ/lei5 + 芋's own yù/wu6. Japanese さといも is genuinely compositional (里's own native さと + 芋's own native いも, a real jukujikun for this exact word) — not a bug. **Found and fixed a real-equivalent-instead-of-own-reading bug on `korean`**: 토란 is actually 芋's own native Korean gloss (not a Sino-Korean compositional reading), matching the real different compound 土卵 rather than 里芋 — corrected to the honest compositional 리우, matching the word's own stored 諺文/羅馬字 (리우/li'u) exactly, which also flips `kwin` from false to genuinely true. Vietnamese lý dụ is the honest compositional reading (里's own lý + 芋's own dụ). **Found and removed three bad-aliases-entries**: `芋頭`, `芋头`, and `土卵` are all genuinely different compounds, not spelling variants of 里芋. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄧㄨ unique among both words and characters).

**Found and fixed a missing citation** on 芋.md's own Words list. Citation on 里(char).md was already correctly present. 芋's own `stand_in` is this exact word (里芋), so 芋 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 里芋; 里's own `stand_in` is instead 里 itself, so this word does not legitimize 里.
