## Notes
- [駕](../characters/駕.md) "drive" + [照](../characters/照%20(char).md) "license, shine" — "driving permit." Their own `stand_in` values: 駕's is [[凌駕]]; 照's is itself; none points here, so 駕照 is an independent compound.

駕照 is a driver's license: the permit to drive a vehicle. Mandarin jiàzhào and Cantonese gaa3 ziu3 are the ordinary Chinese words, a short form of 駕駛執照. Japanese says 運転免許証 and Korean 운전면허증, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[車]] (car) and [[計程車]] (taxi).
