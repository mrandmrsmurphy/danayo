## Notes
- [親](../characters/親.md) "parent; close" + [睦](../characters/睦.md) "friendly" — stand-in for [[睦]], which cannot appear independently

親睦 means friendly, harmonious relations, above all among members of a group: 親 is "close, intimate" and 睦 is "harmonious, amicable". Mandarin qīnmù, Cantonese can1 muk6, Japanese しんぼく (shinboku; 親睦会, "a social gathering of a group"), Korean 친목 (chinmok; 친목회) and Vietnamese thân mục are the same compound; in Japanese and Korean it is very common as the name of a club's or workplace's social event.

Dan'a'yo-specific sense: the shared one. It sits beside [[友好]] ("friendly, amicable; friendship") and [[交友]] ("to make friends"), as the word for cordial, harmonious relations within a group.
