## Notes
- [江](../characters/江.md) "river" + [原](../characters/原.md) "plain" — the name of the Gangwon region of Korea.

江原 (Gangwon) is the historical Joseon province in the northeast of the Korean peninsula, on the east coast and across the Taebaek Mountains. The name combines the first characters of [[江陵]] (Gangneung) and [[原州]] (Wonju), its two chief cities. The province was divided at the armistice line of 1953 between South Korea (now 江原特別自治道, Gangwon Special Self-Governing Province, since June 2023) and North Korea (Kangwon Province, 江原道 in the North). Kangwon is therefore also a North Korean province, with its capital at [[元山]] (Wonsan); the South Korean one has its capital at [[春川]] (Chuncheon). English Wiktionary has 江原 only as the first part of 江原道, and Chinese and Korean Wiktionary likewise have 江原道; Japanese Wiktionary has no entry for the Korean place, and its Japanese entry for 江原 is a surname Ehara. Vietnamese Wiktionary has no entry.

The readings are the compositional ones from the character pages (Mandarin Jiāngyuán is also the reading Chinese Wiktionary gives for 江原道). Japanese ordinarily uses katakana after the Korean reading (カンウォン, from memory, not re-checked).
