## Notes
- [規](../characters/規.md) "rules; compass and square" + [範](../characters/範.md) "pattern, model" — "the compass-and-square and the mould": a norm. Neither character stands alone in Dan'a'yo (規 is carried by [[規律]], 範 by [[模範]]); 規範 is a third compound built from the same two stand-in families.

Wiktionary: Chinese "standard; norm; regulation", "standard; normal; regular", and as a verb "to standardise; to normalise; to regulate"; Japanese "norm; standard"; Korean 규범 "standard; norm"; Vietnamese quy phạm "standard; regulation; model". In Dan'a'yo it is the word of the chapter 文字規範 ("orthographic norms") in [[文法 - 03文字法]], whose maxims include [[一字一音]] and [[毎字明意]]. Compare [[規定]], [[標準]], [[規律]], [[規則]].
