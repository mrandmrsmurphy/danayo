## Notes
- [組](../characters/組.md) "form" + [合 (char)](../characters/合%20(char).md) "join, fit together, combine" — 組's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 合's own is [[合]] itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 組合 legitimizes 組 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

組合 means to form, assemble, or combine — most commonly as a noun meaning "union" or "association" (labor union, cooperative).

Mandarin zǔhé, Cantonese zou2 hap6, Japanese くみあい (kumiai, "union, association"), and Korean 조합 (johap) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 組合 is itself a common attested word in all four languages. Vietnamese tổ hợp is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. **Fixed a real bug**: this word's own `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` had jokap/조캅/ㄐㄛㄎㄚㄆ (合's reading confused with the unrelated syllable belonging to 恰/怯), corrected to jogob/조곱/ㄐㄛㄍㄛㄆ (合's own true syllable, confirmed via `syllables/ㄍㄛㄆ.md`). 組's own syllable here (jo/조/ㄐㄛ) differs from its standalone reading (jǝ/즈/ㄐㄜ) — an established, intentional compound-specific alternation, already documented on `characters/組.md` and `words/組織.md`, not a bug. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
