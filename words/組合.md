## Notes
- [組](../characters/組.md) "form" + [合 (char)](../characters/合%20(char).md) "join, fit together, combine" — 組's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 合's own is [[合]] itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 組合 legitimizes 組 alone.

組合 means to form, assemble, or combine — most commonly as a noun meaning "union" or "association" (labor union, cooperative).

Mandarin zǔhé, Cantonese zou2 hap6, Japanese くみあい (kumiai, "union, association"), and Korean 조합 (johap) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 組合 is itself a common attested word in all four languages. Vietnamese tổ hợp is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading.
