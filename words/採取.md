## Notes
- [採](../characters/採.md) "gather" + [取](../characters/取.md) "take" — no stand-in relationship applies (採's own stand-in is [[採集]]; 取's own is [[取得]]).

採取 means to pick, harvest, or collect.

Mandarin cǎiqǔ, Cantonese coi2 ceoi2, Japanese さいしゅ, Korean 채취, and Vietnamese thái thủ (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄚㄧㄑㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).
