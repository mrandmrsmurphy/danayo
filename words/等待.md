## Notes
- [等](../characters/等%20(char).md) "etc., rank" + [待](../characters/待.md) "wait" — 待's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 等's own is [[等]] itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 待 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word (already correctly noted on the character page). `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 等 is individually `kwin: false`.

等待 means to wait for or await. Note that `korean` 등대 is a genuine coincidental homograph with the unrelated real Korean word 등대 ("lighthouse," 灯臺) — the compositional Sino-Korean reading of 等待 happens to spell identically, purely by chance.

Mandarin děngdài, Cantonese dang2 doi6, and Korean 등대 (deungdae, in this sense "to wait") are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese とうたい (tōtai, the on-yomi compositional reading) is comparatively rare, since everyday Japanese instead uses the bare native verb 待つ (matsu, "to wait") directly rather than the two-character Chinese-style compound 等待. Vietnamese đẳng đãi is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form.
