## Notes
- [古](../characters/古.md) "ancient, old" (stand-in: [古代](古代.md)) + [層 (char)](../characters/層%20(char).md) "layer, stratum" (stand-in: itself) — an ancient layer.

古層 is an ancient stratum, an archaic layer. English Wiktionary has no entry for 古層 in any language, so this is a term of this vault, and the Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese fields are built from the readings of the two characters. [文法 - 02音韻論](../grammar/文法%20-%2002音韻論.md) uses it in 日本語古層, "an archaic layer of Japanese".
