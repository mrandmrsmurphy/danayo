## Notes
- [古 (char)](../characters/古.md) "ancient, old" (stand-in: itself) + [層 (char)](../characters/層.md) "layer, stratum" (stand-in: itself) — an archaic layer, as in a linguistic stratum preserved from an earlier stage of the language.

古層 means an ancient/archaic stratum, as invoked by `文法 - 02音韻論`'s 日本語古層 ("an archaic layer of Japanese"). Japanese こそう is a real, standard term used in historical linguistics, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Korean 고층 is compositional, though note real spoken Korean 고층 most commonly means "high-rise/high floor" (a genuine homograph risk, resolved contextually). Mandarin gùcéng and Vietnamese cổ tầng are compositional only. `kwin` is true: both 古 and 層 are individually true.

No syllable-boundary dot is needed: the concatenated string `ㄍㄛㄑㄜㄫ` admits only one legal partition.
