## Notes
- [消 (char)](../characters/消%20(char).md) "vanish, die out" + [耗](../characters/耗.md) "deplete, waste"

消耗 means to consume or deplete: to use up a finite resource such as energy, fuel or supplies, and to wear something out. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 消耗 (Mandarin xiāohào, Cantonese siu1 hou3) as "to consume; to deplete; to drain; to exhaust; to expend; to use up; to wear out", and Japanese しょうもう as "dissipation, exhaustion; wearing out; to consume, to use up". Korean 소모 and Vietnamese tiêu hao are the hanja readings of the same two characters.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [耗](../characters/耗.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
