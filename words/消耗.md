## Notes
- [消 (char)](../characters/消%20(char).md) "vanish, die out" + [耗](../characters/耗.md) "deplete, waste" — this word is the viable form of [耗](../characters/耗.md): the character's meaning (to deplete; to consume) is identical, but 耗 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 消耗 is its `stand_in` compound.

消耗 covers consuming or depleting any finite resource — energy, fuel, time, supplies, or physical strength — as well as the figurative "wearing down" of a person or force over time: 消耗戦 (a war of attrition), 体力を消耗する ("to exhaust one's physical strength"). Distinct from the near-synonym 消費, which leans toward economic consumption/spending specifically.

Japanese しょうもう and Korean 소모 are both standard compositional Sino-readings. Vietnamese tiêu hao is likewise standard, especially common in energy/resource and military-attrition contexts (tiêu hao sinh lực, "to wear down manpower"). No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼄ㄨㄏㄚㄨ unique across both words and characters).
