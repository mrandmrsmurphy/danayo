## Notes
- [返](../characters/返.md) "return; remit" + [還](../characters/還.md) "return; repatriate"
- 返還 is the stand-in word for [返](../characters/返.md) ("return"), which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo.

Mandarin fǎnhuán, cantonese faan2 waan4, japanese へんかん, korean 반환, and vietnamese phản hoàn are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly.
