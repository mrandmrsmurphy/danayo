## Notes
- [星 (char)](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" + [辰](../characters/辰.md) "star; time"

星辰 are the stars and the heavenly bodies together, as a poetic whole. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xīngchén as "stars in general; heavenly bodies". Cantonese sing1 san4 matches, Korean 성신 is the reading of the characters, and no Japanese reading is attested.

It joins the [星](星.md), star, with 辰, the other bodies of the sky, and is used of the sky as a whole, as in the phrase "sun, moon and stars". The sun and moon are the [日月](日月.md).
