## Notes
- [孟](../characters/孟.md) "eldest, first" + [秋](../characters/秋%20(char).md) "autumn" — no stand-in relationship applies (孟 is a proper-noun-only character with no specific stand-in word; 秋's own stand-in is itself).

First month of autumn. The air begins to turn; the harvest is approaching. Corresponds to [[申月]].

Mandarin mèngqiū, Cantonese maang6 cau1, Korean 맹추, Japanese もうしゅう, and Vietnamese Mạnh Thu are all standard, fully compositional readings.
