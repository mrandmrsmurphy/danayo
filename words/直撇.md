## Notes
- [直 (char)](../characters/直%20(char).md) "straight" + [撇 (char)](../characters/撇%20(char).md) "throw away; left-falling stroke"

Wiktionary has no entry for 直撇; it is a compound of 直 "straight" and 撇, which Wiktionary glosses in Chinese calligraphy as "stroke that falls downwards towards the left (㇒)". It names the straight left-falling stroke, a [[撇]] that runs straight toward the lower left without curving, in contrast to the curved 弧撇. It is listed on the [[Stroke]] page. Its readings are compositional.
