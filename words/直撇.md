## Notes
- [直 (char)](../characters/直%20(char).md) "straight" + [撇 (char)](../characters/撇%20(char).md) "throw away; left-falling stroke"

**直撇** is the *straight left-falling stroke*, a 撇 ([[撇]]) that runs straight toward the lower left without curving, in contrast to the curved 弧撇. In descriptions of Ouyang Xun's regular script it is the long 撇 that begins much like a vertical stroke before the tip is sent out to the left, and it is listed beside the slanting 斜撇 and the curved 弧撇 as one of three kinds of long 撇. Chinese regular-script instruction classes 撇 variants by direction (flat, slanting, vertical) and by shape. It is listed on the [[Stroke]] page under the seventh of the eight principles of the 永字八法.

The Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and North Korean forms given here are compositional (撇 reads *piě* in the stroke sense). The Vietnamese is omitted: no attested form was found.
