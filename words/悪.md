## Notes
- [悪](../characters/悪%20(char).md) "bad, evil" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

悪 means bad or evil — morally wrong or of poor quality.

Mandarin è, Cantonese ok3, Japanese あく, and Korean 악 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed missing glide apostrophe on `羅馬字` (ag→'ag, matching the character's own citation). Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄚㄎ/'ag/악) with the unrelated [[軛]] ("yoke").
