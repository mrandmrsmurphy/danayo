## Notes
- [掏](../characters/掏.md) "pickpocket" + 摸 "feel; grope" (pageless in this vault) — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [掏](../characters/掏.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.
- Note: Japanese reading すり (suri) is a special fused reading, not a concatenation of component readings.

掏摸 means to pickpocket, or to reach in and take.

Mandarin tāomō, Cantonese tou4 mo2, and Korean 도모 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed missing space in `cantonese`. Filled blank `vietnamese: đào mô` — compositional, though 摸's half (mô) is inferred from the phonetic component 莫 rather than a stored citation, since 摸 has no page of its own. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄨㄇㄛ unique across both words and characters).
