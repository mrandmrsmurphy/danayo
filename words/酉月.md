## Notes
- [酉](../characters/酉.md) + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md)
Eighth month of the ancient Chinese lunar calendar, named for the tenth Earthly Branch 酉 (rooster).

Mid-autumn and harvest season. 酉 is the hour of 5–7 p.m. — the sun descending, the day's work complete — mirroring the harvest close of the growing year. The Mid-Autumn Festival (中秋節) falls in this month.

Mandarin yǒuyuè, cantonese jau5 jyut6, korean 유월, japanese とりつき, and vietnamese tháng Dậu are all attested calendrical/zodiac readings, following the proper-noun/named-referent convention used for calendar terms. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 유웓 diverges from Sino-Korean 유월 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ⼜·⼔ㄊ unique among both words and characters).
