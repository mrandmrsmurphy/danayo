## Notes
- [領](../characters/領.md) "territory" + [主](../characters/主.md) "master"

領主 is a feudal lord. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 領主 (Mandarin lǐngzhǔ) as the historical "feudal lord" and Japanese りょうしゅ as "a lord in a feudal society; feudal lord". It lists a Vietnamese entry. The Cantonese and Korean 령주 fields are built from the readings of the two characters. Compare [家臣](家臣.md), a vassal.
