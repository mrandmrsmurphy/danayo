## Notes
- [山 (char)](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain" + [脈](../characters/脈.md) "vein; mountain range" — this word is the stand-in for [脈](../characters/脈.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

山脈 names a mountain range — 脈's "vein, channel" sense (as in 血脈 "blood vessel," 動脈 "artery") extended metaphorically to a connected chain of peaks running like a vein through the land.

Japanese さんみゃく, Korean 산맥, and Vietnamese sơn mạch are all standard readings. No HSK level is given for the compound. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄚㄋㄇㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
