## Notes
- [騰](../characters/騰.md) "inflate; rise" + [貴](../characters/貴.md) "expensive; costly" — legitimizing note: 騰's own `stand_in` is [[騰貴]] itself, already annotated in its own citation; 貴's own `stand_in` is [[貴重]], so transitivity fails — no `#cranberry`.

**Fixed a real bug**: `pos` was the non-canonical `動詞` (also fixed on `characters/騰.md` itself) — corrected to `事詞`, this vault's actual verb category.

Mandarin téngguì, Cantonese tang4gwai3, Japanese とうき, and Korean 등귀 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Sino-Vietnamese đằng quí is the compositional form; everyday Vietnamese instead uses the native phrase vật giá leo thang ("prices climbing the ladder").
