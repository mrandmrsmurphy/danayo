## Notes
- [故](../characters/故.md) "old, former" + [意](../characters/意.md) "intent" — no stand-in relationship applies (故's own stand-in is [[緣故]]; 意's own is [[意味]]).

故意 means intent, purpose, or (legally) mens rea.

Mandarin gùyì, Cantonese gu3 ji3, Japanese こい, Korean 고의, and Vietnamese cố ý are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄜ unique across both words and characters).
