## Notes
- [創](../characters/創.md) "originate, establish" + [造](../characters/造.md) "create" — this word is the stand-in for [造](../characters/造.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo (創's own stand-in is [[創立]]).

創造 means to create — to bring something new into being (the standard verb for artistic, intellectual, or divine creation).

**Real bugs found and fixed**: `羅馬字`/`諺文` had 創's syllable using the divergent "cang"/창 form rather than its own authoritative "cwang"/촹 (matching 創's own character page and its stand-in [[創立]]) — corrected. `vietnamese: tạo nên` was a native colloquial gloss rather than the actual Sino-Vietnamese compound — corrected to `sáng tạo` (verified: the standard, extremely common Hán Việt word for "to create, invent"). `aliases` incorrectly listed the word's own traditional form as if it were an alias — corrected to the simplified form 创造. Japanese そうぞうする and Korean 창조하다 are both standard verbal readings. No homophones (注音 ㄑ⺢ㄫㄑㄚㄨ unique across both words and characters).
