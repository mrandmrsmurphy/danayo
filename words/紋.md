## Notes
- [紋](../characters/紋%20(char).md) "mark, line, symbol" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 紋 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

紋 means a mark, line, or pattern — often a visible trace with symbolic significance, as in 家紋 (family crest) or 指紋 (fingerprint).

Mandarin wén, Cantonese man4, and Korean 문 (mun) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the on-reading モン (MON, as in 紋章 "crest, coat of arms") for the bare character alone, since no native kun-reading is stored. Vietnamese văn is one of several stored candidates. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
