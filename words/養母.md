## Notes
- [養](../characters/養.md) "foster, raise" + [母](../characters/母.md) "mother" — no `#cranberry`: 養's own `stand_in` is [[養育]], 母's is [[母親]]; neither points here.

養母 (yǎngmǔ) is the standard Mandarin word for "adoptive mother," and Cantonese 養母 (joeng5mou5) likewise. Japanese ようぼ (yōbo) and Korean 양모 (yangmo) are both standard terms, as is Sino-Vietnamese dưỡng mẫu (synonymous with the more everyday native mẹ nuôi) — a clean case with full agreement across all five languages.
