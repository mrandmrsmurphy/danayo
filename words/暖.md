## Notes
- [暖](../characters/暖%20(char).md) "warm" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [暖](../characters/暖%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

暖 means warm: pleasantly, moderately hot, especially of weather, rooms, and the body (溫暖, "warm"; 暖和, "comfortably warm"; 暖氣, "heating"). It also extends to emotional warmth (暖心, "heartwarming"). The graph is a 形声 compound of 日 "sun" with the phonetic 爰; the variant 煖 swaps the sun for 火 "fire".

Mandarin nuǎn, Cantonese nyun5, Japanese だん (暖房/だんぼう, "heating"; 温暖/おんだん, "mild climate"), Korean 난 (온난, 난방) and Vietnamese noãn are the standard Sino-xenic readings. The character's other stored Vietnamese reading hoãn belongs to the unrelated sense "postpone, delay" (緩). Japanese uses the native adjective 暖かい (あたたかい) for the everyday word, with on'yomi だん bound to compounds.
