## Notes
- [炎](../characters/炎.md) "flame, inflammation" + [症](../characters/症.md) "disease" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 炎 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (症's own stand-in is [[病症]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

炎症 means inflamed, inflammation.

Mandarin yánzhèng, Cantonese jim4 zing3, Japanese えんしょう, and Korean 염증 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 炎's and 症's own stored citations exactly), and also the real, standard medical term for "inflammation" in each language. Filled the blank `pos` (名詞) and `vietnamese` fields — the compositional viêm chứng (炎's own viêm + 症's own chứng). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases: []`.
