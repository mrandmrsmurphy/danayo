## Notes
- [不](../characters/不%20(char).md) "not" + [久](../characters/久%20(char).md) "long time" — "not long," soon. Their own `stand_in` values: 不's is itself; 久's is itself; none points here, so 不久 is an independent compound.

不久 is soon: not long from now, or not long after a given point. Mandarin bùjiǔ and Cantonese bat1 gau2 are the ordinary words. Japanese says 間もなく and Korean 곧, so those two fields hold the character readings. It is 不 "not" plus 久 "long," the opposite of a long time. Compare [[将来]] (the future) and [[旦夕]] (sooner or later).
