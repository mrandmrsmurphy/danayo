## Notes
- [前 (char)](../characters/前%20(char).md) "before" + [置 (char)](../characters/置%20(char).md) "to place" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "word" — the grammatical term "preposition," literally "a word placed before" (its object). `vietnamese: tiền trí từ` is compositional; Vietnamese linguistics instead has its own established native-rooted term, giới từ (a different root entirely, from 介詞), so this compositional form is a Dan'a'yo-internal calque rather than the term Vietnamese grammarians actually use. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 전치사 matches Sino-Korean 전치사 exactly.
