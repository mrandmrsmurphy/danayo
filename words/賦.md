## Notes
- [賦](../characters/賦%20(char).md) "prose-poetry" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [賦](../characters/賦%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

賦 means prose-poetry (fu, the classical Chinese literary genre), or (as a verb) to bestow or levy.

Mandarin fù, Cantonese fu3, Japanese ふ, Korean 부, and Vietnamese phú are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Retroactive fix**: `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` had all used the wrong initial (푸/pu/ㄆㄨ instead of 뿌/fu/ㄈㄨ, matching the character's own citation) — corrected. Removed redundant `品詞`. Homophone of [[敷]] "spread" (both 注音 ㄈㄨ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words, only discoverable once both pages' contamination bugs were corrected.
