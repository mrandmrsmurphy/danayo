## Notes
- [賦](../characters/賦%20(char).md) "prose-poetry" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [賦](../characters/賦%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

賦 means prose-poetry (fu, the classical Chinese literary genre), or (as a verb) to bestow or levy.

Mandarin fù, Cantonese fu3, Japanese ふ, Korean 부, and Vietnamese phú are all standard, fully compositional readings. It is a coincidental homophone of [[敷]] "spread" (both 注音 ㄈㄨ).
