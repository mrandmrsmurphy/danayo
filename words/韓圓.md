## Notes
- [韓](../characters/韓.md) "Korea" + [圓](../characters/圓%20(char).md) "won, round" — "Korean round," the won. Their own `stand_in` values: 韓's is [[韓国]]; 圓's is itself; none points here, so 韓圓 is an independent compound.

韓圓 is the Korean won. Mandarin hányuán and Cantonese hon4 jyun4 are the ordinary Chinese words; the unit is 圓 "round," as in [[欧圓]] (euro). Japanese says ウォン and Korean 원, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[日圓]] (yen) and [[美圓]] (dollar).
