## Notes
- [隣](../characters/隣%20(char).md) "neighbor, next door" + [接](../characters/接%20(char).md) "touch, connect" — "neighbor and touch," adjacent. Their own `stand_in` values: 隣's is itself; 接's is itself; none points here, so 隣接 is an independent compound.

隣接 is to be adjacent: to lie next to something, touching or very close. Mandarin línjiē (鄰接) and Cantonese leon4 zip3 are the ordinary words. Japanese りんせつ and Korean 인접 are the same compound. It is the static "next to," beside [[傍]] (beside) and [[近]] (near). Compare [[隣]] (neighboring) and [[之間]] (between).
