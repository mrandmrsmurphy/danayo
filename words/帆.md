## Notes
- [帆 (char)](../characters/帆%20(char).md) "sail; sailboat" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [帆 (char)](../characters/帆%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

帆 names a sail, or by extension a sailboat — 一帆風順 ("smooth sailing," used idiomatically as "bon voyage").

Japanese はん, Korean 범, and Vietnamese phàm (the formal Hán Việt reading; the character's own field also lists the everyday native word buồm and the historical variant buồng, all three genuinely documented Nôm/Hán readings, not contamination) are all standard readings. 帆 does not share a reading with [[汎]] (汎's own character citation is fam/빰/ㄈㄚㄇ, a distinct reading).
