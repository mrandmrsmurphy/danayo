## Notes
- [養](../characters/養.md) "foster, raise" + [父](../characters/父.md) "father" — no `#cranberry`: 養's own `stand_in` is [[養育]], 父's is [[父親]]; neither points here.

養父 (yǎngfù) is the standard Mandarin word for "adoptive father," and Cantonese 養父 (joeng5fu6) likewise. Japanese ようふ (yōfu) and Korean 양부 (yangbu) are both standard terms, as is Sino-Vietnamese dưỡng phụ (a formal/literary register term, alongside the more everyday native cha nuôi) — a clean case with full agreement across all five languages.
