## Notes
- [明](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright" + [徳](../characters/徳%20(char).md) "virtue" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 明 and 徳's own stand-ins are themselves).

明徳 means bright virtue, or to show virtue.

Mandarin míngdé, Cantonese ming4 dak1, Japanese めいとく, Korean 명덕, and Vietnamese minh đức (a genuine standard Confucian term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄇ⼶ㄫㄉㄨㄎ unique across both words and characters).
