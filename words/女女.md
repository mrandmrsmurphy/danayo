## Notes
- [女 (char)](../characters/女%20(char).md) "woman" reduplicated — a colloquial Mandarin/Cantonese cutesy diminutive for "little lady, little girl" (niū, a term of endearment). **`japanese`/`korean`/`vietnamese` deliberately left blank**: bare reduplication of a single character doesn't naturalize into these languages as a fixed word, the same pattern already established on [[伯伯]]/[[叔叔]] — Japanese/Korean/Vietnamese instead each use their own native diminutive constructions for "little girl" (おんなのこ, 계집아이, bé gái respectively) rather than a fabricated reduplicated calque. `kwin` is false by construction: no `korean` value exists to compare against.
