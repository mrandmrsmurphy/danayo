## Notes
- [女 (char)](../characters/女%20(char).md) "woman" reduplicated — a colloquial Mandarin/Cantonese cutesy diminutive for "little lady, little girl" (niū, a term of endearment). **The `japanese`, `korean` and `vietnamese` fields are deliberately omitted**: bare reduplication of a single character doesn't naturalize into these languages as a fixed word, the same pattern already established on [[伯伯]]/[[叔叔]] — Japanese/Korean/Vietnamese instead each use their own native diminutive constructions for "little girl" (おんなのこ, 계집아이, bé gái respectively) rather than a fabricated reduplicated calque.
