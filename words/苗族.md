苗族 names the Hmong/Miao ethnic group.

**Found and fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字` had a wrong final consonant (myaujok→myaujog, matching 族's own -g ending); `諺文`/`注音` had already stayed correct throughout. `kwin: false` was already correct per the AND-rule. Filled blank japanese (ミャオ族, mixing the katakana loanword "Myao" with 族, the real attested term) and vietnamese (Miêu tộc). As a proper ethnonym, mandarin Miáozú and korean 묘족 are standard readings using the real transliterated/compositional forms.
