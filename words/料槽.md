## Notes
- [料](../characters/料.md) "material, stuff" + [槽](../characters/槽.md) "vat, trough, tank" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [槽](../characters/槽.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (料's own stand-in is [[材料]]).

料槽 means feed trough or manger.

Mandarin liàocáo, Cantonese liu6 cou4, Japanese りょうそう, Korean 료조, and Vietnamese liệu tào are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄌ⼘ㄨㄐㄚㄨ unique across both words and characters).
