## Notes
- [戦](../characters/戦.md) "war" + [国](../characters/国.md) "nation" — no stand-in relationship applies (戦's own stand-in is [[戦争]]; 国's own is [[国家]]).

戦国 refers to the Warring States period, when China was divided among seven major rival states: [[斉]] (Qí), [[楚]] (Chǔ), [[燕]] (Yān), [[韓]] (Hán), [[趙]] (Zhào), [[魏]] (Wèi), and [[秦]] (Qín).

Mandarin Zhànguó, Cantonese zin3 gwok3, Japanese せんごく, Korean 전국, and Vietnamese Chiến Quốc are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄋㄍㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
