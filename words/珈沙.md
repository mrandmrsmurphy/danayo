## Notes
- [珈](characters/珈.md) + [沙](characters/沙%20(char).md) — the real word is written 袈裟; neither 袈 nor 裟 has its own vault page, so 珈 and 沙 substitute (their phonetic values already coincide with 袈's and 裟's real readings). No stand-in relationship applies (珈's own stand-in is [[珈啡]]; 沙's own is itself).

珈沙 (properly 袈裟) is the kāṣāya, the patchwork outer robe worn by Buddhist monastics.

Mandarin jiāshā and Korean 가사 were already correct and fully compositional. Fixed a missing space in `cantonese` (gaa1saa1 → gaa1 saa1). Japanese けさ (kesa) is the real, standard Japanese Buddhist term (a native/Sanskrit-derived reading, not a compositional on'yomi). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with cà sa, the real, standard Vietnamese Buddhist term, following the same real-word convention as Japanese けさ. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄚㄙㄚ unique across both words and characters).
