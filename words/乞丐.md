## Notes
- [乞 (char)](../characters/乞%20(char).md) "begging" + [丐](../characters/丐.md) "beggar" — this word is the `stand_in` legitimizer for [[丐]], which cannot appear independently.

**Real content bug found and fixed**: the `japanese` field stored かたい (an unrelated real Japanese word meaning "hard, solid, strict"), which doesn't match either character's own on'yomi at all — corrected to こつかい, the compositional first-listed-on'yomi concatenation (乞's KOTSU + 丐's KAI). Also fixed an unspaced `cantonese` field (hat1koi3→hat1 koi3).

乞丐 (qǐgài) is the classical/literary Mandarin term for "beggar," largely superseded in everyday speech by the native compound 乞丐 itself remains standard in formal/written registers, while 乞食 covers the same ground in a more casual register. This word is not attested as ordinary vocabulary in Japanese, Korean, or Vietnamese the way it is in Mandarin/Cantonese — Korean 걸개 and Vietnamese khất cái are compositional readings of the two characters' own stored values rather than independently confirmed fixed terms in either language.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 킫개 diverges from Sino-Korean 걸개, since 乞's own Dan'a'yo reading 킫 doesn't match Sino-Korean 걸, even though 丐's own reading matches exactly (丐 is independently `kwin: true`).
