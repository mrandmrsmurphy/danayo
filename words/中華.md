## Notes
- [中 (char)](../characters/中%20(char).md) "central" + [華](../characters/華.md) "flourishing, splendid" — literally "central flourishing," one of the primary terms by which Chinese civilization refers to itself.

中華 names a civilizational/cultural identity rather than a specific state, which is exactly why it appears in the formal names of both rival Chinas: [[中華民国]] (Republic of China, i.e. Taiwan) and 中華人民共和国 (People's Republic of China). It underlies 中華民族 (the Chinese nation, embracing all ethnic groups of Greater China) and 中華文化 (Chinese culture broadly). Japanese usage adds a common everyday sense: 中華料理 (Chinese cuisine) is the standard term for Chinese food in Japan, and 中華 alone is frequently used as shorthand for it. Vietnamese Trung Hoa is the traditional, now slightly literary term for China/Chinese, distinct from the more everyday Trung Quốc used in [[中国]].
