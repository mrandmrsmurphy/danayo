## Notes
- [賛](../characters/賛.md) "help, support" + [助](../characters/助.md) "assist, help"

Wiktionary: Japanese 賛助 (さんじょ) "support, patronage" and, as a verb, "to support"; Chinese 贊助 "to support; to assist; to sponsor", kept as the traditional-form alias. This word is the stand-in for [賛](../characters/賛.md), which is bound; 助's own stand-in is 援助. Compare [[援助]].
