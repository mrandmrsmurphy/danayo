## Notes
- [賛](../characters/賛.md) "help, support" + [助](../characters/助.md) "assist, help"

賛助 means to support or sponsor: to back an event, organization or cause with money or goodwill (賛助会員, "supporting member"; 賛助出演, "a guest appearance given in support").  賛 contributes approval and 助 the helping, so it differs from the plain [[援助]] ("aid") in stressing patronage that endorses.

This word is the stand-in for [賛](../characters/賛.md), which is bound and cannot appear alone in Dan'a'yo; 助's own stand-in is 援助.  Mandarin zànzhù, Cantonese zaan3 zo6, Japanese さんじょ, Korean 찬조 and Vietnamese tán trợ are the same compound, and the alias 贊助 is the traditional form.  `kwin` is false because the Dan'a'yo 잔조 differs from the Korean 찬조 in its initial.
