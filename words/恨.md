## Notes
- [恨](../characters/恨%20(char).md) "hate, grudge" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

恨 means hate or grudge — a lasting resentment against someone or something.

Mandarin hèn, Cantonese han6, Japanese こん, Korean 한, and Vietnamese hận are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄏㄚㄋ/han/한) with the unrelated [[汗]] ("sweat").
