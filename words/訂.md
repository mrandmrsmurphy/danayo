## Notes
- [訂](characters/訂%20(char).md) "revise, correct" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 訂 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

訂 means to revise, correct (as in 校訂 "revise, proofread").

Mandarin dìng, Cantonese ding3, and Korean 정 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 訂's own stored citations exactly. Fixed literal `vietnamese: null` → đính (the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading, as in 訂正 đính chính; 訂's own citation also lists a variant dính), added the missing `pos`/`japanese` (てい, the primary on'yomi TEI)/`hsk_level` ("2") fields, reformatted `characters` to a proper block-list with the "(char)" suffix.
