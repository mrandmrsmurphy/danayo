## Notes
- [期 (char)](../characters/期%20(char).md) "period; time (stand-in: itself)" + [待](../characters/待.md) "wait (stand-in: [[等待]])"

Wiktionary: Chinese 期待 "to look forward to; to await"; Japanese 期待 "expectation" and "expect; hope for"; Korean 기대 is the hanja form. It fills the "expect" gap beside [[希望]].
