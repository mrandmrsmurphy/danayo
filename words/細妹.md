## Notes
- [細 (char)](../characters/細%20(char).md) "fine, thin, slender" + [妹](../characters/妹.md) "younger sister" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 妹 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (`characters/妹.md`'s own `stand_in` field confirms this directly).

**Real bug corrected 2026-09-21**: this page previously claimed 妹's own stand-in was actually [[妹妹]] instead, calling that "a different, more standard word for the same core meaning" — that claim directly contradicted `characters/妹.md`'s own stored `stand_in` field (which has always read "細妹"), and was taken at face value once, without cross-checking, while auditing [[Kinship]]. 細妹 is Dan'a'yo's plain, ordinary word for "younger sister" — [[妹妹]] is simply its intimate, reduplicated address form, the same relationship [[兄]] has to [[哥哥]] and [[姉]] has to [[姉姉]].

細妹 as a lexical item belongs to Cantonese, where sai3 mui6 (the second syllable's tone shifting from 6 to 2 in casual/affectionate address, a real, documented Cantonese tone-change pattern) is the everyday colloquial term for "younger sister." Hakka broadens the same two characters further: 細妹（仔） in Hakka means "girl, young woman" generally, not restricted to "younger sister" specifically — a genuine semantic widening between two Sinitic varieties using the identical compound. Japanese いもうと, Korean 여동생, and Vietnamese em gái are all just the plain, native words for "younger sister" in their own languages, with no special connection to 細妹 as a lexical item.
