## Notes
- [砒](../characters/砒.md) "arsenic" + [素](../characters/素.md) "element" — this Japanese-coined compound was created by Morisaburō Ichikawa in Rika Nikki (1872), and 砒 itself doubles as this vault's periodic-table abbreviation letter for arsenic (see [[Periodic Table]]). 砒's own alias 砷 historically displaced 砒 as the standard Chinese element symbol for arsenic (not the reverse), though 砒素 as a whole-word compound remains standard in Japanese.

Mandarin pī and Cantonese pei1 sou3 are both fully compositional, matching 砒's and 素's own stored citations. Korean 비소 (biso) and Japanese ひそ (hiso) are likewise compositional and are also the real, standard element names in both languages. Vietnamese asen is instead the real, standard loanword term (from French "arsenic"), not a Sino-Vietnamese calque. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄆㄧㄙㄛ unique among both words and characters).
