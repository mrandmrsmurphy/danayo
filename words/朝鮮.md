## Notes
- [朝](../characters/朝%20(char).md) "morning" + [鮮](../characters/鮮.md) "fresh" — no stand-in relationship applies (朝's own stand-in is itself; 鮮's own is [[新鮮]]). Here 鮮 carries a distinct proper-noun reading (셤/syem/ㄙ⼶ㄇ is the ordinary "fresh" reading, while 션/syen/ㄙ⼶ㄋ, with an -n rather than -m coda, is reserved specifically for this country name and the related [[朝鮮正音]]/[旗幟鮮明](../chengyu/旗幟鮮明.md)) — a genuine, consistently-applied sense split, not a bug.

朝鮮 means North Korea, or historically Chosŏn.

Mandarin Cháoxiǎn, Cantonese ciu4 sin1, Japanese ちょうせん, Korean 조선, and Vietnamese Triều Tiên are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄨㄙ⼶ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
