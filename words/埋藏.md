## Notes
- [埋](../characters/埋.md) "bury; conceal" + [蔵](../characters/蔵.md) "hide; store" — this word is the stand-in for [埋](../characters/埋.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo (蔵's own stand-in is [[収蔵]]).

埋藏 means to bury, conceal, or store underground — natural deposits (埋藏量 "reserves") as well as literal burial.

Filled blank `vietnamese: mai tàng` (compositional). Japanese まいぞう is a standard reading. Korean 매장 happens to be identical to the unrelated word [[埋葬]]'s own Korean reading, but the two are NOT Dan'a'yo-level homophones (their own 注音 differ: ㄇ⼶ㄑㄚㄫ vs ㄇ⼶ㄐㄚㄫ). No homophones (注音 ㄇ⼶ㄑㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
