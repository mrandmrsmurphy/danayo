## Notes
- [演 (char)](../characters/演%20(char).md) "perform" + [劇 (char)](../characters/劇%20(char).md) "drama"

演劇 is theatre as an activity: the performing of plays. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yǎnjù as "to act in a play" and Japanese えんげき as "theatrical play". Korean 연극 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese jin2 kek6 matches.

It is the practice of the [劇](劇.md), drama, and the troupe is the [劇団](劇団.md). The building is the [劇場](劇場.md).
