## Notes
- [扇](../characters/扇.md) "fan" + [子](../characters/子.md) "child (nominal suffix)" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [扇](../characters/扇.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (子's own stand-in is [[児子]]).

扇子 means hand-held fan.

Mandarin shànzi, Cantonese sin3 zi2, Japanese せんす, Korean 선자, and Vietnamese phiến tử are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `諺文`/`羅馬字` used the minority reading 지/ji for 子 instead of the majority-established 즈/jǝ (per `characters/子.md`'s own citation) — corrected to 션즈/syenjǝ, matching the 注音 (ㄙ⼶ㄋㄐㄜ) that was already correct. No homophones.
