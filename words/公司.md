## Notes
- [公 (char)](../characters/公%20(char).md) "public, official" + [司](../characters/司.md) "manage, office" — "company, firm." Mandarin gōngsī, Cantonese gung1 si1, Japanese こうし, and Korean 공사 are all directly attested standard readings; `vietnamese: công ty` is likewise the standard everyday Vietnamese word for "company," directly built from the same two characters. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 공사 matches Sino-Korean 공사 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄫㄙㄚ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 司's own `stand_in` is this exact word (公司), so 司 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 公司.
