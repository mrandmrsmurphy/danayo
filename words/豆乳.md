## Notes
- [豆 (char)](../characters/豆%20(char).md) "bean" + [乳](../characters/乳.md) "milk"

豆乳 is soy milk: a milk-like drink pressed from soaked soybeans. Wiktionary glosses both Mandarin dòurǔ and Japanese とうにゅう as "soy milk". Korean 두유 is the same word, and the Cantonese reading is built from the characters.

It is the liquid of the [大豆](大豆.md), soybean, from which [豆腐](豆腐.md), tofu, is curdled, and it is a drink beside [牛乳](牛乳.md), cow's milk.
