## Notes
- [播](../characters/播.md) "sow, disseminate" + [種](../characters/種.md) "type, seed" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [播](../characters/播.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (種's own stand-in is [[種類]]).

播種 means to sow seeds or disseminate.

Mandarin bōzhǒng, Cantonese bo3 zung2, Japanese はしゅ, Korean 파종, and Vietnamese bá chõng are all standard, fully compositional readings. Trimmed malformed comma-triplet `mandarin` (bōzhòng, bòzhǒng, bōzhǒng — stray tone variants) to the single standard bōzhǒng. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄐㄛㄫ unique across both words and characters).
