## Notes
- [補](../characters/補.md) "supplement" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language"

補語 is the grammatical complement. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 補語 (Mandarin bǔyǔ, Cantonese bou2 jyu5) as the grammar term "complement", Japanese ほご as the grammar and linguistics term "complement", Korean 보어 as a hanja form of "complement", and Vietnamese bổ ngữ the same. Compare [目的語](目的語.md), the object, and [述語](述語.md), the predicate.
