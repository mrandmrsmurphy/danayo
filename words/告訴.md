## Notes
- [告](../characters/告.md) "tell, inform" + [訴](../characters/訴.md) "appeal, sue" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [告](../characters/告.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (訴's own stand-in is [[訴訟]]).

告訴 means to accuse, charge, or file a lawsuit — bringing a formal complaint.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `cantonese` was blank — filled gou3 sou3 (compositional). Filled blank `vietnamese: tố cáo` (verified — the standard Vietnamese term, reversing the modifier order from Chinese, as usual). Japanese こくそ and Korean 고소 are both standard readings. **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 告訴 and [[高素]] ("gallium," a periodictable neologism) share an identical reading — the reciprocal callout was already in place on [[高素]]'s own page, added here to complete the pair.
