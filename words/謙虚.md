## Notes
- [謙](../characters/謙.md) "humble" + [虚 (char)](../characters/虚%20(char).md) "empty; unassuming"

謙虚 is modest: not thinking much of oneself, open to learning from others. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin qiānxū as "humble; modest; unassuming" and Japanese けんきょ as "modest; modesty". Cantonese him1 heoi1 matches, and Korean 겸허 is the reading of the characters.

It is the humility of an open mind, set against the haughty [傲慢](傲慢.md), and a sibling of [謙遜](謙遜.md), self-effacing humility. Its opposite in pride is [自負](自負.md).
