## Notes
- [核](../words/核.md) "nucleus" + [位 (char)](../characters/位.md) "position" (stand-in: [[定位]]) — the vowel-bearing core of a syllable's [[韻母]].

核位 names the nuclear position in 単亜語's syllable structure — the vowel slot, as laid out in `文法 - 02音韻論`'s syllable-structure breakdown (声母/韻母 → 介位・核位・尾位). Mandarin héwèi, Cantonese hat6 wai6, Japanese かくい, Korean 핵위, and Vietnamese hạch vị are all straightforward compositional readings from 核's and 位's own stored citations, not independently verified as standard terms in those languages. `kwin` is false: both 核 and 位 are individually false.

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot**: the concatenated string `ㄏㄚㄎ⼔ㄧ` admits three legal partitions — the intended 核(ㄏㄚㄎ)+位(⼔ㄧ), and two false alternates (ㄏㄚ+ㄎ⼔ㄧ, and ㄏㄚㄎ+⼔+ㄧ) — so the dot at the true 核|位 boundary is required: ㄏㄚㄎ·⼔ㄧ.
