## Notes
- [刹](../characters/刹.md) "temple, an instant" + [那 (char)](../characters/那%20(char).md) "that" — "an instant, a moment, a split second," from Sanskrit kṣaṇa (क्षण) via Buddhist transliteration. Mandarin has two attested readings, chànà (the more common modern pronunciation) and chànuó (an older/alternate reading); Cantonese caat3 naa5, Japanese せつな, Korean 찰나, and Vietnamese sát na are all likewise directly attested. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 찯나 diverges from Sino-Korean 찰나 in the first syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄚㄊㄋㄚ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 刹's own `stand_in` is this exact word (刹那), so 刹 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 刹那.

**`kwin` update (2026-10-09):** `kwin` is now true. The Notes above may describe the ㄷ/ㄹ difference between the Dan'a'yo 諺文 and the Korean reading as a mismatch; under the vault's standing rule a Korean final ㄹ counts as equal to Dan'a'yo's final ㄷ for this comparison, so such a difference alone does not make `kwin` false.
