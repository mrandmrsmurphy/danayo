## Notes
- [注](../characters/注.md) "pour; note" + [釈](../characters/釈.md) "explain; interpret"

注釈 is an annotation or commentary: a note that explains a passage in a text. Wiktionary records Chinese 注釋 as "to annotate, to comment; an annotation, a comment", and the shinjitai spelling 注釈 is the Japanese form (ちゅうしゃく). Korean 주석 and Cantonese zyu3 sik1 match.

It is the work of the scholar who explains a classic, and it is related to [解釈](解釈.md), interpretation, which explains meaning in general, and to [校訂](校訂.md), the revision of a text.
