## Notes
- [外](../characters/外.md) "outside" + [来](../characters/来%20(char).md) "come" — no stand-in relationship applies (外's own stand-in is [[外部]]; 来 is its own stand-in).

外来 means foreign or extrinsic — coming from outside.

Japanese がいらい, Korean 외래, Mandarin wàilái, Cantonese ngoi6 loi4, and Vietnamese ngoại lai are all standard, fully compositional readings.
