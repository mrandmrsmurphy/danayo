## Notes
- [不 (char)](../characters/不%20(char).md) "not" + [可 (char)](../characters/可%20(char).md) "may, permissible" — an independent compound, "not permissible, cannot."

Mandarin bùkě, Cantonese bat1 ho2, Japanese ふか, and Sino-Vietnamese bất khả are all standard compositional readings. Korean 불가 (not 부가) follows the same 不→불 assimilation rule already documented on [[不信]]/[[不及]] — already correctly stored.
