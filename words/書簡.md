## Notes
- [書](../characters/書.md) "book" + [簡](../characters/簡.md) "concise, simple" — no stand-in relationship applies (書's own stand-in is [[書本]]; 簡's own is [[簡単]]).

書簡 means letter.

Mandarin shūjiǎn, Japanese しょかん, and Korean 서간 are all standard, fully compositional readings.
