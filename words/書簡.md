## Notes
- [書](../characters/書.md) "book" + [簡](../characters/簡.md) "concise, simple" — no stand-in relationship applies (書's own stand-in is [[書本]]; 簡's own is [[簡単]]).

書簡 means letter.

Mandarin shūjiǎn, Japanese しょかん, and Korean 서간 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed `cantonese`: was contaminated with an unrelated reading (sau2 zi2) — corrected to syu1 gaan2, matching 書's and 簡's own citations. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field (thư giản) and `pos: 名詞`. Removed `片紙`/`便紙`/`手紙` from `aliases` — these are genuine near-synonyms for "letter" but use the unrelated character 紙, not orthographic variants of 書簡. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼄ㄍㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
