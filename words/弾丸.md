## Notes
- [弾](../characters/弾.md) "bullet" + [丸](../characters/丸.md) "ball, pellet" — stand-in for [[弾]], which cannot appear independently.

弾丸 means "bullet, pellet, projectile" — the specific round shot fired from a weapon, as distinct from 弾 alone or 弾薬 ("ammunition" generally). The idiom 彈丸之地 ("land the size of a pellet") uses it figuratively for a tiny sliver of territory.

Mandarin dànwán and Cantonese daan6 jyun4 preserve the sense directly. Japanese だんがん (dangan) is the ordinary, everyday word for "bullet." Korean 탄환 (tanhwan) parallels the Sino-xenic reading and is likewise standard vocabulary, alongside native 탄알 (tanal, "bullet," a native-Sino hybrid using native 알 "egg, round object" for 丸). Vietnamese đạn hoàn is attested (Hán Nôm dictionary sources cite it directly, including in the idiom đạn hoàn chi địa, "land the size of a pellet," paralleling 彈丸之地), but reads as classical/literary register rather than everyday speech — modern Vietnamese normally just says viên đạn or bare đạn for "bullet." The character page for 丸 also stores hòn/hỏn (genuine lower-register doublets of hoàn from the same root, generalized into the common word for "a rounded lump," as in hòn đá "a stone") and giúp/xóp/xắp (unrelated Nôm phonetic-loan readings for different native words) — none of these displace hoàn as the reading proper to this compound.
