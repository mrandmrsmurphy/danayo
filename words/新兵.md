## Notes
- [新](../characters/新%20(char).md) "new" + [兵](../characters/兵.md) "soldier" — "new soldier," a recruit. Their own `stand_in` values: 新's is itself; 兵's is [[兵士]]; none points here, so 新兵 is an independent compound.

新兵 is a recruit: a newly enlisted or conscripted soldier. Mandarin xīnbīng, Cantonese san1 bing1, Japanese しんぺい and Korean 신병 all use it, and Vietnamese tân binh does too. It is the opposite of [[老兵]] (veteran). Compare [[兵士]] and [[従軍]].
