## Notes
- [分 (char)](../characters/分%20(char).md) "divide" + [娩](../characters/娩.md) "give birth"
- This word is the viable form of [娩](../characters/娩.md): the character's meaning (to give birth) is identical, but 娩 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 分娩 is its `stand_in` compound.

分娩 is the formal and medical word for childbirth. Mandarin fēnmiǎn, Cantonese fan1 min5, Japanese ぶんべん (bunben) and Korean 분만 (bunman) are all the same Sino-Xenic compound, familiar from 分娩室 ("delivery room") and from hospital and legal registers. The everyday verbs for having a baby are different words in each language (Japanese 産む, Korean 낳다, Mandarin 生), and are not the same as this word.

The bound character 娩 appears almost only in this compound and in 娩出 ("to expel, deliver" the fetus); it also occurs in the unrelated literary 婉娩 ("gentle, graceful"). In Dan'a'yo, 分娩 is the unmarked word for the act of giving birth, with a clinical register. Its place in the Life domain beside [[人生]] reflects birth as the start of a life course; no narrower or shifted Dan'a'yo sense is needed.
