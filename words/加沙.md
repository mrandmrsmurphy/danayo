## Notes
- [[加 (char)|加]] ("add"; here standing in for its alias [[加 (char)|袈]], "Buddhist cassock," per its own `aliases` field) + [[沙 (char)|沙]] ("sand"; here standing in for its own alias [[沙 (char)|裟]], per the same convention) — the real word is written 袈裟, now registered as this word's own alias. No stand-in relationship applies (both 加's and 沙's own `stand_in` values point elsewhere, unrelated to this compound).

加沙 (properly 袈裟) is the kāṣāya, the patchwork outer robe worn by Buddhist monastics. This word page was previously titled 珈沙 and cited [[珈]] (a different, unrelated character, "hairpin ornament") as a phonetic stand-in for 袈, back when 袈 had no vault presence at all; retitled 2026-09-26 once 袈 was folded in as a real alias of [[加 (char)|加]], following the same "cite the real alias-holding parent character, keep the true historical spelling as a word-level alias" convention used by [[尭舌]] (尭/饒).

Mandarin jiāshā and Korean 가사 were already correct and fully compositional. Japanese けさ (kesa) is the real, standard Japanese Buddhist term (a native/Sanskrit-derived reading, not a compositional on'yomi). Vietnamese cà sa is the real, standard Vietnamese Buddhist term, following the same real-word convention as Japanese けさ. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄚㄙㄚ unique across both words and characters).
