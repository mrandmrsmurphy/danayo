## Notes
- [加](../characters/加.md) ("add"; here standing in for its alias 袈, "Buddhist cassock," per its own `aliases` field) + [沙 (char)](../characters/沙%20(char).md) ("sand"; here standing in for its own alias 裟, per the same convention) — the real word is written 袈裟, which is registered as this word's own alias. Neither 加's nor 沙's own `stand_in` points to this compound.

加沙 (properly 袈裟) is the kāṣāya, the patchwork outer robe worn by Buddhist monastics. It cites 加, the character that holds 袈 as an alias, rather than the unrelated [珈](../characters/珈.md) ("hairpin ornament"), and keeps the true historical spelling as a word-level alias, the same convention used by [尭舌](尭舌.md) (尭/饒).

Mandarin jiāshā and Korean 가사 are compositional readings of the two characters. Japanese けさ (kesa) is the real, standard Japanese Buddhist term, a native or Sanskrit-derived reading rather than a compositional on'yomi. Vietnamese cà sa is likewise the real, standard Vietnamese Buddhist term. The Dan'a'yo romanization kasa (가사) is built from 加's ga and 沙's sa, and matches the Korean reading 가사 exactly.
