## Notes
- [初](../characters/初.md) "beginning, first" + [等 (char)](../characters/等%20(char).md) "grade, rank" — elementary; of the first and most basic level

Wiktionary: Chinese "elementary; basic; rudimentary"; Japanese, education, "beginning, primary (of a course or period of education)"; Korean 초등 is the hanja form; Vietnamese sơ đẳng is the Han form, "elementary; amateurish". It is the first of the tiers 初等, [[中等]] and [[高等]].
