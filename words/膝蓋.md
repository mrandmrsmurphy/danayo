## Notes
- [膝](../characters/膝.md) "knee, kneecap" + [蓋](../characters/蓋.md) "cover, hide" — stand-in for [[膝]], which cannot appear independently

膝蓋 is transparently compositional: the kneecap (patella) as the "cover" or "lid" (蓋) sitting atop the knee joint (膝) — the same body-part-plus-covering logic recurring across the sphere's anatomical vocabulary. 膝 alone, phonetic 桼 under the flesh radical 肉, is too bound a morpheme to stand as an independent Dan'a'yo word, so 膝蓋 (or, in some registers, bare 膝 in compounds like 膝下 "at one's knee, i.e. under one's care") carries the everyday sense.

Mandarin xīgài, Cantonese sat1 goi3, Japanese しつがい (shitsugai), and Korean 슬개 (seulgae) are all standard clinical/anatomical terms specifically for the kneecap/patella, rather than the knee joint generally — each language's everyday word for "knee" itself is instead native: Japanese ひざ (hiza), Korean 무릎 (mureup). No Sino-Vietnamese reading of this compound is dictionary-attested (checked via hvdic.thivien.net, no results for 膝蓋) — Vietnamese uses the fully native đầu gối for "knee," left blank here rather than forcing an unattested compositional guess.
