## Notes
- [叢](../characters/叢.md) "collection; cluster" + [書](../characters/書.md) "book; writing" — this word is the stand-in for [叢](../characters/叢.md), which cannot appear independently.

叢書 is a book series or anthology — a collection of books. The character 叢 alone means a cluster or collection, but is too bounded to stand by itself in Dan'a'yo, so this compound serves as its viable form.
