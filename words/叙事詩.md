## Notes
- [叙](../characters/叙.md) "narrate" + [事 (char)](../characters/事%20(char).md) "affair; matter" + [詩](../characters/詩.md) "poem"

叙事詩 is an epic: a long poem that tells a story of heroes and great events. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xùshìshī as "narrative poem" and Japanese じょじし as "epic poetry". Korean 서사시 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese zeoi6 si6 si1 matches.

It is a kind of [詩歌](詩歌.md), and the story it tells is often a [神話](神話.md), myth, or a [伝説](伝説.md), legend.
