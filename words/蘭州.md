## Notes
- [蘭](../characters/蘭.md) "orchid" + [州](../characters/州%20(char).md) "state" — "orchid prefecture". No stand-in relationship applies (蘭's is [[蘭花]]; 州 is its own stand-in).

蘭州 (Lanzhou) is the capital of Gansu province in northwestern China. Per Wiktionary the name was coined in 581 with the establishment of Lan Prefecture, named after Mount Gaolan (皋蘭山); the word also names that former prefecture (historical). 兰 is an alias of 蘭, so 兰州 is kept as the word's alias.

Japanese らんしゅう is the Sino-Japanese reading listed on Wiktionary Japanese; Korean 란주 is the North Korean reading derived from the character pages (English Wiktionary has no Korean entry for 蘭州), and the usual South Korean name today is 란저우 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Lan Châu is attested on Vietnamese Wikipedia. Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese and Japanese entries. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄚㄋㄐㄨㄛ unique across both words and characters).
