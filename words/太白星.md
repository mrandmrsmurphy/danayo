## Notes
- [太](../characters/太%20(char).md) "great" + [白](../characters/白%20(char).md) "white" + [星](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" — no stand-in relationship applies (太's own stand-in is itself; 白's is itself; 星's is itself).

太白星 is the classical name for the planet Venus when visible in the west after sunset — literally "great white star." Modern astronomy identifies it with [[啓明]], the morning-star name for the same planet.

Mandarin Tàibáixīng, Cantonese taai3 baak6 sing1, Korean 태백성, and Japanese たいはくせい are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese: thái bạch tinh` (compositional Sino-Vietnamese, matching [[啓明]]'s parallel choice of compositional khải minh over the native classical name Trường Canh).
