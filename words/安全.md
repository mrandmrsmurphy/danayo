## Notes
- [安](../characters/安.md) "peaceful" + [全 (char)](../characters/全%20(char).md) "whole" — no stand-in relationship applies (安's own `stand_in` is [[平安]], 全's is itself).

安全 combines 安's "at peace, settled" with 全's "whole, intact" — freedom from danger construed as a state of undisturbed wholeness, rather than the more purely emotional "peace of mind" carried by [[平安]] or [[安心]]. It is extremely productive across all four languages as a modifier and standalone noun alike: 安全帯 "safety belt," 安全性 "safety (as a property)," 公共安全 "public safety."

Japanese あんぜん, Korean 안전, and Vietnamese an toàn are all standard, non-drifted, everyday readings with no comparable semantic narrowing. HSK level 2 (already correct, matching both constituents' own beginner-level status). No homophones (注音 ㄚㄋㄐ⼔ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
