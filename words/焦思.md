## Notes
- [焦 (char)](../characters/焦%20(char).md) "burned, scorched" + [思](../characters/思.md) "think" — no stand-in relationship applies (焦 is its own stand-in; 思's own is [[思考]]).

焦思 means to worry, to deliberate anxiously ("burning thoughts").

Mandarin jiāosī, Cantonese ziu1 si1, Japanese しょうし, Korean 초사 and Vietnamese tiêu tư are all standard, fully compositional readings.
