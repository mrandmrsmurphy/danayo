## Notes
- [門](../characters/門.md) + [捷](../characters/捷.md) + [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal" — a transliteration of *Mendeleev*, after chemist Dmitri Mendeleev, creator of the periodic table itself. 門捷 renders the first two syllables of 門捷列夫, the standard Mandarin transliteration of "Mendeleev," truncated rather than spelled out in full. Japanese and Korean instead borrow the IUPAC name as a loanword (メンデレビウム, 멘델레븀).

Filled blank `mandarin`/`cantonese`/`vietnamese` with the honest compositional readings of 門+捷+金 (Ménjiéjīn, mun4 zit6 gam1, môn tiệp kim), matching the same transliteration syllables used in 門捷列夫. `kwin` is false, as expected: Dan'a'yo 몬접김 diverges sharply from the Korean field, which (like Japanese) is a IUPAC loanword rather than a compositional Sino-Korean reading. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄛㄋㄐㄝㄆㄍㄧㄇ unique). **Found and fixed a missing citation** on 金(char).md's own Words list; citations on 門.md and 捷.md already correctly present.
