## Notes
- [面](../characters/面.md) "face; side" + [接 (char)](../characters/接%20(char).md) "join, meet" — interview.

面接 is an interview, mostly one used to evaluate a person for a job, promotion or school place. It is "face-meeting." Japanese めんせつ is a noun and する-verb; Korean 면접 is the same compound. Mandarin has no entry on Wiktionary and uses 面試 (miànshì) for the same thing (not verified here), so `mandarin` and `cantonese` are left blank. It sits with [[募集]] (recruitment) and [[雇用]] (employment) at the start of the employment sequence.
