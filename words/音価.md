## Notes
- [音 (char)](../characters/音.md) "sound" (stand-in: [[音楽]]) + [価 (char)](../characters/価.md) "value, price" (stand-in: [[価格]]) — phonetic value, the actual sound realization of a phonemic unit.

Wiktionary lists 音価 only under Japanese: music, "note value"; linguistics, "phone (speech segment)". Used in `文法 - 02音韻論` in 具体音価非限定, "the concrete phonetic value is not fixed".
