## Notes
- [音 (char)](../characters/音.md) "sound" (stand-in: [[音楽]]) + [価 (char)](../characters/価.md) "value, price" (stand-in: [[価格]]) — phonetic value, the actual sound realization of a phonemic unit.

音価 means phonetic value, as invoked by `文法 - 02音韻論`'s 具体音価非限定 ("the concrete phonetic value is not fixed"). Korean 음가 is a real, standard linguistics term, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Mandarin yīnjià and Cantonese jam1 gaa3 are straightforward compositional readings, not independently verified as standard. Vietnamese âm giá is compositional only. `kwin` is false: both 音 and 価 are individually false.

No syllable-boundary dot is needed: the concatenated string `ㄨㄇㄍㄚ` admits only one legal partition.
