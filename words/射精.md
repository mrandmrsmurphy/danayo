## Notes
- [射](../characters/射.md) "shoot; eject; issue forth" + [精](../characters/精.md) "spirit; essence; sperm" — no stand-in relationship applies (射's own `stand_in` is [[射出]], 精's is [[精神]]).

射精 is the plain compositional "to shoot/eject" + "essence/sperm," a transparent medical/biological term used consistently across Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, and Korean with no register drift.

**Vietnamese diverges from strict compositional derivation**: the standard Vietnamese medical term is xuất tinh, using 出 "to exit/emit" rather than 射's own reading "xạ" — a real, attested lexical choice (not a Dan'a'yo-vault error), paralleling how Vietnamese medical vocabulary sometimes substitutes a different but near-synonymous character from the one used in the Chinese/Japanese/Korean spelling, the same class of divergence already documented for [[寮国]] and [[対連]]. No HSK level is given for the compound. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼘ㄐㄝㄫ unique across both words and characters).
