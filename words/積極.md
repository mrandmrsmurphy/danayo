## Notes
- [積](../characters/積.md) "accumulate, pile up" + [極](../characters/極%20(char).md) "extreme, of the highest degree" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (積's is [[蓄積]], 極's is [[極]] itself), so `#cranberry` does not apply.

積極 means positive, active, or proactive — an attitude or approach that pursues something energetically rather than passively, the opposite of 消極 ("passive, negative"). It is one of the most common everyday adjectives in the sphere: 積極的 ("proactive, positive," Japanese), 積極性 ("initiative, proactivity").

Mandarin jījí, Cantonese zik1 gik6, Japanese せっきょく (sekkyoku), and Korean 적극 (jeokgeuk) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese tích cực is likewise compositional and is itself an extremely common everyday Vietnamese word for "positive, active."
