## Notes
- [現 (char)](../characters/現%20(char).md) "present; appear" + [存](../characters/存.md) "survive; exist"

現存 is extant: still existing now, of a thing from the past that has survived. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xiàncún as "to exist; to be extant; to be available; to be in stock" and Japanese げんそん as "existence; to survive, exist, persist". Cantonese jin6 cyun4 and Korean 현존 match.

It is the survivor of [存続](存続.md), continued existence, and sits between [現在](現在.md), now, and [存在](存在.md), to exist.
