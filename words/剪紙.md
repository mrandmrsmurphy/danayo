## Notes
- [剪 (char)](../characters/剪%20(char).md) "cut; clip" + [紙 (char)](../characters/紙%20(char).md) "paper"

剪紙 is paper cutting: the art of cutting designs out of paper. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin jiǎnzhǐ as "jianzhi" and Japanese せんし as "jianzhi". Korean 전지 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese zin2 zi2 matches.

It is a folk craft of the [工芸](工芸.md), and the paper it uses is [紙](紙.md).
