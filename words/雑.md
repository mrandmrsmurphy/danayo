## Notes
- [雑 (char)](../characters/雑%20(char).md) "miscellaneous" — this word is 雑's own stand-in.

雑 means miscellaneous, mixed, or assorted. Mandarin 雜/杂 (zá), Cantonese 雜 (zaap6), Japanese 雑 (ざつ, "rough, careless" as an adjective; hugely productive in compounds like 雑誌, "magazine"), Korean 잡 (jap), and Sino-Vietnamese tạp (tạp chí, "magazine"; tạp hóa, "general/sundry goods") all preserve this meaning, appearing across the CJKV sphere both as a bound prefix in compounds and, in Japanese especially, as a standalone descriptive word.
