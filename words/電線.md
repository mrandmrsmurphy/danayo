## Notes
- [電](../characters/電.md) "electricity" + [線](../characters/線.md) "line, clue" — electric wire.

電線 is an electric wire, a conductor (usually copper or aluminium) wrapped in insulation to carry current; the overhead power line strung between poles is the same word. It is the electrical counterpart of the general [[鉄線]]: both are metal wire, but this one is defined by its use. Mandarin diànxiàn, Cantonese din6 sin3, Japanese でんせん and Korean 전선 are the standard readings. The conductor is usually [[銅]], and the insulation is what separates 電線 from a bare metal wire.
