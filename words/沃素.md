## Notes
- [沃](../characters/沃.md) "fertile, watered" + [素](../characters/素.md) "element" — no stand-in relationship applies (沃's own stand-in is [[肥沃]]; 素's own is [[要素]]). Descriptive coinage for iodine, unlike the toponymic-abbreviation lanthanide series (欧金, 桃金, etc.) — no vault page exists for the real element character 碘, so there is no "avoided character" citation to borrow from.

Mandarin wòsù, Cantonese juk1 sou3, and Korean 옥소 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 沃's and 素's own stored citations exactly. Japanese ヨウソ (yōso) is a genuine, if less common, real Japanese chemistry term for iodine — written 沃素/ヨウ素 in practice, using an irregular reading of 沃 as よう specific to this historical scientific borrowing, not derived from 沃's standard on'yomi (YOKU/OKU); normalized the previously mixed-script katakana/hiragana `ヨウそ` to consistent katakana. Filled the blank `cantonese`/`vietnamese` fields (vietnamese compositional; real-world Vietnamese instead uses the loanword i-ốt). Removed the redundant `品詞` field. No homophones (注音 ㄛㄎㄙㄛ unique across both words and characters).
