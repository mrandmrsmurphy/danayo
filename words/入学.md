## Notes
- [入 (char)](../characters/入%20(char).md) "enter" + [学](../characters/学.md) "learn"

入学 is to start school or enter a school, college or university. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin rùxué as "to start school; to enter college", and Vietnamese nhập học is the same word in Hán Việt form ("admission"). Japanese にゅうがく, Korean 입학 and Cantonese jap6 hok6 match.

It marks the start of a course of study and is paired with [卒業](卒業.md), graduation, which ends it. It is distinct from [入試](入試.md), the exam that comes before it.
