## Notes
- [加](../characters/加.md) "increase" + [入](../characters/入%20(char).md) "enter" — no stand-in relationship applies (入 is its own stand-in; 加's own is [[加算]]).

加入 means to join or enroll — becoming a member of a group, organization, or system.

Japanese かにゅう, Korean 가입, and Vietnamese gia nhập are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄋㄧㄆ unique across both words and characters).
