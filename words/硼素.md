## Notes
- [硼](../characters/硼.md) "borax" + [素](../characters/素.md) "element" — literally "borax-element," boron.
- Not a neologism! Present in Korea and Japan.

Japanese はうそ preserves the historical kana spelling (はう for modern ほう, hōso), the real standard Japanese word, following the same 素-suffixed pattern used for other elements (酸素, 水素, 弗素). Korean 붕소 is likewise a real, standard Sino-Korean compound, genuinely compositional: 硼's own 붕 + 素's own 소. Mandarin péng and Cantonese pang4 are the real bare words (matching 硼's own stored citations exactly), since Chinese has no 素-suffixed convention for this element. Vietnamese bo is an international loanword, unrelated to either 硼 or 素's own citations.

`kwin` is false: 硼 alone diverges from Korean on its own character page, which alone is enough to make the AND-rule false; 素 is independently kwin-true. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄛㄫㄙㄛ unique among both words and characters).
