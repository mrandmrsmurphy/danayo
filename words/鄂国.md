## Notes
- [鄂](../characters/鄂.md) "E (state)" + [国](../characters/国.md) "nation"

Wiktionary lists 鄂國 under Chinese, historical: "a state in ancient China of varying location in present-day Shanxi, Henan, and Hubei". Wikipedia: the state of E was an ancient Chinese state in the area of present-day Henan and Hubei from around the 12th century BCE until its overthrow in 863 BCE; it was a vassal of the Shang dynasty, its ruler one of the Three Ducal Ministers appointed by Di Xin. It was originally in southern Henan and later moved to Hubei, and its name is now the provincial abbreviation for Hubei.
