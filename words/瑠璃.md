## Etymology
- [瑠](../characters/瑠.md) "lapis lazuli" + [璃 (char)](../characters/璃%20(char).md) "glass" — 瑠's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 璃's own is itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 瑠 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

Mandarin liúlí and Cantonese lau4 lei4 were already correct and fully compositional. Japanese るり (matching 瑠's own `japanese_native`) and Korean 유리 (the real Korean word for glass/glaze, historically loaned from this same term, matching the same real-word fix already established on [[玻璃]]) were already correct. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with lưu ly, the real, standard Vietnamese term for lapis lazuli/glaze. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/`aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄌ⼜ㄌㄜ unique across both words and characters).
