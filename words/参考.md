## Notes
- [参](../characters/参.md) "consult, attend" + [考](../characters/考.md) "examine, think over" — to consult for reference; reference material; for your information

Wiktionary: Chinese 參考 "to reference; to consult; to refer to; to take an examination; refer also to, see"; Japanese 参考 (さんこう) "reference; consultation" and, as a verb, "to refer to, to consult"; Korean 참고 is the hanja form, "reference; consultation"; Vietnamese tham khảo is the Han form, "to consult (material); to reference". Compare [[参照]].
