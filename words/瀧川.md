## Notes
- [瀧](../characters/瀧.md) "waterfall, rapids" + [川 (char)](../characters/川%20(char).md) "river"

瀧川 is a Japanese place name, "waterfall river". English Wiktionary has an entry for it only under Chinese (Mandarin Lóngchuān, Cantonese lung4 cyun1), where it is the city of Takikawa (たきかわ) in Hokkaido, Japan. The Japanese field here, たきがわ, is the usual reading of the name, a native compound of たき "waterfall" and かわ "river"; the Dan'a'yo reading comes from the Middle Chinese values of the characters. Korean 롱천 and Vietnamese rông xuyên are built from the readings of the two characters.

The character [瀧](../characters/瀧.md) is a 名専字, so it appears only in proper names such as this one. The common word for a waterfall is [瀑布](瀑布.md).
