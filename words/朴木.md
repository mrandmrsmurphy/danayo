## Notes
- [朴](../characters/朴.md) "magnolia" + [木](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree, bush" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [朴](../characters/朴.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 木's own stand-in is itself.

朴木 refers to the Chinese hackberry (Celtis sinensis) or the Japanese bigleaf magnolia (Magnolia obovata).

Mandarin pǔmù, Cantonese pok3 muk6, and Korean 박목 are all standard, fully compositional readings (matching 朴's and 木's own stored citations exactly). Fixed real bugs: `mandarin` had the wrong tone and was missing the 木 half entirely (pò → pǔmù); `cantonese` was likewise missing the 木 half (pok3 → pok3 muk6). Japanese ほほのき is the real native term for the bigleaf magnolia (written 朴の木, standardly ホオノキ/hoonoki — a minor spelling variant), not a compositional Sino-Japanese reading, kept as-is. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field (phác mộc), removed empty `aliases: []`. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄚㄎㄇㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
