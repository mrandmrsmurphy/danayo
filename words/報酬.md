## Notes
- [報 (char)](../characters/報%20(char).md) "repay" + [酬](../characters/酬.md) "reward, requite" — "reward, remuneration, pay." **Critical homograph-collision caveat**: `vietnamese: báo thù` is compositional from 報's báo + 酬's thù, but this exact syllable sequence is also the extremely common, unrelated Vietnamese word for "revenge, vengeance" (報仇, using 仇 rather than 酬 — a near-total meaning inversion from "reward" to "revenge"). This mirrors a genuine Mandarin-internal near-homophone: 仇 and 酬 share the chóu reading. This is one of this vault's most consequential collision risks flagged so far (parallel to [[可不]]/[[嗣子]]/[[堤防]]) — do not use this compositional reading as a real translation without the strongest possible caveat; everyday Vietnamese instead says thù lao or lương/lương bổng for "reward, remuneration" (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted). Fixed `characters` to use the `報 (char)` filename form. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 밧숫 diverges from Sino-Korean 보수 in both syllables.
