## Notes
- [山 (char)](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain" + [本 (char)](../characters/本%20(char).md) "base, origin" — literally "mountain base/origin," a common Japanese surname and place name.

This is a visual borrowing of the Japanese kanji spelling, not a phonetic transliteration: the real Japanese reading is native kun'yomi <ruby>や<rt>·ㄚ</rt></ruby><ruby>ま<rt>ㄇㄚ</rt></ruby><ruby>も<rt>ㄇㄛ</rt></ruby><ruby>と<rt>ㄊㄛ</rt></ruby> (yamamoto), entirely unrelated to Dan'a'yo's own compositional 山+本 derivation (sanbon). This is the same treatment given to Sino-Japanese/Sino-Korean readings of native Japanese proper nouns elsewhere in this vault: Dan'a'yo borrows the kanji spelling and derives its own reading regularly from the characters' own values, rather than attempting to replicate the native pronunciation. `korean` is filled the same way, compositionally (산+본), rather than as an attested real transliteration convention. `kwin` is true by construction, since both fields are built from the same character-level readings.

Used both as a Japanese surname and as the name of a district in Akita Prefecture ([[秋田]][[県]]).
