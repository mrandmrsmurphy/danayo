## Notes
- [毎 (char)](../characters/毎%20(char).md) "every; each; distributive" + [類](../characters/類.md) "kind; sort; category; type"

毎類 is the distributive correlative in the 類 ("kind") column of the correlative table: "each kind; each type separately; every kind one by one", with the predicate applied to the members of a set of kinds in turn. The character [毎 (char)](../characters/毎%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *moi*, ㄇㄛㄧ; its alias 每 is the traditional form, so 每類 is an `aliases` entry here, not a separate file) is the distributive morpheme of [毎日](毎日.md) "every day", [毎週](毎週.md) "every week", [毎月](毎月.md) "every month" and [毎年](毎年.md) "every year"; the free word is [毎](毎.md). [類](../characters/類.md) (Dan'a'yo *lui*, ㄌㄨㄧ) supplies "kind; sort; category; type". There is no free word 類 in `words/`: the character page names [種類](種類.md) "type; kind" as its `stand_in`, so 類 is a bound character, and 種類 heads the 類 row of the chart in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md` (line 152, where the row header is 種類). The cell 毎類 is therefore a fixed paradigm word, as on [此類](此類.md).

Its part of speech is 副用名詞 (adverbial noun), the class of every cell of the 毎 row ([毎事](毎事.md), [毎物](毎物.md), [毎人](毎人.md), [毎名](毎名.md), [毎処](毎処.md), [毎時](毎時.md), [毎様](毎様.md), [毎多](毎多.md)). `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md` (line 11) lists "Adverbial Nouns" as a word class, and `grammar/Linguistics Features.md` (line 68) states that an adverbial noun may be the topic or frame of a clause with no marking but needs marking when it modifies the main verb. No example in `grammar/` uses 毎類 itself, so its syntax is inferred from that rule. Illustrative only (invented, not from `grammar/`): 毎類検 "check each kind, one kind at a time"; 毎類記 "record each category separately".

Against its column neighbours: the collective [皆類](皆類.md) "all kinds" treats the kinds as one totality, whereas 毎類 goes through them one by one, the same distributive/collective split as [毎物](毎物.md) against [皆物](皆物.md). [某類](某類.md) "some kind" is the indefinite, [何類](何類.md) "what kind?" the interrogative, and [此類](此類.md) "this kind", [其類](其類.md) "that kind" and [彼類](彼類.md) "that other kind" each point at one particular kind, where 毎類 ranges over all of them. Against [毎物](毎物.md) "each thing": 毎物 individuates single objects, 毎類 classes of them. Dan'a'yo keeps the sense shared across CJKV, "each kind, one at a time"; no narrowing is needed.

The cross-linguistic fields were checked against Wiktionary on 2026-10-11. Wiktionary has no entry for 每類 or 毎類, so the compound is a Dan'a'yo construction. The Mandarin and Cantonese fields hold 各類 (gèlèi; Cantonese gok3 leoi6), which has an entry as a pronoun glossed "all sorts of; all kinds of". That is the nearest attested word built on 類, but it is a different quantifier: it is general and collective-ish ("all kinds of"), not strictly "each kind in turn", so it is only an approximation (the earlier field 各種 gèzhǒng has the same limitation; its entry reads "a variety of; all sorts of; every"). Wiktionary also has 類別 (lèibié), a noun "category; sort; kind; class", and the idiom 分門別類 (fēnménbiélèi) "to classify; to categorize into categories", which carry the category-by-category idea but are not quantifiers. For Japanese, the 毎 page on Wiktionary gives the prefix まい "each; every; per" and the kun reading ごと; しゅるいごとに (種類ごとに) "type by type" is built from these but is from memory, not re-checked, as no entry for 種類ごと was found. Korean 종류별로 "by type" and Vietnamese *từng loại* "each type" are likewise from memory, not re-checked; hvdic.thivien.net covers single characters (毎 mỗi, 類 loài/loại), not phrases. The earlier note that the original `english` was "totally, systematically" is obsolete and was removed, and the earlier Mandarin 各種 was replaced by 各類 to match the constituent 類.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md)'s correlative table (line 125; the same chart is in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`, line 152 in the 類 row, with the column header cell 毎 on line 142), 毎類 is the ninth and last cell of the 毎 ("every") row, after [毎事](毎事.md), [毎物](毎物.md), [毎人](毎人.md), [毎名](毎名.md), [毎処](毎処.md), [毎時](毎時.md), [毎様](毎様.md) and [毎多](毎多.md). Down the 類 column it pairs with [此類](此類.md), [其類](其類.md), [彼類](彼類.md), [何類](何類.md), [某類](某類.md) and [皆類](皆類.md). The table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), 63 forms, so 毎類 is parsed compositionally: 毎 = distributive, 類 = kind. It is listed on the Grammar page only.

The 羅馬字 is *moilui*, from the character pages (毎 *moi*, 類 *lui*); the earlier *moylui* disagreed with the 毎 page. 諺文 뫼뤼 is 뫼 + 뤼, as on those pages.

The 注音 ㄇㄛㄧㄌㄨㄧ carries no syllable-boundary dot. The test is whether the string has a second legal partition under the (C)(G)V(C) structure of `grammar/文法 - 02音韻論.md`. ㄌ is not an allowed coda (only the nasals ㄇ ㄋ ㄫ, the glides and the unreleased stops ㄆ ㄊ ㄎ are), so ㄇㄛㄧㄌ is not a syllable and ㄌ must begin the second syllable; the second syllable then cannot be anything but ㄌㄨㄧ. The only other split, ㄇㄛ + ㄧ + ㄌㄨㄧ, would break the diphthong ㄛㄧ, which this vault does not do (as on [毎物](毎物.md) and [毎様](毎様.md)). The only partition is therefore ㄇㄛㄧ + ㄌㄨㄧ (both in `syllables/`), with no ambiguity for a dot to resolve.
