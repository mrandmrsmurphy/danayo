## Notes
- [予](../characters/予.md) "beforehand, in advance" + [言](../characters/言%20(char).md) "say, word" — "say beforehand," a prophecy. Their own `stand_in` values: 予's is [[予様]]; 言's is itself; none points here, so 予言 is an independent compound.

予言 is a prophecy: a statement of what will happen, and to foretell. Mandarin yùyán (預言), Cantonese jyu6 jin4, Japanese よげん and Korean 예언 all use it. The character 予 has the aliases 預 and 豫, so the Chinese spelling 預言 is accepted. A prophet is 予言－者, segmented with the suffix [[－者]]. Compare [[運命]] (fate) and [[命運]].
