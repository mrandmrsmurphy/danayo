## Notes
- [因](../characters/因.md) "cause; reason" + [縁](../characters/縁.md) "fate; chance" — this word is the stand-in for [縁](../characters/縁.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo (因's own stand-in is [[原因]]).

因緣 means fate, karma, or a predestined bond — the Buddhist concept of causally-linked circumstance.

Japanese いんねん features a genuine sandhi-driven n-insertion (rather than the mechanically expected いんえん) — a real, well-attested reading. Korean 인연 and Vietnamese nhân duyên are both standard readings, matching Dan'a'yo's own reading exactly (`kwin: true`). No homophones (注音 ㄧㄋ·⼶ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
