## Notes
- [邾](../characters/邾.md) "Zou" + [国](../characters/国.md) "country, state"
- 邾国 is the stand-in word for [邾](../characters/邾.md) ("Zou"), a naming character restricted to proper names in Dan'a'yo.

Mandarin Zhū guó, cantonese zyu1 gwok3, japanese しゅこく, korean 주국, and vietnamese chu quốc are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 주곡 diverges from Sino-Korean 주국 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄨ·ㄍㄛㄎ unique among both words and characters).

**Found and fixed a missing "(stand-in for 邾)" annotation** on 邾.md's own citation. Citation on 国.md was already correctly present. 邾's own `stand_in` is this exact word (邾国), so 邾 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 邾国; 国's own `stand_in` is instead 国家, so this word does not legitimize 国.
