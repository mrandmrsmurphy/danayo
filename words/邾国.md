## Notes
- [邾](../characters/邾.md) "Zou" + [国](../characters/国.md) "country, state"
- 邾国 is the stand-in word for [邾](../characters/邾.md) ("Zou"), a naming character restricted to proper names in Dan'a'yo.

Mandarin Zhū guó, cantonese zyu1 gwok3, japanese しゅこく, korean 주국, and vietnamese chu quốc are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly.
