## Notes
- [枯 (char)](../characters/枯%20(char).md) "withered" + [木 (char)](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree" — compare the words [枯](枯.md) and [木](木.md).

枯木 is a tree that has died or dried out, or the dead wood of one. In Chinese it is literary, and it carries a strong poetic and Buddhist charge: the dead tree stands for a heart emptied of desire and for a monk or hermit who has gone still (枯木死灰, "dead wood and cold ashes"), and, more cheerfully, for the dead tree that blooms again (see [枯木生花](../chengyu/枯木生花.md)). Japanese has the native 枯れ木 (かれき) beside the Sino-Japanese こぼく, and the latter is the literary one. Korean 고목 is the ordinary word for an old withered tree, and in Korean usage it can also mean an old tree that is still living but ancient.

The pronunciation takes a dot, because ㄎㄛㄇㄛㄎ also splits as ㄎㄛㄇ + ㄛㄎ.
