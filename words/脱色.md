## Notes
- [脱 (char)](../characters/脱%20(char).md) "remove, shed" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — bleaching.

脱色 is decolorizing: the deliberate removal of color from hair, cloth, paper or a liquid, typically by bleach or filtration; and, in Mandarin, the loss of color. It is the reverse of [[着色]] and the controlled form of [[退色]]. Hair lightening in Japanese and Korean is also 脱色 (탈색). Mandarin tuōsè, Cantonese tyut3 sik1, Japanese だっしょく and Korean 탈색 are the standard readings.
