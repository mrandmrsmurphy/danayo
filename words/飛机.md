## Notes
- [飛 (char)](../characters/飛%20(char).md) "fly" + [机](../characters/机.md) "machine" — no `#cranberry`: 飛's own `stand_in` is 飛 itself, 机's is [[机会]]; neither points here. Belongs to 飛's own primary reading branch (fei/뻬/ㄈㄝㄧ), distinct from the separate ㄆㄧ branch seen in [[飛行机]].

飛机/飞机 (fēijī) is the standard, everyday Mandarin word for "airplane" — the short form, bypassing 行 — and Cantonese 飛机 (fei1gei1) likewise. Japanese and Korean, by contrast, use the fuller form built on 飛行機 (ひこうき/비행기) as their standard everyday word rather than a short form matching 飛机's own two characters. Sino-Vietnamese phi cơ matches 飛机 exactly and is a real, somewhat formal/literary synonym of the more common native máy bay.
