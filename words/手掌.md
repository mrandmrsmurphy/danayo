## Notes
- [手](../characters/手%20(char).md) "hand" + [掌](../characters/掌.md) "palm" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [掌](../characters/掌.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (手's own stand-in is itself).

手掌 means palm (of the hand).

Mandarin shǒuzhǎng and Cantonese sau2 zoeng2 are standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese てのひら and Korean 손바닥 both use the everyday native word rather than a Sino-compositional reading (掌's own on-reading SHOU/장 survives only in more formal/technical compounds); Vietnamese gan bàn tay is likewise the ordinary native term. Homophone of [[首長]] "chief; leader; emir" (both 注音 ㄙ⼜ㄐㄚㄫ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated compounds.
