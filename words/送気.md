## Notes
- [送](../words/送.md) "send, deliver" (stand-in: itself) + [気 (char)](../characters/気%20(char).md) "air, breath" (stand-in: [[気]] itself) — aspiration, the puff of breath released with certain stops.

Wiktionary: Chinese 送氣 (sòngqì), phonetics, "to aspirate". Japanese 送気 (そうき) is a different word there, "supplying air; to supply air", not the phonetic term, so the Dan'a'yo sense is the Chinese one. Used in `文法 - 02音韻論`, for aspirated versus unaspirated stops.
