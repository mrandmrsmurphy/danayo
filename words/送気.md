## Notes
- [送](../words/送.md) "send, deliver" (stand-in: itself) + [気 (char)](../characters/気.md) "air, breath" (stand-in: [[気]] itself) — aspiration, the puff of breath released with certain stops.

送気 means aspiration. Mandarin sòngqì and Japanese そうき are both real, standard phonetics terms, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Cantonese sung3 hei3 is a straightforward compositional reading. Korean 송기 and Vietnamese tống khí are compositional only, not independently verified as standard — Korean phonetics more commonly contrasts 유기음/무기음 (aspirated/unaspirated) rather than using bare 송기. `kwin` is false: inherited from 気 (false), despite 送 itself being true.

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot**: the concatenated string `ㄙㄛㄫㄎㄧㄜ` admits two legal partitions — the intended 送(ㄙㄛㄫ)+気(ㄎㄧㄜ), and a false alternate splitting 気's own syllable (ㄎㄧ+ㄜ) — so the dot at the true 送|気 boundary is required: ㄙㄛㄫ·ㄎㄧㄜ.
