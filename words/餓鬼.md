## Notes
- [餓](../characters/餓.md) "hungry" + [鬼](../characters/鬼.md) "devil, demon" — no `#cranberry`: 餓's own `stand_in` is [[飢餓]], 鬼's is [[鬼神]]; neither points here.

餓鬼 (èguǐ) is the standard Mandarin word for "hungry ghost" (preta, a being in Buddhist cosmology tormented by insatiable hunger), and Cantonese 餓鬼 (ngo6gwai2) likewise. Japanese がき (gaki) is the standard term, also used colloquially as a mild insult ("brat"). Korean 아귀 (agwi) and Sino-Vietnamese ngạ quỷ are both standard terms — a clean case with full agreement across all five languages.
