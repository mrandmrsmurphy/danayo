## Notes
- [予](../characters/予.md) "in advance, I" + [定](../characters/定.md) "fix, decide" — to fix/decide something in advance, hence "schedule, plan, appointment."

予定 is one of the most common everyday Japanese words for a scheduled plan or appointment (予定表, schedule/timetable; ご予定, honorific "your schedule"). Mandarin yǔdìng and Cantonese jyu4 ding6 are less common than the everyday 計劃/计划 (jìhuà, "plan") but remain compositionally valid. Korean 여정 is the compositional reading of these exact characters; note that the far more common everyday Korean word for "scheduled, planned" is 예정, but that word is written with the separate (and visually similar) character 豫 rather than 予 — not to be confused with this word's own compositional reading. **Vietnamese content note**: filled with dự định, the real, extremely common everyday Vietnamese word for "plan, intend to" — this uses 予's real Sino-Vietnamese reading dự (as in dự đoán "predict," dự báo "forecast"), not the currently-stored dư/dừ on 予's own character page, which belong to an unrelated character (餘/余, "surplus, remainder") — a bug flagged here for `予.md`'s own alphabetical turn.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 요정 diverges from Sino-Korean 여정, since 予's own Dan'a'yo reading doesn't match Sino-Korean, even though 定's own reading matches exactly (定 is independently `kwin: true`).
