## Notes
- [供](../characters/供.md) "offer, supply" + [養](../characters/養.md) "nourish, support" — "offer and nourish," a religious offering. Their own `stand_in` values: 供's is [[提供]]; 養's is [[養育]]; none points here, so 供養 is an independent compound.

供養 is a religious offering: the giving of food, incense or prayers to the Buddha, to monks or to the dead. Mandarin gōngyǎng, Cantonese gung1 joeng6, Japanese くよう and Korean 공양 all use it; in Chinese it also means to support one's parents. It is the offering to the divine, where [[献上]] is a secular gift to a superior. Compare [[祭祀]] (sacrifice) and [[犠牲]] (sacrificial victim).
