## Notes
- [胆](../characters/胆.md) "gall bladder, bravery" + [嚢](../characters/嚢.md) "bag, pocket" — `#cranberry`: both characters' own `stand_in` point to this exact compound (胆's and 嚢's alike), so transitivity holds (A=B=AB) and both are legitimized as independent Dan'a'yo entries specifically by this word.

胆嚢 means gallbladder — the "gall bag," combining 胆 ("gall bladder") with 嚢 ("bag, pocket").

**Found and fixed a real bug**: `kwin` was stored true, but the AND-rule requires false (嚢 is individually `kwin: true`, but 胆 is individually `kwin: false`). Pronunciation fields (damnang/담낭/ㄉㄚㄇㄋㄚㄫ) were already the correct concatenation. Filled blank vietnamese (đảm nang).
