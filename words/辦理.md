## Notes
- [辦](../characters/辦.md) "manage, run" + [理](../characters/理.md) "reason, principle" — "manage, arrange, run" (an administrative process).

Mandarin bànlǐ, cantonese baan6 lei5, and korean 판리 are all directly attested standard readings. Japanese べんり and vietnamese biện lý are both honest compositional readings (辦's own BEN/biện + 理's own RI/lý); each happens to be a coincidental homophone of an unrelated common word (Japanese 便利/benri, "convenient"; Vietnamese biện lý, historically "public prosecutor" in older administrative Vietnamese) — coincidental, unrelated in meaning, disclosed rather than avoided. `办理` is the standard simplified-character spelling variant (辦→办), correctly listed as an alias.
