## Notes
- [懸](../characters/懸%20(char).md) "hang, suspend, hoist" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

懸 means to hang, suspend, or hoist, as in 懸命 ("with all one's might, lit. suspending one's life") and 懸垂 ("hanging down").

Mandarin xuán, Cantonese jyun4, Japanese けん, Korean 현, and Vietnamese huyền are all standard, fully compositional readings. Part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read hwen/훤/ㄏ⼔ㄋ) with [[玄]] ("deep, profound, mysterious") and [[県]] ("county, district, prefecture").
