## Notes
- [飲 (char)](../characters/飲%20(char).md) "drink" — the bare character used directly as a word, its own `stand_in`.

飲 means to drink: to take liquid into the mouth and swallow (飲水, "drink water"; 飲料, "beverage"; 飲酒, "drink alcohol"; 飲食, "food and drink"), and by extension to swallow or endure (飲恨, "swallow one's bitterness"). The variants 飮 and 饮 are listed as aliases.

Mandarin yǐn, Cantonese jam2, Korean 음 (eum), and Sino-Vietnamese ẩm (as in ẩm thực, "food and drink, cuisine") all preserve the Sino reading directly. Japanese いん (in) survives mainly bound in compounds (飲食/いんしょく, 飲料/いんりょう); the everyday native verb is のむ (nomu). Korean has native 마시다.
