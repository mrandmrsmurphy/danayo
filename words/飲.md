## Notes
- [飲 (char)](../characters/飲%20(char).md) "drink" — this word is 飲's own legitimizing compound (飲's `stand_in` points here).

飲 means "to drink." Mandarin yǐn, Cantonese jam2, Korean 음 (eum), and Sino-Vietnamese ẩm (as in ẩm thực, "food and drink, cuisine") all preserve the Sino reading directly. Japanese いん (in) survives mainly bound in compounds (飲食, 飲料); the everyday native verb is のむ (nomu).
