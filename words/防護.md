## Notes
- [防](../characters/防.md) "guard against, prevent" + [護](../characters/護.md) "safeguard, protect" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes 防 as a bound character (護's own stand-in is [[守護]]; transitivity fails, so no `#cranberry`).

防護 means to guard against, shield, or protect — often in a physical/protective-equipment sense rather than an abstract one. Mandarin 防護 (fánghù), Cantonese 防護 (fong4wu6), Japanese 防護 (ぼうご), Korean 방호 (bangho), and Sino-Vietnamese phòng hộ are all everyday words for physical protection or shielding (防護服/防護服/방호복/quần áo phòng hộ, "protective suit/gear"), with Vietnamese phòng hộ additionally extending to labor-safety contexts (lao động phòng hộ, "labor protection").
