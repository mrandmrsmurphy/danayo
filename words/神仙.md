## Notes
- [神](../characters/神%20(char).md) "god, deity, spirit" + [仙](../characters/仙.md) "xian, fairy" — 仙's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 神's own is [[神]] itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 仙 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

神仙 means an immortal or divine being, especially in the Taoist sense of a hermit-sage who has attained immortality through cultivation — distinct from 神 alone, which names divinity more broadly.

Mandarin shénxiān, Cantonese san4 sin1, Japanese しんせん (shinsen), and Korean 신선 (sinseon, a common standalone Korean word for "Taoist immortal/hermit") are all fully compositional. Vietnamese thần tiên is likewise compositional and is the standard real word for "fairy, immortal, divine being."
