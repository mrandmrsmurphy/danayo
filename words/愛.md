## Notes
- [愛](../characters/愛%20(char).md) "love" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

愛 means love.

Mandarin ài, Cantonese oi3, Japanese あい, Korean 애, and Vietnamese ái are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed missing glide apostrophe on `羅馬字` (ai→'ai, matching the character's own citation). Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄚㄧ/'ai/애) with [[唉]] ("alas, ancient") and [[崖]] ("cliff").
