## Notes
- [星](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" + [群](../characters/群.md) "group, herd" — no stand-in relationship applies (星's own stand-in is itself; 群's own is [[群衆]]).

星群 means asterism.

Mandarin xīngqún, Cantonese sing1 kwan4, Japanese せいぐん, and Korean 성군 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese's 群 half (quần, the well-attested standard reading, as in 群衆 quần chúng) is inferred rather than drawn from the character's own stored citation, which is missing it — flagged for a future character-level check. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄫㄍㄨㄋ unique across both words and characters).
