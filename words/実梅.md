## Notes
- **Real bugs found and fixed**: `mandarin` was stored as bare `méi` (just 梅's own reading, missing 実 entirely) — corrected to the full compositional `shíméi`, matching the two-syllable pattern already on `cantonese: sat6 mui4`. Separately, the `aliases: [實梅]` field's own 梅 was stored as a distinct CJK compatibility-ideograph codepoint (U+FA44) rather than the standard 梅 (U+6885) used everywhere else on this page and in the filename — visually identical but a different character to any exact-match search or wikilink resolution; normalized to the standard codepoint.
- Primarily a Japanese horticultural/kigo (seasonal haiku word) distinction: 実梅 (a Prunus mume cultivar grown for its fruit) versus 花梅 (a cultivar grown for its ornamental blossoms, see [[梅花]]) — the same species, different cultivation purpose. **Left `vietnamese` blank rather than guessing**: this specific fruit-vs-flower cultivar distinction doesn't appear to be a standing Vietnamese term. **Left `hsk_level` unset**: not a standard Mandarin vocabulary item, a Japan-specific horticultural compound.
- `kwin: false` confirmed (実 alone matches Korean, 梅 doesn't — AND-rule). No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄊㄇㄛㄧ unique among both words and characters).
