## Notes
- [形](../characters/形%20(char).md) "shape, form, type" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

形 means shape, form, or type — the visual outline or category something takes, as in 形声 ("phono-semantic character") and 三角形 ("triangle").

Mandarin xíng, Cantonese jing4, Japanese けい, Korean 형, and Vietnamese hình are all standard, fully compositional readings.
