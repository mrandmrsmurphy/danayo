## Notes
- [禁](../characters/禁.md) "prohibit, forbid" + [欲](../characters/欲.md) "desire, want" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (禁's is [[禁止]], 欲's is [[欲望]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 禁 is individually `kwin: false`.

禁欲 means to suppress or abstain from desire, especially bodily/sensory desire — the practice of asceticism, whether for religious discipline (monastic vows, fasting), philosophical self-mastery (Stoic-style restraint), or medical/lifestyle abstinence. It underlies 禁欲主義 ("asceticism" as a formal doctrine) and appears widely across religious traditions that valorize self-denial as a path to spiritual attainment.

Mandarin jìnyù, Cantonese gam3 juk6, Japanese きんよく (kin'yoku), and Korean 금욕 (geumyok) are all standard, fully compositional readings, each the ordinary word for asceticism/abstinence in its language. Vietnamese cấm dục is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form.
