## Etymology
- [出 (char)](../characters/出%20(char).md) "exit" + [奔](../characters/奔.md) "run around"

## Notes
"To flee, elope, run away" (classically, to flee to another state/country — 獻公出奔, "Duke Xian fled"). Both `korean: 출분` (compositional) and `vietnamese: xuất bôn` (directly attested, confirmed via web search — a classical term, synonymous with xuất tẩu and đào vong) fill previously blank fields. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 춛본 diverges from the compositional Sino-Korean 출분 in the second syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄨㄊㄅㄛㄋ unique among both words and characters).
