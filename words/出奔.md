## Etymology
- [出 (char)](../characters/出%20(char).md) "exit" + [奔](../characters/奔.md) "run around"

## Notes
"To flee, elope, run away" (classically, to flee to another state/country — 獻公出奔, "Duke Xian fled"). `vietnamese: xuất bôn` is directly attested (confirmed via web search) — a classical term, synonymous with xuất tẩu and đào vong; `korean: 출분` is compositional.
