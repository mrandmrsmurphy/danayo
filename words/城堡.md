## Notes
- [城](../characters/城.md) "city wall, city" + [堡](../characters/堡.md) "fort" — "walled fort," a castle. Their own `stand_in` values: 城's is [[城郭]]; 堡's is [[堡塁]]; none points here, so 城堡 is an independent compound.

城堡 is a castle: a fortified residence. Mandarin chéngbǎo and Cantonese sing4 bou2 are the ordinary Chinese words, applied especially to European castles. Japanese says 城 or 城郭 and Korean 성, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[城郭]] (city walls) and [[要塞]] (fortress).
