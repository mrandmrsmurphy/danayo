## Notes
- [青 (char)](../characters/青%20(char).md) "blue" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — no `#cranberry`: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here (青's is itself, 色's is [[色彩]]).

青色 (qīngsè) is a standard Mandarin word for the blue-green/cyan range covered by 青, and Cantonese 青色 (cing1sik1) likewise. Japanese あおいろ (aoiro, native reading rather than on'yomi) is the ordinary word for "blue color." Korean 청색 (cheongsaek) is a real, standard word, though everyday Korean more often uses the native 파란색 for "blue" specifically.

Vietnamese màu xanh ("blue/green color," native rather than Sino-Vietnamese) is the standard translation; strikingly, the Vietnamese color word xanh itself is thought to descend etymologically from 青 through an old layer of borrowing, making this less a translation than a doublet of the same root expressed natively rather than through the newer Sino-Vietnamese layer (which would give thanh, as in [[青年]] thanh niên).
