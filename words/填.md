## Notes
- [填 (char)](../characters/填%20(char).md) "fill in; make good on"

**Content corrected**: `korean` was stored as the literal string `"null"` — corrected to `전`, matching the character page's own value.

填 means "to fill in, stuff" (填空, "to fill in a blank") and, by extension, "to make good on, compensate for" (填補, "to fill up, make good").

Mandarin tián and Cantonese tin4 preserve both senses. Japanese テン/チン (TEN/CHIN, on'yomi, formal register) alongside native うず(める) (uzu(meru), "to bury, fill in") cover the sense. Korean 전 (jeon) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese điền is the standard Hán Việt reading, covering the "fill in" sense (điền vào, "to fill in [a form]"; điền đơn, "to fill out a form"). A second candidate, đền, is not a coordinate Hán Việt reading but a genuine Old Sino-Vietnamese doublet — an earlier, separate borrowing layer of the same character (the same phenomenon as 燈's đăng/đèn pair) — that settled onto this word's *other* sense specifically: đền means "to compensate, repay, requite" (đền bù, "to compensate"; đền tội, "to pay for one's crime"), matching 填's "make good on" gloss precisely. `điền` is kept as the field value since it's the regular/standard reading, with đền documented here as the cognate doublet carrying the second sense rather than presented as unrelated noise.
