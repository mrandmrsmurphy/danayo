## Notes
- From [[棲]] "nest/roost" + [[息]] "breath/rest": the place where a creature breathes and settles — its habitat. The full compound 棲息 (栖息 in simplified Chinese, せいそく in Japanese) is widely shared across all five source languages as a verb meaning "to inhabit" or "to dwell" (of animals). Korean 서식 is the direct Sino-Korean reading, composed of 棲's own korean field 서 (distinct from its own 諺文/羅馬字 세/sei, a pre-existing divergence on the character's own citation) + 息's own 식.
- This word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [[棲]] as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 息's own stand-in is [[気息]].

Removed the redundant `品詞` field (duplicate of `pos`). No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄧㄙㄧㄎ unique across both words and characters).
