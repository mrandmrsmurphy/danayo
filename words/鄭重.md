## Notes
Mandarin zhèngzhòng, cantonese zeng6 zung6, japanese ていちょう, and korean 정중 are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. Vietnamese trịnh chuộng is the honest compositional reading (鄭's own trịnh + 重's own chuộng); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. **Found and removed a bad-aliases-entry**: `丁重` uses an entirely different first character (丁, not registered as an alias of 鄭) and a different Mandarin reading (dīngzhòng, not zhèngzhòng) — it happens to converge with 鄭重 only in Japanese (both read ていちょう), a coincidental historical orthographic variant specific to that one language, not a genuine cross-language spelling variant of this word. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 정총 diverges from Sino-Korean 정중 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄫㄑㄛㄫ unique among both words and characters).

**Found and fixed `characters:` disambiguation** (bare 重 → "重 (char)," since [[重]] has its own word page). Both character-page citations already correctly present.
