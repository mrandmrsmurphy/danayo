- [鄭](../characters/鄭.md) "Zheng" + [重 (char)](../characters/重%20(char).md) "heavy"

## Notes
Mandarin zhèngzhòng, cantonese zeng6 zung6, japanese ていちょう, and korean 정중 are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. Vietnamese trịnh chuộng is the honest compositional reading (鄭's own trịnh + 重's own chuộng); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found.
