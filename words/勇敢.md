## Notes
- [勇](../characters/勇.md) "brave" + [敢](../characters/敢.md) "dare" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [勇](../characters/勇.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (敢's own stand-in is [[敢為]]).

勇敢 means brave or courageous — facing danger, difficulty, or fear without flinching.

Japanese ゆうかん, Korean 용감, and Vietnamese dũng cảm are all standard, fully compositional readings.
