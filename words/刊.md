## Notes
- [刊 (char)](../characters/刊%20(char).md) "publication; periodical"

刊 is 形声: semantic [[刀]] ("knife") + phonetic [[干]]. Its oldest sense is literally "to cut, carve" — engraving text into woodblocks or stone for printing (刊石, "to carve characters into stone"; 刊本, "a printed/carved edition") — which extended to "to publish" (刊行, "to publish"; 刊載, "to publish, print [an article]") and, as a noun, "a periodical publication" ([[季刊]], "a quarterly publication").

Mandarin kān, Cantonese hon1, and Japanese かん (kan, on'yomi, fully productive: 週刊 しゅうかん "weekly publication") all preserve the sense. Korean 간 (gan) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese has two attested readings for this character: san, the customary reading familiar from nguyệt san ("a monthly publication," directly matching this word's own sense), and khan, which appears throughout classical dictionary compounds (khan bản, "printed edition"; khan tái, "to publish"; nguyệt khan, an alternate rendering of the same "monthly publication" sense) — both genuinely attested rather than one being contamination.

This word shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading (kan/칸/ㄎㄚㄋ) with another word still awaiting its own turn in this sweep, [[看]] ("watch over") — the reciprocal half of this callout will be completed when it comes up.
