## Notes
- [娃](../characters/娃.md) "beautiful girl" reduplicated — a colloquial Mandarin/Cantonese term for "doll," a toy for children (娃娃 can also colloquially mean "baby, infant," a related but distinct sense not covered by this entry's "doll" gloss). `japanese: 人形` (にんぎょう) and `korean: 인형` both give the "doll" sense. **The `vietnamese` field is deliberately omitted**: bare reduplication doesn't naturalize into Vietnamese, the same pattern already established on [[伯伯]]/[[叔叔]]/[[女女]]/[[姉姉]]/[[姥姥]] — the real Vietnamese word for "doll" is búp bê (a loan from French poupée), unrelated to this compound.
