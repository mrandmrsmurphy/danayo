## Notes
- [娃](../characters/娃.md) "beautiful girl" reduplicated — a colloquial Mandarin/Cantonese term for "doll," a toy for children (娃娃 can also colloquially mean "baby, infant," a related but distinct sense not covered by this entry's "doll" gloss). **Content bug found and fixed**: `japanese` was stored as 赤ん坊 ("baby, infant"), the wrong sense — corrected to 人形 (にんぎょう, "doll"), matching this entry's own "doll" gloss and paralleling `korean: 인형`. **`vietnamese` deliberately left blank**: bare reduplication doesn't naturalize into Vietnamese, the same pattern already established on [[伯伯]]/[[叔叔]]/[[女女]]/[[姉姉]]/[[姥姥]] — the real Vietnamese word for "doll" is búp bê (a loan from French poupée), unrelated to this compound. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 얘얘 diverges from Sino-Korean's unrelated native word 인형 entirely. No homophones (注音 ⼘ㄧ⼘ㄧ unique among both words and characters).
