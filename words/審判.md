## Notes
- [審](../characters/審.md) "judge, try" + [判](../characters/判.md) "decide, judge" — "try and judge," the judgment. Their own `stand_in` values: 審's is [[審査]]; 判's is [[判断]]; none points here, so 審判 is an independent compound.

審判 is judgment: to try and decide a case, and God's final judgment of the world. Mandarin shěnpàn, Cantonese sam2 pun3, Japanese しんぱん and Korean 심판 all use it, and Vietnamese thẩm phán does too. In Christian use it is the Last Judgment; in sport it is the referee. Compare [[裁判官]] (judge) and [[地獄]] (hell).
