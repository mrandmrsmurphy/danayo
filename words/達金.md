## Notes
- [達 (char)](../characters/達%20(char).md) + [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal" — a transliteration of *Darmstadt*, the German city where the GSI Helmholtz Centre that discovered the element is located. 達 is the first syllable of 達姆施塔特, the standard Mandarin transliteration of "Darmstadt," truncated rather than spelled out in full, in the same pattern as 田納素 (Tennessine, from 田納西) and 莫斯素 (Moscovium, from 莫斯科). Japanese and Korean instead borrow the IUPAC name as a loanword (ダームスタチウム, 다름스타튬).

**Content added**: `mandarin`/`cantonese` were both blank; filled with `dá`/`daat6`, the readings of the real standard Chinese element character 鐽 (metal radical + phonetic 達, officially adopted 2003) — which happen to match [[達]]'s own reading exactly, since 達 was chosen for this coinage precisely because it already supplies that transliteration value. `vietnamese` was likewise blank; filled with `Darmstadti`, the attested Vietnamese term (from vi.wikipedia, pronounced "đam-xtat-ti"; the element was formerly called "ununnili" before the 2003 IUPAC naming).

**Flagged, not corrected**: research this iteration turned up a possible spelling discrepancy in the already-stored `korean` value — some sources suggest the standard form may be 다름슈타튬 (darmseutatyum, following German "-st-" transliteration conventions) rather than 다름스타튬 as currently stored — but this came from AI-summarized page extracts rather than a directly verified source, so the existing value was left untouched rather than "corrected" on medium confidence.
