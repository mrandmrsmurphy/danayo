## Notes
- [氷 (char)](../characters/氷%20(char).md) "ice (stand-in: itself)"

Wiktionary: Chinese 冰 "ice; ice-cold; to freeze, to ice" and, in drug slang, "methamphetamine"; Japanese 氷 "ice (frozen water)" and "a cold and sharp object, as a shard of ice". 氷 is the Japanese shinjitai glyph of 冰, listed as the alias. The character page was renamed to 氷 (char) to free the filename for this word; [[氷水]] is the compound "ice water".
