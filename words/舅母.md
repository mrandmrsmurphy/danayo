## Notes
- [舅](../characters/舅.md) "mother's brother" + [母](../characters/母.md) "mother" — maternal uncle's wife.

舅母 is the wife of one's maternal uncle. It is "maternal uncle-mother," the pair of [[舅父]]. No Wiktionary entry was checked for it; the Mandarin reading jiùmǔ is the compositional one and comes from memory, so the other languages are left blank. Compare [[伯母]] and [[叔母]], the wives of the father's brothers, and [[姨母]], the maternal aunt.
