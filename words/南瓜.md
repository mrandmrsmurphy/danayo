## Notes
- [南](../characters/南.md) "south" + [瓜](../characters/瓜.md) "melon"

南瓜 is a pumpkin: the large, orange-fleshed gourd of *Cucurbita*, and its plant. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin nánguā as "pumpkin; winter squash" and Japanese かぼちゃ as "pumpkin; squash". Cantonese naam4 gwaa1 matches, and Korean 남과 is the same word. The Japanese reading is a native word, not the on'yomi.

It is a [瓜](瓜.md), gourd, and it is the plain Chinese word for what Japanese also calls [南京](南京.md), a name taken from a city. Other gourds are the [西瓜](西瓜.md), watermelon, and the [苦瓜](苦瓜.md), bitter melon.
