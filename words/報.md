## Notes
- [報 (char)](../characters/報%20(char).md) "report; tell"

報 is 会意, originally depicting a subduing/convicting act (handcuffs + a figure being subdued) — "to convict, punish, declare guilty" — extended to "to report, announce" (報告, "to report, inform"; 情報, "information, intelligence").

Mandarin bào and Cantonese bou3 preserve the sense directly. Japanese ホウ (HOU, on'yomi, fully productive: 報道, hōdō, "news coverage") alongside native むく(いる) (muku(iru), "to repay, reward," an extended sense) cover the sense. Korean 보 (bo) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese báo is the Hán Việt reading, extremely common in everyday speech (báo cáo, "to report"; báo chí, "the press, media").

This word shares its reading (bau/밧/ㄅㄚㄨ) with [[鮑]] ("abalone"), still unperfected and awaiting its own turn in this sweep.
