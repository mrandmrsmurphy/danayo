## Notes
- [電](../characters/電.md) "electricity" + [子](../characters/子.md) "child, seed"

電子 means electron — the subatomic particle. Mandarin 電子/电子 (diànzǐ), Cantonese 電子 (din6zi2), Japanese 電子 (でんし), Korean 전자 (jeonja), and Sino-Vietnamese điện tử all preserve this scientific term consistently across the CJKV sphere, and further extend to "electronic" as an adjective (電子郵件/이메일 계열/thư điện tử-style compounds, "email," lit. "electronic mail").

**Found and fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字`/`諺文` had drifted to denji/던지, inconsistent with 子's own correctly-stored jǝ/즈 (注音 ㄐㄜ was already correct) — corrected to denjǝ/던즈, the same recurring bug class documented repeatedly earlier in this sweep.
