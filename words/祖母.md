## Notes
- [祖](../characters/祖.md) "ancestor" + [母](../characters/母.md) "mother" — literally "ancestor-mother," i.e. grandmother.

祖母 is the formal, written term for one's paternal grandmother, contrasted with 外祖母 (maternal grandmother). In colloquial Mandarin speech, the suppletive term 奶奶 (nǎinai) or 婆婆 (pópo, regionally) is more commonly used than 祖母 itself, which survives mainly in formal or written registers.

Mandarin zǔmǔ, Cantonese zou2 mou5, Japanese そぼ (sobo), and Korean 조모 (jomo) are all fully compositional. Vietnamese bà nội is the real, standard native term specifically for the paternal grandmother (as opposed to bà ngoại, the maternal grandmother), rather than a Sino-Vietnamese compound.
