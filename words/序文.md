## Notes
- [序](../characters/序.md) "series; sequence" (extended: preface) + [文](../characters/文.md) "writing; text" — no stand-in relationship applies (序's own `stand_in` is [[順序]], 文's is [[文化]]).

序文 names a preface or introductory text preceding a book's main content.

Japanese じょぶん, Korean 서문, and Vietnamese tự văn are all standard, non-drifted readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼄ㄇㄨㄋ unique across both words and characters).
