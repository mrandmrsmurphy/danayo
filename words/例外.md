## Notes
- [例](../characters/例.md) "example, precedent, custom" + [外](../characters/外.md) "outside" — "outside the precedent": an exception. Neither character is free on its own in Dan'a'yo (例 is carried by [実例](実例.md), 外 by [外部](外部.md)).

例外 is an exception. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 例外 (Mandarin lìwài, Cantonese lai6 ngoi6) as "to make an exception; to be excepted" and "exception", and Japanese れいがい and Korean 례외 as "exception". The Vietnamese lệ ngoại field is built from the readings of the two characters. Compare [除外](除外.md) and [以外](以外.md).
