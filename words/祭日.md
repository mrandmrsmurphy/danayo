## Notes
- [祭](../characters/祭.md) "festival, sacrifice" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day"

祭日 is a day of festival or ritual observance. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 祭日 (Mandarin jìrì, Cantonese zai3 jat6) as the literary "day of a memorial ceremony", and Japanese さいじつ as "national holiday". Korean 제일 and Vietnamese tế nhật are built from the readings of the two characters. The legal national holiday is the [祝日](祝日.md).
