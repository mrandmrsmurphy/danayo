## Notes
- [強](../characters/強%20(char).md) "strong, powerful" + [化](../characters/化%20(char).md) "-ification" — no stand-in relationship applies (強/化 are both their own stand-ins).

強化 means to strengthen or reinforce — making something stronger or more robust.

Mandarin qiánghuà, Cantonese koeng4 faa3, Japanese きょうか, Korean 강화, and Vietnamese cường hoá are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼘ㄫㄏ⺢ unique across both words and characters).
