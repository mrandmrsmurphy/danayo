## Notes
- [主](../characters/主.md) "main" + [食 (char)](../characters/食%20(char).md) "food"

主食 is the staple food: the main part of a meal, such as rice or bread, that fills a person. Wiktionary glosses both Mandarin zhǔshí and Japanese しゅしょく as "staple food". Cantonese zyu2 sik6 matches, and Korean 주식 is the reading of the characters.

It is the starch of the meal, set against the side dishes of the [副食](副食.md). In East Asia it is above all [米飯](米飯.md), cooked rice, or [麺](麺.md), noodles.
