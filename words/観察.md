## Notes
- [観](../characters/観.md) "observe" + [察](../characters/察.md) "inspect, examine"

観察 means to observe: to watch something closely and take note of it, and the observation itself. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 觀察 (an alias; Mandarin guānchá, Cantonese gun1 caat3) as "to observe; to watch; to inspect; to examine", Japanese かんさつ as "observation; to observe" (science is based on observation), Korean 관찰 as "observation", and Vietnamese quan sát as "to observe". The five readings agree.

観察 is the stand-in for [観](../characters/観.md), which is too bounded to stand alone. Observing and measuring is [[観測]], and attending as a spectator is [[観覧]].
