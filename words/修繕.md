## Notes
- [修](../characters/修.md) "to repair" + [繕](../characters/繕.md) "to mend, restore" — "to repair, renovate" (e.g. city walls in classical texts like the Zuo Zhuan). `vietnamese: tu thiện` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese, distinct from the unrelated homophone-adjacent thiện "good/virtuous" — this 繕 is a different character (纟-radical, "to mend").

Legitimizing note: 繕's own `stand_in` is this exact word (修繕), so 繕 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 修繕.
