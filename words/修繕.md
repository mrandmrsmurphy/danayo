## Notes
- [修](../characters/修.md) "to repair" + [繕](../characters/繕.md) "to mend, restore" — "to repair, renovate" (e.g. city walls in classical texts like the Zuo Zhuan). `vietnamese: tu thiện` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese (confirmed via web search), distinct from the unrelated homophone-adjacent thiện "good/virtuous" — this 繕 is a different character (纟-radical, "to mend"). Filled the blank `pos: 事詞`. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 숫션 diverges from Sino-Korean 수선 in the first syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨㄛㄙ⼶ㄋ unique among both words and characters).
