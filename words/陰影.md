## Notes
- [陰 (char)](../characters/陰%20(char).md) "Yin, shade" + [影](../characters/影.md) "shadow, form" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes 影 as a bound character (陰's own stand-in is [[陰]] itself; transitivity fails, so no `#cranberry`).

陰影 means shadow or shade, both literally (a cast shadow) and figuratively (a shadow over one's mood, a lingering psychological shadow — 心理的陰影, "psychological shadow/trauma"). Mandarin 陰影/阴影 (yīnyǐng), Cantonese 陰影 (jam1jing2), and Japanese 陰影 (いんえい) all carry both senses in everyday and artistic usage (陰影法, "shading technique" in drawing). Korean 음영 (eumyeong) shares this range but is commonly interchanged in casual speech with the native nouns 그늘 ("shade," from 陰's own native gloss) and 그림자 ("shadow," from 影's own native gloss). Vietnamese âm ảnh is a comparatively rare, literary compositional form; everyday Vietnamese favors the native noun bóng ("shadow") instead.
