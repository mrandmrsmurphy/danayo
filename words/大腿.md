## Notes
- [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big" + [腿](../characters/腿.md) "leg" — "thigh, femur" (大腿骨, "femur bone"). `vietnamese: đại thoái` is an attested Vietnamese medical/anatomical term with the same sense (xương đại thoái, "femur bone"); everyday Vietnamese instead uses the native đùi for "thigh" (domain knowledge, not independently confirmed by search).
