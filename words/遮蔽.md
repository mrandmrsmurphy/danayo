## Notes
- [遮](characters/遮.md) "cover, block" + [蔽](characters/蔽.md) "cover, conceal" — "cover, protect."

Mandarin zhēbì, cantonese ze1 bai3, japanese しゃへい, and korean 차폐 are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. Vietnamese già tế is the honest compositional reading (遮's own già + 蔽's own tế); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 자퍼 diverges from Sino-Korean 차폐 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄆㄝ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 遮's own `stand_in` is this exact word (遮蔽), so 遮 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 遮蔽; 蔽's own `stand_in` is instead 隠蔽, so this word does not legitimize 蔽.
