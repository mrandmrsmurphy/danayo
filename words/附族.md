## Notes
- [附](../characters/附%20(char).md) "attach, join" + [族](../characters/族.md) "tribe, clan" — "the Attached Tribe, Levi." Their own `stand_in` values: 附's is itself; 族's is [[家族]]; none points here, so 附族 is an independent compound.

附族 is the tribe of Levi, Leah's third son, whose name means "joined, attached": "Now this time my husband will be joined to me" (Genesis 29:34). It is a Dan'a'yo **neologism**, a meaning-based name, "Attached Tribe," and the tribe set apart for the service of the sanctuary, whose book is [[附族記]] (Leviticus). Compare [[聴族]] (Simeon) and [[獅族]] (Judah). See [[基督敎]].

- Hebrew name: לֵוִי (Levi) (注音 ㄌㄝㄅㄧ).
