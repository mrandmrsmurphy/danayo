## Notes
- [分 (char)](../characters/分%20(char).md) "divide" + [析](../characters/析.md) "split, parse" — to analyze; analysis — stand-in for [[析]], which cannot appear independently

Wiktionary: Chinese "to analyze; to review; to parse; (Classical, literally) to separate, to divide; analysis; parsing"; Japanese, mathematics and chemistry, "analysis", and as a verb "to analyze"; Korean 분석 is the hanja form, "analysis"; Vietnamese phân tích is the Han form, "to analyze". Used in `文法 - 01序文` and the Mandarin, Cantonese and Japanese booklets.
