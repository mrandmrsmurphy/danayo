## Notes
- [恩](../characters/恩.md) "grace, kindness" + [愛](../characters/愛%20(char).md) "love" — no stand-in relationship applies (恩's own stand-in is [[恩寵]]; 愛's own is itself).

恩愛 means (marital/mutual) love — deep affection paired with kindness.

Mandarin ēn'ài, Cantonese jan1 oi3, Japanese おんあい, Korean 은애, and Vietnamese ân ái are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` had the same recurring 恩-syllable error already fixed on [[恩恵]]/[[恩情]] (안→은), also correcting `kwin` from false to true (Dan'a'yo 은애 now matches Korean 은애). Filled blank `vietnamese`, omitted empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄜㄋ·ㄚㄧ unique across both words and characters).
