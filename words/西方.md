## Notes
- [西](../characters/西.md) "west" is legitimized by this exact compound (its own `stand_in`). [方](../characters/方.md) "direction" is separately legitimized by [[方向]] — transitivity fails, no cranberry.

Mandarin xīfāng, japanese せいほう, and korean 서방 (all also the real common words for "the West") confirmed standard and compositional. Vietnamese hướng tây is a real attested directional phrase.
