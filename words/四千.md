## Notes
- [四 (char)](../characters/四%20(char).md) "four" + [千](../characters/千.md) "thousand"

四千 is four thousand (4,000), a plain compositional numeral, 四 × 1,000. Japanese says よんせん in everyday speech, with the native よん in place of し. Vietnamese tứ thiên is the compositional Hán Việt reading; everyday Vietnamese counts natively instead (from memory, not re-checked), as on [七千](七千.md). Used in the Dan'a'yo numeral pieces such as the year in the 1919 Korean Declaration of Independence on [Korea](../lookup/Korean/Korea.md); there are no words for full numbers, only for these pieces.
