## Notes
- [干](../characters/干.md) "shield" (extended: to concern, interfere) + [犯](../characters/犯.md) "to violate; offend" — no stand-in relationship applies (干's own `stand_in` is [[干渉]], 犯's is [[犯罪]]). (Literary) to infringe upon, encroach upon, transgress.

干犯 is a more formal/literary near-synonym of [[干渉]], both built on the same "干" root of interference — 干犯 leans toward violation of rights, sovereignty, or law (国家主権を干犯する, "to infringe upon national sovereignty"), a graver register than 干渉's more general "meddle, intervene."

Japanese かんぱん, Korean 간범, and Vietnamese can phạm are all standard readings, comparatively rare/formal in everyday use. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄋㄅㄚㄇ unique across both words and characters).
