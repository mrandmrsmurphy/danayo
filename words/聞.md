## Notes
- [聞](../characters/聞%20(char).md) "hear" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 聞 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

聞 means to hear, as in 新聞 (news; newspaper) and 百聞不如一見 ("hearing something a hundred times is not as good as seeing it once").

Mandarin wén, Cantonese man4, and Korean 문 (mun) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading きく (kiku, "to hear") rather than the on-readings ブン/モン (BUN/MON) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese văn is the standard reading. This word completes a genuine 3-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read mun/문/ㄇㄨㄋ) with [[紋]] ("mark, line, symbol") and [[蚊]] ("mosquito, gnat") — re-verified as complete with the corrected homophone-check tooling (吻/問/文 share the syllable at the character level only, none has an independent word page).
