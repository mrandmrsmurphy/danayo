## Notes
- [聞](../characters/聞%20(char).md) "hear" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 聞 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

聞 means to hear: to perceive sound with the ear (聽聞, "hear of"; 見聞, "what one sees and hears, experience"; 新聞, "news; newspaper"; 傳聞, "hearsay"), and in Mandarin also to smell (聞到, "smell"). It figures in 百聞不如一見 ("hearing something a hundred times is not as good as seeing it once"). The simplified form 闻 is listed as an alias. The graph is a 形声 compound of 耳 "ear" with the phonetic 門.

Mandarin wén, Cantonese man4, Japanese ぶん (新聞/しんぶん, "newspaper"; 見聞/けんぶん, "experience") with the alternate on'yomi もん, Korean 문 (신문, 견문) and Vietnamese văn (tân văn, "news"; kiến văn, "experience") are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native verb 聞く (きく) for the everyday word "hear", so on'yomi ぶん is bound to compounds; Korean has native 듣다.
