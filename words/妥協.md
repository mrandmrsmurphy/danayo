## Notes
- [妥](../characters/妥.md) "appropriate, proper" + [協](../characters/協.md) "cooperate, work together" — no stand-in relationship applies (妥's own stand-in is [[妥当]]; 協's own is [[協力]]).

妥協 means compromise, or to settle a dispute by mutual concession — reaching an arrangement that is workable ("appropriate") through cooperation.

Mandarin tuǒxié, Cantonese to5 hip3, Japanese だきょう (using 妥's alternate on'yomi DA), Korean 타협, and Vietnamese thỏa hiệp are all standard, fully compositional readings.
