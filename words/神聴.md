## Notes
- [神](../characters/神%20(char).md) "god, divine" + [聴](../characters/聴%20(char).md) "hear, listen" — "Ishmael, the son of Abraham and Hagar." Their own `stand_in` values: 神's is itself; 聴's is itself; none points here, so 神聴 is an independent compound.

神聴 is Ishmael, Abraham's son by Hagar, whose name means "God hears": "the LORD has listened to your affliction" (Genesis 16:11). It is a Dan'a'yo **neologism**, a meaning-based name, "God hears" (神 god, 聴 hear), where 神 renders El, the generic word for God, on the same principle as [[神助]] (Eleazar), and it names the Genesis section 神聴世代記. The character 聴 has the alias 聽, so the spelling 神聽 is accepted. Compare [[聴族]] (Simeon, "hearing"). See [[基督敎]].

- Hebrew name: יִשְׁמָעֵאל (Yishma'el) (注音 ·ㄧㄙㄇㄚ·ㄝㄌㄜ).
