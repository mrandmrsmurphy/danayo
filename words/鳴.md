## Notes
- [鳴](../characters/鳴%20(char).md) "cry, chirp" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [鳴](../characters/鳴%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

鳴 means to cry or chirp (of birds/animals).

Mandarin míng, Cantonese ming4, Japanese めい, Korean 명, and Vietnamese minh are all standard, fully compositional readings. Three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all myeng/명/ㄇ⼶ㄫ) with [[皿]] ("dish; container") and [[明]] ("bright") — already fully cross-referenced from 皿's side.
