## Notes
- [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day, sun" + [報](../characters/報%20(char).md) "report" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 日 and 報's own stand-ins are themselves).

日報 means daily newspaper.

Mandarin rìbào, Cantonese jat6 bou3, Japanese にっぽう, Korean 일보, and Vietnamese nhật báo are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `cantonese` had the wrong tone (jat4→jat6). No homophones (注音 ㄋㄧㄊㄅㄚㄨ unique across both words and characters).
