## Notes
- [現](../characters/現%20(char).md) "present, current" + [金](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal, money" — "ready money," cash. Their own `stand_in` values: 現's is itself; 金's is itself; none points here, so 現金 is an independent compound.

現金 is cash: money in notes and coins, as against a cheque, a card or a debt. Mandarin xiànjīn, Cantonese jin6 gam1, Japanese げんきん and Korean 현금 all use it. Compare [[金銭]] (money in general), [[紙幣]] (banknote) and [[貨幣]] (currency).
