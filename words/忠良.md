## Notes
- [忠](../characters/忠.md) "loyal" + [良](../characters/良.md) "good; fine"

忠良 is "loyal and good," used of faithful subjects. According to Wiktionary, Chinese 忠良 (zhōngliáng, Cantonese zung1 loeng4) is an adjective, "loyal to and enthusiastically supporting an emperor," and a noun for people with that quality; Japanese ちゅうりょう is listed as a noun, "loyalty." Dan'a'yo keeps the adjective sense, as in 忠良之臣民 "loyal subjects," from the 1945 surrender broadcast on [Japan](../lookup/Japanese/Japan.md). Wiktionary has no Korean or Vietnamese section; `korean` 충량 and `vietnamese` trung lương are the compositional readings, not independently checked.

It is related to [忠誠](忠誠.md) "loyalty" and [忠実](忠実.md) "faithful," which share 忠.
