## Notes
- [後](../characters/後%20(char).md) "after" + [悔](../characters/悔.md) "repent, regret, confess" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [悔](../characters/悔.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (後's own stand-in is itself).

後悔 means to regret — remorse felt after an action, literally "after-remorse."

Mandarin hòuhuǐ, Cantonese hau6 fui3, Japanese こうかい, Korean 후회, and Vietnamese hậu hối are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄨㄛㄏㄛㄧ unique across both words and characters).
