## Notes
- [目 (char)](../characters/目%20(char).md) "eye; item" + [録](../characters/録.md) "record; copy"

目録 is a list, catalogue or inventory: a record of items, such as the books in a library or the goods in a store. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin mùlù as "directory, list, catalogue", and also "table of contents"; the computing sense is "directory". Japanese もくろく, Korean 목록 and Cantonese muk6 luk6 match. Dan'a'yo gives the table-of-contents sense to [目次](目次.md), so 目録 is the list of things.

The alias 目錄 is the traditional spelling. It differs from [索引](索引.md), an index to a text, and from [事典](事典.md), which explains items where a 目録 only names them.
