## Notes
- [慈](../characters/慈.md) "kind, loving" + [善](../characters/善%20(char).md) "good, virtuous" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [慈](../characters/慈.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (善's own stand-in is itself).

慈善 means charity or benevolence — kind, generous acts toward those in need.

Mandarin císhàn, Cantonese ci4 sin6, Japanese じぜん, Korean 자선, and Vietnamese từ thiện are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `cantonese`/`vietnamese`, fixed missing "(char)" suffix on `characters`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄙ⼶ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
