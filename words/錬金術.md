## Notes
- [錬](../characters/錬.md) "refine, temper" + [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal; gold" + [術 (char)](../characters/術%20(char).md) "art, technique" — alchemy.

錬金術 is alchemy, literally "the art of refining gold": the pre-modern practice of trying to transmute base metals such as [[鉛]] into [[黄金]] and to find an elixir of life. The Dan'a'yo headword is the Japanese-style 錬金術; 鍊金術 and 炼金术 are its Mandarin spellings. It is the historical forerunner of chemistry and of [[冶金]], but unlike metallurgy it is not an extant technical field. Mandarin liànjīnshù, Japanese れんきんじゅつ and Korean 연금술 are the standard readings.
