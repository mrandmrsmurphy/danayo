## Notes
- [温](../characters/温.md) "warm, lukewarm, tepid" + [度](../characters/度.md) "degree, manner" — no stand-in relationship applies (温's own stand-in is [[温暖]]; 度's own is [[程度]]).

温度 means temperature.

Mandarin wēndù, Cantonese wan1 dou6, Japanese おんど, and Korean 온도 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 温's and 度's own stored citations exactly). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with the compositional ôn độ (温's own ôn + 度's own citation độ); real-world Vietnamese instead uses nhiệt độ, built on 熱 ("heat") rather than 温. `kwin: true` because 諺文 온도 matches Korean 온도 exactly. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄛㄆㄉㄛ unique across both words and characters).
