## Notes
- [猛](../characters/猛.md) "fierce, strong, furious" + [禽](../characters/禽.md) "fowl" — no stand-in relationship applies (猛's own stand-in is [[猛烈]]; 禽's own is [[禽鳥]]).

猛禽 means bird of prey.

Mandarin měngqín, Cantonese maang5 kam4, Japanese もうきん, Korean 맹금 and Vietnamese mãnh cầm are all fully compositional, matching 猛's and 禽's own citations.
