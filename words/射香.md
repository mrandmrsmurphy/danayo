## Notes
- [射](../characters/射.md) "shoot; eject; emit" + [香](../characters/香.md) "incense; fragrant; aroma" — no stand-in relationship applies (射's own `stand_in` is [[射出]], 香's is [[香気]]).

射香 is musk, the aromatic secretion of the musk deer — 射 here contributes its "emit, secrete" sense (the same sense as [[射精]]) rather than a projectile-shooting one, paired with 香's "fragrance." The real, standard written form of this word across all CJKV languages is actually 麝香, using [[射]]'s alias 麝 (musk deer) rather than 射 itself — kept here as this word's `aliases` entry, per this vault's convention of citing the canonical character while documenting the more common alias form in prose.

Japanese じゃこう, Korean 사향, and Vietnamese xạ hương (both hương and its native-flavored variant nhang exist in 香's own field; hương is the one used here) are all standard, non-drifted readings. No HSK level is given for the compound. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼘ㄏ⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).

**Correction (2026-08-27)**: `japanese` had a kana typo (じゃかう, archaic/incorrect かう) — corrected to じゃこう (じゃ from 射's SHA + こう from 香's own KOU), matching the real attested reading of 麝香.
