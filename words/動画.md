## Notes
- [動](../characters/動%20(char).md) "move" + [画](../characters/画.md) "picture" — "moving picture." Their own `stand_in` values: 動's is itself; 画's is [[絵画]]; none points here, so 動画 is an independent compound.

動画 is animation: drawings or images shown in sequence so that they move. Mandarin dònghuà ("animation; cartoon") and Cantonese dung6 waa6-2 use 動畫, kept as an alias, and Japanese どうが uses 動画 for moving images and video in general. Korean 동화 is the same word read in Sino-Korean. Compare [[映画]] (film) and [[漫画]] (comic).
