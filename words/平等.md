## Notes
- [平](../characters/平.md) "even; level" + [等 (char)](../characters/等%20(char).md) "class; rank; equal" — no stand-in relationship applies (平's own `stand_in` is [[水平]], 等's is itself).

平等 names equality — being on the same level or rank, socially, legally, or morally (男女平等 "gender equality").

**Japanese preserves a genuine Buddhist-derived irregular reading here**: 平等 is read びょうどう (byōdō), using the archaic Go-on (呉音) reading びょう for 平 rather than the far more common Kan-on reading へい (as in 平和, "peace") — this Go-on layer entered Japanese earliest, via Buddhist scripture transmission, and survives specifically in this word's Buddhist-philosophical original sense of the fundamental equality/non-duality of all beings, distinct from the character's own stored on'yomi list (HEI/HYOU/BEN), which doesn't include this reading.

Korean 평등 and Vietnamese bình đẳng are both standard, non-drifted readings. HSK level 2 already correct. No homophones (注音 ㄅ⼶ㄫㄉㄨㄫ unique across both words and characters).
