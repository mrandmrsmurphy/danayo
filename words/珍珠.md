## Notes
- [珍](characters/珍%20(char).md) "rare, treasure" + [珠](characters/珠.md) "pearl" — 珠's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 珍's own is itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 珠 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

珍珠 means pearl.

Mandarin zhēnzhū, Cantonese zan1 zyu1, and Korean 진주 are fully compositional, matching 珍's and 珠's own stored citations exactly. Japanese しんじゅ (SHIN+SHU) uses the reading of 真 rather than 珍 — this is not an alias-divergence but a genuine cross-linguistic orthographic difference: Chinese conventionally writes this word 珍珠, while Japanese conventionally writes it 真珠 (listed here as an alias), and しんじゅ correctly follows the real Japanese spelling's own reading. Vietnamese trân châu was already correct (châu being one of 珠's own listed readings). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄋㄐㄨ unique across both words and characters).
