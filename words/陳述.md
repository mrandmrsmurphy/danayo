## Notes
- [陳](../characters/陳.md) "exhibit, display" + [述](../characters/述.md) "narrate, state" — 陳述 is the stand-in word for [述](../characters/述.md); 述 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse.

陳述 names a formal statement or declaration — laying out facts or a position clearly and in order, often in legal, testimonial, or grammatical contexts: 陳述書 ("written statement"), 被告人の陳述 ("the defendant's statement"). In grammar, 陳述文 names a declarative sentence, as opposed to a question or command. The related 陳情 ("to petition, to explain one's circumstances," trần tình in Vietnamese) shares the same "laying out" root sense but leans toward pleading one's case rather than neutral reporting.

Japanese ちんじゅつ and Korean 진술 are both standard compositional Sino-readings, 진술 being especially common in Korean legal contexts (witness/suspect statements). Vietnamese trần thuật is likewise standard and has its own literary-theory sense too (narrative mode/voice in fiction). No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄋㄙㄨㄊ unique across both words and characters).
