## Notes
- [陳](../characters/陳.md) "exhibit, display" + [述](../characters/述.md) "narrate, state"

陳述 means to state, to declare, and a statement. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 陳述 (alias 陈述; Mandarin chénshù, Cantonese can4 seot6) as "to state; to declare" and "statement; declaration", Japanese ちんじゅつ as "statement, declaration" and "make a statement", Korean 진술 as "statement, declaration" and Vietnamese trần thuật as "to state, to declare".

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [述](../characters/述.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
