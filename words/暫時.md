## Notes
- [暫](../characters/暫.md) "temporary, momentary" + [時 (char)](../characters/時%20(char).md) "time" — this word is the viable form of [暫](../characters/暫.md): the character's meaning (temporary; for a short time) is identical, but 暫 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 暫時 is its `stand_in` compound.

暫時 marks a state or arrangement as explicitly short-lived and expected to change — 暫時休業 ("temporarily closed"), 暫時保留 ("held temporarily, pending a decision") — used constantly in administrative and everyday notices across the CJKV sphere to signal that the current situation is provisional, not the new normal.

Japanese ざんじ, Korean 잠시, and Vietnamese tạm thời are all standard compositional Sino-readings. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 잠시 matches Sino-Korean 잠시 exactly. No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄇㄙㄧ unique across both words and characters).
