## Notes
- [暫](../characters/暫.md) "temporary, momentary" + [時 (char)](../characters/時%20(char).md) "time" — this word is the viable form of [暫](../characters/暫.md): the character's meaning (temporary; for a short time) is identical, but 暫 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 暫時 is its `stand_in` compound.

暫時 means for the time being: lasting only a short while, provisionally. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 暫時 (alias 暂时; Mandarin zànshí, Cantonese zaam6 si4) as "temporary" (everything is temporary), "temporarily" and "for the time being; for now". Vietnamese tạm thời is "temporary". Japanese ざんじ is "(for a) short while", and Korean 잠시 is "a moment, a short time", so in Japanese and Korean the word names a brief span of time more than a provisional state.
