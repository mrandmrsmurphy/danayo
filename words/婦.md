## Notes
- [婦](../characters/婦%20(char).md) "lady, woman" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

婦 means lady or woman — used both independently and productively in compounds like [[夫婦]] ("married couple") and [[主婦]] ("housewife").

Mandarin fù, Cantonese fu5, Japanese ふ, Korean 부, and Vietnamese phụ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄅ⼜/byu/뷰) with the unrelated [[負]] ("carry, bear").
