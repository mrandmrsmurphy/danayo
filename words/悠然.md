## Notes
- [悠](../characters/悠.md) "leisurely" + [然 (char)](../characters/然%20(char).md) "so; like"

悠然 is unhurried and at ease: calm and carefree, in no haste. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yōurán as "leisurely and carefree; comfortable and content" and Japanese ゆうぜん as "calm, with composure". Cantonese jau4 jin4 matches, and Korean 유연 is the reading of the characters.

It is calm as leisure, a gentler kin of [泰然](泰然.md), unruffled, and [逍遥](逍遥.md), free and unfettered. Its opposite is the tense [緊張](緊張.md).
