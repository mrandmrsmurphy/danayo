## Notes
- [福](../characters/福%20(char).md) "blessing" + [州](../characters/州%20(char).md) "state" — "blessing prefecture". No stand-in relationship applies (both characters are their own stand-ins).

福州 (Fuzhou) is the capital of Fujian province in southeastern China. Wiktionary gives no etymology for the name and lists the poetic names 榕城 and 三山 for it. Compounds listed under it, such as 福州話 (the Fuzhou language), 福州菜 and 福州豆, are not claimed here.

Japanese ふくしゅう, Korean 복주 and Vietnamese Phúc Châu are the Sino-Xenic readings listed on Wiktionary; the Korean field holds the North Korean reading derived from the character pages, 복주, and the usual South Korean name today is 푸저우 (listed on Wiktionary). The Chinese entry lists slang alternative forms 虎糾 and 虎究 (not adopted here). Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese entries. No homophones (注音 ㄈㄨㄎㄐㄨㄛ unique across both words and characters).
