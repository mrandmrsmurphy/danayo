## Notes
"Trust, credit, credibility" (信用卡, "credit card"). `vietnamese: tín dụng` is one of the most common everyday Vietnamese words for this exact concept (thẻ tín dụng, "credit card"), a direct match. Filled the blank `pos: 名詞`. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 신용 matches Sino-Korean 신용 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄋ⼄ㄫ unique among both words and characters).
