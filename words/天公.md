## Notes
- [天 (char)](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven" + [公 (char)](../characters/公%20(char).md) "lord; duke" — "the Lord of Heaven."

天公 has three senses in Wiktionary. It is "God, the ruler of Heaven"; in Chinese folk religion and Daoism it is the Jade Emperor, as one of his popular names beside 天公伯; and, in literary or dialectal use, it is "the heavens" and so the weather, as in 天公不作美, "the heavens are not cooperative", said when the weather is bad. Japanese てんこう is listed among the names of the Jade Emperor. Synonyms for "God" include 老天爺, 老天 and 天老爺. The Mandarin and Cantonese readings are the attested ones; the Japanese is attested as a name of the Jade Emperor, and the Korean and Vietnamese fields are compositional (Korean follows the North Korean readings).

See [玉皇](玉皇.md), the Jade Emperor, and the vault's Christian terms for God, [天主](天主.md) and [上帝](上帝.md).
