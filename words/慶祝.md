## Notes
- [慶](../characters/慶%20(char).md) "congratulate, celebrate" + [祝](../characters/祝.md) "celebrate" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [祝](../characters/祝.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (慶's own stand-in is itself).

慶祝 means to celebrate — a near-synonymous doubling of two characters both meaning "celebrate."

Mandarin qìngzhù, Cantonese hing3 zuk1, Japanese けいしゅく, Korean 경축, and Vietnamese khánh chúc are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄎ⼶ㄫㄐㄨㄎ unique across both words and characters).
