## Notes
- [信](../characters/信.md) "trust; believe" + [念](../characters/念.md) "thought"

信念 is belief or conviction: a settled view that one holds to be true. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xìnniàn as "conviction; faith; belief", and Japanese しんねん as "belief, faith, conviction". Korean 신념 and Cantonese seon3 nim6 match.

It is firmer than an opinion ([意見](意見.md)) and need not be proved, which separates it from [知識](知識.md), knowledge. Religious faith has its own words on [Religion](../lexipedia/Religion.md).
