## Notes
- [土](../characters/土%20(char).md) "earth, soil" + [肥](../characters/肥.md) "fertile, manure" — no stand-in relationship applies (土 is its own stand-in; 肥's own is [[肥満]]).

土肥 means farmyard manure or soil fertilizer.

Japanese どひ (using 土's alternate on'yomi DO), Korean 토비, and Vietnamese thổ phì are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄛㄅㄨㄧ unique across both words and characters).
