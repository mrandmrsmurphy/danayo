## Notes
- Short for [越南](越南.md) ("Vietnam") + [共産党](共産党.md) ("communist party") = [越 (char)](../characters/越%20(char).md) + [共 (char)](../characters/共%20(char).md) — literally "Viet-communist."

越共 is the standard abbreviated Chinese name for the Vietcong (the common English name for the National Liberation Front), the communist political and military organization that fought in South Vietnam during the Vietnam War (1955-1975). The abbreviation pattern — truncating a country name plus a political-affiliation term into a two-character blend — mirrors this vault's other -共 political shorthand compounds.

Mandarin Yuègòng and Cantonese jyut6 gung6 are both standard, fully compositional readings, matching 越's and 共's own stored citations. Japanese and Korean both instead transliterate the English "Vietcong" rather than using a kanji/hanja calque — the same real-term-over-compositional-reading pattern already established for other modern political/geographic proper nouns in this vault (compare [新西蘭](新西蘭.md), where Japanese likewise transliterates rather than calques).
