## Notes
- Short for [[越南]] ("Vietnam") + [[共産党]] ("communist party") = [越 (char)](../characters/越%20(char).md) + [共 (char)](../characters/共%20(char).md) — literally "Viet-communist."

越共 is the standard abbreviated Chinese name for the Vietcong (the common English name for the National Liberation Front), the communist political and military organization that fought in South Vietnam during the Vietnam War (1955-1975). The abbreviation pattern — truncating a country name plus a political-affiliation term into a two-character blend — mirrors this vault's other -共 political shorthand compounds.

Mandarin Yuègòng and Cantonese jyut6 gung6 are both standard, fully compositional readings, matching 越's and 共's own stored citations. Japanese and Korean both instead transliterate the English "Vietcong" rather than using a kanji/hanja calque — the same real-term-over-compositional-reading pattern already established for other modern political/geographic proper nouns in this vault (compare [[新西蘭]], where Japanese likewise transliterates rather than calques). **Real gap found and fixed 2026-09-22**: 越's own character page had a stored Vietnamese field of only one item, an attested Nôm reading, and was entirely missing the actual standard Hán Việt reading of 越 (as in Việt Nam itself) — added.

`kwin` is false, correctly, since this word's own `korean` field is a transliteration rather than a compositional Sino-Korean reading, and plainly diverges from Dan'a'yo. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ⼔ㄊㄍ⼄ㄫ unique among both words and characters).
