## Notes
- [乾 (char)](../characters/乾%20(char).md) "dry" + [溜](../characters/溜.md) (stand-in for 餾, "distill") — dry distillation, pyrolysis (destructive distillation of solids, e.g. coal/wood, without water). Attested across Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, and Korean. Written 干溜 in Simplified Chinese; 乾 is the real character here, not 干 ("shield"). `vietnamese: can lưu` is compositional from 乾's own "dry" reading can + 溜's own lưu; no independent attestation as an established Vietnamese chemistry term was found — Vietnamese instead commonly describes this process with the native compound chưng cất khô (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 견류 diverges from Sino-Korean 건류 in the first syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼶ㄋㄌ⼜ unique among both words and characters).
