## Notes
- [昇](../characters/昇.md) "ascend" + [級](../characters/級.md) "level, rank, class" — no stand-in relationship applies (昇's own stand-in is [[上昇]]; 級's own is [[等級]]).

This entry documents 升级/Shēngjí (Tractor), the trick-taking card game popular across China, using the fully compositional Sino-Mandarin/Cantonese pronunciation. The same characters also form the ordinary common-noun/verb compound meaning "level up, promote, advance in rank," for which Korean 승급 and Vietnamese thăng cấp are both genuine standard terms; Japanese ションジー is a phonetic transliteration specific to the card game, since Japanese lacks its own native name for it (the "promotion" sense instead uses しょうきゅう). No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨㄫㄍㄧㄆ unique across both words and characters).
