## Notes
- [卦 (char)](../characters/卦%20(char).md) "trigram"

卦 combines phonetic [[圭]] with [[卜]] ("divination"). It names the trigrams and hexagrams of the I Ching (易經) divination system — [[八卦]] ("the Eight Trigrams"), the foundational set of three-line symbols (each line either broken or unbroken) combined into sixty-four hexagrams used for classical Chinese divination and cosmology.

Mandarin guà, Cantonese gwaa3, and Japanese け (ke, on'yomi, largely confined to divination contexts) alongside native うらかた (urakata, "a divination result, oracle") all preserve the sense. Korean 괘 (gwae) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 점괘 (jeomgwae, "a divination result, fortune"). Vietnamese quái is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in bát quái (八卦, "the Eight Trigrams").

This word shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading (gwai/괘/ㄍ⺢ㄧ) with another word still awaiting its own turn in this sweep, [[掛]] ("hang") — the reciprocal half of this callout will be completed when it comes up.
