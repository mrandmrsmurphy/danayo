## Notes
- [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "ride" + [馬 (char)](../characters/馬%20(char).md) "horse" — horseback riding, to ride a horse.

乗馬 is the standard word for both the activity of horseback riding and, as a suru verb (乗馬する), the act of mounting/riding a horse — 乗馬クラブ (riding club) and 乗馬服 (riding attire) are common Japanese collocations. Mandarin chéng mǎ, Cantonese sing4 maa5, and Korean 승마 (as in 승마장, "riding arena") all parallel this sense. **Vietnamese content note**: thừa mã is a compositionally sound classical Sino-Vietnamese derivation, but modern everyday Vietnamese more commonly says cưỡi ngựa (native, "to ride a horse") for the activity; the same uncertainty caveat already flagged on [[乗船]]'s thừa thuyền and [[乗車]]'s thừa xa applies here — presented as a defensible derivation rather than a separately confirmed everyday term.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 숭마 diverges from Sino-Korean 승마, since 乗's own Dan'a'yo reading 숭 doesn't match Sino-Korean 승, even though 馬's own reading matches exactly (馬 is independently `kwin: true`).
