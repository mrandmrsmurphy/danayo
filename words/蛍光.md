## Notes
- [蛍 (char)](../characters/蛍%20(char).md) "firefly" + [光 (char)](../characters/光%20(char).md) "light"

蛍光 is the light of a firefly, and fluorescence. English Wiktionary glosses Japanese 蛍光 (けいこう) as "the light of a firefly" and, in physics, "fluorescence". It glosses Chinese 螢光 (alias here; Mandarin yíngguāng, Cantonese jing4 gwong1) as "light from fireflies; dim light", and chiefly in Taiwan as "fluorescence" and "fluorescent light". Korean 형광 is a hanja form of the same word, "the light of a firefly; fluorescence", and Vietnamese huỳnh quang is built from the readings of the two characters. See also [蛍火虫](蛍火虫.md), the firefly.
