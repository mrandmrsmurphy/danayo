## Notes
- [百](../characters/百%20(char).md) "hundred" + [貨](../characters/貨.md) "goods" + [店](../characters/店.md) "shop" — "hundred-goods shop," a department store. Their own `stand_in` values: 百's is itself; 貨's is [[貨物]]; 店's is [[商店]]; none points here, so 百貨店 is an independent compound.

百貨店 is a department store: a large shop that sells many kinds of goods. Mandarin bǎihuòdiàn, Cantonese baak3 fo3 dim3, Japanese ひゃっかてん and Korean 백화점 all use it. Mandarin more often says 百貨公司. Compare [[商店]] (shop) and [[市場]] (market).
