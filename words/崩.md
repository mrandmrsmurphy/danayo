## Notes
- [崩 (char)](../characters/崩%20(char).md) "crumble; disintegrate; die; fall apart" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [崩 (char)](../characters/崩%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

崩 means to crumble, collapse, or fall apart — physically (a landslide, [[山崩]]) or figuratively (a system, market, or plan collapsing). Classically, it also carries the elevated, specific sense "the death of an emperor" (崩御), a sense reserved exclusively for the highest rank of sovereign.

Japanese ほう, Korean 붕, and Vietnamese băng are all standard readings. No homophones among other words (注音 ㄅㄨㄫ; the unrelated character [[朋]] shares the same reading, but its own stand-in word doesn't collide with 崩's).
