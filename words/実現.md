## Notes
- [実](../characters/実.md) "true, real" + [現 (char)](../characters/現%20(char).md) "present, appear, manifest" — literally "to make real and manifest," to realize or implement.

Mandarin shíxiàn, Cantonese sat6 jin6, Japanese じつげん (jitsugen), Korean 실현, and Vietnamese thực hiện are all standard, fully compositional readings across the sphere, matching 実's and 現's own stored citations exactly — a clean, uncontested cognate set with no notable sense-divergence between languages. The word covers both the abstract sense (a dream, goal, or plan "coming true") and the concrete sense (a plan or system being put into practice, "implemented").
