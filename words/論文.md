## Notes
- [論](../characters/論.md) "theory; thesis" + [文](../characters/文.md) "culture"

論文 is a thesis, dissertation or scholarly paper. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin lùnwén as "thesis; dissertation; paper" and Japanese ろんぶん as "thesis; dissertation; article". Korean 논문 and Cantonese leon6 man4 match. The word is shared across all of them.

It is the written work of a [研究](研究.md), and a [学位](学位.md) is often awarded for one. Its subject is often a [実験](実験.md) or a [調査](調査.md).
