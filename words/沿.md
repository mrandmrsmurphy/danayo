## Notes
- [沿](characters/沿%20(char).md) "edge, side" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 沿 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

沿 means edge, side (originally the edge of a river; extended to "follow along, run alongside," as in 沿海 "coastal").

Mandarin yán, Cantonese jyun4, Korean 연, and Vietnamese duyên are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 沿's own stored citations exactly. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field, added the missing `pos`/`japanese`(えん, on'yomi EN)/`hsk_level`("2") fields, reformatted `characters` to a proper block-list with the "(char)" suffix. No homophones (注音 ⼔ㄇ unique across both words and characters).
