## Notes
- [帖 (char)](../characters/帖%20(char).md) "invitation; card" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [帖 (char)](../characters/帖%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

帖 names an invitation card, note, or official document — this specific "tiě" reading is one of three distinct senses the same character carries (the others being tiē, "submissive," and tiè, "calligraphy copybook").

Japanese じょう, Korean 첩, and Vietnamese thiếp are all standard readings. No homophones among other words (注音 ㄊㄛㄆ unique).
