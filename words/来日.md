## Notes
- [来](characters/来%20(char).md) "come" + [日](characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 来 and 日's own stand-ins are themselves, each with its own independent word page). Technically just the two free-standing words juxtaposed: <ruby>[来](/words/来.md)<rt>ㄌㄚㄧ</rt></ruby> + <ruby>[日](/words/日.md)<rt>ㄋㄧㄊ</rt></ruby>.

来日 means tomorrow. For a more poetic way to say it, see <ruby>[明日](/words/明日.md)<rt>ㄇ⼶ㄫㄋㄧㄊ</rt></ruby>.

Mandarin láirì, Cantonese loi4 jat6, Japanese らいにち, Korean 래일, and Vietnamese lai nhật are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 来's and 日's own stored citations exactly. Fixed severe contamination: `mandarin`/`cantonese`/`vietnamese` had all been copied from the unrelated compound [[明日]]'s own fields (míngrì/ming4 jat6/ngày mai), and `japanese` held a garbled two-value list mixing 明日's native reading with an extra trailing comma. `korean` was 내일, the South Korean 두음법칙-shifted form — corrected to 래일, the North Korean/문화어 form per the permanent vault convention. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄚㄧㄋㄧㄊ unique across both words and characters).
