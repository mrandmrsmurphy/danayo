## Notes
- [行 (char)](../characters/行%20(char).md) "attend, go to" is separately legitimized by [[行]] itself. [星 (char)](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" is separately legitimized by [[星]] itself. No cranberry.

Mandarin xíngxīng, korean 행성, and vietnamese hành tinh (all also the real common words for "planet") confirmed standard and compositional. Japanese わくせい is the real, standard term (惑星), already documented via the alias — legitimate real-term substitution, not compositional.
