## Notes
- [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" + [節 (char)](../characters/節%20(char).md) "node, joint, festival" — no `#cranberry`: 音's own `stand_in` is [[音楽]], 節's is 節 itself; neither points here.

音節 (yīnjié) is the standard Mandarin word for "syllable," and Cantonese 音節 (jam1zit3) likewise. Japanese おんせつ (onsetsu) and Korean 음절 (eumjeol) are both standard terms in their languages as well. Sino-Vietnamese âm tiết is likewise the standard, everyday Vietnamese word — a clean case across all five languages.

**Fixed a real bug**: `aliases` had wrongly listed 音節 (the headword's own traditional form, identical to the filename) as an alias of itself — trimmed to just the genuine simplified variant, 音节.
