## Notes
- [帆 (char)](../characters/帆%20(char).md) "sail; sailboat" + [船](../characters/船.md) "boat; ship" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 帆's and 船's own `stand_in` fields point to themselves).

帆船 names a sailboat — traditionally a Chinese junk, and more generally any sail-powered vessel. This word is one of the several 船-compounds ([[船舶]], [[漁船]], [[船尾]], [[船籍]], [[乗船]]) that correctly use 船's regular citation reading (ㄙ⼔ㄇ/swem), unlike the flagged exceptions [[艦船]]/[[宇宙船]] documented on [[船]]'s own page.

Japanese はんせん, Korean 범선, and Vietnamese phàm thuyền (verified; everyday Vietnamese prefers the native thuyền buồm, "sail-boat," instead) are all standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄚㄇㄙ⼔ㄇ unique across both words and characters).
