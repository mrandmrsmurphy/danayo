## Notes
- [馬 (char)](../characters/馬%20(char).md) "horse" + [上 (char)](../characters/上%20(char).md) "up, above" — no `#cranberry`: 馬's own `stand_in` is 馬 itself, 上's is 上 itself; neither points here.

馬上 (mǎshàng) very commonly means "immediately, right away" in colloquial modern Mandarin (an idiomatic extension — presumably from the speed of a mounted messenger), not just "horseback." This Dan'a'yo word deliberately keeps only the literal, compositional sense ("horseback, on horseback") and excludes the idiomatic "immediately" extension. Cantonese maa5soeng6, Japanese ばじょう (bajō), and Korean 마상 (masang) likewise retain the literal sense as their primary or sole meaning. Sino-Vietnamese mã thượng is the compositional form, though it is not the everyday Vietnamese way to say "on horseback" (which favors the native phrase trên lưng ngựa).
