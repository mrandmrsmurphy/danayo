## Notes
- [巳](../characters/巳.md) "sixth Earthly Branch, snake" + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — no stand-in relationship applies (巳's own `stand_in` is the special `名専字` naming-restriction marker, not a specific compound).

Fourth month of the ancient Chinese lunar calendar, named for the sixth Earthly Branch 巳 (snake).

Snakes emerge fully from hibernation as the earth warms into late spring. 巳 is also associated with the hour of 9–11 a.m., when the sun climbs high — mirroring the season's rising energy.

Japanese みつき reads both halves natively (巳's kun'yomi み "snake" + 月's kun'yomi つき "month"), the same native-zodiac-name pattern as [[寅月]]'s とらつき. Vietnamese tháng Tị likewise uses the native word tháng ("month") plus the zodiac name, rather than a fully Sino-Vietnamese compound. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄜ·⼔ㄊ unique across both words and characters).
