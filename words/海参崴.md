## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [参](../characters/参.md) "to take part; ginseng" + [崴](../characters/崴.md) "bend (of a mountain or river)" — "sea-cucumber bay"; no stand-in relationship applies (海's own stand-in is [[海洋]], 参's is [[参加]]; 崴 is a 名専字, which appears only in this name). The alias 海參崴 is the traditional spelling, since 參 is an alias of 参.

海参崴 is Vladivostok, the port city on the Sea of Japan in the Russian Far East, near North Korea, and the administrative centre of Primorsky Krai. English Wiktionary gives no etymology (a request for one) and lists 符拉迪沃斯托克 as the synonym; that is not a word in the vault. The name is read as 海参 "sea cucumber" plus 崴 "bend", and the place was known to the Chinese as a trepang-gathering coast (from memory, not re-checked). English Wiktionary records the Mandarin as Hǎishēnwǎi in the mainland and Hǎishēnwēi in Taiwan, and notes that the name was borrowed into English as Haishenwai and into Russian as Хайшэньвэй.

The city stands beside Golden Horn Bay, which is [[金角湾]] in Dan'a'yo (the second sense of that word).

Mandarin and Cantonese hoi2 sam1 wai1 are as at English Wiktionary. Japanese ウラジオストク and Korean 블라디보스토크 are the ordinary transliterations of the Russian name (from memory, not re-checked), so `kwin` is false. The `vietnamese` field holds the compositional Hán-Việt reading hải sâm uy (each character as its page gives it; not attested for this name).

No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄧㄙㄚㄇ⺢ㄧ unique across words and characters, checked 2026-10-12). The 注音 needs no syllable-boundary dot.
