## Notes
- [適](../characters/適.md) "suitable, fitting" + [合](../characters/合%20(char).md) "join, fit" — "fit and join," to suit. Their own `stand_in` values: 適's is [[適宜]]; 合's is itself; none points here, so 適合 is an independent compound.

適合 is to suit or fit: to be suitable for something, or to match it. Mandarin shìhé, Cantonese sik1 hap6, Japanese てきごう and Korean 적합 all use it, and Vietnamese thích hợp does too. It is the "suit" or "fit" of suitability, where [[合]] is the physical fitting together.
