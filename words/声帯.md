## Notes
- [声](../characters/声.md) "voice" + [帯 (char)](../characters/帯%20(char).md) "band; cord"

声帯 are the vocal cords: the folds in the larynx that vibrate to make the voice. Wiktionary glosses Japanese せいたい as "vocal cords". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 성대 matches.

They sit in the [喉頭](喉頭.md), larynx. What they make is the voice, and the cry of pain they may utter is a [呻吟](呻吟.md), groan.
