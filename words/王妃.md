## Notes
- [王](characters/王%20(char).md) "king" + [妃](characters/妃.md) "emperor's wife, queen" — 妃's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 王's own is itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 妃 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

王妃 means queen.

Mandarin wángfēi, Cantonese wong4 fei1, Japanese おうひ (OU+HI), and Korean 왕비 were already correct and fully compositional. Fixed a real bug in `諺文`: 왕피 (using ㅍ) → 왕삐 (using ㅃ), matching 妃's own stored citation (羅馬字 "fi" → 諺文 삐) and the same f→ㅃ transliteration convention seen on [[膚]] (fǝ → 쁘). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with vương phi (王's own vương + 妃's own phi). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/empty `aliases: []`. No homophones (注音 ⺢ㄫㄈㄧ unique across both words and characters).
