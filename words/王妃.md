## Notes
- [王](../characters/王%20(char).md) "king" + [妃](../characters/妃.md) "emperor's wife, queen" — 妃's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 王's own is itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 妃 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

王妃 is a queen consort or princess consort. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 王妃 as "wife of a prince; princess consort; concubine of a king", Japanese as "a queen consort; the princess consort of an East Asian ...", Korean as "queen" and Vietnamese as "princess consort". The queen who reigns in her own right is the [女王](女王.md).
