## Notes
- [天](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven" + [皇](../characters/皇.md) "emperor" — no stand-in relationship applies (天 is its own stand-in; 皇's own is [[皇帝]]).

天皇 means Emperor of Japan (Tennō/Mikado) — literally "heaven's emperor."

Mandarin tiānhuáng, Cantonese tin1 wong4, and Korean 천황 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese てんのう is the real lexicalized term for the Japanese emperor (using ノウ, distinct from 皇's cited KOU/OU on'yomi — a genuine lexical form, not an error). Vietnamese Nhật hoàng ("Japan-emperor") is the standard native term. **Fixed**: `pos` was wrongly 固有名詞 — corrected to 名詞, matching the parallel title/office nouns [[教皇]] (pope), [[皇帝]] (emperor), and [[女皇]] (empress), none of which are treated as proper nouns despite denoting singular offices. Blanked empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄝㄋㄏ⺢ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
