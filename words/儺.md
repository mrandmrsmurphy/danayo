## Notes
- [儺](../characters/儺%20(char).md) "exorcism" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

儺 names the masked ritual procession performed to drive out plague demons and evil spirits — still living today as 儺戲/儺舞 (Nuo opera/dance) in parts of southern China, and genuinely ancient: 論語 10.14 records Confucius standing formally on his eastern steps "when the villagers performed the Nuo exorcism" (鄉人儺，朝服而立於阼階).

儺 fills what would otherwise be a permanently empty Dan'a'yo syllable slot (ㄋㄨㄛ/눗/nuo). Its modern Mandarin reading only superficially resembles Dan'a'yo's -uo final — that final actually derives from an unrelated Middle Chinese rhyme class (尤/侯-type), while 儺's own real ancestry is the open 歌 rhyme, which would organically land alongside [[那]] instead. See [[characters/儺 (char)|儺's character page]] for the full phonological rationale — this is a deliberate exception, not the character's organic reading.
