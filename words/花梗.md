## Notes
- [花](characters/花.md) "flower" + [梗](characters/梗.md) "stem" — stand-in for [[梗]], which cannot appear independently.

花梗 means "pedicel, flower stalk" — the small stem supporting an individual flower.

Mandarin huā gěng and Cantonese faa1 gang2 preserve the sense directly. Japanese かこう (kakō) is standard botanical vocabulary. Korean 화강 (hwagang) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese hoa cánh is the direct compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading (hoa 花 + cánh, 梗's Hán Việt reading, confirmed by its appearance in cát cánh 桔梗, the balloon-flower root); everyday Vietnamese botanical usage instead prefers the native cuống hoa for "flower stalk." 梗's other stored candidates split into two groups: cạnh and ngạnh are also genuine Hán Việt readings of 梗, for its separate "edge" and "barb, obstinate" senses respectively; cành, ngành, nhánh, nhành are legitimate native/Nôm doublets meaning "branch" (ngành most explicitly documented as tied to 梗), while gánh, ngáng, ngánh, chành are unrelated native words with no real connection to 梗.
