## Notes
- [釉](characters/釉.md) "glaze" + [薬 (char)](characters/薬%20(char).md) "medicine" — "glaze, enamel."

**Found and fixed a wrong-compound/incomplete reading bug across nearly every field**: `mandarin`/`cantonese`/`vietnamese` had each been filled with only 釉's own single-syllable reading, entirely omitting 薬's syllable (yòu→yòuyào, jau6/jau2→jau6 yeuk6, dứu→dứu dược); `korean` held a garbled multi-value string of unrelated native Korean words for "gloss/luster" (윤기, 광택), replaced with the honest compositional 유약. Japanese うわぐすり is 釉's own genuinely attested native compound reading (a real jukujikun for this exact word, alongside the technical on-reading ゆうやく) — kept, but **fixed a hidden zero-width space (U+200B) embedded inside it**. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 윳약 diverges from Sino-Korean 유약 entirely. No homophones (注音 ⼜ㄛ⼘ㄎ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 釉's own `stand_in` is this exact word (釉薬), so 釉 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 釉薬; 薬's own `stand_in` is instead 薬 itself, so this word does not legitimize 薬.
