## Notes
- [撇](../characters/撇%20(char).md) "throw away" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [撇](../characters/撇%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

撇 means to throw away, or the left-falling diagonal stroke in calligraphy (㇓).

Mandarin piē, Cantonese pit3, Japanese へつ, Korean 별, and Vietnamese phiết are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones at the word level (注音 ㄆㄝㄊ is shared only with the bound character 瞥, whose own stand-in is [[一瞥]]).
