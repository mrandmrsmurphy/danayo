## Notes
- [科](../characters/科.md) "branch; section" + [学](../characters/学.md) "learn" + [者](../characters/者.md) "-er"

科学者 is a scientist: one who works in a science. It is [科学](科学.md), "science", with 者 "-er", written out as a word. Wiktionary records Japanese かがくしゃ and Korean 과학자 with the same sense. Wiktionary has no Chinese entry, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings.

It stands beside [学者](学者.md), the scholar, and [研究者](研究者.md), the researcher, as one of the names for those who work with knowledge.
