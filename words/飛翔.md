## Notes
- [飛 (char)](../characters/飛%20(char).md) "fly" + [翔](../characters/翔.md) "soar"

飛翔 means to soar: to fly high, as a bird or an aircraft does, and high flight itself. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 飛翔 (an alias, 飞翔; Mandarin fēixiáng, Cantonese fei1 coeng4) as "to fly; to soar", Japanese ひしょう as "high flight; to soar, fly high", and lists Korean 비상 for the hanja form, where 비상 also means "emergency", an unrelated word.

This word is the viable form of [翔](../characters/翔.md): the character's meaning (to soar, glide) is identical, but 翔 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 飛 ("to fly") supplies the first syllable, grounding the compound in active flight. The plain word for flight is [[飛行]].
