## Notes
- [通 (char)](../characters/通%20(char).md) "pass through" + [勤](../characters/勤.md) "diligent; work" — commute.

通勤 is commuting: traveling regularly between home and work. It is "pass-through to work." The compound is a Japanese coinage (wasei kango) that Chinese then adopted, and it is now standard in Mandarin tōngqín, Cantonese tung1 kan4 and Japanese つうきん; Korean 통근 comes from memory. It completes the 勤 family with [[出勤]] (to go to work, clock in) and [[缺勤]] (to be absent from work).
