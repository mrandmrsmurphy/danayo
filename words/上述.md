## Notes
- [上 (char)](../characters/上%20(char).md) "above" + [述](../characters/述.md) "to state, to narrate" — literally "stated above," hence "aforementioned, above-mentioned," referring back to something already stated earlier in the same text.

This is a formal, written-register word rather than a spoken one across every language it appears in — used in reports, contracts, and academic writing to point back at prior content without repeating it (上述問題, "the aforementioned problem"; 上述理由, "the reasons stated above"). `述` itself is bound to compounds throughout this vault (陳述 "to state," 記述 "to describe," 論述 "to discuss/expound") with no standalone use, reinforcing this word's own formal, textual character. Vietnamese thượng thuật (compositional, from 上's own well-attested thượng and 述's own thuật, as in tường thuật "to narrate/report") is likewise confined to formal written Vietnamese. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 샹숟 diverges from Sino-Korean 상술 in both syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼘ㄫㄙㄨㄊ unique among both words and characters).
