## Notes
- [独](../characters/独.md) "alone; sole" + [特](../characters/特.md) "special"

独特 is unique or distinctive: peculiar to one thing and unlike any other. Wiktionary glosses Japanese どくとく as "unique, peculiar (to someone); inimitable". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 독특 and the Cantonese duk6 dak6 match the character readings.

It describes a style, a flavor or a manner. It differs from [唯一](唯一.md), only, which counts one of a kind, and from [特殊](特殊.md), special, which marks a thing as an exception to the usual.
