## Notes
- [鵉](../characters/鵉.md) "luan bird" + [鳳](../characters/鳳.md) "phoenix" — legitimizing note: 鵉's own `stand_in` is [[鵉鳳]] itself, already annotated in its own citation; 鳳's own `stand_in` is [[鳳凰]], so transitivity fails — no `#cranberry`.

Mandarin luánfèng, Cantonese lyun4fung6, Japanese らんほう, Korean 난봉, and Sino-Vietnamese loan phượng are all standard, fully compositional readings, pairing the mythical luan bird with the phoenix as a poetic figure for an exceptional or auspicious pairing.
