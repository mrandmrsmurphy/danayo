## Notes
- [孟](../characters/孟.md) "first, great" + [加](../characters/加.md) "increase" + [拉 (char)](../characters/拉%20(char).md) "pull" — phonetic rendering of *Baṅgāla*

孟加拉 is a phonetic rendering of *Baṅgāla*, the name of the Bengal region, and of the modern state of Bangladesh. English Wiktionary lists the Chinese word (Mandarin Mèngjiālā, Cantonese maang6 gaa1 laai1) first as the region of Bengal in South Asia, and second, chiefly in Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau, Singapore and Malaysia, as Bangladesh; the fuller 孟加拉國 is the form used in mainland China, Hong Kong and Singapore. In Hong Kong Cantonese it is also slang for "fool; idiot", and the name of a drinking game. The archaic Chinese name for Bengal was 榜葛剌 (Bǎnggélà).

For Japanese ベンガル Wiktionary gives "Bengal", and for Korean it lists the country as 방글라데시, so the Japanese and Korean fields here, ベンガル and 벵골, give the name of the region, Bengal, not the country. English Wikipedia says Bengal is named after the ancient Vanga Kingdom, that the province's first partition was attempted between 1905 and 1911, and that Bangladesh became a sovereign nation on 16 December 1971.
