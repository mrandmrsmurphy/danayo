## Notes
- [西](../characters/西.md) "west" is separately legitimized by [[西方]]. [班 (char)](../characters/班%20(char).md) "group, party, section" is separately legitimized by [[班]] itself. [牙](../characters/牙.md) "fang" is separately legitimized by [[長牙]]. No cranberry.

As a proper place name, japanese スペイン (phonetic loan) and korean 에스파냐 (an older but still-used official phonetic transliteration) both legitimately hold the real attested name rather than a compositional gloss. Vietnamese Tây Ban Nha is a genuine historical transliteration, semi-compositional in the same character order.
