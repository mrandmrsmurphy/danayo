## Notes
- [幽 (char)](../characters/幽%20(char).md) "quiet; secluded; dark" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [幽 (char)](../characters/幽%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

幽 names quiet seclusion — a dim, hidden, tranquil place removed from the world (幽玄 "profound and mysterious," a key aesthetic concept in Japanese poetics; 幽霊 "ghost, spirit of the unseen world").

Japanese ゆう, Korean 유, and Vietnamese u are all standard readings. **A genuine triple Dan'a'yo homophone**: 幽, [[猶]], and [[由]] all share the identical reading ('yuo/윳/⼜ㄛ) — added reciprocal homophone callouts to all three pages (none had ever carried one before).
