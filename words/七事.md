## Notes
- [七 (char)](../characters/七%20(char).md) "seven" + [事 (char)](../characters/事%20(char).md) "thing; action" — no `#cranberry`: both 七's and 事's own `stand_in` point to themselves, neither to this word.
- (historical) the Seven Duties of a sovereign: offering sacrifice, giving audience, administering jointly, receiving guests, running the army, attending to farm work, and doing mourning ceremonies

Mandarin qīshì, Cantonese cat1 si6, Japanese しちじ, Korean 칠사, and Sino-Vietnamese thất sự are all plain compositional readings for this classical/historical term. `kwin` is false (AND-rule: both 七 and 事 individually false).
