## Notes
- [鸚](../characters/鸚.md) "parrot" + [哥](../characters/哥.md) "older brother" — parakeet, parrot; a synonym alongside the more standard [[鸚鵡]]. Attested in Mandarin, Cantonese, and Korean (앵가) — no `#cranberry`: 鸚's own `stand_in` is [[鸚鵡]], 哥's is [[哥哥]]; neither points here.

Sino-Vietnamese anh ca is the compositional form; everyday Vietnamese instead uses the native word vẹt.
