## Notes
- [美 (char)](../characters/美%20(char).md) "beautiful, pretty" + [国](../characters/国.md) "country" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (美's is [[美]] itself, 国's is [[国家]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 国 is individually `kwin: false`.

美国 names the United States of America — 美 here transliterates "-mer-" in Měiguó, not a claim about literal beauty.

Mandarin Měiguó, Cantonese mei5 gwok3, and Korean 미국 (miguk) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 美国 is itself the common attested name for the U.S.A. in all three. Japanese uses べいこく (beikoku), built on the alias form 米国 (which the alias list already records) rather than 美国 directly. Vietnamese Mỹ is the standard, common Vietnamese name. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
