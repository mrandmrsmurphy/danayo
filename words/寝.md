## Notes
- [寝 (char)](../characters/寝%20(char).md) "sleep; lie down; rest" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [寝 (char)](../characters/寝%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

寝 covers the act of lying down to sleep or rest, and by extension the bedroom or sleeping quarters itself (寝室 "bedroom," 寝台 "berth, bunk") — historically also extended to an emperor's tomb-chamber, the ultimate resting place. The character's own graphemic root combines semantic 宀 ("roof") and 爿 ("bed," a stylized bed-frame pictograph also seen in [[床]] and [[牀]]) with phonetic [[侵]] — this vault's own choice of a real, attested near-homophone standing in for Wiktionary's archaic and pageless original phonetic 𠬶.

Vietnamese tẩm is a real, if comparatively literary, Sino-Vietnamese reading — it survives mainly in compounds like tẩm cung (寝宮, "the emperor's private chambers") rather than everyday speech, which uses native ngủ ("to sleep") instead. **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 寝 and [[浸]] ("immerse, dunk") share an identical reading (cim/침/ㄑㄧㄇ) despite being semantically unrelated — 浸's own phonetic root is the unlinked 𡩠, an independent *sʰim near-homophone rather than any real derivation from 侵 or 寝, so the collision is coincidental rather than a shared etymology.
