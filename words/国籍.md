## Notes
- [国](../characters/国.md) "country" + [籍](../characters/籍.md) "registry" — literally "country-registry," nationality/citizenship.

Mandarin guójí, Cantonese gwok3 zik6, Japanese こくせき (kokuseki), and Korean 국적 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 国's and 籍's own stored citations exactly. **Found and fixed two real bugs**: `cantonese` was missing a space ("gwok3zik6"), and `vietnamese` was stored as "quốc ca" — which actually means "national anthem," an unrelated word — corrected to "quốc tịch," the real, standard Vietnamese word for nationality, matching 国's own quốc + 籍's own tịch.

`kwin` is false: 国 alone diverges from Korean on its own character page, which alone is enough to make the AND-rule false; 籍 is independently kwin-true. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄎㄐㄝㄎ unique among both words and characters).
