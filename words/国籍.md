## Notes
- [国](../characters/国.md) "country" + [籍](../characters/籍.md) "registry" — literally "country-registry," nationality/citizenship.

Mandarin guójí, Cantonese gwok3 zik6, Japanese こくせき (kokuseki), and Korean 국적 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 国's and 籍's own stored citations. Vietnamese quốc tịch is likewise compositional, built from 国's quốc and 籍's tịch. 籍 originally meant a register or census roll, so the compound frames nationality as one's entry on the state's roll.
