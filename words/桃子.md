## Notes
- [桃](../characters/桃.md) "peach" + [子](../characters/子.md) "nominalizing suffix" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [桃](../characters/桃.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 子's own stand-in is [[児子]].

桃子 means peach.

Mandarin táozi, Cantonese tou4 zi2, Japanese とうし, Korean 도자, and Vietnamese đào are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 桃's and 子's own stored citations exactly). No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄨㄐㄜ unique across both words and characters).

Dan'a'yo keeps the shared CJKV sense: a peach, the fruit. It is placed on the Food page under Fruits & Produce with the other 子-suffixed fruit names. The separate word [[桃果]] also means peach; this page does not decide between them.
