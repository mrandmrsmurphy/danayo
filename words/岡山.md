## Notes
- [岡](../characters/岡.md) "hill" + [山](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain"

岡山 is Okayama, written "hill mountain", and the name is that of the castle town founded on the hill. The reading おかやま is native: 岡 (おか, "hill") plus 山 (やま, "mountain").

Okayama is a mild Seto Inland Sea prefecture, with its capital Okayama, home of the garden Kōraku-en and the black "crow" castle. It also includes the canal quarter of Kurashiki, and it is the setting of the Momotarō folktale.

In Dan'a'yo 岡山 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 오카야마 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
