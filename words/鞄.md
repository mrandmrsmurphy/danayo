## Notes
- [鞄](../characters/鞄%20(char).md) "bag, luggage" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [鞄](../characters/鞄%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

鞄 means bag or luggage.

Mandarin páo, Cantonese pok3, Korean 포, and Vietnamese bào are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native かばん (kaban) rather than the Sino-Japanese on-reading (HOU/BYOU) — the standard, everyday word for "bag" in Japanese. Homophone of [[拍]] "tap; clap; beat; pulse" (both 注音 ㄆㄚㄎ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words.
