## Notes
- [嘗](../characters/嘗.md) "taste, experience" + [試](../characters/試.md) "test, try" — "to try, attempt" (to try something out), not "experience, taste" as literally suggested by 嘗 alone. **Gloss bug found and fixed**: `english` was "experience, taste," mismatching the actual compound meaning — corrected to "try, attempt." **Real content bug found and fixed**: `korean` was stored as "시도," which is actually the unrelated word 試圖 ("attempt, plan to") — corrected to 상시, the proper compositional Sino-Korean reading of this word's own characters 嘗+試. `vietnamese: thường thử` is compositional; everyday Vietnamese instead just uses the native verb thử alone (itself one of 試's own listed readings) for "to try" (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 샹시 diverges from the now-corrected Sino-Korean 상시 in the first syllable.
