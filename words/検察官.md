## Notes
- [検](../characters/検.md) "examine; check" + [察](../characters/察.md) "examine; investigate" + [官](../characters/官.md) "official"

検察官 is the public prosecutor: the official who investigates a crime and brings the charge on behalf of the state. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin jiǎncháguān as "public prosecutor or procurator; the prosecution (in a court case)", and lists Korean 검찰관 with the same sense. Japanese けんさつかん is the standard Japanese title.

The prosecutor is the one who makes the [起訴](起訴.md), the formal charge, and stands opposite the [被告](被告.md) before the [裁判官](裁判官.md).
