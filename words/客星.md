## Notes
- [客](../characters/客.md) "guest" + [星 (char)](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star"

客星 is a guest star: in the old Chinese astronomical records, a star that appeared where none had been seen before, then faded. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin kèxīng as "guest star, a nova or supernova recorded in Chinese astronomy". Cantonese haak3 sing1 matches, Korean 객성 is the reading of the characters, and no Japanese reading is attested.

These records are among the oldest observations of what are now called the [新星](新星.md), nova, and the [超新星](超新星.md), supernova.
