## Notes
- [旋](../characters/旋.md) "revolve, turn" + [転](../characters/転.md) "turn, change" — stand-in for [旋](../characters/旋.md), which cannot appear independently, pairing two near-synonymous "turn" roots.

旋転/旋轉 means to revolve, rotate, or spin — a close near-synonym of [回転](回転.md) (also "rotate, spin," built on the same 転), the two overlapping heavily in meaning, though 旋 leans toward circular/whirling motion (as in [螺旋](螺旋.md), "spiral, helix") while 回 leans toward returning/circling back to a starting point.

Mandarin xuánzhuǎn, Cantonese syun4 zyun2, and Japanese せんてん (senten) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 旋's and 転's own stored citations. Korean 선전 (seonjeon) is from 旋's 선 and 転's 전, not borrowed from the near-synonym 回転's 회전. Vietnamese toàn chuyển is likewise compositional, from 旋's toàn (one of five attested readings) and 転's own chuyển, and is directly attested as a real term for "to rotate, spin."
