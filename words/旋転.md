## Notes
- [旋](../characters/旋.md) "revolve, turn" + [転](../characters/転.md) "turn, change" — this word is the stand-in compound that legitimizes the bound character 旋 (per its own stored `stand_in`), pairing two near-synonymous "turn" roots.

旋転/旋轉 means to revolve, rotate, or spin — a close near-synonym of [[回転]] (also "rotate, spin," built on the same 転), the two overlapping heavily in meaning, though 旋 leans toward circular/whirling motion (as in [[螺旋]], "spiral, helix") while 回 leans toward returning/circling back to a starting point.

Mandarin xuánzhuǎn, Cantonese syun4 zyun2, and Japanese せんてん (senten) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 旋's and 転's own stored citations. **Real bug found and fixed 2026-09-22**: `korean` had been borrowed from the near-synonym [[回転]]'s own reading (회전, hoejeon) rather than 旋転's own compositional reading — corrected to 선전 (seonjeon), from 旋's own 선 and 転's own 전. Vietnamese toàn chuyển is likewise compositional, from 旋's toàn (one of five attested readings) and 転's own chuyển, and is directly attested as a real term for "to rotate, spin." `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 숸줜 diverges from Sino-Korean 선전 in both syllables. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄙ⼔ㄋㄐ⼔ㄋ unique among both words and characters).
