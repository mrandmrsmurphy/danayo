## Notes
- [放](../characters/放.md) "release" + [置](../characters/置%20(char).md) "put, place" — no stand-in relationship applies (放's own stand-in is [[釈放]]; 置's own is itself).

放置 means to put aside, abandon, or shelve.

Mandarin fàngzhì, Cantonese fong3 zi3, Japanese ほうち, Korean 방치, and Vietnamese phóng trí are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄈㄚㄫㄑㄧ unique across both words and characters).
