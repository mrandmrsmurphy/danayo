## Notes
- [四 (char)](characters/四%20(char).md) "four" + [捨 (char)](characters/捨%20(char).md) "discard" + [五 (char)](characters/五%20(char).md) "five" + [入 (char)](characters/入%20(char).md) "enter" — literally "discard four, enter five": the rounding rule that drops a digit below 5 and rounds up a digit of 5 or above.

Mandarin sìshěwǔrù, Cantonese sei3 se2 ng5 jap6, Japanese ししゃごにゅう, Korean 사사오입, and Vietnamese tứ xả ngũ nhập are all directly attested, fully compositional readings — the same four characters name this rounding rule across all five languages. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 싀샤오닙 diverges from Sino-Korean 사사오입.
