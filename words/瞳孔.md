## Notes
- [瞳](../characters/瞳.md) "pupil" + [孔 (char)](../characters/孔%20(char).md) "cavity, opening" — 瞳's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 孔's own is [[孔]] itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 瞳 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word. Synonymous with [[眼睛]].

瞳孔 is the precise anatomical/medical term for the pupil — the opening in the iris through which light enters the eye. Mandarin tóngkǒng, Cantonese tung4 hung2, Japanese どうこう (dōkō), and Korean 동공 (donggong) are all fully compositional and are the standard medical terms in their respective languages.

Vietnamese diverges structurally: the everyday medical term is đồng tử, built from 瞳 + 子 ("child, seed") rather than 瞳 + 孔; đồng khổng (the compositional form matching this word's own characters) is comparatively rare, with native con ngươi also common in everyday speech.
