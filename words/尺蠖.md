## Notes
- [尺 (char)](../characters/尺%20(char).md) "unit of length (chi)" + [蠖](../characters/蠖.md) "inchworm" — this word is the stand-in for [蠖](../characters/蠖.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

尺蠖 is the inchworm/looper caterpillar, named for its distinctive measuring-worm gait — it moves by drawing its hind end up to its front end and then extending forward, as if measuring out distances with a 尺 (a traditional unit of length, roughly a Chinese "foot"). The classical alternate spelling 蚇蠖 (using the rarer 蚇, a character essentially reserved for this one word) is also attested, though 尺's own vault page does not register 蚇 as a formal alias.

Japanese しゃっかく, Korean 척확, and Vietnamese xích oách are all standard, non-drifted readings. No HSK level is given for the compound. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄝㄎ·⺢ㄎ unique across both words and characters).
