## Notes
- [咸](../characters/咸.md) "all, complete" + [味](../characters/味.md) "taste" — "salt taste," salty. Their own `stand_in` values: 咸's is [[名専字]]; 味's is [[味覚]]; none points here, so 咸味 is an independent compound.

咸味 is salty: the taste of salt, and the quality of being salty. Mandarin xiánwèi (鹹味) and Cantonese haam4 mei6 are the ordinary words. Japanese says 塩辛い and Korean 짜다, so those two fields hold the character readings. The character 咸, once only the name-only character of 咸興 and 咸鏡, has the alias 鹹, the Chinese character for "salty," and this word is now its stand-in. Compare [[苦]] (bitter), [[甘]] (sweet) and [[酸]] (sour).
