## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [洋](../characters/洋.md) "ocean; sea" — stand-in for [[海]], which cannot appear independently, smaller in scope than the larger [[大洋]] ("ocean," lit. "great 洋")

海洋 means the sea or ocean in the formal, scientific sense: the great body of salt water covering the earth (海洋學/hải dương học, "oceanography"; 海洋生物, "marine life"; 海洋氣候, "maritime climate"; 太平洋, "Pacific Ocean"). It names the sea as a domain, and the Vietnamese form is also the name of a real coastal province, Hải Dương.

Mandarin hǎiyáng, Cantonese hoi2 joeng4, Japanese かいよう (kaiyō), Korean 해양 (haeyang), and Vietnamese hải dương are all the standard formal or scientific word for "ocean, marine". Each language has a more everyday native word for the sea in casual speech: Japanese 海 (うみ), Korean 바다.
