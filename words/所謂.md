## Notes
- [所](../characters/所%20(char).md) "-ee (nominalizer)" + [謂](../characters/謂.md) "to call, to say" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [謂](../characters/謂.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

所謂 means "so-called."

Mandarin suǒwèi, Cantonese so2 wai6, and Korean 소위 are standard, fully compositional readings; `vietnamese: sở vị` is directly attested via hvdic. Japanese uses the native idiom いわゆる (iwayuru) rather than a Sino-Japanese compositional reading — the standard, expected word for "so-called" in Japanese. Korean also has a native equivalent 이른바 alongside 소위, used interchangeably. Fixed real bug: `諺文`/`羅馬字` had been using the free-standing word 所's register (쇼/syo) instead of the bound-character citation (스/sǝ, per `characters/所 (char).md`) used consistently in 所+X compounds like [[厠所]]/[[居所]]/[[所属]] — corrected to 스위/sǝwi, matching the 注音 (ㄙㄜㄨㄧ) that was already correct — closing out the systemic bug found across [[所在]]/[[所有]]/[[所以]]. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄜㄨㄧ unique across both words and characters).
