## Notes
- [荷](../characters/荷.md) "luggage" is separately legitimized by [[荷物]]. [担](../characters/担.md) "shoulder, carry, bear" is legitimized by this exact compound (its own `stand_in`) — transitivity fails, no cranberry.

Mandarin hèdān correctly uses 荷's alternate verbal tone (hè, "to carry," vs hé, "lotus") — a real, well-attested Mandarin tone alternation, not a bug. Japanese かたん (KA+TAN) and korean 하담 already correct and compositional.
