## Notes
- [擱](../characters/擱.md) "put down; stop" + [筆 (char)](../characters/筆%20(char).md) "pen; brush" — this word is the viable form of [擱](../characters/擱.md): the character's meaning (to stop writing) is identical, but 擱 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. The compound specifies what is being put down — the pen.
- Classical closing formula used at the end of letters and essays to signal the author is setting down the brush.

Mandarin gēbǐ, Cantonese gok3 bat1, Korean 각필 (gakpil), and Japanese かくひつ (kakuhitsu) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 擱's and 筆's own stored citations. Vietnamese các bút is likewise compositional, from 擱's các and 筆's own bút. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 각푿 diverges from Sino-Korean 각필 in the second syllable's coda. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄎㄆㄨㄊ unique among both words and characters).
