## Notes
- [麗](../characters/麗.md) "lovely, beautiful" + [水](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" — the name of Yeosu, a port city in the south of South Korea.

麗水 (Yeosu) is a port city of South Jeolla Province ([[全羅]]南道) in South Korea, on the south coast, and hosted the Expo of 2012 (from memory, not re-checked). The name reads "lovely waters".

English Wiktionary lists 麗水 first as Lishui, a prefecture-level city in Zhejiang, China (Mandarin Líshuǐ or Lìshuǐ), and second as the Korean city (Mandarin Lìshuǐ, Cantonese lai6 seoi2); Chinese and Korean Wiktionary also have the string, the Korean entry a hanja form of 여수. Korean 려수 is the North Korean reading, with 려 kept at the start of the syllable after 麗; the usual South Korean spelling is 여수. The Japanese reading れいすい is composed from the on'yomi. Vietnamese Lệ Thủy is the name the Vietnamese Wikipedia gives for the Chinese Lishui.
