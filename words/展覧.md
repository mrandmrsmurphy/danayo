## Notes
- [展](../characters/展.md) "expand, unfold" + [覧](../characters/覧.md) "look at, perceive, peruse" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (展's is 伸展, 覧's is 観覧).

Mandarin zhǎnlǎn, Cantonese zin2 laam5, Japanese てんらん, Korean 전람, and Vietnamese triển lãm are the standard readings, all meaning "exhibition; to put on display."
