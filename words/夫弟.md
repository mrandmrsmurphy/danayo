## Notes
- [夫 (char)](../characters/夫%20(char).md) "husband" + [弟 (char)](../characters/弟%20(char).md) "younger brother" — husband's younger brother.

夫弟 is the younger brother of one's husband. It is "husband-younger brother," the pair of [[夫兄]] and the husband-side counterpart of [[妻弟]]. No entry was found on English Wiktionary in any language, so the language fields are left blank; it is a schema word. The hypernym is [[姻弟]]. It is a schema word in the family of in-law terms built from the marriage relation and the sibling roots, coined for predictability rather than a claim about ancient usage.
