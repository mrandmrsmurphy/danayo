## Notes
- [羊](../characters/羊.md) "sheep, goat" + [駝](../characters/駝.md) "humpbacked" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (羊's is [[綿羊]], 駝's is [[駝背]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 駝 is individually `kwin: false`.

羊駝 names the alpaca — literally "humpbacked sheep," a South American animal unknown to the Sinosphere before contact with the Americas.

Mandarin yángtuó and Cantonese joeng4 to4 are standard, fully compositional readings, and 羊駝 is itself the common attested word for "alpaca" in both. Korean 알파카 (alpaka), Japanese アルパカ (arupaka), and Vietnamese alpaca instead directly transliterate the animal's Spanish/Quechua-derived English name, since the alpaca is a New World species with no traditional Sino-Korean/Sino-Japanese/Sino-Vietnamese term. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
