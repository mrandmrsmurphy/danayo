## Notes
- [連 (char)](../characters/連%20(char).md) "join; link" + [歌](../characters/歌.md) "song; poem"

連歌 is renga: linked verse, in which poets take turns to add lines to a poem. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin liángē as "renga" and Japanese れんが as "renga, a form of linked poetry where verses of 5-7-5 are linked together.". Korean 련가 is the reading of the characters. The Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the characters.

It grew from the [和歌](和歌.md), and its opening verse became the [俳句](俳句.md).
