## Notes
- [碩](../characters/碩.md) "big; large" + [士 (char)](../characters/士%20(char).md) "scholar"

碩士 is a master's degree, and the person who holds it. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin shuòshì as "master's degree; someone who has a master's degree" and, archaically, "learned person". Korean 석사 and Vietnamese thạc sĩ are the same word. Japanese uses 修士 (しゅうし) for the degree, so the Japanese field gives the Sino-Japanese reading of the characters, せきし.

It stands between [学士](学士.md), the bachelor's degree, and [博士](博士.md), the doctorate. Three of the four languages use this word, so it is the shared form.
