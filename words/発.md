## Notes
- [発](../characters/発%20(char).md) "issue forth, discharge" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 発 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

発 carries the sense of something being sent, shot, or issued forth from a source — a bow releasing an arrow originally, extended to anything that starts, emerges, or is released: departure, discovery, invention, discharge, emission. It is one of the most productive verbal prefixes/suffixes across the Sinosphere, forming an enormous family of compounds: 発展 ("develop"), 出発 ("depart"), 発明 ("invent"), 爆発 ("explode"), 発電 ("generate power"), 発見 ("discover").

Mandarin fā, Cantonese faat3, Japanese はつ (hatsu), Korean 발 (bal), and Vietnamese phát are all fully compositional readings, and the character's productivity as a bound morpheme is remarkably consistent across all five languages — none narrows or drifts the core "issue forth" sense the way many single characters do. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 빧 (fad) diverges from Korean 발 (bal) in its final consonant, matching the `kwin: false` already set on the character page.
