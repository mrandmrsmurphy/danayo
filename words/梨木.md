## Notes
- [梨](../characters/梨%20(char).md) "pear" + [木](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree, bush" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 梨 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

梨木 means pear tree.

Japanese なし is the genuine, standard native Japanese word for the pear tree, kept as-is. Fixed real bugs: `mandarin`/`cantonese` had both been copied from the unrelated compound 梨樹/梨树's own readings (líshù, lei4 syu6) rather than 梨木's own compositional forms (límù, lei4 muk6); `korean` was 배나무속, a native/scientific genus-level term, not the compositional Sino-Korean form — corrected to 리목; `vietnamese` was truncated to just "Lê" (missing 木's half entirely) — corrected to lê mộc. Removed `梨樹`/`梨树` from `aliases` — a different, unrelated compound built on the character 樹/树 ("tree"), a near-synonym rather than an orthographic variant of 梨木. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄧㄜㄇㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
