## Notes
- Etymologically, [新 (char)](../characters/新%20(char).md) "new" + [品 (char)](../characters/品%20(char).md) "products," but it remains decomposable into two independent words: <ruby>[新](../words/新.md)<rt>ㄙㄧㄋ</rt></ruby> + <ruby>[品](../words/品.md)<rt>ㄆㄨㄇ</rt></ruby>.

Mandarin xīnpǐn, Cantonese san1 ban2, Japanese しんぴん (shinpin), and Korean 신품 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 新's and 品's own stored citations exactly. **Found and fixed a real bug**: `korean` was stored as "신품, 새것," mixing the real compositional Sino-Korean word (신품) together with a native vernacular alternative (새것, "new thing," built from native 새 "new" + native 것 "thing") — the latter belongs in Notes prose, not the `korean` field.

`kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 신품 coincides exactly with Sino-Korean 신품, since both 新 and 品 independently carry matching readings on their own character pages. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄋㄆㄨㄇ unique among both words and characters).
