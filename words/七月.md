## Notes
- [七 (char)](../characters/七%20(char).md) "seven(th)" + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — no `#cranberry`: both 七's and 月's own `stand_in` point to themselves, neither to this word.

Mandarin qīyuè, Japanese しちがつ, and Korean 칠월 are standard compositional readings. Vietnamese tháng bảy ("seven month") is the real native month-name, following the same pattern as every other numbered month except [[一月]]'s special tháng giêng.
