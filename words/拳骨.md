## Notes
- [拳](../characters/拳.md) "fist" + [骨](../characters/骨%20(char).md) "bone" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [拳](../characters/拳.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (骨's own stand-in is itself).

拳骨 means fist (an alternate compound to [[拳頭]], with a bonier, knuckle-focused connotation).

Mandarin quángǔ, Cantonese kyun4 gwat1, Japanese げんこつ, Korean 권골, and Vietnamese quyền cốt are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bugs: `mandarin` and `cantonese` had both been contaminated with [[拳頭]]'s readings instead of this word's own compositional readings. Removed `拳頭` from `aliases` — it is a distinct compound (using 頭, not 骨), not an orthographic variant of this word. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼔ㄋㄍㄛㄊ unique across both words and characters).
