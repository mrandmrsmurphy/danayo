## Notes
- [碼 (char)](../characters/碼%20(char).md) "yard" — 碼 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo; functions as a classifier (量詞) for the yard unit of length, e.g. 一碼 ("one yard"). The character originally named a small counting stone or tally marker, later extended to numerals and codes generally (as in 密碼, "password/cipher code," and 條碼/条形码, "barcode") before being borrowed for the English "yard" via sound.

Mandarin mǎ, Cantonese maa5, Japanese ま (ma), Korean 마 (ma), and Vietnamese mã are all standard, fully compositional readings.
