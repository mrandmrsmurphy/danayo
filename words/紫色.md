## Notes
- [紫](../characters/紫.md) "purple, violet" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — stand-in for [紫](../characters/紫.md), which cannot appear independently (色 is separately legitimized by [色彩](色彩.md)).

Mandarin zǐsè, Cantonese zi2 sik1, Japanese むらさきいろ (murasaki-iro, native compound rather than a Sino-Japanese on'yomi reading), and Korean 자색 are all standard, directly attested readings matching 紫's and 色's own stored citations. `vietnamese: tử sắc` is compositional (紫's own Hán Việt tử + 色's own sắc), following the same X色→X sắc pattern already established on [緑色](緑色.md).

- See [Color](../lexipedia/Color.md).
