## Notes
- [紫](../characters/紫.md) "purple, violet" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 紫 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (色 is separately legitimized by [[色彩]]).

Mandarin zǐsè, Cantonese zi2 sik1, Japanese むらさきいろ (murasaki-iro, native compound rather than a Sino-Japanese on'yomi reading), and Korean 자색 are all standard, directly attested readings matching 紫's and 色's own stored citations. `vietnamese: tử sắc` is compositional (紫's own Hán Việt tử + 色's own sắc), following the same X色→X sắc pattern already established on [[緑色]]. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 저식 diverges from Sino-Korean 자색 in the first syllable, since 紫 individually diverges from Korean on its own character page.
