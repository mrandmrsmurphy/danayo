## Notes
- [照](../characters/照%20(char).md) "shine" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [照](../characters/照%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

照 means to shine.

Mandarin zhào, Cantonese ziu3, Japanese しょう, Korean 조, and Vietnamese chiếu are all standard, fully compositional readings. Three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all jou/좃/ㄐㄛㄨ) with [[昭]] ("shine; luminous") and [[焦]] ("burned; scorched") — completing the reciprocal callout.
