## Notes
- [忘](../characters/忘.md) "forget" + [却](../characters/却%20(char).md) "reject, retreat" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [忘](../characters/忘.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (却's own stand-in is itself).

忘却 means to forget — losing memory of something, often completely or deliberately.

Mandarin wàngquè, Cantonese mong4 koek3, Japanese ぼうきゃく, Korean 망각, and Vietnamese vong khước are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄚㄫㄎ⼘ㄎ unique across both words and characters).
