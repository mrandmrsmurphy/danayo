## Notes
- [帰](../characters/帰.md) "return; revert" + [順](../characters/順.md) "in order; smooth" — no stand-in relationship applies (帰's own `stand_in` is [[回帰]], 順's is [[順次]]).

帰順 names submission or surrender to authority — returning to compliance, often used historically of a defector or defeated party pledging allegiance to a new ruler. It carries a note of incomplete voluntariness, submission accepted under pressure rather than full agreement, distinct from a more forced 帰服/quy phục or fully voluntary compliance.

Japanese きじゅん, Korean 귀순, and Vietnamese quy thuận are all standard readings, common in historical/wartime contexts (dynastic transitions, defections). No homophones (注音 ㄍㄨㄧㄙ⼜ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
