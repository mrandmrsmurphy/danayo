## Notes
- [山 (char)](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain" + [岳](../characters/岳.md) "great mountain, peak" — this word is the stand-in for [岳](../characters/岳.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

山岳 is a near-synonym doubling, 山 and 岳 both independently meaning "mountain" — 岳 carries the more elevated, literary sense of a great peak (also the specific title of the Five Great Mountains of China, 五岳). Together the compound generalizes to "mountains" collectively, or mountainous terrain as a domain (山岳地帯 "mountainous region," 山岳部 "mountaineering club").

Japanese さんがく, Korean 산악, and Vietnamese sơn nhạc are all standard, non-drifted readings. No HSK level is given for the compound.

**Correction (2026-08-27)**: `注音` had a single-letter typo (ㄙㄚㄇ·ㄚㄎ → ㄙㄚㄋ·ㄚㄎ) — 山's own reading ends in ㄋ (n), not ㄇ (m); the `諺文`/`羅馬字` fields already correctly used the n-final form (산/san). No homophones after the correction (注音 ㄙㄚㄋ·ㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
