## Notes
- [銅 (char)](../characters/銅%20(char).md) "bronze" + [像](../characters/像.md) "image"

銅像 is a bronze statue: a figure cast in bronze. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin tóngxiàng as "bronze statue" and Japanese どうぞう as "bronze sculpture". Korean 동상 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese tung4 zoeng6 matches.

It is a [彫像](彫像.md), statue, in metal, and the stone kind is the [石像](石像.md).
