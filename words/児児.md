## Notes
- [児 (char)](../characters/児%20(char).md) "baby" doubled — an emphatic/diminutive reduplication of 児 itself, synonymous with the more standard [[嬰児]] and [[幼児]].

**Real content bug found and fixed**: every reading field had been filled with each language's own everyday equivalent word for "baby" instead of 児児's own compositional doubled reading — Mandarin bǎobèi (寶貝, "treasure/darling," different characters entirely), Japanese ベイビー (the English loanword "baby"), Korean 애기 (a native word), and Vietnamese em bé (a native phrase). None of these are built from 児児's own characters at all. Corrected all five fields to the honest compositional doubling of 児's own reading: mandarin érér, cantonese ji4 ji4, japanese じじ, korean 아아, vietnamese nhi nhi — none independently attested as real words for "baby" in their respective languages (this reduplication pattern appears to be a Dan'a'yo-internal construction, not a borrowed compound). Japanese じじ happens to coincide with the unrelated real word 爺 ("old man, grandpa") — a coincidental homograph, disclosed rather than avoided. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 에에 diverges from the compositional Sino-Korean 아아. No homophones (注音 ㄝㄧㄝㄧ unique among both words and characters).
