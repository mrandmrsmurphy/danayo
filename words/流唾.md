## Notes
- [流](../characters/流.md) "flow" + [唾](../characters/唾.md) "spit"

流唾 is to drool: to let saliva run out of the mouth. It is a Dan'a'yo coinage built on the pattern of the attested 垂涎 and 流涎 (Wiktionary: "to water at the mouth; to drool"), with 唾 in place of the character 涎, which has no vault page. The Mandarin and Cantonese readings are those of the characters, Korean 류타 is the reading of the characters, and no Japanese reading is attested.

The saliva is the [唾液](唾液.md), and the reverse act is the [呑咽](呑咽.md), swallowing. A drip from the nose is the [鼻水](鼻水.md).
