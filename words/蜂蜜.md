## Notes
- [蜂 (char)](../characters/蜂%20(char).md) "bee, wasp, hornet" + [蜜](../characters/蜜.md) "honey"

蜂蜜 "bee-honey" is honey itself, paralleling [[蜂巣]] "bee-nest" (beehive) — both are direct concatenations of [蜂 (char)](../characters/蜂%20(char).md) (fong/뽕/ㄈㄛㄫ) with their second component. The Mandarin and Cantonese readings (fēngmì, fung1 mat6) are transparent compounds of "bee" + "honey." The `japanese` field records the Sino-Japanese reading ほうみつ, while the everyday Japanese word is the native はちみつ (hachimitsu), and the Korean field records the Sino-Korean 봉밀 beside the everyday native word 꿀. The recorded alias 蜜蜂 reverses the character order and in ordinary Chinese means "honeybee" (the insect itself, not its product) — a common source of confusion since both orders are attested.

The Animals page lists the word as "honey," and Dan'a'yo keeps that sense, next to [蜂](蜂.md) "bee, wasp, hornet" and [蜂巣](蜂巣.md) "beehive." The Sino-Vietnamese reading is phong mật, and the page carries an `hsk_level` of 2.
