## Notes
- [諸](../characters/諸%20(char).md) "various" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

諸 means various, or all — historically a dependent pronoun/determiner ("many, all, various") that gradually displaced 多 in this function; it survives productively in compounds like [[諸子]] ("the various masters") and [[諸語]] ("the various languages").

Mandarin zhū, Cantonese zyu1, Japanese しょ, Korean 제, and Vietnamese chư are all standard, fully compositional readings. Part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read ja/자/ㄐㄚ) with [[姉]] ("elder sister") and [[借]] ("borrow").
