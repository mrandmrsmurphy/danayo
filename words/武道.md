## Notes
- [武 (char)](../characters/武%20(char).md) "military" + [道 (char)](../characters/道%20(char).md) "way"

The compound is 武 "military" + 道 "way", so the way of martial practice. Wiktionary gives Mandarin wǔdào as "martial arts; martial arts skills", with Cantonese mou5 dou6, Korean 무도 and Vietnamese võ đạo as the matching Sino-Xenic forms. In Japanese ぶどう (budō) names the Japanese martial arts as disciplines of self-cultivation, and gives 武道館 (Budokan) as a derived term.

In Dan'a'yo the word is the general term for martial arts as a way or discipline, as placed on the [Art](../lexipedia/Art.md) page, where specific arts such as [弓道](弓道.md) and [柔道](柔道.md) are each a particular 武道. It is related to [武術](武術.md) and [武芸](武芸.md) and to [武士道](武士道.md), the way of the warrior.
