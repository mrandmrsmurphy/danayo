## Notes
- [冤](../characters/冤.md) "grievance; injustice" + [罪 (char)](../characters/罪%20(char).md) "sin; crime"

冤罪 is a wrongful accusation or conviction: a charge laid on someone who is innocent. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yuānzuì as "miscarriage of justice" and Japanese えんざい as "miscarriage of justice; false charge". Cantonese jyun1 zeoi6 and Korean 원죄 match, and the alias 寃罪 is a variant spelling of 冤. The word is literally "wrongful crime".

The wrong lies in the process: a [無実](無実.md) person is found [有罪](有罪.md) by a mistaken [判決](判決.md), whether through [誣告](誣告.md), a false accusation, [偽証](偽証.md), false testimony, or plain error. The character 冤 is a name-only character (名専字) and appears here in a compound.
