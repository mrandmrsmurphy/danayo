## Notes
- [超 (char)](../characters/超%20(char).md) "super" + [新 (char)](../characters/新%20(char).md) "new" + [星 (char)](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star"

超新星 is a supernova: the explosion at the end of a massive star's life, briefly as bright as a whole galaxy. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin chāoxīnxīng and Japanese ちょうしんせい as "supernova". Vietnamese siêu tân tinh is the same word, and Korean 초신성 and Cantonese ciu1 san1 sing1 match.

It is a far greater event than a [新星](新星.md), nova. Records of such events in the old Chinese chronicles were called [客星](客星.md), guest stars.
