## Notes
- [警](../characters/警.md) "warn, alert" + [察](../characters/察.md) "examine, inspect" + [署](../characters/署.md) "office" — "police office," a police station. Their own `stand_in` values: 警's is [[警戒]]; 察's is [[考察]]; 署's is [[部署]]; none points here, so 警察署 is an independent compound.

警察署 is a police station: the local office of the police. Mandarin jǐngcháshǔ, Cantonese ging2 caat3 cyu5, Japanese けいさつしょ and Korean 경찰서 all use it, though Mandarin more often says 警察局. Compare [[警察]] (the police).
