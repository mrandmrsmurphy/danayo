## Notes
- [川 (char)](../characters/川%20(char).md) "river" + [埼](../characters/埼.md) "peninsula" — stand-in for [埼](../characters/埼.md), which cannot appear independently — literally "river-peninsula," the Japanese city of Kawasaki.

Mandarin Chuānqí, Cantonese cyun1 kei4, and Korean 천기 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 川's and 埼's own stored citations exactly. Japanese かわさき is the real form for this city name, read with kun'yomi; the usual written form 川崎 is stored in `aliases`. Vietnamese has no attested equivalent for the Japanese proper name.
