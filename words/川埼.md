## Notes
- [川 (char)](../characters/川%20(char).md) "river" + [埼](../characters/埼.md) "peninsula" (stand-in for 川埼) — literally "river-peninsula," the Japanese city of Kawasaki.

Mandarin Chuānqí, Cantonese cyun1 kei4, and Korean 천기 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 川's and 埼's own stored citations exactly. Japanese かわさき (Kawasaki, native kun'yomi reading) is the real form for this Japanese city name — read with kun'yomi rather than on'yomi, since Kawasaki is specifically a native-reading proper name (also commonly written 川崎, stored in `aliases`). Vietnamese has no attested equivalent for this specific Japanese proper name and is correctly omitted rather than left blank.

`kwin` is false: both 川 and 埼 individually diverge from Korean on their own character pages, so the AND-rule fails on both counts. No homophones (注音 ㄑ⺢ㄋㄎㄧㄜ unique among both words and characters).
