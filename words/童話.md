## Notes
- [童](../characters/童.md) "child" + [話 (char)](../characters/話%20(char).md) "tale"

童話 is a fairy tale or children's story: a made-up tale told to children. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin tónghuà as "children's story; fairy tale" and Japanese どうわ as "fairy tale". Korean 동화 and Vietnamese đồng thoại are the same word. Cantonese tung4 waa6-2 matches.

It is a short [小説](小説.md), told as a [故事](故事.md); the folk kind is the [民話](民話.md), and the kind with a moral is the [寓話](寓話.md).
