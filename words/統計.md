## Notes
- [統](../characters/統.md) "command, govern, unite" + [計](../characters/計.md) "measure, plan, count" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (統's is [[統率]], 計's is [[計画]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 計 is individually `kwin: false`.

統計 refers to the actual numbers and their presentation about something — statistics in the data sense, as distinct from the academic field of study (統計学, not yet a vault entry).

Mandarin tǒngjì, Cantonese tung2 gai3, Japanese とうけい (tōkei), and Korean 통계 (tonggye) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 統計 is itself a common attested word for "statistics" in all four languages. Vietnamese thống kê is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
