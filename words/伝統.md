## Notes
- [伝](../characters/伝.md) "transmit, propagate" + [統](../characters/統.md) "command, govern, unite" — "tradition, custom," that which is transmitted and holds a lineage together. Directly attested and standard across all five languages (mandarin chuántǒng, japanese でんとう, korean 전통, vietnamese truyền thống). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 줜통 diverges from Sino-Korean 전통 via the AND-rule, since 伝's own field independently diverges (despite 統 itself being `kwin: true`). No homophones (注音 ㄐ⼔ㄋㄊㄛㄫ unique among both words and characters).
