## Notes
- [伝](../characters/伝.md) "transmit, propagate" + [統](../characters/統.md) "command, govern, unite" — "tradition, custom," that which is transmitted and holds a lineage together. Directly attested and standard across all five languages (mandarin chuántǒng, japanese でんとう, korean 전통, vietnamese truyền thống).
