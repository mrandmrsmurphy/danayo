## Notes
- [九 (char)](../characters/九%20(char).md) "nine" + [天 (char)](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven, sky" — the Nine Heavens, a classical Chinese cosmological concept describing the sky as nine concentric or layered tiers, later absorbed into Daoist cosmology as the residence of the highest deities. The number nine itself carries auspicious/maximal significance throughout the classical numerology this vault documents elsewhere (九 as the largest single digit, associated with the emperor and ultimate yang). Vietnamese Cửu Thiên (compositional, from 九's own cửu and 天's own thiên, both extremely well-attested individually) directly parallels the same Daoist cosmological concept as it entered Vietnamese religious/literary tradition. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 큐턴 diverges from Sino-Korean 구천 in both syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄎ⼜ㄊㄝㄋ unique among both words and characters).
