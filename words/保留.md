## Notes
- [保](../characters/保.md) "defend" + [留 (char)](../characters/留%20(char).md) "remain, stay" — "to hold, suspend, defer" (意見保留, "to reserve one's opinion"; 保留意見, "to withhold judgment"). Mandarin bǎoliú, Cantonese bou2 lau4, Japanese ほりゅう, Korean 보류, and Vietnamese bảo lưu are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 밧류 diverges from Sino-Korean 보류 in the first syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄨㄌ⼜ unique among both words and characters).
