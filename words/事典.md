## Notes
- [事 (char)](../characters/事%20(char).md) "matter, affair" + [典](../characters/典.md) "canon, reference work" — a reference work organized around matters/topics/facts, as distinct from [[字典]]'s character-by-character organization.

事典 and 字典 are a genuine minimal pair in more than just Dan'a'yo reading — the distinction they encode (fact/topic-organized vs. character-organized reference works) is itself a real lexicographical one in Japanese, where both terms are in active everyday use side by side (国語辞典 vs. 百科事典, for instance). Vietnamese sự điển (confirmed via web search) matches these exact characters, though modern Vietnamese overwhelmingly prefers the unrelated compound bách khoa toàn thư ("encyclopedia of a hundred branches of knowledge") for this concept in ordinary usage. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 지던 diverges from Sino-Korean 사전 in the first syllable's consonant. No homophones beyond the confirmed [[字典]] pair.
