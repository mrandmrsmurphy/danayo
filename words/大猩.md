## Notes
- [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "large, great" + [猩](../characters/猩.md) "ape" — gorilla (*Gorilla gorilla* / *Gorilla beringei*); shortened from 大猩猩

Wiktionary has no entry for 大猩; it is a Dan'a'yo shortening of 大猩猩, which Wiktionary glosses in Chinese as "gorilla". Wikipedia: gorillas are large, primarily herbivorous great apes of equatorial Africa and the largest living primates; the genus *Gorilla* has two species, the eastern gorilla and the western gorilla.
