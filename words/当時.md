## Notes
- [当 (char)](../characters/当%20(char).md) "at, that (time)" + [時 (char)](../characters/時%20(char).md) "time" — literally "that time," then, back then, at that other time.

Mandarin dāngshí, Cantonese dong1 si4, Japanese とうじ (tōji), and Korean 당시 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 当's and 時's own stored citations exactly. **Found and fixed a real bug**: `korean` was stored as "당시, 그때," mixing the real compositional Sino-Korean word (당시) together with a native vernacular alternative (그때, "that time," built from native 그 "that" + native 때 "time") — the latter belongs in Notes prose, not the `korean` field, which holds only the Sino-Xenic bound reading. Corrected to 당시 alone.

`kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 당시 coincides exactly with Sino-Korean 당시, since both 当 and 時 independently carry matching readings on their own character pages. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄫㄙㄧ unique among both words and characters).
