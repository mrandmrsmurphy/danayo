## Notes
- [当 (char)](../characters/当%20(char).md) "at, that (time)" + [時 (char)](../characters/時%20(char).md) "time" — literally "that time," then, back then, at that other time.

Mandarin dāngshí, Cantonese dong1 si4, Japanese とうじ (tōji), and Korean 당시 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 当's and 時's own stored citations exactly.
