## Notes
- [万](../characters/万.md) "ten thousand" + [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "chariot, vehicle" — no `#cranberry`: both 万's and 乗's own `stand_in` point to themselves, neither to this word.
- Archaic term: a ruler commanding ten thousand war chariots — a classical Chinese expression for supreme imperial power.

Mandarin wànchéng, Japanese まんじょう, Korean 만승, and Sino-Vietnamese vạn thừa are all standard compositional readings for this classical term. `kwin` is false (AND-rule: both 万 and 乗 individually false). **Fixed two real bugs**: `mandarin` had a stray internal space (`wàn chéng`); `cantonese` held `mak6 sing4` — the same phonologically-implausible `mak6` typo for 万 already found and fixed on the character page itself — corrected to `maan6 sing4`.
