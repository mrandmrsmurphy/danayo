## Notes
- [今 (char)](../characters/今%20(char).md) "present" + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — "this month." Following the same pattern as [[今年]] ("this year"): `korean: 금월` is the real, formal/written Sino-Korean reading (as in 금월 말까지, "by the end of this month"), alongside the more common everyday native 이번달; `vietnamese: kim nguyệt` is the honest Sino-Vietnamese compositional reading, while the universal everyday phrase is native tháng này. `mandarin: jīnyuè`/`cantonese: gam1 jyut6` are directly compositional; 今月 does appear in Classical/literary and formal Chinese, though everyday colloquial Mandarin prefers 这个月/本月 instead. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 김웓 diverges from Sino-Korean 금월 via the AND-rule (both constituents independently false). No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄇ⼔ㄊ unique among both words and characters).
