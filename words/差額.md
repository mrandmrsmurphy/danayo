## Notes
- [差](../characters/差.md) "difference; discrepancy" + [額](../characters/額.md) "sum; amount" — no stand-in relationship applies (差's own `stand_in` is [[差別]], 額's is [[額頭]]). 差 here uses its neutral "difference, discrepancy" sense (the same sense underlying Vietnamese sai ngạch below), not the discrimination sense found in [[差別]].

差額 names a numerical difference or balance owed — the differential amount between two sums (a price difference, a balance still to be paid).

Japanese さがく, Korean 차액, and Vietnamese sai ngạch (verified) are all standard readings. No HSK level is given for the compound. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄚㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
