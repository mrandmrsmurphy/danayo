## Notes
- [陶](../characters/陶.md) "pottery" + [汰](../characters/汰.md) "scour" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes 汰 as a bound character (陶's own stand-in is [[陶器]]; transitivity fails, so no `#cranberry`).

陶汰 (more commonly written 淘汰) means to eliminate, weed out, or select against — most familiar from 自然淘汰/자연도태/chọn lọc tự nhiên (đào thải tự nhiên), "natural selection." Mandarin 淘汰 (táotài), Cantonese 淘汰 (tou4taai3), Japanese 淘汰 (とうた), Korean 도태 (dotae), and Sino-Vietnamese đào thải are all standard, everyday words for this concept, spanning biological, competitive, and economic senses alike (被淘汰, "to be eliminated/phased out").
