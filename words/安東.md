## Notes
- [安](../characters/安.md) "peaceful" + [東](../characters/東.md) "east" — the name of Andong, a city of South Korea in the east.

安東 (Andong) is a city of North Gyeongsang Province ([[慶尚]]北道) in South Korea, known for the Hahoe folk village and its traditional mask dance (from memory, not re-checked). The name reads "peaceful east".

English Wiktionary lists 安東 as a Chinese proper noun first for the Korean city (Mandarin Āndōng, Cantonese on1 dung1), then for a historical Chinese province in the north-east, as an old name of 丹東 (Dandong, on the Yalu opposite [[新義州]]), and as a transcription of the Russian male name Anton. The Korean reading 안동 is composed from the character pages (neither English nor Korean Wiktionary lists a Korean section for the string at this check); the Japanese reading あんとう is composed from the on'yomi. Vietnamese An Đông is composed from the character pages; the string An Đông on the Vietnamese Wikipedia is a ward of Ho Chi Minh City, unrelated.
