## Notes

校服 means school uniform.

Mandarin xiàofú, Cantonese haau6 fuk6, Japanese こうふく, and Korean 교복 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field, removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/`aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⼘ㄨㄅㄨㄎ unique across both words and characters).
