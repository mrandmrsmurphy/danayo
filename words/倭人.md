## Notes
- [倭](../characters/倭.md) "dwarf" + [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" — the archaic Chinese term for the Japanese people (as in the 3rd-century Chinese chronicle references to the 倭人, e.g. the Wei Zhi's account of the Wa/Yamatai). Mandarin wōrén, Cantonese wo1 jan4, Japanese わじん, and Korean 왜인 are all directly attested standard readings. `vietnamese: oa nhân` is the honest compositional reading (倭's own oa, the standard reading as in the old name Oa Quốc for Japan, + 人's own nhân); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 웨닌 diverges from Sino-Korean 왜인 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ⼔ㄧㄋㄧㄋ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 倭's own `stand_in` is this exact word (倭人), so 倭 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 倭人.
