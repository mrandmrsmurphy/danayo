## Notes
- [亜](../characters/亜.md) "Asia" + [洲 (char)](../characters/洲%20(char).md) "continent, landmass" — technically short for 亜細亜洲, distinctly geographic (the continent of Asia) as compared to the culturally-oriented [[亜細亜]].

Mandarin has two attested readings, Yàzhōu (the standard modern pronunciation) and Yǎzhōu (an older/dialectal variant), both kept. Cantonese aa3 zau1, Japanese あしゅう, Korean 아주, and Vietnamese Á châu are all standard, fully compositional readings.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 아줏 diverges from Sino-Korean 아주, since 亜's own kwin is true but 洲's own kwin is false, giving false overall.
