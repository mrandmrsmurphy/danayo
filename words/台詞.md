## Notes
- [台 (char)](../characters/台%20(char).md) "platform" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "words"

台詞 is lines: the words an actor speaks in a play or film. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin táicí as "lines" and Japanese せりふ as "a performer’s lines". Korean 대사 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese toi4 ci4 matches.

They are written in the [脚本](脚本.md), script, and spoken by an [俳優](俳優.md), actor, in a given [場面](場面.md), scene.
