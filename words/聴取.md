## Notes
- [聴 (char)](../characters/聴%20(char).md) "hear, listen" + [取](../characters/取.md) "take, gather" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (聴's is [[聴]] itself, 取's is [[取得]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 取 is individually `kwin: false`.

聴取 means to listen to and take in — especially opinions or testimony.

Mandarin tīngqǔ, Cantonese ting1 ceoi2, Japanese ちょうしゅ (chōshu), and Korean 청취 (cheongchwi) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 聴取 is itself a common attested word for "listen to, take in" in all four languages. **Fixed a real bug**: `cantonese` had teng1 ceoi2 (mismatching 聴's real ting1) — corrected. No Vietnamese reading is stored on 聴's own character page, so this word's vietnamese is left blank rather than guessed. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
