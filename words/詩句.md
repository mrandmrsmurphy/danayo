## Notes
- [詩](../characters/詩.md) "poem" + [句 (char)](../characters/句%20(char).md) "sentence; phrase"

詩句 is a line of verse, or a verse: a phrase or line taken from a poem. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin shījù as "verse; line of a poem". Korean 시구 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese si1 geoi3 matches.

It is a piece of a [詩歌](詩歌.md), poem, and the lines may be paired as a [対連](対連.md), couplet.
