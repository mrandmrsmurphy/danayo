## Notes
- [拉](../characters/拉%20(char).md) "drag, pull, play a bowed instrument" + [麺](../characters/麺%20(char).md) "flour" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 拉 and 麺's own stand-ins are themselves).

拉麺 means ramen — the hand-pulled (拉) wheat noodle (麺) dish.

Mandarin lāmiàn and Cantonese laai1 min6 are standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese ラーメン and Korean 라면 are the international loanword forms (written in katakana/hangul as borrowings rather than Sino-Japanese/Sino-Korean on-readings) — the standard, expected forms for this globally-spread dish name; Vietnamese ramen is likewise a direct loanword. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄚㄆㄇㄝㄋ unique across both words and characters).
