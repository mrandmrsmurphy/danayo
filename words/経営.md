## Notes
- [経 (char)](../characters/経%20(char).md) "classic, channel, warp" + [営](../characters/営.md) "operate, manage" — 営's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 経's own is [[経]] itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 経営 legitimizes 営 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 経 is individually `kwin: false`.

経営 means to run or manage — especially a business, as in 経営者 (manager, executive) and 経営学 (business administration).

Mandarin jīngyíng, Cantonese ging1 jing4, Japanese けいえい (keiei), and Korean 경영 (gyeongyeong) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 経営 is itself a common attested word for "manage, run" in all four languages. Vietnamese kinh doanh is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
