## Notes
- [黒 (char)](../characters/黒%20(char).md) "black" + [龍 (char)](../characters/龍%20(char).md) "dragon" + [江](../characters/江.md) "river" — "Black Dragon River". No stand-in relationship applies (黒 and 龍 are their own stand-ins; 江's is [[大江]]). 黑 is the alias of 黒, and 竜, 龙 are aliases of 龍, so the filename uses 黒 and 龍; 黑龍江, 黑龙江 and 黒竜江 are kept as the word's aliases.

The river comes first. 黒龍江 (Heilong Jiang, the Amur) is a long river of northeast Asia, forming for much of its length the border between China and Russia (from memory, not re-checked); English Wiktionary gives 阿穆爾河 as the Chinese synonym for its Russian name. English Wiktionary derives the Chinese name from the earlier 黑水 "Black Water", attested since the Tang, and compares Manchu and Mongolian names for it that also mean "black river".

Heilongjiang, the province, takes the river's name; it is the northernmost province of China, and its capital is [[呵爾浜]] (from memory, not re-checked). The Japanese form is the shinjitai 黒竜江, which English Wiktionary lists as the main entry (with 黑龍江 as its old-form spelling), read こくりゅうこう (on'yomi); it is the Japanese name for both the river and the province, though in Japanese the river is more often called the アムール川 (from memory, not re-checked). The Korean field holds the Sino-Korean 흑룡강, which English Wiktionary glosses as both the province and the river; the usual South Korean name for the province today is 헤이룽장 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Hắc Long Giang is the Sino-Vietnamese name, also glossed for both. The Cantonese field holds the first Jyutping on English Wiktionary (hak1 lung4 gong1); a variant with haak1 is also given. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄨㄎㄌ⼄ㄫㄍㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).

Attestation, checked 2026-10-11: English and Chinese Wiktionary (Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese sections) list the senses above.

1. The Amur River, on the border between China and Russia.
2. Heilongjiang, the province of China, named for the river.
