## Notes
- [介 (char)](../characters/介.md) "mediator, intervening" (stand-in: [[仲介]]) + [位 (char)](../characters/位.md) "position" (stand-in: [[定位]]) — the on-glide slot within a syllable's [[韻母]], between the nucleus and the onset.

介位 names the glide/medial position in 単亜語's syllable structure — the slot an on-glide (y, w) occupies, as laid out in `文法 - 02音韻論`'s syllable-structure breakdown (声母/韻母 → 介位・核位・尾位). Mandarin jièwèi, Cantonese gaai3 wai6, Japanese かいい, Korean 개위, and Vietnamese giới vị are all straightforward compositional readings from 介's and 位's own stored citations, not independently verified as standard terms in those languages. `kwin` is false: both 介 and 位 are individually false at the character level.

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot**: the concatenated string `ㄍ⼶⼔ㄧ` admits two legal partitions — the intended 介(ㄍ⼶)+位(⼔ㄧ), and a false alternate that splits 位's own syllable (ㄍ⼶+⼔+ㄧ) — so the dot at the true 介|位 boundary is required: ㄍ⼶·⼔ㄧ.
