## Notes
- [介 (char)](../characters/介.md) "mediator, intervening" (stand-in: [仲介](仲介.md)) + [位 (char)](../characters/位.md) "position" (stand-in: [定位](定位.md)) — the glide slot of the [韻母](韻母.md).

介位 is the glide position of a syllable, the first slot of the 韻母 in 単亜語. In [文法 - 02音韻論](../grammar/文法%20-%2002音韻論.md) the syllable is an onset, an optional glide (y, w), a nucleus vowel and an optional coda, and the 韻母 is divided into the 介位, the 核位 (nucleus) and the 尾位 (coda). The glide sound itself is the [介音](介音.md). English Wiktionary has no entry for 介位 in any language, so this is a term of this vault, and the Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese fields are built from the readings of the two characters.
