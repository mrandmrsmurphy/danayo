## Notes
- [舌 (char)](../characters/舌%20(char).md) "tongue, clapper" is separately legitimized by [[舌]] itself. [根 (char)](../characters/根%20(char).md) "root, foundation" is separately legitimized by [[根]] itself. No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

舌根 combines 舌 ("tongue") with 根 ("root") — "root of the tongue," and by extension the "taste faculty" (one of the six sense bases in Buddhist philosophy).

**Found and fixed two real bugs**: `羅馬字`/`諺文` had sedgan/섣간 (a vowels mismatches on 根's own syllable, ǝ→a), while `注音` (ㄙㄝㄊㄍㄜㄋ) had already stayed correct — corrected to sedgǝn/섣근. `cantonese` had sit6, mismatching 舌's own sit3 (a tone error) — corrected to sit3gan1. Japanese ぜっこん (zekkon, with gemination), korean 설근 (seolgeun), and vietnamese thiệt căn were all already correct, fully compositional readings.
