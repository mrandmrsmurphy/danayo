## Notes
- [数](../characters/数.md) "count; number" + [学](../characters/学.md) "learn" + [者](../characters/者.md) "-er"

数学者 is a mathematician: one who works in [数学](数学.md), mathematics. Wiktionary lists Korean 수학자 as the matching form. Wiktionary has no Chinese or Japanese entry, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings, and Japanese すうがくしゃ is the Sino-Japanese reading.

It is a field-specific partner of [科学者](科学者.md), the scientist, and [哲学者](哲学者.md), the philosopher.
