## Notes
- [明 (char)](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright, clear" + [度](../characters/度.md) "degree, extent" — lightness.

明度 is lightness: how light or dark a color is, from black through to white, regardless of hue. It is one of the three attributes of color alongside [[色相]] (hue) and [[彩度]] (saturation). Mandarin míngdù, Cantonese ming4 dou6, Japanese めいど and Korean 명도 are the standard readings. It is the color-science sense of brightness, distinct from the physical brightness of a light source, which belongs to the Light domain.
