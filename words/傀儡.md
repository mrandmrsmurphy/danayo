## Notes
- [傀](../characters/傀.md) "puppet" + [儡](../characters/儡.md) "puppet, dummy" — "puppet" (literal, a manipulated figure, and figuratively, a person or regime controlled by another: 傀儡政府, "puppet government"). Mandarin kuǐlěi, Cantonese faai3 leoi5, and Vietnamese quỷ lỗi are all directly attested Sino-xenic readings; Japanese instead uses the native くぐつ (kugutsu, "puppet," historically also referring to itinerant puppeteer-performers), and Korean 괴뢰 (goeroe) is the Sino-Korean reading, though the everyday Korean words for "puppet" are the native 꼭두각시 (kkokdugaksi) and 허수아비 (heosuabi, literally "scarecrow," used figuratively for a puppet figurehead). This word is the `stand_in` legitimizer for both 傀 and 儡 (transitivity holds: A=B=AB, confirmed via both character pages' own `stand_in` fields), hence the `#cranberry` tag. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 쾨뢰 diverges from Sino-Korean 괴뢰 in the first syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄛㄧㄌㄛㄧ unique among both words and characters).
