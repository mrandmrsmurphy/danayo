## Notes
- [巴 (char)](../characters/巴%20(char).md) "tomoe" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [巴 (char)](../characters/巴%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

巴 names the tomoe, the comma-shaped swirl motif common in East Asian heraldry and design (as in the swastika-like 三つ巴 mon crests of Japan) — the character's own oracle-bone origin is uncertain, but its modern shape has long been associated with this swirling form (its native Japanese kun'yomi is literally うずまき, "swirl/spiral"). It also serves phonetically as the transliteration syllable "ba" in loanwords like [[巴基斯坦]] (Pakistan).

Japanese は, Korean 파, and Vietnamese ba are all standard readings. No homophones among other words (注音 ㄆㄚ unique).
