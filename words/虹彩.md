## Notes
- [虹](../characters/虹.md) "rainbow" + [彩](../characters/彩.md) "vibrant, multicolored" — literally "rainbow-color," naming the eye's iris after its ring of color.

Mandarin hóngcǎi, Cantonese hung4 coi2, Korean 홍채 (hongchae), and Japanese こうさい (kōsai) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real everyday anatomical term for "iris" in its language. Vietnamese hồng thải is likewise compositional, from 虹's hồng (the more common of its two attested readings, alongside hống) and one of 彩's four attested readings, thải. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 홍채 matches Sino-Korean 홍채 exactly. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄏㄛㄫㄑㄚㄧ unique among both words and characters).
