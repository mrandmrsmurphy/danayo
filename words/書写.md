## Notes
- [書](../characters/書.md) "write" + [写](../characters/写.md) "copy, transcribe" — "write out." Their own `stand_in` values: 書's is [[書本]]; 写's is [[複写]]; none points here, so 書写 is an independent compound.

書写 is to write: to put words on paper or another surface. Mandarin shūxiě (書寫) and Cantonese syu1 se2 use it as an ordinary verb; Japanese しょしゃ means writing in the sense of penmanship or transcription, and Korean 서사 is the same word. Compare [[読書]] (to read) and [[筆]] (pen).
