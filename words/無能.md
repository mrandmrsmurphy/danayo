## Notes
- [無 (char)](../characters/無%20(char).md) "lack (stand-in: itself)" + [能](../characters/能.md) "ability (stand-in: [[技能]])"

Wiktionary: Chinese 無能 "incompetent; lacking ability"; Japanese 無能 "incompetent, untalented", "incompetence" and "a good-for-nothing"; Korean 무능 is the hanja form, "incompetency". It is the nearest multi-language word to "fallible", which has none.
