## Notes
- [六 (char)](../characters/六%20(char).md) "six" + [百 (char)](../characters/百%20(char).md) "hundred" — six hundred, 600.

Plain digit + 百, like [[二百]], [[七百]] and [[八百]]: the hundred is counted by prefixing the digit to 百, with no further grouping word. Japanese has the sound-changed ろっぴゃく (gemination plus p-voicing of ひゃく), not the plain concatenation ろくひゃく. Korean 육백 follows the Sino-Korean reading directly. `vietnamese: lục bách` is the Sino-Vietnamese compositional reading; everyday Vietnamese uses the native sáu trăm for round numbers like this. Compare [[六千]] and [[六十]].
