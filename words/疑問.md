## Notes
- [疑](../words/疑.md) "doubt, suspect" (stand-in: [[疑義]]) + [問 (char)](../characters/問.md) "ask" (stand-in: [[質問]]) — a question; interrogative.

疑問 means question/interrogative, as invoked by `文法 - 02音韻論`'s 疑問文 ("interrogative sentence") and 非対立疑問文 ("non-polar question"). Mandarin yíwèn, Japanese ぎもん, Korean 의문, and Vietnamese nghi vấn are all real, standard terms, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Cantonese ji4 man6 is a straightforward compositional reading. `kwin` is false: both 疑 and 問 are individually false.

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot**: the concatenated string `ㄧㄇㄨㄋ` admits two legal partitions — the intended 疑(ㄧ)+問(ㄇㄨㄋ), and a false alternate (ㄧㄇ+ㄨㄋ) that disagrees on where the first cut falls — so the dot at the true 疑|問 boundary is required: ㄧ·ㄇㄨㄋ.
