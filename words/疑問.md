## Notes
- [疑](../characters/疑.md) "doubt, suspect" (stand-in: [[疑義]]) + [問 (char)](../characters/問.md) "ask" (stand-in: [[質問]]) — a question; interrogative.

Wiktionary: Chinese "query; question; doubt"; Japanese "a question (implying doubt about something); a doubt or suspicion about whether something is real or true", and as a verb "to ask; to doubt, suspect"; Korean 의문 and Vietnamese nghi vấn are the Han forms of "doubt". Used in `文法 - 02音韻論` in 付加疑問文 and 非対立疑問文.
