## Notes
- [元](../characters/元.md) "first, origin" + [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year" — the inaugural year of an imperial reign or calendar era. Used especially in Japanese historiography to mark the first year of an emperor's era name (元号), e.g. 令和元年 (Reiwa 1, 2019). Mandarin yuánnián, Cantonese jyun4 nin4, Japanese がんねん, and Korean 원년 are all directly attested standard readings. `vietnamese: nguyên niên` is the honest compositional reading (元's own nguyên + 年's own niên); modern Vietnamese more commonly uses the native năm đầu for this concept. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 원넌 diverges from Sino-Korean 원년 in the second syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ⼔ㄋ·ㄋㄝㄋ unique among both words and characters).
