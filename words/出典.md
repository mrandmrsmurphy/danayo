## Notes
- [出 (char)](../characters/出%20(char).md) "exit" + [典](../characters/典.md) "dictionary; subject book"

出典 is the source of a quotation: the book or text from which a saying, phrase or passage comes. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin chūdiǎn and Japanese しゅってん as "authority; source (of a quotation etc.)". Cantonese ceot1 din2 and Korean 출전 match in their readings.

It answers the question that an [引用](引用.md) raises, "from where?". The [史記](史記.md) and the [論語](論語.md) are the 出典 of many idioms.
