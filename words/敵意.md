## Notes
- [敵](../characters/敵.md) "enemy" + [意](../characters/意.md) "intent"

敵意 is hostility: ill will toward an opponent. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin díyì as "enmity; hostility; animosity" and Japanese てきい as "hatred; hostility; hostile feeling". Korean 적의 and Cantonese dik6 ji3 match.

It is the attitude that comes before a fight, where [憎悪](憎悪.md) is hatred as a strong feeling and [敵人](敵人.md) is the enemy it is turned against.
