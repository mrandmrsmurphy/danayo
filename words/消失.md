## Notes
- [消 (char)](../characters/消%20(char).md) "vanish" + [失](../characters/失.md) "lose"

消失 is to vanish: to disappear from sight or from existence, and the disappearance itself. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xiāoshī as "to disappear; to vanish" and Japanese しょうしつ as "the act of vanishing; a disappearance; to vanish". Cantonese siu1 sat1 and Korean 소실 match.

It is the compound form of [消](消.md), which covers vanishing in general. It stresses the loss that follows, since 失 is "lose", and is milder than [消滅](消滅.md), which dies out for good.
