## Notes
- [蘭](../characters/蘭.md) "orchid" is separately legitimized by [[蘭花]]. [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month, moon" is separately legitimized by [[月]] itself. No cranberry.

Seventh month of the ancient Chinese poetic calendar. Wild orchids (蘭) bloom in late summer with a refined, subtle fragrance. Confucius compared the orchid to the virtuous person who cultivates excellence in obscurity. The Qixi Festival (七夕) falls this month.

Pronunciation fields (lan'wed/란웓/ㄌㄚㄋ·⼔ㄊ) are the straightforward concatenation of each constituent's own stored reading, including the null-onset syllable break — no bug; `kwin: false` already correct. Korean 란월 already correctly uses the unshifted North Korean form. Japanese らんつき (on'yomi RAN + つき, since 蘭 has no native kun'yomi) matches the established poetic-month convention precedent set by [[菊月]] (also on'yomi-only). Vietnamese tháng lan matches the *tháng* + Sino-Vietnamese-name pattern. Fixed cantonese's stray space, removed redundant 品詞. No homophones.
