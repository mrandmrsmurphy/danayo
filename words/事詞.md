## Notes
- [事 (char)](../characters/事%20(char).md) "action" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "expression" — a Dan'a'yo grammatical-category term, part of the same word-class-naming pattern as [[性詞]] (stative), 名詞 (noun), and others.

事詞 (jisa) names the "eventive" word class in Dan'a'yo's own grammar — basically transitive verbs, but adapted to the language's own topic-comment (T-C) sentence structure rather than a subject-object one. As with other Dan'a'yo grammatical-category terms, the mandarin/cantonese/japanese/korean/vietnamese fields are compositional readings of the two constituent characters (matching the pattern already established on [[性詞]]) rather than attested real-language grammatical terminology, since this specific category is a Dan'a'yo-internal analytical construct.

Fixed a duplicate `pos`/`品詞` field and added the previously-missing `mandarin`/`cantonese`/`japanese`/`korean`/`vietnamese`/`kwin` fields, all absent from the frontmatter entirely. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 지사 diverges from Sino-Korean 사사, since 事's own Dan'a'yo reading doesn't match Sino-Korean, even though 詞's own reading matches exactly (詞 is independently `kwin: true`).
