## Notes
- [降](../characters/降.md) "descend" + [臨](../characters/臨.md) "draw near, approach"

降臨 means to descend or arrive from above, most often used of a deity, spirit, or exalted figure making an appearance among mortals — a solemn, elevated register distinct from plain "arriving." Mandarin 降臨 (jiànglín), Cantonese 降臨 (gong3lam4), Japanese 降臨 (こうりん), Korean 강림 (gangnim), and Sino-Vietnamese giáng lâm are all used this way across religious contexts (the descent of a god or Buddha, a divine visitation) and, in modern colloquial extension, hyperbolically for the "arrival" of anything treated as momentous or awe-inspiring (e.g. a celebrity's grand entrance).
