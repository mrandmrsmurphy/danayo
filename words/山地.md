## Notes
- [山 (char)](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain" + [地 (char)](../characters/地%20(char).md) "land; area" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 山's and 地's own `stand_in` fields point to themselves).

山地 names mountainous terrain or a mountain region generally — the geographical/geological term for hilly, elevated land as a category, as distinct from a specific named mountain.

Japanese さんち, Korean 산지, and Vietnamese sơn địa (verified) are all standard, non-drifted readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄚㄋㄉㄧㄜ unique across both words and characters).
