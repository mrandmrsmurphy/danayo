## Notes
- [長 (char)](../characters/長%20(char).md) "long" + [靴](../characters/靴.md) "boot"

長靴 is a boot, a rubber or plastic boot. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 長靴 (Mandarin chángxuē, Cantonese coeng4 hoe1) and Japanese ながぐつ as "rubber or plastic boot(s)", and Korean 장화 as "boots". The Vietnamese trường ngoa field is built from the readings of the two characters.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [靴](../characters/靴.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
