## Notes
- [演 (char)](../characters/演%20(char).md) "unfold; develop" + [䋇 (char)](../characters/䋇%20(char).md) "unravel; explain"

演䋇 is deduction: to reason from a general principle to the particular cases that follow from it. The word is the form 演繹 as it is written in Dan'a'yo, where the rare character 繹 is an alias of the stand-in 䋇, so the file carries the stand-in form and keeps 演繹 as an alias. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yǎnyì as "to deduce; to narrate; to elaborate" and Japanese えんえき as "deduction: extending information about one thing to apply to other things". Cantonese jin2 jik6 and Korean 연역 match.

It is the counterpart of [帰納](帰納.md), induction, which reasons from particulars to a general rule. Both are kinds of [推論](推論.md), inference, and each is tested against the facts by [検証](検証.md).
