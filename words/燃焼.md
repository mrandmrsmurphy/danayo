## Notes
- [燃](../characters/燃.md) "burn" + [焼](../characters/焼.md) "burn" — stand-in for both [[燃]] and [[焼]] (transitivity holds: A=B=AB), hence the `#cranberry` tag

燃焼 means combustion or burning: the process of something burning with heat and light (完全燃焼, "complete combustion"; 燃焼室, "combustion chamber"; 燃焼効率, "combustion efficiency"), and figuratively to burn with energy or emotion (燃燒脂肪, "burn fat"). The variants 燃烧 and 燃燒 are listed as aliases. Both halves independently mean "burn", a near-synonym doubling.

Mandarin ránshāo, Cantonese jin4 siu1, Japanese ねんしょう (nenshō), and Korean 연소 (yeonso) are all the standard word for "combustion" in both literal (完全燃焼, "complete combustion") and figurative senses (burning energy/fat, burning with emotion). Vietnamese nhiên thiêu is a real, attested Sino-Vietnamese compound carrying the identical literal/figurative double sense — đốt cháy mỡ thừa, "burn excess fat"; bùng cháy ngọn lửa giận dữ, "burn with a fire of anger."
