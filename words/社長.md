## Notes
- [社](../characters/社.md) "company; society" + [長 (char)](../characters/長%20(char).md) "chief, head" — company president.

社長 is the president or head of a company ([[会社]]). Japanese しゃちょう and Korean 사장 use it for the chief executive of a business, and Dan'a'yo takes that sense. Mandarin shèzhǎng and Cantonese se5 zoeng2 are the readings of the same characters, but Chinese uses the word for the head of an association or society; Mandarin speakers call a company head 總經理, 董事長 or 老闆 (not verified here), so `mandarin` and `cantonese` record the real Chinese readings of the form. Compare [[部長]], a department head, and [[上司]], any boss.
