## Notes
- [伴](../characters/伴.md) "companion" + [侶](../characters/侶.md) "companion, partner" — a synonym-doubling compound, "companion, partner, mate," stand-in for [侶](../characters/侶.md), which cannot appear independently.

伴侶 names a companion in the full sense: a life partner or spouse, a close companion on a journey or through life, and by extension a companion animal (伴侶動物). Both halves already mean "companion," so the compound reinforces rather than combines them, the usual pattern for Sino-Xenic synonym compounds. It is a warmer and more general word than [配偶](配偶.md) ("spouse"), which is legal and clinical in register, and it is not limited to marriage; compare [夫婦](夫婦.md) ("married couple").

Mandarin bànlǚ, Cantonese bun6 leoi5, Japanese はんりょ (hanryo), Korean 반려 (ballyeo) and Vietnamese bạn lữ are all standard, compositional readings of the same two characters. The term is especially familiar in Korean, where 반려 appears in 반려동물 ("companion animal," i.e. a pet) and 반려자 ("life partner").
