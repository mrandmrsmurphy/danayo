## Etymology
- [図](../characters/図.md) "diagram" + [書](../characters/書.md) "book" + [館](../characters/館.md) "hall, building, wing"

## Notes
- "Library, book house" (図書 "books/documents" + 館 "building"). Vietnamese does not calque 図書館 directly — the standard Vietnamese word is thư viện (書院, literally "book institute/academy," a term historically used for private academies in Chinese but repurposed in Vietnamese specifically for "library"). The single-character library coinage 圕 (a fusion of 囗 and 書) was invented by the Chinese librarian Du Dingyou (杜定友) in 1926, not by Japanese.
