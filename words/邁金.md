## Notes
- [邁](../characters/邁.md) + [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal" — a transliteration of *Meitner*, after physicist Lise Meitner, who co-discovered nuclear fission but was denied a share of the Nobel Prize for it. 邁 is the first syllable of 邁特納, the standard Mandarin transliteration of "Meitner," truncated rather than spelled out in full. Japanese and Korean instead borrow the IUPAC name as a loanword (マイトネリウム, 마이트너륨).

**Content added**: `mandarin`/`cantonese` were both blank; filled with `mài`/`maai6`, the readings of the real standard Chinese element character 鿏/䥑 (officially adopted 1998) — `mài` matches [[邁]]'s own Mandarin reading exactly, confirming 邁 was deliberately chosen for this coinage; a Cantonese reading for 鿏/䥑 itself could not be independently confirmed in the dictionary sources checked, so `maai6` was used by extension from [[邁]]'s own value, consistent with the pattern established across this neologism series. `vietnamese` was likewise blank; filled with `Meitneri`, the attested term from Vietnamese Wikipedia.
