## Notes
- [耶 (char)](../characters/耶%20(char).md) "questioning particle; yeah" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 耶 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

耶 is a sentence-final questioning particle, and by extension a standalone interjection of surprise or joy ("yeah!").

Mandarin yé, Cantonese je4, and Korean 야 (ya) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native reading か (ka, itself a questioning particle) rather than the on-reading ヤ (YA) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese gia is one of two stored candidates. No Dan'a'yo homophones (叡/曳/裔/鋭 share the same syllable at the character level, but none has an independent word page).
