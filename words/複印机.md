## Notes
- [複](../characters/複.md) "duplicate" + [印](../characters/印.md) "print, stamp" + [机](../characters/机.md) "machine" — "duplicate-printing machine." 複's own `stand_in` is [[重複]], 印's is [[封印]] and 机's is [[机会]]; none points here, so 複印机 is an independent compound.

複印机 is a photocopier. Mandarin fùyìnjī and Cantonese fuk1 jan3 gei1 are the ordinary Chinese words. Japanese uses 複写機 or コピー機 and Korean 복사기, so the `japanese` and `korean` fields hold the readings of the characters rather than attested words. Dan'a'yo follows the Chinese form because it is the one attested in two languages.

Compare [[複写]] (a reproduced copy) and [[机器]] (machine).
