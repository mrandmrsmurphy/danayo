## Notes
- [厳 (char)](../characters/厳%20(char).md) "strict; rigorous; rigid; stern"

厳 (alternate forms 嚴, 严, 儼) is 形声: semantic 喦/吅 ("talkative," simplified to 𭕄 in the shinjitai, the same simplification pattern as 單→単) + phonetic [[敢]] ("daring," retained intact in the shinjitai) — the "solemn, stern" sense may be a phonetic-loan development rather than transparent from 敢's own meaning. It means "strict, rigorous, stern": [[厳粛]] ("solemn, austere"), [[厳重]] ("serious, rigorous"), [[尊厳]] ("dignity, sanctity"), and 嚴禁 ("strictly forbidden").

Mandarin yán, Cantonese jim4, and Japanese げん/ごん (gen/gon, on'yomi, fully productive: 厳重 げんじゅう "rigorous") alongside native おごそ(か) (ogoso(ka), "solemn, stern," the everyday adjective) all preserve the sense. Korean 엄 (eom) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 엄하다 (eomhada, built on the same Sino-Korean root, "to be strict"). Vietnamese nghiêm is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in nghiêm khắc ("strict, severe") and tôn nghiêm (尊厳, "dignity" — matching this word's own compound directly).
