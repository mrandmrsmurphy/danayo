## Notes
- [暗](../characters/暗%20(char).md) "dark" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [暗](../characters/暗%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

暗 means dark.

Mandarin àn, Cantonese am3, Japanese あん, Korean 암, and Vietnamese ám are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bugs: literal `vietnamese: null` and `羅馬字` missing its leading glottal marker (am→'am). No word-level homophones (岩/庵/癌 bound elsewhere).
