## Notes
- [政](../characters/政.md) "government, political affairs" + [府](../characters/府.md) "government repository, seat of government" — this word is the stand-in compound that legitimizes the bound character 府 (per its own stored `stand_in`).

政府 is the everyday word for "government," pairing 政 (the abstract political-affairs sense, itself bound to [[政治]] "politics") with 府, which originally denoted a government building or repository (its own 形声 etymology is semantic 广 "building" + phonetic 付) before generalizing to the institution itself. With less strength than the Japanese cognate せいふ, this word *probably* refers to a current administration rather than the office in the abstract, though it may also carry that more timeless institutional sense without a specific administration in mind.

Mandarin zhèngfǔ, Cantonese zing3 fu2, Korean 정부 (jeongbu), and Japanese せいふ (seifu) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real everyday word for "government" in its language. Vietnamese chính phủ is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, matching 政's chính and 府's own phủ. **Real bug found and fixed 2026-09-22**: this word's own `注音`/`諺文` had both drifted from 府's own stored syllable (ㄈㄨ/뿌) to a wrong one (ㄆㄨ/징푸, using ㄆ instead of ㄈ) — corrected to ㄈㄨ/뿌, along with five other stale citations of the same error found across the vault ([[府]]'s own Words list, [[政]]'s own Words list, `Buildings.md`, `Sophomore List.md`, `syllables/ㄈㄨ.md`, and `lookup/Korean/Korea.md`) — [[無政府]]'s own citation of 府 was already correct throughout and served as the reference point. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 징뿌 diverges from Sino-Korean 정부 in both syllables. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄫㄈㄨ unique among both words and characters).
