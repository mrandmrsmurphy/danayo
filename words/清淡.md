## Notes
- [清](../characters/清.md) "pure, clear" + [淡](../characters/淡.md) "diluted, watered down, insipid" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 淡 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (清's own stand-in is [[清潔]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

清淡 means insipid, plain, dilute (typically describing light, bland food).

Mandarin qīngdàn, Cantonese cing1 daam6, Japanese せいたん, and Korean 청담 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 清's and 淡's own stored citations exactly). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with thanh đạm (清's own thanh + 淡's own citation đạm) — also the real, standard Vietnamese term for "light, bland" food. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄫㄉㄚㄇ unique across both words and characters).
