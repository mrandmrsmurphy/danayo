## Notes
- [朝 (char)](../characters/朝%20(char).md) "morning" + [食 (char)](../characters/食%20(char).md) "eat"

朝食 is breakfast: the first meal of the day. Wiktionary glosses both Mandarin zhāoshí and Japanese ちょうしょく as "breakfast"; in Mandarin it is also the verb "to have breakfast". Korean 조식 is the reading of the characters, and the Cantonese reading ziu1 sik6 is built from the characters, with 朝 read "morning".

It is the formal word for the morning meal, beside the plain [早飯](早飯.md), which is also breakfast in Chinese and Korean but in Japanese a fast eater. The noon meal is the [午飯](午飯.md).
