## Notes
- [駱](../characters/駱.md) "camel" + [駝](../characters/駝.md) "humpbacked" — legitimizing note: 駱's own `stand_in` is [[駱駝]] itself, already annotated in its own citation; 駝's own `stand_in` is [[駝背]], so transitivity fails — no `#cranberry`.

Mandarin luòtuó, Cantonese lok3to4, Japanese らくだ (rendaku-voiced compound of raku + ta), and Sino-Vietnamese lạc đà are all standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 락타 (rakta) is the North Korean/문화어 form used here per this vault's convention; South Korean orthography would give 낙타 (nakta, with initial 두음법칙 softening) — the form both character pages' `korean_native` fields cite, since 낙타 is also simply the universal everyday word for "camel" regardless of dialect.
