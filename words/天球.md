## Notes
- [天 (char)](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven; sky" + [球 (char)](../characters/球%20(char).md) "ball"

天球 is the celestial sphere: the imagined globe of the sky, with the earth at its centre and the stars fixed on it. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin tiānqiú, Japanese てんきゅう and Vietnamese thiên cầu as "celestial sphere". Korean 천구 and Cantonese tin1 kau4 match.

The sun's path across it is the [黄道](黄道.md), and the points where the sun is overhead or lowest are its [天頂](天頂.md), zenith, and [天底](天底.md), nadir. The earth at its centre is the [地球](地球.md).
