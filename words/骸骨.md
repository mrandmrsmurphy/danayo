## Notes
- [骸](../characters/骸.md) "skeletal remains" + [骨 (char)](../characters/骨%20(char).md) "bone" — no `#cranberry`: 骸's own `stand_in` is [[死骸]], 骨's is 骨 itself; neither points here.

Mandarin háigǔ, Cantonese haai4gwat1, Japanese がいこつ, and Korean 해골 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Sino-Vietnamese hài cốt is likewise the standard, everyday term for "remains, skeleton" (of the deceased).
