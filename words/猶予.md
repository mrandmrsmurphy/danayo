## Notes
- [猶 (char)](../characters/猶%20(char).md) "furthermore, still, yet" + [予](../characters/予.md) "beforehand" — no stand-in relationship applies (猶's own stand-in is itself; 予's own is [[予様]]). 豫 ("in advance, hesitate") is a stored alias of 予; the real word is written 猶豫, using 豫's own divergent real readings (yù/jyu6/예/dự) rather than 予's own (yǔ/jyu4/여/dư) — a polyphonic alias-divergence, matching the pattern already seen on [[無然]]/[[片仮名]]/[[版画]]/[[狐猿]].

猶予 (real form 猶豫) means to hesitate.

Mandarin yóuyù and Japanese ゆうよ (YUU+YO) and Korean 유예 already correctly used 豫's real alias-divergent readings.
