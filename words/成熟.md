## Notes
- [成](../characters/成%20(char).md) "become" + [熟](../characters/熟.md) "ripen" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [熟](../characters/熟.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (成's own stand-in is itself).

成熟 means to ripen or mature.

Mandarin chéngshú, Cantonese sing4 suk6, Japanese せいじゅく, Korean 성숙, and Vietnamese thành thục are all standard, fully compositional readings. Trimmed malformed `mandarin` (stray untoned duplicate). No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄫㄙㄨㄎ unique across both words and characters).
