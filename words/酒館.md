## Notes
- [酒](../characters/酒.md) "alcohol, wine" + [館](../characters/館.md) "lodge, hall" — "alcohol hall." 酒's own `stand_in` is [[酒精]] and 館's is [[公館]]; neither points here, so 酒館 is an independent compound.

酒館 is a tavern, bar or pub: a place that sells drink to be consumed on the premises. Mandarin jiǔguǎn and Cantonese zau2 gun2 are the ordinary Chinese words, and the same characters are read しゅかん in Japanese and 주관 in Korean, where they are uncommon; those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[旅館]] (inn) and [[食堂]] (restaurant).
