## Notes
- [教](../characters/教.md) "teach" + [師](../characters/師.md) "expert" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [師](../characters/師.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (教's own stand-in is [[教授]]).

教師 means teacher or master.

Mandarin jiàoshī, Cantonese gaau3 si1, Japanese きょうし, Korean 교사, and Vietnamese giáo sư (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings.
