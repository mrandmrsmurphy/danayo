## Notes
- [保](../characters/保.md) "defend" + [衛](../characters/衛.md) "guard, protect" — "to defend, safeguard" (保衛國家, "to defend the nation"). Mandarin bǎowèi, Cantonese bou2 wai6, Korean 보위, and Vietnamese bảo vệ are all directly attested standard readings; `korean: 보위` is also the compositional reading (保's own 보 + 衛's own 위) — a match. `japanese: ほえい` is the honest compositional reading (保's own ほ/HO + 衛's own えい/EI); no independently attested Japanese loanword using this exact compound was found (Japanese more commonly uses 防衛 for "defend"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 밧어 diverges entirely from Sino-Korean 보위. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄨㄝ unique among both words and characters).
