## Notes
"Vacation, leave, time off." `vietnamese: hưu hạ` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese (confirmed via web search); the closely related 休假 is more commonly rendered hưu giá in Vietnamese usage, but this word's own characters (休暇) give hưu hạ. **Real content bug found and fixed**: `cantonese` had two comma-separated tone variants crammed into one field ("jau1 haa6,jau1 haa4") — trimmed to jau1 haa6, matching 暇's own confirmed reading. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 휴하 diverges from Sino-Korean 휴가 in the second syllable's initial consonant. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⼜ㄏㄚ unique among both words and characters).
