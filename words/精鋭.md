## Notes
- [精](../characters/精.md) "spirit, essence, sperm" + [鋭](../characters/鋭.md) "sharp, keen, acute, pointed" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (精's is [[精神]], 鋭's is [[鋭利]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 鋭 is individually `kwin: false`.

精鋭 names an elite — the pick of the best, especially in a military sense (精鋭部隊, "elite unit/troops").

Mandarin jīngruì, Cantonese zing1 jeoi6, Japanese せいえい (seiei), and Korean 정예 (jeongye) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese tinh nhuệ is likewise compositional and is itself the standard Vietnamese military term for "elite" (quân tinh nhuệ, "elite troops").
