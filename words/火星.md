## Notes
- [火](../characters/火%20(char).md) "fire" + [星](../characters/星.md) "star" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 火's and 星's own stand-ins are themselves).

火星 means Mars (the planet).

Mandarin Huǒxīng, Cantonese fo2 sing1, Japanese かせい, Korean 화성, and Vietnamese Hỏa Tinh are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 火's and 星's own stored citations exactly), and also the real, standard name for the planet in each language.
