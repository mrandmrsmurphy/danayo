## Notes
- [北](../characters/北.md) "north" + [美 (char)](../characters/美%20(char).md) "beautiful" + [洲 (char)](../characters/洲%20(char).md) "islet, continent"
- [[美洲]] ("the Americas") is divided into northern and southern continents; 北美洲 names the northern one. See also [[南美洲]].
- The shorter [[北美]] is the common everyday form for the same region; 北美洲 is the fuller continental name, in the series 亜洲, 欧洲, 非洲, 北美洲, 南美洲, 大洋洲 and [[南極洲]]. Japanese usually says 北米 (ほくべい) or 北アメリカ instead; the frontmatter holds the bound Sino-Japanese reading. In Dan'a'yo the word names the continent of North America, with no divergence from the shared sense.
