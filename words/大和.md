## Notes
- [大](../characters/大%20(char).md) "great" + [和](../characters/和.md) "harmony" — no stand-in relationship applies (大 is its own stand-in; 和's own is [[和平]]).

大和 (Yamato) names the ancient Japanese state/clan and, by extension, Japan itself or the Japanese people/culture.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `羅馬字` had a typo on the first syllable ("diahwa" → daihwa, matching 大's own citation and the word's own 諺文 대화). `japanese` listed three genuine readings (standard やまと, archaic おほやまと, on'yomi だいわ) comma-joined in a single string — reformatted as a proper YAML list. Korean 야마토 is a phonetic loan for this Japanese proper noun. Vietnamese Đại Hòa is a standard reading. Removed the redundant `品詞` field. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄧㄏ⺢ unique across both words and characters).
