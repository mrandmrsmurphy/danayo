## Notes
- [文](../characters/文.md) "culture" + [化](../characters/化%20(char).md) "-ification" + [圏](../characters/圏%20(char).md) "encircled area" — no stand-in relationship applies (文's own stand-in is [[文化]]; 化's own is itself; 圏's own is itself).

文化圏 means cultural sphere.

Mandarin wénhuàquān, Cantonese man4 faa3 hyun1, Japanese ぶんかけん, and Korean 문화권 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese văn hoá khuyên is compositional. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄨㄋㄏ⺢ㄍ⼔ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
