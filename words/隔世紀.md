## Notes
- [隔](../characters/隔%20(char).md) "every other" + [世](../characters/世.md) "world, era" + [紀](../characters/紀.md) "century" — the latter two together form [世紀](世紀.md), itself a meaningful word

隔世紀 means every other century, occurring once per hundred years at century-scale intervals — following the same "every other" pattern as [[隔年]] ("every other year"), [[隔月]] ("every other month"), and [[隔日]] ("every other day"), all built on the same 隔- prefix. Mandarin 隔世紀 (géshìjì), Cantonese 隔世紀 (gaak3sai3gei2), Japanese 隔世紀 (かくせいき), Korean 격세기 (gyeoksegi), and Sino-Vietnamese cách thế kỷ all preserve this meaning compositionally across the CJKV sphere.
