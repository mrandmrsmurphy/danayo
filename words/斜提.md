## Notes
- [斜](../characters/斜.md) "slant; tilt" + [提](../characters/提.md) "to lift; flick"

**斜提** is the *slanting flick*, the steeper of the two rising strokes ([[平提]] being the shallower), the stroke that climbs more sharply to the upper right. It is a sub-variety of the flick (提), the fifth of the eight principles of the 永字八法, and is listed on the [[Stroke]] page. The classification of 提 by incline into the level and the slanting is described in Chinese regular-script instruction.

The Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and North Korean forms given here are compositional. No Vietnamese form was found attested, so none is given.
