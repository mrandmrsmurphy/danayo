## Notes
- [斜](../characters/斜.md) "slant; tilt" + [提](../characters/提.md) "to lift; flick"

Wiktionary has no entry for 斜提; it is a compound of 斜 "slant; tilt" and 提, which Wiktionary glosses in Chinese calligraphy as "a rising stroke (㇀)". It names the slanting flick, the steeper of the two rising strokes, with [[平提]] the shallower. It is listed on the [[Stroke]] page. Its readings are compositional.
