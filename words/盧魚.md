## Notes
- [盧](../characters/盧.md), standing in for its 借代字 use in the fuller compound [[鱸魚]] "bass, perch" (盧 here is a clipped phonetic stand-in for 鱸, not itself meaning "fish") + [魚 (char)](../characters/魚%20(char).md) "fish".

盧魚/鱸魚 names species of perch and bass, most notably the Japanese seabass (Lateolabrax japonicus), a prized food fish across East Asia. Mandarin lúyú and Cantonese lou4 jyu2 (魚 taking a colloquial changed tone in this culinary compound, as it does in many fish-name terms) are both standard. Japanese uses the native word すずき (suzuki) rather than an on'yomi compound. Korean 로어 keeps the original ㄹ-initial (北) form rather than the South Korean 두음법칙-shifted 노어. Vietnamese lư ngư is compositional.
