## Notes
- [留](characters/留%20(char).md) "remain, stay" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 留 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

留 means to remain or stay.

Mandarin liú, Cantonese lau4, Japanese りゅう, Korean 류, and Vietnamese lưu are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 留's own stored citation exactly).
