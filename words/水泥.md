## Notes
- [水 (char)](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [泥 (char)](../characters/泥%20(char).md) "mud"

水泥 is "cement," literally "water mud," the binder that is mixed with water and sets hard. According to Wiktionary, Chinese 水泥 (shuǐní, Cantonese seoi2 nai4) is a noun "cement" and, obsolete, "mud." Wiktionary has only a Chinese entry, so the Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese fields are compositional readings of 水 and 泥, not attested words. In particular Japanese 水泥 is not the real word; from memory (not re-checked), the real Japanese word is セメント, borrowed from English.

It is used in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [China](../lookup/HSK/China.md) (sentence 12, 為 水泥叢林 ...), inside [水泥叢林](../chengyu/水泥叢林.md) "concrete jungle," together with [叢林](叢林.md) "jungle."
