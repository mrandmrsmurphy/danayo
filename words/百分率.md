## Notes
- [百 (char)](../characters/百%20(char).md) "hundred" + [分 (char)](../characters/分%20(char).md) "divide, part" + [率](../characters/率.md) "rate, ratio" — literally "hundred-part rate," i.e. a rate expressed out of a hundred.

Mandarin bǎifēnlǜ, Cantonese baak3 fan1 leot6, Japanese ひゃくぶんりつ (hyakubunritsu), Korean 백분율 (baekbunyul), and Vietnamese bách phần suất are all fully compositional. Korean's spelling is worth a note: 率 (률) shifts to 율 after a ㄴ-final or vowel-final syllable under a regular Sino-Korean orthographic rule, which is why the compound is written 백분율 rather than the mechanically-expected 백분률 — a real spelling convention, not a vault-internal irregularity.
