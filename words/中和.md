## Notes
- [中](../characters/中.md) "middle" + [和](../characters/和.md) "harmony, peace" — no stand-in relationship applies (中 is its own stand-in; 和's is [和平](和平.md)).

中和 is to neutralize, and neutralization. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 中和 (Mandarin zhōnghé, Cantonese zung1 wo4) as, in chemistry, "to neutralize", then "to find a happy medium; to compromise", and, literary, "just; impartial; fair". It also lists place names and a historical era name (881–885). It glosses Japanese ちゅうわ as neutralization, and Vietnamese trung hòa as "to neutralize" and "neutral". Korean 중화 is built from the readings of the two characters.

The 《中庸》 has the line 致中和，天地位焉，萬物育焉, "attain 中和, and heaven and earth will be in their places and all things will flourish". The word shares its 注音 with [中華](中華.md), which is a proper noun.
