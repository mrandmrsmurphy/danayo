## Notes
- [淡](../characters/淡.md) "diluted, pale" + [紫](../characters/紫.md) "purple"

淡 (pale, diluted) modifies 紫 (purple) to produce lavender: the lighter, cooler range of the purple spectrum. The compound is transparent and analytically compositional — the same pattern as 淡紅 (pale red, pink) and 淡青 (pale blue-green). In CJKV color vocabulary, lavender does not have a single canonical term; 淡紫 is the most widely interpretable option and the recommended Dan'a'yo form.

In Japanese, たんし is not the usual word for lavender, which is borrowed ラベンダー or the native 薄紫 (うすむらさき, "thin purple"); likewise Korean 담자 is a literal descriptive term. Dan'a'yo adopts the descriptive compound as its word for lavender, with the shared CJKV sense of "pale purple" and no divergence.
