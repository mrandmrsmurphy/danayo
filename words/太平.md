## Notes
- [太 (char)](../characters/太%20(char).md) "great, very" + [平](../characters/平.md) "peaceful, level" — peaceful, tranquil; best known as the first half of [[太平洋]] ("Pacific Ocean," literally "peaceful ocean"), the Chinese rendering of the name "Pacific" that Ferdinand Magellan's expedition gave the ocean after its calm crossing (a recollection, not re-checked).

太 here is the intensifier "very, supremely," as in 太古 and 太陽, so 太平 is "supremely peaceful," the state of a realm at ease, and the word has an older variant 泰平 with the same sense. The Conflict page lists it as "peaceful, peace," next to the other peace words, and the Sophomore List uses it as the Dan'a'yo word for English "peace." Dan'a'yo keeps that sense, the condition of peace, with the Pacific's name as its best-known use. It is related to [平安](平安.md) "safe and well, peace," which stresses personal safety, and to [太平洋](太平洋.md), the ocean. The readings are Mandarin tàipíng, Cantonese taai3 ping4, Japanese taihei (たいへい), Korean 태평 and Sino-Vietnamese thái bình. The 泰平 variant and the 太古 and 太陽 examples are from memory and have not been checked against a source.
