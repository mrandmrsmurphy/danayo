## Notes
- [老 (char)](../characters/老%20(char).md) "old" + [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person"

老人 is an old person, and the elderly as a group. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin lǎorén as "old person; the old; the elderly" and also "elder". Japanese ろうじん and Korean 노인 match.

It names the stage of life, where [翁](翁.md) is a venerable old man and [長上](長上.md) are elders by rank. The young counterpart is the [青年](青年.md).
