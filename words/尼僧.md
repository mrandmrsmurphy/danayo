## Notes
- [尼](../characters/尼.md) "nun" + [僧](../characters/僧.md) "priest; monk" — this word is the stand-in for [尼](../characters/尼.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

尼僧 is a Buddhist nun — 尼 itself already carries this sense independently (a phonetic transcription of Sanskrit *bhikṣuṇī*), with 僧 appended for parallelism with the more general "monastic" term.

Japanese にそう and Korean 니승 are both standard readings. **`vietnamese` is left omitted**: Vietnamese does not use this word order at all — it says tăng ni, with the elements reversed (monk-then-nun, covering monastics of both sexes collectively), or ni cô for a nun specifically; no attested "ni tăng" in the Chinese/Japanese/Korean compound's own order was found.

**Real bug found and fixed 2026-09-29**: `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` had a stale "sung"-pattern onset for 僧's syllable (숭/sung/ㄙㄨㄫ) instead of 僧's own authoritative citation reading (승/sǝng/ㄙㄜㄫ). This was previously left alone as one of several words sharing 僧's documented "sung vs. seng" split — but re-deriving that split fresh (see [[僧]]'s own Notes) found no genuine conditioning pattern behind it; it was simply an uncorrected error shared by [[僧伽]]/[[僧侶]]/[[密陀僧]] too. Corrected to 늬승/niǝsǝng/ㄋㄧㄜㄙㄜㄫ.

**Correction (2026-08-27)**: `korean` had been badly wrong — stored 이승, a completely unrelated, extremely common Korean word meaning "this world, the world of the living" (as opposed to 저승, "the afterworld") — corrected to the real reading 니승 (also confirming the vault's standing North-Korean/문화어 convention: 니 unshifted, not South Korean 이). No homophones (注音 ㄋㄧㄜㄙㄜㄫ unique across both words and characters).
