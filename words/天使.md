## Notes
- [天](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven, sky" + [使](../characters/使.md) "envoy, messenger" — "heaven's messenger," an angel. Their own `stand_in` values: 天's is itself; 使's is [[使者]]; none points here, so 天使 is an independent compound.

天使 is an angel: a messenger of heaven. Mandarin tiānshǐ, Cantonese tin1 si3, Japanese てんし and Korean 천사 all use it, and Vietnamese thiên sứ does too. It is mainly a Christian word, used for the angels of the Bible; see [[基督敎]]. Compare [[堕天使]] (fallen angel) and [[天]] (heaven).
