## Notes
- [口](../characters/口%20(char).md) "mouth" + [訣](../characters/訣.md) "formula, knack" — no stand-in relationship applies (口 is its own stand-in; 訣's own is [[秘訣]]).

口訣 means a mnemonic formula or secret oral formula — verse or a set phrase passed down orally to aid memorization (as in traditional multiplication tables or martial arts techniques).

Japanese くけつ (using 口's alternate on'yomi KU rather than the more common KOU), Korean 구결, and Vietnamese khẩu quyết are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄛㄨㄍ⼔ㄊ unique across both words and characters).
