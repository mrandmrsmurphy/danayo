## Notes
- [眼](../characters/眼.md) "eyeball, pupil" + [睛](../characters/睛.md) "eye" — 睛's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 眼's own is [[眼球]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 睛 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word. Synonymous with [[瞳孔]].

眼睛 is a general, everyday word for the eye — in Mandarin especially, it is simply the ordinary word for "eye(s)" (long since broadened past its narrower "pupil" origin), while 瞳孔 is reserved for the more anatomically precise "pupil." Mandarin yǎnjīng, Cantonese ngaan5 zing1, and Japanese がんせい (gansei) are all fully compositional.

Korean 안정 is worth a note: spoken in isolation it is indistinguishable from the far more common unrelated word 안정 (安定, "stability, stabilization") — a coincidental Korean-internal homophone, disambiguated only by context. Vietnamese nhãn tình is compositional, drawing on 睛's own attested "tình" reading.
