## Notes
- [異](../characters/異.md) "different" + [母](../characters/母.md) "mother" — born of a different mother.

異母 is "of a different mother": the relation between siblings who share a father but not a mother. It is "different-mother." Mandarin yìmǔ, Cantonese ji6 mou5 and Japanese いぼ are confirmed on Wiktionary, which lists the derived term 異母兄弟 (half-brother). It combines with the sibling roots ([[兄]], [[弟]], [[姉]], [[細妹]]) for half-siblings; the matching 異父 (a different father) was not found on Wiktionary.
