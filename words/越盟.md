## Notes
- [越 (char)](../characters/越%20(char).md) "exceed" + [盟](../characters/盟.md) "alliance, covenant" — "Viet Minh," the Vietnamese independence movement/coalition founded by Ho Chi Minh in 1941. `korean: 월맹` (Wolmaeng) is a real historical South Korean term for North Vietnam/the Viet Minh; `mandarin: yuèméng`, `cantonese: jyut6 mang4`, and `japanese: えつめい` are the parallel compositional Sino-xenic readings across the other daughter languages (Japanese also commonly uses the direct loanword ベトミン, Betomin). `vietnamese: Việt Minh` is the name itself. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 웓명 diverges from Sino-Korean 월맹 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ⼔ㄊㄇ⼶ㄫ unique among both words and characters).
