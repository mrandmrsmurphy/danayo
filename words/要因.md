## Notes
- [要](../characters/要.md) "essential" + [因](../characters/因.md) "cause"

要因 is a key factor: a main cause of a result. Wiktionary glosses Japanese よういん as "primary factor; reason". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 요인 matches.

It is one of the causes that together produce a result, where [原因](原因.md) is the cause itself. A reason a person acts is [動机](動机.md), a motive, and the thing a result follows is [結果](結果.md).
