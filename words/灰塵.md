## Notes
- [灰](../characters/灰.md) "ashes" + [塵](../characters/塵.md) "dust; dirt" — stand-in for [[塵]], which cannot appear independently

灰塵 means ash and dust: the fine powdery residue of burning together with settled dirt (灰塵滿面, "face covered in dust"; 化為灰塵, "reduced to ashes"; 吸入灰塵, "inhale dust"), and figuratively the remains or ruins of something destroyed, or utter destruction. The compound pairs [[灰]] "ashes" with 塵 "dust; dirt".

Mandarin huīchén, Cantonese fui1 can4, and Japanese かいじん (kaijin) are all real words for "ash and dust" together. Korean 회진 is a genuine three-way homophone: alongside this compound's own sense, the identical spelling covers 回診 ("a doctor's rounds") and 回進 ("to turn and advance"), three unrelated words sharing one pronunciation. No Vietnamese compound for 灰塵 is attested; the everyday Vietnamese word for dust is the native bụi.
