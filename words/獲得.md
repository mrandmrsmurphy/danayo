## Notes
- [獲](../characters/獲.md) "seize, obtain, get" + [得](../characters/得.md) "get" — **`#cranberry` confirmed**: both 獲's and 得's own `stand_in` fields point back to this exact compound (transitivity A=B=AB holds). Fittingly, `korean_native` is byte-for-byte identical for both (얻을, "to obtain"), and Japanese converges too — 獲's え and 得's う both trace to conjugations of the same verb "to obtain," historically interchangeable.
