## Notes
- [純](../characters/純.md) "pure" + [篤](../characters/篤.md) "sincere"
- This word is the viable form of [篤](../characters/篤.md): the character's meaning (sincere, earnest) is identical, but 篤 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 純篤 is its `stand_in` compound.

純篤 is a literary word for a person of unfeigned, steady sincerity: 純 is "pure, unmixed" and 篤 is "sincere, earnest, devoted" (as in 篤実, "sincere and honest", and 篤志, "a charitable, earnest purpose"). Mandarin chúndǔ and Cantonese seon4 duk1 are the compound's readings in the vault's data; the Korean reading 준독 follows the vault's own character readings (純 is 준 in the character data), and I have not found a Japanese reading to record.

In Dan'a'yo it is a praise word for plain, genuine goodness of character, placed beside [[善良]] (kind, benevolent) in Society's virtue group, and set against [[偽善]] (hypocrisy), the counterfeit it is the opposite of.
