## Notes
- [月](../characters/月%20(char).md) "moon" + [食](../characters/食%20(char).md) "eat" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 月 and 食's own stand-ins are themselves, each with its own independent word page).

月食 means lunar eclipse.

Mandarin yuèshí, Japanese げっしょく, Korean 월식, and Vietnamese nguyệt thực are all standard, fully compositional readings.
