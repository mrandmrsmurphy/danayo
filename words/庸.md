## Notes
- [庸 (char)](../characters/庸%20(char).md) "commonplace; ordinary; mediocre" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [庸 (char)](../characters/庸%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

庸 names the commonplace or mediocre — average, unremarkable, "run of the mill" (庸人 "an ordinary/mediocre person," 中庸 "the golden mean," a foundational Confucian philosophical concept naming the moderate middle path).

Japanese よう, Korean 용, and Vietnamese dung are all standard readings. **A genuine triple Middle Chinese homophone**: 庸, [[容]], and [[湧]] all reconstruct to the identical MC reading despite different phonetic roots (谷/用/甬 respectively) — a real convergence, not a Dan'a'yo-introduced collision, already documented on [[容]]'s own page.
