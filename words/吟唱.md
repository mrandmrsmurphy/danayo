## Notes
- [吟](../characters/吟.md) "hum, chant" + [唱](../characters/唱.md) "chant, sing" — a near-synonym doubling: to chant, to recite (poetry).
- Stand-in for [吟](../characters/吟.md), which does not appear as a standalone Dan'a'yo word — 吟唱 is the form the character takes in vocabulary.
