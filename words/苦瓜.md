## Notes
- [苦 (char)](../characters/苦%20(char).md) "suffering, bitter" is separately legitimized by [[苦]] itself. [瓜](../characters/瓜.md) "melon" is separately legitimized by [[胡瓜]]. No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

苦瓜 combines 苦 ("bitter") with 瓜 ("melon") — "bitter melon."

Mandarin kǔguā, japanese にがうり (nigauri, genuinely compositional from 苦's native にが- + 瓜's own native うり), korean 고과 (gogwa), and vietnamese khổ qua (a very common real term) were all already correct, fully compositional readings.
