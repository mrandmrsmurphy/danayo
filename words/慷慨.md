## Notes
- [慷](../characters/慷.md) "ardent" + [慨](../characters/慨%20(char).md) "sigh" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [慷](../characters/慷.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (慨's own stand-in is itself).

慷慨 means impassioned, vehement, or fervent — stirred by strong emotion, especially righteous indignation or generosity.

Mandarin kāngkǎi, Cantonese hong2 koi3, Japanese こうがい, Korean 강개, and Vietnamese khảng khái are all standard, fully compositional readings. Stripped a stray invisible Unicode mark from `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄚㄫㄎㄚㄧ unique across both words and characters).
