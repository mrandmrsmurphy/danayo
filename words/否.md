## Notes
- [否 (char)](../characters/否%20(char).md) "no"
- An important word, on the [Sophomore List](lexipedia/Sophomore%20List.md)

否 is 形声: semantic [[不]] ("not") + phonetic [[口]] ("mouth"). It means "no, not so" — a formal negator and, in classical usage, a standalone answer meaning "no" (as opposed to modern colloquial 不 or 不是). It heads [[否定]] ("negation, denial").

Mandarin fǒu, Cantonese fau2, and Japanese ひ (hi, on'yomi, largely confined to formal/literary compounds) alongside native いな (ina, "no," an archaic/literary native negator) all preserve the sense. Korean 부 (bu) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 아니다 (anida, the everyday negator "to not be"). Vietnamese phầu is the Hán Việt reading.
