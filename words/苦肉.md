## Notes
- [苦 (char)](../characters/苦%20(char).md) "bitter, suffering" + [肉 (char)](../characters/肉%20(char).md) "flesh, meat" — flesh made to suffer; desperate self-sacrifice as a stratagem

Wiktionary: Chinese and Japanese 苦肉 "desperate measure, last resort". It is the basis of 苦肉計 (苦肉之計), which Wiktionary glosses "the ruse of inflicting an injury upon oneself to win the trust of one's enemy" (one of the Thirty-Six Stratagems of ancient China); Vietnamese khổ nhục kế is its Han form, and Wikipedia's Thirty-Six Stratagems article lists it as "Inflict injury on oneself to win the enemy's trust".
