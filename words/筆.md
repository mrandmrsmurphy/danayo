## Notes
- [筆](../characters/筆%20(char).md) "pen, stylus" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 筆 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

筆 names a writing implement — originally the bamboo-handled writing brush (its own 形声 phonetic component 聿 already meant "writing brush" before 竹 was added to specify the bamboo material), extended in modern usage to any pen or stylus. It is highly productive: 鉛筆 ("pencil"), 筆記 ("to take notes"), 擱筆 ("to lay down the pen, stop writing").

Mandarin bǐ, Cantonese bat1, and Korean 필 (pil) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading ふで (fude, "writing brush") rather than the on-reading ヒツ (HITSU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese bút is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading and remains the everyday Vietnamese word for "pen."
