## Notes
- [九 (char)](../characters/九%20(char).md) "nine" + [千](../characters/千.md) "thousand"

九千 is nine thousand (9,000), a plain compositional numeral, 九 × 1,000. Vietnamese cửu thiên is the compositional Hán Việt reading; everyday Vietnamese counts natively instead (from memory, not re-checked), as on [七千](七千.md). Used in the Dan'a'yo numeral pieces such as the year in the 1919 Korean Declaration of Independence ([宣言書](../translation/宣言書.md)); there are no words for full numbers, only for these pieces.
