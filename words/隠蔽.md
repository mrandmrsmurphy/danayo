## Notes
- [隠](../characters/隠.md) "hide" + [蔽](../characters/蔽.md) "cover, hide, conceal" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes 蔽 as a bound character (隠's own stand-in is [[隠蔵]]; transitivity fails, so no `#cranberry`).

隠蔽 means to conceal or cover up, especially in the sense of a deliberate cover-up (of wrongdoing, evidence, or a scandal). Mandarin 隱蔽/隐蔽 (yǐnbì), Cantonese 隱蔽 (jan2bai3), Japanese 隠蔽 (いんぺい, very common in media/legal contexts — 隠蔽工作, "cover-up operation"), and Korean 은폐 (eunpye) all preserve this meaning as everyday, high-frequency words. Sino-Vietnamese ẩn tế is comparatively rare and compositional; everyday Vietnamese instead uses native che giấu or che đậy for this sense.

**Found and fixed the same recurring bug seen on [[隠形]] and [[隠蔵]]**: `羅馬字`/`諺文` had drifted to 'inpe/인퍼, inconsistent with 隠's own correctly-stored 'ǝn/은 (注音 ㄜㄋ was already correct) — corrected to 'ǝnpe/은퍼. Also removed the empty-string `vietnamese: ""` and redundant `品詞` duplicate of `pos`.
