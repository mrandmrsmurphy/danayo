## Notes
- [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big" + [指](../characters/指.md) "finger, toe" — "thumb, big toe." The `japanese` field is deliberately omitted: the real Japanese word for "thumb" is 親指 (おやゆび, "parent finger"), not 大指. `korean: 대지` is compositional but ambiguous — this exact Hangul string also corresponds to the unrelated word 大地 ("land"), a genuine homophone collision in Korean itself. `vietnamese: đại chỉ` is compositional; the real everyday Vietnamese word for "thumb" is the native ngón cái, not a Sino-Vietnamese calque (domain knowledge, not independently confirmed by search). Its homophone [[大旨]] ("main idea, gist") shares the Sino-Vietnamese reading chỉ too, so the compositional Vietnamese readings collide as well.
