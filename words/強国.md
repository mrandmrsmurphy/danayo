## Notes
- [強](../characters/強%20(char).md) "strong, powerful" + [国](../characters/国.md) "country" — no stand-in relationship applies (強's own stand-in is itself; 国's own is [[国家]]).

強国 means a superpower or powerful nation — a country with significant military, economic, or political influence.

Mandarin qiángguó, Cantonese koeng4 gwok3, Japanese きょうこく, Korean 강국, and Vietnamese cường quốc are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼘ㄫㄍㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
