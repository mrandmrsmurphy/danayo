## Notes
- [出 (char)](../characters/出%20(char).md) "exit"

出 is 会意: [[止]] ("foot") + [[凵]] ("cave") — to step out of a cave, "to exit, go out, emerge." It is one of the most productive verbs in the language, heading compounds for physical emergence ([[日出]] "sunrise," [[外出]] "to go out"), appearance and revelation ([[出現]] "to appear, emerge"), origin ([[出身]] "background, origin"), and publication/production ([[出版]] "to publish," [[出発]] "to depart").

Mandarin chū, Cantonese ceot1, and Japanese しゅつ/すい (shutsu/sui, on'yomi, fully productive: 出発 しゅっぱつ "departure") alongside native で(る) (de(ru), "to exit, go out," the everyday verb) all preserve the sense. Korean 출 (chul) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 나다 (nada, "to emerge, arise"). Vietnamese xuất is the Hán Việt reading, well attested throughout compound vocabulary (xuất bản, "to publish"; xuất phát, "to depart"); the character page's other two stored candidates, xọt and xốt, are unrelated and look like corpus noise.
