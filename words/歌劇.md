## Notes
- [歌](../characters/歌.md) "song" + [劇 (char)](../characters/劇%20(char).md) "drama"

歌劇 is an opera: a drama sung throughout to the music of an orchestra. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin gējù as "(Western style) opera" and Japanese かげき as "opera". Korean 가극 and Vietnamese ca kịch are the same word. Cantonese go1 kek6 matches.

It joins the [劇](劇.md), drama, with song, and its light form is the [軽歌劇](軽歌劇.md), operetta. The Chinese tradition is the [京劇](京劇.md).
