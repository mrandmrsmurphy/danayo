## Notes
- [智](../characters/智.md) "wisdom" + [慧](../characters/慧%20(char).md) "intelligent" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [智](../characters/智.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (慧's own stand-in is itself).

智慧 means wisdom or intelligence.

Mandarin zhìhuì, Cantonese zi3 wai6, Japanese ちえ, Korean 지혜, and Vietnamese trí tuệ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed missing space in `cantonese`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄨㄧㄏ⼔ㄧ unique across both words and characters).
