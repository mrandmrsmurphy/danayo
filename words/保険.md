## Notes
- [保](../characters/保.md) "defend" + [険 (char)](../characters/険%20(char).md) "precipitous" — "to insure" (insurance as protection against a precarious/steep contingency: 保険会社, "insurance company"; 生命保険, "life insurance"). Mandarin bǎoxiǎn, Cantonese bou2 him2, Japanese ほけん, Korean 보험, and Vietnamese bảo hiểm are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 밧험 diverges entirely from Sino-Korean 보험. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄨㄏㄝㄇ unique among both words and characters).
