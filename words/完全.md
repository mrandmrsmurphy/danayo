## Notes
- [完](../characters/完.md) "finish, complete" + [全 (char)](../characters/全%20(char).md) "whole" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (完's is 完成, 全's is bare 全).

Mandarin wánquán, Cantonese jyun4 cyun4, Japanese かんぜん, Korean 완전, and Vietnamese hoàn toàn are all everyday words for "complete; perfect."
