## Notes
- [野](characters/野.md) "wild, field" + [猪](characters/猪.md) "pig" — "wild boar."

Mandarin yězhū and cantonese je5 zyu1 are directly attested standard readings. **Found and fixed a real-equivalent-instead-of-own-reading bug across `japanese`/`korean`/`vietnamese`**: イノシシ, 멧돼지, and "Lợn rừng" are each that language's own native everyday word for "wild boar" (멧돼지 is literally 猪's own stored `korean_native` gloss, not a Sino-Korean compositional reading) — corrected to the honest compositional やちょ/야저/dã trư (野's own YA/야/dã + 猪's own CHO/저/trư). `野豬` is the standard traditional-character spelling variant (猪→豬), correctly listed as an alias. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 야조 diverges from Sino-Korean 야저 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ⼘ㄐㄛ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 猪's own `stand_in` is this exact word (野猪), so 猪 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 野猪; 野's own `stand_in` is instead 田野, so this word does not legitimize 野.
