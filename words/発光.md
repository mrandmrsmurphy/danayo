## Notes
- [発](../characters/発%20(char).md) "emit, send out" + [光](../characters/光%20(char).md) "light" — "send out light," to emit light. Their own `stand_in` values: 発's is itself; 光's is itself; none points here, so 発光 is an independent compound.

発光 is to emit light: to give off light of its own, as a lamp, a firefly or a screen does. Mandarin fāguāng (發光), Cantonese faat3 gwong1, Japanese はっこう and Korean 발광 all use it, and Vietnamese phát quang does too. It is the light given off by the thing itself, where [[照]] is to shine on something. Compare [[光]] (light) and [[光輝]] (radiance).
