## Notes
- "Full name" (surname + given name together). **Content bugs found and fixed**: `cantonese` was missing a space between syllables; `korean` had a native gloss ("이름," "name") mixed comma-joined into the reading field alongside the real Sino-Korean reading 성명 — trimmed to the clean reading. Filled blank `pos: 名詞`. `vietnamese: tính danh` is an attested formal/classical Vietnamese term for "full name," though everyday Vietnamese overwhelmingly prefers the native họ tên instead. Normalized `hsk_level` to a quoted string. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 싱밍 diverges from Sino-Korean 성명 in both syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄫㄇㄧㄫ unique among both words and characters).
