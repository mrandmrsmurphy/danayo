## Notes
- [深](../characters/深.md) "deep" + [長 (char)](../characters/長%20(char).md) "long" — no stand-in relationship applies (深's own stand-in is [[深刻]]; 長's own is itself).

深長 means long and deep, profound, abstruse (as in 意味深長, "pregnant with meaning").

Mandarin shēncháng, Cantonese sam1 coeng4, Japanese しんちょう, and Korean 심장 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 深's and 長's own stored citations exactly. Filled the blank `pos` (性詞) and `vietnamese` fields — thâm trường (深's own thâm + 長's own trường).

**A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone with [[心臓]]** ("heart, the organ") is already fully documented and cross-referenced on 心臓's own page — completing the reciprocal callout here.
