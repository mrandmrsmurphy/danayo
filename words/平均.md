## Notes
- [平](../characters/平.md) "flat; level; peaceful" + [均 (char)](../characters/均%20(char).md) "even, equal" — no stand-in relationship applies (平's own `stand_in` is [[水平]], 均's is itself).

平均 names the average or mean — evening out a set of values, or (as an adjective) evenly balanced/level.

Japanese へいきん, Korean 평균, and Vietnamese bình quân are all standard, non-drifted readings. HSK level 2 already correct.

**A genuine Dan'a'yo-only homophone, not present in real Korean**: 平's own `kwin` is `false` because Dan'a'yo 병 diverges from real Sino-Korean 평 for this character (an already-documented, deliberate divergence, not an error) — this happens to make 平均's Dan'a'yo reading (병균/byenggyun/ㅂ⼶ㅫㄍ⼜ㄋ) collide exactly with [[病菌]] ("pathogenic bacteria," whose own real Korean 병균 matches its own Dan'a'yo form directly). The two words are true homophones in Dan'a'yo but not in Korean itself, where 평균 and 병균 remain distinct.
