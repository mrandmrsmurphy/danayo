## Notes
- [悲 (char)](../characters/悲%20(char).md) "sad" + [嘆 (char)](../characters/嘆%20(char).md) "sigh"

悲嘆 is grief: sorrow so great that it is voiced, and to lament aloud. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin bēitàn as "to bewail; to lament; to sigh mournfully" and Japanese ひたん as "grief". Cantonese bei1 taan3 matches, and Korean 비탄 is the reading of the characters.

It is sorrow turned into sighs and words, as the [悲哀](悲哀.md) is its silent form. The sigh itself is the [嘆](嘆.md), and mourning for the dead is [哀悼](哀悼.md).
