## Notes
- [初](../characters/初.md) "beginning; first" + [心](../characters/心%20(char).md) "heart; mind" — "the mind at the beginning". The stand-in for 初 is [最初](最初.md), and 心 is its own stand-in, [心](心.md).

初心 is a beginner's mind, a novice. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 初心 (Mandarin chūxīn, Cantonese co1 sam1) as "one's initial intention; original idea; true feelings; aspiration; purpose; goal; mission", and, in Buddhism, "mind of a novice, an attitude to learn resembling that of a beginner's, possibly in spite of one's mastery". It glosses Japanese しょしん as "immature, naive", "immaturity, naiveté", "original intention, original object", and, in Buddhism, "open, eager attitude to learning, as if one is a beginner". The Korean 초심 and Vietnamese sơ tâm fields are built from the readings of the two characters.

The senses diverge: Chinese gives mainly "original intention", while the Dan'a'yo gloss, "beginner's mind, novice", follows the Japanese Buddhist sense. The word is listed as Avoid on [文法 - 91疑義語](../grammar/文法%20-%2091疑義語.md), with [新手](新手.md) for a beginner and [学習者](学習者.md) for a learner.
