## Notes
- [化](../characters/化%20(char).md) "transform, -ify" + [学](../characters/学.md) "learning, science" + [肥](../characters/肥.md) "fertile" + [料](../characters/料.md) "material, ingredient" — no stand-in relationship applies (化 is its own stand-in; 学's own is [[学習]], 肥's is [[肥満]], 料's is [[材料]]).

化学肥料 means chemical fertilizer — an industrially manufactured, synthetic fertilizer (as opposed to organic/natural fertilizer).

Japanese かがくひりょう, Korean 화학비료, and Vietnamese hóa học phì liệu are all standard, fully compositional readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 화학뷔럇 diverges from Sino-Korean 화학비료 in the third and fourth syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⺢ㄏㄚㄎㄅㄨㄧㄌ⼘ㄨ unique across both words and characters).
