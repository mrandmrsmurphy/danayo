## Notes
- [暦 (char)](../characters/暦%20(char).md) "calendar" + [法 (char)](../characters/法%20(char).md) "method, law"

暦法 is a calendar system: the method by which time is divided into years, months and days. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 曆法 (an alias; Mandarin lìfǎ, Cantonese lik6 faat3) as "calendar (system by which time is divided)", Japanese れきほう as "calendar", Korean 역법 as "calendar", and Vietnamese lịch pháp as "calendar". The readings agree in all four languages.

The kinds of calendar system are the [[太陽暦]], solar, the [[太陰暦]], lunar, and the [[太陰太陽暦]], lunisolar.
