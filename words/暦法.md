## Notes
- [暦 (char)](../characters/暦%20(char).md) "calendar" + [法 (char)](../characters/法%20(char).md) "method, law"

暦法 names a calendrical system as a method or set of rules — how years, months, and days are calculated from astronomical cycles (the sun, the moon, or both together, see [[太陽暦]]/[[太陰暦]]/[[太陰太陽暦]]) — rather than a specific calendar's day-to-day output. Historically, Chinese, Korean, Japanese, and Vietnamese dynasties each maintained specialized astronomical bureaus tasked with calculating and promulgating the official 暦法, since control over the calendar was bound up with political and cosmological legitimacy.

Japanese れきほう and Korean 역법 are both standard compositional Sino-readings. Vietnamese lịch pháp is likewise standard and commonly used in academic and journalistic discussion of calendar systems. No specific HSK level applies to a term this specialized. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄝㄎㄈㄚㄆ unique across both words and characters).
