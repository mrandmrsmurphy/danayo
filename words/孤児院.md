## Notes
- [孤](../characters/孤.md) "lone, lonely" + [児](../characters/児%20(char).md) "baby" + [院](../characters/院.md) "institution, courtyard" — no stand-in relationship applies (孤's own stand-in is [[孤独]]; 児's own is [[児子]]; 院's own is [[院落]]).

孤児院 means orphanage — an institution caring for orphaned children.

Mandarin gū'éryuàn, Cantonese gu1 ji4 jyun6-2, and Korean 고아원 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese trại trẻ mồ côi is the standard native term (literally "camp for orphaned children"). **Real bug found and fixed**: `japanese` had archaic pre-reform kana こじゐん — modernized to こじいん. Reformatted `aliases`/`characters`. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄝㄧ⼔ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
