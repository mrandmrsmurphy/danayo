## Notes
- [文 (char)](../characters/文.md) "text" (stand-in: [[文化]]) + [脈 (char)](../characters/脈.md) "vein, thread" (stand-in: [[山脈]]) — context, literally "the thread running through a text."

Wiktionary: Japanese 文脈 (ぶんみゃく) is "linguistic context"; Vietnamese văn mạch is the Han form of the same, "linguistic context". Used in `文法 - 02音韻論` in the discussion of vowel hiatus.
