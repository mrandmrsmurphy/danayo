## Notes
- [文 (char)](../characters/文.md) "text" (stand-in: [[文化]]) + [脈 (char)](../characters/脈.md) "vein, thread" (stand-in: [[山脈]]) — context, literally "the thread running through a text."

文脈 means context, as invoked by `文法 - 02音韻論`'s discussion of vowel hiatus being tolerated "as long as the surrounding context provides clarity." Japanese ぶんみゃく and Korean 문맥 are both real, standard terms, matching this word's compositional reading. Mandarin wénmài and Cantonese man4 mak6 are straightforward compositional readings — Mandarin more often says 语境 (yǔjìng) for linguistic "context," so this is not independently verified as the standard Mandarin term. Vietnamese văn mạch is compositional only. `kwin` is false: both 文 and 脈 are individually false.

No syllable-boundary dot is needed: the concatenated string `ㄇㄨㄋㄇㄚㄎ` admits only one legal partition.
