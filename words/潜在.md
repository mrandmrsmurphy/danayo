## Notes
- [潜](../characters/潜.md) "latent, hidden" + [在](../characters/在%20(char).md) "exist" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 潜 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (在's own stand-in is itself, so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

潜在 means potential, latent.

Cantonese cim4 zoi6, Japanese せんざい, and Korean 잠재 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 潜's and 在's own stored citations exactly.
