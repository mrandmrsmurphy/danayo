## Notes
- [事](../characters/事%20(char).md) "affair, matter" + [故](../characters/故.md) "reason, cause" — no stand-in relationship applies (事's own stand-in is itself; 故's own is [[緣故]]).

事故 means accident — an unfortunate incident, especially one causing harm.

Mandarin shìgù, Cantonese si6 gu3, Japanese じこ, Korean 사고, and Vietnamese sự cố are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄐㄧㄍㄛ/jigo/지고) with the unrelated [[慈姑]] ("arrowhead").
