## Notes
- [旅](../characters/旅.md) "travel" + [鼠](../characters/鼠.md) "rat" — no stand-in relationship applies (旅's own stand-in is [[旅行]]; 鼠's own is [[熊鼠]]).

旅鼠 means lemming.

Mandarin lǚshǔ, Cantonese leoi5 syu2, Japanese たびねずみ, Korean 려서 (North Korean/문화어, retaining the initial ㄹ), and Vietnamese lữ thử are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese also commonly uses the international loanword レミング for this animal. No homophones (注音 ㄌ⼄ㄙ⼄ unique across both words and characters).
