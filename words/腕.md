## Notes
- [腕](../characters/腕%20(char).md) "arm, wrist" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 腕 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

腕 means the arm or wrist.

Mandarin wàn, cantonese wun2, and korean 완 (wan) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading うで (ude, "arm") rather than the on-reading ワン (WAN) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese oản is one of two stored candidates. A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone with [[碗]] ("bowl, dish") — already fully cross-linked from 碗's own perfecting pass.
