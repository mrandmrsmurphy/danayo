## Notes
- [走 (char)](../characters/走%20(char).md) "run" — stand-in for [[走 (char)|走]], which cannot appear independently

走 is 象形 of a running person, arms swinging, drawn above [[止]] ("foot") — a figure caught mid-stride. The compound family stays close to the literal sense of running or hurrying (奔走 "run around, walk quickly," 競走 "a race") and extends into the reduplicated idiom 東奔西走 ("to run about," literally "run east, run west," describing frantic errand-running or being pulled in many directions at once).

Japanese carries two on'yomi, そう (SOU, used here — 競走 kyōsō) and rarer しゅ, alongside the fully productive native 訓読み はし(る) (hashiru), the ordinary everyday verb for "to run." Vietnamese tẩu is the Sino-Vietnamese reading alive in compounds like đào tẩu ("to flee, to escape") — picked over the character's other stored reading rảo, itself a real Vietnamese word (rảo bước, "to walk briskly") but belonging to a related-but-distinct sense rather than being interchangeable with tẩu.
