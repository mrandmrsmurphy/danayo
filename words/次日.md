## Notes
- [次](../characters/次.md) "order" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day"
- Business/logistics register ("next-day delivery"); same meaning as [[翌日]] ("the following day"), which carries a more literary tone.

Mandarin cìrì, Cantonese ci3 jat6, and Korean 차일 (chail) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese じじつ (jijitsu) is likewise compositional (JI from 次's two attested on'yomi + JITSU from 日's), but coincidentally an exact real-world homophone of the unrelated word [[事実]] ("fact, truth") — a genuine Japanese-language ambiguity, though not a Dan'a'yo-internal one, since the two remain distinct vault words. Vietnamese thứ nhật is likewise compositional, from 次's thứ (the "sequence/order" reading, matching this word's sense, as opposed to thớ, an unrelated "grain/texture" reading) and 日's own nhật. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 츼닏 diverges from Sino-Korean 차일 in both syllables. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄜㄋㄧㄊ unique among both words and characters).
