## Notes
- [混](../characters/混.md) "mix, confuse, blend, bumble" + [濁](../characters/濁.md) "muddy, turbid" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 濁 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (混's own stand-in is [[混合]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

混濁 means muddy, turbid.

Mandarin hùnzhuó, Cantonese wan6 zuk6, Japanese こんだく, and Korean 혼탁 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 混's and 濁's own stored citations exactly).
