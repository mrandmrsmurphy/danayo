## Notes
- [懐](../characters/懐%20(char).md) "miss, think of" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

懐 means to miss or think of, as in 懐かしい ("nostalgic") and 懐疑 ("doubt, skepticism").

Mandarin huái, Cantonese waai4, Japanese かい, and Korean 회 are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `korean` had been stored as the literal string "null" — corrected to compositional 회. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄏ⺢ㄧ/hwai/홰) with the unrelated [[話]] ("speak on, talk about").
