## Notes
- [抗](../characters/抗.md) "resist" + [争](../characters/争.md) "fight" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [争](../characters/争.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (抗's own stand-in is [[抵抗]]).

抗争 means to fight or contend.

Mandarin kàngzhēng, Cantonese kong3 zang1, Japanese こうそう, and Vietnamese kháng tranh are all standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 항쟁 uses the native-shifted 쟁 rather than the compositional 장 (from 争's own 諺文), matching the same divergence documented on [[戦争]]. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄫㄐㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
