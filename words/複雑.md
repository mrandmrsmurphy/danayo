## Notes
- [複](../characters/複.md) "repeat, duplicate" + [雑 (char)](../characters/雑%20(char).md) "miscellaneous"

複雑 literally means "manifold and mixed" — many things jumbled together — hence "complicated, intricate." Note that the first character is 複 "multiple, duplicate" (ふく), not the near-homophone 復 "return, again" (also ふく): 複雑 is a frequent misspelling target in Japanese precisely because the two characters share a reading but not a meaning.
The Mandarin field records two readings, fùzá (standard) and fǔzá, which Wiktionary lists as a common variant of the same word. The Valuation page lists 複雑 as "complex" and sets it against its opposite [単純](単純.md) "mere, simple" in its Complexity & Commonality cluster, and Dan'a'yo keeps that sense. Words that share 複 but mean "duplicate" or "compound," not "complicated," are [複写](複写.md) "to copy" and [複合](複合.md) "compound." The alias 複雜 is the traditional spelling, and the Vietnamese form is phức tạp.
