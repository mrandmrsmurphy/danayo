## Notes
- [振](../characters/振%20(char).md) "shake, excite, rouse" + [動](../characters/動%20(char).md) "move" + [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" — no stand-in relationship applies (振's own stand-in is itself; 動's own is itself; 音's own is [[音楽]]).

振動音 means trill or trill consonant (the phonetic term for a rapid vibration of the articulator, as in a rolled r).

Mandarin zhèndòngyīn, Cantonese zan3 dung6 jam1, Japanese しんどうおん, Korean 진동음, and Vietnamese chấn động âm are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `mandarin`/`cantonese`/`japanese`/`korean` had all been contaminated with an incorrect reading (chànyīn·zhànyīn/zin3 jam1/せんどうおん/전동음, the same mistaken 顫-substitution error found on [[振動]] — no substitution was needed here either) and the erroneous `顫音`/`顫動音` aliases and strikethrough Etymology were removed accordingly. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄋㄉㄛㄫㄨㄇ unique across both words and characters).
