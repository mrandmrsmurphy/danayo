## Notes
- [充](../characters/充.md) "to fill" + [満 (char)](../characters/満%20(char).md) "full"

充満 (traditional 充滿) is "to be filled with; to be brimming with; to fill; to permeate." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 充滿 (chōngmǎn, Cantonese cung1 mun5) is "to be brimming with; to be full of," and "to fill; to permeate," and Japanese 充満 (じゅうまん) is "being filled with; be filled with." Wiktionary has no Korean or Vietnamese section, so `korean` 충만 and `vietnamese` sung mãn are compositional readings, not independently checked. It is used in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [平臥即是正義](../translation/平臥即是正義.md), in 充満 欲望 与 興奮 之 生命体 "life forms filled with desire and excitement." Related: [満](満.md) "full."
