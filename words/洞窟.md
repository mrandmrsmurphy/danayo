## Notes
- [洞](../characters/洞.md) "cave" + [窟 (char)](../characters/窟%20(char).md) "cavern" — stand-in for [洞](../characters/洞.md), which cannot appear independently: its meaning (cave) is identical, but 洞 alone is too bound to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. The compound pairs two near-synonyms for cave to make it usable.

Mandarin dòngkū, Cantonese dung6 fat1, Korean 동굴 (donggul), and Japanese どうくつ (dōkutsu) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real everyday word for "cave" in its language. Vietnamese động quật is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, matching 洞's động and 窟's own quật, directly attested as a real term for "cave, cavern."
