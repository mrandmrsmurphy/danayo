## Notes
- [有 (char)](../characters/有%20(char).md) "to have; there is" + [利](../characters/利.md) "advantage; profit; sharpness" — literally "having advantage," hence "advantageous, favorable." Neither character's `stand_in` points here (有's is itself, 利's is [[利潤]]), so no legitimizer relationship applies.

Wiktionary: Chinese "to be advantageous, beneficial, favourable; advantageous, beneficial, favourable"; Japanese "advantageous" and "advantage"; Korean 유리 "advantageous". Compare [[利益]]. Used in `文法 - 02音韻論`.
