## Notes
- [美 (char)](../characters/美%20(char).md) "beautiful, pretty" + [徳 (char)](../characters/徳%20(char).md) "virtue" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both are stand-ins for themselves), so `#cranberry` does not apply.

美徳 means a virtue.

Mandarin měidé, Cantonese mei5 dak1, Japanese びとく (bitoku), and Korean 미덕 (mideok) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 美徳 is itself a common attested word for "virtue" in all four languages. Vietnamese mỹ đức is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading.
