## Notes
- [美 (char)](../characters/美%20(char).md) "beautiful, pretty" + [徳 (char)](../characters/徳%20(char).md) "virtue" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both are stand-ins for themselves), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 徳 is individually `kwin: false`.

美徳 means a virtue.

Mandarin měidé, Cantonese mei5 dak1, Japanese びとく (bitoku), and Korean 미덕 (mideok) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 美徳 is itself a common attested word for "virtue" in all four languages. Vietnamese mỹ đức is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. **Fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字`/`注音` had midug/ㄇㄧㄉㄨㄎ (a d-initial second syllable), mismatching 徳's real t-initial reading (tug/ㄊㄨㄎ) — 諺文 (미툭) had already been correct. Corrected to mitug/ㄇㄧㄊㄨㄎ. The same wrong 注音 was independently duplicated on both `characters/美 (char).md`'s and `characters/徳 (char).md`'s own citations, fixed there too. No homophones.
