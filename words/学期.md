## Notes
- [学](../characters/学.md) "learn" + [期 (char)](../characters/期%20(char).md) "period; time"

学期 is a school term or semester: one of the periods into which a school year is divided. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin as "school term; semester" and lists Korean 학기. Japanese がっき and Cantonese hok6 kei4 match.

It is a division of the [学年](学年.md), the school year, and it frames the [課程](課程.md), the course of study taught within it.
