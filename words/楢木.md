## Notes
- [楢](../characters/楢.md) "oak" + [木](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree, wood" — "oak tree." Their own `stand_in` values: 木's is itself; this word is the `stand_in` that legitimizes 楢.

楢木 is the oak tree. The character 楢 names the Konara oak (*Quercus serrata*) and cannot stand alone, so it takes 木 "tree" like [[柿木]] and [[桧木]]; Japanese writes it なら, and 楢木 itself is not a set word in Mandarin or Cantonese, which say 橡樹 or 櫟樹 instead. This is a deliberate construction under the vault's convention that trees are named **X木**. Compare [[兪木]] (elm), [[樹木]] (tree) and [[楊樹]] (poplar).
