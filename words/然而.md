## Notes
- [然 (char)](../characters/然%20(char).md) "so" + [而 (char)](../characters/而%20(char).md) "and"

然而 is however, but, yet. English Wiktionary has an entry only for Chinese 然而 (Mandarin ránér, Cantonese jin4 ji4), glossed "but; yet". The Japanese ぜんじ and Korean 연이 fields are built from the readings of the two characters. The plain "but" is [但](但.md), and the clausal "and, but" is [而](而.md).
