## Notes
- [採](../characters/採.md) "gather" + [集](../characters/集.md) "collect" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 採 (集's own stand-in is [[集合]]).

採集 means to gather or collect.

Mandarin cǎijí, Cantonese coi2 zaap6, Japanese さいしゅう, Korean 채집, and Vietnamese thái tập are all standard, fully compositional readings.
