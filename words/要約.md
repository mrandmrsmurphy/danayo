## Notes
- [要](../characters/要.md) "essential, want" is separately legitimized by [[重要]]. [約](../characters/約.md) "bind, condense" is separately legitimized by [[約束]]. No cranberry.

Pronunciation fields ('you'yag/욧약/⼄ㄨ⼘ㄎ) are the straightforward concatenation of each constituent's own stored reading — no bug; `kwin: false` already correct (AND-rule: 要 false, 約 true). Mandarin instead reaches for 摘要 or 概要 for "summary," so this word's pedigree runs through Japanese and Korean rather than the Sinitic branch — Korean's 요약 is extremely common and productive (요약하다, "to summarize"; 요약문, "summary text"), which is sufficient on its own to clear the "more than Japanese" bar. Filled blank mandarin (yāoyuē, real legal term meaning "offer" in contract law), cantonese, and vietnamese (yếu ước, compositional, following each constituent's own established compound-context reading — 要 as "yếu" per [[重要]]/[[必要]]/[[需要]], 約 as "ước" per [[条約]]/[[制約]]). Added missing simplified alias 要约. 約 also appears in [[制約]] and [[綽約]] (both already attested), each putting a different shade on the same "binding, condensing" root. No homophones.
