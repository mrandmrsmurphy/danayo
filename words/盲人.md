## Notes
- [盲](../characters/盲.md) "blind" + [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" — a straightforwardly compositional term for a blind person.

Mandarin mángrén, Cantonese maang4 jan4, Japanese もうじん (moujin), and Korean 맹인 (maengin) are all standard, everyday-register terms for a blind person, used interchangeably with more clinical or legal terminology depending on context. Vietnamese manh nhân is compositional; the more common everyday Vietnamese expression is the native người mù ("person [who is] blind").
