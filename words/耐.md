## Notes
- [耐](../characters/耐%20(char).md) "able to tolerate, endure; resistant" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 耐 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

耐 means to be able to tolerate, endure, or resist — as in 忍耐 (patience, endurance).

Mandarin nài, Cantonese noi6, and Korean 내 (nae) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading たえる (taeru, "to endure") rather than the on-readings タイ/ドウ (TAI/DOU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese nại is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading (as in 耐久, "durable"), one of three stored candidates. This word completes a genuine Dan'a'yo homophone with [[乃]] ("but") — the reciprocal callout was already in place there, cross-referenced here.
