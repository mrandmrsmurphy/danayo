## Notes
- [姓](../characters/姓.md) "surname" + [氏](../characters/氏.md) "clan name" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [姓](../characters/姓.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (氏's own stand-in is [[氏族]]).

姓氏 means surname — a near-synonymous doubling of two characters both denoting a family/clan name.

Mandarin xìngshì, Cantonese sing3 si6, Japanese せいし, and Korean 성씨 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese họ is the standard native term (trimmed the contaminating "tên họ," which means "full name," a different concept). No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄫㄙㄧㄜ unique across both words and characters).
