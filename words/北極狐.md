## Notes
"Arctic fox." `korean: 북극여우` and `vietnamese: cáo Bắc Cực` are both real, everyday hybrid terms (Sino "north pole" + native "fox," paralleling [[北極熊]]'s own 북극곰/gấu Bắc Cực).
