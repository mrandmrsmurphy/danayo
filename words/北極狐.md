## Notes
"Arctic fox." Filled the blank `cantonese: bak1 gik6 wu4`. `korean: 북극여우` and `vietnamese: cáo Bắc Cực` are both real, everyday hybrid terms (Sino "north pole" + native "fox," paralleling [[北極熊]]'s own 북극곰/gấu Bắc Cực). **Real content bug found and fixed**: `japanese` had a malformed small-kana rendering ("ほくきよく" — missing the sokuon っ and using a full-size よ instead of small ょ) — corrected to ほっきょくぎつね. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 북긱호 diverges entirely from the hybrid-native Korean field. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄨㄎㄍㄧㄎㄏㄛ unique among both words and characters).
