## Notes
- [顔](../characters/顔.md) "face, reputation" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — no `#cranberry`: 顔's own `stand_in` is [[顔面]], 色's is [[色彩]]; neither points here.

顔色 (yánsè) is a real Mandarin word for "facial expression, complexion," and Cantonese 顔色 (ngaan4sik1) likewise, though both languages also use 面色/脸色 as close synonyms in everyday speech. Japanese かおいろ (kaoiro, using native readings) is the standard word for "complexion, look on one's face." Korean 안색 (ansaek) is likewise the standard term.

Sino-Vietnamese nhan sắc is real and attested, but its sense has narrowed specifically to "(a woman's) beauty, good looks," rather than the broader "complexion/facial expression" sense found in the other four languages.

**Fixed a real bug**: `cantonese` had `yan2 se2`, an invalid non-jyutping romanization unrelated to either constituent's own stored reading — corrected to `ngaan4 sik1`, matching 顔's own `ngaan4` and 色's own `sik1`.
