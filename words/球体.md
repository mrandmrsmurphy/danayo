## Notes
- [球 (char)](../characters/球%20(char).md) "sphere, ball" + [体](../characters/体.md) "group, body, form" — no stand-in relationship applies (球's own stand-in is itself; 体's own is [[体系]]).

球体 means sphere.

Mandarin qiútǐ, Cantonese kau4 tai2, Japanese きゅうたい and Korean 구체 are fully compositional. Vietnamese quả cầu ("ball-fruit", a native compound) is the standard Vietnamese word for "sphere", used in place of a strictly compositional reading.
