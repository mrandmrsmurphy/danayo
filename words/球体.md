## Etymology
- [球 (char)](../characters/球%20(char).md) "sphere, ball" + [体](../characters/体.md) "group, body, form" — no stand-in relationship applies (球's own stand-in is itself; 体's own is [[体系]]).

球体 means sphere.

Mandarin qiútǐ, Cantonese kau4 tai2, Japanese きゅうたい (KYUU+TAI), and Korean 구체 are fully compositional, matching 球's and 体's own stored citations exactly. Vietnamese quả cầu ("ball-fruit," a native compound) is the real, standard Vietnamese word for "sphere," used instead of a strict compositional reading. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼜ㄊㄝㄧ unique across both words and characters).
