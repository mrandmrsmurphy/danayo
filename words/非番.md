## Notes
- [非 (char)](../characters/非%20(char).md) "not, non-" + [番 (char)](../characters/番%20(char).md) "turn, shift" — off duty.

非番 is being off duty: not currently on one's [[番]] (shift), the pair of [[当番]]. Japanese ひばん ("not on duty; not on night duty") and Korean 비번 (非番, "not on duty or watch") are confirmed on the Japanese and Korean Wiktionaries; Korean 비번 is also slang for a 秘番 "password", an unrelated sense. Mandarin has no confirmed entry and uses 下班 or 歇班 (unverified), so `mandarin` and `cantonese` are left blank. Like the other shift words, it belongs to the 番 family, a systematic schema that Dan'a'yo imposes for predictability rather than a claim about the ancient usage.
