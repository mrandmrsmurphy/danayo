## Notes
- [中 (char)](../characters/中%20(char).md) "middle" + [世](../characters/世.md) "age, era" — the historical period between antiquity and the modern era. Attested across Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, and Korean.

**Vietnamese content note**: trung thế (confirmed via web search) is genuinely used in Vietnamese scholarship, but specifically and mainly in discussions of Japanese periodization — Vietnamese sources on Japanese literary/cultural history use 中世/trung thế for the Kamakura–Muromachi span (roughly late 12th–late 16th century), matching this word's own Japanese reading ちゅうせい exactly. For the more general "Middle Ages" sense (especially European medieval history), Vietnamese instead defaults to trung đại or trung cổ — trung thế was still used here since it's the reading directly matching these written characters and remains genuinely attested, not fabricated, just narrower in scope than the broader European-history term. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄨㄫㄙㄝ unique among both words and characters).
