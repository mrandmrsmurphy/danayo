## Notes
- [仲](../characters/仲.md) "mediator" + [裁](../characters/裁.md) "cut; judge, adjudicate"

仲裁 is arbitration: a neutral third party hears a dispute and decides it. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin zhòngcái as "to arbitrate" and Japanese ちゅうさい as "arbitration; mediation; to arbitrate; to mediate". Cantonese zung6 coi4 and Korean 중재 match. Dan'a'yo takes the narrower sense, where the third party decides, as the 裁 "to judge" in it shows.

The word sits between [調解](調解.md), mediation, where the third party only helps the sides to agree, and [裁判](裁判.md), the trial. It is related to [仲介](仲介.md), the mediator who brings parties together, and it ends a dispute as a [和解](和解.md) does, though by a binding decision.
