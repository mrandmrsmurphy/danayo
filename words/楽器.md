## Notes
- [楽](../characters/楽.md) "music" + [器](../characters/器.md) "utensil; instrument"

楽器 is a musical instrument: a device played to make music. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yuèqì as "musical instrument" and Japanese がっき as "musical instrument". Korean 악기 and Vietnamese nhạc khí are the same word. Cantonese ngok6 hei3 matches.

Played by plucking, blowing or striking; examples are the [琴](琴.md), [鼓](鼓.md), [鐘](鐘.md), [笛](笛.md) and [琵琶](琵琶.md). The person who plays is the [音楽家](音楽家.md).
