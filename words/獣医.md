## Notes
- [獣](../characters/獣.md) "beast" + [医](../characters/医.md) "doctor; medicine"

獣医 is a veterinarian, a doctor of animals: [獣](../characters/獣.md) "beast" + [医](../characters/医.md) "doctor". Japanese writes it 獣医 (jūi), with 獸醫 as the old form; Mandarin shòuyī, Cantonese sau3 ji1, and Vietnamese thú y give the same compound.
