## Notes
- [発 (char)](../characters/発%20(char).md) "issue forth, discharge" + [財](../characters/財.md) "wealth" — literally "to make wealth issue forth." 発's own stand-in is [[発]] itself and 財's own is [[財産]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; neither constituent is legitimized by this word.

発財 means to get rich, strike it rich, or prosper financially. It is most famous as the second half of the New Year's greeting 恭喜發財 ("congratulations and prosperity"), heard throughout the Mandarin- and Cantonese-speaking world during Lunar New Year celebrations — Mandarin fācái, Cantonese faat3 coi4.

Vietnamese phát tài is likewise a real, standard phrase used in its own Tết greetings (vạn sự như ý, phát tài phát lộc). Japanese and Korean, by contrast, do not observe Lunar New Year with this greeting tradition, so はつざい (hatsuzai) and 발재 (baljae) here are straightforward compositional readings rather than attested everyday phrases — the cultural practice that keeps 発財 alive as living vocabulary is specifically a Chinese/Vietnamese one.
