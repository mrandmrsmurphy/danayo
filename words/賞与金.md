## Notes
- [賞 (char)](../characters/賞%20(char).md) "reward, prize" + [与 (char)](../characters/与%20(char).md) "give, bestow" + [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "money; metal" — bonus.

賞与金 is a bonus: money bestowed as a reward on top of regular pay, such as a year-end or performance payment. It is "reward-bestowed money," a Dan'a'yo compound of [[賞]] "reward," [[与]] "bestow" and [[金]] "money." The core is the Japanese 賞与 (しょうよ, "bonus"), and Korean 상여금 is the same compound with 金 and the standard Korean bonus word; both readings come from memory, as Wiktionary has no entry for the three-character form. Mandarin has no matching word (it uses 獎金 or 奖金). It differs from [[賞金]], prize money, and from [[賃金]], the wage itself.
