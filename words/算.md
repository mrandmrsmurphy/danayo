## Notes
- [算](../characters/算%20(char).md) "to calculate" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 算 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo. Its 会意 etymology combines 竹 ("bamboo," counting rods) and 廾 ("hands, a grasp") — hands manipulating counting rods.

Mandarin suàn, Cantonese syun3, and Korean 산 (san) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading かぞえる (kazoeru, "to count") rather than the on-reading サン (SAN) for the bare character alone, though SAN remains standard in compounds (算術, さんじゅつ, "arithmetic"). Vietnamese toán is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading — also the everyday Vietnamese word for "mathematics" as a subject. This word is a genuine Dan'a'yo homophone of [[酸]] ("acidic, sour") — coincidental, unrelated in meaning.
