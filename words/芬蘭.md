## Notes
- [芬](../characters/芬.md) "fragrant" is separately legitimized by [[芬芳]]. [蘭](../characters/蘭.md) "orchid" is separately legitimized by [[蘭花]] (蘭 is also used purely for its sound to transliterate "-land" in country names). No cranberry.

**Found and fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` all had a p-initial reading (Punlan/푼란/ㄆㄨㄋㄌㄚㄋ), mismatching 芬's real f-initial reading (fun/뿐/ㄈㄨㄋ) — the same ㄈ→ㅍ (should be ㅃ) confusion class as the 福-family bug — corrected to funlan/뿐란/ㄈㄨㄋㄌㄚㄋ (also normalizing the stray capitalization). `kwin: false` is correct per the AND-rule (芬 individually false despite 蘭's own `kwin: true`). Fixed cantonese's stray space. As a proper place name, mandarin Fēnlán, japanese フィンランド, korean 핀란드, and vietnamese Phần Lan all legitimately hold the real attested name/transliteration rather than a compositional gloss.
