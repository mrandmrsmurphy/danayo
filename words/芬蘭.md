## Notes
- [芬](../characters/芬.md) "fragrant" is separately legitimized by [[芬芳]]. [蘭](../characters/蘭.md) "orchid" is separately legitimized by [[蘭花]] (蘭 is also used purely for its sound to transliterate "-land" in country names). No cranberry.

As a proper place name, mandarin Fēnlán, japanese フィンランド, korean 핀란드, and vietnamese Phần Lan all legitimately hold the real attested name/transliteration rather than a compositional gloss.
