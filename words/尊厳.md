## Notes
- [尊](../characters/尊.md) "revere; honored" + [厳 (char)](../characters/厳%20(char).md) "strict; rigorous; stern" — this word is the stand-in for [尊](../characters/尊.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

尊厳 pairs 尊's "to hold in high honor/reverence" with 厳's "rigor, strictness," yielding "dignity" — a worth so weighty it demands to be treated with severity and gravity rather than casualness. This differs from more purely relational honorifics like [[尊敬]] (respect directed at someone) — 尊厳 is a quality a person or thing *possesses*, inherent rather than conferred by another's regard.

Japanese そんげん, Korean 존엄, and Vietnamese tôn nghiêm are all standard, non-drifted readings, all carrying the same "inherent dignity/worth" sense (as in Japanese 人間の尊厳, "human dignity"). HSK level 4 already correct. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄛㄋ⼄ㄇ unique across both words and characters).
