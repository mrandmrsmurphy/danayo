## Notes
- [変 (char)](../characters/変%20(char).md) "change" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — discoloration.

変色 means a change of color: the discoloration of metal by [[腐食]], of paper or teeth by age, of meat by spoilage, or of leaves by the season. It is not a deliberate act, and it is not specifically fading (see [[退色]]). Mandarin biànsè and Cantonese bin3 sik1 also use it figuratively, of one's expression changing in fear or anger, and Vietnamese biến sắc has that figurative sense (to turn pale). Japanese へんしょく and Korean 변색 are the standard readings.
