## Notes
- [遠 (char)](../characters/遠%20(char).md) "far" — the bare character used directly as a word, its own `stand_in`.

遠 means far: distant in space, time, or relationship (遙遠, "remote"; 遠方, "distant place"; 永遠, "eternal"; 遠親, "distant relative"), and by extension profound or far-reaching (深遠, "profound"; 遠見, "foresight"). The simplified form 远 is listed as an alias. The graph is a 形声 compound of 辶 "walk" with the phonetic 袁. Its antonym is [[近]].

Mandarin yuǎn, Cantonese jyun5, Japanese えん (遠足/えんそく, "excursion"; 永遠/えいえん, "eternity"; 遠近/えんきん, "far and near"), Korean 원 (원거리, 영원), and Vietnamese viễn (viễn thông, "telecommunications"; viễn cảnh, "prospect") are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native adjective 遠い (とおい) for the everyday word, so on'yomi えん is bound to compounds; Korean has native 멀다.
