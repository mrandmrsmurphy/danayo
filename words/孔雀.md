## Notes
- [孔 (char)](../characters/孔%20(char).md) "cavity" + [雀](../characters/雀.md) "sparrow"

孔雀 is the peacock, or peafowl. The same compound is the word for the bird in Chinese, Japanese (kujaku), Korean (gongjak), and Vietnamese (khổng tước). 雀 alone means sparrow; its stand-in is [麻雀鳥](麻雀鳥.md), so this compound is not a stand-in for either character.
