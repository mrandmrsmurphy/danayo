## Notes
- [偏 (char)](../characters/偏%20(char).md) "lopsided; biased" + [見 (char)](../characters/見%20(char).md) "view"

偏見 is prejudice or bias: a view formed from a one-sided standpoint, without fair consideration. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin piānjiàn as "prejudice; bias" and Japanese へんけん as "prejudice". Korean 편견 and Cantonese pin1 gin3 match.

It is the attitude that leads to [差別](差別.md), discrimination. Its opposite is a fair, open mind.
