## Notes
- [占 (char)](../characters/占%20(char).md) "occupy"

占 is 指事: an oracle-bone crack (卜) combined with an indicator marking where a diviner reads and interprets it — the "occupy" sense (a position taken, a claim staked) developed alongside 占's other meaning "to divine, foretell," both sharing the image of laying claim to information or territory. It appears in [[占領]] ("to capture, occupy, seize") and [[占拠]] ("to occupy, hold [a place]").

Mandarin zhàn, Cantonese zim1, and Japanese せん (sen, on'yomi, fully productive: 占領 せんりょう "occupation") alongside native し(める) (shi(meru), "to occupy, account for [a proportion]") all preserve the "occupy" sense. Korean 점 (jeom) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 점령하다 (jeomnyeonghada, built on the same Sino-Korean root). Vietnamese chiếm is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in chiếm đóng ("to occupy [militarily]") and chiếm hữu ("to possess, take possession of") — the character page's own Vietnamese field stores an unusually large number of near-homophone candidates (chem, chiêm, chàm, chím, chúm, chăm, chầm, chắm, chằm, chẻm, chễm, coi, giếm, xem), almost all of which look like corpus noise; `chiếm` alone is the genuine reading and was already correctly the value used here.
