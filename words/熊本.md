## Notes
- [熊](../characters/熊%20(char).md) "bear" + [本](../characters/本%20(char).md) "foundation"

熊本 is Kumamoto, written "bear origin". The older spelling was 隈本 (Kumamoto, "corner origin"), which Katō Kiyomasa changed to 熊本 around 1600 (from memory, not re-checked). The reading くまもと is native: 熊 (くま, "bear") plus 本 (もと, "origin").

Kumamoto is in central Kyushu, with its capital Kumamoto, whose castle was damaged in the earthquakes of 2016. Mount Aso, with one of the largest calderas in the world, lies in the east. The mascot Kumamon is the prefecture's best-known export.

In Dan'a'yo 熊本 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 구마모토 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
