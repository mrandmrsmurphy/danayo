## Notes
- [及 (char)](../characters/及%20(char).md) "reach out to" — the stand-in that legitimizes the character 及 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

及 is the verb "to reach; to extend to; to reach the level of". The character is a 会意 compound of 人 (person) and 又 (hand), a hand grabbing a person, so the core sense is reaching something and getting hold of it. English Wiktionary lists three groups of senses: to reach or extend to (觸手可及, "within reach"; 及格, "to pass", that is, to reach the passing grade); to catch up or be in time (來得及, "to be in time"; 及時, "timely"; 後悔莫及, "too late to regret"); and to be equal or comparable (愚不可及, "foolish beyond compare"). It suggests the word may be related to Burmese ကပ် "to arrive at" and gives 暨, 迄 and 既 as possible departing-tone relatives.

In the vault 及 is mostly met as a bound root inside compounds: [[普及]] (to spread widely), [[波及]] (to spread to), [[言及]] (to mention), [[追及]] (to catch up with), [[不及]] (not to reach), [[及第]] (to pass an imperial examination) and the place name [[埃及]] (Egypt). Japanese きゅう and Korean 급 are the Sino-Xenic readings used in such compounds; the native Japanese およぶ and Korean 미치다 are the free verbs.

No other word shares the 注音 ㄍㄨㄆ (checked 2026-10-12).
