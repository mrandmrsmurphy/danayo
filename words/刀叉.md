## Etymology
- [刀](../characters/刀.md) "blade, knife" + [叉 (char)](../characters/叉%20(char).md) "fork, prong, tine"

## Notes
"Knife and fork" — the everyday Chinese term (composed of one character each from 刀子 "knife" and 叉子 "fork"), used specifically of Western-style dining. Japanese and Korean instead directly borrow the English loanword phrase (confirmed via web search) rather than using a native/Sino compound — ナイフとフォーク, 나이프와 포크. `vietnamese: dao nĩa` is likewise the everyday native Vietnamese term (native words, not a Sino-Vietnamese calque), well known from Western-dining etiquette.
