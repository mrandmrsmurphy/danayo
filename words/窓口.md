## Notes
- [窓](../characters/窓.md) "window" + [口 (char)](../characters/口%20(char).md) "mouth, opening" — 窓's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 口's own is [[口]] itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 窓 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

窓口 names a window in the sense of a service counter or teller window — the opening through which business is conducted, as at a bank, ticket office, or government window (窓口業務, "counter service"), and by extension a point of contact or channel for information or negotiation. This is distinct from a plain architectural window, for which Japanese instead most commonly uses 窓 alone, and Korean uses the more common everyday 창문 (built from 窓+門, "door," rather than this word's own 窓+口).

Mandarin chuāngkǒu, Cantonese coeng1 hau2, and Japanese まどぐち (madoguchi) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 창구 (changgu) specifically preserves the "service counter" sense.
