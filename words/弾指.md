## Notes
- [弾](../characters/弾.md) "bullet; to flick" + [指](../characters/指.md) "finger" — no stand-in relationship applies (弾's own stand-in is [[弾丸]]; 指's is [[手指]]).

Wiktionary (彈指, Chinese): figuratively "in the blink of an eye; in a flash"; obsolete Buddhist sense, a small unit of time (7.2 or 0.25 seconds depending on source). Japanese Wiktionary (弾指 だんし): Buddhist snapping of the fingers for warning, delight or assent; a very short unit of time; also "to reject, exclude".

Dan'a'yo uses it as the stem of the phonetic term 弾指子音, "flap consonant" (`文法 - 02音韻論`): the tongue-tip flicks once, like a finger snap. Contrast [[振動音]], the trill.
