## Notes
- [存](../characters/存.md) "exist" + [亡](../characters/亡.md) "perish, lose" — "survival" (archaic), a binome contrasting continued existence against destruction (危急存亡の秋, "a critical moment of survival or ruin"). **Real gap found and fixed on [[存]]'s own page**: its `vietnamese` field was missing tồn entirely, its most common Sino-Vietnamese reading (存在 tồn tại, "to exist") — added. `vietnamese: tồn vong` is a directly attested, well-known standard term (vấn đề tồn vong, "a matter of survival"). Filled blank `cantonese: cyun4 mong4` and `japanese: そんぼう`. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 존망 matches Sino-Korean 존망 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄛㄋㄇㄚㄫ unique among both words and characters).
