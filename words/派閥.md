## Notes
- [派](../characters/派.md) "branch" + [閥](../characters/閥.md) "clan"

派閥 is a faction, a clique. English Wiktionary glosses Japanese はばつ as "faction; clique" and lists Korean 파벌. It has no Chinese entry, so the Mandarin and Cantonese fields are built from the readings of the two characters.
