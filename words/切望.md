## Notes
- [切 (char)](../characters/切%20(char).md) "earnest; keen" + [望](../characters/望.md) "hope; look"

切望 is to long for earnestly: to wish with urgency for something that is hoped to come. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin qièwàng as "to eagerly anticipate; to impatiently wait for" and Japanese せつぼう as "earnest desire; long for, be eager to". Cantonese cit3 mong6 and Korean 절망 match, the latter being a homophone of the unrelated 絶望, despair.

It is wishing with urgency, steadier than [渇望](渇望.md), craving, and hotter than [願望](願望.md), wish. Its opposite is [失望](失望.md), disappointment.
