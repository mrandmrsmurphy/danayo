## Notes
- [巣](../characters/巣.md) "nest" + [穴](../characters/穴.md) "hole; cave" — no stand-in relationship applies (巣's own `stand_in` is [[鳥巣]], 穴's is [[洞穴]]).

巣穴 names an animal's burrow or den — a hole or tunnel dug or used as a dwelling, refuge from predators and weather. Distinct from [[巣窟]] (巣+窟, "hideout, den" — often figurative, a criminal gang's hideout — a different compound, not a variant spelling of this one).

Japanese すあな (a native kun'yomi compound; the on'yomi そうけつ also exists as an alternate reading), Korean 소혈, and Vietnamese sào huyệt are all standard readings. No HSK level is given for the compound. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄨㄏ⼔ㄊ unique across both words and characters).

**Correction (2026-08-27)**: `aliases` had wrongly listed 巢窟 alongside the genuine variant 巢穴 — 巢窟/巣窟 is actually a separate, distinct word with its own page and its own different reading (jaukod/잣콛), not a spelling variant of this one; removed. `korean` had similarly been contaminated with "; 소굴" appended (소굴 is [[巣窟]]'s own real Korean reading) — corrected to plain 소혈.
