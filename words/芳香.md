## Notes
- [芳](../characters/芳.md) "fragrant" + [香](../characters/香.md) "fragrant" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [芳](../characters/芳.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (香's own stand-in is [[香気]]).

芳香 means fragrant, aromatic, or perfumed.

Mandarin fāngxiāng, Cantonese fong1 hoeng1, Japanese ほうこう, Korean 방향, and Vietnamese phương hương are all standard, fully compositional readings. Three-way Dan'a'yo-level homophone with [[方響]] "hōkyō (metallophone)" and [[方向]] "direction" (all 注音 ㄈㄚㄫㄏ⼘ㄫ), fully cross-referenced on all three pages.
