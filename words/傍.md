## Notes
- [傍 (char)](../characters/傍%20(char).md) "beside; close"

傍 combines [[人]] ("person") with phonetic [[旁]] ("side"). It means "beside, close by, near" — used as a preposition/coverb (在...傍, "beside...") and in compounds like 傍晚 (bàngwǎn, "toward evening, dusk," literally "close to evening") and 傍观 (bàngguān, "to look on from the side, to be a bystander").

Mandarin bàng, Cantonese bong6, and Japanese ぼう/ほう (bō/hō, on'yomi) alongside native かたわ(ら) (katawa(ra), "beside, nearby," the everyday word) all preserve the sense. Korean 방 (bang) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 곁 (gyeot, "the side, nearby," the everyday native noun). Vietnamese bàng is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in bàng quan ("indifferent, to look on as a bystander," literally "beside-looking"); the character page's other three stored candidates (phàng, phành, vàng) are unrelated and look like corpus noise.

This word is part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read pang/팡/ㄆㄚㄫ): [[紡]] ("spin [yarn]") and [[肪]] ("fat"), both still awaiting their own turn in this sweep — the reciprocal half of this callout will be completed on each when it comes up.
