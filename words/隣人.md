## Notes
- [隣 (char)](../characters/隣%20(char).md) "neighboring, next door" + [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person"

隣人 means neighbor, or by extension a neighborhood/local community. Mandarin 鄰人/邻人 (línrén), Cantonese 鄰人 (leon4jan4), Japanese 隣人 (りんじん), and Sino-Vietnamese lân nhân (a real attested compound, though everyday Vietnamese favors the more common láng giềng or hàng xóm) all use this compositional form.

Korean instead uses the native word 이웃 (the same as 隣's own stored native gloss) rather than a Sino-Korean compound — 隣/鄰's own hanja reading 린 does appear in real compounds like 근린 (近隣, "vicinity"), 인접 (隣接, "adjacency"), and 선린 (善隣, "good-neighborliness"), but "隣人" itself is not established as a standalone Sino-Korean word the way it is in the other four languages.
