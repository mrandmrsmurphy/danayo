## Notes
- [潜](../characters/潜.md) "latent, hidden" + [水](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [艦](../characters/艦.md) "warship" — no stand-in relationship applies (潜's own stand-in is [[潜在]]; 水's own is itself; 艦's own is itself).

潜水艦 means submarine (the warship).

Japanese せんすいかん is fully compositional, matching 潜's/水's/艦's own citations. Fixed real bugs: `mandarin` had been doubled and contaminated with the readings of the near-synonym aliases 潛水艇/潜水艇 (which use 艇 "small boat," not this word's own 艦 "warship") — corrected to qiánshuǐjiàn, matching 艦's own jiàn. `cantonese` was similarly contaminated (teng5 from 艇 instead of laam6 from 艦) — corrected to cim4 seoi2 laam6. `korean` was a garbled comma-joined pair (잠수함,잠수정) mixing the compositional 艦-based form with the 艇-based 잠수정 — corrected to 잠수함. Vietnamese tàu ngầm is the real, standard native term ("submerged boat"), kept as-is. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄇㄙㄨㄏㄚㄇ unique across both words and characters).
