## Notes
- **Genuine `#cranberry`**: both [茉](../characters/茉.md) and [莉](../characters/莉.md) — each meaning "white jasmine" alone but never independently attested — have their own `stand_in` pointing to this exact compound (full transitivity). Borrowed from Sanskrit मालती (mālatī) or मल्लिका (mallikā), "a kind of jasmine."

All other-language fields (mandarin mòlì, japanese まつり, korean 말리, vietnamese mạt lị) confirmed standard, fully compositional, and genuinely attested.
