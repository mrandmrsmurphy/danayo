## Notes
- [五 (char)](../characters/五%20(char).md) "five/fifth" + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — no stand-in relationship applies (both characters are independent Dan'a'yo entries, each its own stand-in).
- [Calendar](../lexipedia/Calendar.md)

五月 names May, the fifth month of the Gregorian calendar.

Japanese ごがつ, Korean 오월, and Vietnamese tháng năm are all standard readings. Fixed missing space in `cantonese` (ng5jut6 → ng5 jut6) and relative-path links. **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 五月 and [[午月]] (the zodiac "horse month" of the traditional lunar calendar) share an identical reading (오웓/'o'wed/ㄛ·⼔ㄊ) — added reciprocal homophone callouts to both pages.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `kwin` was stamped `true`, but the word's own 諺文 (오웓) and its own `korean` field (오월) actually differ in the second syllable's coda (웓 vs 월) — corrected to `false`, consistent with the AND-rule (5's own kwin true + 月's own kwin false = false).
