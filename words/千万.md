## Notes
- [千](../characters/千.md) "thousand" + [万](../characters/万.md) "ten-thousand" — ten million, 10,000,000.

Literally "a thousand myriad" (10⁷) — the last myriad-multiple before 億 (10⁸, see [[一億]]). Mandarin qiānwàn also has a second, adverbial sense "by all means, absolutely" (as in 千万别 "whatever you do, don't"), where it stands for "ten thousand times over"; that sense is a figurative use and does not change the numeral. Japanese せんまん and Korean 천만 are the plain concatenations. `vietnamese` is left blank: everyday Vietnamese says mười triệu, and a bare Sino-Vietnamese calque would be a guess. Compare [[百万]], [[十万]] and [[一億]].
