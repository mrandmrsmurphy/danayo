## Notes
- [女 (char)](../characters/女%20(char).md) "woman" + [優](../characters/優.md) "actor, performer" — "actress, female artist" (the standard Japanese word, じょゆう). `korean: 녀우` is the compositional North Korean reading (the South Korean form 여우 coincidentally collides with the native word for "fox", so modern South Korean usually prefers 여배우, with 俳 "actor", to avoid the ambiguity). `vietnamese: nữ ưu` is compositional; not independently confirmed as a fixed term — everyday Vietnamese says nữ diễn viên (native, "female performer") instead (domain knowledge, not independently confirmed by search).
