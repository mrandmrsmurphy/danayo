## Notes
- [女 (char)](../characters/女%20(char).md) "woman" + [優](../characters/優.md) "actor, performer" — "actress, female artist" (the standard Japanese word, じょゆう). **Korean collision noted, not an error**: `korean: 여우` is the correct compositional Sino-Korean reading, but it coincidentally collides with the native word for "fox" — modern Korean more often prefers 여배우 (with 俳, "actor") to sidestep this ambiguity, though bare 여우 for "actress" is still attested, especially in older or Japanese-influenced usage. `vietnamese: nữ ưu` is compositional; not independently confirmed as a fixed term — everyday Vietnamese says nữ diễn viên (native, "female performer") instead (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 느웃 diverges from Sino-Korean 여우 in both syllables.
