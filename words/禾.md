## Notes
- [禾 (char)](../characters/禾%20(char).md) "grain; cereal" — stand-in for [[禾 (char)|禾]], which cannot appear independently

禾 is 象形 of a grain plant, stalk bent under the weight of its ripening head — the radical anchoring this vault's cereal/crop vocabulary, most visibly in [[禾稲]] ("rice"), the character's own page's sole documented compound. **Note**: `characters/禾 (char).md` remains genuinely unperfected (blank `pos`, `## Notes` holding only dangling CC-initial/final wikilinks plus one bare compound bullet, no `date-last-perfect`) — a fourth character page found in this state this sweep, after [[玄]]/[[皿]]/[[矢]] — flagged for the character-perfection sweep rather than rebuilt here.

**Content bug found and fixed, part of a larger three-way discovery**: the word's own `注音` was the non-canonical literal spelling `ㄏㄨㄚ`, the same bug class already fixed on [[火]] — corrected to `ㄏ⺢`, matching the character's own already-correct stored value. Fixing this exposed the real reason this reading's homophone group had gone incomplete: **[[化]] ("-ification"), perfected back on 2026-07-26, had already correctly identified and anticipated a three-way group with both [[火]] and this word — but 禾's and 火's own `注音` fields were still using the wrong non-canonical spelling at the time, which hid them from a plain-string reading search until now.** Corrected `化`'s own `注音` to `ㄏ⺢` as well, and added the missing reciprocal callout to `火` (perfected two iterations ago in this sweep, but never given its own half of this callout). All three pages — 火, 化, 禾 — now correctly cross-link each other.

Japanese carries a single on'yomi か (KA), overshadowed almost entirely in ordinary use by the native 訓読み いね (ine), the everyday word for a rice plant while still standing (稲, the more usual modern character, is itself often analyzed as 禾 + a phonetic component). Vietnamese hoà survives mainly in learned botanical/agricultural register rather than everyday speech. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 화 (hwa, from Middle Chinese 匣母 ɣ + 戈一合韻 uɑ) coincides exactly with Sino-Korean 화, the same convergence shared by [[火]] and [[化]].
