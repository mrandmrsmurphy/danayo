## Notes
- [禾 (char)](../characters/禾%20(char).md) "grain; cereal" — stand-in for [[禾 (char)|禾]], which cannot appear independently

禾 is 象形 of a grain plant, stalk bent under the weight of its ripening head — the radical anchoring this vault's cereal/crop vocabulary, most visibly in [[禾稲]] ("rice"), the character's own page's sole documented compound.

Japanese carries a single on'yomi か (KA), overshadowed almost entirely in ordinary use by the native 訓読み いね (ine), the everyday word for a rice plant while still standing (稲, the more usual modern character, is itself often analyzed as 禾 + a phonetic component). Vietnamese hoà survives mainly in learned botanical/agricultural register rather than everyday speech.
