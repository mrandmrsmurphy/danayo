## Notes
- [山](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain" + [形](../characters/形%20(char).md) "shape"

山形 is Yamagata, written "mountain shape". It is the name of a castle town in the interior, and of the prefecture formed in 1876. The Japanese reading やまがた is the native reading of 山 (やま) and 形 (かた).

Yamagata lies in the northwest of the Tōhoku region, with its capital Yamagata. It is known for cherries, the three sacred peaks of Dewa Sanzan, the ski resorts of Zaō, and the mountain temple Risshaku-ji that Matsuo Bashō visited on his northern journey.

In Dan'a'yo 山形 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 야마가타 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
