## Notes
- [焼](characters/焼.md) "burn" + [酎](characters/酎.md) "saké, strong liquor" — 酎's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 焼's is [[燃焼]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 酎 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

焼酎 covers the family of East Asian distilled rice/grain liquors known by this name (or its cognates) in each language: shōchū in Japan, soju in Korea, shaojiu in China.

Mandarin shāozhòu, Japanese しょうちゅう (SHOU+CHUU, the real Japanese word "shōchū"), and Korean 소주 (the real Korean word "soju") were already correct. Filled the blank `cantonese` with siu1 zau6, fully compositional. Vietnamese "soju" reflects the common Vietnamese practice of borrowing the Korean name directly for this drink. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼄ㄨㄐㄨㄛ unique across both words and characters).
