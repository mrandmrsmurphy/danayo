## Notes
- [帰](../characters/帰.md) "return; revert" + [還](../characters/還.md) "return; repatriate" — no stand-in relationship applies (帰's own `stand_in` is [[回帰]], 還's is [[送還]]).

帰還 is a near-synonym doubling, both halves independently meaning "to return" — used of people returning home or from expedition (帰還兵 "returning soldier," a spacecraft's re-entry), and of returning objects to their original owner or state.

Japanese きかん, Korean 귀환, and Vietnamese quy hoàn are all standard, non-drifted readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄨㄧㄏ⺢ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
