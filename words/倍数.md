## Notes
- [倍 (char)](../characters/倍%20(char).md) "times (stand-in: itself)" + [数](../characters/数.md) "count; number (stand-in: [[計数]])"

Wiktionary lists 倍數 in Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese, and 倍数 in Chinese and Japanese. It is a number that another divides into evenly, beside [[約数]].
