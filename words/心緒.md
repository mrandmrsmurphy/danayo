## Notes
- [心](../characters/心%20(char).md) "heart" + [緒](../characters/緒.md) "state of mind" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [緒](../characters/緒.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry, which is too bounded to stand alone.

心緒 means feelings or state of mind — the emotional or mental disposition one is in.

Mandarin xīnxù, Cantonese sam1 seoi5, Japanese しんしょ, Korean 심서, and Vietnamese tâm tự are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese`, added missing `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄇ·ㄙ⼄ unique across both words and characters).
