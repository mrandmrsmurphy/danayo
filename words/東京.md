## Notes
- [東](../characters/東.md) "east" + [京](../characters/京.md) "capital" — no stand-in relationship applies (東's own stand-in is [[東方]]; 京's own is [[京城]]).

東京 means Tokyo.

Mandarin Dōngjīng, Cantonese dung1 ging1, Japanese とうきょう, and Vietnamese Đông Kinh are all standard, fully compositional readings. Simplified `korean` to the single compositional Sino-Korean exonym 동경 (dropped the modern loanword transliteration 도쿄, which is not compositional), matching the vault's convention for sibling city names like [[北京]]. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/`aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄫㄍ⼶ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
