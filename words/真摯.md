## Notes
- [真 (char)](../characters/真%20(char).md) "true" + [摯](../characters/摯.md) "sincere, cordial" — stand-in for [[摯]], which cannot appear independently.

真摯 describes sincerity and earnestness in attitude or intent — 真摯な態度 ("a sincere attitude"), 真摯に向き合う ("to face something earnestly/honestly") — closer to "earnest, in good faith" than to the more general "genuine" also covered by near-synonyms like 誠実 or 真心.

Japanese しんし and Korean 진지 are both standard compositional Sino-readings. `vietnamese: chân chí` is compositional, matching both characters' own confirmed readings; not independently attested as a fixed Vietnamese dictionary compound (Vietnamese generally reaches for chân thành instead, the same "sincere" concept built from 真誠). **Previously flagged, now resolved**: this note once observed that `真`'s own stored `vietnamese` field listed only Nôm forms (chan, chang) rather than the standard Hán Việt "chân" — re-checking fresh, `真`'s own character page was independently perfected on 2026-07-23 (after this word's own 2026-05-19 stamp) and already includes chân; the discrepancy no longer exists. No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄋㄐㄧㄜ unique across both words and characters).
