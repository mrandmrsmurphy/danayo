## Notes
- [真 (char)](../characters/真%20(char).md) "true" + [摯](../characters/摯.md) "sincere, cordial"

真摯 means sincere, genuine, earnest. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 真摯 (alias 真挚; Mandarin zhēnzhì, Cantonese zan1 zi3) as "sincere; genuine; earnest", and Japanese しんし as "sincere" and "sincerity; earnestness". Korean 진지 and Vietnamese chân chí are built from the readings of the two characters.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [摯](../characters/摯.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
