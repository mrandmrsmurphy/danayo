## Notes
- [規](../characters/規.md) "rules, regulations" is separately legitimized by [[規律]]. [則](../characters/則.md) "rule" is separately legitimized by [[法則]]. No cranberry.

規則 is a stipulated rule or regulation, one that has been laid down by an authority. It is distinct from 原則, the principle one appeals to, and from 規範, the general norm that specific rules spell out. Mandarin guīzé, Japanese きそく, Korean 규칙 and Vietnamese quy tắc are the real common words for "rule, regulation" in each language, and all are compositional.
