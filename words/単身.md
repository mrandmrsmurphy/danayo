## Notes
"Alone, single, unaccompanied" (単身赴任, "to be posted away from one's family"). **Real content bug found and fixed**: `korean` had a native gloss ("홀몸," native Korean for "alone, single") mixed into the reading field as "단신,홀몸" — trimmed to 단신 alone. **Content added**: `vietnamese: đơn thân`, one of the most common everyday Vietnamese words for this concept (mẹ đơn thân, "single mother"), a direct match. This also corrected `kwin` (previously false, unset before the fix): with the clean reading, Dan'a'yo 단신 now matches Sino-Korean 단신 exactly, so `kwin: true`. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄋㄙㄧㄋ unique among both words and characters).
