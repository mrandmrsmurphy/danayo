## Notes
- [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year" + [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "money; metal" — pension.

年金 is a pension: a regular payment made over a person's later years, usually after retirement ([[退職]]), or any fixed annual payment (an annuity). It is "year-money." Mandarin niánjīn, Cantonese nin4 gam1 and Japanese ねんきん are confirmed; Korean 연금 comes from memory. In Hokkien the same characters also mean an annual salary, a sense Dan'a'yo does not take. Dan'a'yo 年金 and [[軟禁]] (house arrest) are homophones; Korean 연금 is likewise the reading of both, with no relation between them. Compare [[賃金]] and [[収入]].
