## Notes
- [省 (char)](../characters/省%20(char).md) "government ministry" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character 省 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

省 is a government ministry. English Wiktionary lists, among the many senses of the character, a historical "department (of a government)", as in 中書省 (the Central Secretariat), with the sub-sense "ministry of the Japanese or North Korean government", as in 外務省 (the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Japan). Dan'a'yo takes that sense. The Buildings page lists it as "ministry".

The character's other senses are not the Dan'a'yo word. English Wiktionary gives "to omit", "to reduce; to simplify", "to save; to economize" (省電, "to conserve electricity"), a literary "palace" and "province" (福建省, "Fujian Province"), with "provincial capital" by extension. In Dan'a'yo a Chinese province is named without the final 省, as the China page explains, so the Mandarin sense "province" does not fall to this word.

Mandarin shěng and Cantonese saang2 are as at English Wiktionary; Korean 성 and Japanese しょう are the Sino-Xenic readings. I have not checked whether Korean or Vietnamese use 省 for "ministry".

No other word shares the 注音 ㄙ⼶ㄫ (checked 2026-10-12); the character page 省 does.
