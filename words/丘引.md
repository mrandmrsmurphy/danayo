## Notes
- [丘 (char)](../characters/丘%20(char).md) "hill" + [引 (char)](../characters/引%20(char).md) "to pull, draw out" — the standard classical/scientific term for "earthworm" (蚯蚓), 丘引 being an in-vault graphemic respelling using two already-legal Dan'a'yo characters.

Mandarin qiūyǐn, Cantonese jau1 jan5, and Korean 구인 are all standard compositional readings. Japanese みみず is the real, everyday native word for earthworm, used instead of a rare compositional きゅういん. Vietnamese giun đất ("earthworm," a real native compound) is likewise used instead of a Sino-Vietnamese calque. `kwin` is false (AND-rule: 丘's own kwin is false, which alone determines the product regardless of 引's own true).
