## Notes
- From [[甚]] "extremely/very" + [[様 (char)|様]] "appearance/manner": literally "what kind of appearance," functioning as an interrogative meaning "how" or "of what sort." Parallels the Cantonese/Mandarin 怎樣 (zěnyàng) and the Classical Chinese 如何 pattern. The Dan'a'yo form follows the Cantonese usage most closely (點樣 / 甚樣). 甚's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 様's own is itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply.
- Stand-in for the bound character [[甚]].

Mandarin shènyàng, Cantonese sam6 joeng4, Japanese じんよう (JIN+YOU), Korean 심양, and Vietnamese thậm dạng are all fully compositional, matching 甚's and 様's own stored citations exactly. Removed the redundant duplicate `品詞` field (kept `pos`). No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄇ⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
