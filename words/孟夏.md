## Notes
- [孟](../characters/孟.md) "eldest, first" + [夏](../characters/夏%20(char).md) "summer" — no stand-in relationship applies (孟 is a proper-noun-only character with no specific stand-in word; 夏's own stand-in is itself).

First month of summer. Heat begins to build; spring has fully given way. Corresponds to [[巳月]].

Mandarin mèngxià, Cantonese maang6 haa6, Korean 맹하, Japanese もうか, and Vietnamese Mạnh Hạ are all standard, fully compositional readings.
