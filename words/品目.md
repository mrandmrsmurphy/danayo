## Etymology
- [品 (char)](../characters/品%20(char).md) "article, item" + [目 (char)](../characters/目%20(char).md) "eye, category" (used here in its "listed entry, category heading" sense, as in 目次 "table of contents")

## Notes
- "Article, item, inventory listing" — a catalog entry or line-item in a list of goods/categories. `vietnamese: phẩm mục` is a directly attested standard Sino-Vietnamese term with the same "catalog item/category" sense. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 품묵 diverges from Sino-Korean 품목 in the second syllable.
