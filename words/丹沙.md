## Notes
- [丹](../characters/丹.md) "cinnabar red" + [沙 (char)](../characters/沙%20(char).md) "sand, grit" — this word is the `stand_in` legitimizer for [[丹]], which cannot appear independently.

丹沙 (cinnabar, mercury sulfide) is one of the oldest-named minerals in the Sinosphere, historically the primary source of both the vermilion pigment and — through Daoist alchemical elixir-making — of mercury itself, a practice documented to have poisoned at least five Tang-dynasty emperors seeking immortality. It shares its exact referent with two synonymous compounds also attested in Chinese sources, 朱砂 ("vermilion sand," emphasizing the color) and 辰砂 ("Chen-prefecture sand," naming its most famous historical source in Hunan) — three different descriptive names for the same substance, none more "correct" than the others.
