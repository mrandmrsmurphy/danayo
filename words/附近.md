## Notes
- [附 (char)](../characters/附%20(char).md) "affix, adhere" + [近 (char)](../characters/近%20(char).md) "near"

附近 means the vicinity or nearby area — "attached-and-near," combining a sense of proximate attachment with plain nearness. Mandarin 附近 (fùjìn), Cantonese 附近 (fu6gan6), Japanese 附近/付近 (ふきん), Korean 부근 (bugeun), and Sino-Vietnamese phụ cận are all everyday, high-frequency words for "nearby area" across the CJKV sphere, largely interchangeable with the more colloquial 近く/근처/gần đây alternatives in casual speech.
