## Notes
- [借 (char)](../characters/借%20(char).md) "borrow; lend"

借 is 形声: semantic [[人]] ("person") + phonetic [[昔]]. Its core sense covers both directions of a loan — "to borrow" and "to lend" — disambiguated by context or by the coverb pattern (跟...借, "to borrow from..."; 借給..., "to lend to..."). An extended sense, "to make use of, to take advantage of (an opportunity or pretext)," survives as an alternate form of [[藉]] ([[仮借]], "to make use of; borrow" — also the term for phonetic loan characters in the traditional Six Writings classification).

Mandarin jiè, Cantonese ze3, and Japanese しゃく/しゃ/せき (shaku/sha/seki, on'yomi, fully productive: 借金 しゃっきん "a debt, borrowed money") alongside native か(りる) (ka(riru), "to borrow") all preserve the sense. Korean 차 (cha) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 빌리다 (billida, "to borrow"). Vietnamese tá is the Hán Việt reading, attested in tá túc (借宿, "to stay overnight, lodge temporarily" — literally "borrowed lodging").

This word is part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read ja/자/ㄐㄚ): [[姉]] ("elder sister") and [[諸]] ("various").
