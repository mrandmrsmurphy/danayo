## Notes
- [非 (char)](../characters/非%20(char).md) "ain't" + [常](../characters/常.md) "common, normal, frequent, regular, often" — no `#cranberry`: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here (非's is itself, 常's is [[日常]]). Literally "not normal."

非常 (fēicháng) is the standard, everyday Mandarin word for "extremely, very" (as an intensifier) and also "extraordinary, unusual" (as an adjective), and Cantonese 非常 (fei1soeng4) likewise. Japanese ひじょう (hijō) and Korean 비상 (bisang) are both standard words carrying the "extraordinary; emergency" sense (as in 非常口/비상구, "emergency exit"). Sino-Vietnamese phi thường is likewise standard and attested, meaning "extraordinary, exceptional."
