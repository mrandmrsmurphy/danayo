## Notes
- [久 (char)](../characters/久%20(char).md) "long time" + [闊 (char)](../characters/闊%20(char).md) "wide" — literally "long and broad [apart]," a formal expression for a long period of separation from someone, used especially as a greeting upon reunion after not having seen each other for a long time (cf. English "long time no see").

久闊 is a classical/literary expression rather than everyday colloquial vocabulary in any of the sphere's languages. Mandarin jiǔkuò and Japanese きゅうかつ (kyūkatsu) both preserve this formal register, appearing in polite epistolary and reunion greetings (久闊を叙する, "to exchange pleasantries after a long absence"). Cantonese gau2 fut3 follows the same compositional pattern. Korean and Vietnamese equivalents (구활, cửu khoát) are direct compositional readings of 久's and 闊's own stored syllables, but neither is independently confirmed as an attested fixed term in either language — presented here as a defensible derivation rather than a separately verified compound, since 久闊 as a fixed unit appears to be primarily a Sino-Japanese/Mandarin literary expression.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 규콷 diverges from the compositional Sino-Korean 구활, since both 久 and 闊 independently carry `kwin: false` on their own character pages.
