## Notes
- [呆 (char)](../characters/呆%20(char).md) "confused; dazed; foolish"

呆 is 会意: [[口]] ("mouth") over 木 ("tree") — a person standing dumbstruck like a tree trunk, mouth agape. It means "dull-witted, foolish, dazed" (發呆, "to space out, zone out") and, of expressions or manners, "wooden, rigid" (呆板, "rigid, stiff, wooden").

Mandarin dāi and Cantonese ngoi4 preserve the sense directly. Japanese ボウ/ガイ/ホウ (BOU/GAI/HOU, on'yomi, rare, mostly confined to compounds like 痴呆 chihō, "dementia") alongside native ほけ(る) (hoke(ru), "to become senile, absent-minded") cover the sense; this reading is not adopted as productively as in Mandarin/Cantonese. Korean 매 (mae) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 어리석다 (eoriseokda, "foolish"). Vietnamese ngai is the genuine Hán Việt reading, attested in compounds paralleling Mandarin usage — si ngai (癡呆, "foolish, dull-witted," cf. 痴呆 chīdāi), ngai trệ (呆滯, "sluggish, stagnant"), and ngai bản (呆板, "rigid, wooden," directly matching 呆板 dāibǎn). The character page's other stored candidates (dại, ngãi, ngóc, ngố, ngốc) are Nôm loan-character readings — native Vietnamese words 呆 was borrowed to write graphically for shared "foolish/wild" semantics, not genuine Sino-Vietnamese derivations of this character, and are excluded here; ngốc in particular ("stupid, silly," the everyday modern word) is sometimes listed as a Hán Việt gloss, but scholarly review (Trần Văn Chánh) finds no regular phonetic derivation linking it to 呆, so it is treated as contested/excluded rather than confirmed.

呆 is the stand-in for its own character page (the trivial single-character case, needing no separate note).
