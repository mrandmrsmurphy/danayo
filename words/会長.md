## Notes
- [会 (char)](../characters/会%20(char).md) "meeting; society" + [長 (char)](../characters/長%20(char).md) "long; chief"

会長 is the president or chairman of a society, a committee or a company. Wiktionary glosses Japanese かいちょう as "chairman; president (of a committee, company, society, etc.)". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 회장 matches.

The word is built from 会, a body of members, with 長 "chief", as [校長](校長.md) is built from 校. It leads an [協会](協会.md), association, or a [団体](団体.md), and below it is the [班長](班長.md), squad leader.
