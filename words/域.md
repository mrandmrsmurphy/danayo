## Notes
- [域 (char)](../characters/域%20(char).md) "domain; level"

域 means "domain, territory, region" — a bounded tract of land or, by extension, an abstract bounded scope (區域, "area"; 領域, "field, domain").

**Homophone check**: a bopomofo-string search for 注音 `ㄨㄧㄎ` also surfaces [[羽翼]] ("wing; assistance"), but this is a notation artifact rather than a genuine homophone — 羽翼's own 羅馬字 is `'u'ig` (a two-syllable concatenation, 羽 'u + 翼 'ig), distinct from 域's own single-syllable `wig`; the vault's bopomofo (注音) system doesn't separately mark the glide "w-" from plain "u," so the two strings collide only in that one transcription layer, not in the actual Dan'a'yo romanization. No homophone callout was added for this pair.

Mandarin yù and Cantonese wik6 preserve the sense directly. Japanese イキ (IKI, on'yomi, fully productive: 地域, chiiki, "region"; 領域, ryōiki, "domain, field") cover the sense, with no dedicated native kun'yomi. Korean 역 (yeok) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese vực is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in khu vực ("area, zone") and lĩnh vực ("field, domain," an extremely common word). The character page's other stored candidates (bực, vác, vặc, vức) are genuine Nôm readings — attested in dictionary sources, but as vernacular repurposings of the glyph for unrelated native syllables rather than Sino-Vietnamese doublets — and were excluded here.
