## Notes
- [禾 (char)](../characters/禾%20(char).md) "grain, cereal" + [稲](../characters/稲.md) "rice plant" — "unhusked rice." Neither character's own `stand_in` points here (禾's is [[禾]] itself, 稲's is [[水稲]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 稲 is individually `kwin: false`.

禾稲 refers to unhusked rice — the harvested rice plant with its grain still enclosed in the hull, as opposed to milled/polished rice ready for cooking. It is distinguished from [[水稲]] ("paddy rice," specifically rice grown in flooded fields) by naming the plant/grain generically rather than by its cultivation method.

Mandarin hédào and Korean 화도 (hwado) are standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese conventionally writes the rice plant in katakana as イネ (ine) rather than hiragana いね, following the common Japanese convention of rendering plant and animal species names in katakana in biological/taxonomic contexts. Vietnamese hoà đạo is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form.
