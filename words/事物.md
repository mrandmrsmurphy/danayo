## Notes
- [事 (char)](../characters/事%20(char).md) "matter, affair" + [物 (char)](../characters/物%20(char).md) "thing, object" — things and matters together, the concrete-and-abstract counterpart pairing already noted on [[事]]'s own page (物 the concrete object, 事 the abstract happening).

事物 covers "things" in the broadest sense — the totality of objects and matters one might refer to, often used in an academic or philosophical register (事物の本質, "the essential nature of things"). Mandarin shìwù, Cantonese si6 mat6, Japanese じぶつ, Korean 사물, and Vietnamese sự vật are all standard, fully compositional readings, all combining the same processual/concrete pairing.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 지묻 diverges from Sino-Korean 사물, since 事's own Dan'a'yo reading doesn't match Sino-Korean, even though 物's own reading matches exactly (物 is independently `kwin: true`).
