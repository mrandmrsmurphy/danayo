## Notes
- [石](../characters/石%20(char).md) "rock" + [家](../characters/家.md) "house, family" + [荘](../characters/荘.md) "villa, village" — "Shi family village". No stand-in relationship applies (石 is its own stand-in; 家's is [[家庭]]; 荘's is [[別荘]]).

石家荘 (Shijiazhuang) is the capital and largest city of Hebei province in northern China. The real-world spellings are 石家莊 and 石家庄; 莊 and 庄 are listed as aliases of 荘 in the character pages, so by the alias rule the filename uses the Japanese form 荘 and the others are kept as aliases. The name literally means "the village of the Shi family" and, per Wiktionary, the old names 石門 and 石莊 are obsolete.

English Wiktionary has no Japanese, Korean or Vietnamese entry for 石家莊. The Japanese field holds せっかそう, the reading given on Japanese Wikipedia (石家荘市); the Korean field holds 석가장, derived from the character pages (North Korean readings; not attested), and the usual South Korean name today is 스자좡 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Thạch Gia Trang is attested on Vietnamese Wikipedia. Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese entry; Japanese and Vietnamese Wikipedia for the other languages. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄎㄍㄚㄐ⺢ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
