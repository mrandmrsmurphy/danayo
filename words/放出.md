## Notes
- [放](../characters/放.md) "to let go, to set free" + [出 (char)](../characters/出%20(char).md) "to go out" — no stand-in relationship applies (放's own stand-in is [[釈放]]; 出 is its own stand-in).

Wiktionary: Chinese "to release; to emit; to let out"; Japanese "release, emission" and, as a verb, "to release, emit, let out, vent, discharge"; Korean 방출 "release, emission". In `文法 - 02音韻論` it appears in 無放出無声塞音, the unreleased voiceless coda stops. Compare [[釈放]], [[開放]]; the ejective is [[喉破音]].
