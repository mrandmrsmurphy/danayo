## Notes
- [太 (char)](../characters/太%20(char).md) "great, extreme" + [平](../characters/平.md) "peaceful, level" + [洋](../characters/洋.md) "ocean" — literally "peaceful ocean," the world's largest and deepest ocean.

The name is a direct calque, not a native coinage: Ferdinand Magellan named it *Mar Pacífico* ("peaceful sea") in 1520 after experiencing calm winds on entering it from the storm-battered Strait of Magellan, and the Chinese term 太平洋 translates that European name rather than deriving from any older domestic term for the ocean — earlier Chinese renderings of the concept (太平大海, 大東洋) existed before 太平洋 became the standard modern form. [[太平]] itself, "peaceful" (already documented on its own page, including this same etymological note), supplies the first half; 洋 alone means "ocean, vast expanse of water" but is too phonologically bound to stand alone in Dan'a'yo, which is why the character's own `stand_in` is [[大洋]] rather than this word.

Japanese たいへいよう is a straightforward on'yomi concatenation (TAI + HEI + YOU), not a fused or irregular reading. Korean 태평양 and Vietnamese *Thái Bình Dương* are likewise transparent Sino-form compounds, the latter still the official modern name for the ocean in Vietnamese (rather than a native coinage, unlike some other geographic terms).
