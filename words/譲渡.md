## Notes
- [譲](../characters/譲%20(char).md) "yield, hand over" + [渡](../characters/渡%20(char).md) "cross over, hand over" — "hand over." Their own `stand_in` values: 譲's is itself; 渡's is itself; none points here, so 譲渡 is an independent compound.

譲渡 is to transfer ownership of something to another person: to assign, convey or hand over. Mandarin ràngdù (讓渡), Cantonese joeng6 dou6, Japanese じょうと and Korean 양도 all use it, mostly in legal and business writing. Compare [[継承]] (inherit) and [[贈与]] (give as a gift).
