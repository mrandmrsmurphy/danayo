## Notes
- [共 (char)](../characters/共%20(char).md) "together; jointly" + [和](../characters/和.md) "harmony; peace" + [国](../characters/国.md) "nation" — [[共和]] is itself a word

Wiktionary: Chinese 共和國 "republic (country)"; Japanese 共和国 (きょうわこく) "republic (country)"; Korean 공화국 is the hanja form, "republic". The component [[共和]] echoes the Gonghe Regency (Wikipedia: an interregnum in Chinese history from 841 to 828 BC, after King Li of Zhou was exiled by his nobles). It contrasts with [[民国]], and [[総統]] is the executive title within such a state. In Dan'a'yo 共和国 is a republic as a kind of state, built on the abstract 共和.
