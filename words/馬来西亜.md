## Notes
- [馬 (char)](../characters/馬%20(char).md) "horse" + [来 (char)](../characters/来%20(char).md) "come" + [西](../characters/西.md) "west" + [亜](../characters/亜.md) "Asia" — no `#cranberry`: none of the four constituents' own `stand_in` points here.

馬来西亜 (Mǎláixīyà) is a phonetic transliteration of "Malaysia," built from 馬来 (transliterating "Malay") + 西亜 ("West Asia," itself the standard Sino compound for the "-asia" suffix in country names). Japanese マレーシア and Korean 말레이시아 both instead directly transliterate the English name "Malaysia" rather than reading the kanji/hanja compositionally — the same pattern seen in other modern country-name loanwords. Vietnamese uses either the phonetic transcription Ma-lai-xi-a or the older, shorter Sino-Vietnamese name Mã Lai (historically referring to Malaya/the Malay ethnicity, from just 馬來 without 西亜).
