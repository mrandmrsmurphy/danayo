## Notes
- [超 (char)](../characters/超%20(char).md) "exceed" + [過 (char)](../characters/過%20(char).md) "pass; exceed" + [勤](../characters/勤.md) "diligent; work" + [務](../characters/務.md) "duty, affairs" — overtime.

超過勤務 is overtime work: work done beyond the regular hours of duty. It is "exceeding duty." Korean 초과근무 is confirmed on Wiktionary as the hanja form of the noun; Japanese ちょうかきんむ is, from memory, the formal civil-service and labor-law term (超過勤務手当, "overtime allowance"), with the short form 超勤 (alias). Mandarin has no entry on this form and uses 加班 (jiābān, "add shift"), Japanese also uses 残業 (ざんぎょう), and Korean 야근, so `mandarin` and `cantonese` are left blank. Dan'a'yo takes the formal, region-neutral compound. It is built from [[超過]] (excess, to exceed) and [[勤務]] (duty, work at a post).
