## Etymology
- [球 (char)](../characters/球%20(char).md) "sphere, ball" + [場](../characters/場.md) "market" — no stand-in relationship applies (球's own stand-in is itself; 場's own is [[市場]]).

球場 means field, pitch, court.

Cantonese kau4 coeng4, Japanese きゅうじょう (KYUU+JOU), and Korean 구장 were already correct and fully compositional. Fixed `mandarin`: a comma-joined dual pronunciation ("qiúchǎng, qiúcháng") was corrected to the single compositional qiúchǎng, matching 場's own stored citation (chǎng). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with cầu trường (球's own cầu + 場's own trường), also a real, standard Vietnamese term for "stadium." Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/`aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼜ㄐㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
