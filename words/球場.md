## Notes
- [球 (char)](../characters/球%20(char).md) "sphere, ball" + [場](../characters/場.md) "market" — no stand-in relationship applies (球's own stand-in is itself; 場's own is [[市場]]).

球場 means a field, pitch or court for ball games.

Mandarin qiúchǎng (the single reading, matching 場's own chǎng), Cantonese kau4 coeng4, Japanese きゅうじょう, Korean 구장 and Vietnamese cầu trường are all compositional. The Vietnamese is also a standard term for "stadium".
