## Notes
- [俘](characters/俘.md) "to capture (as a POW)" + [虜](characters/虜.md) "captive, prisoner" — "prisoner of war, captive." This word is the `stand_in` legitimizer for both [[俘]] and [[虜]] (transitivity holds: A=B=AB), hence the `#cranberry` tag. `vietnamese: phu lỗ` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese (hvdic gives the genuine reading as `phu lỗ`, not `phù lỗ` — corrected the tone mark to match [[俘]]'s own corrected reading). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 푸로 diverges entirely from Sino-Korean 부로. No homophones (注音 ㄈㄨㄌㄛ unique among both words and characters).
