## Notes
- [北](../characters/北.md) "north" + [辰](../characters/辰.md) "star; time"

北辰 is the North Star in the literary register: the pole star around which the heavens seem to turn. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin běichén as "a literary synonym of 北極星, North Star, Polaris". Cantonese bak1 san4 matches, Korean 북신 is the reading of the characters, and no Japanese reading is attested.

The ordinary word is [北極星](北極星.md). In the classics the 辰 is a position in the heavens, as in the [辰](../characters/辰.md) of the twelve branches.
