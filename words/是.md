## Notes
- [是 (char)](../characters/是%20(char).md) "is; to equal" — 是 is the `stand_in` of its own character page, i.e. the word that legitimizes the bound character 是

是 is the **transitive copula of identity and equation** in Dan'a'yo, the foundational "to be; to equal". The grammar ([[文法 - 04句法]], under 系詞) sorts the six copulas into transitive 是, 非, 有, 無, which "take an explicit complement", and intransitive [[在]], [[莫]]. 是 is the prototypical equative: it asserts that subject and predicate refer to the same referent or category. Its negation is [[非]] "to not be". [[有]] "to have; there is" and [[在]] "to exist, be located" are different words and are not interchangeable with 是; see [[Existence]] for the six-way split, and [[文法 - 97品詞]], which lists 是 first among the copulas ("to equal"). All six carry `pos: 系詞`.

**Grammar and examples.** In the literary mode (書面体), "when both topic and comment are 名詞, a copula (是/非) is required" ([[文法 - 04句法]]), so 我 是魚 "I am a fish" (the grammar's own example; its ruby is 是魚 ㄙㄝ·⼄), and 此 非道 "this is not the Way" is the negative counterpart. The Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and Vietnamese primers each repeat the rule with 此書 是 好書 "this book is a good book" (e.g. [[文法 - 92普通話小冊子]]: 名词述语必须用「是」). 是 does **not** link a noun to a stative: 性詞 serve as comment without a copula (天 高 "the sky is high", [[文法 - 04句法]]), so 此 是好 is not the Dan'a'yo way to say "this is good". Because Dan'a'yo adpositions are suffixing and TAM markers are verbal suffixes, 是 sits between the two nouns and takes no preposition. The vault's Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平 on [China](../lookup/HSK/China.md) shows the emphatic variants: 平臥 乃是 宇宙 客観 之 唯一 真理 ("lying flat is indeed the sole truth of the objective universe"), where 乃 "indeed" precedes 是, and 平臥 即是 正義, with [[即是]] "is precisely". Illustrative sentences (constructed here, not quoted from the grammar): 此是書 "this is a book"; 彼是誰 "who is that?" (the latter is also cited on [[彼]]).

**The demonstrative and "correct" senses.** Wiktionary reports that 是 was originally a demonstrative pronoun, "this", and that the copula sense emerged only by the Eastern Han, replacing the older copular construction with 也 ("A, 是 B 也"); the "right, correct" sense is the opposite of 非 "wrong", as in 是非 "right and wrong". Wiktionary also lists literary "this" (是日 "this day"), the affirmative answer "yes; right", and "true; correct". In Dan'a'yo the demonstrative job belongs to [[此]] "this" (the Swadesh entry for "this"), so 是 keeps only the copular sense as a free word; the demonstrative survives only in compounds such as [[是日]] "same day; this day" (see [[Calendar]]). This is a narrowing relative to classical Chinese, not a divergence from modern Mandarin, Cantonese or Vietnamese.

**Cross-linguistic equivalents** (the `mandarin`, `cantonese`, `japanese`, `korean` and `vietnamese` fields hold the Sino-Xenic bound reading of the character, not the native copula). Mandarin 是 (shì) is the equative copula: 我是学生 "I am a student"; 是...的 marks contrastive clauses: 我是在北京出生的 "it was in Beijing that I was born". Cantonese 係 (hai6) is the spoken copula (我係學生), with 是 (si6) in formal compounds. Japanese has no true equative verb: だ and です are copular auxiliaries, and the character's own readings are on'yomi ゼ (goon; used in 是非) and シ (kan'on), kun これ "this". Korean uses the suffix 이다 (나는 학생이다); Sino-Korean 시 appears in 시비 (是非; from memory); Wiktionary notes the native 이 "this" is semantically paired with 是 in Old Korean. Vietnamese là is the copula (Tôi là học sinh), with Sino-Vietnamese thị in thị phi 是非 (the reading thị was re-checked on hvdic.thivien.net). The native-language equivalents above are from memory and were not re-checked against Wiktionary in this pass; the field readings match the character page.

**Etymology and phonology.** The character page treats 是 as 会意 of uncertain origin (Wiktionary: possibly from an oracle-bone form of 智, composed of 子, 止 and 冊), and it is the phonetic of a large family (匙 堤 提 醍 題). Middle Chinese 禪母 (d͡ʑ) + 支三開韻 (iᴇ) gives Dan'a'yo ㄙㄝ (se, 서). Wiktionary lists variant forms 昰 and 𣆞, which have no pages in the vault and are not aliases here. There are no Dan'a'yo homophones of ㄙㄝ among words (checked with an anchored 注音 search); the syllable page is [[ㄙㄝ]], glossed "be". Compounds in the vault: [[即是]], [[是日]], and the chengyu [[色即是空]] and [[少即是多]].
