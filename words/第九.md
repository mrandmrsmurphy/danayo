## Notes
- [第 (char)](../characters/第%20(char).md) "-th (ordinal prefix)" + [九 (char)](../characters/九%20(char).md) "nine"

第九 is the ninth in a series. The prefix 第 turns cardinal numbers into ordinals across all CJKV languages; 第九 follows the same pattern as [[第一]], [[第二]], [[第八]] and the rest. Japanese also has だいく, which is the reading in 第九 "the Ninth" (Beethoven's Ninth Symphony).
