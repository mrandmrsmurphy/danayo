## Notes
- [異](../characters/異.md) "different, strange" + [常](../characters/常.md) "constant, ordinary" — 異's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 常's own is [[日常]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 異 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

異常 means different, weird; unusual, abnormal.

Mandarin yìcháng, Cantonese ji6 soeng4, Japanese いじょう, Korean 이상 and Vietnamese dị thường are all fully compositional, matching 異's and 常's own citations.
