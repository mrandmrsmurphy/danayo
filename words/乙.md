## Notes
- [乙 (char)](../characters/乙%20(char).md) "second"

乙 is 象形, though its origin is obscure — proposed readings include a sprouting plant (Shuowen), a fish's intestine or gill bone (*Erya*, *Liji*), or a flow of water; none has settled consensus. As the second of the Ten Heavenly Stems ([[天干]]/[[十干]]: 甲, 乙, 丙, 丁...), it came to mean "second" generally, paralleling [[甲]] ("first, A") and [[丙]] ("third, C") the way English grading uses A/B/C.

Mandarin yǐ, Cantonese jyut3, Japanese おつ (otsu, on'yomi) alongside native きのと (kinoto, its name as a stem in the traditional Japanese calendar), and Korean 을 (eul) all preserve the stem/ordinal sense. Vietnamese ất is the standard reading, most familiar today from Vietnamese sexagenary-cycle year names (e.g. Ất Dậu, Ất Mùi) — the same calendrical role [[丙]]'s own bính plays.

1. Second of ten heavenly stems ([[天干]], [[十干]]):
	- [甲](甲.md), 乙, [丙](丙.md), [[丁]], [戊](../characters/戊.md), [己](../characters/己.md), [庚](../characters/庚.md), [辛](../characters/辛.md), [壬](../characters/壬.md), [癸](../characters/癸.md)
2. Second item on a list
	- [[甲]] (“first; A”); [丙](丙.md) (“third; C”); [[丁]] (fourth, D)
