## Notes
- [柴](../characters/柴.md) "firewood, brushwood" + [棍](../characters/棍.md) "stick" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 柴 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 棍's own stand-in is [[棍棒]].

柴棍 is a phonetic transliteration name for Saigon (Ho Chi Minh City), chosen for its jwaihon/좨혼 sound resemblance rather than its literal meaning ("firewood-stick").

`mandarin`/`cantonese` hold the real, established East Asian exonym for the city, 西貢 (Xīgòng/sai1 gung3, listed in `aliases`), rather than 柴's and 棍's own compositional readings (chái/caai4 + gùn/gwan3) — the same "avoided real name" documentation pattern used for phonetic-substitution neologisms elsewhere in the vault (see [[丹金]]). Japanese サイゴン and Korean 사이공 are Vietnamese-name loanword transliterations, the real-world standard forms in both languages (rather than a Sino-Japanese/Sino-Korean reading of 西貢). Vietnamese Sài Gòn is the city's own native name. No homophones (注音 ㄐ⺢ㄧㄏㄛㄋ unique across both words and characters).
