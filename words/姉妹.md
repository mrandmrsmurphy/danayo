## Etymology

## Notes
- "Sisters" (姉妹都市, "sister cities"). `vietnamese: tỷ muội` is a directly attested standard Vietnamese term with the same sense (tỷ muội tương tàn, "sisters/siblings fighting each other"), though everyday Vietnamese more commonly says chị em.
