## Notes
- "Sisters" (姉妹都市, "sister cities"). `vietnamese: tỷ muội` is a directly attested standard Vietnamese term with the same sense (tỷ muội tương tàn, "sisters/siblings fighting each other"), though everyday Vietnamese more commonly says chị em. Fixed `characters` to use the `姉 (char)` filename form. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 자뫼 diverges from Sino-Korean 자매 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄇㄛㄧ unique among both words and characters).
