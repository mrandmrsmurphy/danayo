## Notes
- [無](characters/無%20(char).md) "lack" + [効](characters/効%20(char).md) "effect" — no stand-in relationship applies (無's own stand-in is itself; 効's own stand-in is [[効果]], not this compound).

無効 means ineffective, invalid.

Mandarin wúxiào, Cantonese mou4 haau6 (fixed a missing space: mou4haau6 → mou4 haau6), Korean 무효, and Vietnamese vô hiệu are all fully compositional, matching 無's and 効's own stored citations exactly. Fixed a real bug in `japanese`: むかう (a wholly different, unrelated real word meaning "to face/head toward," 向かう) was substituted for the correct compositional on'yomi reading むこう (MU+KOU), which is also the real, standard Japanese word for "invalid." No homophones (注音 ㄇㄜㄏ⼘ㄨ unique across both words and characters).
