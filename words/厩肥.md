## Notes
- [厩](../characters/厩.md) "stable, barn" + [肥](../characters/肥.md) "fertile, manure" — no stand-in relationship applies (厩's own stand-in is [[馬厩]]; 肥's is [[肥満]]).

厩肥 means stable manure or farmyard manure — animal manure mixed with bedding straw, used as fertilizer.

Japanese きゅうひ, Korean 구비, and Vietnamese cứu phì are all standard, fully compositional readings (`cứu` here is 厩's own reading, coincidentally homophonous with the unrelated native word for "save/rescue," not an error). No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼜ㄅㄨㄧ unique across both words and characters).
