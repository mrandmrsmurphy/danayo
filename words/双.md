## Notes
- [双 (char)](../characters/双%20(char).md) "pair; double; twin; even"

双 (traditional form 雙) is 会意. It means "a pair, two of a kind" — the standard classifier for paired items (一双鞋, "a pair of shoes") and a common prefix/adjective for "double, twin" ([[双子]], "twins"; 双方, "both parties"), extending to "even" as an arithmetic property (双数, "an even number").

Mandarin shuāng, Cantonese soeng1, and Japanese そう (sō, on'yomi, fully productive: 双子, though this specific compound uses the native kun'yomi ふたご instead) alongside native ふた (futa, "a pair") all preserve the sense. Korean 쌍 (ssang) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, doubling here as both the Sino-Korean and native Korean form — a rare case where both columns store the identical value. Vietnamese song is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in song song ("parallel, side by side") and song sinh (双生, "twins," matching this character's own [[双子]] compound); the character page's other three stored candidates (rong, rông, xong) are unrelated and look like corpus noise.

This word shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading (swang/솽/ㄙ⺢ㄫ) with [[霜]] ("frost") — discovered mid-sweep to carry a blank `date-last-perfect:` key (present but empty, rather than absent), which had let it silently slip past this sweep's file-scanning method; perfected alongside 双 in this same iteration since they're a homophone pair.
