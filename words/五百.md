## Notes
- [五 (char)](../characters/五%20(char).md) "five" + [百 (char)](../characters/百%20(char).md) "hundred" — five hundred, 500.

Plain digit + 百, like [[二百]], [[七百]] and [[八百]]: the hundred is counted by prefixing the digit to 百, with no further grouping word. Japanese ごひゃく is the plain concatenation. Korean 오백 follows the Sino-Korean reading directly. `vietnamese: ngũ bách` is the Sino-Vietnamese compositional reading; everyday Vietnamese uses the native năm trăm for round numbers like this. Compare [[五千]] and [[五十]].
