## Notes
- [正](characters/正%20(char).md) "correct" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 正 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

正 means correct.

Mandarin zhèng, Cantonese zing3, Japanese せい, Korean 정, and Vietnamese chính (the primary form among the character's own comma-joined vietnamese variants chiếng/chánh/chênh/chính/chếnh/giêng) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Added the missing `pos`/`japanese`/`hsk_level` fields. Discovered a genuine homophone with [[蒸]] (both 注音 ㄐㄧㄫ, 諺文 징) — cross-referenced both pages.

## Etymology
Phono-semantic compound (形聲 / 形声, OC ^tjeŋ, ^tjeŋs): phonetic 丁 (OC ^rteːŋ, ^teːŋ) + semantic 止 (“foot”) – to go on a long journey. Original form of 征 (OC ^tjeŋ, “to go on long campaign”).  Later, 丁 was simplified into a horizontal stroke, as in 旦. Folk etymologies do not recognize 丁.
