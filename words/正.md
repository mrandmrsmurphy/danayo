## Notes
- [正](../characters/正%20(char).md) "correct" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 正 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

正 means correct: right, proper, free of error (正確, "accurate"; 正常, "normal"; 正式, "formal, official"), and by extension upright or just (正直, "upright"; 公正, "fair"; 正義, "justice"). It also means straight or exactly (正中, "dead center"), and, as a positive number, the opposite of negative (正數, "positive number").

Mandarin zhèng, Cantonese zing3, Japanese せい (正解/せいかい, "correct answer"; 正義/せいぎ, "justice") with the alternate on'yomi しょう (正月/しょうがつ, "New Year"; 正直/しょうじき, "honest"), Korean 정 (정확, 정의) and Vietnamese chính (chính xác, "accurate"; chính thức, "official") are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native adjective 正しい (ただしい) for the everyday word, so on'yomi せい is bound to compounds.
