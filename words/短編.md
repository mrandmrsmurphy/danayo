## Notes
- [短 (char)](../characters/短%20(char).md) "short" + [編](../characters/編.md) "compile; volume"

短編 is a short story, or a short work: one that is complete in a few pages. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin duǎnpiān as "short piece of writing" and Japanese たんぺん as "short stories". Korean 단편 is the same word. Cantonese dyun2 pin1 matches.

It is the short counterpart of the [小説](小説.md), novel. The measure word for pieces is [篇](篇.md).
