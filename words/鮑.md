## Notes
- [鮑 (char)](../characters/鮑%20(char).md) "abalone" — 鮑 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

Mandarin bào, Cantonese baau1, Japanese ほう, Korean 포, and Sino-Vietnamese bào are all standard, fully compositional readings — bào was chosen as the representative Vietnamese form over the character page's other stored candidates (bão, bảo).
