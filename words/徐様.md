## Notes
- [徐](../characters/徐.md) "slowly, gradually" + [様](../characters/様%20(char).md) "manner, way" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [徐](../characters/徐.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (様's own stand-in is itself).

徐様 means slowly, or in a deliberate/gradual manner. [[緩慢様]] connotes a kind of lethargy, whereas this word is more "deliberative."

Mandarin xúyàng, Cantonese ceoi4 joeng6, Japanese じょよう, Korean 서양, and Vietnamese từ dạng are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bugs found and fixed**: `mandarin` had been swapped for the unrelated word 慢 (màn); `korean`/`japanese` had native/differently-formed real adverbs (서서히/천천히, じょじょに/ゆっくり) appended instead of the compositional reading. Filled blank `vietnamese`, removed empty-list `aliases`, filled blank `pos: 副詞`. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼄·⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
