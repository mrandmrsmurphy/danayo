## Notes
- [聴](../characters/聴%20(char).md) "listen" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 聴 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

聴 means to listen, as in 聴衆 (audience) and 聴取 (to listen to, take in).

Mandarin tīng, Cantonese ting1, and Korean 청 (cheong) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading きく (kiku, "to listen") rather than the on-readings チョウ/テイ (CHOU/TEI) for the bare character alone. No vietnamese reading is stored on the character page. This word completes a genuine Dan'a'yo homophone with [[青]] ("blue") — re-verified with the corrected homophone-check tooling (庁/錆/鯖 share the syllable at the character level only).
