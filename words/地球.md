## Notes
- [地](../characters/地%20(char).md) "ground" + [球](../characters/球%20(char).md) "ball, sphere" — no stand-in relationship applies (both characters are independent Dan'a'yo entries, each its own stand-in).

地球 means the Earth or the globe.

Japanese ちきゅう, Korean 지구, and Vietnamese địa cầu are all standard, fully compositional readings.
