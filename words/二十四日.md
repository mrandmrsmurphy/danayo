## Notes
- [[二十]] "twenty" + [四 (char)](../characters/四%20(char).md) "four" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — the twenty-fourth day of the month.

Japanese has a special reading: にじゅうよっか (nijūyokka) — using the native counter よっか ("4th day") rather than the expected compositional にじゅうしにち, the same irregularity seen in bare [[四日]] itself (よっか) and consistent with how Japanese day-counting words (ついたち through とおか) generally resist compositional on'yomi. Mandarin èrshísì rì, Cantonese ji6 sap6 sei3 jat6, and Korean 이십사일 are all standard, fully compositional. Vietnamese filled with ngày hai mươi bốn (native "day twenty-four"), matching the rest of this [[二十一日|二十N日]] cluster.
