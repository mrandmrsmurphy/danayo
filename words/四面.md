## Notes
- [四](../characters/四%20(char).md) "four" + [面](../characters/面.md) "face, side" — no stand-in relationship applies (四 is its own stand-in; 面's own is [[表面]]).

四面 means all sides or all around — the four cardinal directions collectively (四面楚歌 "surrounded on all sides").

Japanese しめん, Korean 사면, and Vietnamese tứ diện are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `pos: 名詞`; fixed empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄜㄇ⼶ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
