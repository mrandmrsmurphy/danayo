## Notes
- [既](../characters/既.md) "already" + [往](../characters/往%20(char).md) "to go towards" — no stand-in relationship applies (既's own stand-in is [[既以]]; 往's own is itself).

既往 means the past.

Mandarin jìwǎng, Cantonese gei3 wong5, Japanese きおう, Korean 기왕, and Vietnamese kí vãng (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄜ⺢ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
