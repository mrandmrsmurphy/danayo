## Notes
- [吐 (char)](../characters/吐%20(char).md) "spit; vomit; cough up; spew"

吐 combines [[口]] ("mouth") with phonetic [[土]]. It means "to expel from the mouth" — spitting, vomiting, or coughing something up — covering both Swadesh #96 ("spit") and #97 ("vomit") with the same single Dan'a'yo word, reflecting the concept's basic, cross-linguistically stable vocabulary status.

Mandarin tǔ, Cantonese tou3, and Japanese と (to, on'yomi, rare outside compounds) alongside native は(く) (ha(ku), "to vomit, spit, cough up," the everyday verb) all preserve the sense. Korean 토 (to) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 토하다 (tohada, built on the same Sino-Korean root, "to vomit"). Vietnamese thổ is the Hán Việt reading, attested in thổ huyết ("to vomit blood") and thổ tả ("a cholera-like illness with vomiting and diarrhea"); the everyday native word for "to spit" is nhổ (nhổ nước bọt, "to spit saliva") — a related but distinct native verb, not itself the Hán Việt reading.
