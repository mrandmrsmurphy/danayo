## Notes
- [吐 (char)](../characters/吐%20(char).md) "spit; vomit; cough up; spew"
- [[Swadesh]] #96 and #97

吐 combines [[口]] ("mouth") with phonetic [[土]]. It means "to expel from the mouth" — spitting, vomiting, or coughing something up — and is one of the Swadesh list's core 100 words, reflecting its basic, cross-linguistically stable vocabulary status.

Mandarin tǔ, Cantonese tou3, and Japanese と (to, on'yomi, rare outside compounds) alongside native は(く) (ha(ku), "to vomit, spit, cough up," the everyday verb) all preserve the sense. Korean 토 (to) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 토하다 (tohada, built on the same Sino-Korean root, "to vomit"). Vietnamese thổ is the Hán Việt reading, attested in thổ huyết ("to vomit blood") and thổ tả ("a cholera-like illness with vomiting and diarrhea"); the everyday native word for "to spit" is nhổ (nhổ nước bọt, "to spit saliva") — a related but distinct native verb, not itself the Hán Việt reading; the character page's remaining candidates, giổ and giỗ, are unrelated and look like corpus noise.

This word completes a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read to/토/ㄊㄛ): [[兎]] ("rabbit; hare") and [[土]] ("earth"), both already perfected and already cross-linking here from earlier in this sweep.
