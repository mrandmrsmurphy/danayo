## Notes
- [援](../characters/援.md) "assist" + [交](../characters/交%20(char).md) "exchange" — no stand-in relationship applies (援's own stand-in is [[援手]]; 交's own is itself). Short for 援助交際.

援交 means school-girl prostitution (a Japanese-origin term).

Mandarin yuánjiāo, Cantonese wun4 gaau1, Japanese えんこう, Korean 원교, and Vietnamese vin giao are all standard, fully compositional readings.
