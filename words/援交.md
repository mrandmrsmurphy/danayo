## Notes
- [援](../characters/援.md) "assist" + [交](../characters/交%20(char).md) "exchange" — no stand-in relationship applies (援's own stand-in is [[援手]]; 交's own is itself). Short for 援助交際.

援交 means school-girl prostitution (a Japanese-origin term).

Mandarin yuánjiāo, Cantonese wun4 gaau1, Japanese えんこう, Korean 원교, and Vietnamese vin giao are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bugs: `諺文` used the wrong final consonant for 援's syllable (옹→온, ㅇ vs ㄴ) and `羅馬字` used the old minority reading "gyau" for 交 instead of the majority-established "gyou" — both corrected, matching the already-correct 注音. No homophones (注音 ㄛㄋㄍ⼄ㄨ unique across both words and characters).
