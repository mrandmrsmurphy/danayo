## Notes
- [痘](../characters/痘.md) "pox, smallpox" + [痕](../characters/痕.md) "trace, scar" — 痘's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 痕's own is [[痕跡]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 痘 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

痘痕 means a pockmark: the pitted scar left by smallpox ([[天花]]) or, by extension, by severe acne. 痘 pairs the sickness radical 疒 with 豆 "bean", for the bean-shaped pustules of the disease. Smallpox was declared eradicated in 1980, so the pockmarked face is now chiefly a historical or literary image.

Mandarin dòuhén and Korean 두흔 are the compositional Sino-readings. Japanese reads the same characters as あばた, a native word and the standard one for a pockmark; the compositional reading would be とうこん. The Japanese proverb あばたもえくぼ ("to a lover, even pockmarks look like dimples") shows how common the word is.
