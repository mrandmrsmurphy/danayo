## Etymology
- [痘](../characters/痘.md) "pox, smallpox" + [痕](../characters/痕.md) "trace, scar" — 痘's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 痕's own is [[痕跡]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 痘 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

Mandarin dòuhén and Korean 두흔 were already correct and fully compositional. Japanese あばた is the real, standard native Japanese word for pockmark, used instead of a compositional on'yomi reading (which would be とうこん). Filled the blank `cantonese` with dau6 han4 and blank `vietnamese` with đậu ngân, both compositional. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/`aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄨㄏㄜㄋ unique across both words and characters).
