## Notes
- [理](characters/理.md) "-ology, reason, logic" + [解](characters/解%20(char).md) "explain" — no stand-in relationship applies (理's own stand-in is itself; 解's own is itself).

理解 means to understand, to comprehend.

Mandarin lǐjiě, Cantonese lei5 gaai2, Japanese りかい (RI+KAI), and Vietnamese lý giải were already correct and fully compositional. Fixed a real North/South Korean bug: 이해 (a South Korean 두음법칙-shifted form) → 리해, retaining word-initial ㄹ per the vault's North Korean/문화어 convention, matching 理's own stored citation (리) and the same fix already applied on [[理想]]/[[理由]]. `kwin` remains false: 諺文 리걔 diverges from korean 리해 in the second syllable (걔 vs 해), a genuine Dan'a'yo/Sino-Korean divergence unrelated to the initial-ㄹ fix. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/empty `aliases: []`. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄧ·ㄍ⼘ㄧ unique across both words and characters).

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot added 2026-10-02**: the concatenated string `ㄌㄧㄍ⼘ㄧ` admits two legal partitions — the intended `ㄌㄧ`(理)+`ㄍ⼘ㄧ`(解), and a false alternate `ㄌㄧ`+`ㄍ⼘`+`ㄧ` (both ㄍ⼘ and ㄧ are independently real syllables) — so the dot at the true 理|解 boundary is required: ㄌㄧ·ㄍ⼘ㄧ. This was missed by the vault's 2026-09-28 full dot audit; caught while deriving [[理解度]]'s own reading.
