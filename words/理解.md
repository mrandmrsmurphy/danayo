## Notes
- [理](../characters/理.md) "-ology, reason, logic" + [解](../characters/解%20(char).md) "explain" — no stand-in relationship applies (理's own stand-in is itself; 解's own is itself).

理解 means to understand, to comprehend.

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot added 2026-10-02**: the concatenated string `ㄌㄧㄍ⼘ㄧ` admits two legal partitions — the intended `ㄌㄧ`(理)+`ㄍ⼘ㄧ`(解), and a false alternate `ㄌㄧ`+`ㄍ⼘`+`ㄧ` (both ㄍ⼘ and ㄧ are independently real syllables) — so the dot at the true 理|解 boundary is required: ㄌㄧ·ㄍ⼘ㄧ. This was missed by the vault's 2026-09-28 full dot audit; caught while deriving [[理解度]]'s own reading.
