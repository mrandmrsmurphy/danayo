## Notes
- [相](../characters/相.md) "mutual" + [克 (char)](../characters/克%20(char).md) "overcome" — no stand-in relationship applies (相's own stand-in is [[相互]]; 克's own is itself). `aliases: 相剋` uses 克's own alias 剋, the historically fuller form of the same character.

相克 names the destructive (overcoming) half of the [[五行]] (Wu Xing) cycle, the counterpart to [[相生]]'s generative one: 木 (Wood) parts 土 (Earth, as roots splitting soil), 土 dams 水 (Water), 水 extinguishes 火 (Fire), 火 melts 金 (Metal), and 金 (as a blade) chops 木 (Wood), closing the loop.

Mandarin xiāngkè, Cantonese soeng1 hak1, Japanese そうこく (sōkoku), Korean 상극 (sanggeuk — now also a common modern word for "a bad match, mutually incompatible things," extended from this cosmological sense), and Vietnamese tương khắc are all real, standard readings of this exact philosophical term. `kwin` is false per the AND-rule: 相 is individually `kwin: true`, but 克 is individually `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄚㄫㄎㄨㄎ unique among both words and characters).
