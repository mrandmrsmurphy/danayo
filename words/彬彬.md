## Notes
- [彬](../characters/彬.md) "cultivated, well-bred" doubled — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

彬彬 is a literary ideophone meaning refined, gentle and elegant — carrying both literary flourish and substance, gathering and fitting harmoniously together, flourishing. Best known from the Analects phrase 文質彬彬 ("refined and substantial in equal measure").

Mandarin bīnbīn, Cantonese ban1 ban1, Japanese ひんぴん, Korean 빈빈, and Vietnamese bân bân are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄧㄋㄆㄧㄋ unique across both words and characters).
