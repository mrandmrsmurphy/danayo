## Notes
- [共 (char)](../characters/共%20(char).md) "together, common" + [同](../characters/同.md) "same" + [体](../characters/体.md) "body" — no stand-in relationship applies (all three characters' own `stand_in` point elsewhere).

共同体 is the standard word for a community — a body of people sharing a place, interests, or identity in common.

Mandarin gòngtóngtǐ, Cantonese gung6 tung4 tai2, Japanese きょうどうたい, and Korean 공동체 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese cộng đồng is the real, standard word — it drops the third syllable (体/thể) that the Han-character form and Dan'a'yo's own reading both keep. `kwin` is false: two of the three constituents (共, 体) are individually `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼄ㄫㄉㄛㄫㄊㄝㄧ unique across both words and characters).
