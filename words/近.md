## Notes
- [近 (char)](../characters/近%20(char).md) "near" — the bare character used directly as a word, its own `stand_in`.

近 means near: close in space, time, or relationship (附近, "nearby"; 近期, "in the near term"; 親近, "close, intimate"; 近代, "the modern era"), and by extension approximately or almost (接近, "approach"; 近似, "similar to"). The graph is a 形声 compound of 辶 "walk" with the phonetic 斤. Its antonym is [[遠]].

Mandarin jìn, Cantonese gan6, Korean 근 (근처, "vicinity"; 최근, "recently"), and Vietnamese cận (cận thị, "nearsighted"; phụ cận, "vicinity") are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native adjective 近い (ちかい) for everyday "near," reserving on'yomi きん for compounds (近代/きんだい, "modern era"). The everyday native Vietnamese word for "near" is gần rather than the Sino-Vietnamese cận; Korean has native 가깝다.
