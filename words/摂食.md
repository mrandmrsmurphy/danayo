## Notes
- [摂](../characters/摂%20(char).md) "vicarious" + [食](../characters/食%20(char).md) "eat" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 摂 and 食's own stand-ins are themselves).

摂食 means to feed or ingest.

Mandarin shèshí, Cantonese sip3 sik6, Japanese せっしょく, and Korean 섭식 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese nhiếp thực is compositional.
