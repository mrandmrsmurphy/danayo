## Notes
- [漫](../characters/漫.md) "random; comic" + [画](../characters/画.md) "picture"

漫画 is a comic or cartoon: a drawn story or caricature, in Japanese manga. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin mànhuà as "comics; caricature; cartoon; manga; manhwa; manhua" and Japanese まんが as "a drawing or painting made as one pleases without restraint, such as". Korean 만화 and Vietnamese mạn hoạ are the same word. Cantonese maan6 waa6-2 matches.

It is a kind of [図画](図画.md), drawing, made for amusement, and a picture magazine is the [画報](画報.md).
