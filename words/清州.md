## Notes
- [清](../characters/清.md) "pure, clear" + [州](../characters/州%20(char).md) "state" — the name of Cheongju, a city of South Korea.

清州 (Cheongju) is the capital of North Chungcheong Province ([[忠清]]北道) in South Korea (from memory, not re-checked). The name reads "clear province". The Jikji, the oldest surviving book printed with movable metal type, was printed at Heungdeok Temple in Cheongju in 1377 (from memory, not re-checked).

English and Chinese Wiktionary have 清州 as the Korean city (Mandarin Qīngzhōu, Cantonese cing1 zau1). Korean Wiktionary and Japanese Wiktionary have no entry listed, so Korean 청주 and Japanese せいしゅう are composed from the characters' readings. The Korean name 청주 is also the word for the rice wine written [[清酒]] in Chinese and Japanese. Vietnamese Thanh Châu is composed from the character pages (the string is the Vietnamese name of other places too, per the Vietnamese Wikipedia's disambiguation page).
