## Notes
- [公 (char)](../characters/公%20(char).md) "public, fair" + [明 (char)](../characters/明%20(char).md) "clear, bright" — "just and impartial" (of a judge, ruling, or decision). `vietnamese: công minh` is one of the most common everyday Vietnamese words for this concept (xét xử công minh, "to judge fairly"), a direct match. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 공명 matches Sino-Korean 공명 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄫㄇ⼶ㄫ unique among both words and characters).
