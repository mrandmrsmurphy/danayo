## Notes
- [楷 (char)](../characters/楷%20(char).md) "model; regular" + [書](../characters/書.md) "writing; script"

楷書 is regular script: the square, upright style of Chinese characters, the standard for printing and learning. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin kǎishū as "regular script" and Japanese かいしょ. Korean 해서 and Vietnamese khải thư are the same word. Cantonese kaai2 syu1 matches.

It is the base of [書法](書法.md), calligraphy, and the flowing styles are the [行書](行書.md), semi-cursive, and [草書](草書.md), cursive.
