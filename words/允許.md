## Etymology
- [允](../characters/允.md) "grant, allow" + [許 (char)](../characters/許%20(char).md) "permit, allow"

## Notes
"To grant, permit, allow" (formal register, historically used of royal assent, e.g. Korean 윤허, "royal permission"). `vietnamese: doãn hứa` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese (confirmed via web search, alongside the equally valid alternate reading duẫn hứa), similar in register to chuẩn hứa. Filled the blank `pos: 事詞`. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 윤효 diverges from Sino-Korean 윤허 in the second syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ⼜ㄋㄏ⼄ unique among both words and characters).
