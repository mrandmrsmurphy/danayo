## Notes
- [介](../characters/介.md) "mediator" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "word" — "adposition" (the cover term for prepositions/postpositions), the grammatical category linking a noun phrase to the rest of a clause. mandarin jiècí, japanese かいし, korean 개사, and vietnamese giới từ are all the standard linguistic-terminology terms for this grammatical category, directly compositional and in near-complete cross-linguistic agreement. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 겨사 diverges from Sino-Korean 개사 via the AND-rule, since 介's own field independently diverges (despite 詞 itself being `kwin: true`). No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼶ㄙㄚ unique among both words and characters).
