## Notes
- [十 (char)](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" + [九 (char)](../characters/九%20(char).md) "nine"

Mandarin shíjiǔ, Cantonese sap6 gau2, Korean 십구 (sipgu), and Japanese じゅうきゅう (jūkyū) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese thập cửu is likewise compositional, from 十's thập and 九's own cửu.
