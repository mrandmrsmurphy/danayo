## Notes
- [十 (char)](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" + [九 (char)](../characters/九%20(char).md) "nine"

Mandarin shíjiǔ, Cantonese sap6 gau2, Korean 십구 (sipgu), and Japanese じゅうきゅう (jūkyū) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese thập cửu is likewise compositional, from 十's thập and 九's own cửu. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 십큐 diverges from Sino-Korean 십구 in the second syllable, matching 九's own individual `kwin: false` on its own character page. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄆㄎ⼜ unique among both words and characters).
