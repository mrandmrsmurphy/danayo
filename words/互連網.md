## Notes
- [互](../characters/互.md) "mutual" + [連](../characters/連.md) "to connect" + [網](../characters/網.md) "net, web" — literally "the mutually-connected network," a calque of "internet" (from *inter-* + *network*) that this vault's own compound preserves faithfully.

互連網 can also be shortened to just [[網]] ("the net/web"), the same abbreviation pattern English follows with "the net." Japanese and Korean both borrow the English word wholesale as a loanword (インターネット, 인터넷) rather than using any compositional calque. Vietnamese hỗ liên mạng is a defensible compositional reading confirmed via web search alongside its real-world abbreviated form liên mạng (dropping 互) — everyday Vietnamese, like Japanese and Korean, most often just uses the English loanword Internet directly rather than any Sino-Vietnamese compound. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 호련망 diverges entirely from the loanword-based Korean field. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄛㄌ⼶ㄋㄇㄚㄫ unique among both words and characters).
