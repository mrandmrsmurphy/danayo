## Notes
- [佐](../characters/佐.md) "help" + [賀](../characters/賀.md) "congratulate"

佐賀 is Saga, written "assist, congratulate". The place name is old and its origin is uncertain; the reading さが is the on-reading of 佐 (さ) plus 賀 (が), so the characters are best read for sound and not for sense.

Saga lies in northwest Kyushu, with its capital Saga, the old Nabeshima domain. It contains the Yayoi-period site Yoshinogari and the porcelain towns of Arita and Imari, and Karatsu.

In Dan'a'yo 佐賀 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 사가 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
