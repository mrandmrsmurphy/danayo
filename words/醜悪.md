## Notes
- [醜](characters/醜.md) "ugly, shameful" + [悪 (char)](characters/悪%20(char).md) "evil, bad" — "ugly, homely."

Mandarin chǒu è, cantonese cau2 ok3, japanese しゅうあく, and korean 추악 are all directly attested standard readings. **Found and fixed a genuinely blank `vietnamese` field on 悪(char).md itself** (filled with the standard attested reading "ác," as in 罪惡/tội ác, "crime"), enabling the honest compositional "xú ác" for this word (醜's own xú + 悪's own ác). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 춧악 diverges from Sino-Korean 추악 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄨㄛㄚㄎ unique among both words and characters).

**Found and fixed `characters:` disambiguation** (bare 悪 → "悪 (char)," since [[悪]] has its own word page). Both character-page citations already correctly present. 醜's own `stand_in` is this exact word (醜悪), so 醜 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 醜悪; 悪's own `stand_in` is instead 悪 itself, so this word does not legitimize 悪.
