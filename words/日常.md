## Notes
- [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" + [常](../characters/常.md) "common, frequent" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [常](../characters/常.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry. The ordinary, recurring stuff of day-to-day life.

Mandarin rìcháng, Cantonese jat6 soeng4, Japanese にちじょう, Korean 일상, and Vietnamese nhật thường are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄧㄊㄙ⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
