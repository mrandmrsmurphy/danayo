## Notes
- [溶](../characters/溶.md) "melt, fuse" + [接 (char)](../characters/接%20(char).md) "contact, connect" — welding.

溶接 is welding: joining metal parts by melting their joint areas so that they fuse. 溶 is the bound "melt, fuse" behind [[溶化]] and [[溶融]]; 接 is "join, connect." It belongs to [[冶金]] broadly, and unlike [[鋳造]] (casting) and [[鍛造]] (forging) it joins existing pieces rather than shaping a new one. The Dan'a'yo headword is the Japanese 溶接 (ようせつ); Korean 용접 is the same compound. Mandarin has róngjiē (熔接, an alias here) but its everyday word for welding is 焊接 (hànjiē), built on a different character, 焊, that Dan'a'yo does not use. Dan'a'yo follows the 溶接 form shared by Japanese and Korean, which Mandarin and Cantonese 熔接 also recognize.
