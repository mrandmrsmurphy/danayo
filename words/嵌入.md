## Notes
- [嵌](../characters/嵌.md) "inlay" + [入 (char)](../characters/入%20(char).md) "enter" — this word is the viable form of [嵌](../characters/嵌.md): the character's meaning (to inlay; to embed) is identical, but 嵌 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 嵌入 is its `stand_in` compound.

嵌入 spans both a concrete craft sense (to inlay one material into another, as with a gemstone set into metal) and an abstract technical sense (to embed one thing structurally within another) — most commonly encountered today in 嵌入式 (qiànrùshì, "embedded," as in 嵌入式系統, "embedded system," a computer system built into a larger device rather than standing alone).

Japanese かんにゅう and Korean 감입 are both standard compositional Sino-readings. **`vietnamese` intentionally left blank**: no attested Sino-Vietnamese compound for this exact word could be confirmed; modern Vietnamese computing terminology instead uses the native verb nhúng for "embedded" (hệ thống nhúng, "embedded system"), paralleling how [[天竺鼠]] and other words in this vault default to a native term over an unconfirmed Sino-Vietnamese guess. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 캄닙 diverges from Sino-Korean 감입 in both syllables. No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄚㄇㄋㄧㄆ unique across both words and characters).
