## Notes
- [友](../characters/友.md) "friend" + [誼](../characters/誼.md) "friendship" — `characters/誼.md`'s own `stand_in` points to this word (友誼), meaning 誼 is a bound morpheme that never appears independently outside this compound; 友's own `stand_in` points elsewhere (朋友). `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: `characters/友.md` is `kwin: false`, `characters/誼.md` is `kwin: true` → compound `false`.
- **Reading**: yǒuyí is a very widespread mispronunciation (誼's phonetic component 宜 is yí, misleading speakers), but the standard, dictionary-correct reading is yǒuyì only — matching `characters/誼.md`'s own stored yì. The parallel Cantonese ji6 likely reflects the same confusion.
- **Vietnamese**: hữu nghị is the genuine, extremely common Sino-Vietnamese reading (verified via search, used constantly in diplomatic/official contexts, e.g. "Cầu Hữu Nghị," the Friendship Bridge); tình bạn, the everyday native Vietnamese word for "friendship," is not a reading of 友誼 itself.
