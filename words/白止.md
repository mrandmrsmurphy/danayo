## Notes
- [白 (char)](../characters/白%20(char).md) "white" + [止](../characters/止.md) "stop" — this word writes what is properly 白芷 (using the phonetic component 芷 rather than the bare radical-free 止), but no character page for 芷 exists in this vault; since 芷 is itself 形声 with 止 as its phonetic component, the pronunciation is unaffected either way, which is why 止's own readings already match the real term exactly.

白止/白芷 (báizhǐ) is Angelica dahurica, a flowering plant in the carrot family whose dried root is a major herb in East Asian traditional medicine, used to relieve headaches, nasal congestion, and pain, and as a common ingredient in Chinese herbal formulas and skincare preparations. Mandarin báizhǐ, Cantonese baak6 zi2, Japanese びゃくし (byakushi), Korean 백지 (baekji), and Vietnamese bạch chỉ are all standard, stable pharmacological and botanical terms across the Sinosphere, with no drift or narrowing.
