## Notes
- [鳥 (char)](../characters/鳥%20(char).md) "bird" + [巣](../characters/巣.md) "nest" — legitimizing note: 巣's own `stand_in` is [[鳥巣]] itself, already annotated in its own citation; 鳥's own `stand_in` is 鳥 itself, so transitivity fails — no `#cranberry`.

Mandarin niǎocháo, Cantonese niu5caau4, and Japanese す (su) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 둥지 is the standard native term; the colloquial synonym 새집 ("bird house") is also common. **Fixed a real bug**: `vietnamese` was làm tổ ("to build a nest," a verb phrase) rather than the noun itself — corrected to tổ chim.
