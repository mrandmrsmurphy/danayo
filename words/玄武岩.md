## Notes
- [玄](characters/玄%20(char).md) "deep, mysterious" + [武](characters/武%20(char).md) "military" + [岩](characters/岩.md) "rock" — literally "Xuanwu rock" (referencing 玄武, the Black Tortoise of the Four Symbols, whose color/shape this dark volcanic rock recalls); no stand-in relationship applies (玄's and 武's own stand-ins are themselves; 岩's own is [[岩石]]). Unlike [[玄暈]], this word genuinely uses 玄's own reading — no alias substitution is involved here.

玄武岩 means basalt.

Mandarin xuánwǔyán, Cantonese jyun4 mou5 ngaam4, Japanese げんぶがん (GEN+BU+GAN), and Korean 현무암 are all fully compositional, matching 玄's, 武's, and 岩's own stored citations exactly. Vietnamese bazan is the real, standard Vietnamese term (a loanword from French "basalte"), rather than a compositional reading. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⼔ㄋㄇㄨㄚㄇ unique across both words and characters).
