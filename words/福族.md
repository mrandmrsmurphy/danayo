## Notes
- [福](../characters/福%20(char).md) "fortune, blessing" + [族](../characters/族.md) "tribe, clan" — "the Fortunate Tribe, Asher." Their own `stand_in` values: 福's is itself; 族's is [[家族]]; none points here, so 福族 is an independent compound.

福族 is the tribe of Asher, Zilpah's second son, whose name means "happy": "Happy am I!" (Genesis 30:13). It is a Dan'a'yo **neologism**, a meaning-based name, "Fortunate Tribe." Compare [[武族]] (Gad) and [[明族]] (Issachar). See [[基督敎]].

- Hebrew name: אָשֵׁר (Asher) (注音 ·ㄚㄙㄝㄌㄜ).

- See [基督敎](../lexipedia/基督敎.md).
