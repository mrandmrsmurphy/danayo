## Notes
- [愛](../characters/愛%20(char).md) "love" + [慕](../characters/慕.md) "admire, yearn" — "love and yearn," to adore. Their own `stand_in` values: 愛's is itself; 慕's is [[思慕]]; none points here, so 愛慕 is an independent compound.

愛慕 is to adore: to love and admire someone, often from a distance. Mandarin àimù, Cantonese oi3 mou6 and Japanese あいぼ are attested, and Korean 애모 is the same compound. Compare [[思慕]] (longing), [[憧憬]] (yearning) and [[恋]] (romantic love).
