## Notes
- [貸](../characters/貸.md) "lend, load" is legitimized by this exact compound (its own stand-in). [出 (char)](../characters/出%20(char).md) "exit" is separately legitimized by [[出]] itself. No cranberry.

Mandarin dàichū and korean 대출 (also the real common words for "loan") confirmed standard and compositional. Japanese かしだし is a real, extremely common native (kun'yomi) reading, kept as-is rather than the rarer Sino form.
## Etymology
