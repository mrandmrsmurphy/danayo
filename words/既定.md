## Notes
- [既](../characters/既.md) "already, previously" + [定](../characters/定.md) "decided, fixed" — already decided; established before the present discussion; predetermined

Wiktionary: Chinese "to have already been established; to be fixed; to be set" and attributive "established; fixed; set"; Japanese 既定 (きてい) "established, given, foregone, predetermined". The traditional spelling 旣定 is kept as an alias. Compare [[決定]].
