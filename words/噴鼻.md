## Notes
- [噴](../characters/噴.md) "spray; spurt" + [鼻 (char)](../characters/鼻%20(char).md) "nose"

噴鼻 is to sneeze: to expel air suddenly and noisily through the nose and mouth. The real word is 噴嚏, which Wiktionary glosses as "sneeze" with Mandarin pēntì and Cantonese pan3 tai3. 嚏 has no vault page, so 鼻 stands in for it, as 玄 stands in for 眩 in [玄暈](玄暈.md); the Mandarin and Cantonese fields keep the readings of the real word. Korean 분비 is the reading of the characters, and no Japanese reading is attested.

The act is done through the [鼻](鼻.md), nose, and the fluid of a cold is the [鼻水](鼻水.md).
