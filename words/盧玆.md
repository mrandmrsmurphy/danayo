## Notes
- [盧](../characters/盧.md) "cottage" + [玆](../characters/玆.md) "here" — a 代用字 form: 盧 and 玆 stand in for the rare characters 鸕 and 鶿 of the real compound 鸕鶿, which are not in the vault.

盧玆 is the cormorant, written for 鸕鶿 (Mandarin lúcí, Cantonese lou4 ci4), which Wiktionary glosses "fishing cormorant" and "cormorant." 盧 matches 鸕 in Mandarin (lú), and 玆 and 鶿 share the Korean reading 자, so the substitution keeps the sound close. The same device gives [白路](白路.md) for 白鷺 and [蒼路](蒼路.md) for the crane.
