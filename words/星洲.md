## Notes
- [星](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" + [洲](../characters/洲%20(char).md) "islet, sandbar" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 星 and 洲's own stand-ins are themselves). The Starry Islet; a classical Chinese sobriquet for Singapore, evoking its island setting. The name is preserved in the masthead of 聯合早報, Singapore's major Chinese-language daily.

Mandarin xīngzhōu, Cantonese sing1 zau1, Japanese せいしゅう, Korean 성주, and Vietnamese tinh châu are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄫㄐㄨㄛ unique across both words and characters).
