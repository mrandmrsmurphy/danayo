## Notes
- [世](../characters/世.md) "world" + [俗 (char)](../characters/俗%20(char).md) "common practice, vulgar" — literally "the customs of the world." No stand-in relationship applies (世's own `stand_in` is [[世界]]; 俗's own is itself).

世俗 means worldly, secular, mundane — the affairs and concerns of ordinary life and society, as opposed to the religious, sacred, or monastic (traditionally contrasted with 僧, "monastic"; see [[俗 (char)]]'s own Notes for this same root sense). This vault's word for Rosenfelder's "worldly."

Mandarin shìsú, Cantonese sai3 zuk6, Japanese せぞく, Korean 세속, and Vietnamese thế tục are all standard, fully compositional readings. `kwin` is false: 世 is `kwin: false`, 俗 is `kwin: true`. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄙㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
