## Notes
- [高 (char)](../characters/高%20(char).md) "high" + [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" — no `#cranberry`: both 高's and 人's own `stand_in` point to themselves; neither points here.

Mandarin gāorén, Cantonese gou1jan4, Japanese こうじん, Korean 고인, and Sino-Vietnamese cao nhân are all standard, fully compositional readings, denoting a person of noble/elevated character.
