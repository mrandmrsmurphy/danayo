## Notes
- [通 (char)](../characters/通%20(char).md) "to pass through; communicate" + [告](../characters/告.md) "inform"

通告 is "to give notice; to notify," and as a noun "a notification." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 通告 (tōnggào, Cantonese tung1 gou3) is a verb "to give notice; to announce" and a noun "notification; announcement; notice"; Japanese つうこく is a noun "notification" and a suru-verb "to give notice, to notify"; Korean 통고 is "notification"; and Vietnamese thông cáo is "announcement; communiqué; report." It differs from [布告](布告.md) "proclaim" in being addressed to a particular party, as when the Imperial Government notifies the four powers in the 1945 surrender broadcast ([玉音放送](../translation/玉音放送.md)).

It is related to [報告](報告.md) "report" and [忠告](忠告.md) "advise," which share 告, and to [通知](通知.md) "notify; inform."
