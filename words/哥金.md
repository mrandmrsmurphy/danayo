## Notes
- [哥](../characters/哥.md) + [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal" — a transliteration of *Copernicus*, after astronomer Nicolaus Copernicus. 哥 is the first syllable of 哥白尼, the standard Mandarin transliteration of "Copernicus," truncated rather than spelled out in full, in the same pattern as 邁金 (Meitnerium) and 西博金 (Seaborgium). Japanese and Korean instead borrow the IUPAC name as a loanword (コペルニシウム, 코페르니슘).

Vietnamese scientific nomenclature likewise borrows the IUPAC name directly as Copernici, rather than a Sino-Vietnamese compositional reading — filled in to match the Japanese/Korean loanword pattern already established above. `mandarin`/`cantonese` are deliberately left blank: 哥金 is this vault's own internal coinage (a truncated transliteration, not the real single-character name officially used in Chinese chemistry — that character, 鎶, is a distinct novel coinage with its own reading), so there is no genuine Mandarin/Cantonese pronunciation of *this specific two-character compound* to record honestly.
