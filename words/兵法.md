## Notes
- [兵](../characters/兵.md) "soldier" + [法 (char)](../characters/法%20(char).md) "rule, law" — "the art of war, tactics." Mandarin bīngfǎ, Cantonese bing1 faat3, Japanese へいほう, Korean 병법, and Vietnamese binh pháp are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 병팝 diverges from Sino-Korean 병법 in the second syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄅ⼶ㄫㄆㄚㄆ unique among both words and characters). The most famous work of this genre, Sun Tzu's *Art of War* (孫子兵法), has no dedicated vault page of its own yet.
