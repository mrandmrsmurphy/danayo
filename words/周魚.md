## Notes
- [周](../characters/周.md) "circumference" (used here purely as the phonetic component) + [魚 (char)](../characters/魚%20(char).md) "fish" — spelled with its phono-semantic components (周 phonetic + 魚 semantic) rather than the single character 鯛/鲷, which is on the vault's [[grammar/文法 - 98違法字|forbidden character list]] specifically because it "can be broken into parts." "Sea bream" (Japanese たい/鯛, a fish especially prominent in Japanese cuisine and symbolism, e.g. celebratory 鯛 dishes). **Genuine cross-linguistic mismatch documented**: Vietnamese has no Sino-Vietnamese calque of this compound — the real Vietnamese name for this fish is the native descriptive term **cá tráp** ("bream fish"); a compositional "chu ngư" from 周+魚 would not be real usage (domain knowledge, not independently confirmed by search).
