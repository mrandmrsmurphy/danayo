## Notes
- [目 (char)](../characters/目%20(char).md) "eye" + [的](../characters/的.md) "target" + [語](../characters/語.md) "word" — "the target word," built on [[目的]] "target, objective" (the stand-in that legitimizes 的) plus 語. 語's own `stand_in` is [[言語]], so no `#cranberry` applies to this compound.

Wiktionary: Japanese 目的語 is the grammar term "object", and Korean 목적어 is its hanja form, "object" in grammar. Chinese 目的語 (mùdìyǔ) means "target language", not "object"; the Chinese grammatical term is 賓語 "object" (grammar), whose Vietnamese reading is tân ngữ, the `vietnamese` field here. A Mandarin reader must not take 目的語 in Japanese or Korean grammar as "target language" (see [[原言語]]). The counterpart is [[主語]]; compare [[他動詞]] and [[対象]]. The vault's grammar pages use 主語–動詞–目的語 for the order of `文法 - 04句法`.
