## Etymology
- [宗](../characters/宗.md) "sect" + [教](../characters/教.md) "teach" — stand-in for [[宗]], which cannot appear independently

## Notes
- "Religion," in the broad, category sense (基督教 "Christianity," 佛教 "Buddhism," etc. are all a 宗教). `korean: 종교` is the standard, extremely common everyday Korean word. `vietnamese: tông giáo, tôn giáo` are both attested Sino-Vietnamese forms — tôn giáo is the modern standard word for "religion," while tông giáo is an older/rarer variant reading of the same two characters (paralleling 宗's own tông/tôn alternation).
- **Flagging a real discrepancy for the character sweep, not touched here**: `characters/教.md` stores its own reading as `注音: ㄍ⼄ㄨ` (羅馬字 gyou, 諺文 굣), but every word built on 教 in this vault — this page, [[教授]], [[佛教]], [[教会]], [[教材]] — consistently uses the *different* syllable ㄍ⼘ㄨ (gyau/걋) instead, including in two already-perfected sibling pages ([[教授]], stamped 2026-07-05; [[教材]], stamped 2026-08-03). The vault's own syllable pages document the tension directly: [[syllables/ㄍ⼄ㄨ]] lists 教 as a "Common" member of that syllable but immediately notes it "requires [[教授]]" — whose own attested ruby reading is ㄍ⼘ㄨ, from [[syllables/ㄍ⼘ㄨ]] instead. This word's own `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` are left as `gyau`/`걋`/`ㄍ⼘ㄨ`-based, matching every other real attestation rather than 教's own stored (and apparently outlying) character-page value.
