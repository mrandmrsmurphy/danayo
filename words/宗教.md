## Etymology
- [宗](../characters/宗.md) "sect" + [教](../characters/教.md) "teach" — stand-in for [[宗]], which cannot appear independently

## Notes
- "Religion," in the broad, category sense (基督教 "Christianity," 佛教 "Buddhism," etc. are all a 宗教). `korean: 종교` is the standard, extremely common everyday Korean word. `vietnamese: tông giáo, tôn giáo` are both attested Sino-Vietnamese forms — tôn giáo is the modern standard word for "religion," while tông giáo is an older/rarer variant reading of the same two characters (paralleling 宗's own tông/tôn alternation).
- **Real bug found and fixed**: `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` had all used the old minority "gyau" reading for 教 instead of the majority-established "gyou" — corrected. (This page previously flagged this as an open discrepancy, reasoning from [[教授]]/[[教材]]'s then-still-buggy state; both have since been independently corrected to "gyou," and `characters/教.md`'s own "## Words" backlink list confirms "gyou" as the overwhelming majority form across its ~20 other compounds — 教's own character-page citation was correct all along, not an outlier.)
- `kwin: false` confirmed (宗 alone matches Korean, 教 doesn't — AND-rule). No homophones (注音 ㄐㄛㄫㄍ⼘ㄨ unique among both words and characters).
