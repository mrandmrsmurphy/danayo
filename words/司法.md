## Notes
- [司](../characters/司.md) "to manage, take charge of" + [法 (char)](../characters/法%20(char).md) "law"

司法 is the judiciary: the administration of justice, and the branch of government that does it. 司 here means "to be in charge of", so the word is literally "in charge of law". Wiktionary glosses Mandarin sīfǎ as "administration of justice; judiciary; judicature", Japanese しほう as "justice, judiciary, a national function", and lists Korean 사법 and Vietnamese tư pháp as the matching forms. Mandarin also uses the word for forensics.

The word names the system as a whole, covering the courts, the judges and the prosecutors, and it pairs with [立法](立法.md), the legislative branch. It overlaps with [Government](../lexipedia/Government.md), since the judiciary is one of the branches of government.
