## Notes
- [放](../characters/放.md) "release" + [逐](../characters/逐.md) "pursue, chase"

放逐 means "to send into exile, to banish" — the transitive act, done by an authority *to* someone else, contrasting with [流亡](流亡.md) and [亡命](亡命.md), both of which describe the exiled person's own state or action. Mandarin fàngzhú is a real, standard (if somewhat literary) word for banishment. Japanese ほうちく (hōchiku) is likewise real, used both in a literal exile sense and figuratively (放逐される, "to be ousted" from a position or organization).

Cantonese fong3 zuk6, Korean 방축, and Vietnamese phóng trục are compositional readings.
