## Notes
- [放](../characters/放.md) "release" + [逐](../characters/逐.md) "pursue, chase" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (放's own is [[釈放]]; 逐's own is [[追逐]]), so 放逐 is an independent compound. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: `characters/放.md` and `characters/逐.md` are both individually `kwin: false`.

放逐 means "to send into exile, to banish" — the transitive act, done by an authority *to* someone else, contrasting with [[流亡]] and [[亡命]] above, both of which describe the exiled person's own state or action. Mandarin fàngzhú is a real, standard (if somewhat literary) word for banishment. Japanese ほうちく (hōchiku) is likewise real, used both in a literal exile sense and figuratively (放逐される, "to be ousted" from a position or organization).

Cantonese fong3 zuk6, Korean 방축, and Vietnamese phóng trục are compositional; none is independently confirmed as a commonly used standard term in living usage.
