## Notes
- [半](../characters/半%20(char).md) "half" + [族](../characters/族.md) "tribe, clan" — "a half-tribe." Their own `stand_in` values: 半's is itself; 族's is [[家族]]; none points here, so 半族 is an independent compound.

半族 is a half-tribe: one of the two halves into which the Double Tribe, [[双族]] (Joseph), is counted. Joseph's two sons each became a tribe, so the twelve tribes are made up by [[忘族]] (Manasseh) and [[豊族]] (Ephraim), each a half-tribe of Joseph. In the books of Numbers and Joshua the phrase "half-tribe of Manasseh" also names the half of Manasseh that settled east of the Jordan. In anthropology the same word names a moiety, one of the two halves of a society. Compare [[部族]] (tribe) and [[氏族]] (clan).
