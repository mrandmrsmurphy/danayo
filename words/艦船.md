## Notes
- [艦](../characters/艦.md) "warship" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 艦 cannot appear independently outside this compound. [船](../characters/船.md) "boat, ship" is separately legitimized by [[船舶]]. No cranberry: transitivity fails since 船's own `stand_in` doesn't point here.

**Resolves a flagged discrepancy**: `characters/船.md` had noted that 艦船 (and [[宇宙船]]) appeared to use a genuinely different Dan'a'yo syllable for 船 (ㄐ⼔ㄋ/jwen) than the majority of citations (ㄙ⼔ㄇ/swem, confirmed regular via 船's own documented Middle Chinese derivation and just re-confirmed on [[船舶]]/[[船尾]]/[[船籍]]). Investigating this word directly: `諺文` had already correctly stored 쉄 (swem's own hangul, matching the majority group), while `羅馬字`/`注音` alone had drifted to the jwen forms (hamjwen/ㄏㄚㄇㄐ⼔ㄋ) — an internal three-way split within this single word's own frontmatter. Corrected `羅馬字`/`注音` to hamswem/ㄏㄚㄇㄙ⼔ㄇ, matching `諺文` and the regular derivation. This means the "genuine split" was actually a bug all along, at least for this word; [[宇宙船]] should be checked the same way when the sweep reaches it.

`kwin` is false per the AND-rule: 船 individually carries `kwin: false` (despite 艦's own `kwin: true`). Filled blank vietnamese (hạm thuyền). Mandarin jiànchuán, cantonese laam6syun4 (space removed), japanese かんせん (kansen), and korean 함선 (hamseon) were all already correct, fully compositional readings.
