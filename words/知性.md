## Notes
- [知 (char)](../characters/知%20(char).md) "know" + [性](../characters/性.md) "nature, quality" — literally "the nature of knowing," i.e. intellect or intelligence as an inherent quality of mind.

知性 names the intellect broadly — the capacity for reasoning, understanding, and knowledgeable judgment, often contrasted with pure emotion or instinct (知性と感性, "intellect and sensibility"). Mandarin zhīxìng, Cantonese zi1 sing3, Japanese ちせい (chisei), Korean 지성 (jiseong), and Vietnamese tri tính are all fully compositional and used in this same intellectual/philosophical register across the sphere.
