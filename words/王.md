## Notes
- [王](../characters/王%20(char).md) "king" — this word is the stand-in for its own character. Using this character alone is considered diminutive or informal; the full form is [[国王]].

Mandarin wáng, Cantonese wong4, Japanese おう, Korean 왕, and Vietnamese vương are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (⺢ㄫ/'wang/왕) with the unrelated [[往]] ("go towards").
