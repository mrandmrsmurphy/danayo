## Notes
- [終](../characters/終.md) "end" + [了 (char)](../characters/了%20(char).md) "done, finished, over" — 終's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 了's own is [[了]] itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 終了 legitimizes 終 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

終了 means an end or conclusion — a formal, procedural finishing (as in the end of a meeting, contract, or process).

Mandarin zhōngliǎo, Cantonese zung1 liu5, Japanese しゆうれう (the historical kana spelling of しゅうりょう, shūryō), and Korean 종료 (jongnyo) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 終了 is itself a common attested word for "end, conclusion" in all four languages. Vietnamese chung liễu is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
