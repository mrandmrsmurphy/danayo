## Notes
- [秀](../characters/秀%20(char).md) "bear fruit, blossom, shoot" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 秀 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

秀 originally described a rice plant bearing grain or coming into bloom — a 会意 combination of 禾 ("rice plant") and 引 ("to draw out"), the grain emerging from the stalk. From this agricultural image the sense extended to "outstanding, excellent" (the best of the crop), the meaning that now dominates in compounds: 優秀 ("excellent, outstanding"), 秀麗 ("elegant, graceful"), and its use as the top grade in some East Asian school marking systems.

Mandarin xiù, Cantonese sau3, Japanese しゅう (shū), and Korean 수 (su) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese tú is likewise the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading, seen in tú tài ("bachelor's degree, talented scholar").
