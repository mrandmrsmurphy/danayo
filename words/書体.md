## Notes
- [書](../characters/書.md) "write" + [体](../characters/体.md) "body; form"

書体 is a script style or typeface: the form in which characters are written or printed. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin shūtǐ as "style of calligraphy; calligraphic style" and Japanese しょたい as "a style of lettering". Korean 서체 is the same word. Cantonese syu1 tai2 matches.

The main styles are the [楷書](楷書.md), [行書](行書.md), [草書](草書.md), [隷書](隷書.md) and [篆書](篆書.md), all taught in [書法](書法.md).
