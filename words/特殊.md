## Etymology
- [特](../characters/特.md) "special, distinguished" + [殊](../characters/殊.md) "special, unique" — 殊's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 特's own is [[特別]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 殊 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

特殊 means special, exceptional.

Mandarin tèshū, Cantonese dak6 syu4, Japanese とくしゅ (TOKU+SHU), Korean 특수, and Vietnamese đặc thù were all already correct and fully compositional. Fixed `hsk_level` formatting (unquoted 2 → quoted "2"). Removed blank `swadesh`/`aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄜㄎㄙㄨ unique across both words and characters).
