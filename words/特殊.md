## Notes
- [特](../characters/特.md) "special, distinguished" + [殊](../characters/殊.md) "special, unique" — 殊's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 特's own is [[特別]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 殊 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

特殊 means special, exceptional: different in kind from the ordinary, as in "special circumstances" or "a special case". It is a near-synonym of [[特別]], but the two lean apart. 特別 is the warm, everyday "special, particular, extra"; 特殊 is the more technical, classificatory word for what falls outside the normal run. The shared CJKV sense is kept in Dan'a'yo.

特 began as a word for a bull, a single choice male animal, and so came to mean "singular, outstanding". 殊 began as "to cut off, to kill" (as in 殊死, "a fight to the death") and by extension "cut apart, different, distinct". Together they say "singled out and set apart". Mandarin tèshū, Japanese とくしゅ, Korean 특수 and Vietnamese đặc thù are all the same compound with the same meaning, and 特殊 is a standard word in all four.
