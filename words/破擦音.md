## Notes
- [破](../words/破.md) "break" (stand-in: itself) + [擦](../words/擦.md) "rub, scrape" (stand-in: itself) + [音 (char)](../characters/音.md) "sound" (stand-in: [[音楽]]) — an affricate consonant: a stop released into a fricative.

破擦音 means an affricate consonant. Mandarin pòcāyīn, Japanese はさつおん, and Korean 파찰음 are all real, standard phonetics terms, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Cantonese po3 caat3 jam1 is a straightforward compositional reading. Vietnamese vỡ sát âm is compositional only; the real term is "âm tắc xát."

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot**: the concatenated string `ㄆㄜㄑㄚㄊㄨㄇ` admits two legal partitions — the intended 破(ㄆㄜ)+擦(ㄑㄚㄊ)+音(ㄨㄇ), and a false alternate that crosses the 擦|音 boundary (ㄆㄜ+ㄑㄚ+ㄊㄨㄇ) — so the dot at the true 擦|音 boundary is required: ㄆㄜㄑㄚㄊ·ㄨㄇ.
