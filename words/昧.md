## Notes
- [昧](../characters/昧%20(char).md) "dark, benighted" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [昧](../characters/昧%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

昧 means dark or benighted (as in 曖昧, "ambiguous," and 蒙昧, "ignorant").

Mandarin mèi, Cantonese mui6, Japanese まい, Korean 매, and Vietnamese muội are all standard, fully compositional readings. Three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all mai/매/ㄇㄚㄧ) with [[呆]] ("confused") and [[苺]] ("strawberry") — completing the reciprocal callout anticipated on 呆's own page.
