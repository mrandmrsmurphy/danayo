## Notes
- [相](../characters/相.md) "mutual" + [思](../characters/思.md) "think" — literally "to think of each other," i.e. to long for someone, especially a distant or unrequited love.

相思 names lovesickness — the aching, preoccupied longing for someone absent or unattainable. It is deeply embedded in classical Chinese poetry, most famously in Wang Wei's 相思 (also known by its opening line 紅豆生南國, "red beans grow in the southern land"), where red beans (相思豆, "lovesickness beans") became a lasting poetic emblem of romantic longing.

Mandarin xiāngsī, Cantonese soeng1 si1, Japanese そうし (sōshi), Korean 상사 (sangsa), and Vietnamese tương tư are all fully compositional and retain the same poetic, romantic register across the sphere.
