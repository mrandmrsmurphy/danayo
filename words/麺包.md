## Notes
- [麺 (char)](../characters/麺%20(char).md) "flour" + [包](../characters/包.md) "to wrap; a bun"

麺包 (also written 麵包 and 面包) is "bread," any baked good with a crust. According to Wiktionary, Chinese 麵包 (miànbāo, Cantonese min6 baau1) is "bread; baked good; roll; bun," and Japanese lists 麵包 as an ateji spelling (a jukujikun) of パン, so `japanese` めんぽう is the compositional reading, not the everyday word. Wiktionary has no Korean or Vietnamese section, so `korean` 면포 and `vietnamese` diện bao are compositional readings too; the everyday words are 빵, a loan, and bánh mì (from memory, not re-checked). 麺 here is the vault form of the character 麵 "flour," and 包 "to wrap" is the baked wrapped loaf. It is used in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [平臥即是正義](../translation/平臥即是正義.md), in 朝 食 麺包 + 鶏卵 "bread and eggs in the morning." Related: [麺](麺.md) "flour," [餅](餅.md) "pastry" and [饅頭](饅頭.md) "steamed bun."
