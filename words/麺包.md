## Notes
- [麺 (char)](../characters/麺%20(char).md) "flour" + [包](../characters/包.md) "wrap, pack" — no `#cranberry`: 麺's own `stand_in` is 麺 itself, 包's is [[包装]]; neither points here.

麺包 covers plain, savory bread specifically; for pastries and sweet bread, use [[餅]].

Japanese パン and Korean 빵 are both loanwords ultimately from Portuguese *pão* (introduced via early trade contact), rather than compositional readings. Sino-Vietnamese/native Vietnamese bánh mỳ is the real everyday term.
