## Notes
- [動](../characters/動%20(char).md) "action" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "word" — no stand-in relationship applies (動 is its own stand-in; 詞's own is [[単詞]]).

動詞 is the grammatical term for "verb" — literally "action word," a word denoting an action, occurrence, or state of being.

Japanese どうし, Korean 동사, and Vietnamese động từ are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄫㄙㄚ unique across both words and characters).
