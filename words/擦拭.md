## Notes
- [擦 (char)](../characters/擦%20(char).md) "wipe, scrub, rub, scour, brush" + [拭](../characters/拭.md) "wipe" — stand-in for [[拭]], which cannot appear independently

擦拭 means to wipe clean: to rub a surface with a cloth or similar to remove dirt or moisture (擦拭乾淨, "wipe until clean"; 擦拭桌面, "wipe the table"; 擦拭傷口, "wipe a wound"). The compound pairs 擦 "wipe, scrub, rub" with 拭 "wipe" in a near-synonym doubling, and is the written, formal-register word.

Mandarin cāshì and Cantonese caat3 sik1 are real written or formal words for "to wipe clean". Vietnamese sát thức is a real Sino-Vietnamese compound, though the everyday native phrase is lau chùi. Neither Japanese nor Korean appears to use 擦拭 as a living compound: the values recorded here (さっしょく, 찰식) are compositional on'yomi and Sino-Korean readings. What each language actually says is the native verb: Japanese 拭く (ふく) or 拭う (ぬぐう), Korean 닦다 (dakda) or 씻다 (ssitda).
