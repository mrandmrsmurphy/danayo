## Notes
- [黙](../characters/黙.md) "silent, quiet" + [示](../characters/示.md) "show" — "silent showing," apocalypse. Their own `stand_in` values: 黙's is [[沈黙]]; 示's is [[開示]]; none points here, so 黙示 is an independent compound.

黙示 is apocalyptic revelation, truth shown in veiled signs, as in the Book of Revelation; in Chinese it can also mean to hint or imply. Mandarin mòshì (默示), Cantonese mak6 si6, Japanese もくし and Korean 묵시 use it. The character 黙 has the alias 默. It is the hidden kind of revelation, where [[啓示]] is the open kind. Compare [[予言]] (prophecy).
