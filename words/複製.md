## Notes
- [複](../characters/複.md) "duplicate" + [製](../characters/製.md) "make" — "make a duplicate." Their own `stand_in` values: 複's is [[重複]]; 製's is [[製作]]; none points here, so 複製 is an independent compound.

複製 is to copy or reproduce: to make a duplicate of something. Mandarin fùzhì, Cantonese fuk1 zai3, Japanese ふくせい and Korean 복제 all use it. A reproduced document is [[複写]], and a photocopier is [[複印机]].
