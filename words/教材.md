## Notes
- [教](../characters/教.md) "teach" + [材](../characters/材.md) "material" — "teaching material(s)."

`vietnamese: giáo tài` is compositional from each character's own confirmed reading (教's giáo, 材's tài); modern Vietnamese more often reaches for giáo trình ("curriculum/textbook") or tài liệu giảng dạy for this concept, but giáo tài is transparently well-formed from the same roots. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 걋채 diverges from Sino-Korean 교재 in the second syllable's vowel — the same divergence pattern shared with its homophone [[交叉]].
