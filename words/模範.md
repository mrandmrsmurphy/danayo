## Notes
- [模](../characters/模.md) "imitate" + [範](../characters/範.md) "pattern; model" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [範](../characters/範.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 模's own stand-in is [[模擬]].

模範 means model or exemplar.

Mandarin mófàn, Cantonese mou4 faan6, Japanese もはん, Korean 모범, and Vietnamese mô phạm are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 模's and 範's own stored citations exactly). No homophones (注音 ㄇㄛㄈㄚㄇ unique across both words and characters).
