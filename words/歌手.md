## Notes
- [歌](../characters/歌.md) "song" + [手 (char)](../characters/手%20(char).md) "hand; person skilled"

歌手 is a singer: a person who sings professionally or well. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin gēshǒu as "singer; vocalist; artist" and Japanese かしゅ as "singer". Korean 가수 is the same word. Cantonese go1 sau2 matches.

The act is [唱歌](唱歌.md), to sing, and the thing sung is the [歌曲](歌曲.md), song. A singer of a chorus takes part in a [合唱](合唱.md).
