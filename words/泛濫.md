## Notes
- [泛](../characters/泛.md) "flood; inundate" + [濫](../characters/濫.md) "flooding; excessive" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 泛 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (濫's own stand-in is [[汎濫]], not this compound, so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character here).

泛濫 means to flood, overflow, spread unchecked — a third orthographic variant alongside [[氾濫]] and [[汎濫]], all built on near-synonymous "overflow" characters (氾, 汎, 泛). Unlike 氾 and 汎, which share an identical Dan'a'yo reading (ㄈㄚㄇ), 泛's own reading is distinctly ㄈㄧㄇ (fim/삠) — confirmed by a fresh grep that 泛濫's 注音 ㄈㄧㄇㄌㄚㄇ is NOT shared with 氾濫/汎濫's ㄈㄚㄇㄌㄚㄇ, so no cross-variant homophone applies here.

Mandarin fànlàn, Cantonese faan3 laam6, Japanese はんらん, Korean 범람, and Vietnamese phiếm lạm are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 泛's and 濫's own stored citations exactly (identical real-language readings to 氾濫/汎濫, since 泛/氾/汎 converge in every daughter language despite their distinct Dan'a'yo-internal readings). No homophones (注音 ㄈㄧㄇㄌㄚㄇ unique across both words and characters).
