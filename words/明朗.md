## Notes
- [明](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright" + [朗](../characters/朗.md) "clear, distinct" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [朗](../characters/朗.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (明's own stand-in is [[明]] itself, an unrelated word).

明朗 means bright, clear, or cheerful.

**Real bug found and fixed**: removed a false homophone claim with [[命令]] ("order, command") — that apparent collision was actually a symptom of a contamination bug on 命令's own page (its 諺文/注音 had been mistakenly copied from this word); once 命令 was corrected to its own proper reading, the two words are no longer homophones. Filled blank `vietnamese: minh lãng`. Mandarin mínglǎng, Cantonese ming4 long5, Japanese めいろう, and Korean 명랑 are all standard readings, matching Dan'a'yo's own reading exactly (`kwin: true`). No homophones (注音 ㄇ⼶ㄫㄌㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
