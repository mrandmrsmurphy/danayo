## Notes
- [唐](../characters/唐.md) "Tang, China" + [詩](../characters/詩.md) "poem" — no stand-in relationship applies (唐's own `stand_in` is `名専字`; 詩's own is [[詩歌]]).

唐詩 names Tang poetry, the celebrated verse tradition of the Tang dynasty (618–907 CE).

Japanese とうし and Vietnamese Đường thi are both standard readings. Korean 당시 is compositional and coincidentally identical to the unrelated everyday word 當時 ("at that time") — not a bug. No homophones (注音 ㄉ⺢ㄫㄙㄧ unique across both words and characters).
