## Notes
- [黄 (char)](../characters/黄%20(char).md) "yellow" + [泉](../characters/泉.md) "fountain, wellspring, spring" — no `#cranberry`: 黄's own `stand_in` is 黄 itself, 泉's is [[源泉]]; neither points here.

**Fixed a real contamination bug**: `japanese` comma-joined "よみ,こうせん" — kept よみ (yomi), the real, culturally significant term for the Japanese mythological underworld (Izanami's realm), over the compositional こうせん.

Mandarin huángquán, Cantonese wong4cyun4, Korean 황천, and Sino-Vietnamese hoàng tuyền are all standard, fully compositional readings for the underworld/realm of the dead.
