## Notes
- [新](../characters/新%20(char).md) "new" + [約](../characters/約.md) "covenant, agreement" — "new covenant," the New Testament. Their own `stand_in` values: 新's is itself; 約's is [[約束]]; none points here, so 新約 is an independent compound.

新約 is the New Testament, and the new covenant: the part of the Bible about [[救偕]] (Jesus), and the covenant God makes through him. Mandarin xīnyuē, Cantonese san1 joek3, Japanese しんやく and Korean 신약 all use it, and Vietnamese tân ước does too. It pairs with [[旧約]] (the Old Testament). Compare [[契約]] (contract) and [[聖書]] (Bible).
