## Notes
- [梁 (char)](../characters/梁%20(char).md) + [州 (char)](../characters/州%20(char).md) — Liang Province, the eighth of the [[九州]]

Wiktionary: Chinese 梁州, archaic, Liangzhou, and the Melody of Liangzhou (the name of an ancient song). The *Tribute of Yu* (尚書, 禹貢, Wikisource) has the section 梁州 beginning 華陽、黑水惟梁州, "from the south of Mount Hua to the Black River is Liangzhou". The word names the province as listed among the [[九州]]; the Korean field gives the North Korean form 량주.
