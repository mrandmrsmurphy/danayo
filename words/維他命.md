## Notes
- [維](../characters/維.md) "maintain" + [他](../characters/他%20(char).md) "other" + [命](../characters/命.md) "life" — a phonetic-semantic transliteration of "vitamin," roughly "sustain-other-life." 維's own `stand_in` is [[維持]], 命's is [[運命]] and 他's is itself; none points here, so 維他命 is an independent compound.

維他命 is a vitamin. Mandarin wéitāmìng and Cantonese wai4 taa1 ming6 are the Taiwan and Hong Kong forms; mainland Mandarin prefers 維生素 (wéishēngsù), and Cantonese also uses 維他命 as the standard word. Japanese uses ビタミン and Korean 비타민, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[抗生素]] (antibiotics).
