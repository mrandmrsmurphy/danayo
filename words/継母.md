## Notes
- [継](../characters/継.md) "continue, persist" + [母](../characters/母.md) "mother" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (継's is [[継続]], 母's is [[母親]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

継母 names a stepmother — literally "continuing mother," the one who continues the mother's role.

Mandarin jìmǔ, Cantonese gai3 mou5, and Korean 계모 (gyemo) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 継母 is itself a common attested word for "stepmother" in all three. Japanese ままはは (mamahaha) is the native reading (まま "step-," はは "mother"), distinct from the on-reading compound. Vietnamese kế mẫu is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
