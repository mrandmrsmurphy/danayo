## Notes
- [字 (char)](../characters/字%20(char).md) "character" + [源](../characters/源.md) "source, origin" — "etymology (of a Chinese character)," the historical origin of a glyph's form and meaning. `vietnamese: tự nguyên` is compositional (domain knowledge, not independently confirmed by search). Korean `자원` is also the extremely common, unrelated word 資源 ("resources," as in natural resources) — a genuine homophone collision in Korean between "character etymology" and "resources," though not a Dan'a'yo-internal one.
