## Notes
- [猫 (char)](../characters/猫%20(char).md) "cat" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character 猫 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

猫 is the domestic cat. The character is a 形声 compound of the animal radical 犬 and the phonetic 苗. The alias 貓 is the traditional spelling; 猫 is the form used in Japan and in simplified Chinese. The word also forms the animal compounds [[豹猫]] (leopard cat) and [[熊猫]] (panda, literally "bear-cat").

Mandarin māo and Cantonese maau1 are the ordinary words for "cat". Japanese has the native word ねこ (neko) as its everyday reading, which English Wiktionary traces to an onomatopoeic ね plus the diminutive suffix こ (first attested in a text of 794), and keeps the Sino-Japanese びょう and みょう for compounds such as 愛猫 (beloved cat). Korean uses the native 고양이 for the animal and the Sino-Korean 묘 only in compounds. Vietnamese has the native word mèo, and the Hán-Việt miêu appears in compounds. In every language but Chinese, then, the character is the written form of a separate native word; the Dan'a'yo word follows the shared Sinitic sense.

No other word shares the 注音 ㄇ⼘ㄨ (checked 2026-10-12).
