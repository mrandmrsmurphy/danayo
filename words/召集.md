## Notes
- [召](../characters/召.md) "summon, convene" + [集](../characters/集.md) "collect" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [召](../characters/召.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (集's own stand-in is [[集合]]).

召集 means to summon or convene — calling people together for a meeting or assembly.

Japanese しょうしゅう, Korean 소집, and Vietnamese triệu tập are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `pos: 事詞`; fixed empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼄ㄨㄐㄧㄆ unique across both words and characters).
