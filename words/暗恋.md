## Notes
- [暗](../characters/暗%20(char).md) "dark, secret" + [恋](../characters/恋%20(char).md) "romantic love" — "secret love," to have a crush. Their own `stand_in` values: 暗's is itself; 恋's is itself; none points here, so 暗恋 is an independent compound.

暗恋 is to have a secret crush: to love someone without telling them. Mandarin ànliàn (暗戀) and Cantonese am3 lyun2 are the ordinary words. Japanese says 片思い and Korean 짝사랑, so those two fields hold the character readings. Compare [[恋]] (romantic love) and [[秋波]] (a flirtatious glance).
