## Notes
- [慶](../characters/慶%20(char).md) "congratulate, celebrate" + [州](../characters/州%20(char).md) "state" — the name of Gyeongju, a city of South Korea, the old capital of Silla.

慶州 (Gyeongju) is a city of North Gyeongsang Province ([[慶尚]]北道) in South Korea and was the capital of [[新羅]] (Silla) for most of a thousand years (from memory, not re-checked). The province name 慶尚 is formed from the first characters of 慶州 and 尙州 (Sangju) (from memory, not re-checked). The name reads "celebration province".

English and Chinese Wiktionary have 慶州 as the Korean city (Mandarin Qìngzhōu, Cantonese hing3 zau1), and Korean Wiktionary has the string as well; English Wiktionary's Japanese section gives the reading キョンジュ (from the Korean) and a dated reading けいしゅう, which is taken here as the Sino-Xenic reading. Vietnamese Khánh Châu is the name used by the Vietnamese Wikipedia for Gyeongju.
