## Notes
- [字 (char)](../characters/字%20(char).md) "character" + [典](../characters/典.md) "canon, reference work" — a character-by-character reference work, i.e. a dictionary in the specific sense of a character/word lookup tool, as distinct from [[事典]]'s topic/fact-organized encyclopedia sense.

Vietnamese tự điển matches these exact characters and remains the standard, everyday Vietnamese word for "dictionary." `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 지던 diverges from Sino-Korean 자전 in the first syllable's consonant, the same divergence pattern shared with its homophone [[事典]].
