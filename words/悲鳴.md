## Notes
- [悲 (char)](../characters/悲%20(char).md) "sad" + [鳴 (char)](../characters/鳴%20(char).md) "cry; sound"

悲鳴 is a scream: a sharp cry of fear, pain or distress, and in the figurative sense a cry of complaint. Wiktionary glosses Japanese ひめい as "scream, shriek" and Mandarin bēimíng as "to produce sad calls; to lament; to wail". Cantonese bei1 ming4 and Korean 비명 match.

It is a cry torn out by [恐怖](恐怖.md), fear, or [苦痛](苦痛.md), pain, shorter and sharper than the [号泣](号泣.md), wail, and the [呻吟](呻吟.md), groan.
