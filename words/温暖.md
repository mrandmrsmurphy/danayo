## Notes
- [温](../characters/温.md) "warm, lukewarm, tepid" + [暖 (char)](../characters/暖%20(char).md) "warm" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 温 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (暖's own stand-in is itself, so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

温暖 means lukewarm, tepid (also "global warming" in 温暖化).

Mandarin wēnnuǎn, Cantonese wan1 nyun5, Japanese おんだん, and Korean 온난 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 温's and 暖's own stored citations exactly). Filled the blank `pos` (性詞) and `vietnamese` fields — ôn noãn (温's own ôn + 暖's own citation noãn, picked over the sibling value hoãn, which has drifted to mean "to postpone" in modern Vietnamese). `kwin: true` because 諺文 온난 matches Korean 온난 exactly. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄛㄆㄋㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
