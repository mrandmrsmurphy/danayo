## Notes
- [睫](../characters/睫.md) "eyelash" + [毛 (char)](../characters/毛%20(char).md) "fur, hair" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 睫 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (毛's own stand-in is [[毛]] itself, so no `#cranberry`: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

睫毛 means "eyelash," the everyday, ordinary word across the Sinitic languages: Mandarin jiémáo and Cantonese zit6 mou4 are both fully compositional, matching 睫's and 毛's own stored citations exactly, and both are the standard words for "eyelash" in daily use. Japanese and Vietnamese instead default to native compounds for this concept — real Japanese uses まつげ (matsuge, native 目 "eye" + 毛 "hair," not this Sino-Japanese compound) and Vietnamese uses lông mi — so Dan'a'yo's しょうもう and tiệp mao here are compositional, dictionary-attested but secondary readings (真 しょうもう does appear in technical/medical Japanese; 睫毛/첩모 likewise exists in Korean dictionaries as a real but secondary term behind native 속눈썹) rather than each language's own everyday word, the same register gap already documented on [[毛]]'s own page for 毛皮/kegawa.

`kwin` is false per the AND-rule: 毛 alone is independently kwin-false (Dan'a'yo 맛 diverges from Sino-Korean 모 in the coda), which alone is enough regardless of 睫's own kwin-true status. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄝㄆㄇㄚㄨ unique among both words and characters).
