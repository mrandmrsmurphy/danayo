## Notes
- [毎 (char)](../characters/毎%20(char).md) "every; each; distributive" + [様 (char)](../characters/様%20(char).md) "manner; way; style; appearance"

毎様 is the distributive correlative in the 様 ("manner") column of the correlative table: "in each way; in every manner one by one; in each case in its own way". The character [毎 (char)](../characters/毎%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *moi*, ㄇㄛㄧ; its alias 每 is the traditional form, so 每様, 每樣 and 每样 are `aliases` entries here, not separate files) is the distributive morpheme of [毎日](毎日.md) "every day", [毎週](毎週.md) "every week", [毎月](毎月.md) "every month" and [毎年](毎年.md) "every year"; the free word is [毎](毎.md). [様 (char)](../characters/様%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *yang*, ⼘ㄫ) is "manner; way".

The word is built on the particle [様](様.md), which this vault documents as the adverbializer "-ly" (the manner case, [様態格](様態格.md)) and which names 毎様 among the correlatives it generates. As in [此様](此様.md), the result is "in the manner of each", a fixed adverbial compound rather than a noun phrase needing further marking. The manner is applied to each method or variant in turn, so it suits instruction and comparison: list several approaches and apply the same predicate to each. Illustrative only (invented, not from `grammar/`): 毎様試 "try (it) each way, one at a time"; 毎様説明 "explain (it) in each manner separately".

Its part of speech is 副用名詞 (adverbial noun), the class of every cell of the 毎 row ([毎事](毎事.md), [毎物](毎物.md), [毎人](毎人.md), [毎名](毎名.md), [毎処](毎処.md), [毎時](毎時.md), [毎多](毎多.md), [毎類](毎類.md)). `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md` (line 11) lists "Adverbial Nouns" as the class able to be the frame with 0-derivation, and `grammar/Linguistics Features.md` (line 68) states that an adverbial noun may be the topic or frame of a clause with no marking but needs marking when it modifies the main verb. No example in `grammar/` uses 毎様 itself, so its syntax is inferred from that rule.

Against its column neighbours: the collective [皆様](皆様.md) "in all ways; in every respect" asserts that the predicate holds across the whole range of manners at once, whereas 毎様 goes through the manners one by one; this is the same distributive/collective split as [毎人](毎人.md) against [皆人](皆人.md). Beware a false friend for Japanese readers: Japanese 皆様 (minasama) is the polite "everyone", but the Dan'a'yo word is the manner correlative and the person sense belongs to [皆名](皆名.md). 毎様 has no such Japanese reading. [某様](某様.md) "in some way" is the indefinite, naming one unspecified manner, and [何様](何様.md) "how?" is the interrogative. [此様](此様.md) "this way", [其様](其様.md) "that way" and [彼様](彼様.md) "that way yonder" each point at one particular manner, where 毎様 ranges over all of them.

The cross-linguistic fields were checked against Wiktionary on 2026-10-11. Wiktionary has no entry for 每樣, 毎様 or Korean 매양, so the compound is a Dan'a'yo construction and no field simply copies it. The nearest Mandarin form, 各樣 (gèyàng), has an entry glossed "a variety of; all sorts of; every", and 各種各樣 (gèzhǒng gèyàng) is glossed "all sorts; various types"; both are plural-collective, "all sorts", which is why the earlier `mandarin: gèzhǒng gèyàng` was dropped (it also gave the wrong sense, being nearer 皆様). The fields instead hold the distributive "each" words that do have entries: Mandarin 各自 (gèzì, "each respectively"; Cantonese gok3 zi6), Japanese それぞれ (sorezore, "each, every, respectively") and Korean 각각 (gakgak, adverb "each, every, respective"). They give the distributive sense but are not manner-specific; the manner is carried compositionally, e.g. Japanese それぞれの方法で "by each one's method" (from memory, not re-checked). The earlier Japanese 各様に and Korean 각각의 방식으로 were removed as unattested or phrasal. Vietnamese *theo từng cách* / *mỗi cách* "in each way" is from memory, not re-checked; hvdic.thivien.net covers single characters (毎 mỗi, 様 dạng), not phrases, so no `vietnamese` field is entered.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md)'s correlative table (line 125; the same chart is in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`, line 150 in the 様 row, with the column header cell 毎 on line 142), 毎様 is the seventh cell of the 毎 ("every") row, after [毎事](毎事.md), [毎物](毎物.md), [毎人](毎人.md), [毎名](毎名.md), [毎処](毎処.md) and [毎時](毎時.md), and before [毎多](毎多.md) and [毎類](毎類.md). The table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), 63 forms, so 毎様 is parsed compositionally: 毎 = distributive, 様 = manner. It is listed on the Grammar page only.

The 羅馬字 is *moi'yang*, from the character pages (毎 *moi*, 様 *'yang*); the earlier *moy'yang* disagreed with the 毎 page. The apostrophe is the null-onset marker carried by 様's own romanization *'yang* (as in [此様](此様.md) *cǝ'yang*), and 諺文 뫼양 is 뫼 + 양, as on those pages.

The 注音 ㄇㄛㄧ⼘ㄫ carries no syllable-boundary dot. `grammar/文法 - 02音韻論.md` gives the structure (C)(G)V(C), with ㄫ barred from onset position and a y-glide barred before the vowel ㄧ, so ㄇㄛㄧ⼘ + ㄫ is impossible and ㄇㄛ + ㄧ⼘ㄫ is impossible (ㄧ⼘ is not a syllable in `syllables/`). The only partition is ㄇㄛㄧ + ⼘ㄫ, both in `syllables/`. The finer split ㄇㄛ + ㄧ + ⼘ㄫ (each also a legal syllable) would treat the diphthong ㄛㄧ as a boundary, which this vault does not do, as on [毎時](毎時.md) and [毎人](毎人.md). The same ㄧ⼘ㄫ sequence appears undotted in 大洋 (ㄉㄚㄧ⼘ㄫ) on `syllables/⼘ㄫ.md`.
