## Notes
- [余](../characters/余.md) "surplus, remainder" + [割 (char)](../characters/割%20(char).md) "to cut, divide" — "cosecant" (csc), the trigonometric reciprocal of sine, following the same 余-prefix pattern as [[余弦]] ("cosine") and [[余接]] ("cotangent") — 余 here marks the "co-" reciprocal/complementary function, matching the real mathematical sense of "co-" in English. `vietnamese: côsec` is the standard modern Vietnamese loanword for this function (from French cosécante). `kwin` is false: both 余 and 割 independently carry `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ⼄ㄍㄚㄊ unique among both words and characters).
