## Notes
- [昨 (char)](../characters/昨%20(char).md) "last, previous" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day, sun" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both are bare self-standing characters); 昨日 is an independent compound.

Mandarin zuórì and Cantonese zok6 jat6 are the readings of 昨日 itself, not of the sibling word 昨天 (zuótiān), which uses 天 "day". The `korean` field holds the Sino-Korean reading 작일, a real, formal or literary word for "yesterday"; the everyday native Korean word is 어제. Likewise the `japanese` field holds the Sino-Japanese reading さくじつ, while the everyday word is the native きのう. Vietnamese has no Sino-Vietnamese calque of 昨日 in use; the standard modern term is the native hôm qua, so the `vietnamese` field is omitted.
