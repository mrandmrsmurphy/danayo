## Notes
- [揚 (char)](../characters/揚%20(char).md) + [州 (char)](../characters/州%20(char).md) — Yang Province, the fifth of the [[九州]]

Wiktionary: Chinese 揚州 is the prefecture-level city Yangzhou in eastern Jiangsu and, historical, an ancient province, one of the Nine Provinces ([[九州]]) of ancient China; Japanese ようしゅう is the Jiangsu city; Vietnamese Dương Châu is the Han form of Yangzhou. The *Tribute of Yu* (尚書, 禹貢) names it with the words 淮、海惟揚州 ("between the Huai and the sea is Yangzhou", with 楊 as a variant). Du Mu's poem 遣懷 has the line 十年一覺揚州夢 (Wikisource). The word names both the province and the city that kept its name.
