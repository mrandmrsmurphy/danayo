## Notes
- [出 (char)](../characters/出%20(char).md) "exit" + [版](../characters/版.md) "edition, version" — "to publish, put out" (出版社, "publishing house"; 出版物, "publication"). Mandarin chūbǎn, Cantonese ceot1 baan2, Japanese しゅっぱん, Korean 출판, and Vietnamese xuất bản are all directly attested standard readings. **Real `kwin` bug found and fixed**: stored as `true`, but byte-level comparison shows Dan'a'yo's own 諺文 춛판 (coda ㅊ on the first syllable) genuinely diverges from this word's own korean field 출판 (coda ㄹ) — corrected to `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄨㄊㄆㄚㄋ unique among both words and characters).

**`kwin` update (2026-10-09):** `kwin` is now true. The Notes above may describe the ㄷ/ㄹ difference between the Dan'a'yo 諺文 and the Korean reading as a mismatch; under the vault's standing rule a Korean final ㄹ counts as equal to Dan'a'yo's final ㄷ for this comparison, so such a difference alone does not make `kwin` false.
