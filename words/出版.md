## Notes
- [出 (char)](../characters/出%20(char).md) "exit" + [版](../characters/版.md) "edition, version" — "to publish, put out" (出版社, "publishing house"; 出版物, "publication"). Mandarin chūbǎn, Cantonese ceot1 baan2, Japanese しゅっぱん, Korean 출판, and Vietnamese xuất bản are all directly attested standard readings.
