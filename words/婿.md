## Notes
- [婿](../characters/婿%20(char).md) "bridegroom, son-in-law" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

婿 means bridegroom or son-in-law — a daughter's husband, or a groom in a wedding.

Mandarin xù, Cantonese sai3, Japanese せい, Korean 서, and Vietnamese rể are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄙㄝㄧ/sei/세) with the unrelated [[細]] ("fine, thin, slender").
