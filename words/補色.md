## Notes
- [補](../characters/補.md) "supplement, make up" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — complementary color.

補色 is a complementary color: the color opposite another on the color wheel, which cancels it to grey when mixed and which contrasts most strongly beside it (red and green, blue and orange, yellow and violet). 補 is "to supplement, to make up for," because each color supplies what the other lacks. Mandarin bǔsè (also hùbǔsè 互補色), Cantonese bou2 sik1, Japanese ほしょく and Korean 보색 are the standard readings. See [[原色]] and [[三原色]].
