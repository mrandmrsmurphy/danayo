## Notes
- [威](../characters/威.md) "power, prestige" + [脅](../characters/脅.md) "coerce, threaten" — no stand-in relationship applies (威's own stand-in is [[威力]]; 脅's own is [[脅威]]).

威脅 is the reverse-order sibling of the already-existing [[脅威]] ("threat, menace"), the same pattern as [[安慰]]/[[慰安]] elsewhere in this vault. Mandarin wēixié and Korean 위협 (wihyeop) are both real, everyday, independently-attested words — in fact the more common form in both languages, especially as the verb "to threaten" (威脅/위협하다), with [[脅威]] leaning more toward the noun "a threat" as an abstract danger. Cantonese wai1 hip3 is the standard compositional reading. Japanese いきょう and Vietnamese oai hiếp are given compositionally; unlike Mandarin/Korean, Japanese more consistently reaches for 脅威 (きょうい) for "threat" specifically, so いきょう is not confirmed as independently attested everyday usage. `kwin` is false per the AND-rule: both 威 and 脅 are individually `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄛㄧㄏㄝㄆ unique among both words and characters).
