## Notes
- [威](../characters/威.md) "power, prestige" + [脅](../characters/脅.md) "coerce, threaten" — no stand-in relationship applies (威's own stand-in is [威力](威力.md); 脅's own is [脅威](脅威.md)).

威脅 is the reverse-order sibling of the already-existing [脅威](脅威.md) ("threat, menace"), the same pattern as [安慰](安慰.md)/[慰安](慰安.md) elsewhere in this vault. Mandarin wēixié and Korean 위협 (wihyeop) are both real, everyday, independently-attested words — in fact the more common form in both languages, especially as the verb "to threaten" (威脅/위협하다), with [脅威](脅威.md) leaning more toward the noun "a threat" as an abstract danger. Cantonese wai1 hip3 is the standard compositional reading. Japanese いきょう and Vietnamese oai hiếp are given compositionally; unlike Mandarin/Korean, Japanese more consistently reaches for 脅威 (きょうい) for "threat" specifically, so いきょう is not confirmed as independently attested everyday usage.
