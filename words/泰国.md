## Notes
- [泰](../characters/泰.md) "peaceful, safe" + [国](../characters/国.md) "nation" — no stand-in relationship applies (泰's own stand-in is [[泰然]]; 国's own is [[国家]]).

泰国 means Thailand.

Mandarin Tàiguó, Cantonese taai3 gwok3, Japanese たいこく, and Korean 태국 (genuinely the real Korean name for Thailand) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 泰's and 国's own stored citations exactly. **Genuine cross-linguistic divergence**: real-world Vietnamese does not calque this compound — the standard name is Thái Lan, a phonetic transcription unrelated to 国/quốc; filled the blank `vietnamese` field with the compositional Thái Quốc instead, the same kind of divergence already documented on [[図書館]]/[[水族館]]. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄚㄧㄍㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
