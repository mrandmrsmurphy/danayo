## Notes
- [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year" + [鑑](../characters/鑑.md) "mirror"

年鑑 is a yearbook or almanac: a reference book published once a year, summarising the year's facts. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin niánjiàn and Japanese ねんかん as "almanac; yearbook", and lists Korean 연감. Cantonese nin4 gaam3 matches.

It belongs with the reference books, beside the [事典](事典.md), encyclopedia, and the [詞典](詞典.md), dictionary. The 鑑 in it is "mirror" in the sense of a model for the year.
