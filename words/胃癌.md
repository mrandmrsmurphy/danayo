## Notes
- [胃 (char)](../characters/胃%20(char).md) "stomach, gizzard" — legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry by [[胃]] itself, not this word. [癌](../characters/癌.md) "cancer, carcinoma" is separately legitimized by [[癌症]].

胃癌 combines 胃 ("stomach") with 癌 ("cancer") — "stomach cancer." No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

**Found and fixed two real bugs**: `羅馬字` had a spurious extra leading apostrophe ('wi'am), but 胃's own reading (wi) carries no null-onset marker — corrected to wi'am; `諺文`/`注音` had already stayed correct (same class as 胃炎/胃痛's earlier fixes). `mandarin` was comma-joined with a stray second value, wèiyán — this is the *pre-1962* reading of 癌 (documented in detail on `characters/癌.md`'s own Notes: Mandarin ái is a deliberate 1962 change away from yán specifically to avoid clinical confusion with the homophonous 炎, i.e. with [[胃炎]] "gastritis"), not a live alternate — trimmed to just the character's own current stored reading, wèi'ái. Filled blank vietnamese (vị nham). `kwin: true` was already correct. Cantonese wai6ngaam4 (space removed), japanese いがん (igan), and korean 위암 (wiam) were already correct, fully compositional readings.
