## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" is separately legitimized by [[自身]]. [動 (char)](../characters/動%20(char).md) "move" is separately legitimized by [[動]] itself. [詞](../characters/詞.md) "word" is separately legitimized by [[単詞]]. No cranberry: none of the three constituents' own `stand_in` points here.

自動詞 combines 自 ("self"), 動 ("move"), and 詞 ("word") — "intransitive verb" (a verb whose action doesn't transfer to an object), the counterpart of [[他動詞]].

Mandarin zìdòngcí, cantonese zi6dung6ci4 (space removed), japanese じどうし (jidoushi), and korean 자동사 (jadongsa) were all already correct, fully compositional readings.
