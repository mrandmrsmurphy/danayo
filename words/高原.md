## Notes
- [高](../characters/高%20(char).md) "high" + [原](../characters/原.md) "plain" — "high plain." 高's own `stand_in` is itself and 原's is [[原始]]; neither points here, so 高原 is an independent compound.

高原 is a plateau: an elevated stretch of fairly flat land. Mandarin gāoyuán, Cantonese gou1 jyun4, Japanese こうげん, Korean 고원 and Vietnamese cao nguyên all use these characters. In Japanese 高原 also suggests a cool upland resort area. Compare [[草原]] (grassland) and [[山脈]] (mountain range).
