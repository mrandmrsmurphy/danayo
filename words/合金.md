## Notes
- [合](../characters/合%20(char).md) "join, combine" + [金](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal" — no stand-in relationship applies (合 is its own stand-in; 金's is [[金]] itself, an unrelated word).

合金 means alloy — a metallic mixture of two or more elements.

Japanese ごうきん, Korean 합금, and Vietnamese hợp kim are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Correction**: an earlier pass here claimed `合` deliberately reads kab/캅 in "mixture/blend" sense compounds as a genuine sense-dependent split from its bare-character gob/곱 — this was wrong. `syllables/ㄍㄛㄆ.md` (size 1) confirms gob/곱/ㄍㄛㄆ is 合's only real syllable; `syllables/ㄎㄚㄆ.md` (size 2) belongs to the unrelated characters 恰/怯. [[癒合]] independently uses the correct gob reading, contradicting the claimed split and exposing it as a copy-forward bug instead — corrected here to gobgim/곱김/ㄍㄛㄆㄍㄧㄇ. [[混合]] and [[組合]] carried the same bug, fixed alongside this one. No homophones.
