## Notes
- [合](../characters/合%20(char).md) "join, combine" + [金](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal" — no stand-in relationship applies (合 is its own stand-in; 金's is [[金]] itself, an unrelated word).

合金 means alloy — a metallic mixture of two or more elements.

Japanese ごうきん, Korean 합금, and Vietnamese hợp kim are all standard, fully compositional readings. `合` here reads kab/캅 rather than its bare-character citation gob/곱 — this matches an established alternate reading used across several "mixture/blend" sense compounds ([[混合]], [[組合]], [[癒合]], [[融合]]), distinct from the gob/곱 reading used in more literal "join/unite" compounds ([[合肥]], [[合成]], [[結合]], [[連合]]) — a genuine sense-dependent split, not an error. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄚㄆㄍㄧㄇ unique across both words and characters).
