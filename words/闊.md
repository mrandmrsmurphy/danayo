## Notes
- [闊 (char)](../characters/闊%20(char).md) "broad; wide" — this word is 闊's own stand-in.

闊 means broad or wide, in both literal (spatial) and figurative (broad-minded, expansive) senses — as in the chengyu [[海闊天空]] ("as boundless as the sky and sea"). Mandarin 闊/阔 (kuò), Cantonese 闊 (fut3), Korean 활 (hwal), and Sino-Vietnamese khoát all share this meaning across everyday and literary registers alike. Japanese 闊 (かつ) is comparatively rare as a standalone word — it survives mainly bound in compounds like 闊達 (kattatsu, "open-minded, magnanimous") and 闊歩 (kappo, "to stride confidently") rather than used alone.

闊 shares its Dan'a'yo syllable (kwad/콷/ㄎ⺢ㄊ) with 闕 (a proper-noun-only character for "imperial palace," with no standalone word of its own) and 蛞 (legitimized only through the compound [[蛞蝓]], "slug," whose own combined two-syllable reading differs from 闊's single-syllable one) — a syllable-level coincidence rather than a genuine word-level homophone, since neither of the other two characters has a matching one-syllable word to cross-link.
