## Notes
- [衝 (char)](../characters/衝%20(char).md) "collide" + [突](../characters/突.md) "dash; thrust"

衝突 is a clash: two things or two parties colliding, in the physical sense of a collision and in the figurative sense of a conflict of interests or opinions. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin chōngtū as "to clash; to conflict; to collide" and Japanese しょうとつ as "collision, crash; conflict of opinion". Vietnamese xung đột is the same word, and Korean 충돌 and Cantonese cung1 dat6 match.

The root [衝](衝.md) supplies the impact. A dispute that has hardened into a standing conflict is a [紛争](紛争.md), and a fight is a [戦闘](戦闘.md).
