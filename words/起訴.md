## Notes
- [起 (char)](../characters/起%20(char).md) "rise up" + [訴](../characters/訴.md) "sue"

起訴 is to bring a formal charge against someone in court: to indict, or to prosecute. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin qǐsù as "to indict; to prosecute; to bring a case against; to sue", and Japanese きそ as "prosecution; to indict". Korean 기소 and Vietnamese khởi tố ("to prosecute") are the matching forms.

The step sits between accusing and trying. [告訴](告訴.md) is the neutral "to accuse" and [誣告](誣告.md) the false accusation. 起訴 is the formal charge that starts the case, and [裁判](裁判.md) is the trial that follows. It is distinct from [控訴](控訴.md), which is an appeal after a first judgment.
