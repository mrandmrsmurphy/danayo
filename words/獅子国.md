## Notes
- [獅](../characters/獅.md) "lion" + [子](../characters/子.md) "nominaliser/suffix" + [国](../characters/国.md) "country" — "Lion Country"

獅子国 is the historical Chinese name of Sri Lanka. English Wiktionary has an entry only for Chinese 獅子國 (alias 獅子国; Mandarin Shīziguó, Cantonese si1 zi2 gwok3), marked historical and glossed "Sinhala; Sri Lanka". Japanese ししこく, Korean 사자국 and Vietnamese Sư tử quốc are built from the readings of the three characters.

The Chinese monk Faxian (法顯) spent two years in Sri Lanka and recorded his travels in India and Ceylon in the 《佛國記》. The older spelling 師子國 uses 師 for "lion".

Compare [獅城](獅城.md), the Lion City, Singapore, which also takes 獅 for "lion".
