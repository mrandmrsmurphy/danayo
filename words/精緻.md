## Notes
- [精](../characters/精.md) "spirit, essence, sperm" + [緻](../characters/緻.md) "delicate, fine" — 緻's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 精's own is [[精神]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 緻 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 緻 is individually `kwin: false`.

精緻 means delicate, fine, or exquisitely crafted — describing meticulous, refined work or design (精緻な, "delicately/exquisitely made"). Note that `korean` 정치 is a genuine coincidental homograph with the unrelated, extremely common word 政治 ("politics"), also read 정치 — the compositional Sino-Korean reading of 精緻 simply happens to spell identically, by chance.

Mandarin jīngzhì, Cantonese zing1 zi3, and Japanese せいち (seichi) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese tinh trí is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form.
