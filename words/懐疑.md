## Notes
- [懐](../characters/懐%20(char).md) "miss, think of" + [疑](../characters/疑.md) "doubt, suspect" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [疑](../characters/疑.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (懐's own stand-in is itself).

懐疑 means doubt or skepticism.

Mandarin huáiyí, Cantonese waai4 ji4, Japanese かいぎ, Korean 회의, and Vietnamese hoài nghi are all standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 회의 coincidentally matches the real Korean word for "meeting" (会議/會議), a real-Korean-only collision not extending to the Dan'a'yo level. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⺢·ㄧ unique across both words and characters).
