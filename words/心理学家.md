## Notes
- [心 (char)](../characters/心%20(char).md) "heart, mind" + [理](../characters/理.md) "reason, principle" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study" + [家](../characters/家.md) "house; family" — together forming [[心理学]] "psychology"; 家 here is the lexicalized "master of a field", as in [[哲学家]], [[運動家]] and [[儒家]]. No stand-in relationship applies (心's own stand-in is itself; 理's is [[理由]]; 学's is [[学習]]; 家's is [[家庭]]).

心理学家 is a psychologist: a person who has made [[心理学]], the study of the mind and behaviour, their field. 家 is not a productive suffix in Dan'a'yo, so the compound is listed on its own; it is the title for the established practitioner, in the same way as [[哲学家]] is the title of the philosopher.

Mandarin xīnlǐxuéjiā, Cantonese sam1 lei5 hok6 gaa1 and Southern Min sim-lí-ha̍k-ka are the forms Wiktionary lists for 心理學家, where it is the ordinary word for "psychologist". Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese use a different ending for the same person: Japanese 心理学者 (しんりがくしゃ), Korean 심리학자 and Vietnamese nhà tâm lý học (from memory, not re-checked). The `japanese`, `korean` and `vietnamese` fields hold the compositional Sino-readings (しんりがくか, 심리학가, tâm lý học gia), which were not checked against a dictionary. Dan'a'yo keeps the Chinese shape, with 家 for the title.
