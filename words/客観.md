## Notes
- [客](../characters/客.md) "guest" + [観](../characters/観.md) "view; to look"

客観 (traditional 客觀) is "objective; objectivity," the stance of seeing things as they are, apart from any one viewer. According to Wiktionary, Chinese 客觀 (kèguān, Cantonese haak3 gun1) is "objective"; Japanese きゃっかん, Korean 객관 ("objectivity") and Vietnamese khách quan ("objective") are the same word. The literal sense, "the view of the guest," contrasts with 主観 "the view of the host" (subjective), which is not a Dan'a'yo word yet. It is used in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [China](../lookup/HSK/China.md), in 宇宙 客観 之 唯一 真理 "the one objective truth of the universe." Related: [真実](真実.md) "truth" and [事実](事実.md) "fact."
