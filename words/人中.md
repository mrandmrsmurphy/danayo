## Notes
- [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" + [中 (char)](../characters/中%20(char).md) "middle" — the philtrum, the vertical groove between the nose and upper lip; a standard anatomical/TCM term (an acupuncture point of the same name) attested identically across Mandarin, Japanese, Korean, and Vietnamese (nhân trung, familiar from Vietnamese physiognomy and traditional medicine).

**Real content bug found and fixed**: `cantonese` had been mistakenly set to the Mandarin pinyin "rénzhōng;" instead of Cantonese jyutping — corrected to jan4 zung1 (compositional from each character's own reading). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 닌중 diverges from Sino-Korean 인중 in the first syllable's initial consonant. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄧㄋㄐㄨㄫ unique among both words and characters).
