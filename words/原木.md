## Notes
- [原](../characters/原.md) "origin, raw" + [木](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree, wood" — "raw wood," a log. Their own `stand_in` values: 原's is [[原始]]; 木's is itself; none points here, so 原木 is an independent compound.

原木 is a log: the unprocessed trunk of a tree, before it is sawn into lumber. Mandarin yuánmù and Cantonese jyun4 muk6 are the ordinary words. Japanese says 丸太 and Korean 통나무, so those two fields hold the character readings. Compare [[材木]] (lumber) and [[樹木]] (tree).
