## Notes
- [就 (char)](../characters/就%20(char).md) "go to; take up" + [寝 (char)](../characters/寝%20(char).md) "sleep"

就寝 is to go to bed: to lie down for the night. Wiktionary glosses Japanese しゅうしん as "going to bed; to go to bed". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 취침 matches.

It is a formal word for the start of the night's [睡眠](睡眠.md), sleep, and its end is [起床](起床.md), getting out of bed.
