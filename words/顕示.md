## Notes
- [顕](../characters/顕.md) "prominent, clear" + [示](../characters/示.md) "show, reveal, indicate" — no `#cranberry`: 顕's own `stand_in` is [[顕著]], 示's is [[開示]]; neither points here.

顕示 (xiǎnshì) is a real Mandarin word meaning "to clearly reveal, display" (often of power or intent, e.g. 顕示武力 "display military power"), and Cantonese 顕示 (hin2si6) likewise. Japanese けんじ (kenji, as in 自己顕示欲 "desire for self-display") and Korean 현시 (hyeonsi) are both standard terms with the same sense. Sino-Vietnamese hiển thị is likewise standard and very common today in a technical-display sense (e.g. màn hình hiển thị, "display screen").
