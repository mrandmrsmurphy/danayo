## Notes
- [名](../characters/名%20(char).md) "name" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "word" — no stand-in relationship applies (名 is its own stand-in; 詞's own is [[単詞]]).

名詞 is the grammatical term for "noun" — literally "name-word," a word denoting a person, place, thing, or idea.
