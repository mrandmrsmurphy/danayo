## Notes
- [名](../characters/名%20(char).md) "name" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "word" — no stand-in relationship applies (名 is its own stand-in; 詞's own is [[単詞]]).

名詞 is the grammatical term for "noun" — literally "name-word," a word denoting a person, place, thing, or idea.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `mandarin` had been stored as the simplified Chinese characters "名词" instead of the pinyin romanization — corrected to míngcí. `cantonese` was blank — filled ming4 ci4 (compositional). Japanese めいし, Korean 명사, and Vietnamese danh từ are all standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄧㄫㄙㄚ unique across both words and characters).
