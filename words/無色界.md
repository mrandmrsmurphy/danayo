## Notes
- [無 (char)](../characters/無%20(char).md) "without" + [色](../characters/色.md) "form; matter" + [界](../characters/界.md) "realm" — a translation of Sanskrit *ārūpya-dhātu*, the realm without form.

無色界 is the highest of the [三界](三界.md): the world of beings who have gone beyond matter as well as desire and have only a mind. It holds no matter at all, only the four mental aggregates of feeling, perception, formations and consciousness. It has four stages, those of boundless space, boundless consciousness, nothingness, and neither perception nor non-perception, and the highest of them, the last, is also called 有頂天. Strictly speaking a realm without form has no place and so does not lie above the [色界](色界.md), but the cosmology puts it at the top for convenience. Schools differ on what "without form" excludes: the Sarvāstivādins say there is no matter whatever, the Mahāsāṃghikas only gross matter. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Japanese readings are the attested ones; the Korean follows the North Korean readings of the characters.
