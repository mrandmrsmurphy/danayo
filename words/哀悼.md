## Notes
- [哀](../characters/哀.md) "sad, mournful" + [悼](../characters/悼%20(char).md) "lament" — no stand-in relationship applies (哀's own stand-in is [[哀傷]]; 悼 is its own stand-in).

哀悼 means to offer condolences or mourn — expressing grief over a death.

**Real bugs found and fixed**: `vietnamese` had literally been set to the Mandarin pinyin string "āidào" instead of an actual Vietnamese reading — corrected to `ai điệu` (compositional; note the closely related term 追悼 "truy điệu" is more commonly discussed in Vietnamese sources). `cantonese` was missing a space. `注音` had two garbled consonant letters (ㄚㄋㄙㄚㄨ instead of the correct ㄚㄧㄉㄚㄨ, matching each character's own citation) — corrected. Japanese あいとう and Korean 애도 are both standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄧㄉㄚㄨ unique across both words and characters).
