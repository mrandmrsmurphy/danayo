## Notes
- [擦](../characters/擦%20(char).md) "wipe, scrub, rub, scour, brush" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [擦](../characters/擦%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

擦 means to wipe, scrub, rub, scour, or brush: to move a surface repeatedly against another, whether to clean it (擦桌子, "wipe the table"; 擦鞋, "polish shoes"), to erase (擦掉, 橡皮擦, "eraser"), or to apply something by rubbing (擦藥, "apply ointment"). It also covers the sense of grazing contact (擦傷, "abrasion"; 擦肩而過, "brush past each other").

Mandarin cā, Cantonese caat3, Japanese さつ (摩擦/まさつ, "friction"; 擦過傷/さっかしょう, "abrasion"), Korean 찰 (마찰, "friction") and Vietnamese sát (ma sát, "friction"; xát, "to rub") are the standard readings. Japanese uses the native verb 擦る (こする) or 擦れる (すれる) for the everyday act, so on'yomi さつ is bound to compounds.
