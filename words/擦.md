## Notes
- [擦](../characters/擦%20(char).md) "wipe, scrub, rub, scour, brush" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [擦](../characters/擦%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

擦 means to wipe, scrub, rub, scour, or brush.

Mandarin cā, Cantonese caat3, Japanese さつ, Korean 찰, and Vietnamese sát are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones at the word level (注音 ㄑㄚㄊ is shared only with bound characters 刹/察).
