## Notes
- [犯](../characters/犯.md) "crime" + [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person"

犯人 is the person who committed a crime: the criminal, offender or culprit. Wiktionary glosses Japanese はんにん as "criminal; culprit", and Mandarin fànrén as "criminal" and also "convict; prisoner". Korean 범인 matches the Japanese sense. Vietnamese phạm nhân is recorded there as "convict", so Vietnamese leans toward the person already convicted.

Dan'a'yo takes the person who did the act, not a convicted one. That keeps it separate from [囚徒](囚徒.md), the prisoner, and from the suspect, who is only suspected. It is the counterpart of [被害者](被害者.md).
