## Notes
- [防](../characters/防.md) "guard against" + [衛](../characters/衛.md) "defend"

防衛 is defense: to guard a country or a position against attack. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin fángwèi as "to defend; to safeguard" and Japanese ぼうえい as "defense, self-defense; protection; to defend". Korean 방위 and Cantonese fong4 wai6 match.

It names the defending as a duty or a policy, where [防御](防御.md) is the defensive act or position and [国防](国防.md) is a nation's defense.
