## Notes
- [外](../characters/外.md) "outside" + [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" — literally "an outside person." No stand-in relationship applies (外's own `stand_in` is [[外部]]; 人's own is itself).

外人 means a stranger or outsider — someone without family or friendship ties to the group in question, not necessarily a foreign national. Mandarin wàirén keeps this broad "outsider, stranger" sense, distinct from the nationality-specific [[外国人]] ("foreigner"). Japanese がいじん (gaijin) and Korean 외인 have both narrowed toward "foreigner" in living colloquial use — the same direction as 外国人 rather than this word's broader Mandarin sense — so this vault's own "stranger, outsider" gloss follows Mandarin usage specifically.

Cantonese ngoi6 jan4 and Vietnamese ngoại nhân are the compositional readings from each character's own attested value. `kwin` is false: both 外 and 人 are individually `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ⺢ㄧㄋㄧㄋ unique across both words and characters).
