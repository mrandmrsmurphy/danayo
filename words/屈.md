## Notes
- [屈 (char)](../characters/屈%20(char).md) "bend; flex; crouch" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [屈 (char)](../characters/屈%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

屈 means to bend, flex, or crouch — physically (屈伸 "bend and stretch"), and by extension figuratively, to yield or submit (屈服 "to submit, capitulate," 屈辱 "humiliation"). It also names reasoned argument in [[理屈]] ("logic, reasoning" — an argument that bends a listener toward agreement).

Japanese くつ, Korean 굴, and Vietnamese khuất are all standard readings. No homophones among other words (注音 ㄎㄨㄊ; the unrelated character [[厥]] shares the same reading, but its own stand-in word doesn't collide with 屈's).
