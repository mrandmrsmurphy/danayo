## Notes
- [礬](../characters/礬.md) "alum" + [素](../characters/素.md) "element" — literally "alum-element," aluminum, named for its association with alum (potassium aluminum sulfate), the substance from which aluminum was first isolated — stand-in for [礬](../characters/礬.md), which cannot appear independently.
- We prohibit the modern Chinese characters 鋁/铝, a novel single-character coinage; Dan'a'yo instead reuses its own existing character 礬 for this element.

`mandarin` lǚ and `cantonese` leoi5 are the real, standard bare words for aluminum in each language (i.e. 鋁/铝's own real readings) — not compositional readings of Dan'a'yo's own 礬+素 (which would give fánsù/faan4 sou3) — following the periodic-table convention of recording each language's genuine attested word even when Dan'a'yo's own coinage takes a structurally different path. Korean's own word for aluminum, 알루미늄, is a direct loan from English, so Dan'a'yo's 반소 is a novel compositional coinage with no real Sino-Korean counterpart. Japanese アルミニウム and Vietnamese nhôm are both the real, standard words for aluminum, unrelated to either character's own citations.
