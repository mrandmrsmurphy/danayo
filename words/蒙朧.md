## Notes
- [蒙](../characters/蒙.md) "Mongolia, cover" is separately legitimized by [[蒙古]]. [朧](../characters/朧.md) "hazy, cloudy" is legitimized by this exact compound (its own `stand_in`) — transitivity fails, no cranberry. Describes something indistinct, visually or mentally.

Mandarin ménglóng, japanese もうろう, korean 몽롱 (also the real common word for "dazed, hazy"), and vietnamese mông lung (also a real, extremely common Vietnamese word for "hazy, vague") all confirmed standard and compositional.
