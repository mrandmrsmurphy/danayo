## Etymology
- [参](../characters/参.md) "to submit (a memorial to impeach)" (a classical administrative sense — not the more familiar "participate" sense) + [劾](../characters/劾.md) "to accuse, indict"

## Notes
A classical term, "to impeach, formally accuse (an official)" — historically, to submit a memorial to the throne charging an official with wrongdoing. `japanese`, `korean` and `vietnamese` readings are compositional (domain knowledge, not independently confirmed by search).
