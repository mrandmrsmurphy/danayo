## Notes
- [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" + [末](../characters/末.md) "end, tip" — literally "month-end".

月末 names the end of the month, contrasting with [月初](月初.md) ("beginning of the month") and [月中](月中.md) ("middle of the month") — the same three-way division documented on this word's own Calendar page entry, alongside the more precise 上旬/中旬/下旬 ten-day-period system.

Mandarin yuèmò, Cantonese jyut6 mut6, Korean 월말 (wolmal), and Japanese げつまつ (getsumatsu) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real everyday term in its language. Vietnamese nguyệt mạt is likewise compositional (月's nguyệt + 末's mạt, one of six candidates stored on 末's own page — the others look like corpus noise); everyday Vietnamese instead uses the native construction cuối tháng ("month's end"),.
