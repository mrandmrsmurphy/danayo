## Notes
- [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" + [末](../characters/末.md) "end, tip" — literally "month-end"; this word is the stand-in compound that legitimizes the bound character 末 (per its own stored `stand_in`).

月末 names the end of the month, contrasting with [[月初]] ("beginning of the month") and [[月中]] ("middle of the month") — the same three-way division documented on this word's own Calendar page entry, alongside the more precise 上旬/中旬/下旬 ten-day-period system.

Mandarin yuèmò, Cantonese jyut6 mut6, Korean 월말 (wolmal), and Japanese げつまつ (getsumatsu) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real everyday term in its language. Vietnamese nguyệt mạt is likewise compositional (月's nguyệt + 末's mạt, one of six candidates stored on 末's own page — the others look like corpus noise); everyday Vietnamese instead uses the native construction cuối tháng ("month's end"), the same "See Calendar" gloss already given on the Calendar page itself. **Real `kwin` bug found and fixed**: was stored as `true`, but Dan'a'yo 웓맏 does not exactly string-match Sino-Korean 월말 — both syllables' codas diverge (Dan'a'yo keeps the MC-checked-tone coda ㄷ in each, while Sino-Korean regularizes both 月's and 末's finals to ㄹ), the same recurring MC-checked-coda-vs-Sino-Korean-regularized divergence pattern documented repeatedly across this vault (including on 月 and 末 individually, and on compounds like [[十一月]]). Corrected to `kwin: false`. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ⼔ㄊㄇㄚㄊ unique among both words and characters).
