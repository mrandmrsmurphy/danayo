## Notes
- [冬 (char)](../characters/冬%20(char).md) "winter" + [瓜](../characters/瓜.md) "melon"

冬瓜 is the winter melon: the large pale gourd of *Benincasa hispida*, eaten as a vegetable and in soup. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin dōngguā as "winter melon; ash gourd; wax gourd; tallow gourd" and Japanese とうがん as "winter melon". Cantonese dung1 gwaa1 matches, and Korean 동과 is the reading of the characters.

It is a [瓜](瓜.md), gourd, that keeps through the winter, as the [南瓜](南瓜.md), pumpkin, also does. It is eaten as a vegetable, the [菜蔬](菜蔬.md) of a meal.
