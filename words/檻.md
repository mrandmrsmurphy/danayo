## Notes
- [檻 (char)](../characters/檻%20(char).md) "cage"

檻 is a cage: an enclosure of bars for an animal or a prisoner, and a fence or railing. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 檻 (alias 槛; Mandarin jiàn, Cantonese haam5) as "fence", "cage (for animal or prisoner)", "railing; balustrade" and, archaic, "prisoner transport cart"; read kǎn it is "doorsill". It lists Japanese おり, Korean 함 and Vietnamese hạm as readings of the hanja without a gloss.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [檻](../characters/檻%20(char).md), whose reading *hyam* is a new syllable. The related cage words are the [籠](籠.md), basket, and the [鳥籠](鳥籠.md), birdcage.
