## Notes
- [恐](../characters/恐.md) "fear, dread" + [龍](../characters/龍%20(char).md) "dragon" — no stand-in relationship applies (恐's own stand-in is [[恐慌]]; 龍's own is itself).

恐龍 means dinosaur — literally "fear dragon," a calque of the international scientific term (Greek deinos "terrible" + sauros "lizard").

Mandarin kǒnglóng, Cantonese hung2 lung4, Japanese きょうりゅう, Korean 공룡, and Vietnamese khủng long are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed missing "(char)" suffix on `characters`. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄛㄫㄌ⼄ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
