## Notes
- [炎](../characters/炎.md) "flame, inflammation" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — the color of a flame

Wiktionary lists 炎色 only under Japanese (えんしょく): "flame colour". 炎's own `stand_in` is 炎症 (inflammation) and 色's is 色彩, so this word is not a legitimizer for either.
