## Notes
- [炎](../characters/炎.md) "flame, inflammation" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — the color of a flame

炎色 is flame color: the hue a flame takes on, and above all the characteristic color a metal salt gives to a flame.  It is used chiefly in the scientific compound 炎色反応 (flame test), the analytical chemistry technique of identifying metal ions by the color their salts impart to a flame: sodium burns yellow, potassium violet, lithium crimson, copper green.  炎 is the flame and 色 the color, a natural-object coinage of the same kind as other X色 words on the Color page.

炎's own `stand_in` is 炎症 (inflammation) and 色's is 色彩, so this word is not a legitimizer for either.  Mandarin yánsè, Cantonese jim4 sik1 and Japanese えんしょく are the readings.  Korean 염색 is the same sound as 染色 ("dyeing"), so the Korean written form carries the difference.  `kwin` is false because the Dan'a'yo 엄식 differs from the Korean 염색.  No Vietnamese form was found attested, so none is given.
