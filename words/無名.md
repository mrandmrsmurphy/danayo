## Notes
- [無 (char)](../characters/無%20(char).md) "without" + [名 (char)](../characters/名%20(char).md) "name" — literally "without-name." Both constituents are also independent Dan'a'yo words ([無](無.md), [名](名.md)), so the compound can equally be parsed as two characters or two free words, with no difference in reading either way.

無名/无名 means namelessness, or to be anonymous/unnamed — describing someone or something without a name or reputation, as in an unsigned work or an unidentified person.

Mandarin wúmíng, Cantonese mou4 ming4, Korean 무명 (mumyeong), and Japanese むめい (mumei) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real everyday word for "nameless, anonymous" in its language. Vietnamese vô danh is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, matching this word's own compositional pattern with 無's more common alternate reading vô rather than a literal transliteration of 無's own stand-alone reading.
