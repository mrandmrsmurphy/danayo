## Notes
- [一 (char)](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" + [刻](../characters/刻.md) "a moment; quarter hour"

一刻 is "a moment, a short time," and in Chinese also "a quarter of an hour" and "stubborn." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 一刻 (yīkè, Cantonese jat1 hak1) is attested as "short time; moment," "fifteen minutes; quarter of an hour," and "stubborn," and Japanese いっこく is attested as "a moment." Wiktionary has no Korean or Vietnamese section, so `korean` 일각 and `vietnamese` nhất khắc are compositional. It is used in the 1919 Korean Declaration of Independence on [Korea](../lookup/Korean/Korea.md) (最後의 一刻ᄭᅡ지, "until the last moment"). Compare [一瞬](一瞬.md).
