## Notes
- [網](../characters/網%20(char).md) "netting, net, network" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 網 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

網 means netting, a net, or a network, as in 網球 (tennis, literally "net ball") and インターネット-derived internet senses.

Mandarin wǎng, Cantonese mong5, and Korean 망 (mang) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading あみ (ami, "net") rather than the on-readings モウ/ボウ (MOU/BOU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese võng is the standard reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones (亡/妄/孟/忘/忙/望/猛/盲/罔/茫/莽/虻 share the same syllable at the character level, but none has an independent word page).
