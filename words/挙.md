## Notes
- [挙](../characters/挙%20(char).md) "raise, example" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [挙](../characters/挙%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

挙 means to raise (as in 挙手, "raise a hand") or an example/instance.

Mandarin jǔ, Cantonese geoi2, Japanese きょ, Korean 거, and Vietnamese cử are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bugs: literal `vietnamese: null` and `korean: "null"`, both corrected using the character's own citation. Homophone of [[鋸]] "saw" (both 注音 ㄍ⼄) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words; checked other candidates sharing this reading (巨/拒/拠/渠/遽/距/裾) and confirmed none has an independent word at this exact reading.
