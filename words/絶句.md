## Notes
- [絶 (char)](../characters/絶%20(char).md) "cut off; end" + [句 (char)](../characters/句%20(char).md) "phrase"

絶句 is a jueju: the Chinese regulated poem of four lines. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin juéjù as "''jueju''" and Japanese ぜっく as "being at a loss for words". Korean 절구 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese zyut6 geoi3 matches.

It is the short sibling of the eight-line [律詩](律詩.md), and the standard form of [唐詩](唐詩.md), Tang poetry.
