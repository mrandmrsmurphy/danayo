## Notes
- [皆 (char)](../characters/皆%20(char).md) "all; every; universal" + [様 (char)](../characters/様%20(char).md) "manner; way; style; appearance"

皆様 is the collective (universal) correlative in the 様 ("manner") column of the correlative table: "in all ways; in every manner; in every respect, taken together". The character [皆 (char)](../characters/皆%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *gye*, ㄍ⼶) is the quantifier "all; every", the free word being [皆](皆.md); [様 (char)](../characters/様%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *yang*, ⼘ㄫ) is "manner; way". The word is built on the particle [様](様.md), which this vault documents as the adverbializer "-ly" (the manner case, [様態格](様態格.md)) and which names 皆様 among the correlatives it generates, so 皆様 is "in the manner of all", a fixed adverbial compound. The manner is treated as one totality: the predicate holds across the whole range of manners or aspects at once. Illustrative only (invented, not from `grammar/`): 皆様優 "excellent in every way"; 皆様同意 "agree in every respect". No example in `grammar/` uses 皆様 itself.

**False friend for Japanese readers.** Japanese 皆様 (minasama; Wiktionary gives 皆様 as an alternative spelling of 皆さま) is the honorific "everybody, everyone", from 皆 "all" + 様 as a politeness marker (Wiktionary's etymology: 皆 みな + 様 さま, "politeness marker"). The Dan'a'yo word is not that. The same two characters compose independently here as "all" + "manner", and the polite "everyone" is [皆名](皆名.md), with the plain "all people" being [皆人](皆人.md). The overlap is a coincidence of spelling, not a borrowing; a Japanese reader meeting 皆様 in a Dan'a'yo text must read it as the manner correlative.

Its part of speech is 副用名詞 (adverbial noun), the class of the whole 様 column ([此様](此様.md), [其様](其様.md), [彼様](彼様.md), [何様](何様.md), [毎様](毎様.md), [某様](某様.md)) and of [皆多](皆多.md), whereas the other cells of the 皆 row ([皆事](皆事.md), [皆物](皆物.md), [皆人](皆人.md), [皆名](皆名.md), [皆処](皆処.md), [皆時](皆時.md), [皆類](皆類.md)) are 代詞 (pronoun). The chart gives only form and 注音, not part of speech, so this follows the column convention, as in [毎様](毎様.md) and 皆多, and is not a statement of the grammar files. `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md` (line 11) lists "Adverbial Nouns" as the class able to be the frame with 0-derivation, and `grammar/Linguistics Features.md` (line 68) states that an adverbial noun may be the topic or frame of a clause with no marking but needs marking when it modifies the main verb.

Against its column neighbours: the distributive [毎様](毎様.md) "in each way" goes through the manners one by one and suits a list of methods, where 皆様 asserts comprehensiveness at once; this is the same collective/distributive split as [皆人](皆人.md) against [毎人](毎人.md). [某様](某様.md) "in some way" is the indefinite and [何様](何様.md) "how?" the interrogative. [此様](此様.md) "this way", [其様](其様.md) "that way" and [彼様](彼様.md) "that way yonder" each point at one particular manner, where 皆様 covers all of them. Within the 皆 row it is the manner counterpart of [皆処](皆処.md) "everywhere" and [皆時](皆時.md) "always". Among existing vault words, [一切](一切.md) "entirety; whole" is a plain noun of totality, not a manner word, and [多様](多様.md) "diverse" (性詞) says that there are many manners, not that something holds in every manner. [明様](明様.md) "brightly" is an unrelated 様-adverb on [Light](../lexipedia/Light.md), not a correlative. The vault has no word files for 全面, 各方面, 方方面面, 様々 or 色々; they are Chinese and Japanese words that express the sense only approximately.

The cross-linguistic fields were checked against Wiktionary on 2026-10-11. Wiktionary has no entry for Chinese 皆樣 or 皆様 (the Japanese 皆様 entry is only the redirect to the honorific above), and none for 皆样, so no `mandarin`, `japanese`, `korean` or `vietnamese` field is entered: the word is a Dan'a'yo compositional construction, and none of the readings the page held before (Mandarin gèfāngmiàn, Japanese あらゆる点で, Korean 모든 면에서, Vietnamese theo mọi cách) is a reading of the compound; they were equivalents of the meaning, and are removed. The Mandarin form was the worst fit: Wiktionary lists 各方面 (gèfāngmiàn) as a noun "each side; in all directions; various aspects", i.e. a distributive-plural word for "sides", and its 方方面面 (fāngfāng miànmiàn) as an idiom "every aspect". Wiktionary also has 全面 (quánmiàn) as an adjective "comprehensive; all-round; overall", a noun "overall situation; all aspects" and an adverb "fully; completely; entirely", the closest everyday Chinese equivalent of "in every respect", though it is not a manner compound. Japanese 色々 (iroiro) is an adjective "various", an adverb "variously, in many ways" and a noun "a variety", which is plural-variety rather than universal. Wiktionary's entry for 様々 gave no usable gloss in the part retrieved. Requests for 一切, 各樣 and 各样 were rate-limited on 2026-10-11; the 毎様 page recorded that Wiktionary has 各樣 (gèyàng) "a variety of; all sorts of; every". From memory, not re-checked: Japanese あらゆる点で, すべての面で and Korean 모든 면에서 as phrasal equivalents; Vietnamese *về mọi mặt* and *theo mọi cách*. hvdic.thivien.net confirms the single characters (皆 giai, 様 dạng), not phrases, so no `vietnamese` field is entered.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md)'s correlative table (line 126 is the 皆 ("all") row; the same chart is in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`, line 150 in the 様 ("manner") row, with the column header cell 皆 on line 142), 皆様 is the seventh cell of the 皆 row, after [皆事](皆事.md) "all matters", [皆物](皆物.md) "all things", [皆人](皆人.md) "all people", [皆名](皆名.md) "all honoured persons", [皆処](皆処.md) "all places" and [皆時](皆時.md) "all times", and before [皆多](皆多.md) "the whole amount" and [皆類](皆類.md) "all kinds". The table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), 63 forms, so 皆様 is parsed compositionally: 皆 = collective, 様 = manner. It is listed on the Grammar page only; [Light](../lexipedia/Light.md) lists 明様 but not this cell.

The spellings are the plain concatenation of the two character pages: 羅馬字 *gye* + *'yang* = gye'yang, 諺文 겨 + 양 = 겨양, 注音 ㄍ⼶ + ⼘ㄫ = ㄍ⼶⼘ㄫ, each checked letter by letter against 皆 (char) and 様 (char). The apostrophe is the null-onset marker carried by 様's own romanization *'yang*, as in [毎様](毎様.md) *moi'yang* and [此様](此様.md) *cǝ'yang*. The 注音 carries no syllable-boundary dot. `grammar/文法 - 02音韻論.md` gives the structure (C)(G)V(C) and bars ㄫ from onset position, so ㄍ⼶⼘ + ㄫ is impossible; `syllables/` has ㄍ⼶, ㄍ⼶ㄇ, ㄍ⼶ㄋ, ㄍ⼶ㄧ and ㄍ⼶ㄫ but no ㄍ⼶⼘, and no syllable ㄍ alone, so ㄍ + ⼶⼘ㄫ is also impossible. The only partition is ㄍ⼶ + ⼘ㄫ, both of which are in `syllables/`. There is no second complete partition, so no dot is needed, as in [毎様](毎様.md) and unlike [皆人](皆人.md), whose second syllable begins with ㄋ.
