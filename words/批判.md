## Notes
- [批](../characters/批.md) "criticism, comment" + [判](../characters/判.md) "judge, decide, determine" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [批](../characters/批.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (判's own stand-in is [[判断]]).

批判 means critique or criticize.

Mandarin pīpàn, Cantonese pai1 pun3, Japanese ひはん, Korean 비판, and Vietnamese phê phán are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄝㄧㄆㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
