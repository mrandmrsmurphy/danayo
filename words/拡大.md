## Notes
- [拡](../characters/拡.md) "expand, enlarge" + [大](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [拡](../characters/拡.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

拡大 means to expand or enlarge.

Mandarin kuòdà, Cantonese kwok3 daai6, Japanese かくだい, Korean 확대, and Vietnamese khuếch đại are all standard, fully compositional readings.
