## Notes
- [拡](../characters/拡.md) "expand, enlarge" + [大](../characters/大.md) "big" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [拡](../characters/拡.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

拡大 means to expand or enlarge.

Mandarin kuòdà, Cantonese kwok3 daai6, Japanese かくだい, Korean 확대, and Vietnamese khuếch đại are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `cantonese` had the wrong reading (kwong3→kwok3, matching 拡's own citation — likely confused with the phonetic component 広/kwong). No homophones (注音 ㄏ⺢ㄎㄉㄚㄧ unique across both words and characters).
