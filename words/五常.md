## Notes
- [五 (char)](../characters/五%20(char).md) "five" + [常](../characters/常.md) "constant, unchanging" — the Five Constant Virtues of Confucian ethics: 仁 (benevolence), 義 (righteousness), 禮 (propriety), 智 (wisdom), and 信 (trustworthiness), the personal-virtue counterpart to [[五倫]]'s relational framework.

五常 is almost always cited paired with 三綱 in the combined formula 三綱五常 (already documented in this vault's own chengyu entry <ruby>[三綱五常](../chengyu/三綱五常.md)<rt>ㄙㄚㄇㄍㄚㄫ·ㄛㄙ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby>), formally systematized together by Han-dynasty scholar Dong Zhongshu. Vietnamese ngũ thường (already confirmed in this vault's own [[三綱]] entry as tam cương ngũ thường) directly parallels the same combined doctrine in Vietnamese Confucian scholarship. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 오양 diverges from Sino-Korean 오상 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄛㄙ⼘ㄫ unique among both words and characters).
