## Notes
- [露](../characters/露.md) "dew" (here used as a transliteration character for "Russia," cf. [[露斯亜]]) + [国](../characters/国.md) "nation" — no `#cranberry`: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here (露's is the word [[露]] itself, 国's is [[国家]]). This is considered a contraction of [[露斯亜]] ("Russia," the fuller transliteration), on the same pattern as [[露語]]/[[露斯亜語]] for "Russian language."

露国 is an older Sino-Japanese/Sino-Korean name for Russia — Japanese ろこく (rokoku) and Korean 로국 (rogug) — built by suffixing 国 "country" directly onto the transliteration character 露, paralleling 米国 (America), 英国 (Britain), etc. Modern Mandarin and Cantonese instead use 俄国/俄國 (Éguó/ngo4gwok3, "the Nation of É"), built on the alternate transliteration character 俄 rather than 露; Sino-Vietnamese Lộ quốc is the corresponding older form of this same naming pattern, though modern Vietnamese now simply uses Nga (from 俄) alone, without 国/quốc.

**Fixed a real bug**: `mandarin`/`cantonese` had been contaminated with the reading for the unrelated word [[露語]]/俄语 ("Russian language," Éyǔ/ngo4jyu5) instead of this word's own "Russia" reading (Éguó/ngo4gwok3).
