## Notes
- [屯 (char)](../characters/屯%20(char).md) "village; hamlet; camp; station" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [屯 (char)](../characters/屯%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

屯 names a small settlement or military encampment/garrison post — its most common modern productive use is in [[駐屯]] ("to be stationed, garrisoned"). Originally a 象形 pictograph of a sprouting plant, the same graphemic root underlying [[春]] ("springtime").

Japanese とん, Korean 둔, and Vietnamese truân are all standard readings. No homophones among other words (注音 ㄉㄨㄋ unique).
