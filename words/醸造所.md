## Notes
- [醸](../characters/醸.md) "brew, ferment" + [造](../characters/造.md) "make" + [所](../characters/所%20(char).md) "place, office" — "brewing place," a brewery. Their own `stand_in` values: 醸's is [[醸造]]; 造's is [[創造]]; 所's is itself; none points here, so 醸造所 is an independent compound.

醸造所 is a brewery: a place where drink is brewed or fermented. It is a **neologism**, built from [[醸造]] (to brew) and [[所]] (place). Japanese じょうぞうじょ is the attested form, Korean says 양조장 with 場 "field," and Mandarin says 釀酒廠 with 廠 "factory," so Dan'a'yo uses the common root with 所 as the place word.
