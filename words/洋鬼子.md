## Notes
- [洋](../characters/洋.md) "ocean, sea" (by extension, "foreign, Western") + [鬼](../characters/鬼.md) "devil, demon" + [子](../characters/子.md) "son, child" (a diminutive/pejorative suffix) — no stand-in relationship applies (洋's own stand-in is [[大洋]]; 鬼's own is [[鬼神]]; 子's own is [[児子]]).

洋鬼子 is a derogatory Mandarin slang term for Westerners, literally "foreign devil."

Mandarin yángguǐzi, Cantonese joeng4 gwai2 zi2, Japanese ようきし, and Korean 양귀자 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 洋's, 鬼's, and 子's own stored citations exactly) — this is primarily a Mandarin colloquialism, so the Japanese/Korean/Vietnamese forms here are compositional references rather than independently-attested loanwords. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with dương quỷ tử (洋's own dương + 鬼's own citation quỷ, the standard reading for "devil, ghost" as in ma quỷ + 子's own tử).
