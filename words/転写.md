## Notes
- [転](../characters/転.md) "to turn; to transfer" + [写](../characters/写.md) "to copy; to depict" — "to copy over from one place or form into another," hence "transcription, transliteration." Neither character's `stand_in` points here (転's is [[転化]], 写's is [[複写]]), so no legitimizer relationship applies. The traditional form is 轉寫, and 转写 is the mainland simplified form.

Wiktionary: Japanese 転写 (てんしゃ) "transcription of text, diagrams, etc.; transcription of DNA", and as a verb "to transcribe"; Chinese 轉寫 "transcription; transliteration"; Korean 전사 is the hanja form, "transcription". The Dan'a'yo grammar uses it for 外来語転写, the transcription of foreign words into the Dan'a'yo sound system (`文法 - 02音韻論`). Compare [[翻訳]], [[表記]].
