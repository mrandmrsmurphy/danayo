## Etymology
- [物 (char)](../characters/物%20(char).md) "thing, result" + [質](../characters/質.md) "essence, nature" — no stand-in relationship applies (物's own stand-in is itself; 質's own is [[質素]]).

物質 means material, substance.

Cantonese mat6 zat1, Japanese ぶっしつ (BUTSU+SHITSU), Korean 물질, and Vietnamese vật chất were already correct and fully compositional. Fixed `mandarin`: a comma-joined dual pronunciation ("wùzhì, wùzhí") was corrected to the single compositional wùzhì, matching 質's own stored citation (zhì) — the same bug pattern already fixed on [[熟知]]/[[熟練]]/[[熟語]]. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄨㄊㄐㄧㄊ unique across both words and characters).
