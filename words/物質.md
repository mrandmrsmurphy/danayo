## Notes
- [物 (char)](../characters/物%20(char).md) "thing, result" + [質](../characters/質.md) "essence, nature" — no stand-in relationship applies (物's own stand-in is itself; 質's own is [[質素]]).

物質 means material, substance.

Cantonese mat6 zat1, Japanese ぶっしつ, Korean 물질 and Vietnamese vật chất are fully compositional. Mandarin has the single reading wùzhì, matching 質's own citation, as on [[熟知]], [[熟練]] and [[熟語]].
