## Notes
- [恒](../characters/恒.md) "constant, ubiquitous" + [星](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" — no stand-in relationship applies (恒's own stand-in is [[恒久]]; 星's own is itself).

恒星 means star (astronomically, a "fixed star") — a luminous celestial body generating its own light, as opposed to a planet.

Mandarin héngxīng, Cantonese hang4 sing1, Japanese こうせい, Korean 항성, and Vietnamese hằng tinh are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Fixed**: `vietnamese` had been swapped for the native everyday word "ngôi sao" — corrected to the compositional astronomical term hằng tinh. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄨㄫㄙㄝㄫ unique across both words and characters).
