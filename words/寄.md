## Notes
- [寄 (char)](../characters/寄%20(char).md) "approach; send; rely on" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [寄 (char)](../characters/寄%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

寄 covers a cluster of related senses built on the idea of placing something (or oneself) somewhere other than its usual home: to send a letter or parcel elsewhere (寄信, "to mail a letter"), to lodge or board away from home ([[寄宿]], [[寄宿舎]]), and figuratively to entrust or place reliance on something external — 寄託 "to entrust," 寄望 "to pin one's hopes on." The character's own graphemic root, phonetic 奇 ("strange, odd," itself literally "one-legged" — a figure standing off-balance) plus semantic 宀 ("roof, house"), is read by this vault as literally depicting living away from one's own home, the root image the entire semantic cluster grows from.

**`vietnamese` is left omitted**: the character's own stored field lists several forms (gửi, ké, kí, ký, kẹ) that don't resolve to one clean standalone Sino-Vietnamese reading — gửi in particular is the ordinary native Vietnamese verb "to send," not a Sino-Vietnamese reflex of 寄, while kí/ký (as in kí túc xá, "dormitory," from 寄宿舎) are the real Sino-Vietnamese readings but only ever surface bound inside compounds, never as a standalone word. No homophones among other words at this reading (注音 ㄍㄨㄧ; several unrelated characters share it, including [[奇]], [[貴]], and [[鬼]], but none of their own stand-in words collide with 寄's).
