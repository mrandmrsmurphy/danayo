## Notes
- [企](../characters/企.md) "plan; scheme" + [劃 (char)](../characters/劃%20(char).md) "mark off; delimit"
- This word is the viable form of [企](../characters/企.md): the character's meaning (to plan, scheme) is identical, but 企 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 劃 ("to demarcate; lay out") adds the sense of defining the scope of a plan.
- Japanese standard form is 企画 (きかく), with the same reading.
- `vietnamese: xí hoạch` is the honest compositional reading (企's own xí + 劃's own hoạch); no direct attestation found — the far more common real Vietnamese term for "plan, project" is kế hoạch, built on the near-synonym compound 計劃 (jìhuà) instead. `kwin` is false: both 企 and 劃 independently carry `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄝㄏ⺢ㄎ unique among both words and characters). Legitimizing note: 企's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, so 企 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.
