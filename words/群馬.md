## Notes
- [群](../characters/群.md) "flock" + [馬](../characters/馬%20(char).md) "horse"

群馬 is Gunma, written "herd of horses". The name comes from the old Gunma district (群馬郡), which was earlier read Kurumi (from memory, not re-checked). The reading ぐんま is the on-reading ぐん of 群 plus ま, the Go-on reading of 馬.

Gunma is a landlocked prefecture in the north-west of the Kantō plain, with its capital Maebashi. It is known for hot springs such as Kusatsu and Ikaho, the Tomioka Silk Mill, and the Takasaki daruma dolls.

In Dan'a'yo 群馬 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 군마 is the standard Korean name of the prefecture.
