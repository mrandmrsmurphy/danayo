## Notes
- [汚](../characters/汚%20(char).md) "dirty" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 汚 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

汚 means dirty or filthy: soiled, stained, unclean (汚物, "filth"; 汚水, "sewage"; 汚點, "stain, blemish"), and by extension to pollute or defile (汚染, "pollution"), or morally tainted (汚職, "corruption in office"). The graph is a 形声 compound of 氵 "water" with the phonetic 于; the variants 污 and 汙 are the commonly seen alternate forms.

Mandarin wū, Cantonese wu1, Japanese お (汚染/おせん, "pollution"; 汚職/おしょく, "corruption"), Korean 오 (오염, 오물) and Vietnamese ô (ô nhiễm, "pollution"; ô uế, "filthy") are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native adjective 汚い (きたない) and verbs 汚す (よごす) and 汚れる (よごれる) for the everyday words, so on'yomi お is bound to compounds.
