## Notes
- [汚](characters/汚%20(char).md) "dirty" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 汚 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

汚 means dirty, filthy (as in 汚染 "pollute," 汚物 "filth").

Mandarin wū, Cantonese wu1, Korean 오, and Vietnamese ô are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 汚's own stored citations exactly. Fixed a malformed literal `羅馬字: '''o'` → `'o`, fixed literal `vietnamese: null` → ô, added the missing `japanese`(お, on'yomi O)/`pos`/`hsk_level`("2") fields, reformatted `characters` to a proper block-list with the "(char)" suffix. `kwin: true` because 諺文 오 matches Korean 오 exactly.

**This word completes a four-way Dan'a'yo homophone group** (all read 'o/오/ㄛ): [[五]] ("five") and [[伍]] ("troops") and [[於]] ("in, at, during") were already perfected and already anticipated this page in their own callouts — added the matching callout here.
