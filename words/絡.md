## Notes
- [絡](../characters/絡%20(char).md) "enmesh, wrap around" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 絡 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

絡 means to enmesh or wrap around, as in 経絡 (a meridian in traditional Chinese medicine) and 連絡 (contact, liaison).

Mandarin luò, Cantonese lok3, and Korean 락 (rak) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading から (kara, "entangled, twined around") rather than the on-reading ラク (RAKU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese lạc is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading (as in kinh lạc, "meridian"), one of two stored candidates. No Dan'a'yo homophones (楽/洛/落/酪/駱 share the same syllable at the character level, but none has an independent word page).
