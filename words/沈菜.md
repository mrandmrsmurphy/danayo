## Notes
- [沈](../characters/沈.md) "sink" + [菜](../characters/菜.md) "vegetable" — no stand-in relationship applies (沈's own stand-in is [[沈没]]; 菜's own is [[野菜]]).

沈菜 (chimchae) is the archaic Sino-Korean term for pickled vegetables, the historical etymon of modern Korean 김치 (kimchi).

Korean 침채 is the real, historically attested term this word directly transcribes, matching 沈's and 菜's own stored citations exactly. Fixed a real bug: `mandarin` had been contaminated to pào cài, the reading of an unrelated real Chinese word 泡菜 ("pickled vegetables") — corrected to the compositional chéncài (沈's own chén + 菜's own cài). Filled the blank `cantonese` (sam2 coi3) and `vietnamese` (chìm thái, compositional) fields. Japanese キムチ is the modern loanword borrowed directly from Korean kimchi, since Japanese has no native compositional equivalent — the same real-world-loanword pattern already seen elsewhere. `kwin: true` because 諺文 침채 matches Korean 침채 exactly. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄇㄑㄚㄧ unique across both words and characters).
