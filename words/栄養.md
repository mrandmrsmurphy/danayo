## Notes
- [栄](../characters/栄.md) "flourish; glory" + [養](../characters/養.md) "nourish; foster"

**栄養** is the East Asian compound for nutrition or nourishment — the process by which living organisms take in and assimilate substances necessary for growth, energy, and health. The compound literally means "flourishing nourishment": 栄 (榮, *róng*) "to flourish; to thrive" + 養 (*yǎng*) "to nourish; to raise." See [[栄養素]] for the individual nutrients that provide it.

A note on character variants: this is the 栄 spelling (the Japanese shinjitai of 榮, *róng*, "flourish/glory"), giving Mandarin *róngyǎng*. The standard Chinese compound for nutrition is **营养** (*yíngyǎng*), written with the distinct character 营/營 (*yíng*, "manage; operate"); Japanese shinjitai writes the same word 営養 (*eiyō*), which is the vault's [[営養]]. In Japanese, 栄 and 営 are both read エイ, so the 栄養 spelling is an old orthographic confusion that has no sense difference from 営養 — the two are spelling variants of one word, as the Food page's Semantic Range Notes record. The aliases 榮養 and 荣养 reflect the Chinese orthographic forms of the characters used here.
