## Notes
- [便 (char)](../characters/便%20(char).md) "convenient" + [箋](../characters/箋.md) "note, label" — びんせん is real, everyday Japanese for "notepaper, letter paper." Confirmed via web search that Korean instead uses 편지지 for this concept (from an entirely different root, 편지 "letter," itself from 片紙/便紙 — not built from 箋 at all), so that real word is documented here rather than the unattested compositional 편전. `vietnamese: giấy viết thư` is likewise the everyday native descriptive phrase rather than a Sino-Vietnamese calque. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 변전 diverges entirely from the 편지지-based Korean field. No homophones (注音 ㄅ⼶ㄋㄐㄝㄋ unique among both words and characters).
