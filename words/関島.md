## Notes
- [関 (char)](../characters/関%20(char).md) "close" + [島 (char)](../characters/島%20(char).md) "island"

関島 is the place name for Guam, the Micronesian island and unincorporated U.S. territory. Mandarin 關島/关岛 (Guāndǎo) and Cantonese 關島 (gwaan1dou2) both use the compositional "closed island" calque as the standard place name in Chinese. Japanese and Korean instead transliterate the English name directly — グアム (Guamu) and 괌 (Gwam) respectively — rather than using a 漢字/한자 calque, which is why this word's own `japanese`/`korean` fields hold transliterations rather than on'yomi/Sino-Korean readings of 関島's own characters. Vietnamese đảo Guam similarly keeps the English name, prefixed with the native word đảo ("island") rather than Sino-Vietnamese quan.
