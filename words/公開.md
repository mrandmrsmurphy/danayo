## Notes
- [公 (char)](../characters/公%20(char).md) "public" + [開](../characters/開.md) "open" — "to open to the public, expose, share" (公開演説, "public speech"; 公開する, "to make public"). Mandarin gōngkāi, Cantonese gung1 hoi1, Korean 공개, and Vietnamese công khai are all directly attested standard readings.

**Real content bug found and fixed**: `japanese` had been stored as こうくわい, an incorrect archaic-looking kana rendering not matching 開's own modern on'yomi (KAI) or any real attested pronunciation of this word — corrected to こうかい (kōkai), the genuine standard Japanese reading. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 공캐 diverges from Sino-Korean 공개 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄫㄎㄚㄧ unique among both words and characters).
