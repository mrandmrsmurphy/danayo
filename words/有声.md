## Notes
- [有](../words/有.md) "to have, there is" (stand-in: itself) + [声 (char)](../characters/声.md) "sound, voice" (stand-in: [[発声]]) — voiced, with vocal-fold vibration.

Wiktionary: Japanese 有声 (ゆうせい), phonetics, "sonant, vocal, phonic; the quality of being voiced"; Chinese 有聲 "to have sound or voice; to have a reputation, be renowned". Used in `文法 - 02音韻論`, in the Mandarin and Cantonese booklets, for voiced consonants.
