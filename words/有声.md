## Notes
- [有](../words/有.md) "to have, there is" (stand-in: itself) + [声 (char)](../characters/声.md) "sound, voice" (stand-in: [[発声]]) — voiced, with vocal-fold vibration.

有声 means voiced. Mandarin yǒushēng, Japanese ゆうせい, Korean 유성, and Vietnamese hữu thanh are all real, standard terms, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Cantonese jau5 sing1 is a straightforward compositional reading. `kwin` is false: inherited from 声 (false), despite 有 itself being true.

No syllable-boundary dot is needed: the concatenated string `⼜ㄙㄧㄫ` admits only one legal partition.
