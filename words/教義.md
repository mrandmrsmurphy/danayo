## Notes
- [教](../characters/教.md) "teach, religion" + [義](../characters/義.md) "meaning, righteousness" — "teaching meaning," doctrine. Their own `stand_in` values: 教's is [[教授]]; 義's is [[意義]]; none points here, so 教義 is an independent compound.

教義 is doctrine: the teaching a religion holds as true. Mandarin jiàoyì, Cantonese gaau3 ji6 and Japanese きょうぎ are attested, and Korean 교의 is the same compound. It is what [[異端]] departs from. Compare [[信条]] (creed) and [[神学]] (theology).
