## Notes
- [黄 (char)](../characters/黄%20(char).md) "yellow" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — yellow.

黄色 is the X色 form of [[黄]], the noun "yellow" that completes the set beside [[黒色]], [[白色]], [[緑色]], [[青色]] and [[紫色]]. Mandarin huángsè, Cantonese wong4 sik1 and Korean 황색 are the ordinary words for the color. Japanese has the native きいろ (黄色), alongside the on'yomi おうしょく, which is literary. In Mandarin and Cantonese, huángsè also has a secondary sense "pornographic," a figurative use that is not carried over here. The traditional spelling 黃色 is an alias.
