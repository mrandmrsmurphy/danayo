## Notes
- [交 (char)](../characters/交%20(char).md) "to interact, cross" + [響](../characters/響.md) "to echo, resound" — "symphonic," from the sense of intertwining resonances/echoes; giao hưởng is the standard everyday Vietnamese word for "symphony/symphonic" (dàn nhạc giao hưởng, "symphony orchestra"), a direct match. Filled the blank `cantonese: gaau1 hoeng2`, compositional from each character's own reading. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 굣향 diverges from Sino-Korean 교향 in the first syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼄ㄨㄏ⼘ㄫ unique among both words and characters).
