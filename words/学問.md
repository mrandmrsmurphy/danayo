## Notes
- [学](../characters/学.md) "learn" + [問](../characters/問.md) "question" — "to learn and to ask".

学問 is "scholarship; learning": organised knowledge and the pursuit of it. English Wiktionary lists two senses for the Chinese word: an academic discipline or branch of knowledge (死亡學是一門新興的學問, "thanatology is a new branch of knowledge"), and learning, knowledge, wisdom or erudition, as in 做學問 ("to do scholarly research") and 有學問 ("learned"). It places 學術 and 見聞 among the synonyms and 才學, 才智 and 智慧 among related terms, none of which is a word in the vault. Korean 학문 ("study; learning") and Vietnamese học vấn ("learning") are both listed as Sino-Korean and Sino-Vietnamese forms of the same two characters, and Japanese 学問 (がくもん, gakumon) is the same word.

The compound pairs 学 (learning) with 問 (asking), the old picture of knowledge as something earned by inquiry. It sits beside the related words [[学者]] (scholar), [[学習]] (study) and [[学生]] (student) on the Knowledge page.

Mandarin xuéwèn and Cantonese hok6 man6 are as at English Wiktionary.

No other word shares the 注音 ㄏㄚㄎㄇㄨㄋ (checked 2026-10-12).
