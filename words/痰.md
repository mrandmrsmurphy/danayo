## Notes
- [痰](../characters/痰%20(char).md) "phlegm, sputum" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 痰 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

痰 means phlegm or sputum.

Mandarin tán, Cantonese taam4, Japanese たん, Korean 담, and Vietnamese đàm are all standard, fully compositional readings (character's own citation also lists đờm as an alternate Vietnamese reading; đàm is used here as the formal Sino-Vietnamese form, matching the pattern of the word's other readings). Fixed literal `vietnamese: null` → đàm, added the missing `pos: 名詞` (the character's own stored `pos` is an empty string — a character-level gap left for future perfecting), `japanese: たん`, and `hsk_level: "4"` (matching the character). Discovered a genuine homophone with [[曇]] (both ㄉㄚㄇ/tán/taam4/담/đàm) — cross-referenced both pages.
