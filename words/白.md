## Notes
- [白 (char)](../characters/白%20(char).md) "white" — stand-in for [[白 (char)|白]], which cannot appear independently

白 is 象形 of uncertain origin — Wiktionary and the character's own page note two competing proposals, an acorn (compare 櫟) or a stylized human head (compare 貌 "face," 黑 "black"), neither one settled. Regardless of pictorial origin, 白 anchors both a huge literal-color compound family (白色, 白菜 "bok choy," 白鳥 "swan," 卵白/蛋白 "egg white") and figurative extensions built on whiteness-as-clarity or whiteness-as-emptiness: 明白 ("clear, obvious," white/bright understanding), 空白 ("blank, empty"). Astronomically it names Venus as the morning/evening star, 太白星 ("the great white star"), continuing this vault's Five-Phases planet-naming pattern alongside [[金]]/[[火]]/[[水]]/[[木]]'s own entries, and within the vault's own periodic-table convention it prefixes platinum via [[白金]] ("white gold/metal," itself a real cross-CJKV descriptive calque rather than a Dan'a'yo-only coinage).

Japanese carries two on'yomi with a functional split: はく (HAKU) is the general-purpose reading (白人 hakujin, 白金 hakkin "platinum"), while びゃく (BYAKU) survives mainly in Buddhist/classical vocabulary (白衣 byakue, a monk's white robes) — the native 訓読み しろ(い) (shiroi) remains the ordinary everyday adjective for the color. Vietnamese distinguishes two readings on the character's own page, bạch (used here, matching the "white" gloss — bạch tuyết "white snow," bạch kim "platinum," directly cognate with [[白金]]) and bạc, which has drifted to mean "silver" as a distinct word rather than remaining a plain color synonym — the two aren't interchangeable in modern Vietnamese despite sharing the same character origin. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 박 (bag, from Middle Chinese 並母 b + 陌二開韻 ɣæk) diverges from Sino-Korean 백 in the vowel, the same MC 陌 rime realized differently in each system. 白 shares its Dan'a'yo reading with [[百]] ("hundred") — a coincidental convergence, unrelated in meaning, previously undocumented on either page despite 百 having been perfected since 2026-06-03.
