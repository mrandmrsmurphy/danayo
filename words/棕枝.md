## Notes
- [棕](../characters/棕.md) "palm; brown" + [[枝]] "foliage" — this word is 棕's own `stand_in`, since 棕 is too bounded to stand alone.

棕枝 is a palm tree, the vault's spelling of 棕櫚 (an alias, with the simplified 棕榈). English Wiktionary glosses Mandarin 棕櫚 (zōnglǘ, Cantonese zung1 leoi5) as "palm tree" and Japanese しゅろ as the windmill palm, *Trachycarpus fortunei*, and the genus; Korean 종려 is its reading.

The character used for the second syllable here, 枝, is a graphic/phonetic substitute: this vault has no character page for the true partner characters 櫚/榈, whose Mandarin reading lǘ matches this word's ㄌ⼄ (*lyo*), so 枝 stands in for them the same way [[牡厉]] stands in for 牡蠣's unavailable 蠣. Chinese also has a distinct compound 棕枝 (zōngzhī), which Wiktionary lists inside 棕枝主日, "Palm Sunday" (Zōngzhī Zhǔrì); see [[棕枝主日]] for that sense. This word's own readings track "palm tree" (zōnglǘ).
