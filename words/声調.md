## Notes
- [声](../characters/声.md) "vocalize; sound" + [調](../characters/調.md) "adjust; tune" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (声's is 発声, 調's is 調整).

Mandarin shēngdiào, Cantonese sing1 diu6, Japanese せいちょう, and Korean 성조 are the standard readings. Vietnamese thanh điệu is the standard linguistics term for "tone," composed of 声's thanh and 調's điệu; the native words giọng and thanh mean "voice" or "tone" more generally and are not readings of this compound.
