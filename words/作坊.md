## Notes
- [作](characters/作.md) "to make" + [坊](characters/坊.md) "workshop, quarter" — a real, specifically Chinese term for a small-scale handicraft workshop (traditionally distinguishing official government workshops from private civilian ones), distinct from the more modern artist's-studio sense of 工房/工作室. Confirmed via web search that neither Japanese nor Korean naturally use 作坊 itself — instead `japanese: こうぼう` (工房) and `korean: 공방` (工房) document each language's own real equivalent word, and `vietnamese: xưởng` is the standard native Vietnamese word rather than a Sino-Vietnamese calque. Filled the blank `cantonese: zok3 fong1`, compositional. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 작빵 diverges entirely from the 工房-based Korean field. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄎㄈㄚㄫ unique among both words and characters).
