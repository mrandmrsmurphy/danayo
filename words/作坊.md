## Notes
- [作 (char)](../characters/作%20(char).md) "to make" + [坊](../characters/坊.md) "workshop, quarter" — a real, specifically Chinese term for a small-scale handicraft workshop (traditionally distinguishing official government workshops from private civilian ones), distinct from the more modern artist's-studio sense of 工房/工作室. Confirmed via web search that neither Japanese nor Korean naturally use 作坊 itself — instead `japanese: こうぼう` (工房) and `korean: 공방` (工房) document each language's own real equivalent word, and `vietnamese: xưởng` is the standard native Vietnamese word rather than a Sino-Vietnamese calque. Legitimizing note: 坊's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, so 坊 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.
