## Notes
- [侮](../characters/侮.md) "insult" + [蔑 (char)](../characters/蔑%20(char).md) "scorn"

侮蔑 is scorn: to treat as worthless, openly, so that the insult is heard. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin wǔmiè as "to despise; to look down on" and Japanese ぶべつ as "contempt; scorn; disdain; to insult". Cantonese mou5 mit6 matches, and Korean 모멸 is the reading of the characters.

It is [軽蔑](軽蔑.md) made public, contempt turned into an insult. The scorn that is only a look is [蔑視](蔑視.md).
