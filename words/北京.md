## Notes
- [北](../characters/北.md) "north" + [京](../characters/京.md) "capital" — no stand-in relationship applies (北's own stand-in is [[北方]], 京's is [[京城]]).

北京 (Beijing/Peking), literally "northern capital," is the capital of the People's Republic of China.

Japanese ぺきん is a phonetic loan reading (mirroring the exonym "Peking," matching similar phonetic-loan place-name readings elsewhere, e.g. しゃんはい for 上海) rather than a compositional on'yomi reading. Korean 북경 and Vietnamese Bắc Kinh are both standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄨㄎㄍ⼶ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
