## Notes
- [幸](../characters/幸.md) "fortunate; happy" + [運](../characters/運.md) "fate; fortune" — this word is the stand-in for [幸](../characters/幸.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

幸運 names good luck or fortune — 幸's "surviving misfortune, unexpectedly happy" sense (the character's own 会意 root depicts enduring hardship, 辛, to arrive at good fortune) combined with 運's "fate, fortune."

Japanese こううん, Korean 행운, and Vietnamese hạnh vận are all standard, non-drifted readings.
