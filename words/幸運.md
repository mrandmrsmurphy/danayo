## Notes
- [幸](../characters/幸.md) "fortunate; happy" + [運](../characters/運.md) "fate; fortune" — this word is the stand-in for [幸](../characters/幸.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

幸運 names good luck or fortune — 幸's "surviving misfortune, unexpectedly happy" sense (the character's own 会意 root depicts enduring hardship, 辛, to arrive at good fortune) combined with 運's "fate, fortune."

Japanese こううん, Korean 행운, and Vietnamese hạnh vận are all standard, non-drifted readings. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄫ·ㄨㄋ unique across both words and characters).

**Correction (2026-08-28)**: `japanese` had an archaic-kana error (かううん → こううん, the same recurring class as [[将軍]]/[[少女]]/[[少年]]/[[帰納]]). `korean: 행운` was already correct — verified against 幸's own real Korean field (행, distinct from Dan'a'yo's own 항, an already-documented deliberate divergence, the same shape as [[平均]]).
