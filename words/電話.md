## Notes
- [電](../characters/電.md) "electricity" + [話 (char)](../characters/話%20(char).md) "speak on, talk about" — no `#cranberry`: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here (電's is [[電気]], 話's is 話 itself).

電話 is the standard, everyday word for "telephone" across all five languages: Mandarin 電話/电话 (diànhuà), Cantonese 電話 (din6waa6-2), Japanese でんわ (denwa), Korean 전화 (jeonhwa), and Sino-Vietnamese điện thoại — a rare case of a modern technological compound retaining its full, uncontested compositional meaning and everyday register in every daughter language, with no substitutions, narrowings, or reorderings to document.
