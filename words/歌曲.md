## Notes
- [歌](../characters/歌.md) "sing, song" + [曲](../characters/曲.md) "tune, bend" — both 歌 and 曲's own stand-ins point to this compound (transitivity holds, confirming the `#cranberry` tag: A = B = AB).

歌曲 means song.

Mandarin gēqǔ, Cantonese go1 kuk1, Japanese かきょく, Korean 가곡, and Vietnamese ca khúc are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 歌's and 曲's own stored citations exactly).
