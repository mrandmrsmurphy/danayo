## Notes
- [開 (char)](../characters/開%20(char).md) "open (stand-in: itself)" + [幕 (char)](../characters/幕%20(char).md) "curtain (stand-in: itself)"

Wiktionary: Chinese 開幕, of plays and cinemas, "to give the first performance", and "to open or inaugurate (a ceremony or conference)"; Japanese 開幕 "rising of the curtain of a stage, the beginning of..." and "opening of a ceremony, festivity or conference". Its counterpart is [[閉幕]].
