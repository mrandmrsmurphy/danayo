## Notes
- [輪 (char)](../characters/輪%20(char).md) "wheel; rotation" + [番 (char)](../characters/番%20(char).md) "turn, shift" — rotating duty.

輪番 is rotating duty: several people taking turns as the person on duty in an agreed order, and the system of such rotation. It is "wheel-turn." Mandarin lúnfān ("alternately, in turn", with the synonyms 輪流 and 交替), Cantonese leon4 faan1 and Japanese りんばん ("several people agree on an order and each takes a turn as the person on duty") are confirmed; Korean 윤번 comes from memory. Japanese also uses りんばん for the temple role in which monks take turns running a temple's affairs, a sense not taken over here. Compare [[交替]], which is the general swapping of places, and [[当番]], the person or period currently on duty. Like the other shift words, it belongs to the 番 family, a systematic schema that Dan'a'yo imposes for predictability rather than a claim about the ancient usage.
