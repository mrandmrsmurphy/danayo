## Notes
- [推](../characters/推%20(char).md) "push; infer" + [定](../characters/定.md) "fix, decide" — "to push toward a fixed conclusion": to infer something and settle on it.

推定 is to arrive at a determinate value or conclusion by inference from evidence, rather than by direct observation: to estimate (推定人口 "estimated population"), to presume (a legal presumption), and, in the sciences of the past, to reconstruct. It is established in Japanese (すいてい, noun and する-verb: 推定する, 推定値, 推定年齢) and Korean (추정, as in 추정치 "estimate" and the legal 무죄 추정 "presumption of innocence"). Mandarin tuīdìng is attested, but Wiktionary glosses it as "to choose, elect; to presume, deduce", so in Chinese it shades toward "to settle on / decide" (推定 a candidate), and the everyday words for estimating are 估计 and 推算. Vietnamese *suy định* is a compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading; the ordinary word is the native/learned *ước tính* or *suy đoán*, and hvdic.thivien.net does not list the compound.

Within the vault it differs from [[推測]] "to surmise, speculate", which is open-ended guessing with no commitment to a result, and from [[仮定]] "to assume", which stipulates a premise for the sake of argument. 推定 is firmer than a guess and weaker than a proof: the conclusion is reasoned from evidence and is held until corrected. That is why it is also the legal word (a 推定 stands unless rebutted), with [[決定]] as the stronger, final decision on the same 定 stem.

In historical linguistics the same reasoning applies to a form no one has heard: a 推定 form is one inferred from the evidence of the daughter languages and the rime tables ([[韻図]]), the counterpart of the Chinese 擬音 / 構擬 and the English "reconstruct". (The linguistics-specific terms in Chinese and Japanese are given from general knowledge and were not separately verified here.) In [[文法 - 03文字法]] it is the second half of the section heading 音源推定, "estimating/reconstructing the sound source": from the stated source readings, each character is given its pronunciation by rule.

Readings: Japanese and Korean from Wiktionary; Mandarin from Wiktionary; Cantonese teoi1 ding6 and Vietnamese suy định are compositional readings from 推 and 定 (matching [[推測]] and [[仮定]]), not separately verified for this compound.
