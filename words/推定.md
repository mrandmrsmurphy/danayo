## Notes
- [推](../characters/推%20(char).md) "push; infer" + [定](../characters/定.md) "fix, decide" — "to push toward a fixed conclusion": to infer something and settle on it.

Wiktionary: Japanese "estimation; inference, presumption; an estimate", and as a verb "to infer, presume"; Chinese "to choose, elect; to presume, determine, deduce"; Korean 추정 "estimation". In [[文法 - 03文字法]] it is the second half of the heading 音源推定, "estimating the sound source": each character's pronunciation is derived by rule from the stated source readings. Compare [[推測]], [[仮定]], [[決定]], [[指定]].
