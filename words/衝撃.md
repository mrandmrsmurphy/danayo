## Notes
- [衝 (char)](../characters/衝%20(char).md) "rush; thrust" + [撃 (char)](../characters/撃%20(char).md) "strike; hit"

衝撃 is shock: a sudden hard blow, or the sudden strong effect of bad news or a violent event. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin chōngjī as "to pound; to strike; to hit; to charge" and Japanese しょうげき as "shock". Korean 충격, Vietnamese xung kích and Cantonese cung1 gik1 match.

It is the physical blow turned into a feeling, as a [戦慄](戦慄.md) is the shudder that follows it. A mere start is [喫驚](喫驚.md), and the blow of a fight is a [攻撃](攻撃.md), attack.
