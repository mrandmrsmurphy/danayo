## Notes
- [雰](../characters/雰.md) "atmosphere" + [囲](../characters/囲.md) "encircle" + [気 (char)](../characters/気%20(char).md) "feeling"
- This word is the viable form of [雰](../characters/雰.md): the character's meaning (atmosphere, ambience) is identical, but 雰 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 雰囲気 is its `stand_in` compound.
- A Japanese coinage; no unified Mandarin/Cantonese reading.

雰囲気 is the atmosphere or ambience of a place, group or occasion: the overall feeling it gives off. It is a Japanese word, read ふんいき (fun'iki), written with the rare character 雰 ("mist, vapour") and formerly 氛囲気 (the alias here); the compound is a Japanese formation from 雰 "atmosphere" + 囲 "to surround" + 気 "feeling, air". Korean uses it as an everyday loanword, 분위기 (bunwigi). Mandarin and Cantonese have no unified reading of this written form and use their own words for the same idea, 氣氛 (qìfēn) and 氛圍 (fēnwéi).

In Dan'a'yo it is the felt tone of a situation, set against [[気分]], the mood of a person; one belongs to the surroundings, the other to someone's inner state.
