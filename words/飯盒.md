## Notes
- [飯](../characters/飯.md) "rice" + [盒](../characters/盒.md) "box"

飯盒 is a lunch box: a lidded container for carrying a meal. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 飯盒 (Mandarin fànhé, Cantonese faan6 hap6) as "lunch box (container)" and lists Japanese はんごう. Korean 반합 is the Sino-Korean reading of the same two characters, which Wiktionary lists without a gloss.

The character [盒](../characters/盒.md) is a 名専字, so it appears only inside compounds such as this one. The ordinary box is the [箱](箱.md).
