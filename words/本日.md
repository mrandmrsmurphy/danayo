## Notes
- [本 (char)](../characters/本%20(char).md) "base, origin" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — literally "base day," today.
- This is a very poetic way to say "today". For the normal way, use <ruby>[今日](../words/今日.md)<rt>ㄍㄧㄇㄋㄧㄊ</rt></ruby>

Mandarin běnrì, Cantonese bun2 jat6, and Japanese ほんじつ (honjitsu) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 本's and 日's own stored citations exactly — all are similarly formal/literary registers, matching this word's own "poetic" sense. Korean 본일 is the formal Sino-Korean compound, not the native 오늘. Vietnamese bữa nay is the real native word.
