## Notes
- [手](../characters/手%20(char).md) "hand" + [術](../characters/術%20(char).md) "art, technique" — "hand technique," surgery. Their own `stand_in` values: 手's is itself; 術's is itself; none points here, so 手術 is an independent compound.

手術 is surgery: an operation in which a surgeon cuts into the body to treat it. Mandarin shǒushù, Cantonese sau2 seot6, Japanese しゅじゅつ and Korean 수술 all use it, and Vietnamese thủ thuật does too. Compare [[医学]] (medicine), [[治療]] (treatment) and [[病院]] (hospital).
