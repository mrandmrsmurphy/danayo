## Notes
- [原](../characters/原.md) "plain; original" + [州](../characters/州%20(char).md) "state" — the name of Wonju, a city of South Korea in the north-east.

原州 (Wonju) is a city of Gangwon Province ([[江原]]特別自治道) in South Korea, the largest city of the province, and with [[江陵]] one of the two towns that gave the province its name (from memory, not re-checked).

English Wiktionary has 原州 as the Korean city (Mandarin Yuánzhōu, Cantonese jyun4 zau1), Chinese Wiktionary has the string, and English and Korean Wiktionary have a Korean entry as a hanja form of 원주. English Wiktionary's Japanese section gives the reading ウォンジュ (from the Korean) and a dated reading げんしゅう, which is taken here as the Sino-Xenic reading. The same string is also Yuanzhou District of Guyuan in Ningxia, China (the Vietnamese Wikipedia has it as Nguyên Châu). Vietnamese Nguyên Châu is otherwise composed from the character pages.
