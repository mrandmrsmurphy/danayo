## Notes
- [平](../characters/平.md) "even; level" + [静](../characters/静.md) "quiet; still" — no stand-in relationship applies (平's own `stand_in` is [[水平]], 静's is [[静寂]]).

平静 names calm, serene composure — an even, undisturbed state of mind or circumstances (心を平静に保つ, "to keep one's mind calm").

Japanese へいせい, Korean 평정, and Vietnamese bình tĩnh are all standard, non-drifted readings — Vietnamese bình tĩnh in particular is the direct compositional match, coincidentally the same everyday word discussed on [[平坦]]'s own page as the near-homophone commonly confused with bình thản ("inner serenity," [[平坦]]'s own drifted sense). HSK level 2 already correct. No homophones (注音 ㄅ⼶ㄫㄐㄝㄫ unique across both words and characters).
