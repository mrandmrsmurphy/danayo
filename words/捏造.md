## Notes
- [捏](../characters/捏.md) "fabricate" + [造](../characters/造.md) "create"
- This word is the viable form of [捏](../characters/捏.md): the character's meaning (to fabricate, make up) is identical, but 捏 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 捏造 is its `stand_in` compound.

捏造 means to fabricate: to make up facts, evidence, a story or a rumour that has no basis and present it as real. 捏 is literally "to pinch, to mould with the fingers" (as with clay) and 造 is "to make", so the picture is shaping something out of nothing. Mandarin niēzào, Cantonese nip6 zou6, Japanese ねつぞう (netsuzō; very common in news in データ捏造, "falsified data") and Korean 날조 (naljo) are the same compound.

In Dan'a'yo it names the act of inventing the false thing, separate from [[虚偽]] (a falsehood, the resulting statement) and from [[騙]] (to deceive a person). No other narrower or shifted sense is needed.
