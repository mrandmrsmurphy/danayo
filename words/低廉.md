## Etymology
- [低](../characters/低.md) "lower, bow, droop" + [廉 (char)](../characters/廉%20(char).md) "upright, honest; cheap, inexpensive"

## Notes
`vietnamese: đê liêm` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese for "cheap, inexpensive" (confirmed via web search — as in vật mĩ giá liêm, "good quality at a low price"), leaning on 廉's secondary "affordable" sense rather than its more familiar "honest, incorrupt" sense (as in 廉恥/liêm sỉ, "sense of shame/integrity"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 데렴 diverges from Sino-Korean 저렴 in the first syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄝㄧㄌ⼶ㄇ unique among both words and characters).
