## Notes
- [凹 (char)](../characters/凹%20(char).md) "concave" + [凸 (char)](../characters/凸%20(char).md) "convex" — "bumpy, uneven, rugged" (a surface with both concave and convex features). Mandarin āotū, Cantonese nap1 dat6, Japanese おうとつ, Korean 요철, and Vietnamese o đột are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 앗덛 diverges from Sino-Korean 요철 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄨㄉㄛㄊ unique among both words and characters).
