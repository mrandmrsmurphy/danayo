## Notes
- [港](../characters/港.md) "harbor, port" + [族](../characters/族.md) "tribe, clan" — "the Harbor Tribe, Zebulun." Their own `stand_in` values: 港's is [[港湾]]; 族's is [[家族]]; none points here, so 港族 is an independent compound.

港族 is the tribe of Zebulun, Leah's sixth son, whose name means "dwelling" (Genesis 30:20). It is a Dan'a'yo **neologism**, "Harbor Tribe," named for Jacob's blessing, "Zebulun shall dwell at the shore of the sea; he shall be a haven for ships" (Genesis 49:13). Compare [[明族]] (Issachar) and [[右族]] (Benjamin). See [[基督敎]].

- Hebrew name: זְבוּלֻן (Zevulun) (注音 ㄐㄝㄅㄨㄌㄨㄋ).

- See [基督敎](../lexipedia/基督敎.md).
