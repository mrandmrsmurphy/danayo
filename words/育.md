## Notes
- [育](../characters/育%20(char).md) "nurture, educate" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 育 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

育 means to nurture, raise, or educate, as in 教育 (education) and 体育 (physical education).

Mandarin yù, cantonese juk6, and korean 육 (yuk) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading そだつ (sodatsu, "to grow, be raised") rather than the on-reading イク (IKU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese dục is the character's sole stored reading. No homophones.
