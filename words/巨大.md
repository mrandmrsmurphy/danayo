## Notes
- [巨](../characters/巨.md) "huge; enormous" + [大](../characters/大.md) "big" — this word is the stand-in for [巨](../characters/巨.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

巨大 is a near-synonym doubling, both halves independently meaning "big/huge" — the standard adjective for something of massive, imposing scale (巨大企業 "giant corporation," 巨大な建物 "an enormous building").

Japanese きょだい, Korean 거대, and Vietnamese cự đại are all standard, non-drifted readings. HSK level 2 already correct. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼄ㄉㄚㄧ unique across both words and characters).
