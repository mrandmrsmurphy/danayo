## Etymology
- [兵](../characters/兵.md) "soldier" + [站](../characters/站.md) "station, site"

## Notes
"Military depot, logistics station." `vietnamese: binh trạm` is directly attested (confirmed via web search) and historically significant — the term used for the relay/logistics stations along the Trường Sơn Road (Ho Chi Minh Trail) during the Vietnam War, each responsible for a road segment's supply and transport. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 병잠 diverges from Sino-Korean 병참 in the second syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄅ⼶ㄫㄐㄚㄇ unique among both words and characters).
