## Notes
- [瓊](../characters/瓊.md) "fine jade; elegance" + [玉 (char)](../characters/玉%20(char).md) "jade; gem"
- This word is the viable form of [瓊](../characters/瓊.md): the character means fine or beautiful jade, but is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo. 瓊玉 is its `stand_in` compound.

Both characters mean jade, so the compound is a synonym pair naming fine jade. Wiktionary's entry for 瓊 glosses it as literary "fine jade, particularly red jade" and lists 瓊玉 among its many compounds, though it has no separate entry for 瓊玉 itself, so the compound is a literary and poetic term rather than an everyday word. Its readings here are the Sino-Xenic ones: Mandarin qióngyù, Cantonese king4 juk6, Japanese ぎょうぎょく and Korean 경옥.

In Dan'a'yo the word names the material, fine jade, not a color; the [Color](../lexipedia/Color.md) page groups it with its other jade vocabulary, such as [翠色](翠色.md) and [燐彬](燐彬.md).
