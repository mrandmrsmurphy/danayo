## Notes
- [俯](../characters/俯.md) "look down; stoop" + [仰](../characters/仰.md) "look up; admire" — "the raising and lowering of the head" (looking down and up), and by extension "in an instant, in the blink of an eye" (俯仰之間, "in the time it takes to look down and up"). `vietnamese: phủ ngưỡng` is the honest compositional reading (俯's own phủ + 仰's own ngưỡng); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 뿌양 diverges entirely from the compositional Sino-Korean 부앙. No homophones (注音 ㄈㄨ·⼘ㄫ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 俯's own `stand_in` is this exact word (俯仰), so 俯 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 俯仰.
