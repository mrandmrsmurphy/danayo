## Notes
- [韓](../characters/韓.md) "Han (Korean)" + [国](../characters/国.md) "nation" — 韓国 is 韓's own legitimizing compound (韓's `stand_in` points here), but since 国's own `stand_in` is [[国家]] instead, transitivity fails and no `#cranberry` tag applies.

韓国 (Hánguó) is the standard word for "(South) Korea" in Mandarin, and Cantonese 韓国 (hon4gwok3) likewise. Japanese かんこく (kankoku) and Korean 한국 (hanguk, the country's own endonym) are both the standard everyday terms, as is Sino-Vietnamese Hàn Quốc — a clean case with full agreement across all five languages.
