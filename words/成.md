## Notes
- [成](../characters/成%20(char).md) "become" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

成 means to become — coming into a state or completing a process, as in 成功 ("success") and 成熟 ("mature, ripen").

Mandarin chéng, Cantonese sing4, Korean 성, Japanese せい, and Vietnamese thành are all standard, fully compositional readings.
