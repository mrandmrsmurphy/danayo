## Etymology
- [辞](../characters/辞.md) "bid farewell, resign (with words)" + [退 (char)](../characters/退%20(char).md) "retreat, recede, withdraw"

## Notes
Mandarin cítuì, cantonese ci4 teoi3, japanese じたい, and korean 사퇴 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese từ thoái is a directly attested standard Vietnamese word ("to decline, withdraw"), matching 辞's own từ + 退's own thoái exactly. `辭退` is the standard traditional-character spelling variant (辞→辭), correctly listed as an alias. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 치퇴 diverges from Sino-Korean 사퇴 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄊㄧㄜ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 辞's `stand_in` (辞職) nor 退's own `stand_in` (退 itself) points to this compound.
