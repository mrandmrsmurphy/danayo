## Notes
- [徐](../characters/徐.md) + [州 (char)](../characters/州%20(char).md) — Xu Province, the fourth of the [[九州]]

Wiktionary: Chinese 徐州 is the prefecture-level city Xuzhou, in northwestern Jiangsu, and, historical, an ancient province, one of the Nine Provinces ([[九州]]) of ancient China; Japanese じょしゅう is the Jiangsu city. The *Tribute of Yu* (尚書, 禹貢, Wikisource) bounds it with the words 海岱及淮惟徐州, "from the sea and Mount Tai to the Huai is Xuzhou". The word names the province, and the foreign-language fields give the readings of that name; the Dan'a'yo reading 쇼줏 is derived from 徐 and 州. The character 徐 has its own `stand_in` 徐様, so this word is not its legitimizer.
