## Notes
- [徐](../characters/徐.md) + [州 (char)](../characters/州%20(char).md) — Xu Province, the fourth of the [[九州]]

徐州 occupied the eastern region south of the Yellow River — roughly modern northern Jiangsu and southern Shandong — in the lower reaches of the Huai River valley. The *Tribute of Yu* bounds it as the land from the sea and Mount Tai to the Huai (海岱及淮). The city of Xuzhou in modern Jiangsu preserves the ancient name and has been continuously significant as a strategic junction between north and south China.

The word names the province 徐州 as listed among the 九州, and the foreign-language fields give the readings of that name: Mandarin xúzhōu, Cantonese ceoi4 zau1, Japanese じょしゅう, Korean 서주 and Vietnamese Từ Châu.  The Dan'a'yo reading 쇼줏 is derived from 徐 and 州, so it differs from the Korean and `kwin` is false.  The character 徐 has its own `stand_in` 徐様, so this word is not its legitimizer.
