## Notes
- [酒](characters/酒.md) "alcohol, liquor" + [糟](characters/糟.md) "dregs, grain lees" — "distiller's grain, lees."

Mandarin jiǔzāo, cantonese zau2 zou1, and korean 주조 are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. **Found and fixed a real-equivalent-instead-of-own-reading bug on `japanese`**: さけかす used the native kun-reading compound (the everyday word for "sake lees"), replaced with the honest compositional on-reading しゅそう (酒's own SHU + 糟's own SOU). Vietnamese tửu tao is the honest compositional reading (酒's own tửu + 糟's own tao); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 줏잣 diverges from Sino-Korean 주조 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄨㄛㄐㄚㄨ unique among both words and characters).

**Found and fixed a missing citation** on 糟.md's own Words list. Citation on 酒.md was already correctly present. Neither 酒's `stand_in` (酒精) nor 糟's own `stand_in` (糟糠) points to this compound.
