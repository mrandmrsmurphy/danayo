## Notes
- [漸](../characters/漸.md) "gradually," reduplicated — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 漸 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

漸漸 means gradually, little by little.

Mandarin jiànjiàn and Korean 점점 are fully compositional, matching 漸's own stored citations exactly. Fixed a malformed hybrid tone notation in `cantonese` (zim6 zim6-2 → zim6 zim2, the real colloquial change-tone pronunciation of the reduplicated second syllable). Japanese ますます is the real, standard native word for "gradually, increasingly" — kept as-is rather than the compositional ぜんぜん, which would collide with the modern false friend 全然 ("not at all"). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with tiệm tiệm (漸's own citation tiệm, as in the real term tiệm tiến "gradual"). No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄇㄐㄝㄇ unique across both words and characters).
