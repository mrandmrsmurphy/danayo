## Notes
- [犀](../characters/犀.md) "rhinoceros" + [角 (char)](../characters/角%20(char).md) "horn"

Wiktionary lists 犀角 as Chinese "rhinoceros horn". Wikipedia (Eight Principles of Yong) records it as Li Puguang's metaphorical name, from his Yuan-era 永字八法解, for the sixth principle, the curved stroke (彎, classically 掠), a tapering curve. See [[怪石]] and the [[Stroke]] page. Its readings are compositional.
