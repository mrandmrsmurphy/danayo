## Notes
- [犀](../characters/犀.md) "rhinoceros" + [角 (char)](../characters/角%20(char).md) "horn"

**犀角** is an ordinary word for the horn of a rhinoceros, long used in Chinese medicine and carving. As a calligraphy term it is the figurative name of the sixth of the eight principles of the 永字八法, the curved stroke (弯, classically 掠): the tapering curve is imagined as the curve of a rhino horn.

Li Puguang (李溥光), writing in the Yuan dynasty in his 永字八法解, gave each of the eight strokes of the 永字八法 a figurative two-character name; 犀角 is the curve's name. See [[怪石]] for the full set, and the [[Stroke]] page.

The Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and North Korean forms are the ordinary compound readings of the word. No Vietnamese form is given.
