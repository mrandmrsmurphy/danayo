## Notes
- [天 (char)](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven; sky" + [体](../characters/体.md) "body"

天体 is a celestial body: any object in the sky, such as a star, planet, moon or comet. Wiktionary glosses Japanese てんたい as "celestial body". The Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the characters, and Korean 천체 matches.

It is the general term under which fall the [星](星.md), [行星](行星.md), [彗星](彗星.md) and the rest. The study of them is [天文学](天文学.md).
