## Notes
- [液](../characters/液.md) "fluid, sap" + [体](../characters/体.md) "group, body, form" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 液 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (体's own stand-in is [[体系]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

液体 means liquid.

Mandarin yètǐ, Cantonese jik6 tai2, Japanese えきたい, and Korean 액체 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 液's and 体's own stored citations exactly).
