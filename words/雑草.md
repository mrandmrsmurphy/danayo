## Notes
- [雑](../characters/雑%20(char).md) "miscellaneous, mixed" + [草](../characters/草%20(char).md) "grass" — "mixed grass," a weed. Their own `stand_in` values: 雑's is itself; 草's is itself; none points here, so 雑草 is an independent compound.

雑草 is a weed: a plant growing where it is not wanted. Mandarin zácǎo (雜草), Cantonese zaap6 cou2 and Japanese ざっそう are attested, and Korean 잡초 is the same compound. Compare [[草]] (grass) and [[雑]] (miscellaneous).
