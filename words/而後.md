## Notes
- [而 (char)](../characters/而%20(char).md) "and" + [後 (char)](../characters/後%20(char).md) "after" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both are stand-ins for themselves), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

而後 is an archaic Classical Chinese connective meaning "after that, only then" — a literary construction that survives mainly in set phrases like 而今而後 ("from now on").

Mandarin érhòu and Cantonese ji4 hau6 are standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese じご (jigo, confirmed via web search — 而 here takes its on-reading ジ, not the kun にread used elsewhere) and Korean 이후 (ihu) are the compositional Sino-readings, paralleling the more common synonym 以後. **Fixed a real bug**: `japanese`/`korean` had been filled with native paraphrases (その後, "after that"; 그 후, "that after") rather than 而後's own attested readings — corrected. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
