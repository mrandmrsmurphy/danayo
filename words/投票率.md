## Notes
- [投](../characters/投%20(char).md) "cast, throw" + [票](../characters/票%20(char).md) "ballot, ticket" + [率](../characters/率.md) "rate, ratio" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 投 and 票's own stand-ins are themselves; 率's own is [[比率]]).

投票率 means voter turnout or voting rate.

Mandarin tóupiàolǜ, Cantonese tau4 piu3 leot6, Japanese とうひょうりつ, and Vietnamese đầu phiếu suất are all standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 투표율 reflects the regular liaison sound-change 률→율 after a vowel-final syllable, not a break from compositionality. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄨㄆ⼘ㄨㄌㄨㄊ unique across both words and characters).
