## Notes
- [氾](../characters/氾.md) "overflow" + [濫](../characters/濫.md) "flooding, excessive, overflowing" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 氾 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

氾濫 means flood, to inundate.

Mandarin fànlàn and Japanese はんらん (HAN+RAN) are standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 범람 matches 氾's own stored Sino citation exactly.
