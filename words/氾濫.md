## Notes
- [氾](../characters/氾.md) "overflow" + [濫](../characters/濫.md) "flooding, excessive, overflowing" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 氾 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

氾濫 means flood, to inundate.

Mandarin fànlàn and Japanese はんらん (HAN+RAN) are standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bugs: `羅馬字`/`諺文` had been contaminated to bamlam/밤람 instead of famlam/빰람, matching 氾's own stored citation (fam/빰). Filled the blank `cantonese` (faan3 laam6) and `vietnamese` (phiếm lạm — 氾's own phiếm + 濫's own lạm, the standard Sino reading despite sitting deep in an apparently-scrambled character-level list; see [[汎濫]] for the same note). Korean 범람 matches 氾's own stored Sino citation exactly.

**Real homophone found**: 氾 and 汎 are distinct characters that happen to share the identical Dan'a'yo reading (ㄈㄚㄇ each). 氾's own `stand_in` is this word, 氾濫 (legitimizing 氾), while 濫's own `stand_in` is [[汎濫]] (legitimizing 濫) — two genuinely independent word pages sharing 注音 ㄈㄚㄇㄌㄚㄇ. Added the full callout here and a matching one to the already-stamped [[汎濫]].
