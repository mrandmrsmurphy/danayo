## Notes
- [毎 (char)](../characters/毎%20(char).md) "every; each; distributive" + [物 (char)](../characters/物%20(char).md) "thing; object; material result"

毎物 is the distributive correlative in the 物 ("thing") column of the correlative table: "each thing; every thing one by one; each object", with the predicate applied to the members of a set of things in turn. The character [毎 (char)](../characters/毎%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *moi*, ㄇㄛㄧ; its alias 每 is the traditional form, so 每物 is an `aliases` entry here, not a separate file) is the distributive morpheme of [毎日](毎日.md) "every day", [毎週](毎週.md) "every week", [毎月](毎月.md) "every month" and [毎年](毎年.md) "every year"; the free word is [毎](毎.md). [物 (char)](../characters/物%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *mud*, ㄇㄨㄊ) is "thing; object; material result".

Its part of speech is 副用名詞 (adverbial noun), the class of every cell of the 毎 row ([毎事](毎事.md), [毎人](毎人.md), [毎名](毎名.md), [毎処](毎処.md), [毎時](毎時.md), [毎様](毎様.md), [毎多](毎多.md), [毎類](毎類.md)). `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md` (line 11) lists "Adverbial Nouns" as a word class, and `grammar/Linguistics Features.md` (line 68) states that an adverbial noun may be the topic or frame of a clause with no marking but needs marking when it modifies the main verb. No example in `grammar/` uses 毎物 itself, so its syntax is inferred from that rule. Illustrative only (invented, not from `grammar/`): 毎物検 "check each thing, one at a time"; 毎物記 "label every object individually".

Against its column neighbours: the collective [皆物](皆物.md) "all things; everything" treats the things as one totality, whereas 毎物 goes through them one by one; this is the same distributive/collective split as [毎人](毎人.md) against [皆人](皆人.md). [某物](某物.md) "something" is the indefinite, naming one unspecified thing; [何物](何物.md) "what?" is the interrogative; and [此物](此物.md) "this thing", [其物](其物.md) "that thing" and [彼物](彼物.md) "that thing yonder" each point at one particular thing, where 毎物 ranges over all of them. Against [毎事](毎事.md) "each matter": 事 is an act, event or affair, whereas 物 is a concrete object. Against [毎類](毎類.md) "each kind": 毎物 individuates single things, not classes. The productive affix [－物](－物.md) forms "thing that is X-ed" from a verb or stative, so it builds new nouns, whereas 毎物 is one fixed cell of a paradigm. Dan'a'yo keeps the sense shared across CJKV, "each thing"; no narrowing is needed.

The cross-linguistic fields were checked against Wiktionary on 2026-10-11. Wiktionary has no entry for 每物 or 毎物, so the compound is a Dan'a'yo construction and no field simply copies it. (Korean 매물 is a different word, 賣物 "article for sale", and is not related.) The fields hold the distributive "one by one" words that do have entries: Mandarin 逐一 (zhúyī; Cantonese zuk6 jat1), an adverb glossed "one by one; one after another"; Japanese 逐一 (ちくいち, chikuichi), an adverb glossed "one by one"; and Korean 하나하나 (hanahana), a noun "every one" and adverb "one by one". They give the distributive sense but are not thing-specific; the thing is carried compositionally, as in Mandarin 每件東西 "each item" and Japanese 一つ一つ "each one" (both from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese *từng cái một* "one thing at a time" is from memory, not re-checked; hvdic.thivien.net covers single characters (毎 mỗi, 物 vật), not phrases, so no `vietnamese` field is entered. The earlier Japanese claims (that 毎物 is read まいぶつ, and the Mandarin 每件东西 and 每一个 as the fields' equivalents) were unverified and have been removed; the earlier statement that classical Chinese 每物 was an attested quantifier is likewise not supported by Wiktionary and was removed.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md)'s correlative table (line 125; the same chart is in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`, line 145 in the 物 row, with the column header cell 毎 on line 142), 毎物 is the second cell of the 毎 ("every") row, after [毎事](毎事.md) and before [毎人](毎人.md), [毎名](毎名.md), [毎処](毎処.md), [毎時](毎時.md), [毎様](毎様.md), [毎多](毎多.md) and [毎類](毎類.md). The table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), 63 forms, so 毎物 is parsed compositionally: 毎 = distributive, 物 = thing. It is listed on the Grammar page only.

The 羅馬字 is *moimud*, from the character pages (毎 *moi*, 物 *mud*); the earlier *moymud* disagreed with the 毎 page. 諺文 뫼묻 is 뫼 + 묻, as on those pages.

The 注音 ㄇㄛㄧㄇㄨㄊ carries no syllable-boundary dot, unlike [此物](此物.md) (ㄑㄜ·ㄇㄨㄊ). The test is whether the string has a second legal partition under the (C)(G)V(C) structure of `grammar/文法 - 02音韻論.md`. For 此物 it does: ㄑㄜㄇ + ㄨㄊ (ㄇ as coda, null-onset ㄨㄊ). Here the analogous parse ㄇㄛㄧㄇ + ㄨㄊ fails, because ㄇㄛㄧㄇ would need both ㄧ and ㄇ as codas and is not in `syllables/`. The only other split, ㄇㄛ + ㄧㄇ + ㄨㄊ (all three are in `syllables/`), would break the diphthong ㄛㄧ, which this vault does not do (as on [毎様](毎様.md) and [毎時](毎時.md)). The only partition is therefore ㄇㄛㄧ + ㄇㄨㄊ, with no ambiguity for a dot to resolve.
