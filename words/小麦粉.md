## Notes
- [小 (char)](../characters/小%20(char).md) "small" + [麦](../characters/麦.md) "wheat" + [粉 (char)](../characters/粉%20(char).md) "powder"

小麦粉 is wheat flour: flour ground from wheat. Wiktionary glosses both Mandarin xiǎomàifěn and Japanese こむぎこ as "wheat flour". Korean 소맥분 is the reading of the characters, and the Cantonese reading is built from the characters.

It is the [小麦](小麦.md), wheat, ground, and the specific kind of [麺](麺.md), flour. Rice flour is the [米粉](米粉.md).
