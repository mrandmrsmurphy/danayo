## Notes
- [理](../characters/理.md) "reason; principle" + [念](../characters/念.md) "thought; to think of"

理念 is "idea; ideal; philosophy; conviction," a guiding idea or principle that someone holds. According to Wiktionary, Chinese 理念 (lǐniàn, Cantonese lei5 nim6) is "thought; idea," "notion; concept; ideology; principle; philosophy," "theory," and "conviction; faith; belief"; Japanese りねん (a goon reading) and Korean 이념 have the senses "philosophy, ideology." Wiktionary has no Vietnamese section, so `vietnamese` lý niệm is the compositional reading. The `korean` field 리념 is the North Korean form, and the everyday South Korean spelling is 이념. In the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [平臥即是正義](../translation/平臥即是正義.md) it renders 观念 "concepts, ideas," in 伝統理念 "traditional ideas." Related: [思想](思想.md) "thought" and [理論](理論.md) "theory."
