## Notes
- [対](../characters/対.md) "face" + [決 (char)](../characters/決%20(char).md) "decide"

対決 is a showdown: to face an opponent in order to settle a matter. Wiktionary glosses Japanese たいけつ as "confrontation, face-off, showdown; to face off, to confront". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 대결 matches.

It is the meeting that decides, where [勝負](勝負.md) is the contest and its outcome and [挑戦](挑戦.md) is the challenge that begins it.
