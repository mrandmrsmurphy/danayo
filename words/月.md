## Notes
- [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "moon; month" — stand-in for [[月 (char)|月]], which cannot appear independently

月 is a 象形 pictograph of a crescent moon with an interior stroke marking shading or a surface feature — a Swadesh-list word (`swadesh: 148`), among the vault's most basic vocabulary. Like [[日]] (sun/day), it carries a double sense split into the celestial body itself (望月 "full moon," 蜜月 "honeymoon," 月食 "lunar eclipse," 月虹 "moonbow") and, independently, the calendar unit "month" derived from the lunar cycle — the latter generating an enormous compound family paralleling 日's: ordinal months (一月–十二月), poetic literary month-names tied to seasonal flora (杏月 "apricot-blossom month," 桂月 "osmanthus month"), and the twelve Earthly Branch month-names (寅月, 卯月, etc.) shared with the traditional sexagenary calendar.

**A genuine `kwin` bug found and fixed on both this word's own page and its character page**: both were stamped `kwin: true`, but Dan'a'yo 웓 (from Middle Chinese 疑母 ŋ + 月合韻 ʉɐt, preserving the checked -t/-d coda as `-d`) and Sino-Korean 월 are not the same string — 웓 (U+C6D3) ends in a written ㄷ coda, while 월 (U+C6D4) ends in ㄹ, the regular MC -t → Korean -l shift found across the whole corpus. Corrected both pages to `kwin: false`, the same bug independently found and fixed on [[越]] (see below).

Japanese carries a genuine on'yomi split by function: げつ (GETSU) is the general-purpose reading (月曜日 getsuyōbi "Monday," 月経 gekkei, 月給 gekkyū), while がつ (GATSU) is reserved specifically for month-counting compounds with numbers (一月 ichigatsu "January," not *ichigetsu) — a complementary distribution rather than free variation. The native 訓読み つき (tsuki) remains the ordinary standalone word for "moon" in everyday speech, parallel to 日's ひ. Vietnamese nguyệt is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading (nguyệt san "monthly magazine," trăng is the native word for "moon" itself) — the character's rarer variant ngoạt was not used here.

**Real homophone group found, the widest of this sweep**: `注音 ⼔ㄊ`/`웓` is also carried by two other existing word pages, [[越]] ("exceed") and [[曰]] ("spake, ancient quotative particle"). Added the full mutual callout across all three. While adding it, found the identical `kwin` string-mismatch bug already described above independently present on `越`'s own word and character pages (諺文 웓 vs korean 월) — fixed both to `false` there too. `曰`'s own `kwin: false` was already correct (its korean 왈 genuinely differs from 웓). This vault-internally also uses 月 as the periodic-table prefix for selenium via [[月素]], the same Dan'a'yo-only naming convention as 日素/helium.
