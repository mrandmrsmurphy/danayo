## Notes
- [考](../characters/考.md) "consider, examine" + [察](../characters/察.md) "inspect, examine" — 察's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 考's own is [[考慮]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 考察 legitimizes 察 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 考 is individually `kwin: false`.

考察 means to investigate, observe, or study.

Mandarin kǎochá, Cantonese haau2 caat3, Japanese こうさつ (kōsatsu), and Korean 고찰 (gochal) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 考察 is itself a common attested word for "investigate, examine" in all four languages. Vietnamese khảo sát is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
