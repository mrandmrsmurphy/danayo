## Notes
- [智](../characters/智.md) "wisdom" + [者](../characters/者.md) "person who"

智者 is "a wise person; a sage." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 智者 (zhìzhě, Cantonese zi3 ze2) is "wise person; person of wisdom; sage." Wiktionary lists it for Chinese only; the word is also in use in Korean (지자) and Vietnamese (trí giả), but those two readings were not independently checked here, and `japanese` ちしゃ is the compositional reading. 者 here is the agent affix [－者](－者.md) "-er." In the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [China](../lookup/HSK/China.md), 智者 運動 stands for the Chinese 智者运动, the Sophists' movement, whose Greek teachers (the 智者) held that "man is the measure of all things." Related: [智慧](智慧.md) "wisdom" and [学者](学者.md) "scholar."
