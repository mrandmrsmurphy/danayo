## Notes
- [墓](../characters/墓.md) "grave" + [碑](../characters/碑.md) "tombstone"
- This word is the viable form of [碑](../characters/碑.md): the character's meaning (tombstone) is identical, but 碑 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 墓碑 is its `stand_in` compound.

墓碑 is the standard word for a gravestone: 墓 is the grave and 碑 an upright inscribed stone (a stele). Mandarin mùbēi, Cantonese mou6 bei1, Japanese ぼひ (bohi) and Korean 묘비 (myobi) are all the same compound. In Japanese the ordinary everyday words for a grave marker are 墓石 and 墓標, so 墓碑 is the more formal or written one; in Chinese and Korean it is the normal word.

In Dan'a'yo it names the marker itself, as opposed to [[墓穴]] (the excavated grave), [[塚]] (a burial mound) and [[墓地]] (the cemetery). No Dan'a'yo-specific narrowing is needed.
