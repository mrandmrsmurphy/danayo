## Notes
- [階](../characters/階.md) "step; stairs" + [級](../characters/級.md) "level; grade"

階級 is a social class, or a rank: a level in a society or an organization. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin jiējí as "class (social division based on socio-economic status)" and, literary, "rank of an official" and "step on a flight of stairs". Japanese かいきゅう is "social standing; social class". Vietnamese giai cấp is the same word, and Korean 계급 and Cantonese gaai1 kap1 match.

It is the structure that [地位](地位.md), a person's standing, is placed in, with [上位](上位.md) and [下位](下位.md) as its upper and lower ranks. The gap between the classes is the [貧富](貧富.md) and the [格差](格差.md).
