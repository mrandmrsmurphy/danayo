## Etymology
- [疑](../characters/疑.md) "doubt, question" + [義](../characters/義.md) "meaning, principle"

## Notes
A doubtful point; a question about what something means. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 疑義 (alias 疑义; Mandarin yíyì, Cantonese ji4 ji6) and Japanese ぎぎ as "doubt; doubtful point", and lists Korean 의의 as a hanja form of the same word. It has no Vietnamese entry, so the `vietnamese` field is omitted.

Neither character's `stand_in` points here: 疑's is [懐疑](懐疑.md) and 義's is [意義](意義.md). The Korean reading 의의 is identical to that of [意義](意義.md), "meaning, significance", while Dan'a'yo keeps the two apart (ㄧ·ㄜㄧ versus ㄜㄜㄧ).

Used in [文法 - 91疑義語](../grammar/文法%20-%2091疑義語.md), "conflicted words", literally "doubtful-meaning words".
