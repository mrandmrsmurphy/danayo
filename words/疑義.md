## Etymology
- [疑](../characters/疑.md) "doubt, question" + [義](../characters/義.md) "meaning, principle"

## Notes
"A doubtful point; a question about what something means." Mandarin yíyì, Cantonese ji4 ji6, Japanese ぎぎ and Korean 의의 are all directly attested standard readings (verified on Wiktionary; the Chinese and Japanese entries both define it as "doubt; doubtful point"). In Japanese it is a formal, often administrative or legal word, as in 疑義を唱える ("to raise a query") and 疑義が生じる ("a doubt arises"). No Vietnamese reading was found on Wiktionary or in the Sino-Vietnamese dictionary checked, so `vietnamese` is omitted.

Neither character's `stand_in` points here: 疑's is [[懐疑]] and 義's is [[意義]], so this compound is not a legitimizer for either. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 이읫 diverges from Sino-Korean 의의 in both syllables.

A trap for Korean speakers: the Sino-Korean reading 의의 is identical to that of [[意義]] ("meaning, significance"), so 疑義 and 意義 are homophones in Korean, and Japanese ぎぎ and いぎ are close. Dan'a'yo keeps them apart (ㄧ·ㄜㄧ versus ㄜㄜㄧ). No homophones within Dan'a'yo itself (注音 ㄧ·ㄜㄧ is unique among both words and characters).

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot added**: the 疑|義 boundary is a genuine resegmentation fork — `ㄧ`(疑)+`ㄜㄧ`(義) and the alternate `ㄧㄜ`(a real assigned syllable, 의/'iǝ, "rely on")+`ㄧ`(also real) are both fully legal syllable sequences — so the dot is required: ㄧ·ㄜㄧ.

Used in [[文法 - 91疑義語]] ("conflicted words", literally "doubtful-meaning words").
