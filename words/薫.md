## Notes
- [薫 (char)](../characters/薫%20(char).md) "fragrant; smoke" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; standalone form, 薫 refers to fragrant smoke or incense, and by extension to a pleasing scent.

薫 shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading (hun/훈/ㄏㄨㄋ) with two other words, [[勲]] ("meritorious deed, merit") and [[訓]] ("interpret, translate") — a three-way homophone group cross-referenced on all three pages.

Mandarin xūn, Cantonese fan1, and Korean 훈 (hun) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese かお (kao, kun'yomi, already 薫's own stored `japanese_native` value — part of かおる, "to be fragrant") is the everyday reading for this word specifically. Vietnamese huân is the standard Hán Việt reading — coincidentally identical to [[勲]]'s own Vietnamese reading despite the two characters being etymologically unrelated, verified independently via hvdic.thivien.net. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 훈 matches Sino-Korean 훈 exactly (**real bug fixed**: was previously stored as `false`).
