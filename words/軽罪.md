## Notes
- [軽 (char)](characters/軽%20(char).md) "light, not heavy" + [罪 (char)](characters/罪%20(char).md) "sin, crime" — "misdemeanor" (a "light crime").

Mandarin qīngzuì, cantonese heng1 zeoi6, and korean 경죄 are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. Japanese けいざい is likewise a directly attested reading. Vietnamese khinh tội is the honest compositional reading (軽's own khinh + 罪's own tội); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `輕罪`/`轻罪` are the standard traditional/simplified spelling variants (軽→輕/轻), correctly listed as aliases. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 킹죄 diverges from Sino-Korean 경죄 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄧㄫㄐㄛㄧ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 軽's own `stand_in` is 軽 itself and 罪's own `stand_in` is 罪 itself, so this compound legitimizes neither constituent.
