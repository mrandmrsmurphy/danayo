## Notes
- [改](../characters/改%20(char).md) "redo, renew" + [善](../characters/善%20(char).md) "good" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 改 and 善's own stand-ins are themselves).

改善 means improvement or reform.

Mandarin gǎishàn, Cantonese goi2 sin6, Japanese かいぜん, Korean 개선, and Vietnamese cải thiện are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄧㄙ·ㄝㄋ unique across both words and characters).
