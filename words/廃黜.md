## Notes
- [廃](../characters/廃.md) "abrogate; terminate" + [黜](../characters/黜.md) "dethrone" — this word is the stand-in for [黜](../characters/黜.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo; the character's own meaning (to dethrone, dismiss) is already essentially identical to the compound's.

Japanese はいちゅつ, Korean 폐출, and Vietnamese phế truất are all standard, formal/literary readings, typical of historical/political contexts (deposing a monarch or official).

**Correction (2026-08-28)**: `cantonese` had been stored as fai3zyut6 — 黜's own real Cantonese reading is zeot1, not zyut6 (an unrelated syllable) — corrected to "fai3 zeot1." No homophones (注音 ㄈㄝㄊㄨㄊ unique across both words and characters).
