## Notes
- [楓](../characters/楓.md) "maple" + [樹](../characters/樹.md) "plant; tree" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [楓](../characters/楓.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

楓樹 means maple tree.

Mandarin fēngshù, Cantonese fung1 syu6, Japanese ふうじゅ, Korean 풍수, and Vietnamese phong thụ are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 楓's and 樹's own stored citations exactly). Discovered a genuine homophone with [[風水]] ("feng shui," both 注音 ㄈㄨㄫㄙㄨ, 諺文 뿡수) — cross-referenced both pages.
