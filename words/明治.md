## Notes
- [明 (char)](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright, enlightened" + [治](../characters/治.md) "govern" — literally "enlightened rule," the era name for Emperor Meiji's reign (1868–1912), drawn from a classical phrase in the Yijing.

Mandarin Míngzhì, Cantonese ming4 zi6, Japanese めいじ (Meiji), Korean 명치, and Vietnamese Minh Trị are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 明's and 治's own stored citations exactly.

- See [Calendar](../lexipedia/Calendar.md) for this era's place among Japan's modern eras; see [Japan](../lexipedia/Japan.md) and [歴史綱要](../lexipedia/歴史綱要.md) for broader historical context.
