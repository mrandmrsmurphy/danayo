## Etymology
- [宝](../characters/宝.md) "treasure" + [物 (char)](../characters/物%20(char).md) "thing" — stand-in for [[宝]], which cannot appear independently

## Notes
- "Treasure, treasury; a precious/valuable object." Filled blank `vietnamese: bảo vật`, directly attested and extremely common, notably in the official "bảo vật quốc gia" (National Treasure) designation, paralleling Japanese's own 国宝 (kokuhō, National Treasure) register. Kept the existing `aliases: [寶物]` (legitimate traditional variant of 宝). **Left `hsk_level` unset**: sources disagree on the constituent character's own level, and no reliable classification for the compound itself was found.
- `kwin: false` confirmed (宝 alone doesn't match Korean, 物 does — AND-rule). No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄨㄇㄨㄊ unique among both words and characters).
