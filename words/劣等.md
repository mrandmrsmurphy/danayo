## Notes
- [劣](../characters/劣.md) "inferior, bad" + [等 (char)](../characters/等%20(char).md) "grade, rank" — of inferior grade; below standard; the lower end of a scale

Wiktionary: Chinese "inferior; low-grade"; Japanese "low-grade, inferior" and "inferiority, a low grade"; Korean 열등 is the hanja form, "inferior; low-grade", the `korean` field here giving the North Korean 렬등. The antonym is [[優等]].
