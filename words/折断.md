## Notes
- [折](../characters/折.md) "fold, break" + [断](../characters/断.md) "sever, break" — "fold and break," to snap. Their own `stand_in` values: 折's is [[折畳]]; 断's is [[割断]]; none points here, so 折断 is an independent compound.

折断 is to snap: to break something stiff, such as a branch or a bone, by bending until it parts. Mandarin zhéduàn (折斷) and Cantonese zit3 tyun5 are the ordinary words. Japanese says 折る and Korean 부러뜨리다, so those two fields hold the character readings. Compare [[破]] (to break, rend) and [[斬]] (to cut).
