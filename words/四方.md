>[!caution] The senses split — the four directions, or "square, cubic"
>四方 is the four directions and, figuratively, "everywhere" in Chinese, Japanese and Korean, and the Dan'a'yo sense. Chinese also uses it attributively for "square; cubic" and as the name of the Quad, so a Chinese reader may hear those. Listed as a Split on [文法 - 91疑義語](../grammar/文法%20-%2091疑義語.md).

## Notes
- [四 (char)](../characters/四%20(char).md) "four (stand-in: itself)" + [方](../characters/方.md) "direction; side (stand-in: [[方向]])"

Wiktionary: Chinese 四方, literally "the four directions (north, south, east and west)" and figuratively "everywhere; far and wide", also the Quadrilateral Security Dialogue, and attributively "square; cube; cubic"; Japanese 四方 "(in) all directions, everywhere"; Korean 사방 is the hanja form, "in all directions, everywhere". The Dan'a'yo sense is the four directions. It sits beside [[五方]].
