## Notes
- [風 (char)](../characters/風%20(char).md) "wind; custom" + [俗 (char)](../characters/俗%20(char).md) "custom; vulgar"

風俗 is the customs of a people or a time: how they live, dress and behave. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin fēngsú as "social custom; practice; cultural habit" and Japanese ふうぞく as "a custom; manners; mores". Both also record Japan's sense of the sex industry, which Dan'a'yo does not take. Vietnamese phong tục is the same word, and Korean 풍속 and Cantonese fung1 zuk6 match.

It is the broad living custom of a place, where [習俗](習俗.md) is a custom or convention and [儀礼](儀礼.md) is ceremony. The vulgar or worldly side is [世俗](世俗.md).
