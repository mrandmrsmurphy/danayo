## Notes
- [出 (char)](../characters/出%20(char).md) "exit" + [谷 (char)](../characters/谷%20(char).md) "valley" + [記](../characters/記.md) "record" — "Exodus," literally "the record of going out of the valley/gorge," a historical Chinese Catholic biblical rendering of the book's name (paralleling the Protestant-associated 出埃及記, "record of going out of Egypt," listed here as an alias rather than the primary name). The reading fields document each daughter language's own real, attested name for this book rather than a compositional Dan'a'yo transliteration, following the vault's standard practice for proper nouns/titles (as with [[冥王星]] and other named entities): Mandarin/Cantonese use the common 出埃及記-based name; Japanese 出エジプト記 (Shutsu Ejiputo Ki) likewise; Korean 출애굽기 is the traditional Protestant-tradition name (출애굽, "coming out of Aegup/Egypt"); Vietnamese Xuất Hành ("the going-forth, departure") is the Catholic-tradition Vietnamese Bible's own name for the book.

- 孝親天賜 — honor father and mother
- 殺姦窃偽 — [do not] kill, commit adultery, steal, [bear] false [witness]
- 勿貪隣物 — do not covet your neighbor's property
