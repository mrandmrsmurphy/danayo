## Notes
- [神](../characters/神%20(char).md) "god, deity, spirit" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 神 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

神 names divinity broadly — a god, deity, or spirit, and by extension anything sacred, mysterious, or extraordinary (神話, "myth"; 神秘, "mystery"; 神聖, "sacred, holy"). Its etymology ties divinity to lightning (its phonetic component 申 originally depicted lightning), reflecting an ancient association between thunder/lightning and divine power.

Mandarin shén, Cantonese san4, Japanese しん (shin), Korean 신 (sin), and Vietnamese thần are all standard, fully compositional readings.
