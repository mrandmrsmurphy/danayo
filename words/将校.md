## Notes
- [将 (char)](../characters/将%20(char).md) "general; commander" + [校](../characters/校.md) "school; military rank" — no stand-in relationship applies (将's own `stand_in` is itself, 校's is [[学校]]).

将校 names a commissioned officer — 校 here draws not on its everyday "school" sense but its older, separate "military rank/command post" sense (as in 校尉, an ancient military office), paired with 将's "commander." 将 again uses its "commander/general" reading (jiàng/zoeng3 in Mandarin/Cantonese), the same sense-switch documented on [[将帥]]/[[将兵]].

Japanese しょうこう, Korean 장교, and Vietnamese tướng hiệu (verified) are all standard, non-drifted readings. No HSK level is given for the compound. No homophones (注音 ㄐ⺢ㄫㄏ⼘ㄨ unique across both words and characters).
