## Notes
- [知](../characters/知%20(char).md) "know" + [識](../characters/識.md) "recognize, know" — literally "knowing and recognizing," i.e. accumulated learning or information.

知識 is the general, everyday word for knowledge — facts, information, and understanding acquired through learning or experience, as distinct from 智慧 ("wisdom," the deeper capacity to apply knowledge well). Mandarin zhīshi, Cantonese zi1 sik1, Japanese ちしき (chishiki), Korean 지식 (jisik), and Vietnamese tri thức are all fully compositional and are the standard, everyday words for "knowledge" in each language.
