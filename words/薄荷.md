## Notes
- [薄](../characters/薄.md) "thin, weak" is separately legitimized by [[希薄]]. [荷](../characters/荷.md) "luggage" is separately legitimized by [[荷物]]. No cranberry.

Mandarin bòhe correctly uses 薄's special reading bò, attested only in this compound (distinct from 薄's usual báo/bó) — not a bug. Korean 박하 and vietnamese bạc hà are both the real, standard, fully attested words for "mint."
