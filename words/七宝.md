## Notes
- [七 (char)](../characters/七%20(char).md) "seven" + [宝](../characters/宝.md) "treasure" — no `#cranberry`: 宝's own `stand_in` is [[宝物]], 七's is 七 itself; neither points here.
- (Buddhism) the seven treasures: gold, silver, pearls, agate, crystal, coral, lapis lazuli.

Mandarin qībǎo, Cantonese cat1 bou2, Korean 칠보, and Sino-Vietnamese thất bảo are standard compositional readings. **Fixed a real bug**: `japanese` was しちほう, a bare unassimilated concatenation — corrected to しっぽう (shippou), the real, standard Japanese reading (with sokuon assimilation and h→p devoicing, the same pattern as [[一半]]/[[一百]]), used both for the Buddhist Seven Treasures and, in art, cloisonné enamel (七宝焼, shippou-yaki). `kwin` is false (AND-rule: both 七 and 宝 individually false).
