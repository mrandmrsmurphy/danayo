## Notes
- [七 (char)](../characters/七%20(char).md) "seven" + [宝](../characters/宝.md) "treasure" — no `#cranberry`: 宝's own `stand_in` is [[宝物]], 七's is 七 itself; neither points here.
- (Buddhism) the seven treasures: gold, silver, pearls, agate, crystal, coral, lapis lazuli.

Mandarin qībǎo, Cantonese cat1 bou2, Korean 칠보, and Sino-Vietnamese thất bảo are standard compositional readings.
