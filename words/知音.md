## Notes
- [知 (char)](../characters/知%20(char).md) "know" + [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" — literally "one who knows the sound," i.e. someone who truly understands you.

知音 names a kindred spirit or soulmate friend — someone who understands you deeply, not merely a companion. The term traces to the classical story of Bo Ya (伯牙), a master guqin player, and the woodcutter Zhong Ziqi (鍾子期), the only listener able to perceive exactly what Bo Ya's music expressed — mountains, flowing water, whatever was in his heart. When Zhong Ziqi died, Bo Ya broke his instrument's strings and never played again, having lost the one person who truly understood him (伯牙絕弦, "Bo Ya cuts the strings").

Mandarin zhīyīn, Cantonese zi1 jam1, Japanese ちいん (chiin), Korean 지음 (jieum), and Vietnamese tri âm are all fully compositional and carry this same emotionally weighty sense of deep mutual understanding across the sphere.
