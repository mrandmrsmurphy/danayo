## Notes
- [半 (char)](../characters/半%20(char).md) "half" + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "moon"

半月 is the half moon: the moon when half its disc is lit. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin bànyuè as "half-moon; fortnight" and Japanese はんげつ as "half-moon; half a month". Korean 반월 and Cantonese bun3 jyut6 match.

It lies between the [新月](新月.md), new moon, and the [満月](満月.md), full moon. The second sense, a fortnight, is the half of a [月](月.md), month.
