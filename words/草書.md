## Notes
- [草 (char)](../characters/草%20(char).md) "grass" + [書](../characters/書.md) "writing; script"

草書 is cursive script: the fastest, most flowing style, in which strokes run together. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin cǎoshū as "cursive script" and Japanese そうしょ as "grass script". Korean 초서 and Vietnamese thảo thư are the same word. Cantonese cou2 syu1 matches.

It is the freest of the scripts, beyond the [行書](行書.md), semi-cursive; the older formal script is the [隷書](隷書.md).
