## Notes
- [気 (char)](../characters/気%20(char).md) "spirit; mood" + [質](../characters/質.md) "nature; substance"

気質 is temperament: the lasting cast of a person's feelings, the disposition one is born with. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin qìzhì as "temperament; disposition; manners" and Japanese きしつ as "temperament, disposition". Korean 기질, Vietnamese khí chất and Cantonese hei3 zat1 match.

It is the inborn side of a person, where [性格](性格.md) is the whole character built on it. The mood of the moment is the [気分](気分.md).
