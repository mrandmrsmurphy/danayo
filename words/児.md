## Notes
- [児 (char)](../characters/児%20(char).md) "baby"

児 (alternate forms 兒, 儿) is 象形: an infant depicted with an imperfect cranium — the still-open fontanelle — hence "child, offspring." It heads a wide family of compounds spanning developmental stages ([[胎児]] "fetus," [[幼児]] "baby, infant," [[嬰児]] "baby") and social categories ([[児童]] "minor, student, child," [[孤児院]] "orphanage," [[女児]] "daughter").

Mandarin ér, Cantonese ji4, and Japanese じ/に/げい (ji/ni/gei, on'yomi, fully productive: 児童 じどう "child") alongside native こ (ko, the everyday word for "child") all preserve the sense. Korean 아 (a) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 아이 (ai, the everyday word for "child"). Vietnamese nhi is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in nhi đồng ("children") and nhũ nhi (乳児, "infant, nursing baby").

This word shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading ('ei/에/ㄝㄧ) with another word still awaiting its own turn in this sweep, [[詣]] ("visit") — the reciprocal half of this callout will be completed when it comes up.
