## Notes
- [資](../characters/資.md) "property, wealth, capital" is legitimized by this exact compound (its own stand-in). [本 (char)](../characters/本%20(char).md) "foundation" is separately legitimized by [[本]] itself. No cranberry.

Mandarin zīběn, japanese しほん, korean 자본, and vietnamese tư bản (all also the real common words for "capital") confirmed standard and compositional.
## Etymology
- [資](../characters/資.md) "property, wealth, capital" + [本 (char)](../characters/本%20(char).md) "foundation"
