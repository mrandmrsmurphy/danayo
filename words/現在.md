## Notes
- [現 (char)](../characters/現%20(char).md) "present, appear" + [在 (char)](../characters/在%20(char).md) "exist, be at" — literally "presently existing," the current moment.

`pos: 名詞`: 現在 is grammatically a noun ("the present, the current time"), not an adverb — despite English "now" often functioning adverbially, Dan'a'yo treats this word the same way Chinese does, as a time-noun that a sentence locates events relative to, not a bare adverbial modifier.

Mandarin xiànzài, Cantonese jin6 zoi6, Japanese げんざい (genzai), Korean 현재, and Vietnamese hiện tại are all standard, fully compositional readings across the sphere, matching 現's and 在's own stored citations exactly — a clean cognate set attested identically in every source language, with no notable sense-divergence.

`kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 현재 coincides exactly with Sino-Korean 현재, since both 現 and 在 independently carry matching readings on their own character pages. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⼶ㄋㄐㄚㄧ unique among both words and characters).
