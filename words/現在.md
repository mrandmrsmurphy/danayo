## Notes
- [現 (char)](../characters/現%20(char).md) "present, appear" + [在 (char)](../characters/在%20(char).md) "exist, be at" — literally "presently existing," the current moment.

`pos: 名詞`: 現在 is grammatically a noun ("the present, the current time"), not an adverb — despite English "now" often functioning adverbially, Dan'a'yo treats this word the same way Chinese does, as a time-noun that a sentence locates events relative to, not a bare adverbial modifier.

Mandarin xiànzài, Cantonese jin6 zoi6, Japanese げんざい (genzai), Korean 현재 and Vietnamese hiện tại are all standard, fully compositional readings across the sphere, matching 現's and 在's own stored citations — a clean cognate set attested identically in every source language, with no notable sense-divergence. The term also has a Buddhist pedigree as the middle member of the three times, 過去 / 現在 / 未来 (past, present, future), which helped fix it as the ordinary word for "the present." Japanese additionally uses 現在 as "as of" in dates and reports ("as of today").

The nearby, more immediate "right before one's eyes" is [[目前]]; the bound adjective is [[現]].
