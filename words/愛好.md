## Notes
- [愛](../characters/愛%20(char).md) "love" + [好](../characters/好%20(char).md) "good, like" — no stand-in relationship applies (愛's own stand-in is itself; 好's own is itself).

愛好 means to like — having a fondness or hobby-level interest in something.

Mandarin àihào, Cantonese oi3 hou3, Japanese あいこう, Korean 애호, and Vietnamese ái hiếu are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese`, reformatted `characters`/`aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄧㄏㄚㄨ unique across both words and characters).
