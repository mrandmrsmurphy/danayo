## Notes
- [君](../characters/君%20(char).md) + [士](../characters/士%20(char).md) + [坦](../characters/坦.md) + [丁](../characters/丁%20(char).md) + [堡](../characters/堡.md) — phonetic transcription of *Constantinople*. No stand-in relationship applies (君, 士, 丁 are each their own stand-in; 坦's own is [[平坦]]; 堡's is [[堡塁]]).
- Capital of the Byzantine Empire and seat of the Ecumenical Patriarchate. Site of the First Council of Constantinople (381 AD). Now Istanbul.

Filled blank `vietnamese: Quân Sĩ Thản Đinh Bảo` (compositional Sino-Vietnamese, verified — though modern everyday Vietnamese typically uses the direct Latin/international loan "Constantinopolis" instead). Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, and Korean are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 unique across both words and characters).
