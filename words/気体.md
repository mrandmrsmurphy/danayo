## Notes
- [気 (char)](../characters/気%20(char).md) "air, feeling" + [体](../characters/体.md) "body" — gas.

気体 is a gas as a state of matter: a substance with neither fixed shape nor fixed volume, filling whatever container holds it. It is the middle one of the three ordinary states, between [[固体]] (solid) and [[液体]] (liquid). Particular gases are named by element ([[養素]], oxygen; [[軽素]], hydrogen) or by compound. Mandarin qìtǐ, Cantonese hei3 tai2, Japanese きたい and Korean 기체 are the standard readings.
