## Notes
- [隅 (char)](../characters/隅%20(char).md) "nook, corner, side" — this word is 隅's own stand-in.

隅 means a nook, corner, or side — an out-of-the-way spot, whether a physical corner of a room or a remote corner of a territory. Mandarin 隅 (yú), Cantonese 隅 (jyu4), Japanese 隅 (すみ natively, ぐう on'yomi in compounds like 一隅 ichigū "a single corner"), Korean 우 (u), and Sino-Vietnamese ngung/ngong all preserve this meaning, though it survives mainly bound in compounds (四隅, "the four corners") rather than as a fully free-standing everyday word in most of these languages.

This word shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading ('uo/웃/ㄨㄛ) with [[又]] ("or again") — the reciprocal half of that callout, already noted on 又's own page as awaiting this word's turn in the sweep.
