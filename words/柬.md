## Notes
- [柬](../characters/柬%20(char).md) "letter, invitation" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

柬 means a letter or invitation — a formal written communication, as in 請柬 (an invitation card).

Mandarin jiǎn, Cantonese gaan2, Japanese かん, Korean 간, and Vietnamese giản are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄍ⼘ㄋ/gyan/갼) with the unrelated [[姜]] ("ginger").
