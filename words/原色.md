## Notes
- [原](../characters/原.md) "source, original" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — primary color.

原色 is a primary color: one of the basic colors from which others are mixed, such as red, yellow and blue in painting or red, green and blue in light. The set is [[三原色]]. In Japanese and Korean 原色 also means a pure, vivid, unmixed color, as in 원색적. 原 is "origin, source," the same sense as in [[原始]]. Mandarin yuánsè, Japanese げんしょく and Korean 원색 are the standard readings. The theory words that go with it are [[補色]] (complementary color) and, in the sciences, [[色相]] (hue).
