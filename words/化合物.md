## Notes
- [化](../characters/化.md) "-ification, change" + [合 (char)](../characters/合%20(char).md) "join, fit together" + [物 (char)](../characters/物%20(char).md) "thing" — chemical compound.

化合物 is a chemical compound: a substance made of two or more elements joined in fixed proportions, such as water, which is written $軽_二養$ in Dan'a'yo's formula notation (two [[軽素]] and one [[養素]]). 化合 is "to combine chemically" and 物 is "thing." It contrasts with [[混合物]], a mixture whose parts are not chemically bonded, and with a pure element ([[元素]]). Mandarin huàhéwù, Cantonese faa3 hap6 mat6, Japanese かごうぶつ and Korean 화합물 are the standard readings.
