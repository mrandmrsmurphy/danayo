## Notes
- [遇](../characters/遇%20(char).md) "meet, encounter" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

遇 means to meet, come across, or encounter — as in 遭遇 ("encounter, plight") and 待遇 ("treatment, remuneration").

Mandarin yù, Cantonese jyu6, Japanese ぐう, Korean 우, and Vietnamese ngộ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Part of a four-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read 'u/우/ㄨ) with [[于]] ("to," dative particle), [[雨]] ("rain"), and [[愚]] ("foolish").
