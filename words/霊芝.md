## Notes
- [霊](../characters/霊.md) "spirit, ghost" + [芝](../characters/芝.md) "sesame, grass" — no `#cranberry`: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here (霊's is [[霊魂]], 芝's is the special "name-only character" marker 名専字, unrelated to this compound).

霊芝/灵芝 (língzhī) is the standard Mandarin name for lingzhi, the medicinal bracket fungus *Ganoderma lucidum*, and Cantonese 靈芝 (ling4zi1) likewise. Japanese れいし (reishi) and Korean 영지 (yeongji) are the standard names for the same mushroom in their respective traditional-medicine traditions. Sino-Vietnamese linh chi is the term itself, most often expanded in everyday Vietnamese with the native classifier "nấm" ("mushroom") to nấm linh chi.
