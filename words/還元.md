## Notes
- [還](../characters/還.md) "return" + [元](../characters/元.md) "element, origin" — reduction (chemistry).

還元 is chemical reduction: gaining electrons, or removing oxygen from a compound, as when iron ore is reduced to metallic iron in a furnace ([[鉄]], [[鉱石]]). It is the opposite of oxidation. The characters mean "to return to the origin," and the word has the same broader senses in Japanese (to restore, to give back, as in profit-sharing). Mandarin huányuán (also written 還原), Cantonese waan4 jyun4, Japanese かんげん and Korean 환원 are the standard readings.
