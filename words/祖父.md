## Notes
- [祖](../characters/祖.md) "ancestor" + [父](../characters/父.md) "father" — literally "ancestor-father," i.e. grandfather.

祖父 is the formal, written term for one's paternal grandfather, contrasted with 外祖父 (maternal grandfather). In colloquial Mandarin speech, the suppletive term 爺爺 (yéye) is far more commonly used than 祖父 itself, which survives mainly in formal or written registers.

Mandarin zǔfù, Cantonese zou2 fu6, Japanese そふ (sofu), and Korean 조부 (jobu) are all fully compositional. Vietnamese ông nội is the real, standard native term specifically for the paternal grandfather (as opposed to ông ngoại, the maternal grandfather), rather than a Sino-Vietnamese compound.
