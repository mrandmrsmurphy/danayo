## Notes
- [退 (char)](../characters/退%20(char).md) "to recede (from)" + [職](../characters/職.md) "duty, post" — literally "to recede from one's post."

退職 means to retire or resign from a position — a general term covering both voluntary resignation and retirement, distinguished in Japanese from the narrower 退社 (leaving a specific company) and 退勤 (clocking out for the day), and from 辞職 (specifically voluntary resignation, without the retirement sense).

Mandarin tuìzhí, Cantonese teoi3 zik1, Korean 퇴직 (toejik), and Japanese たいしょく (taishoku) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real everyday term in its language. Vietnamese thoái chức is likewise compositional, from 退's thoái (one of two attested readings) and 職's own chức, and is directly attested as a real term for leaving a post — though everyday Vietnamese more often uses từ chức ("resign") or hồi hưu/nghỉ hưu ("retire") depending on the specific sense. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 퇴직 matches Sino-Korean 퇴직 exactly. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄊㄛㄧㄐㄧㄎ unique among both words and characters).
