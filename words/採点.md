## Notes
- [採](../characters/採.md) "gather" + [点 (char)](../characters/点%20(char).md) "point"

採点 is to grade or mark: to give a score to work that has been done. Wiktionary glosses Japanese さいてん as "marking, scoring; to mark, to score". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings; Chinese speakers say 評分. Korean 채점 matches.

It is done by the teacher after a [試験](試験.md), and the result is the score ([得点](得点.md)) and then the [成績](成績.md), the grade.
