## Notes
- [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "large; great" + [蛤](../characters/蛤.md) "clam" — this word is the stand-in for [蛤](../characters/蛤.md), which cannot appear independently.

大蛤 means clam. The character 蛤 alone means clam, but is too bounded to stand by itself in Dan'a'yo, so this compound serves as its viable form.
