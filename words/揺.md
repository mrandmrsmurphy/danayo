## Notes
- [揺](../characters/揺%20(char).md) "swing, shake, rock" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [揺](../characters/揺%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

揺 means to swing, shake, or rock.

Mandarin yáo, Cantonese jiu4, Japanese よう, Korean 요, and Vietnamese dao are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bugs: `羅馬字` was missing the leading glottal marker (you→'you) and literal `vietnamese: null`/`korean: "null"`. Homophone of [[窯]] "kiln" (both 注音 ⼄ㄨ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words; checked nine other candidates sharing this reading (夭/姚/曜/腰/耀/要/窈/謡/遥) and confirmed none has an independent word at this exact reading (姚/窈 are name-only, the rest bound to longer stand-in compounds).
