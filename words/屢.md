## Notes
- [屢 (char)](../characters/屢%20(char).md) "frequently; often; repeatedly" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [屢 (char)](../characters/屢%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

屢 is a literary frequency adverb — "repeatedly, again and again, time after time" (屢次 "many times," 屢屢 "repeatedly").

Japanese る (on'yomi; the native しばしば, shibashiba, is the everyday word instead), Korean 루, and Vietnamese lũ are all standard readings. No homophones among other words (注音 ㄌㄨ; the unrelated character [[婁]] shares the same reading, but its own stand-in word doesn't collide with 屢's).
