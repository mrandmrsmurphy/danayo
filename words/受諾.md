## Notes
- [受](../characters/受.md) "to receive; accept" + [諾 (char)](../characters/諾%20(char).md) "to consent"

受諾 is "to accept," used for formally agreeing to a proposal, demand or declaration. According to Wiktionary, Japanese じゅだく is a noun "acceptance" and a suru-verb "to accept" (the Diet record it quotes is "the Potsdam Declaration was accepted"), and Korean 수락 is "acceptance." Wiktionary has no Chinese or Vietnamese section for 受諾; `mandarin` shòunuò, `cantonese` sau6 nok6 and `vietnamese` thụ nặc are compositional readings of the two characters, not independently attested.

The word is used in the 1945 surrender broadcast ([玉音放送](../translation/玉音放送.md)) for the acceptance of the joint declaration. Related: [諾](諾.md) "consent."
