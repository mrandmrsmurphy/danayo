## Notes
- [禁](../characters/禁.md) "prohibit, forbid" + [忌](../characters/忌.md) "taboo, avoid" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (禁's is [[禁止]], 忌's is [[忌諱]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 禁 is individually `kwin: false`.

禁忌 names a taboo — something socially, religiously, or culturally forbidden, typically avoided out of fear of misfortune or offense rather than by written law (unlike the more formal, administrative sense of [[禁制]]). In medicine specifically, it also means "contraindication," a treatment or substance that must be avoided for a given patient.

Mandarin jìnjì, Cantonese gam3 gei6, Japanese きんき (kinki), and Korean 금기 (geumgi) are all standard, fully compositional readings, each functioning as the ordinary everyday word for "taboo" in its language. Vietnamese cấm kỵ is likewise compositional and standard, combining cấm ("forbid") with kỵ ("taboo, to avoid").
