## Notes
- [恭](../characters/恭.md) "respectful" + [敬](../characters/敬.md) "respect" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [恭](../characters/恭.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (敬's own stand-in is [[尊敬]]).

恭敬 means respectful or deferential — a near-synonymous doubling of two characters both meaning "respect."

Mandarin gōngjìng, Cantonese gung1 ging3, Korean 공경, and Vietnamese cung kính are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `japanese` had been contaminated with くぎょう, the reading of the unrelated word 公卿 ("court noble") — corrected to compositional きょうけい. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼄ㄫㄍ⼶ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
