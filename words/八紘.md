## Notes
- [八 (char)](../characters/八%20(char).md) "eight" + [紘](../characters/紘.md) "cord, cosmic boundary" — "the eight cords," a classical cosmological term for the outermost bounds of the world (the eight great cords thought to bind the corners/directions of the cosmos), most familiar today from the WWII-era Japanese imperial slogan 八紘一宇 ("the eight corners of the world under one roof"; cited on [[紘]]'s own Words list, but 八紘一宇 has no dedicated vault page of its own yet). Mandarin bāhóng, Cantonese baat3 wang4, Japanese はっこう, Korean 팔굉, and Vietnamese bát hoành are all directly attested/compositional standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 받훵 diverges from Sino-Korean 팔굉 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄊㄏ⼔ㄫ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 紘's own `stand_in` is this exact word (八紘), so 紘 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 八紘.

One of the earliest occurrences of "八纮" (八紘) is in _Liezi_ (《列子》), in the "Tang Wen / 汤问" chapter. The relevant passage:

> 汤又问：“物有巨细乎？ 有修短乎？ 有同异乎？”  
> 革曰：“渤海之东不知几亿万里，有大壑焉，实惟无底之谷，其下无底，名曰归墟。**八纮 九野之水**，天汉之流，莫不注之，而无增无减焉。”
