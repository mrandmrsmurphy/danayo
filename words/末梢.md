## Notes
- [末](../characters/末.md) "end" + [梢](../characters/梢.md) "tiptop, periphery" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [梢](../characters/梢.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 末's own stand-in is [[末端]].

末梢 means tiptop, periphery, or (figuratively) trivia.

Mandarin mòshāo, Cantonese mut6 saau1, Japanese まっしょう, and Korean 말초 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field (mạt tiêu). No homophones (注音 ㄇㄚㄊㄙ⼘ㄨ unique across both words and characters).
