## Notes
- [終](../characters/終.md) "end" + [身](../characters/身.md) "body" + [刑](../characters/刑.md) "punishment; penalty"

終身刑 is a life sentence: imprisonment for the rest of the offender's life. Wiktionary glosses Japanese しゅうしんけい as "life sentence; life imprisonment" and lists Korean 종신형 with the same sense. Wiktionary has no Chinese entry, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. The word is [終身](終身.md), "lifetime", with 刑 "punishment" added.

It sits between [懲役](懲役.md), a fixed term, and [死刑](死刑.md), the death penalty. Like any sentence it can be reduced by [減刑](減刑.md).
