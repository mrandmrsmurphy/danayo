## Notes
- [勤](../characters/勤.md) "diligent; work" + [務](../characters/務.md) "duty, affairs" — duty.

勤務 is duty or work at a post: the working of one's assigned hours for an employer, organization or the state, and as a verb "to work for, to be on duty." It is "diligent-affairs." Japanese きんむ and Korean 근무 are the ordinary workplace word (Japanese 勤務する, "to work for an employer"); Mandarin qínwù and Cantonese kan4 mou6 are the same compound, with the sense "duties, service" and a military sense of an orderly or odd-jobber. Compare [[服務]] (to serve, work for) and [[出勤]] (to go to work). It is the base of [[超過勤務]] (overtime).
