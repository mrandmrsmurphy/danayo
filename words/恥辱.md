## Notes
- [恥](../characters/恥.md) "shame" + [辱](../characters/辱.md) "disgrace" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [恥](../characters/恥.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (辱's own stand-in is [[羞辱]]).

恥辱 means disgrace or shame — a near-synonymous doubling of two characters both meaning "shame."

Mandarin chǐrǔ, Cantonese ci2 juk6, Japanese ちじょく, Korean 치욕, and Vietnamese sỉ nhục are all standard, fully compositional readings. Trimmed malformed `mandarin` (stray untoned duplicate). Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄋㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
