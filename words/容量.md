## Notes
- [容 (char)](../characters/容%20(char).md) "look; appearance; form; figure" + [量](../characters/量.md) "measure; weight; quantity" — the measurable amount a thing can hold.

容量 pairs 容's "to hold, to contain" sense (as in [[容器]]) with 量's general "measured quantity," yielding the standard modern word for capacity or volume — storage capacity, container volume, or (in Japanese and increasingly elsewhere) digital data capacity alike. It is closely related to but distinct from [[産量]] ("output, yield," built on the unrelated character 産 "to produce"), a superficially similar-looking compound this page's frontmatter had previously (incorrectly) listed as an `aliases` variant — 産量 shares no character, reading, or meaning with 容量 beyond the coincidental final 量, and the erroneous entry (along with its own 产量/產量 simplified/traditional pair, copy-pasted wholesale from 産量's own genuine alias list) has been removed here.

Japanese ようりょう and Korean 용량 are both plain, everyday, non-drifted readings. Vietnamese dung lượng is likewise the standard modern term (Wiktionary-confirmed), extremely common in the specific sense of storage/battery capacity (dung lượng pin "battery capacity," dung lượng ổ đĩa "disk capacity"). No HSK level is confirmed via Wiktionary for the compound itself, though it is independently well-attested as HSK-4 vocabulary in standard teaching materials; kept as `"4"` on that basis rather than removed outright. No homophones (注音 ⼄ㄫㄌ⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
