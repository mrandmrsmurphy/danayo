## Notes
- [統](../characters/統.md) "command, govern, unite" + [治](../characters/治.md) "govern, rule, administer" — 治's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 統's own is [[統率]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 統治 legitimizes 治 alone. `kwin: true` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: true`.

統治 means to govern or rule.

Mandarin tǒngzhì, Cantonese tung2 zi6, Japanese とうち (tōchi), and Korean 통치 (tongchi) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 統治 is itself a common attested word for "govern, rule" in all four languages. Vietnamese thống trị is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
