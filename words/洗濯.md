## Notes
- [洗 (char)](../characters/洗%20(char).md) "wash, rinse" + [濯](../characters/濯.md) "launder, wash, rinse" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 濯 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (洗's own stand-in is itself, so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

洗濯 means to launder, wash, rinse.

Mandarin xǐzhuó, Cantonese sai2 zok6, and Japanese せんたく are all standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 세탁 is the real, universal everyday Korean word for "laundry" — using the common alternate reading 세 for 洗 rather than the vault's stored 선, the same kind of real-word-over-strict-citation divergence documented elsewhere for extremely common everyday terms. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with the compositional tẩy trạc (洗's own tẩy + 濯's own trạc); real-world Vietnamese instead uses the native term giặt. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases: []`. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄋㄉㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
