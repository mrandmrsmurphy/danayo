## Notes
- [天](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven, sky" + [称](../characters/称.md) "scale, weigh" — "heaven scale," a balance. Their own `stand_in` values: 天's is itself; 称's is [[名称]]; none points here, so 天称 is an independent compound.

天称 is a scale or balance: the device that weighs by comparing two pans or by a beam. Mandarin tiānchèng (天秤, also tiānpíng), Cantonese tin1 cing3 and Japanese てんびん all use it, and Korean 천칭 is the same compound; it is also the name of the constellation Libra. The character 称 has the aliases 秤 and 稱, so the Chinese-style spelling 天秤 is accepted. Compare [[重量]] (weight) and [[計量]] (to measure).
