## Notes
- [古](../characters/古.md) "ancient" + [典](../characters/典.md) "classic, standard" — no stand-in relationship applies (古's own stand-in is [[古代]]; 典's is [[事典]]).

古典 means classical — pertaining to a traditional, canonical body of work or style.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` had 典's syllable using a divergent form (dan/전/ㄐㄝㄋ) rather than its own authoritative reading (den/던/ㄉㄝㄋ, confirmed against every other 典-compound in the vault — [[事典]], [[典雅]], [[字典]], [[恩典]], [[瑞典]], [[祭典]], [[詞典]] — all consistently use den/던) — corrected. This also flips `kwin` from true to false, since Dan'a'yo 고던 now correctly diverges from Sino-Korean 고전 in the second syllable (matching the same divergence documented on [[典雅]]). Japanese こてん, Korean 고전, and Vietnamese cổ điển are all standard readings. Removed the redundant `品詞` field. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄉㄝㄋ unique across both words and characters).
