## Notes
- [硬](../characters/硬%20(char).md) "hard" + [盤](../characters/盤%20(char).md) "plate, disk" — "hard disk." Their own `stand_in` values: 硬's is itself; 盤's is itself; none points here, so 硬盤 is an independent compound.

硬盤 is a hard drive: the rigid storage disk of a computer. Mandarin yìngpán and Cantonese ngaang6 pun4 are the ordinary Chinese words (Taiwan and Hong Kong often write 硬碟). Japanese says ハードディスク and Korean 하드디스크, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[硬]] (hard) and [[磁盤]] (disk).
