## Notes
- [絶 (char)](../characters/絶%20(char).md) "cut off" + [滅 (char)](../characters/滅%20(char).md) "wipe out"

絶滅 is extinction: to be wiped out until nothing is left, as of a species. Wiktionary glosses Japanese ぜつめつ as "extinction; annihilation; elimination; to go extinct". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 절멸 matches.

It is final in a way [滅亡](滅亡.md) need not be, because it leaves no survivors. It is used chiefly of animals and plants, and of an idea or a custom that has died out entirely.
