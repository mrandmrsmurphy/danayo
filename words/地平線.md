## Notes
- [地](../characters/地%20(char).md) "ground" + [平](../characters/平.md) "level" + [線](../characters/線.md) "line" — no stand-in relationship applies (地 is its own stand-in; 平's own is [[水平]]; 線's is [[直線]]).

地平線 means the horizon.

Japanese ちへいせん, Korean 지평선, and Cantonese/Mandarin are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese: địa bình tuyến` (compositional; not independently attested as a fixed term — everyday Vietnamese instead uses the native "đường chân trời" or "chân trời"). No homophones (注音 ㄉㄧㄜㄅ⼶ㄫㄙ⼶ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
