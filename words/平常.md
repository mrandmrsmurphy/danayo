## Notes
- [平](../characters/平.md) "even, level" + [常](../characters/常.md) "constant, regular" — literally "level and constant," ordinary/usual.

Mandarin píngcháng, Cantonese ping4 soeng4, Japanese へいじょう (heijō), Korean 평상, and Vietnamese bình thường are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 平's and 常's own stored citations exactly.
