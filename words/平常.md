## Notes
- [平](../characters/平.md) "even, level" + [常](../characters/常.md) "constant, regular" — literally "level and constant," ordinary/usual.

Mandarin píngcháng, Cantonese ping4 soeng4, Japanese へいじょう (heijō), Korean 평상, and Vietnamese bình thường are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 平's and 常's own stored citations exactly. **Found and fixed a real bug while verifying this**: 常's own character-level `korean`/`korean_native` fields were swapped — `korean` held 항상 (the real native Korean adverb "always"), while the correct single-syllable Sino-Korean reading 상 sat mislabeled under `korean_native`. Confirmed against every other 常-compound in this corpus (日常 → 일상, 常識 → 상식, 非常 → 비상, all consistently using 상), and corrected on 常's own page.

`kwin` is false: both 平 and 常 individually diverge from Korean on their own character pages, so the AND-rule fails on both counts. No homophones (注音 ㄅ⼶ㄫㄙ⼘ㄫ unique among both words and characters).
