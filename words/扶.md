## Notes
- [扶](../characters/扶%20(char).md) "hold on to for support" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [扶](../characters/扶%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

扶 means to hold on to for support.

Mandarin fú, Cantonese fu4, Japanese ふ, Korean 부, and Vietnamese phù are all standard, fully compositional readings. Homophone of [[附]] "affix; adhere" (both 注音 ㄅㄨ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words.
