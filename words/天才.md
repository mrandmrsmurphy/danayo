## Notes
- [天](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven" + [才](../characters/才.md) "talent" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [才](../characters/才.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (天's own stand-in is itself).

天才 means talented, gifted, or a genius — literally "heaven's talent."

Japanese てんさい, Korean 천재, Mandarin tiāncái, and Cantonese tin1 coi4 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese thiên tài is a standard reading. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄝㄋㄐㄚㄧ unique across both words and characters).
