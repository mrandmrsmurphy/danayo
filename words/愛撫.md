## Notes
- [愛 (char)](../characters/愛%20(char).md) "love" + [撫](../characters/撫.md) "stroke; pat"

愛撫 is a caress: to stroke or touch with tenderness. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin àifǔ as "to caress; to show tender care for" and Japanese あいぶ as "caress, pet". Korean 애무 and Cantonese oi3 fu2 match.

It is a touch of love, where [抱擁](抱擁.md) is the embrace and [接吻](接吻.md) is the kiss. The plain act of touching is [接触](接触.md).
