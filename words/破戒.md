## Notes
- [破 (char)](../characters/破%20(char).md) "rend, break" + [戒](../characters/戒.md) "warn, caution, admonish" — literally "to break a precept."

破戒 is the standard Buddhist term for breaking one of the precepts (戒) a monk, nun, or lay practitioner has vowed to observe — most famously the Five Precepts (五戒) against killing, stealing, sexual misconduct, lying, and intoxication. By extension it is used more loosely for breaking any solemn vow or rule of conduct.

Mandarin pòjiè, Cantonese po3 gaai3, Japanese はかい (hakai), Korean 파계 (pagye), and Vietnamese phá giới are all fully compositional and retain this same specifically religious/monastic register across the sphere, most often encountered in the context of Buddhist monastic discipline.
