## Notes
- [刷 (char)](../characters/刷%20(char).md) "brush; scrub"

刷 combines 刀/刂 ("knife") with 㕞 (depicting a hand scraping something clean). It means "to brush, scrub, wipe clean" (刷牙, "to brush one's teeth"), and by extension "to print" (from brushing ink onto a woodblock or plate): 印刷 (yìnshuā, "printing"), 刷新 (shuāxīn, "to refresh, update," literally "to brush anew").

Mandarin shuā, Cantonese caat3, and Japanese さつ (satsu, on'yomi, fully productive: 印刷 いんさつ "printing") alongside native す(る) (su(ru), "to print," the everyday verb in this specific printing sense) all preserve the sense. Korean 쇄 (swae) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese loát is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in ấn loát (印刷, "printing"); the character page's other five stored candidates (loét, loạt, loẹt, nhoét, soát) are unrelated and look like corpus noise.
