## Notes
- [亙 (char)](../characters/亙%20(char).md) "across; athwart"

亙 is 指事: the crescent moon ([[月]]) spanning between two horizontal lines marking its poles of waxing and waning — "extending across, spanning." It survives mainly in the classical adverbial sense "throughout, across (time or space)," as in 亙古 (gèngǔ, "since time immemorial," literally "spanning [all of] antiquity") — a sense frequently conflated in practice with the visually similar character [[亘]], with which it shares no Dan'a'yo reading in this vault despite the historical overlap.

Japanese has on'yomi こう (kō) alongside native わた(る) (wata(ru), "to span, extend over" — the same reading found on [[亘]]'s own character page, reflecting their shared semantic field even where Dan'a'yo keeps them phonologically distinct). Korean 긍 (geung) is confined to classical/literary use. Vietnamese cắng is the Hán Việt reading, attested directly in cắng cổ (亙古, "throughout ancient times to now") — the character page's other four stored candidates (cẳng, cứng, gắng, hẵng) are all real Vietnamese words but unrelated in meaning, the same kind of contamination pattern found on [[亘]] itself last iteration.

This word shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading (gung/궁/ㄍㄨㄫ) with another word still awaiting its own turn in this sweep, [[弓]] ("bow") — the reciprocal half of this callout will be completed when it comes up.
