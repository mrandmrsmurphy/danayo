## Notes
- [放](../characters/放.md) "release" + [射](../characters/射.md) "shoot, eject" + [性](../characters/性.md) "nature, quality" — radioactivity.

放射性 is radioactivity: the property of an unstable atomic nucleus of emitting radiation as it decays. 放 "release" and 射 "shoot, emit" are the two characters that Dan'a'yo's element names also use separately: [[放素]] is radium and [[射素]] is radon, both radioactive. 性 "nature, quality" is the same suffix as in [[磁性]]. Mandarin fàngshèxìng, Cantonese fong3 se6 sing3, Japanese ほうしゃせい and Korean 방사성 are the standard readings.
