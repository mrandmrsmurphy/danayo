## Notes
- [偶](../characters/偶.md) "occasional, chance" + [爾 (char)](../characters/爾%20(char).md) (adverbial suffix, "-ly") — "occasionally, by chance, once in a while" (偶爾相遇, "to happen to meet by chance"). `vietnamese: ngẫu nhĩ` is directly attested, a literary/somewhat archaic register — modern everyday Vietnamese favors the more common ngẫu nhiên instead. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 옷너 diverges entirely from Sino-Korean 우이.
