## Etymology

## Notes
"Occasionally, by chance, once in a while" (偶爾相遇, "to happen to meet by chance"). `vietnamese: ngẫu nhĩ` is directly attested (confirmed via web search), a literary/somewhat archaic register — modern everyday Vietnamese favors the more common ngẫu nhiên instead. Filled the blank `pos: 副用名詞`. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 옷너 diverges entirely from Sino-Korean 우이. No homophones (注音 ㄛㄨㄋㄝ unique among both words and characters).
