## Notes
- [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big; great" + [統](../characters/統.md) "to command" + [領](../characters/領.md) "to lead; territory"

大統領 is "president," the head of state of a republic. According to Wiktionary, Japanese だいとうりょう and Korean 대통령 are the ordinary word for a president, and Chinese 大統領 (Dàtǒnglǐng) is attested as an obsolete word for the president of a country, now with a slang revival; Wiktionary has no Vietnamese or Cantonese section, so `cantonese` daai6 tung2 ling5 and `vietnamese` đại thống lĩnh are compositional readings (everyday Vietnamese for "president" is tổng thống, from memory, not re-checked). Dan'a'yo takes it as the word for the president of a republic, used for the presidents of Korea on [Korea](../lookup/Korean/Korea.md). It is the compound of [総統](総統.md) "president" in the vault's usage with 大 "great," and 統領 "commander" is a separate Chinese word.
