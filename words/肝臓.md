## Notes
- [肝](../characters/肝.md) "liver" + [臓](../characters/臓.md) "organ, entrails" — stand-in for [[肝]], which is never used on its own

Korean 간장 (gan-jang) is a homograph: it is both the everyday word for "soy sauce" (native 간 + Sino 醬) and the Sino-Korean medical term for "liver" (from 肝臓). The soy-sauce sense is the far more frequent one in everyday speech (from memory, not re-checked against Wiktionary).

Mandarin gānzàng, Cantonese gon1 zong6, Japanese かんぞう (kanzō), and Vietnamese gan (matching one of `characters/肝.md`'s own attested Vietnamese readings, alongside can and gang) round out the standard clinical/anatomical term for the organ across the sphere.

No other word shares the 注音 ㄍㄚㄋㄐㄚㄫ (checked 2026-10-13).
