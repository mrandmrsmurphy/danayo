## Notes
- [神 (char)](../characters/神%20(char).md) "god, spirit" + [父](../characters/父.md) "father" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (神's is [[神]] itself, 父's is [[父親]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: true` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: true`.

The Eastern Orthodox (and also Catholic) title for a priest, addressed as "Father" — a calque of the honorific rather than a description of the priest's actual family role. Distinct from [[司鈬]] (Catholic ordained priest as an office/rank) and [[牧師]] (Protestant pastor), which name the clerical role itself rather than the address term.

Mandarin shénfù, Cantonese san4 fu6, Japanese しんぷ (shinpu), and Korean 신부 (sinbu) are all standard, fully compositional readings used in their respective Christian communities. Vietnamese thần phụ is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form; colloquial Vietnamese address more often uses the native term cha ("father") directly, or linh mục for the ordained office.
