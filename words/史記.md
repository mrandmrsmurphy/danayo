## Notes
- [史](../characters/史.md) "history" + [記](../characters/記.md) "record" — no stand-in relationship applies (史's own stand-in is [[歴史]]; 記's is [[記憶]]).

史記 (Shǐjì), literally "Records of the Historian," is the seminal Chinese historical text compiled by Sima Qian in the Han dynasty, covering over two millennia of Chinese history.

Japanese しき, Korean 사기, and Vietnamese sử ký are all standard, fully compositional readings.
