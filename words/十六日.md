## Notes
- [十](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" + [六](../characters/六%20(char).md) "six" + [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — no stand-in relationship applies (all three characters are independent Dan'a'yo entries, each its own stand-in).

十六日 names the 16th day of the month.

**Real bugs found and fixed**: `korean` used the South Korean 두음법칙-shifted form (십육일) instead of the required North Korean/문화어 form — corrected to 십륙일 (matching [[十六]]'s own fix). `注音` had a single-letter typo in the last syllable (ㄋㄝㄊ instead of ㄋㄧㄊ, 日's own reading) — corrected. Filled blank `vietnamese: thập lục nhật`. Japanese じゅうろくにち is a standard reading. Fixed `characters` citation, malformed `japanese` list, redundant `品詞`. No homophones.
