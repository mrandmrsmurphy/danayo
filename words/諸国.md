## Notes
- [諸 (char)](../characters/諸%20(char).md) "various" + [国](../characters/国.md) "nation" — "various countries; the nations." 諸 is legitimized by [[諸]] itself and 国 by [[国家]]; neither stand-in points here, so this is not a legitimizer word.

Wiktionary: Japanese 諸国 (しょこく) "various countries; the various countries of"; Korean 제국 is the hanja form of 諸國, "various countries"; no Chinese or Vietnamese entry. Used in `文法 - 03文字法`. Compare [[諸語]], [[他国]].
