## Notes
- [哀](../characters/哀.md) "sorrow" + [愁](../characters/愁.md) "grief; worry"

哀愁 is pathos: a gentle, lingering sorrow, with a sweetness of longing in it. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin āichóu as "sorrow; sadness; distress; grief" and Japanese あいしゅう as "pathos; sorrow". Korean 애수 and Cantonese oi1 sau4 match.

It is the feeling of an autumn evening, softer than [悲痛](悲痛.md), bitter grief, and close to [郷愁](郷愁.md), homesickness. Wistful longing is the [懐愁](懐愁.md).
