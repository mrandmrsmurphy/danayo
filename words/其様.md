## Notes
- [其 (char)](../characters/其%20(char).md) "that; medial demonstrative; its/their (classical)" + [様 (char)](../characters/様%20(char).md) "manner; way; style; appearance"

其様 is the medial manner correlative — "that way; in that manner; as you/we were just discussing." Like all 其-column entries, it picks up a referent from the shared discourse context or the addressee's frame: 其様 refers to the method just demonstrated, the approach already established in conversation, or a manner both speaker and listener understand to be "that way." In conversational use: 其様にすれば良い ("if you do it that way, it'll be fine"); 其様に考えたことはなかった ("I'd never thought of it that way").

Japanese そのように (sono yō ni, "in that way — medial") or そうやって (sō yatte, "doing it that way") are the precise medial manner forms. Korean 그렇게 (geureoke, "like that — medial") is the everyday form, the middle member of the 이렇게/그렇게/저렇게 (this way/that way/that way-distal) set. Mandarin 那樣 (nàyàng, "like that, in that way") covers both medial and distal manner in Chinese, since Mandarin has a binary rather than three-way deictic system. Vietnamese như thế (like that) or như thế đó (in that way there — medial).

The 様 column is unique in the correlative table in that all seven entries (one for each of the table's seven base rows) are adverbial rather than nominal: where 事/物/人/名 correlatives substitute for noun phrases, and 処/時 correlatives substitute for place/time adverbials, 様 correlatives substitute for manner adverbials — answering "how?" rather than "what?", "who?", "where?", or "when?" In Japanese grammar these are pro-adverbs (そう, こう, ああ for manner); in Dan'a'yo they fill the same slot systematically across all seven rows.

The Grammar page places 其様 in the 様 ("manner") column of its correlative table, in the 其 ("that") row, between [此様](此様.md), "this way," and [彼様](彼様.md), "that way yonder," with [何様](何様.md) as the interrogative "how." The word is tagged `neologism`, because the paradigm is a Dan'a'yo construction, so its Mandarin, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese fields give the natural spoken equivalents (那樣, そのように, 그렇게, như thế đó), and no Cantonese property is given. `kwin` is false because 其's character page carries `kwin: false` (様's is true), and the word follows its characters. Dan'a'yo keeps the sense "that way, in that manner." The two example sentences above are illustrations in the vault's mixed style, not attested usage.
