## Notes
"Wolfberry, goji berry." The real Chinese plant name is 枸杞 (gǒuqǐ); this vault's own headword substitutes 句 for 枸 (both read jù/gōu-ish in different senses, but the compound as a whole keeps the gǒuqǐ reading via its alias). `vietnamese: cẩu kỷ` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese for the same plant (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted).

**Real content bug found and fixed**: the stored `注音` (ㄎㄧ) was missing its first syllable entirely — corrected to ㄍㄨㄎㄧ, matching the already-correct two-syllable 諺文/羅馬字 (구키/guki). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 구키 diverges entirely from Sino-Korean 구기. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄨㄎㄧ unique among both words and characters).
