## Notes
- [御](../characters/御.md) "imperial; to govern" + [璽](../characters/璽.md) "emperor's seal"

御璽 is "the imperial seal; the privy seal," the seal an emperor uses to authenticate a document. According to Wiktionary, Chinese 御璽 (yùxǐ, Cantonese jyu6 saai2), Japanese ぎょじ and Korean 어새 all mean "imperial seal; privy seal." `vietnamese` ngự tỉ is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading, not independently checked. Here 御 is the honorific "imperial," a sense that grew from "drive, govern." It is a near relative of [玉璽](玉璽.md) "royal seal; emperor's seal," which is built on 玉 "jade" instead of 御. Together with [御名](御名.md) it forms the formula 御名御璽 "Imperial Signature and Seal" that closes the 1945 surrender broadcast ([玉音放送](../translation/玉音放送.md)).
