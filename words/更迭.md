## Notes
- [更](../characters/更%20(char).md) "more; again" + [迭](../characters/迭.md) "alternate; change" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 迭 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

更迭 means to change, replace, or alternate.

Cantonese gang1 dit6, Japanese こうてつ, and Vietnamese canh điệt are standard, fully compositional readings. Mandarin gēngdié and Korean 경질 use real alternate readings of 更 (gēng/경, the "change" sense) not currently listed on the character's own stored citation (only gèng/갱, the "more" sense, is stored) — the same character-level citation gap already flagged on [[更新]]. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field and added `hsk_level: "無"` (matching 迭's own confirmed no-HSK-level status). No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄫㄉㄚㄊ unique across both words and characters).
