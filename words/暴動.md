## Notes
- [暴 (char)](../characters/暴%20(char).md) "violent" + [動 (char)](../characters/動%20(char).md) "move"

暴動 is a riot: a violent disturbance by a crowd. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin bàodòng as "to rebel; to revolt; to riot" and Japanese ぼうどう as "riot". Vietnamese bạo động is the same word, and Korean 폭동 and Cantonese bou6 dung6 match.

It is disorder without a program, made by a [暴徒](暴徒.md), where a [反乱](反乱.md) is a revolt against authority and a [革命](革命.md) aims to change the order.
