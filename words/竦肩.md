## Notes
- [竦 (char)](../characters/竦%20(char).md) "raise; stand up" + [肩 (char)](../characters/肩%20(char).md) "shoulder"

竦肩 is to shrug: to raise the shoulders briefly, in doubt, indifference or helplessness. Its alias 聳肩 is the form used in Mandarin writing, where 聳 is the alias of 竦. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin sǒngjiān as "to shrug one's shoulders". Cantonese sung2 gin1 matches, Korean 송견 is the reading of the characters, and no Japanese reading is attested.

It is a gesture of the [肩](肩.md), shoulder. The related 聳動 means both "to shrug" and "to stir up", so the shoulder form is the unambiguous one.
