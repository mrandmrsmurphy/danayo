## Notes
- [原](../characters/原.md) "plain, original" + [子](../characters/子.md) "son, child" + [量](../characters/量.md) "measure, quantity" — atomic weight.

原子量 is the atomic weight of an element: its average mass per atom, in atomic mass units, weighted by the natural abundance of its isotopes ([[同位元素]]). It is [[原子]] "atom" plus 量 "quantity." The related quantity for a compound is [[分子量]], and the whole-number count of nucleons in one isotope is [[質量数]]. Mandarin yuánzǐliàng, Cantonese jyun4 zi2 loeng6, Japanese げんしりょう and Korean 원자량 are the standard readings.
