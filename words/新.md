## Notes
- [新](../characters/新%20(char).md) "new" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 新 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

新 means new or fresh, one of the basic Swadesh-list concepts (#183). It is highly productive: 新聞 ("newspaper," literally "new hearing/news"), 新年 ("New Year"), 新鮮 ("fresh"), and 新型 ("new model/type").

Mandarin xīn, Cantonese san1, Korean 신 (sin), and Vietnamese tân are all standard, fully compositional readings.
