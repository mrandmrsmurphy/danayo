## Notes
- [沖](../characters/沖.md) "alluvial, rushing (water)" + [積](../characters/積.md) "accumulate, pile up" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 沖 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (積's own stand-in is [[蓄積]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

沖積 means alluvial (as in sediment deposited by flowing water).

Mandarin chōngjí and Japanese ちゅうせき are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 沖's and 積's own stored citations. Filled the blank `cantonese` (cung1 zik1), `korean` (충적, matching the already-given 諺文), and `vietnamese` (xung tích, using 沖's standard Sino reading xung — as in 沖突 xung đột — rather than the less common trong/trùng variants also listed on 沖's own citation) fields. `kwin: true` because 諺文 충적 matches Korean 충적 exactly. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄨㄫㄐㄝㄎ unique across both words and characters).
