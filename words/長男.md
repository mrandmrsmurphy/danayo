## Notes
- [長 (char)](../characters/長%20(char).md) "eldest, chief" + [男](../characters/男.md) "male" — eldest son.

長男 is the eldest son: the first-born male child of a family. It is "eldest-male." Mandarin zhǎngnán, Cantonese zoeng2 naam4, Japanese ちょうなん (with the series 次男, 三男), Korean 장남 and Vietnamese trưởng nam are all confirmed on Wiktionary. It differs from [[長子]], which can also mean the eldest child of either sex; 長男 is explicitly male. The eldest daughter is [[長女]]; the second son is [[次男]].
