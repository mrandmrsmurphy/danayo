## Notes
- [喜 (char)](../characters/喜%20(char).md) "like; love; enjoy"

喜 is 会意: [[壴]] ("drum") + [[口]] ("mouth") — music and voice together, "joy, delight." It means "to like, be fond of, enjoy" and, as a standalone quality, "joyous, happy" (歡喜, "joyful, delighted"; 喜歡, "to like").

Mandarin xǐ and Cantonese hei2 preserve the sense directly. Japanese キ/シ (KI/SHI, on'yomi, formal register: 喜劇, kigeki, "comedy") alongside native よろこ(ぶ) (yoroko(bu), "to be glad, rejoice," the everyday verb) cover the sense. Korean 희 (hui) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 기쁘다 (gippeuda, "to be glad"). Vietnamese hỷ is the prescriptively correct Hán Việt reading (hoan hỷ, "joyful"; song hỷ 囍, the doubled-happiness wedding symbol; hỷ sự, "a happy occasion"), with hỉ as an extremely common, fully accepted colloquial doublet of the same reading (hoan hỉ, báo hỉ) rather than a separate word. The character page's other stored candidates, hẻ and hởi, are unrelated Nôm readings — 喜 borrowed for its shape/sound to write different native Vietnamese words — and were excluded.
