## Notes
- [調](../characters/調.md) "to adjust" + [解 (char)](../characters/解%20(char).md) "to resolve" — to mediate or settle a dispute, especially in legal contexts (人民調解, "people's mediation"). Chiefly a Mandarin/Cantonese legal term; Japanese instead favors 調停 (chōtei) for this sense, so no on'yomi reading is given here.

The Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese properties are omitted from the frontmatter and not filled with character readings, because the Notes record that the word is a Mandarin and Cantonese legal term and that Japanese uses 調停 instead; an omitted property is the checklist's form for a value that does not exist. The Conflict page lists the word as "to mediate, settle a dispute," and Geography uses it in the sense of international bodies mediating disputes (調解争端). Dan'a'yo keeps the sense "to mediate, to settle a dispute," and the vault has no word file for 調停 or for 和解. Its first element is the character 調, whose stand-in is [調整](調整.md) "adjust," and its second is 解, which stands by itself as the word 解 "to resolve." The 人民調解 example and the Japanese 調停 remark are the page's existing account and have not been checked against a source.
