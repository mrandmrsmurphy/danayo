## Notes
- [一 (char)](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" + [定](../characters/定.md) "fixed, settled" — literally "fixed to one [state]," hence "fixed, regular, given, certain."

一定 functions as both adjective and adverb across the sphere: as an adjective it names something fixed, given, or specific ("a certain degree," "a set standard"), and as an adverb it expresses resolute certainty ("definitely, surely, without fail") — a real distinction Mandarin pedagogy draws explicitly against the near-synonym 肯定 (kěndìng, "to affirm, certainly"), where 一定 leans toward subjective determination/necessity and 肯定 toward objective confirmation of a fact. Vietnamese nhất định preserves exactly this same adjective/adverb split (nhất định sẽ đến, "will definitely come," showing resolve, versus a nhất định mức độ, "a certain/given degree").

Japanese いってい shows the same sokuon gemination already documented on [[一体]] (いち promoted to いっ- before an unvoiced-obstruent-initial reading, here てい), and carries the "fixed, set, uniform" sense (一定の basjun, "a fixed standard") without the resolute-certainty adverbial use that dominates in Mandarin and Vietnamese — Japanese instead uses きっと or 必ず for that emphatic-certainty sense. **Real `kwin` bug found and fixed at its root cause**: this word and `characters/一 (char).md` itself were both stamped `kwin: true`, but Dan'a'yo `諺文: 읻` (ID) and Sino-Korean `korean: 일` (IL) are different Unicode syllables — 一's own Dan'a'yo reading retains the Middle Chinese checked coda (-t → written -d/읻) that Sino-Korean has regularized to -l, the same pattern already documented on [[月]]/[[越]]/[[舌]]/[[骨]] earlier in this sweep. Corrected `kwin` to `false` on both this word and, more importantly, on `一 (char).md` itself — since 一 is one of the most common characters in the entire corpus, any other compound built on 一's first syllable that simply copied its stored `kwin` value (rather than independently checking the full compound's own 諺文 vs `korean`) may carry the same latent error and is worth re-checking in future iterations. No homophones (注音 ㄧㄊㄐㄝㄫ unique among both words and characters).
