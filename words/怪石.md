## Notes
- [怪](../characters/怪.md) "strange" + [石 (char)](../characters/石%20(char).md) "rock; stone"

Wiktionary has no entry for 怪石. Wikipedia (Eight Principles of Yong) records 怪石 (guàishí) as Li Puguang's metaphorical name for the dot stroke, in his Yuan-era 永字八法解 ("Eight Ways to Explain Yong"), where the modern name is 點 ([[点]]); the other principles also receive two-character metaphorical names there, such as 蟹爪, 虎牙, 犀角 and 金刀. They are listed with the modern names on the [[Stroke]] page. Its readings are compositional.
