## Notes
- [大](../characters/大%20(char).md) "great" + [象](../characters/象.md) "elephant" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [象](../characters/象.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (大's own stand-in is itself).

大象 means elephant.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `vietnamese` had two comma-joined values ("đại tượng, con voi") — simplified to just `con voi`, the native everyday Vietnamese word, matching the same native-equivalent pattern as Japanese ゾウ and Korean 코끼리 (both also native animal names rather than compositional Sino-readings; the compositional form đại tượng also exists but is less common). Mandarin dàxiàng and Cantonese daai6 zoeng6 are standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄧㄙ⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
