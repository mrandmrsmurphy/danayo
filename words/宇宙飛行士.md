## Notes
- [宇](../characters/宇.md) "universe" + [宙 (char)](../characters/宙%20(char).md) "fly" + [飛 (char)](../characters/飛%20(char).md) "travel" + [行 (char)](../characters/行%20(char).md) "person; officer"

宇宙飛行士 is an astronaut: a person trained to travel in space. Wiktionary glosses Japanese うちゅうひこうし and Korean 우주비행사 as "astronaut". The Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the characters, and Korean 우주비행사 matches.

Mandarin ordinarily says 宇航員 instead. It names the person who goes into the [宇宙](宇宙.md), the universe, aboard a [宇宙船](宇宙船.md), spaceship. An extraterrestrial being is the [宇宙人](宇宙人.md).
