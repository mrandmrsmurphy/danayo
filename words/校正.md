## Notes
- [校](../characters/校.md) "school" (here in its "proofread, collate" sense) + [正](../characters/正%20(char).md) "correct" — no stand-in relationship applies (校's own stand-in is [[学校]]; 正's own is itself).

校正 means to proofread or correct.

Japanese こうせい and Korean 교정 are standard, fully compositional readings.
