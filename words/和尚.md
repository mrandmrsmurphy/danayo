## Notes
- [和](../characters/和.md) "harmony" + [尚](../characters/尚%20(char).md) "esteem, still" — no stand-in relationship applies (和's own stand-in is [[和平]]; 尚 is its own stand-in).

和尚 means a Buddhist monk or abbot.

Japanese おしょう is a genuine irregular reading (distinct from either character's own regular on'yomi) — a real, well-attested word-level reading, not a bug. Mandarin héshàng, Cantonese wo4 soeng6-2, Korean 화상, and Vietnamese hoà thượng are all standard readings. Fixed `characters` citation "尚" → "尚 (char)"; fixed unquoted `hsk_level`.
