## Notes
- [罹](../characters/罹.md) "suffer, contract (illness)" + [患 (char)](../characters/患%20(char).md) "affliction, illness" — 罹's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 患's own is [[患]] itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 罹患 legitimizes 罹 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 患 is individually `kwin: false`.

罹患 means to suffer from or contract (an illness).

Mandarin líhuàn, Cantonese lei4 waan6, Japanese りかん (rikan), and Korean 이환 (ihwan) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 罹患 is itself a common attested medical term for "to be afflicted with" in all four languages. Vietnamese li hoạn is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
