## Notes
- [毎 (char)](../characters/毎%20(char).md) "every; each; distributive" + [名 (char)](../characters/名%20(char).md) "name; polite person classifier"

毎名 is the distributive correlative in the 名 ("name; polite person") column of the correlative table: "each person (polite), every person individually, per head", the deferential counterpart of [毎人](毎人.md). The character [毎 (char)](../characters/毎%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *moi*, ㄇㄛㄧ; its alias 每 is the traditional form, so 每名 is an `aliases` entry here, not a separate file) is the distributive morpheme of [毎日](毎日.md) "every day" and [毎年](毎年.md) "every year"; the free word is [毎](毎.md). [名 (char)](../characters/名%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *ming*, ㄇㄧㄫ) is "name", and the word page [名](名.md) records its second job as a polite classifier for counting people (五名 "five persons, polite", 何名様 "how many guests, very honorific"). The predicate is applied to each honoured person in turn. Illustrative only (invented, not from `grammar/`): 毎名、資料有 "each person (polite) has the materials".

Its part of speech is 副用名詞 (adverbial noun), like every cell of the 毎 row ([毎事](毎事.md), [毎物](毎物.md), [毎人](毎人.md), [毎処](毎処.md), [毎時](毎時.md), [毎様](毎様.md), [毎多](毎多.md), [毎類](毎類.md)). `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md` (line 11) lists "Adverbial Nouns" as the class able to be the frame with 0-derivation, and `grammar/Linguistics Features.md` (line 68) states that an adverbial noun may be the topic or frame of a clause with no marking but needs marking when it modifies the main verb.

Against its column neighbours: the collective [皆名](皆名.md) treats the polite persons as one group, whereas 毎名 distributes over them one at a time, the same split as [毎人](毎人.md) against [皆人](皆人.md). [某名](某名.md) is the indefinite, the interrogative [何名](何名.md) is "who?" at the very polite end of the ladder "誰 is normal, 何人 is rude, 何名 is very polite" (`grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`, line 154), and [此名](此名.md), [其名](其名.md) and [彼名](彼名.md) point at one particular honoured person, where 毎名 ranges over all of them. Note that [名](名.md)'s own prose glosses 此名 as "this name; what this is called"; that conflicts with the documented function of the 名 column ("polite person"), which this page follows.

The cross-linguistic fields were checked against Wiktionary on 2026-10-11. Wiktionary has no entry for 毎名, 每名 or 每位. Mandarin 每位 (měiwèi) is therefore entered as the composed equivalent, not an attested headword: the 位 entry (also cited on [此名](此名.md)) lists "(often, polite) classifier for persons", and the 毎 sense is the distributive 每. Its use in formal address (每位來賓) is from memory, not re-checked. The earlier `korean` field 각 분 is removed: 각 is 各 "each, various", not 毎, so it did not match the distributive 毎, and it could not be verified. From memory, not re-checked: Korean 모든 분 or 한 분 한 분, Japanese お一人お一人, Vietnamese *mỗi vị*; none is entered. Dan'a'yo keeps the sense shared across CJKV; no narrowing is needed.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md)'s correlative table (line 125; the same chart is in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`, line 147 in the 名 row), 毎名 is the fourth cell of the 毎 ("every") row, after [毎事](毎事.md), [毎物](毎物.md) and [毎人](毎人.md) and before [毎処](毎処.md). The table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), 63 forms, so 毎名 is parsed compositionally: 毎 = distributive, 名 = polite person. The 羅馬字 *moiming* is from the character pages (毎 *moi*, 名 *ming*) and the 諺文 뫼밍 from 뫼 + 밍; the earlier *moyming* disagreed with the character page.

The 注音 ㄇㄛㄧㄇㄧㄫ carries no syllable-boundary dot, which is correct. `grammar/文法 - 02音韻論.md` gives the structure (C)(G)V(C) with a single coda, and the glide ㄧ is a coda in ㄇㄛㄧ, so the only partition into the intended two syllables, ㄇㄛㄧ + ㄇㄧㄫ (both in `syllables/`), is the only legal one: ㄇㄛㄧㄇ + ㄧㄫ would need two codas. The one other reading, ㄇㄛ + ㄧㄇ + ㄧㄫ, has three syllables and splits the diphthong ㄛㄧ, which the vault does not treat as a boundary (compare [毎人](毎人.md), [毎事](毎事.md)). Contrast [此名](此名.md), whose ㄑㄜㄇ + ㄧㄫ is a legal two-syllable alternative and so needs the dot.
