## Notes
- [陽](../characters/陽%20(char).md) "shine" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [陽](../characters/陽%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

陽 means to shine, or (as a noun) the sun/yang principle, as in 陰陽 ("yin and yang").

Mandarin yáng, Cantonese joeng4, Japanese よう, Korean 양, and Vietnamese dương (as in 陰陽 âm dương) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all 'yang/양/⼘ㄫ) with [[揚]] ("scatter, hurl") and [[様]] ("-ly").
