## Notes
- [天](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven" + [鵝](../characters/鵝.md) "domestic goose" — no stand-in relationship applies (天 is its own stand-in; 鵝's own is [[鵝鳥]]).

天鵝 means swan, and by extension the constellation Cygnus — literally "heaven's goose."

Mandarin tiān'é, Cantonese tin1 ngo4, Korean 천아, and Vietnamese thiên nga are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native term はくちょう (白鳥, "white bird") rather than a compositional てんが — a real lexical substitution, not an error, since 白鳥 in Japanese refers specifically to the swan while remaining a broader term that can also denote other white birds. Filled blank `pos: 名詞`. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄝㄋ·ㄚ unique across both words and characters).
