## Notes
- [卓](../characters/卓.md) "profound, brilliant, lofty, eminent" + [越](../characters/越%20(char).md) "exceed" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [卓](../characters/卓.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (越's own stand-in is [[越]] itself, an unrelated word).

卓越 means excellent, outstanding, or remarkable — standing far above the ordinary.

Japanese たくえつ, Korean 탁월, and Vietnamese trác việt are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `pos: 性詞`; fixed empty-list `aliases`.
