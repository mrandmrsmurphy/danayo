## Notes
- [幕 (char)](../characters/幕%20(char).md) "curtain" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [幕 (char)](../characters/幕%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

幕 names a curtain or screen — literally in the theatrical sense (a stage curtain, and by extension a play's "act," 第一幕 "act one"), and extended to a military headquarters or tent set up behind such a screen, the root of 幕府 (bakufu, the Japanese shogunate government).

Japanese まく (the on'yomi MAKU/BAKU both distinguish the theatrical/act sense from the historical shogunate sense), Korean 막, and Vietnamese mạc are all standard readings. **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 幕 and [[莫]] ("not, none," the phonetic root underlying 幕's own graphemic derivation) share an identical reading (mag/막/ㄇㄚㄎ) — added reciprocal homophone callouts to both pages.
