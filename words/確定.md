## Notes
- [確](../characters/確.md) "certain, sure" + [定](../characters/定.md) "decide, fix" — literally "to certainly decide," i.e. to finalize or confirm something so that it is no longer subject to change.

確定 means to fix, establish, or finalize — used for confirming a decision, a schedule, a legal status, or a piece of information as settled and no longer tentative (確定申告, "final tax return"; 確定判決, "final court judgment").

Mandarin quèdìng, Cantonese kok3 ding6, Japanese かくてい (kakutei), Korean 확정 (hwakjeong), and Vietnamese xác định are all fully compositional and are the standard, everyday words for "to determine/finalize" across the sphere.
