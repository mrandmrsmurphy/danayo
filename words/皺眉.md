## Notes
- [皺](../characters/皺.md) "wrinkle" + [眉 (char)](../characters/眉%20(char).md) "eyebrow"

皺眉 is to frown: to draw the eyebrows together, in worry, anger or dislike. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin zhòuméi as "to frown; to knit one's brows; to scowl". Cantonese zau3 mei4 matches, and Korean 추미 is the reading of the characters.

It is a gesture of the [眉](眉.md), eyebrows. The opposite expression is the smile of [笑](笑.md).
