## Notes
- [振](../characters/振%20(char).md) "shake, excite, rouse" + [動](../characters/動%20(char).md) "move" — no stand-in relationship applies (振's own stand-in is itself; 動's own is itself).

振動 means to shake or vibrate.

Mandarin zhèndòng, Cantonese zan3 dung6, Japanese しんどう, Korean 진동, and Vietnamese chấn động are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bugs: `mandarin`/`cantonese`/`japanese`/`korean` had all been contaminated with an incorrect reading (zhàndòng/zin3 dung6/せんどう/전동, apparently mistaken for 顫動 "tremble" — 顫 has no page in this vault, but no substitution was actually needed here since 振動 is a perfectly ordinary standalone word); the erroneous `顫動` alias and strikethrough Etymology note were removed accordingly. Genuine three-way coincidence: 振 (zhèn) and 震 (zhèn) are homophones in real Mandarin as well as Dan'a'yo, so [[振動]] and [[震動]] are homophones at every level (both 注音 ㄐㄧㄋㄉㄛㄫ) — cross-referenced reciprocally.
