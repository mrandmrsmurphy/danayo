## Notes
- [振](../characters/振%20(char).md) "shake, excite, rouse" + [動](../characters/動%20(char).md) "move" — no stand-in relationship applies (振's own stand-in is itself; 動's own is itself).

振動 means to shake or vibrate.

Mandarin zhèndòng, Cantonese zan3 dung6, Japanese しんどう, Korean 진동, and Vietnamese chấn động are all standard, fully compositional readings. Genuine three-way coincidence: 振 (zhèn) and 震 (zhèn) are homophones in real Mandarin as well as Dan'a'yo, so [[振動]] and [[震動]] are homophones at every level (both 注音 ㄐㄧㄋㄉㄛㄫ) — cross-referenced reciprocally.
