## Notes
- [学](../characters/学.md) "learn" + [士 (char)](../characters/士%20(char).md) "scholar"

学士 is a bachelor's degree, and the person who holds it. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xuéshì as "bachelor's degree; a person with a university degree" and also "scholar, literati". Japanese がくし and Korean 학사 match.

It is the first of the academic degrees, above which come [碩士](碩士.md) and [博士](博士.md). The general word is [学位](学位.md), an academic degree.
