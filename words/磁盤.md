## Notes
- [磁](../characters/磁.md) "magnetic" + [盤](../characters/盤%20(char).md) "plate, disk" — "magnetic disk." Their own `stand_in` values: 磁's is [[磁性]]; 盤's is itself; none points here, so 磁盤 is an independent compound.

磁盤 is a computer disk, originally a magnetic one. Mandarin cípán and Cantonese ci4 pun4 are the ordinary Chinese words in Mainland usage. Japanese says ディスク and Korean 디스크, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[磁性]] (magnetism), [[硬盤]] (hard drive) and [[光盤]] (optical disc).
