## Etymology
- [会 (char)](../characters/会%20(char).md) "meet, gather" + [社](../characters/社.md) "company" + [員](../characters/員.md) "personel, employee"

## Notes
"Company employee, office worker" — [[会社]] "company" + 員 "member, staff." Filled the blank `cantonese: wui6 se5 jyun4`, compositional. `vietnamese: hội xã viên` is compositional, built directly on this word's own [[会社]] "hội xã" — everyday Vietnamese more often says nhân viên công ty for the same concept, but hội xã viên is transparently well-formed from the same roots. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 훠샤운 diverges from Sino-Korean 회사원 in the first syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⼔ㄙ⼘ㄨㄋ unique among both words and characters).
