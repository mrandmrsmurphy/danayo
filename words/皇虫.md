## Notes
- [皇](../characters/皇.md) "emperor" + [虫](../characters/虫.md) "bug" — a 代用字 form: 皇 is 蝗 without its 虫 radical, standing in for the rare character 蝗 of the real compound 蝗虫, which is not in the vault.

皇虫 is the locust, written for 蝗虫 (Mandarin huángchóng, Cantonese wong4 cung4), which Wiktionary glosses "locust." In Japanese 蝗虫 (こうちゅう) is a synonym of 稲子 (inago, "locust"). The same device gives [青廷](青廷.md) for the dragonfly.
