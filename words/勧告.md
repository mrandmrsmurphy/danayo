## Notes
- [勧](../characters/勧.md) "advise, counsel" + [告](../characters/告.md) "inform"
- This word is the stand-in for [[勧]], which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo.

勧告 means to advise or urge someone to do something, in the formal sense of a recommendation or advisory. Japanese かんこく (kankoku) is the usual word for an official recommendation from an authority or international body (国連の勧告, "a UN recommendation"; 退職勧告, "a recommendation to resign"), and Korean 권고 (gwongo) is used the same way. Mandarin quàngào (勸告 in traditional script) means to exhort, to advise earnestly, and is also used of persuading someone against doing something. Cantonese is hyun3 gou3.

Dan'a'yo-specific sense: the shared one. It is housed in Speech beside [[忠告]] (earnest, well-meant advice) and [[提案]] (a proposal); 勧告 is the formal, authority-backed recommendation.
