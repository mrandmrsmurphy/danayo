## Notes
- [架 (char)](../characters/架%20(char).md) "rack; stand; prop" (stand-in for 架)

架 names a rack, stand, or frame — a structure built to hold or support something, such as a bookshelf (書架), a clothes rack, or a display stand. It is typically bound, appearing mainly as part of a larger compound rather than standing alone in ordinary speech, though it functions as a free noun in more formal or written registers.

Cross-linguistically the daughter languages diverge in how freely 架 stands alone. In Japanese, か is a bound on-yomi reading used almost exclusively within compounds (書架 shoka "bookshelf," 十字架 jūjika "cross"); the everyday native word for an ordinary shelf is たな (tana), unrelated to this character. Korean 가 behaves similarly as a bound Sino-Korean element, with the real everyday word for "shelf" being the native 선반. Vietnamese giá, by contrast, functions as an ordinary, freely used word on its own — not only for a physical rack or stand but also, in an unrelated but homophonous sense, for "price" (as in giá cả), a common source of ambiguity resolved entirely by context.
