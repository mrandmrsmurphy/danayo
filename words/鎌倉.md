## Notes
- [鎌 (char)](../characters/鎌%20(char).md) "sickle" + [倉](../characters/倉.md) "granary" — Kamakura; seat of the first Japanese shogunate (1185–1333)

Wiktionary: Chinese 鎌倉 is the city Kamakura, in Kanagawa Prefecture, Japan; Japanese 鎌倉 (かまくら) is a placename and surname, on the southeastern part of modern Kanagawa Prefecture. Wikipedia: Kamakura is a city of Kanagawa Prefecture in the Kantō region, designated a city on 3 November 1939; it was Japan's de facto capital from 1185 to 1333 as the seat of the Kamakura shogunate, established by Minamoto no Yoritomo, the first military government in Japan's history.

Neither character is legitimized here: 鎌's `stand_in` is itself and 倉's is 穀倉. Korean 가마쿠라 writes the name by sound, and the Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese fields give the Sinitic readings of the same characters.
