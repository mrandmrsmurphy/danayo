## Notes
- [肖](../characters/肖.md) "portrait" + [像](../characters/像.md) "statue" — 肖's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 像's own is [[彫像]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 肖像 legitimizes 肖 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

肖像 means a portrait or image.

Mandarin xiàoxiàng, Cantonese ciu3 zoeng6, Japanese しょうぞう (shōzō), and Korean 초상 (chosang) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 肖像 is itself a common attested word for "portrait" in all four languages. Vietnamese tiếu tượng is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
