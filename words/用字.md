## Notes
- [用](../characters/用.md) "use" + [字 (char)](../characters/字%20(char).md) "character, glyph" — 用 is legitimized by [[使用]] and 字 by its own standalone word [[字]], so neither is a stand-in here.

English Wiktionary (Chinese): "to use words"; "usage of characters (in a document, etc.); orthography". Japanese Wiktionary (ようじ): 文字の使いかた。また、使用している文字 ("how characters are used; the characters being used"). In Dan'a'yo it is the second element of 人名用字, 特別用字, 韓国用字 and 他国用字 in `文法 - 03文字法`, beside [[常用]] and [[代用字]].
