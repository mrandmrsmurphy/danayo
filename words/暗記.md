## Notes
- [暗 (char)](../characters/暗%20(char).md) "dark; secret" + [記](../characters/記.md) "remember"

暗記 is to memorize: to learn something by heart, without reading it. Wiktionary glosses Japanese あんき as "rote memorization; to memorize" and Mandarin ànjì as "to learn by heart; to commit to memory". Cantonese am3 gei3 and Korean 암기 match. The 暗 in it means "silently, by oneself".

It is the opposite of understanding ([理解](理解.md)), though the two are used together in study. It is practised in [復習](復習.md), review.
