## Etymology
- [版](../characters/版.md) "edition, version" + [本 (char)](../characters/本%20(char).md) "foundation" — 版's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 本's own is itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 版 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

版本 means edition, version.

Mandarin bǎnběn, Cantonese baan2 bun2, Japanese はんぽん (HAN+HON, rendaku), and Korean 판본 are all fully compositional, matching 版's and 本's own stored citations exactly. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with bản bản (版's own bản + 本's own bản, matching the consistent "bản" reading already established for 本 across [[基本]], [[本来]], [[根本]], [[本質]], etc.) — the apparent reduplication is coincidental homophony between the two morphemes' Vietnamese readings, not a duplication bug.
