## Notes
- [雪 (char)](../characters/雪%20(char).md) "snow" + [魚 (char)](../characters/魚%20(char).md) "fish"

雪魚 names cod or whiting — the pale, "snow-white" fish. The real modern name across the CJKV sphere is generally written 牙鱈/鱈 (using an entirely different character, 鱈, "snow-fish" itself, or the fuller 牙鱈, "tooth-snow-fish") rather than a literal reading of 雪's own syllable — mandarin yáxuě, Japanese たら (tara), and Korean 대구 (daegu) all reflect this real, standardized naming rather than a naive concatenation of 雪/魚's own stored fields, the same kind of divergence documented on [[離枝]]. Vietnamese tuyết ngư is compositional; the everyday Vietnamese name for cod is instead cá tuyết ("snow fish," using the native cá "fish").
