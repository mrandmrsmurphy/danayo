## Notes
- [長 (char)](../characters/長%20(char).md) "eldest, chief" + [子](../characters/子.md) "child" — eldest son.

長子 is the eldest son or first-born child. It is "eldest-child." Mandarin zhǎngzǐ (rarely "eldest child"), Cantonese zoeng2 zi2, Japanese ちょうし and Korean 장자 are confirmed on Wiktionary. It is the classical genealogical term that pairs with [[中子]] (middle son) and [[季子]] (youngest son), the system in which each son is named by his rank, different from the modern elder/younger-marked [[兄]]/[[弟]]. The explicitly male modern word is [[長男]].
