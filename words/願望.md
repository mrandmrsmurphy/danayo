## Notes
- [願](../characters/願.md) "wish" + [望](../characters/望.md) "hope; look"

願望 is a wish: a thing one hopes will come true, or the hoping itself. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yuànwàng as "wish; desire; aspiration" and Japanese がんぼう as "wish, hope; to wish, to desire". Vietnamese nguyện vọng, Cantonese jyun6 mong6 and Korean 원망 match.

It is a quiet hope, set below [切望](切望.md), longing, and [熱望](熱望.md), fervent hope. A wish pursued as a goal is the [野心](野心.md), ambition, and the hope in general is [希望](希望.md).
