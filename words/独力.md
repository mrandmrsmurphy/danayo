## Notes
- [独](characters/独.md) "alone, independent" + [力](characters/力%20(char).md) "power, strength" — 独's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 力's own is itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 独 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

独力 means alone, single-handedly.

Mandarin dúlì, Japanese どくりょく (DOKU+RYOKU), and Korean 독력 were already correct and fully compositional. Filled the blank `cantonese` with duk6 lik6 and blank `vietnamese` with độc lực, both compositional and also the real, standard terms in their languages. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄎㄌㄧㄎ unique across both words and characters).
