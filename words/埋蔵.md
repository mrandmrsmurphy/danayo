## Notes
- [埋](../characters/埋.md) "bury; conceal" + [蔵](../characters/蔵.md) "hide; store" — this word is the stand-in for [埋](../characters/埋.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo (蔵's own stand-in is [[収蔵]]).

**Correction (2026-09-12, user-directed):** this page was previously titled `埋藏.md`, using 藏 (蔵's registered alias) directly in its title while `characters:` correctly cited 蔵 — the same class of bug as [[辺疆]]/[[狡滑]]. 藏 has no independent standing in Dan'a'yo and can never appear in any word; renamed to `埋蔵.md` accordingly, with `埋藏` demoted to an `alias`.

埋蔵 means to bury, conceal, or store underground — natural deposits (埋蔵量 "reserves") as well as literal burial.
