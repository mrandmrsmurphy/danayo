## Notes
- [龍](../characters/龍%20(char).md) "dragon" + [仁](../characters/仁.md) "benevolence" — the name of Yongin, a city of South Korea south of Seoul. 竜 and 龙 are other forms of 龍, which is why the filename uses 龍; 竜仁 and 龙仁 are kept as aliases.

龍仁 (Yongin) is a large city of Gyeonggi Province ([[京畿]]道) in South Korea, south-east of [[漢城]] (Seoul), known for its theme park and a large university cluster (from memory, not re-checked). The name reads "dragon benevolence".

English Wiktionary has 龍仁 as the Korean city (Mandarin Lóngrén, Cantonese lung4 jan4) and has a Korean entry as a hanja form of 용인; Chinese Wiktionary has no entry. Korean 룡인 is the North Korean reading, with 룡 kept at the start of the syllable after 龍; the usual South Korean spelling is 용인 (from memory, not re-checked). Japanese Wiktionary has no entry, so Japanese りゅうじん is composed from the on'yomi. Vietnamese Long Nhân is composed from the character pages; no Vietnamese dictionary entry was found.
