## Notes
- [源](../characters/源.md) "origin, spring" + [泉](../characters/泉.md) "wellspring"

源泉 is a source: a spring where water rises, and by extension the origin of anything. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 源泉 (Mandarin yuánquán, Cantonese jyun4 cyun4) literally as "water source; water supply" and figuratively as "origin; fountainhead; source". It glosses Japanese げんせん as "river source or spring source" and "source in general (including of an inspiration)", and Korean 원천 as "source, fountainhead". Vietnamese nguyên tuyền is the Sino-Vietnamese reading of the two characters.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [泉](../characters/泉.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
