## Notes
- [源](../characters/源.md) "origin, spring" + [泉](../characters/泉.md) "wellspring" — this word is the viable form of [泉](../characters/泉.md): the character's meaning (spring; wellspring) is identical, but 泉 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 源泉 is its `stand_in` compound.

源泉 names both a literal water source (a spring, the head of a stream) and, very commonly, a metaphorical fundamental origin — 収入の源泉 ("source of income"), インスピレーションの源泉 ("wellspring of inspiration"), 源泉徴収 (gensen-chōshū, "withholding at source," standard Japanese tax terminology for income tax deducted directly from wages before payment).

Japanese げんせん and Korean 원천 are both standard compositional Sino-readings, 원천 being especially common in Korean legal/tax contexts (원천징수, "withholding tax," directly paralleling 源泉徴収). Vietnamese nguyên tuyền is likewise standard. No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ⼔ㄋㄐ⼔ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
