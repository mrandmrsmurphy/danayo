## Notes
- [𦚖 (char)](../characters/𦚖%20(char).md) "blink; wink"

𦚖 is to blink: to close and open the eyes quickly, and also to wink. Its alias 眨 is the form used in Mandarin and Korean writing. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin zhǎ as "to blink; to wink" and lists Japanese and Korean entries for 眨 as "wink"; the compound 眨眼 is "to blink, to wink; in a blink". The Mandarin and Cantonese readings are those of the character, and no Japanese reading is attested.

It is the act of the [目](目.md), eye, and is done by the [眼瞼](眼瞼.md), eyelid. The bound character [瞬](瞬.md) names the same blink as a unit of time, in [瞬間](瞬間.md) and [一瞬](一瞬.md), "an instant".
