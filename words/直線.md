## Notes
- [直 (char)](../characters/直%20(char).md) "straight" + [線](../characters/線.md) "line" — 線's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 直's own is [[直]] itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 線 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

直線 is the basic geometric term for a straight line. Mandarin zhíxiàn, Cantonese zik6 sin3, Japanese ちょくせん (chokusen), and Korean 직선 (jikseon) all keep this literal geometric sense as their primary meaning.

Vietnamese trực tuyến has drifted: while compositionally "straight line," in modern everyday Vietnamese the phrase means "online" or "live" (as in a live broadcast or an internet connection) — a metaphorical extension of "a direct/straight line [of connection]" onto real-time digital connectivity. The geometric sense of "straight line" in Vietnamese mathematics is instead expressed with the native đường thẳng.
