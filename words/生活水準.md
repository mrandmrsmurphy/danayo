## Notes
- [生](../characters/生.md) "life" + [活](../characters/活%20(char).md) "live" + [水](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [準](../characters/準.md) "level" — "life level." Their own `stand_in` values: 生's is [[生活]]; 活's is itself; 水's is itself; 準's is [[標準]]; none points here, so 生活水準 is an independent compound.

生活水準 is the standard of living: the level of material well-being of a person or country. Mandarin shēnghuó shuǐzhǔn, Cantonese sang1 wut6 seoi2 zeon2, Japanese せいかつすいじゅん and Korean 생활수준 all use it. It is built on [[生活]] (life, living) and 水準 "level, standard," literally the level a liquid settles to. Compare [[経済]] (economy).
