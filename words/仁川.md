## Notes
- [仁](../characters/仁.md) "benevolence" + [川](../characters/川%20(char).md) "river" — the name of Incheon, the western port city on the Yellow Sea coast.

仁川 (Incheon) lies on the coast west of Seoul, in the old 京畿 region (see [[京畿]]), and is the seaport and airport city of the capital area. Its full modern name is 仁川廣域市 (인천광역시), "Incheon Metropolitan City", a separate unit since 1981, first as a 直轄市, and renamed 廣域市 in 1995 (from memory, not re-checked). The name 仁川 is reported from the early Joseon period, replacing earlier names for the same coast (from memory, not re-checked). The older European-language name of the port, Chemulpo (濟物浦), is also recorded in Wiktionary as a Russian translation of the same city; it is a separate local name, not from 仁川.

English Wiktionary has 仁川 as Mandarin Rénchuān and Cantonese jan4 cyun1, Japanese modern インチョン with じんせん as the dated on'yomi reading, Korean 인천, and Vietnamese Nhân Xuyên. Chinese Wiktionary has only a stub (readings, no definition), and no Korean or Vietnamese Wiktionary entry was found.
