## Notes
- [愛](../characters/愛%20(char).md) "love" + [知](../characters/知%20(char).md) "know"

愛知 is Aichi, written "love, knowledge". The name is generally traced to the old Ayuchi district (年魚市), rewritten with the auspicious characters 愛知; the reading あいち is the on-reading of 愛 (あい) plus 知 (ち). The characters are applied to the sound and not read for sense (from memory, not re-checked).

Aichi is the centre of Japan's car industry, with Toyota City and its capital Nagoya, whose castle was the seat of the Owari Tokugawa branch. Oda Nobunaga, Toyotomi Hideyoshi and Tokugawa Ieyasu all came from this area, in the old provinces of Owari and Mikawa.

In Dan'a'yo 愛知 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 아이치 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
