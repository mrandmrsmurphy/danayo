## Notes
- [六 (char)](../characters/六%20(char).md) "six" + [千](../characters/千.md) "thousand"

六千 is six thousand (6,000), a plain compositional numeral, 六 × 1,000. Korean 륙천 keeps the initial ㄹ, per the vault's North Korean convention. Vietnamese lục thiên is the compositional Hán Việt reading; everyday Vietnamese counts natively instead (from memory, not re-checked), as on [七千](七千.md). Used in the Dan'a'yo numeral pieces such as the year in the 1919 Korean Declaration of Independence on [Korea](../lookup/Korean/Korea.md); there are no words for full numbers, only for these pieces.
