## Notes
- [起 (char)](../characters/起%20(char).md) "rise; start" + [源](../characters/源.md) "source"

起源 is origin: where and how something began. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin qǐyuán as "origin; source" and "to originate", and Japanese きげん as "origin, derivation, genesis". Vietnamese khởi nguyên is the same word, and Korean 기원 and Cantonese hei2 jyun4 match.

It names the beginning of a thing in history, such as the origin of a word or a species. It sits beside [根源](根源.md), the root cause, and [由来](由来.md), where something comes from, and [出身](出身.md), a person's origin.
