## Notes
- [旧](../characters/旧%20(char).md) "old" + [港](../characters/港.md) "port" — "old port"; no stand-in relationship applies (旧's own stand-in is the single-character word [[旧]]; 港 is its own stand-in). The alias 舊港 is the traditional spelling.

旧港 is Palembang, the city in South Sumatra, Indonesia, on the Musi River. It is the old Chinese name: English Wiktionary marks 舊港 an obsolete Chinese proper noun for "present-day Palembang", the modern Chinese name being 巨港, and quotes the 1451 *Yingya Shenglan* of Ma Huan, which says that Old Port is the former Srivijaya (三佛齊), called Palembang by the local people, and then under the control of Java. The place was a trading port long before that, and the Ming court's 舊港 appears again in accounts of Zheng He's voyages (from memory, not re-checked).

In Dan'a'yo the old name is the word, since 旧 and 港 are both ordinary characters and the compound is transparent. The name is a translation of the sense of the place, not of its sound: the Malay name Palembang is not derived from "old port". Mandarin Jiùgǎng and Cantonese gau6 gong2 are as at English Wiktionary. The `vietnamese` field holds the compositional Hán-Việt reading Cựu Cảng (旧 cựu, 港 cảng, each as the character pages give them; not attested for this name). Japanese パレンバン and Korean 팔렘방 are the ordinary transliterations of the Indonesian name, as with 旧金山, so `kwin` is false.

Compare [[旧金山]], "Old Gold Mountain", another Chinese-language city name built on 旧. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼜ㄛㄏㄛㄫ unique across words and characters, checked 2026-10-12). The 注音 needs no syllable-boundary dot.
