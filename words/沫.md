## Notes
- [沫](../characters/沫%20(char).md) "froth, foam, bubbles, suds" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [沫](../characters/沫%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

沫 means froth, foam, bubbles, or suds.

Mandarin mò, Cantonese mut6, Japanese まつ, Korean 말, and Vietnamese mạt are all standard, fully compositional readings. Homophone of [[抹]] "rub; wipe" (both 注音 ㄇㄚㄊ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words.
