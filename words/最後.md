## Notes
- [最 (char)](../characters/最%20(char).md) "most" + [後 (char)](../characters/後%20(char).md) "after" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both are bare self-standing characters); 最後 is an independent compound.

A clean, directly parallel compound across the whole sphere — Mandarin zuìhòu, Cantonese zeoi3 hau6, Japanese さいご, Korean 최후, and Vietnamese tối hậu (as in tối hậu thư, "ultimatum," literally "final letter") all converge on the same core sense of "final; last; ultimate," with no notable sense-divergence between languages.
