## Notes
- [限](../characters/限.md) "limit, boundary" + [度](../characters/度.md) "degree, manner" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes 限 as a bound character (度's own stand-in is [[程度]]; transitivity fails, so no `#cranberry`).

限度 means a limit or limitation — the maximum (or minimum) extent something may reach. Mandarin 限度 (xiàndù), Cantonese 限度 (haan6dou6), Japanese 限度 (げんど), Korean 한도 (hando), and Sino-Vietnamese hạn độ are all everyday words for this concept, common in both concrete contexts (信用限度/신용한도/hạn mức tín dụng, "credit limit") and abstract ones (忍耐限度, "limit of patience").
