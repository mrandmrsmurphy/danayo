## Notes
- [提](../characters/提.md) "raise, propose" + [示](../characters/示.md) "show" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [提](../characters/提.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (示's own stand-in is [[開示]]).

提示 means to present, show, or produce.

Mandarin tíshì, Cantonese tai4 si6, Japanese ていじ, Korean 제시, and Vietnamese chề thị are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄍㄝ unique across both words and characters).
