## Notes
- [八 (char)](../characters/八%20(char).md) "eight" + [角 (char)](../characters/角%20(char).md) "angle, corner" — octagon (geometric shape) and, by extension, star anise (Illicium verum), named for its eight-pointed star-shaped seed pod: a common spice native to southern China and northern Vietnam, used in phở broth and in five-spice powder.

Mandarin bājiǎo, Cantonese baat3 gok3, Japanese はっかく (八角形/はっかくけい, "octagon"), Korean 팔각 (팔각형, "octagon"; 팔각, "star anise") and Vietnamese bát giác (covering both senses; bát giác hồi hương for the spice specifically) are the readings.
