## Notes
- [八 (char)](../characters/八%20(char).md) "eight" + [角 (char)](../characters/角%20(char).md) "angle, corner" — "octagon" (geometric shape) and, by extension, "star anise" (Illicium verum, named for its eight-pointed star-shaped seed pod), a common phở-broth spice native to northeastern Vietnam and southern China. `vietnamese: bát giác` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese covering both senses (confirmed via web search, also seen as bát giác hồi hương for the spice specifically). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 받곡 diverges from Sino-Korean 팔각 in both syllables' initial consonants. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄊㄍㄛㄎ unique among both words and characters).
