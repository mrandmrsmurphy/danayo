## Notes
- [普](../characters/普.md) "universal, common" + [通](../characters/通%20(char).md) "pass through, communicate" + [話](../characters/話%20(char).md) "speak on, talk about" — no stand-in relationship applies (普's own stand-in is [[普通]]; 通/話's own are themselves).

普通話 means Mandarin (Standard Chinese, literally "common speech").

Mandarin pǔtōnghuà, Cantonese pou2 tung1 waa6-2, Japanese ふつうわ, Korean 보통화, and Vietnamese phổ thông thoại are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄛㄊㄛㄫㄏ⺢ㄧ unique across both words and characters).
