## Notes
- [小 (char)](../characters/小%20(char).md) "small" + [行 (char)](../characters/行%20(char).md) "walk; travel; move" + [星 (char)](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 行's and 星's own `stand_in` fields point to themselves).

小行星 is an asteroid, a small body orbiting the sun (most densely concentrated in the belt between Mars and Jupiter) — 行 here contributes its "moving, traveling" sense (a body that moves/orbits) rather than "walk."

**Japanese uses an entirely different character in the compound**: 小惑星 (しょうわくせい, using 惑 "confuse, bewilder" rather than 行), listed here as this word's `aliases` entry — a real, standing Japanese-specific spelling variant rather than a typo, the same class of divergence documented on [[審査]]. Korean 소행성 and Vietnamese tiểu hành tinh both keep the 行-based Sino-Chinese spelling directly. No HSK level is given for the compound. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄛㄏㄚㄫㄙㄝㄫ unique across both words and characters).
