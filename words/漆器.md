## Notes
- [漆 (char)](../characters/漆%20(char).md) "lacquer" + [器](../characters/器.md) "utensil"

漆器 is lacquerware: wooden objects coated in lacquer. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin qīqì as "lacquerware" and Japanese しっき as "lacquerware". Korean 칠기 is the same word. Cantonese cat1 hei3 matches.

It is made with [漆](漆.md), lacquer, and decorated with the shell inlay of the [螺鈿](螺鈿.md).
