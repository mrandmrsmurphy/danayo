丹砂 (cinnabar, mercury sulfide) is one of the oldest-named minerals in the Sinosphere, historically the primary source of both the vermilion pigment and — through Daoist alchemical elixir-making — of mercury itself, a practice documented to have poisoned at least five Tang-dynasty emperors seeking immortality. It shares its exact referent with two synonymous compounds also attested in Chinese sources, 朱砂 ("vermilion sand," emphasizing the color) and 辰砂 ("Chen-prefecture sand," naming its most famous historical source in Hunan) — three different descriptive names for the same substance, none more "correct" than the others. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 단사 coincides exactly with Sino-Korean 단사, both constituent characters independently carrying `kwin: true` on their own pages.
