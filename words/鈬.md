## Notes
- [鈬](../characters/鈬%20(char).md) "bronze bell" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

鈬 means a bronze bell — a metal bell historically used in rituals or as a signaling instrument.

Mandarin duó, Cantonese dok6, Japanese たく, Korean 탁, and Vietnamese đạc are all standard, fully compositional readings. Part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read dag/닥/ㄉㄚㄎ) with [[嫡]] ("legitimate wife") and [[摘]] ("pinch, pluck").
