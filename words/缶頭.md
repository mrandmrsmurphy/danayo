## Notes
- [缶](../characters/缶.md) "jar, can" + [頭 (char)](../characters/頭%20(char).md) "head; top"

缶頭 is a can or tin, and so canned goods; its usual spelling is 罐頭, an alias, because 罐 is an alias of 缶. English Wiktionary glosses Mandarin 罐頭 (guàntou, with the variant guàntóu recorded here; Cantonese gun3 tau4-2, a changed tone) as "can; tin" and "jar", and Cantonese also uses it attributively for "canned", meaning standardized or mass-produced. It lists no Japanese or Korean entry for 罐頭: the Japanese word for canned food is 缶詰 (かんづめ) and the Korean is 통조림, so the Japanese かんとう and Korean 관두 here are built from the characters.

This word is the stand-in for [缶](../characters/缶.md): the character means a jar or can, but is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo, so 缶頭 legitimizes it.
