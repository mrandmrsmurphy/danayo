## Notes
- [木](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree" + [浦](../characters/浦%20(char).md) "riverbank, shore" — the name of Mokpo, a port city in the south-west of South Korea.

木浦 (Mokpo) is a port city at the south-western tip of South Jeolla Province ([[全羅]]南道) in South Korea (from memory, not re-checked). The name reads "tree shore".

English Wiktionary has 木浦 as the Korean city (Mandarin Mùpǔ, Cantonese muk6 pou2), Chinese and Korean Wiktionary have the string, and English Wiktionary has a Korean entry as a hanja form of 목포. Its Japanese entry gives モクポ (from the Korean) and a dated reading もっぽ, which is taken here as the Sino-Xenic reading. Vietnamese Mộc Phố is composed from the character pages; no Vietnamese dictionary entry was found.
