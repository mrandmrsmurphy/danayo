## Notes
- [北](../characters/北.md) "north" + [斗 (char)](../characters/斗%20(char).md) "dipper; peck"

北斗 is the Northern Dipper, the Big Dipper: the seven bright stars shaped like a ladle in the northern sky. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin Běidǒu and Japanese ほくと as "Big Dipper". Vietnamese Bắc Đẩu is the same word, and Korean 북두 and Cantonese bak1 dau2 match.

It is the same group as the [七星](七星.md), seven stars, and the [斗](斗.md), the Dipper. Its handle is used to point toward the [北極星](北極星.md), the North Star.
