## Notes
- [腹](../characters/腹%20(char).md) "stomach, belly, abdomen" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 腹 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

腹 means the stomach, belly, or abdomen: the front of the body below the chest (腹部, "abdomen"; 腹痛, "stomachache"; 空腹, "empty stomach"), and by extension the inside or core of something (腹地, "hinterland"; 山腹, "mountainside"), or one's inner thoughts and intentions (腹案, "private plan"; 心腹, "confidant"). The graph is a 形声 compound of 月 (肉) "flesh" with the phonetic 复.

Mandarin fù, Cantonese fuk1, Japanese ふく (腹部/ふくぶ, "abdomen"; 空腹/くうふく, "hunger"; 切腹/せっぷく, "ritual suicide by disembowelment"), Korean 복 (복부, 공복), and Vietnamese phúc (phúc mạc, "peritoneum") are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native noun 腹 (はら) for the everyday word, so on'yomi ふく is bound to compounds; Korean has native 배.
