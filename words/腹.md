## Notes
- [腹](../characters/腹%20(char).md) "stomach, belly, abdomen" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 腹 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

腹 means the stomach, belly, or abdomen, as in 腹部 (abdomen).

Mandarin fù, cantonese fuk1, and korean 복 (bok) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading はら (hara, "belly") rather than the on-reading フク (FUKU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese phúc is one of two stored candidates. Part of a genuine three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group with [[福]] ("bless") and [[副]] ("vice-") — already fully cross-linked from 福's own perfecting pass.
