## Notes
- [半](../characters/半.md) "half" + [島](../characters/島%20(char).md) "island" — no stand-in relationship applies (半's own stand-in is [[一半]]; 島 is its own stand-in).

半島 means peninsula — a piece of land almost surrounded by water, "half island."

**Real bug found and fixed**: `mandarin`/`cantonese` were both entirely blank — filled with bàndǎo/bun3 dou2 (compositional from each character's own reading). Japanese はんとう, Korean 반도, and Vietnamese bán đảo are all standard readings. **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 半島 and [[絆倒]] ("to trip, cause to stumble") share an identical reading — standardized the non-canonical `[!tip]` callouts on both pages into proper reciprocal `>[!warning] Homophones` callouts.
