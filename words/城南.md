## Notes
- [城](../characters/城.md) "castle, wall" + [南](../characters/南.md) "south" — the name of Seongnam, a city of South Korea south of Seoul.

城南 (Seongnam) is a city of Gyeonggi Province ([[京畿]]道) in South Korea, a large satellite city just south of [[漢城]] (Seoul), and the site of the Pangyo technology district (from memory, not re-checked). The name reads "south of the castle".

English and Chinese Wiktionary list 城南 first as Seongnam (Mandarin Chéngnán), and English Wiktionary has a Korean entry as a hanja form of 성남; the same string is also Chengnan, the name of several towns and villages in China. English Wiktionary's Japanese section also gives ソンナム for the city. The Japanese reading じょうなん is composed from the on'yomi, not listed by Wiktionary. Vietnamese Thành Nam is composed from the character pages.
