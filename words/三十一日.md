## Notes
- [三十](../words/三十.md) "thirty" + [一 (char)](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — no stand-in relationship applies (all constituents are independent Dan'a'yo entries).

三十一日 names the 31st (and, by extension, last possible) day of a month. Mandarin sānshíyī rì, Cantonese saam1 sap6 jat1 jat6, Korean 삼십일일, Japanese さんじゅういちにち, and Sino-Vietnamese tam thập nhất nhật are all standard compositional readings, matching the established convention on sibling day-words like [[十一日]]/[[七日]]. **Fixed several real bugs**: `mandarin`/`cantonese` had been entirely blank; `japanese` held a stray middle-dot separator (`さんじゅう・いちにち`) instead of a plain concatenation; `vietnamese` held the cardinal-number phrase `ba mươi mốt` ("thirty-one" as a counted number) rather than the compositional day-of-month form matching sibling words. Fixed the recurring `characters:` disambiguation bug (bare "十" despite `words/十.md` existing) and the duplicate `品詞` field.
