## Notes

水星 means Mercury (the planet).

Mandarin Shuǐxīng, Cantonese seoi2 sing1, Japanese すいせい, and Korean 수성 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 水's and 星's own stored citations exactly). Vietnamese sao Thủy (native "sao," star, + Sino 水's own thuỷ) follows the real, standard Vietnamese planet-naming convention, placing 星's native equivalent first. `kwin: true` because 諺文 수성 matches Korean 수성 exactly.
