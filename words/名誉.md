## Notes
- [名 (char)](../characters/名%20(char).md) "name" + [誉](../characters/誉.md) "reputation" — this word is the viable form of [誉](../characters/誉.md): the character's meaning (reputation, fame) is identical, but 誉 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. The compound pairs it with 名 ("name"), evoking the classical idea that one's name and one's reputation are inseparable.
- Also used attributively: "honorary" (as in 名誉職, honorary position).

Mandarin míngyù, Cantonese ming4 jyu6, Korean 명예 (myeongye), and Japanese めいよ (meiyo) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real everyday word for "honor, reputation" in its language. Vietnamese danh dự is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, matching 名's danh and 誉's own dự, and is itself the real, everyday Vietnamese word for "honor, dignity." `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 밍요 diverges from Sino-Korean 명예 in both syllables. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄇㄧㄫ⼄ unique among both words and characters).
