## Notes
- [名 (char)](../characters/名%20(char).md) "name" + [誉](../characters/誉.md) "reputation" — stand-in for [誉](../characters/誉.md), which cannot appear independently: its meaning (reputation, fame) is identical, but 誉 alone is too bound to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. The compound pairs it with 名 ("name"), evoking the classical idea that one's name and one's reputation are inseparable.
- Also used attributively: "honorary" (as in 名誉職, honorary position).

Mandarin míngyù, Cantonese ming4 jyu6, Korean 명예 (myeongye), and Japanese めいよ (meiyo) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real everyday word for "honor, reputation" in its language. Vietnamese danh dự is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, matching 名's danh and 誉's own dự, and is itself the real, everyday Vietnamese word for "honor, dignity."
