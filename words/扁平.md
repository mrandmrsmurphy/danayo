## Notes
- [扁](../characters/扁.md) "flat" + [平](../characters/平.md) "even"
- This word is the viable form of [扁](../characters/扁.md): the character's meaning (flat, planar) is identical, but 扁 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 扁平 is its `stand_in` compound.

扁平 means flat in the sense of being flattened: thin in one dimension and broad in the other two (a flat nose, a flat fish, a flattened disc). Mandarin biǎnpíng, Cantonese bin2 ping4, Japanese へんぺい (henpei; 扁平足, "flat feet", 扁平上皮, "squamous epithelium") and Korean 편평 (pyeonpyeong) are the same compound, common in anatomy and biology. 扁 is "flat, broad and thin" (compare 扁豆, "lentil, flat bean").

In Dan'a'yo it contrasts with [[平坦]]: 扁平 describes the shape of a thing (squashed flat), while 平坦 describes the evenness of a surface (smooth ground, a level plain). No other narrower or shifted sense is needed.
