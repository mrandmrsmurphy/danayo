## Notes
- [善 (char)](../characters/善%20(char).md) "good"

善 is 会意 (a hand-tied lamb/goat offering, or two 言 flanking 羊 in some analyses — sources differ on the exact composition). It means "good, virtuous, kind" (善良, "good-natured, kind-hearted") and, as a verb, "to be good at, skilled in" (善書, "to write skillfully").

**Content corrected**: the entry previously stored `korean: 착할` and `japanese: よ-い` — both native-reading glosses (Korean 훈 "kind, good," Japanese kun'yomi よい "good"), not the Sino-Korean/Sino-Japanese readings this field is meant to hold. Also corrected `pos` from `実詞` to `性詞`, matching the character page's own classification of this word as descriptive/adjectival rather than a plain action verb. Removed blank placeholder keys `hsk_level:`, `swadesh:` (optional-only-when-nonempty per checklist), and the stray `hanmun_edu_level` field (not a word-page frontmatter field per the checklist; that data belongs to the character page, where it is already present).

Mandarin shàn and Cantonese sin6 preserve the sense directly. Korean 선 (seon) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 착하다 (chakhada, "kind, good-natured"). Vietnamese thiện is the standard Hán Việt reading (thiện lương, "kind, good-natured"; từ thiện, "charity"); the character page also stores a second reading, thiến, which is genuinely attested but for a distinct, rare classical verb sense ("to approve of, consider right," as in 沛公善之, "Bái Công praised it"; or "to be on friendly terms with," as in 相善) — not the "good, virtuous" sense this word covers, so `thiện` alone is kept as the field value.
