## Notes
- [根](characters/根%20(char).md) "root, foundation" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 根 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

根 means root or foundation.

Mandarin gēn, Cantonese gan1, Japanese こん, Korean 근, and Vietnamese căn (the primary form among the character's own comma-joined vietnamese variants căn/cằn/cỗi) are all standard, fully compositional readings.
