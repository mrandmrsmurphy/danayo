## Notes
- [根](../characters/根%20(char).md) "root, foundation" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 根 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

根 means root or foundation: the underground part of a plant that anchors it and draws water (樹根, "tree root"; 根莖, "rhizome"), and by extension the origin or basis of something (根本, "fundamental"; 根源, "origin"; 根據, "basis, according to"). In mathematics it is the root of a number or equation (平方根, "square root"). It also serves as a counter for long thin objects in Mandarin (一根繩子, "a rope"). The graph is a 形声 compound of 木 "tree" with the phonetic 艮.

Mandarin gēn, Cantonese gan1, Japanese こん (根本/こんぽん, "root, basis"; 根気/こんき, "perseverance"), Korean 근 (근본, 근거) and Vietnamese căn (căn bản, "basis"; căn nguyên, "origin"; căn bậc hai, "square root") are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native noun 根 (ね) for the everyday word "root", with on'yomi こん bound to compounds, and Korean has native 뿌리 beside Sino-Korean 근.
