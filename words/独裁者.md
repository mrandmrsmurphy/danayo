## Notes
- [独](../characters/独.md) "alone" + [裁](../characters/裁.md) "decide, cut" + [者](../characters/者.md) "person" — "person who decides alone." 独's own `stand_in` is [[独力]], 裁's is [[裁縫]] and 者's is [[学者]]; none points here, so 独裁者 is an independent compound.

独裁者 is a dictator or autocrat: a ruler who holds absolute power without sharing it. Mandarin dúcáizhě, Cantonese duk6 coi4 ze2, Japanese どくさいしゃ and Korean 독재자 use it; the shorter 独裁 names the rule itself. Compare [[王]] (king) and [[首長]] (leader).
