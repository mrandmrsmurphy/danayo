## Notes
- [事](../characters/事%20(char).md) "thing, action" + [件](../characters/件.md) "item, matter" — an event, incident, or occurrence. Legitimizing note: 件's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, so 件 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

事件 is the standard word for a discrete happening or incident across the Sinosphere: Mandarin shìjiàn, Cantonese si6 gin6-2 (件 taking a tone-sandhi-shifted reading in this compound, per this vault's own established -2 notation), Japanese じけん (a common suffix in news vocabulary, e.g. 殺人事件, "murder case"), Korean 사건 (사건 사고, "incidents and accidents," a standard news phrase), and Vietnamese sự kiện (a common noun, lowercase — not a proper name) are all fully compositional and mutually transparent.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 지견 diverges from Sino-Korean 사건, since both 事 and 件 independently carry `kwin: false` on their own character pages.
