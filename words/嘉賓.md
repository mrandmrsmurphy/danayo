## Notes
- "Guest of honor, distinguished guest" — a classical literary term, famously from the *Book of Odes* (詩經, 小雅·鹿鳴): 我有嘉賓 ("I have distinguished guests"). **Gloss bug found and fixed**: `english` previously included an unrelated "sparrow" entry — removed as a content error with no connection to this word's actual meaning. `japanese: ゲスト` is the English loanword "guest," used here as a deliberate best-fit stand-in since 嘉賓 itself isn't a lexicalized native Japanese compound (来賓 らいひん is the real Japanese word for "distinguished guest"). `vietnamese: gia tân` is compositional from each character's own confirmed reading; no independent attestation as an established modern Vietnamese word was found (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted). `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 가빈 matches Sino-Korean 가빈 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄅㄧㄋ unique among both words and characters).
