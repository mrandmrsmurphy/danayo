## Etymology
- [嘉](../characters/嘉.md) "good" + [賓](../characters/賓.md) "visitor, guest"

## Notes
- "Guest of honor, distinguished guest" — a classical literary term, famously from the *Book of Odes* (詩經, 小雅·鹿鳴): 我有嘉賓 ("I have distinguished guests"). `japanese: ゲスト` is the English loanword "guest," used here as a deliberate best-fit stand-in since 嘉賓 itself isn't a lexicalized native Japanese compound (来賓 らいひん is the real Japanese word for "distinguished guest"). `vietnamese: gia tân` is compositional from each character's own confirmed reading; no independent attestation as an established modern Vietnamese word was found (domain knowledge, not independently confirmed by search).
