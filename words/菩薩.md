Abbreviation of 菩提薩埵／菩提萨埵 (pútísàduǒ), from Sanskrit बोधिसत्त्व ‎(bodhisattva) or Pali bodhisatta.

## Notes
- **Genuine `#cranberry`**: both [菩](../characters/菩.md) and [薩](../characters/薩.md) — each meaning "bodhisattva" alone but never independently attested outside this compound — have their own `stand_in` pointing to this exact compound (full transitivity; tag already correctly present).

Mandarin púsà, japanese ぼさつ, and korean 보살 are all standard, fully attested readings.
