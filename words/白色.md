## Notes
- [白 (char)](../characters/白%20(char).md) "white" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — literally "white color." Distinct from the bare color-word [[白]] itself, which carries the vault's Swadesh entry for "white" (#175); 白色 is the fuller technical/compound form.

白色 is the standard Sino compound for "the color white," used across formal, technical, and scientific registers — mandarin báisè, cantonese baak6 sik1, japanese はくしょく (hakushoku, as in 白色人種 "Caucasian race" or 白色矮星 "white dwarf star"), and vietnamese bạch sắc are all compositional. Korean 백색 patterns the same way, though in everyday speech Korean actually prefers the native 하양 (or adjectival 하얗다) for "white," reserving the Sino form for more formal or technical contexts — the same everyday-native-vs-formal-Sino split found elsewhere in the language, e.g. between 흰곰 (native "polar bear") and 백웅 (unused Sino form).
