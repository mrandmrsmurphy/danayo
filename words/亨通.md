## Notes
- [亨](../characters/亨.md) "smooth, favorable" + [通 (char)](../characters/通%20(char).md) "to pass through, unobstructed" — "to go smoothly, prosper." Both `japanese: こうつう` and `vietnamese: hanh thông` are directly attested (confirmed via web search): the Vietnamese is common in blessings (vạn sự hanh thông, "may all things go smoothly"), while the Japanese reading こうつう is a rare true homophone of the everyday word 交通 "traffic" despite sharing no characters. `cantonese: hang1 tung1` filled in compositionally. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 항통 diverges from Sino-Korean 형통 in the first syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄫㄊㄛㄫ unique among both words and characters).
