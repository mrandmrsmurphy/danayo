## Notes
- [満](../characters/満%20(char).md) "full" + [洲](../characters/洲.md) "islet" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 満's and 洲's own stand-ins are themselves). This is the broad term. For:
- 満洲人 - Manchurian person
- 満洲族 - Manchurian ethnicity
- 満洲語 - Manchurian language

Mandarin Mǎnzhōu, Cantonese mun5 zau1, Japanese まんしゅう, Korean 만주, and Vietnamese Mãn Châu are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 満's and 洲's own stored citations exactly), and also the real, standard name for Manchuria in each language. Removed the redundant `品詞` field. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄚㄋㄐㄨㄛ unique across both words and characters).
