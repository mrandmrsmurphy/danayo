## Notes
- [採](../characters/採.md) "gather" + [用](../characters/用.md) "use" — no stand-in relationship applies (採's own stand-in is [[採集]]; 用's own is [[使用]]).

採用 means to adopt, employ, or use.

Mandarin cǎiyòng, Cantonese coi2 jung6, Japanese さいよう, Korean 채용, and Vietnamese thái dụng are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄚㄧ⼄ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
