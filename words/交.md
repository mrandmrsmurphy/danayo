## Notes
- [交 (char)](../characters/交%20(char).md) "mix; exchange"

交 is 象形: a human figure with crossed legs — the upper part resembles [[立]] ("standing person"), but the lower strokes cross, and that crossing itself carries the meaning of mixing and exchange (unrelated to the visually similar 父). It heads one of the largest compound families in the vault, spanning literal crossing/intersection ([[交差]] "intersection," [[交点]] "point of intersection"), social exchange ([[交友]]/[[交遊]] "make friends," [[社交]] "socializing," [[外交]] "diplomacy"), and transactional/reciprocal exchange ([[交換]] "exchange, swap," [[交易]] "trade," [[交替]] "alternation, taking turns").

Mandarin jiāo, Cantonese gaau1, and Vietnamese giao (the Hán Việt reading, as in giao lưu 交流 "exchange, interaction," and giao thông 交通 "traffic, transportation") all preserve the sense directly. Japanese has two on'yomi, こう/きょう (kō/kyō, fully productive in compounds like 交通 こうつう "traffic" and 社交 しゃこう "socializing") alongside native まじ(わる) (maji(waru), "to associate with, mix with"). Korean 교 (gyo) parallels the Sino-xenic reading throughout its own compound vocabulary, alongside native 사귀다 (sagwida, "to date, associate with").
