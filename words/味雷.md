## Notes
- [味](../characters/味.md) "taste; experience (stand-in: [[味覚]])" + [雷 (char)](../characters/雷%20(char).md) "thunder (stand-in: itself)"

Wiktionary: Chinese 味蕾 "tastebud"; Japanese 味蕾 "a tastebud"; Korean 미뢰 is the hanja form of "tastebud". Dan'a'yo writes 味雷, with the common 雷 standing in for the rare 蕾 ("bud"), which is an alias of 雷; 味蕾 is the alias of this word. 蕾 does not occur outside plant and body-part compounds, so it needs no word of its own.
