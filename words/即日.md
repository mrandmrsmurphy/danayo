## Notes
- [即](../characters/即%20(char).md) "immediately" + [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — no stand-in relationship applies (both characters are independent Dan'a'yo entries, each its own stand-in).

This is formal, business speech, as in "same day service." It is synonymous with [[当日]], but more professional.

**Real bugs found and fixed**: `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` had 即's syllable using a divergent form (jig/직/ㄐㄧㄎ) rather than its own authoritative reading (jǝg/즉/ㄐㄜㄎ, confirmed against 即's own character page and the sibling word [[即位]]) — corrected. `korean` had been contaminated with the synonym 当日's reading (당일) instead of 即日's own compositional reading — corrected to 즉일. Filled blank `vietnamese: tức nhật` (compositional). Japanese そくじつ is a standard reading. Removed the redundant `品詞` field. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄜㄎㄋㄧㄊ unique across both words and characters).
