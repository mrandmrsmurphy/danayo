## Notes
- [全 (char)](../characters/全%20(char).md) "whole; all; entire" — this word is the `stand_in` that legitimizes its own character, so the word and the character page are the same unit of meaning.

全 is the totalizing quantifier in Dan'a'yo: "whole; entire; complete," placed before a noun to say that the thing is taken in its entirety. Wiktionary lists for the Chinese character: "complete; whole; entire", "completely; wholly", "everything; all", "perfect; flawless", and "to preserve; to keep whole or intact"; the oldest sense, "pure jade", is obsolete in standalone Standard Chinese. The character is a 会意 of 入 ("enter") and a jade form (王 without its dot): jade brought in whole and unblemished, extended to "whole, complete". Wiktionary also lists 㒰 as an alternative form, which the vault treats as an alias of the character and never uses independently.

Among the vault's quantifiers, 全 is the "whole" word, not the collective "all" word: [[皆]] (with 皆事/皆人/皆処 and the rest of its correlative row) covers "all, collectively", and 全 is "the whole of one thing", as in [[全体]] "the whole; entirety", [[全部]] "everything; the entirety", [[全球]] "the whole globe" and [[完全]] "complete; perfect". [[一切]] is the abstract "everything without exception", and [[各]] is the distributive counterpart. The translations use it as a prefix on nouns, as in 全世紀 and 全世界 in translation/宣言書.md ("全世界氣運", the spirit of the whole world); these are not words of their own in the vault, just 全 plus a noun.

Cross-linguistic readings, as on the character page: Mandarin quán, Cantonese cyun4, North Korean 전, Japanese ぜん (Wiktionary gives ぜん as Go-on and せん as Kan-on; the character page lists ZEN and SEN) and Vietnamese toàn (Wiktionary also lists tuyền and tồn). Everyday native words for the same meaning are Japanese すべて and まったく, which are kun readings of the character, and Korean 모두 and 다, which are unrelated native words; the fields above hold only the Sino-Xenic readings. Examples of use in the sphere (from memory, not re-checked): Mandarin 全国 and 全面, Japanese 全部 and 全体, Korean 전부 and 전체, Vietnamese toàn bộ and hoàn toàn.
