## Notes
- [同](../characters/同.md) "same" + [族](../characters/族.md) "clan" — literally "same-clan."

同族 describes being of the same clan — a kinsman relationship rather than the group itself (contrast [[氏族]], the lineage-based clan-group noun, or [[部族]], the ethnographic "tribe"). This is one of four genuinely distinct clan-vocabulary terms already audited and cross-referenced on this vault's [[Kinship]] page (alongside [[氏族]], [[部族]], and [[閥族]]).

Mandarin tóngzú, Cantonese tung4 zuk6, Japanese どうぞく (dōzoku), and Korean 동족 (dongjok) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real everyday term in its language. Vietnamese đồng tộc is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, attested specifically in genealogical/historical contexts with a solemn, somewhat archaic register — matching 同's primary Hán Việt reading đồng (not the two Nôm readings, đang/đùng, also stored on its own page) and 族's own tộc. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 동족 matches Sino-Korean 동족 exactly. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄫㄐㄛㄎ unique among both words and characters).
