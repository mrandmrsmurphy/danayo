## Notes
- [明](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright, clear" + [族](../characters/族.md) "tribe, clan" — "the Bright Tribe, Issachar." Their own `stand_in` values: 明's is itself; 族's is [[家族]]; none points here, so 明族 is an independent compound.

明族 is the tribe of Issachar, Leah's fifth son. It is a Dan'a'yo **neologism**, "Bright Tribe," named for the men of Issachar who "understood the times" (1 Chronicles 12:32), not for the Hebrew meaning "reward." Compare [[福族]] (Asher) and [[港族]] (Zebulun). See [[基督敎]].

- Hebrew name: יִשָּׂשכָר (Yissakhar) (注音 ·ㄧㄙㄚㄏㄚㄌㄜ).

- See [基督敎](../lexipedia/基督敎.md).
