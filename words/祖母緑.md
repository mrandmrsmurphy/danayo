## Notes
- [祖](../characters/祖.md) "ancestor" + [母](../characters/母.md) "mother" + [緑](../characters/緑%20(char).md) "green" — "grandmother green," emerald. Their own `stand_in` values: 祖's is [[祖先]]; 母's is [[母親]]; 緑's is itself; none points here, so 祖母緑 is an independent compound.

祖母緑 is an emerald: the deep green gem, a variety of beryl ([[緑柱石]]). Mandarin zǔmǔlǜ (祖母綠) and Cantonese zou2 mou5 luk6 are the ordinary words. Japanese says エメラルド and Korean 에메랄드, so those two fields hold the character readings. Compare [[宝石]] (jewel).
