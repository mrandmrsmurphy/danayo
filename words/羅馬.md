## Notes
- [羅](../characters/羅.md) "Rome" + [馬 (char)](../characters/馬%20(char).md) "horse" — 羅's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 馬's own is [[馬]] itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 羅馬 legitimizes 羅 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 羅 is individually `kwin: false`.

羅馬 names Rome — usually followed by "city" (都市) or "the empire" (帝国) in compound usage.

Mandarin Luómǎ, Cantonese lo4 maa5, and Korean 로마 (roma) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 羅馬 is itself the common attested name for Rome in all three. Japanese uses the loanword ローマ (Rōma) rather than a Sino-Japanese reading. Vietnamese has three attested forms: Roma, Rôma (both direct European borrowings), and La Mã (the older Sino-Vietnamese-adjacent form). No Dan'a'yo homophones.
