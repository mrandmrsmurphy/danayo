## Notes
- [火 (char)](../characters/火%20(char).md) "fire" + [曜](../characters/曜.md) "day of the week" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — literally "fire-day," Tuesday, following the Sino-Japanese seven-day planetary-week naming shared across the sphere.

Japanese かようび (kayōbi) and Korean 화요일 are both standard, fully compositional readings, matching 火's, 曜's, and 日's own stored citations exactly. Mandarin xīngqī'èr and Cantonese sing1 kei4 ji6 instead use the real, everyday 星期 ("week") + number system (星期二, "week-day two," already recorded in this word's own `aliases`) rather than the planetary-element naming — the `mandarin`/`cantonese` fields hold that real term, not a reading of 火曜日's own characters, the same pattern seen throughout this vault's coined vocabulary. Vietnamese thứ ba ("day three") is likewise the real native word, using Vietnamese's own weekday-numbering convention rather than either system.

`kwin` is false: 火 alone is kwin-true, but 曜 independently diverges from Korean on its own character page, which alone is enough to make the AND-rule false. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⺢·⼄ㄨㄋㄧㄊ unique among both words and characters).
