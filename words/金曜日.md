## Notes
- [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal, gold" + [曜](../characters/曜.md) "day of the week" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — literally "gold-day," Friday, following the Sino-Japanese seven-day planetary-week naming shared across the sphere.

Mandarin jīnyàorì, Cantonese gam1 jiu6 jat6, and Japanese きんようび (kin'yōbi) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 금요일 uses 金's regular "gold, metal" reading (금), distinct from the character's other well-attested reading 김, used specifically when 金 functions as the Korean surname "Kim" — a genuine heteronym, not a Dan'a'yo-internal inconsistency. `kwin` is false as a direct result: Dan'a'yo's own 金-derived syllable (김) happens to coincide with the surname reading rather than the "gold" reading this word actually needs, so it diverges from the real Korean 금. Vietnamese thứ sáu ("day six") is unrelated in form — Vietnamese numbers its weekdays natively rather than following the planetary-element naming this compound uses, so it's the real attested word, not a compositional guess.

No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄇ·⼄ㄨㄋㄧㄊ unique among both words and characters).
