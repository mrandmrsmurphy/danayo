## Notes
- [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal, gold" + [曜](../characters/曜.md) "day of the week" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — literally "gold-day," Friday, following the Sino-Japanese seven-day planetary-week naming shared across the sphere.

Japanese きんようび (kin'yōbi) is a standard, fully compositional reading. Mandarin xīngqīwǔ and Cantonese sing1 kei4 ng5 use the real 星期 ("week") + number system (星期五 is recorded in `aliases`) rather than a reading of 金曜日's own characters, the same as every other weekday. Korean 금요일 uses 金's regular "gold, metal" reading (금), distinct from the character's other well-attested reading 김, used specifically when 金 functions as the Korean surname "Kim" — a genuine heteronym, not a Dan'a'yo-internal inconsistency. Vietnamese thứ sáu ("day six") is unrelated in form — Vietnamese numbers its weekdays natively rather than following the planetary-element naming this compound uses, so it's the real attested word, not a compositional guess.
