## Notes
- [偈](../characters/偈.md) "Buddhist verse" + [陀](../characters/陀.md) "slope; uneven" — transliteration of Sanskrit गाथा (gāthā), a verse form used in Buddhist scripture. `japanese: げだ` and `vietnamese: kệ đà` are the honest compositional readings (偈's own げ/kệ + 陀's own だ/đà); no independently attested loanword compounds were found for either. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 걷다 diverges entirely from the compositional Sino-Korean 게타. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄝㄊㄉㄚ unique among both words and characters).

This word is the `stand_in` legitimizer for both 偈 and 陀 (transitivity holds: A=B=AB, confirmed via both character pages' own `stand_in` fields), hence the `#cranberry` tag.
