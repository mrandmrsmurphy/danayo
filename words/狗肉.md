## Notes
- [狗](../characters/狗.md) "hound" + [肉 (char)](../characters/肉%20(char).md) "meat" — no stand-in relationship applies (狗's own stand-in is [[../lookup/List of 名専字]]; 肉's own is itself).

狗肉 means dog meat, and is also used as an insult.

Mandarin gǒuròu, Cantonese gau2 juk6, Japanese くにく, Korean 구육 and Vietnamese cẩu nhục are all fully compositional.
