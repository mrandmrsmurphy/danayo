## Notes
- [即 (char)](../characters/即%20(char).md) "ascend" + [位](../characters/位.md) "throne" + [式](../characters/式.md) "ceremony"

即位式 is a coronation, an enthronement ceremony. English Wiktionary has an entry only for Japanese 即位式 (そくいしき), glossed "enthronement ceremony, coronation". The Mandarin, Cantonese and Korean 즉위식 fields are built from the readings of the three characters. It is [即位](即位.md), to ascend the throne, with the semi-productive suffix [式](../characters/式.md) added for the ceremony, as in [儀式](儀式.md). Because 式 is semi-productive, this derivative has its own page.
