## Notes
- [侵](../characters/侵.md) "invade, encroach, raid, trespass, violate" + [犯](../characters/犯.md) "crime" — "to intrude upon, violate, invade" (national borders, rights, privacy, territory: 侵犯人權, "to violate human rights"; 侵犯領土, "to invade/violate territory"). Mandarin qīnfàn, Cantonese cam1 faan6, Japanese しんぱん, Korean 침범, and Vietnamese xâm phạm are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 침밤 diverges from Sino-Korean 침범 in the second syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄇㄅㄚㄇ unique among both words and characters).
