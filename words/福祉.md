福祉 means welfare or well-being, especially in the institutional sense of social welfare and public assistance programs (社会福祉, "social welfare"; 福祉国家, "welfare state") — a more formal, administrative register than 幸福's everyday "happiness."

Mandarin fúzhǐ, Cantonese fuk1 zi2, Japanese ふくし (fukushi, the standard term for social welfare/services), and Korean 복지 (bokji, equally standard, as in 복지국가 "welfare state") are all fully compositional readings. Vietnamese phúc chỉ is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form; everyday Vietnamese more often uses phúc lợi ("welfare, benefits").
