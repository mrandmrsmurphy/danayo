## Notes
- [姻](../characters/姻.md) "marriage ties (in-laws)" + [弟 (char)](../characters/弟%20(char).md) "younger brother" — affinal younger brother.

姻弟 is the hypernym of the younger in-law brothers: any younger brother by marriage, covering [[妹婿]], [[妻弟]] and [[夫弟]]. It is "affinal younger brother," the pair of [[姻兄]]. No entry was found on English Wiktionary; it is a schema word that gives the in-law set a general term of each age. It is a schema word in the family of in-law terms built from the marriage relation and the sibling roots, coined for predictability rather than a claim about ancient usage.
