## Notes
- [甲 (char)](../characters/甲%20(char).md) "shell, armor" + [板](../characters/板.md) "board, plank" — deck.

甲板 is a deck: the floor of a ship's upper surface. It is "armor-board," originally the armored plank-covering of a hull. Mandarin jiǎbǎn and Cantonese gaap3 baan2 are the Chinese readings; Japanese reads it かんぱん (and also こうはん), and Korean 갑판 follows Japanese. Compare [[船尾]] (stern) and [[船首]] (bow).
