## Notes
- [学](../characters/学.md) "study, learning" + [科](../characters/科.md) "section, department" — "academic subject, department, major." **Important reversed-compound caveat**: `vietnamese: học khoa` is compositional from these same characters in this order, but the far more common Vietnamese term khoa học is the *reversed* compound 科学 ("science") — a different word entirely, not this one. `học khoa` itself is not independently confirmed as standard usage; everyday Vietnamese instead says ngành học or môn học (native) for "academic subject/major" (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 학콰 diverges from Sino-Korean 학과 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄎㄎ⺢ unique among both words and characters).
