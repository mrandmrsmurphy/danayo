## Notes
- [考](../characters/考.md) "consider, examine" + [慮](../characters/慮.md) "consider, worry" — `#cranberry`: both characters' own `stand_in` point to this exact compound (考's and 慮's alike), so transitivity holds (A=B=AB) and both are legitimized as independent Dan'a'yo entries specifically by this word. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

考慮 means to consider, regard, or think about.

Mandarin kǎolǜ, Cantonese haau2 leoi6, and Korean 고려 (goryeo) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 考慮 is itself a common attested word for "consider" in all three. Japanese かうりょ is the historical kana spelling of こうりょ (kōryo). Vietnamese khảo lự is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
