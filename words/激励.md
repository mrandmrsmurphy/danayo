## Notes
- [激](../characters/激.md) "incite, provoke" + [励](../characters/励.md) "encourage" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 励 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (激's own stand-in is [[刺激]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

激励 means to encourage, motivate.

Mandarin jīlì, Cantonese gik1 lai6, Japanese げきれい, Korean 격려, and Vietnamese khích lệ are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 激's and 励's own stored citations exactly), and also the real, standard everyday words for "encourage" in each language. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄝㄎㄌㄝ unique across both words and characters).
