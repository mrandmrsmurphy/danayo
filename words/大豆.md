## Notes
- [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big" + [豆 (char)](../characters/豆%20(char).md) "bean"

大豆 is the soybean: the bean of *Glycine max*, source of tofu, soy sauce and miso. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin dàdòu as "soybean" (it also records "broad bean; fava bean" in some dialects) and Japanese だいず as "soybean, soya". Cantonese daai6 dau6-2 matches, and Korean 대두 is the reading of the characters.

It is the [豆](豆.md), bean, behind [豆腐](豆腐.md), tofu, [醤油](醤油.md), soy sauce, [味噌](味噌.md), miso, [納豆](納豆.md), natto, and [豆乳](豆乳.md), soy milk.
