## Notes
- [科](../characters/科.md) "branch, section" + [挙 (char)](../characters/挙%20(char).md) "raise, example" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (科's is [[学科]], 挙's is [[挙]] itself), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

科挙 was the imperial civil service examination system used across imperial China (from the Sui dynasty until its abolition in 1905) and adopted, with local variations, in Vietnam, Korea (as 과거), and briefly considered in Japan — a merit-based system for selecting government officials through rigorous written examinations on the Confucian classics, rather than by birth or patronage. It profoundly shaped East Asian social mobility, education, and literary culture for over a millennium.

Mandarin kējǔ, Cantonese fo1 geoi2, Japanese かきょ (kakyo), and Korean 과거 (gwageo, the standard historical term, distinct from the more common homophone 과거 meaning "the past") are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese offers two attested terms: khoa cử (the direct compositional Sino-Vietnamese form, "examination science") and khoa bảng ("examination honors list"), both used historically for Vietnam's own imperial examination system under the Nguyễn and earlier dynasties.
