## Notes
- [同](../characters/同.md) "same" + [僚](../characters/僚.md) "colleague; companion"
- 同僚 is the stand-in word for [僚](../characters/僚.md): the character's meaning (colleague) is identical, but 僚 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 同僚 is its `stand_in` compound.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `cantonese` was missing a space (tung4liu4 → tung4 liu4). Filled blank `vietnamese: đồng liêu` (verified — a real, if somewhat formal/archaic, Sino-Vietnamese synonym of đồng nghiệp). Japanese どうりょう and Korean 동료 are both standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄫㄌ⼄ㄨ unique across both words and characters).
