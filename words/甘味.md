## Etymology
- [甘 (char)](../characters/甘%20(char).md) "sweet" + [味](../characters/味.md) "taste, experience" — no stand-in relationship applies (甘's own stand-in is itself; 味's own is [[味覚]]).

甘味 means sweetness, sweet taste.

Mandarin gānwèi, Cantonese gam1 mei6, and Korean 감미 were already correct and fully compositional. Japanese あまみ (甘's native あま + 味's native readings) is the real, standard Japanese word for "sweetness," used instead of a compositional on'yomi reading (which would be かんみ). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with cam vị (甘's own cam + 味's own vị). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/`aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄇㄇㄨㄧ unique across both words and characters).
