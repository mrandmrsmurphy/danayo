## Notes
- [甘 (char)](../characters/甘%20(char).md) "sweet" + [味](../characters/味.md) "taste, experience" — no stand-in relationship applies (甘's own stand-in is itself; 味's own is [[味覚]]).

甘味 means sweetness, sweet taste.

Mandarin gānwèi, Cantonese gam1 mei6, Korean 감미 and Vietnamese cam vị are compositional. Japanese あまみ, with the native readings of 甘 and 味, is the standard Japanese word for "sweetness", used in place of the on'yomi かんみ.
