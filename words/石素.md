## Notes
- A novel word coined for the [Periodic Table](lexipedia/Periodic%20Table.md), copying the etymology:
- [石 (char)](../characters/石%20(char).md) "stone" + [素](../characters/素.md) "element"

石素 is a Dan'a'yo-internal coinage for lithium, echoing the metal's real Chinese name 鋰/锂 (lǐ) — itself coined in the 19th century using a rock/mineral-radical character, reflecting lithium's discovery in mineral (rather than plant/animal) sources, unlike sodium/potassium which were first isolated from plant ash. This vault's 石素 makes that "stone element" etymology explicit and transparent rather than relying on a single specialized character.

Since 鋰/锂 (and by extension this coinage) is a modern chemical-elements neologism rather than a word with deep independent roots in each language, the other CJKV daughters largely converge on international, European-derived borrowings instead: Korean 리튬 (rityum) and Japanese リチウム (ricchiumu) both transliterate "lithium" directly, and Vietnamese liti likewise derives from the European name rather than any Sino-Vietnamese compound.
