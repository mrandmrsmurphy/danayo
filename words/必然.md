## Notes
- [必](../characters/必%20(char).md) "surely, definitely" + [然](../characters/然%20(char).md) "so" — no stand-in relationship applies (必/然 are both their own stand-ins).

必然 means inevitable or certain — bound to happen, following necessarily from the circumstances.

Mandarin bìrán, Cantonese bit1 jin4, Japanese ひつぜん, Korean 필연, and Vietnamese tất nhiên are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `cantonese` had 必's tone mistyped bit2→bit1 (matching 必's own citation). Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄧㄊㄋ⼶ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
