## Notes
- [損](../characters/損.md) "lose, damage" + [傷](../characters/傷.md) "wound, injure" — "damage and injury," to mar. Their own `stand_in` values: 損's is [[損失]]; 傷's is [[傷害]]; none points here, so 損傷 is an independent compound.

損傷 is to damage or mar: to harm something so that it is spoiled, and the damage itself. Mandarin sǔnshāng, Cantonese syun2 soeng1, Japanese そんしょう and Korean 손상 all use it, and Vietnamese tổn thương does too. Compare [[傷害]] (to wound, injure) and [[破]] (to break).
