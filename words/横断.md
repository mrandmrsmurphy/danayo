## Notes
- [横](../characters/横.md) "crossing horizontally" + [断](../characters/断.md) "cut off"
- [[横]] has boundedness 100 and cannot stand alone in Dan'a'yo; this word serves as its legitimizer, carrying the same meaning as the bare character.

The compound means literally to cut across: 横 is the horizontal or transverse direction and 断 is to cut off, so the word describes passing straight across something, as a road, river, continent or sea. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin héngduàn as "to cross; to be transverse" and lists 橫斷山脈 (the Hengduan Mountains), 橫斷步道 and 橫斷面 (cross-section) as derived terms. Cantonese waang4 dyun6, Japanese おうだん and Korean 횡단 are the corresponding readings of the same compound.

In Dan'a'yo the word is the general verb for crossing over or traversing, as placed on the [Movement](../lexipedia/Movement.md) page, and is distinguished on the [Water](../lexipedia/Water.md) page from [渡](渡.md), crossing a river or strait by boat.
