## Notes
- [妙 (char)](../characters/妙%20(char).md) "mysterious, subtle, exquisite" — used independently as a standalone adjective (妙案, "a brilliant idea/plan"; 妙技, "an exquisite skill/technique"), distinct from its bound use in compounds like [[奇妙]]. `vietnamese: diệu` is a directly attested standard Sino-Vietnamese reading with the same "marvelous, exquisite" sense (kỳ diệu, "wondrous").
