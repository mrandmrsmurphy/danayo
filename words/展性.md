## Notes
- [展](../characters/展.md) "spread, unfold" + [性](../characters/性.md) "nature, quality" — malleability.

展性 is the property of a metal that lets it be hammered or rolled into thin sheets without cracking; gold is the classic case, beaten into [[金箔]]. It pairs with [[延性]] (ductility, drawing into wire): 展 spreads across a surface, 延 stretches along a line. 性 "nature, quality" is the same suffix as in [[磁性]]. Mandarin zhǎnxìng, Japanese てんせい and Korean 전성 are the standard readings.
