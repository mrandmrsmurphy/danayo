## Notes
- [欠](../characters/欠%20(char).md) "yawn" + [伸](../characters/伸.md) "extend, stretch out" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [欠](../characters/欠%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 伸's own stand-in is [[伸長]].

欠伸 means to yawn and stretch.

Mandarin qiànshēn, Cantonese him3 san1, Korean 흠신, and Vietnamese khiếm thân are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 欠's and 伸's own stored citations exactly). Japanese あくび is a genuine irregular jukujikun (special whole-word reading), the standard Japanese word for "yawn," not a compositional けっしん — kept as-is. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field. No homophones (注音 ㄎ⼘ㄇㄙㄧㄋ unique across both words and characters).
