## Notes
- [猫 (char)](../characters/猫%20(char).md) "cat" + [頭 (char)](../characters/頭%20(char).md) "head" + [鷹 (char)](../characters/鷹%20(char).md) "hawk"

猫頭鷹 is the owl, literally "cat-head hawk." Mandarin (māotóuyīng) and Cantonese (maau1 tau4 jing1) share the compound. 鷹 alone is the hawk. The owl is the bird with a "cat head."
