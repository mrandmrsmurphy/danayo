## Notes
- [交](characters/交%20(char).md) "mix" + [流](characters/流.md) "flow" — a mingling flow, hence "exchange, interchange."

交流 is the standard, everyday word for exchange and interaction across the Sinosphere — 文化交流 (cultural exchange), 交流会 (exchange meeting/mixer) are common collocations. Mandarin jiāoliú, Cantonese gaau1 lau4, Japanese こうりゅう, Korean 교류, and Vietnamese giao lưu (itself extremely common, e.g. giao lưu văn hóa "cultural exchange") are all standard, fully compositional readings.
