## Notes
- [鍾](../characters/鍾%20(char).md) "alcohol bottle" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

鍾 means an alcohol bottle or wine vessel — historically also used as a unit of measure for grain.

Mandarin zhōng, Cantonese zung1, Japanese しょう, Korean 종, and Vietnamese chung are all standard, fully compositional readings. Part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read jong/종/ㄐㄛㄫ) with [[従]] ("obey, observe") and [[鐘]] ("bell").
