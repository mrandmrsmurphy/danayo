## Notes
- [余](../characters/余.md) "surplus" + [震](../characters/震.md) "shake, quake, excite, vibrate" — "aftershock." Directly attested and standard across all five languages (mandarin yúzhèn, japanese よしん, korean 여진, vietnamese dư chấn). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 요진 diverges from Sino-Korean 여진 via the AND-rule, since 余's own field independently diverges (despite 震 itself being `kwin: true`). No homophones (注音 ⼄ㄐㄧㄋ unique among both words and characters).
