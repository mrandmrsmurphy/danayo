## Notes
- [吝](../characters/吝.md) "petty, stingy" + [嗇](../characters/嗇.md) "miserly, stingy" — this word is the stand-in for both [吝](../characters/吝.md) and [嗇](../characters/嗇.md), neither of which can appear independently.

吝嗇 means miserly. It is a synonym-doubling compound: each half already carries the sense of stinginess, which is why neither stands alone in Dan'a'yo and this compound serves as the shared viable form of both.
