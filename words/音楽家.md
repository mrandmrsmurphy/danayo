## Notes
- [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" + [楽](../characters/楽.md) "music" + [家](../characters/家.md) "house; specialist"

音楽家 is a musician: a person who makes music as a calling. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yīnyuèjiā as "musician" and Japanese おんがくか as "musician". Korean 음악가 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese jam1 ngok6 gaa1 matches.

The art is [音楽](音楽.md), music. The person who writes it is the [作曲家](作曲家.md), composer, and the one who sings is the [歌手](歌手.md).
