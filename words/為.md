## Notes
- [為](characters/為%20(char).md) "namely" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 為 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

為 means "for, because of, to act as, namely" (as in 因為 "because," 作為 "to act as," 為了 "in order to").
