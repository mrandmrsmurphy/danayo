## Notes
- [衣](../characters/衣.md) "clothing" is legitimized by this exact compound (its own `stand_in`). [類](../characters/類.md) "kind, sort, category" is separately legitimized by [[種類]] — transitivity fails, no cranberry.

- 衣類 (いるい) is the ordinary Japanese collective noun for "clothing, clothes" as a category — the word one reaches for on a laundry label, a department-store directory, or a customs form, rather than any single garment. The Korean cognate 의류 (衣類) is likewise the standard word for "clothing" as an industry/category term (e.g. 의류업, "the clothing industry"), not for an individual item of dress. Mandarin/Cantonese readings here are compositional rather than independently attested dictionary headwords.
