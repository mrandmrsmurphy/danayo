## Notes
- [干](../characters/干.md) "shield" + [戈](../characters/戈.md) "dagger-axe" — this word is the stand-in for [戈](../characters/戈.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo. Literally "shield and dagger-axe," metonymically "weapons of war, arms," and by extension "warfare, the chaos of battle."

The metonymy survives directly in set phrases across the CJKV sphere — 化干戈為玉帛 ("turning weapons into jade and silk," i.e. converting hostility into peaceful relations) is a standing classical idiom in all four languages.

Japanese かんか, Korean 간과, and Vietnamese can qua are all standard, non-drifted readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄋㄍ⺢ unique across both words and characters).
