## Notes
- [防](../characters/防.md) "defend, protect, cover" + [御](../characters/御.md) "defend" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes 御 as a bound character (防's own stand-in is [[防護]]; transitivity fails, so no `#cranberry`).

防御 means to defend or protect, spanning military, medical, and everyday senses (defending a position, guarding against disease, playing defense in sport). Mandarin 防禦/防御 (fángyù), Cantonese 防禦 (fong4jyu6), Korean 방어 (bang-eo), and Sino-Vietnamese phòng ngự are all everyday, high-frequency words used identically across these registers — 防禦 is a near-synonym doublet of 防護 in Chinese, the two characters (禦/護) both independently meaning "protect."

Japanese 防御/防禦 (ぼうぎょ) likewise spans military and sporting contexts (防御力, "defense power," common in games; サッカーの防御, "defense in soccer"), contrasting with the offense-side term 攻撃 (kōgeki, "attack/offense") the same way Mandarin/Korean/Vietnamese pair 防禦/방어/phòng ngự against 攻撃/공격/tấn công.
