## Notes
- [聖](../characters/聖.md) "holy, sacred" + [書](../characters/書.md) "book" — "holy book," the Bible. Their own `stand_in` values: 聖's is [[神聖]]; 書's is [[書本]]; none points here, so 聖書 is an independent compound.

聖書 is the Bible as a common noun, and a holy book in general. Mandarin shèngshū, Cantonese sing3 syu1, Japanese せいしょ and Korean 성서 all use it, and Vietnamese thánh thư does too. In Dan'a'yo it is the common noun, where the Bible's own title is 聖経. Compare [[経典]] (scripture, a classic text) and [[福音]] (gospel).
