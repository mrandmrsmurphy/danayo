## Notes
- [妾](../characters/妾%20(char).md) "concubine" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

妾 means concubine — historically, a secondary wife or maidservant kept alongside a principal wife.

Mandarin qiè, Cantonese cip3, Japanese しょう, Korean 첩, and Vietnamese thiếp are all standard, fully compositional readings.
