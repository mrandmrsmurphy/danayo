## Notes
- [浸 (char)](../characters/浸%20(char).md) "immerse, dunk" + [食 (char)](../characters/食%20(char).md) "eat" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 浸's and 食's own stand-ins are themselves).

浸食 means to erode, corrode, siphon off.

Mandarin jìnshí, Japanese しんしょく, and Korean 침식 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 浸's and 食's own stored citations exactly. Fixed real bugs: `mandarin` had been doubled with the reading of the near-synonym alias 侵食 (jìnshí,qīnshí → jìnshí only); `cantonese` had been contaminated to cam1 sik6 (also from 侵's reading) instead of zam3 sik6, matching 浸's own citation (zam3). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with the compositional tẩm thực (浸's own tẩm + 食's own thực); real-world Vietnamese instead uses xâm thực, built on the alias 侵食. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄇㄙㄧㄎ unique across both words and characters).
