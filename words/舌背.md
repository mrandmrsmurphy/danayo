## Notes
- [舌](../words/舌.md) "tongue" (stand-in: itself) + [背](../words/背.md) "back" (stand-in: itself) — the back/dorsum of the tongue.

舌背 means the dorsal, back-of-tongue place of articulation. Mandarin shébèi and Japanese ぜつはい are both used in CJK phonetics for "tongue dorsum," matching this word's compositional reading. Cantonese sit3 bui3 is a straightforward compositional reading. Korean 설배 and Vietnamese thiệt bối are compositional only, not independently verified as standard — Korean phonetics more often says 설근 (tongue root) or 후설 (back of tongue).

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot**: the concatenated string `ㄙㄝㄊㄅㄛㄧ` admits two legal partitions — the intended 舌(ㄙㄝㄊ)+背(ㄅㄛㄧ), and a false alternate splitting 背's own syllable (ㄅㄛ+ㄧ) — so the dot at the true 舌|背 boundary is required: ㄙㄝㄊ·ㄅㄛㄧ.
