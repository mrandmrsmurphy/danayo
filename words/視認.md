## Notes
- [視 (char)](../characters/視%20(char).md) "look at, inspect" + [認](../characters/認.md) "recognize" — no stand-in relationship applies (視's own stand-in is itself; 認's own is [[認識]]).

視認 means visual recognition — to see something and recognize or confirm it, distinct from [[聴取]]'s aural counterpart ("to listen to, take in, hear out"). This vault's own `文法 - 01序文` pairs the two as complementary modes of comprehension (視認 or 聴取).

Japanese しにん is the real, standard technical term, familiar from aviation, driving, and security contexts (視認性, "visibility, legibility"; 視認距離, "visual range"). Mandarin shìrèn, Cantonese si6 jing6, and Vietnamese thị nhận are straightforward compositional readings from 視's and 認's own stored citations, not independently verified as standard terms in those languages — this appears to be primarily a Japanese technical coinage. Korean 시인 is likewise compositional; note that real spoken Korean 시인 is far more commonly encountered as 詩人 ("poet") — a genuine real-world homophone risk, though not a Dan'a'yo-internal one: 視認's own 注音 (ㄙㄧㄜ·ㄋㄧㄋ) is phonologically distinct from [[詩人]]'s (ㄙㄧ·ㄋㄧㄋ), since 視's Dan'a'yo reading carries an extra vowel 詩 doesn't have. `kwin` is false: both 視 and 認 are individually false at the character level.

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot**: the concatenated string `ㄙㄧㄜㄋㄧㄋ` admits three legal partitions — the intended `ㄙㄧㄜ`(視)+`ㄋㄧㄋ`(認), and two false alternates (`ㄙㄧ`+`ㄜ`+`ㄋㄧㄋ` and `ㄙㄧ`+`ㄜㄋ`+`ㄧㄋ`, both using real syllables but wrong word boundaries) — so the dot at the true 視|認 boundary is required to disambiguate: ㄙㄧㄜ·ㄋㄧㄋ.
