## Notes
- [視 (char)](../characters/視%20(char).md) "look at, inspect" + [認](../characters/認.md) "recognize" — no stand-in relationship applies (視's own stand-in is itself; 認's own is [[認識]]).

English Wiktionary has no entry for 視認. Japanese Wiktionary lists しにん as a noun and する-verb, "to see with the eyes and confirm" (English: visual contact). `文法 - 01序文` pairs it with [[聴取]] as the visual counterpart of aural comprehension (視認 or 聴取).
