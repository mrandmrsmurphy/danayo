## Notes
- [魚 (char)](../characters/魚%20(char).md) "fish" + [類](../characters/類.md) "kind, category" — no `#cranberry`: 魚's own `stand_in` is 魚 itself, 類's is [[種類]]; neither points here.

Mandarin yúlèi, Cantonese jyu4leoi6, Japanese ぎょるい, Korean 어류, and Sino-Vietnamese ngư loại are all standard, fully compositional readings for the biological category "fish."
