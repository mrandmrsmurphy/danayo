## Notes
- [腐](../characters/腐.md) "rot, decay" + [食 (char)](../characters/食%20(char).md) "eat, eat away" — corrosion.

腐食 names corrosion, the gradual eating-away of a metal by air, water or chemicals; rust ([[銹]]) is the corrosion of iron. 食 here is the figurative "to eat away at," as in 蝕. The Dan'a'yo headword is the Japanese 腐食; 腐蝕 (the Mandarin and traditional spelling, with 蝕 "to erode") is its alias. Mandarin fǔshí, Japanese ふしょく and Korean 부식 are the standard readings. Compare [[腐敗]] and [[腐朽]], which name rot and decay of organic matter rather than the chemical attack on a metal.
