神道教 is the more explicit, formal name for Shinto, Japan's indigenous polytheistic religion centered on the worship of *kami* (spirits/gods of nature, ancestors, and place) — distinct from [[神道]] alone, which this vault documents in its older classical-Chinese philosophical sense ("natural law"), even though 神道 by itself is by far the more common ordinary Japanese word for the religion. 教 here functions the same way it does in [[道教]] (Daoism), [[儒教]] (Confucianism), and [[仏教]] (Buddhism) — marking a named system of religious teaching.

Mandarin Shéndàojiào, Cantonese san4 dou6 gaau3, Japanese しんとうきょう (shintōkyō), Korean 신도교 (sindogyo), and Vietnamese Thần đạo giáo are all standard, fully compositional readings.
