## Notes
- [枢](characters/枢%20(char).md) "hinge, pivot" + [机](characters/机.md) "machine, mechanism" — no stand-in relationship applies (枢's own stand-in is itself; 机's own is [[机会]]).
- Literally "the pivotal mechanism" — the metaphorical core of Church governance. Used as the stem of [[枢机卿]] (cardinal), the full ecclesiastical title.

Mandarin shūjī, Cantonese syu1 gei1, Japanese すうき, Korean 추기, and Vietnamese khu cơ are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 枢's and 机's own stored citations exactly). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄨㄍㄧㄜ unique across both words and characters).
