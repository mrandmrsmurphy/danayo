## Notes
- [七 (char)](../characters/七%20(char).md) "seven" + [百](../characters/百%20(char).md) "hundred" — a plain compositional numeral, 7 × 100.

Japanese ななひゃく again substitutes なな for しち to avoid confusion with いち ("one") in spoken numerals, the same pattern already documented on [[七千]]/[[七万]]. Vietnamese thất bách is the plain compositional Hán Việt reading; everyday Vietnamese instead counts natively (bảy trăm), the Sino-Vietnamese form staying mostly literary/classical, the same register split already seen on this numeral's other multiples. `kwin` is false: both syllables diverge from their Sino-Korean counterparts (七's Dan'a'yo 칟 vs. Korean 칠; 百's Dan'a'yo 박 vs. Korean 백), the same recurring coda-divergence pattern documented throughout this stretch of the sweep. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄊㄅㄚㄎ unique among both words and characters).
