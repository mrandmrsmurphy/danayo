## Notes
- [大](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big" + [連](../characters/連%20(char).md) "mutual" — "big link". No stand-in relationship applies (both characters are their own stand-ins).

大連 (Dalian) is a sub-provincial port city at the southern tip of Liaoning province in northeastern China. Per Wiktionary the name ultimately comes from the Russian settlement name Dalny (Да́льній, "distant"), and a folk etymology links it to 褡褳, a type of rectangular pouch; the "big link" reading of the characters is only a gloss. 连 is an alias of 連, so 大连 is kept as the word's alias.

Japanese だいれん (older Western spelling Dairen), Korean 대련 and Vietnamese Đại Liên are the Sino-Xenic readings listed on Wiktionary; the usual South Korean name today is 다롄 (from memory, not re-checked). Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese entries. The Chinese entry also cross-refers to 旅大 for the former joint name with Lüshun. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄧㄌ⼶ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
