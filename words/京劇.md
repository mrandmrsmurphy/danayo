## Notes
- [京](../characters/京.md) "capital" + [劇 (char)](../characters/劇%20(char).md) "drama"

京劇 is Peking opera: the Chinese sung theatre that combines music, dance and acrobatics. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin jīngjù as "Peking opera; Beijing opera" and Japanese きょうげき as "Peking opera". Korean 경극 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese ging1 kek6 matches.

It is a form of the [戯曲](戯曲.md), Chinese opera, and the Western kind is the [歌劇](歌劇.md).
