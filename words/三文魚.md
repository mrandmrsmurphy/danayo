## Notes
- [三 (char)](../characters/三%20(char).md) "three" + [文](../characters/文.md) "culture" + [魚 (char)](../characters/魚%20(char).md) "fish"

三文魚 is salmon. Wiktionary describes it as probably a transcription of English "salmon" plus 魚, coming in through Cantonese or Shanghainese, so 三文 only approximates the sound and has nothing to do with "three" or "culture." Mandarin sānwényú and Cantonese saam1 man4 jyu4 share it, and it is the salmon word of Hong Kong, Malaysia, and Shanghai. Taiwan prefers 鮭魚 (guīyú). Japanese and Korean have their own native words for salmon rather than this compound.
