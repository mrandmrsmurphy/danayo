## Notes
- [協](../characters/協.md) "cooperate, work together" + [会](../characters/会%20(char).md) "meet, gather" — no stand-in relationship applies (協's own stand-in is [[協力]]; 会 is its own stand-in).

協会 means an association or society — an organized group formed for a shared purpose.

Japanese きょうかい, Korean 협회, and Vietnamese hiệp hội are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄝㄆㄏ⼔ unique across both words and characters).
