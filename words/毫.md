## Notes
- [毫](../characters/毫%20(char).md) "fine hair; milli-"

毫 means fine hair; by technical extension, the SI prefix milli-.

Mandarin háo, Cantonese hou4, Korean 호, and Vietnamese hào are all standard, fully compositional readings.
