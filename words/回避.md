## Notes
- [回 (char)](../characters/回.md) "return, turn" (stand-in: itself) + [避 (char)](../characters/避.md) "avoid, evade" (stand-in: itself) — to avoid, steer clear of.

回避 means to avoid, as invoked by `文法 - 02音韻論`'s 回避すべき異音 ("allophones that should be avoided"). Korean 회피 is a real, standard term, though note the real Mandarin/Cantonese order is usually reversed (避け, 逃避, or 回避 itself which does exist as a real Mandarin term huíbì — matching compositionally). Cantonese wui4 bei6 is a straightforward compositional reading. Vietnamese hồi tị is compositional only. `kwin` is false: inherited from 避 (false), despite 回 itself being true.

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot**: the concatenated string `ㄏㄛㄧㄅㄝ` admits two legal partitions — the intended 回(ㄏㄛㄧ)+避(ㄅㄝ), and a false alternate splitting 回's own syllable (ㄏㄛ+ㄧ) — so the dot at the true 回|避 boundary is required: ㄏㄛㄧ·ㄅㄝ.
