## Notes
- [回 (char)](../characters/回%20(char).md) "return, turn" + [避 (char)](../characters/避%20(char).md) "avoid, evade" — to avoid, steer clear of.

回避 means to avoid. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 迴避 (alias form here; Mandarin huíbì, Cantonese wui4 bei6) as "to avoid, dodge, evade, shun" and, legal, "to withdraw; to recuse oneself", and Japanese かいひ as "evasion (of responsibility, etc.)", "avoidance of a negative outcome", "to evade" and "to avoid". It lists Korean 회피 as a hanja form. The Vietnamese hồi tị field is built from the readings of the two characters. The phonology page [文法 - 02音韻論](../grammar/文法%20-%2002音韻論.md) uses it of avoided allophones.
