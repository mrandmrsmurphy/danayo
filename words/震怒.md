## Notes
- [震](../characters/震.md) "shake, quake, excite, vibrate" + [怒 (char)](../characters/怒%20(char).md) "angry" — no `#cranberry`: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here (震's is [[震動]], 怒's is 怒 itself).

震怒 describes an intense, thunderous rage — traditionally the wrath of an emperor, deity, or other exalted figure, evoking anger powerful enough to shake the earth. Mandarin zhènnù, Cantonese zan3nou6, Japanese しんど (shindo), Korean 진노 (jinno), and Sino-Vietnamese chấn nộ all preserve this same elevated, formal register and meaning across every daughter language.
