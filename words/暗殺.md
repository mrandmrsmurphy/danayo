## Notes
- [暗](../characters/暗%20(char).md) "dark" + [殺](../characters/殺%20(char).md) "kill" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 暗 and 殺's own stand-ins are themselves).

暗殺 means to assassinate or murder.

Mandarin ànshā, Cantonese am3 saat3, Japanese あんさつ, Korean 암살, and Vietnamese ám sát are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄇㄙㄚㄊ unique across both words and characters).
