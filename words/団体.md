## Notes
- [団](../characters/団.md) "group; ball" + [体](../characters/体.md) "body"

団体 is a group or an organization: people acting together as one body. Wiktionary glosses Japanese だんたい as "an organization; a group". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 단체 matches.

It is a formal, organized body, such as a club or a union, where [集団](集団.md) is any group and [共同体](共同体.md) is a community. A body of the kind that people join is an [協会](協会.md), an association.
