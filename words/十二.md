## Notes
- [十](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" + [二](../characters/二%20(char).md) "two" — no stand-in relationship applies (both characters are independent Dan'a'yo entries, each its own stand-in).

十二 means twelve.

Japanese じゅうに, Korean 십이, and Vietnamese thập nhị are all standard, fully compositional readings.
