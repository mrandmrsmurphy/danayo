## Notes
- [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year" + [歳](../characters/歳.md) "year of age" — this word is the stand-in for [歳](../characters/歳.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

年歳 is a classical/literary doublet for "years, age" — 年 and 歳 both independently name a year, a distinction rooted in ancient Chinese usage (the Zhou dynasty preferred 年, the earlier Xia dynasty 歳, for the same unit of time). In modern Japanese, 年歳 survives mainly in literary or archaic contexts (年月 "years and months," 年齢 "age" being the everyday equivalent) rather than everyday speech.

Japanese ねんさい, Korean 년세, and Vietnamese niên tuế (a real compound, though 歳/tuế rarely stands alone and more often appears in set phrases like 歳月/tuế nguyệt "years and months") are all standard, if somewhat literary, readings.
