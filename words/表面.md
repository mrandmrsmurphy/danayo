## Notes
- [表](../characters/表.md) "show, indicate" + [面](../characters/面.md) "face, surface" — the outer, visible face of something; extended to "surface" generally; this word is the stand-in that legitimizes the bound character [面](../characters/面.md), which cannot appear independently.

Mandarin/Cantonese biǎomiàn/biu2min6 and Korean 표면 all keep this as the ordinary word for "surface," used just as readily in casual speech as in physics or geography. Korean also has two native alternatives in everyday use — 겉 and 거죽 — both meaning "the outside/outer layer," with 거죽 leaning toward a rougher or more concrete texture (an animal hide, tree bark) than the more abstract 표면. Vietnamese breaks from the Sino root entirely in ordinary usage: bề mặt (native "side" + "face") is the standard word for "surface" in every register, technical included, while biểu diện — the literal Sino-Vietnamese reading of 表面 — is essentially a dictionary-only form, not used in real speech or writing.
English Wiktionary gives the Chinese noun two senses: "surface; face; external appearance" (表面光澤, "lustrous surface") and "the superficial aspect; the surface of a problem". It lists dialect synonyms 面頭 (Cantonese), 皮面 (Sichuan) and 表面子 (Xiang), and the derived terms 表面化, 表面積, 表面張力 and 表面上, among others. Its Japanese entry gives "surface, face", the Korean 표면 is the same, and the Sino-Vietnamese descendant is listed as biểu diện, which the paragraph above says is dictionary-only. The Shape page lists 表面 as the everyday, general word and 上面 as a more specific one.

Mandarin biǎomiàn and Cantonese biu2 min6 are as at English Wiktionary.

No other word shares the 注音 ㄅ⼘ㄨㄇ⼶ㄋ (checked 2026-10-12).
