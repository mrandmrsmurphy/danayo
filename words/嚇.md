## Notes
- [嚇 (char)](../characters/嚇%20(char).md) "scare; frighten"

嚇 means "to scare, frighten, threaten" (恐嚇, "to threaten, intimidate"; 威嚇, "to threaten by force"). 嚇 is polysemous: this sense (Middle Chinese departing tone) is distinct from a separate "to be furious, rage" sense tied to a different Middle Chinese tone class.

Mandarin xià and Cantonese haak3 preserve the sense directly. Japanese カク/カ (KAKU/KA, on'yomi, formal register) alongside native おど(す) (odo(su), "to threaten, intimidate," the everyday verb) cover the sense. Korean 하 (ha) parallels the departing-tone Sino-xenic reading specifically (恐嚇, 공하, gongha, "to threaten"; 威嚇, 위하, wiha) — a separate reading, 혁 (hyeok), covers the unrelated "furious, rage" sense instead, from a different Middle Chinese tone. Vietnamese hách is attested as the Hán Việt reading in Hán Nôm dictionary sources (khủng hách 恐嚇, uy hách 威嚇), but these are essentially dictionary-only constructions rather than living Vietnamese vocabulary — modern Vietnamese expresses "to threaten, scare" with đe dọa, hăm dọa, or dọa nạt instead. The character's semantic thread survives in living Vietnamese only in a narrowed form, as the adjective "haughty, overbearing, imposing" (hống hách, "domineering"; hách dịch, "high-handed"), documented here rather than presented as if hách were still a live verb for "to threaten."

This word shares its reading (hag/학/ㄏㄚㄎ) with [[核]] ("nucleus; pit") and [[鶴]] ("crane") — the 3-way group is now fully cross-referenced on all three pages.
