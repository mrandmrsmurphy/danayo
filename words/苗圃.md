## Notes
- [苗](../characters/苗.md) "seedling" + [圃](../characters/圃.md) "seedbed"
- This word is the viable form of [圃](../characters/圃.md): the character's meaning (seedbed, nursery) is identical, but 圃 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 苗圃 is its `stand_in` compound.

苗圃 is a plant nursery or seedbed: a plot where young plants (苗, "seedlings") are raised before being transplanted into the fields, an orchard or a forest. Mandarin miáopǔ, Cantonese miu4 pou2, Japanese びょうほ (byōho; the everyday Japanese is more often 苗畑, なえばたけ) and Korean 묘포 (myopo) are the same compound, mostly used in forestry and horticulture. The bound character 圃 is "a garden plot, a field for vegetables or seedlings".

In Dan'a'yo it is the cultivation-site word in Plants, the place where [[種子]] become transplantable seedlings. No narrower or shifted sense is needed.
