## Notes
- [喇](../characters/喇.md) "horn" + [叭](../characters/叭.md) "trumpet sound" — both characters share this word as their own `stand_in`, appearing only bound together in Dan'a'yo.

喇叭 means a horn or trumpet — a brass wind instrument, or by extension any horn-like noisemaker (a car horn, a loudspeaker).
