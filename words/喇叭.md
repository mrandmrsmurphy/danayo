## Notes
- [喇](../characters/喇.md) "horn" + [叭](../characters/叭.md) "trumpet sound" — both characters share this word as their own `stand_in`, appearing only bound together in Dan'a'yo.

喇叭 means a horn or trumpet — a brass wind instrument, or by extension any horn-like noisemaker (a car horn, a loudspeaker).

**Real bug found and fixed**: `vietnamese` had an extra tone diacritic ("lạt bát" → lạt bá, matching 叭's own citation). Korean 나발 is a genuine native/colloquial Korean word for horn (喇's own `korean_native` field even records the closely related 나팔) rather than a compositional Sino-reading — expected, not an error (`kwin: false`). Japanese らっぱ and Mandarin lǎba/Cantonese laa3 baa1 are standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄚㄅㄚㄊ unique across both words and characters).
