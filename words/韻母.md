## Notes
- [韻](../characters/韻.md) "rhyme" + [母](../characters/母.md) "mother, base" — in traditional Chinese phonology, the part of a syllable remaining after the initial consonant (声母) is removed: the medial, nucleus, and coda taken together, corresponding to the linguistic notion of "rime." Attested across Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese (いんぼ), Korean (운모), and Vietnamese (vận mẫu) as a technical term of historical phonology. No `#cranberry`: 韻's own `stand_in` is [[押韻]], 母's is [[母親]]; neither points here.

**Fixed a real bug**: `cantonese` had `wan5` instead of 韻's own stored jyutping `wan6` — corrected to `wan6 mou5`.
