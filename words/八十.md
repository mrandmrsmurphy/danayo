## Notes
- [八 (char)](../characters/八%20(char).md) "eight" + [十 (char)](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" — "eighty." Mandarin bāshí, Cantonese baat3 sap6, Japanese はちじゅう, Korean 팔십, and Vietnamese bát thập are all directly attested standard readings (modern Vietnamese more commonly uses the native tám mươi in everyday counting). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 받십 diverges from Sino-Korean 팔십 in the first syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄊㄙㄧㄆ unique among both words and characters).
