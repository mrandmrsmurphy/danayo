## Etymology
- [軍](../characters/軍.md) "armed forces" + [隊 (char)](../characters/隊%20(char).md) "group, team"

## Notes
Mandarin jūnduì, cantonese gwan1 deoi6-2, japanese ぐんたい, korean 군대, and vietnamese quân đội are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 군되 diverges from Sino-Korean 군대 in the second syllable's final. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄨㄋㄉㄛㄧ unique among both words and characters).

軍's own `stand_in` is this exact word (軍隊), so 軍 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 軍隊; 隊's own `stand_in` is instead 隊 itself, so this word does not legitimize 隊. Both citations already correctly present on 軍.md and 隊(char).md.
