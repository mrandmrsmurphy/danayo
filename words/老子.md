## Notes
- [老 (char)](../characters/老%20(char).md) "old" + [子](../characters/子.md) "child" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (老's is [[老]] itself, 子's is [[児子]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

老子 names Laotzi (Lao Tzu), the legendary founder of Taoism and putative author of the Tao Te Ching; the term can also mean "old man" generically.

Mandarin Lǎozǐ, Cantonese lou5 zi2, and Korean 노자 (noja) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 老子 is itself the common attested name for the philosopher in all three. Japanese おいご is a native reading. Vietnamese Lão Tử is the standard Sino-Vietnamese name. **Fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字`/`諺文` had lyaujǝ/럇즈 (mismatching 老's real lau/랏, the same bug just fixed on [[老人学]]) — `注音` (ㄌㄚㄨㄐㄜ) had stayed correct throughout. No homophones.
