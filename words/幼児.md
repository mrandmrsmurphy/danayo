## Notes
- [幼](../characters/幼.md) "young; immature" + [児 (char)](../characters/児%20(char).md) "child" — this word is the stand-in for [幼](../characters/幼.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

幼児 names a young child, specifically an infant or toddler (developmentally distinct from 児童, "school-age child," and 幼稚, "infantile/childish" as an adjective).

Japanese ようじ, Korean 유아, and Vietnamese ấu nhi are all standard, non-drifted readings. No homophones (注音 ⼜ㄛㄝㄧ unique across both words and characters).
