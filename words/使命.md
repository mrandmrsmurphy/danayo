## Notes
- [使](../characters/使.md) "send" + [命](../characters/命.md) "order"

使命 is a mission, an assigned task. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 使命 (Mandarin shǐmìng, Cantonese si3 ming6) as, diplomatic and figurative, "mission; set task; assignment" and Japanese しめい as "mission, errand, message". It lists Korean 사명 and Vietnamese entries.
