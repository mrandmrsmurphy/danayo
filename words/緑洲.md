## Notes
- [緑](../characters/緑%20(char).md) "green" + [洲](../characters/洲%20(char).md) "islet, continent" — "green islet," a patch of green in the desert. Both characters are their own `stand_in`, so 緑洲 is an independent compound.

緑洲 is an oasis: a fertile spot in a desert where water allows plants to grow. Mandarin lǜzhōu and Cantonese luk6 zau1 are the ordinary Chinese word; Japanese uses the loanword オアシス, so the Japanese and Korean fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[沙漠]] (desert) and [[三角洲]] (delta).
