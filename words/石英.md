## Notes
- [石 (char)](../characters/石%20(char).md) "rock" + [英](../characters/英.md) "hero, splendor" — a name evoking a particularly fine or "outstanding" kind of stone, rather than a literal translation of "quartz."

石英 is the standard mineralogical term for quartz, one of the most abundant minerals in Earth's crust, valued for jewelry (rock crystal, amethyst, citrine are all quartz varieties), as a key component of sand and granite, and for its piezoelectric properties in electronics (quartz crystal oscillators, 石英時計/水晶手表, "quartz watch").

Mandarin shíyīng, Cantonese sek6 jing1, Japanese せきえい (sekiei), Korean 석영 (seogyeong), and Vietnamese thạch anh are all fully compositional and are the standard scientific terms across the sphere.
