## Notes
- [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" + [律](../characters/律.md) "statute, principle, regulation, discipline" — no `#cranberry`: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here (音's is [[音楽]], 律's is [[法律]]).

音律 (yīnlǜ) is a real Mandarin word for musical temperament, pitch, or metrical rhythm, and Cantonese 音律 (jam1leot6) likewise. Japanese おんりつ (onritsu) and Korean 음률 (eumnyul — no 렬/률→열/율 shift here, since 음 ends in ㅁ rather than a vowel or ㄴ) are both standard terms for musical scale/temperament in their languages. Sino-Vietnamese âm luật is likewise a real, attested compound with the same sense.
