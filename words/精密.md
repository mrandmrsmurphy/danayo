## Notes
- [精](../characters/精.md) "refined, essence" + [密 (char)](../characters/密%20(char).md) "dense, close" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (精's is [[精神]], 密's is [[密]] itself), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false`: both constituents are individually `kwin: true`, but the Dan'a'yo concatenation (정믿) genuinely diverges from real Sino-Korean 정밀 in 密's second syllable — a real, documented divergence, not an error.

精密 means precise, meticulous, or exact — exactness and meticulous attention to detail, leaving no aspect unattended (精密機械, "precision machinery"; 精密検査, "detailed medical examination").

Mandarin jīngmì, Cantonese zing1 mat6, Japanese せいみつ (seimitsu), and Korean 정밀 (jeongmil) are all standard, fully compositional readings, each the ordinary everyday word for "precise, meticulous" in its language. Vietnamese tinh mật is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form; everyday Vietnamese more often uses chính xác ("accurate, precise").
