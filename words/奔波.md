## Etymology
- [奔](../characters/奔.md) "run around" + [波](../characters/波.md) "wave"

## Notes
- "To rush about, run around, toil busily" (often traveling from place to place). `vietnamese: bôn ba` is a directly attested, well-known standard Vietnamese term with the same "toil and travel about" sense (bôn ba vất vả). `korean: 분주하다` documents the real, if differently-built, Korean equivalent (奔走, "busy, bustling," a different second character) rather than a mechanical calque of 奔波 itself. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 본바 diverges from 분주하다 entirely — not a meaningful comparison, since Korean lexicalizes a different compound. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄛㄋㄅㄚ unique among both words and characters).
