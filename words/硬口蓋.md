## Notes
- [硬](../words/硬.md) "hard" (stand-in: itself) + [口](../words/口.md) "mouth" (stand-in: itself) + [蓋](../words/蓋.md) "cover, lid" (stand-in: [[覆蓋]]) — the hard palate; the place of articulation for palatal sounds.

硬口蓋 names the hard palate — the place of articulation behind the alveolar ridge, used for `文法 - 02音韻論`'s palatal consonants (ㄐ, ㄑ, ㄧ). Japanese こうこうがい and Korean 경구개 are both real, standard anatomical/phonetics terms, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Mandarin yìngkǒugài and Cantonese ngaang6 hau2 koi3 are straightforward compositional readings — Mandarin phonetics more often says 硬腭 (yìng'è), so this is not independently verified as the standard Mandarin term. Vietnamese ngạnh khẩu cái is likewise compositional only; the real Vietnamese term is "ngạc cứng" / "khẩu cái cứng," built from different vocabulary. 口's and 蓋's own character pages store no `japanese`/`vietnamese` fields — こう/がい and khẩu/cái above are the real, independently-known Sino-Japanese/Sino-Vietnamese readings for those characters, supplied directly since the character pages left them blank. `kwin` is false: inherited from 硬 and 口 (both false), despite 蓋 itself being true.

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot**: the concatenated string `ㄚㄫㄎㄛㄨㄍㄚㄧ` admits multiple legal partitions, all of which agree 硬 occupies the first syllable but which disagree on whether 口's own `ㄎㄛㄨ` and 蓋's own `ㄍㄚㄧ` each stay whole or split internally — so dots at both true character boundaries are required: ㄚㄫ·ㄎㄛㄨ·ㄍㄚㄧ.
