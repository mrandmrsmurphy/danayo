## Notes
- [硬](../words/硬.md) "hard" (stand-in: itself) + [口](../words/口.md) "mouth" (stand-in: itself) + [蓋](../characters/蓋.md) "cover, lid" (stand-in: [[覆蓋]]) — the hard palate; the place of articulation for palatal sounds.

Wiktionary: Chinese 硬口蓋 is an anatomy term, a synonym of 硬顎, "hard palate"; Japanese 硬口蓋 (こうこうがい) is "a hard palate"; Korean 경구개 is its hanja form. Used in `文法 - 02音韻論` as a place of articulation for the palatal consonants.
