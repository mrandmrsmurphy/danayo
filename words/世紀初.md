## Notes
- [世](../characters/世.md) "generation, world" + [紀](../characters/紀.md) "century, record" + [初](../characters/初.md) "beginning" — no `#cranberry`.

Mandarin shìjìchū, Cantonese sai3 gei2 co1, Japanese せいきしょ, and Korean 세기초 are all standard compositional readings. Vietnamese đầu thế kỷ ("beginning century," native word-order-reversed) follows the same pattern already established on sibling [[世紀中]]'s giữa thế kỷ.
