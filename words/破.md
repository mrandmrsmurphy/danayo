## Notes
- [破](../characters/破%20(char).md) "rend, break" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 破 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

破 means to break, rend, or destroy, and is a highly productive verbal element: 破壊 ("destruction, demolition"), 破裂 ("rupture, burst"), 破綻 ("bankruptcy, breakdown"), 破戒 ("to break a religious commandment/vow"). It often carries a connotation of forceful or violent breaking, as opposed to gentler words for damage or wear.

Mandarin pò, Cantonese po3, Japanese は (ha), Korean 파 (pa), and Vietnamese vỡ are all standard readings, though Vietnamese vỡ is the everyday native word for "to break/shatter" rather than a Sino-Vietnamese borrowing.
