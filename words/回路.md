## Notes
- [回](../characters/回%20(char).md) "turn" + [路](../characters/路.md) "road, path" — no stand-in relationship applies (回 is its own stand-in; 路's own is [[道路]]).

回路 means a circuit or loop — an electrical circuit, or figuratively a return path.

Filled blank `vietnamese: hồi lộ` (compositional). Japanese かいろ and Korean 회로 are both standard readings, matching Dan'a'yo's own reading exactly (`kwin: true`). **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 回路 and [[賄賂]] ("bribe") share an identical reading — standardized the reciprocal callout on both pages.
