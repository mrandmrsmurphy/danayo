## Notes
- [第 (char)](../characters/第%20(char).md) "-th (ordinal prefix)" + [十 (char)](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten"

第十 is the tenth in a series. The prefix 第 turns cardinal numbers into ordinals across all CJKV languages; 第十 follows the same pattern as [[第一]], [[第二]], [[第八]] and the rest.
