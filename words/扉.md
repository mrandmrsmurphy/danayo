## Notes
- [扉](../characters/扉%20(char).md) "front door; cover" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [扉](../characters/扉%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

扉 means front door or cover.

Mandarin fēi, Cantonese fei1, Japanese ひ, Korean 비, and Vietnamese phi are all standard, fully compositional readings. Not a Dan'a'yo-level homophone despite sharing 注音 ㄈㄨㄧ with the bound characters [[斐]] and [[緋]]: 斐 cannot stand alone (its stand-in [[斐然]] has a different multi-syllable reading, ㄈㄨㄧㄋ⼶ㄋ), and 緋 is a name-only character (`stand_in: 名専字`) with no independent word at all.
