Stand-in for [[烏]]

## Notes
- [烏](../characters/烏.md) "crow, raven" + [鳥 (char)](../characters/鳥%20(char).md) "bird" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 烏 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (鳥's own stand-in is itself, so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

烏鳥 means crow, raven.

Japanese からす and Korean 까마귀 are the real, standard native words for "crow" in each language — the same real-world-species-name convention already established on [[龍蝦]]/[[水牛]]/[[河馬]].
