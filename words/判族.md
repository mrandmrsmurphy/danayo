## Notes
- [判](../characters/判.md) "judge" + [族](../characters/族.md) "tribe, clan" — "the Judging Tribe, Dan." Their own `stand_in` values: 判's is [[判断]]; 族's is [[家族]]; none points here, so 判族 is an independent compound.

判族 is the tribe of Dan, the first son of Rachel's maid Bilhah, whose name means "he judged": "God has judged me" (Genesis 30:6). It is a Dan'a'yo **neologism**, a meaning-based name, "Judging Tribe." Compare [[捷族]] (Naphtali) and [[武族]] (Gad). See [[基督敎]].

- Hebrew name: דָּן (Dan) (注音 ㄉㄚㄋ).
