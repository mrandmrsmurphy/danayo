## Notes
- [暗](../characters/暗%20(char).md) "dark" + [黒](../characters/黒%20(char).md) "black" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 暗 and 黒's own stand-ins are themselves).

暗黒 means dark or pitch black.

Mandarin ànhēi, Cantonese am3 haak1, Japanese あんこく, Korean 암흑, and Vietnamese ám hắc are all standard, fully compositional readings.
