## Notes
- [暗](../characters/暗%20(char).md) "dark" + [黒](../characters/黒%20(char).md) "black" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 暗 and 黒's own stand-ins are themselves).

暗黒 means dark or pitch black.

Mandarin ànhēi, Cantonese am3 haak1, Japanese あんこく, Korean 암흑, and Vietnamese ám hắc are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `korean`/`vietnamese`, added `kwin: false` (諺文 암훅 ≠ real Korean 암흑), and removed `暗黑的`/`黑暗的` from `aliases` (adjectival `的`-suffixed forms, not the noun itself; kept `闇黒`, a genuine orthographic variant using 闇 for 暗).
