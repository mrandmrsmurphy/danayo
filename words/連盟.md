## Notes
- [連 (char)](../characters/連%20(char).md) "consecutive, link" + [盟](../characters/盟.md) "oath, alliance" — "covenant, alliance."

Mandarin liánméng, japanese れんめい, and korean 연맹 are all directly attested standard readings.

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 盟's own `stand_in` is this exact word (連盟), so 盟 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 連盟; 連's own `stand_in` is instead 連 itself, so this word does not legitimize 連.
