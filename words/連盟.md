## Notes
- [連 (char)](characters/連%20(char).md) "consecutive, link" + [盟](characters/盟.md) "oath, alliance" — "covenant, alliance."

Mandarin liánméng, japanese れんめい, and korean 연맹 are all directly attested standard readings. **Found and fixed a typo on `cantonese`**: "lyun4 mang4" had the wrong first syllable — 連's own stored cantonese is lin4, not lyun4 — corrected to "lin4 mang4". Vietnamese liên minh is a directly attested, extremely common Vietnamese word ("alliance"), matching 連's own liên + 盟's own minh exactly. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 련명 diverges from Sino-Korean 연맹 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄌ⼶ㄋㄇ⼶ㄫ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 盟's own `stand_in` is this exact word (連盟), so 盟 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 連盟; 連's own `stand_in` is instead 連 itself, so this word does not legitimize 連.
