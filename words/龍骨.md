## Notes
- [龍 (char)](../characters/龍%20(char).md) "dragon" + [骨 (char)](../characters/骨%20(char).md) "bone" — keel.

龍骨 is the keel of a ship: the central beam along the bottom of the hull, to which the ribs are fixed. It is "dragon-bone," the same figure as English "backbone" for the spine of a ship. Wiktionary has the Chinese reading lónggǔ and Cantonese lung4 gwat1; Japanese 竜骨 (りゅうこつ) and Korean 용골 are the same compound but are not confirmed on the page and come from memory. In Chinese 龍骨 is also fossil bones used in medicine, a sense not taken over here.
