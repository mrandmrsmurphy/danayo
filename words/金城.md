## Notes
- [金 (char)](characters/金%20(char).md) "gold, metal" + [城](characters/城.md) "city wall, fortress" — "impenetrable city" (a city walled in metal), extended to the place name Jincheng.

Mandarin jīnchéng, cantonese gam1 sing4, japanese きんじょう, and korean 금성 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese kim thành is the honest compositional reading (金's own kim + 城's own thành); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 김싱 diverges from Sino-Korean 금성 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄇㄙㄧㄫ unique among both words and characters).
