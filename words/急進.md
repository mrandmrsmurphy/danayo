## Notes
- [急 (char)](../characters/急%20(char).md) "urgent" + [進](../characters/進.md) "advance"

急進 is radical, and a rapid advance. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 急進 (Mandarin jíjìn, Cantonese gap1 zeon3) as "to advance rapidly", literary of an official "to urgently seek promotion", and "radical; extreme", Japanese きゅうしん as "rapid progress" and "advance rapidly", and Vietnamese cấp tiến as "radical". The Korean 급진 field is built from the readings of the two characters. Compare [保守](保守.md), conservative.
