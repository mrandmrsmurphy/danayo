## Notes
- [水](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [槽](../characters/槽.md) "trough, tank" — "water trough," a sink or tank. Their own `stand_in` values: 水's is itself; 槽's is [[料槽]]; none points here, so 水槽 is an independent compound.

水槽 is a trough or tank for holding water, and by extension a kitchen sink. Mandarin shuǐcáo and Cantonese seoi2 cou4 are the ordinary Chinese words, and Japanese すいそう is the same word, usually meaning an aquarium tank. Korean 수조 holds the readings of the characters. Compare [[洗碗机]] (dishwasher); [[没]] "to sink" is the verb.
