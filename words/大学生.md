## Notes
- [大](../characters/大%20(char).md) "great" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study" + [生](../characters/生.md) "life, one who does" — no stand-in relationship applies (大 is its own stand-in; 学's own is [[学習]]; 生's is [[生活]]).

大学生 means a college student or undergraduate.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `vietnamese` had the native analytic phrase "sinh viên đại học" — corrected to the directly compositional `đại học sinh` (verified, a real term, historically distinguished from "sinh viên" by connoting a Western-style university student). Japanese だいがくせい, Korean 대학생, Mandarin dàxuéshēng, and Cantonese daai6 hok6 saang1 are all standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄧㄏㄚㄎㄙㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
