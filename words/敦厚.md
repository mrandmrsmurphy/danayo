## Notes
- [敦](../characters/敦.md) "honest, candid, sincere" + [厚](../characters/厚%20(char).md) "thick" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [敦](../characters/敦.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (厚's own stand-in is itself).

敦厚 means honest, candid, or sincere.

Mandarin dūnhòu, Cantonese deon1 hau5, Japanese とんこう, Korean 돈후, and Vietnamese đôn hậu are all standard, fully compositional readings. Trimmed a stray space in `mandarin`. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄋㄏㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).
