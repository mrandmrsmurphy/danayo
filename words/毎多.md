## Notes
- [毎 (char)](../characters/毎%20(char).md) "every; each; distributive" + [多 (char)](../characters/多%20(char).md) "many; much; numerous"

毎多 is the distributive correlative in the 多 ("amount") column of the correlative table: "each amount, every quantity, per amount; so much in each case". The character [毎 (char)](../characters/毎%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *moi*, ㄇㄛㄧ; its alias 每 is the traditional form, so 每多 is an `aliases` entry here, not a separate file) is the distributive morpheme of [毎日](毎日.md) "every day" and [毎年](毎年.md) "every year"; the free word is [毎](毎.md). [多 (char)](../characters/多%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *dǝ*, ㄉㄜ) is "many; much", and the word page [多](多.md) is the stative "many; much". The quantity is applied to each case in turn rather than to the total: where the collective [皆多](皆多.md) refers to the whole amount together, 毎多 refers to the amount that holds in every individual case. Illustrative only (invented, not from `grammar/`): 毎多、足 "each amount is enough".

Its part of speech is 副用名詞 (adverbial noun), like every cell of the 毎 row ([毎事](毎事.md), [毎物](毎物.md), [毎人](毎人.md), [毎名](毎名.md), [毎処](毎処.md), [毎時](毎時.md), [毎様](毎様.md), [毎類](毎類.md)). `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md` (line 11) lists "Adverbial Nouns" as the class able to be the frame with 0-derivation, and `grammar/Linguistics Features.md` (line 68) states that an adverbial noun may be the topic or frame of a clause with no marking but needs marking when it modifies the main verb. The chart itself gives only the form and its 注音, with no gloss, so the sense here is derived compositionally (毎 = distributive, 多 = amount) and from the 毎 character page's gloss of the word.

Against its column neighbours: [皆多](皆多.md) is the collective "the whole amount; all of it", where 毎多 distributes over the cases one at a time, the same split as [毎人](毎人.md) against [皆人](皆人.md). [某多](某多.md) is the indefinite "some amount", [何多](何多.md) the interrogative "how much/many", and [此多](此多.md), [其多](其多.md) and [彼多](彼多.md) point at one particular amount (proximal, medial, distal), where 毎多 ranges over all of them. [多少](多少.md) "amount; quantity" is the plain noun and is not a correlative.

The cross-linguistic fields were checked against Wiktionary on 2026-10-11. Wiktionary has no entry for 毎多, 每多, 每份 or 每个. The earlier fields held Mandarin 各自多少, Japanese それぞれの量, Korean 각자 얼마씩 and Vietnamese *mỗi người một lượng*; they are removed. The Vietnamese one means "each person one amount", which is a person-distributive, not the amount-distributive. Mandarin 各自 (gèzì) is on Wiktionary, glossed "each respectively; each one of them", but it distributes over persons and is not 毎 "every" plus an amount, so it is not an equivalent. The phrases 各自多少, それぞれの量 and 각자 얼마씩 are from memory, not re-checked. From memory, not re-checked: Japanese 毎回/一つ当たり-type per-unit phrases and Mandarin 每份 "per share"; none is entered. Dan'a'yo keeps the sense shared across CJKV; no narrowing is needed.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md)'s correlative table (line 125; the same chart is in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`, line 151, in the 多 row), 毎多 is the eighth cell of the 毎 ("every") row, after [毎事](毎事.md), [毎物](毎物.md), [毎人](毎人.md), [毎名](毎名.md), [毎処](毎処.md), [毎時](毎時.md) and [毎様](毎様.md), and before [毎類](毎類.md). The table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), 63 forms, so 毎多 is parsed compositionally. The 羅馬字 *moidǝ* is from the character pages (毎 *moi*, 多 *dǝ*) and the 諺文 뫼드 from 뫼 + 드; the earlier *moydǝ* disagreed with the 毎 page.

The 注音 ㄇㄛㄧㄉㄜ carries no syllable-boundary dot, which is correct. `grammar/文法 - 02音韻論.md` (line 116) gives the structure (C)(G)V(C), and the only syllable-final consonants it allows are the nasals ㄇ ㄋ ㄫ, the glides and the unreleased stops ㄆ ㄊ ㄎ, so ㄉ cannot be a coda. The only partition is therefore ㄇㄛㄧ + ㄉㄜ (both in `syllables/`, as ㄇㄛㄧ.md and ㄉㄜ), the same as [此多](此多.md). Splitting the diphthong ㄛㄧ (ㄇㄛ + ㄧㄉㄜ) is not treated as a boundary in this vault, and ㄧㄉ is not a legal syllable anyway.
