## Notes
- [手](../characters/手%20(char).md) "hand" + [足](../characters/足%20(char).md) "foot" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 手 and 足's own stand-ins are themselves).

手足 means limbs — the hands and feet.

Mandarin shǒuzú, Cantonese sau2 zuk1, Korean 수족, and Vietnamese thủ túc are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese てあし uses the native reading (native equivalent of 手+足, rather than Sino-Japanese しゅそく) — the standard, expected word. Korean also has the native equivalent 손발, used interchangeably with 수족. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼜ㄐㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
