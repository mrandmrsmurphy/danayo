## Notes
- [斥](../characters/斥.md) "reject, scout" + [候](../characters/候.md) "wait, watch" — "watch ahead," a scout. Their own `stand_in` values: 斥's is [[排斥]]; 候's is [[気候]]; none points here, so 斥候 is an independent compound.

斥候 is a scout: a soldier sent ahead to observe the enemy, and by extension the act. Mandarin chìhòu and Cantonese cik1 hau6 are literary, and Japanese せっこう and Korean 척후 are the same word. It is the literary partner of [[偵察]], the ordinary word. Compare [[哨兵]] (sentry).
