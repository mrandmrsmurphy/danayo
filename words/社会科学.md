## Notes
- [社](../characters/社.md) "company" + [会 (char)](../characters/会%20(char).md) "meet, gather" + [科](../characters/科.md) "branch" + [学](../characters/学.md) "learn" — literally "society" (社会) + "field of learning" (科学).

社会科学 names social science broadly — the family of academic disciplines studying human society (sociology, economics, political science, anthropology), as distinct from natural science (自然科学).

Mandarin shèhuì kēxué, Cantonese se5 wui2 fo1 hok6 (会 taking the same colloquial changed tone as its sibling compounds [[社会学]]/[[社会科]]/[[社会主義]]), Japanese しゃかいかがく (shakaikagaku), and Korean 사회과학 (sahoegwahak) are all fully compositional. Vietnamese khoa học xã hội reorders the elements to fit native syntax, the same pattern seen in [[社会科]].
