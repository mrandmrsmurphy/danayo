## Notes
- [添](../characters/添.md) "add, accompany" + [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "ride"

添乗 means to escort a tour: to travel along with a tour group as its guide. English Wiktionary has an entry only for Japanese 添乗 (てんじょう), glossed "the act or practice of guiding tourists, escorting a tour group" and "to guide a tour; to escort or accompany a tour group; to lead an excursion". Korean 첨승 is built from the hanja readings of the two characters, and the Mandarin and Cantonese fields are likewise built from the characters.
