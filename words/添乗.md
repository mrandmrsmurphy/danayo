## Notes
- [添](../characters/添.md) "add, accompany" + [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "ride"

添乗 is specifically Japanese tourism-industry vocabulary: 添乗員 (tenjōin) is a tour conductor — a company representative who physically accompanies a package tour group throughout the trip, distinct from a local 観光ガイド (sightseeing guide) who only guides at a single site. The word literally combines "to accompany/add on" with "to ride," describing someone who boards and travels along with the group rather than meeting it at a destination.

Korean 첨승 is a standard compositional Sino-Korean reading. **`vietnamese` intentionally left blank**: no attested Sino-Vietnamese compound for this specific Japanese industry term could be confirmed; Vietnamese instead uses the native/general hướng dẫn viên (du lịch) ("tour guide"), without this word's specific accompany-throughout-the-trip distinction.
