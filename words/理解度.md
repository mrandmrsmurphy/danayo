## Notes
- [理解](理解.md) "understand, comprehend" + [度](../characters/度.md) "degree" (stand-in for 度 is [[程度]]; 理解度 is simply another legitimate host, since a character's stand-in doesn't restrict which other compounds it may appear in).

理解度 means degree or level of understanding — how much of something has been comprehended, as in a comprehension rate or test score.

Japanese りかいど is the real, standard term, common in educational and testing contexts (テストの理解度, "test comprehension level"; 理解度調査, "comprehension survey"). Korean 이해도 is likewise real and widely attested; this word's own `korean` field renders it 리해도, retaining word-initial ㄹ per this vault's North Korean/문화어 convention, matching [[理解]]'s own fix. Mandarin lǐjiědù and Cantonese lei5 gaai2 dou6 are straightforward compositional readings, not independently verified as standard terms. Vietnamese lý giải độ is likewise compositional only, built from 度's own stored "độ" citation — not verified as an attested real-world term. `kwin` is false: inherited from 解 (false), despite both 理 and 度 individually being true.

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot**: inherited directly from [[理解]]'s own fix (see that word's Notes for the fork analysis). Appending 度 (ㄉㄛ) introduces no new ambiguity — the full concatenation `ㄌㄧㄍ⼘ㄧㄉㄛ` admits exactly the same two partitions as 理解 alone, both agreeing 度 reads as a clean trailing `ㄉㄛ` — so the single existing dot carries over unchanged: ㄌㄧ·ㄍ⼘ㄧㄉㄛ.
