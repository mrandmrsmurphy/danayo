## Notes
- [天 (char)](../characters/天%20(char).md) "sky, heaven" + [津](../characters/津.md) "ferry crossing, ford" — "heavenly ford". No stand-in relationship applies (天 is its own stand-in; 津's is [[津波]]).

天津 (Tianjin) is a municipality and port city on the Hai River, southeast of Beijing, and one of the four direct-administered municipalities (直轄市, "municipality directly under the central government") with 北京, 上海 and 重慶 (see [China](../lexipedia/China.md) for the others). Wiktionary notes that the origin of the name is disputed; the most common explanation is that the Yongle Emperor of the Ming, who crossed the river there on his march south, named it 天子津渡 "the Emperor's ford".

The word also has older, non-urban senses, listed first below: the Milky Way, and the Chinese astronomical asterism 天津 "Celestial Ford", a bridge across the Milky Way, within the Girl mansion (女宿) (Wiktionary, English and Chinese). Japanese てんしん is the reading in Wiktionary and appears in 天津飯 (tenshinhan, a rice dish; from memory, not re-checked as to its description). A different reading of the same two characters, あまつ, occurs in 天津神 (amatsukami, "heavenly deities"), a Shinto term that this word does not cover (from memory, not re-checked).

The Korean field holds the Sino-Korean 천진 (Wiktionary Korean has Cheonjin 천진); the usual Korean name today is 톈진 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Thiên Tân is attested on Vietnamese Wikipedia. The Cantonese and Mandarin readings were checked on English Wiktionary. The Japanese Wikipedia gives the city as てんしんし. Homophone: [[天真]] shares the 注音 ㄊㄝㄋㄐㄧㄋ.

1. The Milky Way; the asterism Celestial Ford.
2. Tianjin, the municipality.
