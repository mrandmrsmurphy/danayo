## Notes
- [蛮](../characters/蛮.md) "barbarian" is legitimized by this exact compound (its own `stand_in`). [人](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" is separately legitimized by [[人]] itself — transitivity fails, no cranberry.

Mandarin mánrén and japanese ばんじん (both also the real common words for "barbarian") confirmed standard and compositional. Korean 만인 is likewise compositional (a striking real-world coincidence with the unrelated common word 만인 "all people").
