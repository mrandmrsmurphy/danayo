## Notes
- [十 (char)](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" + [万](../characters/万.md) "ten-thousand" — one hundred thousand, 100,000.

Ten myriad: 十 is the bare word [[十]], so no 一 is needed. Counting in myriads gives 100,000 as "ten myriad," with no "hundred thousand" unit of its own, which English-speaking learners must relearn. Mandarin shíwàn, Cantonese sap6 maan6, Japanese じゅうまん and Korean 십만 are the plain concatenations. Vietnamese mười vạn uses vạn as a live numeral. Compare [[二万]], [[七万]] and [[百万]].
