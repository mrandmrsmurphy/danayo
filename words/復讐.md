## Notes
- [復](../characters/復.md) "return; repay" + [讐](../characters/讐.md) "revenge"

復讐 is revenge: to repay a wrong or an injury with harm to the one who did it. Wiktionary glosses Japanese ふくしゅう as "revenge, vengeance; to take revenge". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 복수 matches.

It is a personal, deliberate act of vengeance, where [報復](報復.md) is retaliation and [世仇](世仇.md) is a feud that is passed down. The feeling behind it is [恨](恨.md).
