## Notes
- [精](../characters/精.md) "spirit, essence, sperm" + [油 (char)](../characters/油%20(char).md) "oil" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (精's is [[精神]], 油's is [[油]] itself), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: true` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: true`.

精油 names essential oil — a concentrated, aromatic plant extract used in perfumery, aromatherapy, and traditional medicine, distinguished from ordinary cooking or industrial oils by its refined, essence-like nature (精, "essence").

Mandarin jīngyóu, Cantonese zing1 jau4, Japanese せいゆ (seiyu), and Korean 정유 (jeongyu) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese tinh dầu is the standard term — a mixed Sino-Vietnamese/native compound (tinh, "essence" + native dầu, "oil"), and by far the everyday Vietnamese word for essential oil.
