## Notes
- [噴](../characters/噴.md) "spout, spray" + [水 (char)](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" — fountain.

噴水 is a fountain: water thrown into the air by pressure, either for decoration or as a natural jet. It is "spout-water." Japanese ふんすい and Korean 분수 are the usual noun; Mandarin pēnshuǐ is mainly a verb, "to spray water," with 噴泉 (pēnquán) the usual noun for a fountain. Dan'a'yo uses the noun sense shared by Japanese and Korean.
