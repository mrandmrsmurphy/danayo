結加夫坐 (結跏趺坐) names the full lotus position in seated meditation — each foot resting on the opposite thigh.

Mandarin jiéjiāfūzuò, Cantonese git3 gaa1 fu1 zo6, Japanese けっかふざ (kekkafuza), and Korean 결가부좌 (gyeolgabujwa) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 結跏趺坐 is itself the common attested Buddhist term for full lotus position in all four languages. Vietnamese kết gia phu toạ is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading (the more common spelling uses già for 跏). No Dan'a'yo homophones.
