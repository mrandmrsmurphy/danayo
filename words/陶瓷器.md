## Notes
- [陶](../characters/陶.md) "pottery" + [瓷](../characters/瓷.md) "porcelain" + [器](../characters/器.md) "container, vessel"
- Real Japanese and Korean orthography spell this word 陶磁器/도자기, using [[磁]] instead of [[瓷]] — see [[陶瓷]] for the historical Cizhou-kiln reasoning. Dan'a'yo composes this word with 瓷 to keep 磁 = magnetism and 瓷 = porcelain strictly separate (毎字明意); 陶磁器 is recorded as an alias for the real-world spelling.

陶瓷器 means chinaware, porcelain, or pottery ware collectively — the finished vessels and objects made by ceramic craft, as distinct from [[陶瓷]] (the material/craft itself). Mandarin 陶瓷器 (táocíqì), Japanese 陶磁器 (とうじき), and Korean 도자기 (dojagi) are all standard everyday terms for ceramic ware. Sino-Vietnamese đào sứ khí is compositionally correct but rare; everyday Vietnamese instead uses the native-Sino hybrid đồ gốm sứ ("ceramic-porcelain goods," native đồ + gốm + Sino sứ).
