## Notes
- [総](../characters/総.md) "general, chief" + [主](../characters/主.md) "lord, master" + [教](../characters/教.md) "teaching, religion" — none of the three characters' own `stand_in` points here (総's is [[総数]], 主's is [[主人]], 教's is [[教授]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 総 and 教 are individually `kwin: false`.
- Ranks above [[大主教]] (archbishop) in Christian ecclesiastical hierarchy. The highest-ranking bishop in a given church or region.

Mandarin zǒngzhǔjiào, Cantonese zung2 zyu2 gaau3, Japanese そうしゅきょう (sōshukyō), and Korean 총주교 (chongjugyo) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 総主教 is itself a common attested word for "primate, chief archbishop" in all four languages. Vietnamese tổng chủ giáo is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
