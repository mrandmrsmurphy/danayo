## Notes
- [外](../characters/外.md) "outside" + [交](../characters/交%20(char).md) "interact" — no stand-in relationship applies (外's own stand-in is [[外部]]; 交 is its own stand-in).

外交 means diplomacy or foreign affairs.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `cantonese` was missing a space (ngoi6gaau1 → ngoi6 gaau1). Filled blank `pos: 名詞`; fixed unquoted `hsk_level`; fixed empty-list `aliases`. Japanese がいこう, Korean 외교, and Vietnamese ngoại giao are all standard readings. Note: `交`'s own character page citation (gyou/굣) disagrees with the "gyau/걋" form used consistently across the great majority of 交-compounds in the vault (交叉, 交際, 交付, 交遊/交友, 交点, 交互, 交尾, 交接, 交流, 外交 itself, 援交, 性交, 社交) — this word's own gyau/걋 form matches that majority and was left as-is; the character page itself may need a future fix rather than this word. No homophones (注音 ⺢ㄧㄍ⼄ㄨ unique across both words and characters).
