## Notes
- [翠](../characters/翠.md) "kingfisher, jade green" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — 翠's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 色's own is [[色彩]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 翠色 legitimizes 翠 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 色 is individually `kwin: false`.

翠色 names jade color — a green with the emerald hue of jade or a kingfisher's plumage.

Mandarin cuìsè, Cantonese ceoi3 sik1, Japanese すいしょく (suishoku), and Korean 취색 (chwisaek) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese thuý sắc is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
