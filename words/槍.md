## Notes
- [槍](characters/槍%20(char).md) "spear, lance" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 槍 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

槍 means spear or lance.

Mandarin qiāng, Cantonese coeng1, Japanese そう, Korean 창, and Vietnamese thương (the primary form among the character's own comma-joined vietnamese variants sang/thương) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Added the missing `japanese`/`hsk_level` fields and simplified the doubled `vietnamese` list to its primary form. Discovered a genuine homophone with [[蒼]] (both 注音 ㄑ⺢ㄫ, 諺文 촹) — cross-referenced both pages.
