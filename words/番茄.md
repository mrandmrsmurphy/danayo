## Notes
- [番](../characters/番%20(char).md) "foreign" + [茄](../characters/茄.md) "eggplant" — "foreign eggplant," the tomato, brought from the Americas. Their own `stand_in` values: 番's is itself; 茄's is [[茄子]]; none points here, so 番茄 is an independent compound.

番茄 is a tomato. Mandarin fānqié and Cantonese faan1 ke4-2 are the ordinary Chinese words; the name means "foreign eggplant," since the tomato reached China from the Americas in the nineteenth century (the variant 蕃茄 is kept as an alias). Japanese says トマト, with 蕃茄 only a rare spelling, and Korean 토마토, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[野菜]] (vegetables).
