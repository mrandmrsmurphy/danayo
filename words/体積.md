## Notes
- [体](../characters/体.md) "group, body, form" + [積](../characters/積.md) "accumulate, pile up" — "volume, capacity, bulk" (the accumulated size of a body). Directly attested and standard across all five languages (mandarin tǐjī, japanese たいせき, korean 체적, vietnamese thể tích). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 테적 diverges from Sino-Korean 체적 via the AND-rule, since 体's own field independently diverges (despite 積 itself being `kwin: true`). No homophones (注音 ㄊㄝㄧㄐㄝㄎ unique among both words and characters).
