## Notes
- [刻](../characters/刻.md) "carve; engrave" + [印](../characters/印.md) "stamp; imprint" — this word is the stand-in for [刻](../characters/刻.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

刻印 names the precise, two-dimensional, knife-based incising of marks, text, or seals — distinct from [[彫刻]]'s broader three-dimensional, sculptural sense of carving (see [Art](../lexipedia/Art.md)). Used both literally, for physically engraving a seal or inscription, and figuratively, for an impression left indelibly on someone's memory or character (an "imprint").

Japanese こくいん, Korean 각인, and Vietnamese khắc ấn all preserve this same literal/figurative engraving sense.
