## Notes
- [敲](../characters/敲%20(char).md) "knock, tap, rap" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [敲](../characters/敲%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

敲 means to knock, tap, or rap.

Mandarin qiāo, Cantonese haau1, Japanese こう, Korean 고, and Vietnamese xao are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones at the word level (注音 ㄎ⼘ㄨ is shared only with bound characters 喬/巧, neither of which has an independent word at this exact reading).
