## Notes
- [焦 (char)](../characters/焦%20(char).md) as 代用字 for 鷦 + [明 (char)](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright; luminous"
- 焦明 is already an attested classical Chinese alternate form of 鷦明 — the substitution of 焦 for 鷦 is not a Dan'a'yo invention but an ancient variant. The third alias 鷦䳟 arises because 明 carries 䳟 as an alias.

The 鷦明/焦明 is a divine bird of the phoenix kind, named alongside the fenghuang ([[鳳凰]]) in classical Chinese literature. The *Shuowen Jiezi* glosses 鷦明 as a bird of the fenghuang family, and records an interpretation that identifies it with the southern vermilion bird (南方朱鳥) of the four-direction scheme. Its name pairs 鷦/焦 (*jiāo*) with 明 ("bright; luminous"), evoking a bird of dazzling brilliance.

The substitution of 焦 ("scorched; intensely hot") for 鷦 ("wren") is purely phonetic: 焦 is itself the phonetic element of 鷦, so the two share the reading *jiāo* with no semantic link. Here 焦 lends only its sound; it is accidentally apt for a fire-associated bird of the south, but that fit is not why it was chosen.

In Dan'a'yo 焦明 is the name of this mythical bird, and a proper noun in the animal vocabulary; it is not a word for any real species. It keeps the shared CJKV sense.
