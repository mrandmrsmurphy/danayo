## Notes
- [布](../characters/布.md) "cloth" + [帛](../characters/帛.md) "silk" — this word is the stand-in for [帛](../characters/帛.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

布帛 names woven textiles generally — cloth and silk together, the classical umbrella term for fabric goods (once also serving as a unit of currency/tribute alongside grain in ancient Chinese economies).

Japanese ふはく and Vietnamese bố bạch are both standard, non-drifted readings. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄛㄅㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
