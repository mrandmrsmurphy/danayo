## Notes
- [展](../characters/展.md) "display" + [覧](../characters/覧.md) "view" + [会 (char)](../characters/会%20(char).md) "meeting"

展覧会 is an exhibition: a showing of works of art or other things to the public. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin zhǎnlǎnhuì as "exhibit; exhibition; show" and Japanese てんらんかい as "exhibition". Korean 전람회 is the same word. Cantonese zin2 laam5 wui6-2 matches.

It is held at a [美術館](美術館.md) or [画廊](画廊.md), and the act of viewing it is [観覧](観覧.md).
