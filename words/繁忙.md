## Notes
- [繁](../characters/繁.md) "numerous, complicated" + [忙](../characters/忙.md) "busy" — 忙's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 繁's own is [[繁茂]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 繁忙 legitimizes 忙 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 繁 is individually `kwin: false`.

繁忙 means busy — pressed with numerous tasks.

Mandarin fánmáng, Cantonese faan4 mong4, Japanese はんぼう (hanbō), and Korean 번망 (beonmang) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 繁忙 is itself a common attested word for "busy" in all four languages. Vietnamese phồn mang is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
