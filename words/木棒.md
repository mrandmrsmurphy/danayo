## Notes
- [木](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree, wood" + [棒](../characters/棒.md) "rod, club" — "wooden rod," a stick. Their own `stand_in` values: 木's is itself; 棒's is [[棍棒]]; none points here, so 木棒 is an independent compound.

木棒 is a stick: a plain wooden rod, neither a walking staff nor a weapon. Mandarin mùbàng and Cantonese muk6 paang5 are the ordinary words, and Japanese もくぼう and Korean 목봉 are the same compound. Compare [[杖]] (staff), [[棍棒]] (club) and [[杆]] (rod).
