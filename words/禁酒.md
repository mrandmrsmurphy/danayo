## Notes
- [禁](../characters/禁.md) "prohibit, forbid" + [酒](../characters/酒.md) "alcohol, wine" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (禁's is [[禁止]], 酒's is [[酒精]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

禁酒 means the prohibition of alcohol — whether a personal vow of temperance, a religious restriction, or a historical government policy (as in the U.S. Prohibition era, 禁酒法, or various Islamic-majority countries' alcohol bans). It is the direct counterpart to [[禁煙]] ("no smoking") among common personal-vice prohibitions.

Mandarin jìnjiǔ, Cantonese gam3 zau2, Japanese きんしゅ (kinshu), and Korean 금주 (geumju) are all standard, fully compositional readings, each the ordinary everyday word for alcohol prohibition/temperance in its language. Vietnamese cấm tửu is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form.
