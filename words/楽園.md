## Notes
- [楽](../characters/楽.md) "joy" + [園](../characters/園.md) "garden, park" — "garden of joy," paradise. Their own `stand_in` values: 楽's is [[快楽]]; 園's is [[庭園]]; none points here, so 楽園 is an independent compound.

楽園 is paradise: a place of joy, and the Garden of Eden. Mandarin lèyuán (樂園), Cantonese lok6 jyun4, Japanese らくえん and Korean 낙원 all use it. It is the place of bliss, where [[天国]] is heaven. Compare [[庭園]] (garden) and [[地獄]] (hell).
