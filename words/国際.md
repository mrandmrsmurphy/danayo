## Notes
- [国](../characters/国.md) "nation" + [際](../characters/際%20(char).md) "border, boundary" — no stand-in relationship applies (国's own stand-in is [[国家]]; 際 is its own stand-in).

国際 means international.

**Real bugs found and fixed**: `cantonese` had been contaminated with 語's syllable appended from the sibling word [[国際語]] ("gwok3 zai3 jyu5" → gwok3 zai3). `vietnamese` was wrongly capitalized as a proper noun ("Quốc tế") though this is a common adjective — corrected to lowercase `quốc tế`. Japanese こくさい and Korean 국제 are both standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄎㄐㄝ unique across both words and characters).
