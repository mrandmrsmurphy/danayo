## Notes
- [量](characters/量.md) "measure, quantity" + [詞](characters/詞.md) "word" — "measure word, classifier, counter."

Mandarin liàngcí and vietnamese lượng từ are directly attested standard/compositional readings. **Found and fixed a wrong-compound reading bug on `cantonese`**: loeng6 ci4 had the wrong tone on the first syllable — 量's own cantonese is loeng4, not loeng6 — corrected to loeng4 ci4. **Found and fixed a wrong-compound reading bug on `japanese`/`korean`**: じょすうし (containing a hidden zero-width space, U+200B) and 수분류사 actually belong to the unrelated, differently-composed compounds 助数詞 and 數分類詞 respectively — corrected to the honest compositional りょうし/량사 (量's own RYOU/량 + 詞's own SHI/사; 량사 also matches the word's own stored 諺文/羅馬字 exactly, flipping `kwin` from false to genuinely true). **Found and removed four bad-aliases-entries**: `量詞` (a nonsensical self-referential alias — this word's own title), `助数詞`, `數分類詞`, and `量数詞` (all genuinely different compounds) — kept only `量词`, the legitimate simplified-character variant. No homophones (注音 ㄌ⼘ㄫㄙㄚ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 量's `stand_in` (数量) nor 詞's own `stand_in` (単詞) points to this compound.
