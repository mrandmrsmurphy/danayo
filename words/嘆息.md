## Notes
- [嘆 (char)](../characters/嘆%20(char).md) "sigh" + [息](../characters/息.md) "breath"

嘆息 is a sigh: a long breath let out in sorrow or regret, and to sigh so. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin tànxī as "to sigh, with regret" and Japanese たんそく as "a sigh; to sigh". Cantonese taan3 sik1 matches, and Korean 탄식 is the reading of the characters.

It is the breath of [悲嘆](悲嘆.md), grief, and of [後悔](後悔.md), regret. The sigh as a single sound is the [嘆](嘆.md), and a louder cry is the [悲鳴](悲鳴.md).
