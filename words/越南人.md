## Notes
- [越 (char)](../characters/越%20(char).md) "exceed" + [南](../characters/南.md) "south" + [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" — 越南 ("Vietnam," see [[越南]] for the country name itself) + 人 ("person").

Mandarin Yuènánrén, Cantonese jyut6 naam4 jan4, and Sino-Korean 월남인 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 越's, 南's, and 人's own stored citations exactly. Japanese ベトナム人 and the alternate Korean 베트남인 instead use a phonetic loan of the modern country name "Vietnam" (from French/English) rather than the classical Sino-Xenic 越南, reflecting how "Vietnam" as a country name is more commonly rendered in casual modern usage in both languages. `kwin` is false: the AND-rule fails because 越 and 人 both individually diverge from Korean on their own character pages, even though 南 alone matches. No homophones (注音 ⼔ㄊㄋㄚㄇㄋㄧㄋ unique among both words and characters).
