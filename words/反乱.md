## Notes
- The conventional form 叛乱/叛亂 is written with 叛, but Dan'a'yo folds 叛 into [反 (char)](../characters/反%20(char).md), as Japanese does — a deliberate merge even though 叛's original reading (pàn, preserved in this word's own 注音 ㄅㄚㄋ) differs slightly from 反's (fǎn, ㄈㄛㄋ). Not a data error.

`vietnamese: phản loạn` is one of the most common everyday Vietnamese words for "rebellion, revolt," a direct match built from the same conventional 叛亂 root already noted above. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 반란 matches Sino-Korean 반란 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄋㄌㄚㄋ unique among both words and characters).
