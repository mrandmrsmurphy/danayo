## Notes
- [七 (char)](../characters/七%20(char).md) "seven" + [面](../characters/面.md) "face; surface" + [鳥 (char)](../characters/鳥%20(char).md) "bird" — no `#cranberry`: 面's own `stand_in` is [[表面]], 七's and 鳥's are themselves; none points here.
- Named for the turkey's wattle, which changes color and appears as "seven faces."

Mandarin qīmiànniǎo, Cantonese cat1 min6 niu5, Japanese しちめんちょう, and Korean 칠면조 are all standard compositional readings. Vietnamese gà tây ("western chicken," a real everyday native term with no connection to the "seven faces" imagery) is used here instead of a compositional calque, matching the established real-attested-usage convention. `kwin` is false (AND-rule: 七 false alone determines the whole product, regardless of 面's own true).
