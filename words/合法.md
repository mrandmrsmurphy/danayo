## Notes
- [合 (char)](../characters/合%20(char).md) "join; fit together" + [法 (char)](../characters/法%20(char).md) "rule; law"

合法 is legal or lawful: in accordance with the law, literally "fitting the law". Wiktionary glosses Mandarin héfǎ as "legal; lawful; legitimate" and Japanese ごうほう as "legal, lawful; legality", with Korean 합법 and Vietnamese hợp pháp as the matching forms. The Mandarin word also has a grammar sense, "grammatical", which Dan'a'yo does not take.

It is the counterpart of [違法](違法.md), "illegal", which the Law page listed without an opposite. It is narrower than [正当](正当.md), "legitimate, proper", because 合法 asks only whether the act agrees with the law, while 正当 asks whether it is also justified.
