## Notes
- [虐](../characters/虐.md) "cruel" + [殺 (char)](../characters/殺%20(char).md) "kill"

虐殺 is a massacre: the cruel killing of many who cannot defend themselves. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin nüèshā as "to brutally murder; to torture to death" and Japanese ぎゃくさつ as "massacre, slaughter". Korean 학살 and Cantonese joek6 saat3 match.

The cruelty is part of the meaning. A killing of many without that note is [殺戮](殺戮.md) or [屠殺](屠殺.md), and a single murder is [殺人](殺人.md).
