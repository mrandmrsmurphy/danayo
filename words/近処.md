## Notes
Mandarin jìnchù, cantonese gan6 gyu3, and korean 근처 are all directly attested standard readings. **Found and fixed a real-equivalent-instead-of-own-reading bug on `japanese`**: the stored value きんじょ is actually the reading of the different (though near-synonymous) Japanese word 近所 ("neighborhood," using 所 rather than 処 as its second character) — corrected to the honest compositional きんしょ (近's own KIN + 処's own SHO). Filled a blank `vietnamese` with honest compositional "cận xử." `近處`/`近处` are the standard traditional/simplified spelling variants (処→處/处), correctly listed as aliases; **found and excluded 近所 from the aliases list**, since it is a genuinely different compound (所, not 処), not a spelling variant, matching the same class of bug found elsewhere this session. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 긴초 diverges from Sino-Korean 근처 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄋㄑㄛ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 近's `stand_in` (近 itself) nor 処's own `stand_in` (処 itself) points to this compound.
