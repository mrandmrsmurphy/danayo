## Notes
- [老 (char)](../characters/老%20(char).md) "old; prefix" + [鼠](../characters/鼠.md) "rat"

Wiktionary lists 老鼠 only under Chinese: "mouse and rat in general", "cunning, deceitful person", and, archaic or dialectal (Guilin Mandarin), "bat (animal)". In Dan'a'yo it is the generic word for rat or mouse; see [[老鼠人]] for the slang extension, and [[熊鼠]].
