## Notes
- [送 (char)](../characters/送%20(char).md) "give, send" + [還](../characters/還.md) "return, repatriate"
- This word is the stand-in for [[還]], which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo.

送還 means to send someone back: to return a person, such as a deportee, a prisoner of war, a refugee or a body, to their home or country of origin. 送 is "to send, to see off" and 還 "to return"; the person is returned by someone else, which is the difference from a person's own coming back. Mandarin sònghuán (送还 in simplified script), Cantonese sung3 waan4, Japanese そうかん (sōkan; 強制送還, "forced return, deportation") and Korean 송환 (songhwan) are the same compound, standard in legal and news language.

In Dan'a'yo it is the causative "send back", beside [[復帰]] and [[回帰]], which describe a person's own return.
