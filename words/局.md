## Notes
- [局 (char)](../characters/局%20(char).md) "office; bureau" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [局 (char)](../characters/局%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

局 names an administrative office or bureau, extremely productive as a compound-forming suffix across all four languages: 郵便局 "post office," 放送局 "broadcast station," 造幣局 "mint," 中央情報局 "CIA." It also carries a separate, extended sense of "a bounded situation or predicament" (as in a game's "position, stage"), from the same core image of a confined, curved space.

Japanese きょく, Korean 국, and Vietnamese cục (the primary Sino-Vietnamese administrative-office reading — the character's own field also lists several rarer Nôm variants: cộc/cuộc/gục/ngúc) are all standard readings. No homophones among other words (注音 ㄍ⼄ㄎ unique).
