## Notes
- [飲 (char)](../characters/飲%20(char).md) "drink" + [食 (char)](../characters/食%20(char).md) "eat" — no `#cranberry`: both 飲's and 食's own `stand_in` are themselves; neither points here.

飲食 (yǐnshí) is the standard Mandarin word for "food and drink; eating and drinking," and Cantonese 飲食 (jam2sik6) likewise. Japanese いんしょく (inshoku) and Korean 음식 (eumsik, the everyday Korean word for "food") are both standard terms, as is Sino-Vietnamese ẩm thực — a very common, everyday word covering food and drink culture generally (as in văn hóa ẩm thực, "food culture"). A clean case with full agreement across all five languages.
