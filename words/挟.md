## Notes
- [挟](../characters/挟%20(char).md) "clasp, hold" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [挟](../characters/挟%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

挟 means to clasp or hold (between, as in tongs or under the arm).

Mandarin xié, Cantonese hip3, Japanese きょう, Korean 협, and Vietnamese hiệp are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: literal `vietnamese: null`, corrected using the character's own citation. No homophones at the word level (注音 ㄏㄝㄆ is shared only with bound characters 侠/協/脅, none of which has an independent word at this exact reading).
