## Notes
- [書](../characters/書.md) "write" + [家](../characters/家.md) "house; specialist"

書家 is a calligrapher: an artist who is master of calligraphy. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin shūjiā as "calligrapher" and Japanese しょか as "calligrapher". Korean 서가 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese syu1 gaa1 matches.

The art is [書法](書法.md) or [書道](書道.md), and the tool is the [筆](筆.md), brush.
