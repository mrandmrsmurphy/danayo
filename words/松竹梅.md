## Notes
- [松](../characters/松%20(char).md) "pine, fir" + [竹](../characters/竹%20(char).md) "bamboo" + [梅](../characters/梅.md) "plum, prune, ume" — no stand-in relationship applies here (松's own stand-in is itself; 竹's own is itself; 梅's own is [[梅花]]).

松竹梅 refers to the "Three Friends of Winter" — pine, bamboo, and plum blossom, a traditional East Asian grouping symbolizing resilience and steadfastness through the cold season.

Mandarin sōngzhúméi, Cantonese cung4 zuk1 mui4, Japanese しょうちくばい, Korean 송죽매, and Vietnamese tùng trúc mai are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed a real byte-level bug: `諺文`'s first syllable was 쇽 (U+C1FD, syok) instead of 숑 (U+C211, syong) — the two look visually similar but are distinct codepoints; the 羅馬字 field's "syongjugmoi" already had it right. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field, removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/empty `aliases: []`. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼄ㄫㄐㄨㄎㄇㄛㄧ unique across both words and characters).
