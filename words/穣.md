## Notes
- [穣](../characters/穣%20(char).md) "stalks of grain" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 穣 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

穣 (shinjitai of traditional 穰) names the stalks or ears of standing grain, closely related in sense to [[穂]]. Its native Japanese readings ゆたか ("abundant") and みのる ("to bear fruit") reflect an extended, adjectival/verbal sense of agricultural abundance rather than the bare noun.

Mandarin ráng, Cantonese joeng4, and Korean 양 (yang) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese じょう (jō, on'yomi) is the reading for the bare noun sense.
