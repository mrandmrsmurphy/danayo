## Notes
- [南](../characters/南.md) "south" + [部](../characters/部.md) "part, region" — "south, southern area." `vietnamese: Nam Bộ` is directly attested and extremely well known — it's the actual proper name of southern Vietnam (as distinct from Bắc Bộ "the north" and Trung Bộ "the center"), a direct compositional match to this word's own two characters. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 남봇 diverges from Sino-Korean 남부 in the second syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄚㄇㄅㄛㄨ unique among both words and characters).
