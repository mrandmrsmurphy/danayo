## Notes
- [提](../characters/提.md) "withdraw" + [款](../characters/款%20(char).md) "funds" + [机](../characters/机.md) "machine" — "fund-withdrawing machine," an ATM. Their own `stand_in` values: 提's is [[提示]]; 款's is itself; 机's is [[机会]]; none points here, so 提款机 is an independent compound.

提款机 is an ATM: a machine from which one withdraws cash. Mandarin tíkuǎnjī and Cantonese tai4 fun2 gei1 are the ordinary Chinese words. Japanese says ＡＴＭ and Korean 현금인출기, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[紙幣]] (banknote) and [[机器]] (machine).
