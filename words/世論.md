## Notes
- [世](../characters/世.md) "world; society" + [論](../characters/論.md) "opinion; theory"

世論 is public opinion: what the people at large think about a matter. Wiktionary glosses Japanese せろん and よろん as "public opinion". For Mandarin shìlùn it gives a literary sense, "public opinion". The alias 輿論 is the older spelling, read よろん in Japanese and yúlùn in Mandarin. Korean 세론 and Cantonese sai3 leon6 are the readings of the characters.

It is a force that a government answers to, and it is formed by the views of the many, where [衆議](衆議.md) is the opinion of a particular gathering and [世間](世間.md) is the social world that watches and judges.
