## Notes
- [性](../characters/性.md) "gender, sex, quality, nature" + [質](../characters/質.md) "essence, nature" — no stand-in relationship applies (性's own stand-in is [[個性]]; 質's own is [[質素]]).

性質 means nature, character, or disposition — the inherent qualities of a person or thing.

Mandarin xìngzhì, Cantonese sing3 zat1, Japanese せいしつ, Korean 성질, and Vietnamese tính chất are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄫㄐㄧㄊ unique across both words and characters).
