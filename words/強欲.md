## Notes
- [強 (char)](../characters/強%20(char).md) "strong, powerful" + [欲](../characters/欲.md) "desire, want" — excessive, grasping desire; greed

強欲 is greed in the strong sense: a desire for gain that is overbearing and will not be satisfied, with the harsh, forceful edge that 強 gives it.  In Japanese it is the usual word for a greedy person or act (強欲な商人, "a greedy merchant"; 強欲資本主義, "greed capitalism"), and it carries moral censure, not neutral wanting.  It sits with the other words for desire in the vault, as the vicious extreme of plain [[欲望]], and it is one of the faults named in the lexipedia's Sin domain.

Japanese ごうよく keeps the older on'yomi 強 as ごう (as in 強引 and 強盗), which is why it differs from the Chinese qiáng.  The traditional spelling 強慾, with 慾 for 欲, is listed as an alias.  Mandarin qiángyù, Cantonese koeng4 juk6 and Korean 강욕 are the same compound, and `kwin` is false because the Dan'a'yo 걍욕 differs from the Korean 강욕.  No Vietnamese form was found attested, so none is given.
