## Notes
- [強 (char)](../characters/強%20(char).md) "strong, powerful" + [欲](../characters/欲.md) "desire, want" — excessive, grasping desire; greed

Wiktionary: Japanese 強欲 (ごうよく) "greedy, avaricious" and "greed, avarice", with 強慾 as the traditional spelling, kept as an alias. The Chinese 強欲 is a different word there, Hokkien "to insist on" and Taiwanese Hokkien "almost, nearly", so the Dan'a'yo sense is the Japanese one. Compare [[欲望]].
