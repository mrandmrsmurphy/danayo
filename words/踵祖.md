## Notes
- [踵](../characters/踵.md) "heel" + [祖](../characters/祖.md) "ancestor" — "Jacob, the father of the twelve tribes." Their own `stand_in` values: 踵's is [[踵骨]]; 祖's is [[祖先]]; none points here, so 踵祖 is an independent compound.

踵祖 is Jacob, the son of Isaac, whose name means "he grasps the heel": he was born holding the heel of his twin Esau (Genesis 25:26). It is a Dan'a'yo **neologism**, a meaning-based name, "heel ancestor" (踵 heel, 祖 ancestor), for the forefather of the twelve tribes, and it names the Genesis section 踵祖世代記. Compare [[笑生]] (Isaac) and [[毛父]] (Esau). See [[基督敎]].

- Hebrew name: יַעֲקֹב (Ya'aqov) (注音 ·⼘·ㄚㄎㄛㄆ).
