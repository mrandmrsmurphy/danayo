## Notes
- [後](../characters/後%20(char).md) "after" + [置](../characters/置%20(char).md) "put, place" — no stand-in relationship applies (後's own stand-in is itself; 置's own is itself).

後置 means to place after — putting something in a following position, as in [[後置詞]] ("postposition").

Mandarin hòuzhì, Cantonese hau6 zi3, Japanese こうち, Korean 후치, and Vietnamese hậu trí are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `cantonese`/`vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄨㄛㄑㄧ unique across both words and characters).
