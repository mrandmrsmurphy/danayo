## Notes
- [壴](../characters/壴%20(char).md) "to drum" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

壴 itself has no independent life in any modern CJKV language — it survives only as the shared root component behind 鼓 ("drum"), 喜 ("joy," drumming and singing), 嘉 ("auspicious"), 彭 (an ancient country/surname), 告 ("to announce"), and 豈 ("how can it be that"). Dan'a'yo revives it as a standalone verb, "to drum," reclaiming the sense its own descendant 鼓 later specialized into a noun. Assigned to the otherwise-unused syllable ㄊㄨㄛ by deliberate placement rather than its organic Middle Chinese outcome (which would have landed in the already crowded ㄐㄨ) — see [壴's character page](../characters/壴%20(char).md) for the full phonological rationale.
