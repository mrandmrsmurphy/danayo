## Notes
- [[壴]] "to drum"

壴 itself has no independent life in any modern CJKV language — it survives only as the shared root component behind 鼓 ("drum"), 喜 ("joy," drumming and singing), 嘉 ("auspicious"), 彭 (an ancient country/surname), 告 ("to announce"), and 豈 ("how can it be that"). Dan'a'yo revives it as a standalone verb, "to drum," reclaiming the sense its own descendant 鼓 later specialized into a noun. Assigned to the otherwise-unused syllable ㄊㄨㄛ by deliberate placement rather than its organic Middle Chinese outcome (which would have landed in the already crowded ㄐㄨ) — see [[characters/壴|壴's character page]] for the full phonological rationale.
