## Notes
- [拝](../characters/拝%20(char).md) "bow, kowtow" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [拝](../characters/拝%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

拝 means to bow or kowtow.

Mandarin bài, Cantonese baai3, Japanese はい, Korean 배, and Vietnamese bái are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bugs: literal `vietnamese: null` and `korean: "null"` — both corrected using the character's own citation. No homophones at the word level (注音 ㄅ⼶ is shared only with bound character 俳, whose own stand-in is [[俳優]]).
