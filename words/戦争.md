## Notes
- [戦](../characters/戦.md) "war" + [争](../characters/争.md) "fight" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [戦](../characters/戦.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (争's own stand-in is [[抗争]]).

戦争 means war.

Mandarin zhànzhēng, Cantonese zin3 zang1, Japanese せんそう, and Vietnamese chiến tranh are all standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 전쟁 uses the native-shifted 쟁 rather than the compositional 장 (from 争's own 諺文), hence `kwin: false`. Homophone of [[戦場]] "battlefield" (both 注音 ㄐㄝㄋㄐㄚㄫ).
