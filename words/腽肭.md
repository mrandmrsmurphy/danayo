## Notes
- [腽](../characters/腽.md) "fur seal" + [肭](../characters/肭.md) "fur seal" — `#cranberry`: both characters' own `stand_in` point to this exact compound (腽's and 肭's alike), so transitivity holds (A=B=AB) and both are legitimized as independent Dan'a'yo entries specifically by this word.
- 腽肭 is the stand-in word for [腽](../characters/腽.md) ("fur seal"), which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo. The term derives from Ainu *onnep* via phono-semantic matching.
