## Notes
- [食 (char)](../characters/食%20(char).md) "eat; food" + [道 (char)](../characters/道%20(char).md) "way; passage"

食道 is the esophagus: the tube that carries food from the throat to the stomach. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin shídào and Japanese しょくどう as "esophagus". Korean 식도 and Cantonese sik6 dou6 match.

It runs from the [咽喉](咽喉.md), throat, to the [胃](胃.md), stomach, and lies behind the [気管](気管.md), windpipe.
