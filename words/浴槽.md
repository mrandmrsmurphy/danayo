## Notes
- [浴](../characters/浴.md) "bathe" + [槽](../characters/槽.md) "vat, trough" — bathtub, bath. Mandarin and Cantonese use a different word (浴缸) for this referent, but Korean independently attests 욕조 as the genuine hanja reading of these exact characters, clearing "more than Japanese pedigree" on its own.

The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese fields, which were blank, now hold the compositional readings of the two characters (yùcáo, juk6 cou4, dục tào), following the vault's convention for these fields; as the Notes say, Chinese uses 浴缸 for this referent, so these are character readings and not an attested everyday word. The Korean 욕조 differs from the Dan'a'yo 諺文 욕잣 in the final syllable, so `kwin` is false. The Buildings page lists the entry simply as "bathtub," and Dan'a'yo keeps the sense "bathtub, bath." It sits beside the existing words [浴室](浴室.md) "bathroom" and [浴衣](浴衣.md) "yukata." The Notes' remark about 浴缸 is the page's existing account and has not been re-checked here.
