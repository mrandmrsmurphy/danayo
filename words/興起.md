## Notes
- [興 (char)](../characters/興%20(char).md) "to flourish, be roused" + [起 (char)](../characters/起%20(char).md) "to rise, arise" — to rise up, emerge, or flourish, especially of states, dynasties, or movements (帝国興起 "the rise of empires"). Less commonly carries the literary sense of being seized by a sudden impulse.

The Existence page lists 興起 as "to rise up, emerge, flourish," beside the bound root [興](興.md) ("to entertain, to rise up, to prosper"), and Geography uses it for the rise of empires (帝国興起). Dan'a'yo keeps the sense "to rise up, to emerge, to flourish," of a state, movement or trend coming into being and gaining strength. It is close to the existing word [勃興](勃興.md) "rise," which is the more sudden, vigorous form. The Korean 흥기 differs from the Dan'a'yo 諺文 흥키 in the second syllable's initial, so `kwin` is false. The readings are Mandarin xīngqǐ, Cantonese hing1 hei2, Japanese kōki (こうき) and Sino-Vietnamese hưng khởi. The 勃興 contrast is from memory and have not been checked against a source.
