## Notes
- [気 (char)](../characters/気%20(char).md) "feeling" + [炎](../characters/炎.md) "flame, inflammation" — 炎 here stands for 焰 "flame" in the original 氣焰

Wiktionary: Japanese 気炎 (きえん) "high spirit; enthusiasm"; Chinese 氣焰, derogatory, "arrogance; bluster" and, archaic, of a person, "momentum; imposing manner; force; energy"; Korean 기염 is the hanja form of 氣焰. The original spelling is 氣焰; 気炎 is the Japanese-style spelling, and 氣焰, 気焔 and 気焰 are kept as aliases. The Mandarin and Cantonese fields give the readings of 氣焰.
