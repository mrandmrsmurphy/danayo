## Notes
- [食 (char)](../characters/食%20(char).md) "food" + [品 (char)](../characters/品%20(char).md) "goods; article"

食品 is food as a product: an item of food, especially as made, packaged or sold. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin shípǐn as "food; foodstuff" and Japanese しょくひん as "food, foodstuff". Korean 식품, Vietnamese thực phẩm and Cantonese sik6 ban2 match.

It is food seen as goods to be bought, narrower than the plain [食物](食物.md), what is eaten, and the raw side of it is the [食材](食材.md), ingredients.
