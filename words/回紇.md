## Notes
- [回](../characters/回%20(char).md) "turn" + [紇](../characters/紇.md) "coarse thread" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [紇](../characters/紇.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (回's own stand-in is itself).

回紇 (Huihe/Uyghur) is a historical name for the Uyghur Khaganate (8th–9th century), an ancestor state of the modern Uyghur people, later renamed 回鶻.

Filled blank `korean: 회흘` (compositional). Japanese かいこつ and Vietnamese Hồi Hột (verified — a genuine historical transliteration, alongside the variants Hồi Ngột and Hồi Cốt) are both standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄛㄧㄏㄜㄊ unique across both words and characters).
