## Notes
- [藍 (char)](../characters/藍%20(char).md) "indigo plant" is separately legitimized by [[藍]] itself. [木 (char)](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree, bush" is separately legitimized by [[木]] itself. No cranberry.

Mandarin mù lán and korean 목람 both legitimately use the real term's *reversed* character order (木藍, already listed as this word's own alias), same pattern as [[薬丸]]. Japanese タイワンコマツナギ is the real specific botanical name (Taiwan indigo), not compositional — legitimate.
