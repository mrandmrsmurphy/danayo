## Notes
- [甄](../characters/甄.md) "to discern; to assess" + [選](../characters/選.md) "to choose; to select"
- This word is the viable form of [甄](../characters/甄.md): the character means to examine and discriminate, but is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo. 甄選 is its `stand_in` compound.

Wiktionary marks Mandarin zhēnxuǎn (甄选, 甄選) as literary and glosses it "to select; to pick", with 甄試 (a selection examination) as a derived term. Cantonese jan1 syun2 matches. Japanese けんせん and Korean 견선 are the Sino-Xenic readings.

In Dan'a'yo the word means to select by screening candidates, as placed on the [Mind](../lexipedia/Mind.md) page for the judgment involved, and on [Tools](../lexipedia/Tools.md) as the verb "to screen", not a physical screen. The plain verb for choosing is [選択](選択.md).
