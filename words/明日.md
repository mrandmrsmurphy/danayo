## Notes
- [明 (char)](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — literally "bright day," tomorrow.
1. Tomorrow (poetic). The normal word for 'tomorrow' is [[来日]]
2. the very near future

Mandarin míngrì, Cantonese ming4 jat6, and Korean 명일 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 明's and 日's own stored citations exactly. **Found and fixed a real bug**: `japanese` was stored as あした, a native kun'yomi word — inconsistent with this vault's standing convention that `japanese`/`korean` fields hold the Sino-Xenic bound reading, not the everyday native word. Corrected to みょうにち (myōnichi), the real Sino-Japanese compound built from 明's own MYOU citation + 日's own NICHI citation; あした and あす remain the far more common native words for "tomorrow" in everyday Japanese, with みょうにち reserved for formal/written registers — matching this word's own "poetic"/literary register in Mandarin and Cantonese as well.

`kwin` is false: 日 alone diverges from Korean on its own character page, which alone is enough to make the AND-rule false; 明 is independently kwin-true. No homophones (注音 ㄇ⼶ㄫㄋㄧㄊ unique among both words and characters).
