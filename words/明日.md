## Notes
- [明 (char)](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — literally "bright day," tomorrow.

Mandarin míngrì, Cantonese ming4 jat6, and Korean 명일 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 明's and 日's own stored citations exactly. Japanese みょうにち (myōnichi) is the Sino-Japanese compound built from 明's MYOU and 日's NICHI, reserved for formal and written registers; あした and あす are the far more common native words for "tomorrow," with a register comparable to this word's poetic status in Mandarin and Cantonese.

1. Tomorrow (poetic). The normal word for 'tomorrow' is [[来日]]
2. the very near future
