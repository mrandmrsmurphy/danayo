## Notes
- [閃](../characters/閃.md) "flash" + [光 (char)](../characters/光%20(char).md) "light" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes the bound character 閃 as a Dan'a'yo entry.

閃光 is "a flash of light; a glint". English Wiktionary gives the Chinese word as a noun, "flash (of light)", a verb, "to flash; to glisten", and, in Cantonese fandom slang, an adjective for a shiny Pokémon (色違 is the synonym). Its derived terms are 閃光燈 ("flashlight; camera flash"), 閃光點 and 閃光彈 ("flash grenade"). Japanese 閃光 (せんこう, senkō) is a noun, "flash; glint", with 緑閃光 ("green flash") as a derived term.

Mandarin shǎnguāng and Cantonese sim2 gwong1 are as at English Wiktionary. Korean 섬광 is the Sino-Korean reading; I have not checked its everyday use.

No other word shares the 注音 ㄙㄝㄇㄎ⺢ㄫ (checked 2026-10-12).
