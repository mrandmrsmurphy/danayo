## Notes
- [嫌](../characters/嫌.md) "detest, hate" + [悪 (char)](../characters/悪%20(char).md) "bad, evil" — this word is the viable form of [嫌](../characters/嫌.md): the character's meaning (to detest; to loathe) is identical, but 嫌 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 嫌悪 is its `stand_in` compound.

嫌悪 means aversion or loathing: instinctive dislike, and revulsion at something. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 嫌惡 (an alias; Mandarin xiánwù, Cantonese jim4 wu3) as "to loathe; to detest", Japanese けんお as "hatred, dislike, aversion" and, as a suffix, "-phobia" (as in 同性愛嫌悪, "homophobia"), with a verb "to hate; to dislike; to despise; to detest", and Korean 혐오 as "hatred, aversion, abhorrence". It has no Vietnamese entry, so Vietnamese hiềm ác here is built from the characters. The 悪 is read wù, "to hate", in Mandarin and Cantonese, not è, "evil".

It is a stronger, more instinctive dislike than a mild preference, set beside [[憎悪]], hatred, and the milder [[反感]], antipathy.
