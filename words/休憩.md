## Notes
- [休](../characters/休.md) "rest" + [憩](../characters/憩.md) "to rest, pause" — "to rest, take a break," a more formal/literary register than the everyday near-synonym [[休息]] (hưu tức). `vietnamese: hưu khế` is directly attested (confirmed via web search). **Real gap found and fixed on [[憩]]'s own page**: its `vietnamese` field was missing this reading khế entirely — added. **Content note**: [[休息室]]'s own page carries a note claiming "休憩 isn't [a word]" as the reason for choosing 休息室 over 休憩室 for that headword's characters — this word's own existence here shows that claim is now outdated (this word was evidently created afterward); left as historical context on that page rather than rewritten, since the actual character-choice decision it explains is still correct. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 휴커 diverges from Sino-Korean 휴게 in the second syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⼜ㄎㄝ unique among both words and characters).
