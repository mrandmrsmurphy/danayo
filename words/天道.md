## Notes
- [天 (char)](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven" + [道 (char)](../characters/道%20(char).md) "way, path" — "the Way of Heaven," a fundamental Confucian/Daoist concept of natural cosmic order; also personified as a god of heaven in some folk-religious contexts. **Real content bug found and fixed**: `注音` had the wrong initial consonant for the second syllable (ㄙㄚㄨ instead of ㄉㄚㄨ, mismatching both `道`'s own 注音 and this word's own 諺文/羅馬字 턴닷/tendau) — corrected to ㄊㄝㄋㄉㄚㄨ. Filled blank `pos: 名詞` and `vietnamese: thiên đạo`, a directly attested classical Confucian term for "the Way of Heaven." `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 턴닷 diverges from Sino-Korean 천도 in both syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄝㄋㄉㄚㄨ unique among both words and characters).
