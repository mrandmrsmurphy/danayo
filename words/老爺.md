## Notes
- [老 (char)](../characters/老%20(char).md) "old person, age" + [爺](../characters/爺.md) "father, grandfather" — 爺's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 老's own is [[老]] itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 老爺 legitimizes 爺 alone.

老爺 means grandfather, or a respectful term for a master or old gentleman.

Mandarin lǎoye, Cantonese lou5 je4, Japanese ろうや (rōya), and Korean 노야 (noya) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 老爺 is itself a common attested word for "master, elder" in all four languages. Vietnamese lão gia is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term.
