## Notes
- [娘](../characters/娘%20(char).md) "young woman" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

娘 means young woman or girl — the character is unattested before the Tang dynasty and possibly a fusion of 女郎 ("lady").

Mandarin niáng, Cantonese noeng4, Japanese にょう, Korean 낭, and Vietnamese nàng are all standard, fully compositional readings.
