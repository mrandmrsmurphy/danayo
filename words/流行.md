## Notes
- [流](../characters/流.md) "flow" + [行 (char)](../characters/行%20(char).md) "go; travel"

流行 is fashion or a fad, and to be in vogue; it also means to spread, as a disease does. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin liúxíng as "popular; in vogue; fashionable" and "to spread; to become endemic", and Japanese りゅうこう as "fashion; vogue; prevalence (of disease); become epidemic". Vietnamese lưu hành is the same word, with the sense "circulate". Korean 류행 and Cantonese lau4 hang4 match.

What it describes is something that many people take up for a time. A practice that lasts for generations is a [習俗](習俗.md), a custom.
