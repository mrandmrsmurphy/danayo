## Notes
- [清](../characters/清.md) "pure, clear" + [酒](../characters/酒.md) "alcohol" — no stand-in relationship applies (清's own stand-in is [[清潔]]; 酒's own is [[酒精]]).

清酒 means sake (Japanese rice wine); also the real Korean name for cheongju, a Korean rice wine.

Mandarin qīngjiǔ and Japanese せいしゅ are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 清's and 酒's own stored citations. Vietnamese sake is the real, standard loanword for Japanese sake specifically.
