## Notes
- [清](../characters/清.md) "pure, clear" + [酒](../characters/酒.md) "alcohol" — no stand-in relationship applies (清's own stand-in is [[清潔]]; 酒's own is [[酒精]]).

清酒 means sake (Japanese rice wine); also the real Korean name for cheongju, a Korean rice wine.

Mandarin qīngjiǔ and Japanese せいしゅ are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 清's and 酒's own stored citations. Fixed real bugs: `cantonese` had been doubled with a malformed second variant (cing1 zau2, ceng1 zau2 → cing1 zau2); `korean` had been the bare native word 술 ("alcohol" generically) instead of the compositional 청주 (matching 酒's own Sino citation 주) — corrected, and 청주 is genuinely the real Korean name for this rice wine. Vietnamese sake is the real, standard loanword for Japanese sake specifically. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄫㄐㄨㄛ unique across both words and characters).
