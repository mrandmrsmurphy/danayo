## Notes
- [胡](../characters/胡.md) "reckless" + [蝶](../characters/蝶.md) "butterfly"
- This word is the viable form of [蝶](../characters/蝶.md): the character's meaning (butterfly) is identical, but 蝶 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 胡蝶 is its `stand_in` compound.

The first syllable 胡 does not contribute its own meaning here (the "reckless" gloss above is only the character's standalone sense): it is a sound-supporting syllable that attaches to 蝶, the character that actually carries "butterfly," and was later given the insect radical as 蝴 in the modern Chinese form 蝴蝶 (the alias). The older 胡蝶 is the spelling of the classical texts, most famously the butterfly dream of the *Zhuangzi*, in which Zhuang Zhou dreams he is a butterfly and wakes unsure which he is, and it is the form that survives in Japanese: こちょう (kochō), as in the stage names and motifs of 胡蝶 in classical literature.

The readings are Mandarin húdié, Cantonese wu4 dip6, Japanese kochō and Korean 호접 (Korean also uses the native 나비). Dan'a'yo keeps the shared sense "butterfly" with no divergence, in the classical spelling 胡蝶. It is placed under [Animals](../lexipedia/Animals.md) as the stand-in that makes the bound character 蝶 usable.
