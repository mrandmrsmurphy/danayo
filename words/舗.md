## Notes
- [舗](../characters/舗%20(char).md) "store" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 舗 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

Mandarin pù, cantonese pou3, and korean 포 (po) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese has no native kun-reading (stored as ø on the character page), so the on-reading ホ (HO) is used directly. Vietnamese phô is one of three stored candidates. No homophones.
