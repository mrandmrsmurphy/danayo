## Notes
- [減](../characters/減.md) "decrease, reduce" + [字 (char)](../characters/字%20(char).md) "character" — literally "character-reduction". No stand-in relationship applies (減's own `stand_in` is [[減算]]; 字's is itself).

減字 is the reduction of the number of characters: keeping the inventory of the writing system as small as it can be while it still serves. In the chapter on the writing system it is set against [[複合]][[－化]] (compounding): the language 減字化, "becomes reduced in characters", while words are lengthened by compounding. It is a plain compound and is not a word in the source languages' dictionaries in this sense. (Mandarin 減字 appears only in the names 減字木蘭花 and 減字譜, the guqin notation.) It combines with the fully productive suffix [[－化]] to make 減字化.

The `japanese`, `korean` and `cantonese` fields hold the compositional readings, none separately verified; Korean 감자 is the same sequence as the unrelated word for "potato", which Dan'a'yo's own 감지 avoids, so `kwin` is false. Vietnamese is omitted. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄇㄐㄧ unique across both words and characters).
