## Notes
- [減](../characters/減.md) "decrease, reduce" + [字 (char)](../characters/字%20(char).md) "character" — literally "character-reduction". No stand-in relationship applies (減's own `stand_in` is [[減算]]; 字's is itself).

Wiktionary has no entry for 減字; it is a plain Dan'a'yo compound of 減 "decrease, reduce" and 字 "character", the reduction of the number of characters. In the chapter on the writing system (`文法 - 03文字法`) it is set against [[複合]][[－化]] (compounding): the language 減字化, "becomes reduced in characters", while words are lengthened by compounding. It combines with the fully productive suffix [[－化]] to make 減字化.
