## Notes
- [夫 (char)](../characters/夫%20(char).md) "husband; that" + [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both are bare self-standing characters).

夫人 specifically denotes another person's wife (a term of respect and formality), not one's own. Mandarin fūrén, Cantonese fu1 jan4, Japanese ふじん, Korean 부인, and Vietnamese phu nhân are all standard, everyday readings for "madam; lady; (someone else's) wife."
