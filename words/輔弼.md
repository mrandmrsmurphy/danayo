## Etymology
- [輔](../characters/輔.md) "assist" + [弼](../characters/弼.md) "assistant to the emperor"

## Notes
A rare, classical-register word — pairs two near-synonymous "assist a ruler" roots into a single term for the office/act of ministerial assistance to a monarch.

Mandarin fǔbì, cantonese fu6 bat6, japanese ほひつ, and korean 보필 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese phụ bật is the honest compositional reading (輔's own phụ + 弼's own bật); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 부빋 diverges from Sino-Korean 보필 in both syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄨㄅㄧㄊ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 弼's own `stand_in` is this exact word (輔弼), so 弼 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 輔弼; 輔's own `stand_in` is instead 輔佐, so this word does not legitimize 輔.
