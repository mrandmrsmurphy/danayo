## Notes
- [味](../characters/味.md) "taste" + [噌](../characters/噌.md) "sound of speech"

味噌 is miso: a paste of fermented soybeans and grain, used for soup and seasoning. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin wèicēng as "miso" and Japanese みそ as "miso (fermented soybean paste)", and also "the innards of a crab or shrimp resembling miso". Cantonese mei6 zang1 matches, and Korean 미쟁 is the reading of the characters. The Japanese みそ is a native reading of the characters, and 噌 appears only in this word.

It is made from the [大豆](大豆.md), soybean, and is a seasoning beside [醤油](醤油.md), soy sauce. Seasonings in general are [調味料](調味料.md).
