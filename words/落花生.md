## Notes
- [落](../characters/落.md) "fall" + [花](../characters/花.md) "flower" + [生](../characters/生.md) "born"

落花生 is a peanut: the seed of *Arachis hypogaea*, which ripens underground after its flower falls. Wiktionary glosses both Mandarin luòhuāshēng and Japanese らっかせい as "peanut". Korean 낙화생 is the same word, and Cantonese lok6 faa1 sang1 matches.

It is a legume, a [豆](豆.md), though it grows underground, and its name describes that: the flower falls and the pod is born. It is not the [大豆](大豆.md), soybean.
