## Notes
- [陰 (char)](../characters/陰%20(char).md) "Yin, shade" + [核 (char)](../characters/核%20(char).md) "nucleus"

陰核 is the standard anatomical term for the clitoris. Mandarin 陰核/阴核 (yīnhé), Cantonese 陰核 (jam1hat6), Japanese 陰核 (いんかく), and Korean 음핵 (eumhaek) are all the medical term used across the CJKV sphere. Sino-Vietnamese instead uses âm vật ("yin thing/object") — a native Vietnamese medical euphemism built on 陰's own "âm" but paired with vật ("object, thing," 物) rather than a reading of 核 itself, unlike the other four languages' direct compositional calques.
