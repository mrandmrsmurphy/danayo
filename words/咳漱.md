## Notes
- [咳](../characters/咳.md) "cough" + [漱](../characters/漱.md) "gargle" — `characters/咳.md`'s own `stand_in` points to this word (咳漱), meaning 咳 is a bound morpheme that never appears independently outside this compound. 漱's own `stand_in` points elsewhere (含漱). The word is titled 咳漱 rather than the more familiar 咳嗽 because Dan'a'yo folds 嗽 into 漱 (listed as 漱's alias), the same character-merging convention documented for 叛→反 elsewhere in the vault; 咳嗽 is kept as an `aliases:` entry.
- Korean 해수 and Japanese がいそう are both real, standard words for "cough" (verified via search) — but specifically the formal/medical register (traditional Korean medicine, Japanese clinical terminology), distinct from the everyday native words 기침 (Korean) and せき (Japanese). Not an error, a genuine register distinction.
- `vietnamese: khái thấu` combines 咳's reading khái (confirmed on Wiktionary as a genuine reading of 咳 in the cough sense) with 漱's thấu; another source suggested hài thấu, a reading not listed on 咳's character page.
