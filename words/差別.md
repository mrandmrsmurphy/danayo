## Notes
- [差](../characters/差.md) "difference; discrepancy" + [別 (char)](../characters/別%20(char).md) "distinguish; separate" — this word is the stand-in for [差](../characters/差.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

差別 in Mandarin and classical usage names a plain difference or disparity between things, a comparatively neutral sense. In modern Japanese and Korean, however, さべつ/차별 has narrowed sharply to mean specifically social/legal discrimination against a group of people (差別待遇 "discriminatory treatment," 人種差別 "racial discrimination") — the dominant everyday sense in both languages today.

**Vietnamese sai biệt (verified) preserves the older, neutral "difference/disparity" sense rather than the narrowed discriminatory one** — modern Vietnamese for "discrimination" instead uses phân biệt đối xử, built on a different character (分, "divide," not 差), so the two languages' compounds diverged from a shared root concept along different paths. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄚㄅㄝㄊ unique across both words and characters).
