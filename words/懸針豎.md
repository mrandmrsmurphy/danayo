## Notes
- [懸 (char)](../characters/懸%20(char).md) "hang; suspend" + [針 (char)](../characters/針%20(char).md) "needle" + [豎](../characters/豎.md) "vertical line"

Wiktionary has no entry for 懸針豎; it is a compound of 懸 "to hang; to suspend", 針 "needle" and [[豎]], which Wiktionary glosses in Chinese calligraphy as "a vertical stroke (㇑)". It names the hanging-needle vertical, a vertical stroke tapering to a point at its lower end, the pointed counterpart of the rounded [[垂露豎]]. It is the first sub-variety listed under the vertical stroke on the [[Stroke]] page. Wikipedia (Eight Principles of Yong) lists 弩 and 努 as the classical names of the vertical principle, and 鐵柱 as Li Puguang's name for it. Its readings are compositional.
