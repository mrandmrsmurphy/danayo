## Notes
- [懸 (char)](../characters/懸%20(char).md) "hang; suspend" + [針 (char)](../characters/針%20(char).md) "needle" + [豎](../characters/豎.md) "vertical line"

**懸針豎** is the *hanging-needle vertical*, one of the two principal kinds of vertical stroke ([[豎]]) in regular script. The stroke tapers to a sharp point at its lower end, like a needle hanging by its eye; Chinese calligraphy instruction cites 十, 中, 丰 and 平 as characters whose long vertical ends this way. It is the pointed counterpart of the rounded [[垂露豎]].

On the [[Stroke]] page it is the first sub-variety listed under the vertical stroke, the third of the eight principles of the 永字八法, whose classical name is 努. The pairing 懸針垂露 ("hanging needle, dewdrop") is a standard calligraphers' contrast of the two endings.

The Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and North Korean forms given here are compositional (the Japanese 竪 variant 懸針竪 is listed among the aliases). No Vietnamese form was found attested, so none is given.
