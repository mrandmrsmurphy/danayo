## Notes
- [講](../characters/講.md) "lecture" + [義](../characters/義.md) "meaning; principle"

講義 is a lecture: a spoken lesson given by a teacher, and the act of giving it. Wiktionary glosses Japanese こうぎ as "a spoken lesson; to teach, give a lecture". Mandarin jiǎngyì is recorded there as "teaching materials, printouts" and, archaically, "to expound the classics". Dan'a'yo takes the Japanese sense. Korean 강의 matches.

It is the usual form of teaching in a university, where the [教授](教授.md) lectures to the [大学生](大学生.md). It differs from [授業](授業.md), the lesson at school generally.
