## Notes
- [唱](../characters/唱.md) "sing" + [片](../characters/片%20(char).md) "slice, disc" — "singing disc," a gramophone record. Their own `stand_in` values: 唱's is [[唱歌]]; 片's is itself; none points here, so 唱片 is an independent compound.

唱片 is a record: a disc, originally vinyl, carrying recorded sound. Mandarin chàngpiàn and Cantonese coeng3 pin3-2 are the ordinary Chinese words. Japanese says レコード and Korean 레코드, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[音楽]] (music) and [[録音]] (to record sound).
