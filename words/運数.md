## Etymology
- [運](../characters/運.md) "move, transport" + [数](../characters/数.md) "count, number"

## Notes
Mandarin yùnshù, cantonese wan6 sou3, korean 운수, and vietnamese vận số are all directly attested standard readings. **Found and fixed a real-equivalent-instead-of-own-reading bug on `japanese`**: うんせい is actually the reading of the different (though near-synonymous) word 運勢 ("fortune," using 勢 rather than 数) — corrected to the honest compositional うんすう (運's own UN + 数's own SUU). `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 운수 matches Sino-Korean 운수 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄨㄋㄙㄨ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 運's `stand_in` (運送) nor 数's own `stand_in` (計数) points to this compound.
