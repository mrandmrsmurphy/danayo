## Notes
- [烏](../characters/烏.md) "crow" + [龍 (char)](../characters/龍%20(char).md) "dragon" + [麺 (char)](../characters/麺%20(char).md) "flour, noodles" — literally "black dragon noodles," the standard Chinese-character rendering of Japanese udon; pronunciation follows the native Japanese word うどん rather than a compositional on'yomi reading, matched by the Korean loanword 우동.
