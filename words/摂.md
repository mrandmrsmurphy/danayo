## Notes
- [摂](../characters/摂%20(char).md) "vicarious" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [摂](../characters/摂%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

摂 means vicarious, as in acting on behalf of another (as in 摂政, "regent," or 摂影/攝影, "photography," lit. "capturing" an image).

Mandarin shè, Cantonese sip3, Japanese せつ, and Korean 섭 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bugs: literal `vietnamese: null` and `korean: "null"`. Vietnamese nhiếp (as in 攝影 nhiếp ảnh, "photography") is the well-attested standard reading, though notably absent from the character's own stored `vietnamese` field (currently blank — a gap worth a future character-level check, not fixed here). Homophone of [[捻]] "twist" (both 注音 ㄋㄝㄆ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words.
