## Notes
- [刈 (char)](../characters/刈%20(char).md) "cut; mow"

刈 is 形声: semantic [[刀]] ("knife") + phonetic 乂 (itself originally depicting a sickle, carrying both sound and meaning). It means "to mow, cut, reap" — most concretely of grass, grain, or hair (刈り取る in Japanese, "to reap, harvest").

Mandarin yì, Cantonese ngaai6, and Japanese がい (gai, on'yomi, rare in modern use) alongside native か(る) (ka(ru), "to mow, cut, reap," the everyday verb, as in 芝刈り, "lawn mowing") all preserve the sense. Korean 예 (ye) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 베다 (beda, "to cut, mow"). Vietnamese ngãi is the Hán Việt reading.

This word shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading ('yai/얘/⼘ㄧ) with another word still awaiting its own turn in this sweep, [[涯]] ("horizon") — the reciprocal half of this callout will be completed when it comes up.
