## Notes
- [孔](../characters/孔%20(char).md) "surname (Confucius)" + [教](../characters/教.md) "teaching" — no stand-in relationship applies (孔's own stand-in is itself; 教's own is [[教授]]).

孔教 means Confucianism — literally "the teaching of Confucius (孔子)," the ethical and philosophical tradition.

Mandarin kǒngjiào, Cantonese hung2 gaau3, Japanese こうきょう, Korean 공교, and Vietnamese Khổng giáo are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `羅馬字` had 教's syllable mistyped gyau→gyou (諺文/注音 were already correct, matching every sibling compound of 教). Fixed missing "(char)" suffix on `characters` for 孔. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄛㄫㄍ⼄ㄨ unique across both words and characters).
