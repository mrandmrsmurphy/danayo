## Notes
- [連 (char)](../characters/連%20(char).md) "link, join" + [襟](../characters/襟.md) "lapel, collar" — husbands of sisters.

連襟 is the relation between men married to sisters. It is "link-lapel," the image of their garments joined at the collar. Mandarin liánjīn (the standard written term; Cantonese lin4 kam1) is confirmed on Wiktionary, Chinese only, with regional spoken forms such as 老襟 and 姨夫. It is the term for a man's relation to the husband of his wife's sister, and the counterpart of the in-law terms of ego's generation, [[姉夫]] and [[妹婿]] being sisters' husbands. It is a schema word in the family of in-law terms built from the marriage relation and the sibling roots, coined for predictability rather than a claim about ancient usage.
