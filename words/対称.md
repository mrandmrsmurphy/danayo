## Notes
- [対](../characters/対.md) "oppose; face" + [称](../characters/称.md) "to call; scale" — no stand-in relationship applies (対's own `stand_in` is [[反対]], 称's is [[名称]]).

対称 is "symmetry, symmetric" — 称 contributes not its primary "to call, appellation" sense (as in its own stand-in [[名称]]) but its secondary, polyphonic sense "to weigh, to balance, to match" (chèn/chèng in Mandarin, a distinct reading from the default chēng), reflected in the `mandarin`/`cantonese` fields' dual forms. The underlying image is a balanced scale — two sides matching in weight — extended to visual/mathematical symmetry generally.

Japanese たいしょう (homophonous in Japanese with [[対照]] and [[対象]], though written and read distinctly in the other three languages), Korean 대칭, and Vietnamese đối xứng are all standard, non-drifted terms. No homophones at the Dan'a'yo level (注音 ㄉㄛㄧㄑㄧㄫ unique across both words and characters, unlike the real Japanese three-way collision).
