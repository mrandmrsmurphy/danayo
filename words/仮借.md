## Notes
- [仮](../characters/仮.md) "borrow, temporary" + [借 (char)](../characters/借%20(char).md) "to borrow" — "phonetic loan," one of the traditional 六書 (Six Categories) of Chinese character formation, where an existing character is borrowed purely for its sound to write an unrelated word with no dedicated character of its own. Directly attested across all five languages: mandarin jiǎjiè, japanese かしゃ, korean 가차 (a standard term in Korean character-formation linguistics), vietnamese giả tá. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 가자 diverges from the compositional Sino-Korean 가차 via the AND-rule, since 借's own field independently diverges (despite 仮 itself being `kwin: true`). No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄐㄚ unique among both words and characters). Legitimizing note: 仮's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, so 仮 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.
