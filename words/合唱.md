## Notes
- [合 (char)](../characters/合%20(char).md) "join; together" + [唱](../characters/唱.md) "sing"

合唱 is to sing together, or a chorus: singing by a group in harmony. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin héchàng as "to sing in a chorus" and Japanese がっしょう as "singing in chorus". Korean 합창 and Vietnamese hợp xướng are the same word. Cantonese hap6 coeng3 matches.

It is the group counterpart of the [独唱](独唱.md), solo, and the singers form a [楽団](楽団.md), band, or choir. The plain act of singing is [唱歌](唱歌.md).
