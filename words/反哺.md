## Notes
- [反](../characters/反%20(char).md) "reverse, in return" + [哺](../characters/哺.md) "suckle, feed" — no stand-in relationship applies (反 is its own stand-in; 哺's own is [[哺乳]]).

反哺 means to repay one's parents' care in return — literally "feed back," an idiom for filial devotion (as when a grown child supports aging parents), by analogy with a fledgling crow feeding its parents.

Japanese はんぽ, Korean 반포, and Vietnamese phản bộ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄈㄛㄋㄅㄛ unique across both words and characters).
