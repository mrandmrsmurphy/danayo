## Notes
- [週](../characters/週.md) "week" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 週 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 日 is added specifically to give 週 a two-character legitimizing form, not to form a real cross-linguistic compound.

Japanese しゅう (shū) uses bare 週 alone, matching the real everyday Japanese word for "week." `mandarin`/`cantonese` are likewise stored as the bare real words (zhōu/zau1, matching 週's own stored citations exactly) rather than compositional zhōurì/zau1 jat6 — deliberately, since a compositional reading with 日 attached would actually suggest "Sunday" to a reader of real Chinese, a different and misleading sense. Korean 주일 is compositional (주 + 일) and is itself a real, attested Sino-Korean word for "a week." Vietnamese tuần is the real native word, unrelated in form to either system.

`kwin` is false: 週 alone already diverges from Korean on its own character page, which alone is enough to make the AND-rule false. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄨㄛㄋㄧㄊ unique among both words and characters).
