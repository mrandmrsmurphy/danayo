## Notes
- [税](../characters/税.md) "duty, tax" + [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal, gold, money" — 税's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 金's own is [[金]] itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 税 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 税 is individually `kwin: false`.

**Filled a real-world-verified `korean`**: 金's own character page stores its surname reading 김 (Kim), but in ordinary compounds — as established across [[基金]], [[黄金]], and [[金属]] — 金 is instead read 금 (geum). This word's `korean` is accordingly 세금 (segeum), the extremely common everyday Korean word for "tax," not the mechanical-but-wrong 서김.

税金 is the general word for tax or duty across the sphere — a mandatory payment to the government, whether income tax, sales tax, or customs duty.

Mandarin shuìjīn, Cantonese seoi3 gam1, Japanese ぜいきん (zeikin), and Korean 세금 (segeum) are all standard, fully compositional readings, each the ordinary everyday word for "tax" in its language. Vietnamese thuế kim is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form; everyday Vietnamese overwhelmingly prefers the bare native word thuế alone.
