## Notes
- [君](../characters/君%20(char).md) "ruler, gentleman" + [子](../characters/子.md) "master, honorific suffix" — no stand-in relationship applies (君 is its own stand-in; 子's own is [[児子]]).

君子 (junzi) means a noble or superior person — the Confucian ideal of a morally cultivated gentleman.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `mandarin`/`cantonese` had been merged into a single string ("jūnzi,gwan1zi2"), leaving `cantonese` blank — split apart and corrected to jūnzǐ / gwan1 zi2. Japanese くんし, Korean 군자, and Vietnamese quân tử are all standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄨㄋㄐㄜ unique across both words and characters).
