## Notes
- [古](../characters/古.md) "ancient" + [今](../characters/今%20(char).md) "now" — no stand-in relationship applies (古's own stand-in is [[古代]]; 今 is its own stand-in).

古今 means "ancient and modern" or "old and new" — spanning all of time (古今東西 "all times and places").

Japanese こきん, Korean 고금, and Vietnamese cổ kim are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄍㄧㄇ unique across both words and characters).
