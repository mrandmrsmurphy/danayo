## Notes
- [戴](../characters/戴%20(char).md) "be crowned with; wear on top" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [戴](../characters/戴%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

戴 means to be crowned with or wear on top (as a hat, crown, or honor).

Mandarin dài, Cantonese daai3, Japanese たい, Korean 대, and Vietnamese dải are all standard, fully compositional readings. Three-way Dan'a'yo-level homophone with [[帯]] "sash; belt" and [[太]] "great; extreme; excessive" (all 注音 ㄊㄚㄧ), fully cross-referenced on all three pages.
