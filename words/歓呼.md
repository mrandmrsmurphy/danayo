## Notes
- [歓](../characters/歓.md) "glad, happy" + [呼](../characters/呼%20(char).md) "call, shout" — no stand-in relationship applies (歓's own stand-in is [[歓喜]]; 呼's own is itself).

歓呼 means to hail or cheer.

Mandarin huānhū, Cantonese fun1 fu1, Korean 환호, and Vietnamese hoan hô (the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form, since 歓's own citation has no vietnamese value stored — a character-level gap flagged for future perfecting) are all standard readings. Fixed real bugs: `japanese` was garbled with an obsolete kana spelling (くわんこ) and a duplicate value containing an invisible zero-width character (か​んこ) — corrected to the single modern form かんこ. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⺢ㄋㄏㄛ unique across both words and characters).
