## Notes
- [漸](../characters/漸.md) "gradually" + [進](../characters/進.md) "advance"

漸進 is gradual advance: to move forward step by step, or progress made in that way. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin jiànjìn as "to make gradual progress; to advance gradually" and Japanese as "gradual progress; advance gradually". Cantonese zim6 zeon3 and Korean 점진 match.

It is used of reform, change or growth that does not come all at once. The opposite pace of change is sudden, and the forward step itself is in [進歩](進歩.md), progress.
