## Notes
- [包](../characters/包.md) "to wrap, enclose" + [囲](../characters/囲.md) "to encircle" — "to encircle, surround" (a military siege, or more generally to hem in). `vietnamese: bao vi` is compositional from each character's own confirmed reading (domain knowledge, not independently confirmed by search); everyday Vietnamese more often says bao vây (a different second root) for this concept.
