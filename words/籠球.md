## Notes
- [籠 (char)](../characters/籠%20(char).md) "basket" + [球 (char)](../characters/球%20(char).md) "sphere, ball" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both 籠's and 球's own are themselves), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 球 is individually `kwin: false`.

**Fixed real bugs**: `mandarin`/`vietnamese` had lánqiú/bóng rổ — both belong to the alias 籃球 (the actual standard Chinese term for basketball, "basket-ball" using 籃 "basket" rather than 籠 "cage") and the native Vietnamese term, respectively, not the headword 籠球's own compositional readings. Corrected to lóngqiú (籠's own lóng + 球's own qiú) and lung cầu (籠's own lung + 球's own cầu).

籠球 names basketball — literally "cage/basket ball," an older or more literal Sino-form for the sport now more commonly written 籃球 in Mandarin.

Cantonese lung4 kau4, Japanese ろうきゅう (rōkyū, an older/formal term — modern Japanese overwhelmingly uses the loanword バスケットボール, basukettobōru), and Korean 롱구 (ronggu) are all standard, fully compositional readings.
