## Notes
- [彫](../characters/彫.md) "carve, sculpt" + [刻](../characters/刻.md) "carve, engrave" — no stand-in relationship applies (彫's own stand-in is itself; 刻's own is [[刻印]]).

彫刻 means to carve or sculpt — shaping a form by cutting into a hard material.

Mandarin diāokè, Cantonese diu1 hak1, Japanese ちょうこく, Korean 조각, and Vietnamese điêu khắc are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `諺文` had 彫's syllable missing its final coda (초→촛, the same recurring typo as [[彫像]]; 羅馬字/注音 were already correct). Filled blank `vietnamese`, reformatted `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄛㄨㄎㄨㄎ unique across both words and characters).

- **彫塑 (Chōso / Diāosù / Joso):** Emphasizes that **彫** is the **3D, artistic, subtractive sculpting** of forms (often with a chisel).
- **刻印 (Kokuin / Kèyìn / Gakin):** Emphasizes that **刻** is the **precise, 2D, knife-based incising** of marks, text, or seals.
