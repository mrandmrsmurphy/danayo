## Notes
- [作 (char)](../characters/作%20(char).md) "make" + [品 (char)](../characters/品%20(char).md) "item; product"

作品 is a work: a thing made as art, such as a painting, a piece of music or a book. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin zuòpǐn as "work ; composition" and Japanese さくひん. Korean 작품 and Vietnamese tác phẩm are the same word. Cantonese zok3 ban2 matches.

It is what a [作者](作者.md) or [芸術家](芸術家.md) leaves behind, and the best of them are [傑作](傑作.md), masterpieces. Written works are the [着作](着作.md).
