## Notes
- [魏](../characters/魏.md) "Wei" + [晋](../characters/晋.md) "increase, advance" — no `#cranberry`: 魏's own `stand_in` is [[魏国]], 晋's is [[晋升]]; neither points here.

This is a rare, technical term for discussing the combined Wei-Jin period of Chinese history (as in 魏晋南北朝, the "Wei-Jin, Northern and Southern Dynasties" era), a common unit of periodization in Chinese historiography. Unlike [[魏国]] (which uses only 魏's own bare reading in Japanese/Korean/Vietnamese, since a single historical state is conventionally called by its bare name), this compound period-name is read compositionally: Japanese ぎしん, Korean 위진, and Sino-Vietnamese Ngụy Tấn.
