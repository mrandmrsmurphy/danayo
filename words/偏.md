## Notes
- [偏 (char)](../characters/偏%20(char).md) "slanted; inclined; biased"

偏 is 形声: semantic 亻 ("person") + phonetic [[扁]]. It describes something tilted, leaning, or off-center — literally slanted or inclined — extending naturally to the figurative sense "biased, partial, one-sided" (偏見, "prejudice, bias"; 偏心, "partiality, favoritism"), the opposite of balanced impartiality.

Mandarin piān, Cantonese pin1, and Japanese へん (hen, on'yomi) alongside native かたよ(る) (katayo(ru), "to be biased, lean to one side") all preserve the sense. Korean 편 (pyeon) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 치우치다 (chiuchida, "to be biased, lean toward"). Vietnamese thiên is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in thiên vị ("to be partial, show favoritism," directly matching this word's "biased" sense); the character page's other two stored candidates, xen ("interspersed") and xiên ("slanted, oblique" — itself a real native word close in meaning, though not a genuine reading of 偏), were not used here.

A character-only match (扁) shares the same 注音 but has no independent word page of its own, so is not a real word-level homophone collision.

Legitimizing note: 偏 (char)'s own `stand_in` is this exact word (itself), so 偏 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by itself.
