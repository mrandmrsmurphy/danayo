## Notes
- [光](../characters/光%20(char).md) "light" + [州](../characters/州%20(char).md) "state, province" — the name of Gwangju, the main city of the southwestern region of Korea.

光州 (Gwangju) is the chief city of the old 全羅 territory (see [[全羅]]). Its full modern name is 光州廣域市 (광주광역시), "Gwangju Metropolitan City", a separate unit since 1986, first as a 直轄市, and renamed 廣域市 in 1995 (from memory, not re-checked). The city is best known outside Korea for the Gwangju Uprising of May 1980 (from memory, not re-checked). The name 光州 is reported as given in the early Goryeo period, in place of an earlier name (from memory, not re-checked).

**Not to be confused with** another Korean city of the same Korean reading, 광주, written 廣州 (a city in Gyeonggi near Seoul, from memory, not re-checked), nor with Guangzhou (Canton) in China, also written 廣州 and read Guǎngzhōu; Chinese Wiktionary also records a historical 光州 prefecture in Henan, in today's Huangchuan.

English and Chinese Wiktionary have the Korean city under Mandarin Guāngzhōu and Cantonese gwong1 zau1, and the Korean entry is the hanja form of 광주. No Japanese or Vietnamese entry was found: Japanese usually writes the Korean city in katakana, クァンジュ (from memory, not re-checked); こうしゅう and Quang Châu are the compositional Sino-Xenic readings from the character pages.
