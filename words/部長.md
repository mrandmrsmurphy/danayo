## Notes
- [部 (char)](../characters/部%20(char).md) "part" + [長 (char)](../characters/長%20(char).md) "chief, elder" — "head, chief, secretary, minister."

Mandarin bùzhǎng, cantonese bou6 zoeng2, japanese ぶちょう, and korean 부장 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese bộ trưởng is a directly attested, extremely common Vietnamese word ("minister"), matching 部's own bộ + 長's own trưởng (a variant of trường) exactly. `部长` is the standard simplified-character spelling variant (部→部, 長→长 — the second character simplifies), correctly listed as an alias.
