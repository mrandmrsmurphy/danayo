## Notes
- [助](../characters/助.md) "help" + [動 (char)](../characters/動%20(char).md) "move" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "word"

助動詞 is an auxiliary verb. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 助動詞 (Mandarin zhùdòngcí, Cantonese zo6 dung6 ci4) as the grammar term "auxiliary verb" and Japanese じょどうし as "an auxiliary verb" in grammars of other languages; for Japanese and Korean grammar it names a class of bound forms that attach to inflected words to add grammatical functions. Korean 조동사 and Vietnamese trợ động từ are listed as "auxiliary verb". Compare [動詞](動詞.md), the verb.
