## Notes
- [儀](../characters/儀.md) "ceremonial" + [礼 (char)](../characters/礼%20(char).md) "manners" — "etiquette, ceremony, courtesy" (儀礼的, "ceremonial, as a matter of formality"). Mandarin yílǐ, Cantonese ji4 lai5, Japanese ぎれい, Korean 의례, and Vietnamese nghi lễ are all directly attested standard readings. 儀禮 is also the name of one of the Confucian Three Rites classics (alongside 周禮 and 禮記), a ritual manual traditionally dated to the Warring States period.
