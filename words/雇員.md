## Notes
- [雇](../characters/雇.md) "employ" + [員](../characters/員.md) "personel, employee"

雇員 means an employee, especially a hired staff member (as opposed to a permanent civil servant or officeholder). Mandarin 雇員/雇员 (gùyuán), Cantonese 雇員 (gu3jyun4), Japanese 雇員 (こいん), and Korean 고원 (gowon) all use this compositional term. Sino-Vietnamese instead uses the far more common nhân viên ("staff, employee," built on 人員 "person-member" rather than 雇員's own literal "hire-member") — the standard everyday Vietnamese word for an employee, in contrast to the other four languages' shared compositional form.
