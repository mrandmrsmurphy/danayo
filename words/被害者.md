## Notes
- [被 (char)](../characters/被%20(char).md) "-ee (but a prefix)" + [害](../characters/害.md) "damage; harm" + [者](../characters/者.md) "-er"

被害者 is the victim: the person who suffers harm. It is built from 被 "-ee", 害 "damage, harm" and 者 "-er", literally "the one who is harmed". Wiktionary glosses Mandarin bèihàizhě and Japanese ひがいしゃ as "victim; casualty", with Korean 피해자 the same word. Cantonese bei6 hoi6 ze2 matches.

The word is the counterpart of [犯人](犯人.md), the one who does the harm. It is not limited to crime, since it covers a victim of disaster or accident as well.
