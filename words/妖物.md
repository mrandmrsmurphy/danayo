## Notes
- [妖](../characters/妖.md) "bewitching, demonic" + [物 (char)](../characters/物%20(char).md) "thing, creature" — "monster, bewitching figure" (a supernatural or demonic creature). `japanese: ようぶつ` is an on'yomi compound reading (the more common Japanese word for this concept is 妖怪, ようかい); `vietnamese: yêu vật` is compositional, an attested classical term for a monstrous/bewitching creature, alongside the more common yêu quái (domain knowledge, not independently confirmed by search).
