## Notes
- [教](../characters/教.md) "religion" + [区](../characters/区.md) "district" — "church district," a diocese or parish. Their own `stand_in` values: 教's is [[教授]]; 区's is [[区域]]; none points here, so 教区 is an independent compound.

教区 is a diocese or parish: the district a bishop or a priest serves. Mandarin jiàoqū (教區) and Cantonese gaau3 keoi1 are the ordinary words, and Japanese きょうく and Korean 교구 are the same compound. Compare [[主教]] (bishop) and [[教会]] (church).
