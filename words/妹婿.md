## Notes
- [妹](../characters/妹.md) "younger sister" + [婿 (char)](../characters/婿%20(char).md) "son-in-law" — younger sister's husband.

妹婿 is the husband of one's younger sister. It is "younger sister-son-in-law." Mandarin mèixù (Cantonese mui6 sai3) and Japanese いもうとむこ are confirmed; the more common Chinese form is 妹夫 (mèifū), kept here as an alias. It pairs with [[姉夫]] and is one of the in-law terms of ego's generation; the hypernym for a younger affinal brother is [[姻弟]]. It is a schema word in the family of in-law terms built from the marriage relation and the sibling roots, coined for predictability rather than a claim about ancient usage.
