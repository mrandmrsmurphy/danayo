## Notes
- [百科](百科.md) "all knowledge" + [事典](事典.md) "reference book" — a book covering every branch of knowledge.

Mandarin bǎikēshìdiǎn, Cantonese baak3 fo1 si6 din2, and Japanese ひゃっかじてん (hyakka jiten) are all straightforwardly compositional. Korean 백과사전 (baekgwasajeon) is the standard modern term built the same way, though 百科全書 (백과전서, "complete book of all branches," using 全書 "complete book" instead of 事典 "reference work") is also a real, still-used alternate Korean form with the same meaning. Vietnamese bách khoa toàn thư follows this second pattern — "complete book" rather than "reference work" — making it the standard Vietnamese term for encyclopedia despite the slight mismatch with this word's own 事典-based characters.
