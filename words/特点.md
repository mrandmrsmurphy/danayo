## Notes
- [特](../characters/特.md) "special" + [点](../characters/点%20(char).md) "points" — no stand-in relationship applies (特's own stand-in is [[特別]]; 点's own is itself).

特点 means characteristic, trait, or feature — synonymous with [[特徵]]/特徴.

Mandarin tèdiǎn, Cantonese dak6 dim2, Japanese とくちょう, Korean 특점, and Vietnamese đặc điểm are all standard, fully compositional readings. 特's syllable here (dug/둑/ㄉㄨㄎ) diverges from its own standalone citation (dǝg/득/ㄉㄜㄎ) — likely a deliberate homophone construction pairing this word with [[得点]] ("score"), since 得's own citation (tǝg/특) also independently converges on the same dug form there.
