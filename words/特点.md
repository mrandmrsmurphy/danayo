## Notes
- [特](../characters/特.md) "special" + [点](../characters/点%20(char).md) "points" — no stand-in relationship applies (特's own stand-in is [[特別]]; 点's own is itself).

特点 means characteristic, trait, or feature — a near-synonym of [[特徴]].

Mandarin tèdiǎn, Cantonese dak6 dim2, Japanese とくちょう, Korean 특점, and Vietnamese đặc điểm are all standard, fully compositional readings.
