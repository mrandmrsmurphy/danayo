## Etymology
- [産](../characters/産.md) "give birth" + [量](../characters/量.md) "measure, weight, quantity" — no stand-in relationship applies (産's own stand-in is [[生産]]; 量's own is [[数量]]). 量 is polyphonic: its own citation reflects the "to measure" verb sense (loeng4), but 産量 uses the "quantity" noun sense (loeng6) in Cantonese.

産量 means output, yield.

Mandarin chǎnliàng, Japanese さんりょう (SAN+RYOU), Korean 산량, and Vietnamese sản lượng were already correct and fully compositional. `cantonese`'s loeng6 (rather than 量's own stored loeng4) correctly reflects the "quantity" noun-sense tone. Fixed `hsk_level` formatting (unquoted 2 → quoted "2"). No homophones (注音 ㄙㄚㄋㄌ⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
