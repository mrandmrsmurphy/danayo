## Notes
- [減](../characters/減.md) "reduce; diminish" + [刑](../characters/刑.md) "punishment; penalty"

減刑 is to commute or reduce a sentence: to lower a penalty a court has already imposed. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin jiǎnxíng as "to mitigate a sentence; to lower a penalty" and Japanese げんけい as "lowering a penalty; commutation; to commutate". Vietnamese giảm hình is the same word in Hán Việt form, and Korean 감형 matches.

It reduces the sentence but does not erase it. That makes it distinct from [恩赦](恩赦.md), the pardon or amnesty, which can remove the penalty altogether, and from [釈放](釈放.md), release. It applies to any penalty, including [死刑](死刑.md).
