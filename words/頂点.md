## Notes
- [頂](../characters/頂.md) "peak" + [点 (char)](../characters/点%20(char).md) "point" — 頂点 is 頂's own legitimizing compound (頂's `stand_in` points here), but since 点's own `stand_in` is 点 itself instead, transitivity fails and no `#cranberry` tag applies.

頂点 (dǐngdiǎn) is the standard Mandarin word for "peak, vertex, apex," and Cantonese 頂点 (ding2dim2) likewise. Japanese ちょうてん (chōten) and Korean 정점 (jeongjeom) are both standard terms in their languages, as is Sino-Vietnamese đỉnh điểm — a clean case with full agreement across all five languages.
