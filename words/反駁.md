## Notes
- [反](../characters/反%20(char).md) "reverse, in return" + [駁](../characters/駁.md) "dispute, refute" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [駁](../characters/駁.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (反 is its own stand-in).

反駁 means to refute or rebut — to argue against a claim.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `注音` had the wrong initial consonant on the first syllable (ㄆㄛㄋ instead of 反's own ㄈㄛㄋ, the same class of bug as [[反応]]) — corrected. Japanese はんばく, Korean 반박, and Vietnamese phản bác are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄈㄛㄋㄅㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
