## Notes
- [冗 (char)](../characters/冗%20(char).md) "superfluous; excessive"

冗 depicts, per its own graphemic classification, 儿 ("person") under 冖 ("a covering, roof") — a person idling indoors, at leisure with nothing to do, which extends to "idle, superfluous, redundant." It appears in compounds like 冗員 (rǒngyuán, "redundant staff, sinecure holders"), 冗費 (rǒngfèi, "wasteful expenses"), and 冗長 (rǒngcháng, "long-winded, verbose").

Mandarin rǒng, Cantonese jung2, and Japanese じょう (jō, on'yomi, rare outside literary/formal compounds) all preserve the sense. Korean 용 (yong) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 한가롭다 (hangaropda, "to be idle, at leisure" — the character's own native-reading gloss, reflecting the "idle" side of the sense rather than "excessive"). Vietnamese nhũng is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in nhũng viên (冗員, "redundant staff") and nhũng phí (冗費, "wasteful expenses"); the character page's other three stored candidates (nhõng, nhùng, nũng) are tonal near-misses and look like corpus noise.
