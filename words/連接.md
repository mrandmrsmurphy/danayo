## Notes
- [連 (char)](characters/連%20(char).md) "consecutive, link" + [接 (char)](characters/接%20(char).md) "contact, connect" — "link, join, connect."

Mandarin liánjiē, cantonese lin4 zip3, and japanese れんせつ are all directly attested standard readings. Korean 연접 is the honest compositional reading (連's own 련→연 + 接's own 접). Vietnamese liên tiếp is a directly attested standard Vietnamese word ("continuous, successive"), matching 連's own liên + 接's own tiếp exactly. `连接` is the standard simplified-character spelling variant (連→连), correctly listed as an alias. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 련좁 diverges from Sino-Korean 연접 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄌ⼶ㄋㄐㄛㄆ unique among both words and characters).
