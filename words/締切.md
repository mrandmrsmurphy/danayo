## Notes
- [締 (char)](../characters/締%20(char).md) "tie, conclude" + [切 (char)](../characters/切%20(char).md) "cut off"

締切 is a deadline, the day on which something closes. English Wiktionary has an entry only for Japanese 締切 (しめきり), glossed "closing day; deadline" and "closed, no entrance". The Mandarin, Cantonese and Korean 체절 fields are built from the readings of the two characters. A time limit in general is the [期限](期限.md).
