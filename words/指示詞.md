## Notes
- [指](../characters/指.md) "point" + [示](../characters/示.md) "show" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "word" — no stand-in relationship applies (指's own stand-in is [[手指]]; 示's own is [[開示]]; 詞's own is [[単詞]]).

指示詞 means demonstrative (a grammatical term for words like "this" and "that").

Mandarin zhǐshìcí, Cantonese zi2 si6 ci4, Japanese しじし, and Korean 지시사 are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄜㄍㄝㄙㄚ unique across both words and characters).
