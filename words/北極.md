## Notes
- [北](../characters/北.md) "north" + [極](../characters/極%20(char).md) "extreme, pole" — no stand-in relationship applies (北's own stand-in is [[北方]]; 極 is its own stand-in).

北極 means the North Pole or the arctic/northern polar region (北極熊 "polar bear").

Japanese ほっきょく, Korean 북극, and Vietnamese Bắc cực are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄨㄎㄍㄧㄎ unique across both words and characters).
