## Notes
- [漁](../characters/漁.md) "to fish" + [業 (char)](../characters/業%20(char).md) "profession, industry" — literally "fishing profession."

漁業 names the fishing industry or fishery as an economic sector — the exploitation, cultivation, and commerce of fish and other aquatic products, distinct from the mere activity of fishing itself.

Mandarin yúyè, Cantonese jyu4 jip6, Korean 어업 (eoeop), and Japanese ぎょぎょう (gyogyō) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real everyday term for the fishing industry in its language. Vietnamese ngư nghiệp is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, matching 漁's ngư and 業's own nghiệp, and is directly attested as the real term for this industry, with nghề cá as its native-flavored synonym. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 요업 diverges from Sino-Korean 어업 in the first syllable. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ⼄ㄝㄆ unique among both words and characters).
