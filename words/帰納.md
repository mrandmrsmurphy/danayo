## Notes
- [帰](../characters/帰.md) "return; revert" + [納 (char)](../characters/納%20(char).md) "receive; accept" — no stand-in relationship applies (帰's own `stand_in` is [[回帰]], 納's is itself).

帰納 names induction — the logical method of reasoning from particular observations to general conclusions, the counterpart to 演繹 (deduction). The compound's literal sense, "returning and gathering in," reflects the process of drawing scattered particular cases back together into one general rule.

Japanese きのう, Korean 귀납, and Vietnamese quy nạp (verified, distinguished from 演繹/diễn dịch "deduction") are all standard logical/philosophical terminology. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄨㄧㄋㄚㄆ unique across both words and characters).

**Correction (2026-08-27)**: `japanese` had an archaic-kana error (きなふ → きのう, the same class of error found repeatedly this session on [[将軍]]/[[少女]]/[[少年]]).
