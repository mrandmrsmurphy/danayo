## Notes
- [陶](../characters/陶.md) "pottery; delight" + [酔 (char)](../characters/酔%20(char).md) "drunk"

陶酔 is rapture: to be so carried away by beauty, music or success that one is drunk with it. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin táozuì as "to be intoxicated; to revel in; to be enchanted by; to be engulfed in" and Japanese とうすい as "intoxication; to be intoxicated". Cantonese tou4 zeoi3 matches, and Korean 도취 is the reading of the characters.

It is joy that takes away one's judgement, like [狂喜](狂喜.md), wild joy, but gentler, and it is the state of the [夢中](夢中.md), absorbed. The art that causes it is [音楽](音楽.md), music, or [詩歌](詩歌.md), poetry.
