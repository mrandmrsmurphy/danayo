## Notes
- [教](../characters/教.md) "teaching, religion" + [堂](../characters/堂.md) "hall" — no stand-in relationship applies (教's own stand-in is [[教授]]; 堂's own is [[会堂]]).
- A church or chapel of any size. [[大教堂]] (cathedral) is the larger form, serving as a [[主教]]'s seat.

Mandarin jiàotáng, Cantonese gaau3 tong4, Japanese きょうどう, Korean 교당, and Vietnamese giáo đường (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼄ㄨㄉㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
