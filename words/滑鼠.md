## Notes
- [滑](../characters/滑.md) "slippery" + [鼠](../characters/鼠.md) "rat" — no stand-in relationship applies (滑's own stand-in is itself; 鼠's own is [[熊鼠]]).

滑鼠 means mouse (the computer peripheral) — a Taiwanese Mandarin term, literally "sliding rat."

Mandarin huáshǔ and Cantonese waat6 syu2 are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 滑's and 鼠's own stored citations. Japanese マウス, Korean 마우스, and Vietnamese chuột máy tính ("computer rat," a native calque on the same "mouse=rat" metaphor) are the real, standard everyday words for "computer mouse" in each language. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⺢ㄊㄙ⼄ unique across both words and characters).
