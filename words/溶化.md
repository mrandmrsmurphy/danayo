## Notes
- [溶](../characters/溶.md) "melt, fuse" + [化](../characters/化.md) "become, transform" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 溶 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (化's own stand-in is itself, so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

溶化 means to melt, dissolve.

Mandarin rónghuà, Cantonese jung4 faa3, and Japanese ようか are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 溶's and 化's own stored citations exactly.
