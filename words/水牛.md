## Notes
- [水 (char)](characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [牛 (char)](characters/牛%20(char).md) "cow" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 水's and 牛's own stand-ins are themselves).

水牛 means water buffalo.

Mandarin shuǐniú, Cantonese seoi2 ngau4, and Japanese すいぎゅう are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 水's and 牛's own stored citations. Korean 물소 is the real, standard native Korean word (물 "water" + 소 "cow," a native calque rather than the Sino compositional 수우) — the same real-world-animal-name divergence already documented on siblings like [[龍蝦]]/[[毛蝦]]. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with thủy ngưu (水's own thuỷ + 牛's own ngưu), a genuine Sino-Vietnamese synonym alongside the more colloquial native "trâu." No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨㄋ⼜ unique across both words and characters).
