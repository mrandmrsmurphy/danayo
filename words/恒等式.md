## Notes
- [恒](../characters/恒.md) "constant, ubiquitous" + [等](../characters/等%20(char).md) "equal" + [式](../characters/式.md) "formula" — no stand-in relationship applies (恒's own stand-in is [[恒久]]; 等's own is itself; 式's own is [[様式]]).

恒等式 means identity — in mathematics, an equation that holds true for all values of its variables.

Mandarin héngděngshì, Cantonese hang4 dang2 sik1, Japanese こうとうしき, Korean 항등식, and Vietnamese hằng đẳng thức are all standard, fully compositional readings.
