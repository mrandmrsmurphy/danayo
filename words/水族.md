## Notes
- [水](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [族](../characters/族.md) "tribe, clan" — no stand-in relationship applies (水's own stand-in is itself; 族's own is [[家族]]).

水族 means aquatic animals; also the name of the Sui people, an ethnic group of southern China.

Mandarin shuǐzú, Cantonese seoi2 zuk6, Japanese すいぞく, and Korean 수족 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 水's and 族's own stored citations exactly).
