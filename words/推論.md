## Notes
- [推 (char)](../characters/推%20(char).md) "push" + [論](../characters/論.md) "theory"

推論 is inference: reasoning from what is known to what follows. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin tuīlùn as "to deduce, infer, suppose; deduction, supposition, inference", and Japanese すいろん as "inference, deduction". Korean 추론 and Cantonese teoi1 leon6 match.

It is the step from a [前提](前提.md), a premise, to a [結論](結論.md), a conclusion, and its method is [帰納](帰納.md), induction, or deduction. It is firmer than [推測](推測.md), a guess, because it follows a rule.
