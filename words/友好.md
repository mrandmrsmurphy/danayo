## Notes
- [友](../characters/友.md) "friend" + [好 (char)](../characters/好%20(char).md) "good, like" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (友's is 朋友, 好's is bare 好).

友好 is chiefly an adjective ("friendly, amicable," e.g. 友好国家, 友好关系), with a secondary attributive-noun use ("friendship, amity"). Vietnamese hữu hảo is a real Sino-Vietnamese word meaning "friendly, harmonious," especially of international relations; Mandarin yǒuhǎo, Cantonese jau5 hou2, Japanese ゆうこう, and Korean 우호 are the standard readings.
