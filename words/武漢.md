## Notes
- [武](../characters/武%20(char).md) "military" + [漢](../characters/漢.md) "Han (Chinese)" — "martial Han". No stand-in relationship applies (武 is its own stand-in; 漢's is [[漢族]]).

武漢 (Wuhan) is the capital of Hubei province in central China, at the confluence of the Yangtze and the Han River (from memory, not re-checked). Wiktionary derives the name from 武昌 (Wuchang) and 漢口 (Hankou), two of the three towns merged to form the city. 汉 is an alias of 漢, so 武汉 is kept as the word's alias.

Japanese ぶかん, Korean 무한 and Vietnamese Vũ Hán are all fully compositional Sino-Xenic readings (Wiktionary Japanese 武漢 ぶかん, Korean 무한, Vietnamese Vũ Hán); the usual South Korean name today is 우한 (listed on Wiktionary). The Chinese entry has no sense other than the city. Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese entries. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄨㄏㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
