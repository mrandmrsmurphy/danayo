## Notes
- [鶏 (char)](../characters/鶏%20(char).md) "chicken, rooster" + [鳴 (char)](../characters/鳴%20(char).md) "cry, chirp" — no `#cranberry`: both 鶏's and 鳴's own `stand_in` point to themselves; neither points here.

鶏鳴 refers to the crow of the rooster at dawn, and by extension the early-morning hour it marks (traditionally the third of the twelve two-hour periods of the day, roughly 1–3 a.m.). Mandarin jīmíng, Cantonese gai1ming4, Japanese けいめい, Korean 계명, and Sino-Vietnamese ke minh are all standard, fully compositional readings.
