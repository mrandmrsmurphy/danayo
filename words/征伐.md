## Notes
- [征](../characters/征.md) "conquer" + [伐](../characters/伐.md) "subjugate" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [征](../characters/征.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (伐's own stand-in is [[討伐]]).

征伐 means to conquer or subjugate — a military campaign against an enemy.

Mandarin zhēngfá, Cantonese zing1 fat6, Japanese せいばつ, Korean 정벌, and Vietnamese chinh phạt are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄫㄈㄝㄊ unique across both words and characters).
