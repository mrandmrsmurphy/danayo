## Notes
- [昂](../characters/昂.md) "proud, bold" + [然](../characters/然%20(char).md) "so" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [昂](../characters/昂.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (然's own stand-in is itself).

昂然 means bold or elated.

Mandarin ángrán, Cantonese ngong4 jin4, Japanese こうぜん, Korean 앙연, and Vietnamese ngang nhiên (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄫㄋ⼶ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
