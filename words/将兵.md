## Notes
- [将 (char)](../characters/将%20(char).md) "general; commander" + [兵](../characters/兵.md) "soldier" — no stand-in relationship applies (将's own `stand_in` is itself, 兵's is [[兵士]]).

将兵 names commanders and soldiers together — officers and men, or the army as a body of both ranks. 将 here uses its polyphonic "commander, general" reading (jiàng in Mandarin) rather than its more common "will, shall" auxiliary reading (jiāng, as in [[将来]]) — a real, attested sense-switch reflected in the `mandarin` field's choice of pronunciation.

Japanese しょうへい, Korean 장병, and Vietnamese tướng binh (verified — attested in the classical citation 韓信將兵，多多益善, "the more troops Han Xin commands, the better") are all standard readings. No HSK level is given for the compound. No homophones (注音 ㄐ⺢ㄫㄅ⼶ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
