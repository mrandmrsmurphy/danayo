## Notes
- [控](../characters/控.md) "accuse, charge" + [訴](../characters/訴.md) "sue" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [控](../characters/控.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

控訴 means to formally complain regarding, or to file charges against.

Mandarin kòngsù, Cantonese hung3 sou3, Japanese こうそ, Korean 공소, and Vietnamese khống tố (a genuine standard legal term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `cantonese` was missing a letter (hun3→hung3). No homophones (注音 ㄎㄛㄫㄙㄛ unique across both words and characters).
