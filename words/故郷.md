## Notes
- [故](../characters/故.md) "happening" + [郷](../characters/郷.md) "hometown" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [郷](../characters/郷.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (故's own stand-in is [[緣故]]).

故郷 means hometown.

Mandarin gùxiāng, Cantonese gu3 hoeng1, Japanese こきょう, Korean 고향, and Vietnamese cố hương (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄏ⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
