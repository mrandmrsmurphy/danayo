- [問](../characters/問.md) "question" + [題](../characters/題.md) "topic, title" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (問's is 質問, 題's is 標題).

Mandarin wèntí (问题), Cantonese man6 tai4, Japanese もんだい, Korean 문제, and Vietnamese vấn đề are the standard readings.
