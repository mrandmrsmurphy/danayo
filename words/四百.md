## Notes
- [四 (char)](../characters/四%20(char).md) "four" + [百 (char)](../characters/百%20(char).md) "hundred" — four hundred, 400.

Plain digit + 百, like [[二百]], [[七百]] and [[八百]]: the hundred is counted by prefixing the digit to 百, with no further grouping word. Japanese uses よん rather than し for 四 in counts, which avoids し's homophony with 死 "death." Korean 사백 follows the Sino-Korean reading directly. `vietnamese: tứ bách` is the Sino-Vietnamese compositional reading; everyday Vietnamese uses the native bốn trăm for round numbers like this. Compare [[四千]] and [[四十]].
