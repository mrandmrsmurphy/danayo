## Notes
- [敏](../characters/敏%20(char).md) "fast, quick, clever" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

敏 means fast, quick, clever, or smart — mentally sharp and agile.

Mandarin mǐn, Cantonese man5, Japanese びん, Korean 민, and Vietnamese mẫn are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄇㄧㄋ/min/민) with the unrelated [[憫]] ("pity").
