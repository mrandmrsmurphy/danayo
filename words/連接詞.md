## Notes
- [連 (char)](characters/連%20(char).md) "consecutive, link" + [接 (char)](characters/接%20(char).md) "contact, connect" + [詞](characters/詞.md) "word" — "conjunction" (a grammatical connecting word).

Mandarin liánjiēcí and cantonese lin4 zip3 ci4 are directly attested standard readings. **Found and fixed a wrong-compound reading bug on `japanese`/`korean`**: せつぞくし/접속사 actually belong to the unrelated (though same-meaning) compound 接続詞/接續詞 ("conjunction," using 接続/接續 rather than 連接) — corrected to the honest compositional れんせつし/연접사 (連's own REN/련→연 + 接's own SETSU/접 + 詞's own SHI/사). Vietnamese liên tiếp từ is likewise the honest compositional reading (連's liên + 接's tiếp + 詞's từ), replacing what had been the unrelated phrase "sự liên kết" ("the act of linking") — the standard Vietnamese grammatical term is actually "liên từ" (from the shorter, different compound 連詞). **Found and removed three bad-aliases-entries**: `接續詞`/`接続詞` is that same genuinely different compound (接続/接續, not 連接), and `連詞`/`连词` is a shorter, differently-composed synonym (連+詞, omitting 接) — none is a spelling variant of 連接詞. Kept `连接词`, the legitimate simplified-character variant (連→连). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 련좁사 diverges from Sino-Korean 연접사 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄌ⼶ㄋㄐㄛㄆㄙㄚ unique among both words and characters).

**Found and fixed a missing citation** on 詞.md's own Words list. Citations on 連(char).md and 接(char).md already correctly present. None of the three constituents' `stand_in` fields point to this compound.
