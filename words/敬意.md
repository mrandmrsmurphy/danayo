## Notes
- [敬](../characters/敬.md) "respect" + [意](../characters/意.md) "intention; meaning"

敬意 is respect: the feeling or the show of high regard for a person. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin jìngyì and Japanese けいい alike as "reverence; respect". Cantonese ging3 ji3 matches, and Korean 경의 is the reading of the characters.

It is respect given to a person, as [尊敬](尊敬.md) is the feeling in general, and it shades into [畏](畏.md), awe. Respect for oneself is [自尊](自尊.md).
