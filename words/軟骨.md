## Notes
- [軟](../characters/軟.md) "soft" + [骨 (char)](../characters/骨%20(char).md) "bone"

軟骨 is cartilage: the firm, flexible tissue that is softer than bone. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin ruǎngǔ and Japanese なんこつ as "cartilage". Korean 연골 and Cantonese jyun5 gwat1 match.

It is the "soft bone", found in the ear, the nose and the joints, where the hard bone is the [骨](骨.md).
