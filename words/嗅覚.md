## Notes
- [嗅](../characters/嗅.md) "smell" + [覚](../characters/覚.md) "sense, perceive" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [嗅](../characters/嗅.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (覚's own stand-in is [[感覚]]).

嗅覚 means the sense of smell: the faculty of perceiving odors through the nose (嗅覚が鋭い, "have a keen sense of smell"; 嗅覚障害, "smell disorder"; 嗅覚神経, "olfactory nerve"), and figuratively an instinct for detecting opportunity or danger (商業嗅覚, "business nose"). The compound joins 嗅 "smell" with 覚 "sense, perceive".

Mandarin xiùjué, Cantonese cau3 gok3, Japanese きゅうかく, Korean 후각 (hugak), and Vietnamese khứu giác are all the standard readings of this one anatomical and scientific term.
