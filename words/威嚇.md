## Notes
- [威](../characters/威.md) "awe; might" + [嚇 (char)](../characters/嚇%20(char).md) "scare"

威嚇 is to intimidate: to frighten someone with a show of force. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin wēihè as "to threaten; to intimidate" and Japanese いかく as "threat; to threaten, to intimidate". Korean 위협 and Cantonese wai1 haak3 match the readings of the characters.

It is the display that frightens, where [威脅](威脅.md) is the threat itself and [脅迫](脅迫.md) is using it to force an act.
