## Notes
- [地 (char)](../characters/地%20(char).md) "earth" + [龍 (char)](../characters/龍%20(char).md) "dragon" — "earthworm," literally "earth-dragon" (the same worm can also be called 蚯蚓 qiūyǐn/구인 in Mandarin/Korean, a synonym not otherwise documented in this vault). **Content bugs found and fixed**: `mandarin`, `cantonese`, and `korean` each had two different words' readings crammed comma-joined into one field — 地龍's own reading (dìlóng/지렁이) alongside 蚯蚓's unrelated reading (qiūyǐn/구인); trimmed each to this word's own reading only. The `cantonese` field's second half ("qiu1 yin3") was in fact Mandarin pinyin mistakenly placed there, not valid Jyutping at all. `japanese` had a typo (めめず) — corrected to みみず, the standard Japanese word for earthworm. `vietnamese: địa long` is a directly attested term used in Vietnamese traditional medicine (dried earthworm as a TCM ingredient). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 듸룡 diverges from Sino-Korean 지렁이 (a native-ized form) — no meaningful syllable comparison applies since Korean uses its own native word here rather than a parallel Sino-Korean reading. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄧㄜㄌ·ㄛㄫ unique among both words and characters).
