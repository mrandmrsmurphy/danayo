## Notes
- [地 (char)](../characters/地%20(char).md) "earth" + [龍 (char)](../characters/龍%20(char).md) "dragon" — "earthworm," literally "earth-dragon" (the same worm can also be called 蚯蚓 qiūyǐn/구인 in Mandarin/Korean, a synonym not otherwise documented in this vault). `vietnamese: địa long` is a directly attested term used in Vietnamese traditional medicine (dried earthworm as a TCM ingredient).
