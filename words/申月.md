## Notes
- [申](../characters/申.md) + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) — no stand-in relationship applies (申's own stand-in is [[申告]]; 月's own is itself).
Seventh month of the ancient Chinese lunar calendar, named for the ninth Earthly Branch 申 (monkey).

The first month of autumn. 申 also means "to report" or "to stretch out," fitting a month in which the year begins to extend toward its close. The Qixi Festival (七夕) falls in this month.

Mandarin shēnyuè, Cantonese san1 jyut6, and Korean 신월 are fully compositional. Japanese さるつき ("monkey month," native) and Vietnamese tháng Thân ("month of Thân," the zodiac name) follow the same real-name convention already established on [[丑月]].
