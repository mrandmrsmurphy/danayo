## Notes
- [今 (char)](../characters/今%20(char).md) "now" + [夜 (char)](../characters/夜%20(char).md) "night" + [安](../characters/安.md) "peace, well-being" — "good night" (a bedtime/parting farewell), distinct from [[今晩安]] ("good evening," an arrival greeting) — the same arrival/departure pair English draws with "good evening" vs. "good night." Also part of the same 今+[time of day]+安 coinage family as [[今朝安]] ("good morning") and [[今昼安]] ("good day"). Each language field documents that language's own real everyday "good night" farewell rather than a compositional reading of 今夜安's own three characters: mandarin 晚安 (wǎn'ān, matching the alias already stored), japanese おやすみなさい, korean 안녕히 주무세요 (a genuinely distinct sleep-specific farewell, unlike the general-purpose 안녕하세요 shared by the other siblings), and vietnamese chúc ngủ ngon ("wish [you] a good sleep"). `cantonese: maan5 on1` is the compositional Cantonese reading of 晚安.

**Real content bugs found and fixed**: `mandarin` and `cantonese` had originally been set to "dàishù"/"doi6 sou3" — an evident copy-paste leftover from an unrelated word (代数, "algebra"), with no connection to this word's meaning at all; corrected in an earlier pass to the "good evening" terms 晚上好/wǎnshàng hǎo, but that was itself a **second error** — this word's own `english: good night` field calls for the distinct bedtime farewell, not the arrival greeting, so all five language fields have now been corrected accordingly (晚安/おやすみなさい/안녕히 주무세요/chúc ngủ ngon). `japanese` was stored as kanji-mixed orthography (今晩は) rather than the reading; `vietnamese` had a malformed comma-joined duplicate value. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 김야안 diverges entirely from the native-farewell-based Korean field. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄇ⼘ㄚㄋ unique among both words and characters).
