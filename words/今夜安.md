## Notes
- [今 (char)](../characters/今%20(char).md) "now" + [夜 (char)](../characters/夜%20(char).md) "night" + [安](../characters/安.md) "peace, well-being" — "good evening," part of the same 今+[time of day]+安 greeting family as [[今朝安]] ("good morning") and [[今昼安]] ("good day"). A Dan'a'yo-internal coinage rather than a calque, so — following this vault's established convention for these coinages — the mandarin/cantonese/japanese/korean/vietnamese fields each document that language's own real everyday evening greeting rather than a compositional reading of 今夜安's own three characters: mandarin 晚上好 (wǎnshàng hǎo, matching the alias already stored), japanese こんばんは, korean 안녕하세요 (the same general-purpose greeting used at any time of day, as on the [[今朝安]]/[[今昼安]] siblings), and vietnamese chào buổi tối. `cantonese: maan5soeng6 hou2` is a defensible compositional derivation from 晚上好's own Cantonese reading, presented honestly as not independently confirmed as a fixed idiom (Cantonese may simply default to the general-purpose 你好 here, the way it does for other times of day).

**Real content bug found and fixed**: `mandarin` and `cantonese` had been set to "dàishù"/"doi6 sou3" — an evident copy-paste leftover from an unrelated word (代数, "algebra"), with no connection to this word's meaning at all. `japanese` was stored as kanji-mixed orthography (今晩は) rather than the reading (こんばんは), inconsistent with sibling greeting entries. `vietnamese` had a malformed comma-joined duplicate value; simplified to the single standard term. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 김야안 diverges entirely from the native-greeting-based Korean field. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄇ⼘ㄚㄋ unique among both words and characters).
