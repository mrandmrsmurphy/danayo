## Notes
- [御](../characters/御.md) "imperial; to govern" + [名 (char)](../characters/名%20(char).md) "name"

御名 is "the imperial name," the personal name of the emperor, and by extension his signature on a document. It is a real Japanese word, ぎょめい, but Wiktionary has no entry for it in any language, so it is not independently attested outside Japanese there; it is coined here, by decision, as the first half of the formula 御名御璽 "Imperial Signature and Seal" that closes the 1945 surrender broadcast on [Japan](../lookup/Japanese/Japan.md). `mandarin` yùmíng, `cantonese` jyu6 ming4, `korean` 어명 and `vietnamese` ngự danh are compositional readings of the two characters, not attested words. (From memory, not re-checked: Korean 어명 is also the reading of a different word, 御命 "royal command," so the two would be homophones in Korean.) Here 御 is the honorific "imperial," as in [御璽](御璽.md) "imperial seal."
