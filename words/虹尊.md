## Notes
- [虹](../characters/虹.md) "rainbow" is separately legitimized by [[彩虹]]. [尊](../characters/尊.md) "respect, dignity" is separately legitimized by [[尊厳]] — here 尊 stands in as a `代用字` for its own alias 鱒/鳟 ("trout"), matching the already-listed alias 虹鱒 (and paralleling [[尊魚]] "trout," the analogous case cited on 尊's own character page). No cranberry.

Korean 무지개송어 is the real native compound term.
