## Notes
- [旋](../characters/旋.md) "revolve; turn" + [律](../characters/律.md) "rule; law"

旋律 is a melody: the line of notes that makes a tune. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xuánlǜ as "melody" and Japanese せんりつ as "melody (sequence of notes that makes up a musical phrase)". Korean 선률 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese syun4 leot6-2 matches.

It runs over the [拍子](拍子.md), beat, and above the [和音](和音.md), chord. The notes themselves are [音符](音符.md).
