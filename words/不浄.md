## Notes
- [不](../characters/不%20(char).md) "not" + [浄](../characters/浄.md) "clean" — "not clean." Their own `stand_in` values: 不's is itself; 浄's is [[乾浄]]; none points here, so 不浄 is an independent compound.

不浄 is unclean in the religious sense, a defilement that is not about dirt. It is a neologism for Dan'a'yo's purposes, taken from Japanese ふじょう, which Wiktionary labels religious and non-physical "unclean." The Mandarin, Cantonese and Korean fields are the readings of the two characters, not attested words. It takes the term over from Buddhism and Shinto, in line with the vault's practice of adopting other religions' words.

**Hebrew**: it best translates *ṭāmēʾ* (טָמֵא) "unclean" and, as a state, *ṭumʾāh* (טֻמְאָה) "defilement" (Leviticus 11–15). Its opposite is [[清浄]] (*ṭāhôr*). [[汚穢]] is filth, physical and moral; 不浄 is the ritual state, which is washed away and not forgiven.
