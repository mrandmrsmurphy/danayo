## Notes
- [協](../characters/協.md) "cooperate, work together" + [定](../characters/定.md) "to fix, to decide" — an agreement; a jointly fixed decision; a pact between parties

Wiktionary: Chinese "agreement; treaty; accord; truce; (Taiwan, computing) protocol; to negotiate an agreement"; Japanese, from 1877, "an agreement, an accord, a convention" and, as a verb, "to make an agreement, to forge a pact"; Korean 협정 and Vietnamese hiệp định are the Han forms of "an agreement, an accord, a convention".
