## Notes
- [宝](../characters/宝.md) "treasure" + [石](../characters/石%20(char).md) "stone" — "treasure stone," a jewel. Their own `stand_in` values: 宝's is [[宝物]]; 石's is itself; none points here, so 宝石 is an independent compound.

宝石 is a jewel: a precious stone, cut and valued as a gem. Mandarin bǎoshí (寶石), Cantonese bou2 sek6, Japanese ほうせき and Korean 보석 all use it. It is the general word for a gem, where 玉 is jade, [[琥珀]] amber and [[鑽石]] diamond. Compare [[宝物]] (treasure).
