## Notes
- [顔](../characters/顔.md) "face" + [料](../characters/料.md) "material, fee" — pigment (for paint).

顔料 is pigment as a manufactured material: the powdered coloring substance that is mixed with a binder to make paint, ink or glaze, usually insoluble in its vehicle. It contrasts with [[染料]], a soluble dye that soaks into fabric, and with [[色素]], the general chemical term. 顔 here is the old sense "face; (painted) color," which survives in compounds such as Mandarin yánsè. Mandarin yánliào, Cantonese ngaan4 liu6, Japanese がんりょう and Korean 안료 are the standard readings.
