## Notes
- [広 (char)](../characters/広%20(char).md) "wide" + [東](../characters/東.md) "east" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language" — "Guangdong language," i.e. Cantonese. No stand-in relationship applies (広's own `stand_in` is itself, 東's is [[東方]], 語's is [[言語]]); [[広東]] "Guangdong" is the place name this compound is built on.

Wiktionary: Japanese 広東語 (かんとんご) is "Cantonese (language)", and historically, in Taiwan under Japanese rule, "Hakka"; 廣東語 is the kyūjitai spelling. Korean 광동어 is the hanja form glossed "Cantonese". Chinese 廣東話 is glossed "Yue; Cantonese language". Built on [[広東]] plus 語, like [[中国語]] and [[日本語]]. Used in `文法 - 02音韻論` for the variety that inserts a back close vowel (後舌高母音).
