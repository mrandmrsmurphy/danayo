## Notes

旧 means paleo-, former, or old.

Mandarin jiù, Cantonese gau6, Japanese きゅう, Korean 구, and Vietnamese cựu are all standard, fully compositional readings. Three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group with [[臼]] ("mortar; millstone; socket") and [[糾]] ("twist; investigate") — already fully cross-referenced from 臼's side.
