## Notes
- [慣](../characters/慣.md) "habit, custom" + [性](../characters/性.md) "nature" — "habit nature," inertia. Their own `stand_in` values: 慣's is [[習慣]]; 性's is [[性別]]; none points here, so 慣性 is an independent compound.

慣性 is inertia: the tendency of a body to keep its state of rest or motion. Mandarin guànxìng, Cantonese gwaan3 sing3, Japanese かんせい and Korean 관성 all use it, and Vietnamese quán tính does too. Compare [[運動]] (motion) and [[質量]] (mass).
