## Notes
- [越 (char)](../characters/越%20(char).md) "Yue, exceed" + [南](../characters/南.md) "south" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language" — "the Vietnamese language." Mandarin yuènányǔ and Cantonese jyut6 naam4 jyu5 use this literal compound; Japanese ベトナム語 and Korean 베트남어 instead combine the modern phonetic transliteration of "Việt Nam" with each language's own native word for "language" (語/어) — paralleling the [[越南]]/ベトナム/베트남 split already documented on that word's own page. `vietnamese: tiếng Việt` is the standard native Vietnamese term ("Vietnamese speech/language"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 웓남요 diverges from Sino-Korean-based readings entirely. No homophones (注音 ⼔ㄊㄋㄚㄇ·⼄ unique among both words and characters).
