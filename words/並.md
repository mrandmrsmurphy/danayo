## Notes
- [並 (char)](../characters/並%20(char).md) "side by side"

並 (alternate forms 并/併/竝/幷/倂, orthographic variants converged historically) is 會意: originally two 立 ("stand") figures side by side — two people standing together, "to stand side by side; simultaneously, together." It functions primarily as an adverb/conjunction in modern Mandarin: 並且 (bìngqiě, "moreover, and"), 並非 (bìngfēi, "is not actually," a strong negator implying a common misconception), and in compounds like [[並列]] ("parallel, juxtaposed") and [[並立]] ("to stand side by side; coexist").

Japanese へい (hei, on'yomi) alongside native なみ/ならびに (nami/narabini, "alongside; and") covers the same sense; Korean 병 (byeong) parallels this directly. Vietnamese tịnh is the Hán Việt reading (as in tịnh hành, "to proceed side by side/concurrently"), though modern Vietnamese favors native cùng/song song for everyday "together, side by side."
