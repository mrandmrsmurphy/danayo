## Notes
- [耽](../characters/耽%20(char).md) "indulge in" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 耽 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

耽 means to indulge in or be absorbed in, as in 耽耽 (glaring covetously, an alias of 眈眈) and 虎視耽耽 (to eye covetously like a prowling tiger).

Mandarin dān, Cantonese daam1, and Korean 탐 (tam) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading ふける (fukeru, "to indulge in") rather than the on-reading タン (TAN) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese đam is the standard reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones (彤 shares the same syllable at the character level, but has no independent word page).
