## Notes
- [流](../characters/流.md) "flow" + [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" — no stand-in relationship applies (流's own stand-in is [[流動]]; 音's is [[音楽]]).

Wiktionary: 流音 is a phonetics and phonology term, "liquid consonant", in Chinese (liúyīn), Japanese (りゅうおん) and Korean, where South Korea has 유음 and North Korea 류음. In `文法 - 02音韻論` it appears in the list of initials (ㄋ, ㄌ, ㄅ, ㄆ, ㄇ: nasal, liquid and bilabial) that do not co-occur with a glide. Related manner terms: [[鼻音]], [[塞音]], [[擦音]], [[振動音]].
