## Notes
- [紅 (char)](../characters/紅%20(char).md) "crimson, red" + [茶 (char)](../characters/茶%20(char).md) "tea (drink), tea (plant)" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (紅's is [[紅]] itself, 茶's is [[茶]] itself), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 茶 is individually `kwin: false`.

紅茶 names black tea — literally "red tea," reflecting the reddish color of the brewed liquid (as opposed to the leaf's dark color, which gives English "black tea" its name).

Mandarin hóngchá, Cantonese hung4 caa4, Japanese こうちゃ (kōcha), Korean 홍차 (hongcha), and Vietnamese hồng trà are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 紅茶 is itself a common attested word for "black tea" in all five languages. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
