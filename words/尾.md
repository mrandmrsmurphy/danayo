## Notes
- [尾 (char)](../characters/尾%20(char).md) "tail"

尾 names an animal's tail, extended to "the end/rear part of anything" (船尾 "stern," 首尾 "beginning and end," 接尾辞 "suffix," literally "tail-attached word").

Japanese び, Korean 미, and Vietnamese vĩ (the clean primary Hán Việt reading — the character's own field also lists several Nôm variants: vã/vãi/vả/vải) are all standard readings.
