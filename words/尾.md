## Notes
- [尾 (char)](../characters/尾%20(char).md) "tail" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [尾 (char)](../characters/尾%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

尾 names an animal's tail, extended to "the end/rear part of anything" (船尾 "stern," 首尾 "beginning and end," 接尾辞 "suffix," literally "tail-attached word").

Japanese び, Korean 미, and Vietnamese vĩ (the clean primary Hán Việt reading — the character's own field also lists several Nôm variants: vã/vãi/vả/vải) are all standard readings. **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 尾 and [[未]] ("not yet") share an identical reading (mui/뮈/ㄇㄨㄧ) despite being semantically and etymologically unrelated — added reciprocal homophone callouts to both pages (未.md had been stamped since June without ever carrying one).
