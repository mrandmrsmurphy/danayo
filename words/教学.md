## Notes
- [教](../characters/教.md) "teach, religion" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study" — no stand-in relationship applies (教's own stand-in is [[教授]]; 学's own is [[学習]]).

教学 means education or instruction.

Mandarin jiàoxué, Cantonese gaau3 hok6, Japanese きょうがく, Korean 교학, and Vietnamese giáo học are all standard, fully compositional readings.
