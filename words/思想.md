## Notes
- [思](../characters/思.md) "think" + [想](../characters/想.md) "thought, concept" — `characters/想.md`'s own `stand_in` points to this word (思想), meaning 想 is a bound morpheme that never appears independently outside this compound; 思's own points elsewhere (思考).

Mandarin sīxiǎng, Cantonese si1 soeng2, Japanese しそう, Korean 사상, and Vietnamese tư tưởng are all everyday words for "thought; ideology."

Korean 사상 is a rich multi-way homophone: besides 思想 ("thought/ideology"), the same syllables spell 死傷 ("death and injury," as in 사상자 "casualties"), 史上 ("in history," as in 사상 최초 "first in history"), and 事象 ("phenomena"). Context always disambiguates. This is the same pattern as [[思考]]'s 사고 cluster.
