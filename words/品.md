## Notes
- [品 (char)](../characters/品%20(char).md) "article; item"

品 is 会意: a triplication of [[口]] ("mouth"), originally depicting a multiplicity of objects or people. It means "article, item, product" (品目, "item, inventory entry"), and by extension "grade, rank, quality" (things sorted into categories, as in the traditional nine-rank official system) and "character, moral quality" (人品, "one's character, moral caliber").

Mandarin pǐn and Cantonese ban2 preserve the sense directly. Japanese ヒン/ホン (HIN/HON, on'yomi, fully productive: 品物 shinamono via native しな reading for "goods," 製品 seihin for "product") cover the sense broadly. Korean 품 (pum) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 물건 (mulgeon, "thing, object"). Vietnamese phẩm is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in sản phẩm ("product"), phẩm chất ("quality"), and hàng phẩm ("merchandise").
