## Notes
- [青 (char)](../characters/青%20(char).md) "blue-green" + [州 (char)](../characters/州%20(char).md) — Qing Province, the third of the [[九州]]

青州 occupied the eastern coastal region — roughly modern Shandong peninsula — where the eastern mountains meet the sea. The *Tribute of Yu* bounds it between the sea and Mount Tai (海岱惟青州) and notes its coast (海濱), soils and products. The name persisted through the imperial era as a regional designation; in the Three Kingdoms period, 青州 soldiers (青州兵) famously formed part of Cao Cao's force. The modern city of Qingzhou in Shandong preserves the ancient name.

Neither character is legitimized here: both 青's and 州's `stand_in` are themselves.  The word names the province 青州 as listed among the 九州.  Mandarin qīngzhōu, Cantonese cing1 zau1, Japanese せいしゅう, Korean 청주 and Vietnamese Thanh Châu are the readings of that name.  `kwin` is false because the Dan'a'yo 청줏 differs from the Korean 청주.
