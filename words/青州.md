## Notes
- [青 (char)](../characters/青%20(char).md) "blue-green" + [州 (char)](../characters/州%20(char).md) — Qing Province, the third of the [[九州]]

Wiktionary lists 青州 under Chinese and Japanese (せいしゅう) as the city Qingzhou, in Shandong, China; it has no separate entry for the ancient province. The *Tribute of Yu* (尚書, 禹貢, Wikisource) has the section 青州 beginning 海、岱惟青州, "between the sea and Mount Tai is Qingzhou". Neither character is legitimized here: both 青's and 州's `stand_in` are themselves. The word names the province as listed among the [[九州]].
