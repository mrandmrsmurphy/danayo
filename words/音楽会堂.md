## Notes
- [音](../characters/音.md) "sound, music" + [楽](../characters/楽.md) "music, joy" + [会](../characters/会%20(char).md) "assembly, meeting" + [堂](../characters/堂.md) "hall" — "music assembly hall," a concert hall. Their own `stand_in` values: 音's is [[音楽]]; 楽's is [[快楽]]; 会's is itself; 堂's is [[会堂]]; none points here, so 音楽会堂 is an independent compound.

音楽会堂 is a concert hall: a hall built for musical performances. It is a **neologism**, built from [[音楽]] (music) and [[会堂]] (assembly hall). Mandarin says 音樂廳 and Japanese and Korean say 音楽堂, which is attested in only two of the five languages, so Dan'a'yo lengthens it by one character on the pattern of 会堂. Compare [[劇場]] (theater).
