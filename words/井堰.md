## Notes
- [井](../characters/井.md) "well" + [堰](../characters/堰.md) "weir, dam" — this word is the `stand_in` legitimizer for [[堰]], which cannot appear independently.

井堰 names a small-scale weir associated with a well or irrigation channel, distinct from a large river-blocking dam. Vietnamese yển (堰's own reading) is directly attested in Đô Giang Yển, the Vietnamese name for the famous ancient Sichuan irrigation system 都江堰 — `vietnamese: tỉnh yển` is a compositionally sound pairing of this confirmed reading with 井's own tỉnh, though everyday Vietnamese hydraulic engineering instead uses the native đập (nước) for "weir/dam" generally. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 징언 diverges from Sino-Korean 정언 in the first syllable's vowel.
