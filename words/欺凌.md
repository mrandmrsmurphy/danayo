## Notes
- [欺](../characters/欺.md) "deceive, cheat" + [凌](../characters/凌.md) "insult, bully" — "cheat and abuse," to bully. Their own `stand_in` values: 欺's is [[欺𥈞]]; 凌's is [[凌駕]]; none points here, so 欺凌 is an independent compound.

欺凌 is to bully: to push around, humiliate or oppress someone weaker. Mandarin qīlíng and Cantonese hei1 ling4 are the ordinary words. Japanese says いじめ and Korean 괴롭힘, so those two fields hold the character readings. Compare [[虐待]] (to mistreat) and [[暴君]] (tyrant).
