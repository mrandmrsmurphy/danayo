## Notes
- [同](../characters/同.md) "same" + [等 (char)](../characters/等%20(char).md) "grade, rank" — equal in rank; equivalent in standing

Wiktionary: Chinese, attributive, "of the same class, rank; on a par with"; Japanese "equal, equivalent" and "equality, equivalence"; Korean 동등 is the hanja form, "equality, equivalence"; Vietnamese đồng đẳng is the Han form, "peer" and "homologous". Compare [[平等]].
