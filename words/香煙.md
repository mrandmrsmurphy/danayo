## Notes
- [香](../characters/香.md) "fragrant" + [煙](../characters/煙.md) "smoke" — "fragrant smoke," a cigarette. Their own `stand_in` values: 香's is [[香気]]; 煙's is [[煙気]]; none points here, so 香煙 is an independent compound.

香煙 is a cigarette. Mandarin xiāngyān and Cantonese hoeng1 jin1 are the ordinary Chinese words, from 香 "fragrant" and 煙 "smoke". Japanese says タバコ or 煙草 and Korean 담배, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters; the Sino-Korean 향연 also exists as an unrelated word. Compare 煙草.
