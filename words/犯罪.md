## Notes
- [犯](../characters/犯.md) "crime" + [罪 (char)](../characters/罪%20(char).md) "sin, crime" — 犯's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 罪's own is itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 犯 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

犯罪 means crime.

Mandarin fànzuì, Cantonese faan6 zeoi6, Japanese はんざい, Korean 범죄 and Vietnamese phạm tội (犯's own phạm plus 罪's own tội) are all standard, fully compositional readings.
