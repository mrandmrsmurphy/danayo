## Notes
- [更](../characters/更%20(char).md) "more" + [新](../characters/新%20(char).md) "new" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 更 and 新's own stand-ins are themselves, each with its own independent word page).

更新 means to renew, replace, or refresh.

Cantonese gang1 san1, Japanese こうしん, Korean 갱신, and Vietnamese canh tân are all standard, fully compositional readings. `mandarin` gēngxīn uses 更's "change" reading (gēng), which is not currently listed on the character's own stored citation (only "gèng," the "more" reading, is stored) — a character-level citation gap flagged for future perfecting, not fixed here.
