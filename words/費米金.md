## Notes
- [費 (char)](../characters/費%20(char).md) + [米 (char)](../characters/米%20(char).md) + [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal" — a transliteration of *Fermi*, after physicist Enrico Fermi. 費米 is the standard Mandarin transliteration of "Fermi" (as in 費米子, "fermion"), used here in full since it is already only two syllables. Japanese and Korean instead borrow the IUPAC name as a loanword (フェルミウム, 페르뮴).

Filled blank `mandarin`/`cantonese`/`vietnamese` with the honest compositional readings of 費+米+金 (fèimǐjīn, fai3 mai5 gam1, phí mễ kim), matching the same transliteration syllables used in 費米子. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 빼메김 diverges sharply from the Korean field, which (like Japanese) is a IUPAC loanword rather than a compositional Sino-Korean reading. No homophones (注音 ㄈㄚㄧㄇㄝㄧㄍㄧㄇ unique). Removed a duplicate `pos`/`品詞` key. **Found and fixed a missing citation** on 金(char).md's own Words list; citations on 費(char).md and 米(char).md were already correctly present.
