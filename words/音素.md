## Notes
- [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" + [素](../characters/素.md) "basic element" — the smallest contrastive unit of sound in a language's phonological system. Attested as a linguistics term across Mandarin (yīnsù), Cantonese (jam1sou3), Japanese (おんそ, onso), Korean (음소, eumso), and Vietnamese (âm tố). No `#cranberry`: 音's own `stand_in` is [[音楽]], 素's is [[要素]]; neither points here.
