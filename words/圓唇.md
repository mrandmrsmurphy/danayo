## Notes
- [圓 (char)](../characters/圓%20(char).md) "round" + [唇 (char)](../characters/唇%20(char).md) "lips" — no stand-in relationship applies (圓 and 唇 are both their own stand-ins).

圓唇 means rounded, of a vowel made with rounded lips. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 圓脣 (aliases 圓脣, 圆唇 and 圆脣; Mandarin yuánchún, Cantonese jyun4 seon4) as the linguistics term "(of a vowel) rounded". The Japanese えんしん, Korean 원순 and Vietnamese fields are built from the readings of the two characters. As a modifier it takes 無 for its opposite, so 無圓唇 is "unrounded", as in 無圓唇後舌母音 on [文法 - 02音韻論](../grammar/文法%20-%2002音韻論.md). Compare [両唇](両唇.md), bilabial.
