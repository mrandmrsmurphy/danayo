## Notes
- [終](../characters/終.md) "end" + [焉 (char)](../characters/焉%20(char).md) "here; thus"

終焉 is the end, in the sense of the last moments of something that has lived or lasted: a death, or the close of an age. Wiktionary glosses Japanese しゅうえん as "final moments; death". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 종언 matches.

It is literary and weighty, used for the end of an era, a dynasty or a life. The plain act of ending is [終了](終了.md), and the destruction of a whole people is [滅亡](滅亡.md).
