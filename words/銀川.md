## Notes
- [銀](../characters/銀%20(char).md) "silver" + [川](../characters/川%20(char).md) "river" — "silver river". No stand-in relationship applies (both characters are their own stand-ins).

銀川 (Yinchuan) is the capital of the Ningxia Hui Autonomous Region in northwestern China (from memory, not re-checked as to its Western Xia history). Wiktionary gives no etymology for the name; "silver river" is only a gloss of the characters. 银 is an alias of 銀, so 银川 is kept as the word's alias.

Japanese ぎんせん is the Sino-Japanese reading listed on Wiktionary Japanese; Korean 은천 is the North Korean reading derived from the character pages (English Wiktionary has no Korean entry for 銀川), and the usual South Korean name today is 인촨 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Ngân Xuyên is attested on Vietnamese Wikipedia. Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese and Japanese entries. No homophones (注音 ㄧㄋㄑ⺢ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
