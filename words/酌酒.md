## Notes
- [酌](../characters/酌.md) "pour wine" + [酒](../characters/酒.md) "wine" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [酌](../characters/酌.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

酌酒 means to pour wine.

Mandarin zhuójiǔ, Cantonese zoek3 zau2, Korean 작주, and Vietnamese chước tửu are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese くむ is the native reading (rather than a Sino-Japanese compositional reading), the standard, expected term. Homophone of [[昨週]] "last week" (both 注音 ㄐㄚㄎㄐㄨㄛ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated compounds.
