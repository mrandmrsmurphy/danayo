## Notes
- [所](../characters/所%20(char).md) "place; (marker of what is possessed)" + [有](../characters/有%20(char).md) "have; own" + [権](../characters/権.md) "right, authority" — "right of ownership." Their own `stand_in` values: 所's is itself; 有's is itself; 権's is [[権利]]; none points here, so 所有権 is an independent compound.

所有権 is the right of ownership: legal title to a thing. Mandarin suǒyǒuquán (所有權) and Cantonese so2 jau5 kyun4 are the ordinary legal terms, and Japanese しょゆうけん and Korean 소유권 are the same compound. It is [[所有]] plus [[権]] "right." The owner is segmented as 所有－者, with the productive suffix [[－者]], not coined separately. Compare [[私有]] and [[公有]].
