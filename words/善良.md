## Notes
- [善](../characters/善%20(char).md) "good, virtuous" + [良](../characters/良.md) "good" — no stand-in relationship applies (善 is its own stand-in; 良's own is [[良好]]).

善良 means kind or benevolent — good-natured and morally upright.

Japanese ぜんりょう, Korean 선량, and Vietnamese thiện lương are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese: thiện lương`; removed the redundant `品詞` field. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼶ㄋㄌ⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
