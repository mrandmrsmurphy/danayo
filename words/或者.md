## Notes
- [或 (char)](../characters/或%20(char).md) "or, perhaps" + [者](../characters/者.md) "-er, one who" — literally "perhaps-one," some people, someone unspecified.

Mandarin huòzhě, Cantonese waak6 ze2, Korean 혹자, and Vietnamese hoặc giả are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 或's and 者's own stored citations exactly. Japanese ある者 (aru mono) instead uses a native phrase — 或る (aru, kun'yomi of this same character) + 者 (mono) — rather than a Sino-Japanese on'yomi compound, the real way this sense is expressed in Japanese.
