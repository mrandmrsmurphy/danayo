## Notes
- [此 (char)](../characters/此%20(char).md) "this; proximal demonstrative" + [物 (char)](../characters/物%20(char).md) "thing; object; material result"

此物 is the proximal correlative in the 物 ("thing") column of the correlative table: "this thing; this object", a specific thing near the speaker or already prominent in the shared context. The character [此 (char)](../characters/此%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *cǝ*, ㄑㄜ) is the classical Chinese proximal demonstrative, contrasting with 其 (medial) and 彼 (distal); [物 (char)](../characters/物%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *mud*, ㄇㄨㄊ) is "thing; object; material result". Its character page analyses 物 as a 形声 grapheme with semantic 牛 (ox) and phonetic 勿 (OC \*mɯd); the claim that it first meant the mottled colouring of cattle is from memory, not re-checked.

It differs from its neighbours as follows. [此事](此事.md) "this matter" points to an act, event or affair, whereas 此物 points to a concrete object; [此類](此類.md) "this kind" points to a class rather than an individual; the bare pronoun [此](此.md) is the demonstrative itself, with no noun class; and the productive affix [－物](－物.md) forms "thing that is X-ed" from a verb or stative, so it builds new nouns, whereas 此物 is one fixed cell of a paradigm.

The cross-linguistic fields hold the spoken equivalents. Mandarin 此物 (cǐwù): Wiktionary has no entry for 此物 (checked 2026-10-10), so the word is a Dan'a'yo compositional construction; that it is found in formal or classical prose, and the exclamation 此物何物？ "what is this thing?", are from memory, not re-checked (as an example, treat it as illustrative, not quoted). The colloquial Mandarin equivalent is 這個 (zhège), for which Wiktionary has an entry, a pronoun glossed "this; this one", and this is the word's alias: 這 (proximal demonstrative) + 個 (general classifier). `aliases` names 這個, which has no word file of its own. Japanese これ (kore): Wiktionary has an entry, a pronoun glossed "this" (deictically, "this one (near the speaker)"). Korean 이것 (igeot): Wiktionary has an entry, a pronoun glossed "this". These two are spoken pronouns rather than Sino-Xenic readings of 此物, as with the siblings [其物](其物.md) (そのもの, 그것) and [彼物](彼物.md). Vietnamese *cái này* "this thing" (concrete) is from memory, not re-checked; hvdic.thivien.net covers single characters, not phrases, so no `vietnamese` field is entered. The earlier `vietnamese: điều này` was removed because that phrase is for abstract matters, closer to [此事](此事.md) than to this word.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md)'s correlative table (line 121; the same chart is in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`, line 145, in the 物 row, whose header is the 物 "thing" column), 此物 is the second cell of the 此 ("this") row, after [此事](此事.md) "this matter" and before [此人](此人.md) "this person", [此名](此名.md) "this person (polite)", [此処](此処.md) "this place", [此時](此時.md) "this time", [此様](此様.md) "this manner", [此多](此多.md) "this amount" and [此類](此類.md) "this kind". Down the 物 column it pairs with [其物](其物.md) "that thing" (medial), [彼物](彼物.md) "that thing yonder" (distal), [何物](何物.md) "what", [毎物](毎物.md) "every thing", [某物](某物.md) "something" and [皆物](皆物.md) "all things". The table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), 63 forms, so 此物 is parsed compositionally: 此 = proximal, 物 = thing. Illustrative only (invented, not from `grammar/`): 此物何 "what is this thing?". Dan'a'yo keeps the sense shared across CJKV, "this thing; this object"; no narrowing is needed.

The 注音 ㄑㄜ·ㄇㄨㄊ carries a syllable-boundary dot because the string has a second legal partition: ㄑㄜㄇ + ㄨㄊ, with ㄇ as a nasal coda and ㄨ as the nucleus of a null-onset syllable ㄨㄊ (which exists in `syllables/`) under the (C)(G)V(C) structure in `grammar/文法 - 02音韻論.md`. The dot marks the intended ㄑㄜ + ㄇㄨㄊ.
