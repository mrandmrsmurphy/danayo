## Notes
- [跳](../characters/跳.md) "hop, jump" + [躍](../characters/躍.md) "leap, jump" — "jump; leap."

Mandarin tiàoyuè, cantonese tiu3 joek6, japanese ちょうやく, and korean 도약 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese khiêu dược is the honest compositional reading (跳's own khiêu + 躍's own dược); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found.
