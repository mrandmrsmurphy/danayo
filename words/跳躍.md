## Notes
- [跳](../characters/跳.md) "hop, jump" + [躍](../characters/躍.md) "leap, jump" — "jump; leap."

Mandarin tiàoyuè, cantonese tiu3 joek6, japanese ちょうやく, and korean 도약 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese khiêu dược is the honest compositional reading (跳's own khiêu + 躍's own dược); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 톳약 diverges from Sino-Korean 도약 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄛㄨ·⼘ㄎ unique among both words and characters).

**Found and fixed a bare, un-ruby-formatted citation** on 跳.md's own Words list. 躍's own `stand_in` is this exact word (跳躍), so 躍 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 跳躍; 跳's own `stand_in` is likewise 跳躍, so this word legitimizes both constituents.
