## Notes
- [捕](../characters/捕%20(char).md) "catch, grab" + [捉](../characters/捉.md) "seize" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [捉](../characters/捉.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (捕's own stand-in is itself).

捕捉 means to catch or capture.

Mandarin bǔzhuō, Cantonese bou6 zuk1, Japanese ほそく, Korean 포착, and Vietnamese bổ tróc are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄛㄐㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
