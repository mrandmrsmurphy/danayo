## Notes
- [形](../characters/形%20(char).md) "shape, form" + [成](../characters/成%20(char).md) "become" — no stand-in relationship applies (形/成 are both their own stand-ins).

形成 means to form, shape, or represent — the process of coming into being or taking shape.

Mandarin xíngchéng, Cantonese jing4 sing4, Japanese けいせい, Korean 형성, and Vietnamese hình thành are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄏㄝㄫㄙㄧㄫ/hengsing/헝싱) with the unrelated [[形声]] ("phono-semantic character," a coincidence arising since 成 and 声 both give sing/싱).
