## Notes
- [砕](../characters/砕%20(char).md) "smash, break" + [片](../characters/片%20(char).md) "piece, slice" — "broken piece," a scrap. Their own `stand_in` values: 砕's is itself; 片's is itself; none points here, so 砕片 is an independent compound.

砕片 is a scrap or fragment: a small broken-off piece. Mandarin suìpiàn (碎片) and Cantonese seoi3 pin3 are the ordinary words. Japanese says 破片 and Korean 파편, so those two fields hold the character readings. Compare [[片]] (slice), [[屑]] (scraps) and [[欠]] (chip).
