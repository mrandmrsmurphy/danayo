## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" + [転](../characters/転.md) "turn"

自転 is rotation: the turning of a body around its own axis, as the earth turns once a day. Wiktionary glosses Japanese じてん as "rotation around a fixed axis; to rotate around a fixed axis". The Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the characters, and Korean 자전 matches.

Its counterpart is [公転](公転.md), the circling of another body. The line it turns on is the [地軸](地軸.md), the earth's axis.
