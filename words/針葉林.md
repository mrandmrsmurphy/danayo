## Notes
- [針](../characters/針%20(char).md) "needle" + [葉](../characters/葉%20(char).md) "leaf" + [林](../characters/林%20(char).md) "forest" — "needle-leaf forest," the taiga. Their own `stand_in` values: 針's is itself; 葉's is itself; 林's is itself; none points here, so 針葉林 is an independent compound.

針葉林 is a conifer forest, and so the taiga, the cold northern forest belt. Mandarin zhēnyèlín and Cantonese zam1 jip6 lam4 are the ordinary words. Japanese says タイガ and Korean 타이가, so those two fields hold the character readings. Compare [[針葉樹]] (conifer) and [[森林]] (forest).
