## Notes
- [方](../characters/方.md) "square, direction" + [針](../characters/針%20(char).md) "needle" — no stand-in relationship applies (方's own stand-in is [[方向]]; 針's own is itself).

方針 means policy, principle, or philosophy (literally "compass needle," extended metaphorically to "guiding direction").

Mandarin fāngzhēn, Cantonese fong1 zam1, Japanese ほうしん, Korean 방침, and Vietnamese phương châm are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄈㄚㄫㄐㄧㄇ unique across both words and characters).
