## Notes
- [侍](../characters/侍.md) "to attend, serve" + [者](../characters/者.md) (agent suffix) — "attendant, servant, valet"; in a Buddhist context, specifically a monk who closely attends a master (paralleling Sanskrit antevāsin). Legitimizing note: 侍's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, so 侍 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.
