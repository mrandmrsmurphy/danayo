## Notes
- [侍](../characters/侍.md) "to attend, serve" + [者 (char)](../characters/者%20(char).md) (agent suffix) — "attendant, servant, valet"; in a Buddhist context, specifically a monk who closely attends a master (paralleling Sanskrit antevāsin). Both `korean: 시자` (compositional) and `vietnamese: thị giả` (directly attested, confirmed via web search) fill previously blank fields. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 시차 diverges from Sino-Korean 시자 in the second syllable's coda — the same divergence shared with both homophones.
