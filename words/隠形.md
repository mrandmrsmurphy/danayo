## Notes
- [隠](../characters/隠.md) "hide" + [形 (char)](../characters/形%20(char).md) "shape, form, type"

隠形 means invisible, or the act of concealing one's form or presence. Mandarin 隱形/隐形 (yǐnxíng) is a common modern technical term (隱形飛機, "stealth aircraft"; 隱形眼鏡, "contact lens," literally "invisible glasses"). Japanese 隠形 (おんぎょう) is historically associated with ninjutsu and esoteric practice — 隠形術 (onmyōjutsu-adjacent techniques for concealing one's form from an opponent). Vietnamese uses tàng hình for this concept ("stealth," e.g. máy bay tàng hình, "stealth aircraft," paralleling the modern Mandarin technical sense) rather than the literal compositional ẩn hình built directly on 隠's own stored "ẩn" reading.

**Found and fixed a real reading bug**: `羅馬字`/`諺文` had drifted to 'inheng/인헝, inconsistent with 隠's own correctly-stored 'ǝn/은 (注音 ㄜㄋ was already correct) — the same class of bug (wrong 羅馬字/諺文 with correct 注音 as the tell) documented repeatedly earlier in this sweep.
