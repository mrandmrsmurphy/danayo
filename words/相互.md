## Notes
- [相](../characters/相.md) "mutual" + [互](../characters/互.md) "mutual" — both 相's own stand-in and 互's own stand-in are this exact compound, so transitivity holds (A=B=AB); marked `#cranberry`. Both characters independently mean "mutual," reinforcing rather than combining distinct senses.

相互 means mutual or reciprocal, describing a relationship or action that runs both ways between two or more parties: 相互理解 ("mutual understanding"), 相互作用 ("interaction"), 相互依存 ("mutual dependence, interdependence"). Mandarin xiānghù, Cantonese soeng1 wu6, Japanese そうご (sōgo), and Korean 상호 (sangho) are all fully compositional and used in this formal/technical register.

Korean also has the native 서로 (seoro, "each other, mutually"), which both constituent characters independently cite as their own native gloss — 서로 is the everyday word for reciprocity in ordinary speech, with 상호 reserved for more formal, written, or technical contexts. Vietnamese tương hỗ is compositional and carries the same formal register as its Sino cousins.
