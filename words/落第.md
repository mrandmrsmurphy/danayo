## Notes
- [落](../characters/落.md) "fall" + [第 (char)](../characters/第%20(char).md) "-th"

落第 is to fail: to fail an exam, or to fail to advance to the next grade. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin luòdì as, archaically, "to fail in an imperial examination", and by extension "to fail an exam or test". Japanese らくだい is "failure in an examination; failure to advance to the next grade or graduate from school". Korean 낙제 and Cantonese lok6 dai6 match.

It is the opposite of [合格](合格.md), passing, and it parallels [及第](及第.md), "to pass", whose opposite it is in the old examination vocabulary.
