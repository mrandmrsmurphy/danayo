## Notes
- [陰 (char)](../characters/陰%20(char).md) "lunar, yin" + [暦 (char)](../characters/暦%20(char).md) "calendar" + [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year"

陰暦年 names a year as reckoned by the lunar calendar — synonymous with [[旧正月]] in the specific sense of "the lunar new year," and the direct counterpart to a Gregorian/solar calendar year. See [[太陰暦]] for the underlying calendar system itself.

Japanese いんれきねん and Korean 음력년 are both standard compositional Sino-readings. Vietnamese âm lịch niên is likewise standard, closely related to the everyday năm âm lịch ("lunar year"). No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄧㄇㄌㄝㄎㄋㄝㄋ unique across both words and characters).
