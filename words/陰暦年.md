## Notes
- [陰 (char)](../characters/陰%20(char).md) "lunar, yin" + [暦 (char)](../characters/暦%20(char).md) "calendar" + [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year"

陰暦年 is a lunar year. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 陰曆年 (alias form here; Mandarin yīnlìnián) as "Chinese New Year; Spring Festival". The Japanese いんれきねん, Korean 음력년 and Vietnamese âm lịch niên fields are built from the readings of the three characters. See [旧正月](旧正月.md), the lunar new year, and [太陰暦](太陰暦.md), the lunar calendar.
