## Notes
- [又 (char)](../characters/又%20(char).md) "or again"

又 is 象形, depicting a right hand — the original form of [[右]] ("right, the direction"), which later split off as a dedicated derivative once 又 itself was given over entirely to its grammatical "again, also" sense. That "again" sense is not a direct pictographic extension: it is treated as an adverbial derivation sharing its sound and semantic root with [[有]] ("to have; there is") rather than descending from the hand image itself. As an adverb, 又 marks repetition or addition: 又下雨了 ("it's raining again"), 又便宜又好 ("both cheap and good").

Mandarin yòu, Cantonese jau6, and Japanese ゆう (yū, on'yomi, rare in modern use) alongside native また (mata, "again, also," the everyday adverb) all preserve the sense. Korean 우 (u) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 또 (tto, "again," the everyday native adverb). Vietnamese hựu is the Hán Việt reading, attested directly in dictionary entries glossing "lại, vừa...lại..., cũng" ("again; both...and..."); the character page's other stored candidate, lại, is itself the everyday native Vietnamese word for "again" rather than the Sino-Vietnamese reading, and was not used here.

This word shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading ('uo/웃/ㄨㄛ) with another word still awaiting its own turn in this sweep, [[隅]] ("nook") — the reciprocal half of this callout will be completed when it comes up.
