## Notes
- [義](../characters/義.md) "righteousness; duty" + [務](../characters/務.md) "task; affair"

義務 is a duty or obligation: what a person is bound to do. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yìwù as "duty; obligation; responsibility", and also "compulsory" and "without remuneration". Japanese ぎむ is "duty, obligation". Vietnamese nghĩa vụ is the same word, and Korean 의무 and Cantonese ji6 mou6 match.

It is a duty owed to society or the law, and its counterpart is [権利](権利.md), a right. A legal liability is a [責任](責任.md).
