## Notes
- [婆](../characters/婆.md) "old woman" reduplicated — colloquially "grammy, paternal grandmother" in some regional Mandarin usage, but far more commonly in modern standard Mandarin "mother-in-law" (husband's mother, 老公的妈妈) — a significant additional sense not previously documented, added to `english`. **Real content bug found and fixed**: `cantonese` had two comma-joined tone variants — trimmed to the primary changed-tone form po4 po4-2 (a common pattern for kinship terms in Cantonese). Typo fixed: "grandmather" → "grandmother." **`japanese`/`korean`/`vietnamese` deliberately left blank**: reduplication doesn't naturalize, matching the [[伯伯]]/[[叔叔]]/[[女女]]/[[姉姉]]/[[姥姥]]/[[娃娃]] pattern — each language uses its own distinct native term for "mother-in-law" (姑, しゅうとめ in Japanese; 시어머니 in Korean; mẹ chồng in Vietnamese). Normalized `hsk_level` to a quoted string. `kwin` is false by construction: no `korean` value exists to compare against. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄅㄚ unique among both words and characters).
