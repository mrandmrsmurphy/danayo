## Notes
- [婆](../characters/婆.md) "old woman" reduplicated — colloquially "grammy, paternal grandmother" in some regional Mandarin usage, but far more commonly in modern standard Mandarin "mother-in-law" (husband's mother, 老公的妈妈) — a significant additional sense. `cantonese: po4 po4-2` uses the changed-tone form common for Cantonese kinship terms. **The `japanese`, `korean` and `vietnamese` fields are deliberately omitted**: reduplication doesn't naturalize, matching the [[伯伯]]/[[叔叔]]/[[女女]]/[[姉姉]]/[[姥姥]]/[[娃娃]] pattern — each language uses its own distinct native term for "mother-in-law" (姑, しゅうとめ in Japanese; 시어머니 in Korean; mẹ chồng in Vietnamese).
