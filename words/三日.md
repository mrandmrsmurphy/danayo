## Notes
- [三 (char)](../characters/三%20(char).md) "three/third" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — no stand-in relationship applies.

Japanese みっか (mikka) is a real special native reading, part of the same irregular first-ten-days-of-the-month family as [[一日]]'s ついたち and [[三十日]]'s みそか, not the expected compositional さんにち. Mandarin sānrì, Cantonese saam1 jat6, Korean 삼일, and Sino-Vietnamese tam nhật (all previously missing except Korean) are standard compositional readings. `kwin` is false (AND-rule: 三's own kwin is true, but 日's own is false, so the product is false).
