## Notes
- [昇](../characters/昇.md) "ascend" + [天](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven" — no stand-in relationship applies (昇's own stand-in is [[上昇]]; 天's own is itself).

昇天 means to ascend to heaven, or (euphemistically) to die.

Mandarin shēngtiān, Cantonese sing1 tin1, Japanese しょうてん, Korean 승천, and Vietnamese thăng thiên are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨㄫㄊㄝㄋ unique across both words and characters).
