## Notes
- [創](../characters/創.md) "create" + [立](../characters/立%20(char).md) "stand, establish" — this word is the stand-in for [創](../characters/創.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo (立's own stand-in is [[立]] itself, an unrelated word).

創立 means to establish or found — an institution, organization, or enterprise.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `mandarin` had 立's cantonese reading erroneously appended to the mandarin string ("chuànglì; ong3 laap6"), leaving `cantonese` itself blank — split apart and corrected to `cantonese: cong3 laap6` (from 創's own cong3 + 立's own laap6/lap6 variant, both attested across sibling compounds). `羅馬字` also had 創's syllable using the divergent form "cang" rather than its own authoritative "cwang" — corrected to cwanglib (諺文 촹립 was already correct). Japanese そうりつ, Korean 창립, and Vietnamese sáng lập are standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄑ⺢ㄫㄌㄧㄆ unique across both words and characters).
