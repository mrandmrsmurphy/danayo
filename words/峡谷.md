## Notes
- [峡](../characters/峡.md) "gorge" + [谷](../characters/谷%20(char).md) "valley" — "gorge-valley." 峡's own `stand_in` is [[海峡]] and 谷's is itself; neither points here, so 峡谷 is an independent compound.

峡谷 is a canyon or gorge: a deep, narrow valley with steep sides. Mandarin xiágǔ, Cantonese haap6 guk1, Japanese きょうこく and Korean 협곡 use it; the traditional spelling 峽谷 is kept as an alias. Compare [[谷]] (valley) and [[海峡]] (strait).
