## Notes
- [毛](../characters/毛%20(char).md) "hair, fur" + [父](../characters/父.md) "father" — "Esau, the twin brother of Jacob." Their own `stand_in` values: 毛's is itself; 父's is [[父親]]; none points here, so 毛父 is an independent compound.

毛父 is Esau, Isaac's elder twin, born "all like a hairy cloak" (Genesis 25:25), and the father of the Edomites (36:9). It is a Dan'a'yo **neologism**, a meaning-based name, "hairy father" (毛 hair, 父 father), and it names the Genesis section 毛父世代記. Compare [[踵祖]] (Jacob) and [[笑生]] (Isaac). See [[基督敎]].

- Hebrew name: עֵשָׂו (Esav) (注音 ·ㄝㄙㄚㄆ).
