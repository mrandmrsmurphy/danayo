## Notes
- [俄](../characters/俄.md) "a moment, briefly" + [然 (char)](../characters/然%20(char).md) (adverbial suffix) — "suddenly, all of a sudden," a synonym of 突然/đột nhiên. `korean: 아연` (compositional) and `vietnamese: nga nhiên` are both directly attested. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 아년 diverges from the compositional Sino-Korean 아연 in the second syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄋ⼶ㄋ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 俄's own `stand_in` is this exact word (俄然), so 俄 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 俄然.
