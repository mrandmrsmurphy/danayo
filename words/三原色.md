## Notes
- [三 (char)](../characters/三%20(char).md) "three" + [原](../characters/原.md) "source, original" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — three primary colors.

三原色 is the three primary colors as a set: red, yellow and blue in traditional painting, red, green and blue (RGB) in light and on screens, cyan, magenta and yellow (CMY) in printing. It is [[三]] plus [[原色]]. Mandarin sānyuánsè, Japanese さんげんしょく and Korean 삼원색 are the standard readings. Mixing any two primaries produces a secondary color, and the secondary that sits opposite a primary on the color wheel is its [[補色]].
