## Notes
- [陽 (char)](../characters/陽%20(char).md) "Yang" + [性](../characters/性.md) "nature"

陽性 has two related uses. In traditional yin-yang and grammatical/biological gender contexts, it denotes the "yang," masculine, or positive pole — paired against 陰性 ("yin, feminine, negative"). In modern medical usage, it means a positive test result (陽性反応, "positive reaction/result"), the opposite of 陰性 in that sense too. Mandarin 陽性/阳性 (yángxìng), Cantonese 陽性 (joeng4sing3), Japanese 陽性 (ようせい), Korean 양성 (yangseong), and Sino-Vietnamese dương tính all share both senses across the CJKV sphere, with the medical "positive result" sense now the far more common everyday usage.
