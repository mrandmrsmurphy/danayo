## Notes
- [稍](../characters/稍%20(char).md) "slightly, rather" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 稍 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

稍 is an adverb of small degree — "slightly, a bit, somewhat" — and in older/classical usage also carried a sense of "gradually" (稍稍, reduplicated, "little by little"). It commonly modifies adjectives and verbs: 稍微 ("slightly," Mandarin's more common everyday form), 稍等 ("wait a moment").

Mandarin shāo and Korean 초 (cho) are standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese やや (yaya, native) is the everyday word for "slightly"; the on-readings ソウ/ショウ (SOU/SHOU) are largely confined to compounds and less common in isolation. Vietnamese offers two candidates, sảo and sao, both stored on the character page.
