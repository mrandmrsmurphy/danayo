## Notes
- [発](../characters/発%20(char).md) "issue, start" + [動](../characters/動%20(char).md) "move" + [机](../characters/机.md) "machine" — "starting-moving machine." Their own `stand_in` values: 発's is itself; 動's is itself; 机's is [[机会]]; none points here, so 発動机 is an independent compound.

発動机 is a motor or engine: a machine that turns energy into motion. Mandarin fādòngjī and Cantonese faat3 dung6 gei1 are the ordinary Chinese words, and Korean 발동기 is the same word. Japanese says エンジン or モーター, so the `japanese` field holds the readings of the characters. Compare [[机器]] (machine).
