## Notes
- [三 (char)](../characters/三%20(char).md) "three" + [百 (char)](../characters/百%20(char).md) "hundred" — three hundred, 300.

Plain digit + 百, like [[二百]], [[七百]] and [[八百]]: the hundred is counted by prefixing the digit to 百, with no further grouping word. Japanese さんびゃく voices ひゃく to びゃく after the nasal of 三 rather than using the plain concatenation さんひゃく. Korean 삼백 follows the Sino-Korean reading directly. `vietnamese: tam bách` is the Sino-Vietnamese compositional reading; everyday Vietnamese uses the native ba trăm for round numbers like this. Compare [[三千]] and [[三十]].
