## Notes
- [記](../characters/記.md) "record" + [念](../characters/念.md) "thought, commemorate" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day"

記念日 names a day set aside to commemorate a past event — a wedding anniversary, a founding date, a historical event — need not be an official non-working holiday, unlike [[祝日]]. The word literally combines "to record" with "to hold in mind, to commemorate," describing a day that keeps a memory alive.

Note: Japanese reads きねんび (kinenbi); 日 is read as び (bi), a voiced native reading. Korean 기념일 is a standard compositional Sino-Korean reading. Vietnamese ký niệm nhật is compositionally derived from 記's own "ký" reading — distinct from the everyday Vietnamese ngày kỷ niệm, which uses the near-homophone but different character 紀 (kỷ) rather than 記 (ký). No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧ·ㄋㄝㄇㄋㄧㄊ unique across both words and characters).
