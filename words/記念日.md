## Notes
- [記](../characters/記.md) "record" + [念](../characters/念.md) "thought, commemorate" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day"

記念日 is a commemorative day, an anniversary. English Wiktionary glosses Japanese 記念日 (きねんび) as "anniversary (the day an exact number of years since an event)", and lists Chinese 紀念日 (Mandarin jìniànrì) as "commemoration day; memorial day". Korean 기념일 and Vietnamese ký niệm nhật are built from the readings of the three characters. A national holiday is the [祝日](祝日.md).
