## Notes
- [多 (char)](../characters/多%20(char).md) "many" + [数](../characters/数.md) "number"

多数 is a large number, and the majority: most of a group. Wiktionary glosses Japanese たすう as "a great many, a great number of". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 다수 matches.

It is the opposite of [少数](少数.md). The decision of the majority is the usual ground of a [世論](世論.md), public opinion.
