## Notes
- [分 (char)](../characters/分%20(char).md) "divide" + [類](../characters/類.md) "kind, category" — "to divide into kinds". Neither character's `stand_in` points here (類's is [種類](種類.md)), so this is not a legitimizer word.

分類 means to classify, and classification. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 分類 (alias 分类; Mandarin fēnlèi, Cantonese fan1 leoi6) as the transitive "to classify; to categorize; to sort", Japanese ぶんるい as "classification, categorization" and "to categorize, to classify", Korean 분류 as "classification", and Vietnamese phân loại as "classification" and "to classify". Compare [分析](分析.md), analysis, and [種類](種類.md), kind.
