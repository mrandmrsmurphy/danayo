## Notes
- [一 (char)](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" + [万](../characters/万.md) "ten thousand" — legitimizing note: 万's own `stand_in` is this exact compound (万's own reading 몬 is phonologically "overfull" and must always be spoken as 一万); 一's own `stand_in` is 一 itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply.

Mandarin yīwàn, Cantonese jat1 maan6, Japanese いちまん, Korean 일만, and Sino-Vietnamese vạn are all standard compositional readings; vạn is additionally the everyday Vietnamese word for "ten thousand" (alongside the native numeral phrase mười nghìn, "ten [times a] thousand," not used here). `kwin` is false (AND-rule: both 一 and 万 individually false). **Fixed a real bug on `characters/万.md`**: its own stored `cantonese` was `mak6`, phonologically implausible for a non-entering-tone MC final (元 rhyme -ʉɐn) — corrected to `maan6`, the real, standard Cantonese reading, matching what this word's own field already correctly used. Removed the duplicate `品詞` key.
