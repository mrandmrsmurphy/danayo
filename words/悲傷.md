## Notes
- [悲](../characters/悲%20(char).md) "be sad" + [傷](../characters/傷.md) "wound, injure" — no stand-in relationship applies (悲's own stand-in is itself; 傷's own is [[傷害]]).

悲傷 means sad, sorrowful, or grieving — a near-synonymous doubling of "sadness" and "hurt."

Mandarin bēishāng, Cantonese bei1 soeng1, Japanese ひしょう, Korean 비상, and Vietnamese bi thương are all standard, fully compositional readings. Removed the redundant self-referential alias (悲傷 aliasing itself) and the reversed-order forms 傷悲/伤悲 (a distinct, differently-ordered synonym, not an orthographic variant of this word). Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄧㄜㄙ⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
