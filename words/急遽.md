## Notes
- [急 (char)](../characters/急%20(char).md) "quick" + [遽](../characters/遽.md) "in a hurry"
- This word is the viable form of [遽](../characters/遽.md): the character's meaning (in a hurry) is identical, but 遽 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 急遽 is its `stand_in` compound.

急遽 is an adverb for acting in haste because something has suddenly come up: Japanese きゅうきょ (kyūkyo) is very common in news and business style (急遽中止, "cancelled at short notice"; 急遽帰国, "hurriedly returned home"), and Korean 급거 (geupgeo) is used the same way. Mandarin jíjù and Cantonese gap1 geoi6 exist as literary words, but ordinary Chinese uses other words (匆忙, 急忙) for the same idea. 遽 on its own is "hastily, suddenly" (遽然).

Dan'a'yo-specific sense: the same as the shared one. It marks urgency caused by suddenness, so it sits in the Time domain's "suddenness and speed" group beside [[突然]] ("sudden") and [[急速]] ("rapid"), which describe the event or the speed rather than the hasty reaction.
