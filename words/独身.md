## Notes
- [独](../characters/独.md) "alone" + [身](../characters/身.md) "body, person" — unmarried.

独身 is being unmarried: a person without a spouse, whether never married, divorced or widowed. It is "alone-person." Mandarin dúshēn (synonym 單身; also "alone, by oneself"), Cantonese duk6 san1, Japanese どくしん, Korean 독신 and Vietnamese độc thân are all confirmed on Wiktionary. It names the status, where [[離婚]] (divorce) and [[寡婦]] (widow) name events and a person within it.
