## Notes
- [悉](../characters/悉.md) "entirely" + [率](../characters/率.md) "rate" — a 代用字 form: 悉 and 率 stand in for the rare characters 蟋 and 蟀 of the real compound 蟋蟀, which are not in the vault.

悉率 is the cricket, written for 蟋蟀 (Mandarin xīshuài, Cantonese sik1 seot1). Wiktionary lists the compound for Chinese, Japanese (しっしゅつ, alongside the native こおろぎ), and Korean (실솔, an archaic hanja form), all glossed "cricket." 悉 matches 蟋 in Mandarin (xī) and 率 can be read shuài like 蟀, so the substitution keeps the Mandarin sound close. The same device gives [盧玆](盧玆.md) for the cormorant.
