## Notes
- [大](../characters/大%20(char).md) "great" + [陸](../characters/陸.md) "land" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [陸](../characters/陸.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (大's own stand-in is itself).

大陸 means continent or mainland.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `aliases` incorrectly listed the word's own filename (大陸) as if it were an alias of itself — removed, keeping only the genuine simplified form 大陆. Japanese たいりく, Korean 대륙, Mandarin dàlù, and Cantonese daai6 luk6 are all standard readings. Vietnamese đại lục is a standard reading. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄧㄌㄨㄎ unique across both words and characters).
