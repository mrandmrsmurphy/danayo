## Notes
- [硬 (char)](../characters/硬%20(char).md) "hard, firm" + [度](../characters/度.md) "degree, extent" — hardness.

硬度 is hardness measured as resistance to scratching, denting or indentation, the property by which metals and minerals are ranked (the Mohs scale for minerals, the Vickers and Rockwell scales for metals). It is [[硬]] "hard" plus 度 "degree, extent", the same measuring suffix seen in [[精度]] and [[程度]]. Mandarin yìngdù also covers the "hardness" of water; Japanese こうど and Korean 경도 are the standard readings.
