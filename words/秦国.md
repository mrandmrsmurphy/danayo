## Notes
- [秦](../characters/秦.md) "Qin" + [国](../characters/国.md) "state, country"
- 秦国 is the stand-in for [秦](../characters/秦.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.

Wiktionary lists 秦国 (秦國) as a historical proper noun for Qin, an ancient Chinese state of the Zhou period, with Mandarin Qínguó and Cantonese ceon4 gwok3, and gives 秦 alone as a synonym. It also records a humorous internet-slang use as a deliberate misspelling of 泰國 "Thailand". Japanese しんこく and Korean 진국 are the Sino-Xenic readings.

In Dan'a'yo the word names the historical state, the one that, as the [China](../lexipedia/China.md) page relates, unified the Warring States into China's first empire.
