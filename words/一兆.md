## Notes
- [一](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" + [兆](../characters/兆.md) "trillion" — legitimizing note: neither 一 nor 兆's own `stand_in` (which is [[前兆]]) points to this word, so no `#cranberry`; 一兆 stands as its own independent compound rather than being required by either constituent.

Mandarin yīzhào and Cantonese jat1 siu6 restore the "one" prefix (`一`) that had been dropped in both fields, matching the established pattern on sibling numeral words [[一万]]/[[一億]]. Japanese いっちょう shows the same sokuon assimilation of いち before an unvoiced-obstruent-initial reading already documented on [[一体]] (いち + ちょう → いっちょう) — the previous field, bare ちょう, was missing the いち prefix entirely. Korean 일조 is the standard compositional reading. Vietnamese một triệu triệu ("one million million") is the real circumlocution used for "trillion" in Vietnamese, which has no single native or Sino-Vietnamese word for this magnitude — kept as-is rather than reduced to a bare "triệu" (which alone means only "million" in everyday Vietnamese and would be misleading).
