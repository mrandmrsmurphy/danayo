## Notes
- [披](../characters/披%20(char).md) "expose" + [歴](../characters/歴.md) "pass through" — no stand-in relationship applies (披's own stand-in is itself; 歴's own is [[歴史]]).

披歴 means to state one's view or reveal one's true feelings.

The real-world word is 披瀝/披沥 (using 瀝, "to drip, trickle" — the aliases listed above), attested in Japanese ひれき and read identically to 歴 in every language here (Mandarin lì, Cantonese lik6, Japanese REKI). Since 瀝 has no page of its own in this vault, 披歴 substitutes the phonetically identical 歴 as its written form — the same character-substitution logic that produced this word's `aliases`. Mandarin pīlì, Cantonese pei1 lik6, Japanese ひれき, Korean 피력, and Vietnamese phi lệch are all standard, fully compositional readings under this substitution. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄨㄧㄌㄝㄎ unique across both words and characters).
