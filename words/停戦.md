## Notes
- [停](../characters/停.md) "stop" + [戦](../characters/戦.md) "war"

停戦 is a ceasefire: an agreement to stop fighting for the time being. Wiktionary glosses Japanese as "armistice; to suspend hostilities". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 정전 matches.

It halts the fighting without ending the war. The settlement that ends it is [和平](和平.md), and a lasting reconciliation is [和解](和解.md).
