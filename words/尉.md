## Notes
- [尉 (char)](../characters/尉%20(char).md) "officer" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [尉 (char)](../characters/尉%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

尉 names a junior military or police officer rank — in the modern CJK military-rank hierarchy, it sits below 校 ([[将校]]) and 将, forming the base tier of commissioned officer titles (少尉 "second lieutenant," 中尉 "first lieutenant," 大尉 "captain"). The character's own graphemic root combines 寸 ("hand," a measuring/controlling gesture) and 火 ("fire"), traditionally glossed as depicting a hand holding a hot iron to press or subdue — extended to "one who suppresses/administers," hence "official, officer."

Japanese い, Korean 위, and Vietnamese uý are all standard readings, though 尉 rarely stands alone in ordinary usage outside of rank-name compounds (少尉, 大尉, etc.) in any of the four languages. No homophones among other words (注音 ㄨㄊ unique).
