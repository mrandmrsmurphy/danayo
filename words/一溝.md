## Notes
- [一 (char)](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" + [溝 (char)](../characters/溝%20(char).md) "ditch, gutter" — one of the traditional large-number units in the classical Chinese numeral hierarchy (亿/兆/京/垓/秭/穰/溝/澗/正/載), where 溝 (10³²) sits between 穰 and 澗. The everyday sense "ditch, gutter" has no connection to the number; the character was simply repurposed as a place-value name the same way 兆 ("omen") and 京 ("capital city") were.

This is a classical/scholarly register unit, essentially unused outside enumerating the traditional numeral hierarchy itself — unlike 兆 (still the everyday word for "trillion" in modern Mandarin) or 億 (China's "hundred million"), 溝 as a number rarely if ever appears in ordinary speech in any of the CJKV languages. `vietnamese` is intentionally omitted: no independently attested Sino-Vietnamese reading for 溝 in this specific large-number sense turned up in search, and Vietnamese numeral practice in any case does not productively use the classical Chinese large-unit hierarchy beyond 億/兆-equivalent ranges — per the vault's omit-don't-fabricate rule, left blank rather than guessed. `korean: 일구` is the plain compositional concatenation of 一's own 일 + 溝's own 구. `kwin` is false, the same [[一 (char)]] root-cause pattern already fixed and confirmed across several 一-initial compounds this stretch of the sweep (Dan'a'yo 읻 retains the MC checked coda that Sino-Korean 일 has lost). No homophones (注音 ㄧㄊㄍㄛㄨ unique among both words and characters).
