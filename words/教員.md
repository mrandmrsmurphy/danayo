## Notes
- [教](../characters/教.md) "teach" + [員](../characters/員.md) "personnel, employee" — no stand-in relationship applies (教's own stand-in is [[教授]]; 員's own is [[人員]]).

教員 means schoolteacher, educator, or instructor.

Mandarin jiàoyuán, Cantonese gaau3 jyun4, Japanese きょういん, Korean 교원, and Vietnamese giáo viên are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `羅馬字` used the old minority "gyau" instead of majority-established "gyou" (already correctly reflected in 諺文/注音). No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼄ㄨㄨㄋ unique across both words and characters).
