## Notes
- [貿](../characters/貿.md) "to exchange goods; to trade" + [易](../characters/易.md) "to exchange; to change" — stand-in for [[貿]], which cannot appear independently

貿易 is a synonym compound: both 貿 and 易 can mean "to exchange," and their pairing gives the standard word for trade and commerce across the CJKV sphere. 貿 is a phono-semantic character (semantic 貝, the cowrie shell used as early currency, with phonetic 卯) and carries the commercial connotation; 易 is more general — "to exchange, to change" — and joins it here in the exchange sense. Pairing near-synonyms is a common classical Chinese device for making an unambiguous compound noun out of characters that are individually polysemous.

易 is polysemous: in Mandarin (yì) it covers "easy/simple" (as in [[容易]] and [[簡易]]), "to change/exchange," and the divination tradition of the Yijing ([[易経]]). The 易 in 貿易 is the exchange sense, and the same sense appears in [[交易]]. From memory, not re-checked: the compound is old in written Chinese and was used for long-distance commerce, and 朝貢貿易 ("tribute trade") is a standard historical term for the tribute system between China and neighboring states.

In modern usage 貿易 (Mandarin màoyì, Cantonese mau6 jik6, Japanese bōeki, Korean 무역 muyŏk, Vietnamese mậu dịch) is the dominant formal term for trade, especially international trade, in all five languages; Wiktionary lists the Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese readings given above, and the simplified spelling 贸易 is an alias of this page. It appears in terms such as 自由貿易協定 (free trade agreement) and 世界貿易組織 (World Trade Organization).

The Dan'a'yo pronunciation ㄇㄛㄨ⼶ㄎ is the concatenation of 貿 ㄇㄛㄨ and 易 ⼶ㄎ. No syllable-boundary dot is needed: the only legal split is ㄇㄛㄨ + ⼶ㄎ, because there is no syllable ㄨ⼶ㄎ (so ㄇㄛ + ㄨ⼶ㄎ fails) and no syllable ㄇㄛㄨ⼶ in `syllables/`.

In the Trade domain the Dan'a'yo sense is the shared CJKV one, trade in the international or large-scale sense; the plain transactional sense is left to [[交易]].
