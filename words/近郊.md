## Notes
- [近 (char)](../characters/近%20(char).md) "near" + [郊](../characters/郊.md) "outskirts"
- This word is the viable form of [郊](../characters/郊.md): the character's meaning (outskirts) is identical, but 郊 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 近郊 is its `stand_in` compound.

近郊 means the outskirts or suburbs of a city: the area close to a town but outside its built-up centre, between city and countryside. Mandarin jìnjiāo, Cantonese gan6 gaau1, Japanese きんこう (kinkō; 東京近郊, "the suburbs of Tokyo") and Korean 근교 (geungyo) are the same compound. 郊 is "the outskirts of a city", originally the zone outside a capital's walls, which is also the sense in the old 郊祀 (the sacrifice to Heaven held outside the capital).

In Dan'a'yo it is the plain word for the outskirts, placed beside [[都市]] (city, town) in the city vocabulary. No narrower or shifted sense is needed.
