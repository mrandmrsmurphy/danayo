## Notes
- [逗](../characters/逗.md) "pause" + [留 (char)](../characters/留%20(char).md) "remain; stay"
- 逗留 is the stand-in word for [逗](../characters/逗.md): the character's meaning (to pause; to linger) is identical, but 逗 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 逗留 is its `stand_in` compound.

Mandarin dòuliú, cantonese dau6lau4, japanese とうりゅう, korean 두류, and vietnamese đậu lưu are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 닷류 diverges from Sino-Korean 두류 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄨㄌ⼜ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 留's own `stand_in` is instead 留 itself, so this word does not legitimize 留.
