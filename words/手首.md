## Notes
- [手 (char)](../characters/手%20(char).md) "hand" + [首](../characters/首.md) "neck; head"

手首 is the wrist: the joint between the hand and the arm. Wiktionary glosses Japanese てくび as "wrist". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 수수 matches the readings of the characters.

It is the "neck of the hand", between the [手掌](手掌.md), palm, and the [腕](腕.md), arm. The ankle, its counterpart on the leg, is the [脚踝](脚踝.md).
