## Notes
- [商](../characters/商.md) "commerce" + [標](../characters/標.md) "sign; mark; label"

商標 is a trademark: a name, sign or mark that identifies a business's goods and is protected by law. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin shāngbiāo as "brand; trademark" and Japanese しょうひょう as "trademark", with Korean 상표 and Cantonese soeng1 biu1 matching. It is shared across the sphere. The word is literally "commerce mark".

It is the third of the usual intellectual-property rights on this page, with [特許](特許.md), the patent for an invention, and [版権](版権.md), the copyright for a work. The mark is registered with the state, and using someone else's is an offence.
