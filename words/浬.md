## Notes
- [浬 (char)](characters/浬%20(char).md) "nautical mile" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 浬 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

浬 means nautical mile, or knot (as a unit of speed) — a modern coinage pairing the water radical with 里, the traditional unit of land distance.

Mandarin lǐ, Cantonese lei5, Japanese り, Korean 리, and Vietnamese rí are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 浬's own stored citations exactly.
