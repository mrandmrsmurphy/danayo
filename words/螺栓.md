## Notes
- [螺](../characters/螺.md) "screw, spiral" + [栓](../characters/栓%20(char).md) "peg, bolt" — "threaded peg," a bolt. Their own `stand_in` values: 螺's is [[螺旋]]; 栓's is itself; none points here, so 螺栓 is an independent compound.

螺栓 is a bolt: a metal peg with a thread, used with a nut to fasten parts together. Mandarin luóshuān and Cantonese lo4 saan1 are the ordinary words. Japanese says ボルト and Korean 볼트, so those two fields hold the character readings. Compare [[螺旋]] (screw, spiral) and [[栓]] (peg, stopper).
