## Notes
- [道 (char)](../characters/道%20(char).md) "way, path" + [教](../characters/教.md) "teach, teaching" — "Daoism," the indigenous Chinese religious/philosophical tradition centered on the 道 (the Way).

Mandarin Dàojiào, cantonese dou6 gaau3, japanese どうきょう, korean 도교, and vietnamese Đạo giáo are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 닷걋 diverges from Sino-Korean 도교 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄨㄍ⼄ㄨ unique among both words and characters).
