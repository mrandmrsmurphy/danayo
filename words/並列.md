## Notes
- [並 (char)](../characters/並%20(char).md) "side by side" + [列](../characters/列.md) "row, file" — parallel, juxtaposed, or arranged side by side, as in 並列構造 ("parallel/comparative structure," as opposed to a hierarchical one) or 並列回路 ("parallel circuit"). No `#cranberry`.

Mandarin bìngliè, Cantonese bing6 lit6, Japanese へいれつ, Korean 병렬, and Sino-Vietnamese tịnh liệt are all standard compositional readings.
