## Notes
- [食 (char)](../characters/食%20(char).md) "food" + [材](../characters/材.md) "material"

食材 is an ingredient: a raw material of cooking. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin shícái as "foodstuff; ingredients" and Japanese しょくざい as "ingredient of food". Cantonese sik6 coi4 matches, and Korean 식재 is the reading of the characters.

It is what goes into [料理](料理.md), cooking, and it is the raw side of [食品](食品.md), food as a product.
