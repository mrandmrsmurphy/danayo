## Notes
- [干](../characters/干.md) "trunk; stem" + [支](../characters/支.md) "branch"

干支 is the sexagenary cycle, the Stems and Branches: the ten heavenly stems and twelve earthly branches taken together to make sixty combinations. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin gānzhī as "the Chinese sexagenary cycle expressed as combinations of the ten heavenly stems and the twelve earthly branches" and Japanese かんし, also えと, as "the sexagenary cycle". Cantonese gon1 zi1 matches, and Korean 간지 matches.

It is formed of the [天干](天干.md), the stems, and the [地支](地支.md), the branches. It dates years, months, days and hours, and the twelve animals are the animal names of the branches.
