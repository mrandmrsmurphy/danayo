## Notes
- [数](../characters/数.md) "number" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "word" — no stand-in relationship applies (数's own stand-in is [[計数]]; 詞's own is [[単詞]]).

数詞 means numeral or number word.

Mandarin shùcí, Cantonese sou3 ci4, Japanese すうし, Korean 수사, and Vietnamese số từ (a genuine standard grammatical term) are all standard, fully compositional readings.
