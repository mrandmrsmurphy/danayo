## Notes
- [数](../characters/数.md) "number" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "word" — no stand-in relationship applies (数's own stand-in is [[計数]]; 詞's own is [[単詞]]).

数詞 means numeral or number word.

Mandarin shùcí, Cantonese sou3 ci4, Japanese すうし, Korean 수사, and Vietnamese số từ (a genuine standard grammatical term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `korean` was contaminated with 數字's reading (숫자, "digit/number," a different compound) instead of compositional 수사; `vietnamese` was a loose native paraphrase ("chữ số," roughly "number character") rather than the standard compositional term. Removed mismatched-character `數字` from `aliases` (a distinct compound), kept genuine simplified alias 数词. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨㄙㄚ unique across both words and characters).
