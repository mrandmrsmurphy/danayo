## Notes
- [睾](../characters/睾.md) "testicle" + [丸](../characters/丸.md) "ball, pill" — 睾's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 丸's own is [[薬丸]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 睾 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

睾丸 is the standard anatomical/medical term for the testicle. Mandarin gāowán, Cantonese gou1 jyun2 (丸 taking a colloquial changed tone in this medical compound), Japanese こうがん (kōgan), and Korean 고환 (gohwan) are all fully compositional. Vietnamese tinh hoàn substitutes 精 ("essence, semen") for 睾 — a different first element from the Chinese/Japanese/Korean forms, though still landing on the same anatomical meaning.
