## Notes
- [穂](../characters/穂%20(char).md) "ear of grain" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 穂 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

穂 names the ear or spike of a cereal plant — the seed-bearing head of rice, wheat, or similar grain, as in 稲穂 ("ear of rice"). The vault's own page uses the Japanese shinjitai form 穂, with the traditional/kyūjitai 穗 as the sole genuine alias.

Mandarin suì, Cantonese seui6, and Korean 수 (su) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading ほ (ho) rather than the on-readings ズイ/スイ (ZUI/SUI) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese tuệ is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading.
