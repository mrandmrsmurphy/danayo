## Notes
- [無](characters/無%20(char).md) "lack" + [然](characters/然%20(char).md) "so" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 無's and 然's own stand-ins are themselves). The real word is properly written 憮然 (listed as an alias); 憮 is a stored alias of 無 per vault convention, so the parent form 無 is used in the compositional spelling here.

無然 (real form 憮然) means disappointed, in low spirits.

Mandarin `wǔrán` correctly preserves 憮's own real reading (distinct from 無's own wú), matching the established precedent that an alias character's genuinely divergent real-world reading is kept rather than forced to the parent's citation (cf. [[為人]]). Following that same logic, filled the blank `cantonese` with mou5 jin4 (憮 reads mou5, not 無's own mou4 — confirmed by the same phonetic series 舞/武, both mou5) and the blank `vietnamese` with vũ nhiên (憮 reads vũ, not 無's own vô/mô — confirmed by 武's own citation vũ/võ, same phonetic series). Japanese ぶぜん (BU+ZEN) needed no such adjustment, since BU is already one of 無's own listed on'yomi readings. Filled blank `korean` with 무연 (compositional; Sino-Korean does not preserve the 無/憮 tonal distinction). Removed blank `hsk_level`. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄜㄋ⼶ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
