## Notes
- [経 (char)](../characters/経%20(char).md) "classic, channel, warp" + [典](../characters/典.md) "dictionary, subject book" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (経's is [[経]] itself, 典's is [[事典]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

経典 names a scripture, canonical text, or sutra — a classic or authoritative written work, especially religious.

Mandarin jīngdiǎn, Cantonese ging1 din2, Japanese きょうてん (kyōten), and Korean 경전 (gyeongjeon) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 経典 is itself a common attested word for "scripture, classic" in all four languages. Vietnamese kinh điển is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
