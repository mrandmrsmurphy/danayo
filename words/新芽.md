## Notes
- [新](../characters/新%20(char).md) "new" + [芽](../characters/芽.md) "bud, sprout" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [芽](../characters/芽.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (新's own stand-in is itself).

新芽 means bud or sprout.

Mandarin xīnyá, Cantonese san1 ngaa4, Korean 신아, and Vietnamese tân nha are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese しんめ is a genuine hybrid reading (新's on-reading しん + 芽's native め) and is the standard, expected term. Simplified `korean` from native 움 to compositional 신아, moving the native equivalents (움, 싹) to prose; simplified `vietnamese` from native búp/chồi likewise. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄋ·ㄚ unique across both words and characters).
