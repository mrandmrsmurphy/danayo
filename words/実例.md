## Etymology
- [実](../characters/実.md) "true, accurate" + [例](../characters/例.md) "example" — stand-in for [[例]], which cannot appear independently

## Notes
- "A concrete example, an actual instance, a precedent" (as opposed to a hypothetical one). **Left `vietnamese` blank rather than guessing**: the mechanical Hán-Việt reading "thực lệ" turned up no attestation as a real fixed compound. **Left `hsk_level` unset**: no reliable classification found.
- **Genuine near-homophone in Mandarin only, not a Dan'a'yo homophone**: 実例 and [[実力]] ("strength, ability, capability") are both pronounced *shílì* in Mandarin (例 and 力 are both *lì*), a real coincidence — but the two characters have entirely different readings in Dan'a'yo (例 → ㄌㄝ/le vs. 力 → ㄌㄧㄎ/lig), so this word carries no Dan'a'yo-level homophone.
- `kwin: false` confirmed (実 alone matches Korean, 例 doesn't — AND-rule). No homophones in Dan'a'yo (注音 ㄙㄧㄊㄌㄝ unique among both words and characters).
