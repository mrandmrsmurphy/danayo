## Notes
- [好](../characters/好%20(char).md) "like, fond of" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color, lust" — "fond of lust," a womanizer. Their own `stand_in` values: 好's is itself; 色's is [[色彩]]; none points here, so 好色 is an independent compound.

好色 is a womanizer: a man fond of women and of lust, and lustful as a quality. Mandarin hàosè and Cantonese hou3 sik1 are the ordinary words, and Japanese こうしょく and Korean 호색 are the same compound. Compare [[淫]] (lewd) and [[情欲]] (lust).
