## Notes
- [春](../characters/春%20(char).md) "springtime" + [川](../characters/川%20(char).md) "river" — the name of Chuncheon, a city of South Korea in the north-east.

春川 (Chuncheon) is the capital of Gangwon Province ([[江原]]特別自治道) in South Korea, a lake city north-east of [[漢城]] (Seoul), known for its spicy stir-fried chicken dish dak-galbi (from memory, not re-checked). The name reads "spring river".

English and Chinese Wiktionary have 春川 as the Korean city (Mandarin Chūnchuān, Cantonese ceon1 cyun1) and English Wiktionary has a Korean entry as a hanja form of 춘천; its Japanese section gives チュンチョン (from the Korean) and a dated reading しゅんせん, which is taken here as the Sino-Xenic reading. The same string is also a Japanese surname, Harukawa. Vietnamese Xuân Xuyên is composed from the character pages; no Vietnamese dictionary entry was found.
