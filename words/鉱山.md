## Notes
- [鉱](../characters/鉱.md) "ore, mineral" + [山 (char)](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain" — mine.

鉱山 is a mine, the place from which ore ([[鉱石]]) is extracted, and by extension the mining operation. 鉱 is the bound character behind [[鉱石]] and [[鉱物]]. The Dan'a'yo headword is the Japanese 鉱山; 礦山 and 矿山 are its Mandarin spellings. Mandarin kuàngshān, Japanese こうざん and Korean 광산 are the standard readings. Compare [[金山]], a gold mine.
