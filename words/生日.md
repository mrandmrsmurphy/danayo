## Notes
- [生](../characters/生.md) "life" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day, sun" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (生's own points to `生活`, 日's own to bare `日`); 生日 is an independent compound.

Mandarin shēngrì, Cantonese saang1 jat6, Korean 생일 and Vietnamese sinh nhật are the standard words for "birthday". The `japanese` field is blank. Japanese does not use the bare two-character 生日 for "birthday" in ordinary usage: the word is 誕生日 (tanjōbi), a different, longer compound (誕 + 生 + 日) and not an alternate spelling. No attested on'yomi reading of 生日 (せいにち, しょうじつ) is a real word, so none is recorded.
