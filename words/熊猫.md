properly 大熊猫
## Etymology
- [熊 (char)](../characters/熊%20(char).md) "bear" + [猫 (char)](../characters/猫%20(char).md) "cat" — literally "bear-cat." No stand-in relationship applies (both 熊's and 猫's own stand-ins are themselves).

熊猫 means panda.

Mandarin xióngmāo, Cantonese hung4 maau1, and Korean 참대곰 ("bamboo bear," the North Korean/문화어 term) were already correct. Fixed a real bug in `vietnamese`: it held the literal Mandarin pinyin "xióngmāo" rather than a Vietnamese reading — corrected to gấu trúc ("bamboo bear"), the real, standard Vietnamese term. Fixed `japanese`: くまねこ (a literal, non-standard compositional reading) → パンダ, the real, standard Japanese word for panda (a loanword, like other real-world animal names in this vault, e.g. [[河馬]], [[海豚]]). Added missing `pos`(名詞). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄨㄫㄇ⼘ㄨ unique across both words and characters).
