## Notes
- [熊 (char)](../characters/熊%20(char).md) "bear" + [猫 (char)](../characters/猫%20(char).md) "cat" — literally "bear-cat." No stand-in relationship applies (both are their own stand-ins).

熊猫 means panda, properly 大熊猫, the giant panda.

Mandarin xióngmāo and Cantonese hung4 maau1 are the compound itself. Korean uses 참대곰 ("bamboo bear", the North Korean term) and Vietnamese gấu trúc ("bamboo bear"), while Japanese uses the loanword パンダ, like [[河馬]] and [[海豚]] for other animals.
