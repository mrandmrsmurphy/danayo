## Notes
- [光 (char)](../characters/光%20(char).md) "light, radiance" + [栄](../characters/栄.md) "flourish, honor, glory" — "honorable, glorious" (光栄なこと, "an honor"; 光栄に思う, "to feel honored"). Mandarin guāngróng, Cantonese gwong1 wing4, Japanese こうえい, Korean 광영, and Vietnamese quang vinh are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 쾅윙 diverges from Sino-Korean 광영 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄎ⺢ㄫㄨㄧㄫ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 栄's own `stand_in` is this exact word (光栄), so 栄 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 光栄.
