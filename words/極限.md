## Notes
- [極](../characters/極%20(char).md) "extreme" + [限](../characters/限.md) "limit, boundary" — no stand-in relationship applies (極's own stand-in is itself; 限's own is [[限度]]).

極限 means limit (in the mathematical sense).

Mandarin jíxiàn, Cantonese gik6 haan6, Japanese きょくげん, and Korean 극한 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `vietnamese` was giới hạn, a native descriptive phrase ("boundary," using the unrelated word 界 giới) rather than the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form — corrected to cực hạn, matching 極's and 限's own citations. Removed blank `swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄎㄏㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
