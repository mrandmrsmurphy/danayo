## Notes
- [圓 (char)](../characters/圓%20(char).md) "circle; round"

圓 is 形声, the interior 口 originally representing 〇, the primitive ideogram for "circle" (鼎 and 囗 were added later). It means "circle, round" (圓形, "circular; round shape") and, by extension, "complete, whole" (圓満, "harmonious, satisfactory"). It is one of the four <ruby>[圓錐曲線](/words/圓錐曲線.md)<rt>⼔ㄋㄐㄨㄧㄎ⼘ㄎㄙ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby> ("conic sections").

Mandarin yuán and Cantonese jyun4 preserve the sense directly. Japanese エン (EN, on'yomi, fully productive: 円 in shinjitai, though this vault deliberately retains 圓 as a rare example of divergence from shinjitai, since 円 is too closely associated with Japanese currency/yen) alongside native まる(い) (maru(i), "round," the everyday adjective) cover the sense. Korean 원 (won) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 둥글다 (dunggeulda, "round"). Vietnamese viên is the Hán Việt reading, distinct from but homophonous with 員 (also viên, as in nhân viên, "staff member"); the character page's other stored candidate, vin, is a genuine Nôm (vernacular) reading rather than a second Hán Việt doublet.

This word shares its reading (wen/원/⼔ㄋ) with [[為]] ("for; because of; to act as"), still unperfected and awaiting its own turn in this sweep.
