## Notes
- [堰](../characters/堰.md) "weir" + [堤](../characters/堤.md) "dike, levee" — no stand-in relationship applies (堰's own stand-in is [[井堰]]; 堤's is [[堤防]]).

堰堤 means a dam. Dan'a'yo uses this literal compound (weir + dike) rather than borrowing the more colloquial Chinese character 壩 (bà, dedicated "dam" character).

**Real bugs found and fixed**: `japanese` had two comma-joined values ("えんてい, ダム") — simplified to just ダム, the real everyday Japanese word for a dam (a katakana loan from English), matching the same real-world-native-equivalent pattern as Korean 댐 (also an English loanword) and Vietnamese `đập` (a native Vietnamese word, not Sino-Vietnamese — the capitalization was also fixed, since this is a common noun). Filled blank `pos: 名詞`; fixed empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄝㄋㄙㄝ unique across both words and characters).
