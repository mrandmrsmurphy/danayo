## Notes
- [浦](../characters/浦%20(char).md) "riverbank, shore" + [項](../characters/項%20(char).md) "nape; item" — the name of Pohang, a port city of South Korea on the east coast.

浦項 (Pohang) is a port city of North Gyeongsang Province ([[慶尚]]北道) in South Korea on the east coast, and the home of a large steelworks (from memory, not re-checked). The characters give 浦 "shore" and 項 "nape, item".

English Wiktionary has 浦項 as the Korean city (Mandarin Pǔxiàng, Cantonese pou2 hong6), Chinese Wiktionary has an entry, and Korean Wiktionary has the string as a hanja form of 포항. English Wiktionary's Japanese section gives the reading ポハン (from the Korean) and a dated reading ほこう, which is taken here as the Sino-Xenic reading. Vietnamese Phố Hạng is composed from the character pages; no Vietnamese dictionary entry was found.
