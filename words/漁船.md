## Notes
- [漁](../characters/漁.md) "to fish" + [船](../characters/船.md) "boat" — fishing boat. Attested across Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, and Korean; no Vietnamese attestation was found, so the Vietnamese field holds the compositional reading.

The blank `vietnamese` field now holds the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading of the two characters, ngư thuyền (漁 ngư, 船 thuyền), following the vault's convention for these fields. The Korean 어선 differs from the Dan'a'yo 諺文 요쉄 in both syllables, so `kwin` is false. The Food page lists the word as "a fishing boat," and Dan'a'yo keeps that sense. It sits beside the existing words [漁民](漁民.md) "fisherfolk, fishing people" and [船舶](船舶.md) "ship, boat," the stand-in for 船, of which 漁船 is the fishing-specific kind. The readings are Mandarin yúchuán, Cantonese jyu4 syun4 and Japanese gyosen (ぎょせん).
