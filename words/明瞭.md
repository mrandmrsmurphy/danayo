## Notes
- [明 (char)](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright" + [瞭](../characters/瞭.md) "clear, clear-sighted" — stand-in for [瞭](../characters/瞭.md), which cannot appear independently.

明瞭 means clear, apparent, plainly understood — a near-synonym of [明白](明白.md) and [明確](明確.md), all sharing the "bright/clear" root 明. It's closely related to [瞭然](瞭然.md) (also built on 瞭), as in the chengyu [一目瞭然](../chengyu/一目瞭然.md), "obvious at a single glance."

Mandarin míngliǎo, Cantonese ming4 liu4, Korean 명료 (myeongnyo), and Japanese めいりょう (meiryō) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 瞭's own stored citations rather than its alias 亮 (liàng/loeng6, "bright," the real reading of the near-synonym compound 明亮 — a different, unrelated word this vault has not separately paged, since 明亮 is stored here only as an alias). Vietnamese minh liễu is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, matching 明's minh and one of 瞭's five stored readings, liễu.
