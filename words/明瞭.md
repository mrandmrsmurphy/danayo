## Notes
- [明 (char)](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright" + [瞭](../characters/瞭.md) "clear, clear-sighted" — this word is the stand-in compound that legitimizes the bound character 瞭 (per its own stored `stand_in`).

明瞭 means clear, apparent, plainly understood — a near-synonym of [[明白]] and [[明確]], all sharing the "bright/clear" root 明. It's closely related to [[瞭然]] (also built on 瞭), as in the chengyu [[一目瞭然]], "obvious at a single glance."

Mandarin míngliǎo, Cantonese ming4 liu4, Korean 명료 (myeongnyo), and Japanese めいりょう (meiryō) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 瞭's own stored citations rather than its alias 亮 (liàng/loeng6, "bright," the real reading of the near-synonym compound [[明亮]] — a different, unrelated word this vault has not separately paged, since 明亮 is stored here only as an alias). Vietnamese minh liễu is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, matching 明's minh and one of 瞭's five stored readings, liễu. **Real bugs found and fixed 2026-09-22**: `mandarin`/`cantonese` had both been swapped for 明亮's own readings (míngliàng/ming4 loeng6) rather than 明瞭's own (míngliǎo/ming4 liu4) — corrected. `注音` stored a corrupted first syllable, ㄚ⼶ㄫ instead of 明's own ㄇ⼶ㄫ — corrected, along with the same typo propagated into three other citations of this word ([[明]]'s own Words list, [[瞭]]'s own Words list, and `syllables/ㄌ⼘ㄨ.md`).

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 명럇 diverges from Sino-Korean 명료 in the second syllable. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄇ⼶ㄫㄌ⼘ㄨ unique among both words and characters).

**Not linked to a lexipedia page**: `scratch.md` buckets this word under Knowledge, but no `Knowledge.md` domain page exists in this vault yet, and neither `Grammar.md` nor any other existing domain has a "clear, apparent" adjective slot (its near-synonym [[明確]] is likewise unlinked, cited only as a flagged gap on `Sophomore List.md`). Left unlinked rather than force-fit into an unrelated domain.
