## Notes
- [拷](../characters/拷.md) "torture" + [問](../characters/問.md) "question"
- This word is the stand-in for [拷](../characters/拷.md), which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo.

拷 is a 形声 character with the 手 "hand" radical and the phonetic 考, and means to beat or strike, and so to torture; 問 is "to ask, to question." 拷問 is therefore to inflict pain in order to make someone speak, torture used as a means of interrogation, and it names both the practice and a single instance. The point of the word is the purpose: pain applied to extract a confession or information, which separates it from [刑罰](刑罰.md) "punishment," where suffering is the penalty itself after judgment.

This is why the word lives on two pages, [Law](../lexipedia/Law.md) (as a legal wrong and a matter of criminal procedure, where torture is prohibited in modern law and by international convention) and [Conflict](../lexipedia/Conflict.md) (as an act of coercion in war and detention). The sense is shared across the sphere: Mandarin kǎowèn (alias 拷问), Cantonese haau2 man6, Japanese gōmon (ごうもん) and Korean 고문. Dan'a'yo keeps the shared sense, "torture, interrogation under torture," with no divergence.
