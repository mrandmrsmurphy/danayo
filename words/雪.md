## Notes
- [雪 (char)](../characters/雪%20(char).md) "snow" — the bare character used directly as a word, its own `stand_in`.

雪 means snow: frozen precipitation (下雪, "to snow"; 雪花, "snowflake"; 暴風雪, "blizzard"), and by extension white or pure (雪白, "snow-white"), and to wipe away or avenge a shame (雪恥, "wipe out a disgrace"; 昭雪, "exonerate"). The variants 䨮 and 鱈 are listed as aliases; the graph carries the rain radical 雨.

Mandarin 雪 (xuě), Cantonese 雪 (syut3), Korean 설 (seol), and Sino-Vietnamese tuyết all preserve this meaning as everyday standalone words. Japanese 雪 (せつ, on'yomi) is comparatively rare as a standalone word — the native kun'yomi ゆき (yuki) is the everyday word for snow, with せつ appearing mainly bound in compounds (積雪, sekisetsu, "accumulated snow"; 除雪, josetsu, "snow removal"). Korean has native 눈.
