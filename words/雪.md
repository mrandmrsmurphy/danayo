## Notes
- [雪 (char)](../characters/雪%20(char).md) "snow" — this word is 雪's own stand-in.

雪 means snow. Mandarin 雪 (xuě), Cantonese 雪 (syut3), Korean 설 (seol), and Sino-Vietnamese tuyết all preserve this meaning as everyday standalone words. Japanese 雪 (せつ, on'yomi) is comparatively rare as a standalone word — the native kun'yomi ゆき (yuki) is the everyday word for snow, with せつ appearing mainly bound in compounds (積雪, sekisetsu, "accumulated snow"; 除雪, josetsu, "snow removal").

Exact syllable check: 雪 shares its Dan'a'yo reading (swed/숻/ㄙ⼔ㄊ) with 説, but 説 doesn't stand alone as its own word (its own stand-in is [[学説]]), so there is no genuine word-level homophone to callout.
