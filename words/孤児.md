## Notes
- [孤](../characters/孤.md) "orphan; alone" + [児 (char)](../characters/児%20(char).md) "child" — orphan.

孤児 is an orphan: a child whose parents have died. It is "orphaned child." Mandarin gū'ér, Cantonese gu1 ji4, Japanese こじ (also みなしご), Korean 고아 and Vietnamese cô nhi are all confirmed on Wiktionary. It goes with [[孤児院]] (orphanage), which was already in the vault, and with [[養子]] and [[養育]] on the adoption side.
