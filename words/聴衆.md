## Notes
- [聴 (char)](../characters/聴%20(char).md) "listen" + [衆](../characters/衆.md) "crowd" — literally "listening crowd."

聴衆 means an audience: the people gathered to listen to a speech, a concert or a performance. English Wiktionary glosses Mandarin 聽眾 (an alias, with 听众; tīngzhòng, Cantonese ting1 zung3) as "listener; audience", with a radio sense of "listenership", and Japanese ちょうしゅう as "audience, the people in attendance". Korean 청중 is "audience, assembly, crowd". Wiktionary also lists a separate Buddhist reading of the Japanese, ちょうじゅ, with no definition given.

It is the audience that listens, where a [[観客]] is a spectator who watches, and the act of attending is [[観覧]].
