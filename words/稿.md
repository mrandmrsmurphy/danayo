## Notes
- [稿](../characters/稿%20(char).md) "manuscript, copy, rough draft" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 稿 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

稿 originally meant "straw" — its 形声 phonetic component is 高, but semantically it draws on 禾 ("grain, stalk"), since paper was originally made from straw pulp. From "straw paper" the sense extended to "a rough draft," and thence to "manuscript, copy" generally: 原稿 ("original manuscript, source text"), 草稿 ("rough draft"), 脱稿 ("to complete a manuscript").

**Genuine Dan'a'yo homophone** with [[高]] ("high") — 稿 is directly derived from 高 as its phonetic component (a real phonetic family, not coincidental convergence), and both share the reading gau/갓/ㄍㄚㄨ; see [[高]]'s own Notes for the full derivation-family analysis.

Mandarin gǎo, Cantonese gou2, Japanese こう (kou), and Korean 고 (go) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese cảo is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading, distinct from the character's other stored candidates khao/kháo.
