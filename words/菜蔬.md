## Notes
- [菜](../characters/菜.md) "vegetable" is separately legitimized by [[野菜]]. [蔬](../characters/蔬.md) "vegetables, greens" is legitimized by this exact compound (its own `stand_in`) — transitivity fails, no cranberry. `vietnamese: thái sơ` is compositional (not independently attested via hvdic).

Mandarin càishū, japanese さいそ, and korean 채소 (also the real, extremely common standard word for "vegetables") all confirmed standard and compositional.
