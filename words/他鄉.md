## Notes
- [他 (char)](../characters/他%20(char).md) "other" + [鄉](../characters/郷.md) "hometown, village" — literally "someone else's hometown," i.e. a foreign land, a place away from home. Directly attested and culturally resonant across all source languages: `vietnamese: tha hương` carries strong connotations of homesickness and longing in Vietnamese literature (as with cố hương, đồng hương, all sharing the same 鄉/hương root). **Real gap found and fixed on [[郷]]'s own page**: its `vietnamese` field was blank — added hương, the well-known Sino-Vietnamese reading. Filled the blank `pos: 名詞`. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 타향 matches Sino-Korean 타향 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄚㄏ⼘ㄫ unique among both words and characters).
