## Notes
- [他 (char)](../characters/他%20(char).md) "other" + [郷](../characters/郷.md) "hometown, village" — literally "someone else's hometown," i.e. a foreign land, a place away from home. Directly attested and culturally resonant across all source languages: `vietnamese: tha hương` carries strong connotations of homesickness and longing in Vietnamese literature (as with cố hương, đồng hương, all sharing the same 鄉/hương root). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 트향 diverges from Sino-Korean 타향 via the AND-rule, since 他's own field independently diverges (despite 郷 itself being `kwin: true`).
