## Notes
- [霧](characters/霧%20(char).md) "fog, mist" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 霧 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

霧 means fog or mist.

Mandarin wù, Cantonese mou6, Japanese む, Korean 무, and Vietnamese vụ (the primary form among the character's own comma-joined vietnamese variants vụ/mù) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bugs: `japanese` held a trailing invisible space; `vietnamese` was a malformed comma-joined single string ("vụ, mù") instead of a proper list — corrected to the scalar primary form. Removed the redundant `品詞` field. Discovered a genuine homophone with [[武]] (both 注音 ㄇㄨ, 諺文 무) — cross-referenced both pages.
