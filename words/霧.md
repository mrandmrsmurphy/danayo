## Notes
- [霧 (char)](../characters/霧%20(char).md) "fog, mist" — the bare character used directly as a word, its own `stand_in`.

霧 means fog or mist: water droplets suspended near the ground that reduce visibility (大霧, "heavy fog"; 霧氣, "mist"; 雲霧, "clouds and mist"; 濃霧, "dense fog"), and by extension a fine spray (噴霧, "spray"; 霧化, "atomize"). The simplified form 雾 is listed as an alias. The graph carries the rain radical 雨 above the vault's recorded component 務.

Mandarin wù, Cantonese mou6, Japanese む (濃霧/のうむ, "dense fog"; 霧散/むさん, "dissipation"), Korean 무 (농무, "thick fog"), and Vietnamese vụ (the primary of the character's two stored variants vụ and mù) are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native noun 霧 (きり) for the everyday word, so on'yomi む is bound to compounds; Korean has native 안개.
