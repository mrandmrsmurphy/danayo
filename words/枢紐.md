## Notes
- [枢 (char)](../characters/枢%20(char).md) "hinge, pivot" + [紐](../characters/紐.md) "knot, button" — this word is 紐's own `stand_in`, since it's too bounded to stand alone.

The compound means literally a door's pivot-post and its knob or loop (Wiktionary glosses it "door hinge, object knob"), and is used figuratively for a pivot, hub, axis or key position: a transport hub, a pivotal point in an argument or a political situation. Mandarin shūniǔ and Cantonese syu1 nau2 match Wiktionary, which writes the Mandarin form 枢纽 with the simplified 枢; the vault headword uses the shinjitai-shared 枢 and aliases the traditional 樞紐.

In Dan'a'yo the sense is the abstract hinge-point or crux, as placed on [Directions](../lexipedia/Directions.md) against the physical rotating-shaft sense of [軸](軸.md). The Japanese reading すうちゅう and Korean 추뉴 are the Sino-Xenic readings of the two characters.
