## Notes
- [捕](../characters/捕%20(char).md) "catch, grab" + [鯨](../characters/鯨.md) "whale" — no stand-in relationship applies (捕's own stand-in is itself; 鯨's own is [[鯨魚]]).

捕鯨 means whaling or whale hunting.

Mandarin bǔjīng, Cantonese bou6 king4, Japanese ほげい, Korean 포경, and Vietnamese bổ canh are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `english` gave only "whale" (the animal) instead of "whaling" (the activity, which is what this compound denotes). No homophones (注音 ㄅㄛㄍ⼶ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
