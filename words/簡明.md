## Notes
- [簡](../characters/簡.md) "simple; brief" + [明 (char)](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright; clear" — literally "simple and clear," that is, plain in construction and easy to see through. Neither character's `stand_in` points here (簡's is [[簡単]], 明's is itself), so no legitimizer relationship applies.

Wiktionary: Chinese "succinct; concise; brief; to the point; simple and clear"; Japanese 簡明 (かんめい) "terse (of a concise style or speech)" and "terseness, conciseness". Close to [[簡潔]]; compare [[簡単]]. Used in `文法 - 02音韻論` and `文法 - 03文字法`.
