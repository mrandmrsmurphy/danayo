## Notes
- [満 (char)](../characters/満%20(char).md) "full" + [盈](../characters/盈.md) "filled" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 盈 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (満's own stand-in is itself, so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

満盈 means filled to capacity.

Mandarin mǎnyíng, Japanese まんえい, Korean 만영, and Vietnamese mãn doanh are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 満's and 盈's own stored citations exactly.

Earlier Notes on this page may state a different value or a different reason.
