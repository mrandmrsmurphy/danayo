## Notes
- [行 (char)](../characters/行%20(char).md) "to travel" + [李](../characters/李.md) "luggage"

行李 is luggage: the belongings one carries when travelling. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 行李 (Mandarin xínglǐ, Cantonese hang4 lei5) as "belongings one carries for travelling; luggage; baggage". It lists Japanese こうり and こり, Korean 행리 and Vietnamese hành lý as entries for the same word.

The bag itself is the [鞄](鞄.md), and a heavier lot of goods is [荷物](荷物.md).
