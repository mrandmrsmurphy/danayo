## Notes
- [従 (char)](../characters/従%20(char).md) "obey, follow, collateral" + [姉 (char)](../characters/姉%20(char).md) "older sister" — the female counterpart of [従兄](従兄.md).

従姉 gives Dan'a'yo an unambiguous word for "older female cousin," distinct from [姉](姉.md)'s own broadened sense: 姉 alone now also covers an older female relative of the same generation more loosely (disambiguated only by context from a literal sister — see the Semantic Range Notes on [Kinship](../lexipedia/Kinship.md)), while 従姉 names the cousin relation specifically and only, the same way [従兄](従兄.md) disambiguates against the fictive-or-real ambiguity built into [哥哥](哥哥.md).

Mandarin cóngzǐ and Cantonese cung4 zi2 are real classical/literary Sino compounds, displaced by 堂姐/表姐 in everyday modern speech. Japanese じゅうし (jūshi) is real but, like the rest of this cluster, largely superseded by plain いとこ in casual speech. Korean 종자 is a real but secondary term next to 사촌언니/사촌누나; Vietnamese tòng tỷ is a real, attested Sino-Vietnamese term (chị họ) alongside the more common đường tỷ (堂姊).
