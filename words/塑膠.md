## Notes
- "Plastic" (the modern industrial material). Filled blank `pos: 名詞`. `japanese: ビニール` ("vinyl") and `korean: 플라스틱` ("plastic") are both deliberate English-loanword choices rather than Sino-vocabulary calques, paralleling [[三稜鏡]]'s プリズム — modern synthetic-material vocabulary frequently borrows wholesale across the CJK sphere rather than coining a shared Sino compound. **`vietnamese` deliberately left blank**: for the same reason, Vietnamese doesn't calque this compound either — the standard everyday word is the fully native nhựa ("plastic, resin"), not a Sino-Vietnamese compositional reading (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 소걋 diverges from Sino-Korean's loanword 플라스틱 entirely — not a meaningful comparison, since Korean uses a borrowed word here rather than a parallel Sino-Korean reading.
