## Notes
- [事 (char)](../characters/事%20(char).md) "matter, affair" + [務](../characters/務.md) "duty, task" + [所 (char)](../characters/所%20(char).md) "place" — literally "a place for handling affairs," i.e. an office, firm, or agency, especially a professional-services one.

事務所 is especially associated with professional practices in Japanese — 弁護士事務所 (bengoshi jimusho, "law office"), 会計事務所 (kaikei jimusho, "accounting firm") — rather than an ordinary corporate office (which would more likely be called 会社 or オフィス instead). Vietnamese sự vụ sở (confirmed via web search, "a place for handling affairs," commonly glossing 律師事務所-type professional offices) matches this specific professional-agency sense, distinct from the more generic văn phòng ("office" in the everyday corporate sense). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 지무스 diverges from Sino-Korean 사무소 in the first syllable's consonant. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄇㄨㄙㄜ unique among both words and characters).
