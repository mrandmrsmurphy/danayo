## Notes
- [債](../characters/債.md) "debt, loan" + [権](../characters/権.md) "right, authority" — "debt right," a creditor's claim. Their own `stand_in` values: 債's is [[負債]]; 権's is [[権利]]; none points here, so 債権 is an independent compound.

債権 is a credit in the financial and legal sense: the creditor's right to be repaid. Mandarin zhàiquán (債權), Cantonese zaai3 kyun4, Japanese さいけん and Korean 채권 all use it, and Vietnamese trái quyền does too. It is the creditor's side of [[債務]]. A "credit card" is not this; see [[信用]] and the suffix [[－証]].
