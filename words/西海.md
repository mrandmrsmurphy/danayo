## Notes
- [西](../characters/西.md) "west" + [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" — the Western Sea, one of the [四海](四海.md)

西海 is the Western Sea, the western one of the 四海. English Wikipedia says the Four Seas were four bodies of water that metaphorically made up the boundaries of ancient China, one for each cardinal direction, and that the West Sea is Qinghai Lake. It says only two of the seas, the East and the South, were tied to real locations before the Han dynasty; the Han wars with the Xiongnu tied the West Sea to Qinghai Lake and later to Lake Balkhash. The lands "within the Four Seas" are a literary name for China (see [天下](天下.md)).

English Wiktionary also lists other Chinese senses of 西海: a former commandery in the ancient Liang Province, a lake in the Shichahai area of Beijing, and, archaic, the Mediterranean Sea; and a Japanese city, Saikai, in Nagasaki Prefecture. The Korean 서해 and Vietnamese Tây Hải fields are built from the readings of the two characters. Compare [東海](東海.md), [南海](南海.md) and [北海](北海.md).
