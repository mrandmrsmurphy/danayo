## Notes
- [容 (char)](../characters/容%20(char).md) "look; appearance; form; figure" + [恕](../characters/恕.md) "forgive, pardon" — this word is the stand-in for [恕](../characters/恕.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

容恕 draws on 容's "to hold, to contain" sense (the same sense underlying [[容器]]) rather than its "appearance" sense — to hold another's fault within oneself without retribution, i.e. to forgive. 恕 itself is the more semantically load-bearing half: a 形声 character built on phonetic 如 ("as, like") plus semantic 心 ("heart"), carrying the specific ethical connotation of putting oneself in another's place — the character sits at the core of the Confucian maxim 己所不欲、勿施於人 ("what you do not wish for yourself, do not do to others"), traditionally glossed as 恕 in classical commentary.

Korean 용서 (yongseo) is the direct, ordinary everyday word for "forgiveness" in modern Korean — considerably more common and unmarked than its Mandarin/Cantonese cognate, where 容恕 reads as a somewhat literary or formal register next to the more colloquial 原諒/寛恕. Vietnamese dung thứ is likewise attested (Wiktionary records it as the standing Vietnamese reading of this exact compound), while Japanese ようしょ is comparatively rare in ordinary speech, which more often reaches for 許す (yurusu, native) or 容赦 (yōsha) for everyday forgiveness/leniency. No homophones (注音 ⼄ㄫ·ㄙ⼄ unique across both words and characters); no HSK level is given for the compound (恕 itself is independently HSK 6, a comparatively advanced character).
