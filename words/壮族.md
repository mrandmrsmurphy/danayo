## Notes
- [壮](../characters/壮.md) "robust, strong" + [族](../characters/族.md) "ethnic group" — the Zhuang people, China's largest ethnic minority, concentrated in Guangxi. `japanese: チワンぞく` is a katakana transliteration of "Zhuang" + 族 (壮 isn't conventionally used as this ethnonym's prefix in Japanese). `korean: 장족` is a real standard Korean term for this ethnicity. `vietnamese: Tráng tộc` is compositional from each character's own confirmed reading — Vietnamese sources also commonly call this group người Choang, a direct phonetic transliteration of "Zhuang" rather than a Sino-Vietnamese calque (domain knowledge, not independently confirmed by search).
