## Notes
- [壮](../characters/壮.md) "robust, strong" + [族](../characters/族.md) "ethnic group" — the Zhuang people, China's largest ethnic minority, concentrated in Guangxi. **Content bug found and fixed**: `cantonese` had two tone variants comma-joined without a space (zong3/zong6 for 壮) — trimmed to the primary reading zong3 zuk6. Filled blank `japanese: チワンぞく` (a katakana transliteration of "Zhuang" + 族, since 壮 isn't conventionally used as this ethnonym's prefix in Japanese), `korean: 장족`, a real standard Korean term for this ethnicity, and `vietnamese: Tráng tộc`, compositional from each character's own confirmed reading — Vietnamese sources also commonly call this group người Choang, a direct phonetic transliteration of "Zhuang" rather than a Sino-Vietnamese calque (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 좡족 diverges from the compositional Sino-Korean 장족 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄐ⺢ㄫㄐㄛㄎ unique among both words and characters).
