## Notes
- [今 (char)](../characters/今%20(char).md) "now" + [晩 (char)](../characters/晩%20(char).md) "evening" + [安](../characters/安.md) "peace, well-being" — "good evening," the evening-hours counterpart to [[今昼安]] ("good mid-day"). Same Dan'a'yo-internal coinage pattern: no real Mandarin/Cantonese/Japanese/Korean compound is built from these same three characters, so each language field instead documents that language's own real evening greeting: 晚上好/maan5 soeng6 hou2, こんばんは (structurally parallel to Japanese's own こんにちは), 안녕하세요 (Korean, like English, doesn't distinguish "good day" from "good evening" in its all-purpose greeting), and xin chào. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 김몬안 diverges entirely from the native-greeting-based Korean field. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄇㄇㄛㄋ·ㄚㄋ unique among both words and characters).
