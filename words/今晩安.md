## Notes
- [今 (char)](../characters/今%20(char).md) "now" + [晩 (char)](../characters/晩%20(char).md) "evening" + [安](../characters/安.md) "peace, well-being" — "good evening" (an arrival greeting), the evening-hours counterpart to [[今昼安]] ("good day") and distinct from [[今夜安]] ("good night," a bedtime/parting farewell) — the same arrival/departure distinction English draws between the two. Same Dan'a'yo-internal coinage pattern: no real Mandarin/Cantonese/Japanese/Korean compound is built from these same three characters, so each language field instead documents that language's own real evening arrival greeting: 晚上好/wǎnshàng hǎo (matching the alias now stored)/maan5 soeng6 hou2, こんばんは (structurally parallel to Japanese's own こんにちは), 안녕하세요 (Korean, like English, doesn't distinguish "good day" from "good evening" in its all-purpose greeting), and chào buổi tối. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 김몬안 diverges entirely from the native-greeting-based Korean field. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄇㄇㄛㄋ·ㄚㄋ unique among both words and characters).

**Real content bugs found and fixed**: `pos` was 名詞 (noun), inconsistent with the sibling greetings' own 感詞 (interjection) — corrected. `vietnamese` was the generic "xin chào" ("hello") rather than the evening-specific chào buổi tối. `mandarin` was stored as bare hanzi rather than pinyin, normalized for consistency with sibling entries. Removed dangling empty `hsk_level`/`swadesh`, added `aliases: [晚上好]` matching the stored mandarin greeting.
