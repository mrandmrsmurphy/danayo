## Notes
- [豊](../characters/豊.md) "abundant, fruitful" + [族](../characters/族.md) "tribe, clan" — "the Abundant Tribe, Ephraim." Their own `stand_in` values: 豊's is [[豊富]]; 族's is [[家族]]; none points here, so 豊族 is an independent compound.

豊族 is the tribe of Ephraim, Joseph's younger son, whose name means "fruitful": "God has made me fruitful in the land of my affliction" (Genesis 41:52). It is a Dan'a'yo **neologism**, a meaning-based name, "Abundant Tribe," and one of the two [[半族]] of [[双族]] (Joseph). The character 豊 has the alias 豐, so the traditional spelling is accepted. Compare [[忘族]] (Manasseh). See [[基督敎]].

- Hebrew name: אֶפְרַיִם (Efrayim) (注音 ·ㄝㄆ·ㄌㄚ·ㄧㄇ).
