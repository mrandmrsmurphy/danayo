## Notes
- [口 (char)](../characters/口%20(char).md) "mouth" — the stand-in for the character 口, which cannot appear independently

口 names the mouth, the pictograph directly depicting its open shape. Beyond the plain anatomical sense, 口 is one of the most productive determiners in the vocabulary, extending to "opening, entrance" generally (入口 "entrance," 瘡口 "wound opening"), to speech and orality (口語 "colloquial language," 口臭 "bad breath," 口訣 "oral mnemonic formula"), and — in a use with no anatomical content at all — as the standard Sino-Xenic measure word/determiner for population counts (人口 "population," literally "mouths" as a headcount unit, the same "mouth = person counted" logic found across the CJKV sphere).

Mandarin kǒu, Cantonese hau2, Korean 구 (gu), and Sino-Vietnamese khẩu are all standard, fully compositional readings, though each language's everyday standalone word for "mouth" instead uses a native term (Japanese くち kuchi; Korean 입 ip) — the bound Sino-Xenic reading survives productively only in compounds.
