## Notes
- [来](../characters/来%20(char).md) "come" + [週](../characters/週.md) "week, cycle" — no stand-in relationship applies (来's own stand-in is itself; 週's own is [[週日]]).

来週 means next week.

Japanese らいしゅう is a standard, fully compositional reading. Fixed severe contamination: `mandarin`/`cantonese` had been copied from the unrelated compound 下週/下周 ("next week" using 下 rather than 来) — corrected to 来's and 週's own compositional readings (láizhōu, loi4 zau1); `korean` held the native phrase 다음주 ("next week" using native 다음), corrected to the North Korean/문화어 compositional form 래주. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field. Removed `下周`/`下週` from `aliases` (a different, unrelated compound built on a different character, not an orthographic variant of 来週). No homophones (注音 ㄌㄚㄧㄐㄨㄛ unique across both words and characters).
