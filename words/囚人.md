## Notes
- [囚](../characters/囚.md) "prisoner" + [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person"

囚人 is a prisoner: a person held in custody. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin qiúrén as "prisoner; convict; inmate" and Japanese しゅうじん as "a prisoner", and Vietnamese tù nhân is the same word in Hán Việt form. It is an exact synonym of [囚徒](囚徒.md), which is the stand-in that makes the bound character 囚 usable. Both words are kept, since 囚人 is transparent from its parts, "prisoner person".

A prisoner is held in the [監獄](監獄.md) after a [判決](判決.md), or while awaiting one. The [獄吏](獄吏.md) is the jailer, and [釈放](釈放.md) is release.
