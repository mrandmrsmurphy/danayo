## Notes
- [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "sun" + [光 (char)](../characters/光%20(char).md) "light"

日光 is sunlight: the light of the sun. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin rìguāng as "sunlight; sunshine; sunbeam; daylight" and Japanese にっこう as "sunlight". Korean 일광 and Cantonese jat6 gwong1 match.

It is the daytime counterpart of [月光](月光.md), moonlight. The sun is the [太陽](太陽.md), and [陽光](陽光.md) is a near-synonym.
