## Notes
- [経 (char)](../characters/経%20(char).md) "classic, channel, warp" + [済 (char)](../characters/済%20(char).md) "aid, relieve" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study" — none of the three characters' own `stand_in` points here (経's is [[経]] itself, 済's is [[済]] itself, 学's is [[学習]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 経 is individually `kwin: false`.

経済学 names the academic discipline of economics — the study ([[学]]) of [[経済]] ("economy," literally "aiding the world").

Mandarin jīngjìxué, Cantonese ging1 zai3 hok6, Japanese けいざいがく (keizaigaku), and Korean 경제학 (gyeongjehak) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 経済学 is itself a common attested word for "economics" in all four languages. Vietnamese kinh tế học is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
