## Notes
- [魔](../characters/魔.md) "demon, magic" + [法](../characters/法%20(char).md) "law, method" — "demonic method," magic. Their own `stand_in` values: 魔's is [[魔鬼]]; 法's is itself; none points here, so 魔法 is an independent compound.

魔法 is magic: the working of supernatural power by a spell or art, and to enchant by working it. Mandarin mófǎ, Cantonese mo1 faat3, Japanese まほう and Korean 마법 all use it, and Vietnamese ma thuật does too. Compare [[巫術]] (shamanism, sorcery) and [[魅惑]] (to charm, which is romantic or social, not magical).
