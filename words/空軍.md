## Notes
- [空 (char)](../characters/空%20(char).md) "empty" + [軍](../characters/軍.md) "armed forces" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (空's is [[空]] itself, 軍's is [[軍隊]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 空 is individually `kwin: false`.

空軍 is the air force, the branch of a nation's armed forces operating military aircraft — one of the standard tripartite division of forces alongside 陸軍 ("army") and 海軍 ("navy"), with 空 ("empty, sky") naming the domain of operation.

Mandarin kōngjūn, Cantonese hung1 gwan1, Japanese くうぐん (kūgun), and Korean 공군 (gonggun) are all standard, fully compositional readings, each the ordinary official term for "air force" in its country. Vietnamese không quân is likewise compositional and standard.
