## Notes
- [露 (char)](../characters/露%20(char).md) "dew" (transliteration character) + [斯](../characters/斯.md) "this" (transliteration character) + [亜](../characters/亜.md) "Asia; secondary" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language" — no `#cranberry`: no constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

露斯亜語 is [[露斯亜]] + 語, the fuller-form counterpart to the contracted [[露語]]. Following the same convention as [[露国]] and [[露語]], `mandarin`/`cantonese` hold the real Chinese equivalent term rather than a literal compositional reading of the Japanese-coined transliteration: since [[露語]] pairs with the short real form 俄语/俄語 (Éyǔ/ngo4jyu5), this fuller form pairs with the fuller real form 俄罗斯语/俄羅斯語 (Éluósīyǔ/ngo4lo4si1jyu5).

Japanese ロシアご (roshiago) and Korean 러시아어 (reosia-eo) both build the modern loanword ロシア/러시아 directly rather than the older kanji transliteration, and are the standard, everyday terms for "Russian language" in their respective languages. Sino-Vietnamese Nga ngữ is likewise real and attested, calqued from 俄語 — Vietnamese does not distinguish a separate longer form corresponding to 俄羅斯語.
