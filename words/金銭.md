## Etymology
- [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal, gold" + [銭](../characters/銭.md) "coin" — "money, cash."

## Notes
Mandarin jīnqián, cantonese gam1 cin4, japanese きんせん, korean 금전, and vietnamese kim tiền are all directly attested standard readings — the same characters mean "money, cash" across all five languages. `aliases: [金錢]` is a genuine orthographic variant (銭/錢 traditional-simplified pair).

Earlier Notes on this page may state a different value or a different reason.
