## Notes
- [無](../characters/無%20(char).md) "lack" + [常](../characters/常%20(char).md) "common, regular" — no stand-in relationship applies (無's own stand-in is itself; 常's own is [[日常]]).

無常 means transient, impermanent, mutable (the Buddhist concept of impermanence).

Mandarin wúcháng, Japanese むじょう, and Korean 무상 are all fully compositional, matching 無's and 常's own stored citations exactly.
