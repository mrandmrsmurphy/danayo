## Notes
- [無](characters/無%20(char).md) "lack" + [常](characters/常%20(char).md) "common, regular" — no stand-in relationship applies (無's own stand-in is itself; 常's own is [[日常]]).

無常 means transient, impermanent, mutable (the Buddhist concept of impermanence).

Mandarin wúcháng, Japanese むじょう, and Korean 무상 are all fully compositional, matching 無's and 常's own stored citations exactly. Fixed a real bug on `characters/常.md` itself: its stored `cantonese` (seung4) used a non-jyutping romanization inconsistent with every other word citing 常 ([[五常]], [[天常]], [[常用]], [[恒常]], [[常識]], [[常時]], [[平常]], [[日常]], [[異常]], [[非常]] all use soeng4) — corrected to soeng4, confirming this word's own `mou4 soeng4` was already correct. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with vô thường, the compositional reading and also the real, standard Buddhist term for impermanence. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄜㄙ⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
