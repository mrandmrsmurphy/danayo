## Notes
- [肉 (char)](../characters/肉%20(char).md) "meat" + [桂](../characters/桂.md) "cinnamon, cassia" — 桂's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 肉's own is [[肉]] itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 肉桂 legitimizes 桂 alone.

肉桂 names cinnamon, or more precisely cassia bark (Cinnamomum cassia).

Mandarin ròuguì, Cantonese juk6 gwai3, Japanese にっけい (nikkei), and Korean 육계 (yukgye, the pharmacological Sino-Korean term for cinnamon bark) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 肉桂 is itself a common attested word for "cinnamon" in all four languages. Vietnamese nhục quế is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading.
