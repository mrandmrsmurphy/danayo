## Notes
- [求](../characters/求.md) "seek, ask for" + [愛](../characters/愛%20(char).md) "love" — "seek love," to court. Their own `stand_in` values: 求's is [[要求]]; 愛's is itself; none points here, so 求愛 is an independent compound.

求愛 is to court or woo: to seek someone's love. Mandarin qiú'ài, Cantonese kau4 oi3, Japanese きゅうあい and Korean 구애 all use it, and in biology it also names the courtship display of animals. It comes before [[求婚]] (to propose). Compare [[秋波]] (a flirtatious glance) and [[約会]] (a date).
