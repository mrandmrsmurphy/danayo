## Notes
- [毅](../characters/毅.md) "resolute, strong" + [然 (char)](../characters/然%20(char).md) "so, thus" — 毅然 is the stand-in word for [毅](../characters/毅.md); 毅 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse.

毅然 describes acting with firm, unwavering resolve — 毅然決然 ("resolutely and decisively") is a common intensified set phrase — often specifically praising someone who holds firm under pressure, adversity, or moral difficulty rather than simply "acting with determination" in a neutral sense. 然 here is the common adverb-forming suffix ("-ly," seen in many 性詞→副詞 derivations), attached to 毅's own core sense of resolute strength.

Japanese きぜん, Korean 의연, and Vietnamese nghị nhiên are all standard compositional Sino-readings. This word is a genuine Dan'a'yo homophone with [[依然]] ("as ... as ever, still") — both read 의년/ㄧㄜㄋ⼶ㄋ, distinct characters with unrelated meanings. No specific HSK level is confirmed.
