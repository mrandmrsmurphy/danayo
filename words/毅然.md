## Notes
- [毅](../characters/毅.md) "resolute, strong" + [然 (char)](../characters/然%20(char).md) "so, thus"
- 毅然 is the stand-in word for [毅](../characters/毅.md); 毅 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse.
- This word is a genuine Dan'a'yo homophone with [[依然]] ("as ... as ever, still") — both read 의년/ㄧㄜㄋ⼶ㄋ, distinct characters.
