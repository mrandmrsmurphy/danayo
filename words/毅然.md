## Notes
- [毅](../characters/毅.md) "resolute, strong" + [然 (char)](../characters/然%20(char).md) "so, thus" — 毅然 is the stand-in word for [毅](../characters/毅.md); 毅 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse.

毅然 means resolutely: firmly and without hesitation. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 毅然 (Mandarin yìrán, Cantonese ngai6 jin4) as "firmly; resolutely; without hesitation", and Japanese きぜん as "resolutely, firmly" (being resolute in spite of what was being said) and, as a tari-adjective, "resolute, firm, undaunted". It has no Korean or Vietnamese entry, so Korean 의연 and Vietnamese nghị nhiên here are built from the characters.

然 is the common adverb-forming ending, "-ly", attached to 毅, resolute. The word sounds the same in Dan'a'yo as [[依然]], "as ... as ever, still", an unrelated word, and 毅然 is the stand-in word for [毅](../characters/毅.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
