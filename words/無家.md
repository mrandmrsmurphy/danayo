## Notes
- [無](../characters/無%20(char).md) "lack" + [家](../characters/家.md) "house, family" — no stand-in relationship applies (無's own stand-in is itself; 家's own is [[家庭]]). The full four-character idiom 無家可歸 ("homeless, no home to return to") is listed as an alias.

無家 means homeless.
