## Notes
- [無](characters/無%20(char).md) "lack" + [家](characters/家%20(char).md) "house, family" — no stand-in relationship applies (無's own stand-in is itself; 家's own is [[家庭]]). The full four-character idiom 無家可歸 ("homeless, no home to return to") is listed as an alias.

無家 means homeless.

Fixed a serious bug: `mandarin` (wú jiā kě guī), `korean` (무가, 집없는), and `japanese` (家のない) had all been contaminated with the pronunciation/phrase of the longer four-character alias 無家可歸 rather than this word's own two-syllable form (matching 注音 ㄇㄜㄍㄚ). Corrected to the fully compositional two-morpheme readings: mandarin wújiā, cantonese mou4 gaa1 (blank filled), japanese むか (MU+KA), korean 무가, all matching 無's and 家's own stored citations exactly. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with vô gia (compositional; cf. the common derived term vô gia cư "homelessness"). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`; added missing `kwin: false` (諺文 므가 does not match korean 무가). No homophones (注音 ㄇㄜㄍㄚ unique across both words and characters).
