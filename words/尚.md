## Notes
- [尚 (char)](../characters/尚%20(char).md) "still; even more" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [尚 (char)](../characters/尚%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

尚 is an adverb meaning "still, yet, even more" — marking a state or degree that persists or exceeds expectation (尚早 "still too early," 尚且 "still, moreover"). It extends secondarily to "to esteem, to value highly" (尚武 "to esteem martial valor").

Japanese しょう, Korean 상, and Vietnamese thượng are all standard readings — Vietnamese thượng in particular is shared with the unrelated but identically-pronounced 上 ("above/upper"), a real Sino-Vietnamese homophone collision paralleling the Dan'a'yo-level one documented below. **A genuine triple Dan'a'yo homophone**: 尚, [[上]], and [[賞]] all share the identical reading syang/샹/ㄙ⼘ㄫ — added reciprocal homophone callouts to all three word pages.
