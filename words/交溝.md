## Notes
- [交](../characters/交%20(char).md) "to exchange, mix" + [溝 (char)](../characters/溝%20(char).md) "ditch, channel" — a euphemistic/anatomical compound for sexual intercourse.

交溝 is a formal/literary term for sexual intercourse, alongside its own alias 交媾. Vietnamese giao cấu (also written giao cấu) is the standard Vietnamese legal/medical term for the same act (as in hành vi giao cấu, "the act of sexual intercourse"), distinct from a naive compositional reading of 溝's own câu.

**Real content bugs found and fixed**: `characters:` had bare undisambiguated `溝` despite `溝 (char)` (and a conflicting `words/溝.md`) existing — fixed. `cantonese` was doubly garbled (gaa1gau3, missing a vowel in the first syllable and using the wrong initial/tone in the second) — corrected to gaau1 kau1. `japanese` was stored in obsolete historical kana orthography with a wrong reading entirely (かうこう) — corrected to こうこう (compositional, both characters' own KOU on'yomi). Fixed vietnamese capitalization and normalized `aliases` to block-list format.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 굣곳 diverges from Sino-Korean 교구, since both 交 and 溝 independently carry `kwin: false` on their own character pages.
