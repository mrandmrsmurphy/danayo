## Notes
- [偶](../characters/偶.md) "pair, mate; accidental" + [像](../characters/像.md) "image, statue, resemblance" — "idol, statue" (a carved or molded image, especially of a deity, and by extension a figure of worship or admiration — 偶像崇拜, "idol worship"; 偶像明星, "idol [celebrity]"). Mandarin ǒuxiàng, Cantonese ngau5 zoeng6, Japanese ぐうぞう, Korean 우상, and Vietnamese ngẫu tượng are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 옷샹 diverges from Sino-Korean 우상 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄛㄨㄙ⼘ㄫ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 偶's own `stand_in` is this exact word (偶像), so 偶 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 偶像.
