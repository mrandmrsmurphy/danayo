## Notes
- [陥 (char)](../characters/陥%20(char).md) "submerge" — this word is 陥's own stand-in.

陥 means to fall into, sink into, or submerge — extended to "to cave in, collapse" and, figuratively, "to be caught/trapped" (as in a pitfall). Mandarin 陷/陥 (xiàn), Cantonese 陷 (haam6), Korean 함 (ham), and Sino-Vietnamese hãm all share this range of senses, appearing in compounds like 缺陥/缺陷 ("defect, flaw," literally "a gap one falls into") and 陥没/陷没 ("cave-in, subsidence"). Japanese 陥 is bound almost exclusively in compounds (陥没 kanbotsu, "cave-in"; 欠陥 kekkan, "defect") — the native verb 陥る (ochiiru, "to fall/sink into") carries the standalone everyday sense instead.

Exact syllable check: 陥 shares its Dan'a'yo reading (ham/함/ㄏㄚㄇ) with six other characters (艦, 憾, 含, 喊, 函, 咸), but none of them stand alone as their own word — each requires its own multi-syllable compound — so there is no genuine word-level homophone to callout.
