## Notes
- [氏](../characters/氏.md) "clan" + [族](../characters/族.md) "tribe, clan" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 氏 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (族's own stand-in is [[家族]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

氏族 means clan.

Mandarin shìzú, Cantonese si6 zuk6, Japanese しぞく, and Korean 씨족 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 氏's and 族's own stored citations exactly). Vietnamese thị tộc (氏's own thị + 族's own tộc, the primary form among 族's multi-value list) is likewise the real, standard Vietnamese term for "clan."
