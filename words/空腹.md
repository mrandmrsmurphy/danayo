## Notes
- [空 (char)](../characters/空%20(char).md) "empty" + [腹 (char)](../characters/腹%20(char).md) "belly"

空腹 is an empty stomach: the state of having eaten nothing, hunger. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin kōngfù as "to be on an empty stomach; to fast" and Japanese くうふく as "hungry; hunger". Cantonese hung1 fuk1 matches, and Korean 공복 is the reading of the characters.

It is mild hunger, less than the [飢餓](飢餓.md), starving, and it is what [食欲](食欲.md), appetite, answers.
