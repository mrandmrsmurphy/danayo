## Notes
- [強 (char)](../characters/強%20(char).md) "strong; powerful" + [盗](../characters/盗.md) "burgle; steal"

強盗 is robbery: taking property by force or threat. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin qiángdào as "robber; to rob", and lists Korean 강도 and Vietnamese cường đạo ("robber, bandit, highwayman") as the matching forms. Japanese ごうとう is the same compound.

In Dan'a'yo 強盗 is the crime of taking by force, and by extension the robber. It differs from [窃盗](窃盗.md), theft without confrontation, and from [盗賊](盗賊.md), the armed bandit or brigand, who is the type of person rather than the single offence. Force is what separates it from the pickpocket's [掏模](掏模.md).
