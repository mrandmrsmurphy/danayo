## Notes
- [裁](../characters/裁.md) "tailor, sew" (here in its separate, unrelated sense "to cut; to judge, adjudicate") + [判](../characters/判.md) "judge, decide, determine" + [所 (char)](../characters/所%20(char).md) "-ee, place" — literally "trial-place," none of the three characters' own `stand_in` points here (裁's own is [[裁縫]]; 判's own is [[判断]]; 所's own is itself), so 裁判所 is an independent compound.

裁判所 is Dan'a'yo's word for "courthouse" — the physical building or institutional seat of a court — as distinct from [[法庭]], the general word for "court, courtroom" as a legal proceeding/concept. It decomposes as 裁判 ("trial, judgment, verdict" — a real, independently attested compound across the sphere, though not yet its own Dan'a'yo word) plus 所 ("place"). Japanese さいばんしょ (saibansho) is the real, standard modern Japanese word for a courthouse; Korean 재판소 (jaepanso) is real too, though narrower in living use than the more general 법원 — most visible today in official institution names like 헌법재판소 ("Constitutional Court"). Mandarin cáipànsuǒ and Cantonese coi4 pun3 so2 are compositional; modern Mandarin more commonly says 法院 for this concept instead.

**Flagged, not fixed here**: 所's contribution (ㄙㄜ/sǝ/스, matching `characters/所 (char).md`'s own frontmatter, `syllables/ㄙㄜ.md`'s own stand-alone listing of it, and its own stated Middle Chinese derivation) was cross-checked against the already-established sibling words [[事務所]], [[居所]], and [[厠所]] — all three end in ㄙㄜ/sǝ/스, confirming this is the correct reading for 所 in this "-place" sense. `words/所.md` itself, however, currently stores a different reading entirely (syo/쇼/ㄙ⼄, matching an unrelated syllable, `syllables/ㄙ⼄.md`, whose own character list doesn't include 所 at all) — a real, pre-existing bug on that word's own page, out of scope to fix during this word's own creation, but worth a dedicated look.

Vietnamese tài phán sở is compositional; tài phán itself is a real, independently attested Hán Việt term meaning "jurisdiction, adjudication" (as in cơ quan tài phán, "a judicial/adjudicating body"), but the full three-syllable compound is not independently confirmed as a standard term — living Vietnamese uses the native tòa án for the actual building, the same word it uses for [[法庭]] above.
