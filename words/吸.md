## Notes
- [吸 (char)](../characters/吸%20(char).md) "suck; inhale"

吸 combines [[口]] ("mouth") with phonetic [[及]]. It means "to inhale, to suck in, to absorb" (吸氧, "to breathe oxygen"; 吸水, "to absorb water") and, by extension, "to attract" (磁石能吸鐵, "a magnet can attract iron"). Paired with 呼 ("to exhale"), it forms [[呼吸]] ("to breathe; respiration").

Mandarin xī, Cantonese kap1, and Japanese きゅう (kyū, on'yomi, fully productive: 呼吸 こきゅう "breathing") alongside native す(う) (su(u), "to inhale, suck," the everyday verb) all preserve the sense. Korean 흡 (heup) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 마시다 (masida, "to drink, inhale"). Vietnamese hấp is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in hô hấp (呼吸, "respiration") and hấp thu (吸收, "to absorb"); the character page's other seven stored candidates (cạp, cặp, cộp, gạp, húp, hút, hớp) are unrelated and look like corpus noise.
