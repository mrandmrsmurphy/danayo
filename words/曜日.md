## Notes
- [曜](../characters/曜.md) "day of the week" + [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day, sun" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [曜](../characters/曜.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 日's own stand-in is itself.

曜日 means day of the week.

Mandarin yàorì, Japanese ようび, and Korean 요일 are all standard, fully compositional readings.
