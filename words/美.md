## Notes
- [美](../characters/美%20(char).md) "beautiful, pretty" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 美 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

美 means beautiful or pretty, and productively names "America" as an abbreviation (美国, 美洲), the standard transliteration character for the "-mer-" syllable in Měiguó/America.

Mandarin měi, Cantonese mei5, and Korean 미 (mi) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading うつくしい (utsukushii, "beautiful") rather than the on-readings ビ/ミ (BI/MI) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese mĩ is one of several stored candidates. No Dan'a'yo homophones (no other character shares this syllable).
