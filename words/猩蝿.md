## Notes
- [猩](../characters/猩.md) "red ape; vivid red" + [蝿](../characters/蝿.md) "fly" — fruit fly; *Drosophila* spp.; the red-eyed fly

Wiktionary has no entry for 猩蝿; it is a Dan'a'yo coinage on 猩 and 蝿 "fly", the fruit fly (*Drosophila*), so it is not a word attested in the Chinese or Korean dictionaries. The `japanese` field gives the real Japanese name ショウジョウバエ. 蝿 is the Japanese form of 蠅 "fly", and 猩蠅 and 猩蝇 are kept as aliases. Wikipedia: *Drosophila melanogaster* is a species of fly (family Drosophilidae), often called the fruit fly, widely used as a model organism in genetics and physiology research. 猩's own `stand_in` is 猩猩, so this word is not its legitimizer.
