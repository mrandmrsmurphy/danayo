## Notes
- [一 (char)](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one, unity, whole" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day, sun" — no `#cranberry`: both 一's and 日's own `stand_in` point to themselves, neither to this word.

一日 covers two related senses: "the first day of the month" (calendrical) and, more generally, "one day" (a single day's duration). Mandarin yīrì, Cantonese jat1 jat6, and Korean 일일 cover both senses without a split. Japanese, however, uses two genuinely distinct words: ついたち (tsuitachi, a native reading used specifically for "the first day of the month," the sense used in the field here) versus いちにち (ichinichi, the fully on'yomi compositional reading, used for "one day" as a duration) — **fixed a comma-joined contamination bug** where both had been jammed into a single `japanese` value. Fixed a stray trailing "l" typo in `mandarin` (was `yīrìl`). `kwin` is false (AND-rule: both 一 and 日 individually false).
