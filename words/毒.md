## Notes
- [毒](characters/毒%20(char).md) "poisonous" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 毒 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

毒 means poisonous.

Mandarin dú, Cantonese duk6, Japanese どく, Korean 독, and Vietnamese độc (the primary Sino form among the character's own multi-value vietnamese list — 毒's other value, nọc, belongs to the distinct "venom" sense as in 毒液) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed literal `vietnamese: null`, added the missing `pos`/`japanese`/`hsk_level` fields, `kwin: true` (諺文 독 matches Korean 독 exactly). No word-level homophones — several bound characters ([[篤]], [[独]], [[督]], [[涜]], [[読]]) share this same 注音 (ㄉㄛㄎ) but have no independent word pages of their own.
