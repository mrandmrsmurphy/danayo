## Notes
- [鉄 (char)](../characters/鉄%20(char).md) "iron" + [柱 (char)](../characters/柱%20(char).md) "pillar"

**鉄柱** is an ordinary word for an iron pillar or post. As a calligraphy term it is the figurative name of the third of the eight principles of the 永字八法, the vertical stroke ([[豎]], classically 努): the straight downward stroke is imagined as a rigid iron column holding up the character. The same stroke also has the variant name 鐵杵 ("iron pestle").

Li Puguang (李溥光), writing in the Yuan dynasty in his 永字八法解, gave each of the eight strokes of the 永字八法 a figurative two-character name; 鉄柱 is the vertical's name. See [[怪石]] for the full set, and the [[Stroke]] page.

The Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and North Korean forms are the ordinary compound readings of the word. No Vietnamese form is given.
