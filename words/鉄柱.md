## Notes
- [鉄 (char)](../characters/鉄%20(char).md) "iron" + [柱 (char)](../characters/柱%20(char).md) "pillar"

Wiktionary has no entry for 鉄柱 (traditional 鐵柱). Wikipedia (Eight Principles of Yong) records 鐵柱 (tiězhù, "iron pillar") as Li Puguang's metaphorical name, from his Yuan-era 永字八法解, for the third principle, the vertical stroke (豎, classically 弩 or 努), with 鐵杵 ("iron staff") as a further name marked citation needed. See [[豎]], [[怪石]] and the [[Stroke]] page. Its readings are compositional.
