## Notes
- [包](../characters/包.md) "wrap, pack" + [括](../characters/括.md) "embrace, include" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [括](../characters/括.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (包's own stand-in is [[包装]]).

包括 means to include or encompass — covering everything within a category or scope, comprehensively.

Japanese ほうかつ, Korean 포괄, and Vietnamese bao quát are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄅ⼘ㄨㄍ⺢ㄊ unique across both words and characters).
