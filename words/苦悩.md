## Notes
- [苦 (char)](../characters/苦%20(char).md) "suffering, bitter" + [悩 (char)](../characters/悩%20(char).md) "anguish, troubled mind" — inner torment; mental anguish; existential suffering

Wiktionary: Japanese 苦悩 (くのう) "suffering, distress, anguish, agony" and, as a verb, "to suffer, to be in agony or distress, to agonize over something"; Chinese 苦惱 "annoyed; distressed; ailed", Buddhism "suffering; vexation", and "to ail; to annoy; to distress", kept as the traditional-form alias. Compare [[苦悶]] and [[苦難]].
