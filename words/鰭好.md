## Notes
- [鰭](../characters/鰭.md) "fin" + [好 (char)](../characters/好%20(char).md) "like; fond of"

鰭好 is a taste: a thing one is fond of and indulges in, such as a hobby, a drink or a habit. The usual spelling is 嗜好, which is an alias here, because 嗜 is an alias of 鰭. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin shìhào as "special interest; hobby; indulgence; addiction" and Japanese しこう as "taste; preference". Korean 기호 and Cantonese si3 hou3 match.

It is the pleasure one returns to, a mild form of [欲望](欲望.md), desire, and at its worst the hold of [執着](執着.md), clinging. A strong pull toward one thing is the [耽](耽.md), indulgence.
