## Notes
- [双 (char)](characters/双%20(char).md) "double" + [鷹 (char)](characters/鷹%20(char).md) "eagle" + [国](characters/国.md) "country" — a Dan'a'yo-original symbolic name for Austria, based on the double-headed eagle of the historical Habsburg/Austrian Imperial Standard.

**Content corrected**: `mandarin`/`cantonese` were previously `Shuāngyīngguó`/`soeng1 jing1 gwok3` — a literal reading of this word's own invented Dan'a'yo characters, not the real name any Chinese speaker actually uses for Austria. Investigated rather than assumed correct: confirmed the "double-eagle" name has no real attestation in Chinese-language sources at all (Austria's own modern coat of arms is a single-headed eagle; the double-headed eagle motif is at least as strongly associated with Russia/Byzantium in Chinese sources as with Austria), and corrected to `Àodìlì`/`ou3 dei6 lei6` — the real, standard Mandarin/Cantonese phonetic-transliteration name for Austria. `japanese`/`korean`/`vietnamese` were entirely blank; filled with each language's own real name for the country (オーストリア, 오스트리아, Áo) rather than a reading of the Dan'a'yo characters, consistent with how country-name words are handled elsewhere in this vault (e.g. [[瑞士]]).
