## Notes
- [太](../characters/太%20(char).md) "extreme" + [極](../characters/極%20(char).md) "utmost" + [拳](../characters/拳.md) "fist" — no stand-in relationship applies (太's own stand-in is itself; 極's is [[極]]; 拳's is [[拳骨]]).

太極拳 means Tai Chi (Taijiquan) — literally "Supreme Ultimate fist," the internal Chinese martial art and exercise system based on the concept of [[太極]].

Mandarin tàijíquán, Cantonese taai3 gik6 kyun4, Korean 태극권, Japanese たいきょくけん, and Vietnamese Thái Cực quyền are all standard, fully compositional readings (matching the sibling martial-art naming pattern in [[詠春拳]]'s "Vịnh Xuân quyền"). **Real bugs found and fixed**: `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` all had 極's syllable corrupted (기/gid/ㄍㄧㄊ, with the coda consonant wrongly swapped) — corrected to 긱/gig/ㄍㄧㄎ, matching 極's own citation and its sibling [[太極]]. `pos` was wrongly 固有名詞 — corrected to 名詞, matching [[跆拳道]]/[[詠春拳]]. Removed the redundant self-referential alias (太極拳 aliasing itself) and blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`; kept simplified 太极拳. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄚㄧㄍㄧㄎㄍ⼔ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
