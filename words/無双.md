## Notes
- [無](characters/無%20(char).md) "lack" + [双](characters/双%20(char).md) "pair, double" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 無's and 双's own stand-ins are themselves).

無双 means matchless, peerless, unparalleled, unrivalled ("without pair/equal").

Mandarin wúshuāng, Cantonese mou4 soeng1, Japanese むそう (MU+SOU), Korean 무쌍, and Vietnamese vô song are all fully compositional, matching 無's and 双's own stored citations exactly (song is one of 双's listed Vietnamese readings). Fixed a real bug in `pos`: 連接詞 ("conjunction") was wrong for this adjective-like word; corrected to 性詞, matching the convention already established on sibling 無-compounds ([[無効]], [[無常]], [[無恥]], [[無援]], [[無人]]). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄜㄙ⺢ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
