## Notes
- [翌 (char)](../characters/翌%20(char).md) "the following" + [週](../characters/週.md) "week" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (翌's is [[翌]] itself, 週's is [[週日]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 週 is individually `kwin: false`.

翌週 means the following week.

Mandarin yìzhōu, Cantonese jik6 zau1, Japanese よくしゅう (yokushū), and Korean 익주 (ikju) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 翌週 is itself a common attested word for "the following week" in all four languages. Vietnamese dực chu is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
