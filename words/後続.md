## Notes
- [後 (char)](../characters/後%20(char).md) "after" + [続](../characters/続.md) "to continue, to follow on" — no stand-in relationship applies (後 is its own stand-in; 続's is [[継続]]).

後続 means "following, subsequent": the next thing in a sequence, or what comes after something else. As a modifier (性詞) it names whatever follows, 後続の, and it works for order in time, in a series, or in position. Dan'a'yo uses it for the last of these on the phonology page, where the **following glide** (後続滑音) is the glide that comes after the vowel, in the coda, and where 高母音後続 names the coda option of a high vowel following the nucleus.

It sits among the words of sequence, between [[後]] "after", the plain temporal word, and [[以後]] "from then on", which fixes a starting point. 後続 differs from both: it does not say *when* but *what follows*, the successor, as in a following glide, a following syllable, a following sentence. [[先後]] "one after another, in succession" names the ordering itself, where 後続 names the one that comes next.

Mandarin uses 後續 (hòuxù, with 續 for 続; simplified 后续) as an attributive word meaning "follow-up, ensuing, following", Cantonese hau6 zuk6, and Japanese 後続 (こうぞく) is a noun and verb meaning "following, succeeding". Korean 후속 is the compositional reading of the same characters and was not separately verified in a Korean dictionary; Wiktionary has no Korean or Vietnamese entry, so the `vietnamese` field is omitted. The traditional 後續 and the simplified 后续 are kept among the aliases. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄨㄛㄙ⼄ㄎ unique across both words and characters).
