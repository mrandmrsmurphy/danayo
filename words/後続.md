## Notes
- [後 (char)](../characters/後%20(char).md) "after" + [続](../characters/続.md) "to continue, to follow on" — no stand-in relationship applies (後 is its own stand-in; 続's is [[継続]]).

Wiktionary: Japanese 後続 (こうぞく) is "following, succeeding" and, as a verb, "to follow after, to succeed"; Chinese 後續 (hòuxù) is attributive, "follow-up; ensuing; following". Dan'a'yo uses it for the following glide (後続滑音) and 高母音後続 in `文法 - 02音韻論` and `文法 - 03文字法`. Related: [[後]], [[以後]], [[先後]].
