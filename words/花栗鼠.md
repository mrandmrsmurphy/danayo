## Notes
- [花](../characters/花.md) "flower" is separately legitimized by [[草花]]. [栗](../characters/栗%20(char).md) "chestnut" is separately legitimized by [[栗]] itself. [鼠](../characters/鼠.md) "rat" is separately legitimized by [[熊鼠]]. No cranberry: none of the three constituents' own `stand_in` points here.

花栗鼠 means chipmunk, literally "flower-chestnut-rat" (a striped rodent).

Pronunciation fields (hwalidsyo/화릳쇼/ㄏ⺢ㄌㄧㄊㄙ⼄) are the straightforward concatenation of each constituent's own stored reading — no bug. `kwin` is false per the AND-rule: 栗 and 鼠 both individually carry `kwin: false` (despite 花's own `kwin: true`). Fixed cantonese's stray spaces. Japanese シマリス (shimarisu, native しま "stripe" + リス "squirrel"), korean 다람쥐 (darangjwi), and vietnamese sóc chuột are all genuine, standard native terms rather than compositional constructions — not bugs.
