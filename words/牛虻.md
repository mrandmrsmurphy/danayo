## Notes
- [牛 (char)](../characters/牛%20(char).md) "cow" + [虻](../characters/虻.md) "horsefly; gadfly"
- 牛虻 is the stand-in word for [虻](../characters/虻.md) ("horsefly"), which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo; 牛's own stand-in is itself, so `#cranberry` does not apply.

Mandarin niúméng, Cantonese ngau4 mong4, Japanese ぎゅうぼう (GYUU+BOU), and Korean 우맹 were already correct and fully compositional. Filled the entirely missing `vietnamese` field with ngưu manh (牛's own ngưu + 虻's own manh). No homophones (注音 ㄋ⼜ㄇㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
