## Notes
- [指](../characters/指.md) "finger, toe" + [関](../characters/関%20(char).md) "close" + [節](../characters/節%20(char).md) "node, knuckle, joint, holiday" — no stand-in relationship applies (指's own stand-in is [[手指]]; both 関 and 節's own are themselves).

指関節 means knuckle or finger joint.

Mandarin zhǐguānjié, Cantonese zi2 gwaan1 zit3, Japanese しかんせつ, Korean 지관절, and Vietnamese chỉ quan tiết are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄜㄍ⺢ㄇㄐㄝㄊ unique across both words and characters).
