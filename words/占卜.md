## Notes
- [占](../characters/占%20(char).md) "divine" + [卜](../characters/卜.md) "divine, prophesy" — stand-in for [[卜]], which cannot appear independently

占卜 is a compound of two near-synonyms for divination — 占 in its "to divine, foretell" sense (distinct from its unrelated homographic "to occupy" sense) and 卜, a bound character whose own glyph depicts a crack in an oracle bone used for divination in ancient China. Together they name divination/fortune-telling generally, as in [[卜占]] (the same compound reversed) and 占卦 ("to divine by casting lots").

Mandarin zhānbǔ, Cantonese zim1 buk1, and Japanese せんぼく (senboku, on'yomi) all preserve the compound directly. Vietnamese chiêm bốc is the attested Hán Việt compound — chiêm here is 占's divination-sense reading (as in 占星 chiêm tinh, "astrology"), distinct from chiếm, the reading used for 占's unrelated "occupy" sense (already established on [[占]]'s own word page). Korean 점복 (jeombok) is the compositional Sino-Korean reading, a real attested term (점복술, "the art of divination").
