## Notes
- [毛 (char)](../characters/毛%20(char).md) "fur" + [蝦](../characters/蝦.md) "shrimp" — this compound is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 蝦 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (毛's own stand-in is itself, so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

毛蝦 means shrimp.

Mandarin máoxiā and Cantonese mou4 haa1 are fully compositional, matching 毛's and 蝦's own stored citations. Japanese エビ (ebi, matching 蝦's own native reading えび), Korean 새우, and Vietnamese tôm are the real, standard everyday words for "shrimp" in each language rather than Sino-compositional forms — the same real-world-species-name convention already established on the sibling [[龍蝦]] (lobster), where cross-linguistic biological vocabulary diverges too much for a shared compositional coinage to be natural. `kwin: false` reflects this same divergence (諺文 맛하 vs the wholly unrelated native Korean 새우). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/`aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄚㄨㄏㄚ unique across both words and characters).
