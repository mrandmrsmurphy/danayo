## Notes
- [白 (char)](../characters/白%20(char).md) "white" + [鳥 (char)](../characters/鳥%20(char).md) "bird" — literally "white bird."

The compound splits along a real semantic line. In Mandarin and Cantonese, 白鳥/白鳥 (báiniǎo/baak6 niu5) is a plain literal description — "a white bird" — rather than a fixed species name; the standard word for "swan" in these languages is 天鵝 (tiān'é, "heavenly goose"). In Japanese and Korean, by contrast, 白鳥 has narrowed into the specific, standard word for swan: はくちょう (hakuchō) and 백조 (baekjo) are both the ordinary, everyday terms for the bird, not merely descriptive. Vietnamese bạch điểu stays with the literal "white bird" sense; the everyday Vietnamese word for swan is the native thiên nga.
