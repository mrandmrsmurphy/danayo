## Notes
- [因](../characters/因.md) "cause" + [為 (char)](../characters/為%20(char).md) "for"

因為 is because, since. English Wiktionary has an entry only for Chinese 因為 (Mandarin yīnwèi, Cantonese jan1 wai6), glossed "because; since; as" and "owing to; due to; as a result of; because of". The Japanese いんい and Korean 인위 fields are built from the readings of the two characters. It is the clause-linking "because" that the note on [為](為.md) says was uncoined.
