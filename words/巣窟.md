## Notes
- [巣](../characters/巣.md) "nest" + [窟 (char)](../characters/窟%20(char).md) "cave; den" — no stand-in relationship applies (巣's own `stand_in` is [[鳥巣]], 窟's is itself).

巣窟 names a hideout or den — most often a criminal gang's hideout (盗賊の巣窟, "a den of thieves") or, more loosely, any disreputable hangout. Distinct from [[巣穴]] (巣+穴, an animal's physical burrow/hole) — a different compound, not a variant spelling of this one, despite the superficial similarity.

Japanese そうくつ, Korean 소굴, and Vietnamese sào quật (verified) are all standard readings. No HSK level is given for the compound. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄨㄎㄛㄊ unique across both words and characters).
