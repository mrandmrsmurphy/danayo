## Notes
- [波](../characters/波.md) "wave" + [及 (char)](../characters/及%20(char).md) "reach out to"

波及 means to spread and reach: to ripple out from a source and affect other things. The characters, 波, wave, and 及, to reach, give the image of waves arriving. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 波及 (Mandarin bōjí, Cantonese bo1 kap6) as "to spread to and affect; to spread and influence; to involve", Japanese はきゅう as "spread" and "to spread" (it spread throughout the world), and Korean 파급 as "spread; effect, influence". It has no Vietnamese entry, so the Vietnamese field is left blank.

Compare [[普及]], the welcome spread of something to everyone, where 波及 is the spread of an effect.
