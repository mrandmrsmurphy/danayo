## Notes
Mandarin guòqù, japanese かこ, korean 과거, and vietnamese quá khứ are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. **Found and fixed a missing tone number on `cantonese`** ("gwo heoi3" → "gwo3 heoi3", matching 過's own stored gwo3). **Found and fixed a real-equivalent-instead-of-own-reading bug on `korean`**: a garbled multi-value string "과거, 지난날" mashed the honest compositional reading (과거) together with an unrelated native Korean word for "the past" (지난날) — trimmed to just 과거. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 과쿄 diverges from Sino-Korean 과거 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⺢ㄎ⼄ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 過's `stand_in` (過 itself) nor 去's own `stand_in` (去 itself) points to this compound.
