## Notes
- [崇](../characters/崇.md) "lofty; noble" + [拝 (char)](../characters/拝%20(char).md) "bow; kowtow" — no stand-in relationship applies (崇's own `stand_in` is [[崇高]], 拝's is itself).

崇拝 means to worship or adore — 崇's "to hold in lofty regard" combined with 拝's physical gesture of bowing/prostration, together naming reverent devotion whether religious (神を崇拝する, "to worship a god") or secular (idol worship, hero worship).

Japanese すうはい, Korean 숭배, and Vietnamese sùng bái are all standard, non-drifted readings. HSK level 4 already correct. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄨㄫㄅ⼶ unique across both words and characters).
