## Notes
- [外](../characters/外.md) "outside" + [套 (char)](../characters/套%20(char).md) "cover"

外套 is a coat, an overcoat. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 外套 (Mandarin wàitào, Cantonese ngoi6 tou3) as "overcoat; coat; jacket", Japanese がいとう as "overcoat; greatcoat" and "mantle", and lists Korean 외투 as a hanja noun. The Vietnamese ngoại sáo field is built from the readings of the two characters. The general word for clothes is [衣服](衣服.md).
