## Notes
- [保](../characters/保.md) "protect; safeguard" + [釈](../characters/釈.md) "explain; interpret"

保釈 is release on bail: the temporary release of an accused person from custody on a guarantee, usually money, that they will return for trial. The character 釈 here has the sense "release", as in [釈放](釈放.md). Wiktionary glosses Mandarin bǎoshì as "to release on bail" and lists Korean 보석 as "bail"; Japanese ほしゃく is the same compound written 保釈. Cantonese bou2 sik1 matches.

Bail comes between arrest and verdict. A [被告](被告.md) who has been taken in by [逮捕](逮捕.md) can be released on bail, whereas [釈放](釈放.md) alone is release in general, and [軟禁](軟禁.md) is house arrest.
