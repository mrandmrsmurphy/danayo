## Notes
- [入 (char)](../characters/入%20(char).md) "enter" + [口 (char)](../characters/口%20(char).md) "mouth" — "entrance" (literally "the mouth one enters through"). Mandarin rùkǒu, Cantonese jap6 hau2, Japanese いりぐち, and Korean 입구 are all directly attested standard readings for "entrance." Vietnamese `nhập khẩu` is the same compositional compound (入's own nhập + 口's own khẩu), but it has semantically drifted in modern Vietnamese to specifically mean "import" (as in international trade, nhập khẩu hàng hóa, "to import goods") rather than physical "entrance" — the everyday Vietnamese word for entrance is lối vào or cửa vào. Kept here as the vault's own compositional derivation, with the register/sense divergence disclosed rather than silently avoided. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 닙콧 diverges from Sino-Korean 입구 in the second syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄧㄆㄎㄛㄨ unique among both words and characters).
