## Notes
- [廃](../characters/廃.md) "abandoned, discarded" + [物](../characters/物%20(char).md) "thing, object" — "discarded thing," waste. Their own `stand_in` values: 廃's is [[廃棄]]; 物's is itself; none points here, so 廃物 is an independent compound.

廃物 is waste: material that has been discarded or is of no further use, rubbish. Mandarin fèiwù (廢物) and Cantonese fai3 mat6 are the ordinary words, and Japanese はいぶつ and Korean 폐물 are the same compound. It is the thing, where [[廃棄]] is the act of discarding. Compare [[浪費]] (to waste).
