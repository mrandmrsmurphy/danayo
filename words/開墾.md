## Notes
- [開](../characters/開.md) "open" + [墾](../characters/墾.md) "cultivate" — stand-in for [[墾]], which cannot appear independently.

開墾 means "to cultivate, reclaim (land)" — opening up virgin or fallow land for farming.

Mandarin kāikěn and Cantonese hoi1 han2 preserve the sense directly. Japanese かいこん (kaikon) is standard vocabulary. Korean 개간 (gaegan) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese khai khẩn is the standard Hán Việt reading, equally common in everyday and administrative speech (khai khẩn đất hoang, "to reclaim wasteland").
