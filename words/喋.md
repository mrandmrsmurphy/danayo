## Notes
- [喋 (char)](../characters/喋%20(char).md) "chatter; babble"

喋 means "to chatter, babble on" (喋喋不休, "to chatter on endlessly, without stopping"). A separate, rarer sense means "bloodshed, to tread in blood" (喋血, "bloodshed, a bloody battle").

Mandarin dié and Cantonese dip6 preserve the sense directly. Japanese チョウ/トウ (CHOU/TOU, on'yomi, rare) alongside native しゃべ(る) (shabe(ru), "to chat, chatter," the everyday verb, conventionally written 喋る despite this character's otherwise name-use classification in this vault's own data) cover the sense. Korean 첩 (cheop) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese điệp is the Hán Việt reading, attested in điệp điệp (喋喋, "chattering on without stopping") and điệp huyết (喋血, "bloodshed"); it belongs to a large shared phonetic series with 牒, 蝶, 諜, 疊, 碟, all read điệp. The character page's other stored candidate, nhịp, is not a Hán Việt reading at all but a Nôm phonetic loan — 喋 is one of seven different glyphs historically used to write the wholly unrelated native Vietnamese word nhịp ("beat, rhythm, tempo," as in nhịp điệu) — excluded here.

This word shares its reading (deb/덥/ㄉㄝㄆ) with [[畳]] ("tatami mat"), still unperfected and awaiting its own turn in this sweep — the reciprocal half of this callout will be completed when it comes up.
