## Notes
- [次](../characters/次.md) "second, next" + [女 (char)](../characters/女%20(char).md) "female" — second daughter.

次女 is the second daughter. It is "next-female," the pair of [[次男]]. Mandarin cìnǚ is the listed synonym on the 次男 page and Japanese じじょ appears on the 長女 page; Korean 차녀 is the compositional reading, which was not confirmed. `cantonese` and `vietnamese` are left blank. The eldest daughter is [[長女]].
