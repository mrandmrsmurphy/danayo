## Notes
- [下 (char)](../characters/下%20(char).md) "down, below" + [降](../characters/降.md) "descend" — legitimizing note: 降's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, so 降 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

Mandarin xiàjiàng, Cantonese haa6 gong3, Japanese かこう, Korean 하강, and Sino-Vietnamese hạ giáng are all standard compositional readings.
