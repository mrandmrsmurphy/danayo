## Notes
- [老 (char)](../characters/老%20(char).md) "old person, age" + [師](../characters/師.md) "expert" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (老's is [[老]] itself, 師's is [[教師]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

老師 names a sage or elder monk — not a plain "teacher" (that sense is carried by [[教師]]), but a term of respect for an accomplished elder, especially in Buddhist or martial-arts contexts.

Mandarin lǎoshī, Cantonese lou5 si1, Japanese ろうし (rōshi, a Zen Buddhist honorific for a master teacher), and Korean 로사 (rosa) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese lão sư is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
