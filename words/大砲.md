## Notes
- [大](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big" + [砲](../characters/砲.md) "cannon" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [砲](../characters/砲.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (大's own stand-in is itself).

大砲 means a cannon or gun.

Filled blank `vietnamese: đại pháo` (compositional). Japanese たいほう and Korean 대포 are both standard readings. **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 大砲 and [[代表]] ("represent") share an identical reading — standardized the reciprocal callout on both pages.
