## Notes
- [種](../characters/種.md) "seed, kind" + [族](../characters/族.md) "clan, group" — no stand-in relationship applies (種's own stand-in is [[種類]]; 族's own is [[家族]]).

種族 means ethnicity or race — a group of people sharing common ancestry, culture, or physical characteristics.

Mandarin zhǒngzú, Cantonese zung2 zuk6, Japanese しゅぞく, Korean 종족, and Vietnamese chủng tộc are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄐㄛㄫㄐㄛㄎ/jongjog/종족) with the unrelated [[従属]] ("subordination") — note that this coincidence holds at the Dan'a'yo level, even though 従属's own real Korean reading (종속) differs from 種族's (종족).
