## Notes
- [彫](../characters/彫.md) "carve, sculpt" + [像](../characters/像.md) "statue" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [像](../characters/像.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (彫's own stand-in is [[彫刻]]).

彫像 means statue or image — a carved or sculpted representation.

Mandarin diāoxiàng, Cantonese diu1 zoeng6, Japanese ちょうぞう, Korean 조상, and Vietnamese bức tượng are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `諺文` had 彫's syllable missing its final coda (초→촛, matching 彫's own citation and the file's own correct 羅馬字/注音). Reformatted `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄛㄨㄙ⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
