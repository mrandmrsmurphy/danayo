## Notes
- [虎 (char)](../characters/虎%20(char).md) "tiger" + [牙](../characters/牙.md) "fang; tooth"

Wiktionary lists 虎牙 under Chinese: "tiger's tooth" and "protruding canine tooth of a person". Wikipedia (Eight Principles of Yong) records 虎牙 (hǔyá, "tiger's tooth") as Li Puguang's metaphorical name, from his Yuan-era 永字八法解, for the fifth principle, the rising stroke (提, also 挑, classically 策). See [[怪石]] and the [[Stroke]] page. The flick comes in a level and a slanting form ([[平提]], [[斜提]]). Its readings are compositional.
