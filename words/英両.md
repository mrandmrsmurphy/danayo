## Notes
- [英](../characters/英.md) "hero; English" + [両](../characters/両%20(char).md) "liang, traditional unit of weight" — "English liang," the ounce. Their own `stand_in` values: 英's is [[英雄]]; 両's is itself; none points here, so 英両 is an independent compound.

英両 is the ounce, about 28 grams, named "English liang" after the native [[両]] (a sixteenth of a traditional [[斤]], so the pair keeps the Western pound-and-ounce ratio of 16 to 1). Mandarin yīngliǎng is attested, though Wiktionary marks it dated or dialectal, and the Cantonese reading is the character reading. It is treated as a **neologism** on the [[英里]] template. Compare [[英斤]] (pound).
