## Notes
- [艦](../characters/艦.md) "warship" + [隊 (char)](../characters/隊%20(char).md) "troop, squad" — fleet.

艦隊 is a fleet: a naval force of warships under one command. It is "warship-squad." Mandarin jiànduì, Cantonese laam6 deoi6, Japanese かんたい and Korean 함대 are the same compound, and Vietnamese hạm đội is its Sino-Vietnamese reading. 艦 "warship" is a bound character here; for merchant ships the general term is [[船舶]].
