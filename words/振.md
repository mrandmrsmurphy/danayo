## Notes
- [振](../characters/振%20(char).md) "shake, excite, rouse" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [振](../characters/振%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

振 means to shake, excite, or rouse.

Mandarin zhèn, Cantonese zan3, Japanese しん, and Korean 진 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: literal `vietnamese: null`, corrected to chấn — the well-attested standard Sino-Vietnamese reading (as in 振動 chấn động), though notably absent from the character's own stored `vietnamese` list (a gap worth a future character-level check, not fixed here). Homophone of [[真]] "true" (both 注音 ㄐㄧㄋ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words; checked twelve other candidates sharing this reading (㐱/尽/塵/診/賑/陳/震/晋/津/娠/秦/進) and confirmed none has an independent word at this exact reading.
