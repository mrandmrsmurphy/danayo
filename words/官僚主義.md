## Notes
- **Real derivation bug found and fixed**: `羅馬字` was stored as `gwanlyouju'wi`, ending in `'wi` — but 義's own reading is `'ǝi` (schwa, not `w`), and the word's own `諺文` (관룟주읫) already correctly used the matching 읫 syllable. Corrected `羅馬字` to `gwanlyouju'ǝi`. **This `'wi`/`'ǝi` (위/읫) confusion recurs across several other 義-final words in the vault** ([[主義]], [[定義]], [[定義域]], [[社会主義]], [[大義]], [[正義]] all still show the same `'wi`/위 pattern despite 義's own character page using `'ǝi`/읫) — worth a dedicated check when each of those comes up in the sweep, rather than a one-off fix here.
- Added missing `kwin: false` (官 `true`, 僚 `false`, 主 `true`, 義 `true` — one `false` constituent makes the whole compound `false` under the AND-rule). Filled blank `vietnamese: chủ nghĩa quan liêu`, directly parallel to and consistent with [[官僚]]'s own well-attested `quan liêu` and [[主義]]'s `chủ nghĩa`. **Left `hsk_level` unset**: this is a New-HSK 7–9 (highest, most advanced) tier word with no clear equivalent on the vault's old 1–6 scale.
- The pejorative "red tape, bureaucratism" charge that [[官僚]]'s own Notes section describes as confined to *this* derived term in Japanese (くわんれうしゆぎ, kanryō-shugi) rather than bleeding into the base word — unlike Vietnamese quan liêu, which (per that same page) carries the negative sense even on its own. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⺢ㄋㄌ⼄ㄨㄐㄨㄜㄧ unique among both words and characters).
