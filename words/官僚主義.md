## Etymology
- [官](../characters/官.md) "official" + [僚](../characters/僚.md) "colleague, companion" + [主](../characters/主.md) "lord, master" + [義](../characters/義.md) "meaning, principle" — built by stacking [[官僚]] ("bureaucrat") + [[主義]] ("-ism")

## Notes
- `vietnamese: chủ nghĩa quan liêu` is directly parallel to and consistent with [[官僚]]'s own well-attested `quan liêu` and [[主義]]'s `chủ nghĩa`.
- The pejorative "red tape, bureaucratism" charge that [[官僚]]'s own Notes section describes as confined to *this* derived term in Japanese (くわんれうしゆぎ, kanryō-shugi) rather than bleeding into the base word — unlike Vietnamese quan liêu, which (per that same page) carries the negative sense even on its own.
