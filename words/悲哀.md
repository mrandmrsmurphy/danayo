## Notes
- [悲 (char)](../characters/悲%20(char).md) "sad" + [哀](../characters/哀.md) "grief"

悲哀 is sorrow: deep sadness, in particular the sadness felt at the lot of human life. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin bēi'āi as "sorrowful; sad" and Japanese ひあい as "sorrow, sadness". Korean 비애, Vietnamese bi ai and Cantonese bei1 oi1 match.

It is the quiet, lasting form of sadness, where [悲傷](悲傷.md) is the sad event. A louder grief is [悲嘆](悲嘆.md), and its reverse is the happiness of [幸福](幸福.md).
