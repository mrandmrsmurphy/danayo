## Notes
- [迎](../characters/迎.md) "welcome" + [接 (char)](../characters/接%20(char).md) "receive, contact" — no stand-in relationship applies (迎's own `stand_in` is [[歓迎]]; 接's own is itself).

迎接 means to greet, welcome, receive, or meet — a near-synonym of [[歓迎]] that adds the specific sense of going out to receive or meet an arriving visitor, rather than just extending a general welcome. Korean 영접 in particular carries a formal, sometimes ceremonial register (e.g. 예수를 영접하다, "to receive Christ," in religious usage).

Mandarin yíngjiē, Cantonese jing4 zip3, Japanese げいせつ, Korean 영접, and Vietnamese nghênh tiếp are all standard, fully compositional readings. `kwin` is false: 迎 is `kwin: true`, 接 is `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ⼶ㄫㄐㄛㄆ unique across both words and characters).
