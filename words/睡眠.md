## Notes
- [睡](../characters/睡.md) "drowse, sleep" + [眠](../characters/眠.md) "doze" — both 睡's own stand-in and 眠's own stand-in are this exact compound, so transitivity holds (A=B=AB); marked `#cranberry`. **Corrected a stale reading**: 眠's actual assigned syllable is ㄇㄝㄇ/멈/mem — deliberately shifted to dodge the crowded ㄇㄝㄋ slot already holding 麺 (per `Lookup/CC/finals/韻 先開.md`) — but this word's own `注音`/`羅馬字`/`諺文`, along with citations on both 睡's and 眠's own character pages and even the syllable master page's own example, had all been left with the older, pre-shift ㄇㄝㄋ ending; fixed everywhere.

睡眠 is the standard word for sleep, especially in medical, scientific, and formal registers (睡眠不足, "sleep deprivation"; 睡眠障害, "sleep disorder"). Mandarin shuìmián, Cantonese seoi6 min4, Japanese すいみん (suimin), and Korean 수면 (sumyeon) are all fully compositional and share this formal register — everyday speech in each language typically prefers a simpler native verb ("to sleep") over the compound noun. Vietnamese thụy miên is compositional; everyday Vietnamese uses the native ngủ/giấc ngủ.
