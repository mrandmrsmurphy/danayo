## Notes
- [交 (char)](../characters/交%20(char).md) "exchange" + [代](../characters/代.md) "replace; generation" + [制](../characters/制.md) "system; control" — shift work system.

交代制 is a shift-work system: an arrangement in which workers take their duty in turns, so that work continues across the hours. It is "exchange-replace-system." No English Wiktionary entry was found, so the readings are the compositional ones from the characters (Mandarin jiāodàizhì, Japanese こうたいせい, Korean 교대제); Japanese 交代 (こうたい) is confirmed there as "relief, shift replacement," which makes the compound plausible in all three, but its attestation is unverified. Compare [[交替]] (to take turns) and [[輪番]] (rotating duty). Like the other shift words, it belongs to the 番 family, a systematic schema that Dan'a'yo imposes for predictability rather than a claim about the ancient usage.
