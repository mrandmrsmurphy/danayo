## Notes
- [唯 (char)](../characters/唯%20(char).md) "only" + [一 (char)](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one"

唯一 is only, sole or unique: the single one there is. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin wéiyī as "only; sole; unique" and Japanese ゆいいつ as "uniqueness". Vietnamese duy nhất is the same word, and Korean 유일 and Cantonese wai4 jat1 match.

It counts a single member of a class (the only child, the sole survivor), where [独特](独特.md) says that a thing's character is unlike any other. A thing can be the only one without being strange.
