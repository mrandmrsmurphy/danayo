## Notes
- [史](../characters/史.md) "history; chronicle" + [学](../characters/学.md) "learning; study"

**史学** is the academic study of history — both the events and processes of the past themselves, and the methods, interpretive traditions, and institutional frameworks through which those events are recorded and understood. In East Asian university contexts, 史学 is the standard disciplinary name equivalent to "History" as a faculty or department: 史学科 (history department), 史学者 (historian).

The word is consistent across the CJKV sphere — しがく, 사학, sử học — and contrasts with [[歴史]] (the subject matter, "history" as a sequence of past events): 歴史 is the past, 史学 is the discipline that studies it. In Dan'a'yo this division is kept strict, as [[歴史]] itself notes. 史学 means the study of history as a field; "historiography" in the narrower sense (the study of how history is written) is not a separate word here, and should be rendered as 歴史 + 学 rather than overloading 史学. See also [[史学者]] for the person who practises it.

In Chinese, 史學 (*shǐxué*) is also the traditional label for a branch of scholarship with its own canon — the Standard Histories (正史) and the commentarial tradition on them — and in Japan 史学 is the usual name of the university department (史学科). Korean 사학 (*sahak*) is likewise the standard term; note that it is homophonous in Korean with 私学 ("private school") and 邪学, which context readily separates. Vietnamese *sử học* is the everyday word for the discipline, appearing in faculty names such as *khoa Sử*.
