## Etymology
- [車 (char)](../characters/車%20(char).md) "cart, car" + [庫](../characters/庫.md) "storehouse, warehouse"

## Notes
Mandarin chēkù, cantonese ce1 fu3, japanese しゃこ, and korean 차고 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese xa kho is the honest compositional reading (車's own xa + 庫's own kho), replacing what had been the everyday real-world phrase "nhà để ô tô, ga ra" ("car-house / garage [French loanword]") — a real-equivalent-instead-of-own-reading bug, now fixed. **Also removed a bad `aliases` entry**: `库车` is the real, unrelated place name Kùchē/Kuqa (a city in Xinjiang), coincidentally the reverse character order of 車庫, not a genuine spelling variant. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 촤코 diverges from Sino-Korean 차고 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄑ⺢ㄎㄛ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present (庫's own `stand_in` is 倉庫, not this word; 車's own `stand_in` is 車 itself, so `characters:` correctly disambiguates as "車 (char)" since [[車]] has its own dedicated word page).
