## Notes
- [医](../characters/医.md) "doctor, medicine" + [院](../characters/院.md) "institution, courtyard, public building" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (医's is 医生, 院's is 院落).
Mandarin yīyuàn and Cantonese ji1 jyun2 are the ordinary, general-purpose words for "hospital" (any size). Japanese いいん and Korean 의원, however, specifically denote a small private clinic or practice — legally distinguished from 病院/병원 ("hospital," 20+ inpatient beds). Vietnamese y viện is a real, if less common, Sino-Vietnamese word for "hospital"; the everyday word is bệnh viện, a calque of 病院.
