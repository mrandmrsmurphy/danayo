## Notes
- [米 (char)](../characters/米%20(char).md) "rice" + [粉 (char)](../characters/粉%20(char).md) "powder"

米粉 is rice flour: flour ground from rice, and in Chinese also thin rice noodles. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin mǐfěn as "rice vermicelli" and "rice flour", and Japanese べいふん as "rice flour". Korean 미분 and Cantonese mai5 fan2 match.

It is the [米](米.md), rice, ground, as [小麦粉](小麦粉.md) is wheat ground. The noodles made from it belong with [麺](麺.md).
