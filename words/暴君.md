## Notes
- [暴 (char)](../characters/暴%20(char).md) "violent" + [君 (char)](../characters/君%20(char).md) "lord"

暴君 is a tyrant, a despot. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 暴君 (Mandarin bàojūn, Cantonese bou6 gwan1) and Japanese ぼうくん as "despot; tyrant". The Korean 폭군 field is built from the readings of the two characters. Compare [暴政](暴政.md), tyranny.
