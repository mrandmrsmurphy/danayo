## Notes
- [無](../characters/無%20(char).md) "lack" + [援](../characters/援%20(char).md) "aid, assist" — no stand-in relationship applies (無's own stand-in is itself; 援's own is [[援手]]).

無援 means unsupported.

Mandarin wúyuán, Cantonese mou4 wun4, Japanese むえん (MU+EN), and Korean 무원 are all fully compositional, matching 無's and 援's own stored citations exactly.
