## Notes
- [製](../characters/製.md) "manufacture, produce" is separately legitimized by [[製作]]. [品 (char)](../characters/品%20(char).md) "article, item" is separately legitimized by [[品]] itself. No cranberry.

Mandarin zhìpǐn, japanese せいひん, and korean 제품 (all also the real common words for "product") confirmed standard and compositional. Vietnamese sản phẩm is the real, standard, non-compositional Vietnamese word for "product" — legitimate real-term substitution.
## Etymology
- [製](../characters/製.md) "manufacture, produce" + [品 (char)](../characters/品%20(char).md) "article, item"
