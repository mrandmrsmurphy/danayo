## Notes
- [投](../characters/投%20(char).md) "throw"

投 means to throw or cast, as in 投票 ("cast a vote") and 投資 ("throw in capital, invest").

Mandarin tóu, Cantonese tau4, Japanese とう, and Korean 투 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese đầu (as in 投資 đầu tư "invest," 投票 đầu phiếu "cast a ballot") is the Sino-Vietnamese reading; the character's other stored reading, nhầu, is an unrelated native Vietnamese word meaning "wrinkled, crumpled."
