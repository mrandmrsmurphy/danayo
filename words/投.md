## Notes
- [投](../characters/投%20(char).md) "throw" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [投](../characters/投%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

投 means to throw or cast, as in 投票 ("cast a vote") and 投資 ("throw in capital, invest").

Mandarin tóu, Cantonese tau4, Japanese とう, and Korean 투 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese đầu (as in 投資 đầu tư "invest," 投票 đầu phiếu "cast a ballot") is the correct Sino-Vietnamese reading, picked over the character's other stored reading nhầu, which is the unrelated native Vietnamese word for "wrinkled, crumpled" — the same kind of homophone-field contamination documented on this word's own homophone siblings below. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 돗 (dou, from Middle Chinese 定母 d + 侯韻 əu) diverges from Sino-Korean 투 — the vowel differs from the shared coda pattern of its homophones, but the coda -t survives in Dan'a'yo where Korean has none. Shares its Dan'a'yo reading with two other standalone words, [[斗]] ("peck; Dipper") and [[豆]] ("bean") — a three-way coincidental convergence, unrelated etymologically to any of them (already fully documented from both of their sides).
