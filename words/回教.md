## Notes
- [回](../characters/回%20(char).md) "turn, return" + [教](../characters/教.md) "teach, religion" — no stand-in relationship applies (回 is its own stand-in; 教's own is [[教授]]).

回教 means Islam — a historical Chinese/East Asian term for the religion (from 回回, referring to the Hui people).

**Real bug found and fixed**: `japanese` used the archaic pre-orthographic-reform kana spelling くわいけう instead of modern かいきょう. Korean 회교 and Vietnamese hồi giáo are both standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄛㄧㄍ⼄ㄨ unique across both words and characters).
