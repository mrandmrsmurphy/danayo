## Notes
- [湯 (char)](../characters/湯%20(char).md) "hot water" + [池 (char)](../characters/池%20(char).md) "pond, reservoir" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 湯's and 池's own stand-ins are themselves). Note: the normal Mandarin word for "moat" is 城壕, not this literary compound; 湯池 survives mainly in the fixed idiom 金城湯池 ("sure thing, impenetrable city").

湯池 means city moat (literary), or hot springs.

Mandarin tāngchí and Cantonese tong1 ci4 are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 湯's and 池's own stored citations.
