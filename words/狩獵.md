## Notes
- [狩](../characters/狩.md) "hunt" + [獵 (char)](../characters/獵%20(char).md) "hunt" — 狩獵 is the stand-in word for [狩](../characters/狩.md); 狩 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse.

狩獵 is a near-synonym doubling: both characters independently mean "to hunt," and the compound intensifies rather than shifts the sense — the general, formal word for hunting as an activity, whether for subsistence, sport, or wildlife management, rather than any one specific hunting method.

Japanese しゅりょう and Korean 수렵 are both standard compositional Sino-readings. Vietnamese thú liệp is likewise attested, though modern spoken Vietnamese more commonly uses the native săn bắn for "hunting" in everyday contexts. No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼜ㄌㄛㄆ unique across both words and characters).
