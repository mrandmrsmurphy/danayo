## Notes
- [狩](../characters/狩.md) "hunt" + [獵 (char)](../characters/獵%20(char).md) "hunt"

狩獵 means to hunt, and hunting. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 狩獵 (Mandarin shòuliè, Cantonese sau3 lip6) as the formal "to hunt (wild animals)", and Japanese しゅりょう (written 狩猟, alias here) as "hunting; to hunt". Korean 수렵 and Vietnamese thú liệp are the hanja readings of the same two characters. Both characters mean "hunt", so the compound is a doubling.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [狩](../characters/狩.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
