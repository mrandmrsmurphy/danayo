## Notes
- [故](../characters/故.md) "reason, old" + [障](../characters/障.md) "obstruct" — no stand-in relationship applies (故's own stand-in is [[緣故]]; 障's own is [[障碍]]).

故障 means a breakdown, malfunction, hitch, or bug — an obstruction or fault interrupting normal operation.

Mandarin gùzhàng, Cantonese gu3 zoeng3, Japanese こしょう, and Korean 고장 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄍㄛㄐㄚㄫ/gojang/고장) with the unrelated [[姑丈]] ("aunt's husband") — a coincidence that also holds at the real-Korean level (both read 고장), though the two words diverge in Mandarin/Cantonese/Japanese.
