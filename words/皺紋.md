## Notes
- [皺](../characters/皺.md) "wrinkles, creases" + [紋 (char)](../characters/紋%20(char).md) "mark, line, pattern" — 皺's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 紋's own is [[紋]] itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 皺 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

皺紋 names wrinkles or creases, most commonly of skin (age-related facial wrinkles) but also of fabric or any surface that has folded or crumpled. Mandarin zhòuwén, Cantonese zau3 man4, and Japanese しゅうもん (shūmon) are all compositional. `vietnamese: trứu văn` is likewise compositional, built from each character's own stored reading, though not independently attested as a standalone dictionary entry.

Korean 추문 is worth a note: spoken in isolation it is indistinguishable from the far more common unrelated word 추문 (醜聞, "scandal") — a coincidental homophone at the Korean-language level rather than a Dan'a'yo-internal one, disambiguated only by context or hanja.
