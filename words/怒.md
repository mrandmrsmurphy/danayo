## Notes
- [怒](../characters/怒%20(char).md) "angry" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

怒 means angry — a feeling of displeasure or rage, as in 怒気 ("anger") and 怒号 ("angry roar").

Mandarin nù, Cantonese nou6, Japanese ど, Korean 노, and Vietnamese nộ are all standard, fully compositional readings.
