## Notes
- [半 (char)](../characters/半%20(char).md) "half" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

半 means half. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 半 (Mandarin bàn, Cantonese bun3) as "half", "very little", "halfway, partially, semi-", and, informally in Hong Kong, "half past". It glosses Japanese はん as "half", "odd" (especially of dice), "half, semi-" and "and a half", and lists Korean 반 and Vietnamese bán as hanja and Sino-Vietnamese forms of "half". Words built on it include [半島](半島.md), peninsula, [半導体](半導体.md), semiconductor, [上半期](上半期.md), [下半期](下半期.md) and [夜半](夜半.md).

Three other words share this syllable in Dan'a'yo (ㄅㄚㄋ): [畔](畔.md), [盤](盤.md) and [般](般.md); the homophone callout cross-references them.
