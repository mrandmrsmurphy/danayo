## Notes
- [箸](../characters/箸%20(char).md) "chopsticks" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 箸 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

Mandarin zhù, Cantonese zyu6, and Korean 저 (jeo) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading はし (hashi, "chopsticks") rather than the on-readings チョ/チャク (CHO/CHAKU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese đũa is the standard reading and remains the everyday Vietnamese word for "chopsticks."
