## Notes
- [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" + [程](../characters/程.md) "journey, degree"

日程 means a schedule: the plan of what is to happen on which day, a daily schedule or agenda. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 日程 (Mandarin rìchéng, Cantonese jat6 cing4) as "daily schedule; agenda" (提上日程, "to put on the agenda"), and Japanese にってい as "schedule; the day's schedule" (日程が決まった, "now the schedule is set"). Korean 일정 is "(daily) schedule" and, attributively, "some, a certain". Vietnamese nhật trình is glossed only as "log", a different sense, so Vietnamese is not a witness for "schedule" here.

The two characters are 日, day, and 程, a course or stage.
