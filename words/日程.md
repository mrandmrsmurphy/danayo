## Notes
- [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" + [程](../characters/程.md) "journey, degree"

日程 names a schedule or itinerary broken down by day — the day-by-day plan for a trip, conference, or project, as opposed to a single fixed appointment time. 程 itself carries the sense of "a course, a stage of a journey, a measured degree," so 日程 literally reads "the day's course/stage" — the specific days' worth of a larger plan laid out in sequence.

Japanese reads にってい (nittei); NICHI+TEI assimilates to にっ, a common sokuon (っ) pattern before a following unvoiced consonant. Korean 일정 is the standard compositional Sino-Korean reading. Vietnamese nhật trình is attested, though everyday Vietnamese more often uses the native/mixed lịch trình or chương trình for the same concept. No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄧㄊㄉㄧㄫ unique across both words and characters).
