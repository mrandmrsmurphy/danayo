## Notes
- [異](../characters/異.md) "different" + [教](../characters/教.md) "religion" + [徒](../characters/徒.md) "follower" — "follower of another religion." Their own `stand_in` values: 異's is [[異常]]; 教's is [[教授]]; 徒's is [[信徒]]; none points here, so 異教徒 is an independent compound.

異教徒 is a person of another religion, a heathen or pagan. Mandarin yìjiàotú, Cantonese ji6 gaau3 tou4 and Japanese いきょうと are attested, and Korean 이교도 is the same compound. It is the outsider, where [[異端]] is the insider who departs from the doctrine. Compare [[信徒]] (believer) and [[偶像]] (idol).

- See [基督敎](../lexipedia/基督敎.md).
