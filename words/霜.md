## Notes
- [霜 (char)](../characters/霜%20(char).md) "frost"

霜 is 形声: semantic 雨 ("rain") + phonetic 相. It names frost — the frozen dew that forms on cold, clear nights — and by extension anything frost-like in appearance (糖霜, "powdered sugar, icing"). Figuratively, frost's association with the end of the growing season gives it a poetic connotation of hardship, severity, or the passage of time (風霜, "the hardships of life," literally "wind and frost").

Mandarin shuāng, Cantonese soeng1, and Japanese そう (sō, on'yomi, rare outside compounds) alongside native しも (shimo, the everyday word for "frost") all preserve the sense. Korean 상 (sang) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 서리 (seori, the everyday native word for "frost"). Vietnamese sương is the Hán Việt reading, the everyday word throughout Vietnamese (sương mù, "fog"; sương giá, "frost").
