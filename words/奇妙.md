## Etymology
- [奇](../characters/奇.md) "strange, odd" + [妙 (char)](../characters/妙%20(char).md) "mysterious, subtle, exquisite"

## Notes
- "Amazing, strange, wondrous" — something marvelously unusual. `vietnamese: kỳ diệu` is a directly attested, extremely common standard Vietnamese term with the same "amazing, marvelous" sense. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 귀묫 diverges from Sino-Korean 기묘 in both syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄨㄧㄇ⼄ㄨ unique among both words and characters).
