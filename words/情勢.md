## Notes
- [情](../characters/情.md) "feelings, emotion" + [勢](../characters/勢.md) "power, influence" — no stand-in relationship applies (情's own stand-in is [[感情]]; 勢's own is [[勢力]]).

情勢 means situation or circumstances — the current state of affairs.

Mandarin qíngshì, Cantonese cing4 sai3, Japanese じょうせい, Korean 정세, and Vietnamese tình thế are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄫㄙㄝ unique across both words and characters).
