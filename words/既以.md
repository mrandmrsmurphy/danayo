## Notes
- [既](../characters/既.md) "already" + [以](../characters/以%20(char).md) "by, using" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [既](../characters/既.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (以's own stand-in is itself).

既以 means already, or too late.

Mandarin jìyǐ, Cantonese gei3 ji5, and Korean 기이 (compositional) are standard. Fixed real bug: `korean` was 既's own native reading (이미, "already") instead of compositional 기이. Japanese すでに is likewise a native reading, but the standard/expected form for this common adverb. Vietnamese kí dĩ is compositional. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄜㄧ unique across both words and characters).
