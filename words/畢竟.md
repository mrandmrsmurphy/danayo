## Notes
- [畢](../characters/畢.md) "all in all" + [竟 (char)](../characters/竟%20(char).md) "finally"
- This word is the viable form of [畢](../characters/畢.md): the character's meaning (after all, all in all) is identical, but 畢 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 畢竟 is its `stand_in` compound.

畢竟 is an adverb meaning "after all, in the end, when all is said and done": it introduces the final outcome or the fact that remains true once everything else is taken into account. Mandarin bìjìng, Cantonese bat1 ging2, Japanese ひっきょう (hikkyō; "in the final analysis", often in written argument) and Korean 필경 (pilgyeong) are the same word. 畢 and 竟 both mean "to end, to come to an end", so the compound is "to the very end"; it is also common in Buddhist translations, where 畢竟空 means "ultimate emptiness".

Dan'a'yo-specific sense: the shared one. It is housed in Time as an ending-of-the-sequence word beside [[最後]]; 最後 says something is last in order, while 畢竟 says that this is how things finally stand.
