## Notes
- [冷 (char)](../characters/冷%20(char).md) "cool, cold" + [麺 (char)](../characters/麺%20(char).md) "noodles, flour" — "cold noodles" (a dish, most famously the Korean naengmyeon). Mandarin lěngmiàn, Cantonese laang5 min6, Japanese れいめん, and Korean 랭면 are all directly attested standard readings. `vietnamese: lãnh miến` is the honest compositional reading (冷's own lãnh + 麺's own miến); everyday Vietnamese instead uses the native/mixed phrase mì lạnh. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 랑먼 diverges from Sino-Korean 랭면 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄚㄫㄇㄝㄋ unique among both words and characters).
