## Notes
- [踵](../characters/踵.md) "heel" + [骨 (char)](../characters/骨%20(char).md) "bone" — 踵骨 is the stand-in for [踵](../characters/踵.md), which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo.

踵骨 (calcaneus) is the largest of the tarsal bones, forming the heel's bony prominence and bearing much of the body's weight during standing and walking. しょうこつ is the standard Japanese anatomical term (Wiktionary/dictionary-confirmed); modern Mandarin medical texts more often use 跟骨 (gēngǔ) for this same bone, though 踵骨/zhǒnggǔ is also attested and directly transparent from its characters.

Japanese しょうこつ is already the direct on'yomi compositional reading (踵 SHOU + 骨 KOTSU). Korean 종골 is likewise standard compositional Sino-Korean. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 종곧 diverges from Sino-Korean 종골 in 骨's syllable (곧 vs 골). **`vietnamese` intentionally left blank**: while 踵's own Sino-Vietnamese reading (chủng) and one of 骨's (cốt) would compositionally yield "chủng cốt," this string isn't attested as a real Vietnamese medical term — Vietnamese instead uses the native compound xương gót ("heel bone") for this concept. No specific HSK level applies to a term this specialized. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄛㄫㄍㄛㄊ unique across both words and characters).
