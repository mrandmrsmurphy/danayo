## Notes
- [踵](../characters/踵.md) "heel" + [骨 (char)](../characters/骨%20(char).md) "bone"

踵骨 is the heel bone, the calcaneus. English Wiktionary has an entry for Japanese 踵骨 (しょうこつ), glossed "a heelbone; a calcaneus", and glosses the Chinese anatomical term 跟骨 (Mandarin gēngǔ, Cantonese gan1 gwat1) as "calcaneus; heel bone". The Mandarin, Cantonese and Korean 종골 fields are built from the readings of the two characters.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [踵](../characters/踵.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
