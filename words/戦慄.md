## Notes
- [戦](../characters/戦.md) "war; tremble" + [慄 (char)](../characters/慄%20(char).md) "tremble"

戦慄 is a shudder: trembling from fear or horror, and so terror felt in the body. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin zhànlì as "to tremble; to shudder" and Japanese せんりつ as "a shudder". Korean 전율 and Cantonese zin3 leot6 match; the Korean field gives the North Korean form.

It is fear that has reached the body, stronger than [恐怖](恐怖.md), fear, and followed by [衝撃](衝撃.md), shock. The fearful look is [懍懍](懍懍.md).
