## Notes
- [刑](../characters/刑.md) "punishment; penalty" + [法 (char)](../characters/法%20(char).md) "rule; law"

刑法 is criminal law: the branch of law that defines crimes and sets their punishments, and the code that contains them. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xíngfǎ as "criminal law; criminal code; penal code", and Japanese けいほう as "criminal law". Mandarin also has a colloquial sense, "unlawful corporal punishment imposed on prisoners; torture", which Dan'a'yo does not take.

It pairs with [民法](民法.md), civil law. The offences it defines are those on this page, such as [殺人](殺人.md), [強盗](強盗.md) and [偽造](偽造.md), and it sets the penalties of [刑罰](刑罰.md).
