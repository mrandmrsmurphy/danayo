## Notes
- [魔](../characters/魔.md) "demon, magic" + [鬼](../characters/鬼.md) "devil, demon" — legitimizing note: 魔's own `stand_in` is [[魔鬼]] itself, already annotated in its own citation; 鬼's own `stand_in` is [[鬼神]], so transitivity fails — no `#cranberry`.

Mandarin móguǐ, Cantonese mo1gwai2, Japanese まき, Korean 마귀 (also the everyday Korean word for "devil, evil spirit"), and Sino-Vietnamese ma quỉ (a common, everyday term) are all standard, fully compositional readings.
