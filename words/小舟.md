## Notes
- [小 (char)](../characters/小%20(char).md) "small" + [舟](../characters/舟.md) "boat" — this word is the stand-in for [舟](../characters/舟.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

小舟 is a small boat — a dinghy, skiff, or small craft for rivers and lakes.

Japanese こぶね (a native kun'yomi reading; classical/seasonal poetry instead reads it おぶね), Korean 소주, and Vietnamese tiểu chu (all verified) are standard readings. **Korean 소주 happens to be a pure homophone of the famous drink 소주** ("soju," written with entirely different hanja 燒酒) — a coincidental real-world pun, not a Dan'a'yo-level or etymological connection. No homophones among other Dan'a'yo words (注音 ㄙㄛㄐㄨ unique).
