## Notes
- [高 (char)](../characters/高%20(char).md) "high" + [山 (char)](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain" — a high mountain, or the alpine/highland terrain generally. The same two characters also name the Japanese city of Takayama (高山, たかやま), a separate proper-noun reading not used here.

Mandarin gāoshān, Cantonese gou1saan1, Japanese こうざん, Korean 고산, and Sino-Vietnamese cao sơn are all standard, fully compositional readings — cao sơn is also attested in the classical idiom cao sơn lưu thủy ("high mountains, flowing waters," a metaphor for a deep, understanding friendship, from 高山流水).
