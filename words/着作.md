## Notes
- [着 (char)](../characters/着%20(char).md) "write; compose" + [作 (char)](../characters/作%20(char).md) "make"

着作 is a written work, or the writing of one: a book or the writings of an author, usually written 著作, where 著 is the variant of 着 used here. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin zhùzuò as "work; writings" and Japanese ちょさく as "writing of a book". Korean 저작 is the same word. Cantonese zyu3 zok3 matches.

The one who writes it is the [着者](着者.md), and the thing in the hand is a [書籍](書籍.md), book. The wider word for any made work is [作品](作品.md).
