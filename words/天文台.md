## Notes
- [天 (char)](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven; sky" + [文](../characters/文.md) "pattern; text" + [台 (char)](../characters/台%20(char).md) "platform"

天文台 is an observatory: a place from which the sky is watched. Wiktionary glosses Japanese てんもんだい as "observatory (place where celestial bodies are observed)". The Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the characters, and Korean 천문대 matches.

In China the court kept one to watch the sky and keep the calendar, and the officials observed the [星宿](星宿.md) and recorded the [客星](客星.md). Its instrument is the [望遠鏡](望遠鏡.md), and its field is [天文](天文.md).
