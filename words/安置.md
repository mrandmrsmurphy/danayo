## Etymology

## Notes
- A verb, "to find a place for, help settle down, arrange placement" — used for lodging people (refugees, evacuees, staff) or objects in an appropriate spot. Filled blank `pos: 動詞` and corrected `hsk_level` from an unquoted `4` to the real official level, `"6"` (verified via search). Filled blank `vietnamese: an trí`, a directly attested Sino-Vietnamese term, though now largely confined to Buddhist/classical registers rather than everyday speech (everyday Vietnamese prefers sắp xếp/bố trí for this sense).
