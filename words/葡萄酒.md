## Notes
- [葡](../characters/葡.md) "grape (first half)" + [萄](../characters/萄.md) "grape (second half)" + [酒](../characters/酒.md) "alcohol" — "grape wine"; 葡萄 is a two-character word for the grape. Their own `stand_in` values: 葡's is [[葡萄]]; 萄's is [[葡萄]]; 酒's is [[酒精]]; none points here, so 葡萄酒 is an independent compound.

葡萄酒 is wine, made from grapes. Mandarin pútáojiǔ, Cantonese pou4 tou4 zau2, Japanese ぶどうしゅ and Korean 포도주 all use it. Rice wine and spirits are other words; compare [[醇酒]] (fine wine) and [[酒]] (alcohol in general).
