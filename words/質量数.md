## Notes
- [質](../characters/質.md) "essence, nature" + [量](../characters/量.md) "measure, quantity" + [数](../characters/数.md) "count, number" — mass number.

質量数 is the mass number of an atom: the total count of protons ([[陽子]]) and neutrons ([[中性子]]) in its nucleus ([[核]]), a whole number such as 12 for carbon-12 or 238 for uranium-238. It is written beside an element's abbreviation to name an isotope ([[同位元素]]). It differs from [[原子量]], which is an average and not a whole number. Mandarin zhìliàngshù, Cantonese zat1 loeng6 sou3, Japanese しつりょうすう and Korean 질량수 are the standard readings.
