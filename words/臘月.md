## Notes
- [臘](../characters/臘.md) is separately legitimized by [[臘八]]. [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) is separately legitimized by [[月]] itself. No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

Twelfth and final month of the ancient Chinese poetic calendar. 臘 refers to the ancient year-end sacrifice offered to the gods and ancestors. The 臘八 Festival (8th day) is marked by 臘八粥 (Laba congee), a thick porridge of grains and dried fruit. After 臘月 comes the New Year — this is the month of completion, offering, and preparation. Also sometimes called [[氷月]] "ice month."

Pronunciation fields (lab'wed/랍웓/ㄌㄚㄆ·⼔ㄊ) are the straightforward concatenation of each constituent's own stored reading — no bug. `kwin: false` is already correct per the AND-rule: 月 individually carries `kwin: false` (despite 臘's own `kwin: true`).

**Found and fixed a real bug**: `korean` had 납월, a 두음법칙-shifted (South Korean) form of 랍's real reading — corrected to 랍월, matching the vault's standing rule that `korean` always uses the unshifted North Korean/문화어 pronunciation. Mandarin làyuè, cantonese laap6jyut6 (space removed), and japanese ろうげつ (rougetsu) were all already correct, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese tháng Chạp is the genuine, extremely common native term for the 12th lunar month (Chạp itself historically descends from 臘's own reading, though the field isn't a direct concatenation of each constituent's own stored value). Removed a redundant `品詞` duplicate of `pos`.
