## Notes
- [垂](../characters/垂.md) "hang; dangle" + [露 (char)](../characters/露%20(char).md) "dew" + [豎](../characters/豎.md) "vertical line"

Wiktionary has no entry for 垂露豎; it is a compound of 垂 "hang", 露 "dew" and [[豎]], which Wiktionary glosses in Chinese calligraphy as "a vertical stroke (㇑)". It names the dewdrop vertical ("hanging-dew"), a kind of vertical stroke in regular script, the rounded counterpart of [[懸針豎]]. It is the second sub-variety listed under the vertical stroke on the [[Stroke]] page. Its readings are compositional.
