## Notes
- [垂](../characters/垂.md) "hang; dangle" + [露 (char)](../characters/露%20(char).md) "dew" + [豎](../characters/豎.md) "vertical line"

**垂露豎** is the *dewdrop vertical* (literally "hanging-dew"), the second principal kind of vertical stroke ([[豎]]) in regular script. Its lower end is rounded and blunt, like a drop of dew clinging to the end of a stroke. Chinese instruction cites 下, 木, 本 and 非 as characters that use it. It is the rounded counterpart of the pointed [[懸針豎]].

On the [[Stroke]] page it is the second sub-variety listed under the vertical stroke, the third of the eight principles of the 永字八法. Together with 懸針豎 it makes the classic calligraphers' pair 懸針垂露.

The Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and North Korean forms given here are compositional (the Japanese 竪 variant 垂露竪 is listed among the aliases). No form was found attested, so none is given.
