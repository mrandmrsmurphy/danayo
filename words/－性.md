## Notes
- [性](../characters/性.md) "nature; quality; gender" — a bound character; －性 is its fully productive suffix form.

－性 means "the nature or quality of being X", the abstract noun built on a property: English "-ness", "-ity", "-ic character". Attached to a stative or noun, it names the inherent quality: 可能性 "possibility" (the nature of being possible), 重要性 "importance", 必要性 "necessity", 酸性 "acidity". It is the regular way to turn a quality into a thing that can be topic or comment, and it is read compositionally, so any transparent *X + 性* is valid Dan'a'yo without its own page. A derivative gets a page only when its meaning is lexicalized.

Several already-enumerated words carry 性 and stay valid. [[性質]] "nature, character, disposition" is the closest to the bare noun, and [[磁性]] "magnetism" and [[知性]] "intelligence" are plain －性 derivations. The gender cluster is lexicalized rather than productive: [[男性]], [[女性]], [[陰性]], [[陽性]], [[雌性]], with [[性別]] "sex, gender" as the stand-in word that legitimizes the bare character, and [[跨性別]] and [[同性愛]] built on it. [[率性]] "following one's nature" preserves the "innate nature" sense. Dan'a'yo's own part-of-speech term [[性詞]] "stative" uses 性 in this same sense of "quality", not as the suffix.

Wiktionary lists 性 as the suffix "-ness; -ity (forming nouns and adjectives, denoting qualities)" in Chinese, "-ity, -ness, the nature or quality of something" in Japanese (せい), and 성 as the hanja form of the same in Korean. －性 has no conflict with Dan'a'yo's grammar, so it stays fully productive; the adjectival 的 is held back by its overlap with the genitive particle 之.

The pronunciation is the same as [[成]] "become" (ㄙㄧㄫ), cross-referenced in the callout above; －性 is a bound suffix and never stands alone.

The page filename uses the fullwidth hyphen `－` (U+FF0D) to mark a word that cannot stand alone but is fully productive; the ASCII form `-性` is kept as an alias.
