## Notes
- [茶 (char)](../characters/茶%20(char).md) "tea" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color"

茶色 is brown, the color of tea. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 茶色 (Mandarin chásè, Cantonese caa4 sik1) as "the colour of tea leaves or tea infusion" and "a reddish brown colour resembling that of concentrated tea infusions", Japanese ちゃいろ as "brown", and Korean 차색 as "tea color; brown color".

The Japanese reading ちゃいろ (chairo) is a native reading of 色, where the on'yomi concatenation would be ちゃしょく (chasyoku). The darker shade is 茶褐色, "dark brown", which Wiktionary lists in all three languages, and the plain term for brown in a color list is 褐色. Other colors built from natural objects with 色 are listed with it on [Color](../lexipedia/Color.md).
