## Notes
- [誤](../characters/誤.md) "err" + [解 (char)](../characters/解%20(char).md) "explain"

誤解 is to misunderstand, and a misunderstanding: to take something in the wrong sense. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin wùjiě as "to misunderstand, to misread; a misunderstanding", and Japanese ごかい as "misunderstanding". Korean 오해 and Cantonese ng6 gaai2 match.

It is the failed result of [理解](理解.md), understanding, and is related to [錯誤](錯誤.md), a mistake, which is a wrong act and not a wrong reading.
