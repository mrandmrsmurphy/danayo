## Notes
- [勢](../characters/勢.md) "power, influence" + [力](../characters/力%20(char).md) "force" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [勢](../characters/勢.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (力's own stand-in is [[力]] itself, an unrelated word).

勢力 means power or influence — the sway a person, group, or force holds over others or events.

Japanese せいりょく, Korean 세력, and Vietnamese thế lực are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄌㄧㄎ unique across both words and characters).
