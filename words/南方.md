## Notes
- [南](../characters/南.md) "south" + [方](../characters/方.md) "direction" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [南](../characters/南.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (方's own stand-in is [[方向]]).

南方 means south or the southern direction — the cardinal direction, or the southern region of a place.

**Real bugs found and fixed**: `cantonese` was completely wrong ("nan2 fang1," matching neither character's own reading) — corrected to naam4 fong1 (compositional from each character's own reading). `pos` was mislabeled 連接詞 ("conjunction," clearly wrong for a directional noun) — corrected to 名詞. Japanese なんぽう (matching sibling [[北方]]), Korean 남방, and Vietnamese phương nam are all standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄚㄇㄈㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
