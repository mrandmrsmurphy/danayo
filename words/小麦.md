## Notes
- [小 (char)](../characters/小%20(char).md) "small" + [麦](../characters/麦.md) "wheat"

小麦 is wheat: the grain that flour, bread and noodles are made from. Wiktionary glosses both Mandarin xiǎomài and Japanese こむぎ as "wheat". Korean 소맥, Vietnamese tiểu mạch and Cantonese siu2 mak6 match. The Japanese reading is a native one, not the on'yomi concatenation.

It is the grain behind [麺](麺.md), flour, and [麺包](麺包.md), bread, and it is set against the [大麦](大麦.md), barley. Wheat flour is the [小麦粉](小麦粉.md).
