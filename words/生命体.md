## Notes
- [生](../characters/生.md) "life" + [命](../characters/命.md) "life; fate" + [体](../characters/体.md) "body" — [生命](生命.md) + 体

生命体 (traditional 生命體) is "a life form; a living entity; a being with life." According to Wiktionary, 生命體 is attested in Chinese (shēngmìngtǐ, "lifeform; entity") and 生命体 in Japanese (せいめいたい, "a lifeform"), which is the Chinese-Japanese reading you gave; Korean 생명체 is the Korean form (from memory, not re-checked). Wiktionary has no Korean or Vietnamese section for either spelling, so `korean` 생명체 and `vietnamese` sinh mệnh thể are compositional readings. It is the word [生命](生命.md) "life" with 体 "body." It is used in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [平臥即是正義](../translation/平臥即是正義.md), in 充満 欲望 与 興奮 之 生命体 "life forms filled with desire and excitement." Related: [生物](生物.md) "living being."
