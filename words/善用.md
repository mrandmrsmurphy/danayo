## Etymology
- [善 (char)](../characters/善%20(char).md) "good" + [用](../characters/用.md) "use"

## Notes
- "To put to good use, use skillfully/wisely" (e.g. making the most of one's time or talents). Filled blank `korean: 선용`, a real if formal-register Sino-Korean word (시간을 선용하다, "to make good use of time"), and `vietnamese: thiện dụng`, a directly attested Sino-Vietnamese term with the same sense. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 션용 diverges from Sino-Korean 선용 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄙ·ㄝㄋ·⼄ㄫ unique among both words and characters).
