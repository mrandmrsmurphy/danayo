## Notes
- [熊](characters/熊%20(char).md) "bear" + [鼠](characters/鼠.md) "rat" — literally "bear rat," the name of a specific rodent species (the black rat / roof rat, *Rattus rattus*), distinct from the generic word [[老鼠]] ("rat, mouse" in general). 鼠's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 熊's own is itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 鼠 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

Fixed a serious contamination bug: `mandarin` (lǎoshǔ), `cantonese` (lou5 syu2), and `korean` (쥐) had all been substituted wholesale with the readings of the unrelated word [[老鼠]] rather than this word's own compositional forms — corrected to xióngshǔ, hung4 syu2, and 웅서, all matching 熊's and 鼠's own stored citations exactly. Removed the wrong alias 老鼠 (a genuinely different, already-existing word) and the redundant self-referential alias 熊鼠. Japanese くまねずみ (kuma-nezumi, native 熊+鼠 kun-reading compound) was already correct and is also the real, standard Japanese name for this species. Filled `vietnamese` with chuột đen ("black rat"), the real Vietnamese name for the species, matching the same real-species-name convention as the Japanese reading. Added missing `pos`(名詞). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄨㄫㄙ⼄ unique across both words and characters).
