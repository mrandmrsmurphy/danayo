## Notes
- [富](../characters/富.md) "abundance" + [裕](../characters/裕.md) "abundant; rich" — this word is the stand-in for [裕](../characters/裕.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

富裕 is a near-synonym doubling: both 富 and 裕 independently mean roughly "abundant, well-off," and the compound intensifies rather than shifts the sense, the same construction as [[狡滑]] or [[宏大]] elsewhere in this vault. 富 alone is the more productive of the two, forming its own large family (豊富 "abundant," 富有 "wealthy," 富人 "a rich person"), while 裕 rarely appears outside compounds built on this same "abundance" root (寛裕 "well-off, comfortable," 余裕 "leeway, latitude").

**Real bug found and fixed**: `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` had a stale b-series onset for 富's syllable (buo/뿟/ㄅㄨㄛ) that didn't match 富's own authoritative character-page reading (fuo/뿟/ㄈㄨㄛ, an f-series onset). This was previously noted here as "a real, cross-word-consistent pattern" matching [[豊富]]'s own then-current buo/푼붓 — but 豊富 has since been independently corrected to the same f-series pungfuo/풍뿟, leaving this word as the sole remaining outlier with no documented reason for the divergence — corrected to fuo'yu/뿟유/ㄈㄨㄛ⼜.

Japanese ふゆう and Korean 부유 are both plain, everyday, non-drifted readings. Vietnamese phú dụ (verified) is likewise standard. No specific HSK level number is confirmed (Wiktionary marks the compound only "Intermediate Mandarin"); no homophones (注音 ㄈㄨㄛ⼜ unique across both words and characters).
