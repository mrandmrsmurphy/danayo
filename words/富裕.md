## Notes
- [富](../characters/富.md) "abundance" + [裕](../characters/裕.md) "abundant; rich" — this word is the stand-in for [裕](../characters/裕.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

富裕 is a near-synonym doubling: both 富 and 裕 independently mean roughly "abundant, well-off," and the compound intensifies rather than shifts the sense, the same construction as [[狡滑]] or [[宏大]] elsewhere in this vault. 富 alone is the more productive of the two, forming its own large family (豊富 "abundant," 富有 "wealthy," 富人 "a rich person"), while 裕 rarely appears outside compounds built on this same "abundance" root (寛裕 "well-off, comfortable," 余裕 "leeway, latitude").

Japanese ふゆう and Korean 부유 are both plain, everyday, non-drifted readings. Vietnamese phú dụ (verified) is likewise standard.
