## Notes
- [富](../characters/富.md) "abundance" + [裕](../characters/裕.md) "abundant; rich" — this word is the stand-in for [裕](../characters/裕.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

富裕 is a near-synonym doubling: both 富 and 裕 independently mean roughly "abundant, well-off," and the compound intensifies rather than shifts the sense, the same construction as [[狡猾]] or [[宏大]] elsewhere in this vault. 富 alone is the more productive of the two, forming its own large family (豊富 "abundant," 富有 "wealthy," 富人 "a rich person"), while 裕 rarely appears outside compounds built on this same "abundance" root (寛裕 "well-off, comfortable," 余裕 "leeway, latitude").

**A consistent Dan'a'yo spelling note, not a bug**: this word's own `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` (buo/뿟/ㄅㄨㄛ) render 富's contribution with a plain b-series onset, diverging from 富's own single-character citation-form fields (fuo/뿟/ㄈㄨㄛ, an f-series onset) despite 富 sitting word-initial here, not in any obviously conditioning environment. The same b-series form for 富 also appears in [[豊富]] (pungbuo/푼붓), so this is a real, cross-word-consistent pattern rather than an isolated typo — flagged here for a future dedicated look at whether 富's own character-page citation form or the pattern seen in both compounds is the one that needs correcting, since the two currently disagree.

Japanese ふゆう and Korean 부유 are both plain, everyday, non-drifted readings. Vietnamese phú dụ (verified) is likewise standard. No specific HSK level number is confirmed (Wiktionary marks the compound only "Intermediate Mandarin"); no homophones (注音 ㄅㄨㄛ·⼜ unique across both words and characters).
