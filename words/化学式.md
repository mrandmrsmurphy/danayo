## Notes
- [化](../characters/化.md) "-ification, change" + [学](../characters/学.md) "learn, study" + [式](../characters/式.md) "style, formula" — chemical formula.

化学式 is a chemical formula: the written representation of a substance by its elements and their counts. Dan'a'yo writes it with the single-character abbreviations of each element, so water (H₂O) is $軽_二養$ and acetone is $〔炭軽_三〕_二炭養$; the rules are in the Formula Notation section of the Periodic Table page. It is [[化学]] "chemistry" plus 式 "formula, style." Mandarin huàxuéshì, Cantonese faa3 hok6 sik1, Japanese かがくしき and Korean 화학식 are the standard readings.
