## Notes
- [安](../characters/安.md) "peaceful, settled" + [排 (char)](../characters/排%20(char).md) "row, rank, arrange" — no stand-in relationship applies (安's own stand-in is [[平安]]; 排's own is itself).

安排 means to arrange, to set in order and settle: to schedule events, assign people to places or tasks, and make provision for something ahead of time. The image is of putting things in rows (排) so that they sit at rest (安). It is the verb of arranging, and its near neighbour [[計画]] "plan" is the noun of the thing planned; 安排 is the act of fixing a plan, a timetable or a seating into place. In Chinese the noun use ("an arrangement") is equally common, as in 你明天有安排嗎 "do you have plans tomorrow?".

Wiktionary lists 安排 for Chinese (Mandarin ānpái, Cantonese on1 paai4), Korean (안배, "arrangement") and Vietnamese (an bài, "to prearrange, to predetermine one's fate"), and Japanese has it as a spelling of あんばい (塩梅). The `japanese` field holds the compositional Sino-reading あんぱい, which was not checked against a dictionary.
