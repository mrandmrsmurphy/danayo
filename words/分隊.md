## Notes
- [分](../characters/分%20(char).md) "divide, part" + [隊](../characters/隊%20(char).md) "unit, squad" — "divided unit," a squad. Their own `stand_in` values: 分's is itself; 隊's is itself; none points here, so 分隊 is an independent compound.

分隊 is a squad: a small military unit that is a division of a larger one. Mandarin fēnduì, Cantonese fan1 deoi6, Japanese ぶんたい and Korean 분대 all use it. It is the small unit under a [[部隊]] (unit). Compare [[隊]] and [[隊長]] (captain).
