## Notes
- [性](../characters/性.md) "nature, gender" + [交](../characters/交%20(char).md) "exchange, mix" — no stand-in relationship applies (性's own stand-in is [[個性]]; 交's own is itself).

性交 means to have sex or copulate.

Mandarin xìngjiāo, Cantonese sing3 gaau1, Japanese せいこう, Korean 성교, and Vietnamese tính giao are all standard, fully compositional readings.
