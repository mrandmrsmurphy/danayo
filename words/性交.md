## Notes
- [性](../characters/性.md) "nature, gender" + [交](../characters/交%20(char).md) "exchange, mix" — no stand-in relationship applies (性's own stand-in is [[個性]]; 交's own is itself).

性交 means to have sex or copulate.

Mandarin xìngjiāo, Cantonese sing3 gaau1, Japanese せいこう, Korean 성교, and Vietnamese tính giao are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bugs found and fixed**: `japanese` had archaic kana せいかう — modernized to せいこう; `cantonese` was missing a space. Filled blank `pos: 動詞`. Note: this word's own 諺文/羅馬字 (걋/gyau) match the documented majority "gyau" form used across most 交-compounds ([[外交]], [[交叉]], etc.), diverging from 交's own character-page citation (gyou/굣) — left as-is per the established convention that the character page, not these words, may need a future fix. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄫㄍ⼄ㄨ unique across both words and characters).
