## Notes
"Confucianism, the Ru school/tradition." `vietnamese: Nho gia` is one of the most common everyday Vietnamese terms for Confucianism (alongside Nho giáo), a direct match — Việt culture's own long Confucian tradition makes this an especially well-known term.

**Real content bug found and fixed**: `mandarin` had a stray Cantonese jyutping romanization appended ("Rújiā; jyu4 gaa1") — trimmed to the pinyin alone. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 누가 diverges entirely from Sino-Korean 유가. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄨㄍㄚ unique among both words and characters).
