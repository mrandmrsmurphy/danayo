## Notes
- [豹](../characters/豹.md) "leopard" + [猫 (char)](../characters/猫%20(char).md) "cat"
- This word is the viable form of [豹](../characters/豹.md): the character's meaning (leopard, panther) is identical, but 豹 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 豹猫 is its `stand_in` compound.

豹猫 names the leopard cat (*Prionailurus bengalensis*), a small wild cat about the size of a domestic cat with a spotted coat, widespread from the Russian Far East through China, Korea, Southeast Asia and the Indian subcontinent. The name is descriptive: a cat (猫) with the spots of a leopard (豹). It is therefore a different animal from the leopard that 豹 alone names, and the compound is more specific than its first component, a point worth keeping in mind since the stand-in note above speaks of 豹 in the sense of "leopard, panther."

The Chinese is 豹猫 (traditional 豹貓, the alias), Mandarin bàomāo and Cantonese paau3 maau1. The other languages have their own animal names for the species (such as Korean 삵 and Japanese ベンガルヤマネコ for the leopard cat), so this page leaves their Sino-reading fields blank. Dan'a'yo takes the sense "leopard cat" and no wider. It is placed under [Animals](../lexipedia/Animals.md).
