## Notes
- [角 (char)](../characters/角%20(char).md) "horn; angle" — stand-in for [[角 (char)|角]], which cannot appear independently

角 is 象形 of an animal's horn, its ridged surface and tapering shape both visible in the character's strokes. From the literal anatomical horn, the character extended to the mathematical "angle" — the two senses coexisting in this word's own frontmatter gloss — anchoring this vault's geometric vocabulary (三角 "triangle," 直角 "right angle," 五角形/七角形 "pentagon/heptagon," each concatenating 角 with a numeral and 形 "shape"). 角逐 ("to compete, to contend for") extends the horn image figuratively into rivalry, evoking horned animals locking horns in combat.

Japanese carries an unusually wide three-reading set: かく (KAKU, used here — 三角形 sankakukei, 角度 kakudo "angle, degree") is the dominant reading for the geometric/measurement sense, while ろく and こく survive only in a small number of specialized or archaic compounds; the native 訓読み かど (kado) names a physical corner or edge. Vietnamese giác is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading for the geometric sense (tam giác "triangle," directly paralleling 三角) — picked over the character's noisier remaining stored variants (chác, dạc, giạc, giốc, góc), the last of which is actually the native Vietnamese word for "corner," parallel to Japanese かど rather than a Sino-Vietnamese doublet of giác. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 곡 (gog, from Middle Chinese 見母 k + 覺韻 ɣʌk) diverges from Sino-Korean 각 in the vowel.

**Real homophone found**: `注音 ㄍㄛㄎ`/`곡` is also carried by [[谷]] ("valley"), an existing but unperfected standalone word — added the full callout to `角` and a matching anticipatory one to `谷`. Checked the remaining `ㄍㄛㄎ`-reading characters (穀, 国) — neither has a self-pointing `stand_in` (穀物, 国家 respectively), confirming no third homophone word exists here.
