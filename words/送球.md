## Notes
**Found and fixed a wrong-compound reading bug on `mandarin`/`cantonese`**: the stored values (shǒuqiú/sau2 kau4) actually belong to the unrelated (though same-sport) compound 手球 ("hand-ball," using 手 rather than 送 as its first character) — corrected to the honest compositional sòngqiú/sung3 kau4 (送's own sòng/sung3 + 球's own qiú/kau4). Japanese そうきゅう and korean 송구 are directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese tống cầu is the honest compositional reading (送's own tống + 球's own cầu); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. **Found and removed a bad-aliases-entry**: `手球` is that same genuinely different compound, not a spelling variant of 送球. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 송규 diverges from Sino-Korean 송구 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄛㄫㄍ⼜ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 送's `stand_in` (送 itself) nor 球's own `stand_in` (球 itself) points to this compound.
