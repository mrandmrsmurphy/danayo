## Notes
- [蒼](../characters/蒼%20(char).md) "blue, green, grey" is separately legitimized by [[蒼]] itself. [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" is separately legitimized by [[海洋]]. No cranberry.

Mandarin cānghǎi, korean 창해 (also a real word for "vast blue sea"), and vietnamese thương hải (a real classical term, matching the idiom 滄海桑田/thương hải tang điền cited on both character pages) all confirmed standard and compositional.
