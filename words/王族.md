## Notes
- [王 (char)](../characters/王%20(char).md) "king" + [族](../characters/族.md) "clan"

王族 is royalty, the royal clan. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 王族 (Mandarin wángzú, Cantonese wong4 zuk6) as "royalty; royal clan" and Japanese おうぞく as "royalty, people of royal birth". The Korean 왕족 field is built from the readings of the two characters. Compare [王室](王室.md), the royal family.
