## Notes
- [放](../characters/放.md) "release; let go" + [送 (char)](../characters/送%20(char).md) "send"

放送 is "broadcast," literally "release and send." According to Wiktionary, Japanese ほうそう is a wasei-kango (a compound formed in Japan) attested from around 1920 and means a radio or television transmission, as a noun and as a suru-verb; Korean 방송 is the same compound in Sino-Korean; and Chinese 放送 (fàngsòng, Cantonese fong3 sung3) is a verb "to broadcast" and also "to give out, release," with the noun sense "giveaway" (大放送). Dan'a'yo keeps "broadcast."

`vietnamese` phóng tống is the compositional reading of the two characters; Wiktionary has no Vietnamese section for 放送, and the everyday Vietnamese word for broadcasting is different (from memory, not re-checked). It is the base of [放送局](放送局.md) "broadcasting station," and the second element of 玉音放送, together with [玉音](玉音.md).
