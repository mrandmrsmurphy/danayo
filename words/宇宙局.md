## Notes
- [宇](../characters/宇.md) "universe, spatial extent" + [宙 (char)](../characters/宙%20(char).md) "cosmos, temporal extent" + [局 (char)](../characters/局%20(char).md) "bureau, agency" — space agency

Wiktionary: Japanese 宇宙局 "space agency"; Korean 우주국 is the hanja form, "space agency". It is built on [[宇宙]], glossed in Chinese as "universe; cosmos; macrocosm; space" and "(infinite) space and time", plus 局 "bureau, agency". Dan'a'yo uses it as the generic word for any space agency.
