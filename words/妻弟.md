## Notes
- [妻 (char)](../characters/妻%20(char).md) "wife" + [弟 (char)](../characters/弟%20(char).md) "younger brother" — wife's younger brother.

妻弟 is the younger brother of one's wife. It is "wife-younger brother." Mandarin qīdì and Cantonese cai1 dai6 are confirmed on Wiktionary as a formal or classical term (literary Chinese also uses it for a wife's younger sister, a sense Dan'a'yo does not take); a Korean entry exists but was not shown, so `korean` is left blank. It pairs with [[妻兄]]; the hypernym is [[姻弟]]. It is a schema word in the family of in-law terms built from the marriage relation and the sibling roots, coined for predictability rather than a claim about ancient usage.
