## Notes
- [紀](../characters/紀.md) "century" + [元](../characters/元.md) "element" + [前 (char)](../characters/前%20(char).md) "front, before, preceding" — none of the three characters' own `stand_in` points here (紀's is [[世紀]], 元's is [[元素]], 前's is [[前]] itself), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: true` per the AND-rule: all three constituents are individually `kwin: true`.

紀元前 names the era before the Common Era's starting point — B.C./B.C.E., the counterpart of [[紀元後]] "A.D./C.E."

Mandarin jìyuánqián, Cantonese gei2 jyun4 cin4, Japanese きげんぜん (kigenzen), and Korean 기원전 (giwonjeon) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 紀元前 is itself a common attested word for "BC" in all four languages. Vietnamese kỷ nguyên tiền is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading, though the idiomatic Vietnamese phrase for "BC" is instead the native trước Công Nguyên ("before the Common Era"). No Dan'a'yo homophones.
