## Notes
- [家](../characters/家.md) "house, family" + [蝿](../characters/蝿.md) "fly" — 家蝿 is the stand-in word for [蝿](../characters/蝿.md); 蝿 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse.

家蝿 is the housefly, *Musca domestica*. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 家蠅 (an alias; Mandarin jiāyíng, Cantonese gaa1 jing4) as "housefly", and Japanese 家蝿 as "housefly", read いえばえ, a native compound in which 家 is read いえ and 蝿 takes the rendaku form ばえ of はえ. It has no Korean or Vietnamese entry, so Korean 가승 here is built from the characters; the Vietnamese field is left blank.

English Wikipedia describes the housefly as a species of the family Muscidae that, from a place of origin possibly in the Middle East, has spread around the world as a commensal of humans; the female lays batches of about 100 eggs on decaying organic matter such as food waste, carrion or feces.

家蝿 is the stand-in word for [蝿](../characters/蝿.md), which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo.
