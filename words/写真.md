## Notes
- [写](../characters/写.md) "copy, reproduce" + [真 (char)](../characters/真%20(char).md) "true" — "photograph" (literally "copying the true/real"). Mandarin xiězhēn, Cantonese se2 zan1, Japanese しゃしん, and Korean 사진 are all directly attested standard readings. `vietnamese: tả chân` is the honest compositional reading (写's own tả + 真's own chân), attested as a real term for "realistic, true-to-life depiction" (tranh tả chân, "realistic painting") rather than specifically "photograph" — the everyday Vietnamese word for photograph is tấm hình (or ảnh). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 샤진 diverges from Sino-Korean 사진 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼘ㄐㄧㄋ unique among both words and characters).
