## Notes
- [忠](../characters/忠.md) "loyal" + [告](../characters/告.md) "tell" — no stand-in relationship applies (忠's own stand-in is [[忠誠]]; 告's own is [[告訴]]).

忠告 means to advise — offering earnest, well-meaning counsel.

Mandarin zhōnggào, Cantonese zung1 gou3, Japanese ちゅうこく, Korean 충고, and Vietnamese trung cáo are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `cantonese` had 告's syllable mistyped guk1→gou3 (matching 告's own citation). Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄨㄫㄍㄚㄨ unique across both words and characters).
