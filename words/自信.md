## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" + [信](../characters/信.md) "trust"

自信 is self-confidence: trust in one's own ability to do a thing. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin zìxìn as "to have confidence in oneself; self-confident" and Japanese じしん as "self-confidence". Korean 자신, Vietnamese tự tin and Cantonese zi6 seon3 match.

It is the steady ground under [勇気](勇気.md), courage, and it becomes [自負](自負.md) when it swells.
