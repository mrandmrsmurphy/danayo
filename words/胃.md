## Notes
- [胃](../characters/胃%20(char).md) "stomach, gizzard" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 胃 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

胃 means the stomach or gizzard, as in 胃癌 (stomach cancer) and 胃痛 (stomachache).

Mandarin wèi, cantonese wai6, and korean 위 (wi) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese has no native kun-reading (stored as ø on the character page), so the on-reading イ (I) is used directly. Vietnamese vị is the character's sole stored reading. No homophones.
