## Notes
- [滑 (char)](../characters/滑%20(char).md) "slippery" — the bare character used directly as a word, its own `stand_in`.

滑 means slippery or smooth: having a surface that offers little friction (滑溜, "slick"; 光滑, "smooth"; 滑冰, "ice-skate"; 滑雪, "ski"), and by extension to slide or glide (滑行, "coast"; 滑倒, "slip and fall"; 滑走路, "runway"), or to be slick in manner (油滑, "glib"). The graph is a 形声 compound of 氵 "water" with the phonetic 骨. The same character has a separate reading and sense in 滑稽 ("comical"), which this word does not use.

Mandarin huá, Cantonese waat6, Japanese かつ (滑走路/かっそうろ, "runway"; 円滑/えんかつ, "smooth, harmonious"), Korean 활 (활주로, 원활, "smooth") and Vietnamese hoạt (linh hoạt, "flexible, agile") are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native verb 滑る (すべる) and adjective 滑らか (なめらか) for the everyday words, so on'yomi かつ is bound to compounds.
