## Notes
- [桃](../characters/桃.md) "peach" + [月](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — no stand-in relationship applies (桃's own stand-in is [[桃子]]; 月's own is itself).

Third month of the ancient Chinese poetic calendar. Peach blossoms (桃花) are one of the most beloved symbols in Chinese poetry — fragrant, fleeting, and brilliant pink. The famous Peach Blossom Spring (桃花源) of Tao Yuanming evokes this month's dreamlike quality.

Mandarin táoyuè, Cantonese tou4 jyut6, and Korean 도월 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese ももつき reads both halves natively (桃's kun'yomi もも "peach" + 月's kun'yomi つき "month") rather than the Sino-Japanese on'yomi concatenation とうげつ, matching the established poetic-month convention (see [[寅月]], [[卯月]], [[未月]], [[杏月]], [[桂月]]). Vietnamese tháng đào likewise uses the native *tháng* plus the Sino-Vietnamese peach name đào, the same pattern. Removed the redundant `品詞` field and reformatted `japanese`/`vietnamese` from single-item lists to scalar strings. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄨ·⼔ㄊ unique across both words and characters).
