## Notes
- [煩 (char)](../characters/煩%20(char).md) "troublesome" + [忙](../characters/忙.md) "busy"

煩忙 is "busy; bustling," the literary counterpart of [繁忙](繁忙.md), with 煩 "troublesome" for the pressure of many tasks. According to Wiktionary, Chinese 煩忙 (fánmáng, Cantonese reading not checked) is a literary word for "busy; bustling," Korean 번망 is the hanja form of the same word, and the Japanese entry (はんぼう) redirects to 繁忙, treating 煩忙 as a variant writing. Wiktionary has no Vietnamese section, so `vietnamese` phiền mang is a compositional reading. 忙 on its own is not a word: its stand-in is 繁忙, and 煩忙 is a second compound that uses it. It is used in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [平臥即是正義](../translation/平臥即是正義.md), in 煩忙 之 人 "busy people," standing in for the Chinese 忙碌.
