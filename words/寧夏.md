## Notes
- [寧](../characters/寧.md) "peaceful, calm" + [夏](../characters/夏%20(char).md) "summer; Xia" — the name is generally explained as a reference to the Western Xia state; this is from memory, not re-checked (English Wiktionary's etymology section for 寧夏 is empty). No stand-in relationship applies (寧's own is [[安寧]]; 夏's is [[夏]]). 宁夏 is the simplified spelling, listed on English Wiktionary; 甯夏 follows from 甯 being an alias of 寧 and is not itself listed there.

寧夏 is Ningxia, a region of north-western China, whose capital is [[銀川]] (from memory, not re-checked). English Wiktionary lists only this sense for the Chinese entry. Its Japanese entry gives ねいか, which is the field value. English Wiktionary's Korean entry for 寧夏 lists two readings, 영하 and 녕하; the field holds 녕하, the form not shifted by the initial-ㄴ rule, which differs from the Dan'a'yo 諺文 넝햐 in both vowels. Modern South Korean usage is 닝샤 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Ninh Hạ is the Sino-Vietnamese name. Cantonese ning4 haa6 is the Jyutping on English Wiktionary. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄝㄫㄏ⼘ unique across both words and characters).

**How the official name is written.** The full official name 寧夏回族自治區 is written as separate words: 寧夏 <ruby>[回族](../words/回族.md)<rt>ㄏㄛㄧㄐㄛㄎ</rt></ruby> <ruby>[自治](../words/自治.md)<rt>ㄐㄧㄜㄑㄧ</rt></ruby> <ruby>[区域](../words/区域.md)<rt>ㄎㄨ·ㄨㄧㄎ</rt></ruby>. 回族 (the Hui people) is an existing word, so it is not coined again or split into 回 and 族.
