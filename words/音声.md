## Notes
- [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" + [声](../characters/声.md) "vocalize, emit sound" — no `#cranberry`: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here (音's is [[音楽]], 声's is [[発声]]).

音声 (yīnshēng) is a real Mandarin word for "sound, audio" (especially in technical contexts, e.g. 音声识别 "speech recognition"), and Cantonese 音声 (jam1seng1) likewise. Japanese おんせい (onsei) is the standard word for "voice, audio" in formal/technical registers. Korean 음성 (eumseong) is likewise standard, though everyday spoken Korean also uses the native synonym 말소리 ("speech sound") for the same concept. Sino-Vietnamese âm thanh is the ordinary, everyday Vietnamese word for "sound" in general.
