## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" + [負 (char)](../characters/負%20(char).md) "bear; rely on"

自負 is pride in oneself: a high opinion of one's own ability, justified or not. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin zìfù as "thinking highly of oneself; self-important; conceited" and Japanese じふ as "self-confidence; to be self-proud". Vietnamese tự phụ and Cantonese zi6 fu6 match, and Korean 자부 is the reading of the characters.

It is the pride of one who relies on his own gifts, sounder than [傲慢](傲慢.md), haughtiness, and a step beyond [自信](自信.md), confidence. The respect for oneself is [自尊](自尊.md).
