## Notes
- [副 (char)](../characters/副%20(char).md) "secondary" + [食 (char)](../characters/食%20(char).md) "food"

副食 is side food: what is eaten with the staple, meat, fish and vegetables. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin fùshí as "non-staple food" and Japanese ふくしょく as "side dish". Cantonese fu3 sik6 matches, and Korean 부식 is the reading of the characters.

It is the opposite of the [主食](主食.md), staple, and includes the [肉](肉.md), meat, and [菜蔬](菜蔬.md), vegetables, eaten with it.
