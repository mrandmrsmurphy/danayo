## Notes
- [蔚](../characters/蔚.md) "luxuriant" + [山](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain" — the name of Ulsan, the industrial port city of southeastern Korea.

蔚山 (Ulsan) is a coastal industrial city northeast of Busan, in the old territory of 慶尚南道 (see [[慶尚]]). Its full modern name is 蔚山廣域市 (울산광역시), "Ulsan Metropolitan City", a separate unit apart from 慶尚南道 since 1997 (from memory, not re-checked). It is known as the base of Korea's large shipbuilding, automobile and petrochemical industries (from memory, not re-checked). The character 蔚 is read 울 in Korean and yù in Chinese surnames and place names, but Mandarin wèi in the ordinary word 蔚藍 "azure"; Chinese Wiktionary gives the city as Wèishān, with Yùshān noted there as a rare reading inferred from the Korean.

English, Chinese and Korean Wiktionary have 蔚山 as the Korean city, and English Wiktionary has the Vietnamese Uất Sơn and the Japanese ウルサン, with a remark that the colonial period read it Urusan, unlike other Korean place names read with on'yomi. Japanese うつさん is the compositional on'yomi from the character pages and is not attested for the city.
