## Notes
- [信](../characters/信.md) "believe, trust" + [仰](../characters/仰.md) "look up to" — "trust and look up to," faith. Their own `stand_in` values: 信's is [[信任]]; 仰's is [[仰望]]; none points here, so 信仰 is an independent compound.

信仰 is faith: religious belief and trust in God. Mandarin xìnyǎng, Cantonese seon3 joeng5, Japanese しんこう and Korean 신앙 all use it, and Vietnamese tín ngưỡng does too, for belief in general. It is the noun of faith, where [[信奉]] is the verb of active adherence. Compare [[信徒]] (believer) and [[堅信]] (confirmation).
