## Notes
- [尊](../characters/尊.md) "revere; honored" + [敬](../characters/敬.md) "respect; reverence" — this word is the stand-in for [敬](../characters/敬.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

尊敬 is a near-synonym doubling, both halves independently meaning "to respect, revere" — the standard everyday verb for showing respect toward a person, distinct from [[尊厳]] (the inherent quality of dignity a person or thing possesses, not a regard directed at them).

Japanese そんけい, Korean 존경, and Vietnamese tôn kính are all standard, non-drifted readings for "respect." No specific HSK level number is confirmed (Wiktionary marks the compound only "Elementary Mandarin"). No homophones (注音 ㄐㄛㄋㄍ⼶ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
