## Notes
- [水](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [疱](../characters/疱.md) "blister" — "water blister." Their own `stand_in` values: 水's is itself; 疱's is [[面疱]]; none points here, so 水疱 is an independent compound.

水疱 is a blister: a small bubble of fluid under the skin, from a burn or from friction. Mandarin shuǐpào (水皰), Cantonese seoi2 paau3 and Japanese すいほう are attested, and Korean 수포 is the same compound. Compare [[痘痕]] (pock-mark) and [[傷害]] (to wound).
