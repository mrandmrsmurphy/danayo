## Notes
- [舞](../characters/舞.md) "dance" + [台 (char)](../characters/台%20(char).md) "platform"

舞台 is a stage: the platform on which a performance is given. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin wǔtái as "stage" and Japanese ぶたい as "stage". Korean 무대 and Vietnamese vũ đài are the same word. Cantonese mou5 toi4 matches.

It lies in the [劇場](劇場.md), theatre, and the actors who walk on it are [俳優](俳優.md). The word is also used for the setting of a story.
