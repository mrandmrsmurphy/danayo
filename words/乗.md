## Notes
- [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "to ride"

乗 (alternate form 乘) is 象形: a person 大 climbing a tree 木, with the feet visible — clearer in the alternate form 椉. Originally "to ascend," the sense extended to "to ride" ([[乗馬]] "to ride a horse," [[乗車]] "to board a vehicle") and, in a further extension found across the whole sphere, to arithmetic multiplication ([[乗算]] "to multiply," [[自乗]] "to square," [[乗数]] "multiplier") — riding one quantity "onto" another as a metaphor for repeated addition.

Mandarin chéng, Cantonese sing4, Japanese じょう/しょう (jō/shō, on'yomi) alongside native の(る) (no(ru), the everyday verb "to ride"), and Korean 승 (seung) all preserve both the "ride" and "multiply" senses in their respective compound vocabularies. Vietnamese thừa is the Hán Việt reading — most visible today in the mathematical term lũy thừa (累乗, "exponentiation," literally "accumulated multiplication"); the character page's other stored candidates belong to unrelated or only phonetically-related characters (thặng to the derived character [[剰]] "surplus," thắng/thằng to 勝 "to win" and an unrelated native Vietnamese word respectively) and were not used here.

This word shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading (sung/숭/ㄙㄨㄫ) with another word still awaiting its own turn in this sweep, [[升]] ("litre") — the reciprocal half of this callout will be completed when it comes up.
