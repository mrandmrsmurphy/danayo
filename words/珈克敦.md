## Notes
- [珈](../characters/珈.md) "hair ornament" + [克 (char)](../characters/克%20(char).md) + [敦](../characters/敦.md) — phonetic transcription of *Chalcedon*, the ancient city on the Bosphorus (modern Kadıköy, Istanbul). The source-language spelling 迦克敦 is kept as an alias: 迦 is too rare for the inventory and is an alias of 珈, the character that carries this sound in other transcriptions (珈拿陀 "Canada", 釈珈文尼 "Śākyamuni").
- Site of the Council of Chalcedon (451 AD).

Following this vault's proper-noun/named-referent convention (cf. [[冥王星]], [[出谷記]]), Vietnamese Canxêđoan is that language's own real, independently-attested name for this place (via French "Chalcédoine"), not a compositional transliteration of the Dan'a'yo/Chinese characters. Mandarin Jiākèdūn, cantonese gaa1 hak1 deon1, japanese かこくとん, and korean 가극돈 are all directly attested phonetic transcriptions using these same characters. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 가쿡돈 diverges from Sino-Korean 가극돈 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚ·ㄎㄨㄎㄉㄛㄋ unique among both words and characters). Both character-page citations (克 and 敦) already correctly present.

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot added**: the 珈|克 boundary is a genuine resegmentation fork — `ㄎㄚ`(ka)+`ㄎㄨㄎ`(kuk) and the alternate `ㄎㄚㄎ`(kak, a real assigned syllable)+`ㄨㄎ`(uk, also real) are both fully legal syllable sequences — so the dot is required: ㄎㄚ·ㄎㄨㄎㄉㄛㄋ.
