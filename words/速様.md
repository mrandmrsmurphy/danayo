## Etymology
- [速 (char)](../characters/速%20(char).md) "quick, fast" + [様 (char)](../characters/様%20(char).md) "-ly (adverbializer)"

## Notes
速様 is a Dan'a'yo-internal derivational compound following the same "-様" adverbializer pattern as the correlatives [[此様]]/[[其様]]/[[彼様]]/[[何様]] ("this way," "that way," etc.) — not an independently attested word in any of the five source languages, each of which forms "quickly" by its own separate means (Mandarin 迅速地, Cantonese 快速噉, Japanese 速く/はやく, Korean 빨리, Vietnamese nhanh/mau).

**Found and fixed a wrong-compound-reading bug across all five reading fields**: mandarin/cantonese/korean/vietnamese had each been filled with that language's own real, unrelated everyday word or phrase for "quickly" (including a garbled comma-mashed korean value and a kanji+okurigana japanese value, つ" 速く" rather than a kana reading) instead of the honest compositional combination of 速's own reading + 様's own reading. Corrected to mandarin sùyàng, cantonese cuk1 joeng4, japanese そくよう, korean 속양 (matching the word's own stored 諺文/羅馬字 exactly), and vietnamese tốc dạng. **Found and removed a bad-aliases-entry**: all five previously-listed aliases (迅速地, 快速地, 急速地, 趕快地, 赶快地) are genuinely different real compounds using 地 as their adverbializer, not spelling variants of 速様. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 속양 matches the corrected Sino-Korean 속양 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄛㄎ⼘ㄫ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 速's `stand_in` (速 itself) nor 様's own `stand_in` (様 itself) points to this compound.
