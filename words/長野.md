## Notes
- [長](../characters/長%20(char).md) "long" + [野](../characters/野.md) "wild"

長野 is Nagano, written "long field". It is the name of the city around the temple Zenkō-ji, and the old province in the same region was called Shinano. The reading ながの is native: 長 (なが, "long") plus 野 (の, "field"). In Mandarin the first character is read cháng "long", not zhǎng.

Nagano holds much of the Japanese Alps and hosted the 1998 Winter Olympics. Its capital Nagano grew around Zenkō-ji; other landmarks are Matsumoto Castle and the resort town of Karuizawa, and it is known for soba.

In Dan'a'yo 長野 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 나가노 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
