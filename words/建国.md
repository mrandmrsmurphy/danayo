## Notes
- [建](../characters/建.md) "build; establish" + [国](../characters/国.md) "nation" — no stand-in relationship applies (建's own `stand_in` is [[建設]], 国's is [[国家]]).

建国 names the founding of a nation or state (建国記念日, Japan's National Foundation Day).

Japanese けんこく, Korean 건국, and Vietnamese kiến quốc are all standard, non-drifted readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄝㄋㄍㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
