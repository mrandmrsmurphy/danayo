## Notes
- [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" + [望](../characters/望.md) "hope, esteem" — "popularity, good reputation, being well-regarded by the populace." `vietnamese: nhân vọng` is directly attested (confirmed via web search). Nearly synonymous with [[声望]] ("prestige/reputation" generally, from "sound" + "hope"): 人望 emphasizes being liked/trusted by *people* specifically, while 声望 is the broader "renown, fame" sense.

**Real content bug found and fixed on [[声望]]'s own page**: its `vietnamese` field read "hanh vọng," but 声's own confirmed reading is thanh, not hanh — corrected to thanh vọng (confirmed via web search as the correct attested term for "reputation, prestige"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 닌망 diverges from Sino-Korean 인망 in the first syllable's initial consonant. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄧㄋㄇㄚㄫ unique among both words and characters).
