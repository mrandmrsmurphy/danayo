## Notes
- [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" + [望](../characters/望.md) "hope, esteem" — "popularity, good reputation, being well-regarded by the populace." `vietnamese: nhân vọng` is directly attested (confirmed via web search). Nearly synonymous with [[声望]] ("prestige/reputation" generally, from "sound" + "hope"): 人望 emphasizes being liked/trusted by *people* specifically, while 声望 is the broader "renown, fame" sense.
