## Notes
- [老](../characters/老%20(char).md) "old" + [兵](../characters/兵.md) "soldier" — "old soldier," a veteran. Their own `stand_in` values: 老's is itself; 兵's is [[兵士]]; none points here, so 老兵 is an independent compound.

老兵 is a veteran: an old or long-serving soldier. Mandarin lǎobīng, Cantonese lou5 bing1, Japanese ろうへい and Korean 노병 all use it. It is the opposite of [[新兵]] (recruit). Compare [[兵士]] and [[軍人]].
