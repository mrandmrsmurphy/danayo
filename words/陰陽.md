## Notes
- [陰 (char)](../characters/陰%20(char).md) "Yin, shade" + [陽 (char)](../characters/陽%20(char).md) "shine, sun, Yang"

陰陽 names the foundational Chinese cosmological pair of complementary, interdependent opposite forces — yin (dark, passive, feminine) and yang (bright, active, masculine) — whose dynamic balance underlies traditional Chinese philosophy, medicine, cosmology, and divination (陰陽五行, "yin-yang and the Five Phases"). Mandarin 陰陽/阴阳 (yīnyáng), Cantonese 陰陽 (jam1joeng4), Japanese 陰陽 (いんよう, notably also read おんみょう onmyō in the context of 陰陽道 onmyōdō, "the way of yin-yang," Japan's historical esoteric cosmology/divination practice), Korean 음양 (eumyang), and Sino-Vietnamese âm dương all use this term identically as the borrowed foundational concept, largely without native replacement.
