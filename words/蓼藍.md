## Notes
- [蓼](../characters/蓼.md) "knotweed" + [藍 (char)](../characters/藍%20(char).md) "indigo plant"

蓼藍 is Japanese indigo, also called Chinese indigo: the plant *Persicaria tinctoria*, grown as a source of blue dye. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 蓼藍 (Mandarin liǎolán, Cantonese liu5 laam4) as "Chinese indigo" and Japanese たであい as "Japanese indigo", both *Persicaria tinctoria*. It has no Korean entry, so Korean 료남 here is built from the characters.

The Japanese reading たであい is irregular, not the concatenation of the component readings. [[蓼]] is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo; 蓼藍 serves as its stand-in word. The dye it gives is the 藍, the color of which is the [[藍色]], and dyeing in general is [[染色]].
