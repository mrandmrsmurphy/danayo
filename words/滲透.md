## Notes
- [滲](../characters/滲.md) "soak through" + [透 (char)](../characters/透%20(char).md) "transparent" — to soak right through

Wiktionary: Chinese "to seep; to seep through; to permeate; to penetrate"; in physiology "to osmose"; figuratively, in politics, "to infiltrate"; Korean 삼투 is the hanja form of 滲透. The simplified spelling 渗透 is kept as an alias. 滲 has `stand_in` 滲入, so this word is not its legitimizer. Compare [[滲入]] and [[滲出]].
