## Notes
- [滲](../characters/滲.md) "soak through" + [透 (char)](../characters/透%20(char).md) "transparent" — to soak right through

滲透 means to seep all the way through: a liquid or other substance passing through the whole of a material until it comes out the other side, which gives 透 its sense of "right through".  In science it is osmosis (浸透圧 in Japanese, with 滲透圧 the Chinese and older spelling, "osmotic pressure"), the movement of a solvent through a membrane.  Figuratively it is used for an idea, culture or influence that permeates a society (文化の滲透, "the permeation of a culture"), and for covert infiltration (滲透作戦, "an infiltration operation").  It differs from [[滲入]], which only seeps in, and from [[滲出]], which only seeps out: 滲透 is the full passage through.

滲 has `stand_in` 滲入, so this word is not its legitimizer.  Mandarin shèntòu, Cantonese sam3 tau3, Japanese しんとう and Korean 삼투 (the Korean osmosis term is 삼투) are the readings, and the alias 渗透 is listed.  `kwin` is false because the Dan'a'yo 솜톳 differs from the Korean 삼투.  No Vietnamese form was found attested, so none is given.
