## Notes
- [固](../characters/固.md) "solid, firm" + [体](../characters/体.md) "body" — solid.

固体 is a solid as a state of matter: a substance with fixed shape and volume, whose particles are held in place. It is the counterpart of [[液体]] (liquid) and [[気体]] (gas). The change from solid to liquid is melting ([[溶化]]). Mandarin gùtǐ, Cantonese gu3 tai2, Japanese こたい and Korean 고체 are the standard readings.
