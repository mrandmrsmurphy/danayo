## Notes
- [乃 (char)](../characters/乃%20(char).md) "but; then; as it turned out"

乃 is 象形 in origin, though the oracle-bone form's exact picture (possibly a bent implement or reaching gesture) has no living pictographic reading left today — 乃 functions exclusively as a classical conjunction and copula, surviving almost entirely in literary/classical registers rather than everyday colloquial speech.

Japanese has three on'yomi (だい/あい/ない, dai/ai/nai) but 乃 is rare outside proper names and a handful of classical constructions, classified in this vault's own data as a name-use kanji rather than everyday vocabulary. Korean 내 (nae) is likewise confined to classical/literary contexts. Vietnamese bèn — the one attested, semantically fitting match among several implausible near-homophone candidates stored on the character's own page — is a native Vietnamese conjunction meaning "then, thereupon," closely matching 乃's classical "then" sense.

This word shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading (nai/내/ㄋㄚㄧ) with another word still awaiting its own turn in this sweep, [[耐]] ("able to tolerate") — the reciprocal half of this callout will be completed when it comes up.
