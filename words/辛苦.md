## Etymology
- [辛](../characters/辛.md) "spicy" + [苦 (char)](../characters/苦%20(char).md) "suffering, bitter" — extended metaphorically to "hardship, toil."

## Notes
Mandarin xīnkǔ, cantonese san1 fu2, and japanese しんく are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese tân khổ is a directly attested Sino-Vietnamese word ("bitter hardship"), matching 辛's own tân + 苦's own khổ exactly.
