## Etymology
- [辛](../characters/辛.md) "spicy" + [苦 (char)](../characters/苦%20(char).md) "suffering, bitter" — extended metaphorically to "hardship, toil."

## Notes
Mandarin xīnkǔ, cantonese san1 fu2, and japanese しんく are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese tân khổ is a directly attested Sino-Vietnamese word ("bitter hardship"), matching 辛's own tân + 苦's own khổ exactly. **Found and fixed a real-equivalent-instead-of-own-reading bug on `korean`**: the stored value was 고생 (苦生, "hardship" — a genuinely different, unrelated hanja compound that happens to be near-synonymous), replaced with the honest compositional 신고 (辛's own 신 + 苦's own 고). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 신코 diverges from Sino-Korean 신고 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄋㄎㄛ unique among both words and characters).

**Found and fixed `characters:` disambiguation** (bare 苦 → "苦 (char)," since [[苦]] has its own word page) **and fixed an entirely missing citation on both 辛.md and 苦(char).md's own Words lists**. 辛's own `stand_in` is 辛辣 and 苦's own `stand_in` is 苦 itself, so this compound legitimizes neither constituent.
