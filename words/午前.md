## Notes
- [午](../characters/午.md) "noon" + [前](../characters/前%20(char).md) "before" — no stand-in relationship applies (午's own stand-in is [[正午]]; 前 is its own stand-in).

午前 means "before noon," i.e. a.m. or morning.

**Real bugs found and fixed**: `mandarin`/`cantonese` had been contaminated with the reading of the alias 上午 (shàngwǔ/soeng6 ng5) instead of 午前's own compositional reading — corrected to wǔqián/ng5 cin4. `pos` was blank — filled as 名詞. Japanese ごぜん and Korean 오전 are both standard readings, matching Dan'a'yo's own reading exactly (`kwin: true`). Vietnamese `buổi sáng` is a native paraphrase rather than a direct Sino-Vietnamese calque of 午前 (Vietnamese does not commonly compound this term the same way) — documented, not corrected. No homophones (注音 ㄛㄐㄝㄋ unique across both words and characters).
