## Notes
- [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day, sun" + [食](../characters/食%20(char).md) "eat" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 日 and 食's own stand-ins are themselves). Literally "the sun being eaten."

日食 means solar eclipse.

Mandarin rìshí, Cantonese jat6 sik6, Japanese にっしょく, Korean 일식, and Vietnamese nhật thực are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄧㄊㄙㄧㄎ unique across both words and characters).
