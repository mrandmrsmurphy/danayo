## Notes
- [窒](../characters/窒.md) "suffocate, choke" + [素](../characters/素.md) "element" — literally "suffocation-element," nitrogen (nitrogen does not support respiration or combustion).
- Not the Chinese new character of 氮氣; not a neologism — 窒素 is itself an older, genuine Sino-Japanese-origin term, distinct from the modern mainland Mandarin single-character coinage 氮 (dàn).

Japanese ちっそ (chisso) is the real, standard Japanese word, following the same 素-suffixed pattern used for other gas elements (酸素, 水素, 弗素). Korean 질소 is likewise a real, standard Sino-Korean compound, genuinely compositional: 窒's own 질 + 素's own 소. Mandarin zhìsù and Cantonese zat6 sou3 are also genuinely compositional, matching 窒's and 素's own stored citations exactly — an older Sinitic calque of the same "suffocation-element" logic, still attested despite the modern PRC-standard 氮. Vietnamese nitơ is an international loanword, unrelated to either character's own citations.

`kwin` is false: 窒 alone diverges from Korean on its own character page, which alone is enough to make the AND-rule false; 素 is independently kwin-true. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄝㄊㄙㄛ unique among both words and characters).
