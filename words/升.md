## Notes
- [升 (char)](../characters/升%20(char).md) "litre"

升 is 象形, depicting a dipper or measuring ladle (compare [[斗]], a related but larger measuring vessel). It names both the traditional volume unit (ten 升 make one 斗) and, by extension, the modern metric litre. It also carries an unrelated but equally common sense "to rise, ascend, promote" ([[晋升]], "to be promoted"; 升旗, "to raise a flag"), the two senses sharing the same character via the general idea of a quantity filling and rising within a container.

Mandarin shēng, Cantonese sing1, and Japanese しょう (shō, on'yomi, largely confined to formal/literary contexts) alongside native ます (masu, "a traditional Japanese measuring box") all preserve the volume-measure sense. Korean 승 (seung) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 되 (doe, "a traditional Korean measuring container"). Vietnamese has two attested readings: thăng (the more classical/formal reading) and thưng (the vernacular name for the same traditional unit, especially in southern dialect as "thâng") — both genuinely attested, not a contamination case.

This word shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading (sung/숭/ㄙㄨㄫ) with [[乗]] ("to ride; to multiply," already perfected — its own callout already cross-links here from earlier in this sweep).
