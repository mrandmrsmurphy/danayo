## Notes
- [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" + [事 (char)](../characters/事%20(char).md) "affair, matter" — "human affairs, ways of the world" generally, and "human resources/personnel" in a modern institutional sense (paralleling nhân sự, standard Vietnamese for HR/personnel). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 닌지 diverges from Sino-Korean 인사 in the second syllable's vowel — the same divergence shared with its homophone [[仁慈]].

Also fixed casing on `vietnamese` (was capitalized "Nhân sự", corrected to lowercase to match vault convention).
