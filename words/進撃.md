## Etymology
- [進](../characters/進.md) "advance, progress" + [撃 (char)](../characters/撃%20(char).md) "strike, hit, beat"

## Notes
Mandarin has two attested pronunciations (jìnjī/jìnjí, reflecting 撃's own readings), cantonese zeon3 gik1, japanese しんげき, and korean 진격 are all directly attested standard readings. **Found and fixed a real-equivalent-instead-of-own-reading bug on `vietnamese`**: the stored value was "tấn công," the everyday Vietnamese verb for "attack" (unrelated compositionally — 撃's own reading is kích, not công) — corrected to the honest compositional "tấn kích" (進's own tấn + 撃's own kích). `进击` is the standard simplified-character spelling variant (進→进, 撃→击), correctly listed as an alias. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 진걱 diverges from Sino-Korean 진격 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄋㄍㄝㄎ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 進's `stand_in` (進行) nor 撃's own `stand_in` points to this compound.
