## Notes
- [削 (char)](../characters/削%20(char).md) "scrape; whittle; trim"

削 combines [[刀]] ("knife") with phonetic [[肖]]. It means "to scrape, pare, whittle away" — literally shaving material off with a blade — extending to "to reduce, cut down, strip away": [[削除]] ("to erase, delete"), 削減 (xuējiǎn, "to cut, reduce [a budget, workforce]"), 削奪 (xuēduó, "to strip away, deprive [someone of rank/power]").

Mandarin xuē, Cantonese soek3, and Japanese さく/しょう (saku/shō, on'yomi, fully productive: 削除 さくじょ "deletion") alongside native けず(る) (kezu(ru), "to shave, pare, whittle," the everyday verb) all preserve the sense. Korean 삭 (sak) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 깎다 (kkakda, "to shave, pare, cut down"). Vietnamese tước is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in tước đoạt ("to strip away, deprive") and tước bỏ ("to remove, strip"); the character page's other three stored candidates (tướt, tược, tượt) are unrelated and look like corpus noise.
