## Notes
- [捕](../characters/捕%20(char).md) "catch, grab" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [捕](../characters/捕%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

捕 means to catch or grab.

Mandarin bǔ, Cantonese bou6, Japanese ほ, Korean 포, and Vietnamese bổ are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones at the word level (注音 ㄅㄛ is shared with ten bound characters — 圃/哺/布/歩/補/簿/菩/蒲/譜/葡 — none of which has an independent word at this exact reading).
