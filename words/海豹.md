## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [豹](../characters/豹.md) "leopard, panther" — no stand-in relationship applies (海's own stand-in is [[海洋]]; 豹's own is [[豹猫]]).

海豹 means seal (the animal).

Mandarin hǎibào and Cantonese hoi2 paau3 are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 海's and 豹's own stored citations. Japanese アザラシ, Korean 물범, and Vietnamese hải cẩu ("sea dog") are the real, standard native words for "seal" in each language — the same real-world-species-name convention already established on [[龍蝦]]/[[河馬]]. Fixed real bugs: `korean` was a garbled comma-joined pair (물범, 물개) mixing "seal" with the unrelated animal "sea lion" — corrected to just 물범. `vietnamese` had been dấu niêm, a mistranslation using "seal" in the stamp/mark sense rather than the animal — corrected to hải cẩu. Added the missing `kwin: false` (諺文 해뱟 vs the wholly unrelated native Korean 물범). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄧㄅ⼘ㄨ unique across both words and characters).
