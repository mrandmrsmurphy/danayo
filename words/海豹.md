## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [豹](../characters/豹.md) "leopard, panther" — no stand-in relationship applies (海's own stand-in is [[海洋]]; 豹's own is [[豹猫]]).

海豹 means seal (the animal).

Mandarin hǎibào and Cantonese hoi2 paau3 are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 海's and 豹's own stored citations. Japanese アザラシ, Korean 물범, and Vietnamese hải cẩu ("sea dog") are the real, standard native words for "seal" in each language — the same real-world-species-name convention already established on [[龍蝦]]/[[河馬]].
