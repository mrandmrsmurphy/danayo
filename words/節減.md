## Notes
- [節 (char)](../characters/節%20(char).md) "node, knuckle, joint, holiday" + [減](../characters/減.md) "subtract, reduce, decrease, diminish" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (節's is [[節]] itself, 減's is [[減算]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: true` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: true`.

節減 means to economize or cut down usage/expenditure — 節 here carries its extended sense of "moderation, restraint" (as in 節約, "thrift, frugality") combined with 減's "reduce."

Mandarin jiéjiǎn, Cantonese zit3 gaam2, Japanese せつげん (setsugen), and Korean 절감 (jeolgam) are all standard, fully compositional readings, each the ordinary everyday word for "economize, reduce" in its language. Vietnamese tiết giảm is likewise compositional and is itself a common Vietnamese term for "to cut down, economize."
