## Notes
- [雨 (char)](../characters/雨%20(char).md) "rain" + [中 (char)](../characters/中%20(char).md) "-ing"

雨中 means "in the rain," describing an action or scene taking place while it rains. Mandarin 雨中 (yǔzhōng), Cantonese 雨中 (jyu5zung1), Japanese 雨中 (うちゅう, a more formal/literary register than the everyday 雨の中, ame no naka), Korean 우중 (ujung, often paired or interchanged with the native 빗속, "inside the rain," in everyday speech), and Sino-Vietnamese vũ trung (a poetic, literary register) all preserve this meaning across the CJKV sphere.
