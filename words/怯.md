## Notes
- [怯](../characters/怯%20(char).md) "cowardly, timid" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

怯 means cowardly, timid, or afraid.

Mandarin qiè, Cantonese hip3, Japanese きょう, Korean 겁, and Vietnamese khiếp are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄎㄚㄆ/kab/캅) with the unrelated [[恰]] ("exactly, just").
