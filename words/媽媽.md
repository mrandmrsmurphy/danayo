## Notes
- [媽](../characters/媽.md) "mom" (reduplicated) — 媽's own `stand_in` points to a placeholder (名専字), not here.
- Korean 마마 is a real, if rare/archaic and formal-register Sino-Korean reading for "mom" (verified via search) — modern everyday Korean uses the native 엄마 instead; 마마 is more commonly encountered today in its unrelated historical senses ("Your Majesty," or archaically "smallpox"). The `japanese` field is omitted: Japanese normally uses the katakana loanword ママ (mama) or native お母さん, not a kanji compound built from 媽 (which is 表外字, outside the standard character set) — no genuine 媽媽 kanji word was found. The `vietnamese` field is omitted for the same reason: everyday Vietnamese uses the native mẹ/má (the latter, coincidentally, sounding close to Mandarin māma but with no direct etymological relationship), not a Sino-Vietnamese calque of 媽媽.
