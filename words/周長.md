## Notes
- [周](../characters/周.md) "circumference" + [長](../characters/長%20(char).md) "length" — no stand-in relationship applies (周's own stand-in is [[圓周]]; 長 is its own stand-in). Synonymous with [[周囲]].

周長 means perimeter or circumference — the total length of a boundary.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `cantonese` was missing a space (zau1coeng4 → zau1 coeng4). Filled blank `vietnamese: chu trường` (verified). Japanese しゅうちょう is a standard reading; Korean uses the native 둘레 rather than the compositional 주장, likely to avoid collision with the common unrelated word 주장 ("claim, assertion," from different hanja 主張). **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 周長 and [[周章]] ("troubled, frightened") happen to share an identical reading because 長 and 章 coincidentally share the same Dan'a'yo phonology (jang/장) despite being unrelated characters — standardized the reciprocal callout on both pages.
