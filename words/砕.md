## Notes
- [砕](../characters/砕%20(char).md) "smash, break" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 砕 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

砕 means to smash, shatter, or break into small pieces — 破砕 ("to shatter"), 粉砕 ("to crush, pulverize; figuratively, to utterly defeat"), and the idiom 粉骨砕身 ("to have one's bones ground and body smashed," i.e. utter self-sacrifice, sparing no effort).

Mandarin suì, Cantonese seoi3, Japanese さい (sai), and Korean 쇄 (swae) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese toái likewise traces to the same Sino root, though everyday Vietnamese more often uses native vỡ or đập vỡ for "to smash, break."
