## Etymology
- [遅 (char)](../characters/遅%20(char).md) "late, slow" + [到](../characters/到.md) "arrive"

## Notes
Mandarin chídào and cantonese ci4 dou3 are directly attested standard readings. Japanese ちとう, korean 지도, and vietnamese trì đáo are all honest compositional readings (遅's own CHI/지/trì + 到's own TOU/도/đáo); real everyday usage in each language instead favors the different (though related) compound 遅刻/遲刻 ("be late," using 刻 rather than 到). `遲到`/`迟到` are the standard traditional/simplified spelling variants (遅→遲/迟), correctly listed as aliases. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 츼닷 diverges from Sino-Korean 지도 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄜㄉㄚㄨ unique among both words and characters).

**Found and fixed `characters:` disambiguation** (bare 遅 → "遅 (char)," since [[遅]] has its own word page) **and removed two bad-aliases-entries**: `遅刻`/`遲刻` are genuinely different compounds (刻, not 到), not spelling variants of 遅到. Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 遅's `stand_in` (遅 itself) nor 到's own `stand_in` (到達) points to this compound.
