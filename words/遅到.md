## Etymology
- [遅 (char)](../characters/遅%20(char).md) "late, slow" + [到](../characters/到.md) "arrive"

## Notes
Mandarin chídào and cantonese ci4 dou3 are directly attested standard readings. Japanese ちとう, korean 지도, and vietnamese trì đáo are all honest compositional readings (遅's own CHI/지/trì + 到's own TOU/도/đáo); real everyday usage in each language instead favors the different (though related) compound 遅刻/遲刻 ("be late," using 刻 rather than 到). `遲到`/`迟到` are the standard traditional/simplified spelling variants (遅→遲/迟), correctly listed as aliases.
