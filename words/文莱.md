## Notes
- [文](../characters/文.md) "culture" + [莱](../characters/莱.md) "Penglai" — a transliteration of the name of the country, so the characters carry no meaning of their own; no stand-in relationship applies (文's own stand-in is [[文化]], 莱's is [[蓬莱]]). The alias 文萊 is the traditional spelling, since 萊 is an alias of 莱, and 汶莱 and 汶萊 are the variants with 汶, an alias of 文.

文莱 is Brunei (Brunei Darussalam), the sultanate on the north coast of Borneo ([[婆羅]]). English Wiktionary gives the etymology as from Malay Brunei, borrowed through Hokkien, where 文 is pronounced with a b- initial. It marks 文萊 as used chiefly in mainland China, with 汶萊 in Taiwan, Hong Kong, Brunei, Singapore and Malaysia; the full name is 文萊達魯薩蘭國 in the mainland and 汶萊達魯薩蘭國 elsewhere, neither a word in the vault. It quotes Zhang Xie's *Dongxi yang kao* (1618): "Brunei, that is the country of Boluo, where the Eastern Ocean ends and the Western Ocean begins."

Mandarin Wénlái and Cantonese man4 loi4 are as at English Wiktionary. Japanese ブルネイ and Korean 브루나이 are the ordinary names of the country (from memory, not re-checked), so `kwin` is false. The `vietnamese` field holds the compositional Hán-Việt reading văn lai (each character as its page gives it; not attested for this name).

No homophones (注音 ㄇㄨㄋㄌㄚㄧ unique across words and characters, checked 2026-10-12). The 注音 needs no syllable-boundary dot.
