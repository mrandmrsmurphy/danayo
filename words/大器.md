## Notes
- [大](../characters/大%20(char).md) "great" + [器](../characters/器.md) "vessel, talent" — no stand-in relationship applies (大 is its own stand-in; 器's own is [[容器]]).

大器 means a person of great talent or treasure — literally "great vessel," a person of significant potential (大器晩成 "great talents mature late").

Japanese たいき, Korean 대기, Mandarin dàqì, and Cantonese daai6 hei3 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese đại khí is a standard reading. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄧㄎㄧㄜ unique across both words and characters).
