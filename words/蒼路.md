## Notes
- [蒼 (char)](../characters/蒼%20(char).md) "blue, green, grey" is separately legitimized by [[蒼]] itself. [[路]] "road" is separately legitimized by [[道路]] — no cranberry. Here 路 is used as a `借代字` (homophone substitute glyph) for 鷺 ("heron," listed in `characters/路.md`'s own `aliases`), so 蒼路 represents 蒼鷺 (grey heron), matching the real bird name.
