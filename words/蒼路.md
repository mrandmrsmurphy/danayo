## Notes
- [蒼 (char)](../characters/蒼%20(char).md) "blue, green, grey" is separately legitimized by [[蒼]] itself. [[路]] "road" is separately legitimized by [[道路]] — no cranberry. Here 路 is used as a `代用字` (homophone substitute glyph) for 鷺 ("heron," listed in `characters/路.md`'s own `aliases`), so 蒼路 represents 蒼鷺 (grey heron), matching the real bird name.

Mandarin cānglù matches the real bird name 苍鹭. Japanese あおさぎ, korean 왜가리, and vietnamese con diệc are all the real, standard names for "grey heron" in each language — legitimate real-term substitutions, not compositional.
