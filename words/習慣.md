## Notes
- [習](../characters/習.md) "practice" + [慣](../characters/慣.md) "to be accustomed" — `characters/慣.md`'s own `stand_in` points here, meaning 慣 does not stand independently outside this compound. `kwin: true` per the AND-rule: both constituent characters are individually `kwin: true` (previously stored incorrectly as `false`, contradicting the AND-rule — corrected, the same class of bug found on [[組織]] earlier this sweep).
- A clean, directly parallel compound in meaning across the sphere — Mandarin xíguàn, Cantonese zaap6 gwaan3, Japanese しゅうかん, Korean 습관, and Vietnamese tập quán all converge on "habit; custom; accustomed to."
- **Real bugs found and fixed**: this word's own `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` had been storing both constituents wrong — 習's syllable as sib/십/ㄙㄧㄆ instead of its own authoritative sǝb/습/ㄙㄜㄆ (matching six other sibling compounds: [[練習]], [[学習]], [[復習]], [[補習]], [[予習]], [[習俗]]), and 慣's syllable as gwam/괌/ㄍ⺢ㄇ instead of its own authoritative gwan/관/ㄍ⺢ㄋ (matching its other two compounds, [[慣用]]/[[慣用句]]) — corrected to sǝbgwan/습관/ㄙㄜㄆㄍ⺢ㄋ. No documentation anywhere justified either divergence; both were plain data bugs. (This also resolves the "scattered inconsistency" previously noted here: [[学習]] and [[復習]] were already storing the correct 諺文/注音, just with a stale `羅馬字` spelling — see those pages' own fixes.)
