## Notes
- [季](../characters/季.md) "season, youngest" + [春](../characters/春%20(char).md) "spring" — no stand-in relationship applies (季's own stand-in is [[季節]]; 春's own is itself).

Third and final month of spring. 季 denotes the youngest or last of three — the close of a season's arc. Corresponds to [[辰月]].

Mandarin jìchūn, Cantonese gwai3 ceon1, Korean 계춘, Japanese きしゅん, and Vietnamese Quý Xuân are all standard, fully compositional readings.
