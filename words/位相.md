## Notes
- [位](../characters/位.md) "position, status" + [相](../characters/相.md) "appearance, phase" — "phase," in the physics/math sense (a wave's or periodic function's position within its cycle, as in 位相差 "phase difference"). Directly attested and standard across mandarin wèixiàng, japanese いそう, korean 위상. `vietnamese: pha` is the standard Vietnamese physics term for this sense (as in độ lệch pha, "phase difference"), corrected from the original overly-specific "pha sóng" ("wave phase").
