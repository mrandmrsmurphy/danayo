## Notes
- [粉 (char)](../characters/粉%20(char).md) "powder" + [末](../characters/末.md) "end, tip" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (粉's is [[粉]] itself, 末's is [[末端]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 末 is individually `kwin: false`.

**Fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字` had bunmad, using this character's own phonetic-component reading (分's, not 粉's own) — corrected to funmad, matching 粉's own corrected fun/뿐/ㄈㄨㄋ (`諺文`/`注音` were already correctly using the corrected form). Also cleaned up `korean`, which had comma-joined 분말 with 粉's own *native* gloss 가루 ("powder," a native Korean word) — trimmed to just 분말.

粉末 means fine powder, an emphatic doubling of 粉 with 末 ("tip, fragment") reinforcing the sense of something ground down to its smallest particles.

Mandarin fěnmò, Cantonese fan2 mut6, Japanese ふんまつ (funmatsu), and Korean 분말 (bunmal) are all standard, fully compositional readings, each the ordinary everyday word for "fine powder" in its language. Vietnamese phấn mạt is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form.
