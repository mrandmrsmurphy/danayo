## Notes
- [高 (char)](../characters/高%20(char).md) "high" + [綿](../characters/綿.md) "cotton, silk floss" — characters chosen for sound, not sense, in a phonetic transcription of "Khmer"; 高綿/高棉 is a still-current, if more formal/historical, Chinese name for Cambodia alongside the more common modern 柬埔寨 (Jiǎnpǔzhài).

Mandarin Gāomián and Cantonese gou1min4 are the real Sino names for Cambodia. Japanese カンボジア (Kanbojia) and Korean 캄보디아 (Kambodia) both instead use the modern phonetic loanword from "Cambodia"/"Cambodge" rather than a Sino-reading of these characters — the same pattern seen with [[馬来西亜]]. Sino-Vietnamese Cao Miên is the standard, still commonly used Vietnamese name for Cambodia (alongside the native Campuchia).
