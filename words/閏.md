## Notes
- [閏](characters/閏%20(char).md) "intercalary" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 閏 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

閏 means intercalary (as in 閏年 "leap year," 閏月 "intercalary month").

Mandarin rùn, Cantonese jeon6, Korean 윤, and Vietnamese nhuận (the first-listed value among 閏's own citation) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 閏's own stored citations exactly. Added the missing `japanese`(じゅん, on'yomi JUN)/`hsk_level`(無, matching 閏's own "no HSK level" citation) fields, reformatted `characters` to a proper block-list with the "(char)" suffix.

**Real homophone found**: `注音 ㄋㄨㄋ`/`눈`/`nun` is also carried by [[潤]] ("soft, smooth, wet, increasing") — completed both pages together with reciprocal callouts.
