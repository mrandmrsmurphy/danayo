## Notes
- [[二十]] "twenty" + [八 (char)](../characters/八%20(char).md) "eight" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — the twenty-eighth day of the month; the last day of February in common years.

Mandarin èrshíbā rì, Cantonese ji6 sap6 baat3 jat6, Japanese にじゅうはちにち, and Korean 이십팔일 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese filled with ngày hai mươi tám (native "day twenty-eight"), matching the same native-calendar-date convention already established on the rest of this [[二十一日|二十N日]] cluster.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 늬십받닏 diverges from Sino-Korean 이십팔일, since all four constituent characters independently carry `kwin: false` on their own character pages.
