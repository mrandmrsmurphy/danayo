## Etymology
- [甜](../characters/甜.md) "sweet" + [菜](../characters/菜.md) "vegetable" — no stand-in relationship applies (甜's own stand-in is [[名専字]]; 菜's own is [[野菜]]).

甜菜 means sugar beet.

Mandarin tiáncài, Cantonese tim4 coi3, and Japanese てんさい (TEN+SAI) were already correct and fully compositional. Korean 사탕무 ("sugar radish") is the real, standard Korean term. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with củ cải đường ("sugar radish"), the real, standard Vietnamese term, following the same real-word convention as Korean. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄧㄇㄑㄚㄧ unique across both words and characters).
