## Notes
- [特](../characters/特.md) "special, distinguished" + [徴](../characters/徴.md) "sign, mark" (alias 徵) — no stand-in relationship applies (特's own stand-in is [[特別]]; 徴's own is [[徴税]]; transitivity fails).

特徴 means a distinguishing feature: the mark by which a thing is recognized or told apart from others, as in "the features of a style" or "a species' distinguishing traits". It is a near-synonym of [[特点]], but the two lean apart. 特点 is the plain "characteristic, point", often a good point; 特徴 is the more analytic "diagnostic mark", the sign that identifies.

徴 here is its older sense of "sign, token, evidence" (as in 象徴, "symbol"), not "to summon, levy" as in [[徴税]]. Together 特徴 says "a mark that stands out". The word is standard in Mandarin (tèzhēng), Japanese (とくちょう) and Korean (특징), where it is the usual word for "feature"; Cantonese dak6 zing1 and Vietnamese đặc trưng are compositional and unchecked.

The file is filed under the shinjitai 徴, with the traditional 特徵 as an alias.
