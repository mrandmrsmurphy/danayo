## Notes
- [十](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" + [一](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" + [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — no stand-in relationship applies (all three characters are independent Dan'a'yo entries, each its own stand-in).

十一日 names the 11th day of the month.

Japanese じゅういちにち, Korean 십일일, and Vietnamese thập nhất nhật (matching the established convention on sibling day-words like [[七日]] "thất nhật") are all standard, fully compositional readings.
