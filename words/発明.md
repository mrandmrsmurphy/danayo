## Notes
- [発 (char)](../characters/発%20(char).md) "issue forth, discharge" + [明 (char)](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright" — literally "to bring forth into the light." 発's own stand-in is [[発]] itself and 明's is [[明]] itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; neither constituent is legitimized by this word.

発明 is the standard word for "invention" across the whole Sinosphere, extending 発's "issue forth, emerge" sense to the emergence of a new idea or device into the world, illuminated (明) rather than hidden. It names both the act of inventing and, as a noun, the invention itself.

Mandarin fāmíng, Cantonese faat3 ming4, Japanese はつめい (hatsumei), Korean 발명 (balmyeong), and Vietnamese phát minh are all fully compositional and semantically stable — an unusually clean case where every language in the sphere preserves the same transparent, literal meaning with no drift, narrowing, or native-coinage substitution.
