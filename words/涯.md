## Notes
- [涯](characters/涯%20(char).md) "horizon, shore, border" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 涯 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

涯 means horizon, shore, border (as in 生涯, "one's whole life," or 天涯, "the ends of the earth").

Mandarin yá, Cantonese ngaai4, Korean 애, and Vietnamese nhai are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 涯's own stored citations exactly.
