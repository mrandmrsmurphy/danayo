## Notes
- [監](../characters/監.md) "oversee, supervise" + [禁](../characters/禁.md) "restrict, confine" — literally "to confine under watch."

監禁 means to imprison, detain, or confine someone, whether in a formal prison setting or otherwise (e.g. 軟禁, "house arrest," literally "soft confinement"). Mandarin jiānjìn, Cantonese gaam1 gam3, Japanese かんきん (kankin), and Korean 감금 (gamgeum) are all fully compositional and used interchangeably with more specific terms like [[監獄]] depending on context — 監禁 emphasizes the act of confining, while 監獄 names the institution itself. Vietnamese giám cấm is compositional; the more common everyday Vietnamese verb for "to imprison, detain" is giam cầm.
