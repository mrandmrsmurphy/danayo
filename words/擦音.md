## Notes
- [擦](../words/擦.md) "rub, scrape" (stand-in: itself) + [音 (char)](../characters/音.md) "sound" (stand-in: [[音楽]]) — a fricative consonant.

擦音 means a fricative consonant — a sound made by forcing air through a narrow channel, producing friction. Mandarin cāyīn is a real, standard phonetics term. Cantonese caat3 jam1 is a straightforward compositional reading. Japanese さつおん and Korean 찰음 are compositional only, not independently verified as standard — Japanese phonetics standardly says 摩擦音 (masatsuon) and Korean says 마찰음, both built from a different root (摩, "friction," not yet a Dan'a'yo word) rather than bare 擦. Vietnamese sát âm is likewise compositional; the real term is "âm xát" (reversed order).

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot**: the concatenated string `ㄑㄚㄊㄨㄇ` admits two legal partitions — the intended 擦(ㄑㄚㄊ)+音(ㄨㄇ), and a false alternate (ㄑㄚ+ㄊㄨㄇ) that crosses the true boundary — so the dot at the true 擦|音 boundary is required: ㄑㄚㄊ·ㄨㄇ.
