## Notes
- [油 (char)](../characters/油%20(char).md) "oil" + [画](../characters/画.md) "picture"

油画 is an oil painting: a painting in oil paints. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yóuhuà as "oil painting" and Japanese ゆが. Korean 유화 is the same word. Cantonese jau4 waa6-2 matches.

It is a kind of [絵画](絵画.md), painting, and the water-based kind is the [水彩](水彩.md).
