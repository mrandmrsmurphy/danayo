## Notes
- [種](../characters/種.md) "kind, type, seed, species" + [類](../characters/類.md) "kind, sort, category" — `#cranberry`: both characters' own `stand_in` point to this exact compound (種's and 類's alike), so transitivity holds (A=B=AB) and both are legitimized as independent Dan'a'yo entries specifically by this word. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 類 is individually `kwin: false`.

種類 is the general word for "type, kind, category" across the sphere — the ordinary hypernym for classifying things into groups. It also functions as one of the correlative words (this vault's `tags: correlative`); 類's own character page notes that 類 is the only correlative-chart member that can never stand alone independently, unlike most others in that set.

Mandarin zhǒnglèi, Cantonese zung2 leoi6, Japanese しゅるい (shurui), and Korean 종류 (jongnyu) are all standard, fully compositional readings, each an extremely common everyday word for "type/kind" in its language. Vietnamese chủng loại is likewise compositional and equally common.
