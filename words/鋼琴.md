## Notes
- [鋼](../characters/鋼.md) "steel" + [琴 (char)](../characters/琴%20(char).md) "zither; stringed instrument"

鋼琴 is a piano: a keyboard instrument whose strings are struck by hammers. Wiktionary lists Mandarin gāngqín, "piano", and Cantonese gong3 kam4 matches. Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese write the word in sound, ピアノ, 피아노 and pi-a-nô, so the Chinese form is the only one built from characters. Korean 강금 is the reading of the characters.

It is a modern instrument named for its steel strings, set beside the [琴](琴.md), guqin, and the [提琴](提琴.md), violin family. Its keys are the [鍵盤](鍵盤.md), keyboard.
