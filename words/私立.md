## Notes
- [私](../characters/私.md) "personal, private" + [立 (char)](../characters/立%20(char).md) "stand" — 私's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 立's own is [[立]] itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 私 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 私 is individually `kwin: false`.

私立 describes an institution — most often a school (私立学校, "private school") — established and funded privately rather than by the state, the direct counterpart to 国立/公立 ("national/public"). It is one of the most common everyday adjectival compounds using 私 across the sphere.

Mandarin sīlì, Japanese しりつ (shiritsu — note the common homophone clash with 市立 "municipal," also read しりつ, occasionally disambiguated in speech as わたくしりつ), and Korean 사립 (sarip) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Cantonese has two attested forms: si1 laap6 (the colloquial pronunciation actually used) and si1 lap6 (the literal character-by-character reading). Vietnamese tư lập is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form.
