## Notes
- [灰 (char)](../characters/灰%20(char).md) "ashes" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 灰 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

灰 means ashes: the powdery residue left after something burns (灰燼, "ashes and embers"; 煙灰, "cigarette ash"; 灰塵, "dust"), and by extension the color gray (灰色, "gray"), or dejected and hopeless (灰心, "disheartened"). It is also this vault's periodic-table abbreviation prefix for calcium via <ruby>[[石灰素]]<rt>ㄙㄝㄎㄏㄛㄧㄙㄛ</rt></ruby>, drawing on 石灰 "lime". The graph is classed 会意 in the vault, combining 火 "fire" with a hand-like element.

Mandarin huī, Cantonese fui1, Japanese かい (石灰/せっかい, "lime"; 灰燼/かいじん, "ashes"), Korean 회 (회색, "gray"; 석회, "lime") and Vietnamese khôi are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native noun 灰 (はい) for the everyday word, so on'yomi かい is bound to compounds; Vietnamese uses the native tro and Korean the native 재.
