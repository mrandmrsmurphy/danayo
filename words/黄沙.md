## Notes
- [黄 (char)](../characters/黄%20(char).md) "yellow" + [沙 (char)](../characters/沙%20(char).md) "sand, gravel" — no `#cranberry`: both 黄's and 沙's own `stand_in` point to themselves; neither points here.

Mandarin huángshā, Cantonese wong4saa1, and Korean 황사 are all standard, fully compositional readings for the yellow dust/Asian dust phenomenon. Japanese こうさ (kōsa, using 黄's KOU reading rather than OU) matches the real, standard Japanese term for this weather event — though modern Japanese conventionally writes it with the related character 砂 (黄砂) rather than 沙. Sino-Vietnamese hoàng sa is the compositional reading; note that "Hoàng Sa" is also the Vietnamese name for the Paracel Islands, an unrelated coincidental homograph.
