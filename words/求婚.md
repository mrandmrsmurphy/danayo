## Notes
- [求](../characters/求.md) "seek, ask for" + [婚](../characters/婚.md) "marriage" — "seek marriage," to propose. Their own `stand_in` values: 求's is [[要求]]; 婚's is [[結婚]]; none points here, so 求婚 is an independent compound.

求婚 is to propose: to ask someone to marry you. Mandarin qiúhūn, Cantonese kau4 fan1, Japanese きゅうこん and Korean 구혼 all use it, and Vietnamese cầu hôn does too. It follows [[求愛]] (to court) and leads to [[婚約]] (engagement) and [[結婚]] (marriage).
