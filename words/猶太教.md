## Notes
- [猶](../characters/猶%20(char).md) "furthermore" + [太](../characters/太%20(char).md) "great, extreme" + [教](../characters/教.md) "teaching, religion" — 猶太 is a phonetic transliteration of "Judea/Jewish" (see [[猶太]]), and 教 "teaching" makes it a religion, on the pattern of [[回教]] and [[基督教]]. None of the three characters' own `stand_in` points here (教's is [[教授]]), so 猶太教 is an independent compound.

猶太教 is Judaism. Mandarin Yóutàijiào, Cantonese jau4 taai3 gaau3 and Vietnamese Do Thái giáo all use it. Japanese says ユダヤ教 (a katakana loan) and Korean 유대교 (with the Sino-Korean 유태교 as the hanja form), so those two fields hold the readings of the characters.

Compare [[基督教]] (Christianity), [[回教]] (Islam) and [[佛教]] (Buddhism).
