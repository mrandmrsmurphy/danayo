## Notes
- [浪](../characters/浪.md) "waves, wasteful" + [費](../characters/費%20(char).md) "expense, spend" — "wasteful spending," to waste. Their own `stand_in` values: 浪's is [[波浪]]; 費's is itself; none points here, so 浪費 is an independent compound.

浪費 is to waste: to spend or use something carelessly and to no good end. Mandarin làngfèi, Cantonese long6 fai3, Japanese ろうひ and Korean 랑비 (North Korean 랑비, South Korean 낭비) all use it, and Vietnamese lãng phí does too. It is the opposite of [[利用]] (to use for profit). Compare [[費]] (to spend).
