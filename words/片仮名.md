## Etymology
- [片 (char)](../characters/片%20(char).md) "slice, flake" + [仮](../characters/仮.md) "borrow" + [名 (char)](../characters/名%20(char).md) "name" — no stand-in relationship applies (片's own stand-in is itself; 仮's own is [[仮借]]; 名's own is itself). Here 仮 is used in its "borrowed" sense as a graphic variant of 假 (jiǎ) rather than its own stored rare reading fǎn — a polyphonic character-level gap, matching the mandarin reading already correctly used (cf. [[為人]]).

片仮名 means katakana, the angular Japanese syllabary.

Fixed a serious contamination bug in `cantonese`: jat6 bun2 dou1 (the unrelated word 日本刀 "Japanese sword") had been substituted here entirely — corrected to pin3 gaa2 ming4, matching the 假(jiǎ)-sense compositional pattern already used by `mandarin`. Removed the wrong alias 日本刀 (unrelated) and added 片假名, the legitimate traditional-character alias. Japanese かたかな, Korean 가타카나 (a real transliteration loanword, like [[熊猫]]'s パンダ), and Vietnamese katakana (also a real transliteration loanword) were already correct. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄝㄋㄍㄚㄇㄧㄫ unique across both words and characters).
