## Notes
- [片 (char)](../characters/片%20(char).md) "slice, flake" + [仮](../characters/仮.md) "borrow" + [名 (char)](../characters/名%20(char).md) "name" — no stand-in relationship applies (片's own stand-in is itself; 仮's own is [[仮借]]; 名's own is itself). Here 仮 is used in its "borrowed" sense as a graphic variant of 假 (jiǎ) rather than its own stored rare reading fǎn — a polyphonic character-level gap, matching the mandarin reading already correctly used (cf. [[為人]]).

片仮名 means katakana, the angular Japanese syllabary.

Mandarin and Cantonese (pin3 gaa2 ming4) follow the 假 (jiǎ) sense of 仮. The traditional spelling 片假名 is an alias. Japanese かたかな is native; Korean 가타카나 and Vietnamese katakana are transliteration loanwords, like パンダ in [[熊猫]].
