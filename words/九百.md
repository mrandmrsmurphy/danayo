## Notes
- [九 (char)](../characters/九%20(char).md) "nine" + [百 (char)](../characters/百%20(char).md) "hundred" — nine hundred, 900.

Plain digit + 百, like [[二百]], [[七百]] and [[八百]]: the hundred is counted by prefixing the digit to 百, with no further grouping word. Japanese uses きゅう, not く, for 九 here. Korean 구백 follows the Sino-Korean reading directly. `vietnamese: cửu bách` is the Sino-Vietnamese compositional reading; everyday Vietnamese uses the native chín trăm for round numbers like this. Compare [[九千]] and [[九十]].
