## Notes
- [画](../characters/画.md) "picture" + [像](../characters/像.md) "image; likeness"

画像 is an image or picture: a likeness drawn, printed or shown on a screen. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin huàxiàng as "portrait" and Japanese がぞう as "a portrait, one's likeness". Korean 화상 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese waa6-2 zoeng6 matches.

It is what the [撮影](撮影.md) produces, and a portrait is a [肖像](肖像.md). The art of painting is [絵画](絵画.md).
