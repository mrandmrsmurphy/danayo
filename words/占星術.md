## Notes
- Transparently two words: [[占星]] "divination" + [[術]] "skill" — as well as three characters: [占](../characters/占%20(char).md) + [星](../characters/星%20(char).md) + [術](../characters/術%20(char).md). No stand-in relationship applies (all three characters are independent Dan'a'yo entries, each its own stand-in).

占星術 means astrology — the practice of divination by the positions and movements of celestial bodies.

**Real bugs found and fixed**: `mandarin`/`cantonese` were missing 術's contribution entirely (zhānxīng/blank → zhānxīngshù/zim1 sing1 seot6). `japanese` was truncated (せんせいじ → せんせいじゅつ). Korean 점성술 and Vietnamese thuật chiêm tinh (also attested in the equivalent order chiêm tinh thuật) are standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄇㄙㄝㄫㄙㄨㄊ unique across both words and characters).
