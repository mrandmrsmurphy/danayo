## Notes
- [口](../characters/口%20(char).md) "mouth" + [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" — "mouth sound," an accent. Their own `stand_in` values: 口's is itself; 音's is [[音楽]]; none points here, so 口音 is an independent compound.

口音 is an accent: the way a person pronounces a language because of where they come from. Mandarin kǒuyīn and Cantonese hau2 jam1 are the ordinary words, and Vietnamese khẩu âm does too. Japanese says 訛り and Korean 사투리 or 억양, so those two fields hold the character readings. Compare [[語気]] (tone of speaking) and [[方言]] (dialect).
