## Notes
- [敷](../characters/敷%20(char).md) "spread" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [敷](../characters/敷%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

敷 means to spread.

Mandarin fū, Cantonese fu1, Japanese ふ, Korean 부, and Vietnamese phu are all standard, fully compositional readings. It is a coincidental homophone of [[賦]] "prose-poetry" (both 注音 ㄈㄨ); eight other candidates sharing this reading (俘/俯/付/斧/孚/府/撫/黼) have no independent word at this exact reading.
