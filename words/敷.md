## Notes
- [敷](../characters/敷%20(char).md) "spread" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [敷](../characters/敷%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

敷 means to spread.

Mandarin fū, Cantonese fu1, Japanese ふ, Korean 부, and Vietnamese phu are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` had all used the wrong initial (푸/pu/ㄆㄨ instead of 뿌/fu/ㄈㄨ, matching the character's own citation) — corrected, and fixed literal `vietnamese: null`. Homophone of [[賦]] "prose-poetry" (both 注音 ㄈㄨ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words, discovered only once both pages' contamination bugs were corrected; checked eight other candidates sharing this reading (俘/俯/付/斧/孚/府/撫/黼) and confirmed none has an independent word at this exact reading.
