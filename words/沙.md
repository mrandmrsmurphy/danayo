## Notes
- [沙 (char)](../characters/沙%20(char).md) "sand; gravel; pebbles" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 沙 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

沙 means sand, gravel, or pebbles: loose granular rock material (沙子, "sand"; 沙灘, "sandy beach"; 沙漠, "desert"; 沙丘, "dune"), and by extension anything fine and granular (沙啞, "hoarse, raspy"). The variant 砂 is listed as an alias, and 沙 is the usual form in Mandarin.

Mandarin shā, Cantonese saa1, Japanese さ (沙漠/さばく, "desert"; 土砂/どしゃ, "earth and sand") with the alternate on'yomi しゃ, Korean 사 (사막, "desert") and Vietnamese sa (sa mạc, "desert") are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native noun 砂 (すな) for the everyday word, so on'yomi さ is bound to compounds, and Korean has native 모래.
