## Notes
- [揄](../characters/揄.md) "raise, lift" + [伽](../characters/伽.md) "gya" — no stand-in relationship applies (揄's own stand-in is [[揶揄]]; 伽's own is name-only, though used here as a common-noun compound).

揄伽 means yoga — this vault's rendering of the Buddhist Sanskrit loanword 瑜伽 (using the phonetically identical 揄 in place of 瑜, which coincidentally shares an identical reading across every language here — Mandarin yú, Cantonese jyu4, Korean 유, Vietnamese du — so no "avoided character" convention was needed beyond the substitution itself).

Mandarin yújiā, Cantonese jyu4 gaa1, Japanese ゆが, Korean 유가, and Vietnamese du già are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `諺文`/`羅馬字` had dropped the coda that 揄's own citation carries (윰/yum, from Middle Chinese) — corrected 유갸/'yugya to 윰갸/'yumgya, matching the already-correct 注音. No homophones (注音 ⼜ㄇㄍ⼘ unique across both words and characters).
