## Notes
- [現 (char)](../characters/現%20(char).md) "appear; present" + [状](../characters/状.md) "form; shape; condition"

現状 is "the current state of affairs; the status quo." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 現狀 (xiànzhuàng, Cantonese jin6 zong6) means "the current situation; present conditions; status quo," with 現況 as a synonym; Japanese げんじょう is written 現状; and Korean 현상 glosses it as "the evident condition or state." The traditional form 現狀 is the alias of this page.

`vietnamese` hiện trạng is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading of the two characters; Wiktionary has no Vietnamese section for this word, so the reading is not independently attested.
