## Notes
- [蹄](../characters/蹄%20(char).md) "hoof" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 蹄 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

蹄 names the hoof of a hoofed animal — horses, cattle, deer, and similar ungulates (馬蹄, "horseshoe, horse hoof").

Mandarin tí, Cantonese tai4, and Korean 제 (je) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading ひづめ (hizume, "hoof") rather than the on-reading テイ (TEI) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese đề is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading. This word is part of a genuine three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read dei/데/ㄉㄝㄧ): [[底]] ("bottom") and [[第]] ("-th, ordinal marker").
