## Notes
- [飢](../characters/飢.md) "hungry" + [餓](../characters/餓.md) "hungry" — `#cranberry`: transitivity holds, as both 飢's own `stand_in` and 餓's own `stand_in` point here to 飢餓 itself (A=B=AB); neither character can stand alone.

飢餓/饥饿 (jī'è) is the standard Mandarin word for "hunger, starvation," and Cantonese 飢餓 (gei1ngo6) likewise. Japanese きが (kiga) is the standard formal term. Korean 기아 (gia) is likewise standard, though everyday spoken Korean also uses the native 굶주림 for the same sense. Sino-Vietnamese cơ ngạ is likewise attested, though the everyday Vietnamese word is the native đói.
