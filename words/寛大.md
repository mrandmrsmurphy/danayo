## Notes
- [寛](../characters/寛.md) "lenient, wide, broad" + [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big"
- This word is the viable form of [寛](../characters/寛.md): the character's meaning (lenient; magnanimous; broad) is identical, but 寛 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 寛大 is its `stand_in` compound.

**Correction (2026-09-12, user-directed):** this page was previously titled `寬大.md`, using 寬 (寛's registered alias, the traditional form) directly in its title while `characters:` correctly cited 寛 — the same class of bug as [[辺疆]]/[[狡滑]]/[[埋蔵]]. Renamed to `寛大.md` accordingly, with `寬大` demoted to an `alias`.
