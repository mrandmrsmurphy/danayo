## Notes
- [溶](../characters/溶.md) "melt, fuse" + [融 (char)](../characters/融%20(char).md) "melt, blend, harmonize" — no stand-in relationship applies (溶's own stand-in is [[溶化]]; 融's own is itself).

溶融 means to melt, fuse.

Mandarin róngróng, Cantonese jung4 jung4, Japanese ようゆう, and Korean 용융 are all standard, fully compositional readings — 溶 and 融 happen to be genuine near-total homophones across the sphere (identical Mandarin róng, identical Cantonese jung4).
