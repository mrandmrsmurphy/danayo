## Notes
- [占 (char)](../characters/占%20(char).md) "to occupy" + [領](../characters/領.md) "territory" — "to capture, occupy, seize (territory)." `vietnamese: chiếm lĩnh` is one of the most common everyday Vietnamese words for this concept (chiếm lĩnh thị trường, "to dominate the market"), a direct match. Filled the blank `mandarin: zhànlǐng` and `cantonese: zim1 ling5`.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `aliases` listed 占領 as its own alias (self-referential, identical to the headword) — removed. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 점링 diverges from Sino-Korean 점령 in the second syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄇㄌㄧㄫ unique among both words and characters).
