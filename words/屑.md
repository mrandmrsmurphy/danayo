## Notes
- [屑 (char)](../characters/屑%20(char).md) "scraps; waste tidbits" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [屑 (char)](../characters/屑%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

屑 names small scraps or fragments — sawdust, crumbs, shavings — and by extension (in 不屑, "to disdain") the sense of something too trivial to bother with.

Japanese せつ, Korean 설, and Vietnamese tiết are all standard readings. **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 屑 and [[舌]] ("tongue") share an identical reading (sed/섣/ㄙㄝㄊ) — the reciprocal callout was already correctly in place on both pages.
