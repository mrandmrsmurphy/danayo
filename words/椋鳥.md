## Notes
- [椋](../characters/椋.md) "starling" + [鳥](../characters/鳥%20(char).md) "bird" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 椋 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

椋鳥 means starling.

Mandarin liángniǎo, Cantonese loeng4 niu5, and Japanese むくどり (both halves read natively — 椋's kun'yomi むく + 鳥's kun'yomi とり, with rendaku とり→どり — the genuine standard Japanese word for this bird) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bugs: `諺文`'s first syllable was 령 instead of 량 (not matching 椋's own citation or the word's own `羅馬字`); `korean` was 찌르레기, a native Korean bird name, not the compositional Sino-Korean form — corrected to 량조. Removed the redundant `品詞` field. `vietnamese` was left blank rather than filled with a placeholder: 椋's own stored citation holds only the null marker "ø," meaning no valid Vietnamese reading is recorded for this character — a character-level gap flagged for future perfecting. No homophones (注音 ㄌ⼘ㄫㄑㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).
