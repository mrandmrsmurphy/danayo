## Notes
- [香](../characters/香.md) "incense" + [川](../characters/川%20(char).md) "river"

香川 is Kagawa, written "fragrant river", from the old Kagawa district. The reading かがわ is the on-reading of 香 (か) plus the native 川 (かわ) voiced to がわ.

Kagawa is the smallest prefecture by area, on the Seto Inland Sea side of Shikoku, with its capital Takamatsu. It is known for Sanuki udon, the garden Ritsurin Kōen, the shrine Kotohira-gū, and the art islands of Naoshima and Teshima.

In Dan'a'yo 香川 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 가가와 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
