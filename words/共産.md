## Notes
- [共 (char)](../characters/共%20(char).md) "together, common" + [産](../characters/産.md) "produce, property" — "communist" (literally "common property," 共産主義, "communism"; 共産黨/共産党, "Communist Party"). Mandarin gòngchǎn, Cantonese gung6 caan2, Japanese きょうさん, Korean 공산, and Vietnamese cộng sản are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 굥산 diverges from Sino-Korean 공산 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼄ㄫㄙㄚㄋ unique among both words and characters).
