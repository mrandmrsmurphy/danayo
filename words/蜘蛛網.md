## Notes
- [蜘](../characters/蜘.md) "spider" + [蛛](../characters/蛛.md) "spider" + [網](../characters/網%20(char).md) "net, web" — "spider net," a spider's web. Their own `stand_in` values: 蜘's is [[蜘蛛]]; 蛛's is [[蜘蛛]]; 網's is itself; none points here, so 蜘蛛網 is an independent compound.

蜘蛛網 is a spider's web: the net a spider spins to catch its prey. Mandarin zhīzhūwǎng and Cantonese zi1 zyu1 mong5 are the ordinary words. Japanese says 蜘蛛の巣 and Korean 거미줄, so those two fields hold the character readings. Compare [[蜘蛛]] (spider) and [[網]] (net); the network "web" is [[網絡]].
