## Notes
- [深](../characters/深.md) "deep" + [刻](../characters/刻.md) "carve, engrave" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 深 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (刻's own stand-in is [[刻印]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

深刻 means profound, deep, serious.

Mandarin shēnkè, Japanese しんこく, and Korean 심각 (深's own 심 + 刻's own 각) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 深's and 刻's own stored citations exactly.
