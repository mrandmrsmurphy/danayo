## Notes
- [技](../characters/技.md) "skill, ability" + [能](../characters/能.md) "talent" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes BOTH characters as independent Dan'a'yo entries: 技's own `stand_in` is 技能, and 能's own `stand_in` is also 技能.

技能 means skill or ability.

Mandarin jìnéng, Cantonese gei6 nang4, Japanese ぎのう, Korean 기능, and Vietnamese kỹ năng are all standard, fully compositional readings.
