## Notes
- [技](../characters/技.md) "skill, ability" + [能](../characters/能.md) "talent" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes BOTH characters as independent Dan'a'yo entries: 技's own `stand_in` is 技能, and 能's own `stand_in` is also 技能.

技能 means skill or ability.

Mandarin jìnéng, Cantonese gei6 nang4, Japanese ぎのう, Korean 기능, and Vietnamese kỹ năng are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `羅馬字` used "nung" instead of "nǝng" (schwa), not matching 能's own citation or this word's own 諺文/注音. Added missing space in `cantonese`. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄨㄧㄋㄜㄫ unique across both words and characters).
