## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [狸 (char)](../characters/狸%20(char).md) "small furry mammal (tanuki, etc.)" — no stand-in relationship applies (海's own stand-in is [[海洋]]; 狸's own is itself).

海狸 means beaver — the real, standard Mandarin term, extending 狸's generic "small furry mammal" sense.

Mandarin hǎilí and Cantonese hoi2 lei4 are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 海's and 狸's own stored citations. Fixed a real bug: `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` had all been contaminated to a naive transcription of 狸's real Mandarin/Korean reading (li/리/ㄌㄧ) instead of 狸's own stored Dan'a'yo reading (르/lǝ/ㄌㄜ) — corrected to hailǝ/해르/ㄏㄚㄧㄌㄜ. Japanese かいり remains compositional (KAI+RI, an on'yomi coincidentally matching the real Sino reading despite the Dan'a'yo-internal divergence). Korean 비버 and Vietnamese hải ly are the real, standard terms (비버 a loanword; hải ly compositional and also real). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄧㄌㄜ unique across both words and characters).
