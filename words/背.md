## Notes
- [背](../characters/背%20(char).md) "back" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 背 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

背 means the back (of the body), as in 背景 (background), 背後 (back, rear), and 背骨 (spine). By extension it means to carry on the back (背包, "carry a bag"), to turn one's back on or go against (背叛, "betray"; 違背, "violate"), and to memorize, from reciting with one's back to the text (背誦, "recite"). The graph is classed under 北 in the vault, a picture of two people back to back, with 肉 "flesh" below.

Mandarin bèi, Cantonese bui3, Japanese はい (背景/はいけい, "background"; 背後/はいご, "rear"; 背信/はいしん, "betrayal"), Korean 배 (배경, 배후), and Vietnamese bối (bối cảnh, "background") are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native nouns 背 (せ), 背中 (せなか) and the verb 背く (そむく) for the everyday words, so on'yomi はい is bound to compounds; Korean has native 등.
