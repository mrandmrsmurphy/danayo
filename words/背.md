## Notes
- [背](../characters/背%20(char).md) "back" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 背 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

背 means the back (of the body), as in 背景 (background), 背後 (back, rear), and 背骨 (spine).

Mandarin bèi, cantonese bui3, and korean 배 (bae) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading せなか (senaka, "the back") rather than the on-reading ハイ (HAI) or the shorter native variants せ/せい for the bare character alone. Vietnamese bối is one of four stored candidates. A genuine three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group with [[杯]] ("cup, cupful") and [[陪]] ("accompany, be with, keep company") — all three coincide at boi/뵈/ㄅㄛㄧ, and in fact share the same Sino-Korean reading 배 as well, despite diverging in Mandarin/Cantonese.
