## Notes
- [八 (char)](../characters/八%20(char).md) "eight" + [万](../characters/万.md) "ten-thousand" — eighty thousand, 80,000.

Digit + 万, like [[二万]] and [[七万]]: the myriad (10,000) is the Sinitic grouping unit, so 80,000 is literally "eight myriad" and is not built through a "thousand" step as in English. Japanese はちまん is the plain concatenation. Korean 팔만 follows the Sino-Korean reading directly. Vietnamese tám vạn uses vạn, which is a live everyday numeral rather than only a classical one. Compare [[二万]] and [[七万]].
