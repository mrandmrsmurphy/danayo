## Notes
- [平](../characters/平.md) "level; flat" + [提](../characters/提.md) "to lift; flick"

Wiktionary has no entry for 平提; it is a compound of 平 "level; flat" and 提, which Wiktionary glosses in Chinese calligraphy as "a rising stroke (㇀)". It names the level flick, the shallower of the two rising strokes, with [[斜提]] the steeper. It is listed on the [[Stroke]] page. Its readings are compositional.
