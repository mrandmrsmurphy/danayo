## Notes
- [平](../characters/平.md) "level; flat" + [提](../characters/提.md) "to lift; flick"

**平提** is the *level flick*, the shallower of the two rising strokes ([[斜提]] being the steeper). The brush presses down to the lower right, pauses briefly, and flicks up to the upper right; Chinese instruction places it mostly as the last stroke of a left-hand component at the lower left of a character, as in 土 and 王. It is a sub-variety of the flick (提), the fifth of the eight principles of the 永字八法, and is listed on the [[Stroke]] page.

The rising stroke is also known in the classical set as 策.

The Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and North Korean forms given here are compositional. No Vietnamese form was found attested, so none is given.
