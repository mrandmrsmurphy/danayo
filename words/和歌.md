## Notes
- [和](../characters/和.md) "Japan" + [歌](../characters/歌.md) "song; poem"

和歌 is waka: the classical Japanese poem, in particular the tanka. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin hégē as "waka" and Japanese わか as "Japanese poetry, waka". Korean 화가 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese wo4 go1 matches.

The general term for Japanese poetry before the haiku, its main form is the [短歌](短歌.md). Chinese-style poetry in Japan is the [漢詩](漢詩.md).
