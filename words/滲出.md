## Notes
- [滲](../characters/滲.md) "soak through" + [出 (char)](../characters/出%20(char).md) "exit" — to soak through and come out

滲出 means to seep or ooze out: a liquid working slowly through a surface and appearing on the other side (汗が滲出する, "sweat oozes out"; 血が滲出する, "blood seeps out").  In medicine the 滲出液 ("exudate") is the fluid that leaks from inflamed tissue, and 滲出性中耳炎 is the standard name of otitis media with effusion.  滲 gives the slow soaking-through and 出 the direction outward, so the word is the outward counterpart of [[滲入]] and a close relative of [[滲漏]].

滲 has `stand_in` 滲入, so this word is not its legitimizer.  Mandarin shènchū, Cantonese sam3 ceot1, Japanese しんしゅつ and Korean 삼출 are the compositional readings, and the alias 渗出 is listed.  `kwin` is false because the Dan'a'yo 솜춛 differs from the Korean 삼출.  No Vietnamese form was found attested, so none is given.
