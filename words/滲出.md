## Notes
- [滲](../characters/滲.md) "soak through" + [出 (char)](../characters/出%20(char).md) "exit" — to soak through and come out

Wiktionary lists 滲出 only under Chinese: "to seep out". The simplified spelling 渗出 is kept as an alias. 滲 has `stand_in` 滲入, so this word is not its legitimizer. It is the outward counterpart of [[滲入]], and a close relative of [[滲漏]].
