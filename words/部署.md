## Notes
- [部 (char)](characters/部%20(char).md) "part" + [署](characters/署.md) "office, bureau" — "government office, department."

Mandarin bùshǔ, cantonese bou6 cyu5, japanese ぶしょ, and korean 부서 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese bộ thự is the honest compositional reading (部's own bộ + 署's own thự); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 봇쇼 diverges from Sino-Korean 부서 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄛㄨㄙ⼄ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 署's own `stand_in` is this exact word (部署), so 署 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 部署; 部's own `stand_in` is instead 部 itself, so this word does not legitimize 部.
