## Notes
- [調](../characters/調.md) "adjust" + [教](../characters/教.md) "teach"

調教 is to tame or train an animal. Chinese has it as a verb meaning to feed and train an animal (tiáojiào), and Japanese as a noun and suru verb for the training of animals (chōkyō). In Dan'a'yo it is the verb for taming, since the natural candidate 馴 is a 名専字 and cannot appear in an ordinary word.
