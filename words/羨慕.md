## Notes
- [羨](../characters/羨.md) "envy, admire" + [慕](../characters/慕.md) "long for, admire" — 羨's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 慕's own is [[思慕]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 羨慕 legitimizes 羨 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 羨 is individually `kwin: false`.

羨慕 means to envy or be jealous of.

Mandarin xiànmù, Cantonese sin6 mou6, Japanese せんぼ (senbo), and Korean 선모 (seonmo) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 羨慕 is itself a common attested word for "envy" in all four languages. Vietnamese tiện mộ is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
