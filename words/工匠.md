## Notes
- [工](../characters/工.md) "work; craft" + [匠](../characters/匠.md) "artisan; craftsman" — this word is the stand-in for [匠](../characters/匠.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

工匠 names a skilled artisan or craftsman — someone whose trade is manual craft (traditionally carpentry, smithing, and similar handwork), as opposed to a general laborer.

Japanese こうしょう, Korean 공장, and Vietnamese công tượng (verified) are all standard readings.
