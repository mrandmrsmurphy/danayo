## Notes
- [工](../characters/工.md) "work; craft" + [匠](../characters/匠.md) "artisan; craftsman" — this word is the stand-in for [匠](../characters/匠.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

工匠 names a skilled artisan or craftsman — someone whose trade is manual craft (traditionally carpentry, smithing, and similar handwork), as opposed to a general laborer.

Japanese こうしょう, Korean 공장, and Vietnamese công tượng (verified) are all standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄫㄑ⺢ㄫ unique across both words and characters).

**Correction (2026-08-27)**: this page's `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` had been badly contaminated — 匠's contribution was stored as 장/jang/ㄐㄚㄫ, which is actually [[場]]'s own reading (confirmed by cross-checking 場's own character page, an exact match), not 匠's real citation form (촹/cwang/ㄑ⺢ㄫ, confirmed against its own MC derivation and syllable page). The page even carried a note claiming this word was "a homophone of [[工場]] (factory)" — but that claim was itself just a symptom of the bug: real Mandarin gōngjiàng/gōngchǎng, Cantonese, and Japanese こうしょう/こうじょう are not actually homophones at all; only Korean 공장 coincidentally matches for both words independently of this bug. Removed the false homophone claim and propagated the ruby fix to [[匠]]'s own Words-section entry.
