## Notes
- [市](../characters/市.md) "market; city" + [場](../characters/場.md) "place; venue" — this word is the stand-in for [場](../characters/場.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

市場 names a market, both concrete (a physical marketplace) and abstract (an economic market, 市場経済 "market economy").

Japanese しじょう, Korean 시장, and Vietnamese thị trường are all standard, non-drifted readings. HSK level 2 already correct. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄐㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
