## Notes
- [磁](../characters/磁.md) "porcelain" + [器](../characters/器.md) "utensil"

磁器 is porcelain: hard, fine ceramic ware fired at high heat. Wiktionary lists Mandarin cíqì and Japanese じき as "porcelain". Korean 자기 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese ci4 hei3 matches.

It is the finer sort of [陶器](陶器.md), pottery, and the maker is the [陶工](陶工.md). See also [陶瓷](陶瓷.md).
