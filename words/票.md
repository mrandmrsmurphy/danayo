## Notes
- [票](../characters/票%20(char).md) "ballot, ticket" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 票 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

票 originated as a 会意 combination of fire (火) and a pictograph of hands raising something aloft — sparks rising up — extended figuratively to a slip of paper that "flutters up," and from there to any small marked slip: a ballot, ticket, note, or receipt. It is highly productive in this sense: 郵票 ("postage stamp"), 投票 ("to vote, cast a ballot"), 車票 ("bus/train ticket"), 支票 ("check, cheque").

Mandarin piào, Cantonese piu3, and Korean 표 (pyo) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading ふだ (fuda, "tag, label, slip") rather than the on-reading ヒョウ (hyō) for the bare character alone — the on-reading instead surfaces in compounds like 投票 (とうひょう, "vote"). Vietnamese phiếu is likewise the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading, used independently and in compounds like phiếu bầu ("ballot").
