## Notes
- [狐](../characters/狐.md) "fox" + [狸 (char)](../characters/狸%20(char).md) "tanuki" — 狐's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 狸's own is itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 狐 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

狐狸 means fox.

Mandarin húli and Cantonese wu4 lei2 are the compound. Japanese uses the native きつね and Korean the native 여우, matching 狐's own native readings. Vietnamese hồ ly is compositional and is also the folklore term, as in hồ ly tinh "fox spirit".
