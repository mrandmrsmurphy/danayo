## Notes
- [防](../characters/防.md) "defend, prevent" + [火](../characters/火%20(char).md) "fire" — no stand-in relationship applies (防's own stand-in is [[防護]]; 火's own is itself).

防火 means fire prevention or fireproofing.

Mandarin fánghuǒ, Cantonese fong4 fo2, Japanese ぼうか, Korean 방화, and Vietnamese phòng hoả are all standard, fully compositional readings. **A very important homophone**: [[放火]] "set on fire; arson" shares this exact reading (both 注音 ㄅㄚㄫㄏ⺢) despite being near-opposite meanings.
