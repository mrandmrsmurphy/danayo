## Notes
- [貳 (char)](../characters/貳%20(char).md) "disloyal; betray" — a formal/financial variant of plain "two" (二); by extension, a "secondary" or "assistant" role with respect to money came to mean "unfaithful; disloyal."

This word is a genuine Dan'a'yo homophone with [[二]] ("two") — both read 늬/ㄋㄧㄜ, coincidental given 貳's own role as the anti-forgery financial variant of 二 itself.
