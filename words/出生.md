## Notes
- [出 (char)](../characters/出%20(char).md) "to come out" + [生](../characters/生.md) "to be born, life" — "to be born" (出生地, "birthplace"; 出生率, "birth rate"). `vietnamese: xuất sinh` is compositional from each character's own confirmed reading, matching everyday usage in formal/administrative contexts (giấy khai sinh conventions), though colloquial Vietnamese usually says sinh ra or ra đời.

**Real content bugs found and fixed**: `mandarin` was missing its final -g ("chūshēn" instead of chūshēng); `korean` had a native gloss ("태어나다," native Korean "to be born") mixed into the reading field as "출생,태어나다" — trimmed to 출생 alone. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 춛상 diverges from Sino-Korean 출생 in the second syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄨㄊㄙㄚㄫ unique among both words and characters).
