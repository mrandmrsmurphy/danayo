## Notes
- [曽](../characters/曽%20(char).md) "great-" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 曽 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

曽 means "great-" (as in "great-grandfather," 曽祖父).

Mandarin cēng, Cantonese zang1, Japanese そう, Korean 증, and Vietnamese từng (matching 曽's own stored citation) are all compositional readings; the real-world standard Sino-Vietnamese "great-" reading is actually "tằng" (曾祖 tằng tổ "great-grandparent"), which is missing from 曽's own stored citation (only tâng/tưng/từng are listed) — a character-level citation gap flagged for future perfecting, not fixed here. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field, added the missing `pos`/`japanese`/`hsk_level` fields. No word-level homophones — the bound characters [[増]], [[憎]], and [[贈]] share this same 注音 (ㄐㄜㄫ) but none has an independent word page of its own.
