## Notes
- [動 (char)](../characters/動%20(char).md) "move" + [脈](../characters/脈.md) "vessel; pulse"

動脈 is an artery: a vessel that carries blood away from the heart. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin dòngmài and Japanese どうみゃく as "artery". Vietnamese động mạch is the same word, and Korean 동맥 and Cantonese dung6 mak6 match.

It is the "moving" vessel, where the pulse is felt, and its counterpart is the [静脈](静脈.md). Both are kinds of [血管](血管.md).
