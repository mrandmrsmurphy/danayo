So broad! Use 文言文 for Literary Chinese (basically A.D.) and 古典漢語 for Classical Chinese (basically B.C.)
## Etymology
- [漢](../characters/漢.md) "Han (Chinese)" + [文](../characters/文.md) "culture" — no stand-in relationship applies (漢's own stand-in is [[漢族]]; 文's own is [[文化]]).
- Notice that, by homophony, this block 韓文 being used for Hangǔl, so we use <ruby>[諺文](/words/諺文.md)<rt>ㄋ⼶ㄋㄇㄨㄋ</rt></ruby> instead (韓文 itself was never created as a word here, avoiding the collision entirely).

Mandarin Hànwén, Cantonese hon3 man4, Japanese かんぶん, Korean 한문, and Vietnamese Hán văn are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 漢's and 文's own stored citations exactly), and also the real, standard everyday words for "Chinese writing/literature" in each language. `kwin: true` because 諺文 한문 matches Korean 한문 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄋㄇㄨㄋ unique across both words and characters).
