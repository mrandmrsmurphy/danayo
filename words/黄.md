## Notes
- [黄 (char)](../characters/黄%20(char).md) "yellow" — the bare character used directly as a word, its own `stand_in`.

黄 means yellow: the color of gold and ripe grain (黄色, "yellow"; [[黄金]], "gold"; [[黄昏]], "dusk"; 黄河, "Yellow River"), traditionally the central color of the Five Phases, associated with earth and with imperial authority. By extension it can mean ripe or withered (枯黄, "withered yellow"). The traditional form 黃 and the variant 簧 are listed as aliases.

Mandarin huáng, Cantonese wong4, Japanese おう (黄金/おうごん, "gold"; 黄色/おうしょく, "yellow") with the alternate on'yomi こう (黄河/こうが, "Yellow River"), Korean 황 (황금, 황색), and Sino-Vietnamese hoàng (hoàng kim, "gold"; hoàng hôn, "dusk") are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native adjective 黄色い (きいろい) for the everyday word; Korean has native 누르다 and Vietnamese the native vàng.
