## Notes
- [恰](../characters/恰%20(char).md) "exactly, just" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

恰 means exactly or just — precisely, as in 恰好 ("just right").

Mandarin qià, Cantonese hap1, Japanese こう, Korean 흡, and Vietnamese kháp are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄎㄚㄆ/kab/캅) with the unrelated [[怯]] ("cowardly, timid").
