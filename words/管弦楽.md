## Notes
- [管](../characters/管.md) "pipe; wind" + [弦 (char)](../characters/弦%20(char).md) "string" + [楽](../characters/楽.md) "music"

管弦楽 is an orchestra, or orchestral music: music for winds and strings together. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin guǎnxiányuè as "orchestral music" and Japanese かんげんがく as "orchestra". Korean 관현악 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese gun2 jin4 ngok6 matches.

The player group is the [楽団](楽団.md); its large works are the [交響曲](交響曲.md), symphony, and [協奏曲](協奏曲.md), concerto, and it is led by a conductor, the [指揮](指揮.md).
