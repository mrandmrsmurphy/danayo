## Notes
- [熱](../characters/熱%20(char).md) "hot" + [帯](../characters/帯%20(char).md) "belt, zone" + [雨](../characters/雨%20(char).md) "rain" + [林](../characters/林%20(char).md) "forest" — "tropical rain forest." Their own `stand_in` values: 熱's is itself; 帯's is itself; 雨's is itself; 林's is itself; none points here, so 熱帯雨林 is an independent compound.

熱帯雨林 is a tropical rain forest: dense forest in hot, wet lands. Mandarin rèdàiyǔlín (熱帶雨林) and Cantonese jit6 daai3 jyu5 lam4 are the ordinary words, and Japanese ねったいうりん and Korean 열대우림 are the same compound. Compare [[熱帯]] (the tropics) and [[森林]] (forest).
