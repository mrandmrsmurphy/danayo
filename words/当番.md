## Notes
- [当 (char)](../characters/当%20(char).md) "be on duty; current" + [番 (char)](../characters/番%20(char).md) "turn, number" — duty turn.

当番 is one's turn on duty: the person or the period assigned to a task in rotation (Japanese 掃除当番, "cleaning duty"). It is "on-duty turn." Wiktionary has the Chinese entry only, marked literary, with Mandarin dāngfān and Cantonese dong1 faan1 ("to take turns being on duty"); Japanese とうばん and Korean 당번 come from memory. The evidence is the weakest of the Work coinages. It does not cover the daytime-shift sense of "work shift" (day shift, night shift), for which no shared compound was found. It belongs to the 番 family of shift words ([[番]], [[非番]], [[日番]], [[夜番]], [[輪番]]), a systematic schema that Dan'a'yo imposes for predictability rather than a claim about ancient usage. Compare [[交替]] (to take turns, to swap) and [[班]] (group, shift squad).
