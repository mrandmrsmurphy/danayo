## Notes
- [是 (char)](../characters/是%20(char).md) "is" + [否 (char)](../characters/否%20(char).md) "not"

是否 is whether, whether or not. English Wiktionary has an entry only for Chinese 是否 (Mandarin shìfǒu, Cantonese si6 fau2), glossed "whether or not; if". The Japanese ぜひ and Korean 시부 fields are built from the readings of the two characters.
