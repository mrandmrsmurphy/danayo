## Notes
- [百 (char)](../characters/百%20(char).md) "hundred" + [事 (char)](../characters/事%20(char).md) "thing, matter" — literally "a hundred matters," used idiomatically for "everything, all affairs, myriad issues."

百事 is a fixed idiom of comprehensiveness rather than a literal count — the way English "a hundred things" can mean simply "everything" or "so much." It appears in set phrases like 百事大吉 ("all matters go well, everything is auspicious," a traditional New Year well-wish) and, informally, in the transliterated brand name 百事可樂 (Pepsi-Cola, a phono-semantic pun where 百事 doubles as both the transliteration of "Pepsi" and its literal "all things go well" meaning). Mandarin bǎishì, Cantonese baak3 si6, Japanese ひゃくじ (hyakuji), Korean 백사 (baeksa), and Vietnamese bách sự are all compositional and carry the same idiomatic "everything, all affairs" sense.
