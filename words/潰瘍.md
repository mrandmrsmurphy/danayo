## Notes
- [潰](characters/潰%20(char).md) "burst, rupture" + [瘍](characters/瘍.md) "ulcer, sore" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 瘍 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (潰's own stand-in is itself, so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character). `vietnamese: hội dương` is compositional, cross-verified via hvdic's own "hội sang" (潰瘡, a close synonym compound) entry, which explicitly notes the same "hội" reading extends to 潰瘍.

潰瘍 means ulcer.

Mandarin kuìyáng, Cantonese kui2 joeng4, Japanese かいよう, and Korean 궤양 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 潰's and 瘍's own stored citations exactly). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄛㄧ⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
