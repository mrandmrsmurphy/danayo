## Notes
- [此 (char)](../characters/此%20(char).md) "this; proximal demonstrative" + [様 (char)](../characters/様%20(char).md) "manner; way; style; appearance"

此様 is the proximal correlative in the 様 ("manner") column of the correlative table: "this way; like this; in this manner", the manner of an action as demonstrated or currently in focus. The character [此 (char)](../characters/此%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *cǝ*, ㄑㄜ) is the classical Chinese proximal demonstrative, contrasting with 其 (medial) and 彼 (distal); [様 (char)](../characters/様%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *yang*, ⼘ㄫ) supplies "manner; way; style; appearance". Where [此処](此処.md) "here" and [此時](此時.md) "now" locate an event in space and time, 此様 characterises how it is done.

The word is built on the particle [様](様.md), which this vault documents as the adverbializer "-ly" (the manner case, [様態格](様態格.md)) and which names 此様, 其様, 彼様, 何様 and 某様 among the correlatives it generates. The two are consistent: 此様 is 此 + the manner suffix, "in the manner of this", so it is a fixed adverbial compound (pos 副用名詞, "adverbial noun", like its siblings [其様](其様.md), [彼様](彼様.md) and [何様](何様.md)) rather than a free noun phrase that needs further marking. Any noun or stative can take 様 the same way, as in [速様](速様.md); the correlative cells are simply the deictic, interrogative and quantifier members of that pattern.

The cross-linguistic fields hold the spoken equivalents. Mandarin 這樣 (zhèyàng): Wiktionary has an entry, an adverb glossed "(in) this way; such", e.g. 這樣做不行 "you can't do it like this". Wiktionary has no entry for 此樣 or 此様 itself, so the compound is a Dan'a'yo construction. Cantonese 噉樣 (gam2 joeng6): Wiktionary glosses it "this way; like this; so" (Cantonese), and its pronunciation line gives the second syllable a changed tone (joeng6-2); Wiktionary's other Cantonese reading for 這樣 is ze2 joeng6. Korean 이렇게 (ireoke): Wiktionary glosses it "like this, this way", the adverbial form of 이렇다, which fits the Korean -게 in the manner row of the case table in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md` (line 171). Japanese このように (kono yō ni) is the equivalent I have recorded, but Wiktionary has no entry for it (checked 2026-10-10), so it is from memory, not re-checked; Wiktionary does list こうやって (kō yatte) as an adverb meaning "in this way", with そうやって, ああやって and どうやって as coordinate terms, which is the informal alternative. Vietnamese *như thế này* "like this" is from memory, not re-checked: Wiktionary was rate-limited when I tried, and hvdic.thivien.net covers single characters (it gives 様's reading as dạng), not phrases, so no `vietnamese` field is entered.

Source languages compress manner deixis differently: Mandarin 這樣/那樣 has a two-way split, whereas Dan'a'yo has the three-way 此様/其様/彼様, like Japanese こう/そう/ああ and Korean 이렇게/그렇게/저렇게. From the Dan'a'yo side, Mandarin 那樣 covers both 其様 and 彼様.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md)'s correlative table (line 121; the same chart is in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`, line 150, in the 様 row, whose header is the 様 manner column), 此様 is the seventh cell of the 此 ("this") row, after [此事](此事.md) "this matter", [此物](此物.md) "this thing", [此人](此人.md) "this person", [此名](此名.md) "this person (polite)", [此処](此処.md) "this place" and [此時](此時.md) "this time", and before [此多](此多.md) "this amount" and [此類](此類.md) "this kind". Down the 様 column it pairs with [其様](其様.md) "that way" (medial), [彼様](彼様.md) "that way yonder" (distal), [何様](何様.md) "how", [毎様](毎様.md), [某様](某様.md) "somehow" and [皆様](皆様.md) "in every way". The table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), 63 forms, so 此様 is parsed compositionally: 此 = proximal, 様 = manner. Illustrative only (invented, not from `grammar/`): 此様作 "make (it) this way". Dan'a'yo keeps the sense shared across CJKV, "this way; like this"; no narrowing is needed.

The word's own `lexipedia` placement is Grammar, not [Light](../lexipedia/Light.md), which is where [様](様.md) carries its property: Light lists the manner adverb [明様](../words/明様.md) "brightly" and notes 様 only as a homophone of [陽](../words/陽.md) and [揚](../words/揚.md), whereas 此様 is a grammatical cell of the correlative paradigm and sits with its siblings.

The 注音 ㄑㄜ⼘ㄫ carries no syllable-boundary dot: `grammar/文法 - 02音韻論.md` gives the syllable as (C)(G)V(C) with codas limited to m, n, ŋ, u, i, p, t, k, and ㄫ is not among the initials (m, n, s, h, l, ∅, p, t, c, k, b, d, j, g, f), so the only parse is ㄑㄜ + ⼘ㄫ.
