## Notes
- [劇 (char)](../characters/劇%20(char).md) "drama" + [作 (char)](../characters/作%20(char).md) "make" + [家](../characters/家.md) "house; specialist"

劇作家 is a playwright: a person who writes plays. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin jùzuòjiā as "playwright; dramatist; screenwriter" and Japanese げきさっか as "playwright, dramatist". Korean 극작가 and Vietnamese kịch tác gia are the same word. Cantonese kek6 zok3 gaa1 matches.

The plays are written as [脚本](脚本.md), scripts, for the [劇](劇.md), drama, and the [劇場](劇場.md), theatre.
