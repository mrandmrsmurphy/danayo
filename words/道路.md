## Notes
- [道 (char)](../characters/道%20(char).md) "way" + [路](../characters/路.md) "road" — stand-in for [[路]], which cannot appear independently (道's own `stand_in` is itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply)

Mandarin dàolù, Cantonese dou6 lou6, Japanese どうろ (dōro), Korean 도로 (doro), and Vietnamese đạo lộ are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 道's and 路's own stored citations exactly — though each language's everyday word for a plain "road" tends to favor one character over the compound: Japanese leans on bare 道 (michi) or 道路 in formal/infrastructural contexts, while Korean 도로 is itself the standard word for a paved road specifically, distinct from the more general 길 (native). `kwin` is false per the AND-rule: 路 is individually `true` while 道 is individually `false`.
