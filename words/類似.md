## Notes
- [類](../characters/類.md) "kind, sort, category" + [似 (char)](../characters/似%20(char).md) "like, as, resembling, as if" — no `#cranberry`: 類's own `stand_in` is [[種類]], 似's is 似 itself; neither points here.

類似 (lèisì) is the standard Mandarin word for "similar, resembling," and Cantonese 類似 (leoi6ci5) likewise. Japanese るいじ (ruiji) and Korean 유사 (yusa) are both standard terms in their languages, though everyday Korean also uses the native 비슷하다 for the same sense.

Sino-Vietnamese loại tự is compositionally straightforward but not independently attested as a fixed compound — the standard Vietnamese equivalent, tương tự, is instead built on a different Chinese compound (相似, "mutual resemblance") rather than 類似's own characters.
