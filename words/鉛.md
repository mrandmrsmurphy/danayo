## Notes
- [鉛 (char)](../characters/鉛%20(char).md) "lead"

鉛 is 形声: semantic 金 ("metal") + phonetic 㕣. It names the element lead (Pb, atomic number 82) — the word doubles as this vault's own periodic-table abbreviation for the element. It also appears in [[鉛筆]] ("pencil," literally "lead-writer," reflecting the same historical graphite/lead association found in the English word "pencil lead").

Mandarin qiān, Cantonese jyun4, and Japanese えん (en, on'yomi, fully productive: 鉛筆 えんぴつ "pencil") alongside native なまり (namari, the everyday word for "lead" as a metal) all preserve the sense. Korean 연 (yeon) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 납 (nap, the everyday word for "lead"). Vietnamese duyên is the Hán Việt reading — coincidentally identical in spelling to the common, unrelated native word duyên ("fate, destiny, charm"), verified via search rather than assumed contamination; everyday Vietnamese instead uses the native word chì for the metal itself.
