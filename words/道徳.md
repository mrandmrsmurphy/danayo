## Notes
- [道 (char)](../characters/道%20(char).md) "way" + [徳 (char)](../characters/徳%20(char).md) "virtue"

道徳 is morality: the principles of right and wrong that a society holds. Wiktionary glosses Japanese どうとく as "morality; ethics". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 도덕 matches.

It is the code that people live by, where [倫理](倫理.md) is ethics as a field of thought, and [徳](徳.md) is virtue as a quality of a person. It is judged against the [礼儀](礼儀.md) of daily manners.
