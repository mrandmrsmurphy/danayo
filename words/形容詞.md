## Notes
- [形](../characters/形%20(char).md) "shape, form, type" + [容](../characters/容%20(char).md) "look, appearance" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "word, expression" — no stand-in relationship applies (形's own stand-in is itself; 容's own is itself; 詞's own is [[単詞]]).

形容詞 means adjective — a word describing the appearance or quality of a noun.

Mandarin xíngróngcí, Cantonese jing4 jung4 ci4, Japanese けいようし, and Korean 형용사 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese tính từ is the standard native grammatical term (fixed wrongly-capitalized "Tính từ"→lowercase, as a common noun, not a proper noun).
