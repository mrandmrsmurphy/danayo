## Notes
- [神 (char)](../characters/神%20(char).md) "god, deity, spirit" + [農](../characters/農.md) "farming" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (神's is [[神]] itself, 農's is [[農業]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: true` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: true`.

神農 (Shennong, "Divine Farmer") is a legendary culture-hero and deity credited with inventing agriculture and the plow, discovering medicinal herbs, and identifying edible plants for humanity — traditionally counted among the Three Sovereigns (三皇) of Chinese mythology alongside 伏羲 and 女媧. He is also revered as the founder of Traditional Chinese Medicine, said to have tasted a hundred herbs to test their properties.

Mandarin Shénnóng, Cantonese san4 nung4, Japanese しんのう (Shinnō), Korean 신농 (Sinnong), and Vietnamese Thần Nông are all standard, fully compositional readings of this shared cultural figure's name — Thần Nông in particular is also independently venerated in Vietnamese folk religion as a god of agriculture.
