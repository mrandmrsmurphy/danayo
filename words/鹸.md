## Notes
- [鹸 (char)](../characters/鹸%20(char).md) "base, alkali" — 鹸 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

鹸 also serves as the periodic-table abbreviation prefix for sodium via [[鹸素]] (ㄑㄝㄇㄙㄛ), a fitting choice given sodium's association with alkalinity (e.g. sodium hydroxide/lye).

Mandarin jiǎn, Cantonese gaan2, Korean 첨, Japanese けん, and Sino-Vietnamese kiềm are all standard, fully compositional readings. This word is a genuine Dan'a'yo homophone of [[諂]] ("flatter"); 諂's own `stand_in` points to itself, confirming the collision.
