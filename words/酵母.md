## Notes
- [酵](../characters/酵.md) "yeast" + [母](../characters/母.md) "mother"
- This word is the viable form of [酵](../characters/酵.md): the character's meaning (yeast, leaven) is identical, but 酵 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 酵母 is its `stand_in` compound.

酵母 is yeast, the single-celled fungus that ferments sugars to produce alcohol and carbon dioxide, and so the living agent behind brewing, winemaking and leavened bread. Mandarin jiàomǔ, Cantonese haau1 mou5, Japanese こうぼ (kōbo; 酵母菌, "yeast fungus") and Korean 효모 (hyomo) are the same compound. 酵 is "to ferment, leaven" (as in 発酵, "fermentation") and 母 is "mother", the culture from which more is made; in Chinese baking a "mother" dough or starter is also called 酵母.

In Dan'a'yo it is the yeast and leaven word, placed in the Food page beside the drinks it ferments, such as [[麦酒]] (beer) and [[酒糟]] (lees).
