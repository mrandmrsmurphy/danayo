## Notes
- [満](../characters/満%20(char).md) "full" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 満 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

満 means full: filled to capacity, with no room left (滿杯, "full cup"; 滿月, "full moon"; 客滿, "no vacancy"), and by extension complete, satisfied, or fully reached (滿意, "satisfied"; 圓滿, "perfect, complete"; 滿分, "full marks"; 滿足, "to satisfy"). It also marks a completed term (滿期, "expiry"; 滿十八歲, "a full eighteen years old"). The simplified form 满 and traditional 滿 are listed as aliases; the shinjitai 満 is the headword.

Mandarin mǎn, Cantonese mun5, Japanese まん (満足/まんぞく, "satisfaction"; 満月/まんげつ, "full moon"; 満員/まんいん, "full house") with the alternate on'yomi ばん, Korean 만 (만족, 만월) and Vietnamese mãn (mãn nguyện, "contented"; mãn hạn, "expiry") are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native verb 満ちる (みちる) and 満たす (みたす) for the everyday words, so on'yomi まん is bound to compounds.
