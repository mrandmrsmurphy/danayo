## Notes
- [枢](../characters/枢%20(char).md) "hinge, pivot" + [机](../characters/机.md) "machine" + [卿](../characters/卿%20(char).md) "noble, high officer, sir, m'lord" — no stand-in relationship applies (枢's own stand-in is itself; 机's own is [[机会]]; 卿's own is itself).

枢机卿 means cardinal (the Church office).

Japanese すうききょう and Korean 추기경 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `mandarin` was missing the 卿 syllable entirely (shūjī → shūjīqīng); filled the blank `cantonese`/`vietnamese` fields, both likewise compositional (syu1 gei1 hing1; khu cơ khanh). No homophones (注音 ㄑㄨㄍㄧㄜㄎ⼶ㄫ unique across both words and characters).

Dan'a'yo keeps the shared CJKV sense: a cardinal of the Catholic Church, the short form of the full title 枢机主教, built on [[枢机]] with 卿 "high officer." It is cited on the Christian page under the clergy ranks, beside [[主教]] and [[教皇]].
