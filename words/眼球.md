## Notes
- [眼](../characters/眼.md) "eyeball, pupil" + [球 (char)](../characters/球%20(char).md) "ball, sphere" — 眼's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 球's own is [[球]] itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 眼 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

眼球 is the standard anatomical/medical term for the eyeball as a physical organ, as distinct from 眼 alone (which can refer more loosely to the eye, vision, or gaze in general). Mandarin yǎnqiú, Cantonese ngaan5 kau4, Japanese がんきゅう (gankyū), Korean 안구 (angu), and Vietnamese nhãn cầu are all fully compositional and used consistently in anatomical and ophthalmological contexts across the sphere.
