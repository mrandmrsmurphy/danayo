## Notes
- [尋 (char)](../characters/尋%20(char).md) "inquire for; seek" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [尋 (char)](../characters/尋%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

尋 names the act of searching or seeking out, and by extension "ordinary, common" in the compound 尋常 (as in something one might search for/encounter everywhere) — the search sense and the ordinariness sense are historically the same character, not a coincidental collision.

Japanese じん, Korean 심, and Vietnamese tầm (verified as the true Sino-Vietnamese reading — the character's own field also lists tìm, the coincidentally similar-sounding *native* Vietnamese word for "to find/search," a different etymological source) are all standard readings. `kwin: true` (諺文 심 matches korean 심 exactly). No homophones among other words beyond [[心]] (already noted, coincidental).
