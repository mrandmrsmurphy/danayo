## Notes
- [反](../characters/反%20(char).md) "anti-, wrong side up, rebel" + [切](../characters/切%20(char).md) "cut, mince, carve" — no stand-in relationship applies (both characters are independent Dan'a'yo entries, each its own stand-in).

反切 (fanqie) is the traditional Chinese phonological method of indicating a character's pronunciation using two other characters — one supplying the initial consonant, the other the final and tone.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `cantonese` was missing 反's initial consonant ("faan1cit3" → faan2 cit3, matching every other 反-compound in the vault, e.g. [[反応]], [[反哺]], [[反響]]). `korean: 반절` uses 切's alternate reading 절 (as in 切実, 親切) rather than its citation reading 체 (as in 一切) — a genuine, well-attested alternate reading, not an error. Japanese はんせつ and Vietnamese phiên thiết are standard readings. Fixed empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄈㄛㄋㄑㄝㄊ unique across both words and characters).
