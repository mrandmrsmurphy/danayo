## Notes
- [胞](../characters/胞.md) "womb, cell, membrane" + [子](../characters/子.md) "thing" (noun suffix) — "spore" (biological reproductive unit, as in fungi/ferns). `vietnamese: bào tử` is the standard everyday Vietnamese biology term for this exact concept, a direct match. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 뱟즈 diverges entirely from Sino-Korean 포자 — the same divergence shared with its homophone [[包子]].
