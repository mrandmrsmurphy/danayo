## Notes
- [結 (char)](../characters/結%20(char).md) "tie, join" + [婚](../characters/婚.md) "marry" + [礼 (char)](../characters/礼%20(char).md) "rite, ceremony" — none of the three characters' own `stand_in` points here (結's is [[結]] itself, 婚's is [[結婚]], 礼's is [[礼]] itself), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 結 and 礼 are individually `kwin: false`.

結婚礼 names a wedding ceremony.

Mandarin jiéhūnlǐ, Cantonese git3 fan1 lai5, Japanese けっこんれい (kekkonrei), and Vietnamese kết hôn lễ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 혼례 (hollye) is the real, standard Korean word for "wedding ceremony," but drops 結 rather than including all three characters — a genuine, independently attested 2-character term, not a compositional 3-character reading. **Fixed a real bug**: this word's own `諺文` had been truncated to 걷혼 (missing 礼's own final syllable 레 entirely), corrected to 걷혼레; the other pronunciation fields were already correct. Filled blank cantonese/japanese/vietnamese. No homophones.
