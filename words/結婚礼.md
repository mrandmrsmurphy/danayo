## Notes
- [結 (char)](../characters/結%20(char).md) "tie, join" + [婚](../characters/婚.md) "marry" + [礼 (char)](../characters/礼%20(char).md) "rite, ceremony" — none of the three characters' own `stand_in` points here (結's is [[結]] itself, 婚's is [[結婚]], 礼's is [[礼]] itself), so `#cranberry` does not apply.

結婚礼 names a wedding ceremony.

Mandarin jiéhūnlǐ, Cantonese git3 fan1 lai5, Japanese けっこんれい (kekkonrei), and Vietnamese kết hôn lễ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 혼례 (hollye) is the real, standard Korean word for "wedding ceremony," but drops 結 rather than including all three characters — a genuine, independently attested 2-character term, not a compositional 3-character reading.
