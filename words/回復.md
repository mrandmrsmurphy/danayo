## Notes
- "To recover, restore, rejuvenate" (回復する health, strength, a system). `vietnamese: hồi phục` is a directly attested, extremely common standard Vietnamese word for "to recover" (hồi phục sức khỏe, "to recover one's health"). Fixed `characters` to use the `回 (char)` filename form. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 회북 diverges from Sino-Korean 회복 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄛㄧㄅㄨㄎ unique among both words and characters).
