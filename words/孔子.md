## Notes
- [孔](../characters/孔%20(char).md) "hole; surname" + [子](../characters/子.md) "master, philosopher" — no stand-in relationship applies (孔's own stand-in is itself; 子's own is [[児子]]).

孔子 means Confucius — the surname 孔 honorifically suffixed with 子 ("master"), the standard title for ancient philosophers (compare [[老子]], [[荘子]]).

Mandarin Kǒngzǐ, Cantonese hung2 zi2, Japanese こうし, Korean 공자, and Vietnamese Khổng Tử are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `羅馬字` had 子's syllable mistyped ji→jǝ (諺文/注音 were already correct); `characters` was missing the "(char)" suffix for 孔. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄛㄫㄐㄜ unique across both words and characters).
