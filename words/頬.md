## Notes
- [頬 (char)](../characters/頬%20(char).md) "cheek" — this word is 頬's own legitimizing compound (頬's `stand_in` points here).

頬 means "cheek." Mandarin jiá, Cantonese gaap3, Korean 협 (hyeop), and Sino-Vietnamese giáp all preserve the Sino reading directly. Japanese きょう (kyō) survives mainly in fixed compounds; the ordinary, everyday native reading is ほお (hoo).
