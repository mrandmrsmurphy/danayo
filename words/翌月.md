## Notes
- [翌 (char)](../characters/翌%20(char).md) "following" + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month"

翌月 is "the following month": the month after a stated reference point. English Wiktionary lists it at the Japanese (よくげつ, yokugetsu) and Korean (익월) entries only, as "the following month" and "the next month"; it has no Chinese entry for the word. In the vault it belongs to the series of 翌 words on the Calendar page: [[翌日]] (the following day), [[翌週]] (the following week), 翌月, [[翌年]] (the following year) and [[翌世紀]] (the following century). English Wiktionary's Japanese entry gives the same series, minus 翌世紀, as coordinate terms.

The point of the 翌 series is that it is relative to a reference point in the text, not to the moment of speaking. 翌月 looks forward from some date already named ("it was due in May; the following month it was paid"), while [[来月]] "next month" looks forward from now, and [[今月]] "this month" is the present month. Dan'a'yo keeps the shared sense. The Mandarin reading yìyuè and Cantonese jik6 jyut6 are the readings of the characters; I have not checked how far the word is used in Mandarin or Cantonese speech, where 下個月 and 次月 are the usual words (from memory, not re-checked).

No other word shares the 注音 ㄧㄎ·⼔ㄊ (checked 2026-10-12).
