## Notes
- [香](../characters/香.md) "incense, fragrant, aroma" + [港](../characters/港.md) "harbor" — no `#cranberry`: 香's own `stand_in` is [[香気]], 港's is [[港湾]]; neither points here.

香港 is a proper-noun toponym: Mandarin Xiānggǎng and Cantonese hoeng1gong2 are simply the actual place name (Cantonese being the source of the English "Hong Kong" itself). Japanese ホンコン (Honkon) is likewise the real attested term, a phonetic borrowing rather than the compositional on'yomi reading こうこう. Korean 향항 (hyanghang) follows this vault's consistent convention for CJK toponyms of recording the classical/literary Sino-Korean hanja reading (cf. [[北京]] 북경, [[東京]] 동경, [[上海]] 상해) rather than the modern colloquial transliteration (홍콩). Sino-Vietnamese Hương Cảng is likewise a real, still-used alternate name for the city.
