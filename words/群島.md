## Notes
- [群](../characters/群.md) "crowd, group" + [島 (char)](../characters/島%20(char).md) "island" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (群's is [[群衆]], 島's is [[島]] itself), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 島 is individually `kwin: false`.

群島 names an archipelago — a group of islands.

Mandarin qúndǎo, Cantonese kwan4 dou2, Japanese ぐんとう (guntō), and Korean 군도 (gundo) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 群島 is itself a common attested word for "archipelago" in all four languages. Vietnamese quần đảo is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
