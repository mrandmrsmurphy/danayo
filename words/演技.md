## Notes
- [演 (char)](../characters/演%20(char).md) "perform" + [技](../characters/技.md) "skill"

演技 is acting: the skill of performing a role on stage or screen. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yǎnjì as "acting skills; acting performance; acting" and Japanese えんぎ as "performance". Korean 연기 is the same word. Cantonese jin2 gei6 matches.

It is what an [俳優](俳優.md), actor, shows, as the verb [演](演.md), to perform. The staging as a whole is the [演出](演出.md).
