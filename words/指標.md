## Notes
- [指](../characters/指.md) "finger; to point" + [標](../characters/標.md) "mark, signpost" — indicator; index; benchmark; metric

Wiktionary: Chinese "target; quota; indicator; index; metric"; "goal; aim; objective"; in medicine "marker (of disease, function, etc.)"; in Taiwan programming "pointer"; Japanese 指標 (しひょう) "index; guidepost; guideline; indicator"; Korean 지표 is the hanja form, "index; indicator"; Vietnamese chỉ tiêu is the Han form, "target; quota". Compare [[目標]], [[標準]]. Used in `文法 - 01序文`.
