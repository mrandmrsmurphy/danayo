## Notes
- "To declare, proclaim; a declaration, manifesto" — a formal public statement of intent or principles. `vietnamese: tuyên ngôn` was already present and is directly attested and extremely common (e.g. Tuyên ngôn Độc lập, the Declaration of Independence). **Left `hsk_level` unset**: new-standard sources place it at HSK 3.0 Level 3, which doesn't map cleanly onto the vault's old 1–6 scale.
- **Real derivation bug found and fixed**: `羅馬字`/`諺文` were stored as `swen'en`/`숸원`, which match neither 宣's own reading (`sen`/`선`) nor 言's (`'en`/`언`) nor the word's own already-correct `注音` (ㄙㄝㄋㄝㄋ, the genuine concatenation). Corrected to `sen'en`/`선언`. This also fixed a knock-on error: `kwin` was `false`, but both 宣 and 言 individually carry `kwin: true` on their own character pages, and the corrected Dan'a'yo 諺文 (선언) now exactly matches the real Korean reading (`korean: 선언`) — so `kwin` is properly `true` (AND-rule), not `false`.
- No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄋㄝㄋ unique among both words and characters).
