## Etymology
- [宣](../characters/宣.md) "proclaim, announce" + [言 (char)](../characters/言%20(char).md) "say, word" — stand-in for [[宣]], which cannot appear independently

## Notes
- "To declare, proclaim; a declaration, manifesto" — a formal public statement of intent or principles. `vietnamese: tuyên ngôn` is directly attested and extremely common (e.g. Tuyên ngôn Độc lập, the Declaration of Independence).
