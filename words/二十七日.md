## Notes
- [[二十]] "twenty" + [七 (char)](../characters/七%20(char).md) "seven" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — the twenty-seventh day of the month.

Mandarin èrshíqī rì, Cantonese ji6 sap6 cat1 jat6, Japanese にじゅうしちにち, and Korean 이십칠일 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese filled with ngày hai mươi bảy (native "day twenty-seven"), matching the same native-calendar-date convention already established on [[二十一日]] and [[二十]] itself.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 늬십칟닏 diverges from Sino-Korean 이십칠일, since all four constituent characters independently carry `kwin: false` on their own character pages.

**Found and fixed missing citations**: this word was entirely absent from `二(char).md`, `十(char).md`, and `七(char).md`'s own Words sections (only `日(char).md` had it) — the same gap pattern found on [[二十一日]] — added to all three.
