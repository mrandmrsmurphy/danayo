## Notes
- [尺 (char)](../characters/尺%20(char).md) "foot (unit)" + [八 (char)](../characters/八%20(char).md) "eight"

尺八 is a shakuhachi: the Japanese end-blown bamboo flute. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin chǐbā as "shakuhachi" and Japanese しゃくはち as "shakuhachi, a Japanese end-blown flute". Korean 척팔 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese cek3 baat3 matches.

It is named for its length, one shaku and eight sun, and it is a relative of the [洞簫](洞簫.md), xiao, played by [吹奏](吹奏.md).
