## Notes
- [研](../characters/研.md) "research" + [究](../characters/究.md) "research" + [者](../characters/者.md) "-er"

研究者 is a researcher: one who does [研究](研究.md), research. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yánjiūzhě and Japanese けんきゅうしゃ as "researcher", and Mandarin also as "research worker". Cantonese jin4 gau3 ze2 and Korean 연구자 match.

It differs from [学者](学者.md), a scholar, who is defined by learning, and from [科学者](科学者.md), a scientist, who works in a science. A 研究者 may work in any field.
