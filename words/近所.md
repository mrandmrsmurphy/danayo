## Notes
- [近 (char)](../characters/近%20(char).md) "near" + [所 (char)](../characters/所%20(char).md) "place"

近所 is the neighborhood: the places and people near one's home. Wiktionary glosses Japanese きんじょ as "a neighborhood". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 근소 matches the character readings.

It is the small area around a home, where a neighbor is a [隣人](隣人.md) and [隣](隣.md) means next door. The wider home place is the [故郷](故郷.md).
