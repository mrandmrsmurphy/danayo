## Notes
- [慙](../characters/慙.md) "ashamed" + [愧](../characters/愧.md) "ashamed" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes both [慙](../characters/慙.md) and [愧](../characters/愧.md) as independent Dan'a'yo entries; neither character appears independently (`#cranberry`). An ancient Buddhist Abhidharma distinction: 慚=shame before self, 愧=shame before others, a distinction not maintained today.

Mandarin cánkuì, Cantonese caam4 kwai5, Japanese ざんき, Korean 참괴, and Vietnamese tàm quý are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄇㄍㄨㄧ unique across both words and characters).
