## Notes
- [慙](characters/慙.md) "ashamed" + [愧](characters/愧.md) "ashamed" — stand-in for both [[慙]] and [[愧]], neither of which can appear independently. An ancient Buddhist Abhidharma distinction: 慚=shame before self, 愧=shame before others, a distinction not maintained today.
