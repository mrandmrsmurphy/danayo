## Notes
- [混](../characters/混.md) "mix, confuse" + [合 (char)](../characters/合%20(char).md) "join, fit together" + [物 (char)](../characters/物%20(char).md) "thing" — mixture.

混合物 is a mixture: two or more substances combined without chemical bonding, so each keeps its own properties and can be separated by physical means (salt water, air, an alloy such as [[青銅]]). It is [[混合]] "to mix" plus 物 "thing," and the counterpart of [[化合物]]. Mandarin hùnhéwù, Cantonese wan6 hap6 mat6, Japanese こんごうぶつ and Korean 혼합물 are the standard readings.
