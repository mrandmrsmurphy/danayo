## Notes
- [権](../characters/権.md) "power, authority" + [威](../characters/威.md) "might, prestige" — no stand-in relationship applies (権's own stand-in is [[権利]]; 威's own is [[威力]]).

権威 means the recognized power or prestige to command respect and obedience.

Mandarin quánwēi, Cantonese kyun4 wai1, Japanese けんい, Korean 권위, and Vietnamese quyền uy are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 威's own stored citation, and 権's own for every field except vietnamese, which is blank on 権's own citation — a character-level gap flagged for future perfecting, not fixed here). No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼔ㄋㄛㄧ unique across both words and characters).
