## Notes
- [川 (char)](../characters/川%20(char).md) "river" + [口 (char)](../characters/口%20(char).md) "mouth, opening" — literally "river-mouth."

1. mouth of a river, estuary
2. Kawaguchi, Japan ～市
3. a surname

Mandarin Chuānkǒu, Cantonese cyun1 hau2, Japanese かわぐち is both the ordinary native word for a river mouth and the place name and surname Kawaguchi — read with kun'yomi rather than the on'yomi せんこう (senkō). Vietnamese has no attested equivalent for the Japanese proper name.
