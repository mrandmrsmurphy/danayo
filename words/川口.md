## Notes
- [川 (char)](../characters/川%20(char).md) "river" + [口 (char)](../characters/口%20(char).md) "mouth, opening" — literally "river-mouth."

1. mouth of a river, estuary
2. Kawaguchi, Japan ～市
3. a surname

Mandarin Chuānkǒu, Cantonese cyun1 hau2, Japanese かわぐち (Kawaguchi, native kun'yomi reading — the real form for this Japanese place name/surname), and Korean 천구 are all standard readings matching 川's and 口's own stored citations exactly; Japanese uses its own native reading here rather than the on'yomi せんこう (senkō), since Kawaguchi is specifically read with kun'yomi as a Japanese proper name. Vietnamese has no attested equivalent for this specific Japanese proper name and is correctly omitted rather than left blank.

`kwin` is false: both 川 and 口 individually diverge from Korean on their own character pages, so the AND-rule fails on both counts. No homophones (注音 ㄑ⺢ㄋㄎㄛㄨ unique among both words and characters).
