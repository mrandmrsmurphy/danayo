## Notes
- [転](../characters/転.md) "turn, revolve; transfer" + [載 (char)](../characters/載%20(char).md) "carry, record" — "reprint, reproduce, forward."

Mandarin zhuǎnzài, cantonese zyun2 zoi3, japanese てんさい, and korean 전재 are all directly attested standard readings. `轉載`/`转载` are the standard traditional/simplified spelling variants (転→轉/转), correctly listed as aliases.
