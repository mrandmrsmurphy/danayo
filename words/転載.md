## Notes
- [転](../characters/転.md) "turn, revolve; transfer" + [載 (char)](../characters/載%20(char).md) "carry, record" — "reprint, reproduce, forward."

Mandarin zhuǎnzài, cantonese zyun2 zoi3, japanese てんさい, and korean 전재 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese chuyển tải is a directly attested standard Vietnamese word ("to convey, transmit"), matching 転's own chuyển (filled in on 転.md, previously blank) + 載's own tải. `轉載`/`转载` are the standard traditional/simplified spelling variants (転→轉/转), correctly listed as aliases. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 줜재 diverges from Sino-Korean 전재 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄐ⼔ㄋㄐㄚㄧ unique among both words and characters).
