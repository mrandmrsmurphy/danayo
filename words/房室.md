## Notes
- [房](../characters/房%20(char).md) "room" + [室](../characters/室.md) "room" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [室](../characters/室.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (房's own stand-in is itself).

房室 means house, room, or apartment.

Mandarin fángshì, Cantonese fong4 sat1, Japanese ぼうしつ, and Korean 방실 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `vietnamese` was contaminated with "tâm nhĩ thất" (the specific medical term for "cardiac atrium"), corrected to compositional phòng thất (房's own "phòng" + 室's own "thất"). No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄫㄙㄧㄊ unique across both words and characters).
