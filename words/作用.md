## Notes
- [作 (char)](../characters/作%20(char).md) "make, do" + [用](../characters/用.md) "use, function" — "to act upon, to affect" (a force, drug, or influence producing an effect), and by extension "function, role, effect" as a noun (副作用, "side effect"). Directly attested and standard across all five languages (mandarin zuòyòng, japanese さよう, korean 작용, vietnamese tác dụng). `kwin` is true: both 作 and 用 independently match their own Sino-Korean readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄎ⼄ㄫ unique among both words and characters).
