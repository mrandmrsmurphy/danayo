## Notes
- [帽](../characters/帽.md) "hat; cap" + [子](../characters/子.md) "child; son" (diminutive/nominal suffix here) — `characters/帽.md`'s own `stand_in` points to this word (帽子), meaning 帽 is a bound morpheme that never appears independently outside this compound; 子's own points elsewhere (児子).

Mandarin màozi, Cantonese mou6 zi2, Japanese ぼうし, and Korean 모자 are the standard readings. For Vietnamese the field holds mũ, the native/Nôm reading of 帽, rather than a Sino-Vietnamese compound, since no standalone "mạo tử" is attested; nón is a narrower word for the conical Asian hat and is not a reading of 帽子.
