## Notes
- [昔](../characters/昔.md) "long ago" + [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day, sun" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [昔](../characters/昔.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (日's own stand-in is itself).

昔日 means long ago, or golden days.

Mandarin xīrì, Cantonese sik1 jat6, Japanese せきじつ, Korean 석일, and Vietnamese tích nhật are all standard, fully compositional readings. Trimmed malformed comma-duplicate `mandarin`. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄎㄋㄧㄊ unique across both words and characters).
