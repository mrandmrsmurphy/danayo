## Notes
- [個 (char)](../characters/個%20(char).md) "individual items" + [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" — "individual" (個人主義, "individualism"; 個人的, "personal, individual"). Mandarin gèrén, Cantonese go3 jan4, Japanese こじん, Korean 개인, and Vietnamese cá nhân are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 그닌 diverges from Sino-Korean 개인 in the first syllable's vowel. This word is a genuine Dan'a'yo homophone with [[其人]] ("he, she, they; that person") — both read 그닌/ㄍㄜㄋㄧㄋ; the reciprocal half of this callout will be completed on 其人's own alphabetical turn.
