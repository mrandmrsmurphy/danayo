## Notes
- [毫 (char)](characters/毫%20(char).md) "fine hair, milli-" + [米 (char)](characters/米%20(char).md) "meter (metric unit, borrowed sense)" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 毫's and 米's own stand-ins are themselves).

毫米, literally "milli-meter," is the standard Mandarin/Cantonese compositional term for millimeter. Since this is a modern metric-system coinage, Japanese ミリメートル and Korean 밀리미터 both use the international phonetic loanword "millimeter" rather than a compositional Sino reading, and Vietnamese milimét is likewise the real, standard loanword — the same genuine divergence pattern already established on the sibling [[千米]] (kilometer), not an error.
