## Notes
- [時 (char)](../characters/時%20(char).md) "time" + [制](../characters/制.md) "system, regulate" — literally "time-system," the grammatical system of verb forms marking when an action occurs.

時制/时制 names grammatical tense specifically — distinct from its neighbor on this vault's own Grammar page, [[時相]] ("tense-aspect," the broader aspectual category).

Mandarin shízhì, Cantonese si4 zai3, Korean 시제 (sije), and Japanese じせい (jisei) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real linguistics term for "tense" in its language. Vietnamese thì chế is compositional, from 時's thì and 制's own chế — a real reading previously entirely missing from 制's own character page, replaced there by chơi/choi, an unrelated common word meaning "to play." Vietnamese linguistics itself, notably, typically discusses this concept under the bare native term thì alone rather than a Sino-Vietnamese compound, since Vietnamese verbs don't inflect for tense the way Chinese/Japanese/Korean grammatical description assumes. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 시제 matches Sino-Korean 시제 exactly. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄐㄝㄧ unique among both words and characters).
