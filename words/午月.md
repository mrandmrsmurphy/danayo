## Notes
- [午](../characters/午.md) "seventh Earthly Branch, horse" + [月](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — no stand-in relationship applies (午's own stand-in is [[正午]]; 月 is its own stand-in).

Fifth month of the ancient Chinese lunar calendar, named for the seventh Earthly Branch 午 (horse).

The fifth month contains the summer solstice (夏至) and the Duanwu Festival (端午節). 午 also means noon — the peak of the day — making this the peak month of the solar year.

Japanese うまつき reads both halves natively (午's kun'yomi うま "horse" + 月's kun'yomi つき "month") rather than the Sino-Japanese on'yomi concatenation ごげつ, matching how zodiac-month names are conventionally read in Japanese (see [[寅月]]). Vietnamese tháng Ngọ likewise uses the native word *tháng* plus the zodiac name Ngọ, the same pattern. **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 午月 and [[五月]] ("May") share an identical reading — added reciprocal homophone callouts to both pages.
