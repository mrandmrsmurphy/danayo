## Notes
- [両](../words/両.md) "both, two" + [唇](../words/唇.md) "lip"

両唇 means bilabial, of both lips. English Wiktionary glosses Japanese 両唇 (りょうしん), marked linguistics, as "both lips". The Mandarin, Cantonese, Korean 량순 and Vietnamese lưỡng thần fields are built from the readings of the two characters.
