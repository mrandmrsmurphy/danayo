## Notes
- [両](../words/両.md) "both, two" (stand-in: itself) + [唇](../words/唇.md) "lip" (stand-in: itself) — bilabial, made with both lips.

両唇 means bilabial — a sound made with both lips. Mandarin liǎngchún, Cantonese loeng2 seon4, and Japanese りょうしん are straightforward compositional readings; Mandarin phonetics more often says 双唇 (shuāngchún), so this is not independently verified as the standard term there. Korean retains word-initial ㄹ per this vault's North Korean/문화어 convention (량 rather than South Korean 양). Vietnamese lưỡng thần is compositional only, built from 唇's own somewhat unusual stored Sino-Vietnamese reading "thần"; the real term is "âm môi" or "song thần." `kwin` is false: inherited from 唇 (false), despite 両 itself being true.

No syllable-boundary dot is needed: the concatenated string `ㄌ⼘ㄫㄙㄨㄋ` admits only one legal partition.
