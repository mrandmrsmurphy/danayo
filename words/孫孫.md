## Etymology
- [孫](../characters/孫.md) "grandchild" reduplicated — an intimate, affectionate address for a grandchild of either gender, distinct from the neutral general word [[孫子]] (which has its own separate Dan'a'yo derivation, sonjǝ/손즈).

## Notes
