## Notes
- [糸](../characters/糸.md) "thread, silk" + [帯](../characters/帯%20(char).md) "band, sash" — "silk band," a ribbon. Their own `stand_in` values: 糸's is [[糸線]]; 帯's is itself; none points here, so 糸帯 is an independent compound.

糸帯 is a ribbon: a narrow band of silk or cloth used to tie or decorate. Mandarin sīdài (絲帶) and Cantonese si1 daai3 are the ordinary words. Japanese says リボン and Korean 리본, so those two fields hold the character readings. The character 糸 has the aliases 絲 and 丝, so the Chinese spelling 絲帶 is accepted. Compare [[帯]] (sash, belt) and [[縄]] (rope).
