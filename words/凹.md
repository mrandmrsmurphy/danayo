## Notes
- [凹 (char)](../characters/凹%20(char).md) "concave"

凹 is a pictograph of a dented, sunken surface — "concave, dented, sunken" — the direct opposite of [[凸]] ("convex, protruding"). The pair appears together in [[凹凸]] ("bumpy, uneven, rugged," literally "concave-convex") and in technical vocabulary like [[凹版]] ("gravure, intaglio printing," which uses recessed plates).

Mandarin āo, Cantonese aau3, and Japanese おう (ō, on'yomi, as in 凹凸 おうとつ "unevenness") alongside native くぼ(む) (kubo(mu), "to be sunken, dented") all preserve the sense. Korean 요 (yo) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 오목하다 (omokhada, "to be concave, sunken"). Vietnamese ao is the Hán Việt reading, attested in ao địa ("sunken land") and ao đột bất bình (凹凸不平, "uneven, bumpy") — coincidentally homophonous with the unrelated native word ao ("pond"), but genuinely the correct reading for this character.
