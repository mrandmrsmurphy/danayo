## Notes
- [尻 (char)](../characters/尻%20(char).md) "buttocks" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [尻 (char)](../characters/尻%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

尻 names the buttocks or rump, originally an oracle-bone pictogram of a person with the buttocks highlighted, later reanalyzed as semantic 尸 ("body") + phonetic 九.

Japanese こう (on'yomi; the native kun'yomi しり, shiri, is the everyday word instead), Korean 고, and Vietnamese khào are all standard readings. No homophones among other words (注音 ㄎㄚㄨ; several unrelated characters share it, including [[考]] and [[靠]], but none of their own stand-in words collide with 尻's).
