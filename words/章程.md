## Notes
- [章 (char)](../characters/章%20(char).md) "chapter" + [程](../characters/程.md) "extent, degree, amount" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (章's is [[章]] itself, 程's is [[程度]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 程 is individually `kwin: false`.

章程 names formal written rules or regulations — the bylaws or charter of an organization, or a regulatory code more broadly, combining 章 ("chapter, section") with 程 ("degree, measure, standard") to convey a structured, codified set of rules.

Mandarin zhāngchéng, Cantonese zoeng1 cing4, Japanese しょうてい (shōtei), and Korean 장정 (jangjeong) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese chương trình is likewise compositional (章's own chương + 程's own trình) — coincidentally identical to the modern everyday Vietnamese word for "program, schedule," though the underlying compound here specifically means regulatory rules/bylaws.
