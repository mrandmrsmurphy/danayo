## Notes
- [困](../characters/困.md) "trapped; hard pressed" + [惑 (char)](../characters/惑%20(char).md) "be deluded; puzzled"

困惑 is to be perplexed: to be at a loss, not knowing what to think or do, or the state of perplexity. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin kùnhuò as "confused; perplexed; bewildered; puzzled" and Japanese こんわく as "bemusement, bewilderment". Korean 곤혹 and Cantonese kwan3 waak6 match.

It is the mind stuck, close to the flustered [狼狽](狼狽.md) but quieter, and it often ends in [寿着](寿着.md), hesitation. Its opposite is the settled [安心](安心.md), ease of mind.
