## Notes
- [忠](../characters/忠.md) "loyalty, devotion, fidelity" + [実](../characters/実.md) "true, accurate" — no stand-in relationship applies (忠's own stand-in is [[忠誠]]; 実's own is [[真実]]).

忠実 means truthful or faithful — loyal and accurate, staying true to one's duty or to a source.

Mandarin zhōngshí, Cantonese zung1 sat6, Japanese ちゅうじつ, Korean 충실, and Vietnamese trung thực are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄨㄫㄙㄧㄊ unique across both words and characters).
