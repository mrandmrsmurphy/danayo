## Notes
- [良](../characters/良.md) "good" + [心 (char)](../characters/心%20(char).md) "heart"

良心 is conscience: the inner sense of what is right, and the pang one feels at doing wrong. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin liángxīn as "conscience; conscientious" and Japanese りょうしん as "conscience". Vietnamese lương tâm and Cantonese loeng4 sam1 match, and Korean 양심 is the same word; the Korean field gives the North Korean form 량심.

It is the heart's judge of conduct, source of [慙愧](慙愧.md), shame, and of [後悔](後悔.md), regret. Its absence is the [無恥](無恥.md), shameless.
