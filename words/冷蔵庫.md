## Notes
"Refrigerator, freezer" — real Mandarin/Cantonese/Japanese/Korean all use compounds built from these or closely related characters. `vietnamese: tủ lạnh` breaks the pattern: it's a native Vietnamese compound (tủ "cabinet" + lạnh "cold"), not a Sino-Vietnamese calque of 冷蔵庫 — no such calque is in everyday use, so the real native word is documented instead. **Real gap found and fixed on [[蔵]]'s own page**: its `vietnamese` field was blank — added tàng, the well-known Sino-Vietnamese reading (kho tàng, bảo tàng, tiềm tàng). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 랑창코 diverges from the compositional Sino-Korean 냉장고 in multiple syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄚㄫㄑㄚㄫㄎㄛ unique among both words and characters).
