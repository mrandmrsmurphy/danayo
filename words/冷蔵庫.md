## Etymology
- [冷 (char)](../characters/冷%20(char).md) "cool, cold" + [蔵](../characters/蔵.md) "hide, store" + [庫](../characters/庫.md) "storehouse, warehouse"

## Notes
"Refrigerator, freezer" — real Mandarin/Cantonese/Japanese/Korean all use compounds built from these or closely related characters. `vietnamese: tủ lạnh` breaks the pattern: it's a native Vietnamese compound (tủ "cabinet" + lạnh "cold"), not a Sino-Vietnamese calque of 冷蔵庫 — no such calque is in everyday use, so the real native word is documented instead.
