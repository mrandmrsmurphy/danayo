## Notes
- [女](../characters/女%20(char).md) "woman" + [児](../characters/児%20(char).md) "baby" — no stand-in relationship applies (女's own stand-in is itself; 児's own is itself).

女児 means daughter — literally "female child," drawing on the everyday Mandarin sense of 女儿 (nǚ'ér).

Mandarin nǚ'ér, Cantonese neoi5 ji4, Korean 녀아, and Vietnamese nữ nhi are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese じょじ is the compositional reading of these same characters, but the real Japanese word has narrowed in sense to "female infant/child" (a clinical or demographic term) rather than "daughter" specifically — the everyday Japanese word for "daughter" is 娘 (むすめ), written with an unrelated character. **Real bugs found and fixed**: `諺文`/`羅馬字` had 女's syllable as 뇻/nyou instead of 느/nǝ (contradicting the word's own correct `注音` and every other 女-initial compound); `japanese` had been swapped out for the unrelated word 娘/むすめ; `korean` had the unrelated native word 딸 appended. Trimmed `aliases` to the two genuine orthographic variants (女兒/女儿); the reversed compound 兒女/儿女 means "children" collectively, a distinct word, not an alias, and has no vault page yet. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄜㄝㄧ unique across both words and characters).
