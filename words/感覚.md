## Notes
- [感](../characters/感.md) "feel, sense" + [覚](../characters/覚.md) "perceive" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [覚](../characters/覚.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (感's own stand-in is [[感触]]).

感覚 means to feel, think, or be of the opinion that — a sense or perception, whether physical or intuitive.

Mandarin gǎnjué, Cantonese gam2 gok3, Japanese かんかく, Korean 감각, and Vietnamese cảm giác are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed missing space in `cantonese`, quoted `hsk_level`, reformatted `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄇㄍㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
