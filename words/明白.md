## Notes
- [明](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright" + [白](../characters/白%20(char).md) "white, clear" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 明 and 白's own stand-ins are themselves).

明白 means clear, obvious, or evident.

Mandarin míngbai, Cantonese ming4 baak6, Japanese めいはく, Korean 명백, and Vietnamese minh bạch are all standard, fully compositional readings. Trimmed malformed comma-duplicate `mandarin` (a stray full-tone variant) to the single standard neutral-tone form. No homophones (注音 ㄇ⼶ㄫㄅㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
