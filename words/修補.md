## Notes
- [修](../characters/修.md) "repair" + [補](../characters/補.md) "repair, mend, restore" — `vietnamese: tu bổ` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese — restoration/maintenance work on buildings, antiques, or structures, nuanced between bảo dưỡng ("maintenance") and sửa chữa ("repair"). `korean: 수보` is the compositional reading. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 숫보 diverges from the compositional Sino-Korean 수보 in the first syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨㄛㄅㄛ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 補's own `stand_in` is this exact word (修補), so 補 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 修補.
