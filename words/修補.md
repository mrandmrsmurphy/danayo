## Notes
`vietnamese: tu bổ` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese (confirmed via web search) — restoration/maintenance work on buildings, antiques, or structures, nuanced between bảo dưỡng ("maintenance") and sửa chữa ("repair"). Filled the blank `korean: 수보` (compositional) and `pos: 事詞`. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 숫보 diverges from the compositional Sino-Korean 수보 in the first syllable's coda.
