## Notes
- [敗](../characters/敗.md) "lose" + [者](../characters/者.md) "person"

敗者 is a loser: the one who is defeated in a contest or a battle. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin bàizhě as "loser; underdog" and Japanese はいしゃ as "loser". Korean 패자 and Cantonese baai6 ze2 match.

It is the person, where [敗北](敗北.md) is the defeat. Its opposite is the [勝者](勝者.md).
