>[!caution] The senses split — Chinese and Japanese "South China Sea", Korean a different sea
>南海 is the Southern Sea, and in Chinese and Japanese specifically the South China Sea, which is the Dan'a'yo sense. Korean 남해 is the South Sea between Jeju and Busan, a different body of water, so a Korean reader may hear that sea instead. Listed as a Split on [文法 - 91疑義語](../grammar/文法%20-%2091疑義語.md).

## Notes
- [南](../characters/南.md) "south" + [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" — the Southern Sea; both the southernmost of the classical [[四海]] and the modern South China Sea

南海 is the Southern Sea: in classical Chinese the southernmost of the four seas, and in modern usage the South China Sea. English Wiktionary lists it for Chinese (Nánhǎi, Cantonese naam4 hoi2) as "South China Sea", the literary and figurative "extreme south", the historical Nanhai Commandery, the historical name of Mount Putuo, and a number of place names in China (a district of Foshan, a lake in Beijing, towns and a township). For Japanese なんかい it gives "a southern sea" and, specifically, "South China Sea" (synonym 南シナ海), and also a short form of the road 南海道. For Korean 남해 it gives only "South Sea (between Jeju and Busan)", a different sea, and a county in South Gyeongsang. It has no Vietnamese entry, so Vietnamese Nam Hải here is built from the characters. The word is listed as a Split on [文法 - 91疑義語](../grammar/文法%20-%2091疑義語.md).

English Wikipedia describes Nanhai Commandery as an ancient Chinese commandery that existed from the Qin dynasty to the Tang dynasty, covering present-day Guangdong, Hainan, southeastern Guangxi and the southern tip of Fujian, with its seat at Panyu, in modern Guangzhou. It describes Mount Putuo, in the East China Sea, as the bodhimaṇḍa of the bodhisattva Guanyin.

The modern South China Sea is a contested sea. English Wikipedia notes the "nine-dash line" claim covering almost the entire body, which overlaps with the claims of virtually every other country in the region, and the Philippines' use of the name West Philippine Sea for the part within its exclusive economic zone. Compare [[北海]] and [[西海]], the other seas of the [[四海]].
