## Notes
- [鱗 (char)](../characters/鱗%20(char).md) "fish scale" — 鱗 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

Mandarin lín, Cantonese leon4, Korean 린, Japanese りん, and Sino-Vietnamese lân are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real homophone found**: `注音 ㄌㄧㄋ`/`린`/`lin` is also carried by [[隣]] ("neighbor") — both themselves legitimized bare words. Added the full callout here and a matching one to 隣.
