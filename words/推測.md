## Notes
- [推](../characters/推%20(char).md) "push" + [測](../characters/測.md) "measure, survey" — no stand-in relationship applies (推's own stand-in is itself; 測's own is [[測量]]).

推測 means to surmise, speculate, or conjecture.

Mandarin tuīcè, Cantonese teoi1 cak1, Japanese すいそく, Korean 추측, and Vietnamese suy trắc are all standard, fully compositional readings.
