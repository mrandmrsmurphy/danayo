## Notes
- [内](../characters/内.md) "inside" + [腔](../characters/腔.md) "chest cavity"
- This word is the stand-in for [[腔]], which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo.

内腔 is the hollow space inside something: in anatomy and medicine the lumen, the inner channel of a tubular structure such as a blood vessel, the intestine or a bronchus, and more broadly an internal body cavity. Mandarin nèiqiāng, Cantonese noi6 hong1, Japanese ないくう (naikū; 血管内腔, "the lumen of a blood vessel") and Korean 내강 (naegang) are the same compound. 腔 is "cavity" (as in 口腔, "oral cavity", and 胸腔, "chest cavity").

In Dan'a'yo it is the technical word for the lumen or inner cavity, filed with the internal organs beside [[腸管]].
