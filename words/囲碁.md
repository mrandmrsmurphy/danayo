## Notes
- [囲](../characters/囲.md) "encircle" + [碁](../characters/碁.md) "Go (game)" — this word is the stand-in for [碁](../characters/碁.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo (囲's own stand-in is [[包囲]]).

囲碁 names the board game Go, using 碁 directly (the dedicated "Go stone" character) rather than the phonetic substitute 棋 seen in the near-duplicate entry [[囲棋]] — the two share an identical Dan'a'yo reading.

**Real bugs found and fixed**: `羅馬字` had a spurious leading apostrophe ('wigi → wigi; 囲's own citation "wi" carries no glottal marker). `japanese` used the archaic kana ゐ (merged into い in modern Japanese) — corrected ゐご → いご. `vietnamese` was capitalized as a proper noun ("Cờ vây") though this is a common noun — corrected to lowercase `cờ vây`. Korean 바둑 is the everyday native term for the game (rather than the more literary/Sino-Korean 위기, used on [[囲棋]]'s own page) — kept as the natural real-world choice. No homophones beyond the documented near-duplicate with [[囲棋]] itself (注音 ㄨㄧㄍㄧ).
