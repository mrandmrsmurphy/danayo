## Notes
- [兄 (char)](../characters/兄%20(char).md) "older brother" + [弟 (char)](../characters/弟%20(char).md) "younger brother" — "brothers, brotherhood." One of the [[天常]] (the constant relational bonds). Mandarin xiōngdì, Cantonese hing1 dai6, Japanese きょうだい, Korean 형제, and Vietnamese huynh đệ are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 횽더 diverges from Sino-Korean 형제 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⼄ㄫㄉㄝ unique among both words and characters).
