## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [鮮](../characters/鮮.md) "fresh"

海鮮 is seafood: fish, shellfish and other fresh food from the sea. Wiktionary glosses both Mandarin hǎixiān and Japanese かいせん as "seafood". Cantonese hoi2 sin1 matches, and Korean 해선 is the reading of the characters.

It is a synonym of [海産](海産.md), the Dan'a'yo word for seafood. It is food from the sea, wider than [魚肉](魚肉.md), fish, and it includes the [蟹](蟹.md), crab, the [烏賊](烏賊.md), squid, and the [明蝦](明蝦.md), prawn.
