## Notes
- [望](../characters/望.md) "look" + [遠 (char)](../characters/遠%20(char).md) "far" + [鏡 (char)](../characters/鏡%20(char).md) "mirror; lens"

望遠鏡 is a telescope: an instrument for seeing far, such as the stars. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin wàngyuǎnjìng as "telescope; binoculars" and Japanese ぼうえんきょう as "a telescope". Cantonese mong6 jyun5 geng3 matches, and Korean 망원경 matches.

It is the chief tool of an [天文台](天文台.md), observatory, and a tool for the study of the [天体](天体.md). A mirror in general is a [鏡](鏡.md).
