## Notes
- [死](../characters/死.md) "die" + [亡](../characters/亡.md) "perish" + [率](../characters/率.md) "rate, ratio" — no stand-in relationship applies (率's own stand-in is [[比率]]).

死亡率 means mortality rate or death rate.

Mandarin sǐwánglǜ, Cantonese sei2 mong4 leot6, Japanese しぼうりつ, Korean 사망률, and Vietnamese tử vong suất are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match [[死亡]]'s and 率's own stored citations exactly; 사망률 correctly retains the ㄹ liaison since 망 ends in ㅇ, not a vowel or ㄴ). No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄜㄇㄚㄫㄌㄨㄊ unique across both words and characters).
