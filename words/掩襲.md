## Notes
- [掩](../characters/掩.md) "to cover; to conceal" + [襲](../characters/襲.md) "to attack" — stand-in for both [掩](../characters/掩.md) and [襲](../characters/襲.md), neither of which can appear independently: 襲's meaning (to attack) is identical, but 襲 alone is too bound to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 掩 ("to conceal; to ambush") specifies the manner of attack.

Mandarin yǎnxí, Cantonese jim2 zaap6, Korean 엄습 (eomseup), and Japanese えんしゅう (enshū) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 掩's and 襲's own stored citations. Vietnamese yểm tập is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese.
