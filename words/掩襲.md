## Notes
- 掩 (MC 影母 + 鹽韻 → ㄝㄇ; no independent vault entry) "to cover; to conceal" + [襲](../characters/襲.md) "to attack" — this word is the viable form of [襲](../characters/襲.md): the character's meaning (to attack) is identical, but 襲 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 掩 ("to conceal; to ambush") specifies the manner of attack, making it a near-synonym legitimizer compound.

Mandarin yǎnxí, Cantonese jim2 zaap6, Korean 엄습 (eomseup), and Japanese えんしゅう (enshū) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 襲's own stored citations (掩 has no independent character page in this vault). Vietnamese yểm tập is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 엄습 matches Sino-Korean 엄습 exactly. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄝㄇㄙㄜㄆ unique among both words and characters).

**Not linked to a lexipedia page**: `Conflict.md` (still a skeletal, unmigrated list) has "trap" and "strike" but neither is a precise fit for "sneak attack, ambush" specifically. Left unlinked rather than force-fit into an imprecise match.
