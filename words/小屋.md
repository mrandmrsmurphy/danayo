## Notes
- [小](../characters/小%20(char).md) "small" + [屋](../characters/屋%20(char).md) "house, shed" — "small house," a hut or shed. Their own `stand_in` values: 小's is itself; 屋's is itself; none points here, so 小屋 is an independent compound.

小屋 is a shed or hut: a small, simple building. Mandarin xiǎowū and Cantonese siu2 uk1 are attested, and in Japanese it is the everyday こや. The Japanese and Korean fields hold the Sino readings of the two characters, not the living word.
