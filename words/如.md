## Notes
- [如](../characters/如%20(char).md) "like, as" — this word is the stand-in for its own character (如's own stand-in is itself).

如 means "like" or "as" — a comparative particle used to draw an analogy or equivalence, as in [[如何]] ("how," literally "like what").

Mandarin rú, Cantonese jyu4, Japanese にょ, Korean 여, and Vietnamese như are all standard, fully compositional readings.
