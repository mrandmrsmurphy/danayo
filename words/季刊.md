## Notes
- [季](../characters/季.md) "season" + [刊 (char)](../characters/刊%20(char).md) "publication; periodical"

季刊 names a quarterly — a periodical published once per season, four times a year. It sits alongside a small family of publication-frequency terms built the same way: 日刊 ("daily"), 週刊 ("weekly"), 月刊 ("monthly"), and 年刊 ("annual"), each substituting a different time unit for 季 ("season").

Cross-linguistically the compound is a stable Sino-Xenic loan: Sino-Korean 계간 and Japanese きかん both directly concatenate the component readings and carry the same sense. Vietnamese, however, does not use a direct Sino-Vietnamese concatenation — the everyday term is quý san ("quarterly publication"), pairing quý ("quarter, season") with san, a Sino-Vietnamese reading tied specifically to publishing (as in nguyệt san, "monthly") rather than the more general reading of 刊 used elsewhere.
