## Notes
- [発 (char)](../characters/発%20(char).md) "emit; send out" + [揮](../characters/揮.md) "to wave; command"

発揮 (traditional 發揮) is "to bring into play; to exert; to display," used of skill, talent or strength, and also "to develop (an idea); to express." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 發揮 (fāhuī, Cantonese faat3 fai1) has both senses; Wiktionary has no Japanese, Korean or Vietnamese section, so `japanese` はっき, `korean` 발휘 and `vietnamese` phát huy are the compositional readings, with the Korean and Vietnamese forms matching the ordinary words (from memory, not re-checked). It is used in the 1919 Korean Declaration of Independence ([宣言書](../translation/宣言書.md)) (自由的精神을 発揮).
