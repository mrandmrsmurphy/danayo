## Notes
- [社](../characters/社.md) "company" + [会 (char)](../characters/会%20(char).md) "meet, gather" + [主](../characters/主.md) "lord, master" + [義](../characters/義.md) "meaning, principle" — literally "society" (社会) + "-ism" (主義).

**Fixed a real bug found on re-check**: `羅馬字`/`諺文` had syahweju'wi/샤훠주위, using 義's `'wi`/위 misreading rather than its own correct `'ǝi`/읫 — the same recurring confusion already flagged and fixed on [[官僚主義]], [[定義]], [[定義域]], [[正義]], and [[主義]]. Corrected to syahweju'ǝi/샤훠주읫. This word had already been stamped `date-last-perfect` earlier today without this bug caught — another reminder that a stamp isn't a full correctness guarantee.

社会主義 is the standard term for socialism, the political and economic doctrine advocating collective or state ownership of the means of production. It is built compositionally from [[社会]] ("society") and the productive doctrine-forming suffix 主義 ("-ism," as also seen in 資本主義 "capitalism," 民主主義 "democracy").

Mandarin shèhuì zhǔyì, Cantonese se5 wui2 zyu2 ji6 (会 taking a colloquial changed tone in this compound), Japanese しゃかいしゅぎ (shakaishugi), Korean 사회주의 (sahoejuui), and Vietnamese xã hội chủ nghĩa are all fully compositional and are the standard political-terminology words across the sphere.
