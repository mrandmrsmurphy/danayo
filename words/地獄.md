## Notes
- [地](../characters/地%20(char).md) "earth, ground" + [獄](../characters/獄.md) "prison, hell" — "earth prison," hell. Their own `stand_in` values: 地's is itself; 獄's is [[監獄]]; none points here, so 地獄 is an independent compound.

地獄 is hell: the place of punishment for the dead. Mandarin dìyù, Cantonese dei6 juk6, Japanese じごく and Korean 지옥 all use it, and Vietnamese địa ngục does too. It is the Buddhist hell, and the Christian hell is said with the same word. See [[基督敎]] for its Christian use, and compare [[九泉]] (the Nine Springs, the Chinese netherworld) and [[悪魔]] (devil).
