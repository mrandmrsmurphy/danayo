## Notes
- [斟](../characters/斟.md) "pour wine" + [酌](../characters/酌.md) "serve wine, pour booze" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [斟](../characters/斟.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (酌's own stand-in is [[酌酒]]).

斟酌 means to consider, discuss, or talk over (literally "pour and serve," extended to weighing options carefully).

Mandarin zhēnzhuó, Cantonese zam1 zoek3, Japanese しんしゃく, Korean 짐작, and Vietnamese châm chước (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄇㄐㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
