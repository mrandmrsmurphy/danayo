## Notes
- [実](../characters/実.md) "real; solid" + [体](../characters/体.md) "body"

実体 is an entity or substance: the real thing underneath its appearances. Wiktionary glosses Japanese じったい as "substance; entity". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 실체 matches.

It is what really is, where [現象](現象.md), the phenomenon, is how it appears. It is also the thing that [実在](実在.md) says exists, and its inner nature is [本質](本質.md).
