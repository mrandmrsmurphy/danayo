## Notes
- [呂](../characters/呂.md) "Lü" + [宋](../characters/宋.md) "Song" — a transliteration of the name of the island, so the characters carry no meaning of their own. 呂 is a 名専字, which appears only in this name; 宋's own stand-in is [[宋朝]]. The alias 吕宋 is the simplified spelling.

呂宋 is a proper noun with four senses at English Wiktionary.

1. Luzon, the largest island of the Philippines.
2. Archaic and historical: the country, the archipelago and the former part of the Spanish Empire, now the Philippines ([[菲律賓]]). The word 呂宋人 is a person from the Philippines; Wiktionary quotes Zhang Xie's *Dongxi yang kao* (1618) on the Chinese who stayed in Luzon.
3. Obsolete and historical: Manila, now [[民埠]] in Dan'a'yo.
4. Cantonese, historical, loosely: the Spanish Empire (呂宋人 "subject of the Spanish Empire").

English Wiktionary gives the etymology as from Tagalog (classical) Lusong, the old name of the Pasig River delta between the medieval polities of Maynila and Tondo, reaching Chinese by way of Hokkien.

Mandarin Lǚsòng and Cantonese leoi5 sung3 are as at English Wiktionary. Japanese ルソン and Korean 루손 are the ordinary names of the island (from memory, not re-checked), so `kwin` is false. The `vietnamese` field holds the compositional Hán-Việt reading lữ tống (each character as its page gives it; not attested for this name).

No homophones (注音 ㄌ⼄ㄙㄛㄫ unique across words and characters, checked 2026-10-12). The 注音 needs no syllable-boundary dot.
