## Notes
- [冷 (char)](../characters/冷%20(char).md) "cold" + [酷](../characters/酷.md) "harsh"

冷酷 is heartless: cold and hard, without any feeling for others' pain. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin lěngkù as "cruel; heartless; callous; unfeeling" and Japanese れいこく as "cruel, severe; cruelty, severity". Cantonese laang5 huk6 matches, and Korean 랭혹 is the reading of the characters.

It is cruelty by coldness, where [残酷](残酷.md) is cruelty by harshness, and the opposite of the warm [温厚](温厚.md), gentle. Plain coldness is the [冷淡](冷淡.md), indifferent.
