## Notes
- [佩](../characters/佩.md) "wear on waist" + [戴 (char)](../characters/戴%20(char).md) "wear on top"
- This word is the viable form of [佩](../characters/佩.md): the character's meaning (to wear accessories) is identical, but 佩 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 佩戴 is its `stand_in` compound.
- `japanese: はいたい` and `vietnamese: bội đái` are honest compositional readings (佩's own はい/bội + 戴's own たい/đái); no independent attestation of this specific compound was found. `kwin` is false: both 佩 and 戴 independently carry `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄈㄛㄧㄊㄚㄧ unique among both words and characters). Legitimizing note: 佩's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, so 佩 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.
