## Notes
- [要](../characters/要.md) "important, need" + [請 (char)](../characters/請%20(char).md) "ask, invite, request, please"

要請 is the formal counterpart to [要求](要求.md) (要 "need" + 求 "seek"). Both compounds put 要 "need, want" first, but the second character sets the register:

- 求 "to seek, pursue" gives 要求 the sense of a **demand or claim** — often a personal or unilateral assertion of what one is owed.
- 請 "to politely ask, invite" (cf. 申請 "apply", 招請 "invite formally") gives 要請 the sense of a **formal appeal or official request** — typically directed upward or outward, e.g. a request from a government, organization, or authority for action or cooperation.

So while both translate loosely as "request/demand," 要求 leans toward *insisting*, and 要請 leans toward *formally asking*.

請 is a 形声 character with the semantic 言 "speech" and the phonetic 青, meaning "to ask, invite, request," as its character page records, and 要 is originally a 象形 character for a waist, extended to "need." The Speech page lists 要請 as "to request, demand — a more formal/official register than 要求's forceful claim," beside [催](催.md) "to press, urge," and Dan'a'yo keeps the sense "request, demand," with the formal register as its characteristic. The readings are Mandarin yàoqǐng, Cantonese jiu3 cing2, Japanese yōsei (ようせい) and Korean 요청.
