## Notes
- [十](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" + [四](../characters/四%20(char).md) "four" + [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — no stand-in relationship applies (all three characters are independent Dan'a'yo entries, each its own stand-in).

十四日 names the 14th day of the month. Japanese has a special native reading, じゅうよっか (jūyokka), rather than the expected on'yomi compound じゅうしにち — a genuine irregular calendar reading, kept as-is.
