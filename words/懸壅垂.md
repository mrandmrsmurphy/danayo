## Notes
- [懸](../characters/懸%20(char).md) "hang, suspend, hoist" + [壅](../characters/壅%20(char).md) "obstruct" + [垂](../characters/垂.md) "hang, dangle" — no stand-in relationship applies (懸/壅 are both their own stand-ins; 垂's own is [[垂掛]]).

懸壅垂 means uvula — the small conical piece of tissue hanging from the soft palate.

Mandarin xuányōngchuí, Cantonese jyun4 jung1 seoi4, and Japanese けんようすい are all standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 목젖 and Vietnamese lưỡi gà are each language's own native term for uvula, more common in everyday usage than a compositional reading of these specific characters. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⼔ㄋㄛㄫㄐㄨㄧ unique across both words and characters).
