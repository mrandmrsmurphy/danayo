## Notes
- [遡](../characters/遡.md) "go upstream; trace back" + [及 (char)](../characters/及%20(char).md) "reach out to"

遡及 is retroactivity, the applying of something to what came before. English Wiktionary glosses Japanese 遡及 (そきゅう) as "retroactivity" and "to retroact; to be retroactive", and Korean 소급 as "retroactivity". It has no Chinese entry for the word. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese fields are built from the readings of the two characters, and 溯及 is an alias.
