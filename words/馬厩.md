## Notes
- [馬 (char)](../characters/馬%20(char).md) "horse" + [厩](../characters/厩.md) "stable" — legitimizing note: 厩's own `stand_in` is [[馬厩]] itself, already annotated in its own citation; 馬's own `stand_in` is 馬 itself, so transitivity fails — no `#cranberry`.

Japanese うまや (umaya) is a native kun'yomi compound (literally "horse-house"), matching the alias 馬屋 written with the same sense. Korean 마구간 (magugan) is the real, standard word for "stable," but is structurally a different, longer compound — 馬廏間 ("horse-stable-room"), listed among the aliases — rather than the compositional Sino-Korean reading of bare 馬厩 itself (마구, magu), which 厩's own `korean_native` field already documents. Sino-Vietnamese mã cứu is the compositional form; everyday Vietnamese instead uses the native phrase chuồng ngựa.
