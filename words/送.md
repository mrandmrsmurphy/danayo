## Notes
- [送](../characters/送%20(char).md) "give" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 送 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

送 means to give, send, or see off — its 形声 semantic component 辵 ("walking, motion") reflects the original sense of escorting someone on a journey or sending something off. It is highly productive: 輸送 ("transportation"), 送還 ("repatriate"), 送別 ("farewell, send-off").

Mandarin sòng, Cantonese sung3, and Korean 송 (song) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading おくる (okuru, "to send, give") rather than the on-reading ソウ (SOU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese tống is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading. This word is part of a genuine three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read song/송/ㄙㄛㄫ): [[竦]] ("awe") and [[誦]] ("recite, chant").
