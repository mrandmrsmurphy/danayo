## Notes
- [領](../characters/領.md) "territory" + [土 (char)](../characters/土%20(char).md) "earth" — 領土 is 領's own legitimizing compound (領's `stand_in` points here), but since 土's own `stand_in` is 土 itself instead, transitivity fails and no `#cranberry` tag applies.

領土 (lǐngtǔ) is the standard Mandarin word for "territory," and Cantonese 領土 (ling5tou2) likewise. Japanese りょうど (ryōdo) and Korean 영토 (yeongto, South Korean standard — 령토 in North Korean orthography) are both standard terms in their languages, as is Sino-Vietnamese lãnh thổ — a clean case with full agreement across all five languages.
