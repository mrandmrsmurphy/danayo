## Notes
- [搭](../characters/搭.md) "board" + [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "ride"
- This word is the viable form of [搭](../characters/搭.md): the character's meaning (to board) is identical, but 搭 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 搭乗 is its `stand_in` compound.

**Merged (2026-09-12, user-directed):** a duplicate entry, `words/搭乘.md`, existed for this same compound (same characters, same readings), titled with 乘 (乗's registered alias) and framed with a verb sense ("board, take a conveyance") rather than this page's noun sense ("boarding, embarkation"). Both senses coexist in real usage (搭乗する as a verb, 搭乗 as a noun); folded into this single entry, with `搭乘` demoted to an `alias` — 乘 never appears in a word's title.
