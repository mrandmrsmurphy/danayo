## Notes
- [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big" + [量](../characters/量.md) "quantity"

大量 is a large amount, a lot. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 大量 (Mandarin dàliàng, Cantonese daai6 loeng6) as "great amount of; large quantity of; large number of; a lot of" and "in a large quantity; in a large number", and Japanese たいりょう as "mass, massive" and "large amounts". It lists Korean 대량 as a hanja form of "large amounts". Chinese also has a second sense, "magnanimous; generous", and Vietnamese đại lượng is glossed "quantity" and "generous; magnanimous", so a Vietnamese reader may hear plain "quantity". The Dan'a'yo sense is the large amount shared by Chinese, Japanese and Korean. Compare [多](多.md), many, and [多少](多少.md), amount.
