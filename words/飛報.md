## Notes
- [飛](../characters/飛.md) "fly" + [報 (char)](../characters/報%20(char).md) "report, tell" — no `#cranberry`: both 飛's and 報's own `stand_in` are themselves; neither points here. Belongs to 飛's own primary reading branch (fei/뻬/ㄈㄝㄧ), distinct from the separate ㄆㄧ branch seen in [[飛行机]]/[[飛語]].

飛報 (fēibào) is a real Mandarin word for "to report urgently, rapid dispatch" (a near-synonym of 急報, emphasizing speed rather than urgency), and Cantonese 飛報 (fei1bou3) likewise. Japanese ひほう (hihō) and Korean 비보 (bibo) are both standard compositional terms in their languages. Sino-Vietnamese phi báo is likewise real and attested, meaning "to report as fast as flying, an urgent dispatch."
