## Notes
- [皮](../characters/皮.md) "skin, hide" + [衣](../characters/衣.md) "clothes" — a garment made of fur or hide.

裘 (qiú), "fur robe, fur coat," is an obscure character that the 論語 translation page counts as appearing 6 times. The passages in the Wikisource text are **鄉黨** 10.6 (five occurrences, describing the furs worn by Confucius according to season and occasion: 緇衣羔裘, 素衣麑裘, 黃衣狐裘, 褻裘長, and 羔裘玄冠不以弔), **公冶長** 5.26 (願車馬衣裘) and **雍也** 6.3 (衣輕裘), which gives seven, one more than the translation page's count (a discrepancy to be reconciled there). 裘 lies outside Dan'a'yo's closed character set, so the vault substitutes 皮衣 — joining 衣, whose meaning "clothes, garment" supplies the translation, with 皮, the "skin, hide, fur" component describing what the garment is made of. This is a "Translation + Component" substitution per [[translation/論語 (book)]]'s Vocabulary list.

The Japanese reading かわごろも (lit. "hide robe") is the natural native compound for fur clothing, distinct from the on-yomi readings of the individual characters. The Korean 피의 and the mandarin/cantonese readings píyī/pei4 ji1 follow directly from 皮 and 衣's own readings.

The translation page's vocabulary list records the substitution as "裘 6x --> 皮衣 - Translation + Component." The Clothing page lists the word as "a fur garment, leather clothing," and Dan'a'yo keeps that sense. Related words in the vault are [皮革](皮革.md) "leather, hide" (the stand-in for 皮) and [衣服](衣服.md) "clothes," which is the general word where 皮衣 names a garment made of hide or fur.
