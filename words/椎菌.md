## Notes
- [椎](../characters/椎.md), here in its Japanese kun-reading sense "chinquapin oak" (シイ, the tree shiitake grows on) rather than its usual "vertebrae, spine" sense, + [菌](../characters/菌.md) "fungus, germ" — no stand-in relationship applies (椎's own stand-in is [[脊椎]]; 菌's own is [[細菌]]).

椎菌 means shiitake mushroom, echoing the real Japanese etymology 椎茸 (しいたけ, "shii-tree mushroom").

Dan'a'yo's own reading (羅馬字 cuigyun, 諺文 취균, 注音 ㄑㄨㄧㄍ⼜ㄋ) is fully compositional, matching 椎's and 菌's own citations exactly. Mandarin xiānggū, Cantonese hoeng1 gu1, Korean 표고, and Vietnamese nấm hương are the real, standard terms for shiitake in their respective languages — none is compositionally built from 椎+菌, since Dan'a'yo's own coinage doesn't correspond to any real Chinese/Korean/Vietnamese term (the real Chinese/Korean names, 香菇/표고, are entirely different roots; the aliases 香菇/瓢菰 preserve these). Japanese しいたけ is the genuine standard term and directly informs this coinage's etymology. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄨㄧㄍ⼜ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
