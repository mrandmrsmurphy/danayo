## Notes
- [箱](../characters/箱%20(char).md) "case, box, chest, trunk" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 箱 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo. Its 形声 phonetic component 相 reflects an originally bamboo-woven box.

Mandarin xiāng, Cantonese soeng1, and Korean 상 (sang) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading はこ (hako, "box") rather than the on-readings ショウ/ソウ (SHOU/SOU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese offers three stored candidates (rương, sương, tương), all attested on the character page. This word is a genuine Dan'a'yo homophone of [[甥]] ("sister's child, sororal niece/nephew") — coincidental, unrelated in meaning.
