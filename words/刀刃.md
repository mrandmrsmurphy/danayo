## Etymology
- [刀](../characters/刀.md) "blade, knife" + [刃](../characters/刃.md) "blade, cutting edge"

## Notes
"Knife blade" (literal), and, by figurative extension, "a pivotal, critical moment" (as if standing on a blade's edge). `vietnamese: đao nhận` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese for the literal blade sense (confirmed via web search); no independent attestation was found for the figurative "critical moment" extension in Vietnamese specifically.
