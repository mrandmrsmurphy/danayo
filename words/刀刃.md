## Etymology

## Notes
"Knife blade" (literal), and, by figurative extension, "a pivotal, critical moment" (as if standing on a blade's edge). `vietnamese: đao nhận` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese for the literal blade sense (confirmed via web search); no independent attestation was found for the figurative "critical moment" extension in Vietnamese specifically. Filled the blank `pos: 名詞`. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 탓닌 diverges entirely from Sino-Korean 도인. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄚㄨㄋㄧㄋ unique among both words and characters).
