## Notes
- [肢](../characters/肢.md) "limbs" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 肢 cannot appear independently outside this compound. [体](../characters/体.md) "group, body, form" is separately legitimized by [[体系]].

肢体 combines 肢 ("limbs") with 体 ("body") — "limbs, members [of the body]." Pronunciation fields (jetei/저테/ㄐㄝㄊㄝㄧ) are the straightforward concatenation of each constituent's own stored reading — no bug. `kwin` is false per the AND-rule: both 肢 and 体 individually carry `kwin: false`.

Mandarin zhītǐ, cantonese zi1tai2, and korean 지체 (jiche) are all standard compositional readings, matching each character's own stored value. Japanese したい (shitai) is the real attested word. Vietnamese chi thể is likewise a real, standard Sino-Vietnamese compound. `aliases` lists 肢體, the traditional-orthography spelling (体's own traditional variant 體).
