## Notes
- [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "great" + [正 (char)](../characters/正%20(char).md) "correct"

Mandarin dàzhèng, Cantonese daai6 zing3, Japanese たいしょう (Taishō), Korean 대정, and Vietnamese Đại Chính are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 大's and 正's own stored citations exactly. `kwin` is false: 大 alone is kwin-true, but 正 independently diverges from Korean on its own character page, which alone is enough to make the AND-rule false. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄧㄐㄧㄫ unique among both words and characters).

1. Just, fair — the classical compositional sense, "greatly correct," attested independently of the era name.
2. The Taishō era (1912–1926), the Japanese reign period named after this classical phrase.
