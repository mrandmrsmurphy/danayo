## Notes
- [小 (char)](../characters/小%20(char).md) "small" + [川 (char)](../characters/川%20(char).md) "river" — literally "small river," a small stream.

Mandarin Xiǎochuān, Cantonese siu2 cyun1, and Korean 소천 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 小's and 川's own stored citations exactly. Japanese おがわ is both the ordinary native word for a small stream and the common surname Ogawa — read with kun'yomi rather than the on'yomi しょうせん (shōsen). Vietnamese has no attested equivalent for this Japanese proper name.

1. a small stream
2. Japanese surname
3. A township in Maojian
