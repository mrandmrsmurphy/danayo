## Notes
- [小 (char)](../characters/小%20(char).md) "small" + [川 (char)](../characters/川%20(char).md) "river" — literally "small river," a small stream.
1. a small stream
2. Japanese surname
3. A township in Maojian

Mandarin Xiǎochuān, Cantonese siu2 cyun1, and Korean 소천 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 小's and 川's own stored citations exactly. Japanese おがわ (Ogawa, native kun'yomi reading) is the real form for this common Japanese surname — read with kun'yomi rather than the on'yomi しょうせん (shōsen), since Ogawa is specifically a native-reading proper name. Vietnamese has no attested equivalent for this specific Japanese proper name and is correctly omitted rather than left blank.

`kwin` is false: 川 alone diverges from Korean on its own character page, which alone is enough to make the AND-rule false; 小 is independently kwin-true. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄛㄑ⺢ㄋ unique among both words and characters).
