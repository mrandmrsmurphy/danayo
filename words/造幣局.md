## Notes
- [造](../characters/造.md) "manufacture, create" + [幣 (char)](../characters/幣%20(char).md) "currency, coin" + [局 (char)](../characters/局%20(char).md) "bureau" — mint; the government bureau that manufactures currency

Wiktionary: Chinese 造幣局, in reference to Japan, "mint (coin factory)"; Japanese 造幣局 (ぞうへいきょく) "mint (money-producing building or institution)"; Korean 조폐국 is the hanja form, "mint". The simplified spelling 造币局 is kept as an alias. 造's own `stand_in` is 創造, so this word is not its legitimizer; 幣's and 局's are themselves. 局 is the same bureau element as in [[郵便局]], [[宇宙局]] and [[消防局]].
