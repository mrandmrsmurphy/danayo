## Notes
- [流](../characters/流.md) "flow" + [言 (char)](../characters/言%20(char).md) "say" — no stand-in relationship applies (流's own stand-in is [[流動]]; 言's own is itself).

流言 means rumor, gossip (literally "flowing words").

Mandarin liúyán and Cantonese lau4 jin4 are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 流's and 言's own stored citations.
