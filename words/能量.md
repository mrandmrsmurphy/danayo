## Notes
- [能](../characters/能.md) "ability" + [量](../characters/量.md) "amount" — "capacity amount," the capacity to do work. Their own `stand_in` values: 能's is [[技能]]; 量's is [[数量]]; none points here, so 能量 is an independent compound.

能量 is energy in the physical sense: the capacity of a system to do work. Mandarin néngliàng, Cantonese nang4 loeng6 and Vietnamese năng lượng use it as the standard word. Japanese says エネルギー, with 能量 uncommon, and Korean 에너지, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[電気]] (electricity) and [[光子]] (photon).
