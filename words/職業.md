## Notes
- [職](../characters/職.md) "duty, profession" + [業 (char)](../characters/業%20(char).md) "profession, business" — 職's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 業's own is [[業]] itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 職業 legitimizes 職 alone. `kwin: true` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: true`.

職業 means a career, job, or profession.

Mandarin zhíyè, Cantonese zik1 jip6, Japanese しょくぎょう (shokugyō), and Korean 직업 (jigeop) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 職業 is itself a common attested word for "profession" in all four languages. Vietnamese chức nghiệp is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
