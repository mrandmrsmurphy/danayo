## Notes
- [帰](../characters/帰.md) "return; revert" + [結 (char)](../characters/結%20(char).md) "tie; conclude" — no stand-in relationship applies (帰's own `stand_in` is [[回帰]], 結's is itself).

帰結 names the conclusion or outcome that a chain of reasoning or events eventually settles into — a logical/narrative endpoint, the state a process ultimately returns to and ties off.

Japanese きけつ and Korean 귀결 are both standard, neutral readings for a logical/narrative conclusion. **Vietnamese quy kết has drifted toward a narrower, more negative register**: rather than the neutral "conclusion" sense, it commonly implies concluding or judging someone unfairly, without sufficient evidence — a more subjective, often accusatory usage distinct from the plain kết luận ("conclusion"). No homophones (注音 ㄍㄨㄧㄍㄝㄊ unique across both words and characters).
