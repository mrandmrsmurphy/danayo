## Notes
- [胡](../characters/胡.md) "reckless" (here used in its earlier "foreign, from the Western Regions" sense) is separately legitimized by [[胡乱]]. [瓜](../characters/瓜.md) "melon" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 瓜 cannot appear independently outside this compound. No cranberry: transitivity fails since 胡's own `stand_in` doesn't point here.

胡瓜 literally "foreign melon" — the cucumber was introduced from Central Asia, hence the 胡- ("foreign") prefix, parallel to 胡椒 ("pepper") and 胡麻 ("sesame") on 胡's own Words list.

**Found and fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字` was missing 胡's own -u- glide (hogwa→hougwa); `諺文`/`注音` had already stayed correct (a rare single-field-only instance of this bug). `kwin: false` was already correct per the AND-rule. Fixed cantonese's stray space (wu4 gwaa1→wu4gwaa1) and converted `japanese`'s semicolon-joined value into a proper list: きゅうり (kyuuri) is the modern lexicalized irregular reading, and きうり (kiuri) its historical pre-sound-change spelling — neither decomposes compositionally from 胡's なんぞ + 瓜's うり, but both are genuinely attested. `korean` 오이 (oi) is likewise the real, standard native Korean word for cucumber (also 瓜's own `korean_native` value) rather than a compositional Sino-Korean coinage — kept as the attested real term. Filled blank vietnamese (hồ qua, the classical Sino-Vietnamese compositional reading; the everyday word is the native dưa chuột/dưa leo).
