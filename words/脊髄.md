## Notes
- [脊](../characters/脊.md) "spine" + [髄](../characters/髄.md) "marrow"

脊髄 is the spinal cord: the cord of nerve tissue inside the spine. Wiktionary glosses Japanese せきずい as "spinal cord". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 척수 matches.

It runs inside the [脊椎](脊椎.md), spine, and carries the signals of the [神経](神経.md), nerves, to and from the [脳](脳.md), brain. The [骨髄](骨髄.md) is the marrow inside bones.
