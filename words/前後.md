## Etymology

## Notes
"Front and rear; before and after; consequences" — a broad spatial/temporal compound (前後不一, "inconsistent, contradictory" — tiền hậu bất nhất). `vietnamese: tiền hậu` is compositional from each character's own confirmed reading, well known from this exact idiom.

**Real content bug found and fixed**: `korean` had a native gloss ("앞뒤," native Korean for "front and back") mixed into the reading field as "전후,앞뒤" — trimmed to 전후 alone. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 전홋 diverges from Sino-Korean 전후 in the second syllable's vowel/coda. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄋㄏㄛㄨ unique among both words and characters).
