## Notes
- [失](../characters/失.md) "lose" + [恋](../characters/恋%20(char).md) "romantic love" — "lose love," to be jilted. Their own `stand_in` values: 失's is [[滅失]]; 恋's is itself; none points here, so 失恋 is an independent compound.

失恋 is to lose love: to be jilted, or to have a love end badly. Mandarin shīliàn (失戀), Cantonese sat1 lyun2, Japanese しつれん and Korean 실련 are attested. Compare [[恋]] (romantic love) and [[離婚]] (divorce).
