## Notes
- [激](../characters/激.md) "incite, provoke" + [怒 (char)](../characters/怒%20(char).md) "angry" — no stand-in relationship applies (激's own stand-in is [[刺激]]; 怒's own is itself).

激怒 means to anger, enrage.

Mandarin jīnù, Cantonese gik1 nou6, Japanese げきど, and Korean 격노 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 激's and 怒's own stored citations exactly.
