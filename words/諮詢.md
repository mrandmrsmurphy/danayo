## Notes
- [諮](../characters/諮.md) "consult with" + [詢](../characters/詢.md) "inquire"

諮詢 means to consult, to seek advice. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 諮詢 (alias 咨詢; Mandarin zīxún, Cantonese zi1 seon1) as "to consult; to seek advice; to make enquiries; to receive counseling". The Japanese ししゅん, Korean 자순 and Vietnamese tư tuân fields are built from the readings of the two characters.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes both characters, [諮](../characters/諮.md) and [詢](../characters/詢.md), which are too bounded to stand alone.
