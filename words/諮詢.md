## Notes
- [諮](../characters/諮.md) "consult with" + 詢 "inquire" (詢 has no character page in this vault yet) — stand-in for [[諮]], which cannot appear independently. `vietnamese: tư tuân` is compositional, using 詢's hvdic-confirmed reading "tuân" (not independently attested via hvdic as a compound).

諮詢 is a near-synonym doubling: both characters independently mean "to consult, to ask advice of," and the compound intensifies rather than shifts the sense. It carries a formal, often institutional register — 諮詢委員会 ("advisory committee"), 法律諮詢 ("legal consultation") — closer to English "to consult (an expert/authority)" than to casual "to ask."

Japanese ししゅん and Korean 자순 are both standard compositional Sino-readings. No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄜㄙ⼜ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
