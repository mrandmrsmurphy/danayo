## Notes
- [体](../characters/体.md) "body" + [重 (char)](../characters/重%20(char).md) "heavy; weight"

体重 is body weight: how heavy a person is. Wiktionary glosses Japanese たいじゅう as "body weight". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 체중 matches.

It goes with [身長](身長.md), height, as the measure of a person's build, and an excess of it is [肥満](肥満.md), obesity.
