## Notes
- [宿](../characters/宿.md) "lodging" + [舎](../characters/舎.md) "inn"

宿舎 names a dormitory or lodging house — shared living quarters provided by an institution, most often a school (学生宿舎, "student dormitory") or company (社員宿舎, "employee housing"), combining 宿's sense of "to stay overnight, lodging" with 舎's sense of "building, house, inn." It denotes institutionally provided housing specifically, distinct from an ordinary private residence.

Cross-linguistically the compound is a stable Sino-Xenic loan: Sino-Korean 숙사 and Japanese しゅくしゃ both directly concatenate the component readings and carry the same institutional-housing sense, and Vietnamese túc xá is likewise an ordinary, freely used word for a dormitory (especially a university dormitory, as in ký túc xá).
