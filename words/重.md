## Notes
- [重 (char)](../characters/重%20(char).md) "heavy" — the bare character used directly as a word, its own `stand_in`.

重 means heavy: of great weight (重量, "weight"; 沉重, "heavy, ponderous"; 重傷, "serious injury"), and by extension important, serious, or severe (重要, "important"; 嚴重, "serious"; 尊重, "respect"). It also reads differently, with the sense of "again, layered" (重複, "repeat"), which this word does not use. The graph is classed under 東 in the vault.

Mandarin zhòng, Cantonese cung5, Japanese じゅう (重量/じゅうりょう, "weight"; 重要/じゅうよう, "important") with the alternate on'yomi ちょう (貴重/きちょう, "precious"), Korean 중 (중량, 중요), and Vietnamese trọng (trọng lượng, "weight"; trọng yếu, "important") are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native adjective 重い (おもい) for the everyday word, so on'yomi じゅう is bound to compounds; Korean has native 무겁다.
