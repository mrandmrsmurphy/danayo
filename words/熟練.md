## Notes
- [熟](../characters/熟.md) "ripen" + [練](../characters/練.md) "practice" — no stand-in relationship applies (熟's own stand-in is [[成熟]]; 練's own is [[練習]]).

熟練 means skillful, proficient.

Mandarin shúliàn, Cantonese suk6 lin6, Japanese じゅくれん, Korean 숙련 and Vietnamese thục luyện are all standard, fully compositional readings. Mandarin has only the single reading shúliàn here, as on [[熟知]], not a second shóuliàn.
