## Notes
- [夜 (char)](../characters/夜%20(char).md) "night" + [番 (char)](../characters/番%20(char).md) "turn, shift" — night shift.

夜番 is the night shift: the turn of duty worked at night, and the keeping of watch overnight. It is "night-shift" from [[夜]] and [[番]]. Japanese よばん (also やばん) is confirmed on the Japanese Wiktionary as "a duty assigned for nighttime shifts" and "keeping watch at night, making nightly rounds." Chinese and Korean are not confirmed; the regional words for night shift are Chinese 夜班, Japanese 夜勤 and Korean 야간 (unverified here), so `mandarin`, `cantonese` and `korean` are left blank. The day counterpart is [[日番]]. Like the other shift words, it belongs to the 番 family, a systematic schema that Dan'a'yo imposes for predictability rather than a claim about the ancient usage.
