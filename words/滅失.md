## Notes
- [滅 (char)](../characters/滅%20(char).md) "wipe out" + [失](../characters/失.md) "lose" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 失 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (滅's own stand-in is itself, so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

滅失 means to lose (a legal term for the total loss or destruction of property).

Mandarin mièshī, Cantonese mit6 sat1, Japanese めっしつ, and Korean 멸실 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 滅's and 失's own stored citations exactly). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with the compositional diệt thất (滅's own diệt + 失's own thất). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄝㄊㄙㄧㄊ unique across both words and characters).
