## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [粛](../characters/粛.md) "solemn" — 粛 is used here purely as a phonetic-substitute graph for 嘯 ("whistle, roar"), which has no character page of its own in this vault; the real word is 海嘯 (hǎixiào, "tsunami"), matching the aliases listed. No stand-in relationship applies in the usual sense.

海粛 means tsunami.

Fixed a real bug: `羅馬字`/`注音` had both been missing their final consonant (haisu/ㄏㄚㄧㄙㄨ instead of haisug/ㄏㄚㄧㄙㄨㄎ), inconsistent with `諺文`'s already-correct 해숙 and with 粛's own stored citation (sug/숙/ㄙㄨㄎ) — corrected. Mandarin hǎixiào and Cantonese hoi2 siu3 reflect the real word 海嘯 (using 嘯's real reading, not 粛's own sù/suk1). Fixed a historical kana spelling in `japanese` (かいせう → かいしょう) and reformatted alongside the real everyday word つなみ (from 津波, already listed as an alias). Fixed `korean` (was 해소, likely contamination from an unrelated word 解消 "resolution") → 해일, the real Korean word for tsunami (海溢). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with sóng thần, the real native Vietnamese term ("god wave"). No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄧㄙㄨㄎ unique across both words and characters).
