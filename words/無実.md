## Notes
- [無 (char)](../characters/無%20(char).md) "lack" + [実](../characters/実.md) "true; accurate"

無実 is innocence in fact: not having done the act one is accused of, and so also a charge that has no basis. Wiktionary glosses Japanese むじつ as "false, untrue" and "falsehood; innocence". It is literally "without truth". Wiktionary has no Chinese or Korean entry, so the Mandarin, Cantonese and Korean readings are built from the character readings.

It differs from [無罪](無罪.md), "not guilty". 無罪 is a verdict, a court's finding that the offence is not proved, while 無実 is a fact about the person, who did not do it. A person can be found 無罪 without being 無実, and a 無実 person can still be convicted, which is the situation of [冤罪](冤罪.md).
