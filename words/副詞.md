## Notes
- [副](../characters/副%20(char).md) "secondary" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "word" — no stand-in relationship applies (副 is its own stand-in; 詞's own stand-in is [[単詞]]).

副詞 is the grammatical term for "adverb" — literally "secondary word," a word that modifies a verb, adjective, or another adverb.

Japanese ふくし (FUKU + SHI), Korean 부사, and Vietnamese phó từ are all standard, fully compositional readings.
