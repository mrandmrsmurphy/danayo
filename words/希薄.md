## Notes
- [希](../characters/希.md) "rare; hope" + [薄](../characters/薄.md) "thin" — this word is the stand-in for [薄](../characters/薄.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo. 希 here draws on its "rare, sparse" sense (matching its alias 稀, used in the more common everyday spelling of this word) rather than its "to hope" sense.

希薄 (稀薄) describes thinness or sparseness — of air (酸素が希薄な高地, "high altitude with thin oxygen"), of a solution (dilution), or figuratively of an attitude or resolve grown weak and diminished.

Mandarin xībó (稀薄, "thin, rarefied"; 稀薄空氣, "thin air"), Cantonese hei1 bok6, Japanese きはく, Korean 희박 (희박하다, "to be thin, slim"; 희박한 가능성, "slim chance"), and Vietnamese hi bạc are all standard readings. Vietnamese also has the native mỏng for "thin" and Japanese the native 薄い (うすい).
