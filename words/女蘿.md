## Notes
- [女 (char)](../characters/女%20(char).md) "woman" + [蘿](../characters/蘿.md) "vine, creeper" — dodder; beard moss; this word is the stand-in for 蘿, which cannot appear independently.

女蘿 is dodder, a parasitic vine, and beard moss, the trailing lichen (*Usnea*) that hangs from trees. It is 女 "woman" plus 蘿 "vine, creeper": the two creeping plants that cling to a host were poetically imagined as a woman clinging to a tree. Mandarin nǚluó names both the dodder and the beard moss (simplified 女萝, with the variants 女羅 and 女罗), and Japanese じょら names the beard moss, a synonym of サルオガセ (猿麻桛). Wiktionary has no Korean or Vietnamese entry, so those fields are left blank, and Cantonese was not checked. The Chinese synonyms are 菟絲 for the dodder and 松蘿 for the beard moss, neither of which is in the vault. This word is the stand-in that legitimizes [蘿](../characters/蘿.md), which is too bound to stand alone.
