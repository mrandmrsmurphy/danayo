## Notes
- [生](characters/生.md) "life" + [物](characters/物%20(char).md) "thing" + [学](characters/学.md) "learn" — no stand-in relationship applies (生's own stand-in is [[生活]]; 物's and 学's own are [[物]] itself and [[学習]] respectively).

生物学 means biology.

Mandarin shēngwùxué, Japanese せいぶつがく (SEI+BUTSU+GAKU), Korean 생물학, and Vietnamese sinh vật học were already correct and fully compositional. Fixed a real typo in `cantonese` (saang1 mat6 hok6 → sang1 mat6 hok6, the same recurring bug already fixed on [[生徒]]/[[生死]]). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄚㄫㄇㄨㄊㄏㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
