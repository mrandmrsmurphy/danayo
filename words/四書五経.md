## Notes
- [四 (char)](characters/四%20(char).md) "four" + [書](characters/書.md) "book" + [五 (char)](characters/五%20(char).md) "five" + [経 (char)](characters/経%20(char).md) "classics" — the Four Books and Five Classics, the core canon of the Confucian tradition, combining [[四書]] and [[五経]]:

- [[四書]]/Four Books
  - [[大学]]/大學 (Dàxué, “Great Learning”) 
  - [[中庸]] (Zhōngyōng, “Doctrine of the Mean”) 
  - [[論語]]／论语 (Lúnyǔ, “Analects”) 
  - [[孟子]] (Mèngzǐ, “Mencius”)
- [[五経]]/Five Classics
  - [詩経](詩経.md)/詩經／诗经 (Shījīng, “Classic of Poetry”) 
  - [[書経]]/書經／书经 (Shūjīng, “Book of Documents”) 
  - [[礼記]]/禮記/礼记 (Lǐjì, “Book of Rites”) 
  - [[易経]]/易經／易经 (Yìjīng, “I Ching”) 
  - [[春秋]] (Chūnqiū, “Spring and Autumn Annals”)

Mandarin Sìshūwǔjīng, Cantonese sei3 syu1 ng5 ging1, Japanese ししょごきょう, Korean 사서오경, and Vietnamese Tứ thư Ngũ kinh are all standard, fully compositional readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 싀쇼오겅 diverges from Sino-Korean 사서오경 in three of its four syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄜㄙ⼄·ㄛㄍㄝㄫ unique). **Found and fixed missing citations** on 四(char).md, 書.md, and 五(char).md's own Words lists (only 経(char).md already had one); opportunistically added the similarly-missing [[四書]] citation to 四(char).md and 書.md while there.
