## Notes
- [印](../characters/印.md) "seal, stamp, chop, mark" + [度](../characters/度.md) "degree, manner" + [洋](../characters/洋.md) "ocean, sea" — no stand-in relationship applies (印's own stand-in is [[封印]]; 度's is [[程度]]; 洋's is [[大洋]]).

印度洋 names the Indian Ocean — [[印度]] "India" + 洋 "ocean."

Japanese インドよう mixes the katakana phonetic loan インド (for "India") with the Sino-Japanese on'yomi よう (for 洋), Korean 인도양 and Vietnamese Ấn Độ Dương are standard readings, all matching Dan'a'yo's own reading exactly (`kwin: true`). Fixed empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄧㄋㄉㄛ·⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
