## Notes
- [集](../characters/集.md) "assemble, gather" + [合](../characters/合%20(char).md) "join, fit together, combine" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes 集 as a bound character (合's own stand-in is [[合]] itself; transitivity fails, so no `#cranberry`).

集合 means to assemble or gather — both the everyday sense of people gathering at a meeting point, and the mathematical sense of a "set" (集合論, "set theory"). Mandarin 集合 (jíhé), Cantonese 集合 (zaap6hap6), Japanese 集合 (しゅうごう), Korean 집합 (jiphap), and Sino-Vietnamese tập hợp all carry both senses across the CJKV sphere.

**Found and fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字` had jibkab, mismatched with 合's own correctly-stored gob (諺文 곱 and 注音 ㄍㄛㄆ were both already correct) — corrected to jibgob.
