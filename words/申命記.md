## Notes
- [申](../characters/申.md) "state, extend, restate" + [命](../characters/命.md) "command, life" + [記](../characters/記.md) "record, account" — "Second Life Story," the book of the restated law. Their own `stand_in` values: 申's is [[申告]]; 命's is [[運命]]; 記's is [[記憶]]; none points here, so 申命記 is an independent compound.

申命記 is Deuteronomy, the fifth book of the Torah: Moses's farewell speeches restating the law to the new generation before they enter the land. It is the fifth book of [[bible/聖経 (book)]], after [[民数記]]. Like Numbers it is the real Chinese, Japanese and Korean name: Mandarin shēnmìngjì, Cantonese sen1 meng6 gei3, Japanese しんめいき and Korean 신명기 are all standard. It reads 申 "to state, restate" plus 命 "command" (and "life") plus 記 "record," the law given a second time, a meaning that tracks the Greek "second law"; the draft gloss on the page is "Second Life Story." See [[基督敎]].
