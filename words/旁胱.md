## Notes
- [旁](../characters/旁.md) "right part of a character" + [胱](../characters/胱.md) "bladder" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [胱](../characters/胱.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

旁胱 means urinary bladder.

Mandarin pángguāng, Cantonese pong4 gwong1, Japanese ぼうこう, Korean 방광, and Vietnamese bàng quang are all standard, fully compositional readings. Modernized archaic kana ばうくわう→ぼうこう. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄫㄍ⺢ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
