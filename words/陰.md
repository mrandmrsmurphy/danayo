## Notes
- [陰 (char)](../characters/陰%20(char).md) "Yin, shade" — the shaded, dark, passive, feminine principle of the Yin-Yang (陰陽) cosmological pair, opposite [[陽]]; this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [陰 (char)](../characters/陰%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

Mandarin yīn, Cantonese jam1, Japanese いん (in), Korean 음, and Vietnamese âm are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching this character's own stored citations exactly, and are each the real, standard word for this concept in their language — the Yin-Yang framework is shared essentially unchanged across all five cultural spheres, unlike many concepts that drift or narrow in translation. Concretely, 陰 also denotes the shaded side of a hill or the north bank of a river (as opposed to 陽, the sunlit side/south bank), a literal sense that underlies its later cosmological and philosophical extension.
