## Notes
- [焦](../characters/焦%20(char).md) "scorched, burnt" + [油](../characters/油%20(char).md) "oil" — "burnt oil," tar. Their own `stand_in` values: 焦's is itself; 油's is itself; none points here, so 焦油 is an independent compound.

焦油 is tar: the black, sticky substance obtained by burning or distilling wood, coal or oil. Mandarin jiāoyóu and Cantonese ziu1 jau4 are the ordinary words. Japanese says タール and Korean 타르, so those two fields hold the character readings; Japanese しょうゆ here is the reading of 焦油, not the soy sauce 醤油. Compare [[石油]] (petroleum) and [[油]] (oil).
