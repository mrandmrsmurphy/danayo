## Notes
- [委](../characters/委%20(char).md) "appoint, send, commission" + [託](../characters/託.md) "entrust, rely on, commission" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [託](../characters/託.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (委's own stand-in is itself).

委託 means to entrust or commission — delegating a task or responsibility to another party.

Mandarin wěituō, Cantonese wai2 tok3, Japanese いたく, Korean 위탁, and Vietnamese ủy thác are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `pos: 動詞`. No homophones (注音 ⼔ㄧㄊㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
