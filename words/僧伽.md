## Notes
- [僧](../characters/僧.md) "priest, monk, bonze" + [伽](../characters/伽.md) "gya" — the Sanskrit-transliterating source compound for saṃgha (संघ, "community"), from which the clipped form [[僧]] itself derives. "The sangha, the Buddhist monastic community" (also loosely "a Buddhist monk" by extension). Mandarin sēngqié, Japanese そうぎゃ, Korean 승가, and Vietnamese tăng già are all directly attested standard readings; `cantonese: zang1 ke4` uses 伽's alternate Buddhist-context reading (as in 伽藍, "monastery," kèlán/ke4 laam4) rather than its more common 伽利略-type "gaa1" reading, matching Mandarin's own qié (not jiā) here.
