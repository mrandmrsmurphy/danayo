## Notes
- [僧](../characters/僧.md) "priest, monk, bonze" + [伽](../characters/伽.md) "gya" — the Sanskrit-transliterating source compound for saṃgha (संघ, "community"), from which the clipped form [[僧]] itself derives. "The sangha, the Buddhist monastic community" (also loosely "a Buddhist monk" by extension). Mandarin sēngqié, Japanese そうぎゃ, Korean 승가, and Vietnamese tăng già are all directly attested standard readings; `cantonese: zang1 ke4` uses 伽's alternate Buddhist-context reading (as in 伽藍, "monastery," kèlán/ke4 laam4) rather than its more common 伽利略-type "gaa1" reading, matching Mandarin's own qié (not jiā) here.

**Real content bug found, then found again in the opposite direction.** This word's `注音` (ㄙㄜㄫㄍ⼘, "seng"-pattern) was originally internally inconsistent with its own `諺文`/`羅馬字` (숭갸/sunggya, "sung"-pattern) — flagged from 僧(char)'s own Notes as needing a dedicated word-sweep pass. A first fix (2026-09-11) corrected `注音` to the "sung"-pattern to match 諺文/羅馬字, on the reasoning that three siblings ([[僧侶]], [[尼僧]], [[密陀僧]]) also used "sung." Revisiting the whole cross-word split fresh: 僧's own authoritative citation reading is "seng" (ㄙㄜㄫ), independently supported by its regular Middle Chinese derivation, its entire phonetic family (曽/増/憎/層/謄/騰/贈), and `kwin: true`'s exact match to Sino-Korean 승 — and the "seng"/"sung" split doesn't track any semantic, positional, or register pattern across the six citing words (monks, a nun, a sound-only transliteration, and a stand-in all fall on both sides). The "sung" reading in [[僧伽]]/[[僧侶]]/[[尼僧]]/[[密陀僧]] was simply an old uncorrected error, not a genuine second reading — corrected all three fields here to 승갸/sǝnggya/ㄙㄜㄫㄍ⼘, matching 僧's own regular citation and [[僧家]]/[[阿僧祇]]. Fixed unspaced cantonese (kept from the earlier pass). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 승갸 diverges from Sino-Korean 승가 (a here vs. Sino-Korean's own 가). No homophones (注音 ㄙㄜㄫㄍ⼘ unique among both words and characters).
