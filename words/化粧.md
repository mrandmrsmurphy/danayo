## Notes
- [化 (char)](../characters/化%20(char).md) "-ification, transformation" + [粧](../characters/粧.md) "to apply makeup" — "to put on make-up" (化粧品, "cosmetics"). `vietnamese: hóa trang` is one of the most common everyday Vietnamese words for this concept, a direct match.

**Real content bug found and fixed**: `japanese` had a malformed long-vowel-mark rendering ("けしょー," the same bug pattern found on [[別荘]]) — corrected to けしょう. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 화좡 diverges from Sino-Korean 화장 in the second syllable's vowel/coda. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⺢ㄐ⺢ㄫ unique among both words and characters).
