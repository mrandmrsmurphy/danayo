## Notes
- [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" + [証](../characters/証.md) "proof" — "personal evidence, testimony," specifically evidence given by a person (as opposed to physical/documentary evidence). Contrast with [[物証]] ("physical evidence, material evidence"), the corresponding term for non-testimonial evidence. `vietnamese: nhân chứng` is standard Vietnamese, though there it more commonly means "witness" (the person) rather than "personal evidence/testimony" itself (as in nhân chứng vật chứng, "witnesses and evidence") — documented as a related but not exact semantic match. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 닌징 diverges from Sino-Korean 인증 in the first syllable's initial consonant, the same divergence shared with its homophone [[認証]]. No other homophones (注音 ㄋㄧㄋㄐㄧㄫ matches only [[認証]]).
