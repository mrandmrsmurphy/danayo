## Notes
- [咸](../characters/咸.md) "complete, united" + [興](../characters/興%20(char).md) "entertain; prosper" — the name of Hamhung, a city of North Korea.

咸興 (Hamhung) is a city on the east coast of North Korea and the capital of South Hamgyong Province ([[咸鏡]]南道); English Wiktionary calls it the provincial capital, and the Vietnamese Wikipedia calls it the second-largest city of the country (from memory, not re-checked). The name combines 咸 "all, united" and 興 "rise, prosper".

English and Chinese Wiktionary have 咸興 as the city (Mandarin Xiánxīng, with 興 read xīng, not the xìng of the character page; Cantonese haam4 hing1), and English Wiktionary has a Korean entry as a hanja form of 함흥. Japanese Wiktionary has no entry for the Korean city, so the Japanese reading かんこう is composed from the on'yomi of the characters (the usual Japanese name is katakana ハムン, from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Hàm Hưng is the name used by the Vietnamese Wikipedia.
