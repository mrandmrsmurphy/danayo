## Notes
- [成 (char)](../characters/成%20(char).md) "become (stand-in: itself)" + [果](../characters/果.md) "fruit (stand-in: [[果実]])"

Wiktionary: Chinese 成果 "gain; achievement; positive result; finding"; Japanese 成果 "accomplishments, results, achievements"; Korean 성과 is the hanja form, "result; outcome; fruits; product"; Vietnamese thành quả is the Han form, "fruits; achievements". Compare [[結果]], the plain result.
