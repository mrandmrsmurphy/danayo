## Notes
- [戈](../characters/戈.md) "dagger-axe" + [鉤 (char)](../characters/鉤%20(char).md) "hook"

Wiktionary has no entry for 戈鉤; it is a compound of 戈 and 鉤. Wiktionary glosses 戈 as "(historical) dagger-axe, an ancient Chinese weapon similar to a halberd", and 鉤 as "hook; barb" and, in Chinese calligraphy, "a stroke with a hook". It names the dagger-axe hook, the long slanting hooked stroke of the character 戈, a sub-variety of the hook ([[鉤]]) listed on the [[Stroke]] page beside [[臥鉤]]. Wikipedia (Eight Principles of Yong) lists the hook principle as 趯. Its readings are compositional.
