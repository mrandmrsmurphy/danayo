## Notes
- [戈](../characters/戈.md) "dagger-axe" + [鉤 (char)](../characters/鉤%20(char).md) "hook"

**戈鉤** is the *dagger-axe hook*, the long slanting stroke with a hook at the end that appears in the character 戈 itself and in characters built on it. Chinese instruction also calls it the 斜鉤 ("slanting hook"): the brush presses down to the lower right in a curve, heaviest toward the end, and then flicks up to the upper right to form the hook. It is a sub-variety of the hook ([[鉤]]), the fourth of the eight principles of the 永字八法, and is listed on the [[Stroke]] page beside [[臥鉤]].

戈 names the ancient dagger-axe; the stroke is named for the character in which it is most characteristic.

The Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and North Korean forms given here are compositional. No Vietnamese form was found attested, so none is given.
