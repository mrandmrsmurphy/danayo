## Notes
- [収](../characters/収.md) "gather, collect" + [集](../characters/集.md) "assemble" — no stand-in relationship applies (収's own stand-in is [[回収]]; 集's is [[集合]]).

収集 means to collect or gather — amassing items (収集家 "collector," ゴミ収集 "garbage collection").

Japanese しゅうしゅう, Korean 수집, and Vietnamese thu tập are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `pos: 名詞`. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨㄛㄐㄧㄆ unique across both words and characters). Near-synonym of this vault's own [[聚集]] (also "to gather, collect") — a fully independent word with its own distinct reading (ㄑㄨㄧㄐㄧㄆ), not a spelling variant of this one.
