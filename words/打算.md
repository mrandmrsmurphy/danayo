## Notes
- [打](../characters/打.md) "hit" + [算](../characters/算%20(char).md) "calculate" — no stand-in relationship applies (打's own stand-in is [[打撃]]; 算's own is itself).

打算 means to plan or intend.

Mandarin dǎsuàn, Cantonese daa2 syun3, Japanese ださん, Korean 타산, and Vietnamese đả toán are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄙ⺢ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
