## Notes
- [汎 (char)](../characters/汎%20(char).md) "pan-" + [濫](../characters/濫.md) "flooding, excessive"
- 汎濫 is the stand-in for [濫](../characters/濫.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.

`vietnamese: phiếm lạm` is compositional (汎's own phiếm + 濫's own lạm), the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading also seen in lạm dụng "abuse" and lạm phát "inflation."

[[泛濫]] is not an alias: it is a third, separate independent word built on 泛 (a distinct character from 汎, with its own distinct Dan'a'yo reading ㄈㄧㄇ rather than 汎's ㄈㄚㄇ) See that word's own page for the full three-way 氾濫/汎濫/泛濫 orthographic-variant discussion.
