## Notes
- [汎 (char)](../characters/汎%20(char).md) "pan-" + [濫](../characters/濫.md) "flooding, excessive"
- 汎濫 is the stand-in for [濫](../characters/濫.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.

Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with phiếm lạm (汎's own phiếm + 濫's own lạm, the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading also seen in lạm dụng "abuse" and lạm phát "inflation" — 濫's own citation stores lạm only as one of a long, apparently-scrambled list of native-looking forms; flagged as a character-level gap for future review, not fixed here).

**Real homophone found**: 氾 and 汎 are distinct characters (different composition, 水+𢎘 vs 水+凡) that happen to share the identical Dan'a'yo reading (both MC 敷/奉 ɨɐm → fam/빰/ㄈㄚㄇ). 氾's own `stand_in` is [[氾濫]] (legitimizing 氾), while 濫's own `stand_in` is this word, 汎濫 (legitimizing 濫) — two genuinely independent word pages sharing 注音 ㄈㄚㄇㄌㄚㄇ. Added the full callout here and a matching one to [[氾濫]].
