## Notes
- [正 (char)](../characters/正%20(char).md) "correct" + [体](../characters/体.md) "form; body" — "the correct form." No stand-in relationship applies (正's own stand-in is itself; 体's is [[体系]]).

1. the standard form; a true form or real identity: what a thing really is beneath its appearance, as in Japanese 正体を現す "to show one's true colours".

This word is often used as an abbreviation of the compound [[正体字]].

In Dan'a'yo the standard form is the one that conforms to the vault's own character set, which the language takes as the [[新字体]] forms, so 正体 is not a synonym of [[繁体字]] as it is in Taiwanese usage. Mandarin zhèngtǐ, Cantonese zing3 tai2 and Japanese せいたい are listed on Wiktionary. Korean 정체 and Vietnamese chính thể are the compositional readings and were not checked against a dictionary; note that Vietnamese chính thể is also the usual word for a form of government, which is a different Chinese word (政體).
