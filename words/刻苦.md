## Notes
- [刻](../characters/刻.md) "carve, strict, harsh" + [苦 (char)](../characters/苦%20(char).md) "bitter, suffering" — assiduous; bearing hardship with strict discipline; working hard despite difficulty

Wiktionary: Chinese "industrious; assiduous; diligent"; of life, "basic; frugal"; as an adverb "industriously, assiduously, diligently, hard"; dialectal, "to treat harshly"; Japanese 刻苦 (こっく) "hard work" and, as a verb, "to work hard". The Korean, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are not separately listed.
