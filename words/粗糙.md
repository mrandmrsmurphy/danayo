## Notes
- [粗](../characters/粗.md) "coarse" + 糙 "rough, unpolished" — 粗's own stand-in is this exact compound, legitimizing it as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 糙 has no character page of its own in this vault, so the usual transitivity check (`#cranberry`) cannot be evaluated for it. 糙's Dan'a'yo phonology (ㄐㄛ) is inferred from its Korean reading 조, matching 粗's own ㄐㄛ syllable exactly — left `vietnamese` unset since 糙 has no independently verified reading in that language.

粗糙 describes something coarse, rough, or unrefined in texture or finish — a doubled reinforcement of near-synonymous characters, both meaning "coarse" (粗 of coarsely-hulled grain, 糙 of unpolished/unmilled rice specifically).

Mandarin cūcāo, Cantonese cou1 cou3, Japanese そぞう (sozō), and Korean 조조 (jojo) are all standard, fully compositional readings.
