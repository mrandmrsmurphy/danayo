## Notes
- [容 (char)](../characters/容%20(char).md) "look; appearance" + [疑](../characters/疑.md) "doubt; question" + [者](../characters/者.md) "-er"

容疑者 is the suspect: a person suspected of a crime but not yet charged. Wiktionary glosses Japanese ようぎしゃ as "suspect, a person suspected of committing a crime" and lists Korean 용의자 with the same sense. Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings given are compositional, built from the character readings. Chinese speakers more often say 嫌疑人, but 容疑者 reads transparently as "the one who has the look of doubt about them".

It marks a stage in the sequence. The 容疑者 is suspected, becomes the [被告](被告.md) once [起訴](起訴.md) brings the charge, and is a [犯人](犯人.md) if the act is proved and the finding is [有罪](有罪.md).
