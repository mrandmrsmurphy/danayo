## Notes
- [身](../characters/身.md) "body, self" + [体](../characters/体.md) "body, form" — "body, human body." Mandarin shēntǐ, cantonese san1 tai2, japanese しんたい, korean 신체, and vietnamese thân thể are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. `身體` is the standard traditional-character spelling variant (体→體), correctly listed as an alias. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 신테 diverges from Sino-Korean 신체 in the final. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄋㄊㄝㄧ unique among both words and characters).

身's own `stand_in` is this exact word (身体), so 身 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 身体; 体's own `stand_in` is instead 体系, so this word does not legitimize 体.
