## Notes
- [編](../characters/編.md) "compile, weave" + [集](../characters/集.md) "gather, collect" — 編's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 集's own is [[集合]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 編集 legitimizes 編 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 編 is individually `kwin: false`.

編集 means to compile or edit — assembling and arranging material into a finished work.

Mandarin biānjí, Cantonese pin1 zaap6, and Korean 편집 (pyeonjip) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 編集 is itself a common attested word for "edit, compile" in all three. Japanese へんしふ is the historical kana spelling of へんしゅう (henshū). Vietnamese biên tập is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term for "edit." No Dan'a'yo homophones.
