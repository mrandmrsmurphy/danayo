## Notes
- [前 (char)](../characters/前%20(char).md) "before, previous" + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — "the previous month," part of the same family as [[前年]] tiền niên and [[前世紀]] tiền thế kỷ. `vietnamese: tiền nguyệt` is compositional from each character's own confirmed reading.

**Real kwin bug found and fixed**: `kwin` was stored as true, but byte-level Unicode verification shows Dan'a'yo 전웓 (coda ㄷ, U+C6D3) genuinely diverges from Sino-Korean 전월 (coda ㄹ, U+C6D4) — the same recurring MC-checked-coda-vs-Sino-Korean-regularized divergence pattern found elsewhere in this vault (e.g. 一/七/月/越/舌/骨). Corrected to `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄋ⼔ㄊ unique among both words and characters).
