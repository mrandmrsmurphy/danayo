## Notes
- [競](../characters/競.md) "compete, contend" + [争](../characters/争.md) "fight, dispute" — 競's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 争's own is [[抗争]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 競 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 争 is individually `kwin: false`.

競争 means to compete or contend — rivalry between parties striving for the same goal, whether in business (競争相手, "competitor, rival"), sport, or any contest. It is a broader, more neutral term than 争's own connotation of hostile conflict alone.

Mandarin jìngzhēng, Cantonese ging6 zang1, Japanese きょうそう (kyōsō), and Korean 경쟁 (gyeongjaeng) are all standard, fully compositional readings, each the ordinary everyday word for "competition" in its language. Vietnamese cạnh tranh is likewise compositional and is itself an everyday Vietnamese word for "competition."
