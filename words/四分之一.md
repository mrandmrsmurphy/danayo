## Notes
- [四 (char)](../characters/四%20(char).md) "four" + [分 (char)](../characters/分%20(char).md) "part, divide" + [之 (char)](../characters/之%20(char).md) (classical possessive/linking particle) + [一 (char)](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" — literally "one part of four," the classical Chinese fraction construction (X分之Y = "Y parts of X"), still standard in modern Mandarin/Cantonese/Japanese for expressing fractions. `vietnamese: tứ phân chi nhất` is a literal classical-style calque using each character's own confirmed Sino-Vietnamese reading; modern everyday Vietnamese instead expresses this fraction natively as một phần tư ("one part four"), not with the classical X分之Y construction (not independently confirmed by search).
