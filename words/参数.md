## Etymology
- [参](../characters/参.md) "to refer to, consult" (as in 参考 "reference" — not "participate") + [数](../characters/数.md) "number, quantity" (the shù noun reading)

## Notes
"Parameter" (mathematics, programming, engineering). `vietnamese: tham số` is one of the most common everyday Vietnamese technical words for this exact concept, a direct compositional match. Japanese and Korean instead use their own established terms — the direct loanword パラメータ and the native-compositional 매개변수 ("intermediary variable"), respectively — rather than a Sino-compositional calque.

**Real content bug found and fixed**: `cantonese` had the wrong initial ("sam1" instead of 参's own confirmed caam1) — corrected to caam1 sou3. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 삼수 diverges entirely from the native-compositional Korean field. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄚㄇㄙㄨ unique among both words and characters).
