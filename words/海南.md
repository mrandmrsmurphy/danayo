## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [南](../characters/南.md) "south" — "south of the sea". No stand-in relationship applies (海's own is [[海洋]]; 南's own is [[南方]]).

This is a name shared by several places in China, Japan and Korea, and also an ordinary Japanese word, so the non-province senses come first. English Wiktionary gives the etymology of the Chinese name as literally "south of a sea": for Hainan Island and the province the sea is the Qiongzhou Strait, for the Hainan Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture in Qinghai it is Qinghai Lake (see [[青海]]), and the district of Wuhai in Inner Mongolia is named for its position relative to the Haibowan district.

Hainan, the province, is the southernmost province of China, made up mostly of Hainan Island, and its capital is [[海口]] (from memory, not re-checked). The Japanese field holds かいなん, the reading of the Wakayama city Kainan, which English Wiktionary treats as an on'yomi (go-on) reading of the same characters; the Chinese province itself is usually written in katakana ハイナン in Japanese (English Wiktionary, a loan from Mandarin), and the Korean county has its own Japanese rendering ヘナム. The Korean field holds 해남, which is both the Sino-Korean reading and the name of the Korean county Haenam; South Korean usage for the Chinese province today is 하이난 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Hải Nam is the Sino-Vietnamese name; English Wiktionary Vietnamese lists it for the Chinese senses, and Vietnamese Wiktionary glosses it as the island of Hainan.

Japanese Wiktionary adds two Japanese senses, which come first: a common noun for the southern sea and the land along its coast, and an alternative name of Shikoku (the island of [[四国]]); the same spelling is therefore a Japanese word as well as a Chinese name. Attestation: English Wiktionary lists the island, province, prefecture, district and the Japanese city under Chinese, and the Korean county under Japanese and Korean; Chinese Wiktionary lists the same set. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄧㄋㄚㄇ unique across both words and characters).

1. The southern sea, and the coastal land beside it (Japanese).
2. Shikoku, as an alternative name (Japanese).
3. Hainan Island, the large island off the south coast of China.
4. Hainan Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture, a prefecture of Qinghai province (around Qinghai Lake).
5. Hainan District, a district of Wuhai in Inner Mongolia.
6. Kainan, a city in Wakayama Prefecture, Japan (read かいなん).
7. Haenam, a county in South Jeolla Province, South Korea (read 해남).
8. Hainan, the province of China.
