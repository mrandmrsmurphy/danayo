## Notes
- [特](../characters/特.md) "special" + [許 (char)](../characters/許%20(char).md) "permit; allow"

特許 is a patent: an exclusive right granted for an invention. The senses differ across the sphere. Wiktionary glosses Japanese とっきょ as "letter patent; patent", with Korean 특허 the same. Mandarin tèxǔ is recorded only as the verb "to specially permit", and Chinese speakers say 專利 for a patent. Dan'a'yo takes the Japanese and Korean sense. The word is literally "special permit".

The right is granted by the state and comes with a time limit and conditions, so it fits beside [許可](許可.md), a licence, and [版権](版権.md), copyright. It protects an invention, where [商標](商標.md) protects a brand name.
