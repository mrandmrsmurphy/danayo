## Notes
- [[移住]] "to immigrate" + [者 (char)](../characters/者%20(char).md) "-er, one who" — the agentive form of [[移住]] above; no stand-in relationship applies (者's own `stand_in` is itself).

移住者 means an immigrant or migrant — the person, not the act.

Mandarin yízhùzhě, Cantonese ji4 zyu6 ze2, Japanese いじゅうしゃ, and Korean 이주자 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese người di trú uses the native agentive word người ("person") rather than a Sino-Vietnamese 者/giả suffix — real spoken Vietnamese doesn't form this agentive the same mechanical way the other four languages do. `kwin` is false, inherited from [[移住]] itself (移 false, 住 true, 者 false). No homophones (注音 ⼶ㄧㄐㄨㄑㄚ unique across both words and characters).
