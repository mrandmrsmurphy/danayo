## Notes
- [甘 (char)](../characters/甘%20(char).md) "sweet" + [露 (char)](../characters/露%20(char).md) "dew" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 甘's and 露's own stand-ins are themselves).

甘露 means honeydew, nectar, amrita.

Mandarin gānlù, Cantonese gam1 lou6, Japanese かんろ, Korean 감로 and Vietnamese cam lộ are all fully compositional.
