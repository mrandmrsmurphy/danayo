## Notes
- [虐](../characters/虐.md) "cruel" + [待](../characters/待.md) "treat" + [狂](../characters/狂.md) "mad, crazy" — "abuse-mad," a sadist. Their own `stand_in` values: 虐's is [[暴虐]]; 待's is [[等待]]; 狂's is [[風狂]]; none points here, so 虐待狂 is an independent compound.

虐待狂 is a sadist: a person who takes pleasure in inflicting pain, and sadism itself. Mandarin nüèdàikuáng and Cantonese joek6 doi6 kong4 are the ordinary words. Japanese says サディスト and Korean 가학성, so those two fields hold the character readings. It is [[虐待]] (to mistreat) plus 狂 "mad," the same "mad for" pattern as 愛好狂. Compare [[欺凌]] (to bully) and [[残酷]] (cruel).
