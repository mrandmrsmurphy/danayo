## Notes
- [昨](../characters/昨%20(char).md) "last, previous" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [昨](../characters/昨%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

昨 means last or previous (as in 昨日, "yesterday").

Mandarin zuó, Cantonese zok6, Japanese さく, Korean 작, and Vietnamese tạc are all standard, fully compositional readings.
