## Notes
- [布](../characters/布.md) "cloth; linen" + [告](../characters/告.md) "inform"

布告 is "to proclaim; a proclamation," literally "spread the word." According to Wiktionary, Japanese ふこく is a noun "proclamation" and a suru-verb "proclaim"; Korean 포고 is "proclamation, promulgation, announcement, declaration"; Vietnamese bố cáo is "to announce, to proclaim"; and Chinese 布告 is the same word written 佈告 (bùgào). In Dan'a'yo it is the verb for an authority publicly announcing something to its people, as in the Emperor's 1945 surrender broadcast (see [Japan](../lookup/Japanese/Japan.md)), where it governs the quoted announcement that follows a colon.

[報告](報告.md) "report" and [忠告](忠告.md) "advise" share 告, but 布告 is directed from a ruler to the public rather than a report upward or advice to a friend.
