## Notes
- [家](../characters/家.md) "house" + [臣](../characters/臣.md) "subject"

家臣 is a vassal, a retainer. English Wiktionary glosses Japanese かしん as "vassal" and Chinese 家臣 (Mandarin jiāchén, Cantonese gaa1 san4) as, in the Spring and Autumn period, a counselor to a king or feudal warlord. It lists a Korean entry, 가신. Compare [領主](領主.md), a feudal lord.
