## Notes
- [喧](../characters/喧.md) "noisy, clamorous" + [嘩](../characters/嘩.md) "clamor, hubbub" — "commotion, noise, hubbub" in Mandarin/Cantonese. **Genuine cross-linguistic sense-narrowing**: Japanese けんか has narrowed this to specifically mean "quarrel, fight, argument" between people, distinct from the more general "noisy commotion" sense retained in Chinese. `vietnamese: huyên hoa` is an attested classical/literary term with the same general "noisy, boisterous" sense as the Chinese usage (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted).
