## Notes
- [措](../characters/措.md) "arrange" + [置](../characters/置%20(char).md) "place, set" — synonym compound; this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [措](../characters/措.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

措置 means to handle, arrange, or manage.

Mandarin cuòzhì, Cantonese cou3 zi3, Japanese そち, Korean 조치, and Vietnamese thố trí are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄛㄑㄧ unique across both words and characters).
