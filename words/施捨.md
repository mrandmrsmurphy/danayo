## Notes
- [施](../characters/施.md) "give, bestow" + [捨](../characters/捨%20(char).md) "give up, abandon" — "give away," alms. Their own `stand_in` values: 施's is [[施行]]; 捨's is itself; none points here, so 施捨 is an independent compound.

施捨 is to give alms, and alms themselves: giving money or goods to the poor as charity. Mandarin shīshě and Cantonese si1 se2 are the ordinary words. Japanese says 施し and 布施 and Korean 보시 or 적선, so those two fields hold the character readings. Compare [[贈与]] (a gift) and [[乞丐]] (beggar).
