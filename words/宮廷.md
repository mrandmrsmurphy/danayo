## Etymology
- [宮](../characters/宮.md) "palace, shrine" + [廷](../characters/廷.md) "imperial court" — stand-in for [[宮]], which cannot appear independently

## Notes
- "Royal/imperial court" — the physical and political center of a dynasty's power. `vietnamese: cung đình` is directly attested and extremely common (Nhã nhạc cung đình Huế, "Huế Royal Court Music," a UNESCO-recognized tradition).
