## Notes
- [恭](../characters/恭.md) "respectful" + [喜](../characters/喜%20(char).md) "like, love, enjoy" — no stand-in relationship applies (恭's own stand-in is [[恭敬]]; 喜's own is itself).

恭喜 means congratulations — an expression of respectful joy at someone's good fortune.

Mandarin gōngxǐ, Cantonese gung1 hei2, and Vietnamese cung hỉ are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `japanese`/`korean` had been contaminated with native colloquial congratulatory expressions (おめでとう, 축하해) instead of the compositional readings — corrected to きょうき/공희. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼄ㄫㄏㄧ unique across both words and characters).
