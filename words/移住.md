## Notes
- [移](../characters/移.md) "to move, shift" + [住](../characters/住.md) "to live, dwell" — no stand-in relationship applies (移's own `stand_in` is [移動](移動.md); 住's own is [居住](居住.md)).

移住 means to immigrate, migrate, or relocate — moving to a new place to live, not just a brief trip.

Mandarin yízhù, Cantonese ji4 zyu6, Japanese いじゅう, Korean 이주, and Vietnamese di trú are all standard, fully compositional readings — di trú in particular is the real, everyday Vietnamese word for this sense, not just a mechanical composite.
