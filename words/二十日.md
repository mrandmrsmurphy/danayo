## Notes
- [二 (char)](../characters/二%20(char).md) "two" + [十 (char)](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — the twentieth day of the month.

Japanese has a special reading: はつか (hatsuka), not the expected compositional にじゅうにち — one of the irregular native day-counting readings (alongside ついたち, ふつか, みっか... とおか, and よっか for the 24th) that Japanese uses for calendar dates rather than on'yomi compounds. Mandarin èrshí rì, Cantonese ji6 sap6 jat6, and Korean 이십일 are all standard, fully compositional. Vietnamese filled with ngày hai mươi (native "day twenty"), matching the rest of this [[二十一日|二十N日]] cluster.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 늬십닏 diverges from Sino-Korean 이십일, since all three constituent characters independently carry `kwin: false` on their own character pages.
