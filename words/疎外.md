## Notes
- [疎](../characters/疎.md) "distant; thin" + [外](../characters/外.md) "outside"

疎外 is alienation: to treat as an outsider, and the feeling of being left out. Wiktionary glosses Japanese そがい as "alienation; to alienate". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 소외 matches.

It is the social side of being kept at a distance, where [異化](異化.md) is the philosophical estrangement. It makes a [外人](外人.md), an outsider, of someone, and is the opposite of [団結](団結.md), uniting.
