## Notes
- [衡](../characters/衡.md) "weigh, measure" + [量](../characters/量.md) "measure, quantity"

衡量 means to weigh up: to measure something, judge it and evaluate it, as one balances weights on a scale. English Wiktionary lists it only for Chinese (Mandarin héngliáng, Cantonese hang4 loeng6), glossed "to weigh; to measure; to judge; to evaluate". It has no Japanese, Korean or Vietnamese entry, so Japanese こうりょう and Korean 형량 here are built from the characters.

衡量 is the stand-in for [衡](../characters/衡.md), which is too bounded to stand alone. A more concrete comparison of two things is a [[比較]], and judging worth is an [[評価]].
