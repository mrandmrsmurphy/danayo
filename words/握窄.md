## Notes
- [握](../characters/握.md) "grip" + [窄](../characters/窄.md) "narrow" — no stand-in relationship applies (握's own stand-in is [[把握]]; 窄's own is [[狭窄]]).

握窄 means dirty, petty, or recalcitrant — this vault's rendering of the real term 齷齪/龌龊 (wòchuò), substituting the phonetically related 握窄 since 齷/齪 have no pages of their own. `mandarin`/`cantonese` hold the avoided real term's reading (wòchuò/aak1 cuk1), per the established convention on [[丹金]] and its series; `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` (악착/'agcag/ㄚㄎㄐㄚㄎ) are fully compositional from 握/窄's own citations.

Japanese あくせく, Korean 악착, and Vietnamese ác trác (compositional) are all standard. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄎㄐㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
