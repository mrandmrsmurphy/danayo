## Notes
- [共 (char)](../characters/共%20(char).md) "together, common" + [和](../characters/和.md) "harmony, peace" — English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 共和 as "republicanism; republic", Vietnamese cộng hòa as "republic", Korean as "universal harmony, republicanism", and Japanese きょうわ as "collaboration; cooperation; working together in harmony; republicanism". The republic itself is the [共和国](共和国.md).
