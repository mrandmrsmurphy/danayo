## Notes
- [共 (char)](../characters/共%20(char).md) "together, common" + [和](../characters/和.md) "harmony, peace" — "collaboration, cooperation," and by extension "republicanism" (共和国, "republic," a state governed by collective/shared rule rather than a monarch). Mandarin gònghé, Japanese きょうわ, and Vietnamese cộng hòa are all directly attested standard readings.

**Real content bugs found and fixed**: both `cantonese` and `korean` had been stored as garbled values not matching either constituent character's own reading — `gong4 ho2` corrected to the compositional `gung6 wo4` (共's own gung6 + 和's own wo4), and `고화` corrected to the compositional `공화` (共's own 공 + 和's own 화). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 굥화 diverges from the now-corrected Sino-Korean 공화 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼄ㄫㄏ⺢ unique among both words and characters).
