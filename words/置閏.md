## Notes
- [置 (char)](../characters/置%20(char).md) "put; place; install" + [閏 (char)](../characters/閏%20(char).md) "intercalary"

置閏 is intercalation, the inserting of a leap period into a calendar. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 置閏 (alias 置闰; Mandarin zhìrùn, Cantonese zi3 jeon6) as "to intercalate a leap month, to set a leap year" and "intercalation, system of leap year calculation", and Korean 치윤 as "intercalation". The Japanese ちじゅん and Vietnamese trí nhuận fields are built from the readings of the two characters. The units that are inserted are the [閏日](閏日.md), leap day, the [閏月](閏月.md), leap month, and the [閏秒](閏秒.md), leap second.
