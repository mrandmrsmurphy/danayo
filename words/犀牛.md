## Notes
- Literally "rhinoceros-ox" — [[犀]] is the rhinoceros character, [[牛 (char)|牛]] provides the bovine classifier. The compound 犀牛 is the standard Mandarin and Cantonese word for rhinoceros; Vietnamese tê ngưu is the Sino-Vietnamese reading of the same compound (tê matching 犀's own citation).
- Stand-in for the bound character [[犀]] (牛's own stand-in is itself, so `#cranberry` does not apply).

Fixed a real inconsistency: `japanese` and `korean` held compositional readings (さいぎゅう, 서우) that directly contradicted the page's own Notes, which correctly state that Japanese and Korean use different, non-compositional real words for rhinoceros — corrected to サイ (using 犀's own SAI on'yomi alone, without a 牛-based second element, as real Japanese does) and 코뿔소 (a fully native Korean compound, "nose-horn-cow," unrelated to either character's Sino-reading). Removed the redundant duplicate `品詞` field (kept `pos`). No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄧㄋ⼜ unique across both words and characters).
