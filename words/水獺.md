## Notes
- [水 (char)](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [獺](../characters/獺.md) "otter" — this word is the stand-in for [獺](../characters/獺.md), which cannot appear independently.

水獺 means otter, literally "water otter." Mandarin shuǐtǎ (Taiwan shuǐtà), Cantonese seoi2 taat3, and Korean 수달 (sudal) all use the compound, and Wiktionary glosses the Korean as "river otter." The character 獺 alone is too bounded to stand by itself in Dan'a'yo, so this compound serves as its viable form.
