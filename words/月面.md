## Notes
- [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "moon" + [面](../characters/面.md) "surface"

月面 is the surface of the moon. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yuèmiàn and Japanese げつめん as "lunar surface". The Cantonese reading is built from the characters, and Korean 월면 matches.

It names the moon as a place that can be walked on, where [太陰](太陰.md) names the moon as a heavenly body. The world one walks on is the [地球](地球.md).
