## Notes
- [鋸](../characters/鋸%20(char).md) "saw" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [鋸](../characters/鋸%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

鋸 means saw (the tool).

Mandarin jù, Cantonese geoi3, Japanese きょ, Korean 거, and Vietnamese cứ are all standard, fully compositional readings; Japanese also has the native のこ(ぎり) for everyday use. Simplified `vietnamese` from a three-way list to the single reading matching the character's own citation (cứ). Homophone of [[挙]] "raise; example" (both 注音 ㄍ⼄) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words.
