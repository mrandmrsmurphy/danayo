## Notes
- [援](../characters/援.md) "assist" + [助](../characters/助.md) "help" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [助](../characters/助.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (援's own stand-in is [[援手]]).

援助 means aid or assistance.

Mandarin yuánzhù, Cantonese wun4 zo6, Japanese えんじょ, and Korean 원조 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `cantonese` had a spurious duplicate first syllable (jyun4 zo6, wun4 zo6) — trimmed to the correct compositional wun4 zo6. Vietnamese vin trợ is compositional, though 助's half (trợ, the well-attested standard SV reading, as in 助教 trợ giáo) is inferred rather than drawn from the character's own stored citation, which is missing it — flagged for a future character-level check. No homophones (注音 ㄛㄋㄐㄛ unique across both words and characters).
