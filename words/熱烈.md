## Notes
- [熱 (char)](../characters/熱%20(char).md) "heat up, be hot" + [烈](../characters/烈.md) "fiery, violent, vehement, ardent, extreme" — no stand-in relationship applies (熱's own stand-in is itself; 烈's own is [[激烈]]).

熱烈 means avid, fervent.

Mandarin rèliè, Cantonese jit6 lit6, Japanese ねつれつ, Korean 열렬 and Vietnamese nhiệt liệt are all standard, fully compositional readings.
