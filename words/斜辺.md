## Notes
- [斜](../characters/斜.md) "slant, tilt" + [辺](../characters/辺.md) "side, edge, border" — the slanting side

斜辺 is the hypotenuse: the side of a right triangle opposite the right angle ([[直角]]), and so the longest side of the [[三角形]].  The name is literal, since the side is "slanted" (斜) against the two sides that meet at the right angle, which are the 辺 that stand square.  It is the side that the Pythagorean theorem (三平方の定理) singles out: the square on the 斜辺 equals the sum of the squares on the other two sides.  In Japanese school mathematics the word is first met in the lower secondary years, with 底辺 ("base") and 高さ ("height") as its companions.

The traditional spelling 斜邊, with 邊 for 辺, is listed as an alias.  Mandarin xiébiān, Cantonese ce4 bin1, Japanese しゃへん and Korean 사변 are the same compound; Korean school mathematics also has the native word 빗변.  `kwin` is false because the Dan'a'yo 샤번 differs from the Korean 사변.  No Vietnamese form is given, since the usual Vietnamese term, cạnh huyền, is not a reading of these characters.
