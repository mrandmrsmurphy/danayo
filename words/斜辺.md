## Notes
- [斜](../characters/斜.md) "slant, tilt" + [辺](../characters/辺.md) "side, edge, border" — the slanting side

Wiktionary: Japanese 斜辺 (しゃへん), geometry, "a hypotenuse"; Chinese 斜邊, mathematics, "hypotenuse" and, in mechanics, "bevel edge", kept as an alias here. The hypotenuse is the side opposite the right angle ([[直角]]) of a right triangle ([[三角形]]).
