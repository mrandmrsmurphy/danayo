## Notes
- [缺](../characters/缺.md) "lacking" + [席](../characters/席.md) "seat" — absence (from a meeting, class, etc.). Attested across Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese (written 欠席 in Japan itself, but rendered here with 缺 per the vault's standing choice), and Korean.

The blank `vietnamese` field now holds the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading of the two characters, khuyết tịch (缺 khuyết, 席 tịch), following the vault's convention for these fields. The Korean 결석 differs from the Dan'a'yo 諺文 퀃석 in the first syllable's initial and final, so `kwin` is false. The Existence page lists the word as "absent, not in attendance — the direct opposite of 出席," and Dan'a'yo keeps that sense. It is the opposite of the existing word [出席](出席.md) "attend, present," and is distinct from [缺勤](缺勤.md) "absence from work," which is absence from duty and not from a meeting or class. The readings are Mandarin quēxí, Cantonese kyut3 zik6 and Japanese kesseki (けっせき).
