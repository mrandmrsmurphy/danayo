## Notes
- [乱](../characters/乱.md) "chaos, disorder" + [離](../characters/離.md) "to separate, to part" — the compounded upheaval of war or disaster: social chaos plus the forced separation of families and displacement of refugees fleeing it.

Vietnamese loạn ly (well-attested in Vietnamese literature and historical writing, describing wartime upheaval and the human displacement it causes) carries the same compounded sense directly. Japanese らんり and Korean 난리 (the latter already lexicalized in ordinary Korean as "commotion, uproar, big fuss" in addition to its literal wartime-chaos sense) both preserve the core meaning. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 란레 diverges from Sino-Korean 난리 in both syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄚㄋㄌㄝㄧ unique among both words and characters).
