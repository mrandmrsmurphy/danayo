## Notes
- [知 (char)](../characters/知%20(char).md) "know" + [事 (char)](../characters/事%20(char).md) "matter"

知事 is a governor. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 知事 (Mandarin zhīshì, Cantonese zi6 si6) as, historical, "magistrate or governor of a county or medium-size town" and "governor or magistrate of a Japanese prefecture or South Korean province". It lists Japanese ちじ and Korean 지사 entries.
