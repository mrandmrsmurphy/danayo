## Notes
- [黎](../characters/黎.md) "black; numerous" + [朝 (char)](../characters/朝%20(char).md) "morning" — no stand-in relationship applies (黎's own is [[黎明]]; 朝's own is the single-character word [[朝]]). Here 黎 is the surname Lí (Vietnamese Lê) and 朝 is "dynasty", as in [[王朝]] and [[宋朝]].

This name has no Chinese sense. English Wiktionary lists it as a Chinese proper noun, but both of its senses are Vietnamese dynasties, so the whole entry is Vietnamese history, and the numbered senses are ordered by how common they are.

1. The Lê dynasty of Vietnam, the Later Lê (後黎朝), founded by Lê Lợi after he drove the Ming out in 1428. Japanese Wikipedia dates it 1428–1527 and 1532–1789 (the usual English dating is 1533–1789 for the second part); the gap is the usurpation by the Mạc family. Vietnamese historians divide it into the early Lê (1428–1527), whose reigns were strong, and the restored Lê (from 1533), in which the emperors were figureheads under the Trịnh lords in the north while the Nguyễn lords ruled the south (see [[広南]]), until the Tây Sơn rebellion ended it in 1789 (the division, the Trịnh and Nguyễn lords and the ending are from memory, not re-checked). It is the dynasty meant by 黎朝 when no qualifier is added (Japanese Wikipedia; Chinese Wikipedia says 後黎朝 is "in most cases simply called 黎朝").
2. Rarely, the Early Lê dynasty (前黎朝), 980–1009, founded by Lê Hoàn and replaced by the Lý (see [[李朝]]); English Wiktionary marks this use as rare (the dates and founder are from memory, not re-checked).

The `vietnamese` field holds Hán-Việt Lê triều (lê 黎, triều 朝), the form English Wiktionary gives for the Vietnamese entry. The Mandarin Lícháo, Cantonese lai4 ciu4, Japanese れいちょう (Japanese Wikipedia also gives レちょう), and Korean 려조 are readings built from the characters.

Attestation, checked 2026-10-11. English Wiktionary: 黎朝 with a Chinese proper-noun section (short for 後黎朝 and rarely for 前黎朝) and a Vietnamese section (chữ Hán form of Lê triều). Japanese Wikipedia: 黎朝 (れいちょう, レちょう) with the dates above. Chinese Wikipedia has a disambiguation page (前黎朝, 後黎朝, 黎中興朝). Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese Wiktionaries have no entry, and hvdic.thivien.net has none for the compound. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄝㄧㄐㄚㄨ unique across both words and characters).
