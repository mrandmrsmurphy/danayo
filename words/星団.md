## Notes
- [星 (char)](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" + [団](../characters/団.md) "group; ball"

星団 is a star cluster: a gathering of stars held together by gravity. Wiktionary glosses Japanese せいだん as "star cluster". The Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the characters, and Korean 성단 matches.

The Pleiades are an example, the [昴宿星団](昴宿星団.md). A looser, invented grouping of stars is a [星群](星群.md), and a formal one is a [星坐](星坐.md), constellation.
