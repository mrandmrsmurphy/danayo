## Notes
- [西](../characters/西.md) "west" + [南](../characters/南.md) "south" — literally "west-south," southwest.

Mandarin xīnán, Cantonese sai1 naam4, Japanese せいなん (seinan), Korean 서남, and Vietnamese tây nam are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 西's and 南's own stored citations exactly. The compound puts the east–west element first (west before south), the Chinese and Vietnamese order, in line with 東北 and 東南.
