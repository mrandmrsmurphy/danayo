## Notes
- [西](../characters/西.md) "west" + [南](../characters/南.md) "south" — literally "west-south," southwest.

Mandarin xīnán, Cantonese sai1 naam4, Japanese せいなん (seinan), Korean 서남, and Vietnamese tây nam are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 西's and 南's own stored citations exactly — Dan'a'yo's own compass-direction order (west before south) is preserved consistently across all five languages here.
