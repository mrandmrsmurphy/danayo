## Notes
- [提](../characters/提.md) "raise, propose" + [案](../characters/案.md) "proposal, case" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [案](../characters/案.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (提's own stand-in is [[提示]]).

提案 means to propose, suggest, or a motion.

Mandarin tí'àn, Cantonese tai4 on3, Japanese ていあん, Korean 제안, and Vietnamese chề án are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝ·ㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
