## Notes
- [淫 (char)](../characters/淫%20(char).md) "lewd; obscene" + [靡](../characters/靡.md) "extravagant" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 靡 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (淫's own stand-in is itself, so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

淫靡 means decadent, lascivious, profligate, extravagant.

Mandarin yínmǐ, Cantonese jam4 mei5, Japanese いんび, Korean 음미, and Vietnamese dâm mĩ are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 淫's and 靡's own stored citations exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄧㄇㄇㄧㄆ unique across both words and characters).
