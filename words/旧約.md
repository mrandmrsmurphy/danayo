## Notes
- [旧](../characters/旧%20(char).md) "old" + [約](../characters/約.md) "covenant, agreement" — "old covenant," the Old Testament. Their own `stand_in` values: 旧's is itself; 約's is [[約束]]; none points here, so 旧約 is an independent compound.

旧約 is the Old Testament, and the old covenant: the Hebrew Scriptures and the covenant God made with Israel. Mandarin jiùyuē (舊約), Cantonese gau6 joek3, Japanese きゅうやく and Korean 구약 all use it, and Vietnamese cựu ước does too. It pairs with [[新約]] (the New Testament). Compare [[契約]] (contract) and [[律法]] (law).
