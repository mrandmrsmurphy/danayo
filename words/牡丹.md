## Etymology
- [牡](../characters/牡.md) "male" + [丹](../characters/丹.md) "cinnabar" — 牡's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 丹's own is [[丹砂]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 牡 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

牡丹 means peony.

Mandarin mǔdān, Japanese ぼたん (BO+TAN), and Vietnamese mẫu đơn (mẫu + 丹's own đơn) were already correct and fully compositional, all real standard words for peony. Filled the blank `cantonese` with maau5 daan1, fully compositional. Korean 모란 (rather than compositional 모단) reflects a real historical sound change in this specific loanword and is the actual standard Korean word for peony. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/`aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄛㄨㄉㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
