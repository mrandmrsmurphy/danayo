## Notes
- [牡](../characters/牡.md) "male" + [丹](../characters/丹.md) "cinnabar" — 牡's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 丹's own is [[丹沙]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 牡 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

牡丹 means peony.

Mandarin mǔdān, Cantonese maau5 daan1, Japanese ぼたん and Vietnamese mẫu đơn (mẫu plus 丹's own đơn) are compositional, and all are the standard words for peony. Korean 모란, not the compositional 모단, reflects a real historical sound change in this loanword and is the actual standard Korean word for peony.
