## Notes
- [肪](../characters/肪%20(char).md) "fat, obese" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 肪 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

肪 means fat or obesity, as in 脂肪 (animal fat).

Mandarin fáng, cantonese fong1, and korean 방 (bang) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese has no native kun-reading (stored as ø on the character page), so the on-reading ボウ (BOU) is used directly, rather than the alternate on-reading ホウ (HOU). Vietnamese phòng is one of two stored candidates. A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone with [[紡]] ("spin [yarn]") — already fully cross-linked from 紡's own perfecting pass.
