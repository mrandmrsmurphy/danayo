## Notes
- [測](../characters/測.md) "measure, survey" + [量](../characters/量.md) "measure, quantity"

測量 means to measure and to survey. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 測量 (alias 测量; Mandarin cèliáng, Cantonese cak1 loeng4) as "to measure; to gauge" and "to survey (land)". It glosses Japanese そくりょう as "measurement; surveying; to survey, to measure" and Korean 측량 as "surveying". Vietnamese trắc lượng is the Sino-Vietnamese reading of the two characters.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [測](../characters/測.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
