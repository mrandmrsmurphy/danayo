## Notes
- [測](../characters/測.md) "measure, survey" + [量](../characters/量.md) "measure, quantity" — 測量 is the stand-in word for [測](../characters/測.md); 測 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse.

測量 names measurement and surveying in the technical/professional sense — determining position, distance, elevation, and boundaries for construction, cartography, and land management (測量士, "licensed surveyor"). Both characters independently mean "to measure," so the compound is a near-synonym doubling rather than a compositional shift in meaning.

Japanese そくりょう and Korean 측량 are both standard compositional Sino-readings, and `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 측량 matches Sino-Korean 측량 exactly. Vietnamese trắc lượng is likewise standard, closely related to the everyday native đo đạc ("to measure, survey"). No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄜㄎㄌ⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
