## Notes
- [終](../characters/終.md) "end" + [点 (char)](../characters/点%20(char).md) "point" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (終's is [[終了]], 点's is [[点]] itself), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

終点 names a terminus or terminal — the final stop of a rail or bus line, the end point of a route.

Mandarin zhōngdiǎn, Cantonese zung1 dim2, Japanese しゅうてん (shūten), and Korean 종점 (jongjeom) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 終点 is itself a common attested word for "terminus" in all four languages. Vietnamese chung điểm is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
