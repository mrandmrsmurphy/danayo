## Notes
- [長](../characters/長%20(char).md) "long" + [春](../characters/春%20(char).md) "springtime" — "long spring". No stand-in relationship applies (both characters are their own stand-ins).

長春 (Changchun) is the capital of Jilin province in northeastern China, a sub-provincial city. Wiktionary has no etymology for the name; it lists the historical names 寬城子 and 新京 (the capital name used under the Manchukuo regime, from memory, not re-checked). The literal reading "long spring" is only a gloss of the characters. 长 is an alias of 長, so 长春 is kept as the word's alias.

Japanese ちょうしゅん is the Sino-Japanese reading listed on Wiktionary, which also gives the irregular チャンチュン. Korean 장춘 and Vietnamese Trường Xuân are the Sino-Xenic readings (Vietnamese confirmed on Vietnamese Wikipedia); the usual South Korean name is 창춘 (from memory, not re-checked). English Wiktionary lists no ordinary sense of the bare word; it only cross-refers 長春花 and compounds such as 長春子, which are not claimed here. Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese and Japanese entries; no Korean or Vietnamese Wiktionary entry was found. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄫㄑㄨㄋ unique across both words and characters).
