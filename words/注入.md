## Notes
- [注](../characters/注.md) "pour" + [入 (char)](../characters/入%20(char).md) "enter" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 注 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (入's own stand-in is itself, so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

注入 means to pour into, inject.

Mandarin zhùrù, Cantonese zyu3 jap6, Japanese ちゅうにゅう, and Korean 주입 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 注's and 入's own stored citations exactly).
