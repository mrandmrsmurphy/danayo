## Notes
- [叫](../characters/叫%20(char).md) "cry out" + [喚](../characters/喚%20(char).md) "call out" — no stand-in relationship applies (both characters are independent Dan'a'yo entries, each its own stand-in).

叫喚 means to cry or call out — also, in Buddhist cosmology, short for 叫喚地獄 (Raurava, the "Hell of Screaming," one of the eight hot hells).

Japanese きょうかん, Korean 규환, and Vietnamese khiếu hoán (filled, compositional) are all standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼘ㄏ⺢ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
