## Notes
- [時 (char)](../characters/時%20(char).md) "time" + [局 (char)](../characters/局%20(char).md) "office; bureau; situation"

時局 is "the current political situation," the state of public affairs at this moment. According to Wiktionary, Chinese 時局 (shíjú, Cantonese si4 guk6) is "current political situation" (時局艱難 "a difficult political situation"); Japanese じきょく has the same sense; and Korean 시국 is its Sino-Korean form. `vietnamese` thời cục is the compositional reading; Wiktionary has no Vietnamese section for this word.

It differs from [現状](現状.md) "the current state of affairs": 時局 is specifically the political or national situation, as in the 1945 surrender broadcast on [Japan](../lookup/Japanese/Japan.md) (收拾時局 "to settle the situation").
