## Notes
- [安](../characters/安.md) "peace, rest" + [息](../characters/息.md) "rest, breathe" + [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — "rest day," the Sabbath. Their own `stand_in` values: 安's is [[平安]]; 息's is [[気息]]; 日's is itself; none points here, so 安息日 is an independent compound.

安息日 is the Sabbath: the seventh day, a day of rest from work, the fourth of the [[十戒]]. Mandarin ānxīrì, Cantonese on1 sik1 jat6 and Japanese あんそくにち are attested, and Korean 안식일 is the same compound. In most churches Sunday, the [[主日]], is the day of worship. Compare [[礼拝]] (worship service) and [[契約]] (covenant).
