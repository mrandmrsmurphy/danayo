## Notes
- [審](../characters/審.md) "examine carefully" + [査](../characters/査.md) "investigate" — this word is the stand-in for [審](../characters/審.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

審査 names formal evaluation or screening — contest judging, visa applications, editorial review — built from 審's "examine carefully, deliberate" and 査's plain "investigate." Japanese しんさ is the primary living source for this exact spelling, used across an unusually wide range of formal-review contexts; Korean 심사 carries the identical range and register. **Dan'a'yo deliberately uses the Japanese shinjitai form 査 rather than Mandarin's own 審查** (with 查, not 査) — the two characters are graphemic variants of the same etymon (査's own `aliases` field lists 查), but Mandarin/Cantonese speakers write and read the compound with 查 (chá), not 査 (zhā); the word's `mandarin`/`cantonese` fields here (shěnzhā/sam2 caa4) are the compositional Dan'a'yo-internal reading built from 査's own stored fields, not the real everyday Mandarin pronunciation of the actual word (shěnchá).

Vietnamese thẩm tra (attested; 審's own "thẩm" + 査's own "tra," filled onto 査's previously-blank `vietnamese` field while confirming this word) is likewise the standard modern term, paralleling the Japanese/Korean range rather than any narrower sense. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄇㄐㄚ unique across both words and characters).
