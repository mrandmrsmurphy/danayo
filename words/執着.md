## Notes
- [執](../characters/執.md) "hold; grip" + [着 (char)](../characters/着%20(char).md) "attach"

執着 is to cling: to hold fast to a thing or an idea and not let it go, to the point of obsession. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin zhízhuó as "stubborn; persistent" and Japanese しゅうちゃく as "fixation; obsession; to be fixated on". Cantonese zap1 zoek6 and Korean 집착 match. The usual Chinese spelling is 執著, which is an alias because 著 is a variant of 着.

It is desire that has hardened into a hold, in Buddhism the clinging that binds to suffering. A weaker pull is [思慕](思慕.md), longing, and a fixed craving is [欲望](欲望.md).
