## Notes
- [禁](../characters/禁.md) "restrict, prohibit, forbid" + [止](../characters/止.md) "stop" — 禁's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 止's own is [[中止]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 禁 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

禁止 is the most general, plain word for "forbid, prohibit" across the CJKV sphere — legally, socially, or simply as an instruction (禁止事項, "prohibited items/matters"; 立入禁止, "no entry"). It is more neutral and everyday than [[禁断]] (absolute/intensified prohibition) or [[禁制]] (formal administrative ban).

Mandarin jìnzhǐ, Cantonese gam3 zi2, Japanese きんし (kinshi), and Korean 금지 (geumji) are all standard, fully compositional readings, each the ordinary everyday word for "forbid/prohibit" in its language. Vietnamese offers several near-synonymous options — cấm chỉ (compositional, more formal/written), cấm đoán, and the bare native verb cấm (by far the most common in everyday speech).
