## Notes
- [中 (char)](../characters/中%20(char).md) "middle" + [指](../characters/指.md) "finger" — no `#cranberry`.

Mandarin zhōngzhǐ, Cantonese zung1 zi2, Japanese ちゅうし, Korean 중지, and Vietnamese ngón giữa (native, "middle finger," rather than a Sino-Vietnamese calque) are all standard readings. **Fixed a real bug**: `cantonese` had been `zong1 zi3` — corrected to `zung1 zi2`, the proper compositional concatenation. **Removed a false homophone claim**: the page previously asserted "the same sound as [[曽子]]," but 曽子's own 注音 (ㄐㄜㄫㄐㄜ) and every cross-linguistic reading are entirely different from this word's own (注音 ㄐㄨㄫㄐㄧㄜ) — already investigated and disproven on 曽子's own page, but the stale claim had never been removed here.
