## Notes
- [中](../characters/中.md) "middle" + [指](../characters/指.md) "finger" — no `#cranberry`.

Mandarin zhōngzhǐ, Cantonese zung1 zi2, Japanese ちゅうし, Korean 중지, and Vietnamese ngón giữa (native, "middle finger," rather than a Sino-Vietnamese calque) are all standard readings.
