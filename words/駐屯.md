## Notes
- [駐](../characters/駐.md) "stop-over; stationed" + [屯 (char)](../characters/屯%20(char).md) "village" — legitimizing note: 駐's own `stand_in` is [[駐屯]] itself, already annotated in its own citation; 屯's own `stand_in` is 屯 itself, so transitivity fails — no `#cranberry`. 駐 alone is too bounded to stand as an independent Dan'a'yo word (its meaning, "to be stationed," is identical to the compound's), so 駐屯 serves as its legitimizing form.

Sino-Vietnamese reverses the compound's word order: đồn trú (屯 first, then 駐), rather than the Sino-order trú đồn — a known pattern where Vietnamese military/administrative vocabulary reorders Sino-Vietnamese morphemes (cf. [[限定詞]] từ hạn định, [[除外]] ngoại trừ).
