## Notes
- [血 (char)](../characters/血%20(char).md) "blood" + [管](../characters/管.md) "tube"

血管 is a blood vessel: one of the tubes that carry [血](血.md) through the body. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xuèguǎn and Japanese けっかん as "blood vessel". Vietnamese huyết quản is the same word, and Korean 혈관 and Cantonese hyut3 gun2 match.

It is the general word, where [動脈](動脈.md) carries blood from the heart and [静脈](静脈.md) carries it back. The tube-like organs of the gut are the [腸管](腸管.md).
