## Notes
- [紺 (char)](../characters/紺%20(char).md) "navy blue" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (紺's is [[紺]] itself, 色's is [[色彩]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 色 is individually `kwin: false`.

紺色 names navy blue as a color term — 紺 ("navy blue") plus 色 ("color").

Mandarin gànsè, Cantonese gam3 sik1, Japanese こんいろ (kon'iro), and Korean 감색 (gamsaek) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 紺色 is itself a common attested word for "navy blue" in all four languages. Vietnamese cám sắc is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading, though Vietnamese more commonly names the color with a native or loan term. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
