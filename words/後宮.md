## Notes
- [後](../characters/後%20(char).md) "behind, rear" + [宮](../characters/宮.md) "palace" — "rear palace," a harem. Their own `stand_in` values: 後's is itself; 宮's is [[宮廷]]; none points here, so 後宮 is an independent compound.

後宮 is a harem: the rear palace where an emperor's wives and concubines lived. Mandarin hòugōng, Cantonese hau6 gung1 and Japanese こうきゅう are attested, and Korean 후궁 is the same compound, usually meaning a royal concubine. Compare [[妾]] (concubine).
