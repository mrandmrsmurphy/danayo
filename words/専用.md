## Notes
- [専](../characters/専.md) "dedicated, reserved" + [用](../characters/用.md) "use" — this word is the viable form of [専](../characters/専.md): the character's meaning (dedicated; reserved for) is identical, but 専 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 専用 is its `stand_in` compound.

専用 marks something reserved for one particular person, group or purpose, to the exclusion of general use. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 專用 (an alias; Mandarin zhuānyòng, Cantonese zyun1 jung6) as the attributive "dedicated; reserved; special; exclusive", with examples such as 殘疾人專用停車位, "parking for people with disabilities", and 出租車專用停車位, "taxi parking only", and as a verb "to reserve or dedicate for a specific purpose". Japanese せんよう is "the exclusive use, dedicated, being only for", and "to dedicate, to have for one's exclusive use". Korean 전용 is "exclusive use; private use" (it is also the word for "diversion"), and Vietnamese chuyên dụng is "dedicated; specialized".

専 is the character for "dedicated, reserved", and 用 is "use".
