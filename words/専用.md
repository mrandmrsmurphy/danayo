## Notes
- [専](../characters/専.md) "dedicated, reserved" + [用](../characters/用.md) "use" — this word is the viable form of [専](../characters/専.md): the character's meaning (dedicated; reserved for) is identical, but 専 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 専用 is its `stand_in` compound.

専用 marks something reserved for one particular person, group, or purpose, to the exclusion of general/shared use: 女性専用車両 ("women-only train car"), 社員専用 ("staff only"), 専用ソフトウェア ("dedicated/proprietary software"). Common on Japanese signage in exactly this exclusionary sense — a designated space or resource that is *not* for everyone, unlike the neutral "special-purpose" reading the English gloss might suggest in isolation.

Korean 전용 and Vietnamese chuyên dụng are both standard compositional Sino-readings, chuyên dụng being especially common in technical/administrative register (xe chuyên dụng, "special-purpose vehicle"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 줜용 diverges from Sino-Korean 전용 in 専's syllable (줜 vs 전). No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄐ⼔ㄋ·⼄ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
