## Notes
- [比 (char)](../characters/比%20(char).md) "than, compared to" + [例](../characters/例.md) "example" — no stand-in relationship applies (比's own stand-in is itself; 例's own is [[実例]]).

比例 means ratio, proportion.

Mandarin bǐlì, Cantonese bei2 lai6, Japanese ひれい, and Korean 비례 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 比's and 例's own stored citations exactly).
