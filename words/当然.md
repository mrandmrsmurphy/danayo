## Notes
- [当 (char)](../characters/当%20(char).md) "while" + [然 (char)](../characters/然%20(char).md) "so" — "being so as it should be".

当然 is "of course; naturally; inevitable". English Wiktionary (at the traditional spelling 當然) lists the Chinese word as an adverb, "of course; surely" (這畢竟不是他的東西，他當然不會愛惜了, "after all, this is not his stuff; of course he wouldn't treasure it"), and as an adjective, "natural; reasonable; understandable". Both also carry a second sense, "ex officio" (當然成員, "ex officio member"). The derived term is 理所當然 ("as a matter of course"). It gives 固然 and 自然 as synonyms (of which 自然 is a word in the vault, and 固然 is not), and 肯定, 確定 and 一定 as related terms. The Korean 당연 and Vietnamese đương nhiên are listed as its Sino-Korean and Sino-Vietnamese forms, as adverb and adjective.

The Knowledge page places 当然 as an epistemic-certainty adverb near the top of its certainty gradient, beside [[必然]].

Mandarin dāngrán and Cantonese dong1 jin4 are as at English Wiktionary.

No other word shares the 注音 ㄉㄚㄫㄋ⼶ㄋ (checked 2026-10-12).
