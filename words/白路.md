## Notes
- [白 (char)](../characters/白%20(char).md) "white" + [路](../characters/路.md), standing in for its alias [[鷺]] "heron, egret" — like [[蒼路]] "crane," this word borrows 路 as a 借代字 for the unlinked character 鷺; 路 and 鷺 are true homophones in Mandarin and Cantonese (lù/lou6), which is why 路's own regular reading serves directly here with no special alias reading needed (unlike 就's separate ㄐㄨㄛ reading for 鷲).

**Correction (2026-09-12, user-directed):** this page was previously titled `白鷺.md`, using 鷺 (路's registered alias) directly in its title. 鷺 has no independent standing in Dan'a'yo and can never appear in any word, even though 白路 is not itself a recognizable real-world spelling of "egret" — renamed accordingly, with `白鷺` demoted to an `alias`.

白路 names the egret, a white heron common across East Asia and a recurring image in classical poetry as a symbol of purity and elegance. Mandarin báilù, Cantonese baak6 lou6, Japanese はくろ (hakuro), Korean 백로 (baekno), and Vietnamese bạch lộ are all fully compositional and stable across the sphere, with no drift or native-coinage substitution.
