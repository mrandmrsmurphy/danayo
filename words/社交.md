## Notes
- [社](../characters/社.md) "company, association" + [交 (char)](../characters/交%20(char).md) "mix, exchange" — literally "association-mixing," i.e. socializing.

社交 names social interaction and etiquette — mingling with others in a social setting, and by extension the skills and manners appropriate to it (社交的な人, "a sociable/gregarious person"; 社交界, "high society"; 社交辞令, "polite small talk, social pleasantries").

Mandarin shèjiāo, Cantonese se5 gaau1, Japanese しゃこう (shakō), Korean 사교 (sagyo), and Vietnamese xã giao are all fully compositional and share this same everyday social-interaction sense across the sphere.
