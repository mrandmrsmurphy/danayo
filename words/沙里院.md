## Notes
- [沙](../characters/沙%20(char).md) "sand" + [里](../characters/里%20(char).md) "village" + [院](../characters/院.md) "institution; courtyard" — the name of Sariwon, a city of North Korea.

沙里院 (Sariwon) is a city of North Korea and the capital of North Hwanghae Province ([[黄海]]北道) (from memory, not re-checked). The characters give 沙 "sand", 里 "village" and 院 "institution, courtyard", and the Korean name 사리원 is spelled with the three hanja one for one.

English and Chinese Wiktionary have 沙里院 as the city (Mandarin Shālǐyuàn, Cantonese saa1 lei5 jyun6), and English Wiktionary has a Korean entry as a hanja form of 사리원. Japanese Wiktionary has no entry, so Japanese さりいん is composed from the on'yomi. Vietnamese Sa Lý Viện is composed from the character pages; no Vietnamese dictionary entry was found.
