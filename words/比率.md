## Notes
- [比 (char)](../characters/比%20(char).md) "to compare; than" + [率](../characters/率.md) "rate; ratio; to lead" — stand-in for 率, which cannot appear independently

比率 pairs 比 (to compare, to put side by side — graphemically two people standing parallel to each other) with 率 (rate, ratio, proportion — the measure of one thing against another) to express the mathematical concept of ratio: a comparison of two quantities expressed as a fraction or proportion. The compound is transparently compositional: 比 names the act of comparison, and 率 names the quantitative expression of its result.

率 is notable for its multiple readings in modern Chinese (lǜ for ratio/rate; shuài for to lead, a general, or a fraction of troops) and in Sino-Japanese (RITSU for ratio, SOTSU for leadership/general). These two readings stem from distinct original meanings that happened to be written with the same character, creating the unusual situation where 比率 (bǐlǜ) and 将率 (jiàngshuài, commander) use the same character differently. In mathematical vocabulary, 率 appears widely: 比率 (ratio), 確率 (probability), 効率 (efficiency), 利率 (interest rate), 税率 (tax rate), and 成長率 (growth rate) all use 率 in this sense.

In Japanese, ひりつ (hiritsu) is standard for mathematical ratio, glossed on Wiktionary as "ratio, proportion, percentage"; everyday expressions of proportion often use the native suffix 割 (-wari, tenths: 七割 = 70%) (from memory, not re-checked). Korean 비율 (biyul) in South Korea is 비률 (biryul) in the North Korean orthography used in this vault's `korean` field, where the initial-law shift of 률 is not applied; the Korean entry on Wiktionary is a hanja form of 비율, glossed "ratio, proportion". Mandarin bǐlǜ and Cantonese bei2 leot6 are the standard readings; Wiktionary also lists a Hokkien reading pí-lu̍t. No Vietnamese reading for the compound is attested on Wiktionary or hvdic.thivien.net (which lists only the individual characters), so the `vietnamese` field is omitted; the near-synonym [[比例]] is Vietnamese tỉ lệ.

Dan'a'yo ㄅㄧㄜㄌㄨㄊ is the plain concatenation of 比 ㄅㄧㄜ and 率 ㄌㄨㄊ, with 羅馬字 biǝ + lud and 諺文 븨 + 룯. No syllable-boundary dot is needed: there is no syllable ㄅㄧㄜㄌ in `syllables/`, so the only complete legal partition is ㄅㄧㄜ + ㄌㄨㄊ. This word is the stand-in for 率, which is otherwise bound (here the reading is lǜ/RITSU; the shuài/SOTSU "to lead" reading is a separate usage, as in [[率直]] or [[統率]] in this vault). On [[Measurement]], 比率 additionally covers "rate", which is why it is that page's primary entry over the near-duplicate [[比例]]; Dan'a'yo keeps the shared CJKV sense here, with no divergence.
