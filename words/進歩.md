## Notes
- [進](../characters/進.md) "advance" + [歩](../characters/歩.md) "step"

進歩 is progress: to advance and improve, as a society, a technology or a skill does. Wiktionary glosses Japanese しんぽ as "progress; improvements in social welfare" and "to advance in proficiency". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 진보 matches.

It carries the idea of getting better, not merely of going on. The opposite is [後退](後退.md), and a slow steady form is [漸進](漸進.md). The word for growth in size or age is [成長](成長.md).
