## Notes
- [共 (char)](../characters/共%20(char).md) "together, common" + [同](../characters/同.md) "same, together" — "joint, common, mutual" (性詞 sense: 共同作業, "joint work"; 共同体, "a community"); by extension also used verbally for "to cooperate, collaborate." Mandarin gòngtóng, Cantonese gung6 tung4, Japanese きょうどう, and Korean 공동 are all directly attested standard readings. `vietnamese: cộng đồng` is the same compositional compound, but it has narrowed in modern Vietnamese specifically to the noun "community" rather than the adjective "common, joint" — everyday Vietnamese uses the native chung for the adjectival sense (e.g. hợp tác chung, "joint cooperation"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 굥동 diverges from Sino-Korean 공동 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼄ㄫㄉㄛㄫ unique among both words and characters).
