## Notes
- [旗](../characters/旗.md) "banner, flag" + [艦](../characters/艦.md) "warship" — no stand-in relationship applies (旗's own stand-in is [[旗幟]]; 艦's own is [[艦船]]).

旗艦 means flagship.

Mandarin qíjiàn, Cantonese kei4 laam6, Japanese きかん, and Korean 기함 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed `vietnamese`: the previous value soái hạm uses 帥 ("commander"), a genuinely different real-world Vietnamese formation, not derived from 旗 — corrected to the compositional kì hạm; soái hạm remains the more common everyday term. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄧㄏㄚㄇ unique across both words and characters).
