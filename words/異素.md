## Notes
- [異](../characters/異.md) "different, foreign" + [素](../characters/素.md) "element" — no stand-in relationship applies (異's own stand-in is [[異常]]; 素's own is [[要素]]).

**異素** is a semantic translation for the noble gas **Xenon**, chosen over the alternative 奇素, because 奇 shares its Dan'a'yo reading, ㄍㄨㄧ, with 鬼 (the abbreviation character for cobalt, 鬼金), which would create a pronunciation collision between two unrelated elements on the periodic table; 異 avoids that collision and better matches the underlying Greek etymology besides. 奇素 is kept as an alias.

### 1. Etymological Alignment: The Foreign, the Different
The name **[Xenon](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/xenon#Etymology)** was coined by William Ramsay and Morris Travers in 1898, from Greek **ξένος** (*xénos*), meaning "foreigner," "stranger," or "guest." 異 (Mandarin: *yì*; Japanese: *i*; Korean: *i*; Vietnamese: *dị*) means "different, foreign, other" — arguably an even tighter semantic match to *xénos* than the earlier 奇 ("strange, odd, rare"), since 異 names *otherness* and *foreignness* directly rather than mere rarity or oddity. By using **異素**, the coinage captures the exact sense in which the element was named: a "foreign, other" substance discovered as a vanishingly rare trace component of the atmosphere.

### 2. "Different" Physical and Chemical Behavior
Although it is a noble gas, Xenon behaves quite differently from the rest of its group:

- **The Non-Inert Noble Gas:** Xenon was the first noble gas discovered to [form chemical compounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xenon#History) (such as xenon hexafluoroplatinate). In a group originally thought to be "absolutely stable," it is the outlier that breaks the rules — genuinely *different* from its neighbors.
- **Optical Applications:** Xenon gas produces an extremely high-intensity white light, commonly used in [camera flashes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xenon_flash_lamp) and movie projectors — a "different" kind of light that closely mimics daylight, further justifying the name **異素**.
