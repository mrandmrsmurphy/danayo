## Notes
- [楊](../characters/楊.md) "poplar, aspen tree" + [樹](../characters/樹.md) "plant" — no stand-in relationship applies (楊's own stand-in is [[楊柳]]; 樹's own is [[樹木]]).

楊樹 means poplar tree or aspen tree.

Mandarin yángshù and Cantonese joeng4 syu6 are standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese ポプラ and Korean 포플러 are the real, standard loanword terms for poplar in these languages — no compositional Sino-Japanese/Sino-Korean compound is in common use, kept as-is (the same pattern seen on [[板球]] and [[果醤]]). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with the compositional form dương thụ. No homophones (注音 ⼘ㄫㄙㄨ unique across both words and characters).
