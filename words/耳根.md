## Notes
- [耳 (char)](../characters/耳%20(char).md) "ear" + [根 (char)](../characters/根%20(char).md) "root, foundation" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both are stand-ins for themselves), so `#cranberry` does not apply.

耳根 names the root of the ear — in Buddhist doctrine, one of the six sense faculties (六根), the auditory organ as the basis of hearing-consciousness.

Mandarin ěrgēn, Cantonese ji5 gan1, Japanese にこん (nikon), and Korean 이근 (igeun) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 耳根 is itself a common attested Buddhist term for "auditory faculty" in all four languages. Vietnamese nhĩ căn is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading.
