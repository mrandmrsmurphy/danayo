## Notes
- [格](../characters/格%20(char).md) "case, status" + [式](../characters/式.md) "style" — no stand-in relationship applies (格's own stand-in is itself; 式's own is [[様式]]).

格式 means format, form, or style.

Mandarin géshì, Cantonese gaak3 sik1, Japanese かくしき, Korean 격식, and Vietnamese cách thức are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 格's and 式's own stored citations exactly). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/`aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄎㄙㄧㄎ unique across both words and characters).

Dan'a'yo keeps the shared CJKV sense: the set form or arrangement that something follows. It is placed on the Mind page under Structure, Pattern & Design, beside [[様式]] and [[型式]].
