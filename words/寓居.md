## Notes
- [寓](../characters/寓.md) "residence" + [居](../characters/居.md) "reside" — this word is the stand-in for [寓](../characters/寓.md), which cannot appear independently.

寓居 means lodging or a temporary dwelling. The character 寓 alone means residence, but is too bounded to stand by itself in Dan'a'yo, so this compound serves as its viable form.
