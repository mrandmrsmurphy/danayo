## Notes
- [司](../characters/司.md) "manage, command" + [令](../characters/令%20(char).md) "order, command" + [部](../characters/部%20(char).md) "section, department" — "command department," a headquarters. Their own `stand_in` values: 司's is [[公司]]; 令's is itself; 部's is itself; none points here, so 司令部 is an independent compound.

司令部 is a headquarters: the command center of a military force. Mandarin sīlìngbù, Cantonese si1 ling6 bou6, Japanese しれいぶ and Korean 사령부 all use it. Compare [[拠点]] (base), [[陣地]] (position) and [[将校]] (officer).
