## Notes
- [泰](../characters/泰.md) "peaceful" + [然 (char)](../characters/然%20(char).md) "so"
- This word is the viable form of [泰](../characters/泰.md): the character's meaning (peaceful, safe) is identical, but 泰 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 泰然 is its `stand_in` compound.

泰 means peaceful, secure and at ease (as in 泰平 "peace," and the hexagram 泰 of the *Yijing*, which stands for harmony and things going smoothly), and 然 is a suffix that turns a word into a state, "in the manner of." So 泰然 is "in a state of ease": a calm that holds steady under pressure, not merely the absence of agitation. It is most often used of a person facing a crisis, as in the idiom 泰然自若 "calm and self-possessed."

It is a more dignified, steady word than [平静](平静.md) "calm, tranquil," which can describe a still sea or a mood as readily as a person, and 泰然 more specifically implies composure in the face of something that could have disturbed it. The shared readings are Mandarin tàirán, Cantonese taai3 jin4 and Korean 태연 (Japanese taizen, たいぜん, but the page's Japanese field is left blank). Dan'a'yo keeps the shared sense "calm, composed, unruffled" with no divergence, and places it under [Emotions](../lexipedia/Emotions.md).
