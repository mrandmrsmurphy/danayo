## Notes
- [頻 (char)](../characters/頻%20(char).md) "frequent; again and again" — this word is 頻's own legitimizing compound (頻's `stand_in` points here).

頻 means "frequent, repeatedly." Mandarin pín, Cantonese pan4, Korean 빈 (bin), and Sino-Vietnamese tần all preserve the Sino reading directly. Japanese ひん (hin) survives mainly bound in compounds (頻度, 頻繁); the native reading しきりに (shikirini, "repeatedly, incessantly") is the everyday word for the same sense.
