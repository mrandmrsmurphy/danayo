## Notes
- [存](../characters/存.md) "exist, survive" + [在](../characters/在%20(char).md) "be present, at" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [存](../characters/存.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (在's own stand-in is itself).

存在 means to exist or survive — the state of having being, of being present in reality.

Mandarin cúnzài, Cantonese cyun4 zoi6, Japanese そんざい, Korean 존재, and Vietnamese tồn tại are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄛㄋㄐㄚㄧ unique across both words and characters).
