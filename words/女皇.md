## Notes
- [女](../characters/女%20(char).md) "woman" + [皇](../characters/皇.md) "emperor" — no stand-in relationship applies (女's own stand-in is itself; 皇's own is [[皇帝]]).

女皇 means empress (a female sovereign ruling in her own right) — literally "female emperor," historically used of ruling empresses like Wu Zetian.

Mandarin nǚhuáng, Cantonese neoi5 wong4, and Vietnamese nữ hoàng are all standard, fully compositional readings.
