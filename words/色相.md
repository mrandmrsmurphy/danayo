## Notes
- [色](../characters/色.md) "color" + [相](../characters/相.md) "appearance, aspect" — hue.

色相 is hue: the attribute of a color that makes it red, yellow, green, blue and so on, independent of how light or vivid it is. In a color model it is the angle around the color wheel; the other two attributes are [[彩度]] (saturation) and [[明度]] (lightness). In Buddhist usage 色相 names the outward appearance of material things, from which the color sense is drawn. Mandarin sèxiàng, Cantonese sik1 soeng3, Japanese しきそう and Korean 색상 are the standard readings.
