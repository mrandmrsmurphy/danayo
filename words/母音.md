## Notes
- [母](../characters/母.md) "mother" + [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" — no stand-in relationship applies (母's own stand-in is [[母親]]; 音's own is [[音楽]]).

母音 means vowel.

Mandarin mǔyīn, Cantonese mou5 jam1, and Vietnamese mẫu âm are compositional readings built from 母's and 音's own stored citations. Japanese ぼいん (ボ+イン) and Korean 모음 (모+음) happen to coincide with the real-world Japanese/Korean words for "vowel" — a coincidental match, not a loanword exception, since all five readings here are independently compositional. Fixed `mandarin`/`cantonese`/`vietnamese`, which had been contaminated with readings of the real Mandarin term 元音 ("yuányīn"/"jyun4 jam1"/"Nguyên âm") rather than 母's and 音's own citations; 元音 is correctly kept as an `aliases` entry, reformatted to block-list YAML. `kwin: false` because 諺文 못움 (mechanically derived from 羅馬字 mou'um) diverges from the real Korean reading 모음. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄛㄨㄨㄇ unique across both words and characters).
