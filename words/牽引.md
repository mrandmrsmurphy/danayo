## Notes
- [牽](../characters/牽.md) "tow, pull" + [引 (char)](../characters/引%20(char).md) "pull, tug"
- This word is the stand-in for [[牽]], which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo.

牽引 means to pull along, to tow or haul: 牽 is "to lead by a rope, to drag" (as an ox or horse is led) and 引 is "to pull". Mandarin qiānyǐn, Cantonese hin1 jan5, Japanese けんいん (ken'in; 牽引車, "a tractor unit", 牽引免許, "a towing licence") and Korean 견인 (gyeonin; 견인차, "a tow truck") are the same compound. In medicine it is also "traction", the steady pulling force applied to a limb or the spine (牽引療法), and in Chinese it is used figuratively of one thing driving or pulling another along.

In Dan'a'yo it is the sustained, heavy pulling word, a step up from plain [[引]] ("to pull, tug").
