## Notes
- [第](../characters/第%20(char).md) "-th" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 第 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

第 forms ordinal numbers when prefixed to a cardinal number — 第一 ("first"), 第二 ("second"), 第三 ("third") — and appears in fixed expressions like 及第 ("to pass an imperial examination/grade") and 次第 ("order, sequence").

Mandarin dì, Cantonese dai6, Japanese だい (dai), and Korean 제 (je) are all standard, fully compositional readings, each the ordinary ordinal-forming morpheme in its language. Vietnamese đệ is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading. This word is part of a genuine three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read dei/데/ㄉㄝㄧ): [[底]] ("bottom") and [[蹄]] ("hoof").
