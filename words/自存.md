## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" + [存](../characters/存.md) "to survive; exist"

自存 is "to subsist on one's own; self-preservation," a technical word for existing and maintaining oneself independently, as in 民族自存 "the self-preservation of a people." Wiktionary has no entry for it in any language, so it is not independently attested there; it is coined here, by decision, as an allowed technical word, for the 1919 Korean Declaration of Independence ([宣言書](../translation/宣言書.md)) (民族自存의 正權, "the rightful claim of a people to preserve itself"). `mandarin` zìcún, `cantonese` zi6 cyun4, `japanese` じそん, `korean` 자존 and `vietnamese` tự tồn are compositional readings of the two characters, not attested words. (From memory, not re-checked: Korean 자존 is also the ordinary reading of a different word, 自尊 "self-esteem," so the two would be homophones in Korean.)
