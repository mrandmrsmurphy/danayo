## Notes
- [出 (char)](../characters/出%20(char).md) "to exit, emerge" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color, appearance" — literally "the color/complexion stands out," i.e. outstanding, remarkable.

出色 is a literary-register Mandarin word (chūsè) meaning "outstanding, remarkable, extraordinary" — Wiktionary's own thesaurus groups it directly alongside [優秀](優秀.md) (yōuxiù) and 傑出 (jiéchū) as near-synonyms for exceptional quality or performance. Mandarin chūsè and Cantonese ceot1 sik1 are both standard, fully compositional readings, and Vietnamese xuất sắc is directly confirmed and attested (built from 出's own xuất and 色's own sắc, both already primary on their respective character pages).

Japanese しゅっしょく shows the expected sokuon assimilation of しゅつ (SHUTSU, 出's primary on'yomi) before SHOKU's unvoiced onset, the same pattern seen in [一致](一致.md) and [一体](一体.md); it is compositional and not independently confirmed as live Japanese vocabulary. Korean 출색 is likewise compositional only.
