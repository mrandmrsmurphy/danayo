## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" is separately legitimized by [[自身]]. [足](../characters/足%20(char).md) "foot" is separately legitimized by [[足]] itself. No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

自足 combines 自 ("self") with 足 ("sufficient, enough" — from "foot," extended to "adequate") — "be self-sufficient, be satisfied with oneself," as in 自給自足.

Pronunciation fields (jiǝjog/즤족/ㄐㄧㄜㄐㄛㄎ) are the straightforward concatenation of each constituent's own stored reading — no bug. `kwin` is false per the AND-rule: 自 individually carries `kwin: false` (despite 足's own `kwin: true`).
