## Notes
- [体](../characters/体.md) "body" + [温](../characters/温.md) "warm; temperature"

体温 is body temperature: the heat of a living body. Wiktionary glosses Japanese たいおん as "body temperature". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 체온 matches.

It is one of the signs of health, along with the [脈拍](脈拍.md), pulse, and the [体重](体重.md), weight. A raised one is a fever.
