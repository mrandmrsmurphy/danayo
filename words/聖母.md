## Notes
- [聖](../characters/聖.md) "holy, sacred" + [母](../characters/母.md) "mother" — "holy mother," Mary. Their own `stand_in` values: 聖's is [[神聖]]; 母's is [[母親]]; none points here, so 聖母 is an independent compound.

聖母 is Mary, the mother of Jesus, called the holy mother. Mandarin shèngmǔ, Cantonese sing3 mou5, Japanese せいぼ and Korean 성모 all use it, and Vietnamese thánh mẫu does too, though in Chinese the same word can also name a goddess. In Dan'a'yo it names Mary, the mother of [[救偕]] (Jesus). Compare [[処女]] (virgin) and [[聖人]] (saint).
