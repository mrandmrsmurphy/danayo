## Notes
- [南](../characters/南.md) "south" + [山](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain" — no stand-in relationship applies (南's own stand-in is [[南方]]; 山 is its own stand-in).

南山 (Namsan) names Mount Namsan, the well-known mountain in central Seoul topped by Namsan Tower.

Japanese ナムサン is a phonetic-loan transliteration of the Korean place name (rather than a Sino-Japanese on'yomi reading), matching how foreign place names are typically rendered. Korean 남산 and Vietnamese Nam Sơn are standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄚㄇㄙㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
