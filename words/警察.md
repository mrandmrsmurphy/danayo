## Notes
- [警](../characters/警.md) "watch out" + [察](../characters/察.md) "examine, investigate"

警察 means "police" — a clean, directly parallel compound across the whole sphere. Mandarin jǐngchá, Cantonese ging2 caat3, Japanese けいさつ (keisatsu), Korean 경찰 (gyeongchal), and Vietnamese cảnh sát all converge on the same reading order and the same core sense, with no notable cross-linguistic divergence.
