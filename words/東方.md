## Notes
- [東](../characters/東.md) "east" + [方](../characters/方.md) "direction" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 東 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 方's own stand-in is [[方向]].

東方 means east (direction), or the Orient.

Mandarin dōngfāng, Japanese とうほう, Korean 동방, and Vietnamese đông phương are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `cantonese` had a garbled spelling using pinyin-style vowels instead of Jyutping (dong1 fang1 → dung1 fong1, matching 東's and 方's own citations exactly). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄫㄈㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
