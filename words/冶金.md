## Notes
- [冶](../characters/冶.md) "smelt, refine metal" + [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal; gold" — metallurgy.

冶金 is metallurgy, the science and craft of extracting metals from ore and working them: [[冶錬]] smelts, [[精錬]] refines, [[鋳造]] casts and [[鍛造]] forges. The compound is "smelt" + "metal"; 冶 is the same character as in [[鍛冶]]. Mandarin yějīn, Japanese やきん and Korean 야금 are the standard readings. Not to be confused with [[錬金術]], alchemy.
