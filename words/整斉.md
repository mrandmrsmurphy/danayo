## Notes
- [整](../characters/整%20(char).md) "orderly, neat, tidy" + [斉](../characters/斉.md) "simultaneous" — no stand-in relationship applies (整's own stand-in is itself; 斉's own is [[一斉]]).

整斉 means to arrange, straighten, rectify, or put in order.

Mandarin zhěngqí, Cantonese zing2 cai4, Japanese せいせい, Korean 정제, and Vietnamese chỉnh tề (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄫㄐㄝㄧ unique across both words and characters).
