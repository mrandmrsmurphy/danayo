## Notes
- [暦](../characters/暦%20(char).md) "calendar, almanac" + [数](../characters/数.md) "count, number" — 数's own stand-in is [[計数]] (verb sense "to count"); this compound uses 数's noun sense ("a count, a system of numbers"), which is why `cantonese` here is sou3 rather than the sou2 stored on 数's own citation (a real, documented Cantonese verb/noun split for this character, also seen in [[数学]]'s sou3 vs [[計数]]'s sou2 — not a bug).

暦数 means calendar system.

Mandarin lìshù, Cantonese lik6 sou3, Japanese れきすう, Korean 력수, and Vietnamese lịch số are all standard, fully compositional readings.
