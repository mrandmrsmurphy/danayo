## Notes
- [入 (char)](../characters/入%20(char).md) "enter" + [試](../characters/試.md) "test"

入試 is an entrance exam: the test one sits to be admitted to a school or university. Wiktionary glosses Japanese にゅうし as "entrance exam". Wiktionary has no Chinese or Korean entry for the word, so the Mandarin, Cantonese and Korean readings are built from the character readings.

It is sat by the [受験](受験.md) candidate before [入学](入学.md), and it is a kind of [試験](試験.md). China's national university-entrance exam has its own name, [高考](高考.md).
