## Notes
- [因](../characters/因.md) "cause" + [果](../characters/果.md) "effect, fruit" — no stand-in relationship applies (因's own stand-in is [[原因]]; 果's is [[果実]]).

因果 means causal, karmic, or fateful — the Buddhist doctrine of cause and effect. Calque of Sanskrit हेतु (hetu, "impulse, cause") and फल (phala, "fruit, result") arising from translation of Buddhist texts. (Dassanayake (2021), pg. 124)

**Real bug found and fixed**: `japanese` used the archaic pre-reform kana spelling いんぐわ (reflecting the historical /kwa/ sound now merged with /ka/) — corrected to modern いんが. Korean 인과 and Vietnamese nhân quả are both standard readings, matching Dan'a'yo's own reading exactly (`kwin: true`). Filled blank `pos: 性詞`; fixed empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄧㄋㄍ⺢ unique across both words and characters).
