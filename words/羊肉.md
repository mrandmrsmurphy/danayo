## Notes
- [羊](../characters/羊.md) "sheep" + [肉 (char)](../characters/肉%20(char).md) "meat"

羊肉 is mutton: the meat of the sheep, and in Chinese also lamb and goat meat. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yángròu as "mutton; lamb; goat meat" and Japanese ようにく as "mutton". Cantonese joeng4 juk6 matches, and Korean 양육 is the reading of the characters.

It is the meat of the sheep, beside [牛肉](牛肉.md), beef, and [豚肉](豚肉.md), pork. Meat in general is the [肉](肉.md).
