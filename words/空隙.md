## Notes
- [空 (char)](../characters/空%20(char).md) "empty, sky" (stand-in: itself) + [隙 (char)](../characters/隙%20(char).md) "crack, gap" (stand-in: itself) — a gap, a space left unfilled.

Wiktionary: Chinese and Japanese 空隙 (くうげき) both mean "gap; space; opening; crack" and "interval; spare time; break"; Chinese adds "chance; opportunity; loophole". Used in `文法 - 02音韻論` in 分布空隙, a distributional gap.
