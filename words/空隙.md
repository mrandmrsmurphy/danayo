## Notes
- [空 (char)](../characters/空.md) "empty, sky" (stand-in: itself) + [隙 (char)](../characters/隙.md) "crack, gap" (stand-in: itself) — a gap, a space left unfilled.

空隙 means gap, as invoked by `文法 - 02音韻論`'s 分布空隙 ("distributional gap," a phonotactic combination that happens not to occur). Japanese くうげき and Korean 공극 are both real, standard terms, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Cantonese hung1 gwik1 is a straightforward compositional reading. Mandarin kōngxì and Vietnamese không khích are compositional only. `kwin` is false: both 空 and 隙 are individually false.

No syllable-boundary dot is needed: the concatenated string `ㄎㄛㄫㄎㄝㄎ` admits only one legal partition.
