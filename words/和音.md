## Notes
- [和](../characters/和.md) "harmony" + [音](../characters/音.md) "sound"

和音 is a chord: notes sounded together in harmony. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin héyīn as "harmonious tune; pleasing combination of sounds" and Japanese わおん as "chord". Korean 화음 is the same word. Cantonese wo4 jam1 matches.

Together with the [旋律](旋律.md), melody, and the [拍子](拍子.md), beat, it makes up a piece. The notes are built on the [音階](音階.md), scale.
