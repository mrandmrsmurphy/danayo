## Notes
- [緑 (char)](../characters/緑%20(char).md) "green" — the bare character used directly as a word, its own `stand_in`.

緑 means green: the color of growing plants (緑色, "green"; 緑茶, "green tea"; 緑地, "green space"), and by extension environmentally friendly (緑化, "greening, afforestation"). The traditional form 綠 and simplified 绿 are listed as aliases. It is also this vault's periodic-table abbreviation prefix for beryllium via [[緑柱素]], after the green mineral beryl (緑柱石).

Mandarin lǜ, Cantonese luk6, Korean 록 (rok, the vault's standing North Korean 문화어 form; 녹색 is the South Korean spelling), and Vietnamese lục all use this character productively and independently for "green" (緑色/록색, 緑地/록지). Japanese, by contrast, almost always uses the native みどり (midori) for the color itself, reserving the on'yomi りょく/ろく for compounds (緑化/りょっか, "afforestation, greening"; 緑茶/りょくちゃ, "green tea").
