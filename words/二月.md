## Notes
- [二 (char)](characters/二%20(char).md) "two/second" + [月 (char)](characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — February, the second month.

Mandarin èryuè, Cantonese ji6 jut6, Japanese にがつ, Korean 이월, and Vietnamese tháng hai are all standard, fully compositional readings, following the regular ordinal-month naming pattern used throughout the sphere. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 늬웓 diverges from Sino-Korean 이월, since both 二 and 月 independently carry `kwin: false` on their own character pages.
