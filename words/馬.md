## Notes
- [馬](../characters/馬%20(char).md) "horse" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 馬 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

馬 names the horse, one of the most culturally significant animals across the Sinosphere — central to warfare, transport, and the Chinese zodiac (午, the seventh sign). It is highly productive: 馬車 ("carriage"), 馬力 ("horsepower"), 木馬 ("wooden/rocking horse"), and the idiom 馬到成功 ("instant success upon the horse's arrival," a common good-luck wish).

Mandarin mǎ, Cantonese maa5, Japanese ま (ma), Korean 마 (ma), and Vietnamese mã are all standard, fully compositional readings.
