## Notes
- [天](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven" + [狗](../characters/狗.md) "dog" — no stand-in relationship applies (天 is its own stand-in; 狗's own is [[名専字]]).

天狗 means tengu (Japanese folklore) or Tiangou (Chinese folklore) — a legendary dog- or bird-like creature, literally "heaven's dog."

Mandarin tiāngǒu, Korean 천구, and Cantonese tin1 gau2 are standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese てんぐ uses rendaku (KU→GU voicing) on 狗's on'yomi, the real lexicalized pronunciation of this term in Japanese. Vietnamese thiên cẩu is a standard reading. Filled blank `pos: 名詞` (matching the parallel mythical-creature noun [[信天翁]], not proper-noun place/star names like [[天竺]]/[[天王星]]). Blanked empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄝㄋㄍㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).
