## Notes
- [芸](../characters/芸.md) "art" + [術 (char)](../characters/術%20(char).md) "technique" + [家](../characters/家.md) "house; specialist"

芸術家 is an artist: a person who makes art as a calling. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yìshùjiā as "artist" and Japanese げいじゅつか as "artist". Korean 예술가 is the same word. Cantonese ngai6 seot6 gaa1 matches.

The art itself is [芸術](芸術.md), and the more specific makers are the [画家](画家.md), painter, the [彫刻家](彫刻家.md), sculptor, and the [音楽家](音楽家.md), musician.
