## Notes
- [故](../characters/故.md) "old, former" + [人](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" — no stand-in relationship applies (故's own stand-in is [[緣故]]; 人's own is itself).

故人 means a deceased person, or an old friend.

Mandarin gùrén, Cantonese gu3 jan4, Japanese こじん, Korean 고인, and Vietnamese cố nhân are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄋㄧㄋ unique across both words and characters).
