## Notes
- [溝 (char)](characters/溝%20(char).md) "gutter" + [涜](characters/涜.md) "gutter" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 涜 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (溝's own stand-in is itself, so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).
