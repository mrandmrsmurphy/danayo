## Notes
- [修](../characters/修.md) "repair" + [理](../characters/理.md) "-ology, reason, logic" — `vietnamese: tu lý` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese (more often spelled tu lí), an everyday term for repairing objects, machinery, or electronics. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 숫리 diverges from Sino-Korean 수리 in the first syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨㄛㄌㄧ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 修's own `stand_in` is this exact word (修理), so 修 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 修理.
