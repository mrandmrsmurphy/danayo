## Notes
- [抗](../characters/抗.md) "resist, oppose" + [拒](../characters/拒.md) "repel, refuse" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [拒](../characters/拒.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (抗's own stand-in is [[抵抗]]).

抗拒 means to repel or refuse.

Mandarin kàngjù, Cantonese kong3 keoi5, Japanese こうきょ, Korean 항거, and Vietnamese kháng cự are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄫㄍ⼄ unique across both words and characters).
