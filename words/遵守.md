## Etymology
- [遵](../characters/遵.md) "abide by, obey" + [守](../characters/守.md) "protect"

## Notes
Mandarin zūnshǒu, cantonese zeon1 sau2, japanese じゅんしゅ, korean 준수, and vietnamese tuân thủ are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 준슈 diverges from Sino-Korean 준수 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄨㄋㄙ⼜ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 遵's own `stand_in` is this exact word (遵守), so 遵 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 遵守; 守's own `stand_in` is instead 守衛, so this word does not legitimize 守.
