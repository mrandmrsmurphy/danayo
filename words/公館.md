## Notes
- [公 (char)](../characters/公%20(char).md) "public, communal" + [館](../characters/館.md) "hall, building" — this word is the stand-in for [館](../characters/館.md), which cannot appear independently.

公館 is an official residence — a large building belonging to the public or the state. The character 館 alone means a large hall or building, but is too bounded to stand by itself in Dan'a'yo, so this compound serves as its viable form.

Mandarin gōngguǎn, Cantonese gung1 gun2, Japanese こうかん, and Korean 공관 are all standard, fully compositional readings across the CJKV sphere.
