## Notes
- [正 (char)](../characters/正%20(char).md) "front; straight (stand-in: itself)" + [面](../characters/面.md) "face; surface (stand-in: [[表面]])"

Wiktionary: Chinese 正面 "front; façade; frontage" and "right side; obverse", also "direct; head-on" and "positive; good"; Japanese 正面 "front, façade (as of a building)" and "direct front side of something". The Dan'a'yo sense is the front side; its counterpart is [[背面]].
