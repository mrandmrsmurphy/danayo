## Notes
- [施](../characters/施.md) "execute" + [行](../characters/行%20(char).md) "act, go" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [施](../characters/施.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (行's own stand-in is itself).

施行 means to execute or carry out (a law or plan).

Mandarin shīxíng, Cantonese si1 hang4, Japanese しこう, Korean 시행, and Vietnamese thi hành (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄏㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
