## Notes
- [接](../characters/接%20(char).md) "contact, connect" + [触](../characters/触.md) "contact, touch" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [触](../characters/触.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (接's own stand-in is itself).

接触 means contact or touch.

Mandarin jiēchù, Cantonese zip3 zuk1, Japanese せっしょく, Korean 접촉, and Vietnamese tiếp xúc are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄛㄆㄑㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
