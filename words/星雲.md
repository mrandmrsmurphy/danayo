## Notes
- [星](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" + [雲](../characters/雲%20(char).md) "cloud" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 星 and 雲's own stand-ins are themselves).

星雲 means nebula.

Mandarin xīngyún, Cantonese sing1 wan4, Japanese せいうん, Korean 성운, and Vietnamese tinh vân are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄫㄨㄋ unique across both words and characters).
