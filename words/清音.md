## Notes
- [清](../characters/清.md) "pure, clear" + [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" — no stand-in relationship applies (清's own stand-in is [[清潔]]; 音's own is [[音楽]]).

清音 means unvoiced (voiceless) sound, a linguistic term for consonants produced without vocal cord vibration.

Mandarin qīngyīn, Cantonese cing1 jam1, Japanese せいおん, and Korean 청음 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 清's and 音's own stored citations exactly), and also the real linguistics term in each language.
