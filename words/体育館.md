## Notes
- [体](../characters/体.md) "group, body, form" + [育 (char)](../characters/育%20(char).md) "nurture, educate" + [館](../characters/館.md) "hall, building, wing" — "gymnasium, gym building." Directly attested and standard across mandarin tǐyùguǎn, japanese たいいくかん, korean 체육관. `vietnamese: nhà thể dục` incorporates 館's own building sense (nhà, "house/hall") rather than reusing [[体育]]'s own bare "thể dục" (which names only the subject, not the building) — presented as a defensible compositional construction rather than a confirmed fixed idiom.
