## Notes
- [金](../characters/金%20(char).md) "gold" + [字](../characters/字%20(char).md) "character" + [塔](../characters/塔%20(char).md) "tower" — "golden-character tower," a pyramid. Their own `stand_in` values: 金's is itself; 字's is itself; 塔's is itself; none points here, so 金字塔 is an independent compound.

金字塔 is a pyramid: the monumental building with a square base and sloping triangular sides, and, figuratively, a monumental achievement. Mandarin jīnzìtǎ and Cantonese gam1 zi6 taap3 are the ordinary words, and Japanese きんじとう and Korean 금자탑 are the same compound, used mainly in the figurative sense; Vietnamese kim tự tháp does too. It is named for the shape of the character 金. Compare [[三角形]] (triangle) and [[塔]] (tower).
