## Notes
- [永](../characters/永.md) "eternal" + [珍 (char)](../characters/珍%20(char).md) "rare" — "eternal treasure"; no stand-in relationship applies (永's own stand-in is [[永遠]], 珍's is the single-character word [[珍]]).

永珍 is Vientiane, the capital of Laos ([[寮国]]). English Wiktionary gives the etymology as a phono-semantic matching of Lao ວຽງຈັນ (Viangchan), reaching Chinese through Cantonese, and labels the sense as used in Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan, Malaysia and Singapore. For mainland China it lists the synonym 萬象 (Wànxiàng), which is not a word in the vault, so this spelling is the Dan'a'yo name of the city.

Mandarin Yǒngzhēn and Cantonese wing5 zan1 are as at English Wiktionary. Japanese ビエンチャン and Korean 비엔티안 are the ordinary names of the city (from memory, not re-checked), so `kwin` is false. The `vietnamese` field holds the compositional Hán-Việt reading vĩnh trân (each character as its page gives it; not attested for this name).

No homophones (注音 ㄨㄧㄫㄑㄧㄋ unique across words and characters, checked 2026-10-12). The 注音 needs no syllable-boundary dot.
