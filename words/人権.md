## Notes
- [人](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" + [権](../characters/権.md) "right, authority" — "rights of persons." 権's own `stand_in` is [[権利]] and 人's is itself; neither points here, so 人権 is an independent compound.

人権 is human rights: the entitlements held by every person simply as a person. Mandarin rénquán, Cantonese jan4 kyun4, Japanese じんけん, Korean 인권 and Vietnamese nhân quyền all use these characters. It is a modern Sino-Japanese rendering of the Western concept, spread through Japanese translation in the nineteenth century. Compare [[権利]] (a right, an entitlement).
