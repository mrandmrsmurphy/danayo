## Notes
Originally an abbreviation of 三公 (the Three Excellencies) + 九卿 (the nine ministers). `vietnamese: công khanh` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese (confirmed via web search), a classical administrative term for "high officials, nobility," an abbreviation of tam công cửu khanh (三公九卿).
