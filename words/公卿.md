Originally an abbreviation of 三公 (the Three Excellencies) + 九卿 (the nine ministers)
## Etymology
- [公 (char)](../characters/公%20(char).md) "sir" + [卿 (char)](../characters/卿%20(char).md) "noble, high officer, sir, m'lord"

## Notes
`vietnamese: công khanh` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese (confirmed via web search), a classical administrative term for "high officials, nobility," an abbreviation of tam công cửu khanh (三公九卿) — directly paralleling this word's own 三公+九卿 etymology note. Filled the blank `cantonese: gung1 hing1`, compositional. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 공켱 diverges from Sino-Korean 공경 in the second syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄫㄎ⼶ㄫ unique among both words and characters).
