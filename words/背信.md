## Notes
- [背](../characters/背%20(char).md) "turn away" + [信](../characters/信.md) "faith" — "turn one's back on faith," to break faith. Their own `stand_in` values: 背's is itself; 信's is [[信任]]; none points here, so 背信 is an independent compound.

背信 is to break faith: to turn away from a trust one was given. Mandarin bèixìn, Cantonese bui3 seon3, Japanese はいしん, Korean 배신 and Vietnamese bội tín all use it. In Dan'a'yo it is the opposite of 信, the faith of [[信仰]].

**Hebrew**: it best translates *maʿal* (מַעַל) "breach of faith, treachery," the sin of unfaithfulness toward God or toward what is holy (Leviticus 5:15; Joshua 7:1; Numbers 5:12 for the unfaithful wife), and *bāgad* (בָּגַד) "to act treacherously." The Greek is *apistia*. It is not [[反逆]], which is open rebellion (*peshaʿ*). Compare [[異端]] (heresy).
