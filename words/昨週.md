## Notes
- [昨](../characters/昨%20(char).md) "last, previous" + [週](../characters/週.md) "week" — no stand-in relationship applies (昨's own stand-in is itself; 週's own is [[週日]]).

昨週 means last week.

Mandarin zuózhōu, Cantonese zok6 zau1, Japanese さくしゅう, Korean 작주, and Vietnamese tạc chu are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bugs: `mandarin` was the unrelated compound 上周 (a different character composition) and `cantonese`/`korean` were contaminated to match — all corrected to compositional. Homophone of [[酌酒]] "pour wine" (both 注音 ㄐㄚㄎㄐㄨㄛ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated compounds, both stand-ins for their own characters.
