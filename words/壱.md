## Notes
- [壱 (char)](../characters/壱%20(char).md) "loyal; faithful"

壱 (traditional 壹) is the formal, complex-stroke variant of [[一]] ("one"), used historically in financial and legal documents to prevent fraudulent alteration — the same function as spelling out "ONE" rather than writing "1" on a check. This Dan'a'yo entry tracks its extended sense specifically: "single-minded, wholehearted, unified in purpose, loyal" (壹心/一心, "of one mind, wholehearted"), kept distinct from the plain numeral [[一]] despite sharing the same underlying character and reading.

Mandarin yī and Cantonese jat1 preserve the reading directly (identical to [[一]]'s own, since 壱/壹 is fundamentally the same morpheme in elaborated form, not a phonetically distinct character). Japanese イチ/イツ (ICHI/ITSU, on'yomi) alongside native ひとつ (hitotsu, "one") cover the sense; 壱 was dropped from the Korean middle-school hanja list in 2000 but the reading itself remains valid. Korean 일 (il) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, identical to 一's own. Vietnamese nhất is likewise identical to 一's own Hán Việt reading, attested for this extended sense in nhất tâm (一心/壹心, "wholehearted, unwavering devotion").
