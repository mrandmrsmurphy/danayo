## Notes
- [効](../characters/効.md) "effect" + [率](../characters/率.md) "rate, ratio" — no stand-in relationship applies (効's own stand-in is [[効果]], 率's is [[比率]]).

効率 means efficiency — the ratio of useful output to input effort or resources.

Japanese こうりつ, Korean 효율 (률→율 after a vowel, per standard Korean orthography), and Vietnamese hiệu suất are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⼘ㄨㄌㄨㄊ unique across both words and characters).
