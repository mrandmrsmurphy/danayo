## Notes
- [慧](../characters/慧%20(char).md) "bright, intelligent" + [眼](../characters/眼.md) "eyeball, pupil" — no stand-in relationship applies (慧's own stand-in is itself; 眼's own is [[眼球]]).

慧眼 means discernment or acumen — literally "wise eye." In Buddhism (佛教), it names the mind which perceives both past and future, an all-seeing mind.

Mandarin huìyǎn, Cantonese wai6 ngaan5, Japanese けいがん, Korean 혜안, and Vietnamese tuệ nhãn are all standard, fully compositional readings. Trimmed comma-joined `vietnamese` variant. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⼔ㄧㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
