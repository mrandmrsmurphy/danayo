## Notes
- [地 (char)](../characters/地%20(char).md) "earth, land" + [支](../characters/支.md) "branch" — the twelve cyclic signs (子丑寅卯辰巳午未申酉戌亥) used with the Ten Heavenly Stems to form the sexagenary cycle

Wiktionary: Chinese, Japanese, and Korean 지지 and Vietnamese địa chi (Han forms) all give "earthly branch"; Chinese adds "the twelve earthly branches as a whole". Wikipedia: the Earthly Branches are a system of twelve ordered symbols used throughout East Asia, indigenous to China, whose characters have no concrete meaning beyond their ordinal position; they are used in the sexagenary cycle and in Chinese astrology, and are associated with the ten Heavenly Stems ([[天干]]) in Chinese calendars.
