## Notes
- [暴 (char)](../characters/暴%20(char).md) "violent" + [行 (char)](../characters/行%20(char).md) "act"

暴行 is an act of violence done to a person: an assault or an outrage. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin bàoxíng as "savage act; atrocity; outrage" and Japanese ぼうこう as "violence; assault". The Japanese sense of rape as a euphemism is not taken. Korean 폭행 and Cantonese bou6 hang6 match.

It is the criminal act, where [暴力](暴力.md) is violence in general. The injury that results is a [傷害](傷害.md).
