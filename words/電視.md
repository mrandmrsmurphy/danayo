## Notes
- [電](../characters/電.md) "electricity" + [視 (char)](../characters/視%20(char).md) "look at, inspect" — no `#cranberry`: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here (電's is [[電気]], 視's is 視 itself).

電視 (diànshì) is the standard, everyday Mandarin word for "television," and Cantonese 電視 (din6si6) likewise. Japanese でんし (denshi) is a real, attested reading of 電視, but is confined mainly to contexts describing Chinese-language media; the ordinary Japanese word for television is the loanword テレビ (terebi). でんし happens to coincide exactly with the everyday Japanese reading of an unrelated word, 電子 ("electron") — both 子 and 視 carry the on'yomi シ, making the two genuinely homophonous in real Japanese, a coincidence rather than a vault error.

Korean 전시 (jeonsi, 電視) is likewise attested as a Sino-Korean reading but not the ordinary word — Korean uses the loanword 텔레비전 (or colloquial 티비) for television. 전시 as a live Korean word instead almost always means 展示 ("exhibition, display"), an unrelated but far more common homophone.

Sino-Vietnamese điện thị exists as a dictionary gloss on the Chinese compound, but is not live Vietnamese vocabulary — the standard modern term is the native compound truyền hình ("transmitted image"), or colloquially the loanword tivi.
