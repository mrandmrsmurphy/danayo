## Notes
- [遅](../characters/遅%20(char).md) "late, slow" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 遅 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

遅 means late or slow, as in 遅到 (be late, arrive late).

Mandarin chí, cantonese ci4, and korean 지 (ji) are all standard, fully compositional readings.
