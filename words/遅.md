## Notes
- [遅](../characters/遅%20(char).md) "late, slow" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 遅 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

遅 means late or slow, as in 遅到 (be late, arrive late).

Mandarin chí, cantonese ci4, and korean 지 (ji) are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Found and fixed two real bugs**: `korean` held the literal string `"null"` instead of the character's own real reading 지; `vietnamese` was likewise `null`, filled with trì (the character's sole stored candidate). Japanese uses the native kun-reading おく (oku, stem of 遅れる "to be late") rather than the on-reading チ (CHI) for the bare character alone. A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone with [[致]] ("cause") — coincidental, unrelated in meaning.
