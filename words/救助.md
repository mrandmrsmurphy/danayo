## Notes
- [救](../characters/救.md) "aid, save" + [助](../characters/助.md) "help, assist" — no stand-in relationship applies (救's own stand-in is [救援](救援.md); 助's own is [援助](援助.md)).

Mandarin jiùzhù, Cantonese gau3 zo6, Japanese きゅうじょ (kyūjo), Korean 구조 (gujo), and Vietnamese cứu trợ are all real, standard everyday words for "to rescue, to save" (often specifically disaster/emergency rescue, e.g. 救助隊/구조대, "rescue team"), not compositional guesses.
