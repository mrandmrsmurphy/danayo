## Notes
- [死](../characters/死.md) "die" + [刑](../characters/刑.md) "punishment; penalty"

死刑 is the death penalty: the sentence of execution imposed by a court. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin sǐxíng as "death penalty; capital punishment" and Japanese しけい as "capital punishment", with Korean 사형 and Cantonese sei2 jing4 matching. It is shared across the sphere with the same meaning.

極刑 is already on the Law page as "capital punishment", the ultimate penalty. 死刑 is the plain, explicit name for the sentence. The sentence is carried out by [誅殺](誅殺.md), "to execute". Short of it come [終身刑](終身刑.md) and [懲役](懲役.md), and a death sentence can be lifted by [減刑](減刑.md) or [恩赦](恩赦.md).
