## Notes
- [牧 (char)](../characters/牧%20(char).md) "shepherd" + [民](../characters/民.md) "citizen" — no stand-in relationship applies (牧's own stand-in is itself; 民's own is [[人民]]).

牧民 means herdsman.

Mandarin mùmín, Cantonese muk6 man4, Japanese ぼくみん, Korean 목민 and Vietnamese mục dân (牧's own mục plus 民's own dân) are all standard, fully compositional readings.
