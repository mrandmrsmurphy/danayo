## Notes
- [䔥](../characters/䔥.md) "Xiao" + [国](../characters/国.md) "nation" — legitimizing note: 䔥's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, so 䔥 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word; 国's own `stand_in` is [[国家]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply.

䔥国 is the stand-in word for [䔥](../characters/䔥.md); 䔥 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse. Names the ancient Chinese State of Xiao (a minor vassal state of the Spring and Autumn period). Mandarin Xiāoguó, Cantonese siu1 gwok3, Japanese しょうこく, Korean 소국, and Sino-Vietnamese Tiêu quốc are all standard compositional readings, capitalized as a proper noun matching the convention used on sibling ancient-state words ([[蜀国]], [[鄂国]], [[斉国]]).
