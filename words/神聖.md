## Notes
- [神 (char)](../characters/神%20(char).md) "god" + [聖](../characters/聖.md) "holy, sage"
- 神聖 is the stand-in for [聖](../characters/聖.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.

Wiktionary lists 神聖 as an adjective in Mandarin (shénshèng, "sacred; holy"), with Cantonese san4 sing3, and as a na-adjective in Japanese (しんせい), taken from Middle Chinese. Korean 신성 and Vietnamese `thần thánh` are recorded there as descendants. Derived terms include 神聖不可侵犯 "sacred and inviolable", 神聖羅馬帝國 "Holy Roman Empire" and 神聖同盟 "Holy Alliance", which show the word's use for the sacred in both religious and political contexts.

In Dan'a'yo the word is the general adjective for sacred or holy, placed on the [Religion](../lexipedia/Religion.md) page and cited again on [基督敎](../lexipedia/基督敎.md).
