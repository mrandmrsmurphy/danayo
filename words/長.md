## Notes
- [長 (char)](../characters/長%20(char).md) "long" — stand-in for [[長 (char)|長]], which cannot appear independently

長 is 象形 of an old man with long hair, the character page's own Notes cross-referencing the same top component shared with [[老]] and its variant [[考]] — an ancient alternate form 镸 survives today only in a couple of compounds.

Beyond the literal hair-length imagery, 長 splits into two related but distinct senses already visible in its own compound family: "long" in extent (長矛 "spear, lance," 長靴 "boot," 長短 "length, long and short," 延長 "to extend, prolong") and "elder, chief, senior" (長上 "elder, superior") — the same dual sense recurring in its Vietnamese and Japanese reading sets. Vietnamese trường matches the "long" sense (trường kỳ "long-term," trường thọ "longevity"), while trưởng carries the elder/chief sense (trưởng lão "elder") and trườn is an unrelated verb ("to crawl"). Japanese carries a single on'yomi ちょう (CHOU, 延長 enchō), but the character's own two native readings track the same sense-split directly: おさ (osa, "a chief, an elder") and なが-い (nagai, "long," the ordinary everyday adjective).
