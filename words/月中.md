## Notes
- [月](../characters/月%20(char).md) "moon; month" + [中](../characters/中.md) "-ing, middle" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 月 and 中's own stand-ins are themselves, each with its own independent word page).

月中 means the middle of the month.

Mandarin yuèzhōng, Cantonese jyut6 zung1, Japanese げっちゅう (げつ+ちゅう with sokuon assimilation), Korean 월중, and Vietnamese nguyệt trung are all standard, fully compositional readings.
