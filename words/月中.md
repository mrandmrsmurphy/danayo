## Notes
- [月](../characters/月%20(char).md) "moon; month" + [中](../characters/中%20(char).md) "-ing, middle" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 月 and 中's own stand-ins are themselves, each with its own independent word page).

月中 means the middle of the month.

Mandarin yuèzhōng, Cantonese jyut6 zung1, Japanese げっちゅう (げつ+ちゅう with sokuon assimilation), Korean 월중, and Vietnamese nguyệt trung are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled the blank `korean`/`japanese`/`vietnamese` fields, added `kwin: false` (諺文 웓중 vs real Korean 월중 differ, per the same 月-coda divergence documented on [[月]]'s own page), and removed the redundant `品詞` field (duplicate of `pos`). No homophones (注音 ⼔ㄊㄐㄨㄫ unique across both words and characters).
