## Notes
- [接](../characters/接%20(char).md) "contact, connect" + [近](../characters/近%20(char).md) "near" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 接 and 近's own stand-ins are themselves).

接近 means to approach, or to be similar/close.

Mandarin jiējìn, Cantonese zip3 gan6, Japanese せっきん, Korean 접근, and Vietnamese tiếp cận are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `vietnamese` had a typo (iếp cận→tiếp cận, missing the initial "t"). Removed redundant `品詞`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄛㄆㄍㄧㄋ unique across both words and characters).
