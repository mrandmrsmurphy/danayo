## Notes
- [檀](../characters/檀.md) "sandalwood; hardwood" + [木](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [檀](../characters/檀.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 木's own stand-in is itself.

檀木 means sandalwood or hardwood tree.

Mandarin tánmù, Cantonese taan4 muk6, Japanese たんぼく, Korean 단목, and Vietnamese đàn mộc are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 檀's and 木's own stored citations exactly). No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄋㄇㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
