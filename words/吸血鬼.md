## Notes
- [吸 (char)](../characters/吸%20(char).md) "suck, inhale" + [血 (char)](../characters/血%20(char).md) "blood" + [鬼](../characters/鬼.md) "devil, demon" — literally "blood-sucking demon," the standard Mandarin/Japanese/Korean calque of the Western "vampire." 血's own primary reading is xuè; xiě is a colloquial variant sometimes heard in casual speech (e.g. 流血 liú xiě). Vietnamese has no Sino-Vietnamese calque of this compound in everyday use — the real Vietnamese word for "vampire" is the native folkloric term **ma cà rồng**, unrelated to the 吸血鬼 hanzi; a compositional "hấp huyết quỷ" would not be real usage (domain knowledge, not independently confirmed by search).
