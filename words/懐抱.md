## Notes
- [懐](../characters/懐%20(char).md) "miss, think of" + [抱](../characters/抱.md) "embrace" — no stand-in relationship applies (懐's own stand-in is itself; 抱's own is [[抱擁]]).

懐抱 means to cherish or embrace — holding a feeling, ambition, or idea close.

Mandarin huáibào, Cantonese waai4 pou5, Japanese かいほう, Korean 회포, and Vietnamese hoài bão are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⺢ㄧㄅㄚㄨ unique across both words and characters).
