## Notes
- [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" + [間](../characters/間.md) "between, among" — "the human world, this mortal realm" (as opposed to heaven/the divine), from the sense of "among people." nhân gian is standard Vietnamese for this sense (as in trần gian/nhân gian, "the mortal world"), a direct compositional match. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 닌간 diverges from Sino-Korean 인간 in the first syllable's initial consonant, since 間's own field independently diverges (despite 間 itself being `kwin: true`). No homophones (注音 ㄋㄧㄋㄍㄚㄋ unique among both words and characters).
