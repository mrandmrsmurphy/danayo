## Notes
- [規](../characters/規.md) "rule, regulation" + [模](../characters/模.md) "model, pattern"

規模 is scale, scope. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 規模 (Mandarin guīmó, Cantonese kwai1 mou4) as "scale; size; scope", Japanese きぼ as "scale, scope, extent, dimension", Korean 규모 as the same, and Vietnamese quy mô as "scale, scope".
