## Notes
- [規](../characters/規.md) "rule, regulation" + [模](../characters/模.md) "model, pattern" — the scale or scope of something, as measured against a model or standard.

規模 names the size or extent of an undertaking, organization, or phenomenon — not a physical measurement like length or weight, but the magnitude of something considered as a whole: a project's 規模 (scale), a company's 規模 (size of operations), a disaster's 規模 (scope of damage). 大規模 ("large-scale") and 小規模 ("small-scale") are the most common derived forms, and 規模擴大/規模縮小 ("to expand/contract in scale") are standard collocations in administrative, economic, and journalistic Chinese.

Mandarin guīmó, Cantonese kwai1 mou4, Japanese きぼ (kibo), Korean 규모 (gyumo), and Vietnamese quy mô are all standard, fully compositional readings and genuine everyday words for scale/scope in their respective languages — this is not a case of divergence, unlike some measurement vocabulary where the compositional reading and the living word part ways. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄨㄧㄇㄛ unique across both words and characters).
