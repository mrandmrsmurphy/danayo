## Notes
- [烈](../characters/烈.md) "fiery, violent" + [火 (char)](../characters/火%20(char).md) "fire" — no stand-in relationship applies (烈's own stand-in is [[激烈]]; 火's own is itself).

烈火 means raging inferno, blaze.

Cantonese lit6 fo2, Japanese れっか, and Korean 열화 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 烈's and 火's own stored citations exactly.
