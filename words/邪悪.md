## Notes
- [邪](../characters/邪.md) "wicked, evil" + [悪](../characters/悪%20(char).md) "bad, evil" — "wicked and evil," wicked. Their own `stand_in` values: 邪's is [[邪心]]; 悪's is itself; none points here, so 邪悪 is an independent compound.

邪悪 is wicked: evil in character or intent, with the sense of something crooked or depraved. Mandarin xié'è (邪惡) and Cantonese ce4 ok3 are the ordinary words, and Japanese じゃあく and Korean 사악 are the same compound. Compare [[凶悪]] (heinous), [[悪]] (bad, evil) and [[残酷]] (cruel).
