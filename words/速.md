## Notes
- [速 (char)](characters/速%20(char).md) "quick, fast" — 速 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

Mandarin sù, cantonese cuk1, and korean 속 are all directly attested standard readings, matching the character's own stored values exactly. **Found and fixed a real-equivalent-instead-of-own-reading bug on 速(char).md's own `vietnamese` field**: the native Vietnamese word "nhanh" ("fast," not a Sino-Vietnamese reading) had been mixed in with the genuine Sino-Vietnamese readings tốc/rốc — removed, leaving vietnamese: tốc as this word's own honest reading (as in tốc độ, "speed"). `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 속 matches Sino-Korean 속 exactly. Part of a genuine Dan'a'yo homophone with [[俗]] ("vulgar") — coincidental, unrelated in meaning; 俗's own page already carries the reciprocal callout.

**Found and fixed a missing "(stand-in for 速)" annotation** on 速(char).md's own citation. 速 (char)'s own `stand_in` is this exact word (速, itself), so 速 (char) is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry by this same word.
