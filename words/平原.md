## Notes
- [平](../characters/平.md) "flat" + [原](../characters/原.md) "plain" — "flat plain." Their own `stand_in` values: 平's is [[水平]]; 原's is [[原始]]; none points here, so 平原 is an independent compound.

平原 is a plain: a large expanse of flat land. Mandarin píngyuán, Cantonese ping4 jyun4, Japanese へいげん, Korean 평원 and Vietnamese bình nguyên all use it. It names the landform itself, unlike [[草原]] (grassland), which names the vegetation. Compare [[高原]] (plateau).
