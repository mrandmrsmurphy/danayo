## Notes
- [紡 (char)](../characters/紡%20(char).md) "spin" + [錘](../characters/錘.md) "spindle" — 錘's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 紡's own is [[紡]] itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 紡錘 legitimizes 錘 alone.

紡錘 names a spindle, the rod used in hand-spinning to twist and wind fiber into thread.

Mandarin fǎngchuí, Cantonese fong2 ceoi4, Japanese ぼうすい (bōsui), and Korean 방추 (bangchu) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 紡錘 is itself a common attested word for "spindle" in all four languages. Vietnamese phưởng chuỳ and Korean 방추 are both compositional (not independently attested via hvdic).
