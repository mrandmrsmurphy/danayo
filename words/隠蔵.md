## Notes
- [隠](../characters/隠.md) "hide" + [蔵](../characters/蔵.md) "hide, store" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes 隠 as a bound character (蔵's own stand-in is [[収蔵]]; transitivity fails, so no `#cranberry`).

隠蔵 means to hide or conceal. Mandarin 隱藏/隐藏 (yǐncáng), Cantonese 隱藏 (jan2cong4), Japanese 隠蔵 (いんぞう, also "to harbor a fugitive"; distinct from the related 秘蔵 hizō, "to keep something precious in secret"), Korean 은장 (eunjang), and Sino-Vietnamese ẩn tàng all preserve this meaning across the CJKV sphere.

**Found and fixed two real bugs**: `羅馬字`/`諺文` had drifted to 'incang/인창, inconsistent with 隠's own correctly-stored 'ǝn/은 (注音 ㄜㄋ was already correct) — corrected to 'ǝncang/은창, the same recurring bug class fixed on [[隠形]] one iteration ago. In passing, also found and fixed a typo on `characters/隠.md`'s own `vietnamese` field (ẩng → ẩn) while sourcing this word's own compositional vietnamese reading.
