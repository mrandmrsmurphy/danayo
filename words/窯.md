## Notes
- [窯](../characters/窯%20(char).md) "kiln" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [窯](../characters/窯%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

窯 means kiln.

Mandarin yáo, Cantonese jiu4, Japanese よう, Korean 요, and Vietnamese dao are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `羅馬字` was missing the leading glottal marker (you→'you). Homophone of [[揺]] "swing; shake; rock" (both 注音 ⼄ㄨ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words.
