## Notes
- [厳](../characters/厳%20(char).md) "strict, rigorous, rigid, stern" + [重](../characters/重%20(char).md) "heavy" — no stand-in relationship applies (both characters are independent Dan'a'yo entries, each its own stand-in).

厳重 means serious, strict, or rigorous — treating something with heightened severity or care.

Japanese げんじゅう, Korean 엄중, and Vietnamese nghiêm trọng are all standard, fully compositional readings. `cantonese: jim4 zung6` uses zung6 (重's "heavy/serious" sense) rather than its citation reading cung5 (likely the "repeat" sense) — a legitimate sense-dependent tone split, matching the pattern seen on other multi-reading characters (占, 卓). Fixed `pos` (was mislabeled "修飾語," not a recognized part-of-speech tag) → 性詞. No homophones (注音 ⼄ㄇㄑㄛㄫ unique across both words and characters).
