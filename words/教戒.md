## Notes
- [教](../characters/教.md) "teach" + [戒](../characters/戒.md) "warn, caution, admonish" — to instruct and admonish.

誨 (huì), "to instruct, to teach, to admonish," appears 5 times in the 《論語》, most famously in **述而**'s "誨人不倦" ("tireless in teaching others") and in **為政**'s discussion of true knowledge, where Confucius instructs (誨) Zi Lu on the difference between knowing and not knowing. 誨 lies outside Dan'a'yo's closed character set. The vault's 戒 carries the alias 誡 (a variant of 戒 meaning "to admonish, to warn"), which sits close in sense to 誨 even though 誨 itself is not an alias of 戒, reflecting how closely the senses of "to admonish/instruct" and "to warn/caution" overlap across these characters. The vault substitutes 教戒, joining 教 ("to teach") with 戒 ("to admonish, to caution") — a pure "Translation" substitution that captures both the instructive and admonitory senses of 誨, per [[translation/論語 (book)]]'s Vocabulary list.

The mandarin/cantonese/japanese/korean readings of 教戒 are compositional, built directly from 教 and 戒's own readings (jiào+jiè, gaau3+gaai3, KYOU+KAI, 교+계).

The 論語 translation page's vocabulary list records the substitution directly: "誨 5x --> 教戒 - Translation." The word is therefore a Dan'a'yo substitution word, not necessarily an established compound, and the readings recorded for it are compositional. The Conflict page lists it as "to instruct, admonish, teach and warn," and Dan'a'yo keeps both senses, instruction and admonition, together as one word. The nearby word [教唆](教唆.md) "to instigate, to incite" also begins with 教, but means to urge someone toward wrongdoing, which is a different sense.
