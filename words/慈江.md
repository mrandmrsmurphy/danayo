## Notes
- [慈](../characters/慈.md) "kind" + [江](../characters/江.md) "river" — the name of the Chagang region of Korea, in the north of North Korea.

慈江 (Chagang) is a province of North Korea (慈江道), in the mountainous north on the Chinese border; it was created in 1949 out of part of North Pyongan Province (from memory, not re-checked), and its capital is [[江界]] (from memory, not re-checked). The name is taken from the Chasong (慈城) and Kanggye (江界) areas, in the manner of the other provincial names (from memory, not re-checked).

No Wiktionary (English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean or Vietnamese) has an entry for 慈江 as of this check, so every reading here is composed from the character pages: Mandarin Cíjiāng, Cantonese ci4 gong1, Japanese じこう, Korean 자강 (North Korean) and Vietnamese Từ Giang. This is an unattested but regular compound.
