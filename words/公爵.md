## Notes
- [公 (char)](../characters/公%20(char).md) "public, sir" + [爵](../characters/爵.md) "noble title" — "duke," the highest rank of the traditional East Asian five-rank peerage system (公侯伯子男). `vietnamese: công tước` is the standard, widely-used Vietnamese term for this nobility rank, a direct compositional match. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 공작 matches Sino-Korean 공작 exactly.
