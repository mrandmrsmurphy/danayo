## Notes
- [把](../characters/把.md) "grip, handle" + [手](../characters/手%20(char).md) "hand" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [把](../characters/把.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (手's own stand-in is itself).

把手 means grip, handle, or knob.

Mandarin bǎshǒu, Cantonese baa2 sau2, Japanese はしゅ, Korean 파수, and Vietnamese bả thủ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Removed the non-compositional Japanese synonym とりて (garbled form of 取っ手/とって "handle," a different compound entirely) from the field. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄙ⼜ unique across both words and characters).
