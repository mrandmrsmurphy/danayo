## Notes
- [昨](../characters/昨%20(char).md) "last, previous" + [月](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 昨 and 月's own stand-ins are themselves).

昨月 means last month.

Mandarin zuóyuè, Cantonese zok6 jyut6, Japanese さくげつ, Korean 작월, and Vietnamese tạc nguyệt are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bugs: `mandarin` was the unrelated compound 上個月 (shàng gè yuè, a different character composition entirely) and `korean` was the native 지난달 — both corrected to compositional forms; removed mismatched-character `上個月` from `aliases`, filled blank `cantonese`/`vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄎ⼔ㄊ unique across both words and characters).
