## Notes
- [屎 (char)](../characters/屎%20(char).md) "excrement; excretion" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [屎 (char)](../characters/屎%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

屎 names feces/excrement, a 会意-兼-形声 character depicting droppings beneath a kneeling figure (尸), the body component also carrying the sound.

Japanese し (on'yomi; the native くそ, kuso, is the everyday word instead), Korean 히, and Vietnamese thỉ (the character's own field also lists Nôm variants hi/hy) are all standard readings. No homophones among other words (注音 ㄏㄜ unique).

**Correction (2026-08-27)**: 屎's own character page had `mandarin: xī`/`cantonese: hei1` — 屎 genuinely has two Mandarin readings (shǐ "excrement," the character's own documented sense, and a separate, rarer xī meaning "a groaning/sighing sound"), but the character page had picked the wrong one for its own gloss. Corrected to shǐ/si2, matching this word's own already-correct fields and the earlier syllable-lint fix already applied to `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` (ㄏㄜ, not the character-vs-word 注音 mismatch documented in [[project_perfection_era_methodology]]).
