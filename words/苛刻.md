## Notes
- [苛](../characters/苛.md) "harsh, exacting" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 苛 cannot appear independently outside this compound. [刻](../characters/刻.md) "carve, engrave" is separately legitimized by [[刻印]]. No cranberry: transitivity fails since 刻's own `stand_in` doesn't point here.

苛刻 combines 苛 ("harsh") with 刻 ("carve" — here in an extended "severe, exacting" sense) — "harsh, demanding."

Mandarin kēkè, cantonese ho1hak1 (space removed), japanese かこく (kakoku), and korean 가각 (gagak) were all already correct, fully compositional readings.
