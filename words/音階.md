## Notes
- [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" + [階](../characters/階.md) "step; rank"

音階 is a musical scale: the ladder of notes that a tune is built on. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yīnjiē as "scale" and Japanese おんかい as "musical scale". Korean 음계 and Vietnamese âm giai are the same word. Cantonese jam1 gaai1 matches.

The notes on it are [音符](音符.md), and the gap between two of them is an [音程](音程.md), interval.
