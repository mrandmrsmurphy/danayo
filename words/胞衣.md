## Notes
- [胞](../characters/胞.md) "placenta" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 胞 cannot appear independently outside this compound. [衣](../characters/衣.md) "clothes" is separately legitimized by [[衣類]].

胞衣 combines 胞 ("placenta, that which wraps the fetus") with 衣 ("clothing") — "afterbirth," figuratively the fetus's "wrapping garment."

Pronunciation fields (byau'iǝ/뱟의/ㄅ⼘ㄨㄧㄜ) are the straightforward concatenation of each constituent's own stored reading — no bug. `kwin` is false per the AND-rule: 胞 individually carries `kwin: false` (despite 衣's own `kwin: true`). Mandarin bāoyī, cantonese baau1ji1 (space removed), and korean 포의 (poui) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese えな (ena) is a genuine irregular/special reading (熟字訓-type), not decomposable from 胞's HOU or 衣's I/E — a well-documented traditional term for "afterbirth." Vietnamese bào y is the classical Sino-Vietnamese medical term, using each character's own literary reading (bào for 胞, y for 衣).
