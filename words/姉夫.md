## Notes
- [姉 (char)](../characters/姉%20(char).md) "older sister" + [夫 (char)](../characters/夫%20(char).md) "husband" — elder sister's husband.

姉夫 is the husband of one's older sister. It is "older sister-husband." Mandarin jiěfu (written 姐夫 or 姊夫, its aliases) and Cantonese ze2 fu1 are confirmed on Wiktionary, Chinese only; Japanese and Korean use other words (Japanese 義兄), so those fields are left blank. It is one of the in-law terms of ego's own generation, with [[妹婿]] (younger sister's husband), [[妻兄]], [[妻弟]], [[夫兄]] and [[夫弟]]; the hypernym for an older affinal brother is [[姻兄]]. Men who married sisters of each other are [[連襟]]. It is a schema word in the family of in-law terms built from the marriage relation and the sibling roots, coined for predictability rather than a claim about ancient usage.
