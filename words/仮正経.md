## Notes
- [仮](../characters/仮.md) "false, pretend" + [正](../characters/正%20(char).md) "correct, upright" + [経](../characters/経%20(char).md) "serious, proper" — "pretend-proper," a prude. Their own `stand_in` values: 仮's is [[仮借]]; 正's is itself; 経's is itself; none points here, so 仮正経 is an independent compound.

仮正経 is a prude: someone who makes a show of being proper and serious, and is shocked at anything improper. Mandarin jiǎzhèngjīng (假正經) and Cantonese gaa2 zing3 ging1 are the ordinary words. Japanese says 堅物 and Korean 새침데기, so those two fields hold the character readings. The character 仮 has the alias 假, so the Chinese spelling is accepted. It is the "prude" word, where [[禁欲]] is abstinence and [[貞潔]] is chastity. Compare [[偽善]] (hypocrisy).
