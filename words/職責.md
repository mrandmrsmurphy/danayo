## Notes
- [職](../characters/職.md) "post" + [責](../characters/責.md) "blame"

職責 is duty, responsibility of one's post. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 職責 (Mandarin zhízé, Cantonese zik1 zaak3) as "duty; responsibility" and Japanese しょくせき as "duty, responsibility (at work)". It lists a Korean entry, 직책. Compare [義務](義務.md), duty, and [責任](責任.md), responsibility.
