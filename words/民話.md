## Notes
- [民](../characters/民.md) "people" + [話 (char)](../characters/話%20(char).md) "tale"

民話 is a folktale: a tale handed down among the common people. Wiktionary glosses Japanese みんわ as "folktale, fairy tale". Korean 민화 is the same word. The Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the characters.

It differs from the [神話](神話.md), myth, in being about ordinary people, and from the [伝説](伝説.md), legend, in making no claim to be history.
