## Notes
- [就 (char)](../characters/就%20(char).md) "about, let"

就 is one of Mandarin's most frequent function words — an adverb meaning "then; at once; already; as early as," marking the immediacy or earliness of an action (一…就…, "as soon as…, then…"), among many other grammaticalized uses. Cross-linguistically the family diverges sharply: Mandarin 就 (jiù) is fully grammaticalized and central to everyday syntax, while Japanese しゅう, Korean 취, and Vietnamese tựu all survive today almost exclusively bound inside 成就 (じょうじゅ/성취/thành tựu, "achievement, accomplishment") rather than as an independent particle — the original "to approach, go toward, accomplish" sense (from which Mandarin's grammaticalized "then, at once" derives) is what those three preserve, not the adverb.
