## Notes
- [鳶](characters/鳶%20(char).md) "kite" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 鳶 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

鳶 means kite, either the bird of prey (黒鳶, "black kite") or the flying toy (which in Vietnamese, diều, is actually the more common everyday word).

Mandarin yuān, Cantonese jyun1, Japanese えん, Korean 연, and Vietnamese diên (the Sino-Vietnamese reading among 鳶's citation list, alongside the everyday native-feeling diều) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 鳶's own stored citations exactly. Fixed a real bug: `羅馬字` had been typo'd 'yeng (with a spurious final -g) on both this word's page and 鳶's own character page — corrected to 'yen, matching 諺文 연 and consistent with the real three-way Dan'a'yo homophone this reveals. Removed the empty `品詞` field, filled the blank `pos`/`vietnamese`, and added the missing `hsk_level`(無, matching 鳶's own "no HSK level" citation).

**Real homophone found**: `注音 ⼶ㄋ`/`연`/`'yen` is also carried by [[演]] ("unfold, perform") and [[鉛]] ("lead") — a genuine three-way group, only fully discoverable after fixing 演's own missing `注音` field and this word's romanization typo.
