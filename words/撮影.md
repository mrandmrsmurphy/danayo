## Notes
- [撮 (char)](../characters/撮%20(char).md) "photograph" + [影](../characters/影.md) "shadow; image"

撮影 is to film or photograph: to take pictures with a camera. Wiktionary glosses Japanese さつえい as "filming, photographing". Korean 촬영 is the same word. The Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the characters.

It makes a [映画](映画.md), film, or a [写真](写真.md), photograph, and the pictures taken are the [画像](画像.md), image.
