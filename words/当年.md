## Notes
- [当](../characters/当%20(char).md) "while" + [年](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year" — no stand-in relationship applies (当/年 are both their own stand-ins).

当年 means that year, or at the time — referring back to a specific past year.

Mandarin dāngnián, Cantonese dong1 nin4, Japanese とうねん, Korean 당년, and Vietnamese đương niên are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄫㄋㄝㄋ unique across both words and characters).
