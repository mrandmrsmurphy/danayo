## Notes
- [成 (char)](../characters/成%20(char).md) "become; complete" + [長 (char)](../characters/長%20(char).md) "grow; long"

成長 is to grow, and growth: to develop toward maturity. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin chéngzhǎng as "to grow up; to grow to maturity" and Japanese せいちょう as "growth; maturation; to grow". Cantonese sing4 zoeng2 and Korean 성장 match.

It grows a living thing, a child, a plant or a business, from small to full. It is distinct from [増加](増加.md), which is the increase of an amount, and its opposite in a living thing is [衰退](衰退.md), decline.
