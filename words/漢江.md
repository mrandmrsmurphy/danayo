## Notes
- [漢](characters/漢.md) "Han Chinese" + [江](characters/江.md) "river" — no stand-in relationship applies (漢's own stand-in is [[漢族]]; 江's own is [[大江]]).
1. The Han river through Seoul, South Korea
2. The Han river in Hubei, China (also called 漢水, listed here as an alias, using 水 instead of 江)

Mandarin Hànjiāng, Cantonese hon3 gong1, Japanese かんこう, Korean 한강, and Vietnamese Sông Hán are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 漢's and 江's own stored citations exactly). Fixed doubled/malformed `mandarin`(Hànshuǐ, Hànjiāng → Hànjiāng, the Hànshuǐ reading belonging to the alias 漢水 instead)/`cantonese`(hon3 seoi2, hon3 gong1 → hon3 gong1)/`japanese`(かんすい, かんかう → かんこう) fields. `kwin: true` because 諺文 한강 matches Korean 한강 exactly.

**Real homophone with [[韓江]]** (a different river, in Guangdong, China) — reformatted the pre-existing informal tip note into the canonical `Homophones` callout on both pages.
