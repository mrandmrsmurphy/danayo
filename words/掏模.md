## Notes
- [掏](../characters/掏.md) "pickpocket" + [模](../characters/模.md) "feel; grope" (via its alias 摸) — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [掏](../characters/掏.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.
- Note: Japanese reading すり (suri) is a special fused reading, not a concatenation of component readings.

掏模 means to pickpocket, or to reach in and take.

Mandarin tāomō, Cantonese tou4 mo2, and Korean 도모 are all standard, fully compositional readings.
