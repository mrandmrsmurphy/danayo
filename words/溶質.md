## Notes
- [溶](../characters/溶.md) "melt, dissolve" + [質](../characters/質.md) "essence, nature" — solute.

溶質 is a solute: the substance that dissolves in a solvent ([[溶媒]]) to form a solution ([[溶液]]), such as salt ([[塩]]) in water. 質 "essence, substance" names what is dissolved. Mandarin róngzhì, Cantonese jung4 zat1, Japanese ようしつ and Korean 용질 are the standard readings.
