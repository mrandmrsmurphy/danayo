## Notes
- [蚕](../characters/蚕%20(char).md) "silkworm" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [蚕](../characters/蚕%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

蚕 means silkworm.

Mandarin cán, Cantonese caam4, Japanese さん, and Vietnamese tàm are all standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 천 diverges from the compositional 잠 — a genuine Sino-Korean divergence (kwin: false), not a bug. Fixed real bug: literal `vietnamese: null`. Homophone of [[斬]] "cut; chop; sever" (both 注音 ㄐㄚㄇ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words.
