## Notes
- [洗 (char)](../characters/洗%20(char).md) "to wash; to cleanse" + [礼 (char)](../characters/礼%20(char).md) "rite; ceremony; propriety" — the washing rite; the sacrament of initiation by water into the Christian community

Wiktionary: Japanese 洗礼 (せんれい) "baptism" and, figuratively, "initiation"; Chinese 洗禮, religion, "baptism" and "to baptize", and figuratively "ordeal; severe test"; Korean 세례 is the hanja form of 洗禮, "baptism". 洗禮 is kept as an alias. In the [[基督敎]] lexipedia it is listed alongside [[聖餐]] under [[聖礼]].
