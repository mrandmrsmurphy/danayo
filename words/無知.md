## Notes
- [無 (char)](../characters/無%20(char).md) "lack" + [知 (char)](../characters/知%20(char).md) "know"

無知 is ignorance: a lack of knowledge, and the state of being ignorant. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin wúzhī as "to be ignorant" and Japanese むち as "ignorant; ignorance" (also spelled 無智). Korean 무지 and Cantonese mou4 zi1 match. The word is literally "without knowing".

It is the opposite of [知](知.md), knowing, and of [知識](知識.md), knowledge. It differs from [無学](無学.md), which is a lack of schooling, and from [愚](愚.md), foolishness.
