## Notes
- [微](../characters/微.md) "minute, tiny" + [分 (char)](../characters/分%20(char).md) "divide, portion" — literally "minute portions," differentiation: breaking a function down into infinitesimally small changes to find its rate of change, the inverse operation of [[積分]].

Mandarin wēifēn, Cantonese mei4 fan1, Japanese びぶん, Korean 미분, and Vietnamese vi phân are all standard, fully compositional, and genuinely living calculus terms across the region — no register split with a competing native form.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 뮈분 diverges from Sino-Korean 미분 in the first syllable, since 微 independently carries a diverging reading on its own character page. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄨㄧㄅㄨㄋ unique among both words and characters).
