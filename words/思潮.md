## Notes
- [思](../characters/思.md) "thought; to think" + [潮](../characters/潮.md) "tide; trend"

思潮 is "an intellectual trend; a current of thought," a way of thinking particular to a period. According to Wiktionary, Chinese 思潮 (sīcháo, Cantonese si1 ciu4) is "ideological trend" and "way of thinking particular to a period in history," Japanese しちょう is "trend of thought," and Korean 사조 is attested as well. Wiktionary has no Vietnamese section, so `vietnamese` tư triều is a compositional reading (from memory the everyday form is tư trào, not re-checked). It is used in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [China](../lookup/HSK/China.md), in 人 主体性 之 思潮 "intellectual currents that raise up human subjectivity." Related: [思想](思想.md) "thought" and [主体](主体.md) "subject."
