## Notes
- [蘇](../characters/蘇.md) "revive" + [州](../characters/州%20(char).md) "state" — "Su prefecture". No stand-in relationship applies (蘇's is [[蘇生]]; 州 is its own stand-in).

蘇州 (Suzhou) is a prefecture-level city in Jiangsu province in eastern China, near Shanghai (from memory, not re-checked). Per Wiktionary the name derives from Mount Gusu (姑蘇山); it also names a historical imperial prefecture around the city. 苏 is an alias of 蘇, so 苏州 is kept as the word's alias.

Japanese そしゅう, Korean 소주 and Vietnamese Tô Châu are the Sino-Xenic readings (Wiktionary Japanese そしゅう, Korean 소주, Vietnamese Tô Châu); the Korean field holds the North Korean reading derived from the character pages, 소주, and the usual South Korean name today is 쑤저우 (from memory, not re-checked). Wiktionary lists no ordinary sense for the bare word; compounds such as 蘇杭 (Suzhou and Hangzhou together) and 蘇州碼子 (Suzhou numerals) are not claimed here. Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese entries. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄛㄐㄨㄛ unique across both words and characters).
