## Notes
- [避](../characters/避%20(char).md) "avoid, take refuge" + [難](../characters/難.md) "hardship, disaster" + [所](../characters/所%20(char).md) "place, office" — "disaster-refuge place," a shelter. Their own `stand_in` values: 避's is itself; 難's is [[困難]]; 所's is itself; none points here, so 避難所 is an independent compound.

避難所 is a shelter: a place to take refuge from a disaster or danger. Mandarin bìnànsuǒ, Cantonese bei6 naan6 so2 and Japanese ひなんじょ all use it. Korean says 피난처 (避難處), swapping the last character, so that field holds the compositional reading. Compare [[所]] (place).
