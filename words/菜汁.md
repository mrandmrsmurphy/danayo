## Notes
- [菜](../characters/菜.md) "vegetable" + [汁](../characters/汁%20(char).md) "juice" — no stand-in relationship applies (菜's own stand-in is [[野菜]]; 汁's own is itself).

菜汁 means vegetable juice.

Mandarin càizhī and Cantonese coi3 zap1 are standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed a significant real bug: `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` had all been contaminated with 集's reading (채집/caijib/ㄑㄚㄧㄐㄧㄆ) instead of 汁's own (즙/jǝb/ㄐㄜㄆ) — corrected to 채즙/caijǝb/ㄑㄚㄧㄐㄜㄆ. This also invalidates the false homophone claim this page carried against [[採集]] (which only coincided because of the contamination) — removed; [[採集]]'s own page has been corrected accordingly and no longer claims the collision either. Japanese 野菜ジュース and Korean 야채 주스 are native/loanword compounds (not built from 汁's own on-reading), documented as the standard, expected real-world terms. Filled blank `vietnamese: thái chấp` (compositional). No genuine homophones at the corrected reading.
