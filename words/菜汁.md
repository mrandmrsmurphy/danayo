## Notes
- [菜](../characters/菜.md) "vegetable" + [汁](../characters/汁%20(char).md) "juice" — no stand-in relationship applies (菜's own stand-in is [[野菜]]; 汁's own is itself).

菜汁 means vegetable juice.

Mandarin càizhī and Cantonese coi3 zap1 are standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese 野菜ジュース and Korean 야채 주스 are native/loanword compounds (not built from 汁's own on-reading), documented as the standard, expected real-world terms.
