## Notes
- [修](../characters/修.md) "cultivate" + [女](../characters/女%20(char).md) "woman" — "cultivating woman," a nun. Their own `stand_in` values: 修's is [[修理]]; 女's is itself; none points here, so 修女 is an independent compound.

修女 is a nun: a woman who lives under religious vows in a convent. Mandarin xiūnǚ and Cantonese sau1 neoi5 are the ordinary words, and Japanese しゅうじょ and Korean 수녀 are the same compound. It is the Christian nun, where [[尼僧]] is the nun in general. Compare [[修士]] (monk) and [[修道院]] (monastery).
