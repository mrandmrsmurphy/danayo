## Notes
- [可 (char)](../characters/可%20(char).md) "can" + [能](../characters/能.md) "talent" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (可's is bare 可, 能's is 技能).

Distinct from the semantically related [[可以]]: 可能 marks epistemic possibility ("possible; maybe"), while 可以 marks permission/ability ("can, may do X"). Mandarin kěnéng, Cantonese ho2 nang4, Japanese かのう, Korean 가능, and Vietnamese khả năng are the standard readings.
