## Notes
- [修](../characters/修.md) "cultivate, practice" + [行](../characters/行%20(char).md) "go, conduct" — "cultivate conduct," spiritual discipline. Their own `stand_in` values: 修's is [[修理]]; 行's is itself; none points here, so 修行 is an independent compound.

修行 is spiritual discipline: the practice of cultivating one's virtue or religious attainment through training and asceticism. Mandarin xiūxíng, Cantonese sau1 hang4, Japanese しゅぎょう and Korean 수행 all use it, and Vietnamese tu hành does too. It is the religious "discipline" of the ascetic, not the rule or punishment senses of 規律 and 処分. Compare [[僧侶]] (Buddhist monk) and [[隠者]] (hermit).
