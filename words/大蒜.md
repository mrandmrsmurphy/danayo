## Notes
- [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big" + [蒜](../characters/蒜.md) "garlic"

大蒜 is garlic: the pungent bulb of *Allium sativum*, used whole, sliced or crushed as a seasoning and a vegetable. Wiktionary glosses Chinese 大蒜 (Mandarin dàsuàn, Cantonese daai6 syun3) as "garlic" and "head of garlic; bulb of garlic", Japanese にんにく as "garlic", and Korean 대산 as "garlic" under its hanja form. Japanese にんにく and Vietnamese tỏi are the native everyday words.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [蒜](../characters/蒜.md), whose reading *swam* is a new syllable. It is a seasoning beside the [姜](姜.md), ginger, and [調味料](調味料.md), and its leaves and stem are eaten as a vegetable, the [菜蔬](菜蔬.md) of a meal.
