## Notes
- [彷](../characters/彷.md) "resemble" (here bound, contributing its sound to this disyllabic word rather than its own meaning) + [徨](../characters/徨.md) "wander" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [徨](../characters/徨.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (彷's own stand-in is [[彷彿]]). To wander about aimlessly, hesitating over which way to go.

Mandarin pánghuáng, Cantonese pong4 wong4, Japanese ほうこう, Korean 방황, and Vietnamese bàng hoàng are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese`, omitted empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄚㄫㄏ⺢ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
