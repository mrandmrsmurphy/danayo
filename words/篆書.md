## Notes
- [篆](../characters/篆.md) "seal script" + [書](../characters/書.md) "book, writing"

篆書 is seal script, a style of writing Chinese characters. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 篆書 (alias 篆书; Mandarin zhuànshū, Cantonese syun6 syu1) as "seal script", all the forms of ancient Chinese calligraphy used in Han-era seals and chops. Japanese てんしょ, Korean 전서 and Vietnamese triện thư are glossed the same.

English Wikipedia says seal script evolved from bronze script during the Zhou dynasty, and that the Qin form was standardized and adopted across China under the Qin dynasty. Without qualification it means the small seal script, and it stayed in use for seals and decorative engraving. The later styles include the [隷書](隷書.md) and the [楷書](楷書.md).

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [篆](../characters/篆.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
