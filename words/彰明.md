## Notes
- [彰](../characters/彰.md) "clear, manifest, obvious" + [明](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [彰](../characters/彰.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (明's own stand-in is itself).

彰明 means clear, manifest, or obvious — plainly evident, as in 彰明較著 ("glaringly obvious").

Mandarin zhāngmíng, Cantonese zoeng1 ming4, Korean 창명, and Vietnamese chương minh are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `japanese` had archaic, incomplete kana しやう (missing 明 entirely) — corrected to しょうめい. Filled blank `korean`/`vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄚㄫㄇ⼶ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
