## Notes
- [同](../characters/同.md) "same" + [一](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [同](../characters/同.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (一's own stand-in is [[一]] itself, an unrelated word).

同一 means the same or identical — being one and the same thing.
