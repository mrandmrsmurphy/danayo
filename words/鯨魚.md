## Notes
- [鯨](../characters/鯨.md) "whale" + [魚](../characters/魚%20(char).md) "fish" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [鯨](../characters/鯨.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (魚's own stand-in is itself).

鯨魚 means whale.

Mandarin jīngyú, Cantonese king4 jyu4-2, and Vietnamese kình ngư (compositional) are standard. Japanese くじら is the native word for whale (rather than a Sino-Japanese compositional reading) — the standard, everyday term. Simplified `korean` from comma-joined "경어,고래" to compositional 경어, moving native 고래 to prose. Homophone of [[敬語]] "honorific language; polite speech register" (both gyeng'yo/경요/ㄍ⼶ㄫ⼄) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated compounds.
