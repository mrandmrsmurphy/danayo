## Notes
- [元](../characters/元.md) "foundation" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — "New Year's Day," the first day of the year. Mandarin yuánrì, Cantonese jyun4 jat6, and Japanese がんじつ are all directly attested standard readings. `korean: 원일` and `vietnamese: nguyên nhật` are both honest compositional readings (元's own 원/nguyên + 日's own 일/nhật); the more common everyday term in both languages is 元旦/nguyên đán ("New Year's Day," using 旦 "dawn" instead of 日).
