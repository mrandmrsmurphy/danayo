## Notes
- [仲](../characters/仲.md) "in-between, intermediary" + [介](../characters/介.md) "to intervene, mediate" — "mediator, broker, intermediary" (as in real estate/brokerage agencies). This word is the `stand_in` legitimizer for both [[仲]] and [[介]] (transitivity holds: A=B=AB), hence the `#cranberry` tag. `vietnamese: trọng giới` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese for "broker, middleman" (confirmed via web search), part of the same family as trọng mãi ("buying/selling broker"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 둥겨 diverges entirely from Sino-Korean 중개. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄨㄫㄍ⼶ unique among both words and characters).
