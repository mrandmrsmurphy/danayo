## Notes
- [納 (char)](../characters/納%20(char).md) "receive; store" + [豆 (char)](../characters/豆%20(char).md) "bean"

納豆 is natto: soybeans fermented into a sticky, strong-smelling food. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin nàdòu as "natto" and Japanese なっとう as "natto (a traditional Japanese food product made from fermented soybeans)". Cantonese naap6 dau6-2 matches, and Korean 납두 is the reading of the characters.

It is a ferment of the [大豆](大豆.md), soybean, like [味噌](味噌.md), miso, and it is eaten with rice, the [米飯](米飯.md). Brewing in general is [醸造](醸造.md).
