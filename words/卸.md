## Notes
- [卸 (char)](../characters/卸%20(char).md) "retire; lay down" — stand-in for [[卸 (char)|卸]], which cannot appear independently

卸 means "to unload, take off, remove" — literally unburdening (卸貨, "to unload cargo"; 卸妝, "to remove makeup") and figuratively "to shed a responsibility, retire from a post" (卸任, "to leave office, step down"; 卸責, "to shift blame, shed responsibility").

Mandarin xiè, Cantonese se3, and Japanese しゃ (sha, on'yomi, largely confined to formal/literary contexts) alongside native おろ(す) (oro(su), "to unload, take down," the everyday verb) all preserve the sense. Korean 사 (sa) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 풀다 (pulda, "to undo, loosen"). Vietnamese tá is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in tá trang (卸妝, "to remove makeup") and tá hóa (卸貨, "to unload cargo"); the character page's other three stored candidates (dỡ, a related native word for "unload"; hằm; xả, "to release, discharge") are unrelated to the reading itself and were not used here.

This word shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading (sya/샤/ㄙ⼘) with [[捨]] ("throw away") — a coincidental convergence, unrelated in meaning; 捨 reciprocates the callout and both pages are now perfected.
