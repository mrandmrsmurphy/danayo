## Notes
- [公 (char)](../characters/公%20(char).md) "public" + [益](../characters/益.md) "benefit"

公益 is the public interest: the good of the community as a whole, as opposed to private gain. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin gōngyì as "the public good; the common good; the public interest" and Japanese こうえき as "public welfare, public benefit". Vietnamese công ích is the same word, and Korean 공익 and Cantonese gung1 jik1 match.

It is what a charity or a government claims to serve, and it is linked with [公共](公共.md), public, and [利益](利益.md), benefit. A charitable act of the kind is [慈善](慈善.md).
