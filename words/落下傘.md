## Notes
- [落](../characters/落.md) "fall" is separately legitimized by [[落下]]. [下 (char)](../characters/下%20(char).md) "down, below" is separately legitimized by [[下]] itself. [傘](../characters/傘.md) "umbrella" is separately legitimized by [[雨傘]]. No cranberry.

Pronunciation fields (lakhasan/락하산/ㄌㄚㄎㄏㄚㄙㄚㄋ) are the straightforward concatenation of each constituent's own stored reading — no bug. **Found and fixed a real `kwin` bug** (false→true, AND-rule: all three individually true). Mandarin jiàngluòsǎn and cantonese gong3lok6saan3 legitimately track the real standard Chinese term 降落伞 (a different character order/word than this compound, since 落下傘 itself is not the standard Mandarin term for "parachute" — it is the standard Japanese one). **Found and fixed two real bugs**: `japanese` had らくかさん, missing the well-established euphonic gemination (促音便) that always applies to 落下's own reading (らっか, not らくか, e.g. 自由落下 jiyuu rakka) — corrected to らっかさん. `korean` had 낙하산, a 두음법칙-shifted (South Korean) form — corrected to 락하산, matching the vault's standing rule that `korean` always uses the unshifted North Korean/문화어 pronunciation (matching 落's own stored 락). Vietnamese cái dù is a plausible real term for the concept (not compositional from the three characters' own fields) — kept as-is. Fixed cantonese's stray spaces. No homophones.
