## Notes
- [落](../characters/落.md) "fall" is separately legitimized by [[落下]]. [下 (char)](../characters/下%20(char).md) "down, below" is separately legitimized by [[下]] itself. [傘](../characters/傘.md) "umbrella" is separately legitimized by [[雨傘]]. No cranberry.

Mandarin jiàngluòsǎn and cantonese gong3lok6saan3 legitimately track the real standard Chinese term 降落伞 (a different character order/word than this compound, since 落下傘 itself is not the standard Mandarin term for "parachute" — it is the standard Japanese one). Vietnamese cái dù is a plausible real term for the concept (not compositional from the three characters' own fields) — kept as-is.
