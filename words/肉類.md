## Notes
- [肉 (char)](../characters/肉%20(char).md) "meat" + [類](../characters/類.md) "kind"

肉類 is meats: meat as a category, the kinds of meat taken together. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin ròulèi as "meats; meat dishes" and Japanese にくるい as "meats, sorts of meats". Cantonese juk6 leoi6 matches, and Korean 륙류 is the reading of the characters in its North Korean form.

It is the class that [牛肉](牛肉.md), [羊肉](羊肉.md), [豚肉](豚肉.md) and [鶏肉](鶏肉.md) belong to, and is set against [魚肉](魚肉.md), fish. A prepared meat dish is the [肴](肴.md).
