## Notes
- [烏](../characters/烏.md) "crow, raven" + [龍 (char)](../characters/龍%20(char).md) "dragon" — no stand-in relationship applies (烏's own stand-in is [[烏鳥]]; 龍's own is itself).

烏龍 means "black dragon"; by extension, oolong tea.

Mandarin wūlóng is fully compositional, matching 烏's and 龍's own stored citations. Fixed a malformed hybrid tone notation in `cantonese` (wu1 lung4-2 → wu1 lung2, the real colloquial change-tone pronunciation). Japanese ウーロン is the real, standard loanword for oolong tea (rather than a compositional on'yomi form). Fixed a real bug: `korean` had been 우룡 instead of 오룡, matching 烏's own citation (오). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with ô long (烏's own ô + 龍's own long) — also the real, standard Vietnamese term for oolong tea (trà ô long). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄛㄌ⼄ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
