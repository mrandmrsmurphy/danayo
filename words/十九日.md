## Notes
- [十](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" + [九](../characters/九%20(char).md) "nine" + [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — no stand-in relationship applies (all three characters are independent Dan'a'yo entries, each its own stand-in).
