## Notes
- [植](../characters/植.md) "to plant, cultivate" + [民](../characters/民.md) "people" + [地 (char)](../characters/地%20(char).md) "land" — literally "a planted-people land." No stand-in relationship applies (植's own `stand_in` is [植物](植物.md); 民's is [人民](人民.md); 地's is itself).

植民地 means a colony. Mandarin zhímíndì, Cantonese zik6 man4 dei6, Japanese しょくみんち, and Korean 식민지 are all standard, fully compositional readings — 植 rather than the near-synonymous 殖 ("to breed, multiply") is the standard modern spelling in Japanese and this vault's chosen form, with 殖民地 kept as an alias for the equally-attested variant spelling. Vietnamese thực dân địa is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form; living Vietnamese more often says thuộc địa instead (built on a different root, 屬/thuộc "to belong," not 植/thực), noted here rather than silently substituted.
