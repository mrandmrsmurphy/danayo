## Notes
- [歯](../characters/歯%20(char).md) "teeth, gear" + [齦](../characters/齦.md) "gums, gingiva" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [齦](../characters/齦.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

歯齦 means gingiva or gums.

Mandarin chǐyín, Cantonese ci2 ngan4, and Japanese しぎん are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `korean` was 잇몸, the entirely native Korean word for gums (matching 齦's own `korean_native` field, not its `korean` field), not the compositional Sino-Korean form — corrected to 치간. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field (xỉ ngân, using 歯's real-world standard reading xỉ, per the gap flagged on [[歯]]). Removed `齒仁` from `aliases` — this uses a different second character (仁 "benevolence," likely a corpus/OCR error for 齦), not a genuine variant, kept the genuine traditional/simplified variants `齒齦`/`齿龈`. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄎㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
