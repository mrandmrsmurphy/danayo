## Notes
- [番 (char)](../characters/番%20(char).md) "turn, shift" + [次](../characters/次.md) "order, next" — order of duty.

番次 is the order of turns: the sequence in which people take their shifts, or which shift one is on ("which shift are you on?"). It is "shift-order" from [[番]] and [次](../characters/次.md) "order". No entry was found on English Wiktionary, and the proposed forms (Mandarin fāncì, Japanese banchi, Korean beoncha) are unverified, so the language fields are left blank. It is a Dan'a'yo schema word, coined to complete the family on the same pattern as other order words. Compare [[輪番]], the rotation as a system, and [[当番]]. Like the other shift words, it belongs to the 番 family, a systematic schema that Dan'a'yo imposes for predictability rather than a claim about the ancient usage.
