## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" + [閉](../characters/閉%20(char).md) "shut"

自閉 means to shut oneself off from others. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 自閉 (Mandarin zìbì, Cantonese zi6 bai3) as "to exclude oneself from others; to isolate oneself from society" and "autistic", and Japanese じへい as "social withdrawal". Korean 자폐 is built from the readings of the two characters. The clinical compound is [自閉症](自閉症.md), autism.
