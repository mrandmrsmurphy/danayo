## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [峡](../characters/峡.md) "gorge" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 峡 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (海's own stand-in is [[海洋]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).
