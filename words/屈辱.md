## Notes
- [屈](../characters/屈.md) "bend, flex, crouch" + [辱](../characters/辱.md) "humiliate, insult, embarrass, disgrace" — no stand-in relationship applies (屈's own stand-in is itself; 辱's own is [[羞辱]]). The compound's "humiliation" sense builds on 屈's metaphorical extension of "bending" to submission/subjugation, paired with 辱's own "disgrace" sense.

Mandarin qūrǔ, Cantonese wat1 juk6, Japanese くつじょく (kutsujoku), and Korean 굴욕 (gullyok) are all real, standard everyday words for "humiliation, disgrace" — very common, e.g. 屈辱的/굴욕적, "humiliating." `vietnamese: khuất nhục` is the compositional reading; not independently verified as an everyday attested term (Vietnamese more often reaches for nhục nhã, built from a different root). Near-synonym of [[侮辱]] ("insult, humiliate," an act done to someone) and [[恥辱]] ("disgrace, shame," a state); 屈辱 leans toward humiliation bound up with submission or defeat. `kwin` is false per the AND-rule: both 屈 and 辱 are individually `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄨㄊㄋㄛㄎ unique among both words and characters).
