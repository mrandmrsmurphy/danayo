## Notes
- [十](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" + [字](../characters/字%20(char).md) "character" + [架](../characters/架%20(char).md) "frame, rack" — "ten-shaped frame," the cross. Their own `stand_in` values: 十's is itself; 字's is itself; 架's is itself; none points here, so 十字架 is an independent compound.

十字架 is the cross: the frame on which Jesus was crucified, and the Christian symbol. Mandarin shízìjià, Cantonese sap6 zi6 gaa3, Japanese じゅうじか and Korean 십자가 all use it, and Vietnamese thập tự giá does too. The name is "ten-character frame", from the shape of the character 十. Compare [[受難]] (the Passion) and [[救贖]] (redemption).
