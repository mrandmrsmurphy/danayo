## Notes
- [怒](../characters/怒%20(char).md) "angry" + [涛](../characters/涛.md) "wave" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [涛](../characters/涛.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (怒's own stand-in is itself).

怒涛 means surging waves — literally "angry waves," a violent swell. Related terms include 波涛/波濤 (billow) and 洪涛/洪濤 (great waves).

Mandarin nùtāo, Cantonese nou6 tou4, Japanese どとう, Korean 노도, and Vietnamese nộ đào are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug fixed**: `mandarin` had a stray malformed duplicate ("ùtāo, nùtáo") — corrected to nùtāo. Removed the redundant self-referential alias (怒涛 aliasing itself); kept traditional 怒濤. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄛㄉㄚㄨ unique across both words and characters).
