## Notes
- [接](../characters/接%20(char).md) "contact, connect" + [線](../characters/線.md) "line" — no stand-in relationship applies (接's own stand-in is itself; 線's own is [[直線]]).

接線 means tangent (line).

Mandarin jiēxiàn, Cantonese zip3 sin3, Japanese せっせん, Korean 접선, and Vietnamese tiếp tuyến are all standard, fully compositional readings.
