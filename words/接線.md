## Notes
- [接](../characters/接%20(char).md) "contact, connect" + [線](../characters/線.md) "line" — no stand-in relationship applies (接's own stand-in is itself; 線's own is [[直線]]).

接線 means tangent (line).

Mandarin jiēxiàn, Cantonese zip3 sin3, Japanese せっせん, Korean 접선, and Vietnamese tiếp tuyến are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `mandarin` was contaminated with qiēxiàn (the reading of the near-synonym Chinese term 切線/切线, "cutting line," using 切 qiē rather than 接 jiē) — corrected to compositional jiēxiàn. Removed `切線`/`切线` from `aliases`: they're a distinct compound built on a different character (切, not 接) — the more common Chinese-convention term for the same concept, not an orthographic variant of this word. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄛㄆㄙ⼶ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
