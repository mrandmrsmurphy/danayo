## Notes
- [勇](../characters/勇.md) "brave" + [気 (char)](../characters/気%20(char).md) "spirit"

勇気 is courage: the spirit to face danger or difficulty without giving way. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yǒngqì as "courage; nerve; valour" and Japanese ゆうき as "courage". Korean 용기, Vietnamese dũng khí and Cantonese jung5 hei3 match.

It is the spirit that overcomes [恐怖](恐怖.md), fear, and the quality of the [勇敢](勇敢.md), brave. It rests on [自信](自信.md), confidence, and a failure of it is the coward's [怯](怯.md).
