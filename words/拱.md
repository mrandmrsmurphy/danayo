## Notes
- [拱](../characters/拱%20(char).md) "arch"

拱 means "arch," the curved structural span of a bridge, gate or vault. It is a 形声 character: the hand radical 扌 ("hand") with 共 ("together") as phonetic, which also fits the older verbal sense of joining the hands in a cupped salute (拱手, "to clasp one's hands in greeting").

Mandarin gǒng, Cantonese gung2, Japanese きょう, Korean 공, and Vietnamese củng are all standard, fully compositional readings.
