## Notes
- [拱](../characters/拱%20(char).md) "arch" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [拱](../characters/拱%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

拱 means arch.

Mandarin gǒng, Cantonese gung2, Japanese きょう, Korean 공, and Vietnamese củng are all standard, fully compositional readings. Homophone of [[共]] "with; together with" (both 注音 ㄍ⼄ㄫ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words; checked other candidates sharing this reading (囧/供/恭/憬) and confirmed none has an independent word at this exact reading (囧 is name-only, the rest bound to longer stand-in compounds).
