## Notes
- [哇 (char)](../characters/哇%20(char).md) "wow"

哇 is an exclamation of surprise or wonder, like English "wow". It is written with 口, mouth, and 圭, which gives the sound. English Wiktionary lists the Chinese 哇 (Mandarin wā, Cantonese waa1) as "an interjection indicating surprise or amazement: wow!, gee", the onomatopoeia for the sound of crying and the sound of vomiting, classically the verb "to vomit" (as in the *Mengzi*), and a sentence-final particle equivalent to 啊 that is used only after words ending in -u or -ao, as in 好哇, "hurray!". It gives Japanese only the kanji senses "to vomit" and "lewd or seductive voices or music", and lists Korean 와 and Vietnamese oa and oà as readings with no gloss, so the interjection is attested for Chinese alone.

In Dan'a'yo, 哇 stands as the 感詞 for the syllable ⺢ㄨ, serving as the vault's primary vocabulary word for expressions of wonder or surprise.
