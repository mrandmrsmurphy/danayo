## Notes
- [獵](../characters/獵%20(char).md) "hunt" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 獵 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

獵 means to hunt: to pursue and kill wild animals for food or sport (打獵, "go hunting"; 獵人, "hunter"; 獵犬, "hunting dog"; 狩獵, "hunt"), and by extension to seek out or pursue (獵奇, "seek novelty"; 獵取, "hunt down, seize"; 獵頭, "headhunt"). The traditional form 獵 is the headword; the shinjitai 猟 and simplified 猎 are listed as aliases. The graph is a 形声 compound of 犭 "dog" with the phonetic 巤.

Mandarin liè, Cantonese lip6, Japanese りょう (狩猟/しゅりょう, "hunting"; 猟師/りょうし, "hunter"; 密猟/みつりょう, "poaching"), Korean 렵 (수렵, "hunting") and Vietnamese liệp (the primary Hán Việt reading, with lạp a secondary alternate) are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native verb 狩る (かる) for the everyday word, so on'yomi りょう is bound to compounds, Korean has native 사냥하다 and Vietnamese native săn.
