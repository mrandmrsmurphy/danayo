## Notes
- [花](../characters/花.md) "flower" is separately legitimized by [[草花]]. [弁](../characters/弁%20(char).md) "discuss, distinguish" — here used in its shinjitai stand-in role for 瓣 ("petal"), one of the three traditional characters 弁 substitutes for in Japanese — is separately legitimized by [[弁]] itself. No cranberry.

花弁 combines 花 ("flower") with 弁 (standing in for 瓣, "petal") — "flower petal." Traditional spelling 花瓣, documented as `aliases`.
