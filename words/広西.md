## Notes
- [広](../characters/広%20(char).md) "wide" + [西](../characters/西.md) "west" — "wide west". No stand-in relationship applies (広's own is [[広]]; 西's is [[西方]]). 廣西 and 广西 are the traditional and simplified spellings of 広, kept as aliases.

広西 is Guangxi, a region of southern China on the border with Vietnam, whose capital is [[南寧]] (from memory, not re-checked). English Wiktionary says the name comes from the Song-dynasty Guangnan West Circuit (廣南西路), and its one sense is the region (the autonomous region of the Zhuang people); the quotation it gives is from the Song book Lingwai Daida and uses the name for the older circuit's territory. The Japanese field holds こうせい (English Wiktionary Japanese 広西). The Korean field holds the Sino-Korean 광서, which differs from the Dan'a'yo 諺文 광세 in the vowel of 西; the usual South Korean name today is 광시 (listed as a descendant on English Wiktionary). Vietnamese Quảng Tây is the Sino-Vietnamese name. Cantonese gwong2 sai1 is the Jyutping on English Wiktionary. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⺢ㄫㄙㄝㄧ unique across both words and characters).

**How the official name is written.** The full official name 廣西壯族自治區 is not coined as one long compound but written as separate words: 広西 <ruby>[壮族](../words/壮族.md)<rt>ㄐ⺢ㄫㄐㄛㄎ</rt></ruby> <ruby>[自治](../words/自治.md)<rt>ㄐㄧㄜㄑㄧ</rt></ruby> <ruby>[区域](../words/区域.md)<rt>ㄎㄨ·ㄨㄧㄎ</rt></ruby>, with 区域 standing in for the bound character 区. 壮族 (alias 壯族) is an existing word, so no new word was needed for the Zhuang people.

Attestation, checked 2026-10-11: English Wiktionary Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese entries for 廣西 (the Japanese entry points to 広西).
