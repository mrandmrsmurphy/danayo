## Notes
- [畜](characters/畜.md) "livestock" + [生](characters/生.md) "life" — 畜's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 生's own is [[生活]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 畜 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

畜生 means domestic animal, livestock, brute.

Japanese ちくしょう (CHIKU+SHOU) and Korean 축생 were already correct. Fixed `mandarin`: a comma-joined dual pronunciation ("chùsheng, chùshēng") was corrected to the single compositional chùshēng. Fixed `cantonese`: a comma-joined value mixing the recurring saang1 typo with the correct cuk1 sang1 was corrected to cuk1 sang1 alone. Fixed `vietnamese`: a comma-joined dual value ("súc sinh, súc sanh") was corrected to the single compositional súc sinh, matching 生's own citation. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄨㄎㄙㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
