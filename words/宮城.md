## Notes
- [宮](../characters/宮.md) "royal court" + [城](../characters/城.md) "castle"

宮城 is Miyagi, written "palace castle". It takes its name from the old Miyagi district (宮城郡) around the present Sendai. The Japanese reading みやぎ is native, with 宮 (みや) and ぎ for 城 (an old word for a fortification, as in 茨城), so the characters alone would not predict it.

Miyagi is the largest economic centre of the Tōhoku region, with its capital Sendai, the old seat of the Date clan under Date Masamune. It is known for the pine-covered islands of Matsushima, one of the traditional "Three Views of Japan", and for the 2011 Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami, whose epicentre lay off its coast.

In Dan'a'yo 宮城 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 미야기 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
