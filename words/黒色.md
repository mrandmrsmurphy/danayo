## Notes
- [黒 (char)](../characters/黒%20(char).md) "black" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — no `#cranberry`: 黒's own `stand_in` is 黒 itself, 色's is [[色彩]]; neither points here.

Mandarin hēisè and Cantonese hak1sik1 keep the compositional Sino reading. Japanese くろ and Korean 검정 are both the real, everyday native words for "black" rather than the compositional readings こくしょく/흑색.
