## Notes
- [他 (char)](../characters/他%20(char).md) "another, other" + [国](../characters/国.md) "nation"

他国 is another country, other countries. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 他國 (alias here; Mandarin tāguó, Cantonese taa1 gwok3) as "another country; foreign country", Japanese たこく as "foreign country", and lists Korean 타국 as a hanja form of the same word. It has no Vietnamese entry, so the `vietnamese` field is omitted. Compare [外国](外国.md), "foreign country". The stand-in of 国 is [国家](国家.md), and 他 is the Dan'a'yo word [他](他.md).
