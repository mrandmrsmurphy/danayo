## Notes
- [広 (char)](../characters/広%20(char).md) "wide"

広 means wide or broad, extremely productive as a compound-forming element ([[広場]] "plaza," [[広野]] "wilderness," [[広土]] "vast territory," [[広範]] "wide-ranging") and, as a Japanese surname/place-name element, familiar from names like Hiroshima (広島, "wide island").

Japanese こう (on'yomi; the native ひろ, hiro, is the everyday adjective and common in given names instead), Korean 광, and Vietnamese quảng are all standard readings — Vietnamese quảng in particular is a familiar element in Vietnamese place names (Quảng Nam, Quảng Ngãi), paralleling Japanese place-name usage.
