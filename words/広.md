## Notes
- [広 (char)](../characters/広%20(char).md) "wide" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [広 (char)](../characters/広%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

広 means wide or broad, extremely productive as a compound-forming element ([[広場]] "plaza," [[広野]] "wilderness," [[広土]] "vast territory," [[広範]] "wide-ranging") and, as a Japanese surname/place-name element, familiar from names like Hiroshima (広島, "wide island").

Japanese こう (on'yomi; the native ひろ, hiro, is the everyday adjective and common in given names instead), Korean 광, and Vietnamese quảng are all standard readings — Vietnamese quảng in particular is a familiar element in Vietnamese place names (Quảng Nam, Quảng Ngãi), paralleling Japanese place-name usage. No homophones among other words (注音 ㄍ⺢ㄫ; several unrelated characters share it, including [[洸]], [[狂]], [[恍]], [[胱]], and [[鉱]], but none of their own stand-in words collide with 広's).
