## Notes
- [週](../characters/週.md) "week" + [初](../characters/初.md) "beginning"

週初 is a Dan'a'yo formation, tagged `neologism` in this vault: it is built from 週 "week" and 初 "beginning" on the pattern of [月初](月初.md) "the beginning of the month," and it is not an established headword in the other languages (Wiktionary has no entry for it), so the readings recorded for Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese are the literal readings of the two characters (zhōuchū, zau1 co1, しゅうしょ, 주초) and the natural phrase in Vietnamese, đầu tuần "beginning of the week." Its sibling words on the same pattern are [週中](週中.md) "the middle of the week" and [週末](週末.md) "the end of the week."

The Calendar page lists the three together in its Week section, which also notes that Asian weeks start on Monday, so 週初 is the Monday end of the week. A related word is [週日](週日.md), glossed "week" and the stand-in for the character 週. Dan'a'yo keeps the sense "the beginning of the week."
