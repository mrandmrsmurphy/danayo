## Notes
- [横](../characters/横.md) "crossing horizontally" + [店](../characters/店.md) "shop, store" — no stand-in relationship applies (横's own is [[横断]]; 店's own is [[商店]]).

横店 (Hengdian) is a town in Dongyang, in Jinhua prefecture, Zhejiang province, China. It is best known for 横店影視城 (Hengdian World Studios), reputed to be China's largest film and television studio base and often nicknamed the "Hollywood of China" (from memory, not re-checked). The name is written 橫店 in traditional characters, which is kept as an alias.

Japanese おうてん, Korean 횡점, and Vietnamese hoành điếm are compositional Sino-Xenic readings; Wiktionary gives only the Mandarin reading, so the Cantonese, Japanese, Korean, and Vietnamese fields are derived from the constituent characters. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⺢ㄫㄉㄝㄇ unique across both words and characters).
