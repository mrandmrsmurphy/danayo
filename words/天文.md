## Notes
- [天](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven, sky" + [文](../characters/文.md) "pattern, sign" — no stand-in relationship applies (天 is its own stand-in; 文's own is [[文化]]). The study of these objects is called [[天文学]]/astronomy.

天文 means celestial objects or phenomena — literally "heaven's patterns."

Japanese てんもん (using 文's alternate on'yomi MON, a lexicalized reading specific to this compound), Korean 천문, Mandarin tiānwén, and Cantonese tin1 man4 are all standard readings. Vietnamese thiên văn is a standard reading. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄝㄋㄇㄨㄋ unique across both words and characters).
