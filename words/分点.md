## Notes
- [分 (char)](../characters/分%20(char).md) "divide" + [点 (char)](../characters/点%20(char).md) "point" — the two moments each year when the sun crosses the celestial equator and day and night are of equal length

Wiktionary: Chinese 分點 "equinox"; Japanese 分点 (ぶんてん), astronomy, "an equinox" (also archaic or obsolete senses: a comma; a branching point or junction); Korean 분점 is the hanja form, "equinox". Contrast [[至点]], the solstice.
