## Notes
- [噴](../characters/噴.md) "erupt" + [火](../characters/火%20(char).md) "fire" — this word is the stand-in for [噴](../characters/噴.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo (火's own stand-in is itself).

噴火 means to erupt — a volcano spewing fire and ash (噴火山 "volcano").

Filled blank `vietnamese: phún hoả` (verified — also attested as phun hoả). Japanese ふんか and Korean 분화 are both standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄛㄋㄏ⺢ unique across both words and characters).
