## Notes
- [四 (char)](../characters/四%20(char).md) "four" + [十](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" — "forty." For anti-forgery, use [[肆拾]]. This word is an anagram of [[十四]] (fourteen) — the same digit characters in opposite order name entirely different numbers, worth double-checking when reading either word. **Real content bug found and fixed**: `cantonese` was stored as "si4 si2," an unrelated garbled reading — corrected to "sei3 sap6," matching 四/十's own confirmed readings. **Content added**: `vietnamese: tứ thập`, a directly attested standard Sino-Vietnamese numeral compound, though everyday Vietnamese counts natively as bốn mươi instead. Dropped the duplicate `品詞:` key (already present as `pos:`). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 싀십 diverges from Sino-Korean 사십 in the first syllable. Its only homophone is the anti-forgery form [[肆拾]] (注音 ㄙㄧㄜㄙㄧㄆ), which reads exactly as 四十 does by design.
