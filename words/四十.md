## Notes
- [四 (char)](../characters/四%20(char).md) "four" + [十 (char)](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" — "forty." For anti-forgery, use [[肆拾]]. This word is an anagram of [[十四]] (fourteen) — the same digit characters in opposite order name entirely different numbers, worth double-checking when reading either word. `vietnamese: tứ thập` is a directly attested standard Sino-Vietnamese numeral compound, though everyday Vietnamese counts natively as bốn mươi instead.
