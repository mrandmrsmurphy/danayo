## Notes
- [脊](../characters/脊.md) "spine, ridge" + [椎](../characters/椎.md) "vertebra" — `#cranberry`: both characters' own `stand_in` point to this exact compound (脊's and 椎's alike), so transitivity holds (A=B=AB) and both are legitimized as independent Dan'a'yo entries specifically by this word.

脊椎 means the spine or vertebrae, combining two near-synonymous roots for emphasis/precision.

Pronunciation fields (jegcui/적취/ㄐㄝㄎㄑㄨㄧ) are the straightforward concatenation of each constituent's own stored reading — no bug. `kwin: false` was already correct per the AND-rule (both constituents individually false).
