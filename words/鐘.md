## Notes
- [鐘](../characters/鐘%20(char).md) "bell" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

鐘 means bell — a hollow metal instrument struck to produce a ringing tone.

Mandarin zhōng, Cantonese zung1, Japanese しょう, Korean 종, and Vietnamese chung are all standard, fully compositional readings. Part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read jong/종/ㄐㄛㄫ) with [[従]] ("obey, observe") and [[鍾]] ("alcohol bottle") — the pending "鍾???" note left in this page has now been resolved and confirmed.
