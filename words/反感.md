## Notes
- [反 (char)](../characters/反%20(char).md) "against" + [感](../characters/感.md) "feeling"

反感 is antipathy: a feeling set against a person, an act or a remark, an instinctive dislike. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin fǎngǎn as "bad reaction; antipathy; dislike" and Japanese はんかん as "antipathy; revolt; animosity". Vietnamese phản cảm, Cantonese faan2 gam2 and Korean 반감 match.

It is a milder, more reactive feeling than [憎悪](憎悪.md), hatred, and closer to [嫌悪](嫌悪.md), aversion. The act that follows it is [反抗](反抗.md), resistance.
