## Notes
- [第 (char)](../characters/第%20(char).md) "-th" + [四 (char)](../characters/四%20(char).md) "four" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here, so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false`, as for [[第三]].

Wiktionary: Chinese "fourth" and "fourthly"; Japanese 第四 (だいし) "fourth (4th), number four". It is the ordinal formed by prefixing 第 to 四, in the same pattern as [[第一]], [[第二]], [[第三]] and [[第五]].
