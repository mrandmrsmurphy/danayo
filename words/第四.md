## Notes
- [第 (char)](../characters/第%20(char).md) "-th" + [四 (char)](../characters/四%20(char).md) "four" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here, so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false`, as for [[第三]].

第四 means "fourth," the ordinal formed by prefixing 第 to 四, in the same pattern as [[第一]], [[第二]] and [[第三]].

Mandarin dì-sì, Cantonese dai6 sei3, Japanese だいし and Korean 제사 are all standard, fully compositional readings; Vietnamese đệ tứ is likewise compositional.
