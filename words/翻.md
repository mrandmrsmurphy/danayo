## Notes
- [翻](../characters/翻%20(char).md) "flip" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 翻 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

翻 means to flip, as in 翻訳 (translate, literally "flip over into another language") and 翻身 (turn oneself over, emancipate).

Mandarin fān, Cantonese faan1, and Korean 번 (beon) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading ひるがえす (hirugaesu, "to flip, wave") rather than the on-readings ホン/ハン (HON/HAN) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese phiên is the standard reading. **Fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` had pon/폰/ㄆㄛㄋ (a p-initial), mismatching 翻's real f-initial reading (fon/뽄/ㄈㄛㄋ, per the vault's established ㄈ→ㅃ transliteration convention) — corrected. This exposed a genuine Dan'a'yo homophone with [[反]] ("anti-, wrong side up"), whose own page had independently already fixed the same bug and documented 販/返 as sharing this phonetic family (neither has a word page).
