## Notes
- [翻](../characters/翻%20(char).md) "flip" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 翻 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

翻 means to flip, as in 翻訳 (translate, literally "flip over into another language") and 翻身 (turn oneself over, emancipate).

Mandarin fān, Cantonese faan1, and Korean 번 (beon) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading ひるがえす (hirugaesu, "to flip, wave") rather than the on-readings ホン/ハン (HON/HAN) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese phiên is the standard reading.
