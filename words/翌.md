## Notes
- [翌 (char)](../characters/翌%20(char).md) "the following, after, next" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 翌 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

Mandarin yì, Cantonese jik6, and Korean 익 (ik) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the on-reading ヨク (YOKU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese dực is the standard reading. 億/憶/抑/翼/臆 share the same syllable at the character level, but none has an independent word page.
