## Notes
- [翌 (char)](../characters/翌%20(char).md) "the following, after, next" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 翌 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo. **Fixed a real bug**: `pos` had been set to 格助詞 (case particle, a specific closed class of twelve suffixing particles that 翌 does not belong to) — corrected to 修飾語, matching the character's own page and its actual grammatical role as a temporal modifier (as in 翌月, "the following month").

Mandarin yì, Cantonese jik6, and Korean 익 (ik) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the on-reading ヨク (YOKU) for the bare character alone; the stored value was previously いき, which turned out not to be an attested reading at all (confirmed via web search) — corrected. Vietnamese dực is the standard reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones (億/憶/抑/翼/臆 share the same syllable at the character level, but none has an independent word page).
