## Notes
- [鮮](../characters/鮮.md) "fresh, rare" + [少 (char)](../characters/少%20(char).md) "few, little" — no `#cranberry`: 鮮's own `stand_in` is [[新鮮]], 少's is 少 itself; neither points here.

Mandarin xiǎnshǎo, Cantonese sin2siu2, Japanese せんしょう, Korean 선소, and Sino-Vietnamese tiên thiểu are all standard, fully compositional readings.

**Self-corrected**: `羅馬字`/`諺文` were originally left as syensou/션솟 when this word was first perfected — an unexplained -n substitution for 鮮's own -m coda, missed at the time and caught only when the identical bug turned up on [[鮮美]] and [[鮮花]] — corrected to syemsou/셤솟, matching the `注音` field (ㄙ⼶ㄇㄙㄛㄨ), which had the -m all along.
