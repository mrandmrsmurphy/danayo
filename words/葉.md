## Notes
- [葉 (char)](../characters/葉%20(char).md) "leaf" — the bare character used directly as a word, its own `stand_in`.

葉 means leaf: the flat, usually green organ of a plant (樹葉, "tree leaf"; 落葉, "fallen leaves"; 葉子, "leaf"), and by extension a thin flat piece or a period of time (葉片, "blade"; 中葉, "the middle period of an era"). The simplified form 叶 is listed as an alias.

Mandarin yè, Cantonese jip6, Japanese よう (落葉/らくよう, "fallen leaves"; 葉緑素/ようりょくそ, "chlorophyll"; 紅葉/こうよう, "autumn leaves"), Korean 엽 (낙엽, "fallen leaves"), and Vietnamese diệp (diệp lục, "chlorophyll") are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native noun 葉 (は) for the everyday word, so on'yomi よう is bound to compounds; Korean has native 잎.
