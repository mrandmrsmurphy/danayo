## Notes
- [残 (char)](../characters/残%20(char).md) "cruel; remaining" + [酷](../characters/酷.md) "harsh"

残酷 is cruel: harsh and merciless toward the weak or the suffering, taking no pity on them. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin cánkù as "cruel; ruthless" and Japanese ざんこく as "cruel". Vietnamese tàn khốc, Cantonese caan4 huk6 and Korean 잔혹 match.

It is the active side of the [冷酷](冷酷.md), heartless, and is harsher than [苛酷](苛酷.md), severe. Its victims suffer the [惨](惨.md), wretched, and it is a sin of the kind on [Sin](../lexipedia/Sin.md).
