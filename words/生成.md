## Notes
- [生](../characters/生.md) "be born; grow" + [成 (char)](../characters/成%20(char).md) "become"

生成 is to come into being, or to bring into being: to generate or form. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin shēngchéng as "to form; to come into being; to generate; to produce" and Japanese せいせい as "generation; formation; to create, to form". Cantonese sang1 sing4 and Korean 생성 match.

It is the ontological verb that [発生](発生.md), which only arises, and [生産](生産.md), which manufactures, each cover in part. Its opposite is [消滅](消滅.md), to die out.
