## Notes
- [外](../characters/外.md) "outside; maternal side" + [孫](../characters/孫.md) "grandchild" — grandson through a daughter.

外孫 is the child of one's daughter, a grandchild on the mother's side. It is "outside-grandchild," the descending counterpart of [[外祖父]] and [[外祖母]], with "outside" marking the daughter's line as the family one marries out to. Mandarin wàisūn (a formal term; Beijing and Taiwan speech uses 外孫子, and some varieties 外甥) and Cantonese ngoi6 syun1 are confirmed on Wiktionary, Chinese only; Korean 외손 is from memory (the Korean 외손녀 appears for the granddaughter). The page specifies a grandson; its female pair is [[外孫女]]. A paternal grandson is [[孫子]].
