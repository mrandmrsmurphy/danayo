## Notes
- [生](../characters/生.md) "life" + [活 (char)](../characters/活%20(char).md) "alive" — stand-in for [[生]] (活's own stand-in is itself, so `#cranberry` does not apply)

生活 means life or living, and as a verb to live: the daily activity and conditions of being alive (日常生活, "daily life"; 生活費, "cost of living"; 生活水準, "standard of living"; 生活方式, "lifestyle"). It is built from [[生]] "life" and [[活 (char)|活]] "alive", and is the ordinary word for "lifestyle" and "livelihood" as well as "to live".

Mandarin shēnghuó, Cantonese sang1 wut6, Japanese せいかつ (seikatsu), Korean 생활 (saenghwal), and Vietnamese sinh hoạt are all the standard word. Vietnamese sinh hoạt also has the extended sense of group or community activities (sinh hoạt cộng đồng), beyond plain "life".
