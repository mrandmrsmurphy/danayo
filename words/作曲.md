## Notes
- [作 (char)](../characters/作%20(char).md) "make" + [曲](../characters/曲.md) "tune"

作曲 is to compose music, or a composition. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin zuòqǔ as "to compose; to write music" and Japanese さっきょく as "composition". Korean 작곡 is the same word. Cantonese zok3 kuk1 matches.

The one who does it is the [作曲家](作曲家.md), and the result is written down as a [楽譜](楽譜.md). A song is a [歌曲](歌曲.md).
