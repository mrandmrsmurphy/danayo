## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" + [家](../characters/家.md) "family" — literally "self-family," one's own family or household.

Mandarin zìjiā, Cantonese zi6 gaa1, Japanese じか (jika), and Korean 자가 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 自's and 家's own stored citations exactly. **Found and fixed a real bug**: `cantonese` was stored as "zi4 jia1" — 自's own jyutping tone was wrong (zi4 instead of the real zi6) and 家's own reading was written with Mandarin pinyin ("jia1") instead of jyutping ("gaa1"); corrected to zi6 gaa1.

`kwin` is false: 自 alone diverges from Korean on its own character page, which alone is enough to make the AND-rule false; 家 is independently kwin-true. Genuine homophone (see callout above): 注音/諺文 ㄐㄧㄜㄍㄚㄆ/즤갑 is shared exactly with [[指甲]] "fingernail," an unrelated coincidence.
