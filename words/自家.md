## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" + [家](../characters/家.md) "family" — literally "self-family," one's own family or household.

Mandarin zìjiā, Cantonese zi6 gaa1, Japanese じか (jika), and Korean 자가 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 自's and 家's own stored citations exactly.
