## Notes
- [合](../characters/合%20(char).md) "join" + [流](../characters/流.md) "flow" — "flow together." Their own `stand_in` values: 合's is itself; 流's is [[流動]]; none points here, so 合流 is an independent compound.

合流 is to flow together: for streams, roads or groups to join into one. As a noun it names the confluence where two rivers meet. Mandarin héliú, Cantonese hap6 lau4, Japanese ごうりゅう, Korean 합류 and Vietnamese hợp lưu all use it. Compare [[三角洲]] (delta) and [[河川]] (rivers).
