## Notes
- [維](../characters/維.md) "support" + [持 (char)](../characters/持%20(char).md) "hold" — 維's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 持's own is [[持]] itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 維持 legitimizes 維 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

維持 means to maintain, keep, or uphold.

Mandarin wéichí, Cantonese wai4 ci4, Japanese いじ (iji), and Korean 유지 (yuji) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 維持 is itself a common attested word for "maintain" in all four languages. Vietnamese duy trì is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term for "maintain." No Dan'a'yo homophones.
