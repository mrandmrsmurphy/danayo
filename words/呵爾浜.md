## Notes
- [呵](../characters/呵.md) "ha" + [爾](../characters/爾%20(char).md) ""yes"" + [浜](../characters/浜%20(char).md) "coast" — phonetic spelling of Harbin. No stand-in relationship applies (呵's is [[呵呵]]; 爾 is its own stand-in; 浜 is its own stand-in).

呵爾浜 is the vault spelling of 哈爾濱 (Harbin), the capital and largest city of Heilongjiang province in northeastern China. 哈 is listed as an alias of 呵 and 濱 as an alias of 浜 in the character pages, so by the alias rule the filename uses 呵 and 浜; 哈爾濱, 哈爾浜 (the usual Japanese spelling) and 哈尔滨 are kept as aliases. The characters are phonetic: Wiktionary derives the name from a dialectal Manchu word cognate with Manchu halfiyan "flat", so the character glosses carry no meaning of their own.

Japanese ハルビン is the name listed on Wiktionary Japanese under 哈爾浜 (a katakana reading of an irregularly-read kanji spelling) and is the field value. English Wiktionary has no Korean entry for 哈爾濱; the Korean field holds 가이빈, derived mechanically from the character pages (呵 가, 爾 이, 浜 빈, North Korean readings), and is not an attested reading. The usual South Korean name is 하얼빈 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Cáp Nhĩ Tân is the Sino-Vietnamese name listed on Wiktionary. The 注音 carries a syllable-boundary dot because ㄏㄚㄋㄝㄅㄧㄋ also parses as ㄏㄚㄋ + ㄝ + ㄅㄧㄋ. Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese, Japanese (as 哈爾浜) and Vietnamese entries. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚ·ㄋㄝㄅㄧㄋ unique across both words and characters).
