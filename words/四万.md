## Notes
- [四 (char)](../characters/四%20(char).md) "four" + [万](../characters/万.md) "ten-thousand" — forty thousand, 40,000.

Digit + 万, like [[二万]] and [[七万]]: the myriad (10,000) is the Sinitic grouping unit, so 40,000 is literally "four myriad" and is not built through a "thousand" step as in English. Japanese uses よん, not し, for 四. Korean 사만 follows the Sino-Korean reading directly. Vietnamese bốn vạn uses vạn, which is a live everyday numeral rather than only a classical one. Compare [[二万]] and [[七万]].
