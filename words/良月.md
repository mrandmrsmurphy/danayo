## Notes
- [良](../characters/良.md) is separately legitimized by [[良好]]. [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) is separately legitimized by [[月]] itself. No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

Tenth month of the ancient Chinese poetic calendar. Unlike the other eleven months, 良 (good, fine, kind) is not a plant name. The harvest is complete, the granaries are full, the world is at rest — simply a "fine" month. It is also called 小陽春 ("little spring of yang"), a rare warm spell that can return in early winter. Some traditions call it [[露月]] "dew month".

Pronunciation fields (lyang'wed/량웓/ㄌ⼘ㄫ·⼔ㄊ) are the straightforward concatenation of each constituent's own stored reading, including the null-onset syllable break before 月's own vowel-initial 'wed — no bug. `kwin: false` was already correct per the AND-rule (月 individually false despite 良's own `kwin: true`). Korean 량월 (unshifted, correctly following the standing North-Korean-pronunciation rule), mandarin liángyuè, and cantonese loeng4jyut6 (space removed) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese よいつき (yoitsuki, "good moon/month") is a genuine real native term, using 良's native adjective よい ("good") + 月's own native つき, not decomposable from either character's own stored on'yomi field but nonetheless attested. Vietnamese tháng lương follows native word order (month-first), similar to [[臘月]]'s tháng Chạp. Removed a redundant `品詞` duplicate of `pos`.
