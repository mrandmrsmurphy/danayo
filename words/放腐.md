## Notes
- [放](../characters/放.md) "release, let go" + [腐](../characters/腐.md) "rotten, corrupt"

放腐 is the Dan'a'yo rendering of 摆烂 (bǎi làn), the Chinese internet slang that emerged as a darker successor to 躺平 / [[臥平]]. Where 臥平 describes a passive withdrawal — lying down and refusing to rise — 放腐 describes something more active and nihilistic: not merely declining to participate, but actively embracing and accelerating deterioration.

The term became a buzzword in 2022; one source names it among China's top ten internet buzzwords of 2022 and says its usage surpassed 躺平 that year, and I did not verify any Weibo read counts. Several slang-explainer sites give its origin as the Chinese NBA fanbase's adaptation of the English basketball concept of "tanking" — a team deliberately losing games to secure a higher draft pick. Language Log calls the NBA connection solid but notes a commenter's report that the word had been used in Taiwan Mandarin for several years in the sense of disengaging, so the sports origin may not be the whole story. If the tanking origin holds, it is significant: tanking requires deliberate agency. 摆烂 is not mere resignation; it is a choice to make things worse, a kind of performative collapse.

The backdrop is the same economic pressure that produced 臥平, but sharpened. China's official surveyed unemployment rate for 16-to-24-year-olds reached a record 21.3% in June 2023 (the highest of that series), after which the statistics bureau stopped publishing it and later resumed under a new method that excludes students. A generation that had been raised on the promise of relentless growth found that education, effort, and ambition were no longer reliably converting into outcomes. If 臥平 was the decision to stop running, 摆烂 is the decision to start walking backwards — a sarcastic, self-deprecating declaration that the game is already lost, so one might as well stop pretending.

The arc 臥平 → 放腐 mirrors a broader tonal shift in Chinese online discourse about youth and work: from the relatively dignified quietism of 2021 to the sardonic nihilism of 2022–23. English Wikipedia reports that in 2025 the Cyberspace Administration of China required platforms to censor 躺平 and other "negative worldviews"; it does not name 摆烂 in that passage.

In Dan'a'yo, 放 (release, let go) captures the stance adequately despite the slight passive reading; 腐 (rot, decay, corrupt) carries the right sense of deliberate deterioration. The word is coined expressly to gloss 摆烂 and inherits its full social-political valence.
