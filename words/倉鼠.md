## Etymology
- [倉](../characters/倉.md) "granary" + [鼠](../characters/鼠.md) "rat"

## Notes
Only Mandarin/Cantonese use the real compositional Chinese term (倉鼠, "granary rat," from hamsters' cheek-pouch food-hoarding behavior); Japanese, Korean, and Vietnamese all instead reach directly for the English loanword — ハムスター, 햄스터, and `vietnamese: chuột hamster` (confirmed via web search, alongside native alternatives like chuột đất vàng/chuột hang). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 촹쇼 diverges entirely from the loanword-based Korean field. No homophones (注音 ㄑ⺢ㄫㄙ⼄ unique among both words and characters).
