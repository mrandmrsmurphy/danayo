## Notes
- [衛](../characters/衛.md) "guard; escort" + [星 (char)](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star"

衛星 is a satellite: a body that circles a larger one, whether a natural moon or a made one. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin wèixīng as "satellite; moon" and "man-made satellite", and Japanese えいせい as "a satellite". Vietnamese vệ tinh is the same word, and Korean 위성 and Cantonese wai6 sing1 match.

It is the "guard star" that escorts a [行星](行星.md), planet, along its [軌道](軌道.md), orbit. Our own is the [太陰](太陰.md), the moon.
