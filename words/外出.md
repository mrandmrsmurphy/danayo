## Notes
- [外](../characters/外.md) "outside" + [出](../characters/出%20(char).md) "exit, go out" — no stand-in relationship applies (外's own stand-in is [[外部]]; 出 is its own stand-in).

外出 means to go out — leaving one's home or workplace, typically briefly.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `vietnamese` was nonsensical ("ra, chết" — the second word, "chết," means "to die," entirely unrelated) — corrected to `xuất ngoại` (compositional, with the syllable order reversed as is conventional; verified as a real, common Sino-Vietnamese term, though it specifically connotes "to go abroad" rather than the more general everyday sense of stepping out, for which Vietnamese instead uses the native "đi ra ngoài"). Japanese がいしゅつ and Korean 외출 are both standard readings. Filled blank `pos: 動詞`; fixed empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ⺢ㄧㄑㄨㄊ unique across both words and characters).
