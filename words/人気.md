## Notes
- [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" + [気](../characters/気.md) "spirit; feeling"

人気 (traditional 人氣) is "popularity," and as an attributive "popular." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 人氣 (rénqì, Cantonese jan4 hei3) is "presence of people," "popularity," "popular," and dialectally "personality; character"; Japanese has two readings, にんき "popularity" and ひとけ "sign of people"; and Korean 인기 is "popularity." Wiktionary has no Vietnamese section, so `vietnamese` nhân khí is compositional, not independently attested. In the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [China](../lookup/HSK/China.md) it appears in 人気 検索 単語, "popular search words." Related: [気](気.md) "feeling."
