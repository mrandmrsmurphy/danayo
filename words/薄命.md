## Notes
- [薄](../characters/薄.md) "thin, weak" is separately legitimized by [[希薄]]. [命](../characters/命.md) "life, fate" is separately legitimized by [[運命]]. No cranberry.

Mandarin bómìng, japanese はくめい, korean 박명, and vietnamese bạc mệnh (all also real, standard, fully attested terms — the vietnamese matching the famous idiom "hồng nhan bạc mệnh") are all confirmed compositional.
