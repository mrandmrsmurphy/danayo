## Notes
- [馬 (char)](../characters/馬%20(char).md) "horse" + [𡿺](../characters/𡿺.md) "fontanel, brain" — legitimizing note: 𡿺's own `stand_in` is [[馬𡿺]] itself, already annotated in its own citation; 馬's own `stand_in` is 馬 itself, so transitivity fails — no `#cranberry`.

Mandarin mǎnǎo, Cantonese maa5nou5, Korean 마노, Japanese めのう, and Sino-Vietnamese mã não are all the standard, real terms for "agate" — the vault represents this word with the rare/hapax placeholder character 𡿺 rather than the common orthography 瑪瑙, all of whose variant spellings (瑪瑙, 玛瑙, 碼碯, 码碯, 碼瑙, 码瑙, 馬瑙, 马瑙, 馬腦, 马脑) are recorded as aliases.
