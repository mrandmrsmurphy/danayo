## Notes
- [刺 (char)](../characters/刺%20(char).md) "prick; stitch" + [繍](../characters/繍.md) "embroider"

刺繍 is embroidery: decoration sewn into cloth with a needle and thread. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin cìxiù as "to embroider" and Japanese ししゅう as "embroidery". Korean 자수 is the same word. Cantonese ci3 sau3 matches.

It is a needle art of the [工芸](工芸.md), crafts, worked on cloth.
