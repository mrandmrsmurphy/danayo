## Notes
- [昏](../characters/昏.md) "dim; unconscious" + [倒 (char)](../characters/倒%20(char).md) "fall"

昏倒 is to faint: to fall down unconscious. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin hūndǎo as "to faint; to fall unconscious; to fall into a swoon" and Japanese こんとう as "fainting; to faint". Korean 혼도 and Cantonese fan1 dou2 match.

The stress is on the collapse, where [気絶](気絶.md) is the loss of consciousness itself.
