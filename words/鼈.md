## Notes
- [鼈 (char)](../characters/鼈%20(char).md) "soft-shell turtle" — 鼈 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

Mandarin biē, Cantonese bit3, Korean 별, and Sino-Vietnamese biết are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese すっぽん (suppon) is the real, everyday native word — a well-known culinary delicacy — rather than a Sino on'yomi reading. This word is a genuine Dan'a'yo homophone of [[別]] ("different, separate"); 別's own `stand_in` points to itself, confirming the collision — already documented from 別's own side.
