>[!caution] The senses split — Japanese "investigate", Korean "overtaking", Vietnamese "to access"
>追及 is to investigate and hold accountable in Japanese, and the Dan'a'yo sense. Korean 추급 is "overtaking", and Vietnamese truy cập is "to access", so a Korean or Vietnamese reader may hear something different. Listed as a Split on [文法 - 91疑義語](../grammar/文法%20-%2091疑義語.md); for an investigation prefer [調査](調査.md).


## Notes
- [追 (char)](../characters/追%20(char).md) "follow; hunt" + [及 (char)](../characters/及%20(char).md) "reach out to"

追及 means to pursue until one reaches the goal. English Wiktionary glosses Japanese ついきゅう as "investigation; to investigate", Korean 추급 as "overtaking" and Vietnamese truy cập as "to access". It has no Chinese entry for the word. The Dan'a'yo sense is the Japanese one, and the word is listed as a Split on [文法 - 91疑義語](../grammar/文法%20-%2091疑義語.md). The Mandarin and Cantonese fields are built from the readings of the two characters.
