## Notes
- [暑](../characters/暑%20(char).md) "hot (weather)" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [暑](../characters/暑%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

暑 means hot (of weather), as in 暑中, "midsummer heat," and 避暑, "escape the summer heat."

Mandarin shǔ, Cantonese syu2, Japanese しょ, Korean 서, and Vietnamese thử are all standard, fully compositional readings.

**No longer a homophone of [[所]]**: this word's own reading (syo/쇼/ㄙ⼄) is independently correct and unchanged, but [[所]] was found to have been storing an erroneous syo/쇼/ㄙ⼄ reading of its own — corrected to its true sǝ/스/ㄙㄜ, so the two words no longer collide. (The prior check against fourteen other candidates sharing this ㄙ⼄ reading — 序/庶/徐/屿/抒/叙/恕/書/署/緒/胥/黍/鼠 — still stands: none has an independent word at this exact reading.)
