## Notes
- [暑](../characters/暑%20(char).md) "hot (weather)" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [暑](../characters/暑%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

暑 means hot (of weather), as in 暑中, "midsummer heat," and 避暑, "escape the summer heat."

Mandarin shǔ, Cantonese syu2, Japanese しょ, Korean 서, and Vietnamese thử are all standard, fully compositional readings. Homophone of [[所]] "-ee (nominalizer)" (both syo/쇼/ㄙ⼄) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated words; checked fourteen other candidates sharing this reading (序/庶/徐/屿/抒/叙/恕/書/署/緒/胥/黍/鼠) and confirmed none has an independent word at this exact reading.
