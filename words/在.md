## Notes
- [在 (char)](../characters/在%20(char).md) "exist; to be present" — 在 is the `stand_in` of its own character page, i.e. the word that legitimizes the bound character 在

在 is the **intransitive copula of existence and location** in Dan'a'yo. The grammar ([[文法 - 04句法]], under 系詞) sorts the six copulas into transitive 是, 非, 有, 無, which take an explicit complement, and intransitive 在, 莫, which "indicate existence or location". 在 is the positive member of the second pair, with [[莫]] as its negation. Unlike [[是]] "to equal" (identity) and [[有]] "to have" (possession, "there is a..."), 在 says that a subject is present, exists, or is located: the grammar's own example is 捕已魚之男人 此処於 在 "The man who had caught the fish is here" ([[文法 - 05形態]], under the adnominal 之). All six copulas carry `pos: 系詞`. See [[文法 - 97品詞]] and [[Existence]] for the six-way split.

**Word order.** Dan'a'yo adpositions are suffixing, so the location takes [[於]] after it and 在 closes the clause: [location] 於 在. The grammar's example 此処於 在 "is here" follows this pattern. An illustrative sentence (constructed here, not from the grammar): 人 地於 在 "the person is on the ground". Note that the sample in [[文法 - 04句法]], 人在於地, puts 在 before 於 and 於 before the noun, which conflicts with this pattern and with the suffixing ruling; that page has not been edited, and the inconsistency is flagged for the grammar's author.

**Progressive prefix 在–.** The grammar also lists 在 (with 莫 as its negation) among the copula-derived verbal prefixes, marking the progressive (現進相): 我 在被食中哉! "OMG, I'm being eaten!" ([[文法 - 05形態]], 接頭辞; [[文法 - 97品詞]] lists "Progressive - 在– / 莫–"). This is the Dan'a'yo counterpart of Mandarin's progressive 在 (我在看书 "I am reading"), and is why 在 and 莫 appear among the four copulas that double as prefixes. This page covers the free copula; the prefix is described in the grammar.

**In the vault's translations.** 荘子: 北冥於 (海洋) 在 魚 "in the Northern Darkness [sea] there is a fish" ([[translation/荘子 (book)]], 在 after a place marked with 於). 詩経 国風: 在河小之洲 "(is) on the little islet of the river" (the vault's rendering of the Shijing line 在河之洲; gloss from memory) ([[translation/詩経/国風]]) keeps the classical order 在 + place, as does 大学 在明明徳 "(the Way of the Great Learning) lies in making bright virtue" ([[translation/大学 (book)]]). 在 also occurs in 論語 20 (暦数在爾躬) and 礼記 (車在馬前). The classical order in these texts is source text and is not the Dan'a'yo pattern above. The abstract noun "existence" and "now" are the compounds [[存在]] and [[現在]], and [[在位]] is "to reign". The 玉音放送 text has 共ニ在リ ("be together with") and the Korean 宣言書 has 在 read 재 in its classical sense (在치 안이하도다, roughly "does not lie in", gloss from memory); both are source texts, not Dan'a'yo.

**Cross-linguistic equivalents** (the `mandarin`, `cantonese`, `japanese`, `korean` and `vietnamese` fields hold the Sino-Xenic bound reading of the character, not the native word for "be located"). Mandarin 在 (zài) has three roles: locative preposition (在北京工作 "work in Beijing"), progressive adverb (在看书), and verb "to be at, to exist" (书在桌上 "the book is on the table"). Cantonese 喺 (hai2) is the spoken equivalent; 在 (zoi6) survives in formal compounds. Japanese 在 is on'yomi ザイ (在籍, 現在), with the native verbs いる/ある (東京にいる, 本が机にある) and the kun reading あ(る) recorded on the character page. Korean 있다 covers both possession and location (서울에 있다), while Sino-Korean 재 appears in 현재, 재학. Vietnamese uses ở (tôi ở Hà Nội), with Sino-Vietnamese tại in tại chỗ and hiện tại. These equivalents are from memory and were not re-checked against Wiktionary in this pass; the field readings match the character page.

**Etymology and phonology.** The character is a 形声 compound, phonetic 才 under semantic 士 (later corrupted to 土 "earth") per the character page. Ancient 從母 (d͡z) + 咍韻 (ʌi) gives Dan'a'yo ㄐㄚㄧ (jai, 재), the same shape as Sino-Korean 재.

**Homophones.** 在, [[哉]] and [[載]] are a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group, as is also noted on [[ㄐㄚㄧ]]. They differ entirely in grammatical function: copula (在), vocative suffix (哉), and a noun or verb "carry, record" (載).
