## Notes
- [玉 (char)](../characters/玉%20(char).md) "jade" + [案](../characters/案.md) "table; desk"

Wiktionary has no entry for 玉案. Wikipedia (Eight Principles of Yong) records 玉案 (yù'àn, "jade table") as Li Puguang's metaphorical name, from his Yuan-era 永字八法解, for the second principle, the horizontal stroke (橫, classically 勒). See [[怪石]] and the [[Stroke]] page. Its readings are compositional.
