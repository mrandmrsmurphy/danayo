## Notes
- [玉 (char)](../characters/玉%20(char).md) "jade" + [案](../characters/案.md) "table; desk"

**玉案** is a literary word for a jade-topped table or desk. As a calligraphy term it is the figurative name of the second of the eight principles of the 永字八法, the horizontal stroke ([[横]], classically 勒): the level stroke is imagined as the flat, even top of a fine table.

Li Puguang (李溥光), writing in the Yuan dynasty in his 永字八法解, gave each of the eight strokes of the 永字八法 a figurative two-character name; 玉案 is the horizontal's name. See [[怪石]] for the full set, and the [[Stroke]] page, where each stroke's modern name, classical name and Li's name are listed together.

The Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and North Korean forms are the ordinary compound readings of the word. No Vietnamese form is given.
