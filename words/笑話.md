## Notes
- [笑](../characters/笑%20(char).md) "laugh" + [話](../characters/話%20(char).md) "speech" — "laughing talk," a joke. Their own `stand_in` values: 笑's is itself; 話's is itself; none points here, so 笑話 is an independent compound.

笑話 is a joke: a short spoken story or remark meant to make people laugh. Mandarin xiàohuà and Cantonese siu3 waa6-2 are the ordinary Chinese words, and Japanese しょうわ is the same word, now literary, meaning an amusing tale. Korean 소화 holds the readings of the characters. Compare [[笑]] (to laugh) and [[話]] (speech).
