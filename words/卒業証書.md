## Notes
- [卒](../characters/卒.md) "soldier; graduate" + [業 (char)](../characters/業%20(char).md) "profession; business" + [証](../characters/証.md) "prove; confirm" + [書](../characters/書.md) "book; writing"

卒業証書 is a diploma: the document that certifies a student has completed a course of study. It is built from [卒業](卒業.md), "to graduate", and 証書 "certificate". Wiktionary records Japanese そつぎょうしょうしょ with the same sense. Wiktionary has no Chinese entry, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings; Chinese speakers say 畢業證書.

It is awarded at graduation, and a [学位](学位.md) is the degree it records.
