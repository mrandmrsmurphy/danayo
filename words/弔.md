## Notes
- [弔 (char)](../characters/弔%20(char).md) "condole; mourn; pity" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [弔 (char)](../characters/弔%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

弔 means to mourn or offer condolences — 弔問 ("to pay a condolence visit"), 弔辞 ("a eulogy"). The 会意 origin depicts a person bound with a corded arrow, an ideogram related to 約 ("to bind"), later specializing to this mourning sense.

Japanese ちょう, Korean 조, and Vietnamese điếu are all standard readings.
