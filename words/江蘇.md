## Notes
- [江](../characters/江.md) "river" + [蘇](../characters/蘇.md) "revive; Suzhou" — a blend of the first characters of two cities, Jiangning (modern Nanjing) and Suzhou. No stand-in relationship applies (江's own is [[大江]]; 蘇's own is [[蘇生]]). 苏 is the alias of 蘇, so 江苏 is kept as the word's alias.

江蘇 (Jiangsu) is a province of eastern China, on the Yangtze delta and the Yellow Sea coast, whose capital is [[南京]] and which includes [[蘇州]] (from memory, not re-checked). English Wiktionary gives the name as a short form of 江寧 (Jiangning, the old name of Nanjing) and 蘇州 (Suzhou).

The Japanese field holds こうそ (English Wiktionary Japanese); the Korean field holds the Sino-Korean 강소, matching the Dan'a'yo 諺文 exactly; the usual South Korean name today is 장쑤 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Giang Tô is the Sino-Vietnamese name. Cantonese gong1 sou1 is the Jyutping on English Wiktionary. The name differs from [[江西]] (Jiangxi) only in the second character. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄫㄙㄛ unique across both words and characters).

Attestation, checked 2026-10-11: English and Chinese Wiktionary (Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese sections) list only the province for this name.
