## Notes
- [窃](../characters/窃.md) "steal" + [取](../characters/取.md) "take"

窃取 means to steal. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 竊取 (alias here; Mandarin qièqǔ, Cantonese sit3 ceoi2) as "to steal; to snatch; to seize; to filch", and Japanese せっしゅ as "theft; to steal". Korean 절취 and Vietnamese thiết thủ are built from the readings of the two characters.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [窃](../characters/窃.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
