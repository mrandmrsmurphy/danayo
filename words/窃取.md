## Notes
- [窃](../characters/窃.md) "steal" + [取](../characters/取.md) "take" — 窃取 is the stand-in word for [窃](../characters/窃.md); 窃 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse.

窃取 names theft in a formal, often legal or written register — taking something illicitly, whether a physical object, information, or an abstract right (権利を窃取する, "to usurp/steal a right"; 情報窃取, "information theft, data exfiltration"). More formal than the everyday verb "to steal," closer to English "to purloin, to filch" or the legal "to unlawfully appropriate."

Japanese せっしゅ and Korean 절취 are both standard compositional Sino-readings. Vietnamese thiết thủ is likewise attested, though everyday spoken Vietnamese overwhelmingly prefers the native trộm cắp. No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄝㄊㄑㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).
