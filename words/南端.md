## Notes
- [南](../characters/南.md) "south" + [端](../characters/端.md) "end" — literally "southern end," the southernmost point or tip of something.

Mandarin nánduān, Cantonese naam4 dyun1, Japanese なんたん (nantan), and Korean 남단 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 南's and 端's own stored citations exactly. **Found and fixed two real bugs**: `korean` was stored as "낭단" — a typo of the real word 남단 — and `cantonese` was missing entirely. Also removed a self-referential `aliases: [南端]` entry, which redundantly listed the word's own characters rather than a genuine alternate form.

`kwin` is false: 端 alone diverges from Korean on its own character page, which alone is enough to make the AND-rule false; 南 is independently kwin-true. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄚㄇㄉ⺢ㄋ unique among both words and characters).
