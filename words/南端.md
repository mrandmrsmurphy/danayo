## Notes
- [南](../characters/南.md) "south" + [端](../characters/端.md) "end" — literally "southern end," the southernmost point or tip of something.

Mandarin nánduān, Cantonese naam4 dyun1, Japanese なんたん (nantan), and Korean 남단 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 南's and 端's own stored citations. 端 here has its "extremity, tip" sense rather than "beginning" or "proper," so the word names the far southern limit of a landmass, region or line — the end of a peninsula, island or road.
