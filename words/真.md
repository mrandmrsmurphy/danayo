## Notes
- [真](../characters/真%20(char).md) "true" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [真](../characters/真%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

真 means true.

Mandarin zhēn, Cantonese zan1, Japanese しん, Korean 진, and Vietnamese chân (as in 真理 chân lý, "truth") are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: literal `vietnamese: null`. Homophone of [[振]] "shake; excite; rouse" (both 注音 ㄐㄧㄋ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words.
