## Notes
- [王 (char)](../characters/王%20(char).md) "king" + [室](../characters/室.md) "room"

王室 is the royal family, the royal court. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 王室 (Cantonese wong4 sat1) as "royal family" and "royal court; imperial court; imperial household; imperial government". It lists Japanese and Korean 왕실 entries. Compare [王族](王族.md), royalty, and [王朝](王朝.md), a dynasty.
