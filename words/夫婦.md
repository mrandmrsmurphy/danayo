## Notes
- [夫](../characters/夫%20(char).md) "husband" + [婦](../characters/婦.md) "wife" — no stand-in relationship applies (夫 is its own stand-in; 婦's own is itself).

夫婦 means a married couple — husband and wife. It is one of the [[天常]] (the natural/cardinal human relationships).

Mandarin fūfù, Japanese ふうふ, Korean 부부, and Vietnamese phu phụ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Cantonese fu1 fu5 is likewise compositional (added a missing space between syllables).
