## Notes
- [九 (char)](../characters/九%20(char).md) "nine" + [万](../characters/万.md) "ten-thousand" — ninety thousand, 90,000.

Digit + 万, like [[二万]] and [[七万]]: the myriad (10,000) is the Sinitic grouping unit, so 90,000 is literally "nine myriad" and is not built through a "thousand" step as in English. Japanese uses きゅう, not く, for 九. Korean 구만 follows the Sino-Korean reading directly. Vietnamese chín vạn uses vạn, which is a live everyday numeral rather than only a classical one. Compare [[二万]] and [[七万]].
