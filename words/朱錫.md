## Notes
- [朱](../characters/朱.md) "cinnabar, vermilion" + [錫](../characters/錫.md) "tin" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 錫 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (bare 錫 cannot stand alone).

Mandarin xī and Cantonese sek3 are the real everyday words for "tin" in those languages, using bare 錫 alone rather than the two-character compound; Dan'a'yo prefixes 朱 to give the character a legitimizing compound. Japanese すず (suzu, native kun'yomi) and Vietnamese thiếc are independently attested native words for tin, unrelated in form to 錫 alone or to the 朱錫 compound. Korean 주석 is compositional (朱's 주 + 錫's 석), as in Dan'a'yo's own derivation.
