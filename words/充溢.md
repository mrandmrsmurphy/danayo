## Notes
- [充](../characters/充.md) "to fill" + [溢](../characters/溢.md) "to overflow"

充溢 is "to overflow; to be brimming; to be abundant." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 充溢 (chōngyì) is a compound of 溢 and Japanese 充溢 (じゅういつ) is a derived term of 溢 "overflow." Wiktionary has no Korean or Vietnamese entry, so `korean` 충일 is a compositional Sino-Korean reading, and `vietnamese` is left blank. Where 充満 "be filled with" stops at full, 充溢 goes past it: the vessel is so full that it spills. It is the stand-in for [溢](../characters/溢.md), which cannot appear independently. Related: [充満](充満.md) "be filled with."
