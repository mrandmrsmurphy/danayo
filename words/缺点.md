## Notes
- [缺](../characters/缺.md) "incomplete, lacking" + [点 (char)](../characters/点%20(char).md) "point" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (缺's is [[欠缺]], 点's is [[点]] itself), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

缺点 means a flaw, defect, or drawback.

Mandarin quēdiǎn, Cantonese kyut3 dim2, Japanese けってん (ketten), and Korean 결점 (gyeoljeom) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 缺点 is itself a common attested word for "flaw, defect" in all four languages. Vietnamese khuyết điểm is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
