## Notes
- [怪](../characters/怪.md) "strange, suspicious" + [獣](../characters/獣.md) "animal, beast, mammal" — no stand-in relationship applies (怪's own stand-in is [[怪異]]; 獣's own is [[野獣]]).

怪獣 means titan or monster — a giant creature, especially in the kaiju genre of Japanese media.

Mandarin guàishòu, Cantonese gwaai3 sau3, Korean 괴수, and Vietnamese quái thú are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `japanese` had archaic pre-reform kana くわいじう — modernized to かいじゅう. Reformatted `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⺢ㄧㄙ⼜ unique across both words and characters).
