## Notes
- [太](../characters/太%20(char).md) "great" + [陽](../characters/陽%20(char).md) "sun, yang" + [系](../characters/系.md) "system" — no stand-in relationship applies (太/陽 are both their own stand-ins; 系's own is [[系統]]).

太陽系 means the solar system — literally "great sun's system," the star [[太陽]] and everything gravitationally bound to it.

Mandarin tàiyángxì, Cantonese taai3 joeng4 hai6, Korean 태양계, and Japanese たいようけい are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese Hệ Mặt Trời ("System of the Sun") is the standard, officially recognized native name (fixed a typo, "Thệ"→"Hệ"). **Fixed**: `pos` was blank, `mandarin` was wrongly capitalized as a proper noun — corrected to 名詞/lowercase, matching the parallel astronomical common nouns [[太陽]] and [[恒星系]]. Omitted blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄚㄧ·⼘ㄫㄏㄝㄧ unique across both words and characters).
