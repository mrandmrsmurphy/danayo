## Notes
- [瞻](../characters/瞻.md) "look with reverence" + [仰](../characters/仰.md) "look up to; admire" — 瞻's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 仰's own is [[仰望]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 瞻 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

瞻仰 means to look upon with solemn reverence — most often used of visiting and paying respects at a memorial, shrine, or the remains of a revered figure (瞻仰遺容, "to pay one's last respects to the deceased"; 瞻仰毛主席紀念堂, describing visits to the Chairman Mao Memorial Hall). It carries a heavier, more ceremonial register than ordinary "to look at" or "to admire."

Mandarin zhānyǎng, Cantonese zim1 joeng5, Japanese せんぎょう (sengyō), Korean 첨앙 (cheomang), and Vietnamese chiêm ngưỡng are all fully compositional and retain this solemn, reverential register consistently across the sphere.
