## Notes
- [貴](../characters/貴.md) "expensive, valuable" + [陽](../characters/陽%20(char).md) "shine" — "precious sunny side". No stand-in relationship applies (貴's is [[貴重]]; 陽 is its own stand-in).

貴陽 (Guiyang) is the capital of Guizhou province in southwestern China. English Wiktionary also lists the former Guiyang commandery of the late imperial period as a historical sense. 贵 and 阳 are aliases of 貴 and 陽, so 贵阳 is kept as the word's alias.

Japanese きよう is the Sino-Japanese reading listed on Wiktionary Japanese; Korean 귀양 is the North Korean reading derived from the character pages (English Wiktionary has no Korean entry for 貴陽); the usual South Korean name today is 구이양 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Quý Dương is the Sino-Vietnamese name listed on Wiktionary. The Korean spelling 귀양 also coincides with a native Korean word meaning "exile" (from memory, not re-checked), which is a different word. Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese, Japanese and Vietnamese entries. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄨㄧ⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
