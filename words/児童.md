## Notes
- [児 (char)](../characters/児%20(char).md) "child" + [童](../characters/童.md) "child, servant" — "minor, student, child" (児童文学, "children's literature"; 児童福祉, "child welfare"). Mandarin értóng, Cantonese ji4 tung4, Japanese じどう, Korean 아동, and Vietnamese nhi đồng are all directly attested standard readings; the native Korean 어린이 (eorini) is also very common in everyday speech alongside the Sino-Korean 아동. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 에동 diverges from Sino-Korean 아동 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄝㄧㄉㄛㄫ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 童's own `stand_in` is this exact word (児童), so 童 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 児童.
