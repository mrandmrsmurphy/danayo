## Notes
- [悪](characters/悪%20(char).md) "evil" + [鬼](characters/鬼.md) "demon" — an evil spirit or malevolent demon, distinct from a plain [[鬼]] (which can be a morally neutral ghost/spirit) by the added 悪 ("evil, bad").

Mandarin èguǐ and Cantonese ok3 gwai2 preserve the sense directly. Japanese あっき (akki) is standard vocabulary in both religious/folkloric and figurative contexts (used of cruel or merciless people, as well as literal demons). Korean 악귀 (akgwi) parallels the Sino-xenic reading and is likewise everyday vocabulary — 악귀를 쫓다 ("to exorcise an evil spirit"). Vietnamese ác quỷ is the standard Hán Việt reading, equally common in everyday speech and folklore.

kwin is true: both [[悪]] (char) and [[鬼]] are individually kwin, satisfying the AND-rule for the compound.
