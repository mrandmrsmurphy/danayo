## Notes
- [回](../characters/回%20(char).md) "return, turn" + [生](../characters/生.md) "life" — no stand-in relationship applies (回 is its own stand-in; 生's own is [[生活]]).

回生 means to resurrect, resuscitate, or regenerate — bringing something back to life.

Japanese かいせい, Korean 회생, and Vietnamese hồi sinh are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄛㄧㄙㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
