## Notes
- [脱](../characters/脱%20(char).md) "undress" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 脱 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

脱 means to shed, remove, or undress, as well as to escape, as in 脱稿 (to complete a manuscript, literally "escape the draft").

Mandarin tuō and cantonese tyut3 are standard, fully compositional readings. **Found and fixed two real bugs**: `korean` held the literal string `"null"` instead of the character's own real reading 탈; `vietnamese` was likewise `null`, filled with thoát (one of five stored candidates). Japanese uses the native kun-reading ぬぐ (nugu, "to take off") rather than the on-readings ダツ/タイ/エツ (DATSU/TAI/ETSU) for the bare character alone. No homophones. Fixed a missing "(stand-in for 脱)" self-citation entirely absent from the character page's own Words list.
