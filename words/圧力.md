## Notes
- [圧](../characters/圧%20(char).md) "press, pressure" + [力](../characters/力%20(char).md) "force" — "pressing force," pressure. Their own `stand_in` values: 圧's is itself; 力's is itself; none points here, so 圧力 is an independent compound.

圧力 is pressure: the force pressing on a surface, per unit of area, and by extension pressure on a person. Mandarin yālì (壓力), Cantonese aat3 lik6, Japanese あつりょく and Korean 압력 all use it, and Vietnamese áp lực does too. Compare [[圧]] (to press) and [[力]] (force).
