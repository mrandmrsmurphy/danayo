## Notes
- [窺](../characters/窺%20(char).md) "spy on" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 窺 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

窺 means to peek, spy, or secretly observe — originally to peer through a small opening or crack, extended to covert observation generally (窺視, "to peep, spy on"; 窺見, "to catch a glimpse of, discern").

Mandarin kuī, Cantonese kwai1, and Korean 규 (gyu) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading うかがう (ukagau, "to watch for a chance, peep") rather than the on-reading キ (KI) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese khuy is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading.
