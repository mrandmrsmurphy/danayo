## Notes
- [消 (char)](../characters/消%20(char).md) "extinguish, dissolve" + [防](../characters/防.md) "prevent, defend" + [局 (char)](../characters/局%20(char).md) "bureau, department" — fire department; the institutional body responsible for firefighting and fire prevention

Wiktionary: Chinese 消防局 "fire department; fire station"; Japanese 消防局 "fire department". 消防 is glossed in Chinese as "to prevent and fight fire", in Japanese as "firefighting" and short for 消防署 "fire station, fire department", and 소방 as its Korean hanja form. 局 is the same bureau element as in [[郵便局]] and [[宇宙局]].
