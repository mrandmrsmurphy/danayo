## Notes
- [壁 (char)](../characters/壁%20(char).md) "wall" + [虱 (char)](../characters/虱%20(char).md) "louse" — tick, mite, bedbug, so named because these pests are often found on walls. Attested across Mandarin, Cantonese, and Japanese (壁蝨/だに). Filled blank `korean: 벽슬` and `vietnamese: bích sắt`, both compositional from each character's own confirmed reading; neither is independently confirmed as an established word — everyday Korean says 진드기 and Vietnamese says con ve/con mạt (both native) for these pests instead (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 벅솓 diverges from the compositional Sino-Korean 벽슬 in both syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄝㄎㄙㄛㄊ unique among both words and characters).
