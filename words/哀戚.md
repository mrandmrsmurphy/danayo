## Notes
- [哀](../characters/哀.md) "sad, mournful" + [戚](../characters/戚.md) "grieving, sorrowful" — 哀戚 is the stand-in word for [戚](../characters/戚.md); 戚 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse.

哀戚 means full of grief and sorrow: deep, visible mourning, in a formal, literary register. English Wiktionary lists it only for Chinese, as 哀戚 (alias 哀慼; Mandarin āiqī, Cantonese oi1 cik1), labelled literary and glossed "full of grief and sorrow". It has no Japanese, Korean or Vietnamese entry, so Japanese あいせき, Korean 애척 and Vietnamese ai thích here are built from the characters.

It doubles two grief-words, 哀, sad, and 戚, grieving, and is close to [[哀傷]], which Wiktionary glosses "grieved; distraught; distressed" for Chinese and "grief; sorrow" for Japanese. 戚 also means a relative in other compounds, such as [[親戚]], "relatives", a separate sense of the same character.
