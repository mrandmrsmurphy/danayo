## Notes
- [敢](../characters/敢.md) "dare" + [為](../characters/為%20(char).md) "do" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [敢](../characters/敢.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (為's own stand-in is itself).

敢為 means to dare to do.

Mandarin gǎnwéi, Cantonese gam2 wai4, Japanese かんい, Korean 감위, and Vietnamese dám vi are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄇㄨㄧ unique across both words and characters).
