## Notes
- [懇](../characters/懇.md) "sincere, earnest" + [切](../characters/切%20(char).md) "cut, mince, carve" — this word is 懇's own stand-in (切's own is itself).

懇切 means sincere or earnest — heartfelt and pressing.

Mandarin kěnqiè, Cantonese han2 cit3, Japanese こんせつ, Korean 간절, and Vietnamese khẩn thiết are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄚㄋㄑㄝㄊ unique across both words and characters).
