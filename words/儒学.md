## Notes
- [儒](../characters/儒.md) "Confucian scholar" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study" — "Confucianism," the school of thought founded on the teachings of Confucius. Mandarin rúxué, Cantonese jyu4 hok6, Japanese じゅがく, Korean 유학, and Vietnamese nho học are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 누학 diverges from Sino-Korean 유학 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄨㄏㄚㄎ unique among both words and characters).

**Real content bug found and fixed**: `aliases` had included [[儒教]] and [[孔教]] — both real, separately-attested Confucianism-related terms with their own dedicated vault pages, not spelling variants of 儒学 itself — removed, keeping only the genuine traditional-character form 儒學.

Emphasizes the core Confucian virtues: [[仁徳]], [[意義]], [[礼]], [[智慧]] (corrected from a mismatched link that displayed "智" but pointed to the unrelated word [[知]], "to know"), and [[信任]].
