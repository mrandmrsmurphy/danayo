## Notes
- [儒](../characters/儒.md) "Confucian scholar" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study" — "Confucianism," the school of thought founded on the teachings of Confucius. Mandarin rúxué, Cantonese jyu4 hok6, Japanese じゅがく, Korean 유학, and Vietnamese nho học are all directly attested standard readings.

Emphasizes the core Confucian virtues: [[仁徳]], [[意義]], [[礼]], [[智慧]], and [[信任]].
