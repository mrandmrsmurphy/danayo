## Notes
- [毎](../characters/毎%20(char).md) "each" + [月](../characters/月%20(char).md) "moon; month" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 毎's and 月's own stand-ins are themselves, each with its own independent word page).

毎月 means every month.

Mandarin měiyuè, Cantonese mui5 jyut6, and Japanese まいげつ are all standard, fully compositional readings.
