## Notes
- [毎](../characters/毎%20(char).md) "each" + [月](../characters/月%20(char).md) "moon; month" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 毎's and 月's own stand-ins are themselves, each with its own independent word page).

毎月 means every month.

Mandarin měiyuè, Cantonese mui5 jyut6, and Japanese まいげつ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `korean` was a garbled comma-joined pair (매달,매월) mixing a native descriptive form (매달, using native 달 "month") with the compositional Sino form — corrected to the single form 매월, matching 月's own citation. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field (mỗi nguyệt), added `kwin: false` (諺文 뫼웓 vs Korean 매월, the same 月-coda divergence documented on [[月]]'s own page and on [[来月]]).
