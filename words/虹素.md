## Notes
- backformation from the word [[虹霓]] - neon, instead of the Chinese novel character 氖

No cranberry (虹's own stand-in is [[彩虹]], 素's is [[要素]]). Periodic-table neologism (neon). Mandarin nǎi/cantonese naai5 correctly track the real modern element character 氖 (per this page's own Notes); korean 네온, japanese ネオン, and vietnamese nê-ông are all genuine phonetic transliterations.
