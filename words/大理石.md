## Notes
- [大](../characters/大%20(char).md) "great" + [理](../characters/理.md) "grain, reason" + [石](../characters/石%20(char).md) "stone" — "Dali stone," marble. Their own `stand_in` values: 大's is itself; 理's is [[理由]]; 石's is itself; none points here, so 大理石 is an independent compound.

大理石 is marble: the polished, patterned limestone used for floors, statues and counters. Mandarin dàlǐshí, Cantonese daai6 lei5 sek6, Japanese だいりせき and Korean 대리석 all use it. The name comes from Dali (大理) in Yunnan, famous for its marble. Compare [[石]] (stone) and [[石灰]] (lime).
