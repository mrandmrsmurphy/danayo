## Notes
- [一 (char)](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" + [同](../characters/同.md) "same"

一同 is "together; all as one," and as a noun "everyone present; all of them." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 一同 (yītóng, Cantonese jat1 tung4) is an adverb "together; at the same time and place," and Wiktionary lists its CJKV descendants as Japanese いちどう, Korean 일동 and Vietnamese nhất đồng. Japanese いちどう is a noun "all (present); everyone," as in 一同を驚かす "to surprise everyone present," and Korean 일동 is an adverb "all; everyone." For Vietnamese, hvdic.thivien.net lists 一同 as nhất đồng, "cùng nhau, đồng lòng" ("together, of one mind"), attested in classical Hán Nôm poetry. Because 一 is "one" and 同 is "same," the compound is literally "one-same," several people acting as a single body; Chinese leans on the adverb, Japanese and Korean on the noun, and Dan'a'yo keeps both.

It is used in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [平臥即是正義](../translation/平臥即是正義.md), standing in for the Chinese 一起 "together," which is a colloquial word with no Sino-Xenic counterpart elsewhere. 一同 is the more formal register and is the usual literary equivalent of 一起 in Chinese too. It differs from [一処](一処.md) "in one place; together," which stresses sharing a location; from [偕同](偕同.md) "together with," an elevated word that takes an accompanied party; from [同時](同時.md) "simultaneous," which is about time; and from [一斉](一斉.md) "simultaneous; all at once," which is about acting in unison. Related: [共同](共同.md) "cooperate; collaborate" and [同一](同一.md) "same; identical."
