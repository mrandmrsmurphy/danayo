## Notes
- [笠](../characters/笠.md) "bamboo hat" + [帽](../characters/帽.md) "cap"
- This word is the viable form of [笠](../characters/笠.md): the character's meaning (bamboo hat) is identical, but 笠 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 笠帽 is its `stand_in` compound.

笠 is a broad, shallow rain-and-sun hat woven of bamboo strips, bamboo leaves, straw or sedge, and 帽 "cap, hat" gives the generic category, so 笠帽 is "a hat of the 笠 type," the wide conical or domed field hat worn by farmers, boatmen and travelers across the rice-growing parts of East and Southeast Asia. It is paired in older Chinese with the rain cape, 蓑笠 (a straw raincoat and hat), the emblem of the humble working figure or the wandering recluse in poetry and painting. Compare the general [帽子](帽子.md) "hat, cap," which names headwear of any kind.

The languages keep native words for the same object: Japanese かさ (kasa), Korean 삿갓 (satgat), Vietnamese nón lá. The Sino-readings are Mandarin lìmào, Cantonese lap1 mou6 and Korean 립모 (the North Korean form, keeping initial 립). Dan'a'yo keeps the sense "conical bamboo hat, straw hat" with no divergence. It is placed under [Clothing](../lexipedia/Clothing.md).
