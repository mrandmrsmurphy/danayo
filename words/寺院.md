## Notes
- [寺](../characters/寺.md) "temple (Buddhist)" + [院](../characters/院.md) "institution; courtyard; public building" — no stand-in relationship applies (寺's own `stand_in` is [[寺刹]], 院's is [[院落]]).

寺院 is the general word for a Buddhist temple or monastery complex, pairing 寺 (the specifically Buddhist "temple" sense, distinct from 廟, used for ancestral/folk-deity shrines) with 院's broader "institution, walled precinct" sense — reading literally as "the temple institution/precinct." 院 is independently one of the most productive characters in this vault's compound-word inventory well beyond religious contexts (病院 "hospital," 学院 "academy," 修道院 "monastery/convent" — itself a near-synonym of this word — 衆議院 "House of Representatives"), all sharing the underlying sense of a bounded institutional compound.

Japanese じいん, Korean 사원, and Vietnamese tự viện are all standard, non-drifted readings (all Wiktionary-verified). No HSK level is given for the compound.
