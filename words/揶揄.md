## Notes
- [揶](../characters/揶.md) "deride" + [揄](../characters/揄.md) "deride" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes BOTH characters as independent Dan'a'yo entries: both 揶's and 揄's own `stand_in` is 揶揄.

揶揄 means to deride or ridicule.

Mandarin yéyú, Cantonese je4 jyu4, Japanese やゆ, Korean 야유, and Vietnamese da du (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` had dropped 揄's own coda (윰/yum, from Middle Chinese) — corrected 야유/'ya'yu/⼘·⼜ to 야윰/'ya'yum/⼘·⼜ㄇ. No homophones at the corrected reading.
