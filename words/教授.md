## Notes
- [教](../characters/教.md) "teach" + [授](../characters/授.md) "confer" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 教 (授's own stand-in is [[授与]]).

教授 means to teach, or (as a noun) a professor.

Mandarin jiàoshòu, Cantonese gaau3 sau6, Japanese きょうじゅ, Korean 교수, and Vietnamese giáo thụ are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Retroactive fix**: `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` had all used the old minority "gyau" reading for 教 instead of the majority-established "gyou" (same bug found on [[教会]]/[[教員]]) — corrected. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼄ㄨㄙ⼜ unique across both words and characters).
