## Notes
- [勺 (char)](../characters/勺%20(char).md) "spoon; ladle"

勺 is 象形, depicting a spoon or ladle with a handle and a rounded bowl. It names both the utensil itself ([[叉勺]], "spork," a modern coinage) and, in classical measurement, a small unit of volume — ten 勺 make one 合 (gě), itself one-tenth of a 升 (shēng).

Mandarin sháo, Cantonese zoek3, and Japanese しゃく (shaku, on'yomi, rare outside classical/formal contexts) all preserve the sense. Korean 작 (jak) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 구기 (gugi, "a small ladle"). Vietnamese chước is the Hán Việt reading, covering both the utensil and the volume-measure senses; the character page's other three stored candidates (duộc, giuộc, thược) are unrelated and look like corpus noise.

This word shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading (jwag/좍/ㄐ⺢ㄎ) with [[着]] ("wear").
