## Notes
- [受](../characters/受.md) "to receive" + [託](../characters/託.md) "to entrust, commission"

受託 means "to be entrusted with [a task, responsibility, or assets]; to accept a commission" — the recipient's side of an act of [[嘱託]] (cross-linked from [[託]]'s own Words section, "to commission, to entrust"). 託, a 形声 character with the speech radical 言 as its semantic part and 乇 as its phonetic (as the 託 character page records), means "to entrust, to commission, to rely on" — its aliases 讬/托 reflecting centuries of script simplification around the same root meaning.

受託 belongs to a family of 受-compounds that name the receiving of something: [[受賞]] (receiving an honor), [[受難]] (receiving suffering), [[受理]] (receiving a document for processing), [[受胎]] (receiving new life), and 受託 (receiving a charge or commission). In financial contexts, 受託 underlies words like 受託者 ("trustee") and 信託 ("trust, as in a financial trust") — wherever one party formally takes on responsibility for assets or duties handed over by another, 受託 names that party's side of the transaction.

It is the receiving counterpart of [委託](委託.md) "to entrust, consign" (the giving side) and of [嘱託](嘱託.md) "to commission, entrust," which the Government page also lists. The readings are Mandarin shòutuō, Cantonese sau6 tok3, Japanese jutaku (じゅたく), Korean 수탁 and Sino-Vietnamese thụ thác. Dan'a'yo keeps the sense "to be entrusted with, to accept a commission."
