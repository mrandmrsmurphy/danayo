## Notes
- [曽 (char)](../characters/曽%20(char).md) "great- (a generation further)" + [孫](../characters/孫.md) "grandchild" — great-grandchild.

曽孫 is a great-grandchild: the child of one's grandchild. It is "great-grandchild" from [[曽]] and [[孫]]. Mandarin zēngsūn (formal; spoken varieties use 重孫), Cantonese zang1 syun1, Japanese そうそん (also ひまご), Korean 증손 and Vietnamese tằng tôn are all confirmed on Wiktionary. It sits on the descending line below [[孫子]] and [[孫女]]. Its ascending counterparts are [[曽祖父]] and [[曽祖母]].
