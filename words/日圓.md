## Notes
- [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "Japan, sun" + [圓](../characters/圓%20(char).md) "yen, round" — "Japanese round," the yen. Their own `stand_in` values: 日's is itself; 圓's is itself; none points here, so 日圓 is an independent compound.

日圓 is the yen, the currency of Japan. Mandarin rìyuán and Cantonese jat6 jyun4 are the ordinary Chinese words; the unit's name is 圓 "round," the same character as in [[欧圓]] (euro). Japanese writes it 円 and says えん, and Korean 엔, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[美圓]] (dollar) and [[韓圓]] (won).
