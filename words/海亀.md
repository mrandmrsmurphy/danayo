## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [亀 (char)](../characters/亀%20(char).md) "turtle" — no stand-in relationship applies (海's own stand-in is [[海洋]]; 亀's own is itself).

海亀 means sea turtle.

Mandarin hǎiguī, Cantonese hoi2 gwai1, and Korean 해구 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 海's and 亀's own stored citations.
