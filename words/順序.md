## Notes
- [順](../characters/順.md) "in order, smooth" + [序](../characters/序.md) "series, sequence" — 順序 is 序's own legitimizing compound (序's `stand_in` points here), but since 順's own `stand_in` is [[順次]] instead, transitivity fails and no `#cranberry` tag applies.

順序 (shùnxù) is the standard Mandarin word for "sequence, order," and Cantonese 順序 (seon6zeoi6) likewise. Japanese じゅんじょ (junjo) and Korean 순서 (sunseo) are both standard terms in their languages, as is Sino-Vietnamese thuận tự (alongside the more everyday native thứ tự) — a clean case with full agreement across all five languages.
