## Notes
- [隷](../characters/隷.md) "clerk" + [書](../characters/書.md) "writing; script"

隷書 is clerical script: the Han-dynasty style of squat, flat-topped characters. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin lìshū as "clerical script" and Japanese れいしょ as "clerical script". Korean 례서 and Vietnamese lệ thư are the same word. Cantonese dai6 syu1 matches.

It came from the [篆書](篆書.md), seal script, and led to the [楷書](楷書.md), regular script.
