## Notes
- [郡 (char)](../characters/郡%20(char).md) "county, prefecture, district, commandery"

郡 is a sub-national administrative division: in imperial China, a 郡 ("commandery") was a major territorial unit predating and eventually giving way to the 省 (province) system, though the character survives in historical terminology and place names rather than active modern Chinese administration. Japanese ぐん (gun) remains a formally defined administrative unit — a rural district grouping several towns (町) and villages (村) below the prefecture (都道府県) level — though it has become largely a geographic label since municipal authority now rests directly with the towns and villages themselves. Korean 군 (gun) is an active administrative unit in both South and North Korea, denoting a county-level rural district outside of cities (시, si). Vietnamese quận diverges most sharply: it denotes an urban district specifically, the subdivision used within cities like Hà Nội and Thành phố Hồ Chí Minh (Quận 1, Quận 3, etc.), with the separate term huyện covering rural districts — the reverse of the Sino-Japanese-Korean association of 郡/gun with rural, non-metropolitan territory.

This word is a genuine Dan'a'yo homophone with [[君]] ("you") — both read 군/ㄍㄨㄋ, coincidental and unrelated in meaning.
