## Notes
"Buddhist monk" (formal/written register). `vietnamese: tăng lữ` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese (confirmed via web search), broader than tăng alone and encompassing monks/nuns of the sangha generally.

**Real content bug found and fixed**: `korean` had two extra native glosses ("중, 스님," both native Korean for "monk") mixed into the reading field alongside the correct 승려 — trimmed to 승려 alone. Filled the blank `pos: 名詞`.

**Second bug found 2026-09-29**: `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` had a stale "sung"-pattern onset for 僧's syllable (숭/sung/ㄙㄨㄫ) instead of 僧's own authoritative citation reading (승/sǝng/ㄙㄜㄫ, regularly derived from Middle Chinese and matching its entire phonetic family). This was previously believed to be a genuine, cross-word-consistent alternate reading shared with [[尼僧]]/[[密陀僧]]/[[僧伽]] — but re-deriving the split fresh found no semantic, positional, or register pattern explaining it (see [[僧]]'s own Notes for the full reasoning); it was simply an uncorrected error in all four. Corrected to 승료/sǝnglyo/ㄙㄜㄫㄌ⼄. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 승료 diverges entirely from Sino-Korean 승려. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄜㄫㄌ⼄ unique among both words and characters).
