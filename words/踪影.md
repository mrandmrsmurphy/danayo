## Notes
- [踪](../characters/踪.md) "footprints; tracks" + [影](../characters/影.md) "shadow; image"

踪影 is a trace, a sign. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 蹤影 (alias here; Mandarin zōngyǐng, Cantonese zung1 jing2) as "trace; sign", usually used in the negative. The Japanese そうえい and Korean 종영 fields are built from the readings of the two characters.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [踪](../characters/踪.md), which is too bounded to stand alone. The stand-in of 影 is [陰影](陰影.md).
