## Notes
- [尊](../characters/尊.md) "honor; respect" + [栄](../characters/栄.md) "glory; honorable"

尊栄 (traditional 尊榮) is "honorable and glorious," the dignity and glory that belong to a person or a people. According to Wiktionary, Chinese 尊榮 (zūnróng, Cantonese zyun1 wing4) is attested as "honorable and glorious"; Wiktionary has no Japanese, Korean or Vietnamese section, so `japanese` そんえい, `korean` 존영 and `vietnamese` tôn vinh are compositional readings of the two characters, not independently checked. It is used in the 1919 Korean Declaration of Independence ([宣言書](../translation/宣言書.md)) (正義人道生存尊榮, "justice, humanity, survival and dignity"). It is related to [尊敬](尊敬.md) "respect" and [栄辱](栄辱.md) "honor and disgrace."
