## Notes
- [幾](../characters/幾%20(char).md) "how much/many" + [何](../characters/何%20(char).md) "what" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study" — no stand-in relationship applies (幾/何 are both their own stand-ins; 学's own is [[学習]]).

幾何学 means geometry — literally "the study of how much" (a calque of Greek geōmetria via Matteo Ricci's 幾何, chosen as a phonetic-semantic approximation of "geo-").

Mandarin jǐhéxué, Cantonese gei2 ho4 hok6, Japanese きかがく, Korean 기하학, and Vietnamese hình học (native, meaning "study of shapes") are all standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄜㄏㄚㄏㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
