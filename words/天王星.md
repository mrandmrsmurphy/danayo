## Notes
- [天](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven" + [王](../characters/王%20(char).md) "king" + [星](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" — no stand-in relationship applies (天/王/星 are all their own stand-ins).

天王星 means Uranus — literally "heaven's king star."

Mandarin Tiānwángxīng, Cantonese tin1 wong4 sing1, and Korean 천왕성 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese Thiên Vương Tinh is the standard full-proper-noun astronomical name (matching [[冥王星]]'s "Diêm Vương Tinh" pattern for planets, unlike the "sao X" format used for stars). **Real bug fixed**: `japanese` had archaic pre-reform kana てんわうせい — modernized to てんおうせい (matching sibling planets [[海王星]]'s かいおうせい and [[冥王星]]'s めいおうせい). Blanked empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄝㄋ·⺢ㄫㄙㄝㄫ unique across both words and characters).
