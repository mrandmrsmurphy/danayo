## Notes
- [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "ride" + [員](../characters/員.md) "personnel, employee" — literally "riding personnel," the crew or crew members aboard a vehicle.

乗員 is closely related to [[乗務]] (the duty of serving as crew) but names the personnel themselves rather than the duty: 乗務員 (jōmuin) actually combines both elements into the single most common Japanese word for "crew member." Mandarin chéng yuán, Cantonese sing4 jyun4, and Korean 승운 (with 乗務's own 승무원 the more idiomatic derived form) parallel this sense. Vietnamese thừa viên is the direct Sino-Vietnamese compositional reading (乗's thừa + 員's viên, a well-attested SV morpheme meaning "member, personnel," as in nhân viên "staff member," thành viên "member").

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 숭운 diverges from Sino-Korean 승운 in the first syllable only — 乗's own Dan'a'yo reading 숭 doesn't match Sino-Korean 승, even though 員's own reading 운 matches exactly (both are independently `kwin: true`/`false` respectively on their character pages, giving AND = false).
