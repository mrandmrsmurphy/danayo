## Notes
- [語 (char)](../characters/語.md) "word, language" (stand-in: [[言語]]) + [末 (char)](../characters/末.md) "end" (stand-in: [[末端]]) — word-final; occurring at the end of a word.

Wiktionary lists 語末 only under Japanese: "the end of a word". Used in `文法 - 02音韻論` and `文法 - 94日本語小冊子` for word-final position, as in 語末子音. Compare [[文末]].
