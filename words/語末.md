## Notes
- [語 (char)](../characters/語.md) "word, language" (stand-in: [[言語]]) + [末 (char)](../characters/末.md) "end" (stand-in: [[末端]]) — word-final; occurring at the end of a word.

語末 means word-final, as in `文法 - 02音韻論`'s discussion of 語末子音 ("word-final consonants"). Mandarin yǔmò and Korean 어말 are both real, standard linguistics terms. Cantonese jyu5 mut6 is a straightforward compositional reading. Vietnamese ngữ mạt is compositional only, not independently verified as standard. 語's and 末's own character pages store no `japanese` field. `kwin` is false: inherited from 語 (false), despite 末 itself being true.

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot**: the concatenated string `⼄ㄇㄚㄊ` admits two legal partitions — the intended 語(⼄)+末(ㄇㄚㄊ), and a false alternate (⼄ㄇ+ㄚㄊ) that disagrees on where the first cut falls — so the dot at the true 語|末 boundary is required: ⼄·ㄇㄚㄊ.
