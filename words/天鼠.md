## Notes
- [天](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven" + [鼠](../characters/鼠.md) "rat" — no stand-in relationship applies (天 is its own stand-in; 鼠's own is [[熊鼠]]).

天鼠 means bat — literally "heaven's rat/mouse," a poetic or dialectal alternate to the standard term [[蝙蝠]], with exactly the same sense. It is distinct from [[飛鼠]] (flying squirrel).

Mandarin tiānshǔ, Cantonese tin1 syu2, Korean 천서, Japanese てんそ (the on'yomi 鼠 realized as そ, matching sibling compounds [[田鼠]] and [[老鼠]]) and Vietnamese thiên thử are all standard, fully compositional readings. They are not 蝙蝠's own readings (편복, biên bức).
