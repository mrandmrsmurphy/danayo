## Notes
- [天](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven" + [鼠](../characters/鼠.md) "rat" — no stand-in relationship applies (天 is its own stand-in; 鼠's own is [[熊鼠]]).

天鼠 means bat — literally "heaven's rat/mouse," a poetic or dialectal alternate to the standard term [[蝙蝠]]. It is distinct from [[飛鼠]] (flying squirrel).

Mandarin tiānshǔ, Cantonese tin1 syu2, Korean 천서, Japanese てんそ (SHO on'yomi realized as そ, matching sibling compounds [[田鼠]]/[[老鼠]]), and Vietnamese thiên thử are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bugs found and fixed**: `korean`/`vietnamese` had been contaminated with 蝙蝠's own real-world readings (편복,박쥐 / biên bức) — corrected to compositional 천서/thiên thử. Removed a stray zero-width character from `japanese`. Added missing `kwin: false`. Omitted blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/`aliases` keys per checklist. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄝㄋㄙ⼄ unique across both words and characters).
