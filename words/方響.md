## Notes
- [方](../characters/方.md) "square" + [響](../characters/響.md) "sound, echo" — no stand-in relationship applies (方's own stand-in is [[方向]]; 響's own is [[反響]]).

方響 (fangxiang, or hōkyō in Japanese) is a metallophone: a set of tuned metal plates or bars struck to produce pitched sound, used in traditional Chinese and Japanese court/ceremonial music.

Mandarin fāngxiǎng, Cantonese fong1 hoeng2, Japanese ほうきょう (modernized from archaic はうきやう), Korean 방향, and Vietnamese phương hưởng are all standard, fully compositional readings. Three-way Dan'a'yo-level homophone with [[方向]] "direction" and [[芳香]] "fragrant, aromatic" (all 注音 ㄈㄚㄫㄏ⼘ㄫ), fully cross-referenced on all three pages.
