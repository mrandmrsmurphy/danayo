## Notes
- [去 (char)](../characters/去%20(char).md) "go"

去 is 会意: [[大]] ("man," simplified to 土) + 口 ("mouth, opening," simplified to 厶) — a man departing from an opening; "to go, to leave." It heads a fundamental deictic/temporal vocabulary: [[去年]] ("last year," literally "the year gone by"), [[過去]] ("the past"), 逝去 ("to pass away, die," a euphemistic register), and [[辞去]] ("to take one's leave, resign and depart").

Mandarin qù, Cantonese heoi3, and Japanese きょ/こ (kyo/ko, on'yomi, largely confined to formal/literary compounds) alongside native さ(る) (sa(ru), "to leave, depart") all preserve the sense. Korean 거 (geo) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 가다 (gada, "to go," the everyday verb). Vietnamese khứ is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in quá khứ (過去, "the past," matching this word's own compound directly) and khứ hồi ("round trip," literally "go and return"); the character page's other stored candidate, khử ("to eliminate, remove"), is an unrelated word and was not used here.

This word shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading (kyo/쿄/ㄎ⼄) with another word still awaiting its own turn in this sweep, [[矩]] ("moment [physics]") — the reciprocal half of this callout will be completed when it comes up.
