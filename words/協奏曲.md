## Notes
- [協](../characters/協.md) "cooperate" + [奏](../characters/奏.md) "play (music)" + [曲](../characters/曲.md) "tune"

協奏曲 is a concerto: a work in which a solo instrument plays with an orchestra. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xiézòuqǔ as "concerto" and Japanese きょうそうきょく as "concerto". Korean 협주곡 is the same word. Cantonese hip3 zau3 kuk1 matches.

The soloist plays the [独奏](独奏.md) part against the [管弦楽](管弦楽.md), orchestra, in a form close to the [交響曲](交響曲.md), symphony.
