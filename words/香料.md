## Notes
- [香](../characters/香.md) "fragrant" + [料](../characters/料.md) "material"

香料 is spice: an aromatic substance used to flavor food, and in a wider sense a perfume. Wiktionary glosses both Mandarin xiāngliào and Japanese こうりょう as "spice; perfume". Korean 향료, Vietnamese hương liệu and Cantonese hoeng1 liu6-2 match.

It covers the pungent and aromatic seasonings such as [胡椒](胡椒.md), pepper, [丁香](丁香.md), clove, [肉桂](肉桂.md), cinnamon, and [八角](八角.md), star anise. Seasoning in general is [調味料](調味料.md).
