## Notes
- [感](../characters/感.md) "feel, sense" + [嘆](../characters/嘆%20(char).md) "moan, sigh" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "word, expression" — no stand-in relationship applies (感's own stand-in is [[感触]]; 嘆's own is itself; 詞's own is [[単詞]]).

感嘆詞 means interjection (the grammatical part of speech) — a word expressing sudden emotion. Synonymous with 嘆詞/叹词, 間投詞, and 感動詞, none of which are orthographic variants of this specific compound.

Mandarin gǎntàncí, Cantonese gam2 taan3 ci4, Japanese かんたんし, Korean 감탄사, and Vietnamese thán từ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Removed the redundant self-referential alias and the distinct-character synonyms (嘆詞/叹词, 欹, 間投詞, 感動詞) from `aliases`, keeping only the genuine simplified spelling. Fixed missing "(char)" suffix on `characters`. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄇㄊㄚㄋㄙㄚ unique across both words and characters).
