## Notes
- [午](../characters/午.md) "noon" + [後](../characters/後%20(char).md) "after" — no stand-in relationship applies (午's own stand-in is [[正午]]; 後's own is itself).

午後 means "after noon," i.e. p.m. or afternoon.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `mandarin`/`cantonese` had been contaminated with the reading of the alias 下午 (xiàwǔ/haa6 ng5) instead of 午後's own compositional reading — corrected to wǔhòu/ng5 hau6. `pos` was blank — filled as 名詞. Japanese ごご and Korean 오후 are both standard readings. Vietnamese `buổi chiều` is a native paraphrase rather than a direct Sino-Vietnamese calque (Vietnamese does not commonly compound this term the same way, matching [[午前]]'s buổi sáng).

Dan'a'yo `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` are the plain concatenation of 午's and 後's own citations (*ohuo*/오훗/ㄛㄏㄨㄛ); 後 is read the same way here as in the other "after X" temporal words ([[以後]], [[最後]]) and in [[後裔]] and [[然後]]. No homophones (注音 ㄛㄏㄨㄛ unique across both words and characters).
