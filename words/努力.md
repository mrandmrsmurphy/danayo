## Notes
- [努](../characters/努.md) "endeavor, make an effort" + [力 (char)](../characters/力%20(char).md) "power, strength" — 努's own `stand_in` points to this word (努力), meaning 努 is a bound morpheme that never appears independently outside this compound.

Mandarin nǔlì, Cantonese nou5 lik6, Japanese どりょく (doryoku), and Korean 노력 (noryeok) are all standard, everyday words for "effort; strive." Vietnamese nỗ lực is likewise a common Sino-Vietnamese word, a clean match with no register narrowing.
