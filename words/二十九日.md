## Notes
- [[二十]] "twenty" + [九 (char)](../characters/九%20(char).md) "nine" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — the twenty-ninth day of the month.

Mandarin èrshíjiǔ rì, Cantonese ji6 sap6 gau2 jat6, Japanese にじゅうくにち, and Korean 이십구일 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese filled with ngày hai mươi chín (native "day twenty-nine"), matching the same native-calendar-date convention already established on [[二十一日]]/[[二十七日]]/[[二十三日]]/[[二十]] itself.
