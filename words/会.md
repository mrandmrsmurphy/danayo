## Notes
- [会 (char)](../characters/会%20(char).md) "meet; gather"

会 is 会意: originally 亼 ("to converge, come together") stacked over an abbreviated 曾 (a tiered steamer basket, with 曰 omitted) — a lid fitting together with its vessel, extended to "to meet, to join, to assemble." 会 is the Japanese Jōyō-simplified form of 會. It heads an enormous compound family spanning both concrete gatherings ([[宴会]] "banquet," [[会議]] "meeting, conference," [[会堂]] "meeting hall, church") and abstract social organization ([[社会]] "society," [[協会]] "association," [[会社]] "company").

Mandarin huì, Cantonese wui6, and Japanese かい/え (kai/e, on'yomi, fully productive throughout the compounds above) alongside native あ(う) (a(u), "to meet") all preserve the core sense. Korean 회 (hoe) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese hội is the everyday Hán Việt reading (as in xã hội 社会 "society," hội nghị "meeting, conference"); hụi is a distinct, specifically Vietnamese loanword sense referring to a rotating informal credit/savings circle (chơi hụi, "to participate in a hụi") — both are genuinely attested, not a corpus-noise contamination case like several other characters in this sweep.
