## Notes
- [報 (char)](../characters/報%20(char).md) "repay" + [復](../characters/復.md) "return"

報復 is retaliation: to strike back for a blow received. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin bàofù as "to retaliate; to take revenge" and Japanese ほうふく as "revenge; retribution; retaliation". Korean 보복 and Cantonese bou3 fuk6 match.

It answers a particular act, often between states or groups, where [復讐](復讐.md) is the personal vengeance for a wrong.
