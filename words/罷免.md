## Notes
- [罷](../characters/罷.md) "quit" + [免](../characters/免.md) "excuse" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (罷's is [[罷官]], 免's is [[免除]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 罷 is individually `kwin: false`.

罷免 means to dismiss, discharge, or recall from office.

Mandarin bàmiǎn, Cantonese baa6 min5, Japanese ひめん (himen), and Korean 파면 (pamyeon) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 罷免 is itself a common attested word for "dismiss, discharge" in all four languages. Vietnamese bãi miễn is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
