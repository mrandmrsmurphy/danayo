## Notes
- [徳](../characters/徳%20(char).md) "virtue" + [国](../characters/国.md) "country" — no stand-in relationship applies (徳's own stand-in is itself; 国's own is [[国家]]).

徳国 means Germany — a compositional Chinese-style calque name for the country.

Mandarin Déguó and Cantonese dak1 gwok3 are standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese ドイツ, Korean 독일, and Vietnamese Đức are the standard real-world native names for Germany (loanwords/short forms), rather than compositional readings of these specific characters. **Real bug found and fixed**: `mandarin` had 徳国's cantonese reading erroneously appended after a semicolon, leaving `cantonese` blank — split apart correctly. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄨㄎㄍㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
