## Notes
- [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "multiply" + [算 (char)](../characters/算%20(char).md) "calculate" — literally "multiply-calculate," the operation of multiplication.

乗算 is the standard mathematical/computing term for multiplication as an operation, alongside its sibling operations 加算 (addition), 減算 (subtraction), and 除算 (division). Mandarin chéng suàn, Cantonese sing4 syun3, Japanese じょうさん, and Korean 승산 are all standard, fully compositional terms. **Vietnamese content note**: the real, everyday Vietnamese term for the multiplication operation is phép nhân (literally "the multiply operation," built on the native/SV verb nhân "to multiply") rather than a compositional calque of this word's own characters (乗's thừa + 算's toán, which isn't independently attested as a fixed term) — filled here per the vault's real-usage-over-compositional convention.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 숭솬 diverges from Sino-Korean 승산, since both 乗 and 算 independently carry `kwin: false` on their own character pages.
