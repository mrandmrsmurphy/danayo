## Notes
- [憤](../characters/憤.md) "indignation" + [満 (char)](../characters/満%20(char).md) "full; fill"

憤満 is pent-up anger: resentment and indignation that fill a person and cannot find release. The real-world spelling is 憤懣, which is an alias; 懣 is an alias of 満 in this vault, so the parent form is used here. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin fènmèn as "resentful; discontented; indignant; furious" and Japanese ふんまん as "resentment, indignation; to resent". Korean 분만 and Cantonese fan5 mun6 match.

It is anger that has been held in, where [憤怒](憤怒.md) is anger shown, and it is close to [憤慨](憤慨.md), indignation, and [怨恨](怨恨.md), a grudge.
