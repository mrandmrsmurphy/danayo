## Notes
- [占](../characters/占%20(char).md) "occupy" + [拠](../characters/拠.md) "depend upon" — no stand-in relationship applies (占 is its own stand-in; 拠's own is [[依拠]]).

占拠 means to occupy or hold — seizing and controlling a place by force.

Japanese せんきょ, Korean 점거, and Vietnamese chiếm cứ are all standard, fully compositional readings. `cantonese: zim3 geoi3` uses zim3 rather than 占's own citation tone zim1 — a real alternate Cantonese tone attested for 占 in its "occupy/seize" sense (as in 占領 zim3 ling5), distinct from the "divination" sense that zim1 covers, kept as-is rather than corrected. Fixed `characters` citation "占" → "占 (char)"; fixed unquoted `hsk_level`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄇㄍ⼄ unique across both words and characters).
