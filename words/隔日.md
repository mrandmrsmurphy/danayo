## Notes
- [隔 (char)](../characters/隔%20(char).md) "every other" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day"

隔日 means every other day, occurring once every two days — the same "every other" pattern as [[隔世紀]] and [[隔年]], both built on the same 隔- prefix. Mandarin 隔日 (gérì), Cantonese 隔日 (gaak3jat6), Japanese 隔日 (かくじつ), Korean 격일 (gyeogil), and Sino-Vietnamese cách nhật all preserve this meaning compositionally — cách nhật in particular remains a standard term in traditional/clinical medicine (e.g. "sốt cách nhật," "intermittent fever occurring every other day").
