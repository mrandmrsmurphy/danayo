## Notes
- [信](../characters/信.md) "trust, message" + [号](../characters/号.md) "signal, mark" — "message mark," a signal. Their own `stand_in` values: 信's is [[信任]]; 号's is [[符号]]; none points here, so 信号 is an independent compound.

信号 is a signal: a sign, sound or light that conveys a message, and the traffic light. Mandarin xìnhào (信號), Cantonese seon3 hou6, Japanese しんごう and Korean 신호 all use it, and Vietnamese tín hiệu does too. Compare [[消息]] (message) and [[符号]] (symbol).
