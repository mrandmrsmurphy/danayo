## Notes
- [左](../characters/左.md) "left" + [学](../characters/学.md) "learn" — the Left School, the lower of a pair of state schools recorded in Shang-dynasty education and described in the *Book of Rites* (禮記·王制): "殷人養國老於右學，養庶老於左學" ("the Yin [Shang] people cared for their state elders at the Right School, and their common elders at the Left School"). 左學 trained and housed commoners (庶老) rather than the hereditary elite, making it the functional predecessor of the later [[小学]] tier of education, while its counterpart [[右学]] anticipated [[太学]].

An earlier version of this page cited the Shiji for a record of the minister Fu Yue inspecting both schools in the sixth year of King Wu Ding's reign. That citation could not be verified: a search found no such passage, and the Elders-and-schools material belongs to the ritual texts (the 《禮記·王制》 line above and the 《禮記·明堂位》), not to the Shiji's Yin chapter, so the claim has been removed. What the ritual texts do support is Zheng Xuan's gloss that the Left School was the lower school (小學) in the royal palace area to the east of the capital, with the Right School (右學) the higher school (大學) in the western suburb; this is a commentary reading and not a settled fact. The schools' role in housing and honoring the elders reflects the early Chinese blending of educational, ceremonial and welfare functions, as the Right School page also notes.

The Knowledge page lists the word as "the Left School (its counterpart)," beside [[右学]]. Dan'a'yo keeps that sense, a proper name for one historical institution. The readings are Mandarin zuǒxué, Cantonese zo2 hok6, Japanese sagaku (さがく), Korean 좌학 and Sino-Vietnamese tả học.
