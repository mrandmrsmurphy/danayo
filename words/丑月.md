## Notes
- [丑](../characters/丑.md) "ox (Earthly Branch)" + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — no `#cranberry`. `kwin` is false (AND-rule: 丑 true, 月 false).

Twelfth and final month of the ancient Chinese lunar calendar, named for the second Earthly Branch 丑 (ox). The last month before 寅月 begins the new year. Deep winter — the ox's patient, slow-moving nature suits a month of waiting and preparation. The 臘八 Festival and the year-end sacrifice (臘) fall in this month's counterpart in the poetic calendar.

Japanese うしつき (native, "ox month") and Vietnamese tháng Sửu (native "month" + the zodiac name Sửu) are both real compositional forms. Fixed the duplicate `品詞` field and a missing citation on `characters/丑.md`'s Words list (previously had none at all).
