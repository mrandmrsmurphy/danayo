## Notes
- [天](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven" + [空](../characters/空%20(char).md) "empty" — no stand-in relationship applies (天/空 are both their own stand-ins).

天空 means sky — literally "heaven's emptiness."

Mandarin tiānkōng, Cantonese tin1 hung1, Korean 천공, and Japanese てんくう are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese bầu trời is the standard native term for "sky" (rather than a compositional thiên không, an expected native-equivalent per established convention). Blanked empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄝㄋㄎㄛㄫ unique across both words and characters).
