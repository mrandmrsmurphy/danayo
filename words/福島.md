## Notes
- [福](../characters/福%20(char).md) "blessing" + [島](../characters/島%20(char).md) "island"

福島 is Fukushima, written "fortune island". The name is attached to the castle town that is now the prefectural capital. The Japanese reading ふくしま is the native-sounding reading of 福 (ふく, which is also its on-reading) and 島 (しま).

Fukushima is the third largest prefecture in Japan and spans three regions: the coastal Hamadōri, the central basin around the capital, and the mountainous Aizu in the west, where the castle town of Aizuwakamatsu preserves the history of the Boshin War. The name is also bound up with the 2011 Tōhoku earthquake and the accident at the Fukushima Daiichi nuclear plant.

In Dan'a'yo 福島 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 후쿠시마 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
