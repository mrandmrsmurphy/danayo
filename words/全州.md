## Notes
- [全](../characters/全%20(char).md) "whole" + [州](../characters/州%20(char).md) "state" — the name of Jeonju, a city of South Korea.

全州 (Jeonju) is the capital of North Jeolla Province (全北特別自治道) in South Korea, the seat of the Joseon dynasty founder's ancestral clan and the home of the dish bibimbap (from memory, not re-checked). The province name [[全羅]] is formed from the first characters of 全州 and 羅州 (Naju) (from memory, not re-checked).

English and Chinese Wiktionary have 全州 as the Korean city (Mandarin Quánzhōu, Cantonese cyun4 zau1), with Korean Wiktionary also having the string; the same string is Quanzhou County in Guangxi, China (and the Vietnamese Wikipedia has it as Toàn Châu). Japanese Wiktionary has no entry, so Japanese ぜんしゅう is composed from the on'yomi. Korean 전주 is read the same in the North and the South.
