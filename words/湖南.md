## Notes
- [湖](../characters/湖.md) "lake" + [南](../characters/南.md) "south" — "south of the lake", the lake being Lake Dongting. No stand-in relationship applies (湖's own is [[湖水]]; 南's own is [[南方]]).

This name is shared by a Chinese province and by places in Japan and Korea, and Japanese Wiktionary also lists it as an ordinary noun, "the south side of a lake" (in Japan usually the south of Lake Biwa, in China the south of Lake Dongting), so the non-province senses come first. English and Chinese Wiktionary list the city of Konan in Shiga Prefecture, Japan (read こなん), and the Korean region of Honam (호남, the south-west of the Korean peninsula; the name is literally "south of the lake", from memory, not re-checked).

Hunan, the province, lies south of Lake Dongting in south-central China, and its capital is [[長沙]] (from memory, not re-checked). Its northern neighbour is [[湖北]] (Hubei). English Wiktionary gives the etymology as lying on the south of Lake Dongting. The Japanese field holds こなん, which is both the on'yomi (kan-on and go-on) reading and the name of the Shiga city; the Korean field holds the Sino-Korean 호남, which is also the established Korean name of the Honam region; the usual South Korean name for the Chinese province today is 후난 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Hồ Nam is the Sino-Vietnamese name. Cantonese wu4 naam4 is the Jyutping on English Wiktionary. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄛㄨㄋㄚㄇ unique across both words and characters).

Attestation, checked 2026-10-11: English and Chinese Wiktionary (Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese sections) list the senses above, with the senses marked Japanese taken from Japanese Wiktionary.

1. The south side of a lake (Japanese).
2. Konan, a city in Shiga Prefecture, Japan.
3. Honam, a region of South Korea (호남).
4. Hunan, the province of China.
