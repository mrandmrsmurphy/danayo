## Notes
- [潜](../characters/潜.md) "hide, dive" + [水](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" — "go under the water." Their own `stand_in` values: 潜's is [[潜在]]; 水's is itself; none points here, so 潜水 is an independent compound.

潜水 is to dive: to go under the surface of the water. Mandarin qiánshuǐ, Cantonese cim4 seoi2, Japanese せんすい and Korean 잠수 all use it. In Chinese and Korean internet slang it also means to lurk, to read a forum without posting; that is how Dan'a'yo says "lurker," with no separate word. Compare [[投稿]] (to post).
