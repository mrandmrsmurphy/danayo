## Notes
- [所](../characters/所%20(char).md) "-ee (nominalizer)" + [在](../characters/在%20(char).md) "exist" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (所's own points to bare `所`, 在's own to bare `在`); 所在 is an independent compound. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: `characters/所 (char).md` is `kwin: false`, `characters/在 (char).md` is `kwin: true` → compound `false`.

所在 means place, location, or whereabouts.

Mandarin suǒzài, Cantonese so2 zoi6, Japanese しょざい, Korean 소재, and Vietnamese sở tại are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `諺文`/`羅馬字` had been using the free-standing word 所's register (쇼/syo) instead of the bound-character citation (스/sǝ, per `characters/所 (char).md`) used consistently in 所+X compounds like [[厠所]]/[[居所]]/[[所属]] — corrected to 스재/sǝjai, matching the 注音 (ㄙㄜㄐㄚㄧ) that was already correct. Same systemic bug found and retroactively fixed on already-stamped siblings [[所有]] and [[所以]]; [[所謂]] still carries it and will be fixed on its own turn. No homophones.
