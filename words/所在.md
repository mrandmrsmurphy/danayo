## Notes
- [所](../characters/所%20(char).md) "-ee (nominalizer)" + [在](../characters/在%20(char).md) "exist" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (所's own points to bare `所`, 在's own to bare `在`); 所在 is an independent compound.

所在 means place, location, or whereabouts.

Mandarin suǒzài, Cantonese so2 zoi6, Japanese しょざい, Korean 소재, and Vietnamese sở tại are all standard, fully compositional readings.
