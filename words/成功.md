## Notes
- [成](../characters/成%20(char).md) "become" + [功](../characters/功.md) "achievement" — no stand-in relationship applies (成's own stand-in is itself; 功's own is [[功績]]).

成功 means to succeed, achieve success, or prosper.

Mandarin chénggōng, Cantonese sing4 gung1, Japanese せいこう, Korean 성공, and Vietnamese thành công are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄫㄍㄛㄫ unique across both words and characters).
