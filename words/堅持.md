## Notes
- [堅](../characters/堅.md) "hard, firm" + [持 (char)](../characters/持%20(char).md) "hold" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (堅's is 堅硬, 持's is bare 持).

Mandarin jiānchí, Cantonese gin1 ci4, Japanese けんじ, and Korean 견지 are the standard readings. Vietnamese kiên trì is a real, extremely common Sino-Vietnamese word.
