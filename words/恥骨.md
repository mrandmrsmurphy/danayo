## Notes
- [恥](../characters/恥.md) "shame" + [骨](../characters/骨%20(char).md) "bone" — no stand-in relationship applies (恥's own stand-in is [[恥辱]]; 骨's own is itself).

恥骨 means pubis, the pubic bone.

Mandarin chǐgǔ, Cantonese ci2 gwat1, Japanese ちこつ, Korean 치골, and Vietnamese sỉ cốt are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `kwin` was wrongly true — 諺文 치곧 diverges from `korean` 치골 in the final coda (ㄷ vs ㄹ). Filled blank `cantonese`/`vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄍㄛㄊ unique across both words and characters).

**`kwin` update (2026-10-09):** `kwin` is now true. The Notes above may describe the ㄷ/ㄹ difference between the Dan'a'yo 諺文 and the Korean reading as a mismatch; under the vault's standing rule a Korean final ㄹ counts as equal to Dan'a'yo's final ㄷ for this comparison, so such a difference alone does not make `kwin` false.
