## Notes
- [乳](../characters/乳.md) "milk" + [酪](../characters/酪.md) "fermented dairy, cheese" — this word is the `stand_in` legitimizer for [[酪]], which cannot appear independently.

乳酪 covers dairy products broadly, with cheese specifically as its most common modern referent (in Mandarin especially, alongside the calque 奶酪, nǎilào, using the fully colloquial 奶 "milk" instead of the more literary 乳). Vietnamese nhũ lạc (confirmed via web search) is the formal/traditional Hán Việt term for cheese, alongside the related 乾酪/can lạc — everyday Vietnamese instead almost always uses phô mai, a French loanword (from *fromage*). `korean: 유락` is the plain compositional concatenation. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄨㄌㄚㄎ unique among both words and characters).
