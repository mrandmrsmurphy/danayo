## Notes
- [火 (char)](../characters/火%20(char).md) "fire" + [炎](../characters/炎.md) "flame, inflammation" — blazing fire; flame

火炎 is flame, the visible blaze of burning: 火 is fire and 炎 the licking, rising flame (the character is two 火 stacked, fire doubled).  The word is literary and technical rather than everyday: 火炎瓶 is a Molotov cocktail or "flame bottle", 火炎放射器 a flamethrower, and 火炎樹 (flame tree) a tropical tree with flame-red blossoms.  It is close to [[火焰]], which glosses the same sense, and the Light lexipedia page records the pair as undocumented near-duplicates and not a confirmed split.

炎's own `stand_in` is 炎症 (inflammation), so this word is not its legitimizer.  Mandarin huǒyán, Cantonese fo2 jim4, Japanese かえん and Korean 화염 are the same compound, and the variant 火焔 is listed as an alias.  `kwin` is false because the Dan'a'yo 화엄 differs from the Korean 화염.  No Vietnamese form was found attested, so none is given.
