## Notes
- [火 (char)](../characters/火%20(char).md) "fire" + [炎](../characters/炎.md) "flame, inflammation" — blazing fire; flame

Wiktionary lists 火炎 only under Japanese (かえん): "flame, blaze". The variant 火焔 is kept as an alias. It is close to [[火焰]], which glosses the same sense, and the Light lexipedia page records them as two forms of "flame": 火炎 the Japanese-attested form, 火焰 the literal physical flame in the Chinese form.

炎's own `stand_in` is 炎症 (inflammation), so this word is not its legitimizer.
