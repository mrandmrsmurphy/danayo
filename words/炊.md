## Notes
- [炊](../characters/炊%20(char).md) "cook" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [炊](../characters/炊%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

炊 means to cook (rice or a meal), as in 炊事 ("cooking, kitchen duty") and 自炊 ("cooking for oneself").

Mandarin chuī, Cantonese ceoi1, Japanese すい, Korean 취, and Vietnamese xuy are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: literal `vietnamese: null`, corrected using the character's own citation. Part of the three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all cui/취/ㄑㄨㄧ) with [[吹]] ("blow") and [[推]] ("push") — completing the reciprocal callout anticipated on 吹's own page.
