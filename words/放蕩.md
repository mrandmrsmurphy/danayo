## Notes
- [放](../characters/放.md) "release" + [蕩](../characters/蕩.md) "dissolute"
- This word is the viable form of [蕩](../characters/蕩.md): the character's meaning (dissolute, licentious) is identical, but 蕩 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 放蕩 is its `stand_in` compound.

放蕩 describes a life without restraint: dissolute, debauched, given to drink, women, gambling and spending. Mandarin fàngdàng, Cantonese fong3 dong6, Japanese ほうとう (hōtō; 放蕩息子, "prodigal son") Korean 방탕 (bangtang) and Vietnamese phóng đãng are the same compound. The characters are "to let loose, release" (放) and "to wander, dissolute" (蕩). In Chinese it can also mean unconventional or free-spirited (放蕩不羈), which in Japanese and Korean is rare.

Dan'a'yo-specific sense: the shared one. It is housed in the Reproduction domain for its sexual-licence sense, beside [[姦淫]] (fornication, adultery), but its meaning is broader than that: it covers the whole careless, squandering way of living, so it is not restricted to sexual conduct.
