## Notes
- [実](../characters/実.md) "real, fruit" + [習](../characters/習.md) "practice, learn" — practical training.

実習 is practical training: learning by doing in a real setting, as in an internship, apprenticeship-style placement or hands-on lab work. It is "real-practice." Mandarin shíxí (實習), Cantonese sat6 zaap6, Japanese じっしゅう, Korean 실습 and Vietnamese thực tập are all confirmed on Wiktionary. It contrasts with Japanese 研修 (けんしゅう), in-service training, which in Mandarin means "to do research work" and is not used here. The person undergoing it is a [[実習生]]. Compare [[技能]] (skill) and [[熟練]] (proficient).
