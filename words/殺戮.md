## Notes
- [殺](characters/殺%20(char).md) "kill" + [戮](characters/戮.md) "slaughter" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 戮 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry. `vietnamese: sát lục` is directly attested via hvdic.

殺戮 means slaughter or massacre.

Mandarin shālù, Cantonese saat3 luk6, and Japanese さつりく are all standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 살육 (rather than a naive concatenation 살+륙="살륙") reflects a genuine Sino-Korean liquid-liaison shift after the preceding vowel-final syllable 살, the same class of divergence documented for 률→율 elsewhere in the vault — kept as-is rather than "corrected" to a concatenated form. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄚㄊㄌㄨㄎ unique across both words and characters).
