## Notes
- [河](../characters/河.md) "stream" + [内](../characters/内.md) "inside" — no stand-in relationship applies (河's own stand-in is [[小河]]; 内's own is [[内部]]).

河内 means Hanoi, the capital of Vietnam (literally "inside the river," referring to its position within a bend of the Red River).

Mandarin Hénèi and Cantonese ho4 noi6 are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 河's and 内's own stored citations. Japanese ハノイ and Korean 하노이 are both the international phonetic loanword "Hanoi," and Vietnamese Hà Nội is the real city name itself — also a perfect compositional match (Hà = 河, Nội = 内). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄋㄛㄧ unique across both words and characters).
