## Notes
- [毎](../characters/毎%20(char).md) "each" + [年](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year" — no stand-in relationship applies (毎's own stand-in is itself; 年's own is also itself).

毎年 means every year.

Mandarin měinián, Cantonese mui5 nin4, Japanese まいねん, and Korean 매년 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 毎's and 年's own stored citations exactly).
