星 means star.

Mandarin xīng, Cantonese sing1, Japanese せい, Korean 성, and Vietnamese tinh are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones at the word level (注音 ㄙㄝㄫ is shared only with bound characters 猩/醒, neither of which has an independent word at this exact reading).
