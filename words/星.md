## Notes
- [星](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [星](../characters/星%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

星 means star: a luminous point in the night sky (星空, "starry sky"; 星光, "starlight"), and by extension a celestial body generally, including planets (行星, "planet"; 衛星, "satellite"), as well as a celebrity (明星, "star, celebrity"). The graph is a 形声 compound of 日 "sun, bright body" with the phonetic 生.

Mandarin xīng, Cantonese sing1, Japanese せい (衛星/えいせい, "satellite"; 星座/せいざ, "constellation"), Korean 성 (위성, 성좌) and Vietnamese tinh (hành tinh, "planet"; tinh tú, "stars") are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native noun 星 (ほし) for the everyday word, with 星 on'yomi せい in compounds, and Korean has native 별 beside Sino-Korean 성.
