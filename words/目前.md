## Notes
- [目 (char)](../characters/目%20(char).md) "eye" + [前 (char)](../characters/前%20(char).md) "front, before, preceding" — literally "before the eyes." Neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both are bare self-standing characters); 目前 is an independent compound.

Mandarin mùqián, Cantonese muk6 cin4, Japanese もくぜん, and Korean 목전 all mean "the present moment; right before one's eyes" across the sphere. The `korean` field holds 목전, the Sino-Korean compound reading; 눈앞 (native "eye" + "front") is an unrelated native synonym and not a reading of 目前. No Vietnamese reading is recorded, so the `vietnamese` field is omitted.
