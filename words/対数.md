## Notes
- [対](../characters/対.md) "opposite, corresponding" + [数](../characters/数.md) "number" — literally "corresponding number," the logarithm: log_b(x) is the exponent to which *b* must be raised to yield *x*.

Mandarin duìshù, Cantonese deoi3 sou3, Japanese たいすう, and Korean 대수 are all standard, fully compositional terms, matching 対's and 数's own stored citations exactly. **Vietnamese has no compositional Sino-Vietnamese term for this concept at all**: the standard, and only attested, Vietnamese word is the European loanword lôgarit (from French *logarithme*), filled here rather than a fabricated compositional reading. Note also that Vietnamese đối số — which would look like the expected compositional reading of 對數's own characters — is a real Vietnamese math term, but means something different: "argument" (the input value *x* a logarithm is applied to), not "logarithm" itself; care is warranted not to conflate the two.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 되수 diverges from Sino-Korean 대수 in the first syllable, since 対 independently carries a diverging reading on its own character page. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄧㄙㄨ unique among both words and characters).
