## Notes
- [分](../characters/分.md) "to divide" + [離](../characters/離.md) "to separate, leave" — "to separate, segregate, split apart" (physically or of people/relationships). `vietnamese: phân ly` is one of the most common everyday Vietnamese words for this concept, a direct match. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 분레 diverges from Sino-Korean 분리 in the second syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄨㄋㄌㄝㄧ unique among both words and characters).
