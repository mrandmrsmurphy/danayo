## Notes
- [神](../characters/神%20(char).md) "god" + [諭](../characters/諭.md) "oracle, command" — "divine word," an oracle. Their own `stand_in` values: 神's is itself; 諭's is [[諭示]]; none points here, so 神諭 is an independent compound.

神諭 is an oracle: a message from a god, or the shrine and priest that deliver it. Mandarin shényù and Cantonese san4 jyu6 are the ordinary Chinese words. Japanese says 神託 and Korean 신탁, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[上帝]] (god).
