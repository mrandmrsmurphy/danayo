## Notes
- [新](../characters/新%20(char).md) "new" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language" — no stand-in relationship applies (新's own stand-in is itself; 語's own is [[言語]]).

In Dan'a'yo particular, these refer to "new words" that had to be coined because there was not enough/any agreement amongst CJKV language to make a reliable word. See [List of 新語](../lookup/List%20of%20新語.md).

Mandarin xīnyǔ, Cantonese san1 jyu5, Korean 신어, Japanese しんご, and Vietnamese tân ngữ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Removed redundant `品詞`, fixed missing space in `cantonese`. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄋ·⼄ unique across both words and characters).
