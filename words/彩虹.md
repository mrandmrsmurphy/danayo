## Notes
- [彩](../characters/彩.md) "color" + [虹](../characters/虹.md) "rainbow" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [虹](../characters/虹.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (彩's own stand-in is [[多彩]]).

彩虹 means rainbow — literally "colored rainbow-arc."

Mandarin cǎihóng and Japanese さいこう (compositional SAI+KOU) are standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 무지개 and Vietnamese cầu vồng are the standard native terms for rainbow, more common in everyday usage than any Sino-Korean/Sino-Vietnamese compositional form. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄚㄧㄏㄛㄫ unique across both words and characters).
