## Notes
- [富](../characters/富.md) "abundance" + [山](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain"

富山 is Toyama, written "rich mountain". The reading とやま is native and is said to derive from an older name written 外山, "outer mountain", rewritten with the more auspicious 富 (from memory, not re-checked). The character 富, read ふ or とみ elsewhere, is used here only for the sound とや.

Toyama faces Toyama Bay, famous for firefly squid, and is backed by the Tateyama range, through which the Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route runs. It is also known for its traditional medicine trade and for the village houses of Gokayama.

In Dan'a'yo 富山 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 도야마 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
