## Notes
- [甘 (char)](../characters/甘%20(char).md) "sweet" + [藷](../characters/藷.md) "potato, yam" — no stand-in relationship applies (甘's own stand-in is itself; 藷's own is [[蕃藷]]).

甘藷 means sweet potato.

Mandarin gānshǔ, Cantonese gam1 syu4, Japanese かんしょ and Vietnamese cam thự are fully compositional. Korean 감저 is the single compositional reading, distinct from 감자, which is the white potato.
