## Etymology
- [甘 (char)](../characters/甘%20(char).md) "sweet" + [藷](../characters/藷.md) "potato, yam" — no stand-in relationship applies (甘's own stand-in is itself; 藷's own is [[蕃藷]]).

甘藷 means sweet potato.

Mandarin gānshǔ and Cantonese gam1 syu4 were already correct and fully compositional. Fixed a real spelling bug in `japanese`: かんしよ (raw kana, missing the small ょ) → かんしょ (KAN+SHO), the correct reading. Fixed `korean`: a comma-joined dual value ("감저, 감자") was corrected to the single compositional 감저, matching 藷's own stored citation (감자 refers to the distinct concept of white potato). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with cam thự (甘's own cam + 藷's own thự). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/empty `aliases: []`. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄇㄙㄛ unique across both words and characters).
