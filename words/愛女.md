## Notes
- [愛 (char)](../characters/愛%20(char).md) "love" + [女 (char)](../characters/女%20(char).md) "woman" — literally "love-woman."

愛女 is a Dan'a'yo-original coinage, not an attested compound in any of Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, Korean, or Vietnamese. It was minted by fiat specifically to disambiguate "girlfriend" (an unmarried, romantic-but-not-marital female partner) from [[愛人]], which already exists but carries genuinely contradictory senses across the CJKV sphere — standard "spouse" in mainland Mandarin, "lover" (sometimes illicit) in Taiwan/Hong Kong Mandarin, "mistress/affair partner" in Japanese, and neutral "boyfriend/girlfriend" in Korean and Vietnamese. 愛女 sidesteps that mess with a gender-specific, semantically transparent compound, paired with [[愛男]] for "boyfriend."

All five cross-linguistic readings are compositional, built from 愛's and 女's own stored citations rather than independently attested — the same convention used for every other Dan'a'yo word, coined or classical. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 애느 diverges from Sino-Korean 애녀 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄧㄋㄜ unique among both words and characters).
