## Notes
- [鮫](../characters/鮫.md) "shark" + [魚 (char)](../characters/魚%20(char).md) "fish" — legitimizing note: 鮫's own `stand_in` is [[鮫魚]] itself, already annotated in its own citation; 魚's own `stand_in` is 魚 itself, so transitivity fails — no `#cranberry`.

Mandarin jiāoyú, Cantonese gaau1jyu4, and Korean 상어 (the standard everyday word) are compositional. Japanese さめ (same) is the native, everyday word for "shark" rather than a Sino-Japanese reading. Sino-Vietnamese giao ngư is the compositional form; everyday Vietnamese instead uses the native phrase cá mập.
