## Notes
- [良](../characters/良.md) "kind" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 良 cannot appear independently outside this compound. [好 (char)](../characters/好%20(char).md) "good, like" is separately legitimized by [[好]] itself. No cranberry: transitivity fails since 好's own `stand_in` doesn't point here.

良好 combines 良 ("kind") with 好 ("good") — "good, fine, kind."

Mandarin liánghǎo, cantonese loeng4hou2 (space removed), and japanese りょうこう (ryoukou) were all already correct, fully compositional readings.
