## Notes
- [恣](../characters/恣.md) "selfish, arbitrary" + [肆](../characters/肆.md) "act brazenly" — stand-in for [[肆]], which cannot appear independently

恣肆 means unrestrained, doing as one pleases without holding back: 恣 is "to indulge oneself, to follow one's own will" and 肆 is "to let loose, to act without restraint" (compare 放肆, "impudent, unbridled"). Mandarin zìsì, Cantonese zi3 si3, Japanese しし (shishi) and Korean 자사 (jasa) are the readings in the vault's data. In Chinese it can be negative (wanton, reckless) but also, of writing or style, admiring: 汪洋恣肆 describes prose of vast, free-flowing sweep, a phrase classically applied to the 莊子.

In Dan'a'yo it is the word for self-indulgent licence, placed in Sin beside [[傲慢]] (arrogant pride): 傲慢 is the inflated self-regard, 恣肆 is acting without restraint.
