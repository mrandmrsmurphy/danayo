## Notes
- [浸](characters/浸%20(char).md) "immerse, dunk" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 浸 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

浸 means to immerse, dunk.

Mandarin jìn, Cantonese zam3, Korean 침, and Vietnamese tẩm are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 浸's own stored citations exactly. Fixed literal `vietnamese: null` → tẩm, added the missing `pos`/`japanese`(しん, on'yomi SHIN)/`hsk_level`("3") fields, reformatted `characters` to a proper block-list with the "(char)" suffix.

**A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone with [[寝]]** ("sleep, lie down, rest") is already fully documented and cross-referenced on 寝's own page — 浸's own phonetic root 𡩠 is an independent near-homophone of 寝's phonetic 侵, making the collision coincidental rather than a shared etymology. Checked the remaining ㄑㄧㄇ-reading characters (沈, 侵) — neither has a self-pointing `stand_in` ([[沈没]] and [[侵入]] respectively), confirming no third homophone word exists here.
