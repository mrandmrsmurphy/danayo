## Notes
- [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "gold; metal" + [刀](../characters/刀.md) "blade; knife"

Wiktionary has no entry for 金刀. Wikipedia (Eight Principles of Yong) records 金刀 (jīndāo, "golden knife") as Li Puguang's metaphorical name, from his Yuan-era 永字八法解, for the eighth principle, the right-falling stroke (磔, modern 捺 or 波), which fattens toward its foot where the endpoint is "sharp as a knife". See [[捺]], [[怪石]] and the [[Stroke]] page. Its readings are compositional.
