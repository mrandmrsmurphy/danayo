## Notes
- [紮](../characters/紮%20(char).md) "tie, fasten, bind" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 紮 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

紮 means to tie, fasten, or bind.

Mandarin zā, Cantonese zaat3, Japanese さつ, Korean 찰, and Vietnamese trát are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 紮's own stored citation exactly).
