## Notes
- [成](../characters/成%20(char).md) "become" + [都](../characters/都.md) "capital, metropolis" — "become a capital". No stand-in relationship applies (成 is its own stand-in; 都's is [[首都]]).

成都 (Chengdu) is the capital of Sichuan province in southwestern China (see [四川](四川.md)). Wiktionary notes that the name may be the transcription of a native non-Chinese word, attested on excavated artefacts of the late Warring States period and in the Records of the Grand Historian, and compares it with other southwestern place names ending in 都; the apparent reading "become a capital" is therefore a folk-style reading of the two characters, and no ordinary sense of 成都 as a word is attested in the Chinese, Japanese or Korean Wiktionary entries. It was historically the capital of the kingdom of Shu (see [蜀国](蜀国.md)).

Japanese せいと is the Sino-Japanese reading (Wiktionary Japanese); the usual spoken Japanese name today is チェンドゥ (also given on Wiktionary as チョントゥー). Korean 성도 is the North Korean reading of the characters (Wiktionary Korean 성도); the usual South Korean name is 청두 (listed on Wiktionary). Vietnamese Thành Đô is the Sino-Vietnamese name. Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese entries. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄫㄉㄛ unique across both words and characters).
