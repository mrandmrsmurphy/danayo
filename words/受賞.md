## Notes
- [受](../characters/受.md) "to receive, accept" + [賞 (char)](../characters/賞%20(char).md) "reward, prize"

受賞 means "to win a prize, to be awarded" — receiving (受) a prize or honor (賞) such as a literary award, a medal, or an academic prize (ノーベル賞を受賞する, "to be awarded the Nobel Prize"). 受, a 会意 character (as its character page records: 爫 "hand" + 舟 "boat," visually corrupted in the modern form, + 又 "hand," the image of handing something over across a boat), is the basic verb "to receive, to accept, to undergo," and heads a family of 受-compounds ([[受難]], [[受理]], [[受胎]], [[受託]]) that share its core sense of "receiving" something, whether an honor, a hardship, a case, a pregnancy, or a commission.

賞, a 形声 character (phonetic 商 plus semantic 貝 "cowry shell, money" — money being the original form of many rewards, as the 賞 character page records), means "reward, prize, to bestow a reward." 受賞 forms a natural pair with 授賞 ("to bestow a prize") — 受賞 from the recipient's perspective, 授賞 from the awarding body's, the two verbs describing the same ceremony from opposite ends.

The Conflict page lists it in its Victory, Loss & Defeat section, with the glosses "to win an award, to be awarded," and Dan'a'yo keeps that sense. The readings are Mandarin shòushǎng, Cantonese sau6 soeng2, Japanese jushō (じゅしょう), Korean 수상 and Sino-Vietnamese thụ thưởng.
