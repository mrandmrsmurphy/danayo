## Notes
- [賛](../characters/賛.md) "help, support" + [歌](../characters/歌.md) "song" — a song of praise

Wiktionary: Japanese 賛歌 (さんか) "hymn, paean, song of praise" and "eulogy"; Chinese 讚歌 "song of praise", kept with 贊歌 as aliases; Korean 찬가 is the hanja form, "hymn". Compare [[賛美歌]]. 賛's own `stand_in` is 賛助 and 歌's is 歌曲, so this word legitimizes neither.
