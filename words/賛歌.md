## Notes
- [賛](../characters/賛.md) "help, support" + [歌](../characters/歌.md) "song" — a song of praise

賛歌 means a song of praise: a poem or song written to extol a person, an ideal or a thing (青春賛歌, "a hymn to youth"; 平和賛歌, "a paean to peace").  賛 contributes the praise and 歌 the singing, so the word is the general praise-song, wider than the specifically Christian [[賛美歌]] ("hymn"), which it is often used to translate in the English sense of "paean".  The aliases 讚歌 and 贊歌 are the traditional spellings.

賛's own `stand_in` is 賛助 and 歌's is 歌曲, so this word legitimizes neither.  Mandarin zàngē, Cantonese zaan3 go1, Japanese さんか and Korean 찬가 are the same compound.  No Vietnamese form is given, since none could be verified.  `kwin` is false because the Dan'a'yo 잔그 differs from the Korean 찬가.  The 注音 needs no syllable dot, and no other word shares it.
