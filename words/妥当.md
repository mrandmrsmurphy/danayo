## Notes
- [妥](../characters/妥.md) "appropriate, proper" + [当](../characters/当%20(char).md) "correspond, match" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [妥](../characters/妥.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (当's own stand-in is itself).

妥当 means appropriate, proper, or valid — a near-synonymous doubling of two characters both meaning "fitting, corresponding."

Mandarin tuǒdàng, Cantonese to5 dong3, Japanese だとう, Korean 타당, and Vietnamese thỏa đáng are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `pos: 性詞`. Fixed malformed `mandarin` (had a stray untoned duplicate, "tuǒdang, tuǒdàng"). Reformatted `characters`/`aliases` to block-list. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄚㄉㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
