## Notes
- [横](../characters/横.md) "horizontal; transverse; lateral" + [数](../characters/数.md) "number; count" — a number that lies on the perpendicular (imaginary) axis of the complex plane

The standard Sinospheric term for imaginary numbers is 虚數 (虚数 in Japanese, 허수 in Korean, số ảo in Vietnamese), where 虚 means "empty" or "unreal." The Dan'a'yo term 横数 takes a different approach: it names the number by its geometric position instead of by a judgment of its reality. In the Argand diagram the real numbers lie along one axis and the imaginary numbers along the axis perpendicular to it. Here 横 is read in its sense of "transverse, crossing" (as in [[横断]], to cross over), not "horizontal": 横数 are the numbers that lie across the main line of counting, whichever way that line is drawn on the page.

The imaginary unit itself is √−1; the set of imaginary numbers is all real multiples of it. Together with the real numbers, they form the complex numbers.
