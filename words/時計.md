## Notes
- [時 (char)](../characters/時%20(char).md) "time" + [計](../characters/計.md) "measure" — literally "time-measurer."

Mandarin shíjì and Cantonese si4 gai3 are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 時's and 計's own stored citations exactly. Korean 시계 is likewise compositional (시 + 계) and, per Wiktionary, is itself a loan of the Japanese coinage rather than an independent Sino-Korean formation.

Japanese とけい (tokei) is a fixed, irregular reading — neither character's on'yomi (時 is JI/SHI, 計 is KEI/KAI) accounts for it directly; と traces back to Middle Chinese 土圭 (tʰuoX kwei, "clay tablet [sundial]"), an ancient Zhou-dynasty sundial term later re-analyzed onto the characters 時計 by folk etymology. This is a case of a fused, non-compositional Japanese reading, comparable to 八日/十日.

`vietnamese` is omitted: the real Vietnamese word for "clock, watch" is đồng hồ, an unrelated non-Sino-Vietnamese term (traditionally linked to 銅壺, "bronze [water-]pot," describing a water clock) — 時計 itself has no attested Sino-Vietnamese reading. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 시게 diverges from Sino-Korean 시계 in the second syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄍㄝㄧ unique among both words and characters).
