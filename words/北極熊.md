## Notes
"Polar bear" (literally "north-pole bear"). `korean: 북극곰` and `vietnamese: gấu Bắc Cực` are both real, everyday terms — each a hybrid of the Sino-Korean/Sino-Vietnamese "north pole" (북극/Bắc Cực) with the native word for "bear" (곰/gấu), rather than a fully Sino-compositional calque (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 북긱웅 diverges entirely from the hybrid-native Korean field. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄨㄎㄍㄧㄎㄨㄫ unique among both words and characters).
