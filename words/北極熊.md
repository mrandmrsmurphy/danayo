## Notes
"Polar bear" (literally "north-pole bear"); synonymous with [[白熊]]. `korean: 북극곰` and `vietnamese: gấu Bắc Cực` are both real, everyday terms — each a hybrid of the Sino-Korean/Sino-Vietnamese "north pole" (북극/Bắc Cực) with the native word for "bear" (곰/gấu), rather than a fully Sino-compositional calque (domain knowledge, not independently confirmed by search).
