## Notes
- [偵](../characters/偵%20(char).md) "spy, scout" + [察](../characters/察.md) "investigate" — "scout and investigate," reconnaissance. Their own `stand_in` values: 偵's is itself; 察's is [[考察]]; none points here, so 偵察 is an independent compound.

偵察 is to scout: to go out and observe the enemy or an area before acting. Mandarin zhēnchá, Cantonese zing1 caat3, Japanese ていさつ and Korean 정찰 all use it, and Vietnamese trinh sát does too. It is the ordinary word; [[斥候]] is the literary one, and a scout himself is 偵察－者, segmented with the suffix [[－者]]. Compare [[狙撃]] (snipe).
