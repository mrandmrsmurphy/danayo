## Notes
- [補助](../words/補助.md) "assistance, aid, auxiliary" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language" (here in its bound "language" sense, as in [[言語]]) — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes 補助語 as a multi-character host for 語, alongside its own already-established [[言語]].

補助語 means an auxiliary language — a language deliberately designed or adopted as a secondary medium of communication between speakers of different native languages, rather than as anyone's mother tongue (国際補助語, "international auxiliary language," the term historically used for Esperanto and similar projects). This is the term [[文法 - 01序文]] uses for both 世界語 (Esperanto) and 単亜語 itself.

Mandarin bǔzhùyǔ, Japanese ほじょご, Korean 보조어, and Vietnamese phụ trợ ngữ are all straightforward compositional readings from [[補助]]'s and 語's own stored citations; not independently cross-checked against each language's own standard conlang/Esperantology terminology, which may prefer a fuller phrase (e.g. Mandarin 国际辅助语, Japanese 国際補助語) over this bare compound — flagged rather than silently assumed. `kwin` is false: 語's own character-level divergence (Dan'a'yo 요 vs Sino-Korean 어) carries through to the whole compound (Dan'a'yo 보조요 vs Korean 보조어). No homophones (注音 ㄅㄛㄐㄛ⼄ unique across both words and characters; checked against the alternate partition ㄐㄛ⼄ and ㄅㄛㄐㄛ, neither of which is a legal syllable, confirming no syllable-boundary dot is needed).
