## Notes
- [十](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" + [戒](../characters/戒.md) "precept, warn" — "ten precepts," the Ten Commandments. Their own `stand_in` values: 十's is itself; 戒's is [[警戒]]; none points here, so 十戒 is an independent compound.

十戒 are the Ten Commandments: the ten laws God gave Moses on Sinai. Mandarin shíjiè (十誡) and Cantonese sap6 gaai3 are the ordinary words, and Japanese じっかい and Korean 십계 are the same compound. The character 戒 has the alias 誡, so the Chinese spelling is accepted. They are the heart of the [[律法]] (the Law). Compare [[契約]] (covenant) and [[旧約]].
