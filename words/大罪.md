## Notes
- [大](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big" + [罪](../characters/罪%20(char).md) "sin" — "great sin." Their own `stand_in` values: 大's is itself; 罪's is itself; none points here, so 大罪 is an independent compound.

大罪 is a great sin: one grave enough to cut a person off. Mandarin dàzuì and Japanese たいざい use it, and Korean 대죄 is the same word. The "seven deadly sins" are 七 plus 大罪, "seven grave sins"; the list is on [[Sin]].

**Hebrew**: it best translates *ḥăṭāʾāh gĕdōlāh* (חֲטָאָה גְדֹלָה) "a great sin" (Exodus 32:21, 32:30–31; Genesis 20:9). The Greek "sin leading to death" (1 John 5:16) is the Catholic "mortal sin." Compare [[罪悪]] and [[罪業]] (the weight of any sin).
