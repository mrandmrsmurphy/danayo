## Notes
- [正](../characters/正%20(char).md) "correct" + [午](../characters/午.md) "noon" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [午](../characters/午.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

正午 means noon.

Mandarin zhèngwǔ, Cantonese zing3 ng5, Japanese しょうご, Korean 정오, and Vietnamese chính ngọ are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 正's and 午's own stored citations exactly). Fixed real bug: `vietnamese` was buổi trưa, a native descriptive phrase ("midday"), not the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form — corrected to chính ngọ. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄫㄛ unique across both words and characters).
