## Notes
- [湖](../characters/湖.md) "lake" + [北](../characters/北.md) "north" — "north of the lake", the lake being Lake Dongting. No stand-in relationship applies (湖's own is [[湖水]]; 北's own is [[北方]]).

湖北 (Hubei) is a province of central China, north of Lake Dongting, whose capital is [[武漢]] (from memory, not re-checked). English Wiktionary gives the etymology as lying on the north of Lake Dongting, and lists the one-character abbreviation 鄂, the name of the old state [[鄂国]]. Its southern neighbour is [[湖南]] (Hunan), "south of the lake", and the two names differ only in the second character.

The Japanese field holds こほく, the reading in English Wiktionary Japanese; the Korean field holds the Sino-Korean 호북 (Wiktionary Korean: hanja form of 호북), which differs from the Dan'a'yo 諺文 홋북 only in the final of 湖; the usual South Korean name today is 후베이 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Hồ Bắc is the Sino-Vietnamese name. English Wiktionary also lists a derived term 湖北口, a place name, which is not a sense of this word. Cantonese wu4 bak1 is the Jyutping on English Wiktionary. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄛㄨㄅㄨㄎ unique across both words and characters).

Attestation, checked 2026-10-11: English and Chinese Wiktionary (Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese sections) list only the province for this name.
