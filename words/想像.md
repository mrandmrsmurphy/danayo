## Notes
- [想](../characters/想.md) "think; imagine" + [像](../characters/像.md) "image; likeness"

想像 is to imagine, or imagination: the mind's picturing of what is not before the eyes. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xiǎngxiàng as "to imagine; to picture; to envision" and Japanese そうぞう as "imagination". Korean 상상 and Vietnamese tưởng tượng are the same word. Cantonese soeng2 zoeng6 matches.

It feeds [創作](創作.md), and the mind that does it is the [心](心.md). Making something from nothing is [創造](創造.md).
