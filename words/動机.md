## Notes
- [動 (char)](../characters/動%20(char).md) "move; act" + [机](../characters/机.md) "machine; loom"

動机 is a motive: the reason or desire that sets a person to act. The word is the form 動機 as it is written in Dan'a'yo, where the character 機 is an alias of the stand-in 机, so the file carries the stand-in form and keeps 動機 as an alias. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin dòngjī as "motive; intention" and, in music, "motif", and Japanese どうき as "motive; that which sets one into motion". Vietnamese động cơ is the same word, and Korean 동기 and Cantonese dung6 gei1 match.

It names the reason in a person's mind, where [要因](要因.md) is a factor in events and [原因](原因.md) is a cause. The music sense is not taken.
