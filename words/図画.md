## Notes
- [図](../characters/図.md) "diagram" + [画](../characters/画.md) "picture, drawing" — no stand-in relationship applies (図's own stand-in is [[図表]]; 画's is [[絵画]]).

図画 means a drawing or picture.

**Real bugs found and fixed**: `korean`/`vietnamese` had both been contaminated with the reading of the unrelated word 圖書 ("books, library" — 도서/đồ thư) instead of 図画's own compositional reading — corrected to 도화 (using 画's "picture" sense reading 화, not its "stroke" sense 획) and đồ hoạ. `aliases` accordingly dropped the mistakenly-included 圖書, keeping only genuine orthographic variants of 図画 itself. Japanese ずが and Mandarin túhuà were already correct. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄏ⺢ㄎ unique across both words and characters).
