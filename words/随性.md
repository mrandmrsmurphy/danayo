## Notes
- [随](../characters/随.md) "accompany" + [性](../characters/性.md) "gender, sex, quality, nature"

随性 means to follow one's own nature or disposition — a spontaneous, easygoing, carefree quality, "going with the flow" of one's own inclinations rather than external expectation. Mandarin 隨性/随性 (suíxìng), Cantonese 隨性 (ceoi4sing3), Japanese 随性 (ずいしょう), Korean 수성 (suseong), and Sino-Vietnamese tuỳ tính all preserve this meaning across the CJKV sphere.

Korean 수성 (suseong) is a coincidental homograph with the unrelated common word 水星 ("Mercury," the planet) — a real-Korean-only collision, not a Dan'a'yo homophone, since the two words are written with entirely different characters and are unrelated in meaning.
