## Notes
- [正 (char)](../characters/正%20(char).md) "correct" + [方](../characters/方.md) "direction" + [形 (char)](../characters/形%20(char).md) "shape, form, type" — no stand-in relationship applies (正's own stand-in is itself; 方's own is [[方向]]; 形's own is itself).

正方形 means square — literally "correct/regular square-direction shape," the geometric quadrilateral with four equal sides and four right angles.

Mandarin zhèngfāngxíng and Japanese せいほうけい (seihōkei) are both the real, standard, everyday geometric terms for "square" in their languages. Korean 정방형 is a real and correct Sino-Korean word for a square, though modern Korean math education more often reaches for 정사각형 ("correct four-angle shape") instead — both are genuine, with 정방형 the more classical/formal of the two. Vietnamese chính phương hình is a defensible compositional derivation, but the everyday Vietnamese word for this shape is the native hình vuông; chính phương on its own survives independently in Vietnamese as a number-theory term, "a perfect square" (số chính phương), rather than as part of a shape-word. `kwin` is false per the AND-rule: 正, 方, and 形 are all individually `kwin: false`.
