## Notes
- [迷 (char)](../characters/迷%20(char).md) "lost, confused" + [宮](../characters/宮.md) "palace, mansion" — literally "a lost/confusing palace." No stand-in relationship applies (迷's own `stand_in` is itself; 宮's own is [[宮廷]]).

迷宮 means a maze or labyrinth — specifically the "true" labyrinth sense, where the exit is genuinely hard or impossible to find (as opposed to [[迷路]]'s "garden maze" sense below, which always has a findable exit), and the word this vault uses for the figurative "unsolved mystery" sense as well (迷宮入り, "to go cold/unsolved," lit. "to enter the labyrinth" — a real, standard Japanese idiom).

Mandarin mígōng, Cantonese mai4 gung1, Japanese めいきゅう, Korean 미궁, and Vietnamese mê cung are all standard, fully compositional readings, with mê cung independently the real, standard Vietnamese word for "maze/labyrinth." `kwin` is false: 迷 is `kwin: false`, 宮 is `kwin: true`. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄝㄧㄍㄨㄫ unique across both words and characters).
