## Notes
- [遺 (char)](characters/遺%20(char).md) "leave behind, pass on" + [伝](characters/伝.md) "transmit" + [子](characters/子.md) "child, seed" — "gene," literally "that which transmits inheritance."

Mandarin yíchuánzǐ, cantonese wai4 cyun4 zi2, and korean 유전자 are all directly attested standard readings. **Found and fixed an archaic-kana typo on `japanese`**: ゐでんし used the obsolete kana ゐ (wi), which modern standard Japanese orthography (現代仮名遣い) always writes as い — corrected to いでんし. Vietnamese gen is the real, standard everyday Vietnamese word for "gene" (a direct loanword), used here rather than a compositional calque since no such calque is in genuine use. `遺傳子` is the standard traditional-character spelling variant (伝→傳), correctly listed as an alias. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 예줜즈 diverges from Sino-Korean 유전자 entirely. No homophones (注音 ⼶ㄧㄐ⼔ㄋㄐㄜ unique among both words and characters).

**Found and fixed a bare, un-ruby-formatted citation** on 子.md's own Words list (that page's broader Words section remains flagged for a dedicated cleanup pass). Citations on 遺(char).md and 伝.md were already correctly present. None of the three constituents' `stand_in` fields point to this compound.
