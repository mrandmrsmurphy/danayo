## Notes
- [遺 (char)](../characters/遺%20(char).md) "leave behind, pass on" + [伝](../characters/伝.md) "transmit" + [子](../characters/子.md) "child, seed" — "gene," literally "that which transmits inheritance."

Mandarin yíchuánzǐ, cantonese wai4 cyun4 zi2, and korean 유전자 are all directly attested standard readings.
