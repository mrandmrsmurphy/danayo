## Notes
- [言 (char)](../characters/言%20(char).md) "word, speak" is separately legitimized by [[言]] itself. [語](../characters/語.md) "language" is legitimized by this exact compound (its own stand-in). No cranberry.

Japanese げんご and korean 언어 (the real Japanese/Korean word order for "language") and vietnamese ngôn ngữ (also matching this word's own 言-then-語 order, and the real standard term) confirmed standard and compositional. Mandarin yǔyán and cantonese jyu5jin4 are the same real terms cited on [[語言]] (the reversed Mandarin/Cantonese word order) — Mandarin and Cantonese have no separate real word in this reversed order, so both entries legitimately cite the one real term.
