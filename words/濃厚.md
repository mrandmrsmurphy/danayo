## Notes
- [濃](../characters/濃.md) "concentrated" + [厚 (char)](../characters/厚%20(char).md) "thick" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 濃 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (厚's own stand-in is itself, so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

濃厚 means concentrated, thick, dense, strong.

Mandarin nónghòu, Cantonese nung4 hau5, Japanese のうこう, and Korean 농후 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 濃's and 厚's own stored citations exactly).
