## Notes
- [紀](../characters/紀.md) "chronicle, record" + [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year"

紀年 names the system or method by which years are counted and dated — chronology as a technical practice, encompassing era systems ([[紀元]], "era, epoch"), century counting ([[世紀]]), and dynastic era-name systems ([[改元]]). It's the general term for "how a calendar counts its years," as opposed to any one specific counted year.

Japanese きねん and Korean 기년 are both standard compositional Sino-readings. Vietnamese kỷ niên is likewise standard, related to the more common kỷ nguyên ("era," 紀元) and thế kỷ ("century," 世紀). No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧ·ㄋㄝㄋ unique across both words and characters).
