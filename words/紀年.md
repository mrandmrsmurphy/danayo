## Notes
- [紀](../characters/紀.md) "chronicle, record" + [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year"

紀年 is a chronology, a system of numbering years. English Wiktionary has an entry only for Chinese 紀年 (Mandarin jìnián, Cantonese gei2 nin4), glossed "chronological record of events; annal; chronicle", "year numbering system" and "to record chronologically". The Japanese きねん, Korean 기년 and Vietnamese kỷ niên fields are built from the readings of the two characters. Related are the [紀元](紀元.md), era, the [世紀](世紀.md), century, and the [改元](改元.md), change of era name.
