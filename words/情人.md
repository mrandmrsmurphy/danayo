## Notes
- [情](../characters/情.md) "feeling, affection" + [人](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" — "affection person," a sweetheart. Their own `stand_in` values: 情's is [[感情]]; 人's is itself; none points here, so 情人 is an independent compound.

情人 is a sweetheart: someone dear to one, a beloved. Mandarin qíngrén, Cantonese cing4 jan4 and Japanese じょうじん are attested, and Vietnamese tình nhân does too. By decision it is the sweetheart, set beside [[恋人]] (the lovers, the pair) and [[愛人]] (a lover, partner). Compare [[親愛]] (dear) and [[愛女]] (girlfriend).
