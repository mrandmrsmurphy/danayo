## Notes
- [婚](../characters/婚.md) "marriage" + [約](../characters/約.md) "agreement, promise" — literally "marriage agreement."

婚約 names the state or event of being engaged to be married — the promise itself and the period between it and the wedding — distinct from [[聘]]'s narrower "to betroth" (the act) and from [[嫁娶]]/[[婚姻]]'s own focus on the marriage itself rather than the engagement preceding it.

Mandarin hūnyuē, Cantonese fan1 joek3, Japanese こんやく (konyaku, an extremely common everyday word — 婚約者, "fiancé/fiancée"; 婚約指輪, "engagement ring"), Korean 혼약 (honyak), and Vietnamese hôn ước are all real, standard, fully compositional readings, attested in all five languages. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
