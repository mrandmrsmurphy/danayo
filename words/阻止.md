## Notes
- [阻](../characters/阻.md) "thwart, block, impede" + [止](../characters/止.md) "stop" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes 阻 as a bound character (止's own stand-in is [[中止]]; transitivity fails, so no `#cranberry`).

阻止 means to block, prevent, or thwart. Mandarin 阻止 (zǔzhǐ), Cantonese 阻止 (zo2zi2), Japanese 阻止 (そし), and Sino-Vietnamese trở chỉ are all everyday, straightforwardly compositional words for this meaning across the CJKV sphere.

Korean is the exception: the real Sino-Korean word for this meaning, 저지 (jeoji), is written with a different but phonetically similar character, 沮 (jeo, "to hinder"), rather than 阻 (jo) — so `korean` here is a real attested reading built on 沮止 rather than a naive concatenation of 阻's own stored 조 reading, the same kind of cross-character substitution seen elsewhere in the sweep (cf. [[関数]]'s Chinese/Korean/Vietnamese 函 vs. Japanese 関 split).
