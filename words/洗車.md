## Notes
- [洗 (char)](../characters/洗%20(char).md) "wash, rinse" + [車 (char)](../characters/車%20(char).md) "cart, car" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 洗's and 車's own stand-ins are themselves).

洗車 means to wash a car.

Mandarin xǐchē, Cantonese sai2 ce1, and Japanese せんしゃ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 세차 is the real everyday Korean word, using the common alternate reading 세 for 洗 (the same real-word-over-citation divergence documented on [[洗濯]]).

No existing lexipedia domain page fits: this vault's own [[lexipedia/scratch.md|scratch notes]] already flag 洗車 as a mere collocation of already-slotted [[洗]] ("wash") + 車 ("car"), not a distinct concept warranting its own domain citation — left unplaced per the "don't force a duplicate-synonym word onto a page" convention.
