## Notes
- [洗 (char)](../characters/洗%20(char).md) "wash, rinse" + [車 (char)](../characters/車%20(char).md) "cart, car" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 洗's and 車's own stand-ins are themselves).

洗車 means to wash a car.

Mandarin xǐchē, Cantonese sai2 ce1, and Japanese せんしゃ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 세차 is the real everyday Korean word, using the common alternate reading 세 for 洗 (the same real-word-over-citation divergence documented on [[洗濯]]). Fixed a real bug: `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` had all been contaminated to a naive transcription of 車's real Mandarin reading (차/ca/ㄑㄚ) instead of 車's own stored Dan'a'yo reading (촤/cwa/ㄑ⺢, confirmed against [[汽車]] and [[乗車]], both of which correctly preserve 촤/ㄑ⺢ in 諺文/注音 regardless of 羅馬字's w-simplification after vowel-final syllables). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with tẩy xa (洗's own tẩy + 車's own xa, matching the same citation choice as [[乗車]]'s thừa xa). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases: []`. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄋㄑ⺢ unique across both words and characters).
