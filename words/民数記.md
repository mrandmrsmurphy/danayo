## Notes
- [民](../characters/民.md) "people, population" + [数](../characters/数.md) "number, count" + [記](../characters/記.md) "record, account" — "Population Size Story," the book of the census. Their own `stand_in` values: 民's is [[人民]]; 数's is [[計数]]; 記's is [[記憶]]; none points here, so 民数記 is an independent compound.

民数記 is Numbers, the fourth book of the Torah, named for the two censuses of Israel, "the people counted." It is the fourth book of [[bible/聖経 (book)]], after [[附族記]]. Unlike the neologism for Leviticus, this is the real Chinese, Japanese and Korean name for the book: Mandarin mínshùjì, Cantonese man4 sou3 gei3, Japanese みんすうき and Korean 민수기 are all standard. It reads by meaning, 民 "people" plus 数 "number" plus 記 "record" ("Population Size Story"), which is also the meaning of the English title. The character 数 has the alias 數, so the Chinese spelling 民數記 is accepted. See [[基督敎]].
