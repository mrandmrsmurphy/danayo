## Notes
- [五 (char)](../characters/五%20(char).md) "five" + [万](../characters/万.md) "ten-thousand" — fifty thousand, 50,000.

Digit + 万, like [[二万]] and [[七万]]: the myriad (10,000) is the Sinitic grouping unit, so 50,000 is literally "five myriad" and is not built through a "thousand" step as in English. Japanese ごまん is the plain concatenation. Korean 오만 follows the Sino-Korean reading directly. Vietnamese năm vạn uses vạn, which is a live everyday numeral rather than only a classical one. Compare [[二万]] and [[七万]].
