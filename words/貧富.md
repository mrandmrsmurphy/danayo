## Notes
- [貧](../characters/貧.md) "poor" + [富](../characters/富.md) "rich"

貧富 is rich and poor: wealth and poverty taken together as a single scale. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin pínfù as "the rich and the poor" and Japanese ひんぷ as "wealth and poverty". Cantonese pan4 fu3 and Korean 빈부 match.

The word is usually met in a phrase about the difference between the two, such as the gap in 貧富の差. It pairs the poverty of [貧窮](貧窮.md) with the wealth of [富裕](富裕.md).
