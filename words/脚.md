## Notes
- [脚](../characters/脚%20(char).md) "leg" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 脚 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

脚 means the leg or foot, as in 脚踝 (ankle) and 馬脚 (lit. "horse's leg," figuratively "the cat out of the bag").

Mandarin jiǎo, cantonese goek3, and korean 각 (gak) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading あし (ashi, "leg, foot") rather than the on-readings キャク/キャ (KYAKU/KYA) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese cước is one of two stored candidates. No homophones.
