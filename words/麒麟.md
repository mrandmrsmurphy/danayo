## Notes
- [麒](../characters/麒.md) "male qilin" + [麟](../characters/麟.md) "female qilin" — **`#cranberry`**: both 麒's and 麟's own `stand_in` point to [[麒麟]] itself — a "dead paired-gender" compound (麒=male, 麟=female qilin, distinction now defunct), matching its listed entry on [[lookup/List of 連綿詞]].
- See also [[麒麟羚羊]]

Mandarin qílín, Cantonese kei4leon4, Korean 기린, and Sino-Vietnamese kỳ lân are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese キリン (katakana, though etymologically native/Sino) is used for both the mythical qilin and, by extension, the giraffe — the animal was named after the mythical creature when first described to Japanese observers.
