## Notes
- [侍](../characters/侍.md) "to attend, serve" + [奉](../characters/奉.md) "to serve, revere" — "to serve and attend to" (a master or high-ranking monk in Buddhism, or by extension one's parents/teachers). `korean: 시봉` is directly attested (confirmed via web search — 시봉하다, "to serve and honor"). `vietnamese: thị phụng` is compositional, paralleling well-attested siblings like phụng dưỡng and cung phụng.
