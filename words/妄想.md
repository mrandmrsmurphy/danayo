## Notes
- [妄](../characters/妄.md) "reckless, delusional" + [想](../characters/想.md) "think" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [妄](../characters/妄.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (想's own stand-in is [[思想]]).

妄想 means to have a delusion, or to be caught up in a fantasy — thinking recklessly or without grounding in reality.

Mandarin wàngxiǎng, Cantonese mong5 soeng2, Japanese もうそう, Korean 망상, and Vietnamese vọng tưởng are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄚㄫㄙㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
