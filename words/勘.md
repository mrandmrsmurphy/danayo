## Notes
- [勘 (char)](../characters/勘%20(char).md) "investigate; compare"

勘 combines [[力]] ("strength, effort") with phonetic [[甚]]. It means "to investigate, examine, verify by comparison" — 勘察 (kānchá, "to survey, investigate [a site]"), 勘誤 (kānwù, "to proofread and correct errors," literally "to investigate mistakes"), 校勘 (jiàokān, "textual collation, to compare and correct").

Mandarin kān, Cantonese ham3, and Japanese かん (kan, on'yomi, largely confined to formal/literary compounds) all preserve the sense. Korean 감 (gam) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 헤아리다 (healida, "to gauge, estimate, consider"). Vietnamese khám is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in khám xét ("to inspect, search") and khám nghiệm ("to examine, investigate [forensically]"); the character page's other stored candidate, khóm ("a clump, cluster"), is an unrelated native word and was not used here.

This word is part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read kam/캄/ㄎㄚㄇ): [[堪]] ("withstand") and [[龕]] ("shrine"), both still awaiting their own turn in this sweep — the reciprocal half of this callout will be completed on each when it comes up.
