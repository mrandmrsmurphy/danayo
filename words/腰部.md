## Notes
- [腰](../characters/腰.md) "waist" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 腰 cannot appear independently outside this compound. [部 (char)](../characters/部%20(char).md) "part" is separately legitimized by [[部]] itself.

腰部 combines 腰 ("waist") with 部 ("part") — "waist, waist area."

Mandarin yāobù, cantonese jiu1bou6 (space removed), japanese ようぶ (youbu), and korean 요부 (yobu) were all already correct, fully compositional readings.
