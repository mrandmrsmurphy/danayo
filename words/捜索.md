## Notes
- [捜](../characters/捜.md) "search, seek" + [索](../characters/索.md) "search, seek" — pairing two near-synonymous "search" roots; stand-in for both [捜](../characters/捜.md) and [索](../characters/索.md), neither of which can appear independently.

捜索/搜索 means to search for, seek, or investigate — covering both a literal physical search (a search party, a police search) and the more abstract sense of investigating or looking into something.

Mandarin sōusuǒ, Cantonese sau2 sok3, Korean 수색 (susaek), and Japanese そうさく (sōsaku) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real everyday word for "search" in its language. Vietnamese sưu sách is likewise compositional, from 捜's sưu and 索's own sách.
