## Notes
- [実](../characters/実.md) "real, true, solid" + [数](../characters/数.md) "number" — literally "real number," any number on the continuous number line: the union of the rational and irrational numbers, notated ℝ.

Mandarin shíshù, Cantonese sat6 sou3, Japanese じっすう, Korean 실수, and Vietnamese số thực are all standard, fully compositional, and genuinely living terms for this concept. Note a real Korean-internal homophone collision, unrelated to this word's own accuracy: 실수 (實數, "real number") is a perfect homophone of 실수 (失手, "mistake, blunder"), disambiguated only by context.
