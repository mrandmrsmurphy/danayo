## Notes
- [数](../characters/数.md) "count, number" + [量](../characters/量.md) "measure, weight, quantity" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [量](../characters/量.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (数's own stand-in is [[計数]]).

数量 means amount, weight, measure, or quantity.

Mandarin shùliàng, Cantonese sou3 loeng6, Japanese すうりょう, Korean 수량, and Vietnamese số lượng are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨㄌ⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
