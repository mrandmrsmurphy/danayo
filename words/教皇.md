## Notes
- [教](../characters/教.md) "teach, religion" + [皇](../characters/皇.md) "emperor, sovereign" — literally "religion's emperor," rendering the Pope as the sovereign head of the Church.

Mandarin jiàohuáng, Cantonese gaau3 wong4, Korean 교황 (gyohwang), and Japanese きょうこう (kyōkō) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real everyday term for "pope" in its language. Vietnamese giáo hoàng is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, matching 教's giáo and 皇's own hoàng, and is itself the real, everyday Vietnamese term (Giáo hoàng). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 굣황 diverges from Sino-Korean 교황 in the first syllable. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄍ⼄ㄨㄏ⺢ㄫ unique among both words and characters).
