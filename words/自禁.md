## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" is separately legitimized by [[自身]]. [禁](../characters/禁.md) "restrict, forbid" is separately legitimized by [[禁止]]. No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

自禁 combines 自 ("self") with 禁 ("restrict, forbid") — "self-control." Archaic; prefer [[自制]] in modern usage.

Mandarin zìjīn, cantonese zi6gam3 (space removed), and korean 자금 (jageum — coincidentally also the real, unrelated word for "funds, capital," but genuinely compositional here) were all already correct.
