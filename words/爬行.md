## Notes
- [爬](characters/爬.md) "crawl" + [行 (char)](characters/行%20(char).md) "to move, go" — stand-in for [[爬]], which cannot appear independently (行's own stand-in is itself, so `#cranberry` does not apply).

Mandarin páxíng, Cantonese paa4 hang4, and Korean 파행 were already correct and fully compositional. Fixed a real bug in `japanese`: はかう → はこう (HA+KOU), the correct compositional on'yomi reading. Fixed a contamination bug in `vietnamese`: bò sát (the unrelated word [[爬虫]]'s reading, "reptile") had been substituted here → corrected to bò hành (compositional, using 爬's own bò + 行's own hành). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases: []`. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄏㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
