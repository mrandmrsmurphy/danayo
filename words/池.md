## Notes
- [池](characters/池%20(char).md) "pond, reservoir" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 池 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

池 means pond, reservoir.

Mandarin chí, Cantonese ci4, Korean 지, and Vietnamese tri are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 池's own stored citations exactly.

池 shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading (ㄐㄨㄧ/jui/쥐) with [[知]] ("know") and [[酔]] ("drunk") — three otherwise-unrelated characters converging on the same Middle Chinese syllable; both carry the reciprocal callout.
