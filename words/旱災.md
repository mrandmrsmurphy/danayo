## Notes
- [旱](../characters/旱.md) "drought" + [災](../characters/災.md) "disaster, catastrophe" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [旱](../characters/旱.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (災's own stand-in is [[災害]]).

旱災 means drought.

Mandarin hànzāi, Cantonese hon5 zoi1, Japanese かんさい, Korean 한재, and Vietnamese hạn tai are all standard, fully compositional readings. Removed `旱魃`/`干魃` from `aliases`: both use 魃 ("drought demon"), a distinct character not derived from 災 — a different compound, not an orthographic variant of this word. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄋㄐㄚㄧ unique across both words and characters).
