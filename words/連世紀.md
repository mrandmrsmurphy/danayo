## Notes
- [連 (char)](../characters/連%20(char).md) "consecutive, repeated" + [世紀](世紀.md) "century" — "century after century."

Mandarin liánshìjì, cantonese lin4 sai3 gei2, japanese れんせいき, and korean 연세기 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese liên thế kỷ is the honest compositional reading (連's own liên + 世紀's own attested "thế kỷ"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 련서기 diverges from Sino-Korean 연세기 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄌ⼶ㄋㄙㄝㄍㄧ unique among both words and characters).

All three character-page citations (連, 世, 紀) already correctly present. None of the three constituents' `stand_in` fields point to this compound.
