## Notes
- [学](../characters/学.md) "study" + [説](../characters/説.md) "speak, theory" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [説](../characters/説.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (学's own stand-in is [[学習]]).

学説 means theory or doctrine — a systematic body of ideas explaining a phenomenon.

Mandarin xuéshuō, Cantonese hok6 syut3, Korean 학설, and Vietnamese học thuyết are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese has both the compositional がくせつ and the English loanword セオリー (theory) in active use. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄎㄙ⼔ㄊ unique across both words and characters).
