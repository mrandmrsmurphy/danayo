## Notes
- [違](characters/違.md) "violate, deviate" + [反 (char)](characters/反%20(char).md) "opposite, contrary" — "violate, transgress, infringe."

Mandarin wéifǎn, cantonese wai4 faan2, japanese いはん, korean 위반, and vietnamese vi phản are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 위폰 diverges from Sino-Korean 위반 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄨㄧㄈㄛㄋ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 違's own `stand_in` is this exact word (違反), so 違 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 違反; 反's own `stand_in` is instead 反 itself, so this word does not legitimize 反.
