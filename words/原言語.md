## Notes
- [原](../characters/原.md) "plain; original" + [言 (char)](../characters/言%20(char).md) "say; speech" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language" — "original language", built from [言語](言語.md), "language", with 原 in front. The stand-in of 原 is [原始](原始.md), and that of 語 is [言語](言語.md).

原言語 is a source language. English Wiktionary has no entry for 原言語 in any language, so this is a term of this vault, and the Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and Korean fields are built from the readings of the three characters. The Vietnamese field is omitted. In [文法 - 02音韻論](../grammar/文法%20-%2002音韻論.md) the term is used of the speaker's own language: a high allophonic difference is tolerated among the dialects influenced by each 原言語. A speaker's native language is the [母語](母語.md).
