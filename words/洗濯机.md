## Notes
- [洗](../characters/洗%20(char).md) "wash" + [濯](../characters/濯.md) "rinse" + [机](../characters/机.md) "machine" — "laundering machine." 濯's own `stand_in` is [[洗濯]], whose spelling this word reuses; 洗's is itself and 机's is [[机会]], so 洗濯机 is an independent compound.

洗濯机 is a washing machine. Japanese せんたくき and Korean 세탁기 use exactly these characters, built on [[洗濯]], "to launder". Mandarin instead says 洗衣机 (xǐyījī, "clothes-washing machine") and Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for 洗濯機, so the `mandarin` and `cantonese` fields are the readings of the characters rather than attested words. Dan'a'yo follows the Japanese/Korean form because it sits on the existing word 洗濯. The spelling 洗濯機 is an alias.

Compare [[机器]] (machine).
