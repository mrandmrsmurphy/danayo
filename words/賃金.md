## Notes
- [賃 (char)](../characters/賃%20(char).md) "hire; pay for hire" + [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "money; metal" — wage.

賃金 is a wage: money paid to a worker for labor, usually by the hour, day or piece. Japanese ちんぎん and Korean 임금 are the ordinary word; Mandarin lìnjīn exists (Wiktionary: "rent or wage, especially for hiring people") but is not Mandarin's everyday word, which is 工資 (gōngzī), and Mandarin readers may need the context. Dan'a'yo chose 賃金 as the shared compound. It differs from [[俸給]] and [[俸祿]], the official pay of a government post, and from [[収入]], income of any kind, and [[利潤]], profit. The wage actually paid is [[支付]].
