## Notes
- [恐](../characters/恐.md) "fear, dread" + [慌](../characters/慌.md) "panic, flustered" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes both [恐](../characters/恐.md) and [慌](../characters/慌.md) as independent Dan'a'yo entries.

恐慌 means nervous, frantic, or panicky — a state of alarmed distress, also used for an economic panic/crisis.

Mandarin kǒnghuāng, Cantonese hung2 fong1, Japanese きょうこう, Korean 공황, and Vietnamese khủng hoảng are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄛㄫㄏ⺢ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
