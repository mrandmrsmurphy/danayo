## Notes
- [幸](../characters/幸.md) "lucky, fortunate" + [福 (char)](../characters/福%20(char).md) "blessing, happiness, good fortune, prosperity" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (幸's is 幸運, 福's is bare 福).

Mandarin xìngfú, Cantonese hang6 fuk1, Japanese こうふく, Korean 행복, and Vietnamese hạnh phúc are all everyday words for "happy; blessed."
