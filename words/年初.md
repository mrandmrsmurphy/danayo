## Notes
- [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year" + [初](../characters/初.md) "beginning"

年初 names the early part of a calendar year — used for business and administrative timing (年初計画, "beginning-of-year plan"), in direct contrast with its antonym [[年末]] ("year-end"). A near-synonym, 年首 (niánshǒu), is more literary/formal and less common in everyday usage than 年初.

Japanese ねんしょ and Korean 연초 are both standard compositional Sino-readings. Vietnamese niên sơ (attested, equivalent to the less common niên thủ) likewise means "beginning of the year." No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄝㄋㄑㄛ unique across both words and characters).
