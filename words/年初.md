## Notes
- [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year" + [初](../characters/初.md) "beginning"

年初 is the beginning of the year. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 年初 (Mandarin niánchū, Cantonese nin4 co1) and Japanese ねんしょ both as "beginning of the year". Its Korean entry for 연초 lists only "cigarette" and "tobacco", so Korean 연초 here, for the beginning of the year, is built from the characters. It has no Vietnamese entry, so Vietnamese niên sơ is likewise built from the characters.

Its opposite is [[年末]], the end of the year, and the middle of the year is [[年中]].
