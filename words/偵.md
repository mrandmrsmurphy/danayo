## Notes
- [偵 (char)](../characters/偵%20(char).md) "spy"

偵 combines [[人]] ("person") with phonetic [[貞]]. It means "to spy, to scout, to investigate covertly" — the core sense behind 偵察 (zhēnchá, "to reconnoiter, scout"), 偵探 (zhēntàn, "detective"), and 密偵 ("a covert spy, secret agent").

Mandarin zhēn, Cantonese zing1, and Japanese てい (tei, on'yomi, largely confined to literary/formal compounds like 偵察 ていさつ "reconnaissance") all preserve the sense. Korean 정 (jeong) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 염탐하다 (yeomtamhada, "to spy, snoop around"). Vietnamese trinh is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in trinh sát (偵察, "reconnaissance, scouting") and trinh thám (偵探, "detective"); the character page's other two stored candidates, rình (a native Vietnamese verb meaning "to lurk, watch stealthily" — semantically close but not the Hán Việt reading itself) and triệng (unclear, likely corpus noise), were not used here.
