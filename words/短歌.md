## Notes
- [短 (char)](../characters/短%20(char).md) "short" + [歌](../characters/歌.md) "song; poem"

短歌 is a tanka: a Japanese poem of five lines in 5, 7, 5, 7 and 7 sounds. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin duǎngē as "ballad" and Japanese たんか as "tanka; a form of Japanese verse in five lines of 5-7-5-7-7 syllables.". Korean 단가 is the same word. The Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the characters.

It is the chief form of the [和歌](和歌.md), and its first three lines are the seed of the [俳句](俳句.md).
