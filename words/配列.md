## Etymology
- [配 (char)](../characters/配%20(char).md) "match, pair" + [列](../characters/列.md) "arrange, line up"

## Notes
Mandarin pèiliè, cantonese pui3 lit6, and japanese はいれつ are all directly attested standard readings. Korean 배렬 and vietnamese phối liệt are both honest compositional readings (配's own 배/phối + 列's own 렬/liệt); 배렬 correctly follows this vault's North Korean/문화어 convention (no 두음법칙 shift to 배열). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 푀럳 diverges from Sino-Korean 배렬 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄛㄧㄌㄝㄊ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 列's own `stand_in` is this exact word (配列), so 列 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 配列; 配's own `stand_in` is instead 配 itself, so this word does not legitimize 配.
