## Notes
- [玻](../characters/玻.md) + [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal" — a transliteration of *Bohr*, after physicist Niels Bohr. 玻 is the standard Mandarin transliteration syllable for "Bohr" (as in 玻爾), used alone here rather than the fuller two-character form, in keeping with this system's preference for compact one-syllable eponyms where the source name itself is monosyllabic. Japanese and Korean instead borrow the IUPAC name as a loanword (ボーリウム, 보륨).

**Content added**: `mandarin`/`cantonese` were both blank; filled with `bō`/`bo1`, the readings of the real standard Chinese element character 𨨏 (metal radical + phonetic component related to 玻/波) — coined in 1998 by the PRC's terminology committee for element 107, following the same avoided-single-character convention used throughout this neologism series. This matches [[玻]]'s own reading exactly, confirming 玻 was chosen here precisely because it already carries the "Bohr" transliteration value. `vietnamese` was likewise blank; filled with `bohri`, a direct phonetic loan from the IUPAC name. `kwin` was `false`; both [[玻]] (char) and [[金]] (char) are individually kwin, so the AND-rule was actually satisfied — corrected to `true`.
