## Notes
- [華](../characters/華.md) "Chinese" + [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person"

華人 is a person of Chinese descent or ethnicity, especially one who lives outside China. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin huárén as "person of Chinese origin or ethnicity" and Japanese かじん as "Chinese people". Korean 화인 and Cantonese waa4 jan4 match.

It names descent and ethnicity, while [中国人](中国人.md) names a person of the Chinese state. The civilization behind the name is [中華](中華.md), and the majority ethnic group is [漢族](漢族.md).
