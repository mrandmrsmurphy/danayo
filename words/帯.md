## Notes
- [帯 (char)](../characters/帯%20(char).md) "sash; belt" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [帯 (char)](../characters/帯%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

帯 names a woven sash or belt (象形, depicting a belt with hanging ends), extended figuratively to any belt-like band or zone (地帯 "zone, area," 熱帯 "the tropics," 小行星帯 "asteroid belt") and, more abstractly, to carrying or bearing something with oneself (携帯 "portable, to carry").

Japanese たい, Korean 대, and Vietnamese đai (the character's own field also lists đái/đới, both genuine alternate Sino-Vietnamese readings) are all standard readings. **A genuine triple Dan'a'yo homophone**: 帯, [[太]], and [[戴]] all share the identical reading (tai/태/ㄊㄚㄧ) — added reciprocal homophone callouts to all three pages.

**Correction (2026-08-27)**: `korean` had been stored as the literal string "null" (the same bug shape as [[尚]]/[[巻]]) instead of an actual value — corrected to 대.
