## Notes
- [大](../characters/大%20(char).md) "great" + [使](../characters/使.md) "envoy, messenger" + [館](../characters/館.md) "building, hall" — no stand-in relationship applies (大 is its own stand-in; 使's own is [[使者]]; 館's is [[公館]]).

大使館 means embassy.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `japanese` had a stray invisible character embedded in the reading (たいし​かん → たいしかん). `cantonese: daai6 si3 gun2` uses si3 (使's "envoy" sense) rather than its citation tone si2 ("to use") — a legitimate sense-dependent split, matching the pattern seen on other multi-reading characters. Korean 대사관 and Vietnamese đại sứ quán are both standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄧㄙㄧㄍ⺢ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
