## Notes
- [膣](../characters/膣%20(char).md) "cunt" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 膣 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo. A vulgar/crude term; the neutral anatomical equivalent is [[陰道]].

Mandarin zhì, cantonese zat6, and korean 질 (jil) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the on-reading チツ (CHITSU) — real dictionaries record no native kun-reading for this character; the character page's previously-stored `japanese_native: ちち` was unverifiable and almost certainly erroneous (ちち isn't attested for this meaning in any dictionary; likely confused with 乳 or 父), corrected to `ø`. Vietnamese chất is one of two stored candidates. Part of a genuine three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group with [[直]] ("straight") and [[蛭]] ("leech") — already documented.
