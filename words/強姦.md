## Notes
- [強 (char)](../characters/強%20(char).md) "strong; powerful" + [姦](../characters/姦.md) "fornicate"

強姦 is rape: sexual intercourse forced upon a person. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin qiángjiān as "to rape" and, figuratively, "to impose one's beliefs on others against their will", and gives Japanese ごうかん as "rape; to rape". Korean 강간 and Cantonese koeng4 gaan1 are the matching forms. Dan'a'yo takes only the literal legal sense.

The word is a serious crime in every legal system of the sphere. It is a crime against the person, set beside [傷害](傷害.md), assault, and [誘拐](誘拐.md), kidnapping, and it is distinct from [痴漢](痴漢.md), the molester.
