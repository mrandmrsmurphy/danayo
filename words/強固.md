## Notes
- [強](../characters/強%20(char).md) "strong, powerful" + [固](../characters/固.md) "solid, strong" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [固](../characters/固.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (強's own stand-in is itself).

強固 means strong or solid — firm and unyielding, resistant to change or attack.

Mandarin qiánggù, Cantonese koeng4 gu3, Japanese きょうこ, Korean 강고, and Vietnamese cường cố are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `korean` had been contaminated with the near-synonym 堅固's reading (견고, using 堅 not 強) — corrected to compositional 강고. Filled blank `cantonese`/`vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼘ㄫㄍㄛ unique across both words and characters).
