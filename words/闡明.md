## Notes
- [闡](../characters/闡.md) "to open up; clarify; expound" + [明 (char)](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright; clear" — stand-in for [闡](../characters/闡.md), which cannot appear independently

闡明 is "to clarify; to expound; to explain," and in Japanese and Korean also the noun "explanation." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 闡明 (chǎnmíng, Cantonese zin2 ming4) is a verb "to clarify; to expound; to explain"; Japanese せんめい is both a noun "explanation" and a suru-verb "to explain"; and Korean 천명 is the hanja form of "explanation; clarification." Wiktionary has no Vietnamese entry for it, so Vietnamese xiển minh is a compositional reading from the Sino-Vietnamese xiển (闡) and minh (明), given here as a hedged, unattested-in-dictionaries form.

The word is used in the Dan'a'yo rendering of the 1919 Korean Declaration of Independence ([宣言書](../translation/宣言書.md)), where it is the word for the Korean 克明 "make clear" (the declaration's vow to make the great principle of human equality plain to the world), replacing [克明](克明.md) there. The two differ in emphasis: 克明 stresses thoroughness and discernment, while 闡明 is the act of opening a hidden or obscure matter up to view and setting it out, which suits a declaration addressed to the world.

It is related to [明白](明白.md) "clear; obvious," [明瞭](明瞭.md) "apparent; clear" and [彰明](彰明.md) "clear; manifest," which share 明.
