## Notes
- [佛 (char)](../characters/佛%20(char).md) "Buddha" + [像](../characters/像.md) "image"

佛像 is a statue or image of the Buddha. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin fóxiàng as "any icon or statue depicting a Chinese god, saint, or immortal" and Japanese ぶつぞう. Korean 불상 is the same word. Cantonese fat6 zoeng6 matches.

It is a kind of [彫像](彫像.md), statue, cast in bronze as a [銅像](銅像.md) or carved in wood or stone, and belongs to [佛教](佛教.md).
