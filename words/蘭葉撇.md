## Notes
- [蘭](../characters/蘭.md) "orchid" + [葉 (char)](../characters/葉%20(char).md) "leaf" + [撇 (char)](../characters/撇%20(char).md) "left-falling stroke"

**蘭葉撇** is the *orchid-leaf* left-falling stroke, a 撇 ([[撇]]) that is thin at both ends and thick in the middle, like the long, tapering leaf of an orchid. Chinese instruction says it should be written quickly and with force. The name is attested in Chinese regular-script teaching, for example in descriptions of the strokes of Ouyang Xun's 九成宮醴泉銘 (*Jiuchenggong liquan ming*).

It is the first of the named varieties of 撇 on the [[Stroke]] page, beside the [[直撇]]. The name comes from a natural image, in the same manner as the Yuan-dynasty figurative name [[鳥啄]] for the stroke class as a whole.

The Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and North Korean forms are compositional, built from the constituent words and characters. No Vietnamese form is given.
