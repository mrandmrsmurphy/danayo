## Notes
- [蘭](../characters/蘭.md) "orchid" + [葉 (char)](../characters/葉%20(char).md) "leaf" + [撇 (char)](../characters/撇%20(char).md) "left-falling stroke"

Wiktionary has no entry for 蘭葉撇; it is a compound of 蘭 "orchid", 葉 "leaf" and 撇, which Wiktionary glosses in Chinese calligraphy as "stroke that falls downwards towards the left (㇒)". It names the orchid-leaf left-falling stroke, a variety of 撇 listed on the [[Stroke]] page, beside the [[直撇]]. Wikipedia (Eight Principles of Yong) records 鳥啄 as Li Puguang's name, in the Yuan dynasty, for the left-falling principle, see [[鳥啄]]. Its readings are compositional.
