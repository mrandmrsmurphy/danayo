## Notes
- [旅](../characters/旅.md) "travel" + [館](../characters/館.md) "lodge, hall" — "traveller's lodge." 旅's own `stand_in` is [[旅行]] and 館's is [[公館]]; neither points here, so 旅館 is an independent compound.

旅館 is an inn or hotel: a building where travellers pay to stay. Mandarin lǚguǎn, Cantonese leoi5 gun2, Japanese りょかん, Korean 여관 (려관 in the North) and Vietnamese lữ quán use it. In Japanese 旅館 specifically names a traditional inn with tatami rooms, as against a Western-style hotel. In Korean 여관 is a modest motel-like lodging. Compare [[飯店]] (restaurant, hotel) and [[宿舎]] (lodge, dormitory).
