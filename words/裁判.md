## Notes
- [裁](../characters/裁.md) "cut; judge, adjudicate" + [判](../characters/判.md) "judge, decide"

裁判 is the trial: the proceeding in which a court hears a case and decides it. The senses differ across the sphere. Wiktionary gives Japanese さいばん as "trial; court; to try", while Mandarin cáipàn covers the legal "to judge, to adjudicate; judgement" and also the sports sense "umpire, referee". Dan'a'yo takes the legal trial only; the umpire sense is not carried. Cantonese coi4 pun3, Korean 재판 and Vietnamese tài phán (a Hán Việt form meaning "to act as judge") are the matching readings.

[裁判所](裁判所.md) is the building and [法庭](法庭.md) the court as a proceeding or institution. 裁判 is the process held there, and [判決](判決.md) is what it produces. The person who presides is the [裁判官](裁判官.md).
