## Notes
- [汀](characters/汀%20(char).md) "sand bar" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 汀 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

汀 means sand bar, a water's-edge flat.

Mandarin tīng, Cantonese ting1, Korean 정, and Vietnamese đinh are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 汀's own stored citations exactly.
