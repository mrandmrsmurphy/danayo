## Notes
- [格 (char)](../characters/格%20(char).md) "standard; grade" + [差](../characters/差.md) "difference"

格差 is a gap or disparity: the difference between levels, especially between social groups. Wiktionary glosses Japanese かくさ as "difference, especially in social orders". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 격차 matches.

It names the size of the difference in income, status or opportunity, where [差異](差異.md) is a difference of any kind. The opposite of an unequal society is [平等](平等.md), equality, and its negation is [不均](不均.md).
