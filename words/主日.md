## Notes
- [主](../characters/主.md) "lord; master" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day; sun"

主日 is the Christian liturgical term for Sunday — the Lord's Day, the first day of the week on which the resurrection is commemorated. The compound is used across Chinese, Korean, and Vietnamese Christian communities as the formal term for Sunday in a devotional register, as distinct from the secular weekday name (星期日, 일요일, etc.). It appears in compounds such as 主日崇拜 ("Sunday worship"), 主日學 ("Sunday school"), and — already documented on both character pages — 棕枝主日 ("Palm Sunday"). Japanese しゅじつ is comparatively rare, appearing mainly in translated liturgical and biblical texts rather than everyday Japanese Christian speech, which more often uses 主の日 or simply 日曜日.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 주닏 diverges from Sino-Korean 주일 in the second syllable — 日 reads 닏 (nid/ㄋㄧㄊ) in Dan'a'yo but 일 (il) in Sino-Korean, the same MC 日-initial n-/∅ split seen in [[中国人]], [[中耳]], and [[主人]].
