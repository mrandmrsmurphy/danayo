## Notes
- [水 (char)](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color"

水色 is most established as a color term in Japanese (みずいろ, mizu-iro), where it denotes a pale, transparent sky blue — the color of clear water in a shallow basin or of a winter sky. It is one of the classic traditional Japanese colors (伝統色). English Wiktionary's Japanese entry glosses it "light blue, sky blue, turquoise blue, aqua blue, pale blue". I did not find a Mandarin dictionary entry for it; my impression that Mandarin 水色 is more often the generic color of water, with sky blue usually 天蓝色, is unchecked. In Korean, 수색 can also mean "search" (搜索), creating a homophonic ambiguity that limits the color term's currency.

In Dan'a'yo 水色 takes the Japanese-anchored sense of a pale, transparent light blue, so that the generic "color of water" reading found in Mandarin is not the intended one.
