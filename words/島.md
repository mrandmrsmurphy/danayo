## Notes
- [島 (char)](../characters/島%20(char).md) "island" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [島 (char)](../characters/島%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

島 names an island, a 形声 character combining phonetic 鳥 ("bird" — islands as places birds land and rest) with semantic 山 ("mountain," land rising from the sea).

Japanese とう, Korean 도, and Vietnamese đảo are all standard readings — the character's own field also lists a genuine Nôm-only reading, láo (used phonetically in native words like lếu láo, "insolent," unrelated in meaning). **A genuine triple Dan'a'yo homophone**: 島, [[倒]], and [[超]] all share the identical reading (tau/탓/ㄊㄚㄨ) — the reciprocal callout was already correctly in place on [[倒]]'s page; added it here and to [[超]] as well.
