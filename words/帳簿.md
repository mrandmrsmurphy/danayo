## Notes
- [帳](../characters/帳.md) "tent; screen" (here extended to "records, accounts") + [簿](../characters/簿.md) "register" — this word is the stand-in for [簿](../characters/簿.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

帳簿 names an account book or ledger — the standard word for a record book tracking income and expenditure, still ordinary business vocabulary in all four languages today.

Japanese ちょうぼ, Korean 장부, and Vietnamese trướng bạ (verified — historically also naming a feudal Vietnamese village record-keeper's office) are all standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐ⺢ㄫㄅㄛ unique across both words and characters).
