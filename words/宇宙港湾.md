## Notes
- [宇](../characters/宇.md) "universe, space" + [宙](../characters/宙%20(char).md) "time, space" + [港](../characters/港.md) "harbor, port" + [湾](../characters/湾.md) "bay, harbor" — "space harbor," a spaceport. Their own `stand_in` values: 宇's is [[宇宙]]; 宙's is itself; 港's is [[港湾]]; 湾's is [[海湾]]; none points here, so 宇宙港湾 is an independent compound.

宇宙港湾 is a spaceport: a harbor for spacecraft. It is a **neologism**, built from [[宇宙]] (space) and [[港湾]] (harbor). Japanese says 宇宙港 and Korean 우주항 (宇宙港) and Mandarin 太空港, which are the same idea in shorter or different words, so Dan'a'yo uses the two existing words together. Compare [[海港]] (seaport).
