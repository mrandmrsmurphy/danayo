## Notes
- [境](../characters/境.md) "border" + [界](../characters/界.md) "boundary" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [界](../characters/界.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (境's own stand-in is [[地境]]).

境界 means a boundary or zone — the line or realm dividing one area from another.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `cantonese` was blank — filled ging2 gaai3 (compositional). Japanese きょうかい, Korean 경계, and Vietnamese cảnh giới are all standard readings. **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 境界 and [[警戒]] ("vigilant, be on guard") share an identical reading — standardized the reciprocal callout (see [[警戒]]'s own page for a detailed note on how Vietnamese avoids this collision natively).
