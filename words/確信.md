## Notes
- [確](../characters/確.md) "certain, sure" + [信](../characters/信.md) "believe, trust" — no stand-in relationship applies (確's own `stand_in` is [[確実]]; 信's own is [[信任]]).

確信 means to be confident, convinced, certain of something — the standard word for Rosenfelder's "confident" in this domain.

Mandarin quèxìn, Cantonese kok3 seon3, Japanese かくしん, Korean 확신, and Vietnamese xác tín are all standard, fully compositional readings. `kwin` is false: 確's own `kwin` is false, 信's is true → compound false. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄚㄎㄙㄧㄋ unique across both words and characters).
