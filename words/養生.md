## Notes
- [養](../characters/養.md) "foster, raise" + [生](../characters/生.md) "life" — no `#cranberry`: 養's own `stand_in` is [[養育]], 生's is [[生活]]; neither points here.

養生 (yǎngshēng) is the standard Mandarin word for "health preservation, wellness practice" (a traditional East Asian concept encompassing diet, exercise, and lifestyle), and Cantonese 養生 (joeng5sang1) likewise. Japanese ようじょう (yōjō) and Korean 양생 (yangsaeng, also used in construction for "curing," as of concrete) are both standard terms, as is Sino-Vietnamese dưỡng sinh — a very common, widely used term in traditional health and wellness contexts across all five languages.
