## Notes
- [斐](../characters/斐.md) "graceful" + [然 (char)](../characters/然%20(char).md) "so"
- This word is the viable form of [斐](../characters/斐.md): the character's meaning (graceful, elegant) is identical, but 斐 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 斐然 is its `stand_in` compound.

斐然 describes something striking and accomplished: "brilliant, splendid, remarkable", most often of results, accomplishments or literary talent (成績斐然, "brilliant results"). It goes back to the 論語 (公冶長), where Confucius says his young followers 斐然成章, "are splendidly accomplished in their writing" though still unrefined; 斐 is a patterned, decorative look. Mandarin fěirán, Cantonese fei2 jin4, Japanese ひぜん (hizen; 斐然たる) and Korean 비연 (biyeon) are the same literary word.

Dan'a'yo-specific sense: the shared one, with a literary, formal register. In the Valuation domain it sits in the moral and aesthetic judgment group beside [[妙]] (exquisite, subtle) as the word for conspicuous, admirable achievement.
