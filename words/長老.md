## Notes
- [長 (char)](../characters/長%20(char).md) "long" + [老 (char)](../characters/老%20(char).md) "old"

長老 is an elder. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 長老 (Mandarin zhǎnglǎo, Cantonese zoeng2 lou5) as "elder; senior; oldster" and, in Christianity, "presbyter; elder". It lists Japanese ちょうろう and Korean 장로 entries.
