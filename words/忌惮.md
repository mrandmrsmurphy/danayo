## Notes
- [忌](../characters/忌.md) "taboo" + [惮](../characters/惮.md) "dread; fear" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [惮](../characters/惮.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry, which is too bounded to stand alone (忌's own stand-in is [[忌諱]]).

忌惮 means fear, restraint, or hesitation — being wary of something out of dread or caution.

Mandarin jìdàn, Cantonese gei6 daan6, Japanese きたん, Korean 기탄, and Vietnamese kị đạn are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄉㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
