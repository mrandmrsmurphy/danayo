## Notes
- [辺](../characters/辺.md) "side, edge" + [縁](../characters/縁.md) "border, rim" — "edge and border," a rim. Their own `stand_in` values: 辺's is [[辺界]]; 縁's is [[因縁]]; none points here, so 辺縁 is an independent compound.

辺縁 is a rim: the outer edge or margin of something, as of a plate or a region. Mandarin biānyuán (邊緣) and Cantonese bin1 jyun4 are the ordinary words, and Japanese へんえん and Korean 변연 are the same compound, used in technical writing. Compare [[輪郭]] (outline) and [[周辺]] (surroundings).
