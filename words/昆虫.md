## Notes
- [昆 (char)](characters/昆%20(char).md) "swarm; descendants" + [虫](characters/虫.md) "bug" — stand-in for [[虫]], which cannot appear independently (except as a bare, ruby-less determiner).

昆虫 means "insect." 虫 alone cannot stand as an ordinary Dan'a'yo word — this compound is its stand-in, though 虫 retains a special exception as a determiner, appearing without ruby or accompanying characters in that narrower grammatical role.

Mandarin kūnchóng and Cantonese kwan1 cung4 preserve the sense directly. Japanese こんちゅう (konchū) is the ordinary, everyday word for "insect," fully productive (昆虫学, konchūgaku, "entomology"). Korean 곤충 (gonchung) parallels the Sino-xenic reading and is likewise standard vocabulary. Vietnamese côn trùng is the standard Hán Việt reading, equally common in everyday speech.
