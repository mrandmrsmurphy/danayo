## Notes
- [矮](../characters/矮%20(char).md) "short (person)" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 矮 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

矮 describes a person of short stature (as opposed to 短, which covers physical objects and abstract durations). It also extends to figurative and technical senses: 矮小 ("dwarfish, puny"), and in modern astronomy, 矮行星 ("dwarf planet") and 矮星 ("dwarf star," as in white dwarf).

Mandarin ǎi, Cantonese ai2, Japanese わい (wai), Korean 왜 (wae), and Vietnamese ải are all standard, fully compositional readings.
