## Notes
- [祝](../characters/祝.md) "celebrate" + [賀](../characters/賀.md) "congratulate" — 賀's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 祝's own is [[慶祝]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 賀 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

祝賀 means to congratulate or celebrate an achievement or happy occasion — 祝賀会 ("celebration party"), 祝賀メッセージ ("congratulatory message").

Mandarin zhùhè, Cantonese zuk1 ho6, Japanese しゅくが (shukuga), and Korean 축하 (chukha, an extremely common everyday word for "congratulations") are all fully compositional. Vietnamese chúc hạ is compositional; everyday Vietnamese more often uses chúc mừng for "congratulations."
