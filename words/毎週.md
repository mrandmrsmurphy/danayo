## Notes
- [毎](../characters/毎%20(char).md) "each" + [週](../characters/週.md) "week, cycle" — no stand-in relationship applies (毎's own stand-in is itself; 週's own is [[週日]]).

毎週 means every week.

Mandarin měizhōu, Cantonese mui5 zau1, Japanese まいしゅう, and Korean 매주 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 毎's and 週's own stored citations exactly).
