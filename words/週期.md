## Notes
- [週](characters/週.md) "week, cycle" + [期 (char)](characters/期%20(char).md) "period, term" — "period, cycle."

Mandarin has two attested pronunciations (zhōuqī/zhōuqí, reflecting 期's own multiple readings), cantonese zau1 kei4, japanese しゅうき, korean 주기, and vietnamese chu kỳ are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly (週 and its close relative 周 share identical mandarin/cantonese/korean/vietnamese readings). `周期` is a genuinely common alternate written form using 周 in place of 週 — kept as an alias since 周's own field values are byte-for-byte identical to 週's. **Found and fixed a self-referential aliases-entry bug**: `週期` had been listed as its own alias, which is incoherent (removed). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 줏키 diverges from Sino-Korean 주기 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄨㄛㄎㄧ unique among both words and characters).
