## Notes
"Lipstick." `japanese: くちべに` is itself a native compound (口 kuchi + 紅 beni, "rouge"); `korean: 립스틱` is the direct English loanword. `vietnamese: son môi` is likewise the real, native everyday Vietnamese term ("lip color/rouge," son itself a loan from French but fully naturalized), rather than a Sino-Vietnamese calque of these two characters (domain knowledge, not independently confirmed by search).
