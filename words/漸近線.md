## Notes
- [漸](../characters/漸.md) "gradually" + [近 (char)](../characters/近%20(char).md) "near" + [線](../characters/線.md) "line, clue" — no stand-in relationship applies (漸's own stand-in is [[漸漸]]; 近's own is itself; 線's own is [[直線]]).

漸近線 means asymptote.

Mandarin jiànjìnxiàn, Cantonese zim6 gan6 sin3, Japanese ぜんきんせん, and Korean 점근선 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 漸's, 近's, and 線's own stored citations exactly), and also the real, standard mathematical term in each language.
