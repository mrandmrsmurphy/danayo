## Notes
- [首](../characters/首.md) "head, prime" + [長](../characters/長%20(char).md) "long" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [首](../characters/首.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (長's own stand-in is [[長]]).

首長 means chief, leader, or emir.

Mandarin shǒuzhǎng, Cantonese sau2 zoeng2, Japanese しゅちょう, Korean 수장, and Vietnamese thủ trưởng are all standard, fully compositional readings. Homophone of [[手掌]] "palm" (both 注音 ㄙ⼜ㄐㄚㄫ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated compounds.
