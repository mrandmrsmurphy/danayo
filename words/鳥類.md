## Notes
- [鳥 (char)](../characters/鳥%20(char).md) "bird" + [類](../characters/類.md) "kind, category" — no `#cranberry`: 鳥's own `stand_in` is 鳥 itself, 類's is [[種類]]; neither points here.

Mandarin niǎolèi, Cantonese niu5leoi6, Japanese ちょうるい, Korean 조류, and Sino-Vietnamese điểu loại are all standard, fully compositional readings for the biological category "bird."
