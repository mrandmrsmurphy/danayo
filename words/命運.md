## Etymology
- [命](../characters/命.md) "life, fate" + [運](../characters/運.md) "move, transport"

## Notes
- "Fate, destiny, doom" — the reversed compound of [[運命]] (unmyeng, 運=movement + 命=fate), naming the same concept with the same two characters in opposite order. As [[運命]]'s own page documents, Mandarin actually prefers this 命-first ordering (mìngyùn is more common than yùnmìng), while Japanese strongly prefers the 運命 ordering (うんめい) instead. **Content bug found and fixed**: this page previously claimed "Stand-in for [[命]]" — but [[命]]'s own `stand_in` field points to [[運命]], not this reversed compound; the stray claim was removed. `vietnamese: vận mệnh` is the same fixed Sino-Vietnamese compound as [[運命]]'s own vận mệnh — Vietnamese does not independently vary the character order the way Mandarin does, so there is no separately-attested "mệnh vận" form. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 명운 matches Sino-Korean 명운 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄇ⼶ㄫㄨㄋ unique among both words and characters).
