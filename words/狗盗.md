## Notes
- [狗](../characters/狗.md) "hound" + [盗](../characters/盗.md) "burgle" — no stand-in relationship applies (狗's own stand-in is [[../lookup/List of 名専字]]; 盗's own is [[窃盗]]).

狗盗 means a sneak thief, one who steals like a dog.

Mandarin gǒudào, Cantonese gau2 dou6, Japanese くとう, Korean 구도 and Vietnamese cẩu đạo are all fully compositional, as in the idiom 鷄鳴狗盜, "crowing like a cock and thieving like a dog".
