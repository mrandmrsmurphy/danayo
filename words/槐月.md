## Notes
- [槐](../characters/槐.md) "pagoda tree" + [月](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — no stand-in relationship applies (槐's own stand-in is [[槐樹]]; 月's own is itself).

Fourth month of the ancient Chinese poetic calendar. The pagoda tree (槐, Japanese pagodatree / Chinese scholar tree) was traditionally planted at government offices and academies. Its cream-colored blossoms in early summer gave this month an association with scholarly ambition and civil service.

Mandarin huáiyuè, Cantonese waai4 jyut6, and Korean 괴월 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese えんじゅつき reads both halves natively (槐's kun'yomi えんじゅ "pagoda tree" + 月's kun'yomi つき "month") rather than the Sino-Japanese on'yomi concatenation かいげつ, matching the established poetic-month convention (see [[寅月]], [[卯月]], [[未月]], [[杏月]], [[桂月]], [[桃月]], [[榴月]]). Vietnamese tháng hòe likewise uses the native *tháng* plus the Sino-Vietnamese pagoda-tree name hòe, the same pattern. Removed the redundant `品詞` field and reformatted `japanese`/`vietnamese` from single-item lists to scalar strings. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⺢ㄧ·⼔ㄊ unique across both words and characters).
