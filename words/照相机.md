## Notes
- [照](../characters/照%20(char).md) "shine, photograph" + [相](../characters/相.md) "appearance, image" + [机](../characters/机.md) "machine" — "image-taking machine." Their own `stand_in` values: 照's is itself; 相's is [[相互]]; 机's is [[机会]]; none points here, so 照相机 is an independent compound.

照相机 is a camera: a machine for taking photographs. Mandarin zhàoxiàngjī and Cantonese ziu3 soeng3-2 gei1 are the ordinary Chinese words, often shortened to 相机 (xiàngjī). Japanese says カメラ or 写真機 and Korean 카메라, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. The photograph itself is [[写真]]. Compare [[机器]] (machine).
