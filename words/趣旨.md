## Notes
- [趣](../characters/趣.md) "interest" + [旨](../characters/旨.md) "purpose" — the gist, purport, or underlying point of a statement or action. Attested with the same sense across Mandarin (qùzhǐ), Korean (취지), and Japanese (しゅし). No confirmed Vietnamese usage, so the Vietnamese field holds the compositional reading.
The blank `vietnamese` field now holds the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading of the two characters, thú chỉ (趣 thú, 旨 chỉ), following the vault's convention for these fields. The Korean 취지 differs from the Dan'a'yo 諺文 추즤 in both syllables' vowels, so `kwin` is false. The Mind page lists 趣旨 as "purport, gist, purpose — an undocumented near-duplicate of [主旨](主旨.md)," and no firmer distinction is on record, so Dan'a'yo does not force one. The existing word [要旨](要旨.md) "gist, summary" is a related neighbour. The readings are Mandarin qùzhǐ, Cantonese ceoi3 zi2 and Japanese shushi (しゅし).
