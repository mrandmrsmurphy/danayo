## Notes
- [口 (char)](../characters/口%20(char).md) "mouth" + [論](../characters/論.md) "argue"

口論 is a quarrel: a heated exchange of words. Wiktionary glosses Japanese こうろん as "a quarrel, a verbal dispute; to have a quarrel". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 구론 matches the character readings.

It is spoken and usually short, where [喧嘩](喧嘩.md) can come to blows and [議論](議論.md) is a discussion that need not be angry. A dispute that lasts is a [論争](論争.md).
