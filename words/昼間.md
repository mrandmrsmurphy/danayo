## Notes
- [昼](../characters/昼.md) "daytime" + [間](../characters/間.md) "interval, between" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [昼](../characters/昼.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (間's own stand-in is [[之間]]).

昼間 means daytime.

Mandarin zhòujiān, Cantonese zau3 gaan1, Japanese ひるま, Korean 주간, and Vietnamese trú gian are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `mandarin` was the unrelated phrase 黎明之間 (Límíng zhī jiān, "between dawn," a different compound entirely) instead of compositional zhòujiān; filled blank `cantonese`/`vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄨㄛㄍㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
