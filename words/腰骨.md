## Notes
- [腰](../characters/腰.md) "waist" is separately legitimized by [[腰部]]. [骨 (char)](../characters/骨%20(char).md) "bone, skeleton" is separately legitimized by [[骨]] itself. No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

腰骨 combines 腰 ("waist") with 骨 ("bone") — "hip bone." 髋骨/髖骨 (kuāngǔ) is the precise anatomical/medical synonym, documented as `aliases` (a distinct spelling rather than an orthographic variant of this same headword).

Note: Korean 요골 happens to be a real, unrelated medical term meaning "radius" (the forearm bone) — a coincidental false friend, not a sign this compositional reading is wrong.
