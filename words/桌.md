## Notes
- [桌](characters/桌%20(char).md) "table, desk, stand" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 桌 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

Don't follow Japan's and Korea's conflation of this <ruby>漢字<rt>ㄏㄚㄋㄐㄧ</rt></ruby> with the highly similar character [[卓]]/excellent!

桌 means table, desk, or stand.

Mandarin zhuō, Cantonese cheuk3, Japanese たく, Korean 탁, and Vietnamese trác are all standard, fully compositional readings — the primary form among the character's own comma-joined `mandarin`/`cantonese`/`vietnamese` strings (zhuō/chán/shàn; cheuk3/jeuk3; trác/chác/giạt), a malformed-YAML character-level issue flagged here for a future character-perfecting pass rather than rebuilt in this word-level fix. Added the missing `pos`/`japanese`/`hsk_level` fields. No word-level homophones — the bound characters [[橐]], [[拓]], [[卓]], [[琢]], and [[託]] share this same 注音 (ㄊㄚㄎ) but none has an independent word page of its own.
