## Notes
- [榴](../characters/榴.md) "pomegranate" + [弾](../characters/弾.md) "bullet" — no stand-in relationship applies (榴's own stand-in is [[石榴]]; 弾's own is [[弾丸]]).

榴弾 means high explosives (a shell or grenade).

Mandarin liúdàn and Vietnamese lựu đạn are standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bugs: `cantonese` had a malformed hybrid tone notation (daan6-2 → daan6, matching 弾's own citation); `japanese` used an obsolete historical kana spelling (りうだん → りゅうだん); `korean` was 유탄, the South Korean 두음법칙-shifted form — corrected to 류단, the North Korean/문화어 form matching 榴's own citation. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄌ⼜ㄉㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
