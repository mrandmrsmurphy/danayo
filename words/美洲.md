## Notes
- [美 (char)](../characters/美%20(char).md) "beautiful, pretty" + [洲 (char)](../characters/洲%20(char).md) "continent" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both are stand-ins for themselves), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 洲 is individually `kwin: false`. Combines [[北美洲]] (North America) + [[南美洲]] (South America).

美洲 names the Americas.

Mandarin Měizhōu, Cantonese mei5 zau1, and Korean 미주 (miju) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 美洲 is itself a common attested word for "the Americas" in all three. Japanese びしゅう (bishū) is the compositional reading. Vietnamese Mĩ châu is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
