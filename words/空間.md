## Notes
- [空](../characters/空%20(char).md) "empty, sky" + [間](../characters/間.md) "gap, between" — "empty gap," room or extent. Their own `stand_in` values: 空's is itself; 間's is [[之間]]; none points here, so 空間 is an independent compound.

空間 is space in the abstract: an empty extent or room, as in "storage space" or three-dimensional space. Mandarin kōngjiān, Cantonese hung1 gaan1, Japanese くうかん, Korean 공간 and Vietnamese không gian all use it. Outer space and the universe are [[宇宙]]. Compare [[時期]] and [[位置]] (place).
