## Notes
- [麺 (char)](../characters/麺%20(char).md) "flour" — 麺 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

Originally, this meant "flour." Over time, it came to mean "things made from flour," especially "noodles." We restore the original meaning but allow for metonymy. "Noodles" are [[拉麺]].

Mandarin miàn, Cantonese min6, Korean 면, Japanese めん, and Sino-Vietnamese miến are all standard, fully compositional readings.
