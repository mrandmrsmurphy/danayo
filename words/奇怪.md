## Notes
- [奇](../characters/奇.md) "strange, odd" + [怪](../characters/怪.md) "strange, suspicious" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [奇](../characters/奇.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (怪's own stand-in is [[怪異]]).

奇怪 means odd or unexpected — a near-synonymous doubling of two characters both meaning "strange."

Mandarin qíguài, Cantonese kei4 gwaai3, Japanese きかい, Korean 기괴, and Vietnamese kỳ quái are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed a typo in `english` ("oldd unexpected"→"odd, unexpected"). No homophones (注音 ㄍㄨㄧㄍ⺢ㄧ unique across both words and characters).
