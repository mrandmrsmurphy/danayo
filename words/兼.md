## Notes
- [兼 (char)](../characters/兼%20(char).md) "double as; dual-"

兼 is 會意: [[又]] ("hand") + [[禾]] ("grain stalks," doubled in the full form 秝) — a hand holding two stalks of grain at once, depicting the act of holding or doing two things simultaneously. It survives in the sense "to hold concurrently, to double as" (兼職, "to hold a second job, moonlight"; 兼任, "to serve concurrently [in a post]") and as a prefix meaning "dual-, combined" (兼容, "compatible, able to accommodate both").

Mandarin jiān, Cantonese gim1, and Japanese けん (ken, on'yomi, fully productive: 兼業 けんぎょう "side business") alongside native か(ねる) (ka(neru), "to combine, serve two functions at once") all preserve the sense. Korean 겸 (gyeom) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 겸하다 (gyeomhada, built on the same Sino-Korean root, "to hold concurrently"). Vietnamese kiêm is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in kiêm nhiệm ("to hold a concurrent post") and kiêm chức ("to hold dual roles"); the character page's other four stored candidates (côm, cồm, kem, kèm) are unrelated and look like corpus noise.

This word shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading (gem/검/ㄍㄝㄇ) with another word still awaiting its own turn in this sweep, [[鉗]] ("vice") — the reciprocal half of this callout will be completed when it comes up.
