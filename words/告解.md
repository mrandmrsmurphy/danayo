## Notes
- [告](../characters/告.md) "tell, report" + [解](../characters/解%20(char).md) "explain, release" — "tell and release," confession. Their own `stand_in` values: 告's is [[告訴]]; 解's is itself; none points here, so 告解 is an independent compound.

告解 is confession: telling one's sins to a priest and being absolved, the sacrament of penance. Mandarin gàojiě and Cantonese gou3 gaai2 are the Catholic term, and Japanese こくかい and Korean 고해 are the same compound. It is the sacrament, where [[自白]] is to confess any wrongdoing. Compare [[悔改]] (repentance) and [[赦免]] (pardon).
