## Notes
- [流亡](流亡.md) "to be exiled, to flee into exile" + [者](../characters/者.md) "-er, one who" — the person, agentive form of [流亡](流亡.md).

流亡者 means "an exile, a person living in exile." Vietnamese người lưu vong is the real, standard, commonly used Vietnamese phrase for exactly this — built on the native noun người ("person") plus [流亡](流亡.md) rather than a Hán Việt giả-suffix, the same substitution pattern already noted on [亡命者](亡命者.md). Mandarin liúwángzhě is a real, if formal/written, term. Japanese るぼうしゃ and Korean 류망자 are compositional readings.
