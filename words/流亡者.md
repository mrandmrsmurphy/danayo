## Notes
- [[流亡]] "to be exiled, to flee into exile" + [者 (char)](../characters/者%20(char).md) "-er, one who" — the person, agentive form of [[流亡]]. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: `characters/流.md` and `characters/亡.md` are individually `kwin: true` but `characters/者 (char).md` is `kwin: false` → compound `false`.

流亡者 means "an exile, a person living in exile." Vietnamese người lưu vong is the real, standard, commonly used Vietnamese phrase for exactly this — built on the native noun người ("person") plus [[流亡]] rather than a Hán Việt giả-suffix, the same substitution pattern already noted on [[亡命者]]. Mandarin liúwángzhě is a real, if formal/written, term. Japanese るぼうしゃ and Korean 류망자 are compositional, not independently confirmed as commonly used.
