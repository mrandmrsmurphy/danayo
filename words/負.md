## Notes
- [負](../characters/負%20(char).md) "carry, bear" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

負 means to carry or bear — a burden, responsibility, or debt, as in 負担 ("burden") and 負債 ("debt").

Mandarin fù, Cantonese fu6, Japanese ふ, Korean 부, and Vietnamese phụ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄅ⼜/byu/뷰) with the unrelated [[婦]] ("lady, woman").
