## Notes
"To exempt, excuse, relieve (from an obligation)" (miễn trừ thuế, "tax exemption"). `vietnamese: miễn trừ` is one of the most common everyday Vietnamese words for this exact concept, a direct match. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 면제 matches Sino-Korean 면제 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄇ⼶ㄋㄐㄝㄧ unique among both words and characters).
