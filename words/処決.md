## Etymology
- [処 (char)](../characters/処%20(char).md) "to decide, adjudicate a sentence" (the verb sense, as in 処斬/xử trảm "to sentence to beheading" — distinct from 処's more familiar noun sense "place") + [決 (char)](../characters/決%20(char).md) "to decide; to execute" (a verb sense specific to sentencing, as in 判決/phán quyết "verdict")

## Notes
"To handle and decide (a case); to execute (a sentence)." `vietnamese: xử quyết` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese (confirmed via web search) with the same two senses documented here — "to try and pass judgment" and "to execute, put to death" — paralleling hành quyết. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 초퀃 diverges from Sino-Korean 처결 in the second syllable's vowel/coda. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄛㄎ⼔ㄊ unique among both words and characters).
