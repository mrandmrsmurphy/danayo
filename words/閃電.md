## Notes
- [閃](../characters/閃.md) "flash" + [電](../characters/電.md) "electricity" — lightning, the technically precise term (per the note on 電's character page, lightning is technically 閃電 but is normally just called [[雷電]]). Attested as the standard Mandarin/Cantonese term for lightning.
The Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese fields, which were blank, now hold the compositional readings of the two characters (せんでん, 섬전, thiểm điện), following the vault's convention for these fields; the Notes attest the word chiefly as the Mandarin and Cantonese term, and the everyday Japanese word for lightning is different (稲妻 or 雷). The Korean 섬전 differs from the Dan'a'yo 諺文 섬던 in the second syllable's initial, so `kwin` is false. The Nature page lists the word as "lightning" and notes that [雷電](雷電.md) "thunder and lightning" is the everyday word in practice, with 閃電 the technically precise term; [辟歴](辟歴.md) names the thunderclap. Dan'a'yo keeps the sense "lightning." The readings are Mandarin shǎndiàn and Cantonese sim2 din6. The remark about the everyday Japanese word is from memory and has not been checked against a source.
