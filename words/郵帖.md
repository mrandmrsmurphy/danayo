## Notes
**Found and fixed a wrong-compound reading bug across `mandarin`/`cantonese`/`japanese`/`korean`**: each field had been filled with a different real-world word for "postcard" in that language — míngxìnpiàn/ming4 seon3 pin3-2 (明信片, standard Mandarin/Cantonese term), はがき (葉書, standard Japanese term), 엽서 (also 葉書, standard Korean term) — rather than the honest compositional 郵+帖 reading. Corrected to yóutiě/jau4 tip3/ゆうじょう/우첩 (郵's own yóu/jau4/YUU/우 + 帖's own tiě/tip3/JOU/첩). Vietnamese bưu thiếp was already correctly compositional (郵's own bưu + 帖's own thiếp) — a prior stray note on this page had explicitly flagged the discrepancy ("A Vietnamese word. 葉書 is too J/K. 明信片 is too C.") without resolving it; now resolved. **Found and removed two bad-aliases-entries**: `明信片` and `葉書` are genuinely different, unrelated compounds, not spelling variants of 郵帖. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 웃텁 diverges from Sino-Korean 우첩 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄨㄛㄊㄛㄆ unique among both words and characters).

**Found and fixed a missing citation** on 帖(char).md's own Words list. Citation on 郵.md was already correctly present. Neither 郵's `stand_in` (郵便) nor 帖's own `stand_in` (帖 itself) points to this compound.
