## Notes
- [鼻](../words/鼻.md) "nose" (stand-in: itself) + [音 (char)](../characters/音.md) "sound" (stand-in: [[音楽]]) — a nasal consonant.

鼻音 means a nasal consonant — a sound made with air passing through the nose. Mandarin bíyīn, Japanese びおん, and Korean 비음 are all real, standard phonetics terms, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Cantonese bei6 jam1 is a straightforward compositional reading. Vietnamese tị âm is compositional only; the real term is "âm mũi." `kwin` is false: inherited from 音 (false), despite 鼻 itself being true.

No syllable-boundary dot is needed: the concatenated string `ㄅㄧㄨㄇ` admits only one legal partition.
