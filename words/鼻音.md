## Notes
- [鼻](../words/鼻.md) "nose" (stand-in: itself) + [音 (char)](../characters/音.md) "sound" (stand-in: [[音楽]]) — a nasal consonant.

Wiktionary: Chinese "nasally sound in one's voice; (phonetics) nasal sound"; Japanese 鼻音 (びおん), phonetics and phonology, "a nasal sound", and "a mora that contains a nasal sound"; Korean 비음 is the hanja form. Used in `文法 - 02音韻論`, including in the list of initials (鼻音・流音・両唇子音) that do not co-occur with a glide. Compare [[流音]], [[塞音]], [[擦音]].
