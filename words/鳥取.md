## Notes
- [鳥](../characters/鳥%20(char).md) "bird" + [取](../characters/取.md) "take"

鳥取 is Tottori, written "bird catching". The name goes back to an ancient group of bird-catchers, the 鳥取部, who supplied birds to the court. The reading とっとり is native, with 鳥 (とり) shortened and doubled to とっ, followed by 取 (とり, "take").

Tottori has the smallest population of the 47 prefectures. It lies on the Sea of Japan, with its capital Tottori. It is known for the Tottori Sand Dunes, Mount Daisen, and Sakaiminato, where the manga artist Mizuki Shigeru lived, and for its pears.

In Dan'a'yo 鳥取 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 돗토리 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
