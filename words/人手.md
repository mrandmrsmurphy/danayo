## Notes
- [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" + [手 (char)](../characters/手%20(char).md) "hand" — "manpower, workforce" (as in 人手不足, "insufficient manpower") or literally "a person's hand." `vietnamese: nhân thủ` and `korean: 인수` are both directly attested/compositional matches (confirmed via web search) — nhân thủ specifically colloquial alongside the more formal near-synonym nhân lực.
