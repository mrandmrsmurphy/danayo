## Notes
- [西](../characters/西.md) "west" is separately legitimized by [[西方]]. [瓜](../characters/瓜.md) "cucumber, melon" is separately legitimized by [[胡瓜]]. No cranberry.

Mandarin xīguā is standard compositional. Split a comma-joined `korean` field — 수박 (the everyday native word for "watermelon") was joined in; kept 서과 as the compositional value.
