## Notes
- [以 (char)](../characters/以%20(char).md) "by means of, using" + [便 (char)](../characters/便%20(char).md) "convenient" — a grammatical connector, "in order that, so as to," introducing a purpose clause (e.g. 早点出发以便赶上火车, "leave early in order to catch the train"). Filled the blank `pos: 連接詞`. `vietnamese: để` is the standard everyday Vietnamese purpose-clause conjunction (để kịp giờ, "in order to be on time"), matching the phrase-style `japanese`/`korean` fields already present rather than a Sino-Vietnamese compound. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 이변 diverges entirely from the phrase-based Korean field. No homophones (注音 ㄧㄅ⼶ㄋ unique among both words and characters).
