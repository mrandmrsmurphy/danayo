## Notes
- [姻](../characters/姻.md) "marriage ties (in-laws)" + [兄 (char)](../characters/兄%20(char).md) "older brother" — affinal older brother.

姻兄 is the hypernym of the older in-law brothers: any older brother by marriage, covering [[姉夫]], [[妻兄]] and [[夫兄]]. It is "affinal older brother." No entry was found on English Wiktionary; the characters appear in older letter-writing as a courtesy form for a relative by marriage, but Dan'a'yo gives it a precise role here as the umbrella term. Its pair for younger in-law brothers is [[姻弟]]. 姻 is the same "marriage tie" character as in [[婚姻]]. It is a schema word in the family of in-law terms built from the marriage relation and the sibling roots, coined for predictability rather than a claim about ancient usage.
