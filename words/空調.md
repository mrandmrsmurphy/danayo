## Notes
- [空](../characters/空%20(char).md) "empty, air" + [調](../characters/調.md) "adjust" — "air adjusting," air conditioning. Their own `stand_in` values: 空's is itself; 調's is [[調整]]; none points here, so 空調 is an independent compound.

空調 is air conditioning: the control of a room's temperature and air. Mandarin kōngtiáo and Cantonese hung1 tiu4 are the ordinary Chinese words, Japanese くうちょう is a standard technical and everyday term, and Korean 공조 is the same word. Compare [[空気]] (air).
