## Notes
- [創](../characters/創.md) "create" + [作 (char)](../characters/作%20(char).md) "make"

創作 is to create a work of art or literature, or the work created. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin chuàngzuò as "to create; to write" and Japanese そうさく as "creation". Korean 창작 and Vietnamese sáng tác are the same word. Cantonese cong3 zok3 matches.

It is the act behind every [作品](作品.md), and the faculty behind it is [創造](創造.md), creation, in general. What fires it is [霊感](霊感.md), inspiration.
