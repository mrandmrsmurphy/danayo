## Notes
- [借 (char)](../characters/借%20(char).md) "to borrow" + [鑑](../characters/鑑.md) "mirror; to examine"

借鑑 is "to draw lessons from; to take as a mirror," literally "borrow a mirror": to hold another's example or experience up as a guide. According to Wiktionary, Chinese 借鑒 (jièjiàn, Cantonese ze3 gaam3) is a transitive verb, "to use for reference; to make use of; to draw from; to borrow from; to learn from," with 借鏡 as a synonym. The alias 借鑒 is the traditional form and 借鉴 the simplified form; 鑑 and 鑒 are the same character. Wiktionary lists it for Chinese only, so the Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese fields (`japanese` しゃかん, `korean` 차감, `vietnamese` tá giám) are compositional readings of the two characters, not attested words. The Dan'a'yo coinage is accepted on the strength of the Chinese word and its use to render the Japanese 鑑ミ "having considered" in the 1945 surrender broadcast ([玉音放送](../translation/玉音放送.md)).
