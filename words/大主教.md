## Notes
- [大](../characters/大%20(char).md) "great" + [主](../characters/主.md) "lord, master" + [教](../characters/教.md) "teaching, religion" — no stand-in relationship applies (大 is its own stand-in; 主's own is [[主人]]; 教's is [[教授]]).

Ranks above [[主教]] (bishop) in Christian ecclesiastical hierarchy. In Catholic Japanese usage, the alternative form 大司教 (daishikyō) is also found.

Filled blank `vietnamese: đại chủ giáo` (compositional). Vietnamese Catholic terminology instead uses "Tổng giám mục" ("general supervising-shepherd," built from an entirely different Chinese root, 總監牧) as the real-world standard term for archbishop. Japanese だいしゅきょう, Korean 대주교, Mandarin dàzhǔjiào, and Cantonese daai6 zyu2 gaau3 are all standard readings, matching Dan'a'yo's own reading exactly (`kwin: true`). No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄧㄐㄨㄍ⼄ㄨ unique across both words and characters).
