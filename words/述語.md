## Notes
- [述](../characters/述.md) "to state, to relate" + [語](../characters/語.md) "word, language" — no stand-in relationship applies (述's own stand-in is [[陳述]]; 語's is [[言語]]).

述語 is the grammatical predicate: the part of a clause that says something about its topic, the verb or stative and what goes with it. In Dan'a'yo it is the comment of the topic–comment sentence, and the grammar's word order is fixed around it: frame particles, then the 主題 topic, then the 述語, then any 語気助詞 sentence-final particle. Every clause must have one, the grammar's principle of obligatory predication (述語必須), and a noun predicate takes the copula 是.

Wiktionary: 述語 is the grammar term "predicate" in Chinese (shùyǔ), Japanese (じゅつご) and Korean (술어, the hanja form). Used in `文法 - 04句法`, `文法 - 02音韻論` and `文法 - 94日本語小冊子`. See also [[主題]].
