## Notes
"Miserable, desolate" — cold and bleak, whether physically (a desolate landscape) or emotionally (sorrow, melancholy). Filled the blank `japanese: せいりょう` (compositional) and `vietnamese: thê lương`, directly attested Sino-Vietnamese (confirmed via web search), often describing an evocatively sad, empty scene. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 체량 diverges from Sino-Korean 처량 in the first syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄝㄧㄌ⼘ㄫ unique among both words and characters).
