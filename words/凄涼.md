## Notes
"Miserable, desolate" — cold and bleak, whether physically (a desolate landscape) or emotionally (sorrow, melancholy). `vietnamese: thê lương` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese (confirmed via web search), often describing an evocatively sad, empty scene.
