## Notes
- [色](../characters/色.md) "color" + [彩](../characters/彩.md) "vibrant, multicolored" — literally "color-vibrancy," the general noun for color, contrasting with [[彩色]] (the adjectival "colored, polychromatic" sense of the same two characters reversed).

Mandarin sècǎi, Cantonese sik1 coi2, Korean 색채 (saekchae), and Japanese しきさい (shikisai) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real everyday noun for "color" in its language. Vietnamese sắc thái is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, matching 色's sắc and 彩's own thái — though in living Vietnamese usage it has drifted toward "nuance, tone, shade of meaning" (as in sắc thái biểu cảm, "emotional nuance") rather than the literal RGB sense of "color," a natural semantic extension from the same root. **Real `kwin` bug found and fixed**: was stored as `true`, but Dan'a'yo 식채 does not exactly string-match Sino-Korean 색채 — the first syllable's vowel diverges (식 vs. 색). Corrected to `kwin: false`. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄎㄑㄚㄧ unique among both words and characters).
