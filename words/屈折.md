## Notes
- [屈 (char)](../characters/屈%20(char).md) "bend" + [折](../characters/折.md) "fold"

屈折 is inflection, and bending. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 屈折 (Mandarin qūzhé, Cantonese wat1 zit3) as "to bend; to turn; to warp", the linguistics sense "to inflect" and the physics sense "to refract", and Japanese くっせつ as "refraction", "inflection", "to refract", "to inflect (a word)" and "to be warped". The Korean 굴절 field is built from the readings of the two characters. Compare [活用](活用.md), inflection.
