## Notes
- [胰](../characters/胰.md) "pancreas" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 胰 cannot appear independently outside this compound. [臓](../characters/臓.md) "organ, entrails" is separately legitimized by [[内臓]].

胰臓 combines 胰 ("pancreas") with 臓 ("organ") — "pancreas." No cranberry: transitivity fails since 臓's own `stand_in` doesn't point here.

Pronunciation fields ('iǝjang/의장/ㄧㄜㄐㄚㄫ) are the straightforward concatenation of each constituent's own stored reading — no bug. `kwin: false` is already correct per the AND-rule (胰 individually false despite 臓's own `kwin: true`). Mandarin yízàng and cantonese ji4zong6 (space removed) are standard, fully compositional readings using 胰's own character. Japanese すいぞう (suizou) and korean 췌장 (chwejang), however, are real attested readings of the *alias* spelling 膵臓 — 膵 is a Japanese-coined kokuji specifically for "pancreas," distinct from Chinese 胰, and both readings are genuinely standard in their respective languages rather than compositional derivations of 胰's own い/의 reading. Vietnamese lists both tụy (the standard medical term, likewise tied to 膵's reading rather than 胰's own "di") and lá mía (a native colloquial folk name, literally "sugarcane leaf," for the organ's shape).
