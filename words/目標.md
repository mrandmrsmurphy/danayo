## Notes
- [目 (char)](../characters/目%20(char).md) "eye" + [標](../characters/標.md) "sign, signpost, mark" — literally "the sign one's eye is fixed on," i.e. a target or goal.

目標 is the general, everyday word for a target, aim, or goal — used equally for a literal physical target, a project milestone, or an abstract life ambition. Mandarin mùbiāo, Cantonese muk6 biu1, Japanese もくひょう (mokuhyō), Korean 목표 (mokpyo), and Vietnamese mục tiêu are all fully compositional and stand as the ordinary, unmarked word for "goal" in each language, with no register restriction or narrowing.
