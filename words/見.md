## Notes
- [見 (char)](../characters/見%20(char).md) "see" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 見 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

見 means "to see," the character itself 会意 of [[目]] ("eye") over [[卩]] ("kneeling person") — a kneeling human figure with a large eye for a head, the concept of seeing depicted directly. The simplified form 见 is listed as an alias. Beyond the plain verb, 見 extends into compounds like 意見 ("opinion, view" — literally what one "sees" in one's mind) and 発見 ("discover, find out").

Mandarin jiàn, Cantonese gin3, and Korean 견 (gyeon) all preserve the Sino reading directly. Japanese けん (ken) survives mainly bound in compounds (意見, 発見); the everyday native verb is みる (miru); Korean has native 보다. Sino-Vietnamese kiến is attested in formal/literary compounds (kiến giải, "understanding, view"), though everyday Vietnamese uses the native thấy/nhìn/xem depending on register.
