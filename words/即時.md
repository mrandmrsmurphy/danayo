## Notes
- [即](../characters/即%20(char).md) "at once" + [時](../characters/時%20(char).md) "time" — "at that time," immediately. Their own `stand_in` values: 即's is itself; 時's is itself; none points here, so 即時 is an independent compound.

即時 is immediately: at that very time, without delay, and as an adjective "instant, real-time" (即時通訊, "instant messaging"). Mandarin jíshí and Cantonese zik1 si4 are the ordinary words, and Japanese そくじ and Korean 즉시 are the same compound; Vietnamese tức thì does too. Compare [[瞬間]] (an instant) and [[急遽]] (in haste).
