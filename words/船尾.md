## Notes
- [船](../characters/船.md) "boat, ship" is separately legitimized by [[船舶]]. [尾 (char)](../characters/尾%20(char).md) "tail" is separately legitimized by [[尾]] itself. No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

船尾 combines 船 ("boat") with 尾 ("tail") — "stern (of a ship)."

Mandarin chuánwěi, japanese せんび (senbi), and korean 선미 (seonmi) were all already correct, fully compositional readings.
