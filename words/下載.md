## Notes
- [下](../characters/下%20(char).md) "down" + [載](../characters/載%20(char).md) "carry" — "carry down," load from the network onto one's own machine. Their own `stand_in` values: 下's is itself; 載's is itself; none points here, so 下載 is an independent compound.

下載 is to download: to copy a file from a network to one's own machine. Mandarin xiàzǎi (also xiàzài) and Cantonese haa6 zoi3 are the ordinary Chinese words. Japanese says ダウンロード and Korean 다운로드, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[下]] (down).
