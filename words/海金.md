## Notes
- [海](characters/海.md) "sea" (from [[海王星]] "Neptune") + [金 (char)](characters/金%20(char).md) "metal" — toponymic-derivation naming for neptunium, parallel to [[丹金]]/[[欧金]]/[[沼金]].
- Precludes the new Chinese character: 镎/鎿

Per this series' convention, `mandarin`/`cantonese` hold the avoided real element character's own reading: 镎 `ná`/`naa4`. `korean`/`japanese`/`vietnamese` hold the standard IUPAC loanword transcriptions. Removed the redundant `品詞` field.

**Real homophone found**: 禁 and 金 happen to share the identical Dan'a'yo reading (gim/김/ㄍㄧㄇ), making 海金 and [[海禁]] ("maritime prohibition") genuine two-way homophones. Added reciprocal callouts to both pages. No third word shares this reading.
