## Notes
- [天](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven" + [花](../characters/花.md) "flower" — no stand-in relationship applies (天 is its own stand-in; 花's own is [[草花]]).

天花 means snowflake — literally "heaven's flower" — from the Buddhist tale of flowers blossoming in the heavens and scattering as snow; the same term also names the lotus-petal-shaped confetti scattered before a mortuary tablet at a funeral.

Mandarin tiānhuā, Cantonese tin1 faa1, Korean 천화, Japanese てんか, and Vietnamese thiên hoa are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `pos: 名詞`. Blanked empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄝㄋㄏ⺢ unique across both words and characters).
