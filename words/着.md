## Notes
- [着](../characters/着%20(char).md) "wear" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 着 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

着 means to wear, don, or put on (clothing) — 着物 (kimono, literally "worn thing"), 下着 ("underwear"), 水着 ("swimsuit"). It also functions as a grammatical particle in Mandarin (着/著, zhe), marking a continuous or durative aspect on a verb (e.g. 看着 "while watching"), a use distinct from and unrelated to the "wear" sense shared by the other daughter languages.

Mandarin zhe, Cantonese zoek6, Japanese ちゃく (chaku), Korean 착 (chak), and Vietnamese trước are all standard readings tracing back to the same character.
