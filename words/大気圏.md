## Notes
- [大](../characters/大%20(char).md) "great" + [気](../characters/気%20(char).md) "air" + [圏](../characters/圏%20(char).md) "sphere, zone" — no stand-in relationship applies (all three characters are independent Dan'a'yo entries, each its own stand-in).

大気圏 means the atmosphere — the layer of gases surrounding a planet.

**Real bugs found and fixed**: `mandarin`/`cantonese`/`korean` were all missing 圏's contribution entirely (covering only 大気, not the full compound) — corrected to dàqìquān/daai6 hei3 hyun1/대기권. Japanese たいきけん (already correctly including 圏's KEN) and Vietnamese khí quyển are both standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄧㄎㄧㄜㄍ⼔ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
