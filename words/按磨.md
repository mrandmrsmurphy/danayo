## Notes
- [按](../characters/按.md) "to press, push down" + [磨 (char)](<../characters/磨 (char).md>) "to rub, grind" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [按](../characters/按.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry. Conventionally spelled 按摩; 摩 is registered as an alias of 磨, so the canonical-form title is 按磨 per the vault's alias-parent rule.

按磨 means massage.

Mandarin ànmó, Cantonese on3 mo1, Japanese あんま, Korean 안마, and Vietnamese án ma are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄋㄇㄚ unique across both words and characters).
