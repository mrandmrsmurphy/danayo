## Notes
- [行 (char)](../characters/行%20(char).md) "walk; run" + [書](../characters/書.md) "writing; script"

行書 is semi-cursive script: the style between regular and cursive, written fast but still legible. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xíngshū as "semi-cursive script; running hand; running script" and Japanese ぎょうしょ as "semi-cursive script". Korean 행서 and Vietnamese hành thư are the same word. Cantonese hang4 syu1 matches.

It lies between the [楷書](楷書.md) and the [草書](草書.md), and its strokes are described by the [筆画](筆画.md).
