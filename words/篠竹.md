## Notes
- [篠](../characters/篠.md) "dwarf bamboo" + [竹 (char)](../characters/竹%20(char).md) "bamboo" — 篠竹 is the stand-in word for [篠](../characters/篠.md); 篠 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse.

篠竹 names small, slender bamboo-grass species (genus *Pleioblastus* and related dwarf bamboos) — thin-stalked, low-growing plants used ornamentally, as ground cover, and historically for crafts like arrow shafts and small woven items, distinct from the tall timber bamboo used for construction and scaffolding.

Note: Japanese reads しのだけ (shinodake), a native reading rather than the expected on-yomi しょうちく. Korean 소죽 is a standard compositional Sino-Korean reading. Vietnamese tiểu trúc is compositionally derived from each character's own confirmed reading, though not independently confirmed as an attested fixed compound. No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄛㄨㄐㄨㄎ unique across both words and characters).
