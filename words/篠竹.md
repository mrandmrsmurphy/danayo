## Notes
- [篠](../characters/篠.md) "dwarf bamboo" + [竹 (char)](../characters/竹%20(char).md) "bamboo"

篠竹 is dwarf bamboo, a bamboo grass. English Wiktionary has an entry only for Japanese 篠竹 (しのだけ), glossed "bamboo grass" and as the species *Sasa borealis*. The Mandarin, Cantonese, Korean 소죽 and Vietnamese tiểu trúc fields are built from the readings of the two characters.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [篠](../characters/篠.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
