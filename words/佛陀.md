## Notes
- [佛 (char)](../characters/佛%20(char).md) + [陀](../characters/陀.md) — a phonetic transliteration of Sanskrit Buddha (from budh, "to awaken"), "the enlightened one." All three filled fields are directly attested (confirmed via web search): `japanese: ぶつだ` (alongside the more common katakana ブッダ for the proper noun), `korean: 불타`, and `vietnamese: Phật Đà`, a formal/full transliteration alongside the everyday shortened Phật. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 붇다 diverges from Sino-Korean 불타 via the AND-rule, since 陀's own field independently diverges (despite 佛 itself being `kwin: true`). No homophones (注音 ㄅㄨㄊㄉㄚ unique among both words and characters).

**Real content bugs found and fixed**: `mandarin` was capitalized "Fótuó," inconsistent with [[佛]]'s own lowercase pinyin convention — corrected to fótuó. Removed dangling empty `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/`aliases`. Found and fixed a missing-citation bug: 佛陀 was entirely absent from both `佛(char).md`'s and `陀.md`'s own Words lists (only mentioned in `佛(char).md`'s etymology prose) — added to both.
