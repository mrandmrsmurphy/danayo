## Notes
- [佛 (char)](../characters/佛%20(char).md) + [陀](../characters/陀.md) — a phonetic transliteration of Sanskrit Buddha (from budh, "to awaken"), "the enlightened one." All three filled fields are directly attested (confirmed via web search): `japanese: ぶつだ` (alongside the more common katakana ブッダ for the proper noun), `korean: 불타`, and `vietnamese: Phật Đà`, a formal/full transliteration alongside the everyday shortened Phật. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 붇다 diverges entirely from the Sino-Korean 불타. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄨㄊㄉㄚ unique among both words and characters).
