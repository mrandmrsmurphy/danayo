## Notes
- [蟹 (char)](../characters/蟹%20(char).md) "crab" + [爪 (char)](../characters/爪%20(char).md) "claw"

Wiktionary has no entry for 蟹爪. Wikipedia (Eight Principles of Yong) records 蟹爪 (xièzhuǎ, "crab's pincer") as Li Puguang's metaphorical name, from his Yuan-era 永字八法解, for the fourth principle, the hook (鉤, classically 趯). See [[鉤]], [[怪石]] and the [[Stroke]] page. Its readings are compositional.
