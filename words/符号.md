## Notes
- [符](../characters/符.md) "symbol, sign" + [号](../characters/号.md) "sign, mark" — pairing two near-synonymous "symbol" roots.

符号/符號 names a symbol, mark, or sign — covering everything from punctuation and mathematical notation to badges and insignia.

Mandarin fúhào, Cantonese fu4hou6-2 (with the well-attested colloquial changed-tone shift on 号), Korean 부호 (buho), and Japanese ふごう (fugō) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real everyday word for "symbol, mark" in its language. Vietnamese phù hiệu is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, matching 符's phù (as opposed to its Nôm reading bùa, "amulet, charm") and 号's own hiệu (as opposed to hào, its "ordinal number" sense) — attested specifically in the "badge, insignia" sense (phù hiệu quân đội, "military insignia"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 부핫 diverges from Sino-Korean 부호 in the second syllable's coda. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄅㄨㄏㄚㄨ unique among both words and characters).
