## Notes
- [䋇 (char)](../characters/䋇%20(char).md) "unravel, explain"

䋇 (traditional 繹, simplified 绎) literally means to reel out silk thread, and by extension to unravel, deduce, or explain step by step — the core sense behind 演繹 (yǎnyì, "to deduce; deduction"), the counterpart of 歸納 ("induction") in Mandarin logical/philosophical vocabulary. Cantonese jik6 and Korean 역 (yeok) preserve the same reading and sense, though the word is rare outside this compound and a handful of classical/philosophical contexts in every daughter language. Vietnamese dịch, as in diễn dịch ("deduction, to deduce"), matches the Mandarin compound directly.

This word shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading (`'yeg`/역/⼶ㄎ) with [[駅]] ("station") purely by coincidence of phonetic component: 繹's own phonetic is 尺, the same phonetic that gives 驛 (駅's traditional form) its reading — the two characters are unrelated in meaning but share an Old Chinese phonetic series.
