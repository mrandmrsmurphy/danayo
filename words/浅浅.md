## Notes
- [浅 (char)](../characters/浅%20(char).md) "shallow" + [浅 (char)](../characters/浅%20(char).md) "shallow" — no stand-in relationship applies (浅's own stand-in is itself).
- 浅浅 is the ideophone for the light, quick splash of water running over a shallow bed: a stream chattering across pebbles, drops flicked off a paddle, small waves lapping a ford. It is modelled on Chinese 淺淺 (the literary variant of 濺濺 *jiānjiān* "sound of flowing water; fast-flowing water; liquid splashing out" — en.Wiktionary lists the two as variants of one another). The doubling does what it does in the other sound-words (what Chinese calls 拟声词, Japanese 擬声語, Korean 의성어): it turns one instance into a rhythm. The character 浅 suits it twice over, since "shallow" names both the water and the lightness of the sound.
- 濺 is an alias of [浅 (char)](../characters/浅%20(char).md), so the 濺濺 spelling resolves to this word. In Chinese the same spelling 淺淺 also reads *qiǎnqiǎn* as a literary adjective ("shallow; slight; superficial"); in Dan'a'yo the doubled form is reserved for the splashing ideophone, and the adjective is simply [[浅]].
- Mandarin *jiānjiān* (variant *jiànjiàn*) and Cantonese *zin1 zin1* are the Wiktionary readings of 濺濺. The Japanese せんせん and Korean 천천 are the compositional readings of 浅/濺 (SEN, 천) and have not been checked against a dictionary entry for this exact word; Japanese せんせん is otherwise the standard reading of 潺潺 for the same kind of sound. No Vietnamese reading has been confirmed, so the field is omitted.
