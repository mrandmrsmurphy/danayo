## Notes
- [帝](../characters/帝.md) "emperor" + [国](../characters/国.md) "nation"

帝国 names an empire — a state built around the sovereignty of an emperor (帝) ruling over a nation or realm (国) that typically spans multiple peoples, cultures, or annexed territories under a single centralized crown, distinct from an ordinary kingdom (王国) ruled by a king. Beyond its literal political sense, the word extends figuratively to any dominant, sprawling sphere of influence or power — a media empire, a business empire — mirroring a similar figurative extension of "empire" in English.

Cross-linguistically the compound is a stable Sino-Xenic loan across the sphere: Sino-Korean 제국 and Japanese ていこく both directly concatenate the component on'yomi/Sino-Korean readings, and Vietnamese đế quốc likewise continues as an ordinary, freely used word for "empire" (as in Đế quốc La Mã, "the Roman Empire"), not merely a bound or literary form.
