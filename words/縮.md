## Notes
- [縮](../characters/縮%20(char).md) "shrink, contract" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 縮 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

縮 means to shrink or contract.

Mandarin suō, Cantonese suk1, and Korean 축 (chuk) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading ちぢむ (chijimu, "to shrink") rather than the on-reading シュク (SHUKU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese súc is the standard reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones (叔/塾/宿/淑/熟/粛 share the same syllable at the character level, but none has an independent word page).
