## Notes
- [隣 (char)](../characters/隣%20(char).md) "neighboring, next door" + [国](../characters/国.md) "nation"

隣国 means a neighboring country — a nation sharing a border. Mandarin 鄰國/邻国 (línguó), Cantonese 鄰國 (leon4gwok3), Japanese 隣国 (りんごく), Sino-Vietnamese lân quốc, and Korean 인국 (ingug — using the alternate Sino-Korean reading 인 for 隣/鄰, distinct from the 린 reading seen in [[隣人]]) all use this compositional term across the CJKV sphere.
