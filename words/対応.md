## Notes
- [対](../characters/対.md) "oppose; face" + [応 (char)](../characters/応%20(char).md) "respond; reply" — no stand-in relationship applies (対's own `stand_in` is [[反対]], 応's is itself).

対応 names correspondence or responsiveness between two things — matching parts, appropriate reactions to a situation, or (in institutional contexts) how a person or office handles/deals with a matter. 対 contributes its "facing, paired-with" sense (as in 相対 "relative," 対称 "symmetric") rather than its more purely adversarial "oppose" sense (反対); 応 contributes its core "to respond, answer" sense.

Japanese たいおう is an extremely common, almost bureaucratic-register word — お客様対応 "customer service/handling," 危機対応 "crisis response" — while Korean 대응 and Vietnamese đối ứng (both verified) carry the same "correspondence/response" range without narrowing to any one specific institutional sense. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄧ·ㄧㄫ unique across both words and characters); HSK level 4 is already correct.
