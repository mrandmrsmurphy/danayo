## Notes
- [景](../characters/景.md) "scenery, scene" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [景](../characters/景.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (色's own stand-in is [[色彩]]). Synonymous with [[風景]].

景色 means scenery or view.

Mandarin jǐngsè, Cantonese ging2 sik1, Japanese けしき, Korean 경색, and Vietnamese cảnh sắc are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `cantonese` had the wrong initial (sing2→ging2). Filled missing `korean: 경색` (compositional). No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼶ㄫㄙㄧㄎ unique across both words and characters).
