## Notes
- [価](../characters/価.md) "price" + [格 (char)](../characters/格%20(char).md) "standard, status" — "price." We do not use J/K 料金 ("fee, charge," a narrower sense than general "price"). `vietnamese: giá cách` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese, part of the same 價/giá family as giá tiền, giá trị, vật giá. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 가각 diverges from Sino-Korean 가격 in the second syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄍㄚㄎ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 価's own `stand_in` is this exact word (価格), so 価 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 価格.
