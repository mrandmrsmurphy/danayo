## Notes
- [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "ride, board" + [車 (char)](../characters/車%20(char).md) "vehicle, car" — literally "to board a vehicle," parallel to [[乗船]] ("to board a ship") and [[乗馬]] ("to ride a horse") in the productive 乗 + [vehicle] pattern.

乗車 is the standard transit term for boarding a car, bus, or train: 乗車券 (jōshaken, a boarding/train ticket) and 乗車率 (occupancy/load rate) are common Japanese collocations. Mandarin chéng chē, Cantonese sing4 ce1, and Korean 승차 (승차하다, "to board") are all standard, fully compositional terms. **Vietnamese content note**: thừa xa is a compositionally sound derivation (乗's thừa + 車's own Sino-Vietnamese xa), but everyday Vietnamese more commonly says lên xe ("to get on the vehicle," native) for this action; no independent attestation of thừa xa as a fixed Sino-Vietnamese compound turned up, so it is presented as a defensible derivation rather than a separately confirmed term — the same caveat already flagged on [[乗船]]'s thừa thuyền.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 숭촤 diverges from Sino-Korean 승차, since both 乗 and 車 independently carry `kwin: false` on their own character pages.
