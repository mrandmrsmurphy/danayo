## Notes
- [前 (char)](../characters/前.md) "front, before" (stand-in: itself) + [舌 (char)](../characters/舌.md) "tongue" (stand-in: itself) — the front of the tongue, a vowel-height-independent articulatory parameter, paired with [[後舌]].

前舌 means the front of the tongue, as used in `文法 - 02音韻論`'s vowel table (前舌/中舌/後舌 columns). Japanese ぜんぜつ and Korean 전설 are both real, standard phonetics terms, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Mandarin qiánshé and Cantonese cin4 sit3 are straightforward compositional readings — Mandarin phonetics more often says 前元音 for "front vowel," built differently. Vietnamese tiền thiệt is compositional only. `kwin` is false: inherited from 舌 (false), despite 前 itself being true.

No syllable-boundary dot is needed: the concatenated string `ㄐㄝㄋㄙㄝㄊ` admits only one legal partition.
