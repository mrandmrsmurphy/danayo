## Notes
- [前 (char)](../characters/前%20(char).md) "front, before" + [舌 (char)](../characters/舌%20(char).md) "tongue" — the front of the tongue, paired with [後舌](後舌.md).

前舌 is the front of the tongue. English Wiktionary has no entry for 前舌 in any language, so this is a term of this vault, and the Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese fields are built from the readings of the two characters. In the vowel table of [文法 - 02音韻論](../grammar/文法%20-%2002音韻論.md) the columns are headed 前舌, 中舌 and 後舌, front, central and back of the tongue.
