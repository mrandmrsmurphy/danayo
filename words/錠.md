## Notes
- [錠](../characters/錠%20(char).md) "lock" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [錠](../characters/錠%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

錠 means lock (also used as a counter for tablets/pills in Japanese/Korean/Chinese).

Mandarin dìng, Cantonese ding3, Japanese じょう, Korean 정, and Vietnamese đĩnh are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: literal `vietnamese: null`, corrected using the character's own citation. Homophone of [[整]] "orderly; neat; tidy" (both 注音 ㄐㄝㄫ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words.
