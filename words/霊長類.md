## Notes
- [霊](../characters/霊.md) "spirit, ghost" + [長 (char)](../characters/長%20(char).md) "long" + [類](../characters/類.md) "kind, sort, category" — no `#cranberry`: no constituent's own `stand_in` points here (霊's is [[霊魂]], 長's is 長 itself, 類's is [[種類]]).

霊長類/灵长类 (língzhǎnglèi) is the everyday Mandarin term for "primates," distinct from the more formal taxonomic-rank name 灵长目 (Língzhǎngmù, "Order Primates") — this word matches its own characters (霊+長+類) and takes the 類 reading, not 目's. Cantonese 靈長類 (ling4zoeng2leoi6) follows the same pattern. Japanese れいちょうるい (reichōrui) and Korean 영장류 (yeongjangnyu) are likewise the standard terms in their languages.

Vietnamese linh trưởng ("primate") is a real, standard term, but is built from only two morphemes (linh 靈 + trưởng 長，using 長's alternate "chief, head" reading rather than "long"), dropping 類 entirely — paralleling how Mandarin's own colloquial 中子 drops 性 from 中性子's full form.

Fixed a real bug: `mandarin` and `cantonese` had both been contaminated with the taxonomic-rank reading (Língzhǎngmù/muk6, using 目 "order") instead of this word's own 類-based reading (lèi/leoi6) — likely copied from a reference conflating the common noun with the formal Linnean rank name. Added a missing citation to `characters/長 (char).md`'s `## Words` list (霊's and 類's own lists already had it).
