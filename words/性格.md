## Notes
- [性](../characters/性.md) "nature" + [格 (char)](../characters/格%20(char).md) "pattern; character"

性格 is personality: the whole set of a person's lasting ways of feeling, thinking and acting. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xìnggé as "personality; nature; temperament; disposition" and Japanese せいかく as "character of a human (personality), or of things". Korean 성격, Vietnamese tính cách and Cantonese sing3 gaak3 match.

It is the character as a whole, built on the inborn [気質](気質.md), temperament, and shown in feeling, as in [感情](感情.md). A single trait is gentle [温厚](温厚.md) or cold [冷淡](冷淡.md).
