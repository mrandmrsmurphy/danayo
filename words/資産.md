## Notes
- [資](../characters/資.md) "resources, capital" + [産](../characters/産.md) "produce, property" — "capital property," assets. Their own `stand_in` values: 資's is [[資本]]; 産's is [[生産]]; none points here, so 資産 is an independent compound.

資産 is an asset, or assets: what a person or firm owns that has value. Mandarin zīchǎn (資產) and Cantonese zi1 caan2 are the ordinary words in finance, and Japanese しさん and Korean 자산 are the same compound. Compare [[財産]] (property, wealth), [[資本]] (capital) and [[負債]] (liability), its opposite on the balance sheet.
