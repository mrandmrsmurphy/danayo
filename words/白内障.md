## Notes
- [白 (char)](../characters/白%20(char).md) "white" + [内](../characters/内.md) "inside" + [障](../characters/障.md) "barrier, obstruction" — literally "a white obstruction inside [the eye]," describing the clouded, whitish lens that gives the condition its name.

白内障 is the standard medical term for cataract, the clouding of the eye's natural lens that progressively impairs vision — most commonly age-related, though it can also result from injury, disease, or be present from birth. The name is a descriptive coinage rather than a borrowed or transliterated term, built the same way in every language here: mandarin báinèizhàng, cantonese baak6 noi6 zoeng3, japanese はくないしょう (hakunaishō), korean 백내장 (baengnaejang), and vietnamese bạch nội chướng are all fully compositional and used in ordinary medical speech, with no drift or narrowing anywhere in the sphere.
