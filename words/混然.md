## Notes
- [混](../characters/混.md) "mix, confuse, blend, bumble" + [然 (char)](../characters/然%20(char).md) "so" — no stand-in relationship applies (混's own stand-in is [[混合]]; 然's own is itself).

混然 means integrated, unified, undifferentiated (as in 混然一体, "fused into a single whole").

Mandarin húnrán preserves the classical/literary tone hún (distinct from the everyday hùn "mix, confuse"), matching real usage in this specific compound. Japanese こんぜん and Korean 혼연 are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 混's and 然's own stored citations exactly. Fixed a doubled/malformed `cantonese` value (wan4 jin4 / wan6 jin4 → wan6 jin4, matching 混's own citation wan6).
