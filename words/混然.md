## Notes
- [混](../characters/混.md) "mix, confuse, blend, bumble" + [然 (char)](../characters/然%20(char).md) "so" — no stand-in relationship applies (混's own stand-in is [[混合]]; 然's own is itself).

混然 means integrated, unified, undifferentiated (as in 混然一体, "fused into a single whole").

Mandarin húnrán preserves the classical/literary tone hún (distinct from the everyday hùn "mix, confuse"), matching real usage in this specific compound. Japanese こんぜん and Korean 혼연 are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 混's and 然's own stored citations exactly. Fixed a doubled/malformed `cantonese` value (wan4 jin4 / wan6 jin4 → wan6 jin4, matching 混's own citation wan6). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄛㄋㄋ⼶ㄋ unique across both words and characters).

**Correction (2026-09-20):** `vietnamese` was hỗn nhiên, compositionally derived at the time from 混's stored list — but that list was itself missing the hồn reading. hvdic.thivien.net confirms 混 (as the simplified/variant form of 渾, its own stored alias) carries hồn as a genuine Hán Việt reading, and hồn nhiên is the real, common attested Vietnamese word ("innocent, naive, spontaneous") — not hỗn nhiên, which is not an attested term. Fixed to hồn nhiên; 混's own character page has been updated to include hồn in its vietnamese list.
