## Notes
- [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" + [質](../characters/質.md) "essence; nature; pledge"

人質 is a hostage: a person held to force something from others. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin rénzhì as "hostage" and Japanese as "a hostage", with archaic Japanese sense "indentureship", being held as collateral for a debt. Korean 인질 and Cantonese jan4 zi3 match. The word is literally "a person as pledge", with 質 meaning "pledge" in this word.

The real Japanese word is read ひとじち, a native reading, so the on'yomi じんしつ given in the field is the Sino-Japanese reading of the characters. The crime is [誘拐](誘拐.md), kidnapping, when the taker holds the person for ransom or demands; 人質 names the person held.
