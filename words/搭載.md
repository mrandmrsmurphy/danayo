## Notes
- [搭](../characters/搭.md) "board" + [載](../characters/載%20(char).md) "carry, record" — no stand-in relationship applies (搭's own stand-in is [[搭乗]]; 載's own is itself).

搭載 means to transport or carry (as freight, cargo, or an onboard system).

Mandarin dāzài, Cantonese daap3 zoi3, Japanese とうさい, Korean 탑재, and Vietnamese đáp tải are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄚㄆㄐㄚㄧ unique across both words and characters).
