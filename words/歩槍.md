## Notes
- [歩](../characters/歩.md) "step, foot" + [槍](../characters/槍%20(char).md) "spear, gun" — "infantry gun," a rifle. Their own `stand_in` values: 歩's is [[散歩]]; 槍's is itself; none points here, so 歩槍 is an independent compound.

歩槍 is a rifle: a long firearm carried by a soldier on foot. Mandarin bùqiāng and Cantonese bou6 coeng1 are the ordinary Chinese words, the 步 being the foot soldier of [[歩兵]]. Japanese says 小銃 and Korean 소총, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[銃]] (firearm) and [[大砲]] (cannon).
