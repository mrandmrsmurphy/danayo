## Notes
- [毛 (char)](../characters/毛%20(char).md) "fur" + [皮](../characters/皮.md) "skin, hide, outer layer" — no stand-in relationship applies (毛's own stand-in is itself; 皮's own is [[皮革]]).

毛皮 means fur, pelt.

Mandarin máopí and Korean 모피 are standard Sino compositional readings. Japanese けがわ is a native kun-yomi compound (毛's own native け + 皮's own native かわ, rendaku-voiced to がわ) — this is the real, standard Japanese word for "fur," matching the character's own stored native readings rather than the Sino on'yomi. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with mao bì (毛's mao + 皮's bì), compositional. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄚㄨㄅㄧ unique across both words and characters).
