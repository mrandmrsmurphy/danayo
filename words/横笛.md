## Notes
- [横](../characters/横.md) "horizontal; across" + [笛 (char)](../characters/笛%20(char).md) "flute"

横笛 is a transverse flute: a flute held sideways and blown across a hole in the side. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin héngdí as "transverse flute" and Japanese おうてき as "a transverse flute". Korean 횡적 is the same word. Cantonese waang4 dek6-2 matches.

It is a kind of [笛](笛.md), flute, played by [吹奏](吹奏.md), and the end-blown bamboo flute is the [洞簫](洞簫.md), xiao, or the Japanese [尺八](尺八.md). A mouth organ is the [鳳笙](鳳笙.md).
