## Notes
- [羞](../characters/羞.md) "shame" + [辱](../characters/辱.md) "humiliate, insult, embarrass, disgrace" — 辱's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 羞's own is [[羞恥]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 羞辱 legitimizes 辱 alone.

羞辱 means to humiliate.

Mandarin xiūrǔ (also xiūrù), Cantonese sau1 juk6, Japanese しゅうじょく (shūjoku), and Korean 수욕 (suyok) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese tu nhuốc is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading.
