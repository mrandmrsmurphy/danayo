## Notes
- [羞](../characters/羞.md) "shame" + [辱](../characters/辱.md) "humiliate, insult, embarrass, disgrace" — 辱's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 羞's own is [[羞恥]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 羞辱 legitimizes 辱 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

羞辱 means to humiliate.

Mandarin xiūrǔ (also xiūrù), Cantonese sau1 juk6, Japanese しゅうじょく (shūjoku), and Korean 수욕 (suyok) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese tu nhuốc is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. **Fixed a real bug**: `japanese`/`korean` had くつじょく/굴욕, the real readings of the unrelated word 屈辱 ("humiliation," a different, near-synonymous compound with a different first character) rather than 羞辱's own compositional readings — corrected. Removed the erroneous `屈辱` alias entry (a distinct real word, not a variant spelling of 羞辱; it has no vault page of its own). No homophones.
