## Notes
- [天](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven" + [文](../characters/文.md) "pattern" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study" — no stand-in relationship applies (天 is its own stand-in; 文's own is [[文化]]; 学's is [[学習]]).

天文学 means astronomy — the scientific study of celestial objects, per [[天文]].

**Real bugs found and fixed**: `羅馬字`/`諺文` had 天's syllable malformed (tenmunhak had the right "ten" but 諺文 showed "터," missing the final ㄴ) — corrected to tenmunhag/턴문학. `cantonese` was blank — filled tin1 man4 hok6 (compositional). Japanese てんもんがく, Korean 천문학, and Vietnamese thiên văn học are all standard readings. Removed the redundant `品詞` field. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄝㄋㄇㄨㄋㄏㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
