## Notes
- [凶 (char)](../characters/凶%20(char).md) "unlucky; sinister" + [暴 (char)](../characters/暴%20(char).md) "violent; brutal; fierce; tyrannical"

凶暴 means ferocious and brutal: savagely violent, a sinister intent joined to overwhelming force. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 兇暴 (an alias; Mandarin xiōngbào, Cantonese hung1 bou6) as "fierce and brutal; cruel", and Japanese きょうぼう as a na-adjective "brutal; ferocious" and a noun "brutality; ferocity". It has no Korean or Vietnamese entry, so Korean 흉포 and Vietnamese hung bạo here are built from the characters.

Where [[凶悪]], heinous, stresses the moral side of evil, 凶暴 stresses savage action. The character 暴 here is the "violent" one, and the related [[暴徒]], rioters, shares it.
