## Notes
- [晋](../characters/晋.md) "increase" + [升 (char)](../characters/升%20(char).md) "rise, ascend" — this word is the stand-in for [晋](../characters/晋.md), which cannot appear independently.

晋升 means to promote or advance in rank. The character 晋 alone means increase, but is too bounded to stand by itself in Dan'a'yo, so this compound serves as its viable form.
