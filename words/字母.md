## Notes
- [字](../characters/字%20(char).md) "character, written symbol" + [母](../characters/母.md) "mother, base" — no stand-in relationship applies (字's own stand-in is itself; 母's own is [[母親]]).

字母 means grapheme, or letter of an alphabet — the base written units from which words are composed.

Mandarin zìmǔ, Cantonese zi6 mou5, Japanese じぼ, Korean 자모, and Vietnamese tự mẫu are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄇㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).
