Corrected to the genuine compositional readings ふすい/부수. Also corrected `kwin` from false to true, matching the AND-rule (both constituents are individually `kwin: true`).

符水 names water that has been ritually charmed or blessed with a written talisman (符) dissolved or steeped in it — used in Daoist and folk-religious practice as a healing or protective drink.

Mandarin fúshuǐ, Cantonese fu4 seoi2, Japanese ふすい (fusui), and Korean 부수 (busu) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese phù thuỷ is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form, though in modern everyday Vietnamese phù thủy has instead drifted to mean "sorcerer, wizard" rather than the water itself.
