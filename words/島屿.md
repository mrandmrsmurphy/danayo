## Notes
- [島 (char)](../characters/島%20(char).md) "island" + [屿](../characters/屿.md) "islet, small island" — islands and islets collectively; an archipelago

島屿 means islands, collectively: islands large and small, an archipelago. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 島嶼 (an alias; Mandarin dǎoyǔ, Cantonese dou2 zeoi6), labelled collective, as "island", Japanese とうしょ as "islands large and small", and Korean 도서 as "islands" under the hanja form; Korean 도서 is also the word for "book", an unrelated homograph. The Cantonese field gives Wiktionary's reading of the compound, zeoi6, not the vault's reading of the bare 屿, jyu6. It has no Vietnamese entry, so Vietnamese đảo tự here is built from the characters.

The compound pairs 島, island, with 屿, islet, so that it covers islands of every size. Compare [[南北]], north and south, another pair of related words taken together.
