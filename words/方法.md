## Notes
- [方](../characters/方.md) "square, direction" + [法](../characters/法%20(char).md) "law, method" — no stand-in relationship applies (方's own stand-in is [[方向]]; 法's own is itself).

方法 means method, way, or manner.

Mandarin fāngfǎ, Cantonese fong1 faat3, Japanese ほうほう, Korean 방법, and Vietnamese phương pháp are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄈㄚㄫㄈㄚㄆ unique across both words and characters).
