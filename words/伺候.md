## Notes
- [伺](../characters/伺.md) "to watch, wait upon" (special reading cì, distinct from the more common sì "to spy") + [候](../characters/候.md) "to wait, attend" — "to wait upon, serve, attend to" (a master or superior). `vietnamese: tứ hầu` is directly attested, found in classical literary usage such as Dream of the Red Chamber translations (confirmed via web search), a more literary register than the everyday synonym hầu hạ. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 시홋 diverges from Sino-Korean 사후 in the first syllable's vowel — the same divergence pattern shared with its homophone [[時候]].
