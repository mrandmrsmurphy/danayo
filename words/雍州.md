## Notes
- [雍](../characters/雍.md) + [州 (char)](../characters/州%20(char).md) — Yong Province, the ninth of the [[九州]]

Wiktionary lists 雍州 under Chinese, historical: "Yong; Yongzhou", various regions or provinces of ancient and early medieval China, usually surrounding the Wei River or the imperial capital. The *Tribute of Yu* (尚書, 禹貢, Wikisource) has the section 雍州 beginning 黑水、西河惟雍州, "from the Black River to the West River is Yongzhou". 雍's own `stand_in` is 雍雍, so this word is not its legitimizer; it names the province as listed among the [[九州]].
