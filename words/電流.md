## Notes
- [電](../characters/電.md) "lightning, electric" + [流](../characters/流.md) "flow" — "electric flow," current. Their own `stand_in` values: 電's is [[電気]]; 流's is [[流動]]; none points here, so 電流 is an independent compound.

電流 is electric current: the flow of electric charge along a conductor. Mandarin diànliú, Cantonese din6 lau4, Japanese でんりゅう and Korean 전류 all use it. Compare [[電圧]] (voltage), [[電気]] (electricity) and [[回路]] (circuit).
