## Notes
- [膠](../characters/膠.md) "glue, rubber, gelatin" + [片](../characters/片%20(char).md) "slice, sheet" — "gelatin sheet," photographic film. Their own `stand_in` values: 膠's is [[膠水]]; 片's is itself; none points here, so 膠片 is an independent compound.

膠片 is photographic film: the coated strip on which an image is recorded. Mandarin jiāopiàn and Cantonese gaau1 pin3-2 are the ordinary Chinese words, from 膠 "gelatin" and 片 "sheet". Japanese says フィルム and Korean 필름, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. The finished movie is [[映画]]. Compare [[照相机]] (camera) and [[写真]] (photograph).
