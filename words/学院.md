## Notes
- [学](../characters/学.md) "learn; study" + [院](../characters/院.md) "institution" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (学's is 学習, 院's is 院落).

Mandarin xuéyuàn, Cantonese hok6 jyun2, Japanese がくいん, Korean 학원, and Vietnamese học viện are all everyday words for "institute; academy" (Korean 학원 specifically denotes a private cram school/after-school academy in modern usage, a narrower but consistent sense).
