## Notes
- [廈](../characters/廈.md) "building, mansion" + [門](../characters/門.md) "gate" — gate of the great house (originally 下門 "lower gate"). No stand-in relationship applies (廈's is [[大廈]]; 門's is [[大門]]).

廈門 (Xiamen, Amoy) is a sub-provincial port city on the southeast coast of Fujian province. Per Wiktionary the name is a homophone of the original name 下門 "lower gate" that came into official use for its more elegant sense; it also names a historical coastal fortification and Xiamen Island. 厦 is an alias of 廈 and 门 of 門, so 厦门 (and 厦門) are kept as aliases.

English Wiktionary lists no Sino-Japanese reading for the city: the Japanese names are the borrowings アモイ (from Portuguese "Amoy", from the Hokkien pronunciation) and シアメン (from Mandarin); the field holds アモイ, and no reading かもん is attested. Korean 하문 and Vietnamese Hạ Môn are the Sino-Xenic readings listed on Wiktionary; the Korean field here holds the North Korean reading derived from the character pages, 하문, and the usual South Korean name today is 샤먼 (listed on Wiktionary). Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese entries. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚ·ㄇㄛㄋ unique across both words and characters).
