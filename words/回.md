## Notes
- [回 (char)](../characters/回%20(char).md) "turns"

回 is 象形, depicting a whirlpool — the swirling, spiraling motion of rotating water. It means "to turn, revolve, return" (回答, "to respond, reply"; 回転, "to turn, spin, rotate") and serves as a measure word for repeated occurrences/times.

Mandarin huí and Cantonese wui4 preserve the sense directly. Japanese カイ/エ (KAI/E, on'yomi, fully productive: 回転 kaiten, "rotation"; 回向 ekō, a Buddhist term for transferring merit) cover the sense, with no dedicated native kun'yomi. Korean 회 (hoe) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese hồi is the standard Hán Việt reading, well attested in luân hồi (輪回, "saṃsāra, the cycle of rebirth") and hồi âm ("to reply, echo back"); a secondary literary reading, hối, is also attested but nearly obsolete, glossed only as "to fear, shy away from." The character page's other stored candidate, hòi, is a genuine but distinct Nôm (vernacular) reading — phonologically a different syllable from hồi, not a diacritic variant — used when 回 is borrowed to write an unrelated native Vietnamese word rather than as a Sino-Vietnamese loan reading, and was excluded here.

This word shares its reading (hoi/회/ㄏㄛㄧ) with [[潰]] ("burst; fester") and [[灰]] ("ash"), both still unperfected and awaiting their own turn in this sweep.
