## Notes
- [足 (char)](../characters/足%20(char).md) "foot" + [裏](../characters/裏.md) "inside, reverse side" — "sole (of the foot), the underside opposite the visible top." Japanese あしうら/あしのうら (both real, attested kun-yomi readings of this exact compound) are the standard everyday terms.

**Real content bug found and fixed**: `mandarin` had been stored as jiǎodǐ, the reading of the unrelated compound 腳底 (using entirely different characters, 腳 + 底) rather than 足裏's own characters — corrected to the compositional zúlǐ. Mandarin's actual everyday word for this concept is indeed 腳底/脚底 (jiǎodǐ) or 腳掌/脚掌 (jiǎozhǎng), disclosed here rather than fabricated as aliases of 足裏 itself (these were previously miscategorized as `aliases`, but they use different characters entirely and have no independent vault pages — removed from `aliases`). `korean: 족리` and `vietnamese: túc lí` are likewise honest compositional readings; Korean's real everyday word is the native 발바닥 ("foot-bottom"), and Vietnamese's is the native lòng bàn chân ("palm of the foot"). `kwin` is true: with the corrected compositional `korean` field, Dan'a'yo 족리 now matches Sino-Korean 족리 exactly (it had been impossible to properly evaluate `kwin` while `korean` held an unrelated native word). No homophones (注音 ㄐㄛㄎㄌㄧ unique among both words and characters).
