## Notes
- [菊 (char)](../characters/菊%20(char).md) "chrysanthemum" is separately legitimized by [[菊]] itself. [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month, moon" is separately legitimized by [[月]] itself. No cranberry.

Ninth month of the ancient Chinese poetic calendar. Chrysanthemums (菊花) are the classic flower of autumn — they bloom after all others have faded, symbolizing resilience and integrity. The Double Ninth Festival (重陽節, 9th day of the 9th month) is when people wear chrysanthemums and drink chrysanthemum wine.

Pronunciation fields (gug'wed/국웓/ㄍㄨㄎ·⼔ㄊ) are the straightforward concatenation of each constituent's own stored reading, including the null-onset syllable break — no bug; `kwin: false` already correct. Japanese きくつき (on'yomi KIKU + native つき) already matches the established poetic-month convention. Mandarin júyuè, korean 국월, and vietnamese tháng cúc all standard and compositional. Fixed cantonese's stray space, removed redundant 品詞. No homophones.
