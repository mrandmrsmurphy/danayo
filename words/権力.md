## Notes
- [権](../characters/権.md) "authority" + [力 (char)](../characters/力%20(char).md) "power"

権力 is power, authority. It is spelled with the Dan'a'yo forms of the characters, and 權力 and 权力 are aliases. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 權力 (Mandarin quánlì, Cantonese kyun4 lik6) as "power; authority" and Japanese けんりょく as "power, authority". It lists Korean 권력 and Vietnamese entries. Compare [権威](権威.md), authority and prestige.
