## Notes
- [忌](../characters/忌.md) "taboo" + [諱](../characters/諱%20(char).md) "avoid mentioning, forbidden name" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [忌](../characters/忌.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (諱's own stand-in is itself).

忌諱 means taboo — something forbidden or avoided by custom, especially a subject too sensitive to mention directly.

Mandarin jìhuì, Cantonese gei6 wai5, Japanese きき, Korean 기휘, and Vietnamese kị huý are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `korean` had comma-separated native/loanword contamination (금기, 터부) instead of the compositional reading. Filled blank `vietnamese`, added missing `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄏㄨㄧ unique across both words and characters).
