## Etymology
- [刺 (char)](../characters/刺%20(char).md) "stab, prick, sting" + [身](../characters/身.md) "body"

## Notes
The Japanese word for sashimi (thinly sliced raw fish/seafood). `korean: 회` is a real, native Korean word for the same dish, unrelated to any compositional reading of 刺身 itself. `vietnamese: xẻo thân` (confirmed via web search — "xẻo," a native gloss for "to slice thinly," + 身's own Sino-Vietnamese thân) is a literal descriptive calque directly attested as a Vietnamese rendering of "sashimi," used alongside the more common direct loanword sashimi itself in everyday Vietnamese. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 칙신 diverges entirely from the native-word-based Korean field. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄎㄙㄧㄋ unique among both words and characters).
