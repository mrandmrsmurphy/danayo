## Notes
- [宮](../characters/宮.md) "royal court" + [埼](../characters/埼.md) "peninsula"

宮埼 is Miyazaki, written "shrine cape" (the usual spelling is 宮崎, kept as an alias because 崎 is itself an alias of the parent character 埼). The name is native: 宮 (みや, "shrine") plus 埼 (さき, voiced to ざき). It refers to the shrine Miyazaki-gū, dedicated to Emperor Jimmu.

Miyazaki is the old province of Hyūga, where Japanese myth places the descent of the gods at Takachiho and the departure of Emperor Jimmu on his eastward campaign. It has a warm Pacific coast with surfing beaches and is known for mangoes and chicken nanban.

In Dan'a'yo 宮埼 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 미야자키 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
