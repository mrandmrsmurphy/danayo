## Notes
- [山 (char)](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain" + [嶺](../characters/嶺.md) "mountain ridge; pass" — this word is the stand-in for [嶺](../characters/嶺.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

山嶺 names a mountain ridge or range — a continuous chain of peaks, or the pass cutting through one.

Japanese さんれい, Korean 산령, and Vietnamese sơn lĩnh (verified) are all standard readings. No HSK level is given for the compound. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄚㄋㄌㄧㄫ unique across both words and characters).
