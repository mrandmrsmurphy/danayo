## Notes
- [牆](../characters/牆.md) "wall" + [壁 (char)](../characters/壁%20(char).md) "wall" — 牆's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 壁's own is itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 牆 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

牆壁 means a wall, in the general or ancient sense.

Mandarin qiángbì, Cantonese coeng4 bik1, Japanese しょうへき and Korean 장벽 are fully compositional, and Vietnamese tường bích is 牆's own tường plus 壁's own bích. The 注音 ㄑ⺢ㄫㄅㄝㄎ spells both syllables.
