## Notes
- [手](../characters/手%20(char).md) "hand" + [巾](../characters/巾.md) "towel" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [巾](../characters/巾.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (手's own stand-in is itself).

手巾 means towel.

Mandarin shǒujīn, Cantonese sau2 gan1, Japanese しゅきん, and Korean 수건 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese: thủ cân` (compositional; the everyday Vietnamese word for towel/scarf, khăn, is a native cognate of 巾 rather than the Sino-Vietnamese compound). No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼜ㄎㄧㄋ unique across both words and characters).
