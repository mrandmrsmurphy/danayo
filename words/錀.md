## Notes
- [錀 (char)](../characters/錀%20(char).md) "roentgenium" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 錀 itself doubles as this vault's periodic-table abbreviation letter for roentgenium.

錀 names roentgenium (Rg, atomic number 111), a synthetic superheavy element (all isotopes radioactive, longest half-life about 3.6 seconds) named for Wilhelm Röntgen, discoverer of X-rays, on the 100th anniversary of that discovery in 2004.

Mandarin lún and Cantonese leon4 are the real, standard readings, matching 錀's own stored citations. **Corrected 2026-09-22**: this word's own `korean` and `japanese` previously stored the same compositional readings as the character's own citation form (륜/りん) rather than the real, standard element names — corrected to 뢴트게늄 (roentgenyum) and レントゲニウム (rentogeniumu), both direct transliterations of Röntgen's name, matching the real-term-over-compositional-reading convention already established throughout this vault's periodic table (compare [[徳素]], [[冥金]]); 錀's own character page correctly keeps 륜/りん as its citation-form reading, the same split already seen between constituent characters and their whole-word element names elsewhere (e.g. [[苦土素]]'s 素 keeping そ while the word gets マグネシウム). `vietnamese` was likewise corrected from ø (no attested reading) to Roentgeni, the real, standard IUPAC-recognized Vietnamese name, on both this word's page and the character's own (since no separate Vietnamese citation-form convention exists for this synthetic element). No Dan'a'yo homophones at the word level (注音 ㄌㄛㄋ unique among words; 崙/論 happen to share this reading among characters, but no other *word* does).
