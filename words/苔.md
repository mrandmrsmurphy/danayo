## Notes
- [苔](../characters/苔%20(char).md) "moss, lichen" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 苔 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

Mandarin tái, korean 태 (tae), and cantonese toi4 (one of two stored candidates, the other being toi1) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading こけ (koke, "moss") rather than the on-readings タイ/ダイ (TAI/DAI) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese đài is one of six stored candidates. No homophones.
