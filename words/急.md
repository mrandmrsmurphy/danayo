## Notes
- [急](../characters/急%20(char).md) "quick, urgent" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

急 means quick or urgent — pressing, requiring immediate action, as in 急用 ("urgent matter") and 救急 ("emergency").

Mandarin jí, Cantonese gap1, Japanese きゅう, Korean 급, and Vietnamese cấp are all standard, fully compositional readings.
