## Notes
- [疲](../characters/疲.md) "exhausted" + [困](../characters/困.md) "distressed, sleepy, fatigued" — 困's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 疲's own is [[疲労]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 困 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

[[疲労]] is not an alias: it is a separate, independent word with its own distinct reading (ㄆㄧㄌㄚㄨ) .
