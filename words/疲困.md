## Notes
- [疲](../characters/疲.md) "exhausted" + [困](../characters/困.md) "distressed, sleepy, fatigued" — 困's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 疲's own is [[疲労]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 困 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

Mandarin píkùn and Korean 피곤 were already correct and fully compositional. Fixed a real contamination bug in `japanese`: ひろう (the reading of the unrelated alias [[疲労]]) had been substituted here → corrected to ひこん (HI+KON), the correct compositional reading. Filled the blank `cantonese` with pei4 kwan3 and blank `vietnamese` with bì khốn, both compositional. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄧㄎㄛㄋ unique across both words and characters).
