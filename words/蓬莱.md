## Notes
- [蓬](../characters/蓬.md) "mugwort" + [莱](../characters/莱.md) "Penglai" — this word is 莱's own `stand_in`: the character's meaning (Penglai) is identical, but 莱 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word, so 蓬 supplies the compound's first syllable, lending the mythical name its full form.

In classical Chinese mythology, Penglai is the island paradise of the immortals located in the eastern sea — the best-known of the Three Sacred Mountains (三神山, alongside 方丈 and 瀛洲), none of which are yet their own words in this vault. Also used figuratively for any fairyland or paradise. Mandarin pénglái, Cantonese pung4 loi4, Japanese ほうらい, and Korean 봉래 are all standard, fully compositional readings.
