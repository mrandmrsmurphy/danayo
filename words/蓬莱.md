## Notes
- [蓬](../characters/蓬.md) "mugwort" + [莱](../characters/莱.md) "Penglai" — this word is 莱's own `stand_in`: the character's meaning (Penglai) is identical, but 莱 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word, so 蓬 supplies the compound's first syllable, lending the mythical name its full form.

蓬莱 is Penglai: in Chinese mythology the island of the immortals in the eastern sea, and by extension any fairyland. English Wiktionary lists it as 蓬萊 (an alias), glossed "(Chinese mythology) Penglai, a mythical eastern isle of immortals" and "by extension, fairyland", with 瀛洲 and 方丈 as the related legendary isles. Mandarin pénglái has Cantonese pung4 loi4 (also fung4 loi4). It is also the name of a district of Yantai in Shandong, and, literary, "wilderness". Japanese ほうらい is Horai, "a mythical Japanese paradise developed from the Chinese legend of Penglai", and also "Taiwan" and a surname. It has no Korean entry, so Korean 봉래 here is built from the characters.

The other two islands, 瀛洲 and 方丈, are not yet words in this vault.
