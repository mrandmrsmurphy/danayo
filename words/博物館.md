## Notes
- [博](../characters/博.md) "broad; wide" + [物 (char)](../characters/物%20(char).md) "thing" + [館](../characters/館.md) "hall"

博物館 is a museum: a building where objects of history, science or art are kept and shown. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin bówùguǎn as "museum" and Japanese はくぶつかん as "museum". Korean 박물관 is the same word. Cantonese bok3 mat6 gun2 matches.

The one for art is the [美術館](美術館.md), and a library is the [図書館](図書館.md).
