## Notes
- [晶](../characters/晶.md) "crystal" + [片](../characters/片%20(char).md) "slice, chip" — "crystal slice," a silicon chip. Their own `stand_in` values: 晶's is [[水晶]]; 片's is itself; none points here, so 晶片 is an independent compound.

晶片 is a chip or microchip: a slice of crystalline silicon carrying circuits. Mandarin jīngpiàn and Cantonese zing1 pin3-2 are the ordinary Chinese words in Taiwan (Mainland China writes 芯片). Japanese says チップ or 集積回路 and Korean 칩, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[中央処理器]] (CPU).
