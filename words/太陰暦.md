## Notes
- [太 (char)](../characters/太%20(char).md) "great" + [陰 (char)](../characters/陰%20(char).md) "moon, yin" + [暦 (char)](../characters/暦%20(char).md) "calendar"

太陰暦 means lunar calendar — a calendar based on the phases of the moon, as opposed to [[太陽暦]] (solar calendar). Vietnamese âm lịch is the standard real-world term (dropping 太, a common real-world contraction also seen in colloquial 陰曆/阴历 — these are shorter synonyms of the concept, not orthographic aliases of this word).
