## Notes
- [拌](../characters/拌.md) "mix, blend" + [和](../characters/和.md) "peaceful, harmonious" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [拌](../characters/拌.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (和's own stand-in is [[和平]]).

拌和 means to stir, mix, or blend.

Mandarin bànhuò, Cantonese bun6 wo4, Japanese はんわ, Korean 반화, and Vietnamese phan hoà are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄋㄏ⺢ unique across both words and characters).
