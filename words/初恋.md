## Notes
- [初](../characters/初.md) "first, beginning" + [恋](../characters/恋%20(char).md) "romantic love" — "first love." Their own `stand_in` values: 初's is [[最初]]; 恋's is itself; none points here, so 初恋 is an independent compound.

初恋 is a first love: the first time one falls in love, and the person one loves then. Mandarin chūliàn (初戀), Cantonese co1 lyun2, Japanese はつこい (the native reading, which the field does not hold) and Korean 초련 are attested. Compare [[恋]] (romantic love) and [[失恋]] (to be jilted).
