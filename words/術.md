## Notes
- Single-character stand-in word — [術 (char)](../characters/術%20(char).md)'s own `stand_in` is 術 itself, so 術 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry by itself.

術 means skill, method, or technique — a semantically light, highly productive suffix rather than a word typically used bare. It is one of the most fertile compounding elements in the whole sphere, naming a body of specialized skill or a codified practice: [[技術]] ("technology, technique," combining with 技 "ability"), [[芸術]] ("skill, art, craft"), [[算術]] ("arithmetic," literally "the skill of counting"), [[武術]] ("martial art"), and the divinatory/occult cluster [[巫術]] ("shamanism, sorcery") and [[法術]] ("magic arts"). It also names several of the Six Classical Arts (六藝) directly: [[射術]] (archery) and [[御術]] (charioteering).

Mandarin shù, Cantonese seot6, Korean 술, and Dan'a'yo ㄙㄨㄊ are all straightforward reflexes of the character's own stored reading. Japanese distinguishes two layers here: the Sino-Japanese on'yomi じゅつ (jutsu) is the productive compounding reading seen in 技術/芸術/武術 above, while the native kun'yomi すべ survives as an older, now largely literary word for "a means, a way" (なすすべがない, "there is nothing to be done" — literally "no 術 to do"). Vietnamese thuật is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading, equally productive in compounds like kỹ thuật (技術).
