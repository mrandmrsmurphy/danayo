## Notes
- [開](../characters/開.md) "open" + [城](../characters/城.md) "castle, wall" — the name of Kaesong, a city in the south of North Korea.

開城 (Kaesong) is a city in the south of North Korea, close to the border, and was the capital of Goryeo ([[高麗]]) from 918 to 1392 (from memory, not re-checked). Wiktionary lists it with the status of a special city (特別市). The name reads "open city".

English and Chinese Wiktionary have 開城 as the Korean city (Mandarin Kāichéng, Cantonese hoi1 sing4), and English Wiktionary has a Korean entry as a hanja form of 개성; the Japanese section gives ケソン (from the Korean) for the city, alongside a dated reading かいじょう, which is taken here as the Sino-Xenic reading. The same string 開城 is also an ordinary Japanese word かいじょう "surrender of a castle, capitulation", unrelated to the city. Vietnamese Khai Thành is the Hán-Việt name given on the Vietnamese Wikipedia's Kaesong article.
