## Etymology
- [実](../characters/実.md) "true, accurate" + [情](../characters/情.md) "feelings, emotion"

## Notes
- "The actual state of affairs, the real facts, the true situation" — Japanese/Mandarin/Cantonese/Korean all converge on this circumstantial-truth sense. Filled blank `vietnamese: thực tình` — attested, but with a caveat: Vietnamese has drifted this word toward the emotional-honesty sense ("sincerely, truthfully speaking, without pretense") rather than the "actual factual situation" sense the rest of the family shares, since 情 leans on its "feelings" side rather than its "circumstances" side in Vietnamese usage. **Left `hsk_level` unset**: no reliable classification found. Kept the existing legitimate traditional-variant `aliases: [實情]`.
- `kwin: false` confirmed (実 alone matches Korean, 情 doesn't — AND-rule). No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄊㄑㄧㄫ unique among both words and characters).
