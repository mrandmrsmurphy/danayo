## Notes
- [灰 (char)](../characters/灰%20(char).md) "ash" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color"

Wiktionary: Chinese 灰色 "gray (color)"; Japanese 灰色 (はいいろ) "gray (color), an ashen color", "sad, pessimistic, gloomy" and "arousing doubt or suspicion of dishonesty or criminal activity"; Korean 회색 is the hanja form, "grey (colour)". A related term is [[鼠色]].
