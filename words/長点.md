## Notes
- [長 (char)](../characters/長%20(char).md) "long" + [点 (char)](../characters/点%20(char).md) "point; dot"

Wiktionary has no entry for 長点; it is a compound of 長 "long" and 点. It names the long dot, a variety of the dot stroke ([[点]]) listed on the [[Stroke]] page, where the dot is subdivided into 瓜子点, 長点 and 挑点 (see [[挑点]]). Its readings are compositional.
