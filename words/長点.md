## Notes
- [長 (char)](../characters/長%20(char).md) "long" + [点 (char)](../characters/点%20(char).md) "point; dot"

**長点** is the *long dot*, a variety of the basic dot stroke ([[点]], the first of the eight principles of the 永字八法 listed on [[Stroke]]) in which the dot is drawn out longer than the ordinary short dot. Chinese calligraphy instruction describes it as written in the same way as the short dot, only with a longer middle path, and also calls it the *willow-leaf dot* because its shape resembles a willow leaf.

It is one of several named dot types in regular-script teaching (alongside the short dot, the vertical dot, the 撇-dot and the [[挑点]]) and is listed among the sub-varieties of 点 on the [[Stroke]] page, where the dot stroke is subdivided into 瓜子点, 長点 and 挑点.

Mandarin chángdiǎn and Cantonese coeng4 dim2 are compositional. The Japanese ちょうてん and North Korean 장점 are likewise compositional (the Korean string coincides with the unrelated everyday word 長點 *jangjeom* "strength, merit"), and the Japanese reading coincides with 頂点 (*chōten*, "apex") — these coincidences are in the shared pronunciation of the Sino-Xenic readings only, with no connection between the words. No Vietnamese form was found attested, so none is given.
