## Notes
- [標](../characters/標.md) "mark, sign" + [準](../characters/準.md) "standard, level" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [準](../characters/準.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 標's own stand-in is [[標識]].

標準 means standard or norm.

Mandarin biāozhǔn, Cantonese biu1 zeon2, Japanese ひょうじゅん, Korean 표준, and Vietnamese tiêu chuẩn are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed bare-integer `hsk_level`, removed the self-referential `標準` from `aliases` (duplicate of the word's own title), kept the genuine variants `标准`/`標准`. No homophones (注音 ㄅ⼄ㄐㄨㄋ unique across both words and characters).
