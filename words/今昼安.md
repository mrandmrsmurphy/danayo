## Notes
- [今 (char)](../characters/今%20(char).md) "now" + [昼](../characters/昼.md) "daytime" + [安](../characters/安.md) "peace, well-being" — to be used in the daylight hours, when the sun is up, "good mid-day."

This is a Dan'a'yo-internal coinage, not a calque of any single source word — none of Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, or Korean have a real compound built from these same three characters as a daytime greeting. Following this vault's convention for such coinages (compare [[井戸]], [[乳押]]), the mandarin/cantonese/japanese/korean/vietnamese fields instead each document that language's own real everyday greeting: 你好/nei5 hou2, こんにちは (itself structurally similar, "as for today..."), 안녕하세요, and xin chào. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 김줏안 diverges entirely from the native-greeting-based Korean field. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄇㄐㄨㄛㄚㄋ unique among both words and characters).
