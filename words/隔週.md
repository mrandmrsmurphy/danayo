## Notes
- [隔 (char)](../characters/隔%20(char).md) "every other" + [週](../characters/週.md) "week"

隔週 means every other week, occurring biweekly/fortnightly. Mandarin 隔週/隔周 (gézhōu), Cantonese 隔週 (gaak3zau1), Japanese 隔週 (かくしゅう), and Korean 격주 (gyeokju) are all standard terms across the CJKV sphere. Sino-Vietnamese cách chu is compositional but rare — everyday Vietnamese instead uses the native periphrastic phrase cứ hai tuần một lần ("once every two weeks"), since 週's own Sino-Vietnamese readings (chu, châu) never established themselves as the everyday word for "week" the way native tuần did.
