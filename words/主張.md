## Notes
- [主](../characters/主.md) "lord; master" + [張](../characters/張.md) "to stretch; extend"

主張 is "to assert, to maintain, to advocate," and as a noun "a position, an opinion, a claim." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 主張 (zhǔzhāng, Cantonese zyu2 zoeng1) means "position; point of view; opinion" and "to maintain a position; to advocate; to stand for," with Southern Min "to think" and an archaic "to dominate; to rule"; Japanese しゅちょう is "to maintain, argue, claim"; Korean 주장 is "claim, assertion"; and Vietnamese chủ trương is "position; standpoint; policy." It is used in the 1919 Korean Declaration of Independence ([宣言書](../translation/宣言書.md)) (吾人의 主張과 態度). Related: [表明](表明.md) "make clear."
