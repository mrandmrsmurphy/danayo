## Notes
- [受](../characters/受.md) "accept; receive" + [験 (char)](../characters/験%20(char).md) "check; examine"

受験 is to sit an exam, especially an entrance exam: to be examined, in the passive sense of 受 "receive". Wiktionary glosses Japanese じゅけん as "taking an exam, especially an entrance exam; to take an exam". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 수험 matches.

It is what a candidate does for [入試](入試.md), the entrance exam, and the result is [合格](合格.md) or [落第](落第.md). A [試験](試験.md) is the exam itself.
