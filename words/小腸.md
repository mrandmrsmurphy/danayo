## Notes
- [小 (char)](../characters/小%20(char).md) "small" + [腸](../characters/腸.md) "intestine" — no stand-in relationship applies (小's own `stand_in` is itself, 腸's is [[腸管]]).

小腸 is the anatomical small intestine, paired with its counterpart 大腸 (large intestine) — a standard medical/biological term across all four languages.

Japanese しょうちょう, Korean 소장, and Vietnamese tiểu tràng are all standard, non-drifted medical-register readings. No HSK level is given for the compound. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄛㄑㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
