## Notes
- [洗](../characters/洗%20(char).md) "wash, rinse" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 洗 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

洗 means to wash or rinse: to clean something with water (洗手, "wash hands"; 洗衣, "do laundry"; 洗澡, "bathe"; 洗碗, "wash dishes"), and by extension to purge, cleanse, or wipe out (洗禮, "baptism"; 洗錢, "launder money"; 洗腦, "brainwash"). The graph is a 形声 compound of 氵 "water" with the phonetic 先.

Mandarin xǐ, Cantonese sai2, Japanese せん (洗濯/せんたく, "laundry"; 洗練/せんれん, "refinement"; 洗礼/せんれい, "baptism"), Korean 선 and Vietnamese tẩy (tẩy rửa, "to cleanse"; tẩy não, "brainwash") are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native verb 洗う (あらう) for the everyday word, so on'yomi せん is bound to compounds, and Korean has native 씻다.
