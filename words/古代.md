## Notes
- [古](../characters/古.md) "ancient" + [代](../characters/代.md) "era, generation" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [古](../characters/古.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (代's own stand-in is [[世代]]).

古代 means ancient times or antiquity — a bygone historical era.

Filled blank `vietnamese: cổ đại` (verified — the standard, extremely common Sino-Vietnamese word for "ancient times"). Japanese こだい and Korean 고대 are both standard readings, matching Dan'a'yo's own reading exactly (`kwin: true`). No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄉㄚㄧ unique across both words and characters).
