## Notes
- [抹](../characters/抹%20(char).md) "rub, wipe" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [抹](../characters/抹%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

抹 means to rub or wipe.

Mandarin mǒ, Cantonese mut3, Japanese まつ, Korean 말, and Vietnamese mạt are all standard, fully compositional readings. Homophone of [[沫]] "froth; foam; bubbles; suds" (both 注音 ㄇㄚㄊ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words; checked other candidates sharing this reading (末/茉) and confirmed neither has an independent word at this exact reading (both bound to longer stand-in compounds).
