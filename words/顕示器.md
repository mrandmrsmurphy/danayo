## Notes
- [顕](../characters/顕.md) "show" + [示](../characters/示.md) "display" + [器](../characters/器.md) "device" — "display device." Their own `stand_in` values: 顕's is [[顕着]]; 示's is [[開示]]; 器's is [[容器]]; none points here, so 顕示器 is an independent compound.

顕示器 is a computer monitor or display. Mandarin xiǎnshìqì and Cantonese hin2 si6 hei3 are the ordinary Chinese words (the Mainland term; Taiwan says 螢幕). Japanese says ディスプレイ or モニター and Korean 모니터, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[電脳]] (computer) and [[机器]] (machine).
