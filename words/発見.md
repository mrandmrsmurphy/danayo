## Notes
- [発 (char)](../characters/発%20(char).md) "issue forth, discharge" + [見 (char)](../characters/見%20(char).md) "see" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both are bare self-standing characters); 発見 is an independent compound.

The `mandarin` and `cantonese` readings (fājiàn, faat3 gin3) are those of 発見 itself, written with 見 "see". They are not those of the similar compound 発現/发现 (fāxiàn, with 現 "appear, manifest"), and the aliases 發見 and 发见 are the traditional and simplified forms of this word's own spelling. Mandarin's everyday word for "discover" is in fact written 发现/發現, so the 見-spelled form is comparatively rare or archaic in Mandarin, though it remains this vault's dedicated compound. Japanese はっけん is the extremely common, everyday word 発見する "to discover". Vietnamese phát kiến is a formal or literary Sino-Vietnamese word for "discovery," used in academic and scientific contexts (phát kiến khoa học).
