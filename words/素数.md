## Notes
- [素](../characters/素.md) "element, plain thing" + [数](../characters/数.md) "count, number" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (素's is [[要素]], 数's is [[計数]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: true` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: true`.

素数 names a prime number — literally "elemental number," one not divisible into smaller factors.

Mandarin sùshù, Japanese そすう (sosū), and Korean 소수 (sosu) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 素数 is itself a common attested word for "prime number" in all three. Cantonese sou3 sou2 (confirmed via web search — 数 shifts to its noun-sense sou2 here, distinct from the verb-sense sou3 elsewhere) is likewise standard. Vietnamese số nguyên tố is the standard Vietnamese term, reordered relative to the Chinese characters (số "number" + nguyên tố "element/prime factor"). No Dan'a'yo homophones.
