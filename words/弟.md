## Notes
- [弟 (char)](../characters/弟%20(char).md) "younger brother" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [弟 (char)](../characters/弟%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

弟 names a younger brother, and by extension a junior/disciple relationship ([[弟子]] "apprentice, disciple," literally "younger-brother child"). The character's own 象形 origin depicts cord wound in sequence around a shaft, marking order — the same "ordering" concept underlying its extension to sibling seniority.

Japanese てい (on'yomi; the native おとうと, otōto, is the everyday word instead), Korean 제, and Vietnamese đệ (the character's own field also lists đễ/dễ, both Nôm variants; đệ is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading, as in đệ tử "disciple") are all standard readings.
