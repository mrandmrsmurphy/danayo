## Notes
- [時](../characters/時%20(char).md) "when" + [節](../characters/節%20(char).md) "node, knuckle, joint, holiday" — no stand-in relationship applies (時's own stand-in is itself; 節's own is itself).

時節 means season, or the right time.

Mandarin shíjié, Cantonese si4 zit3, Japanese じせつ, Korean 시절, and Vietnamese thời tiết are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄐㄝㄊ unique across both words and characters).
