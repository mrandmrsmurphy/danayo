## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [湾](../characters/湾.md) "gulf, bay" — the stand-in for [湾](../characters/湾.md), which cannot appear independently

海湾 is a bay or gulf: a body of sea water partly enclosed by land. The bound character 湾 names the curve or inlet itself, and prefixing 海 "sea" fixes it as a sea inlet rather than a bend in a river. Place names show the pattern, as in 波斯湾 (the Persian Gulf) and 東京湾 (Tokyo Bay).

English distinguishes a bay from a gulf mostly by size, but none of the CJKV languages draws that line in the word itself, so Dan'a'yo 海湾 covers both, as the shared CJKV sense, with no divergence.
