## Notes
- [美 (char)](../characters/美%20(char).md) "beauty" + [術 (char)](../characters/術%20(char).md) "art" + [館](../characters/館.md) "hall"

美術館 is an art museum: a building where works of art are kept and shown. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin měishùguǎn as "art gallery; art museum" and Japanese びじゅつかん as "art gallery, art museum". Korean 미술관 is the same word. Cantonese mei5 seot6 gun2 matches.

It shows [美術](美術.md), fine art, and the one for history and nature is the [博物館](博物館.md). A commercial one is a [画廊](画廊.md).
