## Notes
- [飲 (char)](../characters/飲%20(char).md) "drink" + [料](../characters/料.md) "material"

飲料 is a beverage: any drink, hot or cold, alcoholic or not. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yǐnliào as "beverage; drink" and Japanese いんりょう as "drink; beverage". Korean 음료 and Cantonese jam2 liu6-2 match.

It is the class of [茶](茶.md), tea, [麦酒](麦酒.md), beer, [果汁](果汁.md), juice, and the rest. Drinking and eating together are [飲食](飲食.md).
