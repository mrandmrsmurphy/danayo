## Notes
- [外](../characters/外.md) "outside, maternal side" + [祖](../characters/祖.md) "ancestor, grandparent" + [母](../characters/母.md) "mother" — "maternal grandmother" (母方のお婆さん in descriptive Japanese; がいそぼ is the formal Sino-Japanese term). The colloquial Mandarin diminutive for this relation is 姥姥 (lǎolao), not built from these characters at all. `vietnamese: ngoại tổ mẫu` is a directly attested standard Vietnamese term with the same sense.
