## Notes
- [蛮](../characters/蛮.md) "barbarian" + [夷](../characters/夷.md) "barbarian"

蛮夷 is a historical, derogatory term for peoples regarded as outsiders. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 蠻夷 (alias here; Mandarin mányí, Cantonese maan4 ji4) as "non-Han ethnic groups living in modern-day southern and eastern China", marked historical and derogatory, and Vietnamese man di as "barbarian; savage; primitive". The Japanese ばんい and Korean 만이 fields are built from the readings of the two characters.
