## Notes
- [斯](../characters/斯.md) "this" + [文](../characters/文.md) "culture, refinement" — literally "this culture [of ours]." `characters/斯.md`'s own `stand_in` previously read the placeholder "名専字" (name-only character, used only in transliterations like 波斯 "Persia"); corrected to point to this real, non-transliteration compound instead.

斯文 traces to the Analects (9.5): 天之未喪斯文也 ("Heaven has not yet destroyed this culture [of ours]"), Confucius's own term for the civilizational tradition he saw himself transmitting. Living Mandarin narrowed this to describe a **person**: genteel, refined, courteous in manner — this vault's word for Rosenfelder's "gentle" in the sense of a refined temperament, distinct from [[温柔]]'s warmer, more affectionate "gentle."

**Japanese しぶん and Korean 사문 keep the older, narrower classical sense instead** — "this field of study; Confucian learning/teachings" specifically, not a general personality description. Cantonese si1 man4 is the compositional reading. Vietnamese tư văn is given as the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading, though it sits dangerously close to the unrelated, extremely common tư vấn ("to consult, give advice") — independent living attestation of tư văn specifically in this sense was not confirmed, so treat this reading with some caution. `kwin` is false: 斯 is `kwin: false`, 文 is `kwin: true`. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧ·ㄇㄨㄋ unique across both words and characters).
