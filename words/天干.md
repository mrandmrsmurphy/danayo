## Notes
- [天 (char)](../characters/天%20(char).md) "sky, heaven" + [干](../characters/干.md) "dry; stem" — the ten cyclic signs (甲乙丙丁戊己庚辛壬癸) associated with the five elements in their yin and yang aspects

Wiktionary: Chinese "heavenly stem: one of the ten terms used in East Asian cultures to represent...; the set of ten heavenly stems as a whole"; Japanese, uncommon, "heavenly stem"; Korean 천간 and Vietnamese Thiên Can are the Han forms, "the ten heavenly stems". The stems are 甲乙丙丁戊己庚辛壬癸, the counterpart of the twelve Earthly Branches ([[地支]]); together they form the sexagenary cycle. Japanese てんかん is also the reading of the unrelated 癲癇 "epilepsy".
