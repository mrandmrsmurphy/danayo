## Notes
- [形](../characters/形%20(char).md) "shape, form" + [貌](../characters/貌.md) "appearance" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [貌](../characters/貌.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (形's own stand-in is itself).

形貌 means appearance or countenance — the outward look or visage of a person or thing.

Mandarin xíngmào, Cantonese jing4 maau6, Japanese けいぼう, Korean 형모, and Vietnamese hình mạo are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `羅馬字` had a stray extra "u" (hengmyau→hengmya, matching the file's own correct 諺文/注音). Filled blank `japanese`/`korean`/`vietnamese`, added missing `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄝㄫㄇ⼘ unique across both words and characters).
