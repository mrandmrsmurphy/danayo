## Notes
- [促 (char)](../characters/促%20(char).md) "hurry; rush; hasten"

促 combines [[人]] ("person") and [[足]] ("foot") — a person's feet moving quickly, hence "to hurry, to press, to urge on." It heads compounds like [[督促]] ("to supervise and urge"), 催促 (cuīcù, "to press, urge repeatedly"), and 促進 (cùjìn, "to promote, accelerate").

Mandarin cù, Cantonese cuk1, and Japanese そく (soku, on'yomi) alongside native うなが(す) (unaga(su), "to urge, prompt") all preserve the sense. Korean 촉 (chok) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese has two recognized Hán Việt readings: xúc (the standard reading, as in xúc tiến 促進 "to promote, accelerate") and thúc (a recognized alternate, as in thôi thúc/thúc đẩy, "to urge on, push forward") — both genuinely attested rather than one being contamination; the character page's third stored candidate, xóc ("to shake, jolt"), is an unrelated native word and was not used here.
