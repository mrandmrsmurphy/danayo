## Notes
- [約](../characters/約.md) "agree, arrange" + [会 (char)](../characters/会%20(char).md) "to meet" — no stand-in relationship applies (約's own `stand_in` is [[約束]]; 会's own is itself).

約会 is the standard Mandarin word for a romantic date, or the social hangout/outing itself — the real Chinese compound this vault reaches for whenever a source dictionary glosses Japanese デート or Korean 데이트 (both otherwise pure English loanwords) back into Han characters. `kwin` is false per the AND-rule: `characters/約.md` is `kwin: true`, `characters/会 (char).md` is `kwin: false` → compound `false`.

Mandarin yuēhuì is real and standard. Cantonese joek3 wui6, Korean 약회 (yakhoe — real but more neutral/formal than the borrowed 데이트, closer to "an arranged meeting"), and Vietnamese ước hội are built compositionally from each character's own attested reading; Japanese やくかい is likewise the mechanical on'yomi reading (ヤク + カイ, no attested gemination the way [[密会]]'s みっかい takes one) — native Japanese in practice almost always says デート instead, the same way this word's own real-world source is Mandarin rather than Japanese. No homophones (注音 ⼘ㄎㄏ⼔ unique across both words and characters).
