## Notes
- [工](../characters/工.md) "work; craft; industry; artisan" + [廠](../characters/廠.md) "workshop; factory shed; large covered work space" — stand-in for [廠](../characters/廠.md), which cannot appear independently

工廠 is "factory; manufacturing plant" — 工 (work, craft, production) + 廠 (a large covered building for industrial work, a workshop or factory shed). The 廠 character specifically denotes a physical industrial space rather than industry as an abstraction, giving 工廠 the concrete sense of a place where manufacturing happens. This is the standard Mandarin and Cantonese term for factory.

Mandarin 工廠 (gōngchǎng) is the mainland Chinese standard: 汽車工廠 (car factory), 電子工廠 (electronics plant). Cantonese 工廠 (gung1 cong2) is standard in Hong Kong — 工廠區 (industrial estate). Japanese uses 工場 (こうじょう, kōjō or こうば, kōba) rather than 工廠 — 廠 is rare in everyday Japanese vocabulary (it survives mainly in historical terms such as 海軍工廠, "naval arsenal," read こうしょう; from memory, not re-checked), so Japanese defaults to 場 (place/ground). Korean 공장 (gongjang) = 工場 (工 + 場) for the same reason — 廠 does not feature in Korean, and Korean inherited the Japanese/literary form 工場. Vietnamese công xưởng (công = 工, xưởng = 廠 in Sino-Vietnamese) is the standard factory term.

The divergence between 工廠 (Chinese/Dan'a'yo standard, with 廠 = factory shed) and 工場 (Japanese/Korean standard, with 場 = place/ground) reflects different historical channels for this vocabulary within the Sinosphere. Dan'a'yo follows the Mandarin/Cantonese convention with 工廠 as the primary entry. The Japanese 工場 was noted as a possible alternative (こうじょう, kōjō, the cognate form), but 工廠 is retained as the entry headword since it more precisely encodes the "factory building" sense and aligns with the Chinese mainstream.

The Work page lists 工廠 as "factory, manufacturing plant" and points to its Semantic Range Notes for the contrast with the separate word [工場](工場.md); both are kept as independent entries with different pronunciations, and 工廠 is not an alias of 工場. `kwin` is true because 工 and 廠 are both true on their character pages (공, 창). The compound examples (汽車工廠, 電子工廠, 工廠區) are the page's existing account and have not been re-checked against a source.
