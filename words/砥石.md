## Notes
- [砥](../characters/砥.md) "whetstone" + [石 (char)](../characters/石%20(char).md) "rock"

砥石 is a whetstone, a stone for sharpening blades. English Wiktionary has an entry only for Japanese 砥石 (といし), glossed "whetstone; grindstone". The Mandarin, Cantonese, Korean 지석 and Vietnamese chỉ thạch fields are built from the readings of the two characters.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [砥](../characters/砥.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
