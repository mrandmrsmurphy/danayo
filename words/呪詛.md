## Notes
- [呪](../characters/呪.md) "curse, spell" + [詛](../characters/詛.md) "curse, imprecation" — this word is the stand-in for [詛](../characters/詛.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo (呪's own stand-in is [[呪文]]).

呪詛 means a curse or spell — an imprecation calling misfortune upon someone.

Japanese じゅそ (using JU rather than the alternate SHU/SHUU) and Korean 주저 (compositional — coincidentally identical to the unrelated everyday word 주저 "hesitation," not a bug) are both standard readings. Filled blank `vietnamese: chú trù` (compositional, from each character's own reading; not independently verified as a fixed idiom, but both morphemes individually mean "curse"). Removed the redundant `品詞` field. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄨㄛㄐㄛ unique across both words and characters).
