## Notes
- [窪](../characters/窪.md) "hollow, pit, depression" + [地 (char)](../characters/地%20(char).md) "land" — 窪's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 地's own is [[地]] itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 窪 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 地 is individually `kwin: false`.

**Fixed a real bug**: `japanese` had ぼち, the reading for the unrelated word 墓地 ("cemetery") — evidently a copy-paste contamination from an alphabetically adjacent entry on `characters/地 (char).md`'s own Words list. Corrected to わち (wachi), the genuine compositional reading (窪's own WA + 地's own CHI).

窪地 names a hollow, depression, or low-lying pit in the terrain — a natural basin or sunken area of land, as opposed to a raised or level surface.

Mandarin wādì, Cantonese waa1 dei6, Japanese わち (wachi), and Korean 와지 (waji) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese oa địa is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form.
