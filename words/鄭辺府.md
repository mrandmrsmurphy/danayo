## Notes
- [鄭](../characters/鄭.md) "Zheng (surname; place name)" + [辺](../characters/辺.md) "side, edge, border" + [府](../characters/府.md) "government, prefecture" — no stand-in relationship applies (鄭's own is [[鄭国]]; 辺's own is [[辺界]]; 府's own is [[政府]]). The name is normally written 奠邊府; 奠 is treated here as an alias of 鄭 (the vault records 奠 as the phonetic component of 鄭), and 辺 is the parent form of 邊, so the filename uses the parent forms 鄭辺府 and 奠邊府, 奠辺府 and 鄭邊府 are kept as aliases.

鄭辺府 (Dien Bien Phu) is a town and district in the Mường Thanh valley of northwestern Vietnam, close to the Laotian border, and the provincial seat of Điện Biên. It is best known for the battle of March to May 1954, in which Viet Minh ([[越盟]]) forces, led in the field by 武元甲 (Võ Nguyên Giáp), besieged and defeated the French garrison, which effectively ended French rule in Indochina and set the stage for the Geneva Conference (from memory, not re-checked). The characters are usually read as "the prefecture that settles the frontier" (奠 "settle, establish" + 邊 "frontier" + 府 "prefecture") (from memory, not re-checked).

The Dan'a'yo sound (羅馬字, 諺文, 注音) comes from the character pages, so 奠 sounds like 鄭 here: jeng-ben-fu. The `mandarin`, `cantonese`, `korean`, `japanese` and `vietnamese` fields hold the Sino-Xenic readings of the real 奠邊府 spelling instead, which differ from 鄭's own (zhèng, 정, trịnh): Mandarin Diànbiānfǔ, Cantonese din6 bin1 fu2, Korean 전변부 (奠 전 + 邊 변 + 府 부) and Vietnamese Điện Biên Phủ (奠 điện, per hvdic.thivien.net). Japanese てんへんふ is the compositional on'yomi reading, derived and not found in a dictionary; in practice Japanese writes the name ディエンビエンフー (from memory, not re-checked).

Attestation, checked 2026-10-11: English Wiktionary has 奠邊府 as a Chinese proper noun (a city in Vietnam, Mandarin Diànbiānfǔ, Cantonese din6 bin1 fu2) and a Vietnamese Han-form entry for Điện Biên Phủ; Chinese Wiktionary has a stub with Mandarin diànbiānfǔ. Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese Wiktionaries have no entry for it. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄫㄅㄝㄋㄈㄨ unique across both words and characters).
