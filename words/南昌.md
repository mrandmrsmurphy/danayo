## Notes
- [南](../characters/南.md) "south" + [昌](../characters/昌.md) "prosperous" — "southern prosperity". No stand-in relationship applies (南's is [[南方]]; 昌's is [[昌盛]]).

南昌 (Nanchang) is the capital of Jiangxi province in southeastern China. Per Wiktionary the name is attested on the Liye Qin slips and in the Book of Han as the name of a county and the seat of Yuzhang Commandery (est. 202 BC). English Wiktionary also lists Nanchang County (in the same city) and a village of the same name in Hubei, and the archaic synonym 豫章.

Japanese なんしょう is the reading given on Japanese Wikipedia (南昌市, with ナンチャン as the common form); English Wiktionary has no Japanese entry for it, and the reading is not Wiktionary-attested (from memory, not re-checked as a Sino-Japanese reading of 昌). Korean 남창 is the Sino-Korean reading (Wiktionary Korean 남창); the Korean field here holds the North Korean reading derived from the character pages, 남창, and the usual South Korean name today is 난창 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Nam Xương is attested on Vietnamese Wikipedia (Hán-Việt Nam Xương thị). Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese and Korean entries. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄚㄇㄑ⺢ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
