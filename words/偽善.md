## Etymology
- [偽 (char)](../characters/偽%20(char).md) "false, artificial" + [善 (char)](../characters/善%20(char).md) "good"

## Notes
"Hypocrisy, hypocritical" — false virtue, feigning goodness to conceal one's true nature. `vietnamese: ngụy thiện` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese (confirmed via web search), closely related to the broader everyday term đạo đức giả ("moral hypocrisy"). Trimmed the redundant secondary Mandarin tonal variant from `mandarin` (was "wěishàn, wèishàn," now the standard reading alone). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 워션 diverges from Sino-Korean 위선 in the first syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ⼔ㄧㄙ⼶ㄋ unique among both words and characters).
