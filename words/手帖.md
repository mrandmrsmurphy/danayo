## Notes
- [手](../characters/手%20(char).md) "hand" + [帖](../characters/帖%20(char).md) "invitation, card" — no stand-in relationship applies (手's own stand-in is itself; 帖's own is itself).

手帖 means handwritten letter or notebook.

Mandarin shǒutiě, Korean 수첩, and Vietnamese thủ thiếp (compositional) are standard. Fixed real bugs: `諺文`/`羅馬字` (슈텁/syuteb→슈톱/syutob, matching the already-correct 注音) and `cantonese` (tip1→tip3, matching 帖's own citation) both didn't match 帖's own frontmatter. Modernized garbled/archaic `japanese` (ててふ→てちょう, the CHOU on-reading). Removed `手帳` from `aliases` — that is a distinct Japanese synonym built on a different character (帳, not 帖), not an orthographic variant of this word. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼜ㄊㄛㄆ unique across both words and characters).
