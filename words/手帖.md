## Notes
- [手](../characters/手%20(char).md) "hand" + [帖](../characters/帖%20(char).md) "invitation, card" — no stand-in relationship applies (手's own stand-in is itself; 帖's own is itself).

手帖 means handwritten letter or notebook.

Mandarin shǒutiě, Korean 수첩, and Vietnamese thủ thiếp (compositional) are standard.
