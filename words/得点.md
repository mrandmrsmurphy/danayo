## Notes
- [得](../characters/得.md) "get" + [点](../characters/点%20(char).md) "points" — no stand-in relationship applies (得's own stand-in is [[獲得]]; 点's own is itself).

得点 means score — points obtained in a game, test, or competition.

Mandarin dédiǎn, Cantonese dak1 dim2, Japanese とくてん, Korean 득점, and Vietnamese đắc điểm are all standard, fully compositional readings. 得's syllable here (dug/둑/ㄉㄨㄎ) diverges from its own standalone citation (tǝg/특/ㄊㄜㄎ, seen in [[獲得]]/[[取得]]/[[自得]]) — a genuine sense-dependent split, cross-verified by the chengyu [[種瓜得瓜]] which uses the same dug form. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄉㄨㄎㄉㄝㄇ/dugdem/둑덤) with the unrelated [[特点]] ("characteristic, trait") — likely a deliberate homophone construction, since 特's own citation (dǝg/득) independently converges on the same dug form here too.
