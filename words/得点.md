## Notes
- [得](../characters/得.md) "get" + [点](../characters/点%20(char).md) "points" — no stand-in relationship applies (得's own stand-in is [[獲得]]; 点's own is itself).

得点 means score — points obtained in a game, test, or competition.

Mandarin dédiǎn, Cantonese dak1 dim2, Japanese とくてん, Korean 득점, and Vietnamese đắc điểm are all standard, fully compositional readings.
