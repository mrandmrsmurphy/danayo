## Notes
- [溺](../characters/溺%20(char).md) "drown, indulge" + [愛](../characters/愛%20(char).md) "love" — "drown in love," to dote. Their own `stand_in` values: 溺's is itself; 愛's is itself; none points here, so 溺愛 is an independent compound.

溺愛 is to dote: to love someone, usually a child, so much that one indulges them and spoils them. Mandarin nì'ài, Cantonese nik6 oi3 and Japanese できあい are attested, and Korean 익애 is the same compound. Compare [[熱愛]] (to love passionately) and [[愛護]] (to cherish).
