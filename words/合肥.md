## Notes
- [合](../characters/合%20(char).md) "join, combine" + [肥](../characters/肥.md) "fertile, plump" — the name derives from the confluence of the Fei River (肥水) with other waterways. No stand-in relationship applies (合 is its own stand-in; 肥's own is [[肥満]]).

合肥 (Hefei) is the capital of Anhui province, China.

Japanese ごうひ, Korean 합비, and Vietnamese Hợp Phì are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄆㄅㄨㄧ unique across both words and characters).
