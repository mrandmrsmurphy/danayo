## Notes
- [温](../characters/温.md) "warm" + [柔 (char)](../characters/柔%20(char).md) "tender, soft" — literally "warm and tender." No stand-in relationship applies (温's own `stand_in` is [[温暖]]; 柔's own is itself).

温柔 means gentle, loving, sweet — a warm, tender, affectionate temperament, of a person (describing character, often especially in a romantic or nurturing register) or, more literally, of an object (soft and warm to the touch). This vault's primary word for Rosenfelder's "gentle."

Mandarin wēnróu, Cantonese wan1 jau4, Japanese おんじゅう, Korean 온유, and Vietnamese ôn nhu are all standard, fully compositional readings — 온유 in particular is a familiar word from the Korean Beatitudes translation (온유한 자는 복이 있나니, "blessed are the meek," Matthew 5:5). `kwin` is false: 温 is `kwin: false`, 柔 is `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄛㄆㄋ⼜ unique across both words and characters).
