## Notes
- [点 (char)](../characters/点%20(char).md) "dot; nod" + [頭 (char)](../characters/頭%20(char).md) "head"

点頭 is to nod: to bend the head down and up, to agree or to greet. Wiktionary glosses Japanese てんとう as "to nod one's head; to consent, to agree". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 점두 is the reading of the characters.

The nod is a gesture of the [頭](頭.md), head, and the deeper bow to the ground is the [叩頭](叩頭.md).
