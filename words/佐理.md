## Notes
- [佐](../characters/佐.md) "to assist" + [理](../characters/理.md) "to manage, govern" — "to assist in managing/administration" (政務をたすけ治める; appears in classical texts like the Records of the Three Kingdoms). All fields confirmed via web search.

**Real content bugs found and fixed**: `korean` and `aliases` had both been contaminated with content from the unrelated word [[体現]] "embody, reflect" (korean read "체현, 좌리" — mixing 体現's own reading with this word's correct 좌리; aliases wrongly listed 體現, the traditional form of 体現) — evidently a copy-paste error between the two alphabetically-adjacent pages. Removed the contamination; filled the blank `cantonese: zo3 lei5` and `japanese: さり` (compositional, confirmed as a real reading). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 자리 diverges from Sino-Korean 좌리 in the first syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄌㄧ unique among both words and characters).
