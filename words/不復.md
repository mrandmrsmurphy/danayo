## Notes
- [不 (char)](../characters/不%20(char).md) "not" + [復](../characters/復.md) "again; to return"

不復 is "no more; no longer," a literary negative of continuation: the state described does not come back or continue. According to Wiktionary, Chinese 不復 (bùfù, Cantonese bat1 fau6 or bat1 fuk6) is "no more; no longer." The literary 復 here has the sense "again," as in 一去不復返 "gone and never to return," and Dan'a'yo keeps that classical heritage. Wiktionary has no Japanese, Korean or Vietnamese section, so `japanese` ふふく, `korean` 불복 and `vietnamese` bất phục are compositional readings of the two characters, not attested words (the Korean form is also the reading of an unrelated word, from memory, not re-checked). It is used in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [China](../lookup/HSK/China.md), in 我 不復 恐怖 "I no longer fear," standing in for the Chinese 不再. Compare [不](不.md) "not" and [未](未.md) "not yet."
