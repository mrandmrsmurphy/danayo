## Notes
- "Return, retreat, regress" — and specifically "regression" in its modern statistical/mathematical sense (回帰分析 "regression analysis"). `vietnamese: hồi quy` is a directly attested standard Vietnamese term, especially well-known in the mathematical sense (hồi quy tuyến tính, "linear regression"). Fixed `characters` to use the `回 (char)` filename form and filled blank `japanese: かいき`. **Real gap found and fixed on [[帰]]'s own page**: its `vietnamese` field was blank — added quy, the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading (帰 being the Japanese shinjitai of 歸/归). `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 회귀 matches Sino-Korean 회귀 exactly.
