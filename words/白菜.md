## Notes
- [白 (char)](../characters/白%20(char).md) "white" + [菜](../characters/菜.md) "vegetable" — literally "white vegetable," describing the pale, whitish stalks of Chinese/napa cabbage.

Mandarin báicài, Cantonese baak6 coi3, and Japanese はくさい (hakusai) are all straightforward, everyday Sino compounds — 白菜 is the ordinary word for napa cabbage in these languages. Korean breaks the pattern: the everyday word is 배추 (baechu, of disputed but likely non-Sinitic or heavily nativized origin), central to Korean cuisine as the base vegetable for kimchi, rather than the compositional Sino form 백채. Vietnamese bạch thái is compositional; the more common everyday term is the native/Sino-hybrid cải thảo.
