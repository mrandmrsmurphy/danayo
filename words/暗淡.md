## Notes
- [暗](../characters/暗%20(char).md) "dark, dim" + [淡](../characters/淡.md) "pale, light" — "dark and faint," dim. Their own `stand_in` values: 暗's is itself; 淡's is [[清淡]]; none points here, so 暗淡 is an independent compound.

暗淡 is dim: of light, faint and dull, and figuratively gloomy or without prospects. Mandarin àndàn and Cantonese am3 daam6 are the ordinary words, and Vietnamese ảm đạm does too. Japanese says 薄暗い and Korean 어둑하다, so those two fields hold the character readings. It is the dim light between [[暗]] (dark) and [[明]] (bright).
