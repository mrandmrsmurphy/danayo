## Notes
- [一](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one; whole" + [貫](../characters/貫.md) "pierce; string through" — "one thread running through"

一貫 is consistency, being the same all the way through. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 一貫 (alias 一贯; Mandarin yīguàn, Cantonese jat1 gun3) as "consistent" and "consistently; unfailingly; from first to last", and Japanese いっかん as "integration, consistency with regard to policy, manner, approach, etc." and "be consistent, be the same throughout". Korean 일관 and Vietnamese nhất quán are built from the readings of the two characters.

The phrase 吾道一以貫之, "my way is threaded by a single thing", is attributed to Confucius in the Analects. The word shares 貫 with [貫通](貫通.md), the stand-in that legitimizes the bound character 貫.
