## Notes
- [五 (char)](../characters/五%20(char).md) "five" + [倫](../characters/倫.md) "human relationship, moral order" — the Five Cardinal Relationships of Confucian ethics: ruler–subject, father–son, husband–wife, elder–younger sibling, and friend–friend, each carrying its own expected virtue (loyalty, filial piety, distinction of roles, proper order, and trust respectively).

This vault already documents the closely related [[三綱]] ("Three Bonds"), a narrower subset of the same relational framework emphasizing hierarchy and obedience specifically within three of these five pairs — 五倫 is the fuller, more symmetrical enumeration, including the two relationships (siblings, friends) that don't carry the same strict superior/subordinate structure. Vietnamese ngũ luân directly parallels this vault's own [[三綱]]/tam cương pairing in Vietnamese Confucian scholarship. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 오룬 diverges from Sino-Korean 오륜 in the second syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄛㄌㄨㄋ unique among both words and characters).
