## Notes
- [好 (char)](../characters/好%20(char).md) "good; like" + [奇](../characters/奇.md) "strange; odd" + [心 (char)](../characters/心%20(char).md) "heart"

好奇心 is curiosity: a liking for what is strange, and the wish to know more. It is literally "liking-the-strange heart". Wiktionary glosses Mandarin hàoqíxīn and Japanese こうきしん as "curiosity". Cantonese hou3 kei4 sam1 and Korean 호기심 match.

It is the motive behind [研究](研究.md), research, and [学習](学習.md), learning. It is a quality of mind, so it is also at home on [Mind](../lexipedia/Mind.md).
