## Notes
- [岐 (char)](../characters/岐%20(char).md) "fork (in road)" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [岐 (char)](../characters/岐%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

岐 names a fork or branching point in a road, and by extension divergence generally (岐路 "crossroads, fork in the path," often figurative — a decisive life choice). It originated as the place name 岐山 (Mount Qi) and is registered as an alternate form of 歧 (already listed on 岐's own `aliases` field), the traditional form under which it is recorded in Classical Chinese usage rankings.

Japanese き, Korean 기, and Vietnamese kì are all standard readings. No homophones among other words (注音 ㄍㄝ; several unrelated characters share it, including [[伎]], [[示]], and [[芥]], but none of their own stand-in words collide with 岐's).
