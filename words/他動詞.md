## Notes
- [他 (char)](../characters/他%20(char).md) "another; other" + [動 (char)](../characters/動%20(char).md) "move, act" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "word" — "transitive verb," a modern East Asian grammatical-terminology compound. mandarin tādòngcí, japanese たどうし, and korean 타동사 are all standard live grammar terminology, directly compositional and in near-complete cross-linguistic agreement. Vietnamese prefers the real established grammar term ngoại động từ over a forced compositional reading. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 트동사 diverges from Sino-Korean 타동사 via the AND-rule, since 他's own field independently diverges (despite 動 and 詞 both individually being `kwin: true`).
