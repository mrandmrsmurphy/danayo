## Notes
- [他 (char)](../characters/他%20(char).md) "another; other" + [動 (char)](../characters/動%20(char).md) "move, act" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "word" — "transitive verb," a modern East Asian grammatical-terminology compound. mandarin tādòngcí, japanese たどうし, and korean 타동사 are all standard live grammar terminology, directly compositional and in near-complete cross-linguistic agreement. Vietnamese prefers the real established grammar term ngoại động từ over a forced compositional reading.
