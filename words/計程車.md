## Notes
- [計](../characters/計.md) "count, meter" + [程](../characters/程.md) "distance" + [車](../characters/車%20(char).md) "vehicle" — "meter-distance vehicle," a taxi. Their own `stand_in` values: 計's is [[計画]]; 程's is [[程度]]; 車's is itself; none points here, so 計程車 is an independent compound.

計程車 is a taxi: a car for hire that charges by the distance on its meter. Mandarin jìchéngchē and Cantonese gai3 cing4 ce1 are the ordinary Chinese words in Taiwan (Mainland China says 出租车, and Hong Kong Cantonese usually 的士). Japanese says タクシー and Korean 택시, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[車]] (car) and [[貨車]] (truck).
