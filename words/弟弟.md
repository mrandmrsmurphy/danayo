## Notes
- [弟 (char)](../characters/弟%20(char).md) "younger brother" + [弟 (char)](../characters/弟%20(char).md) "younger brother" (reduplicated) — 弟's own `stand_in` is bare 弟, not this word.

The reduplicated Dan'a'yo form is deliberately cuter and more diminutive-sounding than the plain Mandarin 弟弟, which is simply the normal, neutral word for "younger brother." Mandarin dìdi, Cantonese dai6-4 dai6-2, Japanese おとうと, and Vietnamese em trai are the standard words. The `korean` field is blank: there is no attested reduplicated Sino-Korean reading (제제), and Korean instead uses 남동생, 동생 or 아우, which are native or hybrid terms and not readings of 弟弟.
