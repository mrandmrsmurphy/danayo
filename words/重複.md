## Notes
- [重 (char)](../characters/重%20(char).md) "heavy; repeated" + [複](../characters/複.md) "repeat"
- This word is the viable form of [複](../characters/複.md): the character's meaning (to repeat, duplicate) is identical, but 複 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 重複 is its `stand_in` compound.

重 here is read in its "layer upon layer, again" sense (the 重 of 重ねる, to pile up), not "heavy," and 複 is "double, duplicate" (compare [複数](複数.md) "plural," [複写](複写.md) "to copy," [複雑](複雑.md) "complicated"). 重複 is therefore to occur twice or more, to overlap, to be duplicated, and the same word serves as a verb ("to repeat, overlap"), a noun ("duplication") and, with its common modern attribute use, an adjective ("redundant, repetitive"). It is a common technical and administrative word: duplicate entries in a database, overlapping appointments, repeated words in an essay, double registration.

The word is shared across the sphere: Mandarin chóngfù (the alias 重复 is the simplified spelling), Cantonese cung4 fuk1, Korean 중복. In Japanese the standard reading is chōfuku (ちょうふく), and the reading jūfuku (じゅうふく), which treats 重 with its other common reading, is also widespread in speech and is accepted alongside it. Dan'a'yo keeps the shared sense, "repeat, duplicate," with the adjectival use as a natural extension, placed under [Events](../lexipedia/Events.md).
