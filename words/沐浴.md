## Notes
- [沐](../characters/沐.md) "bathe, cleanse, wash" + [浴](../characters/浴.md) "bathe" — `#cranberry`: both 沐's own `stand_in` and 浴's own `stand_in` point back to this exact compound (transitivity A=B=AB confirmed), so 沐浴 is the shared stand_in legitimizing both bound characters at once.

沐浴 means to bathe, cleanse.

Mandarin mùyù, Japanese もくよく, Korean 목욕, and Vietnamese mộc dục are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 沐's and 浴's own stored citations exactly), and also the real, standard everyday words for "bathe" in Mandarin, Japanese, and Korean.
