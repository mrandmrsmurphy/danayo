## Notes
- [開](../characters/開%20(char).md) "open" + [関](../characters/関%20(char).md) "gate, switch" — "open and close," a switch. Their own `stand_in` values: 開's is itself; 関's is itself; none points here, so 開関 is an independent compound.

開関 is a switch: the device that opens or closes an electric circuit. Mandarin kāiguān (開關) and Cantonese hoi1 gwaan1 are the ordinary words. Japanese says スイッチ and Korean 스위치, so those two fields hold the character readings. Compare [[回路]] (circuit) and [[電気]] (electricity).
