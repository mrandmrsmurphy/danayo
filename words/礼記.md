## Notes
- [礼 (char)](../characters/礼%20(char).md) "ritual, manners" + [記](../characters/記.md) "record, document" — literally "the record of rites," one of the Five Classics (五経) of the Confucian canon.

Mandarin Lǐjì, Cantonese lai5 gei3, Japanese らいき (raiki, using 礼's less-common RAI on'yomi rather than the more everyday REI), and Vietnamese Kinh Lễ ("Ritual Classic," using the native "Kinh" prefix Vietnamese applies to the whole Five/Six Classics tradition rather than a direct Sino-Vietnamese compound) are all standard, attested names for this text across the sphere. **Found and fixed a real bug**: `korean` was stored as 예기, the South-Korean 두음법칙-softened form — inconsistent with this vault's standing rule that the `korean` field always holds the North Korean/문화어 reading, and inconsistent with how every other 礼-compound in this corpus handles the same character (礼物 → 례물, 洗礼 → 세례). Corrected to 례기. `kwin` is therefore true, not false: Dan'a'yo 레기 coincides exactly with the corrected Sino-Korean 례기, since both 礼 and 記 independently carry matching readings on their own character pages.

It's a complete classical text — see [[礼記 (book)]] for the working translation. One of the [[五経]] (Five Classics), which together with the Four Books fall under [[四書五経]].
