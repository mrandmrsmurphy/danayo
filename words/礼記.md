## Notes
- [礼 (char)](../characters/礼%20(char).md) "ritual, manners" + [記](../characters/記.md) "record, document" — literally "the record of rites," one of the Five Classics (五経) of the Confucian canon.

Mandarin Lǐjì, Cantonese lai5 gei3, Japanese らいき (raiki, using 礼's less-common RAI on'yomi rather than the more everyday REI), and Vietnamese Kinh Lễ ("Ritual Classic," using the native "Kinh" prefix Vietnamese applies to the whole Five/Six Classics tradition rather than a direct Sino-Vietnamese compound) are all standard, attested names for this text across the sphere. Korean 례기 is the North Korean reading, consistent with 礼物 → 례물 and 洗礼 → 세례.

It's a complete classical text — see [[礼記 (book)]] for the working translation. One of the [[五経]] (Five Classics), which together with the Four Books fall under [[四書五経]].
