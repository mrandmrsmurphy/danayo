## Notes
- [放](../characters/放.md) "release" + [火](../characters/火%20(char).md) "fire" — no stand-in relationship applies (放's own stand-in is [[釈放]]; 火's own is itself).

放火 means to set on fire, or arson.

Mandarin fànghuǒ, Cantonese fong3 fo2, Japanese ほうか, Korean 방화, and Vietnamese phóng hỏa are all standard, fully compositional readings. Modernized archaic kana はうくわ→ほうか. **A very important homophone**: [[防火]] "fire prevention; fireproofing" shares this exact reading (both 注音 ㄅㄚㄫㄏ⺢) despite being near-opposite meanings — the classic 放火/防火 confusion risk in Japanese fire-safety signage.
