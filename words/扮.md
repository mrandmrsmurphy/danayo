## Notes
- [扮](../characters/扮%20(char).md) "dress up" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [扮](../characters/扮%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

扮 means to dress up (as in a costume or role).

Mandarin bàn, Cantonese baan6, Japanese ふん, Korean 분, and Vietnamese phẫn are all standard, fully compositional readings.
