## Notes
- [軽 (char)](../characters/軽%20(char).md) "light" + [素](../characters/素.md) "element" — literally "light element," this vault's own coinage for hydrogen. Also abbreviated to bare 軽 in periodic-table chemical formulae.

A neologism was necessary because the source languages split sharply into two independent naming traditions for hydrogen, neither of which is compositionally "軽素": Mandarin/Cantonese use the novel character 氫/氢 for "light-gas" (qīngqì, hing1 hei3 — the `mandarin`/`cantonese` fields here hold that real term, not a reading of 軽素's own characters), and the older Vietnamese term khinh khí follows the same "light gas" logic (氫氣). Japanese and Korean instead use 水素 ("water element," すいそ/수소), reflecting hydrogen's role in water rather than its lightness — the `korean` field here holds that real term, again not a reading of 軽素's own characters. Dan'a'yo splits the difference: 軽 ("light") supplies the Mandarin/Cantonese/Vietnamese tradition's core idea, while the X素 template supplies the Japanese/Korean tradition's structural pattern — see [[Periodic Table]] for the full element-naming system this follows.
