## Notes
- [衣](../characters/衣.md) "clothes" + [装 (char)](../characters/装%20(char).md) "apparel"

衣装 is costume: clothing worn for a performance or ceremony. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yīshang as "clothes; clothing" and Japanese いしょう as "clothing". Korean 의상 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese ji1 soeng4 matches.

It is worn by an [俳優](俳優.md), actor, and ordinary clothes are covered under [Clothing](../lexipedia/Clothing.md). A mask is a [仮面](仮面.md).
