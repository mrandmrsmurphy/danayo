## Notes
- [分 (char)](../characters/分%20(char).md) "divide" + [解 (char)](../characters/解%20(char).md) "loosen; solve"

分解 is to decompose or break down: to separate a whole into its parts. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin fēnjiě as "to decompose; to break down; to dissolve", and Japanese ぶんかい as "decomposition, disassembly; to decompose, to disassemble", with technical senses in chemistry and mathematics. Vietnamese phân giải is the same word, and Korean 분해 and Cantonese fan1 gaai2 match.

It is the opposite of [構成](構成.md), and is a kind of [分離](分離.md), separation. Its result is a set of [成分](成分.md).
