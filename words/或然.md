## Notes
- [或](../characters/或%20(char).md) "or; perhaps" + [然](../characters/然%20(char).md) "so" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both are already legitimized elsewhere: 或 is its own stand-in, 然 is its own stand-in); 或然 is an independent compound. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: `characters/或 (char).md` is `kwin: true`, `characters/然 (char).md` is `kwin: false` → compound `false`.

或然 means probable, contingent — true of some possible cases but not required by necessity. It is the base of the real Mandarin mathematical/philosophical terms 或然率 (huòránlǜ, "probability," the statistical ratio) and 或然性 (huòránxìng, "probability, contingency," the abstract logical property), and pairs naturally with [[必然]] ("necessary, inevitable") the way classical Chinese logic translations contrast 必然 against 或然 — what must be the case versus what merely may be. Cantonese waak6 jin4, Korean 혹연, and Vietnamese hoặc nhiên are the straightforward compositional readings, each constituent's own stored reading concatenated; Japanese わくぜん is likewise compositional and comparatively rare next to the far more common [[蓋然]]/蓋然性.
