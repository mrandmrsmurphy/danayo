## Notes
- [疎](../characters/疎.md) "loose, neglect" + [忽](../characters/忽.md) "sudden" — 疎's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 忽's own is [[忽然]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply.
- 疎忽 is the stand-in word for [疎](../characters/疎.md) ("loose, neglect"), which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo.

Mandarin shūhū, Cantonese so1 fat1, Japanese そこつ (SO+KOTSU), and Korean 소홀 were already correct and fully compositional. Filled the entirely missing `vietnamese` field with sơ hốt (疎's own sơ + 忽's own hốt). No homophones (注音 ㄙㄜㄏㄛㄊ unique across both words and characters).
