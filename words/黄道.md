## Notes
- [黄 (char)](../characters/黄%20(char).md) "yellow" + [道 (char)](../characters/道%20(char).md) "way; path"

黄道 is the ecliptic: the yearly path of the sun across the stars, along which the moon and planets also move. Wiktionary glosses Japanese こうどう as "ecliptic". The Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the characters, and Korean 황도 matches. Japanese also uses 黄道 for an auspicious day.

The twelve signs of the zodiac, the [十二宮](十二宮.md), lie along it. The sun's turning points on it are the [至点](至点.md), solstices, and its crossings of the equator are the [分点](分点.md), equinoxes.
