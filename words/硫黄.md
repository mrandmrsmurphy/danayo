## Notes
- [硫](../characters/硫.md) "sulfur" + [黄 (char)](../characters/黄%20(char).md) "yellow" — stand-in for [硫](../characters/硫.md), which cannot appear independently.

Mandarin liúhuáng, Cantonese lau4 wong4, and Korean 류황 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 硫's and 黄's own stored citations exactly. Japanese いおう (iō) is the real standard Japanese word for sulfur, not a compositional reading.

- This is the first element on the [Periodic Table](../lexipedia/Periodic%20Table.md) not to feature the character element (素).
