## Notes
- [[硫]] "sulfur" + [黄 (char)](../characters/黄%20(char).md) "yellow" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 硫 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

Mandarin liúhuáng, Cantonese lau4 wong4, and Japanese いおう (iō) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 硫's and 黄's own stored citations exactly. **Found and fixed two real bugs**: `japanese` was stored as いわう — a typo of いおう, the real standard Japanese word for sulfur (easily confused characters, わ vs お). `korean` was stored as 유황, the South-Korean 두음법칙-softened form — inconsistent with this vault's standing rule that `korean` always holds the North Korean/문화어 reading, and directly contradicted by Dan'a'yo's own stored 諺文 (류황), which is correctly built from 硫's real North-Korean citation (류). Corrected to 류황.

`kwin` is therefore true, not false: both 硫 and 黄 are individually kwin-true, and with the Korean field corrected, Dan'a'yo 류황 coincides exactly with the corrected Sino-Korean 류황. No homophones (注音 ㄌ⼜ㄏ⺢ㄫ unique among both words and characters).

- This is the first element on the [Periodic Table](../lexipedia/Periodic%20Table.md) not to feature the character element (素).
