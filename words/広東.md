## Notes
- [広 (char)](../characters/広.md) "wide, broad" (stand-in: itself) + [東](../words/東.md) "east" (stand-in: itself) — Guangdong, the southeastern Chinese province; by extension, Cantonese (広東語).

広東 names Guangdong province, invoked by `文法 - 02音韻論`'s 広東語 ("Cantonese"). Mandarin guǎngdōng, Korean 광동, and Vietnamese quảng đông are all real, standard readings of this place name. Cantonese gwong2 dung1 is the name's own home-language reading. Japanese uses a borrowed reading かんとん (Kanton) for this specific place name rather than a direct on'yomi composition, flagged here as a genuine divergence. `kwin` is true: both 広 and 東 are individually true.

No syllable-boundary dot is needed: the concatenated string `ㄍ⺢ㄫㄉㄛㄫ` admits only one legal partition.
