## Notes
- [洪](../characters/洪.md) "deluge, flood" + [水](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 洪 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (水's own stand-in is itself, so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character). Synonymous with [[大水]].

洪水 means flood, deluge.

Mandarin hóngshuǐ, Cantonese hung4 seoi2, Japanese こうずい (with rendaku voicing すい→ずい), Korean 홍수, and Vietnamese hồng thuỷ are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 洪's and 水's own stored citations exactly), and also the real, standard everyday words for "flood" in each language. `kwin: true` because 諺文 홍수 matches Korean 홍수 exactly. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄛㄫㄙㄨ unique across both words and characters).
