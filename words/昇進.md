## Notes
- [昇](../characters/昇.md) "rise, ascend" + [進](../characters/進.md) "advance" — promotion.

昇進 is promotion: advancement to a higher position or rank in an organization. It is "rise-advance." Japanese しょうしん is a noun and する-verb (課長に昇進する, "to be promoted to section manager"); Korean 승진 and Vietnamese thăng tiến are the same compound. Wiktionary has no Mandarin entry: Chinese uses 升職 or 晉升, so `mandarin` and `cantonese` are left blank. The vault's [[晋升]] is the Chinese-channel word for the same idea; 昇進 is the Japanese/Korean/Vietnamese one. See also [[抜擢]] (to select for promotion) and [[昇叙]].
