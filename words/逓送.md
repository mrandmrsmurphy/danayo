## Notes
- [逓](../characters/逓.md) "hand over" + [送 (char)](../characters/送%20(char).md) "give"
- This word is the viable form of [逓](../characters/逓.md): the character's meaning (to hand over, deliver) is identical, but 逓 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 逓送 is its `stand_in` compound.

逓 is the Japanese shinjitai form of 遞, meaning to pass along in succession, hand to hand or stage to stage, as in the relay-post system of old, and 送 is "to send." 逓送 is therefore sending something onward through a chain of hands: forwarding mail or goods, relaying a message. The same 逓 gives the Japanese 逓信 "postal and telecommunications service" (as in the former 逓信省 "Ministry of Communications"), and the character page's own gloss "hand over, deliver" is the shared core.

It differs from the broader [輸送](輸送.md) "transportation, carrying" of goods and people by the idea of passing along in stages, and the Directions page notes it as the "pass along" sense, distinct from the spatial "forward" for which no Dan'a'yo word exists yet. The readings are Mandarin dìsòng (in Chinese the common spelling is 遞送), Cantonese dai6 sung3, Japanese teisō (ていそう) and Korean 체송. Dan'a'yo keeps the shared sense, "send, forward, transmit," placed under [Directions](../lexipedia/Directions.md).
