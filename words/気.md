## Notes
- [気](characters/気%20(char).md) "feeling" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 気 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

気 means feeling, spirit, energy (as in 気分 "mood," 元気 "vigor," 空気 "air").

Mandarin qì, Cantonese hei3, Korean 기, and Vietnamese khí are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 気's own stored citations exactly. Filled the blank `korean`/`vietnamese` fields, added the missing `japanese` (き, the primary on'yomi KI) and `hsk_level` ("1") fields, reformatted `characters` to a proper block-list with the "(char)" suffix. Reconfirmed with a fresh grep: the other ㄎㄧㄜ-reading characters (器, 埼, 棄, 汽, 豈) each have their own independent stand-in (容器, 川埼, 放棄, 蒸汽, 名専字 respectively), so none has an independent word page of its own — no homophones.
