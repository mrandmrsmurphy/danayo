## Notes
- [干](../characters/干.md) "shield" (extended: to concern, be relevant to, interfere) + [渉 (char)](../characters/渉%20(char).md) "to ford, wade across; to be involved with" — this word is the stand-in for [干](../characters/干.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo. To interfere, intervene, meddle in another's affairs.

干渉 covers both everyday interpersonal meddling (他人の生活に干渉する, "to interfere in someone else's life") and the technical physics sense of wave interference (光の干渉, "optical interference"), a genuine dual usage shared across all four languages' scientific registers.

Japanese かんしょう, Korean 간섭, and Vietnamese can thiệp are all standard, non-drifted readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄋㄙㄝㄆ unique across both words and characters).
