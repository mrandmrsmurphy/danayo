[乎 (char)](../characters/乎%20(char).md) "[question particle]" — a discontinuous circumfix, not a contiguous compound: 不亦 opens the frame and 乎 closes it, with a verb or adjective (represented here by the placeholder "V" in this page's own filename) inserted in the middle.

不亦……乎 is Classical Chinese's canonical rhetorical-question frame, most famous from the opening of the Analects: 學而時習之，不亦說乎 ("to learn and practice it at due times — is this not a joy?"), 有朋自遠方來，不亦樂乎 ("to have friends coming from afar — is this not delightful?"). Grammatically it has the surface form of a genuine question (乎 marking interrogative mood), but semantically it functions as an emphatic affirmative statement — "is it not X?" meaning "it certainly is X!" — the same rhetorical-question logic found in English "isn't it wonderful?" used to assert, not to ask.
