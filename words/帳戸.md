## Notes
- [帳](../characters/帳.md) "ledger, account book" + [戸](../characters/戸.md) "household, account" — "ledger household," an account. Their own `stand_in` values: 帳's is [[蚊帳]]; 戸's is [[門戸]]; none points here, so 帳戸 is an independent compound.

帳戸 is an account: the record under which a customer's money is kept at a bank or a business. Mandarin zhànghù (賬戶) and Cantonese zoeng3 wu6 are the ordinary words. Japanese says 口座 and Korean 계좌, so those two fields hold the character readings. Compare [[帳簿]] (ledger), and [[銀行]] (bank).
