## Notes
- [暴](../characters/暴%20(char).md) "violent, brutal, fierce, tyrannical" + [虐](../characters/虐%20(char).md) "tyrannical, oppressive" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [虐](../characters/虐%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 暴's own stand-in is itself.

暴虐 means tyrannical.

Mandarin bàonüè and Japanese ぼうぎゃく are standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 포학 uses 暴's standalone reading 포 (not the 폭 seen in [[暴徒]]/[[暴風]]/[[暴政]]/[[暴怒]] — a real, lexically irregular exception already present and correct). Filled blank `cantonese` (bou6 joek6) and `vietnamese` (bạo ngược, using the same real alternate "bạo" reading of 暴 flagged as a citation gap on [[暴徒]]).
