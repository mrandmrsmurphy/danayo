## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" + [若 (char)](../characters/若%20(char).md) "as; like"

自若 is self-possessed: remaining just as one was, unmoved by what happens. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin zìruò as "self-possessed; composed; collected; calm and at ease" and Japanese じじゃく the same. Korean 자약 and Cantonese zi6 joek6 match.

It is composure shown in the face of danger, a steadier form of [沈着](沈着.md) and of [泰然](泰然.md). It is most at home in 泰然自若.
