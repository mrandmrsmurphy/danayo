## Notes
- [建](../characters/建.md) "build" + [築](../characters/築.md) "fabricate; make; build" — no stand-in relationship applies (建's own `stand_in` is [[建設]], 築's is [[構築]]).

建築 is a near-synonym doubling, both halves independently meaning "to build" — the base word for construction/architecture, feeding a whole compound family: 建築事 (the act of building), 建築物 (a building, the thing built), 建築学 (architecture, the field of study), 建築家 (an architect).

Japanese けんちく, Korean 건축, and Vietnamese kiến trúc are all standard, non-drifted readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄝㄋㄐㄨㄎ unique across both words and characters).
