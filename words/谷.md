## Notes
- [谷 (char)](../characters/谷%20(char).md) "valley" — stand-in for [[谷 (char)|谷]], which cannot appear independently

谷 is 象形 of water flowing out from an opening (口) between two converging slopes (八) — a ravine or valley depicted directly by its landscape shape. The character's phonetic family is unusually productive and semantically diverse for a landscape term: 俗 ("vulgar, common"), 容 ("appearance"), 浴 ("bathe"), 欲 ("desire"), and 裕 ("abundant, rich") all descend from 谷 phonetically with no surviving trace of the valley sense itself — a wide phonetic-only descendant family already documented on the character's own page.

Japanese carries two on'yomi, こく (KOKU, used here — 渓谷 keikoku "ravine," 峡谷 kyōkoku "gorge") and rarer よく, alongside the native たに (tani), the everyday word for "valley" (谷底 tanizoko "valley floor"), and きわ ("edge, brink"), a related but distinct concept. Vietnamese cốc is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading (sơn cốc "mountain valley") — picked over the character's other stored variants, góc (the native Vietnamese word for "corner") and hốc ("a hollow, a cavity," a related but distinct sense).
