## Notes
- [法 (char)](../characters/法%20(char).md) "rule; law" + [典](../characters/典.md) "dictionary; canonical text"

法典 is a code of law: a systematic body of law gathered in one book. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin fǎdiǎn as "legal code; statute" and Japanese ほうてん as "code of law", with Korean 법전 and Cantonese faat3 din2 matching. The word is literally "the law book".

A 法典 contains many individual [法律](法律.md), the statutes, and it can cover one field of law, as [刑法](刑法.md) and [民法](民法.md) do. The largest code of a state is its [憲法](憲法.md), the constitution.
