## Notes
- [発](../characters/発%20(char).md) "issue, send out" + [票](../characters/票%20(char).md) "ticket, note" — "issued note," an invoice. Their own `stand_in` values: 発's is itself; 票's is itself; none points here, so 発票 is an independent compound.

発票 is an invoice or official receipt: the printed note a seller issues for a sale (the Chinese *fapiao*). Mandarin fāpiào (發票) and Cantonese faat3 piu3 are the ordinary words. Japanese and Korean do not use it in that sense, so those two fields hold the character readings; the Korean reading 발표 is also the reading of [[発表]], an unrelated word. Compare [[帳簿]] (ledger) and [[券]] (ticket, certificate).
