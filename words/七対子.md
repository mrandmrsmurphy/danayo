## Notes
- [七 (char)](../characters/七%20(char).md) "seven" + [対](../characters/対.md) "oppose" + [子](../characters/子.md) "son; child" — no `#cranberry`: none of the three constituents' own `stand_in` points here.
- Japanese special reading: ちいといつ (chiitoitsu) — the hand is named for the concept of seven pairs, not from the literal character readings. **Real content bug found and fixed**: the stored hiragana had both a malformed long-vowel mark (ー instead of い) and was missing its final つ.

Mandarin qīduìzǐ, Cantonese cat1 deoi3 zi2, and Korean 칠대자 are plain compositional readings for this specific mahjong hand. `vietnamese` is intentionally left blank: mahjong terminology isn't standard in Vietnamese, and no independently attested Vietnamese name for this specific hand turned up — per the vault's omit-don't-fabricate rule, left blank rather than guessed. `kwin` is false (AND-rule: all three constituents individually false).
