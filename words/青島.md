## Notes
- [青](../characters/青%20(char).md) "blue" + [島](../characters/島%20(char).md) "island" — "green island". No stand-in relationship applies (both characters are their own stand-ins).

青島 (Qingdao) is a sub-provincial port city in Shandong province on the eastern coast of China. Per Wiktionary the name originally belonged to a small islet at the mouth of Jiaozhou Bay, named for its green appearance, and was extended to the bay, the settlement and the city.

Japanese ちんたお (チンタオ) is an irregular reading borrowed from Mandarin and is the name listed on Wiktionary Japanese; Wikipedia Japanese also gives せいとう (from memory, not re-checked as a Sino-Japanese reading, and not listed on Wiktionary). The same spelling is also a Japanese surname, あおしま (a different word and reading, not covered here). Korean 청도 is the North Korean reading derived from the character pages (Wiktionary Korean lists 청도 as the alternative-form entry for 靑島); the usual South Korean name today is 칭다오 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Thanh Đảo is the Sino-Vietnamese name. Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese entries. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄝㄫㄊㄚㄨ unique across both words and characters).
