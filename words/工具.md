## Notes
- [工](../characters/工.md) "work; craft" + [具](../characters/具.md) "tool; implement" — this word is the stand-in for [具](../characters/具.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

工具 names a tool or instrument in the general mechanical/craft sense — the everyday word for a physical implement used in work (工具箱 "toolbox"). Japanese and Korean instead most commonly use 道具 (dōgu/도구, literally "way-implement," a broader term covering both tools and equipment/gear generally) for this everyday sense, with 工具 itself surviving mainly in more technical/industrial register (precision tools, machine-shop equipment) rather than as the default everyday word.

Mandarin/Cantonese gōngjù/gung1 geoi6 and Vietnamese công cụ are all standard, non-drifted readings — Vietnamese công cụ in particular remains the everyday default word for "tool" with no comparable narrowing. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄫㄍㄨ unique across both words and characters).
