## Notes
- [対](../characters/対.md) "facing, opposing" + [照 (char)](../characters/照%20(char).md) "illuminate, check against" — no stand-in relationship applies (対's own `stand_in` is [[反対]], 照's is itself).

対照 names the act of setting two things side by side to check or contrast them — a broader, more neutral act than [[対応]]'s "correspond/respond," closer to "cross-reference" or "juxtapose for comparison." Attested across Mandarin/Cantonese (as an "Advanced Mandarin" register term per Wiktionary, no specific HSK level given), Japanese たいしょう, Korean 대조, and Vietnamese đối chiếu, all sharing the same "check against" sense with no notable drift. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄧㄐㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).
