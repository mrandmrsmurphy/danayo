## Notes
- [滾](characters/滾%20(char).md) "boil, roll" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 滾 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

滾 means to boil or roll.

Mandarin gǔn, Cantonese gwan2, Japanese こん, Korean 곤, and Vietnamese cuộn (the primary form among the character's own comma-joined vietnamese variants cuộn/cổn/củn/ngổn) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed literal `vietnamese: null` → cuộn, added the missing `japanese` field. Discovered a genuine homophone with [[棺]] (both 注音 ㄍ⺢ㄋ, 諺文 관) — cross-referenced both pages.
