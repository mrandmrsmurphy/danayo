## Notes
- [旦](../characters/旦.md) "daybreak" + [夕](../characters/夕.md) "sunset" — no stand-in relationship applies (旦's own stand-in is [[元旦]]; 夕's own is [[夕陽]]).

旦夕 means sooner or later (literally "morning and evening," extended to imminence, as in 危在旦夕, "danger is imminent").

Mandarin dànxī, Cantonese daan3 zik6, Japanese たんせき, Korean 단석, and Vietnamese đán tịch are all standard, fully compositional readings. Trimmed malformed comma-duplicate `mandarin`. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄋㄙㄝㄎ unique across both words and characters).
