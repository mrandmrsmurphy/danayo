## Notes
- [墜](../characters/墜.md) "fall, crash" + [落](../characters/落.md) "fall" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [墜](../characters/墜.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (落's own stand-in is [[落下]]).

墜落 means to fall or crash — an aircraft or object dropping suddenly.

Japanese ついらく, Korean 추락, and Vietnamese truỵ lạc are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄨㄧㄌㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
