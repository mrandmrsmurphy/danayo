## Notes
- [失](../characters/失.md) "lose" + [望](../characters/望.md) "hope"

失望 is to be disappointed: to lose hope, or the sadness of an expectation unmet. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin shīwàng as "to lose hope; to be disappointed" and Japanese しつぼう as "disappointment". Korean 실망, Vietnamese thất vọng and Cantonese sat1 mong6 match.

It is the reverse of [希望](希望.md), hope, and a lighter grief than [悲痛](悲痛.md). Its low-spirited cousin on this page is [無然](無然.md).
