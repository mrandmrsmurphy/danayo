## Etymology
- [孤](../characters/孤.md) "lone, lonely" + [軍](../characters/軍.md) "armed forces"

## Notes
- "Isolated army, lone forces" (孤軍奮闘, "to fight alone against the odds"). Filled blank `korean: 고군`, compositional, and `vietnamese: cô quân`, a directly attested classical Vietnamese military term (cô quân phấn đấu, "isolated forces fighting alone"). `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 고군 matches the compositional Sino-Korean 고군 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄍㄨㄋ unique among both words and characters).
