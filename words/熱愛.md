## Notes
- [熱](../characters/熱%20(char).md) "hot, passionate" + [愛](../characters/愛%20(char).md) "love" — "hot love," to love passionately. Their own `stand_in` values: 熱's is itself; 愛's is itself; none points here, so 熱愛 is an independent compound.

熱愛 is to love passionately: to have a deep, burning love for a person, a cause or a pursuit. Mandarin rè'ài, Cantonese jit6 oi3, Japanese ねつあい and Korean 열애 all use it. It is stronger than plain [[愛]] and [[愛好]]. Compare [[溺愛]] (to dote) and [[痴情]] (infatuation).
