## Notes
- [千](../characters/千.md) "thousand" + [葉](../characters/葉%20(char).md) "leaf"

千葉 is Chiba, written "thousand leaves". The name is that of the Chiba clan and of the old Chiba district, and the reading ちば is native, so the characters are chosen for their auspicious sense and not their sound.

Chiba occupies the peninsula east of Tokyo Bay, with its capital Chiba. It is the home of Narita International Airport, the coastal resorts of the Bōsō Peninsula, and a large agricultural sector, and the Tokyo Disney Resort lies within it at Urayasu.

In Dan'a'yo 千葉 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 지바 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
