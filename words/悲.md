## Notes
- [悲](../characters/悲%20(char).md) "be sad" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

悲 means to be sad — sorrow or grief.

Mandarin bēi, Cantonese bei1, Japanese ひ, Korean 비, and Vietnamese bi are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄅㄧㄜ/biǝ/븨) with the unrelated [[比]] ("than, compared to") — both preserve the compound-vowel quality of the same Middle Chinese rhyme class where Sino-Korean monophthongizes to -i, per [[比]]'s own detailed note.
