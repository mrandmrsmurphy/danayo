## Notes
- [威](../characters/威.md) "might, power" + [力](../characters/力%20(char).md) "strength" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [威](../characters/威.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (力's own stand-in is itself).

威力 means power or might — overwhelming force or authority, especially of an intimidating or destructive nature.

Mandarin wēilì, Cantonese wai1 lik6, Japanese いりょく, Korean 위력, and Vietnamese uy lực are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `pos: 名詞`. No homophones (注音 ㄛㄧㄌㄧㄎ unique across both words and characters).
