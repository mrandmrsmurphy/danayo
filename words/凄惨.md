## Notes
- [凄](../characters/凄.md) "miserable" + [惨 (char)](../characters/惨%20(char).md) "wretched, cruel" — "miserable, wretched, tragic" (a scene of devastation or suffering). Mandarin qīcǎn, Cantonese cai1 caam2, Japanese せいさん, and Vietnamese thê thảm are all directly attested standard readings.

**Real content bug found and fixed**: `korean` had been filled with 비참한 (a different, unrelated Korean adjective for "miserable," built from entirely different characters 悲慘 plus an adjectival suffix), not this word's own compositional reading — corrected to 처참 (凄's own 처 + 惨's own 참). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 체참 diverges from the now-corrected Sino-Korean 처참 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄝㄧㄑㄚㄇ unique among both words and characters).
