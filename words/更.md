## Notes
- [更](../characters/更%20(char).md) "more" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 更 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

更 means "more" (as an intensifying adverb).

Mandarin gèng, Cantonese gang1, Japanese こう, and Korean 갱 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese` with cánh (the "more" sense; the character's citation also lists canh, used for the unrelated "change" sense seen in [[更新]]) and added the missing `pos`/`japanese`/`hsk_level` fields. No word-level homophones — the many bound characters sharing this 注音 (亢/剛/岡/庚/梗/江/綱/耕/肛/講/鋼/降) all have their own independent stand-in compounds rather than standalone word pages.
