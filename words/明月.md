## Notes
- [明](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright" + [月](../characters/月%20(char).md) "moon" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 明 and 月's own stand-ins are themselves).

明月 means bright moon, or (figuratively) jewel.

Mandarin míngyuè, Cantonese ming4 jyut6, Japanese めいげつ, Korean 명월, and Vietnamese minh nguyệt are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄇ⼶ㄫ⼔ㄊ unique across both words and characters).
