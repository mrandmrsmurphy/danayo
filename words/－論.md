## Notes
- [論](../characters/論.md) "theory; thesis" — a bound character; －論 is its fully productive suffix form.

－論 means "the theory of X" or "X theory": attached to a topic, it names the body of argument built around it, as in English "the theory of evolution", "evolutionary theory", or "Big Bang theory". It is productive in the plainest sense: any noun naming a phenomenon or a position can take it, and the result is read compositionally — 進化論 (theory of evolution), 相対論 (theory of relativity), 無神論 (the position that there is no god), 音韻論 (the theory of sound systems, hence "phonology"). A derivative needs its own page only when its meaning has drifted from "the theory of X", or when the page has something to say beyond the gloss.

Where 論 is the *head* verb or noun rather than a suffix, the word is a separate, explicitly enumerated compound and is not a －論 derivation: [[議論]] "to discuss", [[討論]] "to debate", [[評論]] "to criticize; to comment", [[論争]] "controversy", [[論理]] "logic", and [[論語]] "Analects". [[理論]] "theory" is the stand-in word that legitimizes the bare character, and it stands alone; －論 is what you reach for when the thing being theorized is named first.

Wiktionary lists 論 in Chinese as "to discuss, reason, debate, argue; discussion, discourse; essay; theory, thesis, system", in Japanese as "argument, discussion; theory; opinion, view; essay, treatise; theory, -ology; doctrine, -ism", with 론/논 as the Korean hanja form of "argument, discussion", and luận as the Vietnamese Han form of "essay".

The suffix is distinct from semi-productive 学, which names a *discipline* (the field, 化学 "chemistry") where －論 names a *theory* within or about it: 進化論 is a theory, 進化学 would be the study. Neither implies the other.

Because 論 and the element name [[錀]] "roentgenium" are the same syllable in Dan'a'yo (lon, ㄌㄛㄋ), the homophone callout above cross-references them; context separates them without trouble, since 錀 is a standalone proper-noun word and －論 never appears alone.

The page filename uses the fullwidth hyphen `－` (U+FF0D) to mark a word that cannot stand alone but is fully productive; the ASCII form `-論` is kept as an alias.
