## Notes
- [利](../characters/利.md) + [佛 (char)](../characters/佛%20(char).md) + [素](../characters/素.md) "element" — a transliteration of *Livermore*, after Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, truncated to two syllables rather than spelled out in full the way Mandarin's borrowed-character system (利弗莫尔) would. Japanese and Korean instead borrow the IUPAC name as a loanword (リバモリウム, 리버모륨).

**Form, matching [[佛雷素]]'s pattern.** Like flerovium, this is a two-syllable phonetic transliteration rather than a single avoided-character coinage, so `mandarin`/`cantonese` (lìfú/lei6 fat6) give the direct compositional reading of 利's own lì/lei6 and 佛's own fú/fat6 — not the real Chinese element character it avoids, 鉝 (lì/laap6; its phonetic component is actually 立, not 利, despite 利/立 being Mandarin homophones — Cantonese distinguishes them, giving laap6 rather than lei6). `vietnamese` (Livermori) follows the same "-i" pattern as [[達金]]/[[邁金]]/[[黒金]]/[[佛雷素]].
