## Notes
- [支](../characters/支.md) "branch" + [部](../characters/部%20(char).md) "section" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [支](../characters/支.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (部's own stand-in is itself).

支部 means branch or chapter (of an organization).

Mandarin zhībù, Cantonese zi1 bou6, Japanese しぶ, Korean 지부, and Vietnamese chi bộ (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄅㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).
