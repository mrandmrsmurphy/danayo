## Etymology
- [運](../characters/運.md) "move, transport" + [行 (char)](../characters/行%20(char).md) "attend, go to"

## Notes
Mandarin yùnxíng, cantonese wan6 hang4, japanese うんこう, korean 운행, and vietnamese vận hành (a directly attested, extremely common Vietnamese word — "to operate, function") are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. `运行` is the standard simplified-character spelling variant (運→运), correctly listed as an alias. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 운항 diverges from Sino-Korean 운행 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄨㄋㄏㄚㄫ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 運's `stand_in` (運送) nor 行's own `stand_in` (行 itself) points to this compound.
