## Notes
- [龕 (char)](../characters/龕%20(char).md) "shrine, alcove" — stand-in for [[龕 (char)|龕]], which cannot appear independently

龕 is 形声: semantic [[Radical 212|龍]] ("dragon") + phonetic [[今 (char)|今]] ("now") — a dragon-adorned alcove for housing an idol; "shrine, alcove," as in a wall-niche or small cabinet holding a Buddhist or ancestral image.

Mandarin kān and Cantonese ham1 preserve the sense directly. Japanese こん (KON, on'yomi, formal/technical register) alongside native ずし (zushi), the narrower everyday word for a small altar-cabinet housing an image, cover the sense. Korean 감 (gam) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese khám is the Hán Việt reading, attested in khám thờ ("shrine niche, altar recess"); the character page's other stored candidates (kham, khắm, khẳm) belong to unrelated senses/words and were not used here. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 캄 (kam, from Middle Chinese 溪母 kʰ + 覃韻 ʌm) does not coincide with Sino-Korean 감 — the aspirated MC initial surfaces as Dan'a'yo's aspirated ㅋ but Korean's plain ㄱ.

**Previously anticipated by both other members of its own homophone group**: this word completes a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read kam/캄/ㄎㄚㄇ) with [[勘]] ("investigate; proofread") and [[堪]] ("withstand"), both of which already carried a reciprocal callout here, flagged as "still awaiting its own turn" — now perfected, with the callout added on this page and the stale wording fixed on both siblings.
