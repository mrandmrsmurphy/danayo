## Notes
- [龕 (char)](../characters/龕%20(char).md) "shrine, alcove" — stand-in for [[龕 (char)|龕]], which cannot appear independently

龕 is 形声: semantic [[Radical 212|龍]] ("dragon") + phonetic [[今 (char)|今]] ("now") — a dragon-adorned alcove for housing an idol; "shrine, alcove," as in a wall-niche or small cabinet holding a Buddhist or ancestral image.

Mandarin kān and Cantonese ham1 preserve the sense directly. Japanese こん (KON, on'yomi, formal/technical register) alongside native ずし (zushi), the narrower everyday word for a small altar-cabinet housing an image, cover the sense. Korean 감 (gam) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese khám is the Hán Việt reading, attested in khám thờ ("shrine niche, altar recess"); the character page's other stored candidates (kham, khắm, khẳm) belong to unrelated senses/words and were not used here.
