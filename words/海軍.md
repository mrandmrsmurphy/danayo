## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [軍](../characters/軍.md) "army, military" — "sea army," a navy. Their own `stand_in` values: 海's is [[海洋]]; 軍's is [[軍隊]]; none points here, so 海軍 is an independent compound.

海軍 is a navy: the armed forces that fight at sea. Mandarin hǎijūn, Cantonese hoi2 gwan1, Japanese かいぐん and Korean 해군 all use it, and Vietnamese hải quân does too. It is the sea counterpart of [[空軍]] (air force) and of the land forces ([[軍隊]]). Compare [[艦隊]] (fleet) and [[軍艦]] (warship).
