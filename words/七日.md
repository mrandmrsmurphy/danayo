## Notes
- [七 (char)](../characters/七%20(char).md) "seven" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — the seventh day of the month, or a span of seven days.

Japanese なのか (nanoka) is a fixed native reading rather than the plain on'yomi concatenation しちにち (shichinichi) — the same "irregular native calendar reading" pattern already documented on [[八日]] elsewhere in this vault's day-counting compound family (一日 tsuitachi, 二日 futsuka, and so on through the traditional first ten days of the month, each with its own fossilized native reading rather than a productive on'yomi formula). Mandarin/Cantonese/Vietnamese all use the plain compositional reading instead (qīrì/cat1 jat6/thất nhật), with no equivalent irregular native form in any of those three languages. Korean 칠일 is likewise a straightforward concatenation. `kwin` is false: both syllables diverge from their Sino-Korean counterparts (七's Dan'a'yo 칟 vs. Korean 칠; 日's Dan'a'yo 닏 vs. Korean 일), the same recurring coda-divergence pattern documented throughout this stretch of the sweep. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄊㄋㄧㄊ unique among both words and characters).
