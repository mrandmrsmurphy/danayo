## Notes
- [鑑](../characters/鑑.md) "mirror; to examine clearly" + [定](../characters/定.md) "to decide, to determine" — to appraise; to authenticate; an expert determination

Wiktionary: Japanese 鑑定 (かんてい) "appraisal" and, as a verb, "appraise, judge"; Chinese 鑒定 "to appraise; to verify; to identify; to evaluate; appraisal; verification; identification; evaluation"; Korean 감정 is the hanja form. Neither character is legitimized here: 鑑's `stand_in` is 鏡鑑 and 定's is 決定.
