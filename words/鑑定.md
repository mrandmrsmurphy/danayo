## Notes
- [鑑](../characters/鑑.md) "mirror; to examine clearly" + [定](../characters/定.md) "to decide, to determine" — to appraise; to authenticate; an expert determination

鑑 is the bronze mirror of antiquity (originally a bronze basin filled with water that served as one) — an emblem of clear, objective scrutiny. 鑑定 is the act of holding something up to that mirror and rendering a verdict: the assessment of an expert who sees clearly and decides definitively. The compound covers valuation of antiques (骨董の鑑定), authentication of artworks, forensic analysis, and professional certification.

鑑定士 (appraiser, certified assessor) is the professional title; 不動産鑑定士 (real estate appraiser), 古物鑑定士 (antique authenticator). In legal and forensic contexts, 精神鑑定 (psychiatric evaluation) and DNA鑑定 (DNA analysis) show the compound's extension into evidentiary and clinical assessment — any domain where expert scrutiny produces a binding determination.

The compound is stable across all five languages: Mandarin jiàndìng, Cantonese gaam3 ding6, Japanese かんてい (kantei), Korean 감정 (gamjeong), Vietnamese giám định. `kwin` is true because the Dan'a'yo 감정 equals the Korean 감정, both 鑑 and 定 being `kwin: true` themselves.

Neither character is legitimized here: 鑑's `stand_in` is 鏡鑑 and 定's is 決定.  The 注音 ㄍㄚㄇㄐㄝㄫ needs no syllable dot, and no other word shares it.
