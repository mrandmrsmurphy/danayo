## Notes
- [願](../characters/願.md) "request" + [意](../characters/意.md) "meaning, significance" — 願意 is 願's own legitimizing compound (願's `stand_in` points here), but since 意's own `stand_in` is [[意味]] instead, transitivity fails and no `#cranberry` tag applies.

願意 (yuànyì) is the standard Mandarin word for "willing (to do something)," emphasizing voluntary consent, and Cantonese 願意 (jyun6ji3) likewise. Japanese がんい (gan'i) is real but carries a narrower sense — "the content/purpose of a wish or petition," used especially in formal or religious contexts (written prayer requests). Korean 원의 (wonui) is the standard compositional term. Sino-Vietnamese nguyện ý is likewise real and standard for "willingness."
