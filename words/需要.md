## Notes
- [需](../characters/需.md) "need" + [要](../characters/要.md) "important, need" — 需要 is 需's own legitimizing compound (需's `stand_in` points here), but since 要's own `stand_in` is [[重要]] instead, transitivity fails and no `#cranberry` tag applies.

需要 (xūyào) is the standard Mandarin word for "to need, to require" and also the ordinary economic term for "demand," and Cantonese 需要 (seoi1jiu3) follows the same pattern. Japanese じゅよう (juyō) and Korean 수요 (suyo) are likewise the standard economic terms for "demand" in their respective languages. Sino-Vietnamese nhu yếu is a real, attested compound meaning "necessary, needed" — most familiar today in the fixed phrase nhu yếu phẩm ("daily necessities, essential goods").
