## Notes
- [被 (char)](../characters/被%20(char).md) "suffer; receive" + [害](../characters/害.md) "harm"

被害 is harm suffered: damage or injury that comes to a person from an attack, a crime or a disaster. Wiktionary glosses Japanese ひがい as "suffering damage; being injured; damage, injury"; for Mandarin bèihài it gives "to be persecuted, to become an injured party" and, literary, "to suffer a disaster". Vietnamese bị hại is the same word, and Korean 피해 and Cantonese bei6 hoi6 match.

It is the harm from the side of the one who bears it, and the person is the [被害者](被害者.md), the victim. Harm in general is [災害](災害.md) or [傷害](傷害.md).
