## Notes
- [外](../characters/外.md) "outside" + [来 (char)](../characters/来%20(char).md) "come" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language, word" — "word that came from outside." Built on the adjective [[外来]] "foreign, extrinsic." No stand-in relationship applies (外's `stand_in` is [[外部]], 来's is itself, 語's is [[言語]]).

外来語 is a loanword. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 外來語 (alias here; Mandarin wàiláiyǔ, Cantonese ngoi6 loi4 jyu5) as the linguistics term "loanword; borrowing". For Japanese 外来語 (がいらいご) it gives, from 1895, a loanword borrowed since the modernization of Japan in the Meiji era from a modern foreign language, especially a European one, and says the term was first used by Ueda Kazutoshi in 1895, likely as a calque of German Fremdwort. It lists Korean 외래어 as a hanja form of "foreign loanword". The Vietnamese từ ngoại lai field is built from the readings of the characters. 語 here is "word", as in [単語](単語.md) and [造語](造語.md); the Japanese contrast class is [漢語](漢語.md). The phonology page [文法 - 02音韻論](../grammar/文法%20-%2002音韻論.md) uses it in 外来語転写, the transcription of foreign words. For "foreign language" the vault has [外語](外語.md) and [外国語](外国語.md).
