## Notes
- [副 (char)](../characters/副%20(char).md) "vice-"

副 is 形声: semantic [[刀]] ("knife") + phonetic [[畐]]. It means "secondary, deputy, auxiliary" — the standard "vice-" prefix (副主席 "vice chairman"; [[副業]] "side job, second job"). **Real bug found and fixed**: this word's own `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` previously stored `pug`/푹/ㄆㄨㄎ, but the character page's own authoritative reading is `fug`/뿍/ㄈㄨㄎ — corrected to match. Most languages derive this character's initial as a plain p-, but Dan'a'yo deliberately keeps the f- initial and the -k coda here (matching its phonetic siblings [[福]] and [[腹]], both also read fuk) rather than the otherwise-expected p-initial pattern — a documented but only partially-explained override (the character page's own note on this trails off mid-sentence, preserved as-is rather than completed with guesswork).

Mandarin fù, Cantonese fu3, and Japanese ふく/ふ (fuku/fu, on'yomi, fully productive: 副業 ふくぎょう "side job") all preserve the sense. Korean 부 (bu) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 버금 (beogeum, "second-in-rank, next"). Vietnamese phó is the Hán Việt reading, extremely well attested (phó chủ tịch, "vice chairman"; phó giám đốc, "deputy director"); the character page's second stored candidate, pho, is less clearly attested but kept as-is since it's already consistently stored on both the word and character pages rather than an isolated contamination.

This word is part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read fug/뿍/ㄈㄨㄎ): [[福]] ("bless") and [[腹]] ("stomach"), both still awaiting their own turn in this sweep — the reciprocal half of this callout will be completed on each when it comes up.
