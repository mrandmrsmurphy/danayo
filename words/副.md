## Notes
- [副 (char)](../characters/副%20(char).md) "vice-"

副 is 形声: semantic [[刀]] ("knife") + phonetic [[畐]]. It means "secondary, deputy, auxiliary" — the standard "vice-" prefix (副主席 "vice chairman"; [[副業]] "side job, second job"). Dan'a'yo keeps the f- initial and -k coda here, matching its phonetic siblings [[福]] and [[腹]] (both also read fuk), rather than the otherwise-expected p- initial.

Mandarin fù, Cantonese fu3, and Japanese ふく/ふ (fuku/fu, on'yomi, fully productive: 副業 ふくぎょう "side job") all preserve the sense. Korean 부 (bu) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 버금 (beogeum, "second-in-rank, next"). Vietnamese phó is the Hán Việt reading, extremely well attested (phó chủ tịch, "vice chairman"; phó giám đốc, "deputy director").
