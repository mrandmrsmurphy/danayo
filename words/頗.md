## Notes
- [頗 (char)](../characters/頗%20(char).md) "very" — this word is 頗's own legitimizing compound (頗's `stand_in` points here). The Dan'a'yo reading fa/빠/ㄈㄚ was deliberately chosen to fill a vacant syllable rather than derived from the character's own real Mandarin/Vietnamese readings.

頗 means "very, quite, rather" as an intensifying adverb, and in Classical Chinese also "biased, partial." Mandarin pō is the standard modern reading, with an archaic pǒ (equivalent to 叵, "cannot") in Classical usage. Cantonese po2, Korean 파 (pa), and Sino-Vietnamese phả all preserve the Sino reading directly. Japanese は (ha) survives mainly in fixed literary compounds; the more common native reading is すこぶる (sukoburu, "very, exceedingly").
