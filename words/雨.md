## Notes
- [雨](../characters/雨%20(char).md) "rain" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

雨 means rain.

Mandarin yǔ, Cantonese jyu5, Korean 우, Japanese う, and Vietnamese vũ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Part of a four-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read 'u/우/ㄨ) with [[于]] ("to," dative particle), [[遇]] ("meet, encounter"), and [[愚]] ("foolish").
