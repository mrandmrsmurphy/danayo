## Notes
- [雨](../characters/雨%20(char).md) "rain" — the bare character used directly as a word, its own `stand_in`.

雨 is a 象形 pictograph of rain falling from the sky: a horizontal line for the heavens with drops below. It means rain (下雨, "to rain"; 雨水, "rainwater"; 暴雨, "rainstorm"; 梅雨, "plum rains"), and as a radical (雨部) it heads characters for weather phenomena (雪, 雲, 雷). The variants 雩 and 䨒 are listed as aliases.

Mandarin yǔ, Cantonese jyu5, Japanese う (雨天/うてん, "rainy weather"; 豪雨/ごうう, "torrential rain"; 梅雨/ばいう, "rainy season"), Korean 우 (우산, "umbrella"; 폭우, "heavy rain"), and Vietnamese vũ; the native Vietnamese word is mưa are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native noun 雨 (あめ) for the everyday word, so on'yomi う is bound to compounds; Korean has native 비.
