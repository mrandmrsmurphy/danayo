## Notes
- [樹](../characters/樹.md) "plant" + [懶](../characters/懶.md) "lazy" — no stand-in relationship applies (樹's own stand-in is [[樹木]]; 懶's own is [[懶惰]]).

樹懶 means sloth (the animal).

Mandarin shùlǎn and Cantonese syu6 laan5 are the genuine, standard compositional Chinese term. Japanese ナマケモノ is the real, standard native Japanese word for sloth — no Sino-Japanese compound is in common use, kept as-is (the same pattern seen on [[板球]], [[果醤]], [[楊樹]]). Filled the blank `korean`/`vietnamese` fields with the compositional forms (수란, thụ lãn) since no established real-world Sino-Korean/Sino-Vietnamese loanword was found. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨㄌㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
