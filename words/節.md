## Notes
- [節](../characters/節%20(char).md) "node, knuckle, joint, holiday" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 節 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo. Its 形声 etymology (竹, "bamboo" + phonetic 即) reflects the segmented node of a bamboo stalk, extended figuratively to any joint, segment, or division of time — including a festival or holiday (節句, 春節).

Mandarin jié, Cantonese zit3, and Korean 절 (jeol) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese せつ (setsu, on'yomi) is the standard reading, since the character stores no native kun-reading of its own (`japanese_native: ø`). Vietnamese tiết is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading.
