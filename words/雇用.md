## Notes
- [雇](../characters/雇.md) "employ" + [用](../characters/用.md) "use"
- This word is the stand-in for [[雇]], which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo.

雇用 means to employ or hire someone, and as a noun employment in the sense of the work relation between employer and employee. 雇 is "to hire" and 用 "to use", so the picture is taking someone on for service in return for pay. Mandarin gùyòng (also written 僱用), Cantonese gu3 jung6, Japanese こよう (koyō; 雇用保険, "employment insurance"; 雇用主, "an employer") and Korean 고용 (goyong; 고용주, "an employer") are the same compound, standard in labour and economic writing.

In Dan'a'yo it is the employer's side of the work relation, beside [[募集]] (to recruit, solicit), which is the earlier public call, and [[工作]] (work, a job).
