Pronunciation fields (laghwa/락화/ㄌㄚㄎㄏ⺢) are the straightforward concatenation of each constituent's own stored reading — no bug. Added missing `kwin: true` (AND-rule: both 落 and 花 individually true). Mandarin luòhuā, japanese らっか (with the same well-established 促音便 gemination as [[落下]]/[[落下傘]] — a striking real-world Japanese homograph coincidence with 落下 itself, though the two are not Dan'a'yo-internal homophones since 落下's own Dan'a'yo reading differs), and vietnamese lạc hoa (a real classical Vietnamese poetic term, e.g. "lạc hoa hữu ý, lưu thủy vô tình") all confirmed standard and compositional. **Found and fixed a real bug**: korean had a comma-joined "락화,낙화," mixing the correct unshifted form with a 두음법칙-shifted (South Korean) variant — trimmed to just 락화, matching the vault's standing rule. Fixed cantonese's stray space. No homophones.
