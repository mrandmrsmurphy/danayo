## Notes
- [落](../characters/落.md) "fall" is separately legitimized by [[落下]]. [花](../characters/花.md) "flower" is separately legitimized by [[草花]]. No cranberry.

Mandarin luòhuā, japanese らっか (with the same well-established 促音便 gemination as [[落下]]/[[落下傘]] — a striking real-world Japanese homograph coincidence with 落下 itself, though the two are not Dan'a'yo-internal homophones since 落下's own Dan'a'yo reading differs), and vietnamese lạc hoa (a real classical Vietnamese poetic term, e.g. "lạc hoa hữu ý, lưu thủy vô tình") all confirmed standard and compositional.
