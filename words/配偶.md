## Notes
Mandarin pèi'ǒu and cantonese pui3 ngau5 are directly attested standard readings. **Found and fixed a wrong-compound reading bug on `japanese`/`korean`**: はいぐうしゃ/배우자 actually belong to the longer, different compound 配偶者 ("spouse [person]," which adds 者) — corrected to the honest compositional はいぐう/배우 (配's own HAI/배 + 偶's own GUU/우, dropping the extra 者 syllable). Vietnamese phối ngẫu is the honest compositional reading (配's own phối + 偶's own ngẫu); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. **Found and removed a bad-aliases-entry**: `配偶者` is that same genuinely different (longer) compound, not a spelling variant of 配偶. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 푀옷 diverges from Sino-Korean 배우 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄛㄧㄛㄨ unique among both words and characters).

**Found and fixed a malformed `vietnamese` field on 偶.md itself** (three readings mashed into a single comma-separated list item, split into a proper three-item list). Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 配's `stand_in` (配 itself) nor 偶's own `stand_in` (偶像) points to this compound.
