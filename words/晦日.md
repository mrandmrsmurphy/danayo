## Notes
- [晦](../characters/晦.md) "dark; last day of lunar month" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day"

晦日 is the last day of a lunar month. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 晦日 (Mandarin huìrì, Cantonese fui3 jat6), labelled literary, and Japanese かいじつ both as "the last day of a month in the lunar calendar". The field here gives the Japanese つごもり, which Wiktionary lists under 晦 as an archaic word for "the darkening of moonlight" and, by extension, "the last day of a (lunar) month". Korean 회일 and Vietnamese hối nhật are not entries there, so they are built from the characters; for the single 晦 Wiktionary lists Korean 회 as "last day of a lunar month".

The first day of the lunar month is the [[朔日]], and the full-moon day is the [[望日]].
