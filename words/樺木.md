## Notes
- [樺](../characters/樺.md) "birch" + [木 (char)](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree" — this word is the viable form of [樺](../characters/樺.md): the character's meaning (birch tree) is identical, but 樺 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 樺木 is its `stand_in` compound.

樺木 is the birch, a tree of the genus *Betula*, and its wood. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 樺木 (alias 桦木; Mandarin huàmù, Cantonese waa6 muk6) as "birch (wood or tree)", and Korean 화목 as "birch (wood or tree)" among its senses, with the native synonym 자작나무. Korean 화목 is also the word for "firewood", for "harmony, peace, concord", and for "a flowering tree". For the single character 樺 it gives Japanese as "birch", read かば and かんば, and also "reddish yellow". It has no Japanese entry for the compound, so Japanese かぼく here is built from the characters, and the Vietnamese field is left blank.

樺木 is the viable form of [樺](../characters/樺.md): the character's meaning, a birch, is identical, but 樺 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word.
