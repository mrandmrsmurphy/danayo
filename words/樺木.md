## Notes
- [樺](../characters/樺.md) "birch" + [木 (char)](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree" — this word is the viable form of [樺](../characters/樺.md): the character's meaning (birch tree) is identical, but 樺 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 樺木 is its `stand_in` compound.

樺木 (birch, genus *Betula*) is a hardy deciduous tree of temperate and boreal regions, known for its distinctive thin, often white or silvery peeling bark and its wood's use in furniture, paper pulp, and traditional crafts (birch-bark canoes, Russian and Nordic folk items).

Japanese かぼく and Korean 화목 are both standard compositional Sino-readings. **`vietnamese` intentionally left blank**: Vietnamese doesn't calque 樺木 at all — the standard term is instead cây bạch dương (白楊, literally "white poplar"), borrowed via Russian берёза in translation and applied to birch despite 楊/dương properly naming poplar rather than birch, a real historical mismatch that has simply stuck. **Real bug found and fixed**: `kwin` was stored false, but Dan'a'yo 화목 and Sino-Korean 화목 are identical — corrected to true. No specific HSK level applies to a term this specialized. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⺢·ㄇㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
