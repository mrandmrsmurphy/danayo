## Notes
- [細](../characters/細%20(char).md) "fine, thin, slender" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

細 means fine, thin, slender, or quiet (of a sound) — describing something of small width, delicacy, or subtlety.

Mandarin xì, Cantonese sai3, Japanese せい, Korean 세, and Vietnamese tế are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄙㄝㄧ/sei/세) with the unrelated [[婿]] ("bridegroom, son-in-law").
