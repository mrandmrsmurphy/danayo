## Notes
- [重 (char)](../characters/重%20(char).md) "heavy" + [力 (char)](../characters/力%20(char).md) "force"

重力 is gravity: the force by which a large body, such as the earth, pulls other things toward itself. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin zhònglì as "earth's gravity; by extension gravity of other celestial bodies" and Japanese じゅうりょく as "gravity". Vietnamese trọng lực is the same word, and Korean 중력 and Cantonese cung5 lik6 match.

It holds the [地球](地球.md) in its [軌道](軌道.md) around the sun. The word for weight itself is [重](重.md), heavy.
