## Notes
- [妻 (char)](../characters/妻%20(char).md) "wife" + [兄 (char)](../characters/兄%20(char).md) "older brother" — wife's elder brother.

妻兄 is the older brother of one's wife. It is "wife-older brother." Mandarin qīxiōng is confirmed on Wiktionary as a formal, written-standard term; the page links Japanese and Korean entries that it does not show, so those fields are left blank. Dialects and speech use other terms (大舅子 and so on). It is one of the in-law terms of ego's generation with [[妻弟]] and the husband-side pair [[夫兄]]/[[夫弟]]; the hypernym is [[姻兄]]. It is a schema word in the family of in-law terms built from the marriage relation and the sibling roots, coined for predictability rather than a claim about ancient usage.
