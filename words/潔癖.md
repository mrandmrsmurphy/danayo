## Notes
- [潔](../characters/潔.md) "clean, pure" + [癖](../characters/癖%20(char).md) "habit, obsession" — "obsession with purity," fastidiousness. Their own `stand_in` values: 潔's is [[清潔]]; 癖's is itself; none points here, so 潔癖 is an independent compound.

潔癖 is fastidiousness: an obsessive love of cleanliness and purity, which extends to moral squeamishness about anything improper. Mandarin jiépǐ and Cantonese git3 pik1 and Japanese けっぺき are attested, and Korean 결벽 is the same compound. Compare [[貞潔]] (chastity) and [[仮正経]] (prude).
