## Notes
- [操](../characters/操.md) "to grasp, manipulate, control" + [心 (char)](../characters/心%20(char).md) "heart" — literally "to grip/exercise the heart," i.e. to take active, hands-on care over something.

操心 is a real, attested compound across the CJK sphere, not a Dan'a'yo coinage. Mandarin cāoxīn and Cantonese cou1 sam1 both mean "to worry about, to be concerned about, to take pains over" — an active, responsibility-laden concern (for family, children, an ongoing project), distinct from passive "worry." Korean 조심 (josim) is the same hanja compound, but has narrowed to mean "caution, care" — the everyday word for "be careful" (조심하다, josimhada). Vietnamese thao tâm (verified Hán Việt, built from 操's own thao + 心's own tâm) carries the same "worry, be concerned" sense as the Mandarin/Cantonese usage.

Japanese そうしん is the compositional Sino-Japanese reading (SOU + SHIN); everyday Japanese instead uses 用心 (yōjin) or 注意 (chūi) for "be careful".
