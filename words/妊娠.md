## Notes
- [妊 (char)](../characters/妊%20(char).md) "be pregnant" + [娠](../characters/娠.md) "pregnant" — this word is the stand-in for [娠](../characters/娠.md), which cannot appear independently.

妊娠 means pregnancy. The character 娠 alone means pregnant, but is too bounded to stand by itself in Dan'a'yo, so this compound serves as its viable form.
