## Notes
- [繃](../characters/繃.md) "bandage" + [帯 (char)](../characters/帯%20(char).md) "sash; belt" — this word is 繃's own `stand_in`: the character's meaning (bandage) is identical, but 繃 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word, so the compound pairs it with 帯 (the thing a bandage physically is) to make it usable.

Mandarin bēngdài, Cantonese bang1 daai3, Japanese ほうたい, and Korean 붕대 are all standard, fully compositional readings across the CJKV sphere.
