## Notes
- [現](../characters/現%20(char).md) "present; current" + [用](../characters/用.md) "use" — "in use at present".

English Wiktionary has no entry for 現用. Japanese Wiktionary lists げんよう as a noun, "being currently in use". Used in `文法 - 03文字法` in 現用字庫, the inventory of characters presently in use. Compare [[常用]], [[専用]], [[慣用]], [[使用]], [[現在]], [[用字]].
