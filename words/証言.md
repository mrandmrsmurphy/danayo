## Notes
- [証](../characters/証.md) "to prove, to certify" + [言 (char)](../characters/言%20(char).md) "to speak, word" — to testify, to give witness by speaking.

Vietnamese chứng ngôn (compositional, from 証's own chứng "to prove/certify" and 言's own ngôn "word/speech") parallels the everyday Vietnamese legal vocabulary built on the same 証/證 root (chứng nhân "witness," chứng minh "to prove"). Its homophone [[井堰]] is unrelated in real Korean (정언, from 井's own 정 + 堰's own 언): the two words coincide only at the Dan'a'yo level (注音 ㄐㄧㄫㄝㄋ).
