## Notes
- [証](../characters/証.md) "to prove, to certify" + [言 (char)](../characters/言%20(char).md) "to speak, word" — to testify, to give witness by speaking.

Vietnamese chứng ngôn (compositional, from 証's own chứng "to prove/certify" and 言's own ngôn "word/speech") parallels the everyday Vietnamese legal vocabulary built on the same 証/證 root (chứng nhân "witness," chứng minh "to prove"). Fixed a real character-level bug: `characters/証.md`'s own `korean` field held 정, but every other 証-compound in this vault ([[保証]] 보증, [[検証]] 검증, [[証券]] 증권, [[証拠]] 증거, [[証人]] 증인, [[証明]] 증명, [[認証]] 인증, [[通行証]] 통행증) consistently uses 증 — corrected the character citation to 증, and this word's own `korean` from 정언 to 증언 accordingly. `kwin` remains false: Dan'a'yo 징언 still diverges from the real Sino-Korean 증언. Its homophone [[井堰]] is unrelated in real Korean (정언, from 井's own 정 + 堰's own 언) — the two words coincide only at the Dan'a'yo level (注音 ㄐㄧㄫ·ㄝㄋ), not in real Korean.
