## Notes
- [証](../characters/証.md) "to prove, to certify" + [言 (char)](../characters/言%20(char).md) "to speak, word" — to testify, to give witness by speaking.

Vietnamese chứng ngôn (compositional, from 証's own chứng "to prove/certify" and 言's own ngôn "word/speech") parallels the everyday Vietnamese legal vocabulary built on the same 証/證 root (chứng nhân "witness," chứng minh "to prove"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 징언 diverges from Sino-Korean 정언 in the first syllable's vowel — the same divergence shared with its homophone [[井堰]].
