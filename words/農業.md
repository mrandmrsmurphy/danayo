## Notes
Synonymous with [[農耕]] ("agriculture" as cultivation practice — 農業 more broadly names the industry/sector).

Mandarin nóngyè, cantonese nung4 jip6, japanese のうぎょう, korean 농업, and vietnamese nông nghiệp are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 농업 matches Sino-Korean 농업 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄛㄫㄝㄆ unique among both words and characters).
