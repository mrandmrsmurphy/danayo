## Notes
- [神 (char)](../characters/神%20(char).md) "god, deity, spirit" + [秘](../characters/秘.md) "secret, mysterious" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (神's is [[神]] itself, 秘's is [[秘密]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 秘 is individually `kwin: false`.

神秘 describes something mysterious or arcane in a way tied to the divine or supernatural — the unknowable quality of gods, fate, or hidden ritual — distinguishing it from [[秘密]] ("secret," a plain withheld fact) by adding the numinous, awe-tinged sense carried by 神. It is highly productive as a modifier: 神秘主義 ("mysticism"), 神秘的 ("mysterious, mystical").

Mandarin shénmì, Cantonese san4 bei3, Japanese しんぴ (shinpi), Korean 신비 (sinbi), and Vietnamese thần bí are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each functions as an ordinary everyday adjective/noun for "mysterious/mystery" in its language.
