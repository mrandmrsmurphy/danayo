## Notes
- [藍 (char)](../characters/藍%20(char).md) "indigo plant" is separately legitimized by [[藍]] itself. [色](../characters/色.md) "color" is separately legitimized by [[色彩]]. No cranberry.

Mandarin lánsè is standard compositional. Japanese あいいろ compositionally uses both characters' own *native* readings (あい "indigo" + いろ "color"), matching the established `-色` convention.
