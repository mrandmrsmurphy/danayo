## Notes
- [廃](../characters/廃.md) "abrogate; terminate; discard" + [墟](../characters/墟.md) "ruins; wasteland" — this word is the stand-in for [墟](../characters/墟.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

廃墟 names ruins — an abandoned, decayed city, building, or settlement.

Japanese はいきょ, Korean 폐허, and Vietnamese phế khư are all standard, non-drifted readings. No homophones (注音 ㄈㄝㄎ⼄ unique across both words and characters).
