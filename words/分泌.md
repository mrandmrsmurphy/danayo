## Notes
- [分 (char)](../characters/分%20(char).md) "divide" + [泌](../characters/泌.md) "secrete"
- This word is the viable form of [泌](../characters/泌.md): the character's meaning (to secrete) is identical, but 泌 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 分泌 is its `stand_in` compound.

分泌 is the standard physiological verb "to secrete": a gland (腺体) 分泌 a hormone, enzyme, sweat or mucus. Mandarin fēnmì, Cantonese fan1 bei3, Japanese ぶんぴつ (bunpitsu; also read ぶんぴ) and Korean 분비 (bunbi) are all the same compound, with the familiar derivatives 分泌物 ("secretions"), 分泌腺 ("secretory gland"), and 内分泌 / 外分泌 ("endocrine" / "exocrine").

The bound character 泌 means "to seep or flow out" (also in 泌尿, "urinary"); 分 is "to separate out". In Dan'a'yo the word covers the physiological sense only; the liquid products themselves are named by [[体液]], [[汗]], [[涙]] and [[膿]]. No narrower or shifted sense is needed.
