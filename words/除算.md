## Notes
- [除](../characters/除.md) "divide" + [算 (char)](../characters/算%20(char).md) "to calculate" — "to divide," a near-synonym of [[除法]] but verb-oriented (the action of dividing) rather than naming the operation itself, parallel to how [[加算]]/[[減算]]/[[乗算]] pair with [[加法]]/[[減法]]/[[乗法]].

Japanese じょざん (jozan) and Korean 제산 (jesan) are the standard terms for the verb sense of division, matching 除's and 算's own on'yomi/Sino-Korean citations; modern Korean more often reaches for the native 나눗셈 in everyday speech, the same register split already seen across this whole X算 series. Mandarin chúsuàn and Cantonese ceoi4 syun3 are compositional. Vietnamese trừ toán is compositional (除's own trừ + 算's own toán, matching the pattern of [[加算]]'s gia toán and [[減算]]'s giảm toán); the living Vietnamese term is phép chia.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 제솬 diverges from Sino-Korean 제산 in the second syllable, since 算 independently carries `kwin: false` on its own character page. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄧㄙ⺢ㄋ unique among both words and characters).
