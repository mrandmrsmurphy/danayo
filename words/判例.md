## Notes
- [判](../characters/判.md) "judge, decide" + [例](../characters/例.md) "example"

判例 is a legal precedent: an earlier judgment that guides how later cases are decided. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin pànlì as "precedent" in the legal register, and Japanese はんれい and Korean 판례 with the same sense. Cantonese pun3 lai6 matches. It is shared across the sphere.

The word is literally "judgment example". It comes from the [判決](判決.md), the verdict, and it differs from [法律](法律.md) and [法典](法典.md), which are the written law. In a system that uses precedent, the 判例 is what the courts have said the law means.
