## Notes
- [時 (char)](../characters/時%20(char).md) "when; time (stand-in: itself)" + [机](../characters/机.md) "opportunity; occasion (stand-in: [[机会]])"

Wiktionary (時機): Chinese "time; timing; opportunity"; Japanese "opportunity, chance"; Korean 시기 is the hanja form, "time, opportunity, chance". Spelled with 机 as in [[机会]] and [[危机]], with 時機 as the alias. It is the bare "opportunity" noun that Events was missing, beside [[机会]], "a chance to act". Compare [[契机]].
