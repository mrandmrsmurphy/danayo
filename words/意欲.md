## Notes
- [意](../characters/意.md) "meaning, significance" + [欲](../characters/欲.md) "desire, want" — the will and desire to do something; drive

Wiktionary: Japanese 意欲 (いよく) "will, desire, ambition" and "will to, motivation to"; Korean 의욕 is the hanja form, "will, desire, ambition"; Chinese, literary, "to have the intention of; to intend to". Compare [[欲望]], [[意志]].
