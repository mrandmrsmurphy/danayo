## Notes
- [意](../characters/意.md) "meaning, significance" + [欲](../characters/欲.md) "desire, want" — the will and desire to do something; drive

意欲 is the inner drive toward an action or goal: motivation, eagerness, ambition.  意 supplies the intention or mind-set and 欲 the wanting, so the word names a desire that is directed at doing something, not a bodily craving.  It is the everyday word in education, work and sport (学習意欲, "motivation to learn"; 意欲的, "eager, enterprising"; 意欲を失う, "to lose one's drive"), and it is positive or neutral in tone.

It contrasts with [[欲望]], which is the broader and rawer craving, and with [[意志]], the will as a faculty of deciding.  That is why the vault places it on the border between Mind and Emotions.  Mandarin yìyù, Cantonese ji3 juk6, Japanese いよく and Korean 의욕 are the same compound; Japanese and Korean use it much more often than Mandarin does.  `kwin` is false because the Dan'a'yo 으욕 differs from the Korean 의욕.  No Vietnamese form was found attested, so none is given.
