## Notes
- [解 (char)](../characters/解%20(char).md) "undo, release" + [雇](../characters/雇.md) "hire" — dismissal.

解雇 is dismissal: an employer ending a worker's employment, to fire. It is "release from hire," the opposite of [[雇用]] (to employ). Japanese かいこ is a noun and する-verb for "discharge, dismissal, to fire, sack." Mandarin writes it 解僱 (jiěgù), which is its alias here; Cantonese gaai2 gu3 is the same compound, and Korean 해고 comes from memory. It differs from [[罷免]] and [[廃黜]], the removal of an official from office, and from [[退職]] and [[辞職]], where the worker is the one who leaves.
