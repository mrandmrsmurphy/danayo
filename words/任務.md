## Notes
- [任](../characters/任.md) "responsibility; duty (stand-in: [[責任]])" + [務](../characters/務.md) "serve; do one's duty (stand-in: [[服務]])"

Wiktionary: Chinese 任務 "task; assignment; job; mission"; Japanese 任務 "duty, task, mission"; Korean 임무 is the hanja form, "mission"; Vietnamese nhiệm vụ is the Han form, "task". It fills the "task" gap on Efforts, beside [[使命]] (mission) on Government.
