## Notes
- [岸 (char)](../characters/岸%20(char).md) "bank; shore" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; standalone form, 岸 is sufficiently free in Classical usage to serve as a Dan'a'yo word without a compound.

岸 names a riverbank or shore.

Mandarin àn, Cantonese ngon6, and Korean 안 (an) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese きし (kishi, on'yomi GAN reserved for compounds, already 岸's own stored `japanese_native` value) is the everyday standalone word. Vietnamese ngạn is the standard Hán Việt reading, one of three attested candidates on 岸's own page (alongside ngàn and ngan), well attested in compounds like hà ngạn ("riverbank").
