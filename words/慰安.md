## Notes
- [慰](../characters/慰.md) "comfort, console" + [安](../characters/安.md) "peaceful" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [慰](../characters/慰.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (安's own stand-in is [[平安]]).

慰安 means to comfort or console — soothing distress.

Mandarin wèi'ān, Cantonese wai3 on1, Japanese いあん, Korean 위안, and Vietnamese uý an are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄛㄧㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
