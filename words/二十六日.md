## Notes
- [[二十]] "twenty" + [六 (char)](../characters/六%20(char).md) "six" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — the twenty-sixth day of the month.

Mandarin èrshíliù rì, Cantonese ji6 sap6 luk6 jat6, Japanese にじゅうろくにち, and Korean 이십육일 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese filled with ngày hai mươi sáu (native "day twenty-six"), matching the same native-calendar-date convention already established on the rest of this [[二十一日|二十N日]] cluster.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 늬십룩닏 diverges from Sino-Korean 이십육일, since all four constituent characters independently carry `kwin: false` on their own character pages.

**Found and fixed missing citations**: `六(char).md` and `日(char).md` already had this word cited, but `二(char).md` and `十(char).md` did not — added to both.
