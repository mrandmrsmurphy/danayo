## Notes
- [黒 (char)](../characters/黒%20(char).md) "black" + [貂](../characters/貂.md) "marten" — legitimizing note: 貂's own `stand_in` is [[黒貂]] itself, already annotated in its own citation; 黒's own `stand_in` is 黒 itself, so transitivity fails — no `#cranberry`.

Mandarin hēidiāo and Cantonese hak1diu1 keep the compositional Sino reading. Japanese くろてん ("black marten") and Korean 검은담비 ("black marten") are both real native compounds rather than Sino readings. Sino-Vietnamese hắc điêu is the compositional form.
