## Notes
- [慧](../characters/慧%20(char).md) "bright, intelligent" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 慧 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

慧 means bright or intelligent — sharp-witted, wise.

Mandarin huì, Cantonese wai6, Japanese けい, Korean 혜, and Vietnamese tuệ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄏ⼔ㄧ/hwei/훼) with the unrelated [[毀]] ("destroy, slander").
