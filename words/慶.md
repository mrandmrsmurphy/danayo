## Notes
- [慶](../characters/慶%20(char).md) "congratulate, celebrate" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

慶 means to congratulate or celebrate, as in 慶祝 ("celebration").

Mandarin qìng, Cantonese hing3, Japanese けい, Korean 경, and Vietnamese khánh are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄎ⼶ㄫ/kyeng/켱) with [[卿]] ("noble, high officer") — completing the reciprocal callout its own Notes anticipated.
