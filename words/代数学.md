## Notes
- [代](../characters/代.md) "substitute" + [数](../characters/数.md) "number" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study, field of learning" — "algebra," the branch of mathematics itself, as distinct from [[代数]] ("algebra" as a subject/concept). Directly attested and standard across all five languages. `kwin` is true: 代, 数, and 学 all independently match their own Sino-Korean readings. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄧㄙㄨㄏㄚㄎ unique among both words and characters).
