## Notes
- [制](../characters/制.md) "restrict; control" + [裁](../characters/裁.md) "cut; judge, adjudicate"

制裁 is a sanction: a penalty imposed to punish a wrong or enforce a rule, whether by a court, a group or a state. Wiktionary glosses Japanese せいさい as "punishment; sanction" and Mandarin zhìcái as, in the legal register, "to impose legal sanctions", and, in the literary, "to punish; to penalise". Cantonese zai3 coi4 and Korean 제재 match.

It is broader than [刑罰](刑罰.md), the legal punishment for a crime, because it also covers sanctions that are not criminal, such as economic sanctions between states or a club's penalty on a member. It is related to [懲罰](懲罰.md) and [処分](処分.md).
