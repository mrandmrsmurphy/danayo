## Notes
- [労](../characters/労.md) "labor" + [動](../characters/動%20(char).md) "move, act" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [労](../characters/労.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (動's own stand-in is [[動]] itself, an unrelated word).

労動 means labor or work — physical or mental exertion, especially as an economic category (労動者 "worker, laborer," 労動力 "labor force"). Korean 로동 correctly preserves the unshifted North Korean/문화어 pronunciation (not the South Korean 두음법칙-shifted 노동).

Japanese ろうどう and Vietnamese lao động are both standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄚㄨㄉㄛㄫ unique across both words and characters).
