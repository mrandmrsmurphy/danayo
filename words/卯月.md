## Notes
- [卯](../characters/卯.md) "fourth Earthly Branch, rabbit" + [月](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — 卯's own `stand_in` is `名専字` ("name-exclusive character"), not a specific legitimizing word: 卯 genuinely appears only in zodiac/calendrical contexts (卯月, 卯年, 卯時, etc.) across the daughters, so no single stand-in word is required or expected (月 is its own stand-in).

Second month of the ancient Chinese lunar calendar, named for the fourth Earthly Branch 卯 (rabbit).

Early spring; the earth warms and life reappears. The rabbit's association with the moon made 卯月 auspicious for planting.

Japanese うつき reads both halves natively (卯's kun'yomi う + 月's kun'yomi つき) rather than the Sino-Japanese on'yomi concatenation ぼうげつ, matching the established zodiac-month convention (see [[寅月]], [[午月]]). Vietnamese tháng Mão likewise uses the native *tháng* plus the zodiac name. No homophones (注音 ㄇ⼘ㄨ·⼔ㄊ unique across both words and characters).
