## Notes
- [陶](../characters/陶.md) "pottery" + [器](../characters/器.md) "container, vessel" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes 陶 as a bound character (器's own stand-in is [[容器]]; transitivity fails, so no `#cranberry`).

陶器 means pottery or earthenware. Mandarin 陶器 (táoqì), Cantonese 陶器 (tou4hei3), Japanese 陶器 (とうき), Korean 도기 (dogi), and Sino-Vietnamese đào khí are all the standard technical/ceramics-industry term across the CJKV sphere, though everyday speech in each language often favors a native or broader alternative instead (질그릇, "earthenware," native Korean; đồ gốm, "ceramic ware," native Vietnamese) — both tracing to each constituent character's own native gloss.
