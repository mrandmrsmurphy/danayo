## Notes
- [平](../characters/平.md) "even; ordinary" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day; sun" — no stand-in relationship applies (平's own `stand_in` is [[水平]], 日's is itself).

平日 names an ordinary weekday, as opposed to a weekend or holiday — 平's "ordinary, plain" sense (rather than its "even, level" sense) applied to "day."

Japanese へいじつ, Korean 평일, and Vietnamese bình nhật are all standard, non-drifted readings. No homophones (注音 ㄅ⼶ㄫㄋㄧㄊ unique across both words and characters).
