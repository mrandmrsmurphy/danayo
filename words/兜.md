## Notes
- [兜 (char)](characters/兜%20(char).md) "helmet"

兜 is 會意 (𠑹 "cover" + 皃 "head"), depicting a head covering — a helmet. It appears in classical military vocabulary as 兜鍪 (dōumóu, an old compound term for "helmet") and, by later extension, in the verb sense "to wrap around, to encircle, to canvass/solicit" (兜圈子, "to go in circles"; 兜售, "to peddle, hawk goods").

Mandarin dōu, Cantonese dau1, and Japanese とう (tō, on'yomi, rare outside proper names) alongside native かぶと (kabuto, the everyday word for a traditional Japanese helmet) all preserve the sense. Korean 두 (du) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 투구 (tugu, "helmet"). Vietnamese đâu is the Hán Việt reading, attested in đâu mâu (兜鍪, the classical compound term for "helmet").

This word shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading (du/두/ㄉㄨ) with [[株]] ("stock") — the reciprocal half of this callout is now complete on both pages.
