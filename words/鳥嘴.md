## Notes
- [鳥 (char)](../characters/鳥%20(char).md) "bird" + [嘴](../characters/嘴.md) "mouth, beak" — legitimizing note: 嘴's own `stand_in` is [[鳥嘴]] itself, already annotated in its own citation; 鳥's own `stand_in` is 鳥 itself, so transitivity fails — no `#cranberry`.

Mandarin niǎozuǐ and Cantonese niu5zeoi2 keep the compositional Sino reading. Japanese くちばし and Korean 부리 are both native, everyday words for "beak" (matching 嘴's own `japanese_native`/`korean_native` fields exactly) rather than Sino readings. Sino-Vietnamese điểu chuỷ is the compositional form; everyday Vietnamese instead uses the native word mỏ.
