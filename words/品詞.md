## Notes
- [品](../characters/品%20(char).md) "article, item" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "word, expression" — no stand-in relationship applies (品 is its own stand-in; 詞's own is [[単詞]]).

品詞 means part of speech — the grammatical category of a word.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `mandarin`/`cantonese` had been contaminated with the reading of the alias 詞性 (cíxìng/ci4 sing3, "word nature/property," the more common modern Mandarin term for this concept) instead of 品詞's own compositional reading — corrected to pǐncí/ban2 ci4. Japanese ひんし and Korean 품사 are both standard readings, matching Dan'a'yo's own reading exactly (`kwin: true`). Vietnamese `từ loại` is the standard grammatical term, a native compound rather than a direct calque. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄨㄇㄙㄚ unique across both words and characters).
