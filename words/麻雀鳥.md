## Notes
- [麻](../characters/麻.md) "hemp" + [雀](../characters/雀.md) "sparrow" + [鳥 (char)](../characters/鳥%20(char).md) "bird" — legitimizing note: 雀's own `stand_in` is [[麻雀鳥]] itself, already annotated in its own citation; neither 麻's nor 鳥's own `stand_in` points here, so transitivity fails — no `#cranberry`.

The bare compound [[麻雀]] is deliberately reserved for "mahjong" in this vault, so the literal "sparrow" sense uses this extended, disambiguating form instead. Mandarin máquè and Cantonese maa4zoek3 are compositional (matching 麻雀's own reading, disambiguated here by the added 鳥). Japanese すずめ and Korean 참새 are both real native everyday words rather than Sino readings. Sino-Vietnamese ma tước điểu is the compositional form.
