## Notes
- [騎](../characters/騎.md) "ride" + [兵](../characters/兵.md) "soldier" — "mounted soldier." Neither character's own `stand_in` points here (騎's is [[騎馬]]; 兵's is [[兵士]]), so 騎兵 is an independent compound.

騎兵 is cavalry: soldiers who fight on horseback, the counterpart of [[歩兵]]. It is attested with the same characters in Chinese (Mandarin qíbīng, Cantonese kei4 bing1), Japanese (きへい), Korean (기병) and Vietnamese (kỵ binh). Modern Chinese also has slang senses, a pixel-censored film in Mandarin and a courier on a motorbike in Hong Kong; neither is Dan'a'yo's sense.
