## Notes
- [欲](../characters/欲.md) "desire, want" + [求](../characters/求.md) "request" — no stand-in relationship applies (欲's own stand-in is itself; 求's own is [[要求]]).

欲求 means to crave, desire, or lust after.

Mandarin yùqiú, Cantonese juk6 kau4, Japanese よっきゅう, Korean 욕구, and Vietnamese dục cầu are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 欲's and 求's own stored citations exactly).
