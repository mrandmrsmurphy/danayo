## Notes
- [十](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" + [五](../characters/五%20(char).md) "five" + [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — no stand-in relationship applies (all three characters are independent Dan'a'yo entries, each its own stand-in).

十五日 names the 15th day of the month. The 15th is 望日 in the lunar calendar — the full moon day.

Japanese じゅうごにち, Korean 십오일, and Vietnamese thập ngũ nhật are all standard, fully compositional readings.
