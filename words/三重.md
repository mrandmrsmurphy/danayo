## Notes
- [三](../characters/三%20(char).md) "three" + [重](../characters/重%20(char).md) "heavy"

三重 is Mie, written "three layers". The Kojiki says that the dying Yamato Takeru, exhausted on his journey, said that his legs had become "threefold" (三重) like a bent knot, and that the place was named for this; the reading みえ comes from that story, not from the usual reading of the characters (from memory, not re-checked). In Mandarin the second character is read chóng "layer".

Mie runs along the east coast of the Kii Peninsula, with its capital Tsu. It contains the Ise Grand Shrine (Ise Jingū), the Suzuka motor-racing circuit, the home area of the Iga ninja, and Matsusaka beef.

In Dan'a'yo 三重 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 미에 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
