## Notes
- [一 (char)](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" + [朝 (char)](../characters/朝%20(char).md) "morning" — literally "one morning," hence "a short time, a brief moment" — the same "single sunrise-to-sunrise span" logic behind English "overnight" as a byword for suddenness.

`vietnamese: nhất triêu` (confirmed via web search) picks the morning-sense reading matching this word's own "short time" gloss.
