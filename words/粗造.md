## Notes
- [粗](../characters/粗.md) "coarse" + [造](../characters/造.md) "to make" — here 造 stands in as a 代用字 for 糙 ("rough, unpolished"), which is too rare to create and is an alias of 造. 粗's own stand-in is this exact compound, legitimizing it as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 造's own stand-in is [[創造]].

粗造 describes something coarse, rough, or unrefined in texture or finish — a doubled reinforcement of near-synonymous characters, both meaning "coarse" (粗 of coarsely-hulled grain, 糙 of unpolished/unmilled rice specifically). The source-language spelling 粗糙 is kept as an alias.

Mandarin cūcāo, Cantonese cou1 cou3, Japanese そぞう (sozō) and Korean 조조 (jojo) are the real-world readings of 粗糙. The Dan'a'yo reading is the concatenation of 粗 and 造 (조찻), which differs from the Korean 조조, so `kwin` is false. The `vietnamese` field is left unset.
