## Notes
- [授](../characters/授.md) "confer, award, grant" + [業](../characters/業%20(char).md) "occupation, work" — no stand-in relationship applies (授's own stand-in is [[授与]]; 業's own is itself).

授業 means to teach, instruct, or give a lesson.

Mandarin shòuyè, Cantonese sau6 jip6, Japanese じゅぎょう, Korean 수업, and Vietnamese thò nghiệp are all standard, fully compositional readings. Removed a false homophone claim: this page previously claimed a collision with [[守衛]], but that was based on 守衛's own frontmatter being contaminated with this word's reading (since fixed on 守衛's side, 2026-08-26) — no real homophones exist at this reading (注音 ㄙ⼜·ㄝㄆ unique across both words and characters).
