## Notes
- [際](../characters/際%20(char).md) "border, occasion" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 際 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

際 means border, occasion (as in 交際 "socialize," 国際 "international").

Mandarin jì, Cantonese zai3, Korean 제, and Vietnamese tế are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 際's own stored citations exactly.
