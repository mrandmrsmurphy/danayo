## Notes
- [石](../characters/石%20(char).md) "rock" + [川](../characters/川%20(char).md) "river"

石川 is Ishikawa, written "stone river", from the old Ishikawa district. The reading いしかわ is native: 石 (いし, "stone") plus 川 (かわ, "river") voiced to がわ. The characters read with the same meaning in every language, but the place is Japanese and not Chinese.

Ishikawa is centred on Kanazawa, whose garden Kenroku-en and gold-leaf craft are famous, and on the Noto Peninsula, which suffered a severe earthquake on 1 January 2024. The area also produces Kutani porcelain and lacquerware.

In Dan'a'yo 石川 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 이시카와 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
