## Notes
- [幅](../characters/幅.md) "breadth, width, range" + [度](../characters/度.md) "degree, manner" — this word is the stand-in for [幅](../characters/幅.md), which cannot appear independently.

幅度 means range, extent, or scope. The character 幅 alone means breadth or range, but is too bounded to stand by itself in Dan'a'yo, so this compound serves as its viable form.
