## Notes
- [星 (char)](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" + [空 (char)](../characters/空%20(char).md) "sky"

星空 is the starry sky: the sky as seen at night with its stars. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xīngkōng as "starry sky". Cantonese sing1 hung1 matches, Korean 성공 is the reading of the characters, and no Japanese reading is attested.

It is the night view of the [天空](天空.md), sky, and is made of its [星](星.md), stars.
