## Notes
- [新 (char)](../characters/新%20(char).md) "new" + [郎](../characters/郎.md) "young man; groom" — groom.

新郎 is the groom: the man at his wedding. It is "new-young man," the exact pair of [[新婦]] (bride), as in 新郎新婦. Mandarin xīnláng (also 新郎官), Cantonese san1 long4, Japanese しんろう and Korean 신랑 are all confirmed on Wiktionary. It is distinct from [[婿]], which is the son-in-law, the groom seen from the bride's family; Dan'a'yo keeps both.
