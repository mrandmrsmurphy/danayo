## Notes
- [律](../characters/律.md) "rule" + [詩](../characters/詩.md) "poem"

律詩 is regulated verse: the Chinese poem of eight lines bound by rules of tone and parallelism. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin lǜshī as "regulated verse" and Japanese りっし as "ancient Chinese verse". Korean 률시 and Vietnamese luật thi are the same word. Cantonese leot6 si1 matches.

It is the long sibling of the four-line [絶句](絶句.md), and both are the classical forms of [唐詩](唐詩.md), Tang poetry.
