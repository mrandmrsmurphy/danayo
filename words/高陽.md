## Notes
- [高](../characters/高%20(char).md) "high" + [陽](../characters/陽%20(char).md) "sun; yang" — the name of Goyang, a city of South Korea north-west of Seoul.

高陽 (Goyang) is a large city of Gyeonggi Province ([[京畿]]道) in South Korea, just north-west of [[漢城]] (Seoul), and the site of the Ilsan new town (from memory, not re-checked). The name reads "high sun".

English and Chinese Wiktionary list 高陽 first as Goyang in South Korea (Mandarin Gāoyáng, Cantonese gou1 joeng4), then as Gaoyang County in Hebei, a town in Hubei, and in Chinese mythology an alternate name of Zhuanxu (顓頊). Neither English nor Korean Wiktionary lists a Korean entry, so the Korean reading 고양 is composed from the character pages. The Japanese reading こうよう is likewise composed from the on'yomi. Vietnamese Cao Dương is composed from the character pages; the string Cao Dương on the Vietnamese Wikipedia is a commune in Phú Thọ, unrelated.
