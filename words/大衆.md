## Notes
- [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big, great" + [衆](../characters/衆.md) "crowd, multitude" — "the masses, the public" (大衆文化, "popular/mass culture"). `vietnamese: đại chúng` is a directly attested, extremely common standard Vietnamese term with the same sense (văn hóa đại chúng, "mass/popular culture").
