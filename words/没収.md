## Notes
- [没](../characters/没%20(char).md) "sink, confiscate" + [収](../characters/収.md) "receive, take in" — "take away." Their own `stand_in` values: 没's is itself; 収's is [[回収]]; none points here, so 没収 is an independent compound.

没収 is to confiscate: to take property from its owner by authority, or to forfeit it. Mandarin mòshōu (沒收), Cantonese mut6 sau1, Japanese ぼっしゅう and Korean 몰수 all use it. Compare [[窃取]] (to steal), which takes without authority.
