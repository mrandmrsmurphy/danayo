## Notes
- [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "multiply" + [法 (char)](../characters/法%20(char).md) "method, operation" — literally "multiplying method," the multiplication operation.

乗法 refers to multiplication as a mathematical operation, parallel to 加法 (addition), 減法 (subtraction), and 除法 (division). This is the Mandarin/Cantonese-preferred term for the same concept [[乗算]] names in Japanese/Korean — both compounds are built on the same character 乗/乘 (simplified/traditional variant forms of the same character, sharing the same `乗 (char)` page) paired with a near-synonymous second element (法 "method" vs 算 "calculate"). Mandarin chéng fǎ, Cantonese sing4 faat3, Japanese じょうほう, and Korean 승법 are all standard, fully compositional terms. **Vietnamese content note**: filled with phép nhân, the same real everyday Vietnamese term already established on [[乗算]], rather than a compositional-but-unattested "thừa pháp" — Vietnamese doesn't distinguish a separate term for the 法-vs-算 register split the way Chinese/Japanese do.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 숭빱 diverges from Sino-Korean 승법, since both 乗 and 法 independently carry `kwin: false` on their own character pages.
