## Notes
- [光 (char)](../characters/光%20(char).md) "light" + [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year"

光年 is a light-year: the distance light travels in a year, used to measure the space between stars. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin guāngnián and Japanese こうねん as "light year". Cantonese gwong1 nin4 matches, and Korean 광년 matches.

The distance to a [恒星](恒星.md) is counted in these, and a [銀河系](銀河系.md) is tens of thousands across. The unit is built from [光](光.md), light, and [年](年.md), year.
