## Notes
- [黒 (char)](../characters/黒%20(char).md) "black" + [洞](../characters/洞.md) "cave, ravine, vale" — no `#cranberry`: 黒's own `stand_in` is 黒 itself, 洞's is [[洞窟]]; neither points here.

Mandarin hēidòng and Cantonese hak1dung6 keep the compositional Sino reading, a modern coinage built the same way across Chinese/Japanese/Korean independently. Japanese ブラックホール and Korean 블랙홀 are both direct phonetic loanwords from English "black hole." Vietnamese hố đen ("hole-black") is the real, everyday native term, reversing the Sino word order.
