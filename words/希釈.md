## Notes
- [希](../characters/希.md) "rare, hope" + [釈](../characters/釈.md) "explain, interpret" — 希 here is "thin, sparse" and 釈 "release, dissolve", so the compound is "to thin out and dissolve"

Wiktionary: Japanese 希釈 (きしゃく) "dilution" and, as a verb, "to dilute"; Chinese 稀釋 "to dilute (a liquid); to thin out", kept with 希釋 as aliases here.
