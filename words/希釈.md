## Notes
- [希](../characters/希.md) "rare, hope" + [釈](../characters/釈.md) "explain, interpret" — 希 here is "thin, sparse" and 釈 "release, dissolve", so the compound is "to thin out and dissolve"

希釈 means to dilute: to lower the concentration of a solution by adding solvent, usually water.  希 contributes thinness, a state made sparse, and 釈 the sense of loosening or dissolving, as in 解釈 and 釈放, so the compound is the act of thinning something out.  Typical uses are 希釈液 (a diluted solution), 希釈倍率 (dilution ratio) and 希釈して飲む ("drink it diluted"), and in finance and law the same word names the dilution of shareholdings, 株式の希釈.  Its opposite is concentration (濃縮).

The traditional spelling is 稀釋, with 稀 in place of 希 and 釋 in place of 釈, and the mixed form 希釋 also occurs; both are listed as aliases.  Mandarin xīshì, Cantonese hei1 sik1, Japanese きしゃく and Korean 희석 are the same compound with the same sense, and the Dan'a'yo form 희석 matches the Korean, so `kwin` is true.  No Vietnamese form was found attested, so none is given.
