## Notes
- [肉 (char)](../characters/肉%20(char).md) "meat" + [汁 (char)](../characters/汁%20(char).md) "soup, juice" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both are stand-ins for themselves), so `#cranberry` does not apply.

肉汁 means gravy or broth — literally "meat juice."

Mandarin ròuzhī, Cantonese juk6 zap1, Japanese にくじる (nikujiru), and Korean 육즙 (yukjeup) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 肉汁 is itself a common attested word for "meat juice, gravy" in all four languages. Vietnamese nhục trấp is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading.
