## Notes
- [肉 (char)](../characters/肉%20(char).md) "meat" + [汁 (char)](../characters/汁%20(char).md) "soup, juice" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both are stand-ins for themselves), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 肉 is individually `kwin: false`.

肉汁 means gravy or broth — literally "meat juice."

Mandarin ròuzhī, Cantonese juk6 zap1, Japanese にくじる (nikujiru), and Korean 육즙 (yukjeup) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 肉汁 is itself a common attested word for "meat juice, gravy" in all four languages. Vietnamese nhục trấp is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. **Fixed two real bugs**: `羅馬字`/`諺文` had nugjib/눅집 (mismatching 汁's real -ǝ- vowel reading, jǝb/즙) — `注音` (ㄋㄨㄎㄐㄜㄆ) had stayed correct throughout; `korean`/`vietnamese` had 그레이비 (an English loanword transliteration of "gravy") and nước chấm (an unrelated native Vietnamese dipping-sauce term) rather than 肉汁's own compositional readings — corrected. No homophones.
