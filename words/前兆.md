## Notes
- [前 (char)](../characters/前%20(char).md) "before, front" + [兆](../characters/兆.md) "omen, sign" — "a premonitory sign, harbinger, omen." Mandarin qiánzhào, Cantonese cin4 siu6, Japanese ぜんちょう, and Korean 전조 are all directly attested standard readings. `vietnamese: tiền triệu` is the directly attested Sino-Vietnamese compound (前's own tiền + 兆's own triệu); the everyday Vietnamese word for "omen" is simply điềm. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 전잣 diverges from Sino-Korean 전조 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄋㄐㄚㄨ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 兆's own `stand_in` is this exact word (前兆), so 兆 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 前兆.
