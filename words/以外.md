## Notes
- [以 (char)](../characters/以%20(char).md) "by means of; (as a boundary marker) from X onward" + [外](../characters/外.md) "outside" — "outside the limit set by X". Neither character's own `stand_in` points here (以's is itself, 外's is [外部](外部.md)).

以外 means other than, except for, apart from. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 以外 (Mandarin yǐwài, Cantonese ji5 ngoi6) as "apart from; other than; except for; beyond; excluding; in addition" and "external", and Japanese いがい as "apart from; excepting (something)". It has no Korean or Vietnamese entry, so the Korean 이외 and Vietnamese dĩ ngoại fields are built from the readings of the two characters. Compare [以前](以前.md), [以後](以後.md) and [除外](除外.md).
