## Notes
- [同](../characters/同.md) "same" + [意](../characters/意.md) "meaning, intention" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (同's is 同一, 意's is 意味).

同意 is chiefly a verb ("to agree, consent"; 同意する in Japanese). Mandarin tóngyì, Cantonese tung4 ji3, Japanese どうい, Korean 동의, and Vietnamese đồng ý are the standard readings.
