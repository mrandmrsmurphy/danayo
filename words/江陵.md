## Notes
- [江](../characters/江.md) "river" + [陵](../characters/陵.md) "mausoleum, hill" — the name of Gangneung, a city of South Korea on the east coast.

江陵 (Gangneung) is a city on the east coast of Gangwon Province ([[江原]]特別自治道) in South Korea, and with [[原州]] (原州, Wonju) one of the two towns that gave the province its name (from memory, not re-checked). It hosted ice events of the 2018 Winter Olympics (from memory, not re-checked).

English and Chinese Wiktionary list 江陵 first as Jiangling County in Hubei, China, then as an archaic name for Jingzhou ([[荊州]]), and third as the Korean city (Mandarin Jiānglíng, Cantonese gong1 ling4); the Chinese Jiangling is a different place under the same spelling. English Wiktionary's Japanese section gives the reading カンヌン (from the Korean) and a dated reading こうりょう, which is taken here as the Sino-Xenic reading. Korean 강릉 is the usual spelling in both North and South Korea. Vietnamese Giang Lăng is the Hán-Việt name the Vietnamese Wikipedia gives for the Chinese Jiangling.
