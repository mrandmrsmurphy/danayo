## Notes
- [婚](../characters/婚.md) "marry; marriage" + [費 (char)](../characters/費%20(char).md) "spend; expense" — marriage expenses.

婚費 is the cost of getting married: the expenses of a wedding and the setting up of a marriage. It is "marriage-expense" from [[婚]] and [[費]]. No entry was found on Wiktionary in any language, and no language field is given. Japanese family law has the longer 婚姻費用 (konin hiyō, the living expenses of spouses), of which 婚費 is an abbreviation in my memory only; Dan'a'yo does not take that sense. It is a Dan'a'yo-made word, built templatically on the pattern of other compounds in 費, and carries the `neologism` tag. Compare [[嫁資]] (dowry) and [[聘金]] (bride-price), the two payments between the families, and [[結婚礼]] (the wedding).
