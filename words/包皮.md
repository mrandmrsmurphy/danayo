## Notes
- [包](../characters/包.md) "wrap, pack" + [皮](../characters/皮.md) "skin, hide, outer layer" — no stand-in relationship applies (包's own stand-in is [[包装]], 皮's is [[皮革]]).

包皮 names the foreskin (prepuce), the fold of skin covering the glans. **Real bug found and fixed**: `mandarin` was truncated ("bāop") — corrected to bāopí. Japanese ほうひ and Korean 포피 are standard readings; Vietnamese `bao bì` is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading, though it has drifted in modern everyday usage to mean "packaging" generally, with "bao quy đầu" preferred in medical contexts specifically for foreskin. No homophones (注音 ㄅ⼘ㄨㄅㄧ unique across both words and characters).
