## Notes
- [尊](../characters/尊.md) "honor; respect" + [重 (char)](../characters/重%20(char).md) "heavy; weighty"

尊重 is "to respect; to hold in esteem," "to value an idea," and in Chinese also "serious, proper." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 尊重 (zūnzhòng, Cantonese zyun1 zung6) is a verb "to respect (someone)" and "to value (an idea)," also "serious" of behavior and, literary, "noble"; Japanese そんちょう is "respect, esteem"; Korean 존중 is "respect, esteem"; and Vietnamese tôn trọng is "to respect; to honour; to revere." It is used in the 1919 Korean Declaration of Independence ([宣言書](../translation/宣言書.md)) (秩序를 尊重하야). Related: [尊敬](尊敬.md) "respect."
