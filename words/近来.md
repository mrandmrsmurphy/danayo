## Notes
Mandarin jìnlái, cantonese gan6 loi4-2, and japanese きんらい are all directly attested standard readings. **Found and fixed a real-equivalent-instead-of-own-reading bug on `korean`**: the stored value was a garbled multi-value string "근래, 요사이, 요즈음" mashing the honest compositional reading (근래) together with two unrelated native Korean words for "recently" (요사이, 요즈음) — trimmed to just 근래. Filled a blank `vietnamese` with honest compositional "cận lai." `近來` is the standard traditional-character spelling variant (来→來), correctly listed as an alias. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 긴래 diverges from Sino-Korean 근래 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄋㄌㄚㄧ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 近's `stand_in` (近 itself) nor 来's own `stand_in` (来 itself) points to this compound.
