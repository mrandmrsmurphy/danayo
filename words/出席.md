## Notes
"To attend, be present" (a meeting, ceremony). `vietnamese: xuất tịch` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese (confirmed via web search), meaning "to attend, participate" — everyday Vietnamese more often reaches for tham dự (a different root) for this concept, but xuất tịch is a genuine, if less common, synonym. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 춛석 matches Sino-Korean 출석 exactly.
