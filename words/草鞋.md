## Notes
- [草 (char)](../characters/草%20(char).md) "grass" + [鞋 (char)](../characters/鞋%20(char).md) "shoe"

草鞋 is a straw sandal. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 草鞋 (Mandarin cǎoxié, Cantonese cou2 haai4) as "straw sandal" and Japanese 草鞋 as "traditional Japanese straw sandals"; the Japanese word is read わらじ, a native reading, and the Japanese field here gives the Sino-Japanese そうあい. The Korean 초혜 and Vietnamese thảo hài fields are built from the readings of the two characters. The ordinary shoe is the [鞋](鞋.md).
