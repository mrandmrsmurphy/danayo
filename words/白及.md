## Notes
- [白 (char)](../characters/白%20(char).md) "white" + [及 (char)](../characters/及%20(char).md) "reach out to"

白及 is the Chinese ground orchid, *Bletilla striata*. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 白及 (alias 白芨; Mandarin báijí, Cantonese baak6 kap6) as "bai ji", a Chinese orchid whose pseudobulbs are used in traditional Chinese medicine and which is also widely grown for its flowers and foliage. It glosses Japanese はっきゅう as the rhizomes of *Bletilla striata* used in medicine. Korean 백급 and Vietnamese bạch cập are built from the readings of the two characters.

Wikipedia gives the plant as native to Japan, Korea, Myanmar and China, with magenta-pink flowers. It is used in Asian traditional medicine for ulcers of the alimentary canal, and as a natural glue for making the silk strings of instruments such as the guqin.
