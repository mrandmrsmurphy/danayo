## Notes
- [一](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" + [億](../characters/億.md) "hundred million" — legitimizing note: 億's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, so 億 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word; 一's own `stand_in` is 一 itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply.

Mandarin yīyì, Cantonese jat1 jik1, Korean 일억, and Sino-Vietnamese ức (used alone, without a "one" prefix, matching the same pattern already established on [[一万]]'s vạn) are all standard readings. `kwin` is false (AND-rule: both 一 and 億 individually false). **Fixed a real bug**: `japanese` was `いち かた`, with a stray space and かた matching neither of 億's own stored on'yomi (OKU/YOKU) — corrected to いちおく (ICHI + OKU), the real, standard Japanese word for "hundred million." Removed the duplicate `品詞` key. See [Numbers](../lexipedia/Numbers.md).
