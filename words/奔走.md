## Notes
- [奔](../characters/奔.md) "run around" + [走](../characters/走%20(char).md) "run" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [奔](../characters/奔.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (走's own stand-in is itself).

奔走 means to run around or walk quickly — a near-synonymous doubling of two characters both meaning "to run," extended to the sense of bustling about or exerting effort on someone's behalf (as in [[東奔西走]], "running about in all directions").

Mandarin bēnzǒu, Cantonese ban1 zau2, Japanese ほんそう, Korean 분주, and Vietnamese bôn tẩu are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `羅馬字` had 走's syllable mistyped as "jou" instead of "sou" (諺文/注音 were already correct). No homophones (注音 ㄅㄛㄋㄙㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).
