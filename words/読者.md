## Notes
- [読](../characters/読.md) "read" + [者](../characters/者.md) "person"

読者 is a reader: a person who reads a book, newspaper or other text. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin dúzhě as "reader (of books, periodicals, magazines, etc.)" and Japanese どくしゃ as "reader". Korean 독자 and Vietnamese độc giả are the same word. Cantonese duk6 ze2 matches.

The person reading to themself is a reader; the one watching is the [観客](観客.md), audience member. Its counterpart is the [着者](着者.md), author, and the act is [読書](読書.md).
