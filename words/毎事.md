## Notes
- [毎 (char)](../characters/毎%20(char).md) "every; each; distributive" + [事 (char)](../characters/事%20(char).md) "act; matter; affair"

毎事 is the distributive correlative in the 事 ("matter") column of the correlative table: "each matter, every matter one by one, in each case". The character [毎 (char)](../characters/毎%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *moi*, ㄇㄛㄧ; its alias 每 is the traditional form) is the distributive morpheme, the same one that appears in [毎日](毎日.md) "every day", [毎週](毎週.md) "every week", [毎月](毎月.md) "every month" and [毎年](毎年.md) "every year"; the free word is [毎](毎.md). [事 (char)](../characters/事%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *ji*, ㄐㄧ) supplies "act; matter; affair". 毎事 extends the 毎- pattern to the matter slot: the predicate is applied to each matter in turn, and each case can be checked separately.

Its part of speech is 副用名詞 (adverbial noun), the class of every other cell of the 毎 row ([毎人](毎人.md), [毎物](毎物.md), [毎処](毎処.md), [毎名](毎名.md), [毎時](毎時.md), [毎様](毎様.md), [毎多](毎多.md), [毎類](毎類.md)) and of [毎日](毎日.md). `grammar/Linguistics Features.md` (line 68) states the rule: an adverbial noun may be the topic or the frame of a clause with no case or adverbial marking, but needs marking when it modifies the main verb. So 毎事 can open a clause as a frame. The other cells of this 事 column, [此事](此事.md), [何事](何事.md) and [皆事](皆事.md) among them, are 代詞 (pronouns), so 毎事 is the one cell of the column that is adverbial. Illustrative only (invented, not from `grammar/`): 毎事、尽力 "in every matter, do one's best".

It contrasts with its column neighbours as follows. The collective [皆事](皆事.md) "all matters" treats the matters as one totality, whereas 毎事 distributes over them one at a time; the same distributive/collective split runs through the whole table, as in [毎人](毎人.md) against 皆人. The interrogative [何事](何事.md) asks which matter, and [某事](某事.md) names an unspecified matter, so both are indefinite where 毎事 is quantificational. The deictic cells [此事](此事.md) "this matter", [其事](其事.md) "that matter" (medial) and [彼事](彼事.md) "that matter yonder" (distal) each point at one particular matter, while 毎事 ranges over all of them.

The cross-linguistic fields were rebuilt from Wiktionary (checked 2026-10-10). Mandarin 每事 (měishì) has an entry, a noun glossed "everything; every matter; all things", with 事事 as a synonym; 每事 is the `aliases` form (there is no separate 每事 file in `words/`). Korean 매사 (maesa) has an entry, derived from 每事, a noun glossed "everything" and an adverb glossed "in every affair"; the `korean` field holds this Sino-Korean reading, 毎's 매 + 事's 사. Wiktionary has no entry for the Japanese 毎事 (the shinjitai form), so no `japanese` field is entered; any claim that it is rare or unused in modern Japanese is from memory, not re-checked. Cantonese and Vietnamese fields are omitted because no verified reading was found (Vietnamese *mỗi việc* "every matter" is from memory, not re-checked; hvdic.thivien.net covers single characters, not phrases). A classical use of the same pair is 每事問 "asked about everything", from the Analects, which Wiktionary lists as an idiom (the citation is Wiktionary's, and the Analects text itself was not re-read for this page). The earlier text's claim of a classical 每事必謀 was not found in any source checked and has been removed.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md)'s correlative table (line 125; the same chart is in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`, line 144 in the 事 ("matter") row, with the column header cell 毎 on line 142), 毎事 is the first cell of the 毎 ("every") row, followed by [毎物](毎物.md) "each thing", [毎人](毎人.md) "per person", [毎名](毎名.md) "each honoured person", [毎処](毎処.md) "at each place", [毎時](毎時.md) "each time", [毎様](毎様.md) "each manner", [毎多](毎多.md) "each amount" and [毎類](毎類.md) "each kind". The table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), 63 forms, so 毎事 is parsed compositionally: 毎 = distributive, 事 = matter. Dan'a'yo keeps the sense shared across CJKV, "every matter; each matter"; no narrowing is needed.

The 注音 ㄇㄛㄧㄐㄧ carries no syllable-boundary dot. `grammar/文法 - 02音韻論.md` allows only ㄇ ㄋ ㄫ, the glides and the unreleased stops ㄆ ㄊ ㄎ as syllable-final consonants, and ㄐ is none of them, so ㄐㄧ must be a syllable of its own and ㄇㄛㄧ is the preceding syllable (it exists in `syllables/`, as the syllable of 毎 itself). The only other segmentation would split the diphthong ㄛㄧ into two syllables, ㄇㄛ + ㄧ + ㄐㄧ; the vault treats ㄛㄧ as the single rhyme here, as in the sibling cells such as [毎物](毎物.md), which carry no dot either. This is the vault's convention rather than a rule stated in the grammar file.
