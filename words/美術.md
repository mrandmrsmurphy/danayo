## Notes
- [美 (char)](../characters/美%20(char).md) "beauty" + [術 (char)](../characters/術%20(char).md) "art"

美術 is fine art: art made to be looked at, such as painting and sculpture. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin měishù as "fine arts" and Japanese びじゅつ as "fine arts". Korean 미술 and Vietnamese mĩ thuật are the same word. Cantonese mei5 seot6 matches.

It is the visual part of [芸術](芸術.md), art, and its works are shown in the [美術館](美術館.md), art museum. The quality it seeks is [美](美.md), beauty.
