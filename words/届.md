## Notes
- [届 (char)](../characters/届%20(char).md) "deliver; arrive" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [届 (char)](../characters/届%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

届 means to deliver or to arrive/reach — a notification or document that has been submitted and reached its destination (as in the everyday Japanese noun 届け, "notification/report form," and 届く, "to reach, arrive").

Japanese かい, Korean 계, and Vietnamese giới are all standard readings. **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 届 and [[皆]] ("all, every") share an identical reading (gye/겨/ㄍ⼶) despite being semantically unrelated — added reciprocal homophone callouts to both pages (皆.md had been stamped since June 2026 without ever carrying one).
