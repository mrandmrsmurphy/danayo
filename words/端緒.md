## Notes
- [端](../characters/端.md) "end, extreme, edge" + [緒](../characters/緒.md) "state of mind" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (端's is [[末端]], 緒's is [[心緒]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply.

端緒 means a clue or the beginning thread of something — literally "the end of a thread" (端 "tip, edge" + 緒 "thread's end"), a metaphor for the first trace or starting point that lets one unravel or trace back to a fuller understanding.

Mandarin duānxù, Cantonese dyun1 seoi5, Japanese たんしょ (tansho), and Korean 단서 (danseo, the standard everyday Korean word for "clue") are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese đoan tự is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form.
