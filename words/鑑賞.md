## Notes
- [鑑](../characters/鑑.md) "mirror; examine" + [賞 (char)](../characters/賞%20(char).md) "admire; enjoy"

鑑賞 is to appreciate a work of art: to look at, listen to or read it with understanding and pleasure. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin jiànshǎng as "to appreciate" and Japanese かんしょう as "appreciation, enjoyment, especially of art, music, etc.". Korean 감상 is the same word. Cantonese gaam3 soeng2 matches.

It is what a [観客](観客.md) or [読者](読者.md) does with a [作品](作品.md); judging a work is [批評](批評.md), criticism.
