## Notes
- [包](../characters/包.md) "wrap, pack" + [含](../characters/含.md) "include, contain" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [含](../characters/含.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (包's own stand-in is [[包装]]).

包含 means to include or contain — encompassing something as a part of a larger whole.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `vietnamese` was malformed ("baohán", missing the space and with the wrong tone) — corrected to `bao hàm` (compositional from both characters' own readings). Japanese ほうがん and Korean 포함 are both standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄅ⼘ㄨㄏㄚㄇ unique across both words and characters).
