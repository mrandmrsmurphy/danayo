## Etymology
- [宰](../characters/宰.md) "superintend" + [相](../characters/相.md) "mutual, minister"

## Notes
- "Supreme chancellor, prime minister, premier" — the monarch's highest advisor and head of all officials in feudal/imperial China, with the specific title varying by dynasty. `vietnamese: tể tướng` is directly attested (相's own Vietnamese reading includes tướng, "minister/general").
