## Notes
- [誣](../characters/誣.md) "falsely accuse" + [告](../characters/告.md) "inform; report"

誣告 means to accuse falsely: to bring a false charge against someone, or the false accusation itself. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 誣告 (an alias, 诬告; Mandarin wūgào, also wúgào, Cantonese mou4 gou3) as "to lodge a false accusation against; to bring a false charge against; to trump up a charge against", and Korean 무고 as "a false accusation, a false charge". It has no Japanese entry, so Japanese ふこく here is built from the characters, and it is a homophone of 布告, "proclamation" (Japanese ふこく, which Wiktionary lists); that does not affect Dan'a'yo.

This word is the viable form of [誣](../characters/誣.md): the character's meaning (to falsely accuse) is identical, but 誣 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. The compound adds 告, to report or charge, specifying the legal act of filing a false complaint. Its neutral counterpart is [[告訴]], to accuse, and slander in general is [[誹謗]].
