## Notes
- [中 (char)](../characters/中%20(char).md) "China" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "Japan" + [韓](../characters/韓.md) "Korea" + [越 (char)](../characters/越%20(char).md) "Vietnam" — the standard Sinographic initialism for "Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and Vietnamese," the four cultures/languages this vault's own project is built around.

Mandarin Zhōng Rì Hán Yuè, Cantonese zung1 jat6 hon4 jyut6, Korean 중일한월, Japanese ちゅうにっかんえつ, and Vietnamese Trung-Nhật-Hàn-Việt are all standard compositional readings. **Fixed several real bugs**: `cantonese` used non-breaking spaces instead of regular spaces and was missing 越's own syllable entirely; `mandarin` had a tone-mark placement typo (`Yùe`→`Yuè`, matching 越's own stored `yuè`). Fixed a missing citation on `characters/韓.md`'s Words list and the duplicate `品詞` field.
