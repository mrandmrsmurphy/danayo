## Notes
- [中](../characters/中.md) "China" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "Japan" + [韓](../characters/韓.md) "Korea" + [越 (char)](../characters/越%20(char).md) "Vietnam" — the standard Sinographic initialism for "Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and Vietnamese," the four cultures/languages this vault's own project is built around.

Mandarin Zhōng Rì Hán Yuè, Cantonese zung1 jat6 hon4 jyut6, Korean 중일한월, Japanese ちゅうにっかんえつ, and Vietnamese Trung-Nhật-Hàn-Việt are all standard compositional readings.
