## Notes
- [減](../characters/減.md) "reduce" + [少 (char)](../characters/少%20(char).md) "few; little"

減少 is to decrease: to become fewer or less in amount. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin jiǎnshǎo as "to reduce; to decrease; to cut; to minimise" and Japanese げんしょう as "reduction, decrease, fall, decline". Vietnamese giảm thiểu is the same word in Hán Việt form, and Korean 감소 and Cantonese gaam2 siu2 match.

It is the opposite of [増加](増加.md), increase. It counts how much, where [縮小](縮小.md) lessens the size of a thing.
