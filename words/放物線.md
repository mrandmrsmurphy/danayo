## Notes
- [放](../characters/放.md) "throw, release" + [物 (char)](../characters/物%20(char).md) "object" + [線](../characters/線.md) "line" — literally "the line of a thrown object," describing the trajectory curve of a projectile; a parabola. One of the four <ruby>[圓錐曲線](/words/圓錐曲線.md)<rt>⼔ㄋㄐㄨㄧㄎ⼘ㄎㄙ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby> (conic sections).

Mandarin pāowùxiàn, Cantonese fong3 mat6 sin3, Japanese ほうぶつせん, and Korean 포물선 are all directly attested, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese parabôn is a real, standard loanword from "parabol/parabole," not a compositional calque. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 방묻션 diverges from Sino-Korean 포물선. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄫㄇㄨㄊㄙ⼶ㄋ unique among both words and characters). Citations on 放.md, 物(char).md, and 線.md's own Words lists were already correctly present. Filled a completely missing `cantonese` field with the honest compositional reading.
