## Etymology
- [跳](../characters/跳.md) "hop, jump" + [舞](../characters/舞.md) "dance"

## Notes
Mandarin tiàowǔ and cantonese tiu3 mou5 are directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese khiêu vũ is likewise a directly attested standard loanword (跳's own khiêu + 舞's own vũ). Japanese とうぶ and korean 도무 are both honest compositional readings (跳's own TOU/도 + 舞's own BU/무) rather than the everyday native terms for "dance" (Japanese ダンス/踊る, Korean 춤/춤추다) — とうぶ is independently attested via the medical term 跳舞病 (とうぶびょう, "chorea").
