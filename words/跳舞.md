## Etymology
- [跳](../characters/跳.md) "hop, jump" + [舞](../characters/舞.md) "dance"

## Notes
Mandarin tiàowǔ and cantonese tiu3 mou5 are directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese khiêu vũ is likewise a directly attested standard loanword (跳's own khiêu + 舞's own vũ). Japanese とうぶ and korean 도무 are both honest compositional readings (跳's own TOU/도 + 舞's own BU/무) rather than the everyday native terms for "dance" (Japanese ダンス/踊る, Korean 춤/춤추다) — とうぶ is independently attested via the medical term 跳舞病 (とうぶびょう, "chorea"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 톳무 diverges from Sino-Korean 도무 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄛㄨㄇㄨ unique among both words and characters).

**Found and fixed a missing citation** on 跳.md's own Words list (跳舞 was absent). 舞's own `stand_in` is this exact word (跳舞), so 舞 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 跳舞; 跳's own `stand_in` is instead 跳躍, so this word does not legitimize 跳.
