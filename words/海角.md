## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [角 (char)](../characters/角%20(char).md) "corner, horn" — cape.

海角 is a cape: land that juts out into the sea. It is "sea corner." Mandarin hǎijiǎo, Cantonese hoi2 gok3 and Japanese かいかく are the readings; Chinese also uses the literary phrase 天涯海角, "the ends of the earth." Japanese more often says 岬 (misaki) and Korean uses the native 곶, so `korean` is left blank rather than guessed. Compare [[半島]] (peninsula) and [[海峡]] (strait).
