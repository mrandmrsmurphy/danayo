## Notes
- [[曷]] "what, why"

曷 is a classical synonym of [[何 (char)|何]] ("what, why"), surviving today only as the phonetic root behind a large family: 偈 ("Buddhist verse"), 喝 ("yell"), 掲 ("set up"), 歇 ("intermit"), 渇 ("thirst"), 葛 ("kudzu"), 蝎 ("scorpion"), 褐 ("brown"), and 謁 ("visit"). Dan'a'yo revives it as a standalone interrogative, distinct from 何, filling the previously-unused syllable ㄏㄨㄊ by deliberate placement rather than its organic Middle Chinese outcome (which would have joined 轄/褐's ㄏㄚㄊ) — see [[characters/曷|曷's character page]] for the full phonological rationale.
