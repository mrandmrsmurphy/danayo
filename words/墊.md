## Notes
- [墊 (char)](../characters/墊%20(char).md) "mat; pad; cushion"

**Content removed**: a stray leftover note, "needed dib" — apparently a reminder that the `羅馬字` value (`dib`) still needed filling in; it has since been filled, so the note was removed as stale rather than kept.

墊 means "mat, pad, cushion" and, as a verb, "to prop up, pad out, fill in" (墊付, "to advance money on someone's behalf").

Mandarin diàn and Cantonese din3 preserve the sense directly. Japanese テン (TEN, on'yomi, formal register) alongside native おち(る) (an extended, less directly related sense) cover the reading. Korean 점 (jeom) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese đệm is the everyday, living word for "cushion, mattress" (đệm ghế, "a seat cushion"), a vernacular Nôm-layer doublet of the same character. The character page's own stored candidate, điếm, is also genuinely attested as the formal/literary Hán Việt reading in dictionary sources — but it collides homophonically with an unrelated, unfortunately vulgar modern word ("prostitute"), which traces instead to the unrelated character 店 ("shop, establishment," itself also read điếm); 墊 and 店 converged in Sino-Vietnamese reading by sharing the phonetic component 占. `đệm` was used as the field value both because it matches this word's everyday sense directly and to sidestep that homophone collision.
