## Notes
- [笑](../characters/笑%20(char).md) "laugh, smile" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 笑 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

笑 means to laugh or smile: to show amusement or pleasure with the face and voice (微笑, "smile"; 大笑, "laugh heartily"; 笑話, "joke"; 笑容, "smiling face"), and by extension to mock or deride (嘲笑, "ridicule"; 笑柄, "laughingstock"). The variant 咲 is listed as an alias.

Mandarin xiào, Cantonese siu3, Japanese しょう (微笑/びしょう, "smile"; 苦笑/くしょう, "wry smile"; 失笑/しっしょう, "burst out laughing"), Korean 소 (미소, "smile") and Vietnamese tiếu (vi tiếu, "smile"; tiếu lâm, "humor") are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native verb 笑う (わらう) for the everyday word, so on'yomi しょう is bound to compounds, and Korean has native 웃다.
