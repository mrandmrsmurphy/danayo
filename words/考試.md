## Notes
- [考](../characters/考.md) "consider, examine" + [試](../characters/試.md) "test, try" — 試's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 考's own is [[考慮]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 考試 legitimizes 試 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 考 is individually `kwin: false`.

考試 means a test or exam.

Mandarin kǎoshì, Cantonese haau2 si5, Japanese こうし (kōshi), and Korean 고시 (gosi, especially a national civil-service exam) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 考試 is itself a common attested word for "exam" in all four languages. Vietnamese khảo thí is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
