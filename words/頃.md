## Notes
- [頃 (char)](../characters/頃%20(char).md) "moment; occasion" — this word is 頃's own legitimizing compound (頃's `stand_in` points here).

頃 means "a moment, a brief period" — as a standalone word it is essentially always read with the native kun'yomi ころ (koro) in Japanese rather than its on'yomi (ケイ), which survives mainly bound in compounds; ころ is likewise the reading behind 此の頃 (このごろ, "these days") and other time-adjacent forms built on this character. Mandarin qǐng, Cantonese king2, and Korean 경 (gyeong) all preserve the Sino reading directly. Sino-Vietnamese khoảnh is likewise attested, most familiar today in the compound khoảnh khắc (頃刻, "an instant").
