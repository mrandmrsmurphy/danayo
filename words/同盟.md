## Notes
- [同](../characters/同.md) "same; together" + [盟](../characters/盟.md) "oath; alliance"

同盟 is an alliance: states or groups joined by a pledge to act together, and to form one. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin tóngméng as "alliance; league" and "to form an alliance", and Japanese どうめい as "union; alliance; to ally". Vietnamese đồng minh is the same word, and Korean 동맹 and Cantonese tung4 mang4 match.

It is a pact to stand together against others, often in war, where a [条約](条約.md) is any treaty and [連合](連合.md) is a looser union. The opposite of an ally is the [敵人](敵人.md).
