## Notes
- [説](../characters/説.md) "speak, explain" + [得](../characters/得.md) "obtain, achieve" — literally "to speak [and] achieve [it]." No stand-in relationship applies (説's own `stand_in` is [[学説]]; 得's own is [[獲得]]).

説得 means to persuade — using words to change someone's attitude or behavior in the direction the speaker wants, while still respecting the listener's own free will (as opposed to coercion). This vault's near-synonym of [[説服]] above; where 説服 emphasizes actually winning someone over, 説得 emphasizes the deliberate act of persuading, whether or not it lands.

**A genuine root-choice split across the sphere**: Japanese せっとく and Korean 설득 are both the real, standard, everyday words for this whole concept — while Mandarin and Vietnamese instead build their standard word on 服 ("submit") rather than 得 ("achieve"): see [[説服]]/thuyết phục. Mandarin shuōdé is not an independent fixed word in living use (说得 normally just parses as "speak" + a grammatical resultative particle); Vietnamese thuyết đắc is likewise the mechanical compositional reading rather than an independently attested word. Cantonese syut3 dak1 is the compositional reading. `kwin` is false: both 説 and 得 are individually `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼔ㄊㄊㄜㄎ unique across both words and characters).
