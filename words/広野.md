## Notes
- [広](../characters/広%20(char).md) "wide" + [野](../characters/野.md) "wild, countryside, field, wilderness" — no stand-in relationship applies (広's own stand-in is itself; 野's own is [[田野]]).

広野 means wilderness — a wide, open, uncultivated expanse of land, as in a desert or wasteland.

Mandarin guǎngyě, Cantonese gwong2 je5, Japanese こうや, Korean 광야, and Vietnamese quảng dã are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bugs found and fixed**: `mandarin`/`cantonese` had both been contaminated with the readings of the near-synonym 曠野 (kuàngyě/kwong3, using 曠 not 広/廣) — corrected to guǎngyě/gwong2 je5. Filled blank `vietnamese`. Removed 曠野 from `aliases` (a genuinely different character combination, not an orthographic variant of 広野) and the redundant `品詞`. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⺢ㄫ·⼘ unique across both words and characters).
