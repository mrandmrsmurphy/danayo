## Notes
- [原](../characters/原.md) "origin, source" + [始](../characters/始.md) "begin" — this word is the stand-in for [原](../characters/原.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo (始's own stand-in is [[始作]]).

原始 means primitive or original — in a pristine, unaltered, or earliest state.

Filled blank `vietnamese: nguyên thuỷ` (verified — the standard, extremely common Sino-Vietnamese word for "primitive, original"). Japanese げんし and Korean 원시 are both standard readings, matching Dan'a'yo's own reading exactly (`kwin: true`). No homophones (注音 ⼔ㄋㄙㄧ unique across both words and characters).
