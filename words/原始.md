## Notes
- [原](../characters/原.md) "origin, source" + [始](../characters/始.md) "begin" — this word is the stand-in for [原](../characters/原.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo (始's own stand-in is [[始作]]).

原始 means primitive or original — in a pristine, unaltered, or earliest state. 元始 is an alias: Wiktionary lists it as an alternative form of 原始 in Japanese, where the Bible's opening line reads 元始に神天地を創造たまへり, "In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth"; in Chinese 元始 is also the "Primordial" of the Daoist deity 元始天尊.
