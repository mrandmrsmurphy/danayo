## Notes
- [碧](../characters/碧.md) "emerald, green" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — 碧's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 色's own is [[色彩]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 碧 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

碧色 names the specific blue-green, emerald-like color associated with jade and jasper (碧玉) — a shade distinct from the plainer 緑色 ("green") in carrying connotations of gemstone luster and refinement, often invoked in classical poetic description of water, mountains, or eyes.

Mandarin bìsè, Cantonese bik1 sik1, Japanese へきしょく (hekishoku), Korean 벽색 (byeoksaek), and Vietnamese bích sắc are all fully compositional and share this same literary, gemstone-adjacent register across the sphere.
