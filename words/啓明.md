## Notes
- [啓](../characters/啓.md) "open" + [明](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright" — no stand-in relationship applies (啓's own stand-in is [[開啓]]; 明 is its own stand-in).
- The classical name for the planet Venus when visible before dawn in the east. Modern astronomy identifies it with [[太白星]], the evening star — both are Venus.

Filled blank `vietnamese: khải minh` (verified — the classical Sino-Vietnamese name, paired with Trường Canh for the evening apparition). Mandarin qǐmíng, Cantonese kai2 ming4, Japanese けいめい, and Korean 계명 are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄝㄧㄇ⼶ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
