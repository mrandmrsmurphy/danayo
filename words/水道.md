## Notes
- [水](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [道](../characters/道%20(char).md) "way" — "water way." Their own `stand_in` values: 水's is itself; 道's is itself; none points here, so 水道 is an independent compound.

水道 is a channel or waterway. Mandarin shuǐdào and Cantonese seoi2 dou6 use it for a canal or ditch, and Japanese すいどう, Korean 수도 and the Chinese sense of a water-supply pipe are the same word. Compare [[運河]] (canal) and [[海峡]] (strait).
