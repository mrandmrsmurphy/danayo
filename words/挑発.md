## Notes
- [挑](../characters/挑.md) "challenge authority" + [発](../characters/発%20(char).md) "issue forth, discharge" — no stand-in relationship applies (挑's own stand-in is [[挑戦]]; 発's own is itself).

挑発 means to provoke.

Mandarin tiāofā, Cantonese tiu1 faat3, Japanese ちょうはつ, Korean 도발, and Vietnamese khiêu phát are all standard, fully compositional readings. Trimmed the verb-forming する suffix from `japanese` for consistency with other entries. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄚㄨㄈㄚㄊ unique across both words and characters).
