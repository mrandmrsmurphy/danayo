## Notes
- [冷 (char)](../characters/冷%20(char).md) "cold" + [淡](../characters/淡.md) "pale; weak"

冷淡 is indifferent: cold and uninterested, showing no warmth toward a person or a matter. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin lěngdàn as "indifferent; cold; aloof; apathetic" and Japanese れいたん as "cold-hearted, cold; indifferent". Cantonese laang5 daam6 matches, and Korean 랭담 is the reading of the characters.

It is the lack of feeling where [冷静](冷静.md) is feeling under control, and it is a milder coldness than the cruelty of [刻薄](刻薄.md). Warmth is the [熱情](熱情.md) of the passionate.
