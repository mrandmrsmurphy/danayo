## Notes
- [経 (char)](../characters/経%20(char).md) "classic, channel, warp" + [緯](../characters/緯.md) "lines of latitude" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (経's is [[経]] itself, 緯's is [[緯線]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 経 is individually `kwin: false`.

経緯 literally means "warp and woof" — the vertical and horizontal threads in weaving — extended to "latitude and longitude" and, figuratively, "the ins and outs, the full story" of how something came to be.

Mandarin jīngwěi, Cantonese ging1 wai5, Japanese けいゐ (the historical kana spelling of けいい, keii), and Korean 경위 (gyeongwi) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 経緯 is itself a common attested word for "latitude and longitude" (and figuratively "circumstances") in all four languages. Vietnamese kinh vĩ is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
