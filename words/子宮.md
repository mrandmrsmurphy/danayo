## Notes
- [子](../characters/子.md) "child" + [宮](../characters/宮.md) "palace, chamber" — "womb, uterus" (literally "child's palace"). `vietnamese: tử cung` is a directly attested, extremely common standard Vietnamese medical term with the same sense. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 즈궁 diverges from Sino-Korean 자궁 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄜㄍㄨㄫ unique among both words and characters).
