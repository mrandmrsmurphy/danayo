## Notes
- [忘](../characters/忘.md) "forget" + [族](../characters/族.md) "tribe, clan" — "the Forget Tribe, Manasseh." Their own `stand_in` values: 忘's is [[忘却]]; 族's is [[家族]]; none points here, so 忘族 is an independent compound.

忘族 is the tribe of Manasseh, Joseph's elder son, whose name means "making to forget": "God has made me forget all my hardship" (Genesis 41:51). It is a Dan'a'yo **neologism**, a meaning-based name, "Forget Tribe," and one of the two [[半族]] of [[双族]] (Joseph). Compare [[豊族]] (Ephraim). See [[基督敎]].

- Hebrew name: מְנַשֶּׁה (Menasheh) (注音 ㄇㄝㄋㄚㄙㄝ).

- See [基督敎](../lexipedia/基督敎.md).
