## Notes
- [犬 (char)](../characters/犬%20(char).md) "dog" + [吠](../characters/吠.md) "bark" — 吠's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 犬's own is itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 吠 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

犬吠 means a dog's bark, barking; it is synonymous with [[狗吠]].

Mandarin quǎnfèi, Cantonese hyun2 fai6, Japanese けんはい, Korean 견폐 and Vietnamese khuyển phệ are all fully compositional, matching 犬's and 吠's own citations.
