## Notes
- [乖](../characters/乖.md) "obedient, well-behaved" (a positive colloquial sense distinct from its classical "perverse, contrary" meaning) + [巧](../characters/巧.md) "clever, skillful" — this word is the `stand_in` legitimizer for [[乖]], which cannot appear independently (巧's own `stand_in` instead points to [[巧妙]], a separate compound).

乖巧 describes someone — most often a child or young woman — as both well-behaved and quick-witted at once, a description with genuine cultural warmth rather than mere obedience: it praises social tact and cleverness alongside good behavior, not passive compliance. Vietnamese quai xảo (confirmed via web search) matches this exact sense. **`japanese` deliberately left blank**: 乖巧 has no independent life as a Japanese vocabulary item — every Japanese source found treats it strictly as a Chinese word being glossed for Japanese learners (Chinese-Japanese dictionary entries), not as a word Japanese itself has borrowed and uses, the same "genuinely lacks a corresponding word" finding already documented on [[可以]]/[[不可以]] earlier in this vault's history. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 괘캿 diverges from Sino-Korean 괴교 in both syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⺢ㄧㄎ⼘ㄨ unique among both words and characters).
