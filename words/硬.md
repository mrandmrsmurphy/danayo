## Notes
- [硬](characters/硬%20(char).md) "hard, firm" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 硬 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

硬 means hard or firm.

Mandarin yìng, Cantonese ngaang6, Japanese こう, Korean 경, and Vietnamese ngạnh are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 硬's own stored citation exactly). Fixed the malformed `羅馬字` (ang → 'ang, restoring the leading apostrophe), added the missing `japanese`/`hsk_level` fields, and filled the blank `vietnamese`. Discovered a genuine homophone with [[桜]] (both 注音 ㄚㄫ, 諺文 앙) — cross-referenced both pages.
