## Notes
- [得](../characters/得.md) "get" + [楽](../characters/楽.md) "pleasure" (here read lè, Vietnamese lạc, not the "music" reading) — no stand-in relationship applies (得's own is [[獲得]]; 楽's own is [[快楽]]). The name is written 得樂 in traditional characters (and 得乐 in simplified), kept as aliases because 楽 is the parent form on its character page.

得楽 (Vietnamese Đắk Lắk) is a province of the Central Highlands (Tây Nguyên) of Vietnam, whose chief city is [[邦美蜀]]. It is known for its coffee, which is the main crop of the highlands, and for its ethnic minorities, above all the Rhade (Ê Đê) people (from memory, not re-checked). The 2025 provincial merger absorbed the neighbouring coastal province of Phú Yên into Đắk Lắk (from memory, not re-checked); English Wiktionary's Vietnamese entry notes that, as of 12 June 2025, Đắk Lắk is the only province whose name does not follow Vietnamese spelling rules (read 2026-10-11).

The two spellings. The Chinese characters are a rendering of the Vietnamese name, not a translation: "get pleasure" is not what the name means. English Wiktionary (read 2026-10-11) derives Đắk Lắk from Mnong "dak Lak", i.e. Lak Lake (hồ Lắk), with "dak" the Mnong cognate of Vietnamese nước "water" (Rhade also has dak "water", from memory, not re-checked); the name therefore means roughly "Lak Lake water". The `vietnamese` field holds the Hán-Việt reading đắc lạc (hvdic.thivien.net gives 得 as đắc; 楽 is lạc on its character page), which approximates the real name Đắk Lắk. Mandarin délè and Cantonese dak1 lok6 are the compositional readings of 得楽 (得 dé / dak1, 楽 lè / lok6); they are not attested for this name (see below). The Japanese とくらく (得 TOKU, 楽 RAKU) and Korean 득락 are compositional Sino-Xenic readings; Japanese actually says ダクラク and Korean 닥락 (from memory, not re-checked).

Attestation, checked 2026-10-11, and honestly: 得樂 and 得乐 have no entry on English, Chinese, Vietnamese, Japanese or Korean Wiktionary, so this spelling is unattested there. English Wiktionary's Vietnamese entry for Đắk Lắk gives a Han-script form 得勒 (đắc lặc), and its Chinese entry 多樂 (simplified 多乐; Mandarin Duōlè, Cantonese do1 lok6, "Đắk Lắk province"), which is also its Chinese translation of the name; these are the attested forms, and 勒 and 多 are not used here. 得楽 is therefore a Dan'a'yo rendering chosen by decision, kept as an alias-bearing base. The 注音 ㄊㄜㄎㄌㄚㄎ is shared with no other word or character.

Dan'a'yo keeps the Chinese-sphere style of name: it is the proper noun for the province, with no other sense.
