## Notes
- [柿](../characters/柿.md) "persimmon" + [木](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree, wood" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 柿 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

柿木 means the Japanese persimmon (tree).

Mandarin shìmù and Cantonese ci2 muk6 are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 柿's and 木's own citations. Korean 시목 and Vietnamese thị mộc, likewise compositional, are corrected here — the fields had held 감 (a native Korean word for persimmon) and a blank respectively. Fixed real bugs: `mandarin` was garbled (shì zi, actually pinyin for the unrelated word 柿子 "persimmon fruit") — corrected to shìmù. Japanese カキノキ is the standard katakana biological/species-name spelling of the native word かきのき (kun'yomi of both 柿 and 木), a genuine real-world naming convention for plant names, kept as-is. Added `hsk_level: "無"` (matching 柿's own confirmed no-HSK-level status).

**Real bug found and fixed 2026-09-29**: `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` had drifted to 시목/simog/ㄙㄝㄇㄛㄎ, giving 柿's own syllable a "si"/ㄙㄝ-ish onset that didn't match 柿's own character-page citation (스/sǝ/ㄙㄜ). This had previously been flagged the other way — as a suspected internal inconsistency on `characters/柿.md` itself — but `lookup/CC/finals/韻 之.md` (the finals-grouping lookup page for 柿's own rhyme class) documents 柿's ㄙㄜ reading as a confirmed, deliberate "simplicity principle" pick: `之`-final ㄙ-initial characters split three ways with no conditioning factor (ㄧ: 詩/侍/時/市/etc.; ㄚ: 祠/飼/思/etc.; ㄜ: 柿/祀/嗣/巳), and 柿 is explicitly named in the ㄜ group alongside 祀/嗣/巳. So `柿.md`'s own reading was correct all along — the bug was here, in this word. Corrected to 스목/sǝmog/ㄙㄜ·ㄇㄛㄎ (the boundary dot is needed here — unlike the old ㄙㄝㄇㄛㄎ, ㄙㄜㄇㄛㄎ has a genuine resegmentation fork, since both ㄙㄜㄇ and ㄛㄎ are independently legal syllables). No homophones (注音 ㄙㄜ·ㄇㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
