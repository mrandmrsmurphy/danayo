## Notes
- [柿](../characters/柿.md) "persimmon" + [木](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree, wood" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 柿 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

柿木 means the Japanese persimmon (tree).

Mandarin shìmù and Cantonese ci2 muk6 are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 柿's and 木's own citations. Korean 시목 and Vietnamese thị mộc, likewise compositional, are corrected here — the fields had held 감 (a native Korean word for persimmon) and a blank respectively. Fixed real bugs: `mandarin` was garbled (shì zi, actually pinyin for the unrelated word 柿子 "persimmon fruit") — corrected to shìmù. Japanese カキノキ is the standard katakana biological/species-name spelling of the native word かきのき (kun'yomi of both 柿 and 木), a genuine real-world naming convention for plant names, kept as-is. Added `hsk_level: "無"` (matching 柿's own confirmed no-HSK-level status). Flagged that 柿's own character page has an internal inconsistency (`諺文`/`羅馬字` read 스/sǝ while its own `korean` field reads 시) for a future character-perfecting pass. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄇㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
