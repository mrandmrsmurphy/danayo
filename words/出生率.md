## Notes
- [出 (char)](../characters/出%20(char).md) "go out" + [生](../characters/生.md) "birth, life" + [率](../characters/率.md) "rate, ratio" — "birth rate, natality." Mandarin chūshēnglǜ, Cantonese ceot1 sang1 leot6, Japanese しゅっしょうりつ, Korean 출생률, and Vietnamese xuất sinh suất are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 춛상룯 diverges from Sino-Korean 출생률 entirely, in all three syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄨㄊㄙㄚㄫㄌㄨㄊ unique among both words and characters).
