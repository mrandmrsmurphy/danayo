## Notes
- [苟](../characters/苟%20(char).md) "if only" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 苟 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

苟 means "if only" (a classical conditional conjunction, as in 苟活 "to live on ignobly/merely survive").

Mandarin gǒu, Cantonese gau2, and Korean 구 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 苟's own stored citations exactly.
