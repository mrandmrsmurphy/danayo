## Notes
- [付 (char)](../characters/付.md) "attach, hand over" (stand-in: [支付](支付.md)) + [加](../characters/加.md) "add" (stand-in: [加算](加算.md))

付加 means addition, appending. English Wiktionary has an entry only for Japanese 付加 (ふか), glossed as the noun "addition, appendage, annexation" and the verb "to add to, append, supplement". The Mandarin, Cantonese, Korean 부가 and Vietnamese fields are built from the readings of the two characters.
