## Notes
- [付 (char)](../characters/付.md) "attach, hand over" (stand-in: [[支付]]) + [加](../words/加.md) "add" (stand-in: itself) — addition, appending something extra.

付加 means addition/appending, as invoked by `文法 - 02音韻論`'s 付加疑問文 ("tag question," literally "appended question"). Japanese ふか and Korean 부가 are both real, standard terms, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Cantonese fu6 gaa1 is a straightforward compositional reading. Mandarin fùjiā and Vietnamese phó gia are compositional only. `kwin` is false: inherited from 付 (false), despite 加 itself being true.

No syllable-boundary dot is needed: the concatenated string `ㄈㄨㄍㄚ` admits only one legal partition.
