## Notes
- [画](../characters/画.md) "picture" + [廊](../characters/廊.md) "corridor"

画廊 is an art gallery: a shop or hall where pictures are shown and sold. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin huàláng as "art gallery" and Japanese がろう as "gallery". Korean 화랑 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese waa6 long4 matches.

It is smaller and more commercial than the [美術館](美術館.md), and a one-off show is a [展覧会](展覧会.md).
