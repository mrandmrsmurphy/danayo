## Notes
- [統](../characters/統.md) "command, govern, unite" + [一 (char)](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (統's is [[統率]], 一's is [[一]] itself), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 一 is individually `kwin: false`.

統一 means to unite or unify — to bring together into one.

Mandarin tǒngyī, Cantonese tung2 jat1, Japanese とういつ (tōitsu), and Korean 통일 (tongil) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 統一 is itself a common attested word for "unite, unify" in all four languages. Vietnamese thống nhất is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. **Fixed two real bugs**: `羅馬字` had tong'it (a t-final, mismatching 一's own d-final 'id) — corrected to tong'id; and `kwin` was true, but the AND-rule requires false. Removed the redundant duplicate `品詞` field. No homophones.
