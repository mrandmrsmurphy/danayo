## Notes
- [紫](../characters/紫.md) "purple" + [禁](../characters/禁.md) "forbid, restrict" + [城](../characters/城.md) "city, walled city" — none of the three characters' own `stand_in` points here (紫's is [[紫色]], 禁's is [[禁止]], 城's is [[城郭]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: all three constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

紫禁城 names "the purple forbidden city" — the imperial palace complex in Beijing. Purple marked the celestial pole (紫微垣, the "Purple Forbidden Enclosure" of Chinese astronomy) and by extension the emperor's own domain, forbidden to commoners.

Mandarin Zǐjìnchéng, Cantonese zi2 gam3 sing4, Japanese しきんじょう (shikinjō), Korean 자금성 (jageumseong), and Vietnamese Tử Cấm Thành are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 紫禁城 is itself the common attested name for the Forbidden City in all five languages. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
