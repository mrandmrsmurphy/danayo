## Notes
- [板](../characters/板.md) "plank, board" + [球](../characters/球%20(char).md) "sphere, ball" — no stand-in relationship applies (板's own stand-in is [[木板]]; 球's own is itself).

板球 means cricket (the sport).

Mandarin bǎnqiú and Cantonese baan2 kau4 are the genuine, standard Chinese compositional term for cricket. Japanese クリケット, Korean 크리켓, and Vietnamese cri-kê are all English loanword transliterations — no compositional Sino-Japanese/Sino-Korean/Sino-Vietnamese compound for this sport exists in real-world usage, so the loanwords are kept rather than inventing an unattested compositional form.
