## Notes
- [二 (char)](../characters/二%20(char).md) "two" + [十 (char)](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" + [八 (char)](../characters/八%20(char).md) "eight" + [宿](../characters/宿.md) "lodging; constellation"

二十八宿 are the Twenty-Eight Mansions: the Chinese division of the sky into twenty-eight constellations along the path of the moon, one for each night of its month. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese (nhị thập bát tú) as "the Twenty-Eight Mansions". Cantonese ji6 sap6 baat3 sau3 matches, and Korean 이십팔수 is the reading of the characters.

The mansions are grouped into four, one for each direction, and each is a [星宿](星宿.md). The moon moves through them, and the planets and the sun pass through the same belt as the [黄道](黄道.md), ecliptic.
