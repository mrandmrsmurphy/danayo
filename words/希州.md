## Notes
- [希](../characters/希.md) + [州 (char)](../characters/州%20(char).md) — Ji Province (冀州), the first and central of the [[九州]]; 希 is the Dan'a'yo canonical form, 冀 being its alias

Wiktionary (冀州): Chinese, Ji Province, "one of the Nine Provinces of ancient China", and a modern Hebei city, Jizhou; Japanese, the Hebei city きしゅう. The *Tribute of Yu* (書經, 禹貢) describes 冀州 among the [[九州]] with the line 厥土惟白壤, "its soil is white loam" (cited on Wikisource). The word is 冀州 written with the substitute character 希, the form the vault records for 冀, so the foreign-language fields give the readings of the real name 冀州; the Dan'a'yo reading 희줏 comes from 希 and 州.
