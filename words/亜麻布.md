## Notes
- [亜](../characters/亜.md) "flax-, secondary" + [麻](../characters/麻.md) "hemp" + [布](../characters/布.md) "cloth" — this word is the `stand_in` legitimizer for [[布]], which cannot appear independently.

亜麻布 narrows [[麻布]] ("linen/hemp cloth" generally) specifically to cloth woven from *flax* (亜麻, lit. "secondary hemp," i.e. the flax plant as distinct from true hemp 大麻) — the standard distinction between 麻布 and 亜麻布 found across Mandarin, Japanese, and Korean alike. `korean: 아마포` and `vietnamese: á ma bố` are both fully compositional from each character's own independently confirmed reading (亜's 아/á, 麻's 마/ma, 布's 포/bố), paralleling everyday Sino-Vietnamese vocabulary built on the same 布 root (tuyên bố, bố thí).
