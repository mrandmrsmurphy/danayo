## Notes
- [工](../characters/工.md) "work" + [芸](../characters/芸.md) "art"

工芸 is craft: art made as useful objects, such as pottery, lacquer and textiles. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin gōngyì as "technology and process of turning raw materials into products" and Japanese こうげい as "industrial arts". Korean 공예 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese gung1 ngai6 matches.

It joins the [工匠](工匠.md), artisan, to the [芸術](芸術.md), art, and its fields include the [陶器](陶器.md), [漆器](漆器.md) and [刺繍](刺繍.md).
