## Notes
- [低](../characters/低.md) "low" + [下 (char)](../characters/下%20(char).md) "down, below" — "to lower, drop, decline" (of a level, status, or quality). `vietnamese: đê hạ` is compositional from each character's own confirmed reading (confirmed via web search). **Real gap found and fixed on [[低]]'s own page**: its `vietnamese` field was blank — added đê, the well-known Sino-Vietnamese reading (opposite of cao "high"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 데하 diverges from Sino-Korean 저하 via the AND-rule, since 低's own field independently diverges (despite 下 itself being `kwin: true`). No homophones (注音 ㄉㄝㄧㄏㄚ unique among both words and characters). Legitimizing note: 低's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, so 低 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

In Dan'a'yo 低下 also serves as the general adjective "low" (of height or level), the opposite of [[高]] "high", which the language otherwise lacked — the bare character 低 is bound and is no word of its own, so 低下 carries the sense. The low vowel is 低下 + 母音, 低下母音, as 高母音 is the high one, and a low pitch is 低下 in the same way.
