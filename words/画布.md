## Notes
- [画](../characters/画.md) "picture" + [布](../characters/布.md) "cloth"

画布 is a canvas: the cloth on which an oil painting is done. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin huàbù as "canvas" and Japanese がふ as "canvas". Korean 화포 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese waa2 bou3 matches.

It carries the [油画](油画.md), oil painting, and it is the base on which the painter works.
