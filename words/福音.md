## Notes
- [福](../characters/福%20(char).md) "blessing" + [音](../characters/音.md) "sound, message" — "blessed message," the gospel. Their own `stand_in` values: 福's is itself; 音's is [[音楽]]; none points here, so 福音 is an independent compound.

福音 is the gospel: the good news of salvation through [[救偕]] (Jesus). Mandarin fúyīn, Cantonese fuk1 jam1, Japanese ふくいん and Korean 복음 all use it, and Vietnamese phúc âm does too. It is "blessed sound," the good news itself, announced in the four Gospels. Compare [[聖書]] (Bible) and [[救贖]] (redemption).
