## Notes
- [院](../characters/院.md) "courtyard" + [落](../characters/落.md) "fall"
- 院落 is the stand-in word for [院](../characters/院.md) ("courtyard"), which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo.

院落 means a courtyard or compound, particularly the enclosed courtyard space characteristic of traditional East Asian residential architecture (e.g. the siheyuan/四合院, "quadrangle courtyard house"). Mandarin 院落 (yuànluò) and Cantonese 院落 (jyun2lok6) are both everyday architectural terms. Japanese 院落 (いんらく) and Sino-Vietnamese viện lạc are comparatively rare and literary, with everyday usage favoring native or narrower alternatives (庭/中庭 in Japanese; sân, đình viện for specific senses in Vietnamese) over this compound.
