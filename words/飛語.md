## Notes
- [飛](../characters/飛.md) "fly" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language" — no `#cranberry`: 飛's own `stand_in` is 飛 itself, 語's is [[言語]]; neither points here. Uses 飛's other reading branch (ㄆㄧ), confirmed correct against both constituents' own citations of this exact word.

飛語/蜚語 (fēiyǔ) is a real, classical/literary Mandarin term for "baseless rumor," most familiar today in the four-character idiom 流言飛語/流言蜚語 ("wild rumors flying about"), and Cantonese 飛語 (fei1jyu5) likewise. Japanese ひご (higo) is likewise attested, chiefly in that same idiom (流言飛語/流言蜚語, りゅうげんひご). Korean 비어 (bieo) is the standard compositional term. Sino-Vietnamese phi ngữ is likewise real and attested, meaning unfounded rumor or slanderous speech.
