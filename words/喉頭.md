## Notes
- [喉](../characters/喉.md) "throat" + [頭 (char)](../characters/頭%20(char).md) "head; top"

喉頭 is the larynx, or voice box: the upper end of the windpipe, which holds the vocal cords. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin hóutóu and Japanese こうとう as "larynx". Korean 후두 and Cantonese hau4 tau4 match.

It is the upper part of the throat, [咽喉](咽喉.md), and it leads down into the [気管](気管.md). The cords inside it are the [声帯](声帯.md).
