## Notes
- [羊](../characters/羊.md) "sheep, goat" + [頭 (char)](../characters/頭%20(char).md) "head" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (羊's is [[綿羊]], 頭's is [[頭]] itself), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 頭 is individually `kwin: false`.

羊頭 names a goat's head — most familiar from the idiom 羊頭狗肉 ("selling dog meat while displaying a goat's head," false advertising).

Mandarin yángtóu, Cantonese joeng4 tau4, Japanese ようとう (yōtō), and Korean 양두 (yangdu) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 羊頭 is itself a common attested word for "goat's head" in all four languages. Vietnamese dương đầu is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
