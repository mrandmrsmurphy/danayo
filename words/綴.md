## Notes
- [綴](../characters/綴%20(char).md) "bind, stop" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 綴 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

綴 means to bind, stitch, or connect, as in 点綴 (to embellish, dot with).

Mandarin zhuì, Cantonese zeoi3, and Korean 철 (cheol) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese xuyết is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading (as in điểm xuyết, "to embellish"), one of three stored candidates. A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone with [[絶]] ("discontinue, break off") — coincidental, unrelated in meaning.
