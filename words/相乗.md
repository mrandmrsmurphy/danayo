## Notes
- [相](../characters/相.md) "mutual (stand-in: [[相互]])" + [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "ride (stand-in: itself)"

Wiktionary: Chinese 相乘, in mathematics, "to multiply (each other); to be multiplied (with each other)", and, literary, "to occur one after another"; Japanese 相乗 "multiplication" and "synergism". The spelling 相乘 is the alias. Wiktionary lists no Wu Xing sense; in Dan'a'yo it names the overacting cycle of [[五行]], beside [[相生]] and [[相克]], as a technical use built on "multiplying each other".
