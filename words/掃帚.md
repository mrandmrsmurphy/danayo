## Notes
- [掃](../characters/掃.md) "sweep" + [帚](../characters/帚.md) "broom, broomstick" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [帚](../characters/帚.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (掃's own stand-in is [[掃除]]).

掃帚 means broom.

Mandarin sàozhǒu, Cantonese sou3 zaau2, Japanese そうじゅ, Korean 소추, and Vietnamese tảo trửu are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄚㄨㄐㄨ unique across both words and characters).
