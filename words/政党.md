## Notes
- [政](../characters/政.md) "government, political affairs" + [党](../characters/党.md) "political party" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [党](../characters/党.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (政's own stand-in is [[政治]]).

政党 means political party.

Mandarin zhèngdǎng, Cantonese zing3 dong2, Japanese せいとう, Korean 정당, and Vietnamese chính đảng are all standard, fully compositional readings. Removed self-referential `政党` from `aliases` (matches the filename itself, kept only the genuine traditional variant 政黨). No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄫㄉㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
