## Notes
- [硅](../characters/硅.md) "silicon" + [素](../characters/素.md) "element" — literally "silicon-element," silicon — stand-in for [硅](../characters/硅.md), which cannot appear independently.

Mandarin guī and Cantonese gwai1 are the real bare words for silicon, matching 硅's own stored citations exactly (Chinese has no 素-suffixed convention for this element, only the bare character 硅, itself a modern coinage borrowed from Japanese; 矽 is its alias). Japanese けいそ (keiso) and Korean 규소 are both real, standard compounds, genuinely compositional with 硅's own on'yomi/Sino-Korean readings — but note 硅's own `korean_native` field is stored as 규소, since Korean historically used the 硅-素 compound as its ordinary word rather than treating it as a bound Sino-Korean-only form. Vietnamese silic is an international loanword, unrelated to either character's own citations.
