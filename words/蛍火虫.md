## Notes
- [蛍 (char)](../characters/蛍%20(char).md) "firefly" + [火 (char)](../characters/火%20(char).md) "fire" + [虫](../characters/虫.md) "bug; insect"

蛍火虫 is the firefly. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 螢火蟲 (alias here; Mandarin yínghuǒchóng, Cantonese jing4 fo2 cung4) as "firefly; lightning bug; glowworm". The Japanese field gives the native word ほたる, and Korean 형화충 is built from the readings of the three characters. The light of a firefly is the [蛍光](蛍光.md).
