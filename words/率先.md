## Notes
- [率](../characters/率.md) "lead, head" + [先](../characters/先.md) "first, prior" — no stand-in relationship applies (率's own stand-in is [[比率]]; 先's own is [[優先]]). 率 is polyphonic: its own stored citation reflects the "rate" sense (lǜ/leot6/률), but 率先 uses the distinct "lead, command" sense (shuài/seot1/솔) — a character-level polyphonic gap, matching the pattern already documented on [[為人]].

率先 means to take the lead, to be the first to act.

Mandarin shuàixiān, Korean 솔선, and Japanese そっせん (SOTSU+SEN, using the "lead"-sense on'yomi SOTSU rather than 率's own stored RITSU) already correctly reflected the "lead" sense. Fixed `cantonese`: leot6 sin1 (using 率's own "rate"-sense reading) → seot1 sin1, the correct "lead"-sense reading. Vietnamese suất tiên was already correct. Fixed a real character-level bug on `characters/先.md`: its `pos` field held an empty string rather than a value — corrected to 修飾語. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄨㄊㄙㄝㄋ unique across both words and characters).
