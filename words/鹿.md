## Notes
- [鹿 (char)](../characters/鹿%20(char).md) "deer" — stand-in for [[鹿 (char)|鹿]], which cannot appear independently

鹿 is 象形 of a deer, its antlers and legs visible in the character's strokes.

Japanese carries a single on'yomi ろく (ROKU), rare in ordinary vocabulary since everyday Japanese uses しか (shika) as the free-standing native word for a deer — the character's own stored `japanese_native: か` is a bound reading confined to compounds like 鹿の子 (kanoko, "fawn-spotted pattern"), not the ordinary standalone word. Vietnamese lộc is the reading used here — notably, lộc also carries a distinct figurative sense in Vietnamese as "fortune, blessing, prosperity" (lộc trời "heaven-sent fortune"), an extension not paralleled by the character's other CJKV readings. `注音 ㄌㄛㄎ`/`록` is also carried by [[緑]] ("green," already correctly cross-linked on both pages) — a coincidental convergence unrelated in meaning.
