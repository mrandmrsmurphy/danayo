## Notes
- [開](../characters/開.md) "open" + [啓](../characters/啓.md) "open"
- This word is the stand-in for [[啓]], which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo.

開啓 means to open, and by extension to launch or begin something new: 開 is "to open" and 啓 is "to open, to start" (also "to enlighten", as in 啓蒙), so the compound doubles the idea. Mandarin kāiqǐ (开启 in simplified script, 開啟 in Taiwan), Cantonese hoi1 kai2, Japanese かいけい (kaikei) and Korean 개계 (gyegye), per the vault's data, are the same compound. In Chinese it is used both literally (to open a door, a file or a device) and figuratively (to open up a new era or a new stage).

In Dan'a'yo it is the opening-up word for beginnings, placed in Creation & Destruction beside [[創造]] (to create) as the verb of starting rather than making.
