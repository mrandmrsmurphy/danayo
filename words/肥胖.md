## Notes
- [肥](../characters/肥.md) "plump" + [胖](../characters/胖.md) "fat" — this word is 胖's own `stand_in`: the character's meaning (fat) is identical, but 胖 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word, so the compound pairs it with the near-synonym 肥 to make it usable.

Mandarin féipàng and Cantonese fei4 bun6 are the standard, fully compositional readings, and this is the ordinary living Mandarin/Cantonese word for "fat, obese." Korean 비반 is likewise compositional, though not the everyday Korean word for "fat" (that would typically be native 뚱뚱하다 or Sino-Korean 비만 with a different second syllable). Japanese ひはん is the mechanical Sino-Japanese reading, but not standard usage in Japanese at all — and it happens to collide with the common word 批判 ("criticism"), a coincidence that doesn't affect Dan'a'yo since this vault's readings are compositional constructs, not attestations of real Japanese usage.
