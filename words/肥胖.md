## Notes
- [肥](../characters/肥.md) "plump" + [胖](../characters/胖.md) "fat" — this word is 胖's own `stand_in`: the character's meaning (fat) is identical, but 胖 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word, so the compound pairs it with the near-synonym 肥 to make it usable.

肥胖 means fat or corpulent, and as a noun obesity. English Wiktionary glosses Mandarin féipàng (Cantonese fei4 bun6) as "fat; corpulent" and "obesity", the ordinary Chinese word. It has no Japanese or Korean entry for 肥胖, so Japanese ひはん and Korean 비반 here are built from the characters. The words that do exist there are Japanese 肥満 (ひまん, "obesity; to become obese") and Korean 비만 (written 肥滿, "obesity"), which the vault lists as [[肥満]]. The Japanese reading ひはん happens to coincide with 批判, "criticism".
