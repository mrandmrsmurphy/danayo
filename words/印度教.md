## Notes
- [印](../characters/印.md) "print, stamp" + [度](../characters/度.md) "degree, to cross" + [教](../characters/教.md) "teaching, religion" — 印度 is the Sino-phonetic name of India, and 教 "teaching" makes it a religion, on the pattern of [[猶太教]], [[回教]] and [[基督教]]. 印's own `stand_in` is [[封印]], 度's is [[程度]] and 教's is [[教授]]; none points here, so 印度教 is an independent compound.

印度教 is Hinduism. Mandarin Yìndùjiào, Cantonese jan3 dou6 gaau3 and Vietnamese Ấn Độ giáo use it. Japanese says ヒンドゥー教 and Korean 힌두교, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters rather than attested words.

Compare [[佛教]] (Buddhism).
