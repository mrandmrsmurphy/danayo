## Notes
- [紛 (char)](../characters/紛%20(char).md) "numerous, disorderly" + [争](../characters/争.md) "fight, dispute" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (紛's is [[紛]] itself, 争's is [[抗争]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

紛争 means a dispute or conflict — disorderly contention.

Mandarin fēnzhēng, Cantonese fan1 zang1, Japanese ふんそう (funsō), Korean 분쟁 (bunjaeng), and Vietnamese phân tranh are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 紛争 is itself a common attested word for "dispute, conflict" in all five languages. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
