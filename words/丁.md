## Notes
- [丁](../characters/丁%20(char).md) "fourth" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 丁 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

Mandarin dīng, cantonese ding1, and korean 정 (jeong) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese てい (TEI) is the on-reading, matching the character's own stored value. Vietnamese đinh is the character's sole stored reading. Part of a genuine three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group with [[艇]] ("dinghy, rowboat, boat") and [[釘]] ("nail") — coincidental, unrelated in meaning. Fixed a trailing space on `japanese` and removed a redundant `品詞` duplicate of `pos`.
