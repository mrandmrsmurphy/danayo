## Notes
- [平](../characters/平.md) "level; flat (stand-in: [[水平]])" + [行 (char)](../characters/行%20(char).md) "go; travel (stand-in: itself)"

Wiktionary lists Chinese 平行 as "of ranks etc.: equal", "concurrent; simultaneous", in mathematics "parallel" and "to run parallel"; Japanese 平行 is also listed. It fills the Directions gap for a general word for "parallel", beside [[並列]] and [[並立]].
