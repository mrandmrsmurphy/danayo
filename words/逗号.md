## Notes
Mandarin dòuhào and cantonese dau6 hou6 are directly attested standard readings. Japanese とうごう, korean 두호, and vietnamese đậu hiệu are all honest compositional readings (逗's own TOU/두/đậu + 号's own GOU/호/hiệu), replacing what would otherwise need to be each language's own everyday native term for "comma" (Japanese 読点/とうてん or カンマ; Korean 쉼표; Vietnamese dấu phẩy). `逗號` is the standard traditional-character spelling variant (号→號), correctly listed as an alias. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 닷핫 diverges from Sino-Korean 두호 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄨㄏㄚㄨ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 逗's `stand_in` (逗留) nor 号's own `stand_in` (符号) points to this compound.
