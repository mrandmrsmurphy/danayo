## Notes
- [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" + [工](../characters/工.md) "work, labor" — "artificial, man-made," from the sense of being made by human labor rather than occurring naturally. nhân công is standard Vietnamese, though there it more commonly means "labor, workforce" (as in giá nhân công, "labor cost") rather than "artificial" — documented as a partial, not exact, semantic match. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 닌공 diverges from Sino-Korean 인공 in the first syllable's initial consonant, since 工's own field independently diverges (despite 工 itself being `kwin: true`). No homophones (注音 ㄋㄧㄋㄍㄛㄫ unique among both words and characters).
