## Notes
- [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" + [工](../characters/工.md) "work, labor" — "artificial, man-made," from the sense of being made by human labor rather than occurring naturally. nhân công is standard Vietnamese, though there it more commonly means "labor, workforce" (as in giá nhân công, "labor cost") rather than "artificial" — documented as a partial, not exact, semantic match.
