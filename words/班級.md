## Notes
- [班](../characters/班%20(char).md) "group, section" + [級](../characters/級.md) "grade, rank" — "section and grade," a school class. Their own `stand_in` values: 班's is itself; 級's is [[等級]]; none points here, so 班級 is an independent compound.

班級 is a school class: a group of pupils taught together, or a year's grade. Mandarin bānjí and Cantonese baan1 kap1 are the ordinary Chinese words. Japanese and Korean use 班 or 学級 / 학급 for the same idea, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[班]] (group), [[学校]] (school) and [[課]] (lesson); [[等級]] "grade, ranking" is a different word.
