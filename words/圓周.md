## Notes
- [圓](../characters/圓%20(char).md) "circle" + [周](../characters/周.md) "circumference" — literally "circle-circumference," the circumference of a circle — stand-in for [[周]], which cannot appear independently.

Mandarin yuánzhōu, Cantonese jyun4 zau1, Japanese えんしゅう (enshū; historical kana ゑんしう), and Korean 원주 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 圓's and 周's own stored citations. Vietnamese chu vi is the real, standard math term for circumference/perimeter (built from 周's Sino-Vietnamese reading "chu," paired with 圍 "surround" rather than 圓 — a different compound structure than Dan'a'yo's 圓周, but the genuine external word for this concept); "đường tròn" would instead mean the circle itself.
