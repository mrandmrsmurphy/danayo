## Notes
- [圓](../characters/圓%20(char).md) "circle" + [周](../characters/周.md) "circumference" (stand-in for 圓周) — literally "circle-circumference," the circumference of a circle.

Mandarin yuánzhōu, Cantonese jyun4 zau1, Japanese ゑんしう (historical kana for えんしゅう, enshū), and Korean 원주 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 圓's and 周's own stored citations exactly. **Found and fixed a real bug**: `vietnamese` was stored as "đường tròn," which actually means "circle" (the shape itself), not "circumference" — a mismatch with this word's own English gloss. Corrected to "chu vi," the real, standard Vietnamese math term for circumference/perimeter (built from 周's own real Sino-Vietnamese reading "chu," paired with 圍 "surround" rather than 圓 — a different compound structure than Dan'a'yo's own 圓周, but the genuine external word for this concept).

`kwin` is false: 周 alone diverges from Korean on its own character page, which alone is enough to make the AND-rule false; 圓 is independently kwin-true. No homophones (注音 ⼔ㄋㄐㄨㄛ unique among both words and characters).
