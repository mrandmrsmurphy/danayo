## Notes
- [近 (char)](../characters/近%20(char).md) "near" + [代](../characters/代.md) "generation, era" — literally "near-era," i.e. the era close to the present.

近代 names the early modern period, following [中世](中世.md) ("the Middle Ages") and preceding the contemporary era proper — the specific period boundaries vary somewhat by historiographical convention, but it consistently denotes the pre-contemporary "recent past" era, roughly the 16th through early 20th centuries. This vault's own [歴史綱要](../lexipedia/歴史綱要.md) timeline uses 近代 directly as a section header, spanning the Yuan/Ming/Qing dynasties in China and the Kamakura through Edo periods in Japan.

Mandarin jìndài, Cantonese gan6 doi6, Korean 근대 (geundae), and Japanese きんだい (kindai) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real standard historiographical term in its language. Vietnamese cận đại is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, matching 近's cận and 代's own đại.
