## Notes
- [時](../characters/時%20(char).md) "when" + [宜](../characters/宜.md) "suitable, fitting" — no stand-in relationship applies (時's own stand-in is itself; 宜's own is [[適宜]]).

時宜 means timely, or to be the right time.

Mandarin shíyí, Cantonese si4 ji4, Japanese じぎ, Korean 시의, and Vietnamese thì nghi are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` had all used the wrong second syllable (위/wi/ㄨㄧ instead of 읫/'ǝi/ㄜㄧ, matching 宜's own citation — the same 'ǝi/wi minority-pattern bug documented elsewhere in this vault). No homophones at the corrected reading.
