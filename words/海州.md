## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [州](../characters/州%20(char).md) "state" — the name of Haeju, a port city of North Korea.

海州 (Haeju) is a port city of North Korea on a bay of the Yellow Sea and the capital of South Hwanghae Province ([[黄海]]南道) (from memory, not re-checked). The name reads "sea province".

English and Chinese Wiktionary have 海州 as the North Korean city (Mandarin Hǎizhōu, Cantonese hoi2 zau1), and English Wiktionary has a Korean entry as a hanja form of 해주. The same string is also Haizhou, a district of Lianyungang in Jiangsu and another of Fuxin in Liaoning in China. Japanese Wiktionary has no entry for the Korean city, so Japanese かいしゅう is composed from the on'yomi. Vietnamese Hải Châu is composed from the character pages (Hải Châu is also a district of Da Nang, from memory, not re-checked).
