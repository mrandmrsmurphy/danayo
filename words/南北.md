## Notes
- [南](../characters/南.md) "south" + [北](../characters/北.md) "north" — no stand-in relationship applies (南's own stand-in is [[南方]]; 北's is [[北方]]).

南北 means north and south — the two poles of the north-south axis (南北戦争 "the [American] Civil War," literally "North-South War").

Japanese なんぼく, Korean 남북, and Vietnamese nam bắc are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed missing space in `cantonese` (naam4bak1 → naam4 bak1); removed the redundant `品詞` field. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄚㄇㄅㄨㄎ unique across both words and characters).
