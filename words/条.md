## Notes
- [条 (char)](../characters/条%20(char).md) "long thin objects" — stand-in for [条 (char)](../characters/条%20(char).md), which is too bound to appear independently; this word gives it a usable form.

条 is the Dan'a'yo classifier for long, thin objects — 量詞, counting ropes, rivers, roads, fish, snakes, ribs, fingers, noodles, branches, rules, and news items. Following the grammar's rule (NP + numeral + classifier; see [文法 - 04句法](../grammar/文法%20-%2004句法.md)), the noun comes first and the numeral plus 条 follow it without a space: 路一条 (one road), 魚三条 (three fish), 繩兩条 (two ropes), 規則三条 (three rules), 消息一条 (one piece of news). These example phrases are illustrative, not drawn from a corpus; the grammar book's own attested example is 肋骨六条 与 歯五個 被砕已 "six ribs and five teeth were broken," where 条 counts the ribs and the general classifier 個 counts the teeth. In the grammar's classifier table 条 is the class "long, thin," with the examples "pencils, noodles, fingers." As with every Dan'a'yo classifier, the numeral and 条 are tightly bound in speech, and the noun phrase so counted is definite.

The character is the simplified form of 條 (listed as an alias on both this page and the character page). Per Wiktionary, 條 is a 形声 compound of the phonetic 攸 and the semantic 木 (wood), originally "a small branch," and probably also "a wooden stick"; the classifier function developed from the slender, linear shape of a twig. (An earlier version of this page described the character as a 會意 compound of 木 and 夊; Wiktionary gives no such analysis, and 夂 and 木 are only the visible parts of the simplified form's shape.) From the sense "strip; slip" 条 extended to abstract "items" that can be listed one by one, in legal and news contexts: 規則三条 (three rules), 消息一条 (one piece of news; illustrative).

Senses, following Wiktionary's Chinese entry for 條:
1. Classifier for long thin objects and animals (road, river, fish, snake, trousers, a strand of hair) — the sense of this word.
2. Classifier for items that can be listed one by one (reasons, pieces of news, messages, advice, problems), and for lives (一條命, "a life").
3. Strip, slip, twig, branch; and, of laws or treaties, an item, article or clause. In Dan'a'yo these nominal senses live in compounds such as [[条件]] (condition) and [[条約]] (treaty), not in the bare 条.

The CJKV classifier landscape for long thin objects shows strong convergence around 条/條. Mandarin 條 (tiáo) is listed on Wiktionary as a classifier for long thin objects or animals: 一條魚 (a fish), 一條蛇 (a snake), 一條路 (a road), 一條河 (a river), 一條褲子 (a pair of trousers, the garment treated as a single elongated item), and for lives, as in 九條命 (nine lives); in simplified spelling 一条新闻 (one news item) is from memory, not re-checked. Cantonese 條 (tiu4) is likewise the everyday classifier; from memory, not re-checked, 一條魚, 一條路 and 一條褲 parallel Mandarin closely. Japanese 条 (じょう) is on Wiktionary as a noun "article in a document" and has the kun reading すじ- (as a prefix); from memory, not re-checked, 本 (ほん) is the usual counter for long slender objects (一本の鉛筆, one pencil), and 条 counts articles (第三条, Article 3) and streaks such as 一条の光. Korean 조 (條) appears in Sino-Korean legal compounds: 제3조 (Article 3), 조항 (條項, clause; both from memory, not re-checked); for long objects, from memory, not re-checked, the native 줄 (line, rope) is used. Vietnamese điều (條, confirmed as the Sino-Vietnamese reading on hvdic.thivien.net) is used in legal language: điều 3 (Article 3), điều kiện (條件, condition); from memory, not re-checked, the native classifier for threadlike objects is sợi (strand: một sợi dây, one rope). Dan'a'yo uses 条 for the whole Chinese range of the classifier; its sense is the same as the shared CJKV sense.

条 shares its Dan'a'yo syllable ㄐㄛ with several characters ([佇](../characters/佇.md), [助](../characters/助.md), [猪](../characters/猪.md), [詛](../characters/詛.md)), but no other word. It is listed on [Dimensions](../lexipedia/Dimensions.md), [Measurement](../lexipedia/Measurement.md) (as the shape-based classifier beside 枚) and [Shape](../lexipedia/Shape.md).
