## Notes
- [特](../characters/特.md) "special, distinguished" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "word, expression" — no stand-in relationship applies (特's own stand-in is [[特別]]; 詞's own is [[単詞]]).

Along with [[実詞]], this is a coinage of <ruby>単亜語<rt>ㄉㄚㄋ·ㄚ·⼄</rt></ruby> to express its unique grammar. "Special words" are numerals, classifiers, determiners, interjections, ideophones; independent but never predicative. Unlike [[実詞]] (which turned out to be a genuine, standard piece of East Asian grammatical terminology once checked), 特詞 as a unified grammatical category does not correspond to any established real-world linguistics term in Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, Korean, or Vietnamese — real grammars instead split these functions across separate categories (数词/量词/代词/叹词, etc.) rather than grouping them under one label. Filled in fully compositional readings anyway (mandarin tècí, cantonese dak6 ci4, japanese とくし, korean 특사, vietnamese đặc từ, matching 特's and 詞's own stored citations exactly), since the underlying characters remain readable in each language even though the specific compound is a Dan'a'yo-internal coinage rather than attested vocabulary. Removed the redundant duplicate `品詞` field (kept `pos`). No homophones (注音 ㄉㄨㄎㄙㄚ unique across both words and characters).
