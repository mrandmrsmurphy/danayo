## Notes
- [碗](../characters/碗%20(char).md) "bowl, dish" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 碗 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

碗 names the bowl or dish, the everyday tableware item — 飯碗 ("rice bowl," also figuratively "one's livelihood, one's job"), 茶碗 ("teacup, tea bowl" — in Japanese specifically a rice bowl).

Mandarin wǎn, Cantonese wun2, Japanese わん (wan), Korean 완 (wan), and Vietnamese oản are all standard readings.
