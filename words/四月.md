## Notes
- [四 (char)](../characters/四%20(char).md) "four, fourth" + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — literally "fourth month," April.

Mandarin sìyuè, Cantonese sei3 jyut6, Japanese しがつ (shigatsu), and Korean 사월 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 四's and 月's own stored citations — unlike the weekday names, Chinese month names genuinely are formed this same compositional way in every source language, with no irregularity (April, unlike June's 유월, has no euphonic contraction in Korean). Vietnamese tháng tư ("month four") is the real native word, using Vietnamese's own number-based month naming rather than a Sino-Vietnamese compound.
