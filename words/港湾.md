## Notes
- [港](../characters/港.md) "harbor" + [湾](../characters/湾.md) "gulf, bay" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 港 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (湾's own stand-in is [[海湾]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

港湾 means harbor.

Mandarin gǎngwān, Cantonese gong2 waan1, Japanese こうわん, and Korean 항만 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 港's and 湾's own stored citations exactly). Fixed a real bug: `vietnamese` had been hải cảng, the reading of the unrelated near-synonym 海港 ("seaport," built on 海 rather than this word's own 港+湾) — corrected to the compositional cảng loan (港's own cảng + 湾's own citation loan). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄛㄫ·⺢ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
