## Notes
- [可 (char)](../characters/可%20(char).md) "can, may" + [笑 (char)](../characters/笑%20(char).md) "laugh" — literally "laughable," i.e. funny.

可笑 is real, standard Mandarin vocabulary (kěxiào) confirmed via Wiktionary, and is likewise attested in Cantonese (ho2 siu3). Notably it carries two related but distinct valences: "funny, amusing" (neutral, genuinely comic) and "ridiculous, laughable" (mocking, implying something is absurd or contemptible) — the same duality found in the Korean derivative 가소롭다 (gasoropda, "ridiculous, contemptible"), which independently confirms the dismissive sense survives as a living word.

`japanese` かしょう and `korean` 가소 are compositional Sino-Xenic readings, matching 可's and 笑's own stored citations exactly; Japanese and Korean Wiktionary entries exist for this compound but were not independently verified content-wise. `vietnamese` khả tiếu is likewise compositional (可's own khả + 笑's own tiếu), not independently confirmed as an attested Sino-Vietnamese term. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 크숏 diverges from Sino-Korean 가소 in both syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄜㄙ⼄ㄨ unique among both words and characters).
