## Notes
- [花](../characters/花.md) "flower" is separately legitimized by [[草花]]. [粉 (char)](../characters/粉%20(char).md) "powder" is separately legitimized by [[粉]] itself. No cranberry.

花粉 combines 花 ("flower") with 粉 ("powder") — "pollen."

Mandarin huāfěn, japanese かふん (kafun), korean 화분 (hwabun), and vietnamese phấn hoa (native word order) were all already correct.
