## Etymology
- [物 (char)](../characters/物%20(char).md) "thing, result" + [証](../characters/証.md) "prove, confirm, verify" — no stand-in relationship applies (物's own stand-in is itself; 証's own is [[証明]]).

物証 means material evidence.

Mandarin wùzhèng, Japanese ぶっしょう (BUTSU+SHOU), and Vietnamese vật chứng were already correct and fully compositional. Fixed a serious contamination bug in `cantonese`: wu2 zen4 (matching neither 物's own mat6 nor 証's own zing3) → mat6 zing3, fully compositional. Fixed a real character-level bug on `characters/証.md`: its own `korean` field held 정, but every other 証-compound in this vault ([[保証]] 보증, [[検証]] 검증, [[証券]] 증권, [[証拠]] 증거, [[証人]] 증인, [[証明]] 증명, [[認証]] 인증, [[通行証]] 통행증) consistently uses 증 — corrected the character citation to 증, confirming this word's own 물증 was already right, and retroactively fixed the same bug on the already-stamped [[証言]] (정언 → 증언). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄨㄊㄐㄧㄫ unique across both words and characters).
