## Notes
- [物 (char)](../characters/物%20(char).md) "thing, result" + [証](../characters/証.md) "prove, confirm, verify" — no stand-in relationship applies (物's own stand-in is itself; 証's own is [[証明]]).

物証 means material evidence.

Mandarin wùzhèng, Cantonese mat6 zing3, Japanese ぶっしょう, Korean 물증 and Vietnamese vật chứng are all fully compositional. Korean 증 is the reading of 証 in every compound here ([[保証]], [[検証]], [[証券]], [[証拠]], [[証人]], [[証明]], [[認証]], [[通行証]], [[証言]]).
