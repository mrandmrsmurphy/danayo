## Notes
- [頭 (char)](../characters/頭%20(char).md) "head" — the bare character used directly as a word, its own `stand_in`.

頭 means head: the part of the body above the neck (頭髮, "hair"; 頭痛, "headache"; 低頭, "bow the head"), and by extension the top, front, or leader of something (頭目, "chief"; 排頭, "head of a line"; 領頭, "lead"), or a first or beginning (從頭, "from the start"). As a suffix it also forms nouns (石頭, "stone"; 舌頭, "tongue"). The simplified form 头 is listed as an alias.

Mandarin tóu, Cantonese tau4, Korean 두 (du), and Sino-Vietnamese đầu (đầu tiên, "first") all preserve the Sino reading directly. Japanese あたま (atama) is the ordinary, everyday native word for the body part, with かしら ("head, chief") also native; the on'yomi readings とう and ず survive only bound in compounds (出頭/しゅっとう, 頭痛/ずつう), never standing alone for "head" itself. Korean has native 머리.
