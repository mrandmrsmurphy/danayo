## Notes
- [頭 (char)](../characters/頭%20(char).md) "head" — this word is 頭's own legitimizing compound (頭's `stand_in` points here).

頭 means "head." Mandarin tóu, Cantonese tau4, Korean 두 (du), and Sino-Vietnamese đầu all preserve the Sino reading directly. Japanese あたま (atama) is the ordinary, everyday native word for the body part; the on'yomi readings とう/ず survive only bound in compounds (出頭, 頭痛, etc.), never standing alone for "head" itself.
