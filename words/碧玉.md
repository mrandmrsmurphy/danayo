## Notes
- [碧](../characters/碧.md) "emerald, green" + [玉 (char)](../characters/玉%20(char).md) "jade" — literally "emerald jade."

碧玉 is the standard mineralogical/gemological term for jasper, an opaque, typically green (though sometimes red, yellow, or brown) variety of chalcedony quartz, valued as an ornamental and semiprecious stone since antiquity.

Mandarin bìyù, Cantonese bik1 juk6, Japanese へきぎょく (hekigyoku), Korean 벽옥 (byeogok), and Vietnamese bích ngọc are all fully compositional and are the standard gemological terms across the sphere.
