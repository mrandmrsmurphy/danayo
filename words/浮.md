## Notes
- [浮](../characters/浮%20(char).md) "float, waft" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 浮 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

浮 means to float or waft: to rest on the surface of a liquid or hang in the air without sinking (浮在水上, "float on water"; 浮雲, "floating clouds"; 浮標, "buoy"), and by extension to be superficial, unsettled, or transient (浮躁, "restless"; 浮華, "showy"; 浮生, "this fleeting life"). The graph is a 形声 compound of 氵 "water" with the phonetic 孚.

Mandarin fú, Cantonese fau4, Japanese ふ (浮力/ふりょく, "buoyancy"; 浮上/ふじょう, "surfacing"), Korean 부 (부력, 부상) and Vietnamese phù (phù du, "mayfly", literally "floating ephemeral"; phù hoa, "vain splendor") are the standard Sino-xenic readings. The character's other stored Vietnamese reading, nổi, is the native word for "float". Japanese uses the native verb 浮く (うく) for the everyday word, so on'yomi ふ is bound to compounds, and Korean has native 뜨다.
