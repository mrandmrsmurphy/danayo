## Notes
- [当](../characters/当%20(char).md) "while" + [代](../characters/代.md) "generation, era" — no stand-in relationship applies (当's own stand-in is itself; 代's own is [[世代]]).

当代 means contemporary or modern — belonging to the present era.

Mandarin dāngdài, Cantonese dong1 doi6, Japanese とうだい, Korean 당대, and Vietnamese đương đại are all standard readings (the real Vietnamese term đương đại uses a reading of 代 not currently listed among 代's own citation aliases — flagged for a future character pass).
