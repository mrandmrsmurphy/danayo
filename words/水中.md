## Notes
- [水 (char)](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [中 (char)](../characters/中%20(char).md) "midst" — literally "in the water," underwater.

1. underwater
2. Mizunaka, a district village of Takayama, Kamitakai district, Nagano Prefecture, Japan
3. Mizunaka, a surname

Mandarin shuǐzhōng, Cantonese seoi2 zung1, Japanese すいちゅう (suichū), and Korean 수중 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 水's and 中's own stored citations exactly. **Found and fixed a real bug**: `korean` was stored as "수중, 물속," mixing the real compositional Sino-Korean word (수중) together with a native vernacular alternative (물속, "underwater," built from native 물 "water" + 속 "inside") — the latter belongs in Notes prose, not the `korean` field.

`kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 수중 coincides exactly with Sino-Korean 수중, since both 水 and 中 independently carry matching readings on their own character pages. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨㄐㄨㄫ unique among both words and characters).
