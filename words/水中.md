## Notes
- [水 (char)](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [中](../characters/中.md) "midst" — literally "in the water," underwater.

Mandarin shuǐzhōng, Cantonese seoi2 zung1, Japanese すいちゅう (suichū), and Korean 수중 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 水's and 中's own stored citations exactly.

1. underwater
2. Mizunaka, a district village of Takayama, Kamitakai district, Nagano Prefecture, Japan
3. Mizunaka, a surname
