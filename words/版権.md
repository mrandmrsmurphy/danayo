## Etymology
- [版](../characters/版.md) "edition, version" + [権](../characters/権.md) "power, right, authority" — no stand-in relationship applies (版's own stand-in is [[版本]]; 権's own is [[権利]]).

版権 means copyright.

Mandarin bǎnquán, Cantonese baan2 kyun4, Japanese はんけん (HAN+KEN), Korean 판권, and Vietnamese bản quyền were all already correct and fully compositional. Fixed a real character-level citation gap on `characters/権.md`: its `vietnamese` field was entirely blank despite the reading quyền already being relied on here (bản quyền) and in real terms like nhân quyền "human rights" — added it. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄚㄋㄍ⼔ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
