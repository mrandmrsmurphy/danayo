## Notes
- [虱 (char)](../characters/虱%20(char).md) "louse" — the bare character used directly as a word, its own `stand_in`.

虱 means louse (plural lice): a small wingless parasitic insect that lives on the skin or hair of humans and animals (虱子, "louse"; 頭虱, "head louse"; 虱目魚, "milkfish", a Taiwanese name). The traditional form 蝨 is listed as an alias; the graph is a 形声 compound of 虫 "insect" with the phonetic 卂.

Mandarin shī, Cantonese sat1, Japanese しつ, Korean 슬, and Vietnamese sắt are the Sino-xenic readings, which survive almost only in learned or compound use. Japanese uses the native noun 虱 (しらみ) for the everyday word; Korean uses the native 이 and Vietnamese the native chấy or rận.
