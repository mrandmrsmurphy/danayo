## Notes
- [必](../characters/必%20(char).md) "surely, definitely" + [要](../characters/要.md) "important, need" — no stand-in relationship applies (必's own stand-in is itself; 要's own is [[重要]]).

必要 means necessary or essential — required, indispensable.

Mandarin bìyào, Cantonese bit1 jiu3, Japanese ひつよう, Korean 필요, and Vietnamese tất yếu are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `japanese` had archaic pre-reform kana ひつえう — modernized to ひつよう. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄧㄊ⼄ㄨ unique across both words and characters).
