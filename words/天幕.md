## Notes
- [天](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven, sky" + [幕](../characters/幕%20(char).md) "curtain" — "sky curtain," a tent. Their own `stand_in` values: 天's is itself; 幕's is itself; none points here, so 天幕 is an independent compound.

天幕 is a tent: a canopy of cloth stretched over a frame. Japanese てんまく and Korean 천막 use it for a tent; Mandarin tiānmù and Cantonese tin1 mok6 mean "the canopy of the sky" and are used for a tent only in the literary register, where the ordinary Mandarin word is 帳篷. It is attested in the same characters in four languages, though the sense is not the same everywhere.
