## Notes
- [忽](../characters/忽.md) "sudden" + [然](../characters/然%20(char).md) "so" + [様](../characters/様%20(char).md) "-ly (adverbializer)" — no stand-in relationship applies (忽's own stand-in is [[忽然]]; 然/様 are both their own stand-ins).

忽然様 means suddenly — an adverbial form built on [[忽然]] with the Dan'a'yo adverbializer 様.

Mandarin hūrányàng, Cantonese fat1 jin4 joeng6, Japanese こつぜんよう, Korean 홀연양, and Vietnamese hốt nhiên dạng are all standard, fully compositional readings.
