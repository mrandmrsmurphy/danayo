## Notes
- [洗](../characters/洗%20(char).md) "wash" + [碗](../characters/碗%20(char).md) "bowl" + [机](../characters/机.md) "machine" — "bowl-washing machine." Their own `stand_in` values: 洗's is itself; 碗's is itself; 机's is [[机会]]; none points here, so 洗碗机 is an independent compound.

洗碗机 is a dishwasher. Mandarin xǐwǎnjī and Cantonese sai2 wun2 gei1 are the ordinary Chinese words. Japanese says 食器洗い機 and Korean 식기세척기, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[洗衣机]] (washing machine) and [[机器]] (machine).
