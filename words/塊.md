## Notes
- [塊 (char)](../characters/塊%20(char).md) "lump; piece; chunk; clump"

塊 means "a lump, piece, chunk, clump" and functions as a measure word for chunky/lump-shaped objects (一塊肉, "a piece of meat"; 一塊石頭, "a chunk of stone"; 塊頭, "the size/bulk of a person").

Mandarin kuài and Cantonese faai3 preserve the sense directly. Japanese カイ (KAI, on'yomi, formal register) alongside native かたまり (katamari, "a lump, mass," the everyday noun) cover the sense. Korean 괴 (goe) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese khối is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in khối lượng ("mass," physics) and một khối ("a block, a mass"); it also shares its phonetic series with 傀 (khôi, as in khôi lỗi 傀儡, "puppet"), the level-tone counterpart to 塊's departing tone. The character page's other stored candidates: hòn is a legitimate native Vietnamese doublet assigned by meaning (hòn đá, "a stone," the everyday word for a lump-shaped object); cỏi is plausibly connected to modern cõi ("realm, territory," via 塊's "clod of earth" sense); khói is attested but belongs to an unrelated native word meaning "smoke," reusing 塊's glyph purely for its sound; a further candidate, khỏi ("to avoid, escape"), could not be confirmed in dictionary sources at all and looks like a likely transcription slip (easily confused with khói/khối, differing only in tone) rather than a genuine reading — flagged rather than silently kept.
