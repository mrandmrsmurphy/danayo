## Notes
- [検](../characters/検.md) "to examine; inspect" + [索](../characters/索.md) "cord; to search"

検索 (traditional 檢索) is "to search; to look up; to retrieve," used of looking something up in a database, index or catalogue. According to Wiktionary, Chinese 檢索 (jiǎnsuǒ, Cantonese gim2 sok3 or gim2 saak3), Japanese けんさく and Korean 검색 all mean "to retrieve; to search; to look up," with the Korean sense including "to browse; to surf." Wiktionary has no Vietnamese section, so `vietnamese` kiểm sách is a compositional reading, not independently attested. In the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [平臥即是正義](../translation/平臥即是正義.md) it appears in 人気 検索 単語, "popular search words," standing in for the Chinese 热搜. Related: [捜索](捜索.md) "search for," which looks for something physical or lost, where 検索 looks something up.
