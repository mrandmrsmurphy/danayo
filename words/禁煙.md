## Notes
- [禁](../characters/禁.md) "prohibit, forbid" + [煙](../characters/煙.md) "smoke, tobacco" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (禁's is [[禁止]], 煙's is [[煙気]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

禁煙 means "no smoking" as a posted rule or sign, and by extension "smoking cessation" as a personal or public-health effort (禁煙運動, "anti-smoking campaign"; 禁煙外来, "smoking-cessation clinic"). It is one of the most common everyday prohibition compounds across the sphere, alongside [[禁酒]] ("no alcohol").

Mandarin jìnyān, Cantonese gam3 jin1, Japanese きんえん (kin'en), and Korean 금연 (geumyeon) are all standard, fully compositional readings, each the ordinary everyday word for "no smoking" in its language. Vietnamese cấm yên is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form; everyday Vietnamese more often says cấm hút thuốc ("forbid smoking tobacco") analytically.
