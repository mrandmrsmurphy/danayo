## Notes
- [閏 (char)](../characters/閏%20(char).md) "intercalary" + [秒 (char)](../characters/秒%20(char).md) "second (time)"

閏秒 is the leap second. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 閏秒 (Mandarin rùnmiǎo, Cantonese jeon6 miu5) as "leap second". English Wikipedia says leap seconds were one-second adjustments occasionally applied to Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) between 1972 and 2016, to accommodate the difference between International Atomic Time (TAI) and observed solar time (UT1), which varies with irregularities and the long-term slowdown of the Earth's rotation. In total 27 leap seconds were added, the last on 31 December 2016, so that UTC has differed from TAI by 37 seconds since January 2017. The Japanese うるうびょう and Korean 윤초 fields are built from the readings of the two characters. Compare the [閏日](閏日.md), leap day, and the [閏月](閏月.md), leap month.
