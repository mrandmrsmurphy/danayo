## Notes
- [発 (char)](../characters/発%20(char).md) "issue forth, discharge" + [電](../characters/電.md) "electricity" — literally "to issue forth electricity," i.e. to generate power. 発's own stand-in is [[発]] itself and 電's own is [[電気]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; neither constituent is legitimized by this word.

発電 names the process of generating electrical power — 発電所 ("power plant/power station") is the everyday compound built from it. Mandarin fādiàn, Cantonese faat3 din6, Japanese はつでん (hatsuden), and Vietnamese phát điện are all transparent, compositional, and used without ambiguity.

Korean is the exception worth noting: 발전 is genuinely ambiguous in isolation, since it is also the Sino-Korean reading of 發展 ("development, progress") — 展 and 電 happen to share the same Korean syllable 전. Context (전기를 발전하다 "to generate electricity" vs. plain 발전 "development") normally disambiguates, but the homophony is a real feature of the language, not an artifact of this vault's derivation.
