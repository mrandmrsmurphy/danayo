## Notes
- [延](../characters/延.md) "extend, prolong" + [性](../characters/性.md) "nature, quality" — ductility.

延性 is the property of a metal that lets it be drawn out into wire without breaking, as gold, copper and silver can be. It is the pair of [[展性]] (malleability, spreading under the hammer): 延 is stretching along one dimension, 展 is spreading across a surface. 性 "nature, quality" is the same suffix as in [[磁性]]. Mandarin yánxìng, Japanese えんせい and Korean 연성 are the standard physics and materials-science term. Compare [[金箔]], which depends on gold's extreme malleability.
