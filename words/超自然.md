## Notes
- [超 (char)](../characters/超%20(char).md) "exceed; beyond" + [自](../characters/自.md) "self" + [然 (char)](../characters/然%20(char).md) "so; thus"

超自然 is the supernatural: what lies beyond [自然](自然.md), nature, and its laws. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin chāozìrán as "supernatural" and Japanese ちょうしぜん as "the supernatural". Vietnamese siêu tự nhiên is the same word, and Korean 초자연 and Cantonese ciu1 zi6 jin4 match.

A [奇跡](奇跡.md), miracle, is its typical event, and its beings are the ghosts and gods of [宗教](宗教.md).
