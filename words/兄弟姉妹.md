## Notes
"Siblings, brothers and sisters" — a four-character compound spelling out all four sibling roles individually, common Japanese usage (兄弟姉妹). `vietnamese: anh chị em` is the standard everyday native Vietnamese phrase for "siblings," using kinship terms rather than a Sino-Vietnamese calque of these specific four characters. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 횽더자뫼 diverges from the compositional Sino-Korean 형제자매 in multiple syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⼄ㄫㄉㄝㄐㄚㄇㄛㄧ unique among both words and characters).
