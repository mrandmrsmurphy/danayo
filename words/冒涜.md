## Notes
- [冒](../characters/冒.md) "risk, offend" + [涜](../characters/涜.md) "insult, defile" — "offend and defile," blasphemy. Their own `stand_in` values: 冒's is [[冒険]]; 涜's is [[溝涜]]; none points here, so 冒涜 is an independent compound.

冒涜 is blasphemy: speech or action that insults or profanes what is held sacred. Mandarin màodú (冒瀆), Cantonese mou6 duk6, Japanese ぼうとく and Korean 모독 all use it. The character 涜 has the alias 瀆. Compare [[呪詛]] (curse, damn) and [[悪魔]] (devil).
