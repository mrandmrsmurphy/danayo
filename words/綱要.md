## Notes
- [綱](../characters/綱.md) "heavy rope, outline" + [要](../characters/要.md) "important, need" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (綱's is [[大綱]], 要's is [[重要]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 要 is individually `kwin: false`.

綱要 names an outline, sketch, or compendium of essentials.

Mandarin gāngyào, Cantonese gong1 jiu3, Japanese こうよう (kōyō), and Korean 강요 (gangyo) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 綱要 is itself a common attested word for "outline, compendium" in all four languages. Vietnamese cương yếu is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term. **Fixed a real bug**: `注音` had ㄍㄚㄋ·⼄ㄨ (an -n coda) instead of 綱's real -ng coda (ㄍㄚㄫ) — corrected to ㄍㄚㄫ·⼄ㄨ, matching 羅馬字/諺文, which had already been correct; the same wrong `注音` was independently duplicated on both `characters/綱.md`'s and `characters/要.md`'s own citations of this word, fixed there too. Removed a redundant duplicate `品詞` field and two empty-string fields (`vietnamese: ""`, `swadesh: ""`). No Dan'a'yo homophones.
