## Notes
- [綱](../characters/綱.md) "heavy rope, outline" + [要](../characters/要.md) "important, need" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (綱's is [[大綱]], 要's is [[重要]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply.

綱要 names an outline, sketch, or compendium of essentials.

Mandarin gāngyào, Cantonese gong1 jiu3, Japanese こうよう (kōyō), and Korean 강요 (gangyo) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 綱要 is itself a common attested word for "outline, compendium" in all four languages. Vietnamese cương yếu is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term.
