## Notes
- [新 (char)](../characters/新%20(char).md) "new" + [手 (char)](../characters/手%20(char).md) "hand" — literally "a new hand", a person new to a skill or activity. No stand-in relationship applies (新's own `stand_in` is itself; 手's is itself).

新手 is a beginner, a novice: someone new to a skill, game, or craft. It is the word for "beginner" where [[初心]] is avoided (see [[文法 - 91疑義語]]: Japanese reads 初心 as "beginner", Chinese as "original aspiration"), and it is narrower than [[新人]], which is a newcomer to a group or organization.

Wiktionary records Mandarin xīnshǒu and Cantonese san1 sau2 as "beginner; novice; newcomer". Japanese has あらて and しんて for the same characters, with the senses "reinforcements" and "new method" as well as "beginner"; these are dated, so the `japanese` field gives the compositional on'yomi しんしゅ (not separately verified). Korean 신수 is the compositional Sino-Korean reading and was not verified in a dictionary. Dan'a'yo's own 신슈 differs from it (手 is syu), so `kwin` is false. `vietnamese` is tân thủ, the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading (新 tân + 手 thủ) added at the user's direction; hvdic has no entry for it.
