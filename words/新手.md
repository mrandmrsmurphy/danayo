## Notes
- [新 (char)](../characters/新%20(char).md) "new" + [手 (char)](../characters/手%20(char).md) "hand" — literally "a new hand", a person new to a skill or activity. No stand-in relationship applies (新's own `stand_in` is itself; 手's is itself).

Wiktionary: Chinese "beginner; novice; newcomer"; Japanese 新手 "beginner, novice, newcomer, a greenhorn" (from 1886), "reinforcements, reserves" (from 1220) and "a new method, a new way of doing something"; Vietnamese tân thủ is the Han form, "newbie, rookie, novice". It is the word for "beginner" where [[初心]] is avoided (see [[文法 - 91疑義語]]), and is narrower than [[新人]].
