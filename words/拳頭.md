## Notes
- [拳](../characters/拳.md) "fist" + [頭](../characters/頭%20(char).md) "head" — no stand-in relationship applies (拳's own stand-in is [[拳骨]]; 頭's own is itself).

拳頭 means fist.

Mandarin quántou, Japanese けんとう, and Vietnamese quyền đầu are all standard, fully compositional readings. Cantonese kyun4-2 tau4 shows the regular tone-change (變調) of 拳 from base tone4 to tone2 in this compound. Korean 권두 (compositional) coexists with the more common native word for "fist," 주먹. Fixed a stray zero-width space in `japanese`. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼔ㄋㄊㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).
