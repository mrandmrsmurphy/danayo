## Notes
- [三 (char)](../characters/三%20(char).md) "three" + [猿](../characters/猿.md) "ape, monkey" — names the Three Wise Monkeys (見猿・聞か猿・言わ猿, "see no evil, hear no evil, speak no evil"), most famously carved at Nikkō Tōshō-gū.

Japanese さんざる (san-zaru, with rendaku on さる "monkey") is the real, famous popular reading for this specific cultural icon — **fixed a comma-joined contamination bug** where a less-common alternate on'yomi-based reading, さんえん, had been jammed into the same field; kept さんざる as the primary reading. **Fixed a real garbage-value bug**: `vietnamese` had been `phiên âm` ("phonetic transcription," a completely unrelated term) — corrected to `tam viên`, the plain compositional reading built from 猿's own stored `viên`. Mandarin Sānyuán and Cantonese saam1 jyun4 are standard compositional readings. Korean 세 원숭이 ("three monkeys," native) is kept as a literal descriptive phrase. `kwin` is false (AND-rule: 三 true, 猿 false).
