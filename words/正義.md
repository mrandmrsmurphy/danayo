## Notes
- [正](../characters/正%20(char).md) "correct" + [義](../characters/義.md) "meaning, principle" — no stand-in relationship applies (正's own stand-in is itself; 義's own is [[意義]]).

正義 means justice.

Mandarin zhèngyì, Cantonese zing3 ji6, Japanese せいぎ, Korean 정의, and Vietnamese chính nghĩa are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 正's and 義's own stored citations exactly).
