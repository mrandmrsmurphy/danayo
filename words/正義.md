## Notes
- [正](../characters/正%20(char).md) "correct" + [義](../characters/義.md) "meaning, principle" — no stand-in relationship applies (正's own stand-in is itself; 義's own is [[意義]]).

正義 means justice.

Mandarin zhèngyì, Cantonese zing3 ji6, Japanese せいぎ, Korean 정의, and Vietnamese chính nghĩa are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 正's and 義's own stored citations exactly). Fixed the 'ǝi/wi minority-pattern bug: `諺文`/`羅馬字` had used 위/wi instead of 義's own correct 읫/'ǝi (the same pattern previously found and fixed on [[時宜]]). No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄫㄜㄧ unique across both words and characters).
