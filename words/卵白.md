## Notes
- [卵](../characters/卵.md) "egg" + [白](../characters/白%20(char).md) "white" — no stand-in relationship applies (卵's own stand-in is [[卵子]]; 白 is its own stand-in).

卵白 means egg white (albumen).

[[蛋白]] ("egg white; protein") is not an alias: it is a separate, independent word with its own distinct reading (ㄉㄚㄋㄅㄚㄎ) and its own broader sense extending to "protein."
