## Notes
- [卵](../characters/卵.md) "egg" + [白](../characters/白%20(char).md) "white" — no stand-in relationship applies (卵's own stand-in is [[卵子]]; 白 is its own stand-in).

卵白 means egg white (albumen).

**Real bug found and fixed**: `cantonese` was blank — filled leon2 baak6 (compositional). Fixed a typo in `korean` (횐자위 → 흰자위, the real native Korean word for egg white, not a compositional Sino-Korean reading — a genuine native-equivalent choice, matching the pattern seen elsewhere, e.g. [[単詞]]'s 단어). Japanese らんぱく and Vietnamese lòng trắng (also native, "white part") are both standard. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄚㄋㄅㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).

**Not an alias**: [[蛋白]] ("egg white; protein") was previously listed here as an alias, but it is a separate, independent word with its own distinct reading (ㄉㄚㄋㄅㄚㄎ) and its own broader sense extending to "protein" — removed from `aliases`, related here in prose instead.
