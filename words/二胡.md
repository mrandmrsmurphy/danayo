## Notes
- [二 (char)](../characters/二%20(char).md) "two" + [胡](../characters/胡.md) "barbarian; bow"

二胡 is an erhu: the Chinese two-stringed bowed fiddle. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin èrhú as "erhu" and Japanese にこ as "erhu". Korean 이호 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese ji6 wu4-2 matches.

It is a bowed instrument, as the [提琴](提琴.md), violin family, is, and the plucked one of China is the [琵琶](琵琶.md).
