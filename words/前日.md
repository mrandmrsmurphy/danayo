## Notes
- [前 (char)](../characters/前%20(char).md) "prior" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — literally "prior day."

前日 names the prior day — one of a cluster of near-synonymous "previous day" compounds this vault distinguishes by register (compare [昨日](昨日.md), the everyday "yesterday," and [次日](次日.md), the more literary "the following/prior day" depending on context).

Mandarin qiánrì, Cantonese cin4 jat6, Korean 전일 (jeonil), and Japanese ぜんじつ (zenjitsu) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese tiền nhật is likewise compositional, from 前's tiền (one of two attested readings) and 日's own nhật.
