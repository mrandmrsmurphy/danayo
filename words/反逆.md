## Notes
- [反 (char)](../characters/反%20(char).md) "rebel; anti-" + [逆 (char)](../characters/逆%20(char).md) "rebellion; betrayal"

反逆 is treason: to turn against one's own side, state or ruler. Wiktionary glosses Japanese はんぎゃく as "revolt, rebellion; treason; to revolt, to rebel", and Mandarin fǎnnì as "to betray; to rebel". Korean 반역 is the matching form. The alias 叛逆 is the older spelling.

The word is a legal and political charge, and it differs from the broader [反乱](反乱.md), which names an uprising by force. 反逆 emphasises betrayal of allegiance, and the offender is the 逆, "traitor", of [逆](逆.md).
