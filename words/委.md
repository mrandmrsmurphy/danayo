## Notes
- [委 (char)](../characters/委%20(char).md) "appoint, commission" — used independently as a standalone verb (委員, "committee member"; 委任, "to entrust, appoint"). `vietnamese: uỷ` is a directly attested standard Sino-Vietnamese reading with the same "appoint, entrust" sense (uỷ nhiệm, "to commission/entrust"; uỷ viên, "committee member"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 웨 diverges from Sino-Korean 위 — a single-syllable comparison where the two readings simply differ. **Completing the reciprocal homophone callout already anticipated on [[偽]]'s own page**: this word shares its exact Dan'a'yo pronunciation ('wei/웨/⼔ㄧ) with [[偽]] ("false, artificial") — both pages now cross-link.
