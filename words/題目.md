## Notes
- [題](../characters/題.md) "topic" + [目 (char)](../characters/目%20(char).md) "eye" — no `#cranberry`: 題's own `stand_in` is [[標題]], 目's is 目 itself; neither points here.

題目 (tímù) is the standard Mandarin word for "title, topic," and Cantonese 題目 (tai4muk6) likewise. Japanese だいもく (daimoku) and Korean 제목 (jemok) are both standard everyday words in their languages, as is Sino-Vietnamese đề mục — a clean case with full agreement across all five languages.
