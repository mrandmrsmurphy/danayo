## Notes
- [京](../characters/京.md) "capital" + [畿](../characters/畿.md) "capital district" — "capital province," the territory immediately surrounding and administratively tied to a capital city. `vietnamese: kinh kỳ` is directly attested (confirmed via web search), and this word's own `korean: 경기` is the exact modern name of Gyeonggi Province, the province surrounding Seoul — a live continuation of this same term, not just a historical curiosity. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 경긔 diverges from Sino-Korean 경기 in the second syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼶ㄫㄍㄧㄜ unique among both words and characters). Legitimizing note: 畿's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, so 畿 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word; 京's own `stand_in` is [[京城]], not this word, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply.
