## Notes
- [三 (char)](../characters/三%20(char).md) "three" + [万](../characters/万.md) "ten-thousand" — thirty thousand, 30,000.

Digit + 万, like [[二万]] and [[七万]]: the myriad (10,000) is the Sinitic grouping unit, so 30,000 is literally "three myriad" and is not built through a "thousand" step as in English. Japanese さんまん is the plain concatenation. Korean 삼만 follows the Sino-Korean reading directly. Vietnamese ba vạn uses vạn, which is a live everyday numeral rather than only a classical one. Compare [[二万]] and [[七万]].
