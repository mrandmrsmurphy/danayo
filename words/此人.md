## Notes
- [此 (char)](../characters/此%20(char).md) "this; proximal demonstrative" + [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person; human being"

此人 is the proximal correlative in the 人 ("person") column of the correlative table: it picks out the person nearest the speaker or already in focus, "this person here." The character [此 (char)](../characters/此%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *cǝ*, ㄑㄜ) is the classical Chinese proximal demonstrative, contrasting with 其 (medial, near the addressee or already mentioned) and 彼 (distal, away from both); 人 is the plain, register-neutral word for a person. Dan'a'yo uses the compound as the proximal human deictic, parallel to [此物](此物.md) "this thing" and [此事](此事.md) "this matter" in the other rows. The grammar's own comparative section uses it in a sentence, 此人末一強 "this person is the least strong" (see `grammar/文法 - 05形態.md`).

The nearest cross-linguistic equivalents are: Mandarin 此人 (cǐrén), which Wiktionary lists as a noun glossed "this person" (it carries no register label there; that it is mostly written or formal is from memory, not re-checked); Japanese この人 (kono hito), which Wiktionary has as its own entry (compound of この "this" + 人 "person"), with the native adnominal この rather than the Sino-Japanese 此; and Korean 이 사람 (i saram), the native demonstrative 이 + 사람 "person" (from memory, not re-checked). The `japanese` and `korean` fields therefore hold these spoken phrases, matching the sibling pages [其人](其人.md) and [彼人](彼人.md), rather than Sino-Xenic readings of the two characters. No Dan'a'yo-specific narrowing is needed: the sense is the shared CJKV one.

Unlike [其人](其人.md), which also serves as Dan'a'yo's gender-neutral third-person pronoun "he/she/they", 此人 is only a deictic pointing to someone present or in focus, not a substitute for a personal pronoun in narrative. Its siblings in the person column are [彼人](彼人.md) "that person yonder" (distal), [何人](何人.md) (rude "who?"), [某人](某人.md) "someone; a certain person", and [皆人](皆人.md) "everyone"; the interrogative slot is normally filled by 誰, and the grammar's note on the ladder reads "誰 is normal, 何人 is rude, 何名 is very polite" (`grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`). The 名 row supplies the polite counterpart of this word, [此名](此名.md) "this person (polite)"; 此人 itself is stylistically neutral.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md)'s correlative table, 此人 is the third cell of the 此 ("this") row, after [此事](此事.md) "this matter" and [此物](此物.md) "this thing" and before [此名](此名.md), [此処](此処.md) "this place", [此時](此時.md) "this time", [此様](此様.md) "this manner", [此多](此多.md) "this amount" and [此類](此類.md) "this kind". The table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), 63 forms in all, so 此人 is parsed compositionally: 此 = proximal, 人 = person. The ㄑㄜ·ㄋㄧㄋ 注音 carries a syllable-boundary dot because the string could also be read ㄑㄜㄋ + ㄧㄋ (a coda ㄋ followed by a null-onset syllable), which the (C)(G)V(C) syllable structure in `grammar/文法 - 02音韻論.md` does not rule out; the dot marks the intended ㄑㄜ + ㄋㄧㄋ.
