## Notes
- [毎](../characters/毎%20(char).md) "each" + [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — no stand-in relationship applies (毎's own stand-in is itself; 日's own is also itself).

毎日 means every day, daily, or per day.

Mandarin měirì, Cantonese mui5 jat6, Japanese まいにち, and Korean 매일 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 毎's and 日's own stored citations exactly).
