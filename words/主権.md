## Notes
- [主](../characters/主.md) "lord, sovereign" + [権](../characters/権.md) "authority, right" — sovereignty, the supreme authority of a state over its own territory and affairs, as in 国家主権 ("state sovereignty").

主権 is the standard political-science term for sovereignty across the Sinosphere: Mandarin zhǔquán, Cantonese zyu2 kyun4, Japanese しゅけん, and Korean 주권 are all fully compositional and mutually transparent. Common collocations include 主権国家 (sovereign state), 主権在民 (popular sovereignty, "sovereignty resides in the people"), and 領土主権 (territorial sovereignty). Vietnamese chủ quyền is the direct Sino-Vietnamese reflex (chủ = 主, quyền = 権/權) and is likewise the standard modern term, appearing constantly in Vietnamese political and legal discourse (chủ quyền quốc gia, "national sovereignty"). `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 주권 coincides exactly with Sino-Korean 주권.
