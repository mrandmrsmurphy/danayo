## Notes
- [季](../characters/季.md) "season, youngest" + [冬](../characters/冬%20(char).md) "winter" — no stand-in relationship applies (季's own stand-in is [[季節]]; 冬's own is itself).

Third and final month of winter — the last month of the entire year. The old year gathers itself for sacrifice and giving way. Corresponds to [[丑月]] and [[臘月]].

Mandarin jìdōng, Cantonese gwai3 dung1, Korean 계동, Japanese きとう, and Vietnamese Quý Đông are all standard, fully compositional readings.
