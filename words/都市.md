## Notes
- [都](characters/都.md) "capital, metropolis" + [市](characters/市.md) "market, city" — "city, metropolis."

Mandarin dūshì, cantonese dou1 si5, japanese とし, korean 도시, and vietnamese đô thị (a directly attested, extremely common Vietnamese word) are all directly attested standard readings. **Found and fixed a genuinely missing "đô" candidate on 都's own `vietnamese` field**: only giô/đo/đu had been listed, omitting the standard reading used compositionally here (and in words like 首都, "thủ đô") — added. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 도시 matches Sino-Korean 도시 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄙㄧ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 市's own `stand_in` is this exact word (都市), so 市 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 都市; 都's own `stand_in` is instead 首都, so this word does not legitimize 都.
