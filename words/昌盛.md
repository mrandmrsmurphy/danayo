## Notes
- [昌](../characters/昌.md) "prosperous" + [盛](../characters/盛.md) "flourish" — this word is the stand-in for [昌](../characters/昌.md), which cannot appear independently.

昌盛 means prosperous or flourishing. The character 昌 alone means prosperous, but is too bounded to stand by itself in Dan'a'yo, so this compound serves as its viable form.
