## Notes
- [紀](../characters/紀.md) "century" + [元](../characters/元.md) "element" + [後 (char)](../characters/後%20(char).md) "after" — none of the three characters' own `stand_in` points here (紀's is [[世紀]], 元's is [[元素]], 後's is [[後]] itself), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 後 is individually `kwin: false`.

紀元後 names the era after the Common Era's starting point — A.D./C.E., the counterpart of [[紀元前]] "B.C./B.C.E."

Mandarin jìyuánhòu, Cantonese gei2 jyun4 hau6, Japanese きげんご (kigengo, confirmed via web search), and Korean 기원후 (giwonhu) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 紀元後 is itself a common attested word for "AD" in all four languages. Vietnamese kỷ nguyên hậu is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading, though the idiomatic Vietnamese phrase for "AD" is instead the native sau Công Nguyên ("after the Common Era"). No Dan'a'yo homophones.
