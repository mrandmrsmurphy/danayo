## Notes
- [陝](../characters/陝.md) "Shan (a pass)" + [西](../characters/西.md) "west" — "west of the Shan Pass", the pass being in Shanzhou in Henan. 西's own stand-in is [[西方]]; 陝 is a 名専字 (a character restricted to proper names), and this word is where the vault uses it. 陕 and 陜 are aliases of 陝, so the filename uses 陝; 陕西 and 陜西 are kept as the word's aliases.

陝西 (Shaanxi) is a province of northwest China on the middle Yellow River, whose capital is [[西安]] (from memory, not re-checked). English Wiktionary says the name comes from its position west of the Shan Pass in Shanzhou, Henan, through which the Yellow River flows onto the North China Plain. It is not to be confused with the neighbouring [[山西]] (Shanxi): the Mandarin letters differ only by a tone mark (Shǎnxī against Shānxī), and in Dan'a'yo the two are different words, separated by their first syllable (ㄙㄝㄇ against ㄙㄚㄋ).

The Japanese field holds せんせい (English Wiktionary Japanese). The Korean field holds the Sino-Korean 섬서 (Wiktionary Korean: hanja form of 섬서). Vietnamese Thiểm Tây is the Sino-Vietnamese name. Cantonese sim2 sai1 is the Jyutping on English Wiktionary. The Dan'a'yo reading 陝西 ㄙㄝㄇㄙㄝㄧ shares its 注音 with 繊細 (see the callout above).

Attestation, checked 2026-10-11: English and Chinese Wiktionary (Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese sections) list only the province for this name.
