## Notes
- [怪](../characters/怪.md) "strange, suspicious" + [物](../characters/物%20(char).md) "thing" — no stand-in relationship applies (怪's own stand-in is [[怪異]]; 物's own is itself).

怪物 means monster — a mysterious, strange figure. This is distinct from 妖物, which specifically denotes a bewitching or enchanting creature.

Mandarin guàiwù, Cantonese gwaai3 mat6, Japanese かいぶつ, Korean 괴물, and Vietnamese quái vật are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⺢ㄧㄇㄨㄊ unique across both words and characters).
