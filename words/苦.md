## Notes
- [苦](characters/苦%20(char).md) "suffering, bitter" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 苦 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

苦 means suffering or bitter. Also used as an abbreviation for "magnesium": <ruby>[苦土素](/words/苦土素.md)<rt>ㄎㄛㄊㄛㄙㄛ</rt></ruby>

Mandarin kǔ, Cantonese fu2, Japanese く, Korean 고, and Vietnamese khổ are all standard, fully compositional readings (khổ is the primary form among the character's own comma-joined vietnamese variants khổ/khủ/cỏ). Added the missing `pos`/`japanese`/`hsk_level` fields and filled the blank `vietnamese`. Discovered a genuine 3-way homophone group with [[枯]] and [[袴]] (all 注音 ㄎㄛ, 諺文 코) — cross-referenced all three pages.
