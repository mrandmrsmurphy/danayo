## Notes
- [閏 (char)](../characters/閏%20(char).md) "intercalary" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day; sun"

閏日 is the leap day, the extra day added to a year to keep the calendar year synchronised with the astronomical year. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 閏日 (Mandarin rùnrì, Cantonese jeon6 jat6) and Japanese うるうび as "leap day". The Korean 윤일 and Vietnamese nhuận nhật fields are built from the readings of the two characters. English Wikipedia says a leap year, also called an intercalary or bissextile year, has an additional day compared with a common year, and that astronomical events and seasons do not repeat in a whole number of days. See also [閏年](閏年.md), leap year, [閏月](閏月.md), leap month, [閏秒](閏秒.md), leap second, and [置閏](置閏.md), intercalation.
