## Notes
- [系](../characters/系.md) "system" + [統](../characters/統.md) "command, govern, unite" — 系's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 統's own is [[統率]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 系統 legitimizes 系 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 系 is individually `kwin: false`.

系統 names a system — an organized, interconnected whole, as in a biological, hierarchical, or mechanical system.

Mandarin xìtǒng, Cantonese hai6 tung2, Japanese けいとう (keitō), Korean 계통 (gyetong), and Vietnamese hệ thống are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 系統 is itself a common attested word for "system" in all five languages. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
