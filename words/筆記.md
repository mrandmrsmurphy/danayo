## Notes
- [筆 (char)](../characters/筆%20(char).md) "pen, stylus" + [記](../characters/記.md) "remember, record" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (筆's is [[筆]] itself, 記's is [[記憶]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 筆 is individually `kwin: false`.

筆記 means to take notes or write down by hand — the act of recording information with a pen, as well as, in Japanese specifically, a written (as opposed to oral/practical) examination (筆記試験, "written exam").

Mandarin bǐjì, Cantonese bat1 gei3, Japanese ひっき (hikki), and Korean 필기 (pilgi) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese bút ký is likewise compositional and is itself a real Vietnamese literary term for memoir/essay-style notes (a "pen-record").
