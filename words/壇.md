## Notes
- [壇 (char)](../characters/壇%20(char).md) "altar"

壇 means "altar, raised platform" and, figuratively, "a field, circle, or scene of activity" (文壇, "literary circle"; 論壇, "forum, discussion board").

Mandarin tán and Cantonese taan4 preserve the sense directly. Japanese ダン/タン (DAN/TAN, on'yomi, fully productive: 演壇, endan, "podium, rostrum") cover the sense, with no dedicated native kun'yomi. Korean 단 (dan) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese đàn is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in diễn đàn ("forum") and văn đàn ("literary circle, literary scene"). The character page's other stored candidate, đườn, is not a second meaning-bearing reading — it's a Nôm phonetic loan (rebus use of the glyph) for an unrelated native colloquial expression, "to lie sprawled out" (nằm đườn ra), with no connection to "altar."

This word shares its reading (dan/단/ㄉㄚㄋ) with [[但]] ("but; only," already perfected).
