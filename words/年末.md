## Notes
- [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year" + [末](../characters/末.md) "end"

年末 is the end of the year. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 年末 (Mandarin niánmò, Cantonese nin4 mut6) as "end of the year; the year's end; year-end period", Japanese ねんまつ as "year-end" (its example: the year-end is always a busy time), and Korean 연말 as "the end of the year; the year's end", with the synonym 세모 (歲暮). It has no Vietnamese entry, so Vietnamese niên mạt here is built from the characters.

Its opposite is [[年初]], the beginning of the year, and the middle of the year is [[年中]].
