## Notes
- [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year" + [末](../characters/末.md) "end"

年末 names the closing part of a calendar year — 年末年始 ("year-end and New Year," the combined Japanese holiday period), 年末セール ("year-end sale") — the direct antonym of [[年初]] ("beginning of the year"). Chinese has a cluster of near-synonyms for the same concept — 年終 (niánzhōng), 年尾 (niánwěi), 年底 (niándǐ) — distinguished mainly by register and regional preference rather than meaning.

Japanese ねんまつ and Korean 연말 are both standard compositional Sino-readings. Vietnamese niên mạt is likewise standard, though everyday spoken Vietnamese more often says the native cuối năm ("year's end") instead. No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄝㄋㄇㄚㄊ unique across both words and characters).
