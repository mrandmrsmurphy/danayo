## Notes
- [感](../characters/感.md) "feel, sense" + [嘆](../characters/嘆%20(char).md) "moan, sigh" — no stand-in relationship applies (感's own stand-in is [[感触]]; 嘆's own is itself).

感嘆 means to sigh, exclaim, or interject — an expression of strong feeling, as in 感嘆符 ("exclamation mark") and [[感嘆詞]] ("interjection").

Mandarin gǎntàn, Cantonese gam2 taan3, Japanese かんたん, Korean 감탄, and Vietnamese cảm thán are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄇㄊㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
