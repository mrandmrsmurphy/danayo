## Notes
- [植](../characters/植.md) "plants, vegetation" + [物](../characters/物%20(char).md) "thing" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [植](../characters/植.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 物's own stand-in is itself.

植物 means plants or flora.

Mandarin zhíwù, Cantonese zik6 mat6, Japanese しょくぶつ, Korean 식물, and Vietnamese thực vật are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 植's and 物's own stored citations exactly). Added the missing `pos: 名詞` field, removed empty `aliases: []`. The homophone with [[食物]] was already correctly noted — see that page's own Notes for a real contamination bug fixed in the process of verifying this pair.
