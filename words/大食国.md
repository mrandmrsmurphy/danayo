## Notes
- [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big" + [食 (char)](../characters/食%20(char).md) "eat" + [国](../characters/国.md) "country" — [[大食]] "Tajik" plus 国; no stand-in relationship applies (大 and 食 are their own stand-ins; 国's is [[国家]]).

大食国 is Tajikistan, the country of the Tajiks, the Central Asian republic between Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, China and Afghanistan. It is a Dan'a'yo coinage: the people are [[大食]], and the country is the people plus 国, as in [[韓国]] and [[泰国]]. The modern transliteration 塔吉克 is a doublet of 大食, both borrowed from the same Pahlavi *Tāčīk, Tāzīg* (see [[大食]]).

The Chinese term 大食國 was historically used for the Arab caliphates of the Tang and Song periods (from memory, not re-checked; English Wiktionary has no page for 大食國, only the Abbasid-caliphate sense of 大食). That sense is not a Dan'a'yo one here: this word is the modern Central Asian state.

Mandarin Dàshíguó and Cantonese daai6 sik6 gwok3 are compositional readings, not attested for Tajikistan. Japanese タジキスタン and Korean 타지키스탄 are the ordinary names of the country, as with [[旧金山]], so `kwin` is false. The `vietnamese` field holds the compositional reading đại thực quốc.

No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄧㄙㄧㄎㄍㄛㄎ unique across words and characters, checked 2026-10-12). The 注音 needs no syllable-boundary dot.
