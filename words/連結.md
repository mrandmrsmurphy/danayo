## Notes
- [連 (char)](../characters/連%20(char).md) "mutual" + [結 (char)](../characters/結%20(char).md) "tie" — "to tie things together in a series".

連結 is "to connect; to link; to join; to combine". English Wiktionary gives the Chinese word as a verb, "to connect; to join; to link" (also an alternative form of 鏈接), and as a noun, "link (on a website); hyperlink", with 超連結 ("hyperlink") as a derived term and 連接 under "see also". Japanese 連結 (れんけつ, renketsu) is both a noun ("concatenation; connection; combination; link") and a suru-verb ("to link; to connect; to combine"), with 繋げる as a synonym and 連結器 ("coupler") as a derived term. Korean (련결 in the North Korean spelling, 연결 in the South) and Vietnamese liên kết are listed as its descendants.

The Physical page lists 連結 as a near-duplicate of [[連接]], which is the vault's other word for joining things, and no sharper division between the two is documented.

Mandarin liánjié and Cantonese lin4 git3 are as at English Wiktionary.

No other word shares the 注音 ㄌ⼶ㄋㄍㄝㄊ (checked 2026-10-12).
