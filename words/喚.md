## Notes
- [喚 (char)](../characters/喚%20(char).md) "summon; invite"

喚 is 形声: semantic [[口]] ("mouth") + phonetic [[奐]]. It means "to call out loudly, summon, shout" (叫喚, "to cry out, call out"; 呼喚, "to shout, cry to").

Mandarin huàn and Cantonese wun6 preserve the sense directly. Japanese カン (KAN, on'yomi, formal register: 喚起, kanki, "to evoke, arouse") alongside native わめ(く) (wame(ku), "to shout, yell") cover the sense. Korean 환 (hwan) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 부르다 (bureuda, "to call"). Vietnamese hoán is the Hán Việt reading, attested in hô hoán ("to shout, cry out," paralleling 呼喚).

This word completes a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read hwan/환/ㄏ⺢ㄋ): [[亘]] ("span; extend," already perfected, already cross-linking here) and [[環]] ("ring," still unperfected).
