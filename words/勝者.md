## Notes
- [勝](../characters/勝.md) "win" + [者](../characters/者.md) "person"

勝者 is a winner: the one who wins a contest or a battle. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin shèngzhě and Japanese しょうしゃ as "winner". Korean 승자 and Cantonese sing3 ze2 match.

It is the person, where [勝利](勝利.md) is the victory. Its opposite is the [敗者](敗者.md).
