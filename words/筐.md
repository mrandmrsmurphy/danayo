## Notes
- [筐](../characters/筐%20(char).md) "bamboo basket" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 筐 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

筐 names a square woven bamboo basket, traditionally used for carrying or storing goods.

Mandarin kuāng, Cantonese hong1, and Korean 광 (gwang) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading かご (kago, "basket") rather than the on-reading キョウ (KYOU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese khuông is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading. This word shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading (kwang/쾅/ㄎ⺢ㄫ) with [[光]] ("light") — a genuine Dan'a'yo homophone, coincidental and unrelated in meaning.
