## Notes
- [依](../characters/依%20(char).md) "rely on" + [頼](../characters/頼.md) "depend on" — "to rely on, depend on" (in Japanese, also specifically "to request, commission" — 依頼する). `vietnamese: y lại` (more commonly seen as ỷ lại, "to be dependent on, lean on") is a well-known everyday Vietnamese term. Fixed the malformed `aliases` list (was "依赖 or 依賴" as one string) and filled the blank `pos: 事詞`. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 의래 diverges from Sino-Korean 의뢰 in the second syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄧㄜㄌㄚㄧ unique among both words and characters).
