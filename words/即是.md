## Notes
- [即 (char)](../characters/即%20(char).md) "namely; precisely" + [是 (char)](../characters/是%20(char).md) "is"

即是 is "is precisely; is none other than," the emphatic copula: 是 with 即 "namely, precisely." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 即是 (jíshì, Cantonese zik1 si6) is a literary word meaning "is precisely; is none other than." Wiktionary has no Japanese, Korean or Vietnamese section, so `japanese` そくぜ, `korean` 즉시 and `vietnamese` tức thị are compositional readings of the two characters, not attested words. (From memory, not re-checked: Korean 즉시 is also the ordinary word 即時 "immediately," so the two would be homophones in Korean, and everyday Vietnamese says tức là for this sense.) Dan'a'yo uses it where Chinese 就是 would stand, since 就 is not a copular particle here; it is the copula of identity or definition, as in the title 平臥即是正義 and in sentences such as 平臥 即是 我 之 智者 運動 in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [平臥即是正義](../translation/平臥即是正義.md). Compare plain [是](是.md), which only links two nouns, and [即](即.md) "namely."
