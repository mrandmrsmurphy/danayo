## Notes
- [跆](../characters/跆.md) "trample; kick" + [籍](../characters/籍.md) "registry"
- This word is the viable form of [跆](../characters/跆.md): the character's meaning (kick, trample) is identical, but 跆 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 跆籍 is its `stand_in` compound.

跆籍 is a Dan'a'yo compound built from the vault's own parts rather than a set term of the other languages: 跆 is the "kick, stomp" element familiar from [跆拳道](跆拳道.md) "Taekwondo," and 籍 is a register or roll, as in [戸籍](戸籍.md) "household registry," so 跆籍 is a Taekwondo membership record, the roll on which a practitioner is registered with a school or federation. Its readings follow the character-by-character pattern of the sphere (Mandarin táijí, Cantonese toi4 zik6, Korean 태적, Sino-Vietnamese đài tịch), but they are constructed from the components, not attested usage, and the Japanese field is left blank. The Vietnamese field was corrected from "đài tạ" to "đài tịch" to match the 籍 character page and the parallel 戸籍 (hộ tịch).

Dan'a'yo uses it for the specific sense "Taekwondo registration, martial arts membership record." It is placed under [Society](../lexipedia/Society.md) as a record of membership in an organization.
