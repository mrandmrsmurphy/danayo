>[!caution] The senses split — Japanese and Korean "middle school", Chinese "secondary school"
>中学 is the middle school, junior high school, in Japanese and Korean, and the Dan'a'yo sense. Chinese 中學 covers secondary school as a whole, junior and senior high together, so a Chinese reader may hear the wider range. Listed as a Split on [文法 - 91疑義語](../grammar/文法%20-%2091疑義語.md).


## Notes
- [中](../characters/中.md) "middle" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study, learning" — no stand-in relationship applies: 学's own `stand_in` is [学習](学習.md), and 中's is 中 itself.

中学 is the middle school, junior high school. English Wiktionary glosses Japanese 中学 (ちゅうがく) as "middle school (7th through 9th years overall)" and Korean 중학 as "middle school". It glosses Chinese 中學 (alias here; Mandarin zhōngxué, Cantonese zung1 hok6) as "secondary school (including both junior high school and high school)", in Singapore "secondary school (junior high school; middle school)", and "Chinese traditional learning". Vietnamese trung học is glossed "secondary education". The word is listed as a Split on [文法 - 91疑義語](../grammar/文法%20-%2091疑義語.md). It sits between [小学](小学.md) and [高校](高校.md), and the school itself is the [中学校](中学校.md).
