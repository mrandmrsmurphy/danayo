## Notes
- [中 (char)](../characters/中%20(char).md) "middle" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study, learning" — no stand-in relationship applies: 学's own `stand_in` is [[学習]] (the same bound 学 appears in [[同学]], [[大学]], [[小学]]), and 中's is [[中]] itself.

中学 means **middle school; junior high school**: the middle tier of the three-step schooling vocabulary [[小学]] — 中学 — [[高校]]. It is the short form of [[中学校]], the same relationship that holds between 小学 and [[小学校]]; the Dan'a'yo 中学 is used for the level of education and for tables and labels (such as the Korean middle-school character list, 韓国中学), while 中学校 names the school as an institution, and [[高校]] (a contraction of 高等学校) covers the next tier.

Japanese ちゅうがく (chūgaku) is the "middle school" level (grades 7 to 9, ages roughly 12 to 15) and is usually extended to 中学校 for the building, 中学生 for the pupil. Korean 중학 (junghak) is bound in practice and surfaces as 중학교 (middle school) and 중학생. Mandarin zhōngxué (中學) and Cantonese zung1 hok6 are the ordinary words, but they differ in range: Chinese 中學 covers all of secondary school, junior and senior high together (distinguished as 初中 and 高中), so a Chinese speaker's 中学 is wider than the Japanese/Korean one; Dan'a'yo takes the narrower Japanese/Korean tier so the three-step ladder stays unambiguous. Vietnamese trung học is Sino-Vietnamese and likewise means secondary education broadly (trung học cơ sở is lower secondary, trung học phổ thông upper), the school itself being trường trung học. Chinese 中學 also has an older sense of "traditional Chinese learning" (as against 西學 "Western learning").

`kwin` is true: 중학 equals the concatenation of 中 jung (중) and 学 hag (학), identical to the Korean reading. The 注音 ㄐㄨㄫㄏㄚㄎ is the same sequence that begins [[中学校]].
