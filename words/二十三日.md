## Notes
- [[二十]] "twenty" + [三 (char)](../characters/三%20(char).md) "three" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — the twenty-third day of the month.

Mandarin èrshísān rì, Cantonese ji6 sap6 saam1 jat6, Japanese にじゅうさんにち, and Korean 이십삼일 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese filled with ngày hai mươi ba (native "day twenty-three"), matching the same native-calendar-date convention already established on [[二十一日]]/[[二十七日]]/[[二十]] itself.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 늬십삼닏 diverges from Sino-Korean 이십삼일 — 三's own kwin is true, but 二/十/日 are each independently false, so the compound is false overall.

**Found and fixed missing citations**: same gap as [[二十一日]]/[[二十七日]] — this word was entirely absent from `二(char).md`, `十(char).md`, and `三(char).md`'s own Words sections (only `日(char).md` had it) — added to all three.
