## Notes
- [接](../characters/接%20(char).md) "contact, connect" + [辞](../characters/辞.md) "word, expression" — no stand-in relationship applies (接's own stand-in is itself; 辞's own is [[辞職]]).

接辞 means affix (the grammatical term for a prefix, suffix, or infix).

Japanese せつじ and Korean 접사 are standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `mandarin`/`cantonese` had both been contaminated with the readings of a different Chinese linguistics term for "affix," 詞綴/词缀 (cízhuì/ci4 zeoi3, using 詞 and 綴 rather than 接 and 辞) — corrected to compositional jiēcí/zip3 ci4. Removed `詞綴`/`词缀`/`輔素` from `aliases`: all three are distinct compounds built on different characters, not orthographic variants of this word. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄛㄆㄑㄧ unique across both words and characters).
