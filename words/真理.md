## Notes
- [真 (char)](../characters/真%20(char).md) "true" + [理](../characters/理.md) "reason; principle"

真理 is "truth," the one true account of how things are, truth in its philosophical sense. According to Wiktionary, Chinese 真理 (zhēnlǐ, Cantonese zan1 lei5), Japanese しんり (also a female given name), Korean 진리 and Vietnamese chân lý all mean "truth"; the Chinese entry also lists an internet-slang sense, "gun," which Dan'a'yo does not take. It differs from [真実](真実.md) "truth" in naming the single philosophical Truth, where 真実 is the truth of a matter. It is used in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [China](../lookup/HSK/China.md), in 宇宙 客観 之 唯一 真理 "the one objective truth of the universe."
