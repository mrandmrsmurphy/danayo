## Notes
- [肘](../characters/肘%20(char).md) "elbow" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 肘 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

肘 means the elbow.

Mandarin zhǒu, Cantonese zaau2, and Korean 주 (ju) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading ひじ (hiji, "elbow") rather than the on-reading チュウ (CHUU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese khuỷu is one of two stored candidates. A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone with [[朕]] ("we [royal]") — coincidental, unrelated in meaning.
