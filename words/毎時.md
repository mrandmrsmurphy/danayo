## Notes
- [毎 (char)](../characters/毎%20(char).md) "every; each; distributive" + [時 (char)](../characters/時%20(char).md) "time; moment; season; occasion"

毎時 is the distributive correlative in the 時 ("time") column of the correlative table: "each time, every time, whenever, on every occasion, one occasion at a time". The character [毎 (char)](../characters/毎%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *moi*, ㄇㄛㄧ; its alias 每 is the traditional form, so 每時 is an `aliases` entry here, not a separate file) is the distributive morpheme of [毎日](毎日.md) "every day", [毎週](毎週.md) "every week", [毎月](毎月.md) "every month" and [毎年](毎年.md) "every year"; the free word is [毎](毎.md). [時 (char)](../characters/時%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *si*, ㄙㄧ) is "time", and the word page [時](時.md) lists 毎時 among the correlatives that 時 generates. The predicate is applied to each occasion in turn and can be checked occasion by occasion. Illustrative only (invented, not from `grammar/`): 毎時、確認 "each time, check"; 毎時同誤 "the same mistake every time".

Its part of speech is 副用名詞 (adverbial noun), the class of every cell of the 毎 row ([毎事](毎事.md), [毎物](毎物.md), [毎人](毎人.md), [毎名](毎名.md), [毎処](毎処.md), [毎様](毎様.md), [毎多](毎多.md), [毎類](毎類.md)) and of [毎日](毎日.md). `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md` (line 11) lists "Adverbial Nouns" as the class able to be the frame with 0-derivation, and `grammar/Linguistics Features.md` (line 68) states that an adverbial noun may be the topic or frame of a clause with no marking but needs marking when it modifies the main verb. So 毎時 can open a clause as a time frame. No example in `grammar/` uses 毎時 itself, so its syntax is inferred from that rule.

Against its column neighbours: the collective [皆時](皆時.md) "always; at all times" treats time as one whole, so it suits a standing state ("he is always honest"), whereas 毎時 counts occasions one by one and suits a procedure or a habit ("check each time"); the same distributive/collective split runs through the table, as [毎人](毎人.md) against [皆人](皆人.md). 皆時 is also what the vault uses for "at all times" (無時無刻) in the 躺平 translation (from the task brief; not re-checked in this pass). [某時](某時.md) "sometime" is the indefinite, naming one unspecified occasion, and [何時](何時.md) "when?" is the interrogative. [此時](此時.md) "now", [其時](其時.md) "then" and [彼時](彼時.md) "back then" each point at one particular time, where 毎時 ranges over all of them. The existing words for the present, [今](今.md) and [現在](現在.md), are lexical and unrelated to this quantificational cell. As for the calendar words [毎日](毎日.md), [毎週](毎週.md), [毎月](毎月.md) and [毎年](毎年.md), they distribute over fixed units of the calendar (day, week, month, year), whereas 毎時 distributes over occasions of any length, defined by the event under discussion, not by a clock unit.

Does Dan'a'yo take the Japanese "every hour" sense? No. Wiktionary has an entry for Japanese 毎時 (maiji), glossed only "every hour"; in Japanese, then, 毎時 means "hourly", as in 毎時60キロ "60 km per hour". Dan'a'yo does not take that sense: 時 here is "time; occasion", the 時 of the correlative column, so 毎時 is "each time", not "each hour". A unit of one hour would need a different word; I have not checked which one the vault uses, and I do not assert any here. A Japanese reader may therefore misread 毎時 as "hourly", which is the one place where this word is a false friend across the CJKV sphere.

The cross-linguistic fields were checked against Wiktionary on 2026-10-11. Mandarin 每次 (měicì) has an entry, an adverb glossed "every time", with Cantonese mui5 ci3 (entered in `cantonese`); 次 "occasion; instance" plays the role of Dan'a'yo's 時. Wiktionary has no entry for Mandarin 每時 (nor 每时, which is the simplified form held in `aliases`, along with 每時; neither has a word file of its own). Japanese 毎回 (maikai) has an entry glossed "every time", so it is the equivalent, entered as まいかい. Korean 매번 (maebeon, 每番) has an entry; its single gloss is "all the time", from 每 "every" + 番 "time", although in use it means "every time" (from memory, not re-checked). Korean 매시 has no Wiktionary entry. The earlier `mandarin: měicì`, `japanese: 毎回` and `korean: 매번` stood as they were, though the Japanese field now holds hiragana as the checklist asks. Vietnamese *mỗi lần* "each time" is from memory, not re-checked; hvdic.thivien.net covers single characters, not phrases, so no `vietnamese` field is entered. The English glosses also drop the earlier overreach: other pages (the 毎 and 時 pages) gloss 毎時 as "always", which is the sense of 皆時, not of this word.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md)'s correlative table (line 125; the same chart is in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`, line 149 in the 時 ("time") row, with the column header cell 毎 on line 142), 毎時 is the sixth cell of the 毎 ("every") row, after [毎事](毎事.md), [毎物](毎物.md), [毎人](毎人.md), [毎名](毎名.md) and [毎処](毎処.md), and before [毎様](毎様.md), [毎多](毎多.md) and [毎類](毎類.md). The table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), 63 forms, so 毎時 is parsed compositionally: 毎 = distributive, 時 = time. The 時 and 処 columns are the temporal/spatial pair, so 毎時 lines up with [毎処](毎処.md) "at each place". The 羅馬字 is *moisi*, from the character pages (毎 *moi*, 時 *si*); the earlier *moysi* disagreed with the 毎 page. The 諺文 뫼시 is 뫼 + 시, as on those pages. Like its siblings it is listed on the Grammar page only; the temporal page [Time](../lexipedia/Time.md) mentions 時 itself but not this cell.

The 注音 ㄇㄛㄧㄙㄧ carries no syllable-boundary dot. `grammar/文法 - 02音韻論.md` (line 116) gives the structure (C)(G)V(C), and the only syllable-final consonants it allows are the nasals ㄇ ㄋ ㄫ, the glides and the unreleased stops ㄆ ㄊ ㄎ, so ㄙ cannot be a coda. The only partition is therefore ㄇㄛㄧ + ㄙㄧ (both in `syllables/`). Splitting the diphthong ㄛㄧ (ㄇㄛ + ㄧㄙㄧ) is not treated as a boundary in this vault, as on [毎人](毎人.md), [毎名](毎名.md) and [毎多](毎多.md), and ㄧㄙ is not a legal syllable anyway.
