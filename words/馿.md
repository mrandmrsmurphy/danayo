## Notes
- [馿 (char)](characters/馿%20(char).md) "donkey" — 馿 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

Mandarin lǘ, Cantonese lou4, and Korean 려 (ryeo) are standard. Japanese ろ is the bare on'yomi (per this vault's default convention for bare-character words); real standalone Japanese instead almost always uses the compound ロバ (roba, written 驢馬 — itself built from this character's own on'yomi RO + 馬's BA). Sino-Vietnamese offers two forms — lư (more literal/literary) and lừa (the actual everyday word for "donkey") — lừa was chosen as the representative form.
