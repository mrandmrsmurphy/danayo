## Notes
- [俯](../characters/俯.md) "bow, look down" + [瞰](../characters/瞰.md) "look down from a height" — to look down from above, or the resulting bird's-eye view; attested across Mandarin (俯瞰), Japanese (俯瞰/ふかん), and Korean (부감), most often in compounds like 俯瞰図/鳥瞰図 ("aerial view diagram") or 俯瞰撮影 ("overhead/bird's-eye shot"). `vietnamese: phủ khám` is the honest compositional reading (俯's own phủ + 瞰's own khám); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 뿌캄 diverges entirely from the compositional Sino-Korean 부감. No homophones (注音 ㄈㄨㄎㄚㄇ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 瞰's own `stand_in` is this exact word (俯瞰), so 瞰 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 俯瞰.
