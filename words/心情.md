## Notes
- [心 (char)](../characters/心%20(char).md) "heart; mind" + [情](../characters/情.md) "feeling; emotion"

心情 is "mood; frame of mind; one's heart and feelings." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 心情 (xīnqíng, Cantonese sam1 cing4) is "mood; frame of mind; temper" and "one's heart, one's feelings," Japanese しんじょう is attested, and Korean 심정 is the hanja form glossed "feeling." Wiktionary has no Vietnamese section, so `vietnamese` tâm tình is a compositional reading. It is used twice in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [China](../lookup/HSK/China.md), in 心情 好 "in a good mood" in sentences 9 and 26. Related: [気分](気分.md) "mood" and [感情](感情.md) "emotions."
