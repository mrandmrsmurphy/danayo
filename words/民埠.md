## Notes
- [民](../characters/民.md) "people" + [埠](../characters/埠.md) "port" — "the people's port", a short form of the Chinese name of the city; no stand-in relationship applies (民's own stand-in is [[人民]], 埠's is [[埠頭]]). The alias 岷埠 is the standard Chinese spelling: the character 岷 is itself an alias of 民, so Dan'a'yo writes the name with 民.

民埠 is Manila, the capital of the Philippines. English Wiktionary marks 岷埠 an obsolete Chinese proper noun for Manila, with the Hokkien reading Bîn-po͘, and quotes a 1922 advertisement giving the office and factory address in Juan Luna Street, Manila. It lists 馬尼拉 as the synonym, which is not a word in the vault, and the older name for the city is [[呂宋]].

Mandarin Mínbù is as at English Wiktionary. The Cantonese man4 bou6 is compositional (each character as its page gives it; not attested for this name), as is the `vietnamese` field dân phụ. Japanese マニラ and Korean 마닐라 are the ordinary names of the city (from memory, not re-checked), so `kwin` is false.

No homophones (注音 ㄇㄧㄋㄅㄨ unique across words and characters, checked 2026-10-12). The 注音 needs no syllable-boundary dot.
