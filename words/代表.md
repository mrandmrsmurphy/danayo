## Notes
- [代](../characters/代.md) "substitute, generation" + [表](../characters/表.md) "surface, express" — no stand-in relationship applies (代's own stand-in is [[世代]]; 表's is [[表現]]).

代表 means to represent — standing in for or expressing on behalf of another.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `vietnamese` was wrongly capitalized as a proper noun ("Đại biểu") though this is a common verb/noun — corrected to lowercase `đại biểu`. Japanese だいひょう, Korean 대표, Mandarin dàibiǎo, and Cantonese doi6 biu2 are all standard readings. **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 代表 and [[大砲]] ("cannon, gun") share an identical reading — standardized the non-canonical `[!tip]` callout into the proper reciprocal `>[!warning] Homophones` format.
