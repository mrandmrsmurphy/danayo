## Notes
- [倉](../characters/倉.md) "granary" + [庫](../characters/庫.md) "storehouse" — "warehouse, storehouse." Mandarin cāngkù, Cantonese cong1 fu3, Japanese そうこ, and Korean 창고 are all directly attested standard readings. `vietnamese: thương kho` is the honest compositional reading (倉's own thương + 庫's own kho); the more common everyday Vietnamese word for this concept is simply kho or nhà kho. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 촹코 diverges entirely from Sino-Korean 창고. No homophones (注音 ㄑ⺢ㄫㄎㄛ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 庫's own `stand_in` is this exact word (倉庫), so 庫 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 倉庫.
