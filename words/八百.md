## Notes
- [八 (char)](../characters/八%20(char).md) "eight" + [百 (char)](../characters/百%20(char).md) "hundred" — "eight hundred." Mandarin bābǎi, Cantonese baat3 baak3, Japanese はちひゃく, and Korean 팔백 are all directly attested standard readings. `vietnamese: bát bách` is the Sino-Vietnamese compositional reading; everyday Vietnamese instead uses the native tám trăm for round numbers like this. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 받박 diverges from Sino-Korean 팔백 in the first syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄊㄅㄚㄎ unique among both words and characters).
