## Notes
- [底 (char)](../characters/底%20(char).md) "bottom; underneath; underside; base" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [底 (char)](../characters/底%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

底 names the bottom or base of something — physical (水底 "bottom of the water," 海底 "seabed"), temporal (年底 "year's end"), or figurative (root cause, origin).

Japanese てい, Korean 저, and Vietnamese để (the character's own field also lists several other Nôm variants — đáy, đây, đé, đẻ, đẽ — for related senses) are all standard readings. **A genuine triple Dan'a'yo homophone**: 底, [[第]], and [[蹄]] all share the identical reading (dei/데/ㄉㄝㄧ) — added reciprocal homophone callouts to all three pages.
