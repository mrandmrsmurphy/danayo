## Notes
- [坐](../characters/坐%20(char).md) "sit, seat" + [堂](../characters/堂.md) "hall" — "seat hall," a cathedral. Their own `stand_in` values: 坐's is itself; 堂's is [[会堂]]; none points here, so 坐堂 is an independent compound.

坐堂 is a cathedral: the church that holds the bishop's seat, in full 主教坐堂. Mandarin zuòtáng (座堂) and Cantonese zo6 tong4 are the standard Christian term; 座 is the alias of 坐, so the Chinese spelling 座堂 is accepted. Japanese says 司教座聖堂 and Korean 주교좌성당, so those two fields hold the character readings. It is the bishop's seat, where [[大教堂]] is the great church. Compare [[主教]] (bishop) and [[教会]] (church).
