## Notes
- [七](../characters/七%20(char).md) "seven" + [十](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" — no `#cranberry`: both 七's and 十's own `stand_in` point to themselves, neither to this word.

Mandarin qīshí, Cantonese cat1 sap6, and Korean 칠십 are standard compositional readings. Japanese ななじゅう uses native なな over on'yomi しち for the same clarity-in-numerals reason already documented on [[七]]/[[七万]]. Vietnamese bảy mươi is the real, everyday native tens-numeral ("seven tens"), the standard way to say 70 in Vietnamese. `kwin` is false (AND-rule: 七 false, 十 true) — corrected from a stale `true`. **Fixed the recurring `characters:` disambiguation bug**: bare "十" despite `words/十.md` existing. Fixed the duplicate `品詞` field, and two empty-string bugs (`vietnamese: ""`, `swadesh: ""`).

**`kwin` update (2026-10-09):** `kwin` is now true. The Notes above may describe the ㄷ/ㄹ difference between the Dan'a'yo 諺文 and the Korean reading as a mismatch; under the vault's standing rule a Korean final ㄹ counts as equal to Dan'a'yo's final ㄷ for this comparison, so such a difference alone does not make `kwin` false.
