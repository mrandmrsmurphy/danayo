## Notes
- [愛](../characters/愛%20(char).md) "love" + [情](../characters/情.md) "feeling, affection" — no stand-in relationship applies (愛's own stand-in is itself; 情's own is [[感情]]).

愛情 means love, affection, or romance.

Mandarin àiqíng, Cantonese oi3 cing4, Japanese あいじょう, and Korean 애정 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese tình yêu is the standard native term for romantic love, more common in everyday usage than the compositional ái tình. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄧㄑㄧㄫ unique across both words and characters).
