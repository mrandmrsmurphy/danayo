## Notes
- [打](../characters/打.md) "hit" + [撃](../characters/撃.md) "strike; hit; beat" — stand-in for [[打]], which cannot appear independently

打撃 means a strike, blow, or impact: a physical hit delivered to something (打撃を与える, "deal a blow"; 打撃力, "striking power"), the batting of a baseball (打撃練習, "batting practice"), and figuratively a shock or setback (経済的打撃, "economic blow"). The variants 打擊 and 打击 are listed as aliases.

Mandarin dǎjī, Cantonese daa2 gik1, Japanese だげき (dageki), and Korean 타격 (tagyeok) are all the standard word for a physical "strike, blow, impact" — extending, in every one of these languages, to a figurative "blow" or shock (a psychological/economic "hit"). Vietnamese keeps the field value đánh, the versatile everyday native verb "to hit" in the most general sense (đánh nhau, "to fight"; đánh trống, "to beat a drum"); the real Sino-Vietnamese cognate đả kích is attested but has narrowed specifically to a verbal/rhetorical register — "to attack, to criticize sharply" (in debate, social commentary) — rather than this word's physical-impact sense.
