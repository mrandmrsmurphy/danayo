## Notes
- [凶 (char)](../characters/凶%20(char).md) "unlucky; sinister" + [手 (char)](../characters/手%20(char).md) "hand"

凶手 means a murderer: the person who killed, literally "the sinister hand" that dealt the fatal blow. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 兇手 (an alias, 凶手; Mandarin xiōngshǒu, Cantonese hung1 sau2) as "murderer; killer", Japanese きょうしゅ as "murderer", and Vietnamese hung thủ as "murder culprit". It has no Korean entry, so Korean 흉수 here is built from the characters.

The word joins 凶, ill-omened or sinister, with 手, hand, as a person. The act is [[殺人]], murder, and the broader word for a perpetrator of any crime is [[犯人]].
