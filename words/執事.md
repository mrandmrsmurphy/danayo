## Notes
- [執](../characters/執.md) "hold, handle" + [事](../characters/事%20(char).md) "affair, duty" — "handler of affairs," a deacon. Their own `stand_in` values: 執's is [[執行]]; 事's is itself; none points here, so 執事 is an independent compound.

執事 is a deacon: a church officer who helps with the work of the congregation. Mandarin zhíshì, Cantonese zap1 si6 and Japanese しつじ are attested, and Korean 집사 is the same compound; in everyday Japanese しつじ is a butler. Compare [[牧師]] (pastor) and [[長老]] (elder).
