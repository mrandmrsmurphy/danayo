## Notes
- [暗](../characters/暗%20(char).md) "dark, hidden" + [礁](../characters/礁.md) "reef" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [礁](../characters/礁.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

暗礁 means submerged reef, or (figuratively) hidden obstacle.

Mandarin ànjiāo, Cantonese am3 ziu1, Japanese あんしょう, Korean 암초, and Vietnamese ám tiêu are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄇㄐㄚㄨ unique across both words and characters).
