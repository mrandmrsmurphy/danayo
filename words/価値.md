## Notes
- [価](../characters/価.md) "price" + [値](../characters/値.md) "value" — the standard word for worth or value in a general, philosophical, or economic sense (価値観 "values, sense of worth"). Japanese かち, Korean 가치, and Vietnamese giá trị are all standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 가디 diverges from Sino-Korean 가치 in the second syllable. Homophone with [[加持]] "perform incantations, bless" (identical gadi/가디/ㄍㄚㄉㄧ reading; reciprocal callout confirmed present on both pages).

Legitimizing note: 値's own `stand_in` is this exact word (価値), so 値 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 価値 (価's own `stand_in`, by contrast, is [[価格]]).
