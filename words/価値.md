## Notes
- [価](../characters/価.md) "price" + [値](../characters/値.md) "value" — this word is the stand-in for [値](../characters/値.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo (価's own stand-in is [[価格]]).

価値 means worth or value — the standard word for value in a general, philosophical, or economic sense (価値観 "values, sense of worth").

Japanese かち, Korean 가치, and Vietnamese giá trị are all standard readings. **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 価値 and [[加持]] ("to bless, perform incantations") share an identical reading (gadi/가디/ㄍㄚㄉㄧ) — the reciprocal callout was already noted informally on this page; standardized here.
