## Notes
- [炭](characters/炭%20(char).md) "coal" + [素](characters/素.md) "element" — not a neologism, unlike most other `periodictable` entries.

炭素 (C, atomic number 6) is a real, established compositional word rather than an invented calque: Japanese たんそ (tanso) is the genuine everyday Japanese word for the element, and Mandarin tànsù, Cantonese taan3 sou3, and Korean 탄소 (tanso) all follow the same direct compositional pattern productively, paralleling how [[塩素]] (chlorine) is likewise a real word rather than a coinage. Vietnamese, by contrast, uses the direct phonetic loanword cacbon (from the international/Latin-derived name "carbon") rather than a compositional Sino-Vietnamese rendering — the same pattern seen with [[惰素]] (argon)'s Vietnamese agon.
