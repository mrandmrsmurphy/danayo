## Notes
- [国](../characters/国.md) "country" + [歌](../characters/歌.md) "song" — no stand-in relationship applies (国's own stand-in is [[国家]]; 歌's is [[歌曲]]).

国歌 means national anthem.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `japanese` was missing the sokuon assimilation (こくか → こっか) — 国歌 is a genuine real-world Japanese homophone of 国家, both read こっか (this is a real-language coincidence only; see below for why they no longer share a Dan'a'yo reading). Korean 국가 and Vietnamese quốc ca are both standard readings.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` had a stale "ga" spelling for 歌's syllable (곡가/gogga) that didn't match 歌's own authoritative character-page reading (그/gǝ/ㄍㄜ) — confirmed against 11 other 歌-compounds and 歌's own stand_in [[歌曲]] (itself independently fixed for the same bug), all of which agree on gǝ — corrected to 곡그/goggǝ/ㄍㄛㄎㄍㄜ. This removes the previously-claimed Dan'a'yo homophone with [[国家]] (real-word coincidence in Japanese only): that collision only existed because of this word's own error and no longer applies now that 国歌 reads its correct 곡그.
