>[!warning] Homophones
>[[国家]] "country; nation" — identical 羅馬字/諺文/注音 (gogga/곡가/ㄍㄛㄎㄍㄚ).

## Notes
- [国](../characters/国.md) "country" + [歌](../characters/歌.md) "song" — no stand-in relationship applies (国's own stand-in is [[国家]]; 歌's is [[歌曲]]).

国歌 means national anthem.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `japanese` was missing the sokuon assimilation (こくか → こっか) — 国歌 is a genuine real-world Japanese homophone of 国家, both read こっか. Korean 국가 and Vietnamese quốc ca are both standard readings. Note: `歌`'s own character page stores an inconsistent citation (gǝ/그/ㄍㄜ) that disagrees with both this word and 歌's own stand-in [[歌曲]] (both consistently use ga/가/ㄍㄚ) — flagged for a future character-page fix, not altered here. No other homophones beyond the documented [[国家]] pair.
