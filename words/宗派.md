## Notes
- "Faction, sect, denomination" — a branch or school within a larger religious or philosophical tradition (e.g. the various Buddhist 宗派: Zen, Pure Land). Corrected/expanded `vietnamese` to `tông phái, tôn phái`, both directly attested — tông phái is the more common term in Buddhist/Taoist studies specifically, with tôn phái as a standing variant, the same tông/tôn alternation seen on [[宗教]]. `korean: 종파` is the standard, everyday word. **Left `hsk_level` unset**: no search turned up a reliable official-list classification for this specific compound.
