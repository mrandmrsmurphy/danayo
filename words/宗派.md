## Etymology
- [宗](../characters/宗.md) "sect" + [派](../characters/派.md) "branch"

## Notes
- "Faction, sect, denomination" — a branch or school within a larger religious or philosophical tradition (e.g. the various Buddhist 宗派: Zen, Pure Land). `vietnamese: tông phái, tôn phái` are both directly attested — tông phái is the more common term in Buddhist/Taoist studies specifically, with tôn phái as a standing variant, the same tông/tôn alternation seen on [[宗教]]. `korean: 종파` is the standard, everyday word.
- Neither 宗's `stand_in` (宗教) nor 派's `stand_in` (派生) is this word, so no stand-in note applies here.
