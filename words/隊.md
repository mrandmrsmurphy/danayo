## Notes
- [隊 (char)](../characters/隊%20(char).md) "group, team" — this word is 隊's own stand-in.

隊 means a group, team, or unit — most familiar bound in compounds like 軍隊 ("armed forces"), 部隊 ("military unit"), or 隊伍 ("rank, contingent"). Mandarin 隊/队 (duì), Cantonese 隊 (deoi6), Japanese 隊 (たい, as in 兵隊 heitai "soldier," 一隊 ittai "a squad"), Korean 대 (dae), and Sino-Vietnamese đội all preserve this meaning — Vietnamese đội in particular remains a fully everyday, free-standing word (đội bóng đá, "football team") unlike its more bound status in the other four languages.

Exact syllable check: 隊 shares its Dan'a'yo reading (doi/되/ㄉㄛㄧ) with four other characters (殆, 対, 隹, 堆), but none of them stand alone as their own word — each requires its own multi-syllable compound — so there is no genuine word-level homophone to callout.
