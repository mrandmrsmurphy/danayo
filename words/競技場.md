## Notes
- [競](../characters/競.md) "compete" + [技](../characters/技.md) "skill, art" + [場](../characters/場.md) "place, field" — "competition field," an arena. Their own `stand_in` values: 競's is [[競争]]; 技's is [[技能]]; 場's is [[市場]]; none points here, so 競技場 is an independent compound.

競技場 is an arena or stadium: a field where athletic competitions are held. Mandarin jìngjìchǎng, Cantonese ging6 gei6 coeng4, Japanese きょうぎじょう and Korean 경기장 all use it. Compare [[劇場]] (theater).
