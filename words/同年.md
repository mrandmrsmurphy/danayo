## Notes
- [同](../characters/同.md) "same" + [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year"

同年 means the same year: two events or births falling in the identical calendar year, and by extension of the same age. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 同年 (Mandarin tóngnián, Cantonese tung4 nin4) as "of the same year", "of the same age" and "to be the same age", and, dated, "candidates who passed the imperial examination in the same year", that is, those who took [[及第]] together. Japanese どうねん is "the same year" and "the aforementioned year". Korean 동년 is listed under the hanja form with no gloss, and there is no Vietnamese entry, so Vietnamese đồng niên here is built from the characters.

The first element is 同, same, and the second is the 年 of a year.
