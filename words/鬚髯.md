## Notes
- [鬚](../characters/鬚.md) "beard" + [髯](../characters/髯.md) "beard" — **`#cranberry`**: 鬚's own `stand_in` is [[鬚髯]] itself (already annotated in its own citation) and 髯's own `stand_in` is likewise [[鬚髯]] itself — transitivity holds (鬚=髯=鬚髯, both meaning plain "beard"), so this is a genuine cranberry compound. Added to [[lookup/List of 連綿詞]] as a native alliterative binome, previously missing from that list despite both citations already carrying `stand_in` pointers here.

Mandarin xūrán, Cantonese sou1jim4, Japanese しゅぜん, Korean 수염 (also the character 鬚's own native gloss, a case where the Sino-Korean compound has become the everyday word), and Sino-Vietnamese tu nhiêm are all standard, fully compositional readings.
