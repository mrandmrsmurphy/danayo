## Notes
- [人](../characters/人%20(char).md) "people" + [民](../characters/民.md) "people, citizen" + [幣](../characters/幣%20(char).md) "currency" — "people's currency," the renminbi. Their own `stand_in` values: 人's is itself; 民's is [[人民]]; 幣's is itself; none points here, so 人民幣 is an independent compound.

人民幣 is the renminbi, the currency of China, whose basic unit is the yuan. Mandarin rénmínbì, Cantonese jan4 man4 bai6, Japanese じんみんげん/じんみんへい, Korean 인민폐 and Vietnamese nhân dân tệ all use it. Compare [[日圓]] (yen), [[美圓]] (dollar) and [[韓圓]] (won).
