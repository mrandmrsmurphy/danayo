## Notes
- [常](../characters/常.md) "common, normal, frequent, regular, often" (reduplicated) — 常's own `stand_in` points to 日常, not here. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule, since 常 is individually `kwin: false` (reduplicated, so both copies share the same value).
- Often used of gnomic truths.
- **Readings**: `japanese` is つねづね (also validly read じょうじょう, verified via search). `korean` is 상상, the genuine, if less common/formal-register, Sino-Korean compositional reading (verified via search, meaning "always"); 자주/항상 remain the everyday native alternatives (자주 is a native word, not a reading of 常常 itself). `pos` is 修飾語 (adverb/modifier).
