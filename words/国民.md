## Notes
- [国](../characters/国.md) "country" + [民](../characters/民.md) "people"

国民 are the nationals of a state: its citizens taken together as one people. Wiktionary glosses Japanese こくみん as "nationals (citizens of a state)". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 국민 matches.

It is the people as the members of a country, where [市民](市民.md) are the people of a city and [人民](人民.md) are the people in general. It is the group to which a [同胞](同胞.md), compatriot, belongs.
