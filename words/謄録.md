## Notes
- [謄](../characters/謄.md) "transcribe; copy" is legitimized by this exact compound (its own stand-in): 謄 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. [録](../characters/録.md) "record; write down" is separately legitimized by [[抄録]]. No cranberry.

Mandarin ténglù, japanese とうろく, and korean 등록 (all also the real common words for "registration") confirmed standard and compositional.
