## Notes
- [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" + [道 (char)](../characters/道%20(char).md) "way, path, doctrine" — "humane, humanitarian" (as in 人道主義, "humanitarianism"; 人道支援, "humanitarian aid"), from the sense of "the proper way/path for humans," parallel to 天道 ("the way of heaven") and 地道 ("the way of earth"). nhân đạo is standard everyday Vietnamese for this sense (as in chủ nghĩa nhân đạo, "humanitarianism"), a direct compositional match. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 닌닷 diverges from Sino-Korean 인도 in the second syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄧㄋㄉㄚㄨ unique among both words and characters).
