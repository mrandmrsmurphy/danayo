## Notes
- [颯 (char)](../characters/颯%20(char).md) "sound of wind, gale" — this word is 颯's own legitimizing compound (颯's `stand_in` points here).

颯 describes the sound of a gust of wind, and by extension a brisk, vigorous manner (as in 颯爽, "dashing, spirited"). Mandarin sà, Cantonese saap3, Korean 삽 (sap), and Sino-Vietnamese táp all preserve the Sino reading directly. Japanese さつ (satsu) survives mainly bound in compounds like 颯爽 (さっそう, "dashing").
