## Notes
- [北](../characters/北.md) "north" + [部 (char)](../characters/部%20(char).md) "part, section" — not actually a Dan'a'yo homophone of [[腹部]] despite a formerly-claimed callout: 北部 is bugbou/북봇/ㄅㄨㄎㄅㄛㄨ (matching 北's own b-initial reading) while 腹部 is fugbou/뿍봇/ㄈㄨㄎㄅㄛㄨ (matching 腹's own f-initial reading) — the false claim traced back to a copy-paste bug on 腹部's own `羅馬字` field, now fixed.
