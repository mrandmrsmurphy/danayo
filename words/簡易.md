## Notes
- [簡](../characters/簡.md) "concise; simple" + [易](../characters/易.md) "easy; uncomplicated" — not a stand-in word: 簡's `stand_in` is [[簡単]] and 易's is [[容易]], so 簡易 is an ordinary compound of the two bound characters.

簡易 is "simple; easy" — 簡 (concise, stripped of the superfluous) + 易 (easy, uncomplicated). Wiktionary lists two senses for Mandarin 简易/簡易: "simple and easy", and, of a structure or facility, "simple and crude; unsophisticated". Japanese かんい is listed as a な-adjective and noun, "simple; easy; simplicity". Korean 간이 and Vietnamese giản dị are recorded there as the hanja/chữ Hán form of the native-script reading, without an independent gloss on that page; hvdic.thivien.net lists 簡易 under giản dị, "simple and easy to do, without complications".

Usage, from memory and not re-checked against dictionaries: Japanese uses 簡易 mainly in formal, institutional compounds (簡易裁判所 "summary court", 簡易保険, 簡易宿所, 簡易包装); Korean has 간이역 and 간이식당; Mandarin has 簡易法庭 and 簡易建築-type phrases for makeshift or no-frills things. In Vietnamese, giản dị is a common everyday word for plain, unpretentious manner or lifestyle, a wider use than the others.

Compared with its siblings in this vault: [[簡単]] is the plain, general word for "simple, uncomplicated", and [[容易]] is the difficulty judgment "easy" (see [Efforts](../lexipedia/Efforts.md)). 簡易 is the near-synonym of 簡単 with the formal, "stripped-down, no-frills" colouring described above; this is the same shared CJKV sense, with no Dan'a'yo-specific divergence. The phonology page itself names its design principle 簡易原則 ("simplicity principle", [[文法 - 02音韻論]]).

**Syllable boundary**: 簡 is ㄍㄚㄋ and 易 is ⼶ㄎ, so plain concatenation gives ㄍㄚㄋ⼶ㄎ. That string has a second legal partition, ㄍㄚ + ㄋ⼶ㄎ (onset ㄋ, glide ⼶, coda ㄎ; compare attested ㄋ⼶ㄇ, ㄋ⼶ㄊ, ㄋ⼶ㄋ, which share the ㄋ⼶ onset-plus-glide shape), so the boundary dot is kept: ㄍㄚㄋ·⼶ㄎ.
