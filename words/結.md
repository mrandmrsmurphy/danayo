## Notes
- [結](../characters/結%20(char).md) "tie, join" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 結 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

結 means to tie, join, or bind, as in 結婚 (marry, literally "tie the union") and 結果 (result, literally "the fruit that ties/forms").

Mandarin jié, Cantonese git3, and Korean 결 (gyeol) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading むす (musu, "to tie") rather than the on-readings ケツ/ケイ (KETSU/KEI) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese kết is the standard, common Sino-Vietnamese reading, one of several stored candidates. No Dan'a'yo homophones (偈/傑/桔/潔 share the same syllable at the character level, but none has an independent word page).
