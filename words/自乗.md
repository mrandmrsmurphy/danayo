## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" + [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "multiply"

自乗 is squaring, multiplying a number by itself. English Wiktionary glosses Japanese 自乗 (じじょう) as the mathematical terms "squaring" and "to square", and Chinese 自乘 (alias here; Mandarin zìchéng, Cantonese zi6 sing4) as the arithmetic "to be multiplied by itself". Korean 자승 is built from the readings of the two characters.
