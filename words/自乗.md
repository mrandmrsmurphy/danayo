## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" + [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "multiply" — literally "multiply by itself," i.e. raising a number to the power of two (squaring).

自乗 is primarily Japanese mathematical vocabulary (じじょう), a two-character compact term for squaring, more compositionally transparent than the equivalent English "square." It contrasts with the more general 累乗 ("repeated multiplication," i.e. exponentiation to any power), of which squaring is the specific base case.

Korean 자승 is a standard compositional Sino-Korean reading. **`vietnamese` intentionally left blank**: Vietnamese doesn't calque this compound structurally — the standard mathematical term is bình phương (平方, "flat/level-square"), an entirely different pair of characters. No specific HSK level applies to a term this specialized. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄜㄙㄨㄫ unique across both words and characters).
