## Etymology
- [郵](../characters/郵.md) "mail, post" + [票 (char)](../characters/票%20(char).md) "ballot, ticket"

## Notes
Mandarin yóupiào, cantonese jau4 piu3, and korean 우표 are all directly attested standard readings. **Found and fixed a wrong-compound formatting/reading bug on `japanese`**: the stored value 郵便切手 was written in kanji (not a kana reading) and is in fact the real, unrelated Japanese word for "postage stamp" (yūbin kitte, using 便切手 rather than 票) — corrected to the honest compositional kana reading ゆうひょう (郵's own YUU + 票's own HYOU). Vietnamese bưu phiếu is the honest compositional reading (郵's own bưu + 票's own phiếu); modern Vietnamese instead uses the loanword "tem" for "postage stamp," but no independently attested loanword using these exact characters was found. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 웃퍗 diverges from Sino-Korean 우표 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄨㄛㄆ⼘ㄨ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 郵's `stand_in` (郵便) nor 票's own `stand_in` (票 itself) points to this compound.
