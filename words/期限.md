## Notes
- [期 (char)](../characters/期%20(char).md) "period, term" + [限](../characters/限.md) "limit"

期限 names a bounded time period with a fixed end point — a due date, deadline, or term of validity — central to contracts, loans, and legal/financial obligations (支払期限, "payment deadline"; 有効期限, "expiration date, period of validity"). More formal/structural than [[締切]], which emphasizes the cutoff moment itself rather than the whole bounded period leading up to it.

Japanese きげん, Korean 기한, and Vietnamese kỳ hạn are all standard compositional Sino-readings, kỳ hạn being especially common in Vietnamese banking and legal register (kỳ hạn gửi tiết kiệm, "savings deposit term"). No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄧㄏㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
