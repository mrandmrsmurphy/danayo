## Notes
- [期 (char)](../characters/期%20(char).md) "period, term" + [限](../characters/限.md) "limit"

期限 means a deadline or time limit: a fixed end point of a period, a due date or term. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 期限 (Mandarin qīxiàn, Cantonese kei4 haan6) as "deadline; due date; term; time limit; allotted time" (delivery deadline), Japanese きげん as "time limit, deadline, cutoff" and "time frame" (your password has expired), and Korean 기한 as "time limit, deadline" (a sell-by date). It has no Vietnamese entry, so Vietnamese kỳ hạn here is built from the characters.

Compare [[締切]], the cutoff moment itself.
