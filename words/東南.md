## Notes
- [東](../characters/東.md) "east" + [南](../characters/南.md) "south" — no stand-in relationship applies (東's own stand-in is [[東方]]; 南's own is [[南方]]).

東南 means southeast.

Mandarin dōngnán, Cantonese dung1 naam4, Japanese とうなん, Korean 동남, and Vietnamese đông nam are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 東's and 南's own stored citations exactly). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field, removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄫㄋㄚㄇ unique across both words and characters).

Dan'a'yo keeps the shared CJKV sense: the southeast, the direction halfway between east and south. It is placed on the Directions page beside [[東北]], and is the first part of [[東南亜]] "Southeast Asia."
