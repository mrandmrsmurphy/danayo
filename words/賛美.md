## Notes
- [賛](../characters/賛.md) "help, support" + [美 (char)](../characters/美%20(char).md) "beautiful" — to praise, to extol

賛美 means to praise, extol or glorify: to speak of something as admirable and beautiful (神を賛美する, "to praise God"; 自然を賛美する, "to extol nature").  賛 contributes the approval and 美 the beauty found in the object, so the word praises with warmth and often in a religious or elevated register.  It is the root of [[賛美歌]] ("hymn") and is close to [[賛歌]] ("song of praise").  The aliases 讚美 and 贊美 are the traditional spellings.

賛's own `stand_in` is 賛助 and 美's is itself, so this word legitimizes neither.  Mandarin zànměi, Cantonese zaan3 mei5, Japanese さんび and Korean 찬미 are the same compound.  No Vietnamese form is given, since none could be verified.  `kwin` is false because the Dan'a'yo 잔미 differs from the Korean 찬미 in its initial.  The 注音 needs no syllable dot.
