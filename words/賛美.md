## Notes
- [賛](../characters/賛.md) "help, support" + [美 (char)](../characters/美%20(char).md) "beautiful" — to praise, to extol

Wiktionary: Japanese 賛美 (さんび) "praise; extolment" and, as a verb, "to praise; to extol; to sing the praises of"; Chinese 讚美, transitive, "to extol; to admire; to praise; to eulogize; to commend", kept with 贊美 as aliases. It is the root of [[賛美歌]] and is close to [[賛歌]]. 賛's own `stand_in` is 賛助 and 美's is itself, so this word legitimizes neither.
