Wiktionary: Chinese 大氣, in meteorology, "atmosphere; air", and also "grandeur; magnificence", "heavy breathing" and, of something, "imposing; impressive"; Japanese 大気 "an atmosphere". The spelling 大氣 is the alias. It is the air of the sky, beside [[空気]] and [[風]].
