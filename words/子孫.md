## Notes
- [子](../characters/子.md) "child" + [孫](../characters/孫.md) "grandchild" — no stand-in relationship applies (子's own stand-in is [[児子]]; 孫's own is [[孫子]]).

子孫 means descendants — one's children and grandchildren, and by extension all future generations of a family line.

Mandarin zǐsūn, Cantonese zi2 syun1, Japanese しそん, Korean 자손, and Vietnamese tử tôn are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `諺文`/`羅馬字` had 子's syllable as 지/ji instead of 즈/jǝ (contradicting the word's own correct 注音 and every other 子-compound established this session). No homophones (注音 ㄐㄜㄙㄛㄋ unique across both words and characters).
