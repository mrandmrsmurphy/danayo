## Notes
- [統](../characters/統.md) "command, govern, unite" + [率](../characters/率.md) "rate, ratio, frequency" — 統's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 率's own is [[比率]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 統率 legitimizes 統 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 率 is individually `kwin: false`. 率's syllable here (sud/숟/ㄙㄨㄊ) is a documented, intentional alternate reading shared with [[率性]] (both explicitly noted on `characters/率.md`), distinct from 率's bare-citation reading (lud/룯/ㄌㄨㄊ) — not a bug.

統率 means to command or lead — especially troops or an organization.

Mandarin tǒngshuài, Cantonese tung2 seot1, Japanese とうそつ (tōsotsu), and Korean 통솔 (tongsol) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 統率 is itself a common attested word for "command, lead" in all four languages. Vietnamese thống suất is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
