## Notes
- [軟](../characters/軟.md) "soft, gentle" + [禁](../characters/禁.md) "prohibit, confine" — confinement without hard imprisonment

Mandarin ruǎnjìn, cantonese jyun5 gam3, japanese なんきん, korean 연금, and vietnamese nhuyễn cấm are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 넌김 diverges from Sino-Korean 연금 in both syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄝㄋㄍㄧㄇ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 軟's own `stand_in` is 柔軟 and 禁's own `stand_in` is 禁止, so this compound legitimizes neither constituent.
