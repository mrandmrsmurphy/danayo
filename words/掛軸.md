## Notes
- [掛 (char)](../characters/掛%20(char).md) "hang" + [軸 (char)](../characters/軸%20(char).md) "scroll"

掛軸 is a hanging scroll: a painting or calligraphy mounted on a roll that hangs on a wall. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin guàzhóu as "hanging scroll" and Japanese かけじく. Korean 괘축 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese gwaa3 zuk6 matches.

It carries a [書法](書法.md), calligraphy, or a [水墨画](水墨画.md), and is the portable counterpart of the [壁画](壁画.md).
