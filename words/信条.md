## Notes
- [信](../characters/信.md) "trust" + [条 (char)](../characters/条%20(char).md) "clause, article; classifier for long, thin objects" — "creed, article, precept, tenet" (a belief or principle one holds to, or an article of a formal document). Mandarin xìntiáo, Cantonese seon3 tiu4, Japanese しんじょう, Korean 신조, and Vietnamese tín điều are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 신조 matches Sino-Korean 신조 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄋㄐㄛ unique among both words and characters).
