## Notes
- [旅](../characters/旅.md) "travel; go on a trip" + [券 (char)](../characters/券%20(char).md) "bond; certificate"

旅券 is a passport: an official document that lets a person travel abroad and proves their nationality. Wiktionary glosses Japanese りょけん as "passport" in the legal and official register, and records Korean as 여권 in South Korea and 려권 in North Korea; the Korean field uses the North Korean form. For Chinese it records only a dated Taiwanese Hokkien use, since Mandarin says 護照, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. The word is literally "travel certificate".

The document is issued by the state under the law of [国籍](国籍.md), nationality. It is related to [通行証](通行証.md), a permit or pass, and to [許可](許可.md), permission.
