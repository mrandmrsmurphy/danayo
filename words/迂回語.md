## Notes
- [迂](../characters/迂.md) "roundabout; circuitous" + [回 (char)](../characters/回%20(char).md) "to turn; to go round" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language; expression" — the word [迂回](迂回.md) "circumvent" with 語 added

迂回語 is the periphrastic expression itself: the multi-word phrase that says in a roundabout way what a single form would otherwise say. It is a Dan'a'yo coinage, not an attested term in any of the daughter languages; the fields are compositional readings of the three characters, built on the existing [迂回](迂回.md) "to go around". A form-naming term here follows the general pattern that 〜法 names a rule or mechanism and 〜語 names the actual form or expression it yields; hence 迂回語, the expression, not 迂回法, the method.

It is needed for one rule in the grammar of reduplication (see 文法 - 05形態): a word that is already doubled by nature, such as [[人人]] "everyone", cannot be reduplicated again, so the speaker must use a periphrastic expression instead of the reduplicated diminutive or superlative. In Mandarin the nearest term is 迂回表达 and in Japanese 迂言法 (a method, the -法 side of the distinction above); both name the technique, whereas 迂回語 names the phrase. See also [[指小詞]] and [[最高級]], the forms that reduplication produces.
