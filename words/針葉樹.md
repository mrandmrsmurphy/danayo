## Notes
- [針](../characters/針%20(char).md) "needle" + [葉](../characters/葉%20(char).md) "leaf" + [樹](../characters/樹.md) "tree" — "needle-leaf tree," a conifer. Their own `stand_in` values: 針's is itself; 葉's is itself; 樹's is [[樹木]]; none points here, so 針葉樹 is an independent compound.

針葉樹 is a conifer: a tree with needle-like leaves, such as the pine and the cedar, and so a softwood. Mandarin zhēnyèshù and Japanese しんようじゅ are attested, and Korean 침엽수 is the same compound. Compare [[松]] (pine), [[柏]] (cypress) and [[針葉林]] (conifer forest).
