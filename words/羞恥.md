## Notes
- [羞](../characters/羞.md) "shame" + [恥](../characters/恥.md) "shame, disgrace" — 羞's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 恥's own is [[恥辱]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 羞恥 legitimizes 羞 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 羞 is individually `kwin: false`.

羞恥 means shame.

Mandarin xiūchǐ, Cantonese sau1 ci2, Japanese しゅうち (shūchi), and Korean 수치 (suchi) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 羞恥 is itself a common attested word for "shame" in all four languages. Vietnamese tu sỉ is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
