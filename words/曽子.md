## Notes
- [曽](../characters/曽%20(char).md) "great-" (used here as part of a proper name) + [子](../characters/子.md) "master, philosopher" — no stand-in relationship applies (曽's own stand-in is itself; 子's own is [[児子]]).

曽子 is Zengzi, the Confucian philosopher.

Mandarin Zēngzǐ, Cantonese zang1 zi2, Japanese そうし, Korean 증자, and Vietnamese Tăng Tử are all standard, fully compositional readings. Removed a false "same sound as 中指 (middle finger)" tip callout: 曽子's 注音 (ㄐㄜㄫㄐㄜ) and every cross-linguistic reading differ from 中指's (注音 ㄐㄨㄫㄐㄧㄜ; mandarin zhōngzhǐ, cantonese zong1 zi3, japanese ちゅうし, korean 중지) in all five languages — not a genuine pun or homophone in any of them. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/`aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄜㄫㄐㄜ unique across both words and characters).
