## Notes
- [推](../characters/推%20(char).md) "push" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [推](../characters/推%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

推 means to push: to press on something so that it moves away from the agent (推車, "push a cart"; 推門, "push a door"), and by extension to promote, advance, or put forward (推薦, "recommend"; 推進, "propel forward"; 推行, "carry out"), or to infer (推論, 推理, "reason, deduce"). Its antonym in the physical sense is 拉 "pull".

Mandarin tuī, Cantonese teoi1, Japanese すい (推薦/すいせん, "recommendation"; 推理/すいり, "deduction"), Korean 추 (추천, 추리) and Vietnamese suy (suy luận, "to reason"; suy đoán, "to infer") are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native verb 押す (おす) for the everyday physical act, and 推す (おす) for "recommend, back", so the on'yomi すい is bound to compounds.
