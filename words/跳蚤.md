## Notes
- [跳](../characters/跳.md) "hop, jump" + [蚤](../characters/蚤.md) "flea" — "flea" (literally "jumping flea").

Mandarin tiàozǎo and cantonese tiu3 zou2 are directly attested standard readings. Japanese とうそう, korean 도조, and vietnamese khiêu tảo are all honest compositional readings (跳's own TOU/도/khiêu + 蚤's own SOU/조/tảo), replacing what had been the everyday native-equivalent terms for "flea" in each language (Japanese のみ/ノミ, Korean 벼룩, Vietnamese con bọ chét) — a real-equivalent-instead-of-own-reading bug, now fixed. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 톳잣 diverges from Sino-Korean 도조 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄛㄨㄐㄚㄨ unique among both words and characters).

蚤's own `stand_in` is this exact word (跳蚤), so 蚤 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 跳蚤; 跳's own `stand_in` is instead 跳躍, so this word does not legitimize 跳.
