## Notes
- [凝](../characters/凝.md) "congeal, fix" + [視](../characters/視%20(char).md) "see, look" — "fixed look," to gaze or stare. Their own `stand_in` values: 凝's is [[凝結]]; 視's is itself; none points here, so 凝視 is an independent compound.

凝視 is to gaze or stare: to look fixedly at something. Mandarin níngshì, Cantonese jing4 si6, Japanese ぎょうし and Korean 응시 all use it. It is the fixed look, the steadier cousin of [[視]] (to look) and [[見]] (to see).
