## Notes
- [常](../characters/常.md) "common; normal; frequent; often" + [用](../characters/用.md) "use" — no stand-in relationship applies (常's own `stand_in` is [[日常]], 用's is [[使用]]).

常用 means commonly used, in everyday use — most famously the basis of Japan's own 常用漢字 (jōyō kanji), the official list of characters designated for regular use in ordinary writing.

Japanese じょうよう, Korean 상용, and Vietnamese thường dụng (verified — the genuine compositional reading, distinct from the superficially similar but unrelated everyday Vietnamese adverb thông thường, "usually") are all standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼘ㄫ·⼄ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
