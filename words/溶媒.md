## Notes
- [溶](../characters/溶.md) "melt, dissolve" + [媒](../characters/媒.md) "mediator, go-between" — solvent.

溶媒 is a solvent: the liquid in which a solute ([[溶質]]) dissolves to make a solution ([[溶液]]); water is the commonest. 媒 "mediator, go-between" gives the picture of the solvent as the medium that brings the dissolved substance into the solution. Mandarin usually says 溶劑 (róngjì) for the same thing and treats róngméi as dated, so the Mandarin reading is the less common one; Cantonese jung4 mui4, Japanese ようばい and Korean 용매 are the standard readings.
