## Notes
- [悲 (char)](../characters/悲%20(char).md) "grief" + [憤](../characters/憤.md) "indignation"

悲憤 is grief and anger together: indignation that is full of sorrow, at a wrong one cannot undo. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin bēifèn as "sad and resentful; indignant" and Japanese ひふん as "resentment, grief, indignation". Vietnamese bi phẫn and Cantonese bei1 fan5 match, and Korean 비분 is the reading of the characters.

It joins [悲痛](悲痛.md), grief, with [憤慨](憤慨.md), indignation, and is the feeling of a patriot or of one who has been wronged.
