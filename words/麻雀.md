## Notes
- [麻](../characters/麻.md) "hemp" + [雀](../characters/雀.md) "sparrow" — no `#cranberry`: 麻's own `stand_in` is [[大麻]], 雀's is [[麻雀鳥]]; neither points here.

麻雀 is deliberately reserved in this vault for the tile game "mahjong," while the literal "sparrow" sense uses the extended, disambiguating compound [[麻雀鳥]] (雀's own legitimizing form). Mandarin máquè and Cantonese maa4zoek3-2 both cover the mahjong sense colloquially/dialectally (especially in Cantonese, where 麻雀 is the standard word for the game) alongside the literal "sparrow" reading. Japanese マージャン (a phonetic rendering, mājan) and Korean 마작 are standard game-specific terms, and Sino-Vietnamese mạt chược (a loan via Cantonese) is the standard Vietnamese name for the game.
