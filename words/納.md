## Notes
- [納](../characters/納%20(char).md) "receive, accept" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 納 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

納 means to receive, accept, or take in — extended to "to pay (dues), submit," as in 納税 (pay taxes).

Mandarin nà, Cantonese naap6, and Korean 납 (nap) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the on-reading ノウ (NOU, as in 納入 "payment, delivery") for the bare character alone, since no native kun-reading is stored. Vietnamese nạp is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading (as in nạp thuế, "pay tax"), one of many stored candidates. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
