## Notes
- [直](../characters/直%20(char).md) "straight" + [接](../characters/接%20(char).md) "touch, connect" — "straight contact," direct. Their own `stand_in` values: 直's is itself; 接's is itself; none points here, so 直接 is an independent compound.

直接 is direct: with nothing in between, immediate. Mandarin zhíjiē, Cantonese zik6 zip3, Japanese ちょくせつ and Korean 직접 all use it, and Vietnamese trực tiếp does too. It is the structural "direct" (a direct flight, direct speech), where [[率直]] is the frank, straightforward "direct" of a person. It is the opposite of 間接 (indirect).
