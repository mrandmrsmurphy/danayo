## Notes
- [黄 (char)](../characters/黄%20(char).md) "yellow" + [檗](../characters/檗.md) "cork tree" — legitimizing note: 檗's own `stand_in` is [[黄檗]] itself, already annotated in its own citation; 黄's own `stand_in` is 黄 itself, so transitivity fails — no `#cranberry`.

Mandarin huángbò, Japanese おうばく, Korean 황박, and Sino-Vietnamese hoàng bách are all standard, fully compositional readings for the Amur cork tree (*Phellodendron amurense*).
