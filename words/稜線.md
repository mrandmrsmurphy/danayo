## Notes
- [稜](../characters/稜.md) "ridge, edge" + [線](../characters/線.md) "line" — "ridge line." Their own `stand_in` values: 稜's is [[三稜鏡]]; 線's is [[直線]]; none points here, so 稜線 is an independent compound.

稜線 is a ridgeline: the crest of a mountain range along which the ground falls away on both sides. Mandarin léngxiàn, Japanese りょうせん and Korean 능선 use it; Wiktionary lists no Cantonese reading, so the `cantonese` field is omitted. Compare [[山脈]] (mountain range) and [[高峰]] (peak).
