## Notes
- [停](../characters/停.md) "to stop, stay" + [泊](../characters/泊.md) "to moor (a boat)" — "to berth, anchor, moor" (a ship). Both `korean: 정박` (compositional) and `vietnamese: đình bạc` (directly attested, confirmed via web search) fill previously blank fields; 泊 alone is famous from Zhang Ji's Tang poem 楓橋夜泊 ("Night Mooring at Maple Bridge"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 덩박 diverges from the compositional Sino-Korean 정박 in the first syllable's initial consonant. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄝㄫㄅㄚㄎ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 泊's own `stand_in` is this exact word (停泊), so 泊 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 停泊.
