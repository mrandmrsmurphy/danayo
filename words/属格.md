## Notes
- [属](../characters/属.md) "belong to" + [格 (char)](../characters/格%20(char).md) "case; grid" — no stand-in relationship applies (属's own `stand_in` is [[所属]], 格's is itself).

属格 is the grammatical genitive case — the case marking possession or belonging ("of," "'s"), named directly from 属's "to belong to" sense plus 格's general "grammatical case" sense (as also seen in [[格助詞]] "case particle"). It is synonymous with 所有格 ("possessive case"), a competing term built on 所有 ("to possess") instead.

Japanese ぞっかく, Korean 속격, and Vietnamese thuộc cách (verified as a real linguistic term) are all standard grammatical terminology across the four languages. No HSK level is given for the compound. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄛㄎㄍㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
