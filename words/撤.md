## Notes
- [撤](../characters/撤%20(char).md) "omit, remove, withdraw" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [撤](../characters/撤%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

撤 means to omit, remove, or withdraw.

Mandarin chè, Cantonese cit3, Japanese てつ, Korean 철, and Vietnamese triệt are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones at the word level (注音 ㄉㄝㄊ is shared only with bound characters 姪/窒/跌, none of which has an independent word at this exact reading).
