## Notes
- [汁](characters/汁%20(char).md) "juice" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 汁 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

汁 means soup, juice.

Mandarin zhī, Cantonese zap1, Korean 즙, and Vietnamese chấp are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 汁's own stored citations exactly.
