## Notes
- [第 (char)](../characters/第%20(char).md) "-th" + [五 (char)](../characters/五%20(char).md) "five" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here, so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false`, as for [[第三]].

Wiktionary lists 第五 under Chinese: "fifth" and "fifthly", and as a surname; the video-game reading is informal short for Identity V. It is the ordinal formed by prefixing 第 to 五, in the same pattern as [[第一]], [[第二]], [[第三]] and [[第四]].
