## Notes
- [第 (char)](../characters/第%20(char).md) "-th" + [五 (char)](../characters/五%20(char).md) "five" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here, so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false`, as for [[第三]].

第五 means "fifth," the ordinal formed by prefixing 第 to 五, in the same pattern as [[第一]], [[第二]] and [[第三]].

Mandarin dì-wǔ, Cantonese dai6 ng5, Japanese だいご and Korean 제오 are all standard, fully compositional readings; Vietnamese đệ ngũ is likewise compositional.
