## Notes
- [法](../characters/法%20(char).md) "law" + [西](../characters/西.md) "west" + [斯](../characters/斯.md) "this" + [主](../characters/主.md) "master, main" + [義](../characters/義.md) "principle" — "fascism," 法西斯 transliterating "fascist" with 主義 "-ism." Their own `stand_in` values: 法's is itself; 西's is [[西方]]; 斯's is [[斯文]]; 主's is [[主人]]; 義's is [[意義]]; none points here, so 法西斯主義 is an independent compound.

法西斯主義 is fascism. The first three characters, 法西斯, transliterate Italian fascista by sound, and [[主義]] makes it an "-ism," as in [[共産主義]] and [[社会主義]]. Mandarin fǎxīsī zhǔyì and Cantonese faat3 sai1 si1 zyu2 ji6 use it. Japanese says ファシズム and Korean 파시즘, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[独裁者]] (dictator).
