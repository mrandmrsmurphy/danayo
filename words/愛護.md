## Notes
- [愛](../characters/愛%20(char).md) "love" + [護](../characters/護.md) "protect" — no stand-in relationship applies (愛's own stand-in is itself; 護's own is itself).

愛護 means to protect — caring for and safeguarding something out of affection.

Mandarin àihù, Cantonese oi3 wu6, Japanese あいご, Korean 애호, and Vietnamese ái hộ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 애호 coincidentally matches [[愛好]]'s own Korean reading (since 好 and 護 are both 호 in Sino-Korean), but the two remain distinct at the Dan'a'yo level (aiho/ㄚㄧㄏㄛ vs aihau/ㄚㄧㄏㄚㄨ) — a real-Korean-only coincidence, not a Dan'a'yo homophone. Filled blank `vietnamese`, omitted empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄧㄏㄛ unique across both words and characters).
