## Notes
- [騙](../characters/騙%20(char).md) "deceive, defraud" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 騙 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

騙 means to deceive, cheat, or defraud. Its 形声 semantic component 馬 ("horse") is thought to derive the sense from leaping swiftly and deceptively onto a horse's back — a striking, if indirect, etymological link between horsemanship and trickery.

Mandarin piàn, Cantonese pin3, and Korean 편 (pyeon) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese へん (hen, on'yomi) is the standard reading. Vietnamese phiến is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading; the character page's other stored candidate, biển, is a less common variant. This word is part of a genuine three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read pyen/편/ㄆ⼶ㄋ): [[篇]] ("article") and [[偏]] ("biased, inclined").
