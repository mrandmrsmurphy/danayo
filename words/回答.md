## Notes
- [回 (char)](../characters/回%20(char).md) "turns" + [答 (char)](../characters/答%20(char).md) "answer, reply, assent, return" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both are bare self-standing characters).

Mandarin huídá, Cantonese wui4 daap3, Japanese かいとう, and Korean 회답 are the standard readings. Vietnamese hồi đáp is a real Sino-Vietnamese word, more formal and written than the everyday trả lời.
