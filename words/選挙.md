## Notes
- [選](../characters/選.md) "choose" + [挙 (char)](../characters/挙%20(char).md) "raise"

選挙 is an election. It is spelled with the Dan'a'yo forms of the characters, and 選舉, 選擧 and 选举 are aliases. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 選舉 (Mandarin xuǎnjǔ, Cantonese syun2 geoi2) as "to elect" and, classical, "to nominate for an official post", and Japanese せんきょ as "election" and "campaigning". It lists Korean 선거 and Vietnamese entries. See also [投票](投票.md), to vote.
