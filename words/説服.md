## Notes
- [説](../characters/説.md) "speak, explain" + [服](../characters/服.md) "submit, serve" — literally "to speak [someone] into submission." No stand-in relationship applies (説's own `stand_in` is [[学説]]; 服's own is [[服事]]).

説服 means to convince, to persuade someone successfully — implying the other party actually comes around, distinct from the softer 勧める-type "to urge" which doesn't guarantee success. This vault's word for Rosenfelder's "convince."

Mandarin shuōfú and Vietnamese thuyết phục are both the real, standard, everyday words for this concept (HSK 4 in Mandarin). Cantonese syut3 fuk6 is the compositional reading (also attested as syu3 fuk6 in some sources, following the classical alternate "shuì" reading of 説 for its "persuade" sense specifically, as opposed to "shuō" for plain "speak"). **Japanese and Korean diverge**: Japanese doesn't really use 説服 at all — living Japanese uses [[説得]] instead for this whole concept, so せっぷく here is the mechanical on'yomi rather than an attested word. Korean 설복 is likewise secondary to [[説得]]'s own 설득. `kwin` is false: both 説 and 服 are individually `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼔ㄊㄅㄨㄎ unique across both words and characters).
