## Notes
- [詩](../characters/詩.md) "poem" + [篇 (char)](../characters/篇%20(char).md) "chapter, section (of writing)" — literally "poem-chapters".

詩篇 names both "poems" generally and, capitalized as a proper noun, the Book of Psalms specifically — the standard CJK Bible-translation name for that book, since the Psalms are themselves a collection of sung poems/hymns.

Mandarin shīpiān, Cantonese si1 pin1, and Korean 시편 (sipyeon) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 시편 is itself the standard Korean Bible name for Psalms. Japanese しへん (shihen) is likewise compositional. Vietnamese Thi Thiên is the standard Vietnamese Protestant Bible name for the Book of Psalms, directly compositional from 詩's thi and 篇's thiên (the Catholic liturgical term is Thánh Thi, which is not built from either constituent character).
