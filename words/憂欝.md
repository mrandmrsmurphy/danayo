## Notes
- [憂](../characters/憂.md) "worry" + [欝](../characters/欝.md) "gloom"

憂欝 is melancholy: a heavy, gloomy low mood, and in a medical sense depression. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yōuyù as "sad; depressed; melancholic; dejected" and Japanese ゆううつ as "depressed, gloomy, melancholic". Korean 우울, Vietnamese ưu uất and Cantonese jau1 wat1 match. The usual spelling is 憂鬱, which is an alias here, because 鬱 is written 欝 in this vault.

It is a mood with no single cause, where [悲哀](悲哀.md) has its object, and its remedy is the lightness of [愉快](愉快.md). A troubled worry is [憂慮](憂慮.md).
