## Etymology
- [逍](characters/逍.md) "ramble, stroll, jaunt, loiter" + [遥](characters/遥.md) "far away, distant, remote"

## Notes
Mandarin xiāoyáo, cantonese siu1 jiu4, japanese しょうよう, korean 소요, and vietnamese tiêu dao are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. `逍遙` is the standard traditional-character spelling variant (遥→遙), correctly listed as an alias. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 숏욧 diverges from Sino-Korean 소요 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼄ㄨ⼄ㄨ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 逍's own `stand_in` is this exact word (逍遥), so 逍 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 逍遥; 遥's own `stand_in` is instead 遥遠, so this word does not legitimize 遥.
