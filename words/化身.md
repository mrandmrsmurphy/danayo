## Notes
- [化](../characters/化%20(char).md) "-ification" + [身](../characters/身.md) "body" — no stand-in relationship applies (化 is its own stand-in; 身's own is [[身体]]).

化身 means an incarnation or embodiment — a deity, spirit, or abstract quality made manifest in bodily form (悪の化身 "the embodiment of evil").

Japanese けしん uses 化's alternate on'yomi KE (rather than the more common KA) + SHIN. Korean 화신 and Vietnamese hóa thân are both standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⺢ㄙㄧㄋ unique across both words and characters).
