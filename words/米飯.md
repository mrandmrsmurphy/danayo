## Notes
- [米 (char)](../characters/米%20(char).md) "rice (uncooked)" + [飯](../characters/飯.md) "cooked rice; food" — `characters/飯.md`'s own `stand_in` points here, meaning 飯 does not stand independently outside this compound.

The Korean word 쌀밥 (쌀 "uncooked rice" + 밥 "cooked rice/meal") is a native compound and not a Sino-Korean reading of 米飯, and no attested Sino-Korean compound (미반) is recorded, so the `korean` field is omitted. The `vietnamese` field is omitted too, since modern Vietnamese uses the native cơm for "cooked rice."
