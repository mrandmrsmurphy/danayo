## Notes
- [江](../characters/江.md) "river, cascade" + [湖](../characters/湖.md) "lake" — no stand-in relationship applies (江's own stand-in is [[大江]]; 湖's own is [[湖水]]).

江湖, literally "rivers and lakes," extends to mean the wider countryside, the wandering/itinerant world outside settled society, and by further extension the criminal underworld (as in wuxia fiction) or a reclusive life away from the establishment.

Mandarin jiānghú, Cantonese gong1 wu4, Japanese こうこ, and Korean 강호 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 江's and 湖's own stored citations exactly). Vietnamese giang hồ (江's own giang + 湖's own hồ) is also the real, standard Vietnamese term carrying the same extended "underworld/itinerant" sense. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄫㄏㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).
