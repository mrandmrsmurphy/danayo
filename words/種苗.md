## Notes
- [種](../characters/種.md) "kind, type, seed, species" + [苗](../characters/苗.md) "seedling" — 苗's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 種's own is [[種類]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 苗 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 苗 is individually `kwin: false`.

種苗 names seed and seedling stock together — plants at the earliest stage of cultivation, whether seeds or young sprouts, especially in an agricultural or aquacultural production context (種苗業, "seed/seedling industry"; also used for fish/shellfish fry in aquaculture, 種苗生産, "seed production").

Mandarin zhǒngmiáo, Cantonese zung2 miu4, Japanese しゅびょう (shubyō), and Korean 종묘 (jongmyo, note the homograph clash with the unrelated 宗廟 "royal ancestral shrine," also read 종묘) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese chủng miêu is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form; everyday Vietnamese more often uses the native cây giống ("seedling plant").
