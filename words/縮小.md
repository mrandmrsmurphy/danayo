## Notes
- [縮 (char)](../characters/縮%20(char).md) "shrink" + [小 (char)](../characters/小%20(char).md) "small"

縮小 is to shrink or reduce in size: to make a thing smaller, or to become smaller. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin suōxiǎo as "to shrink; to decrease in size" and Japanese しゅくしょう as "reduction, curtailment, cut; to reduce, curtail". Cantonese suk1 siu2 and Korean 축소 match.

It is the opposite of [拡大](拡大.md), expand, and is for size, scope or scale. A count that goes down is [減少](減少.md).
