## Notes
- [校](../characters/校.md) "school" (here in its "proofread, collate" sense) + [訂](../characters/訂%20(char).md) "revise, correct" — no stand-in relationship applies (校's own stand-in is [[学校]]; 訂's own is itself).

校訂 means to revise.

Japanese こうてい, Korean 교정, and Vietnamese hiệu đính are all standard, fully compositional readings. Mandarin jiàodìng and Cantonese gaau3 ding3 use 校's polyphonic "proofread/collate" reading (jiào/gaau3), the same character-level citation gap already flagged on [[校正]] — 校's own stored citation lists only xiào/haau6 (the "school" sense). No homophones (注音 ㄏ⼘ㄨㄊㄝㄫ unique across both words and characters).
