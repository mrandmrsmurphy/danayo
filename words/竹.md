## Notes
- [竹 (char)](../characters/竹%20(char).md) "bamboo" — stand-in for [[竹 (char)|竹]], which cannot appear independently

竹 is 象形 of two bamboo stalks with drooping leaves, the modern form (ケ+ケ) a stylization of an earlier 个+个-shaped original. As one of the East Asian cultural sphere's most symbolically loaded plants, the character's compound family spans both the botanical (竹筍 "bamboo shoot," 篠竹 "dwarf bamboo," 竿竹 "bamboo pole") and the emblematic: 松竹梅 ("pine, bamboo, and plum" — the Three Friends of Winter, a set that appears repeatedly across Chinese, Japanese, and Korean painting and New Year iconography as a symbol of resilience, since all three stay vibrant through winter).

**Incidental fix**: `characters/竹 (char).md`'s own `japanese_native` field carried the same malformed-duplicate bug already found on [[石]] (a clean scalar たけ followed by a stray repeated list-item line) — corrected to a single scalar. Japanese carries a single on'yomi ちく (CHIKU, 竹林 chikurin "bamboo grove"), alongside the fully productive native 訓読み たけ (take), the everyday word for the living plant. Vietnamese trúc survives mainly in literary/classical register (trúc mai, a poetic pairing evoking constancy) rather than everyday speech. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 죽 (jug, from Middle Chinese 知母 ʈ + 屋三韻 ɨuk) coincides exactly with Sino-Korean 죽. 竹 shares its Dan'a'yo reading with [[粥]] ("congee") — a coincidental convergence, unrelated in meaning.
