## Notes
- [心](../characters/心%20(char).md) "heart, mind" + [理](../characters/理.md) "reason, principle" — no stand-in relationship applies (心's own stand-in is itself; 理's own is [[理由]]).

心理 means mentality or psychology — the workings of the mind.

Mandarin xīnlǐ, Cantonese sam1 lei5, Japanese しんり, Korean 심리, and Vietnamese tâm lý are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄇㄌㄧ unique across both words and characters).
