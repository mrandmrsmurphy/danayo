## Notes
- [語](../characters/語.md) "language" + [幹](../characters/幹.md) "trunk"

語幹 is the stem of a word. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 語幹 (Mandarin yǔgàn) as the linguistics term for "stem", with 詞幹 as an alternative name, and Japanese ごかん as "stem". The Cantonese and Korean 어간 fields are built from the readings of the two characters. Compare [語根](語根.md), the root.
