## Notes
- [痛](../characters/痛.md) "pain" + [罵](../characters/罵.md) "scold"
- This word is the viable form of [罵](../characters/罵.md): the character's meaning (to abuse, scold) is identical, but 罵 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 痛罵 is its `stand_in` compound.

痛罵 means to scold or denounce someone severely: 罵 is "to curse, scold" and 痛 here is the intensifier "bitterly, to the quick". Mandarin tòngmà, Cantonese tung3 maa6, Japanese 痛罵 (つうば, tsūba; 痛罵する, "to lash out at") and Korean 통매 (tongmae) are the same compound, usually written about a public denunciation or a harsh critique rather than a private quarrel.

In Dan'a'yo it is the strong, vituperative end of the scolding range, above [[叱責]] ("rebuke, scold"). No narrower or shifted sense is needed.
