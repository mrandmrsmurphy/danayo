## Notes
- [二 (char)](../characters/二%20(char).md) "two" + [千](../characters/千.md) "thousand" — two thousand.

Mandarin èrqiān, Cantonese ji6 cin1, Japanese にせん, and Korean 이천 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese uses the native numeral hai nghìn (northern-standard form; hai ngàn is the equally valid southern-dialect variant), matching Vietnamese's general preference for native numeral vocabulary over Sino-Vietnamese compounds in everyday counting.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 늬천 diverges from Sino-Korean 이천, since 二's own Dan'a'yo reading doesn't match Sino-Korean, even though 千's own reading matches exactly (千 is independently `kwin: true`).
