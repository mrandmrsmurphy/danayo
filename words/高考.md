## Notes
- [高 (char)](../characters/高%20(char).md) "high" + [考](../characters/考.md) "consider, regard" — no `#cranberry`: 高's own `stand_in` is 高 itself, 考's is [[考慮]]; neither points here.

高考 (gāokǎo) refers specifically to China's national college/university entrance examination. Cantonese gou1haau2 uses the same real Chinese term. Japanese and Korean both instead transliterate the Mandarin name phonetically — ガオカオ (gaokao) and 가오카오 (gaokao) — rather than reading the characters compositionally, likely in part to avoid ambiguity with [[高校]] (こうこう, "secondary school"), which would otherwise collide in Japanese on'yomi. Sino-Vietnamese cao khảo is the compositional form, used descriptively for the Chinese exam system.
