## Notes
- [課 (char)](../characters/課%20(char).md) "lesson; chapter" + [程](../characters/程.md) "extent; degree"

課程 is a course of study: a curriculum, or the body of lessons that makes up a subject. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin kèchéng as "course; curriculum; coursework" (it also records an archaic tax sense, which Dan'a'yo does not take) and Japanese かてい as "course; curriculum". Korean 과정 and Cantonese fo3 cing4 match.

It is broader than [課](課.md), a single lesson or chapter, and is taught over one or more [学期](学期.md).
