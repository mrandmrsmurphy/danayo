## Notes
- [随](../characters/随.md) "accompany" + [意](../characters/意.md) "intention, meaning"

随意 means optional, voluntary, or at one's own discretion — as one wishes, without external compulsion. Mandarin 隨意/随意 (suíyì), Cantonese 隨意 (ceoi4ji3), Japanese 随意 (ずいい), Korean 수의 (suui), and Sino-Vietnamese tuỳ ý are all everyday, high-frequency words for this concept across the CJKV sphere — Vietnamese tuỳ ý in particular is ubiquitous, appearing constantly in casual speech ("as you like," "up to you").
