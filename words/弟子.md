## Notes
- [弟 (char)](../characters/弟%20(char).md) "younger brother" + [子](../characters/子.md) "noun suffix" — no stand-in relationship applies (弟's own `stand_in` is itself, 子's is [[児子]]). 子 here is in its light noun-suffix role (as in [[梯子]]), extending 弟's "younger brother" sense to "junior/apprentice."

弟子 names a disciple or apprentice — someone in the "younger brother" position relative to a master or teacher, the same metaphor of seniority found across the Confucian tradition (子曰：「弟子入則孝」, the Analects' opening use of this exact term).

Japanese ていし, Korean 제자, and Vietnamese đệ tử are all standard, non-drifted readings. **A real cross-word coincidence, not a Dan'a'yo homophone**: this word's Korean reading (제자) happens to be identical to [[梯子]]'s own Korean field, even though the two words are not homophones in Dan'a'yo itself (te-jǝ vs de-jǝ, distinct at the ㄊ/ㄉ initial) — already documented in [[梯子]]'s own Notes as a pure Korean-level pun. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄝㄐㄜ unique across both words and characters).
