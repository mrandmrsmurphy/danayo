## Notes
- [十 (char)](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — literally "ten-day," the tenth day of the month.

Mandarin shí rì, Cantonese sap6 jat6, and Korean 십일 (sibil) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese has a special irregular native reading, とおか (tōka), not the expected compositional じゅうにち (jūnichi) — one of the well-known irregular day-of-month readings (compare [[四日]], [[八日]]). Vietnamese thập nhật is likewise compositional, from 十's thập and 日's own nhật. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 십닏 diverges from Sino-Korean 십일 in the second syllable's coda, the same MC-checked-coda-vs-Sino-Korean-regularized divergence documented throughout this vault's 日-compounds. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄆㄋㄧㄊ unique among both words and characters).
