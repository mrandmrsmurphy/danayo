## Notes
- [光 (char)](../characters/光%20(char).md) "light" + [芒](../characters/芒.md) "ray, point" — the radiating points of light thrown off by a bright source (the sun, a flame, a gem); figuratively, a person's brilliance or dazzling talent.

光芒 is "rays of light; radiance". English Wiktionary gives the Chinese noun two senses: literally "rays of light; radiance" (光芒四射, "rays of light radiating in all directions"), and figuratively "honor; glory". It lists 光線 as a synonym for the literal sense, and 光彩, 光榮, 榮耀 and 榮譽 for the figurative one, and gives 光芒萬丈 as a derived term. Japanese こうぼう and Korean 광망 are listed as descendants. The Light page lists it as "rays, radiance, a beam of light", the literal sense.

Mandarin guāngmáng and Cantonese gwong1 mong4 are as at English Wiktionary.

No other word shares the 注音 ㄎ⺢ㄫㄇ⼘ㄫ (checked 2026-10-12).
