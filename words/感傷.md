## Notes
- [感](../characters/感.md) "feel" + [傷](../characters/傷.md) "hurt"

感傷 is sentimentality: sadness felt because one is moved, often a pleasant, self-indulgent sorrow. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin gǎnshāng as "sad because of being moved; sentimental; melancholic" and Japanese かんしょう as "sentimentality". Vietnamese cảm thương and Cantonese gam2 soeng1 match, and Korean 감상 is the reading of the characters.

It is the soft sadness of memory, set against the real grief of [悲嘆](悲嘆.md), and close to [郷愁](郷愁.md), nostalgia. Feeling in general is [感情](感情.md).
