## Notes
- [満](../characters/満%20(char).md) "full" + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "moon, month" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 満's and 月's own stand-ins are themselves). For "full moon" specifically, use [[望月]].

満月 means one month old (a baby's one-month anniversary), or "full month."

Mandarin mǎnyuè, Cantonese mun5 jyut6, Japanese まんげつ, and Korean 만월 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 満's and 月's own stored citations exactly).
