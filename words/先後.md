## Notes
- [先](../characters/先.md) "prior" + [後 (char)](../characters/後%20(char).md) "after" — "successively, one after another, in succession," describing an ordered sequence (先後順序, "order of precedence"). Not the same as [[前後]] ("before and after, roughly," describing an approximate time range rather than a strict order). Mandarin xiānhòu, Cantonese sin1 hau6, Japanese せんご/せんこう, and Korean 선후 are all directly attested standard readings. `vietnamese: tiên hậu` is the honest compositional reading (先's own tiên + 後's own hậu); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 선훗 diverges from Sino-Korean 선후 in the second syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄋㄏㄨㄛ unique among both words and characters).
