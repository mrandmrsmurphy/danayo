## Notes
- [青 (char)](../characters/青%20(char).md) "blue" + [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year" — no `#cranberry`: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here (both 青's and 年's are themselves).

青年 is the standard, everyday word for "youth, young person" across all five languages: Mandarin qīngnián, Cantonese cing1nin4, Japanese せいねん (seinen), Korean 청년 (cheongnyeon), and Sino-Vietnamese thanh niên — a clean case of a compound retaining its full compositional meaning and everyday register everywhere.
