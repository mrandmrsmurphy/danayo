## Notes
- [額](../characters/額.md) "forehead, plaque" + [頭 (char)](../characters/頭%20(char).md) "head" — 額頭 is 額's own legitimizing compound (額's `stand_in` points here), but since 頭's own `stand_in` is 頭 itself instead, transitivity fails and no `#cranberry` tag applies.

額頭/额头 (étóu) is the standard, everyday Mandarin word for "forehead," and Cantonese 額頭 (ngaak6tau4) likewise. Japanese, Korean, and Vietnamese, by contrast, have no live Sino-derived compound for this body part at all — each instead uses its own native word: Japanese ひたい (hitai), Korean 이마 (ima), and Vietnamese trán, all given here as the accurate real-world equivalent rather than an unattested compositional form.
