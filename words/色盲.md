## Notes
- [色](../characters/色.md) "color" + [盲](../characters/盲.md) "blind" — color blindness.

色盲 is color blindness: the inherited or acquired inability to tell certain colors apart, most commonly red from green. It is 色 plus 盲 "blind." Mandarin sèmáng, Cantonese sik1 maang4, Japanese しきもう and Korean 색맹 are the standard readings. In Japanese 色盲 is now regarded as dated and is replaced in medical writing by 色覚異常 (しきかくいじょう, "abnormal color vision"), a point worth knowing before using the word in a clinical context.
