## Notes
- [嗣](../characters/嗣.md) "heir, continue the line" + [子](../characters/子.md) "son, child" — "heir," specifically the son of a principal wife entitled to inherit/continue the family line, as opposed to a concubine's son. `vietnamese: tự tử` is compositional from each character's own confirmed reading. **Important homograph-collision caveat**: this exact syllable sequence "tự tử" is also the extremely common, unrelated everyday Vietnamese word for "suicide" (自死/自尽, tự "self" + tử "die," from an entirely different character pair) — a coincidental homophone collision, not a translation error, but one that could seriously mislead if the compositional reading is taken as the real usage for "heir" (parallel to [[可不]]'s documented 가불 collision). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 시지 diverges from Sino-Korean 사자 in both syllables.
