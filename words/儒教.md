## Notes
- [儒](../characters/儒.md) "Confucian scholar" + [教](../characters/教.md) "teaching, doctrine" — "Confucianism," framing the tradition as a religious/moral teaching rather than an academic discipline (contrast [[儒学]], which frames it as a field of study). Mandarin rújiào, Cantonese jyu4 gaau3, Japanese じゅきょう, Korean 유교, and Vietnamese nho giáo are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 누걋 diverges from Sino-Korean 유교 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄨㄍ⼄ㄨ unique among both words and characters).

**Real content bug found and fixed**: `aliases` had included [[儒家]] — a separately-attested term (the "Confucian school" of thinkers/scholars) with its own dedicated vault page, not a spelling variant of 儒教 itself — removed, keeping only the genuine old-form character variant 儒敎.
