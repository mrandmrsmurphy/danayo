## Notes
- [儒](../characters/儒.md) "Confucian scholar" + [教](../characters/教.md) "teaching, doctrine" — "Confucianism," framing the tradition as a religious/moral teaching rather than an academic discipline (contrast [[儒学]], which frames it as a field of study). Mandarin rújiào, Cantonese jyu4 gaau3, Japanese じゅきょう, Korean 유교, and Vietnamese nho giáo are all directly attested standard readings.
