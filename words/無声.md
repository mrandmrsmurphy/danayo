## Notes
- [無](../words/無.md) "without, not" (stand-in: itself) + [声 (char)](../characters/声.md) "sound, voice" (stand-in: [[発声]]) — voiceless, without vocal-fold vibration.

無声 means voiceless. Mandarin wúshēng, Japanese むせい, Korean 무성, and Vietnamese vô thanh are all real, standard terms, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Cantonese mou4 sing1 is a straightforward compositional reading. Note this is distinct from the pre-existing [[清音]] ("unvoiced sound"), a different lexical choice for a closely related concept — `文法 - 02音韻論` consistently uses 無声 as its prefix, not 清.  `kwin` is false: both 無 and 声 are individually false.

No syllable-boundary dot is needed: the concatenated string `ㄇㄜㄙㄧㄫ` admits only one legal partition.
