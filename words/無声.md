## Notes
- [無](../words/無.md) "without, not" (stand-in: itself) + [声 (char)](../characters/声.md) "sound, voice" (stand-in: [[発声]]) — voiceless, without vocal-fold vibration.

Wiktionary: Japanese 無声 (むせい), phonetics, "voiceless"; Chinese 無聲 "noiseless; voiceless; silent". `文法 - 02音韻論` uses 無声 as its prefix for voiceless sounds, not [[清音]]. Counterpart: [[有声]].
