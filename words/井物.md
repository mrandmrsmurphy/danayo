## Notes
- [井](../characters/井.md) + [物 (char)](../characters/物%20(char).md) "thing" — Dan'a'yo uses 井 in place of the real character 丼 (donburi, "rice bowl dish"), the same substitution documented on the alias field; likely another Japanese-specific syllable-filling entry in the same family as [[井戸]] rather than a genuine Chinese compound, since 井+物 carries no compositional "bowl of food" meaning of its own.

丼物 (どんぶりもの, donburimono) is the general Japanese category name for rice-bowl dishes — a bowl of rice topped with meat, fish, egg, or vegetables, eaten as a single-dish meal (親子丼 oyakodon "chicken and egg bowl," 牛丼 gyūdon "beef bowl," 天丼 tendon "tempura bowl" are its best-known members). **Content note**: `korean`/`vietnamese` were filled with each language's own real equivalent concept — Korean 덮밥 (deopbap, "covered rice," the standard Korean term for the same rice-bowl-dish category) and Vietnamese cơm tô ("bowl rice") — rather than a forced compositional reading of 井+物, following the same honest-native-word approach already established on [[井戸]]; `cantonese` is left blank for the same reason `mandarin`'s own stored jǐngwù is likely a placeholder compositional reading rather than an attested Cantonese term, since this dish category doesn't have a fixed native Cantonese name built on these characters.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 징묻 diverges from a compositional Sino-Korean reading, since 井's own kwin is false (物's own kwin is true).
