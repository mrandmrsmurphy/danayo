## Notes
- [否 (char)](../characters/否%20(char).md) "no" + [則](../characters/則.md) "rule"

否則 is otherwise, or else. English Wiktionary has an entry only for Chinese 否則 (Mandarin fǒuzé, Cantonese fau2 zak1), glossed "otherwise; or else". The Japanese ひそく and Korean 부칙 fields are built from the readings of the two characters.
