## Notes
- [尽](../characters/尽.md) "exhaust; use up" + [力 (char)](../characters/力%20(char).md) "strength; effort" — no stand-in relationship applies (尽's own `stand_in` is [[用尽]], 力's is itself).

尽力 means to exert all one's strength or effort toward a task — "exhausting one's strength," a common polite/formal expression of commitment (よろしくお願いします contexts, business correspondence) across the CJKV sphere.

Japanese じんりょく, Korean 진력, and Vietnamese tận lực are all standard, non-drifted readings. No HSK level is given for the compound. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄋㄌㄧㄎ unique across both words and characters).
