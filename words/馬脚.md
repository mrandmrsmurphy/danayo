## Notes
- [馬 (char)](../characters/馬%20(char).md) "horse" + [脚 (char)](../characters/脚%20(char).md) "leg" — no `#cranberry`: both 馬's and 脚's own `stand_in` point to themselves; neither points here.

馬脚 (mǎjiǎo, literally "horse's leg/hoof") is used idiomatically for "cloven hoof" and, figuratively, for a telltale flaw that gives someone away (as in 露出馬脚, "to reveal one's true colors/give the game away"). Cantonese maa5goek3, Japanese ばきゃく, Korean 마각, and Sino-Vietnamese mã cước are all standard, fully compositional readings.
