## Notes
- [巻 (char)](../characters/巻%20(char).md) "roll; roll up" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [巻 (char)](../characters/巻%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

巻 means to roll or roll up, and by extension names a roll or volume — a scroll of text (a "chapter" or "book" in classical bibliography, still surviving as a volume-counter), or a rolled foodstuff (as in Japanese 海苔巻き, sushi rolls).

Japanese かん, Korean 권, and Vietnamese quyển are all standard readings. **A genuine triple Dan'a'yo homophone**: 巻, [[圏]], and [[絹]] all share the identical reading (gwen/권/ㄍ⼔ㄋ) — the reciprocal callout was already correctly in place on both of the other two pages, just missing here.

**Correction (2026-08-27)**: `korean` had been stored as the literal string "null" instead of an actual value or a proper omission — corrected to 권.
