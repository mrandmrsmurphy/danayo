## Notes
- [上 (char)](../characters/上%20(char).md) "above, up" + [司](../characters/司.md) "manage; office" — boss.

上司 is a boss or direct superior: the person above one in a workplace hierarchy, to whom one reports. It is "above-officer." Mandarin shàngsī (Mandarin synonyms 上级 and 主管), Cantonese soeng6 si1 and Japanese じょうし are confirmed; Korean 상사 comes from memory. It names a person in a working relationship, where [[上位]] names an abstract rank and [[部長]] a particular post. The opposite, a subordinate, is not yet coined. 司 is a bound character, and [[公司]] already legitimizes it.
