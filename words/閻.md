## Notes
- [閻 (char)](../characters/閻%20(char).md) "village gate" — the bare character used directly as a word, its own `stand_in`.

閻 means the gate of a lane or village quarter: the entrance that closed off a residential ward, hence by extension the ward itself (閻里, "neighborhood"). It is best known through 閻羅 (Yama, the lord of the underworld, from Sanskrit Yama via 閻魔/閻羅王), and as a surname (閻, Yan). The simplified form 阎 is listed as an alias. The graph is a 形声 compound of 門 "gate" with the phonetic 臽, which the vault records as its classification.

Mandarin yán, Cantonese jim4, Japanese えん (閻魔/えんま, "Yama, king of hell"), Korean 염 (염라, "Yama"), and Vietnamese diêm (Diêm Vương, "King Yama") are the standard Sino-xenic readings. The character page lists the native Japanese reading ちまた ("street, quarter") and the native Korean 마을 ("village").
