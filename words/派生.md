## Notes
- [派](../characters/派.md) "branch" + [生](../characters/生.md) "life" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 派 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (生's own stand-in is [[生活]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

派生 means to derive.

Mandarin pàishēng, Cantonese paai3 sang1, Japanese はせい, and Korean 파생 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 派's and 生's own stored citations exactly). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with phái sinh (派's own citation phái + 生's own citation sinh) — also the real, standard Vietnamese term used in mathematics and finance for "derivative."
