## Notes
- [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "multiply" + [数](../characters/数.md) "number" — literally "multiplying number," the multiplier.

In the expression *a* × *b*, 乗数 is the multiplier *b* (as distinct from 被乗数, the multiplicand *a*). Mandarin chéng shù, Cantonese sing4 sou2, Japanese じょうすう, and Korean 승수 are all standard, fully compositional mathematical terms. Vietnamese thừa số is the direct, real Vietnamese math term (乗's thừa + 数's số) — used generically for any factor in a multiplication, without Vietnamese distinguishing multiplier from multiplicand the way 乗数/被乗数 do.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 숭수 diverges from Sino-Korean 승수, since 乗's own Dan'a'yo reading 숭 doesn't match Sino-Korean 승, even though 数's own reading matches exactly (数 is independently `kwin: true`).
