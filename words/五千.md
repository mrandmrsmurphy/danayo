## Notes
- [五 (char)](../characters/五%20(char).md) "five" + [千](../characters/千.md) "thousand"

五千 is five thousand (5,000), a plain compositional numeral, 五 × 1,000. Vietnamese ngũ thiên is the compositional Hán Việt reading; everyday Vietnamese counts natively instead (from memory, not re-checked), as on [七千](七千.md). Used in the Dan'a'yo numeral pieces such as the year in the 1919 Korean Declaration of Independence ([宣言書](../translation/宣言書.md)); there are no words for full numbers, only for these pieces.
