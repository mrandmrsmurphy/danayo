## Notes
- [時 (char)](../characters/時%20(char).md) "when" + [間](../characters/間.md) "between, among" — literally "the space/interval of time." Neither character's own `stand_in` points here (時's own points to bare `時`, 間's own to `之間`); 時間 is an independent compound.

Mandarin shíjiān, Cantonese si4 gaan1, Japanese じかん, Korean 시간, and Vietnamese thời gian all mean "time" as a general concept. 小時 and 鐘頭 ("an hour," a duration unit) are different words, not spellings of 時間.
