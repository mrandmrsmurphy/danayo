## Notes
- [主](../characters/主.md) "main" + [頁 (char)](../characters/頁%20(char).md) "page" — literally "main page," a compositionally straightforward IT term.

主頁 (zhǔyè)/主页 is the standard Chinese and Cantonese (zyu2 jip6) term for a website's homepage, common in both mainland and Cantonese-speaking IT usage; 首頁 is its equally common synonym/near-alias (added here as an `aliases` entry). Japanese, Korean, and Vietnamese, however, don't build this concept compositionally from Sino roots the way Chinese does — each instead uses its own real, standard everyday term: Japanese ホームページ (hōmupēji, an English loanword, universal for "homepage"), Korean 홈페이지 (also the English loanword, equally universal), and Vietnamese trang chủ (a native/Sino-Vietnamese hybrid literally "host page," the standard everyday term — distinct from Trang Chính, the specific proper-noun title of, e.g., Wikipedia's own main page). All three were filled here as real attested usage rather than forced compositional Sino-readings, since no such compositional reading is actually used in any of those three languages for this concept.
