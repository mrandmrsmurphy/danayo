## Notes
- [当](../characters/当%20(char).md) "while" + [世](../characters/世.md) "world, society" + [紀](../characters/紀.md) "era, chronicle" — no stand-in relationship applies (当's own stand-in is itself; 世's own is [[世界]]; 紀's own is [[世紀]]).

当世紀 means "that century" or the current century.

Mandarin dāngshìjì, Cantonese dong1 sai3 gei2, Japanese とうせいき, Korean 당세기, and Vietnamese đương thế kỷ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese`, fixed missing "(char)" suffix on `characters`. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄫㄙㄝㄍㄧ unique across both words and characters).
