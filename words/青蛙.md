## Notes
- [青 (char)](../characters/青%20(char).md) "blue" + [蛙](../characters/蛙.md) "frog" — 青蛙 is 蛙's own legitimizing compound (蛙's `stand_in` points here), but since 青's own `stand_in` is 青 itself, transitivity fails and no `#cranberry` tag applies.

Mandarin 青蛙 (qīngwā) and Cantonese 青蛙 (cing1waa1) are the standard, generic words for "frog" in general, with no narrower connotation. Japanese あおがえる (aogaeru) and Korean 청와/청개구리 (cheongwa/cheong-gaeguri), by contrast, refer specifically to green tree frogs (Hylidae) rather than frogs in general — the generic words are instead the native かえる (kaeru) and 개구리 (gaeguri) respectively, which is why 개구리 (not 청와) is given here to match the headword's broader "frog" sense.

Vietnamese ếch is a native word for "frog" in general (as in ếch xanh, "green frog," built the same way as 青蛙 but with the modifier order reflecting native Vietnamese syntax); no independently attested Sino-Vietnamese compound corresponding to 青蛙 exists.
