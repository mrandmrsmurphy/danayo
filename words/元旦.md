## Notes
- [元](../characters/元.md) "foundational, first" + [旦](../characters/旦.md) "daybreak" — literally "first daybreak"; this word is the stand-in for [旦](../characters/旦.md), which cannot appear independently.

1. New Year's Day (Gregorian calendar, January 1st)
2. Precisely the morning of January 1st
	1. Even more exacting, the sunrise on January 1st

Mandarin Yuándàn, Cantonese jyun4 daan3, Japanese がんたん (gantan), Korean 원단, and Vietnamese nguyên đán are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 元's and 旦's own stored citations exactly — Vietnamese nguyên đán is itself the source of Tết Nguyên Đán, the Vietnamese term for Lunar New Year, though this word's own sense here is specifically the Gregorian New Year.
