## Notes
- [券 (char)](../characters/券%20(char).md) "bond; certificate"

券 depicts a tally or contract document (related to 巻, "scroll") — originally a split tally, one half kept by each party to a contract as proof of agreement. It means "a certificate, ticket, bond, or voucher": 債券 (zhàiquàn, "bond, debenture"), 証券 (zhèngquàn, "securities, negotiable instrument"), and everyday 入場券 ("admission ticket").

Mandarin quàn, Cantonese hyun3, and Japanese けん (ken, on'yomi, fully productive: 商品券 しょうひんけん "gift voucher") all preserve the sense. Korean 권 (gwon) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 문서 (munseo, "document," a descriptive gloss rather than a direct translation). Vietnamese khoán is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in trái khoán (債券, "bond") and khế khoán ("a contract, deed").
