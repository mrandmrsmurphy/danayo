## Notes
- [滋](../characters/滋.md) "thrive" + [賀](../characters/賀.md) "congratulate"

滋賀 is Shiga, written "increase, congratulate". It takes its name from the old Shiga district (志賀郡), later rewritten with the more auspicious 滋賀; the reading しが is the on-reading of 滋 (し) plus 賀 (が).

Shiga surrounds Lake Biwa, the largest lake in Japan, and its capital is Ōtsu. It holds Hikone Castle, the Tendai temple complex on Mount Hiei, the ninja villages of Kōka, and Azuchi, where Oda Nobunaga built his castle.

In Dan'a'yo 滋賀 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 시가 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
