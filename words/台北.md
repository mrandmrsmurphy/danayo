## Notes
- [台](../characters/台%20(char).md) "pedestal, platform" + [北](../characters/北.md) "north" — no stand-in relationship applies (台 is its own stand-in; 北's own is [[北方]]).

台北 (Taipei) is the capital of Taiwan.

Japanese たいぺい and Vietnamese Đài Bắc are both standard, fully compositional readings. Korean uses the modern phonetic loan 타이베이 (from the English/Mandarin exonym "Taipei") rather than the older, more formal Hanja-based reading 대북 — both forms are attested, with 타이베이 the current standard. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄧㄅㄨㄎ unique across both words and characters).
