## Notes
- [立 (char)](../characters/立%20(char).md) "stand" + [方](../characters/方.md) "direction" + [体](../characters/体.md) "group, body, form" — no stand-in relationship applies (立's own stand-in is itself; 方's own is [[方向]]; 体's own is [[体系]], so this word legitimizes none of them).

立方体 means cube — literally "standing-square body," the solid geometric shape with six equal square faces.

Mandarin lìfāngtǐ and Japanese りっぽうたい (rippōtai) are both the real, standard, everyday geometric terms for "cube" in their languages. Korean real usage more often reaches for 정육면체 ("correct six-face body") in everyday math instruction, though 립방체 remains a real, understood Sino-Korean math term for the same shape. Vietnamese lập phương is itself a real, standard Vietnamese word (used independently for "cubed," as in a number raised to the third power), but the standard Vietnamese word for the physical solid shape pairs it with hình or khối instead of thể — hình lập phương or khối lập phương, rather than this word's literal lập phương thể. The 立方 pair is also the mathematical word for "cube" as an operation (立方根, "cube root"; 立方メートル, "cubic meter"), with 体 turning it into the solid.  Korean 립방체 is the North Korean form (립 keeps its initial ㄹ).  The character file of 体 lists the Vietnamese readings thấy and thẩy, not thể, so thể is taken from the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading of the word 体.  The 注音 needs no syllable dot.  `kwin` is false per the AND-rule: 方 and 体 are both individually `kwin: false`, regardless of 立's own `kwin: true`.
