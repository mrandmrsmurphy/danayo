## Notes
- [立 (char)](../characters/立%20(char).md) "stand" + [方](../characters/方.md) "direction" + [体](../characters/体.md) "group, body, form" — no stand-in relationship applies (立's own stand-in is itself; 方's own is [[方向]]; 体's own is [[体系]], so this word legitimizes none of them).

Wiktionary: Chinese 立方體 "cube (polyhedron having six identical square faces)"; Japanese 立方体 (りっぽうたい), geometry, "cube". The traditional spelling 立方體 is the Chinese entry; Korean and Vietnamese are not separately listed.
