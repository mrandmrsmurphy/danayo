**Fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字` had dunggip instead of the correct dunggib (級's own reading is gib/깁, confirmed against `syllables/ㄍㄧㄆ.md`; `諺文`/`注音` were already correct).

等級 names a grade, level, rank, or class — a position within a graded scale or hierarchy, whether academic, social, or qualitative.

Mandarin děngjí, Cantonese dang2 kap1, Japanese とうきゅう (tōkyū), and Korean 등급 (deunggeup) are all standard, fully compositional readings, each the ordinary everyday word for "grade/rank" in its language. Vietnamese đẳng cấp is likewise compositional and is itself a common Vietnamese term for "class, status, rank."
