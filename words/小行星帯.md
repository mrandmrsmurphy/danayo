## Notes
- [小 (char)](../characters/小%20(char).md) "small" + [行 (char)](../characters/行%20(char).md) "travel, go" + [星 (char)](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" + [帯 (char)](../characters/帯%20(char).md) "belt, zone" — the region between the orbits of Mars and Jupiter containing most of the solar system's asteroids

The asteroid belt (小行星帯) encircles the sun between Mars and Jupiter, containing millions of rocky bodies — the asteroids — that never coalesced into a planet. The individual bodies are called [[小行星]]. The discovery of the dwarf planet [[䦧神星]] (Eris) beyond Neptune in 2005 prompted the reclassification of Pluto as a [[矮行星]], demonstrating that belt-like zones of debris exist at multiple distances in the solar system.

Japanese does not use 小行星帯 in everyday astronomy; its word is 小惑星帯 (しょうわくせいたい), with 惑星 "planet" in place of 行星. The frontmatter holds the bound Sino-Japanese reading of this word's own characters. In Dan'a'yo 小行星帯 is the asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter, with no divergence from the shared sense.
