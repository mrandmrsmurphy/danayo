## Notes
- [校](../characters/校.md) "school" + [歌](../characters/歌.md) "song" — no stand-in relationship applies (校's own stand-in is [[学校]]; 歌's own is [[歌曲]]).

校歌 means school anthem or school song.

Mandarin xiàogē, Cantonese haau6 go1, Japanese こうか, and Korean 교가 are all standard, fully compositional readings.
