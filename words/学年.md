## Notes
- [学](../characters/学.md) "learn" + [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year"

学年 is a school year, or a grade level: the year of study a student is in. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xuénián as "academic year; school year". Japanese がくねん, Korean 학년 and Cantonese hok6 nin4 match.

It is divided into [学期](学期.md), terms. The name of a level, such as first-year, is built by counting 学年.
