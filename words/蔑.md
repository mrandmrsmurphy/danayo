## Notes
- [蔑](characters/蔑%20(char).md) "scorn" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 蔑 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

蔑 means to scorn, despise (as in 侮蔑, 軽蔑).

Mandarin miè, Cantonese mit6, Korean 멸, and Vietnamese miệt (the standard reading among 蔑's long multi-value citation list) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 蔑's own stored citations exactly.
