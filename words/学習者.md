## Notes
- [学習](学習.md) "study, learn" + [者](../characters/者.md) "-er, person who" (自 stand-in; 者 is self-standing) — a learner, one who is studying.

学習者 is a learner. English Wiktionary glosses Japanese 学習者 (がくしゅうしゃ) and Chinese 學習者 (Mandarin xuéxízhě), both marked education, as "learner; student". The Cantonese, Korean 학습자 and Vietnamese fields are built from the readings of the characters. It is [学習](学習.md), to study, with the suffix －者 added. Compare [学生](学生.md) and [生徒](生徒.md), students.
