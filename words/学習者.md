## Notes
- [学習](学習.md) "study, learn" + [者](../characters/者.md) "-er, person who" (自 stand-in; 者 is self-standing) — a learner, one who is studying.

学習者 means a learner — someone in the process of learning, distinct from [[学生]]/[[生徒]] (a student formally enrolled in school). Japanese がくしゅうしゃ and Mandarin xuéxízhě/Korean 학습자 are all real, standard terms, especially common in language-teaching contexts (e.g. 日本語学習者, "learner of Japanese"). Cantonese hok6 zaap6 ze2 and Vietnamese học tập giả are straightforward compositional readings, not independently verified as standard terms in those languages. `kwin` is false: inherited from 者 (false), despite 学習 itself being true.

No syllable-boundary dot is needed: the concatenated string `ㄏㄚㄎㄙㄜㄆㄑㄚ` admits only one legal partition.
