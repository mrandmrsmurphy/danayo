## Notes
- [朱](../characters/朱.md) "cinnabar, vermilion" + [沙](../characters/沙%20(char).md) "sand" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [沙](../characters/沙%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry, and (per 朱's own Words section) is also the stand-in for 朱, despite 朱's frontmatter `stand_in` field reading "朱砂" rather than "朱沙" — a minor character-level inconsistency (朱's own Notes documents 朱沙 as the real stand-in with 朱砂 as its alias) flagged for a future character-perfecting pass, not fixed here.

朱沙 means cinnabar.

Mandarin zhūshā, Cantonese zyu1 saa1, Japanese しゅしゃ, Korean 주사, and Vietnamese chu sa are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄨㄙㄚ unique across both words and characters).
