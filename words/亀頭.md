## Notes
- [亀 (char)](../characters/亀%20(char).md) "turtle" + [頭 (char)](../characters/頭%20(char).md) "head" — literally "turtle head," the glans penis, so named for the visual resemblance.

亀頭 is the standard anatomical/clinical term across the Sinosphere: Mandarin guītóu, Cantonese gwai1 tau4, Japanese きとう, Korean 귀두, and Vietnamese quy đầu are all fully compositional and mutually transparent, each language's clinical vocabulary using the same "turtle head" metaphor directly.
