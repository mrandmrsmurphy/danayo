## Notes
- [土 (char)](../characters/土%20(char).md) "earth, soil" + [曜](../characters/曜.md) "day of the week" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — literally "earth-day," Saturday, following the Sino-Japanese seven-day planetary-week naming shared across the sphere.

Japanese どようび (doyōbi) and Korean 토요일 are both standard, fully compositional readings, matching 土's, 曜's, and 日's own stored citations exactly. Mandarin xīngqīliù and Cantonese sing1 kei4 luk6 correctly hold the real 星期 ("week") + number system (星期六, already recorded in this word's own `aliases`) rather than a compositional reading of 土曜日's own characters, the same pattern seen throughout this vault's coined calendar vocabulary. Vietnamese thứ bảy ("day seven") is likewise the real native word, using Vietnamese's own weekday-numbering convention.

`kwin` is false: 土 alone is kwin-true, but 曜 independently diverges from Korean on its own character page, which alone is enough to make the AND-rule false. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄛ·⼄ㄨㄋㄧㄊ unique among both words and characters).
