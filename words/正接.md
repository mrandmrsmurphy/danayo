## Notes
- [正](../characters/正%20(char).md) "correct" + [接](../characters/接%20(char).md) "contact, connect" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 正 and 接's own stand-ins are themselves).

正接 means tangent (trigonometry).

Mandarin zhèngqiē, Cantonese zing3 zip3, Japanese せいせつ, Korean 정접, and Vietnamese chính tiếp are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 正's and 接's own stored citations exactly). Fixed real bugs: `korean` was 탄젠트, an English loanword transliteration, not the compositional Sino-Korean form (matching the parallel pattern used on [[正弦]]'s 정현); `vietnamese` was tang, a French/English-derived loanword, likewise corrected to the compositional form. Filled the blank `cantonese` field. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄫㄐㄛㄆ unique across both words and characters).
