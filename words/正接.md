## Notes
- [正](../characters/正%20(char).md) "correct" + [接](../characters/接%20(char).md) "contact, connect" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 正 and 接's own stand-ins are themselves).

正接 means tangent (trigonometry).

Mandarin zhèngqiē, Cantonese zing3 zip3, Japanese せいせつ, Korean 정접, and Vietnamese chính tiếp are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 正's and 接's own stored citations exactly).
