## Notes
- [体](../characters/体.md) "body" + [液](../characters/液.md) "fluid" — bodily fluid. Attested across Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, and Korean.

**Correction to a prior iteration's finding**: this page previously stated "no Vietnamese attestation found" — a fresh web search this iteration found `thể dịch` is in fact well attested, most visibly in the standard medical/immunology term miễn dịch thể dịch ("humoral immunity," antibody-mediated immunity circulating in bodily fluids). Added.
