## Notes
- [印](../characters/印.md) "seal" + [度](../characters/度.md) "degree" — no stand-in relationship applies (印's own stand-in is [[封印]]; 度's is [[程度]]).

印度 names India — a phonetic-plus-conventional transcription of the country name, historically derived from Sanskrit *Sindhu* via various transliterations.

Japanese インド is a katakana phonetic loan (standard for modern foreign place names) rather than a Sino-Japanese on'yomi reading. Korean 인도 and Vietnamese Ấn Độ are standard readings, matching Dan'a'yo's own reading exactly (`kwin: true`). No homophones (注音 ㄧㄋㄉㄛ unique across both words and characters).
