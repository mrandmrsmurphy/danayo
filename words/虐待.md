## Notes
- [虐](../characters/虐.md) "cruel" + [待](../characters/待.md) "treat"

虐待 is abuse: to treat a person or an animal cruelly. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin nüèdài as "to abuse; to maltreat; to be cruel to" and Japanese ぎゃくたい as "abuse; maltreatment". Vietnamese ngược đãi is the same word, and Korean 학대 and Cantonese joek6 doi6 match.

It is the cruelty of a stronger party toward a weaker one, in the home or in custody, and in the extreme it is the [虐殺](虐殺.md). The tyranny of a ruler is [暴虐](暴虐.md).
