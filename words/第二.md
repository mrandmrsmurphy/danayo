## Notes
- [第 (char)](../characters/第%20(char).md) "-th" + [二 (char)](../characters/二%20(char).md) "two" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both 第's and 二's own are themselves), so `#cranberry` does not apply.

第二 means "second," the ordinal formed by prefixing 第 to 二, and appears widely in formal titles: 第二次世界大戦 ("World War II"), 第二言語 ("second language").

Mandarin dì'èr, Cantonese dai6 ji6, Japanese だいに (daini), and Korean 제이 (jei) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese đệ nhị is likewise compositional and standard in formal register.
