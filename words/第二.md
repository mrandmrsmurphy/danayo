## Notes
- [第 (char)](../characters/第%20(char).md) "-th" + [二 (char)](../characters/二%20(char).md) "two" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both 第's and 二's own are themselves), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

**Fixed a real bug**: `vietnamese` had thứ nhì, a native Vietnamese ordinal (thứ, native ordinal marker + nhì, native "two") rather than this compound's own compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading — corrected to đệ nhị (第's own đệ + 二's own nhị), itself a real, common Vietnamese term for "second."

第二 means "second," the ordinal formed by prefixing 第 to 二, and appears widely in formal titles: 第二次世界大戦 ("World War II"), 第二言語 ("second language").

Mandarin dì'èr, Cantonese dai6 ji6, Japanese だいに (daini), and Korean 제이 (jei) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese đệ nhị is likewise compositional and standard in formal register.
