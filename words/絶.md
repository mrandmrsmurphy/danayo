## Notes
- [絶](../characters/絶%20(char).md) "discontinue, break off" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 絶 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

絶 means to discontinue, sever, or break off, as in 絶対 (absolute) and 絶後 (never happen again).

Mandarin jué, Cantonese zyut6, and Korean 절 (jeol) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading た (ta, from 絶える "taeru," to cease) rather than the on-reading ゼツ (ZETSU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese tuyệt is the standard, common Sino-Vietnamese reading (as in tuyệt đối, "absolute"), one of several stored candidates. A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone with [[綴]] ("bind, stop") — coincidental, unrelated in meaning.
