## Notes
- [篇](../characters/篇%20(char).md) "article, piece of writing" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 篇 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo. Its 形声 phonetic component 扁 links it to the homophone family shared with [[偏]] and [[騙]].

篇 names a written article, chapter, or piece of writing — historically counted as a unit for organizing bamboo-strip texts (its own semantic component 竹, "bamboo," reflects the ancient writing medium), and still used today as a measure word for essays, chapters, and articles (一篇文章, "one article/essay").

Mandarin piān, Cantonese pin1, and Korean 편 (pyeon, as in 단편, "short piece/story") are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the on-reading へん (hen), since the character stores no native kun-reading of its own (`japanese_native: ø`). Vietnamese thiên is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading. This word is part of a genuine three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read pyen/편/ㄆ⼶ㄋ): [[偏]] ("biased, inclined") and [[騙]] ("deceive").
