## Notes
- [正](../characters/正%20(char).md) "correct" + [弦](../characters/弦%20(char).md) "bowstring, chord, hypotenuse" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 正 and 弦's own stand-ins are themselves, each with its own independent word page).

正弦 means sine (trigonometry).

Mandarin zhèngxián, Cantonese zing3 jin4, Japanese せいげん, and Korean 정현 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 正's and 弦's own stored citations exactly). Simplified `cantonese`/`japanese`/`korean`, each of which had held a malformed doubled value pairing the real compositional form with an unrelated English-loanword alternate (jyun4 variant; サイン "sain," from English "sine"; 사인, likewise); Vietnamese sin is itself a real English loanword, kept as the field's sole value since Vietnamese lacks a native compositional term in common use. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄫㄏㄝㄋ unique across both words and characters).
