## Notes
- [崖 (char)](../characters/崖%20(char).md) "cliff" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [崖 (char)](../characters/崖%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

崖 names a cliff or precipice — a steep drop-off, often used figuratively for the edge of a dangerous situation (懸崖 "sheer cliff/precipice," often metaphorical for the brink of disaster).

Japanese がい (on'yomi; the native がけ, gake, is the everyday word instead), Korean 애, and Vietnamese nhai (the character's own field also lists day, a Nôm variant) are all standard readings. **A genuine triple Dan'a'yo homophone**: 崖, [[唉]], and [[愛]] all share the identical reading (ai/애/ㄚㄧ) — the reciprocal callout is already correctly in place on all three pages.
