## Notes
- [捺](../characters/捺%20(char).md) "right-down stroke" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [捺](../characters/捺%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

捺 means the right-down diagonal stroke in Chinese character calligraphy (㇏).

Mandarin nà, Cantonese naat6, Japanese なつ, Korean 날, and Vietnamese nại are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄚㄊ unique across both words and characters).
