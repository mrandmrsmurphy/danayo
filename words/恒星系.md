## Notes
- [恒](../characters/恒.md) "constant, ubiquitous" + [星](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" + [系](../characters/系.md) "system" — no stand-in relationship applies (恒's own stand-in is [[恒久]]; 星/系 are both their own stand-ins).

恒星系 means galaxy — literally "star system," a gravitationally bound collection of stars.

Mandarin héngxīngxì and Cantonese hang4 sing1 hai6 are standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 항성계 is likewise compositional (used in more technical/astronomical contexts). Japanese ぎんが (銀河, "silver river") and Vietnamese thiên hà (天河, "heaven's river") are each language's own native-equivalent term for galaxy, drawing on the Milky Way's imagery rather than a compositional reading of these specific characters — an expected native-equivalent substitution, not an error. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄨㄫㄙㄝㄫㄏㄝㄧ unique across both words and characters).
