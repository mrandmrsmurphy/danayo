## Notes
- [依 (char)](../characters/依%20(char).md) "to rely on" + [存](../characters/存.md) "to exist" — dependence, reliance, or to depend on something. Forms compounds such as 相互依存 ("mutual interdependence," using [[相互]]; no dedicated vault page of its own yet) and 依存症 ("addiction, dependency syndrome"). Directly attested and standard across mandarin yīcún, cantonese ji1 cyun4, japanese いぞん, and korean 의존. `vietnamese: y tồn` is the honest compositional reading (依's own y + 存's own tồn); the more common everyday Vietnamese word for this concept is the native phụ thuộc, kept here as the vault's own compositional derivation rather than a claim of independent attestation. `kwin` is true: both 依 and 存 independently match their own Sino-Korean readings. No homophones (注音 ㄧㄜㄐㄛㄋ unique among both words and characters).
