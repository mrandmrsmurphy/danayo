## Notes
- [社](../characters/社.md) "company" + [会 (char)](../characters/会%20(char).md) "meet, gather" + [科](../characters/科.md) "branch, section" — literally "society" (社会) + "subject/branch of study" (科).

社会科 is the standard term for social studies, the primary/secondary school subject covering history, geography, civics, and society — narrower in scope than the academic discipline [[社会学]] (sociology), which it feeds into at higher levels of education.

Mandarin shèhuì kē, Cantonese se5 wui2 fo1 (会 taking the same colloquial changed tone as in [[社会学]]/[[社会主義]]), Japanese しゃかいか (shakaika), and Korean 사회과 (sahoegwa) are all fully compositional. Vietnamese khoa xã hội reorders the elements to fit native syntax ("branch [of] society" rather than a literal "society-branch"), still landing on the same meaning.
