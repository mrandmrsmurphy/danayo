## Notes
- [短 (char)](../characters/短%20(char).md) "short" + [期 (char)](../characters/期%20(char).md) "period, term" — covering or lasting only a brief span of time. Attested across Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, Korean, and Vietnamese (đoản kỳ).

短期 is the standard word for "short-term," used constantly in financial, contractual, and planning contexts: 短期記憶 ("short-term memory"), 短期借款 ("short-term loan"), 短期目標 ("short-term goal") — contrasted with 長期 ("long-term"). Mandarin duǎnqī, Cantonese dyun2 kei4, Japanese たんき (tanki), Korean 단기 (dangi), and Vietnamese đoản kỳ are all fully compositional and share this same everyday, unmarked register across the sphere.
