## Notes
- [披](../characters/披%20(char).md) "expose" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [披](../characters/披%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

披 means to expose, uncover, or reveal.

Mandarin pī, Cantonese pei1, Japanese ひ, Korean 피, and Vietnamese phi are all standard, fully compositional readings. Simplified `vietnamese` from a raw six-way dictionary dump (phơ, bờ, pha, phê, phi, phơi) to phi, the reading attested in real compounds like 披露 (phi lộ, "disclose"). No homophones (注音 ㄆㄨㄧ unique across both words and characters).
