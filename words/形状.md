## Notes
- [形](../characters/形%20(char).md) "shape, form" + [状](../characters/状.md) "condition, shape" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [状](../characters/状.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (形's own stand-in is itself).

形状 means shape — the physical outline or geometry of an object, as distinguished from [[形]]'s more abstract sense (see [[形]]'s Usage note).

Mandarin xíngzhuàng, Cantonese jing4 zong6, Japanese けいじょう, Korean 형상, and Vietnamese hình trạng are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄝㄫㄐ⺢ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
