## Notes
- [瘡](../characters/瘡.md) "sore, ulcer" + [口 (char)](../characters/口%20(char).md) "mouth, opening"
- 瘡口 is the stand-in for [瘡](../characters/瘡.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.

瘡口 names the open part of a sore or wound, the place where the skin is broken. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin chuāngkǒu (疮口, 瘡口) as "open part of a sore", and Cantonese cong1 hau2 matches. Japanese そうこう and Korean 창구 are the Sino-Xenic readings of the two characters.

In Dan'a'yo the word is placed on the [Body](../lexipedia/Body.md) page. It shares its exact pronunciation with [窓口](窓口.md) "window", an unrelated word, which is why both carry the homophone callout.
