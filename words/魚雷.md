## Notes
- [魚](characters/魚%20(char).md) "fish" + [雷](characters/雷%20(char).md) "thunder" — no `#cranberry`: both 魚's and 雷's own `stand_in` point to themselves; neither points here.

Mandarin yúléi, Cantonese jyu4leoi4, Japanese ぎょらい, Korean 어뢰, and Sino-Vietnamese ngư lôi are all standard, fully compositional readings — "fish thunder," a vivid calque describing the torpedo's explosive underwater strike.
