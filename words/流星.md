## Notes
- [流](characters/流.md) "flowing" + [星 (char)](characters/星%20(char).md) "star"

流星 means "shooting star, meteor" — literally "a flowing/streaking star."

**Content corrected**: `kwin` was `false`; both [[流]] (char) and [[星]] (char) are individually kwin, so the AND-rule was actually satisfied — corrected to `true`.

Mandarin liúxīng and Cantonese lau4 sing1 preserve the sense directly. Japanese りゅうせい (ryūsei) is standard vocabulary (流星群, "meteor shower"). Korean 유성 (yuseong) parallels the Sino-xenic reading and is likewise standard, especially in astronomical contexts (유성우, "meteor shower"). Vietnamese lưu tinh is attested as a Sino-Vietnamese synonym of the native sao băng ("shooting star," literally "star that streaks/flies fast"), but carries a more formal, literary/scientific register — everyday Vietnamese speech prefers sao băng.
