## Notes
- [撞](../characters/撞.md) "hit, bump" + [球](../characters/球%20(char).md) "sphere, ball" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 撞 and 球's own stand-ins are themselves).

撞球 means billiards.

Mandarin zhuàngqiú, Cantonese zong6 kau4, Japanese どうきゅう, Korean 당구, and Vietnamese tràng cầu are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese also commonly uses the international loanword ビリヤード (biriyādo) for this game, alongside the compositional どうきゅう used here. No homophones (注音 ㄊ⺢ㄫㄍ⼜ unique across both words and characters).
