## Notes
- [支](../characters/支.md) "branch" + [付](../characters/付.md) "pay" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [付](../characters/付.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (支's own stand-in is [[支部]]).

支付 means to pay.

Mandarin zhīfù, Cantonese zi1 fu6, Japanese しふ, Korean 지부, and Vietnamese chi phó are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄈㄨ unique across both words and characters).
