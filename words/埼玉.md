## Notes
- [埼](../characters/埼.md) "peninsula" + [玉](../characters/玉%20(char).md) "jade"

埼玉 is Saitama, written "cape jade". The name comes from the old Sakitama district (埼玉郡), and the reading さいたま is a worn form of that older さきたま; the character 埼, read さき, is a cape or promontory. The characters are applied to a native name and not meant to be read for sense.

Saitama lies directly north of Tokyo and is largely a commuter region for it. Its capital, the city of Saitama, was created in 2001 by merging Urawa, Ōmiya and Yono. It is known for the old merchant town of Kawagoe, called "little Edo", and for the Chichibu mountains.

In Dan'a'yo 埼玉 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 사이타마 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
