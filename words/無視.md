## Notes
- [無](../characters/無%20(char).md) "not, without" + [視](../characters/視%20(char).md) "see, look" — "not see," to ignore. Their own `stand_in` values: 無's is itself; 視's is itself; none points here, so 無視 is an independent compound.

無視 is to ignore: to pay no attention to something, to disregard it. Mandarin wúshì, Cantonese mou4 si6, Japanese むし and Korean 무시 all use it. It is the opposite of noticing, with 視 "see" and 無 "not." Compare [[軽視]] (to belittle) and [[観察]] (to observe).
