## Notes
- [蒸](../characters/蒸%20(char).md) "steam, vapor" + [発](../characters/発%20(char).md) "emit, send out" — "vapor out," evaporation. Their own `stand_in` values: 蒸's is itself; 発's is itself; none points here, so 蒸発 is an independent compound.

蒸発 is evaporation: a liquid turning into vapor. Mandarin zhēngfā (蒸發) and Cantonese zing1 faat3 are the ordinary words, and Japanese じょうはつ and Korean 증발 are the same compound; in Japanese it also means to vanish without a trace. Compare [[沸騰]] (to boil) and [[液体]] (liquid).
