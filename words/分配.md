## Etymology
- [分 (char)](../characters/分%20(char).md) "to divide" + [配 (char)](../characters/配%20(char).md) "to match, pair, allocate"

## Notes
"To distribute, allocate, partition" (resources, tasks, goods). `vietnamese: phân phối` is one of the most common everyday Vietnamese words for this concept (phân phối hàng hóa, "goods distribution"), a direct match. Filled the blank `cantonese: fan1 pui3`, compositional. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 분푀 diverges from Sino-Korean 분배 in the second syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄨㄋㄆㄛㄧ unique among both words and characters).
