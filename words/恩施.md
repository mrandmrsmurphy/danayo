## Notes
- [恩](../characters/恩.md) "kindness; mercy" + [施](../characters/施.md) "to bestow; to apply; to carry out" — the active, verbal dimension of grace; the act of conferring blessing or favor upon another

Wiktionary lists 恩施 only under Chinese: literary, "to confer a favour; to give grace" and "kindness; affection; grace; favor"; also the Enshi Tujia and Miao Autonomous Prefecture and the county-level city Enshi, in Hubei. In Dan'a'yo it is the verb of the 恩- words, the act of grace being given, as distinct from [[恩典]], [[恩寵]] and [[恩意]]; the Hubei place name is a different word.
