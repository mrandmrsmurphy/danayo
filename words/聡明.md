## Notes
- [聡](../characters/聡.md) "clever, sharp of hearing" + [明 (char)](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright, clear" — 聡's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 明's own is [[明]] itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 聡明 legitimizes 聡 alone. `kwin: true` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: true`.

聡明 means smart or clever.

Mandarin cōngmíng, Cantonese cung1 ming4, Japanese そうめい (sōmei), and Korean 총명 (chongmyeong) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 聡明 is itself a common attested word for "smart, intelligent" in all four languages. Vietnamese thông minh is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
