## Notes
- [笑 (char)](../characters/笑%20(char).md) "laugh; smile" + [顔](../characters/顔.md) "face"

笑顔 is a smiling face: the face as it looks when one smiles, a happy expression. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xiàoyán as "smiling face" and Japanese えがお as "smile, happy face, smiling face". Cantonese siu3 ngaan4 matches, and Korean 소안 is the reading of the characters.

It is the look of [微笑](微笑.md), smiling, and a sign of [欣喜](欣喜.md), joy. The shameless face is the [厚顔](厚顔.md).
