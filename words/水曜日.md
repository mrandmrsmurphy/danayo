## Notes
- [水 (char)](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [曜](../characters/曜.md) "day of the week" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — literally "water-day," Wednesday, following the Sino-Japanese seven-day planetary-week naming shared across the sphere.

Japanese すいようび (suiyōbi) and Korean 수요일 are both standard, fully compositional readings, matching 水's, 曜's, and 日's own stored citations exactly. Mandarin xīngqīsān/xīngqísān (a well-attested tonal variant on 期) and Cantonese sing1 kei4 saam1 instead use the real, everyday 星期 ("week") + number system (星期三, "week-day three," already recorded in this word's own `aliases`) rather than the planetary-element naming — the same pattern seen throughout this vault's coined calendar vocabulary. Vietnamese thứ tư ("day four") is likewise the real native word, using Vietnamese's own weekday-numbering convention.

`kwin` is false: 水 alone is kwin-true, but 曜 independently diverges from Korean on its own character page, which alone is enough to make the AND-rule false. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨ·⼄ㄨㄋㄧㄊ unique among both words and characters).
