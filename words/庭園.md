## Notes
- [庭](../characters/庭.md) "courtyard" + [園](../characters/園.md) "garden" — this word is the stand-in for [園](../characters/園.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

庭園 names a cultivated garden — often specifically an ornamental, landscaped garden (Japanese traditional gardens, 日本庭園, being a well-known example) rather than a plain courtyard.

Japanese ていえん, Korean 정원, and Vietnamese đình viên are all standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄝㄫ·ㄛㄋ unique across both words and characters).
