## Notes
- [苦 (char)](../characters/苦%20(char).md) "bitter" + [味](../characters/味.md) "taste, flavor" — bitter taste; bitterness as a flavor quality

Wiktionary: Chinese "bitter taste"; Japanese 苦味 (にがみ) "bitter taste"; Korean 고미 is the hanja form. 味's own `stand_in` is 味覚, so this word is not its legitimizer.
