## Notes
- [香](../characters/香.md) "fragrant, aroma" + [蕉](../characters/蕉.md) "banana, plantain" — no `#cranberry`: 香's own `stand_in` is [[香気]], 蕉's is [[甘蕉]]; neither points here.

香蕉 (xiāngjiāo) is the standard, everyday Mandarin word for "banana," and Cantonese 香蕉 (hoeng1ziu1) likewise — a native Chinese term with no felt need for a loanword. Japanese and Korean, by contrast, both borrowed English "banana" directly (バナナ, 바나나) rather than adopting the Sino-compound. Vietnamese similarly reaches for its own native word chuối in everyday speech; the compositional Sino-Vietnamese hương tiêu exists but is not the common term.
