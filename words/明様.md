## Notes
- [明](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright" + [様](../characters/様%20(char).md) "-ly (adverbializer)" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 明 and 様's own stand-ins are themselves).

明様 means brightly, or in a bright way — using Dan'a'yo's manner-adverbializer suffix 様 (see [[様]]).

Mandarin míngyàng, Cantonese ming4 joeng4, Japanese めいよう, Korean 명양, and Vietnamese minh dạng are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: every field had been filled with native descriptive phrases instead of the compositional adverbializer form — Mandarin míngliàng de ("bright" + possessive particle), Japanese あかるく (native adjective inflection), Korean 밝게/똑똑히 (native adverbs), and Vietnamese trong sáng ("clear, pure") — all corrected to the compositional 明+様 reading. No homophones (注音 ㄇ⼶ㄫ⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
