## Notes
- [命](../characters/命.md) "life, fate, decree" + [令](../characters/令%20(char).md) "order" — no stand-in relationship applies (命's own stand-in is [[運命]]; 令 is its own stand-in).

命令 means a decree, order, or command.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `諺文`/`注音` had been contaminated with [[明朗]]'s reading (명랑/ㄌㄚㄫ, "bright, cheerful" — an entirely unrelated word) instead of 命令's own compositional reading, derived from 令's own citation (렁/ㄌㄝㄫ) — corrected to 명렁/ㄇ⼶ㄫㄌㄝㄫ. This also removes the false homophone claim (a broken `[[]]` link) that was itself just a symptom of the bug — once corrected, 命令 and 明朗 are not actually homophones in Dan'a'yo. Mandarin mìnglìng, Cantonese ming6 ling6, Japanese めいれい, Korean 명령, and Vietnamese mệnh lệnh are all standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄇ⼶ㄫㄌㄝㄫ unique across both words and characters).
