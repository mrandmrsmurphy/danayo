## Notes
- [殉](../characters/殉.md) "die for, follow in death" + [教](../characters/教.md) "religion" — "die for the religion," martyrdom. Their own `stand_in` values: 殉's is [[殉難]]; 教's is [[教授]]; none points here, so 殉教 is an independent compound.

殉教 is martyrdom: to die for one's religion. Mandarin xùnjiào, Cantonese seon1 gaau3, Japanese じゅんきょう and Korean 순교 all use it. It is dying for the faith, where [[殉難]] is dying for a just cause. Compare [[信奉]] (to believe in) and [[聖人]] (saint).
