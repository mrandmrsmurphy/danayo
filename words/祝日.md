## Notes
- [祝](../characters/祝.md) "celebrate, bless" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day"

祝日 is specifically the official Japanese legal term for a public/national holiday, as defined by the 国民の祝日に関する法律 ("Act on National Holidays") — 元日, 成人の日, 天皇誕生日, and similar designated dates. It contrasts with [[休日]] (a day off work more generally, which includes weekends and other non-working days beyond the legally designated 祝日) and [[記念日]] (a commemorative day that need not be an official non-working holiday).

Korean 축일 is a standard compositional Sino-Korean reading. **`vietnamese` intentionally left blank**: no attested Sino-Vietnamese compound for this specific Japanese legal-administrative term could be confirmed; Vietnamese uses the native/general ngày lễ ("holiday, festival day") instead.
