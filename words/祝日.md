## Notes
- [祝](../characters/祝.md) "celebrate, bless" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day"

祝日 is a national holiday. English Wiktionary has an entry only for Japanese 祝日 (しゅくじつ), glossed "national holiday". In Japan the 国民の祝日 were established by the 1948 Act on public holidays, the 国民の祝日に関する法律. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Korean 축일 fields are built from the readings of the two characters. A day off in general is the [休日](休日.md), and a commemorative day is the [記念日](記念日.md).
