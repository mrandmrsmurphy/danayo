## Notes
- [相](../characters/相.md) "mutual" + [生](../characters/生.md) "life, generate" — no stand-in relationship applies (相's own stand-in is [[相互]]; 生's own is [[生活]]).

相生 names the generative half of the [[五行]] (Wu Xing) cycle: 木 (Wood) feeds 火 (Fire), 火 leaves behind 土 (Earth, as ash), 土 bears 金 (Metal, as ore), 金 collects 水 (Water, as condensation on cold metal), and 水 nourishes 木 (Wood) again, closing the loop.

Mandarin xiāngshēng, Cantonese soeng1 sang1, Japanese そうしょう (sōshō — distinct from the unrelated placename/surname reading あいおい of the same characters), Korean 상생 (sangsaeng — now also a common modern word for "win-win, mutual benefit," extended from this cosmological sense), and Vietnamese tương sinh are all real, standard readings of this exact philosophical term. `kwin` is false per the AND-rule: 相 is individually `kwin: true`, but 生 is individually `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄚㄫㄙㄚㄫ unique among both words and characters).
