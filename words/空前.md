## Notes
- [空 (char)](../characters/空%20(char).md) "empty" + [前 (char)](../characters/前%20(char).md) "front, before, preceding" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both 空's and 前's own are themselves), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 空 is individually `kwin: false`.

空前 means unprecedented — literally "empty before," i.e. nothing of the kind has come before this — describing a first-of-its-kind event or achievement. It commonly pairs with its mirror opposite in the chengyu [[空前絶後]] ("unprecedented and unrepeatable; without parallel before or after").

Mandarin kōngqián, Cantonese hung1 cin4, Japanese くうぜん (kūzen), and Korean 공전 (gongjeon) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese không tiền is likewise compositional, seen in không tiền khoáng hậu ("unprecedented," a four-character idiom paralleling 空前絶後).
