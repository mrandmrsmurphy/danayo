## Notes
- [霊](../characters/霊.md) "spirit" + [感](../characters/感.md) "feeling"

霊感 is inspiration: the sudden coming of an idea to an artist or writer. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin línggǎn as "inspiration" and Japanese れいかん as "ability to sense the presence of spirits". Korean 령감 is the same word. Cantonese ling4 gam2 matches.

It starts the [創作](創作.md), creative work, and it is helped by [想像](想像.md), imagination.
