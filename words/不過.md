## Notes
- [不 (char)](../characters/不%20(char).md) "not" + [過 (char)](../characters/過%20(char).md) "to pass, to exceed" — here 不 functions as an emphatic particle rather than a plain negator, giving 不過 its dual life as both a limiting adverb ("only, merely," restricting an extent) and a conjunction ("but, however"), the same two-sense spread this word's own frontmatter English gloss and Mandarin usage both reflect.

Vietnamese bất quá (confirmed via web search) preserves exactly this same dual sense — as a limiting particle ("chỉ là, chẳng qua," only/merely) and as a conjunction ("nhưng," but/however) — a clean match rather than a partial one. **No `japanese` field could be filled compositionally**: 不過 has no independent life as a borrowed Sino-Japanese word covering either sense; Japanese instead reaches for entirely native, unrelated vocabulary depending on which sense is meant — でも/しかし ("but, however") for the conjunctive use, or ～に過ぎない ("is nothing more than...") for the limiting-adverb use — so no single `japanese` value could represent this word accurately, and none is given rather than picking one sense arbitrarily. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 볻과 diverges from Sino-Korean 불과 in both syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄛㄊㄍ⺢ unique among both words and characters).
