## Notes
- [伯](../characters/伯.md) "father's older brother" + [父](../characters/父.md) "father" (honorific extension) — "paternal uncle, older." Both `japanese: おじ` (a valid jukujikun reading alongside the more formal はくふ, matching the native reading already chosen for [[伯母]]'s おば) and `vietnamese: bá phụ` are directly attested (confirmed via web search) — bá phụ specifically distinguishes the father's *older* brother from thúc phụ (叔父, the younger brother). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 박부 diverges from Sino-Korean 백부 in the first syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄎㄅㄨ unique among both words and characters).
