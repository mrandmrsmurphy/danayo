## Notes
- [聖](../characters/聖.md) "holy, sacred" + [霊](../characters/霊.md) "spirit" — "holy spirit." Their own `stand_in` values: 聖's is [[神聖]]; 霊's is [[霊魂]]; none points here, so 聖霊 is an independent compound.

聖霊 is the Holy Spirit, the third person of the [[三位一体]]. Mandarin shènglíng (聖靈), Cantonese sing3 ling4, Japanese せいれい and Korean 성령 all use it, and Vietnamese thánh linh does too. Compare [[神聖]] (holy), [[神霊]] (spirit) and [[霊魂]] (soul).
