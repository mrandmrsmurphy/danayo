## Notes
- [懐 (char)](../characters/懐%20(char).md) "miss, think of" + [孕](../characters/孕.md) "pregnant" — 懐孕 is the stand-in word for [孕](../characters/孕.md); 孕 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse.

懐孕 means to be pregnant. English Wiktionary lists it only for Chinese, as 懷孕 (an alias, with 怀孕; Mandarin huáiyùn, Cantonese waai4 jan6), glossed "to be pregnant", with the examples 她懷孕了, "She's pregnant", and 計劃外懷孕, "unplanned pregnancy"; it also records a slang, jocular use for a sound that is pleasing to the ears. It has no Japanese entry, so Japanese かいよう here is built from the characters. The real Japanese word is 懐妊 (かいにん), "pregnancy, conception; to become pregnant, to conceive", and the real Korean is 회임, "gestation; pregnancy", written 懷妊. The Korean field gives the reading of the characters, 회잉, and the Vietnamese field hoài dựng is built from the characters.

Compare [[妊娠]], pregnancy, and the stand-in role of the word: 懐孕 is the stand-in word for [孕](../characters/孕.md), which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo.
