## Notes
- [懐 (char)](../characters/懐%20(char).md) "miss, think of" + [孕](../characters/孕.md) "pregnant" — 懐孕 is the stand-in word for [孕](../characters/孕.md); 孕 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse.

懐孕 is the everyday Mandarin verb for "to be pregnant" (她懐孕了, "she's pregnant"), the ordinary spoken-register counterpart to the more clinical/formal [[妊娠]]. 懐 here draws on its core "to carry within, to hold in one's heart/body" sense (also seen in 懐抱, "to cherish, embrace"), rather than its more common figurative "to miss, long for" sense (懐愁, "wistful longing").

Japanese かいよう is a direct on'yomi compositional reading (懐 KAI + 孕 YOU), though modern Japanese typically uses 妊娠 (ninshin) instead in this exact sense. Korean 회잉 is likewise compositional. Vietnamese hoài dựng is attested as a formal/literary Sino-Vietnamese term, though everyday spoken Vietnamese prefers the native mang thai or có thai. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 홰잉 diverges from Sino-Korean 회잉 in 懐's syllable (홰 vs 회). No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⺢ㄧ·ㄧㄫ unique across both words and characters).
