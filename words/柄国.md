## Notes
- [柄](../characters/柄%20(char).md) "handle, design" + [国](../characters/国.md) "nation" — no stand-in relationship applies (柄's own stand-in is itself; 国's own is [[国家]]).

柄国 means to rule (a literary expression, "to hold the handle of the state").

Mandarin bǐngguó, Cantonese beng3 gwok3, Japanese へいこく, Korean 병국, and Vietnamese bính quốc are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 柄's and 国's own stored citations exactly). Fixed real bugs: `mandarin` had an extra unsupported tone variant (bìngguó) and `cantonese` had the wrong initial vowel (bing3 → beng3, matching 柄's own citation); filled the blank `japanese`/`korean`/`vietnamese` fields; removed `秉國` from `aliases` (a different, unrelated character 秉 "to hold," a near-synonym rather than an orthographic variant), kept the genuine traditional variant `柄國`. No homophones (注音 ㄅ⼶ㄫㄍㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
