## Notes
- [電](../characters/電.md) "electricity" + [荷](../characters/荷.md) "load, burden" — electric charge.

電荷 is electric charge: the property of matter that makes it attract or repel other charged matter, positive on a proton ([[陽子]]) and negative on an electron ([[電子]]). The two characters mean "electricity" and "load, burden": charge as the load that a particle carries. An atom that has gained or lost electrons carries a net charge, which is how an ion is described. Mandarin diànhè, Cantonese din6 ho6, Japanese でんか and Korean 전하 are the standard readings.
