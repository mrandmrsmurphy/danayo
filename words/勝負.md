## Notes
- [勝](../characters/勝.md) "win" + [負 (char)](../characters/負%20(char).md) "lose"

勝負 is a contest, and its outcome: victory or defeat. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin shèngfù as "victory or defeat" and Japanese しょうぶ as "a competition, a match; victory or defeat; to compete". Korean 승부 and Cantonese sing3 fu6 match.

It names the win and the loss together, as a matter still to be decided, where [勝利](勝利.md) is the win alone. A face-to-face one is a [対決](対決.md).
