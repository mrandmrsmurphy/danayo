## Notes
- [映 (char)](../characters/映%20(char).md) "reflect; project" + [画](../characters/画.md) "picture" + [館](../characters/館.md) "hall"

映画館 is a cinema: a building where films are shown. Wiktionary glosses Japanese えいがかん as "cinema, movie theater". Korean 영화관 is the reading of the characters. The Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the characters.

It shows the [映画](映画.md), and the one for live plays is the [劇場](劇場.md).
