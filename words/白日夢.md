## Notes
- [白 (char)](../characters/白%20(char).md) "white" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day, sun" + [夢 (char)](../characters/夢%20(char).md) "dream" — literally "white-day dream," a dream had in broad daylight rather than at night.

白日夢 is the standard word for daydream across the Sinosphere: an idle, wandering fantasy indulged in while awake, as opposed to 夢 proper (a dream during sleep) or 夢嫌 (nightmare). Mandarin báirìmèng, Cantonese baak6 jat6 mung6, Japanese はくじつむ (hakujitsumu), Korean 백일몽 (baegilmong), and Vietnamese bạch nhật mộng are all fully compositional and semantically stable, with no drift across the sphere.
