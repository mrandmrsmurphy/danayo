## Notes
- [廉 (char)](../characters/廉%20(char).md) "upright, honest"

廉 means upright, honest, and incorrupt — especially the specific virtue of resisting bribery and refusing improper gain, the core sense behind 廉潔 (liánjié, honest and clean) and 清廉 (qīnglián, incorruptible). A second, related sense is "cheap, low-priced" (as in 廉價/廉価, lián jià, "low price, cheap"), which developed from the idea of a fair or modest price — one that does not take advantage of the buyer, an extension of the same core "not grasping/not greedy" semantic thread as the moral sense.

Japanese れん (ren) carries both senses in compounds: 廉潔 (renketsu, integrity) alongside 廉価 (renka, low price). Korean 렴 (ryeom, North Korean 문화어 reading — South Korean shifts this word-initial ㄹ to 염 by 두음법칙) appears in 청렴 (cheongnyeom, incorruptible) and 염가 (yeomga, low price, itself already showing the South Korean shift). Vietnamese liêm is the Hán Việt reading, used in liêm khiết (integrity, honesty) and thanh liêm (incorruptible) — the "cheap" sense is far less productive in Vietnamese, which prefers native rẻ for everyday low prices.
