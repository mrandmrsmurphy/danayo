## Notes
- [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big" + [学](../characters/学.md) "learn" + [院](../characters/院.md) "institution"

大学院 is a graduate school: the part of a university that teaches degrees beyond the bachelor's. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin dàxuéyuàn, in contexts of Japan or South Korea, as "graduate school (US); postgraduate school (UK)". Japanese だいがくいん and Korean 대학원 match.

It is entered from a [大学](大学.md) after the [学士](学士.md), and it confers the [碩士](碩士.md) and [博士](博士.md).
