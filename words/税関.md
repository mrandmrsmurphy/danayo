## Notes
- [税](../characters/税.md) "tax" + [関](../characters/関%20(char).md) "checkpoint, gate" — "tax checkpoint," customs. Their own `stand_in` values: 税's is [[税金]]; 関's is itself; none points here, so 税関 is an independent compound.

税関 is customs: the checkpoint where duty is collected on goods that cross a border. Mandarin shuìguān (稅關, now historical, with 海關 the modern word), Cantonese seoi3 gwaan1, Japanese ぜいかん and Korean 세관 all use it, and Vietnamese thuế quan does too. Compare [[税金]] (tax).
