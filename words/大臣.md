## Notes
- [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big, great" + [臣](../characters/臣.md) "minister, subject" — "high official, minister" (also the modern Japanese word for a cabinet minister, 外務大臣 "Foreign Minister"). `vietnamese: đại thần` is a directly attested, well-known standard Vietnamese term (đại thần triều đình, "a minister of the royal court"). Fixed `characters` to use the `大 (char)` filename form. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 대신 matches Sino-Korean 대신 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄧㄙㄧㄋ unique among both words and characters).
