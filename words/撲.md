## Notes
- [撲](../characters/撲%20(char).md) "rush at" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [撲](../characters/撲%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

撲 means to rush at (as in 撲滅, "eradicate," and 相撲, "sumo wrestling").

Mandarin pū, Cantonese pok3, Japanese ぼく, Korean 박, and Vietnamese phốc are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
