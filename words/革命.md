## Notes
- [革](../characters/革.md) "leather" + [命](../characters/命.md) "life, fate" — no `#cranberry`: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here (革's is [[皮革]], 命's is [[運命]]). Literally "to strip away the mandate (of Heaven)," the classical Chinese term for a change of dynasty, extended to "revolution."

革命 (gémìng) is the standard word for "revolution" in Mandarin, and Cantonese 革命 (gaak3ming6) likewise. Japanese かくめい (kakumei) and Korean 혁명 (hyeongmyeong) are both the standard terms in their languages as well.

Vietnamese has two real, attested forms: cách mạng, the modern standard term (as in Cách mạng Tháng Tám, "the August Revolution"), and the older, more literal cách mệnh, using mệnh (the more classical Sino-Vietnamese reading of 命) rather than the nativized mạng.
