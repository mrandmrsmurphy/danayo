## Notes
- [羅](../characters/羅.md) "Rome" + [先](../characters/先.md) "prior" — the name of Rason, a special city of North Korea at the northeastern tip of the country.

羅先 (Rason) is a city and special economic zone at the far northeast of North Korea, by the borders with China and Russia; the name is a contraction of the two former towns 羅津 (Najin) and 先鋒 (Sonbong) (from memory, not re-checked). English Wiktionary lists it with the status of a special city (特別市).

English Wiktionary has 羅先 as a Chinese proper noun (Mandarin Luóxiān, Cantonese lo4 sin1) and has a Korean entry as a hanja form of 라선; Chinese Wiktionary has no entry. Korean 라선 is the North Korean spelling; South Korean spelling applies the word-initial rule and gives 나선 (from memory, not re-checked). Japanese Wiktionary has no entry, so Japanese らせん is composed from the on'yomi (the same sound as the ordinary Japanese word らせん 螺旋 "spiral", by coincidence). Vietnamese La Tiên is composed from the character pages; no Vietnamese dictionary entry was found.
