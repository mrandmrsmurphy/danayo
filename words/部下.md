## Notes
- [部 (char)](../characters/部%20(char).md) "part, department" + [下 (char)](../characters/下%20(char).md) "below, under" — subordinate.

部下 is a subordinate: a person under another's command or supervision in an organization. It is "under the department," the counterpart of [[上司]] (boss) and built on the same 部 as [[部長]] (department head). Japanese ぶか, Korean 부하 and Vietnamese bộ hạ are the standard readings; Mandarin bùxià is tagged military on Wiktionary ("troops under one's command; subordinate") and has the synonyms 下屬 (xiàshǔ) and 部屬, which are not used here. `cantonese` is left blank because the reading was not confirmed.
