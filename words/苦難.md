## Notes
- [苦 (char)](../characters/苦%20(char).md) "bitter, suffering" + [難](../characters/難.md) "difficulty, disaster" — hardship; tribulation; severe suffering

Wiktionary: Chinese 苦難 "hardship; suffering; misery; distress" and "hard; miserable; distressing"; Japanese 苦難 (くなん) "hardship; suffering"; the simplified spelling 苦难 is kept as an alias. 難's own `stand_in` is 困難, so this word is not its legitimizer. Compare [[苦悩]].
