## Notes
- [吹 (char)](../characters/吹%20(char).md) "blow" + [奏](../characters/奏.md) "play (music)"

吹奏 is to play a wind instrument by blowing. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin chuīzòu as "to blow; to play a wind instrument" and Japanese すいそう as "playing wind instruments". Korean 취주 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese ceoi1 zau3 matches.

It is done on a [笛](笛.md), flute, a [喇叭](喇叭.md), horn, or a [鳳笙](鳳笙.md), mouth organ, as against the plain [演奏](演奏.md), to play.
