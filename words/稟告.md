## Notes
- [稟](../characters/稟.md) "report; petition" + [告](../characters/告.md) "inform" — 稟's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 告's own is [[告訴]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 稟 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word (already correctly noted). `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

稟告 means to report or petition to a superior — a deferential, formal act of informing someone of higher rank or authority, distinct from the more neutral [[報告]] ("report" generally) by its built-in sense of hierarchy, inherited from 稟's original meaning of receiving grain bestowed from above.

Mandarin bǐnggào, Cantonese ban2 gou3, Japanese りんこく (rinkoku), and Korean 품고 (pumgo) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese bẩm cáo is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form; bẩm alone survives in classical/period Vietnamese as a deferential verb for addressing a superior (bẩm quan, "to report to the mandarin"), matching 稟's own sense closely.
