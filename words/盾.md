## Notes
- [盾](../characters/盾%20(char).md) "shield" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 盾 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

盾 names the shield, a defensive weapon carried to block blows or projectiles. It survives most vividly today in the idiom 矛盾 (Mandarin máodùn, "spear and shield," i.e. contradiction) — from the classical anecdote of a merchant who claimed his spear could pierce anything and his shield could block anything, an obvious logical contradiction that gave the compound its enduring figurative meaning across the Sinosphere (Vietnamese mâu thuẫn, likewise, means "contradiction").

Mandarin dùn, Cantonese teon5, Japanese じゅん (jun), Korean 순 (sun), and Vietnamese thuẫn are all standard, fully compositional readings.
