## Notes
- [接](../characters/接%20(char).md) "contact, connect" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [接](../characters/接%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

接 means to contact or connect.

Mandarin jiē, Cantonese zip3, Japanese せつ, Korean 접, and Vietnamese tiếp are all standard, fully compositional readings.
