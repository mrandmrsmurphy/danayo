## Notes
- [宣](../characters/宣.md) "proclaim" + [教](../characters/教.md) "teach, religion" + [師](../characters/師.md) "master, teacher" — "mission teacher," a missionary. Their own `stand_in` values: 宣's is [[宣言]]; 教's is [[教授]]; 師's is [[教師]]; none points here, so 宣教師 is an independent compound.

宣教師 is a missionary: a person sent to spread a religion. Mandarin xuānjiàoshī, Cantonese syun1 gaau3 si1 and Japanese せんきょうし are attested, and Korean 선교사 is the same compound. It is built on [[宣教]] with the suffix-like 師 "master." Compare [[使徒]] (apostle) and [[牧師]] (pastor).
