## Notes
- [密 (char)](../characters/密%20(char).md) "secret; close" + [輸](../characters/輸.md) "transport"

密輸 is smuggling: to carry goods across a border in secret, so as to avoid the law or the duty. Wiktionary glosses Japanese みつゆ as "smuggling; to smuggle" and lists Korean 밀수 as "smuggling". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry, since Mandarin says 走私, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. The word is literally "secret transport".

The offence is against the customs rules, not against a person. It pairs with [輸送](輸送.md), transport in the lawful sense, and it is linked to [越境](越境.md), which names crossing a border in general.
