## Notes
- [順](../characters/順.md) "in order, smooth" + [次](../characters/次.md) "order" — 順次 is 順's own legitimizing compound (順's `stand_in` points here), but since 次's own `stand_in` is [[次第]] instead, transitivity fails and no `#cranberry` tag applies.

順次 (shùncì) is a real Mandarin word meaning "in sequence, successively," and Cantonese 順次 (seon6ci3) likewise. Japanese じゅんじ (junji) and Korean 순차 (suncha, as in 순차적 "sequential") are both standard terms in their languages. Sino-Vietnamese thuận thứ is compositionally straightforward, but does not appear to be an independently attested fixed compound the way the other four languages' forms are — modern Vietnamese instead uses the native lần lượt ("one by one, in turn") or tuần tự ("sequentially, in process order").
