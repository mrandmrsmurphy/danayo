## Notes
- [激](../characters/激.md) "intense, stimulate" + [光](../characters/光%20(char).md) "light" — "stimulated light." Their own `stand_in` values: 激's is [[刺激]]; 光's is itself; none points here, so 激光 is an independent compound.

激光 is a laser. Mandarin jīguāng and Cantonese gik1 gwong1 are the ordinary Chinese words in Mainland China and Hong Kong (Taiwan writes 雷射 and some Hong Kong usage 鐳射, from the sound of "laser"). Japanese says レーザー and Korean 레이저, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. The characters give a descriptive name, "stimulated light," from the process of stimulated emission. Compare [[光子]] (photon) and [[光]] (light).
