## Notes
- [吸](../characters/吸%20(char).md) "suck, absorb" + [引](../characters/引%20(char).md) "pull, draw" — "suck and pull," to attract. Their own `stand_in` values: 吸's is itself; 引's is itself; none points here, so 吸引 is an independent compound.

吸引 is to attract: to draw something toward oneself, as a magnet draws iron or a sight draws the eye. Mandarin xīyǐn, Cantonese kap1 jan5, Japanese きゅういん and Korean 흡인 all use it, and Vietnamese hấp dẫn does too. Compare [[引]] (to pull) and [[吸]] (to suck).
