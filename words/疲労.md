## Notes
- [疲](../characters/疲.md) "exhausted" + [労](../characters/労.md) "labour" — 疲's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 労's own is [[労動]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 疲 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

疲労 means to be fatigued, strain.

Mandarin píláo, Cantonese pei4 lou4, Japanese ひろう, Korean 피로 and Vietnamese bì lao (疲's own bì plus 労's own lao) are all fully compositional.
