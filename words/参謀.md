## Notes
- [参](../characters/参.md) "participate, attend" + [謀 (char)](../characters/謀%20(char).md) "plan, scheme, strategize" — staff officer; one who participates in strategic planning; a strategist or advisor

Wiktionary: Chinese 參謀 "staff officer (military rank); advisor; to give advice, to advise"; Japanese 参謀 (さんぼう), military, "a staff officer", and "advisor", and a piece in Ko shogi; Korean 참모 and Vietnamese tham mưu are the Han forms of "military staff".
