## Notes
- [実](../characters/実.md) "true; accurate" + [験 (char)](../characters/験%20(char).md) "check; examine"

実験 is an experiment: a test made to find out what happens, or to try out an idea. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin shíyàn as "experiment; experimentation; testing", and also "to experiment". Japanese じっけん, Korean 실험 and Cantonese sat6 jim6 match. It is shared across the sphere.

It tests a [仮説](仮説.md), a hypothesis, and what it shows is analysed ([分析](分析.md)) and written up as a [論文](論文.md). The word is literally "real test".
