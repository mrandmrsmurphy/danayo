## Notes
- [驚](../characters/驚.md) "startle; astonish" + [愕](../characters/愕.md) "astonished"

驚愕 is to be stunned: to be struck dumb by something sudden and unexpected, or the astonishment itself. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin jīng'è as "to be stunned; to be astonished; to be stupefied" and Japanese きょうがく as "astonishment; to be stunned, to be flabbergasted". Korean 경악, Vietnamese kinh ngạc and Cantonese ging1 ngok6 are the same word.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [愕](../characters/愕.md) as an independent entry; it reads like its relatives 鄂, 顎 and 鰐. It is stronger than the [驚異](驚異.md), wonder, and the plain [喫驚](喫驚.md), being startled, and it is the first reaction to a [衝撃](衝撃.md), shock.
