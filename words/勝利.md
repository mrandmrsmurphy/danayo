## Notes
- [勝](../characters/勝.md) "win, victory" + [利](../characters/利.md) "profit" — `characters/勝.md`'s own `stand_in` points to this word (勝利), meaning 勝 is a bound morpheme that never appears independently outside this compound.

Its part of speech is 事詞, matching its primary living use as a verb ("to win, to triumph"), with a secondary noun use ("victory").

Mandarin shènglì, Cantonese sing3 lei6, Japanese しょうり, Korean 승리, and Vietnamese thắng lợi are all standard, everyday words for "victory; to win."
