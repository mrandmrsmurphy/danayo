## Notes
- [沙 (char)](../characters/沙%20(char).md) "sand" + [糖 (char)](../characters/糖%20(char).md) "sugar"

沙糖 is sugar: the sweet crystals refined from sugar cane or beet, sold in grains like sand. The usual spelling is 砂糖, an alias, because 砂 is an alias of 沙. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin shātáng (砂糖) as "granulated sugar" and Japanese さとう as "sugar". Cantonese saa1 tong4 matches. Korean 사탕 is the same word, though in Korean it names a hard candy.

It is refined [糖](糖.md), sugar, in its granulated form, a sweetener beside [蜂蜜](蜂蜜.md), honey, and [麦芽糖](麦芽糖.md), maltose. The sweet taste is [甘味](甘味.md).
