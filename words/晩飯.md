## Notes
- [晩](../characters/晩%20(char).md) "evening" + [飯](../characters/飯.md) "rice, meal" — no stand-in relationship applies (晩's own stand-in is itself; 飯's own is [[米飯]]).

晩飯 means dinner, supper, or evening meal.

Mandarin wǎnfàn, Cantonese maan5 faan6, Japanese ばんめし, Korean 만반, and Vietnamese vãn phạn are all standard, fully compositional readings. Simplified `korean` from native paraphrase (저녁 식사, "evening meal") to compositional 만반. Removed `夕食` from `aliases`: a distinct Japanese-specific compound built on 夕 rather than 晩/飯, not an orthographic variant of this word. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄛㄋㄅㄛㄋ unique across both words and characters).
