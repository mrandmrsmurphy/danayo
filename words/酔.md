## Notes
- [酔](characters/酔%20(char).md) "drunk" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 酔 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

酔 means drunk.

Mandarin zuì, Cantonese zeoi3, Korean 취, and Vietnamese túy are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 酔's own stored citations exactly.

酔 shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading (ㄐㄨㄧ/jui/쥐) with [[知]] ("know") and [[池]] ("pond, reservoir") — the trio is also documented on [[知]]'s page.
