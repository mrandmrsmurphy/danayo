## Notes
- [筆 (char)](../characters/筆%20(char).md) "pen; brush" + [順](../characters/順.md) "in order; sequence"

**筆順** is the *stroke order* of a character: the sequence and direction in which its strokes ([[筆画]]) are written. Stroke order is taught as a small set of general rules, such as top to bottom, left to right, horizontal before vertical, left-falling before right-falling, outside before inside, the middle before the sides, filling before closing, and dots last; the rules are listed on the [[Stroke]] page.

The Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and North Korean forms are the ordinary compound readings (Japanese ひつじゅん, Korean 필순). The Japanese form is the usual school term for this concept. No Vietnamese form is given.
