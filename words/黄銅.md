## Notes
- [黄 (char)](../characters/黄%20(char).md) "yellow" + [銅 (char)](../characters/銅%20(char).md) "copper" — no `#cranberry`: both 黄's and 銅's own `stand_in` point to themselves; neither points here.

**Fixed a likely typo**: `japanese` was わうどう, the archaic historical kana spelling — corrected to おうどう, the modern standard form (matching 黄's already-corrected modern reading used on [[黄金]]).

Mandarin huángtóng, Cantonese wong4tung4, Korean 황동, and Sino-Vietnamese hoàng đồng are all standard, fully compositional readings; everyday Vietnamese also uses the more specific native phrase đồng thau.
