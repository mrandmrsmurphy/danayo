## Notes
- [主](../characters/主.md) "lord; master; main" + [体](../characters/体.md) "body"

主体 (traditional 主體) is "subject; principal agent; the main body." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 主體 (zhǔtǐ, Cantonese zyu2 tai2) is "the main part; the part making up most of something," in philosophy "subject," and in finance "entity"; Korean 주체 is "main agent, principal agent"; Vietnamese chủ thể is "subject"; and Japanese 主体 (しゅたい) is attested too. In the Chinese, Korean and Vietnamese entries the same word is also the name of Juche, the state ideology of North Korea, and of its calendar; Dan'a'yo does not take that sense, and the context decides. 主体性 is built with the affix [－性](－性.md) "nature of." It is used in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [平臥即是正義](../translation/平臥即是正義.md), in 人 主体性 "human subjectivity."
