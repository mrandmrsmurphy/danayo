## Notes
- [巧](../characters/巧.md) "clever; skillful" + [妙 (char)](../characters/妙%20(char).md) "wondrous; subtle" — this word is the stand-in for [巧](../characters/巧.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

巧妙 is a near-synonym doubling, both halves independently pointing at cleverness/skill — a mind or technique that is ingenious, deft, and cleverly conceived, whether admiringly (a brilliant solution) or with a hint of slyness (a clever ruse).

Japanese こうみょう, Korean 교묘, and Vietnamese xảo diệu are all standard, non-drifted readings. No homophones (注音 ㄎ⼘ㄨㄇ⼄ㄨ unique across both words and characters).
