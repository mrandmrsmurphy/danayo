## Notes
- [無 (char)](../characters/無%20(char).md) "lack" + [極 (char)](../characters/極%20(char).md) "extreme"

In Daoist cosmology, 無極 ("without ultimate" or "limitless") denotes the primordial undifferentiated state that precedes even [[太極]] (the "Great Ultimate"). It represents pure potentiality before any polarity or differentiation arises. See also [[混沌]].

Wiktionary lists two senses for Mandarin wújí: literary "boundless, limitless" (verb), and in Chinese philosophy wuji, the primordial universe or "ultimateless". Cantonese mou4 gik6 matches, and the Japanese むきょく, Korean 무극 and Vietnamese vô cực are recorded there as descendants of the same compound. The Vietnamese form is glossed "infinity". Wiktionary also notes a separate proper-noun use as the name of Wuji County in Hebei, and the word has passed into English as "wuji".

In Dan'a'yo the word is placed on the [Astronomy](../lexipedia/Astronomy.md) page for its cosmological sense, while the general meaning "limitless" remains available.
