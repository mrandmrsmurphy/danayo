## Notes
- [賠](../characters/賠.md) "indemnify" + [償](../characters/償.md) "repay"
- This word is the viable form of [賠](../characters/賠.md): the character's meaning (to indemnify, pay damages) is identical, but 賠 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 賠償 is its `stand_in` compound.

賠償 means to compensate or pay damages for a loss or harm caused: 賠 is "to indemnify, pay for" (a loss) and 償 is "to repay, make up for". Mandarin péicháng, Cantonese pui4 soeng4, Japanese ばいしょう (baishō; 損害賠償, "damages") and Korean 배상 (baesang; 손해배상) are the same compound, standard in civil law and in the phrase 戦争賠償 ("war reparations").

In Dan'a'yo it is compensation for a wrong or loss, distinct from [[償還]], the repaying of a debt or what is owed. It sits in the Trade domain with the money and payment vocabulary.
