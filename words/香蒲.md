## Notes
- [香](../characters/香.md) "incense" + [蒲](../characters/蒲.md) "bulrush" — legitimizing note: 蒲's own `stand_in` is [[香蒲]] itself, already annotated in its own citation; 香's own `stand_in` is [[香気]], so transitivity fails — no `#cranberry`.
- 蒲 alone is too bounded to stand as an independent Dan'a'yo word (the character's meaning, bulrush/cattail, is identical to the compound's), so 香蒲 serves as its legitimizing form.
