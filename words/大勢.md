## Notes
- [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big; great" + [勢](../characters/勢.md) "power; momentum"

大勢 is "the general trend," the overall course of events. According to Wiktionary, Chinese 大勢 (dàshì, Cantonese daai6 sai3) means "general trend," as in 大勢所趨 "the trend of the times"; and Japanese たいせい means "the general trend or situation, the current of the times," and also a major authority or power. The Japanese おおぜい "crowd" is a separate word that happens to be spelled the same, and Dan'a'yo does not take it. Wiktionary has no Korean or Vietnamese section for 大勢; `korean` 대세 and `vietnamese` đại thế are the compositional Sino-readings of the two characters, not independently checked.
