## Notes
- [加](../characters/加.md) "add" + [油](../characters/油%20(char).md) "oil, fuel" + [站](../characters/站.md) "station" — "add-fuel station." Their own `stand_in` values: 加's is [[加算]]; 油's is itself; 站's is [[兵站]]; none points here, so 加油站 is an independent compound.

加油站 is a gas station, a place to fill a vehicle with fuel. Mandarin jiāyóuzhàn and Cantonese gaa1 jau4 zaam6 are the ordinary Chinese words, from 加油 "add fuel" and 站 "station". Japanese says ガソリンスタンド and Korean 주유소, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[汽油]] (gasoline) and [[網站]] (website).
