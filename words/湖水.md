## Notes
- [湖](../characters/湖.md) "lake" + [水 (char)](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" — stand-in for [[湖]], which cannot appear independently.

Mandarin húshuǐ, Cantonese wu4 seoi2, Japanese こすい (kosui), and Korean 호수 (hosu) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 湖's and 水's own stored citations exactly — though in each language the plain, everyday word for "lake" is simply the bare first character alone (Mandarin 湖 hú, Japanese みずうみ mizuumi as the native alternative, Korean 호수 itself doubling as the everyday term), with 湖水 carrying a slightly more literary or emphatic "lake water" flavor. `kwin` is false per the AND-rule: 湖 is individually `false` (Dan'a'yo 홋 vs Sino-Korean 호 diverge in the coda) while 水 is individually `true`.
