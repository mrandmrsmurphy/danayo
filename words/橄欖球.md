## Notes
- [橄](../characters/橄.md) "olive" + [欖](../characters/欖.md) "olive" + [球](../characters/球%20(char).md) "sphere, ball" — no stand-in relationship applies here (both 橄 and 欖's own stand-ins point to [[橄欖]]; 球's own is itself).

橄欖球 literally means "olive ball," referring to rugby (the ball's shape).

Mandarin gǎnlǎnqiú is the genuine, standard compositional Chinese term. Japanese ラグビー and Korean 럭비 are English loanword transliterations — no compositional Sino-Japanese/Sino-Korean term for rugby is in real-world use, kept as-is (the same pattern seen on [[板球]], [[果醤]], [[楊樹]], [[樹懶]]). Vietnamese bóng bầu dục is the real, standard native descriptive term ("oval ball"), likewise kept as-is. Fixed a real bug: `cantonese` had a malformed hybrid tone notation and wrong initial (gam3 laam5-2 kau4 → gaam3 laam2 kau4, matching the simplified form used on [[橄欖]]). No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄇㄌㄚㄇㄍ⼜ unique across both words and characters).
