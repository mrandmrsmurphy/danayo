## Notes
Affectionate term for one's father's younger brother, or any familiar adult male younger than one's parents. 叔叔 (shúshu) is the everyday colloquial Mandarin reduplicated form for "uncle" (father's younger brother, or any friendly adult man of that generation), commonly used in direct address, paralleling [[伯伯]]'s own reduplication of 伯 for the father's *older* brother. Since this specific reduplicated form isn't natural usage in Japanese, Korean, or Vietnamese, each of those fields instead documents that language's own real everyday word for the relationship: `japanese: おじさん` and `korean: 아저씨` (both already present natively on [[叔]]'s own character page), and `vietnamese: chú` (native, extremely common — matching [[叔]]'s own thúc as the formal Sino-Vietnamese counterpart).
