affectionate term for one's father's younger brother, or any familiar adult male younger than one's parents

## Notes
叔叔 (shúshu) is the everyday colloquial Mandarin reduplicated form for "uncle" (father's younger brother, or any friendly adult man of that generation), commonly used in direct address, paralleling [[伯伯]]'s own reduplication of 伯 for the father's *older* brother. Since this specific reduplicated form isn't natural usage in Japanese, Korean, or Vietnamese, each of those fields instead documents that language's own real everyday word for the relationship: `japanese: おじさん` and `korean: 아저씨` (both already present natively on [[叔]]'s own character page), and `vietnamese: chú` (native, extremely common — matching [[叔]]'s own thúc as the formal Sino-Vietnamese counterpart).

**Real content bug found and fixed**: `japanese` had a fabricated non-reduplicated reading ("しゅくふ") and `kwin` was stored true despite `korean` ("숙숙") being an equally implausible bare reduplication — both corrected. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 숙숙 diverges entirely from the native-word-based Korean field. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨㄎㄙㄨㄎ unique among both words and characters).
