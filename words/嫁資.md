## Notes
- [嫁](../characters/嫁.md) "marry; go to a husband's family" + [資](../characters/資.md) "resources, goods" — dowry.

嫁資 is a dowry: the property, goods or money a woman brings with her to her husband's household at marriage. It is "marriage-goods." Japanese かし is confirmed on Wiktionary ("property a woman brings when she marries"), and Mandarin jiàzī (simplified 嫁资) is listed on zh.wiktionary with a Russian gloss "dowry" but no Chinese definition; a variant spelling 嫁貲 is noted. Korean and Vietnamese were not checked, so those fields are left blank. It runs the opposite way to [[聘金]] (the bride-price), which flows from the groom's side; the cost of the wedding itself is [[婚費]]. See also [[嫁娶]] (marriage as an exchange between households) and [[新婦]] (the bride).
