## Notes
- [行 (char)](../characters/行%20(char).md) "go, conduct" + [政](../characters/政.md) "government" — administration; the executive branch of government. Attested across Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese (ぎょうせい), Korean (행정), and Vietnamese (hành chính). Forms compounds such as 行政[[区域]] ("administrative region") and 行政官 ("administrator, official").
The Government page lists 行政 as "administration, government, the executive," and Geography uses it in 行政区域, the administrative regions. Dan'a'yo keeps the sense "administration, the executive," the carrying-out of government as opposed to the making of its laws. It is related to the existing words [政府](政府.md) "government," the body that administers, and [政治](政治.md) "politics," the stand-in for the character 政. The Korean 행정 differs from the Dan'a'yo 諺文 항징 in the first syllable's vowel and the second syllable's initial, so `kwin` is false. The readings are Mandarin xíngzhèng, Cantonese hang4 zing3, Japanese gyōsei (ぎょうせい) and Sino-Vietnamese hành chính. The contrast between executing and legislating is from memory and has not been checked against a source.
