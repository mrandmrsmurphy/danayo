## Notes
- [妖](../characters/妖.md) "goblin, monster" + [怪](../characters/怪.md) "strange, suspicious" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [妖](../characters/妖.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (怪's own stand-in is [[怪異]]).

妖怪 means devil, goblin, or monster — a supernatural being, matching the Japanese folkloric concept of yōkai.

Mandarin yāoguài, Cantonese jiu1 gwaai3, Korean 요괴, and Vietnamese yêu quái are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `japanese` had archaic pre-reform kana えうくわい — modernized to ようかい (yōkai). No homophones (注音 ⼘ㄨㄍ⺢ㄧ unique across both words and characters).
