## Notes
- [四 (char)](../characters/四%20(char).md) "four" + [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" — the four directional seas enclosing [[天下]]

Wiktionary: Chinese, literary and figurative, "the world; the whole world"; Japanese "the seas in the four cardinal directions" and "the world"; Vietnamese Tứ Hải is the Han form of tứ hải. The four seas enclose [[天下]]: [[東海]], [[西海]], [[南海]] and [[北海]].

The *Analects* (顏淵第十二, Wikisource) has 四海之內，皆兄弟也, "Within the four seas, all are brothers".
