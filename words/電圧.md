## Notes
- [電](../characters/電.md) "lightning, electric" + [圧](../characters/圧%20(char).md) "pressure" — "electric pressure," voltage. Their own `stand_in` values: 電's is [[電気]]; 圧's is itself; none points here, so 電圧 is an independent compound.

電圧 is voltage: the electric pressure that drives a current. Mandarin diànyā (電壓), Cantonese din6 aat3, Japanese でんあつ and Korean 전압 all use it, and Vietnamese điện áp does too. Compare [[電流]] (current) and [[電気]] (electricity).
