## Notes
- [幽 (char)](../characters/幽%20(char).md) "quiet; secluded; dark" + [鬼](../characters/鬼.md) "ghost; demon" — no stand-in relationship applies (幽's own `stand_in` is itself, 鬼's is [[鬼神]]).

幽鬼 names the spirit or soul of the deceased — a ghost or specter, an archaic/literary term used metaphorically (幽鬼の如き, "ghost-like") more often than as a plain everyday noun.

Japanese ゆうき, Korean 유귀, and Vietnamese u quỷ are all standard, if somewhat literary, readings. No homophones (注音 ⼜ㄛㄍㄨㄧ unique across both words and characters).
