## Notes
- [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day; sun" + [番 (char)](../characters/番%20(char).md) "turn, shift" — day shift.

日番 is the day shift: the turn of duty worked in daylight hours. It is "day-shift" from [[日]] and [[番]]. Japanese ひばん is confirmed on the Japanese Wiktionary as "a duty shift during the daytime; a guard assigned to daytime duty." Chinese and Korean are not confirmed; the regional words are Chinese 日班 or 白班, Japanese 日勤 and Korean 주간 (unverified here), so those fields are left blank. In Japanese 日番 and [[非番]] are both read ひばん; in Dan'a'yo their readings differ. The night counterpart is [[夜番]]. Like the other shift words, it belongs to the 番 family, a systematic schema that Dan'a'yo imposes for predictability rather than a claim about the ancient usage.
