## Notes
- [管](../characters/管.md) "pipe; tube; control" + [轄 (char)](../characters/轄%20(char).md) "control; linchpin of a wheel"

管轄 is jurisdiction: the authority of a court or office over a matter, a place or a person. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin guǎnxiá as "to have jurisdiction over; to administer; to exercise control over" and Japanese かんかつ as "jurisdiction; to have jurisdiction". Cantonese gun2 hat6 and Korean 관할 match. The word is shared across the sphere.

A [法庭](法庭.md) has 管轄 over the cases and places assigned to it, and the [警察](警察.md) over its district. The word also reaches into [Government](../lexipedia/Government.md), since every office has a jurisdiction.
