## Notes
- [美 (char)](../characters/美%20(char).md) "beautiful" + [味](../characters/味.md) "taste"

美味 is delicious: tasting very good, and a delicacy or fine dish. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin měiwèi as "delicious; delectable; tasty" and "delicacy; fine cuisine", and Japanese びみ as "delicious, tasty" and "delicious food, fine cuisine". Korean 미미, Vietnamese mỹ vị and Cantonese mei5 mei6 match.

It is the praise of taste, near [鮮美](鮮美.md), which is also delicious. The tastes themselves are [甘味](甘味.md) and [苦味](苦味.md).
