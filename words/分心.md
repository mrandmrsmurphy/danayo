## Notes
- [分](../characters/分%20(char).md) "divide, split" + [心](../characters/心%20(char).md) "heart, mind" — "divided mind," to be distracted. Their own `stand_in` values: 分's is itself; 心's is itself; none points here, so 分心 is an independent compound.

分心 is to be distracted: to have one's attention split, or to draw it away. Mandarin fēnxīn and Cantonese fan1 sam1 are the ordinary words. Japanese says 気が散る and Korean 산만해지다, so those two fields hold the character readings. Compare [[注意]] (attention) and [[無視]] (to ignore).
