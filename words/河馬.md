## Notes
- [河](../characters/河.md) "stream" + [馬 (char)](../characters/馬%20(char).md) "horse" — no stand-in relationship applies (河's own stand-in is [[小河]]; 馬's own is itself).

河馬 means hippopotamus.

Mandarin hémǎ, Cantonese ho4 maa5, Korean 하마, and Vietnamese hà mã are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 河's and 馬's own stored citations exactly), and also the real, standard everyday words for "hippopotamus" in each language. Japanese カバ is the real Japanese word (historically derived from this same Sino compound but now written as a katakana loanword-style native term), the same real-world-species-name pattern already established on [[龍蝦]]/[[水牛]]. `kwin: true` because 諺文 하마 matches Korean 하마 exactly. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄇㄚ unique across both words and characters).
