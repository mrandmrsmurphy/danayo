## Notes
- [陵](../characters/陵.md) "mausoleum, hill" + [墓](../characters/墓.md) "grave, graveyard, tomb" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes 陵 as a bound character (墓's own stand-in is [[墓穴]]; transitivity fails, so no `#cranberry`).

陵墓 means a mausoleum, especially a royal or imperial tomb of substantial architectural scale, distinct from an ordinary grave (墓穴/墳墓). Mandarin 陵墓 (língmù), Cantonese 陵墓 (ling4mou6), Japanese 陵墓 (りょうぼ, the official term for imperial tombs administered by Japan's Imperial Household Agency), and Sino-Vietnamese lăng mộ are all standard terms for royal/imperial tombs. Korean 릉묘 keeps this vault's standing North Korean/문화어 pronunciation policy (no 두음법칙 shift) — modern South Korean orthography would instead write 능묘 (neungmyo), shifting the word-initial ㄹ.
