## Notes
- [土 (char)](../characters/土%20(char).md) "earth"

土 means earth, ground, soil. Mandarin tǔ, Cantonese tou2, Korean 토, and Vietnamese thổ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese carries two on'yomi in complementary distribution: と (TO, used here, as in 土地 tochi "land") and ど (DO, as in 土曜日 doyōbi "Saturday," 土木 doboku "civil engineering") — while the native 訓読み つち (tsuchi) remains the everyday word for "dirt, soil" itself. Within this vault's own periodic-table naming convention, 土 additionally serves as the abbreviation prefix for tellurium via [[土素]].
