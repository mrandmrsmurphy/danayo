## Notes
- [以 (char)](../characters/以%20(char).md) "by; from" + [内](../characters/内.md) "inside"

以内 (traditional 以內) is "within; inside of; less than," a limit that something stays under, as in 二百 以内 "within two hundred." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 以內 (yǐnèi, Cantonese ji5 noi6), Japanese いない and Korean 이내 all mean "within." Wiktionary has no Vietnamese section, so `vietnamese` dĩ nội is a compositional reading. It pairs with [以外](以外.md) "other than," and in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [平臥即是正義](../translation/平臥即是正義.md) it appears in 二百 以内 於 控制 "keep within two hundred." The Japanese reading い is the on'yomi of 以.
