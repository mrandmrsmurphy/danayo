## Notes
- [此 (char)](../characters/此%20(char).md) "this; proximal demonstrative" + [時 (char)](../characters/時%20(char).md) "time; moment; season; occasion"

此時 is the proximal correlative in the 時 ("time") column of the correlative table: "now; at this time; this moment", the time of speaking or the time currently in focus. The character [此 (char)](../characters/此%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *cǝ*, ㄑㄜ) is the classical Chinese proximal demonstrative, contrasting with 其 (medial) and 彼 (distal); [時 (char)](../characters/時%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *si*, ㄙㄧ) is "time", and the word page [時](時.md) lists 此時 among the correlatives that 時 generates. Together they give the temporal counterpart of [此処](此処.md) "here" and the proximal member of the temporal triple 此時 "now", [其時](其時.md) "then" and [彼時](彼時.md) "back then".

The cross-linguistic fields hold the spoken equivalents. Mandarin 此時 (cǐshí; simplified 此时, held in `aliases`, which has no word file of its own): Wiktionary has an entry, a noun glossed "this moment; right now", listing 現在 as a synonym and giving Cantonese ci2 si4. Japanese この時 (kono toki): Wiktionary has an entry glossed "this time; this moment" (noun) and "at this time; at this moment; at this juncture" (adverb), with その時 and あの時 as its coordinate terms, which are also the equivalents recorded on [其時](其時.md) and [彼時](彼時.md). Korean 이때 (ittae): Wiktionary glosses it "now; (at) this time; right now", from 이 "this" + 때 "time". Vietnamese *lúc này* "at this time" and *bây giờ* "now" are from memory, not re-checked; hvdic.thivien.net covers single characters, not phrases, so no `vietnamese` field is entered. The earlier `japanese: 今` and `korean: "지금"` were removed from the fields: they are the everyday words for "now" (Wiktionary glosses 지금 "now"), but they are not equivalents of a *this-time* correlative in the way この時 and 이때 are, and they would break the pattern of [其時](其時.md) (その時, 그때) and [彼時](彼時.md) (あの時, 저때).

How 此時 relates to the existing words for "now": [今](今.md) (an adverbial noun, 副用名詞, listed on the Calendar page) and [現在](現在.md) (a noun, "the present; the current time", on Time and Existence) are lexical words for the present as such, and Wiktionary lists 現在 as the colloquial synonym of Mandarin 此時. 此時 is different in kind: it is a cell of the correlative paradigm, so it takes its sense from the 此/其/彼 deictic contrast and can be swapped for 其時 or 彼時 by changing the determiner. A plausible but unattested consequence (not stated in the grammar files, and no example in `grammar/` uses it) is that 此時 can also point to the current moment of a narrative rather than the moment of speaking, as English "now" does in storytelling; treat this as a hypothesis. Illustrative only (invented, not from `grammar/`): 此時在 "(someone) is here now". Dan'a'yo keeps the sense shared across CJKV, "this time; now"; no narrowing is needed, and the choice between 此時, 今 and 現在 is stylistic rather than one of strict meaning.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md)'s correlative table (line 121; the same chart is in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`, line 149, in the 時 row), 此時 is the sixth cell of the 此 ("this") row, after [此事](此事.md) "this matter", [此物](此物.md) "this thing", [此人](此人.md) "this person", [此名](此名.md) "this person (polite)" and [此処](此処.md) "this place", and before [此様](此様.md) "this manner", [此多](此多.md) "this amount" and [此類](此類.md) "this kind". Down the 時 column it pairs with [其時](其時.md) "then" (medial), [彼時](彼時.md) "back then" (distal), [何時](何時.md) "when", [毎時](毎時.md), [某時](某時.md) "sometime" and [皆時](皆時.md) "always". The table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), 63 forms, so 此時 is parsed compositionally: 此 = proximal, 時 = time. The 時 and 処 columns are the spatial/temporal pair, so 此処/此時, 其処/其時 and 彼処/彼時 line up. This word stays on the Grammar page only, like its siblings; the temporal vocabulary page [Time](../lexipedia/Time.md) already mentions it under the entry for 時.

The 注音 ㄑㄜㄙㄧ carries no syllable-boundary dot: the only syllable-final consonants `grammar/文法 - 02音韻論.md` allows are the nasals ㄇ ㄋ ㄫ, the glides y/w and the unreleased stops ㄆ ㄊ ㄎ, and ㄙ is none of them, so the string has exactly one parse, ㄑㄜ + ㄙㄧ.
