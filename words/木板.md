## Notes
- [木](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree" + [板](../characters/板.md) "plank" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [板](../characters/板.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry: the character's meaning (plank, board) is identical, but 板 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word.

木板 means board or plank.

Mandarin mùbǎn, Cantonese muk6 baan2, Japanese もくばん, and Korean 목판 are all standard, fully compositional readings.
