## Notes
- [甲 (char)](../characters/甲%20(char).md) "armor; first heavenly stem"

甲 has two senses.

1. armor, a shell or carapace — the original sense, which also gives nails (指甲, [[指甲]]) and shells (龜甲, tortoise shell).
2. the first of the ten heavenly stems (甲乙丙丁戊己庚辛壬癸), and so "first, top-ranked": a grade of A, "Party A" (甲方) in a contract, and the first year of the sixty-year cycle. See [[Astronomy]].

The oracle-bone form is a cross, which the Shuowen explains as a seed splitting its husk, the first stir of spring; the armor sense and the sequence sense are both old. Japanese こう (and the stem reading かん) and Vietnamese giáp are the Sino-readings; Korean 갑 is the same reading, and in Korean, as in Japanese よろい, the everyday word for armor is a different native one. 甲 shares its Dan'a'yo sound with [[鉀]] "potassium".
