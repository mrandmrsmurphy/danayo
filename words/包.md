## Notes
- [包 (char)](../characters/包%20(char).md) "wrap"

包 is to wrap: to enclose something in a covering and tie it up, and the bundle or package that results. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 包 (Mandarin bāo, Cantonese baau1) as "to wrap; to bundle" and "package; bag"; it lists Japanese ほう and Korean 포 as the Sino-Xenic readings and Vietnamese bao as the Sino-Vietnamese one.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [包 (char)](../characters/包%20(char).md) as a free word. The compounds [包装](包装.md), [包含](包含.md) and [包括](包括.md) build on it, and a wrapped parcel belongs with the [袋](袋.md), sack, and the [鞄](鞄.md), bag.
