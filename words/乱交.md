## Notes
- [乱](../characters/乱.md) "disorder, chaos" + [交](../characters/交%20(char).md) "mix, intercourse" — "disorderly mixing," an orgy. Their own `stand_in` values: 乱's is [[混乱]]; 交's is itself; none points here, so 乱交 is an independent compound.

乱交 is an orgy, or promiscuity: sexual relations with many partners, without ties. Mandarin luànjiāo, Cantonese lyun6 gaau1 and Japanese らんこう are attested, and Korean 란교 is the same compound. Compare [[淫]] (lewd) and [[情欲]] (lust).
