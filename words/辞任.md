## Etymology
- [辞](../characters/辞.md) "bid farewell, resign (with words)" + [任](../characters/任.md) "responsibility, duty"

## Notes
Mandarin círèn, cantonese ci4 jam6, japanese じにん, and korean 사임 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese từ nhiệm is a directly attested standard Vietnamese word ("to resign"), matching 辞's own từ + 任's own nhiệm exactly. `辭任` is the standard traditional-character spelling variant (辞→辭), correctly listed as an alias.
