## Notes
Mandarin círèn, cantonese ci4 jam6, japanese じにん, and korean 사임 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese từ nhiệm is a directly attested standard Vietnamese word ("to resign"), matching 辞's own từ + 任's own nhiệm exactly. `辭任` is the standard traditional-character spelling variant (辞→辭), correctly listed as an alias. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 치님 diverges from Sino-Korean 사임 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄋㄧㄇ unique among both words and characters).
