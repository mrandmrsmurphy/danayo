## Notes
- [引 (char)](../characters/引%20(char).md) "pull" + [用](../characters/用.md) "use"

引用 is to quote or cite: to bring in words from another source and use them. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yǐnyòng as "to quote; to cite" and Japanese いんよう as "quotation". Cantonese jan5 jung6 and Korean 인용 match.

A quotation names its source, the [出典](出典.md), and it differs from [参考](参考.md), consulting a source without quoting it, and from [参照](参照.md), a cross-reference.
