## Notes
- [掻 (char)](../characters/掻%20(char).md) "scratch" + [羊](../characters/羊.md) "sheep" — standing in for 痒 "itchy," which is an alias of 羊 and has no page of its own: 痒 would only ever occur in compounds, and the syllable ⼘ㄫ is already crowded.

掻羊 is "itch": the sensation that makes one want to scratch, and the act of scratching it. It is the compound the daughter languages write 搔癢 or 搔痒 (traditional and simplified Chinese), 掻痒 (Japanese) and 搔痒 (Korean), and 瘙癢 for the sensation alone. Wiktionary glosses the Chinese 搔癢 "to scratch an itch; to tickle", the Japanese 掻痒 (そうよう) "scratching an itch" and the Chinese 瘙癢 "to itch; to feel itchy". The Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and Korean fields are those readings; the Vietnamese field is omitted because no reading is sourced.

The spelling is the Dan'a'yo adaptation. Where the source languages write the rare 痒 (or the traditional 癢), Dan'a'yo writes its phonetic 羊, the common character that carries the same sound, and keeps 掻 for the act of scratching, as in [掻](掻.md). All the source spellings are stored as aliases, so a search for 搔癢, 掻痒 or 瘙痒 lands here. The same substitution is what makes 痒 an alias of 羊.
