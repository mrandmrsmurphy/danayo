## Etymology
- [安](../characters/安.md) "peaceful" + [寧](../characters/寧.md) "peaceful, calm"

## Notes
- "Calm, composed, tranquil" (社会の安寧, "public order/tranquility"). `vietnamese: an ninh` is a directly attested, extremely common standard Vietnamese term with the same sense, especially in the "security, public order" register. Filled blank `pos: 性詞`. `korean: 안녕` is also the everyday Korean greeting ("hello/goodbye") — not a separate word, but this same formal "peaceful/well" sense extended into greeting use (originally short for asking after someone's peace/wellbeing). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 안넝 diverges from Sino-Korean 안녕 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄋㄋㄝㄫ unique among both words and characters).
