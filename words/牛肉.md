## Notes
- [牛 (char)](../characters/牛%20(char).md) "cow" + [肉 (char)](../characters/肉%20(char).md) "meat"

牛肉 is beef: the meat of the cow or ox. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin niúròu as "beef" and Japanese ぎゅうにく as "cow meat, beef". Cantonese ngau4 juk6 matches, and Korean 우육 is the reading of the characters; the ordinary Korean word is native.

It is the meat of the [牛](牛.md), cow, as [豚肉](豚肉.md) is of the pig and [鶏肉](鶏肉.md) of the chicken. Meat in general is the [肉](肉.md).
