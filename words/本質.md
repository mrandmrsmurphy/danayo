## Notes
- [本](../characters/本%20(char).md) "foundation" + [質](../characters/質.md) "essence, nature" — no stand-in relationship applies (本's own stand-in is itself; 質's own is [[質素]]).

本質 means essence, or (intrinsic) nature.

Mandarin běnzhì, Cantonese bun2 zat1, Japanese ほんしつ, Korean 본질, and Vietnamese bản chất are all standard, fully compositional readings. Simplified `mandarin` (dropped the extra běnzhí variant), removed the self-referential `本質` from `aliases` (duplicate of the word's own title), kept the genuine simplified variant `本质`, removed blank `swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄛㄋㄐㄧㄊ unique across both words and characters).
