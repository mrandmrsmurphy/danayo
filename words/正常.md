## Notes
- [正 (char)](../characters/正%20(char).md) "correct" + [常](../characters/常.md) "common, regular" — literally "correctly regular," i.e. normal.

正常 is extremely common, everyday vocabulary across Mandarin (zhèngcháng), Cantonese (zing3 soeng4), Japanese (せいじょう seijō), and Korean (정상 jeongsang), all fully compositional, matching 正's and 常's own stored citations exactly. This session's web-search budget was exhausted before this entry could be created, so these readings rely on established knowledge rather than a fresh Wiktionary check — flagged here for future verification rather than silently presented as freshly confirmed.

Vietnamese chính thường is compositional (正's own chính + 常's own thường); note that everyday spoken Vietnamese more often reaches for bình thường ("normal, ordinary," built from 平 "level" + 常) instead — chính thường may be comparatively less colloquial and is worth double-checking against a dictionary later. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 징샹 diverges from Sino-Korean 정상 in both syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄫㄙ⼘ㄫ unique among both words and characters).
