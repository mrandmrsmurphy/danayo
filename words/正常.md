## Notes
- [正 (char)](../characters/正%20(char).md) "correct" + [常](../characters/常.md) "common, regular" — literally "correctly regular," i.e. normal.

正常 is extremely common, everyday vocabulary across Mandarin (zhèngcháng), Cantonese (zing3 soeng4), Japanese (せいじょう seijō), and Korean (정상 jeongsang), all fully compositional, matching 正's and 常's own stored citations exactly. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Japanese readings are confirmed on en.Wiktionary; Korean 정상 is the compositional Sino-Korean reading.

Vietnamese chính thường is compositional (正's own chính + 常's own thường); everyday spoken Vietnamese more often reaches for bình thường ("normal, ordinary," built from 平 "level" + 常) instead.
