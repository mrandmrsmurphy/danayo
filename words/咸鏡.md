## Notes
- [咸](../characters/咸.md) "complete, united" + [鏡](../characters/鏡%20(char).md) "mirror" — the name of the Hamgyong region of Korea.

咸鏡 (Hamgyong) is the historical Joseon province in the northeast of the Korean peninsula, bordering China and Russia. The name joins the first characters of its two chief towns, [[咸興]] (Hamhung) and 鏡城 (Kyongsong) (from memory, not re-checked). The province was divided in 1896 into 南 and 北 halves, and North Korea now has South Hamgyong (咸鏡南道, capital Hamhung) and North Hamgyong (咸鏡北道, capital [[清津]]), both in the north; the Korean peninsula's Hamgyong mountains are named from it.

English and Chinese Wiktionary have 咸鏡 as a historical province of Korea (Mandarin Xiánjìng, Cantonese haam4 geng3), and English Wiktionary has a Korean entry 함경 covering Joseon's Hamgyong province, North Korea's two Hamgyong provinces and the Hamgyong mountains. Japanese Wiktionary has no entry, so Japanese かんきょう is composed from the on'yomi. Vietnamese Hàm Kính is composed from the character pages (the first Hán-Việt of 鏡 is kính); no Vietnamese dictionary entry was found.
