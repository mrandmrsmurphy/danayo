## Notes
- [看](../characters/看%20(char).md) "watch over, look after, watch" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 看 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

看 means to watch, look after, or keep an eye on — the character itself depicts a hand shading the eyes to gaze into the distance. It is highly productive: 看病 ("to nurse, tend a patient"), 看守 ("to guard, a watchman"), 看護 ("nursing care"), 看板 ("a signboard").

Mandarin kàn, Cantonese hon1, Japanese かん (kan), Korean 간 (gan), and Vietnamese khán are all standard, fully compositional readings.
