## Notes
- [水 (char)](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [壷](../characters/壷.md) "pot" — this word is the viable form of [壷](../characters/壷.md): the character's meaning (pot; vessel) is identical, but 壷 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 水壷 is its `stand_in` compound.

水壷 is a kettle or water bottle: a vessel for water. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 水壺 (an alias, with 水壶; Mandarin shuǐhú, Cantonese seoi2 wu4) as "kettle; pitcher" and "water bottle". It has no Japanese, Korean or Vietnamese entry for the word, so Japanese すいこ and Korean 수호 here are built from the characters, and the Vietnamese field is left blank. Korean 수호 is also, unrelatedly, the word for "guard; protection" (守護), as in 수호천사, "guardian angel".

水壷 is the stand-in word for [壷](../characters/壷.md): the character's meaning, a pot or vessel, is identical, but 壷 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word.
