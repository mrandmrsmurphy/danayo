## Notes
- [粒](../characters/粒.md) "particle, grain" + [子](../characters/子.md) "thing" (nominalizer)
- 粒子 is the stand-in for [粒](../characters/粒.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.

Wiktionary lists two Mandarin readings: lìzǐ for the physics term "particle", with derived terms such as 基本粒子 "elementary particle", 粒子加速器 "particle accelerator" and 反粒子 "antiparticle", and lìzi, with the neutral-tone 子, for "grain; pellet; granule". Cantonese nap1 zi2 (also lap1 zi2) matches the page. The vault's reading follows the physics reading lìzǐ. Japanese りゅうし and Korean 립자 are the Sino-Xenic readings.

In Dan'a'yo the word covers both the physical-science "particle" and the ordinary "grain", as placed on the [Physics](../lexipedia/Physics.md) page.
