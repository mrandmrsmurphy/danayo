## Notes
- [曽 (char)](../characters/曽%20(char).md) "great- (a generation further)" + [祖](../characters/祖.md) "ancestor" + [父](../characters/父.md) "father" — great-grandfather.

曽祖父 is a great-grandfather: the father of one's grandfather. It is "great-ancestor-father" from [[曽]], [[祖]] and [[父]]. Mandarin zēngzǔfù (formal; many regional synonyms such as 太公), Cantonese zang1 zou2 fu6 and Japanese そうそふ are confirmed on Wiktionary, and Korean 증조부 is listed as a descendant of the term. The Chinese entry specifies the paternal line; Dan'a'yo takes the general sense. His pair is [[曽祖母]]; the downward counterpart is [[曽孫]].
