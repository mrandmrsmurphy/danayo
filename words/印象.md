## Notes
- [印](../characters/印.md) "seal, stamp" + [象](../characters/象.md) "form, image" — literally "a form stamped [into the mind] like a seal." No stand-in relationship applies (印's own `stand_in` is [[封印]]; 象's own is [[大象]]).

印象 means an impression — the noun (a strong impression left on the mind), and by extension "to impress" someone, Rosenfelder's item. Despite the genuinely old seal-imprint metaphor for the mind it draws on (心印, "mind-seal," a real Zen Buddhist term describing insight stamped from master to disciple — not itself a vault entry), 印象 as this exact fixed compound is a 19th-century (Meiji-era, c. 1881) Japanese coinage built specifically to translate the Western psychological concept "impression" — a calque using old material, not an independently ancient native term.

Mandarin yìnxiàng, Cantonese jan3 zoeng6, Japanese いんしょう, Korean 인상, and Vietnamese ấn tượng are all standard, fully compositional readings — a coinage that spread successfully and became universal across the whole CJKV sphere. `kwin` is false: 印 is `kwin: true`, 象 is `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄧㄋㄙ⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
