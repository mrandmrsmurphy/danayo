## Notes
- [挿](../characters/挿.md) "insert" + [入](../characters/入%20(char).md) "enter" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [挿](../characters/挿.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (入's own stand-in is itself).

挿入 means to insert or stick into.

Mandarin chārù, Cantonese caap3 jap6, Japanese そうにゅう, Korean 삽입, and Vietnamese sáp nhập are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄚㄆㄋㄧㄆ unique across both words and characters).
