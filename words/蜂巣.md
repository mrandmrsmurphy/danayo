## Notes
- [蜂 (char)](../characters/蜂%20(char).md) "bee, wasp, hornet" + [巣](../characters/巣.md) "nest"

蜂巣 "bee-nest" is the beehive or honeycomb, a straightforward concatenation of [蜂 (char)](../characters/蜂%20(char).md) (fong/뽕/ㄈㄛㄫ) and [巣](../characters/巣.md) (jau/잣/ㄐㄚㄨ). The `japanese` field records the Sino-Japanese reading ほうそう, while the everyday Japanese word is the native compound はちのす, written 蜂の巣 (literally "the bee's nest"); without the genitive の, はちす would instead read as 蓮 "lotus" — an unrelated homophone trap. Korean has two equally common terms: 봉소 (Sino-Korean, from 蜂巢) and 벌집 (native "bee-house").

The Animals page lists it as "beehive," and Dan'a'yo keeps that sense. It is the nest counterpart of [蜂](蜂.md) "bee, wasp, hornet" and sits beside [蜂蜜](蜂蜜.md) "honey" among the bee words. The alias 蜂巢 is the standard Chinese spelling, with 巢 the traditional form of 巣 (an alias of the character 巣 here).
