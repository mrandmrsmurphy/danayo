## Notes
- [沈](../characters/沈.md) "sink" + [静](../characters/静.md) "still"

沈静 is calm and quiet: a settled, still state, or to grow calm after agitation. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin chénjìng as "quiet; calm; serene; placid" and Japanese ちんせい as "tranquil, calm, quiet; tranquility". Cantonese cam4 zing6 matches, and Korean 침정 is the reading of the characters.

It is calm as a state, close to [平静](平静.md) and [安静](安静.md), and it is what comes after [激昂](激昂.md), being roused.
