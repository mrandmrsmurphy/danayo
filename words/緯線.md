## Notes
- [緯](../characters/緯.md) "lines of latitude" + [線](../characters/線.md) "line" — 緯's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 線's own is [[直線]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 緯線 legitimizes 緯 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 線 is individually `kwin: false`.

緯線 names a line of latitude.

Mandarin wěixiàn, Cantonese wai5 sin3, Japanese いせん (isen), and Korean 위선 (wiseon) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 緯線 is itself a common attested word for "line of latitude" in all four languages. Vietnamese vĩ tuyến is directly attested via hvdic. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
