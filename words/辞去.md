## Etymology
- [辞](../characters/辞.md) "bid farewell, resign (with words)" + [去 (char)](../characters/去%20(char).md) "go"

## Notes
Mandarin cíqù, cantonese ci4 heoi3, japanese じきょ, and korean 사거 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese từ khứ is the honest compositional reading (辞's own từ + 去's own khứ); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `辭去` is the standard traditional-character spelling variant (辞→辭), correctly listed as an alias.

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 辞's `stand_in` (辞職) nor 去's own `stand_in` (去 itself) points to this compound.
