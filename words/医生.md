## Notes
- [医](../characters/医.md) "medicine" + [生](../characters/生.md) "life, one who does" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [医](../characters/医.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (生's own stand-in is [[生活]]).

医生 means doctor or physician — the standard everyday Mandarin word for a medical practitioner.

**Real bugs found and fixed**: `mandarin`/`cantonese`/`korean`/`aliases` had all been contaminated with forms belonging to the near-synonym word 医師/医师 (yīshī, a different, more formal Chinese word for "doctor" built from 医+師 rather than 医+生) — `mandarin: yīshī` corrected to yīshēng, `cantonese: ji1 si1` corrected to ji1 sang1, `korean: 의사,의생` (mixing in the unrelated real Korean word 의사 "doctor," itself based on 醫師) corrected to the compositional 의생, and `aliases` (which listed 醫師/医师, forms of the wrong word entirely) trimmed to just 醫生, the genuine traditional form of this word. `pos` was blank — filled as 名詞. Japanese いせい and Vietnamese y sinh were already correct. No homophones (注音 ㄜㄙㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
