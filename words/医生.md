## Notes
- [医](../characters/医.md) "medicine" + [生](../characters/生.md) "life, one who does" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [医](../characters/医.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (生's own stand-in is [[生活]]).

医生 means doctor or physician — the standard everyday Mandarin word for a medical practitioner.
