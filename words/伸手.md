## Notes
- [伸](../characters/伸.md) "stretch" + [手 (char)](../characters/手%20(char).md) "hand"

伸手 is to reach out: to extend the hand, to grasp or to take. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin shēnshǒu as "to extend out one's hand" and, figuratively, "to ask for a handout; to meddle in". Cantonese san1 sau2 matches, and Korean 신수 is the reading of the characters.

It is the movement of the [手](手.md), hand, toward something, where [握手](握手.md) is the clasp of hands in greeting and [持](持.md) is to hold.
