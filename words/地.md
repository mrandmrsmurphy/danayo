## Notes
- [地 (char)](../characters/地%20(char).md) "land"

**Content corrected**: `mandarin` was stored as `"de dì"`. `dì` is the correct reading for this word's own "land, earth" sense (地方, "region"; 地球, "earth, globe"); `de` is 地's separate, unrelated neutral-tone grammatical-particle reading (functioning like 的/得, as in 慢慢地, "slowly," an adverbial suffix) — not part of this word's own sense, so dropped rather than kept as a spurious second reading.

Mandarin dì and Cantonese dei6 preserve the "land" sense directly. Japanese チ/ジ (CHI/JI, on'yomi, fully productive: 地球, chikyū, "earth"; 地面, jimen, "ground") cover the sense, with no dedicated native kun'yomi. Korean 지 (ji) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 땅 (ttang, "land, ground"). Vietnamese địa is the standard Hán Việt reading (địa lý, "geography"; địa phương, "locality"), forming a literary/vernacular doublet pair with the native đất ("earth, soil, ground," as in đất nước, "country," literally "land and water"). The character page's other stored candidate, rịa, is not a genuine secondary reading at all — it's a one-off Nôm phonetic loan confined to the place name Bà Rịa, with no independent meaning of "land," and was excluded.

This word shares its reading (diǝ/듸/ㄉㄧㄜ) with [[雉]] ("pheasant"), still unperfected and awaiting its own turn in this sweep.
