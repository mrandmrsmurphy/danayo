## Notes
- [羊](../characters/羊.md) "sheep, goat" + [毛 (char)](../characters/毛%20(char).md) "fur" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (羊's is [[綿羊]], 毛's is [[毛]] itself), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 毛 is individually `kwin: false`.

羊毛 names wool or fleece.

Mandarin yángmáo, Cantonese joeng4 mou4, Japanese ようもう (yōmō), and Korean 양모 (yangmo) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 羊毛 is itself a common attested word for "wool" in all four languages. Vietnamese dương mao is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
