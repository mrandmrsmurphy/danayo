## Notes
- [土 (char)](../characters/土%20(char).md) "earth" + [星 (char)](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star"

土星 is Saturn, literally the "earth star": 土, earth or soil, plus 星, star. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 土星 (Mandarin Tǔxīng, Cantonese tou2 sing1), Japanese どせい and Korean 토성 all as "Saturn (planet)", and Vietnamese Thổ tinh as "Saturn".

Saturn is one of the five planets visible to the naked eye, which Wiktionary lists under 五星 as Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter and Saturn. Its orbit takes about 29½ years, according to English Wikipedia. The others are [[木星]], [[火星]], [[金星]] and [[水星]]. The same 土 gives Saturday its name, [[土曜日]].
