## Notes
- [少 (char)](../characters/少%20(char).md) "few" + [数](../characters/数.md) "number"

少数 is a small number, and a minority: a few among many. Wiktionary glosses Japanese しょうすう as "minority; few, a small number". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 소수 matches.

It is the opposite of [多数](多数.md). A minority people is a [民族](民族.md) or [族群](族群.md) outnumbered by another, but 少数 itself only counts.
