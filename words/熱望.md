## Notes
- [熱 (char)](../characters/熱%20(char).md) "hot; fervent" + [望](../characters/望.md) "hope; look"

熱望 is fervent hope: a wish held with heat and enthusiasm. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin rèwàng as "to fervently hope; fervent hope" and Japanese ねつぼう the same. Cantonese jit6 mong6 and Korean 열망 match.

It is the warm form of hoping, where [渇望](渇望.md) is the thirsty form, and it comes from the [熱情](熱情.md), passion, of the hoper. Plain hope is [希望](希望.md).
