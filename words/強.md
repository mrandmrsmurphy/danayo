## Notes
- [強 (char)](../characters/強%20(char).md) "strong; powerful" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [強 (char)](../characters/強%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

強 means strong or powerful — physical strength, intensity, or force (強国 "superpower," 弱肉強食 "survival of the fittest").

Japanese きょう, Korean 강, and Vietnamese cường are all standard readings (matching sibling compounds 強化/強固/強国/剛強). No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼘ㄫ shared only with the character [[僵]], whose own stand-in is the unrelated multi-syllable word [[僵屍]]).
