## Notes
- [養](../characters/養.md) "foster, raise" + [成 (char)](../characters/成%20(char).md) "become" — no `#cranberry`: 養's own `stand_in` is [[養育]], 成's is 成 itself; neither points here.

養成 (yǎngchéng) is the standard Mandarin word for "to cultivate, train" (a person's skills or character), and Cantonese 養成 (joeng5sing4) likewise. Japanese ようせい (yōsei) and Korean 양성 (yangseong) are both standard terms, as is Sino-Vietnamese dưỡng thành — a clean case with full agreement across all five languages.
