## Notes
- [圧 (char)](../characters/圧%20(char).md) "press; control"

**Content corrected**: `cantonese` was stored as `zong1`, which doesn't correspond to this character's phonetic series at all (壓/圧's phonetic is 厭, giving *aat*-type readings, not *zong*) — corrected to `aat3`. `korean` was stored as the literal string `"null"` — corrected to `압`, matching the character page's own value.

圧 (simplification of 壓) means "to press, push down; to control, suppress" and, as a noun, "pressure" (圧力, "pressure"; 血圧, "blood pressure").

Mandarin yā and Cantonese aat3 preserve the sense directly. Japanese アツ (ATSU, on'yomi, fully productive: 圧力, atsuryoku, "pressure"; 圧倒, attō, "to overwhelm") alongside native お(す) (o(su), here an extended sense of "to push") cover the sense. Korean 압 (ap) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese áp is the Hán Việt reading, extremely common in everyday and technical speech alike (áp lực, "pressure"; huyết áp, "blood pressure"; áp suất, "pressure," physics unit).
