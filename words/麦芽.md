## Notes
- [麦](../characters/麦.md) "wheat, barley, oats" + [芽](../characters/芽.md) "bud, sprout" — no `#cranberry`: 麦's own `stand_in` is [[大麦]], 芽's is [[新芽]]; neither points here.

Mandarin màiyá, Cantonese mak6ngaa4, Japanese ばくが, Korean 맥아, and Sino-Vietnamese mạch nha (also the real everyday Vietnamese term for malt syrup) are all standard, fully compositional readings.
