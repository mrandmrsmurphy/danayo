## Notes
- [姜](../characters/姜%20(char).md) "ginger" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

姜 means ginger, the pungent rhizome used widely in CJKV cuisine and medicine.

Mandarin jiāng, Cantonese goeng1, Japanese きょう, Korean 강, and Vietnamese khương are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄍ⼘ㄋ/gyan/갼) with the unrelated [[柬]] ("letter, invitation").
