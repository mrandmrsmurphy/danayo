## Notes
- [山 (char)](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain" + [芋](../characters/芋.md) "taro" — 芋's own `stand_in` is 里芋, so this compound is not its legitimizer

Wiktionary: Japanese 山芋 (やまいも) is *Dioscorea japonica*, a type of yam; Chinese 山芋 is dialectal or literary and varies by region: sweet potato (*Ipomoea batatas*) in several dialects, potato in Yinchuan Mandarin, taro (literary, Wenzhounese Wu), taro grown on dry land (Sichuan), *Alocasia odora* (Taiwanese Hokkien), and an alternative name for 薯蕷, Chinese yam (*Dioscorea polystachya*). The Dan'a'yo sense is the Japanese one, "mountain yam".
