## Notes
- [山 (char)](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain" + [芋](../characters/芋.md) "taro" — 芋's own `stand_in` is 里芋, so this compound is not its legitimizer

The Japanese reading やまいも uses the native kun'yomi (mountain + potato) rather than the Sino-Japanese on'yomi サンウ. Refers to the yam *Dioscorea japonica* in Japan; in Chinese dialectal usage the referent varies (sweet potato, taro, or potato depending on region).

Korean 산우 is the compositional Sino-Korean reading, not separately verified (the usual Korean word for the yam is 마), so `kwin: true` reflects only that the Dan'a'yo form and the compositional field agree.  The 注音 ㄙㄚㄋ·ㄨ takes its dot because ㄙㄚㄋㄨ could also parse as ㄙㄚ + ㄋㄨ.
