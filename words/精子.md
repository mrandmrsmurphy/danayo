## Notes
- [精](../characters/精.md) "essence, refined" + [子](../characters/子.md) "child" — no stand-in relationship applies (精's own stand-in is [[精神]]; 子's own is [[児子]]).

精子 means sperm.

Mandarin jīngzǐ, Cantonese zing1 zi2, Japanese せいし, Korean 정자, and Vietnamese tinh tử (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Removed a false homophone claim: this page previously claimed a collision with [[整理]], but that was based on 整理's own frontmatter being contaminated with the wrong second-syllable reading (since fixed on 整理's side) — no real homophone exists (注音 ㄐㄝㄫㄐㄜ unique across both words and characters).
