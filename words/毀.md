## Notes
- [毀](../characters/毀%20(char).md) "destroy, slander" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

毀 means to destroy or slander — to ruin something physically, or to damage a reputation.

Mandarin huǐ, Cantonese wai2, Japanese き, Korean 훼, and Vietnamese huỷ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄏ⼔ㄧ/hwei/훼) with the unrelated [[慧]] ("bright, intelligent").
