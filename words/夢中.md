## Notes
- [夢 (char)](../characters/夢%20(char).md) "dream" + [中](../characters/中.md) "middle; in"

夢中 is absorbed: so taken up with something that one is off in another world, unaware of what is around. Wiktionary glosses Japanese むちゅう as "absorbed; fully occupied with one's thoughts; engrossed; a trance". Mandarin mèngzhōng and Cantonese mung6 zung1 are literally "in a dream". Korean 몽중 is the reading of the characters.

It is the state of the [熱中](熱中.md), absorbed in a pursuit, but seen from outside as being away in a dream. Being carried off by delight is the [陶酔](陶酔.md), intoxication.
