## Notes
- [仰](../characters/仰.md) "to look up" + [望](../characters/望.md) "to hope, gaze" — "to look up to, admire, revere," carrying connotations of noble aspiration and reverence rather than mere observation (碧空を仰望する, "to gaze up at the blue sky"; used figuratively of admired people or ideals). Both `japanese: ぎょうぼう` and `vietnamese: ngưỡng vọng` are directly attested (confirmed via web search). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 양망 diverges from Sino-Korean 앙망 in the first syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ⼘ㄫㄇㄚㄫ unique among both words and characters).
