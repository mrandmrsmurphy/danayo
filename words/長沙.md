## Notes
- [長](../characters/長%20(char).md) "long" + [沙](../characters/沙%20(char).md) "sand" — "long sandbank". No stand-in relationship applies (both characters are their own stand-ins).

長沙 (Changsha) is the capital of Hunan province in south-central China. English Wiktionary lists the city and the county of the same name in the same city (Changsha County) as separate senses, with a number of smaller places of the same name (a former island in the Yangtze estuary now part of Chongming Island, a town in Chongqing, a township in Anhui, a village in Fujian, and the Cheung Sha area on Lantau Island, Hong Kong). 长 is an alias of 長, so 长沙 is kept as the word's alias.

Japanese ちょうさ and Korean 장사 are the Sino-Xenic readings (Wiktionary Japanese 長沙 ちょうさ, Korean 장사); the usual South Korean name today is 창사 (listed on Wiktionary). Vietnamese Trường Sa is the Sino-Vietnamese reading listed on English Wiktionary; the same Vietnamese form is also the usual name of the Spratly Islands (from memory, not re-checked), and the Wiktionary Vietnamese gloss links to that. Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese entries. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄫㄙㄚ unique across both words and characters).
