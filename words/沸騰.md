## Notes
- [沸](../characters/沸.md) "boil" + [騰](../characters/騰.md) "rise, leap" — "boil and leap," to boil. Their own `stand_in` values: 沸's is [[煮沸]]; 騰's is [[騰貴]]; none points here, so 沸騰 is an independent compound.

沸騰 is to boil: a liquid bubbling at its boiling point, and figuratively to seethe. Mandarin fèiténg, Cantonese fai3 tang4, Japanese ふっとう and Korean 비등 all use it. Compare [[蒸発]] (to evaporate) and [[燃焼]] (to burn).
