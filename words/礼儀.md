## Notes
- [礼 (char)](../characters/礼%20(char).md) "manners" + [儀](../characters/儀.md) "rite; ceremony"

礼儀 is etiquette: the manners and courtesy that are expected in dealing with others. Wiktionary glosses Japanese れいぎ as "manners". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 례의 matches the reading of the characters.

It is everyday politeness, where [儀礼](儀礼.md) is formal ceremony and [礼](礼.md) is manners as a whole. Its breach is [失礼](失礼.md), rudeness.
