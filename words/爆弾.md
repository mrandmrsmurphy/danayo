## Notes
- [爆](../characters/爆.md) "explode" + [弾](../characters/弾.md) "bullet, projectile" — "exploding projectile." Neither character's own `stand_in` points here (爆's is [[爆発]]; 弾's is [[弾丸]]), so 爆弾 is an independent compound.

爆弾 is a bomb. Japanese ばくだん and Korean 폭탄 use these characters as the ordinary word; in Chinese 爆彈 is listed only as a dated, rare form, the everyday word being 炸彈 (zhàdàn), so Mandarin bàodàn and Cantonese baau3 daan6 are the readings of the characters rather than the common words. Dan'a'yo takes the Japanese/Korean form because it is built from characters already in the vault.

Compare [[大砲]] (cannon) and [[爆発]] (explosion).
