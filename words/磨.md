## Notes
- [磨](../characters/磨%20(char).md) "grind, polish, rub, wear out" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 磨 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

磨 means to grind, polish, sharpen, or wear away through friction — 磨耗 ("wear and tear"), 研磨 ("to hone, to polish"), and figuratively 磨練/磨鍊 ("to temper oneself through hardship, to train rigorously").

Mandarin mó, Cantonese mo4, Japanese ま (ma), Korean 마 (ma), and Vietnamese mài are all standard, fully compositional readings.
