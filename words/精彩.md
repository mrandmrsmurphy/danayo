## Notes
- [精](../characters/精.md) "essence, refined" + [彩](../characters/彩.md) "vibrant, multicolored" — literally "refined-brilliance," praising something as both refined and dazzling.

精彩 describes something brilliant, splendid, or outstanding — commonly used to praise a performance, an artwork, or an impressive event as vivid and distinctive, combining 精's "refined, essential" sense with 彩's "vibrant, colorful" sense.

Mandarin jīngcǎi, Cantonese zing1 coi2, Korean 정채 (jeongchae), and Japanese せいさい (seisai) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 精's and 彩's own stored citations. Vietnamese tinh thải is likewise compositional, from 精's tinh and one of 彩's four attested readings, thải.
