## Notes
- [幼](../characters/幼.md) "young; immature" + [稚](../characters/稚.md) "young; immature" — this word is the stand-in for [稚](../characters/稚.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

幼稚 is a near-synonym doubling, both halves independently meaning "young/immature" — used both neutrally of young age (幼稚園 "kindergarten," literally "young-and-immature garden") and, more commonly today, pejoratively of childish, unsophisticated behavior or thinking in an adult.

Japanese ようち, Korean 유치, and Vietnamese ấu trĩ are all standard, non-drifted readings. HSK level 3 already correct. No homophones (注音 ⼜ㄛㄉㄧㄜ unique across both words and characters).
