## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" is separately legitimized by [[自身]]. [在 (char)](../characters/在%20(char).md) "exist" is separately legitimized by [[在]] itself. No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

自在 combines 自 ("self") with 在 ("exist, be present") — "comfortable, carefree, adjustable" (existing as oneself, unconstrained).

Mandarin zìzai (neutral tone on zai, the real attested reading), cantonese zi6zoi6 (space removed), japanese じざい (jizai, with rendaku), and korean 자재 (jajae) were all already correct.
