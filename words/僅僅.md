## Notes
- [僅](../characters/僅.md) "only, merely" reduplicated — an intensified form of "only, merely, barely," the reduplication adding emphasis (仅仅是, "it's merely..."). `korean: 근근` is directly attested (confirmed via web search, usually as the adverb 근근이, "barely, with difficulty" — 근근이 살아가다, "to barely get by"). `vietnamese: cẩn cẩn` is compositional; no independent attestation of the reduplicated form was found in Vietnamese. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 긴긴 diverges entirely from Sino-Korean 근근. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄋㄍㄧㄋ unique among both words and characters).
