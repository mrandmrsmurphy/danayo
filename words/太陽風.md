## Notes
- [太 (char)](../characters/太%20(char).md) "great" + [陽 (char)](../characters/陽%20(char).md) "sun" + [風 (char)](../characters/風%20(char).md) "wind" — "solar wind" (the stream of charged particles from the sun). `vietnamese: thái dương phong` is compositional, used in formal/scientific Vietnamese; everyday Vietnamese instead says gió Mặt Trời (native).
