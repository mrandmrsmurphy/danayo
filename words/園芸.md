## Notes
- [園](../characters/園.md) "garden, park, orchard" + [芸](../characters/芸.md) "art, skill" — no stand-in relationship applies (園's own stand-in is [[庭園]]; 芸's is [[芸術]]).

園芸 means gardening or horticulture.

The character 芸 here represents 藝/艺 ("art, craft") rather than its own unrelated identity as a plant name (yún, "rue") — in Japanese shinjitai, 芸 directly replaced 藝, so all its Japanese and Sino-xenic readings already reflect 藝: mandarin yì, cantonese ngai6, korean 예, matching the real-world word exactly, none derived from 芸's own citation fields. Filled blank `vietnamese: viên nghệ` (verified Sino-Vietnamese compound); modern everyday Vietnamese instead prefers the native "làm vườn." Filled blank `pos: 名詞`. No homophones (注音 ㄛㄋㄝ unique across both words and characters).
