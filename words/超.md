## Notes
- [超 (char)](../characters/超%20(char).md) "transcend, surpass" — "to transcend, surpass" (超越, "to exceed, transcend"; 超人, "superman"). Mandarin chāo, Cantonese ciu1, Japanese ちょう, Korean 초, and Vietnamese siêu are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 탓 diverges from Sino-Korean 초 entirely. This word is part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read tau/탓/ㄊㄚㄨ) with [[島]] ("island") and [[倒]] ("turn over, take turns") — both reciprocal callouts already correctly present on their own pages.

Legitimizing note: 超 (char)'s own `stand_in` is this exact word (itself), so 超 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by itself.
