## Notes
- [私](../characters/私.md) "private" + [有](../characters/有%20(char).md) "have; own" — "privately owned." Their own `stand_in` values: 私's is [[私立]]; 有's is itself; none points here, so 私有 is an independent compound.

私有 is to be privately owned, the opposite of [[公有]]. Mandarin sīyǒu, Cantonese si1 jau5, Japanese しゆう and Korean 사유 all use it, and Vietnamese tư hữu does too. Compare [[所有]] and [[財産]].
