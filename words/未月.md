## Notes
- [未](../characters/未%20(char).md) "eighth Earthly Branch, goat; not yet" + [月](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [未](../characters/未%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

Sixth month of the ancient Chinese lunar calendar, named for the eighth Earthly Branch 未 (goat).

Midsummer. 未 also carries the meaning "not yet" — the harvest has not yet come, but the year's fullness is building. The goat's association with gentleness and abundance fits the lush, heavy heat of this month.

Japanese ひつじつき reads both halves natively (未's kun'yomi ひつじ "goat" + 月's kun'yomi つき "month") rather than the Sino-Japanese on'yomi concatenation みげつ, matching the established zodiac-month convention (see [[寅月]], [[卯月]]). Vietnamese tháng Mùi likewise uses the native *tháng* plus the zodiac name Mùi (未's own native reading), the same pattern. Removed the redundant `品詞` field (duplicate of `pos`) and reformatted `japanese`/`vietnamese` from single-item lists to scalar strings. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄨㄧ·⼔ㄊ unique across both words and characters).
