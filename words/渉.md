## Notes
- [渉](characters/渉%20(char).md) "ford" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 渉 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry. 渉 is a Japanese shinjitai variant of the standard form 涉.

渉 means to ford, to wade across water on foot (extended to "to be involved with, to interfere," as in 干渉).

Mandarin shè, Cantonese sip3, and Korean 섭 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 渉's own stored citations exactly.
