## Notes
- [柔 (char)](../characters/柔%20(char).md) "soft, gentle" + [軟](../characters/軟.md) "soft, tender"

柔軟 describes physical or figurative pliability — a material, body, or surface that bends or gives easily rather than resisting, as in 柔軟性 ("flexibility," of joints and muscles) or 柔軟剤 ("fabric softener"). Figuratively it extends to a flexible attitude, stance, or way of thinking (柔軟な発想, "flexible thinking"), in contrast to rigidity. It is also used of colors and tones to describe a soft, muted, or desaturated quality rather than a bold or harsh one.

Cross-linguistically the compound is a stable Sino-Xenic loan: Sino-Korean 유연 and Japanese じゅうなん both directly concatenate the component readings and carry the same physical and figurative senses, and Vietnamese nhu nhuyễn is likewise an ordinary, freely used word for softness or suppleness.
