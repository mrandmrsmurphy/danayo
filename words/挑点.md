## Notes
- [挑](../characters/挑.md) "to lift, flick up" + [点 (char)](../characters/点%20(char).md) "point; dot"

Wiktionary has no entry for 挑点; it is a compound of 挑 and 点. Wiktionary glosses 挑 in Chinese calligraphy as "a rising stroke (㇀)", the same stroke it glosses 提; the Eight Principles of Yong article on Wikipedia also gives 挑 (tiāo) as an alternative name of the rising principle, marked citation needed. 挑点 names the rising dot, a dot stroke finished with an upward flick, a sub-variety of [[点]] on the [[Stroke]] page. It is related to but different from [[平提]] and [[斜提]], the rising strokes. Its readings are compositional.
