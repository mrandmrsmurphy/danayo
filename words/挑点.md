## Notes
- [挑](../characters/挑.md) "to lift, flick up" + [点 (char)](../characters/点%20(char).md) "point; dot"

**挑点** is the *rising dot*, a dot stroke finished with an upward flick of the brush. In regular-script teaching it generally sits on the left side of a character and often combines with a following 撇 stroke or with other dots in a composite shape. It is a sub-variety of [[点]], listed on the [[Stroke]] page beside 瓜子点 and [[長点]].

The word is not to be confused with the unrelated 提 stroke ([[平提]], [[斜提]]), the rising flick that closes a left-hand component such as 土 or 王; the rising dot is a dot, short and attached to its stroke, while the 提 is a full diagonal stroke.

The Japanese ちょうてん coincides with [[長点]] and 頂点 (*chōten*, "apex"), and the Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and North Korean forms are all compositional (the Korean 도점 is built from 挑's Sino-Korean 도). No Vietnamese form was found attested, so none is given.
