## Notes
- [兌](../characters/兌.md) "exchange" + [換 (char)](../characters/換%20(char).md) "exchange" — this word is the stand-in for [兌](../characters/兌.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

兌換 is a synonym-doubling compound, same shape as 宏大/狡滑 elsewhere in this vault: both halves already mean "to exchange," and 換 simply reinforces 兌's own sense rather than adding a distinct second idea — the same tell already noted on [換 (char)](../characters/換%20(char).md)'s own page, whose `korean_native` gloss (바꿀, "exchange") is identical to 兌's.

Mandarin (duìhuàn), Cantonese (deoi3 wun6), and Japanese (だかん, dakan) are all directly confirmed via Wiktionary. Korean (태환) and Vietnamese (đoài hoán) are compositional concatenations of each character's own reading, not independently attested by name in the sources checked, but both characters' own readings are solidly confirmed individually.
