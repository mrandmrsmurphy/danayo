## Notes
- [掃](../characters/掃.md) "sweep" + [除](../characters/除.md) "exclude" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [掃](../characters/掃.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (除's own stand-in is [[除外]]).

掃除 means to sweep or clean up.

Mandarin sǎochú, Cantonese sou3 ceoi4, Japanese そうじ, Korean 소제, and Vietnamese tảo trừ are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄚㄨㄐㄝㄧ unique across both words and characters).
