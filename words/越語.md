## Notes
- [越 (char)](../characters/越%20(char).md) "Yue, exceed" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language" — "the Vietnamese language," the compact form of [[越南語]]. Neither character's own `stand_in` points here (越's is itself, 語's is [[言語]]).

Wiktionary: Chinese 越語 (yuèyǔ), chiefly in Taiwan and Vietnam, "Vietnamese language"; Japanese 越語 (えつご) "Vietnamese language"; Vietnamese Việt ngữ is the Han form, labelled dated, overseas Vietnamese or formal, "Vietnamese language". It is the compact form of [[越南語]], like [[韓語]], with 南 dropped, and shares its reading with 粤語 (see the homophone callout). Used in `文法 - 02音韻論` in the grouping 漢語・韓語・越語.
