## Notes
- [天](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven, sky" + [国](../characters/国.md) "country, kingdom" — "heavenly country," heaven. Their own `stand_in` values: 天's is itself; 国's is [[国家]]; none points here, so 天国 is an independent compound.

天国 is heaven: the kingdom of heaven, the place of the blessed. Mandarin tiānguó (天國), Cantonese tin1 gwok3, Japanese てんごく and Korean 천국 all use it, and Vietnamese thiên quốc does too. It is the heaven of reward and of God's rule, where [[天]] is the sky. Compare [[楽園]] (paradise) and [[地獄]] (hell).
