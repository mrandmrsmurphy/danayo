## Notes
- [寡](../characters/寡.md) "widow; few, scant" + [婦 (char)](../characters/婦%20(char).md) "lady; woman" — this word is the stand-in for [寡](../characters/寡.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

寡婦 names a woman whose husband has died, built from 寡's core sense of "alone, bereft of" (extended from its more general "few, scant" — the character's own graphemic root depicts a single person alone in a house) plus 婦's plain "woman." 寡 alone still carries this bereaved-of-spouse sense in classical registers (寡人, a ruler's humble self-address, literally "the friendless/bereaved one"), distinct from its everyday modern sense of numerical scarcity (as in 寡言 "taciturn, sparing of words," 多寡 "amount, quantity").

Japanese かふ, Korean 과부, and Vietnamese quả phụ (drawing on 寡's own "quả" reading rather than its synonymous "goá") are all plain, standard, non-drifted terms for "widow" in their respective languages. No specific HSK level number is confirmed (Wiktionary marks the compound only "Intermediate Mandarin"); no homophones (注音 ㄍ⺢ㄅ⼜ unique across both words and characters).
