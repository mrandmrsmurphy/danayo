## Notes
- [五 (char)](../characters/五%20(char).md) "five" + [臓](../characters/臓.md) "internal organ" — the Five Solid Organs of traditional Chinese medicine: 心 (heart), 肝 (liver), 脾 (spleen), 肺 (lungs), and 腎 (kidneys), each governing a corresponding function and paired with the Five Elements and Five Sense Organs ([[五官]]) in the classical correspondence system.

五臓 is often paired with 六府 (the Six Hollow Organs — stomach, gallbladder, small/large intestine, bladder, and the triple burner) in the compound [[五臓六府]], the standard traditional-medicine term for "all the internal organs" collectively. Mandarin wǔzàng, Cantonese ng5 zong6, Japanese ごぞう, Korean 오장, and Vietnamese ngũ tạng are all standard, fully compositional readings.

**Real content bug found and fixed**: the `japanese` field was stored as ござう, which doesn't match either of 臓's own on'yomi (ZOU/SOU) — corrected to ごぞう (5's ご + 臓's first-listed ZOU).

`kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 오장 coincides exactly with Sino-Korean 오장, since both 五 and 臓 independently carry `kwin: true` on their own character pages.
