## Notes
- [郷](../characters/郷.md) "home village" + [愁](../characters/愁.md) "worry; longing"

郷愁 is homesickness: longing for one's home or for a time gone by. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xiāngchóu as "homesickness; nostalgia for one's home" and Japanese きょうしゅう as "nostalgia". Cantonese hoeng1 sau4 matches, and Korean 향수 is the reading of the characters.

It is [哀愁](哀愁.md) with a place in it, and it is a gentler word than the wistful grief of [懐愁](懐愁.md). The home itself is the [故郷](故郷.md).
