## Notes
- [難](../characters/難.md) "calamity, disaster" + [民](../characters/民.md) "people" — literally "calamity people." No stand-in relationship applies (難's own `stand_in` is [[困難]]; 民's own is [[人民]]).

難民 means a refugee. Mandarin nànmín reads 難 with its "calamity, disaster" tone (nàn) rather than its more common "difficult" tone (nán, as stored on the character's own primary citation) — the same legitimate tone-based dual-life pattern documented elsewhere in this vault (see e.g. [[好]]/[[愛好]]). Cantonese follows the same split: naan6 (calamity) rather than naan4 (difficult), confirmed naan6 man4. Japanese なんみん and Korean 난민 are both standard, fully compositional readings, matching this word's own Dan'a'yo citation exactly (`kwin: true`). Vietnamese nạn dân is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form; living spoken Vietnamese more often says "người tị nạn" instead, noted here rather than silently substituted. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄚㄋㄇㄧㄋ unique across both words and characters).
