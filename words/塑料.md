## Notes
- [塑](../characters/塑.md) "model, mold" + [料](../characters/料.md) "material, stuff" — no stand-in relationship applies (塑's own stand-in is [[塑造]]; 料's is [[材料]]).

塑料 means plastic — the synthetic material.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `mandarin` had the wrong vowel on the first syllable ("suòliào" → sùliào, matching 塑's own citation). Japanese プラスチック and Korean 플라스틱 are both real-world English loanwords (rather than compositional readings), and Vietnamese `nhựa` is the native word for plastic/resin — all genuine real-world equivalents for this modern material, since Mandarin 塑料 itself is the actual everyday Chinese term (unlike the other languages, which favor the international loanword). Filled blank `pos: 名詞`; fixed empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄛㄌ⼘ㄨ unique across both words and characters).
