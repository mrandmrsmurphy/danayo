## Notes
- [潜](../characters/潜.md) "hidden, latent" + [素](../characters/素.md) "element" — literally "hidden element," a direct semantic calque of the element's own English/Greek name: "krypton" itself derives from Ancient Greek κρυπτός (kruptós, "hidden") + the "-on" noble-gas suffix, so this vault's coinage and the international name share the exact same etymology rather than merely sounding alike.
- Avoids the Chinese novel character 氪 (kept here as this word's own alias), coining a transparent compound instead — the same naming strategy used throughout this vault's periodic table (compare [[苦土素]] for magnesium, [[石灰素]] for calcium).

Mandarin kè and Cantonese hak1 are the real, standard readings for krypton in those languages (of 氪, the character this vault's compound deliberately avoids in writing while still matching its pronunciation, the same convention already established on [[苦土素]] and [[石灰素]]). Korean 크립톤 (keuripton), Japanese クリプトン (kuriputon), and Vietnamese kripton are all the real, standard loanword terms (from English "krypton"), not kanji/hanja calques. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄇㄙㄛ unique among both words and characters).
