## Notes
- [除](../characters/除.md) "exclude" + [籍](../characters/籍.md) "record, register, list"

除籍 means to strike someone from a register or roll — expulsion from a school, organization, or citizenship record. Mandarin 除籍 (chújí), Cantonese 除籍 (ceoi4zik6), Japanese 除籍 (じょせき, used e.g. for expulsion from a school's student register), and Korean 제적 (jejeok, common in the same academic-expulsion sense) are all standard administrative/legal terms.

**Caution**: the compositional Sino-Vietnamese trừ tịch (除's trừ + 籍's tịch) is spelled identically to the much better-known unrelated word trừ tịch (除夕, "New Year's Eve" — trừ here meaning "changing/passing" and tịch meaning "evening," an entirely different character pair). To avoid ambiguity, modern Vietnamese generally expresses this specific meaning with the native phrase xóa tên khỏi danh sách ("strike the name from the list") rather than the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form.
