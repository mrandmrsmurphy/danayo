## Notes
- [現 (char)](../characters/現%20(char).md) "present; appear" + [実](../characters/実.md) "real; solid"

現実 is reality: what is actually the case, as opposed to what is imagined or hoped for. Wiktionary glosses Japanese げんじつ as "reality". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 현실 matches.

It is the ground that [幻想](幻想.md), fantasy, and [虚構](虚構.md), fiction, are measured against, and it is the actual state of [実現](実現.md), a plan made real. Its sense of what is actually so overlaps [実際](実際.md), actual, and [事実](事実.md), fact.
