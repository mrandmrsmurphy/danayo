## Notes
- [八 (char)](../characters/八%20(char).md) "eight" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — literally "eight-day," the eighth day of the month.

Mandarin bā rì, Cantonese baat3 jat6, and Korean 팔일 (paril) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese has a special irregular native reading, ようか (yōka), not the expected compositional はちにち (hachinichi) — one of the well-known irregular day-of-month readings that make the first ten (plus the 14th, 20th, and 24th) days of the Japanese month notoriously tricky for learners (compare [[四日]], also irregular). Vietnamese bát nhật is likewise compositional, from 八's bát and 日's own nhật; everyday Vietnamese instead uses the native mùng tám ("the eighth"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 받닏 diverges from Sino-Korean 팔일 in both syllables. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄊㄋㄧㄊ unique among both words and characters).
