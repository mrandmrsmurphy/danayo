## Notes
- [不 (char)](../characters/不%20(char).md) "not" + [均 (char)](../characters/均%20(char).md) "equal, even" — uneven, unequal, as in 貧富不均 ("inequality between rich and poor") or 発展不均 ("uneven development").

Filled entirely missing `japanese: ふきん` (compositional, 均's own on'yomi KIN) and `korean: 불균` — the real attested reading (confirmed via 불균형, "imbalance," a very common word), following the same 不→불 assimilation rule already documented on [[不信]]/[[不及]]/[[不可]], not the naive 부균.
