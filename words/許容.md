## Notes
- [許 (char)](../characters/許.md) "permit" (stand-in: itself) + [容 (char)](../characters/容.md) "allow, contain" (stand-in: itself) — to permit, tolerate.

許容 means to permit/tolerate, as invoked by `文法 - 02音韻論`'s 許容異音 ("a tolerated allophone"). Japanese きょよう and Korean 허용 are both real, standard terms, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Cantonese heoi2 jung4 is a straightforward compositional reading. Mandarin xǔróng and Vietnamese hứa dong are compositional only, not independently verified as standard. `kwin` is false: both 許 and 容 are individually false.

No syllable-boundary dot is needed: the concatenated string `ㄏ⼄⼄ㄫ` admits only one legal partition.
