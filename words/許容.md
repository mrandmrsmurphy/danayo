## Notes
- [許 (char)](../characters/許%20(char).md) "permit" (stand-in: itself) + [容 (char)](../characters/容%20(char).md) "allow, contain" (stand-in: itself) — to permit, tolerate.

Wiktionary: Japanese 許容 (きょよう) "acceptance, permission, toleration" and, as a verb, "to accept, allow, permit, tolerate"; Korean 허용 is its hanja form. Used in `文法 - 02音韻論` in 許容異音. Compare [[容認]].
