## Notes
- [可](../characters/可%20(char).md) "can, -able" + [愛](../characters/愛%20(char).md) "love" — no stand-in relationship applies (both characters are independent Dan'a'yo entries, each its own stand-in).

可愛 means cute or lovable — literally "can be loved," one of many 可-prefixed "-able" adjectives (see [[可読]], [[可能]]).

**Real bugs found and fixed**: `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` had 可's syllable using a divergent form (ka/카/ㄎㄚ) rather than its own authoritative reading (kǝ/크/ㄎㄜ, confirmed against every other 可-compound in the vault — [[可]], [[不可]], [[可不]], [[可能]], [[可以]], [[可読]], [[不可不]], [[不可以]] — all consistently use kǝ/크) — corrected. `japanese` had a typo (かはいい → かわいい). Mandarin kě'ài, Cantonese ho2 oi3, Korean 가애, and Vietnamese khả ái are all standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄜ·ㄚㄧ unique across both words and characters).
