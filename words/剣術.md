## Notes
- [剣](../characters/剣%20(char).md) "sword" + [術](../characters/術%20(char).md) "skill, method, technique" — no stand-in relationship applies (both characters are independent Dan'a'yo entries in their own right, each its own stand-in).

剣術 names swordsmanship or fencing — the martial art or skill of the sword.

Japanese けんじゅつ, Korean 검술, and Vietnamese kiếm thuật are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄇㄙㄨㄊ unique across both words and characters).
