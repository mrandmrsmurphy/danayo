## Notes
- [異](../characters/異.md) "different" + [化](../characters/化.md) "-ify, to become" — no stand-in relationship applies (異's own stand-in is [[異常]]; 化's is [[変化]]).

異化 is "to alienate; to dissimilate; to make or become dissimilar," and as a noun, "alienation" or "dissimilation." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 異化 (yìhuà, Cantonese ji6 faa3) is a verb with four senses: ergative "to make dissimilar"; in philosophy "to alienate"; in phonology, and more generally, "to dissimilate"; and in translation studies "to foreignize," the antonym of 歸化 "to domesticate." Japanese いか is a noun and suru-verb with the senses "dissimilation" (general and phonological), "catabolism" in biology (also 異化作用), and "alienation" in philosophy, the rendering of German *Verfremdung*; its antonym is [[同化]] "assimilation." Wiktionary has no Korean or Vietnamese entry. From memory, not re-checked: Korean 이화 and Vietnamese *dị hóa* are the same word, and *dị hóa* is the usual Vietnamese term for catabolism; hvdic.thivien.net has no entry for the compound itself.

This is a deliberate exception to the affix policy. Normally a transparent stem plus the fully productive suffix [[－化]] ("-ification, to become or render X") gets segmented and has no page of its own. But 異化 is not predictable from 異 "different" + 化: a segmented reading would be "to become different" or, with [[異常]], 異常化 "to become abnormal," whereas the word actually means the lexicalised "alienation" of Marxist and Hegelian philosophy (a person estranged from their labour, products or society) and the linguistic "dissimilation" (two like sounds becoming unlike). It is the mirror of [[同化]] "assimilation," and the pair is used as such in phonology. Coining it was approved by the user.

It is used on [China](../lookup/HSK/China.md) in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 异化的世界何时消亡 "when will the alienated world die out?," as 異化 之 世界. The sense there is the philosophical one. It differs from [差異](差異.md) "difference," which is a static state of being unlike rather than a process of becoming so.

1. to alienate; alienation (philosophy, sociology)
2. to dissimilate; dissimilation (phonology)
3. to make or become dissimilar (general)

No homophones (注音 ㄧㄏ⺢ unique across both words and characters).
