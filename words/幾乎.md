## Notes
- [幾 (char)](../characters/幾%20(char).md) "several" + [乎 (char)](../characters/乎%20(char).md) "particle"

幾乎 means almost, nearly. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 幾乎 (Mandarin jīhū, Cantonese gei1 fu4) as "almost; nearly; all but", and lists Vietnamese cơ hồ as a Sino-Vietnamese form of "almost, nearly". It has no Japanese or Korean entry for the word, so the Japanese きこ and Korean 기호 fields are built from the readings of the two characters; the Japanese word for "almost" is written with the single character 殆. Both characters are free words in Dan'a'yo: [幾](幾.md), several, and [乎](乎.md).
