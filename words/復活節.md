## Notes
- [復](../characters/復.md) "restore" + [活](../characters/活%20(char).md) "alive" + [節](../characters/節%20(char).md) "node, knuckle, joint, holiday" — no stand-in relationship applies (復's own stand-in is [[回復]]; 活/節 are both their own stand-ins).

復活節 means Easter — the Christian holiday commemorating the resurrection ([[復活]]) of Jesus Christ.

Mandarin Fùhuójié, Cantonese fuk6 wut6 zit3, Japanese ふっかつさい, Korean 부활절, and Vietnamese phục hoạt tiết are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄨㄎㄏ⺢ㄊㄐㄝㄊ unique across both words and characters).
