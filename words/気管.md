## Notes
- [気 (char)](../characters/気%20(char).md) "air; breath" + [管](../characters/管.md) "tube"

気管 is the windpipe: the tube that carries air from the throat to the lungs. Wiktionary glosses Japanese as "trachea". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 기관 matches.

It lies below the [咽喉](咽喉.md), throat, and leads to the [肺臓](肺臓.md), lungs. The act it serves is [呼吸](呼吸.md), breathing, and its blocking is [窒息](窒息.md).
