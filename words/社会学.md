## Notes
- [社](../characters/社.md) "company" + [会 (char)](../characters/会%20(char).md) "meet, gather" + [学](../characters/学.md) "learn" — literally "society" (社会) + "study" (学), i.e. the study of society.

社会学 is the standard academic term for sociology, the systematic study of society, social institutions, and social relationships. It follows the same "field + 学" pattern used across the full range of academic disciplines in this vocabulary system: 心理学 (psychology), 経済学 (economics), 人類学 (anthropology).

Mandarin shèhuìxué, Cantonese se5 wui2 hok6 (会 taking a colloquial changed tone, matching [[社会主義]]), Japanese しゃかいがく (shakaigaku), Korean 사회학 (sahoehak), and Vietnamese xã hội học are all fully compositional and are the standard academic terms across the sphere.
