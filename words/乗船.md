## Notes
- [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "ride, board" + [船](../characters/船.md) "ship, boat" — literally "to board a ship," to embark.

乗船 parallels 乗車 ("to board a train/car") and 乗馬 ("to ride a horse") in the productive 乗 + [vehicle] pattern. Mandarin chéng chuán, Cantonese sing4 syun4, Japanese じょうせん, and Korean 승선 (승선하다, "to board a ship," used e.g. of sailors and passengers alike) are all standard, fully compositional terms. **Vietnamese content note**: thừa thuyền is a compositionally sound derivation (乗's thừa + 船's own stored thuyền), but everyday Vietnamese more commonly says lên thuyền/lên tàu ("to get on the boat/ship," native) for this action; no independent attestation of thừa thuyền as a fixed Sino-Vietnamese compound turned up, so it is presented as a defensible derivation rather than a separately confirmed term.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 숭쉄 diverges from Sino-Korean 승선, since both 乗 and 船 independently carry `kwin: false` on their own character pages.
