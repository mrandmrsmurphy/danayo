## Notes
- **Genuine `#cranberry` case**: both 釣's and 漁's own `stand_in` point to this exact compound (already correctly annotated on both character pages). japanese つる, korean 낚시하다, and vietnamese câu cá all confirmed as the genuine attested native terms (not bugs), like [[良月]]'s native readings. Aliases 釣魚/漁釣 confirmed real orthographic/inversion variants. Built the missing `## Notes` and `## Etymology` sections.

## Etymology
- [釣](../characters/釣.md) "angling" + [漁](../characters/漁.md) "to fish" — two near-synonymous verbs compounded into the general word for fishing; the alias 釣魚 is the standard Mandarin orthography, 漁釣 a classical inversion.
