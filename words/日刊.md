## Notes
- [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day, sun" + [刊](../characters/刊%20(char).md) "publication, periodical" — no stand-in relationship applies (日's own stand-in is itself; 刊's own is itself).

日刊 means daily publication or daily periodical.

Mandarin rìkān, Cantonese jat6 hon1, Japanese にっかん, Korean 일간, and Vietnamese nhật san (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄧㄊㄎㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
