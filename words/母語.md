## Notes
- [母](../characters/母.md) "mother" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language" — mother tongue; the language acquired naturally in early childhood

Wiktionary: Chinese "mother tongue; native language; first language; heritage language", and in Singapore "official mandated language of one's ethnic group"; Japanese 母語 (ぼご) "a first language, a mother tongue, a native tongue", and, linguistics, uncommon, "a proto-language, a mother language from which later daughter languages evolve"; Korean 모어 is the hanja form, "native language". Compare [[外語]]. Used in `文法 - 01序文`.
