## Notes
- [項](characters/項%20(char).md) "nape, item" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 項 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

項 means nape, or item.

Mandarin xiàng, Cantonese hong6, Japanese こう, Korean 항, and Vietnamese hạng (the primary form among the character's own comma-joined vietnamese variants háng/hạng/hảng) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed literal `vietnamese: null` → hạng, added the missing `pos`/`japanese` fields. Reformatted the existing 3-way homophone group with [[行]] and [[桁]] (all 注音 ㄏㄚㄫ, 諺文 항) to the canonical multi-line callout.
