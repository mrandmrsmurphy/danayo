## Notes
- [擬](../characters/擬%20(char).md) "intend, plan" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [擬](../characters/擬%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

擬 means to intend or plan (as in 擬態, "mimicry," and 模擬, "simulation").

Mandarin nǐ, Cantonese ji5, Japanese ぎ, Korean 의, and Vietnamese nghĩ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `羅馬字` was missing the leading glottal marker (i→'i, per `characters/擬 (char).md`'s own citation). Homophone of [[以]] "by; using; instrumental" (both 注音 ㄧ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words; checked four other candidates sharing this reading (異/疑/飴/頤) and confirmed none has an independent word at this exact reading (飴/頤 are name-only, the rest bound to longer stand-in compounds).
