## Notes
- [庵](../characters/庵.md) "hermitage; thatched hut" + [子](../characters/子.md) "noun suffix" — this word is the stand-in for [庵](../characters/庵.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo. 子 here is in its light noun-suffix role (as in [[梯子]]), not "child."

庵子 is a genuine dialectal word carrying two distinct regional senses in Mandarin: in the Guanzhong dialect (Shaanxi region), a simple thatched hut or field shelter built by farmers to guard crops or store grain; in other regions (e.g. Zichuan, Shandong), a small Buddhist nunnery — the two senses reflected in this word's own gloss ("greenhouse" loosely capturing the agricultural-hut sense, "monastery" the religious one).

Korean 암자 preserves the religious-building sense specifically (a small hermitage or nunnery, the everyday sense used in Korean Buddhism). **Japanese あんじ is primarily attested as a given-name reading rather than an ordinary common noun** — 庵 itself (read あん/いおり) is the productive standalone word for a hermitage in Japanese (as in the poet's retreat 芭蕉庵, Bashō-an), and 子 rarely attaches to it in everyday vocabulary. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄇㄐㄜ unique across both words and characters).
