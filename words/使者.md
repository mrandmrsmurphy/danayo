## Notes
- [使](../characters/使.md) "to send, envoy" + [者 (char)](../characters/者%20(char).md) (agent suffix, "one who...") — "envoy, emissary, messenger" (traditionally, one sent by a king to conduct diplomacy). `vietnamese: sứ giả` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese (confirmed via web search), formal/ceremonial register, familiar from history and culture.

**Real content bug found and fixed**: `cantonese` had been written in Mandarin-pinyin-style romanization ("shi3 ze3") instead of Jyutping — corrected to si2 ze2, matching 使's own si2 and 者's own ze2. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 시차 diverges from Sino-Korean 사자 in the first syllable's vowel — the same divergence shared with both homophones.
