## Notes
- [圭](../characters/圭.md) "jade tablet; salmon" + [魚 (char)](../characters/魚%20(char).md) "fish"

圭魚 is a salmon: the fish of the genus *Oncorhynchus* and its relatives, eaten fresh, smoked or cured. The usual spelling is 鮭魚, an alias, because 鮭 is an alias of 圭. Wiktionary glosses the Taiwanese Mandarin 鮭魚 (guīyú, Cantonese gwai1 jyu4-2) as "salmon", and the character 鮭 as "salmon" in Chinese and "chum salmon; salmon" in Japanese, where it is read さけ. Japanese さけ, Korean 연어 and Vietnamese cá hồi are the native everyday words, as with [鯖魚](鯖魚.md), mackerel.

圭 is a 名専字, a character used only in names, but it may appear inside a compound like this one. The fish is a [魚](魚.md) of the sea and of rivers, and its flesh is [魚肉](魚肉.md).
