## Notes
- [鼾 (char)](../characters/鼾%20(char).md) "snore"

鼾 is a snore, and to snore: the rough noise of breathing through the nose in sleep. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin hān as "to snore" (literary) and "snore; snoring", the compound 打鼾 being the ordinary "to snore". Japanese かん is a bound affix "snoring" (the free word is いびき), and Korean 한 and Cantonese hon4 match.

Its Dan'a'yo syllable is ㄏ⼘ㄋ (*hyan*), an otherwise unused syllable, rather than the regular ㄏㄚㄋ, which already holds ten characters. It is a sound of [睡眠](睡眠.md), sleep, made through the [鼻](鼻.md), nose, and it is a disturbance of [呼吸](呼吸.md), breathing.
