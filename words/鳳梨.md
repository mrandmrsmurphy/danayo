## Notes
- [鳳](../characters/鳳.md) "phoenix, joy" + [梨 (char)](../characters/梨%20(char).md) "pear" — no `#cranberry`: 鳳's own `stand_in` is [[鳳凰]], 梨's is 梨 itself; neither points here.

Mandarin fènglí and Cantonese fung6lei4-2 keep the compositional Sino reading (a name likening the fruit's crown of spiky leaves to a phoenix's plumage). Japanese パイナップル and Korean 파인애플 are both direct phonetic loanwords from English "pineapple." Vietnamese dứa is the real, everyday native word (also thơm/khóm regionally).
