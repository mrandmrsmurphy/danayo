## Notes
- [荊](../characters/荊.md) + [州 (char)](../characters/州%20(char).md) — Jing Province, the sixth of the [[九州]]

Wiktionary: Chinese 荊州 is the prefecture-level city Jingzhou in Hubei (with a district and a development zone of the same name) and, historical, Jing Province, one of the Nine Provinces ([[九州]]) of ancient China; Japanese けいしゅう is the Hubei city. The *Tribute of Yu* (尚書, 禹貢, Wikisource) has the section 荊州 beginning 荊及衡陽惟荊州, "from Mount Jing to the south of Mount Heng is Jingzhou". 荊's own `stand_in` is 荊棘, so this word is not its legitimizer; the word names the province as listed among the 九州.
