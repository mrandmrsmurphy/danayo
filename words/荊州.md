## Notes
- [荊](../characters/荊.md) + [州 (char)](../characters/州%20(char).md) — Jing Province, the sixth of the [[九州]]

荊州 is bounded in the *Tribute of Yu* as the land from Mount Jing to the south of Mount Heng (荊及衡陽惟荊州), and covered the south-central region — roughly modern Hubei and Hunan — the territory of the powerful ancient state of Chu (楚). Chu was the great southern rival of the Zhou cultural sphere, absorbing and transforming it over centuries; 荊州 was its heartland. In the Three Kingdoms period, 荊州 became the most strategically contested territory in China, fought over by Cao Cao, Liu Bei, and Sun Quan, and its control or loss determined the fate of each state. The phrase 荊州 still carries this resonance of strategic centrality and violent contest.

荊's own `stand_in` is 荊棘, so this word is not its legitimizer.  The word names the province 荊州 as listed among the 九州.  Mandarin jīngzhōu, Cantonese ging1 zau1, Japanese けいしゅう, Korean 형주 and Vietnamese Kinh Châu are the readings of that name.  `kwin` is false because the Dan'a'yo 켱줏 differs from the Korean 형주.
