## Notes
- [悲 (char)](../characters/悲%20(char).md) "sad" + [劇 (char)](../characters/劇%20(char).md) "drama"

悲劇 is a tragedy: a serious play that ends in disaster; also any sad event. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin bēijù as "tragedy" and Japanese ひげき as "tragedy". Korean 비극 and Vietnamese bi kịch are the same word. Cantonese bei1 kek6 matches.

It is the opposite of the [喜劇](喜劇.md), comedy, and a tragic fate is also called by this word outside the theatre.
