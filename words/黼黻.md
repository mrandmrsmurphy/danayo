## Notes
- [黼](../characters/黼.md) "axe-pattern embroidery" + [黻](../characters/黻.md) "亞-pattern embroidery" — `#cranberry`, listed on [[lookup/List of 連綿詞]]: an axe and a 亞-shaped figure embroidered on an official's robe as a mark of distinction (two of the Twelve Ornaments, in black-and-white or black-and-blue-green).

Both 黼 and 黻 share the same Middle Chinese f- initial, and their own stored 諺文 (뿌/뿓, using the tense ㅃ) is a consistent vault convention for approximating this MC-f-initial class, since Korean lacks a native /f/ phoneme — the word's own concatenation follows this pattern correctly. Sino-Vietnamese phủ phất is the compositional form.
