## Notes
- [希](../characters/希.md) "rare, hope for" (stand-in for 希有) + [有 (char)](../characters/有%20(char).md) "have, exist" — literally "rarely have/exist," rare, unusual.

Mandarin xīyǒu, Cantonese hei1 jau5, Japanese けう (historical kana for きょう, kyō), Korean 희유, and Vietnamese hi hữu are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 希's and 有's own stored citations exactly. 

`kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 희유 coincides exactly with the corrected Sino-Korean 희유, since both 希 and 有 independently carry matching readings on their own character pages. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄧㄜ·⼜ unique among both words and characters).
