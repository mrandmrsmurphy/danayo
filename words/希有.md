## Notes
- [希](../characters/希.md) "rare, hope for" + [有 (char)](../characters/有%20(char).md) "have, exist" — literally "rarely have/exist," rare, unusual — stand-in for [希](../characters/希.md), which cannot appear independently.

Mandarin xīyǒu, Cantonese hei1 jau5, Japanese きゆう and けう (both standard), Korean 희유, and Vietnamese hi hữu are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 希's and 有's own stored citations exactly.
