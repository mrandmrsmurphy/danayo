## Notes
- [艶 (char)](../characters/艶%20(char).md) "plump; voluptuous; beautiful" — the bare character used directly as a word, its own `stand_in`.

艶 describes a glossy, sensuous, or striking beauty, used in both aesthetic and erotic registers: lustrous, charming, alluring (艶やか, "glamorous"; 艶麗, "gorgeous"; 艶聞, "romantic rumor, scandal"; 妖艶, "bewitchingly beautiful"), and by extension gloss or sheen (艶, "luster"). The traditional form 豔 is listed as an alias.

Mandarin yàn, Cantonese jim6, Japanese えん (艶福/えんぷく, "success in love"; 艶笑/えんしょう, "bawdy humor"; 妖艶/ようえん, "bewitching"), Korean 염 (염문, "love affair"; 요염, "bewitching") and Vietnamese diễm (diễm lệ, "beautiful"; diễm tình, "romance") are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native nouns 艶 (つや, "gloss") and 艶やか (あでやか) for the everyday words, so on'yomi えん is bound to compounds; Korean has native 곱다 for "pretty".
