## Notes
- [合 (char)](../characters/合%20(char).md) "join; fit together; combine"

合 is 会意: [[亼]] ("to converge") + [[口]] ("mouth") — two mouths speaking together, closing on the same word. It means "to join, fit together, combine, unite" — one of the most productive characters in the vault, heading [[合金]] ("alloy"), [[結合]] ("to combine, unite"), [[組合]] ("to assemble; a union/cooperative"), and [[合成]] ("synthesis, compound"). The character's own Middle Chinese reading had two variants (voiceless and voiced onset); Dan'a'yo's single reading represents a deliberate merger of the two rather than a choice between them.

Mandarin hé, Cantonese hap6, and Japanese ごう/かつ/がつ (gō/katsu/gatsu, on'yomi, fully productive: 合成 ごうせい "synthesis") alongside native あ(う)/あ(わせる) (a(u)/a(waseru), "to fit, come together/to combine," the everyday verbs) all preserve the sense. Korean 합 (hap) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese hợp is the Hán Việt reading, extremely well attested (hợp tác, "to cooperate"; phù hợp, "suitable, fitting"; kết hợp, "to combine," matching this character's own [[結合]] directly) — the character page's remaining stored candidates (cáp, cóp, góp, gộp, họp, hạp) include several plausible native cognates in the same semantic neighborhood ("to gather, pool, meet"), but hợp alone is the attested Hán Việt reading and was used here.
