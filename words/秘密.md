## Notes
- [秘](../characters/秘.md) "secret, mysterious" + [密 (char)](../characters/密%20(char).md) "dense, thick, close, intimate, secret" — 秘's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 密's own is [[密]] itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 秘 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 秘 is individually `kwin: false`.

**Fixed a real bug**: `aliases` had wrongly listed 秘密 (this word's own headword) as an alias of itself — trimmed to just 祕密, the genuine traditional-orthography variant.

秘密 is the plain, general word for "secret" across the sphere — information deliberately withheld or hidden, whether personal, commercial (企業秘密, "trade secret"), or state (国家秘密, "state secret"). It is broader and more neutral than [[神秘]] (which carries a numinous/mystical connotation from 神).

Mandarin mìmì, Cantonese bei3 mat6, Japanese ひみつ (himitsu), and Korean 비밀 (bimil) are all standard, fully compositional readings, each the ordinary everyday word for "secret" in its language. Vietnamese bí mật is likewise compositional and standard.
