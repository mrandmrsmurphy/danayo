## Notes
- [文 (char)](../characters/文.md) "text, sentence" (stand-in: [[文化]]) + [末 (char)](../characters/末.md) "end" (stand-in: [[末端]]) — sentence-final; occurring at the end of a sentence.

文末 means sentence-final, as in `文法 - 02音韻論`'s 文末詞 ("sentence-final particle"), distinct from [[語末]] ("word-final"). Mandarin wénmò and Korean 문말 are real, standard linguistics terms. Cantonese man4 mut6 is a straightforward compositional reading. Vietnamese văn mạt is compositional only. `kwin` is true: both 文 and 末 are individually true.

No syllable-boundary dot is needed: the concatenated string `ㄇㄨㄋㄇㄚㄊ` admits only one legal partition.
