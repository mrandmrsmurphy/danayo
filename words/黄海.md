## Notes
- [黄](../characters/黄.md) "yellow" + [海](../characters/海.md) "sea, ocean" — no `#cranberry`: both 黄's and 海's own `stand_in` point elsewhere; neither points here.

Named for the yellow-brown color the Yellow River's silt sediment gives the water. Mandarin Huáng Hǎi, Cantonese wong4hoi2, Japanese こうかい, and Sino-Vietnamese Hoàng Hải are all standard, fully compositional readings. Koreans instead call it 西海 (Seohae, "West Sea"), reflecting its position relative to the Korean peninsula rather than China. **Not an alias**: [[西海]] was previously listed here as an alias, but it is a separate, independent word denoting the classical "Western Sea" of the 四海 — a distinct concept from the Yellow Sea that only happens to share this modern Korean place-name usage, as 西海's own page explicitly notes.
