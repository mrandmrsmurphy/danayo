## Notes
- [惜](../characters/惜%20(char).md) "begrudge, rue" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

惜 means to begrudge or rue — feeling regret at losing or parting with something, as in 惜別 ("regret at parting").

Mandarin xī, Cantonese sik1, Japanese せき, Korean 석, and Vietnamese tiếc are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄙㄝㄎ/seg/석) with the unrelated [[石]] ("rock, stone") — see [[石]]'s own Notes for the fuller account of this coincidental convergence.
