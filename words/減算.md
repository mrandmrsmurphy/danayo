## Notes
- [減](../characters/減.md) "subtract, reduce, decrease, diminish" + [算 (char)](../characters/算%20(char).md) "calculate" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 減 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (算's own stand-in is itself, so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

減算 means to subtract.
