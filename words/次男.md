## Notes
- [次](../characters/次.md) "second, next" + [男](../characters/男.md) "male" — second son.

次男 is the second son. It is "next-male." Mandarin cìnán, Cantonese ci3 naam4, Japanese じなん (with 長男, 三男), Korean 차남 and Vietnamese thứ nam are all confirmed on Wiktionary. Japanese also reads 次男 as a given name, Tsugio. The female counterpart is [[次女]]; the eldest is [[長男]].
