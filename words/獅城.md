## Notes
- [獅](../characters/獅.md) "lion" + [城](../characters/城.md) "city, walled city" — the Lion City; a literary Chinese rendering of Malay *Singapura* (singa = lion, pura = city). Singapore's name and its Chinese sobriquet share the same underlying etymology. No stand-in relationship applies (獅's own stand-in is [[獅子]]; 城's own is [[城郭]]).

Mandarin shīchéng, Cantonese si1 sing4, Japanese しじょう (SHI+JOU), Korean 사성, and Vietnamese sư thành are all fully compositional, matching 獅's and 城's own stored citations exactly.
