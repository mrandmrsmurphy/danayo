## Notes
- [石 (char)](../characters/石%20(char).md) "rock" + [榴](../characters/榴.md) "pomegranate"
- This word is the viable form of [榴](../characters/榴.md): the character's meaning (pomegranate) is identical, but 榴 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 石榴 is its `stand_in` compound.

石榴 is the pomegranate, both the tree and its seed-filled fruit. It is traditionally said to have been brought to China from the Western Regions in the Han period (the account credits Zhang Qian), and the fruit is a symbol of many children and fertility because of its many seeds, a decorative motif in China, Korea and Japan. Mandarin shíliú, Cantonese sek6 lau4 and Korean 석류 (seongnyu) are the compound's regular readings; Japanese is ざくろ (zakuro), a special reading given to the written form 石榴 (also written 柘榴), not the sum of the characters' own sounds.

In Dan'a'yo it is the name of the fruit, filed with the other fruits on the Food page.
