## Notes
- [陳](../characters/陳.md) "exhibit; display" + [朝 (char)](../characters/朝%20(char).md) "morning" — no stand-in relationship applies (陳's own is [[陳列]]; 朝's own is the single-character word [[朝]]). Here 陳 is the surname Chén (Vietnamese Trần) and 朝 is "dynasty", as in [[王朝]] and [[宋朝]]. The simplified form 陈朝 is kept as an alias because 陳 is the parent form on its character page.

This name is a collision between a Chinese dynasty and a Vietnamese one, so the Chinese sense comes first.

1. The Chen dynasty (557–589), the last of the Southern Dynasties of China, founded by Chen Baxian (陳霸先) with its capital at Jiankang, the modern Nanjing, and conquered by the Sui in 589 (the dates are English Wiktionary's; the founder, capital and conqueror are from memory, not re-checked).
2. The Trần dynasty of Vietnam, 1225–1400 (English Wiktionary and Japanese Wiktionary agree), the dynasty that followed the Lý (see [[李朝]]) and ruled from Thăng Long, the later Hanoi; its state name was Đại Việt (Japanese Wikipedia). It is remembered for repelling the Mongol invasions of the 13th century, the best known leader being Trần Hưng Đạo, and it ended when Hồ Quý Ly took the throne in 1400 (all from memory, not re-checked). The `vietnamese` field holds Hán-Việt Trần triều (trần 陳, triều 朝); Trần is also a very common Vietnamese surname.

The two dynasties are unrelated except by name. The Vietnamese Trần house is said to have descended from migrants out of Fujian or Guizhou (桂州, in Guangxi) (Japanese Wikipedia), which is why the surname is the Chinese 陳.

Attestation, checked 2026-10-11. English Wiktionary: 陳朝 as a Chinese proper noun with both senses, Chinese first. Japanese Wiktionary: 陳朝 (ちんちょう), Vietnamese dynasty only, 1225–1400. Japanese Wikipedia gives 陳朝 as both ちんちょう and チャンちょう. Chinese Wiktionary has no page (the simplified form redirects). hvdic.thivien.net has no entry for the compound; trần triều is built from 陳 and 朝. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Korean values are compositional. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄋㄐㄚㄨ unique across both words and characters).
