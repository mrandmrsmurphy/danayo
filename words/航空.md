## Notes
- [航](../characters/航.md) "sail, cruise" is separately legitimized by [[航行]]. [空 (char)](../characters/空%20(char).md) "empty" is separately legitimized by [[空]] itself. No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

航空 combines 航 ("sail, cruise") with 空 ("empty, sky") — "aviation."

Mandarin hángkōng, cantonese hong4hung1, korean 항공 (hanggong), and vietnamese hàng không were all already correct, fully compositional readings.
