## Etymology
- [辞](../characters/辞.md) "bid farewell, resign (with words)" + [令 (char)](../characters/令%20(char).md) "cause (causative)"

## Notes
Narrowly, an official letter formally severing a position (dismissal or resignation); more broadly, this extends to any formally composed letter or piece of flowery, diplomatic rhetoric.

Mandarin cílìng, cantonese ci4 ling6, and japanese じれい are all directly attested standard readings. Korean 사령 and vietnamese từ lệnh are both honest compositional readings (辞's own 사/từ + 令's own 령/lệnh); **found and fixed a genuinely missing "lệnh" candidate on 令(char).md's own `vietnamese` field**, which had only listed rarer variants (lanh/liệng/loanh) — "lệnh" is the standard reading already used elsewhere in the vault (e.g. [[命令]], "mệnh lệnh"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 치렁 diverges from Sino-Korean 사령 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄌㄝㄫ unique among both words and characters).

Citation on 辞.md was already correctly present. Neither 辞's `stand_in` (辞職) nor 令's own `stand_in` (令 itself) points to this compound.
