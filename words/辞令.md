## Etymology
- [辞](../characters/辞.md) "bid farewell, resign (with words)" + [令 (char)](../characters/令%20(char).md) "cause (causative)"

## Notes
Narrowly, an official letter formally severing a position (dismissal or resignation); more broadly, this extends to any formally composed letter or piece of flowery, diplomatic rhetoric.

Mandarin cílìng, cantonese ci4 ling6, and japanese じれい are all directly attested standard readings.
