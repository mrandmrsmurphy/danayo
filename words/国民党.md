## Notes
- [国](../characters/国.md) "nation" + [民](../characters/民.md) "people" + [党](../characters/党.md) "party" — Nationalist Party; most prominently the Kuomintang (KMT) of China and Taiwan

Wiktionary: Chinese 國民黨 "Kuomintang; Guomindang; nationalist party"; Japanese 国民党 (こくみんとう) "Kuomintang, Chinese Nationalist Party", and also several other parties (Japan Nation Party, Partido Popular of Spain, National Party of South Africa, Partido Nacional of Uruguay) and generically "people's party; nationalist party; national party"; Korean 국민당 and Vietnamese Quốc dân Đảng are the Han forms of "Kuomintang" and "nationalist party".

Wikipedia: the Kuomintang (KMT) was founded by Sun Yat-sen in 1894 in Honolulu as the Revive China Society and reformed in 1919 in Shanghai under its current name. It was the sole ruling party of China from 1928 to 1949, and retreated to Taiwan in December 1949, where it is a major political party today.
