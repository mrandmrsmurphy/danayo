## Notes
- [投 (char)](../characters/投%20(char).md) "throw" + [票 (char)](../characters/票%20(char).md) "ballot"

投票 is to vote, to cast a ballot. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 投票 (Mandarin tóupiào, Cantonese tau4 piu3) as "to vote; to cast a vote; to cast a ballot; to poll" and Japanese とうひょう as "voting". It lists Korean 투표 and Vietnamese entries. Compare [投票率](投票率.md), voter turnout.
