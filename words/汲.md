## Notes
- [汲](characters/汲%20(char).md) "draw water" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 汲 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

汲 means to draw water (reaching down into water with the hand).

Mandarin jí, Cantonese kap1, Korean 급, and Vietnamese cấp are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 汲's own stored citations exactly. Fixed literal `vietnamese: null` → cấp, added the missing `pos`/`japanese`(きゅう, on'yomi KYUU)/`hsk_level`(無, matching 汲's own "no HSK level" citation) fields, reformatted `characters` to a proper block-list with the "(char)" suffix. Checked the other ㄎㄨㄆ-reading character (湆) — its own `stand_in` is 名専字, not itself, confirming no homophone word exists here.
