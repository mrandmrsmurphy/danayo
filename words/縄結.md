## Notes
- [縄](../characters/縄%20(char).md) "rope, cord" + [結](../characters/結%20(char).md) "tie, knot" — "rope knot," a knot. Their own `stand_in` values: 縄's is itself; 結's is itself; none points here, so 縄結 is an independent compound.

縄結 is a knot: a tie made in rope, cord or string. Mandarin shéngjié (繩結) and Cantonese sing4 git3 are the ordinary words. Japanese says 結び目 and Korean 매듭, so those two fields hold the character readings. The character 縄 has the alias 繩, so the Chinese spelling is accepted. It is the physical knot, where [[締]] is the abstract "tying up." Compare [[縄]] (rope) and [[束縛]] (to bind).
