## Notes
- [列](../characters/列.md) "row, file, line up" + [車 (char)](../characters/車%20(char).md) "cart, car" — a "line of vehicles." No stand-in relationship applies (列's own stand-in is [[配列]]; 車's is itself).

列車 is a train: a string of coupled rail vehicles running as one unit, whatever draws it. It is the general word, the one to use when the kind of traction does not matter. Dan'a'yo has deliberately narrowed the other train words: [[汽車]] is the steam train or locomotive (the Japanese sense, not Mandarin's "car"), [[電車]] is the electric tram or trolley, [[火車]] is the Korean hwacha, a fire-arrow weapon, and [[鉄道]] is the railway as a system. The grammar's note on false friends (`文法 - 91疑義語`) names 列車 as the safe general "train" and [[自動車]] as the safe "car".

Mandarin lièchē, Cantonese lit6 ce1 and Japanese れっしゃ are listed on Wiktionary, and Korean has 열차 (written 렬차 here, in the North Korean reading the vault uses). Wiktionary notes that in Chinese 列車 means the train itself, not rail travel in general, so it does not replace 火車 in everyday Mandarin words such as "train ticket"; Dan'a'yo's 火車 is not the train, so that case does not arise. The Vietnamese liệt xa is the compositional Sino-reading and was not checked against a dictionary; everyday Vietnamese says tàu hỏa.
