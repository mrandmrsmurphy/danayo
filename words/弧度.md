## Notes
- [弧](../characters/弧.md) "arc" + [度](../characters/度.md) "degree" — no stand-in relationship applies (弧's own `stand_in` is [[弧線]], 度's is [[程度]]).

弧度 names the radian, the SI unit of angular measurement — an arc's degree, defined as the angle subtended by an arc equal in length to the circle's radius.

Japanese こど, Korean 호도, and Vietnamese hồ độ are all standard readings. Note that 弧度 is *not* actually a Dan'a'yo homophone of [[糊塗]] ("muddled, confused") despite both transcribing to "ho-do" in Chinese/Korean sources — 弧's own syllable is ㄏㄛ (ho), while 糊塗's first character 糊 is ㄏㄛㄨ (hou); 糊塗's correct Dan'a'yo reading is houdo/홋도/ㄏㄛㄨㄉㄛ, distinct from 弧度's hodo/호도/ㄏㄛㄉㄛ. A stray callout claiming a shared reading had been added to both pages in error and is removed here.
