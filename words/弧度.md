## Notes
- [弧](../characters/弧.md) "arc" + [度](../characters/度.md) "degree" — no stand-in relationship applies (弧's own `stand_in` is [[弧線]], 度's is [[程度]]).

弧度 names the radian, the SI unit of angular measurement — an arc's degree, defined as the angle subtended by an arc equal in length to the circle's radius.

Japanese こど, Korean 호도, and Vietnamese hồ độ are all standard readings. **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 弧度 and [[糊塗]] ("muddled, sloppy") share an identical reading — the reciprocal callout was already correctly in place on [[糊塗]]'s own page, just using a non-canonical format; standardized here.
