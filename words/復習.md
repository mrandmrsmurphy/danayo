## Notes
- [復](../characters/復.md) "restore" + [習](../characters/習.md) "practice" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (復's is 回復, 習's is 練習).

Mandarin fùxí, Cantonese fuk6 zaap6, Japanese ふくしゅう, and Korean 복습 are the standard readings. Vietnamese phục tập is documented in a Hán Nôm dictionary; the common everyday synonym is ôn tập.
