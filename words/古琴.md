## Notes
- [古](../characters/古.md) "ancient, old" + [琴](../characters/琴%20(char).md) "zither" — no stand-in relationship applies (古's own stand-in is [[古代]]; 琴 is its own stand-in).

古琴 names the guqin, the ancient seven-stringed Chinese zither.

Japanese こきん and Korean 고금 happen to be identical, real-world homophones of [[古今]]'s own readings (since 今 and 琴 coincidentally share the same on'yomi KIN and Sino-Korean 금) — but the Dan'a'yo readings themselves differ (古今's own 注音 is ㄍㄛㄍㄧㄇ vs 古琴's ㄍㄛㄍㄨㄇ), so no Dan'a'yo-level homophone exists. Vietnamese cổ cầm is a standard reading. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄍㄨㄇ unique across both words and characters).
