## Notes
- [李](../characters/李.md) "plum" + [子](../characters/子.md) "son, child"
- [[李]] is primarily used as a surname in Dan'a'yo and cannot stand alone as common vocabulary; this word serves as its legitimizer, carrying the meaning of the plum fruit.

The compound is the ordinary Mandarin and Cantonese word for the plum, written 李子 in both and read lǐzi and lei5 zi2 (Wiktionary); the suffix 子 is the common nominal marker that turns the bound tree-name 李 into the name of its fruit. Dan'a'yo adopts this shared sense unchanged.

Across the sphere the plum is named quite differently. Japanese has the native すもも (李), and 李子 is not an everyday Japanese word, so the vault's `japanese` reading りし is the Sino-Xenic reading of the two characters rather than a native term. Korean likewise has the native 자두, so 리자 is the Sino-Korean rendering. This matters for the design goal: a Japanese or Korean learner meets the plum under a different native name and must learn 李子 as new vocabulary, while a Mandarin or Cantonese speaker already knows it.

It sits in the same 子-suffixed fruit series as [杏子](杏子.md) "apricot" and [桃子](桃子.md) "peach", all named for a tree plus 子 in the Mandarin manner. See also [李朝](李朝.md), where 李 appears as the dynastic surname.
