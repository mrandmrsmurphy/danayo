## Notes
- [同](../characters/同.md) "same" + [胞](../characters/胞.md) "womb, born-of" — no stand-in relationship applies (同's own stand-in is [[同一]]; 胞's is [[胞衣]]).

同胞 means compatriot or countryman — literally "born of the same womb," extended to fellow citizens of the same nation.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `japanese` had a typo (どうはう → どうほう, 胞's own on'yomi HOU). Korean 동포 and Vietnamese đồng bào are both standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄫㄅ⼘ㄨ unique across both words and characters).
