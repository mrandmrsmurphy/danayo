## Notes
- [此 (char)](../characters/此%20(char).md) "this; proximal demonstrative" + [多 (char)](../characters/多%20(char).md) "many; much; numerous"

此多 is the proximal quantity correlative in the 多 ("amount") column of the correlative table: "this much; this many; this great an amount." Where [此処](此処.md) "this place" and [此時](此時.md) "this time" anchor an event spatially and temporally to the speaker, 此多 anchors a quantity to the immediate speech context: the amount being shown, felt, or just experienced. The matching question word is [何多](何多.md) "how much/many", so 此多 is the proximal answer to it. Its part of speech is 副用名詞 (adverbial noun), the category defined in `grammar/Linguistics Features.md` (line 68) as a noun that may be the topic or frame of a clause without case or adverbial marking, and which needs marking when it modifies the verb of the main clause. [多](多.md) alone is the stative "many; much" (人多 "there are many people"), and the 此 prefix turns that into a pointed amount. Illustrative only (invented, not from `grammar/`): 此多でいい "this much will do"; 此多の人が来た "this many people came".

The cross-linguistic fields hold the spoken equivalents, not readings of the two characters, since this is a Dan'a'yo compositional construction (tagged `neologism`). Mandarin 這麼多 (zhème duō): Wiktionary has no entry for 這麼多 itself, but its entry for 這麼 (simplified 这么) glosses it "this; so; such" modifying adjectives, with the example 這麼多人！ "So many people!"; the `aliases` hold 這麼多 and 这么多 for cross-reference, and neither has a word file of its own. Cantonese 咁多 (gam3 do1): Wiktionary glosses it "so many; so much" and, separately, "and no more" (as in 只係一晚咁多 "just one night"); it is not specifically proximal, so it is the nearest colloquial match rather than an exact one. Japanese これほど (korehodo): Wiktionary glosses it as an adverb, "to this degree, extent", and lists これだけ (kore dake) as a synonym. Korean 이만큼 (imankkeum): Wiktionary derives it from 이 "this" + 만큼 "the degree or extent to which" and glosses it "this much". Vietnamese *nhiều thế này* is from memory, not re-checked; hvdic.thivien.net covers single characters, not phrases, so it could not be verified there.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md)'s correlative table (line 121; the same chart is in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`, line 151, in the 多 row), 此多 is the eighth cell of the 此 ("this") row, after [此事](此事.md) "this matter", [此物](此物.md) "this thing", [此人](此人.md) "this person", [此名](此名.md) "this person (polite)", [此処](此処.md) "this place", [此時](此時.md) "this time" and [此様](此様.md) "this manner", and before [此類](此類.md) "this kind". Down the 多 column it pairs with [其多](其多.md) "that much" (medial), [彼多](彼多.md) "that much yonder" (distal), [何多](何多.md) "how much", [毎多](毎多.md), [某多](某多.md) "some amount" and [皆多](皆多.md). The table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), 63 forms, so 此多 is parsed compositionally: 此 = proximal, 多 = amount. The 注音 ㄑㄜㄉㄜ has no syllable-boundary dot: the only syllable-final consonants `grammar/文法 - 02音韻論.md` allows are the nasals ㄇ ㄋ ㄫ, the glides y/w and the unreleased stops ㄆ ㄊ ㄎ, so ㄉ cannot be a coda and the string has no second legal partition besides ㄑㄜ + ㄉㄜ. Dan'a'yo's sense is the shared CJKV "this much"; no narrowing is needed.
