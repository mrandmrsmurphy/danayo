## Notes
- [五 (char)](../characters/五%20(char).md) "five" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — the fifth day of the month, part of the same day-counting family as [[一日]]/[[二日]]/[[七日]]/[[八日]] elsewhere in this vault.

Japanese いつか (itsuka) continues the same fixed native-reading pattern already documented on [[七日]] (なのか), rather than the plain on'yomi concatenation ごじつ. Mandarin/Cantonese/Vietnamese (wǔrì/ng5 jat6/ngũ nhật) all use the plain compositional reading instead, with no equivalent irregularity. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 오닏 diverges from Sino-Korean 오일 in the second syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄛㄋㄧㄊ unique among both words and characters).
