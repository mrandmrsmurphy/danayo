## Notes
- [均 (char)](../characters/均%20(char).md) "equal; even; uniform"

均 is 形声: semantic [[土]] ("earth") + phonetic [[勻]]. It means "equal, even, uniform" (均衡, "balance, equilibrium"; 平均, "average, mean").

Mandarin jūn and Cantonese gwan1 preserve the sense directly. Japanese キン/イン (KIN/IN, on'yomi, fully productive: 均等, kintō, "equality, uniformity") alongside native なら(す) (nara(su), "to level, even out") cover the sense. Korean 균 (gyun) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese quân is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in quân bình ("balance, equilibrium," directly paralleling 均衡) and bình quân ("average, on average," paralleling 平均).
