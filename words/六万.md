## Notes
- [六 (char)](../characters/六%20(char).md) "six" + [万](../characters/万.md) "ten-thousand" — sixty thousand, 60,000.

Digit + 万, like [[二万]] and [[七万]]: the myriad (10,000) is the Sinitic grouping unit, so 60,000 is literally "six myriad" and is not built through a "thousand" step as in English. Japanese ろくまん is the plain concatenation. Korean 육만 follows the Sino-Korean reading directly. Vietnamese sáu vạn uses vạn, which is a live everyday numeral rather than only a classical one. Compare [[二万]] and [[七万]].
