## Notes
- [基](../characters/基.md) "foundation, base" + [準](../characters/準.md) "level, standard" — a reference point or measure against which other things are judged. Attested across Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, and Korean. `vietnamese: cơ chuẩn` is compositional from 基's own cơ + 準's chuẩn, but the real standard Vietnamese term for "standard, criterion" is tiêu chuẩn (標準, using 標 rather than 基 as the first character) — the same kind of character-choice mismatch already documented on [[名刺]]/[[国道]] (domain knowledge, not independently confirmed by search).
