## Notes
- [屏](../characters/屏.md) "folding screen" + [風 (char)](../characters/風%20(char).md) "wind"
- This word is the viable form of [屏](../characters/屏.md): the character's meaning (folding screen) is identical, but 屏 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 屏風 is its `stand_in` compound.
- Japanese reading びょうぶ is a fused reading, not the concatenation of component pronunciations.

屏風 is a folding screen: a freestanding panelled partition, often painted or lacquered, used to divide a room, block a draught or a view, or as a backdrop. Mandarin píngfēng, Cantonese ping4 fung1, Japanese びょうぶ (byōbu; the reading is fused, not the sum of the two characters' own sounds) and Korean 병풍 (byeongpung) are the same word. The characters mean "to shield" (屏) and "wind" (風), as in a barrier against draughts; the screen was also a status object, and in Korea the 병풍 is used at ancestral rites and funerals.

In Dan'a'yo it is the plain word for the object, placed with furniture beside [[幕]] (a curtain). No narrower or shifted sense is needed.
