## Notes
- [此 (char)](../characters/此%20(char).md) "this; proximal demonstrative" + [事 (char)](../characters/事%20(char).md) "act; matter; affair"

此事 is the proximal correlative in the 事 ("matter") column of the correlative table: it picks out a specific act, event, or matter near the speaker or already prominent in shared discourse context. The character 此 (Dan'a'yo *cǝ*, ㄑㄜ) is the classical Chinese proximal demonstrative ("this", labelled literary on Wiktionary, which lists 彼 as its antonym), preserved in formal and literary registers; it contrasts with 其 (medial, near the addressee) and 彼 (distal, away from both). 事 (act, matter, affair) nominalises events from everyday business to formal affairs of state. Together, 此事 means "this thing we are currently dealing with", the matter at hand.

The nearest cross-linguistic equivalents are: Mandarin 此事 (cǐshì), a formal-register phrase (the illustrative sentence 此事已結案 "this matter is closed" is invented for demonstration, not quoted from a source); Japanese このこと (kono koto), using the native adnominal この rather than the Sino-Japanese 此; Korean 이 일 (i il), the native demonstrative 이 + 일 (matter, work); Vietnamese *việc này* (this affair; from memory, not re-checked). Wiktionary has no entry for 此事 itself, so the word is a Dan'a'yo compositional construction, and the cross-linguistic glosses above are the spoken equivalents rather than attested readings of the compound. The pattern across these languages is that the literary sinographic demonstratives (此, 其, 彼) have been displaced in speech by native equivalents, while Dan'a'yo restores the classical forms as compact, cross-linguistically neutral correlatives.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md)'s correlative table, 此事 is the first cell of the 事 column, in the 此 ("this") row, followed along the row by [此物](此物.md) "this thing," [此人](此人.md) "this person," [此名](此名.md) "this person (polite)," [此処](此処.md) "this place," [此時](此時.md) "this time," [此様](此様.md) "this manner," [此多](此多.md) "this amount" and [此類](此類.md) "this kind"; down the column it is paired with [其事](其事.md) "that matter" (medial), [彼事](彼事.md) "that matter yonder" (distal), [何事](何事.md) "what matter," [毎事](毎事.md) "every matter," [某事](某事.md) "a certain matter" and [皆事](皆事.md) "all matters." The table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), giving 63 forms, and the grammar's preface cites Esperanto's regularity as a model for such a chart. Because the system is fully compositional, 此事 can be parsed and produced by anyone who knows its two component values: 此 = proximal and 事 = matter. Its sense is the same as the shared CJKV one; no Dan'a'yo-specific narrowing is needed.
