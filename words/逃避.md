## Notes
- [逃](../characters/逃.md) "escape, avoid" + [避 (char)](../characters/避%20(char).md) "evade, dodge" — "shirk, evade."

Mandarin táobì, cantonese tou4 bei6, japanese とうひ, and korean 도피 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese đào tị is the honest compositional reading (逃's own đào + 避's own tị); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 닷버 diverges from Sino-Korean 도피 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄨㄅㄝ unique among both words and characters).

**Found and fixed `characters:` disambiguation** (bare 避 → "避 (char)," since [[避]] has its own word page). Both character-page citations already correctly present. 逃's own `stand_in` is this exact word (逃避), so 逃 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 逃避; 避's own `stand_in` is instead 避 itself, so this word does not legitimize 避.
