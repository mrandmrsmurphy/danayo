## Notes
- [逃](../characters/逃.md) "escape, avoid" + [避 (char)](../characters/避%20(char).md) "evade, dodge" — "shirk, evade."

Mandarin táobì, cantonese tou4 bei6, japanese とうひ, and korean 도피 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese đào tị is the honest compositional reading (逃's own đào + 避's own tị); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found.
