## Notes
- [山](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain" + [口](../characters/口%20(char).md) "mouth"

山口 is Yamaguchi, written "mountain mouth". The reading やまぐち is native: 山 (やま, "mountain") plus 口 (くち, "mouth", voiced to ぐち), which in place names means an entrance or pass.

Yamaguchi occupies the western tip of Honshu, facing Kyushu across the Kanmon Strait, with Shimonoseki known for fugu. It was the old Chōshū domain, which supplied many leaders of the Meiji restoration, among them Itō Hirobumi. It holds the Kintai Bridge and the limestone plateau of Akiyoshidai.

In Dan'a'yo 山口 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 야마구치 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
