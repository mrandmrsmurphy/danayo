## Notes
- [週](characters/週.md) "week" + [刊 (char)](characters/刊%20(char).md) "publish, publication" — "weekly periodical."

Mandarin zhōukān, cantonese zau1 hon1, japanese しゅうかん, and korean 주간 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese chu khan is the honest compositional reading (週's own chu + 刊's own khan); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 줏칸 diverges from Sino-Korean 주간 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄨㄛㄎㄚㄋ unique among both words and characters).

**Found and fixed `characters:` disambiguation** (bare 刊 → "刊 (char)," since [[刊]] has its own word page). Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 週's `stand_in` nor 刊's own `stand_in` (刊 itself) points to this compound.
