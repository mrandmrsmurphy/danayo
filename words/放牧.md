## Notes
- [放](../characters/放.md) "release" + [牧 (char)](../characters/牧%20(char).md) "shepherd; herd"

放牧 is to let livestock out to feed on grass: grazing, or putting animals out to pasture. In Japanese it is both a noun and a suru verb (hōboku), and in Korean (bangmok) it names keeping livestock on open pasture rather than penned. It is the verb that goes with [牧場](牧場.md).
