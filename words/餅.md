## Notes
- [餅](../characters/餅%20(char).md) "pastry, cake, mochi" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

餅 means pastry, cake, or mochi — a flat baked or steamed dough item.

Mandarin bǐng, Cantonese beng2, Japanese へい, Korean 병, and Vietnamese bánh are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄅㄧㄫ/bing/빙) with the unrelated [[憑]] ("rely on, based on, evidence").
