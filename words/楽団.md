## Notes
- [楽](../characters/楽.md) "music" + [団](../characters/団.md) "group"

楽団 is a band or orchestra: a group of musicians who play together. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yuètuán as "orchestra" and Japanese がくだん as "band; orchestra". Korean 악단 is the same word. Cantonese ngok6 tyun4 matches.

They perform the [合奏](合奏.md), and the large symphonic body is the [管弦楽](管弦楽.md). A theatre company is a [劇団](劇団.md).
