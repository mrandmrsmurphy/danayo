## Notes
- [卍](../characters/卍.md) "swastika" + [字](../characters/字%20(char).md) "character" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [卍](../characters/卍.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (字's own stand-in is [[字]] itself, an unrelated word).

卍字 names the swastika symbol (a traditional Buddhist/Hindu auspicious symbol, distinct from its co-opted use by the Nazi party).

Fixed missing space in `cantonese` (maan6zi6 → maan6 zi6). Japanese まんじ (卍's own lexicalized reading for the symbol itself, matching its native kun reading), Korean 만자, and Vietnamese chữ vạn (reversing the word order from Chinese, the standard Vietnamese term for this symbol) are all correct. Dan'a'yo's own `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` (monji/몬지/ㄇㄛㄋㄐㄧ) deliberately diverge from all real-world readings of 卍 — a documented idiosyncratic Dan'a'yo choice on the character's own page, not an error (`kwin: false` correctly reflects this). No homophones (注音 ㄇㄛㄋㄐㄧ unique across both words and characters).
