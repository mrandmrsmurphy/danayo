## Notes
- [上 (char)](../characters/上%20(char).md) "above" + [衣](../characters/衣.md) "clothes"

上衣 is the upper garment, a top. English Wiktionary has an entry only for Chinese 上衣 (Mandarin shàngyī, Cantonese soeng6 ji1), glossed "upper garment; top" and "upper outer garment; jacket; coat". The Japanese じょうい and Korean 상의 fields are built from the readings of the two characters. In Dan'a'yo it is the general word for a garment worn on the upper body, of which the coat, [外套](外套.md), and the undershirt, [親衣](親衣.md), are kinds. The character [衣](../characters/衣.md) is bound, and its stand-in is 衣類.
