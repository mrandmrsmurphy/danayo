## Notes
- [距](../characters/距.md) "distant, apart" + [離](../characters/離.md) "separate, leave" — "distance." Mandarin jùlí, Cantonese keoi5 lei4, Japanese きょり, Korean 거리, and Vietnamese cự ly are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 교레 diverges from Sino-Korean 거리 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼄ㄌㄝㄧ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 距's own `stand_in` is this exact word (距離), so 距 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 距離.
