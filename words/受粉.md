## Notes
- [受](../characters/受.md) "accept; receive" + [粉 (char)](../characters/粉%20(char).md) "powder"

受粉 is pollination: pollen reaching the stigma of a flower. Japanese uses it as a noun and a suru verb (jufun), and Korean's standard form is 수분. Mandarin reads both 受粉 and 授粉 shòufěn, with 授粉 the more common spelling in mainland usage, so 授粉 is kept here as an alias.
