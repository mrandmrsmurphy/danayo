## Notes
- [和](../characters/和.md) "peaceful; harmonious" + [解 (char)](../characters/解%20(char).md) "explain; resolve"

和解 is a settlement: an agreement that ends a dispute, and more broadly a reconciliation. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin héjiě as "to become reconciled; to make peace" and, in the legal register, "to reach a settlement; to settle a dispute". Japanese わかい is recorded as "reconciliation; compromise; to be reconciled; to be settled". Cantonese wo4 gaai2 and Korean 화해 match.

The word covers both peace between people and a legal settlement between parties, which suits a page on law. It is distinct from [調解](調解.md), mediation, where a third party helps the two sides, and from [仲裁](仲裁.md), arbitration, where the third party decides. A 和解 is reached by the parties themselves, and it avoids a [判決](判決.md).
