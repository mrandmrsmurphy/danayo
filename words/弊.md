## Notes
- [弊 (char)](../characters/弊%20(char).md) "evil; wrong" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [弊 (char)](../characters/弊%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

弊 names a malpractice, evil, or wrongdoing — a 会意/形声 character depicting ruin brought about with both hands (弊害 "harmful effect, evil consequence," 疲弊 "exhaustion, worn-out condition").

Japanese へい, Korean 폐, and Vietnamese tệ (the character's own field also lists giẻ, a Nôm variant; tệ is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading, as in tệ nạn "social ills") are all standard readings. No homophones among other words (注音 ㄆㄝㄧ; the unrelated character [[批]] shares the same reading, but its own stand-in word doesn't collide with 弊's).
