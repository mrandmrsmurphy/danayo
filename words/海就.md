## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [就](../characters/就%20(char).md) "about, let" — 就 is used here purely as a phonetic-substitute graph for 鷲 ("eagle, vulture"), which has no character page of its own in this vault; both share the same real-Mandarin reading jiù. No stand-in relationship applies in the usual sense — this is a substitution coinage, matching the aliases 海鹫/海鷲.

海就 means sea eagle.

Mandarin hǎijiù, matching the real character 海鷲, is fully compositional. `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` (해줏/haijuo/ㄏㄚㄧ·ㄐㄨㄛ) deliberately diverge from 就's own stored Dan'a'yo reading (쵀/cwai) — a distinct Dan'a'yo-internal reading assigned to this substitution use, avoiding collision with 就's regular "about, let" sense. Filled the blank `cantonese`(hoi2 zau6)/`korean`(해취, matching 就's own real Sino-Korean citation, since 就 and 鷲 are genuine homophones in Cantonese/Korean)/`vietnamese`(hải thứu, using the real, distinct Sino-Vietnamese reading for 鷲 specifically — as in Linh Thứu Sơn, "Vulture Peak" — rather than 就's own tựu) fields. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄧ·ㄐㄨㄛ unique across both words and characters).
