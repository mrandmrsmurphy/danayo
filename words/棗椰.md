## Notes
- [棗](../characters/棗.md) "date, date palm" + [椰](../characters/椰.md) "coconut" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 棗 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 椰's own stand-in is [[椰子]].

棗椰 means date palm.

Japanese ナツメヤシ and Vietnamese trái chà là are the real, standard terms for this specific species (Phoenix dactylifera) in their respective languages, kept as-is. Fixed real bugs: `mandarin` had a stray extra "zi" suffix (zǎoyēzi → zǎoyē); `cantonese` was blank, filled compositionally (zou2 je4); `korean` was 대추, 棗's own native word for "jujube" (from its `korean_native` field), not the compositional Sino-Korean form — corrected to 조야. Removed a trailing invisible character from `japanese`. Removed `棗樹`/`枣树` from `aliases` (a different, unrelated compound meaning "jujube tree," built on the character 樹/树, not an orthographic variant of 棗椰), kept the genuine variants `棗椰樹`/`枣椰树`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄨ⼘ unique across both words and characters).
