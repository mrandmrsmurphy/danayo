## Notes
- [堕](../characters/堕.md) "fall, degrade" + [天 (char)](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven" + [使](../characters/使.md) "envoy, messenger" — "fallen angel, Lucifer." **Real content bug found and fixed on [[堕]]'s own page**: its `vietnamese` field was stored as "tuỳ," which is actually the reading of the unrelated character 隨/随 ("to follow") — corrected to đọa, the genuine Sino-Vietnamese reading of 堕/墮 (as in 墮落 đọa lạc, "to fall/degrade morally"). Filled blank `korean: 타천사`, the real, standard Korean word for "fallen angel," and `vietnamese: đọa thiên sứ`, compositional from each character's own (now-corrected) reading. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 돠턴시 diverges from the compositional Sino-Korean 타천사 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄉ⺢ㄊㄝㄋㄙㄧ unique among both words and characters).
