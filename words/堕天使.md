## Notes
- [堕](../characters/堕.md) "fall, degrade" + [天 (char)](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven" + [使](../characters/使.md) "envoy, messenger" — "fallen angel, Lucifer." `korean: 타천사` is the real, standard Korean word for "fallen angel"; `vietnamese: đọa thiên sứ` is compositional from each character's own reading (đọa for 堕/墮, as in 墮落 đọa lạc, "to fall/degrade morally").
