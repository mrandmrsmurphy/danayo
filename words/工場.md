## Notes
- [工](../characters/工.md) "work; craft" + [場](../characters/場.md) "place; venue".

工場 names a factory or workplace — a place (場) where work (工) is done, ranging in scale from a small workshop to a large industrial plant. It is distinct from [工廠](工廠.md), which stands independently (it is the `stand_in` that legitimizes the character [廠](../characters/廠.md)), carries its own Dan'a'yo reading (ㄍㄛㄫㄑㄚㄫ vs. this word's ㄍㄛㄫㄐㄚㄫ), and is the Mandarin/Cantonese-channel term for "factory" against this word's Japanese/Korean-channel term — see [工廠](工廠.md)'s own Notes and the Semantic Range Notes on [Work](../lexipedia/Work.md).

Japanese こうじょう, Korean 공장, and Vietnamese công trường are all standard, non-drifted readings.
