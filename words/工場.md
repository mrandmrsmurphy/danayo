## Notes
- [工](../characters/工.md) "work; craft" + [場](../characters/場.md) "place; venue" — no stand-in relationship applies (工's own `stand_in` is [[工作]], 場's is [[市場]]).

工場 names a factory or workplace — a place (場) where work (工) is done, ranging in scale from a small workshop to a large industrial plant. **Corrected a real bug (2026-09-21)**: 工廠 was wrongly listed here as an `aliases` entry, but it is a genuinely distinct word — it stands independently (it is the `stand_in` that legitimizes the character [[廠]]), carries its own distinct Dan'a'yo reading (ㄍㄛㄫㄑㄚㄫ vs. this word's ㄍㄛㄫㄐㄚㄫ), and represents the Mandarin/Cantonese-channel term for "factory" against this word's Japanese/Korean-channel term — see [[工廠]]'s own Notes and Semantic Range Notes on [[Work]].

Japanese こうじょう, Korean 공장, and Vietnamese công trường (verified) are all standard, non-drifted readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄫㄐㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).

**Correction (2026-08-27)**: this page had claimed to be "a homophone with [[工匠]]" — that claim was actually the symptom of a contamination bug on 工匠's own page (its Dan'a'yo fields had been accidentally copied from 場's reading rather than 匠's own); now that 工匠 is fixed, the two words are no longer homophones at any level, and the claim has been removed. This word's own fields were never affected and remain correct as originally stored.
