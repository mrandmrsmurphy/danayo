## Notes
- [黎](../characters/黎.md) "black" + [明 (char)](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright" — legitimizing note: 黎's own `stand_in` is [[黎明]] itself, already annotated in its own citation; 明's own `stand_in` is 明 itself, so transitivity fails — no `#cranberry`. "Black-bright" evokes the transitional moment between night and daylight.

Mandarin límíng, Cantonese lai4ming4, Japanese れいめい, Korean 려명, and Sino-Vietnamese lê minh are all standard, fully compositional readings.
