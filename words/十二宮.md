## Notes
- [十 (char)](../characters/十%20(char).md) "twelve" + [二 (char)](../characters/二%20(char).md) "palace; house"

十二宮 are the twelve signs of the Western zodiac: the twelve parts of the ecliptic, each named for a constellation. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin shí'èrgōng as "twelve signs of the zodiac" and the Japanese as a clipping of 黄道十二宮. Cantonese sap6 ji6 gung1 matches, and Korean 십이궁 is the reading of the characters.

These are the signs of [占星術](占星術.md), astrology, and are different from the twelve animals of the Chinese cycle, which belong to the twelve earthly branches, the [地支](地支.md). They lie along the [黄道](黄道.md).
