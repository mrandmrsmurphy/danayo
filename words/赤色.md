## Notes
- [赤 (char)](../characters/赤%20(char).md) "red" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — red.

赤色 is the X色 form of [[赤]], red as a color name in the older, more literary register. Mandarin chìsè survives mainly in fixed compounds and in a political sense ("Red," communist); Japanese あかいろ is the plain everyday color word, and Japanese also has せきしょく in the political sense; Korean 적색 is the standard technical and everyday term. In Dan'a'yo the everyday default for red is [[紅色]], with 赤色 for classical, literary or technical use.
