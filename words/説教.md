## Notes
- [説](../characters/説.md) "speak, explain" + [教](../characters/教.md) "teach, religion" — "explain the teaching," a sermon. Their own `stand_in` values: 説's is [[学説]]; 教's is [[教授]]; none points here, so 説教 is an independent compound.

説教 is to preach, and a sermon: the speaking of religious teaching to a congregation. Mandarin shuōjiào (說教), Cantonese syut3 gaau3, Japanese せっきょう and Korean 설교 all use it, and Vietnamese thuyết giáo does too; in everyday Chinese and Japanese it can also mean to lecture someone about morals. Compare [[宣教]] (to spread a religion) and [[祈祷]] (to pray).
