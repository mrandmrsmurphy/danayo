## Notes
- [約](../characters/約.md) "agree" + [束 (char)](../characters/束%20(char).md) "bundle" — 約's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 束's own is [[束]] itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 約束 legitimizes 約 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 束 is individually `kwin: false`.

約束 means a promise, agreement, or pledge — literally "to agree and bind," extending 約's core sense of an accord together with 束's "to tie, bundle."

Mandarin yuēshù, Cantonese joek3 cuk1, Japanese やくそく (yakusoku), Korean 약속 (yaksok), and Vietnamese ước thúc are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 約束 is itself a common attested word in all five languages (though the Vietnamese sense leans toward "restraint, restriction" rather than "promise"). No Dan'a'yo homophones.
