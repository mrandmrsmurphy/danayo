## Notes
- [除](../characters/除.md) "remove" + [非 (char)](../characters/非%20(char).md) "not"

除非 is unless. English Wiktionary has an entry only for Chinese 除非 (Mandarin chúfēi, Cantonese ceoi4 fei1), glossed "unless", "only if; only when" and "except; except for". The Japanese じょひ and Korean 제비 fields are built from the readings of the two characters.
