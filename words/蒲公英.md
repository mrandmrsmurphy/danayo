## Notes
- [蒲](../characters/蒲.md) "bulrush, cattail" is separately legitimized by [[香蒲]]. [公 (char)](../characters/公%20(char).md) "sir" is separately legitimized by [[公]] itself. [英](../characters/英.md) "hero" is separately legitimized by [[英雄]]. No cranberry.

Mandarin púgōngyīng and vietnamese bồ công anh (the real standard Vietnamese word for "dandelion") are both fully compositional and genuinely attested. Japanese たんぽぽ is the real standard Japanese word for "dandelion," not compositional — legitimate real-term substitution. Korean 포공영 is compositional.
