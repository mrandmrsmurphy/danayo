## Notes
- [討](../characters/討.md) "debate, discuss" is legitimized by this exact compound (its own stand-in). [論](../characters/論.md) "theory, thesis" is separately legitimized by [[理論]]. No cranberry.

Mandarin tǎolùn, japanese とうろん, korean 토론, and vietnamese thảo luận (all also the real common words for "debate") confirmed standard and compositional.
