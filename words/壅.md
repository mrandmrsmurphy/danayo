## Notes
- [壅 (char)](../characters/壅%20(char).md) "obstruct"

壅 means "to obstruct, block up" and, of plants, "to bank up with earth, mound soil around" (壅塞, "congestion, blockage"; 壅蔽, "to cover up, obscure").

Mandarin yōng and Cantonese jung1 preserve the sense directly. Japanese ヨウ (YOU, on'yomi, formal register) alongside native ふさ(ぐ) (fusa(gu), "to block, obstruct") cover the sense. Korean 옹 (ong) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese ung is a genuine attested Hán Việt reading (explicitly noted as an alternate by Thiều Chửu's dictionary), though the more commonly cited classical reading is ủng (hỏi tone), attested in ủng tế ("to cover up, obscure") and ủng trệ ("stagnation, congestion").

This word shares its reading (ong/옹/ㄛㄫ) with [[翁]] ("old man," already perfected).
