## Notes
- [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "ride" + [務](../characters/務.md) "duty, task" — literally "riding duty," the duty of serving aboard a vehicle (train, ship, aircraft) as crew.

乗務 names the specific occupational duty of crew members aboard a vehicle — 乗務員 (jōmuin, "crew member," the most common derived form) is the everyday Japanese word for train conductors, flight attendants, and similar staff. Mandarin chéng wù and Korean 승무 (승무원, "crew member") parallel this exact sense. Cantonese sing4 mou6 follows the same pattern.
