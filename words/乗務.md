## Notes
- [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "ride" + [務](../characters/務.md) "duty, task" — literally "riding duty," the duty of serving aboard a vehicle (train, ship, aircraft) as crew.

乗務 names the specific occupational duty of crew members aboard a vehicle — 乗務員 (jōmuin, "crew member," the most common derived form) is the everyday Japanese word for train conductors, flight attendants, and similar staff. Mandarin chéng wù and Korean 승무 (승무원, "crew member") parallel this exact sense. Cantonese sing4 mou6 follows the same pattern. **Vietnamese content note**: filled with thừa vụ, a compositionally sound derivation (乗's thừa, well-attested; 務's real Sino-Vietnamese reading vụ, extremely productive as in nhiệm vụ, công vụ, dịch vụ — not the mùa/múa currently stored on 務's own character page, which are unrelated native words and appear to be a separate bug on that page, flagged for its own turn), but no independent dictionary attestation of the exact compound turned up, so it is presented as a defensible derivation rather than a separately confirmed fixed term.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 숭무 diverges from Sino-Korean 승무, since 乗's own Dan'a'yo reading 숭 doesn't match Sino-Korean 승 (both independently confirmed on 乗's own character page), even though 務's own reading matches exactly.
