## Notes
- [方](../characters/方.md) "square, direction" + [言](../characters/言%20(char).md) "speech" — no stand-in relationship applies (方's own stand-in is [[方向]]; 言's own is itself).

方言 means dialect.

Mandarin fāngyán, Cantonese fong1 jin4, Korean 방언, Japanese ほうげん, and Vietnamese phương ngôn are all standard, fully compositional readings.
