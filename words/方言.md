## Notes
- [方](../characters/方.md) "square, direction" + [言](../characters/言%20(char).md) "speech" — no stand-in relationship applies (方's own stand-in is [[方向]]; 言's own is itself).

方言 means dialect.

Mandarin fāngyán, Cantonese fong1 jin4, Korean 방언, Japanese ほうげん, and Vietnamese phương ngôn are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` had all used the wrong initial (방/pang/ㄆㄚㄫ instead of 빵/fang/ㄈㄚㄫ, matching 方's own citation) — corrected. No homophones at the corrected reading.
