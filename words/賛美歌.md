## Notes
- [賛](../characters/賛.md) "help, support" + [美 (char)](../characters/美%20(char).md) "beautiful" + [歌](../characters/歌.md) "song"

Wiktionary: Japanese 賛美歌 (さんびか) "hymn; song of praise" and "hymnal"; Chinese 讚美歌, religion, "psalm; hymn", kept with 贊美歌 as aliases. It is built from [[賛美]] and 歌, and is narrower than [[賛歌]]. None of the three characters is legitimized here: 賛's `stand_in` is 賛助, 美's is itself and 歌's is 歌曲.
