## Notes
- [賛](../characters/賛.md) "help, support" + [美 (char)](../characters/美%20(char).md) "beautiful" + [歌](../characters/歌.md) "song"

賛美歌 is a hymn: a religious song of praise, above all the congregational Christian hymn (the Japanese hymnal is the 賛美歌集).  It is built from [[賛美]] ("to praise") and 歌 ("song"), and is narrower than [[賛歌]], the general song of praise.  The aliases 讚美歌 and 贊美歌 are the traditional spellings.

None of the three characters is legitimized here: 賛's `stand_in` is 賛助, 美's is itself and 歌's is 歌曲.  Mandarin zànměigē, Cantonese zaan3 mei5 go1, Japanese さんびか and Korean 찬미가 are the same compound.  No Vietnamese form is given, since none could be verified.  `kwin` is false because the Dan'a'yo 잔미그 differs from the Korean 찬미가.  The 注音 needs no syllable dot, and no other word shares it.
