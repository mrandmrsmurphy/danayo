## Notes
- [高 (char)](../characters/高%20(char).md) "high" + [低](../characters/低.md) "low" — no stand-in relationship applies (高 is its own stand-in; 低's is [[低下]]).

Wiktionary: Chinese "high and low; height; (music) pitch; superiority or inferiority; appropriateness, severity, weight; anyhow, no matter what; in the end, finally"; Japanese 高低 (こうてい) "high and low; rise and fall". Used in `文法 - 02音韻論` in the 高低原則, the high-low principle for pitch, and in the Cantonese and Japanese booklets.

The bare character 低 is bound in Dan'a'yo; its stand-in is [[低下]], so 高低 puts it beside 高 as a fixed pair.
