## Notes
- [徳](../characters/徳%20(char).md) "virtue" + [島](../characters/島%20(char).md) "island"

徳島 is Tokushima, written "virtue island", with 徳 the Japanese form of 德. The reading とくしま is the on-reading of 徳 (とく) plus the native 島 (しま).

Tokushima lies on the eastern side of Shikoku, with its capital Tokushima, known for the Awa Odori dance festival each August. The Naruto whirlpools, the Iya Valley and indigo dyeing are other features.

In Dan'a'yo 徳島 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 도쿠시마 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
