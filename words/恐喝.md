## Notes
- [恐](../characters/恐.md) "panicky; be afraid" + [喝 (char)](../characters/喝%20(char).md) "yell; shout"

恐喝 is to extort or blackmail: to obtain money or advantage from someone by threat. Wiktionary glosses Japanese きょうかつ as "intimidation, threat; blackmail" and "to intimidate, to threaten; to blackmail", and lists Korean 공갈. For Chinese it records only a Taiwan Hokkien sense, "to threaten, to intimidate", so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. The word is literally "to frighten by shouting".

Dan'a'yo keeps the Japanese legal sense, extortion and blackmail. It is narrower than [脅迫](脅迫.md), the general word for threatening or coercing, because 恐喝 aims at getting something by the threat.
