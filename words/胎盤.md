## Notes
- [胎](../characters/胎.md) "fetus" is separately legitimized by [[胎児]]. [盤 (char)](../characters/盤%20(char).md) "tray" is separately legitimized by [[盤]] itself. No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

胎盤 combines 胎 ("fetus") with 盤 ("tray, disc" — extended to "a flat supporting structure") — "placenta."

Mandarin tāipán, cantonese toi1pun4 (space removed), japanese たいばん (taiban, with rendaku), and korean 태반 (taeban) were all already correct, fully compositional readings.
