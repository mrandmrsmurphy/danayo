## Notes
- [沈](../characters/沈.md) "sink; steady" + [着 (char)](../characters/着%20(char).md) "settle"

沈着 is composed: staying steady and unflustered when things are going wrong. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin chénzhuó (沉著) as "cool-headed; composed; calm" and Japanese ちんちゃく as "calm, composed; emotional composure, calmness". Cantonese cam4 zoek6 matches, and Korean 침착 is the reading of the characters.

It is the calm that holds under pressure, the opposite of the flustered [狼狽](狼狽.md) and the [動揺](動揺.md), agitation. The calm of thought is [冷静](冷静.md), and the calm of bearing is [泰然](泰然.md).
