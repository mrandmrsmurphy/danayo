## Notes
- [彷](../characters/彷.md) "resemble" + [彿](../characters/彿.md) "be like"
- This word is the viable form of [彿](../characters/彿.md): the character's meaning (to resemble) is identical, but 彿 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 彷彿 is its `stand_in` compound.

彷彿 is a bound-pair word (a 連綿詞-type compound whose two characters are not used on their own) meaning "to seem like, to resemble vaguely" and as an adverb "as if, seemingly". Mandarin fǎngfú (more often written 仿佛), Cantonese fong2 fat1, Japanese ほうふつ (hōfutsu, usually written 彷彿 or 髣髴) and Korean 방불 (bangbul) are the same word. In Japanese and Korean it is typically used of a likeness that strongly recalls something ("recalling the past in vivid detail"); in Mandarin it is also a common colloquial "as if".

Dan'a'yo-specific sense: the same as the shared one. Against [[類似]] ("similar, analogous", a neutral relation between two things), 彷彿 expresses an impression of likeness or an "as if" and is the better choice for a vague, felt resemblance.
