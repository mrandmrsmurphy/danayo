## Notes
- [由 (char)](../characters/由%20(char).md) "from" + [来 (char)](../characters/来%20(char).md) "come"

由来 is origin or derivation: where a thing comes from, and how it got its present form. Wiktionary glosses Japanese ゆらい as "origin; source; to derive, to proceed from, to originate", and also "originally". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 유래 matches.

It is used for the story of a name, a custom or a word. It is about the line from the source to now, where [起源](起源.md) points to the beginning itself.
