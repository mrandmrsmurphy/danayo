## Notes
- [造](../characters/造.md) "make, create" + [語](../characters/語.md) "word, speech" — literally "make-word."

造語 means to coin a word, or the coinage/neologism itself — the vault's own name for exactly the kind of word-formation process this vault itself performs throughout its character-compound system.

Mandarin zàoyǔ, Cantonese zou6 jyu5, Korean 조어 (joeo), and Japanese ぞうご (zōgo) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 造's and 語's own stored citations. Vietnamese tạo ngữ is likewise compositional, from 造's tạo (one of two attested readings, alongside tháo) and 語's ngữ. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 찻요 diverges from Sino-Korean 조어 in both syllables. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄑㄚㄨ·⼄ unique among both words and characters).
