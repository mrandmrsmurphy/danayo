## Notes
- [光 (char)](../characters/光%20(char).md) "light" + [子](../characters/子.md) "particle, small thing" — "photon," the quantum of electromagnetic radiation. Mandarin guāngzǐ, Cantonese gwong1 zi2, Japanese こうし, and Korean 광자 all directly use this compositional compound; `vietnamese: photon` is instead the direct English/international scientific loanword, the standard Vietnamese term for this concept (rather than a Sino-Vietnamese calque). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 쾅즈 diverges from Sino-Korean 광자 in both syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄎ⺢ㄫㄐㄜ unique among both words and characters).
