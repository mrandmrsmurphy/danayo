## Notes
- [傲](../characters/傲.md) "proud, overbearing" + [慢](../characters/慢.md) "slow, sluggish; disrespectful" — "arrogant, haughty, overbearing" (傲慢な態度, "an arrogant attitude"). `vietnamese: ngạo mạn` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese, a well-known everyday term. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 앗만 diverges entirely from Sino-Korean 오만. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄨㄇㄚㄋ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 傲's own `stand_in` is this exact word (傲慢), so 傲 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 傲慢.
