## Notes
- [汚 (char)](../characters/汚%20(char).md) "dirty" + [染](../characters/染.md) "dye" — no stand-in relationship applies (汚's own stand-in is itself; 染's own is [[染色]]).

汚染 means to pollute, contaminate.

Mandarin wūrǎn, Cantonese wu1 jim5, Japanese おせん, and Korean 오염 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 汚's and 染's own stored citations exactly).
