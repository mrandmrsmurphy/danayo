## Notes
- [誦](../characters/誦%20(char).md) "recite, chant, repeat" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 誦 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

誦 means to recite aloud, chant, or repeat from memory — reading or intoning a text, prayer, or scripture (誦経, "sutra chanting"; 暗誦, "recitation from memory"). Its simplified form 诵 is a genuine orthographic variant.

Mandarin sòng, Cantonese zung6, and Korean 송 (song) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading となえる (tonaeru, "to recite, chant") rather than the on-readings ショウ/ズ (SHOU/ZU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese offers three stored candidates: tụng (the most standard, seen in tụng kinh, "sutra chanting"), tọng, and tộng. This word is part of a genuine three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read song/송/ㄙㄛㄫ): [[竦]] ("awe") and [[送]] ("give").
