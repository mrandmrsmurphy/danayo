## Notes
- [後](../characters/後%20(char).md) "after" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

後 means after — following in time or sequence, as in 後悔 ("regret," literally "after-remorse") and 今後 ("from now on").

Mandarin hòu, Cantonese hau6, Japanese ご, Korean 후, and Vietnamese hậu are all standard, fully compositional readings.
