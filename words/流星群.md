## Notes
- [流](../characters/流.md) "flow" + [星 (char)](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" + [群](../characters/群.md) "group"

流星群 is a meteor shower: many meteors falling in a short time from one part of the sky. Wiktionary glosses Japanese りゅうせいぐん as "meteor shower". The Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the characters, and Korean 류성군 matches.

It is a swarm of [流星](流星.md), shooting stars, and the debris often comes from a [彗星](彗星.md), comet.
