## Notes
- [果](../characters/果.md) "fruit, berry, candy, pastry" + [汁](../characters/汁%20(char).md) "soup, juice" — no stand-in relationship applies (果's own stand-in is [[果実]]; 汁's own is itself).

果汁 means fruit juice.

Mandarin guǒzhī, Cantonese gwo2 zap1, Japanese かじゅう, and Korean 과즙 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `vietnamese` was Nước sinh tố, a native descriptive phrase ("smoothie/fruit drink," not compositional) — corrected to quả chấp, matching 果's and 汁's own citations. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⺢ㄐㄜㄆ unique across both words and characters).
