## Notes
- [地](../characters/地%20(char).md) "land" + [下](../characters/下%20(char).md) "down, under" + [牢](../characters/牢.md) "prison" — literally "underground prison," built the same way as [[地下室]] "basement" above, none of the three characters' own `stand_in` points here (地's own is itself; 下's own is itself; 牢's own is [[牢獄]]), so 地下牢 is an independent compound. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: `characters/地 (char).md` is `kwin: false`, `characters/下 (char).md` is `kwin: true`, `characters/牢.md` is `kwin: false` → compound `false`.

地下牢 means "dungeon." Living Mandarin more often shortens this to bare 地牢 (dìláo), but Dan'a'yo deliberately keeps the fuller, symmetric three-morpheme form to parallel [[地下室]] rather than the shorter compound. Japanese ちかろう (chikarō) uses the same full form and is the standard word in fantasy/game/historical registers.

Korean and Vietnamese don't independently attest a fixed compound here: Korean more commonly uses the descriptive phrase 지하 감옥 ("underground prison") rather than a single Sino-Korean word, and Vietnamese uses the native hầm tối ("dark pit"). 지하뇌 and địa hạ lao are kept as the compositional field values for citation consistency.
