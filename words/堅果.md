## Notes
- [堅](../characters/堅.md) "hard, firm" + [果](../characters/果.md) "fruit" — "hard fruit," a nut. Their own `stand_in` values: 堅's is [[堅硬]]; 果's is [[果実]]; none points here, so 堅果 is an independent compound.

堅果 is a nut: a dry fruit with a hard shell and a seed inside. Mandarin jiānguǒ, Cantonese gin1 gwo2, Japanese けんか and Korean 견과 all use it. Compare [[果実]] (fruit) and [[種子]] (seed).
