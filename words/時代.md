## Notes
- [時](../characters/時%20(char).md) "time" + [代](../characters/代.md) "generation, era" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [時](../characters/時%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (代's own stand-in is [[世代]]).

時代 means time, age, or epoch.

Mandarin shídài, Cantonese si4 doi6, Japanese じだい, Korean 시대, and Vietnamese thời đại are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `cantonese` was missing its initial consonant (i4doi6→si4 doi6). No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄉㄚㄧ unique across both words and characters).
