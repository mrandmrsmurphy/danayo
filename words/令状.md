## Notes
- [令 (char)](../characters/令%20(char).md) "command, order" + [状](../characters/状.md) "form; document"

令状 is a written order from a court: a warrant, such as for an arrest or a search, or a writ. Wiktionary glosses Japanese れいじょう as "a court or judge-issued document ordering or permitting compulsory disposition; a warrant; a writ", and Mandarin lìngzhuàng as "writ; warrant". Wiktionary has no Cantonese reading, so the Cantonese is built from the character readings. The Korean reading is given in its North Korean form, 령장; the everyday Korean word is 영장.

It is the document that authorises a [逮捕](逮捕.md) or a search, and it is related to the [召喚状](召喚状.md), the summons that calls someone to appear.
