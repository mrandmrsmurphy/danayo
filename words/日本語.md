## Notes
- [日本](../characters/日%20(char).md) "Japan" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language" — no stand-in relationship applies (日/本's own stand-ins are themselves; 語's own is [[言語]]).

日本語 means the Japanese language.

Mandarin Rìběnyǔ, Cantonese jat6 bun2 jyu5, Japanese にほんご, and Korean 일본어 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese tiếng Nhật is the standard, everyday term (rather than a compositional 日本語 form, which is rarely used). In informal speech, this word is commonly shortened to 日語. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄧㄊㄅㄛㄋ·⼄ unique across both words and characters).
