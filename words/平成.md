## Notes
- [平](../characters/平.md) "even, peaceful" + [成 (char)](../characters/成%20(char).md) "become, accomplish" — literally "peace achieved," the era name for Emperor Akihito's reign (1989–2019).

Mandarin píngchéng, Cantonese ping4 sing4, Japanese へいせい (Heisei), Korean 평성, and Vietnamese Bình Thành are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 平's and 成's own stored citations exactly.

1. The Heisei era (1989-2019)
2. a well-made plan
