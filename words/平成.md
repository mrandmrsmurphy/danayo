## Notes
- [平](../characters/平.md) "even, peaceful" + [成 (char)](../characters/成%20(char).md) "become, accomplish" — literally "peace achieved," the era name for Emperor Akihito's reign (1989–2019).
1. The Heisei era (1989-2019)
2. a well-made plan

Mandarin píngchéng, Cantonese ping4 sing4, Japanese へいせい (Heisei), Korean 평성, and Vietnamese Bình Thành are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 平's and 成's own stored citations exactly. **Found and fixed a real bug**: `cantonese` was stored as "Bìng-sìng" — Mandarin pinyin-style tone marks and hyphenation rather than jyutping — corrected to "ping4 sing4."

`kwin` is false: both 平 and 成 individually diverge from Korean on their own character pages, so the AND-rule fails on both counts. No homophones (注音 ㄅ⼶ㄫㄙㄧㄫ unique among both words and characters).
