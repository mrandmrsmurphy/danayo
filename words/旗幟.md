## Notes
- [旗](../characters/旗.md) "banner, flag" + [幟](../characters/幟%20(char).md) "pennant, banner" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [旗](../characters/旗.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (幟's own stand-in is itself).

旗幟 means flag, banner, or (figuratively) attitude/stance.

Mandarin qízhì, Cantonese kei4 ci3, Japanese きし, Korean 기치, and Vietnamese kì xí are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄧㄑㄧ unique across both words and characters).
