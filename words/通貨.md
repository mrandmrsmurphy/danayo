## Etymology
- [通 (char)](../characters/通%20(char).md) "pass through, communicate" + [貨](../characters/貨.md) "goods, commodities, products"

## Notes
Synonymous with [[貨幣]] ("currency, money").

Mandarin tōnghuò and korean 통화 are directly attested standard readings.

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 通's `stand_in` (通 itself) nor 貨's own `stand_in` (貨物) points to this compound.
