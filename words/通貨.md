## Etymology
- [通 (char)](../characters/通%20(char).md) "pass through, communicate" + [貨](../characters/貨.md) "goods, commodities, products"

## Notes
Synonymous with [[貨幣]] ("currency, money").

Mandarin tōnghuò and korean 통화 are directly attested standard readings. **Found and fixed a typo on `cantonese`**: "tong1 fo3" had the wrong first syllable — 通's own stored cantonese is tung1, not tong1 — corrected to "tung1 fo3". **Found and fixed an invisible zero-width space (U+200B) inside `japanese`**: つ​うか rendered visually identical to つうか but contained a hidden character between つ and う, diagnosed via `repr()`; fixed to つうか. Vietnamese thông hoá is the honest compositional reading (通's own thông + 貨's own hoá). `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 통화 matches Sino-Korean 통화 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄛㄫㄏ⺢ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 通's `stand_in` (通 itself) nor 貨's own `stand_in` (貨物) points to this compound.
