## Notes
- [軟](../characters/軟.md) "soft" (stand-in: [[柔軟]]) + [口](../words/口.md) "mouth" (stand-in: itself) + [蓋](../characters/蓋.md) "cover, lid" (stand-in: [[覆蓋]]) — the soft palate (velum); the place of articulation for velar sounds.

Wiktionary: Chinese 軟口蓋 is an anatomy term, a synonym of 軟顎, "soft palate"; Japanese 軟口蓋 (なんこうがい) is "a soft palate"; Korean 연구개 is its hanja form. Used in `文法 - 02音韻論` as a place of articulation for the velar consonants. Counterpart: [[硬口蓋]].
