## Notes
- [軟](../words/軟.md) "soft" (stand-in: [[柔軟]]) + [口](../words/口.md) "mouth" (stand-in: itself) + [蓋](../words/蓋.md) "cover, lid" (stand-in: [[覆蓋]]) — the soft palate (velum); the place of articulation for velar sounds.

軟口蓋 names the soft palate — the place of articulation used for `文法 - 02音韻論`'s dorsal/velar consonants (ㄎ, ㄍ, ㄫ). Japanese なんこうがい and Korean 연구개 are both real, standard terms, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Mandarin ruǎnkǒugài and Cantonese jyun5 hau2 koi3 are compositional — Mandarin phonetics more often says 软腭 (ruǎn'è), so this is not independently verified as the standard Mandarin term. Vietnamese nhuyễn khẩu cái is likewise compositional only; the real term is "ngạc mềm" / "khẩu cái mềm." 口's and 蓋's own character pages store no `japanese`/`vietnamese` fields — なん/こう/がい and nhuyễn/khẩu/cái above are the real, independently-known readings, supplied directly. `kwin` is false: all three components are false.
