## Notes
- [秩](../characters/秩.md) "regularity, order" + [序](../characters/序.md) "series, sequence" — 秩's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 序's own is [[順序]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 秩 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

秩序 means order in the broadest sense — social, legal, or cosmic — from public order maintained by law (治安秩序) to the orderly arrangement of a system or process, distinct from [[順序]]'s narrower sense of a sequence or procedural order.

Mandarin zhìxù, Cantonese dit6 zeoi6, Japanese ちつじょ (chitsujo), and Korean 질서 (jilseo) are all standard, fully compositional readings, each the ordinary everyday word for "order" in its language. Vietnamese trật tự is likewise compositional and standard, used equally for social/public order (trật tự công cộng) as for orderliness generally.
