## Notes
- [外](../characters/外.md) "outside; maternal side" + [孫](../characters/孫.md) "grandchild" + [女 (char)](../characters/女%20(char).md) "female" — granddaughter through a daughter.

外孫女 is the daughter of one's daughter, a granddaughter on the mother's side. It is "outside-grandchild-woman," the pair of [[外孫]]. Mandarin wàisūnnǚ, Cantonese ngoi6 syun1 neoi5 and Korean 외손녀 are confirmed on Wiktionary. A paternal granddaughter is [[孫女]].
