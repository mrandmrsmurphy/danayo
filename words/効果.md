## Notes
- [効](../characters/効.md) "effect" + [果](../characters/果.md) "fruit, result" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [効](../characters/効.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (果's own stand-in is [[果実]]).

効果 means effect or result — the outcome produced by an action, treatment, or cause (効果的 "effective").

Japanese こうか, Korean 효과, and Vietnamese hiệu quả are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⼘ㄨㄍ⺢ unique across both words and characters).
