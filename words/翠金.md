## Notes
- A novel word coined for the [Periodic Table](lexipedia/Periodic%20Table.md), copying the etymology:
- [翠](../characters/翠.md) "emerald-green, kingfisher green" + [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal"

翠金 is a Dan'a'yo-internal coinage for terbium. The real element name honors Ytterby, a Swedish locality (shared with yttrium, erbium, and ytterbium), an etymology too opaque to render usefully as a Dan'a'yo compound. This vault instead names the element after a distinctive property — Tb³⁺ compounds are known for bright green luminescence, and 翠 denotes a vivid, saturated green (associated with jade and kingfisher feathers), more specific than generic 緑/青 — following the same fallback strategy used elsewhere (cf. [[硬金]], [[磁金]], [[吸金]]).

Since the real element name (铽/terbium) is a modern chemical-elements neologism rather than a word with deep independent roots in each language, the other CJKV daughters converge on international, European-derived borrowings instead: Mandarin tè and Cantonese tik1 transliterate "terbium" directly (via 铽), Korean 터븀 (teobyum) and Japanese テルビウム (terubiumu) both transliterate the European name, and Vietnamese terbi likewise derives from the European name rather than any Sino-Vietnamese compound. `kwin: true` per the AND-rule: both 翠 and 金 are individually `kwin: true`.
