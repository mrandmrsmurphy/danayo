## Notes
- [清](../characters/清.md) "pure, clear" + [教](../characters/教.md) "teaching" + [徒](../characters/徒.md) "follower" — "pure-teaching follower," a Puritan. Their own `stand_in` values: 清's is [[清潔]]; 教's is [[教授]]; 徒's is [[信徒]]; none points here, so 清教徒 is an independent compound.

清教徒 is a Puritan: a follower of the English Protestant movement that sought to "purify" the church. Mandarin qīngjiàotú, Cantonese cing1 gaau3 tou4, Japanese せいきょうと and Korean 청교도 all use it. Mandarin also uses it figuratively for a person of strict, joyless morals, close to a prude. Compare [[仮正経]] (prude) and [[基督教]] (Christianity).
