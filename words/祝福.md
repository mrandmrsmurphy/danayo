## Notes
- [祝](../characters/祝.md) "wish, celebrate" + [福](../characters/福%20(char).md) "blessing" — "wish a blessing," to bless. Their own `stand_in` values: 祝's is [[慶祝]]; 福's is itself; none points here, so 祝福 is an independent compound.

祝福 is to bless: to wish someone good fortune, and in worship to call God's blessing on them; also a blessing itself. Mandarin zhùfú, Cantonese zuk1 fuk1, Japanese しゅくふく and Korean 축복 all use it, and Vietnamese chúc phúc does too. It is the act of blessing, where [[福]] is the blessing itself. Compare [[恩寵]] (favor) and [[恩典]] (grace).
