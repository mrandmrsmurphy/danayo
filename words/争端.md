## Notes
- [争](../characters/争.md) "contend, dispute" + [端](../characters/端.md) "end, extremity, cause" — literally "the contested point," a dispute or conflict, especially the specific issue or incident that triggers one (挑起争端, "to provoke a dispute/conflict").

争端 is the standard term for a dispute or conflict, especially in diplomatic, legal, and territorial contexts: Mandarin zhēngduān, Cantonese zang1 dyun1, Japanese そうたん, and Korean 쟁단 are all fully compositional, though Japanese and Korean use it less frequently than Chinese, where 争端 is common in news and diplomatic writing (国际争端, "international dispute"; 边境争端, "border dispute"). **Vietnamese content note**: filled with tranh đoan, using 争's real Sino-Vietnamese reading tranh (as in đấu tranh "struggle," tranh chấp "dispute" — not the chanh/danh currently stored on 争's own character page, which look like a separate bug flagged for its own turn) + 端's own đoan; not independently confirmed as a fixed compound, so presented as a defensible derivation — everyday Vietnamese more often uses tranh chấp for this exact concept.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 장돤 diverges from Sino-Korean 쟁단, since both 争 and 端 independently carry `kwin: false` on their own character pages. This is a genuine, already-documented Dan'a'yo homophone with [[長短]] ("length; long and short") — 争/端 happen to be homophonous with 長/短 respectively.
