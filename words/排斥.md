## Notes
- [排 (char)](../characters/排%20(char).md) "row, rank" + [斥](../characters/斥.md) "reject" — 排斥 is the stand-in word for [斥](../characters/斥.md); 斥 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse.

排斥 covers both interpersonal/social exclusion (ostracizing a person from a group) and ideological/political rejection (排斥異己, "to exclude those who think differently"; 排斥外来文化, "to reject foreign culture") — historically associated with exclusionary movements and policies across East Asian history, from anti-Buddhist campaigns to xenophobic backlashes against foreign influence.

Japanese はいせき and Korean 배척 are both standard compositional Sino-readings. Vietnamese bài xích is likewise standard and especially common in historical/political register (bài xích ngoại bang, "to reject foreign powers"; chủ nghĩa bài xích, "exclusionism"). No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄚㄧㄑㄝㄎ unique across both words and characters).
