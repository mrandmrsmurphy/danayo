## Notes
- [排 (char)](../characters/排%20(char).md) "row, rank" + [斥](../characters/斥.md) "reject" — 排斥 is the stand-in word for [斥](../characters/斥.md); 斥 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse.

排斥 means to reject or exclude: to shut someone or something out, to ostracize. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 排斥 (Mandarin páichì, Cantonese paai4 cik1) as "to reject; to exclude; to ostracize", Japanese はいせき as "rejection, exclusion, boycott" and a verb "to reject, to exclude, to boycott", and Korean 배척 as "exclusion; ostracization; rejection". Vietnamese bài xích is glossed "to be critical of; to disapprove of; to criticize", which is a narrower, critical sense.

排斥 is the stand-in word for [斥](../characters/斥.md); 斥 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo.
