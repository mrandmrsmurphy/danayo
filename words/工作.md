## Notes
- [工](../characters/工.md) "work" + [作 (char)](../characters/作%20(char).md) "do" — `characters/工.md`'s own `stand_in` points to this word (工作), meaning 工 is a bound morpheme that never appears independently outside this compound; 作's own is bare 作.

工作 is chiefly a verb/action-noun ("to work; job"). Mandarin gōngzuò, Cantonese gung1 zok3, Japanese こうさく, and Korean 공작 are the standard readings. Vietnamese công tác is a real word documented in Hán Nôm dictionaries.
