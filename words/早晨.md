## Notes
- [早 (char)](../characters/早%20(char).md) "early" + [晨](../characters/晨.md) "early morning, daybreak" — this word is the stand-in for [晨](../characters/晨.md), which cannot appear independently.

早晨 means early morning. The character 晨 alone means early morning or daybreak, but is too bounded to stand by itself in Dan'a'yo, so this compound serves as its viable form.
