## Notes
- [喉](../characters/喉.md) "throat" + [破 (char)](../characters/破%20(char).md) "burst" + [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" — no stand-in relationship applies (喉's own stand-in is [[咽喉]]; 破 is its own stand-in; 音's is [[音楽]]).

喉破音 is the ejective, the "throat burst". English Wiktionary has no entry for 喉破音; it glosses Japanese 放出音 (ほうしゅつおん) as the phonetics term "ejective". The word is a coinage of this vault, so its Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and Korean 후파음 fields are built from the readings of the three characters. The plain outward burst is the [破裂音](破裂音.md), and the inward burst is the [入破音](入破音.md).
