## Notes
- [辛](../characters/辛.md) "spicy" + [辣 (char)](../characters/辣%20(char).md) "spicy, hot"

辛辣 is spicy, pungent, and figuratively biting. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 辛辣 (Mandarin xīnlà, Cantonese san1 laat6) as "spicy; pungent" of taste, and figuratively "biting; incisive" of wording, criticism and the like. It glosses Japanese しんらつ only in the figurative sense, "biting, incisive; bite, incisiveness". Korean 신랄 is built from the readings of the two characters.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [辛](../characters/辛.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
