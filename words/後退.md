## Notes
- [後 (char)](../characters/後%20(char).md) "behind" + [退 (char)](../characters/退%20(char).md) "retreat"

後退 is to move back: to retreat, or to regress to a worse state. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin hòutuì as "to step back; to move back; to retreat" and Japanese こうたい as "regress, regression, retreat, recession". Cantonese hau6 teoi3 and Korean 후퇴 match.

It is the opposite of [進歩](進歩.md) when it means falling back to a worse state. A gradual weakening of strength is [衰退](衰退.md), decline.
