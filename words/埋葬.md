## Notes
- [埋](../characters/埋.md) "bury" + [葬](../characters/葬.md) "funeral, inter" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [葬](../characters/葬.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (埋's own stand-in is [[埋藏]]).

埋葬 means to bury or inter — laying a body to rest.

Japanese まいそう, Korean 매장, and Vietnamese mai táng are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄇ⼶ㄐㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
