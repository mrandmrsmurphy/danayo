## Notes
- [墨](../characters/墨.md) "ink" + [水](../characters/水.md) "water" — "ink" (originally "ink-water," referring to liquid ink as opposed to a solid ink stick). Japanese すみ and Korean 먹 both use just 墨 alone (its own native-ized reading) for "ink," without needing 水 — the same pattern holds in Vietnamese, where `vietnamese: mực` is the standard everyday word, itself a direct native descendant of 墨's own reading (mực, mặc, mức are all listed on [[墨]]'s own character page). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 묵수 diverges from Sino-Korean's bare-character 먹 entirely — not a meaningful two-syllable comparison, since Korean doesn't lexicalize the full compound. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄨㄎㄙㄨ unique — confirmed via exact match; an earlier substring-only check falsely flagged [[目宿]], whose own 注音 ㄇㄨㄎㄙㄨㄎ merely shares this as a prefix).
