## Notes
- [墨](../characters/墨.md) "ink" + [水 (char)](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" — "ink" (originally "ink-water," referring to liquid ink as opposed to a solid ink stick). Japanese すみ and Korean 먹 both use just 墨 alone (its own native-ized reading) for "ink," without needing 水 — the same pattern holds in Vietnamese, where `vietnamese: mực` is the standard everyday word, itself a direct native descendant of 墨's own reading (mực, mặc, mức are all listed on [[墨]]'s own character page).
