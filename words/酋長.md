## Notes
- [酋](../characters/酋.md) "chief" + [長 (char)](../characters/長%20(char).md) "long"

酋長 is a tribal chief, a chieftain. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 酋長 (Mandarin qiúzhǎng, Cantonese jau4 zoeng2) as "tribal chief; headman; leader; chieftain" and "emir; sheik; raja". It lists Japanese, Korean 추장 and Vietnamese entries. Compare [首領](首領.md), a supreme leader.
