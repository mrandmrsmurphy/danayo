## Notes
- [着 (char)](../characters/着%20(char).md) "write; compose" + [者](../characters/者.md) "person"

着者 is the author named on a book: the person who wrote it, usually written 著者, where 著 is the variant of 着 used here. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin zhùzhě as "author; writer" and Japanese ちょしゃ as "author". Korean 저자 is the same word. Cantonese zyu3 ze2 matches.

It is the formal, bibliographic counterpart of the [作者](作者.md), and its work is the [着作](着作.md). The book is the [書本](書本.md).
