## Notes
- [𦜝 (char)](../characters/𦜝%20(char).md) "navel" — the character stands on its own as a word.

𦜝 means the navel or belly button, the mark on the abdomen left where the umbilical cord was attached. The character pairs the flesh radical 肉 with the phonetic 斉. Its traditional form is 臍 and its simplified Chinese form is 脐.

Mandarin qí and Cantonese ci4 preserve the anatomical meaning. Japanese ざい is the Go-on reading, while へそ is the everyday word. Korean 제 and Vietnamese tề are the Sino-Xenic readings. The everyday terms are Korean 배꼽 and Vietnamese rốn. [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/臍) · [Từ điển Hán Nôm](https://hvdic.thivien.net/hv/t%E1%BB%81)
