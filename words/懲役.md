## Notes
- [懲](../characters/懲.md) "punish" + [役](../characters/役.md) "serve"

懲役 is a prison sentence: a fixed term of imprisonment, originally with forced labour. The senses differ across the sphere. Wiktionary glosses Japanese ちょうえき as "time in prison, prison sentence" and Korean 징역 as "imprisonment; prison sentence", while Chinese 懲役 is recorded as a literary word for "forced labor; servitude". Wiktionary gives no pinyin for it, so the Mandarin reading is built from the character readings. Dan'a'yo takes the Japanese and Korean sense, a term of imprisonment.

It is the ordinary custodial sentence, between [罰金](罰金.md), a fine, and [終身刑](終身刑.md), a life sentence. The offender serves it in the [監獄](監獄.md) as a [囚人](囚人.md).
