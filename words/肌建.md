## Notes
- [肌](../characters/肌.md) "muscle; flesh" + [建](../characters/建.md) "build"

肌建 is a tendon: the cord of tissue that joins a muscle to a bone. The real word is 肌腱, which Wiktionary glosses as "tendon; sinew" with Mandarin jījiàn and Cantonese gei1 gin3. 腱 has no vault page, so its phonetic 建 stands in for it, with the same sound ㄍㄝㄋ; 腱 is listed as an alias of 建. Korean 기건 is the reading of the characters, and no Japanese reading is attested; Japanese uses 腱 alone.

It joins the [筋肉](筋肉.md), muscle, to the [骨](骨.md), bone, and is part of the [関節](関節.md), joint.
