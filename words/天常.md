## Notes
- [天 (char)](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven" + [常](../characters/常.md) "constant" — "natural law, the constant order of Heaven," the cosmic counterpart to human ethical order (as in the vault's own [[君臣]] entry, which lists the 天常/Five Cardinal Relationships as its human-ethics parallel). `korean: 천상` is compositional from 天's own 천 + 常's own 상 (the bound-form reading, not the 항상 reading). `vietnamese: thiên thường` is compositional; not independently confirmed as a fixed classical term (domain knowledge, not independently confirmed by search).
