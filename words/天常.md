## Notes
- [天 (char)](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven" + [常](../characters/常.md) "constant" — "natural law, the constant order of Heaven," the cosmic counterpart to human ethical order (as in the vault's own [[君臣]] entry, which lists the 天常/Five Cardinal Relationships as its human-ethics parallel). **Real content bug found and fixed**: `korean` was stored as "티안창," an apparent phonetic transcription of the Mandarin pronunciation "tiān" into Hangul rather than a real Sino-Korean reading — corrected to 천상, compositional from 天's own 천 + 常's own 상 (used in 항상 rather than bare 상, though 상 alone is the expected bound-form reading here). `vietnamese: thiên thường` is compositional; not independently confirmed as a fixed classical term (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 턴샹 diverges from the compositional Sino-Korean 천상 in both syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄝㄋㄙ⼘ㄫ unique among both words and characters).
