## Notes
- [冶](../characters/冶.md) "smelt, cast" + [錬](../characters/錬.md) "temper, refine" — a synonym compound generalizing over both components' more precise senses; no longer a `stand_in` for either, since [[鍛冶]] (smithing, casting apparatus) and [[鍛錬]] (temper, forge; train/discipline) now each carry the distinct nuance 冶錬 used to blur together — both, notably, via the same third character 鍛, which legitimately spans both senses on its own.

`japanese: やれん` and `korean: 야련` are both compositional from each character's own confirmed reading; `vietnamese: dã luyện` is likewise compositional — no independent attestation of this exact compound was found via web search in any of the three languages (everyday Vietnamese instead reaches for the unrelated-root luyện kim, "metallurgy," for this general concept), so all three are documented as compositional rather than fabricated usage. **Real gap found and fixed on [[錬]]'s own page**: its `vietnamese` field was blank — added luyện, the well-known Sino-Vietnamese reading (huấn luyện, "to train"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 야런 diverges from the compositional Sino-Korean 야련 in the second syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ⼘ㄌㄝㄋ unique among both words and characters).
