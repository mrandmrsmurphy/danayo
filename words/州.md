## Notes
- [州 (char)](../characters/州%20(char).md) "state" — stand-in for [[characters/州 (char)|州]], which cannot appear independently

州 is an administrative division — historically a Chinese prefecture/province (九州, "the Nine Provinces," an ancient poetic name for all of China), and today the standard translation for foreign administrative units: US/Australian "state" (德州 Texas, 加州 California), German/Austrian Land, etc. It survives richly in East Asian place names — 九州 (Kyūshū, Japan), 済州 (Jeju, Korea), 廣州/广州 (Guangzhou), 蘇州 (Suzhou) — where it marks a region's historical administrative status rather than a modern one. In modern Japanese orthography, 州 has additionally absorbed most of the work once done by its own phonetic derivative [[洲]] (see that page's Notes), since 洲 fell outside the jōyō kanji list after the postwar script reform.
