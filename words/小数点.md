## Notes
- [小 (char)](../characters/小%20(char).md) "small" + [数](../characters/数.md) "count; number" + [点 (char)](../characters/点%20(char).md) "point; dot" — no stand-in relationship applies (小's and 点's own `stand_in` fields point to themselves, 数's is [[計数]]).

小数点 is the decimal point — 小数 ("small/fractional number," i.e. a decimal fraction) + 点 ("dot, point"), the mark separating the integer and fractional parts of a number.

Japanese しょうすうてん and Korean 소수점 are both standard, non-drifted readings. **`vietnamese` is left omitted**: no Sino-Vietnamese reading was confirmed via Wiktionary or search — Vietnamese instead uses the native descriptive phrase dấu phẩy thập phân ("decimal comma," reflecting that Vietnamese, like much of continental Europe, uses a comma rather than a point as the decimal separator). No HSK level is given for the compound. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄛㄙㄨㄉㄝㄇ unique across both words and characters).
