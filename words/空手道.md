## Notes
- [空 (char)](../characters/空%20(char).md) "empty" + [手 (char)](../characters/手%20(char).md) "hand" + [道 (char)](../characters/道%20(char).md) "way" — none of the three characters' own `stand_in` points here, so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: all three constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

空手道 (karate) is the Okinawan martial art of unarmed striking and self-defense, literally "the way of the empty hand" — [[空手]] ("empty hand") plus the martial-discipline suffix 道 also seen in [[柔道]] (judo) and [[剣道]] (kendo). Japanese からてどう (karatedō) uses the native compound からて for "karate" rather than the Sino-Japanese くうしゅ seen in bare [[空手]].

Mandarin kōngshǒudào, Cantonese hung1 sau2 dou6, and Korean 공수도 (gongsudo) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese không thủ đạo is likewise compositional and is itself the standard Vietnamese term for karate.
