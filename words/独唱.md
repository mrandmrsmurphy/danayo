## Notes
- [独](../characters/独.md) "alone" + [唱](../characters/唱.md) "sing"

独唱 is to sing alone, or a vocal solo. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin dúchàng as "to sing a solo" and Japanese どくしょう as "vocal solo". Korean 독창 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese duk6 coeng3 matches.

It is the single voice set against the [合唱](合唱.md), chorus, and the singer is a [歌手](歌手.md).
