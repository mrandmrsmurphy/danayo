one's father's younger brother's wife ; any woman younger than one's parents

## Notes
`japanese: おば` documents the everyday native Japanese word (already present). `vietnamese: thím` is the real, native Vietnamese word for specifically the wife of one's father's younger brother — distinct from bá/bác gái, the wife of the father's *older* brother (paralleling [[伯母]]'s own bá mẫu) — rather than a Sino-Vietnamese calque of these two characters. Filled the blank `pos: 名詞`. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 숙못 diverges from Sino-Korean 숙모 in the second syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨㄎㄇㄛㄨ unique among both words and characters).
