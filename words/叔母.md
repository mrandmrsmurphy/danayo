## Notes
One's father's younger brother's wife; any woman younger than one's parents. `japanese: おば` documents the everyday native Japanese word (already present). `vietnamese: thím` is the real, native Vietnamese word for specifically the wife of one's father's younger brother — distinct from bá/bác gái, the wife of the father's *older* brother (paralleling [[伯母]]'s own bá mẫu) — rather than a Sino-Vietnamese calque of these two characters.
