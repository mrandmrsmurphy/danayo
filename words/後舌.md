## Notes
- [後 (char)](../characters/後%20(char).md) "back, behind" (stand-in: itself) + [舌 (char)](../characters/舌%20(char).md) "tongue" (stand-in: itself) — the back of the tongue, paired with [[前舌]].

Wiktionary has no entry for 後舌; it is a Dan'a'yo compound of 後 "back" and 舌 "tongue", the back of the tongue, used in the vowel table and the description of 広東語 in `文法 - 02音韻論`. Counterpart: [[前舌]].
