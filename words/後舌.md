## Notes
- [後 (char)](../characters/後.md) "back, behind" (stand-in: itself) + [舌 (char)](../characters/舌.md) "tongue" (stand-in: itself) — the back of the tongue, paired with [[前舌]].

後舌 means the back of the tongue, as used in `文法 - 02音韻論`'s vowel table. Japanese こうぜつ and Korean 후설 are both real, standard phonetics terms, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Mandarin hòushé and Cantonese hau6 sit3 are straightforward compositional readings. Vietnamese hậu thiệt is compositional only. `kwin` is false: both 後 and 舌 are individually false.

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot**: the concatenated string `ㄏㄨㄛㄙㄝㄊ` admits two legal partitions — the intended 後(ㄏㄨㄛ)+舌(ㄙㄝㄊ), and a false alternate splitting 後's own syllable (ㄏㄨ+ㄛ) — so the dot at the true 後|舌 boundary is required: ㄏㄨㄛ·ㄙㄝㄊ.
