## Notes
- [眉](../characters/眉%20(char).md) "eyebrows" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 眉 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

眉 names the eyebrow, a small but expressively significant feature across East Asian aesthetics and physiognomy — eyebrow shape and grooming carried (and still carry) cosmetic and even fortune-telling significance in classical culture. It appears in figurative compounds like 眉間 ("glabella, the space between the eyebrows," also a term for a furrowed, worried expression) and 焦眉 ("burning eyebrows," an idiom for an urgent, pressing matter).

Mandarin méi, Cantonese mei4, Japanese び (bi), Korean 미 (mi), and Vietnamese mày (as in lông mày, "eyebrow") are all standard, fully compositional readings.
