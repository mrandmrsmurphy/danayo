## Notes
- [航](../characters/航.md) "sail, cruise" + [行 (char)](../characters/行%20(char).md) "attend, go to"

航行 means to sail or navigate: to travel by ship, and in Chinese also by aircraft. English Wiktionary glosses Mandarin hángxíng (Cantonese hong4 hang4) as "to sail or fly; to navigate; to cruise", Japanese こうこう as "sailing; to sail, to cruise", and Korean 항행 as "sailing, navigation". The four readings agree, and the ship sense is the core.

航行 is the stand-in for [航](../characters/航.md), which is too bounded to stand alone. Its route is the [航路](航路.md), and the flight sense is carried by [航空](航空.md), aviation.
