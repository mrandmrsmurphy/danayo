## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [産](../characters/産.md) "produce"

海産 is seafood: the fish, shellfish, seaweed and other foods that the sea produces. The traditional spelling 海產 is an alias, since 產 is an alias of 産. Wiktionary glosses Chinese 海產 (Mandarin hǎichǎn, Cantonese hoi2 caan2) as "seafood" and Japanese かいさん as "marine products" (also, in zoology, "marine"). Vietnamese hải sản is "seafood", and Korean 해산 is the reading of the characters.

This is the Dan'a'yo word for seafood. [海鮮](海鮮.md) is a synonym of it. It covers the [魚](魚.md), the [蟹](蟹.md), the [烏賊](烏賊.md) and the [海苔](海苔.md), and what the sea gives is set against the farmland's [農業](農業.md), agriculture.
