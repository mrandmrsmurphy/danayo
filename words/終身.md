## Notes
- [終](../characters/終.md) "end" + [身](../characters/身.md) "body" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (終's is [[終了]], 身's is [[身体]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 終 is individually `kwin: false`.

終身 means a lifetime, or "for life" — spanning one's whole life, as in 終身雇用 (lifetime employment) or 終身刑 (life sentence).

Mandarin zhōngshēn, Cantonese zung1 san1, Japanese しゅうしん (shūshin), and Korean 종신 (jongsin) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 終身 is itself a common attested word for "lifetime" in all four languages. Vietnamese chung thân is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
