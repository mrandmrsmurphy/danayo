## Notes
- [古](../characters/古.md) "ancient, old" + [形 (char)](../characters/形%20(char).md) "shape, form" — no stand-in relationship applies: 古's own `stand_in` is [古代](古代.md) and 形's is [形](形.md) itself.

古形 is an old form, an archaic form. English Wiktionary has no entry for 古形 in any language, so this is a term of this vault, and the Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and Korean fields are built from the readings of the two characters. Compare [古語](古語.md), an archaism, and [古層](古層.md), an archaic stratum.
