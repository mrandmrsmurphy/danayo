## Notes
- [置 (char)](../characters/置%20(char).md) "put, place" + [換 (char)](../characters/換%20(char).md) "interchange, exchange" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both are stand-ins for themselves), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 換 is individually `kwin: false`.

置換 means to permute, substitute, or displace — most notably as the mathematical term for a permutation.

Mandarin zhìhuàn, Cantonese zi3 wun6, Japanese ちかん (chikan), and Korean 치환 (chihwan) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 置換 is itself a common attested word for "substitution, permutation" in all four languages. Vietnamese trí hoán is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard mathematical term. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
