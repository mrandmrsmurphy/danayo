## Notes
- [災](../characters/災.md) "disaster, catastrophe" + [厄](../characters/厄.md) "burden, trouble" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 厄 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (災's own stand-in is [[災害]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

災厄 means trouble, burden, disaster.

Mandarin zāi'è, Japanese さいやく, and Korean 재액 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 災's and 厄's own stored citations. Fixed a doubled/malformed `cantonese` value (zoi1 ak1, zoi1 aak1 → zoi1 ak1, matching 厄's own citation ak1). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with tai ách (災's own tai + 厄's own ách) — also the real, standard Vietnamese term for "calamity." Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄧㄜㄎ unique across both words and characters).
