## Notes
- [呑](../characters/呑.md) "swallow; gulp" + [咽](../characters/咽.md) "pharynx; swallow"

呑咽 is to swallow: to pass food or drink from the mouth down the throat. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin tūnyàn (written 吞嚥) as "to swallow". The alias 吞 is the usual form of 呑, and 嚥 is the alias of 咽. Korean 탄인 is the reading of the characters, and no Japanese reading is attested.

It names the act of swallowing as a whole, where [吐](吐.md) is the reverse, to vomit or spit out. The food passes through the [咽喉](咽喉.md), throat, and down the [食道](食道.md), esophagus.
