## Notes
- [推 (char)](../characters/推%20(char).md) "push; move" + [移](../characters/移.md) "shift; move"

推移 is to shift or pass over time: a situation moving from one state to another, or time itself going by. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin tuīyí as "to elapse; to pass; to develop; to evolve (of time, situations, etc.)" and Japanese すいい as "transition; undergo a change". Cantonese teoi1 ji4 and Korean 추이 match.

It describes a trend as it moves, such as prices over a year, and is the movement within a [変遷](変遷.md), the longer course of change.
