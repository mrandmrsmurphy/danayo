## Notes
- [蕃](../characters/蕃.md) "flourish" is legitimized by this exact compound (its own `stand_in`). [息](../characters/息.md) "breathe; multiply" is separately legitimized by [[気息]] — transitivity fails, no cranberry.

Mandarin fánxī, japanese はんそく, and korean 번식 (also the real common word for "reproduce/proliferate") all confirmed standard and compositional.
