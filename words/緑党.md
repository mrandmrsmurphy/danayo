## Notes
- [緑](../characters/緑%20(char).md) "green" + [党](../characters/党.md) "party" — "green party." Their own `stand_in` values: 緑's is itself; 党's is [[政党]]; none points here, so 緑党 is an independent compound.

緑党 is a Green party: a political party built around environmentalism. Mandarin lǜdǎng and Cantonese luk6 dong2 are the ordinary Chinese words. Japanese says 緑の党 or グリーン党 and Korean 녹색당, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[政党]] (political party) and [[緑]] (green).
