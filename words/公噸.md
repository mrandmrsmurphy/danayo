## Notes
- [公 (char)](../characters/公%20(char).md) "public, metric" + [噸 (char)](../characters/噸%20(char).md) "ton" — "tonne, metric ton." 噸 is itself a purely phonetic loan-character for English "ton" (per its own page, with no genuine native gloss in any daughter language), so 公噸's readings largely follow that same pattern. Mandarin gōngdūn and Cantonese gung1 deon1 are both directly attested standard terms for "metric ton" in Chinese-speaking regions.
