## Notes
- [窒](../characters/窒.md) "suffocate; choke" + [息](../characters/息.md) "breathe" — 窒's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 息's own is [[気息]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 窒 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word (already correctly noted). `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 窒 is individually `kwin: false`.

窒息 means to suffocate or asphyxiate — the cutting-off of breath, whether by strangulation, drowning, lack of oxygen, or obstruction (窒息死, "death by suffocation").

Mandarin zhìxī, Cantonese zat6 sik1, Japanese ちっそく (chissoku), and Korean 질식 (jilsik) are all standard, fully compositional readings, each the ordinary medical/everyday term for "suffocation" in its language. Vietnamese trất tức is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form; everyday Vietnamese more often uses the native nghẹt thở ("choked breath").
