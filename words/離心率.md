## Notes
- [離](../characters/離.md) "depart, leave" + [心 (char)](../characters/心%20(char).md) "heart, center" + [率](../characters/率.md) "rate, ratio"

離心率 is the mathematical term for eccentricity — the measure of how much a conic section (an ellipse, in particular) deviates from being circular, literally "the rate of departure from the center." Mandarin 離心率/离心率 (líxīnlǜ), Cantonese 離心率 (lei4sam1leot6), Japanese 離心率 (りしんりつ), and Korean 리심률 (risimnyul, already correctly using this vault's standing North Korean/문화어 form) all use this compositional mathematical term. Sino-Vietnamese instead uses the native-calque phrase độ lệch tâm ("degree of off-center deviation") rather than a Sino-Vietnamese compound.
