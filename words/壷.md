## Notes
- [壷 (char)](../characters/壷%20(char).md) "pot"

壷 is a pot: a vessel for liquids, a jar, a jug or a vase. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 壺 (alias 壷; Mandarin hú, Cantonese wu4) as "pot; jar; kettle; jug; vase; bottle; flask". Japanese つぼ, "container or depression", is written with the same character, and its Sino-Japanese reading is こ. Korean 호 and Vietnamese hồ are the hanja readings.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [壷 (char)](../characters/壷%20(char).md) as a free word. The compound [水壷](水壷.md), kettle, builds on it, and the narrower bottles and jars are the [瓶](瓶.md) and the [甕](甕.md).
