## Notes
- [電](../characters/電.md) "electricity" + [灯 (char)](../characters/灯%20(char).md) "lamp" — no `#cranberry`: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here (電's is [[電気]], 灯's is 灯 itself).

電灯 is the ordinary word for "electric light/lamp" in Japanese (でんとう, dentō) and Korean 전등 (jeondeung), both following the Sino-order 電+灯/燈 compound directly. Mandarin 電燈/电灯 (diàndēng) and Cantonese 電燈 (din6dang1) are likewise the standard everyday words for an electric lamp/light bulb fixture.

Vietnamese instead uses the native word-order-reversed đèn điện ("lamp, electric" — literally "lamp" + "electric," modifier following the noun) rather than a Sino-Vietnamese-order **điện đăng**; the latter is not an attested live compound in Vietnamese (đăng in isolation now reads mainly as "to post/publish/register" in everyday use, with "lamp" surviving only in fixed Sino-Vietnamese compounds like đăng ký). This is another instance of the same word-order-reversal pattern already seen in [[限定詞]] (→ từ hạn định) and [[除外]] (→ ngoại trừ): modern Vietnamese grammatical/everyday vocabulary built from the same morphemes as the Chinese-order compound but reordered into a native noun-then-modifier pattern.
