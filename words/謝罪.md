## Notes
- [謝](../characters/謝.md) "thank; apologize" + [罪 (char)](../characters/罪%20(char).md) "crime; fault"

謝罪 is an apology: to admit a fault and ask pardon for it. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xièzuì as "to acknowledge one's error and seek forgiveness" and Japanese しゃざい as "apology; to apologize". Vietnamese tạ tội is the same word, and Korean 사죄 and Cantonese ze6 zeoi6 match.

It is the formal, serious apology for a wrong, and it comes with the amends of [賠償](賠償.md). It is the step toward [和解](和解.md), reconciliation.
