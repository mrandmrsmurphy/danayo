## Notes
- [市](../characters/市.md) "city" + [民](../characters/民.md) "people"

市民 is a citizen in the sense of a city dweller: a member of the community of a town or city. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin shìmín as "city resident; townsperson" and Japanese しみん as "citizen(s); townspeople". Vietnamese thị dân is the same word, and Korean 시민 and Cantonese si5 man4 match.

It stresses life in a place and a share in its public affairs. The legal member of a state is the [公民](公民.md), and the nationals of a state taken together are the [国民](国民.md).
