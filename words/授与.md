## Notes
- [授](../characters/授.md) "confer, award, grant" + [与](../characters/与%20(char).md) "give" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [授](../characters/授.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (与's own stand-in is itself).

授与 means to confer, award, or grant.

Mandarin shòuyǔ, Cantonese sau6 jyu5, Japanese じゅよ, Korean 수여, and Vietnamese thò và are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼜⼄ unique across both words and characters).
