## Notes
- [鳩](../characters/鳩.md) "pigeon" + [鳥 (char)](../characters/鳥%20(char).md) "bird" — legitimizing note: 鳩's own `stand_in` is [[鳩鳥]] itself, already annotated in its own citation; 鳥's own `stand_in` is 鳥 itself, so transitivity fails — no `#cranberry`.

**Fixed real bugs**: `mandarin` was the bare single-syllable `jiū` (missing 鳥's own reading entirely) — corrected to the compositional jiūniǎo. `cantonese` comma-joined an extra, unexplained "kau1" alongside the correct gau1, and was missing 鳥's own niu5 entirely — corrected to gau1 niu5. `japanese` comma-joined the correct native はと with an unrecognizable "きう" (matching none of 鳩's own listed on'yomi KYUU/KU) — dropped, keeping just はと, the real everyday word. `korean: 비둘기` was already correct (matching 鳩's own `korean_native` field — the native word has become the standard everyday term). `vietnamese` was "Bồ câu nhà" ("domestic pigeon," over-specified relative to this word's plain "pigeon" sense) — simplified to bồ câu.
