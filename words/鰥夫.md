## Notes
- [鰥](../characters/鰥.md) "widower" + [夫 (char)](../characters/夫%20(char).md) "husband" — widower; this word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character 鰥, which is too bound to appear alone.

鰥夫 is a widower: a man whose wife has died and who has not remarried, the male pair of [[寡婦]] (widow). Mandarin guānfū and Cantonese gwaan1 fu1 are confirmed on Wiktionary, as is Japanese かんぷ; Japanese also says やもめ and, archaically, やもお. Wiktionary has no Korean or Vietnamese entry for the compound, so those fields are left blank. In Mencius the lone word 鰥 names an old man without a wife, and it is the first of the four classes of the destitute in [[鰥寡孤独]].
