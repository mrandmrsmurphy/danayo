## Notes
- [斑](../characters/斑.md) "spotted" + [点](../characters/点%20(char).md) "dot, point" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [斑](../characters/斑.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (点's own stand-in is itself).

斑点 means spot or speck.

Mandarin bāndiǎn, Cantonese baan1 dim2, Japanese はんてん, Korean 반점, and Vietnamese ban điểm are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄋㄉㄝㄇ unique across both words and characters).
