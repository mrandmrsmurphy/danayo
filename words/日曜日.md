## Notes
- [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "sun" + [曜](../characters/曜.md) "day of the week" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — literally "sun-day-day," Sunday, following the Sino-Japanese seven-day planetary-week naming shared across the sphere. Also cross-references [[曜日]] ("day of the week" generically), the meaningful sub-compound formed by the latter two characters.

Japanese にちようび (nichiyōbi) and Korean 일요일 are both standard, fully compositional readings, matching 日's and 曜's own stored citations exactly. **Found and fixed a real bug**: `mandarin`/`cantonese` had been stored as literal compositional readings of 日曜日's own characters (rìyàorì / jat6 jiu6 jat6) — but that's not how Mandarin or Cantonese actually name Sunday; like the other weekdays, both languages use the real 星期 ("week") system instead, corrected here to xīngqītiān / sing1 kei4 jat6 (星期天, already recorded in this word's own `aliases`, alongside the older 禮拜日/礼拜日). Vietnamese Chủ nhật ("Lord's day," from Catholic missionary influence) is unrelated in form to either naming system — the real native Vietnamese word for Sunday, not a compositional guess.

`kwin` is false: 日 alone is kwin-true, but 曜 independently diverges from Korean on its own character page, which alone is enough to make the AND-rule false. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄧㄊ·⼄ㄨㄋㄧㄊ unique among both words and characters).
