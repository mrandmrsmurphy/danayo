## Notes
- [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "sun" + [曜](../characters/曜.md) "day of the week" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — literally "sun-day-day," Sunday, following the Sino-Japanese seven-day planetary-week naming shared across the sphere. Also cross-references [[曜日]] ("day of the week" generically), the meaningful sub-compound formed by the latter two characters.

Japanese にちようび (nichiyōbi) and Korean 일요일 are both standard, fully compositional readings, matching 日's and 曜's own stored citations exactly. Mandarin xīngqītiān and Cantonese sing1 kei4 jat6 use the real 星期 ("week") system rather than a compositional reading of 日曜日's own characters (星期天 is recorded in `aliases`, alongside the older 禮拜日/礼拜日). Vietnamese Chủ nhật ("Lord's day," from Catholic missionary influence) is unrelated in form to either naming system — the real native Vietnamese word for Sunday, not a compositional guess.
