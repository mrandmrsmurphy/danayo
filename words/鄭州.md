## Notes
- [鄭](../characters/鄭.md) "Zheng" + [州](../characters/州%20(char).md) "state" — "Zheng prefecture". No stand-in relationship applies (鄭's is [[鄭国]]; 州 is its own stand-in).

鄭州 (Zhengzhou) is the capital of Henan province in central China (see [河南](河南.md)). Wiktionary notes that the city was renamed from 滎州 in 583; the 鄭 is the old state name (see [鄭国](鄭国.md)), the Zheng of the Zhou period in what is now Henan. 郑 is an alias of 鄭, so 郑州 is kept as the word's alias.

Japanese ていしゅう is the Sino-Japanese reading listed on Wiktionary Japanese; Japanese Wikipedia gives the same. Korean 정주 is the North Korean reading derived from the character pages (English Wiktionary has no Korean entry for 鄭州); the usual South Korean name today is 정저우 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Trịnh Châu is attested on Vietnamese Wikipedia. Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese and Japanese entries. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄫㄐㄨㄛ unique across both words and characters).
