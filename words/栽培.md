## Notes
- [栽](../characters/栽.md) "plant, cultivate" + [培](../characters/培.md) "cultivate"
- [[栽]] has boundedness 80 and cannot stand alone in Dan'a'yo; this word serves as its legitimizer, carrying the same meaning as the bare character.

Both characters mean to cultivate, so the compound is a synonym pair. Wiktionary gives three senses for Mandarin zāipéi: to cultivate or grow plants, to foster or train talent, and to advance someone's career. Cantonese zoi1 pui4 and Japanese さいばい (also a suru-verb) match Wiktionary, and Korean 재배 is the Sino-Korean reading.

In Dan'a'yo the main sense is the agricultural one, to grow plants on purpose, which suits the placement on the [Nature](../lexipedia/Nature.md) page as farming and on [Life](../lexipedia/Life.md) as growth. The figurative sense of nurturing a person is attested for Mandarin and is secondary. The sister word [培養](培養.md) is the stand-in for the bound 培.
