## Notes
- [九 (char)](characters/九%20(char).md) "nine(th)" + [月 (char)](characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — September, the ninth month.

Mandarin jiǔyuè, Cantonese gau2 jut6, Korean 구월, and Vietnamese tháng chín (native "month nine," the everyday way Vietnamese names months) are all standard, fully compositional. Japanese くがつ (kugatsu) is a genuine irregularity worth flagging: Japanese month names generally use きゅう for 九 elsewhere, but 九月 specifically retains the older on'yomi く — the same pattern seen in 四月 (しがつ, not よんがつ) and 七月 (しちがつ), where a handful of month names preserve archaic readings rather than the productive modern on'yomi.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 큐웓 diverges from Sino-Korean 구월, since both 九 and 月 independently carry `kwin: false` on their own character pages.
