## Notes
- [昭](../characters/昭%20(char).md) "shine, luminous" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [昭](../characters/昭%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

昭 means to shine or be luminous.

Mandarin zhāo, Cantonese ciu1, Japanese しょう, Korean 소, and Vietnamese chiêu are all standard, fully compositional readings. Three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all jou/좃/ㄐㄛㄨ) with [[焦]] ("burned; scorched") and [[照]] ("shine") — checked six other candidates sharing this reading (俎/沼/椒/藻/蕉/詔) and confirmed none has an independent word at this exact reading.
