## Notes
- [平](../characters/平.md) "flat, ordinary" + [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year"

平年 is a common year: an ordinary calendar year, as against a [[閏年]], leap year. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 平年 (Mandarin píngnián, Cantonese ping4 nin4) as "common year", and Japanese へいねん as "common year" in the calendar sense (its example is that 1900 was a common year but 2000 a leap year), as a "normal, average year" in climate (the first snow fell earlier than in average years), and, in agriculture, an "average year; neither a rich year nor a poor year". Korean 평년 is listed under the hanja form as "common year". It has no Vietnamese entry, so Vietnamese bình niên here is built from the characters.

The two characters are 平, flat or ordinary, and the 年 of a year. The leap month of the lunisolar calendar is the [[閏月]].
