## Notes
- [平](../characters/平.md) "flat, ordinary" + [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year"

平年 names an ordinary calendar year of 365 days, as opposed to [[閏年]] (leap year, 366 days with its extra day in February in the Gregorian calendar). In the older lunisolar context, 平年 instead contrasts with a leap year that inserts a whole extra month (see [[閏月]]) to keep the calendar synchronized with the seasons — the same "ordinary vs. adjusted" logic applied to a different underlying calendar system.

Japanese へいねん and Korean 평년 are both standard compositional Sino-readings. Vietnamese bình niên is likewise standard, contrasting with nhuận niên ("leap year"). No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄅ⼶ㄫㄋㄝㄋ unique across both words and characters).
