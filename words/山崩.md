## Notes
- [山 (char)](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain" + [崩 (char)](../characters/崩%20(char).md) "crumble; disintegrate; collapse" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 山's and 崩's own `stand_in` fields point to themselves).

山崩 names a landslide or mountainside collapse — specifically rock/earth sliding, as distinguished from a snow avalanche.

Japanese やまくずれ is a native kun'yomi-based reading (the character compound is also written with okurigana as 山崩れ, listed here as the alias). Korean 산붕 is a real dictionary term, though modern everyday Korean prefers the broader native term 산사태 ("landslide," covering all forms of slope failure) instead. **`vietnamese` is left omitted**: no standing Vietnamese term for this exact compound was confirmed — Vietnamese uses the native sạt lở núi ("mountain landslide") instead. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄚㄋㄅㄨㄫ unique across both words and characters).
