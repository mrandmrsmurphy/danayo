## Notes
- [君](../characters/君%20(char).md) "ruler, gentleman" + [等](../characters/等%20(char).md) "and others, class" — no stand-in relationship applies (both characters are independent Dan'a'yo entries, each its own stand-in).

君等 is a literary/classical second-person plural pronoun, "you (plural)," an honorific-plural form built from 君 "sir" + 等 "and others."

Filled blank `mandarin: jūnděng` / `cantonese: gwan1 dang2` / `japanese: くんとう` (all compositional). Korean 너희 and Vietnamese bay/chúng mày/tụi mày are native colloquial second-person plural pronouns rather than compositional Sino-readings — the everyday equivalent, since 君等 itself is a literary form without a corresponding everyday Sino-Korean/Sino-Vietnamese compound in common use. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄨㄋㄉㄨㄫ unique across both words and characters).
