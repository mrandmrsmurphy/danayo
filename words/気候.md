## Notes
- [気 (char)](../characters/気%20(char).md) "feeling, energy" + [候](../characters/候.md) "climate, expectation" — this word is the viable form of [候](../characters/候.md): the character's meaning (climate) is identical, but 候 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 気候 is its `stand_in` compound.

気候 means climate: the typical weather pattern of an area over the long run. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 氣候 (an alias; Mandarin qìhòu, Cantonese hei3 hau6) as "climate (an area's typical weather pattern)" (climate change), "climate (a particular situation)" (political climate), and, in literary use, the solar terms, "season", "weather", "prospects", "style or tone", and a person's "appearance and bearing". Japanese きこう is "climate" in meteorology, and Korean 기후 is "climate (long-term atmospheric conditions)". Vietnamese khí hậu is "climate" in meteorology. The Dan'a'yo sense is the meteorological one.

気候 is the stand-in for [候](../characters/候.md), which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo.
