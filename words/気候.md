## Notes
- [気 (char)](../characters/気%20(char).md) "feeling, energy" + [候](../characters/候.md) "climate, expectation" — this word is the viable form of [候](../characters/候.md): the character's meaning (climate) is identical, but 候 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 気候 is its `stand_in` compound.

気候 names climate — the long-run pattern of weather conditions for a region — as distinct from day-to-day 天気/天候 (weather). 熱帯気候 ("tropical climate") and 温帯気候 ("temperate climate") are standard classificatory compounds built on this word; 気候変動 (climate change) is its most prominent modern extension.

Japanese きこう, Korean 기후, and Vietnamese khí hậu are all standard compositional Sino-readings. No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄧㄜㄏㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).
