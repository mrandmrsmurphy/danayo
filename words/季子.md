## Notes
- [季](../characters/季.md) "youngest (of siblings)" + [子](../characters/子.md) "child" — youngest son.

季子 is the youngest son. It is "youngest-child," the classical genealogical term that completes the rank series with [[長子]] (eldest) and [[中子]] (middle). The evidence is thin: Mandarin jìzǐ (a dated noun, "youngest son; baby son") and Cantonese gwai3 zi2 are confirmed on Wiktionary, and Japanese きし is given as "last child"; Korean and Vietnamese are not confirmed, so those fields are left blank. The sex-neutral word for the youngest child is [[末子]].
