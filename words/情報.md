## Notes
- [情](../characters/情.md) "feelings, emotion" + [報](../characters/報%20(char).md) "report" — no stand-in relationship applies (情's own stand-in is [[感情]]; 報's own is itself).

情報 means information or intelligence — data or knowledge, especially in a strategic/military sense.

Mandarin qíngbào, Cantonese cing4 bou3, Japanese じょうほう, Korean 정보, and Vietnamese tình báo are all standard, fully compositional readings. Removed 諜報 from `aliases` (a distinct synonym built on a different first character 諜, not an orthographic variant of this word). Fixed missing "(char)" suffix on `characters`. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄫㄅㄚㄨ unique across both words and characters).
