## Notes
- [桂](../characters/桂.md) "cinnamon, cassia, osmanthus" + [月](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — no stand-in relationship applies (桂's own stand-in is [[肉桂]]; 月's own is itself).

Eighth month of the ancient Chinese poetic calendar. Osmanthus (桂花) blooms in early autumn with an intensely sweet fragrance. The Mid-Autumn Festival (中秋節) falls this month, and according to legend a cassia / osmanthus tree grows on the moon — making 桂 the quintessential moon-month.

Mandarin guìyuè and Cantonese gwai3 jyut6 are standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 계월 is likewise compositional. Japanese かつらつき reads both halves natively (桂's kun'yomi かつら "osmanthus" + 月's kun'yomi つき "month") rather than the Sino-Japanese on'yomi concatenation けいげつ, matching the established poetic-month convention (see [[寅月]], [[卯月]], [[未月]], [[杏月]]). Vietnamese tháng quế likewise uses the native *tháng* plus the Sino-Vietnamese osmanthus name quế, the same pattern. Removed the redundant `品詞` field and reformatted `japanese`/`vietnamese` from single-item lists to scalar strings. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼔ㄧ·⼔ㄊ unique across both words and characters).
