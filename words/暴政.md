## Notes
- [暴](../characters/暴%20(char).md) "violent, brutal, fierce, tyrannical" + [政](../characters/政.md) "government, political affairs" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 暴 and 政's own stand-ins are themselves).

暴政 means tyranny or despotism.

Mandarin bàozhèng, Cantonese bou6 zing3, and Japanese ぼうせい are standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 폭정 and Vietnamese bạo chính use the same real alternate reading of 暴 (폭/bạo, as first element) already flagged as a citation gap on [[暴徒]], consistent with sibling compounds 暴風/暴飲/暴食/暴怒.
