## Notes
- [余](../characters/余.md) "remainder, surplus" + [弦 (char)](../characters/弦%20(char).md) "chord (of a circle)" — "cosine," coined by Jesuit missionary Johann Schreck's 大測 (1631) alongside 正弦 (sine) and 正切 (tangent), and still in formal use across the Sinosphere today. `korean: 여현` is the historical/formal Sino-Korean term (still seen in 여현법칙, "law of cosines," though the loanword 코사인 is more common in everyday teaching) — confirmed via web search. `vietnamese: côsin` breaks the pattern: modern Vietnamese mathematics uses the direct Western loanword exclusively rather than any Sino-Vietnamese calque of 余弦, so that real usage is documented here instead of an unattested compositional guess. No homophones (注音 ⼄ㄏㄝㄋ unique among both words and characters).
