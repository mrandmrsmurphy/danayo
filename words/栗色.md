## Notes
- [栗 (char)](../characters/栗%20(char).md) "chestnut" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color"

栗色 is the color of the chestnut shell: a deep reddish-brown with warm undertones, darker than 茶色 and redder than 褐. In Japanese, くりいろ (kuri-iro) is an established traditional color name. Korean 률색 (ryul-saek) is given in the North Korean reading, which keeps 栗 as 률; South Korean 두음법칙 shifts the word-initial 률 to 율, giving 율색 (yul-saek).
