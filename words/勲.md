## Notes
- [勲 (char)](../characters/勲%20(char).md) "meritorious deed; merit" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

勲/勳 is a Classical term for military or civil merit — a meritorious deed or the honor/achievement earned through it — used in formal and literary registers (compare English "distinction, honors"). It shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading (hun/훈/ㄏㄨㄋ) with two other words, [[薫]] ("fragrant, incense") and [[訓]] ("interpret, translate"), a three-way homophone group cross-referenced on all three pages.

Mandarin xūn, Cantonese fan1, and Korean 훈 (hun) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese いさお (isao, already 勲's own stored `japanese_native` value) is the everyday native reading for this word specifically, since 勲 in this "merit" sense is read with kun'yomi rather than on'yomi. Vietnamese huân is the standard Hán Việt reading, well attested in huân chương ("medal, decoration") and huân công ("meritorious achievement"). **Real `kwin` bug found and fixed**: was stored as `false`, but Dan'a'yo 훈 is byte-identical to Sino-Korean 훈 — corrected to `kwin: true`. No Dan'a'yo homophones beyond [[薫]]/[[訓]] themselves (注音 ㄏㄨㄋ unique among these three words; no other word or character shares it).
