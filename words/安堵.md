## Notes
- [安](../characters/安.md) "safe" + [堵](../characters/堵.md) "wall; fence"

安堵 is relief: the easing of the mind when a danger or worry has passed. Wiktionary glosses Japanese あんど as "relief" and Mandarin āndǔ as "to live in peace and comfort", the older sense of living safe within one's walls. Korean 안도 is the reading of the characters, and the Cantonese reading is built from the characters.

It is the feeling that follows [不安](不安.md), anxiety, ending, and the quieter side of [満足](満足.md), satisfaction. The relief of settling is [安心](安心.md).
