## Notes
- [眺](../characters/眺.md) "stare; gaze" + [望](../characters/望.md) "hope; view"
- This word is the viable form of [眺](../characters/眺.md): the character's meaning (to stare, gaze) is identical, but 眺 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 眺望 is its `stand_in` compound.

眺 is to look into the distance, especially from a height, and 望 is to look far out, so 眺望 is the act of taking in a wide view from a vantage point: a hilltop, a high floor, a ship's deck, a balcony. It is a verb, but it also works as a noun for the view itself, the sense in 眺望がよい "to have a fine view," common in Japanese real-estate and hotel descriptions (眺望 as the outlook a room has). Compare [景色](景色.md) "scenery," which names what is seen; 眺望 names the looking, or the prospect from where one stands.

The shared readings are Mandarin tiàowàng, Cantonese tiu3 mong6, Japanese chōbō (ちょうぼう) and Korean 조망. Dan'a'yo keeps the shared sense, "to gaze into the distance, view from a height," with the noun use of "a view" as a natural extension, not a separate sense. It is placed under [Dimensions](../lexipedia/Dimensions.md) for the distance-and-height notion.
