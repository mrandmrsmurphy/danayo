## Notes
- [磁](../characters/磁.md) "magnetism" + [性](../characters/性.md) "nature, quality" — 磁's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 性's own is [[性別]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 磁 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

磁性 names magnetism as an inherent physical property of a material — the standard scientific term, as distinct from 磁 alone (which can also mean the lodestone/magnet itself, or magnetism more loosely).

Mandarin cíxìng, Cantonese ci4 sing3, Japanese じせい (jisei), Korean 자성 (jaseong), and Vietnamese từ tính are all fully compositional and are the standard physics terms for magnetism across the sphere.
