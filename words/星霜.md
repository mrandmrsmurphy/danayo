## Notes
- [星](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" + [霜](../characters/霜%20(char).md) "frost" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 星 and 霜's own stand-ins are themselves).

星霜 means time and years (literally "stars and frost," poetically counting the passage of seasons).

Mandarin xīngshuāng, Cantonese sing1 soeng1, Japanese せいそう, Korean 성상, and Vietnamese tinh sương are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `注音` had a typo (ㄙㄝㄋ→ㄙㄝㄫ, wrong final on 星's syllable). No homophones at the corrected reading (注音 ㄙㄝㄫㄙ⺢ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
