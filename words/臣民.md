## Notes
- [臣](../characters/臣.md) "minister" is separately legitimized by [[大臣]]. [民](../characters/民.md) "citizen" is separately legitimized by [[人民]]. No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

臣民 combines 臣 ("minister, official") with 民 ("citizen, commoner") — "officials and commoners, subjects [of a ruler]."

Mandarin chénmín, cantonese san4man4 (space removed), japanese しんみん (shinmin), korean 신민 (sinmin), and vietnamese thần dân were all already correct, fully compositional readings.
