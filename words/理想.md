## Notes
- [理](../characters/理.md) "-ology, reason, logic" + [想](../characters/想.md) "thought, concept" — no stand-in relationship applies (理's own stand-in is [[理由]]; 想's own is [[思想]]).

理想 means ideal, dream, theory.

Mandarin lǐxiǎng, Cantonese lei5 soeng2, Japanese りそう, Korean 리상 and Vietnamese lí tưởng are all fully compositional. Korean 리상 keeps the word-initial ㄹ of the North Korean convention, matching 理's own citation 리, as in [[理由]] and [[理解]].
