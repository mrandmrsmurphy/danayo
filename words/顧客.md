## Notes
- [顧](../characters/顧.md) "look back at; patronize" + [客](../characters/客.md) "guest, customer" — "patron," a customer. Their own `stand_in` values: 顧's is [[照顧]]; 客's is [[客人]]; none points here, so 顧客 is an independent compound.

顧客 is a customer: someone who buys from or uses the services of a business. Mandarin gùkè, Cantonese gu3 haak3, Japanese こきゃく and Korean 고객 all use it. It is the buyer's side of [[商人]]. Compare [[購買]] (to buy) and [[商店]] (shop).
