## Notes
- [論](../characters/論.md) "discourse, theory" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language, sayings" — literally "discoursed sayings," the compiled sayings and discourse of Confucius and his disciples.

Mandarin Lúnyǔ, Japanese ろんご (rongo), and Vietnamese Luận Ngữ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Cantonese carries two attested tone variants on 論 itself — leon4 (verb-like, "to discuss") and leon6 (noun-like, "theory, discourse") — both genuinely used for this title, a tone-based register distinction rather than a transcription error. **Found and fixed a real bug**: `korean` was stored as 논어, the South-Korean 두음법칙-softened form — inconsistent with this vault's standing rule that `korean` always holds the North Korean/문화어 reading, and inconsistent with how the same character is handled elsewhere in this corpus (理論 → 리론, not 이론). Corrected to 론어. `kwin` remains false even after that correction: 論's own portion now matches (론), but 語 independently diverges from Korean on its own character page (Dan'a'yo ⼄/yo vs Korean 어/eo), which alone is enough to keep the AND-rule false.

The _Analects_ of Confucius — for the working translation of the text itself, see [[論語 (book)]].
