## Notes
- [時 (char)](../characters/時%20(char).md) "when; time" + [効](../characters/効.md) "effect"

時効 is the statute of limitations, or prescription: the period after which a right can no longer be enforced, or an offence can no longer be prosecuted. Wiktionary glosses Chinese 時效 as "effectiveness for a given period of time; prescription; limitation" in the legal register. Japanese じこう and Korean 시효 are the matching forms. The word is literally "the effect of time".

It limits what [起訴](起訴.md) and [訴訟](訴訟.md) can do. Once the period has run, the charge cannot be brought, whatever the evidence.
