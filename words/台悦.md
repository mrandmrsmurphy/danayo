## Notes
- [台 (char)](../characters/台%20(char).md) "platform, pedestal" + [悦](../characters/悦.md) "pleased, overjoyed" — happy, pleasant.

怡 (yí), "happy, joyful, content," appears 6 times in the 《論語》, in two passages: **子路** (13.28), where 怡怡如也 and "兄弟怡怡" describe brothers getting along, and **鄉黨** (10.4), where "怡怡如也" describes Confucius relaxing his expression on leaving the ruler's audience hall. 怡 lies outside Dan'a'yo's closed character set, so the vault substitutes 台悦 — pairing 台, which is the phonetic component of 怡, with 悦, whose meaning "pleased, joyous" supplies the translation. This is a "Component + Translation" substitution per [[translation/論語 (book)]]'s Vocabulary list.

The mandarin/cantonese/japanese/korean readings of 台悦 are drawn from the attested compound 怡悅/怡悦 (yíyuè), "happy, pleasant, cheerful." 諺文/羅馬字/注音 remain compositional from 台 and 悦's own fixed Dan'a'yo values (대+엳), diverging from 怡's Sino-Korean reading 이.

The 論語 translation page's vocabulary list records the substitution directly: "怡 6x --> 台悦 - Component + Translation." The six occurrences are the four characters in 子路 13.28 (怡怡如也, 兄弟怡怡) and the two in 鄉黨 10.4 (怡怡如也). The word is therefore a Dan'a'yo substitution word, not an established compound, and the real-world readings recorded for it (yíyuè, ji4 jyut6, いえつ, 이열) are those of the attested compound 怡悅, as noted above. The Emotions page lists it as "happy, pleased, joyful," and Dan'a'yo keeps that sense.
