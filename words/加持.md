## Notes
- [加](../characters/加.md) "add" + [持](../characters/持%20(char).md) "support, hold" — no stand-in relationship applies (持 is its own stand-in; 加's own is [[加算]]).

加持 is a Buddhist term (Sanskrit *adhiṣṭhāna*) meaning to bless or empower through ritual incantation — a priest's or deity's spiritual support conferred upon a person or object (加持祈祷, ritual blessing and prayer).

Japanese かじ, Korean 가지, and Vietnamese gia trì are all standard readings. **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 加持 and [[価値]] ("worth, value") share an identical reading (gadi/가디/ㄍㄚㄉㄧ) — added reciprocal homophone callouts to both pages.
