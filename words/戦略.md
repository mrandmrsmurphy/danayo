## Notes
- [戦](../characters/戦.md) "war, battle" + [略](../characters/略%20(char).md) "plan, strategy" — "war plan," strategy. Their own `stand_in` values: 戦's is [[戦争]]; 略's is itself; none points here, so 戦略 is an independent compound.

戦略 is strategy: the large-scale plan for winning a war or reaching a goal. Mandarin zhànlüè (戰略), Cantonese zin3 loek6, Japanese せんりゃく and Korean 전략 all use it, and Vietnamese chiến lược does too. It is the broad plan, where [[兵法]] is the art of war and tactics. Compare [[戦争]] (war).
