## Notes
- [拿](../characters/拿.md) "take, grasp" + [捕](../characters/捕%20(char).md) "catch, grab" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [拿](../characters/拿.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (捕's own stand-in is itself).

拿捕 means to seize or capture (as of a ship or vessel).

Mandarin nábǔ, Cantonese naa4 bou6, Japanese だほ, Korean 나포, and Vietnamese nã bổ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Trimmed a stray space in `mandarin`. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄚㄅㄛ unique across both words and characters).
