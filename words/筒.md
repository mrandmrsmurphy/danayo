## Notes
- [筒](../characters/筒%20(char).md) "cylinder, tube" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 筒 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

筒 names a hollow cylindrical tube — originally a thick section of bamboo, extended to any tube, pipe, or cylindrical container (封筒, "envelope," literally "sealed tube"; 水筒, "water bottle, canteen").

Mandarin tǒng, Cantonese tung4, and Korean 통 (tong) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading つつ (tsutsu, "tube, pipe") rather than the on-reading トウ (TOU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese đồng is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading. This word is part of a genuine three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read dong/동/ㄉㄛㄫ): [[動]] ("move") and [[銅]] ("copper").
