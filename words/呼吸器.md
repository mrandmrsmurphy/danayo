## Notes
- [呼 (char)](../characters/呼%20(char).md) "exhale" + [吸 (char)](../characters/吸%20(char).md) "inhale" + [器](../characters/器.md) "instrument, device" — "respirator, ventilator" (the medical breathing-assistance device); 呼吸 alone ("respiration, breathing") is one of the most common Sino-vocabulary words across the whole CJKV sphere. **Content bug found and fixed**: `japanese` contained a stray zero-width space (U+200B) hidden between ゅ and う — removed, restoring こきゅうき. `vietnamese: hô hấp khí` is compositional (hô hấp itself, "respiration," is a completely standard Vietnamese word); no independent attestation of the full three-character compound as an established device name was found — everyday Vietnamese instead uses the native máy thở ("breathing machine") for this specific device (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 호힙킈 diverges from Sino-Korean 호흡기 in the second and third syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄛㄏㄧㄆㄎㄧㄜ unique among both words and characters).
