## Notes
- [鞭](../characters/鞭.md) "whip" + [打](../characters/打.md) "hit" — 鞭打 is the stand-in word for [鞭](../characters/鞭.md) ("whip"), which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo. No `#cranberry`: 打's own `stand_in` is [[打撃]], not this word, so transitivity fails.

鞭打 (biāndǎ) is the standard Mandarin word for "to whip, to flog," and Cantonese 鞭打 (bin1daa2) likewise. Japanese べんだ (benda) and Korean 편타 (pyeonta) are both real, attested words carrying the same literal and figurative senses (striking with a whip; urging/encouraging someone on). Sino-Vietnamese tiên đả is likewise attested with the same meaning — a clean case across all five languages.
