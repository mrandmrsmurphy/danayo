## Notes
- [政](../characters/政.md) "government, political affairs" + [治](../characters/治.md) "govern, rule" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [政](../characters/政.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (治's own stand-in is [[統治]]).

政治 means politics.

Mandarin zhèngzhì, Cantonese zing3 zi6, Japanese せいじ, Korean 정치, and Vietnamese chính trị are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `羅馬字` had a garbled/contaminated value (jing'yog→jingci, matching 治's own citation "ci"). No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄫㄑㄧ unique across both words and characters).
