## Notes
- [怒 (char)](../characters/怒%20(char).md) "angry" + [視 (char)](../characters/視%20(char).md) "look"

怒視 is to glare: to look at someone with open anger. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin nùshì as "to glare at; to glower at; to scowl at". Cantonese nou6 si6 matches, Korean 노시 is the reading of the characters, and no Japanese reading is attested.

It is built from [怒](怒.md), anger, and [視](視.md), to look, and is the look of the angry face. A frown without a target is [皺眉](皺眉.md).
