## Etymology
- [爬](characters/爬.md) "crawl" + [虫](characters/虫.md) "bug" — no stand-in relationship applies (爬's own stand-in is [[爬行]]; 虫's own is [[昆虫]]).

爬虫 means reptile.

Mandarin páchóng, Cantonese paa4 cung4, Japanese はちゅう (HA+CHUU), and Korean 파충 were already correct and fully compositional. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with bò sát, the real, standard Vietnamese biological term for "reptile" (literally "crawl-cling"); notably, bò is also one of 爬's own listed readings. Fixed a real character-level bug on `characters/虫.md`: its `pos` field held an empty string rather than a value — corrected to 名詞. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄐㄨㄫ unique across both words and characters).
