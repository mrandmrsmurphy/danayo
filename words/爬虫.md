## Notes
- [爬](../characters/爬.md) "crawl" + [虫](../characters/虫.md) "bug" — no stand-in relationship applies (爬's own stand-in is [[爬行]]; 虫's own is [[昆虫]]).

爬虫 means reptile.

Mandarin páchóng, Cantonese paa4 cung4, Japanese はちゅう and Korean 파충 are fully compositional. Vietnamese bò sát (literally "crawl-cling") is the standard biological term for "reptile"; bò is also one of 爬's own listed readings.
