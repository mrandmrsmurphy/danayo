## Notes
- [陳](../characters/陳.md) "exhibit, display" + [列](../characters/列.md) "arrange, line up" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes 陳 as a bound character (列's own stand-in is [[配列]]; transitivity fails, so no `#cranberry`).

陳列 means to display, exhibit, or lay out (goods, artifacts, information) for viewing. Mandarin 陳列/陈列 (chénliè), Cantonese 陳列 (can4lit6), Japanese 陳列 (ちんれつ), and Sino-Vietnamese trần liệt are all standard commercial/museum-display terms. Korean 진열 (jinyeol) shows a real, mandatory Korean orthographic softening — 列's own Sino-Korean reading is 렬 (ryeol), but standard Korean spelling rules convert 렬/률 to 열/율 after a vowel or ㄴ-final syllable, giving 진열 rather than a naive 진렬 (the same rule seen in 나열/羅列, 분열/分裂).
