## Notes
- [宣](../characters/宣.md) "proclaim" + [教](../characters/教.md) "teach, religion" — "proclaim the religion," to evangelize. Their own `stand_in` values: 宣's is [[宣言]]; 教's is [[教授]]; none points here, so 宣教 is an independent compound.

宣教 is to spread a religion: to carry its teaching to those outside it, a mission. Mandarin xuānjiào, Cantonese syun1 gaau3 and Japanese せんきょう are attested, and Korean 선교 is the same compound. It is the outward preaching, where [[説教]] is the sermon to a congregation. Compare [[宣教師]] (missionary) and [[伝道]] (to preach).
