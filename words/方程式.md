## Notes
- [方](../characters/方.md) "method" + [程](../characters/程.md) "process, procedure" + [式](../characters/式.md) "formula" — literally "method-process-formula."

方程式 names a mathematical equation, a statement of equality between two expressions connected by "=". This word's own alias, 方程 (dropping 式), is itself already the everyday term in some registers, since 式 functions here as a productive but semantically light "formula/expression" suffix.

Mandarin fāngchéngshì, Cantonese fong1 cing4 sik1, Korean 방정식 (bangjeongsik), and Japanese ほうていしき (hōteishiki) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real everyday mathematical term in its language. Vietnamese phương trình is likewise the real, standard term — built from 方's phương and 程's trình, with 式's own reading (thức) not separately pronounced in ordinary usage, the same way this word's own 方程 alias drops 式 in Chinese registers. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 빵딩식 diverges from Sino-Korean 방정식 in the first syllable. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄈㄚㄫㄉㄧㄫㄙㄧㄎ unique among both words and characters).
