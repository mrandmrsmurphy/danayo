## Notes
- [天 (char)](../characters/天%20(char).md) "sky" + [地 (char)](../characters/地%20(char).md) "land" + [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person"

天地人 is the three powers or "three talents" of classical East Asian cosmology: Heaven, Earth, and Humankind, with humans understood as the mediating principle between celestial and terrestrial forces.

English Wiktionary has an entry only for Korean 천지인, glossed "(Eastern philosophy) heaven, earth, and man"; it has none for Chinese or Japanese 天地人. The same triad is its entry 三才 (Mandarin sāncái, Cantonese saam1 coi4, Japanese さんさい), glossed historically as the "three talents": sky, earth and people, where one who knows all three well would be an all-rounded person. The Mandarin tiāndìrén, Cantonese tin1 dei6 jan4 and Japanese てんちじん readings here are built from the characters.

The first two characters alone are [[天地]], "heaven and earth", the world.
