## Notes
- [風 (char)](../characters/風%20(char).md) "wind" + [刺 (char)](../characters/刺%20(char).md) "stab, prick, sting" — no `#cranberry`: both 風's and 刺's own `stand_in` are themselves; neither points here. Written with the alias 諷/讽 rather than 風 itself — 諷 has its own distinct real-language readings for this "satirize" sense (fěng, phúng), differing from 風's own "wind" readings (fēng, phong).

諷刺 (fěngcì) is the standard Mandarin word for "to satirize, mock; irony," and Cantonese 諷刺 (fung3ci3, also fung2ci3) likewise. Japanese ふうし (fūshi) and Korean 풍자 (pungja) are both standard terms in their languages. Sino-Vietnamese phúng thích is likewise attested (e.g. văn học phúng thích, "satirical literature"), though the more common everyday Vietnamese terms are the native châm biếm or trào phúng.

**Fixed real bugs**: `mandarin` had a spurious second variant `fèngcì` (諷 has no attested fèng reading) — trimmed to the sole correct `fěngcì`. Fixed a typo in `english` (satarize → satirize).
