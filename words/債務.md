## Notes
- [債](../characters/債.md) "debt, loan" + [務](../characters/務.md) "duty, task" — "debt duty," a debtor's obligation. Their own `stand_in` values: 債's is [[負債]]; 務's is [[服務]]; none points here, so 債務 is an independent compound.

債務 is a debt in the legal sense: the debtor's obligation to repay, and by extension a debit. Mandarin zhàiwù, Cantonese zaai3 mou6, Japanese さいむ and Korean 채무 all use it. It is the debtor's side of [[債権]]. Compare [[負債]] (liabilities, the balance-sheet word).
