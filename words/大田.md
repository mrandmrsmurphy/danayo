## Notes
- [大](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big" + [田](../characters/田.md) "field" — the name of Daejeon, the central city of Korea.

大田 (Daejeon) is a central inland city, near the 忠清 region (see [[忠清]]), and a hub of Korean railways and research institutes. Its full modern name is 大田廣域市 (대전광역시), "Daejeon Metropolitan City", a separate unit since 1989, first as a 直轄市, and renamed 廣域市 in 1995 (from memory, not re-checked). The hanja are a rendering of the older native place-name Hanbat, "great field" (한밭), and the city grew from a small village when the Seoul–Busan railway came through around 1905 (from memory, not re-checked).

English Wiktionary has 大田 as Mandarin dàtián and Cantonese daai6 tin4 for the Korean city, with the Korean entry hanja form of 대전 and the Vietnamese Đại Điền; Japanese Wiktionary lists テジョン for the Korean city. The same string 大田 in Japan is the city Ōda (Shimane) and the Tokyo ward Ōta, and in China Datian county in Fujian, none of which is the word here. Japanese だいでん is the compositional on'yomi from the character pages and is not attested as the city name.
