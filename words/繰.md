## Notes
- [繰](../characters/繰%20(char).md) "reel, bolt of cloth" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [繰](../characters/繰%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

繰 means reel, or a bolt of cloth.

Mandarin zǎo, Cantonese zou2, Japanese そう, Korean 조, and Vietnamese sào are all standard, fully compositional readings. Three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all jau/잣/ㄐㄚㄨ) with [[早]] ("early") and [[朝]] ("morning") — completing the reciprocal callout.
