## Notes
- [茂](../characters/茂.md) "overgrown, thick" + [密 (char)](../characters/密%20(char).md) "dense, close" — this word is the stand-in compound that legitimizes the bound character 茂 (per its own stored `stand_in`); 密 is independently free-standing (its own `stand_in` is itself).

茂密 describes dense, thick growth — most often vegetation (a lush, overgrown thicket or forest canopy), extending by the ordinary "thick" sense to density more generally. 茂 alone leans toward "overgrown, luxuriant" (as in [[繁茂]], "lush, flourishing"), while 密 alone carries a broader range of "dense/close" senses, including the unrelated "secret, intimate" extension (as in [[秘密]], "secret") not shared by this compound.

Mandarin màomì and Cantonese mau6 mat6 are both standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bugs found and fixed 2026-09-22**: `japanese` previously stored 密生している, a fully conjugated sentence fragment ("is growing densely") rather than a citation-form reading — corrected to もみつ (momitsu), compositional from 茂's on'yomi モ + 密's on'yomi ミツ. `korean` previously stored 밀생 (milsaeng), the Sino-Korean reading of an entirely different, unrelated compound (密生, "dense growth") — corrected to 무밀 (mumil), the actual compositional reading of 茂密 itself (茂's 무 + 密's 밀). `vietnamese` previously stored mậu tài, where mậu correctly matches 茂 but tài doesn't correspond to either constituent character at all — corrected to mậu mật, using 密's own attested Hán Việt reading mật. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 못믿 diverges from Sino-Korean 무밀 in both syllables. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄇㄛㄨㄇㄧㄊ unique among both words and characters).
