## Notes
- [茂](../characters/茂.md) "overgrown, thick" + [密 (char)](../characters/密%20(char).md) "dense, close" — stand-in for [茂](../characters/茂.md), which cannot appear independently; 密 is independently free-standing (its own `stand_in` is itself).

茂密 describes dense, thick growth — most often vegetation (a lush, overgrown thicket or forest canopy), extending by the ordinary "thick" sense to density more generally. 茂 alone leans toward "overgrown, luxuriant" (as in [繁茂](繁茂.md), "lush, flourishing"), while 密 alone carries a broader range of "dense/close" senses, including the unrelated "secret, intimate" extension (as in [秘密](秘密.md), "secret") not shared by this compound.

Mandarin màomì and Cantonese mau6 mat6 are both standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese もみつ (momitsu, from 茂's on'yomi モ and 密's ミツ), Korean 무밀 (mumil, from 茂's 무 and 密's 밀) and Vietnamese mậu mật (from 茂's mậu and 密's mật) are likewise compositional readings.
