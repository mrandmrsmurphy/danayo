## Notes
- [意](../characters/意.md) "meaning, intention" + [志](../characters/志.md) "aspiration, will" — literally "intention-will."

意志 names the will, willpower, or resolve — the mental faculty of intention and determination, distinct from mere "meaning" (意 alone, as in [[意味]]) by pairing it with 志's specifically volitional sense.

Mandarin yìzhì, Cantonese ji3 zi3, Korean 의지 (uiji), and Japanese いし (ishi) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real everyday term for "will/willpower" in its language. Vietnamese ý chí is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, matching 意's ý (one of five candidates stored on 意's own page) and 志's own reading. **Real bug found and fixed 2026-09-22**: this word's own `注音`/`諺文`/`羅馬字` all stored a corrupted first syllable — ㄧ/이/'i instead of the correct ㄜ/으/'ǝ, 意's own stored reading everywhere else it's cited (18 other compounds on 意's own page all correctly use ㄜ; 意志 was the sole outlier, and the same wrong reading had also propagated into 意's own Words-list citation of this word — fixed there too). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 으지 diverges from Sino-Korean 의지 in the first syllable. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄜㄐㄧ unique among both words and characters).
