## Notes
- [意](../characters/意.md) "meaning, intention" + [志](../characters/志.md) "aspiration, will" — literally "intention-will."

意志 names the will, willpower, or resolve — the mental faculty of intention and determination, distinct from mere "meaning" (意 alone, as in [意味](意味.md)) by pairing it with 志's specifically volitional sense.

Mandarin yìzhì, Cantonese ji3 zi3, Korean 의지 (uiji), and Japanese いし (ishi) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real everyday term for "will/willpower" in its language. Vietnamese ý chí is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, matching 意's ý (one of five candidates stored on 意's own page) and 志's own reading.
