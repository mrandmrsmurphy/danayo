## Notes
- [慄](../characters/慄%20(char).md) "tremble, shudder, shiver" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

慄 means to tremble, shudder, or shiver — as in 戦慄 ("shudder, tremor").

Mandarin lì, Cantonese leot6, Japanese りつ, Korean 률, and Vietnamese lật are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄌㄧㄊ/lid/릳) with the unrelated [[栗]] ("chestnut").
