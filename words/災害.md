## Notes
- [災](../characters/災.md) "disaster, catastrophe" + [害](../characters/害.md) "harm" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 災 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (害's own stand-in is [[残害]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

災害 means disaster, calamity.

Mandarin zāihài, Cantonese zoi1 hoi6, Japanese さいがい, Korean 재해, and Vietnamese tai hại are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 災's and 害's own stored citations exactly), and also the real, standard everyday words for "disaster" in each language. `kwin: true` because 諺文 재해 matches Korean 재해 exactly. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases: []`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄧㄏㄚㄧ unique across both words and characters).
