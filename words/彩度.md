## Notes
- [彩](../characters/彩.md) "color, hue" + [度](../characters/度.md) "degree, extent" — saturation.

彩度 is saturation or chroma: how vivid a color is, as opposed to dull or grayish; a fully saturated red is pure, a desaturated one is near grey. It is one of the three attributes of color alongside [[色相]] (hue) and [[明度]] (lightness). It is attested in Japanese さいど and Korean 채도, where it is the standard technical term; Mandarin usually says 飽和度 (bǎohédù, "saturation degree") instead, so no Mandarin or Cantonese reading is given.
