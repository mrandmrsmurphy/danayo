## Notes
- [陪 (char)](../characters/陪%20(char).md) "accompany; be with" + [審](../characters/審.md) "examine; investigate"

陪審 is the jury: ordinary citizens who sit with the court to hear a case and help decide the verdict, literally "accompany the examination". The senses differ slightly. Wiktionary gives Japanese ばいしん as "a jury", and lists Korean 배심 and Vietnamese bồi thẩm as "jury". Mandarin péishěn is recorded as a verb: "to serve on a jury; to serve as an assessor in a court trial". Dan'a'yo takes the noun, the jury itself.

The jury sits in the [法庭](法庭.md) beside the [裁判官](裁判官.md), and it reaches the [判決](判決.md): the finding of [有罪](有罪.md) or [無罪](無罪.md).
