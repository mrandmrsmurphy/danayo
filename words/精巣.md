## Notes
- [精](../characters/精.md) "spirit, essence, sperm" + [巣](../characters/巣.md) "nest" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (精's is [[精神]], 巣's is [[鳥巣]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 巣 is individually `kwin: false`.

**Fixed a real bug**: `korean` had 불알, a native colloquial Korean word for testicle(s), not this compound's own Sino-Korean reading — corrected to 정소, itself a genuine, standard medical term for testicle in Korean.

精巣 names the testicle — literally "sperm nest," a fittingly descriptive anatomical compound naming the organ where sperm (精) is produced and stored.

Mandarin jīngcháo, Cantonese zing1 caau4, Japanese せいそう (seisō), and Korean 정소 (jeongso, the standard medical term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese tinh hoàn is likewise compositional and is itself the standard medical Vietnamese term for testicle.
