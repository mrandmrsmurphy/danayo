## Notes
- [精](../characters/精.md) "spirit, essence, sperm" + [巣](../characters/巣.md) "nest" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (精's is [[精神]], 巣's is [[鳥巣]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply.

精巣 names the testicle — literally "sperm nest," a fittingly descriptive anatomical compound naming the organ where sperm (精) is produced and stored.

Mandarin jīngcháo, Cantonese zing1 caau4, Japanese せいそう (seisō), and Korean 정소 (jeongso, the standard medical term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese tinh hoàn is likewise compositional and is itself the standard medical Vietnamese term for testicle.
