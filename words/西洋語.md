## Notes
- [西](../characters/西.md) "west" + [洋](../characters/洋.md) "ocean" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language" — "the languages of the Western world," built on [[西洋]] "the West" (literally "western ocean") plus 語. No stand-in relationship applies (西's own `stand_in` is [[西方]], 洋's is [[大洋]], 語's is [[言語]]).

Wiktionary has no entry for 西洋語; it is a transparent compound of [[西洋]] "the West" and 語 "language", the languages of the West, as against the CJKV languages ([[中日韓越]]). `文法 - 02音韻論` uses it as the contrast class for Japanese, which is said to be more similar to 西洋語 in being organized around a voiced versus voiceless contrast.
