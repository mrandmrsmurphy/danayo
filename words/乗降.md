## Notes
- [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "ride, board" + [降](../characters/降.md) "descend, alight" — boarding and alighting, embarkation and disembarkation.

乗降 names the paired action of getting on and off a vehicle, most visible in transit infrastructure vocabulary: 乗降口 (jōkōguchi, "boarding/alighting door"), 乗降客数 (passenger boarding/alighting counts, a standard transit-statistics term). Mandarin chéng jiàng, Cantonese sing4 gong3, and Korean 승강 (승강장, "platform," the most common derived form) all parallel this sense. **Vietnamese content note**: filled with lên xuống, the real, everyday Vietnamese term for "boarding and alighting" (native lên "go up/board" + xuống "go down/alight," as in cửa lên xuống, "boarding door") — preferred over a forced Sino-Vietnamese compositional calque (乗's thừa + 降's giáng), which isn't independently attested as a fixed compound.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 숭강 diverges from Sino-Korean 승강, since 乗's own Dan'a'yo reading 숭 doesn't match Sino-Korean 승, even though 降's own reading matches exactly (降 is independently `kwin: true`).
