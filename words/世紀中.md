## Notes
- [世](../characters/世.md) "generation, world" + [紀](../characters/紀.md) "century, record" + [中 (char)](../characters/中%20(char).md) "middle" — no `#cranberry`.

Mandarin shìjìzhōng, Cantonese sai3 gei2 zung1, Japanese せいきちゅう, and Korean 세기중 are all standard compositional readings. Vietnamese giữa thế kỷ ("middle century," native word-order-reversed, giữa "middle" placed before thế kỷ) is the real, natural phrase, rather than a Sino-order calque.
