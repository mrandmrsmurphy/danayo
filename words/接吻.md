## Notes
- [接](../characters/接%20(char).md) "contact, connect" + [吻](../characters/吻.md) "kiss, lips" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [吻](../characters/吻.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (接's own stand-in is itself).

接吻 means to kiss.

Mandarin jiēwěn, Cantonese zip3 man5, Japanese せっぷん, and Korean 접문 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Simplified `korean` from a native/loanword dump (뽀뽀, 키스 — both everyday colloquial words for "kiss") to the compositional 접문, moving the colloquial forms to prose. Vietnamese hôn is the everyday native word, used here instead of a Sino-Vietnamese compound. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄛㄆㄇㄨㄋ unique across both words and characters).
