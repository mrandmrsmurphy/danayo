## Notes
- [胸](../characters/胸.md) "chest" is separately legitimized by [[胸部]]. [臆](../characters/臆.md) "feelings, thoughts" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 臆 cannot appear independently outside this compound. No cranberry: transitivity fails since 胸's own `stand_in` doesn't point here.

胸臆 combines 胸 ("chest") with 臆 ("feelings, thoughts") — "feelings, thoughts, opinions" (the chest as the metaphorical seat of one's inner feelings).

Mandarin xiōngyì, japanese きょうおく (kyouoku), and korean 흉억 (hyungeok) were all already correct, fully compositional readings.
