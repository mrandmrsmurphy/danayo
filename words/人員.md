## Notes
- [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" + [員](../characters/員.md) "member, personnel" — "staff, personnel, employee." nhân viên is standard everyday Vietnamese for this sense (as in nhân viên bán hàng, "sales staff"), a direct compositional match. Legitimizing note: 員's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, so 員 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.
