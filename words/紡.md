## Notes
- [紡](../characters/紡%20(char).md) "spin (yarn)" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 紡 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

紡 means to spin yarn or thread, as in 紡織 (spinning and weaving) and 紡錘 (spindle).

Mandarin fǎng, Cantonese fong2, and Korean 방 (bang) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading つむ (tsumu, "to spin") rather than the on-readings ボウ/ホウ (BOU/HOU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese phưởng is one of several stored candidates. A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone with [[肪]] ("fat, obese") — found via a retroactive re-check after discovering the homophone-check tooling had been silently broken (see memory); 肪 is still unperfected, so full cross-referencing awaits its own turn in the sweep.
