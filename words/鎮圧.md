## Notes
- [鎮 (char)](../characters/鎮%20(char).md) "calm; suppress" + [圧 (char)](../characters/圧%20(char).md) "press"

鎮圧 is suppression: to put down a riot or a revolt by force. Wiktionary glosses Japanese ちんあつ as "suppression; to suppress". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 진압 matches.

It is done by the authorities against a [暴動](暴動.md) or a [反乱](反乱.md), where [弾圧](弾圧.md) is the longer repression of a people or a view.
