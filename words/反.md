## Notes
- [反 (char)](../characters/反%20(char).md) "anti-; wrong side up"

反 is 形声: semantic [[又]] ("hand") + phonetic [[厂]]. It means "reverse, opposite, anti-," used as a modifier/prefix across a huge compound family: [[反応]] ("to react, respond"), [[反対]] ("to be opposite, to oppose"), [[違反]] ("to violate, transgress"), [[反響]] ("echo"). By vault policy this character also absorbs 叛 ("to rebel, betray") — close enough in sense to fold together — though compounds historically written with 叛 keep that character's own sound rather than 反's own reading: [[反乱]] ("rebellion, revolt") is read from pàn, not from 反's own fǎn.

Mandarin fǎn, Cantonese faan2, and Japanese はん/たん/ほん (han/tan/hon, on'yomi, fully productive: 反対 はんたい "opposition") alongside native そ(る) (so(ru), "to warp, curve backward") all preserve the "reverse, opposite" sense. Korean 반 (ban) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 돌이키다 (dorikida, "to turn back, reverse"). Vietnamese phản is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in phản đối (反対, "to oppose") and phản ứng (反応, "to react").

**Real bug found and fixed**: this word's own `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` previously stored `pon`/폰/ㄆㄛㄋ, but the character page's own authoritative reading is `fon`/뽄/ㄈㄛㄋ — corrected to match, cross-verified against the two other characters sharing this phonetic family, [[販]] and [[返]] (both independently already stored as `fon`, though neither has a word page yet).
