## Notes
- [南](../characters/南.md) "south" + [浦](../characters/浦%20(char).md) "riverbank, shore" — the name of Nampo, a port city of North Korea.

南浦 (Nampo) is a port city of North Korea at the mouth of the Taedong River (大同江), the outer port of Pyongyang ([[平壌]]) on the Yellow Sea side (from memory, not re-checked). It was formerly called 鎭南浦 (Chinnampo) (from memory, not re-checked). Wiktionary lists it with the status of a special city (特別市). The name reads "south shore".

English and Chinese Wiktionary have 南浦 as the North Korean city (Mandarin Nánpǔ, Cantonese naam4 pou2), and English Wiktionary has a Korean entry as a hanja form of 남포. The Chinese entry also has a literary noun 南浦 "a place of farewell", a different use of the same string. The Japanese entry gives ナムポ (from the Korean) and なんぽ; なんぽ is taken here as the Sino-Xenic reading. Vietnamese Nam Phố is the name used by the Vietnamese Wikipedia.
