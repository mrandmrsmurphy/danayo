## Notes
- [蟻](../characters/蟻%20(char).md) "ant" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

蟻 means ant. Wiktionary analyzes the character as 虫 ("insect") with the phonetic 義, and lists 蛾 among its alternative forms; Dan'a'yo keeps 蟻 "ant" and [蛾](蛾.md) "moth" as separate words.

Mandarin yǐ (the usual Mandarin compound is 螞蟻 mǎyǐ), Cantonese ngai5, Japanese ぎ (the Sino-Japanese reading; the native word is あり), Korean 의 (the native word is 개미), and Vietnamese nghĩ (Hán Việt). Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄜㄧ/'ǝi/읫) with the unrelated [倚](倚.md) ("rely on").
