## Notes
- [主](../characters/主.md) "lord, master" + [義](../characters/義.md) "meaning, principle" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (主's is [[主人]], 義's is [[意義]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply.

主義 is the productive doctrine-forming suffix "-ism," attaching to a wide range of concepts to name the associated ideology or systematic belief: 社会主義 ("socialism"), 資本主義 ("capitalism"), 民主主義 ("democracy"), 禁欲主義 ("asceticism"). Standing alone, it can also mean "one's own principles or creed" in a personal sense.

Mandarin zhǔyì, Cantonese zyu2 ji6, Japanese しゅぎ (shugi), and Korean 주의 (juui) are all standard, fully compositional readings, each the ordinary morpheme for "-ism" in its language. Vietnamese chủ nghĩa is likewise compositional and is the standard, everyday Vietnamese equivalent (chủ nghĩa xã hội, "socialism").
