## Notes
- [監](../characters/監.md) "watch over, prison" + [房](../characters/房%20(char).md) "room" — "guard room," a prison cell. Their own `stand_in` values: 監's is [[監督]]; 房's is itself; none points here, so 監房 is an independent compound.

監房 is a prison cell: a locked room for a prisoner. Mandarin jiānfáng, Cantonese gaam1 fong4 and Korean 감방 use it, and Japanese uses 独房 (solitary) or the same 監房 in writing. Compare [[監獄]] (prison).
