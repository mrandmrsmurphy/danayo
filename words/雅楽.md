## Notes
- [雅](../characters/雅.md) "graceful" + [楽](../characters/楽.md) "entertainment"

雅楽 means elegant, refined court ritual music — the formal ceremonial music tradition of the East Asian imperial and royal courts. Mandarin 雅樂/雅乐 (yǎyuè), Cantonese 雅樂 (ngaa5ngok6), Japanese 雅楽 (ががく, gagaku — Japan's own living tradition, performed at the Imperial court and major shrines), Korean 아악 (aak, Korea's own court-music tradition), and Sino-Vietnamese nhã nhạc (Vietnam's own tradition, UNESCO-recognized as Nhã nhạc cung đình Huế, "Hue royal court music") all name this shared concept, each referring to that country's own distinct living musical tradition descending from the same ancient Chinese ritual-music ideal.
