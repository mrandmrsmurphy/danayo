## Notes
- [掻](../characters/掻%20(char).md) "scratch" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [掻](../characters/掻%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

掻 means to scratch: to rub or scrape the skin with the nails, typically to relieve an itch (掻痒, "scratch an itch"), and by extension to scrape at or stir up something. The graph is a 形声 compound of 扌 "hand" with the phonetic 蚤; the character 搔 is the traditional-form variant.

Mandarin sāo (搔癢, "scratch an itch"), Cantonese sou1, Japanese そう (掻痒/そうよう, "pruritus"; 掻爬/そうは, "curettage"), Korean 소 (소양, "itch") and Vietnamese tao are standard readings in compounds. Japanese uses the native verb 掻く (かく) for the everyday act; the Dan'a'yo word adopts the bare character as the general verb "to scratch".
