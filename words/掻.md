## Notes
- [掻](../characters/掻%20(char).md) "scratch" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [掻](../characters/掻%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

掻 means to scratch.

Mandarin sāo, Cantonese sou1, Japanese そう, Korean 소, and Vietnamese tao are all standard, fully compositional readings. Homophone of [[騒]] "boisterous" (both 注音 ㄙㄚㄨ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words; checked five other candidates sharing this reading (嫂/愁/掃/燥/驟) and confirmed none has an independent word at this exact reading.
