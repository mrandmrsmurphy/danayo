## Notes
- [譲](../characters/譲%20(char).md) "permit" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 譲 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

**Fixed a real bug**: `korean` had the literal string "null" instead of an actual value — corrected to 양, matching the character's own field. `vietnamese` had likewise been set to the literal string "null"; removed entirely since the character page itself stores no vietnamese candidate to draw from.

譲 means to yield, concede, or permit — giving way to another out of courtesy or deference, as in 譲歩 ("to make a concession, compromise") and 謙譲 ("humility, modesty").

Mandarin ràng, Cantonese joeng6, and Korean 양 (yang) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading ゆずる (yuzuru, "to yield, hand over") rather than the on-reading ジョウ (JOU) for the bare character alone.
