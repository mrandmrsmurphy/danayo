## Notes
- [穿](../characters/穿%20(char).md) "pierce, bore" + [孔](../characters/孔%20(char).md) "hole" — "pierce a hole," to bore. Their own `stand_in` values: 穿's is itself; 孔's is itself; none points here, so 穿孔 is an independent compound.

穿孔 is to bore a hole: to drill or pierce right through. Mandarin chuānkǒng, Cantonese cyun1 hung2 and Japanese せんこう are attested, and Korean 천공 is the same compound; in medicine it means a perforation, as of an ulcer. Compare [[穿]] (to pierce) and [[掘]] (to dig).
