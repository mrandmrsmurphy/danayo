## Notes
- [娯](../characters/娯.md) "joy, delight" + [楽](../characters/楽.md) "pleasure, joy" — pairing two near-synonymous "joy" roots, literally "joy-pleasure."

娯楽/娛樂 means entertainment or amusement — the activities and diversions that bring joy and relaxation, from games and hobbies to public entertainment more broadly.

Mandarin yúlè, Cantonese jyu4 lok6, Korean 오락 (orak), and Japanese ごらく (goraku) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real everyday word for "entertainment" in its language. Vietnamese ngu lạc is likewise compositional, from 娯's ngu — a real but low-frequency Hán Việt reading — and 楽's own lạc (as opposed to its other reading nhạc, "music," an unrelated sense of the same polyphonic character).
