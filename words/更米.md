## Notes
- [更 (char)](../characters/更%20(char).md) "change; more" + [米 (char)](../characters/米%20(char).md) "rice"

更米 is ordinary non-glutinous rice: the polished, round-grained japonica rice that is cooked as plain rice. The usual spelling is 粳米, an alias, because 粳 is an alias of 更. Wiktionary lists Chinese 粳米 (Cantonese gang1 mai5) as "polished, round-grained, non-glutinous rice; grains of japonica rice", and has no Japanese or Korean entry for it, so the word rests on Chinese alone. Korean 멥쌀, "plain rice", is the native word for it.

It is the sticky rice's opposite, set against [需米](需米.md), and it is the grain cooked as [米飯](米飯.md). Brown rice is the [玄米](玄米.md).
