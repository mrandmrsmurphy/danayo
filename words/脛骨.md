## Notes
- [脛](../characters/脛.md) "shinbone" + [骨 (char)](../characters/骨%20(char).md) "bone"

脛骨 is the tibia, the shinbone. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 脛骨 (Mandarin jìnggǔ, Cantonese ging3 gwat1) as the skeletal term "tibia; shinbone", and Japanese けいこつ as "a shinbone; a tibia". Korean 경골 and Vietnamese hĩnh cốt are built from the readings of the two characters.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [脛](../characters/脛.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
