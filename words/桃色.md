## Notes
- [桃](../characters/桃.md) "peach" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color"

桃色 is the color of a ripe peach blossom: a warm, soft pink. English Wiktionary lists the colour sense for Chinese ("peach; pink") and for Japanese ももいろ (pink or peach, marked slightly dated and usually pinker than English "peach", with ピーチ and ピンク as synonyms). It has no Korean entry for the word, so the Korean reading rests on this page's field. The association with 桃花源 (Peach Blossom Spring) gives the color a faint utopian or romantic literary resonance in Chinese contexts.

In Chinese the word also has a figurative sense of illicit love, gossip and sex (Wiktionary), most visibly in 桃色新聞 (a sex scandal in the press) and 桃色事件 (a love affair or scandal), so the neutral color sense and the romantic-erotic sense coexist. I did not confirm an erotic sense for the Japanese or Korean word. Dan'a'yo keeps the shared CJKV sense of the color, "pink, peach", with no divergence.
