## Notes
- [関 (char)](../characters/関%20(char).md) "gate; connect" + [節 (char)](../characters/節%20(char).md) "joint"

関節 is a joint: the place where two bones meet and move. Wiktionary glosses Japanese かんせつ as "joint (anatomy)". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 관절 matches.

It is the anatomical term, where [節](節.md) is a joint or node in general and [指関節](指関節.md) is a knuckle. The bones it joins are in the [骨格](骨格.md).
