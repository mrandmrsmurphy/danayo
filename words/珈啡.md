## Notes
- Phonetic: [珈](../characters/珈.md) "coffee" + [啡](../characters/啡.md) "caffeine" — a Chinese phonetic transliteration of "coffee," not a semantically compositional compound. This word is the stand-in for 珈, which cannot appear independently; 啡's own stand-in is 珈啡因.

Mandarin kāfēi, Cantonese gaa3 fe1, and Vietnamese cà phê are all real, standard phonetic transliterations of "coffee" in their own language, matching 珈's and 啡's own stored citations exactly. Japanese コーヒー (kōhī) is a direct katakana loanword from the same source rather than a Sino-Japanese on'yomi reading. Korean 가배 is a real, historically attested early Korean transliteration of "coffee" (from the same era as English "coffee" first entered Korea), traditionally written with the alternate hanja pair 珈琲 (stored in `aliases`) rather than Dan'a'yo's own 珈啡 — the compositional reading of Dan'a'yo's own characters (珈's 가 + 啡's 비) would instead give 가비, a form with no real attestation.
