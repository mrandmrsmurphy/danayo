## Notes
- [句 (char)](../characters/句%20(char).md) "sentence, clause" + [号](../characters/号.md) "mark, sign" — "period, full stop" (punctuation). `japanese: くてん` is 句点, the standard Japanese term for this punctuation mark. `korean: 고리점` is a real, if now somewhat dated, native Korean name for this mark (modern Korean usually says 마침표). `vietnamese: dấu chấm câu` is the standard everyday Vietnamese term (native, "sentence-dot mark"), not a Sino-Vietnamese calque of these two characters (domain knowledge, not independently confirmed by search).
