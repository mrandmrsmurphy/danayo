## Notes
- [磁](../characters/磁.md) "magnetic" + [帯](../characters/帯%20(char).md) "belt, tape" — "magnetic tape." Their own `stand_in` values: 磁's is [[磁性]]; 帯's is itself; none points here, so 磁帯 is an independent compound.

磁帯 is magnetic tape, the medium of cassettes, reels and videotapes. Mandarin cídài and Cantonese ci4 daai3-2 are the ordinary Chinese words. Japanese says テープ and Korean 테이프, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[磁盤]] (disk), [[光盤]] (CD) and [[磁性]] (magnetism).
