## Notes
- [陽 (char)](../characters/陽%20(char).md) "shine" + [傘](../characters/傘.md) "umbrella"

陽傘 means a parasol — an umbrella for shade from sunlight, as opposed to rain. Mandarin 陽傘/阳伞 (yángsǎn) and Sino-Vietnamese dương tán (classical register; modern Vietnamese favors the native ô dù/ô dù che nắng) both use this exact compositional term.

Japanese ようさん is itself rare/marginal (confirmed via web search as more of a Chinese-loan gloss than a live Japanese word) — the standard Japanese term for this concept is 日傘 (ひがさ, higasa), and 陽傘/遮陽傘/太陽傘 are all near-synonyms in Mandarin as well, sharing the core "sun-blocking umbrella" concept with slightly different emphasis (太陽傘 emphasizes "sun," 遮陽傘 emphasizes "shading").
