## Notes
- [皆 (char)](../characters/皆%20(char).md) "all; every; universal" + [類](../characters/類.md) "kind; sort; category; type"

皆類 is the collective (universal) correlative in the 類 ("kind") column of the correlative table: "all kinds; every kind; every category; all sorts", the predicate holding of the whole set of kinds at once. The character [皆 (char)](../characters/皆%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *gye*, ㄍ⼶) is the quantifier "all; every", the free word being [皆](皆.md); [類](../characters/類.md) (Dan'a'yo *lui*, ㄌㄨㄧ) supplies "kind; sort; category; type". There is no free word 類 in `words/`: the character page names [種類](種類.md) "type; kind" as its `stand_in`, so 類 is a bound character, and 種類 heads the 類 row of the chart in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md` (line 152, where the row header is 種類). The cell 皆類 is therefore a fixed paradigm word, as on [此類](此類.md). Illustrative only (invented, not from `grammar/`): 皆類、有 "every kind exists"; 皆類問題 "problems of every kind".

Its part of speech is 代詞 (pronoun), like [此類](此類.md), [其類](其類.md), [彼類](彼類.md), [何類](何類.md) and [某類](某類.md) in the same column, and like [皆事](皆事.md), [皆物](皆物.md), [皆人](皆人.md), [皆名](皆名.md), [皆処](皆処.md) and [皆時](皆時.md) in the same row. The only exceptions to 代詞 in the 皆 row are [皆様](皆様.md) and [皆多](皆多.md), which are 副用名詞 (adverbial noun) because the 様 and 多 columns are adverbial; and [毎類](毎類.md) is 副用名詞 as a cell of the 毎 row. The chart gives only form and 注音, with no part of speech, so this is a convention inferred from the sibling pages, not something the grammar files state; 皆類 takes 代詞 because both its row (collective universal) and its column (kind, as in 此類) are pronoun-like here.

It contrasts with its neighbours as follows. The distributive [毎類](毎類.md) "each kind" goes through the kinds one by one, the same split as [毎物](毎物.md) against [皆物](皆物.md); 皆類 treats them as one totality. [某類](某類.md) is the indefinite "some kind" (a 某 form is indefinite, not a universal), [何類](何類.md) is the interrogative "what kind?", and [此類](此類.md) "this kind", [其類](其類.md) "that kind" and [彼類](彼類.md) "that other kind" each point at one particular kind, where 皆類 covers all of them. Against [皆物](皆物.md) "all things": 皆物 quantifies over individual objects, 皆類 over classes of them. Against [皆様](皆様.md) "in every way": 様 is the manner of an action, 類 a category of things. Related vault words: [種類](種類.md) "type; kind" (the lexical noun), [各種](各種.md) "every kind; all kinds; full range" and [多様](多様.md) "diverse" (a 性詞). They are ordinary vocabulary, whereas 皆類 is the regular correlative form built from the table's own 皆. Wiktionary also has 諸種 (Chinese zhūzhǒng, "a wide variety (of)"; Japanese しょしゅ, "various kinds"), 種種 (zhǒngzhǒng, "a variety of; all sorts of; every") and the idiom 多種多樣 (duōzhǒngduōyàng, "varied; manifold"), but none of these has its own file in `words/`, so none is linked.

The cross-linguistic fields were checked against Wiktionary on 2026-10-11. Wiktionary has no entry for 皆類 or 皆类, so the word is a Dan'a'yo compositional construction and no `mandarin`, `japanese`, `korean` or `vietnamese` field is entered; none of the readings the page held before (Mandarin gèlèi, Japanese あらゆる種類, Korean 모든 종류, Vietnamese mọi loại) is a reading of the compound; they were equivalents of the meaning, and are removed. The nearest attested word is Chinese 各類 (gèlèi; simplified 各类; Cantonese gok3 leoi6), a pronoun glossed "all sorts of; all kinds of"; Wiktionary's 各種 (gèzhǒng) is a pronoun "a variety of; all sorts of; all kinds of; a number of; every", and the Japanese 各種 (かくしゅ) is a noun "all kinds of; every kind; a full range", matching the vault's [各種](各種.md). The earlier Notes text calling 各種各類 "the more emphatic" form was not found on Wiktionary and is dropped. The Korean 모든 종류, Japanese あらゆる種類 and Vietnamese *mọi loại* as natural spoken phrases are from memory, not re-checked; hvdic.thivien.net covers single characters, not phrases, so no Vietnamese reading was verified or entered. Dan'a'yo keeps the sense shared across CJKV, "all kinds; every kind"; no narrowing is needed.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md)'s correlative table (line 126 is the 皆 ("all") row; the same chart is in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`, line 152 in the 類 ("kind") row, with the column header cell 皆 on line 142), 皆類 is the ninth and last cell of the 皆 row, after [皆事](皆事.md) "all matters", [皆物](皆物.md) "all things", [皆人](皆人.md) "all people", [皆名](皆名.md) "all honoured persons", [皆処](皆処.md) "all places", [皆時](皆時.md) "all times", [皆様](皆様.md) "all manners" and [皆多](皆多.md) "the whole amount". Down the 類 column it pairs with [此類](此類.md), [其類](其類.md), [彼類](彼類.md), [何類](何類.md), [毎類](毎類.md) and [某類](某類.md). The table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), 63 forms, so 皆類 is parsed compositionally: 皆 = collective, 類 = kind. It is listed on the Grammar page only. The alias 皆类 is the simplified spelling (the character page 類 lists 类 as its alias); no separate word file exists for it.

The spellings are the plain concatenation of the two character pages: 羅馬字 *gye* + *lui* = gyelui, 諺文 겨 + 뤼 = 겨뤼, 注音 ㄍ⼶ + ㄌㄨㄧ = ㄍ⼶ㄌㄨㄧ, each checked letter by letter against 皆 (char) and 類. The 注音 carries no syllable-boundary dot. `grammar/文法 - 02音韻論.md` allows only the nasals ㄇ ㄋ ㄫ, the glides and the unreleased stops ㄆ ㄊ ㄎ as syllable-final consonants, and ㄌ is none of them, so ㄍ⼶ㄌ is not a syllable (`syllables/` has ㄍ⼶, ㄍ⼶ㄇ, ㄍ⼶ㄋ, ㄍ⼶ㄧ and ㄍ⼶ㄫ, but no ㄍ⼶ㄌ) and ㄌㄨㄧ must be a syllable of its own (it exists in `syllables/`). There is no second complete partition, the same as [此類](此類.md), [毎類](毎類.md), [皆処](皆処.md) and [皆多](皆多.md), and in contrast to [皆物](皆物.md) and [皆人](皆人.md), whose second syllables begin with ㄇ/ㄋ and so need the dot.
