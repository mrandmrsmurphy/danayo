## Notes
- [陣](../characters/陣.md) "camp (army)" + [地 (char)](../characters/地%20(char).md) "land"

陣地 means a military position or defensive emplacement — the ground a military force holds and fortifies. Mandarin 陣地/阵地 (zhèndì), Cantonese 陣地 (zan6dei6), Japanese 陣地 (じんち), Korean 진지 (jinji), and Sino-Vietnamese trận địa are all standard military terms across the CJKV sphere, and all extend figuratively to any position defended in a broader contest — political, ideological, or competitive (思想陣地, "ideological ground"; 陣地戦, "positional/trench warfare," as distinct from mobile warfare).
