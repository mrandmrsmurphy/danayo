## Notes
- [星 (char)](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" + [宿](../characters/宿.md) "lodging; constellation"

星宿 is a star mansion: one of the twenty-eight divisions of the sky in the Chinese system, and by extension a constellation or the stars generally. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xīngxiù as "constellation (archaic); the stars (literary); star mansion, one of the Twenty-Eight Mansions". Cantonese sing1 sau3 matches, Korean 성수 is the reading of the characters, and no Japanese reading is attested.

The full set is the [二十八宿](二十八宿.md). The modern Western constellation is the [星坐](星坐.md).
