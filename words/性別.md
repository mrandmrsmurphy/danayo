## Notes
- [性](../characters/性.md) "nature, gender" + [別](../characters/別%20(char).md) "distinguish, separate" — no stand-in relationship applies (性's own stand-in is [[個性]]; 別's own is itself).

性別 means sex or gender — the biological or social category of male/female.

Mandarin xìngbié, Cantonese sing3 bit6, Japanese せいべつ, Korean 성별, and Vietnamese tính biệt are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `pos: 名詞`/`vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄫㄅㄝㄊ unique across both words and characters).
