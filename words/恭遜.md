## Notes
- [恭](../characters/恭.md) "respectful" + [遜](../characters/遜.md) "humble, modest, yielding" — no stand-in relationship applies (恭's own `stand_in` is [[恭敬]]; 遜's own is [[遜色]]).

恭遜 means politeness, courteousness, civility — a genuinely deferential and respectful bearing, without the auto-antonym risk of [[客気]] (see that word's own page): respectful conduct grounded in real regard for the other person, not a performance of boundary-marking that can flip to coldness or (in Japanese/Korean) to reckless bravado depending on context.

Mandarin gōngxùn and Japanese きょうそん are both real, standard words for a modest, courteous bearing. Korean 공손 is an extremely common, everyday word for "polite, courteous" (공손하다, "to be polite"). Cantonese gung1 seon3 and Vietnamese cung tốn are the compositional readings from each character's own attested value; Vietnamese specifically is not independently attested as a fixed compound in the sources checked, so this is the mechanical rather than a confirmed living reading. `kwin` is false: 恭 is `kwin: false`, 遜 is `kwin: true`. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼄ㄫㄙㄛㄋ unique across both words and characters).
