## Notes
- [推](../characters/推%20(char).md) "push" + [薦](../characters/薦.md) "recommend" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [薦](../characters/薦.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (推's own stand-in is itself).

推薦 means to recommend.

Mandarin tuījiàn, Cantonese teoi1 zin3, Japanese すいせん, Korean 추천, and Vietnamese suy tiến are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄨㄧㄐㄝㄋ unique across both words and characters).
