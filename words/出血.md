## Notes
- [出 (char)](../characters/出%20(char).md) "exit" + [血 (char)](../characters/血%20(char).md) "blood" — "to bleed, hemorrhage" (出血性, "hemorrhagic"). Mandarin chūxuě, Cantonese ceot1 hyut3, Japanese しゅっけつ, Korean 출혈, and Vietnamese xuất huyết are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 춛훧 diverges from Sino-Korean 출혈 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄨㄊㄏ⼔ㄊ unique among both words and characters).
