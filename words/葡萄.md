- **Genuine `#cranberry`**: both [葡](../characters/葡.md) and [萄](../characters/萄.md) — each meaningless alone but never independently attested — have their own `stand_in` pointing to this exact compound (full transitivity; tag already correctly present).

Pronunciation fields (bodau/보닷/ㄅㄛㄉㄚㄨ) are the straightforward concatenation of each constituent's own stored reading — no bug; `kwin: false` already correct (AND-rule). **Found and fixed a real bug**: `cantonese` had "pu2 tao2," an invalid romanization matching neither constituent's own stored jyutping (pou4/tou4) — corrected to pou4tou4. Mandarin pútáo, japanese ぶどう, korean 포도 (also the real common word for "grape"), and vietnamese bồ đào (also a real classical term, e.g. 葡萄牙 Bồ Đào Nha "Portugal") all confirmed standard and compositional. No homophones.
