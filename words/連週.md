## Notes
- [連 (char)](characters/連%20(char).md) "consecutive, link" + [週](characters/週.md) "week, cycle" — "week after week."

Mandarin liánzhōu, cantonese lin4 zau1, japanese れんしゅう, and korean 연주 are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. Vietnamese liên chu is the honest compositional reading (連's own liên + 週's own chu); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 련줏 diverges from Sino-Korean 연주 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄌ⼶ㄋㄐㄨㄛ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 連's `stand_in` (連 itself) nor 週's own `stand_in` (週日) points to this compound.
