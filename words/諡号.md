## Notes
- [諡](../characters/諡.md) "posthumous name" + [号](../characters/号.md) "symbol, title"
- This word is the viable form of [諡](../characters/諡.md): the character's meaning (posthumous name) is identical, but 諡 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 諡号 is its `stand_in` compound.

諡号 is the posthumous name: an honorific title conferred on a ruler, noble or notable person after death, assessing their reign or character. 諡 is "posthumous name" and 号 "title, designation". Mandarin shìhào, Cantonese si3 hou6, Japanese しごう (shigō), Korean 시호 (siho) and Vietnamese thụy hiệu are the same compound, and the practice itself is an old institution of the Chinese sphere, taken up by Korea, Japan and Vietnam alike.

Dan'a'yo-specific sense: the shared one. It is kept apart from a ruler's personal name and from a reign title; it is the name bestowed after death, a closing judgment on the person. It sits with the monarchy vocabulary beside [[王位]] and [[在位]], which concern a ruler's time in office.
