## Notes
- [架 (char)](../characters/架%20(char).md) "frame; shelf" + [空 (char)](../characters/空%20(char).md) "empty; sky"

架空 is imaginary: not real, having no existence outside a story or an idea. Wiktionary glosses Japanese かくう as "fictitious" and, in other uses, "overhead". For Mandarin jiàkōng it gives "to fabricate; to make up" and "to be unfounded", as well as the physical "to build on stilts". Dan'a'yo takes the sense of the made-up. Cantonese gaa3 hung1 and Korean 가공 match.

It is literally "built on empty air". It describes a character, a place or a company that does not exist, and it stands opposite [実在](実在.md). The made thing itself is [虚構](虚構.md).
