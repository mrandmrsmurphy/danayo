## Notes
- [紅 (char)](../characters/紅%20(char).md) "red, crimson" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — red.

紅色 is the X色 form of [[紅]], red as a color name. In Mandarin hóngsè is the ordinary word for "red," and Cantonese hung4 sik1 and Korean 홍색 are the matching forms; Mandarin also uses hóngsè figuratively for "revolutionary" and for "prosperity." Japanese 紅色 is narrower: べにいろ or くれないいろ names crimson, a vivid red leaning toward pink, while plain "red" is 赤 (あか). That split is the same Japanese reversal described under Semantic Range Notes on the Color page. In Dan'a'yo, 紅色 is the default "red" for modern conversational register, and [[赤色]] is reserved for classical, literary or fixed-compound contexts.
