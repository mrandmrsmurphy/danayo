## Notes
- [陽 (char)](../characters/陽%20(char).md) "Yang" + [極 (char)](../characters/極%20(char).md) "extreme, pole"

陽極 means the anode — the positive electrode in an electrochemical or electronic device, paired against [[陰極]] ("cathode," the negative electrode). Mandarin 陽極/阳极 (yángjí), Cantonese 陽極 (joeng4gik6), Japanese 陽極 (ようきょく), Korean 양극 (yanggeuk), and Sino-Vietnamese dương cực are all the standard scientific/technical term for this concept across the CJKV sphere, straightforwardly compositional and consistent in every language.
