## Notes
- [鹿](../characters/鹿%20(char).md) "deer" + [児](../characters/児%20(char).md) "baby" + [島](../characters/島%20(char).md) "island"

鹿児島 is Kagoshima, written "deer child island". The reading かごしま is irregular: 鹿児 is read かご (probably "fawn" or an older word) with 児 read ご, so the characters are not read by their ordinary on-yomi, and 島 is しま. In Mandarin the second character is read ér.

Kagoshima lies at the southern tip of Kyushu across the bay from the active volcano Sakurajima. It was the Satsuma domain, home of Saigō Takamori, and the first European guns reached Japan on Tanegashima in 1543. It includes Yakushima, a World Heritage island, and is known for sweet potatoes and black pork.

In Dan'a'yo 鹿児島 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 가고시마 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
