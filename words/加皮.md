## Notes
- [加](../characters/加.md) "add" + [皮](../characters/皮.md) "skin" — no stand-in relationship applies (加's own stand-in is [[加算]], 皮's is [[皮革]]).

加皮 means a scab — the crust that forms over a healing wound. The character 加 here is not semantic ("add") but a same-Mandarin-reading (jiā) phonetic substitute for the rarer 痂 (no character page yet exists for it), which paired with 皮 "skin" forms the real, standard word 痂皮 — recorded as this word's alias.

**Real bugs found and fixed**: `mandarin`/`cantonese` were incomplete, covering only 加's own reading (jiā/gaa1) rather than the full compound (jiāpí/gaa1 pei4). Japanese かひ (KA + HI) and Korean 딱지 (a native word, no Sino-Korean reading is in common use for this term) were already correct; Vietnamese vảy (also native — verified as the standard translation) was likewise already correct. Removed the redundant `品詞` field. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄅㄧ unique across both words and characters).
