## Notes
- [密 (char)](../characters/密%20(char).md) "secret, intimate" + [会 (char)](../characters/会%20(char).md) "to meet" — no stand-in relationship applies (both characters' own `stand_in` point elsewhere: 密's is itself, 会's is itself).

密会 means a secret meeting — most often, two people meeting in secret to conduct a clandestine romantic affair, i.e. a secret date or tryst.

Mandarin mìhuì, Cantonese mat6 wui6, Japanese みっかい (mikkai — the correct reading, easily confused with the wrong みつかい), Korean 밀회, and Vietnamese mật hội are all standard, fully compositional readings. `kwin` is false per the AND-rule: `characters/密 (char).md` is `kwin: true`, `characters/会 (char).md` is `kwin: false` → compound `false`. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄧㄊㄏ⼔ unique across both words and characters).
