## Notes
- [十 (char)](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" + [七 (char)](../characters/七%20(char).md) "seven"

Mandarin shíqī, Cantonese sap6 cat1, Korean 십칠 (sipchil), and Japanese じゅうなな (jūnana, mixing Sino-Japanese じゅう with native なな for 七, an alternate to じゅうしち) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese thập thất is likewise compositional, from 十's thập and 七's own thất.
