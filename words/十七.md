## Notes
- [十 (char)](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" + [七 (char)](../characters/七%20(char).md) "seven"

Mandarin shíqī, Cantonese sap6 cat1, Korean 십칠 (sipchil), and Japanese じゅうなな (jūnana, mixing Sino-Japanese じゅう with native なな for 七, an alternate to じゅうしち) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese thập thất is likewise compositional, from 十's thập and 七's own thất. **Real `kwin` bug found and fixed**: was stored as `true`, but Dan'a'yo 십칟 does not exactly string-match Sino-Korean 십칠 — the second syllable's coda diverges (ㄷ vs. ㄹ), matching 七's own individual `kwin: false` on its own character page. Corrected to `kwin: false`. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄆㄑㄧㄊ unique among both words and characters).
