## Notes
- [甕](../characters/甕%20(char).md) "jar, jug, vat" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

甕 names a large earthenware jar or urn, traditionally used for storing water, grain, wine, or pickled food. See [[lexipedia/Containers]].

Mandarin wèng, Cantonese ung3, and Korean 옹 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese has no common on'yomi compound use of this character (it appears mainly with native kun readings such as かめ), so う represents the bare Sino-Japanese reading rather than an attested everyday word.
