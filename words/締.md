## Notes
- [締](../characters/締%20(char).md) "connection, knot, conclusion" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 締 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

締 means a connection, knot, or conclusion — most productively in 締結 (to conclude, as a treaty or contract) and 締切 (deadline, closing date).

Mandarin dì, Cantonese dai3, and Korean 체 (che) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the on-reading テイ (TEI) for the bare character alone, since no native kun-reading is stored. Vietnamese đế is one of three stored candidates. This word completes a genuine 3-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read tei/테/ㄊㄝㄧ) with [[剃]] ("shave") and [[諦]] ("truth [Buddhist]") — the reciprocal callout was already in place on both siblings, cross-referenced here.
