## Notes
- [偶](../characters/偶.md) "image, doppelganger" + [然 (char)](../characters/然%20(char).md) "so" — "accidental, coincidental, random" (偶然性, "randomness, contingency"; 偶然一致, "coincidence"). Mandarin ǒurán, Cantonese ngau5 jin4, Japanese ぐうぜん, Korean 우연, and Vietnamese ngẫu nhiên are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 옷년 diverges from Sino-Korean 우연 in the second syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄛㄨㄋ⼶ㄋ unique among both words and characters).
