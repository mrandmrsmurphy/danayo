## Notes
- Avoids the Chinese novel character 鍺/锗, coining a transparent compound instead — the same naming strategy used throughout this vault's periodic table (compare [[苦土素]] for magnesium, [[石灰素]] for calcium).
- [徳 (char)](../characters/徳%20(char).md) "virtue" + [素](../characters/素.md) "element" — literally "virtue-element," clipped from [[徳国]] ("Germany," itself a phonetic-plus-honorific rendering of the country name) + 素.

徳素 names germanium (Ge, atomic number 32), named after the country the way many elements are (compare [[美洲金]] "americium," [[法素]] "francium"). Mandarin zhě and Cantonese ze2 are the real, standard readings for germanium in those languages (of 鍺/锗, the character this vault's compound deliberately avoids in writing while still matching its pronunciation, the same convention already established on [[苦土素]] and [[石灰素]]). Japanese ゲルマニウム (gerumaniumu), Korean 저마늄 (jeomanyum), and Vietnamese gecmani are all the real, standard loanword terms (from English/German "germanium"), not kanji/hanja calques. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄊㄨㄎㄙㄛ unique among both words and characters).
