## Notes
- [水](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [族](../characters/族.md) "tribe, clan" + [館](../characters/館.md) "hall, building" — builds on [[水族]] ("aquatic animals") + 館 ("building"). No stand-in relationship applies (館's own stand-in is [[公館]], not this compound).

水族館 means aquarium.

Mandarin shuǐzúguǎn, Cantonese seoi2 zuk6 gun2, Japanese すいぞくかん, and Korean 수족관 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 水's, 族's, and 館's own stored citations exactly). **Genuine cross-linguistic divergence**: Vietnamese does not calque 水族館 directly — the standard Vietnamese word is thủy cung (水宮, literally "water palace"), the same kind of real-word divergence already documented on [[図書館]]'s own thư viện.
