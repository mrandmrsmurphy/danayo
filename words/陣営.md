## Notes
- [陣](../characters/陣.md) "camp (army)" + [営](../characters/営.md) "operate, manage" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes 陣 as a bound character (営's own stand-in is [[経営]]; transitivity fails, so no `#cranberry`).

陣営 means a military camp or encampment, and by extension a faction or side in a broader conflict or debate (political camps, opposing factions). Mandarin 陣營/阵营 (zhènyíng), Cantonese 陣營 (zan6jing4), Japanese 陣営 (じんえい), Korean 진영 (jinyeong), and Sino-Vietnamese trận doanh all share both senses — the literal military camp and the figurative "side" (e.g. 保守陣営/보수 진영/phe bảo thủ, "the conservative camp").
