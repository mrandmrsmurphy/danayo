## Notes
- [異](../characters/異.md) "different, other" + [端](../characters/端.md) "tip, beginning" — "other beginning," heresy. Their own `stand_in` values: 異's is [[異常]]; 端's is [[末端]]; none points here, so 異端 is an independent compound.

異端 is heresy: a teaching that departs from the doctrine a church holds as right. Mandarin yìduān, Cantonese ji6 dyun1, Japanese いたん and Korean 이단 all use it, and Vietnamese dị đoan does too. It is the boundary that the creeds and councils exist to draw; see [[信条]] and [[教義]]. Compare [[異教徒]] (an adherent of another religion).
