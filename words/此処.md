## Notes
- [此 (char)](../characters/此%20(char).md) "this; proximal demonstrative" + [処 (char)](../characters/処%20(char).md) "place; location"

此処 is the proximal place correlative in the 処 ("place") column of the correlative table: "here; at this place", the spot where the speaker is or which is being pointed to. The character 此 (Dan'a'yo *cǝ*, ㄑㄜ) is the classical Chinese proximal demonstrative, contrasting with 其 (medial) and 彼 (distal); 処 (Dan'a'yo *co*, ㄑㄛ) is the Japanese shinjitai form of 處, which the character page lists as an alias along with 处, and it supplies "place; location". Wiktionary analyses 処 as a compound of 夂 and 几 ("table"). Dan'a'yo 此処 is the compositional sinographic form generated by the correlative table, not a borrowed word.

The aliases are the orthographic variants of the same word: 此處 (traditional, via 處) and 此处 (simplified). Wiktionary has a Chinese entry 此處 (cǐchù; Cantonese ci2 cyu3), a pronoun labelled formal, glossed "here", whose zh-forms line gives 此处 as the simplified form, and a Japanese entry 此処 that redirects to ここ. 這裡 is deliberately not an alias: it is a different Chinese word (zhèlǐ, "here", with the variants 這裏 and 这里), not a variant spelling of 此處, and its reading is already held in the `mandarin` field.

The spoken equivalents held in the cross-linguistic fields are: Mandarin 這裡 (zhèlǐ, per Wiktionary "here"; the erhua form 這兒 zhèr is also listed there); Cantonese 這裡 read ze2 leoi5 (Wiktionary's Cantonese reading of that word), while Wiktionary separately lists the Cantonese-specific 呢度 (ni1 dou6) as "here", which is what a Cantonese speaker would more naturally say; Japanese ここ (koko), glossed there as "here; this place, area, or part (near the speaker)", with 此処 as its kanji spelling; Korean 여기 (yeogi, "here"); Vietnamese *tại đây* (from memory, not re-checked: hvdic.thivien.net has no entry for 此處, only for the single characters, and bare *đây* is the usual "here"). Because the correlative paradigm is a Dan'a'yo construction, these fields give the natural spoken equivalents, not compositional readings of 此 and 処.

In the grammar, 此処 appears in the sentence 捕已魚之男人 此処於 在 "The man who had caught the fish is here" (`grammar/文法 - 05形態.md`, relative-clause example 1), where 於 follows the noun as a postposition, as with 市場於 elsewhere in that file; the form is not 於此処.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md), 此処 is the fifth cell of the 此 ("this") row of the correlative table, after [此事](此事.md) "this matter", [此物](此物.md) "this thing", [此人](此人.md) "this person" and [此名](此名.md) "this person (polite)", and before [此時](此時.md) "this time", [此様](此様.md) "this manner", [此多](此多.md) "this amount" and [此類](此類.md) "this kind"; it is also in the page's short "here" list. Down the 処 column it pairs with [其処](其処.md) "there (medial)", [彼処](彼処.md) "yonder" (distal), [何処](何処.md) "where", [毎処](毎処.md), [某処](某処.md) and [皆処](皆処.md); the table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), 63 forms, and the same chart appears in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`. The 処 row is parallel to the temporal 時 row, so 此処/此時, 其処/其時 and 彼処/彼時 pair spatial and temporal deixis. 此処 is also entry 9 ("here") on the vault's [Swadesh](../lexipedia/Swadesh.md) list, matching the `swadesh: 9` property.

The 注音 ㄑㄜㄑㄛ carries no syllable-boundary dot: the only legal codas in `grammar/文法 - 02音韻論.md` are ㄇ, ㄋ, ㄫ, the glides y/w, and the unreleased stops ㄆ, ㄊ, ㄎ, and ㄑ is none of them, so ㄑㄜㄑㄛ has exactly one parse, ㄑㄜ + ㄑㄛ. Dan'a'yo keeps the sense shared across CJKV ("here"); no narrowing is needed.
