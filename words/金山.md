## Etymology
- [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal, gold" + [山 (char)](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain"

## Notes
Mandarin jīnshān and cantonese gam1 saan1 are directly attested standard readings. Japanese has two genuinely attested readings: きんざん (on-reading, "gold mine" as a generic term) and かなやま (native kun-reading, common as a surname/place name) — both kept, fixed from a malformed comma-string into a proper list. Korean 금산 and vietnamese kim sơn are both honest compositional readings (金's own 김/kim + 山's own 산/sơn).

Earlier Notes on this page may state a different value or a different reason.
