## Etymology
- [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal, gold" + [山 (char)](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain"

## Notes
Mandarin jīnshān and cantonese gam1 saan1 are directly attested standard readings. Japanese has two genuinely attested readings: きんざん (on-reading, "gold mine" as a generic term) and かなやま (native kun-reading, common as a surname/place name) — both kept, fixed from a malformed comma-string into a proper list. Korean 금산 and vietnamese kim sơn are both honest compositional readings (金's own 김/kim + 山's own 산/sơn). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 김산 diverges from Sino-Korean 금산 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄇㄙㄚㄋ unique among both words and characters).

**Found and fixed a missing citation** on 山(char).md's own Words list (only the longer compound 旧金山 was previously present). Citation on 金(char).md was already correctly present. Neither 金's `stand_in` (金 itself) nor 山's own `stand_in` points to this compound.
