## Notes
- [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "gold" + [辺](../characters/辺.md) "side, edge" — "golden edge"; no stand-in relationship applies (金 is its own stand-in; 辺's is [[辺界]]). The alias 金邊 is the traditional spelling and 金边 the simplified one, since 邊 and 边 are aliases of 辺.

金辺 is Phnom Penh, the capital of Cambodia ([[高綿]]). English Wiktionary gives the etymology as the earlier name 金塔 (still the Teochew form) plus a borrowing of Khmer ពេញ (Penh), and quotes Zhang Xiangshi's *Huaqiao zhongxin zhi Nanyang* (1927), which names it as the capital of Cambodia on the lower Mekong. The synonyms 百囊奔 and 南旺 are not words in the vault.

Mandarin Jīnbiān and Cantonese gam1 bin1 are as at English Wiktionary. Japanese プノンペン and Korean 프놈펜 are the ordinary transliterations of the Khmer name (from memory, not re-checked), so `kwin` is false. The `vietnamese` field holds Kim Biên, which English Wiktionary gives as the Vietnamese reading of the Han form (rare as a name).

No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄇㄅㄝㄋ unique across words and characters, checked 2026-10-12). The 注音 needs no syllable-boundary dot.
