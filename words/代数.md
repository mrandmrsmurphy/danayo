## Notes
- [代](../characters/代.md) "substitute" + [数](../characters/数.md) "number" — "algebra," the branch of mathematics using symbols to substitute for numbers. Directly attested and standard across Mandarin dàishù, Cantonese doi6 sou3, Japanese だいすう, Korean 대수, and Vietnamese đại số. `kwin` is true: both 代 and 数 independently match their own Sino-Korean readings. For the study of algebra itself, see [[代数学]].
