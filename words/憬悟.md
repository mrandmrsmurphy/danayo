## Notes
- [憬](characters/憬.md) "awaken" + [悟](characters/悟.md) "realize, awaken to understanding" — a synonym-doubling of two "awaken" roots. This word is the viable form of [憬](characters/憬.md): 憬 alone is too bound to stand as a Dan'a'yo word, and 憬悟 is its `stand_in` compound — an undistorted match, since 憬's own gloss already is simply "awaken," with nothing left over.

憬悟 (jǐngwù) is a real but literary classical compound for "to awaken to understanding, come to one's senses" — the same semantic territory as 悟's own stand-in [[覚悟]] ("resolve," lit. "awakened readiness"), just without 覚悟's narrowing toward firm resolve specifically. Worth flagging: Japanese けいご (keigo) is an exact homophone of the extremely common 敬語 ("honorific language") — 憬悟 itself is rare/obscure in Japanese, so in practice the sound けいご almost always means 敬語, not this word. **A second, more serious homograph-collision caveat**: `vietnamese: cảnh ngộ` is compositional from each character's own confirmed reading, but this exact syllable sequence is also the extremely common, unrelated Vietnamese word for "circumstance, situation, plight" (usually written 境遇, not 憬悟) — a coincidental homophone collision, not a translation error, but one that could seriously mislead if taken as the real usage for "awaken to understanding" (parallel to [[可不]]/[[嗣子]]/[[堤防]]/[[報酬]]'s documented collisions). No independent attestation of 憬悟's own Vietnamese reading was found via hvdic.
