## Notes
- [来 (char)](../characters/来%20(char).md) "come" + [客](../characters/客.md) "guest, traveller" — literally "a guest who comes".

来客 is a visitor or caller: someone who arrives at a house, office or shop. It is a synonym of [[客人]], and the two differ in angle. 客人 names a guest or traveller in general, while 来客 looks at the arrival from the host's side ("we have a visitor"), and so is the usual word for callers at a home or customers at a business. For the invited guest of honour at an event, use [[来賓]]. The shared CJKV sense is kept in Dan'a'yo.

Mandarin láikè and Cantonese loi4 haak3 are listed on Wiktionary for 來客, and Japanese らいきゃく is a standard word, as in 来客中 "(we) have a visitor". Korean 래객 is the compositional Sino-Korean reading (the South Korean form is 내객); everyday Korean says 손님. The Vietnamese lai khách is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and was not checked against a dictionary.
