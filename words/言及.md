## Notes
- [言 (char)](../characters/言%20(char).md) "say" + [及 (char)](../characters/及%20(char).md) "reach out to"

言及 means to mention, to refer to. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 言及 (Mandarin yánjí, Cantonese jin4 kap6) as the literary "to mention; to raise; to refer to", Japanese げんきゅう as "reference, mention" and "to refer to, to mention", and Korean 언급 as "mention".
