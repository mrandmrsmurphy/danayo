## Notes
- [学](../characters/学.md) "study" + [区](../characters/区.md) "district" — no stand-in relationship applies (学's own stand-in is [[学習]]; 区's own is [[区域]]).

学区 means school district — the administrative area a school serves, synonymous with [[校区]].

Mandarin xuéqū, Cantonese hok6 keoi1, Japanese がっく, Korean 학구, and Vietnamese học khu are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄎㄎㄨ unique across both words and characters).
