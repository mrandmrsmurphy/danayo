## Notes
- [拍](../characters/拍%20(char).md) "tap, clap, beat, pulse" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [拍](../characters/拍%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

拍 means to tap, clap, beat, or pulse.

Mandarin pāi, Cantonese paak3, Japanese はく, Korean 박, and Vietnamese phách are all standard, fully compositional readings. Homophone of [[鞄]] "bag; luggage" (both 注音 ㄆㄚㄎ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words; checked other candidates sharing this reading (朴/珀) and confirmed neither has an independent word at this exact reading.
