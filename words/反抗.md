## Notes
- [反 (char)](../characters/反%20(char).md) "against" + [抗](../characters/抗.md) "resist"

反抗 is to resist: to push back against authority or against a force, to rebel. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin fǎnkàng as "to revolt; to resist; to rebel" and Japanese はんこう as "resistance; rebellion; to resist, to rebel". Korean 반항, Vietnamese phản kháng and Cantonese faan2 kong3 match.

It is the act that grows out of [反感](反感.md), antipathy, and the opposite of obedience. A rising of many is a [暴動](暴動.md), riot.
