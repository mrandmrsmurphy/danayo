## Notes
- [六 (char)](../characters/六%20(char).md) "six" + [十 (char)](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" — "sixty." Mandarin liùshí, Cantonese luk6 sap6, Japanese ろくじゅう, Korean 육십, and Vietnamese lục thập are all directly attested standard readings (modern Vietnamese more commonly uses the native sáu mươi in everyday counting).
