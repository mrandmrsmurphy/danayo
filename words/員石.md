## Notes
- [員](../characters/員.md) "personnel; member (standing in for 隕, "to fall")" + [石 (char)](../characters/石%20(char).md) "stone"

員石 is a meteorite: a stone that has fallen from space to the ground. The real word is 隕石, which Wiktionary glosses as "meteorite" with Mandarin yǔnshí, Cantonese wan5 sek6, Japanese いんせき and Vietnamese vẫn thạch. 隕 has no vault page, so its phonetic 員 stands in for it, and 隕 is listed as an alias of 員; the Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and Vietnamese fields keep the readings of the real word. Korean 운석 matches the real word.

It is the solid remains of a [流星](流星.md), shooting star, that reaches the ground. A shower of them is the [流星群](流星群.md), and what falls is pulled by [重力](重力.md), gravity.
