## Notes
- [硼](../characters/硼.md) "borax" + [沙 (char)](../characters/沙%20(char).md) "sand" (written 砂) — 硼's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 沙's own is [[沙]] itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 硼 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

硼砂 is borax (sodium borate), a mineral historically mined from dried salt-lake deposits and used since antiquity as a flux in metalworking and glazing, and in modern times in glass, ceramics, cleaning products, and as a mild antiseptic. The character 硼 also serves as the abbreviation for boron (硼素) in this vault's periodic-table system, reflecting borax's historical role as the primary source through which the element was first isolated.

Mandarin péngshā, Cantonese pang4 saa1, Japanese ほうしゃ (hōsha), and Korean 붕사 (bungsa) are all fully compositional and are the standard mineralogical/chemical terms. Vietnamese hàn the is the real, standard Vietnamese term, not itself derived compositionally from 硼's own character reading.
