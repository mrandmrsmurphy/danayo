## Notes
- [蔑 (char)](../characters/蔑%20(char).md) "scorn" + [視 (char)](../characters/視%20(char).md) "look"

蔑視 is to look down on: to regard with scorn, contempt shown in the gaze. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin mièshì as "to despise; to scorn; to look down upon" and Japanese べっし as "contempt, scorn; despise". Korean 멸시, Vietnamese miệt thị and Cantonese mit6 si6 match.

It is the look that goes with [軽蔑](軽蔑.md), the feeling, and with the insult of [侮蔑](侮蔑.md). Its root is [蔑](蔑.md), scorn.
