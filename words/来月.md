## Notes
- [来](../characters/来%20(char).md) "come" + [月](../characters/月%20(char).md) "moon; month" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 来 and 月's own stand-ins are themselves, each with its own independent word page).

来月 means next month.

Mandarin láiyuè, Cantonese loi4 jyut6, and Japanese らいげつ are all standard, fully compositional readings.
