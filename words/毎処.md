## Notes
- [毎 (char)](../characters/毎%20(char).md) "every; each; distributive" + [処 (char)](../characters/処%20(char).md) "place; location"

毎処 is the distributive correlative in the 処 ("place") column of the correlative table: "at each place, per location, everywhere place by place". The character [毎 (char)](../characters/毎%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *moi*, ㄇㄛㄧ; its alias 每 is the traditional form) is the distributive morpheme of [毎日](毎日.md) "every day", [毎事](毎事.md) and [毎人](毎人.md); the free word is [毎](毎.md). [処 (char)](../characters/処%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *co*, ㄑㄛ) is the Japanese shinjitai form of 處, which the character page lists as an alias along with 处, and it supplies "place; location". The predicate is applied to each place in turn, and each case can be checked separately. Illustrative only (invented, not from `grammar/`): 毎処、標識有 "at each place there is a sign".

The aliases are the orthographic variants of the same word: 每處 (traditional, via 處), 每处 (simplified) and 毎處 (shinjitai 毎 with traditional 處); none of them is a separate word file in the vault.

Its part of speech is 副用名詞 (adverbial noun), the class of every cell of the 毎 row ([毎事](毎事.md), [毎物](毎物.md), [毎人](毎人.md), [毎名](毎名.md), [毎時](毎時.md), [毎様](毎様.md), [毎多](毎多.md), [毎類](毎類.md)) and of [毎日](毎日.md); `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md` (line 11) lists "Adverbial Nouns" as the class able to be the frame with 0-derivation. `grammar/Linguistics Features.md` (line 68) states the rule: an adverbial noun may be the topic or the frame of a clause with no case or adverbial marking, but needs marking when it modifies the main verb. So 毎処 can open a clause as a frame, whereas the other cells of the place column, [此処](此処.md), [其処](其処.md), [彼処](彼処.md), [何処](何処.md), [某処](某処.md) and [皆処](皆処.md), are pronoun-like.

It contrasts with its column neighbours as follows. The collective [皆処](皆処.md) "everywhere; all places" treats the places as one whole, whereas 毎処 distributes over them one at a time: the same distributive/collective split runs through the whole table, as in [毎人](毎人.md) against [皆人](皆人.md). [某処](某処.md) "somewhere" is the indefinite and [何処](何処.md) "where" the interrogative, so both are non-quantificational where 毎処 ranges over all places. [此処](此処.md) "here", [其処](其処.md) "there (medial)" and [彼処](彼処.md) "yonder" each point at one particular place. The 処 column is parallel to the temporal 時 column, so 毎処 pairs with [毎時](毎時.md) "each time".

The cross-linguistic fields were checked against Wiktionary on 2026-10-10. Wiktionary has no entry for 每處, 每处 or 毎処, so no `mandarin`, `japanese` or `korean` field is entered. It does have related but non-identical entries: Chinese 處處 (chùchù, "everywhere; in all respects"), 各處 (gèchù, "each area; each region", literary "everywhere") and 到處 (dàochù, "everywhere"), Japanese 至る所 (itaru tokoro, "everywhere, throughout"), and Korean 곳곳 (gotgot, a reduplication of 곳 "place", glossed "here and there; everywhere"). These are idiomatic neighbours, not equivalents of the compositional 毎処. From memory, not re-checked: Mandarin 每處 (měi chù) is a transparent compositional phrase and Korean 곳마다 uses the distributive -마다; both are omitted as fields. Vietnamese is omitted: hvdic.thivien.net covers single characters, not phrases. Dan'a'yo keeps the sense shared across CJKV, "at each place"; no narrowing is needed.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md)'s correlative table (line 125; the same chart is in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`, line 148 in the 処 ("place") row, with the column header cell 毎 on line 142), 毎処 is the fifth cell of the 毎 ("every") row, after [毎事](毎事.md) "every matter", [毎物](毎物.md) "each thing", [毎人](毎人.md) "per person" and [毎名](毎名.md) "each honoured person", and before [毎時](毎時.md) "each time", [毎様](毎様.md) "each manner", [毎多](毎多.md) "each amount" and [毎類](毎類.md) "each kind". The table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), 63 forms, so 毎処 is parsed compositionally: 毎 = distributive, 処 = place. The 羅馬字 is *moico*, from the character pages (毎 *moi*, 処 *co*); the earlier *moyco* disagreed with them.

The 注音 ㄇㄛㄧㄑㄛ carries no syllable-boundary dot. `grammar/文法 - 02音韻論.md` (line 116) gives the structure (C)(G)V(C) with a single coda, and the only legal codas are ㄇ, ㄋ, ㄫ, the glides y/w and the unreleased stops ㄆ, ㄊ, ㄎ; ㄑ is none of them, so ㄇㄛㄧㄑ + ㄛ is illegal, and ㄇㄛ + ㄧㄑㄛ fails because ㄧㄑㄛ is not a syllable (it would need ㄑ as a coda and has no initial). That leaves one parse, ㄇㄛㄧ + ㄑㄛ (both exist in `syllables/`), the same as [毎日](毎日.md) and [毎事](毎事.md), which carry no dot either.
