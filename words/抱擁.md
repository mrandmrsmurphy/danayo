## Notes
- [抱](../characters/抱.md) "embrace" + [擁](../characters/擁.md) "embrace, hug" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes BOTH characters as independent Dan'a'yo entries: both 抱's and 擁's own `stand_in` is 抱擁.

抱擁 means to embrace or hug.

Mandarin bàoyōng, Cantonese pou5 jung2, Japanese ほうよう, Korean 포옹, and Vietnamese bão ủng are all standard, fully compositional readings. Trimmed malformed comma-duplicate `mandarin` (bàoyǒng, a non-standard tone variant) to single bàoyōng. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄨㄛㄫ unique across both words and characters).
