## Notes
- [匈](../characters/匈.md) "Xiongnu" + [奴](../characters/奴.md) "slave" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [匈](../characters/匈.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (奴's own stand-in is [[奴隷]]).

匈奴 names the Xiongnu, the ancient confederation of nomadic peoples on the Mongolian steppe, longtime rivals of the Han dynasty.

Filled the blank `japanese: きょうど` (KYOU + DO, verified as the standard Japanese term).
