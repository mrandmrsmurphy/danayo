## Notes
- [袋 (char)](../characters/袋%20(char).md) "sack, bag" is separately legitimized by [[袋]] itself. [鼠](../characters/鼠.md) "rat" is separately legitimized by [[熊鼠]]. No cranberry.

Mandarin dàishǔ is standard compositional. Japanese カンガルー and korean 캥거루 are both genuine English loanword transliterations ("kangaroo"), not compositional; vietnamese chuột túi ("pocket mouse") is the real, standard, non-compositional Vietnamese term — legitimate.
