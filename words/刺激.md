## Notes
- [刺 (char)](../characters/刺%20(char).md) "stab, prick, sting" + [激](../characters/激.md) "incite, provoke" — "to provoke, irritate, stimulate" (刺激的, "stimulating, exciting"). Mandarin cìjī, Cantonese ci3 gik1, Japanese しげき, and Korean 자극 are all directly attested standard readings; Vietnamese `kích thích` (from 激's own kích + 刺's own thích, in reversed order from the Chinese original — a common pattern in Sino-Vietnamese loans) is likewise standard and everyday. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 칙걱 diverges from Sino-Korean 자극 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄎㄍㄝㄎ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 激's own `stand_in` is this exact word (刺激), so 激 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 刺激.
