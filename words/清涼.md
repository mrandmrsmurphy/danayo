## Notes
- [清](../characters/清.md) "pure, clear" + [涼](../characters/涼.md) "refreshing, cool" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes the bound character 涼 as a Dan'a'yo entry (涼's `stand_in` is 清涼; 清's own is [[清潔]]).

清涼 is "cool; refreshing": a pleasant coolness, such as a breeze, a drink or a mountain morning, in contrast to the neutral or unpleasant cold of [[冷]]. English Wiktionary gives the Chinese adjective as "cool; refreshing", with the antonym 燥熱 ("hot and dry") and the derived terms 清涼剔透 and 清涼油. It adds a second, euphemistic sense, sometimes used especially of women: "scantily clad", said of clothing. Japanese 清涼 (せいりょう, seiryō) is both a na-adjective, "refreshing; cool", and a noun for "the cool of the day".

Mandarin qīngliáng and Cantonese cing1 loeng4 are as at English Wiktionary. Korean 청량 is the Sino-Korean reading of the characters; I have not checked its everyday use. Dan'a'yo keeps the shared sense, which the Physics page lists as a pleasant coolness distinct from the neutral cold of 冷.

No other word shares the 注音 ㄑㄧㄫㄌ⼘ㄫ (checked 2026-10-12).
