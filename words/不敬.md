## Notes
- [不](../characters/不%20(char).md) "not" + [敬](../characters/敬.md) "respect" — "no respect," irreverence. Their own `stand_in` values: 不's is itself; 敬's is [[尊敬]]; none points here, so 不敬 is an independent compound.

不敬 is irreverence: the lack of the respect owed to a person of rank or to something sacred. Mandarin bùjìng, Cantonese bat1 ging3, Japanese ふけい, Korean 불경 and Vietnamese bất kính all use it. In East Asian law it was a crime against the ruler, so it reads as an offence against the highest authority.

**Hebrew**: it best translates the godlessness of *ḥānēf* (חָנֵף) "godless, profane" (Job 8:13; Isaiah 9:16), and the lack named by "there is no fear of God in this place" (Genesis 20:11). The Greek is *asebeia*, "ungodliness." Compare [[神聖]] (holy), the thing it fails to honor.
