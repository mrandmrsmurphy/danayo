## Notes
- [限](../characters/限.md) "limit, boundary" + [定](../characters/定.md) "decide"

限定 means to restrict or limit — to fix a definite boundary on a quantity, scope, or eligibility. Mandarin 限定 (xiàndìng), Cantonese 限定 (haan6deng6), Japanese 限定 (げんてい), Korean 한정 (hanjeong), and Sino-Vietnamese hạn định are all everyday words for this concept, common in commercial ("limited edition," 限定版/한정판/phiên bản hạn định) and legal/administrative contexts alike (限定條件, "restrictive condition").
