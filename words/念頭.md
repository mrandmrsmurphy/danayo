## Notes
- [念](../characters/念.md) "think, mindfulness" + [頭](../characters/頭%20(char).md) "head" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [念](../characters/念.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (頭's own stand-in is itself).

念頭 means idea or thought — a notion or intention that comes to mind, as in 念頭に浮かぶ ("to occur to one").

Mandarin niàntou, Cantonese nim6 tau4, Japanese ねんとう, Korean 염두, and Vietnamese niệm đầu are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed missing "(char)" suffix on `characters` for 頭. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄝㄇㄊㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).
