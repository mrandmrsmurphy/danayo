## Notes
- [先](../characters/先.md) "before, earlier" + [祖](../characters/祖.md) "ancestor" — ancestor.

先祖 is an ancestor or forebear. It is "earlier-ancestor." Mandarin xiānzǔ (marked literary; Cantonese sin1 zou2), Japanese せんぞ and Korean 선조 are all confirmed on Wiktionary, with synonyms Mandarin 祖先 and 先人 and Japanese 祖先; its antonym in Japanese is 子孫. Dan'a'yo's general word is [[祖先]], and 先祖 is the near-synonym with the characters in the opposite order, a little more literary; the founding ancestor is [[始祖]], and the descendants are [[子孫]].
