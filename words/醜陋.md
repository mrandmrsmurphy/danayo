## Notes
- [醜](../characters/醜.md) "ugly" + [陋](../characters/陋.md) "crude"


醜陋 means ugly: unattractive and unsightly in looks, and in Chinese also loathsome, as of conduct. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 醜陋 (Mandarin chǒulòu, Cantonese cau2 lau6) as "ugly; unattractive; plain-looking; unsightly" and "abominable; loathsome; detestable", and Japanese しゅうろう as "ugly" and "ugliness". It has no Korean entry, so Korean 추루 here is built from the characters.

[[陋]] is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo; 醜陋 serves as its stand-in word. The other word for ugly built on 醜 is [[醜悪]], and its opposite is [[美]], beautiful.
