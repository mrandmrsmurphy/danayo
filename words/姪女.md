## Notes
- [姪 (char)](../characters/姪%20(char).md) "niece" + [女 (char)](../characters/女%20(char).md) "woman, girl" — "niece," explicitly marked female.

姪女 is a real, valid, more explicit compound (literally "niece-woman"), now a near-synonym of the bare word [姪](姪.md), which stands independently as Dan'a'yo's general word for "niece." Japanese めい and Korean 조카딸 are real native-word choices rather than Sino calques, consistent with the vault's "real word over mechanical derivation" convention. Vietnamese điệt nữ is a formal, classical Sino-Vietnamese term; everyday Vietnamese uses the native cháu gái instead.
