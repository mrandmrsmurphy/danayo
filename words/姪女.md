## Notes
- [姪 (char)](../characters/姪%20(char).md) "niece" + [女 (char)](../characters/女%20(char).md) "woman, girl" — "niece," explicitly marked female.

**No longer 姪's legitimizer as of 2026-09-21**: 姪 (char) was previously bound, legitimized only by this compound; it has since been broadened to stand independently as its own word, [[姪]] — see that word's own Notes. 姪女 remains a real, valid, more explicit compound (literally "niece-woman"), now simply a near-synonym of bare 姪 rather than the sole way to say "niece" in Dan'a'yo. `japanese: めい` and `korean: 조카딸` are both real native-word choices rather than Sino calques, consistent with the vault's "real word over mechanical derivation" convention. `vietnamese: điệt nữ` is a formal/classical Sino-Vietnamese term; everyday Vietnamese uses the native cháu gái instead (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 덛느 diverges entirely from Korean's native 조카딸. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄝㄊㄋㄜ unique among both words and characters).
