## Notes
- [攻 (char)](../characters/攻%20(char).md) "attack" + [撃 (char)](../characters/撃%20(char).md) "strike"

攻撃 is an attack, and to attack: to move against an enemy or a target. Wiktionary glosses Japanese こうげき as "an attack; to attack". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 공격 matches.

It is the general word, where [進撃](進撃.md) is a charge forward and [襲撃](襲撃.md) is a sudden raid. The two sides of a fight are the 攻 and the defense, as in [攻防](攻防.md).
