## Notes
- [循](../characters/循%20(char).md) "abide by, follow" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

循 means to abide by, follow, or comply with — proceeding along an established path or rule, as in 循環 ("cycle, circulation").

Mandarin xún, Cantonese ceon4, Japanese じゅん, Korean 순, and Vietnamese tuần are all standard, fully compositional readings.
