## Notes
- [軽 (char)](../characters/軽%20(char).md) "light" + [重 (char)](../characters/重%20(char).md) "heavy" — literally "lightness and heaviness"; by extension, the relative importance of things, or the proper weighing of priorities against one another.

Wiktionary: Japanese 軽重 "heavy and light", "significant and negligible; serious and slight" and "relative importance" (with a variant reading, keijū); Chinese 輕重 "lightness and heaviness; weight; force, intensity; volume of a sound; importance; seriousness, severity; propriety", kept as the traditional-form alias. Neither character is legitimized here: both 軽's and 重's `stand_in` are themselves.
