## Notes
- [筋](../characters/筋.md) "tendon, sinew, muscle" + [肉 (char)](../characters/肉%20(char).md) "meat, flesh" — 筋's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 肉's own is [[肉]] itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 筋 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word (already correctly noted). `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

筋肉 is the general anatomical term for muscle tissue — the fibrous tissue responsible for movement, distinct from 筋 alone (which more narrowly denotes tendon/sinew) by naming the muscle organ as a whole.

Mandarin jīròu, Cantonese gan1 juk6, Japanese きんにく (kinniku), and Korean 근육 (geunyuk) are all standard, fully compositional readings, each the ordinary everyday medical/anatomical word for "muscle" in its language. Vietnamese cân nhục is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form; everyday Vietnamese more often uses the native cơ bắp.
