## Notes
- [肯 (char)](../characters/肯%20(char).md) "willing" + [定](../characters/定.md) "fixed"

肯定 is affirmation, to affirm. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 肯定 (Mandarin kěndìng, Cantonese hang2 ding6) as "affirmative; positive", "sure", "surely; definitely" and "to approve; to confirm; to affirm", Japanese こうてい as "affirmation" and "to affirm, answer yes", and lists Korean 긍정 as a hanja form of "affirmation". Its counterpart is [否定](否定.md), negation.
