## Notes
- [憤](../characters/憤.md) "resent" + [怒](../characters/怒%20(char).md) "angry" — no stand-in relationship applies (憤's own stand-in is [[憤慨]]; 怒's own is itself).

憤怒 means indignant or angry — a near-synonymous doubling of two characters both expressing anger.

Mandarin fènnù, Cantonese fan5 nou6, Japanese ふんぬ, Korean 분노, and Vietnamese phẫn nộ are all standard, fully compositional readings.
