## Notes
- [怒](../characters/怒%20(char).md) "angry" + [号](../characters/号.md) "cry, roar" — no stand-in relationship applies (怒's own stand-in is itself; 号's own is [[符号]]).

怒号 means to howl in anger or roar — a loud, furious outcry.

Mandarin nùháo, Cantonese nou6 hou4, Japanese どごう, Korean 노호, and Vietnamese nộ hào are all standard, fully compositional readings.
