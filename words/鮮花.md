## Notes
- [鮮](../characters/鮮.md) "fresh" + [花](../characters/花.md) "flower" — no `#cranberry`: 鮮's own `stand_in` is [[新鮮]], 花's is [[草花]]; neither points here.

Mandarin xiānhuā, Cantonese sin1faa1, Japanese せんか, Korean 선화, and Sino-Vietnamese tiên hoa are all standard, fully compositional readings; everyday Vietnamese instead more often uses the native phrase hoa tươi ("fresh flower").
