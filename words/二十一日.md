## Notes
- [[二十]] "twenty" + [一 (char)](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — the twenty-first day of the month.

Mandarin èrshíyī rì, Cantonese ji6 sap6 jat1 jat6, Japanese にじゅういちにち, and Korean 이십일일 are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Vietnamese content note**: filled with ngày hai mươi mốt (native "day twenty-one," using the everyday native numeral hai mươi mốt for 21, matching the pattern already established on [[二十]] itself), rather than a compositional Sino-Vietnamese calque — Vietnamese calendar dates are always expressed with native numerals in ordinary usage.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 늬십읻닏 diverges from Sino-Korean 이십일일, since all four constituent characters independently carry `kwin: false` on their own character pages.

**Found and fixed missing citations**: this word was entirely absent from `一(char).md`, `十(char).md`, and `二(char).md`'s own Words sections — added to all three.
