## Notes
- [依 (char)](../characters/依%20(char).md) "rely on, accord with" + [然 (char)](../characters/然%20(char).md) "so" — "as before, still, unchanged" (依然として, 舊態依然 "unchanged old ways"). `vietnamese: y nhiên` is directly attested, familiar from classical poetry — e.g. Tào Đường's 桃花流水依然在. `korean` is 의연, the genuine Sino-Korean reading of 依然 itself (as in 의연하다/구태의연) — distinct from the different etymon 如前 (yeojeon, "as before" via a different character pair). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 의년 diverges from Sino-Korean 의연 in the second syllable's vowel. Homophone with [[毅然]] "resolutely, firmly" (注音 ㄧㄜㄋ⼶ㄋ shared, distinct characters).
