## Etymology
- [依 (char)](../characters/依%20(char).md) "rely on, accord with" + [然 (char)](../characters/然%20(char).md) "so"

## Notes
"As before, still, unchanged" (依然として, 舊態依然 "unchanged old ways"). `vietnamese: y nhiên` is directly attested (confirmed via web search, familiar from classical poetry — e.g. Tào Đường's 桃花流水依然在). Filled the blank `pos: 副用名詞`.

**Real content bug found and fixed**: `korean` had been set to "여전," which is actually the reading of the different etymon 如前 (yeojeon, "as before" via a different character pair) — corrected to 의연, the genuine Sino-Korean reading of 依然 itself (confirmed via web search, as in 의연하다/구태의연). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 의년 diverges from the now-corrected Sino-Korean 의연 in the second syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄧㄜㄋ⼶ㄋ unique among both words and characters).
