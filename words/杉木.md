## Notes
- [杉](../characters/杉.md) "cedar" + [木 (char)](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree" — 杉木 is the stand-in word for [杉](../characters/杉.md); 杉 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse.

杉木 is the Chinese fir, *Cunninghamia lanceolata*, and its wood. English Wiktionary lists it only for Chinese (Mandarin shānmù, standard in Taiwan, and shāmù, standard in the mainland; Cantonese caam3 muk6), glossed "Chinese fir wood". English Wikipedia says the Chinese fir is a species of the cypress family, native to south-central and southeast China. It has no entry for Japanese or Korean, so Japanese さんもく and Korean 삼목 here are built from the characters; the Vietnamese field is built the same way.

The bare character 杉 means different trees by language. In Chinese it is "fir (various species of conifers)", with 杉木 as the Chinese fir; in Japanese すぎ and in Korean it is the Japanese cedar, *Cryptomeria japonica*. 杉木 is the stand-in word for [杉](../characters/杉.md), which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo.
