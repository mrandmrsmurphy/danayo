## Notes
- [臘](../characters/臘.md) "Laba" (this word is its stand-in) + [八 (char)](../characters/八%20(char).md) "eight"

The 8th day of the 12th lunar month (臘月). This word serves as the stand-in for [臘](../characters/臘.md), which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse — the character's meaning is inseparable from this specific date. See [[臘八節]] for the festival itself.
The date is the Laba Festival's day, traditionally the Buddhist commemoration of the Buddha's enlightenment, marked by eating 臘八粥 (Laba porridge); see [[臘八粥]]. Japanese ろうはち and the Korean and Vietnamese readings are the bound Sino-Xenic readings, since the festival is a Chinese observance rather than a native one. Dan'a'yo uses it as the name of the date, with the festival itself being [[臘八節]].
