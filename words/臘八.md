## Notes
- [臘](../characters/臘.md) "Laba" (this word is its stand-in) + [八 (char)](../characters/八%20(char).md) "eight"

Wiktionary lists 臘八 under Chinese as the Laba Festival. Wikipedia: the Laba Festival (臘八節) is a traditional Chinese holiday on the eighth day of the twelfth month of the Chinese calendar, when Laba congee is customarily eaten; it was fixed on that day under Buddhist influence, being the Buddha's enlightenment day, and corresponds to the Japanese Rohatsu. This word is the stand-in for 臘, which is bound. See [[臘八節]] for the festival and [[臘八粥]] for the porridge.
