## Notes
- [必](../characters/必%20(char).md) "surely, definitely" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

必 means surely or definitely — expressing certainty, as in 必要 ("necessary") and 未必 ("not necessarily").

Mandarin bì, Cantonese bit1, Japanese ひつ, Korean 필, and Vietnamese tất are all standard, fully compositional readings.
