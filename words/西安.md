## Notes
- [西](../characters/西.md) "west" + [安](../characters/安.md) "peaceful" — "western peace". No stand-in relationship applies (西's is [[西方]]; 安's is [[平安]]).

西安 (Xi'an) is the capital of Shaanxi province in northwestern China, on the site of the ancient capital Chang'an (from memory, not re-checked). Per Wiktionary the name is usually glossed "Western Peace", also "Western Pacification" or "Pacified Area", and was first adopted under the Ming dynasty in 1369 when the city was taken from the Yuan and given a new wall. The same spelling also names two districts of the same name, one in Liaoyuan (Jilin) and one in Mudanjiang (Heilongjiang).

Japanese せいあん, Korean 서안 and Vietnamese Tây An are the Sino-Xenic readings listed on Wiktionary; the Korean field here holds the North Korean reading derived from the character pages, 서안 (the character 西 has North Korean 서), and the usual South Korean name today is 시안 (from memory, not re-checked). Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese entries. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄧㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
