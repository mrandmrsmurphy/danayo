## Notes
- 迦 + [克 (char)](../characters/克%20(char).md) + [敦](../characters/敦.md) — phonetic transcription of *Chalcedon*, the ancient city on the Bosphorus (modern Kadıköy, Istanbul).
- Site of the Council of Chalcedon (451 AD). Note: 迦 does not yet have a character file; its Dan'a'yo phonology (ㄍㄚ) is inferred from Korean 가 and Japanese か.

Following this vault's proper-noun/named-referent convention (cf. [[冥王星]], [[出谷記]]), Vietnamese Canxêđoan is that language's own real, independently-attested name for this place (via French "Chalcédoine"), not a compositional transliteration of the Dan'a'yo/Chinese characters. Mandarin Jiākèdūn, cantonese gaa1 hak1 deon1, japanese かこくとん, and korean 가극돈 are all directly attested phonetic transcriptions using these same characters. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 가쿡돈 diverges from Sino-Korean 가극돈 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄎㄨㄎㄉㄛㄋ unique among both words and characters). Both character-page citations (克 and 敦) already correctly present.
