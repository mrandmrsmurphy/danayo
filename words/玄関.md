## Notes
- [玄](../characters/玄%20(char).md) "dark, deep" + [関](../characters/関%20(char).md) "gate, barrier" — "deep gate," the entry hall. Their own `stand_in` values: 玄's is itself; 関's is itself; none points here, so 玄関 is an independent compound.

玄関 is the vestibule: the entry hall of a house, where shoes come off in Japan. Mandarin xuánguān (玄關) and Cantonese jyun4 gwaan1 are literary, and Japanese げんかん and Korean 현관 are the everyday words. Compare [[会堂]] and [[門]].
