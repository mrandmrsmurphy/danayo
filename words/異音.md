## Etymology
- [異](../characters/異.md) "different, strange" + [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" — no stand-in relationship applies (異's own stand-in is [[異常]]; 音's own is [[音楽]]).

異音 means allophony.

Japanese いおん (I+ON) and Korean 이음 were already correct and fully compositional. Fixed a serious contamination bug in `mandarin`: tóngwèiyìyīn (the full four-character technical term 同位異音, "allophone") had been substituted for this word's own two-character reading → corrected to yìyīn. Filled the blank `cantonese` with ji6 jam1, fully compositional. Vietnamese tha âm vị is the real, standard Vietnamese linguistics term for "allophone," kept as-is rather than forced to a strict compositional reading. Fixed two malformed YAML entries on `characters/音.md` (a garbled `japanese_native` mixing hyphen and comma notation, and a comma-joined single-string `vietnamese` list). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/empty `aliases: []`. No homophones (注音 ㄧㄨㄇ unique across both words and characters).
