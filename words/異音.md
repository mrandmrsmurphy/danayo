## Notes
- [異](../characters/異.md) "different, strange" + [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" — no stand-in relationship applies (異's own stand-in is [[異常]]; 音's own is [[音楽]]).

異音 means allophony.

Japanese いおん, Korean 이음 and Cantonese ji6 jam1 are fully compositional, and Mandarin is the two-character yìyīn, not the full technical term 同位異音 tóngwèiyìyīn, "allophone". Vietnamese tha âm vị is the standard linguistics term for "allophone", kept in place of a strictly compositional reading.
