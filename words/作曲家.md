## Notes
- [作 (char)](../characters/作%20(char).md) "make" + [曲](../characters/曲.md) "tune" + [家](../characters/家.md) "house; specialist"

作曲家 is a composer: a person who writes music. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin zuòqǔjiā as "composer" and Japanese さっきょくか as "composer". Korean 작곡가 is the same word. Cantonese zok3 kuk1 gaa1 matches.

The act is [作曲](作曲.md), and the written music is the [楽譜](楽譜.md). The wider term for the maker is the [音楽家](音楽家.md), musician.
