## Notes
- [活 (char)](../characters/活%20(char).md) "live" + [用](../characters/用.md) "use"

活用 is inflection, conjugation. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 活用 (Mandarin huóyòng, Cantonese wut6 jung6) as "to flexibly put to use" and "to inflect; to conjugate", Japanese かつよう as "application (the use of a thing)", "inflection" of a verb, adjective or noun, and the verbs "to utilize" and "to inflect, to conjugate", and Korean 활용 as "application" and the grammar senses "conjugation, declension, inflection". Compare [屈折](屈折.md), inflection.
