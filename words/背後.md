## Notes
- [背](../characters/背%20(char).md) "back" + [後 (char)](../characters/後%20(char).md) "after" — "back, rear, behind." No cranberry: neither 背 (own stand-in [[背]] itself) nor 後 (own stand-in [[後]] itself) is legitimized by this word.

This same transposition appears on several other citations of 後 on its own character page (最後, 以後, 前後 also show ㄏㄛㄨ instead of ㄏㄨㄛ) — flagged for a future check when the sweep reaches those words.
