## Notes
- [資](../characters/資.md) "property; wealth" + [料](../characters/料.md) "material; stuff"

資料 is materials, or data: the information that a study or an argument is built from. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin zīliào as "material; data" and also "means". Japanese しりょう, Korean 자료 and Cantonese zi1 liu6 match.

It is what the [調査](調査.md) gathers and the [分析](分析.md) examines. A [証拠](証拠.md) is data used as proof.
