## Notes
- [汚 (char)](../characters/汚%20(char).md) "dirty" + [璃 (char)](../characters/璃%20(char).md) "glass" + [他 (char)](../characters/他%20(char).md) "another" + [雲 (char)](../characters/雲%20(char).md) "cloud" — a Dan'a'yo-internal phonetic transliteration of "Oort" (the astronomer Jan Oort) + 雲 "cloud," matching the vault's naming pattern for the outer solar system (cf. [[冥王星]], [[䦧神星]]), except here the source name has no meaning to translate, only a sound to approximate.

**A genuine coinage, not a translated loan**: none of the source languages' own real term for the Oort Cloud is built from these four characters' own readings — Mandarin uses 奥尔特云 (àoěrtè yún), Japanese オールトの雲 (ōruto no kumo), Korean 오르트 구름 (oreuteu gureum), and Vietnamese đám mây Oort, each its own independent transliteration or partial calque of the astronomer's name. The frontmatter fields above are the compositional readings of 汚+璃+他+雲 themselves, given for cross-reference only, not claims of attested usage. `kwin` is false (AND-rule: 汚 true, 璃 false, 他 false, 雲 true — 他 alone would suffice to make the compound false). No homophones (注音 ㄛㄌㄜㄊㄜ·ㄨㄋ unique among both words and characters).
