## Notes
- [個 (char)](../characters/個%20(char).md) "individual" + [性](../characters/性.md) "nature; quality" — "the nature of the individual." No stand-in relationship applies (個's own stand-in is itself; 性's is [[性別]]).

個性 is a person's individuality: the distinctive character, temperament and manner that set one individual apart from others. It is the word for "personality" in the sense of what is particular to someone, and it also carries the Japanese sense of originality, as in making the most of each person's individual qualities. It is built on [[個人]] "the individual" and the suffix －性, but the meaning is lexicalized (not simply "the quality of being an individual"), which is why it has its own page. For a thing's general nature, see [[性質]], and for the bare suffix see [[－性]].

Mandarin gèxìng, Cantonese go3 sing3, Japanese こせい and Korean 개성 are all listed on Wiktionary with this meaning. Vietnamese writes the same characters as cá tính, the real Sino-Vietnamese reading (個 is cá), so the `vietnamese` field holds that rather than a different compositional reading.
