## Notes
- [聖](../characters/聖.md) "holy" + [礼 (char)](../characters/礼%20(char).md) "manners" — literally "holy rite."

聖礼 means a sacrament: a formal religious rite such as baptism or communion. Mandarin shènglǐ, Cantonese sing3 lai5, Japanese せいれい, and Korean 성례 are all standard, fully compositional readings — this reflects the Protestant-leaning Chinese Christian usage of 聖禮/圣礼 for "sacrament," alongside the parallel Catholic term 聖事 (not yet its own word in this vault), which would compose 聖 with 事 ("matter, affair") instead of 礼 ("rite"). Keeping 聖礼 anchored to this specific rite sense leaves 聖事 free for its own, more literal "holy matter/affair" sense whenever it's created.
