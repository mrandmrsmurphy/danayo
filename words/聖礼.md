## Notes
- [聖](../characters/聖.md) "holy" + [礼 (char)](../characters/礼%20(char).md) "manners" — literally "holy rite."

聖礼 means a sacrament: a formal religious rite such as baptism or communion. English Wiktionary lists 聖禮 (an alias, with 圣礼) as the Chinese Protestant word for "sacrament" (Mandarin shènglǐ, Cantonese sing3 lai5) and lists the parallel Catholic word 聖事 (shèngshì, Korean 성사) as "sacrament" too. It has no Japanese or Korean entry for 聖禮, so Japanese せいれい and Korean 성례 here are built from the characters. Its Vietnamese entry thánh lễ is a Han-form headword with no gloss given there, so no Vietnamese field is recorded.

聖事 is not yet a word in this vault. Keeping 聖礼 anchored to the rite sense leaves 聖事, which composes 聖 with 事 ("matter, affair"), free for its own more literal "holy matter" sense. Two particular sacraments are the [[洗礼]], baptism, and the [[聖餐]], Holy Communion.
