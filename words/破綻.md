## Notes
- [破 (char)](../characters/破%20(char).md) "break, rend" + [綻](../characters/綻.md) "tear, rip" — 綻's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 破's own is [[破]] itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 綻 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word. `vietnamese: phá trán` is compositional, using 破's hvdic-confirmed Hán Việt "phá" (now also added to 破's own character-page vietnamese field).

破綻 means a breakdown, collapse, or failure — most often financial (bankruptcy, insolvency) but also extended to plans, relationships, or negotiations coming apart at the seams, echoing the character's literal image of a seam tearing open (破綻を来す, "to come apart, to fail").

Mandarin pòzhàn, Cantonese po3 zaan6, Japanese はたん (hatan), Korean 파탄 (patan), and Vietnamese phá trán are all fully compositional and share this same figurative "collapse/failure" sense across the sphere.
