## Notes
- [栓](../characters/栓%20(char).md) "peg, stopper" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 栓 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

栓 means peg or stopper.

Mandarin shuān, Cantonese saan1, Japanese せん, Korean 전, and Vietnamese thoen are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 栓's own stored citation exactly). Added the missing `japanese`/`hsk_level` fields. No word-level homophones — the bound characters [[旋]] and [[選]] share this same 注音 (ㄙ⼔ㄋ) but neither has an independent word page of its own.

Dan'a'yo keeps the shared CJKV sense: a stopper or peg that closes an opening. It is placed on the Containers page, with the words for closing and covering a vessel, as the nearest existing home.
