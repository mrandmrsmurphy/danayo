## Notes
- [縦](../characters/縦%20(char).md) "selfish, arbitrary" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 縦 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

縦 means selfish or arbitrary — unrestrained, let loose — extended in a separate sense to "lengthwise, vertical," as in 縦縞 (vertical stripes).

Mandarin zòng, Cantonese zung3, and Korean 종 (jong) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading たて (tate, "vertical, length") rather than the on-readings ジュウ/ショウ (JUU/SHOU) for the bare character alone. No Vietnamese reading is stored on the character page. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
