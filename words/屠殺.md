## Notes
- [屠](../characters/屠.md) "butcher; slaughter" + [殺 (char)](../characters/殺%20(char).md) "kill" — this word is the viable form of [屠](../characters/屠.md): the character's meaning (to slaughter) is identical, but 屠 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 殺 ("to kill") reinforces and licenses it as a compound.

屠殺/屠杀 means to slaughter or massacre — covering both the literal butchering of livestock and, by extension, the mass killing of people.

Mandarin túshā, Cantonese tou4 saat3, Korean 도살 (dosal), and Japanese とさつ (tosatsu) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 屠's and 殺's own stored citations. Vietnamese đồ sát is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, matching 屠's đồ and 殺's own sát. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 도삳 diverges from Sino-Korean 도살 in the second syllable's coda, the same MC-checked-coda-vs-Sino-Korean-regularized divergence documented throughout this vault. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄙㄚㄊ unique among both words and characters).
