## Notes
- [屠](../characters/屠.md) "butcher; slaughter" + [殺 (char)](../characters/殺%20(char).md) "kill" — stand-in for [屠](../characters/屠.md), which cannot appear independently: its meaning (to slaughter) is identical, but 屠 alone is too bound to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 殺 ("to kill") reinforces it as a compound.

屠殺/屠杀 means to slaughter or massacre — covering both the literal butchering of livestock and, by extension, the mass killing of people.

Mandarin túshā, Cantonese tou4 saat3, Korean 도살 (dosal), and Japanese とさつ (tosatsu) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 屠's and 殺's own stored citations. Vietnamese đồ sát is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, matching 屠's đồ and 殺's own sát.
