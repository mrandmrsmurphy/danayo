## Notes
- [夏 (char)](../characters/夏%20(char).md) "summer"

夏 means "summer" (夏至, "summer solstice"; part of the canonical 春夏秋冬, "the four seasons").

Mandarin xià and Cantonese haa6 preserve the sense directly. Japanese カ/ゲ (KA/GE, on'yomi, formal register) alongside native なつ (natsu, "summer," the everyday word) cover the sense. Korean 하 (ha) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 여름 (yeoreum, "summer"). Vietnamese hạ is the standard Hán Việt reading (hạ chí 夏至, "summer solstice"; xuân hạ thu đông, "the four seasons"), though notably it is the less common of two genuine readings here: hè, an Old Sino-Vietnamese doublet of the same character from an earlier borrowing layer (the same pattern as trà/chè, "tea"), has become the dominant everyday word (mùa hè, "summer"; nghỉ hè, "summer vacation" — essentially fixed, unlike the more literary mùa hạ).
