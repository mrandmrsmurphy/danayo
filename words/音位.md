## Notes
- [音 (char)](../characters/音.md) "sound" (stand-in: [[音楽]]) + [位 (char)](../characters/位.md) "position, status" (stand-in: [[定位]]) — a phoneme, the abstract sound-unit of a language.

音位 means phoneme/phonemic, as invoked by `文法 - 02音韻論`'s 非音位的 ("not phonemic"). Mandarin yīnwèi and Korean 음위 are real, standard linguistics terms (the standard Vietnamese linguistics term "âm vị" matches compositionally too). Cantonese jam1 wai6 is a straightforward compositional reading. `kwin` is false: both 音 and 位 are individually false.

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot**: the concatenated string `ㄨㄇ⼔ㄧ` admits two legal partitions — the intended 音(ㄨㄇ)+位(⼔ㄧ), and a false alternate splitting 位's own syllable (⼔+ㄧ) — so the dot at the true 音|位 boundary is required: ㄨㄇ·⼔ㄧ.
