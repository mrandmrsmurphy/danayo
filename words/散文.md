## Notes
- [散](../characters/散.md) "scatter; loose" + [文](../characters/文.md) "writing"

散文 is prose: writing in ordinary sentences rather than in verse. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin sǎnwén as "prose" and Japanese さんぶん as "prose". Korean 산문 and Vietnamese tản văn are the same word. Cantonese saan2 man4 matches.

It is set against the verse of the [韻文](韻文.md). The informal kind is the [随筆](随筆.md), essay, and a longer piece is the [文章](文章.md).
