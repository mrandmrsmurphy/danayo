## Notes
- [往](../characters/往%20(char).md) "go towards" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

往 means to go towards — as in 往復 ("round trip") and 往来 ("coming and going, interaction").

Mandarin wǎng, Cantonese wong5, Japanese おう, Korean 왕, and Vietnamese vãng are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (⺢ㄫ/'wang/왕) with the unrelated [[王]] ("king").
