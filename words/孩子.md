## Notes
- [孩](../characters/孩.md) "baby; child" + [子](../characters/子.md) "child; son" — `characters/孩.md`'s own `stand_in` points to this word (孩子), meaning 孩 is a bound morpheme that never appears independently outside this compound; 子's own points elsewhere (児子).

孩子 covers children of any age from infancy through the teens, and can also mean "my child/children," even of an adult child. Mandarin háizi, Cantonese haai4 zi2, and Japanese がいし are the standard readings. Korean 해자 is the genuine Sino-Korean compositional reading, though narrow and uncommon (specifically a child of two to three years old); it is also an exact homophone of the unrelated and far more common 해자 ("moat"), a homophone across different hanja. The everyday Korean words for "child" are 어린이 and 아이. Vietnamese hài tử is a real Sino-Vietnamese word; the everyday native equivalent is trẻ con.
