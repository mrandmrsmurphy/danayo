## Notes
- [堆](../characters/堆.md) "pile, heap" + [肥](../characters/肥.md) "fertile, manure" — no stand-in relationship applies (堆's own stand-in is [[堆積]]; 肥's is [[肥満]]).

堆肥 means compost or composted manure.

Japanese たいひ, Korean 퇴비, and Vietnamese đôi phì are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄧㄅㄨㄧ unique across both words and characters).
