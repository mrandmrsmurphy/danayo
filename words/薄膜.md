## Notes
- [薄](../characters/薄.md) "thin" + [膜](../characters/膜.md) "membrane"
- This word is the viable form of [膜](../characters/膜.md): the character's meaning (membrane; thin layer) is identical, but 膜 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 薄膜 is its `stand_in` compound.

薄膜 is the general technical term for a thin film or membrane: a layer of material whose thickness is negligible next to its other dimensions, from a biological membrane to a metal or plastic coating. In physics and engineering (薄膜 in Mandarin, はくまく in Japanese, 박막 in Korean) it names the thin-film technologies behind coatings, solar cells and semiconductors.

The compound pairs 薄 "thin" with 膜 "membrane", so the whole is "thin membrane" and the two elements reinforce each other. Because the character 薄 is bound (its stand-in is [[希薄]]) and 膜 is bound as well (its stand-in is this word), 薄膜 is a free Dan'a'yo word that stands in for 膜. In Dan'a'yo the sense is the shared CJKV one, covering both "membrane" and "thin film", with no divergence.
