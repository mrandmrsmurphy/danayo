## Notes
- [累](../characters/累.md) "accumulate" + [計](../characters/計.md) "measure"
- This word is the viable form of [累](../characters/累.md): the character's meaning (to accumulate) is identical, but 累 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 累計 is its `stand_in` compound.

累計 means a cumulative or running total: figures added up from the start to a given point, as in 累計売上 ("cumulative sales to date") or 累計人数 ("total number of people so far"). 累 here is "to pile up, accumulate" (Mandarin lěi, not lèi "tired"; Korean 누, Japanese るい) and 計 is "to total". Mandarin lěijì, Cantonese leoi5 gai3, Japanese るいけい (ruikei) and Korean 누계 (nugye) are the same compound.

Dan'a'yo-specific sense: the shared one. Among the counting and totalling words it names the total that keeps growing as more is added, where [[総数]] is the grand total of a whole and [[計数]] is the act of counting.
