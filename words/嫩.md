## Notes
- [嫩](../characters/嫩%20(char).md) "delicate, tender" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 嫩 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

嫩 means delicate or tender — soft, young, or unhardened, as of a plant shoot or skin.

Mandarin nèn, Cantonese nyun6, Japanese どん, Korean 눈, and Vietnamese nộn are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄛㄋ unique across both words and characters).
