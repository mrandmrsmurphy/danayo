## Notes
- [展](../characters/展.md) "expand; unfold" + [示](../characters/示.md) "show; reveal; indicate" — no stand-in relationship applies (展's own `stand_in` is [[伸展]], 示's is [[開示]]).

展示 means to display or exhibit — unfolding something outward so it can be seen, the everyday verb for putting things on show (展示会 "exhibition event," 展示品 "exhibit item").

Japanese てんじ and Korean 전시 are both standard, non-drifted readings. **`vietnamese` is left omitted**: no Sino-Vietnamese reading was confirmed via Wiktionary or search — Vietnamese instead uses the distinct compound triển lãm (展覽, "exhibition") for this concept. No specific HSK level number is confirmed (Wiktionary marks the compound only "Advanced Mandarin"). No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄋㄍㄝ unique across both words and characters).
