## Notes
- [掘](../characters/掘%20(char).md) "dig, excavate" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [掘](../characters/掘%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

掘 means to dig or excavate: to open a hole, trench, or shaft in the ground by removing earth, and by extension to unearth something buried (Mandarin 挖掘, "excavate"; 發掘, "unearth, discover"; 掘井, "dig a well"). The character is a 形声 compound of 扌 "hand" with the phonetic 屈, and the sense is the same across the sphere.

Mandarin jué, Cantonese gwat6, Japanese くつ (採掘/さいくつ, "mining"; 発掘/はっくつ, "excavation"), Korean 굴 (채굴, 발굴) and Vietnamese quật (khai quật, "to excavate"; the character page lists several further Nôm-style variants, but quật is the Sino-Vietnamese reading) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native verb 掘る (ほる) for the everyday act of digging; the on'yomi くつ occurs only in compounds.
