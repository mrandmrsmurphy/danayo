## Notes
- [無](characters/無%20(char).md) "lack" + [定](characters/定%20(char).md) "settle" + [河](characters/河%20(char).md) "river" — a river in Shaanxi, China; no stand-in relationship applies (無's own stand-in is itself; 定's own is [[決定]]; 河's own is [[小河]]).

無定河 is the Wuding River. Unlike foreign-name transliterations (e.g. [[尼羅河]] "Nile," which uses non-compositional loanwords in Japanese/Korean), this is a genuine Chinese place name written in Han characters, so Japanese and Korean use the standard Sino-reading convention seen elsewhere ([[上海]], [[九天]], etc.): filled the blank `japanese` with むていが (on'yomi MU+TEI+KA) and `korean` with 무정하, both fully compositional and matching 無's/定's/河's own citations. Filled blank `cantonese` with mou4 ding6 ho4 (compositional) and blank `vietnamese` with the capitalized Sino-Vietnamese place-name form Vô Định Hà, matching the capitalization convention on other proper-noun word pages. Reformatted `characters` to a proper block-list with "(char)" suffixes. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`; added missing `kwin: false` (諺文 므정하 does not exactly match korean 무정하, matching the same 므/무 divergence seen on sibling 無-compounds). No homophones (注音 ㄇㄜㄐㄝㄫㄏㄚ unique across both words and characters).
