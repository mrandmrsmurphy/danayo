## Notes
- [無](../characters/無%20(char).md) "lack" + [定](../characters/定.md) "settle" + [河](../characters/河.md) "river" — a river in Shaanxi, China; no stand-in relationship applies (無's own stand-in is itself; 定's own is [[決定]]; 河's own is [[小河]]).

無定河 is the Wuding River. Unlike foreign-name transliterations (e.g. [[尼羅河]] "Nile," which uses non-compositional loanwords in Japanese/Korean), this is a genuine Chinese place name written in Han characters, so Japanese and Korean use the standard Sino-reading convention seen elsewhere ([[上海]], [[九天]], etc.): filled the blank `japanese` with むていが (on'yomi MU+TEI+KA) and `korean` with 무정하, both fully compositional and matching 無's/定's/河's own citations.
