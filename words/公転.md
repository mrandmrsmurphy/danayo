## Notes
- [公 (char)](../characters/公%20(char).md) "public; common" + [転](../characters/転.md) "turn"

公転 is orbital revolution: the travel of one body around another, as the earth goes around the sun. Wiktionary glosses Japanese as "revolution (the traversal of one body through an orbit around another body); to revolve". The Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the characters, and Korean 공전 matches.

It follows the body's [軌道](軌道.md), orbit, and takes a year for the [地球](地球.md). Its counterpart is [自転](自転.md), turning on its own axis.
