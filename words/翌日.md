## Notes
- [翌 (char)](../characters/翌%20(char).md) "the following" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — literally "following day."

翌日 names the following day — one of this vault's cluster of "next day" compounds, alongside the more literary [次日](次日.md) and everyday [明日](明日.md)/[来日](来日.md), distinguished mainly by register (翌日 leans formal/written, common in news and formal narration).

Mandarin yìrì, Cantonese jik6 jat6, Korean 익일 (igil), and Japanese よくじつ (yokujitsu) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese dực nhật is likewise compositional, from 翌's dực and 日's own nhật, and is directly attested as a real term for "the next day."
