## Notes
- [尭](../characters/尭.md) "lofty; excessive" (here standing in for its alias [[饒]], "abundant, plentiful") + [舌 (char)](../characters/舌%20(char).md) "tongue" — no stand-in relationship applies (舌's own `stand_in` is itself; 尭's own designation is 名専字, unrelated to this compound).

饒舌/尭舌 literally "abundant tongue," i.e. an overflowing, unstoppable tongue — talkative, glib, garrulous. 尭 here is this vault's chosen simplified stand-in for its true etymological source 饒 (already registered as 尭's own alias), the same simplification-substitute pattern as [[舎]] standing in for 舍, or [[対]] for 對.

Japanese じょうぜつ, Korean 요설, and Vietnamese nhiêu thiệt (verified — using 饒's own "nhiêu" reading, not 堯/尭's more famous "nghiêu" reading tied to the legendary sage-king Nghiêu/Yao) are all standard readings for talkativeness, generally carrying a mildly negative, glib-or-verbose connotation rather than neutral chattiness.
