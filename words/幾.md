## Notes
- [幾 (char)](../characters/幾%20(char).md) "several; a few; some" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [幾 (char)](../characters/幾%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

幾 marks an indefinite small quantity — "several, a few" — and interrogatively, "how many?" (幾個 "how many [items]," 幾點 "what time"). It combines with 何 to form [[幾何学]] ("geometry"), an old calque of the interrogative sense ("how much/many [measurement]").

Japanese き, Korean 기, and Vietnamese kỉ (the character's own field also lists cơ, which belongs to a different reading/sense of 幾, "small sign, omen" — not this "several/how many" sense) are all standard readings. No homophones among other words (注音 ㄍㄧㄜ; many unrelated characters share it, including [[机]], [[璣]], [[磯]], [[肌]], [[飢]], and [[畿]], but none of their own stand-in words collide with 幾's).
