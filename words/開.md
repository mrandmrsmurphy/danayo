## Notes
- [開 (char)](../characters/開%20(char).md) "open"

開 is to open: to unlatch a door, lid or container, and by extension to start or to turn something on. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 開 (alias 开; Mandarin kāi, Cantonese hoi1) as "to open; to unlatch" and "to start; to turn on"; it lists Japanese かい and Korean 개 as the Sino-Xenic readings and Vietnamese khai as the Sino-Vietnamese one.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [開 (char)](../characters/開%20(char).md) as a free word. The compounds [開放](開放.md), [開始](開始.md) and [公開](公開.md) build on it; its opposite is [閉](閉.md), to shut.
