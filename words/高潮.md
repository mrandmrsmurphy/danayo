## Notes
- [高](../characters/高%20(char).md) "high" + [潮](../characters/潮.md) "tide, surge" — "high tide," a climax, orgasm. Their own `stand_in` values: 高's is itself; 潮's is [[潮汐]]; none points here, so 高潮 is an independent compound.

高潮 is a climax, and, in the sexual sense, an orgasm. Its first meaning is "high tide" (the peak of the sea), extended to the peak of anything, a play's climax, and the peak of sexual pleasure. Mandarin gāocháo, Cantonese gou1 ciu4, Japanese こうちょう and Korean 고조 all use it, and Vietnamese cao trào does too; Mandarin and Cantonese use it for orgasm directly, while Japanese says 絶頂. Compare [[性交]] (to have sex) and [[情欲]] (lust).
