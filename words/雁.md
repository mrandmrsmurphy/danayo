## Notes
- [雁 (char)](../characters/雁%20(char).md) "wild goose" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; standalone form, 雁 is a 名専字 in Dan'a'yo, used chiefly in proper names and classical poetry.

雁 names the wild goose, a recurring image in classical Chinese poetry (associated with autumn migration, distant travel, and letters — wild geese were traditionally believed to carry messages) and in the chengyu [沈魚落雁](../chengyu/沈魚落雁.md) ("so beautiful that fish sink and geese fall from the sky," a stock phrase for great beauty). 雁 itself also directly supplies the phonetic component of [贋](贋.md) ("counterfeit"), one of this word's own two homophones — a genuine etymological link, not just a coincidental shared Dan'a'yo reading.

Mandarin yàn, Cantonese ngaan6, and Korean 안 (an) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese かり (kari, already 雁's own stored `japanese_native` value) is the everyday native reading. Vietnamese nhạn is the standard Hán Việt reading, one of two candidates stored on 雁's own page (alongside nhẹn), the same reading as its homophone [贋](贋.md).
