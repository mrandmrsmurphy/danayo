## Notes
- [法 (char)](../characters/法%20(char).md) "rule, law" + [則](../characters/則.md) "rule" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 則 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (法's own stand-in is itself, so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

法則 means law, rule, code.

Mandarin fǎzé, Cantonese faat3 zak1, Japanese ほうそく, and Korean 법칙 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 法's and 則's own stored citations exactly).
