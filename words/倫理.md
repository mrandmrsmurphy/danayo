## Notes
- [倫](../characters/倫.md) "ethics" + [理](../characters/理.md) "-ology, reason, logic" — "ethic, morality." Mandarin lúnlǐ, Cantonese leon4 lei5, Japanese りんり, Korean 윤리, and Vietnamese luân lý are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 룬리 diverges from Sino-Korean 윤리 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄨㄋㄌㄧ unique among both words and characters). The study of ethics itself would be 倫理学 ("ethics, the discipline"), but no dedicated vault page exists for it yet.

Legitimizing note: 倫's own `stand_in` is this exact word (倫理), so 倫 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 倫理.
