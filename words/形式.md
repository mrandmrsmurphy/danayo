## Notes
- [形 (char)](../characters/形%20(char).md) "shape" + [式](../characters/式.md) "style; formula"

形式 is form: the visible shape of a thing, and form as opposed to content; as an attribute it means formal, concerned with outward form. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 形式 (Mandarin xíngshì, Cantonese jing4 sik1) as "form (visible shape)", "form as opposed to content" and, attributively, "formal", Japanese けいしき as "format", Korean 형식 as "form; template", and Vietnamese hình thức as "appearance; look".

It is not the same word as [型式](型式.md), a model or type, although the two share their Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings and their Dan'a'yo sound. The first character, [形](形.md), is shape.
