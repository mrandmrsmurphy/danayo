## Etymology
- [軍](../characters/軍.md) "armed forces" + [艦](../characters/艦.md) "warship"

## Notes
Mandarin jūnjiàn, cantonese gwan1 laam6, japanese ぐんかん, korean 군함, and vietnamese quân hạm are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. `军舰` is the standard simplified-character spelling variant (軍→军, 艦→舰), correctly listed as an alias. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 군함 matches Sino-Korean 군함 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄨㄋㄏㄚㄇ unique among both words and characters).

Semantically narrower than [[艦船]] ("seagoing vessels" generically) — 軍艦 specifies a military warship. Citation already correctly present on 艦.md; 艦's own `stand_in` is 艦船 and 軍's own `stand_in` is 軍隊, so this compound legitimizes neither constituent.
