## Notes
- [索](../characters/索.md) "rope; search" + [引 (char)](../characters/引%20(char).md) "pull"

索引 is an index: a list at the end of a book that tells where each topic is found. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin suǒyǐn and Japanese さくいん as "index", and lists Korean 색인. Cantonese sok3 jan5 matches.

It is a tool for finding things in a [事典](事典.md) or [詞典](詞典.md), and it is also used of a database index. The word is literally "search and pull".
