## Notes
- [闘](../characters/闘.md) "struggle, fight, compete, contend" + [争](../characters/争.md) "fight, dispute" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes 闘 as a bound character (争's own stand-in is [[抗争]]; transitivity fails, so no `#cranberry`).

闘争 means struggle or fight, particularly a sustained, organized contest — political, social, or class struggle rather than a single physical scuffle. Mandarin 鬥爭/斗争 (dòuzhēng) is closely associated with Marxist and revolutionary political vocabulary (階級鬥爭, "class struggle"), a usage shared by Sino-Vietnamese đấu tranh (đấu tranh giai cấp, "class struggle") and Korean 투쟁 (tujaeng, common in labor and civil-rights contexts, e.g. 노동 투쟁, "labor struggle"). Japanese 闘争 (とうそう) carries the same politically-charged register, prominent in the vocabulary of postwar student and labor movements (闘争心, "fighting spirit"; 学生闘争, "student struggle").
