## Notes
- [鞍](../characters/鞍.md) "saddle" + [装 (char)](../characters/装%20(char).md) "dress up, clothe" — 鞍装 is 鞍's own legitimizing compound (鞍's `stand_in` points here), but since 装's own `stand_in` is [[装]] itself, transitivity fails and no `#cranberry` tag applies.

鞍装/鞍裝 (ānzhuāng) is a real, attested Mandarin compound meaning "saddle equipment; to saddle" (cf. 鞍装具, "saddlery"), though the plainer everyday word for a physical saddle is 鞍子 (ānzi, listed here as an alias) or 马鞍 (mǎ'ān). Cantonese 鞍裝 (on1zong1) follows the same compositional pattern. Japanese あんそう (ansō) is likewise attested, especially in horse-racing terminology (cf. 装鞍所, "saddling enclosure"), distinct from 鞍's own bare native reading くら used for the object itself. Korean 안장 (anjang) is the standard, everyday word for "saddle."

Sino-Vietnamese yên ngựa ("horse saddle") is used here rather than the more general/bicycle-associated yên xe, matching 鞍's specifically equine sense.

**Fixed real bugs**: `mandarin` had held [[鞍子]]'s own reading (ānzi) instead of this compound's own (ānzhuāng); `japanese` had held 鞍's bare native reading (くら) instead of the compound's own on'yomi (あんそう); `注音` had a spurious extra ㄋ inserted before the final ㄫ (ㄚㄋㄐ⺢**ㄋ**ㄫ → correct ㄚㄋㄐ⺢ㄫ) — the same extra-ㄋ error had propagated into both character pages' citations and has been fixed there too.
