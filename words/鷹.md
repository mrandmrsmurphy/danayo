## Notes
- [鷹](../characters/鷹%20(char).md) "hawk" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

鷹 means hawk — a bird of prey.

Mandarin yīng, Cantonese jing1, Korean 응, Japanese おう, and Vietnamese ưng are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄧㄫ/'ing/잉) with the unrelated [[応]] ("respond, reply").
