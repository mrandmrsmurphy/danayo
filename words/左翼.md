>[!caution] The senses split — the left wing or flank, or the political left
>左翼 is the left wing of a body or army, the left flank. Chinese and Japanese also use it for the political left and for a left winger in sports, so a listener may hear the political sense. Listed as a Split on [文法 - 91疑義語](../grammar/文法%20-%2091疑義語.md).

## Notes
- [左](../characters/左.md) "left (stand-in: [[左側]])" + [翼](../characters/翼.md) "wing (stand-in: [[羽翼]])"

Wiktionary: Chinese 左翼, literally "left wing" and in politics "left wing", in sports "left-winger" and in the military "left flank"; Japanese 左翼 (さよく), politics, "left wing", sports "left wing", military "left flank". Its counterpart is [[右翼]].
