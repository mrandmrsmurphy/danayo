## Notes
- [践](../characters/践.md) "tread" + [踏](../characters/踏.md) "step on"

践踏 means to trample. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 踐踏 (alias here; Mandarin jiàntà, Cantonese cin5 daap6) literally as "to trample (on)" and figuratively as "to do damage to; to ravage (dignity, sovereignty, etc.); to ruin". The Japanese せんとう, Korean 천답 and Vietnamese tiễn đạp fields are built from the readings of the two characters.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [踏](../characters/踏.md), which is too bounded to stand alone. The stand-in of 践 is [実践](実践.md).
