## Notes
- [践](../characters/践.md) "tread" + [踏](../characters/踏.md) "step on" — 践踏 is the stand-in for [踏](../characters/踏.md), which is too bounded to stand alone (践's own stand-in is instead [[実践]]).

践踏 means "to trample" — both the literal sense (to tread on and crush underfoot) and, more commonly in modern usage, the figurative sense of forcefully disregarding or violating something abstract (rights, dignity, the law), as in 请勿践踏草坪 ("please keep off the grass") versus 践踏人权 ("to trample on human rights"). The two characters are near-synonyms (践 "to tread, to fulfill" + 踏 "to step on"), the same intensifying-doublet pattern as other 動詞+動詞 compounds in this vault.

Japanese せんとう is the direct on'yomi compositional reading (践 SEN + 踏 TOU), matching 践's and 踏's own character-page citations exactly. Korean 천답 and Vietnamese tiễn đạp are likewise standard compositional Sino-readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 전탑 diverges from Sino-Korean 천답 in both syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄋㄊㄚㄆ unique across both words and characters).
