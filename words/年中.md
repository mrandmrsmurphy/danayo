>[!caution] The senses split — "middle of the year" against "throughout the year"
>年中 is the middle of the year in Mandarin, and throughout the whole year in Japanese and Cantonese. A speaker meaning one may be heard as meaning the other. Listed as a Split on [文法 - 91疑義語](../grammar/文法%20-%2091疑義語.md).

## Notes
- [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year" + [中](../characters/中.md) "middle"

年中 has two senses: the middle of the year, and throughout the whole year. English Wiktionary glosses Mandarin niánzhōng as "middle of the year; mid-year period", and Cantonese nin4 zung1 also as "often; throughout the year". For Japanese it lists ねんじゅう as "the period of a year", "a one-year period" and "being continuous through a whole year, all year", and a separate ねんちゅう as a four-year-old child in education. It has no Korean or Vietnamese entry, so Korean 연중 and Vietnamese niên trung here are built from the characters. The word is listed as a Split on [文法 - 91疑義語](../grammar/文法%20-%2091疑義語.md).

The two senses come from the two readings of 中: spatial, "the middle of", and durational, "within the span of". Both senses are given on this page, the Mandarin "mid-year" first, and the Japanese and Cantonese "throughout the year" second.
