## Notes
- [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year" + [中](../characters/中.md) "middle"

年中 splits into two related but distinct senses across the CJKV sphere. In Mandarin, it most commonly names the **midpoint of the year** — 年中考試 ("midyear exam"), 年中報告 ("midyear/half-year report") — 中 read spatially, "the middle of." In Japanese, ねんちゅう/ねんじゅう instead means **throughout the whole year, year-round** — 年中無休 ("open every day of the year, no holidays"), 年中行事 ("annual events/observances") — 中 read durationally, "within the span of." Korean 연중 follows the Japanese pattern (연중무휴, "open year-round"). This is a genuine cross-linguistic sense split rather than an error in either language — both readings derive naturally from 中's own dual "middle point" / "within a span" senses.

Vietnamese niên trung (attested as "within the span of a year," per 一年之中 "within the span of one year") aligns with the durational, Japanese-style sense. No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄝㄋㄐㄨㄫ unique across both words and characters).
