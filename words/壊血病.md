## Notes
- [壊](../characters/壊.md) "spoil, ruin" + [血](../characters/血%20(char).md) "blood" + [病](../characters/病.md) "illness" — "blood-ruin illness," scurvy. Their own `stand_in` values: 壊's is [[破壊]]; 血's is itself; 病's is [[疾病]]; none points here, so 壊血病 is an independent compound.

壊血病 is scurvy: the disease caused by a lack of vitamin C, which makes the gums bleed and the body weaken. Mandarin huàixuèbìng (壞血病), Cantonese waai6 hyut3 beng6, Japanese かいけつびょう and Korean 괴혈병 all use it. Compare [[維他命]] (vitamin) and [[病院]] (hospital).
