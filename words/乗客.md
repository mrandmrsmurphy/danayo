## Notes
- [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "ride" + [客](../characters/客.md) "guest, visitor" — literally "riding guest," a passenger.

乗客 is the standard word for "passenger" across the Sinosphere: Mandarin chéng kè, Cantonese sing4 haak3, Japanese じょうきゃく (jōkyaku), and Korean 승객 are all fully compositional and mutually transparent, appearing constantly in transit contexts (乗客名簿, passenger manifest; 승객 여러분, "attention passengers"). **Vietnamese content note**: the real, everyday Vietnamese term for "passenger" is hành khách (行客, literally "traveling guest") rather than a direct compositional calque of this word's own characters (乗's thừa + 客's khách, which isn't independently attested) — filled here per the vault's real-usage-over-compositional convention.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 숭칵 diverges from Sino-Korean 승객, since both 乗 and 客 independently carry `kwin: false` on their own character pages.
