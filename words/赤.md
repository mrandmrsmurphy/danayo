## Notes
- [赤 (char)](../characters/赤%20(char).md) "red" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 赤 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

赤 is the older, more literary Sinitic word for "red," gradually displaced by [[紅]] as the everyday term for red across most of the modern Sinosphere — Mandarin hóngsè, not chìsè, is now the ordinary word for "red," and 赤 survives mainly in set compounds with an older or more formal register (赤道 "equator," 赤字 "deficit," literally "red characters"). Japanese reverses this division of labor: 赤 (あか, aka) remains the ordinary native word for "red," while 紅 is reserved for a narrower, more vivid "crimson" sense (口紅 "lipstick," 紅葉 "autumn leaves"). Mandarin chì, Cantonese cek3, Korean 적, Japanese せき (on'yomi, used in Sino-Japanese compounds like 赤道 sekidō), and Vietnamese xích are all standard, fully compositional readings matching 赤's own stored citations. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 척 diverges from Sino-Korean 적 in the initial consonant, since 赤 individually diverges from Korean on its own character page.
