## Notes
- [繁](../characters/繁.md) "lush, complicated" + [茂](../characters/茂.md) "overgrown, thick" — 繁's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 茂's own is [[茂密]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 繁茂 legitimizes 繁 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

繁茂 means lush or thick — flourishing, overgrown vegetation.

Mandarin fánmào, Cantonese faan4 mau6, Japanese はんも (hanmo), and Korean 번무 (beonmu) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 繁茂 is itself a common attested word for "lush, luxuriant" in all four languages. Vietnamese phồn mậu is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
