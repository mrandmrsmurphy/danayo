## Notes
- [原](../characters/原.md) "plain, original" + [来 (char)](../characters/来%20(char).md) "come" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (原's is 原始, 来's is 来).

Mandarin yuánlái, Cantonese jyun4 loi4, and Korean 원래 are standard, everyday words for "originally; as it turns out." Japanese has two genuine alternate readings, げんらい and がんらい. Vietnamese nguyên lai is a real Sino-Vietnamese word, though more literary and formal than the everyday equivalents (vốn dĩ, hóa ra).
