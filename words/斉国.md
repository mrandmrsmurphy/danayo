## Notes
- [斉](../characters/斉.md) "simultaneous" (here, the state of Qi) + [国](../characters/国.md) "nation" — no stand-in relationship applies (斉's own stand-in is [[一斉]]; 国's own is [[国家]]).

斉国 refers to the ancient state of Qi, one of the seven major Warring States powers.

Mandarin Qíguó, Cantonese cai4 gwok3, Japanese せいこく, Korean 제국, and Vietnamese Tề quốc are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `japanese` was missing the 国 half entirely (せい→せいこく), and filled blank `cantonese`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄧㄍㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
