## Notes
- [頑](../characters/頑.md) "stubborn, naughty" + [皮](../characters/皮.md) "skin, leather" — "stubborn skin," naughty. Their own `stand_in` values: 頑's is [[頑固]]; 皮's is [[皮革]]; none points here, so 頑皮 is an independent compound.

頑皮 is naughty: mischievous and unruly, said mostly of children. Mandarin wánpí and Cantonese waan4 pei4 are the ordinary words. Japanese and Korean do not use it that way, so those two fields hold the character readings. Compare [[悪]] and [[愚]] (foolish).
