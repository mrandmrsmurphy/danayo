## Notes
- [傑](../characters/傑.md) "remarkable, masterful" + [作 (char)](../characters/作%20(char).md) "make, do" — "masterpiece" (文学傑作, "a literary masterpiece"). Mandarin jiézuò, Cantonese git6 zok3, Japanese けっさく, Korean 걸작, and Vietnamese kiệt tác are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 걷작 diverges from Sino-Korean 걸작 in the first syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄝㄊㄐㄚㄎ unique among both words and characters).
