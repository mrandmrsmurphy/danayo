## Notes
- [陸](../characters/陸.md) "land" + [上](../characters/上%20(char).md) "above, on" — "on the land." Their own `stand_in` values: 陸's is [[大陸]]; 上's is itself; none points here, so 陸上 is an independent compound.

陸上 is "on land": the land side of things, as opposed to the sea or the air. Mandarin lùshàng, Cantonese luk6 soeng6 and Japanese りくじょう all use it (Japanese 陸上競技 is "track and field"); Korean 륙상 is built from the character readings. It is the first half of the way Dan'a'yo says "portage," 陸上運送, "transport on land," which needs no word of its own. Compare [[陸地]] (land) and [[運送]] (transport).
