## Notes
- [戦](../characters/戦.md) "war, battle" + [時](../characters/時%20(char).md) "when" — no stand-in relationship applies (戦's own stand-in is [[戦争]]; 時's own is itself).

戦時 means wartime.

Mandarin zhànshí, Japanese せんじ, and Korean 전시 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `cantonese` (zin3 si4) and `vietnamese` (chiến thời), both compositional. Homophone of [[展翅]] "to spread the wings; to take flight" (both jensi/전시/ㄐㄝㄋㄙㄧ) — a genuine Dan'a'yo-level coincidence between two otherwise unrelated compounds, already cross-referenced from 展翅's side.
