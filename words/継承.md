## Notes
- [継](../characters/継.md) "continue, persist" + [承](../characters/承.md) "inherit, receive" — 承's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 継's own is [[継続]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 継承 legitimizes 承 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

継承 means to accede to or inherit — a title, position, or tradition passed down.

Mandarin jìchéng, Cantonese gai3 sing4, Japanese けいしょう (keishō), and Korean 계승 (gyeseung) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 継承 is itself a common attested word for "inherit, succeed" in all four languages. Vietnamese kế thừa is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term for "inherit." No Dan'a'yo homophones.
