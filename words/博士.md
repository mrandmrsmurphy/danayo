## Notes
- [博](../characters/博.md) "broad, extensive" + [士](../characters/士%20(char).md) "scholar, gentleman" — no stand-in relationship applies (博's own stand-in is [[博大]]; 士 is its own stand-in).

博士 means a doctorate holder (PhD) — someone who has earned the highest academic degree.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `mandarin`/`cantonese` were both entirely blank — filled with bóshì/bok3 si6 (compositional from each character's own reading). Japanese はかせ (a special native reading, alongside the on'yomi はくし) and Korean 박사 are both standard readings. Vietnamese `bác sĩ` is compositionally correct but has drifted semantically in modern usage to mean specifically "medical doctor" — the term for a PhD holder in modern Vietnamese is "tiến sĩ" instead — documented here rather than corrected, since the compositional reading itself is accurate. Fixed empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄎㄙㄚㄧ unique across both words and characters).
