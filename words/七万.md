## Notes
- [七 (char)](../characters/七%20(char).md) "seven" + [万](../characters/万.md) "ten thousand" — a plain compositional numeral, 7 × 10,000.

**Real content bug found and fixed**: this entire page had previously been a mistaken copy of [[七千]]'s content — every field except `characters:` itself (mandarin, japanese, korean, 羅馬字/諺文/注音, and the English gloss) described "seven thousand" rather than "seventy thousand," inheriting 千's reading instead of using 万's own. Rewritten from scratch with 万's real Dan'a'yo/mandarin/cantonese/korean/japanese readings.

Vietnamese bảy vạn uses vạn as a fully live, ordinary Vietnamese numeral unit for 10,000 (as in vạn sự như ý, "may ten thousand things go as you wish") rather than a purely classical/literary form — unlike [[七千]]'s own thất thiên, which stays confined to Sino-Vietnamese register while everyday speech uses native bảy nghìn. Japanese ななまん again avoids しち (shichi, 七's on'yomi) in favor of なな for the same clarity-in-numerals reason already noted on [[七千]]. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 칟몬 and Sino-Korean 칠만 diverge in the first syllable's coda, the same [[七 (char)]] root-cause pattern (and the same [[一 (char)]]-class MC-checked-coda-vs-Korean-liquid divergence) just fixed one iteration ago.
