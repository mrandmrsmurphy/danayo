## Notes
- [七 (char)](../characters/七%20(char).md) "seven" + [万](../characters/万.md) "ten thousand" — a plain compositional numeral, 7 × 10,000.

Vietnamese bảy vạn uses vạn as a fully live, ordinary Vietnamese numeral unit for 10,000 (as in vạn sự như ý, "may ten thousand things go as you wish") rather than a purely classical/literary form — unlike [[七千]]'s own thất thiên, which stays confined to Sino-Vietnamese register while everyday speech uses native bảy nghìn. Japanese ななまん again avoids しち (shichi, 七's on'yomi) in favor of なな for the same clarity-in-numerals reason already noted on [[七千]].
