## Notes
- [振](../characters/振%20(char).md) "shake, excite, rouse" + [子](../characters/子.md) "son, child" — no stand-in relationship applies (振's own stand-in is itself; 子's own is [[児子]]).

振子 means pendulum (or, more technically, oscillator).

Mandarin zhènzǐ and Cantonese zan3 zi2 are standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `mandarin` was contaminated with bǎi (the reading of 擺/摆, an unrelated character used in the native Chinese word for "pendulum," 摆/擺), corrected to compositional zhènzǐ. Korean 진자 is also compositional. Japanese ふりこ (native, the everyday word for pendulum) and Vietnamese con lắc (native equivalent) are documented as expected. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄋㄐㄜ unique across both words and characters).
