## Notes
- [季](../characters/季.md) "season, youngest" + [秋](../characters/秋%20(char).md) "autumn" — no stand-in relationship applies (季's own stand-in is [[季節]]; 秋's own is itself).

Third and final month of autumn. Chrysanthemums are in full bloom; the Double Ninth Festival (重陽節) falls here. Corresponds to [[戌月]] and [[菊月]].

Mandarin jìqiū, Cantonese gwai3 cau1, Korean 계추, Japanese きしゅう, and Vietnamese Quý Thu are all standard, fully compositional readings.
