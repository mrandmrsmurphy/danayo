## Notes
- [激](../characters/激.md) "intense" + [昂](../characters/昂.md) "raise"

激昂 is to be roused: to be stirred to high, intense feeling, in anger or in passion. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin jī'áng as "roused; impassioned" and Japanese げきこう as "having extremely strong emotions; excitement, enraged, furious". Cantonese gik1 ngong4 matches, and Korean 격앙 is the reading of the characters.

It is anger or excitement at its height, beyond the settled [憤怒](憤怒.md), indignation, and kin to [昂揚](昂揚.md), exaltation, and [興奮](興奮.md), excitement.
