## Notes
- [分 (char)](../characters/分%20(char).md) "divide, part" + [子](../characters/子.md) "small thing, particle" — has two related senses: "molecule" (chemistry, phân tử in Vietnamese) and "numerator" (mathematics, phần tử in Vietnamese — two distinct tones/words for the two distinct senses). Mandarin fēnzǐ, Cantonese fan1 zi2, Japanese ぶんし, and Korean 분자 are all directly attested standard readings, covering both senses. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 분즈 diverges from Sino-Korean 분자 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄨㄋㄐㄜ unique among both words and characters).
