## Notes
- [悠](../characters/悠.md) "remote" + [久](../characters/久%20(char).md) "long time" — this word is the stand-in for its own character 悠, which is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo.

悠久 means long-lasting or time-honoured — enduring across a vast span of time.

Mandarin yōujiǔ, Cantonese jau4 gau2, Japanese ゆうきゅう, Korean 유구, and Vietnamese du cửu are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ⼜ㄛㄍ⼜ unique across both words and characters).
