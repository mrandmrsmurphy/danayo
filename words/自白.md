## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" + [白 (char)](../characters/白%20(char).md) "white; clear"

自白 is a confession, or to confess: to state of oneself what one has done. The character 白 is used here in the sense "to make clear", so the word is literally "to make oneself clear". Wiktionary glosses Japanese じはく as "confession; to confess" and Mandarin zìbái as "confession; to confess; to make oneself clear, to justify one's position". Cantonese zi6 baak6 and Korean 자백 match.

The word covers a legal confession to a crime. It also fills a gap noted on the Sin page, where "confess" had no Dan'a'yo word. It is the statement of the [犯人](犯人.md) or the [被告](被告.md), and it is distinct from [証言](証言.md), the testimony given by a witness.
