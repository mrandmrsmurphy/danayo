## Notes
- [関](../characters/関%20(char).md) "close" + [係](../characters/係.md) "be in relationship" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes 係 as a bound character (関's own stand-in is [[関]] itself; transitivity fails, so no `#cranberry`).

関係 means a relationship or connection, and by extension "to be related, to concern." Mandarin 關係/关系 (guānxì) — often heard toneless in casual speech as guānxi — is the term behind the well-known sociological concept of *guanxi*, the networks of reciprocal social and business obligation central to Chinese social life. Cantonese 關係 (gwaan1hai6), Japanese 関係 (かんけい), Korean 관계 (gwangye), and Sino-Vietnamese quan hệ are all likewise everyday, high-frequency words across registers from casual interpersonal relationships to formal diplomatic and international relations (国際関係/quan hệ quốc tế, "international relations").
