## Notes
- [依 (char)](../characters/依%20(char).md) "rely on, accord with" + [傍 (char)](../characters/傍%20(char).md) "beside, close"

In Dan’a’yo, 依傍 places something close against a landmark. In [[Classical Poetry]], 白日依傍山尽 pictures the setting sun against the mountains. The landmark is the object of 依傍.

Mandarin 依傍 also expresses reliance on someone or following a model. Vietnamese *y bàng* is attested in Buddhist writing with the sense of relying on or following something. The Dan’a’yo usage foregrounds spatial proximity, the sense contributed by 傍.
