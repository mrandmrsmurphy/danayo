## Notes
- [七](../characters/七%20(char).md) "seven" + [夕](../characters/夕.md) "evening, sunset" — no `#cranberry`: 夕's own `stand_in` is [[夕陽]], 七's is 七 itself; neither points here.

Names Qixi (the "Double Seventh Festival," China's traditional Valentine's Day, falling on the seventh day of the seventh lunar month). Mandarin qīxī, Cantonese cat1 zik6, and Korean 칠석 (Chilseok, the Korean observance of the same festival) are standard compositional readings. Japanese たなばた (Tanabata) is a native jukujikun reading applied to the whole compound rather than built from 七/夕's individual on'yomi — the real, standard Japanese name for the festival. `kwin` is false (AND-rule: 七 false, 夕 true) — corrected from a stale `true`. Fixed a missing word-space in `cantonese` and the duplicate `品詞` field.
