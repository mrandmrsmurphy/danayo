## Notes
- [降](../characters/降.md) "descend, lower oneself" + [参](../characters/参.md) "present oneself, attend" — to surrender; to give up and present oneself to the opponent

Wiktionary lists 降参 only under Japanese (こうさん): "surrender" and, as a verb, "to surrender". Neither character is legitimized here: 降's `stand_in` is 下降 and 参's is 参加. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Korean fields hold compositional readings; Vietnamese đầu hàng is a native phrase, not a reading of these characters.
