## Etymology
- [釈](../characters/釈.md) "explain" + [珈](../characters/珈.md) "coffee" + [文](../characters/文.md) "culture" + [尼](../characters/尼.md) "nun" — a purely phonetic transliteration of Sanskrit *Śākyamuni* ("sage of the Śākya clan"); the standard CJK orthography is 釋迦牟尼 (see aliases), with 釈珈文尼 as this vault's Dan'a'yo spelling.
