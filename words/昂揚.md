## Notes
- [昂](../characters/昂.md) "proud, bold" + [揚](../characters/揚%20(char).md) "scatter, hurl" — no stand-in relationship applies (昂's own stand-in is [[昂然]]; 揚's own is itself).

昂揚 means high-spirited or exalted.

Mandarin ángyáng, Cantonese ngong4 joeng4, Korean 앙양, and Vietnamese ngang dương are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese こうよう uses 昂's on-reading KOU (an irregular Sino-Japanese borrowing unrelated to the Mandarin/Korean initial), not a bug. Vietnamese's 揚 half (dương, the well-attested standard reading, as in 表揚 biểu dương) is inferred rather than drawn from the character's own stored citation, which is missing it — flagged for a future character-level check. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄫ⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
