## Notes
- [東](../characters/東.md) "east" + [部](../characters/部%20(char).md) "part, section" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [部](../characters/部%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 東's own stand-in is [[東方]].

東部 means east, or the eastern part.

Mandarin dōngbù, Japanese とうぶ, and Korean 동부 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `cantonese` had the same garbled pinyin-style spelling seen on [[東方]] (dong1 bou6 → dung1 bou6). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field, removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/`aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄫㄅㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).

Dan'a'yo keeps the shared CJKV sense: the eastern part of a place or region. It is placed on the Directions page beside [[北部]] and [[南部]], the same pattern with a different direction.
