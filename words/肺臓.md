## Notes
- [肺](../characters/肺.md) "lungs" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 肺 cannot appear independently outside this compound. [臓](../characters/臓.md) "organ, entrails" is separately legitimized by [[内臓]].

肺臓 combines 肺 ("lungs") with 臓 ("organ, entrails") — "lungs." See also [[肺腑]].

**Found and fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字`/`諺文` had pyejang/펴장 (a p-initial reading), mismatching 肺's real f-initial reading (fe/뻐) — same failure class as the 福-family ㄈ→ㅍ confusion seen repeatedly this session (should map to ㅃ, not ㅍ); `注音` (ㄈㄝㄐㄚㄫ) had stayed correct throughout. Also fixed `korean`: had 폐장; 허파, wrongly appending 肺's own native gloss 허파 ("lungs," a real unrelated word) onto the correct compositional 폐장 — trimmed to just 폐장. Filled blank vietnamese (phế tạng, standard attested term). `kwin` is false per the AND-rule: 肺 individually carries `kwin: false` (despite 臓's own `kwin: true`).
