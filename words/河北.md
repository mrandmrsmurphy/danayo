## Notes
- [河](../characters/河.md) "stream, river" + [北](../characters/北.md) "north" — "north of the river", the river being the Yellow River. No stand-in relationship applies (河's own is [[小河]]; 北's own is [[北方]]).

This name has several non-province senses, listed first. English Wiktionary gives a historical region, the land north of the lower Yellow River and east of the Taihang Mountains (the Tang-era Hebei Circuit is an example of the name in use), and Chinese Wiktionary lists a Hebei District of Tianjin and two Japanese places read かほく: a city in Ishikawa Prefecture and a town in Yamagata Prefecture. English Wiktionary's Japanese entry lists the province and "a place name, such as Kahoku Town in Yamagata".

Hebei, the province, surrounds Beijing and Tianjin on three sides and its capital is [[石家荘]] (from memory, not re-checked). It is not to be confused with [[河南]] (Henan), "south of the river", which differs only in the second character, or with [[湖北]] (Hubei), "north of the lake". The Japanese field holds かほく, which is the same reading as the two Japanese places; the Korean field holds the Sino-Korean 하북, matching the Dan'a'yo 諺文 exactly; the usual South Korean name for the province today is 허베이 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Hà Bắc is the Sino-Vietnamese name; Vietnamese Wiktionary has an entry for it, but its gloss there is garbled, so only English Wiktionary's Vietnamese entry is relied on here. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄅㄨㄎ unique across both words and characters).

Attestation, checked 2026-10-11: English and Chinese Wiktionary (Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese sections) list the senses above.

1. Hebei, the historical region north of the lower Yellow River.
2. Hebei District, a district of Tianjin.
3. Kahoku, a city in Ishikawa Prefecture, Japan.
4. Kahoku, a town in Yamagata Prefecture, Japan.
5. Hebei, the province of China.
