## Notes
- [四 (char)](../characters/四%20(char).md) "four(th)" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — literally "four-day," the fourth day of the month.

Mandarin sì rì, Cantonese sei3 jat6, and Korean 사일 (sail) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese よっか (yokka) is a well-known irregular native reading — Japanese day-of-month names for the 1st through 10th (and 14th, 20th, 24th) are irregular native counter words rather than compositional Sino-Japanese readings, one of the more famously tricky parts of learning Japanese dates. Vietnamese tứ nhật is likewise compositional, from 四's tứ and 日's own nhật, and is directly attested as a real term for "the fourth day (of the month)."
