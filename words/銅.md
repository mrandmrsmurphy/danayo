## Notes
- [銅 (char)](../characters/銅%20(char).md) "copper" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 銅 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

銅 names copper, the reddish transition metal — one of the earliest metals worked by humans, foundational to the Bronze Age (青銅, "bronze," literally "blue-green copper"). It is highly productive: 銅像 ("bronze statue"), 銅貨 ("copper coin").

Mandarin tóng, Cantonese tung4, Japanese どう (dō), and Korean 동 (dong) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese đồng is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading, and remains the everyday Vietnamese word both for "copper" and for the Vietnamese currency unit (đồng, named after old copper coins). This word is part of a genuine three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read dong/동/ㄉㄛㄫ): [[動]] ("move") and [[筒]] ("cylinder, tube").
