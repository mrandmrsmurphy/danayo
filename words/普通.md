## Notes
- [普](../characters/普.md) "universal" + [通](../characters/通%20(char).md) "through" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [普](../characters/普.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (通's own stand-in is itself).

普通 means ordinary, plain, average, or common.

Mandarin pǔtōng, Cantonese pou2 tung1, Japanese ふつう, Korean 보통, and Vietnamese phổ thông are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄛㄊㄛㄫ unique across both words and characters).
