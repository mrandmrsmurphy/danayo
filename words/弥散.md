## Notes
- [弥](../characters/弥%20(char).md) "increasingly, all the more" + [散](../characters/散.md) "scatter, spread" — "spreading all the more," the Mass, a phonetic transcription of Latin missa. Their own `stand_in` values: 弥's is itself; 散's is [[散布]]; none points here, so 弥散 is an independent compound.

弥散 is the Mass: the Catholic liturgy of the Eucharist, from Latin *missa*. The Chinese name 彌撒 (Mandarin mísa, Cantonese nei4 saat3, Japanese ミサ, Korean 미사) is a phonetic transcription, not a meaning. In Dan'a'yo the characters are 弥 (alias 彌) and 散, and 散 has the alias 撒, so 弥撒 and 彌撒 are accepted spellings; the vault takes 弥散 as the word. The same two characters also form an unrelated ordinary word meaning to diffuse or spread out (Mandarin and Japanese 彌散), which is a different word in Chinese and Japanese. The fields hold the mechanical readings of 弥 and 散; the real readings of the Mass are given above. It is the liturgy as a whole, where [[聖餐]] is the communion and the Eucharist, and [[礼拝]] is worship in general. Compare [[洗礼]] (baptism) and [[教会]] (church).
