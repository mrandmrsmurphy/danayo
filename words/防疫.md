## Notes
- [防](../characters/防.md) "defend, protect" + [疫](../characters/疫.md) "pestilence, plague, epidemic"

防疫 means epidemic prevention or quarantine — public-health measures taken to stop the spread of infectious disease. Mandarin 防疫 (fángyì), Cantonese 防疫 (fong4jik6), Japanese 防疫 (ぼうえき), Korean 방역 (bang-yeok), and Sino-Vietnamese phòng dịch are all standard, everyday public-health terms across the CJKV sphere, prominently used during outbreaks (防疫措置/방역 조치/biện pháp phòng dịch, "epidemic prevention measures") and in routine public-health administration alike.
