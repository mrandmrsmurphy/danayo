## Notes
- [吹 (char)](../characters/吹%20(char).md) "blow; puff; bluster"

吹 is 会意: [[口]] ("mouth") + [[欠]] ("to open the mouth wide, exhale") — the literal action of blowing air through an open mouth, combining iconically rather than phonetically. It means "to blow" (吹風, "to blow wind, dry with a hairdryer") and, colloquially, "to boast, brag" (吹牛, "to boast," literally "to blow up a cow" — the modern figurative sense).

Mandarin chuī, Cantonese ceoi1, and Japanese すい (sui, on'yomi, largely confined to formal/literary contexts) alongside native ふ(く) (fu(ku), "to blow," the everyday verb) all preserve the sense. Korean 취 (chwi) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 불다 (bulda, "to blow"). Vietnamese xuy is the Hán Việt reading, attested in xuy ngưu (吹牛, "to boast, brag") and phong xuy ("wind blowing"); the character page's remaining sixteen stored candidates are unrelated and look like corpus noise.

This word is part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read cui/취/ㄑㄨㄧ) with [[推]] ("push") and [[炊]] ("cook") — now fully cross-referenced from all three sides.
