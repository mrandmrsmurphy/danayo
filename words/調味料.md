## Notes
- [調](../characters/調.md) "adjust" + [味](../characters/味.md) "taste" + [料](../characters/料.md) "material"

調味料 is seasoning: a substance added to food to give it flavor, such as salt, soy sauce or spice. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin tiáowèiliào as "seasoning; flavoring; relish; dressing; spice" and Japanese ちょうみりょう as "seasoning, flavoring material". Korean 조미료 and Cantonese tiu4 mei6 liu6-2 match.

Its main members here are [塩](塩.md), salt, [糖](糖.md), sugar, [醤油](醤油.md), soy sauce, and the spices [胡椒](胡椒.md) and [丁香](丁香.md).
