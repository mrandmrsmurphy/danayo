## Notes
- [梅](../characters/梅.md) "plum, prune, ume" + [雨](../characters/雨%20(char).md) "rain" — no stand-in relationship applies (梅's own stand-in is [[梅花]]; 雨's own is itself).

梅雨 means the East Asian rainy season, or summer rain.

Mandarin méiyǔ, Cantonese mui4 jyu5, Korean 매우, and Vietnamese mai vũ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Simplified `japanese` to つゆ, the everyday native reading and the standard real-world term for the rainy season, dropping the formal on'yomi variant ばいう (also real, but secondary). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field. Kept `霉雨` in `aliases`, a genuine variant spelling using 霉 ("mold," reflecting the season's dampness) for 梅. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄛㄧㄨ unique across both words and characters).
