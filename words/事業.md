## Notes
- [事 (char)](../characters/事%20(char).md) "affair, matter" + [業 (char)](../characters/業%20(char).md) "profession, business" — project, undertaking.

事業 is a project or undertaking: a sizeable organized enterprise carried out for some end, whether a company's venture, a public works scheme or a cause. The word is old: it comes from the I Ching, 繫辭上 chapter 12, 「舉而錯之天下之民謂之事業」 ("to lift it up and apply it to the people of the world is called an undertaking"), where it closes a sequence defining 道, 器, 變 and 通; the text as printed on Wikisource writes 錯 where some editions have the variant 措. Japanese じぎょう ("project or work; enterprise, business"), Korean 사업 ("business, project") and Mandarin shìyè / Cantonese si6 jip6 ("undertaking, cause, project") share the sense Dan'a'yo takes. Two senses are not taken over: Mandarin also uses it for a state-run institution (事業單位), set against private [[企業]], and Vietnamese sự nghiệp keeps only the "career" meaning, which in Dan'a'yo is [[職業]]. It differs from [[計画]], which is the plan or schedule of a project, and from [[作業]], one task within it.
