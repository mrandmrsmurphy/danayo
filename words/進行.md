## Notes
- [進](../characters/進.md) "advance, progress" + [行 (char)](../characters/行%20(char).md) "attend, go to" — "advance, progress."

Mandarin jìnxíng, cantonese zeon3 hang4, japanese しんこう, korean 진행, and vietnamese tiến hành (a directly attested, extremely common Vietnamese word — "to carry out, proceed") are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. `进行` is the standard simplified-character spelling variant (進→进), correctly listed as an alias. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 진항 diverges from Sino-Korean 진행 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄋㄏㄚㄫ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 進's own `stand_in` is this exact word (進行), so 進 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 進行; 行's own `stand_in` is instead 行 itself, so this word does not legitimize 行.
