## Notes
- [習](../characters/習.md) "practice, learn" + [俗 (char)](../characters/俗%20(char).md) "custom" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (習's is [[練習]], 俗's is [[俗]] itself), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: true` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: true`.

習俗 means a custom, mores, or convention — cultural practices handed down.

Mandarin xísú, Cantonese zaap6 zuk6, Japanese しゅうぞく (shūzoku), and Korean 습속 (seupsok) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 習俗 is itself a common attested word for "custom" in all four languages. Vietnamese tập tục is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term. **Fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字`/`注音` had sibsog/ㄙㄧㄆㄙㄛㄎ (using an i-vowel first syllable), mismatching 習's real ǝ-vowel reading (sǝb/ㄙㄜㄆ, confirmed via `syllables/ㄙㄜㄆ.md`) — 諺文 (십속) had also been wrong (십 instead of 습). Corrected to sǝbsog/습속/ㄙㄜㄆㄙㄛㄎ. The same wrong 注音 was independently duplicated on `characters/習.md`'s own citation, fixed there too. Also corrected `kwin` (false→true per the AND-rule) and removed the redundant duplicate `品詞` field and two empty-string fields (vietnamese/swadesh). No homophones.
