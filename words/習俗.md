## Notes
- [習](../characters/習.md) "practice, learn" + [俗 (char)](../characters/俗%20(char).md) "custom" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (習's is [[練習]], 俗's is [[俗]] itself), so `#cranberry` does not apply.

習俗 means a custom, mores, or convention — cultural practices handed down.

Mandarin xísú, Cantonese zaap6 zuk6, Japanese しゅうぞく (shūzoku), and Korean 습속 (seupsok) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 習俗 is itself a common attested word for "custom" in all four languages. Vietnamese tập tục is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term.
