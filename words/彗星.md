## Notes
- [彗](../characters/彗.md) "comet, broom" + [星](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [彗](../characters/彗.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (星's own stand-in is itself).

彗星 means comet — literally "broom star," from the broom-like appearance of a comet's tail.

Mandarin huìxīng, Japanese すいせい, Korean 혜성, and Vietnamese tuệ tinh are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `cantonese` had 彗's syllable as seoi6 instead of wai6 (matching 彗's own citation). No homophones (注音 ㄏㄝㄙㄝㄫ unique across both words and characters).
