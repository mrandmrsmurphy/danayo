## Notes
- [伝](../characters/伝.md) "transmit, hand down" + [道](../characters/道%20(char).md) "way, doctrine" — "hand down the way," to preach. Their own `stand_in` values: 伝's is [[伝播]]; 道's is itself; none points here, so 伝道 is an independent compound.

伝道 is to preach: to hand on the faith to others. Mandarin chuándào (傳道), Cantonese cyun4 dou6, Japanese でんどう and Korean 전도 all use it, and Vietnamese truyền đạo does too. It is the everyday word for preaching, where [[宣教]] is a mission. Compare [[説教]] (sermon) and [[使徒]] (apostle).
