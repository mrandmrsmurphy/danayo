## Notes
- [佳 (char)](../characters/佳%20(char).md) "beautiful; good; excellent"

佳 is 形声: semantic [[人]] ("person") + phonetic [[圭]]. It describes anything excellent, fine, or beautiful — most often applied to people ([[佳人]], "a beautiful person," as in the chengyu [[佳人薄命]], "a beautiful woman has an ill fate," a well-worn literary trope) but also to occasions and works generally (佳作, "an excellent work"; 佳節, "a happy, festive occasion").

Mandarin jiā, Cantonese gaai1, and Japanese か (ka, on'yomi, largely confined to literary compounds) all preserve the sense. Korean 가 (ga) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 아름답다 (areumdapda, "to be beautiful"). Vietnamese giai is the Hán Việt reading, attested in giai nhân (佳人, "a beautiful person") — the character page's other three stored candidates (dai, lai, trai) are all real Vietnamese words but unrelated in meaning, the same kind of corpus contamination found repeatedly in this sweep.
