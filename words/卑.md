## Notes
- [卑 (char)](../characters/卑%20(char).md) "lowly; inferior" — stand-in for [[卑 (char)|卑]], which cannot appear independently

卑 is 会意: [[甲]] ("fan") + 𠂇 ("hand") — a man holding a fan, indicating that he is a servant; the image evolved to mean "low, inferior, humble." It appears in 自卑 ("to feel inferior, an inferiority complex") and 卑賤 (bēijiàn, "lowly, base"), the self-deprecating classical register also seen in 卑職 ("this humble official," a self-referential term of modesty).

Mandarin bēi, Cantonese bei1, and Japanese ひ (hi, on'yomi, largely confined to formal/literary compounds) alongside native いや(しい) (iya(shii), "lowly, vulgar," the everyday adjective) all preserve the sense. Korean 비 (bi) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 낮다 (natda, "to be low"). Vietnamese ti is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in tự ti ("inferiority complex, to feel inferior") and ti tiện ("lowly, base, mean"); the character page's other two stored candidates (bấy, te) are unrelated and look like corpus noise.

This word shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading (be/버/ㄅㄝ) with [[避]] ("evade") — a coincidental convergence, unrelated in meaning; 避 reciprocates the callout and both pages are now perfected.
