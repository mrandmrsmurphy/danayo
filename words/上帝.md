## Notes
- [上 (char)](../characters/上%20(char).md) "above, supreme" + [帝](../characters/帝.md) "emperor" — no `#cranberry`: 帝's own `stand_in` is [[帝王]], 上's is 上 itself; neither points here.

Names Shangdi, the supreme deity of Chinese antiquity, and by extension the standard Chinese Christian/Islamic term for "God." Mandarin Shàngdì, Japanese じょうてい, Korean 상제, and Sino-Vietnamese Thượng Đế are all standard compositional readings, capitalized as a proper noun.
