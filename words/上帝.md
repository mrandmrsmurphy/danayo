## Notes
- [上 (char)](../characters/上%20(char).md) "above, supreme" + [帝](../characters/帝.md) "emperor" — no `#cranberry`: 帝's own `stand_in` is [[帝王]], 上's is 上 itself; neither points here.

Names Shangdi, the supreme deity of Chinese antiquity, and by extension the standard Chinese Christian/Islamic term for "God." Mandarin Shàngdì, Japanese じょうてい, Korean 상제, and Sino-Vietnamese Thượng Đế are all standard compositional readings, capitalized as a proper noun. **Fixed a real spelling-consistency bug**: `cantonese` had been `soeng6 dai3` — corrected to `seong6 dai3`, matching 上's own stored cantonese and the spelling convention already used on several other 上-compounds this stretch (though a genuine split exists across the vault's 上-words between `seong6` and `soeng6` spellings — worth a fuller consistency audit if the pattern keeps recurring). `kwin` is false (帝's own kwin alone determines the AND-rule result). Fixed a missing citation on `characters/上 (char).md`'s Words list.
