## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" is separately legitimized by [[自身]]. [分](../characters/分.md) "divide" is separately legitimized by [[分]] itself. No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

自分 combines 自 ("self") with 分 (here in its "one's own portion, status, duty" sense, as in 本分/名分) — "to self-examine, assess one's own place."

Pronunciation fields (jiǝbun/즤분/ㄐㄧㄜㄅㄨㄋ) are the straightforward concatenation of each constituent's own stored reading — no bug. `kwin` is false per the AND-rule: 自 individually carries `kwin: false` (despite 分's own `kwin: true`). Added missing `kwin: false`.

Filled blank cantonese (zi6fan1) and korean (자분), compositional. Japanese じぶん (jibun) is the extremely common everyday Japanese word for "oneself, myself" — the same spelling carries a broader, more general meaning in Japanese than the narrower literary Chinese sense documented here, but the reading itself is genuinely real and standard.
