## Notes
- [星](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" + [条](../characters/条%20(char).md) "long, thin (strip)" + [旗](../characters/旗.md) "banner, flag" — no stand-in relationship applies (星/条's own stand-ins are themselves; 旗's own is [[旗幟]]).

星条旗 means the Stars and Stripes (the flag of the United States).

Mandarin xīngtiáoqí, Cantonese sing1 tiu4 kei4, Japanese せいじょうき, Korean 성조기, and Vietnamese tinh điều kì are all standard, fully compositional readings. Removed `花旗` from `aliases`: a distinct compound built on 花 ("flower"), not 星/条 — a different real-world name for the same flag/country, not an orthographic variant of this word. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄫㄐㄛㄎㄧ unique across both words and characters).
