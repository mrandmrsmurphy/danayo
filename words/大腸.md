## Notes
- [大](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big" + [腸](../characters/腸.md) "intestine" — no stand-in relationship applies (大 is its own stand-in; 腸's own is [[腸管]]).

大腸 means the large intestine, bowel, or colon.

Japanese だいちょう, Korean 대장, Mandarin dàcháng, and Cantonese daai6 coeng4 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese đại tràng is a standard reading. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄧㄑㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
