## Notes
- [楽](../characters/楽.md) "music" (its "music" sense yuè, not the "pleasure" sense lè stored on its own citation — the same character-level gap flagged on [[楽経]]) + [譜](../characters/譜.md) "musical score" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [譜](../characters/譜.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

楽譜 means music score or music manuscript.

Japanese がくふ and Korean 악보 are standard, fully compositional readings; Mandarin yuèpǔ and Cantonese ngok6 pou2 correctly use 楽's real "music" reading, consistent with [[楽経]]. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field (nhạc phổ). No homophones (注音 ㄌㄚㄎㄅㄛ unique across both words and characters).
