## Notes
- [錬](../characters/錬.md) "refine, smelt" + [獄](../characters/獄.md) "prison, hell" — "refining prison," purgatory. Their own `stand_in` values: 錬's is [[鍛錬]]; 獄's is [[監獄]]; none points here, so 錬獄 is an independent compound.

錬獄 is purgatory: in Catholic teaching, the place where souls are purified after death. Mandarin liànyù (煉獄), Cantonese lin6 juk6, Japanese れんごく and Korean 연옥 all use it, and Vietnamese luyện ngục does too. The character 錬 has the alias 煉, the form Chinese uses. Compare [[地獄]] (hell) and [[天国]] (heaven).
