## Notes
- [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "gold" + [蘭](../characters/蘭.md) "orchid" — "gold orchid"; no stand-in relationship applies (金 is its own stand-in; 蘭's is [[蘭花]]).

金蘭 is a proper noun with two senses.

1. Cam Ranh (Vietnamese Cam Ranh), a city of Khánh Hòa province on the south-central coast of Vietnam, on the shore of Cam Ranh Bay, which is [[金蘭湾]]. English Wiktionary has it as a Chinese proper noun, "Cam Ranh (a city in Vietnam)" (Mandarin jīnlán, Cantonese gam1 laan4), and Chinese Wikipedia writes the place the same way. The Vietnamese name is not Hán-Việt: Vietnamese Wikipedia explains Cam Ranh as a transcription of Cham (also Jarai and Ede) <i>Kăm M'ran</i>, "a place where ships and boats gather, a port". Under the 2025 reorganization of Vietnam's administrative units (as English and Vietnamese Wikipedia read it on 2026-10-11) the former city of Cam Ranh was dissolved into wards of Khánh Hòa province, and the name is now officially borne by Cam Ranh ward. The sense here is the place as people use it, the city and the naval port around it.
2. A literary common noun, "profound friendship; sworn brotherhood", which English Wiktionary gives from the 易經 (from memory, not re-checked on the page itself); it is not a place and has no lexipedia page of its own yet.

This word is distinct from [[金角]], which is the demon king of *Journey to the West*. An earlier version of the vault wrongly gave 金角 the Cam Ranh sense; the two differ in one character, 角 "horn" against 蘭 "orchid", and they are separate words.

The readings are compositional: Mandarin jīnlán and Cantonese gam1 laan4 as at English Wiktionary; Japanese きんらん, Korean 금란 (the Sino-Korean reading of 金 and 蘭) and Hán-Việt kim lan (hvdic gives each character) are compositional readings, not checked on a dictionary page. The real Vietnamese name of the city is Cam Ranh.

No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄇㄌㄚㄋ unique across words and characters). The 注音 needs no syllable-boundary dot.
