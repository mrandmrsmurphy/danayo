## Notes
- [経](../characters/経%20(char).md) "pass through, manage" + [理](../characters/理.md) "reason, principle" — "one who manages the affairs." Their own `stand_in` values: 経's is itself; 理's is [[理由]]; none points here, so 経理 is an independent compound.

経理 is a manager: a person in charge of running a business or department. Mandarin jīnglǐ and Cantonese ging1 lei5 are the ordinary Chinese words. In Japanese けいり and Korean 경리 the same characters mean accounting or bookkeeping, not a manager; Dan'a'yo takes the Chinese sense. Compare [[上司]] (boss) and [[官僚]] (bureaucrat).
