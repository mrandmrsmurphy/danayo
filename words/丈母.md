## Notes
- [丈 (char)](../characters/丈%20(char).md) "husband" + [母](../characters/母.md) "mother" — no `#cranberry`: 丈's own `stand_in` is 丈 itself, 母's is [[母親]]; neither points here.

**Fixed the same Sino-Korean-vs-Dan'a'yo mix-up already found on [[丈夫]]**: `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` had been built from Sino-Korean 장 (丈's own Korean reading) rather than Dan'a'yo's own stored 창 — corrected to `cangmou`/`창못`/`ㄑㄚㄫㄇㄛㄨ`, the proper mechanical concatenation. The identical typo had propagated into this word's own citations on both `丈 (char).md` and `母.md`, fixed there too. Mandarin zhàngmǔ, Cantonese zoeng6 mou5, Japanese じょうぼ, and Korean 장모 are all standard compositional readings. Vietnamese mẹ vợ ("wife's mother," a real native compound, matching the [[丈人]] "bố vợ" pattern) is used instead of a Sino-Vietnamese calque, filling an entirely missing field. `kwin` is false (AND-rule: both 丈 and 母 individually false).
