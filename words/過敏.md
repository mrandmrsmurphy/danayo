## Notes
- [過 (char)](../characters/過%20(char).md) "excessive" + [敏 (char)](../characters/敏%20(char).md) "quick, sensitive" — literally "excessively sensitive." No stand-in relationship applies (過's own `stand_in` is itself; 敏's own is itself).

過敏 means oversensitive, allergic, hypersensitive — both the medical sense (an allergic reaction) and the psychological sense (touchy, easily offended or alarmed). Distinguishes genuine tenderness of manner ([[温柔]]) or refinement ([[斯文]]) from an excessive, often unwelcome degree of sensitivity.

Mandarin guòmǐn, Cantonese gwo3 man5, Japanese かびん, Korean 과민, and Vietnamese quá mẫn are all standard, fully compositional readings. `kwin` is true: both 過 and 敏 are individually `kwin: true`. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⺢ㄇㄧㄋ unique across both words and characters).
