## Notes
- [不](../characters/不%20(char).md) "not" + [義](../characters/義.md) "righteousness" — "not righteous," unrighteousness. Their own `stand_in` values: 不's is itself; 義's is [[意義]]; none points here, so 不義 is an independent compound.

不義 is what is not right: injustice, and in particular a wrong done to another. Mandarin bùyì, Cantonese bat1 ji6, Japanese ふぎ, Korean 불의 and Vietnamese bất nghĩa all use it; Japanese Bibles use it for the Hebrew "injustice."

**Hebrew**: it best translates *ʿawlāh* (עַוְלָה) and *ʿāwel* (עָוֶל) "injustice, crookedness," as in "you shall do no injustice in judgment" (Leviticus 19:15). The Greek is *adikia*, "unrighteousness." Its opposite is [[正義]], the *ṣedeq* (צֶדֶק) of "justice, righteousness." Compare [[罪悪]], which is guilt before God rather than wrong done to a neighbour.
