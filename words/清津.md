## Notes
- [清](../characters/清.md) "pure, clear" + [津](../characters/津.md) "ferry crossing, ford" — the name of Chongjin, a port city of North Korea.

清津 (Chongjin) is a port city on the northeast coast of North Korea and the capital of North Hamgyong Province ([[咸鏡]]北道), usually counted as the third-largest city of the country (from memory, not re-checked). The name reads "clear ford". English Wiktionary labels it with the province North Hamgyong.

English and Chinese Wiktionary have 清津 as the city (Mandarin Qīngjīn, Cantonese cing1 zeon1). English Wiktionary has no Korean section for it and Japanese Wiktionary has no entry, so Korean 청진 and Japanese せいしん are composed from the characters' readings; the usual Japanese name is katakana チョンジン (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Thanh Tân is composed from the character pages; no Vietnamese dictionary entry was found.
