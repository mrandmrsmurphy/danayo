## Notes
- [処 (char)](../characters/処%20(char).md) "place" + [分 (char)](../characters/分%20(char).md) "divide" — to deal with a matter by assigning it its portion, hence to dispose of it, and from there to punish.

処分 is "to punish; to discipline", and in the other CJKV usages "to dispose of". English Wiktionary gives the Chinese verb (at the traditional spelling 處分) as "to punish; to discipline" (你是不能逃過處分的, "you can't get away with it"), and, in literary use, "to handle; to take care of" (不起訴處分, "closing a case without prosecution") and "to instruct". It also gives a noun, "punishment; disciplinary action", and lists 處罰 and 辦理 as synonyms for the first two senses, neither a word in the vault. Japanese 処分 (しょぶん, shobun) is a noun and a suru-verb meaning "disposal" (ゴミ処分, "garbage disposal"; selling off one's house), with the further sense "penalty; punishment". Its derived terms include 行政処分 (administrative disposition), 仮処分 (provisional disposition) and the historical 琉球処分 (the "disposition" of the Ryūkyūs).

Dan'a'yo takes the punishment sense, which is the Chinese one and the Japanese second sense, and which the Law page lists beside [[懲罰]], an independently attested synonym. The Japanese "disposal" sense (garbage, property) is not the Dan'a'yo sense.

Mandarin chǔfèn and Cantonese cyu2 fan1 are as at English Wiktionary. Korean 처분 and Japanese しょぶん are the Sino-Xenic readings.

No other word shares the 注音 ㄑㄛㄅㄨㄋ (checked 2026-10-12).
