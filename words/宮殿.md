## Etymology
- [宮](../characters/宮.md) "palace, shrine" + [殿](../characters/殿.md) "palace, hall" — stand-in for [[殿]], which cannot appear independently

## Notes
- "Palace" — near-synonymous with [[宮廷]] ("royal court"), though 宮殿 emphasizes the physical building/architecture over the political institution. `vietnamese: cung điện` is directly attested and extremely common.
