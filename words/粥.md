## Notes
- [粥](../characters/粥%20(char).md) "congee" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 粥 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo. Its 会意 etymology traces to 鬻 (pageless in this vault), the original character for congee.

Mandarin zhōu, Cantonese zuk1, and Korean 죽 (juk) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading かゆ (kayu, "rice porridge") rather than the on-readings シュク/イク (SHUKU/IKU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese cháo is the standard reading and the everyday Vietnamese word for congee/rice porridge. This word is a genuine Dan'a'yo homophone of [[竹]] ("bamboo") — coincidental, unrelated in meaning.
