## Notes
- [慶](../characters/慶%20(char).md) "celebrate" + [尚](../characters/尚%20(char).md) "still, esteem" — the name of the Gyeongsang region of Korea.

慶尚 (Gyeongsang) is the historical Joseon province in the southeast of the Korean peninsula, covering the old territory of 新羅 (see [[新羅]]). The name combines the first characters of 慶州 (Gyeongju) and 尙州 (Sangju), its two chief cities. The province was divided in 1896 into 慶尚北道 (North Gyeongsang) and 慶尚南道 (South Gyeongsang), which are the two provinces of South Korea today, and 慶尚 survives as the name of the regional dialect group and region. The variant 慶尙 uses the older form 尙 of 尚, which is recorded on the character page as an alias.
