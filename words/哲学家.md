## Notes
- [哲](../characters/哲.md) "wise, sagacious" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study" + [家](../characters/家.md) "house; family" — together forming [[哲学]] "philosophy"; 家 here is the lexicalized "master of a field", as in [[運動家]], [[儒家]] and [[法家]].

哲学家 is the philosopher as a recognised title: a thinker of standing in [[哲学]], such as a founder of a school or a figure in the canon. Where [[哲学者]] is anyone who does philosophy, 哲学家 names the person who holds that place. 家 is not a productive suffix in Dan'a'yo, so the compound is listed on its own.

Mandarin zhéxuéjiā and Cantonese zit3 hok6 gaa1 are the standard words for "philosopher" in Chinese. Japanese てつがくか exists beside the more usual 哲学者. Korean usually says 철학자 instead; the `korean` field holds the compositional Sino-Korean reading 철학가. Vietnamese says nhà triết học in everyday speech, with triết học gia as the compositional reading. The `japanese`, `korean` and `vietnamese` fields hold those compositional Sino-readings, as elsewhere in the vault. Dan'a'yo keeps the title reading that Chinese has for 家, and uses 哲学者 for the plain practitioner.
