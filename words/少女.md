## Notes
- [少 (char)](../characters/少%20(char).md) "few; young" + [女 (char)](../characters/女%20(char).md) "woman; female" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 少's and 女's own `stand_in` fields point to themselves).

少女 is a young girl or young woman — 少 here contributes its "young, youthful" sense (少年 "boy," 年少 "young in years") rather than its "few, scant" sense.

Japanese しょうじょ (famously the source of the "shōjo" genre label in manga/anime), Korean 소녀, and Vietnamese thiếu nữ are all standard, non-drifted readings. No HSK level is given for the compound.
