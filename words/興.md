## Notes
- [興](../characters/興%20(char).md) "entertain" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 興 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

興 means to entertain, to rise up, to prosper, as in 興起 (to rise up, emerge) and 高興 (glad, pleased).

Mandarin xìng, cantonese hing1, and korean 흥 (heung) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading おこ (oko, stem of 興る "to rise, prosper") rather than the on-readings キョウ/コウ (KYOU/KOU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese hưng is one of seven stored candidates. No homophones.
