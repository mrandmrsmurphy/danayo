## Notes
- [林](../characters/林%20(char).md) "grove" + [業](../characters/業%20(char).md) "profession, business" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 林 and 業's own stand-ins are themselves, each with its own independent word page).

林業 means forestry.

Mandarin línyè, Cantonese lam4 jip6, and Vietnamese lâm nghiệp are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bugs: `cantonese` was missing its internal space (lam4jip6 → lam4 jip6); `japanese` used an obsolete historical kana spelling (りんげふ), not matching the modern reading used consistently across every other 業-compound in the vault (企業/作業/副業/卒業 all use ぎょう) — corrected to りんぎょう; `korean` was 임업, the South Korean 두음법칙-shifted form — corrected to 림업, the North Korean/문화어 form matching 林's own citation. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄧㄇㄝㄆ unique across both words and characters).
