## Notes
- [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

年 names a year, and is one of the most productive characters in the entire vault as a compound-forming suffix/prefix (年中 "throughout the year," 年末 "year's end," 年齢 "age," and dozens more).

Japanese ねん (on'yomi; the native とし, toshi, is the everyday standalone word instead), Korean 년, and Vietnamese niên (the character's own field also lists năm, actually the native Vietnamese everyday word for "year," and nên, an unrelated grammatical particle — niên is the correct Sino-Vietnamese reading) are all standard readings. No homophones among other words (注音 ㄋㄝㄋ unique).
