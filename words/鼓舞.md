## Notes
- [鼓 (char)](../characters/鼓%20(char).md) "drum, play (instrumental)" + [舞](../characters/舞.md) "dance" — no `#cranberry`: 鼓's own `stand_in` is 鼓 itself, 舞's is [[跳舞]]; neither points here.

Literally "to drum and dance" — the same rousing, morale-lifting image across the sphere (鼓舞士気, "to boost morale"). This vault's word for Rosenfelder's "inspire."
