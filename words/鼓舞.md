## Notes
- [鼓 (char)](../characters/鼓%20(char).md) "drum, play (instrumental)" + [舞](../characters/舞.md) "dance" — no `#cranberry`: 鼓's own `stand_in` is 鼓 itself, 舞's is [[跳舞]]; neither points here.

Mandarin gǔwǔ, Cantonese gu2 mou5, Korean 고무, and Sino-Vietnamese cổ vũ (a real, everyday Vietnamese verb, "to cheer on, root for, rally") are all standard readings; `kwin` is true (both 鼓 and 舞 individually true, so the AND-rule holds). Japanese こぶ (kobu) is the real attested on'yomi compound (鼓 KO + 舞's alternate on'yomi BU) — fixed a stray zero-width space (U+200B) silently embedded between the two kana, which had made the field's displayed text look correct while its actual value was corrupted.
