## Notes
- [北](../characters/北.md) "north" + [方](../characters/方.md) "direction" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [北](../characters/北.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (方's own stand-in is [[方向]]).

北方 means north or the northern direction — the cardinal direction, or the northern region of a place.

**Real bugs found and fixed**: `japanese` had ぱう instead of ほう for 方's own on'yomi (which never rendaku-shifts after ク, matching sibling compounds 五方 ごほう, 平方 へいほう, 地方 ちほう) — corrected to ほくほう. `vietnamese: hướng bắc` was a native paraphrase rather than the real Sino-Vietnamese compound — corrected to `phương bắc` (verified; Vietnamese reverses the modifier-noun order from Chinese, as usual). Korean 북방 is a standard reading. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄨㄎㄈㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
