## Notes
- [北](../characters/北.md) "north" + [方](../characters/方.md) "direction" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [北](../characters/北.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (方's own stand-in is [[方向]]).

北方 means north or the northern direction — the cardinal direction, or the northern region of a place.
