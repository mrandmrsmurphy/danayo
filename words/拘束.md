## Notes
- [拘](../characters/拘.md) "constrain, restrain" + [束](../characters/束%20(char).md) "bundle" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [拘](../characters/拘.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (束's own stand-in is itself).

拘束 means to constrain or restrain.

Mandarin jūshù, Japanese こうそく, Korean 구속, and Vietnamese câu thúc are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `cantonese` had the wrong initial (ceoi1→keoi1, matching 拘's own citation). No homophones (注音 ㄍㄨㄙ⼄ㄎ unique across both words and characters).
