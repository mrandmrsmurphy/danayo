## Notes
- [青](../characters/青%20(char).md) "blue" + [森](../characters/森.md) "forest"

青森 is Aomori, written "blue (or green) forest". The name is attached to the port town that the Tsugaru domain developed in the early seventeenth century, said to be named after a green wooded rise that served as a landmark for ships (from memory, not re-checked). The Japanese reading あおもり is the plain native reading of 青 (あお) and 森 (もり).

Aomori occupies the northern tip of Honshu, facing Hokkaido across the Tsugaru Strait, with the Seikan Tunnel running beneath it. It is Japan's leading apple-growing prefecture and is known for the Nebuta summer festival, the old castle town of Hirosaki, and heavy winter snow.

In Dan'a'yo 青森 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 아오모리 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
