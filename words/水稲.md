## Notes
- [水](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [稲](../characters/稲.md) "rice plant" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 稲 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (水's own stand-in is itself, so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

水稲 means paddy rice, rice planted in a water-logged paddy (as opposed to 陸稲, dry-field rice).

Mandarin shuǐdào, Cantonese seoi2 dou6, Japanese すいとう, and Korean 수도 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 水's and 稲's own stored citations exactly). Filled the blank `pos` and `vietnamese` fields — thủy đạo (水's own thuỷ + 稲's own primary citation đạo). `hsk_level: "2"` matches 稲's own stored value. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨㄉㄚㄨ unique across both words and characters).
