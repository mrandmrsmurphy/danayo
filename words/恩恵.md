## Notes
- [恩](../characters/恩.md) "grace, kindness" + [恵](../characters/恵.md) "favor, benefit" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [恵](../characters/恵.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (恩's own stand-in is [[恩寵]]).

恩恵 means blessing or benefit — grace or favor bestowed upon someone.

Mandarin ēnhuì, Cantonese jan1 wai6, Japanese おんけい, Korean 은혜, and Vietnamese ân huệ are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` all had 恩's syllable using the wrong reading 안/'an/ㄚㄋ instead of the correct 은/'ǝn/ㄜㄋ (confirmed against 恩's own citation and siblings [[恩典]], [[恩徳]], [[恩施]], [[感恩]]). No homophones (注音 ㄜㄋㄏ⼔ㄧ unique across both words and characters).

**Flagged for a future iteration**: [[恩情]] (still unstamped) has the identical bug pattern in its 諺文/羅馬字 — will fix when its own turn comes up.
