## Notes
- [艶 (char)](../characters/艶%20(char).md) "plump, voluptuous, beautiful" is separately legitimized by [[艶]] itself. [福 (char)](../characters/福%20(char).md) "blessing, good fortune" is separately legitimized by [[福]] itself. No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

艶福 combines 艶 ("voluptuous, beautiful") with 福 ("good fortune") — "success in love."

Mandarin yànfú, japanese えんぷく (enpuku), korean 염복 (yeombok), and vietnamese diễm phúc were all already correct, fully compositional readings. In passing, fixed the 20th empty-string field bug on `characters/艶 (char).md` (`hsk_level: ""` → "無").
