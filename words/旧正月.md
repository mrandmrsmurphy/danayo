## Notes
- [旧 (char)](../characters/旧%20(char).md) "old, former" + [正 (char)](../characters/正%20(char).md) "principal" + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — literally "the old principal month," i.e. the lunar new year under the calendar Japan no longer officially uses.

旧正月 is the old new year: the Lunar New Year, as against the 正月 of 1 January. English Wiktionary glosses Japanese 旧正月 as "Lunar New Year". It has no entry for the Chinese form 舊正月, so Mandarin jiùzhèngyuè and Cantonese gau6 zing3 jyut6 here are built from the characters. For Korean it lists 구정 (the 旧正) as dated, "Korean New Year; Lunar New Year celebrated in Korea", with the synonyms 설 and 설날 and the antonym 신정 (新正); the Korean field 구정월 here is built from the characters. The Vietnamese field is left blank.

The related words are [[春節]], the Spring Festival, and [[陰暦年]], the lunar year.
