## Notes
- [磨](../characters/磨%20(char).md) "grind" + [坊](../characters/坊.md) "workshop" — "grinding workshop," a mill. Their own `stand_in` values: 磨's is itself; 坊's is [[作坊]]; none points here, so 磨坊 is an independent compound.

磨坊 is a mill, a building where grain is ground. Mandarin mòfáng and Cantonese mo6 fong1 are the ordinary Chinese words. Japanese says 製粉所 and 水車小屋 and Korean 방앗간, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[房]] (room).
