## Notes
- [華](../characters/華.md) "gorgeous, splendid" + [美 (char)](../characters/美%20(char).md) "beautiful" — radiant splendor, beauty that shines. This word is the viable form of [華](../characters/華.md): 華 alone is too bound to stand as a Dan'a'yo word, and 華美 is its `stand_in` compound, keeping 華's sense the "radiant, resplendent" one — matching its own `korean_native` gloss 빛날 ("to shine, be radiant") — in contrast to [[秀麗]] (the "lovely, graceful" sense that [[麗]] stands in for instead), splitting apart the two flavors the collapsed compound [[華麗]] used to blur together. 美's own `stand_in` is [[美]] itself — transitivity fails, no cranberry.

Worth flagging: Japanese かび (kabi) is an exact homophone of カビ (kabi, "mold, mildew"), an extremely common everyday word — context always disambiguates, but it's a striking coincidence for a word meaning "gorgeous." Mandarin huáměi and Cantonese waa4mei5 both describe something showy and splendid — clothing, decoration, prose — leaning toward ornateness rather than plain loveliness, distinguishing it from 秀麗's more restrained, graceful register.

Pronunciation fields (hwami/화미/ㄏ⺢ㄇㄧ) are the straightforward concatenation of each constituent's own stored reading — no bug. **Found and fixed a real `kwin` bug** (false→true, AND-rule: both 華 and 美 individually true). Filled blank vietnamese (hoa mỹ, a real standard Vietnamese word meaning "flowery, ornate," also fully compositional). Fixed cantonese's stray space. Added the simplified alias 华美. No homophones.
