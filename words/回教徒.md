## Notes
- [回](../characters/回%20(char).md) "turn" + [教](../characters/教.md) "teach, religion" + [徒](../characters/徒.md) "disciple, follower" — no stand-in relationship applies (回 is its own stand-in; 教's own is [[教授]]; 徒's own is [[信徒]]).

回教徒 means a Muslim — a follower of [[回教]] (Islam).

Japanese かいきょうと and Korean 회교도 are both standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese `người Hồi giáo` ("person of Islam") is a native descriptive phrase rather than a direct Sino-Vietnamese compound. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄛㄧㄍ⼄ㄨㄉㄛ unique across both words and characters).
