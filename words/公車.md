## Notes
- [公 (char)](../characters/公%20(char).md) "public" + [車 (char)](../characters/車%20(char).md) "vehicle" — literally "public vehicle."

公車 is most directly "a vehicle belonging to an organization, for the use of its members" (government car, official car), and by extension, in Taiwan Mandarin specifically, the standard everyday word for a city **bus** (gōngchē). Mandarin gōngchē and Cantonese gung1 ce1 are both standard, fully compositional readings, matching 公's and 車's own stored citations exactly. Japanese こうしゃ and Korean 공차 are compositional Sino-Xenic readings built from each character's own on'yomi/Sino-Korean reading rather than independently attested live words for "bus" in either language — Japanese uses the English loanword バス (basu) and Korean 버스 (beoseu) for the everyday word instead.

`vietnamese` is omitted: the everyday Vietnamese word for "bus" is xe buýt, an unrelated French loanword, and no attested Sino-Vietnamese reading of 公車 meaning "bus" could be confirmed. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 공촤 diverges from Sino-Korean 공차 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄫㄑ⺢ unique among both words and characters).
