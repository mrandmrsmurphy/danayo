## Notes
- [終](../words/終.md) "end, finish" (stand-in: [[終了]]) + [止 (char)](../characters/止.md) "stop" (stand-in: [[中止]]) — cessation; the sentence-final predicate form in `文法 - 02音韻論`'s 動詞終止文 ("verb-final sentence").

終止 means cessation / the final predicate form. Mandarin zhōngzhǐ, Japanese しゅうし (the real Japanese grammar term 終止形, "terminal form," is built from this exact root), and Korean 종지 are all real, standard terms, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Cantonese zung1 zi2 is a straightforward compositional reading. Vietnamese chung chỉ is compositional only. `kwin` is false: inherited from 終 (false), despite 止 itself being true.

No syllable-boundary dot is needed: the concatenated string `ㄐㄨㄫㄐㄧ` admits only one legal partition.
