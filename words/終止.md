## Notes
- [終](../characters/終.md) "end, finish" (stand-in: [[終了]]) + [止 (char)](../characters/止.md) "stop" (stand-in: [[中止]]) — cessation; the sentence-final predicate form in `文法 - 02音韻論`'s 動詞終止文 ("verb-final sentence").

Wiktionary: Chinese "to stop; to end; to discontinue; to abort; to terminate" (computing: to abort a process); Japanese 終止 (しゅうし) "stopping, termination", music "cadence", and as a verb "to stop, terminate". Used in `文法 - 02音韻論` in 動詞終止文 and in `文法 - 97品詞`, for the sentence-final predicate form.
