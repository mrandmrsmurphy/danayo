## Notes
- [乱](../characters/乱.md) "disorder, profusion" + [漫](../characters/漫.md) "pervade, fill, go everywhere" — a profusion spreading everywhere, hence "natural, unaffected, unrestrained" — most familiar in the everyday Japanese chengyu-style compound [[天真乱漫]] ("innocent and artless").

乱漫/爛漫 (らんまん, ranman) describes flowers blooming in vivid profusion (爛漫と咲き誇る, "blooming in glorious profusion") and, by metaphorical extension, an unaffected, spontaneously natural quality in a person's character — most commonly seen as part of 天真爛漫, describing childlike innocence and artlessness. Mandarin lànmàn, Cantonese laan6 maan6, and Korean 란만 all preserve this same dual floral/personality sense. **Vietnamese content note**: filled with hồn nhiên, the real, everyday Vietnamese word for "innocent, artless, natural" (as in ngây thơ hồn nhiên, "innocent and artless" — the same phrase-shape as 天真爛漫) — a native term rather than a forced Sino-Vietnamese compositional calque, since 乱's own loàn/loạn and 漫's own man/máng don't independently attest to a fixed compound in this sense.

`kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 란만 coincides exactly with Sino-Korean 란만.
