## Notes
- [鞀](../characters/鞀.md) "pellet drum" + [鼓 (char)](../characters/鼓%20(char).md) "drum"
- 鞀鼓 is the stand-in word for [鞀](../characters/鞀.md): the character's meaning (a pellet drum) is identical, but 鞀 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 鞀鼓 is its `stand_in` compound. No `#cranberry`: 鼓's own `stand_in` is 鼓 itself, not this word, so transitivity fails.

鞀鼓/鼗鼓 (táogǔ) is the classical Chinese term for the pellet drum — a small double-headed hand drum with two pellets on strings that strike the drum face when the handle is twirled — better known today by its colloquial name 拨浪鼓 (bōlanggǔ, "rattle-drum"), a traditional East Asian children's toy and historically a peddler's signal instrument. Japanese とうこ (tōko) and Korean 도고 (dogo) are the compositional Sino readings; Sino-Vietnamese đào cổ likewise names the same instrument.
