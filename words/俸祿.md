## Notes
- [俸](../characters/俸.md) "salary" + [祿](../characters/祿.md) "wealth; official emolument" — "official salary, stipend, emolument." `vietnamese: bổng lộc` is directly attested, a well-known everyday Vietnamese word for "perks, benefits, emoluments." `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 뽕록 diverges from Sino-Korean 봉록 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄈㄛㄫㄌㄛㄎ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 祿's own `stand_in` is this exact word (俸祿), so 祿 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 俸祿.
