## Notes
- [世](../characters/世.md) "world, generation" + [界](../characters/界.md) "boundary, realm" — legitimizing note: 世's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, so 世 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word; 界's own `stand_in` is [[境界]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply.

Mandarin shìjiè, Cantonese sai3 gaai3, Japanese せかい, Korean 세계, and Sino-Vietnamese thế giới are all standard compositional readings.
