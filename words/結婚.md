## Notes
- [結 (char)](../characters/結%20(char).md) "tie, join" + [婚](../characters/婚.md) "marry" — 婚's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 結's own is [[結]] itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 結婚 legitimizes 婚 alone.

結婚 means to marry, or marriage itself.

Mandarin jiéhūn (the more common reading; jiēhūn is a rarer variant), Cantonese git3 fan1, Japanese けっこん (kekkon), and Korean 결혼 (gyeolhon) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 結婚 is itself a common attested word for "marry" in all four languages. Vietnamese kết hôn is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading.
