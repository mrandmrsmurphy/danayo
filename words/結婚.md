## Notes
- [結 (char)](../characters/結%20(char).md) "tie, join" + [婚](../characters/婚.md) "marry" — 婚's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 結's own is [[結]] itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 結婚 legitimizes 婚 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 結 is individually `kwin: false`.

結婚 means to marry, or marriage itself.

Mandarin jiéhūn (the more common reading; jiēhūn is a rarer variant), Cantonese git3 fan1, Japanese けっこん (kekkon), and Korean 결혼 (gyeolhon) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 結婚 is itself a common attested word for "marry" in all four languages. Vietnamese kết hôn is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. **Fixed a real bug**: `cantonese` had jie2 hun1 (a garbled reading, unrelated to either character's own jyutping — 結's is git3, 婚's is fan1) — corrected to git3 fan1. Also fixed a duplicate-headword `aliases` entry (結婚 listed as its own alias) and a comma-joined `mandarin` string. No homophones (a stray field-level match on the sibling [[結婚礼]] traced to a truncated 諺文 there — fixed in passing).
