## Notes
- [珈](../characters/珈.md) + [拿](../characters/拿.md) + [陀](../characters/陀.md) — an older Chinese phonetic transliteration of "Canada," alongside the modern standard form 加拿大 (listed as an alias); no stand-in relationship applies (珈's own stand-in is [[珈啡]]; 拿's own is [[拿捕]]; 陀's own is [[偈陀]]).

Japanese カナダ and Korean 캐나다 are the real, standard transliteration loanwords in their languages.
