## Notes
- [感](../characters/感.md) "feel, sense" + [恩](../characters/恩.md) "kindness, mercy" — no stand-in relationship applies (感's own stand-in is [[感触]]; 恩's own is [[恩寵]]).

感恩 means to feel thankful or be grateful — appreciation for kindness received.

Mandarin gǎn'ēn, Cantonese gam2 jan1, Japanese かんおん, and Korean 감은 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese cảm ơn is the standard everyday native term for "thank you" (trimmed the comma-joined variant spelling "cám ơn"). No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄇㄜㄋ unique across both words and characters).
