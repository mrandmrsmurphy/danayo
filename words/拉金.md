## Notes
- [拉](../characters/拉%20(char).md) "drag; pull" + [金](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal" — `mandarin`/`cantonese` fields hold the avoided real element character's reading: 𬬻/鑪 `lú`/`lou4` (verified against dictionary sources), per the established convention on [[丹金]] and its series.

**Anomaly flagged, not resolved here**: unlike the other person-eponym elements in this series — [[居里金]] (Curie), [[愛因金]] (Einstein), [[費米金]] (Fermi), [[諾貝金]] (Nobel), [[門捷金]] (Mendeleev), [[羅倫金]] (Lawrence) — which all use a **two-character** phonetic reduction of the surname before 金, 拉金 uses only a single character (拉) for rutherfordium (named after Ernest Rutherford, transliterated 卢瑟福/Lúsèfú in Mandarin). This breaks the otherwise consistent two-character person-name pattern; a fuller reduction such as 卢瑟金/拉瑟金 might be more consistent, but renaming the entry is a naming-architecture decision beyond the scope of this pass — documented here for a future dedicated review rather than acted on unilaterally.

Korean 러더포듐, Japanese ラザホージウム, and Vietnamese rutherfordi are all the standard IUPAC loanword forms, matching the pattern already established across this series (see [[丹金]]). Removed redundant `品詞` field. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄚㄆㄍㄧㄇ unique across both words and characters).
