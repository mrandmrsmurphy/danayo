## Notes
- [凶 (char)](../characters/凶%20(char).md) "unlucky; sinister" + [器](../characters/器.md) "container; vessel; implement"

凶器 means a lethal weapon: an implement used to kill or seriously injure, in particular the weapon of a crime. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 凶器 (an alias, 兇器; Mandarin xiōngqì, Cantonese hung1 hei3) as "lethal weapon" and "weapon for criminal purposes", with the example 殺人凶器, "murder weapon". It also records a jocular use, an alternative for 胸器, and two archaic senses: "weapon", and "funerary implement; burial device". Japanese きょうき is "deadly item; weapon", Korean 흉기 is "deadly weapon; lethal weapon", and Vietnamese hung khí is "tool for homicide; murder weapon". The five readings agree.

The word joins 凶, ill-omened or sinister, with 器, a crafted implement, "the sinister tool". The ordinary word for a weapon is [[武器]].
