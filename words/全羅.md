## Notes
- [全](../characters/全%20(char).md) "whole" + [羅](../characters/羅.md) "net, Rome" — the name of the Jeolla region of Korea.

全羅 (Jeolla) is the historical Joseon province in the southwest of the Korean peninsula, a rice-growing region. The name combines the first characters of 全州 (Jeonju) and 羅州 (Naju), its two chief cities. The province was divided in 1896 into 全羅北道 (North Jeolla) and 全羅南道 (South Jeolla); North Jeolla has been called 全北特別自治道 (Jeonbuk Special Self-Governing Province) since January 2024. Korean Wiktionary has 全羅 as the hanja form of 전라; English, Chinese, Japanese and Vietnamese Wiktionary have no entry.

The Chinese, Japanese and Vietnamese readings are the compositional ones from the character pages (not re-checked on a dictionary page). Japanese ordinarily uses katakana after the Korean reading (チョルラ, from memory, not re-checked). 羅 here is the same character as in [[新羅]], where it also stands for a Korean place name.
