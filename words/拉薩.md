## Notes
- [拉](../characters/拉%20(char).md) "drag, pull" + [薩](../characters/薩.md) "bodhisattva" — phonetic spelling of Lhasa. No stand-in relationship applies (拉 is its own stand-in; 薩's is [[菩薩]]).

拉薩 (Lhasa) is the capital of the Tibet Autonomous Region in southwestern China and the traditional capital of Tibet. Per Wiktionary the Chinese name is a Qing-era Mandarin rendering via Manchu of the Tibetan ལྷ་ས་ (lha sa), so the characters are phonetic and carry no meaning of their own; obsolete 喇薩 and the historical name 邏些 are also listed. 萨 is an alias of 薩, so 拉萨 is kept as the word's alias.

English Wiktionary has no Japanese, Korean or Vietnamese entry for 拉薩. The Japanese field holds ラサ, the name used in Japanese (Japanese Wikipedia, ラサ), which is not a Sino-Japanese reading; the Korean field holds the North Korean reading derived from the character pages, 랍살 (not an attested reading), and the usual South Korean name today is 라싸 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Lạp Tát is the Hán-Việt form given on Vietnamese Wikipedia, which uses Lhasa as its usual name. The 注音 is ㄌㄚㄆㄙㄚㄊ; the compound 拉薩犬 (Lhasa apso) is listed in Wiktionary and is not claimed here. Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese entry; Vietnamese and Japanese Wikipedia for the other languages. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄚㄆㄙㄚㄊ unique across both words and characters).
