## Notes
- [越 (char)](../characters/越%20(char).md) "exceed" + [境](../characters/境.md) "boundary, border, frontier" — "to transgress, infringe on the border of" (越境, "cross-border," as in 越境攻撃, "cross-border attack"). Mandarin yuèjìng, Cantonese jyut6 ging2, Japanese えっきょう, and Korean 월경 are all directly attested standard readings.

**Real `kwin` bug found and fixed**: stored as `true`, but byte-level comparison shows Dan'a'yo's own 諺文 웓경 genuinely diverges from this word's own korean field 월경 (first syllable) — corrected to `kwin: false`. `vietnamese: việt cảnh` is the honest compositional reading (越's own việt + 境's own cảnh); the more common everyday Vietnamese phrase for illegally crossing a border is vượt biên (using native vượt, "to cross," rather than Sino việt). No homophones (注音 ⼔ㄊㄍ⼶ㄫ unique among both words and characters).
