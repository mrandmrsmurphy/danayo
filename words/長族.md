## Notes
- [長](../characters/長%20(char).md) "eldest, chief" + [族](../characters/族.md) "tribe, clan" — "the Eldest Tribe, Reuben." Their own `stand_in` values: 長's is itself; 族's is [[家族]]; none points here, so 長族 is an independent compound.

長族 is the tribe of Reuben, Jacob's firstborn son by Leah (Genesis 29:32; Jacob calls him "my firstborn" in 49:3). It is a Dan'a'yo **neologism**, named by role, "Eldest Tribe," on the vault's translation principles, not by the Hebrew etymology "behold, a son." It is one of the twelve tribes of Israel. Compare [[聴族]] (Simeon) and [[獅族]] (Judah). See [[基督敎]].

- Hebrew name: רְאוּבֵן (Re'uven) (注音 ㄌㄝ·ㄨㄅㄝㄋ).
