## Notes
- [鼠](../characters/鼠.md) "rat" + [類](../characters/類.md) "kind, sort, category" — no `#cranberry`: 鼠's own `stand_in` is [[熊鼠]], 類's is [[種類]]; neither points here. `kwin` is false (AND-rule: both constituents individually false).

Names the taxonomic superfamily Muroidea (mice, rats, and their relatives). Japanese ネズミ上科 (nezumi-jōka) and Korean 쥐상과 (jwi-sangkwa) are both real attested taxonomic terms, each built the same way as the Dan'a'yo headword: native "mouse" word + Sino "superfamily" rank term. Vietnamese thử loại and Mandarin shǔlèi/Cantonese syu2 leoi6 are the plain compositional forms, matching the same pattern already used on the sibling words [[鳥類]] (điểu loại) and [[魚類]] (ngư loại).

**Fixed two real bugs**: `mandarin` had been truncated to bare "shǔ" (missing 類's own "lèi" entirely), and `cantonese` held "su3," an unrelated garbage value matching neither 鼠's own syu2 nor 類's own leoi6 — both corrected to the proper compositional readings. Also fixed the `aliases` entry: "即鼠總科" contained a stray leading "即" ("namely/i.e.") — explanatory prose accidentally left inside the alias value rather than a genuine variant written form — trimmed to the real alternate title 鼠總科.
