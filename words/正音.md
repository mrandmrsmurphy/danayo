## Notes
- [正](../characters/正%20(char).md) "correct, proper" + [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" — no stand-in relationship applies (正's own stand-in is itself; 音's own is [[音楽]]). The standard or prescriptively correct pronunciation of a character or word, as opposed to a regional or colloquial reading. Most famously the second word of 訓民正音 (Hunminjeongeum), the Korean document introducing Hangul. Attested across Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, Korean, and Vietnamese. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄫㄨㄇ unique across both words and characters).
