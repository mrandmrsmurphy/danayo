## Notes
- [変 (char)](../characters/変%20(char).md) "change" + [遷](../characters/遷.md) "move; shift"

変遷 is change over time: the successive changes something goes through, as of a language, a custom or a city. Wiktionary glosses Japanese へんせん as "transition; changes; vicissitudes; to undergo a change". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 변천 matches.

It names the whole course of change, where [変化](変化.md) is a change and [推移](推移.md) is the passing of a state along that course.
