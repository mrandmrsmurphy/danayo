## Notes
- [桜](../characters/桜%20(char).md) "cherry blossom" + [桃](../characters/桃.md) "peach" — no stand-in relationship applies (桜's own stand-in is itself; 桃's own is [[桃子]]).

桜桃 means cherry (the fruit).

Mandarin yīngtáo, Cantonese jing1 tou4, Japanese おうとう, and Vietnamese anh đào are all standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 앵두 is the real, standard modern Korean word for cherry — a lexicalized irregular form (compositional would be 앵도, from 桜's own 앵 + 桃's own 도), kept as-is rather than treated as a bug. Added the missing `pos: 名詞` field. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄫㄉㄚㄨ unique across both words and characters).
