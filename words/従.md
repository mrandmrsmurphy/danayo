## Notes
- [従](../characters/従%20(char).md) "obey, observe" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

従 means to obey or observe — following a rule, order, or precedent.

Mandarin cóng, Cantonese cung4, Japanese じゅう, Korean 종, and Vietnamese tùng are all standard, fully compositional readings. Part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read jong/종/ㄐㄛㄫ) with [[鐘]] ("bell") and [[鍾]] ("alcohol bottle").
