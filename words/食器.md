## Notes
- [食 (char)](../characters/食%20(char).md) "food" + [器](../characters/器.md) "vessel"

食器 is tableware: the vessels and implements that food is served and eaten from. Wiktionary glosses Japanese しょっき as "tableware" and Mandarin shíqì as "food vessel". Korean 식기 is the reading of the characters, and the Cantonese reading is built from the characters.

It covers the [碗](碗.md), bowl, the [皿](皿.md), dish, the [箸](箸.md), chopsticks, and the [叉](叉.md), fork. The pot it was cooked in is the [鍋](鍋.md).
