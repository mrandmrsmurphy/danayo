## Notes
- [蒼](../characters/蒼%20(char).md) "pale blue-green" + [白](../characters/白%20(char).md) "white" — "ashen white," pale. Their own `stand_in` values: 蒼's is itself; 白's is itself; none points here, so 蒼白 is an independent compound.

蒼白 is pale: of a face, wan and ashen from fear, illness or shock, and by extension of a colour without life. Mandarin cāngbái, Cantonese cong1 baak6 and Japanese そうはく are attested, and Korean 창백 is the same compound. Compare [[白]] (white) and [[退色]] (to fade).
