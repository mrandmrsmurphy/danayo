## Notes
- [奮](../characters/奮.md) "stirred up, arose" + [闘](../characters/闘.md) "fight" — no stand-in relationship applies (奮's own stand-in is [[奮発]]; 闘's own is [[闘争]]).

奮闘 means to struggle, fight, or strive — putting forth vigorous, sustained effort against difficulty, as in [[孤軍奮闘]] ("fighting alone against the odds").

Mandarin fèndòu, Cantonese fan5 dau3, Japanese ふんとう, Korean 분투, and Vietnamese phấn đấu are all standard, fully compositional readings. Removed the redundant self-referential alias (奮闘 aliasing itself); kept traditional 奮鬥 and simplified 奋斗. No homophones (注音 ㄈㄨㄋㄉㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).
