## Notes
- [上 (char)](../characters/上%20(char).md) "upper" + [旬](../characters/旬.md) "10 day period of the month" — no `#cranberry`: 旬's own `stand_in` is [[旬日]], 上's is 上 itself; neither points here.

Mandarin shàngxún, Japanese じょうじゅん, Korean 상순, and Sino-Vietnamese thượng tuần are all standard compositional readings.
