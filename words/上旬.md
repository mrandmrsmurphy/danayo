## Notes
- [上 (char)](../characters/上%20(char).md) "upper" + [旬](../characters/旬.md) "10 day period of the month" — no `#cranberry`: 旬's own `stand_in` is [[旬日]], 上's is 上 itself; neither points here.

Mandarin shàngxún, Japanese じょうじゅん, Korean 상순, and Sino-Vietnamese thượng tuần are all standard compositional readings. **Fixed a real garbage-value bug**: `cantonese` had been `sang4 xun2` — neither syllable matched anything real; `xun2` in particular appears to be leftover Mandarin pinyin (旬's own mandarin is `xún`) mistakenly placed in the cantonese field — corrected to `seong6 ceon4`, the proper compositional concatenation of 上's own `seong6` and 旬's own `ceon4`. Fixed a missing citation on `characters/上 (char).md`'s Words list.
