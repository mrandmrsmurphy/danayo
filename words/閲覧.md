## Notes
- [閲](../characters/閲.md) "inspect, read" + [覧](../characters/覧.md) "view" — "look over." Their own `stand_in` values: 閲's is [[検閲]]; 覧's is [[観覧]]; none points here, so 閲覧 is an independent compound.

閲覧 is to look over a document or page: to peruse, view or browse. Mandarin yuèlǎn, Cantonese jyut6 laam5, Japanese えつらん and Korean 열람 use it, and in Japanese it is the ordinary word for browsing a site. Chinese names the browser program 瀏覽器 from the same last character; Dan'a'yo says it with 閲覧 and the device suffix 器, as in [[顕示器]], without a word of its own. Compare [[読書]] (to read).
