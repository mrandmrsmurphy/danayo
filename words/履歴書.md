## Notes
- [履 (char)](../characters/履%20(char).md) "tread; record" + [歴](../characters/歴.md) "history, record" + [書](../characters/書.md) "writing; document" — resume.

履歴書 is a résumé or curriculum vitae: a written summary of a person's education and work history, submitted when applying for a job. It is "record-document." Mandarin lǚlìshū (written 履歷書), Cantonese lei5 lik6 syu1, Japanese りれきしょ and Korean 이력서 are all confirmed on Wiktionary; Mandarin also has the synonyms 簡歷 (jiǎnlì), 履歷 and 履歷表. It goes with [[面接]] (interview) and [[募集]] (recruitment).
