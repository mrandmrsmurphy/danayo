## Notes
- [継](../characters/継.md) "continue; step-" + [子](../characters/子.md) "child" — stepchild.

継子 is a stepchild: a child of one's spouse by a former marriage. It is "continuing-child," the child counterpart of [[継母]] and [[継父]], built on the same "continuing [relation]" construction. Mandarin jìzǐ and Cantonese gai3 zi2 are confirmed on Wiktionary, where the Chinese entry also gives the sense "adopted son"; Japanese and Korean were not confirmed. For an adopted child see [[養子]].
