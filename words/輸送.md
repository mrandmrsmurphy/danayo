## Notes
- [輸](../characters/輸.md) "transport" + [送 (char)](../characters/送%20(char).md) "send, deliver"
- This word is the viable form of [輸](../characters/輸.md): the character's meaning (to transport) is identical, but 輸 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 輸送 is its `stand_in` compound.

輸送 means to transport, to carry goods or people from one place to another, typically by vehicle and on a considerable scale. 輸 is "to transport, to convey" and 送 is "to send, to deliver". Mandarin shūsòng (输送 in simplified script), Cantonese syu1 sung3, Japanese ゆそう (yusō; written 輸送) and Korean 수송 (susong) are the same compound.

In Dan'a'yo it is the transporting itself, beside [[運転]] (to operate or drive a vehicle) and [[送還]] (to send a person back).
