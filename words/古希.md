## Notes
- [古](../characters/古.md) "ancient, old" + [希](../characters/希.md) "rare, hope"

From the line 人生七十古來稀, "it has been rare since ancient times for people to live to seventy", in 杜甫's 曲江二首 (Wikisource).

Wiktionary: Chinese 古稀, literary, "70 years of age, septuagenarian"; Japanese 古希 (こき) "70 years of age" and, short-form, "Ancient Greek"; Korean 고희 is the hanja form of 古稀. The original spelling is 古稀; 古稀 is kept as an alias here.
