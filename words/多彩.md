## Notes
- [多 (char)](../characters/多%20(char).md) "many" + [彩](../characters/彩.md) "vibrant, multicolored" — literally "many-colored"; stand-in for [彩](../characters/彩.md), which cannot appear independently.

多彩 describes something multicolored, vibrant, or flamboyant — a near-synonym of [彩色](彩色.md) ("colored," the plainer adjectival sense of the same 彩), distinguished by 多彩's added emphasis on variety and richness of color rather than merely being colored at all. It's also productive in the larger four-character set phrase 丰富多彩/豐富多彩, "richly varied and colorful."

Mandarin duōcǎi, Cantonese do1 coi2, Korean 다채 (dachae), and Japanese たさい (tasai) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real everyday word for "multicolored, varied" in its language. Vietnamese đa thái is likewise compositional, from 多's đa and 彩's own thái.
