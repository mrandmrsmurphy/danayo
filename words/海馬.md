## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [馬 (char)](../characters/馬%20(char).md) "horse" — no stand-in relationship applies (海's own stand-in is [[海洋]]; 馬's own is itself).

海馬 means seahorse.

Mandarin hǎimǎ, Cantonese hoi2 maa5, and Korean 해마 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 海's and 馬's own stored citations exactly. Japanese うみうま (native compound, う み+うま) and Vietnamese cá ngựa ("horse fish") are the real, standard native words for "seahorse" — the same real-world-species-name convention already established on [[河馬]]/[[海牛]]/[[海豚]]. `kwin: true` because 諺文 해마 matches Korean 해마 exactly. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄧㄇㄚ unique across both words and characters).
