## Notes
- [賄](../characters/賄.md) "bribe" + [賂](../characters/賂.md) "bribe" — both characters share this word as their own `stand_in`, appearing only bound together in Dan'a'yo.

賄賂 means a bribe.

**Real bugs found and fixed**: `cantonese` had 賄's syllable misspelled fui2 instead of its own citation kui2 — corrected. `korean` mixed a native word (뇌물) with the compositional Sino-Korean form (회뢰) into a single malformed string — corrected to just 회뢰. Japanese わいろ and Vietnamese hối lộ are both standard readings, matching Dan'a'yo's own reading exactly (`kwin: true`). **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 賄賂 and [[回路]] ("circuit") share an identical reading — standardized the non-canonical `[!Tip]` callout into the proper reciprocal `>[!warning] Homophones` format.
