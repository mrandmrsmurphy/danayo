## Notes
- [高 (char)](../characters/高%20(char).md) "high" + [校](../characters/校.md) "school" — contraction of 高等学校; no `#cranberry`: 校's own `stand_in` is [[学校]], 高's is 高 itself, neither points here.

Mandarin gāoxiào, Cantonese gou1haau6, Japanese こうこう, and Korean 고교 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Sino-Vietnamese cao hiệu is the compositional form; everyday Vietnamese instead uses the native phrase trường trung học ("secondary school").
