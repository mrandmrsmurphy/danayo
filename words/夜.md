## Notes
- [夜 (char)](../characters/夜%20(char).md) "night"

夜 means "night" (夜市, "night market"; 夜光, "phosphorescent, glow-in-the-dark"; 昼夜, "day and night").

**Content corrected**: the character page's `vietnamese` field held the malformed string `"dạ, dà"`. `dà` was flagged as likely erroneous rather than kept as a second candidate — it carries the huyền tone, which regularly corresponds to a level-tone Middle Chinese syllable, but 夜 is a departing-tone syllable (which regularly yields the nặng tone, i.e. dạ); `dà` also matches the exact pattern already stored for unrelated characters elsewhere in the vault (遮, 陀), suggesting accidental duplication rather than a genuine attested reading of 夜. Removed rather than kept as unverified noise.

Mandarin yè and Cantonese je6 preserve the sense directly. Japanese ヤ (YA, on'yomi, formal register) alongside native よ/よる (yo/yoru, "night," the everyday word) cover the sense. Korean 야 (ya) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 밤 (bam, "night"). Vietnamese dạ is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in dạ tiệc ("evening party"), bán dạ ("midnight"), and dạ thị ("night market," directly paralleling 夜市).

This word shares its reading ('ya/야/⼘) with [[也]] ("also; too," already perfected, already cross-linking here).
