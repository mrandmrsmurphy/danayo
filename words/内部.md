## Notes
- [内](../characters/内.md) "inside" + [部 (char)](../characters/部%20(char).md) "part, section" — "interior, inside" (also, figuratively, "internal" as in an organization — nội bộ công ty, "within the company"). `vietnamese: nội bộ` is one of the most common everyday Vietnamese words for this concept, a direct match. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 뇌봇 diverges from Sino-Korean 내부 in the first syllable's vowel.
