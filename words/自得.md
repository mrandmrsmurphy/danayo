## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" is separately legitimized by [[自身]]. [得](../characters/得.md) "get, obtain" is separately legitimized by [[獲得]]. No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

自得 combines 自 ("self") with 得 ("get, obtain") — "to come to a realization of one's own accord," and by extension "self-satisfied, complacent."

Mandarin zìdé, cantonese zi6dak1 (space removed), and japanese じとく (jitoku) were all already correct, fully compositional readings.
