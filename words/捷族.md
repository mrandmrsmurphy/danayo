## Notes
- [捷](../characters/捷.md) "swift, quick" + [族](../characters/族.md) "tribe, clan" — "the Swift Tribe, Naphtali." Their own `stand_in` values: 捷's is [[敏捷]]; 族's is [[家族]]; none points here, so 捷族 is an independent compound.

捷族 is the tribe of Naphtali, Bilhah's second son. It is a Dan'a'yo **neologism**, named for Jacob's blessing, "Naphtali is a doe let loose" (Genesis 49:21), "Swift Tribe," not for the Hebrew meaning "my wrestling." Compare [[判族]] (Dan) and [[武族]] (Gad). See [[基督敎]].

- Hebrew name: נַפְתָּלִי (Naftali) (注音 ㄋㄚㄆㄊㄚㄌㄧ).

- See [基督敎](../lexipedia/基督敎.md).
