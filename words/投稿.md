## Notes
- [投](../characters/投%20(char).md) "throw, send in" + [稿](../characters/稿%20(char).md) "manuscript" — "send in a manuscript." Their own `stand_in` values: 投's is itself; 稿's is itself; none points here, so 投稿 is an independent compound.

投稿 is to send in a piece of writing for publication: to a newspaper, a magazine or, today, a website or forum. Mandarin tóugǎo, Cantonese tau4 gou2, Japanese とうこう and Korean 투고 all use it, and in Japanese it is the ordinary verb for posting to a site. Dan'a'yo takes it as "post" in the online sense. Compare [[網站]] (website), [[部落格]] (blog) and [[郵便]] (postal mail), which is a different "post".
