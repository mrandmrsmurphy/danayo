## Notes
- [懸](../characters/懸%20(char).md) "hang, suspend, hoist" + [垂](../characters/垂.md) "hang, dangle" — no stand-in relationship applies (懸's own stand-in is itself; 垂's own is [[垂掛]]).

懸垂 means to hang over, or an overhand grip/pull-up.

Mandarin xuánchuí, Cantonese jyun4 seoi4, Japanese けんすい, Korean 현수, and Vietnamese huyền thuỳ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `korean`/`vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⼔ㄋㄐㄨㄧ unique across both words and characters).
