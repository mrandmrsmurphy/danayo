## Notes
- [国](../characters/国.md) "nation" + [旗](../characters/旗.md) "flag" — no stand-in relationship applies (国's own stand-in is [[国家]]; 旗's is [[旗幟]]).

国旗 means (national) flag.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `vietnamese` had a native paraphrase ("lá cờ," literally "flag leaf") rather than the real Sino-Vietnamese compound — corrected to `quốc kỳ` (compositional, extremely common term). Japanese こっき and Korean 국기 are both standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄎㄎㄧ unique across both words and characters).
