## Notes
- [文](../characters/文.md) "culture" + [書](../characters/書.md) "book" — no stand-in relationship applies (文's own stand-in is [[文化]]; 書's own is [[書本]]).

文書 means letter or document.

Mandarin wénshū, Cantonese man4 syu1, Japanese ぶんしょ, Korean 문서, and Vietnamese văn thư (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Simplified `vietnamese` from a native-paraphrase pair (tài liệu, văn kiện) to the standard compositional term. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄨㄋㄙ⼄ unique across both words and characters).
