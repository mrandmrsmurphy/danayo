## Notes
- [曽 (char)](../characters/曽%20(char).md) "great- (a generation further)" + [祖](../characters/祖.md) "ancestor" + [母](../characters/母.md) "mother" — great-grandmother.

曽祖母 is a great-grandmother: the mother of one's grandparent. It is "great-ancestor-mother," the pair of [[曽祖父]]. Mandarin zēngzǔmǔ and Cantonese zang1 zou2 mou5 are confirmed on Wiktionary (the entry specifies the mother of a paternal grandfather; Dan'a'yo takes the general sense); Japanese そうそぼ and Korean 증조모 are the compositional readings and come from memory. The downward counterpart is [[曽孫]].
