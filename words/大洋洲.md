## Notes
- [大](../characters/大%20(char).md) "great" + [洋](../characters/洋.md) "ocean" + [洲](../characters/洲%20(char).md) "continent" — no stand-in relationship applies (大's own stand-in is itself; 洋's is [[大洋]] itself, meaning 洋 stands independently via that word; 洲 is its own stand-in).

大洋洲 (Oceania) names the continental region encompassing Australia and the Pacific islands.

Japanese たいようしゅう, Korean 대양주, Mandarin Dàyángzhōu, and Cantonese daai6 joeng4 zau1 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese Châu Đại Dương reverses the word order, matching the standard Vietnamese convention for continent names (Châu Á, Châu Âu, etc.). Removed the redundant `品詞` field. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄧ·⼘ㄫㄐㄨㄛ unique across both words and characters).
