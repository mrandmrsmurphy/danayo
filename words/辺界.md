## Notes
Mandarin biānjiè, cantonese bin1 gaai3, korean 변계, and vietnamese biên giới are all directly attested standard readings. **Found and fixed a wrong-compound reading bug on `japanese`**: the stored value へんきょう actually belongs to the unrelated (though similar-meaning) compound [[辺境]] ("frontier," 辺+境, on-reading KYOU) — corrected to the honest compositional へんかい (辺's own HEN + 界's own KAI). **Found and removed a bad-aliases-entry**: `辺境` is that same genuinely different compound (different second character, 境 not 界, with its own dedicated page), not a spelling variant of 辺界; kept `邊界`, the legitimate traditional-character variant (辺→邊). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 번겨 diverges from Sino-Korean 변계 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄝㄋㄍ⼶ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 辺's own `stand_in` is this exact word (辺界), so 辺 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 辺界; 界's own `stand_in` is instead 境界, so this word does not legitimize 界.
