## Notes
- [水](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [田](../characters/田.md) "field" — no stand-in relationship applies (水's own stand-in is itself; 田's own is [[田野]]).

水田 means rice paddy, paddy field.

Mandarin shuǐtián, Cantonese seoi2 tin4, Japanese すいでん, and Korean 수전 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 水's and 田's own stored citations exactly). Vietnamese ruộng lúa is the real, standard Vietnamese term for "rice paddy" (native ruộng "field" + lúa "rice"), kept as-is.
