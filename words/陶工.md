## Notes
- [陶](../characters/陶.md) "pottery" + [工](../characters/工.md) "artisan"

陶工 is a potter: a craftsperson who makes pottery. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin táogōng as "potter" and Japanese とうこう as "potter". Korean 도공 is the same word. Cantonese tou4 gung1 matches.

The product is [陶器](陶器.md), pottery, and the maker belongs among the [工匠](工匠.md), artisans. Glazed, high-fired ware is the [磁器](磁器.md).
