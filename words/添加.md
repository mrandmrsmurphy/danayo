## Notes
- [添](../characters/添.md) "add to" + [加](../characters/加.md) "increase"
- This word is the viable form of [添](../characters/添.md): the character's meaning (to add to) is identical, but 添 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 添加 is its `stand_in` compound.

添加 means to add something extra to something that is already there: an ingredient, an additive, a supplement. Mandarin tiānjiā, Cantonese tim1 gaa1, Japanese てんか (tenka; 添加物, "food additives") and Korean 첨가 (cheomga) are the same compound, familiar from food and chemical labelling (无添加 / 無添加, "additive-free"). 添 is "to add to, to supplement" and 加 "to add, increase".

Dan'a'yo-specific sense: the shared one. In the Physical domain it stands beside the mixing words as the act of putting a further thing in, while [[加多]] (in Dimensions) is about increasing a quantity, and arithmetic addition is the separate [[加法]].
