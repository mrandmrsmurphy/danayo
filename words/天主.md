## Notes
- [天](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven" + [主](../characters/主.md) "lord" — no stand-in relationship applies (天 is its own stand-in; 主's own is [[主人]]).

天主 (literally "Lord of Heaven") is the traditional Catholic term for God, as introduced by early Jesuit missionaries (天主教, "Catholicism").

**Real bugs found and fixed**: `japanese` had been contaminated with the unrelated native word 神様 (かみさま, "God/deity" in a general Shinto/Protestant sense) instead of 天主's own compositional on'yomi reading — corrected to てんしゅ (the genuine historical Japanese Catholic term, as in 天主堂 "Catholic church"). `korean` similarly had native Protestant-preferred terms (하나님, 하느님) instead of the direct compositional Catholic term — corrected to 천주 (as in 천주교 "Catholicism"). `aliases` incorrectly listed 神様, which is not an orthographic variant of 天主 but an unrelated near-synonym — removed. Mandarin Tiānzhǔ, Cantonese tin1 zyu2, and Vietnamese Thiên Chúa are all standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄝㄋㄐㄨ unique across both words and characters).
