## Notes
- [離](../characters/離.md) "separate, leave" + [子](../characters/子.md) "son, child" — ion.

離子 is an ion: an atom or molecule that has gained or lost electrons ([[電子]]) and so carries a net electric charge ([[電荷]]). Positive ions are 陽離子 (cations) and negative ones 陰離子 (anions) in Mandarin. 離 "separate" refers to the way ions separate from one another when a salt ([[塩]]) dissolves in water. Mandarin lízǐ and Cantonese lei4 zi2 are the same compound; Japanese ion is written イオン (and Korean 이온), borrowed from English, and `japanese` and `korean` hold those real words.
