## Notes
- [再](../characters/再.md) "again" + [度](../characters/度.md) "time, occasion" — "again, once more, a second time" (再度確認, "to reconfirm"). Mandarin zàidù, Cantonese zoi3 dou6, and Japanese さいど are all directly attested standard readings; Korean 재도 is compositional and less common in everyday speech than the native 다시/또 (both "again"). `vietnamese: tái độ` is likewise compositional and formal/literary; everyday Vietnamese uses the native lại for "again." `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 재도 matches the compositional Sino-Korean 재도 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄧㄉㄛ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 再's own `stand_in` is this exact word (再度), so 再 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 再度.
