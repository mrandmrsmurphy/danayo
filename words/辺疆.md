## Notes
- [辺](../characters/辺.md) "edge, border, side" + [疆](../characters/疆.md) "boundary, territory" — this word is the stand-in for [疆](../characters/疆.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

辺疆 names the frontier region itself — the outlying territory near a country's border, as opposed to 邊界/辺界 ("the border line" as a boundary) or 邊境/辺境 ("the border area" more generally). The headword is written with 辺, this vault's canonical parent form for the character that also appears as traditional 邊 and simplified 边; those two are registered aliases on [[辺]]'s own page and are never used independently anywhere in the vault, including in a word's own title — so `边疆`/`邊疆` are recorded here only as spelling-variant `aliases:`, not as the filename itself.

**Correction (2026-09-12, user-directed):** this page was originally created and titled `边疆.md`, on the theory that an alias glyph could still be used as a word's own conventional spelling even while its `characters:` frontmatter cited the parent form. The user identified this as wrong — Dan'a'yo never spells a word with anything but a character's canonical form; an alias glyph with no page of its own (like 边) cannot appear anywhere outside its parent's `aliases:` field, full stop. Renamed to `辺疆.md` accordingly; see the equivalent fix on [[狡滑]] and the corrected [[feedback_alias_parent_form]] memory.

**Japanese preserves 辺疆 (henkyō) as the historically original form of a still-current word** — 辺境, the everyday modern spelling, substitutes 境 ("boundary") for 疆 for legibility (疆 is not a Jōyō character), the same kind of phonetic/semantic kanji substitution seen elsewhere in this vault (e.g. 罔/両 standing in for 魍/魎). [[辺境]] is already a Dan'a'yo word in its own right; 辺疆 and 辺境 are near-synonyms sharing the same first syllable, differing mainly in register — 辺疆 leans toward the classical/literary and toward Chinese/Korean usage specifically, while 辺境 is the default in Japanese.

Korean 변강 (byeon-gang) and Vietnamese biên cương both concatenate their characters' own independent readings transparently. **Flagging, not fixing**: [[辺]]'s own stored `cantonese` field reads `dou6`, which does not correspond to any attested reading of 邊/边/辺 — the real, Wiktionary-confirmed Cantonese for this word is `bin1 goeng1`, used here for 辺疆's own frontmatter; `辺.md`'s stored value looks like a plain data error, worth a dedicated look outside the scope of this word-creation task.
