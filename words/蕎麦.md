## Notes
- [蕎](../characters/蕎.md) "buckwheat" + [麦](../characters/麦.md) "wheat"

蕎麦 is buckwheat: the plant and grain of *Fagopyrum*, and in Japanese the noodles made from it. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin qiáomài and Japanese そば as "buckwheat", and Japanese also as "soba, a variety of Japanese noodle made from buckwheat". Korean 교맥, Vietnamese kiều mạch and Cantonese kiu4 mak6 match. The Japanese reading is a native one.

It is a grain, not a true wheat, like the [大麦](大麦.md), barley, and the noodle dishes are listed beside [麺](麺.md) and [烏龍麺](烏龍麺.md).
