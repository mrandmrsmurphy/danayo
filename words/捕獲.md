## Notes
- [捕 (char)](characters/捕%20(char).md) "catch; grab" + [獲](characters/獲.md) "seize; obtain" — a synonym-doubling meaning "to capture, catch."

Mandarin bǔhuò and Cantonese bou6 wok6 are standard, everyday verbs for "to capture" (捕獲率, "capture rate," used of hunting/fishing yields and predator-prey studies). Japanese ほかく (hokaku) and Korean 포획 (pohwaek) follow the same pattern productively — 捕獲量, hokakuryō/pohoekryang, "catch volume."

Vietnamese bộ hoạch is the standard Hán Việt reading for this specific compound — attested directly in the Hán Nôm dictionary's compound listing for 捕, and in real use in formal/legal and wildlife-protection registers (săn bắt, bộ hoạch động vật hoang dã, "hunting/capturing wildlife"). Note that 捕 itself has two attested Hán Việt readings, bổ and bộ (compare 逮捕/tái bộ, "to arrest," which likewise uses bộ) — bộ is the one conventionally paired with 獲ʼs hoạch (thu hoạch 收獲, "to harvest, gain") in this exact compound. Everyday spoken Vietnamese normally uses the native bắt or bắt giữ instead, reserving bộ hoạch for the bookish/technical register. The character pages' other stored candidates — bõ, bố, bủa (and its variant spelling buả) for 捕; oách for 獲 — are legitimate Nôm readings for unrelated native words (bủa vây, "to encircle, cast a net," among them), not Hán Việt readings; a further candidate, huếch, appears to be a mix-up with the unrelated character 擴 (khoách/huếch, "to expand") rather than a reading of 獲 at all, and was dropped rather than kept as ambiguous.
