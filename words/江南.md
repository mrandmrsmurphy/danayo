## Notes
- [江](../characters/江.md) "river, cascade" + [南](../characters/南.md) "south" — no stand-in relationship applies (江's own stand-in is [[大江]]; 南's own is [[南方]]).

江南 refers to the Jiangnan region south of the Yangtze in China, and by the same compositional formula, to the Gangnam district of Seoul.

Mandarin Jiāngnán, Cantonese gong1 naam4, Japanese こうなん, Korean 강남 (genuinely the real Korean district name Gangnam), and Vietnamese Giang Nam are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 江's and 南's own stored citations exactly). `kwin: true` because 諺文 강남 matches Korean 강남 exactly. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄫㄋㄚㄇ unique across both words and characters).
