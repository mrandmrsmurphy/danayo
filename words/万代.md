## Notes
- [万](../characters/万.md) "ten thousand" + [代](../characters/代.md) "generation; era"

万代 (traditional 萬代) is "ten thousand generations," and so "forever; for all time." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 萬代 (wàndài, Cantonese maan6 doi6) is literary, "ten thousand generations; forever," with an unrelated intensive sense in Southern Min dialects. Wiktionary has no Japanese, Korean or Vietnamese section, so `japanese` ばんだい, `korean` 만대 and `vietnamese` vạn đại are compositional readings, with the Japanese and Korean forms matching real words (from memory, not re-checked). It is used in the Dan'a'yo rendering of the 1919 Korean Declaration of Independence ([宣言書](../translation/宣言書.md)), in 子孫万代 "posterity for ten thousand generations." It is related to [万年](万年.md) "ten thousand years."
