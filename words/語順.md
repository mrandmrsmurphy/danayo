## Notes
- [語](../characters/語.md) "language" + [順](../characters/順.md) "order"

語順 is word order. English Wiktionary glosses Japanese 語順 (ごじゅん) as "the order in which words are laid out in a phrase; word order" and Chinese 語序 (Mandarin yǔxù, Cantonese jyu5 zeoi6) as the grammar term "word order". 語序 is an alias of this word. The Korean 어순 field is built from the readings of the two characters.
