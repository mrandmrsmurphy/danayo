## Notes
- [俳](../characters/俳.md) "haikai; comic" + [句 (char)](../characters/句%20(char).md) "verse"

俳句 is a haiku: a Japanese poem of three lines in 5, 7 and 5 sounds. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin páijù as "haiku" and Japanese はいく as "haiku, a poem that usually consists of 5-7-5 morae". Korean 배구 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese paai4 geoi3 matches.

It is a short form of the [詩歌](詩歌.md), poem, and it grew out of the opening verse of the [連歌](連歌.md), linked verse.
