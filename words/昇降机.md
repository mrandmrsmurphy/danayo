## Notes
- [昇](../characters/昇.md) "rise" + [降](../characters/降.md) "descend" + [机](../characters/机.md) "machine" — "rise-and-descend machine." 昇's own `stand_in` is [[上昇]], 降's is [[下降]] and 机's is [[机会]], so 昇降机 is an independent compound.

昇降机 is an elevator or lift. Mandarin shēngjiàngjī (properly written 升降機, of which 昇降機 is a variant), Japanese しょうこうき and Korean 승강기 all use the compound; in everyday Japanese the loanword エレベーター has largely replaced it, and Wiktionary lists the Japanese as a synonym of that. The same form can also name other lifting platforms, such as an aerial work platform, in Chinese.

Compare [[机器]] (machine).
