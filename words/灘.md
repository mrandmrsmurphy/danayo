## Notes
- [灘](characters/灘%20(char).md) "bank, shoal" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 灘 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

灘 means bank, shoal (a sandbar or shallow river ford).

Mandarin tān, Cantonese taan1, Korean 탄, and Vietnamese than are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 灘's own stored citations exactly.
