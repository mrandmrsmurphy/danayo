## Notes
- [南](../characters/南.md) "south" + [寧](../characters/寧.md) "peaceful, calm" — "southern peace". No stand-in relationship applies (南's is [[南方]]; 寧's is [[安寧]]).

南寧 (Nanning) is the capital of the Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region in southern China. Per Wiktionary the name was coined in 1324 when Yongzhou Circuit (邕州路) was renamed Nanning Circuit; 邕 remains a one-character short name for the city (from the Vietnamese Wikipedia, which gives it as Ung). 宁 and 甯 are aliases of 寧, so 南宁 is kept as the word's alias, along with the variant 南甯 that Wiktionary lists.

Japanese なんねい, Korean 남녕 and Vietnamese Nam Ninh are the Sino-Xenic readings listed on Wiktionary; the usual South Korean name today is 난닝 (listed on Wiktionary). Wiktionary treats the Chinese entry as a noun rather than a proper noun, which does not change the sense here. Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese entry, with the Japanese entry (なんねい) and the Korean and Vietnamese forms in its descendants. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄚㄇㄋㄝㄫ unique across both words and characters).
