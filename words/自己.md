## Notes
- [自](characters/自.md) "self" + [己](characters/己.md) "self" — stand-in for [[己]], which cannot appear independently.

### Definition
自己 denotes the reflexive self. It refers back to the grammatical subject of a clause and marks that the actor and the affected party are the same.

### Function
It operates primarily at the syntactic level. It answers the question: who performs or receives the action? It does not inherently express philosophical identity or intrinsic essence.

### Usage Principle
Use 自己 when indicating reflexive action or self-reference within discourse. Do not use it for abstract discussions of identity, ego, or intrinsic nature. Not [[自身]], nor [[自我]]

### Cross-linguistic notes
Mandarin zìjǐ and Cantonese zi6 gei2 preserve the sense directly. Japanese じこ (jiko) is standard vocabulary, though often in more formal/written registers alongside native 自分 (jibun), the everyday reflexive pronoun. Korean 자기 (jagi) parallels the Sino-xenic reading and is likewise everyday vocabulary, doubling as an informal term of address between intimates ("honey, dear").

Vietnamese bản thân (本身, a different but closely related Chinese compound, literally "root-body") is the standard modern term matching 自己's pronoun-like uses (bản thân tôi, "myself"; bảo vệ bản thân, "to protect oneself," a near-exact structural match to 保護自己). The direct Sino-Vietnamese compositional reading of 自己 itself, tự kỷ, is compositionally valid and survives in fixed compounds (vị kỷ 為己, "selfish"; khắc kỷ 克己, "self-restraint, stoic"), but as a free-standing word it has been almost entirely captured by an unrelated modern medical sense, "autism" (trẻ tự kỷ, "autistic child") — using it alone to mean "oneself" would now likely be misread. Native tự mình ("oneself, by oneself, on one's own") covers 自己's adverbial/emphatic uses (tự mình làm, "to do it oneself") rather than its pronoun uses. `bản thân` was kept as the field value since it is the best general-purpose match; the other two are documented here rather than conflated into one field.
