## Notes
- [観](../characters/観.md) "observe" + [覧](../characters/覧.md) "look at, perceive"

観覧 means to view: to look at something as a visitor or spectator, such as an exhibit, a performance or a sight. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 觀覽 (an alias; Mandarin guānlǎn, Cantonese gun1 laam5) as "to view; to look at, especially as a visitor, spectator, etc.", Japanese かんらん as "watching, viewing; to watch, to view", and Korean 관람 as "viewing, watching". The readings agree.

観覧 is the stand-in for [覧](../characters/覧.md), which is too bounded to stand alone. The one who views is a [[観客]], and what is shown at an [[展覧会]], exhibition, is viewed this way.
