## Notes
- [結 (char)](../characters/結%20(char).md) "tie; join" + [論](../characters/論.md) "theory"

結論 is a conclusion: the result reached at the end of an argument or an inquiry. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin jiélùn as "conclusion; verdict" and, in logic, "conclusion of a syllogism"; Japanese けつろん is "conclusion (a decision reached after careful thought)". Korean 결론 and Cantonese git3 leon6 match.

It follows from a [前提](前提.md), the premise, by [推論](推論.md), inference. It differs from [判決](判決.md), the conclusion of a court case.
