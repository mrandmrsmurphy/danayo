## Notes
- [建](../characters/建.md) "build" + [設](../characters/設.md) "establish, build" — both characters' own `stand_in` points to this word (建設), meaning both 建 and 設 are bound morphemes that never appear independently outside this compound.

Mandarin jiànshè, Cantonese gin3 cit3, Japanese けんせつ, and Korean 건설 are the standard readings. Vietnamese kiến thiết is a real Sino-Vietnamese word, more formal and large-scale in connotation than the everyday xây dựng.
