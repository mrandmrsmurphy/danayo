## Notes
- [主](../characters/主.md) "master" + [導](../characters/導.md) "to guide" — to lead, to take the guiding/directing role, close in sense to but distinct from [[主宰]]'s stronger "dominate, rule" register.

主導 sits within a small family of similar 導-compounds this vault has yet to create (指導 "to instruct/direct," 引導 "to guide," 領導 "to lead," per Vietnamese's own parallel set chỉ đạo/dẫn đạo/lãnh đạo) — 主導 specifically marks the "principal, central" leading role rather than mere guidance or instruction, as in vai trò chủ đạo ("the leading/central role," commonly used of a dominant economic or organizational sector). Vietnamese chủ đạo (confirmed via web search) matches this exact "central, decisive, leading" sense. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 주닷 diverges from Sino-Korean 주도 in the second syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄨㄉㄚㄨ unique among both words and characters).
