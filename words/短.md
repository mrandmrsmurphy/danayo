## Notes
- [短](../characters/短%20(char).md) "short" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 短 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

短 means short, both spatially (短距離, "short distance") and temporally (短期, "short-term"), and figuratively as a shortcoming or weak point (短所, "a flaw, a weak point," contrasted with 長所, "a strength"). It pairs constantly with its antonym 長 in idioms weighing pros and cons, most directly 一長一短 ("one strength, one weakness").

Mandarin duǎn, Cantonese dyun2, Japanese たん (tan), Korean 단 (dan), and Vietnamese đoản are all standard, fully compositional readings — Vietnamese everyday speech more often uses the native ngắn for "short," with đoản reserved for more literary or compound contexts.
