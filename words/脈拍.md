## Notes
- [脈](../characters/脈.md) "pulse; vessel" + [拍 (char)](../characters/拍%20(char).md) "beat"

脈拍 is the pulse: the regular beat of the heart felt in an artery. Wiktionary glosses Japanese みゃくはく as "pulse (regular beat caused by the heart)". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 맥박 is the reading of the characters.

It is felt at the wrist, [手首](手首.md), in an [動脈](動脈.md), and it is driven by the [心臓](心臓.md), heart.
