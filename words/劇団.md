## Notes
- [劇 (char)](../characters/劇%20(char).md) "drama" + [団](../characters/団.md) "group"

劇団 is a theatre company: a troupe that performs plays together. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin jùtuán as "theatrical company; troupe" and Japanese げきだん as "theatrical troupe". Korean 극단 is the same word. Cantonese kek6 tyun4 matches.

The members are [俳優](俳優.md), actors, and they perform in a [劇場](劇場.md). A musical group is a [楽団](楽団.md).
