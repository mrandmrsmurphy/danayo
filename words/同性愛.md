## Notes
- [同](../characters/同.md) "same" + [性](../characters/性.md) "sex, gender, nature" + [愛 (char)](../characters/愛%20(char).md) "love" — no stand-in relationship applies (同's own stand-in is [[同一]]; 性's own is 性別; 愛's own is itself; so this word legitimizes none of them).

同性愛 means homosexuality — literally "same-sex love."

This is a clean, directly parallel compound across the whole Sinosphere, with no real sense-divergence between languages. Mandarin tóngxìng'ài, Cantonese tung4 sing3 oi3, Japanese どうせいあい (dōseiai), and Korean 동성애 (dongseongae) are all the real, standard, everyday words for homosexuality in their languages. Vietnamese đồng tính ái is the full compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading; everyday spoken Vietnamese most often shortens this to bare đồng tính, dropping ái, though the fuller form remains correct and attested. The 注音 ㄉㄛㄫㄙㄧㄫㄚㄧ carries no syllable dot, since the fork test finds no alternate legal parse (the 羅馬字 apostrophe in dongsing'ai marks the null initial of 愛 only).  `kwin` is false per the AND-rule: 性 is individually `kwin: false` (its own Dan'a'yo reading 싱 diverges from Sino-Korean 성), regardless of 同's and 愛's own values.
