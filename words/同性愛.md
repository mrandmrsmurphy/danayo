## Notes
- [同](../characters/同.md) "same" + [性](../characters/性.md) "sex, gender, nature" + [愛 (char)](../characters/愛%20(char).md) "love" — no stand-in relationship applies (同's own stand-in is [[同一]]; 愛's own is itself).

同性愛 means homosexuality — literally "same-sex love."

This is a clean, directly parallel compound across the whole Sinosphere, with no real sense-divergence between languages. Mandarin tóngxìng'ài, Cantonese tung4 sing3 oi3, Japanese どうせいあい (dōseiai), and Korean 동성애 (dongseongae) are all the real, standard, everyday words for homosexuality in their languages. Vietnamese đồng tính ái is the full compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading; everyday spoken Vietnamese most often shortens this to bare đồng tính, dropping ái, though the fuller form remains correct and attested. `kwin` is false per the AND-rule: 性 is individually `kwin: false` (its own Dan'a'yo reading 싱 diverges from Sino-Korean 성), regardless of 同's and 愛's own values.
