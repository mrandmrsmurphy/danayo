## Notes
- [同](../characters/同.md) "same" + [性](../characters/性.md) "sex, gender, nature" + [愛 (char)](../characters/愛%20(char).md) "love" — no stand-in relationship applies (同's own stand-in is [[同一]]; 性's own is 性別; 愛's own is itself; so this word legitimizes none of them).

Wiktionary: Chinese 同性愛, dated, "homosexuality" (同性戀 is the current form, "homosexuality; homosexual"); Japanese 同性愛 (どうせいあい) "homosexuality"; Korean 동성애 is the hanja form of "homosexuality". Literally "same-sex love".
