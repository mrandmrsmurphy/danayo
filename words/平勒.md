## Notes
- [平](../characters/平.md) "level; flat" + [勒](../characters/勒.md) "to rein in; horizontal stroke"

Wiktionary has no entry for 平勒; it is a compound of 平 "level; flat" and 勒, which Wiktionary glosses in calligraphy (Japanese) as "the horizontal stroke; the second of the Eight Principles of Yong". It names the level horizontal stroke, in contrast to the slanting horizontal 斜勒, and appears in the stroke discussion on the [[Stroke]] page. Its readings are compositional.
