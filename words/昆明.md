## Notes
- [昆](../characters/昆%20(char).md) "swarm, descendants" + [明](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright" — "bright Kun". No stand-in relationship applies (both characters are their own stand-ins).

昆明 (Kunming) is the capital of Yunnan province in southwestern China. Wiktionary notes that the name was originally the endonym of an ethnic group: the Kunming Yi, an ancient nomadic tribe of mountainous southwestern China from at least the Han dynasty, considered ancestors of the Yi people, attested in the Shiji (from the Wiktionary quotation). As a place name it is first attested in the 13th century. That tribal sense is the ordinary, non-place sense, so it is listed first below.

Japanese こんめい, Korean 곤명 and Vietnamese Côn Minh are the Sino-Xenic readings listed on Wiktionary; the usual South Korean name today is 쿤밍 (listed on Wiktionary). Compounds such as 昆明湖 (Kunming Lake) and 昆明池 are not claimed here. Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese entries. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄋㄇ⼶ㄫ unique across both words and characters).

1. (historical) the Kunming Yi, an ancient nomadic tribe of southwestern China.
2. Kunming, the city.
