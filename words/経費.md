## Notes
- [経 (char)](../characters/経%20(char).md) "classic, channel, warp" + [費 (char)](../characters/費%20(char).md) "expense" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (経's is [[経]] itself, 費's is [[費]] itself), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

経費 names expenditures or expenses — the costs of running an operation, business, or project.

Mandarin jīngfèi, Cantonese ging1 fai3, Japanese けいひ (keihi), and Korean 경비 (gyeongbi) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 経費 is itself a common attested word for "expenses" in all four languages. Vietnamese kinh phí is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
