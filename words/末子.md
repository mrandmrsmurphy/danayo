## Notes
- [末](../characters/末.md) "end, last" + [子](../characters/子.md) "child" — youngest child.

末子 is the youngest child of a family, of either sex. It is "last-child." It is confirmed in Japanese only: すえこ (also ばっし, まっし), with 末っ子 (suekko) far more common in speech. Mandarin 末子 (mòzi) means "end; powder; dust" and is a different word, so `mandarin` and `cantonese` are left blank; Korean and Vietnamese are not confirmed. The classical male counterpart is [[季子]], and the eldest is [[長子]].
