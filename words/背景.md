## Notes
- [背 (char)](../characters/背%20(char).md) "back" — legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry by [[背]] itself, not this word. [景](../characters/景.md) "scenery, scene" is separately legitimized by [[景色]].

背景 combines 背 ("back") with 景 ("scenery") — "background." No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

Mandarin bèijǐng, cantonese bui3ging2 (space removed), korean 배경 (baegyeong), japanese はいけい (haikei), and vietnamese bối cảnh were all already correct, fully compositional readings.
