## Notes
- [仍 (char)](../characters/仍%20(char).md) "yet, still" — 仍 is a Classical Chinese adverb meaning "yet, still, as before" (as in the fuller, more explicit [[仍旧]], "still, as before, unchanged"). `vietnamese: nhưng` is the real Sino-Vietnamese reading of 仍, distinct from (though homographic with) the common native Vietnamese conjunction "nhưng" ("but"). Legitimizing note: 仍 (char)'s own `stand_in` is this exact word (itself), so 仍 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by itself.
