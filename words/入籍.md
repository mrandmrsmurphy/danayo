## Notes
To enter someone in the registry. `vietnamese: nhập tịch` is directly attested (confirmed via web search) and extremely common everyday Vietnamese specifically for naturalization ("to acquire citizenship/nationality"), a more specific modern narrowing of this word's own broader "enter into a registry" sense.
