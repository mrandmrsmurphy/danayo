## Notes
Both `korean: 입적` (compositional) and `vietnamese: nhập tịch` (directly attested, confirmed via web search) fill previously blank fields. `nhập tịch` is extremely common everyday Vietnamese specifically for naturalization ("to acquire citizenship/nationality"), a more specific modern narrowing of this word's own broader "enter into a registry" sense. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 닙적 diverges from the compositional Sino-Korean 입적 in the first syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄧㄆㄐㄝㄎ unique among both words and characters).
