## Notes
- [左](../characters/左.md) "left" + [右](../characters/右.md) "right" — no stand-in relationship applies (左's own `stand_in` is [[左側]], 右's is [[右側]]).

左右 names both sides — left and right together — and by extension "approximately" (as a suffix on quantities, e.g. 十歳左右 "around ten years old") and, classically, "attendants, close subordinates" (courtiers positioned at a ruler's left and right).

Japanese さゆう, Korean 좌우, and Vietnamese tả hữu (verified, sharing all three extended senses) are standard, non-drifted readings. HSK level 2 already correct. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚ⼜ unique across both words and characters).

**Note on the Dan'a'yo spelling**: this word's own `諺文`, 자유, happens to be byte-identical to the unrelated, extremely common Korean word 자유 ("freedom," 自由) — a coincidental collision at the hangul-spelling level, unconnected to `kwin` (which is independently `false` here, since `諺文` 자유 doesn't match the `korean` field 좌우 either).
