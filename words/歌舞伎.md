## Notes
- [歌](../characters/歌.md) "song" + [舞](../characters/舞.md) "dance" + [伎](../characters/伎.md) "performer"

歌舞伎 is kabuki: the traditional Japanese theatre of song, dance and stylised acting. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin gēwǔjì as "kabuki (a form of Japanese theatre)" and Japanese かぶき as "a bizarrely-dressed person". Korean 가무기 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese go1 mou5 gei6 matches.

It is a younger theatre than the [猿楽](猿楽.md), sarugaku, ancestor of Noh, and its music uses the [三味線](三味線.md), shamisen. The Chinese equivalent is the [京劇](京劇.md).
