## Notes
- [原](../characters/原.md) "origin, source" + [罪](../characters/罪%20(char).md) "sin, guilt" — no stand-in relationship applies (原's own stand-in is [[原始]]; 罪 is its own stand-in).
- The Christian theological concept of the inherited sinful nature of humanity, tracing to the Fall. Entered East Asian languages through 17th-century missionary texts.

- See [基督敎](../lexipedia/基督敎.md).
