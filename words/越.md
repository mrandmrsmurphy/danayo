## Notes
- [越](../characters/越%20(char).md) "exceed" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 越 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

越 means to exceed.

Mandarin yuè, Cantonese jyut6, Japanese えつ, Korean 월, and Vietnamese việt are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 越's own stored citation exactly). Added the missing `pos`/`japanese`/`vietnamese`/`hsk_level` fields. `kwin: false` was already correct (諺文 웓 ends in a written ㄷ coda while Sino-Korean 월 ends in ㄹ, the regular MC -t → Korean -l shift — a real, documented divergence, per [[月]]'s Notes). Reformatted the Homophones callout to the canonical multi-line format. Homophones with [[月]] and [[曰]] (all 注音 ⼔ㄊ) were already correctly cross-referenced.

**`kwin` update (2026-10-09):** `kwin` is now true. The Notes above may describe the ㄷ/ㄹ difference between the Dan'a'yo 諺文 and the Korean reading as a mismatch; under the vault's standing rule a Korean final ㄹ counts as equal to Dan'a'yo's final ㄷ for this comparison, so such a difference alone does not make `kwin` false.
