## Notes
- [地](../characters/地%20(char).md) "ground" + [点](../characters/点%20(char).md) "point" — no stand-in relationship applies (地 is its own stand-in; 点 is its own stand-in).

地点 means a point, spot, or location.

Japanese ちてん, Korean 지점, and Vietnamese địa điểm are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄧㄜㄉㄝㄇ unique across both words and characters).
