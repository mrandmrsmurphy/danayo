>[!warning] Homophones
> [[海上]] "marine, maritime, on the sea"

## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [象](../characters/象.md) "elephant" — no stand-in relationship applies (海's own stand-in is [[海洋]]; 象's own is [[大象]]).

海象 means walrus (literally "sea elephant").

Mandarin hǎixiàng, Cantonese hoi2 zoeng6, and Korean 해상 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 海's and 象's own stored citations exactly. Vietnamese hải tượng (海's own hải + 象's own tượng) is also compositional. Japanese セイウチ is the real, standard Japanese word for "walrus" (a loanword from Ainu), the same real-world-species-name convention already established on [[龍蝦]]/[[水牛]]/[[河馬]].

**Real homophone found**: 上 and 象 happen to share the identical Dan'a'yo reading (syang/샹/ㄙ⼘ㄫ, despite distinct Middle Chinese initials that both collapse to the same Dan'a'yo syllable) — making 海象 and [[海上]] ("marine, maritime") genuine two-way homophones. Added reciprocal callouts to both pages. A fresh grep confirms no third word shares this reading.
