## Notes
- [失](../characters/失.md) "lose" + [敗](../characters/敗.md) "be defeated" — "to lose and be defeated".

失敗 is "to fail; to be defeated" and "failure; defeat". English Wiktionary gives the Chinese word as an intransitive verb with two senses, "to be defeated" and "to fail; to come to nothing; to fall through", as a noun ("failure; defeat", as in 以失敗告終 "to end in failure") and as an adjective ("failed; unsuccessful"). Its antonym for "to fail" is [[成功]] (success), and its antonym for "to be defeated" is 勝利; the synonyms for the two senses are 落敗 and 未成功 (not words in the vault). A derived term is 失敗國家 ("failed state"). Japanese 失敗 (しっぱい, shippai) is a noun ("failure; mistake; fault") and a suru-verb ("to fail; to make a mistake"), with 錯誤 as a synonym for "mistake" and 成功 as the antonym; Korean 실패 and Vietnamese thất bại are listed as descendants of the Chinese word.

Mandarin shībài and Cantonese sat1 baai6 are as at English Wiktionary. In Dan'a'yo the word keeps the shared sense and is a verb (事詞), listed on the Efforts page.

No other word shares the 注音 ㄙㄧㄊㄅㄚㄧ (checked 2026-10-12).
