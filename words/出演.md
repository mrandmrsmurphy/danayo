## Etymology
- [出 (char)](../characters/出%20(char).md) "to come out, appear" + [演 (char)](../characters/演%20(char).md) "to perform"

## Notes
`vietnamese: xuất diễn` is compositional, matching this word's own character order (出 first). Note that the everyday Vietnamese word diễn xuất reverses the order — it corresponds to the related-but-distinct compound 演出 (yǎnchū, "a performance, show," 演 first), not to 出演 itself; the two Sino-Vietnamese phrases mirror the two different underlying Chinese compounds rather than being free variants of one another. Filled the blank `cantonese: ceot1 jin2`, compositional. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 춛연 matches Sino-Korean 출연 exactly.
