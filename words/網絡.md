## Notes
- [網](../characters/網%20(char).md) "net" + [絡](../characters/絡%20(char).md) "web, connect" — "net and web," a network. Their own `stand_in` values: 網's is itself; 絡's is itself; none points here, so 網絡 is an independent compound.

網絡 is a network, and in Mainland China, Hong Kong and Singapore the word for the internet and the web. Mandarin wǎngluò and Cantonese mong5 lok3 are the ordinary Chinese words. Japanese says ネットワーク and Korean 네트워크, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[互連網]] (internet), [[網站]] (website) and [[網]] (net).
