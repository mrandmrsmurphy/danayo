## Notes
- [陰 (char)](../characters/陰%20(char).md) "Yin, shade" + [極 (char)](../characters/極%20(char).md) "extreme, pole"

陰極 means the cathode — the negative electrode in an electrochemical or electronic device, paired against 陽極 ("anode," the positive electrode). Mandarin 陰極/阴极 (yīnjí), Cantonese 陰極 (jam1gik6), Japanese 陰極 (いんきょく), Korean 음극 (eumgeuk), and Sino-Vietnamese âm cực are all the standard scientific/technical term for this concept across the CJKV sphere, straightforwardly compositional and consistent in every language.
