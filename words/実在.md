## Notes
- [実](../characters/実.md) "real; solid" + [在 (char)](../characters/在%20(char).md) "exist"

実在 is real existence: to exist in fact, not only in thought. Wiktionary glosses Japanese じつざい as "existence; reality" and "to exist in real life". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 실재 matches.

It is narrower than [存在](存在.md), which says only that a thing is. 実在 says that it is really so, and it is the opposite of [架空](架空.md), imaginary, and [虚構](虚構.md), invented.
