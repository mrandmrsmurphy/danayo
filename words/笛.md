## Notes
- [笛 (char)](../characters/笛%20(char).md) "flute" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 笛 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

笛 is a flute: a pipe played by blowing across or into it. The word names the instrument in general, whatever its build: bamboo, wood or metal, held sideways or blown from the end. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin dí as "flute; whistle" and Japanese as a woodwind instrument, and Vietnamese địch and Korean 적 write the same character. Cantonese dek6 matches.

The compound [口笛](口笛.md) is whistling. The specific kinds are the [横笛](横笛.md), transverse flute, the [洞簫](洞簫.md), xiao, and the [尺八](尺八.md), shakuhachi. The player [吹奏](吹奏.md)s it, and a mouth organ is the [鳳笙](鳳笙.md).
