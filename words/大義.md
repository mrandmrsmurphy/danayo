## Notes
- [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "great" + [義](../characters/義.md) "righteousness, justice" — "the great cause, principles of justice" (a moral imperative overriding personal interest, e.g. loyalty to nation or duty). `vietnamese: đại nghĩa` is a directly attested, well-known standard Vietnamese term with the same sense (đại nghĩa dân tộc, "the great national cause").

**Fixed a real bug found on re-check**: `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` had used 義's `'wi`/위/ㄨㄧ misreading rather than its own correct `'ǝi`/읫/ㄜㄧ (confirmed against `syllables/ㄜㄧ.md`) — the same recurring confusion already flagged and fixed on [[官僚主義]], [[定義]], [[定義域]], [[正義]], and [[主義]]; this instance also affected `注音` itself (ㄨㄧ→ㄜㄧ), unlike the others where only 羅馬字/諺文 were wrong. Corrected to dai'ǝi/대읫/ㄉㄚㄧ·ㄜㄧ; also fixed the same wrong rt-annotation on `characters/義.md` and `characters/大 (char).md`'s own Words-list citations. `kwin` remains false: Dan'a'yo 대읫 still diverges from Sino-Korean 대의 in the second syllable, even after the correction. No homophones (`grep -xF` on the corrected 注音/羅馬字/諺文 all unique among both words and characters).
