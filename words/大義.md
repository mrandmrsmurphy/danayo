## Notes
- [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "great" + [義](../characters/義.md) "righteousness, justice" — "the great cause, principles of justice" (a moral imperative overriding personal interest, e.g. loyalty to nation or duty). `vietnamese: đại nghĩa` is a directly attested, well-known standard Vietnamese term with the same sense (đại nghĩa dân tộc, "the great national cause"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 대위 diverges from Sino-Korean 대의 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄧ·ㄨㄧ unique among both words and characters).
