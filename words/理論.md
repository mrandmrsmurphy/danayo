## Notes
- [理](../characters/理.md) "-ology, reason, logic" + [論](../characters/論.md) "theory, thesis" — no stand-in relationship applies (理's own stand-in is itself; 論's own is itself).

理論 means theory, thesis.

Mandarin lǐlùn, Cantonese lei5 leon6, Japanese りろん, Korean 리론 and Vietnamese lí luận are all fully compositional. Korean 리론 keeps the word-initial ㄹ of the North Korean convention, as in [[理想]], [[理由]] and [[理解]].
