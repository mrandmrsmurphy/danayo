## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [上 (char)](../characters/上%20(char).md) "up, above" — no stand-in relationship applies (海's own stand-in is [[海洋]]; 上's own is itself).

海上 means marine, maritime, on the sea.

Mandarin hǎishàng, Cantonese hoi2 soeng6, Korean 해상, and Japanese かいじょう are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 海's and 上's own stored citations exactly. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with hải thượng (海's own hải + 上's own thượng) — also the real, standard Vietnamese term for "maritime." Removed the redundant `品詞` field.

**Real homophone found**: 上 and 象 happen to share the identical Dan'a'yo reading (syang/샹/ㄙ⼘ㄫ, despite distinct Middle Chinese initials, 澄/禪 vs 邪, that both collapse to the same Dan'a'yo syllable) — making 海上 and [[海象]] ("walrus") genuine two-way homophones. Added reciprocal callouts to both pages. A fresh grep confirms no third word shares this reading.
