## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [上 (char)](../characters/上%20(char).md) "up, above" — no stand-in relationship applies (海's own stand-in is [[海洋]]; 上's own is itself).

海上 means marine, maritime, on the sea.

Mandarin hǎishàng, Cantonese hoi2 soeng6, Korean 해상, and Japanese かいじょう are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 海's and 上's own stored citations exactly.
