## Notes
Mandarin jiàngyóu, cantonese zoeng3 jau4, and japanese しょうゆ are all directly attested standard readings. **Found and fixed a real-equivalent-instead-of-own-reading bug on `korean`**: a garbled multi-value string "장유, 간장" mashed the honest compositional reading (장유) together with 간장, the everyday native Korean word for "soy sauce" (a different formation) — trimmed to just 장유. **Found and fixed the same bug on `vietnamese`**: "xì dầu, nước tương" listed two real everyday Vietnamese terms (a Cantonese loanword and a native descriptive phrase), neither compositional — corrected to the honest compositional "tương du" (醤's own tương + 油's own du). **Found and removed three bad-aliases-entries**: `荳油`, `豉油`, `豆油` are all genuinely different compounds (different first characters — 荳/豉/豆, not 醤), not spelling variants; kept only `醬油`, the legitimate traditional-character variant. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 장유 matches Sino-Korean 장유 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄫ⼜ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 醤's `stand_in` (果醤) nor 油's own `stand_in` (油 itself) points to this compound.
