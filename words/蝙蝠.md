## Notes
- **Genuine `#cranberry`**: both [蝙](../characters/蝙.md) and [蝠](../characters/蝠.md) — each carries the "bat" sense in its own right, even though 蝙 is never used solo in running text — have their own `stand_in` pointing to this exact compound (full transitivity; tag already present on both character pages, added here to the word page too).

Mandarin's dual reading (biānfú/biǎnfú) converted from comma-joined string to a proper list. Japanese こうもり is the real, standard native word for "bat," matching both constituents' own native readings. Korean 편복 is the standard Sino-Korean compositional reading; the native word 박쥐 (bakjwi, comma-joined in the original field) is the everyday native term, mentioned here in prose rather than the field. Vietnamese biên bức is compositional.
