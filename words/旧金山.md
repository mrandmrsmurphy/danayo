## Notes
- [旧](../characters/旧%20(char).md) "paleo-, former, old" + [金](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal, gold" + [山](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain" — no stand-in relationship applies (all three characters' own stand-ins are themselves).

旧金山 is the Chinese name for San Francisco ("Old Gold Mountain," from the Gold Rush era, distinguishing it from 新金山/"New Gold Mountain" Melbourne).

Mandarin Jiùjīnshān, Cantonese gau6 gam1 saan1, and Vietnamese Cựu Kim Sơn are the Sinitic calque used compositionally here. Japanese サンフランシスコ and Korean 샌프란시스코 are direct phonetic transliterations of the English name, the standard, expected forms in those languages. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼜ㄛㄍㄧㄇㄙㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
