## Notes
- [経](../characters/経%20(char).md) "classic, channel, warp" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 経 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

経 originally named the warp thread on a loom, extended figuratively to a "classic" text (経典, 五経), a bodily "channel" (経絡, 神経), and "to regulate, manage" (経営, 経済).

Mandarin jīng, Cantonese ging1, and Korean 경 (gyeong) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the on-reading ケイ (KEI) for the bare character alone, since no native kun-reading is stored. Vietnamese kinh is the standard reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
