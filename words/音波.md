## Notes
- [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" + [波](../characters/波.md) "wave" — no `#cranberry`: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here (音's is [[音楽]], 波's is [[波浪]]).

音波 (yīnbō) is the standard Mandarin word for "sound wave," and Cantonese 音波 (jam1bo1) likewise. Japanese おんぱ (onpa) and Korean 음파 (eumpa) are both standard terms in their languages, as is Sino-Vietnamese âm ba — a clean case with full agreement across all five languages.
