## Notes
- [周](../characters/周.md) "circumference" + [章](../characters/章%20(char).md) "chapter" = "the part of the story where you walk around in circles because you're so worried" — no stand-in relationship applies (周's own stand-in is [[圓周]]; 章 is its own stand-in).

周章 means troubled or flustered — thrown into confusion or consternation.

Japanese しゅうしょう, Korean 주장 (verified — a genuine, if less common, Sino-Korean compound distinct from the unrelated everyday word 주장 "claim, assertion"), and Vietnamese chu chương (verified) are all standard readings. **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 周章 and [[周長]] ("perimeter, circumference") happen to share an identical reading because 章 and 長 coincidentally share the same Dan'a'yo phonology despite being unrelated characters — standardized the reciprocal callout on both pages.
