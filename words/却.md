## Notes
- [却 (char)](../characters/却%20(char).md) "but; still; rather; all the more"

却 (alternate form 卻) combines phonetic [[去]] with 卩. Its core classical sense is "to withdraw, retreat, decline, reject" (却下, "to reject, dismiss [a request]"; 卻步, "to step back, recoil"), which grammaticalized into a contrastive adverb marking an unexpected or reversed turn — "but, yet, still, rather" (却不, "but not..."; 却是, "but actually is...").

Mandarin què, Cantonese koek3, and Japanese きゃく (kyaku, on'yomi, largely confined to formal/literary contexts) alongside native かえ(って) (kae(tte), "on the contrary, rather," the everyday adverb echoing the same reversed-expectation sense) all preserve the contrastive use. Korean 각 (gak) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 물리치다 (mullichida, "to reject, repel," reflecting the character's "withdraw/reject" core sense). Vietnamese khước is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in khước từ ("to decline, refuse").
