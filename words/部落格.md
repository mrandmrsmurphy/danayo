## Notes
- [部](../characters/部%20(char).md) "tribe, village" + [落](../characters/落.md) "grid, pattern" — "blog"; a Taiwan transliteration of "blog" (部落 "tribe, community"). Their own `stand_in` values: 部's is itself; 落's is [[落下]]; none points here, so 部落格 is an independent compound.

部落格 is a blog. It is the Taiwan term, a sound-and-sense rendering of "blog" in which 部落 means "tribe, community". Mandarin bùluògé and Cantonese bou6 lok6 gaak3 use it (Mainland China prefers 博客 and Hong Kong 網誌). Japanese says ブログ and Korean 블로그, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[網站]] (website).
