## Notes
- [紛](../characters/紛%20(char).md) "numerous, disorderly" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 紛 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo. Its own 形声 semantic component 糸 ("silk") reflects the original image of a tangle of threads.

紛 means numerous, disorderly, or chaotic, as in 紛争 (dispute, conflict).

Mandarin fēn, Cantonese fan1, and Korean 분 (bun) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the on-reading フン (FUN) for the bare character alone, since no native kun-reading is stored. Vietnamese phân is one of two stored candidates. No Dan'a'yo homophones — a stale reciprocal callout claiming a shared reading with [[盆]] ("basin") had been added to both pages in error (盆's real reading pun/푼/ㄆㄨㄋ had been mistakenly copied into this word's own pronunciation fields; corrected here to 紛's real reading fǝn/쁜/ㄈㄜㄋ), removed on both sides.
