## Notes
- [孫](../characters/孫.md) "grandchild" + [悟](../characters/悟.md) "realize" + [空](../characters/空%20(char).md) "empty" — no stand-in relationship applies (孫's own stand-in is [[孫子]]; 悟's own is [[覚悟]]; 空's own is itself).

孫悟空 is the Monkey King — the protagonist of the classical Chinese novel Journey to the West (西遊記), a monkey born from stone who gains supernatural powers.

Mandarin Sūn Wùkōng, Cantonese syun1 ng6 hung1, Japanese そんごくう, Korean 손오공, and Vietnamese Tôn Ngộ Không are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄛㄋ·ㄛㄎㄛㄫ unique across both words and characters).
