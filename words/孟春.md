## Notes
- [孟](../characters/孟.md) "eldest, first" + [春](../characters/春%20(char).md) "spring" — no stand-in relationship applies (孟 is a proper-noun-only character with no specific stand-in word; 春's own stand-in is itself).

First month of spring in the ancient seasonal calendar. 孟 denotes the eldest or first of three — the beginning of a season's arc. Corresponds to [[寅月]] and roughly to the modern first lunar month.

Mandarin mèngchūn, Cantonese maang6 ceon1, Korean 맹춘, Japanese もうしゅん, and Vietnamese Mạnh Xuân are all standard, fully compositional readings.
