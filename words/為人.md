## Notes
- [為](../characters/為%20(char).md) "to act as" + [人](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" — this compound uses 為 in its "to act as, to become" sense (Mandarin wéi, as opposed to wèi "for"). Dan'a'yo stores a single reading for 為 (wen/원/⼔ㄋ), used for all of its senses, so the word's 諺文/羅馬字/注音 (원닌/wennin/⼔ㄋㄋㄧㄋ) are the plain concatenation of 為's and 人's own citations.

為人 means to behave, conduct oneself (one's character, as in 為人處世, "how one conducts oneself in life").

Mandarin wéirén and Cantonese wai4 jan4 preserve the sense directly. Japanese ひととなり is the real, standard native Japanese reading for this exact compound (rather than a Sino on'yomi form). Vietnamese vi nhân (為's own vi + 人's own nhân) is also the real, standard Vietnamese term, as in the idiom vi nhân xử thế ("how to conduct oneself in life"). `korean` left blank: no established Korean loanword or compositional form for this specific sense was confirmed — a naive compositional 위인 would collide with the unrelated real word 偉人 ("great person, hero"). No homophones (注音 ⼔ㄋㄋㄧㄋ unique across both words and characters).
