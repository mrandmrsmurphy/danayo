## Notes
- [為](../characters/為%20(char).md) "to act as" + [人](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" — this compound uses 為 in its polyphonic "to act as, to become" sense (Mandarin wéi), distinct from the "namely, for" sense (wèi) stored as 為's own primary Dan'a'yo citation ('wen/원/⼔ㄋ) — a character-level citation gap (為 lacks a second stored reading for this sense) flagged here for future review, not fixed. The 諺文/羅馬字/注音 given (워닌/'wenin/ㄨㄧㄋㄧㄋ) reflect this distinct polyphonic reading rather than 為's own documented citation.

為人 means to behave, conduct oneself (one's character, as in 為人處世, "how one conducts oneself in life").

Mandarin wéirén and Cantonese wai4 jan4 preserve the sense directly. Japanese ひととなり is the real, standard native Japanese reading for this exact compound (rather than a Sino on'yomi form). Vietnamese vi nhân (為's own vi + 人's own nhân) is also the real, standard Vietnamese term, as in the idiom vi nhân xử thế ("how to conduct oneself in life"). `korean` left blank: no established Korean loanword or compositional form for this specific sense was confirmed — a naive compositional 위인 would collide with the unrelated real word 偉人 ("great person, hero"). No homophones (注音 ㄨㄧㄋㄧㄋ unique across both words and characters).
