## Notes
- [世](../characters/世.md) "world" + [紀](../characters/紀.md) "record, era" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 紀 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (世's own stand-in is [[世界]]).

世紀 means century.

Mandarin shìjì, Cantonese sai3 gei2, Japanese せいき, Korean 세기, and Vietnamese thế kỷ are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 世's and 紀's own stored citations exactly). Removed blank `swadesh`. Discovered mid-perfection of the dependent compound [[毎世紀]] to have never actually been stamped despite otherwise-complete content — retroactively completed and stamped here. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄍㄧ unique across both words and characters).
