## Notes
- [隔 (char)](../characters/隔%20(char).md) "every other" + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month, moon"

隔月 means every other month, occurring once every two months — the last of the four sibling terms in this "every other" set alongside [[隔世紀]], [[隔年]], and [[隔日]], all built on the same 隔- prefix. Mandarin 隔月 (géyuè), Cantonese 隔月 (gaak3jyut6), Japanese 隔月 (かくげつ), Korean 격월 (gyeogwol), and Sino-Vietnamese cách nguyệt all preserve this meaning compositionally across the CJKV sphere (隔月刊/격월간, "bimonthly publication").
