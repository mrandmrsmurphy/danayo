## Notes
- [急 (char)](../characters/急%20(char).md) "urgent, swift" + [流](../characters/流.md) "flow" — rapids.

急流 is rapids or a torrent: fast, turbulent water, either a stretch of a river or a fast current in general. It is "swift flow." Mandarin jíliú, Cantonese gap1 lau4 and Japanese きゅうりゅう are the readings on Wiktionary; Korean 급류 is the same compound. It contrasts with the calm [[流水]] and the small [[渓流]] (mountain stream), and its figurative sense, "a swift current of events," is shared across the languages.
