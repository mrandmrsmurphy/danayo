## Notes
- [空 (char)](../characters/空%20(char).md) "empty" + [手 (char)](../characters/手%20(char).md) "hand" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both 空's and 手's own are themselves), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

空手 literally means "empty hand(s)" — being unarmed or carrying nothing — and is the source term for [[空手道]] ("karate," literally "empty-hand way"), the Okinawan martial art built on unarmed striking and self-defense.

Mandarin kōngshǒu, Cantonese hung1 sau2, Japanese くうしゅ (kūshu), and Korean 공수 (gongsu, as in 공수도, the Korean name for karate) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese không thủ is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form.
