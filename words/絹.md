## Notes
- [絹](../characters/絹%20(char).md) "silk" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 絹 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

絹 means silk — the fine, lustrous fabric woven from filament produced by silkworms. See also [[糸線]] for the "silk thread" sense.

Mandarin juàn, Cantonese gyun3, and Korean 견 (gyeon) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading きぬ (kinu) rather than the on-reading ケン (KEN) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese quyến is one of two stored candidates. A genuine 3-way Dan'a'yo homophone group with [[圏]] ("encircled area") and [[巻]] ("roll, roll up") — re-verified as the complete set with the corrected homophone-check tooling (see memory); 倦/拳/権 share the same syllable at the character level only.
