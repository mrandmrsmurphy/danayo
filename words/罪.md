## Notes
- [罪](../characters/罪%20(char).md) "sin, crime" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 罪 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

罪 means a sin or crime — a wrongdoing, as in 原罪 (original sin) and 犯罪 (crime).

Mandarin zuì, Cantonese zeoi6, and Korean 죄 (joe) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading つみ (tsumi) rather than the on-readings ザイ/サイ (ZAI/SAI) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese tội is the standard, common reading, one of two stored candidates. No Dan'a'yo homophones (no other character shares this syllable).
