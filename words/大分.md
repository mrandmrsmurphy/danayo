## Notes
- [大](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big" + [分](../characters/分%20(char).md) "divide"

大分 is Ōita, written "large division". The reading おおいた is irregular, since 分 is normally read ぶん, ふん or わ(ける) and not いた; the name is an old one whose spelling was fixed later (from memory, not re-checked). In Mandarin dàfēn is the compositional reading, while dàfèn "considerably" is a separate common word and not this one.

Ōita has more hot spring sources than any other prefecture, with Beppu and Yufuin the best known. The Usa Hachiman shrine, the head shrine of all Hachiman shrines, and the Kunisaki Peninsula with its stone Buddhas are also here.

In Dan'a'yo 大分 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 오이타 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
