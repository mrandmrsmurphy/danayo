## Notes
- [遠 (char)](characters/遠%20(char).md) "far" + [方](characters/方.md) "direction, place" — "distant place, far away location."

Mandarin yuǎnfāng, cantonese jyun5 fong1, japanese えんぽう, korean 원방, and vietnamese viễn phương are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. `远方` is the standard simplified-character spelling variant (遠→远), correctly listed as an alias. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 온빵 diverges from Sino-Korean 원방 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄛㄋㄈㄚㄫ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 遠's `stand_in` (遠 itself) nor 方's own `stand_in` (方向) points to this compound.
