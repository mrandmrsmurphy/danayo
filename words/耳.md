## Notes
- [耳 (char)](../characters/耳%20(char).md) "ear" — stand-in for [[耳 (char)|耳]], which cannot appear independently

耳 names the ear, both the anatomical organ and its extension to "eyes and ears" as instruments of perception and surveillance (耳目 "spies, informants"), and figuratively to anything ear-shaped or ear-adjacent (耳朶 "earlobe," 巻耳 "field chickweed," named for its ear-shaped leaves).

Mandarin ěr, Cantonese ji5, Japanese じ (耳鼻/じび, "ear and nose"; 中耳炎/ちゅうじえん, "otitis media"), Korean 이 (이목, "eyes and ears, attention"), and Sino-Vietnamese nhĩ are all standard readings. Japanese uses the native noun 耳 (みみ) for the everyday word, so on'yomi じ is bound to compounds; Korean has native 귀.
