## Notes
- [断](../characters/断.md) "cut off" + [念](../characters/念.md) "thought" — no stand-in relationship applies (断's own stand-in is [[割断]]; 念's own is [[念頭]]).

断念 means to give up or abandon.

Mandarin duànniàn, Cantonese dyun6 nim6, Japanese だんねん, Korean 단념, and Vietnamese đoạn niệm are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed missing space in `cantonese`. No homophones (注音 ㄉ⺢ㄋㄋㄝㄇ unique across both words and characters).
