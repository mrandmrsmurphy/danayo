## Notes
- [婚](../characters/婚.md) "marry" + [姻](../characters/姻.md) "marriage" — no stand-in relationship applies (婚's own stand-in is [[結婚]]; this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes 姻).

婚姻 means marriage or matrimony — the institution and legal bond of being married.

Mandarin hūnyīn, Cantonese fan1 jan1, Japanese こんいん, Korean 혼인, and Vietnamese hôn nhân are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄛㄋㄧㄋ unique across both words and characters).
