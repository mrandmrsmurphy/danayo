## Notes
- [壁 (char)](../characters/壁%20(char).md) "wall" + [画](../characters/画.md) "picture"

壁画 is a mural: a painting done directly on a wall. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin bìhuà as "mural (painting); fresco" and Japanese へきが as "wall painting such as a fresco or a mural". Vietnamese bích hoạ is the same word, and Korean 벽화 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese bik1 waa2 matches.

It is a kind of [絵画](絵画.md), and the hanging kind is the [掛軸](掛軸.md), scroll.
