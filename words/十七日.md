## Notes
- [十](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" + [七](../characters/七%20(char).md) "seven" + [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — no stand-in relationship applies (all three characters are independent Dan'a'yo entries, each its own stand-in).

十七日 names the 17th day of the month.

Japanese じゅうしちにち, Korean 십칠일, and Vietnamese thập thất nhật (matching the established convention on sibling day-words like [[七日]] "thất nhật") are all standard, fully compositional readings.
