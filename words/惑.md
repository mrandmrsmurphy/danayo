## Notes
- [惑 (char)](../characters/惑%20(char).md) "confused" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes the bound character 惑 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

惑 is 形声, built with [[或]] itself as its phonetic component, which is why the two are exact homophones in Dan'a'yo (and near-homophones throughout the family) despite naming unrelated concepts — 或 the disjunctive "or," 惑 "to be confused, bewildered, in doubt" (心, "heart," added to mark the emotional/mental sense). The core meaning holds steady across Chinese, Japanese, and Korean: 疑惑 "doubt, suspicion," 魅惑 "to charm, captivate" (to bewilder through allure), 惑星 (わくせい, "planet" — literally "a bewildering star," from the way planets appear to wander unpredictably against the fixed stars, unlike the old Chinese term 行星 which describes the same phenomenon more plainly as "a moving star").
English Wiktionary confirms the structure as 或 (phonetic) plus 心 (semantic, "heart"), and gives the Chinese senses "confused; puzzled; deluded" (大惑不解, and the Analects line "the wise are not confused"), "to confuse; to delude; to mislead" (妖言惑眾), a literary "to infatuate; to be obsessed", and a Buddhist "sensory delusion". Its compounds include 困惑, 誘惑, 迷惑, 疑惑, 魅惑, 不惑 and 惑星; of these only [[魅惑]] is a word in the vault. The Mind page lists the word as "confusing, bewildering; to doubt".

Mandarin huò and Cantonese waak6 are as at English Wiktionary.

The homophone listed above, 或, is the only other word sharing the 注音 ㄏㄛㄎ (checked 2026-10-12).
