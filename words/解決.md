## Notes
- [解 (char)](../characters/解%20(char).md) "explain" + [決 (char)](../characters/決%20(char).md) "decide"

解決 means "to solve, resolve, settle" — 解 originally "to separate, untangle" (会意 of 角 "horn" + 刀 "knife" + 牛 "cow," a knife separating a horn from a cow) extended to "explain, unravel a problem," combined with 決 (originally "to breach a dike, channel water," extended to "to decide, determine").

Mandarin jiějué and Cantonese gaai2 kyut3 preserve the sense directly. Japanese かいけつ (kaiketsu) is standard, everyday vocabulary (解決策, "solution, remedy"). Korean 해결 (haegyeol) parallels the Sino-xenic reading and is likewise standard everyday vocabulary. Vietnamese giải quyết is the standard Hán Việt reading, equally common in everyday and formal speech (giải quyết vấn đề, "to solve a problem").
