## Notes
- [拘](../characters/拘.md) "constrain, restrain" + [泥](../characters/泥%20(char).md) "mud" — no stand-in relationship applies (拘's own stand-in is [[拘束]]; 泥's own is itself).

拘泥 means inflexible or overly particular (about small details).

Mandarin jūnì, Japanese こうでい, Korean 구니, and Vietnamese câu nê are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bugs: `cantonese` was missing a space and used the wrong reading for 泥 (nei6→nai4, matching 泥's own citation). No homophones (注音 ㄍㄨㄋㄝㄧ unique across both words and characters).
