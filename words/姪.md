## Notes
- [姪 (char)](../characters/姪%20(char).md) "niece" — this word is the stand-in for its own character, broadened 2026-09-21 (user-directed) from the character's previously bound status (legitimized only by [[姪女]]) to stand independently.

姪 is Dan'a'yo's general word for "niece" — a sibling's daughter, regardless of whether the sibling is a brother or sister, and regardless of whether 姪 or [[姪女]] is used. Classically, 姪 named specifically a *fraternal* niece (a brother's daughter) — the female counterpart of [[甥]]'s classical "sister's child" restriction, with the two characters together (甥姪) covering both lineages. Dan'a'yo follows the same broadened, lineage-general pattern established for 甥: Japanese めい (mei) already covers a niece via either a brother or a sister, with sex — not lineage — being the distinguishing feature, exactly mirroring how おい (甥) does the same for "nephew." Korean 조카 is the native word (not a Sino-Korean reading of 姪 itself), used generally for "niece/nephew" and specified further as 조카딸 ("niece," literally "nephew-daughter") when sex needs marking — already the reading stored on [[姪女]]. Mandarin zhí and Cantonese zat6 are the bare, standard Sino readings, productive in 姪女/姪子 ("niece"/"nephew") and 姪孫 ("great-niece/nephew"). Vietnamese điệt is the Sino-Vietnamese reading, formal/classical register; everyday Vietnamese uses the native cháu gái instead.
