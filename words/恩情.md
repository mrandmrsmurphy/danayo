## Notes
- [恩](../characters/恩.md) "grace, kindness" + [情](../characters/情.md) "feeling, affection" — no stand-in relationship applies (恩's own stand-in is [[恩寵]]; 情's own is [[心情]]).

恩情 means kindness, affection, grace, or favor.

Mandarin ēnqíng, Cantonese jan1 cing4, Japanese おんじょう, Korean 은정, and Vietnamese ân tình are all standard, fully compositional readings.
