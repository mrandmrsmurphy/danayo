## Notes
- [恩](../characters/恩.md) "grace, kindness" + [情](../characters/情.md) "feeling, affection" — no stand-in relationship applies (恩's own stand-in is [[恩寵]]; 情's own is [[心情]]).

恩情 means kindness, affection, grace, or favor.

Mandarin ēnqíng, Cantonese jan1 cing4, Japanese おんじょう, Korean 은정, and Vietnamese ân tình are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bugs found and fixed**: `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` had 恩's syllable using the wrong reading 안/'an instead of 은/'ǝn (same bug class just fixed on [[恩恵]]); `korean` had been contaminated with the Vietnamese value "ân tình" — corrected to compositional 은정, with the genuine Vietnamese moved into its own field. Omitted empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄜㄋㄑㄧㄫ unique across both words and characters).
