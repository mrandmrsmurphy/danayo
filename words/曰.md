## Notes
- [曰](../characters/曰%20(char).md) "saith, spake" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 曰 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

曰 is an archaic quotative particle meaning "spake" or, verbally, "to dub/call (ancient)" — used to introduce quoted speech in Classical Chinese (子曰 "the Master said").

Mandarin yuē, Korean 왈, and Vietnamese viết are standard, fully compositional readings matching 曰's own stored citation exactly.
