## Notes
- [雰](../characters/雰.md) "atmosphere, ambience" + [囲](../characters/囲.md) "encircle"

雰囲 means mood or atmosphere — the two-character root of the far more common three-character compound 雰囲気/氛囲気 (with 気/氣, "air/energy," added). In practice this bare two-character form is rarely used standalone in any of the five languages: Japanese always says 雰囲気 (ふんいき, fun'iki) rather than ふんい alone, and Korean likewise always says 분위기 (bunwigi) rather than 분위 alone — this word's own `japanese`/`korean` fields hold the compositional readings for the two-character form specifically, distinct from (and shorter than) those far more common attested compounds. Sino-Vietnamese phân vi is likewise rare and compositional; everyday Vietnamese uses the native phrase bầu không khí ("atmosphere, vibe") instead.
