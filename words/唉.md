## Notes
- [唉 (char)](../characters/唉%20(char).md) "alas (ancient)" — stand-in for [[唉 (char)|唉]], which cannot appear independently

唉 is the mouth-radical form of [[矣]] ("indeed," a sentence-final particle), which supplies its phonetic. The two are separate words with different meanings and readings, so 唉 is not folded into 矣.

`注音 ㄚㄧ`/`애` is also carried by two other existing standalone words, [[崖]] ("cliff") and [[愛]] ("love"). `vietnamese: ai` is the reading for the "alas, lament" sense, chosen from the character's longer stored list (ai, hãy, hĩ, hơi, hẽ, hễ, hởi, hỡi).
