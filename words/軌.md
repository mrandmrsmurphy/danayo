## Notes
- [軌 (char)](../characters/軌%20(char).md) "track, path, rut" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 軌 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

軌 originally named a wheel rut or track worn into a road by repeated passage, extended to any fixed path, course, or track — including a railway track (軌道, "orbit; railway track") and by extension a norm or standard to follow (常軌, "normal course, usual practice"; 越軌, "to overstep the bounds").

Mandarin guǐ, Cantonese gwai2, and Korean 궤 (gwe) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading わだち (wadachi, "wheel rut") rather than the on-reading キ (KI) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese quỹ is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading.
