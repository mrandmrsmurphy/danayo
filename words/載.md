## Notes
- [載 (char)](../characters/載%20(char).md) "carry, record" — 載 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

Mandarin zài, cantonese zoi3, and korean 재 are all directly attested standard readings, matching the character's own stored values exactly. Vietnamese tải is the character's own standard Sino-Vietnamese reading (as used compositionally in [[転載]], "chuyển tải").

載 (char)'s own `stand_in` is this exact word (載, itself), so 載 (char) is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry by this same word.
