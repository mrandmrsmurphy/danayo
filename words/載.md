## Notes
- [載 (char)](../characters/載%20(char).md) "carry, record" — 載 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

Mandarin zài, cantonese zoi3, and korean 재 are all directly attested standard readings, matching the character's own stored values exactly. Vietnamese tải is the character's own standard Sino-Vietnamese reading (as used compositionally in [[転載]], "chuyển tải"). `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 재 exactly matches Sino-Korean 재. Note that 載 (ㄐㄚㄧ/jai/재) is a Dan'a'yo homophone of [[哉]] (ㄐㄚㄧ/jai/재, "O — vocative particle") and [[在]] (ㄐㄚㄧ/jai/재, "to exist") — a genuine three-way coincidental homophone group, all three pages mutually cross-linked; found and fixed this group's missing third member on 哉.md and 在.md's own existing (previously two-way) callouts.

載 (char)'s own `stand_in` is this exact word (載, itself), so 載 (char) is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry by this same word.
