## Notes
- [俳](../characters/俳.md) "actor, vaudeville" + [優](../characters/優.md) "excellent, superior" — "actor." Mandarin páiyōu, Cantonese paai4 jau1, Korean 배우 (baeu), and Japanese はいゆう (haiyū) all directly use this exact compound as the standard word for "actor" — the stored `japanese` field previously held 役者 (yakusha), a different everyday Japanese word for the same concept written with entirely different characters, not built from 俳優 at all; corrected to the compound's own reading. `vietnamese: diễn viên` is the standard native Vietnamese term (not a Sino-Vietnamese calque of 俳優 itself). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 벼웃 diverges from Sino-Korean 배우 in the first syllable's vowel and the final coda. **Real content bug found and fixed**: `aliases` had included 演員/演员 — the standard Mandarin word for "actor" but a *different* word entirely, unrelated in characters to 俳優, not a spelling variant of it; removed, keeping only 俳优 (俳優's own genuine simplified-character form). No homophones (注音 ㄅ⼶ㄨㄛ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 俳's own `stand_in` is this exact word (俳優), so 俳 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 俳優.
