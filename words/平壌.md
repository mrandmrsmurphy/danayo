## Notes
- [平](../characters/平.md) "level" + [壌](../characters/壌.md) "soil, earth" — the name of Pyongyang. 壤 is the other form of 壌, which is why the filename uses 壌; 平壤 is kept as the word's alias.

平壌 (Pyongyang) is the capital and largest city of North Korea, on the Taedong River (大同江), and a directly governed city (直轄市) outside the provinces (from memory, not re-checked). The name reads "level land", from 平 and 壌. The city was the capital of Goguryeo (高句麗) from 427 and the site of the Lelang commandery (樂浪郡) before it (from memory, not re-checked).

English Wiktionary has 平壤 as a Chinese proper noun (Mandarin Píngrǎng, Cantonese ping4 joeng6) and has Korean and Vietnamese entries for it (the Korean entry a hanja form of 평양, the Vietnamese entry a Han form of Bình Nhưỡng); Chinese, Japanese and Korean Wiktionary also have the string. The Japanese entry 平壌 gives ピョンヤン as the usual modern name (from the Korean) and へいじょう as a dated reading; へいじょう is taken here as the Sino-Xenic reading. Korean 평양 is the North Korean reading and is spelled the same in South Korea. Vietnamese Bình Nhưỡng is also the name used by the Vietnamese Wikipedia.
