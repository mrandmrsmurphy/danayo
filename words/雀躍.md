## Notes
- [雀](characters/雀.md) "sparrow" + [躍](characters/躍.md) "leap" — literally "hop like a sparrow."

雀躍 means to jump for joy.

Mandarin quèyuè, Cantonese zoek3 joek6, and Japanese じゃくやく are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 雀's and 躍's own stored citations. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with the compositional tước dược (雀's own tước + 躍's own dược).

**Real homophone with [[炸薬]]** ("explosive") — reformatted the pre-existing informal tip note into the canonical `Homophones` callout on both pages.
