## Notes
- [雀](../characters/雀.md) "sparrow" + [躍](../characters/躍.md) "leap" — literally "hop like a sparrow."

雀躍 means to jump for joy.

Mandarin quèyuè, Cantonese zoek3 joek6, and Japanese じゃくやく are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 雀's and 躍's own stored citations.
