## Notes
- [懺](../characters/懺.md) "repent, confess sins" + [悔](../characters/悔.md) "regret" — "repent and regret," confession of sins. Their own `stand_in` values: 悔's is [[後悔]]; this word is the `stand_in` that legitimizes 懺.

懺悔 is to repent: to confess one's sins and regret them, in Buddhism and in Christianity. Mandarin chànhuǐ (忏悔), Cantonese caam3 fui3, Japanese ざんげ and Korean 참회 all use it, and Vietnamese sám hối does too. The character 懺 is bound to this word, whose Buddhist origin is the clipping of 懺摩; it is the stand-in for 懺, and 忏 is its simplified alias. It is repentance with confession, where [[悔改]] is to repent and change one's ways, [[告解]] is the sacrament of confession, and [[自白]] is to confess any wrongdoing. Compare [[赦免]] (pardon) and [[罪]] (sin).
