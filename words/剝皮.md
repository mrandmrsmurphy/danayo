## Notes
- [剝](../characters/剝.md) "peel" + [皮](../characters/皮.md) "skin"
- This word is the viable form of [剝](../characters/剝.md): the character's meaning (to peel) is identical, but 剝 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. The compound is a verb-object construction — to peel (剝) the skin (皮).
- 皮 is itself bounded (its own `stand_in` is [[皮膚]]), lending itself here to 剝's legitimizer.

Mandarin bōpí, Cantonese mok1 pei4 (剝's Cantonese reading mok1 is a genuine irregular sound correspondence, not a typo — confirmed against 剝's own stored citation), Korean 박피 (bakpi), and Japanese はくひ (hakuhi) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese bác bì is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, matching 剝's bác (one of two attested readings, alongside bóc — the everyday colloquial descendant of the same root) and 皮's own bì. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 복비 diverges from Sino-Korean 박피 in both syllables. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄅㄛㄎㄅㄧ unique among both words and characters).
