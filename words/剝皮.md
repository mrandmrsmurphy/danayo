## Notes
- [剝](../characters/剝.md) "peel" + [皮](../characters/皮.md) "skin" — a verb-object construction, "to peel the skin," and the stand-in for [剝](../characters/剝.md), which cannot appear independently (the character's meaning, to peel, is identical, but 剝 alone is too bound to stand as a Dan'a'yo word). 皮 is itself bound; its own stand-in is [皮革](皮革.md).

Mandarin bōpí, Cantonese mok1 pei4 (剝's Cantonese reading mok1 is a genuine irregular sound correspondence, matching 剝's own stored citation), Korean 박피 (bakpi), and Japanese はくひ (hakuhi) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese bác bì is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, matching 剝's bác (one of two attested readings, alongside bóc — the everyday colloquial descendant of the same root) and 皮's own bì.
