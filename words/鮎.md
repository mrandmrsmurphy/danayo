## Notes
- [鮎](../characters/鮎%20(char).md) "sweetfish, sheatfish" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 鮎 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

鮎 names the catfish or sheatfish in its original Chinese sense. Japanese uniquely reinterprets the same character to mean あゆ (ayu, "sweetfish," *Plecoglossus altivelis*), an entirely different fish — a semantic divergence unique to Japanese usage, distinct from the Chinese catfish sense.

Mandarin nián, Cantonese nim4, and Korean 점 (jeom) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese niêm is one of three stored candidates on the character page. This word is a genuine Dan'a'yo homophone of [[粘]] ("sticky") — coincidental, unrelated in meaning.
