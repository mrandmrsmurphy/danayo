## Etymology
- [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal, gold" + [融 (char)](../characters/融%20(char).md) "melt, blend, harmonize" — "finance," the circulation/blending of capital.

## Notes
Mandarin jīnróng, cantonese gam1 jung4, japanese きんゆう, and korean 금융 are all directly attested standard readings — the same characters mean "finance" across Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, and Korean. Filled a blank `vietnamese` with honest compositional "kim dung" (the real Vietnamese term "tài chính" is not built from these characters). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 김융 diverges from Sino-Korean 금융 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄇ⼜ㄫ unique among both words and characters). Fixed `characters:` disambiguation (bare 金/融 → "金 (char)"/"融 (char)," since both [[金]] and [[融]] have their own word pages). Citations on both 金(char).md's and 融(char).md's own Words lists already correctly present.
