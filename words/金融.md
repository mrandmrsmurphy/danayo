## Etymology
- [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal, gold" + [融 (char)](../characters/融%20(char).md) "melt, blend, harmonize" — "finance," the circulation/blending of capital.

## Notes
Mandarin jīnróng, cantonese gam1 jung4, japanese きんゆう, and korean 금융 are all directly attested standard readings — the same characters mean "finance" across Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, and Korean. Citations on both 金(char).md's and 融(char).md's own Words lists already correctly present.

Earlier Notes on this page may state a different value or a different reason.
