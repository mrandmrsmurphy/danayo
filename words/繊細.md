## Notes
- [繊](../characters/繊.md) "fine, delicate, minute" + [細 (char)](../characters/細%20(char).md) "fine, thin, slender, quiet (sound)" — 繊's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 細's own is [[細]] itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 繊細 legitimizes 繊 alone.

繊細 means slender, delicate, or fine — a refined, sensitive delicacy.

Mandarin xiānxì, Cantonese cim1 sai3, Japanese せんさい (sensai), and Korean 섬세 (seomse) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 繊細 is itself a common attested word for "delicate, fine" in all four languages. Vietnamese tiêm tế is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term. Homophone: [[陝西]] (Shaanxi) shares the 注音 ㄙㄝㄇㄙㄝㄧ.
