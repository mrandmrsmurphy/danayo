## Notes
- [竣](../characters/竣.md) "to finish" + [工](../characters/工.md) "work; construction"

竣工 is "to complete a construction project; completion of construction." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 竣工 (jùngōng, Cantonese zeon3 gung1) is "to complete a project; to finish construction," Japanese 竣工 (しゅんこう) is "completion of construction" and also a suru-verb (antonyms 起工 and 着工), and Korean 준공 is the hanja form for "completion (of construction)." Wiktionary has no Vietnamese entry, so `vietnamese` is left blank. Where [完成](完成.md) "complete" applies to anything, 竣工 is limited to building and engineering works. It is the stand-in for [竣](../characters/竣.md), which cannot appear independently.
