## Notes
- **Genuine `#cranberry`**: both [螳](../characters/螳.md) and [螂](../characters/螂.md) — each independently means "mantis" on its own — have their own `stand_in` pointing to this exact compound (full transitivity; tag already correctly present).

Mandarin tángláng and korean 당랑 are both standard compositional readings. Vietnamese bọ ngựa is the real, standard, non-compositional Vietnamese word for "mantis" — legitimate.
