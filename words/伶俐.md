## Notes
- [伶](../characters/伶.md) "quick, nimble" + [俐](../characters/俐.md) "clever, sharp" — "clever, quick-witted" (聰明伶俐, "intelligent and sharp," a common compliment for children). This word is the `stand_in` legitimizer for both [[伶]] and [[俐]] (transitivity holds: A=B=AB), hence the `#cranberry` tag. Both `japanese: れいり` and `vietnamese: linh lợi` are directly attested (confirmed via web search).
