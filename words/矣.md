## Notes
- [矣](../characters/矣%20(char).md) "indeed" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 矣 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

矣 is a Classical Chinese sentence-final particle marking completion, emphasis, or a change of state — roughly comparable to Mandarin's modern 了 or 啦, but confined to the literary register. It appears throughout the Classical canon closing declarative statements with a sense of finality ("...and so it is/was").

Mandarin yǐ, Cantonese ji5, Japanese い (i, kanbun kundoku usage), Korean 의 (ui), and Vietnamese hĩ are all standard readings, though the word survives almost exclusively in Classical Chinese, kanbun-derived Japanese, and quotations of classical texts rather than in ordinary modern speech in any of these languages.
