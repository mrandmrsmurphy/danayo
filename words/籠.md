## Notes
- [籠](../characters/籠%20(char).md) "basket, cage" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 籠 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

籠 names a woven basket or cage — originally a bamboo basket (its own 形声 phonetic component 龍 is purely phonetic, unrelated in meaning), extended to any cage-like enclosure: 鳥籠 ("birdcage"), 灯籠 ("lantern," a light enclosed in a woven or paper frame).

Mandarin lóng, Cantonese lung4, and Korean 롱 (rong) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading かご (kago, "basket, cage") rather than the on-reading ロウ (ROU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese lồng is the standard reading and the everyday Vietnamese word for "cage" (lồng chim, "birdcage"). This word is a genuine Dan'a'yo homophone of [[弄]] ("tamper with, mess with") — coincidental, unrelated in meaning.
