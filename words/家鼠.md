## Notes
- [家](../characters/家.md) "house; family" + [鼠](../characters/鼠.md) "rat; mouse"

家鼠 is the house mouse or house rat: the small rodents that live in and around human homes. English Wiktionary lists it only for Chinese (Mandarin jiāshǔ, Cantonese gaa1 syu2), glossed as the "house mouse" (*Mus musculus*) and as "rat" (genus *Rattus*), especially the house rat (*Rattus rattus*) and the brown rat (*Rattus norvegicus*). It has no entry for Japanese or Korean, so Japanese いえねずみ and Korean 가서 here are built from the characters, the Japanese being a native compound, いえ "house" plus ねずみ "rat; mouse", like いえばえ in [[家蝿]]. The Vietnamese field is left blank.

The second character, 鼠, is the rodent, and the other rodent words on this page are [[小鼠]] and [[鼠類]].
