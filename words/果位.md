## Notes
- [果](../characters/果.md) "fruit, result" + [位](../characters/位.md) "position, rank" — "the position of the fruit," the stage of attainment a practitioner has reached. Their own `stand_in` values are [[果実]] and [[定位]]; neither points here, so 果位 is an independent compound.

果位 is a Buddhist technical term for the stage of realization reached through practice, the "fruit" that follows the "cause" of cultivation. Chinese Buddhist reference works set it against 因位, the stage of practice before Buddhahood is reached, and treat it beside 證果, the act of entering such a stage by realizing the truth with right wisdom. In the Buddhist scheme of teaching, practice, principle, conduct and fruit, 果位 belongs to the last: the result and its verification. The Śrāvaka path counts four stages of fruit (the Stream-enterer as the first, the Once-returner as the second, the Non-returner as the third, and the Arhat as the fourth), and the Tiantai school's classification of the bodhisattva path runs to fifty-two stages, ending in the perfect awakening of a buddha. 果位 names the position on any such ladder at which a practitioner stands, not the ladder itself and not the ordinary sense of 結果 "result."

The practice that earns it is [[修行]]; the figures who hold the higher stages include the [[菩薩]] and the [[佛陀]] (see also [[佛教]]). The term is attested in Chinese Buddhist sources; I did not find a Wiktionary entry for it, and I did not confirm its use in Japanese, Korean or Vietnamese texts, so the `japanese`, `korean` and `vietnamese` fields give the ordinary Sino-compound readings of 果 and 位.
