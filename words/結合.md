## Notes
- [結 (char)](../characters/結%20(char).md) "tie, knot" + [合 (char)](../characters/合%20(char).md) "join, unite" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (結's is [[結]] itself, 合's is [[合]] itself), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

結合 means to combine, unite, or bond — to join two or more things into one.

Mandarin jiéhé, Cantonese git3 hap6, Japanese けつごう (ketsugō), and Korean 결합 (gyeolhap) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 結合 is itself a common attested word for "combine, unite" in all four languages. Vietnamese kết hợp is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
