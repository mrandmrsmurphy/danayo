## Notes
- [浮](../characters/浮%20(char).md) "float" + [力](../characters/力%20(char).md) "force" — "floating force," buoyancy. Their own `stand_in` values: 浮's is itself; 力's is itself; none points here, so 浮力 is an independent compound.

浮力 is buoyancy: the upward force of a fluid on a body in it. Mandarin fúlì, Cantonese fau4 lik6 and Japanese ふりょく are attested, and Korean 부력 is the same compound. Compare [[重力]] (gravity) and [[液体]] (liquid).
