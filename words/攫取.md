## Notes
- [攫](../characters/攫.md) "seize; grab" + [取](../characters/取.md) "take" — stand-in for [[攫]], which cannot appear independently

攫取 means to seize, grab or snatch: to take hold of something quickly and forcibly. 攫 carries the image of a bird of prey clutching its quarry in its claws, and 取 supplies the plain sense of taking, so the compound is the physical act of snatching, extended in use to grabbing things that are not in the hand, such as profit, power or opportunity.

It is a close relative of [[奪取]] ("to seize by force"), the usual synonym: 奪取 stresses wresting something away from someone, while 攫取 stresses the speed and greed of the grab. The same character gives the chengyu [[一攫千金]] ("a fortune in a single grab"), which keeps the sense of an abrupt, opportunistic take.

Mandarin juéqǔ is the standard reading, and Japanese かくしゅ (kakushu) is the established on-yomi reading of the compound.  Cantonese fok3 ceoi2 and Korean 확취 are compositional, built from each character's own reading.  No Vietnamese form was found attested, so none is given.
