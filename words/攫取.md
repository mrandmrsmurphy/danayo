## Notes
- [攫](../characters/攫.md) "seize; grab" + [取](../characters/取.md) "take" — stand-in for [[攫]], which cannot appear independently

Wiktionary lists 攫取 only under Chinese: "to seize; to grab; to capture". Related: [[奪取]], and the chengyu [[一攫千金]]. 攫 is a bound character whose stand-in is this word.
