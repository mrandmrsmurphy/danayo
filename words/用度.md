## Notes
- [用](../characters/用.md) "use" + [度](../characters/度.md) "degree, manner" — no stand-in relationship applies (用's own stand-in is [[使用]]; 度's own is [[程度]]).

用度 means supplies, expenditure.

Mandarin yòngdù, Cantonese jung6 dou6, Japanese ようど, Korean 용도 and Vietnamese dụng độ (用's own dụng plus 度's own độ) are all fully compositional. The native Korean 씀씀이 is the everyday word, not recorded here.
