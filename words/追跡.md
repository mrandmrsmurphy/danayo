## Notes
- [追 (char)](../characters/追%20(char).md) "follow, hunt" + [跡](../characters/跡.md) "vestige; trace"

追跡 is to follow the traces of something: to track, trail, or pursue. Japanese uses it as a noun and a suru verb (tsuiseki), "chase, pursuit, tracking," and Korean has chujeok. Mandarin's usual form is 追蹤 (zhuīzōng), written with 蹤 rather than 跡. In Dan'a'yo it is the verb for stalking prey.
