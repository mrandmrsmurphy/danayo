## Notes
- [鵰 (char)](../characters/鵰%20(char).md) "eagle" — 鵰 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

Mandarin diāo, Cantonese diu1, Korean 조, Japanese ちょう, and Sino-Vietnamese điêu are all standard, fully compositional readings. This word is a genuine Dan'a'yo homophone of [[秋]] ("autumn"); 秋's own `stand_in` points to itself, confirming the collision.
