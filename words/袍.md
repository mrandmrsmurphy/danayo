## Notes
- [袍 (char)](../characters/袍%20(char).md) "robe"

袍 is a robe, a long gown. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 袍 (Mandarin páo, Cantonese pou4) as "(Chinese-style) long gown, robe, or cloak". For Japanese ほう it gives a round-collared, skirted over-robe, worn as the outermost layer of court dress by officials, which is narrower than the Chinese sense. Korean 포 and Vietnamese bào are listed as readings of the hanja.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [袍](../characters/袍%20(char).md) as a free word. Its sound ㄆ⼘ㄨ is shared with [票](票.md), a ballot. The general word for clothes is [衣服](衣服.md), and the Buddhist robe is [加沙](加沙.md).
