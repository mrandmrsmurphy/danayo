## Notes
- [演 (char)](../characters/演%20(char).md) "perform" + [奏](../characters/奏.md) "play music" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 奏 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (演's own stand-in is itself, so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

演奏 means to play (music).

Mandarin yǎnzòu, Cantonese jin2 zau3, Japanese えんそう, Korean 연주, and Vietnamese diễn tấu are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 演's and 奏's own stored citations exactly), and also the real, standard everyday words for "to perform music" in each language.
