## Notes
- [意](../characters/意.md) "intention" + [味](../characters/味.md) "taste, flavor" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [意](../characters/意.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (味's own stand-in is [[味覚]]).

意味 means to signify or mean — the sense or significance of something.

Mandarin yìwèi, Cantonese ji3 mei6, Japanese いみ, Korean 의미, and Vietnamese ý vị are all standard, fully compositional readings.
