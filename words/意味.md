## Notes
- [意](../characters/意.md) "intention" + [味](../characters/味.md) "taste, flavor" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [意](../characters/意.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (味's own stand-in is [[味覚]]).

意味 means to signify or mean — the sense or significance of something.

Mandarin yìwèi, Cantonese ji3 mei6, Japanese いみ, Korean 의미, and Vietnamese ý vị are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `諺文`/`羅馬字` had 意's syllable using the wrong reading 이/'i instead of 으/'ǝ (matching 意's own citation and the already-correct 注音). Trimmed `korean`'s comma-joined native contamination (뜻). Filled blank `pos`/`vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄜㄇㄨㄧ unique across both words and characters).
