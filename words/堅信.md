## Notes
- [堅](../characters/堅.md) "firm, steadfast" + [信](../characters/信.md) "believe" — "firm faith," confirmation. Their own `stand_in` values: 堅's is [[堅硬]]; 信's is [[信任]]; none points here, so 堅信 is an independent compound.

堅信 is confirmation: the sacrament that strengthens a baptized person's faith. Mandarin jiānxìn and Cantonese gin1 seon3 mean to believe firmly, and Japanese けんしん and Korean 견신 are the same compound, in the church usually 堅信礼. Compare [[洗礼]] (baptism) and [[信仰]] (faith).
