## Notes
- [哲](../characters/哲.md) "wise, sagacious" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [哲](../characters/哲.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (学's own stand-in is [[学習]]).

哲学 means philosophy — the academic and intellectual discipline.

**Real bugs found and fixed**: `mandarin`/`cantonese` had been stored as Chinese characters themselves ("哲學"/"哲理," the latter a different word meaning "philosophical principle") instead of romanizations — corrected to zhéxué/zit3 hok6. Japanese てつがく, Korean 철학, and Vietnamese triết học are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄊㄏㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).

For a practitioner of philosophy, use [[哲学者]] ("one who does philosophy"). For the academic title "philosopher," use 哲学家.
