## Etymology
- [甜](../characters/甜.md) "sweet" + [瓜](../characters/瓜.md) "cucumber, melon" — no stand-in relationship applies (甜's own stand-in is [[名専字]]; 瓜's own is [[胡瓜]]).

甜瓜 means melon, muskmelon (specifically the Oriental/Korean melon, *Cucumis melo* var. *makuwa*).
