## Notes
- [便 (char)](../characters/便%20(char).md) "convenient, opportunity" + [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "ride" — "to hitchhike, take a free ride, deadhead" (also biology's "phoresy," an organism riding another for transport). Japanese びんじょう, Korean 편승 (pyeonseung), and Mandarin biànchéng/Cantonese bin6 sing4 are all directly attested standard readings. `vietnamese: tiện thừa` is the honest compositional reading (便's own tiện + 乗's own thừa); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this specific sense was found. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 변숭 diverges from Sino-Korean 편승 in the first syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄅ⼶ㄋㄙㄨㄫ unique among both words and characters).
