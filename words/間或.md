## Notes
- [間](../characters/間.md) "between, among" + [或 (char)](../characters/或%20(char).md) "or, perhaps"

間或 means sometimes, occasionally, now and then — a classical/literary register word, less common in casual speech than everyday alternatives like 有時/有時候. Mandarin 間或 (jiànhuò), Cantonese 間或 (gaan3 waak6), and Sino-Vietnamese gián hoặc all take a distinct minority reading of 間 (jiàn/gaan3/gián, "interval"), diverging from the character's own stored default reading (jiān/gaan1/gian, "between, space") — the same kind of sense-conditioned reading split documented as a recurring pattern in the sweep (cf. [[拓]]'s *tà/*tuò split); other 間-initial compounds carrying this same minority reading include 間諜 ("spy") and Cantonese 間中 ("occasionally").

Korean 간혹 (ganhok) shares this meaning but keeps the ordinary Sino-Korean concatenation with no reading split, and is commonly paired or interchanged with the native adverb 이따금 ("once in a while") in everyday speech. Japanese does not use 間或 as an attested compound at all — かんわく is only the compositional on'yomi concatenation, included here for the Dan'a'yo derivation; real Japanese instead expresses this meaning with 時折 (tokiori) or 時々 (tokidoki).
