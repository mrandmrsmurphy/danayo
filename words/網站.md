## Notes
- [網](../characters/網%20(char).md) "net" + [站](../characters/站.md) "station, stand" — "net station," a place on the web. Their own `stand_in` values: 網's is itself; 站's is [[兵站]]; none points here, so 網站 is an independent compound.

網站 is a website. Mandarin wǎngzhàn and Cantonese mong5 zaam6 are the ordinary Chinese words, formed from 網 "net" (as in [[互連網]], the internet) and 站 "station". Japanese says ウェブサイト and Korean 웹사이트, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[網]] (net) and [[互連網]] (internet).
