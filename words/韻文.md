## Notes
- [韻](../characters/韻.md) "rhyme" + [文](../characters/文.md) "writing"

韻文 is verse: writing that rhymes or keeps a metre. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yùnwén as "literary composition in rhyme; verse" and Japanese いんぶん as "literary composition in rhyme; verse". Korean 운문 and Vietnamese vận văn are the same word. Cantonese wan5 man4 matches.

It is set against [散文](散文.md), prose. Its rhyme is [押韻](押韻.md), its metre the [韻律](韻律.md), and the general word for a poem is [詩歌](詩歌.md).
