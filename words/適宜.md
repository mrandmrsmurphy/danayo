## Notes
- [適](../characters/適.md) "suit, fit" + [宜](../characters/宜.md) "suitable, fitting" — "suitable, appropriate."

Mandarin shìyí, cantonese sik1 ji4, and japanese てきぎ are all directly attested standard readings. Korean 적의 and vietnamese thích nghi (a directly attested Vietnamese word — "to adapt, adjust") are both compositional (適's own 적/thích + 宜's own 의/nghi).
