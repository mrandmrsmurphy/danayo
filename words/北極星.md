## Notes
- [北](../characters/北.md) "north" + [極 (char)](../characters/極%20(char).md) "pole" + [星 (char)](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star"

北極星 is the North Star: the star near the north celestial pole, which seems to stay still while the others turn. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin běijíxīng as "North Star; Polaris" and Japanese ほっきょくせい as "the North Star, Pole Star, Polaris". Cantonese bak1 gik6 sing1 matches, and Korean 북극성 is the reading of the characters.

The classical name for it is [北辰](北辰.md). It is a [恒星](恒星.md), fixed star, in the sense that it hardly moves, and it is used for finding north.
