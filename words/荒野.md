## Notes
- [荒](../characters/荒.md) "wild, desolate" + [野](../characters/野.md) "field, wilds" — "wild field," the wilderness. Their own `stand_in` values: 荒's is [[荒廃]]; 野's is [[田野]]; none points here, so 荒野 is an independent compound.

荒野 is the wilderness: untamed, empty land away from towns and farms. Mandarin huāngyě, Cantonese fong1 je5, Japanese こうや and Korean 황야 all use it, and Vietnamese hoang dã does too. It is wild land as a place, not a personality, which is why 奔放 ("wild" of a person) is not the word. Compare [[田野]] (open countryside) and [[草原]] (grassland).
