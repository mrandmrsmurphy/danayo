## Notes
- [族](../characters/族.md) "clan, ethnicity" + [譜](../characters/譜.md) "record, chart" — no stand-in relationship applies (族's own stand-in is [[家族]]; 譜's own is [[楽譜]]).

族譜 means clan registry or genealogy book.

Mandarin zúpǔ, Cantonese zuk6 pou2, Japanese ぞくふ, Korean 족보, and Vietnamese tộc phả (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄛㄎㄅㄛ unique across both words and characters).
