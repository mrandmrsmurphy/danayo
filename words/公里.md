## Notes
- [公](../characters/公%20(char).md) "public, metric" + [里](../characters/里%20(char).md) "li, distance" — "metric li," a thousand metres. Both characters are their own `stand_in`, so 公里 is an independent compound.

公里 is the kilometre. Mandarin gōnglǐ and Cantonese gung1 lei5 are the ordinary Chinese words, built on the same 公 "metric" prefix as [[公尺]] (metre) and [[公斤]] (kilogram). Japanese writes it キロメートル or 粁 and Korean 킬로미터, so the `japanese` and `korean` fields hold the readings of the characters. It is a unit word: it takes a numeral directly (三公里). Compare the traditional [[里]] and the Western [[英里]].
