## Notes
- [米 (char)](../characters/米%20(char).md) "rice (uncooked)" + [粟](../characters/粟.md) "millet, grain" — 粟's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 米's own is [[米]] itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 粟 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word (already correctly noted). `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

米粟 names grain generally — rice and millet together, the staple cereals of ancient East Asian agriculture, used as a collective term for provisions or grain stores.

Mandarin mǐsù, Cantonese mai5 suk1, Japanese べいぞく (beizoku), and Korean 미속 (misok) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese mễ túc is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form.
