## Notes
- [弯](../characters/弯.md) "bend" + [曲](../characters/曲.md) "bend" — this word is the stand-in for [弯](../characters/弯.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo (曲's own stand-in is [[歌曲]]).

弯曲 describes something curved or bent — a bent road, a warped board, a curving line.

Japanese わんきょく (WAN + KYOKU, 弯's own on'yomi, not the alternate EN), Korean 만곡 (compositional from both characters' own readings), and Vietnamese loan khúc (verified — a genuine Sino-Vietnamese compound meaning "winding, curved") are all standard readings. No homophones (注音 ⺢ㄇㄎ⼄ㄎ unique across both words and characters).
