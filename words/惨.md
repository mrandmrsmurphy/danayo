## Notes
- [惨](../characters/惨%20(char).md) "wretched, cruel" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

惨 means wretched or cruel — miserable, tragic, or brutal, as in 惨劇 ("tragedy") and 悲惨 ("miserable, tragic").

Mandarin cǎn, Cantonese caam2, Japanese さん, Korean 참, and Vietnamese thảm are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `korean` had been stored as the literal string "null" — corrected to compositional 참, matching the file's own already-correct 諺文.
