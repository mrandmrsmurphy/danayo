## Notes
- [傑](../characters/傑.md) "remarkable, masterful" + [出 (char)](../characters/出%20(char).md) "come out, exceed" — "remarkable, outstanding, brilliant" (a person or achievement that stands out from the ordinary; 傑出人才, "outstanding talent"). Mandarin jiéchū, Cantonese git6 ceot1, Japanese けっしゅつ, Korean 걸출, and Vietnamese kiệt xuất are all directly attested standard readings.

Legitimizing note: 傑's own `stand_in` is this exact word (傑出), so 傑 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 傑出.
