## Notes
- [暴 (char)](../characters/暴%20(char).md) "violent" + [力 (char)](../characters/力%20(char).md) "force"

暴力 is violence: the use of physical force to hurt or to compel. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin bàolì as "violence; brutality; force" and Japanese ぼうりょく as "violence (especially criminal), force". Vietnamese bạo lực is the same word, and Korean 폭력 and Cantonese bou6 lik6 match.

It names the force itself, where [暴行](暴行.md) is an act of violence done to someone and [凶暴](凶暴.md) describes a savage temper. A mob that uses it is a [暴徒](暴徒.md).
