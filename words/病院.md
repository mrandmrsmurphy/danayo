## Notes
- [病](characters/病.md) "sickness" + [院](characters/院.md) "institution, courtyard" — no stand-in relationship applies (病's own stand-in is [[疾病]]; 院's own is [[院落]]).

病院 means hospital, written with the same characters (and same real word) as Japanese びょういん, Korean 병원, and Vietnamese bệnh viện. Chinese itself instead uses a different real term built on 醫/医 ("medicine") rather than 病 ("sickness") — 醫院/医院, listed here as aliases — so `mandarin`/`cantonese` correctly give the real Chinese term (yīyuàn/ji1 jyun2) rather than a non-existent literal reading of 病院. Fixed a malformed hybrid tone notation in `cantonese` (ji1 jyun6-2 → ji1 jyun2, matching 院's own stored citation exactly). No homophones (注音 ㄅ⼶ㄫ⼔ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
