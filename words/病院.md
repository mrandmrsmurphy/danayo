## Notes
- [病](../characters/病.md) "sickness" + [院](../characters/院.md) "institution, courtyard" — no stand-in relationship applies (病's own stand-in is [[疾病]]; 院's own is [[院落]]).

病院 means hospital, written with the same characters (and same real word) as Japanese びょういん, Korean 병원, and Vietnamese bệnh viện. Chinese itself instead uses a different real term built on 醫/医 ("medicine") rather than 病 ("sickness") — 醫院/医院 — so `mandarin`/`cantonese` correctly give the real Chinese term (yīyuàn/ji1 jyun2) rather than a non-existent literal reading of 病院.

`醫院`/`医院` are not aliases: [[医院]] is a separate, independent word with its own distinct reading (ㄜ⼔ㄋ) and its own clinic-vs-hospital scope nuance  (the traditional form 醫院 belongs on 医院's own page, where it is already listed).
