## Notes
- [閏 (char)](../characters/閏%20(char).md) "intercalary, leap" + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month"

閏月 is the extra thirteenth month inserted into a lunisolar calendar year — see [[置閏]] for the full mechanics — to keep a purely lunar 12-month calendar (about 354 days) synchronized with the ~365.25-day solar year, inserted roughly 7 times per 19-year cycle (the Metonic cycle). Unlike the Gregorian [[閏日]] (a single extra day), 閏月 corrects drift on a much larger scale, and which month becomes the 閏月 in any given year is determined astronomically rather than by a fixed calendar position.

Japanese うるうづき (a native compound) and Korean 윤월 are both attested. Vietnamese nhuận nguyệt is likewise standard, part of the same nhuận family as nhuận nhật/nhuận niên. No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄨㄋ⼔ㄊ unique across both words and characters).
