## Notes
- [閏 (char)](../characters/閏%20(char).md) "intercalary, leap" + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month"

閏月 is the leap month, an extra month inserted into some years of a lunisolar calendar. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 閏月 (Mandarin rùnyuè, Cantonese jeon6 jyut6) as "intercalary month in the lunar calendar; leap month", and Japanese うるうづき as "leap month". The Korean 윤월 and Vietnamese nhuận nguyệt fields are built from the readings of the two characters. The extra day of a year is the [閏日](閏日.md), and the mechanics are under [置閏](置閏.md).
