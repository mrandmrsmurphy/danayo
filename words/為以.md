## Notes
- [為](../characters/為%20(char).md) "to act as" (the same reading as in [[為人]]) + [以](../characters/以%20(char).md) "by, using" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 為's and 以's own stand-ins are themselves).

為以 means "in order that, so that" — a Dan'a'yo compound combining two purpose-conveying morphemes (為 "for the purpose of" + 以 "by means of, in order to").
