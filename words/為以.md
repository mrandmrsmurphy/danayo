## Notes
- [為](../characters/為%20(char).md) "to act as" (polyphonic sense, matching [[為人]]'s use of the same reading) + [以](../characters/以%20(char).md) "by, using" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 為's and 以's own stand-ins are themselves).

為以 means "in order that, so that" — a Dan'a'yo compound combining two purpose-conveying morphemes (為 "for the purpose of" + 以 "by means of, in order to").

Fixed real bugs: `mandarin`/`cantonese` had been contaminated with the readings of the unrelated real word 為了 ("in order to," wèile/wai6 liu5, built on 了 rather than 以) — corrected to the compositional wéiyǐ/wai4 ji5, matching 為's (polyphonic "act as" sense) and 以's own stored citations. `japanese`/`korean` had been full phrase templates (～のために / ~을 위해서) rather than readings — corrected to the compositional いい/위이. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with vi dĩ (為's own vi + 以's own dĩ). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases: []`. No homophones (注音 ⼔·ㄧ unique across both words and characters).
