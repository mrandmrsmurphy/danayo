## Notes
- [顕](../characters/顕.md) "prominent, clear" + [現 (char)](../characters/現%20(char).md) "present, revealed" — no `#cranberry`: 顕's own `stand_in` is [[顕着]], 現's is 現 itself; neither points here.

顕現 (xiǎnxiàn) is a real Mandarin word for "to manifest, become visible/apparent," and Cantonese 顕現 (hin2jin6) likewise. Japanese けんげん (kengen) and Korean 현현 (hyeonhyeon) are both standard terms, often in philosophical or religious registers (theophany, manifestation of the divine). Sino-Vietnamese hiển hiện is likewise standard and attested — a clean case with full agreement across all five languages.
