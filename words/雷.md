## Notes
- [雷 (char)](../characters/雷%20(char).md) "thunder" — this word is 雷's own stand-in.

雷 means thunder. Mandarin 雷 (léi), Cantonese 雷 (leoi4), Japanese 雷 (らい, on'yomi — the native かみなり, kaminari, is far more common in everyday speech), Korean 뢰 (roe, this vault's standing North Korean/문화어 pronunciation policy; South Korean orthography would instead give 뇌 via 두음법칙), and Sino-Vietnamese lôi all preserve this meaning, though the character survives mainly bound in compounds (雷電, "thunder and lightning"; 魚雷, "torpedo") rather than as a fully everyday free-standing word in most of these languages.

Exact syllable check: 雷 shares its Dan'a'yo reading (loi/뢰/ㄌㄛㄧ) with 磊 and 儡, but neither stands alone as its own word (磊 is a name-only character; 儡's own stand-in is [[傀儡]]), so there is no genuine word-level homophone to callout.
