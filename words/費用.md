## Notes
- [費 (char)](../characters/費%20(char).md) "to spend; expense" + [用](../characters/用.md) "to use"

費用 is "an expense; a cost; a fee," the money spent on something. According to Wiktionary, Chinese 費用 (fèiyòng, Cantonese fai3 jung6) is "fee; expense; charge; cost," and Japanese ひよう is "cost." Wiktionary has no Korean or Vietnamese section, so `korean` 비용 and `vietnamese` phí dụng are compositional readings, with the Korean form matching the ordinary word (from memory, not re-checked). It is used in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [平臥即是正義](../translation/平臥即是正義.md), in 毎月 之 費用 "monthly expenses," standing in for the Chinese 花销. Related: [費](費.md) "expense" and [経費](経費.md) "expenditures."
