## Notes
- [澄](../characters/澄.md) "clear, pure" + [清](../characters/清.md) "pure, clear" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 澄 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (清's own stand-in is [[清潔]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

澄清 means clear, pure; to clarify.

Mandarin chéngqīng, Cantonese cing4 cing1, Japanese ちょうせい, and Korean 징청 (澄's own real Sino korean 징, distinct from the Dan'a'yo-internal 창) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 澄's and 清's own stored citations exactly.
