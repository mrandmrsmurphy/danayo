## Notes
- [拘](../characters/拘.md) "constrain, hold" + [禁](../characters/禁.md) "prohibit, confine" — no stand-in relationship applies (拘's own stand-in is [[拘束]]; 禁's own is [[禁止]]).

拘禁 means to detain or hold in custody.

Mandarin jūjìn, Cantonese keoi1 gam3, Japanese こうきん, Korean 구금, and Vietnamese câu cấm are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄨㄍㄧㄇ unique across both words and characters).
