## Notes
- [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "gold" + [蘭](../characters/蘭.md) "orchid" + [湾](../characters/湾.md) "bay" — "gold orchid bay"; no stand-in relationship applies (金 is its own stand-in; 蘭's is [[蘭花]]; 湾's is [[海湾]]). The alias 金蘭灣 is the traditional spelling, since 灣 is an alias of 湾.

金蘭湾 is Cam Ranh Bay (Vietnamese Vịnh Cam Ranh), the deep-water bay on the south-central coast of Vietnam in Khánh Hòa province, between Nha Trang and Phan Rang, known as a naval base under the French, Americans and Soviets. Chinese Wikipedia writes the bay 金蘭灣, and English Wiktionary lists it under the derived terms of 灣; the city on its shore is [[金蘭]]. Japanese Wikipedia has the bay only as カムラン湾. The Vietnamese name is not Hán-Việt; it transcribes Cham <i>Kăm M'ran</i> (see [[金蘭]]).

Do not confuse it with [[金角湾]], the Golden Horn of Istanbul and Vladivostok: the two differ in one character, 蘭 "orchid" against 角 "horn". An earlier version of the vault wrongly gave 金角湾 the Cam Ranh sense.

The readings are compositional: Mandarin jīnlánwān and Cantonese gam1 laan4 waan1 (the bay's Cantonese reading is not separately attested; from memory, not re-checked), Japanese きんらんわん, Korean 금란만 and Hán-Việt kim lan loan (each character as at hvdic.thivien.net; hvdic has no entry for the compound). In Dan'a'yo the word keeps the shared CJKV sense: [[海湾]] is the common noun for a bay or gulf, and 金蘭湾 only the proper name.

No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄇㄌㄚㄋ⺢ㄇ unique across words and characters). The 注音 needs no syllable-boundary dot because ㄋ⺢ㄇ is not a syllable.
