## Notes
- [孤](../characters/孤.md) "lone, lonely" + [立](../characters/立%20(char).md) "stand" — no stand-in relationship applies (孤's own stand-in is [[孤独]]; 立's own is itself).

孤立 means isolated or unrelated — standing alone, cut off from connection or support.

Mandarin gūlì, Cantonese gu1 lap6, Japanese こりつ, Korean 고립, and Vietnamese cô lập are all standard, fully compositional readings. Simplified `cantonese` (dropped a redundant "/ gu1 laap6" alternate, keeping the reading matching 立's own citation). No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄌㄧㄆ unique across both words and characters).
