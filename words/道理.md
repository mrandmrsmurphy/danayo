Mandarin dàolǐ, cantonese dou6 lei5, japanese どうり, korean 도리, and vietnamese đạo lý are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 닷리 diverges from Sino-Korean 도리 entirely.
