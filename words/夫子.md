## Notes
- [夫 (char)](../characters/夫%20(char).md) "man, master" + [子](../characters/子.md) "master, philosopher" — an honorific title for a revered teacher, especially Confucius (孔夫子), and by extension "master" generally. **Major bugs found and fixed**: `羅馬字` was stored as "bujǝ" and `注音` as "ㄅㄨㄐㄜ" — both mechanically copied from [[父子]]'s pattern rather than derived from [夫 (char)](../characters/夫%20(char).md)'s own confirmed romanization (羅馬字 fǝ, 注音 ㄈㄜ) — corrected to fǝjǝ/ㄈㄜㄐㄜ. **As a consequence, the previously-documented homophone with [[父子]] is false and has been removed**: 夫 and 父 have genuinely different Middle Chinese initials (voiceless f vs. voiced v) and thus different Dan'a'yo pronunciations (諺文 쁘즈 vs. 부즈, which was already correct on both pages even while the romanization/bopomofo fields were wrong) — they only coincidentally share the same Cantonese-adjacent shape, not the same Dan'a'yo sound. **A genuine, separate homophone was found instead — at the Korean level, not the Dan'a'yo level**: Sino-Korean does not distinguish 夫 and 父's MC voicing the way Dan'a'yo does, so both read 부 — making 夫子/부자 and [[父子]]/부자 real Korean homophones (a case where Dan'a'yo is phonologically more granular than Korean for this particular pair). Corrected `korean` from the wrong "공자" (Confucius's proper name, 孔子, an unrelated word) to 부자. `vietnamese: phu tử` is compositional. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 쁘즈 diverges from Sino-Korean 부자 in the first syllable. No homophones within the Dan'a'yo system itself (注音 ㄈㄜㄐㄜ unique among both words and characters).
