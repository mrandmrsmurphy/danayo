## Notes
- [心](../characters/心%20(char).md) "heart, mind" + [理](../characters/理.md) "reason, principle" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study" — no stand-in relationship applies (心's own stand-in is itself; 理's own is [[理由]]; 学's own is [[学習]]).

心理学 means psychology — the academic discipline studying the mind and behavior. For the "state of mind" sense, see [[心理]]; for the practitioner, see [[心理学家]] ("psychologist").

Mandarin xīnlǐxué, Cantonese sam1 lei5 hok6, Japanese しんりがく, Korean 심리학, and Vietnamese tâm lý học are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄇㄌㄧㄏㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
