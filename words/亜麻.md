## Notes
- [亜](../characters/亜.md) "second, sub-" + [麻](../characters/麻.md) "hemp" — flax, linseed, literally "sub-hemp," named for its resemblance to hemp as a fiber crop.

亜麻 (flax/linseed) is the standard term across the Sinosphere: Mandarin yàmá (also yǎmá), Cantonese aa3 maa4, and Korean 아마 are all fully compositional. Japanese uses the katakana アマ for the botanical/agricultural term, following the common Japanese convention of writing plant and animal species names in katakana regardless of their kanji etymology. Vietnamese cây lanh ("flax plant," a native term) is the standard everyday word — 亜麻仁 (linseed) and 亜麻布 (linen cloth, [[亜麻布]]) are common derived compounds.

`kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 아마 coincides exactly with Sino-Korean 아마, since both 亜 and 麻 independently carry `kwin: true` on their own character pages.
