## Notes
- [十 (char)](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" + [二 (char)](../characters/二%20(char).md) "two" + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — literally "ten-two month" (compare [十二](十二.md) "twelve," the same two characters).

十二月 is the standard Gregorian-calendar name for December, formed the same transparent "number + 月" way as every other month in this vault's calendar vocabulary (compare [十一月](十一月.md) "November," [一月](一月.md) "January").

Mandarin shí'èryuè, Cantonese sap6 ji6 jyut6, and Korean 십이월 (sibiwol) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese じゅうにがつ (jūnigatsu) is a fully Sino-Japanese compound (十 jū + 二 ni + 月 gatsu). Vietnamese tháng mười hai is a native construction ("month ten-two"), not a Sino-Vietnamese compound — the everyday way all Vietnamese month names are formed.
