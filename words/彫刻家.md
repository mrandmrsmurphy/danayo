## Notes
- [彫](../characters/彫.md) "carve" + [刻](../characters/刻.md) "engrave" + [家](../characters/家.md) "house; specialist"

彫刻家 is a sculptor: an artist who carves or models figures. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin diāokèjiā as "sculptor" and Japanese ちょうこくか as "sculptor". Korean 조각가 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese diu1 hak1 gaa1 matches.

The art is [彫刻](彫刻.md), and its products are the [彫像](彫像.md), statue, and [塑像](塑像.md), modelled statue. The wider term is [芸術家](芸術家.md), artist.
