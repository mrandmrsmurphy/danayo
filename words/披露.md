## Notes
- [披](../characters/披%20(char).md) "expose" + [露](../characters/露%20(char).md) "dew" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 披 and 露's own stand-ins are themselves).

披露 means to announce or display publicly.

Mandarin pīlù, Cantonese pei1 lou6, Japanese ひろう, Korean 피로, and Vietnamese phi lộ (a real, standard term — "to disclose") are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese ひろう and Korean 피로 both coincidentally match the real-world readings of 疲労 ("fatigue," using unrelated characters 疲/労) — a coincidence confined to the real-world languages, not a Dan'a'yo-level homophone, since 労's own Dan'a'yo reading differs from 露's. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄨㄧㄌㄛ unique across both words and characters).
