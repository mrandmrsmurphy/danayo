## Notes
- [極](characters/極%20(char).md) "extreme, of the highest degree, utmost" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 極 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

極 means extreme, of the highest degree, or utmost.

Mandarin jí, Cantonese gik6, Japanese きょく, Korean 극, and Vietnamese cực (the primary form among the character's own comma-joined vietnamese variants cặc/cọc/cực) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Added the missing `pos`/`japanese`/`hsk_level` fields and filled the blank `vietnamese`. No word-level homophones — the bound character [[棘]] shares this same 注音 (ㄍㄧㄎ) but has no independent word page of its own.
