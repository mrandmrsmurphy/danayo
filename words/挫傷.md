## Notes
- [挫](../characters/挫.md) "break, frustrate" + [傷](../characters/傷.md) "wound, injure" — "crush injury," a bruise. Their own `stand_in` values: 挫's is [[挫折]]; 傷's is [[傷害]]; none points here, so 挫傷 is an independent compound.

挫傷 is a bruise: an injury under unbroken skin from a blow. Mandarin cuòshāng, Cantonese co3 soeng1 and Japanese ざしょう are attested, and Korean 좌상 is the same compound. Compare [[傷害]] (to wound) and [[損傷]] (to damage).
