## Notes
- [地 (char)](../characters/地%20(char).md) "land" + [帯 (char)](../characters/帯%20(char).md) "belt, sash" — a geographic zone or belt, as in 安全地帯 ("safety zone") or 心臓地帯 ("heartland"). The Vietnamese reading "địa đới" uses the same alternate reading of 帯 (đới rather than đái) seen in [[熱帯]] (nhiệt đới).
The Shape page lists 地帯 as "area, zone — a broader geographic area than [[区域]]'s bounded district," and the Geography page uses it in running prose for belts of terrain, such as desert zones (沙漠地帯) and steppe regions (草原地帯). Dan'a'yo keeps the sense "zone, belt, area," a stretch of land defined by a shared character or function. It is distinct from [地域](地域.md) "region, area" and [地方](地方.md) "district, locality," which name a region without the belt-like or functional sense. The readings are Mandarin dìdài, Cantonese dei6 daai3, Japanese chitai (ちたい), Korean 지대 and Sino-Vietnamese địa đới. The shared examples and the Vietnamese đới/đái remark above are the page's existing account and have not been re-checked here.
