## Notes
- [周](../characters/周.md) "circumference" + [辺](../characters/辺.md) "side, edge, border"

周辺 names the area around something: its surroundings, vicinity or periphery.  周 contributes the sense of going all the way around, a circuit, and 辺 the side or edge, so the compound is the zone on every side of a center, with a leaning toward its outer margin.  It is the usual word for the neighborhood of a place (駅周辺, "the area around the station"), for the states around a country (周辺国, "neighboring countries"), and for the surrounding conditions of a situation (周辺環境).  In computing it gives 周辺機器 ("peripheral devices"), equipment attached around a central unit.

The word is close to [[周囲]], the area or circumference around something, and the two overlap widely.  周囲 stresses what encloses a thing, even the measure of its circumference, while 周辺 stresses the region around a core and its edge, and is the one that implies a center with a periphery.  It also stands apart from [[辺境]], the remote frontier at the far edge of a territory.

Mandarin 周边 (zhōubiān, alias 周邊), Japanese しゅうへん, and Korean 주변 are the same compound with the same sense.  Cantonese zau1 bin1 is the compositional reading.  No Vietnamese form is given.
