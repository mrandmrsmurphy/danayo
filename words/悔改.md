## Notes
- [悔](../characters/悔.md) "regret" + [改](../characters/改%20(char).md) "change, correct" — "regret and amend," repentance. Their own `stand_in` values: 悔's is [[後悔]]; 改's is itself; none points here, so 悔改 is an independent compound.

悔改 is to repent: to regret one's wrong and change one's ways. Mandarin huǐgǎi and Cantonese fui3 goi2 are the ordinary words, the standard Chinese Christian term for repentance. Japanese says 悔い改め and Korean 회개, so those two fields hold the character readings. Compare [[罪]] (sin) and [[赦免]] (pardon).
