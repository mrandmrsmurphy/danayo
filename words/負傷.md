## Notes
- [負 (char)](../characters/負%20(char).md) "bear; suffer" + [傷](../characters/傷.md) "wound"

負傷 is to be wounded: to take an injury, especially in a fight or an accident. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin fùshāng as "to sustain an injury; to be wounded" and Japanese ふしょう as "an injury; to be injured". Korean 부상 and Cantonese fu6 soeng1 match.

It is the wounded state of the one hurt, where [傷害](傷害.md) is the injury as an offense done by another.
