## Notes
- [揚](../characters/揚%20(char).md) "scatter, hurl" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [揚](../characters/揚%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

揚 means to scatter or hurl (as in 揚聲, "raise one's voice").

Mandarin yáng, Cantonese joeng4, Japanese よう, Korean 양, and Vietnamese dương are all standard, fully compositional readings. Part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all 'yang/양/⼘ㄫ) with [[様]] ("-ly") and [[陽]] ("shine") — checked eight other candidates sharing this reading (央/仰/洋/殃/羊/楊/瘍/養) and confirmed none has an independent word at this exact reading.
