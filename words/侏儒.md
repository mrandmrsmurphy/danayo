## Notes
- [侏](../characters/侏.md) "dwarf, short" + [儒](../characters/儒.md) "scholar" (here, a bound phonetic-adjacent component rather than its usual Confucian-scholar sense) — "dwarf, pygmy, midget." `vietnamese: chu nhu` is the honest compositional reading (侏's own chu + 儒's own nhu); no independent attestation of this specific compound was found. Legitimizing note: 侏's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, so 侏 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.
