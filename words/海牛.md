## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [牛 (char)](../characters/牛%20(char).md) "cow" — no stand-in relationship applies (海's own stand-in is [[海洋]]; 牛's own is itself).

海牛 means sea cow, manatee.

Mandarin hǎiniú and Cantonese hoi2 ngau4 are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 海's and 牛's own stored citations. Japanese マナティー and Korean 매너티 are both the international loanword "manatee," and Vietnamese lợn biển ("sea pig") is the real, standard native Vietnamese term — the same real-world-species-name convention already established on [[龍蝦]]/[[水牛]]/[[河馬]]. Fixed erroneous capitalization on `vietnamese` (Lợn biển → lợn biển, a common noun, not a proper noun). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄧㄋ⼜ unique across both words and characters).
