## Notes
- [羊](../characters/羊.md) (stand-in for 佯, "feign") + [狂](../characters/狂.md) "mad" — to feign madness; pretend to be insane. A classical Chinese term describing a historical survival strategy used by officials and scholars to avoid political danger. Attested in Mandarin; other languages not yet confirmed.
