## Notes
- [皆 (char)](../characters/皆%20(char).md) "all; every; universal" + [時 (char)](../characters/時%20(char).md) "time; moment; season; occasion"

皆時 is the collective (universal) correlative in the 時 ("time") column of the correlative table: "always, at all times, at every time taken together, throughout all time". The character [皆 (char)](../characters/皆%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *gye*, ㄍ⼶) is the quantifier "all; every", the free word being [皆](皆.md); [時 (char)](../characters/時%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *si*, ㄙㄧ) is "time", and the word page [時](時.md) lists the correlatives that 時 generates. 皆時 treats time as one totality: the predicate holds across the whole range of time at once, so it suits a standing state or a general truth. Illustrative only (invented, not from `grammar/`): 皆時、正直 "(he) is always honest".

The vault itself uses 皆時 in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 in [平臥即是正義](../translation/平臥即是正義.md) (sentence 2): 其 皆時 汝 周辺 於 出現, "they appear around you at all times". Per the task brief this stands in for Chinese 無時無刻 "at every moment" (the Chinese original was not re-checked in this pass).

Contrast with the column neighbours. The distributive [毎時](毎時.md) "each time; on every occasion" goes through the occasions one by one, so it suits a procedure or a habit ("check each time"), where 皆時 asserts one standing fact; the same collective/distributive split runs through the table, as [皆人](皆人.md) against [毎人](毎人.md). [某時](某時.md) "sometime" is the indefinite, naming one unspecified occasion, and [何時](何時.md) "when?" is the interrogative. The deictic cells [此時](此時.md) "now", [其時](其時.md) "then" and [彼時](彼時.md) "back then" each point at one particular time, whereas 皆時 covers all of it. The existing words for the present, [今](今.md) and [現在](現在.md), are lexical and unrelated to this quantificational cell. Against the vault's other words for permanence and frequency: [永遠](永遠.md) (実詞, "eternal; forever") and [永久](永久.md) (性詞, "permanent; lasting") speak of unending duration, whereas 皆時 quantifies over occasions and does not claim eternity ("he is always honest" does not mean "honest forever"); [常常](常常.md) "often; frequently" is weaker still, a high frequency and not a universal; and [常時](常時.md) is a noun, "constantness; frequentness". Words in the vault also gloss 毎時 as "every time; always" ([時](時.md) and [毎](毎.md)); that is the sense of 皆時, and [毎時](毎時.md) itself is the distributive "each time". Those two pages were left as they are.

Its part of speech is 代詞 (pronoun), like the rest of the 皆 row apart from [皆多](皆多.md) and [皆様](皆様.md), and like the other cells of the 時 column ([此時](此時.md), [其時](其時.md), [彼時](彼時.md), [何時](何時.md), [某時](某時.md)); only [毎時](毎時.md) is adverbial (副用名詞), as in the whole 毎 row. The chart gives only form and 注音, not part of speech, so 代詞 is the sibling convention and not a statement of the grammar files. If 皆時 is used as an adverbial time frame, `grammar/Linguistics Features.md` (line 68) says that an adverbial noun (副用名詞) may be the topic or frame of a clause with no marking; that rule is stated for 副用名詞, not for 代詞, so how a 代詞 time word behaves as a frame is not settled by `grammar/`, and no example in `grammar/` uses 皆時.

The cross-linguistic fields were checked against Wiktionary on 2026-10-11. Wiktionary has no entry for 皆時 or its simplified form 皆时 (the latter is held in `aliases`, and has no word file of its own), so no `mandarin`, `cantonese`, `japanese`, `korean` or `vietnamese` field is entered: the word is a Dan'a'yo compositional construction, and none of the readings the page held before (Mandarin shíshí, Japanese いつも, Korean 항상) is a reading of the compound; they were equivalents of the meaning, and are removed. Established neighbours on Wiktionary: Chinese 時時 (simplified 时时; shíshí) is an adverb "(very) often; constantly; always", and 時時刻刻 is an adverb "continuously; constantly; always; all the time"; 總是 (simplified 总是; zǒngshì) is an adverb "always; invariably" and "anyway; after all; eventually"; 常常 (chángcháng) is "frequently; often", which supports the weaker frequency reading above rather than the universal one. Japanese いつも (itsumo) is an adverb "always, invariably, all the time", "usually", and, with a negative verb, "never"; Korean 항상 (hangsang) is an adverb "always". These are the real everyday equivalents, not readings of 皆時. From memory, not re-checked (Wiktionary requests for 언제나 and 恒常 failed on 2026-10-11): Korean 언제나 "always, any time" and Japanese 常に "always, habitually". Vietnamese *luôn luôn* and *lúc nào cũng* are also from memory, not re-checked; hvdic.thivien.net covers single characters, not phrases, so no `vietnamese` field is entered. Dan'a'yo keeps the sense shared across CJKV, "always; at all times"; no narrowing is needed.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md)'s correlative table (line 126 is the 皆 ("all") row; the same chart is in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`, line 149 in the 時 ("time") row, with the column header cell 皆 on line 142), 皆時 is the sixth cell of the 皆 row, after [皆事](皆事.md) "all matters", [皆物](皆物.md) "all things", [皆人](皆人.md) "all people", [皆名](皆名.md) "all honoured persons" and [皆処](皆処.md) "all places", and before [皆様](皆様.md) "all manners", [皆多](皆多.md) "the whole amount" and [皆類](皆類.md) "all kinds". The table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), 63 forms, so 皆時 is parsed compositionally: 皆 = collective, 時 = time. The 時 and 処 columns are the temporal/spatial pair, so 皆時 lines up with [皆処](皆処.md) "everywhere", and together they are the spatio-temporal universals of the paradigm. Like its siblings 皆時 is listed on the Grammar page only: [Time](../lexipedia/Time.md) mentions 時 itself and the lexical [常時](常時.md) "constantly, at all times", but not this cell.

The spellings are the plain concatenation of the two character pages: 羅馬字 *gye* + *si* = gyesi, 諺文 겨 + 시 = 겨시, 注音 ㄍ⼶ + ㄙㄧ = ㄍ⼶ㄙㄧ, each checked letter by letter against 皆 (char) and 時 (char). The 注音 carries no syllable-boundary dot. `grammar/文法 - 02音韻論.md` allows only the nasals ㄇ ㄋ ㄫ, the glides and the unreleased stops ㄆ ㄊ ㄎ as syllable-final consonants, and ㄙ is none of them, so ㄍ⼶ㄙ is not a syllable (`syllables/` has ㄍ⼶, ㄍ⼶ㄇ, ㄍ⼶ㄋ, ㄍ⼶ㄧ and ㄍ⼶ㄫ, but no ㄍ⼶ㄙ) and ㄙㄧ must be a syllable of its own (it exists in `syllables/`). There is no second complete partition, the same as [皆処](皆処.md), [皆多](皆多.md) and [皆事](皆事.md), and in contrast to [皆人](皆人.md), whose second syllable begins with ㄋ and so needs the dot.
