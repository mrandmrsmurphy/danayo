## Notes
- [島](../characters/島%20(char).md) "island" + [根](../characters/根%20(char).md) "root"

島根 is Shimane, written "island root". It is the name of the old Shimane district, and the reading しまね is native: 島 (しま, "island") plus 根 (ね, "root"). The meaning of the name is itself uncertain.

Shimane is the old Izumo country, with the Izumo Grand Shrine, and its capital is Matsue, where Lafcadio Hearn lived. It also holds the Iwami Ginzan silver mine, a UNESCO site, and the Oki Islands.

In Dan'a'yo 島根 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 시마네 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
