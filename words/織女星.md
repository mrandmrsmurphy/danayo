## Notes
- [織](../characters/織.md) "weave" + [女 (char)](../characters/女%20(char).md) "woman" + [星 (char)](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" — none of the three characters' own `stand_in` points here, so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 女 is individually `kwin: false`.

織女星 names Vega — literally "Weaving Maid star," named for 織女 (the Weaver Girl), the mythological figure who, per the East Asian Qixi/Tanabata legend, is separated from her cowherd lover Niulang (represented by Altair) by the Milky Way.

Mandarin Zhīnǚxīng, Cantonese zik1 neoi5 sing1, and Korean 직녀성 (jingnyeoseong) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 織女星 is itself the common attested name for Vega in all three. Japanese おりひめぼし (Orihime-boshi) is the native reading, using 織姫 (Orihime, "Weaving Princess") rather than a Sino-Japanese compound. Vietnamese chức nữ tinh is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
