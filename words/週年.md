## Notes
- [週](../characters/週.md) "week, cycle" + [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year" — an anniversary, the completed cycle of a year

週年 is an anniversary counted in whole years, always attached to a number: 創立百周年 (the hundredth anniversary of founding), 結婚十週年 (the tenth wedding anniversary), and 一周年 for the first.  週 here has the sense of a completed cycle or circuit (a week is a cycle of days, a 週年 a cycle of years), and 年 gives the year, so the word does not mean the date itself but the full-year milestone reached by it.  It is used for institutions, events and relationships alike.  The alias 周年 is the variant spelling of 週, written 周 in Chinese and in some Japanese usage.

週's own `stand_in` is 週日, so this word is not its legitimizer; 年's is itself.  The dot in the 注音 ㄐㄨㄛ·ㄋㄝㄋ is needed because ㄐㄨㄛㄋㄝㄋ could also parse as ㄐㄨㄛㄋ + ㄝㄋ.  Mandarin zhōunián, Cantonese zau1 nin4, Japanese しゅうねん, Korean 주년 and Vietnamese chu niên are the same compound.  `kwin` is false because the Dan'a'yo 줏넌 differs from the Korean 주년.
