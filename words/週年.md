## Notes
- [週](../characters/週.md) "week, cycle" + [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year" — an anniversary, the completed cycle of a year

Wiktionary lists 周年 (alias 周年) with the meaning "anniversary" in Chinese and Japanese, and gives Korean 주년 and Vietnamese chu niên as the Han forms, "anniversary". 週's own `stand_in` is 週日, so this word is not its legitimizer; 年's is itself.
