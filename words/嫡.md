## Notes
- [嫡](../characters/嫡%20(char).md) "legitimate wife" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 嫡 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

嫡 means legitimate wife, or by extension a principal/rightful line of descent, as opposed to a secondary or collateral one.

Mandarin dí, Cantonese dik1, Japanese ちゃく, Korean 적, and Vietnamese đích are all standard, fully compositional readings. Part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read dag/닥/ㄉㄚㄎ) with [[摘]] ("pinch, pluck") and [[鈬]] ("bronze bell").
