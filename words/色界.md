## Notes
- [色](../characters/色.md) "form; matter" + [界](../characters/界.md) "realm" — a translation of Sanskrit *rūpa-dhātu*, the realm of form.

色界 is the middle one of the [三界](三界.md): the world of beings who have given up the desires for food and for sex but are still bound to matter. Here 色 means not "colour" but the physical, material form (Sanskrit *rūpa*); the realm holds only pure, refined matter, with no craving but with a body. It is where those who have mastered the four meditations (四禅) are reborn, and it divides into the four meditation heavens, which the sources count as 17 heavens, or 16 or 18 in other schools. Its highest heaven, 色究竟天, is identified in Kumārajīva's Lotus Sutra with the 有頂天. It lies above the [欲界](欲界.md) and below the [無色界](無色界.md). The Mandarin, Cantonese and Japanese readings are the attested ones; the Korean follows the North Korean readings of the characters.
