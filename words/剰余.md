## Notes
- [剰](../characters/剰.md) "surplus, excess" + [余](../characters/余.md) "remainder, surplus" — a synonym-doubling compound, "surplus, remainder" (剰余価値, "surplus value," a Marxist economics term). `vietnamese: thặng dư` is one of the most common everyday Vietnamese words for this concept (giá trị thặng dư, "surplus value"), a direct match.

**Real content bug found and fixed**: `korean` had three comma-separated glosses ("잉여, 여분, 나머지" — the latter two native Korean synonyms) crammed into the reading field — trimmed to the compositional 잉여 alone, matching 剰's own 잉 + 余's own 여. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 징요 diverges entirely from Sino-Korean 잉여. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄫ·⼄ unique among both words and characters).
