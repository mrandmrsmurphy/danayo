## Notes
- [剰](../characters/剰.md) "surplus, excess" + [余](../characters/余.md) "remainder, surplus" — a synonym-doubling compound, "surplus, remainder" (剰余価値, "surplus value," a Marxist economics term). `vietnamese: thặng dư` is one of the most common everyday Vietnamese words for this concept (giá trị thặng dư, "surplus value"), a direct match.
