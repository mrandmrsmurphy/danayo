## Notes
- [共 (char)](../characters/共%20(char).md) "together, with" + [産](../characters/産.md) "give birth" + [党](../characters/党.md) "political party"

共産党 is the standard CJKV name for a Communist Party. [[共産]] is the calque for "communist", literally "common property" (共 "together, shared" + 産 in its sense of "holdings, property", as in [[財産]], not the "give birth" sense listed above), and 党 is "party" (compare [[政党]], political party). The compound entered the Sinosphere from Japanese translations of European socialist literature in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, and from Japanese passed into Chinese, Korean and Vietnamese. It names the party of a state or a movement: the Communist Party of China, founded in 1921; the Japanese Communist Party, founded in 1922; and, in Vietnamese, Đảng Cộng sản Việt Nam, founded in 1930.

The word is listed in the Government lexipedia and appears in the 歴史綱要 chronology. In Dan'a'yo it is the generic name for a Communist Party, with no divergence from the shared sense; which party is meant is a matter of context.
