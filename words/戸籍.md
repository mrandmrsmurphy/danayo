## Notes
- [戸](../characters/戸.md) "door" + [籍](../characters/籍.md) "record, register, list" — no stand-in relationship applies (戸's own stand-in is [[門戸]]; 籍's own is [[書籍]]).

戸籍 means family register — the official record of household and lineage.

Mandarin hùjí, Cantonese wu6 zik6, Japanese こせき, Korean 호적, and Vietnamese hộ tịch are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄛㄐㄝㄎ unique across both words and characters).
