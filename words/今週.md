## Notes
- [今 (char)](../characters/今%20(char).md) "present" + [週](../characters/週.md) "week" — "this week." `korean: 금주` is the real, formal/written Sino-Korean reading (as in 금주 중, "during this week"). `vietnamese: kim chu` is the honest Sino-Vietnamese compositional reading (週's own chu, as in chu kỳ "cycle/period"); the universal everyday Vietnamese phrase is native tuần này instead. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 김줏 diverges from Sino-Korean 금주 via the AND-rule (both constituents independently false). Homophone with [[禁酒]] re-verified via anchored grep — genuine (注音 ㄍㄧㄇㄐㄨㄛ matches both).
