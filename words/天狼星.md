## Notes
- [天](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven" + [狼](../characters/狼%20(char).md) "wolf" + [星](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" — no stand-in relationship applies (天 is its own stand-in; 狼's own is [[狼]]; 星's is [[星]]).

天狼星 means Sirius, the Dog Star — literally "heaven's wolf star," the brightest star in the night sky.

Mandarin Tiānlángxīng, Cantonese tin1 long4 sing1, Korean 천랑성, and Japanese てんろうせい are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese sao Thiên Lang is the standard astronomical name for Sirius (fixed wrongly-capitalized "Sao"→"sao," matching the lowercase-common-noun-prefix convention of [[水星]]'s "sao Thủy").
