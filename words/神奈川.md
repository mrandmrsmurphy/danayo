## Notes
- [神](../characters/神%20(char).md) "god" + [奈](../characters/奈.md) "apple tree" + [川](../characters/川%20(char).md) "river"

神奈川 is Kanagawa, written "god, Nara-tree, river". It was originally the name of a post town on the Tōkaidō, now a ward of Yokohama, and its origin is uncertain, so the characters are best read for sound and not for sense. The reading かながわ is native, with 川 (かわ) voiced to がわ.

Kanagawa runs along the western shore of Tokyo Bay, with its capital Yokohama, Japan's second largest city. It includes Kawasaki, the old shogunal city of Kamakura and the mountain resort of Hakone. The Treaty of Kanagawa of 1854, signed after Commodore Perry's visit, opened Japan to American ships.

In Dan'a'yo 神奈川 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 가나가와 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
