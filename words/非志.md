## Notes
- [非 (char)](../characters/非%20(char).md) "not" + [志](../characters/志.md) "will, intention" — Dan'a'yo neologism for Fiji; the characters are chosen purely for phonetic fit: 羅馬字 *fiji*

非志 is a Dan'a'yo coinage for Fiji, with the characters chosen for phonetic fit (romanisation *fi* + *ji*); Wiktionary has no entry for it. The standard Chinese name 斐濟 (Wiktionary: Fiji) is kept as an alias. Wikipedia: Fiji is an archipelagic country in Melanesia, Oceania, in the South Pacific Ocean, of more than 330 islands, about 110 of them permanently inhabited. Fiji sits outside the East Asian cultural sphere that [[南亜]] addresses.
