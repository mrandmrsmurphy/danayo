## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [港](../characters/港.md) "harbor" — "sea harbor," a seaport. Their own `stand_in` values: 海's is [[海洋]]; 港's is [[港湾]]; none points here, so 海港 is an independent compound.

海港 is a seaport: a harbor on the sea that ships use. Mandarin hǎigǎng, Cantonese hoi2 gong2, Japanese かいこう and Korean 해항 all use it, and Vietnamese hải cảng does too. It is a port on the open sea, where [[港湾]] is the harbor itself.
