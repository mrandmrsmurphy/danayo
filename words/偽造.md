## Notes
- [偽 (char)](../characters/偽%20(char).md) "false; artificial" + [造](../characters/造.md) "create"

偽造 is forgery: to make a false copy of a document, signature, banknote or the like and pass it as genuine. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin as "to forge, falsify, fabricate, counterfeit, fake" and Japanese ぎぞう as "forgery; falsification; fabrication; counterfeiting". Korean 위조 and Vietnamese nguỵ tạo ("fake") are the matching forms.

The word is literally "to create falsely". It differs from [偽善](偽善.md), false goodness, which is hypocrisy and not a legal wrong, and from [偽証](偽証.md), false testimony. The forger makes a thing, and the perjurer makes a statement.
