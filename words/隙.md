## Notes
- [隙 (char)](../characters/隙%20(char).md) "fissure, grudge" — this word is 隙's own stand-in.

隙 means a fissure, gap, or crack — extended figuratively to a rift or grudge between people (隙を生じる, "a rift develops"). Mandarin 隙 (xì), Cantonese 隙 (gwik1), Japanese 隙 (げき in compounds like 間隙 kangeki, "gap/interval"; natively すき, "a gap or opening," also "a chance/opportunity"), Korean 극 (geuk), and Sino-Vietnamese khích all preserve this range of senses.
