## Notes
- [弥 (char)](../characters/弥%20(char).md) "all the more; increasingly" + [漫](../characters/漫.md) "pervade; fill; go everywhere" — this word is the stand-in for [漫](../characters/漫.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

弥漫 means to pervade or spread throughout — smoke, mist, an atmosphere, or an emotion filling an entire space (不穏な空気が弥漫する, "an uneasy atmosphere pervades").

Korean 미만 and Vietnamese di mạn (verified — as in the set phrase 煙霧彌漫/yên vụ di mạn, "smoke and mist covering everywhere") are both standard readings. **`japanese: びまん` uses a lexicalized reading distinct from either character's own regular on'yomi** (弥's own MI, 漫's own MAN) — a real, independently attested word-level reading, not a copy-paste error. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄝㄇㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
