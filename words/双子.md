## Notes
- [双 (char)](../characters/双%20(char).md) "pair; double; even" + [子](../characters/子.md) "child; small suffix" — two children born at the same birth; a pair

The 子 suffix here functions as it does in 椅子 (chair) or 餃子 (dumpling): a noun-forming particle attached to a root that gains concreteness from it. 双子 is the ordinary Japanese written form of ふたご (twins), where it is read with the native reading, and the Sino-Japanese reading そうし is the bound one in the frontmatter. Mandarin more often says 雙胞胎 for twins in everyday speech, with 雙子 as the literary and astrological form. Identical twins are 一卵性双子, fraternal twins are 二卵性双子. In astrology, Gemini is 双子座. Dan'a'yo uses 双子 as its word for twin or twins, with no divergence from the shared sense.
