## Notes
- [姨](../characters/姨.md) "maternal aunt" + [母](../characters/母.md) "mother" — this word is the stand-in for its own character 姨, which cannot appear independently (母's own stand-in is [[母親]]).

姨母 means maternal aunt — one's mother's sister.

Mandarin yímǔ, Cantonese ji4 mou5, and Korean 이모 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese おば is the real native word for aunt (matching 姨's own native reading, rather than a compositional on'yomi いぼ). Vietnamese dì is the standard native term (trimmed the redundant "của mẹ," "of mother"). Reformatted `characters` to block-list. Omitted empty-list `aliases`/blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄧㄜㄇㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).
