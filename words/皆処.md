## Notes
- [皆 (char)](../characters/皆%20(char).md) "all; every; universal" + [処 (char)](../characters/処%20(char).md) "place; location"

皆処 is the collective (universal) correlative in the 処 ("place") column of the correlative table: "everywhere, all places, in every place, the places taken together". The character [皆 (char)](../characters/皆%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *gye*, ㄍ⼶) is the quantifier "all; every", the free word being [皆](皆.md); [処 (char)](../characters/処%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *co*, ㄑㄛ) is the Japanese shinjitai form of 處, which the character page lists as an alias along with 处, and it supplies "place; location". Where the distributive [毎処](毎処.md) goes through the places one at a time, 皆処 treats them as one totality: the predicate holds across the whole range of places at once. Its part of speech is 代詞 (pronoun), like the rest of the 皆 row ([皆事](皆事.md), [皆人](皆人.md), [皆物](皆物.md), [皆名](皆名.md), [皆時](皆時.md) and so on) and like [此処](此処.md), [其処](其処.md), [彼処](彼処.md), [何処](何処.md) and [某処](某処.md); only [毎処](毎処.md) in this column is adverbial (副用名詞). Illustrative only (invented, not from `grammar/`): 皆処、雪 "there is snow everywhere".

The aliases are the orthographic variants of the same word: 皆處 (traditional, via 處) and 皆处 (simplified); neither is a separate word file in the vault.

It contrasts with its column neighbours as follows. The distributive [毎処](毎処.md) "at each place" ranges over the places one by one, as [毎人](毎人.md) does against [皆人](皆人.md). [某処](某処.md) "somewhere" is the indefinite, naming an unspecified place (not a universal), and [何処](何処.md) "where" is the interrogative. The deictic cells [此処](此処.md) "here", [其処](其処.md) "there (medial)" and [彼処](彼処.md) "yonder" each point at one particular place, while 皆処 covers all of them. The 処 column is parallel to the temporal 時 column, so 皆処 pairs with [皆時](皆時.md) "at all times; always".

Cross-linguistic fields were checked against Wiktionary on 2026-10-11. Wiktionary has no entry for 皆處, 皆处 or 皆処, so no `mandarin`, `cantonese`, `japanese`, `korean` or `vietnamese` field is entered: the word is a Dan'a'yo compositional construction, and none of the readings below is an attested reading of the compound. The earlier fields (Mandarin dàochù, Cantonese dou3 cyu3, Japanese どこでも, Korean 어디에나) were real equivalents of the meaning, not readings of 皆処, and are removed for that reason. Established neighbours on Wiktionary: Chinese 到處 (simplified 到处; dàochù, Cantonese dou3 cyu3) is an adverb "everywhere"; 處處 (simplified 处处; chùchù, Cantonese cyu3 cyu3) is an adverb "everywhere; in all respects"; 各處 (simplified 各处; gèchù, Cantonese gok3 cyu3) is "each area; each region", literary "everywhere"; and Japanese 至る所 (itaru tokoro) is a noun "everywhere, throughout there". The Wiktionary entry for 到處 gives the Mandarin and Cantonese readings above but no further comment on its structure, and 至る所 is said there to derive from Literary Chinese 到處 (with an unresolved editor flag on whether to call it Literary or Classical). The vault has no word file for 到処, 處處 (処処) or 各処, so these are mentioned unlinked. From memory, not re-checked (Wiktionary lookup of Japanese どこでも returned no usable page on 2026-10-11): Japanese どこでも "anywhere; everywhere" (the earlier page cited the Doraemon どこでもドア), and Korean 어디에나 and 곳곳; these are idiomatic equivalents, not readings. Vietnamese is omitted: the `vietnamese` fields on both character pages are blank, and hvdic.thivien.net covers single characters, not phrases. Dan'a'yo keeps the sense shared across CJKV, "everywhere; in all places"; no narrowing is needed.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md)'s correlative table (line 126 is the 皆 ("all") row; the same chart is in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`, line 148 in the 処 ("place") row, with the column header cell 皆 on line 142), 皆処 is the fifth cell of the 皆 row, after [皆事](皆事.md) "all matters", [皆物](皆物.md) "all things", [皆人](皆人.md) "all people" and [皆名](皆名.md) "all honoured persons", and before [皆時](皆時.md) "all times", [皆様](皆様.md) "all manners", [皆多](皆多.md) "the whole amount" and [皆類](皆類.md) "all kinds". The table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), 63 forms, so 皆処 is parsed compositionally: 皆 = collective, 処 = place.

The spellings are the plain concatenation of the two character pages: 羅馬字 *gye* + *co* = gyeco, 諺文 겨 + 초 = 겨초, 注音 ㄍ⼶ + ㄑㄛ = ㄍ⼶ㄑㄛ, each checked letter by letter against 皆 (char) and 処 (char). The 注音 carries no syllable-boundary dot. `grammar/文法 - 02音韻論.md` allows only ㄇ ㄋ ㄫ, the glides and the unreleased stops ㄆ ㄊ ㄎ as syllable-final consonants, and ㄑ is none of them, so ㄍ⼶ㄑ is not a syllable and ㄑㄛ must be a syllable of its own (it exists in `syllables/`), leaving ㄍ⼶ as the preceding syllable (also in `syllables/`); there is no second complete partition. This is the same as [皆事](皆事.md), [毎処](毎処.md) and [此処](此処.md), and in contrast to [皆人](皆人.md), whose second syllable begins with ㄋ and so needs the dot.
