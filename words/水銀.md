## Notes
- [水 (char)](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water, liquid" + [銀 (char)](../characters/銀%20(char).md) "silver" — literally "water-silver," quicksilver, reflecting mercury's liquid metallic appearance.

Mandarin shuǐyín, Cantonese seoi2 ngan4, Japanese すいぎん (suigin), Korean 수은, and Vietnamese thủy ngân are all standard, fully compositional readings across the sphere, matching 水's and 銀's own stored citations exactly — the same "water-silver" logic is independently attested in every source language, not a Dan'a'yo-only coinage.

`kwin` is false: 水 alone is kwin-true, but 銀 independently diverges from Korean on its own character page, which alone is enough to make the AND-rule false. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨ·ㄧㄋ unique among both words and characters).
