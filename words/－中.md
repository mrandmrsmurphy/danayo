>[!info] Bound suffix
> －中 never appears alone: it always follows a verb.  It marks an action as continuous (-ing), and is one of the tense and aspect suffixes with 已 (perfective), 未 (negative perfective) and 将 (prospective).

## Notes
- [中](../characters/中.md) "middle; in the midst of" — as a suffix, "-ing"

－中 is the continuous aspect suffix in Dan'a'yo, "-ing; in the process of", postfixing to a verb to mark an action currently underway, complementing [[已]] (completed) and [[将]] (prospective). Wiktionary glosses the Chinese suffix sense of 中 as "while; in the process of; during; in the middle of; -ing". The unsuffixed character means "middle; center".
