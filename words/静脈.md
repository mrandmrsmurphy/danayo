## Notes
- [静](../characters/静.md) "still" + [脈](../characters/脈.md) "vessel; pulse"

静脈 is a vein: a vessel that carries blood back to the heart. Wiktionary glosses Japanese じょうみゃく as "a vein". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Vietnamese tĩnh mạch is the same word, and Korean 정맥 matches.

It is the "still" vessel, in contrast to the [動脈](動脈.md), the artery. Both are kinds of [血管](血管.md).
