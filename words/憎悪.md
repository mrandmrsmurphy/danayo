## Notes
- [憎](../characters/憎.md) "hate" + [悪](../characters/悪%20(char).md) "bad, evil" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [憎](../characters/憎.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (悪's own stand-in is itself).

憎悪 means to hate or loathe — intense dislike or hostility.

Mandarin zēngwù, Cantonese zang1 ok3, Japanese ぞうお, Korean 증오, and Vietnamese tăng ố are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `cantonese`/`vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄜㄫㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
