## Notes
- [当](../characters/当%20(char).md) "while" + [週](../characters/週.md) "week" — no stand-in relationship applies (当's own stand-in is itself; 週's own is [[週日]]).

当週 means the current week.

Mandarin dāngzhōu, Cantonese dong1 zau1, Japanese とうしゅう, Korean 당주, and Vietnamese đương chu are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄫㄐㄨㄛ unique across both words and characters).
