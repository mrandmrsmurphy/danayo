## Notes
- [転](../characters/転.md) "change, turn" + [化 (char)](../characters/化%20(char).md) "-ification, change" — stand-in for [[転]], which cannot appear independently

転化 is a near-synonym doubling: both components already mean "change, transform" on their own, combined into the standard word for a change or transformation of state or kind (Mandarin/Japanese biology and chemistry both use it for a substance changing form, e.g. metabolic conversion). Mandarin zhuǎnhuà, Cantonese zyun2 faa3, Japanese てんか (tenka), and Korean 전화 (jeonhwa) are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Added missing `vietnamese: chuyển hoá`**, verified via hvdic.thivien.net (traditional form 轉化) — a real, common Sino-Vietnamese compound, most familiar in the biological sense "metabolism" (chuyển hoá chất, "metabolism").
