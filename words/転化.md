## Notes
- [転](../characters/転.md) "change, turn" + [化](../characters/化.md) "-ification, change" — stand-in for [[転]], which cannot appear independently

転化 means to change or transform: for something to pass from one state or kind into another (轉化為, "turn into"; 轉化率, "conversion rate"; 能量轉化, "energy conversion"; 代謝轉化, "metabolic conversion"). 転化 is a near-synonym doubling: both components already mean "change, transform" on their own, combined into the standard word for a change or transformation of state or kind (Mandarin/Japanese biology and chemistry both use it for a substance changing form, e.g. metabolic conversion). Mandarin zhuǎnhuà, Cantonese zyun2 faa3, Japanese てんか (tenka), and Korean 전화 (jeonhwa) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese chuyển hoá (traditional form 轉化) is a real, common Sino-Vietnamese compound, most familiar in the biological sense "metabolism" (chuyển hoá chất, "metabolism").
