## Notes

Mandarin jiū, Cantonese gau2, Japanese きゅう, Korean 규, and Vietnamese củ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group with [[臼]] ("mortar; millstone; socket") and [[旧]] ("paleo-; former; old") — already fully cross-referenced from 臼's side.
