## Notes
- [言](../characters/言%20(char).md) "say" — the bare character used directly as a word, its own `stand_in`.

言 means to say: to speak or utter words (言語, "language"; 發言, "speak up"; 名言, "famous saying"; 言論, "speech, discourse"), and also a word or remark (一言, "a word"; 序言, "preface"). In classical Chinese it is also a particle, and as a measure of text it counts characters (五言詩, "five-character poetry"). The graph is classed 指事 in the vault, and as a radical (言, 訁) it heads characters concerning speech.

Mandarin yán, Cantonese jin4, Japanese げん (言語/げんご, "language"; 発言/はつげん, "statement") with the alternate on'yomi ごん (遺言/ゆいごん, "will, last words"), Korean 언 (언어, 발언) and Vietnamese ngôn (ngôn ngữ, "language"; ngôn luận, "discourse") are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native verb 言う (いう) for the everyday word, so on'yomi げん is bound to compounds; Korean has native 말.
