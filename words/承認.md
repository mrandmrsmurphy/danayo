## Notes
- [承](../characters/承.md) "receive, accept" + [認](../characters/認.md) "recognize, acknowledge" — "accept and acknowledge," to admit. Their own `stand_in` values: 承's is [[継承]]; 認's is [[認識]]; none points here, so 承認 is an independent compound.

承認 is to admit or acknowledge: to accept that something is so, or to approve it. Mandarin chéngrèn, Cantonese sing4 jing6, Japanese しょうにん and Korean 승인 all use it, and Vietnamese thừa nhận does too. It is the neutral "admit" of acknowledging a fact, where [[自白]] is a confession of wrongdoing.
