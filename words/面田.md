## Notes
- [面](../characters/面.md) "face" + [田](../characters/田.md) "field" — no `#cranberry`: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here (面's is [[表面]], 田's is [[田野]]). 面 and 田 here serve as phonetic transliteration characters, standing in for the real 緬甸's own 緬 and 甸.

Following the same convention as the 露-transliteration family (e.g. [[露国]]), `mandarin`/`cantonese`/`korean`/`japanese`/`vietnamese` hold the real reading of the actual word 緬甸/缅甸 rather than a literal compositional reading of 面田's own characters: Mandarin Miǎndiàn and Cantonese min5din6 are the standard modern names for Myanmar; Japanese めんでん (menden) is a real, historically attested reading (first recorded 1798), now largely superseded in writing by ミャンマー; Korean 면전 (myeonjeon) is likewise a real, dated Sino-Korean name (attested from at least 1930), superseded by 미얀마 since 1991; Sino-Vietnamese Miến Điện remains the standard, current Vietnamese name for the country.
