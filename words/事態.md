## Notes
- [事 (char)](../characters/事%20(char).md) "thing; action (stand-in: itself)" + [態](../characters/態.md) "manner; attitude (stand-in: [[態度]])"

Wiktionary: Chinese 事態 "situation (especially negative); state of affairs"; Japanese 事態 "situation (especially negative)"; Korean 사태 is the hanja form, "situation (especially negative)". It is the word for the state of things in an unfolding event, beside [[事件]].
