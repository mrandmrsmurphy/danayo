## Etymology
- [猿](../characters/猿.md) "ape, monkey" + [痘](../characters/痘.md) "pox, smallpox" — no stand-in relationship applies (猿's own stand-in is [[猿猩]]; 痘's own is [[痘痕]]). 猴 ("monkey") is a stored alias of 猿; the real term is written 猴痘, so `mandarin`/`cantonese` correctly use 猴's own real readings (hóu/hau4), matching the pattern already used on [[狐猿]].

猿痘 (real form 猴痘) means monkeypox/mpox.

Mandarin hóudòu and Cantonese hau4 dau6 were already correct, using 猴's alias-divergent readings. Japanese エムポックス (Mpox, the current WHO-adopted English loanword) and Korean 원숭이두창 ("monkey pox sore," a native+Sino hybrid) are the real, standard medical terms in their languages. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with đậu mùa khỉ ("monkey smallpox"), the real, standard Vietnamese medical term. Added missing `pos`(名詞). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/empty `aliases: []`. No homophones (注音 ㄛㄋㄉㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).
