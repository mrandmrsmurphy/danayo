## Notes
- [猿](../characters/猿.md) "ape, monkey" + [痘](../characters/痘.md) "pox, smallpox" — no stand-in relationship applies (猿's own stand-in is [[猿猩]]; 痘's own is [[痘痕]]). 猴 ("monkey") is a stored alias of 猿; the real term is written 猴痘, so `mandarin`/`cantonese` correctly use 猴's own real readings (hóu/hau4), matching the pattern already used on [[狐猿]].

猿痘 (the real form is 猴痘) means monkeypox, now called mpox.

Mandarin hóudòu and Cantonese hau4 dau6 use the readings of 猴, the stored alias of 猿. Japanese エムポックス (Mpox, the current WHO-adopted English loanword) and Korean 원숭이두창 ("monkey pox sore", a native-plus-Sino hybrid) are the standard medical terms in their languages. Vietnamese đậu mùa khỉ ("monkey smallpox") is the standard Vietnamese term.
