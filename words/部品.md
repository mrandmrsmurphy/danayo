## Notes
- [部 (char)](../characters/部%20(char).md) "section, part" + [品 (char)](../characters/品%20(char).md) "article, item" — literally "a part-item", a piece of a larger whole. No stand-in relationship applies (部's own `stand_in` is itself; 品's is itself).

Wiktionary: Chinese "part, component"; Japanese 部品 (ぶひん) "part, component", short for 部分品; Korean 부품 is the hanja form, "part, component". It is the generic word the vault uses for "component", for example in 部品同処 and 部品一律 in `文法 - 03文字法`, and by extension a sub-shape of a character.
