## Notes
- [部 (char)](../characters/部%20(char).md) "section, part" + [品 (char)](../characters/品%20(char).md) "article, item" — literally "a part-item", a piece of a larger whole. No stand-in relationship applies (部's own `stand_in` is itself; 品's is itself).

部品 is the general word for a part or component: a piece of a machine, a kit, or any assembled whole, and by extension a sub-shape of a character. It is the generic word the vault uses for "component", for example in 部品同処 and 部品一律 in the chapter on the writing system. It replaces the Chinese specialist term 部件, which Japanese and Korean do not use.

Wiktionary records Mandarin bùpǐn, Japanese ぶひん (buhin, "part, component", short for 部分品) and Korean 부품 (bupum) as "part, component". hvdic has no entry, so `vietnamese` is omitted. Cantonese bou6 ban2 is the compositional reading. Dan'a'yo's own reading 봇품 differs from Korean 부품 (部 is bou), so `kwin` is false.
