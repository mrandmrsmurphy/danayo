## Notes
- [臥 (char)](../characters/臥%20(char).md) "lie down, crouch" + [平](../characters/平.md) "even, flat"

Wiktionary has no entry for 臥平; it is the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平, which Wiktionary glosses, literally, "to lie flat" and, figuratively, as a neologism, "to be unmoved; to lose passion; to be unwilling to struggle; to quiet quit". 臥 is used for 躺 because it stays legible across the CJKV languages; Wiktionary glosses 臥 as "to lie down; to sleep; to rest; (of animals) to crouch". On the Efforts lexipedia page it sits under Laziness beside [[怠惰]] and [[懶惰]], but is not a synonym of either: it names a chosen disengagement, not idleness as a trait. In Dan'a'yo it keeps this narrower sense and should not be used for mere laziness.
