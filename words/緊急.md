## Notes
- [緊](../characters/緊.md) "tense; tight (stand-in: [[緊張]])" + [急 (char)](../characters/急%20(char).md) "quick; urgent (stand-in: itself)"

Wiktionary: Chinese 緊急 "urgent; emergency; pressing"; Japanese 緊急 "urgent, pressing", "emergency" and "urgency"; Korean 긴급 is the hanja form, "emergency"; Vietnamese khẩn cấp is the Han form, "emergency; critical, urgent". It is the word for a crisis that cannot wait, beside [[危机]].
