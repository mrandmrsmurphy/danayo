## Notes
- [傷](../characters/傷.md) "wound, injury" + [害](../characters/害.md) "harm" — "to wound, injure, assault" (physically or mentally), matching this compound's use in Mandarin, Japanese, and Korean.

**Genuine cross-linguistic register mismatch, disclosed rather than forced**: Vietnamese `thương hại` (confirmed via web search) has drifted to mean "pity, to feel sorry for" — a compassionate-but-somewhat-patronizing sentiment toward someone else's misfortune — rather than "to injure" itself. It is the same two characters and a directly attested reading, just a different sense than the one this word documents in its other four languages. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 샹해 diverges from Sino-Korean 상해 in the first syllable's coda — the same divergence shared with its homophone [[上海]].
