## Notes
- [傷](../characters/傷.md) "wound, injury" + [害](../characters/害.md) "harm" — "to wound, injure, assault" (physically or mentally), matching this compound's use in Mandarin, Japanese, and Korean.

**Genuine cross-linguistic register mismatch, disclosed rather than forced**: Vietnamese `thương hại` (confirmed via web search) has drifted to mean "pity, to feel sorry for" — a compassionate-but-somewhat-patronizing sentiment toward someone else's misfortune — rather than "to injure" itself. It is the same two characters and a directly attested reading, just a different sense than the one this word documents in its other four languages.

Legitimizing note: 傷's own `stand_in` is this exact word (傷害), so 傷 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 傷害.
