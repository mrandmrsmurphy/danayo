## Notes
- [恒](../characters/恒.md) "constant, ubiquitous" + [久](../characters/久%20(char).md) "long time (ago)" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [恒](../characters/恒.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (久's own stand-in is itself).

恒久 means permanent or perpetual — enduring without change over a long time.

Mandarin héngjiǔ, Cantonese hang4 gau2, Japanese こうきゅう, Korean 항구, and Vietnamese hằng cửu are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄨㄫㄍ⼜ unique across both words and characters).
