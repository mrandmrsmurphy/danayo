## Notes
- [三 (char)](../characters/三%20(char).md) "three" + [味](../characters/味.md) "taste" + [線](../characters/線.md) "line; string"

三味線 is a shamisen: the three-stringed Japanese plucked instrument. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin sānwèixiàn as "shamisen" and Japanese しゃみせん as ": the shepherd's purse,". Korean 삼미선 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese saam1 mei6 sin3 matches.

It accompanies the singing and acting of [歌舞伎](歌舞伎.md), kabuki, and is a plucked instrument like the [琵琶](琵琶.md), pipa.
