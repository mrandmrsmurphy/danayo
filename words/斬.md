## Notes
- [斬](../characters/斬%20(char).md) "cut, chop, sever" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [斬](../characters/斬%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

斬 means to cut, chop, or sever.

Mandarin zhǎn, Cantonese zaam2, Japanese ざん, Korean 참, and Vietnamese trảm are all standard, fully compositional readings. Homophone of [[蚕]] "silkworm" (both 注音 ㄐㄚㄇ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words; checked three other candidates sharing this reading (慙/暫/站) and confirmed none has an independent word at this exact reading.
