## Notes
- [君 (char)](../characters/君%20(char).md) "ruler, lord" + [臣](../characters/臣.md) "minister, subject" — "ruler and subject," the classical relationship of sovereign fealty. One of the 天常 (Five Cardinal/Constant Relationships of Confucian ethics, alongside father–son, husband–wife, elder–younger sibling, and friend–friend). `vietnamese: quân thần` is a directly attested, standard Confucian term for this exact relationship (one of the ngũ luân). `japanese: くんしん` reflects the on'yomi compound 君臣 (くんしん), used in classical/political contexts such as 君臣関係 "ruler-subject relationship." **Real gap found and fixed on [[君 (char)]]'s own page**: its `vietnamese` field crammed two readings into one malformed list item ("quân, vua") — split into separate list entries. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 군신 matches Sino-Korean 군신 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄨㄋㄙㄧㄋ unique among both words and characters).
