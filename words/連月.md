## Notes
- [連 (char)](characters/連%20(char).md) "consecutive, link" + [月 (char)](characters/月%20(char).md) "month, moon" — "month after month."

Mandarin liányuè, cantonese lin4 jyut6, japanese れんげつ, and korean 연월 are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. Vietnamese liên nguyệt is the honest compositional reading (連's own liên + 月's own nguyệt); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 련웓 diverges from Sino-Korean 연월 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄌ⼶ㄋ⼔ㄊ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 連's `stand_in` (連 itself) nor 月's own `stand_in` points to this compound.
