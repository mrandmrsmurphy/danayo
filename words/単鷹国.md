## Notes
- [単](../characters/単.md) "single" + [鷹](../characters/鷹%20(char).md) "eagle" + [国](../characters/国.md) "nation" — a Dan'a'yo coinage, "single-eagle nation," for Prussia. No stand-in relationship applies (単's own stand-in is [[簡単]]; 鷹 is its own stand-in; 国's is [[国家]]).

単鷹国 names Prussia, the historical German kingdom whose coat of arms bore a single-headed eagle (as opposed to the double-headed eagle of Austria/the Holy Roman Empire).

Since Prussia is a historical foreign polity, the real-world CJKV fields are phonetic loanwords rather than compositional readings: Japanese プロイセン and Korean 프로이센 both transliterate German *Preußen*, and Vietnamese Phổ Lỗ Sĩ is the traditional Sino-Vietnamese phonetic transliteration of "Prussia" — all genuine, expected divergences for this kind of neologism (`kwin: false` as expected). Fixed `characters` citation "鷹" → "鷹 (char)". No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄋㄧㄫㄍㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
