## Notes
- [主](../characters/主.md) "main, governing" + [題](../characters/題.md) "title, subject" — the governing subject of a piece of writing, speech, work, or discussion.

主題 covers "topic, theme, subject, motif" — the central idea or focus a piece of writing, music, or conversation is organized around, distinct from [[主旨]]'s narrower sense of "main point/gist" and [[主格]]/[[主語]]'s grammatical use of 主. Mandarin zhǔtí, Cantonese zyu2 tai4, Japanese しゅだい, Korean 주제, and Vietnamese chủ đề are all standard, fully compositional readings and the ordinary everyday word for "topic/theme" in each language: 主題曲 (theme song), 主題公園 (theme park), 討論主題 (discussion topic).

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 주테 diverges from Sino-Korean 주제 in the second syllable — 題 reads 테 (tei/ㄊㅔㅣ) in Dan'a'yo but 제 (je) in Sino-Korean.
