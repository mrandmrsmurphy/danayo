## Notes
- [合 (char)](../characters/合%20(char).md) "join; together" + [奏](../characters/奏.md) "play (music)"

合奏 is to play together, or an ensemble: instruments played by a group. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin hézòu as "to memorialize an emperor" and Japanese がっそう as "musical ensemble". Korean 합주 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese hap6 zau3 matches.

It is the instrumental counterpart of the [合唱](合唱.md), and the opposite of the [独奏](独奏.md), solo. The big form is the [管弦楽](管弦楽.md), orchestra.
