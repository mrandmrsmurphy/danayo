## Notes
- [遭](characters/遭.md) "encounter, meet with" + [遇 (char)](characters/遇%20(char).md) "meet, encounter" — "encounter."

Mandarin zāoyù, cantonese zou1 jyu6, japanese そうぐう, korean 조우, and vietnamese tao ngộ are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 잣우 diverges from Sino-Korean 조우 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄨ·ㄨ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 遭's own `stand_in` is this exact word (遭遇), so 遭 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 遭遇; 遇's own `stand_in` is instead 遇 itself, so this word does not legitimize 遇.
