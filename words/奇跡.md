## Notes
- [奇](../characters/奇.md) "strange; rare" + [跡](../characters/跡.md) "trace; footprint"

奇跡 is a miracle: an event that cannot be explained by ordinary means, or a marvel. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin qíjī as "miracle; wonder; marvel", and Vietnamese kì tích is the same word in Hán Việt form. Japanese きせき, Korean 기적 and Cantonese kei4 zik1 match.

It may be religious or simply surprising. The religious sense belongs with [神](神.md) and [宗教](宗教.md), and the unexplained sense is the edge of [超自然](超自然.md), the supernatural.
