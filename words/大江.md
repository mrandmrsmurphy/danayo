## Notes
- [大](../characters/大%20(char).md) "great" + [江](../characters/江.md) "river" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [江](../characters/江.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (大's own stand-in is itself).

大江 means a big river — especially the Yangtze (長江), for which 大江 is a traditional alternate name. In Japanese it is also a common surname (Ōe, おおえ), read with native kun'yomi rather than the Sino-Japanese on'yomi compound たいこう.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `japanese` used the archaic pre-reform kana spelling おほえ instead of modern おおえ. Filled blank `vietnamese: đại giang` (verified). Korean 대강, Mandarin dàjiāng, and Cantonese daai6 gong1 are all standard readings, matching Dan'a'yo's own reading exactly (`kwin: true`). **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 大江 and [[大綱]] ("outline") share an identical reading — standardized the non-canonical `[!tip]` callout into the proper reciprocal `>[!warning] Homophones` format.
