## Notes
- [鯖](../characters/鯖.md) "mackerel" + [魚 (char)](../characters/魚%20(char).md) "fish" — legitimizing note: 鯖's own `stand_in` is [[鯖魚]] itself, already annotated in its own citation; 魚's own `stand_in` is 魚 itself, so transitivity fails — no `#cranberry`.

Mandarin qīngyú and Cantonese cing3jyu4-2 keep the compositional Sino reading. Japanese さば (saba), Korean 고등어 (godeungeo), and Vietnamese cá thu are all native, everyday words for "mackerel" rather than Sino-Japanese/Sino-Korean/Sino-Vietnamese readings.
