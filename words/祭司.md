## Notes
- [祭](../characters/祭.md) "sacrifice, ritual" + [司](../characters/司.md) "manage, officer" — "ritual officer," a priest. Their own `stand_in` values: 祭's is [[祭典]]; 司's is [[公司]]; none points here, so 祭司 is an independent compound.

祭司 is a priest: a person who performs the rituals and offerings of a religion. Mandarin jìsī, Cantonese zai3 si1 and Japanese さいし are attested; Korean says 사제 for a priest, so that field holds the character reading. It is the generic, non-denominational word for a priest, where [[僧侶]] is specifically a Buddhist monk. Compare [[祭祀]] (sacrifice, the rite) and [[神巫]] (shaman).
