## Notes
- [凸 (char)](../characters/凸%20(char).md) "convex; protruding"

凸 is a 象形 pictograph of a raised, protruding surface, and as a word it means "convex; protruding." It is the direct opposite of [[凹]] ("concave, dented"); the pair combine as [[凹凸]] "bumpy, uneven, rugged."

Mandarin tū, Cantonese dat6 and Vietnamese đột preserve the sense directly. Japanese とつ (TOTSU, on'yomi) appears in compounds, alongside the native でこ (deko), a protrusion or bump. Korean 철 (cheol) is the Sino-xenic reading.
