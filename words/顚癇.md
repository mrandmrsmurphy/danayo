## Notes
- [顚](../characters/顚.md) "summit, peak, epilepsy" + [癇](../characters/癇.md) "epilepsy" — `#cranberry`: transitivity holds, as both 顚's own `stand_in` and 癇's own `stand_in` point here to 顚癇 itself (A=B=AB); neither character can stand alone.

顚癇/癲癇 (diānxián) is the standard Mandarin word for "epilepsy," and Cantonese 顚癇 (din1haan4) likewise. Japanese てんかん (tenkan) is the standard medical term. Korean 간질 (ganjil) is likewise the standard medical term; the compositional Sino form 전간 survives mainly as a dated/formal synonym, while the colloquial 땡깡 — though etymologically borrowed directly from Japanese てんかん — has shifted entirely away from the medical sense to mean "a tantrum, an unreasonable fit" and is now considered a discouraged Japanese-origin slang term, not interchangeable with 간질.

Sino-Vietnamese điên giản is compositionally exact but not the standard medical term — modern Vietnamese instead uses the native động kinh for epilepsy.
