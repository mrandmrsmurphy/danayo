## Notes
- [賃](../characters/賃%20(char).md) "rent" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

賃 means rent, or payment for the use of something — originally "wages, payment for labor," extended to "to rent, hire."

Mandarin lìn, Cantonese jam6, Japanese ちん, Korean 임, and Vietnamese nhẫm are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄋㄧㄇ/nim/님) with the unrelated [[妊]] ("be pregnant").
