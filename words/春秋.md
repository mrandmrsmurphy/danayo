## Notes
- [春](../characters/春%20(char).md) "springtime" + [秋](../characters/秋%20(char).md) "autumn" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 春 and 秋's own stand-ins are themselves).

Mandarin chūnqiū, Cantonese ceon1 cau1, Japanese しゅんじゅう, Korean 춘추, and Vietnamese Xuân Thu are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄨㄋㄑㄨㄛ unique across both words and characters).

## Definition
1. Spring and autumn
2. short for the whole year - 春夏秋冬
3. a year, one's age
4. The Spring and Autumn Period, fully [[春秋時代]] (770–476 BC)
5. The Spring and Autumn Annals, part of [[四書五経]]
	1. also called [[麟経]]/麟經 or [[麟史]]
	2. 呂氏春秋
	3. 晏子春秋
	4. 春秋三傳
### Chengyu
1. [[春秋鼎盛]] - the prime of life
2. 春秋大夢
3. 春秋筆法
4. 春秋大一統
