## Notes
- [東](characters/東.md) "east" + [夷](characters/夷.md) "barbarian" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [夷](characters/夷.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 東's own stand-in is [[東方]].

東夷 means "Eastern barbarian" — the historical Sinocentric exonym for peoples east of ancient China (Korea, Japan, and other coastal regions).

Mandarin dōngyí, Cantonese dung1 ji4, Japanese とうい, Korean 동이, and Vietnamese đông di are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled the blank `japanese`/`vietnamese` fields, removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/`aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄫㄧㄜ unique across both words and characters).
