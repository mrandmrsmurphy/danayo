## Notes
- [艾](../characters/艾.md) "mugwort; wormwood" + [草 (char)](../characters/草%20(char).md) "grass; herb"

艾草 is mugwort: the plant *Artemisia*, especially Chinese mugwort, *Artemisia argyi*, whose leaves are used to make moxa for [[艾灸]], moxibustion. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 艾草 (Mandarin àicǎo, Cantonese ngaai6 cou2) as "Chinese mugwort". It has no Japanese or Korean entry for the compound. For the bare 艾 it gives Japanese よもぎ (also もぐさ, moxibustion), Korean "moxa; mugwort" (the native word is 쑥), and Vietnamese ngải, "various plants of the genus Artemisia", so Japanese がいそう and Korean 애초 here are built from the characters.

This word is 艾's own `stand_in`, since 艾 is too bounded to stand alone. The plant is a [[草]], grass or herb.
