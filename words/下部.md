>[!caution] The senses split — lower part, or a euphemism for the genitals
>下部 is the lower part in Chinese, Japanese and Korean, and the Dan'a'yo sense. Chinese also uses it as a euphemism for the genital region, and Vietnamese hạ bộ means the external male genitalia, so a listener may hear that. Listed as a Split on [文法 - 91疑義語](../grammar/文法%20-%2091疑義語.md).

## Notes
- [下 (char)](../characters/下%20(char).md) "below (stand-in: itself)" + [部 (char)](../characters/部%20(char).md) "part; section (stand-in: itself)"

Wiktionary: Chinese 下部 "lower part; lower portion; substructure", euphemistically "genitalia region", and "the last part (in a television or book series)"; Japanese 下部 "lower part"; Korean 하부 is the hanja form, "lower part"; Vietnamese hạ bộ is glossed "external male genitalia", so the `vietnamese` field is omitted. Its counterpart is [[上部]].
