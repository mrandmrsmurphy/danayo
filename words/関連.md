## Notes
- [関 (char)](../characters/関%20(char).md) "gate; relate (stand-in: itself)" + [連 (char)](../characters/連%20(char).md) "connect (stand-in: itself)"

Wiktionary: Chinese 關聯 "to be related; to be connected; to be linked" and, in computing, "to associate"; Japanese 関連 "relation" and "to be related"; Korean 관련 is the hanja form, "tie-in". The spelling 關聯 is the alias. It fills the "relate" gap, beside [[関係]].
