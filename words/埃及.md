## Notes
- [埃](../characters/埃.md) "dust" + [及](../characters/及%20(char).md) "reach, and" — a phonetic transcription of "Egypt." No stand-in relationship applies (埃's own stand-in is [[塵埃]]; 及 is its own stand-in).

埃及 (Egypt) is the country in North Africa.

Japanese エジプト is a katakana phonetic loan for the international name, while Korean 애굽, Mandarin Āijí, Cantonese aai1 kap6, and Vietnamese Ai Cập are all standard readings, matching Dan'a'yo's own reading exactly (`kwin: true`). Fixed empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄧㄍㄨㄆ unique across both words and characters).
