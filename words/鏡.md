## Notes
- [鏡](../characters/鏡%20(char).md) "mirror, lens" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 鏡 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo. Its phonetic component 竟 is also the source of this genuine Dan'a'yo homophone.

鏡 names a mirror or, by extension, an optical lens. It is highly productive: 眼鏡 ("glasses, eyeglasses"), 三稜鏡 ("prism"), and the chengyu 鏡花水月 ("flower in a mirror, moon on water" — an unattainable illusion or fleeting beauty).

Mandarin jìng, Cantonese geng3, and Korean 경 (gyeong) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading かがみ (kagami, "mirror") rather than the on-readings キョウ/ケイ (KYOU/KEI) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese gương is the standard reading and the ordinary everyday Vietnamese word for "mirror."
