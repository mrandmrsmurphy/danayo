## Notes
- [舞](../characters/舞.md) "dance" + [踊](../characters/踊.md) "leap"

舞踊 is dance as an art: a formal or stage dance. Wiktionary glosses Japanese ぶよう as "dance". Korean 무용 is the same word. The Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the characters.

It is the art word for [跳舞](跳舞.md), to dance, and a [舞妓](舞妓.md) is a dancer by trade.
