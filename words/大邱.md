## Notes
- [大](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big" + [邱](../characters/邱.md) "hill, mound (surname)" — the name of Daegu, the inland city of southeastern Korea.

大邱 (Daegu) is the main city of the southeast inland basin, in the old territory of 慶尚北道 (see [[慶尚]]). Its full modern name is 大邱廣域市 (대구광역시), "Daegu Metropolitan City", a separate unit since 1981, first as a 直轄市, and renamed 廣域市 in 1995 (from memory, not re-checked). The older spelling 大丘 is kept as the alias: the second character was reportedly changed from 丘 to 邱 in 1767 to avoid writing the personal name of Confucius (孔丘), the pattern also seen in the Qing taboo of adding 阝 to 丘 (from memory, not re-checked). The vault treats 邱 as a character in its own right, a name character, and not as an alias of 丘.

English, Chinese, Japanese and Korean Wiktionary have 大邱 (Chinese Dàqiū, daai6 jau1; Korean 대구, which Japanese gives as the modern Japanese name テグ, with たいきゅう as the dated on'yomi reading), and English Wiktionary has the Vietnamese Đại Khâu. These are the readings used here. No homophone shares the 注音.
