## Notes
- [少 (char)](../characters/少%20(char).md) "few; young" + [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 少's and 年's own `stand_in` fields point to themselves).

少年 is a boy or young male — the direct male counterpart to [[少女]], both using 少's "young" sense. It also names the "shōnen" demographic/genre label in Japanese media, targeting young male readers/viewers, alongside 少女 for the young-female equivalent.

Japanese しょうねん, Korean 소년, and Vietnamese thiếu niên are all standard, non-drifted readings. No HSK level is given for the compound. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄛㄨㄋㄝㄋ unique across both words and characters).
