## Notes
- [景](../characters/景.md) "scenery, scene" + [致](../characters/致%20(char).md) "cause, sense" — no stand-in relationship applies (景's own stand-in is [[景色]]; 致's own is itself).

景致 means scenery, scene, or view.

Mandarin jǐngzhì, Cantonese ging2 zi3, Japanese けいち, Korean 경치, and Vietnamese cảnh trí (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed missing space in `cantonese`. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼶ㄫㄑㄧㄜ unique across both words and characters).
