## Notes
- [冷 (char)](../characters/冷%20(char).md) "cool; cold"

冷 is 形声: semantic 冫 ("ice") + phonetic [[令]] — "cold as ice," extended figuratively to "desolate, indifferent, unpopular" (冷淡, "cold, indifferent"; 冷門, "an unpopular/overlooked subject or candidate"). It heads everyday compounds like [[冷蔵庫]] ("refrigerator") and [[冷麺]] ("cold noodles").

Mandarin lěng, Cantonese laang5, and Japanese れい (rei, on'yomi, fully productive: 冷蔵庫 れいぞうこ "refrigerator") alongside native つめ(たい) (tsume(tai), "cold [to the touch]," the everyday adjective) all preserve the sense. Korean has two readings depending on position: 랭 (raeng, the reading proper to this vault's standing North Korean convention, used word-medially/finally as in 寒冷 한랭) and 냉 (naeng, the South Korean 두음법칙-shifted form used word-initially, as in 冷蔵庫 냉장고, 冷麺 냉면 — the same two compounds already listed on the character page). Vietnamese lãnh is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in lãnh đạm ("cold, indifferent") and the historical term lãnh cung ("cold palace," where disfavored concubines were banished); the everyday native word for "cold" is lạnh, phonetically close but not itself the Hán Việt reading.
