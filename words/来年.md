## Notes
- [来](../characters/来%20(char).md) "come" + [年](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 来 and 年's own stand-ins are themselves, each with its own independent word page).

来年 means next year.

Mandarin láinián, Cantonese loi4 nin4, and Japanese らいねん are all standard, fully compositional readings.
