## Notes
- [刀](../characters/刀.md) "blade, knife" + [剣 (char)](../characters/剣%20(char).md) "sword" — "sword, dagger, knife" (a general term for edged weapons). Mandarin dāojiàn, Cantonese dou1 gim3, Japanese とうけん, Korean 도검, and Vietnamese đao kiếm are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 탓곰 diverges from Sino-Korean 도검 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄚㄨㄍㄛㄇ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 刀's own `stand_in` is this exact word (刀剣), so 刀 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 刀剣.
