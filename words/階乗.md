## Notes
- [階](../characters/階.md) "step, rank" + [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "multiply"

階乗 is the factorial, written *n*!, the product of the positive integers up to *n*. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 階乘 (alias here; Mandarin jiēchéng, Cantonese gaai1 sing4), marked combinatorics, and Japanese かいじょう as "factorial", and Korean 계승 and Vietnamese giai thừa as "factorial".
