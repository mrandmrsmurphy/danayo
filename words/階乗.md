## Notes
- [階](../characters/階.md) "step, rank" + [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "multiply" — the two characters together describe successive ("step-by-step, rank-by-rank") multiplication, a compositionally apt name for the operation.

The factorial of *n* (written *n*!) is the product of all positive integers up to *n* (5! = 5×4×3×2×1 = 120), fundamental to combinatorics — counting permutations and combinations — and to definitions in calculus and probability (e.g. the terms of a Taylor series). 0! is defined as 1 by convention, not left undefined.

Japanese かいじょう and Korean 계승 are both standard compositional Sino-readings. Vietnamese giai thừa is the standard, widely-used mathematical term (already correctly present). No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼶ㄧㄙㄨㄫ unique across both words and characters).
