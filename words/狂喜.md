## Notes
- [狂](../characters/狂.md) "mad; wild" + [喜 (char)](../characters/喜%20(char).md) "joy"

狂喜 is ecstasy: joy so great that it is wild, beyond the control of the one who feels it. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin kuángxǐ as "ecstatic; rapt; overjoyed" and Japanese きょうき as "ecstasy, raptures; to exult". Cantonese kong4 hei2 matches, and Korean 광희 is the reading of the characters.

It is the top of the joy words, past [痛快](痛快.md), exhilaration, and [歓喜](歓喜.md), joy, and its bodily form is the leaping of [踊躍](踊躍.md). Its mood is the opposite of [悲痛](悲痛.md).
