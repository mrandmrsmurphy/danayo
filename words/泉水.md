## Notes
- [泉](../characters/泉.md) "spring" + [水](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" — "spring water." Their own `stand_in` values: 泉's is [[源泉]]; 水's is itself; none points here, so 泉水 is an independent compound.

泉水 is spring water, and by extension a spring: a place where water comes naturally out of the ground. Mandarin quánshuǐ and Cantonese cyun4 seoi2 are the ordinary Chinese words, and Japanese せんすい is the same word. Korean 천수 holds the readings of the characters. The season "spring" is [[春]], an unrelated homograph in English only. Compare [[温泉]] (hot spring).
