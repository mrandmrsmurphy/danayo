## Notes
- [意](../characters/意.md) "meaning; significance" + [思](../characters/思.md) "think" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (意's is 意味, 思's is 思考).

Mandarin yìsi, Japanese いし, and Korean 의사 are the standard readings. Cantonese ji3 si3 (rather than ji3 si1) is a genuine, documented changed-tone pronunciation. Vietnamese ý tứ is a real word with its own dictionary entry.
