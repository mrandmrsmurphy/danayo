## Notes
- [和](../characters/和.md) "harmony" + [平](../characters/平.md) "level, peace" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [和](../characters/和.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (平's own stand-in is [[水平]]).

和平 means peace or peaceful — the absence of conflict, especially between nations.

Japanese わへい is the correct reading for this specific character order (distinct from へいわ, the reading for the reversed compound 平和, listed here as an alias but genuinely a separate, more common word in real Japanese/Chinese — わへい survives in more formal/classical contexts such as 和平交渉 "peace negotiations"). Korean 화평 and Vietnamese hoà bình are both standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⺢ㄅ⼶ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
