## Notes
- [由 (char)](../characters/由%20(char).md) "from" + [鼠](../characters/鼠.md) "rat" — a 代用字 form: 由 is 鼬 without its 鼠 radical, standing in for the rare character 鼬 of the real compound 鼬鼠, which is not in the vault.

由鼠 is the weasel, written for 鼬鼠 (Mandarin yòushǔ, Cantonese jau6 syu2), which Wiktionary glosses "weasel." Japanese has the animal as 鼬 (いたち, itachi), with 鼬鼠 as an alternative spelling. The same device gives [皇虫](皇虫.md) for the locust and [青廷](青廷.md) for the dragonfly.
