## Notes
- [虚 (char)](../characters/虚%20(char).md) "empty; void" + [構](../characters/構.md) "build; construct"

虚構 is fiction in the sense of something made up: a story or claim built without a basis in fact, and the act of building it. Wiktionary glosses Japanese きょこう as "fabrication; fiction; to fabricate, invent, make up". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 허구 matches.

It is the opposite of [現実](現実.md), reality, and is close to [架空](架空.md), imaginary, which names a thing as unreal where 虚構 stresses that it was constructed. Its falsehood is distinct from [虚偽](虚偽.md), a lie, which is told to deceive.
