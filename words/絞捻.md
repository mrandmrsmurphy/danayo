## Notes
- [絞](../characters/絞.md) "twist" + [捻 (char)](../characters/捻%20(char).md) "pinch; spin" — stand-in for [[絞]], which cannot appear independently

絞捻 is a near-synonym doubling: both components already mean "twist, wring" on their own, combined into the standard word for twisting or wringing (a rope, wet cloth, thread) or squeezing by twisting motion. 絞 is 形声, semantic 糸 ("thread") + phonetic 交 ("cross, intersect" — the crossing motion of twisting strands together); 捻 is likewise built on a hand-action semantic with its own phonetic component.

Mandarin jiǎoniǎn, Cantonese gaau2 nip6, Japanese こうねん, and Korean 교념 (gyonyeom) are all fully compositional readings. 絞捻 is a compound used in the vault rather than a common word in the source languages. No Sino-Vietnamese compound is dictionary-attested for this pairing, so no Vietnamese reading is recorded. Japanese uses the native verbs 絞る (しぼる) and 捻る (ひねる) for the everyday actions.
