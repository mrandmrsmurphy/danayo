## Notes
- [声 (char)](../characters/声.md) "sound, voice" (stand-in: [[発声]]) + [門 (char)](../characters/門.md) "gate" (stand-in: [[大門]]) — the glottis, the space between the vocal folds.

声門 is the glottis. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 聲門 (Mandarin shēngmén, Cantonese seng1 mun4) as the anatomy term "glottis", Japanese せいもん as "glottis", and lists Korean 성문 as a hanja form of "glottis". The Vietnamese thanh môn field is built from the readings of the two characters. [文法 - 02音韻論](../grammar/文法%20-%2002音韻論.md) uses it in 声門擦音 and 声門塞音. Compare [擦音](擦音.md), the fricative, and [塞音](塞音.md), the stop.
