## Notes
- [声 (char)](../characters/声.md) "sound, voice" (stand-in: [[発声]]) + [門 (char)](../characters/門.md) "gate" (stand-in: [[大門]]) — the glottis, the space between the vocal folds.

声門 means the glottis — the place of articulation for the glottal stop and /h/. Mandarin shēngmén, Japanese せいもん, Korean 성문, and Vietnamese thanh môn are all real, standard anatomical/phonetics terms, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Cantonese sing1 mun4 is a straightforward compositional reading. `kwin` is false: both 声 and 門 are individually false.

No syllable-boundary dot is needed: the concatenated string `ㄙㄧㄫㄇㄛㄋ` admits only one legal partition.
