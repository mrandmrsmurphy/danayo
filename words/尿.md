## Notes
- [尿 (char)](../characters/尿%20(char).md) "urine; urinate" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [尿 (char)](../characters/尿%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

尿 originally depicted 尾 ("tail") + 水 ("water") in seal script — urine, and by extension the act of urinating.

Japanese にょう, Korean 뇨, and Vietnamese niệu are all standard readings, mostly surviving in medical/compound registers (尿道 "urethra," 尿意 "urge to urinate") rather than everyday standalone speech, which favors more colloquial native terms in each language. No homophones among other words (注音 ㄋ⼘ㄨ; the unrelated character [[閙]] "noisy" shares the same reading, but its own stand-in word doesn't collide with 尿's).
