## Notes
- [美](../characters/美%20(char).md) "America, beautiful" + [圓](../characters/圓%20(char).md) "dollar, round" — "American round," the dollar. Their own `stand_in` values: 美's is itself; 圓's is itself; none points here, so 美圓 is an independent compound.

美圓 is the US dollar. Mandarin měiyuán and Cantonese mei5 jyun4 are the ordinary Chinese words, usually written 美元 (kept as an alias); the unit is 圓 "round," as in [[欧圓]] (euro). Japanese says ドル and Korean 달러, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[日圓]] (yen) and [[韓圓]] (won).
