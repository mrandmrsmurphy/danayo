## Notes
- [成](../characters/成%20(char).md) "become" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language" — no stand-in relationship applies (成's own stand-in is itself; 語's own is [[言語]]).

成語 means chengyu, the traditional four-character idiomatic sayings.

Mandarin chéngyǔ, Cantonese sing4 jyu5, Japanese せいご, Korean 성어, and Vietnamese thành ngữ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Removed `四字熟語` from `aliases` — that is a distinct Japanese synonym term (a different character composition), not an orthographic variant of this word; kept `成语` (genuine simplified-script alias). No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄫ⼄ unique across both words and characters).
