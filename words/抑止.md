## Notes
- [抑](../characters/抑.md) "repress, press down" + [止](../characters/止.md) "stop" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [抑](../characters/抑.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (止's own stand-in is [[中止]]).

抑止 means to repress or restrain.

Mandarin yìzhǐ, Cantonese jik1 zi2, Japanese よくし, Korean 억지, and Vietnamese ức chỉ are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄧㄎㄐㄧ unique across both words and characters).
