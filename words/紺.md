## Notes
- [紺](../characters/紺%20(char).md) "navy blue" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 紺 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

紺 names navy blue — a dark purplish-blue dye color, as in 紺色 (navy blue) and 紺青 (Prussian blue).

Mandarin gàn, Cantonese gam3, and Korean 감 (gam) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the on-reading こん (KON, as in 紺色 "navy blue") for the bare character alone, since no native kun-reading is stored. Vietnamese cám is one of several stored candidates.
