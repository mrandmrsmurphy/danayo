Mandarin wánjù, Cantonese wun6 geoi6, Japanese がんぐ (GAN+GU), and Korean 완구 were already correct and fully compositional. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with ngoạn cụ (玩's own ngoạn + 具's own cụ). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/empty `aliases: []`.
