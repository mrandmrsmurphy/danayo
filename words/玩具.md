## Notes
- [玩](../characters/玩.md) "toy" + [具](../characters/具.md) "tool, implement" — 玩's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 具's own is [[工具]]; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 玩 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

玩具 means toy.

Mandarin wánjù, Cantonese wun6 geoi6, Japanese がんぐ, Korean 완구 and Vietnamese ngoạn cụ (玩's own ngoạn plus 具's own cụ) are all fully compositional.
