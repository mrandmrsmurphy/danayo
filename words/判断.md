## Notes
- [判](../characters/判.md) "judge, decide, determine" + [断](../characters/断.md) "cut off" — "to judge, decide, determine" (判断力, "power of judgment"; 判断ミス, "an error of judgment"). Mandarin pànduàn, Cantonese pun3 dyun3, Japanese はんだん, Korean 판단, and Vietnamese phán đoán are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 판돤 diverges from Sino-Korean 판단 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄚㄋㄉ⺢ㄋ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 判's own `stand_in` is this exact word (判断), so 判 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 判断.
