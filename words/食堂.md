## Notes
- [食 (char)](../characters/食%20(char).md) "eat" + [堂](../characters/堂.md) "meeting hall" — no `#cranberry`: 食's own `stand_in` is 食 itself, 堂's is [[会堂]]; neither points here.

食堂 (shítáng) is the standard Mandarin word for "dining hall, canteen," and Cantonese 食堂 (sik6tong4) likewise. Japanese しょくどう (shokudō) and Korean 식당 (sikdang) are both standard everyday words for "restaurant/cafeteria" in their languages.

Sino-Vietnamese thực đường is compositionally straightforward but not independently attested — Vietnamese instead uses the native nhà ăn ("eating house") or the loanword căng tin ("canteen").
