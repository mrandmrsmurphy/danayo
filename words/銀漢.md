## Notes
- [銀 (char)](../characters/銀%20(char).md) "silver" + [漢](../characters/漢.md) "Han river"

銀漢 is the Milky Way in the literary register: the "silver Han", a river across the sky. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yínhàn as "Milky Way (literary)" and Japanese ぎんかん as a synonym of 銀河系. Cantonese ngan4 hon3 matches, and Korean 은한 is the reading of the characters.

The ordinary word is [銀河](銀河.md). In the legend of [牛郎](牛郎.md) and [織女](織女.md) it is the river that parts the two lovers.
