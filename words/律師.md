## Notes
- [律](../characters/律.md) "statute; principle; regulation" + [師](../characters/師.md) "expert"

律師 is a lawyer or attorney: one who is expert in the law. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin lǜshī and Cantonese leot6 si1 as "lawyer; attorney", and Vietnamese luật sư is the same word in Hán Việt form.

Japanese and Korean use 弁護士 / 변호사 for a lawyer, and the word is a trap for Japanese readers. Wiktionary gives Japanese りっし only as a Buddhist term: a monk well-versed in the canon, and the third-highest rank of prelate. A Japanese speaker will not read it as "lawyer", where Chinese and Vietnamese speakers will. The Korean reading 률사 is compositional, since Korean uses 변호사 for a lawyer. The first character 律 is the one in [法律](法律.md), so the word parses as "expert in statute".
