## Notes
- [各 (char)](../characters/各%20(char).md) "each; individually"

各 is 会意: 夂 ("sole of a foot") + [[口]] ("mouth, opening") — originally "to come, to arrive" (the ancestor form of 𢓜 and [[格]]), later narrowed to the distributive sense "each, every, individually." It appears in [[各種]] ("every kind, all kinds") and [[各位]] ("ladies and gentlemen," a respectful plural address).

Mandarin gè, Cantonese gok3, and Japanese かく (kaku, on'yomi, fully productive: 各種 かくしゅ "various kinds") alongside native おのおの (onoono, "each, respectively," the everyday distributive word) all preserve the sense. Korean 각 (gak) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 각각 (gakgak, "each, respectively," built on the same Sino-Korean root doubled). Vietnamese các is the Hán Việt reading, extremely well attested as the everyday plural/distributive marker (các bạn, "everyone, all of you"; các nước, "various countries"); the character page's other four stored candidates (cắc, gác, gạc, gật) are unrelated and look like corpus noise.

This word is part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read gag/각/ㄍㄚㄎ): [[格]] ("case," still awaiting its own turn) and [[隔]] ("every other," already perfected — its own callout has been added alongside this iteration).
