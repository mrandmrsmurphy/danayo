## Notes
- [弾](../characters/弾.md) "spring, elastic" + [性](../characters/性.md) "nature" — "springing nature," elasticity. Their own `stand_in` values: 弾's is [[弾丸]]; 性's is [[性別]]; none points here, so 弾性 is an independent compound.

弾性 is elasticity: the property of springing back after being stretched or squeezed. Mandarin tánxìng (彈性) and Cantonese daan6 sing3 are the ordinary words, and Japanese だんせい and Korean 탄성 are the same compound. Compare [[慣性]] (inertia) and [[力]] (force).
