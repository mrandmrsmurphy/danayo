## Notes
- [第 (char)](../characters/第%20(char).md) "-th" + [三 (char)](../characters/三%20(char).md) "three" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both 第's and 三's own are themselves), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 第 is individually `kwin: false`.

**Fixed a real bug**: `korean` had 셋째, 三's own *native* ordinal ("third," from `korean_native: 셋`), not this compound's own Sino-Korean reading — corrected to 제삼.

第三 means "third," the ordinal formed by prefixing 第 to 三, and appears in the fixed legal/administrative term 第三者 ("third party").

Mandarin dì-sān, Cantonese dai6 saam1, Japanese だいさん (daisan), and Korean 제삼 (jesam, as in 제삼자, "third party") are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese đệ tam is likewise compositional and is itself a common Vietnamese term (đệ tam thế giới, "Third World").
