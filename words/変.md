## Notes
- [変 (char)](../characters/変%20(char).md) "change; transform; alter"

変 (shinjitai of 變) means "to change, transform, alter, become" (変化, "to change, vary"; 変動, "fluctuation").

Mandarin biàn and Cantonese bin3 preserve the sense directly. Japanese ヘン (HEN, on'yomi, fully productive: 変化, henka, "change"; also a common everyday adjective sense, 変, "strange, odd") alongside native か(わる) (ka(waru), "to change," the everyday verb) cover the sense. Korean 변 (byeon) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese biến is the standard Hán Việt reading, extremely common in everyday speech (biến đổi, "to change, transform"; biến mất, "to disappear").

This word shares its reading (byen/변/ㄅ⼶ㄋ) with [[便]] ("convenient; excrement," already perfected, already cross-linking here).
