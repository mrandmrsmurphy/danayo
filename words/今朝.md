## Notes
- [今](../characters/今%20(char).md) "now" + [朝](../characters/朝%20(char).md) "morning" — "this morning." `vietnamese: kim triêu` uses 朝's own morning-specific reading triêu (distinct in tone from triều, the dynasty/court sense, e.g. 朝廷 triều đình) — confirmed via web search. **Real gap found and fixed on [[朝 (char)]]'s own page**: its `vietnamese` field crammed three readings into one malformed list item ("triều, chầu, chào") and was missing triêu entirely — reformatted into a proper list and added the missing reading. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 김잣 diverges entirely from the native-word-based Korean field 오늘아침. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄇㄐㄚㄨ unique among both words and characters). Not to be confused with [[今朝安]] "good morning," the same-family greeting coinage.
