## Notes
- [今](../characters/今%20(char).md) "now" + [朝](../characters/朝%20(char).md) "morning" — "this morning." `vietnamese: kim triêu` uses 朝's own morning-specific reading triêu (distinct in tone from triều, the dynasty/court sense, e.g. 朝廷 triều đình) — confirmed via web search. Not to be confused with [[今朝安]] "good morning," the same-family greeting coinage.
