## Notes
- [星](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" + [坐](../characters/坐%20(char).md) "seat" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 星 and 坐's own stand-ins are themselves).

星坐 means constellation.

Mandarin xīngzuò, Cantonese sing1 zo6, Japanese せいざ, Korean 성좌, and Vietnamese tinh toạ (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄫㄐ⺢ unique across both words and characters).
