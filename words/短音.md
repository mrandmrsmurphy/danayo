## Notes
- [短](../characters/短%20(char).md) "short" + [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" — literally "short sound."

短音 is a phonetics term for a short vowel or short tone/duration in a language's sound system, contrasted with 長音 ("long vowel/tone"). It is especially prominent in the description of Japanese, where vowel length is phonemically distinctive (おばさん "aunt" vs おばあさん "grandmother," distinguished purely by vowel length).

Mandarin duǎnyīn, Cantonese dyun2 jam1, Japanese たんおん (tan'on), Korean 단음 (daneum), and Vietnamese đoản âm are all fully compositional and used as the standard linguistic/phonetic term across the sphere.
