## Notes
- [椅](../characters/椅.md) "chair" + [子](../characters/子.md) "child" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 椅 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

Mandarin yǐzi, Cantonese ji2 zi2, Japanese いす (isu), and Korean 의자 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 椅's and 子's own stored citations exactly. `vietnamese` is left blank: both constituents carry multiple attested Vietnamese candidates (椅: ghế, kỉ, ỷ, y; 子: tí, tử) with no single compositional pairing confidently established as the real fixed word for "chair," rather than guessing among them.

`kwin` is false: 椅 alone is kwin-true (its own Dan'a'yo reading matches Sino-Korean), but 子 independently diverges from Korean (Dan'a'yo 즈 vs Korean 자) on its own character page, which alone is enough to make the AND-rule false. No homophones (注音 ㄜㄧㄐㄜ unique among both words and characters).
