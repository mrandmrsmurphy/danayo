## Notes
- [来](../characters/来%20(char).md) "come" + [臨](../characters/臨.md) "draw near, approach" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 臨 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

来臨 means to come near or arrive.

Mandarin láilín, Cantonese loi4 lam4, and Japanese らいりん are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bugs: `korean` was 다가가다, a native Korean verb ("to approach"), not the compositional Sino-Korean form — corrected to 래림 (North Korean/문화어, matching 来's own citation and 臨's own already-North-Korean 림). Removed the stray する suffix from `japanese` (らいりんする → らいりん), matching the vault's convention of storing the bare Sino-Japanese noun/verb-stem form. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field, removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄚㄧㄌㄧㄇ unique across both words and characters).
