## Notes
- [捕](../characters/捕%20(char).md) "catch, grab" + [手](../characters/手%20(char).md) "hand" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 捕 and 手's own stand-ins are themselves).

捕手 means catcher (the baseball position).

Mandarin bǔshǒu, Cantonese bou6 sau2, Japanese ほしゅ, Korean 포수, and Vietnamese bổ thủ are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄛㄙ⼜ unique across both words and characters).
