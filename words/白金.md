## Notes
- [白 (char)](characters/白%20(char).md) "white" + [金 (char)](characters/金%20(char).md) "gold" — "white gold," a real compositional word rather than a coined neologism.

白金 (Pt, atomic number 78) is a real, established word across the Sinosphere, not an invented calque: Mandarin báijīn, Cantonese baak6 gam1, Japanese はっきん (hakkin), Korean 백금 (baekgeum), and Vietnamese bạch kim all follow the same direct compositional pattern productively — paralleling how [[塩素]] and [[炭素]] are likewise real compositional words. The name reflects platinum's appearance: a silvery-white precious metal, distinguished from ordinary 金 ("gold").

kwin is false: [[金]] (char) is kwin, but [[白]] (char) is not, and the AND-rule requires every constituent to be kwin for the compound to qualify.
