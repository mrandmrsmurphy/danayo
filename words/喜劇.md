## Notes
- [喜 (char)](../characters/喜%20(char).md) "joyful" + [劇 (char)](../characters/劇%20(char).md) "drama"

喜劇 is a comedy: a play that is light in tone and ends happily. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xǐjù as "comedy; farce" and Japanese きげき as "comedy; farce". Korean 희극 is the same word. Cantonese hei2 kek6 matches.

It is the opposite of the [悲劇](悲劇.md), tragedy, and both are kinds of [劇](劇.md), drama.
