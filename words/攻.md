## Notes
- [攻](../characters/攻%20(char).md) "attack, criticize" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [攻](../characters/攻%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

攻 means to attack or criticize (as in 攻撃, "attack," and 攻略, "strategy guide").

Mandarin gōng, Cantonese gung1, Japanese こう, Korean 공, and Vietnamese công are all standard, fully compositional readings. Homophone of [[公]] "honorific; sir" (both 注音 ㄍㄛㄫ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words; checked four other candidates sharing this reading (功/工/貢/龔) and confirmed none has an independent word at this exact reading (龔 is name-only, the rest bound to longer stand-in compounds).
