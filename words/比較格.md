## Notes
- [比 (char)](../characters/比%20(char).md) "than, compared to" + [較](../characters/較.md) "compare" + [格 (char)](../characters/格%20(char).md) "case (grammatical)" — this compound builds on [[比較]] ("compare"), plus the fixed case-name suffix 格; part of the same matched set of case-term coinages as [[与格]], [[主格]], and [[呼格]]. No stand-in relationship applies (較's own stand-in is [[比較]], not this compound; 格's own is itself).

比較格 is the grammatical comparative case, marking the standard of comparison ("than ___").

Mandarin bǐjiàogé, Cantonese bei2 gaau3 gaak3, Japanese ひかくかく, and Korean 비교격 are all standard, fully compositional readings. `vietnamese: so sánh cách` follows the same pattern as its siblings, combining [[比較]]'s own native Vietnamese term so sánh with 格's own cách. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/`aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄧㄜㄍㄚㄎㄍㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
