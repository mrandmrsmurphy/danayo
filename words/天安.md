## Notes
- [天](../characters/天%20(char).md) "sky" + [安](../characters/安.md) "peaceful" — the name of Cheonan, a city of South Korea south of Seoul.

天安 (Cheonan) is a city of South Chungcheong Province ([[忠清]]南道) in South Korea, an important railway junction south of [[漢城]] (Seoul), and the site of the Independence Hall of Korea (from memory, not re-checked). The name reads "heavenly peace".

English Wiktionary lists 天安 first as the Korean city and second as a Chinese era name of 466 to 467 (Mandarin Tiān'ān; no Cantonese is listed, so tin1 on1 is composed). Chinese and Japanese Wiktionary also have the string; the Japanese entry is the Ten'an era of 857 to 859, with the reading てんあん, which is the reading used here (Wiktionary lists it for the era, not the city). English Wiktionary has a Korean entry as a hanja form of 천안. Vietnamese Thiên An is composed from the character pages.
