## Notes
- [寿](../characters/寿.md) "old age; long life; lifespan" + [命](../characters/命.md) "life; fate" — this word is the stand-in for [寿](../characters/寿.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

寿命 names the span or duration of life — how long something lives or lasts — extending beyond biological life to the "lifespan" of inanimate things (電池の寿命 "battery life," 機械の寿命 "a machine's service life"), unlike [[宿命]] or [[運命]], which describe the *character* of a life (fated/destined) rather than its *duration*.

Japanese じゅみょう, Korean 수명, and Vietnamese thọ mệnh are all standard, non-drifted readings, all sharing the same extension to inanimate "service life."
