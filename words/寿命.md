## Notes
- [寿](../characters/寿.md) "old age; long life; lifespan" + [命](../characters/命.md) "life; fate" — this word is the stand-in for [寿](../characters/寿.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

寿命 names the span or duration of life — how long something lives or lasts — extending beyond biological life to the "lifespan" of inanimate things (電池の寿命 "battery life," 機械の寿命 "a machine's service life"), unlike [[宿命]] or [[運命]], which describe the *character* of a life (fated/destined) rather than its *duration*.

Japanese じゅみょう, Korean 수명, and Vietnamese thọ mệnh are all standard, non-drifted readings, all sharing the same extension to inanimate "service life." No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼜ㄇ⼶ㄫ unique across both words and characters); HSK level 3 already correct.

**Correction (2026-08-26)**: `諺文`/`羅馬字` had a palatalization typo — stored 슈멍/syumeng, dropping the palatal glide from 命's own 명/myeng, instead of the correct 슈명/syumyeng (the `注音` field, ㄙ⼜ㄇ⼶ㄫ, had the palatal marker ⼶ correct all along).
