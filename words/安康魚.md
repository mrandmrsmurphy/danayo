## Etymology
- [安](../characters/安.md) "peaceful" + [康](../characters/康.md) "healthy" + [魚 (char)](../characters/魚%20(char).md) "fish" — **not the real etymology**: 安康 here is a phonetic loan standing in for the actual fish-name characters 鮟鱇 (ānkāng, "anglerfish"), not a semantic compound meaning "peaceful and healthy fish." 鮟鱇 itself is listed in this page's own `aliases`.

## Notes
- "Anglerfish." **Real content bug found and fixed**: `mandarin` had two comma-joined tonal variants — trimmed to the standard ānkāng; `cantonese` was missing 魚's own syllable entirely — corrected to on1 hong1 jyu4. **`vietnamese` deliberately left blank**: since 安康 is purely a phonetic substitute for 鮟鱇 rather than a semantic compound, a compositional reading (an khang ngư) would be actively misleading; no independent Vietnamese name for this fish was confirmed, and anglerfish isn't a traditional food fish in Vietnamese cuisine (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 안캉요 diverges entirely from Sino-Korean 안강 (only two syllables, since Korean's own 안강 doesn't include a separate 魚 syllable either). No homophones (注音 ㄚㄋㄎㄚㄫ·⼄ unique among both words and characters).
