## Notes
- [紙](../characters/紙%20(char).md) "paper" + [幣](../characters/幣%20(char).md) "currency" — "paper currency." Both characters are their own `stand_in`, so 紙幣 is an independent compound.

紙幣 is a banknote: money printed on paper, as opposed to coin. Mandarin zhǐbì, Cantonese zi2 bai6, Japanese しへい and Korean 지폐 use it. Compare [[貨幣]] (money, currency) and [[幣]] (cash).
