## Notes
- [小 (char)](../characters/小%20(char).md) "small" + [[鼠]] "rat" — literally "small rat," i.e. mouse, distinguishing this smaller rodent from [[熊鼠]] ("bear-rat," the vault's word for the larger rat proper, aliased to 老鼠).

Mandarin xiǎoshǔ, Cantonese siu2 syu2, Japanese しょうそ (shōso), Korean 소서 (soseo), and Vietnamese tiểu thử are all standard, fully compositional readings built directly from 小's and 鼠's own stored citations. **Corrected 2026-09-21**: the `japanese` and `korean` fields previously stored the real everyday native words for "mouse" (ねずみ nezumi and 생쥐 saengjwi respectively) rather than the compositional Sino-Xenic readings this vault's fields are meant to hold — per standing convention, moved here: everyday Japanese uses ねずみ (nezumi, native, and also 鼠's own stored kun'yomi) rather than しょうそ, and everyday Korean uses the native compound 생쥐 (saengjwi, literally "raw/wild-rat") rather than Sino-Korean 소서. `vietnamese` was also entirely missing; tiểu thử is confirmed compositional Hán Việt, used specifically for "laboratory mouse," while everyday Vietnamese instead uses native chuột nhắt. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 소소 diverges from Sino-Korean 소서 in the second syllable. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄙㄛㄙ⼄ unique among both words and characters).
