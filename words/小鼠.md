## Notes
- [小 (char)](../characters/小%20(char).md) "small" + [鼠](../characters/鼠.md) "rat" — literally "small rat," i.e. mouse, distinguishing this smaller rodent from [熊鼠](熊鼠.md) ("bear-rat," the vault's word for the larger rat proper, aliased to 老鼠).

Mandarin xiǎoshǔ, Cantonese siu2 syu2, Japanese しょうそ (shōso), Korean 소서 (soseo), and Vietnamese tiểu thử are all standard, fully compositional readings built directly from 小's and 鼠's own stored citations. Everyday Japanese uses ねずみ (nezumi, native, and also 鼠's own stored kun'yomi) rather than しょうそ, and everyday Korean uses the native compound 생쥐 (saengjwi, literally "raw/wild-rat") rather than Sino-Korean 소서. Vietnamese tiểu thử is the compositional Hán Việt, used specifically for "laboratory mouse," while everyday Vietnamese uses native chuột nhắt.
