## Notes
- [皇](../characters/皇.md) "emperor" + [后](../characters/后.md) "empress" — this word is the viable form of [后](../characters/后.md): the character's meaning (empress) is identical, but 后 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 皇后 is its `stand_in` compound.

皇后 is specifically the principal consort of an emperor ([[皇帝]]), rank-matched to 皇 rather than to the lower-ranked 王 ("king") — a king's principal consort is instead 王后 ("queen consort"), a separate, lower title in the traditional East Asian rank system. 皇后 names the position by marriage, not by rule in her own right: a woman who reigns as sovereign in her own right (as with Wu Zetian) is instead [[女皇]] ("female emperor, empress regnant").

Japanese reading こうごう uses GO (the secondary on-yomi of 后) rather than the expected KOU, yielding an elongated second syllable. Korean 황후 and Vietnamese hoàng hậu are both standard compositional Sino-readings. No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⺢ㄫㄏㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).
