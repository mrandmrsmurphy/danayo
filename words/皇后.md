## Notes
- [皇](../characters/皇.md) "emperor" + [后](../characters/后.md) "empress"

皇后 is an empress, the consort of an emperor ([皇帝](皇帝.md)). English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 皇后 (Mandarin huánghòu, Cantonese wong4 hau6) as "empress, particularly an empress consort", and Japanese こうごう as "empress consort". Korean 황후 is glossed "empress" and Vietnamese hoàng hậu "empress consort". A woman who rules in her own right is the [女皇](女皇.md).

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [后](../characters/后.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
