## Notes
- [区](../characters/区.md) "area, ward" + [域](../characters/域%20(char).md) "domain, territory" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [区](../characters/区.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (域's own stand-in is [[域]] itself, an unrelated word).

区域 means an area, district, or zone — a bounded or designated region.

**Real bugs found and fixed**: `mandarin`/`cantonese` were both entirely blank — filled with qūyù/keoi1 wik6 (compositional from each character's own reading). Japanese くいき, Korean 구역, and Vietnamese khu vực are all standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄨ·ㄧㄎ, with a disambiguating middle dot marking the boundary between two adjacent ㄨ vowels, unique across both words and characters).
