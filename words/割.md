## Notes
- [割 (char)](../characters/割%20(char).md) "cut; divide; separate"

割 is 形声: phonetic 害 + semantic 刂 ("knife"). It means "to cut, divide, separate" — [[割断]] ("to cut off, sever"), [[割引]] ("a discount," literally "a cut [in price]"), and in mathematics [[余割]]/[[正割]] ("cosecant/secant"). [[割包皮]] ("circumcision") uses the same literal-cutting sense.

Mandarin gē, Cantonese got3, and Japanese かつ (katsu, on'yomi) alongside native わ(る)/さ(く) (wa(ru)/sa(ku), "to divide, split/to cut, tear," the everyday verbs) all preserve the sense. Korean 할 (hal) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 나누다 (nanuda, "to divide"). Vietnamese cát is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in phân cát (分割, "to divide, partition") and cát cứ (割據, "to divide and hold territory, warlordism"); the everyday native word for "to cut" is cắt, phonetically close but not itself the classical Hán Việt reading — the character page's third stored candidate, xắt ("to dice, slice thinly"), is likewise native and unrelated to the reading itself.
