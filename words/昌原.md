## Notes
- [昌](../characters/昌.md) "prosperous" + [原](../characters/原.md) "plain; original" — the name of Changwon, a city of South Korea on the south-east coast.

昌原 (Changwon) is the capital of South Gyeongsang Province ([[慶尚]]南道) in South Korea, a planned industrial city on the south coast near 釜山, which merged with Masan and Jinhae in 2010 (from memory, not re-checked). The name reads "prosperous plain".

English and Chinese Wiktionary have 昌原 as the Korean city (Mandarin Chāngyuán, Cantonese coeng1 jyun4), and English Wiktionary has a Korean entry as a hanja form of 창원; its Japanese section gives チャンウォン (from the Korean) only, so Japanese しょうげん is composed from the on'yomi. Vietnamese Xương Nguyên is composed from the character pages; no Vietnamese dictionary entry was found.
