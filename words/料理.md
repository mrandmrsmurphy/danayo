## Notes
- [料](../characters/料.md) "materials; fee" + [理](../characters/理.md) "manage"

料理 is cooking: preparing food, and so a dish or a cuisine; in Chinese also the older sense of handling or arranging a matter. Wiktionary glosses Japanese りょうり as "cooking; cuisine; to cook, to prepare (food)" and Mandarin liàolǐ (Cantonese liu6 lei5) as "to arrange; to handle" and "to cook". Vietnamese liệu lí and Korean 요리 are the same word, the Korean field giving the North Korean form 료리.

It is the art and the act of preparing food, where [食物](食物.md) is the food itself. The dish styles built on technique are shown in the 炒, 煎 and 炸 grids below.
