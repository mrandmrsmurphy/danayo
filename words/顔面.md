## Notes
- [顔](../characters/顔.md) "face, reputation" + [面](../characters/面.md) "surface, face" — 顔面 is 顔's own legitimizing compound (顔's `stand_in` points here), but since 面's own `stand_in` is [[表面]] instead, transitivity fails and no `#cranberry` tag applies.

顔面 (yánmiàn) is a real, formal Mandarin word for "face" (especially in medical/technical registers, e.g. 顔面神経 "facial nerve"), and Cantonese 顔面 (ngaan4min6) likewise. Japanese がんめん (ganmen) is the standard formal/medical term. Korean 안면 (anmyeon) is likewise standard, though everyday spoken Korean more often uses the native 얼굴 or the more colloquial/dismissive 낯 for "face."

None of the five languages' everyday word for "face" is this exact Sino compound — Vietnamese instead uses the entirely native mặt, with no live Sino-Vietnamese equivalent (nhan diện is not an attested term).
