## Notes
- [愉](../characters/愉.md) "pleasure" + [快 (char)](../characters/快%20(char).md) "sharp, quick, happy" — `characters/愉.md`'s own `stand_in` points to this word (愉快), meaning 愉 is a bound morpheme that never appears independently outside this compound.

Mandarin yúkuài, Japanese ゆかい, Korean 유쾌, and Vietnamese du khoái are the standard readings. Cantonese has two documented tone variants, jyu4 faai3 and jyu6 faai3.
