## Notes
- [七 (char)](../characters/七%20(char).md) "seven" + [星 (char)](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" — no `#cranberry`: both 七's and 星's own `stand_in` point to themselves, neither to this word.

Names the Big Dipper (the seven-star asterism), an object of folk-religious veneration across the sphere — Korean 칠성 (Chilseong) refers to the Big Dipper deity in Korean folk religion and Buddhism, a well-known concept matching this word's own compositional reading exactly. Mandarin qīxīng, Cantonese cat1 sing1, Japanese しちせい, and Sino-Vietnamese thất tinh are all standard compositional readings. `kwin` is false (AND-rule: both 七 and 星 individually false).
