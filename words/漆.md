## Notes
- [漆 (char)](../characters/漆%20(char).md) "varnish, lacquer" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

漆 names lacquer or varnish — originally written 桼 (a pictogram of sap oozing from a lacquer tree), with 水 ("water") added later to form the modern character. 漆 is also used as the anti-forgery/formal numeral form of [[七]] ("seven") in financial and banking contexts, the same role 壹/貳/... play for other digits — a direct, deliberate consequence of the two words sharing this exact Dan'a'yo reading.

Mandarin qī, Cantonese cat1, and Korean 칠 (chil) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese しち (shichi, on'yomi) is productive in compounds; the native kun'yomi うるし (urushi, already 漆's own stored `japanese_native` value) is the everyday word for lacquer itself (as in urushi-ware). Vietnamese tất is the standard Hán Việt reading. **Real `kwin` bug found and fixed**: was stored as `true` (both here and on 漆's own character page), but Dan'a'yo 칟 does not exactly string-match Sino-Korean 칠 — the coda diverges (ㄷ vs. ㄹ), the same MC-checked-coda-vs-Sino-Korean-regularized divergence pattern documented repeatedly across this vault. Corrected to `kwin: false` on this word's page. No Dan'a'yo homophones beyond [[七]] itself (注音 ㄑㄧㄊ also shared by 叱 among characters, but no other *word*).
