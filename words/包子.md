## Notes
- [包](../characters/包.md) "wrapped" + [子](../characters/子.md) "thing" (noun suffix) — "steamed bun," a wrapped-dough bun with a filling. `vietnamese: bánh bao` is directly attested everyday Vietnamese — itself a loanword-calque of this exact term (bánh, "cake/bun" + bao, this word's own bāo), a well-established borrowing from Chinese cuisine. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 뱟즈 diverges entirely from Sino-Korean 만두 (the loose Korean equivalent, though 만두/mandu itself refers to a somewhat different dumpling style) — the same divergence shared with its homophone [[胞子]].
