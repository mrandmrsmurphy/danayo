## Notes
- [規](../characters/規.md) "rules; compass and square" + [定](../characters/定.md) "to fix, to decide" — to stipulate; a regulation; a fixed rule

Wiktionary: Chinese "to regulate; to stipulate" and "rule; regulation; stipulation"; Japanese 規定 (きてい) "rule" and "prescribe, stipulate"; Korean 규정 is the hanja form. The simplified spelling 规定 is kept as an alias. 規's own `stand_in` is 規律, so this word is not its legitimizer. The Japanese きてい is also the reading of [[既定]], so the written form carries the distinction.
