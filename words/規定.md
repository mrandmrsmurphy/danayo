## Notes
- [規](../characters/規.md) "rules; compass and square" + [定](../characters/定.md) "to fix, to decide" — to stipulate; a regulation; a fixed rule

規 is the compass, the instrument for drawing circles; paired with 矩 (the carpenter's square) it gives 規矩 and the idea of a standard that things must conform to. 規定 is the act of using such an instrument on conduct or procedure: to fix, in rule form, what is required or permitted. The compound thus sits between the generality of 規則 (rule, general pattern) and the formality of 法律 (law, statutory norm).

In everyday usage, 規定 refers to the specific provisions of an organizational or contractual text — the clauses of a contract, the articles of a bylaw, the terms of a policy: 規定に従う (to follow the regulations), 規定通り (as stipulated). In mathematical and logical contexts, 規定 means to define or specify a domain or condition: 規定された範囲 (the specified range).

Vietnamese quy định is particularly common as an administrative term — it appears on official signage, notices, and policy documents as the standard word for "regulations" or "provisions," showing how the compound has been fully naturalized into modern Vietnamese bureaucratic language.

規's own `stand_in` is 規律, so this word is not its legitimizer.  The Japanese きてい is also the reading of [[既定]] ("already decided"), so the written form carries the distinction.  Mandarin guīdìng, Cantonese kwai1 ding6, Japanese きてい, Korean 규정 and Vietnamese quy định are the same compound, and the alias 规定 is the simplified form.  `kwin` is false because the Dan'a'yo 귀정 differs from the Korean 규정 in its vowel.  The 注音 needs no syllable dot.
