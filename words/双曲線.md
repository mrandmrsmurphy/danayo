## Notes
- [双](../characters/双%20(char).md) "pair, double" + [曲](../characters/曲.md) "bend" + [線](../characters/線.md) "line" — no stand-in relationship applies (双 is its own stand-in; 曲's own is [[歌曲]]; 線's is [[直線]]).
- One of the four <ruby>[圓錐曲線](../words/圓錐曲線.md)<rt>⼔ㄋㄐㄨㄧㄎ⼘ㄎㄙ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby>

双曲線 names the hyperbola, one of the four conic sections.

Japanese そうきょくせん and Korean 쌍곡선 are both standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese `hyperbol` (verified) is the standard term used in Vietnamese mathematics — an international loanword for the noun "hyperbola" itself, distinct from the Sino-Vietnamese compound "song khúc," which instead covers the adjective "hyperbolic" (as in hyperbolic functions). No homophones (注音 ㄙ⺢ㄫㄎ⼄ㄎㄙ⼶ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
