## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [防](../characters/防.md) "defend, prevent" — no stand-in relationship applies (海's own stand-in is [[海洋]]; 防's own is [[防護]]).

海防 refers to the defense of a coastline — the military, naval, and fortification systems by which a maritime nation protects its shores from attack or infiltration. The compound is used in historical writing (Ming and Qing coastal defense against wokou pirates and Western navies), in modern military doctrine, and in place names.

The word has two senses: the common noun "coastal defense" comes first, and the place name Haiphong (Vietnamese: Hải Phòng) follows it. 海防 is the Chinese name of Haiphong, a major port city of northern Vietnam and one of its largest cities. The city's name records the same compound in its Hán-Việt form: hải (海, sea) + phòng (防, defense) — named for its strategic role guarding the approaches to Hanoi via the Red River Delta. In Japanese (かいぼう, kaibō), the term appears in Meiji-era military history when coastal defense became a central concern during Japan's opening to the West.

In Korean, 해방 (海防) is a near-homophone of 해방 (解放, liberation/emancipation), the word most strongly associated in Korean cultural memory with Korea's liberation from Japanese colonial rule in 1945. Context always disambiguates, but the overlap gives 海防 an unusual resonance in Korean.

Mandarin hǎifáng, Cantonese hoi2 fong4, Japanese かいぼう, and Korean 해방 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 海's and 防's own stored citations exactly. Vietnamese hải phòng is likewise compositional and, as noted above, also the real city name. `kwin: true` because 諺文 해방 matches Korean 해방 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄧㄅㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).

Attestation of the place-name sense, checked 2026-10-11: English Wiktionary lists 海防 as a Chinese proper noun (a city in Vietnam, Haiphong) beside the noun "coastal defense", and has Japanese (かいぼう, a military term) and Vietnamese entries; Chinese Wiktionary lists the proper noun as a city in northern Vietnam. Korean Wiktionary has no entry. Japanese and Korean do not normally use 海防 for the city (Japanese ハイフォン, Korean 하이퐁, from memory, not re-checked).

1. Coastal defense, the military and fortification systems guarding a coastline.
2. Haiphong (Hải Phòng), the port city of northern Vietnam.
