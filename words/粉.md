## Notes
- [粉](../characters/粉%20(char).md) "powder" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 粉 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

**Fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` had bun/분/ㄅㄨㄋ — matching this character's own phonetic component 分 rather than 粉's own independently assigned Dan'a'yo syllable — corrected to fun/뿐/ㄈㄨㄋ, confirmed against `characters/粉 (char).md` and `syllables/ㄈㄨㄋ.md`.

粉 originally named face powder made from rice (its own 形声 semantic component 米, "rice"), extended to any fine powder generally: 花粉 ("pollen"), 粉末 ("fine powder").

Mandarin fěn, Cantonese fan2, and Korean 분 (bun) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading こな (kona, "powder," also read こ in some compounds) rather than the on-reading フン (FUN) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese phấn is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading.
