## Etymology
- [廟](characters/廟.md) "ancestral temple" + [堂](characters/堂.md) "meeting hall" — "the ancestral shrine of the king; the imperial court," directly attested via hvdic (miếu đường), citing Nguyễn Du's Đoạn trường tân thanh. Stand-in for [[廟]], which cannot appear independently.
