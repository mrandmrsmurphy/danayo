## Notes
- [廟](../characters/廟.md) "ancestral temple" + [堂](../characters/堂.md) "meeting hall" — this word is the stand-in for [廟](../characters/廟.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo. "The ancestral shrine of the king; the imperial court" — directly attested via hvdic (miếu đường), citing Nguyễn Du's Đoạn trường tân thanh.

廟堂 names the ancestral temple of a ruling house, and by metonymy the royal court or central government itself (the seat of state ritual and governance combined) — a classical/literary term, not everyday vocabulary in any of the four languages.

Japanese びょうどう (a genuine compositional reading — 廟's BYOU + 堂's DOU — coincidentally identical in sound to [[平等]]'s own irregular Go-on reading, but arrived at independently and unrelated in etymology), Korean 묘당, and Vietnamese miếu đường are all standard, if literary, readings. No homophones (注音 ㄇ⼘ㄨㄉㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
