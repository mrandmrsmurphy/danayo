## Notes
- [前 (char)](../characters/前%20(char).md) "front (stand-in: itself)" + [進](../characters/進.md) "advance (stand-in: [[進行]])"

Wiktionary: Chinese 前進 "to advance; to go forward", "to progress; to improve"; Japanese 前進 "advance, advancement, progress" and "to advance, to progress"; Korean 전진 is the hanja form, "advance". It is the spatial "forward" word the Directions page lacked, set against [[後退]].
