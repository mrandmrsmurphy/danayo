## Notes
- [需](../characters/需.md) "need; glutinous" + [米 (char)](../characters/米%20(char).md) "rice"

需米 is glutinous rice, or sticky rice: the short, starchy rice, low in amylose, that is steamed or pounded for rice cakes, dumplings and sweets. The usual spelling is 糯米, an alias, because 糯 is an alias of 需. Wiktionary glosses Chinese 糯米 (Mandarin nuòmǐ, Cantonese no6 mai5) as "glutinous rice; sticky rice" and Japanese もちごめ as "glutinous rice, a variety of Oryza sativa low in amylose". Japanese もちごめ and Korean 찹쌀 are the native words.

It is the sticky counterpart of ordinary rice, the [米](米.md) of [米飯](米飯.md), and is set against the non-glutinous [更米](更米.md). The rice cake made from it is the [粄](粄.md).
