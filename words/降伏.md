## Notes
- [降](../characters/降.md) "descend; yield" + [伏 (char)](../characters/伏%20(char).md) "bow; submit"

降伏 is surrender: to yield to an enemy and give up the fight. Wiktionary glosses Japanese こうふく as "surrender, capitulation; to surrender". Mandarin xiángfú is glossed there only as "to tame; to subdue", so the Dan'a'yo word follows the Japanese and Korean sense. Korean 항복 matches.

It is the formal, complete giving-up, where [降参](降参.md) is the ordinary "I give up". The opposite is [抵抗](抵抗.md), resistance.
