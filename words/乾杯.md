## Notes
- [乾 (char)](../characters/乾%20(char).md) "dry" + [杯 (char)](../characters/杯%20(char).md) "cup"

乾杯 is a toast: to raise one's glass and drink to someone, and the cry "cheers". Wiktionary glosses Mandarin gānbēi as "to toast; to drink a toast" and "cheers; bottoms up", and Japanese かんぱい as "drinking one's glass dry" and "toast". Korean 건배 and Cantonese gon1 bui1 match.

It is the shared act of drinking at a [宴会](宴会.md), banquet, and it is done with a full [杯](杯.md), cup, of [酒精](酒精.md) or [麦酒](麦酒.md).
