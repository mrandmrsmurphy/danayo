## Notes
- [伝](../characters/伝.md) "transmit" + [播](../characters/播.md) "sow" — "to propagate, disseminate, spread" (ideas, diseases, information). `vietnamese: truyền bá` is directly attested and extremely common (truyền bá tư tưởng, "to propagate ideas"), compositional from each character's own reading. Legitimizing note: 伝's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, so 伝 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.
