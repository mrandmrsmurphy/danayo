## Notes
- [伝](../characters/伝.md) "transmit" + [播](../characters/播.md) "sow" — "to propagate, disseminate, spread" (ideas, diseases, information). `vietnamese: truyền bá` is directly attested and extremely common (truyền bá tư tưởng, "to propagate ideas"), compositional from each character's own reading. `kwin` is false: both 伝 and 播 independently carry `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄐ⼔ㄋㄅㄚ unique among both words and characters). Legitimizing note: 伝's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, so 伝 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

**Real content bug found and fixed**: `mandarin` was a malformed comma-joined duplicate ("chuánbō,chuánbò"), the second an incorrect tone variant — trimmed to the single correct chuánbō.
