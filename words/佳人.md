## Notes
- [佳 (char)](../characters/佳%20(char).md) "beautiful, good, excellent" + [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" — "a beautiful person" (traditionally a beautiful woman), as in the chengyu [[佳人薄命]] ("a beautiful woman has an ill fate," a well-worn literary trope). `vietnamese: giai nhân` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese, matching [[佳]]'s own already-noted giai nhân example.
