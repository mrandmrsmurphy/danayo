## Notes
- [百 (char)](../characters/百%20(char).md) "hundred" + [科](../characters/科.md) "branch, section" — literally "all the branches/fields," i.e. the full range of knowledge.

百科 rarely stands alone; it functions chiefly as a bound prefix meaning "encyclopedic, comprehensive" that combines with other terms — most notably [[百科事典]] "encyclopedia" and, in institutional naming, "polytechnic" (a school covering many technical branches). Mandarin bǎikē, Cantonese baak3 fo1, Japanese ひゃっか (hyakka), Korean 백과 (baekgwa), and Vietnamese bách khoa (as in Đại học Bách khoa, "Polytechnic University") are all fully compositional and used in this same bound, comprehensive sense across the sphere.
