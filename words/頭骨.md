## Notes
- [頭 (char)](../characters/頭%20(char).md) "head" + [骨 (char)](../characters/骨%20(char).md) "bone, skeleton" — no `#cranberry`: both 頭's and 骨's own `stand_in` are themselves; neither points here.

頭骨 (tóugǔ) is a real Mandarin word for "skull, cranium," and Cantonese 頭骨 (tau4gwat1) likewise. Japanese とうこつ (tōkotsu) and Korean 두골 (dugol) are both standard anatomical terms in their languages. Sino-Vietnamese đầu cốt is likewise attested in medical/anatomical registers, though everyday Vietnamese more often uses the native sọ or hộp sọ ("skull box").
