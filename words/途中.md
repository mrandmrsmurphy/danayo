## Notes
- [途](../characters/途.md) "en route" + [中](../characters/中.md) "-ing; midst"
- 途中 is the stand-in word for [途](../characters/途.md): the character's meaning (en route; on the way) is identical, but 途 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 途中 is its `stand_in` compound.

Mandarin túzhōng, cantonese tou4zung1, japanese とちゅう, korean 도중, and vietnamese đồ trung are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly.
