## Notes
- [灌](../characters/灌.md) "to irrigate; to pour" + [漑](../characters/漑.md) "to irrigate"
- This word is the viable form of [灌](../characters/灌.md): the character means to irrigate or pour, but is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo. 灌漑 is its `stand_in` compound.

Both characters mean to pour water over land, so the compound is a synonym pair meaning to irrigate, and by extension the noun irrigation. Wiktionary gives Mandarin guàngài and Cantonese gun3 koi3 and lists it among the Mandarin agriculture terms, with 灌溉渠 (irrigation channel) as a derived term. Japanese かんがい and Korean 관개 are the same compound, written 灌漑 in both; the Chinese-language entry also carries the variant spelling 灌溉.

In Dan'a'yo the word has the shared sense and is placed on the [Water](../lexipedia/Water.md) page, as the standard word for irrigation. It is the `stand_in` for 灌, and the second character 漑 is bound to this compound.
