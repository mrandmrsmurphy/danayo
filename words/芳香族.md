## Notes
- [芳](../characters/芳.md) "fragrant" is separately legitimized by [[芳香]]. [香](../characters/香.md) "fragrant, aroma" is separately legitimized by [[香気]]. [族](../characters/族.md) "clan, group" is separately legitimized by [[家族]]. No cranberry: none of the three constituents' own `stand_in` points here.

芳香族 combines 芳/香 ("fragrant") with 族 ("group, class") — "aromatic compounds" (in chemistry, ring-based compounds named for their historical association with fragrant substances).

Mandarin fāngxiāngzú, japanese ほうこうぞく (houkouzoku), and korean 방향족 (banghyangjok) were all already correct, fully compositional readings.
