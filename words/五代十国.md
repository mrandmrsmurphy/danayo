## Notes
- [五 (char)](../characters/五%20(char).md) "five" + [代](../characters/代.md) "generation, era, dynasty" + [十 (char)](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" + [国](../characters/国.md) "state, country" — literally "Five Dynasties, Ten Kingdoms."

五代十国 names a chaotic interregnum in Chinese history (907–979 CE) between the fall of the Tang dynasty and the rise of the Song, during which five short-lived dynasties succeeded one another in rapid succession in the north while ten regional kingdoms coexisted, mostly in the south. The period is a byword for political fragmentation and rapid dynastic turnover, comparable in role to the earlier Three Kingdoms period.

Mandarin Wǔdài Shíguó, Cantonese ng5 doi6 sap6 gwok3, Japanese ごだいじゅうこく, and Korean 오대십국 are all standard, fully compositional readings of this historical-period name. Vietnamese Ngũ Đại Thập Quốc (capitalized as the proper name of a specific historical era) parallels the same usage in Vietnamese historiography.
