## Notes
- [割 (char)](../characters/割%20(char).md) "to cut" + [引 (char)](../characters/引%20(char).md) "to pull, deduct" — both わりびき (Japanese, 割引券 "discount coupon") and 할인 (Korean, 할인쿠폰/할인율 "discount coupon/rate") are real, everyday words built directly from this compound. Real Mandarin/Cantonese instead standardly use 折扣 (zhékòu) for this concept, so `cantonese: got3 jan5` is filled compositionally rather than representing confirmed everyday usage (this session's search quota is exhausted, so this reflects domain knowledge, not a fresh verification). `vietnamese: chiết khấu` is likewise the real, everyday Vietnamese word for "discount," sharing the same root as 折扣 rather than calquing this word's own 割引 characters. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 갇인 diverges entirely from Sino-Korean 할인. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄊㄧㄋ unique among both words and characters).
