## Notes
- [養](../characters/養.md) "foster, raise" + [育 (char)](../characters/育%20(char).md) "nurture, educate" — 養育 is 養's own legitimizing compound (養's `stand_in` points here), but since 育's own `stand_in` is 育 itself instead, transitivity fails and no `#cranberry` tag applies.

養育 (yǎngyù) is the standard Mandarin word for "to foster, raise (a child)," and Cantonese 養育 (joeng5juk6) likewise. Japanese よういく (yōiku) and Korean 양육 (yangyuk) are both standard terms, as is Sino-Vietnamese dưỡng dục — a clean case with full agreement across all five languages.
