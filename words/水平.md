## Notes
- [水](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [平](../characters/平.md) "horizontal" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 平 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (水's own stand-in is itself, so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

水平 means level, horizontal (literally "water-level," the surface of standing water as the natural reference for horizontality).

Mandarin shuǐpíng, Cantonese seoi2 ping4, Japanese すいへい, and Korean 수평 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 水's and 平's own stored citations exactly). Filled the blank `pos` (性詞) and `vietnamese` fields — thủy bình (水's own thuỷ + 平's own bình) is also the real, standard Vietnamese term for "horizontal, level." Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases: []`. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨㄅㄧㄫ unique across both words and characters).
