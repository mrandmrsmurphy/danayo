## Notes
- [龍 (char)](../characters/龍%20(char).md) "dragon" + [王 (char)](../characters/王%20(char).md) "king" — no `#cranberry`: both 龍's and 王's own `stand_in` point to themselves, neither to this word.

龍王 names the Dragon King, the East Asian deity/spirit ruling over bodies of water and rain — a mythological title, hence the capitalized `mandarin: Lóngwáng` as a proper noun. Mandarin Lóngwáng, Cantonese lung4 wong4, Korean 룡왕, and Sino-Vietnamese long vương are all standard compositional readings.
