## Notes
- [龍 (char)](../characters/龍%20(char).md) "dragon" + [王 (char)](../characters/王%20(char).md) "king" — no `#cranberry`: both 龍's and 王's own `stand_in` point to themselves, neither to this word. `kwin` is true (AND-rule: both constituents individually true).

龍王 names the Dragon King, the East Asian deity/spirit ruling over bodies of water and rain — a mythological title, hence the capitalized `mandarin: Lóngwáng` as a proper noun. Mandarin Lóngwáng, Cantonese lung4 wong4, Korean 룡왕, and Sino-Vietnamese long vương are all standard compositional readings. Fixed a real orthography bug in `japanese`: was written in obsolete historical kana spelling ("りゆうわう," with a full-size ゆ and the historical "-わう" ending) rather than modern hiragana; corrected to りゅうおう (RYUU + OU), matching the same modern "-おう" spelling convention already used consistently on every sibling 王-compound in this vault ([[帝王]], [[冥王]], [[国王]], [[天王星]], [[海王星]]). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and an empty `aliases: []`.
