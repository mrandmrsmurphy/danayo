## Etymology
- [会 (char)](../characters/会%20(char).md) "meet, gather" + [社](../characters/社.md) "company"

## Notes
The famous Japanese wasei-kango (和製漢語) for "company." Confirmed via web search that 會社/会社 (huìshè) does exist as a real, if dated/regional, Mandarin and Cantonese word alongside the far more common modern 公司 (gōngsī) — not a fabrication. `vietnamese: hội xã` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 훠샤 diverges from Sino-Korean 회사 in the first syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⼔ㄙ⼘ unique among both words and characters).
