## Notes
- [雑](../characters/雑%20(char).md) "miscellaneous, mixed" + [言](../characters/言%20(char).md) "say, speak" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language" — "mixed language," a pidgin. Their own `stand_in` values: 雑's is itself; 言's is itself; 語's is [[言語]]; none points here, so 雑言語 is an independent compound.

雑言語 is a pidgin: a simplified mixed language that people with no shared language build to trade and talk with one another. It is a **neologism**, built from [[雑]] "miscellaneous, mixed" and [[言語]] "language"; no two of the five languages share a set compound, since Chinese pidgin is 洋涇浜 and Japanese borrows ピジン. The shorter 雑語 would read as "miscellaneous words," so the longer form parallels 言語. Compare [[言語]] (language) and [[方言]] (dialect).
