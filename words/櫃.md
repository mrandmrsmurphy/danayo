## Notes
- [櫃 (char)](../characters/櫃%20(char).md) "cabinet"

櫃 is a cabinet: a piece of furniture for storing things, a cupboard or wardrobe, and a chest. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 櫃 (alias 柜; Mandarin guì, Cantonese gwai6) as "cabinet; cupboard; wardrobe" and "counter", and, in Hong Kong and Taiwan, a short form of 貨櫃, "shipping container"; it glosses Japanese ひつ as "chest, an item of furniture used for storage". It lists Korean 궤 and Vietnamese quỹ as readings of the hanja without a gloss.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [櫃](../characters/櫃%20(char).md), whose reading *gwe* is a new syllable. The smaller storage vessel is the [箱](箱.md), box, and a [棚](棚.md) is a shelf.
