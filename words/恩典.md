## Notes
- [恩](../characters/恩.md) "kindness; mercy" + [典](../characters/典.md) "canon; authoritative text; standard" — grace as a doctrinal category; the formal theological principle that God gives freely without merit

Wiktionary: Chinese "favour; grace; kindness; blessing"; in Christianity "grace (an attribute of God)"; intransitive, "to give favours"; Japanese 恩典 (おんてん) "a (special) favor; a privilege"; Korean 은전 and Vietnamese ân điển are the Han forms. The doctrinal sense, grace as a principle, is the Dan'a'yo-specific sense assigned to this word, which the 基督敎 lexipedia domain differentiates from [[恩寵]] and [[恩施]].
