## Notes
- [苦](../characters/苦.md) "suffering, bitter" is separately legitimized by [[苦]] itself. [渋](../characters/渋.md) "astringent, harsh" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 渋 cannot appear independently outside this compound. No cranberry: transitivity fails since 苦's own `stand_in` doesn't point here.

苦渋 combines 苦 ("bitter") with 渋 ("astringent") — figuratively "bitterness, hardship, distress," not merely a literal taste.

Converted the comma-joined `cantonese` (fu2 gip3, fu2 sap1 — both genuine attested readings of 渋/澀) into a proper list, with spaces removed.
