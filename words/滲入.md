## Notes
- [滲](../characters/滲.md) "soak through" + [入 (char)](../characters/入%20(char).md) "enter" — stand-in for [[滲]], which cannot appear independently. `vietnamese: sấm nhập` is compositional (not independently attested via hvdic).

Wiktionary lists 滲入 only under Chinese: "to seep into; to permeate" and, figuratively, of ideas, spies and so on, "to penetrate". The simplified spelling 渗入 is kept as an alias. Compare [[滲出]] and [[滲漏]].
