## Notes
- [滲](../characters/滲.md) "soak through" + [入 (char)](../characters/入%20(char).md) "enter" — stand-in for [[滲]], which cannot appear independently. `vietnamese: sấm nhập` is compositional (not independently attested via hvdic).

滲入 means to seep or soak in: a liquid working its way into something through its pores, and by extension an influence or substance penetrating gradually (水が地中に滲入する, "water seeps into the ground"; 思想の滲入, "the infiltration of an idea").  滲 contributes the slow soaking-through and 入 the direction inward, so the word is the inward counterpart of [[滲出]] ("to seep out") and is related to [[滲漏]] ("to leak through").

Mandarin shènrù, Cantonese sam3 jap6, Japanese しんにゅう and Korean 삼입 are the compositional readings of the two characters, and the traditional/simplified alias 渗入 is listed.  `kwin` is false because the Dan'a'yo 솜닙 differs from the Korean 삼입.  The Japanese しんにゅう is the same sound as 侵入 ("intrusion"), so the written form carries the distinction.
