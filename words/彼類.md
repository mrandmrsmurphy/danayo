## Notes
- [彼 (char)](../characters/彼%20(char).md) "yon; distal demonstrative; he/she (literary)" + [類](../characters/類.md) "kind; sort; category; type"

彼類 is the distal kind/category correlative — "that other kind; that sort (remote or unfamiliar)." Like 彼処 and 彼時, 彼類 refers to a category that is socially, culturally, or contextually remote — a type associated with another sphere, era, or group. Where 其類 is the kind already in conversation, 彼類 is the kind observed from outside or looked back upon with distance (these two sentences are illustrative Japanese-style constructions, not attested quotations): 彼類の習慣は理解しにくい ("customs of that other kind are hard to understand"); 彼類の思想はもう廃れた ("ideas of that sort have already died out").

Japanese ああいう (ā iu, "that sort — distal; often with a note of wonder, distance, or retrospective assessment") or あのような (ano yō na, "like that — distal"). Korean 저런 종류 (jeoreon jongnyu, "that kind — distal") or 저런 alone — the distal member of the 이런/그런/저런 deictic kind-triad, implying a category not in the speaker's or addressee's immediate sphere. Vietnamese loại kia (that kind over there — distal) uses the distal demonstrative kia.

Korean provides a clean three-way deictic kind-demonstrative system (이런/그런/저런 = this kind/that kind/that other kind) matching Dan'a'yo's 此類/其類/彼類 precisely. Japanese has a similar three-way system with この/その/あの + ような/いう. Mandarin collapses all three into the 這種/那種 binary. This makes Korean and Japanese the most direct cross-linguistic parallels to the 類 row's full three-way deictic contrast.
The Grammar page sets 彼類 in its correlative table, in the 類 ("kind") column and the 彼 ("that yonder") row, after [此類](此類.md) and [其類](其類.md); the same row has [彼物](彼物.md) "that thing yonder." 類 is not bound to this word: its `stand_in` is 種類. The word is tagged `neologism`, so no compositional readings were added; its `japanese`, `korean` and `vietnamese` fields hold the spoken equivalents (ああいう, 저런 종류, loại kia) rather than Sino-Xenic readings, as on the sibling correlative pages.
