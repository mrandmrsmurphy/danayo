## Notes
- [寓](../characters/寓.md) "lodge; entrust" + [話 (char)](../characters/話%20(char).md) "tale"

寓話 is a fable: a tale, often about animals, that carries a moral. Wiktionary glosses Japanese ぐうわ as "an allegory, a fable". Korean 우화 is the same word. The Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the characters.

It is a kind of [童話](童話.md) with a moral, and its point is a [比喩](比喩.md), metaphor, worked out as a story.
