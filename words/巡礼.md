## Notes
- [巡](../characters/巡.md) "go around, patrol" + [礼](../characters/礼%20(char).md) "ritual, courtesy" — "ritual circuit," a pilgrimage. Their own `stand_in` values: 巡's is [[巡回]]; 礼's is itself; none points here, so 巡礼 is an independent compound.

巡礼 is a pilgrimage: a journey to a holy place, and the pilgrim who makes it (巡礼－者, with the suffix [[－者]]). Mandarin xúnlǐ (巡禮), Cantonese ceon4 lai5, Japanese じゅんれい and Korean 순례 all use it. Compare [[寺院]] (temple) and [[聖人]] (saint).
