## Notes
- [耳 (char)](../characters/耳%20(char).md) "ear" + [目 (char)](../characters/目%20(char).md) "eye" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both are stand-ins for themselves), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

耳目 means eyes and ears — literally, and figuratively "snoops and spies," people watching and listening on another's behalf.

Mandarin ěrmù, Cantonese ji5 muk6, Japanese じもく (jimoku), and Korean 이목 (imok) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 耳目 is itself a common attested word for "eyes and ears" (attention, scrutiny) in all four languages. Vietnamese nhĩ mục is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
