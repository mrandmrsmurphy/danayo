## Notes
- [静](../characters/静.md) "still; quiet" + [止](../characters/止.md) "to stop"

静止 (traditional 靜止) is "stillness; to be motionless; to come to a standstill." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 靜止 (jìngzhǐ, Cantonese zing6 zi2) is "to be static; to be still; to be stationary"; Japanese 静止 (せいし) is "stillness, being still, calm, or motionless" and "to come to a standstill"; and Korean 정지 is the hanja form of "being static; being without movement." Wiktionary has no Vietnamese section, so `vietnamese` tĩnh chỉ is a compositional reading. It is used in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [China](../lookup/HSK/China.md), in 静止 与 懶怠 之 某 人 "someone still and idle." Related: [停止](停止.md) "stop" and [安静](安静.md) "peaceful."
