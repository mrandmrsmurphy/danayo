## Notes
- [遥](characters/遥.md) "distant" + [遠 (char)](characters/遠%20(char).md) "far" — stand-in for [[遥]], which cannot appear independently. `vietnamese: dao viễn` is compositional (not independently attested via hvdic).

Mandarin yáoyuǎn, cantonese jiu4 jyun5, japanese ようえん, and korean 요원 are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. `遙遠` is the standard traditional-character spelling variant (遥→遙), correctly listed as an alias. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 욧온 diverges from Sino-Korean 요원 entirely. No homophones (注音 ⼄ㄨ·ㄛㄋ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 遥's own `stand_in` is this exact word (遥遠), so 遥 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 遥遠; 遠's own `stand_in` is instead 遠 itself, so this word does not legitimize 遠.
