## Notes
- [淡](../characters/淡.md) "diluted, watered down, insipid" + [麻](../characters/麻.md) "hemp" — no stand-in relationship applies (淡's own stand-in is [[清淡]]; 麻's own is [[大麻]]).

淡麻 means nettle.

Korean 담마 is compositional (淡's own 담 + 麻's own 마), matching `kwin: true`. Fixed real bugs: `mandarin` had been contaminated to qiánmá, the reading of the near-synonym alias 蕁麻 — corrected to the compositional dànmá (淡's own dàn + 麻's own má); `cantonese` had been similarly contaminated to cam4 maa4 — corrected to daam6 maa4. Japanese いらくさ and Vietnamese tầm ma are the real, standard native/plant-name terms for "nettle" in each language — the same real-world-species-name convention already established for animal names (e.g. [[龍蝦]]). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄇㄇㄚ unique across both words and characters).
