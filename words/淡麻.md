## Notes
- [淡](../characters/淡.md) "diluted, watered down, insipid" + [麻](../characters/麻.md) "hemp" — no stand-in relationship applies (淡's own stand-in is [[清淡]]; 麻's own is [[大麻]]).

淡麻 means nettle.

Japanese いらくさ and Vietnamese tầm ma are the real, standard native/plant-name terms for "nettle" in each language — the same real-world-species-name convention already established for animal names (e.g. [[龍蝦]]).
