## Notes
- [掲](../characters/掲%20(char).md) "set up, install, put up" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [掲](../characters/掲%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

掲 means to set up, install, or put up (as a sign or notice).

Mandarin jiē, Cantonese kit3, Japanese けい, Korean 게, and Vietnamese yết are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄝㄊ unique across both words and characters).
