## Notes
- [女 (char)](../characters/女%20(char).md) "woman" + [王 (char)](../characters/王%20(char).md) "king"

女王 is a queen who reigns in her own right. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 女王 (Mandarin nǚwáng, Cantonese neoi5 wong4) as "queen regnant; queen", and also a woman who represents the dead person in a Ryukyuan rite. It lists Japanese じょおう, Korean 여왕 (North Korean 녀왕) and Vietnamese entries. The consort of a king is the [王妃](王妃.md).
