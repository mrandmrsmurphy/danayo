## Notes
- [分 (char)](../characters/分%20(char).md) "divide" + [裂](../characters/裂.md) "split; crack"

Mandarin fēnliè, Cantonese fan1 lit6, Japanese ぶんれつ (bunretsu), and Korean 분렬 (bullyeol, North Korean orthography) are all the standard word for "to split, divide, fracture" (人格分裂, "split personality"; 細胞分裂, "cell division"). Vietnamese phân liệt is a real, attested Sino-Vietnamese compound though the everyday native word for splitting/dividing generally is simply chia (or chia rẽ for a rift/division between people).
