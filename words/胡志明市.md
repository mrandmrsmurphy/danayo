## Notes
- [胡](../characters/胡.md) "reckless" + [志](../characters/志.md) "will, intention" + [明 (char)](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright" + [市](../characters/市.md) "city, market" — no cranberry: none of the four constituents' own `stand_in` points here.

胡志明市 is the Sino-Vietnamese name of Ho Chi Minh City (formerly Saigon), renamed in honor of Hồ Chí Minh.

**Found and fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字`/`諺文` had ho/호 instead of 胡's real reading hou/홋 — corrected to houjimyengsi/홋지명시; `注音` (ㄏㄛㄨㄐㄧㄇ⼶ㄫㄙㄧ) had already stayed correct throughout. `kwin: false` was already correct per the AND-rule (胡 individually false). Filled blank cantonese (wu4zi3ming4si5, compositional). As a proper place name, mandarin, japanese, korean, and vietnamese legitimately hold the real attested name/transliteration rather than a compositional gloss: mandarin Hú Zhìmíng shì (the standard Mandarin reading of the same characters), japanese ホーチミン市 (katakana transliteration + 市), korean 호찌민시 (Hangul transliteration + 시), and vietnamese Hồ Chí Minh (the native Vietnamese name itself).
