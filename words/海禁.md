## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [禁](../characters/禁.md) "prohibit, forbid" — the Ming and Qing dynasty policy restricting maritime trade. No stand-in relationship applies (海's own stand-in is [[海洋]]; 禁's own is [[禁止]]).

Mandarin hǎijìn, Cantonese hoi2 gam3, Japanese かいきん, Korean 해금, and Vietnamese hải cấm are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 海's and 禁's own stored citations exactly. Added the missing `kwin: false` (諺文 해김 vs Korean 해금 diverge).

**Real homophone found**: 禁 and 金 happen to share the identical Dan'a'yo reading (gim/김/ㄍㄧㄇ), making 海禁 and [[海金]] ("neptunium," a periodic-table neologism) genuine two-way homophones. Added reciprocal callouts to both pages. No third word shares this reading.
