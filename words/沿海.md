## Notes
- [沿](../characters/沿%20(char).md) "edge, side" + [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" — no stand-in relationship applies (沿's own stand-in is itself; 海's own is [[海洋]]).

沿海 means coastal.

Mandarin yánhǎi, Cantonese jyun4 hoi2, Japanese えんかい, and Korean 연해 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 沿's and 海's own stored citations exactly). Vietnamese duyên hải (沿's own duyên + 海's own hải) is also the real, standard Vietnamese term for "coastal."
