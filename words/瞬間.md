## Notes
- [瞬](../characters/瞬.md) "wink, blink" + [間](../characters/間.md) "between, interval" — literally "the interval of a blink," i.e. an instant.

瞬間 names a single moment or instant, drawing its imagery from the blink of an eye as the paradigm of briefness — comparable to English "in the blink of an eye" or "in a flash." It functions both as a noun ("a moment, an instant") and adjectivally ("momentary, instantaneous," 瞬間的な, momentary/instantaneous).

Mandarin shùnjiān, Cantonese seun3 gaan1, Japanese しゅんかん (shunkan), and Korean 순간 (sungan) are all fully compositional and are the ordinary, everyday words for "moment" in each language. Vietnamese thuấn gian is compositional; everyday Vietnamese instead prefers the native khoảnh khắc.
