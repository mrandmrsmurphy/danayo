## Notes
- [判](../characters/判.md) "judge, decide" + [決 (char)](../characters/決%20(char).md) "determine, decide"

判決 is the court's decision at the end of a case: the verdict, judgment or ruling. Wiktionary glosses Japanese はんけつ as "sentence, judgement, ruling, verdict" and Mandarin pànjué as both the act ("to find, of a judge") and the noun ("court decision"). Korean 판결 and Vietnamese phán quyết ("to pass verdict") are the matching forms, so the word is shared across all five languages.

In Dan'a'yo 判決 names the decision itself. It is what [裁判](裁判.md) produces, and it comes before the penalty: [有罪](有罪.md) and [無罪](無罪.md) are the two findings it can reach, and [刑罰](刑罰.md) is what follows from a guilty one.
