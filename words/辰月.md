## Notes
- [辰](../characters/辰.md) + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md)

Third month of the ancient Chinese lunar calendar, named for the fifth Earthly Branch 辰 (dragon).

The height of spring. The dragon is the only mythological creature among the twelve branches, signaling the month's grandeur and the full arrival of warmth.

Mandarin chényuè, cantonese san4 jyut6, and korean 진월 are all directly attested standard readings. Japanese たつつき and vietnamese tháng Thìn are both attested calendrical/zodiac phrasings, following the proper-noun/named-referent convention used for calendar terms rather than an ordinary compositional reading. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 신웓 diverges from Sino-Korean 진월 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄋ·⼔ㄊ unique among both words and characters).
