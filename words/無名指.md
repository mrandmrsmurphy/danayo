## Notes
- [無](characters/無%20(char).md) "lack" + [名](characters/名%20(char).md) "name" + [指](characters/指%20(char).md) "finger" — literally "nameless finger." No stand-in relationship applies (無's and 名's own stand-ins are themselves; 指's own is [[手指]]).

無名指 means ring finger, fourth toe.

Mandarin wúmíngzhǐ is fully compositional. Fixed a serious contamination bug in `cantonese`: wu2 min2 zi3 (matching none of the three constituents' own citations) → mou4 ming4 zi2, the correct compositional reading (無's own mou4 + 名's own ming4 + 指's own zi2). Japanese ななしゆび ("nameless finger") is the real, standard native-reading alternate term, matching the alias 名無し指; the more common real alternate 薬指 (kusuriyubi, "medicine finger") is also listed as an alias. Korean 무명지 is fully compositional and the real standard term. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with vô danh chỉ, the Sino-Vietnamese reading of this exact hanja compound (無's own vô + 名's own danh + 指's own chỉ). Reformatted `characters` and `aliases` to proper block-lists with "(char)" suffixes where applicable. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄜㄇㄧㄫㄐㄧㄜ unique across both words and characters).
