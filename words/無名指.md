## Notes
- [無](../characters/無%20(char).md) "lack" + [名](../characters/名%20(char).md) "name" + [指](../characters/指%20(char).md) "finger" — literally "nameless finger." No stand-in relationship applies (無's and 名's own stand-ins are themselves; 指's own is [[手指]]).

無名指 means ring finger, fourth toe.

Mandarin wúmíngzhǐ is fully compositional. Japanese ななしゆび ("nameless finger") is the real, standard native-reading alternate term, matching the alias 名無し指; the more common real alternate 薬指 (kusuriyubi, "medicine finger") is also listed as an alias. Korean 무명지 is fully compositional and the real standard term.
