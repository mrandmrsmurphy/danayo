## Notes
- [孟](../characters/孟.md) "eldest, first" + [冬](../characters/冬%20(char).md) "winter" — no stand-in relationship applies (孟 is a proper-noun-only character with no specific stand-in word; 冬's own stand-in is itself).

First month of winter. The harvest is fully in; the world quiets. Corresponds to [[亥月]] and [[良月]].

Mandarin mèngdōng, Cantonese maang6 dung1, Korean 맹동, Japanese もうとう, and Vietnamese Mạnh Đông are all standard, fully compositional readings.
