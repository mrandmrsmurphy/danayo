## Notes
- [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "sun" + [本](../characters/本%20(char).md) "origin" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 日 and 本's own stand-ins are themselves). Literally "origin of the sun," the source of the English "Japan" (via Chinese/Malay intermediaries).

日本 means Japan.

Mandarin Rìběn, Cantonese jat6 bun2, Japanese にほん, Korean 일본, and Vietnamese Nhật Bản are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese also officially uses にっぽん (nippon), a more formal/emphatic reading used in contexts like currency and national ceremonies — both readings are equally official. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄧㄊㄅㄛㄋ unique across both words and characters).
