## Notes
- [運](../characters/運.md) "move, transport" + [転 (char)](../characters/転.md) "turn, revolve" — "operate, drive."

Mandarin yùnzhuàn, cantonese wan6 zyun2, japanese うんてん, korean 운전, and vietnamese vận chuyển (a directly attested, extremely common Vietnamese word — "to transport, transfer") are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. `運轉` is the standard traditional-character spelling variant (転→轉), correctly listed as an alias.
