## Notes
- [罪](../characters/罪%20(char).md) "sin" + [悪](../characters/悪%20(char).md) "evil" — "sin and evil." Their own `stand_in` values: 罪's is itself; 悪's is itself; none points here, so 罪悪 is an independent compound.

罪悪 is sin seen as wrong and evil together: iniquity. Mandarin zuì'è, Cantonese zeoi6 ok3, Japanese ざいあく, Korean 죄악 and Vietnamese tội ác all use it. Korean Bibles use 죄악 for the Hebrew "iniquity."

**Hebrew**: it best translates *ʿāwōn* (עָוֺן) "iniquity," the twisted, guilty condition of a person and the weight of it ("wash me thoroughly from my iniquity," Psalm 51:2). *ʿĀwōn* also means the punishment for the guilt (Genesis 4:13), and that second sense is [[業報]]. For *ʾāshām* (אָשָׁם) "guilt," use [[有罪]]. Compare [[罪]] (the general word), [[罪業]] (sin as a weighty deed) and [[邪悪]] (wicked).
