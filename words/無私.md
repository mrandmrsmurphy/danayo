## Notes
- [無](characters/無%20(char).md) "lack" + [私](characters/私%20(char).md) "personal, private" — no stand-in relationship applies (無's own stand-in is itself; 私's own is [[私立]]).

無私 means selfless, disinterested ("without self-interest").

Mandarin wúsī, Cantonese mou4 si1, Japanese むし (MU+SHI), Korean 무사, and Vietnamese vô tư are all fully compositional, matching 無's and 私's own stored citations exactly, and also the real, standard everyday words in each language. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄜㄙㄧㄜ unique across both words and characters).
