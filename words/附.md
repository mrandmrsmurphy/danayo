## Notes
- [附](../characters/附%20(char).md) "affix; adhere" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [附](../characters/附%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

附 means to affix or adhere.

Mandarin fù, Cantonese fu6, Japanese ふ, Korean 부, and Vietnamese phò are all standard, fully compositional readings. Homophone of [[扶]] "hold on to for support" (both 注音 ㄅㄨ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words.
