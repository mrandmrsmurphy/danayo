## Notes
- [脳](../characters/脳%20(char).md) "brain" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 脳 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

脳 means the brain, as in 樟脳 (camphor) and 電脳 (computer).

Mandarin nǎo and cantonese nou5 are standard, fully compositional readings. **Found and fixed two real bugs**: `korean` and `vietnamese` both held the literal string `"null"` — fixed to 뇌 and não (the character's own sole stored values). Japanese uses the native reading なずき (nazuki, an older/literary word for "brain") rather than the on-readings ノウ/ドウ (NOU/DOU) for the bare character alone. A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone with [[悩]] ("angered, mad") — coincidental, unrelated in meaning.
