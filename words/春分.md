## Notes
- [春 (char)](../characters/春%20(char).md) "springtime" + [分 (char)](../characters/分%20(char).md) "divide" — literally "spring's division," the point at which day and night split evenly.

春分 is one of the 二十四節氣 (twenty-four solar terms), falling around March 20-21. It's the spring counterpart to [[秋分]] (autumn equinox), both using 分 ("division," an even day/night split) in contrast to the solstices [[冬至]]/[[夏至]], which use 至 ("extreme") instead, since a solstice reaches a peak rather than an even division.

Mandarin chūnfēn, Cantonese ceon1 fan1, Korean 춘분 (chunbun), and Japanese しゅんぶん (shunbun) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real everyday term for the spring equinox in its language. Vietnamese xuân phân is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, matching 春's xuân and 分's own phân. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 춘분 matches Sino-Korean 춘분 exactly. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄑㄨㄋㄅㄨㄋ unique among both words and characters).
