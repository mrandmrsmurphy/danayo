## Notes
- [毎 (char)](../characters/毎%20(char).md) "every; each; distributive" + [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person; human being"

毎人 is the distributive correlative in the 人 ("person") column of the correlative table: "each person, every person individually, per person". The character [毎 (char)](../characters/毎%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *moi*, ㄇㄛㄧ; its alias 每 is the traditional form, so 每人 is an `aliases` entry here, not a separate file) is the distributive morpheme of [毎日](毎日.md) "every day", [毎週](毎週.md) "every week", [毎月](毎月.md) "every month" and [毎年](毎年.md) "every year"; the free word is [毎](毎.md). [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *nin*, ㄋㄧㄋ) is the plain word for a person. The predicate is applied to each person in turn, and each case can be checked separately. Illustrative only (invented, not from `grammar/`): 毎人、役割有 "each person has a role".

Its part of speech is 副用名詞 (adverbial noun), the class of every cell of the 毎 row ([毎事](毎事.md), [毎物](毎物.md), [毎名](毎名.md), [毎処](毎処.md), [毎時](毎時.md), [毎様](毎様.md), [毎多](毎多.md), [毎類](毎類.md)) and of [毎日](毎日.md); `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md` (line 11) lists "Adverbial Nouns" as the class able to be the frame with 0-derivation. `grammar/Linguistics Features.md` (line 68) states the rule: an adverbial noun may be the topic or the frame of a clause with no case or adverbial marking, but needs marking when it modifies the main verb. So 毎人 can open a clause as a frame, while the other cells of the person column, [此人](此人.md), [其人](其人.md), [彼人](彼人.md), [某人](某人.md) and [皆人](皆人.md), are pronoun-like, and the interrogative slot is normally filled by 誰.

It contrasts with its column neighbours as follows. The collective [皆人](皆人.md) "everyone; all people" treats the people as one group, whereas 毎人 distributes over them one at a time: the same distributive/collective split runs through the whole table, as in [毎事](毎事.md) against [皆事](皆事.md). [某人](某人.md) "someone; a certain person" is the indefinite, naming an unspecified individual, and the interrogative slot (誰 normally; [何人](何人.md) is rude, and [何名](何名.md) very polite, per the note in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`, line 154) asks which person, so both are indefinite where 毎人 is quantificational. [其人](其人.md), which is also Dan'a'yo's gender-neutral third-person pronoun, and the deictic [此人](此人.md) "this person" and [彼人](彼人.md) "that person yonder" each point at one particular person, whereas 毎人 ranges over all of them. The 名 column supplies the polite counterpart, [毎名](毎名.md) "each honoured person".

The cross-linguistic fields were checked against Wiktionary on 2026-10-10. Mandarin 每人 (měirén) has an entry, a noun glossed "each person; everybody; per person", with Cantonese reading mui5 jan4 (entered in `cantonese`); 每人 is the `aliases` form. Wiktionary has no entry for the Japanese 毎人, for Korean 매인, or for the phrases 一人当たり or 사람마다 (the last lookups were rate-limited, so those absences are weakly checked). The earlier `japanese` and `korean` fields held 一人当たり and 사람마다; they are removed because they could not be verified. From memory, not re-checked: Japanese 一人当たり (hitori atari) "per person, per head" is a real, common phrase (一人当たりGDP "GDP per capita"), and Korean 사람마다 "each person" uses the distributive -마다. Vietnamese is omitted: *mỗi người* is from memory, not re-checked, and hvdic.thivien.net covers single characters, not phrases. Dan'a'yo keeps the sense shared across CJKV; no narrowing is needed.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md)'s correlative table (line 125; the same chart is in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`, line 146 in the 人 ("person") row, with the column header cell 毎 on line 142), 毎人 is the third cell of the 毎 ("every") row, after [毎事](毎事.md) "every matter" and [毎物](毎物.md) "each thing" and before [毎名](毎名.md), [毎処](毎処.md) "at each place", [毎時](毎時.md) "each time", [毎様](毎様.md) "each manner", [毎多](毎多.md) "each amount" and [毎類](毎類.md) "each kind". The table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), 63 forms, so 毎人 is parsed compositionally: 毎 = distributive, 人 = person. The 羅馬字 is *moinin*, from the character pages (毎 *moi*, 人 *nin*); the earlier *moynin* disagreed with them.

The 注音 ㄇㄛㄧㄋㄧㄋ carries no syllable-boundary dot. `grammar/文法 - 02音韻論.md` (line 116) gives the structure (C)(G)V(C) with a single coda, and the glide ㄧ (y) is a coda in ㄇㄛㄧ, so ㄇㄛㄧㄋ + ㄧㄋ is illegal (two codas), leaving ㄇㄛㄧ + ㄋㄧㄋ (both exist in `syllables/`). The only other segmentation would split the diphthong ㄛㄧ, as ㄇㄛ + ㄧㄋ + ㄧㄋ; the vault treats ㄛㄧ as the single rhyme here, as in [毎日](毎日.md) and [毎事](毎事.md), which carry no dot either. This is the vault's convention rather than a rule stated in the grammar file.
