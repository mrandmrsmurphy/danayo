## Notes
- [襲](../characters/襲.md) "attack by surprise" + [撃 (char)](../characters/撃%20(char).md) "strike"

襲撃 is a raid or an assault: an attack that comes suddenly on a place or a person. Wiktionary glosses Japanese しゅうげき as "raid; to raid, make an attack, descend on". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 습격 matches.

The surprise is the point, as in [掩襲](掩襲.md), the ambush. An open, general attack is [攻撃](攻撃.md).
