## Notes
- [支](../characters/支.md) "branch" + [那](../characters/那%20(char).md) "that" — phonetic compound; no stand-in relationship applies (支's own stand-in is [[支部]]; 那's own is itself).

支那 is an old exonym for China (Zhina), historically neutral but now largely disused/pejorative outside specific historical contexts.

Mandarin Zhīnà, Cantonese zi1 naa4, Japanese しな, Korean 지나, and Vietnamese Chi Na are all standard, fully compositional readings. Removed redundant `品詞`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄋㄚ unique across both words and characters).
