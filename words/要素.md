## Notes
- [要](../characters/要.md) "important, need" is separately legitimized by [[重要]]. [素](../characters/素.md) "element, plain thing" is legitimized by this exact compound — but since 要's own stand-in points elsewhere, transitivity fails. No cranberry.

Mandarin yàosù, korean 요소, and vietnamese yếu tố (all also the real common words for "element/factor") confirmed standard and compositional.
