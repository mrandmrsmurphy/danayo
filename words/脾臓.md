## Notes
- [脾](../characters/脾.md) "spleen" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 脾 cannot appear independently outside this compound. [臓](../characters/臓.md) "organ, entrails" is separately legitimized by [[内臓]].

脾臓 combines 脾 ("spleen") with 臓 ("organ") — "spleen." In traditional Chinese medicine, 脾 (the TCM "spleen") was historically conflated with the anatomical pancreas, a mismatch that persists in some folk usage even though modern medical Chinese/Japanese/Korean all correctly distinguish 脾臓/膵臓 as spleen/pancreas respectively.
