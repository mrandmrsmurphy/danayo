## Notes
- [軽 (char)](../characters/軽%20(char).md) "light, trivial" + [視 (char)](../characters/視%20(char).md) "regard, look upon" — to look down on; to regard as insignificant; to underestimate the worth or danger of something.

Wiktionary: Japanese 軽視 (けいし) "belittlement, despisal, disdain, slight, neglect" and, as a verb, "to belittle, disdain, despise, slight, neglect"; Chinese 輕視 "to not attach importance to; to look down on; to think little of; to despise", kept as the traditional-form alias; Korean 경시 and Vietnamese khinh thị are the Han forms, "contempt; negligence; disdain; slight" and "to think little of; to despise". Neither character is legitimized here: both 軽's and 視's `stand_in` are themselves.
