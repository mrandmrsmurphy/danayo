## Notes
- [当 (char)](../characters/当%20(char).md) "current, ongoing" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — literally "the current/that very day," i.e. the same day (as some referenced event).

当日/當日 is one of three near-synonymous "same day" compounds in this vault alongside [[即日]] and [[是日]], distinguished by register and nuance: 当日 emphasizes "the day in question" from an administrative/scheduling standpoint (as in event announcements), while 即日 stresses immediacy ("this very day, right away") and 是日 is the more literary/classical of the three.

Mandarin dāngrì, Cantonese dong1 jat6, Korean 당일 (dangil), and Japanese とうじつ (tōjitsu) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese đương nhật is likewise compositional, built from 当's "đương" reading (one of three attested Hán Việt readings for 当/當 — the "holding/bearing" sense, as in 当番/đương phiên "on duty") + 日's "nhật." `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 당닏 diverges from Sino-Korean 당일 in the second syllable. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄫㄋㄧㄊ unique among both words and characters).
