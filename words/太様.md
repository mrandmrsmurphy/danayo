## Notes
- [太 (char)](../characters/太%20(char).md) "extremely, too" + [様 (char)](../characters/様%20(char).md) (adverbial suffix) — "greatly, exceedingly."

太様 is an adverb meaning greatly or exceedingly, built by adding the adverbial suffix 様 to 太 "extremely, too". It is a Dan'a'yo compositional coinage rather than a word attested in the source languages: Mandarin tàiyàng, Cantonese taai3 joeng6, Japanese たいよう, Korean 태양 and Vietnamese thái dạng are its compositional readings. Its Korean and Vietnamese forms are not independently attested as an adverb, and the Korean 태양 collides exactly with the real Korean word for "sun" ([[太陽]]), as does the Dan'a'yo pronunciation, hence the homophone callout above. Japanese とても (written 迚も) is the nearest everyday equivalent for "very", an ad-hoc kanji rendering (当て字) rather than a reading of this compound.
