## Notes
- [綿](../characters/綿.md) "cotton" + [花](../characters/花.md) "flower" — 綿's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 花's own is [[草花]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 綿花 legitimizes 綿 alone. `kwin: true` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: true`.

綿花 names cotton — literally "cotton-flower," the fibrous boll of the cotton plant.

Mandarin miánhua, Cantonese min4 faa1, Japanese めんか (menka), and Korean 면화 (myeonhwa) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 綿花 is itself a common attested word for "cotton" in all four languages. Vietnamese miên hoa is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
