## Notes
- [催 (char)](../characters/催%20(char).md) "press (a person for); urge (a person to do); request; demand"

催 combines [[人]] ("person") with phonetic [[崔]] (an alternate form also appears as 摧). It means "to press, urge, hasten (someone to do something)" — the core sense behind 催促 (cuīcù, "to urge on, press, hasten," the same word seen from [[促]]'s own side earlier in this sweep), 催眠 (cuīmián, "to hypnotize," literally "to induce sleep"), and 催化 (cuīhuà, "to catalyze").

Mandarin cuī, Cantonese ceoi1, and Japanese さい (sai, on'yomi, as in 開催 かいさい "to hold [an event]") alongside native もよお(す) (moyō(su), "to hold, sponsor [an event]; to feel [an urge]") all preserve the sense. Korean 최 (choe) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 재촉하다 (jaechokhada, "to press, urge"). Vietnamese thôi is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in thôi thúc (催促, "to urge on, press") — the character page's other three stored candidates (thoi, thui, thòi) are tonal/orthographic near-misses and look like corpus noise.
