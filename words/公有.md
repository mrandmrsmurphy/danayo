## Notes
- [公](../characters/公%20(char).md) "public" + [有](../characters/有%20(char).md) "have; own" — "publicly owned." Their own `stand_in` values: 公's is itself; 有's is itself; none points here, so 公有 is an independent compound.

公有 is to be publicly owned, the opposite of [[私有]]. Mandarin gōngyǒu, Cantonese gung1 jau5, Japanese こうゆう and Korean 공유 all use it, and Vietnamese công hữu does too. Do not confuse it with [[共有]] "to share, co-own," which is a near homophone. Compare [[所有]].
