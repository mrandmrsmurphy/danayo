## Notes
- [色](../characters/色.md) "color" + [素](../characters/素.md) "plain, element" — pigment.

色素 is pigment in the chemical and biological sense: a substance that gives color to a material, a tissue or a food (melanin, chlorophyll, carotene). It is 色 plus 素 "element, basic constituent," the same "basic constituent" sense of 素 as in [[要素]]. Mandarin sèsù, Cantonese sik1 sou3, Japanese しきそ and Korean 색소 are the standard readings, and Vietnamese sắc tố is the same compound. The industrial coloring agents applied to materials are [[顔料]] (pigment for paint) and [[染料]] (dye for cloth).
