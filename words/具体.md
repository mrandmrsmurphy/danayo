## Notes
- [具 (char)](../characters/具.md) "tool, implement" (stand-in: [工具](工具.md)) + [体 (char)](../characters/体.md) "body, form" (stand-in: [体系](体系.md))

具体 means concrete, specific. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 具體 (alias here; Mandarin jùtǐ, Cantonese geoi6 tai2) as "specific; particular; concrete; exact", Japanese ぐたい as "something concrete, tangible", and Vietnamese cụ thể as "specific; concrete; detailed". It lists Korean 구체 as a hanja form of the same word.
