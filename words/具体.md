## Notes
- [具 (char)](../characters/具.md) "tool, implement" (stand-in: [[工具]]) + [体 (char)](../characters/体.md) "body, form" (stand-in: [[体系]]) — concrete, specific; the opposite of abstract.

具体 means concrete/specific, as invoked by `文法 - 02音韻論`'s 具体音価非限定 ("the concrete phonetic value is not fixed"). Mandarin jùtǐ, Japanese ぐたい, Korean 구체, and Vietnamese cụ thể are all real, standard terms, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Cantonese geoi6 tai2 is a straightforward compositional reading. `kwin` is false: both 具 and 体 are individually false.

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot**: the concatenated string `ㄍㄨㄊㄝㄧ` admits four legal partitions — the intended 具(ㄍㄨ)+体(ㄊㄝㄧ), and three false alternates that either split 体's own syllable or disagree on the first cut entirely — so the dot at the true 具|体 boundary is required: ㄍㄨ·ㄊㄝㄧ.
