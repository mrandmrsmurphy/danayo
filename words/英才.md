## Notes
- [英](../characters/英.md) "outstanding, brilliant" + [才](../characters/才.md) "talent" — a gifted talent, prodigy; widely used in education (e.g. Korean 영재교육, "gifted education"). Attested across all five languages.

The Knowledge page lists 英才 as "a gifted talent, prodigy," and Dan'a'yo keeps that sense, a person of outstanding ability, often young and worth cultivating. It is related to the existing words [天才](天才.md) "talented, genius," the stand-in for the character 才, and [英雄](英雄.md) "hero," the stand-in for 英, which shares 英 "outstanding" but names courage and not talent. The Korean 영재 differs from the Dan'a'yo 諺文 엉재 in the first syllable's vowel, so `kwin` is false. The readings are Mandarin yīngcái, Cantonese jing1 coi4, Japanese eisai (えいさい) and Sino-Vietnamese anh tài. The usage note about education is the page's existing account and has not been re-checked here.
