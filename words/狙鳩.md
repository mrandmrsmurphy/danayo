## Notes
- [狙](../characters/狙.md) "ambush" + [鳩](../characters/鳩.md) "pigeon" — no stand-in relationship applies (狙's own stand-in is [[狙撃]]; 鳩's own is [[鳩鳥]]). The classical form is properly written 雎鳩 (a rare character 雎 without its own vault page); 狙 serves as the practical substitute here, and coincidentally already shares 雎's own Mandarin reading (jū), so no reading correction was needed.

狙鳩 (properly 雎鳩) is the osprey, named in the opening line of the *Classic of Poetry* (詩經): 關關雎鳩.

Japanese みさご is the real, standard native word for osprey.
