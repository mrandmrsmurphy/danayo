## Notes
- [狙](characters/狙.md) "ambush" + [鳩](characters/鳩.md) "pigeon" — no stand-in relationship applies (狙's own stand-in is [[狙撃]]; 鳩's own is [[鳩鳥]]). The classical form is properly written 雎鳩 (a rare character 雎 without its own vault page); 狙 serves as the practical substitute here, and coincidentally already shares 雎's own Mandarin reading (jū), so no reading correction was needed.

狙鳩 (properly 雎鳩) is the osprey, named in the opening line of the *Classic of Poetry* (詩經): 關關雎鳩.

Mandarin jūjiū and Cantonese zeoi1 kau1 were already correct and fully compositional. Japanese みさご is the real, standard native word for osprey. Filled the entirely missing `korean` with 저구 and `vietnamese` with thư cưu — the latter is also the actual traditional Vietnamese rendering of this term in translations of the *Classic of Poetry* ("Quan quan thư cưu"). Reformatted `japanese` from a single-item list to a scalar, matching page convention. Removed the redundant duplicate `品詞` field and added missing `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄛㄎ⼜ unique across both words and characters).
