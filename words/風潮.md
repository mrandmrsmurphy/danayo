## Notes
- [風 (char)](../characters/風%20(char).md) "wind" + [潮](../characters/潮.md) "tide" — no `#cranberry`: 風's own `stand_in` is 風 itself, 潮's is [[潮汐]]; neither points here.

風潮 (fēngcháo) is a real Mandarin word covering several related senses: literally "storm, tempest," and — its most common modern use — "trend, tendency" (as in 学界风潮, "trends in academic circles"), as well as "mass movement, unrest." Cantonese 風潮 (fung1ciu4), Japanese ふうちょう (fūchō), and Korean 풍조 (pungjo) all carry the same range. Sino-Vietnamese phong trào has narrowed specifically to the "trend, movement" sense — it is in fact one of the most common words in Vietnamese, used for everything from social movements to fashions.
