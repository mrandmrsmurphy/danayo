## Notes
- [前 (char)](../characters/前%20(char).md) "front; before"

前's original form 歬 combined 止 ("foot") + [[舟]] ("boat") — a foot on a boat, moving forward. In the modern glyph, 止 simplified to 䒑 and 舟 to 月, with 刀 ("knife") added later; that later addition now belongs to the derived character [[剪]] ("scissors"), while 前 itself absorbed the "front, before" sense entirely, shedding any cutting meaning. It heads an enormous compound family for both spatial "front" ([[目前]] "right before one's eyes," [[眼前]] "before one's eyes") and temporal "before, previous" ([[前年]] "previous year," [[前世紀]] "previous century," [[紀元前]] "B.C.").

Mandarin qián, Cantonese cin4, and Japanese ぜん/せん (zen/sen, on'yomi, fully productive: 前提 ぜんてい "premise, prerequisite") alongside native まえ (mae, the everyday word for "front, before") all preserve both spatial and temporal senses. Korean 전 (jeon) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 앞 (ap, "front," the everyday native word). Vietnamese tiền is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in tiền đề (前提, "premise, prerequisite") and tiền tuyến ("front line"); the character page's other stored candidate, tèn, doesn't correspond to any attested reading and looks like corpus noise.

This word shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading (jen/전/ㄐㄝㄋ) with its own derived character's word, [[剪]] ("scissors," still awaiting its own turn) — the reciprocal half of this callout will be completed when it comes up.
