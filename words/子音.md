## Notes
- [子](../characters/子.md) "child" + [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" — no stand-in relationship applies (子's own stand-in is [[児子]]; 音's own is [[音楽]]).

子音 means consonant — a speech sound produced with obstruction of the airflow, as opposed to a vowel.

Mandarin zǐyīn, Cantonese zi2 jam1, Japanese しいん, Korean 자음, and Vietnamese phụ âm are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `羅馬字` had 子's syllable mistyped ji→jǝ (諺文/注音 were already correct). No homophones (注音 ㄐㄜㄨㄇ unique across both words and characters).
