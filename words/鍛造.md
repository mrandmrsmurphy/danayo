## Notes
- [鍛](../characters/鍛.md) "forge" + [造](../characters/造.md) "make, build" — forging.

鍛造 is forging: shaping metal by hammering or pressing it, usually hot, as the pair of [[鋳造]], casting. This is the literal metalworking act. [[鍛錬]], which the vault glosses "to forge," has drifted toward the figurative sense of rigorous training, and [[鍛冶]] names the trade; 鍛造 is the word for the forging process itself. Mandarin duànzào, Japanese たんぞう and Korean 단조 are the standard readings.
