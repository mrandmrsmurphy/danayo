## Notes
- [守](../characters/守.md) "protect" + [衛](../characters/衛.md) "protect" — this word is the stand-in for [守](../characters/守.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

守衛 is a near-synonym doubling, both halves independently meaning "to guard, protect" — 守 leans toward passive defense/custody (as in 遵守 "abide by," 防守 "defend"), while 衛 leans toward active protective escort (as in 護衛, 前衛). Together they name both the act of guarding and, by extension, the person who does it — a watchman or security guard.

Japanese しゅえい, Korean 수위, and Vietnamese thủ vệ (verified) are all plain, standard, non-drifted readings. No HSK level is given for the compound.
