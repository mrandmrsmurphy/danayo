## Notes
- [今 (char)](../characters/今%20(char).md) "now" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — "today." mandarin `jīnrì` matches the headword 今日 directly (日's own rì reading); the far more common colloquial pronunciation jīntiān belongs to the alias 今天 (built on 天's own tiān reading) instead — kept distinct rather than conflated. See also <ruby>[[今日安]]<rt>ㄍㄧㄇㄋㄧㄊ·ㄚㄋ</rt></ruby> (a greeting built on this word) and the more poetic/formal synonym <ruby>[[本日]]<rt>ㄏㄛㄋㄋㄧㄊ</rt></ruby>. `kwin` is false: both 今 and 日 independently carry `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄇㄋㄧㄊ unique among both words and characters).

**Real content bugs found and fixed**: `mandarin` was `jīntiān` and `cantonese` was `gam1 tin1` — both using 天's own tiān/tin1 reading rather than 日's own rì/jat6, an apparent mix-up with the alias 今天. Corrected to `jīnrì`/`gam1 jat6`, compositional from 今日's own two characters.
