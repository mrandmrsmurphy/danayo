## Notes
- [濁](../characters/濁.md) "muddy, turbid" + [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" — no stand-in relationship applies (濁's own stand-in is [[混濁]]; 音's own is [[音楽]]).

濁音 means voiced sound, a linguistic term for consonants produced with vocal cord vibration (the counterpart to [[清音]]).

Mandarin zhuóyīn, Cantonese zuk6 jam1, Japanese だくおん, Korean 탁음, and Vietnamese trọc âm are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 濁's and 音's own stored citations exactly), and also the real linguistics term in each language.
