## Notes
- [翻 (char)](../characters/翻%20(char).md) "flip" + [身](../characters/身.md) "body" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (翻's is [[翻]] itself, 身's is [[身体]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply.

翻身 means to turn oneself over, and figuratively to emancipate oneself — to throw off oppression.

Mandarin fānshēn, Cantonese faan1 san1, Japanese ほんしん (honshin), and Korean 번신 (beonsin) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 翻身 is itself a common attested word for "turn over, emancipate" in all four languages. Vietnamese phiên thân is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading.
