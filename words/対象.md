## Notes
- [対](../characters/対.md) "face; pair with" + [象](../characters/象.md) "elephant; image, symbol" — no stand-in relationship applies (対's own `stand_in` is [[反対]], 象's is [[大象]]).

対象 names the object or target of an action, attention, or study — 対 contributing its "facing, paired-with" sense and 象 its extended "image, appearance, figure" sense (from "elephant" → a thing's visible form/likeness → an object of reference), not its literal "elephant" meaning. Along with [[対照]] and [[対称]], this is the third member of a genuine Japanese-only homophone cluster: all three read たいしょう in Japanese despite being fully distinct at the Dan'a'yo/Mandarin/Cantonese/Korean/Vietnamese level (対象's own korean 대상 and vietnamese đối tượng are both plainly distinct from 対照's 대조/đối chiếu and 対称's 대칭/đối xứng).

No homophones at the Dan'a'yo level (注音 ㄉㄛㄧㄙ⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters); HSK level 2 already correct.
