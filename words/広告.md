## Notes
- [広](../characters/広%20(char).md) "wide, broad" + [告](../characters/告.md) "announce, tell" — "announce widely." Their own `stand_in` values: 広's is itself; 告's is [[告訴]]; none points here, so 広告 is an independent compound.

広告 is an advertisement: a public notice that offers goods or services. Mandarin guǎnggào (廣告), Cantonese gwong2 gou3, Japanese こうこく and Korean 광고 all use it, and Vietnamese quảng cáo does too. Dan'a'yo takes it as both the notice and, with 事詞 use, the act of advertising. Compare [[宣伝]] (publicity, propaganda) and [[商業]].
