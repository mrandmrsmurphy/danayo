## Notes
- [辺](../characters/辺.md) "edge, side" + [境](../characters/境.md) "border, boundary" — "frontier, border."

Mandarin biānjìng, cantonese bin1 ging2, japanese へんきょう, korean 변경, and vietnamese biên cảnh are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. `边境`/`邊境` are the standard simplified/traditional spelling variants (辺→边/邊), correctly listed as aliases.

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 辺's `stand_in` (辺界) nor 境's own `stand_in` (地境) points to this compound.
