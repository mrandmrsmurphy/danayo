## Notes
- [下 (char)](../characters/下%20(char).md) "below, down" + [水 (char)](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [道 (char)](../characters/道%20(char).md) "road, way" — sewer.

下水道 is a sewer: the underground channel that carries used water and waste away from a city. It is "below-water-way," the same compound in Mandarin xiàshuǐdào, Cantonese haa6 seoi2 dou6, Japanese げすいどう and Korean 하수도. Japanese uses it also for a rainwater drainage channel, and Dan'a'yo takes the wider sense: any piped or tunneled public drain. A small open ditch is a [[溝]] and a street gutter or culvert is an [[排水溝]]; 下水道 is the large, usually covered, municipal system. Vietnamese leans native (cống) but also has a formal Sino-Vietnamese form.
