## Notes
- [執](../characters/執.md) "carry out" + [行](../characters/行%20(char).md) "act, go" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [執](../characters/執.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (行's own stand-in is itself).

執行 means to execute, perform, or carry out — putting a plan, order, or law into effect.

Japanese しっこう, Korean 집행, and Vietnamese chấp hành (using 行's "act, carry out" sense reading hành, distinct from its "row, goods" sense hàng) are all standard readings. Fixed unquoted `hsk_level`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄆㄏㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
