## Notes
- [人](../characters/人%20(char).md) "people" + [口](../characters/口%20(char).md) "mouth"
	- _mouth_ was formerly a measure word, for people, livestock or utensils

`vietnamese: nhân khẩu` is directly attested everyday Vietnamese for "population" (the standard census term, also seen in hộ khẩu "household registration"), a fully natural match. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 닌콧 diverges from Sino-Korean 인구 in the second syllable's coda.
