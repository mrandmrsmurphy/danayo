## Notes
- [感](../characters/感.md) "moved" + [服](../characters/服.md) "submit; admire"

感服 is to admire: to be so impressed by someone's ability or conduct that one yields to them. Wiktionary glosses Japanese かんぷく as "admiration; to admire" and Vietnamese cảm phục as "to hold in respect; to admire; to esteem". Korean 감복 is the reading of the characters, and the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the characters.

It is admiration strong enough to bow to its object, beyond the surprise of [賛嘆](賛嘆.md), and a deeper form of [尊敬](尊敬.md), respect. Looking up to someone is [仰望](仰望.md).
