## Notes
- [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "great" + [帝](../characters/帝.md) "emperor" — "the Great Emperor."

大帝 is a title of honour in two settings. In Daoism it is the title of some deities, as in 紫微大帝, the Ziwei Emperor, and of the Jade Emperor in the form 玉皇大帝. For monarchs it is the epithet "the Great", used of Chinese emperors such as 漢武大帝 and, in translation, of foreign rulers: 彼得大帝, Peter the Great, and 蘇萊曼大帝, Suleiman the Magnificent. Japanese たいてい is used the same way, for Meiji, Charles the Great and Peter the Great. The Mandarin and Japanese readings are the attested ones; the Cantonese, Korean and Vietnamese fields are compositional (Korean follows the North Korean readings).

See [皇帝](皇帝.md), "emperor", and [玉皇](玉皇.md).
