## Notes
- [浩](../characters/浩.md) "vast, great" + [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big" — this word is the viable form of [浩](../characters/浩.md): the character's meaning (vast; great; abundant) is identical, but 浩 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 浩大 is its `stand_in` compound.

浩大 means vast or grand in scale. English Wiktionary lists it only for Chinese 浩大 (Mandarin hàodà, Cantonese hou6 daai6), glossed "great; huge; vast" and "magnificent; grand; splendid". It has no Japanese, Korean or Vietnamese entry, so Japanese こうだい, Korean 호대 and Vietnamese hạo đại here are built from the characters.

浩大 is the stand-in word for [浩](../characters/浩.md): the character's meaning, vast, is identical, but 浩 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word.
