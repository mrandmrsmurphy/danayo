## Notes
- [浩](../characters/浩.md) "vast, great" + [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big" — this word is the viable form of [浩](../characters/浩.md): the character's meaning (vast; great; abundant) is identical, but 浩 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 浩大 is its `stand_in` compound.

浩大 describes something vast or magnificent in scale — 浩大工程 ("a massive engineering project"), 声勢浩大 ("of tremendous scale and momentum") — near-synonyms built on the same 浩 root include 浩瀚 ("boundless, vast," especially of water or expanses) and 浩蕩 ("vast, boundless, sweeping"), while the idiomatic 浩然之気 ("the spirit of moral integrity and righteousness") extends 浩's "vast" sense figuratively into moral/spiritual grandeur.

Japanese こうだい and Korean 호대 are both standard compositional Sino-readings. Vietnamese hạo đại is likewise standard. No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄨㄉㄚㄧ unique across both words and characters).
