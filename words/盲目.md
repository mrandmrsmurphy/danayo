## Notes
- [盲](../characters/盲.md) "blind" + [目 (char)](../characters/目%20(char).md) "eye" — 盲's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 目's own is [[目]] itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 盲 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

盲目 means blind, both literally (loss of sight) and, very commonly, figuratively — a "blind" love, faith, obedience, or pursuit that proceeds without judgment or awareness of consequences (盲目的な信頼, "blind trust"; 盲目崇拝, "blind worship"). This figurative extension is the word's dominant modern use across the sphere.

Mandarin mángmù, Cantonese maang4 muk6, Japanese もうもく (mōmoku), and Korean 맹목 (maengmok) are all fully compositional and share both the literal and figurative senses. Vietnamese uses the native mù rather than a Sino-Vietnamese compound; the fuller native expression đui mù (using two near-synonymous native words for emphasis) is also common.
