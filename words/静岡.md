## Notes
- [静](../characters/静.md) "quiet" + [岡](../characters/岡.md) "hill"

静岡 is Shizuoka, written "quiet hill". The city was renamed from Sunpu (駿府) in 1869, supposedly after the nearby hill Shizuhata (from memory, not re-checked); the reading しずおか is native: 静 (しず, "quiet") plus 岡 (おか, "hill"). The first character is the shinjitai form of 靜.

Shizuoka stretches along the Pacific coast between Tokyo and Nagoya, with the south side of Mount Fuji and the Izu Peninsula. It grows more tea than any other prefecture and is home to the manufacturing city of Hamamatsu.

In Dan'a'yo 静岡 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 시즈오카 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
