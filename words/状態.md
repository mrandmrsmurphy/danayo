## Notes
- [状](../characters/状.md) "form; shape (stand-in: [[形状]])" + [態](../characters/態.md) "manner; attitude (stand-in: [[態度]])"

Wiktionary: Chinese 狀態 "state (of a situation, object, person, etc.)"; Japanese 状態 "the state or condition (of something at a certain time)"; Korean 상태 and Vietnamese trạng thái are the Han forms, "status; condition" and "status; state". The spelling 狀態 is the alias. It fills the "state" gap, distinct from [[陳述]], "to state".
