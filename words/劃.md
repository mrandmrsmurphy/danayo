## Notes
- [劃 (char)](../characters/劃%20(char).md) "divide; mark off; delimit"

劃 (alternate form 划) is 形声: semantic 刂 ("knife") + phonetic 畫 — to cut a dividing line, hence "to demarcate, mark off, plan out." It appears in [[企劃]] ("to plan, scheme") and, via its phonetic 畫/画, is closely related to that character's own "picture, drawing, stroke" senses — in Japanese, 劃 is sometimes directly replaced by 画 (daiyōji, a substitute-character convention).

Mandarin huà, Cantonese waak6, and Japanese かく (kaku, on'yomi, largely confined to compounds like 企劃/企画 きかく "planning") alongside native わ(る) (wa(ru), "to divide") all preserve the sense. Korean 획 (hoek) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 긋다 (geutda, "to draw a line"). Vietnamese hoạch is the Hán Việt reading, extremely well attested in kế hoạch (計劃/計画, "a plan") and quy hoạch ("planning, zoning"); the character page's other three stored candidates (gạch "brick," vạch "a line, mark," đạch unclear) are unrelated or noise and were not used here.
