## Notes
- [木](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree, wood" + [版](../characters/版.md) "edition, version" — no stand-in relationship applies (木's own stand-in is itself; 版's own is itself).

木版 means printing block.

Mandarin mùbǎn, Cantonese muk6 baan2, Japanese もくはん, and Korean 목판 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field (mộc bản); `kwin: true` was already correct (諺文 목판 = real Korean 목판 exactly).
