## Notes
- [孫](../characters/孫.md) "grandchild" + [女 (char)](../characters/女%20(char).md) "girl, woman" — "granddaughter." Filled blank `pos: 名詞`. `japanese` deliberately left blank: the real Japanese word for "granddaughter" is 孫娘 (まごむすめ), a different second character, not 孫女. **Collision caveat**: `vietnamese: tôn nữ` is compositional, but this exact form is historically also a surname/title for female descendants of the Nguyễn royal family (Tôn Nữ) — a coincidental collision, not an error; everyday Vietnamese instead says cháu gái (native) for "granddaughter" (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 손느 diverges from Sino-Korean 손녀 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄛㄋㄋㄜ unique among both words and characters).
