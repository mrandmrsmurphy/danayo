## Notes
- [召](../characters/召.md) "summon, convene" + [喚 (char)](../characters/喚%20(char).md) "summon, invite" — a synonym-doubling compound; neither character's own `stand_in` points here (召's own is [[召集]]; 喚's own is itself), so 召喚 is an independent compound. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: `characters/召.md` is `kwin: false`, `characters/喚 (char).md` is `kwin: true` → compound `false`.

召喚 means "to summon" — the base verb behind [[召喚状]], the legal document. Mandarin zhàohuàn, Japanese しょうかん (shōkan), and Korean 소환 (sohwan) are all real, standard, fully compositional readings, attested in both general and specifically legal/judicial contexts (a court summoning a witness, an official body summoning someone to appear). Cantonese ziu6 wun6 is compositional.

Vietnamese triệu hoán is the compositional Hán Việt reading, but is not independently confirmed as a commonly used term; living Vietnamese instead reaches for triệu tập (matching [[召集]], already 召's own established stand-in word) or triệu hồi for "to summon" in general use.
