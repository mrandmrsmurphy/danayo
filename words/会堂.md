## Notes
`vietnamese: hội đường` is standard everyday Vietnamese for a large assembly/meeting hall, well known specifically as hội đường Do Thái, "synagogue" — a direct match to this word's own "meeting hall, church" senses. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 훠당 diverges from Sino-Korean 회당 in the first syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⼔ㄉㄚㄫ unique among both words and characters).
