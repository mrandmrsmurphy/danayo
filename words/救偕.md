## Notes
- [救](../characters/救.md) "rescue, relieve, save" + [偕](../characters/偕.md) "accompany" — no stand-in relationship applies (救's own stand-in is [[救援]]; 偕's own is [[偕同]]).

救偕 is this vault's semantic-compositional name for Jesus ("the one who saves and accompanies"), a meaning-based coinage rather than a phonetic transliteration — paralleling the meaning-based logic used elsewhere in this vault's neologism series (e.g. [[丹金]]) rather than borrowing a phonetic loan. `mandarin`/`cantonese`/`japanese`/`korean`/`vietnamese` hold the standard real-world loanword names for Jesus in each language (Yēsū/je1 sou1/イエス/예수/Giê-su) rather than a compositional reading of 救偕 itself, since no such compositional name exists outside this vault. `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` (규겨/gyugye/ㄍ⼜ㄍ⼶) are fully compositional from 救/偕's own citations. No homophones.
