## Notes
- [肌](../characters/肌.md) "skin, surface, texture" + [理](../characters/理.md) "-ology, reason, logic" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (肌's is [[肌膚]], 理's is [[理由]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 肌 is individually `kwin: false`.

肌理 means skin texture or wood grain — the fine surface pattern of a substance.

Mandarin jīlǐ, Cantonese gei1 lei5, and Korean 기리 (giri) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 肌理 is itself a common attested word for "skin texture, grain" in all three. Japanese has two readings, きめ (kime, the everyday word for "grain, texture") and きり (kiri, a rarer literary reading). Vietnamese cơ lý is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
