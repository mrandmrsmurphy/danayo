## Notes
- [外](../characters/外.md) "outside" + [国](../characters/国.md) "nation" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language" — no stand-in relationship applies (外's own stand-in is [[外部]]; 国's is [[国家]]; 語's is [[言語]]).

外国語 means a foreign language. In Mandarin the shorter form 外語/外语 (dropping 国) is more commonly used in casual speech.

Japanese がいこくご, Korean 외국어, Mandarin wàiguóyǔ, and Cantonese ngoi6 gwok3 jyu5 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese `ngoại ngữ` is the standard, extremely common term (corresponding to the shorter 外語 form). No homophones (注音 ⺢ㄧㄍㄛㄎ·⼄ unique across both words and characters).
