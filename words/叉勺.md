## Notes
- [叉 (char)](../characters/叉%20(char).md) "fork; prong" + [勺 (char)](../characters/勺%20(char).md) "spoon; ladle"

叉勺 is a spork: an eating utensil shaped like a spoon whose bowl is divided into tines like a fork, so that it does the work of both. The name is transparent: 叉, fork, plus 勺, spoon. English Wiktionary lists it only for Chinese (Mandarin chāsháo, Cantonese caa1 zoek3), glossed "spork", with the synonym 叉匙 (chāchí). It has no entry for Korean, so Korean 차작 here is built from the characters. The Japanese field gives the real native word 先割れスプーン (さきわれスプーン, "tip-split spoon"), which Wiktionary glosses "a spork" and dates from after 1860; Japanese has no kanji compound for the object, as with [[刀叉]], fork and knife.

The two parts are the [[叉]], fork, and the [[勺]], spoon.
