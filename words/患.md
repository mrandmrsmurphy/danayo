## Notes
- [患](../characters/患%20(char).md) "suffer from" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

患 means to suffer from — afflicted by an illness or trouble, as in 患者 ("patient").

Mandarin huàn, Cantonese waan6, Japanese かん, Korean 환, and Vietnamese hoạn are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `注音` was stored as the spelled-out literal ㄏㄨㄚㄇ, but no syllable page exists under that string — the vault's canonical syllable page is `ㄏ⺢ㄇ.md`, using the corpus's own single-glyph rime notation (⺢ for the -ua rime); corrected to match the character's own citation (the same character-vs-word 注音 divergence bug class documented on 屎/破/即, 恩/慣, 調/酒, and 火). Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄏ⺢ㄇ/hwam/홤) with the unrelated [[換]] ("interchange, exchange").
