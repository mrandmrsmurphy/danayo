## Notes
- [丘](../characters/丘%20(char).md) "hill" + [陵](../characters/陵.md) "mound" — "hill and mound." Their own `stand_in` values: 丘's is itself; 陵's is [[陵墓]]; none points here, so 丘陵 is an independent compound.

丘陵 is a stretch of low, rounded hills. Mandarin qiūlíng, Cantonese jau1 ling4, Japanese きゅうりょう and Korean 구릉 all use it. It is the closest word for the English "downs", a stretch of rolling grassy upland. Compare [[丘]] (hill) and [[高原]] (plateau).
