## Notes
- [入 (char)](../characters/入%20(char).md) "enter" + [破 (char)](../characters/破%20(char).md) "burst" + [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" — no stand-in relationship applies (入 and 破 are their own stand-ins; 音's is [音楽](音楽.md)).

入破音 is the implosive, the "inward burst". English Wiktionary has an entry only for Japanese 入破音 (にゅうはおん), marked phonetics and glossed "implosive", and glosses the Chinese term 內爆音 as "implosive consonant". The Mandarin, Cantonese and Korean 입파음 fields give the literal reading of the characters, and are not the native terms. In [文法 - 02音韻論](../grammar/文法%20-%2002音韻論.md) a stop may sometimes be produced as a 入破音, which is non-standard but remains broadly understandable. The outward burst is the [破裂音](破裂音.md), and the throat-driven burst is the [喉破音](喉破音.md).
