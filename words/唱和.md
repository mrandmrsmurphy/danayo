## Notes
- [唱](../characters/唱.md) "sing, call out" + [和](../characters/和.md) "respond in harmony" — the classical literary practice of call-and-response composition: one poet initiates (唱) with a verse, another responds in kind (和), matching its rhyme or theme. `vietnamese: xướng họa` is a directly attested standard Sino-Vietnamese term for this exact practice (thơ xướng họa, "call-and-response poetry"), using 唱's own xướng reading and 和's own họa/hoạ reading.
