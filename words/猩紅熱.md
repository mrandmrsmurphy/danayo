## Notes
- [猩](../characters/猩.md) "orangutan; vivid red" + [紅 (char)](../characters/紅%20(char).md) "crimson" + [熱 (char)](../characters/熱%20(char).md) "fever" — scarlet fever (*Streptococcus pyogenes* infection)

Wiktionary: Chinese 猩紅熱, disease, "scarlet fever"; Japanese 猩紅熱 (しょうこうねつ) "scarlet fever". The simplified spelling 猩红热 is kept as an alias. Wikipedia: scarlet fever is an infectious disease caused by *Streptococcus pyogenes*, a group A streptococcus, with sore throat, fever, headache, swollen lymph nodes and a characteristic rash that typically feels like sandpaper; the tongue may be red and bumpy. 猩's own `stand_in` is 猩猩, so this word is not its legitimizer.
