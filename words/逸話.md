## Notes
Mandarin yìhuà, cantonese jat6 waa6, japanese いつわ, and korean 일화 are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. Vietnamese dật thoại is the honest compositional reading (逸's own dật + 話's own thoại); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 읻홰 diverges from Sino-Korean 일화 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄧㄊㄏ⺢ㄧ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 逸's `stand_in` (逸 itself) nor 話's own `stand_in` (話 itself) points to this compound.
