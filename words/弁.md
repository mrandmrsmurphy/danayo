## Notes
- [弁 (char)](../characters/弁%20(char).md) "discuss; distinguish" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [弁 (char)](../characters/弁%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

弁 in this sense is used as the modern Japanese shinjitai stand-in for the traditional characters 辯 ("to debate, discuss") and 辨 ("to distinguish") — a separate role from 弁's own original, unrelated "ceremonial cap" meaning (see the character's own Notes for the full donor-character explanation). It also appears bound in 花弁 ("flower petal," standing in for a third traditional character, 瓣).

Japanese べん, Korean 변, and Vietnamese biện are all standard readings. No homophones among other words (注音 ㄅ⼘ㄋ unique).
