## Notes
- [週](../characters/週.md) "week" + [番 (char)](../characters/番%20(char).md) "time, occasion" — weekly duty

Wiktionary lists 週番 only under Japanese (しゅうばん): "weekly duty". 週's own `stand_in` is 週日, so this word is not its legitimizer; 番's is itself. The other-language fields hold compositional readings.
