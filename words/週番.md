## Notes
- [週](../characters/週.md) "week" + [番 (char)](../characters/番%20(char).md) "time, occasion" — weekly duty

週番 is the duty of a given week: the turn of responsibility passed around a group in rotation, one week at a time, or the person holding it.  The best-known use is in Japanese schools, where a 週番 is the pupil or teacher on duty for the week, and in the military and company settings, where the 週番 is the officer or staff member on duty.  週 gives the week and 番 the turn or watch, as in 当番 ("person on duty"), 番人 ("watchman") and 順番 ("order, turn").

週's own `stand_in` is 週日, so this word is not its legitimizer; 番's is itself.  The 注音 ㄐㄨㄛㄈㄚㄋ needs no syllable dot, and no other word shares it.  Japanese しゅうばん and Korean 주번 are the real compound; Mandarin zhōufān and Cantonese zau1 faan1 are compositional readings of the two characters.  No Vietnamese form is given, since none could be verified.  `kwin` is false because the Dan'a'yo 줏빤 differs from the Korean 주번.
