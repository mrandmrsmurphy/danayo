## Notes
- [聖](../characters/聖.md) "holy, sacred" + [歌](../characters/歌.md) "song" — "holy song," a hymn. Their own `stand_in` values: 聖's is [[神聖]]; 歌's is [[歌曲]]; none points here, so 聖歌 is an independent compound.

聖歌 is a hymn: a sacred song of worship. Mandarin shènggē, Cantonese sing3 go1, Japanese せいか and Korean 성가 all use it, and Vietnamese thánh ca does too. It is the sacred song in general, where [[賛美歌]] is the hymn of praise. Compare [[礼拝]] (worship service) and [[詩篇]] (psalm).
