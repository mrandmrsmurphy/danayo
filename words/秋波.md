## Notes
- [秋 (char)](../characters/秋%20(char).md) "autumn, fall" + [波](../characters/波.md) "wave" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (秋's is [[秋]] itself, 波's is [[波浪]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

秋波 literally means "autumn ripples/waves," a poetic image originally used by poets such as Li Bai for its literal, scenic meaning. Later poets such as Su Shi repurposed the phrase as a metaphor for the clear, glancing beauty of a woman's eyes, and this figurative sense — a flirtatious or amorous glance — is now the dominant meaning across the sphere (送秋波, "to cast an amorous glance").

Mandarin qiūbō, Cantonese cau1 bo1, Japanese しゅうは (shūha), and Korean 추파 (chupa, still commonly used in 추파를 던지다, "to cast a flirtatious glance") are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese thu ba is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form.
