## Notes
- [天 (char)](../characters/天%20(char).md) "sky, heaven" + [頂](../characters/頂.md) "top, apex, crown" — no stand-in relationship applies (天 is its own stand-in; 頂's own is [[頂点]]).

天頂 means zenith — the point directly overhead in the sky.

Mandarin tiāndǐng, Cantonese tin1 ding2, Korean 천정, and Vietnamese thiên đỉnh are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese てんちょう is the real astronomical term for zenith, using 頂's CHOU on'yomi. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄝㄋㄐㄝㄫ unique across both words and characters).
