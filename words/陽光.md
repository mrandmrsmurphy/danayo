## Notes
- [陽 (char)](../characters/陽%20(char).md) "shine, Yang" + [光 (char)](../characters/光%20(char).md) "light"

陽光 means sunshine or sunlight, both literally and, in a common figurative extension, "cheerful, bright, optimistic" (of a person's disposition). Mandarin 陽光/阳光 (yángguāng) carries both senses very actively in everyday speech (阳光男孩, "sunny/cheerful boy"). Japanese 陽光 (ようこう) and Korean 양광 (yanggwang) are both real but comparatively literary/formal, with everyday speech favoring native alternatives (日差し hizashi in Japanese; 햇빛 haetbit in Korean). Sino-Vietnamese dương quang shares the same literal-and-figurative range as Mandarin, though modern everyday Vietnamese more often uses ánh nắng or ánh sáng mặt trời.
