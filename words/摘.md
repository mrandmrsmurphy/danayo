## Notes
- [摘](../characters/摘%20(char).md) "pinch, pluck" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

摘 means to pinch or pluck — picking something (typically a fruit or flower) by pinching it off, as in 摘要 ("summary/abstract," literally "to pluck the essentials").

Mandarin zhāi, Cantonese zaak6, Japanese てき, Korean 적, and Vietnamese trích are all standard, fully compositional readings. Part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read dag/닥/ㄉㄚㄎ) with [[嫡]] ("legitimate wife") and [[鈬]] ("bronze bell").
