## Notes
- [胡](../characters/胡.md) "reckless" + [乱](../characters/乱.md) "chaotic, confused" — this word is the viable form of [胡](../characters/胡.md): the character's meaning (reckless; careless) is identical, but 胡 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 胡乱 is its `stand_in` compound.

In Mandarin, 胡乱 is a common adverb meaning "carelessly, at random, without proper thought" — 胡乱猜測 ("to guess wildly"), 胡乱應付 ("to deal with something halfheartedly/sloppily"). The 胡- prefix (as in 胡説, "nonsense"; 胡來, "to act recklessly") productively marks careless or ill-considered action across several compounds.

Japanese reading うろん is a special established reading; the on-reading concatenation would be こらん. In Japanese, 胡乱 means "suspicious-looking" (a na-adjective), shifted from the Chinese sense of "careless/random." Korean 호란 is a standard compositional Sino-Korean reading. Vietnamese hồ loạn is compositionally derived from each character's own confirmed reading — distinct from the unrelated, far more common near-homophone 混亂/hỗn loạn ("chaos, disorder"), which uses a different first character (混, not 胡) despite the similar sound. No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄛㄨㄌㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
