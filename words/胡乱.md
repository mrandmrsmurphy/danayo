>[!caution] The senses split — Chinese "carelessly, at random", Japanese "suspicious-looking"
>胡乱 is carelessly or at random in Chinese, and the Dan'a'yo sense. Japanese うろん means "suspicious looking", so a Japanese reader may hear suspicion rather than carelessness. Listed as a Split on [文法 - 91疑義語](../grammar/文法%20-%2091疑義語.md).


## Notes
- [胡](../characters/胡.md) "reckless" + [乱](../characters/乱.md) "chaotic, confused"

胡乱 means carelessly, at random. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 胡亂 (alias here; Mandarin húluàn, Cantonese wu4 lyun6) as "at random; casually; carelessly" and "as one pleases; as one likes; whatever one wants". It glosses Japanese うろん as "suspicious looking". The word is listed as a Split on [文法 - 91疑義語](../grammar/文法%20-%2091疑義語.md). The Korean 호란 and Vietnamese hồ loạn fields are built from the readings of the two characters.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [胡](../characters/胡.md), which is too bounded to stand alone. The character [乱](../characters/乱.md) has [混乱](混乱.md), chaos, as its stand-in.
