## Etymology
- [安](../characters/安.md) "peaceful" + [楽](../characters/楽.md) "entertainment"

## Notes
- "At ease, contented, peaceful and happy." Filled blank `pos: 性詞` and `vietnamese: an lạc`, directly attested and especially prominent in Buddhist usage (an lạc quốc, "the Land of Bliss/Ease," a name for the Pure Land) — the same register 安楽 carries in Japanese Buddhist contexts. **Left `hsk_level` blank rather than guessing**: 安乐/安樂 does not appear as its own standalone entry in standard HSK vocabulary lists (only its constituent 乐 does, at HSK 1), so no level is asserted.
- Japanese 安楽 has two well-known compounds worth flagging: 安楽死 (anrakushi, "euthanasia") and 安楽椅子 (anraku isu, "easy chair, armchair") — both extend the base "at ease" sense into specific fixed modern usages that go well beyond a simple gloss.
- `kwin: true` confirmed: both constituents individually carry `kwin: true` on their own character pages (安's Dan'a'yo reading matches Sino-Korean 안, 楽's matches 락), so the AND-rule gives `true` for the compound — consistent with 안락 above matching Korean's 안락 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄋㄌㄚㄎ unique among both words and characters).
