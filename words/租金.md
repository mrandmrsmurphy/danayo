## Notes
- [租](../characters/租.md) "rent, duty, tariff" + [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal, gold" — 租's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 金's own is [[金]] itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 租 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word (already correctly noted). `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 租 is individually `kwin: false`.

租金 means rent, the periodic payment made for the use of property — 租's own sense narrowed from an older, broader "land tax paid in grain" to the modern "rent, hire, lease" meaning, with 金 ("metal, money") specifying the payment as monetary.

Mandarin zūjīn, Cantonese zou1 gam1, Japanese そきん (sokin), and Korean 조김 are all fully compositional readings — note that everyday Korean instead almost always uses the native 세 (se, "rent," as in 월세, "monthly rent") or 임대료 (imdaeryo, "rental fee") rather than a Sino-Korean 租金-based term. Vietnamese to kim is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form; everyday Vietnamese uses the native tiền thuê ("rental money") instead.
