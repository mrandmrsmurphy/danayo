## Notes
- [啓](../characters/啓.md) "open, reveal" + [示](../characters/示.md) "show" — "open and show," revelation. Their own `stand_in` values: 啓's is [[開啓]]; 示's is [[開示]]; none points here, so 啓示 is an independent compound.

啓示 is revelation: God's showing of what could not be known otherwise. Mandarin qǐshì (啟示) and Cantonese kai2 si6 are the ordinary words, and Japanese けいじ and Korean 계시 are the same compound. The character 啓 has the alias 啟. It is revelation as an act, where [[黙示]] is the apocalyptic, hidden-meaning kind. Compare [[予言]] (prophecy) and [[福音]] (gospel).
