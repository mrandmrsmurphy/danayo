## Notes
- [密 (char)](../characters/密%20(char).md) "dense; thick; close; intimate; secret" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [密 (char)](../characters/密%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

密 spans two related but distinct senses that both derive from a single image — things (or people) packed closely together: physical density and thickness ([[密度]] "density," 茂密 "lush, dense growth"), and, by extension, the closeness of a confidence or intimacy so tight nothing leaks out — secrecy ([[秘密]] "secret," 精密 "precise, meticulous," reading closeness of fit rather than closeness of people). The character's own graphemic root is semantic 山 ("mountain") + phonetic 宓 (itself unlinked in this vault, no character page) — "hidden away in the mountains," the original image behind both the "dense" and "secret" senses.

Japanese みつ, Korean 밀, and Vietnamese mật are all standard, non-drifted readings — Vietnamese mật in particular remains productive well beyond a bound compound-only role, surfacing as its own adverb meaning "secretly, covertly" (kín đáo, ngầm) and naming the Buddhist esoteric school 密宗 (Mật tông). No homophones among other words (注音 ㄇㄧㄊ; the unrelated characters [[蜜]] "honey" and [[謐]] "tranquil" share the same reading, but neither's own stand-in word collides with 密's).
