## Notes
- [専](../characters/専.md) "special; dedicated; exclusive" + [念](../characters/念.md) "thought" — no stand-in relationship applies (専's own `stand_in` is [[専用]], 念's is [[念頭]]).

専念 names devoting one's undivided attention to a single thing — 専's "exclusively, only this" sense (as in [[専用]] "exclusive use," [[専門]] "specialty") applied to 念's "thought, mindfulness," yielding "to concentrate wholly on."

Japanese せんねん and Korean 전념 are both standard, everyday, non-drifted terms. **`vietnamese` is left omitted**: no standing everyday Vietnamese term "chuyên niệm" turned up in search — 念 in Vietnamese survives mainly in Buddhist-register compounds (niệm Phật, chánh niệm) rather than this secular "concentration" sense. No specific HSK level number confirmed; no homophones (注音 ㄐ⼔ㄋㄋㄝㄇ unique across both words and characters). `kwin: false` is correct: `諺文` (줜넘) does not match `korean` (전념).
