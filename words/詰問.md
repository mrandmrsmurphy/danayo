## Notes
- [詰](../characters/詰.md) "investigate, interrogate" + [問](../characters/問.md) "question" — stand-in for [[詰]], which cannot appear independently

詰問 means to question someone closely and sharply, pressing them to explain or justify themselves, often in a tone of reproach or suspicion (to cross-examine, to grill). 詰 is "to press, to demand an account of" (as in 詰責, "to reproach") and 問 is "to ask". Mandarin jiéwèn (诘问 in simplified script), Cantonese kit3 man6, Japanese きつもん (kitsumon; 詰問する), Korean 힐문 (hilmun) and Vietnamese cật vấn are the same compound.

In Dan'a'yo it is the severe end of the asking range, against [[質問]] (to ask a question, neutrally). The questioner is demanding an answer, not seeking information.
