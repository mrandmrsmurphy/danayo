## Notes
- [形](../characters/形%20(char).md) "shape, form" + [態](../characters/態.md) "state, condition" — no stand-in relationship applies (形's own stand-in is itself; 態's own is [[態度]]).

形態 means morphology, or form/shape as a state — the structural pattern something takes.

Mandarin xíngtài, Cantonese jing4 taai3, Japanese けいたい, Korean 형태, and Vietnamese hình thái are all standard, fully compositional readings. Removed redundant `品詞`. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄝㄫㄊㄚㄧ unique across both words and characters).
