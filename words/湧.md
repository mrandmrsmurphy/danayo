## Notes
- [湧 (char)](../characters/湧%20(char).md) "gush; rush up" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [湧 (char)](../characters/湧%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

湧 means to gush or well up — water rising suddenly (湧水 "spring water"), or figuratively an emotion or thought surging up (勇気が湧く, "courage wells up").

Japanese よう, Korean 용, and Vietnamese dũng are all standard readings. **A genuine triple Middle Chinese homophone**: 湧, [[容]], and [[庸]] all reconstruct to the identical MC reading despite different phonetic roots (谷/用/甬 respectively) — a real convergence, not a Dan'a'yo-introduced collision, already documented on [[容]]'s own page.
