## Notes
- [木](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree, wood" + [偶](../characters/偶.md) "image, doppelganger" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [偶](../characters/偶.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 木's own stand-in is itself.

木偶 means a wooden puppet or marionette (carved figure).

Mandarin mù'ǒu, Cantonese muk6 ngau5, and Korean 목우 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese でく is a genuine irregular jukujikun (special whole-word reading), the standard Japanese term for this concept, not a compositional もくぐう — kept as-is. Fixed `vietnamese`: was contaminated with an unrelated native-descriptive phrase (tượng gỗ, "wooden statue") — replaced with the compositional Sino-Vietnamese mộc ngẫu, matching 木's and 偶's own citations (偶's own vietnamese is comma-joined "ngẫu, ngẩu, ngậu" — used the primary form ngẫu). No homophones (注音 ㄇㄛㄎㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).
