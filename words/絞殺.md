## Notes
- [絞](../characters/絞.md) "twist, turn" + [殺 (char)](../characters/殺%20(char).md) "kill" — no stand-in relationship applies (絞's own stand-in is [[絞捻]], so this word is not its legitimizer; 殺's own is itself).

Wiktionary: Chinese, literal and figurative, "to strangle; to hang; to garrotte"; Japanese 絞殺 (こうさつ) "strangulation, strangling" and, as a verb, "to strangle".
