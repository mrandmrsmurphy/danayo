## Notes
- [絞](../characters/絞.md) "twist, turn" + [殺 (char)](../characters/殺%20(char).md) "kill" — no stand-in relationship applies (絞's own stand-in is [[絞捻]]; 殺's own is itself).

絞殺 means to strangle — literally "to kill by twisting/wringing," 絞 contributing the twisting, constricting motion of a cord or hands around the throat.

Mandarin jiǎoshā, Japanese こうさつ (kōsatsu), and Korean 교살 (gyosal) are all standard, real, everyday words for strangulation in their languages — used in ordinary and forensic/legal registers alike (Japanese 絞殺魔, "strangler," as in a serial killer). Cantonese gaau2 saat3 is directly compositional and understood the same way. Vietnamese giảo sát is a defensible compositional reading (絞's giảo, also seen in giảo hình, a historical Vietnamese execution method by strangulation/hanging; 殺's extremely common sát). `kwin` is false per the AND-rule: 絞 is individually `kwin: false`, regardless of 殺's own `kwin: true`.
