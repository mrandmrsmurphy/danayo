## Notes
- [恣](../characters/恣.md) "selfish, unrestrained" + [意](../characters/意.md) "intention" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [恣](../characters/恣.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (意's own stand-in is [[意味]]).

恣意 means selfish or arbitrary — acting according to one's own unrestrained will, without regard for rules or others.

Mandarin zìyì, Cantonese zi3 ji3, Japanese しい, Korean 자의, and Vietnamese tứ ý are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese`, added missing `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄜ·ㄜ unique across both words and characters).
