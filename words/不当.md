## Notes
- [不 (char)](../characters/不%20(char).md) "not" + [当 (char)](../characters/当%20(char).md) "fitting, appropriate" — the negation of 当; "unjust, unfair, improper."

Mandarin bùdāng, Cantonese bat1 dong3, Japanese ふとう, and Sino-Vietnamese bất đáng are all standard compositional readings. Korean 부당 (not 불당) shows the 不→불 assimilation rule does not apply before this particular consonant — a real, correctly-stored reading. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`.
