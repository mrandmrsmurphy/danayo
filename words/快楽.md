## Notes
- [快](../characters/快%20(char).md) "sharp, quick, happy" + [楽](../characters/楽.md) "entertainment" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [楽](../characters/楽.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (快's own stand-in is itself).

快楽 means cheerful or pleasurable — a state of happiness or enjoyment.

Mandarin kuàilè, Cantonese faai3 lok6, Japanese かいらく, Korean 쾌락, and Vietnamese khoái lạc are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄎ⺢ㄧㄌㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
