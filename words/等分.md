## Notes
- [等](../characters/等%20(char).md) "etc., rank" + [分](../characters/分%20(char).md) "divide" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both 等's and 分's own are themselves), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 等 is individually `kwin: false`.

等分 means to divide into equal parts — sharing something out so that each portion is the same size, whether a cake sliced into 三等分 ("three equal parts") or a mathematical division.

Mandarin děngfēn, Cantonese dang2 fan1, Japanese とうぶん (tōbun), and Korean 등분 (deungbun) are all standard, fully compositional readings, each the ordinary everyday word for "equal division" in its language. Vietnamese đẳng phân is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form.
