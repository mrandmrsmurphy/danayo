## Notes
- [型](../characters/型.md) "model, pattern" + [式](../characters/式.md) "style" — this word is the viable form of [型](../characters/型.md): the character's meaning (model; type) is identical, but 型 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 型式 is its `stand_in` compound.

型式 means a model or type: the kind that a manufactured thing belongs to, most concretely the model of an aircraft, car or machine, and more widely a form or version. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 型式 (Mandarin xíngshì, Cantonese jing4 sik1) as "model; form; type; version", Japanese かたしき as "model of an aircraft, automobile, machine, etc.", and Vietnamese hình thức as "model; form". It has no Korean entry, so Korean 형식 here is built from the characters; Wiktionary's Korean 형식, "form, template", is the word for 形式.

型式 sounds like [[形式]], written with 形, "shape", where 型式 uses 型, "mold, model"; the two share Mandarin xíngshì, Cantonese jing4 sik1, Vietnamese hình thức and the Dan'a'yo sound. Wiktionary glosses 形式 as "form", "form as opposed to content" and "formal" in Chinese, and "format" in Japanese. The Japanese reading かたしき is mixed: 型 is read かた and 式 しき.
