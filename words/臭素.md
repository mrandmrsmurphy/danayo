## Notes
- [臭 (char)](../characters/臭%20(char).md) "stinky" + [素](../characters/素.md) "element" — literally "stinky element," a direct semantic calque sharing the same etymology as the international name: "bromine" itself derives from Ancient Greek βρῶμος (brômos, "stench"), for the element's famously sharp, unpleasant odor — the same parallel already documented on [[潜素]]/krypton.
- Avoids the Chinese novel character 溴 (kept here as this word's own alias), coining a transparent compound instead — the same naming strategy used throughout this vault's periodic table (compare [[苦土素]], [[石灰素]]). 臭 itself also doubles as this vault's periodic-table abbreviation letter for bromine.

Mandarin xiù is the real, standard reading for bromine (of 溴, the character this vault's compound deliberately avoids in writing while still matching its pronunciation) — Cantonese cau3 is likewise compositional, matching 臭's own stored citation. Korean 취소 (chwiso) and Japanese しゅうそ (shūso) are both compositional and also the real element names in each language. Vietnamese brom is instead the real, standard loanword term (from French "brome"), not a Sino-Vietnamese calque. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄑㄨㄙㄛ unique among both words and characters).
