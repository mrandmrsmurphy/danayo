## Notes
- [中](../characters/中.md) "middle" + [庸 (char)](../characters/庸%20(char).md) "common, ordinary" — no `#cranberry`: both constituents' own `stand_in` point to themselves, neither to this word.

中庸 names both the general philosophical concept of moderation/the golden mean and, capitalized, the Confucian classic *The Doctrine of the Mean* (中庸), one of the Four Books. Mandarin zhōngyōng, Cantonese zung1 jung4, Japanese ちゅうよう, Korean 중용, and Sino-Vietnamese Trung Dung are all standard compositional readings.
