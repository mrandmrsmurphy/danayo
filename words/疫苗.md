## Notes
- [疫](../characters/疫.md) "epidemic" + [苗](../characters/苗.md) "seedling" — "epidemic seedling," a cultured strain planted in the body to build immunity. 疫's own `stand_in` is [[疫病]] and 苗's is [[種苗]]; neither points here, so 疫苗 is an independent compound.

疫苗 is a vaccine. Mandarin yìmiáo and Cantonese jik6 miu4 are the ordinary Chinese words; the "seedling" image comes from the Chinese inoculation tradition, in which smallpox scabs were "planted" in the nose. Japanese says ワクチン and Korean 백신, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[疫病]] (epidemic) and [[病毒]] (virus).
