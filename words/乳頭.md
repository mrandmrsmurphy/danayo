## Notes
- [乳](../characters/乳.md) "milk, breast" + [頭 (char)](../characters/頭%20(char).md) "head, tip" — literally "breast tip/head," the nipple.

乳頭 is the standard anatomical/clinical term across the Sinosphere: Mandarin rǔtóu, Cantonese jyu5 tau4, Japanese にゅうとう (clinical register; everyday Japanese instead says 乳首, chikubi, native kun'yomi), and Korean 유두 are all fully compositional.
