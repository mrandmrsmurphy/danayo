## Notes
- [乳](../characters/乳.md) "milk, breast" + [頭 (char)](../characters/頭%20(char).md) "head, tip" — literally "breast tip/head," the nipple.

乳頭 is the standard anatomical/clinical term across the Sinosphere: Mandarin rǔtóu, Cantonese jyu5 tau4, Japanese にゅうとう (clinical register; everyday Japanese instead says 乳首, chikubi, native kun'yomi), and Korean 유두 are all fully compositional. **Content bug found and fixed**: the `japanese` field had stored a garbled mix, "乳首,にゅうとう" — the kanji spelling of the everyday native word alongside the compositional on'yomi reading, comma-joined into one malformed value — corrected to just にゅうとう (乳's NYUU + 頭's first-listed TOU), with 乳首's everyday-usage role now documented here in prose instead. Also fixed vietnamese capitalization (Núm vú → núm vú, a common noun, not a proper noun).

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 누톳 diverges from Sino-Korean 유두, since both 乳 and 頭 independently carry `kwin: false` on their own character pages.
