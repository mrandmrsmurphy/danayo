## Notes
- [竄](../characters/竄.md) "hide" + [奪](../characters/奪.md) "seize"

竄奪 is to usurp, to seize a throne or office. The spelling is a phonetic substitution: 竄 reads as 篡 does, and 篡 and 簒 are aliases of [竄](../characters/竄.md), so 篡奪, 簒奪 and 篡夺 are aliases of this word. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 篡奪 (Mandarin cuànduó, Cantonese saan3 dyut6) as "to usurp; to seize" and Japanese 簒奪 (さんだつ) as "usurpation" and "to usurp". It lists a Korean entry, 簒奪 (찬탈). The characters carry no meaning of their own here.
