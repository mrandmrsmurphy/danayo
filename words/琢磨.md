## Notes
- [琢](../characters/琢.md) "polish jewels" + [磨 (char)](../characters/磨%20(char).md) "grind, polish, rub, wear out" — 琢's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 磨's own is itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 琢 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

琢磨 means to polish jewels, and so to hone or refine.

Mandarin zhuómó uses 琢's irregular reading in this common compound, distinct from its citation zuó, as in 切磋琢磨. Cantonese doek3 mo4, Japanese たくま, Korean 탁마 and Vietnamese trác ma (琢's own trác plus 磨's own ma) are compositional.
