## Etymology
- [琢](../characters/琢.md) "polish jewels" + [磨 (char)](../characters/磨%20(char).md) "grind, polish, rub, wear out" — 琢's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 磨's own is itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 琢 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

Mandarin zhuómó (琢's real irregular reading in this common compound, distinct from its citation's zuó, as in 切磋琢磨), Cantonese doek3 mo4, Japanese たくま (TAKU+MA), and Korean 탁마 were already correct and fully compositional. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with trác ma (琢's own trác + 磨's own ma). Added missing `pos`(事詞). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/empty `aliases: []`. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄚㄎㄇㄚ unique across both words and characters).
