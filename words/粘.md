## Notes
- [粘](../characters/粘%20(char).md) "sticky" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 粘 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo. Its own 形声 semantic component 米 ("rice") reflects the original sense of glutinous rice.

Mandarin zhān, Cantonese zim1, and Korean 점 (jeom) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading ねばる (nebaru, "to be sticky") rather than the on-reading ネン (NEN) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese niêm is one of several stored candidates. This word is a genuine Dan'a'yo homophone of [[鮎]] ("sweetfish, sheatfish") — coincidental, unrelated in meaning.
