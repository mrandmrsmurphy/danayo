## Notes
- [屁 (char)](../characters/屁%20(char).md) "fart; flatulence" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [屁 (char)](../characters/屁%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

屁 names a fart, and colloquially (in Mandarin especially) extends to "worthless, nonsense" (屁話 "bullshit talk," 放屁 literally "to fart," also "to talk nonsense").

Japanese ひ (on'yomi; the native kun'yomi へ, he, is the everyday word instead), Korean 비, and Vietnamese thí are all standard readings. No homophones among other words (注音 ㄆㄧㄜ unique).
