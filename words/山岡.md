## Notes
- [山 (char)](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain" + [岡](../characters/岡.md) "hill, hillock" — synonym-doubling compound for a hill or hillock; also this word is the `stand_in` legitimizer for [[岡]], which cannot appear independently

山岡 as a place-name-shaped compound survives most vividly in Japanese, where やまおか (Yamaoka, using the native readings of both characters rather than the on'yomi) is a common Japanese surname and place name built on exactly this "mountain + hill" image — the word's own frontmatter stores this native compound reading rather than the on'yomi concatenation しょうこう one might otherwise expect, matching how the word is actually used in Japanese. 岡 itself is a comparatively minor character in the corpus (added to this vault's character list specifically because of its role in Japanese prefecture names), so this compound's real cross-linguistic life leans heavily toward Japanese.
