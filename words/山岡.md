## Notes
- [山 (char)](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain" + [岡](../characters/岡.md) "hill, hillock" — synonym-doubling compound for a hill or hillock; also this word is the `stand_in` legitimizer for [[岡]], which cannot appear independently

**Real content bug found and fixed**: the word's own `korean` field held a native Korean gloss — 언덕, 구릉 ("hill, mound") — rather than an actual Sino-Korean pronunciation, a genuine data-format error rather than a simple blank, and very likely the reason this page had stayed open across many prior iterations of this sweep without being resolved. The real Sino-Korean reading is the plain compositional concatenation of 山's own 산 and 岡's own 강, giving 산강 — which happens to coincide exactly with the word's already-correct Dan'a'yo 諺文 field, since both constituent characters independently carry `kwin: true` (Dan'a'yo matching Sino-Korean) on their own pages. `kwin: true` was added to this word accordingly, following directly from that coincidence rather than being a separate fact to establish. `vietnamese: sơn cương` was similarly missing entirely (not just blank) — filled compositionally from 山's sơn and 岡's cương, the character's own sole stored reading.

山岡 as a place-name-shaped compound survives most vividly in Japanese, where やまおか (Yamaoka, using the native readings of both characters rather than the on'yomi) is a common Japanese surname and place name built on exactly this "mountain + hill" image — the word's own frontmatter already stored this native compound reading rather than the on'yomi concatenation しょうこう one might otherwise expect, a deliberate choice matching how the word is actually used/attested in Japanese. 岡 itself is a comparatively minor character in the corpus (added to this vault's own character list specifically because of its role in Japanese prefecture names, per the character's own Notes bullet), so this compound's real cross-linguistic life leans heavily toward Japanese rather than being evenly attested across the whole CJKV sphere the way most compounds documented in this sweep are.
