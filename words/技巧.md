## Notes
- [技](../characters/技.md) "skill, ability" + [巧](../characters/巧.md) "skillful, clever" — no stand-in relationship applies (技's own stand-in is [[技能]]; 巧's own is [[巧妙]]).

技巧 means technique or skill.

Mandarin jìqiǎo, Cantonese gei6 haau2, Japanese ぎこう, Korean 기교, and Vietnamese kỹ xảo are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄨㄧㄎ⼘ㄨ unique across both words and characters).
