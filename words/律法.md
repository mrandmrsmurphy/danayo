## Notes
- [律](../characters/律.md) "law, rule" + [法](../characters/法%20(char).md) "law, method" — "law and method," the Law. Their own `stand_in` values: 律's is [[法律]]; 法's is itself; none points here, so 律法 is an independent compound.

律法 is the Law: the commandments of the Torah given to Israel, and law in general. Mandarin lǜfǎ, Cantonese leot6 faat3, Japanese りっぽう and Korean 율법 all use it, and Vietnamese luật pháp does too. In Dan'a'yo it is the Law of Moses, where [[十戒]] are its ten commandments. Compare [[旧約]] (the Old Testament) and [[規律]] (rule).
