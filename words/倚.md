## Notes
- [倚 (char)](../characters/倚%20(char).md) "rely on; depend upon"

倚 combines [[人]] ("person") with phonetic [[奇]]. It means "to lean on, to rely on, to depend upon" — both physically (倚靠, "to lean against") and figuratively (倚賴, "to be dependent on"), and appears in the classical idiom 倚老賣老 ("to presume upon one's seniority").

Mandarin yǐ, Cantonese ji2, and Japanese い/き (i/ki, on'yomi, rare outside classical/literary contexts) alongside native よ(る) (yo(ru), "to lean on, rely on") all preserve the sense. Korean 의 (ui) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 의지하다 (uijihada, "to rely on, depend on" — itself built on the same Sino-Korean root). Vietnamese ỷ is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in ỷ lại ("to depend on, rely on [someone/something], often with a negative connotation of laziness"); the character page's other two stored candidates, ấy ("that," a native demonstrative) and ỉa (a vulgar native word for "to defecate"), are both unrelated and were not used here. **Real `kwin` bug found and fixed**: stored as `true`, but byte-level comparison shows Dan'a'yo's own 諺文 읫 (U+C76B, with a final ㅅ coda) genuinely differs from this word's own korean field 의 (U+C758, no coda) — two visually similar but distinct Hangul syllables. Corrected to `kwin: false`. Character-only matches (議, 誼, 義, 儀, 椅, 宜) share the same 注音 but have no independent word pages of their own, so are not real word-level homophone collisions.
