## Notes
- [太 (char)](../characters/太%20(char).md) "great" + [易](../characters/易.md) "easy"

In this compound 易 carries the sense of "change" rather than "easy". 太易 is the first of four primordial stages in the _Liezi_ (列子) cosmogony — the state before even qi (氣) has appeared. The sequence runs: 太易 → [太初](太初.md) → [太始](太始.md) → [太素](太素.md). See also [天地](天地.md), [混沌](混沌.md).
