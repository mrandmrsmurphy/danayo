## Notes
- [英](../characters/英.md) "hero; English" + [里 (char)](../characters/里%20(char).md) "li, traditional unit of distance"

英里 is the mile, the statute mile of 1,609.344 metres, named "English li" after the native unit [里](里.md). English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 英里 (Mandarin yīnglǐ, Cantonese jing1 lei5) as "mile", and Japanese えいり as an uncommon word for "mile" and as a female given name. Korean 영리 and Vietnamese anh lý are built from the readings of the two characters.
