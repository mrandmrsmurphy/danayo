## Notes
- [程](../characters/程.md) "procedure, course" + [序](../characters/序.md) "order, sequence" — "ordered procedure." Their own `stand_in` values: 程's is [[程度]]; 序's is [[順序]]; none points here, so 程序 is an independent compound.

程序 is a procedure or sequence of steps, and by extension a computer program. Mandarin chéngxù and Cantonese cing4 zeoi6 are the ordinary Chinese words; Mainland Chinese also uses it for a computer program, which is the sense Dan'a'yo takes for "program". Japanese says プログラム and Korean 프로그램, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[電脳]] (computer).
