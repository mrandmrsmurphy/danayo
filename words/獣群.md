## Notes
- [獣](characters/獣.md) "animal, beast" + [群](characters/群.md) "flock, crowd" — no stand-in relationship applies (獣's own stand-in is [[野獣]]; 群's own is [[群衆]]).

獣群 means herd, group (of animals).

Mandarin shòuqún and Cantonese sau3 kwan4 were already correct and fully compositional. Fixed a real contamination bug in `korean`: 군중 — the real Korean reading of 群's own stand-in compound [[群衆]] ("crowd"), evidently confused with this word — had been substituted here → corrected to 수군, fully compositional. Japanese むれ (群's own native reading alone) is the real, standard way to express "herd/group" in Japanese, which rarely uses the full binomial compound. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with bầy thú ("herd of animals," native word order), the real, natural Vietnamese phrase for this concept. Added missing `pos`(名詞). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼜ㄍㄨㄋ unique across both words and characters).
