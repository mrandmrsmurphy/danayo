## Notes
- [憂](../characters/憂.md) "worry" + [慮](../characters/慮.md) "consider, ponder" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [憂](../characters/憂.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (慮's own stand-in is [[考慮]]).

憂慮 means to worry or be anxious — apprehensive concern about a future matter.

Mandarin yōulǜ, Cantonese jau1 leoi6, Japanese ゆうりょ, Korean 우려, and Vietnamese ưu lự are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ⼜ㄌ⼄ unique across both words and characters).
