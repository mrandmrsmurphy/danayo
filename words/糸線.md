## Notes
- [糸](../characters/糸.md) "silk thread" + [線](../characters/線.md) "line, clue" — 糸's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 線's own is [[直線]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 糸線 legitimizes 糸 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 線 is individually `kwin: false`.

糸線 is a compositional Dan'a'yo coinage for "silk thread," distinct from the real Japanese word 絹糸 ("silk thread," kenshi/kinuito) which uses 絹 ("silk fabric") rather than 線 ("line"). Mandarin sīxiàn, Cantonese si1 sin3, Japanese シセン (shisen), Korean 사선 (sa-seon), and Vietnamese tơ tuyến are all straightforward compositional readings built from each character's own real-language values, rather than attested loanwords. No Dan'a'yo homophones.

See also [[絹]] for the "silk fabric" sense.
