## Notes
- [戦](../characters/戦.md) "war" + [場](../characters/場.md) "market" — no stand-in relationship applies (戦's own stand-in is [[戦争]]; 場's own is [[市場]]).

戦場 means battlefield or battleground.

Trimmed malformed comma-duplicate `mandarin` (zhàncháng is a rare erhua-adjacent variant not matching 場's own citation) to single zhànchǎng. Mandarin zhànchǎng, Cantonese zin3 coeng4, Japanese せんじょう, Korean 전장, and Vietnamese chiến trường are all standard, fully compositional readings. Homophone of [[戦争]] "war" (both 注音 ㄐㄝㄋㄐㄚㄫ).
