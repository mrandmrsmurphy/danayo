## Notes
- [杜](../characters/杜.md) "careless; fabricating" + [選](../characters/選.md) (via its alias 撰; MC 崇母 + 仙韻 → ㄐ⼶ㄋ) "to compose; write"
- This word is the viable form of [杜](../characters/杜.md): the character's sense of "fabricated, careless" is historically derived from this compound, which originally described the poorly composed writings of the Song-dynasty poet 杜默 (Dù Mò). 撰 ("to write; compose") licenses the compound.
- In Japanese, the compound survives as ずさん (*zuzan* → *zusan*), meaning "sloppy; slapdash."

**Correction (2026-09-12, user-directed):** this page's own `characters:` field previously cited bare "撰" directly, treating it as an unlinkable pageless component — but 撰 is actually a registered alias of [[選]]. Fixed the citation and renamed the page from `杜撰.md` to `杜選.md` accordingly, with `杜撰` demoted to an `alias` — 撰 never appears in a word's title.
