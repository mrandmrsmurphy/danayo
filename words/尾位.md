## Notes
- [尾](../words/尾.md) "tail" + [位 (char)](../characters/位.md) "position" (stand-in: [[定位]]) — the trailing/tail position of a syllable's [[韻母]].

Wiktionary has no entry for 尾位; it is a Dan'a'yo compound of 尾 ("tail; end; last") and 位. It names the coda position of the syllable, the third slot after 介位 and 核位 in `文法 - 02音韻論`'s syllable-structure breakdown.
