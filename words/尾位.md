## Notes
- [尾](../words/尾.md) "tail" + [位 (char)](../characters/位.md) "position" (stand-in: [[定位]]) — the trailing/tail position of a syllable's [[韻母]].

尾位 names the coda position in 単亜語's syllable structure — the slot a nasal, off-glide, or unreleased stop occupies after the nucleus, as laid out in `文法 - 02音韻論`'s syllable-structure breakdown (声母/韻母 → 介位・核位・尾位). Mandarin wěiwèi, Cantonese mei5 wai6, Japanese びい, Korean 미위, and Vietnamese vĩ vị are all straightforward compositional readings from 尾's and 位's own stored citations, not independently verified as standard terms in those languages. `kwin` is false: both 尾 and 位 are individually false.

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot**: the concatenated string `ㄇㄨㄧ⼔ㄧ` admits four legal partitions — the intended 尾(ㄇㄨㄧ)+位(⼔ㄧ), and three false alternates splitting across ㄇㄨ/ㄧ and/or ⼔/ㄧ — so the dot at the true 尾|位 boundary is required: ㄇㄨㄧ·⼔ㄧ.
