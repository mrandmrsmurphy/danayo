## Notes
- [歌](../characters/歌.md) "song" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "words"

歌詞 is the lyrics of a song: the words that are sung. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin gēcí as "lyrics" and Japanese かし as "lyrics". Korean 가사 and Vietnamese ca từ are the same word. Cantonese go1 ci4 matches.

It is the word half of a [歌曲](歌曲.md), song, the other half being the [旋律](旋律.md), melody. A poem not meant to be sung is a [詩歌](詩歌.md).
