## Notes
- [七 (char)](../characters/七%20(char).md) "seven" + [千](../characters/千.md) "thousand" — a plain compositional numeral, 7 × 1,000.

Japanese ななせん uses the native counting-number replacing しち (shichi, 七's on'yomi) with なな — the same avoidance already seen with other 七-compounds, since しち can be misheard for 一 (いち, ichi) in spoken contexts, so なな is preferred whenever clarity matters, especially in numerals. Vietnamese thất thiên is the plain compositional Hán Việt concatenation — everyday Vietnamese instead counts natively (bảy nghìn/bảy ngàn), the Sino-Vietnamese form staying confined to classical/literary contexts or as a component of larger compound numerals rather than standing alone in speech.
