## Notes
- [家](../characters/家.md) "house; family" + [庭](../characters/庭.md) "courtyard" — this word is the stand-in for [家](../characters/家.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

家庭 is literally "the courtyard of the house" — a household's enclosed physical space — extended metonymically to name the family unit itself, the same living-space-for-its-occupants shift many languages make (compare English *household*). It is one of the most basic words in the modern CJKV vocabulary, used everywhere from legal/administrative registers (家庭法 "family law") to everyday speech, and forms the base of a large family of compounds: 家庭教育 ("home education"), 家庭主婦 ("housewife"), 家庭作業 ("homework," in some varieties).

Japanese かてい additionally carries a distinct formal-register sense in 家庭科 (*katei-ka*), the standard school subject "home economics," and 家庭裁判所 (*katei saibansho*, "family court") — both institutional coinages built on this same word rather than separate borrowings. Korean 가정 and Vietnamese gia đình are equally ordinary, everyday terms with no comparable semantic narrowing; none of the four languages show any real drift away from the plain "family/household" sense, unlike many other 家-compounds in this vault.

No HSK level is given for this word on Wiktionary despite its being extremely basic vocabulary — the two constituent characters are each independently HSK 1 (`家`, `庭`), so the gap is presumably a listing omission rather than a genuine indication that the compound is advanced.
