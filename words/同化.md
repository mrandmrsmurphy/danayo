## Notes
- [同](../characters/同.md) "same" + [化](../characters/化.md) "-ify, to become" — no stand-in relationship applies (同's own stand-in is [同一](同一.md); 化's is [変化](変化.md)).

同化 means to assimilate, and assimilation. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 同化 (Mandarin tónghuà, Cantonese tung4 faa3) as the ergative "to make similar" and, in phonology or generally, "to assimilate", Japanese どうか as "assimilation" and "to assimilate", Korean 동화 as "assimilation", and Vietnamese đồng hóa as "to assimilate".
