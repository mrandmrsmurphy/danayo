## Notes
- [滲](../characters/滲.md) "soak through" + [漏 (char)](../characters/漏%20(char).md) "leak" — to leak through a barrier

Wiktionary lists 滲漏 only under Chinese: "(of gases or liquids) to seep; to leak; to filter out; to filter through; to effuse". The simplified spelling 渗漏 is kept as an alias. 滲's own `stand_in` is 滲入, so this word is not its legitimizer. Related: [[滲入]] and [[滲出]].
