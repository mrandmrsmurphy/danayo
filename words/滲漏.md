## Notes
- [滲](../characters/滲.md) "soak through" + [漏 (char)](../characters/漏%20(char).md) "leak" — to leak through a barrier

滲漏 means to seep or leak through: liquid (or gas) passing slowly through a barrier that should have held it, such as a wall, a seam, a pipe or a seal (屋根が滲漏する, "the roof leaks"; 滲漏水, "seepage water").  滲 gives the slow soaking-through and 漏 the leaking, so the word names leakage that comes through the material itself rather than out of an open hole.  That is the sense the Water lexipedia page keeps for it, as against the plain [[漏]]-type leaking from an opening.  It is related to [[滲入]] ("seep in") and [[滲出]] ("seep out"), which give the direction of the same soaking-through.

Mandarin shènlòu, Cantonese sam3 lau6, Japanese しんろう and Korean 삼루 are the compositional readings, and the alias 渗漏 is listed.  滲's own `stand_in` is 滲入, so this word is not its legitimizer.  `kwin` is false because the Dan'a'yo 솜롯 differs from the Korean 삼루.  No Vietnamese form was found attested, so none is given.
