## Notes
- [農](../characters/農.md) "farming" + [作](../characters/作%20(char).md) "make, work" + [物](../characters/物%20(char).md) "thing, product" — "farm product," a crop. Their own `stand_in` values: 農's is [[農業]]; 作's is itself; 物's is itself; none points here, so 農作物 is an independent compound.

農作物 is a crop: a plant grown on a farm for food or sale, and the produce it gives. Mandarin nóngzuòwù, Cantonese nung4 zok3 mat6 and Japanese のうさくぶつ are attested, and Korean 농작물 is the same compound. It is the crop as a product of farming, where [[収穫]] is the harvest, the act or the yield. Compare [[農業]] (agriculture) and [[農場]] (farm).
