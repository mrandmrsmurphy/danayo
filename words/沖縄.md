## Notes
- [沖](../characters/沖.md) "alluvial" + [縄](../characters/縄%20(char).md) "rope"

沖縄 is Okinawa, written "offing, rope". The characters are an ateji for the native name Uchinā; the reading おきなわ is native, with 沖 (おき, "offing") and 縄 (なわ, "rope"), and 縄 is the Japanese form of 繩. The name 沖縄 for the main island appears in documents from the early eighteenth century (from memory, not re-checked).

Okinawa is a chain of islands stretching towards Taiwan, with its capital Naha. The Ryukyu Kingdom was independent from 1429 until it was annexed by Japan in 1879, and the Battle of Okinawa in 1945 was followed by American administration until 1972. It is known for karate, Shuri Castle and the Churaumi Aquarium.

In Dan'a'yo 沖縄 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 오키나와 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
