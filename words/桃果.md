## Notes
- [桃](../characters/桃.md) "peach" + [果](../characters/果.md) "fruit, berry, candy, pastry" — no stand-in relationship applies (桃's own stand-in is [[桃子]]; 果's own is [[果実]]).

桃果 means peach (the fruit).

Mandarin táoguǒ, Cantonese tou4 gwo2, Japanese とうか, Korean 도과, and Vietnamese đào quả are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 桃's and 果's own stored citations exactly. Fixed severe contamination: `mandarin` had been copied from the unrelated compound 桃子's reading (táozi, not táoguǒ); `cantonese` was garbled (mat6 tou4, matching neither character); `japanese` held only 桃's own bare native reading (もも) instead of the compositional form; `korean` held 복숭아, the entirely native Korean word for peach, not the compositional Sino-Korean form. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄨㄍ⺢ unique across both words and characters).
