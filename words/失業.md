## Notes
- [失](../characters/失.md) "lose" + [業](../characters/業%20(char).md) "profession, business" — no stand-in relationship applies (失's own stand-in is [[滅失]]; 業's own is itself).

失業 means to become unemployed, or to lose one's job — literally "losing one's profession."

Mandarin shīyè, Cantonese sat1 jip6, Japanese しつぎょう, Korean 실업, and Vietnamese thất nghiệp are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bugs found and fixed**: `羅馬字` had 失's coda mistyped (sit→sid, matching 失's own citation); `kwin` was wrongly true — 諺文 싣업 does not equal `korean` 실업, so it is false. Reformatted `characters` to block-list, quoted `hsk_level: "2"`, omitted empty-list `aliases`. Filled blank `pos: 動詞`. Renamed the body's non-standard `## Etymology` header to `## Notes`. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄊㄝㄆ unique across both words and characters).
