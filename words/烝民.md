## Notes
- [烝](../characters/烝.md) "masses, crowd" + [民](../characters/民.md) "people"
- 烝民 is the stand-in for [烝](../characters/烝.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.

烝民 is a literary term for the common people, the populace or the multitudes. Wiktionary marks Mandarin zhēngmín as literary and quotes the Shijing line 天生烝民、有物有則 ("Heaven, in giving birth to the multitudes of the people, to every faculty and relationship annexed its law"), the classical source of the word. Cantonese zing1 man4, Japanese じょうみん and Korean 증민 are the Sino-Xenic readings of the same two characters.

In Dan'a'yo the word keeps this shared literary sense, as placed on the [Society](../lexipedia/Society.md) page, and is distinct from the everyday [人民](人民.md), the stand-in for 民 on its own.
