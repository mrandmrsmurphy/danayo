## Notes
- [疑](../characters/疑.md) "doubt" + [問](../characters/問.md) "ask" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "word"

疑問詞 is an interrogative word. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 疑問詞 (Mandarin yíwèncí, Cantonese ji4 man6 ci4) and Japanese ぎもんし as the grammar term for interrogatives or question words, including the interrogative pronoun, adverb and adjective. The Korean 의문사 field is built from the readings of the three characters. Compare [疑問](疑問.md), a question.
