## Etymology
- [逆 (char)](../characters/逆%20(char).md) "rebellion, betrayal, traitor, opposite, reverse" + [数](../characters/数.md) "count, number"

## Notes
**Found and fixed a wrong-compound reading bug on `mandarin`/`cantonese`**: the stored values (dàoshǔ/dou3 sou2) actually belong to the unrelated (though same-meaning) compound 倒數/倒数 ("reciprocal," using 倒 rather than 逆 as its first character) — corrected to the honest compositional nìshù/jik6 sou2 (逆's own nì/jik6 + 数's own shù/sou2). Japanese ぎゃくすう and korean 역수 are directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese nghịch số is the honest compositional reading (逆's own nghịch + 数's own số); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. **Found and removed a bad-aliases-entry**: `倒數`/`倒数` is that same genuinely different compound, not a spelling variant of 逆数. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 억수 diverges from Sino-Korean 역수 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄝㄎㄙㄨ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 逆's `stand_in` (逆 itself) nor 数's own `stand_in` (計数) points to this compound.
