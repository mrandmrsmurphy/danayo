## Notes
- [小 (char)](../characters/小%20(char).md) "small" + [河](../characters/河.md) "river" — this word is the stand-in for [河](../characters/河.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

小河 is a small river or stream, less commonly used than the everyday native words each daughter language actually prefers for this concept: Japanese usually says 小川 (おがわ, ogawa — a *different* character combination, using 川 not 河, and also a very common surname), Korean usually says the native 시내 (sinae), and Vietnamese usually says the native suối. All three compositional Sino-forms recorded here (しょうが, 소하, tiểu hà) are real and attested, but comparatively formal/literary/dictionary-register next to these everyday native alternatives.

**Correction (2026-08-27): `japanese` had been contaminated** with おがわ, copied from the unrelated word [[小川]] (a different character combination, 小+川 not 小+河, primarily a Japanese surname) — corrected to しょうが, the real reading of 小河 itself. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄛㄏㄚ unique across both words and characters).
