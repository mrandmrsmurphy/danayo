## Notes
- [整 (char)](../characters/整%20(char).md) "whole, orderly" + [数](../characters/数.md) "number" — literally "whole number," the mathematical integer: ..., −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, ...

Mandarin zhěngshù, Cantonese zing2 sou3, Japanese せいすう, and Korean 정수 are all standard, fully compositional terms, matching 整's and 数's own stored citations exactly. Vietnamese chỉnh số is likewise compositional (整's own Hán Việt reading chỉnh + 数's own số); everyday Vietnamese math instruction instead uses the native phrase số nguyên.
