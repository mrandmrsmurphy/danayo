## Etymology
- [塵](../characters/塵.md) "dirt, dust" + [埃](../characters/埃.md) "fine dirt, dust"

## Notes
- "Dust, dirt" — and figuratively, "the dusty/mundane mortal world" (as opposed to a transcendent or spiritual realm). `vietnamese: trần ai` is a directly attested, well-known classical Vietnamese term with the same figurative sense (cõi trần ai, "this drab/mortal world" — matching a stray note this page previously carried, now folded into this section). `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 진애 matches Sino-Korean 진애 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄋㄚㄧ unique among both words and characters).
