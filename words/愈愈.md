## Notes
- [愈](../characters/愈.md) "more and more, even more" doubled — this word is the stand-in for its own character 愈, which cannot appear independently; the grammatical correlative pattern 愈...愈... ("the more X, the more Y") is inherited directly from Classical Chinese.

Mandarin yùyù, Cantonese jyu6 jyu6, Japanese いよいよ, Korean 유유, and Vietnamese dũ dũ are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `諺文`/`羅馬字` were missing 愈's final coda consonant (유유/'yu'yu instead of 윰윰/'yum'yum, matching 愈's own citation and the already-correct 注音). Omitted empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ⼜ㄇ⼜ㄇ unique across both words and characters).
