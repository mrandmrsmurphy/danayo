## Notes
- [公 (char)](../characters/公%20(char).md) "public" + [演 (char)](../characters/演%20(char).md) "to perform" — "to give a public performance" (concert, play, show). `vietnamese: công diễn` is one of the most common everyday Vietnamese words for this concept (buổi công diễn, "public performance"), a direct match. **Real content bug found and fixed**: `cantonese` had two comma-separated tone variants crammed into one field — trimmed to gung1 jin2, matching 演's own confirmed reading. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 공연 matches Sino-Korean 공연 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄫ⼶ㄋ unique among both words and characters).
