## Notes
- [排](../characters/排%20(char).md) "arrange, expel" + [水](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [溝](../characters/溝%20(char).md) "ditch" — no stand-in relationship applies (all three characters' own stand-ins are themselves).

排水溝 means gutter or culvert.

Mandarin páishuǐgōu, Cantonese paai4 seoi2 kau1, Japanese はいすいこう, Korean 배수구, and Vietnamese bài thuỷ câu are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄚㄧㄙㄨㄍㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).
