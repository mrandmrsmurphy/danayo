## Etymology
- [姥](../characters/姥.md) "maternal grandmother" reduplicated — the colloquial Mandarin term for "grammy, maternal grandmother" (lǎolao), a Northern-Chinese-dialect-flavored counterpart to the more formal 外祖母.

## Notes
- **`japanese`/`korean`/`vietnamese` deliberately left blank**: bare reduplication doesn't naturalize into these languages, the same pattern already established on [[伯伯]]/[[叔叔]]/[[女女]]/[[姉姉]] — each instead uses its own native term for "maternal grandmother": Japanese おばあちゃん (colloquial) or 母方のお祖母さん (formal); Korean 외할머니 (already the vault's own [[外祖母]] equivalent); Vietnamese bà ngoại. Normalized `hsk_level` to a quoted string. `kwin` is false by construction: no `korean` value exists to compare against. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄛㄇㄛ unique among both words and characters).
