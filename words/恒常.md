## Notes
- [恒](../characters/恒.md) "constant, ubiquitous" + [常](../characters/常.md) "usual, ordinary" — no stand-in relationship applies (恒's own stand-in is [[恒久]]; 常's own is [[日常]]).

恒常 means constant, usual, or common — an ongoing, unchanging state.

Mandarin héngcháng, Cantonese hang4 soeng4, Japanese こうじょう, Korean 항상, and Vietnamese hằng thường are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `korean` had a comma-joined native adverb (언제나) appended; `vietnamese` was a descriptive phrase ("không thay đổi," "does not change") rather than a compositional reading — corrected to hằng thường. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄨㄫㄙ⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
