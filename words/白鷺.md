## Notes
- [白 (char)](../characters/白%20(char).md) "white" + [路](../characters/路.md), standing in for its alias [[鷺]] "heron, egret" — like [[蒼路]] "crane," this word borrows 路 as a 借代字 for the unlinked character 鷺; 路 and 鷺 are true homophones in Mandarin and Cantonese (lù/lou6), which is why 路's own regular reading serves directly here with no special alias reading needed (unlike 就's separate ㄐㄨㄛ reading for 鷲).

白鷺 names the egret, a white heron common across East Asia and a recurring image in classical poetry as a symbol of purity and elegance. Mandarin báilù, Cantonese baak6 lou6, Japanese はくろ (hakuro), Korean 백로 (baekno), and Vietnamese bạch lộ are all fully compositional and stable across the sphere, with no drift or native-coinage substitution.
