## Notes
- [子](../characters/子.md) "child" + [婦 (char)](../characters/婦%20(char).md) "woman, wife" — daughter-in-law.

子婦 is a daughter-in-law: the wife of one's son. It is "son-wife," built on the same pattern as [[継母]] and [[養母]]. Mandarin zǐfù and Cantonese zi2 fu5 are confirmed on Wiktionary, where it is marked literary and also covers "son and daughter-in-law" together; everyday Mandarin uses 媳婦 (xífù) or 兒媳婦, which needs a character 媳 not present here. Korean 자부 comes from memory, and Japanese and Vietnamese are not confirmed. The son-in-law is [[婿]]; the husband's mother is [[婆婆]].
