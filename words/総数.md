## Notes
- [総](../characters/総.md) "whole; total" + [数](../characters/数.md) "number; count"
- This word is the viable form of [総](../characters/総.md): the character means total or complete, but is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo. 総数 is its `stand_in` compound.

Wiktionary glosses Mandarin zǒngshù (总数, 總數) as "total; sum", with 總額 as a synonym, and gives Cantonese zung2 sou3, the noun tone of 數 also used in words such as 代數 and 分數. Japanese そうすう and Korean 총수 are the Sino-Xenic readings of the same compound.

In Dan'a'yo the word is the final overall count of a set, as placed on the [Measurement](../lexipedia/Measurement.md) page, as against [累計](累計.md), a running total accumulated up to a point in time.
