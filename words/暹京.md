## Notes
- [暹](../characters/暹.md) "Siam" + [京](../characters/京.md) "capital" — "the capital of Siam"; no stand-in relationship applies (暹 is a 名専字, so the word is the only way it appears here; 京's own stand-in is [[京城]]).

暹京 is Bangkok, the capital of Thailand. English Wiktionary marks it an obsolete Chinese proper noun and gives 曼谷 (the phonetic spelling now in use), 泰京 and 本京 as synonyms; it is the capital of the kingdom named by [[暹羅]] (Siam), just as 京 forms 京城 and 京都. Neither 曼谷 nor 泰京 is a word in the vault, so this spelling is the Dan'a'yo name of the city.

Mandarin Xiānjīng and Cantonese cim3 ging1 are as at English Wiktionary. Japanese バンコク and Korean 방콕 are the ordinary transliterations of the name, as with [[旧金山]], so `kwin` is false. The `vietnamese` field holds the compositional Hán-Việt reading Xiêm Kinh (暹 xiêm, 京 kinh; not attested for this name).

No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄇㄍ⼶ㄫ unique across words and characters, checked 2026-10-12). The 注音 needs no syllable-boundary dot.
