## Notes
- [新 (char)](../characters/新%20(char).md) "new" + [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" — literally "a new person." No stand-in relationship applies (新's own `stand_in` is itself; 人's own is itself).

新人 means a newcomer, rookie, new face — someone new to a group, organization, or activity, regardless of where they came from. This vault's word for Rosenfelder's "newcomer," distinct from [[移住者]] above, which specifically implies international migration.

Mandarin xīnrén, Cantonese san1 jan4, Japanese しんじん, Korean 신인, and Vietnamese tân nhân are all standard, fully compositional readings, used consistently across professional, organizational, and entertainment contexts (a "rookie" actor, employee, or player). `kwin` is false: 新 is `kwin: true`, 人 is `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄋㄋㄧㄋ unique across both words and characters).
