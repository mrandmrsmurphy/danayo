## Notes
- [興 (char)](../characters/興%20(char).md) "to be fond of; interest" + [奮](../characters/奮.md) "to rouse; exert"

興奮 is "excitement; to be excited; to excite," a state of stimulated, aroused feeling. According to Wiktionary, Chinese 興奮 (xīngfèn, Cantonese hing1 fan5) is "excited," "to excite; to stimulate," and in biology "stimulation; excitation"; Japanese こうふん (also written 昂奮 or 亢奮) is "excitement, stimulation, agitation, arousal"; Korean 흥분 is "be excited, be stimulated"; and Vietnamese hưng phấn is the same word. Its sense is a stronger, bodily arousal than ordinary pleasure. It is used in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [China](../lookup/HSK/China.md), in 充満 欲望 与 興奮 之 生命体 "life forms filled with desire and excitement." Related: [欲望](欲望.md) "desire."
