## Notes
- [尺 (char)](../characters/尺%20(char).md) "a foot (unit of length)" + [度](../characters/度.md) "degree; measure"

尺度 is "a measure; a yardstick; a criterion," the thing by which something is measured. According to Wiktionary, Chinese 尺度 (chǐdù, Cantonese cek3 dou6) is "scale; measure," "yardstick; standard; criterion," "boundary (outside which it becomes unacceptable)," "a measuring tool," and "length"; Japanese しゃくど is attested as well. It is a Chinese and Japanese word; `korean` 척도 and `vietnamese` thước độ are compositional readings of the two characters, not independently checked. It is used in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [China](../lookup/HSK/China.md), in 人 乃 是 万物 之 尺度 "man is the measure of all things" (Protagoras). Related: [標準](標準.md) and [基準](基準.md) "standard," and [尺](尺.md) "foot (distance)."
