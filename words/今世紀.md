## Notes
- [今 (char)](../characters/今%20(char).md) "now" + [世紀](../words/世紀.md) "century" — "this century." 금세기 is the standard, common Korean word for this sense (compositional from 今's own 금 + 世紀's own 세기, as in 금세기 최고, "the best of this century"). Vietnamese prefers the native phrase thế kỷ này ("this century," literally "century this") over a forced Sino-Vietnamese compound — kept as the honest, natural term. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 김서기 diverges from Sino-Korean 금세기 via the AND-rule (今 and 世 independently false, despite 紀 itself being `kwin: true`). No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄇㄙㄝㄍㄧ unique among both words and characters).
