## Notes
- [沈](../characters/沈.md) "sink" + [陽](../characters/陽%20(char).md) "shine" — north of the Shen (River). No stand-in relationship applies (沈's is [[沈没]]; 陽 is its own stand-in).

沈陽 (Shenyang) is the capital and largest city of Liaoning province in northeastern China. The real-world spelling is 瀋陽; 瀋 is listed as an alias of 沈 in the character pages, so by the alias rule the filename uses 沈 and 瀋陽 is kept as an alias (with the simplified 沈阳 and 瀋阳). Per Wiktionary the name refers to its position on the north (the sunny side) of the Shen River, coined in 1296 when Shen Prefecture was converted to Shenyang Circuit. Its older names include 奉天 (Fengtian) and 盛京 (Shengjing).

The reading follows [沈](../characters/沈.md), whose Dan'a'yo sound is ㄑㄧㄇ; the 注音 carries a syllable-boundary dot because ㄑㄧㄇ⼘ㄫ also parses as ㄑㄧ + ㄇ⼘ㄫ. Japanese しんよう is the Sino-Japanese reading (Wiktionary Japanese 瀋陽); the usual Japanese name is also given there as シェンヤン. The Korean field holds 침양, derived from the character page of 沈 (North Korean 침); Wiktionary Korean gives 심양 for 瀋陽 (the reading of 瀋 itself), and the usual South Korean name today is 선양 (listed on Wiktionary). Vietnamese Thẩm Dương is the Sino-Vietnamese name. Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese, Japanese and Korean entries; the Vietnamese form is listed in the Chinese entry's descendants. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄇ·⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
