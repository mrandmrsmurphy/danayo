## Notes
- [皆 (char)](../characters/皆%20(char).md) "all; every; universal" + [物 (char)](../characters/物%20(char).md) "thing; object; material result"

皆物 is the collective (universal) correlative in the 物 ("thing") column of the correlative table: "all things; everything; every object taken together", a predicate that holds of the whole set of things at once. The character [皆 (char)](../characters/皆%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *gye*, ㄍ⼶) is the quantifier "all; every", the free word being [皆](皆.md); [物 (char)](../characters/物%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *mud*, ㄇㄨㄊ) is "thing; object; material result". Its part of speech is 代詞 (pronoun), like [皆事](皆事.md), [皆人](皆人.md), [皆名](皆名.md) and the rest of the 皆 row, and like [此物](此物.md), [其物](其物.md), [彼物](彼物.md) and [某物](某物.md). Illustrative only (invented, not from `grammar/`): 皆物、在 "everything is present".

It contrasts with its column neighbours as follows. The distributive [毎物](毎物.md) "each thing" goes through the things one by one (the same split as [毎人](毎人.md) against [皆人](皆人.md)); 皆物 treats them as one totality. [某物](某物.md) is the indefinite "something; a certain object" (a 某 form is indefinite, not a universal), [何物](何物.md) is the interrogative "what thing?", and [此物](此物.md) "this thing", [其物](其物.md) "that thing" and [彼物](彼物.md) "that thing yonder" each point at one particular thing, where 皆物 covers all of them. Against [皆事](皆事.md) "all matters": 事 is an act, event or affair, whereas 物 is a concrete object. Against [皆類](皆類.md) "every kind": 皆物 quantifies over individual things, not classes. The productive affix [－物](－物.md) forms "thing that is X-ed" from a verb or stative, so it builds new nouns, whereas 皆物 is one fixed cell of a paradigm.

Several lexical words overlap in meaning but are separate vault words, not aliases. [万物](万物.md) (its page glosses it "everything; universe"; Wiktionary's 萬物 entry gives "everything; all things", "all the universe" and "every manifestation of nature") is the lexical "all things of the universe", and it is the word used in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义, 人 乃 是 万物 之 尺度 ("man is the measure of all things"). 皆物 is instead the correlative cell, chosen for compositional consistency with the grid rather than for the cosmological register of 万. Also near it are [万象](万象.md) "all things; every manifestation of nature", [全部](全部.md) "entirety; everything", [全体](全体.md) "whole", [一切](一切.md) "entirety; whole" and [所有](所有.md) "all; every" (determiner). No vault word 凡物 or 諸物 exists. That 万物 is a cosmological term of Daoist and Confucian philosophy (including a line in the Laozi) is from memory, not re-checked here.

Cross-linguistic fields were checked against Wiktionary on 2026-10-11. Wiktionary has no entry for 皆物 (the same spelling is used in traditional and simplified Chinese), so the word is a Dan'a'yo compositional construction and no `mandarin`, `cantonese`, `japanese`, `korean` or `vietnamese` field is entered; none of the readings below is an attested reading of the compound. The earlier Notes text offering Japanese 全て and Korean 모든 것 as natural spoken equivalents is kept only as from memory, not re-checked, and the Japanese example 皆物を愛でる / 毎物を愛でる was removed as unsupported. The established equivalent with an entry is 萬物 / 万物 (Mandarin wànwù, Cantonese maan6 mat6; Japanese ばんぶつ; Korean 만물; Vietnamese vạn vật), glossed "everything; all things"; hvdic.thivien.net covers single characters, not phrases, so no `vietnamese` field is entered. Dan'a'yo keeps the sense shared across CJKV, "all things; everything"; no narrowing is needed.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md)'s correlative table (line 126 is the 皆 ("all") row; the same chart is in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`, line 145 in the 物 ("thing") row, with the column header cell 皆 on line 142), 皆物 is the second cell of the 皆 row, after [皆事](皆事.md) "all matters" and before [皆人](皆人.md) "all people", [皆名](皆名.md), [皆処](皆処.md), [皆時](皆時.md), [皆様](皆様.md), [皆多](皆多.md) and [皆類](皆類.md). The table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), 63 forms, so 皆物 is parsed compositionally: 皆 = collective, 物 = thing. It is listed on the Grammar page only.

The spellings are the plain concatenation of the two character pages: 羅馬字 *gye* + *mud* = gyemud, 諺文 겨 + 묻 = 겨묻, 注音 ㄍ⼶ + ㄇㄨㄊ = ㄍ⼶ㄇㄨㄊ, each checked letter by letter against 皆 (char) and 物 (char). The 注音 carries a syllable-boundary dot, ㄍ⼶·ㄇㄨㄊ, because the string has a second complete partition under the (C)(G)V(C) structure of `grammar/文法 - 02音韻論.md`: ㄍ⼶ㄇ exists in `syllables/` (ㄇ as nasal coda) and ㄨㄊ exists as a null-onset syllable, so ㄍ⼶ㄇ + ㄨㄊ would also be a legal reading. The dot marks the intended ㄍ⼶ + ㄇㄨㄊ, exactly as on [此物](此物.md) and [皆人](皆人.md).
