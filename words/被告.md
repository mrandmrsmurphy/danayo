## Notes
- [被 (char)](../characters/被%20(char).md) "-ee (but a prefix)" + [告](../characters/告.md) "inform"

被告 is the defendant: the person charged in a case, built from the passive prefix 被 "-ee" and 告 "inform", literally "the one informed against". Wiktionary glosses Mandarin bèigào as "defendant; the accused; respondent" and dates it from the 19th century. Japanese ひこく, Korean 피고 and Vietnamese bị cáo are the same word in the local readings, so it is shared across all five languages.

It answers [原告](原告.md), the plaintiff. Once the charge is made by [起訴](起訴.md) the accused is the 被告, and the [判決](判決.md) finds that person [有罪](有罪.md) or [無罪](無罪.md).
