## Notes
- [退 (char)](../characters/退%20(char).md) "retreat, recede" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — fading.

退色 is fading: the loss of color from cloth, paint, a photograph or a flag through light, washing or age; and by extension the fading of splendor or of a memory. The Dan'a'yo headword uses 退 "recede" because 褪, the Mandarin and Cantonese character for "fade," is not a Dan'a'yo character; 褪色 is the alias. Japanese たいしょく is written 退色 or 褪色, and Korean 퇴색 is the same compound. Mandarin tuìsè and Cantonese teoi3 sik1 are the standard readings of 褪色. Compare [[変色]], which names any change of color rather than fading specifically, and [[脱色]], the deliberate removal of color.
