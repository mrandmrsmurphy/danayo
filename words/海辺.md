## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [辺](../characters/辺.md) "side, edge, border" — the edge of the sea; shore, beach

海辺 is the seashore: the strip of land where the sea meets the land, and by extension the beach as a place to walk, play or rest (海辺の町, "a seaside town"; 海辺で遊ぶ, "to play at the beach").  海 gives the sea and 辺 the edge or neighborhood of it, so the word names the zone beside the water, not the sand itself.  It is the everyday word for a place by the sea, and is close to 海岸 ("coast"), which stresses the coastline as a geographic feature.

Japanese うみべ (umibe) is the native reading, written with the same characters; the compositional on'yomi would be かいへん, which is not the usual word.  The usual Korean word is 해변 (haebyeon) and the Mandarin 海边 is hǎibiān, so the Sinitic compound is shared.  The traditional spelling 海邊 is listed as an alias.  `kwin` is false because the Dan'a'yo 해번 differs from the Korean 해변.  No Vietnamese form is given, since the usual Vietnamese term, bờ biển, is native and not a reading of these characters.
