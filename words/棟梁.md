## Notes
- [棟](../characters/棟.md) "ridgepole" + [梁 (char)](../characters/梁%20(char).md) "girder" — stand-in for [[棟]], which cannot appear independently.

1. (Architecture) **ridgepole and beam** — the main structural members of a roof
2. **pillar; mainstay** — a person or thing of central importance to an organization or society
3. (Japanese) **master carpenter** — a head craftsman who oversees a building project

The figurative "mainstay" sense (2) extends naturally from the literal architectural one (1): just as the ridgepole and main beam bear the structural weight of a roof, a 棟梁 bears the weight of an organization or society. Sense (3) is a further Japanese-specific narrowing, naming the carpenter responsible for those very structural members.

Mandarin dòngliáng, Cantonese dung6 loeng4, Korean 동량 (dongryang), and Japanese とうりょう (tōryō) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 棟's and 梁's own stored citations. Vietnamese đống lương is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese. **Real `kwin` bug found and fixed**: was stored as `false`, but Dan'a'yo 동량 is byte-identical to Sino-Korean 동량 — corrected to `kwin: true`. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄫㄌ⼘ㄫ unique among both words and characters).
