## Notes
- [聘](../characters/聘%20(char).md) "engage, betroth" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 聘 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

聘 means to engage or betroth — an official inquiry or invitation, as in 聘用 (to hire, engage) or 聘礼 (betrothal gifts).

Mandarin pìn, Cantonese ping3, and Korean 빙 (bing) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading めす (mesu, "to summon, engage") rather than the on-reading ヘイ (HEI) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese sính is the standard reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones (no other character shares this syllable).
