## Notes
- [泡](../characters/泡.md) "bubbles" + [沫 (char)](../characters/沫%20(char).md) "froth, foam"
- This word is the viable form of [泡](../characters/泡.md): the character's meaning (bubble; froth) is identical, but 泡 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 泡沫 is its `stand_in` compound.

泡沫 is foam, froth, or a mass of bubbles, whether on water, in a drink, or in soap. The two characters are near-synonyms (泡 "bubble", 沫 "froth, spume"), so the compound names the foamy mass rather than a single bubble.

Across the CJKV sphere the word also carries a figurative sense of something showy and insubstantial that bursts without lasting. This is most visible in economic vocabulary: Mandarin 泡沫经济 and Japanese バブル経済 (泡沫経済) both name an asset bubble, and Mandarin 泡沫 on its own can mean "bubble" in the financial sense. Dan'a'yo keeps the shared sense, covering both literal foam and the figurative bubble, with no divergence.
