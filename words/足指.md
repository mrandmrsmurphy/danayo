## Notes
- [足 (char)](../characters/足%20(char).md) "foot" + [指](../characters/指.md) "finger" — "toe." Japanese あしゆび (a native compound, "foot-finger") is the standard everyday term. `cantonese: zuk1 zi2`, `korean: 족지`, and `vietnamese: túc chỉ` are the honest compositional Sino-xenic readings; each daughter language's own everyday word for "toe" is instead a native compound built the same way as Japanese (Korean 발가락, "foot-finger"; Vietnamese ngón chân, "finger of the foot"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 족즤 diverges from the compositional Sino-Korean 족지 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄛㄎㄐㄧㄜ unique among both words and characters).
