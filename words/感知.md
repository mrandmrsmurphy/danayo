## Notes
- [感](../characters/感.md) "feel, sense" + [知](../characters/知%20(char).md) "know" — "sense and know," to perceive. Their own `stand_in` values: 感's is [[感触]]; 知's is itself; none points here, so 感知 is an independent compound.

感知 is to perceive: to take in something through the senses. Mandarin gǎnzhī, Cantonese gam2 zi1, Japanese かんち and Korean 감지 all use it. It is perception as the senses' work, where [[察覚]] is to notice and [[自覚]] is to be aware. Compare [[認識]] (to recognize) and [[感覚]] (sensation).
