## Notes
- [外](../characters/外.md) "outside" + [部](../characters/部%20(char).md) "part, section" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [外](../characters/外.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (部 is its own stand-in).

外部 means outside, exterior, or external.

Japanese がいぶ, Korean 외부, Mandarin wàibù, and Cantonese ngoi6 bou6 are all standard readings. Filled blank `pos: 名詞`; fixed empty-list `aliases`.
