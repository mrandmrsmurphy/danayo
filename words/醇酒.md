## Notes
- [醇](../characters/醇.md) "pure, mellow (of wine)" + [酒](../characters/酒.md) "alcohol, liquor" — "good wine."

Mandarin chúnjiǔ, cantonese seon4 zau2, and korean 순주 are all directly attested standard readings. Japanese じゅんしゅ and vietnamese thuần tửu are both honest compositional readings (醇's own JUN/thuần + 酒's own SHU/tửu).
