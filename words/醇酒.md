## Notes
- [醇](characters/醇.md) "pure, mellow (of wine)" + [酒](characters/酒.md) "alcohol, liquor" — "good wine."

Mandarin chúnjiǔ, cantonese seon4 zau2, and korean 순주 are all directly attested standard readings. Japanese じゅんしゅ and vietnamese thuần tửu are both honest compositional readings (醇's own JUN/thuần + 酒's own SHU/tửu). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 순줏 diverges from Sino-Korean 순주 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨㄋㄐㄨㄛ unique among both words and characters).

**Found and fixed an entirely missing `## Words` section** on 醇.md. Citation on 酒.md was already correctly present. 醇's own `stand_in` is this exact word (醇酒), so 醇 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 醇酒; 酒's own `stand_in` is instead 酒精, so this word does not legitimize 酒.
