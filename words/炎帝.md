## Notes
- [炎](../characters/炎.md) "flame, inflammation" + [帝](../characters/帝.md) "emperor" — no stand-in relationship applies (炎's own stand-in is [[炎症]]; 帝's own is [[帝王]]).

炎帝 refers to the Flame Emperor (Yan Di), a mythological Chinese emperor also identified with Shennong.

Mandarin Yándì, Cantonese jim4 dai3, Japanese えんてい, Korean 염제, and Vietnamese Viêm Đế are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 炎's and 帝's own stored citations exactly), and also the real, standard name for this figure in each language's own historical/mythological tradition.
