## Notes
- [晴](../characters/晴.md) "clear" + [朗](../characters/朗.md) "clear, distinct" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [晴](../characters/晴.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (朗's own stand-in is [[明朗]]).

晴朗 means clear, serene, bright, or sunny.

Mandarin qínglǎng, Cantonese cing4 long5, Japanese せいろう, Korean 청랑, and Vietnamese thanh lãng are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄫㄌㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
