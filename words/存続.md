## Notes
- [存](../characters/存.md) "survive; exist" + [続](../characters/続.md) "continue"

存続 is to continue to exist: to go on being, as an institution or a custom does. Wiktionary glosses Japanese そんぞく as "continuation in existence; continue to exist". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 존속 matches.

It differs from [継続](継続.md), to continue an activity, because it concerns whether a thing is still there at all. Its opposite is [滅亡](滅亡.md), and its result is [現存](現存.md), the extant.
