## Notes
- [信](../characters/信.md) "trust, believe" + [任](../characters/任.md) "to appoint, entrust" — "to trust in, confide in" (信任投票, "vote of confidence"; 信任危機, "crisis of confidence"). Mandarin xìnrèn, Cantonese seon3 jam6, Japanese しんにん, Korean 신임, and Vietnamese tín nhiệm are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 신님 diverges from Sino-Korean 신임 in the second syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄋㄋㄧㄇ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 信's own `stand_in` is this exact word (信任), so 信 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 信任.
