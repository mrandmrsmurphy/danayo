## Notes
- [雨 (char)](../characters/雨%20(char).md) "rain" + [傘](../characters/傘.md) "umbrella" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes 傘 as a bound character (雨's own stand-in is [[雨]] itself; transitivity fails, so no `#cranberry`).

雨傘 means a (rain) umbrella, distinguished from [[陽傘]] ("parasol," for sun protection). Mandarin 雨傘/雨伞 (yǔsǎn), Cantonese 雨傘 (jyu5saan3), Korean 우산 (usan, the standard everyday word for umbrella in Korean), and Sino-Vietnamese vũ tản (classical register; modern Vietnamese uses native ô/dù) all preserve this meaning.

**Found and fixed a real bug**: `japanese` held かさ, the bare general word for "umbrella" (ambiguous between rain and sun use) rather than this specific compound's own reading — corrected to あまがさ, the real word distinguishing rain umbrellas from 日傘 (sun parasols).
