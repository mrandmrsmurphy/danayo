## Notes
- [昆](../characters/昆%20(char).md) "swarm, descendants" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [昆](../characters/昆%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

昆 means swarm, or descendants (as in 昆虫, "insect," literally "swarming creatures," and 昆仲, "brothers").

Mandarin kūn, Cantonese kwan1, Japanese こん, Korean 곤, and Vietnamese côn are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones at the word level (注音 ㄍㄛㄋ is shared only with the bound character 鯤, whose own stand-in is [[鯤魚]]).
