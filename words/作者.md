## Notes
- [作 (char)](../characters/作%20(char).md) "make" + [者](../characters/者.md) "person"

作者 is the maker of a particular work: its author, composer or artist. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin zuòzhě as "author; writer" and Japanese さくしゃ as "the creator of a work, such as an author, writer, poet, or artist". Korean 작자 and Vietnamese tác giả are the same word. Cantonese zok3 ze2 matches.

It is the maker of one [作品](作品.md), work, where the [作家](作家.md) is a writer by calling. For a book's named author see the [着者](着者.md).
