## Notes
- [恩](../characters/恩.md) "kindness; mercy" + [意](../characters/意.md) "intention; mind; sentiment" — the affective, intentional dimension of God's goodwill; grace understood as the inner disposition of love that motivates God's acts

Wiktionary lists 恩意 only under Chinese, literary: "friendly regard; affection" and "kindness; affection; grace; favor". In Dan'a'yo it names the affective, intentional dimension of goodwill, as distinct from [[恩典]], [[恩寵]] and [[恩施]].
