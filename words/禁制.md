## Notes
- [禁](../characters/禁.md) "prohibit, forbid" + [制](../characters/制.md) "restrict, regulate" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (禁's is [[禁止]], 制's is [[抑制]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 禁 is individually `kwin: false`.

禁制 names a formal prohibition or regulatory restriction imposed by authority — a legal or institutional ban, closer in register to [[禁止]] ("forbid") crossed with 制 ("regulate, system") than to a casual "don't." It appears in legal, administrative, and religious contexts alike: a 禁制品 ("prohibited/contraband item") or a court-ordered 禁制令 ("injunction, restraining order").

Mandarin jìnzhì, Cantonese gam3 zai3, Japanese きんせい (kinsei), and Korean 금제 (geumje) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese cấm chế is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form; everyday Vietnamese more often expresses the same sense analytically (e.g. lệnh cấm, "prohibition order").
