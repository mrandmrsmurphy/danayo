## Notes
- [悲](../characters/悲%20(char).md) "be sad" + [痛](../characters/痛.md) "torment, pain" — no stand-in relationship applies (悲's own stand-in is itself; 痛's own is [[苦痛]]).

悲痛 means grieved or sorrowful — deep sadness paired with pain.

Mandarin bēitòng, Cantonese bei1 tung3, Japanese ひつう, Korean 비통, and Vietnamese bi thống are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄧㄜㄊㄛㄫ unique across both words and characters).
