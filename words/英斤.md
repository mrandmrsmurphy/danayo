## Notes
- [英](../characters/英.md) "hero; English" + [斤](../characters/斤.md) "jin, traditional unit of weight" — "English jin," the pound. Their own `stand_in` values: 英's is [[英雄]]; 斤's is [[公斤]]; none points here, so 英斤 is an independent compound.

英斤 is the pound, the Western unit of weight of about 454 grams, named "English jin" after the native [[斤]] (about 500 grams, the same scale), exactly as [[英里]] is the "English li." Chinese writes the pound 磅 or 英鎊 and Japanese and Korean use ポンド and 파운드, none of which are available here, so this is a **neologism** coined on the 英里 template rather than an attested word (Wiktionary has 英斤 for Japanese only). Compare [[公斤]] (kilogram), which is also built on 斤, and [[英両]] (ounce).
