## Notes
- [苦 (char)](../characters/苦%20(char).md) "bitter, suffering" + [役](../characters/役.md) "service, labor" — hard labor; forced labor; drudgery

Wiktionary: Chinese "hard labor; forced labor"; Japanese 苦役 (くえき) "drudgery, toil" and "hard labor"; Vietnamese khổ dịch is the Han form, "corvée". 役's own `stand_in` is 苦役, so this word legitimizes the bound character 役.
