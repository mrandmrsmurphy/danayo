## Notes
- [災](../characters/災.md) "disaster, catastrophe" + [殃](../characters/殃.md) "disaster, misfortune, calamity" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 殃 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (災's own stand-in is [[災害]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

災殃 means disaster.

Mandarin zāiyāng and Korean 재앙 (also the real Korean word for "disaster") are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 災's and 殃's own stored citations. Japanese わざわい is the real, standard native Japanese word for "calamity," matching 殃's own stored native reading exactly.
