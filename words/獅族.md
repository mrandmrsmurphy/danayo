## Notes
- [獅](../characters/獅.md) "lion" + [族](../characters/族.md) "tribe, clan" — "the Lion Tribe, Judah." Their own `stand_in` values: 獅's is [[獅子]]; 族's is [[家族]]; none points here, so 獅族 is an independent compound.

獅族 is the tribe of Judah, Leah's fourth son, from whom the kings of Israel, David, and the Messiah descend. It is a Dan'a'yo **neologism**, named for Jacob's blessing, "Judah is a lion's cub" (Genesis 49:9), "Lion Tribe," not for the Hebrew meaning "praise." It sounds exactly like [[氏族]] (clan), so the two are homophones in Dan'a'yo. Compare [[長族]] (Reuben) and [[捷族]] (Naphtali). See [[基督敎]].

- Hebrew name: יְהוּדָה (Yehudah) (注音 ·⼶ㄏㄨㄉㄚ).
