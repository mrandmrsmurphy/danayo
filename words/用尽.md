## Notes
- [用](../characters/用.md) "use" + [尽](../characters/尽.md) "exhaust, use up"
- 用尽 is the stand-in for [尽](../characters/尽.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.

Both parts point the same way: 用 "use" plus 尽 "exhaust, run out", so the compound means to use something up completely. Wiktionary gives Mandarin yòngjìn (用尽, 用盡) as "to exhaust; to use up completely; to deplete", and Cantonese jung6 zeon6 matches. Japanese ようじん and Korean 용진 are the Sino-Xenic readings of the same characters.

In Dan'a'yo the word has the shared sense of depleting a supply, as placed on the [Existence](../lexipedia/Existence.md) page, where running out is the end of availability.
