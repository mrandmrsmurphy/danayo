## Notes
- [姉](../characters/姉%20(char).md) "elder sister" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

姉 means elder sister.

Beyond the literal "elder sister," 姉 also covers an older female relative of the same generation, a cousin most concretely, partially filling the vault's documented "no word for cousin" gap on [Kinship](../lexipedia/Kinship.md). This follows the general pattern of "elder sibling" terms extending to peers of the same generation (Mandarin 大姐, Korean 언니/누나). [姉姉](姉姉.md) is the intimate, colloquial address-register counterpart of this same sense.

Mandarin zǐ, Cantonese zi2, Japanese し, Korean 자, and Vietnamese chị are all standard, fully compositional readings.
