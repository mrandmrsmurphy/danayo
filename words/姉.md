## Notes
- [姉](../characters/姉%20(char).md) "elder sister" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

姉 means elder sister.

**Sense broadened 2026-09-21**, mirroring the same day's broadening of [[兄]] (see that word's own Notes for the cross-linguistic justification — Mandarin 大姐, Korean 언니/누나, and the general pattern of "elder sibling" terms extending to peers of the same generation): 姉 now also covers an older female relative more generally, a cousin most concretely, partially filling the vault's documented "no word for cousin" gap on [[Kinship]]. [[姉姉]] is the intimate, colloquial address-register counterpart of this same broadened sense.

Mandarin zǐ, Cantonese zi2, Japanese し, Korean 자, and Vietnamese chị are all standard, fully compositional readings. Part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read ja/자/ㄐㄚ) with [[借]] ("borrow") and [[諸]] ("various").
