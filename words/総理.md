## Notes
- [総](../characters/総.md) "whole" + [理](../characters/理.md) "reason; manage"

総理 is "premier; prime minister," the head of a government who is not also head of state, and more loosely the person with overall charge of an organization. According to Wiktionary, Chinese 總理 (zǒnglǐ, Cantonese zung2 lei5) means "head of government who is not also a head of state, e.g. prime minister, premier, chancellor," "director of a certain organization," and, as a verb, "to assume overall responsibility for"; Vietnamese tổng lý is "premier, prime minister, general manager." Wiktionary has no Japanese or Korean section for the bare word (Japanese uses it inside 総理大臣 "prime minister"), so `japanese` そうり and `korean` 총리 are compositional readings of the two characters, not independently checked. Dan'a'yo keeps the noun sense "premier; prime minister." It is the middle word of 内閣総理大臣 (内閣 "cabinet" + 総理 + 大臣 "minister"), the signature line of the 1945 surrender broadcast on [Japan](../lookup/Japanese/Japan.md). The list on that page uses [宰相](宰相.md) for the office; 総理 is the formal modern title.
