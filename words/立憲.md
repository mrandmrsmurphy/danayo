## Notes
- [立 (char)](../characters/立%20(char).md) "establish, stand" + [憲](../characters/憲.md) "constitution, statute, law" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (立's is [[立]] itself, 憲's is [[憲法]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: true` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: true`.

立憲 means to establish or operate under a constitution — constitutional governance or constitutionalism as a system of rule bound by a written constitution rather than by arbitrary or absolute authority. It is highly productive: 立憲君主制 ("constitutional monarchy"), 立憲主義 ("constitutionalism" as a political philosophy).

Mandarin lìxiàn, Cantonese lap6 hin3, Japanese りっけん (rikken), and Korean 입헌 (ipheon) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese lập hiến is likewise compositional and standard, seen in quân chủ lập hiến ("constitutional monarchy").
