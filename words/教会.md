## Notes
- [教](../characters/教.md) "teach, religion" + [会](../characters/会%20(char).md) "meeting" — no stand-in relationship applies (教's own stand-in is [[教授]]; 会's own is itself).

教会 means church.

Mandarin jiàohuì, Cantonese gaau3 wui6, Japanese きょうかい, Korean 교회, and Vietnamese giáo hội are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` had all used the old minority reading "gyau" for 教 instead of the majority-established "gyou" — corrected. No homophones at the corrected reading.
