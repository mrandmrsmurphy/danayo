## Notes
- [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day, sun" + [記](../characters/記.md) "write, document" — no stand-in relationship applies (日's own stand-in is itself; 記's own is [[記憶]]).

日記 means diary, journal, or annal.

Mandarin rìjì, Cantonese jat6 gei3, Japanese にっき, Korean 일기, and Vietnamese nhật ký are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄧㄊㄍㄧ unique across both words and characters).
