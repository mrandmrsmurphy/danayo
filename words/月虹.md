## Notes
- [月](../characters/月%20(char).md) "moon" + [虹](../characters/虹.md) "rainbow" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [虹](../characters/虹.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 月's own stand-in is itself.

月虹 means moonbow.

Mandarin yuèhóng, Cantonese jyut6 hung4, Japanese げっこう, and Vietnamese nguyệt hống are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed `korean`: was garbled with an unclear extra value ("월홍, 문활") — simplified to the clean compositional 월홍, matching 月's and 虹's own citations exactly. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field, added `kwin: false` (諺文 웓홍 vs Korean 월홍, same 月-coda divergence documented on [[月]]'s own page). No homophones (注音 ⼔ㄊㄏㄛㄫ unique across both words and characters).
