## Notes
- [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "moon" + [光 (char)](../characters/光%20(char).md) "light"

月光 is moonlight: the light of the moon. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yuèguāng and Japanese as "moonlight". Korean 월광 and Cantonese jyut6 gwong1 match.

It is the night counterpart of [日光](日光.md), sunlight, and it is the reflected light of the [太陰](太陰.md), moon. The word for light in general is [光](光.md).
