## Notes
- [一 (char)](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" + [斉](../characters/斉.md) "uniform, aligned" — literally "one and aligned," hence "simultaneous, all at once"; this word is also the `stand_in` legitimizer for [[斉]], which cannot appear independently

**Structural bug found and fixed**: the traditional/simplified variant forms 一齊/一齐 had been stored in the `swadesh:` field (which should hold a Swadesh-list number or be blank) rather than `aliases:` (which was sitting empty) — a field-swap, not a blank-fill. Moved to `aliases:` as a proper list and cleared `swadesh:`.

一斉 names an action performed by many people or things at exactly the same moment, with no leader-follower stagger — 一斉に (issei ni) in Japanese is the ordinary adverbial form ("all at once, in unison"), used for anything from a starting gun firing on a race to a chorus of voices. Vietnamese nhất tề (confirmed via web search) preserves the same "together, in unison" sense, and Cantonese/Mandarin both stay within this literal simultaneity range without the figurative extensions many other compounds in this vault pick up. **Content added**: `korean: 일제`, the compositional concatenation of 一's own 일 + 斉's own 제 (a genuinely attested Sino-Korean reading, distinct from the Dan'a'yo 諺文); `vietnamese: nhất tề`.

**Real `kwin` bug, the same root-cause pattern already found on [[一定]] one iteration ago**: Dan'a'yo `諺文: 읻제` and the newly-filled `korean: 일제` diverge in the first syllable's coda (읻 vs 일) — 一's own Dan'a'yo reading retains the MC checked coda that Sino-Korean has regularized away, exactly the [[一 (char)]] bug already fixed at its root. Set `kwin: false` accordingly, rather than assuming `true` from a naive reading of the newly-filled fields. No homophones (注音 ㄧㄊㄐㄝㄧ unique among both words and characters).
