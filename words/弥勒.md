## Notes
- [弥 (char)](../characters/弥%20(char).md) "all the more; increasingly" (here used purely phonetically) + [勒](../characters/勒.md) "rein in" (also phonetic) — no stand-in relationship applies (弥's own `stand_in` is itself, 勒's is [[勒馬]]). Together they transliterate Sanskrit *Maitreya*, the future Buddha.

弥勒 (Maitreya/Mile) is one of Buddhism's most important figures — the bodhisattva prophesied to become the next Buddha, often depicted in East Asian art as the jovial "laughing Buddha" (布袋, Budai/Hotei, popularly conflated with Maitreya in folk religion though originally a separate historical monk).

Japanese みろく, Korean 미륵, and Vietnamese Di Lặc are all standard readings. **`cantonese` uses mei4, one of two real attested Cantonese variants for 彌** (the other, nei4, is 弥's own citation form on its character page) — both are genuinely documented readings of this character, with mei4 being the specific one conventionally used in this proper name, so no correction was made. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄝㄌㄨㄎ unique across both words and characters).
