## Notes
- [恵](../characters/恵.md) "favor, grace" + [山](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain" — the name of Hyesan, a city of North Korea on the Yalu. 惠 is the other form of 恵, which is why the filename uses 恵; 惠山 is kept as the word's alias.

恵山 (Hyesan) is a city of North Korea on the Yalu River (鴨緑江), facing China, and the capital of Ryanggang Province ([[両江]]道) (from memory, not re-checked). The name reads "grace mountain".

English and Chinese Wiktionary have 惠山 as the North Korean city (Mandarin Huìshān, Cantonese wai6 saan1), and English Wiktionary has a Korean entry as a hanja form of 혜산. The same string is also Huishan District of Wuxi in Jiangsu, China (the Vietnamese Wikipedia has it as Huệ Sơn). Japanese Wiktionary has no entry for the Korean city, so Japanese けいさん is composed from the on'yomi.
