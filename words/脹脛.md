## Notes
- [脹](../characters/脹.md) "swell" is separately legitimized by [[腫脹]]. [脛](../characters/脛.md) "shinbone" is separately legitimized by [[脛骨]]. No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

脹脛 means the calf, literally the "swollen shin" — 小腿 is the modern standard synonym (documented as `aliases`, a distinct spelling rather than an orthographic variant), and 腓 is another single-character synonym.
