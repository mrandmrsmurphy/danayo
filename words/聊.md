## Notes
- [聊](../characters/聊%20(char).md) "chat" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 聊 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

聊 means to chat or converse; by extension, in literary compounds it can also mean "somewhat, slightly" or "for the time being."

Mandarin liáo, Cantonese liu4, and Korean 료 (ryo) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading いささか (isasaka, "somewhat, slightly") rather than the on-reading リョウ (RYOU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese liêu is the standard reading. This word completes a genuine Dan'a'yo homophone with [[了]] ("done, finished, over") — the reciprocal callout was already in place there, cross-referenced here.
