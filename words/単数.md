## Notes
- [単](../characters/単.md) "single" + [数](../characters/数.md) "number"

単数 is the singular number. English Wiktionary glosses Japanese 単数 (たんすう) as "singularity" and, in grammar, "the singular number", and Chinese 單數 (Mandarin dānshù, Cantonese daan1 sou3) as "odd number" and, in grammar, "singular". The Dan'a'yo sense is the grammatical singular, the counterpart of [複数](複数.md), plural. The Korean 단수 field is built from the readings of the two characters.
