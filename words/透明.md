## Notes
- [透 (char)](../characters/透%20(char).md) "transparent, pass through" + [明 (char)](../characters/明%20(char).md) "bright, clear" — transparent.

透明 means transparent: letting light pass so that what is behind can be seen clearly, as of glass, clean water or a thin film. It is the visual counterpart of [[無色]] (colorless): a thing can be colored and still transparent (tinted glass), or colorless and opaque (white paint). Figuratively it means open to scrutiny, as in a transparent process, in Mandarin and Japanese. The opposite is built with the negator 不, giving 不透明 "opaque, not transparent," which is a regular compound and needs no separate entry. Mandarin tòumíng, Cantonese tau3 ming4, Japanese とうめい and Korean 투명 are the standard readings.
