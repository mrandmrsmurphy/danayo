## Notes
- [三十](../words/三十.md) "thirty" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — no stand-in relationship applies.

Japanese has a special reading: みそか (misoka), not the expected さんじゅうにち — the last day of a month is 晦日 (みそか), and the last day of the year specifically is 大晦日 (おおみそか). Mandarin sānshí rì, Cantonese saam1 sap6 jat6, Korean 삼십일, and Sino-Vietnamese tam thập nhật are all standard readings.
