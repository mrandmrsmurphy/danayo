## Notes
- [一 (char)](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" + [生](../characters/生.md) "life; birth"

一生 is "a lifetime; one's whole life; all one's life." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 一生 (yīshēng, Cantonese jat1 sang1) is "lifetime; all one's life; one's whole life," Japanese いっしょう is "a lifetime, one's whole life" (and the same characters are also a given name), and Korean 일생 is attested as well. Wiktionary has no Vietnamese section, so `vietnamese` nhất sinh is a compositional reading. It is used in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 躺平即是正义 on [China](../lookup/HSK/China.md), in 一生 … 活 之 人生 "a life lived one's whole life for…," standing in for the Chinese 一辈子. Related: [人生](人生.md) "human life," [終身](終身.md) "lifetime" and [生](../characters/生.md) "life."
