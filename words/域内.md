## Notes
- [域 (char)](../characters/域%20(char).md) "domain, region" + [内](../characters/内.md) "inside" — stand-in for neither: 域's own stand-in is the bare word [[域]], and 内's is [[内部]].

域内 is within the region, intra-regional. English Wiktionary has an entry only for Japanese 域内 (いきない), glossed "within the area; inside the area". The Mandarin, Cantonese and Korean 역내 fields are built from the readings of the two characters. [文法 - 03文字法](../grammar/文法%20-%2003文字法.md) uses it in 域内可読. Compare [校内](校内.md), on campus, [区域](区域.md), an area, and [地域](地域.md), a district.
