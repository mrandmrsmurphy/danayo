## Notes
- [教](../characters/教.md) "teach, religion" + [化](../characters/化%20(char).md) "transform" — no stand-in relationship applies (教's own stand-in is [[教授]]; 化's own is itself).

教化 means to educate, cultivate, or enlighten.

Mandarin jiàohuà, Cantonese gaau3 faa3, Japanese きょうか, Korean 교화, and Vietnamese giáo hoá (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼄ㄨㄏ⺢ unique across both words and characters).
