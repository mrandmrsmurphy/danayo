## Notes
- [弥 (char)](../characters/弥%20(char).md) "all the more; increasingly" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [弥 (char)](../characters/弥%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

弥 is an intensifying adverb — "all the more, increasingly" (弥漫 "to pervade, fill" — literally "increasingly spread") — and also serves as a phonetic transliteration element in Buddhist vocabulary borrowed from Sanskrit, most famously 弥勒 (Maitreya, the future Buddha).

Japanese み, Korean 미, and Vietnamese di are all standard readings. **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 弥 and [[袂]] ("sleeve") share an identical reading (me/머/ㄇㄝ) — added reciprocal homophone callouts to both pages.
