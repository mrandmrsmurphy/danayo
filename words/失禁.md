## Notes
- [失](../characters/失.md) "lose" + [禁](../characters/禁.md) "prohibit, forbid" — no stand-in relationship applies (失's own stand-in is [[滅失]]; 禁's own is [[禁止]]).

失禁 means incontinence — the loss of bladder or bowel control, literally "losing one's ability to withhold/restrict."

Mandarin shījìn, Cantonese sat1 gam3, Japanese しっきん, Korean 실금, and Vietnamese thất cấm are all standard, fully compositional readings. Added missing `kwin: false` (諺文 싣김 ≠ korean 실금). No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄊㄍㄧㄇ unique across both words and characters).
