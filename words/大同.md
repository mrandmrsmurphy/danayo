## Notes
- [大](../characters/大%20(char).md) "great" + [同](../characters/同.md) "same, together" — no stand-in relationship applies (大 is its own stand-in; 同's own is [[同一]]).

大同 means "Great Unity" — the Confucian utopian ideal of universal harmony. The philosophical sense is from the Book of Rites, Book 9 (《禮記·禮運》).

Japanese だいどう, Korean 대동, Mandarin dàtóng, and Cantonese daai6 tung4 are all standard readings, matching Dan'a'yo's own reading exactly (`kwin: true`). Vietnamese đại đồng is a standard reading. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄧㄉㄛㄫ unique across both words and characters).
