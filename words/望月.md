## Notes
- [望](../characters/望.md) "hope; gaze" + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "moon" — the full moon, traditionally the 15th night of the lunar month. Attested across Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese (もちづき/ぼうげつ, less common than 満月 but real), and Korean (망월, a literary Sino-Korean synonym alongside native 보름달).
The `japanese` field now holds the Sino-Japanese reading ぼうげつ, following the vault's convention for these fields; the everyday Japanese word for the full moon is the native もちづき (a jukujikun reading of the same characters), which is also well known as the surname Mochizuki, and the Notes above already give both. The blank `vietnamese` field now holds the compositional reading vọng nguyệt from the two character pages. The Korean 망월 differs from the Dan'a'yo 諺文 망웓 in the final consonant, so `kwin` is false.

The Calendar page lists 望月 as "the full moon itself," and says to compare 望日, "the day it falls on," so Dan'a'yo keeps the sense "the full moon" as an object in the sky and leaves the day to [望日](望日.md). The existing word [満月](満月.md) is its everyday rival ("full moon; also a 'full month' anniversary"). The claim that 満月 is more common than もちづき in Japanese is the page's own and has not been re-checked here.
