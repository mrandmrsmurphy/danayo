## Notes
- [二 (char)](../characters/二%20(char).md) "two/second" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day"

Like its siblings [[三日]] and [[四日]], this word's primary sense is the calendar-day name ("the 2nd of the month"), matching Japanese ふつか directly. **Content removed/restructured**: the frontmatter previously mixed this date sense with an unrelated "two days" duration sense — `mandarin: liǎng tiān` ("two days," using the colloquial duration-counter liǎng rather than the ordinal/compositional èr) and `korean: 이일, 이틀` (이틀 being the native Korean word for "a two-day span," crammed alongside the real Sino-Korean date reading 이일) — plus a bare body note "obsolete: two days" documenting the same duration sense yet again. Following the [[三日]] precedent (which stores its own analogous duration sense as the alias 三天, not embedded in the main fields), consolidated all of this into a single alias, `二天` ("two days"), and corrected `mandarin`/`korean` to the compositional date reading (`èr rì`/이일) matching the sibling family. Also filled previously blank `cantonese` with the compositional `ji6 jat6`, and added missing `kwin: false` (matching both siblings).
