## Notes
- [圓 (char)](../characters/圓%20(char).md) "circle" + [[錐]] "awl" + [[曲]] "bent" + [[線]] "thread", a portmanteau of [[圓錐]] "cone" + [[曲線]] "curve" — the family of curves formed by slicing a cone:
	1. <ruby>[圓](../words/圓.md)<rt>⼔ㄋ</rt></ruby> - circles
	2. <ruby>[楕圓](../words/楕圓.md)<rt>ㄊㄚ·⼔ㄋ</rt></ruby> - ellipses
	3. <ruby>[放物線](../words/放物線.md)<rt>ㄅㄚㄫㄇㄨㄊㄙ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby> - parabola
	4. <ruby>[双曲線](../words/双曲線.md)<rt>ㄙ⺢ㄫㄎ⼄ㄎㄙ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby> - hyperbola

Mandarin yuánzhuī qūxiàn, Cantonese jyun4 zeoi1 kuk1 sin3, Japanese えんすいきょくせん (ensui kyokusen), and Korean 원추 곡선 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 圓's, 錐's, 曲's, and 線's own stored citations exactly. **Found and fixed two real bugs**: `mandarin` was missing its initial "y" (stored as "uánzhuī qūxiàn"), and `korean` mixed the real compositional word (원추 곡선) together with a native/colloquial alternative (원뿔 곡선, using native 원뿔 "cone" in place of Sino-Korean 원추) — the latter belongs in Notes prose, not the `korean` field. Vietnamese Đường conic ("conic line/curve") mixes a native word (đường, "line") with the international loanword "conic," rather than a Sino-Vietnamese compound.

`kwin` is false: 錐, 曲, and 線 each individually diverge from Korean on their own character pages, which alone is enough to make the AND-rule false; 圓 is independently kwin-true. No homophones (注音 ⼔ㄋㄐㄨㄧㄎ⼘ㄎㄙ⼶ㄋ unique among both words and characters).
