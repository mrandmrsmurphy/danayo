## Notes
- [圓 (char)](../characters/圓%20(char).md) "circle" + [錐](../characters/錐.md) "awl" + [曲](../characters/曲.md) "bent" + [線](../characters/線.md) "thread", a portmanteau of [圓錐](圓錐.md) "cone" + [曲線](曲線.md) "curve" — the family of curves formed by slicing a cone:
	1. <ruby>[圓](../words/圓.md)<rt>⼔ㄋ</rt></ruby> - circles
	2. <ruby>[楕圓](../words/楕圓.md)<rt>ㄊㄚ⼔ㄋ</rt></ruby> - ellipses
	3. <ruby>[放物線](../words/放物線.md)<rt>ㄈㄚㄫㄇㄨㄊㄙ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby> - parabola
	4. <ruby>[双曲線](../words/双曲線.md)<rt>ㄙ⺢ㄫㄎ⼄ㄎㄙ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby> - hyperbola

Mandarin yuánzhuī qūxiàn, Cantonese jyun4 zeoi1 kuk1 sin3, Japanese えんすいきょくせん (ensui kyokusen), and Korean 원추 곡선 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 圓's, 錐's, 曲's, and 線's own stored citations exactly. Korean 원뿔 곡선 (native 원뿔 "cone") is a colloquial alternative to the Sino-Korean 원추 곡선. Vietnamese Đường conic ("conic line/curve") mixes a native word (đường, "line") with the international loanword "conic," rather than a Sino-Vietnamese compound.
