## Notes
- [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big" + [小 (char)](../characters/小%20(char).md) "small"

大小 is size, the large and the small. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 大小 (Mandarin dàxiǎo, Cantonese daai6 siu2) as "big and small" and "size; proportions; dimensions; magnitude", and Japanese だいしょう as "large and small". Chinese has further senses, "old and young; adults and children", "senior and junior" and "at any rate; anyway", which Dan'a'yo does not take. The Korean 대소 and Vietnamese đại tiểu fields are built from the readings of the two characters. Compare [規模](規模.md), scale, and [長短](長短.md), length.
