## Notes
"To depend on, to be the basis for." `vietnamese: y cứ` (y cứ vào, "to rely on/base oneself on") is directly attested (confirmed via web search), especially familiar in Buddhist-teaching contexts, paralleling the more common everyday sibling căn cứ (same 拠/據 root). **Real gap found and fixed on [[拠]]'s own page**: its `vietnamese` field crammed two readings into one malformed list item ("cớ, cứ") — reformatted into a proper list. Filled the blank `pos: 事詞`. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 의교 diverges from Sino-Korean 의거 in the second syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄧㄜㄍ⼄ unique among both words and characters).
