## Notes
- [依 (char)](../characters/依%20(char).md) "rely on, accord with" + [拠](../characters/拠.md) "depend upon" — "to depend on, to be the basis for." `vietnamese: y cứ` (y cứ vào, "to rely on/base oneself on") is directly attested, especially familiar in Buddhist-teaching contexts, paralleling the more common everyday sibling căn cứ (same 拠/據 root). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 의교 diverges from Sino-Korean 의거 in the second syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄧㄜㄍ⼄ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 拠's own `stand_in` is this exact word (依拠), so 拠 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 依拠.
