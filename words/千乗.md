## Etymology
- [千](../characters/千.md) "thousand" + [乗 (char)](../characters/乗%20(char).md) "chariot" (a classical counter for war chariots — not the "to ride" verb sense)

## Notes
A classical term, literally "a thousand chariots" — historically the standard measure of a state's military strength, and by extension a "vassal lord of the Son of Heaven" (a feudal lord capable of fielding a thousand war chariots, as distinguished from the Son of Heaven's own 萬乘, "ten thousand chariots").
