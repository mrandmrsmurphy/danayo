## Notes
- [禁](../characters/禁.md) "prohibit, forbid" + [獵 (char)](../characters/獵%20(char).md) "hunt, hunting" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (禁's is [[禁止]], 獵's is [[獵]] itself), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

禁獵 names a hunting ban — a regulatory prohibition on hunting certain species, in certain areas, or during certain seasons, the land-animal counterpart to [[禁漁]] ("fishing ban"). Both terms happen to share the exact same Japanese reading きんりょう (kinryō) as a real-world coincidence — 漁 and 獵 both carry the on-yomi RYOU — though this is unrelated to the Dan'a'yo-internal reading system and does not trigger a homophone callout here, since their Dan'a'yo 注音 differ (ㄍㄧㄇ·⼄ vs ㄍㄧㄇㄌㄛㄆ).

Mandarin jìnliè, Cantonese gam3 lip6, and Korean 금렵 (geumnyeop) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese cấm liệp is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form; everyday Vietnamese more often says cấm săn bắn ("forbid hunting") analytically.
