## Notes
- [字](../characters/字%20(char).md) "glyph, character" + 喃 "mutter, chatter" (no character page yet for 喃 — flagged for a future character-creation pass) — no stand-in relationship applies (字's own stand-in is itself).

字喃 (chữ Nôm) is the historical Vietnamese writing system that adapted Chinese characters to write the Vietnamese vernacular — as opposed to 漢字/chữ Hán, which wrote Sino-Vietnamese/Literary Chinese directly. It was displaced by the Latin-based Quốc Ngữ alphabet in the 20th century.

Japanese チュノム and Korean 쯔놈 are phonetic transliterations of the Vietnamese name "chữ Nôm" itself, rather than compositional on'yomi readings — since Chu Nom is a specifically Vietnamese phenomenon with no independent East Asian equivalent term. Mandarin zìnán and Cantonese zi6 naam4 are compositional. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄋㄚㄇ unique across both words and characters).
