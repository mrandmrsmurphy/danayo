## Notes
- [構](../characters/構.md) "build; construct" + [成 (char)](../characters/成%20(char).md) "become; complete"

構成 is to compose or constitute: to make up a whole from parts, and the way the parts are arranged. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin gòuchéng as "to constitute; to form; to make up; to pose" and "constitution; formation", and Japanese こうせい as "configuration, structure, construct; to compose, to constitute". Vietnamese cấu thành is the same word, and Korean 구성 and Cantonese kau3 sing4 match.

The parts are each a [成分](成分.md), and the whole may be taken apart again by [分解](分解.md). Its closest relative is [結合](結合.md), to combine.
