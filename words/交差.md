## Notes
- [交](../characters/交%20(char).md) "to hand over, submit" + [差](../characters/差.md) "errand, dispatched task" — a genuine colloquial Mandarin idiom, 交差 (jiāochāi), "to complete an assigned errand and report back" (as in 回去交差, "go back and report the job done"), distinct from the unrelated look-alike [[交叉]] "intersect." Real Japanese conflates both under a single spelling こうさ (see that page's Notes), but Dan'a'yo keeps them separate compounds with distinct romanizations.

`vietnamese: giao sai` is compositional (交's giao + 差's own errand-sense reading sai, as in 出差 xuất sai "business trip/dispatched errand") rather than a confirmed fixed idiom — no independently attested Vietnamese equivalent for this specific colloquial usage was found via web search. Fixed corrupted `japanese` field (was かうさ, historical kana orthography) to modern こうさ, and added the missing `korean: 교차` (compositional). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 굣차 diverges from Sino-Korean 교차 in the first syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼄ㄨㄑㄚ unique among both words and characters).
