## Notes
- [散](../characters/散.md) "scatter" + [布](../characters/布.md) "linen" — this word is itself the stand-in for its own character (散's own stand-in is 散布 itself).

散布 means to scatter, spread, or disseminate.

Mandarin sànbù, Cantonese saan3 bou3, Japanese さんぷ, Korean 산포, and Vietnamese tán bố are all standard, fully compositional readings. Removed a self-referential `散布` from `aliases` (matched the filename itself, flagged from [[散歩]]'s own turn), kept genuine traditional variant 散佈. Fixed callout spacing to canonical (blank line after header). Homophone of [[散歩]] "walk; stroll" (both sanbo/산보/ㄙㄚㄇㄅㄛ) — a genuine coincidence despite Mandarin/Japanese distinguishing them, already fully cross-referenced from 散歩's side.
