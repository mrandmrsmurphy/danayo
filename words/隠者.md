## Notes
- [隠](../characters/隠.md) "hide, conceal" + [者](../characters/者.md) "person" — "hidden person," a hermit. Their own `stand_in` values: 隠's is [[隠蔵]]; 者's is [[学者]]; none points here, so 隠者 is an independent compound.

隠者 is a hermit: someone who withdraws from society to live apart, often for religious reasons. Mandarin yǐnzhě (隱者) and Cantonese jan2 ze2 are the ordinary words, and Japanese いんじゃ and Korean 은자 are the same compound. The character 隠 has the alias 隱. Compare [[修行]] (spiritual discipline) and [[僧侶]] (monk).
