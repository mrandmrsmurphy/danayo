## Notes
- [泳](../characters/泳%20(char).md) "swim" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 泳 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

泳 means to swim: to propel oneself through water with the limbs (游泳, "swim"; 泳衣, "swimsuit"; 泳池, "swimming pool"; 蛙泳, "breaststroke"). The graph is a 形声 compound of 氵 "water" with the phonetic 永.

Mandarin yǒng, Cantonese wing6, Japanese えい (水泳/すいえい, "swimming"; 競泳/きょうえい, "competitive swimming"), Korean 영 (수영, "swimming"; 영법, "stroke") and Vietnamese vạnh are the standard Sino-xenic readings. Japanese uses the native verb 泳ぐ (およぐ) for the everyday word, so on'yomi えい is bound to compounds, and Korean has native 헤엄치다.
