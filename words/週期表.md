## Notes
- [週](characters/週.md) "cyclic" + [期 (char)](characters/期%20(char).md) "period" + [表](characters/表.md) "indicator, table"

週期表 names the periodic table of chemical elements — the chart organizing all known elements by increasing atomic number into rows ("periods," 週期) and columns ("groups") that share recurring chemical properties. The compound is transparent: 週期 ("cyclic period") names the periodicity itself — the recurring pattern of properties as atomic number increases — and 表 ("table, chart") names its tabular presentation.

Dan'a'yo's own element-naming system, laid out on [[Periodic Table]], follows a systematic scheme rather than borrowing each element's name piecemeal: elements in groups 1, 2, and 13–18 take the suffix 素 ("element"), while d-block transition metals and the lanthanide/actinide series take the suffix 金 ("metal"), with the preceding character chosen to reflect each element's own etymology (mythological, toponymic, or descriptive) wherever possible.

Cross-linguistically the compound is a stable Sino-Xenic loan: Sino-Korean 주기률표 and Japanese しゅうきひょう both directly concatenate the component readings, and Vietnamese bảng tuần hoàn ("cyclic table") renders the same underlying concept using native Vietnamese vocabulary rather than a direct Sino-Vietnamese transliteration.
