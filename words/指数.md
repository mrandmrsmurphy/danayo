## Notes
- [指](../characters/指.md) "point to, indicate" + [数](../characters/数.md) "number" — literally "pointing number," the exponent *n* in *xⁿ*, or an index number more generally (e.g. a statistical index).

Mandarin zhǐshù, Cantonese zi2 sou3, Japanese しすう, Korean 지수, and Vietnamese chỉ số are all standard, fully compositional, and genuinely living terms for this concept across the region — no register split with a competing native form.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 즤수 diverges from Sino-Korean 지수 in the first syllable's vowel, since 指 independently carries a diverging reading on its own character page (Dan'a'yo 즤 vs Korean 지, the same MC-final split documented on [[数字]]). No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄜㄙㄨ unique among both words and characters).
