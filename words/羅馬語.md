## Notes
- [羅](../characters/羅.md) "Rome" + [馬 (char)](../characters/馬%20(char).md) "horse" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language" — none of the three characters' own `stand_in` points here (羅's is [[羅馬]], 馬's is [[馬]] itself, 語's is [[言語]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 羅 and 語 are each individually `kwin: false`.

羅馬語 is a Dan'a'yo-internal coinage for the Latin language, literally "Roman language" — but Latin is real-world known throughout the Sinosphere by the transliterated name 拉丁語/拉丁语/拉丁 (Lādīng, from "Latin"), not a calque of "Roman." Mandarin lādīngyǔ, Cantonese laai1 ding1 jyu5, Korean 라틴 (ratin, a direct loanword transliteration), and Japanese ラテン語 (Raten-go) all therefore reflect the real, attested term for Latin in each language rather than a compositional reading of 羅馬語 itself. Vietnamese Latinh is likewise the standard Vietnamese name. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
