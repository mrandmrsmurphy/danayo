## Notes
- [光 (char)](../characters/光%20(char).md) "light"

光 is 会意: [[火]] ("fire") over [[卩]] ("kneeling person") — fire held above a person's head, emphasizing radiant shining (Shuowen: 明也，从火在人上, "brightness; from fire above a person"). It is one of the most productive characters in the vault, spanning literal light and radiance ([[光芒]] "rays," [[閃光]] "flash of light," [[陽光]] "sunshine") and figurative brightness/glory ([[光明]] "brightness; hope," [[光栄]] "honorable, glorious," and the chengyu [[光明正大]] "aboveboard, fair and square").

Mandarin guāng, Cantonese gwong1, and Japanese こう (kō, on'yomi, fully productive: 光線 こうせん "ray of light") alongside native ひか(る) (hika(ru), "to shine, glow," the everyday verb) all preserve the sense. Korean 광 (gwang) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 빛 (bit, "light," the everyday noun). Vietnamese quang is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in hào quang ("aura, halo, radiance") and quang minh ("bright, glorious"); the character page's other three stored candidates (cuông, quàng, quăng) are unrelated and look like corpus noise.

This word shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading (kwang/쾅/ㄎ⺢ㄫ) with another word still awaiting its own turn in this sweep, [[筐]] ("bamboo basket") — the reciprocal half of this callout will be completed when it comes up.
