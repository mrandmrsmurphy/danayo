## Notes
- [成 (char)](../characters/成%20(char).md) "become; complete" + [分 (char)](../characters/分%20(char).md) "part"

成分 is a component or ingredient: one of the parts that make up a whole, or what a substance is made of. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin chéngfèn as "ingredient; element; component" and "composition; make-up", and Japanese せいぶん as "ingredient". Vietnamese thành phần is the same word, and Korean 성분 and Cantonese sing4 fan6 match.

Mandarin also uses it for a person's class background, which Dan'a'yo does not take. It is what [構成](構成.md) puts together, and what [分解](分解.md) separates out, and a looser name for a part is [要素](要素.md).
