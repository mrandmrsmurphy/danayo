## Notes
- [横](../characters/横.md) "crossing horizontally; across laterally" + [領](../characters/領.md) "territory"

横領 is embezzlement: to take for oneself property entrusted to one's care. Wiktionary glosses Chinese 橫領 as "embezzlement; to embezzle; peculation" and lists Korean 횡령 as "embezzlement". Wiktionary gives no pinyin for the Chinese entry, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings, and Japanese おうりょう is the same compound in its Japanese reading. Here 横 means "wrongfully, unreasonably", so the word is literally "to seize wrongfully".

What distinguishes the crime is the prior trust. The embezzler is entrusted with the property and then converts it, so the word differs from [窃盗](窃盗.md), theft by one with no right to it, and from [詐取](詐取.md), obtaining by deception.
