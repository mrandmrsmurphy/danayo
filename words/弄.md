## Notes
- [弄 (char)](../characters/弄%20(char).md) "tamper with; mess with" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [弄 (char)](../characters/弄%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

弄 means to toy with, tamper with, or mess with — a 会意 character depicting two hands holding a piece of jade, playing with it. It extends idiomatically to "to end up doing X" (弄假成真/lộng giả thành chân, "playing pretend turns real") and, in the Vietnamese-attested sense, "to abuse/overstep" (弄權/lộng quyền, "to abuse power").

Japanese ろう, Korean 농, and Vietnamese lộng (the character's own field also lists several other Nôm variants — luồng, lòng, lóng, etc. — for related senses) are all standard readings. **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 弄 and [[籠]] ("cage, basket") share an identical reading (long/롱/ㄌㄛㄫ) — added reciprocal homophone callouts to both pages.
