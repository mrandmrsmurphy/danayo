## Notes
- [恭](../characters/恭.md) "respectful" + [喜](../characters/喜%20(char).md) "like, love, enjoy" + [発](../characters/発%20(char).md) "issue forth, discharge" + [財](../characters/財.md) "wealth" — no stand-in relationship applies (恭's own stand-in is [[恭敬]]; 喜/発 are both their own stand-ins; 財's own is [[財産]]).

恭喜発財 is a festive New Year's greeting wishing prosperity — originated in post-Opium War Guangzhou Cantonese, now a general Asian phrase.

Mandarin gōngxǐ fācái, Cantonese gung1 hei2 faat3 coi4, and Vietnamese cung hỉ phát tài are all standard, fully compositional readings (財's syllable follows the same cai/채 pattern already established on the sibling word [[発財]], consistent throughout). **Real bugs found and fixed**: a stray backspace control character was corrupting `諺文`; `諺文`/`羅馬字` had 発's syllable mistyped pad→fad (注音 was already correct); `japanese`/`korean` had been contaminated with full native sentence-length greetings ("金持ちになれますように"/"부자가 될 수 있기를") instead of compositional readings — corrected to きょうきはっさい/공희발재. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼄ㄫㄏㄧㄈㄚㄊㄐㄚㄧ unique across both words and characters).
