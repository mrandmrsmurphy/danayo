## Notes
- [樟](../characters/樟.md) "camphor tree" + [脳](../characters/脳%20(char).md) "brain" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [樟](../characters/樟.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 脳's own stand-in is itself.

樟脳 means camphor.

Mandarin zhāngnǎo, Japanese しょうのう, Korean 장뇌, and Vietnamese chương não are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `cantonese` had the wrong initial (zeong1 → zoeng1, matching 樟's own citation). No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄫㄋㄚㄨ unique across both words and characters).
