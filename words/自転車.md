## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" is separately legitimized by [[自身]]. [転](../characters/転.md) "turn, change" is separately legitimized by [[転化]]. [車](../characters/車%20(char).md) "car" is separately legitimized by [[車]] itself. No cranberry: none of the three constituents' own `stand_in` points here.

自転車 combines 自 ("self"), 転 ("turn"), and 車 ("vehicle") — "bicycle," literally "self-turning vehicle."

Korean 자전거 (jajeongeo) uses a genuine alternate Sino-Korean reading of 車 (거, not the character's stored primary 차) specific to this and a few other compounds (인력거, etc.) — not a bug. Vietnamese xe đạp is the real, standard native Vietnamese term for "bicycle," not decomposable from any constituent's own stored reading — likewise not a bug.
