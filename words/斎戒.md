## Notes
- [斎](../characters/斎.md) "fast, study" + [戒](../characters/戒.md) "warn, caution" — this word is the stand-in for [斎](../characters/斎.md), which cannot appear independently.

斎戒 means to fast or observe abstinence. The character 斎 alone is too bounded to stand by itself in Dan'a'yo, so this compound serves as its viable form.
