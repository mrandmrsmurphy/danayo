## Notes
- [数](../characters/数.md) "number, count" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study, learning" — literally "the study of numbers."

Mandarin shùxué, Cantonese sou3 hok6, Japanese すうがく (sūgaku) and Korean 수학 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Cantonese reads 数 as sou3 in the noun sense here, not the verb sou2 "to count" stored on the character page. Vietnamese toán học is the standard word for mathematics as a whole discipline; số học (the Sino-Vietnamese form of the same characters 數學) means only "arithmetic," the narrower elementary branch.

Song-dynasty mathematician Qin Jiushao's treatise of 1247 is known as 數書九章 or 數學九章, one of the early uses of 數學 as a name for the whole field; the older Chinese tradition more often spoke of 算術 or 算學. In modern Japanese, Chinese, and Korean usage 数学 is the umbrella term, with 算術/算数 reserved for the elementary subject, a division Dan'a'yo keeps (see [算術](算術.md)).

Ancient mathematics (following _The Nine Chapters on the Mathematical Art_) divided the field into nine categories:
1. **方田 (_Fāngtián_)**: land surveying and area.
2. **粟米 (_Sùmǐ_)**: exchange of grains and trade ratios.
3. **差分 (_Chāfēn_)**: proportional distribution (taxation).
4. **少廣 (_Shǎoguǎng_)**: square and cube roots.
5. **商功 (_Shānggōng_)**: engineering (calculating volumes for walls/canals).
6. **均輸 (_Jūnshū_)**: fair taxation based on distance and population.
7. **盈不足 (_Yíngbùzú_)**: "excess and deficit" (linear equations).
8. **方程 (_Fāngchéng_)**: simultaneous linear equations.
9. **勾股 (_Gōugǔ_)**: right-angled triangles (Pythagorean theorem).
