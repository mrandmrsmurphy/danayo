## Notes
- [幟 (char)](../characters/幟%20(char).md) "pennant; banner" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [幟 (char)](../characters/幟%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

幟 names a pennant or banner, most familiar in the compound 旗幟 ("flag, banner," also figuratively "a declared stance or position").

Japanese し (on'yomi; the native のぼり, nobori, is the everyday word for a banner/flag instead), Korean 치, and Vietnamese xí are all standard readings. **A genuine triple Dan'a'yo homophone**: 幟, [[歯]], and [[置]] all share the identical reading (ci/치/ㄑㄧ) — added reciprocal homophone callouts to all three pages.
