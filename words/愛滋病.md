## Notes
- [愛](../characters/愛%20(char).md) "love" + [滋](../characters/滋.md) "nourish" + [病](../characters/病.md) "illness" — a phonetic transliteration of "AIDS" (愛滋 ≈ "aids") plus 病 "illness." 滋's own `stand_in` is [[滋生]], 病's is [[疾病]] and 愛's is itself; none points here, so 愛滋病 is an independent compound.

愛滋病 is AIDS. Mandarin àizībìng and Cantonese oi3 zi1 beng6 are the Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macau forms; mainland China writes 艾滋病. Japanese uses エイズ and Korean 에이즈, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. The characters 愛 and 滋 are chosen for sound, so the word does not literally mean "love-nourish disease." Compare [[病毒]] (virus) and [[疾病]] (disease).
