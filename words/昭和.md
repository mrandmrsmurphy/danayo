## Notes
- [昭 (char)](../characters/昭%20(char).md) “bright, glorious” + [和](../characters/和.md) “harmony, peace” — literally “glorious harmony,” the era name for Emperor Shōwa's reign (1926–1989), drawn from this passage in the 書経 (Shokyō, “Classic of History”): 百姓昭明，協和萬邦, “The hundred surnames are gloriously enlightened; the myriad polities are united and harmonious.” Calls to mind Japanese imperialism in the minds of many.
- Also a ward in Nagoya, Aichi Prefecture.

Japanese せうわ preserves the historical kana spelling (せう for modern しょう), matching how the era name was written at the time. Mandarin Zhāohé, Cantonese ciu1 wo4, and Vietnamese Chiêu Hòa are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 昭's and 和's own stored citations exactly. **Found and fixed a real bug**: `korean` was stored as 쇼와, a phonetic transliteration of the Japanese pronunciation (ショウワ) rather than a genuine Sino-Korean reading — inconsistent with this vault's standing convention (matching [明治](明治.md)'s own 명치) of using the real compositional Sino-Korean reading for Japanese era names. Corrected to 소화, matching 昭's own 소 and 和's own 화.

`kwin` is false: 昭 alone diverges from Korean on its own character page, which alone is enough to make the AND-rule false. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄛㄨㄏ⺢ unique among both words and characters).
