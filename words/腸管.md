## Notes
- [腸](../characters/腸.md) "intestine, sausage" + [管](../characters/管.md) "pipe, tube" — stand-in for [[腸]], which is never used on its own

腸's Sino-Japanese and Sino-Korean readings (ちょう/장) coincide with 長 ("long, chief"), so Japanese ちょうかん and Korean 장관 for 腸管 are homophones of the unrelated, very common 長官 ("minister, director-general"; in Korean 장관 is also "spectacle" via 壯觀). The Korean field holds the compositional Sino-Korean reading, not the native 창자 "intestines". These Japanese and Korean remarks are from memory, not re-checked.

Vietnamese tràng quản is compositional; "tràng" is a productive Sino-Vietnamese morpheme for "intestine" (trực tràng "rectum", đại tràng "colon", tá tràng "duodenum"), though the two-syllable compound itself is not confirmed. The everyday native word for intestines is ruột.

The colloquial compound 腸子 is not an orthographic variant of 腸管, so it is not listed as an alias; it has no word file yet and is a possible future coinage. No other word shares the 注音 ㄑㄚㄫㄍ⺢ㄋ (checked 2026-10-13).
