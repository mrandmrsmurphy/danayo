## Notes
- [破 (char)](../characters/破%20(char).md) "rend, break" + [裂](../characters/裂.md) "split, crack" — 裂's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 破's own is [[破]] itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 裂 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

破裂 means to rupture, burst, or crack open — used for physical ruptures (a pipe, a blood vessel, a balloon) as well as figuratively for negotiations, relationships, or agreements breaking down entirely (関係が破裂する, "the relationship ruptures/breaks down").

Mandarin pòliè, Cantonese po3 lit6, Japanese はれつ (haretsu), Korean 파열 (payeol), and Vietnamese phá liệt are all fully compositional and share both the literal and figurative senses across the sphere.
