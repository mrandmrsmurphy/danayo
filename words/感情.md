## Notes
- [感](../characters/感.md) "feel, sense" + [情](../characters/情.md) "feelings, emotion" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [情](../characters/情.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (感's own stand-in is [[感触]]).

感情 means emotions or feeling — the subjective experience of affect.

Mandarin gǎnqíng, Cantonese gam2 cing4, Japanese かんじょう, Korean 감정, and Vietnamese cảm tình are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄇㄑㄧㄫ unique across both words and characters).
