## Notes
- [成](../characters/成%20(char).md) "become" + [立](../characters/立%20(char).md) "stand" — no stand-in relationship applies (成's own stand-in is itself; 立's own is itself).

成立 means to come into existence, be established, or gain approval.

Mandarin chénglì, Cantonese sing4 lap6, Japanese せいりつ, Korean 성립, and Vietnamese thành lập are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `諺文` had a typo (신립→싱립, ㅇ→ㄴ) not matching the compositional 싱+립. Trimmed redundant alternate-spelling `cantonese` variant. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄫㄌㄧㄆ unique across both words and characters).
