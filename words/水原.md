## Notes
- [水](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [原](../characters/原.md) "plain, original" — no stand-in relationship applies (水's own stand-in is itself; 原's own is [[原始]]).

水原 covers two distinct proper-noun referents: the South Korean city Suwon, and the Japanese surname Mizuhara. `japanese` correctly carries two distinct readings for these two senses — スウォン (a phonetic transliteration loanword for the Korean city name) and みずはら (the native kun'yomi reading, as a Japanese surname) — following the same pattern as other dual-sense proper-noun/homograph words (cf. [[一日]], [[万歳]]). Mandarin Shuǐyuán, Cantonese seoi2 jyun4, Korean 수원 (genuinely the real Korean city name), and Vietnamese Thủy Nguyên are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 水's and 原's own stored citations. `kwin: true` because 諺文 수원 matches Korean 수원 exactly. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases: []`. For the specific administrative city, see 水原市. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨ⼔ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
