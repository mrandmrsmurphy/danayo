## Notes
- [京](../characters/京.md) "capital" + [城](../characters/城.md) "walled city, castle" — "capital city, metropolis." Kinh thành is well-established everyday Vietnamese for a walled imperial capital (as at Huế's Kinh thành Huế). Notably, 京城 (Gyeongseong) was also the official Japanese-colonial-era name for Seoul (1910–1945), directly reflected in this word's own `korean: 경성` reading — this vault documents the plain compositional meaning "capital city" rather than the specific historical toponym. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 경싱 diverges from Sino-Korean 경성 in the second syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼶ㄫㄙㄧㄫ unique among both words and characters).
