## Notes
- [地](../characters/地%20(char).md) "ground" + [理](../characters/理.md) "principle" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study" — no stand-in relationship applies (地 is its own stand-in; 理's own is [[理由]]; 学's is [[学習]]).

地理学 means geography as an academic discipline — the systematic study of the earth's features.

Japanese ちりがく, Korean 지리학, and Vietnamese địa lí học are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄧㄜㄌㄧㄏㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
