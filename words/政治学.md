## Notes
- [政](../characters/政.md) "government" + [治](../characters/治.md) "govern" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study" — no stand-in relationship applies (政's own stand-in is [[政治]] itself; 治's own is [[統治]]; 学's own is [[学習]]).

政治学 means political science.

Mandarin zhèngzhìxué, Cantonese zing3 zi6 hok6, Japanese せいじがく, Korean 정치학, and Vietnamese chính trị học are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed the same real bug found on [[政治]]: `羅馬字` had a garbled/contaminated value (jing'yoghag→jingcihag). No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄫㄑㄧㄏㄚㄎ unique across both words and characters).
