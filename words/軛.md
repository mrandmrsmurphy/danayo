## Notes
- [軛](../characters/軛%20(char).md) "yoke" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

軛 means yoke — a wooden crossbar harnessing draft animals.

Mandarin è, Cantonese aak1, Japanese あく, Korean 액, and Vietnamese ách are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bugs found and fixed**: `korean` had been stored as the literal string "null" (rather than a blank YAML value or the real reading) — corrected to compositional 액; `羅馬字` was missing its glide apostrophe (ag→'ag). Added missing `pos`/`japanese`/`kwin`. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄚㄎ/'ag/악) with the unrelated [[悪]] ("bad, evil").
