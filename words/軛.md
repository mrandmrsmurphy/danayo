## Notes
- [軛](../characters/軛%20(char).md) "yoke" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

軛 means yoke — a wooden crossbar harnessing draft animals.

Mandarin è, Cantonese aak1, Japanese あく, Korean 액, and Vietnamese ách are all standard, fully compositional readings.
