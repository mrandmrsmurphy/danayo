## Notes
- [全 (char)](../characters/全%20(char).md) "whole, entire" + [球 (char)](../characters/球%20(char).md) "sphere, ball" — literally "whole sphere," i.e. the whole globe.

全球 means "global," the whole world taken as a unit — the standard modern term for globalism/globalization vocabulary (全球化, "globalization").

Mandarin quánqiú, Cantonese cyun4 kau4, Korean 전구 (jeon-gu), and Japanese ぜんきゅう (zenkyū) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 全's and 球's own stored citations. Vietnamese toàn cầu is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, matching 全's toàn (one of two attested readings) and 球's own cầu, and is itself the real, everyday Vietnamese word for "global" (toàn cầu hóa, "globalization"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 줜규 diverges from Sino-Korean 전구 in both syllables. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄐ⼔ㄋㄍ⼜ unique among both words and characters).
