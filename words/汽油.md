## Notes
- [汽](../characters/汽.md) "vapor, steam" + [油 (char)](../characters/油%20(char).md) "oil" — no stand-in relationship applies (汽's own stand-in is [[蒸汽]]; 油's own is itself).

汽油 means gasoline.

Mandarin qìyóu and Cantonese hei3 jau4 are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 汽's and 油's own stored citations. Japanese ガソリン and Korean 가솔린 are both the international phonetic loanword "gasoline" rather than a compositional Sino reading — the same genuine divergence pattern already established on [[千米]]/[[毫米]] for modern industrial/technical terms — and Vietnamese dầu xăng is the real native term (dầu "oil" + xăng "gasoline/petrol").
