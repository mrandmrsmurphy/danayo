## Notes
- [六 (char)](../characters/六%20(char).md) "six, sixth" + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — literally "sixth month," June.

Mandarin liùyuè, Cantonese luk6 jyut6, and Japanese ろくがつ (rokugatsu) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 六's and 月's own stored citations exactly. Korean is a genuine exception: 유월, not the compositionally-expected 륙월 — Korean month names for 6月 and 10月 are famous, well-documented irregular contractions that drop the number's own coda consonant for euphony, in both North and South Korean alike, so this isn't a dialect choice but a real irregularity independent of this vault's usual North-Korean-reading convention.
