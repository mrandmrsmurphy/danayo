## Notes
- [取](../characters/取.md) "take" + [得](../characters/得.md) "get" — `characters/取.md`'s own `stand_in` points to this word (取得), meaning 取 is a bound morpheme that never appears independently outside this compound; 得's own `stand_in` points elsewhere (獲得).

Mandarin qǔdé, Cantonese ceoi2 dak1, Japanese しゅとく, and Korean 취득 are the standard readings. Vietnamese thủ đắc is a real Sino-Vietnamese word, used especially in legal and formal contexts ("acquire," e.g. property or rights).
