## Notes
- [南](../characters/南.md) "south" + [亜](../characters/亜.md) "Asia" — South Asia; the Indian subcontinent and surrounding nations

南亜 is South Asia: the Indian subcontinent and its surroundings. It joins 南, south, with 亜, the standard abbreviation for 亜細亜, Asia. English Wiktionary lists it for Chinese as 南亞, "South Asia" (an alias; Mandarin Nán Yà, Cantonese naam4 aa3), and for Vietnamese Nam Á as "South Asia" and "South Asian". It has no Japanese or Korean entry, so Japanese なんあ here is built from the characters; the Korean field gives 남아시아.

English Wikipedia lists the modern states of South Asia as Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, the Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan and Sri Lanka, with Afghanistan often included. The regional body SAARC (South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation) has those eight as members and was founded in Dhaka on 8 December 1985.

The region matters to the Sinosphere through Buddhism. Chinese monks travelled there for texts and teachers: Faxian (法顯), who went on foot from the Later Qin state to classical India; Xuanzang (玄奘), whose travelogue covers his journey to the Indian subcontinent in 629–645; and Yijing (義淨), whose account covers the sea route between China and India.
