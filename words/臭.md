## Notes
- [臭](characters/臭%20(char).md) "stinky, smelly" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 臭 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

臭 means stinky or smelly. Also used as a short form for "bromine": <ruby>[臭素](/words/臭素.md)<rt>ㄑㄨㄙㄛ</rt></ruby>

Mandarin chòu, Cantonese cau3, Japanese しゅう, Korean 취, and Vietnamese xú are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 臭's own stored citation exactly). Filled the blank `japanese`/`vietnamese` fields. Discovered a genuine 3-way homophone group with [[枢]] and [[趨]] (all 注音 ㄑㄨ, 諺文 추) — cross-referenced all three pages.
