## Notes
- [不 (char)](../characters/不%20(char).md) "not" + [真 (char)](../characters/真%20(char).md) "genuine, real" — "not genuine."

不真 is the plain negation of [[真]]: not real, not genuine, not authentic. It is a Dan'a'yo coinage formed on the template of 不 + stative, as in [[不安]] and [[不満]]; Wiktionary has no entry for it in any of the daughter languages, so the Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese fields are compositional readings of the two characters, not attested words. The Korean field follows the usual rule that 不 reads 부 before ㅈ, hence 부진; `kwin` is false because the Dan'a'yo 諺文 is 볻진.

It sits beside [[贋]] "counterfeit, fake" as the second way to say "not real". The two differ in register and reach. 贋 is the attributive adjective for a made imitation, a forged object; 不真 says only that something fails to be genuine, and so reaches statements, claims and appearances as well as objects (compare [[真実]] "truth" and [[偽]] "false"). The Sophomore List pairs real [[真]] with fake 贋 *or* 不真.
