## Notes
- [原](../characters/原.md) "origin" + [因](../characters/因.md) "cause" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [因](../characters/因.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (原's own stand-in is [[原始]]).

原因 means the reason or cause — the origin of an effect or event.

Japanese げんいん, Korean 원인, and Vietnamese nguyên nhân are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching Dan'a'yo's own reading exactly (`kwin: true`). Filled blank `pos: 名詞`; fixed empty-list `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ⼔ㄋㄧㄋ unique across both words and characters).
