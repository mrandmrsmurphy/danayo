## Notes
- [規](../characters/規.md) "rule; compass" + [律](../characters/律.md) "statute; regulation"

規律 means a rule that gives order: a regulation, and the discipline or regularity it produces. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 規律 (Mandarin guīlǜ, Cantonese kwai1 leot6) as "rule; law; precept; regular pattern; regularity; rhythm; routine", Japanese きりつ as "discipline, order" and "regulation, rule", and Korean 규율 as "regulations or rules that enable discipline or order" (as in army discipline). Vietnamese quy luật is "rule, law". The shared core is a rule that sets order; Chinese leans toward a regular pattern, and Japanese and Korean toward discipline.

This word is the viable form of [規](../characters/規.md): the character means a rule or compass, but is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo, so 規律 is its `stand_in` compound. The plain rule is a [[規則]], and a rule of conduct is a [[規範]].
