## Notes
- [混](../characters/混.md) "mix, confuse, blend, bumble" + [乱](../characters/乱.md) "chaotic, confused, riotous" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 乱 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (混's own stand-in is [[混合]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

混乱 means chaotic, confused, riotous.

Mandarin hùnluàn, Japanese こんらん, and Korean 혼란 (retaining the initial liquid per North Korean/문화어 convention) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 混's and 乱's own stored citations exactly. Fixed a doubled/redundant `mandarin` value (hùnluàn; hùnluàn → hùnluàn). Filled the blank `cantonese` (wan6 lyun6) and `vietnamese` fields — hỗn loạn (混's own citation hỗn + 乱's own loạn) is also the real, standard Vietnamese term for "chaos, disorder." Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄛㄋㄌㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
