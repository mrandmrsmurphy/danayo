## Notes
- [押](../characters/押%20(char).md) "mortgage" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [押](../characters/押%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

押 means to mortgage or pawn.

Mandarin yā, Cantonese aat3, Japanese おう, Korean 압, and Vietnamese áp are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `羅馬字` was missing the leading glottal marker (ab→'ab, per `characters/押 (char).md`'s own citation) and `vietnamese` was a literal null. Homophone of [[鴨]] "duck" (both 注音 ㄚㄆ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words.
