## Notes
- [新 (char)](../characters/新%20(char).md) "new" + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "moon"

新月 is the new moon: the moon at the start of its cycle, when it shows no face or only a thin crescent. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xīnyuè as "new moon; first crescent moon" and Japanese しんげつ as "new moon". Vietnamese tân nguyệt is the same word, and Korean 신월 and Cantonese san1 jyut6 match.

It is the start of the month in the lunar calendar, where the full moon is the [満月](満月.md) and the half moon is the [半月](半月.md). The moon itself is the [太陰](太陰.md).
