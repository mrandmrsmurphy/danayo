## Notes
- Reduplication of <ruby>[父](../characters/父.md)<rt>ㄅㄨ</rt></ruby> "father" — an ideophonic/diminutive derivation (like the doubled forms found across many languages for parent-address terms), not a literal "father-father."

Mandarin bàba, Cantonese baa1 baa4, Japanese パパ (papa), Korean 아빠 (appa), and Vietnamese bố/ba are each the real, standard informal word for "daddy" in their own language — independently reduplicated or borrowed in each, rather than sharing a single compositional Sino-Xenic form.
