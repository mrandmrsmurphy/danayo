## Notes
- Reduplication of <ruby>[父](../characters/父.md)<rt>ㄅㄨ</rt></ruby> "father" — an ideophonic/diminutive derivation (like the doubled forms found across many languages for parent-address terms), not a literal "father-father."

Mandarin bàba, Cantonese baa1 baa4, Japanese パパ (papa), Korean 아빠 (appa), and Vietnamese bố/ba are each the real, standard informal word for "daddy" in their own language — independently reduplicated or borrowed in each, rather than sharing a single compositional Sino-Xenic form. **Found and fixed a real bug**: `mandarin` and `cantonese` were stored as the bare Chinese characters "爸爸" rather than romanized readings, inconsistent with every other word's frontmatter format; corrected to bàba and baa1 baa4.

`kwin` is false: there is no real Sino-Korean compound "부부" meaning "daddy" — Dan'a'yo's own 부부 has no genuine Sino-Korean counterpart to match against (real Korean 부부/夫婦 "married couple" happens to be spelled identically, but this is a coincidence in real-world Korean, unrelated to this word's own vault entry). No vault-internal homophones (注音 ㄅㄨㄅㄨ unique among both words and characters; [[夫婦]] is spelled differently in Dan'a'yo, 注音 ㄅㄨㄅ⼜).
