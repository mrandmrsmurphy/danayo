## Notes
- [古](../characters/古.md) "ancient" + [風](../characters/風%20(char).md) "style, manner" — no stand-in relationship applies (古's own stand-in is [[古代]]; 風 is its own stand-in).

古風 means antique or old-fashioned in style — an elegant classical manner or aesthetic.

Japanese こふう, Korean 고풍, and Vietnamese cổ phong are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `aliases` incorrectly listed 古雅 (a distinct compound built from 雅 "elegant," not 風, with no separate word page of its own) as if it were an orthographic variant — removed, keeping only the genuine simplified form 古风. Also removed duplicated Notes content (the page had the same etymology line twice, once under a stray bullet and once under a non-standard `## Etymology` heading). No homophones (注音 ㄍㄛㄈㄨㄫ unique across both words and characters).
