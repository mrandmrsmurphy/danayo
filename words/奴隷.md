## Notes
- [奴](../characters/奴.md) "slave" + [隷](../characters/隷.md) "slave" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes both [奴](../characters/奴.md) and [隷](../characters/隷.md) as independent Dan'a'yo entries.

奴隷 means slave — a near-synonymous doubling of two characters both meaning "slave, servant."

Mandarin núlì, Cantonese nou4 dai6, Japanese どれい, Korean 노례, and Vietnamese nô lệ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Omitted blank `swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄛㄌㄝㄧ unique across both words and characters).
