## Notes
- [克 (char)](../characters/克%20(char).md) "practice self-restraint; overcome (stand-in: itself)" + [服](../characters/服.md) "serve (stand-in: [[服事]])"

Wiktionary: Chinese 克服 "to overcome; to conquer; to surmount" and "to put up with"; Japanese 克服 "conquest, overcoming" and "to overcome"; Korean 극복 is the hanja form. It is the success side of [[困難]], the difficulty to be overcome.
