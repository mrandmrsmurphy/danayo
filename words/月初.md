## Notes
- [月](../characters/月%20(char).md) "moon; month" + [初](../characters/初.md) "beginning" — no stand-in relationship applies (月's own stand-in is itself; 初's own is [[最初]]).

月初 means the beginning of the month.

Mandarin yuèchū, Japanese げっしょ, and Korean 월초 are all standard, fully compositional readings.
