## Notes
- [容 (char)](../characters/容.md) "allow, contain" (stand-in: itself) + [認](../words/認.md) "recognize" (stand-in: [[認識]]) — to accept, acknowledge as permissible.

容認 means to accept, as invoked by `文法 - 02音韻論`'s 容認異音 ("an accepted/acceptable allophone variant"). Japanese ようにん and Korean 용인 are both real, standard terms, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Cantonese jung4 jing6 is a straightforward compositional reading. Mandarin róngrèn and Vietnamese dong nhận are compositional only. `kwin` is false: both 容 and 認 are individually false.

No syllable-boundary dot is needed: the concatenated string `⼄ㄫㄋㄧㄋ` admits only one legal partition.
