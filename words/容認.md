## Notes
- [容 (char)](../characters/容%20(char).md) "allow, contain" (stand-in: itself) + [認](../characters/認.md) "recognize" (stand-in: [[認識]]) — to accept, acknowledge as permissible.

Wiktionary lists 容認 only under Japanese: "acceptance, permission, toleration"; as a verb, "accept, permit, approve, tolerate". Used in `文法 - 02音韻論` for 容認異音, an acceptable allophone variant.
