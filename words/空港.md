## Notes
- [空 (char)](../characters/空%20(char).md) "empty" + [港](../characters/港.md) "harbor" — literally "sky-harbor." Neither character's own `stand_in` points here (空's own points to bare `空`, 港's own to `港湾`); 空港 is an independent compound.

The Chinese synonyms 飛機場 and 機場 are unrelated to this word: they are written with different characters (機 "machine" + 場 "field", not 空 "sky" + 港 "harbor") and are not alternate spellings of 空港. The compound corresponds most directly to Japanese くうこう (kūkō) and Korean 공항, while Mandarin's everyday word for "airport" is 机场/飛機場 (jīchǎng), a separate compound not yet given its own page here. Cantonese hung1 gong2 matches 港's own gong2. Vietnamese sân bay is a native construction rather than a Sino-Vietnamese calque, but is the correct, standard everyday term for "airport."
