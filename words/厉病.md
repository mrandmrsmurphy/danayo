## Notes
- [厉 (char)](../characters/厉%20(char).md) "whetstone" — here purely a sound-loan for 癩/疠 ("leprosy"), one of two unrelated 借代字 senses documented on 厉's own page (the other being 蛎/蠣 "oyster," in [[牡厉]]) + [病](../characters/病.md) "sickness" — literally "leprosy-sickness."

厉病 (properly 癩病/癘病, of which this is the vault's phonetic-substitute spelling) names leprosy (Hansen's disease). Unlike most of this vault's phonetic-loan spellings, 厉 itself is not bound here — it's a stand-alone word in its own right (see [[厉]], "whetstone") — so 厉病 is a genuine homograph-by-loan rather than a legitimizing compound for a bound character.

Mandarin làibìng, Cantonese lai6beng6, and Korean 라병 (rabyeong) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 厉's "disease" sense and 病's own stored citations. Japanese らいびょう (raibyō) likewise uses らい, the reading 厉 itself reserves specifically for its disease sense. Vietnamese phong is the real, standard Vietnamese term for leprosy (bệnh phong) — a native word, not a Sino-Vietnamese reading of either constituent character, kept as-is since it's the actually attested everyday term. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 러병 diverges from Sino-Korean 라병 in the first syllable. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄌㄝㄅ⼶ㄫ unique among both words and characters).
