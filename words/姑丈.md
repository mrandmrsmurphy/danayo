## Notes
- [姑](../characters/姑.md) "paternal aunt" + [丈](../characters/丈%20(char).md) "husband" — no stand-in relationship applies (姑's own stand-in is [[姑母]]; 丈's own is itself).

姑丈 means one's father's sister's husband — an uncle by marriage on the paternal aunt's side.

Mandarin gūzhàng, Cantonese gu1 zoeng6-2, and Korean 고장 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese こじょう is a compositional literary reading (using 丈's JOU on'yomi, paralleling [[丈人]]'s じょうじん); everyday spoken Japanese would instead describe this relationship via 伯父/叔父 (おじ). Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄍㄛㄐㄚㄫ/gojang/고장) with the unrelated [[故障]] ("malfunction") — a coincidence that also holds at the real-Korean level (both read 고장), though the two words diverge in Mandarin/Cantonese/Japanese.
