## Notes
- [鳥 (char)](../characters/鳥%20(char).md) "bird" + [啄 (char)](../characters/啄%20(char).md) "peck"

**鳥啄** is the act of a bird pecking. As a calligraphy term it is the figurative name of the seventh of the eight principles of the 永字八法, the left-falling stroke ([[撇]], classically 啄, which in modern terms is the short 撇): the short, quick stroke is imagined as a bird's peck.

Li Puguang (李溥光), writing in the Yuan dynasty in his 永字八法解, gave each of the eight strokes of the 永字八法 a figurative two-character name; 鳥啄 is the left-falling stroke's name. See [[怪石]] for the full set, and the [[Stroke]] page.

The Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and North Korean forms are the ordinary compound readings of the word. No Vietnamese form is given.
