## Notes
- [妊](../characters/妊%20(char).md) "be pregnant, conceive" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

妊 means to be pregnant, or to conceive — the state of carrying a child, as in [[妊娠]] ("pregnancy").

Mandarin rèn, Cantonese jam4, Japanese にん, Korean 임, and Vietnamese nhâm are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄋㄧㄇ/nim/님) with the unrelated [[賃]] ("rent").
