## Notes
- [交 (char)](../characters/交%20(char).md) "intersect; mix" + [響](../characters/響.md) "echo; resound" + [曲](../characters/曲.md) "tune"

交響曲 is a symphony: a long work for a full orchestra, usually in several movements. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin jiāoxiǎngqǔ as "symphony" and Japanese こうきょうきょく as "symphony". Korean 교향곡 is the same word. Cantonese gaau1 hoeng2 kuk1 matches.

It is played by the [管弦楽](管弦楽.md), orchestra. The work with a solo instrument is the [協奏曲](協奏曲.md), concerto.
