## Etymology
- [違](../characters/違.md) "violate, disobey" + [法 (char)](../characters/法%20(char).md) "rule, law"

## Notes
Mandarin wéifǎ, cantonese wai4 faat3, japanese いほう, and korean 위법 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese vi pháp is the honest compositional reading (違's own vi + 法's own pháp); modern Vietnamese instead uses the different (though related) compound "vi phạm," but no independently attested loanword using this exact character pairing was found.

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 違's `stand_in` (違反) nor 法's own `stand_in` (法 itself) points to this compound.
