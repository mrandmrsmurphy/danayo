## Notes
- [倍 (char)](../characters/倍%20(char).md) "times"

倍 is 形声: semantic [[人]] ("person") + phonetic [[咅]]. An obsolete sense "to turn one's back on, betray" survives as an alternate form of [[背]] ("the back"), sharing the same phonetic element and body-part imagery; the modern "times, -fold" sense plausibly extends from "back-to-back" — a second, opposing counterpart, then a duplicate, then multiplicative "-fold" — though this semantic bridge is not explicitly attested. As a measure word, 倍 counts multiples: 三倍 ("three times, threefold"), 加倍 ("to double").

Mandarin bèi, Cantonese pui5, and Japanese ばい (bai, on'yomi, fully productive: 二倍 にばい "double") all preserve the sense. Korean 배 (bae) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 곱 (gop, "-fold, multiple," the everyday native counting word). Vietnamese bội is the Hán Việt reading, attested in gấp bội ("manifold") and bội số ("a multiple," in mathematics) — the character page's other five stored candidates (buạ, bạu, bậu, bụa, vội) are the same kind of corpus-noise pattern found repeatedly in this sweep.

This word is part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read bai/배/ㄅㄚㄧ): [[唄]] ("ugh") and [[牌]] ("playing card"), both still awaiting their own turn in this sweep — the reciprocal half of this callout will be completed on each when it comes up.
