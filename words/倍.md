## Notes
- [倍 (char)](../characters/倍%20(char).md) "times"

倍 is 形声: semantic [[人]] ("person") + phonetic [[咅]]. An obsolete sense "to turn one's back on, betray" survives as an alternate form of [[背]] ("the back"), sharing the same phonetic element and body-part imagery; the modern "times, -fold" sense plausibly extends from "back-to-back" — a second, opposing counterpart, then a duplicate, then multiplicative "-fold" — though this semantic bridge is not explicitly attested. 倍 is a plain noun (名詞), not a measure word (量詞): it counts nothing, but multiplies a degree, as in 三倍 聡明 ("three times smarter", illustrative), 加倍 ("to double"). Like a unit word it takes its numeral directly on its left, but it is not a unit of measure and is not on [[List of 単位詞]].

Mandarin bèi, Cantonese pui5, and Japanese ばい (bai, on'yomi, fully productive: 二倍 にばい "double") all preserve the sense. Korean 배 (bae) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 곱 (gop, "-fold, multiple," the everyday native counting word).

Several character-only matches (敗, 貝, 罷, 稗, 狽) share the same 注音 but have no independent word pages of their own, so are not real word-level homophone collisions.

Legitimizing note: 倍 (char)'s own `stand_in` is this exact word (itself), so 倍 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by itself.
