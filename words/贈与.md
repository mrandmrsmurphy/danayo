## Notes
- [贈](../characters/贈.md) "present, bestow" + [与 (char)](../characters/与%20(char).md) "and" (here in its older sense "give, grant") — not `#cranberry`: 贈's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 与's own is [[与]] itself, so transitivity fails; 贈 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry by this word.

贈与 means to give or bestow: to transfer something to another person without payment (贈与稅, "gift tax"; 贈与契約, "deed of gift"; 生前贈与, "gift during one's lifetime"; 贈与者, "donor"). It is the formal and legal word for a gift, with a more everyday word for "give as a present" in each language (Japanese 贈る, おくる). The variants 赠与, 贈與 and 贈予 are listed as aliases.

Mandarin zèngyǔ, Cantonese zang6 jyu5, Japanese ぞうよ (zōyo), Korean 증여 (jeungyeo, a real attested word), and Vietnamese tặng dữ (the real Sino-Vietnamese compound) are the standard readings.
