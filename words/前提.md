## Notes
- [前 (char)](../characters/前%20(char).md) "before" + [提](../characters/提.md) "produce" — "prerequisite, premise" (前提条件, "precondition"). Mandarin qiántí, Cantonese cin4 tai4, Japanese ぜんてい, Korean 전제, and Vietnamese tiền đề are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 전서 diverges from Sino-Korean 전제 in the second syllable.
