## Notes
- [膿](../characters/膿%20(char).md) "pus" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 膿 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

膿 means pus.

Mandarin nóng, cantonese nung4, and korean 농 (nong) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading う (u) rather than the on-readings ドウ/ノウ (DOU/NOU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese nung is one of two stored candidates. No homophones.
