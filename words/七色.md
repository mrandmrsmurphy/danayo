## Notes
- [七 (char)](../characters/七%20(char).md) "seven" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — no `#cranberry`: 色's own `stand_in` is [[色彩]], 七's is 七 itself; neither points here.
- The seven colors of the visible spectrum, equivalent to the English mnemonic ROYGBIV (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet).

Japanese なないろ (native "seven colors," avoiding on'yomi しちしょく) is the real, everyday Japanese term for the rainbow's seven colors, matching the established real-attested-usage convention. Mandarin qīsè, Cantonese cat1 sik1, Korean 칠색, and Sino-Vietnamese thất sắc are standard compositional readings. `kwin` is false (AND-rule: both 七 and 色 individually false).
