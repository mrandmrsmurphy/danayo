## Notes
- [鬼](../characters/鬼.md) "devil, demon" + [老 (char)](../characters/老%20(char).md) "old" — no `#cranberry`: 鬼's own `stand_in` is [[鬼神]], 老's is 老 itself; neither points here.

While the literal, compositional gloss is "male demon," this word's alias 鬼佬 (gwai2lou2) is the well-known Cantonese colloquial term for a Western/white foreigner — an extremely common, if informal and sometimes considered impolite, usage far more prevalent than the literal demon sense. Mandarin, Japanese, Korean, and Vietnamese fields here are compositional readings; the slang "foreigner" sense is specifically a Cantonese regionalism.
