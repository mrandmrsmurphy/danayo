## Notes
- [繞](../characters/繞%20(char).md) "entwine, surround, enclose" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 繞 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

繞 means to entwine, surround, or enclose — to wind or wrap around.

Mandarin rào, Cantonese jiu5, and Korean 요 (yo) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading まとう (matou, "to wrap around") rather than the on-readings ジョウ/ニョウ (JOU/NYOU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese nhiễu is the standard reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
