## Notes
- [子](../characters/子.md) "child, son" reduplicated — an affectionate, intimate way of referring to one's own child, distinct from the general word for "child" already covered by [[孩子]] (which has its own separate Dan'a'yo derivation, haijǝ/해즈, and its own dedicated character 孩). This reduplicated word has no real-world reading of its own: [[孩子]] (háizi) is a separate, unrelated word, so it is not an alias, and こ/애 are single-character glosses rather than readings.
