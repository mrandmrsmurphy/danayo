## Notes
- [独](../characters/独.md) "alone" + [奏](../characters/奏.md) "play (music)"

独奏 is to play alone, or an instrumental solo. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin dúzòu as "to perform a solo" and Japanese どくそう as "musical solo". Korean 독주 and Vietnamese độc tấu are the same word. Cantonese duk6 zau3 matches.

It is the single instrument set against the [合奏](合奏.md), ensemble, and the general word for playing is [演奏](演奏.md).
