## Notes
- [大](../characters/大%20(char).md) "great" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study" + [校](../characters/校.md) "school" — no stand-in relationship applies (大 is its own stand-in; 学's own is [[学習]]; 校's is [[学校]]).

大学校 means university or college; commonly abbreviated to [[大学]] in everyday usage.

Japanese だいがっこう (with sokuon assimilation), Korean 대학교, Mandarin dàxuéxiào, and Cantonese daai6 hok6 haau6 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese `trường đại học` is the standard everyday term (native "trường" + "đại học," rather than a fully Sino-compositional calque). No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄧㄏㄚㄎㄏ⼘ㄨ unique across both words and characters).
