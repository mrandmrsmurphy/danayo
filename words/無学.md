## Notes
- [無 (char)](../characters/無%20(char).md) "lack" + [学](../characters/学.md) "learn"

無学 means unlearned: without schooling or learning. It is literally "without study". Japanese むがく and Korean 무학 are the same compound. Wiktionary has no entry for it under 無學, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings.

It is the opposite of [学問](学問.md), scholarship. It differs from [無知](無知.md), ignorance, because it concerns a lack of schooling, not a lack of knowledge.
