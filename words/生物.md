## Notes
- [生](characters/生.md) "life" + [物](characters/物%20(char).md) "thing" — no stand-in relationship applies (生's own stand-in is [[生活]]; 物's own is itself).

生物 means living being, organism.

Mandarin shēngwù, Cantonese sang1 mat6, Japanese せいぶつ (SEI+BUTSU), and Vietnamese sinh vật were already correct and fully compositional. Fixed a real bug in `korean`: 생믈 → 생물, matching 物's own stored citation (물). Removed the self-referential alias 生物 (the word's own title, not a genuine alternate form) and fixed `hsk_level` formatting (unquoted 2 → quoted "2"). No homophones (注音 ㄙㄚㄫㄇㄨㄊ unique; [[生物学]] merely shares the prefix as a longer compound).
