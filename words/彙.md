## Notes
- [彙](../characters/彙%20(char).md) "category, class" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

彙 means category or class — a grouping of similar things, as in 語彙 ("vocabulary, lexicon").

Mandarin huì, Cantonese wui6, Korean 휘, Japanese い, and Vietnamese vị are all standard, fully compositional readings.
