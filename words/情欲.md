## Notes
- [情](../characters/情.md) "feelings, emotion" + [欲](../characters/欲.md) "desire, want" — desire that arises from feeling; sexual desire

Wiktionary: Japanese 情欲 (じょうよく) "lust, sexual desire"; Chinese 情慾 "lust; sexual desire", kept as an alias here; Korean 정욕 is the hanja form of 情慾, "lust, sexual desire"; Vietnamese tình dục is the Han form, "sex (act of sexual intercourse)". Compare [[欲望]], [[強欲]].
