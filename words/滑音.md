## Notes
- [滑 (char)](../characters/滑%20(char).md) "slippery, to slide" + [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" — no stand-in relationship applies (滑 is its own stand-in; 音's is [[音楽]]).

Wiktionary lists 滑音 only for Chinese, in the musical sense: "glissando; portamento; sliding tone". It has no entry for the phonetic sense, which is the Dan'a'yo one: the glide, a sound that slides into or out of a vowel, here ㄧ /j/ and ㄨ /w/. The on-glide is the [[介音]] and the off-glide is the 後続滑音 of `文法 - 02音韻論`; 半母音 "semivowel" is an alternative term.
