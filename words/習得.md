## Notes
- [習 (char)](../characters/習.md) "practice, study" (stand-in: [[練習]]) + [得 (char)](../characters/得.md) "obtain" (stand-in: [[獲得]]) — acquisition, mastery, as in language acquisition.

習得 means acquisition/mastery, as invoked by `文法 - 02音韻論`'s 習得困難 ("difficult to acquire/master," of new tones). Mandarin xídé, Japanese しゅうとく, and Korean 습득 are all real, standard terms, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Cantonese zaap6 dak1 is a straightforward compositional reading. Vietnamese tập đắc is compositional only. `kwin` is false: both 習 and 得 are individually false.

No syllable-boundary dot is needed: the concatenated string `ㄙㄜㄆㄊㄜㄎ` admits only one legal partition.
