## Notes
- [習 (char)](../characters/習.md) "practice, study" (stand-in: [[練習]]) + [得 (char)](../characters/得.md) "obtain" (stand-in: [[獲得]]) — acquisition, mastery, as in language acquisition.

Wiktionary: Chinese "to acquire (a skill); to learn (a subject); acquisition; learning"; Japanese 習得 (しゅうとく) "acquisition of a skill; learning of a subject", and as a verb "to acquire a skill; to learn a subject"; Korean 습득 is its hanja form. Used in `文法 - 02音韻論` in 習得困難.
