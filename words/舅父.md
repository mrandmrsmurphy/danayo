## Notes
- [舅](../characters/舅.md) "mother's brother" + [父](../characters/父.md) "father" — maternal uncle.

舅父 is the maternal uncle: the brother of one's mother. It is "maternal uncle-father." Mandarin jiùfù and Cantonese kau5 fu6 are confirmed on Wiktionary as the formal written term; spoken Chinese uses 舅舅 or 舅, and Vietnamese cậu (Nôm 舅) is the same uncle. Japanese and Korean have no matching compound: Japanese 舅 (しゅうと) means a father-in-law, and both languages use their general uncle words, so `japanese` and `korean` are left blank. It completes the uncle set with [[伯父]] and [[叔父]] (father's brothers) and sits beside [[姑母]] and [[姨母]]; his wife is [[舅母]].
