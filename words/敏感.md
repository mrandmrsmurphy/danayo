## Notes
- [敏](../characters/敏%20(char).md) "fast, quick, clever, smart" + [感](../characters/感.md) "feel, sense" — no stand-in relationship applies (敏's own stand-in is itself; 感's own is [[感触]]).

敏感 means sensitive.

Mandarin mǐngǎn, Cantonese man5 gam2, Japanese びんかん, Korean 민감, and Vietnamese mẫn cảm are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄧㄋㄍㄚㄇ unique across both words and characters).
