## Notes
- [越 (char)](../characters/越%20(char).md) "Yue, exceed" + [南](../characters/南.md) "south" — "Vietnam." Mandarin Yuènán, Cantonese jyut6 naam4, and Vietnamese Việt Nam are all directly attested standard readings. Japanese and Korean each have real, coexisting names: the older Sino-xenic compound (えつなん/월남, built from these same characters, 월남 also appearing as the North Korean/문화어-orthography variant 윁남), and the modern phonetic transliteration of "Việt Nam" itself (ベトナム/베트남, with 벳남 as an alternate transliteration), which has become the more common form in everyday use in both languages. See also [Vietnam](../lexipedia/Vietnam.md) and [[越南人]] ("Vietnamese person").
