## Notes
- [騒](../characters/騒%20(char).md) "boisterous" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [騒](../characters/騒%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

騒 means boisterous or noisy commotion (as in 騒動, "disturbance," and 騒音, "noise").

Mandarin sāo, Cantonese sou1, Japanese そう, and Korean 소 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese tao (as in 騷擾 tao nhiễu, "to harass, disturb") is the well-attested standard reading, though notably absent from the character's own stored `vietnamese` field (currently blank — a gap worth a future character-level check, not fixed here). Homophone of [[掻]] "scratch" (both 注音 ㄙㄚㄨ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words.
