## Notes
- [騒](../characters/騒%20(char).md) "boisterous" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [騒](../characters/騒%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

騒 means boisterous or noisy commotion (as in 騒動, "disturbance," and 騒音, "noise").

Mandarin sāo, Cantonese sou1, Japanese そう, Korean 소, and Sino-Vietnamese tao (as in 騷擾 tao nhiễu, "to harass, disturb") are all standard, fully compositional readings — **filled the character page's own previously-blank `vietnamese` field** with tao to close this gap. Homophone of [[掻]] "scratch" (both 注音 ㄙㄚㄨ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words, confirmed via 掻's own `stand_in` pointing to itself.
