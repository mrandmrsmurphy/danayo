## Notes
- [偽 (char)](../characters/偽%20(char).md) "false; artificial"

偽 (alternate forms 僞, simplified 伪) combines [[人]] ("person") with phonetic [[為]]. Its core sense is "man-made, artificial" — literally "what a person makes" as opposed to what occurs naturally — extending to "false, fake, counterfeit" ([[偽善]], "hypocrisy," literally "false goodness"; 偽造, "to forge, counterfeit"; 偽裝, "camouflage, disguise").

Mandarin wěi, Cantonese ngai6, and Japanese ぎ/か (gi/ka, on'yomi, as in 偽造 ぎぞう "forgery") alongside native いつわ(る) (itsuwa(ru), "to lie, deceive") all preserve the sense. Korean 위 (wi) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 거짓 (geojit, "a lie, falsehood," the everyday word). Vietnamese nguỵ is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in nguỵ trang ("camouflage, disguise") and nguỵ biện ("sophistry, a fallacious argument").

This word shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading ('wei/웨/⼔ㄧ) with another word still awaiting its own turn in this sweep, [[委]] ("appoint") — the reciprocal half of this callout will be completed when it comes up.
