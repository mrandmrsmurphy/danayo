## Notes
- [翌 (char)](../characters/翌%20(char).md) "the following" + [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both are stand-ins for themselves), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 年 is individually `kwin: false`.

翌年 means the following year.

Mandarin yìnián, Cantonese jik6 nin4, Japanese よくねん (yokunen), and Korean 익년 (ingnyeon) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 翌年 is itself a common attested word for "the following year" in all four languages. Vietnamese dực niên is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
