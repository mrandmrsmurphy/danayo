## Notes
- [露 (char)](../characters/露%20(char).md) "dew" (transliteration character) + [語](../characters/語.md) "language" — no `#cranberry`: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here (露's is the word [[露]] itself, 語's is [[言語]]). A contraction of [[露斯亜語]], the fuller form.

Following the same convention as [[露国]] and [[露斯亜語]], `mandarin`/`cantonese` hold the real Chinese equivalent term rather than a literal compositional reading of this Japanese-coined transliteration: 俄语/俄語 (Éyǔ, also Èyǔ in a Taiwan-standard variant reading) and ngo4jyu5. Japanese ロシアご (roshiago) and Korean 러시아어 (reosia-eo), both built on the modern loanword ロシア/러시아, are the standard everyday terms for "Russian language" in their languages. Sino-Vietnamese Nga ngữ is likewise real and attested, calqued from 俄語.
