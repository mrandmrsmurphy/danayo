## Notes
- [磚](../characters/磚.md) "brick" + [石 (char)](../characters/石%20(char).md) "stone, rock"
- 磚石 is the stand-in for [磚](../characters/磚.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.

磚石 is a collective noun for bricks and stones taken together: 磚 "brick" + 石 "stone". Wiktionary glosses Mandarin zhuānshí (砖石, 磚石) as "bricks and stones" and files it among Chinese building materials, with Cantonese zyun1 sek6. Japanese せんせき and Korean 전석 are the Sino-Xenic readings of the two characters.

In Dan'a'yo the word names masonry material in general, as placed on the [Buildings](../lexipedia/Buildings.md) page.
