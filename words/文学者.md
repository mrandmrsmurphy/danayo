## Notes
- [文](../characters/文.md) "literature, writing" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study" + [者 (char)](../characters/者%20(char).md) "one who, -er" — "literati, a person of letters." Japanese ぶんがくしゃ is directly attested and standard.

**Real content bugs found and fixed**: `mandarin` (wénxuéjiā), `cantonese` (man4 hok6 gaa1), and `korean` (문학가, crammed together with 문학자) had all been filled with the reading of the ALIAS 文学家 (using 家, "house/specialist," instead of this word's own 者) rather than 文学者's own characters — corrected to 文学者's own compositional readings: wénxuézhě, man4 hok6 ze2, and 문학자 alone. `vietnamese: văn học giả` is the honest compositional reading (文's own văn + 学's own học + 者's own giả), directly paralleling the real term học giả ("scholar"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 문학차 diverges from Sino-Korean 문학자 in the final syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄨㄋㄏㄚㄎㄑㄚ unique among both words and characters).
