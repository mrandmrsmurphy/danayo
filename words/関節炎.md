## Notes
- [関](../characters/関%20(char).md) "gate, joint" + [節](../characters/節%20(char).md) "joint" + [炎](../characters/炎.md) "inflammation" — "joint inflammation," arthritis. Their own `stand_in` values: 関's is itself; 節's is itself; 炎's is [[炎症]]; none points here, so 関節炎 is an independent compound.

関節炎 is arthritis: inflammation of the joints. Mandarin guānjiéyán (關節炎), Cantonese gwaan1 zit3 jim4, Japanese かんせつえん and Korean 관절염 all use it. Compare [[関節]] (joint) and [[病人]] (patient).
