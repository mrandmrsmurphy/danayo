## Notes
- [遼](../characters/遼.md) "distant, far" + [寧](../characters/寧.md) "peaceful, calm" — "peace of the Liao River", adopted in 1929. 寧's own stand-in is [[安寧]]; 遼 is a 名専字 (a character restricted to proper names), and this word is where the vault uses it. 辽 is the alias of 遼, and 宁 and 甯 are aliases of 寧, so the filename uses 遼寧; 辽宁 and the English Wiktionary variant 遼甯 are kept as the word's aliases.

遼寧 (Liaoning) is a province of northeast China on the Liaodong peninsula and the Bohai Sea, whose capital is [[沈陽]] and which includes the port city [[大連]] (from memory, not re-checked). English Wiktionary says the name was adopted in 1929, replacing the former name 奉天 (Fengtian), from 遼 (the Liao River) and 寧 (peace), meaning "eternal peace in the Liao River basin" (the wording 遼河永久安寧 contains both characters).

The Japanese field holds りょうねい (English Wiktionary Japanese). The Korean field holds the North Korean reading 료녕 derived from the character pages; English Wiktionary Korean lists the South Korean form 요녕 as the hanja form, and the usual South Korean name today is 랴오닝 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Liêu Ninh is the Sino-Vietnamese name. Cantonese liu4 ning4 is the Jyutping on English Wiktionary. No homophones (注音 ㄌ⼘ㄨ·ㄋㄝㄫ unique across both words and characters).

Attestation, checked 2026-10-11: English and Chinese Wiktionary (Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese sections) list only the province for this name.
