## Notes
- [銑](../characters/銑.md) "mill, pig iron" + [鉄 (char)](../characters/鉄%20(char).md) "iron"

銑鉄 is pig iron. English Wiktionary has an entry for Japanese 銑鉄 (せんてつ), glossed "pig iron", and glosses the Chinese word 生鐵 (Mandarin shēngtiě, Cantonese saang1 tit3) as "pig iron". The Mandarin, Cantonese and Korean 선철 fields here are built from the readings of the two characters.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [銑](../characters/銑.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
