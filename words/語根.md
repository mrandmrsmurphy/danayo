## Notes
- [語](../characters/語.md) "language" + [根 (char)](../characters/根%20(char).md) "root"

語根 is the root of a word. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 語根 (Mandarin yǔgēn, Cantonese jyu5 gan1) as "root (of a word)", Japanese ごこん as "root (of a word)", and lists Korean 어근 as a hanja form of the same. Compare [語幹](語幹.md), the stem.
