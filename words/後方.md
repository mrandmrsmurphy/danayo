## Notes
- [後 (char)](../characters/後%20(char).md) "back; behind (stand-in: itself)" + [方](../characters/方.md) "direction; side (stand-in: [[方向]])"

Wiktionary: Chinese 後方 "rear" and, in the military, "behind the frontline"; Japanese 後方 "in the rear, behind"; Korean 후방 is the hanja form, "rear; back; behind the frontline"; Vietnamese hậu phương is the Han form, "behind the frontline; rear". Its counterpart is [[前方]].
