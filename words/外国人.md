## Notes
- [外](../characters/外.md) "outside" + [国](../characters/国.md) "nation" + [人](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" — no stand-in relationship applies (外's own stand-in is [[外部]]; 国's is [[国家]]; 人 is its own stand-in).

外国人 means a foreigner.

Japanese がいこくじん, Korean 외국인, and Mandarin/Cantonese are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese `người nước ngoài` is a native calque of the Sino-Vietnamese "ngoại quốc nhân" (verified — the everyday standard term, more common than the formal Sino compound). Filled blank `pos: 名詞`. No homophones (注音 ⺢ㄧㄍㄛㄎㄋㄧㄋ unique across both words and characters).
