## Notes
- [皮](../characters/皮.md) "skin, hide, outer layer" + [革](../characters/革.md) "leather" — both 皮's own stand-in and 革's own stand-in are this exact compound, so transitivity holds (A=B=AB); marked `#cranberry`.

皮革 is the general term for processed animal hide — leather — covering the raw material broadly, from tanning through finished goods (皮革製品, "leather goods"). 皮 alone denotes skin or hide in its natural, unprocessed sense (as in 毛皮 "fur," 樹皮 "bark"), while 革 specifically denotes hide that has been treated or tanned into leather; 皮革 fuses the two into the general commercial/industrial term for the material.

Mandarin pígé, Cantonese pei4 gaak3, Japanese ひかく (hikaku), Korean 피혁 (pihyeok), and Vietnamese bì cách are all fully compositional and used consistently in this industrial/commercial sense across the sphere.
