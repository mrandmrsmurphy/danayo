## Notes
- [婆](../characters/婆.md) "granny" + [羅](../characters/羅.md) "Rome" — a transliteration of the name of the island, so the characters carry no meaning of their own; no stand-in relationship applies (婆's own stand-in is [[婆婆]], 羅's is [[羅馬]]). The alias 婆罗 is the simplified spelling.

婆羅 is Borneo, the large island of Southeast Asia, shared today by Brunei ([[文莱]]), Malaysia and Indonesia. English Wiktionary marks it archaic. Its compounds include 婆羅洲, which Wiktionary glosses as Borneo and gives 加里曼丹 (Kalimantan) as a synonym, quoting Xu Jiyu's *Yinghuan zhilüe* (1848); neither is a word in the vault. 婆羅門 (Brahmin) is a different word. Zhang Xie's *Dongxi yang kao* (1618) equates 文萊 with the 婆羅 country: "Brunei, that is the country of Boluo".

Mandarin Póluó and Cantonese po4 lo4 are as at English Wiktionary. Japanese ボルネオ and Korean 보르네오 are the ordinary names of Borneo (from memory, not re-checked), so `kwin` is false. The `vietnamese` field holds the compositional Hán-Việt reading bà la (each character as its page gives it; not attested for this name).

No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄌㄛ unique across words and characters, checked 2026-10-12). The 注音 needs no syllable-boundary dot.
