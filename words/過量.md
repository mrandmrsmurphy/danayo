## Notes
Mandarin guòliàng, cantonese gwo3 loeng4, japanese かりょう, and vietnamese quá lượng are all honest compositional readings (過's own guò/gwo3/KA/quá + 量's own liàng/loeng4/RYOU/lượng). **Found and fixed a korean reading bug**: the stored value 과령 used the wrong second syllable — 量's own korean is 량, not 령 — corrected to 과량, matching the word's own stored 諺文/羅馬字 (과량/gwalyang) exactly. `过量` is the standard simplified-character spelling variant (過→过), correctly listed as an alias. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 과량 matches the corrected Sino-Korean 과량 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⺢ㄌ⼘ㄫ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 過's `stand_in` (過 itself) nor 量's own `stand_in` (数量) points to this compound.
