## Etymology
- [過 (char)](../characters/過%20(char).md) "than, too" + [量](../characters/量.md) "measure, weight, quantity"

## Notes
Mandarin guòliàng, cantonese gwo3 loeng4, japanese かりょう, and vietnamese quá lượng are all honest compositional readings (過's own guò/gwo3/KA/quá + 量's own liàng/loeng4/RYOU/lượng).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 過's `stand_in` (過 itself) nor 量's own `stand_in` (数量) points to this compound.
