## Notes
- [蓬](../characters/蓬.md) "mugwort" is legitimized by this exact compound (its own `stand_in`). [藁](../characters/藁.md) "straw" is used here as a `代用字` (homophone/orthographic substitute glyph) for the pageless real character 蒿 ("mugwort, wormwood") — 蓬藁 represents 蓬蒿, already listed as its own alias.
