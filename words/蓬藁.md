## Notes
- [蓬](../characters/蓬.md) "mugwort" is legitimized by this exact compound (its own `stand_in`). [藁](../characters/藁.md) "straw" is used here as a `借代字` (homophone/orthographic substitute glyph) for the pageless real character 蒿 ("mugwort, wormwood") — 蓬藁 represents 蓬蒿, already listed as its own alias.

**Found and fixed two real bugs**: `羅馬字`/`諺文` had bonggao/봉 (the latter truncated to a single syllable entirely), neither matching `注音`'s already-correct ㄅㄛㄫㄏㄚㄨ (蓬's own bong + the independently-derived syllable [[syllables/ㄏㄚㄨ|ㄏㄚㄨ]]/hau for 蒿) — corrected to bonghau/봉핫. **Found and fixed a real `kwin` bug** (true→false, AND-rule: 藁's own field is false). Mandarin pénghāo, cantonese pung4hou1, japanese ほうこう, and korean 봉호 all correctly use 蒿's own real reading (hāo/hou1/こう/호) rather than 藁's (gǎo/gou2), confirming the intended referent — no bug there. Filled blank vietnamese (bồng cao, compositional, paralleling the real term 青蒿/thanh cao). No homophones (checked with the corrected reading).
