## Notes
- [不 (char)](../characters/不%20(char).md) "not" + [幸](../characters/幸.md) "fortunate, lucky" — the negation of 幸; "unfortunate, unhappy, unlucky."

Mandarin bùxìng, Cantonese bat1 hang6, Japanese ふこう, and Sino-Vietnamese bất hạnh are all standard compositional readings. Korean 불행 follows the 不→불 assimilation rule already documented on several sibling 不-compounds this stretch. Removed blank `swadesh`/`aliases`.
