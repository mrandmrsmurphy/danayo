## Notes
- [組](../characters/組.md) "organize, form, group up" + [織](../characters/織.md) "weave" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (組's own points to `組合`, 織's own to `編織`); 組織 is an independent compound.

組's reading is jǝ/즈/ㄐㄜ on its character page, and this word, like its sibling [[組合]], uses it: 組織 reads jǝjig/즈직/ㄐㄜㄐㄧㄎ. Mandarin zǔzhī, Cantonese zou2 zik1, Japanese そしき, Korean 조직, and Vietnamese tổ chức all converge on "to organize; an organization." The alias 组织 is the simplified spelling.
