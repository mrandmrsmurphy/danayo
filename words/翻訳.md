## Notes
- [翻 (char)](../characters/翻%20(char).md) "flip" + [訳](../characters/訳.md) "translate, decode" — 訳's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 翻's own is [[翻]] itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 翻訳 legitimizes 訳 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 翻 is individually `kwin: false`.

翻訳 means to translate.

Mandarin fānyì, Cantonese faan1 jik6, Japanese ほんやく (hon'yaku), and Korean 번역 (beonyeok) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 翻訳 is itself a common attested word for "translate" in all four languages. Vietnamese phiên dịch is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term. **Fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字`/`諺文` had pon'yeg/폰역 (a p-initial), mismatching 翻's real f-initial reading (fon/뽄) already fixed in the previous iteration — 注音 (ㄈㄛㄋ⼶ㄎ) had stayed correct. Filled blank cantonese. No homophones.
