## Notes
- [地](../characters/地%20(char).md) "ground" + [理](../characters/理.md) "principle, order" — no stand-in relationship applies (地 is its own stand-in; 理's own is [[理由]]).

地理 means geography or topography.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `japanese` had been contaminated with the sibling word [[地理学]]'s reading (ちりがく, which includes 学's GAKU) instead of this word's own bare reading — corrected to ちり. Korean 지리 and Vietnamese địa lý are both standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄧㄜㄌㄧ unique across both words and characters).
