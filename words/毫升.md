## Notes
- [毫](../characters/毫%20(char).md) "milli-, fine hair" + [升](../characters/升%20(char).md) "litre" — "milli-litre," a thousandth of a litre, built like [[毫米]] (millimetre) from the same 毫 "milli-" prefix. Both characters are their own `stand_in`, so 毫升 is an independent compound.

毫升 is the millilitre. Mandarin háoshēng and Cantonese hou4 sing1 are the ordinary Chinese words. Japanese writes it ミリリットル and Korean 밀리리터, so the `japanese` and `korean` fields hold the readings of the characters. It is a unit word and takes a numeral directly (五百毫升). The litre itself is [[升]] and a hundred litres is [[百升]].
