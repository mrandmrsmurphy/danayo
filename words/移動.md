## Notes
- [移](../characters/移.md) "change" + [動 (char)](../characters/動%20(char).md) "move" — 移's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 動's own is [[動]] itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 移 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 移 is individually `kwin: false`.

**Fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字`/`諺文` had 'yedong/여동, missing/altering 移's own final -i (`注音` ⼶ㄧㄉㄛㄫ was already correct, confirmed against `syllables/⼶ㄧ.md`). Corrected to 'yeidong/예동.

移動 means mobile or moveable — capable of shifting or being relocated, as in 移動電話 ("mobile phone," an older, more formal Mandarin term than the now-standard 手機) or 移動式 ("mobile, portable, as a modifier"). It combines 移's sense of gradual shift with 動's more general "to move."

Mandarin yídòng, Cantonese ji4 dung6, Japanese いどう (idō), and Korean 이동 (idong) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese di động is likewise compositional and is itself an everyday word, most familiarly in điện thoại di động ("mobile phone").
