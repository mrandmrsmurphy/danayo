## Notes
- [二 (char)](../characters/二%20(char).md) "two" + [度](../characters/度.md) "time, occurrence" — literally "two times," twice, a specific count distinct from [[再度]] ("again, once more"), which names repetition without specifying a count.

Japanese にど (nido) is a real, everyday living word for "twice." Mandarin èrdù, Cantonese ji6 dou6, Korean 이도, and Vietnamese nhị độ are compositional; modern colloquial usage in these languages more often prefers a native or numeral-plus-counter construction (Mandarin 两次 liǎngcì, Korean 두 번 du beon) over this Sino compound for everyday speech, though the compositional form remains understandable and analogous to how 一度/三度 etc. extend the same pattern.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 늬도 diverges from Sino-Korean 이도 in the first syllable, since 二 independently diverges from Korean 이 on its own character page. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄧㄜㄉㄛ unique among both words and characters). See [Numbers](../lexipedia/Numbers.md).
