## Notes
- [肉 (char)](../characters/肉%20(char).md) "meat" — stand-in for [[肉 (char)|肉]], which cannot appear independently

肉 is 象形 of an animal's ribs, or alternatively a slice of meat — the same shape stylized into 月 (the "meat radical," graphically identical to but semantically unrelated to the "moon" 月 this vault covered earlier in this sweep) whenever it sits beside a phonetic component, as in [[育 (char)|育]] and 祭. Beyond the plain substance, the compound family spans culinary vocabulary (鶏肉, 豚肉, 狗肉 — the last one an outright insult in some CJKV contexts despite being a real cuisine elsewhere) and figurative extensions built on flesh-as-kinship (骨肉 "flesh and bone," 骨肉相連 echoing the same "bone of my bone" idiom found in Biblical language) and flesh-as-vulnerability (弱肉強食 "survival of the fittest," literally "weak flesh, strong eats"). 苦肉 ("desperate self-sacrifice," from the idiom of a general injuring his own flesh to fool the enemy) and 道成肉身 ("the Word became flesh," this vault's own theological compound) both extend the character into strategic and doctrinal registers respectively.

Japanese carries two on'yomi, にく (NIKU, the overwhelmingly dominant reading — 焼肉 yakiniku, 筋肉 kinniku "muscle," 肉 niku standing alone as the everyday word for meat) and rarer じゅう (JUU, confined to a small set of specialized compounds), with no separate native 訓読み at all — にく itself functions as the ordinary everyday word, an unusual case in this sweep of a Sino-Japanese reading fully absorbed into daily vocabulary rather than staying in a technical register. Vietnamese nhục survives mainly in a more classical/formal register (nhục thể "the flesh, the body," in a philosophical/religious sense) rather than everyday speech, which instead uses the native thịt for ordinary "meat." `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 눅 (nug, from Middle Chinese 日母 ȵ + 屋三韻 ɨuk) diverges from Sino-Korean 육, which has lost the nasal onset entirely — the same 日母-onset-loss pattern already seen on this vault's other 日母 characters, including [[日]] itself earlier in this sweep. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄨㄎ shared only by 衂, a `名専字`-restricted character with no independent standing).
