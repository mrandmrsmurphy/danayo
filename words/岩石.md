## Notes
- [岩](../characters/岩.md) "rock; crag; cliff" + [石 (char)](../characters/石%20(char).md) "stone" — this word is the stand-in for [岩](../characters/岩.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

岩石 is the general geological/scientific term for rock — 岩 leans toward large rock formations and cliff faces, 石 toward stone generally (including small pebbles, as in 石頭); together they name rock as a category, more academic in register than the everyday 石頭/石 alone.

Japanese がんせき, Korean 암석, and Vietnamese nham thạch (verified) are all standard, non-drifted scientific-register readings. No HSK level is given for the compound. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄇㄙㄝㄎ unique across both words and characters).
