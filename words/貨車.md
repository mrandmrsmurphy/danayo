## Notes
- [貨](../characters/貨.md) "goods, cargo" + [車](../characters/車%20(char).md) "cart, car" — "goods vehicle." Neither character's own `stand_in` points here (貨's is [[貨物]]; 車's is itself), so 貨車 is an independent compound.

貨車 is the Dan'a'yo word for a truck: a motor vehicle that carries cargo. It is attested with the same characters in Chinese (Mandarin huòchē, Cantonese fo3 ce1, where it covers both "truck" and a railway "goods wagon"), Japanese (かしゃ, now chiefly a railway freight car, with the truck itself usually the loanword トラック) and Korean (화차, a hanja form for a freight car). Dan'a'yo takes the widest of these senses, a goods-carrying vehicle, and uses it for the road truck.

It is an exact homophone of [[火車]] (ㄏ⺢ㄑ⺢), which Dan'a'yo reserves for the Joseon-era rocket-cart weapon; the two are never in competition, since one carries goods and the other fire. A passenger train is [[列車]].
