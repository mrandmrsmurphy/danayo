## Notes
- [応](../characters/応%20(char).md) "respond, reply" + [訊](../characters/訊.md) "question, interrogate" — no stand-in relationship applies (応's own stand-in is itself; 訊's own is [[審訊]]).

応訊 means to be questioned or subpoenaed — responding to a formal interrogation or legal summons.

Mandarin yìngxùn, Cantonese jing3 seon3, Japanese おうじん, Korean 응신, and Vietnamese ứng tấn are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `cantonese` had the Mandarin pinyin string duplicated in place of jyutping — corrected to compositional jing3 seon3. Filled blank `japanese`/`korean`/`vietnamese`, added missing `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄧㄫㄙㄧㄫ unique across both words and characters).
