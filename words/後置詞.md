## Notes
- [後](../characters/後%20(char).md) "after" + [置](../characters/置%20(char).md) "put, place" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "word, expression" — no stand-in relationship applies (後/置 are both their own stand-ins; 詞's own is [[単詞]]).

後置詞 means postposition — a grammatical particle placed after the word it governs, as opposed to a preposition.

Mandarin hòuzhìcí, Cantonese hau6 zi3 ci4, Japanese こうちし, Korean 후치사, and Vietnamese hậu trí từ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `cantonese`/`vietnamese`, fixed missing "(char)" suffix on `characters`. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄨㄛㄑㄧㄙㄚ unique across both words and characters).
