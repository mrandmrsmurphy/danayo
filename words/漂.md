## Notes
- [漂](characters/漂%20(char).md) "drift, float" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 漂 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

漂 means to drift, float (as in 漂流 "drift," 漂泊 "wander").

Mandarin piào, Cantonese piu1, Korean 표, and Vietnamese phiêu (the standard reading among 漂's long multi-value citation list, as in phiêu lưu "adventure") are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 漂's own stored citations exactly. Fixed literal `vietnamese: null` → phiêu, added the missing `pos`/`japanese`(ひょう, on'yomi HYOU)/`hsk_level`("1") fields, reformatted `characters` to a proper block-list with the "(char)" suffix. No homophones (注音 ㄆ⼄ unique across both words and characters).
