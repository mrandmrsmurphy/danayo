## Notes
- [映 (char)](../characters/映%20(char).md) "reflect; project" + [画](../characters/画.md) "picture"

映画 is a film or movie: a story told in moving pictures. Wiktionary glosses Japanese えいが as "movie, film". Korean 영화 is the reading of the characters. The Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the characters.

The building is the [映画館](映画館.md), and the making is [撮影](撮影.md). The related word [電影](電影.md) is the other film word of Chinese.
