## Notes
- [弱 (char)](../characters/弱%20(char).md) "weak" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [弱 (char)](../characters/弱%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

弱 means weak or feeble — of physical strength, structural integrity, intensity, or ability (弱点 "weak point," 弱肉強食 "survival of the fittest").

Japanese じゃく, Korean 약, and Vietnamese nhược are all standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄋ⼘ㄎ unique across both words and characters).
