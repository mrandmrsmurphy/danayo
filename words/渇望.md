## Notes
- [渇 (char)](../characters/渇%20(char).md) "thirst" + [望](../characters/望.md) "hope; look"

渇望 is to long for: to want something with a thirst that is hard to bear, or the longing itself. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin kěwàng as "to long for; to crave; to thirst for; to desire" and Japanese かつぼう as "earnest desire; thirst". Korean 갈망, Vietnamese khát vọng and Cantonese hot3 mong6 match.

It is the strongest of the longing words, beyond the plain [欲望](欲望.md), desire, and close to the greedy [貪欲](貪欲.md). The bearable kind is [熱望](熱望.md), fervent hope.
