## Notes
- [熱 (char)](../characters/熱%20(char).md) "hot; fervent" + [中](../characters/中.md) "middle; in"

熱中 is to be absorbed: to throw oneself eagerly into an activity or a pursuit. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin rèzhōng as "to be fond of; to be keen on; to be engrossed in" and Japanese ねっちゅう as "enthusiasm for something; to get absorbed in something". Cantonese jit6 cung1 and Korean 열중 match. The usual Chinese spelling is 熱衷, which is an alias.

It is eager devotion, warmer than the cool interest of the [関心](関心.md), and it grows from [熱情](熱情.md), passion. A fixation that will not let go is [執着](執着.md).
