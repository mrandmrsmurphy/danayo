## Notes
- [跆](../characters/跆.md) "kick, stomp" + [拳](../characters/拳.md) "fist" + [道 (char)](../characters/道%20(char).md) "way, path" — "taekwondo," the Korean martial art. Mandarin táiquándào, Cantonese toi4 kyun4 dou6, and Korean 태권도 use this compositional compound directly (taekwondo's own etymology). Japanese テコンドー and Vietnamese taekwondo both instead use the direct phonetic loanword from Korean, the more common form in everyday use. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 퇴권닷 diverges from Sino-Korean 태권도 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄛㄧㄍ⼔ㄋㄉㄚㄨ unique among both words and characters).
