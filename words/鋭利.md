## Notes
- [鋭](../characters/鋭.md) "sharp; keen; acute" + [利](../characters/利.md) "profit" — stand-in for [[鋭]], which cannot appear independently; here 利 contributes its older "sharp, keen" sense (as in 利器, "a sharp weapon"; 鋒利, "sharp-edged"), not its more familiar modern "profit" sense

鋭利 means sharp or keen: having a fine cutting edge or point (鋭利な刃物, "a sharp blade"; 鋭利な目, "a piercing gaze"), and by extension acute in perception or intellect (鋭利な批評, "incisive criticism"). It is a near-synonym doubling of 鋭 and 利. The variants 锐利 and 銳利 are listed as aliases.

Mandarin ruìlì, Cantonese jeoi6 lei6, Japanese えいり (eiri), and Korean 예리 (yeri) are all the standard word for "sharp, keen" — both literally (a blade) and figuratively (a sharp mind or observation, 鋭利な観察/예리한 관찰). Vietnamese nhuệ lợi is a real, attested Sino-Vietnamese compound carrying the same double sense.
