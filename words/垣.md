## Notes
- [垣 (char)](../characters/垣%20(char).md) "low wall; enclosure"

垣 means "a low wall, enclosure, rampart" (城垣, "city wall"), and by extension "a city, government office" (省垣, "provincial capital").

Mandarin yuán and Cantonese wun4 preserve the sense directly. Japanese エン (EN, on'yomi, formal register) alongside native かき (kaki, "fence, hedge") cover the sense. Korean 원 (won) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese viên is the Hán Việt reading, attested in viên y ("wall moss/lichen," moss growing on walls); it belongs to the same phonetic/reading series as 圓, 員, 園 (all also read viên), a case of legitimate homophony among distinct characters rather than a mix-up.

This word shares its reading ('on/온/ㄛㄋ) with [[遠]] ("far; distant"), still unperfected and awaiting its own turn in this sweep.
