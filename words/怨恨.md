## Notes
- [怨](../characters/怨.md) "resent" + [恨 (char)](../characters/恨%20(char).md) "hate"

怨恨 is a grudge: hatred kept alive over a wrong done to one. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yuànhèn as "hatred; enmity; resentment; grudge" and Japanese えんこん as "enmity, grudge". Korean 원한 and Cantonese jyun3 han6 match.

It is the long memory of an injury, deeper than [不満](不満.md), dissatisfaction, and close to [仇恨](仇恨.md), enmity, and to the cold grievance of [怨望](怨望.md). The act it leads to is [復讐](復讐.md), revenge.
