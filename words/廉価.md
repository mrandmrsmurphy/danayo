## Notes
- [廉 (char)](../characters/廉%20(char).md) "cheap; inexpensive" (extended: incorruptible, upright) + [価](../characters/価.md) "price" — no stand-in relationship applies (廉's own `stand_in` is itself, 価's is [[価格]]). 廉 here draws on its "inexpensive" sense rather than its more common "upright, incorruptible" sense (as in 廉潔, "honest and incorruptible").

廉価 names a low price — inexpensive, budget-priced (廉価版 "budget edition/version").

Japanese れんか and Vietnamese liêm giá are both standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄌ⼶ㄇㄍㄚ unique across both words and characters).

**Correction (2026-08-28)**: `korean` had been stored as the malformed multi-value string "염가, 싼값" — same contamination class as [[年刊]]/[[年歳]]/[[年齢]] (염가 South-Korean-shifted, 싼값 an entirely unrelated native Korean word for "cheap price") — corrected to the proper North Korean 렴가.
