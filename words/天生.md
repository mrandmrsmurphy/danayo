## Notes
- [天 (char)](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven" + [生](../characters/生.md) "born, life" — "natural, intrinsic, innate" (born of Heaven, inborn). **Typo fixed**: `english` had "instrinsic" — corrected to "intrinsic." `vietnamese: thiên sinh` is a directly attested standard Vietnamese term with the same "innate, born" sense (tài năng thiên sinh, "an innate talent"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 턴상 diverges from Sino-Korean 천생 in both syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄝㄋㄙㄚㄫ unique among both words and characters).
