## Notes
"Cotangent." `korean: 여접` is the formal Sino-Korean term (confirmed via web search, alongside the equally valid 여절/餘切), paralleling [[余弦]]'s own 여현. `vietnamese: côtang` follows the same pattern as [[余弦]]'s côsin: modern Vietnamese math uses the direct Western loanword rather than any Sino-Vietnamese calque. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 요좁 diverges entirely from the compositional Sino-Korean 여접. No homophones (注音 ⼄ㄐㄛㄆ unique among both words and characters).
