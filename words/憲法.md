## Notes
- [憲](../characters/憲.md) "law, statute" + [法](../characters/法%20(char).md) "law, method" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [憲](../characters/憲.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (法's own stand-in is itself).

憲法 means constitution — the fundamental body of law governing a state.

Mandarin xiànfǎ, Cantonese hin3 faat3, Japanese けんぽう, Korean 헌법, and Vietnamese hiến pháp are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄝㄋㄈㄚㄆ unique across both words and characters).
