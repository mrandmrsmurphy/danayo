## Notes
- [更 (char)](../characters/更%20(char).md) "again, more" (extended from its older "change, replace" sense) + [改 (char)](../characters/改%20(char).md) "redo, renew" — literally "change-renew."

更改 means to change, revise, or alter — a near-synonym of [[改善]] ("improvement, reform") and [[改革]] ("reform"), all built on 改, distinguished by 更改's more neutral, general-purpose sense of simply altering something (a schedule, a plan, a document) without the improvement connotation those other compounds carry.

Mandarin gēnggǎi, Cantonese gang1 goi2, Korean 갱개 (gaeng-gae), and Japanese こうかい (kōkai) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 更's and 改's own stored citations. Vietnamese canh cải is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, matching 更's canh (one of three attested readings — canh/cánh/ngạnh, and the specific one carrying the "change" sense) and 改's cải. **Real `kwin` bug found and fixed**: was stored as `true`, but Dan'a'yo 강개 does not exactly string-match Sino-Korean 갱개 — the first syllable diverges (강 vs. 갱), consistent with 更's own individual `kwin: false` (改's own is `kwin: true`, but the compound as a whole does not match). Corrected to `kwin: false`. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄫㄍㄚㄧ unique among both words and characters).
