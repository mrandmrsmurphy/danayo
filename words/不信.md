## Notes
- [不 (char)](../characters/不%20(char).md) "not" + [信](../characters/信.md) "trust"

Korean 불신 (not 부신) reflects a regular Sino-Korean assimilation rule where 不 surfaces as 불 before certain consonant-initial syllables (the same pattern seen in 불가능, 불편) — a real phonological regularity, not a contamination bug.
