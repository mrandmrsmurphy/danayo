## Notes
- [随](../characters/随.md) "accompany" + [行 (char)](../characters/行%20(char).md) "go" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes 随 as a bound character (行's own stand-in is [[行]] itself; transitivity fails, so no `#cranberry`).

随行 means to accompany or go along with, typically in a supporting, serving, or protective role rather than as an equal companion — an entourage member accompanying a dignitary, for instance. Mandarin 隨行/随行 (suíxíng), Cantonese 隨行 (ceoi4hang4), Japanese 随行 (ずいこう), Korean 수행 (suhaeng), and Sino-Vietnamese tuỳ hành all preserve this meaning across the CJKV sphere.
