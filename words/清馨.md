## Notes
- [清](../characters/清.md) "pure, clear" + [馨](../characters/馨.md) "fragrant"

清馨 is a delicate fragrance: a faint, pure scent. English Wiktionary has an entry only for Chinese 清馨 (Mandarin qīngxīn, Cantonese cing1 hing1), marked literary and glossed "delicate fragrance; faint scent". Japanese せいけい, Korean 청형 and Vietnamese thanh hinh are built from the readings of the two characters.

馨 also stands for virtue as a fragrance that spreads: the line 斯是陋室，惟吾德馨 in Liu Yuxi's 《陋室銘》 ("this is a humble room, but my virtue is its fragrance") uses it so. This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [馨](../characters/馨.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
