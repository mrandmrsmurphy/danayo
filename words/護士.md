## Notes
- [護](../characters/護.md) "guard, protect" + [士](../characters/士%20(char).md) "scholar, person" — "protector person," a nurse. Their own `stand_in` values: 護's is [[守護]]; 士's is itself; none points here, so 護士 is an independent compound.

護士 is a nurse: a person trained to care for the sick, in the profession. Mandarin hùshì and Cantonese wu6 si6 are the ordinary words. Japanese says 看護師 and Korean 간호사, so those two fields hold the character readings. It is the nurse as a person, where [[看護]] is the care. Compare [[医学]] (medicine) and [[病院]] (hospital).
