## Notes
- [憫](../characters/憫%20(char).md) "pity" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 憫 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

Mandarin mǐn, Cantonese man5, Japanese びん, Korean 민, and Vietnamese mẫn are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄇㄧㄋ/min/민) with the unrelated [[敏]] ("fast, quick, clever").
