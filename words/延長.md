## Notes
- [延](../characters/延.md) "prolong" + [長 (char)](../characters/長%20(char).md) "long" — this word is the stand-in for [延](../characters/延.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

延長 means to extend or prolong — in time (延長戦 "overtime, extra time," 会議を延長する "to extend a meeting") or space.

Japanese えんちょう, Korean 연장, and Vietnamese diên trường are all standard, non-drifted readings. No homophones (注音 ⼶ㄋㄐㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
