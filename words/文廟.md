## Notes
- [文](../characters/文.md) "culture, literature" + [廟](../characters/廟.md) "ancestor temple, shrine" — literally "temple of culture/literature," a Confucian temple.
- From 文帝 (Wén Dì, "God of Literature, Culture, etc.") as a Taoist god or epithet of Confucius.

Mandarin wénmiào, Cantonese man4 miu6, Japanese ぶんびょう (bunbyō), Korean 문묘, and Vietnamese văn miếu are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 文's and 廟's own stored citations exactly. This is a real, internationally recognized institution across the sphere: Korea's 문묘 (Munmyo, in Seoul) and Vietnam's Văn Miếu (Temple of Literature, in Hanoi) are both major historical landmarks bearing this exact name.
