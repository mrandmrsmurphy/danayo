## Notes
- [文](../characters/文.md) "culture, literature" + [廟](../characters/廟.md) "ancestor temple, shrine" — literally "temple of culture/literature," a Confucian temple.

Mandarin wénmiào, Cantonese man4 miu6, Japanese ぶんびょう (bunbyō), Korean 문묘, and Vietnamese văn miếu are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 文's and 廟's own stored citations exactly. 文廟 is the temple of Confucius ([孔子](孔子.md)), the sage of 文, learning and letters, and is conventionally paired with 武廟, the temple of the martial cult. This is a real, internationally recognized institution across the sphere: Korea's 문묘 (Munmyo, in Seoul) and Vietnam's Văn Miếu (Temple of Literature, in Hanoi) are both major historical landmarks bearing this exact name.
