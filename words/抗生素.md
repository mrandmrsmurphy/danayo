## Notes
- [抗](../characters/抗.md) "resist" + [生](../characters/生.md) "life" + [素](../characters/素.md) "element, substance" — "substance that resists life," i.e. against microbes. 抗's own `stand_in` is [[抵抗]], 生's is [[生活]] and 素's is [[要素]]; none points here, so 抗生素 is an independent compound.

抗生素 is an antibiotic. Mandarin kàngshēngsù and Cantonese kong3 saang1 sou3 are the ordinary Chinese words. Japanese says 抗生物質 and Korean 항생제, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[細菌]] (bacteria) and [[病毒]] (virus), which antibiotics do not treat.
