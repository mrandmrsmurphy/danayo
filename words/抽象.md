## Notes
- [抽](../characters/抽.md) "draw out" + [象](../characters/象.md) "image; shape"

抽象 is abstract: drawn out from particular things into a general idea, and the act of doing so. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin chōuxiàng as "abstract (not specific)" and "to form a general idea from particular circumstances", and Japanese ちゅうしょう as "abstraction". Vietnamese trừu tượng is the same word, and Korean 추상 and Cantonese cau1 zoeng6 match.

It is the opposite of [具体](具体.md), concrete. Mandarin also uses it as internet slang for something bizarre, which Dan'a'yo does not take.
