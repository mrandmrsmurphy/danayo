## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [藻](../characters/藻.md) "seaweed, alga" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 藻 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (海's own stand-in is [[海洋]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

海藻 means seaweed.

Mandarin hǎizǎo, Cantonese hoi2 zou2, and Japanese かいそう (a genuine coincidence: 草 and 藻 share the identical on'yomi SOU, distinct from this word's sibling [[海草]] "seagrass" despite the identical Japanese reading) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 海's and 藻's own stored citations exactly. Fixed a real bug: `korean` was 해초, contaminated from the near-synonym [[海草]] (whose own second character 草 does read 초) — corrected to 해조, matching 藻's own citation. Vietnamese tảo biển matches 藻's own citation and is also the real, standard term. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄧㄐㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).
