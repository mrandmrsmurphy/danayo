## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [藻](../characters/藻.md) "seaweed, alga" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 藻 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (海's own stand-in is [[海洋]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

海藻 means seaweed.

Mandarin hǎizǎo, Cantonese hoi2 zou2, and Japanese かいそう (a genuine coincidence: 草 and 藻 share the identical on'yomi SOU, distinct from this word's sibling [[海草]] "seagrass" despite the identical Japanese reading) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 海's and 藻's own stored citations exactly. Vietnamese tảo biển matches 藻's own citation and is also the real, standard term.
