## Notes
- [間](../characters/間.md) "between, among" + [隔](../characters/隔%20(char).md) "intervene, separate, every other"

間隔 means an interval or gap, or (as a stative) to be divided/separated by one. Like its neighbor [[間或]], it draws on 間's minority "interval" reading (jiàn/gaan3/gián) rather than the character's own stored default "between" reading — mandarin jiàngé, Cantonese gaan3gaak6, and Sino-Vietnamese gián cách all reflect this consistently. Korean 간격 (gangyeok) instead keeps the ordinary Sino-Korean concatenation, commonly interchanged with the native nouns 사이 and 틈 (both "gap, space between") in everyday speech.

Japanese 間隔 (かんかく) is the standard, everyday word for a physical or temporal interval (spacing between objects, intervals between events) — unlike [[間或]], where Japanese lacks a real cognate, here the compositional on'yomi reading is itself the ordinary attested word.
