## Notes
- [版](characters/版.md) "edition, version" + [画](characters/画.md) "draw" — no stand-in relationship applies (版's own stand-in is [[版本]]; 画's own is [[絵画]]). 画 carries two distinct Sino-readings depending on sense: literary "to draw/delineate" (画's own stored citation, waak6/획) versus colloquial "picture, painting" (waa2/화, used here) — a polyphonic character-level gap, matching the pattern already seen on [[為人]]/[[片仮名]].

版画 means a print (a picture copied from a painting or photograph).

Japanese はんが (HAN+GA) was already correct. Fixed a malformed hybrid tone notation in `cantonese` (baan2 waa6-2 → baan2 waa2), using 画's colloquial "picture" reading rather than its own stored literary citation. Filled the blank `korean` with 판화 (also using the colloquial "화" sense, the real standard Korean word for "print/engraving," rather than 画's own stored 획) and blank `vietnamese` with bản hoạ (using 画's own listed reading hoạ). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄚㄋㄏ⺢ㄎ unique across both words and characters).
