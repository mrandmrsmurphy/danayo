## Notes
- Phonetic: [涅](../characters/涅.md) + [盤](../characters/盤%20(char).md) — a Chinese phonetic transliteration of Sanskrit nirvāṇa, not a semantically compositional compound.

Mandarin nièpán, Cantonese nip6 pun4, Japanese ねはん (nehan), Korean 녈반, and Vietnamese niết bàn are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 涅's and 盤's own stored citations exactly. **Found and fixed a real bug**: `korean` was stored as 열반, the South-Korean 두음법칙-softened form — inconsistent with this vault's standing rule that `korean` always holds the North Korean/문화어 reading (녈, matching 涅's own stored citation exactly, unsoftened at word-initial position).

`kwin` is false: 涅 alone diverges from Korean on its own character page, which alone is enough to make the AND-rule false; 盤 is independently kwin-true. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄝㄊㄅㄚㄋ unique among both words and characters).
