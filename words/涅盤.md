## Notes
- Phonetic: [涅](../characters/涅.md) + [盤](../characters/盤%20(char).md) — a Chinese phonetic transliteration of Sanskrit nirvāṇa, not a semantically compositional compound.

Mandarin nièpán, Cantonese nip6 pun4, Japanese ねはん (nehan), Korean 녈반, and Vietnamese niết bàn are all standard readings, matching 涅's and 盤's own stored citations exactly. Korean 녈반 is the North Korean reading, matching 涅's own 녈 without word-initial softening.
