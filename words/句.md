## Notes
- [句 (char)](../characters/句%20(char).md) "phrase; sentence; stanza"

句 is 形声: semantic 丩 ("to entangle") + phonetic [[口]]. It means "a sentence, phrase, clause" — the basic grammatical/rhetorical unit, and by extension a classifier for utterances (一句話, "one sentence/remark"). It appears in [[句法]] ("syntax"), [[句点]]/[[句号]] ("period, full stop"), and [[慣用句]] ("an idiom, idiomatic phrase").

Mandarin jù, Cantonese geoi3, and Japanese く/こう (ku/kō, on'yomi, fully productive: 慣用句 かんようく "idiom") all preserve the sense. Korean 구 (gu) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 글귀 (geulgwi, "a passage, phrase [of writing]"). Vietnamese has two attested readings: cú, the classical Hán Việt reading (cú pháp, 句法, "syntax"), and câu, the everyday native word for "sentence" (câu văn, câu nói) — both genuinely in use, not a contamination case.

This word is part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read gu/구/ㄍㄨ): [[倶]] ("accompany; be with") and [[衢]] ("crossroads"), both already perfected and already cross-linking here from earlier in this sweep.
