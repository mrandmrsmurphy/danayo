## Notes
- [喝 (char)](../characters/喝%20(char).md) "shout" + [采](../characters/采.md) "gather; colour"

喝采 is to cheer or applaud, or applause: a shout of praise for a performer. Wiktionary lists Mandarin hècǎi and Japanese かっさい as "applause". Korean 갈채 is the same word.

It is the voice side of applause, where [拍手](拍手.md) is the clapping side, and it is given by the [聴衆](聴衆.md), audience.
