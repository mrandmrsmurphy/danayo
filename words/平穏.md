## Notes
- [平](../characters/平.md) "even; peaceful" + [穏](../characters/穏.md) "calm; stable" — this word is the stand-in for [穏](../characters/穏.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

平穏 is a near-synonym doubling, both halves independently meaning "calm, peaceful" — used of a settled, untroubled state of affairs (平穏な生活, "a peaceful life") or the absence of disturbance/incident.

Japanese へいおん, Korean 평온, and Vietnamese bình ổn are all standard, non-drifted readings. No homophones (注音 ㄅ⼶ㄫㄛㄋ unique across both words and characters).
