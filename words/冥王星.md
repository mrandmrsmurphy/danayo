## Notes
- [冥 (char)](../characters/冥%20(char).md) "underworld, dark" + [王 (char)](../characters/王%20(char).md) "king" + [星 (char)](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" — "Pluto," named for the Roman god of the underworld, matching the mythological register of its aliased Chinese name [[冥王]] ("king of the underworld"). Mandarin Míngwángxīng, Cantonese ming4 wong4 sing1, Japanese めいおうせい, Korean 명왕성, and Vietnamese Diêm Vương Tinh (using the Sino-Vietnamese name for the underworld king Yama, Diêm Vương, rather than a literal calque of 冥) are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 멍왕성 diverges from Sino-Korean 명왕성 in the first syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄝㄫ·⺢ㄫㄙㄝㄫ unique among both words and characters).
