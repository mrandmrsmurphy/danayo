## Notes
- [染](../characters/染.md) "to dye" + [料](../characters/料.md) "material, fee" — dye.

染料 is a dye: a coloring agent that dissolves in a liquid and soaks into cloth, leather or paper, as opposed to [[顔料]], a pigment that coats a surface. It is 染 "to dye" plus 料 "material," the same 料 as in [[顔料]]. The act of dyeing is [[染色]]. Mandarin rǎnliào, Cantonese jim5 liu6, Japanese せんりょう and Korean 염료 are the standard readings.
