## Notes
- [麻](../characters/麻.md) "hemp" + [布](../characters/布.md) "linen" — no `#cranberry`: 麻's own `stand_in` is [[大麻]], 布's is [[亜麻布]]; neither points here.

**Fixed a real contamination bug**: `japanese` comma-joined two forms, "あさふ,あさぬの" — kept あさぬの (asa + nuno, "hemp" + the common modern word for "cloth"), a more naturally-attested compound than the more artificial-sounding あさふ hybrid.

Mandarin mábù, Cantonese maa4bou3, Korean 마포, and Sino-Vietnamese ma bố are all standard, fully compositional readings.
