## Notes
- [偽 (char)](../characters/偽%20(char).md) "false; artificial" + [証](../characters/証.md) "prove; confirm; verify"

偽証 is perjury: to give false evidence or testimony, especially under oath. Wiktionary glosses Chinese 偽證 as "perjury; false evidence" in the legal register. Japanese ぎしょう and Korean 위증 are the matching forms.

The offence is the opposite of honest [証言](証言.md), the testimony of a witness, and it is committed under the [誓約](誓約.md), the oath, which is what makes the lie a crime. It differs from [偽造](偽造.md), which makes a false object.
