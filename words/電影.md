## Notes
- [電](../characters/電.md) "electricity" + [影](../characters/影.md) "shadow, form"

電影 means film, movie, or video. Mandarin 電影/电影 (diànyǐng) is the standard, everyday word for "movie" in Chinese, and Sino-Vietnamese điện ảnh is likewise the standard term for cinema (điện ảnh Việt Nam, "Vietnamese cinema"). Cantonese 電影 (din6jing2) follows the same pattern.

Japanese and Korean instead use an entirely different compound for "movie": 映画 (えいが, eiga) and 영화 (yeonghwa) respectively — built on 映 ("to project, reflect") rather than 電. The compositional readings でんえい/전영 given here are not independently attested as live words in either language.
