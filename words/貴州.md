## Notes
- [貴](../characters/貴.md) "expensive, valuable" + [州 (char)](../characters/州%20(char).md) "state, province" — per Wiktionary a corruption of an earlier name, 矩州. No stand-in relationship applies (貴's own is [[貴重]]; 州 is its own stand-in). 贵 is the alias of 貴, so 贵州 is kept as the word's alias.

貴州 (Guizhou) is a mountainous province of southwest China whose capital is [[貴陽]] (from memory, not re-checked). English Wiktionary says the name is a corruption of the earlier 矩州 and has been used since the Song dynasty; it also gives 黔 (Qian) as another name for the province. The second character 州 is the historical "prefecture" character.

The Japanese field holds きしゅう (English Wiktionary Japanese). The Korean field holds the Sino-Korean 귀주 (Wiktionary Korean: hanja form of 귀주), which differs from the Dan'a'yo 諺文 귀줏 only in the final of 州; the usual South Korean name today is 구이저우 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Quý Châu is the Sino-Vietnamese name; the character page for 貴 gives the Vietnamese as quí, the older spelling of the same syllable, and the word follows Wiktionary's modern spelling. Cantonese gwai3 zau1 is the Jyutping on English Wiktionary.

Attestation, checked 2026-10-11: English and Chinese Wiktionary (Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese sections) list only the province for this name. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄨㄧㄐㄨㄛ unique across both words and characters).
