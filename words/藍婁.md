## Notes
- [藍 (char)](../characters/藍%20(char).md) "indigo" + [婁](../characters/婁.md) "drag"

藍婁 is a rag, and tattered, ragged. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 襤褸 (alias here; Mandarin lánlǚ, Cantonese laam4 leoi5) as "tattered; ragged; shabby" of clothes and, classical, "tattered clothes; ragged clothes; rag", Japanese らんる as "torn clothes, ragged clothes, rag" and "desperation; nonsense", and lists Korean 남루 (North Korean 람루) as a hanja form meaning "rags, shreds, scraps". The native Japanese word for a rag is ぼろ.

The spelling is a phonetic substitution, and the two characters carry no meaning of their own here. Wiktionary lists 藍縷, 藍褸, 藍蔞, 襤縷, 籃縷 and 褴褛 as variants of 襤褸. 藍 reads ㄌㄚㄇ and 婁 reads ㄌㄨ, so the word reads as 襤褸 does. 襤 and 褴 are aliases of [藍 (char)](../characters/藍%20(char).md), and 褸, 縷, 蔞, 褛 and 缕 are aliases of [婁](../characters/婁.md).
