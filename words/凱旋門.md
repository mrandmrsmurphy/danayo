## Notes
- [凱](../characters/凱.md) "triumphant song" + [旋](../characters/旋.md) "revolve" + [門](../characters/門.md) "gate" — "triumphal arch," and in particular the Arc de Triomphe in Paris. Mandarin kǎixuánmén, Cantonese hoi2 syun4 mun4, Japanese がいせんもん, Korean 개선문, and Vietnamese khải toàn môn are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 캐숸몬 diverges from Sino-Korean 개선문 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄚㄧㄙ⼔ㄋㄇㄛㄋ unique among both words and characters).
