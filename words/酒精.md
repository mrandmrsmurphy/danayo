## Notes
- [酒](../characters/酒.md) "alcohol, liquor" + [精](../characters/精.md) "essence, spirit" — "alcohol, ethanol."

Mandarin jiǔjīng, cantonese zau2 zing1, and japanese しゅせい are all directly attested standard readings.

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 酒's own `stand_in` is this exact word (酒精), so 酒 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 酒精; 精's own `stand_in` is instead 精神, so this word does not legitimize 精.
