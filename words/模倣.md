## Notes
- [模](../characters/模.md) "imitate" + [倣](../characters/倣.md) "imitate, emulate" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [倣](../characters/倣.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 模's own stand-in is [[模擬]].

模倣 means to imitate or emulate.

Mandarin mófǎng, Cantonese mou4 fong2, Japanese もほう, Korean 모방, and Vietnamese mô phảng are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 模's and 倣's own stored citations exactly). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field, reformatted `aliases` to block-list YAML. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄛㄈㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).

Dan'a'yo keeps the shared CJKV sense: to imitate or emulate. It is placed on the Mind page under Structure, Pattern & Design, beside [[様式]] and [[型式]].
