## Notes
- [調](../characters/調.md) "transfer, adjust" is separately legitimized by [[調整]]. [査](../characters/査.md) "investigate" is legitimized by this exact compound (its own stand-in). No cranberry.

Mandarin diàochá, japanese ちょうさ, korean 조사, and vietnamese điều tra (all also the real common words for "investigation") confirmed standard and compositional. The same missing-ㄛ bug also existed on [[酒精]] (a genuine reciprocal homophone of 調整, since 酒 independently shares 調's exact own reading juo/줏/ㄐㄨㄛ) — fixed there too, plus its citations on `characters/酒.md` and `characters/精.md`.
