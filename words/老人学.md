## Notes
- [老 (char)](../characters/老%20(char).md) "old" + [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study" — none of the three characters' own `stand_in` points here (老's is [[老]] itself, 人's is [[人]] itself, 学's is [[学習]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 老 and 人 are each individually `kwin: false`.

老人学 names gerontology, the study of aging.

Mandarin lǎorénxué, Cantonese lou5 jan4 hok6, Japanese ろうじんがく (rōjingaku), and Korean 노인학 (noinhak) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 老人学 is itself a common attested word for "gerontology" in all four languages. Vietnamese lão nhân học is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term. **Fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字`/`諺文` had lyau/럇 (mismatching 老's real lau/랏) — `注音` (ㄌㄚㄨㄋㄧㄋㄏㄚㄎ) had stayed correct throughout. No homophones.
