## Notes
- [捨](../characters/捨%20(char).md) "throw away, discard" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [捨](../characters/捨%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

捨 means to throw away or discard.

Mandarin shě, Cantonese se2, Japanese しゃ, Korean 사, and Vietnamese xả are all standard, fully compositional readings. Homophone of [[卸]] "retire; lay down" (both 注音 ㄙ⼘) — completing the reciprocal callout anticipated on 卸's own page; checked ten other candidates sharing this reading (写/射/奢/斜/社/紗/舎/謝/赦/邪) and confirmed none has an independent word at this exact reading (奢/紗 are name-only, the rest bound to longer stand-in compounds).
