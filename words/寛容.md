## Notes
- [寛](../characters/寛.md) "wide; generous" + [容 (char)](../characters/容%20(char).md) "contain; allow"

寛容 is tolerant: willing to put up with others' faults and differences, generous in forgiving. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin kuānróng as "lenient; tolerant; charitable" and Japanese かんよう as "tolerant, lenient; magnanimous". Korean 관용, Vietnamese khoan dung and Cantonese fun1 jung4 match.

It is the settled kindness of the [温厚](温厚.md), carried into dealing with others, and the reverse of the [苛酷](苛酷.md), harsh. Patience in bearing is the [忍耐](忍耐.md).
