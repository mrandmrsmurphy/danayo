## Notes
- [無](../characters/無%20(char).md) "not, without" + [神](../characters/神%20(char).md) "god" + [論](../characters/論.md) "theory, doctrine" — "no-god doctrine," atheism. Their own `stand_in` values: 無's is itself; 神's is itself; 論's is [[理論]]; none points here, so 無神論 is an independent compound.

無神論 is atheism: the doctrine that there is no god. Mandarin wúshénlùn, Cantonese mou4 san4 leon6, Japanese むしんろん and Korean 무신론 all use it, and Vietnamese vô thần luận does too. An atheist is 無神論－者, segmented with the productive suffix [[－者]], not coined separately. Compare [[神]] (god) and [[宗教]] (religion).
