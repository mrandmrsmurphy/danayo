## Notes
- [追 (char)](../characters/追%20(char).md) "follow; hunt" + [逐](../characters/逐.md) "pursue"
- This word is the viable form of [逐](../characters/逐.md): the character's meaning (to pursue, chase) is identical, but 逐 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 追逐 is its `stand_in` compound.

Both 追 and 逐 carry the radical for walking and both mean "to chase," so 追逐 is a synonym compound that stresses the sustained, running nature of the pursuit: following hard after a fleeing target, whether prey, a rival or a prize. It is used literally (hounds chasing game, children chasing one another) and figuratively (追逐名利 "chase fame and profit," a recurring Chinese phrase for the pursuit of worldly gain).

It overlaps with [追及](追及.md), which the Movement page notes means specifically "to catch up to," the endpoint of a chase, while 追逐 describes the chase itself; and it differs from [追求](追求.md) "to seek, pursue (a goal or a person's affection)," which is more abstract and aspirational, and from [追随](追随.md) "to follow, to be a follower," where there is no fleeing target. The readings are Mandarin zhuīzhú, Cantonese zeoi1 zuk6 and Korean 추축 (this page leaves the Japanese field blank). Dan'a'yo keeps the shared sense, "chase, pursue," with no divergence. It is placed under [Movement](../lexipedia/Movement.md).
