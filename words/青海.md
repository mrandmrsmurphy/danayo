## Notes
- [青 (char)](../characters/青%20(char).md) "blue" + [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" — "blue sea", the Chinese name of Qinghai Lake. No stand-in relationship applies (青 is its own stand-in; 海's is [[海洋]]). 靑 is the alias of 青, so 靑海 is kept as the word's alias.

The ordinary and lake senses come first. Japanese Wiktionary lists 青海 as a plain noun, "a blue sea" (青々とした海) (the same characters have a separate native reading あおうみ, not covered here). 青海 (Qinghai, Kokonor) is the large salt lake on the northeast edge of the Tibetan plateau, the largest lake in China (from memory, not re-checked); English Wiktionary compares its Tibetan and Mongolian names, which also mean "blue lake", and quotes Du Fu's line 君不見青海頭 for the lake. The 海 in the name is used of a large lake, as in several Chinese lake names (from memory, not re-checked).

Qinghai, the province, takes the lake's name and lies on the Tibetan plateau in western China; its capital is [[西寧]] (from memory, not re-checked). The Japanese field holds せいかい (English Wiktionary Japanese, listed for both senses). The Korean field holds the Sino-Korean 청해, matching the Dan'a'yo 諺文 exactly; the usual South Korean name for the province today is 칭하이 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Thanh Hải is the Sino-Vietnamese name. Cantonese cing1 hoi2 is the Jyutping on English Wiktionary. The Hainan Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture in the province is named for its position south of the lake (see [[海南]]). No homophones (注音 ㄑㄝㄫㄏㄚㄧ unique across both words and characters).

Attestation, checked 2026-10-11: English and Chinese Wiktionary (Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese sections) list the senses above, with the senses marked Japanese taken from Japanese Wiktionary.

1. A blue, verdant sea (Japanese 青々とした海).
2. Qinghai Lake (Kokonor), a large salt lake of western China.
3. Qinghai, the province of China.
