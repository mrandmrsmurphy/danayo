## Notes
- [河](../characters/河.md) "stream" + [川 (char)](../characters/川%20(char).md) "river" — a near-synonym compound, "rivers and streams" collectively, rivers in general.

Mandarin héchuān, Cantonese ho4 cyun1, Japanese かせん (kasen), Korean 하천, and Vietnamese hà xuyên are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 河's and 川's own stored citations exactly.
