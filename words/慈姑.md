## Notes
- [慈](../characters/慈.md) "kind" + [姑](../characters/姑.md) "aunt" — no stand-in relationship applies (慈's own stand-in is [[慈善]]; 姑's own is [[姑母]]).

慈姑 means arrowhead (Sagittaria sagittifolia), an aquatic plant. Concerning the name of the plant, the Compendium of Materia Medica says: 一根歲生十二子，如慈姑之乳諸子，故以名之 — "one arrowroot yields twelve fruits, as a kind mother gives birth to many sons, hence it is so called."

Mandarin cígū, Cantonese ci4 gu1, Korean 자고, and Vietnamese từ cô are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `japanese` had archaic pre-reform kana くわゐ — modernized to くわい. Filled blank `korean`/`vietnamese`. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄐㄧㄍㄛ/jigo/지고) with the unrelated [[事故]] ("accident").
