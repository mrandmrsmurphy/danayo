## Notes
- [交 (char)](../characters/交%20(char).md) "to exchange" + [換 (char)](../characters/換%20(char).md) "to change, swap" — a near-synonym-doubling compound emphasizing mutual exchange.

交換 is the standard word for "exchange, swap" across the Sinosphere, from everyday usage (交換留学, "exchange study/student") to technical vocabulary (交換手, "telephone switchboard operator"; 交換機, "switch/exchanger" in telecommunications and computing). Mandarin jiāohuàn, Cantonese gaau1 wun6, Japanese こうかん, and Korean 교환 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese giao hoán is likewise standard, though everyday Vietnamese also frequently uses the near-synonym trao đổi for general exchange/swapping.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 굣홤 diverges from Sino-Korean 교환, since both 交 and 換 independently carry `kwin: false` on their own character pages.
