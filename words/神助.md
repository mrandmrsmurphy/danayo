## Notes
- [神](../characters/神%20(char).md) "god, divine" + [助](../characters/助.md) "help, assist" — "God has helped," the name Eleazar. Their own `stand_in` values: 神's is itself; 助's is [[援助]]; none points here, so 神助 is an independent compound.

神助 is the Dan'a'yo rendering of the Hebrew name אֶלְעָזָר (Eleazar), "God has helped," and of its Greek form Lazarus (Lazaros). It is built on the vault's translation principles, by meaning and not by sound: the first element El is the generic word for God (the Elohim word, which in the three divine names is [[上帝]], not YHWH), so the first element is 神 "god" and not 偕, which is kept for YHWH, the "-yah" of [[偕者]]; the second element is 助 "help." This follows the precedent of 偕神書 (Joel, "YHWH is God"), where El is also 神. 神助 is also a natural Chinese phrase, "divine help." It replaces the earlier draft 助偕, which used 偕 where the Hebrew has El. Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and Korean readings in the fields are mechanical, built from the two characters, and are not claims of use; the real names are Eleazar, Lazarus, エレアザル, ラザロ and 엘르아살, 나사로. See [[基督敎]].
