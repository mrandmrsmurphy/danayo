## Notes
- [直](../characters/直%20(char).md) "straight, erect, aligned" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 直 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

直 means straight, upright, or aligned — both physically (a straight line) and, very productively, morally or characterologically (straightforward, honest, forthright, as in 率直 "frank, candid"). It is one of the most compound-productive characters in the language: 直線 ("straight line"), 垂直 ("vertical, perpendicular"), 直径 ("diameter"), 直角 ("right angle"), 直観 ("intuition").

Mandarin zhí, Cantonese zik6, Japanese ちょく (choku), Korean 직 (jik), and Vietnamese trực are all standard, fully compositional readings, consistent across both the physical and moral senses of the word.
