## Notes
- [編](../characters/編.md) "compile; edit" + [纂](../characters/纂.md) "compile; edit" — this word is 纂's own `stand_in`: the character's meaning (to compile) is identical, but 纂 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word, so the compound pairs it with the near-synonym 編 instead.

編纂 means to compile or edit — specifically the large-scale, often institutional work of assembling a text from many sources (a dictionary, an encyclopedia, an official history), distinct from ordinary line-editing prose. Mandarin biānzuǎn, Cantonese pin1 zyun2, Japanese へんさん, Korean 편찬, and Vietnamese biên soạn are all standard, fully compositional readings across the CJKV sphere.

Wiktionary glosses Mandarin biānzuǎn (编纂, 編纂) as "to compile (a written text); to edit", with a usage note that it describes editing at a more macro level than [編集](編集.md), and confirms Cantonese pin1 zyun2. Its Japanese entry gives へんさん as "compilation (of a book)". On the [Art](../lexipedia/Art.md) page the two words sit together as a pair, and in Dan'a'yo 編纂 is the compiling of a whole work, while 編集 is ordinary editing.
