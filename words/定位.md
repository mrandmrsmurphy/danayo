## Etymology
- [定](../characters/定.md) "decide, fixed" + [位](../characters/位.md) "position" — stand-in for [[位]], which cannot appear independently

## Notes
- "To position, to locate; positioning" — both a verb (measuring/determining a location, or figuratively "to characterize, find one's niche") and the resulting noun, extremely common today in the technical sense of GPS/satellite positioning. `vietnamese: định vị` is directly attested and extremely common in exactly this modern technical register (hệ thống định vị toàn cầu, "GPS"). **Left `hsk_level` unset**: no reliable official classification found.
- `kwin: false` confirmed (定 alone matches Korean, 位 doesn't — AND-rule). No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄫ⼔ㄧ unique among both words and characters).
