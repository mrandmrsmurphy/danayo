## Notes
- [抵](../characters/抵.md) "resist" + [抗](../characters/抗.md) "resist" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes BOTH characters as independent Dan'a'yo entries: both 抵's and 抗's own `stand_in` is 抵抗 (transitivity holds, hence `#cranberry`).

抵抗 means to resist.

Mandarin dǐkàng, Cantonese dai2 kong3, Japanese ていこう, Korean 저항, and Vietnamese để kháng are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄝㄧㄏㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
