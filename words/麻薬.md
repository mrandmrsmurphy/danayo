## Notes
- [麻](../characters/麻.md) "numb" + [薬](../characters/薬%20(char).md) "drug" — "numbing drug," a narcotic. Their own `stand_in` values: 麻's is [[大麻]]; 薬's is itself; none points here, so 麻薬 is an independent compound.

麻薬 is a narcotic or illicit drug. Japanese まやく and Korean 마약 use it as the ordinary word for narcotics. In Chinese 麻藥 (máyào, maa4 joek6) is informal and mostly means an anesthetic; Dan'a'yo takes the Japanese and Korean sense. Compare [[毒]] (poison) and [[薬]] (medicine).
