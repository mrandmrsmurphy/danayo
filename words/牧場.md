## Notes
- [牧 (char)](../characters/牧%20(char).md) "shepherd; herd" + [場](../characters/場.md) "place"

牧場 is a ranch or livestock farm — the land on which livestock are kept and grazed. Chinese also uses it for pasture or grazing land, and Japanese (bokujō, also read makiba) extends it to the dairy farm. Korean has it as mokjang.
