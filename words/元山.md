## Notes
- [元](../characters/元.md) "element; origin" + [山](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain" — the name of Wonsan, a port city of North Korea.

元山 (Wonsan) is a port city on the east coast of North Korea and the capital of Kangwon Province ([[江原]]道, the northern one) (from memory, not re-checked). It is the base of the Kalma coastal resort area (from memory, not re-checked).

English and Chinese Wiktionary have 元山 as the city (Mandarin Yuánshān, Cantonese jyun4 saan1), and English and Korean Wiktionary have a Korean entry as a hanja form of 원산. Japanese Wiktionary has no entry, so Japanese げんさん is composed from the characters' on'yomi; the colonial-period Japanese name is read Genzan and the modern one is ウォンサン (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Nguyên Sơn is the name given on the Vietnamese Wikipedia ("Nguyên Sơn thị").
