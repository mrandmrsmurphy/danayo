## Notes
- [流](../characters/流.md) "flow" + [暢](../characters/暢.md) "smooth, free, unrestrained" — no stand-in relationship applies (流's own stand-in is [[流動]]; 暢's own is [[暢達]]).

流暢 means fluent, flowing.

Mandarin liúchàng, Cantonese lau4 coeng3, and Japanese りゅうちょう are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 流's and 暢's own stored citations.
