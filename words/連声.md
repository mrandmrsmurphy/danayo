## Notes
- [連](../words/連.md) "mutual, successive" (stand-in: itself) + [声 (char)](../characters/声.md) "sound, voice" (stand-in: [[発声]]) — linking sound, sandhi: a sound change triggered across a morpheme boundary.

Wiktionary: Japanese 連声 is read れんじょう, a linguistics term for a change of the initial syllable of the second kanji under the influence of the historical coda of the first, as in 天皇 (ten'nō, not ten'ō); the `japanese` field holds the compositional れんせい, not this attested reading. Chinese 連聲 (liánshēng) is a different word there, "repeatedly (of speech or sound)". The Dan'a'yo sense is the general sandhi one, used in `文法 - 02音韻論`, which forbids it in 単亜語.
