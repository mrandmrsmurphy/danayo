## Notes
- [連](../words/連.md) "mutual, successive" (stand-in: itself) + [声 (char)](../characters/声.md) "sound, voice" (stand-in: [[発声]]) — linking sound, sandhi: a sound change triggered across a morpheme boundary.

連声 means sandhi — the general linguistic phenomenon of sound change at a morpheme or word boundary, as invoked by `文法 - 02音韻論`'s discussion of why 単亜語 forbids it ("no sandhi... is permitted").

**Heteronym flag**: the real Japanese term 連声 is actually read れんじょう (renjō), an irregular historical reading specific to this one word (as in 天皇 tennō < ten + ō), not the compositional れんせい (REN + SEI) stored here from 連's and 声's own on'yomi citations. This is a known, deliberate divergence — documenting it rather than silently asserting れんせい is the real attested term. Mandarin liánshēng, Cantonese lin4 sing1, Korean 련성, and Vietnamese liên thanh are compositional readings, not independently verified as standard terms. `kwin` is false: inherited from 声 (false), despite 連 itself being true.

No syllable-boundary dot is needed: the concatenated string `ㄌ⼶ㄋㄙㄧㄫ` admits only one legal partition.
