## Notes
- [異](../characters/異.md) "different, strange" + [体](../characters/体.md) "group, body, form" — no stand-in relationship applies (異's own stand-in is [[異常]]; 体's own is [[体系]]).

異体 means variant.

Mandarin yìtǐ, Cantonese ji6 tai2, Japanese いたい, Korean 이체 and Vietnamese dị thể are all fully compositional, matching 異's and 体's own citations.
