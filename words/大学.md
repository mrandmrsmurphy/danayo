## Notes
- [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big" + [学](../characters/学.md) "learn; study" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (both are bare self-standing characters).

This word is chiefly the abbreviated form of "university" used inside institution names (e.g. 東京大学, "Tokyo University"); for the standalone generic noun "university," use the fuller sibling word [[大学校]] (abbr 大学). Its secondary sense is "Great Learning," the Confucian classic, whose translation is at [[translation/大学 (book)]]. Mandarin dàxué, Cantonese daai6 hok6, Japanese だいがく, Korean 대학, and Vietnamese đại học are the standard readings.
