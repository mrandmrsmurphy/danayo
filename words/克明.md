## Etymology
- [克 (char)](../characters/克%20(char).md) "to overcome, be able to" + [明 (char)](../characters/明%20(char).md) "clear, bright"

## Notes
"Discerning, meticulous, morally clear-sighted" — the capacity for thorough, penetrating understanding (overcoming confusion to attain clarity). Both `korean: 극명하다` (directly attested, confirmed via web search — 극명한 대비, "a stark contrast") and `vietnamese: khắc minh` (directly attested, "thorough clarity and comprehensive understanding") confirm this compound is genuinely used across languages. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 쿡명 diverges from Sino-Korean 극명 in the first syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄨㄎㄇ⼶ㄫ unique among both words and characters).
