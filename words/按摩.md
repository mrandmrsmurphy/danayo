## Notes
- [按](../characters/按.md) "to press, push down" + 摩 "to rub" (pageless in this vault) — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [按](../characters/按.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

按摩 means massage.

Mandarin ànmó, Cantonese on3 mo1, Japanese あんま, Korean 안마, and Vietnamese án ma are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄋㄇㄚ unique across both words and characters).
