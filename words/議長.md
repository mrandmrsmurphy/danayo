## Notes
- [議](../characters/議.md) "debate" + [長 (char)](../characters/長%20(char).md) "long"

議長 is the chair of a meeting, the speaker of an assembly. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 議長 (Mandarin yìzhǎng, Cantonese ji5 zoeng2) as "chairman; speaker" and Japanese ぎちょう as "chairman (person presiding over a meeting), speaker (in a parliament)". It lists Korean 의장 and Vietnamese entries. Compare [主席](主席.md), chairperson.
