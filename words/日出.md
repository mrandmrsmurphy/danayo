## Notes
- [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day, sun" + [出](../characters/出%20(char).md) "exit" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 日 and 出's own stand-ins are themselves).

日出 means sunrise.

Mandarin rìchū, Cantonese jat6 ceot1, Japanese にっしゅつ, Korean 일출, and Vietnamese nhật xuất (a genuine standard term) are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄧㄊㄑㄨㄊ unique across both words and characters).
