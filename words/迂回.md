## Notes
- [迂](../characters/迂.md) "roundabout, circuitous" + [回 (char)](../characters/回%20(char).md) "turns" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 迂 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

Mandarin yūhuí, Cantonese jyu1 wui4, Japanese うかい (ukai), and Korean 우회 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 迂's and 回's own stored citations exactly. `vietnamese: vu hồi` is likewise compositional (迂's own vu + 回's primary hồi reading, over the alternate hòi), and 迂's vu and 回's hồi are both confirmed as Sino-Vietnamese readings.
