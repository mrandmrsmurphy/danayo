## Notes
- [迂](../characters/迂.md) "roundabout, circuitous" + [回 (char)](../characters/回%20(char).md) "turns" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 迂 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

Mandarin yūhuí, Cantonese jyu1 wui4, Japanese うかい (ukai), and Korean 우회 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 迂's and 回's own stored citations exactly. `vietnamese: vu hồi` is likewise compositional (迂's own vu + 回's primary hồi reading, over the alternate hòi), not independently verified against a dictionary — flagged for a future spot-check rather than asserted with full confidence.

`kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 우회 coincides exactly with Sino-Korean 우회, since both 迂 and 回 independently carry matching readings on their own character pages. This field was missing from the frontmatter entirely and has been added. No homophones (注音 ㄨㄏㄛㄧ unique among both words and characters).
