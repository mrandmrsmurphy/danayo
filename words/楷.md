## Notes
- [楷](characters/楷%20(char).md) "model, pattern" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 楷 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

楷 means model or pattern (as in 楷書, "regular script").

Mandarin kǎi, Cantonese kaai2, Japanese かい, Korean 해, and Vietnamese giai are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 楷's own stored citation exactly). Added the missing `japanese`/`hsk_level` fields. No homophones (注音 ㄎ⼶ unique across both words and characters).
