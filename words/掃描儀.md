## Notes
- [掃](../characters/掃.md) "sweep" + [描](../characters/描.md) "trace" + [儀](../characters/儀.md) "instrument" — "sweeping instrument," a scanner. Their own `stand_in` values: 掃's is [[掃除]]; 描's is [[素描]]; 儀's is [[儀式]]; none points here, so 掃描儀 is an independent compound.

掃描儀 is a scanner: a device that sweeps across a page or object and turns it into digital data. Mandarin sǎomiáoyí and Cantonese sou3 miu4 ji4 are the ordinary Chinese words. Japanese says スキャナー and Korean 스캐너, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[複印机]] (copier) and [[顕示器]] (monitor).
