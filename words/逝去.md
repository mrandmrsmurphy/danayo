## Notes
- [逝](characters/逝.md) "to pass away" + [去 (char)](characters/去%20(char).md) "to leave, depart" — stand-in for [[逝]], which cannot appear independently

Mandarin shìqù, cantonese sai6 heoi3, japanese せいきょ, and korean 서거 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese thệ khứ is the honest compositional reading (逝's own thệ + 去's own khứ); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 서쿄 diverges from Sino-Korean 서거 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄝㄎ⼄ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 去's own `stand_in` is instead 去 itself, so this word does not legitimize 去.
