## Notes
- [此 (char)](../characters/此%20(char).md) "this; proximal demonstrative" + [類](../characters/類.md) "kind; sort; category; type"

此類 is the proximal correlative in the 類 ("kind") column of the correlative table: "this kind; this sort; this type", the class or category that the referent belongs to, near the speaker or already prominent in the shared context. The character [此 (char)](../characters/此%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *cǝ*, ㄑㄜ) is the classical Chinese proximal demonstrative, contrasting with 其 (medial) and 彼 (distal); [類](../characters/類.md) (Dan'a'yo *lui*, ㄌㄨㄧ) supplies "kind; sort; category; type". Its sense is the same as the sibling [彼類](彼類.md) "that other kind", used for "that kind of" in the Dan'a'yo renderings of sentences 12 and 13 in `lookup/HSK/China.md`, and [其類](其類.md) "that kind"; only the deictic distance differs.

It differs from its neighbours as follows. [此物](此物.md) "this thing" points to one concrete object, [此事](此事.md) "this matter" to an act or affair, and [此様](此様.md) "this way" to the manner of an action, whereas 此類 points to a class and so can stand for any member of it. [此](此.md) alone is the demonstrative with no noun class. There is no free word 類 in `words/`: the character page [類](../characters/類.md) names [種類](種類.md) "type; kind" as its `stand_in`, so 類 is a bound character, and 種類 is the lexical word for "kind" that heads the 類 row of the chart in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md` (line 152, where the row header is 種類 rather than 類). The cell 此類 is therefore a fixed paradigm word, not a phrase built on a free noun. Unlike 此様 and [此多](此多.md), which are 副用名詞, 此類 is a 代詞 (pronoun), like [此事](此事.md), [此物](此物.md) and [此人](此人.md).

The cross-linguistic fields hold the spoken equivalents, as on the sibling pages. Mandarin 這種 (zhèzhǒng, "this kind"): Wiktionary has no entry for 這種 or 这种 (checked 2026-10-10), so the word being the everyday form is from memory, not re-checked; 這類 (zhèlèi) likewise has no entry. Wiktionary does have 此類 (cǐlèi), a noun glossed "this kind; these kinds; such" (Cantonese ci2 leoi6), which is the written-register Mandarin cognate of this Dan'a'yo word. Cantonese 呢種 (ni1 zung2) is from memory, not re-checked (no Wiktionary entry found); the sibling pages [其類](其類.md) and [彼類](彼類.md) carry no `cantonese` field. Japanese こういう (kō iu): Wiktionary has an entry, an adnominal glossed "this kind", with そういう, ああいう and どういう as coordinate terms, matching the equivalents on [其類](其類.md) and [彼類](彼類.md); このような (kono yō na) is from memory, not re-checked. Korean 이런 (ireon): Wiktionary has an entry, a determiner glossed "like this; such", derived from 이렇다; the `korean` field's 이런 종류 is the expanded "this kind" form, and 이런 alone is the adnominal, the proximal member of the 이런/그런/저런 triad. Vietnamese *loại này* "this kind" is from memory, not re-checked; hvdic.thivien.net covers single characters, not phrases, so it was not consulted for the phrase.

Mandarin has a two-way split (這種/那種), so it covers [其類](其類.md) and [彼類](彼類.md) with one form, whereas Dan'a'yo, Japanese and Korean keep three distances.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md)'s correlative table (line 121; the same chart is in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`, line 152, in the 類 ("kind") row, whose header cell is 種類), 此類 is the ninth and last cell of the 此 ("this") row, after [此事](此事.md) "this matter", [此物](此物.md) "this thing", [此人](此人.md) "this person", [此名](此名.md) "this person (polite)", [此処](此処.md) "this place", [此時](此時.md) "this time", [此様](此様.md) "this manner" and [此多](此多.md) "this amount". Down the 類 column it pairs with [其類](其類.md) "that kind" (medial), [彼類](彼類.md) "that other kind" (distal), [何類](何類.md) "what kind", [毎類](毎類.md) "every kind", [某類](某類.md) "some kind" and [皆類](皆類.md) "all kinds". The table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), 63 forms, so 此類 is parsed compositionally: 此 = proximal, 類 = kind. Illustrative only (invented, not from `grammar/`): 此類問題多 "problems of this kind are many". Dan'a'yo keeps the sense shared across CJKV, "this kind; this sort"; no narrowing is needed.

The 注音 ㄑㄜㄌㄨㄧ carries no syllable-boundary dot: the only syllable-final consonants `grammar/文法 - 02音韻論.md` allows are the nasals ㄇ ㄋ ㄫ, the glides y/w and the unreleased stops ㄆ ㄊ ㄎ, and ㄌ is none of them, so the string has exactly one parse, ㄑㄜ + ㄌㄨㄧ (both syllables exist in `syllables/`).
