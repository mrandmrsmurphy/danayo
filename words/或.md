## Notes
- [或 (char)](../characters/或%20(char).md) "or; perhaps; some; a certain"

或 is a linker (連接詞) in Dan'a'yo meaning "or" — the disjunctive conjunction, listed among the Linkers in [[文法 - 97品詞]] and under Conjunctions on [[Grammar]] ("or"). It contrasts with [[而]] (clausal "and; but") and [[与]] (nominal "and"): where those add or contrast, 或 offers alternatives of equal status, of which one (but not necessarily both) holds — A 或 B, "A or B". It joins noun phrases or whole clauses and, like the other linkers, is a free word that stands between the things it joins rather than attaching to either as a suffix. Illustrative examples (invented, not attested): 茶或水 "tea or water"; 行或止 "go or stop".

In Classical Chinese 或 also meant "someone; some; perhaps; sometimes" (Wiktionary lists "occasionally; sometimes", "if; assuming" and the literary "some; someone" among its senses). Dan'a'yo takes 或 in its conjunctive role only; the indefinite and modal senses are carried by compounds instead: [[或者]] "some people", [[間或]] "sometimes, occasionally" and [[或然]] "probable, contingent" (the last placed on [[Knowledge]], in the necessity-to-possibility gradient). There is no divergence from the shared CJKV sense of "or" to record.

Cross-linguistically: Mandarin huò (formal/written; spoken usually 或者 or 還是 in questions), Cantonese waak6, Korean 혹 (bound in 혹은 and 혹시; the plain "or" is 또는/혹은), Japanese WAKU (bound; the native equivalents are あるいは, written 或いは, and または), and Vietnamese hoặc (verified on hvdic), which is the ordinary everyday word for "or" (alongside hay in questions), so here the Sino-Vietnamese reading has displaced any native equivalent. The Japanese and Korean fields above hold only the Sino-Xenic bound readings, per vault convention.

或 (ㄏㄛㄎ/hog/혹) — MC 匣母 (ɣ) + 德韻 (wək), entering tone. The character 或 is the graphic origin of [[国]] (country; state): per the character page it is a 会意 of 戈 "weapon" and 囗 "territory" over 一 "ground", a bounded region defended by a weapon, and 國 later added the outer 囗 to disambiguate. The "or; some" sense arose by borrowing; Wiktionary additionally derives the word from Old Chinese 有 "there is" plus a distributive suffix *-k, cognate with 又 "again". The initial ɣ devoices to ㄏ in Dan'a'yo, as in Korean ㅎ, and the entering-tone -k is kept as ㄎ, as in Korean ㄱ, Cantonese and Vietnamese; Mandarin and Japanese lose it.

1. or (disjunctive conjunction): "A or B".
2. perhaps; some (secondary, classical; realized in Dan'a'yo through 或者, 間或, 或然).
