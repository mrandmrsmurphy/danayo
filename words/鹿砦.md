## Notes
- [鹿 (char)](../characters/鹿%20(char).md) "deer" + [砦](../characters/砦.md) "fort, stockade" — legitimizing note: 砦's own `stand_in` is [[鹿砦]] itself, already annotated in its own citation; 鹿's own `stand_in` is 鹿 itself, so transitivity fails — no `#cranberry`.

鹿砦 (lùzhài) is an abatis — a defensive barricade of felled trees with sharpened branches pointed outward, named for resembling a deer's antlers. Cantonese luk6zaai6, Japanese ろくさい, Korean 록채, and Sino-Vietnamese lộc trại are all standard, fully compositional readings.
