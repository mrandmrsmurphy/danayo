## Etymology
- [辦](../characters/辦.md) "manage, run" + [公 (char)](../characters/公%20(char).md) "sir"

## Notes
Mandarin bàngōng, cantonese baan6 gung1, and korean 판공 are all directly attested standard readings. Japanese べんこう and vietnamese biện công are both honest compositional readings (辦's own BEN/biện + 公's own KOU/công); no independently attested loanword was found in either language (modern Japanese instead uses native phrasing like 仕事をする for "do business"). `办公` is the standard simplified-character spelling variant (辦→办), correctly listed as an alias.
