## Notes
Mandarin bàngōng, cantonese baan6 gung1, and korean 판공 are all directly attested standard readings. Japanese べんこう and vietnamese biện công are both honest compositional readings (辦's own BEN/biện + 公's own KOU/công); no independently attested loanword was found in either language (modern Japanese instead uses native phrasing like 仕事をする for "do business"). `办公` is the standard simplified-character spelling variant (辦→办), correctly listed as an alias. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 반공 diverges from Sino-Korean 판공 in the first syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄋㄍㄛㄫ unique among both words and characters).
