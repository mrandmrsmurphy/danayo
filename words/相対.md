## Notes
- [相](../characters/相.md) "mutual" + [対](../characters/対.md) "to face, oppose" — relative, as opposed to absolute (相対性理論, "theory of relativity"); also "to face each other" or "to oppose."

The Existence page lists 相対 as "relative, opposite — existence and identity considered relationally rather than absolutely," and the Mind page as "relative, opposite, relativity," noting that it is "a looser 'opposite' than a strict logical inverse." Dan'a'yo keeps the sense "relative, opposite, relativity," with the relational "as opposed to absolute" sense as its core. It stands against the existing word [絶対](絶対.md) "absolute, total," and is related to [比較](比較.md) "compare." The alias 相對 is the traditional spelling, and the Korean 상대 differs from the Dan'a'yo 諺文 상되 in the second syllable, which is why `kwin` is false. The readings are Mandarin xiāngduì, Cantonese soeng1 deoi3, Japanese sōtai (そうたい) and Sino-Vietnamese tương đối. The Notes' example 相対性理論 and the contrast with 絶対 are standard; I have not checked them against a source.
