## Notes
- [風 (char)](../characters/風%20(char).md) "wind" + [采](../characters/采.md) "form, demeanor" — 風采 is 采's own legitimizing compound (采's `stand_in` points here), but since 風's own `stand_in` is 風 itself instead, transitivity fails and no `#cranberry` tag applies.

風采 (fēngcǎi) is the standard Mandarin word for a person's outward bearing and demeanor, and Cantonese 風采 (fung1coi2) likewise. Japanese ふうさい (fūsai) and Korean 풍채 (pungchae) are both standard terms with the same sense. Sino-Vietnamese phong thái is likewise standard and very commonly used, covering not just outward appearance but overall manner, attitude, and style.
