## Notes
- [定](../characters/定.md) "to fix, settle" + [居](../characters/居.md) "to dwell" — no stand-in relationship applies (定's own `stand_in` is [[決定]]; 居's own is [[居所]]).

定居 means to settle down, to take up permanent residence — including the "settle new land, colonize" sense that sits alongside [[植民地]] above. Mandarin dìngjū is real and standard; Vietnamese định cư is likewise real and standard, matching exactly. Japanese and Korean, however, don't really use this compound — native usage prefers 定住 (teijū) and 정착 (jeongchak) instead, both built on different roots. Japanese ていきょ and Korean 정거 are given here as the mechanical, compositional readings rather than living native forms, the same treatment [[約会]] gives Japanese デート.

Cantonese ding6 geoi1 is likewise compositional. `kwin` is false: 定 is `kwin: true` but 居 is `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄫㄍㄧ unique across both words and characters).
