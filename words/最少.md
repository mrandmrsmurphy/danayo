## Notes
- [最 (char)](../characters/最%20(char).md) "most" + [少 (char)](../characters/少%20(char).md) "few"

最少 is the least, the fewest. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 最少 (Mandarin zuìshǎo, Cantonese zeoi3 siu2) as "the least; the minimum; lower limit" and Japanese さいしょう as "small; little; few" and "the youngest". It lists Korean 최소 and Vietnamese entries. It is the pole opposite [最](最.md), most, and [少](少.md), few.
