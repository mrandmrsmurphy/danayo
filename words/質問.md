## Notes
- No cranberry (問's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 質's own is [[質素]]) — transitivity fails, though 問 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry by this word. **Found and fixed two real bugs**: `羅馬字` had jitmun (t-final), mismatching 質's real d-final reading (jid) — `諺文`/`注音` had already stayed correct; `cantonese` held "zhíwèn," garbled Mandarin-pinyin-like text rather than a real Cantonese reading — corrected to zat1man6 (concatenation of each constituent's own reading). Filled blank vietnamese (chất vấn, standard attested term). Removed blank hsk_level/swadesh/aliases, added simplified alias 质问. No homophones.
