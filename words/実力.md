## Etymology
- [実](../characters/実.md) "true, accurate" + [力 (char)](../characters/力%20(char).md) "power, strength"

## Notes
- "Real strength, actual capability" — objective, verified-in-practice ability, as opposed to strength that's merely claimed or theoretical. `vietnamese: thực lực` was already present and is directly attested, carrying exactly that "verified, objective" nuance. Corrected `hsk_level` from `4` to `6` (verified against per-word listings; sources citing lower levels for "shílì" were describing the unrelated word 势力/勢力, "power, influence," not this one).
- Shares its Mandarin pronunciation (*shílì*) with [[実例]] ("concrete example") purely coincidentally — 力 and 例 are both *lì* in Mandarin but diverge sharply in Dan'a'yo (ㄌㄧㄎ vs. ㄌㄝ), so the two share no Dan'a'yo homophony; see [[実例]]'s own Notes for the full comparison.
- `kwin: false` confirmed (実 alone matches Korean, 力 doesn't — AND-rule). No homophones in Dan'a'yo (注音 ㄙㄧㄊㄌㄧㄎ unique among both words and characters).
