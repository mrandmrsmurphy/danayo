## Notes
- [苔](../characters/苔%20(char).md) "moss" + [原](../characters/原.md) "plain, field" — "moss plain," the tundra. Their own `stand_in` values: 苔's is itself; 原's is [[原始]]; none points here, so 苔原 is an independent compound.

苔原 is the tundra: the cold, treeless plain where only moss and lichen grow. Mandarin táiyuán and Cantonese toi4 jyun4 are the ordinary words, and Vietnamese đài nguyên is the same idea from the Chinese. Japanese says ツンドラ and Korean 툰드라, so those two fields hold the character readings. Compare [[苔]] (moss) and [[草原]] (grassland).
