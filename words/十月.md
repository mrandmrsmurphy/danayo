## Notes
- [十 (char)](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten(th)" + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — literally "tenth month."

十月 is the standard Gregorian-calendar name for October, formed the same transparent "number + 月" way as every other month in this vault's calendar vocabulary (compare [九月](九月.md) "September," [十一月](十一月.md) "November"). It's distinct from [戌月](戌月.md) ("dog month"), the corresponding month of the older lunar/zodiacal calendar, which does not align neatly with October on the solar calendar.

Mandarin shíyuè, Cantonese sap6 jyut6, and Korean 시월 (siwol) are all standard, fully compositional readings — Korean 시월 is a known irregular contraction of 십월, the same pattern seen in 유월 for 六月, and is the attested standard form. Japanese じゅうがつ (jūgatsu) is a fully Sino-Japanese compound (十 jū + 月 gatsu). Vietnamese tháng mười is a native construction ("month ten"), not a Sino-Vietnamese compound — the everyday way all Vietnamese month names are formed.
