## Notes
- [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "gold, metal" + [属](../characters/属.md) "category, class" — "metal."

Mandarin jīnshǔ, cantonese gam1 suk6, japanese きんぞく, korean 금속, and vietnamese kim thuộc are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. `金屬` is the standard traditional-character spelling variant (属→屬), correctly listed as an alias.

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 金's `stand_in` (金 itself) nor 属's own `stand_in` points to this compound.
