## Notes
- [罷](../characters/罷.md) "stop, dismiss" + [工](../characters/工.md) "work" — strike.

罷工 is a strike: workers collectively refusing to work as a protest or to win demands. It is "stop-work." Mandarin bàgōng, Cantonese baa6 gung1, Japanese ひこう and Vietnamese bãi công are confirmed. Korean uses 파업 (罷業), which is not confirmed here, so `korean` is left blank. It sits with [[罷免]] (to dismiss from office), which shares the character 罷 "to stop." It differs from [[失業]] (unemployment), which is not by choice.
