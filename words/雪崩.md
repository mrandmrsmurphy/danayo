## Notes
- [雪 (char)](../characters/雪%20(char).md) "snow" + [崩 (char)](../characters/崩%20(char).md) "crumble, disintegrate, die, fall apart"

雪崩 means an avalanche. Japanese 雪崩 is a classic example of 熟字訓 (jukujikun) — the whole compound is read なだれ (nadare), a dedicated native reading that doesn't decompose into either character's own on'yomi (there is no everyday せっぽう reading). Mandarin 雪崩/雪崩 (xuěbēng), Cantonese 雪崩 (syut3bang1), and Sino-Vietnamese tuyết băng are all compositional. Korean 설붕 (seolbung) is the attested Sino-Korean compositional form, though the native/mixed 눈사태 ("snow disaster") is considerably more common in everyday speech.
