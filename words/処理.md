## Notes
- [処 (char)](../characters/処%20(char).md) "to deal with" (the verbal sense, as in [処決](処決.md) and [処分](処分.md)) + [理](../characters/理.md) "to put in order, manage"

処理 is "to handle, to deal with, to process": to take a matter, a material or a batch of data and work it through to a result. It is attested in all four daughter languages: Mandarin chǔlǐ and Cantonese cyu2 lei5, Japanese しょり, Korean 처리 and Vietnamese xử lí. Wiktionary's 處理 entry gives three senses: "to handle a matter", "to treat with a special process" (sewage treatment, waste-gas treatment) and "to punish". The Japanese noun and verb add "processing", as in 画像処理, image processing; that is the sense behind [[中央処理器]], the CPU.

The word fills a gap among its neighbours rather than duplicating them. [管理](管理.md) is to supervise or administer, with an ongoing charge over people or resources. [整理](整理.md) is to straighten, arrange and tidy. [処決](処決.md) is to resolve a case and [処分](処分.md) is to punish, which are the legal senses only. None of them means to deal with something so as to dispose of it, which is the everyday sense of 処理.

In Dan'a'yo 処 is the noun for "place", and its verbal sense survives only inside compounds, so 処理 is the compound that carries "to deal with" in a general sense. The Dan'a'yo readings follow the character pages (処 ㄑㄛ, 理 ㄌㄧ); `kwin` is false because 초리 differs from the Korean 처리.
