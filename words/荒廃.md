## Notes
- [荒](../characters/荒.md) "laid waste" + [廃](../characters/廃.md) "abrogate, terminate, discard"

荒廃 means to fall into ruin: to be laid waste, dilapidated or neglected. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 荒廢 (an alias; Mandarin huāngfèi, Cantonese fong1 fai3) as "to leave land uncultivated; to abandon a field", "to become wasted; to fall into disuse" and "to waste; to neglect", and Japanese こうはい as "dilapidation, ruin" and "to be dilapidated, fall into ruin". It has no Korean entry, so Korean 황폐 here is built from the characters.

[[荒]] has boundedness 90 and cannot stand alone in Dan'a'yo; this word serves as its legitimizer, carrying the same meaning as the bare character. The ruin itself is a [[廃墟]].
