## Notes
- "Peaceful, quiet, steady" — a smooth, untroubled state, as in an uneventful life or an undisturbed sleep. Filled blank `pos: 性詞` and `korean: 안온` (compositional, matching the word's own 諺文 안온 exactly). **Removed the previously-stored `hsk_level: 4`**: neither Mandarin 安稳 nor traditional 安穩 turned up as a standalone-listed entry in any standard HSK vocabulary tier during a search — this reads as a fairly advanced, literary-register word (well past the everyday level `4` would imply), so the old value looks like an unverified guess rather than an attested classification; left the field absent rather than substituting another unverified number.
- `vietnamese: an ổn` was already present and is directly attested, a common everyday Vietnamese adjective ("things are settled/fine," as in mọi việc đã an ổn).
- Added the missing `kwin: true`: both constituents individually carry `kwin: true` on their own character pages (安's Dan'a'yo reading matches Sino-Korean 안, 穏's matches 온), so the AND-rule gives `true` for the compound. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄋ·ㄛㄋ unique among both words and characters).
