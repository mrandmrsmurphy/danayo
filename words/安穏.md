## Etymology
- [安](../characters/安.md) "peaceful" + [穏](../characters/穏.md) "calm"

## Notes
- "Peaceful, quiet, steady" — a smooth, untroubled state, as in an uneventful life or an undisturbed sleep. Neither Mandarin 安稳 nor traditional 安穩 appears as a standalone entry in any standard HSK vocabulary tier, so no `hsk_level` is set; this reads as a fairly advanced, literary-register word.
- `vietnamese: an ổn` is directly attested, a common everyday Vietnamese adjective ("things are settled/fine," as in mọi việc đã an ổn).
