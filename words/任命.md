## Notes
- [任](../characters/任.md) "trust" + [命](../characters/命.md) "order"

任命 is to appoint to an office. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 任命 (Mandarin rènmìng, Cantonese jam6 ming6) as "to appoint; to assign a job; to assign a role" and, literary, "to resign oneself to fate" and "to receive instructions", and Japanese にんめい as an appointment, assignment or installation of a person to an office. The Korean field gives the North Korean form 림명 built from the readings of the two characters.
