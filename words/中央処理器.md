## Notes
- [中](../characters/中.md) "center" + [央](../characters/央.md) "center" + [処](../characters/処%20(char).md) "handle" + [理](../characters/理.md) "reason" + [器](../characters/器.md) "vessel" — "central processing device." Their own `stand_in` values: 中's is [[中心]]; 央's is [[中央]]; 処's is itself; 理's is [[理由]]; 器's is [[容器]]; none points here, so 中央処理器 is an independent compound.

中央処理器 is a CPU, the part of a computer that carries out instructions. Mandarin zhōngyāng chǔlǐqì and Cantonese zung1 joeng1 cyu2 lei5 hei3 are the ordinary Chinese words. Japanese says 中央処理装置 (ちゅうおうしょりそうち) and Korean 중앙처리장치, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. It is built on [[中央]] (center), [[処理]] (to handle) and [[器]] (device), in the same way as [[机器]] (machine). Compare [[電脳]] (computer).
