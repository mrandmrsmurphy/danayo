## Notes
- [救](../characters/救.md) "rescue" + [援](../characters/援.md) "assist" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [救](../characters/救.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (援's own stand-in is [[援手]]).

救援 means to rescue or relieve.

Mandarin jiùyuán, Cantonese gau3 wun4, Japanese きゅうえん, Korean 구원, and Vietnamese cứu vin are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼜·ㄛㄋ unique across both words and characters).
