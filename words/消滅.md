## Notes
- [消 (char)](../characters/消%20(char).md) "vanish, die out" + [滅 (char)](../characters/滅%20(char).md) "wipe out" — no stand-in relationship applies (消's own stand-in is [[消]], 滅's is [[滅]]).

消滅 is "to vanish; to die out; to become extinct," and transitively "to annihilate; to wipe out." According to Wiktionary, Chinese 消滅 (xiāomiè, Cantonese siu1 mit6) is a verb with two senses: transitive "to wipe out; eliminate; exterminate; annihilate; cause (something) to perish," and intransitive "to die out; become extinct; perish." Japanese しょうめつ is a noun and suru-verb (transitive or intransitive) meaning "lapse of a right," "death, extinction" and "vanishing," with the verb senses "to lapse" (of a right), "to die, go extinct" and "to vanish." Korean 소멸 is glossed "elimination; annihilation," and Vietnamese *tiêu diệt* "to eliminate; to annihilate." From memory, not re-checked: the Korean noun is also used intransitively for things ceasing to exist (a right, a species), and hvdic.thivien.net returned no entry for the compound itself, so the Vietnamese reading rests on Wiktionary and the characters' own *tiêu* and *diệt*.

Dan'a'yo takes the compound as a verb that can be either intransitive or transitive, as in Chinese. It is used on [平臥即是正義](../translation/平臥即是正義.md) in the Dan'a'yo rendering of 异化的世界何时消亡 "when will the alienated world die out?," standing in for the Chinese 消亡, which has no counterpart in the other languages; the intransitive "die out" sense is the one needed there.

It differs from its parts and neighbours as follows. [消](消.md) "vanish; die out; melt away" is the plain intransitive word, and [滅](滅.md) "wipe out" the plain destroying one; 消滅 joins the two, so it can go either way. [滅失](滅失.md) is the legal term for the total loss or destruction of property, narrower than 消滅. [寂滅](寂滅.md) is the Buddhist technical term for nirvana, and does not mean annihilation in the ordinary sense. [隠滅](隠滅.md) is to destroy or make vanish without trace, especially evidence. [死亡](死亡.md) is the formal and clinical word for a person or animal dying, whereas 消滅 is for things, species, rights and abstractions ceasing to exist. [消耗](消耗.md) "consume; deplete" is a gradual using up of a resource, while 消滅 is a thorough ceasing to exist.

1. to vanish; to die out; to become extinct (intransitive)
2. to wipe out; to annihilate (transitive)

No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼄ㄨ·ㄇㄝㄊ unique across both words and characters).
