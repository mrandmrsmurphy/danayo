## Notes
- [聖](../characters/聖.md) "holy, sacred" + [像](../characters/像.md) "image, statue" — "holy image," an icon. Their own `stand_in` values: 聖's is [[神聖]]; 像's is [[彫像]]; none points here, so 聖像 is an independent compound.

聖像 is an icon: a sacred image of Christ, Mary or a saint. Mandarin shèngxiàng, Cantonese sing3 zoeng6, Japanese せいぞう and Korean 성상 all use it, and Vietnamese thánh tượng does too. It is the sacred image, where [[偶像]] is an idol. Compare [[聖人]] (saint) and [[崇拝]] (worship).
