## Notes
- [分 (char)](../characters/分%20(char).md) "to divide" + [類](../characters/類.md) "category, kind" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study" — taxonomy, the science of finding, describing, classifying, and naming organisms (or, by extension, any system of classification).

The Animals page opens its Taxonomy (分類学) section, "the eight-rank hierarchy," with this word, glossed "taxonomy, classification science," before the rank words (域階, 界階, 門階 and so on); the Knowledge page notes that "the science of it is 分類学," where the act of sorting into kinds is [分類](分類.md) "to classify" and the kinds themselves are [種類](種類.md). Dan'a'yo keeps the sense "taxonomy, the science of classification," which is the general discipline and not only the biological one. The Korean 분류학 differs from the Dan'a'yo 諺文 분뤼학 in the second syllable's vowel, so `kwin` is false. The readings are Mandarin fēnlèixué, Cantonese fan1 leoi6 hok6, Japanese bunruigaku (ぶんるいがく) and Vietnamese phân loại học. The alias 分類學 is the traditional spelling.
