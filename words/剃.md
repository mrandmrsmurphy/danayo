## Notes
- [剃 (char)](../characters/剃%20(char).md) "shave"

剃 combines [[刀]] ("knife") with phonetic [[弟]]. It means "to shave" (hair, beard) — 剃頭 (tìtóu, "to shave the head"), and in the Buddhist context specifically, 剃髮 (tìfà, "to shave one's head and take monastic vows," the tonsure ritual marking ordination).

Mandarin tì, Cantonese tai3, and Japanese てい (tei, on'yomi, rare outside classical/formal contexts) all preserve the sense. Korean 체 (che) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese thế is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in thế đầu ("to shave the head") and thế phát (剃髮, "to take the tonsure, become a Buddhist monk").
