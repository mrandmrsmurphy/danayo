## Notes
- [剃 (char)](../characters/剃%20(char).md) "shave"

剃 combines [[刀]] ("knife") with phonetic [[弟]]. It means "to shave" (hair, beard) — 剃頭 (tìtóu, "to shave the head"), and in the Buddhist context specifically, 剃髮 (tìfà, "to shave one's head and take monastic vows," the tonsure ritual marking ordination).

Mandarin tì, Cantonese tai3, and Japanese てい (tei, on'yomi, rare outside classical/formal contexts) alongside a rare native reading all preserve the sense. Korean 체 (che) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese thế is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in thế đầu ("to shave the head") and thế phát (剃髮, "to take the tonsure, become a Buddhist monk").

This word is part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read tei/테/ㄊㄝㄧ): [[締]] ("connection") and [[諦]] ("truth [Buddhist]"), both still awaiting their own turn in this sweep — the reciprocal half of this callout will be completed on each when it comes up.
