## Notes
- [済](../characters/済%20(char).md) "cross over" + [州](../characters/州%20(char).md) "province" — the name of the Jeju Island and province of Korea.

済州 (Jeju) is the large island south of the Korean peninsula and the province that covers it, long known as Tamna (耽羅) before the name 濟州 was adopted under Goryeo (roughly 1105, from memory, not re-checked). It was a province of Joseon and has been 濟州特別自治道 (Jeju Special Self-Governing Province) since July 2006; the capital is the city of Jeju. The traditional form 濟州 is what the Wiktionaries record, and the variant is an alias of the page name because 済 is the parent form on its character page. "Province across the water" is the usual reading of the characters, 済 meaning "to cross".

English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese Wiktionary all have a 濟州 entry; Japanese Wiktionary lists the Korean place as 濟州/済州 チェジュ (Cheju) beside the on'yomi さいしゅう, which also names a former prefecture in China; Vietnamese Wiktionary gives Tế Châu. The Chinese place Jizhou is a separate set of historical prefectures sharing the spelling.
