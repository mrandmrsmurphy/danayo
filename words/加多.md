## Notes
- [加](../characters/加.md) "to add" + [多 (char)](../characters/多%20(char).md) "many, much" — "to add more; to augment, increase."

**加多** (*jiāduō*) is a transparent verb compound meaning "to increase the quantity of, to add more of" — combining 加 ("to add to") with 多 ("many, much") to give the resulting sense "add-to-make-more." It is an ordinary Mandarin verb (Wiktionary glosses it "to add; to augment; to increase; to raise," lists 減少 as its antonym, and gives the literary near-synonyms 增益 and 附益). Wiktionary has no entries for it in Cantonese, Japanese, Korean or Vietnamese, so the readings recorded here for those languages are the literal readings of the two characters, with the meaning following from them.

In Dan'a'yo, 加多 additionally serves as the phonetic-semantic basis for **[[加多金]]** ("gadolinium"): 加多 approximates the *gado-* of "gadolinium" while its literal sense ("adding more, increasing") supplies an internally meaningful — if chemically arbitrary — gloss, with [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) ("metal") marking the element's category. See [[加多金]] for the full account of that formation.

The Dimensions page lists it as "to add, augment, increase," and the Physical page notes the distinction from [添加](添加.md): 添加 is the act of adding in, putting an ingredient or a further item into something already there (添加剤, "an additive"), while 加多 is making something larger by adding to it. That is the Dan'a'yo sense kept here, increase by addition. A related word is [加算](加算.md) "to add (in arithmetic)." The Korean field reads 가다, the plain readings of 加 and 多.
