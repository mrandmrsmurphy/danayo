## Notes
- [呼](../characters/呼%20(char).md) "call, shout" + [和](../characters/和.md) "peaceful, harmonious" + [浩](../characters/浩.md) "great, vast" + [特](../characters/特.md) "special" — phonetic spelling of Hohhot. No stand-in relationship applies (呼 is its own stand-in; 和's is [[和平]]; 浩's is [[浩大]]; 特's is [[特別]]).

呼和浩特 (Hohhot) is the capital of the Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region in northern China. Per Wiktionary the Chinese name is borrowed from Mongolian Kökeqota (ᠬᠥᠬᠡᠬᠣᠲᠠ), literally "blue city", so the characters are phonetic and carry no meaning of their own. Wiktionary lists the short form 呼市, the old name 歸綏 and the historical forms 厚和豪特 and 庫庫和屯.

English Wiktionary has no Japanese, Korean or Vietnamese entry for 呼和浩特. The Japanese field holds フフホト, the name used in Japanese (Japanese Wikipedia, フフホト市), which is not a Sino-Japanese reading; the Korean field holds 호화호득, derived from the character pages (North Korean readings; not attested; the character 特 is recorded there as 득), and the usual South Korean name today is 후허하오터 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese Hô Hòa Hạo Đặc is the Hán-Việt form given on Vietnamese Wikipedia. Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese entry; Japanese and Vietnamese Wikipedia for the other languages. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄛㄏ⺢ㄏㄚㄨㄉㄜㄎ unique across both words and characters).
