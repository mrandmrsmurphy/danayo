## Notes
- [貪 (char)](../characters/貪%20(char).md) "greedy" is separately legitimized by [[貪]] itself. [林 (char)](../characters/林%20(char).md) "grove" is used here as the documented Dan'a'yo stand-in for its alias 婪 ("avaricious"). 林's own `stand_in` field is 林 itself, so this isn't a cranberry despite the borrowed-alias relationship.

Mandarin tānlán, cantonese taam1laam4, japanese たんらん, korean 탐람, and vietnamese tham lam all cite 婪's own real readings (lán/laam4/らん/람/lam, not 林's own lín/lam4/りん/림/lâm) — legitimate for the borrowed-alias sense, matching the [[豊尭]] precedent.
## Etymology
- [貪 (char)](../characters/貪%20(char).md) "greedy" + [林 (char)](../characters/林%20(char).md) "grove" (used here as the Dan'a'yo stand-in for its alias 婪, "avaricious" — phonetic component shared, and 婪 itself carries a secondary Japanese on-reading りん borrowed from 林)
