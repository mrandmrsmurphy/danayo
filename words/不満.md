## Notes
- [不 (char)](../characters/不%20(char).md) "not" + [満 (char)](../characters/満%20(char).md) "full" — the negation of 満; "not full, unsatisfied," hence dissatisfaction, resentment, or a felt lack.

Mandarin bùmǎn, Cantonese bat1 mun5, Japanese ふまん, and Sino-Vietnamese bất mãn are all standard compositional readings. Korean 불만 follows the 不→불 assimilation rule already documented on several sibling 不-compounds this stretch, and is additionally the everyday word for "complaint, dissatisfaction."
