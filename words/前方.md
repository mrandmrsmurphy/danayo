## Notes
- [前 (char)](../characters/前%20(char).md) "front (stand-in: itself)" + [方](../characters/方.md) "direction; side (stand-in: [[方向]])"

Wiktionary: Chinese 前方 "front; ahead" and, in the military, "frontline"; Japanese 前方 "the front, the area before one"; Korean 전방 is the hanja form, "front side; (military) frontline"; Vietnamese tiền phương is the Han form, "frontline". Its counterpart is [[後方]].
