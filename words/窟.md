## Notes
- [窟](../characters/窟%20(char).md) "cavern" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 窟 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

窟 names a cave, cavern, or den — a hollow chamber in rock or earth, whether natural or dug out, and by extension a hideout or lair (窟穴, "lair"). It is highly productive: 洞窟 ("cave, cavern"), 巣窟 ("den, hideout, hangout" of criminals or animals).

Mandarin kū, Cantonese fat1, and Korean 굴 (gul) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading あな (ana, "hole") rather than the on-readings クツ/コツ (KUTSU/KOTSU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese quật is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading.
