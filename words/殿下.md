## Notes
- [殿](../characters/殿.md) "hall" + [下 (char)](../characters/下%20(char).md) "below"

殿下 is Your Highness, the form of address for a prince or princess. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 殿下 (Mandarin diànxià, Cantonese din6 haa6) as "Your Highness; His Highness; Her Highness". It lists Japanese でんか, Korean 전하 and Vietnamese entries. Compare [陛下](陛下.md), Your Majesty.
