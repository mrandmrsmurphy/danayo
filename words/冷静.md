## Notes
- [冷 (char)](../characters/冷%20(char).md) "cold" + [静](../characters/静.md) "still"

冷静 is calm and clear-headed: not carried away by feeling, able to think. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin lěngjìng as "sober; calm" and Japanese れいせい as "calm, composed; calmness, composure". Korean 냉정 is the same word, and Cantonese laang5 zing6 matches; the Korean field gives the North Korean form.

It is calm by thought, where [安穏](安穏.md) and [平静](平静.md) are calm of mood. Its steadier kin under pressure is [沈着](沈着.md), and the cold-hearted side is [冷淡](冷淡.md).
