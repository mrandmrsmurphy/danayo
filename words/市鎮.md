## Notes
- [市](../characters/市.md) "city, market" + [鎮](../characters/鎮%20(char).md) "town" — "market town." Their own `stand_in` values: 市's is [[都市]]; 鎮's is itself; none points here, so 市鎮 is an independent compound.

市鎮 is a town: a settlement larger than a village ([[里]]) and smaller than a city ([[都市]]). Mandarin shìzhèn, Cantonese si5 zan3 and Vietnamese thị trấn use it. Japanese uses 町 or 市街 and Korean 읍, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters.
