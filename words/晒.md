## Notes
- [晒](../characters/晒%20(char).md) "expose, dry in the sun, bleach" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [晒](../characters/晒%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

晒 means to expose, dry in the sun, or bleach.

Mandarin shài, Cantonese saai3, Japanese せい, Korean 쇄, and Vietnamese sái are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼘ㄧ unique across both words and characters).
