## Notes
- [正](../characters/正%20(char).md) "correct" + [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "great"

正大 means proper and above board: of behaviour or actions, legitimate and honest. English Wiktionary lists it only for Chinese 正大 (Mandarin zhèngdà), glossed "of behaviour, actions, etc.: proper; appropriate; legitimate; reasonable; above board; upright; honest". For Japanese it lists 正大 only as a male given name. It has no Korean or Vietnamese entry, so Cantonese zing3 daai6, Japanese せいだい, Korean 정대 and Vietnamese chính đại here are built from the characters.

It appears in the chengyu [[公明正大]], open, fair and above board. In Dan'a'yo 正大 is used as a 性詞, a stative adjective. The reversed form 大正 is the name of the Japanese imperial era, 大正, and should not be used in this adjectival sense.
