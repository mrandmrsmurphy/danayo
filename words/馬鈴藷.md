## Notes
- [馬 (char)](../characters/馬%20(char).md) "horse" + [鈴 (char)](../characters/鈴%20(char).md) "bell" + [藷](../characters/藷.md) "potato; yam"

馬鈴藷 is the potato: the starchy tuber of *Solanum tuberosum*, and its plant. The usual spelling is 馬鈴薯, an alias, because 薯 is an alias of 藷. The characters mean "horse bell" potato, from the tuber's round shape. Wiktionary glosses Chinese 馬鈴薯 (Mandarin mǎlíngshǔ, Cantonese maa5 ling4 syu4) as "potato" and also the "air potato", and lists Japanese ばれいしょ for the same word. The Korean field follows the vault's reading of 藷 as 저, as in [甘藷](甘藷.md).

It is a tuber like the [甘藷](甘藷.md), sweet potato, and the [里芋](里芋.md), taro, but a different plant from the [山芋](山芋.md), mountain yam. It is eaten as a vegetable, the [菜蔬](菜蔬.md) of a meal.
