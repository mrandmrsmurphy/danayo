## Notes
- [三 (char)](../characters/三%20(char).md) "three" + [位](../characters/位.md) "position" + [一 (char)](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" + [体](../characters/体.md) "body" — no `#cranberry`: none of the four constituents' own `stand_in` points here.

Mandarin sānwèiyītǐ, Cantonese saam1 wai6 jat1 tai2, Korean 삼위일체, and Sino-Vietnamese tam vị nhất thể are all standard compositional readings. Japanese さんみいったい uses み, a special classical/Buddhist alternate on'yomi for 位 (rather than its ordinary い), specifically in this religious term — the real, standard Japanese reading for "Trinity," not a bug. `kwin` is false (AND-rule: 三 true, 位/一/体 all false — any single false makes the whole product false).
