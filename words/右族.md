## Notes
- [右](../characters/右.md) "right (side)" + [族](../characters/族.md) "tribe, clan" — "the Right Tribe, Benjamin." Their own `stand_in` values: 右's is [[右側]]; 族's is [[家族]]; none points here, so 右族 is an independent compound.

右族 is the tribe of Benjamin, Rachel's second son and Jacob's youngest, whose name means "son of the right hand," renamed by Jacob from Ben-oni (Genesis 35:18). It is a Dan'a'yo **neologism**, a meaning-based name, "Right Tribe." Compare [[港族]] (Zebulun) and [[双族]] (Joseph). See [[基督敎]].

- Hebrew name: בִּנְיָמִין (Binyamin) (注音 ㄅㄧㄋ·⼘ㄇㄧㄋ).

- See [基督敎](../lexipedia/基督敎.md).
