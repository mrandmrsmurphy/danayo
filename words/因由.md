## Notes
- [因](../characters/因.md) "cause, reason" + [由 (char)](../characters/由%20(char).md) "from, since" — an independent compound; neither character's own `stand_in` points here (因's is `原因`, 由's is bare `由`)

因由 means the reason or the cause: a noun for the grounds or origin of a matter (事情的因由, "the cause of an affair"; 說明因由, "explain the reason"), sometimes with the Buddhist sense of a predestined connection. It is a synonym of 原因, 理由 and 縁故, closest to 原因 in register and function: a plain noun, not a connective.

Mandarin yīnyóu, Cantonese jan1 jau4, Japanese いんゆ, Korean 인유 (inyu), and Vietnamese nhân do all name "the reason, the cause" as a noun. Vietnamese has close synonyms lý do, nguyên nhân and duyên cớ. A cluster of related words exists across the sphere built from overlapping roots: 因為 and 由於/由于 (Mandarin, more conjunction-like "because"), 原因 (nguyên + nhân), and 理由 (lý + do). The Korean everyday phrase 때문에 ("because of") is native and not a reading of this word.
