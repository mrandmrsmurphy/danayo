## Notes
- [乳](../characters/乳.md) "milk" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color"

乳色 is the color of fresh milk or cream: a warm, slightly yellowish off-white. It occupies the space between pure white (白色) and ivory (象牙色), and is the recommended Dan'a'yo term for beige and cream tones. The character 乳's radical is 乙 (the second Heavenly Stem), though the character's phonetic component is 孚; its primary meanings are milk, breast, and suckling, making the color term a direct extension of the material's appearance.

Mandarin rǔsè, Cantonese jyu5 sik1, Japanese にゅうしょく, and Korean 유색 are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Vietnamese content note**: nhũ sắc is a compositionally sound derivation (乳's own nhũ + 色's own sắc), but this session's search budget is exhausted, so its independent real-world attestation as a fixed Vietnamese color term (as opposed to native alternatives like màu kem, "cream color," or trắng sữa, "milk white") couldn't be verified — presented as a defensible derivation pending a future check.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 누식 diverges from Sino-Korean 유색, since both 乳 and 色 independently carry `kwin: false` on their own character pages.
