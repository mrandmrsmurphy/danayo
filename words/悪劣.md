## Notes
- [悪](../characters/悪%20(char).md) "bad, evil" + [劣](../characters/劣.md) "inferior, bad" — no stand-in relationship applies (悪's own stand-in is itself; 劣's own is itself).

悪劣 means inferior, vile, or nasty — a near-synonymous doubling of two characters both meaning "bad." Not to be confused with the reversed-order synonym 劣悪, which has no vault page of its own.

Mandarin èliè, Cantonese ok3 lyut3, Japanese あくれつ, Korean 악렬, and Vietnamese ác liệt are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `cantonese`/`japanese`/`korean` had all been contaminated with the reversed-order sibling word 劣悪's real readings (ngok3 lyut3, れつあく, 열악) — corrected to this word's own compositional forms. Removed 劣悪 from `aliases` (a distinct reversed-order synonym, not an orthographic variant). Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄎㄌㄝㄊ unique across both words and characters).
