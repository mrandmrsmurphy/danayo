## Notes
- [悪](../characters/悪%20(char).md) "bad, evil" + [劣](../characters/劣.md) "inferior, bad" — no stand-in relationship applies (悪's own stand-in is itself; 劣's own is itself).

悪劣 means inferior, vile, or nasty — a near-synonymous doubling of two characters both meaning "bad." Not to be confused with the reversed-order synonym 劣悪, which has no vault page of its own.

Mandarin èliè, Cantonese ok3 lyut3, Japanese あくれつ, Korean 악렬, and Vietnamese ác liệt are all standard, fully compositional readings.
