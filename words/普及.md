## Notes
- [普](../characters/普.md) "universal; common; everywhere" + [及 (char)](../characters/及%20(char).md) "reach out to"

普及 means to spread widely: to become common, to reach everyone, and to popularize. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 普及 (Mandarin pǔjí, Cantonese pou2 kap6) as "to propagate; to spread; to diffuse", "to popularise; to universalise; to normalize" (its example: education is at the root of the popularization of science and democracy), and "catered for the masses; popularised". Japanese ふきゅう is "diffusion", with a verb "spread, diffuse, propagate" (the Internet has spread widely). Korean 보급 is "spread, diffusion, spreading" (the spread of high-speed internet) and also "supply". It has no Vietnamese entry, so Vietnamese phổ cập here is built from the characters.

The two characters are 普, universal, and 及, to reach. It is the welcome spread of something to everyone, set beside [[波及]], the spread of an effect.
