## Notes
- [散](../characters/散.md) "scatter" + [歩](../characters/歩.md) "walk; step; pace" — `characters/歩.md`'s own `stand_in` points to this word (散歩), meaning 歩 is a bound morpheme that never appears independently outside this compound. 散's own `stand_in` is [[散布]], the homophone partner.

Mandarin sànbù, Cantonese saan3 bou6, Japanese さんぽ, Korean 산보, and Vietnamese tản bộ are the standard readings. Korean 산보 is the Sino-Korean reading of this compound and is the everyday word for "a walk" in North Korea; South Korea has shifted toward the near-synonym 산책 (散策, "a walking-stick stroll," built on 策 and not 歩), which is slightly more literary and suggests a longer distance. Both are real, current Korean words. The everyday native Vietnamese phrase is đi dạo, "to go for a walk."

It is an exact Dan'a'yo homophone of [[散布]] (both sanbo/산보/ㄙㄚㄋㄅㄛ), even though Mandarin and Japanese distinguish them (sànbù/さんぽ versus sànbù/さんぷ).
