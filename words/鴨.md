## Notes
- [鴨](../characters/鴨%20(char).md) "duck" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [鴨](../characters/鴨%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

鴨 means duck.

Mandarin yā, Korean 압, and Vietnamese áp are all standard, fully compositional readings. Cantonese aap3 (vs. its homophone 押's aat3) and Japanese おう (the Sino-Japanese on-reading, corrected from the garbled/non-standard あふ — native かも is the everyday word for the animal) reflect real sub-syllable distinctions that Dan'a'yo's coarser 注音 merges. Fixed redundant `品詞` field. Homophone of [[押]] "mortgage" (both 注音 ㄚㄆ) — a genuine coincidence between two unrelated single-character words.
