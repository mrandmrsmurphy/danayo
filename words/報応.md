## Notes
- [報 (char)](../characters/報%20(char).md) "repay" + [応 (char)](../characters/応%20(char).md) "respond" — "retribution, karmic retribution." Mandarin/this word's title prefers the 報応 ordering, while Japanese and Korean both prefer the reversed 応報/응보 (因果応報, "karmic cause and retribution"), already reflected in this page's own `aliases`. `vietnamese: báo ứng` is a directly attested, extremely common standard Vietnamese Buddhist/folk term (nhân quả báo ứng, "cause and karmic retribution"). **Real content bug found and fixed on [[応 (char)]]'s own page**: its `vietnamese` field crammed three readings into one malformed list item — split into separate entries. Filled blank `pos: 名詞`. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 밧잉 diverges from Sino-Korean 응보 entirely (different character order and readings). No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄨㄧㄫ unique among both words and characters).
