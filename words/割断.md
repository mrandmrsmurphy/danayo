## Notes
- [割 (char)](../characters/割%20(char).md) "cut" + [断](../characters/断.md) "cut off"
- This word is the viable form of [断](../characters/断.md): the character's meaning (to cut off) is identical, but 断 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 割断 is its `stand_in` compound.

割断 means "to cut off, to cut through": 割 is to cut and 断 is to sever, so the compound stresses that the cutting is complete and the thing is divided in two. Mandarin gēduàn, Cantonese got3 dyun6 and Korean 할단 (haldan) are the same compound; it is used both literally and figuratively (割断联系, "to sever ties").

Dan'a'yo-specific sense: no shift from the shared one. It sits in the Physical domain's cutting group beside [[割]] (to cut or divide in general), [[斬]] (to chop, often violently) and [[刈]] (to mow), as the word that makes the result explicit: cut through and separated.
