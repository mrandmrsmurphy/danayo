## Notes
- [砲](../characters/砲.md) "cannon" + [兵](../characters/兵.md) "soldier" — "cannon soldier." 砲's own `stand_in` is [[大砲]] and 兵's is [[兵士]]; neither points here, so 砲兵 is an independent compound.

砲兵 is artillery: the arm of service that fires cannon, and the soldier who serves it. Mandarin pàobīng and Cantonese paau3 bing1 are usually written 炮兵, which is kept as an alias; Japanese ほうへい, Korean 포병 (also "gunner, artillery unit") and Vietnamese pháo binh use 砲兵. The weapon itself is [[大砲]], distinct from [[銃]], the handheld firearm.
