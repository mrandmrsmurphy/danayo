## Notes
- [階](../characters/階.md) "stairs, storey" + [段](../characters/段.md) "step, section" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes 階 as a bound character (段's own stand-in is [[段]] itself; transitivity fails, so no `#cranberry`).

階段 shows a genuine semantic split across the CJKV sphere. Japanese 階段 (かいだん) and Korean 계단 (gyedan) both keep the literal, physical sense: a staircase or flight of stairs. Mandarin 階段/阶段 (jiēduàn) and Sino-Vietnamese giai đoạn have instead generalized to the dominant abstract sense of "stage, phase, period" (of a process, project, or life) — in Mandarin, the everyday word for physical stairs is instead 樓梯/楼梯 (lóutī). Both senses ultimately derive from the same image: discrete steps that mark progress upward, whether up a physical staircase or through stages of a process.
