## Notes
- [訓 (char)](../characters/訓%20(char).md) "interpret; translate; explicate" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; standalone form, 訓 means to explain or interpret a text, particularly to render Classical Chinese into vernacular equivalents (as in 訓読み, kun'yomi, the native-reading gloss system this very word's name describes).

Mandarin xùn, Cantonese fan3, and Korean 훈 (hun) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese has two attested on'yomi (KUN, the everyday reading used in 訓読み itself, and the rarer KIN); the native kun'yomi おし(える)/よ(む) (oshi(eru)/yo(mu), "to teach"/"to read," already 訓's own stored `japanese_native` values) cover its broader "teach, instruct" sense. Vietnamese huấn is the standard Hán Việt reading, well attested in huấn luyện ("to train") and giáo huấn ("instruction, teaching").
