## Notes
- [肋](../characters/肋.md) "rib" + [骨 (char)](../characters/骨%20(char).md) "bone" — 肋's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 骨's own is [[骨]] itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 肋骨 legitimizes 肋 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 骨 is individually `kwin: false`.

肋骨 names a rib bone.

Mandarin lèigǔ, Cantonese lak6 gwat1, Japanese ろっこつ (rokkotsu), and Korean 륵골 (reukgol) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 肋骨 is itself a common attested word for "rib" in all four languages. Vietnamese lặc cốt is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. **Fixed two real bugs**: `羅馬字` had luggod (mismatching 肋's real -ǝ- vowel reading, lǝg — 諺文/注音 had already been correct) — corrected; `kwin` was true, but the AND-rule requires false (骨 is individually `kwin: false`) — corrected. Filled blank cantonese/vietnamese. No homophones.

**`kwin` update (2026-10-09):** `kwin` is now true. The Notes above may describe the ㄷ/ㄹ difference between the Dan'a'yo 諺文 and the Korean reading as a mismatch; under the vault's standing rule a Korean final ㄹ counts as equal to Dan'a'yo's final ㄷ for this comparison, so such a difference alone does not make `kwin` false.
