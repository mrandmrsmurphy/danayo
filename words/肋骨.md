## Notes
- [肋](../characters/肋.md) "rib" + [骨 (char)](../characters/骨%20(char).md) "bone" — 肋's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 骨's own is [[骨]] itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 肋骨 legitimizes 肋 alone.

肋骨 names a rib bone.

Mandarin lèigǔ, Cantonese lak6 gwat1, Japanese ろっこつ (rokkotsu), and Korean 륵골 (reukgol) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 肋骨 is itself a common attested word for "rib" in all four languages. Vietnamese lặc cốt is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading.
