## Notes
- [材](../characters/材.md) "material, stuff, talent" + [木](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree, bush" — no stand-in relationship applies (材's own stand-in is [[材料]]; 木's own is itself).

材木 means lumber or timber.

Mandarin cáimù, Cantonese coi4 muk6, Japanese ざいもく, Korean 재목, and Vietnamese tài mộc are all standard, fully compositional readings (matching 材's and 木's own stored citations exactly).
