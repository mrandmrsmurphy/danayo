## Notes
- [材](../characters/材.md) "material, stuff, talent" + [木](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree, bush" — no stand-in relationship applies (材's own stand-in is [[材料]]; 木's own is itself).

材木 means lumber or timber.

Mandarin cáimù, Cantonese coi4 muk6, Japanese ざいもく, Korean 재목, and Vietnamese tài mộc are all standard, fully compositional readings (matching 材's and 木's own stored citations exactly). Fixed severe contamination: `mandarin`/`cantonese`/`japanese` had all been swapped for the reversed-order compound 木材's own readings (mùcái, muk6 coi4, もくざい) rather than 材木's own compositional forms; `korean` held 제목 ("title"), an entirely unrelated word; `vietnamese` held two garbled native descriptive phrases ("bulky item," "shop selling wood") rather than a real translation. Removed `木料`/`木材` from `aliases` — genuine near-synonyms for lumber, but distinct compounds (with a different or reversed character order) rather than orthographic variants of 材木 itself. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄚㄧㄇㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
