## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [苔 (char)](../characters/苔%20(char).md) "moss"

海苔 is nori: edible seaweed, especially laver dried in sheets. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin hǎitái as "nori, especially in its dried and processed form" and "edible seaweed; sea vegetable", and Japanese のり as "nori, laver, dried seaweed". Cantonese hoi2 toi4 matches, and Korean 해태 is the same word.

It is the seaweed that wraps [寿司](寿司.md), sushi, and it is a food seaweed like [昆布](昆布.md), kelp. Seaweed in general is the [海藻](海藻.md).
