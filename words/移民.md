## Notes
- [移](../characters/移.md) "move; shift" + [民](../characters/民.md) "people"

移民 is a migrant: a person who moves to another country to live, and the act of doing so. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yímín as "to immigrate; to emigrate; to migrate" and "immigrant; emigrant; migrant", and Japanese いみん as "immigrant, emigrant". It also records a Cantonese euphemism for dying, which Dan'a'yo does not take. Vietnamese di dân is the same word, and Korean 이민 and Cantonese ji4 man4 match.

The word does not say which direction, so it covers the person leaving and the person arriving. A settler is a [移住者](移住者.md), and the movement is [移住](移住.md).
