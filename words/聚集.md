## Notes
- [聚](../characters/聚.md) "gather, assemble" + [集](../characters/集.md) "gather, collect" — 聚's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 集's own is [[集合]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 聚集 legitimizes 聚 alone. `kwin: true` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: true`.

聚集 means to gather, assemble, or collect.

Mandarin jùjí, Cantonese zeoi6 zaap6, Japanese しゅうしゅう (shūshū), and Korean 취집 (chwijip) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 聚集 is itself a common attested word for "gather, assemble" in all four languages. Vietnamese tụ tập is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term. Japanese occasionally conflates 聚 and 集 (both meaning "gather"), as they are near-synonymous and graphically similar in cursive forms. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
