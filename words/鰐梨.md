## Notes
- [鰐](../characters/鰐.md) "alligator, crocodile" + [梨 (char)](../characters/梨%20(char).md) "pear" — no `#cranberry`: 鰐's own `stand_in` is [[鰐魚]], 梨's is 梨 itself; neither points here.

Mandarin èlí and Cantonese ngok6lei4 keep the compositional "alligator pear" calque (echoing the historical English name for avocado). Japanese アボカド and Korean 아보카도 are both direct phonetic loanwords from English "avocado," rather than a literal calque or compositional Sino reading. Vietnamese bơ ("butter") is the real, everyday native term — itself borrowed semantically from French *beurre*, referencing the fruit's buttery texture.
