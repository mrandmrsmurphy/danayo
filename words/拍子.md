## Notes
- [拍 (char)](../characters/拍%20(char).md) "clap; beat" + [子](../characters/子.md) "child; thing"

拍子 is the beat or time of music: the regular pulse that the music keeps. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin pāizi as "beat; time" and Japanese ひょうし as "a beat". Korean 박자 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese paak3-2 zi2 matches.

It carries the [旋律](旋律.md), melody, and a single stroke of it is a [拍](拍.md), beat. Clapping to it is [拍手](拍手.md).
