## Etymology
- [邦](../characters/邦.md) "federation, commonwealth" + [畿](../characters/畿.md) "capital district"

## Notes
Mandarin bāngjī, cantonese bong1 gei1, japanese ほうき, and korean 방기 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese bang kì is the honest compositional reading (邦's own bang + 畿's own kì); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found.
