## Etymology
- [邦](../characters/邦.md) "federation, commonwealth" + [畿](../characters/畿.md) "capital district"

## Notes
Mandarin bāngjī, cantonese bong1 gei1, japanese ほうき, and korean 방기 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese bang kì is the honest compositional reading (邦's own bang + 畿's own kì); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 방긔 diverges from Sino-Korean 방기 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄫㄍㄧㄜ unique among both words and characters).

**Found and fixed a missing citation** on 畿.md's own Words list. Citation on 邦.md was already correctly present. Neither 邦's `stand_in` (連邦) nor 畿's own `stand_in` (京畿) points to this compound.
