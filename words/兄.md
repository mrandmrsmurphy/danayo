## Notes
- [兄 (char)](../characters/兄%20(char).md) "older brother" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

兄 is the biological/literal term for "older brother" — a direct sibling only, not extended to cousins or fictive relationships. **Corrected 2026-09-21**: this page briefly (same day) broadened 兄 itself to also mean "older male relative of the same generation," treating [[哥哥]] as simply its reduplicated intimate form — that was wrong. 哥 is not a reduplication of 兄 at all; it's an etymologically separate root (it never surfaces as bare 兄兄, and doesn't appear in the classical sinographic stratum the way 兄 does). 兄 has no reduplicated form of its own. The intimate/informal register for addressing one's own older brother, the broader "fictive older brother" sense (English "bro"), and the extended-kinship "an actual older male relative, cousin included" sense are all carried instead by the separate word [[哥哥]] — see that word's own Notes.
