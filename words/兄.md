## Notes
- [兄 (char)](../characters/兄%20(char).md) "older brother" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

兄 is the classical, formal register for "older brother." **Sense broadened 2026-09-21**, matching real attested usage already documented on [[哥哥]] (Mandarin 大哥, Korean 형/오빠, Vietnamese anh all extend the "older brother" word to any older male of roughly one's own generation, not strictly a literal sibling): 兄 in Dan'a'yo now also covers an older male relative more generally — a cousin, most concretely, partially filling the vault's documented "no word for cousin" gap on [[Kinship]]. [[哥哥]] is the intimate, colloquial address-register counterpart of this same broadened sense, the way [[伯伯]] pairs with [[伯父]] and [[叔叔]] with [[叔父]].
