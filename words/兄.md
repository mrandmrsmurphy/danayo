## Notes
- [兄 (char)](../characters/兄%20(char).md) "older brother" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

兄 is the biological/literal term for "older brother" — a direct sibling only, not extended to cousins or fictive relationships. 兄 has no reduplicated form of its own: 哥 is an etymologically separate root, not a reduplication of 兄, and it never surfaces as bare 兄兄. The intimate and informal register for addressing one's own older brother, the broader "fictive older brother" sense (English "bro"), and the extended-kinship sense of an older male relative, cousins included, are all carried by the separate word [哥哥](哥哥.md) — see that word's own Notes.
