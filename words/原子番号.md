## Notes
- [原](../characters/原.md) "plain, original" + [子](../characters/子.md) "son, child" + [番 (char)](../characters/番%20(char).md) "number, turn" + [号](../characters/号.md) "number, signal" — atomic number.

原子番号 is the atomic number: the number of protons ([[陽子]]) in an atom's nucleus ([[核]]), which fixes its element and its position in the periodic table ([[週期表]]); hydrogen is 1 and oganesson 118. It is [[原子]] "atom" plus [[番号]] "number, code." Japanese げんしばんごう and Korean 원자번호 are the readings of this exact compound. Mandarin uses a different word, 原子序數 (yuánzǐ xùshù, "atomic ordinal number"), and `mandarin` and `cantonese` hold that real word. The mass number is [[質量数]].
