## Notes
- [素](../characters/素.md) "element, plain thing" + [描](../characters/描.md) "sketch" — 描's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 素's own is [[要素]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 素描 legitimizes 描 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 描 is individually `kwin: false`.

素描 names a sketch or drawing — literally "plain depiction," a preliminary or unadorned rendering, typically in pencil or charcoal.

Mandarin sùmiáo, Cantonese sou3 miu4, Japanese そびょう (sobyō), and Korean 소묘 (somyo) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 素描 is itself a common attested word for "sketch" in all four languages. Vietnamese tố miêu is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading, though the more common idiomatic Vietnamese term is instead phác họa. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
