## Notes
- [欄](../characters/欄.md) "railing, handrail" + [杆](../characters/杆%20(char).md) "rod" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [欄](../characters/欄.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 杆's own stand-in is itself.

欄杆 means railing or handrail.

Mandarin lángān, Cantonese laan4 gon1, and Japanese らんかん are all standard, fully compositional readings.
