## Notes
- [安](../characters/安.md) "peaceful" + [養](../characters/養.md) "foster, raise" — the name of Anyang, a city of South Korea south of Seoul.

安養 (Anyang) is a city of Gyeonggi Province ([[京畿]]道) in South Korea, just south of [[漢城]] (Seoul) (from memory, not re-checked). The name reads "peaceful nurture".

English Wiktionary lists 安養 first as a Chinese verb "to foster, to provide care (especially for the elderly)" (Mandarin ānyǎng, Cantonese on1 joeng5) and second as the Korean city (Mandarin ānyǎng); Chinese, Japanese and Korean Wiktionary also have the string. The Japanese reading あんよう is composed from the on'yomi (the Buddhist reading あんにょう of the same string is from memory, not re-checked). Korean 안양 is the spelling in both North and South Korea. Vietnamese An Dưỡng is composed from the character pages; no Vietnamese dictionary entry was found.
