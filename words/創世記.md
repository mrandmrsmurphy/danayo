## Notes
This is the first book of the [[聖経]]. [創](../characters/創.md) "create" + [世](../characters/世.md) "world" + [記](../characters/記.md) "record" — no stand-in relationship applies (創's own stand-in is [[創立]], 世's is [[世界]], 記's is [[記憶]]). **Real bug found and fixed**: `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` had 創's syllable contaminated from a divergent form (cang/창/ㄑㄚㄫ) rather than its own authoritative reading (cwang/촹/ㄑ⺢ㄫ, confirmed against 創's own character page and its stand-in word 創立) — corrected. Korean 창세기 and Vietnamese sáng thế ký are standard real-world readings (not Dan'a'yo-derived, `kwin: false` as expected). No homophones.
