## Notes
- [枕](../characters/枕.md) "pillow" + [頭](../characters/頭%20(char).md) "head" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 枕 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 頭's own stand-in is itself.

枕頭 means pillow.

Mandarin zhěntou, Cantonese zam2 tau4, and Japanese ちんとう are all standard, fully compositional readings.
