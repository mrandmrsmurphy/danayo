## Notes
- [画](../characters/画.md) "paint" + [家](../characters/家.md) "house; specialist"

画家 is a painter: an artist who works in paint or ink on a flat surface. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin huàjiā as "painter; artist who specialises in drawing" and Japanese がか as "painter". Korean 화가 is the same word. Cantonese waa6-2 gaa1 matches.

The art is [絵画](絵画.md), painting, and the larger term for the maker is the [芸術家](芸術家.md), artist. The painter's brush is the [筆](筆.md).
