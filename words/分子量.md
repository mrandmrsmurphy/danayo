## Notes
- [分 (char)](../characters/分%20(char).md) "divide" + [子](../characters/子.md) "son, child" + [量](../characters/量.md) "measure, quantity" — molecular weight.

分子量 is the molecular weight of a substance: the sum of the atomic weights ([[原子量]]) of all the atoms in one molecule. Water, $軽_二養$, has a molecular weight of about 18. It is [[分子]] "molecule" plus 量 "quantity." Mandarin fēnzǐliàng (also written 相對分子質量), Cantonese fan6 zi2 loeng6, Japanese ぶんしりょう and Korean 분자량 are the standard readings.
