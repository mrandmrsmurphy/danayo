## Notes
- [狗](../characters/狗.md) "hound" + [吠](../characters/吠.md) "bark" — no stand-in relationship applies (狗's own stand-in is [[../lookup/List of 名専字]], a special proper-noun-only designation; 吠's own is [[犬吠]]). Synonymous with [[犬吠]].

狗吠 means a dog's bark, barking; it is synonymous with [[犬吠]].

Mandarin gǒufèi, Cantonese gau2 fai6, Japanese くはい, Korean 구폐 and Vietnamese cẩu phệ are all fully compositional.
