## Notes
- [占](../characters/占%20(char).md) "divine, foretell" + [星](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" — no stand-in relationship applies (both characters are independent Dan'a'yo entries, each its own stand-in).

占星 means divination by the stars — astrology (占星術).

Japanese せんせい, Korean 점성, and Vietnamese chiêm tinh are all standard, fully compositional readings. `mandarin: zhānxīng` uses 占's "divine, foretell" tone (zhān) rather than its citation tone zhàn ("occupy," covered in [[占拠]]) — the same kind of legitimate sense-dependent tone split, kept as-is. Fixed `characters` citation "占" → "占 (char)"; filled blank `pos: 名詞`; removed redundant blank `品詞`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄇㄙㄝㄫ unique across both words and characters).
