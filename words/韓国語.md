## Notes
- [韓](../characters/韓.md) "Han (Korean)" + [国](../characters/国.md) "nation" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language" — no `#cranberry`: no constituent's own `stand_in` points here (韓's is [[韓国]], 国's is [[国家]], 語's is [[言語]]).

韓国語/韩国语 (Hánguóyǔ) is the standard, official Mandarin term specifically for the language of South Korea — the name used in textbooks and dictionaries, based on South Korean standard vocabulary — distinguished from 朝鮮語/朝鲜语 ("Korean," referring to the language as used by ethnic Koreans/North Korea in official PRC usage), with the shorter 韓語/韩语 as a common colloquial synonym (given here as an alias). Cantonese 韓國語 (hon4gwok3jyu5) follows the same compositional pattern. Japanese かんこくご (kankokugo) and Korean 한국어 (hangugeo, the country's own endonym for its language) are both standard everyday terms. Sino-Vietnamese tiếng Hàn Quốc, using the native word tiếng ("language") before the country name, is the fuller and more precise Vietnamese form (as opposed to the shorter, more colloquial tiếng Hàn), matching this word's own explicit "(South)" sense — 朝鮮語 is the language of North Korea.
