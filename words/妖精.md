## Notes
- [妖](../characters/妖.md) "bewitching, demonic" + [精](../characters/精.md) "spirit, essence" — "witch, elf, fairy" — often specifically an alluring magical woman/spirit in classical usage, and more broadly "fairy, elf" in modern Japanese ようせい. `vietnamese: yêu tinh` is a directly attested, well-known standard Vietnamese term for this kind of folkloric creature, common in Vietnamese fairy tales.
