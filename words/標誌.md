## Notes
- [標](../characters/標.md) "mark, sign" + [誌](../characters/誌.md) "symbolize" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [誌](../characters/誌.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 標's own stand-in is the similar but distinct sibling word [[標識]].

標誌 means to symbolize, mark, or indicate.

Mandarin biāozhì, Cantonese biu1 zi3, Korean 표지, and Vietnamese tiêu chí are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bugs: `mandarin` had an extra variant biāoshí, actually the reading of the similar sibling word [[標識]] (誌 vs. 識), not 標誌's own; `japanese` was へうしき — an obsolete historical kana spelling combined with しき, likewise borrowed from 標識's reading — corrected to modern ひょうし, matching 誌's own citation シ. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field. No homophones (注音 ㄅ⼄ㄐㄧ unique across both words and characters).
