## Notes
- [標](../characters/標.md) "mark, sign" + [誌](../characters/誌.md) "symbolize" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [誌](../characters/誌.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 標's own stand-in is the similar but distinct sibling word [[標識]].

標誌 means to symbolize, mark, or indicate.

Mandarin biāozhì, Cantonese biu1 zi3, Korean 표지, and Vietnamese tiêu chí are all standard, fully compositional readings.
