## Notes
- [寒](../characters/寒.md) "cold; chilly" + [冷 (char)](../characters/冷%20(char).md) "cool; cold" — this word is the stand-in for [寒](../characters/寒.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

寒冷 is another near-synonym doubling, both halves independently meaning "cold" — 寒 leans toward the wintry, seasonal cold of weather and climate (寒假 "winter vacation," 寒流 "cold front/current"), while 冷 is the more general, everyday word for coldness of any kind (temperature, food, or figuratively a cold reception), and is by far the more productive of the two outside this compound (冷蔵庫 "refrigerator," 冷麺 "cold noodles"). Together they intensify into the specific register of "cold" used for climate and weather description — 寒冷地帯 "cold/frigid zone," 寒冷前線 "cold front" — rather than everyday incidental coldness.

Japanese かんれい, Korean 한랭, and Vietnamese hàn lãnh (verified) are all plain, everyday, non-drifted readings used specifically in meteorological and climatic contexts, paralleling the Mandarin/Cantonese register. No specific HSK level number is confirmed (Wiktionary marks the compound only "Elementary Mandarin"); no homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄋㄌㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
