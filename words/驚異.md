## Notes
- [驚](../characters/驚.md) "startle; be amazed" + [異](../characters/異.md) "strange; wonderful"

驚異 is wonder: amazement at something extraordinary, or the thing itself that causes it. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin jīngyì as "amazed; astonished" and Japanese きょうい as "a marvel, a wonder". Korean 경이 and Vietnamese kinh dị are the same word, and Cantonese ging1 ji6 matches.

It is the pleasant side of being startled, where [喫驚](喫驚.md) is the plain jolt and [衝撃](衝撃.md) is the blow. Awe before something greater is [畏](畏.md).
