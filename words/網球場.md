## Notes
- [網 (char)](../characters/網%20(char).md) "netting, net, network" + [球 (char)](../characters/球%20(char).md) "sphere, ball" + [場](../characters/場.md) "market" — none of the three characters' own `stand_in` points here (網's and 球's are stand-ins for themselves, 場's is [[市場]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 球 is individually `kwin: false`. A contraction of [[網球]] "tennis" + 球場 "field, pitch," dropping the shared 球.

Mandarin wǎngqiúchǎng (also wǎngqiúcháng) and Cantonese mong5 kau4 coeng4 are standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese テニスコート (tenisu kōto, "tennis court") and Korean 테니스장 (tenniseujang, loanword + native 場) both follow the same loanword pattern established on [[網球]] itself, rather than a fully Sino-compositional reading. Vietnamese sân quần vợt ("court" + "tennis," native) is likewise the standard real term. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
