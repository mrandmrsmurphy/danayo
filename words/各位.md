## Notes
- [各 (char)](../characters/各%20(char).md) "each, every" + [位](../characters/位.md) "position, honorific classifier for people" — "ladies and gentlemen, everyone" (a respectful form of address to a group). `vietnamese: các vị` is one of the most common everyday Vietnamese forms of address for this concept, a direct match. Filled the blank `korean: 각위`, compositional (though the more idiomatic Korean equivalent for direct address is 여러분). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 각웨 diverges from the compositional Sino-Korean 각위 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄎ⼔ㄧ unique among both words and characters).
