## Notes
- [圓 (char)](../characters/圓%20(char).md) "round" + [頭 (char)](../characters/頭%20(char).md) "head; end" + [捺 (char)](../characters/捺%20(char).md) "right-falling stroke"

**圓頭捺** is the *round-headed* right-falling stroke, a long 捺 ([[捺]]) whose foot ends in a rounded finish instead of the angular one of the [[方頭捺]]. Chinese instruction lists the round end among the three kinds of finish for a long press stroke, alongside the square and the pointed. The name is attested in Chinese regular-script teaching, for example in descriptions of the strokes of Ouyang Xun's 九成宮醴泉銘 (*Jiuchenggong liquan ming*).

It is the second of the two named varieties of 捺 on the [[Stroke]] page.

The Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and North Korean forms are compositional, built from the constituent words and characters. No Vietnamese form is given.
