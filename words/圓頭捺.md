## Notes
- [圓 (char)](../characters/圓%20(char).md) "round" + [頭 (char)](../characters/頭%20(char).md) "head; end" + [捺 (char)](../characters/捺%20(char).md) "right-falling stroke"

Wiktionary has no entry for 圓頭捺; it is a compound of 圓 "round", 頭 "head; end" and [[捺]], which Wiktionary glosses in Chinese calligraphy as "a stroke that falls downwards towards the right (㇏)". It names the round-headed right-falling stroke, as against the [[方頭捺]]. It is the second of the two named varieties of 捺 on the [[Stroke]] page. Its readings are compositional.
