## Notes
- [関 (char)](../characters/関%20(char).md) "close" + [心 (char)](../characters/心%20(char).md) "heart"

関心 means to be concerned about or interested in something — literally "a heart engaged with" a matter, following the widespread East Asian metaphor of 心/心 as the seat of attention and feeling. Mandarin 關心/关心 (guānxīn), Cantonese 關心 (gwaan1sam1), Japanese 関心 (かんしん), Korean 관심 (gwansim), and Sino-Vietnamese quan tâm are all everyday, high-frequency words across the CJKV sphere, used identically for personal concern ("to care about someone"), intellectual interest ("an interest in a subject"), and institutional attention (関心を持つ, "to take an interest in").

Japanese distinguishes 関心 (interest/concern) from the near-homophone 感心 (kanshin, "admiration"), a common source of confusion for learners since both are read かんしん but write with different first characters and carry distinct meanings.
