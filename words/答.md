## Notes
- [答](../characters/答%20(char).md) "answer, reply" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 答 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

答 means to answer, reply, assent, or return a response — originally a variant of 荅 (the grass radical 艸 mistakenly replaced with bamboo 竹 over time). It is highly productive: 回答 ("to respond, reply"), 報答 ("to reward, requite"). Note: don't use 對答 as a synonym form here — it names a distinct, more formal "questioning and answering" exchange rather than the plain act of answering.

Mandarin dá, Cantonese daap3, and Korean 답 (dap) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading こたえる (kotaeru, "to answer") rather than the on-reading トウ (TOU) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese đáp is the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading.
