## Notes
- [雌](../characters/雌.md) "female" + [性](../characters/性.md) "gender, sex" — 雌性 is the stand-in word for [雌](../characters/雌.md); 雌 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse.

雌性 is the biological/scientific register term for "female," used specifically of the sex of animals and plants (雌性ホルモン, "female hormone"; 雌蕊, "pistil, the female part of a flower") — distinct from the everyday human-register word for "woman/female" ([[女性]]), the same specialization pattern seen in English "female" (biological) versus "woman" (social). Its antonym is 雄性 ("male," biological register).

Japanese しせい and Korean 자성 are both standard compositional Sino-readings. Vietnamese thư tính is likewise standard biological terminology, contrasted with hùng tính ("male," biological). No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄝㄙㄧㄫ unique across both words and characters).
