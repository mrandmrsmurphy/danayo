## Notes
- [雌](../characters/雌.md) "female" + [性](../characters/性.md) "gender, sex"

雌性 means female. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 雌性 (Mandarin cíxìng, Cantonese ci1 sing3) as "female (one of the feminine sex or gender)" and Japanese しせい as "female characteristics, femaleness". The Korean 자성 and Vietnamese thư tính fields are built from the readings of the two characters.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [雌](../characters/雌.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
