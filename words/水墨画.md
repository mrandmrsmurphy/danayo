## Notes
- [水 (char)](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [墨](../characters/墨.md) "ink" + [画](../characters/画.md) "picture"

水墨画 is ink wash painting: painting in black ink thinned with water, the main art of East Asian brush painting. Wiktionary lists Mandarin shuǐmòhuà and Japanese すいぼくが as "water ink pictures". Korean 수묵화 is the same word. Cantonese seoi2 mak6 waa6-2 matches.

It is done with the [筆](筆.md), brush, and [墨水](墨水.md), ink, on paper, and it shares the brush with [書法](書法.md), calligraphy.
