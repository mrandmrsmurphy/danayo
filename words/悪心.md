## Notes
- [悪](../characters/悪%20(char).md) "bad, evil" + [心](../characters/心%20(char).md) "heart, mind" — no stand-in relationship applies (悪's own stand-in is itself; 心's own is itself).

悪心 means nauseated or nauseous — a queasy, sick feeling.

Mandarin ěxīn and Cantonese ok3 sam1 are standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese 吐き気 ("vomit feeling"), Korean 구역질, and Vietnamese buồn nôn are each language's own native term for nausea, more common in everyday usage than a compositional reading of these specific characters — an expected native-equivalent substitution, not an error. Trimmed malformed `mandarin`/`cantonese` (each had a stray comma-joined duplicate reading). No homophones (注音 ㄚㄎㄙㄧㄇ unique across both words and characters).
