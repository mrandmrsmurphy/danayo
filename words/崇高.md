## Notes
- [崇](../characters/崇.md) "lofty; noble" + [高 (char)](../characters/高%20(char).md) "high" — this word is the stand-in for [崇](../characters/崇.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

崇高 is a near-synonym doubling, both halves independently meaning "lofty, elevated" — the standard word for something of the highest moral or spiritual value: a sublime ideal, a noble sacrifice, an exalted cause, rather than mere physical height.

Japanese すうこう, Korean 숭고, and Vietnamese sùng cao are all standard, non-drifted readings. No HSK level is given for the compound. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄨㄫㄍㄚㄨ unique across both words and characters).
