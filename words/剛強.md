## Notes
- [剛](../characters/剛.md) "firm; unyielding" + [強 (char)](../characters/強%20(char).md) "strong; powerful" — stand-in for [[剛]], which cannot appear independently

剛強 is a compound of two near-synonyms, "firm/unyielding" and "strong/powerful," reinforcing each other to mean "firm, strong, rigid" — used of both physical hardness and unyielding character or temperament.

Mandarin gāngqiáng, Cantonese gong1 koeng4, and Japanese ごうきょう (gōkyō, on'yomi) all preserve the compound directly. Vietnamese cương cường is the attested Hán Việt compound reading (a real Vietnamese adjective for firm, unyielding strength of character), not a simple concatenation of the characters' own much broader candidate lists. Korean 강강 (ganggang) is the compositional Sino-Korean reading — both constituent characters happen to share the identical syllable 강.

`kwin: false` per the AND-rule: `characters/剛.md`'s own `kwin` is `true`, but `characters/強 (char).md`'s own `kwin` is `false` — one false constituent makes the whole compound false.
