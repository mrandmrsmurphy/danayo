## Notes
- [絶 (char)](../characters/絶%20(char).md) "discontinue, break off" + [後 (char)](../characters/後%20(char).md) "after" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (絶's is [[絶]] itself, 後's is [[後]] itself), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

絶後 means "to never happen again" or "to break the mould" — most often in the fixed expression 空前絶後 (unprecedented and unrepeatable). It can also mean "to not have children," continuing a family line's end.

Mandarin juéhòu, Cantonese zyut6 hau6, Japanese ぜつご (zetsugo), and Korean 절후 (jeolhu) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese tuyệt hậu is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
