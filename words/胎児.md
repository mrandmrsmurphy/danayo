## Notes
- [胎](../characters/胎.md) "fetus" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 胎 cannot appear independently outside this compound. [児 (char)](../characters/児%20(char).md) "baby" is separately legitimized by [[児]] itself.

胎児 combines 胎 ("fetus") with 児 ("baby, child") — "fetus."

Mandarin tāi'ér, cantonese toi1ji4 (space removed), japanese たいじ (taiji), korean 태아 (taea), and vietnamese thai nhi were all already correct, fully compositional readings.
