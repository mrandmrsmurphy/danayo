## Notes
- [話 (char)](../characters/話%20(char).md) "to speak, talk about" + [者](../characters/者.md) "-er, one who" (the agentive suffix already behind [[学者]], [[記者]], [[使者]], etc.) — no stand-in relationship applies (both 話's and 者's own stand-ins are themselves).

Wiktionary: Japanese 話者 (わしゃ) "one who speaks; a speaker"; Korean 화자 is its hanja form, "speaker". Used throughout `文法 - 02音韻論`, `文法 - 94日本語小冊子` and `文法 - 01序文`, as in 日本語話者. Built on the agentive 者 of [[学者]], [[記者]] and [[使者]].
