## Notes
- [話 (char)](../characters/話%20(char).md) "to speak, talk about" + [者](../characters/者.md) "-er, one who" (the agentive suffix already behind [[学者]], [[記者]], [[使者]], etc.) — no stand-in relationship applies (both 話's and 者's own stand-ins are themselves).

話者 means a speaker — one who speaks, especially in the linguistic sense of a speaker of a given language (日本語話者, "a speaker of Japanese"). This word is already used extensively, unlinked, throughout this vault's own `文法 - 02音韻論` and `文法 - 94日本語小冊子` grammar documents — this page formalizes an already-established in-vault term.

Japanese わしゃ and Korean 화자 are both real, standard linguistics terms (Korean pairs it directly with 청자, "listener, addressee"). Mandarin huàzhě and Cantonese waa6 ze2 are straightforward compositional readings from 話's and 者's own stored citations; not independently verified as the single most common colloquial Mandarin equivalent (說話人/说话人, "speaking person," is also widely used), but attested in technical/linguistic registers. Vietnamese thoại giả is likewise compositional (話's own thoại + 者's own giả), not independently cross-checked against standard Vietnamese linguistics terminology — flagged rather than silently assumed. `kwin` is false: both 話 and 者 are individually false at the character level, so the AND-rule gives false overall.

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot**: the 話|者 boundary is a genuine resegmentation fork — the concatenated string `ㄏ⺢ㄧㄑㄚ` admits both the intended `ㄏ⺢ㄧ`(話)+`ㄑㄚ`(者) and an alternate `ㄏ⺢`+`ㄧ`+`ㄑㄚ` (ㄏ⺢ is itself a real, separately assigned syllable) — so the dot is required: ㄏ⺢ㄧ·ㄑㄚ.
