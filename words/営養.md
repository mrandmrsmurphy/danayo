## Notes
- [営](../characters/営.md) "operate, manage" + [養](../characters/養.md) "foster, raise" — no stand-in relationship applies (営's own stand-in is [[経営]]; 養's is [[養育]]).

営養 means nutrition or nourishment.

**Real bug found and fixed**: `cantonese` had 営's syllable misspelled ying4 instead of its own citation jing4 (confirmed against sibling compounds [[国営]], [[経営]]) — corrected to jing4 joeng5. Japanese えいよう, Korean 영양, and Vietnamese dinh dưỡng are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching Dan'a'yo's own reading exactly (`kwin: true`). Fixed unquoted `hsk_level`. No homophones (注音 ⼶ㄫ⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).

**Not an alias**: [[栄養]] was previously listed here as an alias, but it is a separate, independent word built on the distinct character 栄 (róng, "flourish") rather than 営 (yíng, "manage") — different form, different Mandarin reading, and its own Dan'a'yo reading (ㄨㄧㄫ⼘ㄫ) — removed from `aliases`. As 栄養's own page notes, the two are only homophonous in Japanese (both えいよう), not a real orthographic-variant pair.
