## Notes
- [営](../characters/営.md) "operate, manage" + [養](../characters/養.md) "foster, raise" — no stand-in relationship applies (営's own stand-in is [[経営]]; 養's is [[養育]]).

営養 means nutrition or nourishment.

[[栄養]] is not an alias: it is a separate, independent word built on the distinct character 栄 (róng, "flourish") rather than 営 (yíng, "manage") — different form, different Mandarin reading, and its own Dan'a'yo reading (ㄨㄧㄫ⼘ㄫ) As 栄養's own page notes, the two are only homophonous in Japanese (both えいよう), not a real orthographic-variant pair.
