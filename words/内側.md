## Notes
- [内](../characters/内.md) "inside (stand-in: [[内部]])" + [側](../characters/側.md) "side (stand-in: [[側面]])"

Wiktionary lists 内側 under Japanese only: "the interior; the inside; the inner part", attributive "insideness", and, in anatomy, "mediality"; it is allowed by templatic reasoning, as the clear opposite of the attested [[外側]], not by usage; the Mandarin, Cantonese and Korean fields are compositional. The Japanese field gives the Sino-Japanese reading; the native reading is うちがわ. Its counterpart is [[外側]].
