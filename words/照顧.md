## Notes
- [照](characters/照%20(char).md) "shine, illuminate" + [顧](characters/顧.md) "look after" — 顧's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 照's own is itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 顧 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

照顧 means to care for, to look after.

Mandarin zhàogù, Cantonese ziu3 gu3, and Japanese しょうこ (SHOU+KO) were already correct and fully compositional. Filled the blank `korean` with 조고 and blank `vietnamese` with chiếu cố (照's own chiếu + 顧's own cố), both compositional — chiếu cố is also the real, standard Vietnamese term for "to look after, to take into consideration." Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/`aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄛㄨㄍㄛ unique across both words and characters).
