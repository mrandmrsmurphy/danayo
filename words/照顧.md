## Notes
- [照](../characters/照%20(char).md) "shine, illuminate" + [顧](../characters/顧.md) "look after" — 顧's own stand-in is this exact compound, but 照's own is itself; transitivity fails, so `#cranberry` does not apply, though 顧 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word.

照顧 means to care for, to look after.
