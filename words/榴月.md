## Notes
- [榴](../characters/榴.md) "pomegranate" + [月](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — no stand-in relationship applies (榴's own stand-in is [[石榴]]; 月's own is itself).

Fifth month of the ancient Chinese poetic calendar. Pomegranate flowers (榴花) burst open in vivid scarlet in early summer. The fifth month contains the Duanwu Festival (端午節), and the brilliant red of pomegranate blossoms was said to ward off evil — a natural match for the apotropaic spirit of that festival.

Mandarin liúyuè and Cantonese lau4 jyut6 are standard, fully compositional readings. Korean 류월 already correctly used the North Korean/문화어 form. Japanese ざくろつき reads both halves natively (榴's kun'yomi ざくろ "pomegranate" + 月's kun'yomi つき "month") rather than the Sino-Japanese on'yomi concatenation りゅうげつ, matching the established poetic-month convention (see [[寅月]], [[卯月]], [[未月]], [[杏月]], [[桂月]], [[桃月]]). Vietnamese tháng lựu likewise uses the native *tháng* plus the Sino-Vietnamese pomegranate name lựu, the same pattern. Removed the redundant `品詞` field and reformatted `japanese`/`vietnamese` from single-item lists to scalar strings. No homophones (注音 ㄌ⼜·⼔ㄊ unique across both words and characters).
