## Notes
- [小 (char)](../characters/小%20(char).md) "small" + [冊 (char)](../characters/冊%20(char).md) "book, volume" + [子](../characters/子.md) "noun suffix" — no stand-in relationship applies (none of the three constituents' own `stand_in` fields point here).

小冊子 is transparently [[小]] "small" + [[冊子]] "book, booklet" (子 in its light noun-suffix role, not "child" — the same role documented on [[梯子]]) — a small book, i.e. a booklet or pamphlet. **Japanese cannot abbreviate this to plain 小冊** the way some 小X compounds shorten; 小冊子 must keep all three characters in ordinary use.

Japanese しょうさっし and Korean 소책자 are both standard, non-drifted readings. Vietnamese has a genuine Sino-Vietnamese reading, tiểu sách tử (attested per Hán Nôm dictionary sources), though everyday spoken Vietnamese defaults to the native compound cuốn sách nhỏ/sách nhỏ ("small book") instead — the same register split as [[私讐]]'s literary-vs-everyday pattern. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄛㄑㄚㄎㄐㄜ unique across both words and characters).
