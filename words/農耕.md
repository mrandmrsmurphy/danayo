## Etymology
- [農](../characters/農.md) "farming" + [耕](../characters/耕.md) "plow, cultivate"

## Notes
Synonymous with [[農業]] ("agriculture" as an industry — 農耕 more narrowly denotes the practice of tilling/cultivation itself).

Mandarin nónggēng, cantonese nung4 gaang1, japanese のうこう, and korean 농경 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese nông canh is the honest compositional reading (農's own nông + 耕's own canh); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword using this exact character order was found (some Vietnamese historical usage instead inverts the order, "canh nông"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 농강 diverges from Sino-Korean 농경 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄛㄫㄍㄚㄫ unique among both words and characters).
