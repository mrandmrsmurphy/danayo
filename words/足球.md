## Notes
- [足 (char)](../characters/足%20(char).md) "foot" + [球 (char)](../characters/球%20(char).md) "sphere, ball" — "football," used as generically/vaguely as the English word itself (could mean association football/soccer, or by extension other foot-and-ball sports); see also [[蹴球]] for the more specific, traditional Sino-Japanese term for the sport. Mandarin zúqiú and Cantonese zuk1 kau4 are directly attested standard readings; `vietnamese: túc cầu` is likewise a real, historically attested (if now slightly old-fashioned) Vietnamese term for football/soccer. `japanese: そっきゅう` and `korean: 족구` are honest compositional readings — 족구 happens to coincidentally name a real, different Korean sport ("foot volleyball"), while Korean's actual everyday word for soccer, 축구, is built from [[蹴球]] instead. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 족규 diverges from the compositional Sino-Korean 족구 in the second syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄛㄎㄍ⼜ unique among both words and characters).
