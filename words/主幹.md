## Notes
- [主](../characters/主.md) "main" + [幹](../characters/幹.md) "tree trunk" — this word is the `stand_in` legitimizer for [[幹]], which cannot appear independently.

主幹 works equally well botanically (the main trunk of a tree, as distinct from its branches) and figuratively (the core, backbone, or central part of an organization or system). Japanese しゅかん additionally carries a specific professional sense — "chief editor, managing editor" or a general "person in charge" — extending the "trunk/core" image to organizational leadership, the same figurative move behind English "backbone of the operation." **Vietnamese content note**: chủ cán is a compositionally sound reading (主's chủ, well-attested; 幹's cán, well-attested as in cán bộ "cadre, official"), but no independent dictionary attestation of the exact compound turned up during this search, so it is presented as a defensible derivation rather than a separately confirmed fixed term. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 주간 coincides exactly with Sino-Korean 주간. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄨㄍㄚㄋ unique among both words and characters).
