## Notes
- [十 (char)](../characters/十%20(char).md) "ten" + [八 (char)](../characters/八%20(char).md) "eight"

Mandarin shíbā, Cantonese sap6 baat3, Korean 십팔 (sippal), and Japanese じゅうはち (jūhachi) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese thập bát is likewise compositional, from 十's thập and 八's own bát, and is directly attested (as in Thập Bát La Hán, "the Eighteen Arhats," a well-known Buddhist iconographic set). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 십받 diverges from Sino-Korean 십팔 in the second syllable, matching 八's own individual `kwin: false` on its own character page. No Dan'a'yo homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄆㄅㄚㄊ unique among both words and characters).
