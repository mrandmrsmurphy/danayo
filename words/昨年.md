## Notes
- [昨](../characters/昨%20(char).md) "last, previous" + [年](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 昨 and 年's own stand-ins are themselves).

昨年 means last year.

Mandarin zuónián, Cantonese zok6 nin4, Japanese さくねん, Korean 작년, and Vietnamese tạc niên are all standard, fully compositional readings. Simplified `korean` from comma-joined "작년, 지난해" to compositional 작년, moving native 지난해 to prose. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄎㄋㄝㄋ unique across both words and characters).
