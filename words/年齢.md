## Notes
- [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year" + [齢](../characters/齢.md) "age" — this word is the stand-in for [齢](../characters/齢.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

年齢 is the standard, everyday word for "age" across the CJKV sphere — for the more formal/honorific alternative, see [[年歳]].

Japanese ねんれい, Korean 년령, and Vietnamese niên linh (verified — attested in derived compounds like 年齢順/niên linh thuận "age order," 年齢差/niên linh sai "age difference") are all standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄝㄋㄌㄝㄫ unique across both words and characters).

**Correction (2026-08-28)**: `korean` had been stored as the malformed multi-value string "연령, 나이" — 연령 itself South-Korean-shifted (same bug class as [[年刊]]/[[年歳]]) and 나이 an entirely unrelated native Korean word for "age" — corrected to the proper North Korean 년령. `cantonese` had a missing space (ling4 without the space after nin4); `vietnamese` was blank, filled with the verified niên linh.
