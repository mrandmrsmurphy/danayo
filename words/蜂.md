## Notes
- [蜂 (char)](../characters/蜂%20(char).md) "bee, wasp, hornet"

蜂 is the general term for stinging hymenopteran insects — bees, wasps, and hornets alike — rather than being restricted to honeybees specifically (compare [[蜂蜜]] "honey" and [[蜂巣]] "beehive," both of which lean toward the bee sense). The Mandarin, Cantonese, Sino-Japanese and Sino-Korean readings (fēng, fung1, ほう, 봉) are all reflexes of the same Middle Chinese root. The `japanese` field records the Sino-Japanese reading ほう (the 蜂 character page's on'yomi HOU), while the everyday Japanese word for the insect is the native はち (hachi), recorded on the character page as its native reading. Vietnamese likewise has the native word *ong* "bee" (recorded here) beside the Sino-Vietnamese reading *phong*, which surfaces instead in words built on the "seal/enfeoff" sense of the homophone character [[封]].

The Animals page lists the word as "bee, wasp, hornet," and Dan'a'yo keeps that broad sense. Related words are [蜂蜜](蜂蜜.md) "honey" and [蜂巣](蜂巣.md) "beehive." The Dan'a'yo homophone with [[封]] is flagged in the callout above.
