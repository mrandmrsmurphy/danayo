## Notes
- [沈](../characters/沈.md) "sink" + [淀](../characters/淀.md) "sediment, precipitate"
- This word is the viable form of [淀](../characters/淀.md): the character's meaning (sediment, settled matter) is identical, but 淀 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 沈淀 is its `stand_in` compound.

沈淀 names sediment and the process of settling: solid particles in a liquid sinking and collecting at the bottom, or a chemical precipitate. It is written 沉淀 in modern Mandarin (chéndiàn; also the alias here) and 沈淀 in the form this vault uses; Cantonese is sam2 din6 in the page's data. In Japanese the same word is written 沈殿 or 沈澱 (ちんでん, chinden) and in Korean 침전 (沈澱, chimjeon), with different final characters from the Chinese 淀, so the written forms differ although the word is the same.

In Dan'a'yo it covers both the process (to settle) and the result (sediment, 沉淀物), and is placed in the Substances domain beside [[渣]] (dregs, lees), which names the residue without saying how it formed.
