## Notes
- [済](../characters/済%20(char).md) "help, ferry" + [南](../characters/南.md) "south" — south of the Ji (River). No stand-in relationship applies (済 is its own stand-in; 南's is [[南方]]).

済南 (Jinan) is the capital of Shandong province in eastern China. Per Wiktionary the name is literally "south of the Ji River" (濟水). 濟 and 济 are aliases of 済, so 濟南 and 济南 are kept as aliases. English Wiktionary also lists a second sense under the Chinese entry: Jenam-dong (제남동), a neighbourhood (洞) of Tokchon in South Pyongan, North Korea.

Japanese さいなん is the Sino-Japanese reading (attested on Japanese Wikipedia, 済南市, not on Wiktionary; the common Japanese form is the shinjitai 済), and Vietnamese Tế Nam is attested on Vietnamese Wikipedia. Korean 제남 is the Sino-Korean reading (Wiktionary Korean 제남); the usual South Korean name today is 지난 (from memory, not re-checked). Cantonese zai2 naam4 is the prescriptive reading, zai3 naam4 a common variant. The 注音 carries a syllable-boundary dot because ㄐㄝㄧㄋㄚㄇ also parses as ㄐㄝ + ㄧ + ㄋㄚㄇ and as ㄐㄝ + ㄧㄋ + ㄚㄇ. Attestation: English Wiktionary Chinese and Korean entries. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄧ·ㄋㄚㄇ unique across both words and characters).
