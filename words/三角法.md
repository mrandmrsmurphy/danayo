## Notes
- [三 (char)](../characters/三%20(char).md) "three" + [角 (char)](../characters/角%20(char).md) "angle" + [法 (char)](../characters/法%20(char).md) "method" — no `#cranberry`: none of the three constituents' own `stand_in` points here.

Mandarin sānjiǎofǎ, Cantonese saam1 gok3 faat3, Japanese さんかくほう, and Korean 삼각법 are standard compositional readings. Vietnamese lượng giác học ("angle-measurement study") is the real, standard Vietnamese term for trigonometry, used instead of a literal calque.
