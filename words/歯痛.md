## Notes
- [歯](../characters/歯%20(char).md) "teeth, gear" + [痛](../characters/痛.md) "torment, pain" — no stand-in relationship applies (歯's own stand-in is itself; 痛's own is [[苦痛]]).

歯痛 means toothache.

Mandarin chǐtòng, Cantonese ci2 tung3, Japanese しつう, Korean 치통, and Vietnamese xỉ thống are all standard, fully compositional readings. 歯 has no vietnamese value stored on its own citation (a character-level gap already flagged on [[歯]]'s own page), so xỉ is used here as the standard Sino-Vietnamese reading for this character, matching real-world usage. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄊㄛㄫ unique across both words and characters).
