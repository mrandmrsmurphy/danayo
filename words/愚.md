## Notes
- [愚](../characters/愚%20(char).md) "foolish" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

愚 means foolish — lacking sense or judgment, as in 愚か者 ("fool").

Mandarin yú, Cantonese jyu4, Japanese ぐ, Korean 우, and Vietnamese ngu are all standard, fully compositional readings. Part of a four-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read 'u/우/ㄨ) with [[于]] ("to," dative particle), [[雨]] ("rain"), and [[遇]] ("meet, encounter").
