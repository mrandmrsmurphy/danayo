## Notes
- [紀](../characters/紀.md) "century" + [元](../characters/元.md) "element" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (紀's is [[世紀]], 元's is [[元素]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: true` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: true`.

紀元 names an era or epoch — the starting point of a calendrical reckoning, most commonly the Christian/Common Era (A.D./C.E., as distinguished from [[紀元前]] "B.C./B.C.E.").

Mandarin jìyuán, Cantonese gei2 jyun4, Japanese きげん (kigen), Korean 기원 (giwon), and Vietnamese kỷ nguyên are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 紀元 is itself a common attested word for "era" in all five languages. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
