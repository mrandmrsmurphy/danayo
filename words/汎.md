## Notes
- [汎](characters/汎%20(char).md) "pan-" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 汎 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

汎 means "pan-, all-encompassing" (as in 汎濫 "flood, overflow," 汎亜 "Pan-Asian").

Mandarin fàn, Cantonese faan4, Korean 범, and Vietnamese phiếm are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 汎's own stored citations exactly.
