## Notes
- [汎](characters/汎%20(char).md) "pan-" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 汎 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

汎 means "pan-, all-encompassing" (as in 汎濫 "flood, overflow," 汎亜 "Pan-Asian").

Mandarin fàn, Cantonese faan4, Korean 범, and Vietnamese phiếm are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 汎's own stored citations exactly. **Fixed a false homophone bug**: `羅馬字`/`諺文`/`注音` had been contaminated to pam/팜/ㄆㄚㄇ, matching 帆's reading instead of 汎's own (fam/빰/ㄈㄚㄇ, confirmed by 汎's own character page and consistent with [[汎濫]]'s famlam/빰람) — an easy mix-up, since 帆 is one of 汎's own listed Derived Characters, sharing the phonetic component 凡. Corrected to 汎's own reading, added the missing `pos`/`japanese`/`vietnamese` fields, reformatted `characters` to a proper block-list with the "(char)" suffix. **Removed the false Homophones callout with [[帆]]** — with the reading corrected to ㄈㄚㄇ, 汎 and 帆 (ㄆㄚㄇ) are no longer homophones at all; the reciprocal callout on [[帆]]'s own page has also been removed. A fresh grep confirms ㄈㄚㄇ is not shared by any other independent word page.
