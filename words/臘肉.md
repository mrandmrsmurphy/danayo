## Notes
- [臘](../characters/臘.md) "Laba" + [肉 (char)](../characters/肉%20(char).md) "meat"

Wiktionary lists 臘肉 only under Chinese: "cured (pork) meat; salted and dried meat", and, internet slang, derogatory and offensive, "Mao Zedong or his corpse". The simplified spelling 腊肉 is kept as an alias. In Dan'a'yo 臘肉 keeps the sense of cured meat; the slang use is an internet-register extension and not a separate sense. 臘 is tied to the festival date in [[臘八]].
