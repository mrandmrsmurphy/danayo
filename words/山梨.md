## Notes
- [山](../characters/山%20(char).md) "mountain" + [梨](../characters/梨%20(char).md) "pear"

山梨 is Yamanashi, written "mountain pear". It is the name of an old district, and the reading やまなし is native: 山 (やま, "mountain") plus 梨 (なし, "pear"). Despite the characters, the prefecture is chiefly associated with grapes and peaches.

Yamanashi lies around the north side of Mount Fuji and the Fuji Five Lakes, with its capital Kōfu, the base of the Takeda clan under Takeda Shingen. It is Japan's leading wine and grape region.

In Dan'a'yo 山梨 is the place name only, a proper noun kept in the Japanese form; the unit it names is a [県](県.md), an ordinary prefecture. The Mandarin, Cantonese and Vietnamese readings are compositional Sino-Xenic readings of the characters (from memory, not re-checked), and the Korean 야마나시 is the standard Korean transliteration of the Japanese name, not a Sino-Korean reading.
