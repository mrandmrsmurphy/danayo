## Notes
- [合 (char)](../characters/合%20(char).md) "join; fit together" + [格 (char)](../characters/格%20(char).md) "case; status"

合格 is to pass: to meet the standard in an exam, or to qualify. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin hégé as "to qualify; to reach standard; to pass", and also "qualified". Japanese ごうかく is "passing (as in a test); to pass a test". Korean 합격 and Cantonese hap6 gaak3 match.

It is the happy result of an [受験](受験.md) or a [試験](試験.md), and the opposite of [落第](落第.md), to fail. The older word [及第](及第.md) means the same for the imperial examinations.
