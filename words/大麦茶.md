## Notes
- [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big" + [麦](../characters/麦.md) "wheat, barley" + [茶 (char)](../characters/茶%20(char).md) "tea" — "barley tea." Japanese 麦茶 (むぎちゃ) is technically ambiguous between "wheat tea" and "barley tea" since 麦 covers both grains, but in practice always refers to barley tea, the common East Asian roasted-grain beverage. **Content bugs found and fixed**: `mandarin` had a stray capital/space ("Dàmài chá") — normalized to dàmàichá; `cantonese` was missing 茶's own syllable entirely — corrected to daai6 mak6 caa4; `japanese` was malformed (オオムギ, "barley" the plant, with a trailing stray comma) — corrected to むぎちゃ. `korean: 보리차` is a genuine native Korean word for barley tea (보리 "barley" + 차 "tea"), kept as-is rather than forced into a Sino-Korean calque. `vietnamese: trà đại mạch` is compositional, using Vietnamese's natural head-first noun order (trà [đại mạch], paralleling trà sữa "milk tea") rather than a direct character-order calque (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 대막차 diverges from Korean's native 보리차 entirely — not a meaningful comparison, since Korean lexicalizes a completely different, native word. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄧㄇㄚㄎㄑㄚ unique among both words and characters).
