## Notes
- [覇](../characters/覇.md) "hegemony" is separately legitimized by this exact compound (its own stand-in). [権](../characters/権.md) "power, right, authority" is separately legitimized by [[権利]]. No cranberry.

English Wiktionary glosses Japanese 覇権 (はけん) as "hegemony". The Mandarin bàquán and Korean 패권 fields are built from the readings of the two characters.
