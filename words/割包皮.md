## Notes
- [割 (char)](../characters/割%20(char).md) "to cut" + [包](../characters/包.md) "to wrap" + [皮](../characters/皮.md) "skin" — "circumcision," literally "to cut the wrapping skin (foreskin)." Real Mandarin/Cantonese use this compositional phrase directly, but Japanese and Korean instead reach for their own established medical term built on a different root — 包茎手術/포경수술 (hōkei shujutsu/pogyeong-susul, literally "phimosis surgery," this session's finding based on domain knowledge rather than a fresh web search, quota exhausted). `vietnamese: cắt bao quy đầu` is likewise the real, specific everyday Vietnamese medical term (literally "to cut the glans-covering sheath") rather than a direct compositional calque of these three characters. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 갇뱟비 diverges entirely from the specialized-term-based Korean field. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄚㄊㄅ⼘ㄨㄅㄧ unique among both words and characters).
