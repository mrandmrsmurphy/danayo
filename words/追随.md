## Etymology
- [追 (char)](../characters/追%20(char).md) "follow, hunt" + [随](../characters/随.md) "accompany"

## Notes
Mandarin zhuīsuí, cantonese zeoi1 ceoi4, japanese ついずい, and korean 추수 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese truy tuỳ is the honest compositional reading (追's own truy + 随's own tuỳ); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `追隨` is the standard traditional-character spelling variant (随→隨), correctly listed as an alias. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 튀쉬 diverges from Sino-Korean 추수 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄨㄧㄙㄨㄧ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. Neither 追's `stand_in` (追 itself) nor 随's own `stand_in` (随行) points to this compound.
