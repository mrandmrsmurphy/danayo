## Notes
- [振](../characters/振%20(char).md) "shake, excite, rouse" + [幅](../characters/幅.md) "breadth, width" — no stand-in relationship applies (振's own stand-in is itself; 幅's own is [[幅度]]).

振幅 means amplitude.

Mandarin zhènfú, Cantonese zan3 fuk1, Japanese しんぷく, Korean 진폭, and Vietnamese chấn bức are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄧㄋㄈㄨㄎ unique across both words and characters).
