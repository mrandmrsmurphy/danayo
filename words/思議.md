## Notes
- [思](../characters/思.md) "think" + [議](../characters/議.md) "comment, discuss" — no stand-in relationship applies (思's own stand-in is [[思考]]; 議's own is [[議論]]).

思議 means to imagine or comprehend — chiefly seen in the negated form 不可思議 ("inconceivable, mysterious").

Mandarin sīyì, Cantonese si1 ji5, Japanese しぎ, Korean 사의, and Vietnamese tư nghị are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `諺文`/`羅馬字`/`注音` all had 議's syllable using the wrong reading 위/wi/ㄨㄧ instead of the correct 읫/'ǝi/ㄜㄧ (matching 議's own citation and sibling [[協議]]). Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄚㄜㄧ unique across both words and characters).
