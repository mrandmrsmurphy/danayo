## Notes
- [随](../characters/随.md) "follow; at will" + [筆 (char)](../characters/筆%20(char).md) "pen; writing"

随筆 is an essay: a short piece of informal prose that follows the writer's thoughts. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin suíbǐ as "informal essay; casual literary notes" and Japanese ずいひつ as "an essay". Korean 수필 and Vietnamese tuỳ bút are the same word. Cantonese ceoi4 bat1 matches.

It is a form of [散文](散文.md), prose, written without a set plan. A formal article is a [文章](文章.md).
