## Notes
- [夢 (char)](../characters/夢%20(char).md) "dream"

夢 means "dream" (夢想, "to dream of, aspire to"; 夢嫌, "nightmare").

Mandarin mèng and Cantonese mung6 preserve the sense directly. Japanese ム (MU, on'yomi, formal register) alongside native ゆめ (yume, "dream," the everyday word) cover the sense. Korean 몽 (mong) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 꿈 (kkum, "dream"). Vietnamese mộng is the standard Hán Việt reading (giấc mộng, "a dream"; ác mộng, "nightmare"; mộng du, "sleepwalking"), with a rarer secondary reading, mông, also attested. The character page's other stored candidates are mostly noise: mồng derives from an entirely different character (孟, mạnh), sharing 夢's glyph only as a Nôm phonetic loan (mồng một, "the first day of the lunar month"); muống is likewise an unrelated word ("funnel") borrowed purely for sound; mọng ("plump, juicy"), mống ("rainbow, sprout"), mòng ("biting fly"), and mụn ("pimple, cloth scrap") all appear to be automated near-rhyme artifacts rather than attested Nôm usages of this character at all.
