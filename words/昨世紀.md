## Notes
- [昨](../characters/昨%20(char).md) "last, previous" + [世](../characters/世.md) "world, generation" + [紀](../characters/紀.md) "era, century" — no stand-in relationship applies (昨's own stand-in is itself; 世's own is [[世界]]; 紀's own is [[世紀]]).

昨世紀 means last century.

Mandarin zuóshìjì, Cantonese zok6 sai3 gei2, Japanese さくせいき, Korean 작세기, and Vietnamese tạc thế kỷ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Simplified `korean` from native paraphrase 지난세기 ("passed century") to compositional 작세기. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄎㄙㄝㄍㄧ unique across both words and characters).
