## Notes
- [巫](../characters/巫.md) "shaman" + [術 (char)](../characters/術%20(char).md) "technique" — no stand-in relationship applies (巫's own `stand_in` is [[巫女]], 術's is itself).

巫術 names shamanic magic or witchcraft — the practiced arts of a 巫 (shaman/sorcerer): divination, spirit-medium ritual, and folk magic broadly. It is essentially synonymous with [[法術]] ("magical technique"), the two overlapping heavily in scope.

Japanese ふじゅつ, Korean 무술, and Vietnamese vu thuật (verified — practitioners are called vu bà/vu sư, related to phù thủy "witch/sorcerer") are all standard readings. **A genuine Dan'a'yo homophone**: 巫術 and [[武術]] ("martial arts") share an identical reading (musud/무숟/ㄇㄨㄙㄨㄊ) despite being completely unrelated — the reciprocal callout was already correctly in place on both pages, just in a non-canonical format; standardized here.
