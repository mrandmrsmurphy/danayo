## Notes
- [禁](../characters/禁.md) "forbid, prohibit" + [錮 (char)](../characters/錮%20(char).md) "confine, imprison" — literally "forbid-confine."

禁錮 means to imprison or confine, pairing 禁 (the broad "prohibit" root, bound to [禁止](禁止.md)) with 錮, whose own etymology is concretely physical — originally "to solder or seal metal into cracks," extended to "to block, confine, imprison." Compare [監禁](監禁.md) and [軟禁](軟禁.md) (house arrest), other 禁-compounds for confinement, and 錮's own standalone use as a word in its own right.

Mandarin jìngù, Cantonese gam3 gu3, Korean 금고 (geumgo), and Japanese きんこ (kinko) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and each is the real legal/everyday term for imprisonment in its language. Vietnamese cấm cố is likewise standard Sino-Vietnamese, matching 禁's cấm (one of three attested readings on its own page) and 錮's own cố.
