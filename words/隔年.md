## Notes
- [隔 (char)](../characters/隔%20(char).md) "every other" + [年 (char)](../characters/年%20(char).md) "year"

隔年 means every other year, occurring once every two years — the same "every other" pattern as [[隔世紀]], [[隔月]], and [[隔日]], all built on the same 隔- prefix. Mandarin 隔年 (génián), Cantonese 隔年 (gaak3nin4), Japanese 隔年 (かくねん), Korean 격년 (gyeongnyeon), and Sino-Vietnamese cách năm all preserve this meaning compositionally across the CJKV sphere.
