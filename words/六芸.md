## Notes
- [六 (char)](../characters/六%20(char).md) "six" + [芸](../characters/芸.md) "art, skill" — the Six Arts, the core curriculum of a Zhou-dynasty nobleman's education, taught by the 保氏 official per the **《周禮·地官司徒·保氏》**. They originate in early Confucian tradition and became the model of elite education across East Asia. Mandarin liùyì, Cantonese luk6 ngai6, Japanese りくげい, Korean 육예, and Vietnamese lục nghệ are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 룩어 diverges from Sino-Korean 육예 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄨㄎ·ㄝ unique among both words and characters).

### The Six Arts
Each art names a general skill, and each also has its own specific numbered sub-curriculum documented elsewhere in the vault — the two levels are complementary, not competing:

1. **[[礼]] (Rei / Lǐ) — Rites.** Ritual propriety, ceremony, social order, moral formation; not mere etiquette, but the structure of civilization. Elaborated by [[五礼]], the Five Rites (auspicious, inauspicious, guest, military, festive).
2. **[[音楽]] (Gaku / Yuè) — Music.** Moral and emotional cultivation through ordered sound, harmonizing the soul with cosmic order. Elaborated by [[六楽]], the Six Musics.
3. **[[射術]] (Sha / Shè) — Archery.** Martial discipline combined with ritual precision; archery contests were moral exercises, not just military training. Elaborated by [[五射]], the Five Archery Techniques.
4. **[[御術]] (Gyo / Yù) — Charioteering.** Mastery of coordinated action, composure, and physical control. Elaborated by [[五馭]], the Five Charioteering Techniques.
5. **[[書法]] (Sho / Shū) — Calligraphy, Writing.** Literacy, textual transmission, cultural continuity. Elaborated by [[六書]], the Six Writings — though note that [[六書]]'s own page documents the well-known, separate Han-dynasty theory of six categories of character *formation* (象形/指事/形声/会意/転注/仮借, from the *Shuowen Jiezi*) rather than six calligraphic styles as such; the vault reuses this well-attested term for the Six Arts' writing component since both concepts genuinely share the name 六書 in real-world usage, but they are historically distinct ideas that happen to coincide under one name.
6. **[[数学]] (Sū / Shù) — Mathematics, Calculation.** Practical reckoning, calendrics, administration. Elaborated by [[九数]], the Nine Numbers (nine branches of practical mathematics later collected into the *Nine Chapters on the Mathematical Art*, 《九章算術》).

### Structural Insight
The Six Arts combine:
- Moral formation (礼, 楽)
- Martial discipline (射, 御)
- Intellectual skill (書, 数)
