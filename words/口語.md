## Notes
- [口 (char)](../characters/口%20(char).md) "mouth" + [語](../characters/語.md) "language" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (口's is bare 口, 語's is 言語).

Mandarin kǒuyǔ, Cantonese hau2 jyu5, Japanese こうご, and Korean 구어 are the standard readings. Vietnamese khẩu ngữ is a real, standard Sino-Vietnamese word.
