## Notes
- [治](../characters/治.md) "govern" + [安](../characters/安.md) "peaceful" — public order, security; law and order (維護治安 "to maintain public order"). Attested across Mandarin, Japanese, Korean, and Vietnamese (trị an).

The Law page lists 治安 in its Order & Restriction group, glossed "public order, public security," next to [秩序](秩序.md) "law and order, regularity." The difference is one of focus: 治安 is the state of public safety that police and authorities maintain (the object of 維護治安), while 秩序 is orderliness in general. It is also distinct from [安全](安全.md) "safety, security" and [平安](平安.md) "safe and well," which are not about public authority. Dan'a'yo keeps the sense "public order, public security." The Korean 치안 matches the Dan'a'yo 諺文 치안 exactly, so `kwin` is true. The readings are Mandarin zhì'ān, Cantonese zi6 on1, Japanese chian (ちあん) and Sino-Vietnamese trị an. The contrast with 秩序 and the 維護治安 example are from memory and the Law page's own wording, and have not been checked against a source.
