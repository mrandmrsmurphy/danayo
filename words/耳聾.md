## Notes
- [耳 (char)](../characters/耳%20(char).md) "ear" + [聾](../characters/聾.md) "deaf"
- This word is the viable form of [聾](../characters/聾.md): the character's meaning (deaf) is identical, but 聾 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 耳聾 is its `stand_in` compound.

聾 is a 形声 character with 耳 "ear" as its meaning component and 龍 as its sound, and means deaf or dull of hearing. Adding 耳 "ear" on the front spells the location out and makes the bound character a free word: "ear-deaf." In Classical Chinese 聾 is also used figuratively of the unheeding (as in the pairing of 聾 "deaf" with 瞽 "blind"), but the compound here is the plain physical condition, covering both complete deafness and being hard of hearing, as the English glosses indicate.

The readings are Mandarin ěrlóng, Cantonese ji5 lung4 and Korean 이롱. The Japanese form is not recorded in this page's frontmatter, so the field is left blank. Dan'a'yo keeps the shared sense, "deaf, hard of hearing," with no divergence. It is placed under [Sensation](../lexipedia/Sensation.md), beside the sibling words built on 耳 such as [耳根](耳根.md) and [耳目](耳目.md).
