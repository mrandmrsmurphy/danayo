## Notes
- [親](../characters/親.md) "close, dear" + [愛](../characters/愛%20(char).md) "love" — "close love," dear. Their own `stand_in` values: 親's is [[親戚]]; 愛's is itself; none points here, so 親愛 is an independent compound.

親愛 is dear: beloved, held close in affection, as in 親愛的朋友 "dear friend." Mandarin qīn'ài, Cantonese can1 oi3, Japanese しんあい and Korean 친애 all use it, and Vietnamese thân ái does too. It is the "dear" of affection; the "dear" of price is [[高価]] and the "dear" of worth is [[貴重]]. Compare [[愛]] (love) and [[可愛]] (cute).
