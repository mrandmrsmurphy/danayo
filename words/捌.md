## Notes
- [捌](../characters/捌%20(char).md) "to break apart, disentangle; of goods, to sell well" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [捌](../characters/捌%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

捌 means to break apart or disentangle; of goods, to sell well; also used as a formal financial numeral for "eight" (in place of 八, to prevent forgery).

Mandarin bā, Cantonese baat3, Japanese はち, Korean 팔, and Vietnamese bát are all standard, fully compositional readings. As the financial form of 八, 捌 deliberately shares 八's pronunciation, 注音 ㄅㄚㄊ, so that a written amount is read the same whichever form of "eight" is used; it also shares the syllable with [[鉢]] "bowl" (see the callout above).
