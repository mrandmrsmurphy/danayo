## Notes
- [水 (char)](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" + [彩](../characters/彩.md) "colour"

水彩 is watercolour: paint thinned with water, or a painting done in it. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin shuǐcǎi as "watercolour" and Japanese すいさい as "watercolour". Korean 수채 is the same word. Cantonese seoi2 coi2 matches.

It is set against the [油画](油画.md), oil painting; the East Asian ink kind is the [水墨画](水墨画.md).
