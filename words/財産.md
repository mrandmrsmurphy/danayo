## Notes
- [財](../characters/財.md) "wealth" + [産](../characters/産.md) "give birth"

財産 means property: what a person or an organization owns, and so wealth or a fortune. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 財產 (an alias, with 财产; Mandarin cáichǎn, Cantonese coi4 caan2) as "property" (property inheritance, company property), Japanese ざいさん as "property, fortune, riches" (to make a fortune, to inherit one's fortune), Korean 재산 as "public or private property", "monetary assets" in law, and "treasure", and Vietnamese tài sản as "property; assets; wealth".

[[財]] has boundedness 90 and cannot stand alone in Dan'a'yo; this word serves as its legitimizer, carrying the same meaning as the bare character. The act of owning it is [[所有]].
