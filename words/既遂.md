## Notes
- [既](../characters/既.md) "already" + [遂](../characters/遂.md) "accomplish" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [遂](../characters/遂.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (既's own stand-in is [[既以]]).

既遂 means to accomplish or complete.

Mandarin jìsuì, Cantonese gei3 seoi6, Japanese きすい, Korean 기수, and Vietnamese dĩ toại are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄜㄙㄨㄧ unique across both words and characters).
