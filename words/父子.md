## Notes
- [父](../characters/父.md) "father" + [子](../characters/子.md) "son, child" — "father and son," the parent-child relationship. One of the 天常 (Five Cardinal Relationships), paralleling [[君臣]] (ruler-subject). `vietnamese: phụ tử` is a directly attested classical Vietnamese term for this exact Confucian relationship. **The previously-documented homophone with [[夫子]] was found to be false and has been removed**: 父 and 夫 have genuinely different Middle Chinese initials (voiced v vs. voiceless f) and thus different Dan'a'yo pronunciations (諺文 부즈 vs. 쁘즈) — [[夫子]]'s own `羅馬字`/`注音` fields had mechanically copied this word's pattern instead of 夫's own correct romanization, creating the illusion of a shared sound; both pages have now been corrected. **A genuine homophone exists instead at the Korean level**: Sino-Korean doesn't distinguish 夫/父's MC voicing, so both read 부, making 父子/부자 and [[夫子]]/부자 real Korean homophones even though they diverge in Dan'a'yo. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 부즈 diverges from Sino-Korean 부자 in the second syllable. No homophones within the Dan'a'yo system itself (注音 ㄅㄨㄐㄜ unique among both words and characters).
