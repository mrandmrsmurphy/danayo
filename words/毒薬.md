## Notes
- [毒](../characters/毒%20(char).md) "poison" + [薬](../characters/薬%20(char).md) "medicine, drug" — "poison medicine," poison. Their own `stand_in` values: 毒's is itself; 薬's is itself; none points here, so 毒薬 is an independent compound.

毒薬 is poison: a substance that harms or kills. Mandarin dúyào (毒藥), Cantonese duk6 joek6 and Japanese どくやく are attested, and Korean 독약 is the same compound. It is the noun, where [[毒]] is the adjective "poisonous." Compare [[薬]] (medicine) and [[麻薬]] (narcotic).
