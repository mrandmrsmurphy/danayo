## Notes
- [恐](../characters/恐.md) "fear, dread" + [惧](../characters/惧.md) "fear, apprehend" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [惧](../characters/惧.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (恐's own stand-in is [[恐慌]]).

恐惧 means to be afraid of, dread, or have a phobia of — a near-synonymous doubling of two characters both meaning "fear."

Mandarin kǒngjù, Cantonese hung2 geoi6, Japanese きょうく, Korean 공구, and Vietnamese khủng cụ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄎㄛㄫㄍㄨ unique across both words and characters).
