## Notes
- [絵](../characters/絵.md) "draw, sketch" + [画](../characters/画.md) "draw" — `#cranberry`: both characters' own `stand_in` point to this exact compound (絵's and 画's alike), so transitivity holds (A=B=AB) and both are legitimized as independent Dan'a'yo entries specifically by this word. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

絵画 means to draw or paint, and as a noun, a painting or picture.

Mandarin huìhuà, Cantonese kui2 waa6-2 (with the tone-2 change common to this kind of compound noun), and Japanese かいが (kaiga) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 絵画 is itself a common attested word for "painting" in all three. Korean 회화 (hoehwa) uses 画's alternate reading 화 (as in 영화 "movie") rather than its own char-page default 획 (as in 計画/계획) — a genuine compound-specific alternation, analogous to 率/[[統率]]. Vietnamese hội hoạ is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard term for "painting, fine art." No Dan'a'yo homophones.
