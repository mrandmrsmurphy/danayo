## Notes
- [溝 (char)](../characters/溝%20(char).md) "ditch, drain" + [股 (char)](../characters/股%20(char).md) "crotch, groin, fork" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 溝's and 股's own stand-ins are themselves). 溝 here stands for the classical mathematical term 勾股 (the legs of a right triangle) — 溝 and 勾 happen to already be genuine Dan'a'yo homophones (both gou/곳/ㄍㄛㄨ), so no special reading substitution is needed.

溝股 means the Pythagorean theorem (from 勾股定理, the classical Chinese formulation using the "shorter leg" 勾/溝 and "longer leg" 股 of a right triangle).

Mandarin gōugǔ, Cantonese kau1 gu2, and Japanese こうこ are standard, fully compositional readings, matching 溝's and 股's own stored citations.
