## Notes
- [何 (char)](../characters/何%20(char).md) "what; which; interrogative" + [様 (char)](../characters/様%20(char).md) "manner; way; style; appearance"

何様 is the interrogative manner correlative — "how; in what way; by what method?" As a 副用名詞, it modifies a predicate by questioning its manner: 何様にすれば良いのか ("how should one go about it?"); 何様に解釈するか ("how should this be interpreted?"). The alias 怎么样 (zěnmeyàng, simplified) is the ubiquitous Mandarin "how/what's it like?" — perhaps the most frequently used manner interrogative in any CJKV language, ranging from method ("how do you do it?") to state inquiry ("how are you doing?") to suggestion ("how about it?").

Mandarin 怎麼樣 (zěnmeyàng) and 怎麼 (zěnme, "how; in what way") are the everyday equivalents, distinct from 何様's own compositional héyàng. Cantonese 點樣 (dim2 joeng6) or the shortened 點 (dim2) is the colloquial form, distinct from 何様's own ho4 joeng4. Japanese どのように (dono yō ni) is the everyday formal-manner phrase — but 何様 itself is also a real, attested Japanese word in its own right: なにさま (nanisama), used rhetorically/sarcastically ("何様のつもりだ", "who do you think you are?!"), built from 何's own native なに + 様's own native さま. Korean 어떻게 (eotteoke) is the everyday all-purpose manner interrogative, distinct from 何様's own Sino-Korean compositional 하양.

The alias 怎么样 is the simplified-character form of 怎麼樣, retained as a cross-reference for Mandarin readers who might not recognise the Dan'a'yo form immediately.
