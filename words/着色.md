## Notes
- [着 (char)](../characters/着%20(char).md) "to apply, put on" + [色](../characters/色.md) "color" — coloring.

着色 is coloring: applying color to something, whether by painting, staining or adding a coloring agent, and the finished state of being colored. It is the intentional counterpart of [[変色]] and the reverse of [[脱色]]. The artificial coloring agent added to food or cosmetics is a 着色料 in Japanese. Japanese ちゃくしょく and Korean 착색 are the standard readings; Mandarin writes it 著色 (zhuósè), which is the alias.
