## Notes
- [隼 (char)](../characters/隼%20(char).md) "peregrine falcon" — this word is 隼's own stand-in.

隼 refers to the peregrine falcon and, more broadly, falcons in general. Mandarin 隼 (sǔn), Cantonese 隼 (zeon2), Japanese 隼 (じゅん, though the native はやぶさ hayabusa is far more common — famously the name of Japan's asteroid-sample-return spacecraft), Korean 준 (jun), and Sino-Vietnamese chuẩn all preserve this meaning, though the character survives mainly bound in compounds or as a name element rather than everyday standalone vocabulary in most of these languages.
