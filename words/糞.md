## Notes
- [糞](../characters/糞%20(char).md) "poop, shit" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 糞 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo. The character's own reading was deliberately altered from the expected -n final to -m (fum rather than fun) to avoid crowding the ㄈㄨㄋ cluster already shared by 奮/芬/粉/雰.

Mandarin fèn, Cantonese fan3, and Korean 분 (bun) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading くそ (kuso) rather than the on-reading フン (FUN) for the bare character alone. Vietnamese phân is one of two stored candidates (alongside phẩn). No Dan'a'yo homophones.
