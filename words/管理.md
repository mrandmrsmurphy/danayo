## Etymology
- [管](../characters/管.md) "pipe, tube" + [理](../characters/理.md) "reason, manage"

## Notes
- "To manage, supervise, administer" — the standard, everyday Mandarin word for management in any domain (people, projects, resources, time). `vietnamese: quản lí` is directly attested and extremely common, the standard Vietnamese verb for the same sense. Filled blank `hsk_level: "4"` (verified).
- Unlike its exact homophone [[官吏]] (a classical noun for "government official," built from two "official"-sense roots), 管理's own roots are semantically unrelated to officialdom — 管 ("pipe/channel," extended to "administer, be in charge of") + 理 ("reason, principle," extended to "put in order, manage") — the identical Dan'a'yo pronunciation is coincidental, not a shared etymology. `kwin: true` confirmed (both constituents individually `true`, AND-rule). No other homophones beyond [[官吏]].
