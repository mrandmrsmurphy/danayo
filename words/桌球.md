## Notes
- [桌](../characters/桌%20(char).md) "table, desk, stand" + [球](../characters/球%20(char).md) "sphere, ball" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 桌 and 球's own stand-ins are themselves, each with its own independent word page).

桌球 means table tennis, or ping pong.

Mandarin zhuōqiú and Korean 탁구 are standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bugs: `cantonese` had a garbled initial (zoek3 → cheuk3, matching 桌's primary form) and `japanese` used an obsolete historical kana spelling (たくきう → たくきゅう). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field, added `pos: 名詞`. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄚㄎㄍ⼜ unique across both words and characters).
