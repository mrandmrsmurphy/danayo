## Notes
- [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" + [符](../characters/符.md) "token, ID, symbol" — no `#cranberry`: 音's own `stand_in` is [[音楽]], 符's is [[符号]]; neither points here.

音符 (yīnfú) is the standard Mandarin word for "musical note" (as a written symbol, e.g. ♪), and Cantonese 音符 (jam1fu4) likewise. Japanese おんぷ (onpu) and Korean 음부 (eumbu, a real synonym of the more common 음표) are both standard terms for the same sense. Sino-Vietnamese âm phù is likewise real and attested (alongside the more everyday native compound nốt nhạc).

In Chinese linguistics, 音符 also has a distinct secondary sense: "phonetic component" of a character (as opposed to 意符, "semantic component") — a different meaning from the musical-note sense treated here; 意符 does not yet have its own vault word page.
