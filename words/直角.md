## Notes
- [直 (char)](../characters/直%20(char).md) "straight, erect, aligned" + [角 (char)](../characters/角%20(char).md) "horn, angle" — literally "a straight/right angle," i.e. an angle formed by two perpendicular lines.

直角 is the basic geometric term for a right angle (90°), foundational to 直角三角形 ("right triangle") and countless other geometric compounds. Mandarin zhíjiǎo, Cantonese zik6 gok3, Japanese ちょっかく (chokkaku), and Korean 직각 (jikgak) are all fully compositional. Vietnamese uses the native góc vuông ("square angle") rather than a Sino-Vietnamese compound, paralleling how Vietnamese mathematical vocabulary often prefers native descriptive terms for basic geometric concepts.
