## Notes
- [容 (char)](../characters/容%20(char).md) "look; appearance; form; figure" + [器](../characters/器.md) "container; vessel" — this word is the stand-in for [器](../characters/器.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

容器 draws on 容's sense of "holding, containing" (rather than its "appearance" sense) paired with 器's own general word for a container or utensil — the compound is a near-synonym doubling, both halves independently pointing at the same core idea of an enclosed holding-vessel, the same construction pattern as [[狡猾]] or [[宏大]] elsewhere in this vault. 器 itself is broadly productive well beyond this one compound (机器 "machine," 陶器 "pottery," 呼吸器 "respirator," 凶器 "murder weapon"), with senses ranging from a literal vessel to any purpose-built implement or apparatus.

Japanese ようき and Korean 용기 are both plain, everyday, non-drifted readings matching the Mandarin/Cantonese sense directly. **`vietnamese` is left omitted**: neither Wiktionary nor any other checked source attests a standing Sino-Vietnamese compound for this word — modern Vietnamese uses native terms like *đồ đựng*/*hộp đựng* instead, consistent with 容 itself having no solid standalone Vietnamese life (see [[容]]'s own Notes). `kwin: false` follows directly from the AND-rule, since 器's own `kwin` is `false` even though 容's is `true`. No homophones (注音 ⼄ㄫㄎㄧㄜ unique across both words and characters).
