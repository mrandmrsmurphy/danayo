## Notes
- [胡](../characters/胡.md) "reckless" (here "foreign," as in the loanword-adjacent sense seen in [[胡瓜]]) is separately legitimized by [[胡乱]]. [芦](../characters/芦.md) "rushes, reed" is separately legitimized by [[芦葦]]. No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

胡芦 means bottle gourd, calabash — one of several traditional/simplified character-spelling variants of the same word (see `aliases`).

**Found and fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字` was missing 胡's own -u- glide (holo→houlo), the same single-field-only bug class as [[胡瓜]]'s fix — `諺文`/`注音` had already stayed correct. `kwin: false` was already correct per the AND-rule. Mandarin húlu, cantonese wu4lou4 (space removed), and korean 호로 (horo) are all standard readings. Vietnamese hồ lô is the real, commonly attested Vietnamese loanword for this term (not decomposable from 芦's own stored "lư," but independently verified as genuine). Japanese ころ is the compositional reading; Japanese has no independently attested native word for this specific compound (the everyday Japanese word for gourd, ひょうたん, is unrelated).
