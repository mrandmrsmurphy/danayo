## Notes
- [伯](../characters/伯.md) "father's older brother" + [母](../characters/母.md) "mother" (here, "wife of") — "aunt" specifically, the wife of one's father's older brother. Unlike the colloquial reduplication [[伯伯]], both `korean: 백모` and `vietnamese: bá mẫu` are genuine formal Sino-Korean/Sino-Vietnamese kinship terms directly attested (confirmed via web search) — bá mẫu, "bác gái," parallels the everyday native equivalent. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 박못 diverges from Sino-Korean 백모 in the second syllable's vowel/coda. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄎㄇㄛㄨ unique among both words and characters).
