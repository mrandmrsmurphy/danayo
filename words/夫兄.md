## Notes
- [夫 (char)](../characters/夫%20(char).md) "husband" + [兄 (char)](../characters/兄%20(char).md) "older brother" — husband's elder brother.

夫兄 is the older brother of one's husband. It is "husband-older brother," the husband-side counterpart of [[妻兄]]. No entry was found on English Wiktionary in any language, so the language fields are left blank; it is a schema word, completing the in-law set on the same pattern as [[妻兄]], [[妻弟]] and [[夫弟]]. The hypernym is [[姻兄]]. It is a schema word in the family of in-law terms built from the marriage relation and the sibling roots, coined for predictability rather than a claim about ancient usage.
