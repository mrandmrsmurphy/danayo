## Notes
- [長 (char)](../characters/長%20(char).md) "eldest, chief" + [女 (char)](../characters/女%20(char).md) "female" — eldest daughter.

長女 is the eldest daughter: the first-born female child of a family. It is "eldest-female." Mandarin zhǎngnǚ, Cantonese zoeng2 neoi5, Japanese ちょうじょ, Korean 장녀 and Vietnamese trưởng nữ are all confirmed on Wiktionary. Its pair is [[長男]]; the second daughter is [[次女]].
