## Notes
- [精](../characters/精.md) "spirit, essence, sperm" + [舎](../characters/舎.md) "inn, hut, mansion" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (精's is [[精神]], 舎's is [[宿舎]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 舎 is individually `kwin: false`.

精舎 translates Sanskrit विहार (vihāra) — a Buddhist monastery, temple, or retreat where monks or nuns dwell and practice, most famously the Jetavana vihāra (祇園精舎) associated with the Buddha's teachings.

Mandarin jīngshè, Cantonese zing1 se3, Japanese しょうじゃ (shōja), and Korean 정사 (jeongsa) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese tinh xá is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese form, and is itself the standard Vietnamese Buddhist term for a vihara/monastic retreat (as in the many Vietnamese monasteries named "Tinh xá ___").
