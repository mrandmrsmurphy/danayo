## Notes
- [硯](../characters/硯.md) "inkstone" + [石 (char)](../characters/石%20(char).md) "rock" — stand-in for [[硯]], which cannot appear independently.

硯石 is the stone slab on which an ink stick is ground with water to produce liquid ink for brush writing and painting — one of the **文房四寶** ("Four Treasures of the Study"), alongside the brush ([[筆]]), ink stick, and paper. Fine inkstones, carved from specific prized stone sources (端硯 from Duanzhou, 歙硯 from Shexian being the most celebrated), are themselves valued as art objects and collector's items, not merely functional tools.

Japanese けんせき and Korean 연석 are both standard compositional Sino-readings. Vietnamese nghiễn thạch is likewise standard. No specific HSK level applies to a term this specialized. No homophones (注音 ㄝㄋㄙㄝㄎ unique across both words and characters).
