## Notes
- [硯](../characters/硯.md) "inkstone" + [石 (char)](../characters/石%20(char).md) "rock"

硯石 is an inkstone, a stone mortar for grinding and holding ink. English Wiktionary has no entry for 硯石 itself; it glosses the character 硯 (Chinese) as "inkstone". English Wikipedia describes the inkstone as part of traditional Chinese stationery, used in calligraphy and painting, and names two rock types used for the best ones: the Duanxi stone (端溪) and the Shexian stone. The Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese fields are built from the readings of the two characters.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [硯](../characters/硯.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
