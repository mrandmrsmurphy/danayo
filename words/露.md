## Notes
- [露 (char)](../characters/露%20(char).md) "dew" — this word is 露's own legitimizing compound (露's `stand_in` points here).

露 means "dew," the moisture that condenses overnight on cool surfaces. Mandarin lù, Cantonese lou6, and Sino-Korean 로 (ro) all use this Sino form productively in fixed compounds (e.g. [[甘露]] "nectar," 露天 "outdoors"), though everyday Korean instead uses the native word 이슬 for ordinary dew. Japanese has both this Sino-Japanese reading ろ (ro, in compounds like 披露) and the native つゆ (tsuyu), the latter being the everyday word — and, when written with different characters (梅雨), also the word for the East Asian rainy season. Sino-Vietnamese lộ is likewise attested but the ordinary Vietnamese word for dew is the native sương (or sương mù for fog/mist generally).
