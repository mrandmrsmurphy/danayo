## Etymology
- [辦](../characters/辦.md) "manage, run" + [公 (char)](../characters/公%20(char).md) "sir" + [室](../characters/室.md) "room"

## Notes
Denotes the room itself, not the whole office building (for the building, use [[事務所]]).

Mandarin has two attested pronunciations (bàngōngshì/bàngōngshǐ, reflecting 室's own multiple readings), cantonese baan6 gung1 sat1, and korean 판공실 are all directly attested standard readings. Japanese べんこうしつ and vietnamese biện công thất are both honest compositional readings (辦's own BEN/biện + 公's own KOU/công + 室's own SHITSU/thất); modern Japanese instead uses オフィス (the English loanword) or 事務室 for "office," but no independently attested reading using these exact characters was found.
