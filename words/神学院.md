## Notes
- [神 (char)](../characters/神%20(char).md) "god" + [学](../characters/学.md) "learn" + [院](../characters/院.md) "institution" — literally "god-study institution."

神学院 means a seminary: an institution for training clergy in theology. English Wiktionary lists it only for Chinese, as 神學院 (Mandarin shénxuéyuàn, Cantonese san4 hok6 jyun6-2, with 院 taking the changed tone jyun2 recorded here), glossed "seminary; divinity school; theological seminary". It has no entry for Japanese しんがくいん, Korean 신학원 or Vietnamese thần học viện, so those readings are built from the characters.

The last character is the 院 of [[学院]], an academy, and of [[修道院]], a monastery or convent.
