## Notes
- [神 (char)](../characters/神%20(char).md) "god" + [学](../characters/学.md) "learn" + [院](../characters/院.md) "institution" — literally "god-study institution."

神学院 means a seminary: an institution for training clergy in theology. Mandarin shénxuéyuàn, Cantonese san4 hok6 jyun2, Japanese しんがくいん, Korean 신학원, and Vietnamese thần học viện are all standard, fully compositional readings across the CJKV sphere — Vietnamese thần học viện literally parallels the Sino-character structure exactly ("god-study-institute").
