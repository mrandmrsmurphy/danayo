## Notes
- [凍](../characters/凍.md) "freeze" + [結 (char)](../characters/結%20(char).md) "tie; join" — stand-in for [[凍]], which cannot appear independently

凍結 means to freeze: literally, for water or other liquid to turn to ice (凍結成冰, "freeze into ice"; 冰凍, "frozen"), and figuratively to suspend or hold in place, as assets, wages, an account, or a process (資産凍結, "asset freeze"; 凍結帳戶, "freeze an account"; 凍結計畫, "suspend a plan"). The compound joins 凍 "freeze, cold" with 結 "tie, bind", the sense of water binding into a solid.

Mandarin dòngjié, Cantonese dung3 git3, Japanese とうけつ (tōketsu), Korean 동결 (donggyeol), and Vietnamese đông kết all cover both senses in parallel: the literal physical "freeze" and the shared figurative extension to "freeze" financial assets or a process (資産凍結/자산동결, "asset freeze"), the same metaphor independently applied across the whole sphere. Vietnamese also has the native đóng băng tài khoản for freezing an account.
