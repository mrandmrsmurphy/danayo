## Notes
- [不 (char)](../characters/不%20(char).md) "not" + [在 (char)](../characters/在%20(char).md) "exist; be at"

不在 is absence: not being at the place where one is expected. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin bùzài as "to be not at; to be out (of something)" and, as a euphemism, "to be dead". Japanese ふざい is "absence". Cantonese bat1 zoi6 and Korean 부재 match.

It is the opposite of [在](在.md) and is close to [缺席](缺席.md), being absent from a gathering, which is the more specific case. [無](無.md) says that a thing is not there at all, where 不在 says it is not here.
