## Notes
- [皇](../characters/皇.md) "emperor" + [太 (char)](../characters/太%20(char).md) "grand" + [子](../characters/子.md) "child"

皇太子 is the crown prince. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 皇太子 (Mandarin huángtàizǐ, Cantonese wong4 taai3 zi2) as "crown prince" and Japanese こうたいし as "crown prince". It lists Korean 황태자 and Vietnamese entries. Compare [王子](王子.md), a prince.
