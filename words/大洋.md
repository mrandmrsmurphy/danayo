## Notes
- [大](../characters/大%20(char).md) "great" + [洋](../characters/洋.md) "ocean" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [洋](../characters/洋.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (大's own stand-in is itself).

大洋 means ocean — larger in scope than [[海洋]] (sea).

Japanese たいよう, Korean 대양, Mandarin dàyáng, and Cantonese daai6 joeng4 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching Dan'a'yo's own reading exactly (`kwin: true`). Vietnamese đại dương is a standard reading. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄧ·⼘ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
