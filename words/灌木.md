## Notes
- [灌](../characters/灌.md) "bush, shrub" + [木](../characters/木%20(char).md) "tree, wood" — "bush tree," a shrub. Their own `stand_in` values: 灌's is [[灌漑]]; 木's is itself; none points here, so 灌木 is an independent compound.

灌木 is a bush or shrub: a low woody plant with several stems, with no single trunk. Mandarin guànmù, Cantonese gun3 muk6, Japanese かんぼく and Korean 관목 all use it. It follows the vault's X木 naming convention for woody plants, as in [[樹木]]. Compare [[林]] (grove) and [[森林]] (forest).
