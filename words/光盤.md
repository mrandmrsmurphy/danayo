## Notes
- [光](../characters/光%20(char).md) "light" + [盤](../characters/盤%20(char).md) "plate, disk" — "optical disc." Their own `stand_in` values: 光's is itself; 盤's is itself; none points here, so 光盤 is an independent compound.

光盤 is an optical disc such as a CD. Mandarin guāngpán and Cantonese gwong1 pun4 are the ordinary Chinese words in Mainland usage (Taiwan and Hong Kong write 光碟). Japanese says コンパクトディスク and Korean 시디, so those two fields hold the readings of the characters. Compare [[磁盤]] (disk) and [[光]] (light).
