## Notes
- [服](../characters/服.md) "serve" + [事 (char)](../characters/事%20(char).md) "thing, affair, duty" — to serve, to attend to; to be in someone's service or carry out a duty — stand-in for [[服]], which cannot appear independently.

An alternative form of the more common 服侍, with the same meaning. Japanese ふくじ (also read ぶくじ) and Korean 복사 are both attested, straightforward on'yomi/Sino-Korean concatenations of the two characters' own readings — Korean 복사 additionally carries a specific liturgical sense in Christian usage, "a layperson who assists a priest during Mass," alongside the general "serve in obedience" meaning. Vietnamese *phục sự* is a transparent Sino-Vietnamese compound of the same two morphemes.
