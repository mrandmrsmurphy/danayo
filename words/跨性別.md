## Notes
- [跨 (char)](../characters/跨%20(char).md) "straddle, ride" + [性](../characters/性.md) "sex, gender, nature" + [別 (char)](../characters/別%20(char).md) "distinct, other" — no stand-in relationship applies (跨's own stand-in is itself; 別's own is itself).

跨性別 means transgender — literally "crossing/straddling sex/gender," i.e. a gender identity that differs from the sex assigned at birth.

This compound is genuinely current in Mandarin (kuà xìngbié) and Cantonese (kwaa3 sing3 bit6), the standard written translation for "transgender" across the Sinosphere's press and LGBT advocacy. Japanese and Korean, however, do not use a compositional kanji/hanja reading in everyday speech — the real living words are the English loanwords トランスジェンダー (toransujendā) and 트랜스젠더 (teuraenseujendeo) respectively; こせいべつ and 과성별 above are the compositional readings this vault stores for consistency, not attested native terms. Vietnamese likewise prefers its own native/calque term, người chuyển giới ("person who changes gender") or bare chuyển giới, over any Sino-Vietnamese compositional form; khoá tính biệt above is a defensible derivation, not an independently attested fixed term. `kwin` is false per the AND-rule: 跨, 性, and 別 are all individually `kwin: false`.
