## Notes
- [復](../characters/復.md) "restore" + [活](../characters/活%20(char).md) "alive" — no stand-in relationship applies (復's own stand-in is [[回復]]; 活's own is itself).

復活 means to revive, or to bring back to life — as in the Christian Easter (復活祭, [[復活節]]) or the resurgence of something dormant.

Mandarin fùhuó, Cantonese fuk6 wut6, Japanese ふっかつ, Korean 부활, and Vietnamese phục hoạt are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed missing space in `cantonese`. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄨㄎㄏ⺢ㄊ unique across both words and characters).
