## Notes
- [地 (char)](../characters/地%20(char).md) "earth" + [軸 (char)](../characters/軸%20(char).md) "axle; axis"

地軸 is the earth's axis: the line through the poles on which the earth turns. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin dìzhóu and Japanese ちじく as "the axis of the earth's rotation". The Cantonese reading is built from the characters, and Korean 지축 matches.

The earth turns on it in a day, the [自転](自転.md), and its slant to the orbit makes the seasons, with the [冬至](冬至.md) and [夏至](夏至.md) marking the extremes. The planet itself is the [地球](地球.md).
