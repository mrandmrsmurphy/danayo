## Notes
- [爪 (char)](../characters/爪%20(char).md) "claw" + [哇 (char)](../characters/哇%20(char).md) "wow" — a transliteration of the name of the island, so the characters carry no meaning of their own; no stand-in relationship applies (爪's own stand-in is the single-character word [[爪]], 哇's is [[哇]]).

爪哇 is Java, the island of Indonesia. English Wiktionary describes it as a transcription of the island's name (Javanese Jawa), attested from the Yuan dynasty in Wang Dayuan's *Daoyi zhilüe* and the *History of Yuan*, and ultimately from Sanskrit यवद्वीप (yavadvīpa, "island of Yava"), from यव "barley" + द्वीप "island". Earlier Chinese names for the island were the transcriptions 闍婆, 社婆, 訶陵 and 耶婆提 (the last from yavadvīpa), none of which is a word in the vault. English Wiktionary also lists a rare sense, the programming language Java, and the Hokkien synonym 交拉吧.

Mandarin Zhǎowā and Cantonese zaau2 waa1 are as at English Wiktionary. Japanese ジャワ is the standard name, and English Wiktionary notes that 爪哇 is the obsolete ateji spelling of it. Korean 자바 is the ordinary Korean name of the island (from memory, not re-checked), so `kwin` is false. The `vietnamese` field holds the compositional Hán-Việt reading trảo oa (each character as its page gives it; not attested for this name).

Mandarin 爪 is read zhǎo in this name, and the Dan'a'yo reading of 爪 is ㄐ⺢ㄨ throughout. No homophones (注音 ㄐ⺢ㄨ⺢ㄨ unique across words and characters, checked 2026-10-12). The 注音 needs no syllable-boundary dot.
