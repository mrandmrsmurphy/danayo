## Notes
- [昇](../characters/昇.md) "ascend" + [叙](../characters/叙.md) "express, state, relate, narrate" — no stand-in relationship applies (昇's own stand-in is [[上昇]]; 叙's own is [[叙述]]).

昇叙 means to be promoted or advanced (in rank).

Mandarin shēngxù, Cantonese sing1 zeoi6, Japanese しょうじょ, Korean 승서, and Vietnamese thăng tự are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed garbled `japanese` (しょう叙じょ, a stray kanji embedded mid-reading) to しょうじょ. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨㄫㄙ⼄ unique across both words and characters).
