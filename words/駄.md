## Notes
- [駄 (char)](/characters/駄%20(char).md) "pack, carry" — the modern Japanese shinjitai form of 馱, a genuine ancient Chinese variant rather than a Japan-only invention. 駄 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

Japanese だ (da) is the practically-attested on'yomi (as in 駄目, 無駄, 駄菓子), used here in preference to た (ta), which is technically listed on the character page but essentially unused in real vocabulary. Sino-Vietnamese offers two forms — đà and thồ (chosen here as the representative form, matching everyday usage for "to carry a pack load," e.g. ngựa thồ, "pack horse").
