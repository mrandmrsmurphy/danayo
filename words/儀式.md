## Notes
- [儀](../characters/儀.md) "ceremony, deportment" + [式](../characters/式.md) "form, rite" — "ritual ceremony" (卒業儀式, "graduation ceremony"; 結婚儀式, "wedding ceremony"). Mandarin yíshì, Cantonese ji4 sik1, Japanese ぎしき, Korean 의식, and Vietnamese nghi thức are all directly attested standard readings.

Legitimizing note: 儀's own `stand_in` is this exact word (儀式), so 儀 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 儀式.

Earlier Notes on this page may state a different value or a different reason.
