## Notes
- [支](../characters/支.md) "branch" + [配](../characters/配%20(char).md) "match, pair" — no stand-in relationship applies (支's own stand-in is [[支部]]; 配's own is itself).

支配 means to rule, control, manage, govern, or dominate.

Mandarin zhīpèi, Cantonese zi1 pui3, Japanese しはい, Korean 지배, and Vietnamese chi phối are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄝㄆㄛㄧ unique across both words and characters).
