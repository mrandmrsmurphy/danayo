## Notes
- [陰 (char)](../characters/陰%20(char).md) "Yin, shade" + [道 (char)](../characters/道%20(char).md) "way, path"

陰道 is the standard anatomical term for the vagina. Mandarin 陰道/阴道 (yīndào), Cantonese 陰道 (jam1dou6), Japanese 陰道 (いんどう), Korean 음도 (eumdo), and Sino-Vietnamese âm đạo are all the medical term used across the CJKV sphere, straightforwardly compositional and consistent in every language, unlike the "opposite polarity" divergence seen with 陰茎/dương vật.
