## Etymology
- [内](../characters/内.md) "inside" + [省 (char)](../characters/省%20(char).md) "to examine, reflect" (the xǐng reading, as in 反省 "self-reflection" — not the unrelated shěng "province, ministry" reading that is this character's more common Mandarin sense)

## Notes
**Real content bug found and fixed**: the previous Etymology gloss for 省 read "government ministry" — wrong sense entirely for this word. 省 is polysemous (shěng "province/ministry" vs. xǐng "to examine, reflect"), and 内省 uses the reflective xǐng sense exclusively, paralleling 反省 (phản tỉnh, "self-reflection").

`vietnamese: nội tỉnh` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese (confirmed via web search), a formal/academic term for introspection, distinct in register from the more common phản tỉnh ("to reconsider, reflect on past actions"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 뇌셩 diverges from Sino-Korean 내성 in the first syllable's vowel.
