## Notes
- [拂](../characters/拂%20(char).md) "shake off, brush off" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [拂](../characters/拂%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

拂 means to shake off or brush off.

Mandarin fú, Cantonese fat1, Japanese ふつ, Korean 불, and Vietnamese phất are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones at the word level (注音 ㄈㄨㄊ is shared only with bound characters 弗/沸/黻/彿, none of which have an independent word at this exact reading — all bound to longer stand-in compounds).
