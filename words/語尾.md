## Notes
- [語](../characters/語.md) "language" + [尾 (char)](../characters/尾%20(char).md) "tail"

語尾 is the ending of a word or phrase. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 語尾 (Mandarin yǔwěi, Cantonese jyu5 mei5) as the grammar term "end of a sentence" and, with 詞尾 as an alternative name, "suffix; ending", and Japanese ごび as "end of a spoken phrase" and the grammar term "ending; inflectional suffix". It lists Korean 어미 as a hanja form of "ending (of a word)". The Dan'a'yo gloss is the ending of a word, the sense the Chinese and Japanese grammar senses share. Compare [語幹](語幹.md), the stem.
