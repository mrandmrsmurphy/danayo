## Notes
- [禁](../characters/禁.md) "prohibit, forbid" + [漁](../characters/漁.md) "fish, fishing" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (禁's is [[禁止]], 漁's is [[釣漁]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

禁漁 names a fishing ban — a regulatory closed season or prohibited zone where fishing is not permitted, typically to protect fish stocks during spawning or to allow depleted populations to recover. It appears in compounds like 禁漁期 ("closed fishing season") and 禁漁区 ("no-fishing zone"), a common category of environmental/fisheries regulation alongside 禁猟 ("hunting ban") for land animals.

Mandarin jìnyú, Cantonese gam3 jyu4, Japanese きんりょう (kinryō), and Korean 금어 (geumeo, as in 금어기, "closed fishing season") are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese cấm ngư is likewise compositional.
