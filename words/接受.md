## Notes
- [接](../characters/接%20(char).md) "contact, connect" + [受](../characters/受.md) "accept, receive" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [受](../characters/受.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (接's own stand-in is itself).

接受 means to accept or receive.

Mandarin jiēshòu, Cantonese zip3 sau6, Japanese せつじゅ, Korean 접수, and Vietnamese tiếp thụ are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄛㄆㄙ⼜ unique across both words and characters).
