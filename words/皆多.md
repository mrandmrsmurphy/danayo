## Notes
- [皆 (char)](../characters/皆%20(char).md) "all; every; universal" + [多 (char)](../characters/多%20(char).md) "many; much; numerous"

皆多 is the collective (universal) correlative in the 多 ("amount") column of the correlative table: "the whole amount, all of it, the total quantity; so much in total". The character [皆 (char)](../characters/皆%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *gye*, ㄍ⼶) is the quantifier "all; every", the free word being [皆](皆.md); [多 (char)](../characters/多%20(char).md) (Dan'a'yo *dǝ*, ㄉㄜ) is "many; much", and the word page [多](多.md) is the stative "many; much". Where the distributive [毎多](毎多.md) "each amount" applies a quantity to each case in turn, 皆多 treats the quantity as one totality: the whole amount, taken together, with nothing left out. Illustrative only (invented, not from `grammar/`): 皆多、足 "the whole amount is enough".

Its part of speech is 副用名詞 (adverbial noun), as in the other cells of the 多 column ([此多](此多.md), [其多](其多.md), [彼多](彼多.md), [何多](何多.md), [毎多](毎多.md), [某多](某多.md)). `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md` (line 11) lists "Adverbial Nouns" as the class able to be the frame with 0-derivation, and `grammar/Linguistics Features.md` (line 68) states that an adverbial noun may be the topic or frame of a clause with no marking but needs marking when it modifies the main verb. This differs from most of the 皆 row, whose cells ([皆事](皆事.md), [皆物](皆物.md), [皆人](皆人.md), [皆名](皆名.md), [皆処](皆処.md), [皆時](皆時.md), [皆類](皆類.md)) are 代詞 (pronoun); only [皆様](皆様.md) in that row is 副用名詞. The chart itself gives only form and 注音 with no part of speech, so the choice follows the sibling convention: the column (quantity, adverbial) takes precedence over the row here, as it does for [皆様](皆様.md). Note that this is a convention inferred from the sibling pages, not something the grammar files state.

It contrasts with its column neighbours as follows. [毎多](毎多.md) is the distributive "each amount", the same split as [毎人](毎人.md) against [皆人](皆人.md). [某多](某多.md) is the indefinite "some amount" (not a universal) and [何多](何多.md) the interrogative "how much/many". [此多](此多.md), [其多](其多.md) and [彼多](彼多.md) point at one particular amount (proximal, medial, distal), where 皆多 covers the whole. [多](多.md) alone is the stative "many; much", and [多少](多少.md) "amount; quantity" is the plain noun and not a correlative. Among the vault's words for totality, [全部](全部.md) "entirety; everything", [全体](全体.md) "whole; entirety", [一切](一切.md) "entirety; whole" and [所有](所有.md) "all; every (determiner)" are ordinary vocabulary, while 皆多 is the regular correlative form specifically of quantity, built from the table's own 皆.

The cross-linguistic fields were checked against Wiktionary on 2026-10-11. Wiktionary has no entry for 皆多, so no `mandarin`, `japanese`, `korean` or `vietnamese` field is entered: the word is a Dan'a'yo compositional construction, and none of the readings the page held before (Mandarin quánbù, Japanese 全部, Korean 전부, Vietnamese tất cả) is a reading of the compound; they were equivalents of the meaning, and are removed. The earlier claim that 皆多 is "identical in form" to Japanese 全部 was wrong: the characters differ. Established neighbours on Wiktionary: Chinese 全部 (quánbù; Cantonese cyun4 bou6) is a noun "all; everything; whole; entirety", an attributive adjective "entire; whole" and an adverb "all; entirely; completely"; the same entry gives Japanese 全部 (ぜんぶ) as a noun "the whole thing; everything; all" and an adverb "all, entirely, totally, completely", Korean 전부 and Vietnamese *toàn bộ* as the hanja/Han-tu spellings glossed "everything, all". Chinese 一切 (yīqiè) is a pronoun "all; every; everything; anything" (the Japanese 一切 is a noun "all; entirety; the whole" and, with negation, an adverb "at all"). The vault has word files for [全部](全部.md) and [一切](一切.md), so the real everyday equivalents are linked there rather than copied here. Korean 모두 (modu) and Vietnamese *tất cả* are idiomatic equivalents from memory, not re-checked; hvdic.thivien.net covers single characters, not phrases, so no Vietnamese reading was verified or entered. Dan'a'yo keeps the sense shared across CJKV, "the whole amount; all of it"; no narrowing is needed.

On [Grammar](../lexipedia/Grammar.md)'s correlative table (line 126 is the 皆 ("all") row; the same chart is in `grammar/文法 - 97品詞.md`, line 151 in the 多 ("amount") row, with the column header cell 皆 on line 142), 皆多 is the eighth cell of the 皆 row, after [皆事](皆事.md) "all matters", [皆物](皆物.md) "all things", [皆人](皆人.md) "all people", [皆名](皆名.md) "all honoured persons", [皆処](皆処.md) "all places", [皆時](皆時.md) "all times" and [皆様](皆様.md) "all manners", and before [皆類](皆類.md) "all kinds". The table is 7 determiners (此, 其, 彼, 何, 毎, 某, 皆) × 9 noun classes (事, 物, 人, 名, 処, 時, 様, 多, 類), 63 forms, so 皆多 is parsed compositionally: 皆 = collective, 多 = amount. The chart gives no gloss, so the sense is derived from the two character glosses and from the contrast with [毎多](毎多.md).

The spellings are the plain concatenation of the two character pages: 羅馬字 *gye* + *dǝ* = gyedǝ, 諺文 겨 + 드 = 겨드, 注音 ㄍ⼶ + ㄉㄜ = ㄍ⼶ㄉㄜ, each checked letter by letter against 皆 (char) and 多 (char). The 注音 carries no syllable-boundary dot. `grammar/文法 - 02音韻論.md` allows only the nasals ㄇ ㄋ ㄫ, the glides and the unreleased stops ㄆ ㄊ ㄎ as syllable-final consonants, and ㄉ is none of them, so ㄍ⼶ㄉ is not a syllable (`syllables/` has ㄍ⼶, ㄍ⼶ㄇ, ㄍ⼶ㄋ, ㄍ⼶ㄧ and ㄍ⼶ㄫ, but no ㄍ⼶ㄉ) and ㄉㄜ must be a syllable of its own (it exists in `syllables/`). There is no second complete partition, the same as [毎多](毎多.md), [皆処](皆処.md) and [皆事](皆事.md), and in contrast to [皆人](皆人.md), whose second syllable begins with ㄋ and so needs the dot.
