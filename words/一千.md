## Notes
- [一](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" + [千](../characters/千.md) "thousand" — legitimizing note: 千's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, so 千 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word; 一's own `stand_in` is 一 itself, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply.

Mandarin yīqiān, Cantonese jat1 cin1, and Korean 일천 are all standard compositional readings. Japanese いっせん shows the same sokuon assimilation of いち before an unvoiced-obstruent-initial reading already documented on [[一体]]/[[一兆]] (いち + せん → いっせん) — **fixed a real bug**: the field had been bare せん, missing the いち prefix entirely. Vietnamese một nghìn is the real, everyday native numeral phrase (rather than Sino-Vietnamese "nhất thiên," which is not the standard term), matching the same native-numeral preference already established on [[一万]]'s "mười nghìn." **Fixed a real `kwin` bug**: was `true`, contradicting the AND-rule (一 false, 千 true) — corrected to `false`.
