## Notes
- [肌](../characters/肌.md) "skin, surface, texture" + [膚](../characters/膚.md) "skin" — 肌's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 膚's own is [[皮膚]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 肌膚 legitimizes 肌 alone.

肌膚 means skin.

Mandarin jīfū, Cantonese gei1 fu1, Japanese きふ (kifu), and Korean 기부 (gibu) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 肌膚 is itself a common attested word for "skin" in all four languages. Vietnamese cơ phu is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading.
