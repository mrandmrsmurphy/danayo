## Notes
- [肌](../characters/肌.md) "skin, surface, texture" + [膚](../characters/膚.md) "skin" — 肌's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 膚's own is [[皮膚]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 肌膚 legitimizes 肌 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: both constituents are individually `kwin: false`.

肌膚 means skin.

Mandarin jīfū, Cantonese gei1 fu1, Japanese きふ (kifu), and Korean 기부 (gibu) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 肌膚 is itself a common attested word for "skin" in all four languages. Vietnamese cơ phu is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. **Fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字`/`諺文` had giǝpu/긔푸 (a p-initial), mismatching 膚's real f-initial reading (fǝ/쁘, per the vault's established ㄈ→ㅃ convention) — `注音` (ㄍㄧㄜㄈㄜ) had stayed correct throughout. Filled blank korean/vietnamese, added missing kwin. No homophones.
