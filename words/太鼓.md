## Notes
- [太 (char)](../characters/太%20(char).md) "big" + [鼓 (char)](../characters/鼓%20(char).md) "drum"

太鼓 is a drum: a big drum, and in particular the Japanese taiko. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin tàigǔ as "taiko drums" and Japanese たいこ as "a drum". Korean 태고 is the reading of the characters. Cantonese taai3 gu2 matches.

It is a large [鼓](鼓.md), drum, and the hand-held rattle drum is the [鞀鼓](鞀鼓.md), pellet drum.
