**Found and fixed a real bug**: `羅馬字` had mid'wet, a wrong final consonant on 月's own syllable (諺文/注音 had already stayed correct: 믿웓/ㄇㄧㄊ·⼔ㄊ both correctly end in -d/ㄊ) — corrected to mid'wed. **Found and fixed a real `kwin` bug** (true→false, AND-rule: 月 individually false). Mandarin mìyuè, japanese みつげつ, korean 밀월 (all also the real common words for "honeymoon"), and vietnamese mật nguyệt (a real attested classical term) all confirmed standard and compositional. **`kwin` update (2026-10-09):** `kwin` is now true. The Notes above may describe the ㄷ/ㄹ difference between the Dan'a'yo 諺文 and the Korean reading as a mismatch; under the vault's standing rule a Korean final ㄹ counts as equal to Dan'a'yo's final ㄷ for this comparison, so such a difference alone does not make `kwin` false.
