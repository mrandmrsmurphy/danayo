## Notes
- [昭 (char)](../characters/昭%20(char).md) + [明 (char)](../characters/明%20(char).md) — a literary fixed compound, "shining and bright." Attested as a Mandarin/Cantonese dictionary headword distinct from the two characters merely standing side by side; also the basis of 昭明太子/昭明文選 (Crown Prince Zhaoming, the Liang-dynasty literary anthology). No Japanese or Korean dictionary entry was found for the combined form.

The Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese fields, which had been left blank, now hold the compositional Sino-xenic readings of the two characters (しょうめい, 소명, chiêu minh), following the vault's convention that these fields record the character readings even where the compound is not an attested headword in that language. The Korean 소명 differs from the Dan'a'yo 諺文 좃명 in the first syllable, so `kwin` is false. The Light page lists the word as "shining and bright, luminous — a literary fixed compound," and Dan'a'yo keeps that sense. It is a literary relative of the plain [光明](光明.md) "light, brightness," and it is the phrase that, as I recall, supplies the era name [昭和](昭和.md) (Shōwa): the 《尚書·堯典》 line 百姓昭明，協和萬邦 contains both 昭明 and 協和 (a recollection, not re-checked against the text). The 昭明太子 and 昭明文選 references are the page's existing account and have not been re-checked here.
