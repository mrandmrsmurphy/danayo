## Notes
- [賞 (char)](../characters/賞%20(char).md) "reward, prize" + [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "money; metal" — prize money.

賞金 is prize money or a monetary reward: money given for winning a contest, catching a fugitive, or doing something well. It is "reward-money." Mandarin shǎngjīn, Cantonese soeng2 gam1, Japanese しょうきん and Korean 상금 are all confirmed on Wiktionary, and Mandarin lists 獎金 (jiǎngjīn) as a synonym. Dan'a'yo uses 賞金 for prize or reward money as such, and [[賞与金]] for pay-linked bonuses: the first leans toward a prize, as in English "prize money," the second toward a bonus added to pay. Compare [[報償]] (compensation).
