## Etymology
- [参](../characters/参.md) + [差](../characters/差.md) — a classical binome (聯綿詞), "uneven, irregular, jagged" (参差不齊, "uneven, of varying quality/height"); the two characters don't carry independent meanings here the way they do elsewhere.

## Notes
Real Mandarin uses a special reading, cēncī, that diverges from 参's own more common cān ("to participate") and shēn ("ginseng") readings — a classical binomial-word phenomenon where the reading is fixed to the two-character unit rather than composed from each character's regular value. `japanese`, `korean`, and `vietnamese` are filled compositionally from each character's own regular reading rather than this special binome value, since no independent attestation was found for how (or whether) this specific classical term is naturalized in those languages (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted) — flagged here rather than asserted as confirmed usage. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 삼차 matches the compositional Sino-Korean 삼차 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄚㄇㄑㄚ unique among both words and characters).
