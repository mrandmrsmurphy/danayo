## Notes
`vietnamese: tuấn kiệt` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese (confirmed via web search), well known as a common given name — "an outstanding, exceptionally talented person," leaning toward intellect/wisdom rather than 英雄/anh hùng's action-and-military connotation. Filled the blank `cantonese: zeon3 git6`, compositional.

**Real kwin bug found and fixed**: `kwin` was stored as true, but byte-level Unicode verification shows Dan'a'yo 준걷 (U+AC77, coda ㄷ) genuinely diverges from Sino-Korean 준걸 (U+AC78, coda ㄹ) — the same recurring Middle-Chinese-checked-coda-vs-Sino-Korean-regularized pattern seen elsewhere in this vault. Corrected to `kwin: false`. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄨㄋㄍㄝㄊ unique among both words and characters).
