## Notes
- [俊](../characters/俊.md) "outstanding talent" + [傑](../characters/傑.md) "masterful, remarkable" — `vietnamese: tuấn kiệt` is directly attested Sino-Vietnamese, well known as a common given name — "an outstanding, exceptionally talented person," leaning toward intellect/wisdom rather than 英雄/anh hùng's action-and-military connotation. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 준걷 (U+AC77, coda ㄷ) genuinely diverges from Sino-Korean 준걸 (U+AC78, coda ㄹ). No homophones (注音 ㄐㄨㄋㄍㄝㄊ unique among both words and characters).
