## Notes
- [冷 (char)](../characters/冷%20(char).md) "cold" + [笑 (char)](../characters/笑%20(char).md) "laugh" — literally "a cold laugh." No stand-in relationship applies (both characters' own `stand_in` point to themselves).

冷笑 means to sneer, to laugh coldly — a contemptuous, mocking laugh expressing disdain, derision, or bitter dissatisfaction rather than amusement. This is the closest existing word to English "cynical" as a describable action (the sneer itself); see [皮肉](皮肉.md) for the ironic/critical mode of speech and [玩世不恭](../chengyu/玩世不恭.md) for the broader worldview.

Mandarin lěngxiào, Cantonese laang5 siu3, Japanese れいしょう, Korean 냉소, and Vietnamese lãnh tiếu are all standard, fully compositional readings, confirmed real and current in both Mandarin and Japanese.
