## Notes
- [懍](../characters/懍.md) "fear, awe" doubled — this word is 懍's own stand-in.

1. (literary, ideophonic) a fearful or terrified appearance (書經).
2. (literary, ideophonic) a stern and rigorous appearance (後漢書).

Mandarin lǐnlǐn, Cantonese lam5 lam5, Japanese りんりん, Korean 름름, and Vietnamese lẫm lẫm are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed non-standard katakana `japanese` to hiragana. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄜㄇㄌㄜㄇ unique across both words and characters).
