## Etymology
- [准](../characters/准.md) "allow, permit" + [許 (char)](../characters/許%20(char).md) "permit, allow"

## Notes
A synonym-doubling compound, "to permit, allow" — the everyday counterpart to the more formal [[允許]] (doãn hứa/duẫn hứa, historically of royal assent). Both `korean: 준허` (compositional) and `vietnamese: chuẩn hứa` (directly attested, confirmed via web search) fill previously blank fields. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 준효 diverges from the compositional Sino-Korean 준허 in the second syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄨㄋㄏ⼄ unique among both words and characters).
