## Etymology
- [准](../characters/准.md) "allow, permit" + [許 (char)](../characters/許%20(char).md) "permit, allow"

## Notes
A synonym-doubling compound, "to permit, allow" — the everyday counterpart to the more formal [[允許]] (doãn hứa/duẫn hứa, historically of royal assent). `vietnamese: chuẩn hứa` is directly attested (confirmed via web search); `korean: 준허` is compositional.
