## Notes
- [亦 (char)](../characters/亦%20(char).md) "too; also"

亦 is the Classical Chinese adverb for "also; likewise," typically preceding the verb it modifies: 亦可 (also acceptable), 不亦樂乎 (is it not a joy?). Legitimizing note: 亦 (char)'s own `stand_in` is this exact word (itself), so 亦 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by itself.
