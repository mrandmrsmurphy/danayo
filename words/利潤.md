## Notes
- [利](../characters/利.md) "profit" + [潤 (char)](../characters/潤%20(char).md) "soft, smooth, wet, increasing" — "profit" (利潤率, "profit margin"). Mandarin lìrùn, Cantonese lei6 jeon6, Japanese りじゅん, Korean 이윤, and Vietnamese lợi nhuận are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 릐눈 diverges from Sino-Korean 이윤 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄧㄜㄋㄨㄋ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 利's own `stand_in` is this exact word (利潤), so 利 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 利潤.
