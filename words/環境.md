## Notes
- [環 (char)](../characters/環%20(char).md) "ring, circle" + [境](../characters/境.md) "boundary, border, frontier" — no stand-in relationship applies (環's own stand-in is itself; 境's own is [[地境]]).

環境 means environment, surroundings.

Mandarin huánjìng, Cantonese waan4 ging2, Japanese かんきょう, Korean 환경 and Vietnamese hoàn cảnh are all fully compositional, matching 環's and 境's own citations.
