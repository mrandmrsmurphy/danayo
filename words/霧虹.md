## Notes
- [霧 (char)](../characters/霧%20(char).md) "fog, mist" + [虹](../characters/虹.md) "rainbow" — no `#cranberry`: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here (霧's is the word [[霧]] itself, 虹's is [[彩虹]]).

霧虹 (wùhóng) is a real, attested Mandarin term for "fogbow," the faint, near-colorless bow that forms in fog rather than rain. Japanese きりにじ (kirinizi, using native readings rather than on'yomi) and Korean 무홍 (muhong) are likewise real, attested terms for the same phenomenon.

Sino-Vietnamese vụ hồng is compositionally exact, but the standard Vietnamese term for the phenomenon is instead the native compound cầu vồng sương mù or cung sương mù (both literally "rainbow/arc of fog").
