## Notes
- [流](../characters/流.md) "flow" + [水](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" — no stand-in relationship applies (流's own stand-in is [[流動]]; 水's own is itself).

流水 means flowing water; by extension, the inevitable passage of events (as in the idiom 高山流水, "high mountains and flowing water," symbolizing deep mutual understanding between friends).

Mandarin liúshuǐ, Cantonese lau4 seoi2, Japanese りゅうすい, and Vietnamese lưu thủy (also the real, standard term, as in the same idiom cao sơn lưu thủy) are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 流's and 水's own stored citations exactly. Fixed real bug: `korean` was 유수, a South Korean 두음법칙-shifted form — corrected to the North Korean/문화어 compositional form 류수, matching 流's own citation. Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh` and empty `aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄌ⼜ㄙㄨ unique across both words and characters).
