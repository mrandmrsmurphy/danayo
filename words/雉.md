## Notes
- [雉 (char)](../characters/雉%20(char).md) "green pheasant" — this word is 雉's own stand-in.

雉 refers to the pheasant (specifically the green pheasant, Japan's national bird). Mandarin 雉 (zhì), Cantonese 雉 (zi6), Korean 치 (chi), and Sino-Vietnamese trĩ all preserve this meaning, though the character survives mainly bound in compounds or literary use rather than everyday standalone vocabulary. Japanese 雉 (ち, on'yomi) is comparatively rare next to the far more common native きじ (kiji) — the everyday word for pheasant and a culturally prominent bird in Japan, appearing in the Momotarō folktale among the hero's animal companions.

This word shares its reading (diǝ/듸/ㄉㄧㄜ) with [[地]] ("land"), whose own page had already anticipated this callout.
