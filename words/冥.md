## Notes
- [冥 (char)](../characters/冥%20(char).md) "dark; gloomy"

冥 is 會意: 冖 ("a covering") over elements depicting the sun obscured — hence darkness. It describes deep obscurity and, by extension, the unseen realm of the dead: [[冥王]] ("Pluto; Hades; Yama; King of the Underworld"), 冥界/冥府 ("the underworld, the afterlife"), and 幽冥 (yōumíng, "dark and gloomy, obscure").

Mandarin míng, Cantonese ming4, and Japanese めい (mei, on'yomi, as in 冥王星 めいおうせい "the planet Pluto") alongside native くら(い) (kura(i), "dark," the everyday adjective) all preserve the sense. Korean 명 (myeong) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 어둡다 (eodupda, "to be dark"). Vietnamese minh is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in u minh ("dark and gloomy, obscure") and minh giới (冥界, "the underworld"); the character page's other two stored candidates, mênh and mưng, are unrelated and look like corpus noise.
