## Notes
- [歌](../characters/歌.md) "song" + [頌](../characters/頌.md) "laud, hymn" — stand-in for [[頌]], which cannot appear independently.

歌頌 means to extol or praise, typically in a formal, public, or written register rather than everyday casual compliment — praising a hero's deeds, a nation's achievements, or a virtuous figure in song, poetry, or ceremonial speech. 頌 itself names a specific ancient poetic genre — the 頌 ("eulogy/hymn") section of the 《詩經》(Classic of Poetry), verses composed for ancestral temple sacrifices and dynastic praise — so 歌頌 literally combines "song" with this genre name, reinforcing the sense of praise delivered through formal composition rather than plain speech.

Japanese かしょう, Korean 가송, and Vietnamese ca tụng are all standard compositional Sino-readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 그숑 diverges from Sino-Korean 가송 in both syllables. No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄜㄙ⼄ㄫ unique across both words and characters).
