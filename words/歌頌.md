## Notes
- [歌](../characters/歌.md) "song" + [頌](../characters/頌.md) "laud, hymn" — stand-in for the character 頌, which cannot appear independently.

歌頌 means to extol or praise, typically in song or chanting. English Wiktionary lists it only for Chinese 歌頌 (alias 歌颂; Mandarin gēsòng, Cantonese go1 zung6), as a transitive verb glossed "to extol; to praise (often in the form of singing or chanting)". It has no Japanese, Korean or Vietnamese entry, so Japanese かしょう, Korean 가송 and Vietnamese ca tụng here are built from the characters.

頌 is also the name of one of the sections of the *Classic of Poetry*: English Wikipedia says its "Hymns" and "Eulogies" are the oldest material in the *Poetry*, performed to slow, heavy accompaniment from bells, drums and stone chimes. 歌頌 is the stand-in for [頌](../characters/頌.md), which cannot appear independently.
