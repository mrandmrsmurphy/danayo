## Notes
- [楕](characters/楕.md) "oval" + [圓 (char)](characters/圓%20(char).md) "circle" — stand-in for [[楕]], which cannot appear independently.

楕圓 means "ellipse" — literally "oval-circle." It is one of the four <ruby>[圓錐曲線](/words/圓錐曲線.md)<rt>⼔ㄋㄐㄨㄧㄎ⼘ㄎㄙ⼶ㄋ</rt></ruby> ("conic sections").

**Content corrected**: the stored `諺文` read `슈원` — inconsistent with both constituent characters' own readings ([[楕]]'s own 諺文 is 타; [[圓]]'s is 원) and with this word's own `羅馬字` (ta'wen) and `注音` (ㄊㄚ·⼔ㄋ), all of which point to 타원. Corrected to `타원`. Also corrected `kwin` from `false` to `true`: both [[楕]] (char) and [[圓]] (char) are individually kwin, so the AND-rule is satisfied — the compound should have been true.

Mandarin tuǒyuán and Cantonese to5 jyun4 preserve the sense directly. Japanese だえん (daen) is standard mathematical vocabulary. Korean 타원 (tawon) parallels the Sino-xenic reading and is likewise standard. Vietnamese thỏa viên is directly attested in Hán Nôm dictionary sources as the Sino-Vietnamese rendering of 橢圓 (glossed "hình bầu dục," oval/ellipse) — combining thỏa (楕/橢/椭, the standard Hán Việt reading) with viên (圓, "circle"; distinct from but homophonous with 員, as in nhân viên). This is a classical/literary compound rather than live colloquial vocabulary; modern Vietnamese normally says hình elip (a loanword) or hình bầu dục ("duck-egg shape," native) for "ellipse/oval." [[圓]]'s other stored candidate, vin, is a genuine Nôm (vernacular) reading rather than a second Hán Việt doublet, and was not used here.
