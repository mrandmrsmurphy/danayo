## Notes
- [錀 (char)](characters/錀%20(char).md) "roentgenium" + [琴 (char)](characters/琴%20(char).md) "zither"

錀琴 names the röntgen (symbol R), the older unit of ionizing radiation exposure, named after the German physicist Wilhelm Röntgen, discoverer of X-rays. The formation is purely phonetic rather than semantic: 錀 supplies the "rön-" sound (borrowed from its own use as the Dan'a'yo name for element 111, roentgenium, itself named for the same physicist) and 琴 (qín, "zither") supplies the "-gen" sound, echoing the established real Chinese transliteration 倫琴/伦琴 (Lúnqín) for the same physicist's name — listed here as aliases. The choice deliberately swaps 倫 for the phonetically similar 錀 to visually tie the unit back to the element that shares its namesake.

Cross-linguistically the term is a specialist scientific unit rather than everyday vocabulary: Sino-Korean 뢴트겐 and Japanese レントゲン are both direct phonetic loans of "Röntgen" itself (Japanese レントゲン is additionally the ordinary word for "X-ray" in casual usage), and Vietnamese rơn-ghen is likewise a phonetic transliteration.
