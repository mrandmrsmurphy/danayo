## Notes
- [晩](../characters/晩%20(char).md) "evening" + [餐](../characters/餐%20(char).md) "meal" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 晩 and 餐's own stand-ins are themselves).

晩餐 means supper.

Mandarin wǎncān, Cantonese maan5 caan1, Japanese ばんさん, Korean 만찬, and Vietnamese vãn xan are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bugs: `mandarin`/`cantonese` had both been contaminated with the sibling word [[晩飯]]'s readings (wǎnfàn/wan3 fan4) instead of compositional wǎncān/maan5 caan1. Removed mismatched-character `晚飯` from `aliases`: a distinct compound built on 飯 rather than 餐, not an orthographic variant of this word. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄛㄋㄑㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
