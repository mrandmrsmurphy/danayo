## Notes
- [失](../characters/失.md) "lose" + [物](../characters/物%20(char).md) "thing" — "lost thing." Their own `stand_in` values: 失's is [[滅失]]; 物's is itself; none points here, so 失物 is an independent compound.

失物 is lost property: something that its owner has lost. Mandarin shīwù and Cantonese sat1 mat6 are the ordinary words (失物招領 is the lost-and-found); Japanese and Korean do not use it in that sense, so those two fields hold the character readings. Compare [[喪失]] (to lose) and [[遺]].
