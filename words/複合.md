Wiktionary: Chinese "to combine multiple components; to mix several ingredients"; attributive "compound; complex; composite; combined"; Japanese "combined, composition", and as a verb "to be combined, to be composed of"; Korean 복합 "compound; complex; combination"; Vietnamese phức hợp "to combine; compound, composite, combined". Used in `文法 - 01序文`, `文法 - 02音韻論` and `文法 - 03文字法`. See also [[複合語]].
