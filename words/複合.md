## Notes
- [複](../characters/複.md) "repeat, duplicate" + [合 (char)](../characters/合%20(char).md) "join, fit together, combine" — no stand-in relationship applies (複's own stand-in is [[重複]]; 合's own is itself).

複合 means compound, composite — made of multiple combined parts (複合機, "multi-function device"; 複合現実, "mixed reality"; 複合体, "a composite, complex").

Mandarin fùhé, Cantonese fuk1 hap6, Japanese ふくごう, Korean 복합, and Vietnamese phức hợp are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 複's and 合's own stored citations exactly. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 북곱 diverges from Sino-Korean 복합. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄨㄎㄍㄛㄆ unique across both words and characters).
