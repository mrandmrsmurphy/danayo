## Notes
- [僧](../characters/僧.md) "priest, monk, bonze" + [家](../characters/家.md) "house, family" — "a Buddhist monk," treating the monastic community as a household/order (家 in the extended sense of "school, house, order," as in 儒家/道家). Mandarin sēngjiā, Cantonese zang1 gaa1, Japanese そうか, and Korean 승가 are all directly attested standard readings. `vietnamese: tăng gia` is the honest compositional reading (僧's own tăng + 家's own gia); it happens to collide with the unrelated, much more common everyday Vietnamese phrase tăng gia ("to increase, augment production," as in tăng gia sản xuất) — a coincidental homograph, not a claim of independent Buddhist-register attestation. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 승가 matches Sino-Korean 승가 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄜㄫㄍㄚ unique among both words and characters).

Legitimizing note: 僧's own `stand_in` is this exact word (僧家), so 僧 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 僧家.
