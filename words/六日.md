## Notes
- [六 (char)](../characters/六%20(char).md) "six" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — "the sixth day of the month" (matching the pattern of [[十六日]], "sixteenth," and [[二十六日]], "twenty-sixth"). Japanese むいか is the genuine native kun-reading specifically for the 6th day of the month (distinct from the on'yomi-based counting readings used for most other days). Mandarin liùrì, Cantonese luk6 jat6, and Korean 육일 are all directly attested standard readings. `vietnamese: lục nhật` is the honest compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading; everyday Vietnamese instead uses the native phrase mùng sáu ("the sixth [day]"). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 룩닏 diverges from Sino-Korean 육일 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄌㄨㄎㄋㄧㄊ unique among both words and characters).
