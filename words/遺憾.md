## Notes
- [遺 (char)](../characters/遺%20(char).md) "lose, leave behind" + [憾](../characters/憾.md) "regrettable" — this word is the stand-in for [[憾]], which cannot appear independently.

遺憾 means regrettable, pitiable.

Mandarin yíhàn, Cantonese wai4 ham6, Japanese いかん and Korean 유감 are compositional. Vietnamese di hám is directly attested in hvdic.
