## Notes
- [獄](../characters/獄.md) "prison" + [吏](../characters/吏.md) "officer" — no stand-in relationship applies (獄's own stand-in is [[監獄]]; 吏's own is [[官吏]]).

獄吏 means jailer.

Mandarin yùlì, Cantonese juk6 lei6, Japanese ごくり, Korean 옥리 and Vietnamese ngục lại are all fully compositional.
