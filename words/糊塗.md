## Notes
- [糊](../characters/糊.md) "muddled, confused" + [塗](../characters/塗.md) "smear" — 糊's own `stand_in` is [[糊塗]] itself, but 塗's is [[塗抹]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 糊塗 legitimizes 糊 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 塗 is individually `kwin: true`, but 糊 is `kwin: false`.

糊塗 describes something muddled, confused, or unclear — figuratively extended from 糊's literal senses of paste/porridge (a formless, sticky mass) plus 塗's "to smear." In Chinese it also verbs as "to gloss over, muddle through" (糊塗账, a hopelessly confused account).

Mandarin hútú, Cantonese wu4 tou4, and Vietnamese hồ đồ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese こと (koto, 糊塗する "to gloss over, patch up") and Korean 호도 (hodo, 호도하다 "to whitewash, cover up") are likewise standard attested readings, though both happen to coincide in shape with 弧度's own reading ("ho-do") purely by coincidence of transcription — this is not a real Dan'a'yo homophone, since 糊's own syllable is ㄏㄛㄨ (hou), not 弧's ㄏㄛ (ho); 糊塗's actual Dan'a'yo reading is houdo/홋도/ㄏㄛㄨㄉㄛ, distinct from 弧度's hodo/호도/ㄏㄛㄉㄛ.
