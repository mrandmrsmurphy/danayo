## Notes
- [過 (char)](../characters/過%20(char).md) "pass, exceed" (stand-in: itself) + [程 (char)](../characters/程.md) "schedule, journey" (stand-in: [[程度]]) — a process, a course of change over time.

Wiktionary: Chinese "process; course" (Mainland China, computing: "procedure"); Japanese "process"; Korean 과정 and Vietnamese quá trình are the Han forms of "process". Used in `文法 - 02音韻論` in 非音位的過程.
