## Notes
- [過 (char)](../characters/過.md) "pass, exceed" (stand-in: itself) + [程 (char)](../characters/程.md) "schedule, journey" (stand-in: [[程度]]) — a process, a course of change over time.

過程 means process, as invoked by `文法 - 02音韻論`'s 非音位的過程 ("a non-phonemic process"). Mandarin guòchéng and Korean 과정 are both real, standard terms. Cantonese gwo3 cing4 is a straightforward compositional reading. Vietnamese quá trình is real — matches the standard Vietnamese term for "process" (though "trình" there is a different character than 程; this word's own 程 reading is cited for consistency with its stored citation). `kwin` is false: inherited from 程 (false), despite 過 itself being true.

No syllable-boundary dot is needed: the concatenated string `ㄍ⺢ㄉㄧㄫ` admits only one legal partition.
