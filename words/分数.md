## Notes
- [分 (char)](../characters/分%20(char).md) "divide, portion" + [数](../characters/数.md) "number" — literally "divided number," a fraction: an integer *a* (the numerator, 分子) divided by a non-zero integer *b* (the denominator, 分母), written *a*/*b*.

Mandarin fēnshù, Cantonese fan1 sou3, Japanese ぶんすう, Korean 분수, and Vietnamese phân số are all standard, fully compositional, and genuinely the living everyday terms for this concept in each language — unlike several sibling Math-domain words in this series, there is no register split here between the compositional form and vernacular usage.

`kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 분수 coincides exactly with Sino-Korean 분수. One notable cross-linguistic quirk, noted for interest rather than affecting Dan'a'yo: Mandarin reads a fraction denominator-first (分之), the reverse of the English/Dan'a'yo numerator-first convention. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄨㄋㄙㄨ unique among both words and characters).
