## Notes
- [電](../characters/電.md) "electricity" + [車 (char)](../characters/車%20(char).md) "cart, car" — no `#cranberry`: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here (電's is [[電気]], 車's is 車 itself).

Mandarin 電車/电车 (diànchē), Cantonese 電車 (din6ce1), Japanese でんしゃ (densha), and Korean 전차 (jeoncha) are all standard, everyday compositional words for "tram/trolley" in their respective languages.

Vietnamese instead uses the native word-order-reversed xe điện ("vehicle" + "electric," modifier following the noun) rather than a Sino-order **điện xa**; this is a further instance of the word-order-reversal pattern already seen in [[限定詞]] (→ từ hạn định), [[除外]] (→ ngoại trừ), and [[電灯]] (→ đèn điện vs. unattested điện đăng).

Fixed a real bug: `羅馬字`/`諺文` had been contaminated to a naive w-glide-dropped transcription (denca/던차) instead of 車's own stored Dan'a'yo reading (cwa/촤). The w-glide drops only after a *vowel-final* syllable (cf. [[汽車]] kiǝca/킈촤, [[火車]] hwaca/화촤), but 電's own reading ends in a consonant (-n), so the glide is retained here — confirmed against [[自動車]] (jiǝdongcwa/즤동촤) and [[自転車]] (jiǝjwencwa/즤줜촤), both also consonant-final before 車. `注音` (ㄉㄝㄋㄑ⺢) had already stayed correct, as is typical for this bug class.
