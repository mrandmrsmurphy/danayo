## Notes
- [一 (char)](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" + [夫 (char)](../characters/夫%20(char).md) "husband" + [一 (char)](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" + [妻 (char)](../characters/妻%20(char).md) "wife" — monogamy.

一夫一妻 is monogamy: one husband and one wife. It is "one husband, one wife," a four-character expression whose structure is the same in Mandarin yīfūyīqī, Cantonese jat1 fu1 jat1 cai1 and Japanese いっぷいっさい (all confirmed on Wiktionary); the longer form 一夫一妻制 names the system. A Korean entry exists but was not confirmed, so `korean` is left blank. It contrasts with the polygynous household in which a [[妾]] (concubine) or a second wife is kept beside the [[嫡]] (principal wife).
