## Notes
- [臥 (char)](../characters/臥%20(char).md) "lie down" + [鉤 (char)](../characters/鉤%20(char).md) "hook"

Wiktionary has no entry for 臥鉤; it is a compound of 臥 "to lie down" and 鉤, which Wiktionary glosses in Chinese calligraphy as "a stroke with a hook". It names the lying hook, a sub-variety of the hook ([[鉤]]) listed on the [[Stroke]] page beside [[戈鉤]]. Its readings are compositional.
