## Notes
- [話](../characters/話%20(char).md) "speak on, talk about" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

話 means to speak on or talk about, as in 会話 ("conversation") and 電話 ("telephone").

Mandarin huà, Cantonese waa6, Japanese わ, Korean 화, and Vietnamese thoại are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄏ⺢ㄧ/hwai/홰) with the unrelated [[懐]] ("miss, think of").
