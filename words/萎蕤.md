## Notes
- [萎](../characters/萎.md) "wither" + [蕤](../characters/蕤.md) "green shoots" — stand-in for [[蕤]], which cannot appear independently

萎蕤 names Solomon's seal, the arching, drooping-stemmed herb Polygonatum (in particular the fragrant Solomon's seal, Polygonatum odoratum), whose rhizome is used in Chinese medicine, where the drug is also called 玉竹. Mandarin wěiruí, Cantonese wai2 jeoi4 and Korean 위유 (wiyu) are the compound's readings; the plant is Japanese アマドコロ and Korean 둥굴레 under its own names. The compound is also a literary word for hanging down and drooping (蕤 is a drooping flower or tassel), which is the image behind the plant's name; the character page of 蕤 in this vault glosses it as "green shoots".

In Dan'a'yo it is the plant, filed with the medicinal plants beside [[人参]] (ginseng) and [[白及]].
