## Notes
- [従 (char)](../characters/従%20(char).md) "obey, follow, collateral" + [姉 (char)](../characters/姉%20(char).md) "older sister" + [妹](../characters/妹.md) "younger sister" — spelling out [[従姉]] and [[従妹]] together, the female counterpart of [[従兄弟]].

従姉妹 is the collective/generic form: female cousins as a class, or "cousin" without specifying older or younger. As with the rest of this cluster, the classical Chinese sense is narrower (specifically a father's brother's daughters) than the living Japanese one, which covers cousins of either parent's side; casual Japanese speech reaches for plain いとこ (itoko) rather than the fully kanji-differentiated reading. Mandarin cóngzǐmèi and Cantonese cung4 zi2 mui6 are real, attested Sino compounds tracking 姊妹 (tỷ muội, "sisters") — the same pattern as 從兄弟/從姊 above — though modern colloquial Mandarin reaches for 堂姊妹 instead. Korean 종자매 and Vietnamese tòng tỷ muội (chị em họ con nhà bác/chú) are both real Sino compounds, secondary to native/other-Sino alternatives (사촌 자매; đường tỷ muội).
