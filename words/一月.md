## Notes
- [一 (char)](../characters/一%20(char).md) "first" + [月 (char)](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — no `#cranberry`: both 一's and 月's own `stand_in` point to themselves, neither to this word.

Mandarin yīyuè, Korean 일월, and Japanese いちがつ are standard compositional readings. Vietnamese tháng giêng ("the first month") is a special native idiom used specifically for January — distinct from every other month, which are named with the native word tháng plus an ordinary Sino-Vietnamese-derived number (tháng hai "February," tháng ba "March," etc.); giêng itself is an archaic native word found in no other context. **Fixed two real bugs**: `cantonese` was `jat1jut6`, missing both a word-space and the glide in 月's own stored `jyut6` — corrected to `jat1 jyut6`. `注音` held a typo, `ㄧㄊ·⼔ㄋ` (wrong final consonant, ㄋ for ㄊ) — corrected to `ㄧㄊ·⼔ㄊ`, matching 月's own stored `注音`; the same typo also existed in this word's own citation on `characters/月 (char).md`'s `## Words` list and was fixed there too. **Fixed a real `kwin` bug**: was `true`, contradicting the AND-rule (一 false, 月 false) — corrected to `false`. Fixed the duplicate `品詞` field.

**Note for a future iteration**: the identical `⼔ㄋ`/`⼔ㄜ`-for-`⼔ㄊ` typo appears to also affect [[三月]] and [[十一月]]'s own citations on `月 (char).md`'s `## Words` list — left unfixed here since perfecting those words is a separate task in this backlog, but worth checking when the sweep reaches them.
