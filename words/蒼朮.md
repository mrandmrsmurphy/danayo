## Notes
- [蒼 (char)](../characters/蒼%20(char).md) "blue, green" + [朮](../characters/朮.md) "atractylodes" — 蒼朮 is the stand-in word for [朮](../characters/朮.md); 朮 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse.

蒼朮 (*Atractylodes lancea*) is the dried rhizome of a perennial plant in the daisy family, a staple herb of traditional Chinese, Japanese (漢方), Korean, and Vietnamese medicine (đông y). Classified in TCM as a "dampness-drying" herb, it is traditionally used to support digestion and address bloating and diarrhea; modern pharmacological study has identified essential-oil compounds (atractylon, β-eudesmol, hinesol) as its active constituents. It is closely related to, but distinct from, 白朮 (*Atractylodes macrocephala*), a different species in the same genus used for a related but distinguishable set of indications.

Japanese そうじゅつ and Korean 창출 are both standard compositional Sino-readings. Vietnamese thương truật is a well-established term in Vietnamese traditional medicine, also known there as mao truật or xích truật. No specific HSK level applies to a term this specialized. No homophones (注音 ㄑ⺢ㄫㄙㄨㄊ unique across both words and characters).
