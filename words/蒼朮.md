## Notes
- [蒼 (char)](../characters/蒼%20(char).md) "blue, green" + [朮](../characters/朮.md) "atractylodes"

蒼朮 is the rhizome of *Atractylodes lancea*, an herb used in traditional Chinese medicine. English Wiktionary glosses both Chinese 蒼朮 (alias 蒼术; Mandarin cāngzhú, Cantonese cong1 seot6) and Japanese そうじゅつ with that sense. Korean 창출 is a hanja form of the same word, and Vietnamese thương truật is built from the readings of the two characters.

This word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character [朮](../characters/朮.md), which is too bounded to stand alone.
