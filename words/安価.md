## Etymology
- [安](../characters/安.md) "cheap, low" (used here in this sense, not its more familiar "peaceful" sense) + [価](../characters/価.md) "price"

## Notes
- "Cheap, inexpensive," with a connotation of "crappy, cheaply-made" — nearly synonymous with [[低廉]]. `korean: 싼` is a deliberate real native-word choice rather than a mechanical Sino-Korean calque, since 안가 isn't independently attested as a common Korean word for this sense. Filled blank `pos: 性詞` and `cantonese: on1 gaa3`. `vietnamese: an giá` is compositional; not independently confirmed as a fixed term — everyday Vietnamese says giá rẻ (native) for "cheap" (domain knowledge, this session's search quota exhausted). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 안가 diverges entirely from Korean's native 싼. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄋㄍㄚ unique among both words and characters).
