## Notes
- [凶 (char)](../characters/凶%20(char).md) "unlucky; sinister" + [徒](../characters/徒.md) "disciple; follower; member of a group"

凶徒 means a villain or thug: a ruffian, or a member of a criminal band, and collectively a mob or gang. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 兇徒 (an alias, 凶徒; Mandarin xiōngtú, Cantonese hung1 tou4) as "bad person; vile creature; villain" and "thug; mob; rioter", and Japanese きょうと as "thug, mob, rioter". It lists Korean 흉도 for the hanja form with no gloss, and has no Vietnamese entry, so Vietnamese hung đồ here is built from the characters.

The character 徒, follower or group member, appears in positive and negative compounds alike: [[使徒]], apostle, and [[信徒]], believer, name devoted followers, while 凶徒 and [[暴徒]], rioter, name those whose group is defined by violence.
