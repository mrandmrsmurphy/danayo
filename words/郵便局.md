## Notes
- [郵](characters/郵.md) "mail, post" + [便 (char)](characters/便%20(char).md) "convenience" + [局 (char)](characters/局%20(char).md) "office, bureau" — "post office."

Mandarin yóubiànjú and japanese ゆうびんきょく are directly attested standard readings. **Found and fixed a wrong-compound reading bug across `cantonese`/`korean`/`vietnamese`**: jau4 zing3 guk6, 우체국, and "sở bưu điện, phòng bưu điện" (a garbled multi-value string) all actually belong to unrelated (though same-meaning) compounds — 郵政局 ("postal administration bureau," using 政 zing3), 郵遞局 (using 遞, real Korean 우체국), and 郵電所/郵電房 (using 電, real Vietnamese phrasing) — corrected to the honest compositional jau4 bin6 guk6 / 우편국 / bưu tiện cục (郵's own jau4/우/bưu + 便's own bin6/편/tiện + 局's own guk6/국/cục). **Found and removed four bad-aliases-entries**: `郵遞局`, `郵局` (a shorter, differently-composed form omitting 便), and `所郵電`/`房郵電` (garbled/reversed forms of the unrelated 郵電所/郵電房) — none is a spelling variant of 郵便局; kept only `邮便局`, the legitimate simplified-character variant. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 웃변굑 diverges from Sino-Korean 우편국 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄨㄛㄅ⼶ㄋㄍ⼄ㄎ unique among both words and characters).

**Found and fixed a missing citation** on 便(char).md's own Words list. Citations on 郵.md and 局(char).md were already correctly present. None of the three constituents' `stand_in` fields point to this compound (郵→郵便, 便→便, 局→局).
