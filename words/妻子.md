## Notes
- [妻](../characters/妻%20(char).md) "wife" + [子](../characters/子.md) "child" — no stand-in relationship applies (妻's own stand-in is itself; 子's own is [[児子]]).

妻子 means wife and child(ren), or more broadly one's wife and children collectively — not just "wife" alone, despite the literal reading suggesting otherwise.

Mandarin qīzǐ, Cantonese cai1 zi2, Japanese さいし, Korean 처자, and Vietnamese thê tử are all standard, fully compositional readings. Removed the redundant `品詞` field. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄝㄧㄐㄜ unique across both words and characters).
