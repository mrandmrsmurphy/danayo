## Notes
- [時 (char)](../characters/時%20(char).md) "when" + [間](../characters/間.md) "between" + [表](../characters/表.md) "table; chart"

時間表 is a timetable or schedule: a table of when things happen, such as a school's lessons. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin shíjiānbiǎo and Japanese じかんひょう as "timetable; schedule". Korean 시간표 matches.

In a school it sets the order of the [授業](授業.md) through the day and the week.
