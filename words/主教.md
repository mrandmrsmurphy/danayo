## Notes
- [主](../characters/主.md) "lord, master" + [教](../characters/教.md) "teaching, religion"

主教 is the Christian ecclesiastical title for a bishop or overseer, ranking above a priest and below an archbishop ([[大主教]]) or primate ([[総主教]]). Mandarin zhǔjiào, Cantonese zyu2 gaau3, Japanese しゅきょう, and Korean 주교 are all standard, fully compositional Sino-language readings used in the Chinese-, Cantonese-, Japanese-, and Korean-speaking Christian churches alike. Vietnamese giám mục is filled here as the real, standard Catholic term for "bishop" — preferred over the compositional-but-unattested chủ giáo (主's chủ + 教's giáo), since Vietnamese ecclesiastical vocabulary developed largely independently of the Sino-Vietnamese layer (cf. linh mục "priest," hồng y "cardinal," none built compositionally from 主/教-style Sino roots).

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 주굣 diverges from Sino-Korean 주교 in the second syllable — 教 reads 굣 (gyou/ㄍ⼄ㄨ) in Dan'a'yo but 교 (gyo) in Sino-Korean.
