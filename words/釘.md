## Notes
- [釘](../characters/釘%20(char).md) "nail" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 釘 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

Mandarin dīng, cantonese ding1, and korean 정 (jeong) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese uses the native kun-reading くぎ (kugi, "nail") rather than the on-readings テイ/チョウ (TEI/CHOU) for the bare character alone. **Found and fixed a real bug**: `vietnamese` held the literal string `"null"` — fixed to đinh (one of two stored candidates). Added missing pos/kwin. Part of a genuine three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group with [[艇]] ("dinghy, rowboat, boat") and [[丁]] ("fourth on a list of four") — coincidental, unrelated in meaning.
