## Notes
- [倶 (char)](../characters/倶%20(char).md) "accompany; be with"

倶 (alternate form 俱) means "together, accompanying, all/both" — most familiar today in the loanword [[倶楽部]] (kurabu, "club," a phonetic transliteration of English "club" that happens to also carry the appropriate sense of people being "together"). Classically it also functions as an adverb meaning "all, entirely" (面面倶到, "to attend to every aspect").

Mandarin jù, Cantonese keoi1, and Japanese く (ku, on'yomi) alongside native とも/ともに (tomo/tomoni, "together, along with," the everyday reading) all preserve the sense. Korean 구 (gu) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 함께 (hamkke, "together"). Vietnamese câu is the Hán Việt reading, directly attested in câu lạc bộ (俱樂部, "club," the same loanword-transliteration compound as the Japanese 倶楽部); the character page's other stored candidate, cu, doesn't correspond to any attested reading of this character and looks like noise.

This word is part of a three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group (all read gu/구/ㄍㄨ): [[句]] ("phrase," still awaiting its own turn) and [[衢]] ("crossroads," already perfected — its own callout has been added alongside this iteration).
