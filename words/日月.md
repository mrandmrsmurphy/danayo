## Notes
- [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day, sun" + [月](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month, moon" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 日 and 月's own stand-ins are themselves).

日月 means sun and moon, or (figuratively) life and times.

Mandarin rìyuè, Cantonese jat6 jyut6, Japanese じつげつ, Korean 일월, and Vietnamese nhật nguyệt are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄧㄊ⼔ㄊ unique across both words and characters).
