## Notes
- [封 (char)](../characters/封%20(char).md) "seal; enfeoff" + [印](../characters/印.md) "stamp; imprint" — this word is the stand-in for [印](../characters/印.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

封印 combines 封's "sealing/closing" sense (see [[封]]'s own Notes for its dual feudal/sealing etymology) with 印's "stamp, seal-impression" — a physical or figurative seal that both closes something off and marks it as sealed, from mundane document-sealing to the popular-culture sense of magically sealing away a person or power (widely familiar from Japanese fiction).

Japanese ふういん, Korean 봉인, and Vietnamese phong ấn (verified) are all standard readings sharing this same dual literal/figurative sealing sense. No HSK level is given for the compound. No homophones (注音 ㄈㄛㄫㄧㄋ unique across both words and characters).

**Correction (2026-08-26)**: `羅馬字` had a typo (bong'in → fong'in) — 封's own citation form uses "fong" (already internally consistent with its own 諺文/注音, 뽕/ㄈㄛㄫ), which the word had swapped to "bong" for no apparent reason while its own `諺文`/`注音` fields were already correct.
