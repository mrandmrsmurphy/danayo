## Notes
- [周](../characters/周.md) "circumference" + [囲](../characters/囲.md) "encircle" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (周's is 圓周, 囲's is 包囲).

Mandarin zhōuwéi, Cantonese zau1 wai4, Japanese しゅうい, and Korean 주위 are the standard readings; the pre-reform Japanese kana spelling しうゐ is the same reading, not a separate one. Vietnamese chu vi is a real, extremely common word (the standard math term for "perimeter/circumference").
