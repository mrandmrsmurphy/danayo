## Notes
- [看](../characters/看%20(char).md) "look after, watch" + [護](../characters/護.md) "guard, protect" — "watch and guard," to nurse. Their own `stand_in` values: 看's is itself; 護's is [[守護]]; none points here, so 看護 is an independent compound.

看護 is to nurse: to look after a sick or injured person. Mandarin kānhù, Cantonese hon1 wu6, Japanese かんご and Korean 간호 all use it, and Vietnamese khán hộ does too. It is the care itself, where [[護士]] is the nurse as a profession and [[看病]] is to tend a patient. Compare [[病人]] (patient) and [[治療]] (treatment).
