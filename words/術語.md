## Notes
- [術](../characters/術%20(char).md) "art, technique" + [語](../characters/語.md) "word, language" — "technical word," a term. Their own `stand_in` values: 術's is itself; 語's is [[言語]]; none points here, so 術語 is an independent compound.

術語 is a term: a word used in a specialized field, a technical term. Mandarin shùyǔ, Cantonese seot6 jyu5 and Japanese じゅつご all use it, and Vietnamese thuật ngữ does too. Korean 술어 (術語) is the same compound. Compare [[単語]] (word) and [[語彙]] (vocabulary).
