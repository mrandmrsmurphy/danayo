## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" is separately legitimized by [[自身]]. [由](../characters/由%20(char).md) "from, since" is separately legitimized by [[由]] itself. No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

自由 combines 自 ("self") with 由 ("from, since" — here "arising from oneself") — "free, at liberty, unconstrained."

Mandarin zìyóu, korean 자유 (jayu), and vietnamese tự do were all already correct, fully compositional readings.
