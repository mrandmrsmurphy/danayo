## Notes
- [兜 (char)](../characters/兜%20(char).md) "helmet; wrap" + [当 (char)](../characters/当%20(char).md) "crotch" + [布](../characters/布.md) " cloth"

兜当布 is a loincloth. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 兜襠布 (alias here; Mandarin dōudāngbù, Cantonese dau1 dong1 bou3) as "fundoshi (traditional Japanese loincloth)". It has no Japanese, Korean or Vietnamese entry under this spelling, so the Japanese とうとうふ and Korean 두당포 fields are built from the readings of the three characters; the Japanese word for the garment is written 褌 and read ふんどし.

The spelling uses 当 in place of 襠, "crotch" in Wiktionary's Chinese entry, which has no page of its own. 襠 and its simplified form 裆 are aliases of [当 (char)](../characters/当%20(char).md), and 兜襠布 and 兜裆布 are aliases of this word. Both characters read dāng. The wrapping cloth [布](../characters/布.md) is the bound character whose stand-in is 亜麻布, and [兜](兜.md) is a free word.
