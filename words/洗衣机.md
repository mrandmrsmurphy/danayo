## Notes
- [洗](../characters/洗%20(char).md) "wash" + [衣](../characters/衣.md) "clothes" + [机](../characters/机.md) "machine" — "clothes-washing machine." 衣's own `stand_in` is [[衣類]], 洗's is itself and 机's is [[机会]]; none points here, so 洗衣机 is an independent compound.

洗衣机 is a washing machine. Mandarin xǐyījī and Cantonese sai2 ji1 gei1 are the ordinary words in Chinese. Japanese says 洗濯機 (せんたくき) and Korean 세탁기, built on [[洗濯]], so the `japanese` and `korean` fields here hold the readings of the characters rather than attested words. Dan'a'yo follows the Chinese form, which is the one attested in two languages.

Compare [[乾燥机]] (dryer) and [[机器]] (machine).
