## Notes
A rare, classical/literary synonym-doubling compound for "to consent, permit," specifically of royal assent. Web search found no independent attestation of the full compound as an actively-used word in Japanese, Korean, or Vietnamese — the `cantonese`, `japanese`, `korean` and `vietnamese` fields are compositional from each character's own confirmed reading.
