## Notes
- [贋 (char)](../characters/贋%20(char).md) "counterfeit; fake" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; standalone form, 贋 functions as an attributive adjective meaning "fake, sham." Its own 形声 etymology is semantic 貝 ("money, goods," since counterfeiting is fundamentally a matter of money) + phonetic [雁](雁.md) — meaning 贋's phonetic component is literally its own homophone [雁](雁.md) ("wild goose"), not just a coincidental Dan'a'yo reading overlap.

Mandarin yàn, Cantonese ngaan6, and Korean 안 (an) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese にせ (nise, already 贋's own stored `japanese_native` value) is the everyday native reading for "fake." Vietnamese nhạn is the standard Hán Việt reading, identical to [雁](雁.md)'s own Vietnamese reading, as expected since 雁 is 贋's phonetic component.
