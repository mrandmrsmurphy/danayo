## Notes
- [贋 (char)](../characters/贋%20(char).md) "counterfeit; fake" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; standalone form, 贋 functions as an attributive adjective meaning "fake, sham." Its own 形声 etymology is semantic 貝 ("money, goods," since counterfeiting is fundamentally a matter of money) + phonetic [[雁]] — meaning 贋's phonetic component is literally its own homophone [[雁]] ("wild goose"), not just a coincidental Dan'a'yo reading overlap.

贋 shares its exact Dan'a'yo reading (an/안/ㄚㄋ) with two other words, [[岸]] ("bank, shore") and [[雁]] ("wild goose") — a three-way homophone group cross-referenced on all three pages.

Mandarin yàn, Cantonese ngaan6, and Korean 안 (an) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese にせ (nise, already 贋's own stored `japanese_native` value) is the everyday native reading for "fake." Vietnamese nhạn is the standard Hán Việt reading, confirmed via hvdic.thivien.net — a genuine, independently-verified coincidence that it's identical to [[雁]]'s own Vietnamese reading, since 贋 and 雁 are etymologically distinct characters despite the shared phonetic component. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 안 matches Sino-Korean 안 exactly.
