## Notes
- [至 (char)](../characters/至%20(char).md) "until, extreme" is separately legitimized by [[至]] itself. [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day, sun" is separately legitimized by [[日]] itself. No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.
- The day when the sun reaches its extreme point in declination. See [[夏至]] (summer solstice) and [[冬至]] (winter solstice) for the specific terms.

Mandarin zhìrì, cantonese zi3jat6 (space removed), japanese しじつ (shijitsu), and korean 지일 (jiil) were all already correct, fully compositional readings.
