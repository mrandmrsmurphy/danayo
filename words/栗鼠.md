## Notes
- [栗](../characters/栗%20(char).md) "chestnut" + [鼠](../characters/鼠.md) "rat" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 栗 and 鼠's own stand-ins are themselves; 栗 has its own independent word page [[栗]], while 鼠's own stand-in is [[熊鼠]]).

栗鼠 means squirrel. (Chipmunk is technically 花栗鼠, "flower squirrel.")

Mandarin lìshǔ and Cantonese leot6 syu2 are standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese りす is the genuine, standard native Japanese word for squirrel (unrelated to the Sino-Japanese on-reading りっそ), kept as-is. Fixed real bug: `korean` was 다람쥐, a native Korean word for squirrel, not compositional — corrected to 률서, matching 栗's and 鼠's own citations (North Korean/문화어 initial ㄹ retained). Filled the blank `vietnamese` field and added `hsk_level: "無"` (matching 栗's own confirmed no-HSK-level status). No homophones (注音 ㄌㄧㄊㄙ⼄ unique across both words and characters).
