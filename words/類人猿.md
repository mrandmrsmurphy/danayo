## Notes
- [類](../characters/類.md) "kind, sort, category" + [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" + [猿](../characters/猿.md) "ape, monkey" — no `#cranberry`: no constituent's own `stand_in` points here (類's is [[種類]], 人's is 人 itself, 猿's is [[猿猩]]).

類人猿 (lèirényuán) is a real Mandarin biology term for "simian, great ape/anthropoid," and Cantonese 類人猿 (leoi6jan4jyun4) likewise. Japanese るいじんえん (ruijin'en) and Korean 유인원 (yuinwon — 류→유 by word-initial 두음법칙) are both standard biology terms in their languages.

Vietnamese instead uses the native, reversed-order vượn người ("ape-person," vs. the Sino "kind-person-ape" order) as the standard term for "great ape," with no live Sino-Vietnamese equivalent.

**Fixed a real bug**: `japanese` had るいじんゑん, using the obsolete kana ゑ (abolished in the 1946 orthography reform) instead of modern え — corrected to るいじんえん.
