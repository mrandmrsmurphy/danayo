## Notes
- [潤](characters/潤%20(char).md) "soft, smooth, wet, increasing" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 潤 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

潤 means soft, smooth, wet; extended to "to enrich, increase" (as in 利潤 "profit").

Mandarin rùn, Cantonese jeon6, Korean 윤, and Vietnamese nhuận (as in nhuận bút, "royalty payment") are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 潤's own stored citations exactly. Fixed literal `vietnamese: null` → nhuận, added the missing `pos`/`japanese`(じゅん, on'yomi JUN)/`hsk_level`("3") fields, reformatted `characters` to a proper block-list with the "(char)" suffix.

**Real homophone found**: `注音 ㄋㄨㄋ`/`눈`/`nun` is also carried by [[閏]] ("intercalary") — added the full callout here and a matching one to [[閏]].
