## Notes
- [始](../characters/始.md) "begin" + [作 (char)](../characters/作%20(char).md) "make, do" — "to begin, start." `korean: 시작` is an extremely common, everyday Korean word for "beginning, start" — much more frequent in daily use than its exact homophone [[詩作]] ("poem, poetry composition"), a resonance already discussed on that page's own Notes, where the pun between "every poem is a beginning" (始作) and "a making" (詩作) is noted as culturally exploited by Korean poets and critics. `japanese: スタート` is a deliberate English loanword choice ("start"), rather than a native calque. `vietnamese: thủy tác` is compositional; everyday Vietnamese instead says bắt đầu (native) for "to begin" (domain knowledge, not independently confirmed by search).
