## Notes
- [株](characters/株%20(char).md) "stock, root, stump" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 株 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

株 means stock, root, or stump.

Mandarin zhū, Cantonese zyu1, Japanese ちゅう, Korean 주, and Vietnamese châu are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 株's own stored citation exactly). Discovered a genuine homophone with [[兜]] (both 注音 ㄉㄨ, 諺文 두), already anticipated by 兜's own Notes — completed the reciprocal callout.
