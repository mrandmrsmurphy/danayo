## Notes
- [蒐](../characters/蒐.md) "collect, gather" + [集](../characters/集.md) "collect"

蒐集 means to collect or gather: to bring things together, as a hobby, a study or a task. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 蒐集 (Mandarin sōují, Cantonese sau1 zaap6) as "to gather; to collect", and Korean 수집 as "collection, collecting". It has no Japanese entry for 蒐集, so Japanese しゅうしゅう here is built from the characters.

蒐集 is the stand-in for [蒐](../characters/蒐.md), which is too bounded to stand alone. It has the same meaning as [[収集]], with 収 in place of 蒐, and Wiktionary glosses 收集 (Mandarin shōují) the same way.
