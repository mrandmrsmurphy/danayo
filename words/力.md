## Notes
- [力 (char)](../characters/力%20(char).md) "power; strength"

力 is 象形, though interpretation is disputed — either an arm bending out from the side of the body, or a wooden plough (the original form 𠠲 also inspired the shape of the zhuyin letter ㄌ). It means "power, strength, force," and heads an enormous compound family: [[勢力]] ("power, influence"), [[能力]] ("ability, capability"), [[協力]] ("to work together"), [[魔力]] ("magical power").

Mandarin lì, Cantonese lik6, and Japanese りょく/りき (ryoku/riki, on'yomi, fully productive: 能力 のうりょく "ability") alongside native いさお (isao, an archaic native reading meaning "merit, achievement") all preserve the sense. Korean 력 (ryeok) is the Sino-Korean reading used throughout compounds, alongside native 힘 (him, "strength, power," the everyday native word). Vietnamese lực is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in năng lực ("capability") and quyền lực ("power"); the everyday native word for "strength" is sức (sức khỏe, "health"; sức mạnh, "strength").
