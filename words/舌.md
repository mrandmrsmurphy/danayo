## Notes
- [舌 (char)](../characters/舌%20(char).md) "tongue; clapper" — stand-in for [[舌 (char)|舌]], which cannot appear independently

舌 is 象形 of a snake's forked tongue emerging from a mouth ([[口]]) — the oracle-bone form showed the fork directly, with later variants adding a horizontal stroke at the base that itself often evolved into a second, illusory fork. The character page documents its whole phonetic family (活, 刮, 括, 筈, 蛞, 聒) as descending from an unlinkable ancestor glyph 𠯑, the same "recognizable descendant with no page for the source" pattern already seen on 悟/五 and 栽/才 elsewhere in this vault. Beyond the anatomical organ, 舌 extends to "clapper" (the tongue-like striking piece inside a bell) and to speech itself — 舌根 names both the physical root of the tongue and, in traditional East Asian medicine/Buddhist philosophy, the taste faculty as one of the six sense-roots.

**Real content bug found and fixed on two pages**: both this word's own page and `characters/舌 (char).md` were stamped `kwin: true`, but a byte-level check shows Dan'a'yo `諺文: 섣` (SEOD, ending in ㄷ) and Sino-Korean `설` (SEOL, ending in ㄹ) are different Unicode syllables — corrected both to `kwin: false`. This reflects the same MC-checked-coda-vs-Korean-liquid pattern already documented on [[月]]/[[越]] earlier in this sweep: Dan'a'yo 섣 (sed, from Middle Chinese 禪母 d͡ʑ + 薛A三開韻 iᴇt) preserves the MC checked coda as a written -d, where Sino-Korean 설 has regularized the same ending to -l.

Japanese carries two on'yomi with a real functional split: ぜつ (ZETSU, the dominant reading — 舌戦 zessen "war of words," 毒舌 dokuzetsu "malicious tongue, backbiting") and rarer せつ, alongside the fully productive native 訓読み した (shita), the everyday word for the physical organ. Vietnamese thiệt is the reading used here (thiệt đài "tongue coating," a term from traditional medicine), picked over the character's other stored variant thịt — which is actually the ordinary modern Vietnamese word for "meat, flesh," an unrelated homophone contamination rather than a genuine second reading of 舌, the same stored-field-noise pattern already documented multiple times in this sweep.

**Real homophone found**: `注音 ㄙㄝㄊ`/`섣` is also carried by [[屑]] ("scraps, waste tidbits"), an existing but unperfected standalone word — added the full callout to `舌` and a matching anticipatory one to `屑`. Checked the remaining `ㄙㄝㄊ`-reading characters (設, 洩, 偰, 楔, 禼, 薛) — none has a self-pointing `stand_in`, confirming no third homophone word exists here.
