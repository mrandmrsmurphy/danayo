## Notes
- [勉 (char)](../characters/勉%20(char).md) "exert; urge"

勉 is 形声: semantic [[力]] ("strength, effort") + phonetic [[免]] — "to make a strong effort," 力 supplying the force and 免 the sound. It appears in [[勤勉]] ("hard-working, diligent"), [[勉励]] ("to exhort, urge on"), and [[勉強]] ("to force [someone/oneself] to do [something]" in Mandarin/Cantonese, but narrowed in Japanese specifically to "to study").

Mandarin miǎn, Cantonese min5, and Japanese べん (ben, on'yomi, fully productive: 勉強 べんきょう "to study") alongside native つと(める) (tsuto(meru), "to make an effort, endeavor") all preserve the sense. Korean 면 (myeon) parallels the Sino-xenic reading, alongside native 힘쓰다 (himsseuda, "to exert oneself" — built on the same root as [[力]]'s own native gloss 힘). Vietnamese miễn is the Hán Việt reading, well attested in miễn cưỡng (勉強, "reluctant, forced" — the same compound already documented above); the character page's other three stored candidates (mến, mễn, mịn) are unrelated and look like corpus noise.
