## Notes
- [引](../characters/引%20(char).md) "pull, attract" + [力](../characters/力%20(char).md) "force" — "pulling force," attraction. Their own `stand_in` values: 引's is itself; 力's is itself; none points here, so 引力 is an independent compound.

引力 is attraction: the force by which one body pulls another, and so gravitation. Mandarin yǐnlì, Cantonese jan5 lik6, Japanese いんりょく and Korean 인력 all use it. It is the pull between two bodies, where [[重力]] is gravity, the pull of the earth. Compare [[力]] (force) and [[質量]] (mass).
