## Notes
- [杏](../characters/杏.md) "apricot" + [月](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — no stand-in relationship applies here (杏's own stand-in is [[杏子]]; 月's own is itself).

Second month of the ancient Chinese poetic calendar. Apricot trees (杏) are among the earliest to bloom in spring, their pink-white blossoms appearing before the leaves. Their fragile beauty made them a symbol of early spring's brief, unreliable warmth.

Japanese あんずつき reads both halves natively (杏's kun'yomi あんず "apricot" + 月's kun'yomi つき "month") rather than the Sino-Japanese on'yomi concatenation きょうげつ, matching the established poetic-month convention (see [[寅月]], [[卯月]], [[未月]]). Vietnamese tháng hạnh likewise uses the native *tháng* plus the Sino-Vietnamese apricot name hạnh (matching 杏's own citation). Removed the redundant `品詞` field (duplicate of `pos`) and reformatted `japanese`/`vietnamese` from single-item lists to scalar strings. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄫ·⼔ㄊ unique across both words and characters).
