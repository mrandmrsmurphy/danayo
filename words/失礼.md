## Notes
- "Rude, impolite" — literally "losing propriety/manners." **Real gap found and fixed on [[礼 (char)]]'s own page**: its `vietnamese` field was missing lễ entirely, despite this being 礼/禮's most common and well-known Sino-Vietnamese reading (禮儀 lễ nghi, 禮貌 lễ phép) — added. `vietnamese: thất lễ` is a directly attested standard Vietnamese term with the same "impolite" sense, also used as a polite interjection ("excuse me, pardon me"), paralleling Japanese 失礼します. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 싣레 diverges from Sino-Korean 실례 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄊㄌㄝㄧ unique among both words and characters).
