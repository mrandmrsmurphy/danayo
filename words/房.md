## Notes
- [房](../characters/房%20(char).md) "room" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [房](../characters/房%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

房 means room.

Mandarin fáng, Cantonese fong4, Japanese ぼう, Korean 방, and Vietnamese phòng are all standard, fully compositional readings. Homophone of [[棚]] "shelf" (both 注音 ㄅㄚㄫ).
