## Notes
- [地](../characters/地%20(char).md) "land" + [下](../characters/下%20(char).md) "down, under" + [室](../characters/室.md) "room" — literally "underground room."

地下室 means "basement, cellar" — a full three-way exact match: Mandarin dìxiàshì is identical in structure to Japanese ちかしつ (chikashitsu) and Korean 지하실 (jihasil), all built on the same three morphemes in the same order. Cantonese dei6 haa6 sat1 is compositional.

Vietnamese địa hạ thất is the compositional Hán Việt reading; living Vietnamese instead uses the native tầng hầm ("underground floor").
