## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [狗](../characters/狗.md) "dog" — literally "sea-dog," the fur seal (named for its dog-like bark and face).

Mandarin hǎigǒu, Cantonese hoi2 gau2, and Vietnamese hải cẩu are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 海's and 狗's own stored citations exactly — Vietnamese hải cẩu shares the exact same "sea-dog" literal construction as Dan'a'yo's own word. Korean 물개 is the far more common everyday word for "seal" (native 물 "water" + 개 "dog"). Japanese オットセイ (ottosei) is a native/loan term, unrelated to either character's own on'yomi.
