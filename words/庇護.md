## Notes
- [庇](../characters/庇.md) "shield; protect" + [護](../characters/護.md) "safeguard; protect" — this word is the stand-in for [庇](../characters/庇.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

庇護 is a near-synonym doubling, both halves independently meaning "to shield, protect" — used both concretely (庇護所 "shelter, refuge") and in the modern legal/political sense of asylum (政治庇護 "political asylum").

Japanese ひご, Korean 비호, and Vietnamese tí hộ (the character's own field also lists tý hộ, an orthographic i/y variant of the same reading) are all standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄧㄜㄏㄛ unique across both words and characters).
