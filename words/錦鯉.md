## Notes
- No cranberry (錦's own `stand_in` is [[錦繍]]; 鯉's own is this exact compound) — transitivity fails, though 鯉 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry by this word.

## Etymology
- [錦](../characters/錦.md) "brocade" + [鯉](../characters/鯉.md) "koi, carp" — "brocade carp," the ornamental nishikigoi; stand-in for [[鯉]], which cannot appear independently
