## Notes
- [変](../characters/変%20(char).md) "change, strange" + [態](../characters/態.md) "state, form" — "strange state," a perversion. Their own `stand_in` values: 変's is itself; 態's is [[態度]]; none points here, so 変態 is an independent compound.

変態 is a perversion, and a pervert: sexual behavior or a person that departs from what is thought normal. Mandarin biàntài, Cantonese bin3 taai3, Japanese へんたい and Korean 변태 all use it, and Vietnamese biến thái does too. The same compound also means "metamorphosis" in biology (a larva turning into an adult), and in everyday Chinese and Japanese it is a common insult for a pervert. Compare [[異常]] (abnormal) and [[淫]] (lewd).
