## Notes
- [温](../characters/温.md) "warm; mild" + [厚 (char)](../characters/厚%20(char).md) "thick; kind"

温厚 is gentle and kind: warm of manner, not given to anger or harshness. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin wēnhòu as "gentle and kind; good-natured" and Japanese おんこう as "mild-mannered, gentle, affable". Cantonese wan1 hau5 matches, and Korean 온후 is the reading of the characters.

It is a settled disposition, the reverse of the sharp [刻薄](刻薄.md), and the mild side of a nature whose proud side is the [傲慢](傲慢.md). A forgiving mind is the [寛容](寛容.md).
