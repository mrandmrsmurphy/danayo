## Notes
- [装 (char)](../characters/装%20(char).md) "dress up, clothe" + [飾](../characters/飾.md) "decorate" — stand-in for [飾](../characters/飾.md), which cannot appear independently.

装 and 裝 are variants of the same character (装 is the shinjitai and simplified form) with identical readings in every language, so there is no phonetic divergence to reconcile. 裝飾/装飾 (zhuāngshì) is the general word for "decoration" or "ornament" — anything added to an object, room, or person to make it more attractive, from architectural detail to jewelry and clothing accessories. The Vietnamese trang sức has narrowed in common usage to mean "jewelry" specifically, while the Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, and Korean forms retain the broader sense of decoration in general.

Because 装 and 飾 are the vault's own characters and 装/裝 are true variants, the 羅馬字, 諺文, 注音, mandarin, cantonese, japanese, korean, and vietnamese fields are all a straightforward concatenation of 装's and 飾's own values, with no divergence adjustment needed.

The Art page lists the word as "decoration, ornament, accessory," and Dan'a'yo keeps that broad sense, with the narrower Vietnamese "jewelry" sense treated as a regional specialization. A related word is [装置](装置.md) "device, equipment," which shares 装 but means an installed apparatus, not an ornament. The aliases 裝飾 and 装饰 are the traditional and simplified Chinese spellings.
