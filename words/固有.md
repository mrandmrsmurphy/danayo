## Notes
- [固](../characters/固.md) "solid, firm" + [有 (char)](../characters/有%20(char).md) "have, possess" — "proper, inherent, intrinsic" (a characteristic belonging fixedly to something). `vietnamese: cố hữu` is a directly attested, extremely common standard Vietnamese term with the same "inherent, characteristic" sense (tính cố hữu, "inherent trait").
