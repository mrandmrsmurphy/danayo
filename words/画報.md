## Notes
- [画](../characters/画.md) "draw" + [報](../characters/報%20(char).md) "report, tell" — no stand-in relationship applies (画's own stand-in is [[絵画]]; 報's own is itself). Like [[版画]], this word uses 画's colloquial "picture" sense (waa2/화) rather than its own stored literary citation (waak6/획).

画報 means pictorial, picture magazine.
