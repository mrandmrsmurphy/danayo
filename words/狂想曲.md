## Notes
- [狂](../characters/狂.md) "crazy, wild, be a lunatic" + [想](../characters/想.md) "thought, concept" + [曲](../characters/曲.md) "tune, bend" — no stand-in relationship applies (狂's own stand-in is [[風狂]]; 想's own is [[思想]]; 曲's own is [[歌曲]]).

狂想曲 means rhapsody, capriccio.

Mandarin kuángxiǎngqǔ, Cantonese kong4 soeng2 kuk1 (as in [[狂風]], [[羊狂]] and [[風狂]]), Japanese きょうそうきょく, Korean 광상곡 and Vietnamese cuồng tưởng khúc are all fully compositional. The Vietnamese is also the standard musical term for "rhapsody".
