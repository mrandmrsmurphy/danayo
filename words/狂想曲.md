## Etymology
- [狂](../characters/狂.md) "crazy, wild, be a lunatic" + [想](../characters/想.md) "thought, concept" + [曲](../characters/曲.md) "tune, bend" — no stand-in relationship applies (狂's own stand-in is [[風狂]]; 想's own is [[思想]]; 曲's own is [[歌曲]]).

狂想曲 means rhapsody, capriccio.

Fixed a real bug in `cantonese`: kwong4 (an incorrect romanization of 狂, inconsistent with [[狂風]], [[羊狂]], [[風狂]] which all use kong4) → kong4, giving kong4 soeng2 kuk1. Mandarin kuángxiǎngqǔ, Japanese きょうそうきょく (KYOU+SOU+KYOKU), and Korean 광상곡 were already correct and fully compositional. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with cuồng tưởng khúc (狂's own cuồng + 想's own tưởng + 曲's own khúc), also the real, standard Vietnamese musical term for "rhapsody." Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`/`aliases`. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⺢ㄫㄙㄚㄫㄎ⼄ㄎ unique across both words and characters).
