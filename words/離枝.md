## Notes
- [離](../characters/離.md) "depart, leave" + [枝](../characters/枝.md) "branch, twig"

離枝 is an early/alternate written form of the fruit name lychee — 離枝 (lízhī) appears in classical sources (notably describing the fruit's tendency to spoil once its branch is cut, "departing from the branch"), later standardized to the now-universal spelling 荔枝 (lìzhī), which is listed as this word's alias. Because the real modern pronunciation belongs to that standardized spelling rather than a literal reading of 離's own syllable, mandarin lìzhī, Cantonese lai6zi1, Japanese れいし (reishi), and Korean 리치 (a transliteration of the English "litchi") all reflect 荔枝's real attested readings rather than a naive concatenation of 離/枝's own stored fields — 離枝 also, historically, named an ancient state (as reflected in the "kingdom of Lizhi" sense). Vietnamese uses the native word vải for the fruit rather than a Sino-Vietnamese form.
