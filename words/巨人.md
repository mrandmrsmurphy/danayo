## Notes
- [巨](../characters/巨.md) "huge; enormous" + [人](../characters/人.md) "person" — no stand-in relationship applies (巨's own `stand_in` is [[巨大]], 人's is itself).

巨人 names a giant — a person of enormous size, whether mythological, literary (famously the man-eating giants of *Attack on Titan*, 進撃の巨人), or figuratively (a towering figure in a field, "a giant of industry").

Japanese きょじん, Korean 거인, and Vietnamese cự nhân are all standard, non-drifted readings — Vietnamese everyday speech more often uses the native người khổng lồ ("giant person") instead.
