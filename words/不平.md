## Notes
- [不 (char)](../characters/不%20(char).md) "not" + [平](../characters/平.md) "even, level, fair" — the negation of 平; "unjust, unfair, uneven," and by extension the resentment felt at injustice.

Mandarin bùpíng, Cantonese bat1 ping4, Japanese ふへい, and Sino-Vietnamese bất bình are all standard compositional readings. Korean 불평 follows the 不→불 assimilation rule already documented on [[不信]]/[[不及]]/[[不可]]/[[不均]] — already correctly stored, and is additionally the everyday word for "complaint, grumbling" (불평하다, "to complain"). Removed blank `swadesh`/`aliases`.
