## Notes
- [拉](../characters/拉%20(char).md) "drag, pull, play a bowed instrument" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [拉](../characters/拉%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

拉 means to drag, pull, or play a bowed instrument.

Mandarin lā, Cantonese laai1, Japanese らつ, Korean 랍, and Vietnamese lạp are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones at the word level (注音 ㄌㄚㄆ is shared only with bound characters 蠟/臘, neither of which has an independent word at this exact reading).
