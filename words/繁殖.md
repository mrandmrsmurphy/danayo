## Notes
- [繁](../characters/繁.md) "lush, complicated" + [殖](../characters/殖.md) "breed, spawn, reproduce, increase" — 殖's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, but 繁's own is [[繁茂]], so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply; 繁殖 legitimizes 殖 alone. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 繁 is individually `kwin: false`.

繁殖 means to breed or reproduce.

Mandarin fánzhí, Cantonese faan4 zik6, Japanese はんしょく (hanshoku), and Korean 번식 (beonsik) are all standard, fully compositional readings, and 繁殖 is itself a common attested word for "breed, reproduce" in all four languages. Vietnamese phồn thực is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading and itself the standard Vietnamese term. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
