## Notes
- [基督](../words/基督.md) "Christ" (a transliteration of "Christ," ultimately from Greek Χριστός) + [教 (char)](../characters/教.md) "teaching, religion" — literally "Christ-religion," Christianity.

Mandarin Jīdūjiào, Cantonese gei1 duk1 gaau3, Korean 기독교, and Vietnamese Cơ đốc giáo are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 基's, 督's, and 教's own stored citations exactly. Japanese キリストきょう (kirisutokyō) mixes 基督's own real Japanese form (キリスト, kirisuto — Japanese borrows the transliteration directly rather than reading 基督 compositionally) with 教's own on'yomi きょう (kyō), matching the real standard Japanese word キリスト教.

`kwin` is false: 教 alone diverges from Korean on its own character page, which alone is enough to make the AND-rule false; 基 and 督 are each independently kwin-true. No homophones (注音 ㄍㄧㄉㄛㄎㄍ⼄ㄨ unique among both words and characters).

- For Christian terminology, see [基督敎](../lexipedia/基督敎.md).
