## Notes
- [仲](../characters/仲.md) "in-between, intermediary" + [媒](../characters/媒.md) "matchmaker, intermediary" — "matchmaker" (traditionally, one who arranges marriages). `japanese: なこうど` and `vietnamese: mai mối` are each language's own real native/native-adjacent word for this concept, matching this word's own real Chinese alias 媒人 (méirén, already listed) rather than a strict reading of 仲媒's own characters.

**Real content bugs found and fixed**: `mandarin` had been set to "méirén" — the reading of the alias 媒人, not of the headword 仲媒 itself — corrected to zhòngméi; filled the blank `cantonese: zung6 mui4`; and removed a self-referential alias (仲媒 was listed as its own alias). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 둥뫼 diverges entirely from Sino-Korean 중매.
