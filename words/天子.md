## Notes
- [天](../characters/天%20(char).md) "heaven" + [子](../characters/子.md) "child" — no stand-in relationship applies (天 is its own stand-in; 子's own is [[児子]]).

天子 means "Son of Heaven" — the traditional East Asian title for the emperor, who ruled by the Mandate of Heaven.

Japanese てんし, Korean 천자, Mandarin tiānzǐ, and Cantonese tin1 zi2 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese thiên tử is a standard reading. Removed the redundant `品詞` field. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄝㄋㄐㄜ unique across both words and characters).
