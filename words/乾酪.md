## Notes
- [乾 (char)](../characters/乾%20(char).md) "dry" + [酪](../characters/酪.md) "cheese; dairy; fermented drink" — cheese, a dried/aged dairy product. Written 干酪 in Simplified Chinese; 乾 is the real character here, not 干 ("shield").

Vietnamese can lạc (confirmed via web search alongside [[乳酪]]'s own nhũ lạc) is the formal Hán Việt term for cheese specifically — everyday Vietnamese instead uses the French loanword phô mai. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 견락 diverges from Sino-Korean 건락 in the first syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼶ㄋㄌㄚㄎ unique among both words and characters).
