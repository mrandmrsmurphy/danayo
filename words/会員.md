## Notes
- [会 (char)](../characters/会%20(char).md) "meet, gather" + [員](../characters/員.md) "personnel, employee" — "member" (of a club, organization, or association). Directly attested and standard across all five languages (mandarin huìyuán, japanese かいいん, korean 회원, vietnamese hội viên). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 훠운 diverges from Sino-Korean 회원 via the AND-rule, since 会's own field independently diverges (despite 員 itself being `kwin: true`). No homophones (注音 ㄏ⼔ㄨㄋ unique among both words and characters).
