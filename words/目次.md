## Notes
- [目 (char)](../characters/目%20(char).md) "eye; item" + [次](../characters/次.md) "order"

目次 is a table of contents: the list at the front of a book that gives its headings in order. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin mùcì as "table of contents" and Japanese もくじ as "contents, table of contents". Korean 목차 and Cantonese muk6 ci3 match. The word is literally "items in order".

It differs from [目録](目録.md), a list, catalogue or inventory of things, and from [索引](索引.md), the index at the back that points to topics by name. A 目次 follows the order of the book, where an index follows the alphabet or the sound.
