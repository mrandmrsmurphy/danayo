## Notes
- [霊](../characters/霊.md) "spirit; numinous" + [宝](../characters/宝.md) "treasure" — the traditional spelling 靈寶 is an alias, since 霊 and 宝 are the shinjitai of 靈 and 寶.

霊宝 is a "numinous treasure", a sacred object or text. It is best known in Daoism as 靈寶 (Lingbao), "Numinous Treasure" or "Sacred Jewel": the name of the Daoist school that arose in the early fifth century and was absorbed into the Shangqing and Zhengyi currents under the Tang, and of the scriptures it produced. It also names one of the Three Pure Ones, 靈寶天尊, whose other title is 太上玉宸大道君. In Japanese, 霊宝 (れいほう) is the treasure of a temple or shrine, the sacred objects it keeps. The same two characters are also the name of the city of Lingbao in Henan. The Mandarin and Cantonese fields are the readings of the characters, and the Japanese field is the attested Japanese reading; the Korean and Vietnamese fields are compositional (Korean follows the North Korean readings).

The full name 靈寶天尊 (here 霊宝天尊) is compositional, 霊宝 plus [天尊](天尊.md), and so needs no entry of its own. Compare [霊魂](霊魂.md), the soul, and [宝物](宝物.md), a treasure.
