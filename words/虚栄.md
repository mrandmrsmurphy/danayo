## Notes
- [虚 (char)](../characters/虚%20(char).md) "empty" + [栄](../characters/栄.md) "glory"

虚栄 is vanity: the love of show and of others' praise, glory with nothing in it. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xūróng as "vanity; empty glory; vainglorious" and Japanese きょえい as "vanity". Korean 허영 and Cantonese heoi1 wing4 match.

It is [傲慢](傲慢.md), pride, that lives on other people's eyes, and it is the pretense of the [逞](逞.md), show-off. The honest pride is [自尊](自尊.md).
