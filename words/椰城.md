## Notes
- [椰](../characters/椰.md) "coconut" + [城](../characters/城.md) "castle, wall" — "coconut city"; no stand-in relationship applies (椰's own stand-in is [[椰子]], 城's is [[城郭]]).

椰城 is Jakarta, the capital of Indonesia ([[印尼]]). English Wiktionary marks it dated and quotes a 2009 broadcast in which a speaker says he had never taken the four-hour train from Bandung to the capital, 椰城. The Hokkien reading it lists is Giâ-siâⁿ. It gives 雅加達, the phonetic spelling now in use, as the synonym; that is not a word in the vault, so this translated name is the Dan'a'yo one. The name is a translation, "coconut city", not a transliteration of Jakarta; English Wiktionary gives no etymology for it.

Mandarin Yéchéng and Cantonese je4 sing4 are as at English Wiktionary. Japanese ジャカルタ and Korean 자카르타 are the ordinary names of the city (from memory, not re-checked), so `kwin` is false. The `vietnamese` field holds the compositional Hán-Việt reading gia thành (as 椰子 reads gia tử; 城 thành; not attested for this name).

No homophones (注音 ⼘ㄙㄧㄫ unique across words and characters, checked 2026-10-12). The 注音 needs no syllable-boundary dot.
