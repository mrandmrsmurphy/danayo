## Notes
- [賛](../characters/賛.md) "help, support" + [嘆 (char)](../characters/嘆%20(char).md) "moan, sigh" — to sigh in admiration

賛嘆 means to praise with a sigh: to admire so greatly that one gasps or exclaims (賛嘆の声, "voices of admiration"; 賛嘆に値する, "worthy of admiration").  賛 contributes the approval and 嘆 the audible sigh, so the word describes admiration that is felt and voiced at once, stronger than a calm compliment.  The aliases 讚嘆, 讚歎 and 贊嘆 are the traditional spellings; 嘆 and 歎 are variants of one character.

賛's own `stand_in` is [[賛助]], so this word is not its legitimizer.  Mandarin zàntàn, Cantonese zaan3 taan3, Japanese さんたん and Korean 찬탄 are the same compound.  No Vietnamese form is given: 嘆's character file lists han and hen, not the usual thán, so the compound could not be derived from the vault's own fields.  `kwin` is false because the Dan'a'yo 잔탄 differs from the Korean 찬탄 in its initial.
