## Notes
- [賛](../characters/賛.md) "help, support" + [嘆 (char)](../characters/嘆%20(char).md) "moan, sigh" — to sigh in admiration

Wiktionary: Japanese 賛嘆 (さんたん) "high praise" and, as a verb, "to highly praise"; Chinese 讚嘆 "to highly praise; to gasp in admiration". The aliases 讚嘆, 讚歎 and 贊嘆 are the traditional spellings. 賛's own `stand_in` is [[賛助]], so this word is not its legitimizer.
