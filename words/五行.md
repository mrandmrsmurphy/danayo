## Notes
- [五 (char)](../characters/五%20(char).md) "five" + [行 (char)](../characters/行%20(char).md) "phase, element" — Wu Xing, the Five Phases/Elements: 木 Wood, 火 Fire, 土 Earth, 金 Metal, 水 Water, the cyclical framework of generative and destructive relationships underlying traditional Chinese cosmology, medicine, astrology, and divination.

五行 interlocks with the Five Colors ([[五色]]) and Five Directions ([[五方]]) already documented in this vault. Mandarin wǔxíng, Cantonese ng5 hang4, and Korean 오행 are all standard, fully compositional readings.
