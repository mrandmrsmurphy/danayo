## Notes
- [戦](../characters/戦.md) "war, battle" + [車](../characters/車%20(char).md) "cart, car" — "war vehicle." Neither character's own `stand_in` points here (戦's is [[戦争]]; 車's is itself), so 戦車 is an independent compound.

戦車 is a tank, an armoured fighting vehicle. The compound is old in Chinese, where 戰車 (zhànchē, zin3 ce1) means the horse-drawn war chariot of the Spring and Autumn period, and in Taiwan also a modern tank (mainland usage prefers the loanword 坦克). Japanese せんしゃ and Korean 전차 use the same characters for the modern armoured vehicle, and Vietnamese chiến xa shares the form. Dan'a'yo takes the modern tank as its designated sense; the ancient chariot is the older, literary reading of the same characters.

Compare [[大砲]], the cannon, and [[銃]], the handheld firearm.
