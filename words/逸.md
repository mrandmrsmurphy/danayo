## Notes
- [逸 (char)](characters/逸%20(char).md) "flee, escape" — 逸 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

Mandarin yì, cantonese jat6, korean 일, and vietnamese dật are all directly attested standard readings, matching the character's own stored values exactly. **Found and fixed a `kwin` miscalculation on 逸(char).md itself**: it stored `kwin: true`, but byte-level comparison shows Dan'a'yo 읻 (U+C77B) and Sino-Korean 일 (U+C77C) are visually near-identical but genuinely different Hangul syllables (differing only in final consonant) — the same class of divergence bug found elsewhere this session (cf. 읫/의, 랃/랄). Corrected to `kwin: false` on both pages. Also **fixed a corrupted `羅馬字` value** (`'''id'`, malformed triple-quote) to the standard single-leading-apostrophe form `'id`. Part of a genuine three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group with [[一]] ("one") and [[壱]] ("loyal, faithful") — coincidental, unrelated in meaning; all three pages already mutually cross-linked.

逸 (char)'s own `stand_in` is this exact word (逸, itself), so 逸 (char) is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry by this same word.
