## Notes
- [坐 (char)](../characters/坐%20(char).md) "sit"

坐 is 会意: [[留]] ("to stay") + [[土]] ("ground") — to remain stationary on the ground; "to sit."

Mandarin zuò and Cantonese zo6 preserve the sense directly. Japanese ザ (ZA, on'yomi, formal/technical register: 座る suwaru uses the related graph 座 for the everyday verb) cover the sense. Korean 좌 (jwa) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese toạ is the Hán Việt reading (tọa lạc, "located, situated"; tọa thiền, "sitting meditation"; an tọa, "please be seated"). The character page's other stored candidates: ngồi is the everyday native Vietnamese verb for "to sit," an unrelated core-vocabulary word rather than a Sino-Vietnamese doublet (base body-position verbs like this typically resist borrowing cross-linguistically); toà/tòa reflects this vault's decision to fold the closely related character 座 ("seat," a later graphic split from 坐 distinguishing the verb from its derived noun sense, paralleling the verb/noun tone alternation seen in 好 hǎo/hào) into this same page as an alias — tòa nhà ("building"), tòa án ("court") — rather than a second reading of the "sit" verb itself.

This word shares its reading (jwa/좌/ㄐ⺢) with [[渣]] ("dregs; sediment"), still unperfected and awaiting its own turn in this sweep.
