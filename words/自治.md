## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" + [治](../characters/治.md) "govern" — autonomy, self-government (自治区/자치/tự trị). Attested across Mandarin, Japanese, Korean, and Vietnamese.
The Government page lists 自治 as "autonomy, self-government," and Dan'a'yo keeps that sense, the administration of one's own affairs by a community or region. It differs from the existing word [自主](自主.md) "be independent, autonomy," which is independence in decision for a person or group, and from [主権](主権.md) "sovereignty," the supreme authority of a state. Its second element is the same 治 "govern" found in [統治](統治.md) "to rule," the stand-in for 治. The Korean 자치 differs from the Dan'a'yo 諺文 즤치 in the first syllable, so `kwin` is false. The readings are Mandarin zìzhì, Cantonese zi6 zi6, Japanese jichi (じち) and Sino-Vietnamese tự trị. The contrasts with 自主 and 主権 are from the glosses on their own pages and have not been checked against a source.
