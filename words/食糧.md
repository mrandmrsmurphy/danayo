## Notes
- [食 (char)](../characters/食%20(char).md) "food" + [糧 (char)](../characters/糧%20(char).md) "grain; provisions"

食糧 is provisions: the grain and food that a people or an army lives on. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin shíliáng as "grain; food" and Japanese しょくりょう as "food, dietary provisions". Cantonese sik6 loeng4 matches, and Korean 식량 is the same word; the Korean field gives the North Korean form 식량.

It is the food of a whole group, set against the single meal, and it is the stock that [飢饉](飢饉.md), famine, runs out of. The grain it is made of is the [穀物](穀物.md).
