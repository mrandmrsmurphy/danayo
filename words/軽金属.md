## Notes
- [軽 (char)](../characters/軽%20(char).md) "light" + [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal" + [属](../characters/属.md) "category, kind" — light metals.

Wiktionary: Japanese 軽金属 (けいきんぞく), sciences, "light metal"; Chinese 輕金屬 "light metal(s)", kept as the traditional-form alias. Of the three characters, 属's own `stand_in` is 所属, so this word is not its legitimizer; 軽's and 金's are themselves.
