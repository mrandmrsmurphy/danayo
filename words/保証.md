## Notes
- [保](../characters/保.md) "defend" + [証](../characters/証.md) "prove, confirm, verify" — "to ensure, attest, stipulate, guarantee" (合同保証, "contract guarantee"; 保証人, "guarantor"). Mandarin bǎozhèng, Cantonese bou2 zing3, Japanese ほしょう, Korean 보증, and Vietnamese bảo chứng are all directly attested standard readings. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 밧징 diverges from Sino-Korean 보증 in the first syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄨㄐㄧㄫ unique among both words and characters).
