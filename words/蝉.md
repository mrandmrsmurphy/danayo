## Notes
- [蝉](characters/蝉%20(char).md) "cicada" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 蝉 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

蝉 means cicada.

Mandarin chán, Cantonese sim4, Korean 선, and Vietnamese thiền are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 蝉's own stored citations exactly. Japanese せみ is the real, standard native word for "cicada" (rather than the on'yomi せん/ぜん), matching 蝉's own stored native reading.
