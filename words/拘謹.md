## Notes
- [拘](../characters/拘.md) "restrain, bind" + [謹](../characters/謹.md) "careful, prudent" — "bound and careful," prim. Their own `stand_in` values: 拘's is [[拘束]]; 謹's is [[謹慎]]; none points here, so 拘謹 is an independent compound.

拘謹 is prim: stiff, reserved and overcautious, holding oneself to strict propriety. Mandarin jūjǐn and Cantonese keoi1 gan2 are the ordinary words. Japanese and Korean do not use it that way, so those two fields hold the character readings. Compare [[拘束]] (to restrain) and [[仮正経]] (prude).
