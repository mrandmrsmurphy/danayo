## Etymology
- [輸](../characters/輸.md) "transport" + [血 (char)](../characters/血%20(char).md) "blood"

## Notes
Mandarin has three attested pronunciations for this compound (shūxuè/shūxuě/shūxiě, reflecting 血's own multiple readings), cantonese syu1 hyut3, japanese ゆけつ, and korean 수혈 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese thâu huyết is the honest compositional reading (輸's own thâu + 血's own huyết); modern Vietnamese instead uses the unrelated native term "truyền máu" for blood transfusion, but no independently attested Sino-Vietnamese loanword using these exact characters was found. `输血` is the standard simplified-character spelling variant (輸→输), correctly listed as an alias. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 수훧 diverges from Sino-Korean 수혈 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨㄇㄏ⼔ㄊ unique among both words and characters).

Both character-page citations already correctly present. 輸's own `stand_in` is 輸送 and 血's own `stand_in` is 血 itself, so this compound legitimizes neither constituent.
