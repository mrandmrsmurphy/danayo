## Notes
- [学](../characters/学.md) "study" + [堂](../characters/堂.md) "hall" — no stand-in relationship applies (学's own stand-in is [[学習]]; 堂's own is [[会堂]]).

学堂 means an old-style private school — a traditional term for a place of learning, largely displaced by 学校 in modern usage.

Mandarin xuétáng, Cantonese hok6 tong4, Japanese がくどう, Korean 학당, and Vietnamese học đường are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `cantonese` was garbled invalid jyutping ("xuo2 tang2") — corrected to compositional hok6 tong4. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄎㄉㄚㄫ unique across both words and characters).
