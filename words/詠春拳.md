## Notes
- [詠 (char)](../characters/詠%20(char).md) "recite, chant" is separately legitimized by [[詠]] itself. [春 (char)](../characters/春%20(char).md) "springtime" is separately legitimized by [[春]] itself. [拳](../characters/拳.md) "fist" is separately legitimized by [[拳骨]]. No cranberry.

Mandarin/cantonese/japanese/korean/vietnamese all confirmed standard, real-attested transliterations of this proper martial-art name.

Technically different than 永春拳, though they are often confused.

- See [Art](../lexipedia/Art.md).
