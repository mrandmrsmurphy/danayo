## Notes
- [魑](../characters/魑.md) "mountain demon" + [魅](../characters/魅.md) "charm, captivate" — legitimizing note: 魑's own `stand_in` is [[魑魅]] itself, already annotated in its own citation; 魅's own `stand_in` is [[魅惑]], so transitivity fails — no `#cranberry`.

Korean uses the irregular reading 이매 rather than the naively compositional 치매 (which would collide with 치매, "dementia," in modern Korean) — 이매 is the real, attested reading, notably surviving in the classical four-character idiom 이매망량 (魑魅魍魎, "every kind of evil spirit"). Mandarin chīmèi, Cantonese ci1mei6, Japanese ちみ, and Sino-Vietnamese si mị are all standard, compositional readings.
