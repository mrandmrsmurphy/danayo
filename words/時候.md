## Notes
- [時 (char)](../characters/時%20(char).md) "when" + [候](../characters/候.md) "climate; expectation" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (時's own points to bare `時`, 候's own to `気候`); 時候 is an independent compound.

The two senses differ by language. In Mandarin and Cantonese, 时候/si4 hau6 is one of the most common words, meaning "time, moment, when" (什么时候 "when," 有时候 "sometimes"), essentially a grammaticalized time-word and not "season/climate." Japanese 時候 jikō genuinely means "season; the time of year" in a formal register (時候の挨拶, the seasonal greeting that opens formal letters). The `english` field documents both senses, and the Cantonese reading si4 hau6 matches 時's own si4 and 候's own hau6.

The `vietnamese` field is omitted: no Sino-Vietnamese compound "thời hậu" is attested as an actual word in modern Vietnamese. Vietnamese expresses "time" with thời gian (the reading of [[時間]]) and not with a calque of 時候.
