## Etymology
- [金 (char)](../characters/金%20(char).md) "metal, gold" + [柑](../characters/柑.md) "mandarin orange" — "kumquat," named for its golden-orange color.

## Notes
Japanese キンカン (kinkan) is the real, directly attested standard word for kumquat and is exactly compositional (金 KIN + 柑 KAN).

Earlier Notes on this page may state a different value or a different reason.
