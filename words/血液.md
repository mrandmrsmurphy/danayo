## Notes
- [血 (char)](../characters/血%20(char).md) "blood" + [液](../characters/液.md) "fluid" — blood, in formal/scientific register (血液型 "blood type," 血液検査 "blood test," 血液循環 "blood circulation"), distinct from the colloquial bare [[血]]. Attested across Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, and Korean.
The blank `vietnamese` field now holds the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading of the two characters, huyết dịch (血 huyết, 液 dịch), following the vault's convention for these fields; everyday Vietnamese uses the native máu for blood, so this is the formal, medical register. The Korean 혈액 differs from the Dan'a'yo 諺文 훧역 in both syllables, so `kwin` is false. The Body page lists 血 and 血液 together on one line as "blood," and Dan'a'yo keeps the sense "blood," with 血液 the formal register and the bare [血](血.md) the plain word. It is related to [体液](体液.md) "bodily fluid" and [液体](液体.md), the stand-in for the character 液. The readings are Mandarin xuèyè, Cantonese hyut3 jik6 and Japanese ketsueki (けつえき). The Vietnamese register remark is from memory and has not been checked against a source.
