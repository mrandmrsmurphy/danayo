## Notes
- [朔](../characters/朔.md) "first day of the lunar month" + [日](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 朔 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 日's own stand-in is itself.

朔日 means the first day of the lunar month.

Mandarin shuòrì, Cantonese sok3 jat6, Japanese さくじつ, Korean 삭일, and Vietnamese sóc nhật are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 朔's and 日's own stored citations exactly).
