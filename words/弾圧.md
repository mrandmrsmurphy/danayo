## Notes
- [弾](../characters/弾.md) "bullet" + [圧](../characters/圧%20(char).md) "press, control" — no stand-in relationship applies (弾's own stand-in is [[弾丸]]; 圧's own is itself).

弾圧 means to subjugate or repress — using force to suppress opposition or dissent.

Mandarin tányā, Cantonese taan4 aat3, Japanese だんあつ, Korean 탄압, and Vietnamese đàn áp are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄋㄚㄊ unique across both words and characters).
