## Notes
- [敗](../characters/敗.md) "lose" + [北](../characters/北.md) "north; flee"

敗北 is defeat, and to be defeated. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin bàiběi as "to be defeated; to lose a battle" and Japanese はいぼく as "defeat, loss; to be defeated". Korean 패배 and Cantonese baai6 bak1 match.

The 北 is the old sense of turning one's back to flee. It is the opposite of [勝利](勝利.md), and the person who suffers it is the [敗者](敗者.md). A setback short of defeat is a [挫折](挫折.md).
