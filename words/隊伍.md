## Notes
- [隊 (char)](../characters/隊%20(char).md) "group, team" + [伍 (char)](../characters/伍%20(char).md) "troops"

隊伍 means a rank, contingent, or line of troops — an organized body of people moving or standing in formation. Mandarin 隊伍/队伍 (duìwǔ), Cantonese 隊伍 (deoi6ng5), Japanese 隊伍 (たいご), Korean 대오 (daeo), and Sino-Vietnamese đội ngũ are all standard terms across the CJKV sphere — đội ngũ in particular is an extremely common everyday Vietnamese word, extending beyond the literal military sense to any organized group or workforce (đội ngũ nhân viên, "staff/personnel").
