## Notes
- [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" + [叉 (char)](../characters/叉%20(char).md) "fork; prong"

音叉 is a tuning fork. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 音叉 (Mandarin yīnchā, Cantonese jam1 caa1) as "tuning fork", and Japanese おんさ as a musical-instrument term, "a tuning fork". The Korean 음차 and Vietnamese âm thoa fields are built from the readings of the two characters.
