## Notes
- [気 (char)](../characters/気%20(char).md) "breath; spirit" + [絶 (char)](../characters/絶%20(char).md) "cut off"

気絶 is to faint: to lose consciousness for a time. Wiktionary glosses Japanese きぜつ as "a faint, knockout or other temporary loss of consciousness; to faint". Wiktionary has no Chinese entry for the word, so the Mandarin and Cantonese readings are built from the character readings. Korean 기절 matches.

The stress is on the loss of consciousness, where [昏倒](昏倒.md) is the fall. A long loss of consciousness is a coma, and its end is [覚醒](覚醒.md).
