## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" is separately legitimized by [[自身]]. [給](../characters/給.md) "supply, give" is separately legitimized by [[補給]]. No cranberry: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here.

自給 combines 自 ("self") with 給 ("supply") — "self-sufficient, self-reliant," as in 自給自足.

Mandarin zìjǐ uses 給's genuine literary jǐ reading (as in 供給 gōngjǐ) rather than the character's stored colloquial gěi — a real attested variant, not contamination from the unrelated homophone 自己, despite sharing the same reading coincidentally. Cantonese zi6kap1 (space removed), japanese じきゅう (jikyuu), and korean 자급 (jageup) were all already correct, fully compositional readings.
