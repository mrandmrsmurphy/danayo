## Notes
- [海](../characters/海.md) "sea" + [賊](../characters/賊.md) "thief" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here (海's own is [[海洋]]; 賊's own is [[盗賊]]), so 海賊 is an independent compound. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: `characters/海.md` is `kwin: true`, `characters/賊.md` is `kwin: false` → compound `false`.

海賊 means "pirate" — a clean, directly parallel compound across the whole sphere. Mandarin hǎizéi, Cantonese hoi2 caak6, Japanese かいぞく (kaizoku), Korean 해적 (haejeok), and Vietnamese hải tặc all converge on the same reading order and sense, with no notable cross-linguistic divergence. Vietnamese tặc (rather than 賊's other stored reading giặc, the more general native word for "bandit, invader") is the register used specifically in compounds like this one.
