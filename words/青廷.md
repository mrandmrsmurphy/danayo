## Notes
- [青 (char)](../characters/青%20(char).md) "blue" + [廷](../characters/廷.md) "imperial court" — a 代用字 form: each character is the original without its 虫 radical, 青 for 蜻 and 廷 for 蜓, which are not in the vault.

青廷 is the dragonfly, written for 蜻蜓 (Mandarin qīngtíng, Cantonese cing1 ting4), which Wiktionary glosses "dragonfly." Japanese has it as せいてい (seitei), for a large hawker dragonfly of the family Aeshnidae, beside the more common とんぼ (tonbo), which is written 蜻蛉. The same device gives [悉率](悉率.md) for the cricket and [盧玆](盧玆.md) for the cormorant.
