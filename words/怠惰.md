## Notes
- [怠](../characters/怠.md) "lazy" + [惰](../characters/惰.md) "indolent" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes both [怠](../characters/怠.md) and [惰](../characters/惰.md) as independent Dan'a'yo entries; neither character appears independently (`#cranberry`).

怠惰 means lazy or indolent — a near-synonymous doubling of two characters both meaning "lazy."

Mandarin dàiduò, Cantonese toi5 do6, Japanese たいだ, Korean 태타, and Vietnamese đãi noạ are all standard, fully compositional readings. Trimmed malformed multi-value `cantonese` to the single compositional reading. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄧㄉ⺢ unique across both words and characters).
