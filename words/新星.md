## Notes
- [新](../characters/新%20(char).md) "new" + [星](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 新 and 星's own stand-ins are themselves). A star that suddenly brightens dramatically; a "new star" appearing where none was visible before.

Mandarin xīnxīng, Cantonese san1 sing1, Japanese しんせい, Korean 신성, and Vietnamese tân tinh are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄧㄋㄙㄝㄫ unique across both words and characters).
