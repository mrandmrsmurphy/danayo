## Notes
- [才](../characters/才.md) "ability, genius" + [能](../characters/能.md) "technique, craft" — no stand-in relationship applies (才's own stand-in is [[天才]]; 能's own is [[技能]]).

才能 means ability or talent.

Mandarin cáinéng, Cantonese coi4 nang4, Japanese さいのう, Korean 재능, and Vietnamese tài năng are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄚㄧㄋㄨㄫ unique across both words and characters).
