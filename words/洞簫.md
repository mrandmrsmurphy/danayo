## Notes
- [洞](../characters/洞.md) "cave, hole" + [簫](../characters/簫.md) "xiao" — 洞簫 is the stand-in word for [簫](../characters/簫.md); 簫 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse.

洞簫 is the xiao: the vertical, end-blown bamboo flute of the Chinese musical tradition. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 洞簫 (an alias, 洞箫; Mandarin dòngxiāo, Cantonese dung6 siu1) as "xiao, a vertical, notched, end-blown bamboo flute used since ancient times in traditional and classical Chinese music", and, in Southern Min, figuratively "opium pipe". It glosses Japanese どうしょう as "xiao, vertical end-blown Chinese flute". For the single 簫 it lists, in Cantonese and some Min varieties, a "transverse flute", and, for Vietnamese tiêu, "the xiao, a vertical bamboo flute used in traditional Vietnamese music". It has no Korean entry, so Korean 동소 here is built from the characters, and Vietnamese động tiêu is as well.

The bound character 簫 is the xiao, and 洞簫 is its stand-in word. The generic flute is the [[笛]], and a Japanese end-blown flute is the [[尺八]].
