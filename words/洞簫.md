## Notes
- [洞](../characters/洞.md) "cave, hole" + [簫](../characters/簫.md) "xiao" — 洞簫 is the stand-in word for [簫](../characters/簫.md); 簫 is too bounded to stand alone in Dan'a'yo discourse.

洞簫 is the vertical, end-blown bamboo flute of the Chinese musical tradition — a deeper, warmer-toned relative of the horizontally-held 笛 (dízi). 洞 ("cave, hollow") here describes the instrument's open, hollow bore rather than any literal cave. The instrument and its name spread throughout the East Asian musical sphere, including into Vietnamese traditional music, where động tiêu remains a recognized folk instrument in its own right rather than merely a dictionary calque.

Japanese どうしょう, Korean 동소, and Vietnamese động tiêu are all standard compositional Sino-readings. No specific HSK level is confirmed. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄫㄙㄛㄨ unique across both words and characters).
