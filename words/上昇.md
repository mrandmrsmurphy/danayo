## Notes
- [上 (char)](../characters/上%20(char).md) "up, above" + [昇](../characters/昇.md) "to rise" — this word is the `stand_in` legitimizer for [[昇]], which cannot appear independently.

上昇 is the ordinary word for "to rise, to ascend, to increase" across the sphere, equally at home describing a literal rise (temperature, altitude, smoke) and a figurative one (prices, rank, morale) — Korean 상승 in particular is the everyday word for a market/statistic "rising," among the most common words built on this pairing. Vietnamese thượng thăng (compositionally derived, from 上's own well-attested thượng and 昇's own thăng) carries a more elevated, literary register than the equally valid native Vietnamese lên ("to go up"), closer in feel to English "to ascend" than the plainer "to rise." `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 샹숭 diverges from Sino-Korean 상승 in both syllables. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼘ㄫㄙㄨㄫ unique among both words and characters).
