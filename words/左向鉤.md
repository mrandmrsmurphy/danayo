## Notes
- [左](../characters/左.md) "left" + [向 (char)](../characters/向%20(char).md) "toward; facing" + [鉤 (char)](../characters/鉤%20(char).md) "hook"

Wiktionary has no entry for 左向鉤; it is a compound of 左 "left", 向 "toward; facing" and [[鉤]], Wiktionary's Chinese "hook; barb". It names the left-facing hook, a stroke whose tip turns to the left, spelled out with 向 so the first piece reads as a direction. On the [[Stroke]] page it is the first of the three varieties of hook, beside the [[戈鉤]] and the [[臥鉤]]. Its readings are compositional.
