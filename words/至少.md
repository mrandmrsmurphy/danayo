## Notes
- [至 (char)](../characters/至%20(char).md) "to" + [少 (char)](../characters/少%20(char).md) "few"

至少 is at least. English Wiktionary has an entry only for Chinese 至少 (Mandarin zhìshǎo, Cantonese zi3 siu2), glossed "at least; (to say the) least". The Japanese ししょう and Korean 지소 fields are built from the readings of the two characters. Compare [最少](最少.md), the least.
