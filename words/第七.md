## Notes
- [第 (char)](../characters/第%20(char).md) "-th (ordinal prefix)" + [七 (char)](../characters/七%20(char).md) "seven"

第七 is the seventh in a series. The prefix 第 turns cardinal numbers into ordinals across all CJKV languages; 第七 follows the same pattern as [[第一]], [[第二]], [[第八]] and the rest. Japanese also has だいしち, but 七 is usually read なな in counts, as on [[七百]].
