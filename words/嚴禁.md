## Notes
- [厳](../characters/厳%20(char).md) "strict, rigorous" + [禁](../characters/禁.md) "prohibit, forbid" — no stand-in relationship applies (厳 is its own stand-in; 禁's own is [[禁止]]).

嚴禁 means strictly forbidden or strictly prohibited.

Japanese げんきん, Korean 엄금, and Vietnamese nghiêm cấm are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ⼄ㄇㄍㄧㄇ unique across both words and characters).
