## Notes
- [運](../characters/運.md) "transport, move" + [河](../characters/河.md) "river" — canal.

運河 is a canal: an artificial waterway cut for transport or irrigation. It is "transport-river"; Mandarin yùnhé, Cantonese wan6 ho4, Japanese うんが and Korean 운하 are the same compound. The Grand Canal of China is the standard example. A canal differs from a natural river ([[川]]) and from an irrigation channel ([[灌漑]]) in carrying boats.
