## Notes
- [庶](../characters/庶.md) "common; numerous" + [民](../characters/民.md) "people" — this word is the stand-in for [庶](../characters/庶.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

庶民 names the common people or ordinary citizens — historically drawn from the feudal class distinction between nobility/officials and everyone else, still current today in the sense of "ordinary, everyday people" (庶民の生活, "the life of ordinary people").

Japanese しょみん, Korean 서민, and Vietnamese thứ dân are all standard, non-drifted readings. No homophones (注音 ㄙ⼄ㄇㄧㄋ unique across both words and characters).
