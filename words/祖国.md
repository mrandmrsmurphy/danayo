## Notes
- [祖](../characters/祖.md) "ancestor" + [国](../characters/国.md) "nation" — literally "ancestors' nation."

祖国 means motherland or homeland — the nation of one's ancestors, invoked with strong patriotic and emotional resonance in national anthems, wartime rhetoric, and diaspora identity (as in Vietnamese Tổ quốc, a central term in Vietnam's own national anthem).

Mandarin zǔguó, Cantonese zou2 gwok3, Japanese そこく (sokoku), Korean 조국 (joguk), and Vietnamese tổ quốc are all fully compositional and share this same patriotic register across the sphere.
