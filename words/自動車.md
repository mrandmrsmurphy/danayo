## Notes
- [自](../characters/自.md) "self" is separately legitimized by [[自身]]. [動 (char)](../characters/動%20(char).md) "move" is separately legitimized by [[動]] itself. [車 (char)](../characters/車%20(char).md) "cart, car" is separately legitimized by [[車]] itself. No cranberry: none of the three constituents' own `stand_in` points here.

自動車 combines 自 ("self"), 動 ("move"), and 車 ("car") — "automobile," literally "self-moving vehicle."
