## Notes
- [除](../characters/除.md) "exclude" + [湿 (char)](../characters/湿%20(char).md) "wet, damp"

除湿 means to dehumidify — to remove excess moisture from the air. Mandarin 除濕/除湿 (chúshī), Cantonese 除濕 (ceoi4sap1), Japanese 除湿 (じょしつ), and Korean 제습 (jeseup) are all standard, everyday terms for this (除湿機/제습기, "dehumidifier"). Sino-Vietnamese trừ thấp is less common in casual speech, where native hút ẩm ("absorb moisture") and khử ẩm ("eliminate moisture") are the everyday terms; trừ thấp still appears in more formal/technical registers.
