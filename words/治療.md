## Notes
- [治](../characters/治.md) "govern, rule" + [療](../characters/療.md) "treat, cure" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 療 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (治's own stand-in is [[統治]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

治療 means to treat, cure.

Mandarin zhìliáo, Cantonese zi6 liu4, Japanese ちりょう, and Korean 치료 are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 治's and 療's own stored citations exactly). Vietnamese trị liệu (治's own trị + 療's own liệu) is also the real, standard Vietnamese term for "treatment, therapy." Fixed real bugs: `羅馬字`/`諺文` had been contaminated to 'yoglyou/욕룟 instead of cilyou/치룟, matching 治's and 療's own stored citations exactly (the `注音` field ㄑㄧㄌ⼄ㄨ was already correct, confirming only these two fields were affected). Removed blank `hsk_level`/`swadesh`. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄧㄌ⼄ㄨ unique across both words and characters).
