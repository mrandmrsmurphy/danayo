## Notes
- [食 (char)](../characters/食%20(char).md) "eat" + [欲](../characters/欲.md) "desire, want" — the desire to eat; appetite

食欲 is appetite: the desire to eat, felt as hunger and as the wish for a particular food (食欲がない, "to have no appetite"; 食欲旺盛, "a hearty appetite"; 食欲不振, "loss of appetite").  食 gives eating and 欲 the wanting, and 欲 reappears in 意欲 and 欲望 as the general word for desire.  The alias 食慾 is the traditional spelling, with 慾 for 欲.

Neither character is legitimized here: 食's `stand_in` is itself and 欲's is 欲望.  The dot in the 注音 ㄙㄧㄎ·⼄ㄎ is needed because ㄙㄧㄎ⼄ㄎ could also parse as ㄙㄧ + ㄎ⼄ㄎ.  Mandarin shíyù, Cantonese sik6 juk6, Japanese しょくよく and Korean 식욕 are the same compound, and `kwin` is true because the Dan'a'yo 식욕 equals the Korean 식욕.  No Vietnamese form is given, since none could be verified.
