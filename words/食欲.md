## Notes
- [食 (char)](../characters/食%20(char).md) "eat" + [欲](../characters/欲.md) "desire, want" — the desire to eat; appetite

Wiktionary: Japanese 食欲 (しょくよく) "appetite (desire of or relish for food)"; Chinese 食慾 "appetite (desire for food or drink)", kept as the traditional-form alias. Neither character is legitimized here: 食's `stand_in` is itself and 欲's is 欲望.
