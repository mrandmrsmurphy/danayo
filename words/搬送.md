## Notes
- [搬](../characters/搬.md) "convey" + [送](../characters/送%20(char).md) "give" — a synonym-doubling compound, "to transport; to convey." This word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [搬](../characters/搬.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (送's own stand-in is itself).

Mandarin bānsòng, Cantonese bun1 sung3, Japanese はんそう, Korean 반송, and Vietnamese ban tống are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄅㄚㄋㄙㄛㄫ unique across both words and characters).
