## Notes
Mandarin nóngcūn, cantonese nung4 cyun1, japanese のうそん, korean 농촌, and vietnamese nông thôn are all directly attested standard readings, matching both characters' own stored values exactly. `农村` is the standard simplified-character spelling variant (農→农), correctly listed as an alias. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 농촌 matches Sino-Korean 농촌 exactly. No homophones (注音 ㄋㄛㄫㄑㄛㄋ unique among both words and characters).

**Found and fixed a missing "(stand-in for 村)" annotation** on 村.md's own citation for this word — 村's own `stand_in` is this exact word (農村), so 村 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by 農村. 農's own `stand_in` is instead 農業, so this word does not legitimize 農.
