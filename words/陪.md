## Notes
- [陪](../characters/陪%20(char).md) "accompany, be with, keep company" — this word is the stand-in for its own character; 陪 stands alone as a word in Dan'a'yo.

陪 means to accompany, be with, or keep company — standing alongside someone, as its 形声 etymology (semantic 阜 "mound, hill" + phonetic 咅) literally depicts standing alongside a mound.

Mandarin péi, cantonese pui4, and korean 배 (bae) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Japanese has no native kun-reading (stored as ø on the character page), so the on-reading バイ (BAI) is used directly, rather than the alternate on-reading ハイ (HAI). Vietnamese bồi is the character's sole stored reading. A genuine three-way Dan'a'yo homophone group with [[背]] ("back") and [[杯]] ("cup, cupful") — all three coincide at boi/뵈/ㄅㄛㄧ, and in fact share the same Sino-Korean reading 배 as well.
