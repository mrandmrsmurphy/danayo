## Notes
- [翌 (char)](../characters/翌%20(char).md) "the following" + [世](../characters/世.md) "world, era, generation" + [紀](../characters/紀.md) "century" — none of the three characters' own `stand_in` points here (翌's is [[翌]] itself, 世's is [[世界]], 紀's is [[世紀]]), so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false` per the AND-rule: 世 is individually `kwin: false`.

翌世紀 means the following century.

Mandarin yìshìjì, Cantonese jik6 sai3 gei2, Japanese よくせいき (yokuseiki), and Korean 익세기 (ikseigi) are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese dực thế kỷ is the compositional Sino-Vietnamese reading. No Dan'a'yo homophones.
