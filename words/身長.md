## Notes
- [身](../characters/身.md) "body" + [長 (char)](../characters/長%20(char).md) "long; tall"

身長 is a person's height: how tall the body is. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin shēncháng as "height (of a person)", formal, and Japanese しんちょう as "height; stature". Korean 신장 and Cantonese san1 coeng4 match.

It goes with [体重](体重.md), weight. The word covers the standing body from head to foot, the [身体](身体.md).
