## Notes
- [満 (char)](characters/満%20(char).md) "full" + [足 (char)](characters/足%20(char).md) "foot, satisfied" — no stand-in relationship applies (both 満's and 足's own stand-ins are themselves).

満足 means satisfied, contented.

Mandarin mǎnzú, Cantonese mun5 zuk1, Japanese まんぞく, and Korean 만족 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 満's and 足's own stored citations exactly. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field with mãn túc (満's own mãn + 足's own túc).

**Real Dan'a'yo homophone with [[満族]]**: 足 and 族 happen to share the identical Dan'a'yo reading (jog/족/ㄐㄛㄎ), making 満足 and 満族 genuine homophones in Dan'a'yo, Mandarin, and Korean (though not Cantonese, where 足=zuk1 and 族=zuk6 differ). Reformatted the pre-existing informal tip callout into the canonical `Homophones` format on both pages.
