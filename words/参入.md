## Notes
- [参](../characters/参.md) "participate, join" + [入 (char)](../characters/入%20(char).md) "enter" — to enter and participate; to join a field or market

Wiktionary: Japanese 参入 (さんにゅう) "entering or breaking into (the market, a field, etc.); launching (a business); visiting a noble", and as a verb "to enter a market; break into a field; launch a venture; to visit a noble"; Chinese 參入 is literary, "to join; to take part in; to participate in".
