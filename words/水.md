## Notes
- [水 (char)](../characters/水%20(char).md) "water" — stand-in for [[水 (char)|水]], which cannot appear independently

水 is a 象形 pictograph of flowing water, one of the vault's most fundamental radicals — as a standalone character it depicts flowing streams directly, while beside another component it compresses into the ubiquitous three-dot form 氵 (a shape unrelated to the two-dot ice radical 冫, despite superficial visual similarity), and rarely survives in its full form 氺 in a handful of characters (泰, 录, 黎). Beyond the literal substance, 水 anchors both a huge concrete-noun compound family (湖水 "lake," 淡水 "fresh water," 大水 "flood," 水位 "water level") and figurative/technical extensions: 風水 (feng shui, "wind and water," the geomantic practice), 水平 ("level, lateral," from a level water surface), and 水星 ("Mercury," the planet — paralleling how [[金]]/[[火]]/[[木]] name the other visible planets through the Five Phases system this vault documents elsewhere).

Japanese carries a single on'yomi すい (SUI), used in nearly all compounds including technical and scientific vocabulary (水素 suiso "hydrogen," 水曜日 suiyōbi "Wednesday" — the weekday actually named directly after this element in the Five-Phases day-naming scheme), while the native 訓読み みず (mizu) remains the ordinary standalone word for "water" in everyday speech — mirroring the pattern already seen on [[日]]/[[月]]/[[火]] in this same sweep, where the Sino-Japanese layer dominates technical/compound contexts and the native layer holds the plain word. Vietnamese thuỷ likewise stays largely in the learned/technical register (thuỷ ngân "mercury," thuỷ tinh "crystal, glass") while everyday speech uses the native nước for "water" itself. `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 수 (su, from Middle Chinese 書母 ɕ + 脂B合韻 ɣiuɪ) coincides exactly with Sino-Korean 수, a straightforward convergence with no coda or vowel divergence to note, unlike several of the `kwin: false` cases earlier in this sweep. Within this vault's own periodic-table naming convention, 水 additionally serves as the abbreviation prefix for mercury via [[水銀]] ("silver water," itself a calque already attested across the CJKV sphere rather than a Dan'a'yo-only coinage like [[日素]]/[[月素]]).
