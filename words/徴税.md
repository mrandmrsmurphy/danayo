## Notes
- [徴](../characters/徴.md) "summon" + [税](../characters/税.md) "tax"
- This word is the viable form of [徴](../characters/徴.md): the character's meaning (to levy, collect) is identical, but 徴 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. 徴税 is its `stand_in` compound.

徴 originally meant to summon or call up (the sense in 徴兵 "conscription"), and extended to exacting what is owed to the state: taxes, labor, grain. 税 is a 形声 character with the grain radical 禾, since early taxes were paid in produce. 徴税 is the state's act of levying, in contrast to words like 納税 "to pay taxes," which describe the payer's side. (Note that 徴 is a different character from 微 "tiny," a common visual confusion.)

The reading is shared: Mandarin zhēngshuì, Cantonese zing1 seoi3, Japanese chōzei (ちょうぜい), Korean 징세. Dan'a'yo keeps the shared sense "collect taxes, levy taxes" with no divergence. It is placed primarily under [Government](../lexipedia/Government.md), beside 税金, and secondarily under [Containers](../lexipedia/Containers.md) in the "collecting" cluster.
