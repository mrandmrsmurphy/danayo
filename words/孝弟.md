## Etymology
- [孝 (char)](../characters/孝%20(char).md) "filial piety" + [弟 (char)](../characters/弟%20(char).md) "younger brother" (used here in its "brotherly deference" sense, as in 悌)

## Notes
- "Filial piety and fraternal duty" — the two foundational Confucian virtues of respecting one's parents and elder siblings, often written 孝悌 (with 悌, "brotherly respect," rather than bare 弟). **Real gap found and fixed on [[弟 (char)]]'s own page**: its `vietnamese` field was missing đễ, the classical reading needed for this compound — added, alongside the already-listed đệ (used in everyday words like huynh đệ). `vietnamese: hiếu đễ` is an attested classical Confucian Vietnamese term for this exact pairing. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 햣더 diverges from Sino-Korean 효제 in the second syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄏ⼘ㄨㄉㄝ unique among both words and characters).
