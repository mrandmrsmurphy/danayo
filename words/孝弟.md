## Etymology
- [孝 (char)](../characters/孝%20(char).md) "filial piety" + [弟 (char)](../characters/弟%20(char).md) "younger brother" (used here in its "brotherly deference" sense, as in 悌)

## Notes
- "Filial piety and fraternal duty" — the two foundational Confucian virtues of respecting one's parents and elder siblings, often written 孝悌 (with 悌, "brotherly respect," rather than bare 弟). `vietnamese: hiếu đễ` is an attested classical Confucian Vietnamese term for this exact pairing.
