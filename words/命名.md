## Notes
- [命](../characters/命.md) "life, fate" + [名](../characters/名%20(char).md) "name" — no stand-in relationship applies (命's own stand-in is [[運命]]; 名 is its own stand-in).

命名 means to name, dub, or christen — assigning a designation to someone or something.

Japanese めいめい, Korean 명명, and Vietnamese mệnh danh are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄇ⼶ㄫㄇㄧㄫ unique across both words and characters).
