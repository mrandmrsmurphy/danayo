## Notes
- [激](../characters/激.md) "incite, provoke" + [烈](../characters/烈.md) "fiery, violent, vehement, ardent, extreme" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 烈 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (激's own stand-in is [[刺激]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

激烈 means violent, severe, acute.

Mandarin jīliè, Cantonese gik1 lit6, Japanese げきれつ, Korean 격렬, and Vietnamese kích liệt are all standard, fully compositional readings (all match 激's and 烈's own stored citations exactly), and also the real, standard everyday words for "intense, fierce" in each language.
