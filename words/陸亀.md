## Notes
- [陸](characters/陸.md) "continent; land" + [亀 (char)](characters/亀%20(char).md) "turtle" — a land turtle, i.e. a tortoise.

Mandarin lùguī and Cantonese luk6 gwai1 preserve the sense directly. Japanese りくがめ (rikugame) is standard, everyday vocabulary. Korean 륙구 (yukgu, North Korean/문화어 form, avoiding the South Korean 두음법칙-shifted 육) parallels the Sino-xenic reading. Vietnamese lục quy combines lục (陸, "land, continent") with quy, the standard Hán Việt reading of 亀/龜 ("turtle," attested in the traditional Four Auspicious Beasts long ly quy phượng and linh quy 靈龜, "sacred turtle"). The character page's other candidate, qui, is simply an older/alternate spelling of quy (same pronunciation, a common qu- spelling inconsistency, like kỹ/kĩ); a further candidate, quân, is likely a genuine but semantically narrow secondary reading of 龜 tied to its separate "cracked, chapped" sense (龜裂, guījiè/jūnliè in Mandarin) rather than "turtle" — flagged as plausible but not independently confirmed this session, since search tooling was unavailable.

kwin is false: neither [[陸]] (char) nor [[亀]] (char) is individually kwin, so the AND-rule fails on both counts.
