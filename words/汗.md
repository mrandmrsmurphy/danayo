## Notes
- [汗](../characters/汗%20(char).md) "sweat" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

汗 means sweat — perspiration.

Mandarin hàn, Cantonese hon6, Japanese かん, Korean 한, and Vietnamese hãn are all standard, fully compositional readings. Shares its Dan'a'yo pronunciation (ㄏㄚㄋ/han/한) with the unrelated [[恨]] ("hate, grudge").
