## Notes
- [強](../characters/強%20(char).md) "strong, powerful" + [迫](../characters/迫.md) "force, press" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [迫](../characters/迫.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (強's own stand-in is itself).

強迫 means to force or compel — pressuring someone into an action against their will.

Mandarin qiǎngpò, Cantonese koeng5 bik1, Japanese きょうはく, Korean 강박, and Vietnamese cường bách are all standard, fully compositional readings.
