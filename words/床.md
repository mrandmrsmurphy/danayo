## Notes
- [床 (char)](../characters/床%20(char).md) "bed; couch; chassis; frame" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [床 (char)](../characters/床%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

床 names a bed, and by extension any bed-shaped frame or base structure (riverbed, machine chassis). The character is itself an unorthodox variant of the older 牀.

Japanese しょう (on'yomi; the native とこ/ゆか, toko/yuka, are the everyday words for "bed"/"floor" instead), Korean 상, and Vietnamese sàng (the character's own field also lists giường, the everyday native Vietnamese word for "bed" that historically derives from sàng via a s→gi sound shift — documented, not a false lead) are all standard readings. No homophones among other words (注音 ㄙ⼘ㄇ unique).
