## Notes
- [音](../characters/音.md) "sound" + [程](../characters/程.md) "extent, degree, amount" — no `#cranberry`: neither constituent's own `stand_in` points here (音's is [[音楽]], 程's is [[程度]]).

音程 (yīnchéng) is the standard Mandarin music-theory term for "musical interval," and Cantonese 音程 (jam1cing4) likewise. Japanese おんてい (ontei) and Korean 음정 (eumjeong) are both standard terms in their languages as well. Sino-Vietnamese âm trình is likewise real and attested, in an academic/music-theory register (paralleling quãng âm, a more common near-synonym).
