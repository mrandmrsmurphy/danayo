## Notes
- [眼](../characters/眼.md) "eyeball, pupil" + [前 (char)](../characters/前%20(char).md) "front, before, preceding" — literally "in front of the eye(s)."

眼前 means right before one's eyes, immediately present or apparent — used both literally (something visibly in front of you) and figuratively (an issue immediately at hand, a pressing present concern, as in 眼前の問題 "the problem at hand"). Vietnamese nhãn tiền carries a related figurative extension: "immediate, evident, plain to see" (as in a swift or self-evident consequence).

Mandarin yǎnqián, Cantonese ngaan5 cin4, and Japanese がんぜん (ganzen) are all fully compositional. Korean uses the native 눈앞 (nunap, literally "eye-front") rather than the mechanical Sino form 안전 — which would collide, as spoken, with the far more common unrelated word 안전 (安全, "safety"), making the native form the natural choice for clarity.
