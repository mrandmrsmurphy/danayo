## Notes
- [烏](../characters/烏.md) "crow, raven" + [龍 (char)](../characters/龍%20(char).md) "dragon" + [茶 (char)](../characters/茶%20(char).md) "tea" — no stand-in relationship applies (烏's own stand-in is [[烏鳥]]; 龍's and 茶's own are each themselves).

烏龍茶 means oolong tea.

Mandarin wūlóngchá is fully compositional. Japanese ウーロンちゃ is the real, standard mixed-script reading.
