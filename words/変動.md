## Notes
- [変 (char)](../characters/変%20(char).md) "to change" + [動 (char)](../characters/動%20(char).md) "to move" — change, fluctuation, or (in political/social contexts) upheaval, as in 変動構造 ("changing structures").

The Existence page separates the change words by manner. [変化](変化.md) is the neutral, general-purpose "change"; 変動 implies ongoing fluctuation or instability and not a single discrete change; and 転化 implies converting into something categorically different. The page also pairs 変動 with [起伏](起伏.md) "to undulate, fluctuate," a near-synonym leaning more figurative ("the ups and downs"), and notes the bound root [変](変.md) behind 変化, 変動 and [変成](変成.md). Dan'a'yo keeps the sense "change, fluctuation, upheaval," with the ongoing, unstable movement as its characteristic. The Geography page uses it in the phrase 変動構造, "changing structures." The alias 變動 is the traditional spelling, and the readings are Mandarin biàndòng, Cantonese bin3 dung6, Japanese hendō (へんどう), Korean 변동 and Sino-Vietnamese biến động.
