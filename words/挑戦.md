## Notes
- [挑](../characters/挑.md) "challenge authority" + [戦](../characters/戦.md) "war, battle" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [挑](../characters/挑.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (戦's own stand-in is [[戦争]]).

挑戦 means to challenge.

Mandarin tiǎozhàn, Cantonese tiu1 zin3, Japanese ちょうせん, Korean 도전, and Vietnamese khiêu chiến are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄚㄨㄐㄝㄋ unique across both words and characters).
