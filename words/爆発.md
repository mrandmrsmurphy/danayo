## Notes
- [爆](../characters/爆.md) "explode" + [発 (char)](../characters/発%20(char).md) "issue forth"
- This word is the viable form of [爆](../characters/爆.md): the character's meaning (to explode) is identical, but 爆 alone is too bounded to stand as a Dan'a'yo word. The compound adds 発 ("to burst forth"), making the outward force of the explosion explicit.

The senses differ across the sphere. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin bàofā (爆发, 爆發) as a verb for a volcano to erupt, and for an emotional display or violent event to burst out or flare up, with derived terms 爆發力 (explosive power), 火山爆發 (volcanic eruption) and 爆發音 (plosive). Japanese ばくはつ and Korean 폭발 are recorded there as descendants, and are the usual words for a physical explosion or blast. Cantonese baau3 faat3 matches Wiktionary.

In Dan'a'yo the word covers both uses: a physical explosion, as placed on the [War](../lexipedia/War.md) page as a blast and on [Light](../lexipedia/Light.md), and the figurative bursting out of a force or emotion.
