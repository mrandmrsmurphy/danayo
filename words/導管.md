## Notes
- [導](../characters/導.md) "guide; lead" + [管](../characters/管.md) "pipe; tube" — this word is the stand-in for [管](../characters/管.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

導管 names a pipe, tube, or duct that conducts something — 導's "to guide, lead, conduct" sense applied concretely to 管's "pipe, tube," covering both engineering conduits and biological ducts alike (as in the anatomical term for a gland's excretory duct).

Japanese どうかん and Korean 도관 are both standard, non-drifted readings. **`vietnamese` is left omitted**: no standing everyday Vietnamese term "đạo quản" was confirmed in search — modern Vietnamese defaults to the native compound ống dẫn ("conducting tube") for this concept instead. No HSK level is given for the compound. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄨㄍ⺢ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
