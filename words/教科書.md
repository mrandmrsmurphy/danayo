## Notes
- [教](../characters/教.md) "teach" + [科](../characters/科.md) "branch, section" + [書](../characters/書.md) "book" — "book for a teaching subject." None of the three is a stand-in here: 教 is legitimized by [[教授]], 科 by [[学科]] and 書 by [[書本]], and this compound simply uses all three as bound morphemes.

Wiktionary: Chinese 教科書 "textbook"; Japanese 教科書 (きょうかしょ) "a textbook"; no Korean or Vietnamese entry. Related: [[教材]], [[学科]], [[書籍]], [[書本]]. Used in `文法 - 03文字法`.
