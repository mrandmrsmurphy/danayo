## Notes
- [微](../characters/微.md) "slight" + [笑 (char)](../characters/笑%20(char).md) "laugh; smile"

微笑 is a smile: a slight, quiet laugh that shows pleasure without sound. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin wēixiào and Japanese びしょう as "smile; to smile". Korean 미소 and Cantonese mei4 siu3 match.

It is the quiet cousin of the [笑](笑.md), laugh, and it shows [愉快](愉快.md), pleasure. The smiling face itself is the [笑顔](笑顔.md).
