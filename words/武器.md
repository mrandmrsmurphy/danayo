## Notes
- [武 (char)](../characters/武%20(char).md) "martial" + [器](../characters/器.md) "tool; vessel"

武器 is a weapon: any instrument used in fighting. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin wǔqì as "weapon; arms" and, figuratively, "a means of gaining an advantage", and Japanese ぶき as "weapon, arms". Vietnamese vũ khí is the same word, and Korean 무기 and Cantonese mou5 hei3 match.

It is the general word, where [凶器](凶器.md) is the weapon used in a crime and the [剣](剣.md) and [槍](槍.md) are particular weapons.
