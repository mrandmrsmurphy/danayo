- [通 (char)](../characters/通%20(char).md) "pass through, communicate" + [行 (char)](../characters/行%20(char).md) "attend, go to" + [証](../characters/証.md) "prove, confirm, verify"

## Notes
Mandarin tōngxíngzhèng, cantonese tung1 hang4 zing3, japanese つうこうしょう, and korean 통행증 are all directly attested standard readings. Vietnamese thông hành chứng is the honest compositional reading (通's own thông + 行's own hành + 証's own chứng); no independently attested Vietnamese loanword for this compound was found. `通行证`/`通行證` are the standard simplified/traditional spelling variants (証→证/證), correctly listed as aliases. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 통항징 diverges from Sino-Korean 통행증 in the third syllable. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄛㄫㄏㄚㄫㄐㄧㄫ unique among both words and characters).
