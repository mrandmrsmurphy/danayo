## Notes
- [超 (char)](../characters/超%20(char).md) "transcend, surpass" + [越 (char)](../characters/越%20(char).md) "exceed" — "to surpass, transcend" (超越性, "transcendence"). Mandarin chāoyuè, Cantonese ciu1 jyut6, Korean 초월, and Vietnamese siêu việt are all directly attested standard readings.

**Real content bug found and fixed**: `japanese` had been stored as てうゑつ, an archaic-looking kana rendering not matching either constituent character's own stored on'yomi (超's CHOU, 越's ETSU) or any attested modern reading — corrected to the genuine standard reading ちょうえつ (chōetsu). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 탓웓 diverges from Sino-Korean 초월 entirely. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄚㄨ⼔ㄊ unique among both words and characters).
