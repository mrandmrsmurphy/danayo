## Notes
- [富](../characters/富.md) "abundance" + [国](../characters/国.md) "nation, state" — no stand-in relationship applies (富's own is [[豊富]]; 国's own is [[国家]]).

富国 (Phú Quốc, "Phu Quoc") is the largest island of Vietnam, in the Gulf of Thailand off the Cambodian coast, formerly part of Kiên Giang province (from memory, not re-checked). The island is also called 富国島 in full, the compound being listed as a derived term at English Wiktionary's 富國 entry. The Sino-Vietnamese name means "rich country", a descriptive or auspicious name (from memory, not re-checked); it has a well-known pearl, fish-sauce and tourism economy (from memory, not re-checked).

**Homograph.** 富国 is also an ordinary Chinese, Japanese and Korean word meaning "a rich country; to enrich the state", the first half of 富国強兵 "enrich the country, strengthen the army", the slogan of the Meiji period (Japanese fukoku kyōhei) and an older Chinese statecraft phrase. English Wiktionary lists that sense at the same entry (noun "rich country", verb "to make one's country rich") with the same readings Mandarin fùguó, Cantonese fu3 gwok3, Japanese ふこく, Korean 부국, Vietnamese phú quốc. No separate word for the common-noun sense exists in the vault at the time of writing (there is no 富国強兵 page either, though [[強国]] exists), so this page is currently the only 富国; the "rich country" sense is recorded here and may later need splitting into its own page or numbered sense.

Attestation, checked 2026-10-11: English Wiktionary has 富國 (Chinese, with Mandarin, Cantonese and Min Nan readings, and a Vietnamese Han-form entry with the proper noun Phú Quốc); Chinese Wiktionary has a 富國 stub with Mandarin fùguó; Korean Wiktionary has 富國 as the hanja form of 부국. Japanese and Vietnamese Wiktionaries have no entry. The Korean 부국 and Japanese ふこく are the readings of the common-noun sense; neither language uses 富国 for the island, which Japanese writes フークォック and Korean 푸꾸옥 (from memory, not re-checked). No homophones (注音 ㄈㄨㄛㄍㄛㄎ unique across both words and characters).
