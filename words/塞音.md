## Notes
- [塞](../characters/塞.md) "block, stop up" (stand-in: [[梗塞]]) + [音 (char)](../characters/音.md) "sound" (stand-in: [[音楽]]) — a stop consonant, plosive.

塞音 is a stop consonant, a plosive. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 塞音 (Mandarin sèyīn or sāiyīn, Cantonese sak1 jam1) as the phonetics term "stop consonant; plosive consonant". The Japanese そくおん, Korean 색음 and Vietnamese tắc âm fields are built from the readings of the two characters. The consonant table in [文法 - 02音韻論](../grammar/文法%20-%2002音韻論.md) uses 有声塞音 and 送気塞音. Compare [破裂音](破裂音.md), the plosive, and [擦音](擦音.md), the fricative.
