## Notes
- [塞](../words/塞.md) "block, stop up" (stand-in: [[梗塞]]) + [音 (char)](../characters/音.md) "sound" (stand-in: [[音楽]]) — a stop consonant, plosive.

塞音 means a stop/plosive consonant — a sound made by fully blocking airflow. Mandarin sāiyīn and Japanese そくおん are both real, standard phonetics terms, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Cantonese sak1 jam1 is a straightforward compositional reading. Korean 색음 and Vietnamese tắc âm are compositional only — Korean phonetics more commonly says 파열음, and Vietnamese says "âm tắc" (reversed order), so neither is independently verified as the standard term in its language. `kwin` is false: both 塞 and 音 are individually false.

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot**: the concatenated string `ㄙㄚㄧㄨㄇ` admits two legal partitions — the intended 塞(ㄙㄚㄧ)+音(ㄨㄇ), and a false alternate splitting 塞's own syllable (ㄙㄚ+ㄧ) — so the dot at the true 塞|音 boundary is required: ㄙㄚㄧ·ㄨㄇ.
