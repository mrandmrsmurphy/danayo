## Notes
- [神](../characters/神%20(char).md) "god, divine" + [学](../characters/学.md) "study" — "study of God," theology. Their own `stand_in` values: 神's is itself; 学's is [[学習]]; none points here, so 神学 is an independent compound.

神学 is theology: the study of God and of religious doctrine. Mandarin shénxué (神學), Cantonese san4 hok6, Japanese しんがく and Korean 신학 all use it, and Vietnamese thần học does too. Compare [[教義]] (doctrine) and [[経典]] (scripture).
