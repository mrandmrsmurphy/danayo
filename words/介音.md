## Notes
- [介 (char)](../characters/介.md) "mediate, intervene" (stand-in: [仲介](仲介.md)) + [音 (char)](../characters/音.md) "sound" (stand-in: [音楽](音楽.md)) — the glide of a syllable; its slot is the [介位](介位.md).

介音 is the glide, the medial sound of a syllable. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 介音 (Mandarin jièyīn, Cantonese gaai3 jam1), marked phonetics, as "glide; semivowel", and Japanese かいおん, marked phonetics, as "medial". The Korean 개음 and Vietnamese giới âm fields are built from the readings of the two characters. In [文法 - 02音韻論](../grammar/文法%20-%2002音韻論.md) the 単亜語 glide is the optional y or w after the onset.
