## Notes
- [介 (char)](../characters/介.md) "mediate, intervene" (stand-in: [[仲介]]) + [音 (char)](../characters/音.md) "sound" (stand-in: [[音楽]]) — the on-glide/medial of a syllable, as in `文法 - 02音韻論`'s [[介位]] (the glide position within a 韻母).

介音 means glide/medial sound. Mandarin jièyīn and Korean 개음 are both real, standard phonetics terms, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Japanese かいおん and Cantonese gaai3 jam1 are straightforward compositional readings. Vietnamese giới âm is compositional only. `kwin` is false: both 介 and 音 are individually false.
