## Notes
- [滅 (char)](../characters/滅%20(char).md) "wipe out" + [亡](../characters/亡.md) "perish"

滅亡 is to perish or be destroyed: to die out as a people, a state or a line. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin mièwáng as "to be destroyed; to become extinct; to die out" and, transitively, "to destroy; to annihilate", and Japanese めつぼう as "extinction; to die, to perish". Vietnamese diệt vong is the same word, and Korean 멸망 and Cantonese mit6 mong4 match.

Its scale is that of a nation or a species. It is stronger than [消滅](消滅.md), to die out, and of a lesser kind than [絶滅](絶滅.md), which is the complete extinction of a species. The older word for survival and ruin together is [存亡](存亡.md).
