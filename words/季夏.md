## Notes
- [季](../characters/季.md) "season, youngest" + [夏](../characters/夏%20(char).md) "summer" — no stand-in relationship applies (季's own stand-in is [[季節]]; 夏's own is itself).

Third and final month of summer. The lotus blooms; the heat is heaviest. Corresponds to [[未月]] and [[荷月]].

Mandarin jìxià, Cantonese gwai3 haa6, Korean 계하, Japanese きか, and Vietnamese Quý Hạ are all standard, fully compositional readings.
