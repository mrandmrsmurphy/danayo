## Notes
- [木 (char)](../characters/木%20(char).md) "wood" + [曜](../characters/曜.md) "day of the week" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — literally "wood-day," Thursday, following the Sino-Japanese seven-day planetary-week naming shared across the sphere.

Japanese もくようび (mokuyōbi) and Korean 목요일 are both standard, fully compositional readings, matching 木's, 曜's, and 日's own stored citations exactly. **Found and fixed two real bugs**: `mandarin` had been stored as mùyàorì, a compositional reading of 木曜日's own characters rather than the real Mandarin word for Thursday — corrected to xīngqīsì (星期四, already recorded in this word's own `aliases`), matching the real 星期 system used by every other weekday in this corpus. `cantonese` was missing entirely — added sing1 kei4 sei3, the same real system. Vietnamese thứ năm ("day five") is likewise the real native word, using Vietnamese's own weekday-numbering convention.

`kwin` is false: 木 alone is kwin-true, but 曜 independently diverges from Korean on its own character page, which alone is enough to make the AND-rule false. No homophones (注音 ㄇㄛㄎ·⼄ㄨㄋㄧㄊ unique among both words and characters).
