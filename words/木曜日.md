## Notes
- [木 (char)](../characters/木%20(char).md) "wood" + [曜](../characters/曜.md) "day of the week" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — literally "wood-day," Thursday, following the Sino-Japanese seven-day planetary-week naming shared across the sphere.

Japanese もくようび (mokuyōbi) and Korean 목요일 are both standard, fully compositional readings, matching 木's, 曜's, and 日's own stored citations exactly. Mandarin xīngqīsì and Cantonese sing1 kei4 sei3 use the real 星期 ("week") system rather than a compositional reading of 木曜日's own characters (星期四 is recorded in `aliases`), the same as every other weekday. Vietnamese thứ năm ("day five") is likewise the real native word, using Vietnamese's own weekday-numbering convention.
