## Notes
- [安](../characters/安.md) "peaceful" + [徽](../characters/徽.md) "badge, insignia; fine" — a blend of the first characters of two prefectures, Anqing and Huizhou. No stand-in relationship applies (安's own is [[平安]]; 徽's own is [[徽章]]).

安徽 (Anhui) is a province of eastern China, on the lower Yangtze and Huai rivers; its capital is [[合肥]] (from memory, not re-checked). English Wiktionary says the name was coined in 1667, in the Qing dynasty, as a portmanteau of two major prefectures: 安慶 (Anqing), itself a blend of older names, and 徽州 (Huizhou), named after the Hui River and Hui Ridge.

The Japanese field holds あんき, the Sino-Japanese reading that English Wiktionary lists for the province; the usual spoken name today is Mandarin-based アンホイ (from memory, not re-checked). The Korean field holds the Sino-Korean 안휘 (Wiktionary Korean: hanja form of 안휘); the usual South Korean name today is 안후이 (from memory, not re-checked). Vietnamese An Huy is the Sino-Vietnamese name and is given by English and Chinese Wiktionary. Cantonese on1 fai1 is the Jyutping on English Wiktionary.

Attestation, checked 2026-10-11: English and Chinese Wiktionary (Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese sections) list only the province for this name. No homophones (注音 ㄚㄋㄏㄨㄧ unique across both words and characters).
