## Notes
- [斧](../characters/斧.md) "axe" + [子](../characters/子.md) "nominalizing suffix" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [斧](../characters/斧.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (子's own stand-in is [[児子]]).

斧子 means axe or hatchet.

Mandarin fǔzi, Cantonese fu2 zi2, Japanese ふし, Korean 부자, and Vietnamese phủ tử are all standard, fully compositional readings. No homophones (注音 ㄈㄨㄐㄜ unique across both words and characters).
