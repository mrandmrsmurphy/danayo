## Notes
- [大 (char)](../characters/大%20(char).md) "big" + [夫 (char)](../characters/夫%20(char).md) "man" — "official, senior official/grand master" (a classical bureaucratic rank). In modern colloquial Mandarin, 大夫 (dàifu) has further shifted to mean "physician, doctor," distinct from this word's classical "official" sense. `vietnamese: đại phu` is compositional with the same "senior official" sense; modern Vietnamese medical usage instead borrows the term differently (bác sĩ, native, for "doctor"). `kwin` is true: Dan'a'yo 대부 matches Sino-Korean 대부 exactly. Homophone with [[大釜]] (注音 ㄉㄚㄧㄅㄨ), both pages now cross-linking with proper `>[!warning]` callouts.
