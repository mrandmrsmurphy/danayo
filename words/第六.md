## Notes
- [第 (char)](../characters/第%20(char).md) "-th" + [六 (char)](../characters/六%20(char).md) "six" — neither character's own `stand_in` points here, so `#cranberry` does not apply. `kwin: false`, as for [[第三]].

第六 means "sixth," the ordinal formed by prefixing 第 to 六, in the same pattern as [[第一]], [[第二]] and [[第三]].

Mandarin dì-liù, Cantonese dai6 luk6, Japanese だいろく and Korean 제륙 are all standard, fully compositional readings; Vietnamese đệ lục is likewise compositional.
