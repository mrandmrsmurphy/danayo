## Notes
- [判](../characters/判.md) "judge, decide, determine" + [別 (char)](../characters/別%20(char).md) "distinct, other" + [式](../characters/式.md) "formula" — "discriminant" (the mathematical formula that determines the nature of a polynomial's roots). Mandarin pànbiéshì, Cantonese pun3 bit6 sik1, Japanese はんべつしき, and Korean 판별식 are all directly attested standard readings. `vietnamese: biệt thức` is the standard Vietnamese mathematical term, genuinely shortened from the full compound rather than missing a syllable by error. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 판벋식 diverges from Sino-Korean 판별식 in the second syllable's coda. No homophones (注音 ㄆㄚㄋㄅㄝㄊㄙㄧㄎ unique among both words and characters).
