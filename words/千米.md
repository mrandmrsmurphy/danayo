## Notes
- [千](../characters/千.md) "thousand" + [米](../characters/米%20(char).md) "meter (metric unit, borrowed sense)" — no stand-in relationship applies (千's own stand-in is [[一千]]; 米 is its own stand-in).

千米, literally "thousand meters," is the standard Mandarin word for kilometer (a synonym of 公里, recorded as an alias). Since this is a modern metric-system coinage, Japanese キロメートル and Korean 킬로미터 both use the international phonetic loanword "kilometer" rather than a compositional Sino-reading, and Vietnamese cây số is the everyday colloquial term for kilometer — all genuine, expected divergences for this kind of measurement term, not errors. No homophones (注音 ㄑㄝㄋㄇㄝㄧ unique across both words and characters).
