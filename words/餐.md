## Notes
- [餐 (char)](../characters/餐%20(char).md) "eat, dine" — this word is 餐's own legitimizing compound (餐's `stand_in` points here).

餐 means "to eat, to dine" and, as a noun, "a meal." Mandarin cān, Cantonese caan1, Korean 찬 (chan), and Sino-Vietnamese xan all preserve the Sino reading directly. Japanese さん (san) survives mainly bound in compounds (晩餐, 午餐); the everyday native verb is たべる.
