## Notes
- [積](../characters/積.md) "accumulate, product" + [分 (char)](../characters/分%20(char).md) "divide, portion" — literally "accumulated portions," integration: summing infinitesimally small portions to find a total area, volume, or accumulated quantity, the inverse operation of [[微分]].

Mandarin jīfēn, Cantonese zik1 fan1, Japanese せきぶん, Korean 적분, and Vietnamese tích phân are all standard, fully compositional, and genuinely living calculus terms across the region — no register split with a competing native form.
