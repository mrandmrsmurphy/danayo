## Notes
- [沈](../characters/沈.md) "sink" + [黙](../characters/黙.md) "silent, quiet, still, dark, reticent" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character 黙 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (沈's own stand-in is [[沈没]], so no #cranberry: only one side of the pair is a bound character).

沈黙 means silent, reticent.

Mandarin chénmò, Japanese ちんもく, and Korean 침묵 are all standard, fully compositional readings, matching 沈's and 黙's own stored citations.
