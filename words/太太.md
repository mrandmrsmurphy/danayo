## Notes
- [太](../characters/太%20(char).md) "great, extreme" doubled — an intensifying ideophone, not a stand-in relationship (太's own stand-in is itself).

太太 means "really great" or "super-duper" — a Dan'a'yo-internal reduplicative ideophone built by doubling 太 ("extreme, excessive"), distinct in sense from the real Mandarin word 太太 ("Mrs.; wife"), which happens to share the same characters and pronunciation.

Mandarin tàitai and Korean 태태 are the direct compositional readings of the doubled character (coincidentally identical in form to the real Mandarin word for "wife," but read here for its literal ideophone sense). Cantonese taai3 taai3-2 reflects the real tone-sandhi pattern of the coincidental Cantonese homograph. Japanese ふてぶて is drawn from the real Japanese mimetic root ふてぶて(しい) ("brazen, audacious"), the closest phonetic match to 太's native root ふと available as a genuine ideophone. Removed the redundant `品詞` field. No homophones (注音 ㄊㄚㄧㄊㄚㄧ unique across both words and characters).
