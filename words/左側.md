## Notes
- [左](../characters/左.md) "left" + [側](../characters/側.md) "side; aspect" — stand-in for [[左]], which cannot appear independently

左側 means the left side: the side of a thing or person on the left hand (左側通行, "keep to the left, left-hand traffic"; 左側面, "left flank"; 道路左側, "the left of the road"). Its counterpart is [[右側]].

Mandarin zuǒcè and Cantonese zo2 zak1 are the standard word for "the left side". Japanese uses the native ひだりがわ (hidarigawa, mirroring 右側's みぎがわ) for the everyday word, with the on'yomi さそく (sasoku) recorded here for the formal register. Korean 좌측 (jwacheuk) is the Sino-Korean reading, directly paralleling 右側's 우측, and the native 왼쪽 is its everyday equivalent. Vietnamese tả trắc is the compositional reading (左=tả, as in tả hữu "left and right"; 側=trắc, as in trắc diện "side face"); the everyday native word for "left" is simply trái.
