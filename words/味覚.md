## Etymology
- [味](../characters/味.md) "taste, experience" + [覚](../characters/覚.md) "perceive" — one of the 五覚 (Five Senses: 視覚 sight, 聴覚 hearing, 嗅覚 smell, 味覚 taste, 触覚 touch).

## Notes
- **Gloss corrected**: `english` previously read "taste, smell" — 味覚 specifically means "sense of taste," distinct from 嗅覚 "sense of smell"; narrowed to "sense of taste." Also corrected the Etymology bullet's gloss for 覚 from "wake" (a homograph-adjacent but wrong sense) to its actual meaning "perceive," matching this character's own `english` field. **Content added**: `vietnamese: vị giác`, a directly attested standard Sino-Vietnamese term (part of the same five-senses set: thị giác, thính giác, khứu giác, vị giác, xúc giác). **Real content bug found and fixed on [[覚]]'s own page**: its `vietnamese` field crammed four readings into one malformed list item — split into separate entries. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 뮈각 diverges from Sino-Korean 미각 in the first syllable.
