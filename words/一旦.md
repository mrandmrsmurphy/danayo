## Notes
- [一 (char)](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" + [旦](../characters/旦.md) "dawn, daybreak" — literally "one dawn," hence "once, in a single day," extended to "once [something happens], as soon as."

一旦 covers a real semantic range from the literal ("in a single day/morning," emphasizing swiftness — e.g. a dynasty collapsing 一旦, "in one day," after centuries) to the conditional-conjunctive ("once, as soon as [X happens]," introducing a hypothetical trigger) to, in modern Vietnamese specifically, an adverb meaning "suddenly, unexpectedly." Vietnamese nhất đán (confirmed via web search) is a genuinely attested classical/literary Hán Việt term carrying all three of these senses, not a mechanical concatenation — 一's own component gloss "if, once, as soon as" (hễ, một khi) independently reinforces the conditional sense on its own.
