## Notes
- [一 (char)](../characters/一%20(char).md) "one" + [旦](../characters/旦.md) "dawn, daybreak" — literally "one dawn," hence "once, in a single day," extended to "once [something happens], as soon as."

**Structural bug found and fixed**: the stray body line before the `meta-bind-embed` block ("once, on one day, in one day") duplicated the frontmatter's own `english` gloss and violated the meta-bind-embed-must-come-first rule — removed. **Content bug found and fixed**: `korean` held a reading-plus-gloss mix, "일단, 먼저 한번" ("first time," a Korean-language explanation appended after the actual reading) — trimmed to the clean pronunciation 일단, the compositional concatenation of 一's own 일 + 旦's own 단.

一旦 covers a real semantic range from the literal ("in a single day/morning," emphasizing swiftness — e.g. a dynasty collapsing 一旦, "in one day," after centuries) to the conditional-conjunctive ("once, as soon as [X happens]," introducing a hypothetical trigger) to, in modern Vietnamese specifically, an adverb meaning "suddenly, unexpectedly." Vietnamese nhất đán (confirmed via web search) is a genuinely attested classical/literary Hán Việt term carrying all three of these senses, not a mechanical concatenation — 一's own component gloss "if, once, as soon as" (hễ, một khi) independently reinforces the conditional sense on its own. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 읻단 and Sino-Korean 일단 diverge in the first syllable's coda (읻 vs 일) — the same [[一 (char)]] root-cause pattern already fixed on [[一定]]/[[一斉]] earlier in this stretch of the sweep. No homophones (注音 ㄧㄊㄉㄚㄋ unique among both words and characters).
