## Notes
- [飲 (char)](../characters/飲%20(char).md) "drink" + [酒](../characters/酒.md) "liquor"

飲酒 is drinking alcohol: to drink wine, beer or spirits. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin yǐnjiǔ as "to drink alcohol" and Japanese いんしゅ as "drinking alcohol; to drink". Korean 음주 and Cantonese jam2 zau2 match.

It is the act of drinking [酒精](酒精.md), alcohol, which in the Dan'a'yo sense includes the drinks, and its excess is the [暴飲](暴飲.md), binge drinking. Its result is being [酔](酔.md), drunk.
