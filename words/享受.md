## Notes
- [享](../characters/享.md) "to enjoy, receive" + [受](../characters/受.md) "to accept, receive" — "to enjoy, relish," from the sense of receiving and taking pleasure in something. hưởng thụ is standard everyday Vietnamese for this sense (as in hưởng thụ cuộc sống, "enjoy life"), a direct compositional match; japanese きょうじゅ is likewise compositional (each character's own on'yomi). `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 향슈 diverges from Sino-Korean 향수 via the AND-rule, since 受's own field independently diverges (despite 享 itself being `kwin: true`). No homophones (注音 ㄏ⼘ㄫㄙ⼜ unique among both words and characters). Legitimizing note: 享's own `stand_in` is this exact compound, so 享 is legitimized as an independent Dan'a'yo entry specifically by this word; 受's own `stand_in` is [[接受]], not this word, so transitivity fails and `#cranberry` does not apply.
