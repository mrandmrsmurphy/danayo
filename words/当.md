## Notes
- [当](../characters/当%20(char).md) "while, current" — this word is the stand-in for its own character.

当 means while or current — used to indicate the present or a specific point in time, as in 当時 ("at that time") and 当日 ("the day itself").

Mandarin dāng, Cantonese dong1, Japanese とう, Korean 당, and Vietnamese đáng are all standard, fully compositional readings.
