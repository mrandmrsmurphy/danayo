## Notes
- [揺](../characters/揺%20(char).md) "shake, sway" + [籃](../characters/籃.md) "basket, cradle" — "swaying basket," a cradle. Their own `stand_in` values: 揺's is itself; 籃's is [[名専字]]; none points here, so 揺籃 is an independent compound.

揺籃 is a cradle: a baby's rocking bed, and figuratively the place where something begins ("the cradle of civilization"). Mandarin yáolán (搖籃), Cantonese jiu4 laam4, Japanese ようらん and Korean 요람 all use it. It is the cradle character 揺 "to shake" and 籃 "basket," and needs no new character.
