## Notes
- [震](../characters/震.md) "shake, quake" + [動](../characters/動%20(char).md) "move" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [震](../characters/震.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (動's own stand-in is itself).

震動 means to vibrate or shake.

Mandarin zhèndòng, Cantonese zan3 dung6, Japanese しんどう, Korean 진동, and Vietnamese chấn động are all standard, fully compositional readings. Genuine three-way coincidence: 震 (zhèn) and 振 (zhèn) are homophones in real Mandarin as well as Dan'a'yo, so [[振動]] and 震動 are homophones at every level (both 注音 ㄐㄧㄋㄉㄛㄫ) — cross-referenced reciprocally.
