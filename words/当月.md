## Notes
- [当](../characters/当%20(char).md) "while" + [月](../characters/月%20(char).md) "month" — no stand-in relationship applies (当/月 are both their own stand-ins).

当月 means the current month.

Mandarin dāngyuè, Cantonese dong1 jyut6, Japanese とうげつ, Korean 당월, and Vietnamese đương nguyệt are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Fixed**: `kwin` was wrongly true — 諺文 당웓 does not equal `korean` 당월. Filled blank `vietnamese`. No homophones (注音 ㄉㄚㄫ⼔ㄊ unique across both words and characters).
