## Notes
- [離](../characters/離.md) "depart, leave" + [婚](../characters/婚.md) "marry"

離婚 means divorce — the legal dissolution of a marriage. Mandarin 離婚/离婚 (líhūn), Cantonese 離婚 (lei4fan1), Japanese 離婚 (りこん), Korean 리혼 (rihon — this vault's standing North Korean/문화어 pronunciation policy; South Korean orthography would instead give 이혼 via 두음법칙, the same shift documented on [[離別]]), and Sino-Vietnamese ly hôn all preserve this everyday, high-frequency legal/social term across the CJKV sphere.
