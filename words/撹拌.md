## Notes
- [撹](../characters/撹.md) "stir up, agitate" + [拌](../characters/拌.md) "mix, blend" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [撹](../characters/撹.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

撹拌 means to stir up or agitate.

Mandarin jiǎobàn, Cantonese gaau2 bun6, Japanese かくはん, Korean 교반, and Vietnamese cảo bạn are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed real bug: `羅馬字` used the old minority reading "gyau" instead of the majority-established "gyou" (already correctly reflected in 諺文/注音). No homophones (注音 ㄍ⼄ㄨㄅㄚㄋ unique across both words and characters).
