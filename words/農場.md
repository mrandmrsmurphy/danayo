## Notes
- [農](../characters/農.md) "farming" + [場](../characters/場.md) "field, place" — "farming place." 農's own `stand_in` is [[農業]] and 場's is [[市場]]; neither points here, so 農場 is an independent compound.

農場 is a farm: land worked for crops or livestock, together with its buildings. Mandarin nóngchǎng, Cantonese nung4 coeng4, Japanese のうじょう, Korean 농장 and Vietnamese nông trường all use these characters. In Vietnamese the word often names a large state or collective farm. A pasture for animals is [[牧場]].
