## Notes
- [学](../characters/学.md) "study" + [者](../characters/者%20(char).md) "person, one who" — no stand-in relationship applies (学's own stand-in is [[学習]]; 者's own is itself).

学者 means scholar — one who studies, an academic or learned person.

Mandarin xuézhě, Cantonese hok6 ze2, Japanese がくしゃ, Korean 학자, and Vietnamese học giả are all standard, fully compositional readings. **Real bug found and fixed**: `羅馬字` had 学's coda mistyped k→g (hakca→hagca, matching the file's own correct 諺文/注音). Trimmed a trailing space from `vietnamese`. Removed redundant `品詞`. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄚㄎㄑㄚ unique across both words and characters).
