## Notes
- [隊 (char)](../characters/隊%20(char).md) "unit" + [長 (char)](../characters/長%20(char).md) "long"

隊長 is a captain, the leader of a unit. English Wiktionary glosses Chinese 隊長 (Mandarin duìzhǎng, Cantonese deoi6 zoeng2) as "captain; team leader" and Japanese たいちょう as "leader, commander, captain". It lists Korean 대장 and Vietnamese entries.
