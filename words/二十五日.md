## Notes
- [[二十]] "twenty" + [五 (char)](../characters/五%20(char).md) "five" + [日 (char)](../characters/日%20(char).md) "day" — the twenty-fifth day of the month.

Mandarin èrshíwǔ rì, Cantonese ji6 sap6 ng5 jat6, Japanese にじゅうごにち, and Korean 이십오일 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Vietnamese filled with ngày hai mươi lăm (native "day twenty-five" — lăm rather than năm, the irregular final-digit form Vietnamese uses for 5 in compound numerals from 15 onward), matching the same native-calendar-date convention already established on the rest of this [[二十一日|二十N日]] cluster.

`kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 늬십오닏 diverges from Sino-Korean 이십오일 — 五's own kwin is true, but 二/十/日 are each independently false, so the compound is false overall.

**Found and fixed missing citations**: same gap as the rest of this cluster — this word was entirely absent from `二(char).md`, `十(char).md`, and `五(char).md`'s own Words sections (only `日(char).md` had it) — added to all three.
