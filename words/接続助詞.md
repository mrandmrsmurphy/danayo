## Notes
- [接](../characters/接%20(char).md) "contact, connect" + [続](../characters/続.md) "continue" + [助](../characters/助.md) "assist, help" + [詞](../characters/詞.md) "word, expression" — no stand-in relationship applies (all four characters' own stand-ins point elsewhere).

接続助詞 means conjunctive particle — a grammatical term for particles that link clauses (as in Japanese grammar).

Japanese せつぞくじょし and Korean 접속조사 (both established linguistics terms in their respective grammatical traditions) are standard, fully compositional readings. `mandarin`/`cantonese`/`vietnamese` are deliberately left blank: Chinese and Vietnamese grammatical traditions don't categorize this class of particle under a directly corresponding term — functionally synonymous with [[連接詞]] in a more general sense. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄛㄆㄙ⼄ㄎㄐㄛㄙㄚ unique across both words and characters).
