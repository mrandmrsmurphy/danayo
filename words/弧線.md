## Notes
- [弧](../characters/弧.md) "arc" + [線](../characters/線.md) "line" — this word is the stand-in for [弧](../characters/弧.md), which cannot appear independently in Dan'a'yo.

弧線 names an arc — a curved line, geometric or figurative (an arcing trajectory).

Japanese こせん, Korean 호선, and Vietnamese hồ tuyến are all standard readings. No homophones (注音 ㄏㄛㄙ⼶ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
