## Notes
- [歯](../words/歯.md) "tooth" (stand-in: itself) + [茎](../words/茎.md) "stem, stalk" (stand-in: itself) — the alveolar ridge; the place of articulation just behind the teeth.

歯茎 means the alveolar ridge. Japanese しけい and Korean 치경 are both real, standard phonetics terms, matching this word's compositional reading exactly. Cantonese ci2 hang1 is a straightforward compositional reading. Mandarin chǐjīng and Vietnamese xỉ hành are compositional only, not independently verified as standard — Mandarin phonetics says 齿龈 (chǐyín), built from a different root for "ridge/gum" than 茎 ("stem, stalk").

**Bopomofo syllable-boundary dot**: the concatenated string `ㄑㄧㄎㄧㄫ` admits two legal partitions — the intended 歯(ㄑㄧ)+茎(ㄎㄧㄫ), and a false alternate (ㄑㄧㄎ+ㄧㄫ) that disagrees on where the first cut falls — so the dot at the true 歯|茎 boundary is required: ㄑㄧ·ㄎㄧㄫ.
