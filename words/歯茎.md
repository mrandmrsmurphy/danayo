## Notes
- [歯](../words/歯.md) "tooth" (stand-in: itself) + [茎](../words/茎.md) "stem, stalk" (stand-in: itself) — the alveolar ridge; the place of articulation just behind the teeth.

Wiktionary: Japanese 歯茎 is read はぐき (kun) or しけい (on) and is glossed "gingiva; gums (flesh round the teeth)"; its derived term is 歯茎音, "alveolar consonant". "Alveolar ridge" is the Dan'a'yo phonetics sense, used in `文法 - 02音韻論`.
