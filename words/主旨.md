## Notes
- [主](../characters/主.md) "main" + [旨](../characters/旨.md) "meaning, intent" — this word is the `stand_in` legitimizer for [[旨]], which cannot appear independently.

主旨 names the main idea, central point, or primary purpose of a piece of writing, speech, or plan — the takeaway a reader or listener is meant to grasp. Vietnamese chủ chỉ (confirmed via web search, "chủ ý, mục đích chính") is directly attested for this exact sense, though the near-synonym 宗旨/tôn chỉ ("guiding principle, mission statement," commonly used in official documents of organizations and media) is the more frequently seen term for closely related "guiding purpose" contexts in Vietnamese — chủ chỉ was used here since it matches this word's own written characters. `kwin` is false: Dan'a'yo 주즤 diverges from Sino-Korean 주지 in the second syllable's vowel. No homophones (注音 ㄐㄨㄐㄧㄜ unique among both words and characters).
