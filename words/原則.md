## Notes
- [原](../characters/原.md) "source, original" + [則](../characters/則.md) "rule" — principle; a fundamental guiding rule

Wiktionary: Chinese "principle (of doing or believing something)" and, in Cantonese, "original or unaltered floor plan or layout"; Japanese "principle"; Korean 원칙 "principle; rule" and Vietnamese nguyên tắc "principle" are the Han forms. Used in `文法 - 01序文`, `02音韻論`, `03文字法` and `05形態`.
