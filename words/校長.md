## Notes
- [校](../characters/校.md) "school" + [長 (char)](../characters/長%20(char).md) "long; chief"

校長 is the head of a school: the principal or headteacher. Wiktionary glosses Mandarin xiàozhǎng as "principal (of a school); headteacher", and also "president of a school or university". Japanese こうちょう and Korean 교장 are the same word.

The word is built from [学校](学校.md), "school", shortened to 校, and 長 "chief". It sits above the [教師](教師.md) and [教員](教員.md), the teachers, in a school's order.
