## Notes
- [瓜](../characters/瓜.md) "melon" + [子](../characters/子.md) "seed; child" + [点 (char)](../characters/点%20(char).md) "point; dot"

**瓜子点** is the *melon-seed dot*, the standard rounded form of the dot stroke ([[点]], the first of the eight principles of the 永字八法 on the [[Stroke]] page). Chinese instruction describes it as full in the middle, because the brush makes an outward-arching curve as it moves, giving the dot the plump, pointed-oval shape of a melon seed. The name is attested in Chinese regular-script teaching, for example in descriptions of the strokes of Ouyang Xun's 九成宮醴泉銘 (*Jiuchenggong liquan ming*).

It is the first of the named dot varieties on the [[Stroke]] page, followed by the drawn-out [[長点]] and the flicked [[挑点]]. Both 瓜 and 子 are bound characters here, with 子 in the sense of a small seed rather than "child".

The Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese and North Korean forms are compositional, built from the constituent words and characters. No Vietnamese form is given.
