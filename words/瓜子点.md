## Notes
- [瓜](../characters/瓜.md) "melon" + [子](../characters/子.md) "seed; child" + [点 (char)](../characters/点%20(char).md) "point; dot"

Wiktionary has no entry for 瓜子点; it is a compound of 瓜子 and 点. Wiktionary glosses 瓜子 in Chinese as "melon seed" and "any edible seed consumed as a snack, e.g. watermelon seeds, pumpkin seeds, sunflower seeds". It names the melon-seed dot, a variety of the dot stroke ([[点]]) on the [[Stroke]] page, followed by the drawn-out [[長点]] and the flicked [[挑点]]. Its readings are compositional.
