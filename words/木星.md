## Notes
- [木](../characters/木%20(char).md) "wood, Jupiter (in Wu Xing correspondence)" + [星](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [星](../characters/星%20(char).md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry; 木's own stand-in is itself.

木星 means Jupiter (the planet), named via the Chinese Wu Xing (Five Elements) tradition, which associates 木 "wood" with Jupiter.

Mandarin Mùxīng, Cantonese muk6 sing1, Japanese もくせい, and Korean 목성 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Simplified `vietnamese` to a single form (Mộc tinh, matching the format of the vault's already-perfected sibling [[土星]]) rather than the doubled "Sao Mộc, Mộc tinh." No homophones (注音 ㄇㄛㄎㄙㄝㄫ unique across both words and characters).
