## Notes
- [人 (char)](../characters/人%20(char).md) "person" reduplicated — "everyone, every person," the same reduplication pattern found across all source languages: Mandarin rénrén, Japanese hitobito (native reading, not on-reading), and Vietnamese người người (as in người người nhà nhà, "everybody, every household"), a fully natural everyday match using 人's own native word rather than a Sino-Vietnamese reading.
