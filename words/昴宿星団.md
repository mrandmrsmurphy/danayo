## Notes
- [昴](../characters/昴.md) "Pleiades" + [宿](../characters/宿.md) "lodging" + [星](../characters/星%20(char).md) "star" + [団](../characters/団.md) "group" — this word is itself the stand-in that legitimizes the character [昴](../characters/昴.md) as an independent Dan'a'yo entry (宿's own stand-in is [[寄宿]]; 団's own is [[集団]]).

昴宿星団 means the Pleiades star cluster.

Mandarin Mǎoxiùxīngtuán, Cantonese maau5 sau3 sing1 tyun4, Japanese ぼうしゅくせいだん, Korean 묘수성단, and Vietnamese Tua Rua (the traditional Vietnamese folk name) are the readings used here. Japanese more commonly uses the native word スバル (subaru, from which the car brand takes its name and logo). Fixed a stray space in `mandarin`, simplified `japanese` from a malformed native+compositional mix to the compositional form (with スバル noted here in prose). No homophones (注音 ㄇ⼘ㄨㄙㄨㄎㄙㄝㄫㄉ⺢ㄋ unique across both words and characters).
