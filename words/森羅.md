## Notes
- [森](../characters/森.md) "forest, dense" + [羅](../characters/羅.md) "spread out, net" — no stand-in relationship applies (森's own stand-in is [[森林]]; 羅's own is [[羅馬]]).

森羅 means all of nature, or dense foliage (as in 森羅万象, "all things in nature").

Mandarin sēnluó, Cantonese sam1 lo4, Japanese しんら, and Korean 삼라 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Fixed a real bug: `羅馬字`/`諺文` read simlo/심로, not matching either 森's own citation (sum/숨) or the word's own 注音 (ㄙㄨㄇㄌㄛ, which decodes to sum+lo) — corrected to sumlo/숨로. Filled the blank `vietnamese` field. No homophones (注音 ㄙㄨㄇㄌㄛ unique across both words and characters).
