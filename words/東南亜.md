## Notes
- [東](characters/東.md) "east" + [南](characters/南.md) "south" + [亜](characters/亜.md) "Asia" — no stand-in relationship applies (東's own stand-in is [[東方]]; 南's own is [[南方]]; 亜's own is [[亜細亜]]).

東南亜 means Southeast Asia (a geographic region of Asia, comprising the territories of Brunei, Cambodia, East Timor, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar (Burma), the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, and Vietnam).

Mandarin Dōngnányǎ, Cantonese dung1 naam4 aa3, Japanese とうなんあ (compositional character-by-character, distinct from real-world Japanese 東南アジア), and Korean 동남아 are all standard, fully compositional readings. Removed the redundant `品詞` field (duplicate of `pos`). No homophones (注音 ㄉㄛㄫㄋㄚㄇ·ㄚ unique across both words and characters).
