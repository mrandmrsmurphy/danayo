## Notes
- [仰](../characters/仰.md) "look up to" + [光 (char)](../characters/光%20(char).md) "light" — a transliteration of the name of the city, so the characters carry no meaning of their own; no stand-in relationship applies (仰's own stand-in is [[仰望]], 光's is the single-character word [[光]]).

仰光 is Yangon (Rangoon), the largest city of Myanmar ([[面田]]), a former capital. English Wiktionary gives the etymology as borrowed from Burmese ရန်ကုန် (Yangon), and quotes Xue Fucheng's diary of 1891, which counts over thirty thousand Chinese merchants and workers in Yangon, a third of them Hokkien and the rest mostly Cantonese. It also records the Sino-Vietnamese form Ngưỡng Quang as the Vietnamese reading of the Han spelling.

Mandarin Yǎngguāng and Cantonese joeng5 gwong1 are as at English Wiktionary. Japanese ヤンゴン and Korean 양곤 are the ordinary names of the city (from memory, not re-checked), so `kwin` is false. The `vietnamese` field holds Ngưỡng Quang, which English Wiktionary lists for this spelling.

No homophones (注音 ⼘ㄫㄎ⺢ㄫ unique across words and characters, checked 2026-10-12). The 注音 needs no syllable-boundary dot.
