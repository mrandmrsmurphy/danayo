## Notes
- [洒 (char)](../characters/洒%20(char).md) "sprinkle" — this word is the stand-in that legitimizes the character 洒 as an independent Dan'a'yo entry.

洒 is the simplified/alternate form of 灑 (also its own alias here), "to sprinkle, to spray" — most familiar in 灑脫/洒脱 (sǎtuō, "free and easy, unrestrained in manner") and 灑水/洒水 (to sprinkle water). The character is genuinely ambiguous on its own: beyond "sprinkle," it also carries an archaic sense "to wash" (a variant of 洗) and an entirely unrelated classical sense "solemn, dignified" — the three senses take three different Korean readings (세, 쇄, and 선 respectively), and only 쇄 belongs to the "sprinkle" sense used here.

English Wiktionary files 洒 under five separate etymologies. The first two simply point to 灑 ("to sprinkle") and 洗 ("to wash"). The others are the obsolete-in-standard-Chinese senses "high and steep" (cuǐ), "deep and steep; solemn" (xiǎn), and the first-person pronoun "I; me" (sǎ), which also names a surname and survives in the compound 洒家. Its Japanese entry lists the on readings さい, しゃ, せ and せん, and many compounds, such as 洒落 (しゃれ) and 洒脱 (しゃだつ). The page's `japanese` field holds さい.

Mandarin sǎ and Cantonese saa2 are as at English Wiktionary.

No other word shares the 注音 ㄙ⺢ㄧ (checked 2026-10-12).
