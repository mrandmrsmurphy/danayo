### Forbidden
- 亪 - 口訣: Korean 구결 for '란/난/역'
- 乲 - 口訣: Korean 구결 for '잘'
- 㐘 - 국자: Korean-coined hanja for '쌀' (rice)
- 扭 - 官话: Used only in Chinese (Mandarin)
- 吶 - 混乱: Used for sound, but we'd never agree.  Use 叫 for the meaning and 那 for the sound.
- ¡你! - 違法字
- 找 - 中国制造: HSK/1, but mainly Chinese.  Please use 捜 or 探 or 尋 instead.
- 吵 - 中国制造: "to argue" Use 弁論.  HSK/2
- 呀 - 官话: HSK/1 and 한문교육용기초한자/이름.  Even in Mandarin, equivalent to 啊.
- 扯 - 官话: HSK/3, but not used elsewhere.
- 抖 - 官话: HSK/3.
- 盯 - 官话: HSK/3, Chinese only.
- 抓 - 官话: HSK/2, but too Mandarin, and the Japanese meaning is different.  For "grab" use 捕; for "pinch" use 捏.
- 寽 - 混乱: "To seize, grasp with the hand" (ancient). Component in 埒/蜉, neither of which exist in this vault either. Zero existing citations.
- 肚 - 官话: Mandarin only. "Belly." Use 腹 instead. No existing vault citations.
- 竍 - 国字: 'decaliter' --> 十升
- 妛 - 幽霊文字: Misspelling of 𡚴.  Use 蚩 + 女
- 杣 - 国字: 'lumberjack' Use 樵夫.
- 瓧 - 国字: 国字 for the decagram; a dated symbol for ten grams
- 苆 - 国字: Rare Japanese character; Wiktionary records no definition
- 𬀧 - 国字: Abbreviation for 日下; used only in old place names such as 𬀧部 (Kusakabe)
- 𣏒 - 国字: Name of a place in Nakayamamoto, Tanagura, Fukushima; the reading comes from くるみ, 'walnut'
- 狆 - 国字: The Japanese Chin, a breed of dog
- 迚 - 国字: 'very, exceedingly, awfully' (とても)
- 杢 - 国字: "Woodworker."
