## 【[[永]]】<ruby>[[字]]<rt>ㄐㄧ</rt></ruby><ruby>[[八]]<rt>ㄅㄚㄊ</rt></ruby><ruby>[[法]]<rt>ㄈㄚㄆ</rt></ruby>
![永](images/forever.svg)

<ruby>[[筆画]]<rt>ㄆㄨㄊㄏ⺢ㄎ</rt></ruby>
1. "dot" or "point" - <ruby>[[点]]<rt>ㄉㄝㄇ</rt></ruby> or 【[[側]]】 (Li Puguang: <ruby>[[怪石]]<rt>ㄍ⺢ㄧㄙㄝㄎ</rt></ruby>)
	1. 【[[瓜]][[子]]】<ruby>[[点]]<rt>ㄉㄝㄇ</rt></ruby> - melon-seed dot
	2. <ruby>[[長点]]<rt>ㄐㄚㄫㄉㄝㄇ</rt></ruby> - long dot
	3. <ruby>[[挑点]]<rt>ㄊㄚㄨㄉㄝㄇ</rt></ruby> - rising dot
2. "horizontal" "left-to-right" - 【[[横]]】 = 【[[勒]]】 (Li Puguang: <ruby>[[玉案]]<rt>⼄ㄎ·ㄚㄋ</rt></ruby>)
	1. 【[[平]][[勒]]】
	2. 【[[仰]][[勒]]】 - A stroke that is slightly concave (curving upward like a smile). This is often used for the top stroke in characters like 【[[五 (char)|五]]】 or 【[[立 (char)|立]]】 to create a sense of stability.
	3. 【[[俯]][[勒]]】 - A stroke that is slightly convex (curving downward like a rainbow). It is often used for the bottom-most stroke of a character (like the last stroke of 【[[三 (char)|三]]】) to "cover" or ground the character.
3. "vertical-falling" (【[[豎]]】) or perpendicular (Li Puguang: <ruby>[[鉄柱]]<rt>ㄊㄝㄊㄐㄨ</rt></ruby>). Notice the initial dot.  【[[努]]】's come in two kinds
	1. <ruby>[[懸針豎]]<rt>ㄏ⼔ㄋㄐㄧㄇㄙㄨ</rt></ruby> - hanging needle crossbow - The tip tapers off into a sharp point at the bottom (like the bottom of the character 【[[中 (char)|中]]】).
	2. <ruby>[[垂露豎]]<rt>ㄐㄨㄧㄌㄛㄙㄨ</rt></ruby> - dropping dew crossbow - The bottom is rounded and blunt, looking like a heavy drop of dew clinging to a needle (like the vertical strokes in 【[[門]]】).
4. "hook" (<ruby>[[鉤]]<rt>ㄍㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>). Some hooks go left, others down = 【[趯](characters/躍.md)】 (Li Puguang: <ruby>[[蟹爪]]<rt>ㄏ⼘ㄧㄐ⺢ㄨ</rt></ruby>)
	1. 【[[左]]】<ruby>[[向]]<rt>ㄏ⼘ㄫ</rt></ruby><ruby>[[鉤]]<rt>ㄍㄛㄨ</rt></ruby>
	2. <ruby>[[戈鉤]]<rt>ㄍ⺢ㄍㄛㄨ</rt></ruby> - scythe hook
	3. <ruby>[[臥鉤]]<rt>⺢ㄍㄛㄨ</rt></ruby> - lying hook: A flattened, curved hook that looks like someone lying down (seen in the character 【[[心 (char)|心]]】).
5. "flick" (【[[提]]】=【[[挑]]】) or up-cutting stroke = 【[[策]]】 (Li Puguang: <ruby>[[虎牙]]<rt>ㄏㄛㄚ</rt></ruby>)
	1. <ruby>[[平提]]<rt>ㄅ⼶ㄫㄙㄝ</rt></ruby>: A rising stroke that is more horizontal, used in the left-side radicals like 【[[土 (char)|土]]】 or 【[[王 (char)|王]]】.
	2. <ruby>[[斜提]]<rt>ㄙ⼘ㄙㄝ</rt></ruby>: A more vertical/diagonal rising stroke, often seen in the radical for "Ice" (【[冫](lookup/Radicals/Radical%20015.md)】).
6. "concave" (【[[弯]]】) or curving path  = 【[[掠]]】 (Li Puguang: <ruby>[[犀角]]<rt>ㄙㄝㄧㄍㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>)
	1. 【[[斜]][[掠]]】: The standard 45-degree long sweep. It maintains a consistent diagonal path (found in characters like 【[[人 (char)|人]]】).
	2. 【[[豎]][[掠]]】: This stroke starts out almost perfectly vertical and only curves out to the left at the very bottom. You see this in the left side of 【[[月 (char)|月]]】 or 【[[用]]】.
	3. 【[[弧]][[掠]]】: A much more rounded, "bow-like" sweep. This is used when the character needs a softer, more circular flow, often appearing in the "grass" radical (【[艹](lookup/Radicals/Radical%20140.md)】).
7. "throw away" (<ruby>[[撇]]<rt>ㄆㄝㄊ</rt></ruby>) or left-descending stroke =【[[啄 (char)|啄]]】 (Li Puguang: <ruby>[[鳥啄]]<rt>ㄑㄛㄨ·ㄊㄛㄎ</rt></ruby>)
	1. 【[[蘭]]】<ruby>[[葉]]<rt>⼄ㄆ</rt></ruby><ruby>[[撇]]<rt>ㄆㄝㄊ</rt></ruby> - orchid leaf sweep: A long, elegant sweep that starts thin, gets thick in the middle, and ends thin—mimicking an orchid leaf.
	2. <ruby>[[直撇]]<rt>ㄐㄧㄊㄆㄝㄊ</rt></ruby> - vertical sweep: A sweep that stays more vertical than diagonal before flicking at the very end.
8. "press" (<ruby>[[捺]]<rt>ㄋㄚㄊ</rt></ruby>) or right-descending stroke =【[磔](characters/捺%20(char).md)】 (Li Puguang: <ruby>[[金刀]]<rt>ㄍㄧㄇㄊㄚㄨ</rt></ruby>)
	1. 【[[方]]】<ruby>[[頭]]<rt>ㄊㄛㄨ</rt></ruby><ruby>[[捺]]<rt>ㄋㄚㄊ</rt></ruby>: A "press" stroke with a very sharp, angular "foot" at the end.
	2. <ruby>[[圓]]<rt>⼔ㄋ</rt></ruby><ruby>[[頭]]<rt>ㄊㄛㄨ</rt></ruby><ruby>[[捺]]<rt>ㄋㄚㄊ</rt></ruby>: A "press" where the ending is more rounded and soft.

Writing must be in order:
- Top to bottom
- Left to right
- Horizontal then vertical
- Outside then inside
- Fill then close  
- Left descenders then right
- Middle, then left, then right
- Dots at the end
