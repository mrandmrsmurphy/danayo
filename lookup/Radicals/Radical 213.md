> [[Radicals]]
> Radical 213 is 龜 (also 龟 or 亀), "turtle", 16 strokes. It is a pictograph of a turtle seen from above, with head, shell, legs, and tail; the oracle-bone form is nearly a drawing. The turtle shell was the medium of Shang divination, so the character is bound up with oracle bones and with longevity. Only the Japanese form 亀 is filed here, standing for all three.
