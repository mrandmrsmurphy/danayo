> [[Radicals]]
> Radical 146 is 襾, "cover", 6 strokes, a variant of 西 "west". The glyph 西 began as a pictograph of a bird's nest or basket, and "west" is a loan (birds go to roost at sunset); as a radical the form 襾 reads as a lid or covering, as in 覆 "cover" and 覇 "hegemony". 西 and 要 are filed here by shape.
