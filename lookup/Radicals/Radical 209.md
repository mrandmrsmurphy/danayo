> [[Radicals]]
> Radical 209 is 鼻, "nose", 14 strokes. It combines 自, an older pictograph of a nose (later borrowed for "self" once 鼻 took over the literal meaning), with 畀 added as a phonetic. By extension it covers smell and, as in 鼻祖 "originator, first ancestor," beginnings.
