> Radical 177 is 革, "leather", 9 strokes. The ancient form shows an animal hide stretched out with its head, body and tail, and it heads characters for leather goods such as 靴 "boot" and 鞍 "saddle". The sense "revolution" comes from the metaphor of a hide being turned over and reworked.
