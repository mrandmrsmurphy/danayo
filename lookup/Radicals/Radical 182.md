> [[Radicals]]
> Radical 182 is 風, "wind", 9 strokes. Early writing borrowed the phoenix 鳳 to write wind, and the later form pairs 凡 with 虫. It heads characters for wind and, by extension, custom, manner and style, as in 風習. 颯 "sound of wind" is the only other character filed under it in the corpus.
