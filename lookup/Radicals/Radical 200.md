> [[Radicals]]
> Radical 200 is 麻, "hemp", 11 strokes. It pictures hemp stalks stripped and hung to dry under a shed (广), and covers hemp fibre, cloth, and by extension numbness and roughness. Few characters are filed under it.

- 麽 --> variant of 麼, forbidden with it
