> [[Radicals]]
> Radical 204 is 黹, "embroidery", 12 strokes. It is a pictogram (象形) of a cloud-like decorative pattern stitched onto bronzes and ceremonial dress, and the compounds under it name specific woven or embroidered patterns. 黹 itself is not used as a standalone character in the corpus.
