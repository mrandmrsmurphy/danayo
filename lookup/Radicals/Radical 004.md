> [[Radicals]]
> Radical 4 is 丿, the falling stroke — a single diagonal line (and one of the eight basic strokes of Chinese characters). The mirror-image 乀 and 乁 are listed here too. Characters classified here are grouped by this stroke rather than by a shared meaning.
