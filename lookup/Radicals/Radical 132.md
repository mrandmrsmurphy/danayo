> [[Radicals]]
> Radical 132 is 自, "self", 6 strokes. It began as a pictograph of a nose; because people point to their nose when speaking of themselves, it came to mean "self" and "from", and the nose itself was later written 鼻 with a phonetic added. In 臭 "stinky" it keeps its original nose sense, paired with 犬 "dog", the sniffer.
