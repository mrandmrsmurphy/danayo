> [[Radicals]]
> Radical 138 is 艮, "mountain", 6 strokes. In oracle-bone forms the glyph shows a person turning to look back, hence "to stop" and "stubborn"; the sense "mountain" comes from its place among the eight trigrams of the 易經, where ☶ 艮 stands for the mountain and for stillness. In 艱 "difficult" it supplies the sound.
