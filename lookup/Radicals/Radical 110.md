> [[Radicals]]
> Radical 110 is 矛, "spear" or "lance", 5 strokes. The character is a pictograph of a long-shafted thrusting weapon with a pointed head. In the classical story from the Han Feizi it is paired with 盾 "shield": a merchant boasts that his spears can pierce anything and that his shields can resist anything, which gave Chinese its word for "contradiction" (矛盾). Only 矛 itself is filed under this radical here.
