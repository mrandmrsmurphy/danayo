# Kana to Bopomofo (仮名 → 注音)

How Japanese kana are written in 注音 when a Japanese sound has no character assigned to it, as in the native readings of names on [[Japan]] (written with `◌` ruby). Dan'a'yo has no moras, only syllables of the form (C)(G)V(C), so each kana is mapped to the nearest legal Dan'a'yo syllable. The sound inventory and the syllable gaps are from [[文法 - 02音韻論]]; the symbols are in [[Bopomofo]].

## Principles

- **Voicing is the contrast.** Japanese contrasts voiced and voiceless where Dan'a'yo contrasts unaspirated-voiced and aspirated: voiceless か/た/ぱ are ㄎ/ㄊ/ㄆ, voiced が/だ/ば are ㄍ/ㄉ/ㄅ. This follows the examples already used on the Japan page (ト ㄊㄛ, キ ㄎㄧ, ヒ ㄏㄧ).
- **Japanese う/ウ is the reduced vowel ㄜ**, not ㄨ. Every u-column kana takes ㄜ (く ㄎㄜ, す ㄙㄜ, つ ㄐㄜ, む ㄇㄜ, る ㄌㄜ). Japanese speakers will have to learn the full cardinal /u/, ㄨ, which no kana is written with.
- **Affricates are ㄐ.** つ is ㄐㄜ (with ㄜ for う, per the rule above), and the other Japanese affricates (ち, ぢ, づ, じ, ず and the z-series) use ㄐ as well. This is the user-set rule; Dan'a'yo ㄐ may be realized as [dʑ] or a post-alveolar affricate without restriction. ㄑ (aspirated) is not used for kana.
- **ふ is ㄏㄜ.** The vault warns Japanese speakers against replacing /h/ with [ɸ], so ふ is written with ㄏ, not ㄈ.
- **ん is ㄋ**, the final nasal. Dan'a'yo allows no assimilation across syllables (see Morphophonology in [[文法 - 02音韻論]]), so ん is always ㄋ, whatever follows.
- **Geminate stops are possible.** ッ is written as the voiceless final stop (ㄎ, ㄊ or ㄆ) closing the preceding syllable, matching the stop that begins the next one: _natto_ → ㄋㄚㄊㄊㄛ.
- **Gaps in the syllable table.** ㄐ, ㄊ, ㄉ and ㄑ cannot take a y on-glide, so ちゃ-type sounds drop the glide (see the combinations below).

## Combinations (拗音)

The small や/ゆ/ょ add the y on-glide, which Dan'a'yo writes with the glide letters ⼘ (ya), ⼜ (yu) and ⼄ (yo).

| | ya | yu | yo |
|---|---|---|---|
| k | きゃ ㄎ⼘ | きゅ ㄎ⼜ | きょ ㄎ⼄ |
| g | ぎゃ ㄍ⼘ | ぎゅ ㄍ⼜ | ぎょ ㄍ⼄ |
| s | しゃ ㄙ⼘ | しゅ ㄙ⼜ | しょ ㄙ⼄ |
| z / j | じゃ ㄐㄚ | じゅ ㄐㄜ | じょ ㄐㄛ |
| t / ch | ちゃ ㄐㄚ | ちゅ ㄐㄜ | ちょ ㄐㄛ |
| n | にゃ ㄋ⼘ | にゅ ㄋ⼜ | にょ ㄋ⼄ |
| h | ひゃ ㄏ⼘ | ひゅ ㄏ⼜ | ひょ ㄏ⼄ |
| b | びゃ ㄅ⼘ | びゅ ㄅ⼜ | びょ ㄅ⼄ |
| p | ぴゃ ㄆ⼘ | ぴゅ ㄆ⼜ | ぴょ ㄆ⼄ |
| m | みゃ ㄇ⼘ | みゅ ㄇ⼜ | みょ ㄇ⼄ |
| r | りゃ ㄌ⼘ | りゅ ㄌ⼜ | りょ ㄌ⼄ |

The じゃ and ちゃ rows drop the glide because ㄐ cannot take a y on-glide, so they coincide with ざ-series syllables (ㄐㄚ, ㄐㄜ, ㄐㄛ).

## Sokuon (っ/ッ)

ッ closes the preceding syllable with a voiceless unreleased stop, the same as the stop that opens the next syllable:

| Before | Final | Example |
|---|---|---|
| k | ㄎ | ばっく → ㄅㄚㄎㄎㄜ |
| p | ㄆ | ろっぷ → ㄌㄛㄆㄆㄜ |
| t, ch, ts | ㄊ | なっと → ㄋㄚㄊㄊㄛ |
| s, sh | ㄊ | ざっし → ㄐㄚㄊㄙㄧ |

## Examples

- ヒロヒト → ㄏㄧ ㄌㄛ ㄏㄧ ㄊㄛ
- ムツヒト → ㄇㄜ ㄐㄜ ㄏㄧ ㄊㄛ
- ヨシヒト → ⼄ ㄙㄧ ㄏㄧ ㄊㄛ
- アキヒト → ㄚ ㄎㄧ ㄏㄧ ㄊㄛ
- ナルヒト → ㄋㄚ ㄌㄜ ㄏㄧ ㄊㄛ

## Long vowels

Long vowels violate Dan'a'yo phonotactics, so they are written with workarounds that fit the allowed long-vowel shapes (iy and uw):

| Japanese | 注音 | Note |
|---|---|---|
| おう | ㄛㄨ | mirrors the Japanese spelling |
| うう (long u) | ㄨㄛ | a workaround; the first vowel is the cardinal ㄨ |
| いい (long i) | ㄧㄜ | a workaround |
