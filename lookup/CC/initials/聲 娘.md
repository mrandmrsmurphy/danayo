> **Initial 娘** evolved into ㄋ

## CJKV Evolution
娘 [ɳ] is the 清濁 (sonorant) member of the retroflex-stop series (知組), the nasal counterpart of [[聲 知|知]]/[[聲 徹|徹]]/[[聲 澄|澄]] the way [[聲 泥|泥]] is for [[聲 端|端]]/[[聲 透|透]]/[[聲 定|定]]. As with every nasal documented so far, it's completely uniform among genuinely-inherited vocabulary: 12 of 13 characters land on **ㄋ (n)** — nasals keep proving the most stable class in this whole project. The lone exception, **檸**, isn't a natural counterexample: it's unattested in the Classical Chinese corpus (`mc_id: 0`), confirmed `#cranberry` (its only usage is 檸檬 "lemon"), and its Dan'a'yo reading was deliberately overridden to **ㄌㄝ** rather than following this initial's regular ㄋ outcome — see [[AIOS/memory/feedback_cranberry_transitivity.md]].
