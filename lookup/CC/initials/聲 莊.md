> **Initial 莊** mostly evolved into ㄐ, with one singleton outcome (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
莊 [t͡ʃ] is the 清 (plain) member of the retroflex series (莊組), paired with [[聲 初|初]] (次清)/[[聲 崇|崇]] (濁)/[[聲 生|生]] (清擦)/[[聲 俟|俟]] (濁擦). It's almost entirely uniform: **ㄐ for 26 of 27 characters**. The lone exception, **鄒 → ㄑ**, is unaspirated in both Mandarin (zōu) and Cantonese (zau1), but its Korean reading (추, aspirated ㅊ) explains the outcome — the same Korean-over-Mandarin pattern now seen repeatedly across this series.
