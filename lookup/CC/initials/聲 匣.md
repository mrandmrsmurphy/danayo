> **Initial 匣** mostly evolved into ㄏ, with small ㄎ and null/glide outcomes (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
匣 [ɣ] is the voiced counterpart of [[聲 曉|曉]], and the two initials mirror each other almost exactly. The overwhelming majority — **ㄏ (h) for 169 of 176 characters** — matches 曉's own outcome (voicing doesn't survive as a separate contrast here any more than aspiration did for the light-labial fricatives). Three characters — 茎, 劦, 雇 — shift to the stop **ㄎ**, the direct parallel of 曉's own 硅/軣/霍 trio, the same fricative-to-stop manner reassignment recurring in the same initial pair. The remaining four — 盂, 韋, 佑, 粤 — lose the initial entirely to a **null/glide** outcome, and unlike most of this project's minority pockets, all four are fully regular: Mandarin, Cantonese, and Korean agree unanimously that none of them ever had an h- to begin with.
