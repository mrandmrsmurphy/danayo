> **Initial 來** evolved into ㄌ

## CJKV Evolution
來 [l] is the lateral liquid, the 半舌音 ("half-dental") member of the 三十六字母 with no series partner at all — a standalone category. Liquids join nasals and fricatives as the most cross-linguistically stable consonant classes in this whole project: all 214 characters, the largest page in this entire set, land on a single outcome, **ㄌ (l)**, with no exceptions.
