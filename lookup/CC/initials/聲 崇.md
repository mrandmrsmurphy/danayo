> **Initial 崇** mostly evolved into ㄐ, with ㄙ/ㄑ minority outcomes (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
崇 [d͡ʒ] is the 濁 (voiced) member of the retroflex series (莊組), the affricate counterpart of [[聲 莊|莊]] (清)/[[聲 初|初]] (次清), and — like [[聲 從|從]]/[[聲 禪|禪]] — mostly resolves to the affricate outcome: **ㄐ for 14 of 19 characters**. Four characters (士, 床, 愁, 驟) instead landed on the fricative **ㄙ** rather than the affricate — a manner reassignment rather than an aspiration difference, and one without a clean single-language explanation (驟's Korean 취 is aspirated, but the others show no such support). The lone **ㄑ** singleton, **雛**, is aspirated in both Cantonese (co1) and Korean (추), fully explaining that jump.
