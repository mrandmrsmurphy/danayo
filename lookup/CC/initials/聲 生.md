## CJKV Evolution
生 [ʃ] is the 清擦 (voiceless fricative) member of the retroflex series (莊組), pairing with [[聲 莊|莊]]/[[聲 初|初]]/[[聲 崇|崇]] the way [[聲 心|心]] and [[聲 書|書]] pair with their own series' stops/affricates. As with every fricative documented so far, it's completely uniform: all 44 characters land on **ㄙ (s)**, with no exceptions.
