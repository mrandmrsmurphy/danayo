> **Initial 邪** mostly evolved into ㄙ, with three singleton outcomes (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
邪 [z] is the 濁擦 (voiced fricative) counterpart of [[聲 心|心]], and like every fricative documented so far it's overwhelmingly regular: **ㄙ for 46 of 49 characters**. The three singleton exceptions are each explainable on their own terms rather than forming a pattern: **辞 → ㄑ** matches its own Mandarin reading (cí, genuinely aspirated); **鱏 → ㄏ** tracks Mandarin xún, whose `x-` happens to match [[聲 曉|曉]]'s own IPA symbol rather than staying a plain sibilant; and **俟 → ㄐ** is the deliberate, already-documented case from [[聲 俟|俟]]'s own page — 俟母 has essentially no real character inventory of its own, so this vault's sole 俟-initial character was assigned here instead, explaining why this page's true ground-truth count (49) exceeds what a strict "z only" reading would suggest.
