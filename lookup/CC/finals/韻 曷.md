> **Final 曷** evolved into ㄚㄊ, with four singleton escapes (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
曷 [ɑt] converges mostly: 9 of 13 characters land on **ㄚㄊ**, matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`at`).

**喝** (x) is a true homophone of ɣ-initial 褐 (Mandarin *hè*, Cantonese *hot3*, Korean 갈), so the regular ㄏㄚㄊ slot is already taken, and it shifts its vowel to **ㄛㄊ**. **葛** (k) is a near-homophone of k-initial 割 (Cantonese *got3* for both, Vietnamese *cát* for both; Mandarin *gé* against *gē* and Korean 갈 against 할), and shifts its vowel to **⼘ㄊ** instead of taking ㄍㄚㄊ, which holds only 割.

**曷** itself is a true homophone of ɣ-initial 褐 (Cantonese *hot3*, Korean 갈 and Vietnamese *hạt* for both; Mandarin *hé* against *hè*), and so of 喝 as well, so the regular ㄏㄚㄊ slot is already taken; it dodges by shifting its vowel, landing alone on **ㄨㄊ**.

**獺** (tʰ) is a near-homophone of tʰ-initial 達 (Korean 달 for both; Cantonese *taat3* against *daat6*, Mandarin *tǎ* against *dá*), so the regular ㄊㄚㄊ slot is already taken; it dodges by shifting its vowel, landing alone on **ㄊ⺢ㄊ**.
