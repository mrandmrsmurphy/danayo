> [Classical Chinese](../Classical%20Chinese.md)
> **Final 曷** evolved into ㄚㄊ, with two unconditioned singleton escapes (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
曷 [ɑt] converges mostly: 9 of 11 characters land on **ㄚㄊ**, matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`at`).

**喝** and **葛** both escape, and both are phonetic relatives of the mainline characters — 喝 (x) shares the same phonetic series as ɣ-initial 褐, and 葛 (k) shares it with k-initial 割. Neither escape is crowding-driven (their would-be slots, ㄏㄚㄊ and ㄍㄚㄊ, each hold only one other member), so these read as unconditioned same-family, same-or-similar-initial splits: 喝 shifts vowel to **ㄛㄊ**, 葛 shifts vowel to **⼘ㄊ**.
