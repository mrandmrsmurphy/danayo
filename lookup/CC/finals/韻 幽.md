## CJKV Evolution
幽 [iɪu] lands 4 of 6 characters on **⼜ㄛ**, matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`yu/yuu`).

**赳** is a true homophone of k-initial 糾 (both Mandarin *jiū*, Cantonese *gau2*, Korean 규) and dodges the collision via coda-drop, landing on plain **⼜**. **謬**, the only m-initial character in this final, lands there too, unconditioned.
