> **Final 佳合** splits between 掛/卦 (⺢ㄧ) and 咼 (⼶ㄧ)

## CJKV Evolution
佳合 [ɣuɛ] has only 3 characters, all carrying a glide as expected for a 合 (rounded) final. 掛 and 卦 (both plain k-initial) land on **⺢ㄧ**; 咼 (aspirated kʰ-initial) lands on **⼶ㄧ** instead. There's no crowding to explain the split — it simply tracks the aspiration difference, though with a sample of 3 this isn't enough to generalize into a rule.
