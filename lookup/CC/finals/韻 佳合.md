> [Classical Chinese](../Classical%20Chinese.md)
> **Final 佳合** splits between 掛/卦 (⺢ㄧ) and 咼 (⼶ㄧ)

## CJKV Evolution
佳合 [ɣuɛ] has only 3 characters, all carrying a glide as expected for a 合 (rounded) final. 掛 and 卦 (both plain k-initial) land on **⺢ㄧ**; 咼 (aspirated kʰ-initial) lands on **⼶ㄧ** instead. The Vowels table's documented winner is `wai` (row 032 of the rime table), which is the ⺢ㄧ group. The aspiration difference only explains 咼's different *initial* (ㄎ against ㄍ); it does not explain its different *glide* (y, *kyei*, against w, *gwai*). There is no crowding to explain that — ㄎ⺢ㄧ is empty — and ㄎ is not barred from a w-glide, and 咼's own readings (Mandarin *guō*, Cantonese *waa1*, Korean 괘, Vietnamese *òa*) do not point to a y-glide either; Korean 괘 is in fact identical to 掛 and 卦's. So 咼 is an unconditioned outcome, and with a sample of 3 this is not enough to generalize into a rule.
