> [Classical Chinese](../Classical%20Chinese.md)
> **Final 清合** splits between a y-glide group and a plain group along initial lines (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
清合 [wiᴇŋ] splits 3–2: 営, 瓊, 頴 land on **⼶ㄫ** (y-glide), matching the Vowels table's documented value (`yeng/eng`, row 122 of 文法 - 99韻図), which covers both groups; 頃 and 傾 — near-homophones, both kʰ-initial, differing only in tone (Mandarin *qǐng*/*qīng*, Cantonese *king2*/*king1*, Korean 경 for both) — land on plain **ㄝㄫ** instead.

瓊's y-glide isn't a phonotactic violation despite being g-initial — velar+y-glide combines freely, unlike the labial w-glide ban. With only one g-initial and two kʰ-initial members, the sample is too small to confirm whether aspiration conditions the split or whether it's simply arbitrary: 瓊 (*qióng*, *king4*, 경) shares Korean 경 and the Cantonese `king` rime with 頃 and 傾 yet takes the y-glide.
