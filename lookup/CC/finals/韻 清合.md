> [Classical Chinese](../Classical%20Chinese.md)
> **Final 清合** splits between a y-glide group and a plain group along initial lines (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
清合 [wiᴇŋ] splits 3–2: 営, 瓊, 頴 land on **⼶ㄫ** (y-glide), matching the y-quality of the Vowels table's documented winner (`yeng`); 頃 and 傾 — true homophones, both kʰ-initial, Mandarin *qīng*/*qǐng* — land on plain **ㄝㄫ** instead.

瓊's y-glide isn't a phonotactic violation despite being g-initial — velar+y-glide combines freely, unlike the labial w-glide ban documented elsewhere in this sweep. With only one g-initial and two kʰ-initial members, the sample is too small to confirm whether aspiration conditions the split or whether it's simply arbitrary.
