> [Classical Chinese](../Classical%20Chinese.md)
> **Final 支三合** evolved into ㄨㄧ, with one homophony-driven exception (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
支三合 [iuᴇ] is the rounded counterpart of [[韻 支三開|支三開]], and carries the expected w-glide: 12 of 13 characters land on **ㄨㄧ**, matching the Vowels table's own documented winner (`ui`) for this final.

The lone exception is **瑞** (d͡ʑ-initial; Mandarin *ruì*, Cantonese *seoi6*, Korean 서, Vietnamese *thụy*), which lands on **⼔** instead. Its regular output would be ㄙㄨㄧ, but that slot is already held by 睡, 随 and 髄, whose initials (ʑ, z, s) are in the same sibilant family as 瑞's own. 瑞 is a Cantonese homophone of 睡 (*seoi6* for both) and shares the Vietnamese *th-* of 睡 (*thuỵ*), so it is a crowding escape from a slot with near-identical neighbours.
