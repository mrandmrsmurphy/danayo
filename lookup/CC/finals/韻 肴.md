> **Final 肴** splits by velar aspiration: ⼄ㄨ (plain k) vs ⼘ㄨ (everything else) (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
肴 [ɣau] fractures into several groups, with the cleanest split tracking a genuine initial-conditioned rule rather than homophony: all 10 plain-k-initial characters (交, 酵, 餃, 膠, 鮫, 教, 郊, 狡, 絞, 撹) land on **⼄ㄨ**, while the two aspirated-kʰ characters on this final (巧, 敲) land on **⼘ㄨ** alongside the rest of the 19-member main group — plain and aspirated velars cleanly diverge, matching the y-glide quality of the Vowels table's documented winner (`yau`).

The 8-member **ㄚㄨ** group notably contains every pʰ-initial character on this final (抛, 疱, 泡) — none appear in the main ⼘ㄨ group at all, suggesting labial aspirates simply don't combine with this final's glide.

The remaining five ㄚㄨ members (**嘲** ɖ, **巣** d͡ʒ, **鍬** t͡sʰ, **抄** t͡ʃʰ, all ㄑ or ㄐ, and **鮑** b) fall under two mechanisms: the affricates 嘲/巣/鍬/抄 are barred from the y-glide of the main ⼘ㄨ group by the ㄑ/ㄐ phonotactic constraint, and 鮑 dodges a 6-member ㄅ⼘ㄨ (砲, 包, 豹, 胞, 跑, 飽).

Three further singletons: **貌** dodges a 3-member ㄇ⼘ㄨ (卯, 茅, 昴) by dropping the vowel entirely, landing on bare **⼘**. **爪** and **櫂** are each the only members of their initial class on this final (t͡ʃ and ɖ respectively) and land on unconditioned singletons, **⺢ㄨ** and **ㄛㄨ**.
