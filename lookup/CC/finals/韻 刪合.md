> **Final 刪合** splits evenly: ⺢ㄇ (m-coda) vs ⺢ㄋ (n-coda) (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
刪合 [ɣuan] splits perfectly evenly, 4 characters each way, between an **⺢ㄇ (m-coda)** group (患, 関, 弯, 湾) and an **⺢ㄋ (n-coda) group** (環, 慣, 還, 頑). Neither MC initial (both groups mix real consonants and null initials) nor tone (患/waan6 and 環/waan4 land on opposite sides despite sharing an identical Korean reading 환) explains the split.

Worth flagging: [[文法 - 99韻図]]'s own reference table records this final's Dan'a'yo outcome as `wam` (m-coda) — but 刪 is historically a 山攝 rime, the -n-coda class (parallel to 寒/桓/元/仙 etc.), not a 咸攝 -m-coda rime like 談/覃/鹽. The m-coda documented in the table only matches half this corpus; the other half's n-coda is what MC rime-class history would actually predict. This may be worth a second look at the reference table itself, though that's outside the scope of this page.
