> **Final 刪合** splits 5 to 4: ⺢ㄇ (m-coda) vs ⺢ㄋ (n-coda) (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
刪合 [ɣuan] now splits 5 to 4 between an **⺢ㄇ (m-coda)** group (患, 関, 弯, 湾, 鰥) and an **⺢ㄋ (n-coda) group** (環, 慣, 還, 頑). Neither MC initial (both groups mix real consonants and null initials) nor tone (患/waan6 and 環/waan4 land on opposite sides despite sharing an identical Korean reading 환) explains the split. The sharpest pair is 関 and 慣 (Mandarin *guān*/*guàn*, Cantonese *gwaan1*/*gwaan3*, Korean 관 for both, same initial k): 関 is on ⺢ㄇ and 慣 on ⺢ㄋ. The daughters' codas do not predict either group — every reading in the character files, including the Vietnamese ones (*quan*, *quán*, *hoàn*, *loan*), has an -n.

The Vowels table (row 070 of 文法 - 99韻図) records `wam/wan` for this final, covering both groups; 刪 is historically a 山攝 rime, the -n-coda class (parallel to 寒/桓/元/仙), so the n-coda group is the one Middle Chinese rime-class history would predict, and the m-coda group is the corpus anomaly.
