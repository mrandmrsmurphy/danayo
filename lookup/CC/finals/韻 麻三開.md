## CJKV Evolution
麻三開 [ia] lands 20 of 27 characters on **⼘**, matching the Vowels table's documented value (`ya`, row 100 of 文法 - 99韻図).

The 5-member **ㄚ** group (姉, 遮, 借, 者, 諸) gives a direct confirmation of the vault's documented phonotactic rule — "ㄉ, ㄊ, ㄑ, ㄐ cannot combine with a y-glide": every one of these 5 characters has a t͡s/t͡ɕ initial (mapping to ㄐ/ㄑ), and no affricate-initial character appears anywhere in the main ⼘ group. 諸 (*zhū*, *zyu1*, 제, *chư*) carries the 麻 reading /t͡ɕia/ that [Wiktionary's entry for 諸](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/諸) lists alongside its 魚 reading (read on 2026-11-13).

**耶**, sharing its j-initial with the 7 characters already on ⼘ (野, 夜, 冶, 揶, 爺, 椰, 也), dodges that crowd via a different glide marker, landing on **⼶**; 耶 is identical to 爺 and 椰 in Cantonese (*je4*) and Korean (야) and shares Mandarin *yé* with 爺, while Vietnamese differs (*gia* against *da*). **車**, the only aspirated affricate (t͡ɕʰ) on this final, lands on its own unconditioned **⺢**: its readings (*chē*, *ce1*, 차, *xa*) show nothing that points to the w-glide. 者 and 遮 share the t͡ɕ initial yet differ in initial letter (ㄑ for 者, ㄐ for 遮).
