## CJKV Evolution
麻三開 [ia] lands 20 of 26 characters on **⼘**, matching the y-glide quality of the Vowels table's documented winner (`ya`).

The 4-member **ㄚ** group (姉, 遮, 借, 者) gives a direct confirmation of the phonotactic rule documented in [[skill_cc_phonology]] — "ㄉ, ㄊ, ㄑ, ㄐ cannot combine with a y-glide": every one of these 4 characters has a t͡s/t͡ɕ initial (mapping to ㄐ/ㄑ), and no affricate-initial character appears anywhere in the main ⼘ group.

**耶**, sharing its j-initial with the 7 characters already on ⼘ (野, 夜, 冶, 揶, 爺, 椰, 也), dodges that crowd via a different glide marker, landing on **⼶**. **車**, the only aspirated affricate (t͡ɕʰ) on this final, lands on its own unconditioned **⺢**.
