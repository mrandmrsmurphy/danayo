## CJKV Evolution
質開 [ɣiɪt] lands 4 of 6 characters on **ㄧㄊ**, matching the Vowels table's documented value (`it`, row 049 of 文法 - 99韻図). 筆 (→ **ㄨㄊ**) and 乙 (→ **ㄜㄊ**) have no same-initial neighbour in this final, but each avoids a syllable held by characters of 質三 ([[韻 質三|質三]]). 筆 (*bǐ*, *bat1*, 필, *bút*) shares Korean 필 with 匹 and 畢, which hold ㄆㄧㄊ, and its Vietnamese *bút* has the u vowel of ㄨㄊ. 乙 (*yǐ*, *jyut3*, 을, *ất*) would regularly take the null-initial ㄧㄊ held by 一, 逸, 溢 and 壱; its Cantonese *jyut3* differs from theirs (*jat1*).
