## CJKV Evolution
鈬開 [ɑk] is completely uniform: all 34 characters land on **ㄚㄎ**, matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`ak`, row 103 of 文法 - 99韻図). The daughters differ among themselves, with Mandarin `-e`/`-uo`/`-ao` (悪 *è*, 作 *zuò*, 落 *luò*), Cantonese `-ok` or `-aak` (*ok3*, *zok3*, *gaak3*), Korean 악/작/락 and Vietnamese `-ac`/`-ạc` (*ác*, *tác*, *lạc*), but all keep a velar stop coda in Cantonese, Korean and Vietnamese, and none of the shared slots is split: ㄌㄚㄎ holds six characters (絡, 楽, 駱, 酪, 落, 洛), ㄇㄚㄎ five (漠, 膜, 寞, 幕, 莫), ㄚㄎ five (悪, 鰐, 鄂, 顎, 愕), and ㄍㄚㄎ and ㄐㄚㄎ four each.
