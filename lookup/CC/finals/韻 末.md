> **Final 末** splits along the labial w-glide ban, plus one true-homophone escape (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
末 [uɑt] splits cleanly by initial class: 10 of 19 characters land on **⺢ㄊ** (w-glide), all non-labial (t͡s, ɣ, k, d, kʰ, ʔ); 6 land on plain **ㄚㄊ**, all labial (m, p, b) — a clean confirmation of the labial w-glide ban, which also shows on [[韻 戈一合|戈一合]], [[韻 鈬合|鈬合]] and [[韻 陽合|陽合]].

Within the w-glide group, **聒** (k) and **腽** (ʔ) both shift coda to **⺢ㄆ** instead — each a crowding or homophone escape. 聒 shares its initial (k) with 筈 and 括 (both ⺢ㄊ) and is a true homophone of 筈 in Cantonese (*kut3*), Korean (괄) and Vietnamese (*quát*). 腽 shares its null initial with 斡 (⺢ㄊ) and is a near-homophone of it (Vietnamese *oát* for both; Mandarin *wà* against *wò*, Cantonese *wat1* against *waat3*, Korean 올 against 알).

**犮** is a true homophone of 跋 (both b-initial, Mandarin *bá*, Cantonese *bat6*, Korean 발, Vietnamese *bạt*) and dodges the collision with a vowel-shift escape, landing alone on **⼘ㄊ**.
