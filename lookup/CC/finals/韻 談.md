## CJKV Evolution
談 [ɑm] converges almost completely: 24 of 25 characters land on **ㄚㄇ**, matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`am`, row 143 of 文法 - 99韻図).

**姏** (Mandarin *mán*, Cantonese *maan4*, Korean 담, Vietnamese *kiềm*), the only m-initial character in this final, lands alone on **ㄛㄇ**. It has no same-initial neighbour here, but its regular ㄇㄚㄇ is already held by 㒼 on [[韻 桓|桓]], which shares its Mandarin *mán*, so this is a crowding escape across finals; its Korean 담 matches the d-initial characters of this final, not the shift itself.
