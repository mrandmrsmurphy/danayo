> **Final 夬開** evolved into ㄚㄧ (no exceptions)

## CJKV Evolution
夬開 [ɣai] is fully uniform: all 5 characters land on **ㄚㄧ**, with no w-glide — exactly as expected for an 開 (unrounded) final, per the 合→w-glide rule seen across the other 開/合 pairs. The daughters agree: Mandarin `-ài`, Cantonese `-aai`, Korean 패/채/매 and Vietnamese *bại*/*trại* all keep the `-ai` rime (only 邁 *mười* looks like a native Vietnamese word).

The Vowels table's documented value for this final (row 029 of 文法 - 99韻図) is `ai`, matching the corpus, and it is distinct from its rounded sibling [[韻 夬合|夬合]], whose documented value is `wai`.
