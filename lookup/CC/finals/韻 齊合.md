> [Classical Chinese](../Classical%20Chinese.md)
> **Final 齊合** evolved into ⼔ㄧ (wei), with one exception (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
齊合 [wei] is the rounded counterpart of [[韻 齊開|齊開]], and carries the w-glide as expected: 5 of 6 characters land on **⼔ㄧ**, matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`wei`).

**閨** dodges the crowded ㄍ⼔ㄧ (shared by 桂 and 圭, both also k-initial) by dropping the glide marker, landing on **ㄨㄧ** instead. 閨 is identical to 圭 in Mandarin (*guī*), Cantonese (*gwai1*), Korean (규) and Vietnamese (*khuê*), while 桂 differs (*guì*, *gwai3*, 계, *quế*). The syllable ㄍㄨㄧ it lands on is also held by 季, 愧 and 癸 on [[韻 脂A合|脂A合]].
