> [Classical Chinese](../Classical%20Chinese.md)
> **Final 江** mostly evolved into ㄚㄫ, with a retroflex-initial-conditioned ⺢ㄫ subgroup (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
江 [ɣʌŋ] splits 10-3 between **ㄚㄫ** and **⺢ㄫ** (w-glide) — and unlike most splits documented so far, this one has a clean, findable conditioning factor. All three ⺢ㄫ members (双, 撞, 窓) have a retroflex or palatal-affricate MC initial (ʃ, ɖ, t͡ʃʰ respectively), while all ten plain ㄚㄫ members have a velar or labial initial (k, kʰ, ɣ, p, b). This matches a well-known Mandarin sound change — retroflex initials (zh/ch/sh) trigger rounding of a following -ang to -uang (hence 雙 shuāng, 撞 zhuàng, 窗 chuāng) — and Dan'a'yo's split tracks it directly. The Vowels table's own D-column entry for this final, `ang/wang`, already anticipated exactly this dual outcome.
