## CJKV Evolution

**墊** (d) and **怗** (tʰ) both belong to the large d/tʰ cluster crowding the main group (喋, 畳, 牒, 蝶, 諜, 貼 — 6 members) and dodge via vowel-shift, landing together on **ㄧㄆ**.

Notably, **帖** itself — the character this final is named after — doesn't land on the majority group at all: it lands alone on a third group, **ㄛㄆ**.
