## CJKV Evolution
狎 [ɣap] converges mostly: 3 of 4 characters land on **ㄚㄆ**, matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`ap`).

**圧** is a true homophone of 押/鴨 (all null-initial ʔ, Mandarin *yā*) — which already crowd the null-initial slot with two members — and dodges via coda-shift (-p → -t), landing on **ㄚㄊ**.

Data-quality flag: 圧's Cantonese field (*zong1*) doesn't fit a null-initial 狎 word at all; an *aat*-type reading (as in 押/鴨) would be expected instead.
