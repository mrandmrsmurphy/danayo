> [Classical Chinese](../Classical%20Chinese.md)
> **Final 乏** evolved into ㄚㄆ, with a true-homophone escape and two further scatter cases (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
乏 [iɐp] converges mostly: 5 of 9 characters land on **ㄚㄆ**, matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`ap`; the table's own display glyph `iap` omits a diacritic present in the corpus's actual `iɐp`).

**姂** is a true homophone of 乏 itself (both v-initial, Mandarin *fá*, Cantonese *fat6*, Korean 핍) and dodges the collision via vowel-shift, landing on **ㄛㄆ**. It's joined there by **灋** — an archaic variant form of 法 (same word, same Mandarin *fǎ*/Cantonese *faat3*/Korean 법 as 法 itself, which stays on the main group) — landing separately despite being, historically, the very same character.

**猲** shares its initial (kʰ) with 怯 in the main group and dodges via w-glide-add, landing on **⺢ㄆ**. **脅**, unconditioned, lands alone on **ㄝㄆ**.
