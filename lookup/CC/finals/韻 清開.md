> [Classical Chinese](../Classical%20Chinese.md)
> **Final 清開** evolved into ㄧㄫ, with a large same-initial unconditioned split into ㄝㄫ (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
清開 [iᴇŋ] lands 35 of 43 characters on **ㄧㄫ**, matching the y-quality of the Vowels table's documented winner (`ing`).

The remaining 6 — 整, 征, 浄, 精, 鄭, 静 — all share the ㄐ initial letter (from t͡ɕ/d͡z/t͡s/ɖ) with 8 members of the main group (正, 井, 政, 晶, 睛, 禎, 鉦, 靖), yet land on a separate **ㄝㄫ** instead, and the daughters show why for most of them: this is a crowding escape from ㄐㄧㄫ, whose members are homophones of the escapees.
- 精 is identical to 晶 and 睛 in every daughter (Mandarin *jīng*, Cantonese *zing1*, Korean 정, Vietnamese *tinh*).
- 静 and 浄 are identical to 靖 (*jìng*, *zing6*, 정, *tĩnh*).
- 征 is identical to 鉦 in Mandarin, Cantonese, Korean and Vietnamese (*zhēng*, *zing1*, 정, *chinh*).
- 整 (*zhěng*, *zing2*, 정, *chỉnh*) and 鄭 (*zhèng*, *zeng6*, 정, *trịnh*) differ from 正 and 政 only in tone, in Cantonese vowel and in Vietnamese, so they are near-homophones of the main group.

The 2-member **⼶ㄫ** group (嬴, 盈, both y-initial and identical in Mandarin *yíng*, Cantonese *jing4*, Korean 영 and Vietnamese *doanh*) isn't really an exception — ⼶ is this vault's standard spelling for the 以 y-initial, as on [[韻 庚三開|庚三開]].
