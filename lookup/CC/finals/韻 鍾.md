> **Final 鍾** splits into ⼄ㄫ vs ㄛㄫ, plus two singleton exceptions (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
鍾 [ɨoŋ] is a division-3 final and fractures accordingly — the largest split documented on this project so far. Of 49 characters, 20 land on **⼄ㄫ** and 27 on **ㄛㄫ**, with 2 further singleton exceptions. Unlike most initial-driven finals splits seen elsewhere, this one is *not* simply null-initial vs. real-initial: both groups mix real consonant initials freely (velars, sibilants, and 來/l all appear in the ⼄ㄫ group; labials, dentals, retroflexes, and even another velar appear in the ㄛㄫ group), so this looks like the genuine unconditioned per-character "simplicity principle" split expected of a division-3 final, not a phonotactic rule.

Both singleton exceptions look like homophony avoidance rather than noise:
- **凶** (ㄏ⼜ㄫ) shares its MC initial (x) and this final with 匈 and 胸, which already both land on the identical slot ㄏ⼄ㄫ. Rather than piling a third character onto that syllable, 凶 alone shifts to ㄏ⼜ㄫ.
- **縦** (ㄐㄛㄇ, m-coda) shares an *identical* MC initial+final combination (t͡s + ɨoŋ) with 踪, which already occupies ㄐㄛㄫ. 縦 dodges the exact collision by changing coda entirely, landing on ㄐㄛㄇ instead.
