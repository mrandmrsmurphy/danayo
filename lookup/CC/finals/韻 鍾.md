> [Classical Chinese](../Classical%20Chinese.md)
> **Final 鍾** splits into ⼄ㄫ vs ㄛㄫ, plus two singleton exceptions (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
鍾 [ɨoŋ] splits 20 of its 50 characters onto **⼄ㄫ** and 28 onto **ㄛㄫ**, with 2 further singleton exceptions; the Vowels table's documented value (`ong/yong`, row 007 of 文法 - 99韻図) names both main groups. The split is partly initial-conditioned. All 20 characters whose spelling starts with ㄐ, ㄑ, ㄅ, ㄆ or ㄈ (seven, five, three, one and four) take ㄛㄫ, which fits the phonotactic ban on a y-glide after ㄐ/ㄑ. The ⼄ㄫ group is made of null-initial characters (8), ㄍ (4), ㄙ (3), ㄋ (2), ㄏ (2) and ㄌ (1), but these initials also appear on ㄛㄫ (three null-initial, two ㄙ, one each ㄍ, ㄎ, ㄋ), so for those initials the choice is not predicted by the initial.

Both singleton exceptions look like homophony avoidance rather than noise:
- **凶** (ㄏ⼜ㄫ) shares its MC initial (x) and this final with 匈 and 胸, which already both land on the identical slot ㄏ⼄ㄫ. Rather than piling a third character onto that syllable, 凶 alone shifts to ㄏ⼜ㄫ; it is identical to 匈 and 胸 in Mandarin (*xiōng*), Cantonese (*hung1*), Korean (흉) and Vietnamese (*hung*).
- **縦** (ㄐㄛㄇ, m-coda) shares an *identical* MC initial+final combination (t͡s + ɨoŋ) with 踪, which already occupies ㄐㄛㄫ. 縦 dodges the exact collision by changing coda entirely, landing on ㄐㄛㄇ instead; its readings (*zòng*, *zung3*, 종) differ from 踪's (*zōng*, *zung1*, 종) only in tone and none shows an m coda.
