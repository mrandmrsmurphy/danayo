> **Final 灰** evolved into ㄛㄧ, with a new kind of homophony-driven escape (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
灰 [uʌi] lands 39 of 43 characters on **ㄛㄧ**, matching the Vowels table's documented value (`oi`, row 042 of 文法 - 99韻図).

This final's bilabial-initial (p/b) cohort shows a homophone-avoidance move that changes the initial letter instead of the vowel. Of the 9 p/b-initial characters, 5 (陪, 賠, 背, 杯, 悖) land on the expected **ㄅㄛㄧ**, but the other 4 (徘, 佩, 培, 輩) land on **ㄈㄛㄧ** instead — reusing the labiodental ㄈ letter normally reserved for 非/敷/奉-type MC initials, even though all four are bilabial in every daughter (徘: *pái*/*pui4*/배; 佩: *pèi*/*pui3*/패; 培: *péi*/*pui4*/배; 輩: *bèi*/*bui3*/배). The daughters show why: 培 is identical to 陪 and 賠 (*péi*, *pui4*, 배, *bồi*), and 輩 is identical to 背 (*bèi*, *bui3*, 배, *bối*), so the regular ㄅㄛㄧ would make them literal homophones of its members.

Two smaller pairs round out the exceptions: **呆** and **苺** (both m-initial) dodge a 6-member ㄇㄛㄧ (毎, 梅, 煤, 媒, 妹, 枚) by shifting to **ㄚㄧ**. **腿** and **退** (both tʰ-initial) land on **ㄧㄜ** with no crowded slot to explain it, since no other t/tʰ-initial character occupies this final's main group at all; the two are near-homophones of each other (Cantonese *teoi2*/*teoi3*, Korean 퇴, Vietnamese *thoái*), but none of their readings points to ㄧㄜ.
