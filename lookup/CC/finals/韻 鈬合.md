## CJKV Evolution
鈬合 [wɑk] splits cleanly by initial type, giving another confirmation of the labial w-glide ban first documented on [[韻 戈一合|戈一合]]: all 5 labial-initial characters (博, 泊, 箔, 薄, 爆 — all p/b) drop the glide entirely, landing on plain ㄚㄎ/ㄛㄎ, while the remaining 5 characters (all non-labial: k, kʰ, ɣ, ʔ, x) carry the w-glide and land on **⺢ㄎ**, matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`wak`).

Of the labial subgroup, **博, 泊, 箔, 薄** converge on **ㄚㄎ**; **爆** dodges that 4-member crowd via a vowel-shift, landing on **ㄛㄎ**.
