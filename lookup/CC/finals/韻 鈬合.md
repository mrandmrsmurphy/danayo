## CJKV Evolution
鈬合 [wɑk] splits cleanly by initial type, confirming the labial w-glide ban also seen on [[韻 戈一合|戈一合]]: all 5 labial-initial characters (博, 泊, 箔, 薄, 爆 — all p/b) drop the glide entirely, landing on plain ㄚㄎ/ㄛㄎ, while the remaining 5 characters (all non-labial: k, kʰ, ɣ, ʔ, x) carry the w-glide and land on **⺢ㄎ**, matching the Vowels table's documented value (`wak/ak`, row 104 of 文法 - 99韻図), which covers both groups.

Of the labial subgroup, **博, 泊, 箔, 薄** converge on **ㄚㄎ**; **爆** dodges that 4-member crowd (together with 駁 on [[韻 覺|覺]]) via a vowel-shift, landing on **ㄛㄎ**; its Korean 폭 has an o vowel, unlike 박 for 博, 泊, 箔 and 薄, so the readings support the shift. 博 is identical to 駁 in Mandarin, Cantonese, Korean and Vietnamese (*bó*, *bok3*, 박, *bác*). In the w-glide group, 郭 and 霍 share Korean 곽, and 穫 and 蠖 are identical in Mandarin (*huò*), Cantonese (*wok6*), Korean (확) and Vietnamese (*hoạch*).
