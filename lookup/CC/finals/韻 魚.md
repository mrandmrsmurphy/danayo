> [Classical Chinese](../Classical%20Chinese.md)
> **Final 魚** splits into ⼄ vs ㄛ by initial type, with recurring intra-class fracturing (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
魚 [ɨʌ] has 75 characters: 44 land on **⼄**, 21 on **ㄛ**, and the rest scatter further. The Vowels table's documented value (`yo`, row 022 of 文法 - 99韻図) is the ⼄ outcome. The ⼄/ㄛ split loosely tracks initial type: null/yod, velar, lateral and most sibilant initials land on ⼄, while retroflex and palatal-affricate initials dominate ㄛ, but the split is not clean by initial class.

Two initial classes split internally:
- The retroflex sibilant **ʃ** (書母/生母-type) splits: 梳 lands on ㄙㄛ (the main ㄛ group), while 所, 蔬, 疎 and 疋 — sharing the identical MC initial — land on **ㄙㄜ**. The daughters do not decide it: 梳, 蔬 and 疎 are identical in Mandarin (*shū*), Cantonese (*so1*), Korean (소) and Vietnamese (*sơ*), 所 differs only in tone (*suǒ*, *so2*), and 疋 has unrelated readings (*shū*, *pat1*, 필, *nhã*). **疽** (t͡s-initial, Mandarin *jū*, Cantonese *zeoi1*, Korean 저) also lands on **ㄜ** with no same-initial neighbour on the main ⼄ group (its regular slot ㄐ⼄ is empty), an unconditioned singleton.
- The palatal nasal **ȵ** splits three ways: 如 lands on the main ⼄ group (ㄋ⼄), 女 lands on ㄋㄜ, and 汝 lands on a unique **ㄛㄧ** (ㄋㄛㄧ), three different vowel outcomes for one MC initial on one final. 如 and 汝 share Cantonese *jyu* and Korean 여.

The remaining singletons (箸 → ⺢, 俎 → ㄛㄨ, 除 → ㄝㄧ) each dodge a slot already held by same-initial characters (猪/貯 on ㄐㄛ for 箸; 阻/詛 for 俎; 儲/佇 for 除). 箸 is a near-homophone of 貯 (*zhù*, Cantonese *zyu6*/*cyu5*, Korean 저), 俎 is identical to 阻 in Mandarin (*zǔ*), Cantonese (*zo2*) and Korean (조), and 除 is a near-homophone of 儲 and 佇 (Cantonese *ceoi4* against *cyu5*, Korean 제 against 저).
