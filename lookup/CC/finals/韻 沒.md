## CJKV Evolution
沒 [uət] is nearly uniform: 10 of 11 characters land on **ㄛㄊ**, matching the Vowels table's tentative winner (`~ot~`). **肭** dodges the only other n-initial character on this final, 訥, by shifting its vowel and landing on **ㄨㄊ**.
