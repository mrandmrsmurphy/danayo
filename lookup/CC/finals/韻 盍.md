> **Final 盍** evolved into ㄚㄆ, with one crowding-driven singleton escape (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
盍 [ɑp] converges mostly: 5 of 6 characters land on **ㄚㄆ**, matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`ap`, row 144 of 文法 - 99韻図). 臘 and 蠟 are identical in Mandarin (*là*), Cantonese (*laap6*) and Korean (랍) and share the syllable ㄌㄚㄆ.

**魶**, the only n-initial character in this final, lands alone on **ㄨㄆ**. Its regular syllable ㄋㄚㄆ is already held by 納 and 衲 on [[韻 合|合]], and 魶 is identical to both in Mandarin (*nà*), Cantonese (*naap6*) and Korean (납), so it is a crowding escape across finals.
