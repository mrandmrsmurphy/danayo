## CJKV Evolution
葉三 [iᴇp] scatters across four groups: 4 of 8 characters land on **ㄛㄆ** (op), matching the Vowels table's documented value (`op`, row 155 of 文法 - 99韻図), 2 (捷, 睫) on **ㄝㄆ** (ep), and 1 (枼) on **ㄨㄆ** (up), while 葉 itself lands separately on **⼄ㄆ**.

**葉** and **枼** share identical readings (both j-initial, Mandarin *yè*, Cantonese *jip6*, Korean 엽), yet land on two different groups (⼄ㄆ vs ㄨㄆ). [Wiktionary's entry for 枼](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/枼) describes it as the earlier form of 葉 (read 2026-11-14), so they are the same word written two ways; their Vietnamese readings differ (*diếp* against *diệp*).

**睫** and **捷** share the phonetic component 疌 and are identical in Mandarin (*jié*), Cantonese (*zit6*), Korean (첩) and Vietnamese (*tiệp*); both take ㄝㄆ, with ㄐ for 捷 (d͡z) and ㄑ for 睫 (t͡sʰ).
