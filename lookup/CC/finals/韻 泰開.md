> [Classical Chinese](../Classical%20Chinese.md)
> **Final 泰開** evolved into ㄚㄧ, with one homophone-driven exception (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
泰開 [ɑi] is a division-1 final and converges as expected: 18 of 19 characters land on **ㄚㄧ**, matching the Vowels table's documented value (`ai`, row 025 of 文法 - 99韻図); its rounded counterpart is [[韻 泰合|泰合]].

The lone exception is 奈, which lands on **⼘ㄧ** instead. No other character on this final shares 奈's n-initial, but its regular syllable ㄋㄚㄧ is already held by 耐 and 乃 on [[韻 咍|咍]], and 奈 is a true homophone of 耐 in Mandarin (*nài*), Cantonese (*noi6*) and Korean (내), so the crowding is across finals rather than within this one. Vietnamese *nại* differs from 耐's *nài*.
