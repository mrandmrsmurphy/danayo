> **Final 覺** evolved into ㄚㄎ, with 4 homophony-driven exceptions on ㄛㄎ (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
覺 [ɣʌk] lands 18 of 22 characters on **ㄚㄎ**, matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`ak`, row 010 of 文法 - 99韻図). The remaining 4 — 角, 剝, 擢, 渥 — all shift to **ㄛㄎ**, and each is dodging a same-initial slot that was already occupied:

- 角 (would regularly be ㄍㄚㄎ) dodges a slot already shared by 較 and 覚; 角 is identical to 覚 in Cantonese (*gok3*) and Korean (각).
- 剝 (would regularly be ㄅㄚㄎ) dodges 駁, which already holds that slot; they share Korean 박 and Vietnamese *bác*, while Cantonese differs (*mok1* against *bok3*).
- 擢 (would regularly be ㄐㄚㄎ) dodges 捉, which already holds that slot; their readings differ (*zok6*, 탁 against *zuk1*, 착), so crowding is the only explanation.
- 渥 (would regularly be ㄚㄎ, null-initial) dodges a slot already shared by 岳 and 握; 渥 is identical to 握 in Mandarin (*wò*), Cantonese (*ak1*) and Korean (악).

All four exceptions shift in the same direction (ㄚ → ㄛ).
