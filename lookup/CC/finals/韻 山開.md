## CJKV Evolution
山開 [ɣɛn] lands 13 of 16 characters on **ㄚㄋ**, matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`an`).

The 3 exceptions are 柬, 揀 and 弁, and all three converge on the same escape route, **⼘ㄋ**, from two different stories:
- **柬 and 揀** (both k-initial) are true homophones of 簡 (all Mandarin *jiǎn*, Cantonese *gaan2*, Korean 간), and they dodge a 3-member ㄍㄚㄋ crowd (艱, 簡, 間).
- **弁** (b-initial) has no real collision: ㄅㄚㄋ holds only 辦. What sets it apart is its readings, since its Cantonese *bin6* and Korean 변 carry a front vowel, unlike 辦's *baan6* and 판, so the y-glide of ⼘ㄋ follows the daughters (Mandarin *biàn* against *bàn* agrees).
