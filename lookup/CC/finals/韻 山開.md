## CJKV Evolution
山開 [ɣɛn] lands 13 of 16 characters on **ㄚㄋ**, matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`an`).

The 3 exceptions — 柬, 揀, and 弁 — form another shared overflow bucket like the one seen on [[韻 魂|魂]]: 柬 and 揀 (both k-initial) dodge a 3-member ㄍㄚㄋ (艱, 簡, 間), while 弁 (b-initial) dodges a 1-member ㄅㄚㄋ (辦). Despite starting from two different crowded slots with two different initials, all three converge on the same escape route, **⼘ㄋ**.
