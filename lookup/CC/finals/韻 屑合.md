> [Classical Chinese](../Classical%20Chinese.md)
> **Final 屑合** evolved into ⼔ㄊ (wet)

## CJKV Evolution
屑合 [wet] is fully uniform: all 4 characters (決, 血, 訣, 穴) land on **⼔ㄊ**, matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`wet`) exactly, with no exceptions. The daughters agree on a rounded front-vowel rime with a stop coda: Mandarin `-ue` (*jué*, *xuè*, *xué*), Cantonese `-yut`/`-yut` (*kyut3*, *hyut3*, *jyut6*), Korean 결/혈 and Vietnamese *quyết*/*hoét*. 決 and 訣 have the same Middle Chinese initial (k) and identical readings in all four daughters, yet are spelled ㄎ⼔ㄊ and ㄍ⼔ㄊ; that is an initial-side fact recorded on their character pages, not a property of this final.
