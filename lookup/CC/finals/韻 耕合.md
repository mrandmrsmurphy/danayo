> **Final 耕合** has three characters, all on ⼔ㄫ

## CJKV Evolution
耕合 [ɣwɛŋ] has only three characters: 宏 and 紘 — true homophones (both ɣ-initial, Mandarin *hóng*, Cantonese *wang4*, Korean 굉) — and **軣** (x-initial, Mandarin *hōng*, Cantonese *gwang1*, Korean 굉). All three converge on **⼔ㄫ**, close to the Vowels table's documented winner (`weng`); no exceptions (軣 differs only in its initial, ㄍ rather than ㄏ).
