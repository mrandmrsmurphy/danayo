> **Final 陽開** fractures into five groups, with retroflex/affricate initials splitting massively between glide and no-glide outcomes (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
陽開 [ɨɐŋ] has 89 characters, in five groups. 45 land on **⼘ㄫ**, matching the Vowels table's documented value (`yang`, row 105 of 文法 - 99韻図).

The retroflex and affricate-initial characters (t͡ɕ/ʈ/ɖ/t͡ʃ/d͡z/d͡ʒ-type, 40 characters combined: 24 on ㄚㄫ and 16 on ⺢ㄫ, including **脹** (ʈ, ㄑㄚㄫ) and **醤** (t͡s, ㄐㄚㄫ), both on ㄚㄫ) split between two groups, and the initial letter itself varies for the same Middle Chinese initial. **帳** and **張** share the identical MC initial (ʈ) yet land on opposite sides, 帳 on the w-glide-bearing **ㄐ⺢ㄫ** and 張 on the plain **ㄑㄚㄫ**, with 脹 on ㄑㄚㄫ. 帳 and 脹 are identical in Mandarin (*zhàng*) and Cantonese (*zoeng3*), so the readings do not decide it. **唱** and **昌** (both t͡ɕʰ) land on ⺢ㄫ, while **廠** (also t͡ɕʰ) lands on ㄚㄫ; its Cantonese *cong2* has no rounded `-oe-` unlike 唱 (*coeng3*) and 昌 (*coeng1*), so here the daughter reading agrees with the spelling.

Two small coda-shift subgroups round out the page. **疆** and **姜** (both k-initial; *jiāng*, *goeng1*, 강, identical to 僵) dodge a 2-member ㄍ⼘ㄫ slot (僵, 強) by changing coda, landing on **⼘ㄋ**. **昜**, sharing its j-initial with the large null-initial cluster on ⼘ㄫ (11 members: 殃, 央, 仰, 洋, 楊, 陽, 揚, 様, 養, 羊, 瘍), dodges the same way, landing on **⼘ㄇ** alongside 床; 昜 is identical to 洋 and 陽 in Mandarin (*yáng*), Cantonese (*joeng4*) and Korean (양). None of the three has an n or m coda in any daughter reading.
