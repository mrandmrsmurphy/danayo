> **Final 陽開** fractures into five groups, with retroflex/affricate initials splitting massively between glide and no-glide outcomes (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
陽開 [ɨɐŋ] is the largest and most fractured final documented in this sweep. 45 of 87 characters land on **⼘ㄫ**, matching the y-glide quality of the Vowels table's documented winner (`yang`).

The retroflex and affricate-initial characters (t͡ɕ/ʈ/ɖ/t͡ʃ/d͡z/d͡ʒ-type, 38 characters combined) split between two groups with no findable conditioning factor — the largest-scale confirmation of the "same initial, arbitrary outcome" pattern found anywhere in this sweep. **帳** and **張** share the identical MC initial (ʈ) yet land on opposite sides: 帳 on the w-glide-bearing **⺢ㄫ**, 張 on the plain **ㄚㄫ**. **唱** and **昌** (both t͡ɕʰ) land on ⺢ㄫ, while **廠** (also t͡ɕʰ) lands on ㄚㄫ — the same initial, split three ways across just two characters' worth of company.

Two small coda-shift subgroups round out the page. **疆** and **姜** (both k-initial) dodge a 2-member ⼘ㄫ crowd (僵, 強) by changing coda, landing on **⼘ㄋ**. **昜**, sharing its j-initial with the large 7-member y-glide cluster (洋, 楊, 陽, 揚, 養, 羊, 瘍), dodges the same way, landing on **⼘ㄇ** alongside 床.
