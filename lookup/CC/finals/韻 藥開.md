> **Final 藥開** evolved into ⼘ㄎ, with a t͡ɕ-initial split among the exceptions (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
藥開 [ɨɐk] lands 10 of 19 characters on **⼘ㄎ**, matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`yak`, row 107 of 文法 - 99韻図).

The 6-member **ㄚㄎ** group is made of affricates and sibilants (t͡s/t͡ɕ/ɕ/t͡ɕʰ: 雀, 灼, 酌, 爵, 鑠, 綽), which the y-glide of ⼘ㄎ cannot follow after ㄐ or ㄑ. **勺** (t͡ɕ) is a true homophone of 酌 in Cantonese (*zoek3*) and Korean (작) and shares Vietnamese *chước* with 灼, and the ㄐㄚㄎ slot already holds 雀, 灼, 酌 and 爵, so it lands on **⺢ㄎ** alongside 着 (whose readings, *zhe*, *zoek6*, 착, *nước*, differ). **溺**, sharing its ȵ initial with 弱 (in the main ⼘ㄎ group), takes **ㄧㄎ**, which its readings support: Cantonese *nik6*, Korean 닉 and Vietnamese *nịch* have the high vowel, unlike 弱 (*joek6*, 약, *nhược*).
