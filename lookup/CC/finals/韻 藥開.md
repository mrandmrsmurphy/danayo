## CJKV Evolution
藥開 [ɨɐk] lands 10 of 19 characters on **⼘ㄎ**, matching the y-glide quality of the Vowels table's documented winner (`yak`).

The 6-member **ㄚㄎ** group is dominated by affricates (t͡s/t͡ɕ/ɕ), and **勺**, sharing its t͡ɕ initial with 灼 and 酌 (both in ㄚㄎ), instead lands on **⺢ㄎ** alongside 着 — another instance of the "same initial, arbitrary outcome" pattern. **溺**, sharing its ȵ initial with 弱 (in the main ⼘ㄎ group), dodges via a vowel-shift to **ㄧㄎ**.
