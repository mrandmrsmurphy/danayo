> **Final 鎋開** evolved into ㄚㄊ (at)

## CJKV Evolution
鎋開 [ɣɛt] is completely uniform: all 9 characters land on **ㄚㄊ**, matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`at`, row 075 of 文法 - 99韻図) exactly, with no exceptions. The daughters keep a stop coda throughout in Cantonese (*baat3*, *zaat3*, *saat3*, *aat3*), Korean (팔, 찰, 살, 알) and Vietnamese (*bát*, *sát*, *trát*); 八, 捌 and 叭 are identical in Mandarin (*bā*) and Korean (팔) and share the syllable ㄅㄚㄊ, and 札, 紮 and 察 share Korean 찰.
