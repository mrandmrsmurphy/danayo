> **Final 麻二合** evolved into ⺢ (wa), with two complete-homophone-yet-diverging exceptions (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
麻二合 [ɣua] is nearly uniform: 14 of 16 characters land on **⺢**, matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`wa`).

The 2 exceptions are the richest concentration of "identical daughter readings, different outcome" found in this sweep. **佤** and **瓦** share the *exact same* Mandarin, Cantonese, and Korean readings (wǎ, ngaa5, 와) — true homophones in every checked daughter language — yet 瓦 stays on ⺢ while 佤 lands on **⺢ㄊ**. Separately, **哇** shares its readings (wā, waa1, 와) with 窪 and 蛙, both of which stay on ⺢; 哇 alone dodges what would otherwise be a three-way pileup, landing on **⺢ㄨ**.
