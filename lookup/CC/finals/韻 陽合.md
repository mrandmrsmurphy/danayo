> [Classical Chinese](../Classical%20Chinese.md)
> **Final 陽合** splits perfectly by labial vs non-labial initial (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
陽合 [ʉɐŋ] gives the cleanest confirmation yet of the labial w-glide ban (previously confirmed on [[韻 戈一合|戈一合]] and [[韻 鈬合|鈬合]]): all 16 labial-initial characters (f/ɱ-type: 妨, 芳, 坊, 罔, 訪, 望, 倣, 肪, 妄, 亡, 紡, 方, 防, 放, 忘, 網) land glide-free on **ㄚㄫ**, while all 8 non-labial characters (匡, 枉, 旺, 王, 狂, 況, 筐, 往) carry the w-glide and land on **⺢ㄫ**, matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`wang`). Zero exceptions on either side.
