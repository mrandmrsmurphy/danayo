> **Final 蕭** splits into ⼘ㄨ vs ㄛㄨ, with four dental-stop escapees taking four different routes (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
蕭 [eu] splits 12-9 between **⼘ㄨ** and **ㄛㄨ**, roughly matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`au`).

The ㄛㄨ group is dominated by dental stops (d/t/tʰ: 跳, 鳥, 窕, 彫, 眺, 釣 — 6 of 9 members), and four further dental-stop characters dodge that crowd via four completely different routes: **条** and **貂** both drop the -ㄨ entirely, landing on bare **ㄛ**; **鵰** adds a w-glide instead, landing on **ㄨㄛ**; **挑** shifts vowel, landing on **ㄚㄨ**. Four characters, one crowded initial class, four distinct escapes — echoing the multi-route scatter already seen on [[韻 諄|諄]] and [[韻 豪|豪]].

Two further singletons: **窈** (null-initial) lands on **⼄ㄨ**, and **叫** (the only k-initial character on this final) drops the -ㄨ, landing on bare **⼘**.
