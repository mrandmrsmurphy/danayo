> **Final 咍** mostly evolved into ㄚㄧ (ai), with two small exceptions (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
咍 [ʌi] is overwhelmingly regular: **ㄚㄧ (ai) for 57 of 62 characters**, matching the reference table's own already-recorded M/C/J/K/V/D row (all converging on `ai`-type outcomes). Two small exceptions:
- **苔/該/殆/跆 → ㄛㄧ (oi)**: three of these four — 苔, 殆, 跆 — share the same phonetic component (台), and land on `oi` together despite their own phonetic root 台 itself staying perfectly regular (台 → ㄉㄚㄧ, the plain majority outcome). This is the same phonetic-family clustering ("guessability") seen throughout the initials work, just ironically diverging from the very root character it's borrowed from. Cantonese doesn't explain the split either way — both the main `ㄚㄧ` group and this `ㄛㄧ` pocket freely mix `-aai` and `-oi` Cantonese rimes (e.g. 台 itself is `toi4` yet stays in the regular group). 該 (phonetic 亥, unrelated to 台) sits in this same `ㄛㄧ` bucket with no found explanation.
- **啡 → ㄧ (i)**: drops the diphthong entirely. This one is explained: 啡's own Korean reading (비) has a bare `i` vowel with no diphthong at all, and the Dan'a'yo output matches it exactly.
