> **Final 庚三合** splits cleanly by initial: ㄨㄧㄫ (null-initial 永, 栄, 泳, 詠) vs ⼄ㄫ (velar 兄, 囧, 憬)

## CJKV Evolution
庚三合 [ɣwiæŋ] has seven characters, and they split 4–3 into two groups with **no exceptions**. The four null-initial characters — 永, 栄, 泳, 詠 — land on **ㄨㄧㄫ** (`'wing`), matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`wing`); all four have Korean 영 and Cantonese *wing*. The three velar-initial characters — 兄 (x), 囧 and 憬 (k) — land instead on **⼄ㄫ** (`hyong`/`gyong`), with Korean 형/경 and Cantonese *hing*/*gwing*/*ging*, so the table's `wing` is not what they follow.

This is a genuine initial-conditioned rule rather than an arbitrary same-initial split: the outcome tracks whether the initial is null or velar with zero exceptions across all seven members, and each group's members share their initial class. The rime table does not distinguish the two outcomes, so its `wing` entry describes only the null-initial group.
