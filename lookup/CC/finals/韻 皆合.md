## CJKV Evolution
皆合 [ɣuɛi] is fully uniform: all 7 characters land on **⺢ㄧ**, matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`wai`, row 034 of 文法 - 99韻図) — a clean w-glide confirmation for a 合 final, unlike its 開 counterpart [[韻 皆開|皆開]], whose outcomes are more varied. The daughters keep the rounded onset: Mandarin *huái*/*guāi*/*wǎi*, Cantonese *waai*/*gwaai*/*wai*, Korean 회/괴/외, Vietnamese *hoài*/*quai*/*uy*. 懐 and 淮 share Mandarin *huái*, Cantonese *waai4* and Korean 회, and four characters (懐, 壊, 槐, 淮) share ㄏ⺢ㄧ, so the uniformity includes homophones that stay together.
