> **Final 物** evolved into ㄨㄊ, with two exception groups (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
物 [ɨut] is nearly uniform: 14 of 17 characters land on **ㄨㄊ**, matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`ut`). Four characters share the null initial (ʔ), and they form two homophone pairs, each split between the plain and the glide-fused spelling: **尉** (Korean 위, Vietnamese *uý*) and **熨** (also 위 and *uý*) are one pair, and **蔚** and **欝** are identical in all four daughters (Mandarin *yù*, Cantonese *wat1*, Korean 울, Vietnamese *uất*) and are the other. The plain ㄨㄊ is held by 尉 and 蔚, while **欝** and **熨** fuse to **⼜ㄊ** rather than collide with them, which agrees with the Mandarin *yù*/*yùn* and Cantonese *wat1*/*wan6* readings of the glide-fused pair. **不** (p-initial, ㄅ) lands on **ㄛㄊ**: its expected slot ㄅㄨㄊ is already held by **佛** (v-initial, also ㄅ), so it dodges by a vowel shift, a crowding-driven escape.
