> **Final 物** evolved into ㄨㄊ, with two exception groups (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
物 [ɨut] is nearly uniform: 13 of 16 characters land on **ㄨㄊ**, matching the Vowels table's documented winner (`ut`). Three characters share the null initial (ʔ): **尉** takes the plain ㄨㄊ, while **欝** and **熨** (both ʔ-initial, with Mandarin yù/yùn and Cantonese wat1/wan6 showing the glide) fuse to **⼜ㄊ** rather than collide with 尉. **不** (p-initial, ㄅ) lands on **ㄛㄊ**: its expected slot ㄅㄨㄊ is already held by **佛** (v-initial, also ㄅ), so it dodges by a vowel shift, a crowding-driven escape.
