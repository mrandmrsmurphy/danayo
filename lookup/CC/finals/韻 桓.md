> [Classical Chinese](../Classical%20Chinese.md)
> **Final 桓** splits into a w-glide group and a plain group along initial-class lines, with three coda-shift escapees (see below)

## CJKV Evolution
桓 [uɑn] splits into two large groups: 34 of 63 characters land on **⺢ㄋ** (w-glide), and 26 land on plain **ㄚㄋ** — both are documented Vowels-table forms (`wan`/`an` respectively appear as competing D values across nearby finals), so this final effectively carries both outcomes as a genuine split rather than one dominant winner with exceptions.

The split tracks initial class closely but not simply the labial w-glide ban seen elsewhere ([[韻 戈一合|戈一合]], [[韻 鈬合|鈬合]], [[韻 陽合|陽合]]): labials (b, p, m, f, v) avoid the w-glide as expected, but so do the coronal sonorants n and l, while the velar nasal ŋ (玩, 翫) patterns *with* the velars/dorsals in keeping the w-glide. The split is best read as place-of-articulation-conditioned rather than purely labial-conditioned.

Three further escapes complicate the picture: **換** (ɣ) and **浣** (ɣ) both dodge the crowded 4-member ɣ+w-glide slot (桓, 丸, 完, 緩) via coda shifts, to ⺢ㄇ and ⺢ㄎ respectively — a third same-initial arbitrary-outcome case for ɣ on top of its main-group scatter. **㒼** (m) dodges the crowded 6-member m slot on ㄚㄋ (満, 曼, 漫, 蔓, 饅, 𥈞) via a coda shift to ㄚㄇ.

Data-quality flag: **缶** (mandarin *fǒu*, Cantonese *fau2*, Korean 부) is tagged with initial k and this final, but its readings don't match a k-initial 桓 word at all — 缶's real Middle Chinese affiliation is the unrelated 幽 rime (*pjuwX*). Likely mistagging, following the same pattern flagged earlier for 屎/靡/鄙/豕.
