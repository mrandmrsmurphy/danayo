## CJKV Evolution
櫛 [ɪt] has only 3 characters, matching the Vowels table's own uncertain triple-outcome note (`it/ot/ut`) with a genuine split: 櫛 and 瑟 land on **ㄜㄊ**, while 虱 lands on **ㄛㄊ**. 瑟 and 虱 are a particularly clean illustration of an unconditioned split — both sibilant-initial (ʃ/s) and sharing the *identical* Korean reading (슬), yet landing on different vowels anyway.
