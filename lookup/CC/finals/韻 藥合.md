> [Classical Chinese](../Classical%20Chinese.md)
> **Final 藥合** has two characters, 縛 and 攫, on two different outcomes

## CJKV Evolution
藥合 [ʉɐk] has only two characters in the corpus. **縛** lands on **ㄚㄎ** — glide-free, consistent with the labial w-glide ban confirmed repeatedly elsewhere in this sweep ([[韻 戈一合|戈一合]], [[韻 鈬合|鈬合]], [[韻 陽合|陽合]]), since 縛's initial (v) is labial. Despite this, the Vowels table's documented winner for this final is `wak` — worth noting the mismatch, though a sample this small can't confirm which is representative. **攫** (k-initial, Mandarin *jué*, Cantonese *fok3*, Korean 확) lands on **ㄛㄎ** by fiat on its character page (its 注音 is ㄈㄛㄎ rather than the strictly derived ㄍ⺢ㄎ), so it is a deliberate free-syllable choice, not a phonological outcome.
