> **Final 戈三開** has two characters, 伽 and 諸, each landing on its own group (and 諸 looks mis-assigned; see below)

## CJKV Evolution
戈三開 [ɨɑ] has only two characters in the corpus. **伽** lands on **ㄍ⼘** — matching the y-glide quality of the Vowels table's documented winner (`ya`). **諸** (t͡ɕ-initial) lands on plain **ㄐㄚ** instead, with no y-glide.

Data-quality flag: 諸's own Mandarin (*zhū*), Cantonese (*zyu1*) and Korean (제) readings don't resemble 伽's (*jiā*, *gaa1*, 가) and look like a 魚-class final rather than a 戈-class one, so its `middle_chinese_final` may be mis-assigned. Its Notes link this page, so it stays here until the final is re-checked.
