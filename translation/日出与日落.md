## Analysis

### Line 1

> 写《自然与人生》的日本作家德富芦花，观察过落日。

#### 1. Structure

One topic, one comment.

| Slot | Chinese | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Relative clause | 写《自然与人生》的 | "who wrote *Nature and Life*", 的 as linker |
| Head noun phrase | 日本作家 德富芦花 | "Japanese writer" plus appositive name |
| Verb | 观察过 | observe, experiential aspect |
| Object | 落日 | the setting sun / the sunset as an event |

- **Relative clause:** 修飾先行 ("modifiers come first") already matches Chinese order. 之 replaces 的 in every case (05形態, 連体修飾), so the clause becomes `作…之`.
- **Aspect:** Chinese 过 marks experience ("has done this at least once"). Dan'a'yo has a dedicated 経験相 for that. Bare 已 would mean "completed", which is a different claim.
- **Syntax (書面体):** a 事詞 needs a subject and an object, and a name needs a 限定詞. All of this assumes 書面体 because the text is expository prose. 古雅体 is the alternative.

#### 2. Word by word

| Chinese | Vault status | Dan'a'yo candidate |
|---|---|---|
| 的 | 之 always | **之** |
| 日本 | word exists, ㄋㄧㄊㄅㄛㄋ | **日本** |
| 自然 | word exists, ㄐㄧㄜㄋ⼶ㄋ | **自然** |
| 与 | word exists, ⼄ | **与** |
| 人生 | word exists, ㄋㄧㄋㄙㄚㄫ | **人生** |

- **Experiential aspect:** the grammar's example is 無行已 for "has not been", so the experiential prefix and 已 both appear: **有観察已**.
- **Aliases:** the simplified 芦 in the source is already the vault headword. 德 is an alias of 徳, and 观 is an alias of 観.
- **日出:** this word exists but 日落 does not. Lines 3–5 use both, so the missing half matters.

#### 3. Decisions

**A. "Write" (写).**
1. **作**: free 事詞 "make/do", and it matches Classical Chinese "compose" (作《春秋》).
2. **筆記** or a similar compound.
3. Coin a verb.

Provisional: 作. Its generic gloss is wide, but the object (a book title) disambiguates it.

**B. "Writer" (作家).**
- **文学者** exists (alias 文学家), but its gloss is only "literati", which is broader than Chinese 作家.
- **作者** would be segmented, because 者 is a fully productive affix. It would mean "maker", not "author".
- **作家** would be a coinage. Split-before-coining argues against it, and 家 has no affix word, so it is only semi-productive.

Provisional: 文学者. Roka was a novelist and essayist, so it is accurate even if broad.

**C. "Sunset" (落日).**
- **日落事**: a 日+落 clause nominalized with －事, as 書面体 requires for a clause serving as topic or object.
- **日落** or **日没** as a coinage beside 日出. CJKV overlap:

| | Mandarin | Cantonese | Japanese | Korean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 日落 | yes | yes | no (uses 日没) | no (uses 일몰) |
| 日没 | rare | – | yes | yes |

- **黄昏** (glossed "sunset") and **夕陽** ("evening sun") already exist. But Mandarin 黄昏 is dusk as a time of day, and 夕陽 is the object, not the event.

Provisional: 日落事. Leaning 日落 for parallelism with 日出, but the CJKV overlap argues for 日没. Open.

**D. The name (徳富芦花).**
- Readings: 徳 ㄊㄨㄎ, 富 ㄈㄨㄛ, 芦 ㄌㄛ, 花 ㄏ⺢, giving ㄊㄨㄎㄈㄨㄛㄌㄛㄏ⺢. Syllable-boundary dot check not yet run.
- **Determiner position, unresolved inconsistency:** 04句法 says the 限定詞 follows the name ("must be followed by a 限定詞"), while the 阿 word page says it appears as a prefix. The draft above puts 阿 after the name, pending a ruling.
- **Title marker:** does the vault have a convention for 《》? The grammar does not mention one. The draft currently leaves the title unmarked.
